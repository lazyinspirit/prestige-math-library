These calculations use the A-page CAR relations, the fixed Majorana sign convention, and the occupation-basis matrix units.

- [[ex-one-mode-car-majorana-and-pauli-matrices]] computes the one-mode algebra as $M_2(\mathbb C)$ and its two Majorana generators as $\sigma_x,\sigma_y$.
- [[ex-two-mode-car-occupation-matrix-units]] checks the two-mode fermionic sign and an off-diagonal matrix unit in $M_4(\mathbb C)$.
- [[cex-countable-car-representations-are-not-all-fock]] refutes uniqueness of the irreducible Fock representation with the particle-hole-twisted representation.

The infinite-mode example distinguishes the countable CAR algebra from its chosen Fock representation; it does not assert a classification of irreducibles or physical spin-statistics.
