## Statement

Let $n\ge0$, let $V=\mathbb C^{2n}$ with ordered basis $e_1,\ldots,e_{2n}$, and put $Q(\sum_r z_re_r)=\sum_r z_r^2$. Use the plus-$Q$ convention of [[def-clifford-algebra-of-a-quadratic-space]]. Give $\mathrm{Cl}^+(V,Q)$ the conjugate-linear anti-involution that fixes each $e_r$; it is defined by conjugating coefficients, reversing words, and fixing the basis generators. Then there is a $*$-isomorphism

$$ \mathrm{Cl}^+(V,Q)\cong\mathsf{CAR}^{\mathrm{alg}}_n $$

specified by

$$ e_{2j-1}\longmapsto \gamma_{2j-1}=a_j+a_j^\dagger,\qquad e_{2j}\longmapsto \gamma_{2j}=i(a_j^\dagger-a_j). $$

The inverse sends $a_j$ to $(e_{2j-1}+ie_{2j})/2$ and $a_j^\dagger$ to $(e_{2j-1}-ie_{2j})/2$. These are finite-mode algebra maps; they assert no relation between CAR and CCR.

## Facts & Assumptions
**Given:** An integer $n\ge0$, the finite-mode CAR $*$-algebra, and the complex quadratic space $(V,Q)$ with its ordered basis and plus-$Q$ convention.

[F1] In $\mathsf{CAR}^{\mathrm{alg}}_n$, $\{a_i,a_j\}=0$, $\{a_i^\dagger,a_j^\dagger\}=0$, and $\{a_i,a_j^\dagger\}=\delta_{ij}1$; its quotient presentation gives the unique $*$-homomorphism for every tuple satisfying these relations ([[def-finite-mode-car-algebra]]).

[F2] The plus-$Q$ Clifford algebra is the quotient with canonical generators satisfying $\bar v^{\,2}=Q(v)1$ ([[def-clifford-algebra-of-a-quadratic-space]]).

[F3] Its polarized relation is $\bar v\bar w+\bar w\bar v=2B(v,w)1$ ([[lem-clifford-polarized-relations]]).

[F4] Every linear map into a unital associative $k$-algebra satisfying $f(v)^2=Q(v)1$ extends uniquely to a unital algebra map from $\mathrm{Cl}(V,Q)$ ([[thm-universal-property-of-clifford-algebra]]).

## Proof

1.1 On $T_{\mathbb C}(V)$ define $e_r^*=e_r$, conjugate scalars, and reverse each tensor word. For $v=\sum_r z_re_r$, let $\overline v=\sum_r\overline{z_r}e_r$. Then the adjoint of the defining relation $j(v)^2-Q(v)1$ is $j(\overline v)^2-\overline{Q(v)}1=j(\overline v)^2-Q(\overline v)1$, another defining relation. The generated two-sided ideal is therefore $*$-stable, so this operation descends to the stated anti-involution on the Clifford quotient. [given, F2, construct, algebra]

1.2 By [F1], $\gamma_{2j-1}^*=\gamma_{2j-1}$ and $\gamma_{2j}^*=\gamma_{2j}$. The same-mode relations give $\gamma_{2j-1}^2=a_ja_j^\dagger+a_j^\dagger a_j=1$ and $\gamma_{2j}^2=-(a_j^\dagger-a_j)^2=a_j^\dagger a_j+a_ja_j^\dagger=1$. Also $\{\gamma_{2j-1},\gamma_{2j}\}=i\{a_j+a_j^\dagger,a_j^\dagger-a_j\}=0$. For distinct modes every term in the expanded anticommutator vanishes by [F1]. Thus $\{\gamma_r,\gamma_s\}=2\delta_{rs}1$. [F1, algebra]

2.1 For $v=\sum_r z_re_r$, linearity and step 1.2 give $(\sum_r z_r\gamma_r)^2=\sum_r z_r^2\,1+\sum_{r<s}z_rz_s\{\gamma_r,\gamma_s\}=\sum_r z_r^2\,1=Q(v)1$. Consequently [F4] gives a unique unital algebra map $\Phi:\mathrm{Cl}^+(V,Q)\to\mathsf{CAR}^{\mathrm{alg}}_n$ with $\Phi(e_r)=\gamma_r$. Since each $\gamma_r$ is self-adjoint, $\Phi$ preserves $*$. [given, F4, step 1.2, construct]

2.2 Put $b_j=(e_{2j-1}+ie_{2j})/2$ and $b_j^\dagger=(e_{2j-1}-ie_{2j})/2$ in the Clifford algebra. By [F3], $\{e_r,e_s\}=2\delta_{rs}1$. Expanding the three CAR anticommutators for the $b_j,b_j^\dagger$ therefore gives $\{b_i,b_j\}=0$, $\{b_i^\dagger,b_j^\dagger\}=0$, and $\{b_i,b_j^\dagger\}=\delta_{ij}1$. The anti-involution from step 1.1 gives $b_j^*=b_j^\dagger$. The quotient definition in [F1] now yields a unique $*$-homomorphism $\Psi:\mathsf{CAR}^{\mathrm{alg}}_n\to\mathrm{Cl}^+(V,Q)$ with these generator images. [given, F1, F3, step 1.1, construct]

3.1 On each generator, $\Phi\Psi(a_j)=(\gamma_{2j-1}+i\gamma_{2j})/2=a_j$ and $\Phi\Psi(a_j^\dagger)=(\gamma_{2j-1}-i\gamma_{2j})/2=a_j^\dagger$; likewise $\Psi\Phi(e_{2j-1})=e_{2j-1}$ and $\Psi\Phi(e_{2j})=e_{2j}$. The two composites are identities because each algebra is generated by the displayed generators and both maps preserve its operations. Thus $\Phi$ and $\Psi$ are inverse $*$-isomorphisms. [step 2.1, step 2.2, algebra] ∎

## Remarks

The scalar-product pairing convention is irrelevant to these generator relations: the modes are indexed by an orthonormal basis and the CAR use only $\delta_{ij}$. The sign is fixed by $e_r^2=+1$.
