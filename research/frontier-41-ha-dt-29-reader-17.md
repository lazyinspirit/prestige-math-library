### `lem-restriction-of-formal-immersion-data-has-the-parametric-lifting-property`

- Location: Statement; Facts [L1]–[L2]; Proof 1.1–4.1 (especially 4.1).
- Class: `false-claim`; severity: `fatal`; subject: `in-flight-item`.

The unrestricted genuine-restriction fibration is false. Take X=[0,1], A=[0,1/4] union [3/4,1], N=R, initial immersion id, and collar homotopy g_t(x)=x+2t on the left and x-2t on the right. A corrected formal-only theorem needs a complete section-extension/fibration proof; genuine lifting needs the actual dimension-qualified Hirsch–Smale argument. Withdrawal/replacement requires Step 5b; item retained.
