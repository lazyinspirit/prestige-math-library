## Mathematical review

F2 gives smooth factorization only for connected integral immersions. Delta:H×H→G is not an integral immersion of D (its source generally has twice the leaf dimension). More seriously, the assertion that any ambient-smooth map with image setwise in a leaf is smooth for the intrinsic leaf topology is false without a local plaque or tangency argument: immersed leaves may revisit one Frobenius chart in distinct plaques. Step 3.1 has neither. Setwise subgroup closure from Step 2.1 is sound, but it does not itself make multiplication/division smooth as H-valued maps.

## Repair disposition

First show every exp_G(tX), X∈h, lies in H by the maximal-leaf factorization of its integral curve; the resulting exp_G|_h has identity differential and hence gives an intrinsic H-chart near e by the inverse-function theorem. Connectedness makes H generated by this intrinsic identity neighbourhood. For each b∈H, prove Ad_b(h)=h, first for b=exp_G X using Ad_exp X=exp(ad X) and then for finite products. Only now does dδ on T(H×H) land in D: left translations preserve D and right translation/inversion preserve it by Ad_b(h)=h. In a Frobenius chart, the transverse coordinates of δ are then locally constant on connected source patches, forcing its image into one plaque; its longitudinal coordinates give a smooth H-valued factor. This avoids the false claim that setwise leaf image alone suffices and requires the exact exponential/adjoint suppliers to be declared.
