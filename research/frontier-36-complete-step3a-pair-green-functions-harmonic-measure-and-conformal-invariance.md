The material omission is a **general conformal covariance theorem for Green kernels**. The planned `thm-green-function-simply-connected-plane-domain` gives the Riemann-map formula only for simply connected domains. `thm-harmonic-measure-conformal-invariance` concerns harmonic measure and requires a homeomorphism of closures. The B slit-plane item demonstrates one pullback, but there is no reusable result for a conformal bijection between arbitrary Greenian plane domains. That covariance is a basic planar conformal refinement of the Green-kernel definition and is needed to make the title and CA-HM-1 role complete. The *Encyclopedia of Mathematics*, “Green function in function theory,” states conformal invariance and gives the minimum-property setting (https://encyclopediaofmath.org/index.php?title=Green_function, lines 164–170). The complete argument is local: composition with a conformal bijection preserves positivity and harmonicity off the pole; the local expansion (f(z)-f(a)=f'(a)(z-a)+O((z-a)^2)) preserves the unit logarithmic coefficient with a harmonic correction; transporting competing candidates by (f) and (f^{-1}) and applying leastness in both directions gives equality. No boundary extension is needed for this Green-kernel statement.

**Recommended owner action:** enrich A with the theorem that for a conformal bijection (f:\Omega\to\Omega') between Greenian plane domains, (g_\Omega(z,a)=g_{\Omega'}(f(z),f(a))). Have the slit-plane example use it. No pair merger is indicated. This is a scope recommendation only; no scaffold was edited. The only interpretive uncertainty is whether the title intended “conformal invariance” to mean harmonic measure alone; if so, that narrower title/scope should be made explicit before proceeding.
