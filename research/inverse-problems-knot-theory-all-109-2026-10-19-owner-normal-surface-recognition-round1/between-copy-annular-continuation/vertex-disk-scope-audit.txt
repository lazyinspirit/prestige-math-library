Hass–Lagarias, The number of Reidemeister moves needed for unknotting,
arXiv math/9807012; JAMS 14 (2001), 399–428.
Read actual locally cached /tmp/knot-normal-source/reidemeister.txt, Theorem 3.1,
complete adjacent proof and Remark (printed pages 10–11 in this arXiv text).
The theorem states fundamental normal essential disk existence for irreducible,
orientable, compact M with boundary on a specified boundary component.
Its proof cites normalisation and JT Cor. 6.4, explicitly noting that the latter
has the stronger vertex conclusion. The following Remark asserts that
compactness and irreducibility can be removed, but gives no argument.
Its referent is linguistically ambiguous between the stated theorem and the
stronger assertion just noted in its proof. It is therefore evidence for an
author assertion of an extension, not an unambiguous standalone statement or
proof of arbitrary-M vertex disk existence. We should not say the authors
explicitly deny vertex strength, nor that this Remark proves it.
The public author-hosted PDF web reader has a version of Theorem 3.1 without
orientability in the statement; the same proof/Remark remain. The binary was
not downloadable locally (403); its mathematical proof has not been newly read
in full. URL: https://websites.umich.edu/~lagarias/doc/reidemeister.pdf
