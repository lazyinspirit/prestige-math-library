2. **Central actions — `lem-central-actions-on-nilradical-cohomology-factor-through-harish-chandra`, Proof 1.3.** Naturality for coefficient-module homomorphisms alone does not prove Cartan equivariance because the cochain Lie derivative is not generally an n-linear coefficient endomorphism. Added the cocycle/lift construction of the connecting map and commutation with θ_h and d. This proves equivariance for U(h), hence pr(z), without changing the unshifted identity. Al-Faisal Proposition 3.2.9, printed p.70, gives the same lift argument; Theorem 3.3.1, p.72, supplies the full dimension-shifting route.

The subset bijection, coefficient-one invariant, equality-case chain of inequalities and unique degree/weight argument remain intact.

4. **Laplacian — `lem-kostant-laplacian-is-scalar-on-weight-components`, Statement and Proof 1.1/1.3.** Clarified that the positive Hermitian form is invariant under the compact real form. The old passage asserted root-metric positivity, root adjoints and weight orthogonality without supplying their derivations. Added negative definiteness of B on the compact real form from the unitarized adjoint representation, positivity of −B(x,τx), the compatible Cartan/root conjugation, root-vector normalization, π(x)*=−π(τx) and self-adjointness on the real Cartan span. The published contraction suppliers are stated for real spaces; added the orthonormal complex-wedge basis calculation to justify their complex use. The normal-ordering, Jacobi, trace and Casimir calculations were reviewed with their written coefficients and signs.
