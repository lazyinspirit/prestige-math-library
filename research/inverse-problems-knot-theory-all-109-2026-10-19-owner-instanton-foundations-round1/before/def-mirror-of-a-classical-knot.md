## Definition

Fix the orientation-reversing involution $r:S^3\to S^3$ given by reflection across a fixed equatorial two-sphere containing a chosen standard unknot $U$. For a smooth knot $K\subset S^3$, its mirror is $\overline K:=r(K)$, with the orientation transported by $r$. On a diagram this reflects the projection and interchanges the over- and under-crossing data. Since $r^2=\mathrm{id}$ and $r(U)=U$, mirroring twice returns $K$, and $K$ is the unknot exactly when $\overline K$ is.
