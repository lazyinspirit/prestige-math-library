## Repairs

1. In the A-page Sobolev summary, replaced the claim that the bracket and Laplacian weights “differ by the factor $4\pi^2$” away from zero. For real $t$, their ratio is
   \[
   \left(\frac{1+4\pi^2|\xi|^2}{1+|\xi|^2}\right)^{t/2},
   \]
   which is bounded above and below by positive constants, differs from $1$ at every nonzero frequency when $t\ne0$, and tends to $(2\pi)^t$ at high frequency. This follows directly from `def-japanese-bracket-bessel-potential-operator` and step 1.2 of `lem-bessel-potentials-shift-sobolev-order-isometrically`.

2. Repaired step 3.1 of `items/lem-bessel-potentials-shift-sobolev-order-isometrically.md`. The previous witness set $\chi=1-b$ for a compactly supported bump $b$; it is constant $1$ outside a bounded set, so it is not Schwartz and does not have compact support. This gives a nonzero $\chi\in C_c^\infty\subset\mathcal S$, supported where the ratio is strictly above or below $1$; its inverse Fourier transform is Schwartz and its weighted class is nonzero in $L^2$.
