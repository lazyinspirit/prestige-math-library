## Complete arguments checked

- Ample projective immersion: choose finite affine section opens, extend their finite coordinate generators after clearing section powers, bring all sections to one tensor degree, and obtain surjective chart coordinate maps. Target locality makes the resulting map a closed immersion into the union of those charts, hence locally closed in projective space. Separated finite type and the ample hypothesis are sufficient; emptiness is covered.
- Ampleness field descent: for each coherent sheaf, finite affine-cover equalizers commute with flat scalar extension, and faithful flatness kills the cokernel of its evaluation map. Serre's eventual generation criterion applies on the Noetherian finite-type scheme. The same given bundle descends; no chosen geometric bundle is asserted to descend.
- Smooth group ampleness: finite étale parameter family over a principal affine-space open pushes the translated Cartier boundary to a codimension-one support on the smooth product. Local factoriality makes it Cartier, affine-space parameter constancy supplies a bundle over k, and geometric parameter points avoiding each prescribed point give affine nonvanishing loci covering the group. Their affineness follows from finite intersections of affine opens in a separated scheme. Ampleness of this bundle descends. The argument permits finite and imperfect fields and handles dimension zero.
- Abelian projectivity: the preceding k-defined ample bundle on a proper smooth geometrically integral group, followed by the proper ample-power theorem, gives a closed immersion over k. No Barsotti–Chevalley input or polarization is used.
- Cube: double Čech Künneth gives injectivity of H¹ restriction to the two axes. A line bundle and its dual give a closed fibre-triviality locus through nonnegative finite-complex determinants. Square-zero induction kills each obstruction using that H¹ injection; normalization at the identity gives compatible sections. Faithful completion and the degree-zero base-change map yield a genuine local generator. Properness removes its zero locus, so the locus is open as well as closed; connectedness and identity normalization give global triviality. The seven-factor specialization cancels on all three faces. AC/DC and arbitrary residue-field fibres are retained.
- Multiplication: Segre constructs a symmetric very ample bundle; the cube recurrence gives [n]*N=N^(n²), even when the characteristic divides n. A positive-dimensional proper fibre component would have a trivial ample bundle, impossible by scalar global functions. Finite image dimension gives surjectivity. Generic flatness over an algebraic closure spreads by translations to all closed source points; ideal injectivity and openness of finite-module freeness then give global flatness, which descends. Hilbert leading coefficients give rank |n|^(2g). Scheme kernel, finite-cover divisibility, negative n and g=0 are included.
- Perfect-field pseudo-abelian completeness: after separable algebraic scalar extension, A=(Z_red)^0 is proper by the dichotomy and pseudo-abelian condition, since its affine subgroups are central. Stable jets embed G/Z in GL(V); Z/A is finite, so G/A is affine. A smooth normal almost-complement H is affine as an extension by its finite intersection with A. Pseudo-abelianness kills H, and the faithfully flat closed immersion A→G is an isomorphism. Properness descends. This avoids a claim that G already has only scalar functions and avoids assuming an abelian quotient in advance.
- Perfect-field Barsotti–Chevalley: the exact pseudo-abelian reduction supplies the largest smooth connected affine normal N; completeness supplies an abelian quotient. Uniqueness follows from the reduction's uniqueness, since proper smooth connected groups have no nontrivial smooth connected affine subgroup. The current two-step proof is complete with perfectness, smooth connected source, scheme kernel and fppf projection intact.
- Arbitrary-field Barsotti–Chevalley: a smooth source is first handled over the perfection, then at a finite purely inseparable stage; finite equations descend the normal affine subgroup, and faithful extension verifies its properties. Power ideals supply a connected affine nilpotent thickening over k. The proper abelian source surjects onto the new quotient, proving properness after every base change and then descent. For a nonsmooth source, high Frobenius uses its smooth image, with finite connected kernel, and pulls back the smooth-source affine kernel. The final coset sheaf is the abelian quotient. Existence is preserved, without adding smoothness or uniqueness of N.
