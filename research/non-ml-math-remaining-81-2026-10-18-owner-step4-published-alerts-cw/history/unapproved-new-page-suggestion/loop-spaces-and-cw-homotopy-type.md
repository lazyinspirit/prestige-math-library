The based loop space of an arbitrary connected CW complex has CW homotopy
type. The proof retains arbitrary cell cardinalities and unbounded dimension:
cellwise homotopy lifting turns CW domination into an actual equivalence,
a compatible simplicial replacement supplies a metric homotopy model, and
explicit local loop contractions produce a nerve domination. This gives the
local prerequisite for the strict path-loop Eilenberg–Mac Lane comparison.
