The global condition is not rewritten as an `L²` normal-cone claim because the supplied KKT normal-cone definition has a finite-dimensional domain.

2. The broad LQR branch has `A,B∈L∞` equivalence classes, while RK stages sample pointwise values. An equivalence class has no canonical stage value. The LQR existence model therefore keeps measurable coefficients, while `def-ctrl1-smooth-transcription-data` is a separate pointwise-defined branch with continuous data and the smoothness needed for finite NLP derivatives. No stage-sampling or convergence claim is made for an `L∞` class without an added representative and rule.

The plan also mentions discretization rates. Ordinary ODE RK order conditions alone do not imply the same order for an optimal-control objective, adjoint, or gradient. The numerical order theorem is conditional on the additional state, adjoint, derivative-data, endpoint, and quadrature bounds supplied by the actual discrete/continuous adjoint interface. The local mesh theorem below assumes uniform objective consistency and a feasible recovery sequence; it makes no rate claim. The finite error conversion assumes the objective error is already bounded and does not treat an embedded adaptive difference as a certified global error.

## Proof routes and axiom use

- The measurable linear state map is constructed by contraction on a finite partition, with a full uniqueness and norm argument. The control set is weakly closed by countable rational support inequalities. Weakly convergent bounded controls have uniformly convergent states by a finite-grid equicontinuity argument; no Arzelà–Ascoli dependency is hidden.
- Cell averages stay in an arbitrary compact convex actuator set by a nearest-point separation proof, then converge strongly using the (L^2) contraction and density of interval step functions. No recovery claim is made for additional path or terminal constraints.
- LQR existence uses the direct-method theorem and exactly the ultrafilter lemma, Dependent Choice, and Hahn–Banach; Dependent Choice supplies Countable Choice for the (L^2) Hilbert/reflexivity route. No full Axiom of Choice is asserted. The weak lower semicontinuity proof is local: the state and terminal terms converge under uniform state convergence, and the positive control quadratic is weakly lower semicontinuous by its supremum-of-affine-minorants identity.
- The adjoint terminal equation is solved by time reversal of the measurable linear state lemma. The gradient identity uses the declared absolutely continuous integration-by-parts supplier and the complete quadratic remainder. The VI uses only the global feasible-set condition unless the feasible set is control-only; the countable dense-set argument uses Dependent Choice/Countable Choice explicitly.
- The indirect shooting algorithm is restricted to an interior smooth control branch with a supplied C2 feedback satisfying Hamiltonian stationarity. It calls the BVP solver with a certified evaluator and reports only its computed residual status.
- The transcription defines explicit-map single/multiple shooting and stage collocation for general explicit or implicit RK tableaux. In the implicit case, the coupled stage equations stay as equality constraints in the NLP; no stage root is assumed solved during construction. Its optional adaptive estimate does not define the smooth NLP map or certify global error. The QP lemma requires nonnegative quadrature weights, positive-semidefinite quadratic forms, polyhedral controls, and affine constraints. The convex solver route also requires an admissible strict start; otherwise the finite wrapper returns/falls back to an explicit SQP status.
- The mesh-limit theorem assumes exact discrete feasibility inside the continuous feasible set, uniform objective consistency, global discrete minimizers, and strong feasible recovery. The control-only cell-average lemma supplies recovery in that special case. The LQR error theorem derives the bound (2\delta_N+\eta_N+\rho_N) and the (\sqrt{2/r}) control estimate from uniform objective error, optimization gap, and comparison recovery. It claims no a posteriori certificate for unvalidated embedded differences.
- Exact-Hessian SQP convergence is stated for a fixed smooth transcription after correct active-set identification, under LICQ, strict complementarity, SOSC, and a locally Lipschitz active KKT Jacobian. Finite dispatcher budgets may stop before convergence. No mesh-limit KKT theorem is claimed.
