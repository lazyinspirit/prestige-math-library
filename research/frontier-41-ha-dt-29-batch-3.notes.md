## Dependency and mathematical audit

Conventions used throughout: the library's descending convention (`df(X)<0`, `X=(2u,-2v)` in Morse charts); a $k$-handle has attaching sphere $S^{k-1}$ and belt sphere $S^{n-k-1}$; in the middle boundary after the lower handle, an adjacent pair has $\dim A+\dim B=k+(n-k-1)=n-1$. Two endpoint cases are printed in the definition because one of the two spheres is then a whole boundary component: $k=0$ (the belt sphere of the $0$-handle is the new $S^{n-1}$ component, and one point of the attaching $0$-sphere lies in it) and $k=n-1$ (dually, the $n$-handle's attaching sphere is a whole component and exactly one point of the belt $0$-sphere lies in it). Wall's Lemma 5.4.2 and Lück's Example 1.11/Lemma 1.12 cover both endpoints, so the cancellation and creation theorems are stated for $0\le k\le n-1$.
