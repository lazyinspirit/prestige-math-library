**Status: integrated draft; 37 proposed A/B pairs; no pages or items authored.**
All six assigned source dossiers and their source manifests are complete and
read. Their source anchors, proof routes, limitations and the remaining local
proof obligations are distinguished below.
Pair labels MSF-01–MSF-37 are relative labels, not
canonical orders. The proposed A-page slugs are the stable cross-category interface for this
draft; each B companion is the same slug followed by `-examples`. The proposed category
directory is `mathematical-statistics/`.

This block is Category 1. Its new-page dependencies point only backward to
earlier MSF A pages or to the inspected, published general-mathematics pages
listed below. It takes no definition, theorem or result from statistical
models/experiments (Category 2), Bayesian/computational statistics (Category
3), or machine learning/adaptive decision methods (Category 4). Numerical
analysis and numerical algorithms remain separate suppliers. Operational
procedures are specified by algorithm items with definition bodies; their
mathematical guarantees are separate theorem items.
Data-generating assumptions are stated as hypotheses and are not presented as
empirically established by mathematical proof.
