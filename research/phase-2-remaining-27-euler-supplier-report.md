## 2. The mathematics of the new supplier

Statement (four clauses): ρ(q)(v) = qvq^{-1} is a continuous surjective
two-sheeted covering homomorphism S³ → SO(3) with kernel {±1} (and
SO(3) ≅ S³/{±1}); covering projections induce isomorphisms on π_n for n ≥ 2, so
ρ_*: π₃(S³,1) → π₃(SO(3),I) is an isomorphism; π₃(S³,1) ≅ Z by degree, hence
π₃(SO(3),I) ≅ Z is generated by [ρ]; and the clutching construction over the
equatorial S³ gives a nontrivial oriented rank-three real bundle E_ρ → S⁴.

Proof architecture (all prerequisites are earlier in reading order and inside
the A page's `requires` closure, verified by `validate-plan`):

* Quaternion algebra: (xy)‾ = ȳx̄ and N(xy) = N(x)N(y) from the displayed
  product formula, so |x|² = N(x) and q^{-1} = q̄ for unit q (steps 1.1, 2.1).
* Conjugation formula: for unit q = a + r with s = |r| and u = r/s, expanding by
  distributivity gives quq̄ = u, qwq̄ = (a²−s²)w + 2as·uw and
  q(uw)q̄ = (a²−s²)uw − 2as·w for w ⊥ u (step 3.1); hence norm preservation
  (step 4.1), orthogonality with determinant 1 computed from the Leibniz formula
  (step 5.1), and the explicit inverse rotation
  q₀ = cos(θ/2) + sin(θ/2)u using the double-angle identities (steps 3.2, 4.2),
  which proves surjectivity by the fixed-vector argument det(R − I) = 0
  (steps 1.3, 3.2).
* Covering: the {±1} action is a covering-space action because a unit ball
  about q is disjoint from its negative by the parallelogram identity
  (step 2.3); ρ is constant on orbits with fibres exactly the two-point orbits,
  so it factors through a continuous bijection S³/{±1} → SO(3) (steps 6.1, 7.1)
  which is a homeomorphism by the compact-to-Hausdorff clause (step 8.1), and
  composition with the orbit covering makes ρ a two-sheeted covering (step 9.1).
* π_n for n ≥ 2: injectivity by homotopy lifting plus constancy of the lift along
  the boundary paths (step 1.7); surjectivity by the covering lifting criterion,
  using that I^n is convex (hence path-connected, locally path-connected, with
  trivial π₁) and that ∂I^n is connected for n ≥ 2 (steps 1.4, 1.5, 2.4); the
  general isomorphism and the case ρ_* are assembled in step 10.1.
* π₃ computation and clutching: π₃(S³,1) ≅ Z with the identity as generator and
  the constant class of degree 0 (step 1.6); π₃(SO(3),I) ≅ Z generated by [ρ]
  (step 11.1); ρ is not nullhomotopic, because a nullhomotopy lifts to a free
  nullhomotopy of the identity of S³, contradicting degree (step 10.2); a
  constant clutching map gives the trivial bundle by re-parametrising the lower
  cone (step 1.8); hence E_ρ is nontrivial (step 12.1).

No choice principle is used; the only selections are single vectors in steps 1.3
and 3.2 and finite data elsewhere. The counterexample's own AC assumption is
unchanged and is still used by its other steps (UCT, bundle splitting).
