# Clifford PBW: a noncircular proof supplier plan

Root assistance, 2026-10-17. This is an independently checked mathematical argument for the planning team, not an authored or accepted library item. The literature statement remains source-backed by the Clifford dossier. It addresses the insufficient phrase “reduce and compare leading terms,” which by itself proves spanning but not independence.

Let k be a field of characteristic different from 2, V a supplied finite-dimensional vector space with ordered basis e_1,…,e_n, and B a symmetric bilinear form. Set Q(v)=B(v,v). The Clifford algebra is the quotient of T(V) by the two-sided ideal generated by v⊗v−Q(v)1.

On ΛV define ε_v(ω)=v∧ω. Define contraction on a decomposable degree-r wedge by

$$\iota_v(w_1\wedge\cdots\wedge w_r)=\sum_{j=1}^r(-1)^{j-1}B(v,w_j)w_1\wedge\cdots\wedge\widehat{w_j}\wedge\cdots\wedge w_r,$$

and set ι_v(1)=0. Well-definedness follows because the displayed multilinear function is alternating in its w_j; it therefore factors through the exterior power. Checking on decomposable wedges gives ε_vε_w+ε_wε_v=0, ι_vι_w+ι_wι_v=0 and ι_vε_w+ε_wι_v=B(v,w)I. For the contraction-contraction identity, each term deleting two distinct positions occurs twice with opposite signs; there are no same-position terms. For the mixed identity, the term contracting the newly inserted w is B(v,w) times the original wedge; all terms contracting an old factor cancel the corresponding terms in ε_wι_v.

Consequently c(v)=ε_v+ι_v satisfies c(v)^2=Q(v)I and c(v)c(w)+c(w)c(v)=2B(v,w)I. The universal property of the tensor-algebra quotient gives a homomorphism Cl(V,Q)→End_k(ΛV). This constructs a representation without assuming Clifford PBW.

The relations e_j e_i=−e_i e_j+2B(e_i,e_j)1 and e_i²=Q(e_i)1 show that increasing square-free words span the quotient. One termination argument orders each word first by length and then by inversion count: deleting a repeated adjacent generator or taking a scalar correction lowers length, while swapping an inverted adjacent pair lowers inversions in the same length; a sorted word with repetitions has an adjacent repetition. Induction therefore terminates. This uses no independence assumption.

For an increasing word I=(i_1<⋯<i_r), apply c(e_i1)⋯c(e_ir) to 1. Its exterior degree-r component is exactly e_i1∧⋯∧e_ir, and every other component has smaller degree, because contractions lower rather than raise degree. In a putative nontrivial linear relation among the increasing words, choose the greatest length r with nonzero coefficient and apply its representation to 1. The degree-r component is a relation among distinct exterior-basis wedges, so every coefficient at that length vanishes. Descending in r proves independence. This argument works for degenerate B and does not need an orthogonal basis.

Thus the square-free ordered words form a basis and dim Cl(V,Q)=2^n. The word filtration has degree-r quotient spanned independently by its length-r words. Its symbol multiplication is alternating since the Clifford correction has length zero, so the natural map ΛV→gr Cl(V,Q) is an isomorphism degree by degree. The graded-isomorphism conclusion is obtained after independence; it is not assumed to prove independence.

Potential reused local suppliers: tensor algebra and quotient universal properties, exterior algebra and its ordered-wedge basis, filtered/graded algebra definitions, elementary endomorphism algebra. Their exact existing IDs and statements must be checked before native authoring. Missing contraction/wedge identities should be intermediate local lemmas rather than silently assumed. Characteristic-2 quadratic forms need a separate contract and are outside this note.
