# Source manifest — other physics-connected algebras

Research snapshot: 2026-10-17. PDFs were retrieved into this lane directory and text extracted with Python/PyMuPDF 1.27.2.3. Extraction is not evidence that every extracted page was read. Page locators below refer to the local PDF page numbering unless marked “printed”. This is selected full-text research, not an exhaustive reading of any field. The only source opened as a complete browser-extracted PDF without a local mirror is Williamson’s short Brauer/BMW note; a direct shell request to its server returned 404 in this environment.

## Weyl/CCR, operator algebras, and AQFT

- **N. P. Landsman, _Mathematical Topics Between Classical and Quantum Mechanics_ (Springer Monographs in Mathematics, 1998).** URL: https://www.math.ru.nl/~landsman/1998book.pdf. Local files: `landsman-1998-mathematical-topics-classical-quantum.pdf/.txt` (547 PDF pages; about 58 MB). Read PDF pp. 55–61 (printed pp. 37–43), the Jordan–Lie/C*-algebra and observable-algebra set-up, and PDF pp. 149–157 (printed pp. 131–139), the Heisenberg/Weyl representation, Fock creation-annihilation operators, coherent states, and strict Berezin quantization on flat space. Consulted the contents and introductory overview for the monograph’s scope and chapter locators. The rest of the book was not read.
- **Roland Speicher, _Mathematical Aspects of Quantum Mechanics_ (Saarland University notes, winter term 2020/21).** URL: https://www.uni-saarland.de/fileadmin/upload/lehrstuhl/speicher/Math_of_QM/MathematicalAspects.pdf. Local files: `saarland-2020-mathematical-aspects-quantum-mechanics.pdf/.txt` (68 pages). Read selected sections on strongly continuous one-parameter groups and Stone’s theorem, the Schrödinger position/momentum representation, Weyl relations, finite-dimensional Stone–von Neumann uniqueness, CCR Fock space, and the CCR C*-algebra/GNS perspective (PDF pp. 38–49, 59–62; selected surrounding pages). The source explicitly distinguishes finite from infinite degrees of freedom; the latter need not have unique representations.
- **Christopher J. Fewster and Kasia Rejzner, _Algebraic Quantum Field Theory—an introduction_, arXiv:1904.04051v2, 18 Nov 2019.** URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1904.04051v2. Local files: `fewster-rejzner-2019-aqft-introduction.pdf/.txt` (47 pages). Read selected PDF pp. 2–3, 10–15, and 18–20: the role of inequivalent CCR representations; the van Hove model; the split between algebraic relations and Hilbert-space realization; the local-net axioms; scalar Weyl relations and C*-completion; and the nonregular tracial Weyl state. Other AQFT topics in the article were not comprehensively read.
- **Klaus Fredenhagen and Katarzyna Rejzner, _Perturbative algebraic quantum field theory_, arXiv:1208.1428v2, 27 Feb 2013.** URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1208.1428. Local files: `fredenhagen-rejzner-2012-perturbative-aqft.pdf/.txt` (42 pages). Consulted PDF pp. 23–28: the normal-ordered product on microcausal functionals, explicit Wick-square product, CCR commutator and Fock GNS state, formal interaction series, and the obstruction to multiplying distributions at coincident points followed by the extension/local-counterterm theorem. The broader renormalization and interacting-theory development was not audited here.
- **Geordie Williamson, _An Introduction to the Brauer and BMW Algebras (with an emphasis on the canonical basis and cells of BMW)_.** Author-hosted complete PDF: https://people.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/geordie/BMW.pdf (38 pages; no publication date is printed in the accessed copy). The complete PDF text was retrieved by the browser tool; the attempted local shell download returned 404, so no PDF/TXT mirror is present in this directory. Read selected PDF pp. 3–11 and 16–27: Brauer diagrams and concatenation, tangles/BMW relations, monoid-algebra bases, canonical bases/cells, and the small BMW₂/BMW₃ calculations. Do not treat this note as coverage of the general BMW cellular/PBW theory.

## Poisson geometry and deformation quantization

- **Maxim Kontsevich, _Deformation quantization of Poisson manifolds_, final version of q-alg/9709040.** URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/q-alg/9709040. Local files: `kontsevich-1997-deformation-quantization.pdf/.txt` (57 pages). Read the introductory formulation, the star-product and formality statements, the graph formula, and selected proof sections (PDF pp. 2–15, 20–24, 28–45, with selected later discussion pp. 49–57). The article proves formality by configuration-space integrals and boundary-stratum/Stokes analysis; this dossier does not claim a line-by-line independent audit of the entire proof.
- **Nima Moshayedi, _Lectures on Symplectic Geometry, Poisson Geometry, Deformation Quantization and Quantum Field Theory_, arXiv:2012.14662v1, 29 Dec 2020.** URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.14662. Local files: `moshayedi-2020-poisson-deformation-qft.pdf/.txt` (165 pages). Read the Poisson-bivector/Schouten criterion and examples, star-product definitions, Moyal and Fedosov products, DGLA/L∞ formality and Kontsevich graph construction, and selected Poisson sigma-model/BV discussion (PDF pp. 75–83, 85–104, 150–158). The path-integral discussion is a formal perturbative derivation, not a construction of a rigorous measure.

## Graded, homotopy, vertex, and W-algebras

- **Bernhard Keller, _Introduction to A-infinity algebras and modules_, arXiv:math/9910179v2, 12 Jan 2001.** URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9910179. Local files: `keller-1999-a-infinity.pdf/.txt` (32 pages). Read selected PDF pp. 1–10 and 18–28: motivation, sign/degree conventions, A∞ identities, morphisms, minimal models, higher multiplications/Massey products, modules, and twisted objects. Keller states that some results are given without proof in this introduction; the notes do not constitute a local proof of every minimal-model or derived-category theorem.
- **Tom Lada and Jim Stasheff, _Introduction to sh Lie algebras for physicists_, arXiv:hep-th/9209099v1, 24 Sep 1992.** URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9209099. Local files: `lada-stasheff-1992-sh-lie.pdf/.txt` (13 pages). Read PDF pp. 1–8: coderivation-on-the-cofree-cocommutative-coalgebra formulation, shifted signs, (l_1,l_2,l_3) interpretation, Jacobi up to BRST-exact homotopy, and string-field-theory motivation. Their formulas are convention-dependent and require the stated shift/Koszul signs.
- **Ezra Getzler, _Batalin–Vilkovisky algebras and two-dimensional topological field theories_, arXiv:hep-th/9212043v3, 4 May 1993.** URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9212043. Local files: `getzler-1992-bv-tft.pdf/.txt` (23 pages). Read PDF pp. 1–12: the differential graded commutative product, degree-one square-zero BV operator, derived degree-one bracket, odd symplectic divergence model, master equation, and the TFT/operad motivation. The operadic proof later in the article was not audited in detail.
- **Christophe Nozaradan, _Introduction to Vertex Algebras_, arXiv:0809.1380v3 (PDF header 11 Nov 2008; manuscript says last edited 26 Nov 2024).** URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/0809.1380. Local files: `nozaradan-2008-vertex-algebras.pdf/.txt` (94 pages). Read selected PDF pp. 13–18, 28–38, 41–58, and 73–80: formal distributions and expansion conventions, locality, Lie-conformal λ-brackets and Virasoro/free-field examples, OPE and normal ordering, and the vacuum/translation/locality vertex-algebra axioms with examples. No full monograph-level representation-theory audit is claimed.
- **Tomoyuki Arakawa, _Introduction to W-algebras and their representation theory_, arXiv:1605.00138v2, 14 Feb 2017.** URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1605.00138. Local files: `arakawa-2016-introduction-w-algebras.pdf/.txt` (58 pages). Read PDF pp. 1–3, 15, 29, 32, 35–36, 40, and 53: scope and finite W-algebra reduction; the vertex-algebra formal-series axioms; the affine BRST complex and spectral-sequence argument for the principal type-A W-algebra; Zhu/Poisson-module context; and the lisse/rational minimal-model results. Many results are stated with citations to deeper papers; this introduction is not a full independent proof of all W-algebra theorems.
- **Tomoyuki Arakawa, Igor Alarcon Blatt, Jethro van Ekeren, and Wenbin Yan, _Characters and fusion rules of boundary W-algebras_, arXiv:2509.09039v1, 10 Sep 2025.** URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.09039. Local files: `arakawa-et-al-2025-characters-fusion-boundary-w-algebras.pdf/.txt` (33 pages). Read selected PDF pp. 1–3, 12–16: admissible-level setup, principal admissibility, modular data and type-A results. The authors explicitly warn that equality of irreducible-module labels, q-characters, and modular data does not generally imply vertex-algebra isomorphism (they give lattice-VOA examples); fusion conclusions require the stated rational/lisse/modular hypotheses.
- **Tomoyuki Arakawa and Xuanzhong Dai, _Classical freeness of affine vertex algebras of sl2 at boundary admissible levels_, arXiv:2609.33348v1, 27 Sep 2026.** URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.33348. Local files: `arakawa-dai-2026-classical-freeness-affine-sl2.pdf/.txt` (26 pages). Read PDF pp. 1–3, including the main theorem: the simple affine vertex algebra L_{-2+2/(2m+1)}(sl_2) is classically free for every integer m≥1, proved by comparing an arc-algebra Hilbert-series bound with the vacuum character. This is a 2026 preprint frontier result, not an independently checked proof here.

## Hall, quivers, and DT/CoHA

- **Olivier Schiffmann, _Lectures on Hall Algebras_, arXiv:math/0611617v2, 23 Oct 2009.** URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0611617. Local files: `schiffmann-2006-hall-algebras.pdf/.txt` (128 pages). Read PDF pp. 6–19 (finitary categories, both Hall normalizations, full flag associativity, Euler twist, Green coproduct/completion, hereditary bialgebra compatibility and proof outline), pp. 22–31 (Jordan quiver, Hall polynomials, symmetric-function/Hall–Littlewood correspondence), pp. 32–41 (loopless quiver hypotheses, Ringel–Green embedding of the extended positive Borel/composition algebra for general type, finite-type iff the full Hall algebra map is an isomorphism, and the PBW dimension argument), pp. 45–53 (cyclic quiver and tame composition algebra), pp. 54–63 (coherent sheaves on curves, torsion/Jordan-quiver blocks, projective-line Hall/Yangian comparison and completed coproduct examples), and pp. 99–105 (2-periodic Hall Lie algebra, Kapranov and Toën derived Hall, and research frontiers). The Green proof invokes an imported proposition and leaves two counting facts as exercises; the future local supplier must fill those gaps. The exact category remark permits coproduct variants but the Green theorem stated here is abelian. Geometry/DT is explicitly mostly outside this survey.
- **Bertrand Toën, _Derived Hall algebras_, arXiv:math/0501343v4, 25 Apr 2006 (article dated June 2005).** URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0501343. Local files: `toen-2005-derived-hall-algebras.pdf/.txt` (24 pages). Read PDF pp. 1–4, 8–23: finite-total-Hom-cohomology conditions on a dg-category over F_q, the homotopy-type convolution definition, the homotopy-cartesian associativity proof, the explicit higher-Ext formula, the heart embedding, and the hereditary case. The paper notes that naïve triangle-counting does not give an associative product; the dg enhancement and homotopy factors are essential.
- **Maxim Kontsevich and Yan Soibelman, _Stability structures, motivic Donaldson–Thomas invariants and cluster transformations_, arXiv:0811.2435v1, 16 Nov 2008.** URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/0811.2435. Local files: `kontsevich-soibelman-2008-stability-motivic-dt-wall-crossing.pdf/.txt` (148 pages). Read selected PDF pp. 3–12 and 20–34: the 3-Calabi–Yau/stability motivation, central charge and support property, stability data in a completed graded Lie algebra, sector factorization, formal wall-crossing and dilogarithm automorphisms. The authors identify orientation data and the motivic/quasi-classical integrality statements as extra choices/open conjectures in this version; several proofs in the introduction are sketches.
- **Maxim Kontsevich and Yan Soibelman, _Cohomological Hall algebra, exponential Hodge structures and motivic Donaldson–Thomas invariants_, arXiv:1006.2706v2, 11 Jul 2011.** URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1006.2706. Local files: `kontsevich-soibelman-2010-coha-motivic-dt.pdf/.txt` (119 pages). Read selected PDF pp. 3–10 and 12–15 (quiver-without-potential stack cohomology, incidence convolution and associativity), pp. 43–47 (potential/critical-cohomology set-up), pp. 48–60 (stability, HN spectral sequence and factorization), and pp. 95–105 (vanishing cycles and critical CoHA). This source carefully distinguishes the off-shell/rapid-decay and on-shell/vanishing-cycle constructions; the relation of BPS objects to physical BPS states is a proposed interface, not an automatic algebra theorem.
- **Yaping Yang and Gufang Zhao, _The cohomological Hall algebra of a preprojective algebra_, arXiv:1407.7994v6, 13 Dec 2017.** URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1407.7994. Local files: `yang-zhao-2014-coha-preprojective.pdf/.txt` (44 pages). Read PDF pp. 1–5, 11–14, and 24–30: arbitrary oriented-cohomology-theory definitions, Lagrangian incidence correspondences, associativity/shuffle construction, action on A-homology of Nakajima varieties, and the additive Chow/Yangian comparison. Their Yangian theorem requires the stated no-edge-loop hypothesis; the isomorphism claim is type ADE and uses a specified sign twist and extension/specialization.
- **Hiraku Nakajima, _Introduction to quiver varieties—for ring and representation theorists_, arXiv:1611.10000v1, 30 Nov 2016.** URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1611.10000. Local files: `nakajima-2016-introduction-quiver-varieties.pdf/.txt` (19 pages). Read the complete note: doubled quiver and moment-map construction, framing/stability quotient, projectivity and Lagrangian core, Hecke correspondences, and the integrable highest-weight Kac–Moody action. The main theorem assumes no edge loops for the ordinary Kac–Moody correspondence.
- **Olivier Schiffmann and Eric Vasserot, _Cohomological Hall algebras of quivers and Yangians_, arXiv:2312.15803v2, 21 Jun 2024.** URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.15803. Local files: `schiffmann-et-al-2023-cohas-quivers-yangians.pdf/.txt` (55 pages). Read selected PDF pp. 1–9, 19–30, and 46–55: equivariant set-up, preprojective/nilpotent COHA, stable-envelope Yangian construction, triangular decomposition, the positive-half isomorphism and intertwining of Nakajima-variety actions. This is advanced source evidence, not a local proof supplier.
- **Duiliu-Emanuel Diaconescu, Mauro Porta, Francesco Sala, Olivier Schiffmann, and Eric Vasserot, _Cohomological Hall algebras of one-dimensional sheaves on surfaces and Yangians_, arXiv:2603.03386v2, 8 Jun 2026.** URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.03386. Local files: `diaconescu-et-al-2026-coha-one-dimensional-sheaves-yangians.pdf/.txt` (152 pages). Read the abstract, contents and introduction (PDF pp. 1–9) plus theorem announcements. The authors state a completed/nonstandard positive-half affine Yangian description for the COHA of modifications on a fixed proper curve in a Kleinian-surface resolution, with a Picard/affine-braid action. This is a 2026 preprint frontier sample; its hypotheses and proofs were not audited in full.

## Diagram algebras, quantum affine, and modern symmetry extensions

- **Tom Halverson and Arun Ram, _Partition Algebras_, arXiv:math/0401314v2, 11 Feb 2004.** URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0401314. Local files: `halverson-ram-2004-partition-algebras.pdf/.txt` (47 pages). Read selected PDF pp. 1–11, 15, 19, 22–24, and 31–41: set-partition diagram composition and loop parameter, presentation and cellular/basic-construction route, semisimplicity criterion, symmetric-group and S_{n-1} centralizers on tensor powers, and branching/cell-module data. The paper states the threshold theorem with special cases and gives the stable centralizer quotient explicitly.
- **Ilaria Damiani, _Drinfeld realization of affine quantum algebras: the relations_, arXiv:1406.6729v1, 25 Jun 2014.** URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1406.6729. Local files: `jiang-2014-drinfeld-realization-affine-quantum-relations.pdf/.txt` (79 pages; local filename is historical/misleading—the title/author in the paper are Damiani). Read the introduction and selected Drinfeld/Drinfeld–Jimbo relation sections. The paper proves a surjective map from the current realization to the Drinfeld–Jimbo presentation for twisted and untwisted affine types; the introduction emphasizes that surjectivity alone does not prove injectivity and identifies the integral-form/PBW issue. It is not a Yangian/RTT proof.
- **Justin Kaidi, _Introduction to Generalized Symmetries_, arXiv:2603.08798v3, 18 Sep 2026.** URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.08798. Local files: `kaidi-2026-generalized-symmetries.pdf/.txt` (106 pages). Read selected PDF pp. 1–2, 33–43, 69–85: higher-form and defect language, fusion-category objects/junctions/F symbols/pentagon, rank-2/3 examples including Fibonacci/Ising, and selected Maxwell, QED, QCD, axion and neutrino applications. These are physics lecture notes; gauge-theory action manipulations are conditional on their stated spacetime, anomaly, spin, boundary and coupling assumptions.
- **Shadi Ali Ahmad, Marc S. Klinger, and Yifan Wang, _The Many Faces of Non-invertible Symmetries_, arXiv:2509.18072v3, 23 Mar 2026.** URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.18072. Local files: `ahmad-klinger-wang-2025-noninvertible-symmetries.pdf/.txt` (77 pages). Read the abstract/intro and selected PDF pp. 11–24, 31–40: algebraic endomorphism actions, fusion and strip algebras, weak-Hopf/Tannaka–Krein realization, nonuniqueness via module categories, Rieffel induction to an extended system, and finite-dimensional TQFT/Ising-boundary examples. The paper states that the weak-Hopf action generally acts on an enlarged/boundary-dependent system, not canonically on the original algebra. Its abstract says “prepared for submission to JHEP”; this manifest does not claim journal publication.
- **David Hernandez, _Representations and characters of quantum affine algebras at the crossroads between cluster categorification and quantum integrable models_, ICM 2026 invited lecture.** Official source: https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/25M1802614. Local files: `hernandez-2026-icm-quantum-affine-categorification-integrable-models.pdf/.txt` (19 pages). Read PDF pp. 1–6: the generic non-root-of-unity parameter assumption, finite-dimensional category O-type examples, evaluation-module limitations outside type A, commutative Grothendieck ring versus non-isomorphic non-semisimple tensor products, universal R-matrix/transfer-matrix statements and the cluster/integrable-model interface. Later details are not comprehensively read.

## Search and audit boundary

The 2026 research cutoff is 2026-10-17. This lane has dated primary evidence through September 2026 but did not conduct an exhaustive arXiv/citation search. A local `rg` pass across `items/`, `physics/items/`, and `library/` for Hall/CoHA, Brauer/BMW/partition, vertex/chiral/W, A∞/L∞/BV, and Weyl/CCR terms is a discovery check only; false positives include ordinary graph vertices, finite W-algebras, and the unrelated analytic (L^infty) notation. The dossier records actual found overlaps and does not infer field-wide absence from filenames.
