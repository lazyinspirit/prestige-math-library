# Clifford dossier source ledger

Research cut-off: **2026-10-17**. Every source described as read below has a local PDF and a text extraction beside it. PDFs were extracted with `mutool draw -F txt`; extraction is for search/reading and does not replace checking equations, signs, or diagrams against the PDF. The local archive is in this directory. Read coverage is stated per source; “full” means the whole article/notes segment listed, not merely the abstract. This is a curated, targeted source set, not an exhaustive bibliography or a comprehensive search of every 2025–26 result.

## Coverage and verification notes

- Gallier, ABS, Witten, Kitaev, Fidkowski–Kitaev, Johnson et al., and Li were read in full at the article/note lengths stated above. Wernli and Hörmann were read in the named sections/pages only; remaining pages are available locally but are not represented as read.
- Yuan–Qi was read in the explicitly listed main sections and appendices only. Its skipped appendices are not assumed as support for dossier claims. The conjecture and assumptions are directly checked at PDF pp. 6 and 32–33; `/root/algebra_audit` independently read those passages and confirmed the conditional wording.
- All ten retained source pairs are local PDF plus text extraction. The duplicate failed Witten extraction is not retained. OCR/extraction alone was not treated as proof of correctness; sign and source-typo checks were compared with displayed PDF statements and independent sources.
- No unavailable source is used as a theorem supplier. The unrecorded direct URL for the University of Vienna notes is a provenance limitation, not an unavailable text: the complete local PDF is available, and its title page, author, institution, version date, page count, and read coverage are recorded above.
- For current research, the sample reaches the publication dates listed above, through 2026-10-17. It is evidence of specific developments and caveats, not an exhaustive bibliometric or “no results exist” search.
