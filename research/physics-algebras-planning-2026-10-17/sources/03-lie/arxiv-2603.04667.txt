THE KAZHDAN-LUSZTIG CATEGORY OF W-ALGEBRAS OF
SIMPLY-LACED LIE ALGEBRAS AT IRRATIONAL LEVELS

THOMAS CREUTZIG, GURBIR DHILLON, AND SHIGENORI NAKATSUKA

Abstract. Let g be a simple, simply-laced Lie algebra and f ∈g nilpo-
tent.
The Kazhdan-Lusztig category of the W-algebra Wκ(g, f) associated
with (g, f) at level κ ∈C is obtained from the Kazhdan-Lusztig category of
the affine vertex algebra V κ(g) via quantum Hamiltonian reduction associated
with f. We show that this is a braided tensor equivalence for any f and any
irrational level κ ∈C\Q.

1. Introduction

associates the affine vertex algebra of g at level κ, V κ(g). Modules for V κ(g) are

module parabolically induces to a V κ(g)-module. The Kazhdan-Lusztig category

for κ + h∨̸∈Q≥0 is braided tensor equivalent to the category of finite-dimensional

So far braided tensor categories of W-algebras have only been known for the
Virasoro algebra [15], the N = 1 super Virasoro algebra [22] as well as exceptional
simple W-algebras at admissible levels [4, 10, 13]. The proof of our Theorem is
similar to ideas of [13].
The Kazhdan-Lusztig category of universal W-algebras
at rational levels is usually not semi-simple and in particular in the case that its
level shifted by the dual Coxeter number is a positive rational number its abelian
structure is unknown, except for the recent studies of the case sl2 [40].
The first issue in proving such theorems has been the mere existence of a braided
tensor category structure. Thanks to a recent Theorem of Yi-Zhi Huang [30] this
is now guaranteed for the category of C1-cofinite modules of any vertex operator
algebra. Equivalences of categories of vertex algebras can now be established if
one has a suitable relation between the involved vertex algebras. For us these are
the GKO-coset realizations of principal W-algebras of types ADE [7]. These allow
us to determine important fusion rules of modules of principal W-algebras. The
modules T κ

we crucially show the fusion rule T κ

together with the Urod-version of the GKO-coset [6] then allows us to prove our
main Theorem.
One would like to obtain a similar result for the other simple Lie algebras and
there are many more GKO-type cosets (see the introduction of [19]). In particular
there are three GKO-type cosets that involve the affine vertex algebras of types B, C
and also osp1|2n as well as their principal W-(super)algebras [18, 14, 21]. We have
two works in progress. One on general structural results about W-superalgebras
and one on algebras of chiral differential operators on supergroups. These two will
give us more detailed information about the GKO-cosets of orthosymplectic types
of [18, 14, 21] and hence we expect to be able to report on a similar Theorem for
types B, C and also osp1|2n in the future.
