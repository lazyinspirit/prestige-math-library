Introduction

Huang and Lepowsky developed the tensor product theory of the module category for
vertex operator algebra in [HL95-1, HL95-2, HL95-3, H95-4]. Furthermore, Huang proved
that the category of modules for vertex operator algebra V is a modular tensor category if
V is of CFT type, simple, self-dual, rational and C2-cofinite in [H08]. Thus the category
of modules for simple affine vertex operator algebra L�g(k, 0) at positive integer level k
is a modular tensor category.
If the level k is not a positive integer, L�g(k, 0) is not
C2-cofinite [DM06, Zh96]. Level k is called an admissible level if simple affine vertex
operator superalgebra L�g(k, 0) is an admissible �g-module, where admissible modules were
first studied by Kac and Wakimoto to explore the modular invariant representations of
affine Lie (super)algebras in [KW88]. For g = sl2 and admissible level k, Adamovi´c and
Milas proved that L�g(k, 0) is rational in the category O and presented a conjecture for
arbitrary affine Lie algebras in [AM95]. This conjecture was proved by Arakawa in [A16]
and extended to the only non-isotropic basic Lie superalgebra g = osp(1|2n) by Gorelik
and Serganova in [GS22]. On the other hand, Creutzig, Huang and Yang proved that the
ordinary module category of L�g(k, 0) is a braided tensor category for any simple finite-
dimensional Lie algebra g at admissible level k in [CHY18]. In [C19], Creutzig proved
that the ordinary module category of L�g(k, 0) associated to any simply laced Lie algebra
g at admissible level k is rigid and thus a braided fusion category. By using the theory of
vertex superalgebra extensions [CKM24], Creutzig, Genra and Linshaw proved that the
ordinary module category of L
