## A small independent check that matters for the frontier

Distinguish three meanings of invertibility:

1. A tensor-invertible **object** X has an object Y with X⊗Y≅1 and Y⊗X≅1.
2. A **unit in a fusion ring** has an inverse in that ring, whose coefficients can be negative.
3. An **invertible operator** is invertible in a specified representation on a specified space.

These are different tests. In the Fibonacci ring with basis {1,x} and relation x²=1+x, direct multiplication gives x(x−1)=1=(x−1)x. Thus x is a ring unit. But it cannot have an inverse with nonnegative integer coefficients: if y=a+bx, a,b≥0, then xy=b+(a+b)x; equality to 1 forces b=1 and a+b=0, impossible. Consequently the corresponding simple object is noninvertible even though its ring class is a unit. This supplies an elementary proof obligation/example and prevents an incorrect general definition of categorical symmetry by ring nonunits. It does not by itself construct the Fibonacci category or prove its pentagon equations.

Shao's displayed anyonic-chain construction assumes multiplicity-free fusion for simplicity (footnote 26, PDF p.35). A general page must include bases of the junction/multiplicity spaces and their associator matrices, or explicitly remain multiplicity-free. Its constrained fusion-path space is generally not a tensor product of local sites. The Ising discussion also distinguishes the doubled path Hilbert space with D²=1+η from a single tensor-product lattice realization whose relation involves translation. These should not be merged by notation.

## Bounded additional family: Jordan and alternative algebras

A further important physics-facing algebra family is genuinely nonassociative: Jordan algebras, spin factors, and their alternative/octonionic exceptional examples. Root's focused filename/body search found no dedicated Jordan-algebra/Albert/octonion carrier in active math or physics items/pages. That observation does not certify exhaustive absence. Root retrieved Baez, *The Octonions*, https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0105155, actual v4 dated23April2002,56PDF pages; read selected full-text PDF pp.20–23 and27–28, not the entire article. Local PDF/text are in sources/00-root/. This classical source motivates a bounded explicit branch; it is not evidence for a2026 experimental or universal physics claim.

For an associative algebra over a field of characteristic other than2, set a∘b=(ab+ba)/2. The product is commutative. Both a∘(b∘a²) and (a∘b)∘a² expand to `(aba²+a³b+ba³+a²ba)/4`, proving the Jordan identity directly. In a complex *-algebra the self-adjoint subspace is a real vector space closed under this Jordan product, though generally not under associative multiplication. This supplies a concise reusable bridge from existing associative/*-algebra foundations. The real spin factor on an inner-product space V is V⊕R with `(v,α)∘(w,β)=(αw+βv,〈v,w〉+αβ)`; compare Clifford anticommutators and Lorentz cones only after supplying an exact convention map.

A general Jordan module/representation is not an ordinary unital associative module unless a particular enveloping construction and its scope are proved. The exceptional Albert algebra of Hermitian3×3 octonion matrices needs octonionic alternativity and its own identity/classification suppliers; the preceding associative expansion does not prove its Jordan identity. Do not extend ordinary associative matrix determinant/projective-space formulas to octonions without proof, or claim that all Hermitian matrix sizes over octonions are Jordan algebras. Finite-dimensional formally real classification, ordered state-space reconstructions and exceptional/supersymmetric physics applications need separate precise hypotheses and full proof obligations. A Jordan observable model is a model choice, not proof of its physical realisation.

This branch may be recorded as a bounded specialist extension with concrete missing suppliers; it should not displace required Hall, Temperley–Lieb and Clifford representation theory.
