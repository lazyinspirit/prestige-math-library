# Coverage, reuse, and gap ledger

This ledger is intentionally stricter than a title match. “Reuse” below means
that a specific current carrier was inspected sufficiently to identify what it
establishes and what it does not. All six specialist source dossiers and their
manifests are integrated; the independent planning audit records per-claim carrier
limits and outstanding proof closure. An existing page or draft is not proof evidence
merely because it has a useful title. A citation records provenance and is
never a logical supplier by itself.

The `physics/` library is a separate content model and build. Its carriers are
searched to prevent needless duplicate authoring. A physics item with
`domain: mathematics` remains mathematical and may be semantically eligible as
a supplier when its full dependency closure has no physical premise. Current
math `depcheck` scans root `items/` only, however, so a physics-only path has no
direct resolver route. Before a native dependency is planned, the owner must
identify a supported same-canonical-ID import/adoption route. Do not copy or
rewrite a physics carrier to evade this limitation. Physical postulates,
physics theorems, and empirical results are not eligible mathematical
prerequisites. Until the route is verified, record the math physics carrier as
a semantically eligible mathematical supplier candidate and its dependency
resolution as operationally unresolved. Do not label it mathematically
ineligible and do not copy or rewrite it.
