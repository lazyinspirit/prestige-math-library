## foundations-and-applications

Smooth manifolds, tangent spaces, submanifolds, vector bundles and flows open the pathway. Differential forms lead to Stokes and de Rham theory, while metrics and connections lead to geodesics, curvature, Jacobi fields and comparison, and Gauss–Bonnet. On compact Hermitian vector bundles, Laplace-type operators with positive principal symbol admit parametrices and a Fredholm Hodge decomposition whose finite smooth kernel gives harmonic representatives of de Rham and Dolbeault cohomology. Symplectic geometry and characteristic forms complete the core; Lie groups and Lie-algebra structure theory have their own category, Lie Theory.
