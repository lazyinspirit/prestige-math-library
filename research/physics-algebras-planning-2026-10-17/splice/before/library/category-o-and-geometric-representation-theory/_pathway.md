## representation-theory

Kazhdan–Lusztig bases and Soergel Hodge theory lead to projective functors, character formulas and singular or parabolic multiplicities. Parabolic category O and Arakawa–Suzuki functors develop the Fock-space and higher-level Schur–Weyl interfaces. The geometric branch supplies proper cohomological descent, constructible hypercovers and equivariant convolution. Their KLR and Hecke applications are linked from Hecke Algebras and Categorification.
