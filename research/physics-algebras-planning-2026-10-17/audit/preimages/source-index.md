It is a navigation record, not a proof stamp. A downloaded PDF or extracted text is not evidence that every page was read; the ranges below are the ranges the researchers report having inspected. ## How to read this index

Each proposed claim in [`proposed-pages.json`](proposed-pages.json) cites one or more of these dossier/manifest paths. The dossier records the mathematical scope and exact local supplier boundary; the manifest records the source version and actual read span. `coverage.md` maps existing library items to their exact limits. An external theorem citation establishes provenance and guides a future proof; it is not an accepted local mathematical dependency. Every claim remains pending local proof closure and audit.

## Source and proof limits

This is a targeted, source-grounded planning index through the stated cutoff, not an exhaustive literature survey. Each lane says what was read and what was not; source hashes, local artifacts, access failures and partial reads remain in the associated manifest. Use exact theorem hypotheses and source proof cautions from the dossiers. If the source omits a proof, imports a result, is only an introduction, or labels a physics scaling/interpretation as heuristic, the proposed local page must either supply the missing proof or mark the claim as deferred. The independent audit may correct this index and the shared planning files after ownership handoff.
