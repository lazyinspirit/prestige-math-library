## Concrete repairs

1. **Jones–Wenzl existence and coefficient rings.** The inherited statement incorrectly deleted the `n=aℓ−1` branch at `p=∞`. Spencer's `p(1)=ℓ` survives, so characteristic-zero fields of finite quantum characteristic admit that branch for every finite positive integer a, as well as `n<ℓ`. Generic `ℓ=∞` covers every finite index. Field existence is separated from integral descent/unit conditions: the rational `JW₂` at δ=2 does not descend to Z. Normalization requires identity coefficient one. The explicit `TL₃(0)` element `1−u₁u₂−u₂u₁` verifies why an intermediate recursion denominator failure is not nonexistence. The attribution and original bytes are preserved. Quantum-integer recurrence, generator relations and the valid-range projector recursion are now concrete contracts.
2. **Weyl, exponentiated CCR and CAR.** Polynomial Weyl modules, regular unitary Weyl representations, C*-completion and unbounded common domains are separate. The ordinary polynomial differential action was removed as a characteristic-p faithfulness argument. Root's checked `R[x,t]` action `P_i=h∂_i+t_i` supplies a characteristic-free PBW independence plan, since ordered monomials applied to1 give distinct `x^αt^β`. The trace obstruction explicitly requires `n≥1`, characteristic zero and nonzero central character. Stone–von Neumann retains finite-dimensional real symplectic, irreducibility, regularity and fixed-character hypotheses. Finite CAR/Majorana comparison and its inverse are separate from CCR; no CCR–Clifford isomorphism is proposed.
3. **Existing results needing proof repair.** Existing pivotal, spherical, trace and ribbon definitions are reused.
