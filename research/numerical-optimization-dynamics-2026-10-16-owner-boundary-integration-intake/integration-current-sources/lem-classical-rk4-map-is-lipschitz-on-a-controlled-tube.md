## Statement

Let $f:[0,T]\times U\to\mathbb R^m$ be uniformly $L$-Lipschitz in its state variable on a set containing every stage generated by the classical RK4 step of [[def-euler-midpoint-rk4-tableaus]] from any two admitted starting states $u,v$ at the same base time $t$. Assume all these stage evaluations are defined, and let $\Psi_h(t,\cdot)$ be the resulting one-step map for $0<h\le h_0$. Then
$$|\Psi_h(t,u)-\Psi_h(t,v)|\le(1+L_*h)|u-v|,\qquad L_*=Le^{Lh_0}.$$

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The explicit RK4 stage equations, a time-dependent vector field uniformly Lipschitz in state with constant $L$, admitted stages at common stage times, and $0<h\le h_0$.

[F1] An explicit tableau has stages $Y_i=u+h\sum_{j<i}a_{ij}K_j$ and output $u+h\sum_i b_iK_i$ ([[def-explicit-runge-kutta-tableau-and-stage-map]]).

[F2] The classical RK4 coefficients are $(1/2,1/2,1)$ in the stage rows and $(1/6,1/3,1/3,1/6)$ in the output ([[def-euler-midpoint-rk4-tableaus]]).

[F3] The Euclidean norm satisfies the triangle inequality and is homogeneous ([[thm-cauchy-schwarz-and-the-euclidean-norm]], [[def-euclidean-inner-product]]).

## Proof
1.1 Put $\delta=|u-v|$ and let $d_i=|K_i(t,u)-K_i(t,v)|$. The stage times are identical for the two starting states. The stage equations [F1]-[F2] and the uniform state-Lipschitz assumption give, in order, $d_1\le L\delta,\quad d_2\le L(1+hL/2)\delta,\quad d_3\le L(1+hL/2+h^2L^2/4)\delta,$ and $d_4\le L(1+hL+h^2L^2/2+h^3L^3/4)\delta.$ Each bound follows by substituting the preceding stage bound into $|f(t_i,Y_i(u))-f(t_i,Y_i(v))|\le L|Y_i(u)-Y_i(v)|$. [F1, F2, F3, given, algebra]

2.1 The output formula in [F1]-[F2] gives $|\Psi_h(t,u)-\Psi_h(t,v)|\le\left(1+hL+\frac12(hL)^2+\frac16(hL)^3+\frac1{24}(hL)^4\right)\delta\le e^{hL}\delta.$ For $h\le h_0$, $e^{hL}-1=\int_0^{hL}e^sds\le hLe^{Lh_0}$, so $e^{hL}\le1+L_*h$. This proves the claim. [F1, F2, F3, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
