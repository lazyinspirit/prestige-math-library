These examples instantiate the finite fusion and braid-compilation interfaces: fusion-measurement branches, the distinction between projective equivalence and a controlled operation, a joint six-strand target search, and a complete output-error budget. They use the exact encoding, spectator action and cost conventions of the companion page.

The examples keep mathematical approximation guarantees separate from the stated physical preparation, transport and measurement inputs. In particular a global phase on a fixed sector does not automatically remain irrelevant after adding coherent control.
