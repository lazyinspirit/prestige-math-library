## Example

For the one-vertex, one-loop quiver with loop $x$, take the potential
$W=x^2$. In dimension one, the representation space is $\mathbb C$, the
group $\operatorname{GL}_1$ acts trivially, and the trace function is
$W_1(z)=z^2$. Its critical locus is the origin, with critical value zero.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** the one-loop quiver, the potential $W=x^2$, and the local Milnor-fiber normalization in [[def-quiver-potential-and-monodromic-vanishing-cycle-data]].

[F1] [[def-quiver-potential-and-monodromic-vanishing-cycle-data]]: the dimension-one trace of a loop potential is its value on the scalar endomorphism, and an isolated critical point has vanishing-cycle stalk given by reduced cohomology of its local Milnor fiber, with monodromy from a positive loop.

[F2] [[lem-quiver-potential-trace-is-additive-on-extensions]]: the trace of the potential on an extension is the sum of its traces on the subrepresentation and quotient.


## Verification

1.1 The trace definition in [F1] gives $W_1(z)=z^2$. Its differential is $dW_1=2z\,dz$; since the coefficient field has characteristic zero, this vanishes exactly at $z=0$, where $W_1(0)=0$. Thus the critical locus and critical value are as stated. [F1, given, algebra]

2.1 For any sufficiently small $\epsilon\ne0$, the local Milnor fiber $z^2=\epsilon$ in a disk about zero consists of the two distinct points $\sqrt\epsilon$ and $-\sqrt\epsilon$. Its reduced zeroth cohomology is $\mathbb Q$, generated by the difference of the two point classes. When $\epsilon$ goes once around zero, the two roots exchange, so this generator maps to its negative. The on-shell coefficient at this critical point is therefore one-dimensional with monodromy $-1$, in the stated convention. [F1, step 1.1, construct]

3.1 This calculation identifies the local critical datum for $W=x^2$; it does not establish the existence of the full equivariant Thom–Sebastiani/proper-pushforward package or an associative critical CoHA. A full critical-CoHA construction requires additional operation and coherence data, as described on the paired A page; this local calculation does not establish that package for the standard realization. [F1, F2, step 2.1] ∎
