These examples test the root-data and parameter conventions on the affine $B_2$ Coxeter system and compare a positive p-adic parameter with a finite type-A root-of-unity parameter. The computations prove only the displayed relations and scalar boundary; they supply no general Bernstein PBW or tempered/Langlands classification theorem.

## Affine root-data example

[[ex-hh-affine-b2-unequal-parameters]] gives the paired B2 lattices, short and long coroots, affine simple reflections, Coxeter diagram, two positive parameters, and the resulting Iwahori–Matsumoto basis.

## Parameter-boundary example

[[ex-hh-affine-padic-versus-root-of-unity-parameters]] computes quantum characteristic $\infty$ for the positive real parameter $3$ and $4$ for the finite parameter $i$. This distinguishes parameter regimes without asserting that the affine and finite Hecke algebras cannot be equivalent.
