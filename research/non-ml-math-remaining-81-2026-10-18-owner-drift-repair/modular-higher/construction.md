## PA-47: exact hypotheses, normalization, helpers and order

Throughout use a complex finite semisimple rigid linear tensor category with simple unit and finitely many simple isomorphism classes I, together with a fixed braiding and ribbon twist. State that the associated pivotal structure is spherical, identify its trace, and use positive dimensions only in the unitary examples, not as an implicit general assumption. Write 0 for the unit label and i* for the dual. Put d_i=tr(id_i), s_ij=tr(c_ji c_ij), D²=sum_i d_i², choose a square root D, and S=s/D. General simple dimensions and D² are nonzero; their proofs are required. “Modular” means det(s)≠0. One cannot prove det(s)≠0 for every ribbon fusion category: symmetric Rep(G), |I|>1, gives s_ij=d_i d_j and rank one.

1. **Ribbon trace identities and Hopf-link characters.** Consume the repaired existing pivotal/spherical/Drinfeld/tangle items, without copying their statements. Locally prove balancing s_ij=(theta_i theta_j)^(-1) sum_k N_ij^k theta_k d_k and the partial-trace identity s_xi s_xj=d_x sum_k N_ij^k s_xk. Expand both hexagons, close the Z-strand, and use Schur's lemma to identify the resulting endomorphism of x as (s_xZ/d_x)id_x. Use explicit composites of evaluations/coevaluations; a picture alone is not the proof. Thus h_x(i)=s_xi/d_x is a character of the fusion ring.

2. **Character orthogonality and modular inversion.** For distinct characters χ,ψ of a fusion ring, P_χ=sum_i χ(i)i* satisfies yP_χ=χ(y)P_χ by Frobenius reciprocity. Applying ψ proves sum_i χ(i)ψ(i*)=0. Nondegenerate s gives distinct h_x. Combine this orthogonality with the character identity to obtain s²=D² C, C_ij=δ_i,j*, including the diagonal calculation sum_x s_xi s_x,i*=D². Prove nonzero d_i locally from rigidity plus simple-unit semisimplicity, and nonzero D² from invertibility and s²=D² C in this modular case. Do not invoke a general global-dimension theorem unnecessarily. The general algebraic Verlinde formula is

   N_ij^k = sum_x S_ix S_jx S_k*,x / S_0x.

   It follows by multiplying the character identity by s_x,k* and summing, then using s²=D²C. Complex conjugation may replace * only after proving the extra unitary relation; it is not the algebraic general formula. Show the precise matrix convention when writing simultaneous diagonalization of fusion matrices. This proof does not need to assume a modular representation to establish Verlinde; the torus representation is constructed below from the same data.

   s_ab=sin((a+1)(b+1)pi/ell)/sin(pi/ell),
