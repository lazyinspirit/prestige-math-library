## Source corrections and mathematical checks

1. Do not copy the 2016 index claim as uniform over all winding components. The text already introduces a fixed winding; 2021 Propositions 5.6, 5.8 and 7.6 provide the exact conditions. Winding is essential, including at the terminal S^0 stage.
2. 2021 Proposition 5.4's statement prints `index > r−1`, while its proof counts at least r−1 independent directions. A lower bound ≥r−1 with a strictly larger stabilization rank suffices throughout; the construction uses that conservative version.
3. 2021 §10 writes an inverse quaternion to turn a right scalar action into a left scalar action. Inversion on arbitrary H is not additive. Use H^op and conjugation and check the balanced evaluation map directly. This is ordinary finite-dimensional Morita linear algebra, not an uncertainty about the theorem.
4. In §8 distinguish eigenspaces of the **left volume involution** on a module from the even/odd word decomposition of Cl(V). The regular-algebra example's prose mixes the descriptions. The construction chooses and checks the needed grading explicitly.
5. Real Bott comes from the rank-eight trivial-bundle specialization of the real geometric theorem. Low coefficients follow from clutching/fibration calculations and the rank-four KSp version; then the actual ABS symbols are checked in degrees 0,1,2,4,8 and propagated by the proved product formula. Complexification is unnecessary for real periodicity or its coefficients.
6. The table q−d is the signed Clifford mass extension calculation. Ordinary parameter-sphere mapping into R_q has the other shift; the physical momentum involution cannot be silently discarded.
