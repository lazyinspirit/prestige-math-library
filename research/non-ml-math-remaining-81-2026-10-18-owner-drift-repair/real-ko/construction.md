**Stage 1: geometric path-space helpers (about 6–8 items).** Follow 2021 §§3–7, with these exact finite-dimensional/relative statements.

- For an orthogonal Clifford family J_1,…,J_l on R^p, prove that the appropriate midpoint components in SO(p) are the components of `{J: J²=−I, JJ_i=−J_iJ (i<l)}`. Compute the tangent condition: `exp(tA)J_l` remains in the component iff A anticommutes with J_l and commutes with the preceding J_i. Verify geodesics, endpoints, total geodesy and the restriction that antipodes lie in the same component. The exceptional-group classification in source Table 3.2 is unnecessary and is not a dependency.
- Prove the **relative path-shortening connectivity lemma**: in a component of paths between poles, if every nonminimal geodesic has index at least d+1, the inclusion of minimizing geodesics is d-connected for finite CW pairs. Use uniform polygon approximation with mesh fine relative to convexity radius and the bounded energy of the compact parameter family. On the finite-dimensional polygon manifold prove the critical sets are the geodesic families, identify Hessian negative directions, and use the Morse–Bott local normal form/relative handle argument to avoid stable manifolds of codimension >d; decrease energy into a tubular neighborhood of the minima and retract. Supply these local Morse–Bott and index-form facts if ordinary Morse suppliers do not cover them. Do not assert that negative gradient flow converges continuously for every path, or that an unbounded entire path space retracts globally. 2021 Theorem 4.2 and Lemmas 4.3–4.4 provide the argument but leave these finite-dimensional details to fill.
- Supply **uniform real index estimates** by decomposing R^p into minimal invariant Clifford blocks and using block rotations between pairs of blocks. Conjugation by a real two-block rotation produces Jacobi fields; cutting beyond their first meeting gives independent negative directions. For l not congruent 2 mod 4 a sufficient conservative bound is `index ≥ p/s_(l+1)−1`; choose p strictly large enough for the desired connectivity. For l=4m−2 retain winding w and the explicit sufficient inequality `p−4|w| ≥ 4 d s_l`; source Proposition 5.6 then gives index >d for nonminimal geodesics. Prove independence of counted variation directions with the index form. No bound uniform over all winding components is claimed. Source Proposition 5.4 prints a strict bound while its proof establishes the weaker non-strict bound; use the weaker one with slack.
- Fix a meridian by homotopy extension. Identify maps from a suspension with maps from its equator into a path space. Iterate shortening and account for the parameter dimension. At l=4m−2 preceding extendibility forces zero winding (Proposition 5.8); at the last step retain the appropriate winding component. For k=4m−1 and positive C_k-representation rho, prove the inclusion of affine Hopf maps into `Map_*(S^k,SO(p))_eta` is d-connected when p≥p0(d) and `s_k d ≤ eta ≤ p/4` (Theorem 7.10). **Stage 2: family clutching decomposition and Clifford–Thom (about 4 items).** Let X be finite CW and V→X oriented Euclidean of rank n=4m>0. Use sphere bundle `Vhat=S(R⊕V)`, sections s_±, disk halves, and triples `(E_+,sigma,E_−)`. For a C(V)-module bundle Lambda, use the ± eigenspaces of the volume involution and multiplication by unit v to construct its Hopf bundle. Distinguish this chirality splitting from the parity splitting of the regular algebra; do not identify eigenspaces of left volume multiplication with even/odd words.

Prove 2021 Theorem 9.2 completely, including **uniqueness**: after addition of trivial bundles and regular Clifford–Hopf bundles, any bundle over Vhat is a pulled-back bundle plus a Clifford–Hopf bundle. Arrange eta and p in Theorem 7.10's range, embed E_+ into a sufficiently large regular module half, and induct over cells. On a cell, normalize at a chosen unit vector, extend the boundary gauge over `D×D^n` by contraction of D^n to that vector, and apply relative d-connectivity. Extend both module summands and the lower-half gauge. For uniqueness stabilize the upper halves to agree, apply the explicit block-rotation Lemma 9.6 to make the upper gauge identity, and repeat the relative cell argument over `X×I` fixing both ends; use dimension dim X+1. This last step is essential for injectivity.

Pass to group completion, quotient the section via `chi([E])=[E]−pi^*[s_+^*E]`, and identify `KO(Vhat,s_+(X))` with reduced KO(Th(V)). The result is the real Clifford–Thom isomorphism of 2021 Theorem 10.3. Prove the cancellation/group-completion step, rather than treating stable decomposition as immediately injective. This argument uses no real or complex Bott theorem.

**Stage 3: real spin Morita, Bott and coefficients (about 4–5 items).** For a given spin V of rank 8m, form its real spinor bundle Sigma and prove evaluation `Hom_(C(V))(Sigma,Lambda) tensor_R Sigma -> Lambda` is an isomorphism fiberwise and locally, hence globally. For rank 8m+4 use H and its opposite algebra with conjugation in the balanced tensor product; never define the scalar action by taking the inverse of an arbitrary nonzero quaternion (that is not additive). Check local transition compatibility and stable complements. This supplies 2021 Proposition 10.6 and Theorem 10.7 independently of their shorthand scalar formulas.

Apply rank eight **trivial** V to every finite X: product by the real spinor Hopf class beta gives `KO^0(X) ≅ reduced KO^0(X_+ smash S^8)`. Derive reduced, relative and all suspended degrees by the section/wedge split exact sequence. This proves multiplicative real Bott periodicity and extends the grading to all integers. It is not inferred from complexification.

For low coefficients, prove clutching `reduced KO(S^n)=pi_(n−1)(O)` and the symplectic analog. Use `SO(n) -> SO(n+1) -> S^n`, SO(3)=S^3/{±1}, and `Sp(r−1) -> Sp(r) -> S^(4r−1)` (local trivializations from orthonormal frames/Gram–Schmidt) to get `KO^−1=Z/2`, `KO^−2=Z/2`, `KO^−3=0` and `KSp^−j(pt)=0` for j=1,2,3. The rank-four spin Thom theorem gives `KO^(−j−4)(pt)=KSp^(−j)(pt)`, so degrees 4–7 are Z,0,0,0. Rank zero is real dimension. Eightfold Bott gives all coefficients. Write these elementary fibration/low homotopy calculations locally if their exact canonical suppliers are not available; do not assume the KO table in a Fact.
