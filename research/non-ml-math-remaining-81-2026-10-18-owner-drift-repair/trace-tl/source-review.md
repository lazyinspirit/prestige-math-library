## Second focused cycle: unit scalars and tensor traces

Root found a mathematical error in the first local replacement, not a formatting issue: the two actions of a unit scalar on an object need not coincide, despite commutativity of End(1). The matrix category Mat_2(Vec) gives a genuine counterexample. With Eij tensor Ekl zero unless j=k, otherwise Eil, and dual Eij*=Eji, the left traces of 1_E12 and 1_E21 are e1 and e2; their product is zero, while the tensor product has trace e1. This is preserved in the repaired carrier's Remarks. The first-cycle carrier and first-cycle evidence are retained byte-for-byte under archive/cycle-2/.

The corrected Statement keeps clauses 1/2/4 general and replaces clause 3 by the exact nested formula Tr_L(a tensor b)=Tr_L(r_Xdoubledual(Tr_L(b)) a). Multiplicativity follows if this right action equals the corresponding left action. Its mirrored right formula is Tr_R(p tensor q)=Tr_R(q ell_Ydoubledual(Tr_R(p))). The proof computes both formulas by expansion of the chosen tensor dual. It then proves centrality in a braided category from unit hexagons/naturality, and in a bilinear k-linear category with End(1)=k by linearity. These are sufficient hypotheses, not a global restriction of the theorem.
