They are separable by cyclicity and second countability, or separability may be explicitly assumed. Identify translation characters by χ_p(a)=exp(iη(p,a)); with this convention U(t e_0)=exp(-itP^0). The exact classification is a bijection between equivalence classes of these positive-energy representations and pairs (orbit O, irreducible unitary θ of K_p), with fixed representatives p and action induced from χ_p⊗θ on R^D⋊K_p. Fixed representatives avoid redundant conjugate labels; different representatives in one orbit use conjugated θ. Different orbits never give equivalent representations.
