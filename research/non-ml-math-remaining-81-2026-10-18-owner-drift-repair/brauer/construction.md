5. `thm-orthogonal-and-symplectic-multilinear-fft-sft`. Extract multidegree (1,…,1) from helper 4. For t odd the invariant space is zero. For t=2r it is spanned by products ∏ B(v_i,v_j) indexed by perfect matchings, always orienting each pair i<j. In O dimension m all relations are spans of

   det(B(v_i,v_j))_{i in I,j in J} times a matching contraction on the remaining labels,

   where I,J are disjoint ordered (m+1)-subsets. In Sp dimension 2n all relations are spans of

   Pf((B(v_i,v_j))_{i,j in S}) times a matching contraction on remaining labels,

   where S is an ordered (2n+2)-subset. Prove this from multihomogeneity of the ideal generators: in the multilinear piece repeated label degrees are impossible, so symmetric minors have disjoint row/column labels and the remaining monomials are pairings; Pfaffian generators already use distinct labels. No polarization denominators are needed for this extraction.

   Pairing contractions are independent exactly in the stable ranges r≤m for O and r≤n for Sp. The ideal has no elements of multidegree (1,…,1) before the indicated first relation degree. Beyond it the displayed determinant or Pfaffian relation is a nonzero formal linear combination of distinct matchings, extended by any matching on leftover vertices. Thus both sufficiency and failure outside the stable range are proved. Note the O first relation involves 2(m+1) vector slots, while the Sp first relation involves only 2n+2 slots. Confusing these gives the wrong symplectic faithfulness threshold.
