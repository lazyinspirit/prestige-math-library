## 1. Filtered affine algebra and a genuine PBW independence proof

Let d_i=x_i−x_(i+1), let s_i permute polynomial variables, and define partial_i=(1−s_i)/d_i and T_i=s_i−partial_i. The divisibility of p−s_i p makes these polynomial operators. Directly partial_i x_(i+1)−x_i partial_i=−1, partial_i x_i−x_(i+1)partial_i=1, partial_i²=0, s_i partial_i=partial_i and partial_i s_i=−partial_i. Thus T_i²=1 and T_i x_(i+1)−x_i T_i=1. Distant operators commute. For adjacent braid relations write T_i=(1+1/d_i)s_i−1/d_i in the skew group algebra of the rational function field. Multiply the three factors on each side, moving rational coefficients past permutations, and compare the six S3 coefficients; clearing d_12 d_23 d_13 gives identical polynomials. This is a finite identity, with no Hecke PBW theorem imported.

The algebra's defining relations reorder arbitrary words into x_1^a1…x_d^ad T_w. Independence follows in the rational skew group algebra: the coefficient of permutation w in T_w is the nonzero product of the factors 1+1/(x_i−x_j) from its reduced word, and every other permutation has smaller Bruhat order. The triangular matrix from T_w to the permutation basis is invertible over the rational field. Distinct field automorphisms act independently: from a shortest nontrivial relation, multiply the argument by a function separated by two automorphisms and subtract an appropriate scalar multiple of the original relation to obtain a shorter one. Hence the rational skew-group representation is faithful, and the reordered polynomial monomials are independent. The cross relation contains the constant1, so this is filtered by deg x_i=1, deg T_i=0, not a graded algebra with that assignment. Its associated graded is the polynomial semidirect product.
