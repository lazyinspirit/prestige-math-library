Its plan-spec A/B records, order 2668/2669, repeat this requirement. Therefore the existing conditional theorem is a valid implication but does not complete the approved scope. It does not narrow the problem to a ball or claim frequency-uniform constants.

The complete PE18 design (lines 1714–1744) permits a specified obstacle subclass, and the actual64 Definition and uniqueness theorem explicitly select a spherical obstacle. That theorem cannot be imported as general-C2 Dirichlet uniqueness. Its actual outgoing-mode lemma, however, applies on the exterior of any enclosing sphere independently of obstacle trace data. The proposed general-C2 uniqueness theorem uses that mode lemma and flux identities outside an enclosing ball; zero obstacle trace establishes zero enclosing-sphere flux, not zero enclosing-sphere trace. Harmonic lifting w(x,t)=exp(kt)u(x), Weyl regularity and published unique continuation propagate the vanishing through the connected exterior.

The current57 Laplace trace/mapping suppliers explicitly assume a polyhedral boundary. They cannot be imported as C2 Statements. A separate complete local C2 trace/extension lemma preserves their original hypotheses and proves the actual C2 branch from flat Fourier trace/lifting, graph changes of variables, a cutoff-kernel Fourier estimate and a screened interior lift.
