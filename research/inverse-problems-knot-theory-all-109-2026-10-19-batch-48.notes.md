### `cex-ip-finite-training-fit-fails-under-operator-shift`

- Refuted claim: a finite constant uniformly controls both forward residual and squared test risk over all scalar operator shifts whenever the one-sample squared training loss is zero.
- Witness: the one-step map $F_\theta(a,y)=\theta ay$ has singleton training sample $(1,1,1)$ and zero loss at $\theta=1$. On the shifted operator/data/test truth $(t,t,1)$, $t>1$, it predicts $t^2$, has forward residual $t(t^2-1)$, and has Dirac test risk $(t^2-1)^2$; both are unbounded as $t\to\infty$.

Its supplier item level is 4, and the A-definition level is 4, so this item is level 5.
