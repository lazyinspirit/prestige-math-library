Revisiting Cox’s PH Model: Asymptotic Theory
Earlier we sketched how the Martingale CLT can be applied to
the score function arising from Cox’s proportional hazards model.
This section shows this in greater detail and then sketches why the
”MPLE” from Cox’s partial likelihood function is consistent and
asymptotically normal.
Consider the usual setting in which the survival time and covari-
ate value for the ith of n subjects are denoted Ti and Zi, where the
potential censoring time is denoted Ci, and where we assume nonin-
formative censoring; i.e.,
Ti ⊥Ci | Zi .
Assume that the random variables corresponding to diﬀerent subjects
are independent, and that the Zi are bounded. The observation for
the ith subject consists of (Ui, δi, Zi), where
Ui = min (Ti, Ci), δi = 1(Ti ≤Ci).
The hazard function for a subject with covariate value z is the usual
Cox proportional hazards model
h(t|Z = z) = λ0(t)eβz.
We assume that there exists a τ such that Ci ≤τ with probability 1,
and that P(Yi(τ) = 1) > 0. Thus, τ is the largest possible observed
time.
