The worked example calculates a no-tie risk-set contribution, distinguishes an instantaneous hazard ratio from a cumulative event-risk ratio, and shows how time-varying covariate contrasts produce time-varying hazard ratios. Tied-event likelihoods require an explicitly stated grouped-time or coarsened-order convention.
