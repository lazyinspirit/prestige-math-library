## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice and Dependent Choice. Let $G$ be a locally compact Hausdorff abelian group, $\phi:G\to\mathbb C$ continuous positive definite with $k=\phi(0)$, and let $F_\phi$ be the positive functional on the transform core constructed in the preceding transform-core lemma. Then $F_\phi$ extends uniquely to a bounded positive linear functional on $C_0(\widehat G)$ with norm $k$, and there is a unique finite positive Radon measure $\mu_\phi$ on $\widehat G$ with
$$F_\phi(h)=\int_{\widehat G}h\,d\mu_\phi\quad(h\in C_0(\widehat G)),\qquad \mu_\phi(\widehat G)=k .$$
Uniqueness of $\mu_\phi$ follows from the uniqueness theorem for Fourier-Stieltjes transforms proved earlier on this page.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The Axiom of Choice and Dependent Choice, a locally compact Hausdorff abelian group $G$ with Haar measure $m_G$, a continuous positive definite $\phi$ with $k=\phi(0)$, and the positive bounded functional $F_\phi$ on the transform core constructed in [[lem-positive-definite-functions-give-positive-bounded-functionals-on-the-transform-core]].

[F1] The preceding transform-core lemma gives: $L_\phi(f)=\int_Gf(x)\phi(-x)\,dm_G(x)$ is well defined and linear on $A=L^1(G,m_G)$ with $|L_\phi(f)|\le k\|f\|_1$, $L_\phi(f*f^*)\ge0$, $|L_\phi(f)|^2\le kL_\phi(f*f^*)\le k^2\|\widehat f\|_\infty^2$, $L_\phi$ vanishes on $\{f:\widehat f=0\}$, and $F_\phi(\widehat f):=L_\phi(f)$ is a well-defined positive linear functional on the transform core $\{\widehat f:f\in L^1(G,m_G)\}\subseteq C_0(\widehat G)$ with $|F_\phi(h)|\le k\|h\|_\infty$ ([[lem-positive-definite-functions-give-positive-bounded-functionals-on-the-transform-core]], [[def-fourier-transform-on-an-lca-group]], [[def-positive-definite-function-on-an-abelian-group]]).

[F2] The transform algebra is a self-adjoint algebra: $\widehat{f*g}=\widehat f\widehat g$ and $\widehat{f^*}=\overline{\widehat f}$ ([[lem-lca-fourier-transform-intertwines-translation-modulation-and-convolution]]), its elements lie in $C_0(\widehat G)$ ([[thm-riemann-lebesgue-lemma-on-lca-groups]], [[def-compact-support-c-c-and-c-zero-on-an-lch-space]]), and the characters of $A^+=\mathbb C\oplus A$ are exactly $q(z,f)=z$ and $h_\gamma^+(z,f)=z+\widehat f(\gamma)$ on the compact Hausdorff space $\Delta(A^+)$ with Gelfand topology generated by the functions $\chi\mapsto\chi(a)$ ([[lem-lca-scalar-unitization-character-space-and-spectrum]], [[def-gelfand-transform]], [[thm-maximal-ideal-space-is-compact-hausdorff]], [[def-axiom-of-choice]]).

[F3] If $B\subseteq C(X,\mathbb C)$ is a point-separating self-adjoint complex function algebra on a compact Hausdorff space $X$ with exactly one common zero $x_0$, then its uniform closure is $\{F\in C(X,\mathbb C):F(x_0)=0\}$ ([[thm-complex-stone-weierstrass-self-adjoint]]).

[F4] A bounded positive linear functional on $C_0(X;\mathbb R)$ for an LCH space $X$ is integration against a unique finite regular Borel measure, whose total mass is its norm ([[lem-positive-c-zero-functionals-have-finite-regular-representing-measures]], [[def-radon-measure-on-an-lch-space]]).

[F5] The normalized local approximate identity $\{u_U\}\subseteq C_c(G)$ satisfies $u_U\ge0$, $\int_Gu_U\,dm_G=1$, $\operatorname{supp}u_U\subseteq U$, $\widehat{u_U}\in C_0(\widehat G)$ with $|\widehat{u_U}|\le1$; the evaluation pairing is jointly continuous, so $\widehat{u_U}\to1$ uniformly on compact subsets of $\widehat G$ as $U$ shrinks ([[lem-lca-translations-and-normalised-local-approximate-identities]], [[lem-character-evaluation-pairing-is-jointly-continuous]], [[def-pontryagin-dual-and-compact-open-topology]], [[lem-haar-measure-is-positive-on-nonempty-open-sets-and-finite-on-compact-sets]]).

[F6] Finite regular complex Borel measures on $\widehat G$ with the same inverse transform coincide ([[lem-fourier-stieltjes-transforms-determine-finite-radon-measures]], [[def-regular-complex-borel-measure-on-an-lch-space]]); the inverse transform $x\mapsto\int_{\widehat G}\gamma(x)\,d\sigma(\gamma)$ of a finite measure $\sigma$ is continuous, because $|\sigma|$ is inner regular and the characters converge uniformly on compact sets ([[def-radon-measure-on-an-lch-space]], [[lem-character-evaluation-pairing-is-jointly-continuous]]); Fubini applies to $f\in C_c(G)$ against a finite measure ([[thm-fubini-theorem-for-l-one-on-sigma-finite-product-spaces]]); and Haar measure is positive on nonempty open sets with Urysohn cutoffs available ([[lem-haar-measure-is-positive-on-nonempty-open-sets-and-finite-on-compact-sets]], [[lem-lch-urysohn-cutoff-for-a-compact-set-inside-an-open-set]]).

[F7] Bounded linear maps extend uniquely from a dense subspace with the same norm ([[thm-extension-of-a-bounded-map-from-a-dense-subspace]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 (Density of the transform core in $C_0(\widehat G)$.) The functions $\chi\mapsto\chi(0,f)$, $f\in A$, form a self-adjoint complex subalgebra $B$ of $C(\Delta(A^+))$ by [F2]. It is point-separating: distinct characters of $A^+$ are separated by some $a\in A^+$ because the Gelfand topology is Hausdorff, and since all characters agree on the constants this element may be taken as $(0,f)$, so $\widehat f$ separates them. Its only common zero is $q$: $q(0,f)=0$ for all $f$, while $h_\gamma^+\ne q$ gives some $f$ with $\widehat f(\gamma)\ne0$. By the vanishing-at-one-point case of [F3], the uniform closure of $B$ is $\{G\in C(\Delta(A^+)):G(q)=0\}$, which under $\Delta(A^+)\cong\widehat G\cup\{q\}$ is exactly $C_0(\widehat G)$; hence the transform core is uniformly dense in $C_0(\widehat G)$. [F2, F3]

1.2 (The approximate identity.) For the approximate identity of [F5], $L_\phi(u_U)=\int_Gu_U(x)\phi(-x)\,dm_G(x)\to\phi(0)=k$, because $u_U\ge0$, $\int_Gu_U=1$, $\operatorname{supp}u_U\subseteq U$ and $\phi$ is continuous at $0$. Also $\widehat{u_U}\in C_0(\widehat G)$, $|\widehat{u_U}|\le1$ and $\widehat{u_U}\to1$ uniformly on compact subsets of $\widehat G$. [F1, F5]

1.3 (Continuity of inverse transforms.) Let $\sigma$ be a finite regular complex Borel measure on $\widehat G$ and $\Psi(x):=\int_{\widehat G}\gamma(x)\,d\sigma(\gamma)$. For a net $x_i\to x_0$ and $\varepsilon>0$, inner regularity of $|\sigma|$ gives a compact $K\subseteq\widehat G$ with $|\sigma|(\widehat G\setminus K)<\varepsilon/4$; by joint continuity of the pairing and compactness of $K$ one has $\sup_{\gamma\in K}|\gamma(x_i)-\gamma(x_0)|<\varepsilon/(2|\sigma|(\widehat G)+2)$ eventually, so $$|\Psi(x_i)-\Psi(x_0)|\le\int_K|\gamma(x_i)-\gamma(x_0)|\,d|\sigma|+2|\sigma|(\widehat G\setminus K)<\varepsilon .$$ Hence $\Psi$ is continuous. [F6]

2.1 (Extension of $F_\phi$.) By step 1.1 the transform core is dense in $C_0(\widehat G)$, and by [F1] $F_\phi$ is linear on it with norm at most $k$. By [F7] it has a unique bounded linear extension $L:C_0(\widehat G)\to\mathbb C$ with $\|L\|=\|F_\phi\|\le k$. [F1, F7, step 1.1]

3.1 (Positivity of $L$.) Let $H\in C_0(\widehat G)$ with $H\ge0$. Since $\sqrt H\in C_0(\widehat G)$, step 1.1 gives $g_n\in\{\widehat f:f\in A\}$ with $\|g_n-\sqrt H\|_\infty\to0$; then $|g_n|^2\in\{\widehat f:f\in A\}$ by the self-adjoint algebra property [F2] and $|g_n|^2\to H$ uniformly, because $\||g_n|^2-H\|_\infty\le\|g_n-\sqrt H\|_\infty(\|g_n\|_\infty+\|\sqrt H\|_\infty)$ is eventually bounded by a constant times $\|g_n-\sqrt H\|_\infty$. Each $|g_n|^2=\widehat{f_n*f_n^*}$ for some $f_n\in A$, so $F_\phi(|g_n|^2)=L_\phi(f_n*f_n^*)\ge0$ by [F1]. Passing to the limit along step 2.1 gives $L(H)=\lim_nF_\phi(|g_n|^2)\ge0$. [F1, F2, step 1.1, step 2.1]

4.1 (Riesz-Markov representation.) The real part of $L$ is a bounded positive linear functional on $C_0(\widehat G;\mathbb R)$, so by [F4] there is a unique finite regular Borel measure $\mu_\phi\ge0$ on $\widehat G$ with $L(H)=\int_{\widehat G}H\,d\mu_\phi$ for all $H\in C_0(\widehat G)$, and $\mu_\phi(\widehat G)=\|L\|$. [F4, step 3.1]

5.1 (Mass $\mu_\phi(\widehat G)=k$.) By step 4.1 and step 2.1, $\int_{\widehat G}\widehat{u_U}\,d\mu_\phi=L(\widehat{u_U})=F_\phi(\widehat{u_U})=L_\phi(u_U)$ for every identity neighbourhood $U$. By step 1.2, $L_\phi(u_U)\to k$. By step 1.2 and step 4.1, $\int_{\widehat G}\widehat{u_U}\,d\mu_\phi\to\int_{\widehat G}1\,d\mu_\phi=\mu_\phi(\widehat G)$: for $\varepsilon>0$ choose compact $K$ with $\mu_\phi(\widehat G\setminus K)<\varepsilon/4$, then use $|\widehat{u_U}|\le1$ and eventual $\sup_K|1-\widehat{u_U}|<\varepsilon/(2(1+\mu_\phi(\widehat G)))$. Hence $\mu_\phi(\widehat G)=k=\|L\|$, and $L$ has norm exactly $k$. [F1, F5, step 1.2, step 2.1, step 4.1]

6.1 (Uniqueness of $\mu_\phi$.) Let $\nu$ be another finite positive Radon measure with $L(H)=\int_{\widehat G}H\,d\nu$ for all $H\in C_0(\widehat G)$; then $\sigma:=\mu_\phi-\nu$ is a finite regular complex Borel measure with $\int_{\widehat G}\widehat f\,d\sigma=0$ for every $f\in C_c(G)$. By Fubini [F6], $$0=\int_{\widehat G}\widehat f\,d\sigma=\int_Gf(x)\Bigl(\int_{\widehat G}\gamma(-x)\,d\sigma(\gamma)\Bigr)dm_G(x)=\int_Gf(x)\,\Psi(-x)\,dm_G(x)$$ with $\Psi(x):=\int_{\widehat G}\gamma(x)\,d\sigma(\gamma)$ continuous by step 1.3. If $\Psi(y_0)\ne0$, choose $c\in\mathbb C$ with $|c|=1$ and $\operatorname{Re}(c\Psi(y_0))>0$; by continuity, $\operatorname{Re}(c\Psi)>0$ on a nonempty open neighbourhood $V$ of $y_0$. Choose a nonzero nonnegative $f\in C_c(G)$ supported in $-V$, as provided by [F6]. Then $\operatorname{Re}(c\Psi(-x))>0$ on $\operatorname{supp}f$, so $\int_G f(x)\operatorname{Re}(c\Psi(-x))\,dm_G(x)>0$ by positivity of Haar measure on nonempty open sets, contradicting $c\int_Gf(x)\Psi(-x)\,dm_G(x)=0$. Hence $\Psi\equiv0$. The Fourier-Stieltjes uniqueness theorem [F6] now gives $\sigma=0$, that is, $\nu=\mu_\phi$. [F6, step 1.3, step 5.1]

7.1 Steps 2.1 and 5.1 exhibit the unique bounded linear extension $L$ of $F_\phi$ with $\|L\|=k$, step 3.1 proves it positive, step 4.1 represents it by the finite positive Radon measure $\mu_\phi$ of mass $\mu_\phi(\widehat G)=k$, and step 6.1 proves that this representing measure is unique. [step 1.2, step 2.1, step 3.1, step 4.1, step 5.1, step 6.1] ∎
