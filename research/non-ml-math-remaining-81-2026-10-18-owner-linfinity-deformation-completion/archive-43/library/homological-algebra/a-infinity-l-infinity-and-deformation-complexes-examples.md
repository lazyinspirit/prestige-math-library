These worked examples check the A-page conventions on explicit algebras. The
finite DGA has a singleton triple Massey product and an explicit minimal
A∞-model whose $m_3$ realizes that class. The filtered L∞ example computes a
Maurer–Cartan set directly.

The counterexamples mark two limits: without a complete filtration the
infinite Maurer–Cartan expression need not be an element of the underlying
direct sum, and the solution equation alone does not select an equivalence
relation. None of these examples supplies a general deformation classification
or gauge action.
