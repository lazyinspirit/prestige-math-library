## Remarks

This pair proves that a complete filtration makes the Maurer–Cartan series
well-defined. It separately defines A∞-morphisms, but it does not define
L∞-morphisms or a filtered gauge action. It therefore makes no claim that
Maurer–Cartan solutions classify a general deformation problem, either as a
set or modulo equivalence. Such a correspondence needs an explicitly defined
deformation object, a filtered morphism and gauge construction, and a proof
that the gauge relation matches the chosen equivalence of deformations. The
nilpotent dg Lie construction in the cited text is not imported as a proof for
this broader L∞ claim.
