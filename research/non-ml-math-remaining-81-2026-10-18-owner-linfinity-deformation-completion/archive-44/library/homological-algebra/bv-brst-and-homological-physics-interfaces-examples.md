This B page checks the degree-$+1$ BV convention on a one-variable polynomial
algebra, computes BRST cohomology for a dilation action, and refutes
nilpotence when the Lie algebra action fails the representation identity. The
items depend on the corresponding A-page interfaces and do not supply general
theorems.

Companion: [[bv-brst-and-homological-physics-interfaces]].

## Examples and counterexample

[[ex-one-variable-bv-algebra-solves-classical-master-equation]] verifies the
operator order, degree, square-zero, bracket, and classical master equation
for $A=\mathbb C[x,\theta]/(\theta^2)$ with $|\theta|=-1$.

[[ex-dilation-action-classical-brst-cohomology]] computes both the invariant
polynomials and the degree-one quotient for the one-dimensional dilation
action. [[cex-nonrepresentation-action-breaks-brst-nilpotence]] shows by an
explicit derivation action on $\mathbb C[x,y]$ why a Lie representation is
required.

## Boundaries

These are algebraic examples. They do not construct a measure or quantization,
and the counterexample does not alter the A-page representation hypothesis.
All three items remain draft and use only A-page items whose full arguments
are present in this pair.
