1. V. Dolgushev and C. Rogers, *A Version of the Goldman–Millson Theorem for Filtered L∞-Algebras*, arXiv:1407.6735v2, 6 March 2015. URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1407.6735 . The parent also retrieved the full PDF at ../sources/dolgushev-rogers.pdf, SHA256 `63ca5ac00752a230e2ae3d312625d68acbbc110c9bc83b6704ce412fee2c9649`. Its full text is ../sources/dolgushev-rogers.txt. Relevant locally inspected spans are PDF pp. 1–9, especially pp. 5–8, equations (2.6)–(2.24). The exact local extracted text, with original line numbers, is retained in sources/dolgushev-rogers-read-spans.txt. The original PDF, rather than occasional corrupted extracted summation indices, controls the meaning.

   On PDF p. 6, equations (2.8)–(2.11) define complete descending filtrations, their bracket and morphism compatibility, and `U_*(α):=Σ_{m≥1}(1/m!)U'(α^m)`. On p. 7, Proposition 2.1, equation (2.14), states `curv(U_*(α))=Σ_{m≥0}(1/m!)U'(α^m curv(α))`. The paper explicitly says the proposition's proof is in its reference [6]; the exponential proof supplied here is an independent local derivation of that particular formula, not a claim that this paper proves it on p. 7. Equations (2.17)–(2.19) give the twist and the marked-input morphism coefficients.

   On PDF p. 3, the paper defines `L ⊗̂ Ω_n:=lim_k ((L/F_kL)⊗Ω_n)`; p. 8 uses the same completion. Its p. 8 describes 1-cells as `β=β_0(t_0)+dt_0 β_1(t_0)`, with equations (2.20), (2.21): `curv(β_0(t_0))=0` and `(d/dt_0)β_0(t_0)=∂^{β_0(t_0)}β_1(t_0)`. Equation (2.22) extends the Taylor maps to polynomial forms using the Koszul sign, and (2.23)–(2.24) state simplicial MC functoriality. Our right-placed expression `w−a dt` equals this left-placed `w+dt a` since a has odd degree. Our labels 0 and 1 denote direct evaluations; no simplicial face-index convention is assumed.

2. E. Getzler, *Lie theory for nilpotent L∞-algebras*, arXiv:math/0404003v4, 2 May 2007. URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0404003 . Parent's full PDF ../sources/getzler.pdf, SHA256 `6835ae195110a40ca3cc5ad5171381adeac6384989d80db6d7f7631eb6e9fbce`; text ../sources/getzler.txt. Relevant inspected spans: PDF pp. 13–18, in particular p. 14, Definition 4.2, Definition 4.3 and Proposition 4.4; p. 15, proof of Proposition 4.4, Lemma 4.5 and its proof, and the dg scalar extension formulas directly following it. Exact text spans are retained in sources/getzler-read-spans.txt.

   Getzler explicitly says on p. 14 that nilpotent curvature “is defined, and polynomial in α.” That is a nilpotent statement; our inverse-limit extension only asserts polynomiality modulo every F^N. His proof of Proposition 4.4 sums the (m+n)-Jacobi relations after inserting m copies of α; Lemma 4.5 sums the n-Jacobi relations to prove Bianchi. The tensor-product formulas on p. 15 include `[x⊗a]=[x]⊗a+(−1)^{|x|}x⊗da` and the Koszul signed scalar product in higher brackets. We derive the scalar-crossing sign for the explicitly declared shifted W⊗A ordering, rather than importing an unshifted bracket sign without checking it.

## Added A-infinity suspension interface

Retrieved the complete Bernhard Keller, *Introduction to A-infinity algebras and modules*, arXiv:math/9910179v2 PDF from https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9910179v2 into sources/keller-9910179v2.pdf (SHA256 `fddc7ad7039e2d1a7f2248f1b74904b0b7873103466482e1f452b92fa648a144`), with full local text extraction and numbered read spans. Inspected §3.1 (native operation identities and Koszul rule), §3.4 (native morphism identities and right-hand eta sign), §3.6 (tensor coderivations, suspension square, square-zero and morphism correspondence). Relevant PDF pp. 5–6, 8–9, 11–12. Keller defines the suspension by the commuting square b_n s^⊗n=s m_n. With graded tensor-map inverses this is exactly b_n=(−1)^binom(n,2) s m_n u^⊗n, not an unsigned use of u^⊗n. The new canonical lemma proves the full sign comparison for operations and morphisms locally and proves coderivation/coalgebra-map extension and uniqueness directly.
