## Definition

Let $k$ be a field, $n\geq 1$, and $\hbar\in k$. Let $E_n$ be the $2n$-dimensional $k$-vector space with basis $x_1,\ldots,x_n,p_1,\ldots,p_n$, and form its tensor algebra $T(E_n)$ ([[def-tensor-algebra-of-a-vector-space]]). Let $J_{n,\hbar}$ be the two-sided ideal generated by

$$x_ix_j-x_jx_i,\qquad p_ip_j-p_jp_i,\qquad p_ix_j-x_jp_i-\hbar\delta_{ij}1\quad(1\leq i,j\leq n).$$

The **polynomial Weyl algebra with central parameter $\hbar$** is

$$W_n(k;\hbar):=T(E_n)/J_{n,\hbar},$$

where the quotient is the ring quotient from [[def-quotient-ring]] and $J_{n,\hbar}$ is the generated ideal from [[def-generated-and-principal-ideals]]. The standard $n$th polynomial Weyl algebra is $A_n(k):=W_n(k;1)$. The phrase “polynomial Weyl algebra” here means this algebraic associative quotient; no topology, completion, or unitary exponential generators are part of the definition.

The exact presentation, normal-ordered basis, and equivalence with the central quotient of $U(\mathfrak h_n(k))$ are established in [[thm-polynomial-weyl-algebra-normal-ordered-basis]].
