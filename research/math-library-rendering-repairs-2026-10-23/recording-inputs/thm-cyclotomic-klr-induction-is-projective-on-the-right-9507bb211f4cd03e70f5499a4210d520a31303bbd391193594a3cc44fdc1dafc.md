## Statement

Let $R^\Lambda$ be the cyclotomic KLR algebra of
[[def-cyclotomic-klr-quotient-at-a-dominant-weight]], and let
$E^\Lambda_i,F^\Lambda_i$ be the functors of
[[def-cyclotomic-klr-induction-and-restriction-functors]]. For every $i\in I$
and $\beta\in Q_+$:

(a) the bimodule $R^\Lambda(\beta+\alpha_i)e(\beta,i)$ is a projective right
$R^\Lambda(\beta)$-module, and $e(\beta,i)R^\Lambda(\beta+\alpha_i)$ is a
projective left $R^\Lambda(\beta)$-module;

(b) consequently $E^\Lambda_i$ and $F^\Lambda_i$ are exact and send finitely
generated graded projectives to finitely generated graded projectives;
moreover $F^\Lambda_i$ preserves all projectives and $E^\Lambda_i$ preserves
finitely generated ones;

(c) there is a functorial exact sequence in
$\mathrm{Mod}(R(\beta+\alpha_i))$
$$0\longrightarrow q^{(\alpha_i\mid 2\Lambda-\beta)}\bar F_iM\longrightarrow F_iM\longrightarrow F^\Lambda_iM\longrightarrow 0,\qquad M\in\mathrm{Mod}(R^\Lambda(\beta)),$$
where $F_i$ is the right-append and $\bar F_i$ the left-append generic
induction of
[[lem-generic-klr-induction-and-restriction-mixed-relations]], regarded on
$R^\Lambda$-modules through the quotient, and the exponent is the Cartan
pairing $(\alpha_i\mid 2\Lambda-\beta)=2(\alpha_i\mid\Lambda)
-(\alpha_i\mid\beta)$
([[def-quantum-integers-factorials-and-divided-powers-at-q-i]] records the
normalization of the powers of $q$).

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The cyclotomic quotient $R^\Lambda$ of [[def-cyclotomic-klr-quotient-at-a-dominant-weight]]; put $S=R(\beta+\alpha_i)$, $A=R^\Lambda(\beta)$, $e_R=e(\beta,i)$, $e_L=e(i,\beta)$.

[F1] $S e_R$ is a free right $R(\beta)$-module and $e_LS$ a free left $R(\beta)$-module by the PBW/coset decomposition ([[thm-klr-pbw-basis-theorem]], [[def-klr-induction-and-restriction-bimodules]]); the generic functors $F_i,\bar F_i,E_i$ and their definitions and exactness are [[lem-generic-klr-induction-and-restriction-mixed-relations]].

[F2] The cyclotomic ideal is the two-sided ideal generated by $a_{\nu_1}(x_1)e(\nu)$; in the standard graded case it is homogeneous and the quotient is graded. Multiplication by any dot $x_a$ on $R^\Lambda(\beta)$ is killed by a monic polynomial with scalar coefficients ([[lem-cyclotomic-klr-dot-annihilators-and-finite-root-strings]]).

[F3] A finite projective module is a direct summand of a finite free module. For a central monic polynomial $f\in Z(A)[t]$ of degree $m$, polynomial division gives an $A$-linear decomposition $A[t]=fA[t]\oplus A[t]_{<m}$, with $A[t]_{<m}\cong A^m$. The resolution splitting needed below is proved explicitly in step 1.2.

[F4] The polynomial realization of [[thm-polynomial-representation-of-a-klr-algebra]] is faithful, and its localized crossing words expand into independent permutation operators over the rational function field.

## Proof

**Proof technique:** compare the cyclotomic towers over the generic ones with an explicit monic comparison map, then use the monic-annihilator projective splitting to deduce projectivity, exactness and the exact sequence with its shift.

If $A=0$, the comparison modules and functor values are zero, so all assertions are vacuous; polynomial degrees below are used only for $A\ne0$.

1.1 Comparison modules and their projectivity. Put $n=|\beta|$ and let $R_1(\beta)=\xi_nR(\beta)$ act on positions $2,\ldots,n+1$. For $n>0$ set $J_0=Sa(x_1)R(\beta)e_R$ and $J_1=Sa(x_2)R_1(\beta)e_L$; for $n=0$ set $J_0=J_1=0$, since the old block has no cyclotomic relation. Put $K_0=Se_R/J_0$, $K_1=Se_L/J_1$. The right action of $A[t]$ on $K_0$ uses the first $n$ strands and $t=x_{n+1}$; on $K_1$ it uses $\xi_n$ and $t=x_1$. The labelled PBW cosets make $Se_R$ and $Se_L$ finite projective over the corresponding separated-block algebras $R(\beta)[t]$: each homogeneous coset summand is a shifted principal word-idempotent module. Tensoring these summands with $A[t]$ gives precisely $K_0,K_1$, so both are finite projective over $A[t]$. At $n=0$ both are $k[t]$. [F1, F2, algebra]

1.2 Monic annihilators and projectivity, locally. Let $0\to P_1\xrightarrow{j}P_0\to M\to0$ be an exact sequence of right $A[t]$-modules with finite projective $P_0,P_1$, and suppose a central monic $f$ annihilates $M$. Multiplication by $f$ is injective on a free $A[t]$-module by its unit leading coefficient, hence on its projective summands. On a finite free module monic division gives an $A$-linear operator $p_{\mathrm{free}}$ taking a polynomial to its quotient by $f$, with $p_{\mathrm{free}}(xf)=x$. Choose split inclusion $u:P_1\to A[t]^N$ and projection $v:A[t]^N\to P_1$; then $p=vp_{\mathrm{free}}u$ satisfies $p(xf)=x$ on $P_1$, since $u$ is $A[t]$-linear and $vu=1$. Since $fM=0$, multiplication by $f$ on $P_0$ has image in $j(P_1)$, defining $h:P_0\to P_1$ by $jh=f$. Injectivity of $j$ gives $hj=f$ on $P_1$, so $phj=1$. Thus $j$ has the $A$-linear retraction $ph$, and $M$ is a direct summand of the $A$-projective module $P_0$ and is $A$-projective. Equivalently, $P_r/fP_r$ is a summand of $(A[t]/fA[t])^N\cong A^{mN}$ and is $A$-projective, which is the monic-quotient reason for the splitting. This argument uses finite projective presentations and division and does not assume $f$ invertible or the target $M$ projective. [F3, algebra]

2.1 The cyclotomic ideal and the map $P$. Throughout put $\kappa_a=-\tau_a$. The first-position PBW cosets give $S=\sum_{r=0}^n(k^I[x_1]\otimes R_1(n))\kappa_1\cdots\kappa_r$. Since $a(x_1)$ commutes with this coefficient algebra, $Sa(x_1)S=\sum_{r=0}^nSa(x_1)\kappa_1\cdots\kappa_r$. After multiplying by $e_R$, terms $r<n$ lie in $J_0$; the remaining term is the image of $\widetilde P:Se_L\to K_0$, $u\mapsto ua(x_1)\kappa_1\cdots\kappa_n$. Thus its image is the kernel of $K_0\to Be_R$, where $B=R^\Lambda(\beta+\alpha_i)$. To check right linearity, move $x_{a+1}$ through $a(x_1)\kappa_1\cdots\kappa_n$ to $x_a$ and move $x_1$ to $x_{n+1}$. Each mixed-relation correction omits one transfer crossing and has the form, up to its color projector and sign, $(\kappa_{a+1}\cdots\kappa_n)a(x_1)\kappa_1\cdots\kappa_{a-1}$, hence belongs to $J_0$. Moving $\kappa_{a+1}$ to $\kappa_a$ introduces only the braid correction $a(x_1)\kappa_1\cdots\kappa_{a-1}D_a\kappa_{a+2}\cdots\kappa_n$, with $D_a$ a dot polynomial; the far crossings can be moved left and the last-dot powers commute with the shorter word, again putting it in $J_0$. Idempotents follow the cyclic transfer. These generator identities prove $\widetilde P$ is right $R(\beta)[t]$-linear. It therefore kills $J_1$, and induces an $(S,A[t])$-map $P:K_1\to K_0$ with the same image. [step 1.1, F1, algebra]

3.1 Intertwiners and the map $Q$. Define $g_a=\kappa_a$ on unequal adjacent colors and $g_a=(x_{a+1}-x_a)-(x_{a+1}-x_a)^2\kappa_a$ on equal colors. The source mixed relations give $x_{s_a(b)}g_a=g_ax_b$ for every $b$. They also give $\kappa_ag_{a+1}g_a=g_{a+1}g_a\kappa_{a+1}$: subtract the two sides and use the mixed relations to see that the difference intertwines $x_a$ with $x_{a+2}$ and fixes $x_{a+1}$. In the faithful localized polynomial realization [F4], its longest-permutation coefficients cancel, since both products cross the same three color pairs with the same factors. All remaining permutations have length at most two, and none interchanges $a$ and $a+2$ while fixing the middle variable. Independence of the permutation operators then forces every coefficient to vanish. Consequently right multiplication by $g_n\cdots g_1$ intertwines old dots/crossings with their shifted copies and $x_{n+1}$ with $x_1$. It descends to $Q:K_0\to K_1$: the first-position cosets write $J_0$ as the sum of the terms $Sa(x_1)\kappa_1\cdots\kappa_r e_R$, $r<n$, and the intertwining identities send each such term to $Sg_n\cdots g_1a(x_2)\kappa_2\cdots\kappa_{r+1}e_L\subseteq J_1$. Thus $Q$ is an $(S,A[t])$-map; at $n=0$ it is the identity. [step 2.1, F1, F4, algebra]

4.1 The comparison polynomial, including repeated colors. For a prefix $\nu=(\nu_1,\ldots,\nu_n)$ set $C_\nu=a_i(x_1)\prod_{a:\nu_a\ne i}Q_{i\nu_a}(x_1,x_{a+1})$. We prove $a(x_1)\kappa_1\cdots\kappa_ng_n\cdots g_1e(i,\nu)\equiv C_\nu e(i,\nu)\pmod{J_1}$ by induction on $n$. At $n=0$ it is multiplication by $a_i(t)$; at $n=1$ unequal colors use the quadratic relation. For equal colors, $\kappa_1g_1=\kappa_1(x_2-x_1)$, and $\kappa_1(x_2-x_1)-1$ interchanges $x_1,x_2$; hence $a_i(x_1)\kappa_1g_1\equiv a_i(x_1)$ modulo $Sa_i(x_2)$. For $n>1$, if $\nu_n\ne i$, the quadratic relation and the dot-intertwining identities peel off $Q_{i\nu_n}(x_1,x_{n+1})$, leaving the shorter comparison. If $\nu_n=i$ and $\nu_{n-1}=j\ne i$, the braid correction splits the comparison into two terms whose reductions, for $\nu''=(\nu_1,\ldots,\nu_{n-2})$, are $C_{\nu''}Q_{ji}(x_n,x_{n+1})$ and $-C_{\nu''}(Q_{ij}(x_{n+1},x_n)-Q_{ij}(x_1,x_n))$. Their sum is $C_{\nu''}Q_{ij}(x_1,x_n)=C_\nu$ by $Q$ symmetry. If the last two colors are $i$, expansion of $g_{n-1}$, the ordinary equal-color braid, and the shorter comparison reduce the expression to $C_{\nu'}[(x_{n+1}-x_1)\kappa_n-\kappa_n(x_{n+1}-x_1)\kappa_n(x_n-x_1)]$, where $\nu'=\nu''i$. Here $C_{\nu'}$ commutes with $\kappa_n$, $\kappa_n(x_{n+1}-x_1)\kappa_n=\kappa_n$ and $\kappa_n(x_n-x_1)=(x_{n+1}-x_1)\kappa_n-1$, so the bracket is $1$ and $C_{\nu'}=C_\nu$. All congruences remain valid under dot multiplication because the shifted cyclotomic ideal defining $J_1$ is a right dot submodule. These cases exhaust the induction and establish $QP=C(t)$, with $C(t)=\sum_{\nu\in I^\beta}a_i(t)\prod_{a:\nu_a\ne i}Q_{i\nu_a}(t,x_a)e(\nu)\in A[t]$. [step 2.1, step 3.1, F1, F2, induction, algebra]

5.1 Injectivity and the exact resolution. The degree of $C(t)$ in $t$ is $\lambda_i+\sum_{j\ne i}(-a_{ij})\beta_j$, and its leading coefficient is the nonzero scalar $\prod_{j\ne i}t_{ij}^{\beta_j}$, independent of $\nu$. Its coefficients are central in $A$: unequal crossings merely permute the color-labelled factors, and on equal adjacent colors the factor product is symmetric, so its divided-difference correction vanishes. Normalize the leading coefficient to $1$. Multiplication by this central monic polynomial is injective on a free $A[t]$-module by its leading term, and hence on a projective summand. Step 4.1 therefore makes $QP$, and consequently $P$, injective. With step 2.1 this proves $0\to q^sK_1\xrightarrow{P}K_0\to Be_R\to0$. In the standard homogeneous case $\deg P=2d_i\lambda_i-\sum_a b_{i\nu_a}=(\alpha_i\mid2\Lambda-\beta)=s$, so this is a graded resolution; the monic branch is ungraded. [step 1.1, step 2.1, step 4.1, F1, algebra]

6.1 Projectivity of the cyclotomic bimodules. The appended dot on $M=Be_R$ is annihilated by a central monic scalar-coefficient polynomial by [F2]. Apply step 1.2 to the projective $A[t]$-resolution in step 5.1, with $M=Be_R$, to conclude that $Be_R$ is right $A$-projective. This module is finitely generated over $A$: its finite $A[t]$-generators together with dot powers below the degree of the annihilator generate it over $A$. In the homogeneous standard case the resolution and $M$ are graded; decompose the resulting $A$-linear splitting into homogeneous components on finite homogeneous generators, and take its degree-zero component to get a graded splitting. The generator-fixing anti-involution sends $Be_R$ to $e_RB$ and converts this right projectivity into left $A$-projectivity. The same monic argument is valid for arbitrary ungraded monic cyclotomic deformations. [F1, F2, step 1.2, step 5.1]

7.1 Exactness and preservation. Tensoring by the right $A$-projective module $Be_R$ is exact, so $F_i^\Lambda$ is exact, and idempotent restriction $E_i^\Lambda$ is exact. The left $B$-module $Be_R$ is a direct summand of $B$, hence induction sends any projective $A$-module, presented as a summand of a free module, to a projective $B$-module; finite generators remain finite under induction. A finitely generated projective $B$-module is a summand of a finite direct sum of shifts of $B$, and its restriction is consequently a summand of the corresponding finite direct sum of shifts of $e_RB$. Left $A$-projectivity and finite generation of $e_RB$ from step 6.1 therefore show that restriction preserves finitely generated projectives. These arguments apply ungraded to monic deformations as well. [step 6.1, algebra]

7.2 The exact sequence (c). Tensor the bimodule resolution $0\to q^sK_1\xrightarrow{P}K_0\to Be_R\to0$ of step 5.1 over $A$ with a left $A$-module $M$. It remains exact because its cokernel $Be_R$ is right $A$-projective by step 6.1, so the original sequence is split as a sequence of right $A$-modules. By their definitions in step 1.1, $K_1\otimes_A M$ is the left-append generic induction $\bar F_iM$ and $K_0\otimes_A M$ is the right-append generic induction $F_iM$, while $Be_R\otimes_A M=F_i^\Lambda M$. The map $P$ is left $R(\beta+\alpha_i)$-linear and has homogeneous degree $s=(\alpha_i\mid 2\Lambda-\beta)$, giving exactly the functorial sequence in (c) in the declared target category. On the ungraded monic-deformation branch the same sequence is interpreted without a grading shift. [F1, step 1.1, step 5.1, step 6.1]

8.1 Conclusion. The projectivity, preservation and exact-sequence arguments prove (a), (b) and (c) with the displayed shift, in the graded standard case with graded projectives and verbatim ungraded for a monic deformation; the comparison is monic and no filtered-colon citation is used. [step 6.1, step 7.2] ∎
