## Definition

Use the data of [[def-rational-cherednik-reflection-data-and-parameters]]. The tensor algebra $T(\mathfrak V)=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}\mathfrak V^{\otimes n}$ is the unital associative algebra generated by $\mathfrak V$ with no relations. The smash product $T(\mathfrak V)\rtimes\mathbb C W$ is the vector space $T(\mathfrak V)\otimes\mathbb C W$ with multiplication determined by $(a w)(b v)=a\,w(b)\,(wv)$, for $a,b\in T(\mathfrak V)$ and $w,v\in W$.

For $t\in\mathbb C$, define $H_{t,c}(W,\mathfrak h)$ to be the quotient of this smash product by the two-sided ideal generated by
$$[x,x']=0,\qquad [y,y']=0,\qquad [y,x]=t\,x(y)1-\sum_{s\in S}c_s\alpha_s(y)\alpha_s^\vee(x)s,$$
for all $x,x'\in\mathfrak h^*$ and $y,y'\in\mathfrak h$. Here $[a,b]=ab-ba$. The product $\alpha_s(y)\alpha_s^\vee(x)$ is the normalization from the data item; the parameter $c_s$ is constant on conjugacy classes. This quotient is an associative unital complex algebra by construction. The case $t=1$ is denoted $H_{1,c}$ and is used for category O.

This definition makes no PBW or noncollapse assertion. Those are proved in [[thm-rational-cherednik-pbw-theorem]].

## Remarks

For $W=\{1\}$ and $\dim\mathfrak h=1$, the presentation is $[y,x]=t$. For the order-two reflection on a one-dimensional $\mathfrak h$, the normalization in the preceding definition gives $[y,x]=t-2cs$.
