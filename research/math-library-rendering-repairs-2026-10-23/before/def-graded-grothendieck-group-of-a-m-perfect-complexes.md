## Definition

Fix $m\ge1$, let $A_m$ be the Khovanov–Seidel type A algebra, and let
$$C_m=K^b(\operatorname{proj}^{gr}A_m)$$
be the bounded homotopy category of finite graded projective left
$A_m$-modules of [[def-bounded-projective-homotopy-category-for-a-m]], with its
distinguished triangles and its homological shift $[1]$. The **graded
Grothendieck group** of $A_m$ is the triangulated Grothendieck group
$$G(A_m):=K_0(C_m)=K_0^{\mathrm{tri}}\bigl(K^b(\operatorname{proj}^{gr}A_m)\bigr)$$
of [[def-triangulated-grothendieck-group]] applied to the small coded
presentation of [[def-triangulated-k-zero-of-khovanov-seidel-projectives]]: the
free abelian group on the set of isomorphism classes of objects of $C_m$ modulo
the subgroup generated by the relations $[Y]=[X]+[Z]$ for every distinguished
triangle $X\to Y\to Z\to X[1]$ of $C_m$. Write $[X]$ for the class of $X$.

**Class rules.** The definition is the triangulated one, so:

- the cone triangle $X\oplus Y\to Y\to X[1]\to X[1]\oplus Y[1]$ of a biproduct
  gives $[X\oplus Y]=[X]+[Y]$;
- the rotated triangle $X\to0\to X[1]\to X[1]$ gives $[X[1]]=-[X]$, the
  statement of [[lem-homological-and-internal-shifts-on-khovanov-seidel-k-zero]];
- the internal shift $\{1\}$, which is an exact automorphism of $C_m$ distinct
  from $[1]$, makes $G(A_m)$ into a module over $\mathbb Z[q,q^{-1}]$ with
  $[X\{r\}]=q^r[X]$, again by
  [[lem-homological-and-internal-shifts-on-khovanov-seidel-k-zero]].

The two shifts are never identified: $[1]$ is homological and contributes a
sign $-1$, $\{1\}$ is internal and contributes a unit $q$.

**Comparison with perfect complexes.** The composite
$$\Theta\colon C_m=K^b(\operatorname{proj}^{gr}A_m)\longrightarrow D^b(A_m\text{-mod})$$
of inclusion and localization is exact, full, faithful and essentially
surjective by
[[lem-bounded-finite-projective-model-for-khovanov-seidel-modules]]; the graded
clause of [[thm-perfect-complex-k-zero-agrees-with-projective-k-zero]] identifies
the target's triangulated Grothendieck group with $K_0^{\mathrm{split}}$ of the
finite graded projectives. Consequently $G(A_m)$ is canonically isomorphic to
the Grothendieck group of the graded perfect complexes of
[[def-perfect-complex-over-a-ring]] on the bounded projective model, and $[X]$
corresponds to the Euler characteristic $\sum_n(-1)^n[P^n]$ of any bounded
finite graded projective complex representing $X$.

**Scope and warnings.** This is emphatically the *triangulated* $K_0$ of
projective complexes and **not** the short-exact $G_0$ of the abelian category
of all finitely generated graded $A_m$-modules, and no identification of the two
is claimed; $G_0$ may a priori be a quotient of $G(A_m)$. No structure theorem
for finite-dimensional algebras over a field is applied to the integral algebra
$A_m$. The freeness of $G(A_m)$ on the shifted vertex-projective classes is not
asserted here: it is the content of
[[lem-graded-grothendieck-group-of-a-m-is-free-on-the-shifted-vertex-projectives]],
which uses the classification of finite graded projectives and the comparison
above.

**Notation used below.** Since the internal shift is an automorphism, write
$q^r[P_i]:=[P_i\{r\}]$, so that $G(A_m)$ is generated as a $\mathbb Z[q,q^{-1}]$-module
by the classes of the vertex projectives $P_0,\dots,P_m$; the next lemma on this
page shows these generate freely.
