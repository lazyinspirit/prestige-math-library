## Definition

Let $d\ge1$, let $K\subseteq\mathbb R^d$ be a nonempty bounded closed convex set with nonempty interior, and let $U\subseteq\mathbb R^d$ be an open convex set such that $\operatorname{int}(K)\subseteq U$ and $K\subseteq\overline U$. If a decision set is lower-dimensional in an ambient Euclidean space, take $\mathbb R^d$ to be orthonormal affine coordinates on its affine hull (the smallest affine subspace containing it); thus interior, gradients, Hessians, and norms are relative to that affine hull. A **mirror regularizer** on $(K,U)$ is a real-valued function $R$ on $K\cup U$ that is continuous on $K$, twice continuously differentiable on $U$, agrees on $K\cap U$, and has symmetric positive-definite Hessian $H_R(z):=\nabla^2R(z)$ for every $z\in U$. We call $R$ $m$-strongly convex on $K$ in the Euclidean norm if, for some $m>0$, all $x,y\in K$ and $\lambda\in[0,1]$ satisfy

$$R((1-\lambda)x+\lambda y)\le (1-\lambda)R(x)+\lambda R(y)-\frac m2\lambda(1-\lambda)\|x-y\|_2^2.$$

For $x\in K\cup U$ and $y\in U$, the **Bregman divergence** generated by $R$ is

$$B_R(x\|y):=R(x)-R(y)-\langle\nabla R(y),x-y\rangle.$$

For $z\in U$ and vectors $v,h\in\mathbb R^d$, define the squared **local primal norm** and **local dual norm** by

$$\|v\|_z^2:=v^\top H_R(z)v,\qquad \|h\|_{*,z}^2:=h^\top H_R(z)^{-1}h.$$

## Remarks

The vertical gap below illustrates the divergence using the one-dimensional regularizer $R(t)=t^2/2$, with $K=[0,4]$ and $U=\mathbb R$. At $y=1$, the tangent approximation is $T_y(t):=R(y)+R'(y)(t-y)=t-1/2$. For $x=3$, the gap is $R(3)-T_y(3)=9/2-5/2=2=B_R(3\|1)$. Thus the second argument specifies the point of tangency. The curve is shown only on part of its domain; its drawn ends are not domain boundaries. Here $H_R(z)=1$, so both local norms are the ordinary absolute value. The illustration supplements the definitions.
