## Repairs and exact evidence

1. **Chern connection — missing locality prerequisite**, `items/thm-chern-connection-of-a-hermitian-holomorphic-line-bundle.md`, Proof 1.1 and 4.1. The connection was defined only on global smooth sections, but the construction/uniqueness calculations used local frames without establishing that an arbitrary connection restricts locally. Added the cutoff proof: if a section vanishes near p, chi s=0 with chi=1 near p forces nabla s(p)=0 by Leibniz; compactly supported local extensions therefore give a well-defined local action. The already opened `lem-manifold-bump-for-a-compact-set-inside-an-open-set`, Statement, supplies the cutoffs without a choice hypothesis. Added its dependency, F5 and exact contract citation; synchronized derivations and uses. The supplied-metric theorem and its conclusions remain unchanged.

2. **Chern connection — inaccurate source locator**, same carrier, first source reference. Demailly’s Proposition 10.3 is on printed page 267, whereas the reference said 266. Page 266 contains the start of §10 and (10.1)–(10.2); page 267 contains the proposition. Corrected the locator to 267. The local formula (12.2)–(12.3) remains correctly located at 268–269.

3. **Adjoint/local-formula lemma — inaccurate source locators**, `items/lem-dbar-adjoint-and-dolbeault-laplacian-local-formulas.md`, first and third source references. Demailly VI §1 (1.8), the injective-symbol definition, occurs on printed page 289, outside the claimed 287–288 range; enlarged that range to 287–289. VI §7’s opening paragraph immediately preceding (7.1) occurs on printed page 309, whereas the reference said 308; corrected to 309. No formula or statement changed; its contract derivations were preserved and synchronized.

4. **Gårding theorem — cutoff derivative/computation gap**, `items/thm-garding-estimate-for-the-dolbeault-laplacian-on-a-compact-riemann-surface.md`, Proof 1.1. The old passage said to multiply w by a chart cutoff and then asserted that the derivative of its mollification was h*rho_epsilon, still using the original h. The actual derivative is eta h+(dbar eta)w. Replaced this passage by the compact-support argument before localization, obtained convolution commutation directly by testing the weak identity against the translated kernel, and then explicitly retained both cutoff terms in the local application. Explained why local L2 convergence becomes global for uniformly compactly supported mollifications. The normalized mollifier and cutoffs are supplied by the opened compact-set bump lemma (new F13); positivity of its normalizing integral follows from positivity on a neighborhood. This repairs the computation without changing the estimate.

5. **Gårding theorem — unmet all-order comparison/completion prerequisite**, same carrier, Proof 2.2. The proof summed local H^(k+2) estimates as if arbitrary-order local norms were already controlled by the fixed global norms, and as if local Sobolev membership automatically meant membership in the global completion. The preceding maximal-operator item only proves norm comparison in orders 1 and 2. Added the finite-order chain/Leibniz argument on compact overlaps with bounded transition derivatives and nonvanishing coordinate Jacobians; it controls the local datum norms by the fixed global norms. Added chartwise smooth approximation, cutoff and zero extension, then finite summation to place the solution in the completed global space. Also established continuous injective inclusion in L2 by testing derivative limits. F14 cites the opened smooth differential chain rule; F3, F6, F7 and F13 supply Hilbert testing, approximation and cutoff interfaces. Updated the exact derivation and citation-use mappings. Corrected stale contract boundary references to F10 (choice) and Step 1.2 (ellipticity).

6. **Nonharmonic exact form — missing quotient-topology prerequisites**, `items/ex-nonharmonic-exact-dbar-form.md`, Verification 1.1. Disjoint translation charts and compactness had been shown, but the application of compact Riemann-surface Hodge theory also requires Hausdorffness, second countability, connectedness and nonemptiness. Added the open quotient-map argument, positive separation from the square lattice, images of a countable plane base, and connected/nonempty continuous-image argument. Added F4 citing the already declared/opened covering-action, covering-quotient and Riemann-surface suppliers, and their contract mappings. The explicit function and derivative are unchanged.

7. **Constant zero mode — citation/prerequisite mismatch**, `items/ex-one-dimensional-constant-zero-mode-of-dolbeault-laplacian.md`, F3 and its dependency/contract citation, used in Verification 1.1. The old extreme-value target assumed a compact metric space; the application was to the compact manifold topology and supplied no distance-topology bridge. The opened `thm-compactness-under-continuous-maps`, Statement clause 2, gives exactly the topological extreme-value assertion needed. Replaced the dependency and F3 link/qualification and quoted that target in the contract. The proof and claim are unchanged.

8. **Contract-only inaccuracies**, `research/frontier-43-complex-representation-15-batch-9.proof-contracts.json`. The line-bundle definition’s degenerate-case row incorrectly claimed a positive metric hypothesis; replaced it by the actual rank-one/nonvanishing-transition restriction.
