# Completion and idempotent helper for arbitrary Hecke specialization

Research draft, 2026-10-18. No canonical edits or acceptance claims.
This supersedes the DVR construction in `local-basic-set-proof.md`.
The completion route retains every θ:Z[v,v^{-1}]→k and does not need
Schur cyclotomic factorization, a root-of-unity reduction, or an embedding
into a p-adic field. Assume the Axiom of Choice, as required by the canonical
commutative-algebra suppliers below.

## 1. The correct complete local domain for θ

Put A=Z[v,v^{-1}], p=kerθ, B=A_p. The map A/p→k is injective, so
k_0=Frac(A/p) is canonically a subfield of k. The residue field of B is
k_0: B/pB is the localization of A/p at all its nonzero elements. B is a
nonzero Noetherian local domain. We prove it regular in every prime case.

If p∩Z=0, localization by the nonzero integers identifies B with
Q[v,v^{-1}]_q, where q is the prime extending p. This is either Q(v), if
q=0, or localization at an irreducible Laurent polynomial. In the latter
case the maximal ideal is principal and nonzero. Its dimension is1 by the
height theorem and the chain (0)⊊q; its embedding dimension is1 because a
nonzero generator a of a maximal ideal in a local domain cannot lie in
(a)^2: a=a²b would give1=ab with a a nonunit. Thus B is regular of dimension
0 or1. This argument avoids needing to identify primitive integer
contractions explicitly.

If p∩Z=(ell), then p/(ell) is a prime in F_ell[v,v^{-1}], a PID. It is
zero or generated by an irreducible Laurent polynomial f. In the first
case p=(ell), and the same principal-ideal argument makes B regular of
dimension1. In the second, choose a Laurent lift F of f. Then
p=(ell,F), and in B the maximal ideal m=(ell,F). The strict chain
(0)⊊(ell)B⊊m shows dim B≥2. The height theorem gives dim B≤2, because m
is itself minimal over the2-generated ideal m. Thus dim B=2.

The classes of ell,F form a basis of m/m². They generate; to prove
independence suppose a ell+b F∈m². Reduce modulo ell in the domain
B/(ell)=F_ell[v,v^{-1}]_(f). Then bbar f∈(f²), so b∈m. Hence a ell∈m².
We show ell∉m². Otherwise write
 ell=r ell²+s ell F+t F².
Reduction modulo ell in that domain gives tbar f²=0, so t=ell t′.
Cancel ell in the domain B to get
 1=r ell+s F+t′ F²∈m,
a contradiction. If a were a unit, a ell∈m² would force ell∈m²; hence
a∈m. Both coefficients lie in m, proving independence. Thus edim B=2,
so B is regular. This classifies all primes because the contraction of a
prime to Z is0 or a prime ideal.

Let R=completion of B at m. The cited completion theorem makes R nonzero
Noetherian local, complete and separated, with maximal ideal mR and residue
k_0. Regularity is preserved, and the cited regular-domain theorem makes
R a domain. Faithful flatness implies B→R injective: if b maps to0, the
injection bB→B remains injective after flat tensoring; its image is the
zero multiplication-by-b image, so bB⊗R=0. Faithfulness gives bB=0 and
b=0. Consequently A→R is injective and Q(v) embeds in F=FracR. The
composite A→R→k_0→k is precisely θ. A field prime p=0 is allowed; then
B=R=Q(v) and the topology is the zero-adic topology.

## 2. Finite projectives have constant generic and residue rank

Let (R,n,k_0) be any local ring and P a finite projective module. Choose
lifts of a basis of P/nP, of cardinality r. Nakayama applied to the finite
cokernel makes R^r→P surjective. Projectivity splits it, so
R^r=P⊕Q with Q finite. Reduction is still a direct sum. Since k_0^r→P/nP
is an isomorphism, Q/nQ=0; Nakayama gives Q=0. Therefore P is free of
rank r. If R is a domain with fraction field F, then
 dim_F(F⊗P)=rank_R P=dim_{k_0}(P/nP).
This proof supplies exactly the finite-projective/free assertion used
below, rather than citing a theorem about projectives of unspecified rank.

For an idempotent q acting R-linearly on a finite free module L,
L=qL⊕(1−q)L. Hence qL is finite projective, therefore finite free, and its
generic dimension equals its residue dimension. This holds in dimension2;
no DVR lattice theorem is being used.

## 3. Integral idempotent lifting in finite complete algebras

Let C be an associative finite R-algebra over a complete Noetherian local
ring (R,n), with central R-action. C is n-adically complete/separated by
the finite-module completion theorem. Let x_0 lift an idempotent of C/nC,
and put δ_i=x_i²−x_i and
 x_{i+1}=x_i+(1−2x_i)δ_i.
Since δ_i is a polynomial in x_i, all terms commute with x_i. A direct
integer-polynomial expansion gives
 δ_{i+1}=δ_i²(4δ_i−3).
Initially δ_0∈nC; induction gives δ_i∈n^{2^i}C and
x_{i+1}−x_i∈n^{2^i}C. Completeness gives a limit q congruent to x_0
modulo nC. Continuity of multiplication and separatedness give q²=q.
No inverse of2 or3 is used. A single lift suffices for each primitive
idempotent needed in reciprocity; orthogonality of a whole lifted family
is unnecessary. If desired, lift successive idempotents in complete
corners (1−q)C(1−q), which are direct R-module summands of C.

## 4. Exact projection, free layers, and integral split J-modules

For any right C-module X and an idempotent q∈C, the image Xq is the image
of an R-linear projection. The operation X↦Xq is exact: an epimorphism
X→Y lifts yq by xq from any lift x of y; kernel and injection statements
follow by the same projection. It commutes with every scalar extension
since the direct-sum decomposition X=Xq⊕X(1−q) is preserved.

Suppose a finite free C_R has a filtration I_i by right ideals with finite
free layers B_i=I_i/I_{i+1}. Exact projection gives
 0→I_{i+1}q→I_iq→B_iq→0.
Each B_iq is a direct summand of the finite free R-module B_i, so it is
finite free by §2. The displayed sequence therefore splits as R-modules,
and survives generic and residue scalar extension with its exact layer.
This justifies all filtration/base-change operations used in reciprocity.

Suppose separately that J_R is already proved isomorphic to
⊕_E M_{d_E}(R), by integral Z-lattices and matrix-coefficient orthogonality
with every f_E inverted. No stable-lattice theorem over the dimension2
ring R is required. If X is an R-finite-free J_R-module, its E-multiplicity
module Q_E=e^E_{11}X is finite projective over R (an idempotent image), hence
free. The map R^{d_E}⊗Q_E→e_EX sending standard-vector_i⊗x to e^E_{i1}x
is an isomorphism, with inverse given by matrix units. Thus the generic
and residue E-multiplicities of X are both rank_R Q_E. This proves
multiplicity preservation locally and explicitly.

## 5. Application to decomposition numbers and reciprocity

Use C_R=R⊗_A H, J_R split as above, and J standard modules L_E=R^{d_E}
restricted through φ. Their fraction modules are the split generic
Hecke E_F and their residues are *V_E. First prove H_{k_0} split by the
separate End_H(M)→k_0 argument in `local-basic-set-proof.md`; that argument
uses only the split J_{k_0}, homogeneous layers, and multiplicity-one
π:B_a⊗M→M, so it does not use decomposition numbers or completion.

For a simple M choose primitive e∈H_{k_0} with projective cover top M,
and lift e to q∈C_R by §3. As H_{k_0} is split, eM has dimension1 and
annihilates other simple labels under the exact idempotent functor. For
any R-free model L with generic representation E_F, §§2–4 give
 [k_0⊗L:M]=dim_{k_0} e(k_0⊗L)=rank_R qL=dim_F qE_F.
Thus free-model reduction multiplicities are independent. In particular
 d_{E,M}=[*V_E:M]
if d is defined by this construction. If a prior definition uses an A_p
free model, base change to R gives an R-free model, proving agreement.
Existence of an A_p free model must not be silently presumed if not
already established: the present J-standard construction itself provides
a complete definition and proof of independence among available free
models, without Du–Parshall–Scott as an unproved logical supplier.

Now filter C_Rq by I_iq. Each layer B_iq is finite free and a J_R-module.
By §4 its E-multiplicity is unchanged in F and k_0. The left H_F action
factors through φ_F, which is an isomorphism. The degree supplier says
only layer i=α_E can contain E. Additivity of generic composition
multiplicities along the filtration therefore gives
 mult_{J_{k_0}}(V_E,B_{i,k_0}e)=δ_{i,α_E} mult_{H_F}(E_F,H_Fq).
In the split regular bimodule, the last multiplicity is dim_F qE_F.
By the preceding rank identity this is d_{E,M}. Hence exact reciprocity:
 mult(V_E,B_{i,k_0}e)=δ_{i,α_E}d_{E,M}.
Only commutation of the left J and right H action is used; no right-φ or
right-φ′ identification is required.

For any field extension k/k_0, the already split finite-dimensional algebra
H_{k_0} has quotient by its radical a product of full matrix algebras.
The scalar-extended radical is nilpotent, and the quotient remains split
semisimple. Thus this is exactly the radical over k. Simple labels and
all composition multiplicities are preserved under extension (extend a
composition series). The arbitrary original θ therefore has the same
canonical basic-set theorem as its k_0 specialization. This field-extension
step is independent of whether θ(v) is algebraic or transcendental, a root
of unity or not, and independent of characteristic.
