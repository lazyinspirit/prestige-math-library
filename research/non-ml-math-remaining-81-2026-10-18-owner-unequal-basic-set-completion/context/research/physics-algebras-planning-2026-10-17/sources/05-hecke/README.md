# Hecke sources and reading log

Research cutoff: **2026-10-17**. PDFs and extracted text are stored beside
this file. PDF text companions were produced locally with PyMuPDF (`fitz`),
one `===== PDF PAGE n =====` block per page. Extraction is not evidence that
every page was read: the reading ranges below distinguish manual reading from
full-file extraction. The files are research material, not library suppliers
and not proof of local items.

## Downloaded but not relied on as read sources

These complete files were retrieved to support possible follow-up reading, but
the dossier does not use them as checked theorem suppliers. Some pages were
sampled during source triage; the proof ranges were not read closely enough to
support theorem-level claims. They must not be cited as though their proofs
were read: `brundan-kleshchev-degenerate-ariki-0901.0057.pdf/.txt` (44 pp; <https://arxiv.org/abs/0901.0057>), `hu-mathas-seminormal-klr-1304.0906.pdf/.txt` (51 pp; <https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.0906>), `klr-foundation-0803.4121.pdf/.txt` (53 pp; <https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.4121>), and `rouquier-2-kac-moody-0812.5023.pdf/.txt` (66 pp; <https://arxiv.org/abs/0812.5023>). Their relevant claims are sourced through the locally published carriers and the fully read survey/articles listed above.

The attempted primary-source fetch of Ariki’s 1996 article, “On the decomposition
numbers of the Hecke algebra of G(m,1,n),” did **not** produce a PDF. The local
`ariki-1996.pdf` is a 1.2 KB Incapsula HTML access-denial page, not an article;
no text was extracted and no part of the primary paper was read. Citation:
*J. Math. Kyoto Univ.* 36 (1996), 789–808. Its theorem is treated via the
independent full-text sources above, especially Brundan–Kleshchev and Kleshchev.

The contemporary search was selective, not a census of every Hecke, cell,
Soergel, DAHA, or Cherednik publication through 2026-10-17. Do not infer that
unread sources are unavailable or that every conjecture mentioned historically
remains open today.
