### `lem-formal-immersion-homotopies-extend-over-a-collar` — false-claim, fatal

Location: Statement; Proof 1.1–4.1. Assigned consumer: `prop-smale-hirsch-makes-rank-reduction-sufficient-for-euclidean-immersion-in-positive-codimension`.

The proof identifies Imm(X×I,N) with paths in Imm(X,N). A smooth family is only slice-wise immersive; its derivative in the I direction need not be independent of the X derivatives. Take X=[0,1], N=R: Imm(X,R) is nonempty, but Imm(X×I,R) is empty by dimensions, so restriction cannot be a weak equivalence. Even for X a point and N=R, the fibre of Imm(I,R)→R has increasing and decreasing components. The opened def-compact-parameter-pair and smoothing lemma supply families, not immersions of the full product. Replace this with a collar extension of unchanged source dimension and prove the actual restriction homotopy equivalence; adding a target-dimension bound alone does not make the fibres contractible.
