## Auxiliary arguments to include explicitly

Work in ZFC. Write H_(theta+) for sets with root-inclusive transitive closure of size at most infinite cardinal theta.

1. If M is transitive, contains all ordinals, models ZFC and is closed under ambient mu-sequences, every ambient set of at most mu elements of M belongs to M: enumerate it by an ordinal at most mu, pad to length mu, and restrict internally. Also H_(mu+) is contained in M. For x in that class, enumerate TC({x}) by an ordinal beta<=mu and code membership by a subset of beta×beta. The relation belongs to M by sequence closure and cardinal-square coding. M regards it as well-founded/extensional because it really is; its set Mostowski collapse, computed in M, agrees with the external one by uniqueness, and the distinguished root is x. The enumeration and coding are actual sets, and AC is explicit.

2. Let j:V→M have critical point kappa and theta-sequence closure, j(kappa)>theta. Derive the normal fine measure U on I=P_kappa(theta) using s=j``theta. Let j0:V→M0 be its normal fine ultrapower. Define k:M0→M by k(pi_U([f]))=j(f)(s). Equality and every fixed formula are preserved and reflected: by Los the coordinate truth set A belongs to U exactly when s belongs to j(A), and elementarity identifies the latter with the formula at the evaluated functions. Thus k is a well-defined elementary injection and k j0=j. The normal seed s0=j0``theta maps to s.

For every alpha<=theta, s0∩j0(alpha)=j0``alpha has order type alpha. Elementarity for the definable ordinal order-type operation therefore gives k(alpha)=otp(s∩j(alpha))=alpha. In particular k(kappa)=kappa; this is the needed critical-point bound, not merely that k fixes ordinals below kappa. M0 is theta-sequence closed by the existing closed-embedding theorem, so argument1 puts H_(theta+) inside M0. For any y in this hereditary class, M0 contains an enumeration e:beta→y with beta<=theta, by closure. Then k(y)=k(e)``beta=k``y. Well-founded induction on TC({y}) consequently proves k(y)=y, including arbitrary anticipated objects of hereditary size theta, not only ordinals.

3. Fix ell:kappa→V_kappa, and choose mu at least the cardinality of P(P_kappa(theta)), with theta>=kappa. Suppose j:V→M is mu-supercompact and j(kappa)>mu. Then V and M have the same normal fine kappa-complete measures on P_kappa(eta), for every cardinal eta between kappa and theta. Indeed the index set, all its subsets, and all subsets of its power set belong to M by argument1; all relevant functions/sequences for completeness and normality belong to M as well. Cardinal comparisons up to theta and H_(eta+) agree by the same hereditary coding argument.

The internal and external value j_U(ell)(kappa) also agrees. At index x, kappa is represented by otp(x∩kappa), as seen from the normal seed identity; this value is <kappa on every coordinate. Therefore j_U(ell)(kappa) is the collapse of the class of r(x)=ell(otp(x∩kappa)). Let T be TC({ell})∪kappa∪{empty}, a transitive set of cardinality at most kappa. This bound follows from the inaccessible size lemma and a kappa-indexed union of sets of size at most kappa. All functions I→T belong to M by mu-closure; their collection also belongs to M, since its size is at most kappa^|I|<=2^|I|<=mu. Form the set quotient of this collection by coordinate U-equivalence, using ordinary equivalence classes within this set rather than internal Scott rank codes. Its predecessor relation and all predecessor classes of [r] are identical in M and V: patch any predecessor function into T with empty fallback. This relation is well-founded and extensional, with a transitive collapse. Uniqueness of the set collapse identifies the two values. This avoids assuming that internal least-rank Scott representative sets are literally the same objects. All anticipated x in H_(eta+) themselves belong to M, so the full anticipation predicate is absolute for these parameters.

## Bounded construction and contradiction

Supercompact kappa is measurable by the embedding characterization and hence inaccessible. Fix a set well-order W of V_kappa using AC. Recursively define ell:kappa→V_kappa as follows. At a regular uncountable gamma<kappa, look among cardinals gamma<=eta<kappa and x in V_kappa∩H_(eta+) for failure of anticipation by every normal fine gamma-complete measure on P_gamma(eta), applied to the already defined ell|gamma. If such a pair exists, take the least eta and then the W-least x; otherwise put ell(gamma)=empty. At other gamma put empty. This is a first-order set rule: ultrapower evaluation is expressed by the definable collapse, not by quantifying over elementary class embeddings. The candidate range is the set V_kappa, and transfinite recursion applies. No unsupported bound on an unbounded least failure at stages below kappa is necessary.

For any critical-kappa embedding, j fixes V_kappa pointwise. Prove this by induction: each y in V_kappa has size<kappa, an enumeration e:beta→y with beta<kappa gives j(y)=j(e)``beta=j``y, and the inductive hypothesis fixes all its members. Hence j(ell)|kappa=ell.

Suppose ell fails to anticipate some set with the requested closure. Increasing the cardinal to dominate the hereditary size of that set gives failure for a normal fine ultrapower (which would otherwise be a valid requested embedding). Choose the least cardinal theta>=kappa with some x in H_(theta+) not anticipated by any normal fine measure on P_kappa(theta). Choose mu>=|P(P_kappa(theta))| and a mu-supercompact embedding j with j(kappa)>mu. Argument3 shows M computes exactly the same least failure theta. Every candidate x in H_(theta+) belongs to V_(j(kappa))^M: internally its transitive closure has size<=theta<j(kappa); well-founded induction on that closure bounds all ranks below the regular j(kappa). Thus the cutoff in the j-transformed recursion excludes none of these failures. The rule at kappa selects some such failing a, so j(ell)(kappa)=a. The well-order j(W) may select a different failure from an externally named witness; this does not matter.

Derive the theta-measure from j and factor through j0 as in argument2. Since k fixes kappa and a, k(j0(ell)(kappa))=j(ell)(kappa)=a=k(a). Injectivity gives j0(ell)(kappa)=a, contradicting its failure of anticipation. Therefore ell anticipates every x at every theta dominating its hereditary size. Given arbitrary requested lambda>=kappa, choose theta>=lambda and |TC({x})|; the theta-closed normal ultrapower is also lambda-closed and moves kappa above lambda. This proves exactly the target Laver definition, including empty x and lambda=kappa.
