## Mathematical route and source comparison

The power convention is X_{nu,rho}(K) = {|K|^nu w : ||w|| <= rho}, equivalently x = (K*K)^(nu/2)w. The compact SVD places these source elements in N(K) perpendicular; the local lemma proves their exact-data minimum-norm property. The interpolation lemma expands in the positive singular system and proves its finite weighted scalar inequality locally. The calculus dependencies that establish monotonicity of positive real powers are explicit. The minimax argument uses the indistinguishable datum 0 for truths x and -x, and proves the factor-two two-truth convention directly. The modulus theorem covers zero operator and finite rank separately; only infinite rank gives a null sequence of equality radii.

For the gap example gamma_j = j(j-1)/2, the proof splits coordinates at j, obtains a normalized modulus bound by sqrt(r_j + r_j^nu) with r_j = exp(-j) -> 0, and proves delta-star_j -> 0. This establishes the stated little-o result and rules out a uniform positive matching lower constant for all sufficiently small radii.
