### ML-67 — Mirror descent and adaptive regularization

**Pages:** mirror-descent-and-adaptive-regularization / mirror-descent-and-adaptive-regularization-examples. **Scope:** On a nonempty bounded closed convex decision set $K$, define a twice-differentiable strongly convex regularizer $R$ with positive-definite Hessian on the relevant interior, $B_R(x\|y)=R(x)-R(y)-\nabla R(y)^\top(x-y)$, and, at interior $z$, $\|v\|_z^2=v^\top\nabla^2R(z)v$ and $\|h\|_{*,z}^2=h^\top(\nabla^2R(z))^{-1}h$. Take $\|g_t\|_{*,t}$ at the intermediate Hessian point on the segment from $x_t$ to $y_{t+1}$. Require every dual-gradient update and Bregman projection to be well-defined. For agile OMD state $\nabla R(y_{t+1})=\nabla R(x_t)-\eta g_t$ and $x_{t+1}\in\arg\min_{x\in K}B_R(x\|y_{t+1})$, with $g_t=\nabla f_t(x_t)$. For convex losses state Hazan's bound $\mathrm{Regret}_T(u)\le\frac{\eta}{4}\sum_{t=1}^T\|g_t\|_{*,t}^2+\frac{R(u)-R(x_1)}{2\eta}$; if $D_R^2=\max_{x,y\in K}(R(x)-R(y))$ and $\|g_t\|_{*,t}\le G_R$, the choice $\eta=D_R/(G_R\sqrt T)$ gives $D_RG_R\sqrt T$. Show locally that $R(x)=\frac12\|x-x_0\|_2^2$ yields projected OGD and that negative entropy on the simplex yields the normalized multiplicative Hedge update; prove any entropy-diameter $\log n$ claim rather than importing Hazan's exercise. Give AdaGrad's algorithmic updates only: $G_t=G_{t-1}+g_tg_t^\top$, choose the full-matrix scale $H_t=G_t^{1/2}$ or a diagonal scale $H_t=\operatorname{diag}(\sqrt{(G_t)_{ii}})$, set $y_{t+1}=x_t-\eta H_t^\dagger g_t$, and choose $x_{t+1}\in\arg\min_{x\in K}\|y_{t+1}-x\|_{H_t}^2$, where $H_t^\dagger$ is the Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse and a minimizer is selected if nonunique. The source prints its diagonal scale as $\operatorname{diag}(G_t^{1/2})$; resolve the notation by proving the coordinatewise diagonal update locally. Do not state AdaGrad's regret rate unless the omitted optimizer calculation is proved locally. Proposed interfaces: `def-mirror-map-and-bregman-divergence`, `alg-online-mirror-descent`, `thm-mirror-descent-regret`, `alg-adagrad`. **Proof route:** derive the Bregman three-point identity and projection Pythagorean inequality from their definitions; combine convexity, local dual-norm Cauchy–Schwarz, nonnegativity, and telescoping. Verify the entropy normalization and diameter separately on the simplex; do not rely on the book's exercises. For AdaGrad, prove the matrix optimizer identity and changing-metric telescoping bounds before adding any theorem. **Examples:** entropy on the simplex, Euclidean OGD, a singular adaptive metric. **Requires:** ML-65 — Online protocols and regret benchmarks; ML-66 — Hedge and online gradient descent; `def-convex-and-strictly-convex-functions-on-euclidean-sets`, `def-subgradient-and-subdifferential`, and `thm-differentiable-convex-functions-and-gradient-inequality`. **Sources:** Hazan PDF pp. 93–122 (printed pp. 71–100): regularizer/Bregman definitions and RFTL, pp. 93–99; OMD Algorithm 14, Theorem 5.6 and displayed proof, pp. 100–103; Euclidean/entropy reductions, pp. 103–105; AdaGrad Algorithm 19 and Theorem 5.12 proof, pp. 113–118; optimizer Proposition 5.16 and related derivations deferred to Exercise 11, pp. 117–121.
