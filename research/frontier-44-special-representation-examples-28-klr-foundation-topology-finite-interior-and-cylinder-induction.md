## Descending mapping-cylinder gluing

Fix the resulting interior triangulation `g_n:J_n→W`. Its image is the disjoint union of all stratum cores. Descend in `i`. The invariant is that the image of `g_i` is this union plus `W\T_(i−1)(d)`, all stratum portions and future lower level sets are subpolyhedra, and the projection from every future lower level to its current base portion is PL. Only these future projections need be retained; no claim is made that every old higher projection becomes a PL map on the completed space.

For a stratum `X` of dimension `i−1`, let `A_X=g_i^{-1}(S_X(d))` and `B_X=g_i^{-1}(X)`. The base portion is precisely `X_d^0`: the smaller-dimensional frontier tubes have not yet been filled. The normal level portion is correspondingly truncated over this base. It is compact. Its projection `f_X:A_X→B_X` is PL by the invariant. Finite subdivision makes it simplicial: subdivide the finitely many source polytopes on which it is affine and their inverse images of target simplices, then cone the resulting common face subdivisions. Mark all intersections with future lower levels and stratum closures. One further barycentric subdivision makes all these marked subcomplexes full.

Use the actual mapping-cylinder helper for `f_X`, allowing simplex vertex identifications. Its finite domain and explicit homeomorphism have already been constructed with the threshold inverse. Map its topological cylinder to `W` by `(q,t)↦R_X(td)g_i(q)` for `t>0`, with the zero end mapped to `pi_X g_i(q)`. The radial lemma supplies continuity at that zero end and the homeomorphism onto the closed `X` collar over `X_d^0`. The new collar meets the old image only in its outer level `A_X` and base `B_X`: every old higher collar began at a core with `rho_X≥d`, and its radial transports preserve `rho_X`. Therefore no old point has `0<rho_X<d` over this base. Two distinct same-dimensional `X` collars are disjoint because their tubes are incomparable. Thus gluing along the two boundary copies is injective. The domain is finite compact, the target `W` is Hausdorff, and the radial inverse proves surjectivity onto exactly the newly filled collar. The map onto the new image is consequently a homeomorphism.

For a future lower stratum `Y<X`, `rho_Y` and `pi_Y` factor through `pi_X` on the `X` collar. Hence the part of `S_Y(d)` inside this new cylinder is exactly the cylinder over the corresponding marked source/base subcomplex pair. The mapping-cylinder helper makes it a subpolyhedron. Its projection to the future `Y` base is the cylinder retraction followed by the existing base projection `pi_Y|X`; both are PL. This proves the descending invariant. Stratum-closure intersections are retained by the same subcylinder compatibility: the radial maps preserve strata, so an upper stratum or its closure contributes its marked top subset together with the appropriate zero-end base subset. No dimension used here is a complex perversity dimension.

After all `i` have been processed the image is `W`, and all strata and prescribed unions of strata are unions of open simplices. The finite simplicial cylinder maps are smooth inside positive-height cells, and radial transport is smooth on each smooth stratum.
