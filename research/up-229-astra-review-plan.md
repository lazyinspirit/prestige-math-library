# Independent audit of 229 published U-P items

## Owner direction — 2026-09-23

Freeze the 229 active U-P published items from the canonical
`research/published-consumer-supplier-ledger.md` classification index. Ten
concurrent `gpt-6-astra` reviewers at medium reasoning effort each audit a
disjoint ordered shard, one item at a time. Their decisions are `accept`
(sound as used), `repair` (necessary and mathematically secure local edit), or
`defer` (specific unresolved defect or uncertainty, retained in U-P). Source
authority, prior approvals, and old publication stamps do not establish
logical validity. Reviewers must consult the published library and
authoritative sources whenever their own understanding is uncertain, stating
what they actually read.

For any change to the original mathematical claim, including an item's
Statement, Definition, Example, Counterexample, or refuted claim, the
responsible reviewer traces the complete published direct and indirect
consumer closure. It reads exact uses, repairs a consumer only when the use
requires it, and records sound unaffected boundaries. A changed consumer
claim starts another complete closure trace. Cross-shard and outside-shard
edits are coordinated by the root so that two writers never edit one item.
No blanket U-P propagation follows from a mere reference.

Reviewers may propose and fully author a genuinely necessary definition or
lemma after the root reserves its unique ID and page. They must satisfy the
repository's registry, page, proof, source, and focused validation rules.
If a substantial prerequisite cannot be proved and integrated soundly,
the item remains U-P and an owner-escalation event states the exact missing
claim, consuming proof step, attempted sources, and viable repair route.

The root freezes IDs, hashes and original ledger reasons, launches and
monitors the ten reviewers, routes ownership events, independently checks
repair and impact evidence, and reconciles final decisions into the single
canonical ledger. Acceptances become bounded-clear receipts outside the
active defect classes; fully repaired items become A-R; deferred items stay
U-P. This review does not run an autopilot transition or manufacture an
independent judge verdict or publication stamp.

## Completion checks

1. Verify the frozen 229 IDs are published and assigned exactly once across
   ten ordered shards.
2. Require an item-specific receipt for every ID, and a complete published
   consumer-impact record for every changed original claim. Resolve or
   escalate every cross-shard and outside-shard finding.
3. Run focused precheck/rendercheck for edits, verify ledger uniqueness and
   counts, and report remaining U-P and owner escalations with exact evidence.

## Completed audit — 2026-09-23

All ten `gpt-6-astra` medium reviewers filed an ordered item-specific receipt
for every one of the 229 frozen published U-P items. Final decisions were 24
bounded acceptances, 27 approved repairs, and 178 exact-obligation deferrals.
Six outside-frozen published proof/premise repairs were recorded by the root;
four outside-frozen published items with unresolved prerequisites were added
to U-P. The canonical classification index now has U-P 182, U-C 0, A-R 539,
and A-P 319, with 3,260 unique classified/cleared rows and no duplicate ID.

All 33 approved repaired item files passed focused precheck and rendercheck;
`git diff --check` passed. Every changed original claim has a published
direct/indirect consumer impact file, with affected uses repaired or explicitly
left in U-P. The owner-facing exact-item obligations are in
`research/up-229-astra-review/owner-escalations.md`, with full evidence in
the shard receipts and event files. No autopilot transition or new judge stamp
was performed.
