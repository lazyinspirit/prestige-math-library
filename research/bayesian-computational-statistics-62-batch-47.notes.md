## Axiom, support, and finite-case notes

AC is carried through the actual path-law, RN derivative, and general MH
suppliers; finite index sums, categorical probabilities, and the displayed
finite counterexamples are choice-free. The backward-simulation variant is
restricted to measurable transition densities and positive finite backward
denominators. The genealogical particle Gibbs route uses the kernel-RN support
conditions and does not pretend those densities exist in its general-kernel
form.

The MCWM counterexample uses strictly positive unbiased estimates, so no
zero-denominator convention is hidden in its calculation. The support
counterexample does not assert that every arbitrary completion on a
posterior-null state has a particular stationary law; it shows directly that
the required target/proposal RN derivative does not exist and the stated exact
conditional-particle-filter construction cannot be formed on full posterior
support.
