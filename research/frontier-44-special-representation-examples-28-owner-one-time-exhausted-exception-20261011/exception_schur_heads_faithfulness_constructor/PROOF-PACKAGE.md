## 1. The actual detector and standard socles

Tensor by V preserves projectives because tensor by V* is its exact adjoint; it preserves injectives because the same adjunction works in the other direction. Restricted contravariant duality commutes with tensor by V (this is transposition duality of weight spaces, not the ordinary contragredient isomorphism V*=V). Thus T=P_c tensor V^tensor d is projective, injective and self-dual.

Every Std simple is a quotient of T: induct on d using C1. A quotient T_(d-1)→L(B) yields a quotient f_i T_(d-1)→f_i L(B)→L(A), and f_i T_(d-1) is a summand of T_d. Conversely, if L(A) is a quotient of T, its cover P(A) is a summand: lift T→L(A) to T→P(A) by projectivity of T; essentiality of the projective-cover epimorphism makes the lift surjective, and projectivity of P(A) splits it. No inference that every indecomposable summand of a self-dual object is individually self-dual is needed. All that the construction below needs is add(T) stable under restricted duality, which follows directly from T self-dual.

Every standard embeds into an iterated e_i image of a Std simple. In source17 Cor4.4 choose column shifts k_j so that entries of column j of shifted B are never ≤ entries in earlier columns. B is minimal in Bruhat order, N(B) is simple and Std. Lower column j entries individually from smallest entry to largest by e_{a_{j,t}} e_{a_{j,t}+1} ... e_{a_{j,t}+k_j-1}, for j=2,...,l. The standard-flag formula places N(A) at the bottom because the flag can be reordered by Bruhat order using Ext^1(N(C),N(D))=0 unless C<D. Thus there is an actual monomorphism N(A)→e_{i_1}...e_{i_r}L(B), not merely a character occurrence. If L(A) embeds in N(C), adjunction yields a nonzero map f_{i_r}...f_{i_1}L(A)→L(B). C1 stability of the non-Std Serre subcategory forces A to be Std.

Consequently the socle of EVERY standard is Std. For any standard-filtered X, a simple submodule intersects the first nonzero filtration section injectively, hence is a submodule of a standard and has a Std label. In particular this holds for projectives and T. Duality fixes each simple and sends a standard flag to a costandard flag; self-duality of T therefore makes its head Std too. Now every projective summand of T has Std head. Conversely each Std P(A) occurs by the quotient argument. Every such P(A) is injective because it is a summand of T. For a Std label A, its injective hull is D(P(A)), because the simple-preserving restricted duality interchanges projective covers and injective hulls. Hence I(A) belongs to add(D(T))=add(T). This is sufficient for every copresentation and unit argument below. It does not require the stronger identification I(A)=P(A), or an unproved inference that a self-dual direct sum has individually self-dual summands.

For every nonzero submodule X⊆T its socle is nonzero (finite length) and is Std. Exact Wh then gives Wh(X)≠0. It is FALSE that all composition factors of T are Std: source17 Lemma4.2 says every Col label occurs as a composition factor. This is the exact correction needed in endomorphism F2 and step1.2.

## 2. Concrete all-projective copresentations

Write Z_A=⊗_boxes Sym^{a_i}(V_col(i)), where V_j spans columns1,...,j, and twist by c. In characteristic zero the averaging idempotent embeds each symmetric power in the corresponding tensor power. Therefore Z_A⊆V^tensor d as an actual p-submodule. PBW makes U(g) free as a right U(p)-module, so induction preserves this injection and its quotient. Tensor identity identifies the induced ambient module with T. Thus Z(A)⊆T and

T/Z(A)=Ind_p^g(C_-rho tensor (V_c^tensor d/Z_A)).

The finite p-module quotient is Levi-semisimple and has a finite p-composition filtration: its sections are simple polynomial Levi modules V(B), with B in the degree-d Col set. The positive p-radical kills every finite-dimensional simple section (its nilpotent action and Levi stability give the usual invariant-vector proof). Inducing the filtration exactly gives a standard flag of T/Z(A).

By C2 every indecomposable projective P is a direct summand of some Z(A). Write Z(A)=P⊕Q and regard P as its actual embedded summand in T. Then 0→Q→T/P→T/Z(A)→0 makes T/P standard-filtered because Q is projective and has its BGG standard flag. Section1 gives Std socle of T/P. Its finite injective hull I(T/P) is a sum of the injective hulls of its Std socle simples, all in add(T). Compose T→T/P with T/P→I(T/P). The resulting

0→P→T→I(T/P)

is exact at P and T. This is the required two-step copresentation with BOTH terms in add(T); it is not a short exact sequence with surjective last arrow. Finite direct sums supply the same conclusion for every projective. The old text about 'terms in add(T) and its injective hull' is inadequate unless the hull membership is proved exactly as above.

## 3. Elementary corner proof of all-projective and tilting full faithfulness

Pass to a finite summand algebra A=End(G)^op using the direct sum G of all projective covers; do not replace G by T. Choose e cutting one copy of every Std projective-injective. Multiplicities in T only change the corner by ordinary Morita equivalence, so first work with F(X)=eX and B=eAe. The exact functor F has right adjoint G_e(M)=Hom_B(eA,M). Its unit is η_X(x)(ea)=eax. The restricted unit eη_X is the natural isomorphism eX→Hom_B(eAe,eX). Thus kerη_X and cokerη_X are both killed by e.

If socle X is Std, kerη_X=0, since any nonzero kernel has a simple submodule supported by e. In particular η_T is monic. Since the SOURCE T is injective, η_T splits. Write G_eFT=T⊕C. Then eC=0, and adjunction gives Hom_A(C,G_eFT)=Hom_B(eC,FT)=0. The inclusion of C forces C=0. Hence η is an isomorphism on add(T). Apply the exact F and left-exact G_e to the copresentation in section2. The two right vertical units are isomorphisms, so the kernel comparison proves η_P is an isomorphism for every projective P. Adjunction now gives Hom_A(X,P)=Hom_B(FX,FP) for every X and every projective target P; in particular F is fully faithful on all projectives. The same holds for Wh once its genuine Morita comparison is established in section4.

There is a separate short proof for tiltings. If head X and socle Y are Std, η_Y is monic and its cokernel C is killed by e. Any nonzero map X→C would have a nonzero image, a quotient of X whose simple head is Std; C has only non-Std composition factors, a contradiction. Adjunction sends any B-map FX→FY to X→G_eFY. Its composite with C vanishes and hence it factors uniquely through Y. This proves full faithfulness on maps from Std-head objects to Std-socle objects. Standard-filtered tiltings have Std socle by section1; costandard flags give Std head by restricted duality. Hence full faithfulness on tiltings is proved without asserting that arbitrary quotienting preserves head or socle.

## 4. Genuine finite-W projective covers and the natural comparison

Put Q(A)=Wh(P(A)) for Std. The ground Q is projective by W1. For A=f_iB, P(A) is a summand of P(B) tensor V, as follows by lifting the crystal-head quotient and splitting the cover. Wh(P(B) tensor V)=Q(B) circledast V. This object is projective because its right adjoint circledast V* is exact on the entire rational finite-W category. That category is the union of determinant-shifted polynomial summands, not the assertion that V alone generates every rational object.

To prove Q(A) has exactly the required head, write P(B) tensor V=⊕_C P(C)^m_C, with Std C by section1. Exactness yields dim Hom_W(Q(C),L_W(D))≥δ_CD. On the other hand adjunction, exactness, the induction hypothesis and W1 give

dim Hom_W(Q(B) circledast V,L_W(D))=[L_W(D) circledast V*:L_W(B)]
=[L(D) tensor V*:L(B)]=dim Hom_g(P(B) tensor V,L(D))=m_D.

Comparing this with the sum ∑_C m_C dim Hom_W(Q(C),L_W(D)) forces equality δ_CD for every C with m_C>0. Thus each Q(A) is projective with simple head L_W(A), hence its projective cover; no module isomorphism is inferred merely from equal characters. Therefore U=Wh(T) is a direct sum of all these covers with positive multiplicity, so is a genuine finite-W progenerator. This is the proof of the Morita hypothesis; double centralizer alone is insufficient.

For each X define γ_X(f)=Wh(f) composed with the fixed inverse U≅Wh(T). Both Hom_g(T,-) and Hom_W(U,Wh(-)) are exact. Check on every simple L(B): for B non-Std both sides vanish; for B Std the summand calculations above give matching multiplicities, and on each one-dimensional Hom(P(A),L(B)) the nonzero quotient map stays nonzero under exact Wh. Hence γ is an isomorphism on simples. The short-exact-sequence diagram and induction on finite length prove it for all X. This is source17 Lemma5.11, including its actual map, not an inference of a natural transformation from a Morita equivalence. Evaluating γ at X=T gives the full endomorphism comparison. The independently proved socle detection gives its injectivity directly as well.

The exact functor Hom(T,-) is the idempotent Serre quotient. Its kernel consists exactly of modules with no Std composition factors. The elementary idempotent construction and its right adjoint give the quotient category equivalence with B-mod. Through γ and the genuine finite-W Morita equivalence this identifies Wh's Serre quotient as claimed.

## 5. Simple images and genuine simple heads

Define D(A)=πL(A). The preceding exact quotient or the finite-W Morita equivalence says D(A) is simple for Std, zero otherwise, and nonzero D(A) form the distinct complete Hecke-image simple list. To prove the head of S(A)=πN(A) for Std, lift the standard's quotient N(A)→L(A) through P(A)→L(A). Essentiality makes P(A)→N(A) surjective. Since Std P(A) is in add(T), Y(A)=πP(A) is an indecomposable projective B-module with simple head D(A). Exact π gives a surjection Y(A)→S(A) and a nonzero quotient S(A)→D(A). Every nonzero quotient of the indecomposable projective Y(A) has head D(A): its unique maximal submodule contains the kernel. Hence head S(A)=D(A). This proves source17 Theorem6.15 without falsely saying exact quotients always preserve heads. Likewise Y(A) is the genuine cover of D(A), and left-adjoint evaluation recovers P(A) on add(T). For off-Std projectives no projective-image claim is inferred. Composition multiplicities follow from exactness and the simple-image list.

## 6. Faithful central corner under ground-row deletion

Use D1 with large pyramid λ and small pyramid barλ. A label is contractible if each deleted row i has entries c_j+1-i. If A contractible and θ(A)=θ(B), the minimum entry in each charge coset in its bottom row is c_j+1-n. Column strictness and the admissible-origin inequalities force those entries into the same bottom row of B. Remove that row and repeat. Thus B is contractible. This proves that contractible simples are a union of entire W central-character blocks, not merely a collection of simple labels. Let f be their central support in W_big.

For a contractible Std A, its highest vector belongs to eV(A); it survives in the simple quotient L_W(A). Thus eL_W(A)≠0 and is a quotient of V(barA). It is simple over eWe: if 0≠v∈eL, then L=Wv and eL=eWe v, so each nonzero vector generates. Uniqueness of the highest simple quotient gives eL_W(A)=L_W(barA). For noncontractible A, eV(A)=0 gives eL_W(A)=0 by exactness of e(-), not by an arbitrary head-preservation claim.

Now e is FULL in fW_big f. Otherwise the nonzero finite-dimensional quotient fW_big f/(fW_big eW_big f) has a simple module killed by e, contrary to survival of every simple in the f-blocks. This explicitly supplies fullness. Write f=∑_k a_k e b_k. Standard multiplication maps W_fe⊗_(eWe)eM→M and eW_f⊗_(W_f)M→eM are inverse equivalences, with surjectivity and inverse formulas provided by the displayed expression for f. Thus e(-) is a faithful Morita equivalence on ALL supported-block modules, not only a simple-label bijection.

Since U_big is a progenerator by section4, fU_big is a progenerator of fW_big f; its Morita image eU_big=U_small is a progenerator of W_small. In the mutual-centralizer algebra B_big=End_W(U_big)^op the operator f is also a central idempotent: it lies in W and commutes with W, hence lies in B, and commutes with B because B commutes with W. Therefore

fB_big f=End_(fWf)(fU_big)^op=End_(eWe)(eU_big)^op=B_small.

This proves actual faithful supporting-block Morita and the central Hecke corner. Enlarging by r FULL rows increases q_j and n by the same r, so each root a_j=c_j+q_j-n stays fixed. In the enlarged stable range B_big=H_d(a), giving B_small=fH_d(a)f. e is a W row-weight idempotent selecting tensors using retained rows (ZERO occupancy of removed rows), not an idempotent 'selecting the added ground rows'. Individual residue idempotents e(i) are not central; admissible central-block support f is what makes this identification two-sided.
