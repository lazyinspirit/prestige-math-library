2.13 Every standard $N(C)$ embeds in an iterated lowering translate of a standard simple. At each step the distinguished change in column $j$ is valid: its smaller entries already have their original values, while its larger entries and the later columns remain above the required range. Every other valid change occurs in an earlier column and gives a strictly smaller label in the prefix Bruhat order. The distinguished standard is therefore the largest section of the multiplicity-free residue standard flag. Move it to the bottom by splitting adjacent extensions.
