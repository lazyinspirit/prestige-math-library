## Statement

Over any commutative base ring $A$ with invertible nondegenerate parameter,
use the defining relations of
[[def-type-a-affine-presentations-and-charged-cyclotomic-quotients]], writing
$q_H$ for the nondegenerate Hecke parameter. For a monic cyclotomic polynomial
$f$ of degree $\ell>0$, with unit constant term in the nondegenerate branch,
the quotient by $f(X_1)=0$ is spanned by
$$X_1^{a_1}\cdots X_d^{a_d}T_w,\qquad 0\le a_r<\ell,\ w\in S_d,$$
and the degenerate quotient by $f(x_1)=0$ is spanned by
$$x_1^{a_1}\cdots x_d^{a_d}w,\qquad 0\le a_r<\ell,\ w\in S_d .$$
In particular, over a field $F$ each quotient has dimension at most
$\ell^dd!$. This applies to $f(t)=\prod_{i\in I}(t-q_H^i)^{\lambda_i}$ and
$f(t)=\prod_{i\in I}(t-i)^{\lambda_i}$ in the two finite-quantum-characteristic
branches, with $\ell=\sum_i\lambda_i$. No independence and no separate relation
$f(X_r)=0$ or $f(x_r)=0$ for $r>1$ is asserted. If $\ell=0$ and $d>0$, the
imposed relation $1=0$ gives the zero algebra with empty spanning family; for
$d=0$ the algebra is $A$ with the singleton empty monomial.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The two presentations of
[[def-type-a-affine-presentations-and-charged-cyclotomic-quotients]] over $A$,
a monic cyclotomic polynomial $f$ of degree $\ell>0$, and the quotients of the
Statement.

[F1] In the nondegenerate presentation the mixed relation is
$T_rX_rT_r=q_HX_{r+1}$ with $q_H$ invertible, $T_r^2=(q_H-1)T_r+q_H$, the
distant and braid relations hold, and $T_r$ is invertible with
$T_r^{-1}=q_H^{-1}(T_r-q_H+1)$; in the degenerate presentation
$s_rx_r=x_{r+1}s_r-1$, $s_rx_{r+1}=x_rs_r+1$, $s_rx_t=x_ts_r$ for
$t\notin\{r,r+1\}$, and the Coxeter relations hold
([[def-type-a-affine-presentations-and-charged-cyclotomic-quotients]],
[[def-generic-type-a-hecke-algebra]]).

[F2] The $T_r$ generate a quotient of the generic type-A Hecke algebra with
standard basis $\{T_w:w\in S_d\}$, and the $s_r$ generate the quotient of
$A[S_d]$ with basis $S_d$; finite straightening in the Coxeter group reduces
each word in the $T_r$ (respectively $s_r$) to a linear combination of the
standard words ([[thm-standard-basis-of-the-generic-type-a-hecke-algebra]],
[[thm-the-symmetric-group-has-the-coxeter-presentation]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

**Given:** The two presentations over $A$, the cyclotomic quotient by

$f(X_1)=0$ (nondegenerate) or $f(x_1)=0$ (degenerate), and the data of

[F1]–[F2].

1.1 Elementary straightening of polynomials past a simple generator. In the nondegenerate branch the mixed relation gives, for a polynomial $p$ in the commuting variables, $T_rp=(s_rp)T_r-(q_H-1)X_{r+1}\frac{p-s_rp}{X_r-X_{r+1}},$ and in the degenerate branch $s_rp=(s_rp)s_r-\frac{p-s_rp}{x_r-x_{r+1}} .$ Both divided differences are polynomials: for $p=X_r^m$ the first identity is $T_rX_r^m=X_{r+1}^mT_r-(q_H-1)(X_r^{m-1}+X_r^{m-2}X_{r+1}+\dots+X_{r+1}^{m-1})X_{r+1}$, which follows from [F1] by induction on $m$, and the second is $s_rx_r^m=x_{r+1}^ms_r-\sum_{k=0}^{m-1}x_r^kx_{r+1}^{m-1-k}$, again by induction; the general case follows from the divided-difference product rule for the two variable replacements $X_r\leftrightarrow X_{r+1}$ and $x_r\leftrightarrow x_{r+1}$. Consequently any word in the generators and polynomial variables rewrites as an $A$-linear combination of ordered monomials $X^aT_w$ in the nondegenerate branch (the $T$-word reduced by [F2], polynomial factors collected by commutation) and of $x^aw$ in the degenerate branch. The nondegenerate straightening preserves total polynomial degree; the degenerate one lowers degree by one in the correction term. [F1, F2, algebra]

2.1 The nondegenerate quotient is generated by positive powers. In the nondegenerate branch the relation $f(X_1)=0$ has nonzero constant term $a_0$, so $X_1^{-1}=-a_0^{-1}(X_1^{\ell-1}+a_{\ell-1}X_1^{\ell-2}+\dots+a_1)$ is a polynomial in $X_1$; and from $T_rX_rT_r=q_HX_{r+1}$ one gets $X_r=q_H^{-1}T_r^{-1}X_{r+1}T_r^{-1}$, hence by induction each $X_r^{-1}$ is expressed through $X_1^{-1}$ and the $T$'s. Therefore the quotient is already generated, as an $A$-module, by words in $X_1,\dots,X_d$ and the $T_r$, and step 1.1 applies to them. [F1, step 1.1, algebra]

2.2 The transported relations in the nondegenerate branch. Put $R_1:=f(X_1)$ and $R_{r+1}:=T_rR_rT_r$; each $R_r$ is zero in the quotient. We claim by induction on $r$ that ordered straightening writes $R_r=q_H^{r-1}X_r^{\ell}+\sum_{a,w}c_{a,w}X_1^{a_1}\cdots X_r^{a_r}T_w,$ where $w\in S_r$, $\sum_ja_j\le\ell$, $a_r<\ell$, and no variable of index above $r$ occurs. The case $r=1$ is the definition, with constant coefficient rearranged. For the induction step, direct summation of the mixed relation gives, for $m\ge1$, $T_rX_r^mT_r=q_HX_{r+1}^m-(q_H-1)\sum_{k=1}^{m-1}X_r^kX_{r+1}^{m-k}T_r,$ and for $m=0$ one uses $T_r^2=(q_H-1)T_r+q_H$. Transporting the leading term $q_H^{r-1}X_r^{\ell}$ therefore produces exactly $q_H^rX_{r+1}^{\ell}$ plus terms of $X_{r+1}$-degree below $\ell$. Each remainder monomial has $X_r$-degree $a_r<\ell$; variables of index below $r$ commute with $T_r$ and with $X_{r+1}$, and $T_w\in H_r$ for $w\in S_r$, so applying the displayed formula to $X_r^{a_r}$ and then straightening the finite words $T_rT_wT_r$, $T_wT_r$ by [F2] produces terms of $X_{r+1}$-degree at most $a_r<\ell$ and total polynomial degree at most $\ell$. This proves the claim. [F1, F2, step 1.1, algebra]

2.3 The transported relations in the degenerate branch. Put $R_1:=f(x_1)$ and $R_{r+1}:=s_rR_rs_r$. Straightening and induction on $r$ show $R_r=x_r^{\ell}+\sum_{a,w}c_{a,w}x^aw,$ where $w\in S_r$, all occurring variables have index at most $r$, and $\sum_ja_j<\ell$: conjugation transports $x_r^m$ by $s_rx_r^ms_r=x_{r+1}^m-\sum_{k=0}^{m-1}x_r^kx_{r+1}^{m-1-k}s_r,$ so the leading term $x_r^{\ell}$ produces $x_{r+1}^{\ell}$ plus correction terms of total degree $\ell-1$, and every old remainder, when straightened, stays of degree below $\ell$. [F1, F2, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Termination of the nondegenerate reduction. Order exponent vectors lexicographically as $(a_d,a_{d-1},\dots,a_1)$, larger first differing coordinate larger; this is a well-order on $\mathbb N^d$ preserved by addition. Suppose $a_r\ge\ell$ for some $r$ and some spanning monomial $X^aT_w$. Multiplying the zero relation $R_r$ of step 2.2 on the left by $X^{a-\ell e_r}$ and on the right by $T_w$ gives a relation whose leading term is $q_H^{r-1}X^aT_w$ and whose remaining terms all have the same coordinates above $r$ and a strictly smaller $r$-th coordinate (increases in coordinates below $r$ do not affect the lexicographic comparison), with $w\in S_r$ and finite straightening changing no dot exponents. Since $q_H^{r-1}$ is a unit, this solves for $X^aT_w$ as a combination of spanning monomials with strictly smaller exponent vector; well-founded induction on the lexicographic order terminates and expresses every element using only monomials with every $a_r<\ell$. Hence the bounded ordered monomials span the nondegenerate quotient, over $A$ and after any base change. [step 2.2, algebra]

3.2 Termination of the degenerate reduction. If $a_r\ge\ell$, multiply the zero relation $R_r$ of step 2.3 on the left by $x^{a-\ell e_r}$ and on the right by $w$; the leading term is $x^aw$, and every other term has total degree strictly below $\sum_ja_j$ (all other coefficients come from the remainder of $R_r$, of degree $<\ell$). Induction on the total degree $\sum_ja_j$ therefore expresses every element in terms of monomials $x^aw$ with all $a_r<\ell$. This also shows that no relation $f(x_r)=0$ for $r>1$ is claimed: at level one $f(t)=t$, and $x_1=0$ gives $x_2=s_1$, which is exactly the form of the reduced spanning family. [step 2.3, algebra]

4.1 Conclusion. Steps 1.1, 2.1 and 3.1 give the spanning statement for the nondegenerate quotient by $f(X_1)=0$, and steps 1.1, 2.3 and 3.2 for the degenerate quotient by $f(x_1)=0$, with all exponents $a_r<\ell$ in both branches and no independence asserted. Since the spanning families have at most $\ell^dd!$ elements, each quotient has dimension at most $\ell^dd!$ over a field. The specialisations $f(t)=\prod_i(t-q_H^i)^{\lambda_i}$ and $f(t)=\prod_i(t-i)^{\lambda_i}$ are instances of the two branches with $\ell=\sum_i\lambda_i$. For $\ell=0$ the imposed relation is $1=0$, giving the zero algebra for $d>0$ and $A$ for $d=0$, as stated. [step 2.1, step 3.1, step 3.2] ∎

## Remarks

The lemma is a pure spanning statement: no independence of the bounded ordered
monomials is asserted here (the later cyclotomic basis theorem supplies it in
the finite-dimensional setting). The two straightening identities are the only
place where the difference between the nondegenerate and degenerate branches
matters, and the reduction terminates by a well-order in the nondegenerate
case and by total degree in the degenerate case. No choice principle is used.
