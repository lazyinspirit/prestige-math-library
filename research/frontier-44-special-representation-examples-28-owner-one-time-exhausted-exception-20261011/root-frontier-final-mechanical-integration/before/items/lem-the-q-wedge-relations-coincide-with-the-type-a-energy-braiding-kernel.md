## Statement

On $V_{\mathrm{aff}}\otimes V_{\mathrm{aff}}$ put $a=Y_1/Y_2$ and let $P$ be the
flip of the two tensor factors including their loop variables. Define the
completed normalized left operator

$$R_{\mathrm{fix}}:=(q^2-a)^{-1}\bigl[(a-1)S+q^2-1\bigr],\qquad \psi_{\mathrm{fix}}:=1-q^2a^{-1},$$

where $S$ is the two-factor Hecke operator of
[[def-the-affine-hecke-algebra-action-on-finite-tensor-powers-of-the-vector-representation]]
and $(q^2-a)^{-1}$ is expanded $q$-adically as
$-a^{-1}\sum_{r\ge0}(q^2/a)^r$, so $\psi_{\mathrm{fix}}=1-q^2/a$ has constant term $1$. Then:

(i) $R_{\mathrm{fix}}$ swaps the two loop variables, fixes every extremal
pure tensor $u\otimes u$, and commutes with the fixed $U_q'(\mathfrak g)$-action;
the cleared identity
$\psi_{\mathrm{fix}}(R_{\mathrm{fix}}-1)=(a^{-1}-1)(S+1)$ holds in the Laurent
domain, and since multiplication by $a^{-1}-1$ is injective on the original
Laurent-loop module one has $\ker(R_{\mathrm{fix}}-1)=\ker(S+1)$ there, before
any localization.

(ii) $R_{\mathrm{fix}}$ satisfies the normalized Yang-Baxter equation
$R_{12}R_{23}R_{12}=R_{23}R_{12}R_{23}$ on the three-factor completed module.

(iii) The flipped operator $R_{\mathrm{op}}:=PR_{\mathrm{fix}}P$ with
$\psi_{\mathrm{op}}=1-q^2a$ is the opposite-channel ($\otimes_+$) normalized
braiding of
[[def-the-level-one-vector-representation-its-affinization-and-energy-function]]:
its $q^0$-leading term is
$z^{H_{\mathrm{op}}}b_1\otimes z^{-H_{\mathrm{op}}}b_2$ with the energy
$H_{\mathrm{op}}(z^ab_j\otimes z^bb_k)=\chi(j>k)+b-a$, and its kernel is
$N_{\mathrm{op}}=P\ker(S+1)$, the $U_q'(\mathfrak g)$- and symmetric-loop orbit of the extremal
diagonal generated by
$C_{j,k}=v_j\otimes z^{-\chi(j>k)}v_k+q\,z^{-\chi(j>k)}v_k\otimes v_j$ $(j\ne k)$
and $C_{j,j}=v_j\otimes v_j$. Flipping back gives the fixed-channel quotient
and the normal-ordering relations of
[[def-finite-q-wedge-space-and-normal-ordering-relations]].

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The two-factor Hecke operator $S$ with $S^2=(Q-1)S+Q$, $Q=q^2$, its loop extension $S(fw)=s(f)S(w)+(1-Q)Y_2\frac{f-s(f)}{Y_1-Y_2}w$, the loop variables $Y_1,Y_2$, the flip $P$, and the energy $H_{\mathrm{op}}$ and tensor rule of the opposite channel.

[F1] The finite residue operator $S_0$ satisfies the three-case formula of the affine Hecke action. Writing $s$ for loop-variable exchange, its Laurent-loop extension is $S=S_0s+(1-Q)Y_2(1-s)/(Y_1-Y_2)$; $S$ commutes with $Y_1Y_2$, but not with $a=Y_1/Y_2$ ([[def-the-affine-hecke-algebra-action-on-finite-tensor-powers-of-the-vector-representation]]).

[F2] $S$ commutes with the fixed $U_q'(\mathfrak g)$-action on two factors, and the loop variables act invertibly with $Y_ju_m=u_{m-e}$; the Laurent polynomial ring $\mathbb Q(q)[Y_1^{\pm1},Y_2^{\pm1}]$ is a domain, so multiplication by a nonzero element is injective on every free module ([[def-the-affine-hecke-algebra-action-on-finite-tensor-powers-of-the-vector-representation]]).

[F3] On residue pairs $(j,k)$ the opposite-channel rule is $f$ on the first factor iff $\varepsilon_i(b_1)\ge\varphi_i(b_2)$, and $H_{\mathrm{op}}(z^ab_j\otimes z^bb_k)=\chi(j>k)+b-a$ satisfies the two loop-shift axioms and $H_{\mathrm{op}}>0\iff m_1>m_2$ ([[def-the-level-one-vector-representation-its-affinization-and-energy-function]]).

[F4] In the full-color derived affine vector crystal, $B\otimes B$ is connected and each $b_j$ is an extremal vector, giving the diagonal tensors $b_j\otimes b_j$ ([[lem-the-level-one-vector-representation-has-a-perfect-crystal-with-lower-global-basis]]).



## Proof

**Proof technique:** clear the denominator of $R_{\mathrm{fix}}$, read the kernel off the cleared identity, verify Yang-Baxter on the Baxterized numerator, and transport the result through the flip.

1.1 The left fraction and loop swap. From [F1], since $(a-1)Y_2=Y_1-Y_2$, the numerator simplifies as an operator to $(a-1)S+Q-1=[(a-1)S_0+Q-1]s$. Thus $R_{\mathrm{fix}}=R_0(a)s$ with $R_0(a)=((a-1)S_0+Q-1)/(Q-a)$, proving $R_{\mathrm{fix}}f=s(f)R_{\mathrm{fix}}$ for every Laurent loop function $f$. In particular it swaps $Y_1,Y_2$. All denominators are LEFT coefficients; no commutation of $S$ with $a$ is used. The inverse is $-a^{-1}\sum_{r\ge0}(Q/a)^r$, defined in the separated $q$-adic loop-module completion. For an extremal diagonal residue tensor $w$, $S_0w=-w$ and the numerator is $(Q-a)w$, so $R_{\mathrm{fix}}w=w$; common loop shifts give the same conclusion for each $u\otimes u$. By [F2] the original Hecke action and loop functions commute with $U_q'$; ratios have total degree zero, so the same operator intertwines the full affine action. [F1, F2, given, algebra]

2.1 The exact original-space kernel. Subtracting the identity with LEFT denominator gives $R_{\mathrm{fix}}-1=(Q-a)^{-1}(a-1)(S+1)$. Multiplication on the left by $\psi_{\mathrm{fix}}=-(Q-a)/a$ gives exactly $(a^{-1}-1)(S+1)$. The formal $q$-adic coefficient $\psi_{\mathrm{fix}}$ is invertible, and multiplication by the nonzero Laurent polynomial $a^{-1}-1$ is injective on the original free Laurent-loop module by [F2]. Hence an ORIGINAL Laurent-loop vector is fixed by $R_{\mathrm{fix}}$ exactly when it lies in $\ker(S+1)$. No localization of the original kernel and no finite-dimensional weight-space assumption is needed. [step 1.1, F2, given, algebra]

2.2 Yang--Baxter. Use the FINITE residue operators $S_{0,1},S_{0,2}$ with commuting external variables $x,y$, and put $B_i(x)=(x-1)S_{0,i}+Q-1$. Expand $B_1(x)B_2(xy)B_1(y)$ and $B_2(y)B_1(xy)B_2(x)$. Reduce each square with $S_{0,i}^2=(Q-1)S_{0,i}+Q$ and each three-letter braid with $S_{0,1}S_{0,2}S_{0,1}=S_{0,2}S_{0,1}S_{0,2}$. The coefficients of the remaining Hecke words agree, using $(x-1)(y-1)+(x-1)+(y-1)=xy-1$ for the one-letter terms. The denominator is the common commuting product $(Q-x)(Q-xy)(Q-y)$. In step 1.1 the loop swaps supply precisely $x,y,xy$ on the three factors, so this finite-residue identity proves $R_{12}R_{23}R_{12}=R_{23}R_{12}R_{23}$ in the stated completion. It is not an expansion of full affine $S_i$ against noncommuting loop coefficients. [step 1.1, F1, given, algebra]

3.1 The opposite channel and its leading term. Define $R_{\mathrm{op}}=PR_{\mathrm{fix}}P$ with the full loop-and-factor flip. It intertwines the opposite coproduct because $P$ transports the checked fixed action, and its denominator is $1-Qa$. Evaluate $R_0(a)$ from step 1.1 on the three residue cases. At $q=0$ its pre-loop-swap factor is $a^{-1}$ for $j<k$, and $1$ for $j=k$ or $j>k$. Full flip exchanges both the residue order and $a$ with $a^{-1}$; hence the opposite pre-swap factors are $a,1,1$ for $j>k,j=k,j<k$. On $z^av_j\otimes z^bv_k$ the loop swap adds the exponent difference $b-a$, giving $H_{\mathrm{op}}=\chi(j>k)+b-a$ exactly as in [F3]. The opposite normalization and Yang--Baxter identities follow by the same full flip. [step 1.1, step 2.2, F3, algebra]

4.1 The full kernel generators. Step 2.1 and flip give $\ker(R_{\mathrm{op}}-1)=P\ker(S+1)$. The actual two-factor Laurent-loop kernel is generated over symmetric loop functions by the diagonal tensors and, for $j<k$, $h_{jk}=v_j\otimes v_k+q^{-1}v_k\otimes v_j$ and $h'_{jk}=Y_1v_k\otimes v_j+q^{-1}Y_2v_j\otimes v_k$ ([[lem-the-q-antisymmetrizer-determines-the-finite-q-wedge-kernel]]). Their flips, after multiplication by the units $q$ and $(Y_1Y_2)^{-1}$, are exactly the two order cases of the displayed $C_{j,k}$. Thus the displayed $C$ generate the entire original opposite kernel with symmetric loop shifts. To verify the full quantum orbit explicitly in the fixed channel, write $d_j=v_j\otimes v_j$. For $1\le j<e$, $\Delta F_jd_j=q h_{j,j+1}$ and $(\Delta F_j)^2d_j=(q+q^{-1})d_{j+1}$; hence all diagonals and all constant $h_{jk}$ are reached from $d_1$, extending the second index by the intervening finite lowering operators. Also $\Delta E_0d_1=Y_2(v_1\otimes v_e)+qY_1(v_e\otimes v_1)=q h'_{1e}$. For $j+1<k$, $\Delta F_jh'_{jk}=h'_{j+1,k}$, and for $j<k-1$, $\Delta E_{k-1}h'_{jk}=h'_{j,k-1}$; the partner factor has toral eigenvalue $1$ in both cases. These paths reach every $h'_{jk}$ with $j<k$, including $e=2$ where the first affine step already reaches the only such pair. Since symmetric Laurent multiplication commutes with the derived action, this proves that its symmetric-loop orbit contains the entire kernel basis. Conversely every such orbit element remains in the kernel by intertwining and symmetric-loop stability. Flipping proves the exact opposite quantum/symmetric-loop orbit asserted for the $C$ generators. Common loop shifts and symmetric powers produce every Laurent gap. This is the stated quantum AND symmetric-loop orbit, not arbitrary individual loop multiplication of a kernel vector. Flip back therefore gives precisely the full finite straightening quotient. [step 2.1, step 3.1, F2, F3, F4, algebra]

5.1 Conclusion. The checked completed left fraction swaps loops, fixes extremal diagonals, intertwines the fixed action and satisfies normalized Yang--Baxter. Its polynomial-cleared identity has injective Laurent multiplier on original vectors, giving the exact original-space kernel. The opposite flip has the stated energy, denominator and full symmetric-loop/quantum kernel generators, and transports back to the fixed finite-wedge quotient. [step 1.1, step 2.1, step 2.2, step 3.1, step 4.1] ∎

## Remarks

- The formula is a **left**-coefficient fraction: $S$ and $a$ do not commute,
  and no right-fraction interpretation of $(q^2-a)^{-1}$ is asserted.

- The norm of the flip is a loop-and-factor flip only; it does not identify the
  source's positive (descending) wedge chart with the library chart, which is
  proved locally in the finite q-wedge space.
