## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $\mu_0$ be a Borel probability measure on $\mathbb R^d$, where $d\ge1$, and let $K=L^2(\mu_0;\mathbb R^d)$ be the space of measurable vector fields modulo equality $\mu_0$-almost everywhere with finite squared norm. With
$$\langle X,Y\rangle_K=\int_{\mathbb R^d}X(s)\cdot Y(s)\,d\mu_0(s),\qquad \|X\|_K^2=\int_{\mathbb R^d}|X(s)|^2\,d\mu_0(s),$$
$K$ is a separable real Hilbert space.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC, the Borel probability $\mu_0$, and $d\ge1$.

[F1] Scalar $L^2(\mu_0)$ is the quotient of square-integrable measurable functions by equality almost everywhere, and its norm is complete by the Riesz–Fischer theorem. [[def-l-p-space-as-a-quotient-by-null-functions]] [[thm-riesz-fischer-completeness-of-l-p]]

[F2] Finite-measure simple functions are dense in scalar $L^2$. [[thm-simple-functions-with-finite-measure-support-are-dense-in-l-p-for-finite-p]]

[F3] AC implies Countable Choice. [[def-axiom-of-choice]] [[thm-choice-implies-dependent-implies-countable-choice]] [[def-countable-choice]]

[F4] Finite-measure sets in a sigma-finite countably generated space can be approximated in symmetric-difference measure by a countable generating algebra. [[lem-countable-generators-yield-countable-set-algebras]] [[lem-finite-measure-sets-are-approximable-by-a-countable-generating-algebra]]

[F5] Cauchy–Schwarz gives $|\langle X,Y\rangle|\le\|X\|_2\|Y\|_2$ for the displayed componentwise pairing. [[thm-cauchy-schwarz-in-an-inner-product-space]]

## Proof


**Given:** AC and $\mu_0$ as in the Statement.

1.1 The Borel sigma-algebra of $\mathbb R^d$ is generated by the countable family of open boxes with rational endpoints: every open set is the union of the members of this basis that it contains. By [F3] and [F4], there is a countable algebra $\mathcal A_0$ of Borel sets such that each Borel set $E$ and each $\varepsilon>0$ admit $A\in\mathcal A_0$ with $\mu_0(E\triangle A)<\varepsilon$. [given, F3, F4]

2.1 The functions $\sum_{j=1}^m q_j\mathbf1_{A_j}$, with $q_j\in\mathbb Q$ and $A_j\in\mathcal A_0$, form a countable subset of scalar $L^2(\mu_0)$. It is dense: by [F2], approximate any $f$ by a finite-measure simple function $\sum_j c_j\mathbf1_{E_j}$; approximate each $E_j$ in measure using [F4], then approximate each coefficient by a rational. The error satisfies $\|c\mathbf1_E-q\mathbf1_A\|_2\le |c-q|\sqrt{\mu_0(E)}+|q|\sqrt{\mu_0(E\triangle A)}$, so the finitely many choices make the total error arbitrarily small. [F2, F4, step 1.1, choose]

3.1 The vector space $K$ is the finite product of $d$ copies of scalar $L^2(\mu_0)$; its displayed pairing is the sum of the coordinate pairings. [F5] gives a positive-definite inner product, and coordinatewise completeness from [F1] makes the finite product complete. The finite product of the countable dense scalar sets from step 2.1 is countable and dense in $K$. Hence $K$ is a separable real Hilbert space. [F1, F5, step 2.1] ∎
