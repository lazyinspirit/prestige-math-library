# Bounded second reading of the new prerequisite scaffolds

Scope: the new GT-4C/A/L/S/E/D/P and BF-FOUND/BERGE/SPGT prose in `research/plan-group-theory-track.md` and `research/plan-combinatorics-and-categories.md`, checked against current item homes and contracts. This is a read-only mathematical/DAG review of the scaffold prose, not a source-proof audit of CFSG or SPGT. Findings were sent to the root and prerequisite-scaffold author; the author is addressing them. No plan or item edit was made in this review.

1. **Duplicate group-theory suppliers.** GT-4A proposes `def-outer-automorphism-group-of-a-finite-simple-group`, but published `def-outer-automorphism-group` already defines $\operatorname{Aut}(G)/\operatorname{Inn}(G)$ for every group, with normality and quotient dependencies. GT-4S proposes `lem-solvable-extension-of-solvable-groups-is-solvable`, but published `thm-extensions-and-direct-products-of-solvable-groups-are-solvable` already states and proves the exact extension result by derived series. Reuse these existing IDs.

2. **BF-FOUND-A page prerequisites.** Rehomed `def-perfect-graph-for-the-bull-route` directly depends on `def-proper-vertex-colouring-and-chromatic-number`, whose home is `graph-colouring`. The draft's “requires only” finite-simple-graph and induced-subgraph interfaces omit that page. `graphs-walks-and-connectivity` and `induced-subgraphs-and-hereditary-graph-classes` cover the other rehomed definitions' home requirements. Add `graph-colouring` to BF-FOUND-A's page prerequisites. With the root-approved Lovász rehome in finding 4, also add `linear-independence-bases-and-dimension`: the theorem directly depends on `cor-independent-set-is-no-larger-than-a-finite-spanning-set` and `lem-standard-basis-of-f-n`, both homed there.

3. **GT-4P argument overclaim.** The initial proof description for $Y=M$ said a solvable stabilizer image in $\operatorname{Out}(T)^k$ “therefore centralizes” a normal simple inner-automorphism image. Solvability of an outer quotient alone does not imply that centralization: $\operatorname{Inn}(A_5)$ acts nontrivially on itself while $\operatorname{Out}(A_5)$ is solvable. The first revision inserted an intersection argument but claimed, without its branch hypothesis, that the preimage of $\operatorname{Inn}(T_1)$ under $N_\alpha\to\operatorname{Aut}(T_1)$ maps onto all of $\operatorname{Inn}(T_1)$. Root checked LPS p.394: in Case 2(a) the extra assumption $T_1^*\le N_\alpha^*$ yields the required surjectivity. The final wording must state that assumption, the factor-action kernel, and the $R_1=1$ branch before the simple intersection step.

4. **SPGT page cycle.** The draft BF-SPGT-BASE cites the published Lovász complement theorem and BF-SPGT-OBS cites the published replication lemma. Both are currently homed on the bull A page, which would be after BF-SPGT-END and BF-BERGE on the proposed route. Thus BASE/OBS would depend on a later consumer page. Rehome `lem-replicating-a-vertex-of-a-perfect-graph-preserves-perfection` and `thm-lovasz-perfect-graph-criterion-and-complement-invariance` to an earlier approved page before making that DAG buildable. The root has approved BF-FOUND-A as their home; their direct item dependencies are earlier graph, colouring, perfection, and linear-algebra foundations.

5. **Bipartite line graph proof supplier.** BF-SPGT-BASE says to prove bipartite line graphs perfect “by König edge coloring”. Current item search found a published König matching-cover theorem, but no edge-colouring/chromatic-index theorem. The required edge-coloring result must be fully proved on BASE or supplied on an earlier page. Matching-cover alone is not the cited contract.

6. **Classification wording.** GT-4C's “belongs to exactly the standard alternating, finite Lie-type or 26 sporadic families, allowing ... isomorphisms” should say that the list is exhaustive and permits membership under more than one family name at small isomorphisms. The current wording can be read as claiming disjoint family membership while acknowledging collisions.

No cycle was found in the intended abstract GT-4C/A/L/S, GT-4E/D, GT-4P branch graph itself or the BF-FOUND → BF-SPGT → BF-BERGE branch graph after the home repairs above. The scaffold correctly labels CFSG exhaustiveness, the inaccessible restricted bull-free perfection proof, the imported SPGT obstruction lemmas, and source-numbered SPGT internals as open proof obligations. This review does not certify those mathematical proofs or a complete internal item inventory.
