## Statement

Assume Countable Choice. Let complete balanced disk systems of rank $m=h+k-1$ on a genus-h surface belong to ONE specified handlebody, with one w and one z mark in every cut component, and with the same actual trivial arcs and marks. Realize a specified own-system handleslide and its sufficiently small auxiliary copies by the circular ribbon model of the named suppliers; take the other curve copies sufficiently small. A handleslide requires $m\ge2$. Small isotopy copies alone are allowed for $m\ge1$. The actual ribbon and its common exterior avoid all marks.

The full marked index-one differential of each required model pair kills its top generator. Both actual top-input, index-zero triangle products in the circular move construction equal the stated top output with coefficient one, with NO other output or positive marked term. These assertions hold over the polynomial ring with all individual w and z variables retained, and hence after setting every z variable equal to one. They hold for sufficiently small regular coherent protected perturbations of the model.

This applies to every actual balanced h,k, including dependent meridian lists and genus zero when the required number of curves exists. No inference is made about mixed-system triangles involving the opposite handlebody. The proof computes actual zero-Chern restrictions and same-corner index differences; it does not assume every formal degree-one periodic domain has a Whitney representative.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The balanced handlebody, its supplied cut balls, the slide band and the small auxiliary copies, all with the same mark and arc data.

[F1] The explicit circular ribbon has actual bigon and slit-annulus counts, index-one model operators and top cycles. Its two top-triangle products have actual index zero, bounded inverse and count one; the local lower-output reference has index equal to the number of lost plus coordinates. All source end angles are the actual transverse angles. ([[lem-circular-handleslide-slit-annuli-give-actual-top-cycles]], [[lem-circular-handleslide-top-triangles-have-actual-count-one]])

[F2] Finite smooth ribbon and complementary surface presentations match with prescribed collars and finite germs. The model maps and their active root images may therefore be placed in the actual thin ribbon, keeping the unused root positions outside it. The actual local Beltrami/Neumann coordinate construction supplies an integrable atlas for a smooth positive surface tensor, including prescribed already holomorphic patches. ([[lem-finite-smooth-surface-presentations-have-prescribed-collar-matches]], [[lem-finite-parameter-relative-symmetric-products-have-adapted-parallel-transports]], full Proof1.1)

[F3] For actual balanced polygon Morse data, $H_2(W)=\ker(\bigoplus_i A_i\to H_1(\Sigma;\mathbb Z))$, where $A_i$ is the integral meridian lattice of its handlebody. The fundamental surface and all one-system cut domains cap to zero. Actual difference lines classify the difference of two supplied Spin-c lifts by the finite cellular $H^2$, and determinants differ by twice that class. ([[lem-heegaard-polygon-spin-c-difference-is-the-capped-periodic-domain]], full Proof1.1 and1.3)

[F4] For two actual same-corner polygons with the same Spin-c label, their actual index difference is $\langle c_1(\mathfrak s),H(P)\rangle+2\sum_i n_{w_i}(P)$. The two-edge case and every balanced m, including m=1, are proved. Actual realized periodic-strip indices satisfy the same formula. ([[lem-heegaard-polygon-relative-maslov-variation-is-chern-plus-marks]], [[lem-realized-periodic-whitney-index-is-morse-chern-plus-marks]])

[F5] Strict normalized strong admissibility supplies actual full-marked good end paths and coherent finite polygon counts, arbitrarily close to a supplied protected reference. Their finite weighted triangle and quadrilateral identities do not compute a top generator. ([[thm-strictly-admissible-triple-free-polygons-have-full-marked-low-dimensional-counts]])

## Proof

1.1 Realize the model in a thin regular neighborhood of the two circles and their slide band. This neighborhood is the actual finite planar ribbon, independently of whether either circle separates the ambient surface; its cyclic edge orders, vertex disks and edge collars give the match [F2]. The two-sheet slit-annulus root images and the triangle root images in [F1] lie in this ribbon; no unused global handle is in their image. On each other circle place its small two-intersection copy in a disjoint thin collar. The additional active bigons and triangles are the one-root circular models of [F1]. Constant roots stay outside the active ribbon. Thus their actual equations and operator factors are LITERALLY the local ones of [F1], with the same finite-sheet budgets. The global paired-hole model in that supplier is not being asserted to have the same genus as this surface; only its stated local maps and their supported equations are transported. A positive surface tensor agreeing with these local complex charts extends over the remaining surface by finite patches; its actual complex atlas is obtained from [F2]'s proved local Beltrami construction. On the already round patches these are charts for the SAME tensor, so their overlap maps are holomorphic and the model charts are retained. No almost-complex tensor has been declared integrable without that coordinate proof. [F1, F2, given, construct, algebra]

1.2 Keep track of the marks in the COMMON exterior. A cut component is planar. The slide band joins faces of two DISTINCT disk labels, hence joins two distinct boundary components of that planar piece. Cutting along that arc leaves a connected piece: join its two banks by a collar through either incident boundary circle, or cut first along a finite tree joining all boundary circles to obtain the usual one-disk presentation. Removing the thin boundary collars and this one boundary-joining ribbon therefore leaves one connected common exterior for that cut component. Smaller parallel copies only change its boundary collars. It contains the original w mark and its own-system partner z mark; the partner permutation need not be the identity. Both marks remain in the SAME component of the complement of every model curve. Consequently EVERY domain, signed or positive, has equal w and partner-z coefficient there. No mark has been moved, and no statement that a band joining two points of the SAME boundary circle is nonseparating was used. [F2, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 A normalized model domain with all w coefficients zero has zero coefficient in each of these common exterior pieces. Its remaining jumps are entirely the local ribbon and the small-copy collars. Integrate its full-circle boundary coefficients there: the slide block has exactly the local basis $P_1=I_{\beta_1}+I_{\beta_2}-I_{\gamma_1}$ and $P_2=I_{\gamma_2}-I_{\beta_2}$ of [F1]; each other pair adds its signed small-copy trace. With a third or further nearby system subtract its signed trace relative to the preceding system first, leaving that pair problem. This is integral subtraction of actual indicator domains; no meridian independence is required. Any residual with zero edge jumps has constant coefficient on the connected surface and is zero at its w marks. The private positive and negative lune tests in [F1] force every coefficient to vanish if the normalized domain is nonnegative. Thus the required full-list w-normalized multiperiodic lattice has mixed signs. Its local positive zero-mark lists and counts are exactly those of [F1], for ALL balanced h,k; extra one-system directions have already been fixed to zero by the exterior marks. [F1, step 1.1, step 1.2, construct, algebra]

2.2 Compute the actual pair-sector Chern class. Let A be the meridian lattice of this handlebody; its rank is h even when its m listed disks are dependent. The finite pair sequence in [F3] identifies $H_2(Y_{ij})$ with $\{(a,-a):a\in A\}$. For a small-copy pair, the difference of its TWO actual index-one bigons has index zero, all marked degrees zero, and caps to that listed meridian. In the slide block the actual difference between D1 and the existing slit-annulus D2+D4 gives $P_1+P_2$, and the actual D3-minus-D4 bigon difference gives $P_2$. They cap to $[\beta_1]+[\beta_2]$ and $-[\beta_2]$, up to their boundary orientation signs. Their smooth periodic maps are obtained by composing the actual strip and its reverse along ONE matching end; no three-corner gluing is involved. Together with the other local bigon differences their capped classes span A integrally, because the listed meridians span A and that two-by-two change is unimodular. Apply [F4] to these ACTUAL periodic classes: their indices and w degrees are zero, so $c_1$ evaluates to zero on every pair H2 class. All pair generators join the top by actual local bigon concatenations; hence their Morse Spin-c labels are the same sector, with this zero Chern evaluation. [F1, F3, F4, step 1.1, construct, algebra]

3.1 For a triangle whose three systems all belong to this handlebody, [F3] gives $$H_2(W)=\{(a,b,c)\in A^3:a+b+c=0\}.$$ Its elements are sums of $(a,-a,0)$ and $(0,b,-b)$, the actual images of the two adjacent boundary-pair H2 groups. Thus EVERY actual polygon lift restricting to these model pair labels has Chern evaluation zero on ALL H2(W), by step 2.2. There are no omitted surface or cut-component directions: they cap to zero by [F3]. The finite relative handle sequence also gives $H_1(W)=H_1(\Sigma)/A$, which is free, since A is the primitive meridian lattice of a handlebody. Finite integral cochain elimination therefore makes $H^2(W)$ torsion free. Two actual lifts with the prescribed restrictions and zero Chern differ by a line class a with $2a=0$, so they are the SAME lift by [F3]. The same argument gives uniqueness of the model pair label, since $H_1(Y_{ij})$ is free. Thus [F4]'s SAME-label hypothesis is verified rather than inferred merely from rational Chern equality. [F3, F4, step 2.2, construct, algebra]

4.1 At the top pair generator choose the actual local strip concatenation to any output y, flipping its lost plus coordinates one at a time. Its index is $\ell(y)$, the number of these coordinates, and all its marked degrees vanish. Any actual strip with that input and output has the same pair label by step 2.2. [F4] and the Chern vanishing therefore give $$\mu(u)=\ell(y)+2\sum_i n_{w_i}(u).$$ Every term is nonnegative. At index one the only possibility is $\ell(y)=1$ and every w degree zero; the alternative $\ell(y)=0$ cannot give the odd index. Step 1.2 then makes every z degree zero too. Thus the FULL marked differential out of the top generator is exactly its actual zero-mark model differential in [F1], which is zero, with every surviving coefficient equal to the monomial one. This is the required weighted cycle assertion; no statement is made that the differential out of a lower generator has no positive-U terms. [F1, F4, step 1.2, step 2.2, step 3.1, construct, algebra]

5.1 For either top-triangle product take its ACTUAL local index-zero triangle with the specified top output. For an output y append the output bigon concatenation to get a smooth reference triangle of index $\ell(y)$ and zero marked degrees. This is one-corner disk gluing, with the common cap frames cancelled. By step 3.1 every other actual triangle with those corners has the SAME Spin-c label and zero Chern term. Thus its index is again $$\mu(u)=\ell(y)+2\sum_i n_{w_i}(u).$$ At index zero this forces the top output and every w degree zero. Step 1.2 forces every z degree zero. Consequently the entire full-marked count is precisely the actual zero-mark triangle product in [F1], with its bounded inverse and count one. No term with positive degree of either type can enter, and all lower outputs are excluded by actual index. [F1, F3, F4, step 1.2, step 3.1, step 4.1, construct, algebra]

6.1 These counts can be used with actual full-marked coherent data. On every relevant pair or triple, step 2.1 gives global normalized w-weakness. Steps 2.2–4.1 give zero Chern on its capped lattice; the strict strong inequality therefore has N equal to zero and is exactly that mixed-sign test. Apply [F5] in this actual label and choose its good end paths and coherent coefficients arbitrarily close to the local reference, keeping its full protected tensors and actual angles. Every candidate counted out of the top, or in either index-zero top product, is forced by steps 4.1 and 5.1 to have BOTH degree vectors zero. The actual compactness and inverse arguments in [F1] therefore retain exactly those local counts under the small perturbation; the index argument excludes a new positive-mark term for every parameter, not just in the limit. All identities are over the ring of the individual marked variables and remain so after specialization. If $m=1$ only the one-circle-copy calculation is used, and [F4] already supplies its actual degree-one case; if $m=0$ the empty top input and matching point triangle are the direct rank-zero model. This proves the stated full balanced same-system operation, without an assertion about the opposite handlebody. [F1, F3, F4, F5, step 2.1, step 2.2, step 3.1, step 4.1, step 5.1, construct, algebra] ∎
