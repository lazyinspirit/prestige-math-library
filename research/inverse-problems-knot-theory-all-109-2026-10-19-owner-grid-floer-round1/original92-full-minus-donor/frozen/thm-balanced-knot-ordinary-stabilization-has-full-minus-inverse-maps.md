## Statement

Assume Countable Choice. An ordinary Heegaard stabilization of an actual balanced multipointed knot diagram in S3, disjoint from the attaching curves, marks and actual knot arcs, gives a filtered chain homotopy equivalence of its full minus complexes over $R=\mathbb F_2[U_1,\ldots,U_k]$. Take actual finite square-zero good endpoint counts: no nonconstant strip has index at most zero, and all index-one strips are regular. All positive z terms and individual marked variables are retained. Maps have relative Maslov degree zero, homotopies degree plus one, and are Alexander filtered after identifying the relative origins. This asserts no absolute centering or marked-pair stabilization.

For chosen data the nearest bijection $x\mapsto x\times c$ literally identifies the actual rigid counts, including their individual w and z vectors. The stabilized chosen path has no nonconstant nonpositive-index strip and has regular index-one strips. Independent good endpoint choices are compared by actual full-minus continuations. The proof solves the torus mean obstructions and exhausts changing-target limits; it does not assume a transverse split disk disjoint union of horizontal tori.
