## Mathematical disposition

The serial-relation formulation includes a nonempty carrier and the prescribed
start equivalence is proved in ZF by a tagged finite-path construction. For
DC implies Baire, the states include centres and rational radii, with closed
balls nested inside the preceding open set and diameters tending to zero. DC is
used exactly once to choose the state sequence; completeness and the tail
argument put the limit in every dense open set.

For the converse, the discrete sequence metric is proved complete in ZF using
least stabilization indices. The successor-occurrence sets are open and dense
by a one-cylinder extension. A point in their intersection supplies a successor somewhere in its range
for each coordinate; recursively taking the least witness index gives the
required R-chain without a new choice principle. Witness indices need not
increase. Thus the claimed equivalence
and its axiom-strength labels are correct.
