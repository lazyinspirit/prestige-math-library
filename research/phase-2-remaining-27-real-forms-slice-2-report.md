## Content summary (what was actually proved)

- **`def-theta-stable...`**: definition of θ-stability, the decomposition
  h_0 = t_0 ⊕ a_0 with t_0 = h_0 ∩ k_0, a_0 = h_0 ∩ p_0, compact/noncompact
  dimensions, maximally compact / maximally noncompact terminology, the bracket
  inclusions and the Killing-form signs.
- **`thm-every-real-cartan-subalgebra...`** (Knapp Prop. 6.59): complexification
  of a real Cartan is a complex Cartan; a compact conjugation is adjusted so that
  it fixes the complexified Cartan (via conjugacy of complex Cartans and the
  Cartan h_c = a ⊕ ia of a maximal abelian subspace of the compact form); the
  self-adjoint positive-automorphism construction of `thm-existence-of-a-cartan-involution`
  is re-run with the extra h-invariance; the resulting Cartan involution η fixes
  h_0; conjugacy of Cartan involutions finishes.
- **`prop-real-cartan-subalgebras-need-not-be-conjugate`**: explicit witness in
  sl_2(R) using B(h,h) = 8 and B(k,k) = −8 for k = e − f and invariance of the
  Killing form under every automorphism.
- **`def-cayley-transform...`**: root types, the Hermitian form and the norm
  |α|² = |B(H_α,H_α)|, and the two transforms c_β = exp((π/4)ad(σE_β − E_β)) and
  d_α = exp(i(π/4)ad(θE_α − E_α)) with the normalizations
  B(E_β,σE_β) = 2/|β|² and B(E_α,θE_α) = −2/|α|².
- **`thm-cayley-transforms-connect...`** (Knapp Props. 6.69–6.70 + Prop. 6.61):
  computed images g_0 ∩ c_β(h) = ker β ⊕ R(E_β + σE_β) (noncompact dimension +1)
  and g_0 ∩ d_α(h) = ker α ⊕ R(E_α + θE_α) (compact dimension +1); verified the
  exact inverse c_{α''} = d_α^{-1} with α'' = d_α(α), E_{α''} = −i d_α(E_α)
  (including |α''|² = |α|² and the normalization of E_{α''}); proved
  "no real roots ⇔ maximally compact" and "no noncompact imaginary roots ⇔
  maximally noncompact" via the centralizers of t_0 and a_0; proved conjugacy of
  maximally compact Cartans via maximal tori of K and the uniqueness of the
  θ-stable Cartan with a given compact part.
- **`def-vogan-diagram`**: compatible positive systems, the diagram of a triple,
  the marked-root-system data (isometry τ plus the multiplicative marking on
  imaginary roots determined by the painting of the τ-fixed simple roots) and the
  equivalence relation generated by diagram isomorphisms and changes of base
  inside one marked root system.
- **`thm-vogan-diagram-...-well-defined...`**: Ad(K)-conjugacy of maximally
  compact Cartans transports the marked root systems including markings; the two
  compatible positive systems are related by a unique Weyl element commuting with
  the involution, so the diagrams differ by an isomorphism move plus a
  change-of-base move.

## Exact unresolved gap (open obligation)

`thm-classification-of-real-forms-by-vogan-diagrams` is **escalated**. The proof
in the item establishes well-definedness and injectivity (Knapp Theorem 6.74's
argument, printed pp. 399-402, with the alignments of complexifications, compact
forms, Cartans and positive systems and the a_α, b_α computation). Surjectivity is
not proved. The missing step is: for the involutive automorphism θ of g built from
an abstract Vogan diagram by signs on the Serre generators, find a compact real
form u_0 with θ(u_0) = u_0. Knapp's proof (Chapter VI, Theorem 6.88, printed
pp. 403-405) and Etingof's Theorem 40.1 (printed pp. 186-188) both use the
normalized Chevalley root-vector system of Knapp Theorem 6.6 (printed pp. 341-347):
real structure constants N_{αβ} with |N_{αβ}| = |N_{θα,θβ}|, B(X_α,X_{−α}) = 1 and
the induction a_{α+β} = N^{-1}_{αβ} N_{θα,θβ} a_α a_β = ±1. No library item
supplies that normalized system; the same missing supplier is recorded in the
pair's own `thm-existence-and-uniqueness-up-to-isomorphism-of-the-split-real-form`
(step 3.1 remark). Proposed remedy: add one item supplying the normalized
Chevalley system (Knapp Theorem 6.6) before this item, then finish the induction
and the verification that (g_0, θ, h_0, Δ+) realizes the given diagram.
The escalation receipt `research/phase-2-remaining-27-step3b-review-thm-classification-of-real-forms-by-vogan-diagrams.json`
was recorded before the final item edits (the deps-list correction and the
