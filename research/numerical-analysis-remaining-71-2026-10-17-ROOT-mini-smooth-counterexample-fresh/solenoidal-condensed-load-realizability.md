# Exact smooth-solenoidal condensed-load realizability on a MINI fan

Research only, 2026-10-18. This proves the smooth-solenoidal source interface for the **full** mass projector in the candidate toothed-fan construction. It does not prove the candidate geometry, its fractional lower bound, a corresponding constrained-projector lower bound, or a pressure counterexample. No canonical/runtime/hash/readiness files or tests were touched.

Use the normalized two-dimensional MINI bubble b_K=27 lambda_0 lambda_1 lambda_2. Its moments give

    t:=(lambda_i,b_K)_K/(b_K,b_K)_K=14/27,

independently of i and K. After eliminating bubble coefficients, the scalar modified nodal test is

    phi_tilde_i|K=lambda_i-t b_K,   i in K.

Thus the vector condensed load generated by a smooth velocity u is L_i=(u,phi_tilde_i)_2, componentwise. Bubble loads need not vanish.

## One triangle cannot produce a nonzero root-only solenoidal condensed load

Let u=J grad psi, psi in Cc-infinity(K), with J the counterclockwise quarter-turn. Integration by parts shows that all affine nodal loads are determined by the single number m=integral_K psi, up to an immaterial common sign:

    l_i=m J grad lambda_i.

The two bubble loads B=(u,b_K)_2 can be prescribed independently of m. Indeed the three scalar polynomial functions 1, partial_1 b_K and partial_2 b_K are linearly independent; finite-dimensional smooth moment duality realizes arbitrary moments against them. For example on the reference triangle b is proportional to xy(1-x-y), and the x^2 and y^2 coefficients establish this independence immediately.

Consequently the exact condensed nodal loads are

    L_i=m J grad lambda_i-t B.                              (1)

If the loads at two distinct vertices vanish, subtracting their equations forces m=0, since their barycentric gradients differ. Then B=0 and the remaining load is also zero. The scalar single-triangle source in the candidate cannot simply be renamed as an exact solenoidal vector source.

There is nevertheless a useful different single-triangle source. Impose integral psi=0 and prescribe B=(u,b_K)_K arbitrarily. Then all raw affine loads vanish, but the bubble load B need not vanish. The exact-solenoidal u has integral_K u=0 and zero trace on every mesh face. Consequently the existing face-average-plus-cell-mean MINI Fortin map satisfies Pi_h u=0: all its face nodal functionals vanish and its cell-mean bubble correction vanishes. Its special initial defect is exactly r=Pi_hu-u=-u. For every actual MINI vector z with local bubble coefficient beta_K,

    (u,z)_2=B dot beta_K,
    (r,z)_2=-B dot beta_K.                                    (1a)

These identities are exact, since all the affine moments of u vanish. A proposed actual kernel mode whose beta_K has a uniformly nonzero prescribed component can thus be excited by one fixed smooth no-slip solenoidal source, independently of mesh recursion outside K. This is a source-excitation statement conditional on that actual mode; it does not prove the mode exists or has the required pressure/time observation.

## Exact annihilator on any source patch

Let Omega be the interior of a union of finitely many source triangles. Consider the map from psi in Cc-infinity(Omega) to all vector condensed nodal loads generated by u=J grad psi. A coefficient family a_i in R^2 annihilates its range exactly when the continuous modified nodal field

    w=sum_i a_i phi_tilde_i

has distributional curl zero in Omega. The field is continuous across interior faces, so its distributional curl has no jump measures. On each triangle,

    w|K=sum_(i in K) a_i lambda_i-t b_K sum_(i in K)a_i.

Independence of 1, partial_1 b_K and partial_2 b_K therefore makes the annihilator conditions precisely

    sum_(i in K)a_i=0,
    curl(sum_(i in K)a_i lambda_i)=0,           every source K.     (2)

This criterion is exact for the actual MINI condensed loads, not a dimension heuristic.

## A sufficiently rich fan has no annihilator

Translate a fan root to 0 and let its ordered outer vertices be v_1,...,v_m. The fan triangles are (0,v_j,v_{j+1}), j=1,...,m-1; an open sector fan suffices. Suppose each of the even-index and odd-index subsets of outer vertices contains three noncollinear points. Then the map above is surjective onto **all** vector condensed nodal loads on the fan.

To prove this, write a_0 for the root coefficient in an annihilator. The first condition in (2) gives

    a_j=-a_0/2+(-1)^j d                                   (3)

for a fixed vector d. On the triangle (0,v_j,v_{j+1}), the affine nodal field is

    -a_0/2+(3/2)a_0 lambda_0
                  +(-1)^j d(lambda_j-lambda_(j+1)).

Using grad lambda_0=J(v_(j+1)-v_j)/(2 area K) and grad(lambda_j-lambda_(j+1))=-J(v_j+v_(j+1))/(2 area K), its zero curl condition becomes

    [3a_0-2(-1)^j d] dot v_(j+1)
       =[3a_0+2(-1)^j d] dot v_j.                         (4)

Put p=3a_0+2d and q=3a_0-2d. Consecutive equations (4) say

    p dot v_even=q dot v_odd=C                            (5)

with one scalar C along the entire fan chain. Three noncollinear even vertices force p=0 and C=0. Three noncollinear odd vertices force q=0. Therefore a_0=d=0 and every a_j=0. The annihilator is trivial. A proper linear subspace of a finite-dimensional Euclidean space has a nonzero annihilating linear functional; hence the load map is surjective.

The actual toothed fan has many subdivisions on several nonparallel long sides, so both parity subsets contain three noncollinear points once the subdivisions are sufficiently numerous. This is directly checkable in its fixed finite root template and is not affected by later recursion outside that fan.

In particular, for any prescribed vector e there is psi in Cc-infinity(Omega) for which the actual smooth, exactly divergence-free, no-slip u=J grad psi has condensed load e at the root and zero at every other fan vertex. All other mesh condensed loads vanish because u is supported inside Omega. This realizes the full-R_h candidate's root-only vector load without requiring zero bubble loads. Since the source fan is unchanged by recursive child replacement, the same u works at all recursion depths. Multiplication by a bounded depth-dependent scalar g_0 preserves uniform H2 and smooth norms. Constants may depend on the one fixed finite template, which is allowed; they do not depend on recursion depth.

## Exact remaining constrained-projection issue

The conclusion concerns R_h u. The original initialization is P_Zh u. The scalar correction

    P_Zh u=R_hu-R_h grad phi_h

can alter precisely the deep nodal coefficients used in a full-mass lower bound. No estimate here proves that it leaves a bad subtree intact.

There is an exact condensed description of that additional issue. Let a_i be the nodal coefficients of P_Zh u, let beta_K be its bubble coefficient, and write B_K=(u,b_K)_K/|K|. The mixed mass projection equation, with the convention b(v,phi)=(v,grad phi), gives

    beta_K=(280/81) B_K-(14/27)sum_(i in K)a_i
                              -(14/9)grad phi|K.

Consequently

    integral_K P_Zh u
       =|K|[(1/10)sum_(i in K)a_i+(14/9)B_K
                                      -(7/10)grad phi|K].          (6)

Define the usual scalar P1 stiffness K, and define G by

    (G phi)_i=sum_(K incident i)|K|grad phi|K.

With C the actual bubble-condensed vector nodal mass matrix and ell the condensed source load, the global equations are

    C a+(1/10)G phi=ell,
    (7/10)K phi=(1/10)G^T a+F_B,
    F_B(q)=sum_K |K|(14/9)B_K dot grad q|K.                         (7)

Thus eliminating phi gives the positive nodal operator

    C+(1/70)G K^(-1)G^T,                                          (8)

but also changes the right-hand side to ell-(1/7)G K^(-1)F_B. The source's bubble moments cannot be dropped. On arbitrary vector nodal coefficients the positive correction in (8) is at most 3/7 times the C energy: the scalar energy projection bounds the K^(-1) pairing by sum_K |K||sum_i a_i|^2, while the local condensed matrix satisfies C_K[a,a]>=|K||sum_i a_i|^2/30. This is an energy bound only; it gives no relative control of exponentially small descendant coefficients.

In particular the fixed smooth source constructed above has no reason to make F_B vanish. Indeed exact solenoidality gives sum_i(raw nodal load)_i=integral u=0. Since ell_i=(raw nodal load)_i-t sum_(K incident i)(u,b_K)_K, a root-only condensed load e forces

    sum_K (u,b_K)_K=-e/(3t)=-(9/14)e.                              (9)

Testing F_B against an affine q with gradient e already detects this nonzero total bubble moment (where such a q is used in the mean-zero pressure gauge). Therefore the missing constrained transfer cannot be solved simply by retaining the root-only full-mass load and silently setting the pressure-driving bubble moments to zero.

The complete finite-dimensional compatibility test for a desired pair (ell,F_B) is also explicit. Pair it with arbitrary vector nodal coefficients a and scalar nodal coefficients q on the source patch. The associated smooth-source test field is

    w|K=sum_i a_i lambda_i
          +b_K[-(14/27)sum_i a_i+(14/9)grad q|K].                 (10)

Consequently the pair (a,q) annihilates every exact-solenoidal smooth source precisely when

    curl(sum_i a_i lambda_i)=0,
    sum_i a_i=3 grad q|K,                       every source K.   (11)

A candidate (ell,F_B) is realizable if and only if it annihilates every pair satisfying (11). Necessity follows by integration against J grad psi. Sufficiency follows because the range is a subspace of a finite-dimensional space, so every failure of membership has a nonzero annihilator. Equation (11) is thus a complete exact algebraic test, with all bubble-pressure coupling retained. It is not asserted that its solution space consists only of the affine example above.

Equivalently, for a fixed mesh the velocities attainable as P_Zh(J grad psi), psi compactly supported in Omega, are exactly the L2-orthogonal complement inside Z_h of

    {z_h in Z_h: curl z_h=0 in Omega}.                           (12)

This follows from self-adjointness of P_Zh and the same finite-dimensional annihilator argument. It gives an exact test for any proposed constrained bad vector. The current full-mass candidate has not supplied a bad vector in Z_h satisfying that test, nor proved that projection of its existing bad vector retains the required descendant coefficients.

For comparison, the exact local annihilator of source excitation into the constrained space is different: z_h in Z_h is invisible to all u=J grad psi supported in Omega exactly when curl z_h=0 on Omega. The full MINI curl then forces its bubble coefficients to vanish on the source triangles, while its P1 coefficients satisfy only the affine curl condition there. The additional vertex-sum condition in (2) is specific to **condensed nodal loads**, not to arbitrary constrained tests. Thus surjectivity of condensed full-mass loads cannot be recycled as surjectivity onto Z_h, and no constrained bad-mode realization is claimed.
