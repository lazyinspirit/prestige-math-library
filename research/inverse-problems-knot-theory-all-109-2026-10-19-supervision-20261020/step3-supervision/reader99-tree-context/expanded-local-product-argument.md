Choose vertices u,v of tau with d=h(v)-h(u) nonzero. The barycentric weights of both at p are positive. In a sufficiently small neighborhood of p they stay bounded below by a positive eta.

Every incident affine simplex agrees with h on tau, so it has the SAME derivative d along v-u. In any such simplex define Phi_t by subtracting t/d from the u-weight, adding t/d to the v-weight, and leaving every other barycentric weight unchanged. For sufficiently small |t| all weights remain nonnegative, and h(Phi_t(x))=h(x)+t. The formulas agree on common faces: u,v belong to every incident coface of tau and the other weights remain unchanged. Moreover Phi_{-t} is the inverse. Thus (y,t)↦Phi_t(y), for y on h=0, is one PL product collar, with inverse x↦(Phi_{-h(x)}(x),h(x)); it is not a separate sheet construction for each tetrahedron.
