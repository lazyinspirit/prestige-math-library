The relatively open set V=D² minus the union of the original CLOSED D_i contains that closure and lies in P. Therefore closure_X(Z)⊂V⊂int_X(P). Points on the outer circle cause no exception: their neighborhoods are relative half-disk neighborhoods inside D², and the positive buffer from the removed endpoint disks keeps those neighborhoods in P. Excision leaves exactly the finite disjoint union (D_i^+,D_i^+ minus int D_i), as written. The radial collar retracts this pair to the original disk and its boundary, hence to (D²,S¹).
