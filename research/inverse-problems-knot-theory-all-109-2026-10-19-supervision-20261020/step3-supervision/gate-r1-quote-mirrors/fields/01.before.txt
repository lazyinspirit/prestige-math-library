Assume Countable Choice.

Let $(M,g)$ be a compact smooth Riemannian manifold with smooth boundary, with no orientability assumption. Fix a finite smooth atlas of interior and boundary charts and a smooth partition of unity subordinate to it. For an integer $k\geq0$, define $H^k(M)$ as the completion of $C^\infty(M)$ in the sum of the Euclidean $W^{k,2}$ norms of the localized coordinate representatives; derivatives in a boundary chart are taken on the corresponding half-space. Define $H_0^1(M)$ as the closure of $C_c^\infty(M^\circ)$ in $H^1(M)$. Define $H^{1/2}(\partial M)$ by the fixed finite boundary atlas and partition of unity, using the Euclidean fractional Slobodeckij space $W^{1/2,2}(\mathbb R^{n-1})$ for each localized representative. For real scalars, define $H^{-1/2}(\partial M)$ as the continuous linear dual of $H^{1/2}(\partial M)$. For complex scalars, define it as the continuous conjugate-linear dual: its pairing with $h\in H^{1/2}(\partial M)$ is linear in the dual element and conjugate-linear in $h$, using the Riemannian boundary density. These are the fixed-atlas norms used on this page; no atlas-independence assertion is part of this definition.
