# Boundaryless finite two-end coverage: a new geometric construction

Author reconstruction, not independently reviewed. This is the boundaryless ordinary operation only; no codimension-two pair or prescribed side slide is asserted here.

Let Q be a connected smooth five-manifold without boundary. Suppose it has precisely two signed ends, π₁Q=Π finitely presented, and each end has stable fundamental group Π mapping isomorphically to π₁Q. Assume also that each end neighborhood contains a smaller connected neighborhood V for which π₂V→π₂Q is onto. In the intended proper-homotopy-product input this last condition follows by pushing the finite section to the designated end under the proper homotopy inverse. An actual topological compact-section product supplies it directly. Given end neighborhoods U−, U+, the construction gives a compactly supported smooth ambient isotopy f with f(U−)∪U+=Q.

Replace U± by smaller connected end neighborhoods V± with π₁V±→π₁Q isomorphisms and π₂V±→π₂Q onto. Thus (Q,V±) is2-connected. Covering with the smaller neighborhoods suffices. Their existence uses the finite clean-end construction explained below, together with the stipulated π₂-surjectivity condition; it does not assert that each initially supplied arbitrary neighborhood already has these properties.

## A smooth negative cut and its exact connectivity

Inside V− construct a clean negative end neighborhood A with compact connected smooth frontier H, such that H→A and A→Q induce Π-isomorphisms. Its complement W=Q−int A is connected. Here is the complete group ledger, avoiding the additional ambient-boundary premise in Siebenmann1.9(B).

First the stable-end construction of1.9(A) gives a cofinal connected clean neighborhood A₀ with π₁A₀=Π and connected compact frontier H₀. Concretely, in nested0-neighborhoods the stable imageΠ is a retract of the middle neighborhood group. A compact intervening slab expresses that middle group by adjoining finitely many generators and relations to the deeper group. Replacing the finitely many extra generators by words in the stable image gives finitely many normal kernel generators. Represent them by embedded2-disks in a larger end neighborhood and trade their innermost frontier intersections; this produces the stable-image neighborhood. Only2-disks in dimensionfive are used. Frontier components are connected by finite proper arcs, discarding bounded components.

Choose finitely many generators ofΠ as loops in A₀, with paths to H₀, and put their union in general position. Carve thin open neighborhoods of the corresponding proper arcs from A₀. Removing a properly embedded1-dimensional core in dimensionfive has codimensionfour, so paths and nullhomotopy2-disks can be made disjoint from it; the neighborhood group remainsΠ. Each new boundary tube supplies a loop representing the chosen generator, so the resulting compact connected frontier H₁ maps ONTOΠ. Write its finite presentation G=⟨x:r⟩ and a finite presentationΠ=⟨y:s⟩. Choose preimage wordsη_j(x) for the y_j and wordsξ_i(y) for the images of x_i. The kernel is normally generated by the finite list x_i ξ_i(η(x))⁻¹ and s_l(η(x)), as is verified by adjoining y_j=η_j(x) and eliminating the y_j. Thus no infinitely generated kernel is assumed finite.

Represent that list by pairwise disjoint frontier loops and choose proper embedded spanning2-disks in the neighborhood, fixing their boundary collars. General position gives pairwise disjoint disks because2+2<5; individual disks are embedded for the same inequality. Carve their small open tubular neighborhoods. The disk cores have codimensionthree: paths and their homotopy2-disks can be perturbed off them, so this carving again preserves the neighborhood groupΠ. On the frontier each operation is surgery on a circle in a four-manifold, replacing S¹×D³ by D²×S²; Van Kampen quotients its group by precisely the normal closure of that circle. After the finite list, π₁H=Π and H→A is an isomorphism. These codimension-at-least-three surgeries preserve connectedness; retain the connected unbounded neighborhood and frontier. All arcs, disks and thickenings lie in V−. The map A→Q is the original stable-end Π-isomorphism.

The actual full primary source is Siebenmann, *On Detecting Open Collars*,1969, Proposition1.9(A) and its finite disk-trading proof printed210–211, cached `sources/siebenmann-detecting-open-collars-full.pdf`. The subsequent direct arc-and-disk CARVING argument above supplies the frontier isomorphism without invoking1.9(B), whose ambient-boundary isomorphism hypothesis is not available here. No four-dimensional Whitney cancellation is used.

Write V=V−∩W, including its collar at H. Van Kampen applied to Q=W∪A and V−=V∪A shows that π₁W→π₁Q and π₁V→π₁V− are isomorphisms: in each amalgam the map fromπ₁H toπ₁A is an isomorphism. Therefore V→W is a Π-isomorphism.

Excision, applied in the Π-cover with collars at H, gives

H_i(W̃,Ṽ) ≅ H_i(Q̃,Ṽ−).

The right side vanishes for i≤2 because (Q,V−) is2-connected. Since the frontier and end groups are the specified Π groups, these are precisely the universal covers needed for relative Hurewicz. Consequently (W,V) is2-connected. This is the crucial distinction: one does not assume (W,V+) is2-connected after cutting the negative end, and no such assertion is needed.

## Two finite engulfings in the correct order

Choose a locally finite compatible smooth triangulation of W, with H a subcomplex. Put J₁=H∪W^(2), and let J₂ be the subpolyhedron of the barycentric subdivision consisting of simplices disjoint from J₁. Its dimension is≤5−2−1=2. It is closed in Q and disjoint from H. Since W has only the positive end and a compact lower frontier, J₂−V+ is compact.

Apply the explicit finite smooth engulfing construction in the WHOLE Q to the closed2-polyhedron J₂ and the2-connected pair(Q,V+). It gives a compactly supported smooth isotopy h with J₂⊂h(V+). This first move need not fix A or H. Set C=Q−V+ and K=h(C)∩W. The set K is compact: C is bounded on the positive end; h has compact support; W is bounded on the negative end. Also K∩J₂=∅.

The primary/dual barycentric join decomposition now places K in a regular neighborhood of H∪Y for some finite subpolyhedron Y⊂W^(2). Explicitly, in each simplex of the barycentric subdivision, separate its vertices into the initial face in J₁ and the terminal face in J₂. Away from J₂ the coefficient on the initial face is positive. The compact set K is covered by finitely many such simplices. Restrict the J₁ part to their finitely many faces and include the compact H; subdivision and shrinking the terminal join coefficients give a compact derived neighborhood Z with K⊂Z and a finite simplicial collapse Z↘H∪Y. This is the same actual join decomposition used in Hudson7.11, printed172, `sources/hudson-178.png`; it is finite here because K and H are compact.

Use finite smooth engulfing a SECOND time, now in W with boundary H, for the2-connected pair(W,V) proved by excision. The finite2-polyhedron Y meets H only inside V, so the boundary-fixed version gives a compactly supported smooth isotopy k fixing H and putting Y⊂k(V). It extends by the identity across A. The open set k(V−) contains H∪Y. Apply the elementary smooth collapse flows to the finite collapse Z↘H∪Y: inverse flow expands k(V−) to contain Z, fixes H∪Y, and is supported in W away from its boundary: compactness of H gives a uniform collar already contained in k(V−); multiply the collapse fields by a cutoff zero on a smaller collar. Any trajectory arrested in that collar is already in the target open set, and the positive minimum argument on its compact complement is unchanged. Absorb this finite additional motion into k. Thus K⊂k(V−), while A⊂k(V−) because A⊂V− and k fixes A. Hence

h(Q−V+) ⊂ k(V−).

It follows that Q−V+⊂h⁻¹k(V−). The compactly supported diffeomorphism f=h⁻¹k is smoothly isotopic to the identity by concatenating the two finite isotopies and reversing h. Therefore f(U−)∪U+=Q.

The construction never tries to engulf an infinite dual skeleton from a single end of Q: it first makes that skeleton closed and bounded on the negative side by the smooth frontier H. It also never asks the first move to preserve H. The second move alone preserves the negative cut; its connectivity comes from universal-cover excision.

## Scope still outstanding

The finite smooth engulf and collapse modules are NEW author constructions; they have not received independent review. The finite end-cut construction is reconstructed above from the actual primary argument, not adopted from the separately frozen q6/q7 S theorem. It needs an explicit countable-template/least-choice packaging for an ACω-only global use; all moves for a fixed instance are finite.

For the marked-torus consumer, the ordinary manifold is an exterior and has side boundary. One must supply the relative version with a single common R slide on every side component, then use the stable owner's tube-extension reduction. The proof above does not silently fix a codimension-two core or establish that boundary prescription. In particular a relative primary/dual decomposition must keep the compact side portion of K within the already covered boundary germ. Until that relative construction and the foundational packaging are supplied and reviewed, original E remains conditional.
