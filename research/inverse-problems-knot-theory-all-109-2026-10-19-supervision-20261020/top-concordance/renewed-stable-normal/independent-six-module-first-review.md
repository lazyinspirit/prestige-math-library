## Consolidated findings

### SN1 — Missing actual marked furling and comparison construction

Location: five-dimensional device, `Interfaces of the two separately owned
operations`, together with the last boundary-normalization paragraph of the
stretching module.

The stretching proof does prove end-compression diffeomorphisms from the stated
finite table, with the forward/inverse stabilization and BASE-product control
it claims. It does not yet construct the compact smooth pair furl from those
maps or establish the relative comparison between that original-dimensional
furl and a furl obtained from the later S product. These are used as completed
steps in the device:

* The first paragraph obtains a structure Σ1 on the compact torus pair by
  “the relative pair engulfing construction.” The subsequent two original-
  dimensional unroll/refurl operations similarly produce Σ2. No explicit
  quotient atlas, underlying standard pair identification, or required
  relative central-box identification is supplied in these six modules.
* Most importantly, S gives smooth product structures on the unrolled
  seven- and six-dimensional manifolds. It does not itself give the claimed
  compact diffeomorphism f:V×T²→(Σ2×T²_BASE). To infer that map one must compare
  the S-product furlings with the already chosen original-dimensional
  furlings, keeping the specified side germ and both NORMAL covering classes.
  Identifying an end generator or naming the lift criterion does not perform
  this comparison or make a chosen product map descend through an unrelated
  deck transformation.

This is an actual missing local argument at the bridge between the adopted S
contract and the new compact construction, not an objection to the supplied S
or F theorem. E is finite coverage, not a furnished marked uniqueness-of-furling
contract. The source summary's phrase “uniqueness of furling” is not a proof.

Focused repair: construct the smooth compact quotient and its topological
marked-pair identification from the exact end maps, relative to the central
box and side collar; then supply the exact comparison needed for the two S
splits. Track both normal cohomology classes through that comparison and show
the final target remains the original furl times the BASE T². Do not simply
assume an arbitrary product diffeomorphism is deck-equivariant, or perform a
new seven-dimensional furl mixing BASE factors. If a new genuine prerequisite
is needed, identify it explicitly rather than describing it as part of E.

countable handle smoothing on an overlap, and the last paragraph of the

Focused repair: give a finite-level schedule or a genuine deterministic

may be iterated deterministically once its coordinate data have been chosen.

### SN4 — Nonblocking precision in the Kister induction

The simultaneous existence/uniqueness induction is a plausible closed route,
but expose its exact use at the boundary of a d-simplex: use U_(d−1) to
identify the old admissible bundle there with the chart's trivial bundle
before writing a global family g_p:R^q→R^q. Its existence is not immediate
from a fiberwise inclusion alone. The last paragraph does announce this use,
so this is a clarification rather than a separate mathematical rejection.
In the uniqueness paragraph describe the global map as successive
REPLACEMENTS of a fiberwise inclusion by D(g_p,u(p)), not a composition of
maps whose domain and codomain are two different bundles. The onto-preserving
property then gives the advertised induction.
