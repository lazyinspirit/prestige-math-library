or of Armstrong and Zeeman [6]. It is just a filtered topological space having a few

submersions. Although the definition is complex, it is so formal that one can define 
piecewise-linear and topological stratified sets in the sense of Thorn. Our CS sets 
are simpler, cruder and (as unfiltered spaces) slightly more general [33].
