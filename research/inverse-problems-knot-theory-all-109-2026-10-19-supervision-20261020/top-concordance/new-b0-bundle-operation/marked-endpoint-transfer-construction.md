## Exact family and required conclusion

Let Q=T5 minus {p}. Let f:P∞→N be the actual CE map, where P∞ has its supplied DIFF5 structure, and let H:I×R5→I×N be the open embedding supplied by an isotopy of the chosen target chart. Put

V'={(t,x) in I×P∞ : f(x) lies in H_t(R5)},

and f'(t,x)=(t,H_t^{-1}f(x)). Pull this back along id_I×alpha, where alpha:Q→R5 is the actual marked punctured-torus local homeomorphism. Denote the pullback by E=W0', its projection to I by p_E, and the resulting map to I×Q by G. Write X_t=p_E^{-1}(t) and g_t=G|X_t. The actual data say G is proper CE, g_1 is a homeomorphism, and X_0 is the punctured auxiliary manifold.

The needed conclusion is a homeomorphism h:X_0→X_1 with g_1 h homotopic to g_0. This suffices; no continuous choice of the h_t and no bundle projection on all E is required. Its proper one-point extension gives the marked closed auxiliary homeomorphism to T5.

## Construction from those inputs

1. The smooth family is actual. V' is open in I×P∞, so its projection is the restriction of a smooth submersion. The pullback map beta:E→V' is a local homeomorphism. Transfer the DIFF6 atlas through its local inverse branches: beta is then a local diffeomorphism, and p_E is a smooth submersion with DIFF5 fibers, including the standard one-sided interpretation at the ends of I. This does not require f, H or the target N to be smooth. The projection p_E is nonproper and no proper-submersion theorem is applied to it.

2. Each g_t is proper CE. Apply (C) to g_t and its open restrictions. In particular g_t:X_t→Q is an ordinary homotopy equivalence. For a decreasing nested family of punctured target balls U_n about p, g_t^{-1}(U_n) is homotopy equivalent to U_n≈S4×R and is connected and simply connected. Properness makes these sets cofinal neighborhoods of infinity: every compact subset of X_t maps into a compact subset of Q, so misses a sufficiently small U_n; conversely g_t^{-1}(Q minus U_n) is compact. Their connectedness and trivial fundamental groups prove one-endedness and 1-LC at that end directly. No preservation-of-ends theorem for a supposed proper homotopy inverse is needed.

3. By (E), any such annular preimage g_t^{-1}(U_n) is an actual S4×R end neighborhood. Choose the product end corresponding to escape toward p in Q. Properness and cofinality ensure a sufficiently small punctured target ball has preimage inside that positive half of the product. Taking a cross-section therefore cuts off an end collar whose complement is contained in the compact preimage of the complement of that smaller target ball. Thus X_t has a compact core D_t with collared boundary S4, and inclusion D_t→X_t is a homotopy equivalence. Here and below a compact core is a compact TOP codimension-zero submanifold. Its boundary need not be a smooth hypersurface: the later ambient smooth flow acts on its whole compact set and preserves its locally flat collar topologically.

4. Fix one parameter u and one such core D⊂X_u. Choose a smooth horizontal vector field on a neighborhood of D in E with dp_E(v)=1. In finitely many submersion charts this is supplied by the parameter coordinate vector field; a finite partition of unity on a slightly larger relatively compact neighborhood preserves dp_E(v)=1. Compactness gives a parameter interval J about u on which its flow is defined on all of D and a neighborhood of D. Denote the resulting embeddings by phi_t:D→X_t, phi_u=inclusion, and their images by D(t). They carry the actual boundary collar as well. This is only compact flow existence, not a claim that a horizontal field on all of E is complete.

5. The map (t,x)↦g_t(phi_t(x)) is continuous on J×D. It supplies a homotopy between g_t phi_t and g_u|D. Since g_u|D is a homotopy equivalence, so is g_t phi_t. Since g_t itself is a homotopy equivalence, the inclusion D(t)→X_t is a homotopy equivalence. This uses the actual common target Q and not a freely chosen desired marking.

6. Let Y(t)=closure(X_t minus int D(t)). It is connected. Indeed its connected collared boundary S4 meets every component; a component missing the boundary would be both open and closed in connected X_t. Van Kampen for X_t=D(t) union Y(t), with its collared simply connected intersection S4, gives pi1(X_t)=pi1(D(t))*pi1(Y(t)). Since the first free factor includes isomorphically by step 5, pi1(Y(t)) is trivial. Excision and step 5 give H_*(Y(t),S4)=H_*(X_t,D(t))=0. Relative Hurewicz, inductively, then gives that S4→Y(t) is a homotopy equivalence, since both spaces are simply connected and have manifold CW type. Removing D(t) does not change the cofinal neighborhoods at infinity. Thus Y(t) has one end, and it is 1-LC by step 2.

7. Apply the HALF form of (E), not merely open two-end recognition, to Y(t). Obtain an end product S4×[0,infinity)→Y(t). Any initial boundary parametrization can be made the prescribed one by precomposing with a constant-in-the-second-coordinate homeomorphism of S4. Thus it agrees on the boundary with phi_t and with the corresponding end product at u. Glue phi_t on D to the two end products to obtain a homeomorphism H_{u,t}:X_u→X_t extending phi_t|D. No continuity in t of these end homeomorphisms is assumed or selected.

8. The restrictions of g_t H_{u,t} and g_u to D are homotopic by step 5. Because D→X_u is a homotopy equivalence, these two maps are homotopic on X_u. One can verify this directly by precomposing their restriction homotopy with a homotopy inverse X_u→D and concatenating the two homotopies from the composite to the identity. Thus H_{u,t} preserves the actual homotopy marking to Q, not just the abstract homeomorphism type.

9. Define two parameters to be equivalent if their marked fibers are related by a homeomorphism preserving this homotopy class. This is an equivalence relation and every class is open by steps 4–8. Since I is connected it has only one such class. Consequently there exists a marked homeomorphism H:X_0→X_1. Equivalently one may take a finite overlapping chain of the local intervals and compose the finitely many local homeomorphisms. This argument never makes an uncountable selection of end parametrizations.

10. A homeomorphism of locally compact Hausdorff spaces is proper, so H extends to their one-point compactifications. The collared S4 ends make these compactifications TOP5 manifolds. Since g_1 is the specified homeomorphism to Q, the extension of g_1 H is a homeomorphism X_0^+→T5. The open marking homotopy proves equality on pi1. Adding the single standard end point does not change pi1, by the S4 collar and van Kampen. Since T5 is aspherical, the resulting closed homeomorphism is homotopic to the extended original CE marking. This is exactly the marked zero-handle homeomorphism required by the torus argument.

## Relative scope and status
