# Actual finite fundamental-group comparison through kernel cuts

This is a NEW input for the integrated product criterion, not a reopening of
the completed local-review histories. It proves a finite surgery comparison.
It does not infer that an arbitrary end neighborhood has the fundamental
group of the product end. All manifolds, surgery tubes, normal-form
identifications and local nullhomotopies below are actual supplied data.

Put m=n−1≥5. Let A be the smooth old frontier, and let X be its outgoing
level after the lower (n−3)-handles. Thus X is obtained from A by the actual
surgeries

S^(m−3)×D³ replaced by D^(m−2)×S².

Let B be a finite disjoint union of framed (m−2)-spheres which are the
attaching spheres of the selected kernel (n−2)-handles. Suppose the actual
Whitney/normal-form operation has made B disjoint from the lower belt
S²-spheres, and its actual radial identification has put B in the common
part Q between A and X, away from the lower attaching tubes. This is an
actual handle-normal-form input, not just an isotopy class of abstract spheres.

Also suppose the upper description of X supplies the complement comparison

π₁(X−B)→π₁(X)

as an isomorphism, with its actual based-path/nullhomotopy witnesses. The
accepted local belt-complement contract supplies exactly this when X is
obtained from the upper old frontier by two-handle surgeries whose attaching
loops have actual nullhomotopy disks in that upper frontier. It applies to
any selected subset of the belt spheres, so B need not be the entire family.
No loop is declared null merely because it is close in the control base.

## Transfer the complement comparison to the old frontier

First all four maps

π₁(Q)→π₁(A),  π₁(Q)→π₁(X),
π₁(Q−B)→π₁(A−B),  π₁(Q−B)→π₁(X−B)

are isomorphisms. To see this, attach the finitely many removed or replacement
pieces one at a time. Their attaching intersections are
S^(m−3)×S², and both the old pieces S^(m−3)×D³ and the replacement pieces
D^(m−2)×S² are simply connected since m−3≥2. The intersections are
connected and simply connected as well. Van Kampen proves the assertions.
The same calculation holds with B removed because all the surgery pieces
and their collars are disjoint from the actual B. Removing these codimension-
three core spheres does not disconnect the common part: paths can avoid
them, since 1+(m−3)<m. Use the supplied paths to compare basepoints.

The square of the above comparison maps commutes with inclusions of the
B-complements. Since the right vertical map π₁(X−B)→π₁(X) is an
isomorphism by its supplied actual upper-belt resource, so is

π₁(A−B)→π₁(A).

This statement concerns the actual B transported into the common part by
the supplied normal-form identification. If that identification is expressed
by an outgoing-boundary diffeomorphism, carry B, its tubes, base paths and
upper nullhomotopy witnesses by that same diffeomorphism. A different
unmarked representative cannot silently replace it.

## Perform the kernel cut

Deleting the kernel (n−2)-handle interiors from the end changes its frontier
by the actual surgeries

B_j×D² replaced by D^(m−1)×S¹.

Write A' for the resulting frontier. The common part obtained by removing
the interiors of the B_j tubes has the same fundamental group as A−B,
by radial motion in the punctured normal two-disks. Each new piece has
fundamental group Z, and its intersection B_j×S¹ has fundamental group Z
as well because B_j=S^(m−2) is simply connected. Inclusion of the
intersection into D^(m−1)×S¹ is an isomorphism on π₁. Van Kampen therefore
gives an isomorphism from the common part to A'. Combining the comparisons,

π₁(A') ≅ π₁(A−B) ≅ π₁(A).

In particular no unproved claim that the newly visible meridian loops are
trivial has been used. Their triviality in the complement is precisely the
previously supplied upper-belt complement resource transferred above.

Zero stabilizations delete lower (n−3)-handles. Their frontier effect is
exactly the codimension-three surgery used in the first step, so it too
preserves the fundamental group. Identity-pair stabilization changes only
the presentation. Changes by actual boundary diffeomorphisms carry the
comparison witnesses. Slides require their actual supplied paths and the
carried disk witnesses; algebraic propagation alone does not preserve this
geometric resource.

## Keep the upper two-handle nullhomotopies through the actual slides

For clarity, the upper resource need not be reinvented after each move.
A change of the lower handle presentation leaves the actual upper two-handle
cobordism unchanged; if boundary coordinates are changed, carry its entire
attaching data and disk witnesses by the same actual diffeomorphism.

For an upper two-handle slide along an actual band path γ in the upper old
frontier, the new attaching loop is the band sum of λ_a with a conjugate
of λ_b or λ_b⁻¹. Its actual nullhomotopy is the boundary-connected sum of
the supplied disk maps for those two loops, joined along the specified
band γ. The resulting map is a disk map; it need not embed. It stays in
the union of those two disks, the band and their chosen thin collars.
Orientations and the choice of inverse are carried by the actual slide.
This gives the required witness, rather than only the assertion that a
word in null classes is null. New cancelling pairs in a supplied standard
small product chart have the standard small disk for the new upper attaching
loop.

If an actual slide arc is initially supplied on the intermediate X instead,
its one-dimensional interior can first be made disjoint from every upper
belt B_j. The inequality is 1+(m−2)<m, and its endpoint collars are kept
in their chosen normal-ray models. In each upper surgery piece
D²×S^(m−2), radial motion in D²−{0} pushes the arc to
S¹×S^(m−2), which is the old attaching boundary on the upper frontier.
At an endpoint on B_j use its specified short normal-ray push-off first.
Thus the arc, with its endpoint paths, is transported to the actual upper
frontier through the actual two-handle product coordinates. Its based label
is carried by those same paths. This is not a choice of an unrelated path
with nearby projected endpoints. The full tubes and radial tracks must be
inside the designated control package, so this transfer has the reserved
handle-diameter allowance. The band-disk construction then gives the new
upper nullhomotopy witness there.

Consequently slides retain the upper resource when these actual models,
paths and disk maps are supplied and carried. An algebraic factorization
without geometric arcs still supplies none of this. Nor does this argument
create an operated-slab reference channel from the initial θ by itself.

## Controlled local form and the actual lifting resource

The calculation applies in an actual open local surgery package A⁺,X⁺
provided it contains the full lower surgery tubes, the B tubes, the radial
normal-form tracks, the upper two-handle tubes and their actual nullhomotopy
disks. Require these packages to project into one prescribed enlarged control
set. The identical finite Van Kampen calculation then gives the comparisons
inside those packages. The nullhomotopies and based paths used in the
comparison remain there. This is an input about full supports; it is not
implied by endpoint proximity or by the initial product homeomorphism.

Consequently a controlled path/π₁ lifting resource on the old frontier is
retained on the new frontier, with these reserved enlarged buffers. For
paths use the common-part representatives given by the gluing descriptions.
For a new-frontier loop, push its finitely many portions in the new pieces
into their attaching intersections; the maps on π₁ above justify and realize
this. Its common-part representative is a loop of A. If it has a supplied
old-frontier nullhomotopy, the transferred complement injection gives a
nullhomotopy in A−B, hence in the common part and then in A'. All maps are
inside the designated local package. Finite relative two-complex lifting is
obtained cell by cell from those path and loop operations, retaining its
already specified boundary data. A finite subdivision allocates the cells
to the chosen control sets. Thus this is actual local path/nullhomotopy
transport, rather than a bare unmarked abstract group isomorphism.

The earlier frontier-improvement output can serve as the old-frontier
resource at its exact reviewed hypotheses. This finite comparison then
passes that resource through zero stabilizations and the final kernel cuts.
After the finite approximate completion supplies its actual deformation
U→A', composing that deformation with these frontier path/nullhomotopy
resources gives the corresponding actual comparison for U. It is not
inferred solely from U being a neighborhood of the product end.

Every operation here is finite, uses actual smooth surgery models, and adds
no arbitrary family choice. It retains the protected germ when all supplied
supports avoid its smaller protected open neighborhood. This new lemma needs
its own integration review at these exact inputs; it does not declare the
external actual local support packages or upper nullhomotopy disks automatic.
