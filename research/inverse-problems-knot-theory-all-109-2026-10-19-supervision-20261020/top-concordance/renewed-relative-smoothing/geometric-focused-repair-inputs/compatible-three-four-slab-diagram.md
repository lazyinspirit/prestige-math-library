# One actual compatible finite three-/four-handle slab diagram

This is the focused repair of geometric first-review finding 2. It supplements
the frozen n=6 inputs without replacing their history. It works in an actual
smooth six-dimensional product end Θ:P→B×R and uses the finite frontier
improvement operations already described. Matrix-to-arcs realization and
the two-ended controlled slab product remain separately owned operations.

## Use the W_j=∅ conclusion on the same frontier

In the special product-end problem every inner exclusion W_j is empty.
The excision/Hurewicz maps in frontier improvement therefore have the form

(U−W_a,A)→(U−W_b,A) = id_(U,A),

where A=∂₀U is the single newly constructed frontier. Thus the output is
actual relative controlled three-connectivity of **that same pair** (U,A),
not only a map to a different frontier. To see why the finite generators
suffice, put every cut below a reserved product-tail level. A vertical clamp
above all the cuts is the identity on the modified frontier and dominates
the whole end by a finite slab. Its finite core generators therefore generate
the required relative homology of the end. Excision for the actual cut disks
kills those generators; with W_a=W_b=∅ the resulting homological map is
the identity of the new pair. The finite controlled Hurewicz operation
applied to repeated identities supplies the claimed actual pair contraction.
No implication from a map between distinct pairs to a same-frontier
contraction is being used.

Retain three output neighborhoods U_i⊃U_j⊃U_l from one sufficiently long
improved tame structure, with frontiers A_i,A_j,A_l. Each frontier has the
actual approximate two-complex lifting property; each same pair (U_r,A_r)
is relatively controlled three-connected; the buffered annular inclusions
retain the original controlled one-connectedness. Choose the input levels
in separated height subintervals of a fixed band. All the finite frontier
cuts lie between their prescribed outer and inner levels, so their outputs
retain these separated bounds. Arbitrarily long finite initial structures
are available in any such subinterval by the explicit product construction.

## Obtain compatible presentations of two consecutive slabs

Put K=closure(U_i−U_j) and J=closure(U_j−U_l). The buffered relative
homology of (U_i,K) is that of (U_j,A_j), by excision. It vanishes through
degree three by the actual same-frontier conclusion above. The actual
one-connected annular/cover maps are retained by the tame structure, so
the finite controlled Hurewicz operation makes (U_i,K) relatively controlled
three-connected, after the prescribed buffer thinning.

Here is the actual relative-two contraction for (K,A_i). Contract a given
finite relative two-complex in (U_i,A_i). For each disk, its contraction,
with initial disk and terminal cap, is a relative three-disk in (U_i,K).
The latter relative three-connectivity moves this three-disk into K while
fixing its boundary. It is then the required contraction in (K,A_i).
This tracks the same frontier A_i throughout. Track allowances add only the
two preselected contraction bounds. The corresponding argument applies to
(J,A_j).

At the other end, the actual annular lifting property and the lifting
property of A_j give relative controlled one-connectivity of (K,A_j):
lift a close frontier path with the specified endpoints, use local
1-connectedness of the base to compare its projection with the original
path, and lift that finite two-complex comparison inside the buffered
annulus. This is the degree-one construction of the finite controlled
Hurewicz argument. Likewise (J,A_l) is relatively one-connected.

Apply the zero-/one-/two-handle operations from the bottom of K, and the
zero-/one-handle operations from its top. Their index budget leaves only
degrees three and four. Retain the boundary collars A_i and A_j exactly.
Do the same on J, retaining A_j and A_l exactly. These presentations are
not unrelated decompositions of the same slab: K and J are consecutive
actual submanifolds, and their product collars at their common actual A_j
are the same collar. Concatenating gives L=K∪_(A_j)J with only degrees
three and four and a presentation extending the chosen presentation of K.
Thus there is no degree-five chain-homotopy term to discard.

## Choose the domination and skeleton contraction without losing the band

Choose c strictly above the height bound of every modification of A_i,
and strictly below the lower height bound of A_j. The entire product tail
at height at least c lies in U_i. The actual formula

r_s(x,t)=(x,(1−s)t+s min(t,c))

in Θ coordinates, with points below c fixed, is a homotopy in U_i relative
A_i. It is exactly zero-controlled over B. Its terminal image lies in K.
Every point of K has height below the upper frontier bound of A_j. Choose
the lower bound of A_l above that upper bound. The tracks r_s(K) then lie
in L. These requirements use the reserved gaps between the three finite
height subintervals; no output frontier is allowed to drift out of the
original band.

Let g_s be the actual controlled contraction of K's three-skeleton into
A_i supplied by the same-pair relative three-connectivity of (U_i,A_i),
extended over K by finite controlled HEP. Define h=r g_1:K→K.
Use the homotopy

r_s|K, followed by r g_s,

from inclusion to h. Its first part stays in L by the preceding height
bounds. Its second part stays in K because r(U_i)⊂K. The original g_s
may enter deeper parts of the end; it is **not** included as an uncomposed
part of this containing-slab homotopy. This is why choosing L after an
arbitrary HEP extension is unnecessary. Since r preserves the B-coordinate,
it does not enlarge g_s's projected control.

The terminal selfmap h sends K's three-skeleton into A_i. Cellularize it
inside K and the displayed homotopy inside the actual compatible L. The
chain-map and chain-homotopy identities now give dh₄=0 and Td=1−h₄ on
the actual first degree-four summand. Consequently h₄²=h₄ and
im(h₄)=ker(d). Every object required by that calculation—frontier,
domination, same-frontier contraction, first presentation and compatible
containing presentation—has now been specified in one finite diagram.

## Regular-value normal form and framing of the three-disk bands

Here are the disk details used in the index-two and relative-degree-three
operations. For each existing three-handle D³_core×D³_normal, put the
finite disk maps in general position and choose a regular value of their
projection to its core, avoiding the finitely many projected double points.
The preimage is finite. Inverse-function neighborhoods give disjoint
preimage three-balls over a sufficiently small common core ball; compactness
excludes further preimages. Each is an embedded graph over that core ball,
and different graphs are disjoint there because their normal coordinates
at the regular value are distinct.

In the product handle, homotope those finitely many graphs to parallel
core disks, choosing distinct normal points on the outgoing normal sphere.
The complementary domain lies outside the smaller core ball, so radial
motion of its core coordinate sends it to the old attaching side. This is
a homotopy of maps in the handle; it is not incorrectly called an ambient
homeomorphism collapsing an annulus. The selected outgoing parallel core
disks are actual embedded disks with their product normal frames. Their
boundary homotopies are retained on the old level.

Join the selected preimage balls by finite trees. Embed the image arcs
disjointly in the level, avoiding the actual parallel disks, using
1+3<5 and 1+1<5. Thin bands along these arcs give the actual embedded
three-disks used in the construction. Their rank-two normal frames in the
outgoing boundary extend across the interval bands; with the collar line
they give the rank-three attaching frames. Orient the finitely many bands
consistently with their disk trees. Nothing here asserts that arbitrary
three-disk maps embed in six dimensions.

When a boundary Whitney isotopy is performed on the old level, extend it
through its boundary collar and carry all later attaching maps with it.
The glued handle maps identify the old and new outgoing boundaries; carry
the actual embedded band disks and their frames by that diffeomorphism.
They remain embedded, disjoint and framed. Their moved boundary spheres
are therefore still boundaries of the actual disks used to add cancelling
handle pairs, rather than only abstractly homotopic spheres.
