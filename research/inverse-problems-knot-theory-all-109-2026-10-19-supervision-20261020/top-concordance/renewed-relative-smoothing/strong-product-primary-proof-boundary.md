## Actual proof route now read

I5–17 gives the transverse-vector-field smoothing engine: straighten finitely many nonvanishing field charts, represent the hypersurface as graph functions, smooth one graph at a time while retaining previously smooth regions, and use integral curves to build the nonsingular PD interpolation. I17 states the relative form on an already smooth embedded part. Its inputs are a compact PD hypersurface and an actual transverse field. It does not itself create that field for an arbitrary product collar.

III1–4 reduces smoothing a PD embedding V×I→W to making its zero slice transverse to a smooth field. III5–9 performs the reduction using a second-derived regular-neighborhood boundary. Its join-line field has a positive secant-angle bound after a finite nonsingular linear approximation; approximation of that field by a smooth one preserves transversality. The collar-boundary push is a PD ambient isotopy with support control. Applying I and then the elementary comparison of two collars gives the codimension-one smoothing. III1–2 includes an existing smooth subembedding; III9 explains which earlier relative clauses have to be used.

IV2–5 proves uniqueness of two such smoothings by applying the relative codimension-one result to their concatenated PD isotopy×I, stationary near its two parameter ends. The resulting smooth zero-slice is a concordance with the ACTUAL endpoint markings. This is stronger than an unmarked assertion that two low-dimensional spheres have the same DIFF type. IV1 explicitly does not invoke Smale h-cobordism triviality.

IV5 treats boundary and stronger relative variants; IV5–6 states and proves the finite I^r version by induction, since the preceding V×I^(r−1) has boundary. There is NO dimension cutoff in this argument: K=S0,S1,S2 and cylinders may be low-dimensional, while the stabilized ambient dimension is any finite value. Thus this route does not depend on a high-dimensional classification/fibering theorem or a restriction that the stabilized source remain dimension≤3.

To obtain LR's entire R^r-domain product-embedding formulation from the compact I^r local statement, one still needs the actual PD collar/radial-compression and relative localization steps. Ordinary existence near the compact zero slice is suggested by IV's theorem. Exact preservation on an already prescribed OPEN product subset, or a sliced parameter family, is not to be inferred merely from that suggestion. IV's uniqueness proof does supply a two-end-marked concordance; it is not a general arbitrary-parameter fibration theorem.
