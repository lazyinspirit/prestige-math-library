# Finite relative domination keeping the entire collared subspace

This is a NEW compact-pair construction, not an assertion that a noncompact end has already supplied a compact truncation or controlled collar. No accepted supplier body is reread or amended.

Assume D is an actual compact manifold with boundary and A is a closed union of incoming faces admitting an actual collar c:A×[0,2]→D with c(a,0)=a. In the cornered case the collar and the permitted protected faces must already be compatible; no corner collar is inferred here. The retained choice-free Statement `thm-compact-manifolds-are-retracts-of-finite-polyhedra-without-choice` supplies a finite Euclidean cubical complex L and actual maps i:D→L, r:L→D with ri=id_D. Only this Statement is consumed. Then there is a finite relative CW space T over the ENTIRE actual A, maps v:D→T and u:T→D restricting to id_A, and an actual homotopy id_D≃uv relative A. It uses only finite choices beyond the supplied retract and collar. Its nonconstant tracks lie in arbitrarily short supplied collar fibers of A; it does not create control for the map u on the other relative cells.

To construct it, choose a collar depth d>0 within the given collar. On c(A×[0,d)) write t for the collar coordinate. The compact subset i(A) of L has an open neighborhood O whose closure is contained in r^{-1}(c(A×[0,d))). Shrink O if needed. By sufficiently many cubical subdivisions of the finite L, choose a finite subcomplex L_A with i(A)⊂int_L(L_A) and L_A⊂O. The existence follows directly from the finite mesh tending to zero and the positive distance of compact i(A) from L−O. Choose a continuous function β:L→[0,1] equal to one on L_A and zero off O, for example the ratio of distances to the disjoint closed sets L_A and L−O.

Define r_s:L→D by r_s(x)=r(x) off O and, when r(x)=c(a,t),

r_s(x)=c(a,(1−s β(x))t),   0≤s≤1.

These formulas glue continuously because β vanishes off O; a collar chart is only used where r(x) lies in its domain. At every i(a), t=0, so r_s i(a)=a for all s. Put r'=r_1. Then r'(L_A)⊂A, and r_s i is an actual homotopy id_D≃r'i relative A. Each moved point follows ONE original collar fiber, without a composition proportional to the number of cells. If the chosen collar fibers have small control image under q:D→B, the homotopy has that same control bound. A shorter d gives the requested finite compact-pair collar allowance whenever the supplied q and collar permit it.

Form the adjunction space T=A∪_{r'|L_A}L. Since (L,L_A) is a finite CW pair, T is obtained from the actual space A by attaching the finitely many cells of L−L_A. This is a finite relative CW construction; A itself is neither replaced nor asserted to have a CW structure. The copy of A is embedded: for instance the equivalence relation on the compact Hausdorff disjoint union A⊔L is closed, since only the compact subcomplex L_A is identified with its image under the continuous map r'|L_A. Thus the quotient is Hausdorff and its continuous inclusion of compact A is an embedding.

Let q_L:L→T be the quotient map. Define u on A by identity and on L by r'; these agree on L_A, so u exists continuously. Set v=q_L i. For a∈A, i(a)∈L_A and q_L i(a)=r'i(a)=a in the copy of A. Hence v|A=id_A, u|A=id_A, and uv=r'i. The displayed collar homotopy proves the actual relative domination over all of A.

There is NO dimension bound ≤dim(D) in this construction: the supplied finite cubical L can have larger dimension. Consequently it does not yet fit the adopted H_i constructor, whose supplied relative nullwitnesses only extend through source dimension n. Taking an n-skeleton does not automatically preserve the actual domination; no such truncation is claimed. An independent dimension reduction or the actual n-dimensional source handle collapse is still required.

For end-deformation use, additional application data remain explicit: an actual compact truncation D containing the desired entire incoming frontier portion, compatible side/protected collars, the extension by identity or product on the rest of the end, and control of each characteristic map u on its finite cells. Arbitrarily small collar tracks alone do not imply that r'(cell) has arbitrarily small control diameter. The separate source filtration/control packages supply that missing cellwise information when needed. This lemma therefore supplies an actual relative finite model, not a whole-end controlled H_i and not the index-sorted surgery filtration.
