# Actual bottom slab retraction from two nested approximate completions

This finite NEW operation supplies a product-slot input. It is not an
assertion that the earlier generic three-/four-handle normal form is acyclic.
It assumes the actual approximate-completion homotopies, not a tame push or
vanishing homology stated without its witnesses.

Let P be the actual ambient smooth manifold, possibly with boundary. Supply
closed codimension-zero end neighborhoods U_i,U_j whose actual relative
frontiers A_i,A_j are smooth frontier faces. Assume the STRICT nesting

U_j ⊂ int_P(U_i),

where int_P is interior relative to the ambient P, so this condition also
has its literal meaning on ambient boundary faces. Put

K=U_i−int_P(U_j).

Require this to be the actual slab with frontier faces A_i,A_j and their
specified compatible collars. In particular U_i=K∪U_j, K∩U_j=A_j,
A_j⊂K and A_i∩U_j=∅. These are honest frontier/slab conditions, not
consequences of the weaker U_i⊃int_P(U_j) alone. In the compact application
K is compact; the local application supplies the actual finite operating
slab package and its buffers.

Supply ACTUAL homotopies

H_i:U_i×I→U_i,   H_j:U_j×I→U_j

beginning at the identities, fixing the respective frontier faces pointwise,
and ending in those faces. These strong homotopies are inputs which must
actually be exported by the finite approximate-completion construction.
They are not inferred from merely naming a neighborhood an approximate
completion. Suppose their projected tracks have diameter at most η_i and
η_j. The local over-a-compact-set version requires them on sufficiently
large open buffers to contain every track used below; the maps are not
extended to points outside their actual domains.

Strict nesting is required also on the protected product region. Choose
the two frontier sections at distinct prescribed heights in its same known
product collar; their product germs agree while the frontier point sets
are disjoint. If a relative formulation instead allows coincident protected
frontier patches, this lemma does not apply there without a separately
specified active-slab/gluing construction. No such variant is assumed here.

Define R_s:U_i→U_i by the identity on K and H_j(−,s) on U_j. Both formulas
are the identity on their overlap A_j, so the finite closed-set pasting lemma
gives an actual continuous homotopy. It stays in U_i, fixes K, and its final
map R has image in K. Thus it is an actual retraction of U_i onto K, with
a specified homotopy and track bound η_j. No slab product has been used.

Now define

G_s(x)=R(H_i(x,s)),   x∈K.

At s=0 this equals x because R fixes K. At s=1 its image is in A_i,
because H_i ends there and R fixes A_i. For x∈A_i it equals x at all times.
Every value is in K, by the range of R. Consequently G is an actual relative
homotopy retraction of K onto A_i. Its projected track diameter is at most
η_i+2η_j: each R(H_i(x,s)) is within η_j of H_i(x,s), and the latter
points have diameter at most η_i. It inherits the protected product agreement
of the two supplied homotopies. In the local version first reserve these
three allowances inside the larger buffers, then restrict the result to
the smaller finite operating package.

Suppose, separately, an actual small relative handle presentation of (K,A_i)
has been supplied with only degrees q,q+1 (q=n−3 for the intended product).
Cellularize the actual homotopy G relative A_i and its endpoints using those
same handles. The relative terminal map has zero chain map. Thus its actual
cellular chain homotopy S satisfies dS+Sd=1. There is only one possible
nonzero homotopy component,

g=S_q:C_q(K,A_i)→C_(q+1)(K,A_i),

and the two identities are

dg=1 on C_q,   gd=1 on C_(q+1).

Hence the actual boundary matrix d is an isomorphism with actual inverse g.
These identities are not inferred merely from a rank equality or from an
idempotent kernel projection. Finite free group-ring ranks agree by applying
augmentation and then rational dimension, so the matrix is square as well.

The inverse is controlled by the actual cellular homotopy: a coefficient
joins only handles encountered by one of its tracks, with the actual lifted
track supplying the group label in the given reference marking. The finite
cellularization pushes across the same small handles; its dimension-dependent
finite handle-diameter allowance must be reserved before choosing tolerances.
The matrices do not export geometric slide arcs for arbitrary elementary
factorizations; that is the separately owned path-realization operation.

There is no conclusion here about the upper-end relative homotopy or its
belt-complement resource. Those require the actual frontier fundamental-group
comparison and the appropriate local nullhomotopies. This lemma only supplies
the bottom retraction and, once the genuine two-degree normal form is present,
the boundary-matrix isomorphism and its controlled inverse. All added choices
are finite, so it is available under Countable Choice at these exact inputs.
It is a new input for integration review, not a third review of closed local
bodies or a claim that a complete end theorem has already been proved.
