# Finite low-sphere diffeomorphism types, before the marked concordance bridge

NEW finite proof unit, requiring first review. Assume Countable Choice. For n=0,1,2, an actual smooth manifold S whose underlying space is the standard sphere S^n is diffeomorphic to the standard smooth sphere. For n=1,2 an orientation-preserving diffeomorphism exists after choosing the corresponding orientation. No claim of PD isotopy to a supplied PL marking, smoothing concordance, or a computation of π_n(PL/O) is made. Those are separate, still-held bindings; the present unit supplies the terminal DIFF-type input only.

The retained interfaces consumed were consulted only in their Statements and declared choice scope:

- `thm-compact-smooth-manifolds-with-corners-admit-relative-finite-triangulations`: actual finite whole-star triangulation, including boundary data; its Statement expressly says its ambient full-AC hypothesis is dispensable.
- `lem-finite-smooth-surface-presentations-have-prescribed-collar-matches`: under CC, actual finite triangle presentations of connected oriented smooth bordered surfaces with equal Euler characteristic and boundary count give an orientation-preserving diffeomorphism with the prescribed circle maps and inward collar germs.

No provider proof, verification stamp or receipt was checked. The source motivation is the low-sphere endpoint in Hirsch–Mazur PartII proof4.13 and Lashof–Rothenberg Corollary(I) at printed384. Neither source is used here as a proof that an unmarked diffeomorphism preserves a PL concordance class.

For S^2, choose an oriented smooth chart c on a neighborhood of the closed radius-two Euclidean disk. Put D=c({|x|≤1}) and F=S−int D. It is an actual compact smooth surface with one smooth boundary circle and a two-sided radial collar c((1+s)e^(iθ)). It is connected: any path joining exterior points can be adjusted across the finitely many portions inside the chart disk by paths on its boundary; alternatively use a finite transverse path and replace each of its finitely many passages through int D by a boundary arc. All replacements take place in the actual disk/collar, with the endpoints fixed. Orient F by the orientation of S.

Triangulate the parametrized boundary circle by finitely many smooth arcs and use the retained relative whole-star triangulation Statement on F and D. Their matching boundary subdivisions glue to a finite cell decomposition of S. Since the underlying S is homeomorphic to S^2, its Euler characteristic is2. Additivity for this finite decomposition gives χ(F)=χ(S)−χ(D)+χ(∂D)=2−1+0=1. In particular this computation imports no arbitrary classification of compact TOP surfaces. Take the same standard chart disk D0 in the standard smooth sphere and its complement F0; these have the same actual finite smooth triangle data, χ(F0)=1, and one boundary component.

Prescribe the circle map c(e^(iθ))↦c0(e^(iθ)) and the inward collar identification c((1+s)e^(iθ))↦c0((1+s)e^(iθ)), for small s≥0 on F. Relative whole-star triangulations supply the facewise nonsingular triangle presentations demanded by the collar-matching Statement. That Statement supplies an orientation-preserving diffeomorphism B:F→F0 with these EXACT germs. On D use c0 c^−1. The two maps agree on a whole two-sided collar of the seam: on the disk side use the same chart formula for small s≤0, while B agrees with it for s≥0. Hence they glue to an actual smooth diffeomorphism S→standard S^2; the inverse glues by the same formula. No smoothing of a merely topological seam or PL equator is inferred.

For S^1, the same retained compact triangulation Statement gives an actual finite whole-star smooth graph. Each vertex has exactly two incident edge germs, and connectedness makes it one finite cycle. Choose a smooth positive metric from finitely many circle charts and a finite subordinate partition. Give each oriented edge its metric arclength coordinate. The finitely many edge lengths sum to a positive length L. Their cumulative arclength coordinates agree at the vertices as smooth coordinates because they are the oriented arclength coordinates of the same smooth metric across each two-edge star. They therefore define an orientation-preserving smooth diffeomorphism S→R/LZ. Rescale to the standard circle. This is a finite construction; no smooth circle mapping-class or diffeomorphism-group theorem is used.

For S^0, the two single-point charts give the asserted diffeomorphism immediately. All new selections above are finite. Countable Choice enters only through the retained surface matching interface; no upstream full-AC classification theorem is consumed.

Application boundary: this unit does not supply the strong Cairns–Hirsch product theorem imported by Lashof–Rothenberg at printed377, the PD marked-sphere isotopy/concordance bridge, the PL–PD classifying-space comparison, or the actual finite-rank relative tangent reduction. In particular it does not change the held low-connectivity gate.
