5. The main theorem

The following theorem is the main result of the paper. Essentially it
says that if U is a subset of a manifold M and if C is a compact subset in
the interior of U, then any proper imbedding of U into M which is sufficiently
close to the identity can be isotoped to an imbedding which is the identity
on C, such that the isotopy itself is a continuous function of the imbedding.
The theorem is proved by applying Lemma 4.1 to the handles of a carefully
chosen handlebody cover of C.
