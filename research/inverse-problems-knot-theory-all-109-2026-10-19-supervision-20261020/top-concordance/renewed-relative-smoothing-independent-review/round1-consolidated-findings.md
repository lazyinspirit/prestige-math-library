## 1. Realize the finite algebra by actual controlled geometry

`projection-kernel-handle-realization.md` explicitly requires labelled slide
arcs. The accepted Laurent-window output is an idempotent with algebraic
agreement on its specified finite summand, not a geometric placement of an
auxiliary summand. The accepted elementary splitting output gives factors and
propagation estimates, not paths in the intermediate frontier. The author has
confirmed both distinctions.

Supply the actual placement of all added handle generators, the labelled arcs
for each elementary factor, and the buffered contractions used by their
Whitney corrections. Endpoint proximity in the control base does not alone
realize a prescribed deck label by a small path. Explain how each label is
carried by actual original tracks/local lifts and how concatenation, parallel
copies, orientations and successive slides preserve the stated control budget.
All stabilization summands must be realized in the actual finite slab with the
correct zero/identity sign convention. The sign convention already written is
correct; it does not supply this missing placement and arc construction.

diagram is supplied.

In the same finite diagram, spell out the regular-value normal form of the

## 4. Correct the local properness buffer

In the local variant, the starting patch is the inverse image of an open
innermost buffer E. A sequence of starting points can approach the boundary
of E while its limiting ray images lie in a compact subset K of E, if the
allowed total motion is larger than the distance from K to that boundary.
Containment in the image of a compact set from the *whole* first frontier
does not show that the restricted patch preimage is closed in that compact
set. Thus the displayed proof of properness over every compact K in E fails.

Use separate start and output buffers with closed output buffer contained in
the start buffer by more than the entire movement allowance. Complete the
larger start patch and restrict the resulting map over the smaller output
neighborhood. Then the possible starting points over any compact output set
lie in a compact subset strictly inside the start patch, so the closedness
argument applies. Keep this extra buffer in the finite operations and relative
agreements; do not assert properness on the original entire open start image.

## 5. Boundary cellularity is not a consequence of the Cauchy estimate

The summable collar estimate proves a continuous boundary limit. It does not
by itself prove that its fibers are cell-like or cellular. The sentence
“Turning its cellular boundary map into a homeomorphism” assumes an additional
property not proved in this bundle. Either prove the exact fiber/contraction
criterion required by the intended five-dimensional approximation operation,
using the actual controlled approximate-completion data, or explicitly leave
that endpoint as an additional unresolved operation. Do not invoke the
cellular-map approximation supplier before verifying its input. Quinn §2.8
itself constructs a completion map; its Cauchy paragraph does not establish
the stronger cellular adjective used here.
