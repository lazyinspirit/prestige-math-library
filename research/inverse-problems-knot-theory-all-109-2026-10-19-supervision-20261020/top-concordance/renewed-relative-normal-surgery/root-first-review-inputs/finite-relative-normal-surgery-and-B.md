## 1. Finite bundle transport and removal of harmless stabilization

All bundle choices in a surgery datum can be made as actual finite-rank maps. For a vector bundle on a compact parameter polyhedron, a finite trivializing cover and subordinate finite partition of unity embed it as an actual subbundle of a trivial finite-dimensional bundle; let P(z) denote its continuous orthogonal projection. For a prescribed homotopy of base maps, uniform continuity on the compact parameter product gives a finite time partition on which consecutive projections differ in norm by less than1. Orthogonal projection between their images is then an isomorphism. Composing those isomorphisms, with polar orthonormalization if desired, gives actual continuous bundle transport starting at identity. On a closed subset where the base homotopy is stationary use the same projection, so transport is identity there. This proves the bundle-transport step used below without treating a homotopy of classifying maps as an already supplied isomorphism.

In particular a pullback over a disk is explicitly trivialized by transport along its radial contraction, starting with one frame at the center. The same construction handles collar-supported homotopies of F while leaving the protected normal data unchanged.

The stable framing along a sphere of dimension r≤7 can be represented at rank n≥14, with any already prescribed collar data retained. Here is the finite stable-range argument used, rather than an unstated cancellation. In a stable frame identify the extra trivial vectors with an orthonormal frame in R^(n+s). Successively move its last vector to the standard last vector, relative the subset where it is already standard. The parameter dimension is at most r+1≤8, below each sphere dimension encountered, which is at least n≥14 while removing an extra vector. A finite smooth approximation avoids a point different from the prescribed standard vector; stereographic coordinates contract it to that standard vector relative the fixed subset. Lift that contraction by orthogonal rotations on a finite subdivision in the time coordinate. Repeat for the remaining extra columns. Their orthogonal complement is now the original rank-n factor. This yields an actual rank-n frame and the explicit stable frame homotopy to the original datum. No assertion about unstable O(7) follows or is needed.

## 2. The actual B-compatible framed surgery datum

Take a relative homotopy class represented by u:S^r→int W and a:D^(r+1)→Y with a on the boundary equal to F u, where r≤6 for output I and r=7 for the odd subtraction preparation. First use a small interior homotopy to make u smooth. Transport the actual data along this homotopy, keeping the resulting target disk and the stable framing comparison, rather than discarding them.

Trivialize a*ξ over its disk by the finite radial transport above. Combining this frame on the boundary with u*beta gives a stable framing of u*TW. Since TS^r⊕epsilon^(n-r) is canonically trivial after choosing the standard sphere in R^(r+1), the preceding rank-n reduction gives an actual full-column formal isomorphism

    TS^r ⊕ epsilon^(n-r) → u*TW

and a specified stable homotopy comparing it with beta and the chosen disk frame. This homotopy is the normal compatibility datum. It is not inferred from the nullhomotopy alone.

Realize its core monomorphism by the declared positive-codimension immersion theorem. Carry the normal columns through its formal regular homotopy to frame the normal bundle of the resulting core immersion. Equivalently the full-column subcritical-handle contract can be applied to the thickened sphere's finite handle presentation, whose indices are0 and r<n; it gives the same regular class while preserving prescribed open source germs. The formal framing can first be smoothed on compact coordinate packets; retain its actual homotopy to beta for the trace gluing. Here 2r<n, including r=7,n=15. General position makes the core an embedding within that regular class: the expected double-point dimension is2r−n<0. This is finite smooth general position in the actual source, not a Whitney-disk cancellation. A sufficiently small normal radius, by compactness, thickens the framed core to an embedding S^r×D^(n-r)→int W.

Construct T=(W×I) union (D^(r+1)×D^(n-r)), with the handle attached by that framing. The map extends across the core by the chosen target disk. To match it on the entire attaching tube, first collapse the smaller normal fibers in the old map to their core values, tapering this precomposition to identity on a slightly larger tube. This is an explicit homotopy supported in the source interior. Transport beta along it by section1; the target disk incorporates the earlier core regular homotopy. On the trace handle extend the disk map constantly in the normal coordinates.

The actual bundle extension is as follows. The handle tangent bundle and its pulled-back target bundle have product frames. On the attaching S^r×D^(n-r), their stable frame differs from the old beta by exactly the comparison homotopy retained above: that homotopy was how the regular/framed class was chosen. Use it on the attaching collar, constant near both collar ends, and use the two given frames away from the collar. They now agree literally where pieces are glued. Thus there is an actual stable trivialization of TT⊕Psi*ξ, restricting to beta with the stated extra normal line on the incoming copy of W, and to the induced beta1 on the new W1. It is product on every original boundary collar.

To express this as the requested normal map B_T, take a fixed normal inverse νT with TT⊕νT trivial, add the finite trivial factors just used, and compose the inverse of the trace tangent trivialization with this fixed inverse identification. Algebraically, νT⊕epsilon^R identifies with νT⊕TT⊕Psi*ξ⊕epsilon^s, and then with Psi*ξ plus a trivial factor. This is an ACTUAL stable bundle isomorphism. Choose the inverse and extra line on the incoming and side collars as their prescribed product inverses; the construction therefore restricts to the original B there, not just its stable homotopy class. This produces precisely the B extension which the previously declared surgery-preserves-normal-bordism statement takes as input.

The orientation at the two feet of a0-surgery uses the standard opposite S^0 boundary orientations. All traces are supported away from the protected collars. The relative fundamental-chain boundary of T proves preservation of degree whenever that degree was part of the input.

## 3. Finitely many interior surgeries, fixed entire boundary

Use the actual finite handle model of W and a finite cellular replacement of its map to Y to construct a finite CW model of its mapping cylinder. The comparison homotopies transfer all characteristic maps back to the ACTUAL map F; they need not modify F or its prescribed boundary germ. Consider the finite list of relative cells through dimension7 in increasing dimension. The mapping-cylinder model starts with W and attaches precisely that list; call its successive partial complexes Y_i. The following finite induction is Wall1.2's argument, with the boundary location explicit.

At stage i−1 the actual trace T_(i−1) is homotopy equivalent over Y to Y_(i−1), relative its initial source model. A next d-cell, d≤7, gives a disk-pair class. The trace so far has handles of indices≤7, hence its reverse presentation from the CURRENT source W_(i−1) has indices at least n+1−7≥8. It follows that the relative groups of (T_(i−1),W_(i−1)) vanish through degree7. Consequently the relative d-cell class lifts to a class for the current map F_(i−1). Choose one lift and representative disk; this is one finite choice, not an assumption that every homotopy group is finitely generated.

Apply section2 with r=d−1. For d≥1 the core representative can be pushed into the interior by the actual boundary collar before general position. Its surgery trace is, relative W_(i−1), the attachment of the same d-cell up to the retained comparison homotopy. The characteristic map therefore extends the previous homotopy equivalence to one over Y_i by the elementary cell-gluing construction. This proves the induction. Dimension0 cells, if present, create the standard framed ball component;1-handles connect components as dictated by the finite relative0/1-skeleton. In the connected oriented case they can instead be arranged initially by finitely many arcs. All boundary collars remain products because every attaching tube lies in the interior.

At the final stage T_final is equivalent over Y to the relative7-skeleton model. That partial model→Y is7-connected. Its outgoing W1→T_final is7-connected by the reverse handle indices just computed. The triple exact sequence gives that F1:W1→Y is7-connected. This proves I. It uses finite relative cells, not an unproved finite-generation assertion for arbitrary group-ring kernels.

For II apply the mapping-cylinder triple for M0→W1→Y. The exact segment is

    pi_(i+1)(F1) → pi_i(W1,M0) → pi_i(f0) → pi_i(F1).

For i≤6 the outside terms needed to bound the middle group vanish. The component/π1 statements follow from the same low-dimensional exact sequence and the connected source/target convention. Thus the incoming inclusion is6-connected, in particular a π1-isomorphism. The actual F1/B1 are unchanged by the subsequent same-W presentation diffeomorphisms; that stage has a separate author and is not used to create the normal trace here.

## 4. Odd handle subtraction, outgoing changes stated explicitly

Assume the additional input III, n=15, and write M=∂W. First run I with boundary fixed if no finite cell comparison has already been supplied, even when F was already7-connected; its output is still7-connected and normally bordant relative the entire boundary to the input. The interior construction through degree7 has reverse trace handles of indices≥9. Consequently pi8(F) at its output is generated by the finite8-cell list of the same relative mapping-cylinder model: comparison with the trace is an isomorphism in that degree, and the first relative Hurewicz group of the7-connected remaining model is generated by those8-cells. This supplies finitely many actual representatives with their target nullhomotopies. If a later input is supplied with this finite comparison it can be used directly; otherwise the preceding finite normalization constructs it. Thus a bare abstract assertion of finite generation is not substituted for that comparison.

Represent those pi8(F) generators by disjoint B-compatible framed embedded S^7×D^8 in int W using section2. Choose disjoint arcs from small disks in these spheres to disjoint feet in the stipulated outgoing ball, carrying their actual base paths and Lambda labels as part of the finite output, away from incoming/side collars and other spheres. Finite general position permits these arcs and their thin tubes. Remove a small disk from each sphere and prolong its boundary along the corresponding tube to its foot. This gives disjoint properly embedded D^7×D^8 in (W,M). The boundary S^6×D^8 lies in the outgoing ball and is a TRIVIAL framed embedding: its framing is transported from the disk removed from the framed sphere, over that disk and along the contractible tube, so it extends over the foot disk in the ball. This is stronger than claiming an arbitrary framed boundary sphere is trivial.

The old target nullhomotopy plus the whisker along this tube supplies the relative disk-pair nullhomotopy used in Wall1.3. Homotope F on these handle neighborhoods as a map of pairs to the constant foot value, with its boundary homotopy in X. Extend by a collar taper and keep A fixed. Transport beta along the homotopy. The framing just constructed is the class determined by beta and that relative nullhomotopy, by exactly the frame-comparison calculation of section2, now relative the foot. Remove the interiors of these proper handles, obtaining W0. Its map to Y takes the new boundary into X.

The normal bordism for subtraction is W×I with the indicated handle regions reinterpreted as the changing side, corners rounded in their actual product collars. Its map and B are the transported product data just constructed. On each local corner the same frame-comparison homotopy identifies the normal-line convention; interpolate it on that corner collar as in section2. Thus the side normal data are actual extensions. On A the entire construction is the original product. This is the relative handle-subtraction step1.3, not an inference that any removed proper disk automatically gives a B-compatible trace.

Here is its homological effect. Let H denote the removed proper handles and Mnew=∂W0. Excision gives (W0,Mnew)→(W,H union Mnew), and the associated mapping-cone exact sequence contains

    H7(H union Mnew,M;Lambda) → H8(C(F_pair))
      → H8(C(F0_pair)) → H6(H union Mnew,M;Lambda).

The last group is zero: the first pair excises to a disjoint union of (D^7×D^8,S^6×D^8), with homology only in degree7. The first map is onto. Indeed the boundary map M→X is7-connected, so H8(C(F))→H8(C(F_pair)) is onto, and the chosen proper disks represent the images of its generating pi8(F) classes under the first relative Hurewicz map. Hence H8(C(F0_pair))=0. The same sequence gives the previous vanishings through degree7. This is the specific degree calculation in Wall1.4, not an assertion that kernel homology equals homotopy-fiber homology in all degrees.

Reattaching H builds W from W0 by8-handles. Thus W0→W is7-connected, and W0→Y remains7-connected. On M the change is surgery on the trivial framed S^6 feet, hence connected sum with standard S^7×S^7 summands in the chosen outgoing ball, with the normal map extended over their trivial traces. Collapsing these summands back to their feet has no new homotopy in degrees below7 and is onto in degree7. The boundary map to X therefore remains7-connected. This proves III. Incoming and side data are literally unchanged; the outgoing stabilization must be carried into any later kernel/basis comparison.

## 6. Choice ledger and current status

The input is one finite normal map/handle model, a finite target, and finite protected collars. Mapping-cylinder cells, comparison homotopies, frame homotopies, surgery cores, tubes, cutoffs and trace collars are selected in finite lists. A countable independently specified family may choose whole finite packets by ACω. No stronger parametric family theorem is exported.
