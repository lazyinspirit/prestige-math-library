### Actual P/Q join blocks and the supported stretch

Each simplex of βK_c splits uniquely into its low face in βP and its high face in Q. Away from P∪Q a point has the unique expression

    x=(1−t)u+tv,   u∈P, v∈Q, 0<t<1,

in that simplex. The coordinate t is the sum of the barycentric weights on its high-rank vertices. This is a single continuous PL function on K_c; face restrictions agree. The endpoint-pair space E is the finite union of products of these low/high faces, a compact subset of P×Q. At t=0 all pairs with the same u are identified, and at t=1 all pairs with the same v are identified. These are the only degeneracies.

Consequently the closed blocks t≤δ and t≥1−δ are explicit finite PL join neighborhoods of P and Q. Each is the mapping-cylinder block of the corresponding projection of E onto its endpoint face: its whole interval parameter and collapse to the endpoint are displayed, not asserted from a general regular-neighborhood recognition theorem. These are all the neighborhood geometry used by this finite operation.

Pull the two open images bᵢ(Uᵢ) back by g. Compactness of P and Q gives ε∈(0,1/4) so that the block t≤2ε lies in the first open and t≥1−2ε in the second. Let T:[0,1]→[0,1] be increasing and piecewise linear, fixing 0,1 and taking 1−ε to ε. It has positive slopes ε/(1−ε) and (1−ε)/ε. Thus it expands the second, Q-end neighborhood to cover t≥ε, while the first open still covers t≤2ε.

For support at the outer cube boundary, let E_a⊂E consist of endpoint pairs in simplices contained in C_a, and E_∂⊂E of endpoint pairs in simplices contained in ∂C_a′. These are disjoint compact sets because C_a misses that boundary. Choose a continuous λ:E→[0,1], equal to 1 on E_a and 0 on E_∂, by the explicit distance-to-the-two-closed-sets formula. Define, on each join segment,

    s_τ((1−t)u+tv)=(1−T_{τλ(u,v)}(t))u+T_{τλ(u,v)}(t)v,
    T_η(t)=(1−η)t+ηT(t),     0≤τ≤1.

Every T_η is an increasing interval homeomorphism. The formulas agree on shared faces because the low/high barycentric partition and endpoint pair agree. They fix P and Q. Continuity at the collapsed endpoints follows from the uniform bounds on the slopes of T_η and their inverses; the choice of the unused endpoint there cannot change the limit. The map is therefore a continuous bijection of the compact cube to itself, with continuous inverse, at every τ, and the same formula gives joint continuity. On ∂C_a′ either one is at a fixed endpoint or its endpoint pair lies in E_∂, so s_τ is exactly identity there. Each simplex of βK_c, and hence each ORIGINAL simplex of K_c, is preserved setwise by s_τ and its inverse.

Extend g s_τ g⁻¹ by identity outside g(C_a′); call it s̄_τ. This is a compactly supported ambient isotopy of M. Its support lies in the fixed chart disjoint from A₂, so it fixes an actual open neighborhood of A₂. Every point of C_a is an endpoint or lies in an E_a segment, where λ=1; the preceding block calculation gives

    g(C_a)⊂b₁(U₁)∪s̄₁ b₂(U₂).

This is actual whole INNER-CUBE coverage, not only coverage of P and Q.

## The finite dual complex and the join stretch

Choose compact X containing C, the support of h, and M−U. Enlarge it to X̄ by adding all whole simplices in the closed PL buffer which meet X.     Q⊂g(O₁),    g|neighborhood(D)=id.

Here is the finite join operation used next. Every barycentrically subdivided simplex of L′ is the join of its face in P and its face in Q: write its points as (1−t)a+tb. On the compact union, small initial join segments lie in h(U), and small terminal segments lie in g(O₁). Choose ε>0 with that property. Replace the join coordinate by the increasing piecewise linear homeomorphism T of [0,1] fixing endpoints and sending ε to 1−ε. This stretches h(U) to cover the complement of the terminal g(O₁)-segments. The same formula agrees on faces. Extend it to the finite surrounding simplicial star by replacing T with T_λ=(1−λ)id+λT, where λ is a continuous cutoff on the compact space of join endpoint pairs, equal to 1 for pairs in L′ and zero outside its surrounding star. Such a cutoff is obtained by distances between its disjoint closed endpoint-pair subsets. Each T_λ is increasing, so the inverse is continuous and the formula is a homeomorphism. At t=0,1 it fixes the endpoints independently of λ. Extend by identity outside the surrounding star. Call the resulting compactly supported map θ. It preserves each simplex and

    L′⊂θh(U)∪g(O₁).

Choose the surrounding star within the closed PL buffer. Simplex preservation implies θ(X)⊂X̄: affected points of X remain in their whole simplices already added to X̄, and unaffected points are fixed. Because h(U) contains M−X, it follows that θh(U) contains M−X̄. Also D⊂g(O₁), since g fixes D. The three regions M−X̄, D, L′ cover M. Hence

    M=θh(U)∪g(O₁).

As C∩U is empty, the ambient image (θh)⁻¹g(O₁) contains C. All maps fix B. This first supplies an actual open collar containing C. Its source collar O₁ may be the stretched one, so an additional compression is needed before claiming an ambient image of an arbitrary initial collar.
