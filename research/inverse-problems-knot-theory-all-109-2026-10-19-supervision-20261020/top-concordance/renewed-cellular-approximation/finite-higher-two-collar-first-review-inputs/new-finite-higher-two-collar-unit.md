### Unit statement and fixed marks

Let m≥6. Let M be a metrizable TOP m-manifold with boundary B, with B→M a homotopy equivalence. Let U₁,U₂ be actual parametrized open collars of B, with predetermined standard AMBIENT-CLOSED parallel subcollars Aᵢ⊂Uᵢ. Supply their common ambient-closed parallel collar coordinates; these exist in the exact adopted reparametrization interface. Given compact K⊂M, there are compactly supported ambient isotopies hᵢ,t, starting at identity and fixing an actual OPEN neighborhood of Aᵢ, such that

    K⊂h₁,1(U₁)∪h₂,1(U₂).

The isotopies fix B pointwise throughout. In the initial E2 instance M=W−V₁, their supports are compact in M and therefore extend by identity near V₁ as well. Thus the given boundary/side product maps and the based boundary π₁ marking are retained. No arbitrary interior transverse path is claimed pointwise fixed.

The only engulfing inputs are the adopted finite TOP Statements: CLOSED locally p-dominated X in a boundaryless p-connected open pair (O,V), X−V compact, admits compactly supported ambient isotopy with X⊂h₁(V), for p=m−3; and its retained p≤2 counterpart. A top-level high-dimensional PL guard is not used. Every collar shield is removed from the work manifold, with its open-pair connectivity separately earned.

### One chart addition with two compact covered ledgers

It suffices first to add one inner chart cube while retaining compact ledgers Lᵢ⊂Uᵢ. Choose an actual chart embedding g of a neighborhood of the closed cube C₁=[−1,1]ᵐ into int M, with its whole image disjoint from A₂. Fix 0<a<a′<1. The desired new covered compact is g(C_a). Enlarge the closed parallel collar levels to Dᵢ⁻⊂int Dᵢ⊂Dᵢ⊂Uᵢ, with Aᵢ∪Lᵢ⊂int Dᵢ⁻. All these levels are ambient-closed; a compact ledger has bounded height in its collar coordinates. Their outer collar push buffers also lie in Uᵢ.

Choose a finite straight simplicial triangulation K_c of C_a′ with C_a a subcomplex and sufficiently fine that every closed simplex Δ of K_c meeting g⁻¹(L₂) satisfies

    g(Δ)⊂int D₂⁻.

Such a mesh exists: the compact set L₂∩g(C_a′) lies in the open int D₂⁻, so in the actual single chart it has a positive neighborhood margin. Begin with the finite subdivision by the inner cube faces, then refine sufficiently. ALL mesh refinements occur before the following P/Q definitions.

Put P=K_c^(m−3). In βK_c let Q be the full subcomplex on vertices corresponding to ORIGINAL faces σ of K_c of rank dimσ≥m−2. The complementary low vertices correspond to dimσ≤m−3 and form βP. Every simplex of βK_c is a strictly increasing chain of faces of K_c; its high-rank part has at most the three ranks m−2,m−1,m. Thus dim Q≤2. This proves the dimension for this actual Q; no dual of a later arbitrary subdivision is assumed two-dimensional.

For i=1,2 put Oᵢ=M−Dᵢ and Vᵢ=Uᵢ−Dᵢ. The actual collar-height push from `selected-e2-higher-top-consumer.md` proves Oᵢ→M and Vᵢ→Uᵢ homotopy equivalences, using a cutoff beyond Dᵢ and identity outside a larger ambient-closed collar level. Since Uᵢ→M is a homotopy equivalence, so is Vᵢ→Oᵢ. Hence these are boundaryless open work pairs with every required relative homotopy group zero. This earns their witnesses; deleting an arbitrary guard is not invoked.

The set X₁=g(P)−D₁ is CLOSED in O₁, locally (m−3)-dominated in the actual chart g, and X₁−V₁=g(P)−U₁ is compact. Local domination is geometric: intersect the fixed finite P with a small closed Euclidean cube inside g⁻¹(O₁); the finite convex cell intersections triangulate in dimension≤m−3. Apply the high-p Statement in O₁ and extend its compactly supported isotopy by identity across D₁. Its endpoint b₁ gives

    g(P)⊂b₁(U₁),    b₁=id near D₁.

Likewise X₂=g(Q)−D₂ is CLOSED and locally 2-dominated in O₂, with compact difference from V₂. The p≤2 Statement gives b₂ with

    g(Q)⊂b₂(U₂),    b₂=id near D₂.

P and Q remain the SAME fixed chart polyhedra throughout these calls. Only the two open sets move, separately. No TOP image of a polyhedron is reclassified as PL in another chart. In particular all ledger carrier simplices in int D₂⁻ are fixed pointwise by b₂ before the subsequent join stretch.

### Actual P/Q join blocks and the supported stretch

Each simplex of βK_c splits uniquely into its low face in βP and its high face in Q. Away from P∪Q a point has the unique expression

    x=(1−t)u+tv,   u∈P, v∈Q, 0<t<1,

in that simplex. The coordinate t is the sum of the barycentric weights on its high-rank vertices. This is a single continuous PL function on K_c; face restrictions agree. The endpoint-pair space E is the finite union of products of these low/high faces, a compact subset of P×Q. At t=0 all pairs with the same u are identified, and at t=1 all pairs with the same v are identified. These are the only degeneracies.

Consequently the closed blocks t≤δ and t≥1−δ are explicit finite PL join neighborhoods of P and Q. Each is the mapping-cylinder block of the corresponding projection of E onto its endpoint face: its whole interval parameter and collapse to the endpoint are displayed, not asserted from a general regular-neighborhood recognition theorem. These are all the neighborhood geometry used by this finite operation.

Pull the two open images bᵢ(Uᵢ) back by g. Compactness of P and Q gives ε∈(0,1/4) so that the block t≤2ε lies in the first open and t≥1−2ε in the second. Let T:[0,1]→[0,1] be increasing and piecewise linear, fixing 0,1 and taking 1−ε to ε. It has positive slopes ε/(1−ε) and (1−ε)/ε. Thus it expands the second, Q-end neighborhood to cover t≥ε, while the first open still covers t≤2ε.

For support at the outer cube boundary, let E_a⊂E consist of endpoint pairs in simplices contained in C_a, and E_∂⊂E of endpoint pairs in simplices contained in ∂C_a′. These are disjoint compact sets because C_a misses that boundary. Choose a continuous λ:E→[0,1], equal to 1 on E_a and 0 on E_∂, by the explicit distance-to-the-two-closed-sets formula. Define, on each join segment,

    s_τ((1−t)u+tv)=(1−T_{τλ(u,v)}(t))u+T_{τλ(u,v)}(t)v,
    T_η(t)=(1−η)t+ηT(t),     0≤τ≤1.

Every T_η is an increasing interval homeomorphism. The formulas agree on shared faces because the low/high barycentric partition and endpoint pair agree. They fix P and Q. Continuity at the collapsed endpoints follows from the uniform bounds on the slopes of T_η and their inverses; the choice of the unused endpoint there cannot change the limit. The map is therefore a continuous bijection of the compact cube to itself, with continuous inverse, at every τ, and the same formula gives joint continuity. On ∂C_a′ either one is at a fixed endpoint or its endpoint pair lies in E_∂, so s_τ is exactly identity there. Each simplex of βK_c, and hence each ORIGINAL simplex of K_c, is preserved setwise by s_τ and its inverse.

Extend g s_τ g⁻¹ by identity outside g(C_a′); call it s̄_τ. This is a compactly supported ambient isotopy of M. Its support lies in the fixed chart disjoint from A₂, so it fixes an actual open neighborhood of A₂. Every point of C_a is an endpoint or lies in an E_a segment, where λ=1; the preceding block calculation gives

    g(C_a)⊂b₁(U₁)∪s̄₁ b₂(U₂).

This is actual whole INNER-CUBE coverage, not only coverage of P and Q.

### Ledger membership, fixed germs, and finite whole-K coverage

The first ledger L₁ lies in D₁ and is fixed by b₁. For y∈L₂ outside the chart, s̄₁⁻¹(y)=y∈D₂⊂b₂(U₂). For y∈L₂∩g(C_a′), choose any ORIGINAL simplex Δ containing g⁻¹(y). By mesh choice g(Δ)⊂int D₂⁻. Simplex preservation implies s̄₁⁻¹(y)∈g(Δ)⊂D₂⊂b₂(U₂). Hence

    L₁⊂b₁(U₁),    L₂⊂s̄₁b₂(U₂).

L₂ is retained as the SAME POINT SET covered by the second image, although the second homeomorphism need not fix it pointwise. The entire carrier simplices, not merely the ledger points, were placed in the preserved shield before Q placement. This is the marked-set content of Luft's property(c).

Take the first output isotopy b₁,τ and the second obtained by first performing b₂,τ and then s̄_τ. Both have compact support. The first fixes an open neighborhood of A₁; the second fixes an open neighborhood of A₂, by the raw support complement and the chart disjointness. Each fixes B pointwise. Their images are again parametrized boundary collars, with Aᵢ still their original parallel subcollars, since the relevant ambient map fixes a neighborhood of Aᵢ. Ambient images of the other predetermined closed collar levels remain ambient-closed.

For the initial compact K, the part not already in U₁∪U₂ is a compact subset of int M−A₂. Choose finitely many such chart inner cubes covering it. Together with U₁,U₂ their interiors cover K. A finite compact shrinking of this cover gives compact initial pieces Lᵢ⊂Uᵢ and compact pieces in the chart inner cubes whose union is K. This uses only finite metric shrinking of an open cover of compact K.

Apply the one-chart addition finitely many times. At a new stage the two current collars remain actual ambient images, so the same collar-pair witnesses apply. After each inner cube has been covered, split that compact cube image into two compact subsets of the current open images by a finite closed shrinking; adjoin those subsets to the two respective old compact ledgers. At the next stage choose the larger closed shields to contain these ledgers before triangulating the new chart. All already covered pieces are therefore retained. Charts were chosen disjoint from the original A₂, which every second map fixes. Finite concatenation gives the claimed hᵢ,τ, fixes actual open neighborhoods of Aᵢ, and covers all K. No infinite selection or limit is used in this unit.
