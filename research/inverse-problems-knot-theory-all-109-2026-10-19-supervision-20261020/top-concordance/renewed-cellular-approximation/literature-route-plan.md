# Literature plan for the actual CE consumer

Plan only, following the instruction to pause new proof-detail authoring. No original theorem or unreviewed assembly is accepted by this document. Previously accepted proofs remain frozen.

## Actual theorem needed

The completion consumer supplies, at each of finitely many desuspension stages, an actual smooth source and a proper cell-like map β onto a boundaryless TOP target. It also supplies an open region A where β is already the homeomorphism carrying the prescribed smooth end/K germs. One needs one homeomorphism approximating β, exact on a smaller protected open germ. One does not need a family of approximations, an isotopy through CE maps, a concordance theorem, or a quantitative estimate uniform in the stabilizing rank.

For the fixed finite rank k the actual target dimensions are m=5,…,4+k. The available higher compact local-contractibility contract through 5+k is sufficient for these dimensions. The source and the larger target M₀ used before final restriction are boundaryless. Hence the dimension-five boundary exception is vacuous in this application.

Siebenmann, *Approximating cellular maps by homeomorphisms*, Topology 11 (1972), 271–294, Theorem A on p.271, states the needed ordinary approximation for metric TOP manifolds in every m≥5; when boundary is present and m=5 the boundary restriction must be a HOMEOMORPHISM, not merely CE. Its Complement on p.272 is stronger and is not needed here. The full primary has been read; cached at `../renewed-relative-smoothing/sources/siebenmann-cellular-approximation-primary.pdf`, 24 pages, with page-by-page text in `primary-pages/`.

## Page-backed noncircular route in that paper

1. **CE homotopy information.** Theorem 1.1, p.274, gives hereditary proper homotopy equivalence for CE maps between ENRs, citing Lacher, *Cell-like mappings I*, Pacific J. Math.30 (1969), 717–731. For our reconstruction the accepted finite UV lifting and ordinary end arguments can be used at their exact scopes. The stronger newly reconstructed proper lifting statement is optional, not a new indispensable prerequisite for all subsequent steps.

2. **Local handle problem.** Main Theorem2.1 and Main Lemma2.2, p.275, concern f:V→Bᵏ×Rⁿ, m=k+n, already homeomorphic near the side ∂Bᵏ×Rⁿ. Main Lemma asks for a homeomorphism φ:V→Bᵏ×Rⁿ and a CE model F, identity near the side and outside a finite normal radius, with Fφ=f over the side and the core block. These are the exact local demands; arbitrary compact-set engulfing is stronger than required.

3. **Immersed punctured torus and compactification.** The diagram is pp.277–280. A punctured-torus immersion is a local homeomorphism; its pullback gives the actual local manifold, not an abstract mapping cylinder. Lemma2.5 is used on p.280 to make the one-point compactification a manifold. Its convenient statement on p.279 uses proper homotopy type S^{m−1}×R. The actual AppendixI statement on p.288 is weaker: ORDINARY homotopy type S^{m−1}×R plus 1-LC at both ends suffices for every m≥5. Thus a separately strengthened general proper-homotopy-equivalence theorem and all of its triangulation packets are not necessary to feed this recognition step if the accepted ordinary UV/end input is used.

4. **E1 annular recognition.** AppendixI, pp.288–289, constructs a proper locally flat line, proves its complement is contractible and 1-LC at infinity, recognizes that complement as Rᵐ, and enlarges a meridian disk to an actual separating S^{m−1}. The last enlargement uses Kirby, *The union of flat (n−1)-balls is flat in Rⁿ*, Bull.AMS74 (1968), 614–617, reference[30]. This is the actual published cap/isolated-point-flatness step. Ordinary homology and end information alone do not supply the embedded sphere. In dimension five it is exactly an initial S⁴, before the already adopted prescribed-S⁴ half-product operation can be applied. The Cantrell–Lacher/flat-arc input under investigation belongs here, not after one has silently declared that sphere available.

5. **E2 positive-index torus homeomorphism.** Three alternatives are explicitly separated on pp.280–281: TOP surgery; relative PL reduction plus homotopy-torus analysis; or the pure geometric finite-cover alternative of AppendixII. The geometric alternative, PropositionII.1 pp.289–292, needs only a homotopy equivalence g:W→Bᵏ×Tⁿ, k≥1, homeomorphic on boundary, and allows a sufficiently large STANDARD finite cover. It first makes the associated h-cobordism invertible using TOP engulfing, p.290. It then wraps the two collars to a homeomorphism of B^{k−1}×S¹×Tⁿ, tracks its infinite-cyclic cobordism invariant, passes to large finite covers, and uses compact local contractibility, pp.291–292. The footnote on p.292 explicitly says the displayed two-step proof establishes a composite sufficiently large cover; one need not strengthen this to every sufficiently large integer for the local CE application. This avoids surgery and smoothing the original TOP W₅.

6. **Zero-index replacement.** AppendixII pp.292–293 uses a family of punctured-torus problems and a bundle theorem, citing the technical deformation/engulfing results [32,6.7] and [46A,6.9]. A nonproper smooth submersion alone does not earn that bundle. Our root-adopted marked-endpoint replacement avoids the full family-bundle theorem, but its application remains conditional on the actual initial S⁴ core from E1. It needs ordinary hereditary homotopy equivalence and end control, not a general parametric CE approximation theorem.

7. **E3 is an exact geometric expansion, not weak-equivalence recognition.** On p.282 the required maps are

       E:f⁻¹(Bᵏ×2Bⁿ)→V, fixing f⁻¹(Bᵏ×Bⁿ),
       E′:F⁻¹(Bᵏ×2Bⁿ)→Bᵏ×Rⁿ, fixing F⁻¹(Bᵏ×Bⁿ).

   Their conjugation with the natural local homeomorphism ψ gives the global φ′. The source explicitly names the engulfing method of Stallings and says that the needed argument is repeated in Siebenmann1968[42], §4. Its printed bracket references at this sentence are [47],[41], which do not identify Stallings in the final bibliography; this cross-reference mismatch must not be silently treated as a proof locator. The final bibliography does list Stallings, *The piecewise linear structure of Euclidean space*, Proc.Cambridge Philos.Soc.58 (1962), 481–488, as [48]. The unambiguous operative locator here is [42], §4; any additional Stallings source should be identified by title and actual proof rather than those mismatched numbers. It does not claim that an arbitrary weak-equivalence open subset can engulf every compact set. The saturated core and actual complement/end homotopies must be retained. For a completely general TOP source the paper introduces a PL structure extending the one near the core, using Kirby–Siebenmann obstruction theory[31] or Lees TOP immersion theory[37]. Importing that branch without checking its independence from the original product-structure theorem would risk a cycle. In the present application the actual smooth completion source provides an alternative: interior work regions outside the protected core inherit DIFF. This removes the need to smooth the original target, but the local side/collar and complement homotopy bindings still have to be supplied. A finite p-dimensional smooth engulfing theorem by itself does not constitute E or E′.

8. **Exact local side.** Lemma2.6, p.283, is the explicit translating Alexander isotopy that changes φ′ to φ with the side fixed. This is distinct from a broad relative-homeomorphism deformation theorem.

9. **Handle modification and globalization.** The double use of Main Lemma with inversion, plus actual Schoenflies filling, is pp.276–277. The local-to-global passage is pp.283–284: source/target error conversion by Lemma3.1, m+1 Euclidean-chart families, then 0-,1-,…,m-handle stages in a fine triangulation of each Euclidean target. The original TOP target is not triangulated globally. The number of dependent whole-map stages is finite. At each stage countably many disjoint local packets may be chosen simultaneously under Countable Choice; the choices must be formulated this way rather than as an unspecified recursive infinite sequence. The stronger Complement, its noncompact homeomorphism-space deformation discussion, and boundary treatment pp.284–286 are unnecessary for the actual boundaryless, nonparametric consumer.

10. **Protected germ.** Absolute approximation on the open complement of a closed buffer C⊂A, with error bounded by a constant times distance to C and then glued to β over C, preserves an actual smaller open germ. This is the concrete author development `relative-germ-localization.md`, not an extra assumption that the arbitrary protected set has a smooth boundary. It requires independent review before adoption. In the comparison application the larger source/target are kept boundaryless, and restriction to the final W occurs only after the atlas is transferred.

## Accepted inputs versus remaining mathematics

Accepted exact contracts include finite UV lifting; the proper-lifting theorem conditional on actual source triangulation packets; raw TOP p≤2 finite engulfing, R⁵ recognition, and half-product recognition GIVEN an actual S⁴; compact product local contractibility in all fixed finite required dimensions; finite two-collar coverage in dimension five; its countable two-collar stretching assembly; and the marked-endpoint replacement conditional on an actual initial sphere core. These contracts do not collectively assert full CE approximation.

The remaining published-proof obligations are: (i) E1's actual initial separating sphere/cap, including its TOP/PL or flatness binding; (ii) the E2 invertible-cobordism/collar assembly and wrapping/marking realization, with the new higher-p raw operation still in its allotted focused review/repair cycle; (iii) exact E3 global expansion from the saturated-core and end data; (iv) the finite-stage/countable-packet globalization and relative-germ glue. New author files for compact-one-collar enlargement, side unwrapping, proper end π₂ witnesses, and source triangulation packets are developments, not adopted closure. No new detail should be added until this route is selected against the alternative product-structure route.

## Recommendation and alternatives

For a literature-supported application, the shortest complete theorem-level route is: actual completion ⇒ proper CE β with known germ ⇒ Siebenmann1972 TheoremA in the actual boundaryless dimension ⇒ elementary vanishing-error localization ⇒ transfer the source DIFF atlas. Quinn's product-structure exposition explicitly uses CE approximation independently of product structure; the CE theorem itself must not be obtained by assuming the original smoothing conclusion.

If the library must reconstruct that supplier from its actual proof, the minimal pure-geometric plan is the numbered DAG above, keeping E1 and E3 as genuine geometric steps. Do not add a general nonproper bundle theorem, arbitrary compact-set engulfing, arbitrary closed-set relative triangulation, uniform-in-rank control, or parametric CE approximation. The alternative is to use an independently established relative TOP/PL product-structure theorem and low-dimensional smoothing lift directly for the original comparison problem; the stable TOP lead owns whether its precise relative hypotheses are already available. That alternative should be chosen before paying for the remaining CE reconstruction, not combined circularly with it.
