## 1. Keep the punctured propagation within the adopted overlap contract

The declared finite provider allows an empty protected germ or one existing
overlap, not an unproved arbitrary finite union of old protected subarcs.
The draft's instruction to protect “all old portions lying in its support”
needs its interval/support bookkeeping made precise. Arrange the ordered
interval chain and support so its only transition to the unmodified old arc
is a single protected PL overlap; parts already covered by the new compact
piece will themselves be polyhedralized by that operation. Begin inside the
known boundary-normal PL seed so no extension of a compact axis through the
manifold boundary is tacitly required. Alternatively give the exact additional
provider contract if it is actually available; do not rereview its proof.

The forward/inverse assembly is otherwise appropriately supported away from
p at each finite stage. Unlike a support accumulating on E in every stage,
each such compact support has a positive minimum of a continuous positive
control function, so the stated smallness choices are feasible here.

## 2. The disk exchange does not remove an arbitrary innermost circle

The draft chooses an innermost intersection circle e on the ambient spanning
disk and always replaces its subdisk by a disk pushed **outside** N_i.
If that innermost subdisk was already outside N_i, its retained adjacent
annulus is inside N_i. An outside replacement then does not remove the
intersection circle. For example, two nested intersection circles can bound
an inside annulus with an outside inner disk. The stated “one fewer circle”
does not follow. Cantrell–Edwards instead chooses the replacement on the
opposite side from the removed innermost disk.

An alternative for the present outside-only construction would use an
outermost intersection circle relative to the original outside boundary,
replace everything inside it, and retain an outside collar. Whichever route
is chosen, specify the actual collar exchange and seam push: merely keeping
e fixed on ∂N_i leaves an intersection there. The seam must be rounded and
moved to the retained side without a new intersection. Record the conormal
collar compatibility and its five-dimensional support; general position
away from a shared boundary alone does not settle that pasting.

There is also finite-avoidance bookkeeping: first make the ambient disk
disjoint from p. Its compact image then meets only finitely many pieces
of the punctured PL curve. The stated avoidance of “other finite curve
pieces” is legitimate at that point; it must not discard the infinitely
many pieces accumulating at p before disjointness has been obtained.

The four-dimensional boundary-circle construction is not being rejected
by a false dimension count: a PL circle in S4 is in the codimension-three
unknot range, and the proposed projection/crossing-change route is relevant.
But a boundary disk constructed there still needs the actual side and collar
exchange above. The strict `2+2<5` ambient avoidance cannot replace it with
a four-dimensional immersed-disk argument.

## 3. Supply the actual compatible annulus maps and inverse control

The sentences “apply ... in alternating annuli” and “matching at the already
fixed endpoints” do not supply the claimed whole homeomorphism at p.
An excursion cleanup moving an arc from outside N_i to inside N_i cannot fix
∂N_i pointwise. Its proper support boundaries are the surrounding reserved
cubes, for example N_(i−1) and N_(i+1); state the disjoint schedule and the
preservation of already cleaned boundaries. A disk used for the actual
motion must lie in that support annulus, not just somewhere in the outer
ball. The fixed outer distinguished component must remain protected.

Two intersection points on every cube are also not yet two fixed radial
rays. Their positions on successive boundaries can differ. Specify whole
boundary homeomorphisms sending the two marked points and their crossing
germs to the chosen reference rays, not just endpoint matches. Adjacent
annulus maps must restrict to exactly the same entire boundary map and
collar germ. Give the finite embedded-disk moves in the actual standard
annulus and their relative boundary condition. Finally control both maps
and inverses at p, for example by an actual exhaustion whose source and
target cubes are both preserved. Shrinking supports alone does not specify
the target exhaustion if the whole boundary maps have not been given.

This is precisely where the published proof has more information than the
draft: printed453 defines the marked boundary motions φ_i and writes each
annulus map as ψ_i φ_(i+1) φ_i. On the shared whole boundary the adjacent
formulas both reduce to φ_(i+1). Its source/target cubical annuli give the
continuity of both map and inverse at p. The draft has not yet instantiated
that formula or an equivalent full-chart construction.
