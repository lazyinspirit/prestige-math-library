## 4. Exact published low-connectivity route if the DIFF lane is retained

KS III AppendixC, printed145–146 (PDF153–154), explicitly connects smoothing handles of index at most two with π_i(TOP/DIFF), i≤2, and points to DIFF surgery. The needed relative section theorem is standard low-connectivity, not a new unusual obstruction generated by this project.

FQ8.3B supplies the efficient published package: TOP/PL≃K(Z/2,3), and PL/DIFF six-connected. The fiber sequence PL/O→TOP/O→TOP/PL therefore makes TOP/O two-connected. Finite relative cellular obstruction theory gives the section over the three-dimensional pair, followed by actual relative realization. This requires reductions with the given germs, not arbitrary prescribed bases. It also requires the actual choice assumptions of the theorem imported into the project; the published book does not by itself certify an ACω formalization.

If instead one reconstructs this low-connectivity calculation through KS IV10.12 and V AppendixB, its complete logical chain is as follows.

1. **Six-dimensional torus realization.** IV10.12 printed201 (PDF209), using V5.3 printed247–250 (PDF255–258), realizes a j-family by a relative CAT structure on X_j=I^j×T^(6−j), j=0,1,2. Dimension six avoids the five-dimensional boundary qualification. Forgetting to homotopy-CAT structures lands in the subset invariant under all standard finite torus covers.
2. **Normal invariants.** V AppendixB printed265–268 (PDF273–276) identifies normal maps with relative G/CAT classes via the Pontryagin–Thom construction and splits the torus factors. A cover of degree λ multiplies a summand by λ when that summand uses the covered circle. Transfer invariants therefore leave just π_j(G/CAT).
3. **The low normal calculation.** Printed267 gives the low normal groups and Arf map. For j=0,1 the invariant normal class is zero directly. For j=2 it is Z/2, detected injectively by the two-dimensional Arf obstruction. The degree-four signature/Rohlin computation is not needed for this restricted j≤2 target.
4. **Geometric Arf exclusion.** PropositionB.1, printed269 (PDF277), crosses with P=(CP²)². For a homotopy equivalence f, it arranges P×f as a bundle map over torus directions and restricts to a homotopy equivalence of fibers. The fiber normal obstruction is then zero. Ordinary transversality alone does NOT supply that homotopy equivalence of fibers.
5. **Fibering supplier.** KS IV AppendixB.3, printed211–212 (PDF219–220), gives Farrell's circle-fibering hypotheses: compact CAT manifold dimension at least six; already fibered boundary; pulled-back infinite cyclic cover of finite homotopy type; free-abelian (or specified larger) fundamental group. It cites Farrell's fibering proof and the K₀/Whitehead inputs. The KS bibliography identifies Farrell, *The obstruction to fibering a manifold over a circle*, ICM Nice1970 vol.2,69–72. That paper itself was not acquired/read in this task.
6. **Actual surgery action and exactness.** V AppendixB printed269 (PDF277) uses the five-term normal-invariant/surgery/structure sequence. Its geometric L(X) is represented by actual normal bordisms with specified incoming and side data; the identification with L_(m+1)(π₁X) is Wall's §§5.8 and6.5, not a conclusion of the Whitehead calculation.
7. **Geometric circle splitting.** PropositionB.4, printed270 (PDF278), uses fibering and transverse restriction before cutting along the circle. A restricted normal nullbordism is thickened laterally to make a representative the identity at the cut. The complementary representative is then a cut-interval problem. This gives the L-group circle splitting and its transfer action. The cited sources are Shaneson, *Wall's surgery obstruction groups for Z×G*, Ann.Math.90(1969),296–334, and Wall §13A.8. Their full proofs were not read here.
8. **Periodicity and invariant cokernel.** Printed266 and271 (PDF274,279) use Wall geometric periodicity §9.9 for product with P and decompose the action. Transfer invariants retain only the cokernel of π_(j+1)(G/CAT)→L_(j+1)(Z). For j=0,1,2 those cokernels vanish: the odd targets in degrees one and three are zero and the degree-two Arf map is onto. Thus the invariant structure class is trivial.
9. **Geometric identification and concordance.** An actual smooth/PL relative h-cobordism, with zero torsion, gives a marked CAT identification by relative s-cobordism. KS I introduction printed4 (PDF12) explicitly states the relative boundary version in dimensions at least six. V5.3 printed248–250 then uses finite covers and a small homeomorphism argument; IV10.12 printed201 gives the bounded universal-cover dilation alternative for injectivity. This returns the actual nullconcordance of the original family.

The dimensions are explicit. X_j has dimension6; the structure-comparison h-cobordism has dimension7. The periodic normal map P×f has dimension14. Its final fiber has dimension8+j, so successive circle-fibering stages operate in dimensions at least9+j, safely above the six-dimensional threshold. In the periodic surgery action, the normal bordism starts in dimension15; after circle cuts its base normal-bordism dimension is9+j. These are not instances of an adopted theorem restricted to source dimension6 or7.
