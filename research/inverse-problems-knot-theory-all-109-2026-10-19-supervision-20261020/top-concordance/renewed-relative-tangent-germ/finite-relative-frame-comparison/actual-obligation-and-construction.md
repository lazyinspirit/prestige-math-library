## Actual data and conclusion required

Ambient height a and tube height b remain distinct. Over an open protected neighborhood U_A of both ends and the seam K, these germs prescribe an actual stable vector reduction of the tangent TOP microbundle. They are not merely prescribed vector-bundle classes.

The desired output is an actual vector bundle ξ on a neighborhood U of C and an actual finite-stabilized TOP tangent-bundle isomorphism to ξ^TOP, whose reduction agrees with the prescribed reduction on a smaller open protected neighborhood. A homotopy of classifying maps without this relative realization is not the output.

Choose nested closed collar subpairs A₀⊂int_C A₁⊂int_C A₂ of the protected part of C, all contained in U_A∩C, and a finite cell decomposition of (C,A₀) respecting their boundaries. The entire open protected germ can be shrunk to A₀ without changing the original local requirement. The dimension is three.

## Why the absolute reduction does not finish the cells

After an absolute TOP-frame identification, the two end reductions are two actual sections of the same TOP/O bundle. The required extension is a relative concordance of those sections. The absolute reduction supplies a reference section, but not a nullhomotopy of their difference. Already with N=S², comparison of the two sections can carry a π₂(TOP/O) obstruction. In the torus case the lower skeleta also involve π₀ and π₁. Neither w₁,w₂ nor vanishing of a fourth cohomology group computes these relative section obstructions.

There is no established counterexample here: the needed low groups are known to vanish. The hold is the missing actual proof and finite relative realization of that established vanishing under the declared choice assumptions.
