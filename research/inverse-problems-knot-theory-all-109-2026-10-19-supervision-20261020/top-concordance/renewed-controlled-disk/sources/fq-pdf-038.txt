2.2 Proper immersions

A proper immersion of a capped surface is an immersion in which the
body has no selfintersections or intersections with the caps. A proper
immersion can be thought of as an immersion of the starting surface in
which all the selfintersections are “more subtle,” in the sense discussed
in 2.1.
