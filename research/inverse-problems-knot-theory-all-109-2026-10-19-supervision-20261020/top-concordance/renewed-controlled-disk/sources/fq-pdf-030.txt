1.9 Transverse spheres

A transverse sphere for a connected surface A is a framed immersed
sphere whose image intersects A in exactly one point. If A is not con-
nected then transverse spheres for A are transverse spheres for each
component disjoint from the other components. We will use a super-
script t to denote transverse spheres: A‘.

Note that if there is one transverse sphere for A then we can get
arbitrarily many by taking parallel copies.
