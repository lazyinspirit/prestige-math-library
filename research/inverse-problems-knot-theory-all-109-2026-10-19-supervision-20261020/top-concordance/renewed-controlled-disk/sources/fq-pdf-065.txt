3.8 Convergent infinite towers

Proper immersions of towers are embeddings except for intersections
among the tower caps. The idea here is that if we raise tower height
infinitely then there will be no tower caps, so no extra intersections. For
this to be useful the infinite constriction must converge in an appropri-
ate sense, which is arranged with the tower squeezing lemma 3.7.
