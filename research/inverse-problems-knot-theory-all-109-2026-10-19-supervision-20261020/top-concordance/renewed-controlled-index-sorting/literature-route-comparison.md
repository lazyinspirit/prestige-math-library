## Milnor: genuine rearrangement, without smallness

Primary source: J. Milnor, *Lectures on the h-cobordism theorem*, Section4.
Cached PDF: `../renewed-marked-torus/sources/milnor-h-cobordism.pdf`.
New reading extent: Theorem4.1/proof printed37--39/PDF42--44 in full;
Theorem4.4, Lemmas4.6--4.7 and their proofs printed40--43/PDF45--48 in full;
Theorem4.8 statement printed44/PDF49. Printed37 was visually confirmed.

Theorem4.1 keeps the vector field when the two complete critical trajectory
sets are disjoint. A separator constant on trajectories and increasing
interval maps change critical values. Theorem4.4 first alters a field near
a regular level, using Sard to separate the relevant spheres; Lemma4.7
implements a level isotopy by changing the field in a regular band.
Theorem4.8 then gives self-indexing. These prove index order, but state no
control-image bound for whole products or initial fibers. Their arbitrary
iteration therefore cannot by itself justify our controlled smallness.
A separate controlled neighborhood mechanism is needed before or during
that iteration. The fixed-field color argument in our new proof is one
such larger mechanism, not a claim found in Milnor.

## Direct finite simplicial route and its exact category boundary

Primary source: C. P. Rourke and B. J. Sanderson, *Introduction to
Piecewise-Linear Topology*, Chapter6, printed82--83/PDF90--91,
Proposition6.9 and its full proof. Also read Corollary3.9 and its short
proof, printed34/PDF42. Cached full PDF:
`sources/rourke-sanderson-pl.pdf`, acquired from
https://www.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/rsplx.pdf .

For a relative triangulation (K,K0), order simplices outside K0 by dimension
and take the indicated closed stars in the second derived subdivision.
Proposition6.9 constructs actual PL characteristic products of index equal
to simplex dimension, together with a derived-neighborhood incoming collar.
Its proof uses pseudo-radial projection and a regular-neighborhood product
recognition. All material is in the derived stars. Consequently sufficiently
fine stars give geometric smallness directly, and the collar can be kept
in the prescribed end product. This avoids a handle-count ancestry chain.
It is a PL construction; it is not, as stated, a smooth-rounding theorem.

Statement-only local inputs consulted (NO proofs audited):

* `thm-compact-smooth-manifolds-with-corners-admit-relative-finite-triangulations`:
  although its metadata includes ambient AC, its Statement explicitly makes
  that ambient assumption dispensable for its finite construction. It supplies
  finite corner-adapted whole-star triangulations, admissible relative
  boundary/protected data, and compatible subdivision. Thus the declared
  Statement does not block CC use.
* `def-relative-smooth-triangulation-and-projection-preserving-fitting`:
  whole-star means the differential on the ENTIRE tangent fan is injective,
  with the correct corner-orthant fan. Simplexwise nondegeneracy alone is
  insufficient. Relative boundary data must satisfy that fan compatibility.

For a smooth-only consumer the remaining narrow bridge would be to choose
and round those finite star/normal products compatibly in their prepared
smooth stars, with actual attaching collars and protected-end tracks. One
must earn that finite smooth bridge; PL-to-smooth is not automatic from
Proposition6.9. This report does not author it. If compatible PL handles are
already sufficient, the bridge is unnecessary, but the consumer category
