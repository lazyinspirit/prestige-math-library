2. **The flat-disk boundary-neighborhood passage is still an assertion at its decisive point.** The sentence that every remaining mapping-cylinder interval starts at the collapsed disk and meets the boundary neighborhood in an initial interval needs an actual mapping cylinder and monotonicity verification. There is a concrete focused construction: in flat coordinates let `D={(x,0):|x|<=1}` and let `B={dist(v,D)<=R}` be its small rounded convex normal neighborhood. Nearest-point projection to the convex disk is unique. Every point of `B-D` has the unique form `p+t(b-p)` with `p=projection_D(b)`, `b in boundary B`, and `0<t<=1`. Hence `B` is the mapping cylinder of `projection_D|boundary B`, and `B/D` is the cone on `boundary B=S^3`. Choose the standard boundary-circle neighborhood `N_r={dist(v,boundary D)<=r}`, `r<R`. If `p` is interior to `D`, its boundary-distance along that interval is `sqrt(dist(p,boundary D)^2+t^2 R^2)`; if `p` lies on `boundary D`, it is `tR`. These are nondecreasing. Thus `D union N_r` meets each interval in an initial segment and its disk quotient is actually starlike. State the relative standard-neighborhood identification which transports the actual 4.4 fiber to this model. The author's independently supplied explanation agrees with this construction; it must be written in the proof.
