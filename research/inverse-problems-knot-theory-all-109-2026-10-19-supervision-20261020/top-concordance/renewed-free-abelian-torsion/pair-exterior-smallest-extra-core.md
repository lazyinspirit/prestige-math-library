# Exact additional algebra for the punctured-torus exterior

Research analysis by `/root/top_free_abelian_torsion`, 2026-10-20. This is a
missing-supplier formulation, not a vanishing proof or an open-problem item.

The smooth pair owner identifies the complement group as Z^r×F_2, with r≤4 for
the current base-dimension-three construction and potentially r≤5 in the next
dimension. It must not be replaced by its abelianization Z^{r+2}. Ambient
Whitehead vanishing gives no automatic assertion about a complement torsion
class in the kernel of the induced homomorphism.

Let R=Z[Z^r]. For free generators a,b of F_2, the group-ring normal form gives

R[F_2]=R[a,a^{-1}] *_R R[b,b^{-1}].

A smallest sufficient additional algebraic statement is the surjectivity

K_1(R[a,a^{-1}]) ⊕ K_1(R[b,b^{-1}]) → K_1(R[F_2]).

Equivalently, every invertible matrix over R[F_2], after stabilization, is a
product of elementary matrices and matrices invertible over one of the two
vertex Laurent rings. This is the precise matrix-factorization obligation.
It is not a consequence of ordinary Gaussian elimination over R, which is
not a PID when r>0.

The reduction from this statement to the needed vanishing is complete:
the separate new local-algebra-proof.md computes each vertex K_1 as generated
by -1, the r central Laurent variables, and its own free generator a or b.
Surjectivity therefore makes K_1(R[F_2]) generated by the classes of group-ring
units ±g. Its quotient Wh(Z^r×F_2) is zero. For this implication neither a full
Mayer–Vietoris sequence nor a K_0 computation for R[F_2] is needed.

A generalized-free-product K-theory route would prove the displayed
surjectivity by regular-base Nil vanishing and the exact K_1–K_0 sequence.
The base R is noetherian of finite global dimension by the explicit arguments
in the new algebra draft. Both vertex rings split as R plus an R-bimodule
free on nonzero powers of their generator, so the required purity/flatness
conditions must be checked in their literal untwisted form. The map on base
K_0 is n↦(n,−n) into Z⊕Z and is injective. Once the genuine no-Nil exact
sequence has been proved, exactness and that injection do imply surjectivity.
This paragraph is a plan identifying the missing theorem, not a local proof
of that sequence or of Nil vanishing for a generalized free product.

Actual source work for this additional statement: no full primary generalized-
free-product proof has yet been read in this lane. No claim of its local
completion or canonical availability is made. The freeabelian argument has not
been extended to this noncommutative group ring.
