# Exact marked disk assembly from the shared relative normal construction

Renewed user-authorized development. This file does not assert that the shared four-dimensional normal construction has been completed. It records the disk-specific geometric assembly so that no framing or actual-germ issue is left hidden when that construction is supplied. No canonical carrier, accepted supplier, metadata or native state is changed.

Let `e:D²→B⁴` be the actual locally flat proper disk, collared at its boundary. Its prescribed positive normal disk germ is `n:S¹×D²_ε→S³`, with zero longitude. On the actual end collar the prescribed disk-bundle embedding is `(s,u,z)↦c(n(s,z),u)`.

Double the collared pair along its actual boundary collar. The double ambient manifold is the standard topological `S⁴`, the doubled disk is a locally flat sphere, and the supplied germ defines an actual embedded oriented vector two-plane bundle on an annulus of that sphere about its equator. Take a smaller closed annulus as the protected set in the shared relative normal construction. Local pair charts show that each local complement group is `Z`; the sphere and its product with a line are manifolds. These are the normal construction's actual local hypotheses. Its required output is an embedded normal vector bundle on the whole doubled sphere, equal to this particular embedded bundle on a neighborhood of the protected annulus. An arbitrary unmarked tube does not meet the requirement.

Restrict to the original disk. On its fixed end annulus retain the supplied tube; elsewhere shrink its fiber radii continuously so the image lies in the correct hemisphere and avoids the ambient boundary. This is possible because the zero section is in the interior there, and local compact subsets have positive distance from the ambient boundary. A partition of unity gives one positive radius function below the finitely many local admissible radii. On the compact disk, after trivialization, restrict to a uniform smaller closed disk radius.

The oriented vector bundle on the disk admits a global frame. For completeness its possible disagreement with the prescribed boundary frame is not automatically trivial. Choose the first vector of a global frame and a small parallel section. Its image is a proper disk disjoint from the zero disk. Its boundary push therefore has linking zero with the boundary of the zero disk: represent the two proper fundamental classes by finite singular chains in their disjoint neighborhoods and approximate the chains, including their boundary cycles in `S³`, by piecewise smooth chains in the same disjoint neighborhoods. Their intersection is empty. The usual boundary-linking/intersection identity yields zero; the approximations preserve the linking number by the small homologies inside those neighborhoods. This argument uses the actual disjoint compact disks, and does not assert that either disk is smooth.

Write the global frame in the specified end frame. Its first vector has winding equal to the linking of this parallel boundary with the knot, since the specified end frame has zero longitude. Thus the winding is zero. The full change-of-frame matrix `A:S¹→GL⁺(2,R)` also has zero winding. Its polar decomposition is `A=R_φ P`, where `P` is positive symmetric and the rotation angle has a periodic real lift `φ` precisely because that winding is zero. Choose a radial cutoff `χ` equal to zero near the center and one on an outer annulus. The formula

`R_{χ(r)φ(s)}((1−χ(r))I+χ(r)P(s))`

extends the boundary change of frame to an invertible positive-determinant matrix on the whole disk. To retain the entire specified end germ, first take the actual change-of-frame matrix on a restricted end annulus, extend its inner boundary by this formula over the remaining inner disk, and leave its values on that annulus untouched. Correcting the global frame by the resulting matrix gives a frame equal to the prescribed one on that actual annulus. Uniform fiber-radius restriction then gives the required embedding `T:D²×D²_η→B⁴` with literal equality `T((s,u),z)=c(n(s,z),u)` on the restricted end collar, not merely an isomorphism class of normal bundles.

The only shared normal input used above is the actual relative embedded vector-bundle extension, including its agreement on the protected annulus. The zero-longitude framing correction and whole-disk restriction are explicit. If the input were merely an unmarked neighborhood or a topological bundle without the stated relative vector-bundle data, it would not license this assembly.
