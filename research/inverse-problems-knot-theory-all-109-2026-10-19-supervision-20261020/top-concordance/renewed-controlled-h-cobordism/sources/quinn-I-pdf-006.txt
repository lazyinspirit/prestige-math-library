In recent years so many of the consequences of the end theorem were
proved independently that it became doubtful that there could be very many
obstructions. The investigation was resumed in the fall of 1977. A proof
of the theorem for 1-LC ends was obtained in early 1978 by “classifying”
tame ends, and then comparing with the 1-sided taming theorem of

Cernavskii, Seebeck, and Ferry (Ferry [1]) to see that they all had comple-
tions.

Finally the arrival of T. A. Chapman’s preprint [3] made it inconceivable
that the geometric group conjectures were false. The treatment presented
here followed shortly.

Since this paper was written there have been two further developments.
In Part II the main theorem is extended to ends without the Wh( x Z") = 0
restriction. An obstruction is encountered (true to our suspicions) in the
generalized homology group Hi/(X; 5(z)), where S(z) is a spectrum similar
to Hatcher’s space.

The second development is a surgery theory for ends. The simplest
application is that every ANR homology manifold has a manifold resolution.
Transversality is used to construct a “degree 1” end over the homology
manifold, surgery is used to make it tame, and this paper provides a
completion which is automatically a resolution. (See 3.4.2.)
