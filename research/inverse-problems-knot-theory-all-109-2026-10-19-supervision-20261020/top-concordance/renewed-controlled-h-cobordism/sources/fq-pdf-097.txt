5.5. References

The embedding theorem for disks in a simply connected manifold ap-
pears in Freedman [2], and the poly-(finite or cyclic) case is sketched in
Freedman [4]. The 7 -null theorem has several antecedents, eg. Freed-
man [4], theorem 5. The statement given—which we suspect to be the
best possible without fundamental group restrictions—is new here.

Embedding up to s-cobordism is also considered here for the first time,
but the result is essentially equivalent to theorem 1 of Freedman [6]. The
controlled embedding theorem comes from Quinn [3].
