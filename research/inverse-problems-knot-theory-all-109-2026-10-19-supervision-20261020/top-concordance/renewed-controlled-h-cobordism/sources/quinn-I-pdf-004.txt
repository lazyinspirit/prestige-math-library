The area of the applications has been dominated until now by engulfing
techniques. Except for a few 5-dimensional cases, these results can now be
obtained in stronger forms by straightforward, almost routine, applications
of the end theorem and related techniques. Roughly speaking, the end
theorem replaces most of the engulfing. The results used in the proofs of
the applications are those of this paper, some surgery theory, the theorem
of Edwards [1] that cell-like manifold images with the 2-dise property are
manifolds, and an “unerumpling” theorem of Cannon, Bryant, and Lacher
ful.

In brief the results are:

3.1.1 A 1-LC embedding of an ANR in a manifold of dimension >6 has a
mapping cylinder neighborhood.

3.2.2 If dim X = 5, the following are equivalent: (a) X is a cell-like image
of a manifold. (b) X is a manifold factor. (c) X x R’ is a manifold.

3.3.2. An approximate fibration with total space a manifold of dimension
=n + 5, and base a polyhedron of dimension n, is concordant to a projection
of a block bundle with topological manifold fibers (there is also a 7, condi-
tion on the fiber).

3.4.1 If Mis a manifold of dimension >6, and the codimension of XC M is
28, X is locally flat if and only if X is a manifold factor and the embedding
is 1-LC.

Section 4 contains proofs of two of the results of Section 3 which were

The remainder of the paper is devoted to the proof of the end theorem.
In outline it is very similar to the classical case. We begin with nested
sequences of manifold neighborhoods of the end. Handles are deleted from
these neighborhoods to make them highly connected. Finally when the
neighborhoods are n—3 connected a “stable algebra” difficulty is encountered.
