Ends of maps, I

Abstract

Suppose e: M—> X is a map from a (finite dimensional) manifold to a
locally compact space. Conditions on e are given under which it can be
extended to a proper map e’: M’— X by adding boundary to M. This theorem
is applied to construct a mapping cylinder neighborhood for a 1-LC ANR in
a manifold, resolutions of manifold factors, block bundle approximations to
approximate fibrations, local flatness of embeddings, and locally flat approxi-
mations of wild embeddings.
