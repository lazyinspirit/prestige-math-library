The basic result is that certain 5-dimensional s-cobordisms are prod-

7.1. Compact s-cobordisms

Recall that an h-cobordism (rel boundary) is a manifold W with
OW = MoU ™M, a union of submanifolds, such that W deformation
retracts to each M;. A compact h-cobordism has a Whitehead torsion
+(W, Mo) € Wh(,;W) defined (see Cohen [1j, Milnor [2]), and an s-
cobordism is defined to be an h-cobordism with vanishing torsion. In
the controlled and proper cases more elaborate “torsions” are defined,
and the vanishing of these is used to define s-cobordisms.

7.1A Theorem. A compact s-cobordism of dimension 5 with good fun-
damental group has a topological product structure.

Whitehead groups of poly-(finite or cyclic) groups are known to be
primarily determined by finite subgroups (Quinn [9]). In particular if
the group is torsion free the Whitehead group vanishes (Farrell and
Hsiang [1]), and every h-cobordism is a product.
