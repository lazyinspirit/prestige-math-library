The main theorem applied to this example gives the following theorem:
If XCM is a 1-LC embedding without interior of an ANR in a closed

Organization of the paper

We have tried to arrange the paper for the convenience of those more
interested in the applications of the theorem than savoring every intricacy
of the proof.

Section 1 contains definitions, and prototypical theorems: existence of
completions (1.4), uniqueness up to isotopy (1.5), and a homological charac-
terization of the tameness condition (1.7).

In Section 2, a relative version of the end theorem is given. After that
is an approximate version of the end theorem, and the thin h-cobordism
theorem. The thin h-cobordism theorem is the source of the uniqueness
statements, and it together with the approximate end theorem and some
homotopy theory from Section 5 are shown to imply the end theorem.

Section 3 is devoted to applications. Strictly speaking we use the
theorems stated in Sections 1 and 2, and the homological characterizations of
the latter part of Section 5. Actually the proofs are intelligible after reading
Section 1 and the thin h-cobordism theorem (2.6, 2.7).
