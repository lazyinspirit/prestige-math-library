## Actual equivariant lift and the integer D

is a homeomorphism lifting h through the standard torus cover. Its torus displacement is at most C/D, while its ball displacement need not be small.

At a boundary component, F(x,z)=x and A(x,z) is a constant integer vector: h fixes the boundary, and the continuous integer-valued A is constant on each connected boundary component of B^s×R^q. When s≥2, choose the lift so that this constant is zero. When s=1, there are two constants b−,b+; do not equate them. Let b(x) be their affine interpolation on the interval B^1. The explicit shear
