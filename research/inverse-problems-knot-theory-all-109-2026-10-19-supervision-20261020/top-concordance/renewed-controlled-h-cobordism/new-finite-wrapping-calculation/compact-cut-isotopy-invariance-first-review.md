## Actual compact cut cobordisms

For any λ choose μ>λ+C. Then H_t(V×(−∞,λ]) is disjoint from V×{μ}, lies below that horizontal cut, and contains all points of sufficiently negative height. Define

Q_t(λ,μ)=H_t(V×[λ,∞)) ∩ (V×(−∞,μ]).

This is an actual compact TOP cobordism triad, from the parametrized lower cut H_t(V×λ) to V×μ. Indeed the lower cut is locally flat with its collar supplied by H_t(V×[λ−η,λ+η]); the upper cut has its standard vertical collar and is disjoint from that collar after making η smaller. The side is the portion in ∂V×R; these actual collars also give the product corners. The bound C puts Q_t inside a compact slab. No global triangulation or source smoothing is asserted.

Changing λ or μ only changes this triad by finite product-collar absorption. For λ<λ′ choose μ above the compact H_t(V×[λ,λ′+η]). In the collar H_t(V×[λ,λ′+η]) use an increasing interval homeomorphism [λ,λ′+η]→[λ′,λ′+η], equal to identity near the right endpoint. Extend by identity elsewhere in Q_t. This sends the lower boundary λ to λ′ and fixes the upper cut; it explicitly removes the added collar. Changing μ uses the same construction in the standard vertical upper collar. Comparisons for arbitrary pairs of cut choices pass through a common larger μ. Thus no abstract h-cobordism classification is involved in cut independence.

## The endpoint localization gives the actual comparison

Choose the localization G above and then increase μ beyond its compact support, beyond both lower cuts, and beyond the bounds needed for Q_0,Q_1. Thus G fixes an OPEN neighborhood of V×μ. On the lower cut,

G H_0(x,λ)=H_1(x,λ).

Since G is identity outside a compact set, it preserves both ends of V×R. The lower cut separates the two ends, so G carries the positive closed side of H_0(V×λ) to the positive closed side of H_1(V×λ). Equivalently, the positive side is the component whose closure contains every sufficiently high horizontal level; this characterizes the side without a graph assumption. G also preserves ∂V×R. Consequently its restriction is an ACTUAL homeomorphism of cobordism triads

G:Q_0(λ,μ) → Q_1(λ,μ).

It is identity near the upper boundary and agrees with H_1H_0^−1 near the lower boundary. The side is preserved setwise. Its side parametrization is not asserted fixed when the original H_t moves that boundary; this is precisely the unmarked triad invariant used in the first wrapping step. Later full boundary marking is a different operation.

Since H_1=T^a, the terminal cut is literally

Q_1(λ,μ)=V×[λ+a,μ].

It is a product triad. Therefore the initial actual cut cobordism is a product triad, PROVIDED the exact pending TOP localization operation has been supplied. This is an explicit restriction of G, not an inference from a proper homotopy equivalence or from the cover calculation alone.
