# Five-dimensional geometric stage

This is the geometric portion of the intended controlled proof. It assumes an independently supplied small DIFF/PL handlebody and the controlled deformation retractions. It does not assume TOP handlebody existence and therefore does not invoke the later normal-bundle conclusion. The controlled matrix trivialization and controlled embedded Whitney-disk construction remain separate suppliers under construction.

## Removing indices0,1,4,5

Here is the five-dimensional specialization of the argument actually read in Quinn I6.1–6.3, printed308–314. Work first over a compact control region with a buffer, and keep the existing collar fixed over the smaller protected region. All new paths and homotopies below are chosen inside that buffer; tracks are estimated before choosing the input smallness constant.

For each0-handle, the deformation to the incoming boundary gives a small path to the incoming collar. Push this path into the0/1-skeleton. It traverses only handles whose image meets its track, so its control diameter increases by at most a fixed dimension-dependent multiple of the handle size. Delete loops and terminate the path at its first meeting with the collar. The resulting paths can share0- and1-handles. Resolve this combinatorially: if m paths traverse a0-handle, replace its ball by m ordered0-handles joined by m−1 short1-handles; if p paths traverse a1-handle, subdivide its interval direction into p parallel ordered1-handles connected by p−1 short2-handles. Associate each path with its own copy in the same order at both ends. The order agreement makes the resolved paths pairwise disjoint. These are actual local handle equivalences in the product neighborhoods, not a general-position assertion about arbitrary higher attaching maps. Transport every higher attaching map by the induced boundary diffeomorphism/PL isomorphism of the local equivalence. Thus later handles retain their full attaching germs, even if their old maps met a subdivision strip.

Each resolved path together with its0/1-handles is a thickened tree meeting the incoming collar in one boundary ball. Its regular neighborhood is a ball: remove leaf vertices inductively, at each stage gluing two balls along a boundary ball, using the collar coordinates on that gluing ball to straighten the union to one ball. Absorb these pairwise disjoint balls into the collar. This removes the original0-handles and changes only the chosen small neighborhoods. The same description estimates the enlarged collar tracks by the diameter of the resolved paths. Quinn's displayed bound at this stage is (n+4) times the input size.

For a1-handle, choose its core interval and use the deformation retraction to obtain a small relative nullhomotopy of that interval in the cobordism, with its two ends carried to the incoming collar. Push its interior into the2-skeleton. Because a2-disc and the1-handle cores have dimensions2 and1 in dimension5, a small general-position perturbation makes the disc disjoint from those cores. Push the remaining portions radially out to the intermediate boundary. This gives the relative nullhomotopy in the union of the intermediate boundary and the2-handles.

Intersect the disc with the old2-handles after transverse perturbation and cut out the resulting finite preimage discs. In an old5-dimensional2-handle D²×D³, replace each such passage by an actual parallel disc D²×{v} in its OUTGOING boundary D²×S², choosing finitely many distinct v∈S² and retaining their product framings. These are not the interior core D²×{0}. In the common complement of the old attaching tubes join the corresponding preimage components by a tree of arcs. Their images can be made embedded and pairwise disjoint in the four-dimensional boundary by1-dimensional general position, avoiding the chosen parallel discs except at their designated ends. After the arcs are fixed, choose their bands narrow enough that the bands and parallel discs are disjoint except for the prescribed band attachments. Their union is an actual embedded disc B in the upper boundary AFTER the old2-handles. This establishes embedding of the image, not merely a regular neighborhood in the domain.

The remainder of the original small nullhomotopy gives a homotopy of∂B in that upper boundary to a loop ℓ in the common complement of the old attaching tubes. Viewed in the old boundary AFTER the1-handles and BEFORE the2-handles, ℓ crosses the belt sphere of the selected1-handle once and every other such belt sphere zero times. Keep this level distinction: the isotopy from∂B toℓ is initially in the UPPER boundary. A loop homotopy in a4-manifold can be made an isotopy rel its ends: potential coincidences of distinct loop points have dimension1+1+1−4<0, and the derivative-zero condition is also avoidable. For the finite collection the same perturbation keeps distinct loops disjoint. Extend this already given smooth/PL isotopy ambiently in its tubular products, carrying all later attaching maps.

Introduce a cancelling2/3-handle pair along B in the upper boundary, with the2-handle framing from B. Apply the upper-boundary isotopy so its attaching circle becomesℓ. Sinceℓ is in the common complement, the new2-handle may now be moved BEFORE the old2-handles using the product identification on that complement. Cancel it with the selected old1-handle. Carry every old2-handle attaching map, the new3-handle, and every higher attaching map by the resulting actual boundary isomorphism. The new3-handle remains allowed. Thus the old1-handle is eliminated without silently identifying the two different level boundaries or discarding later attaching data.

All of these operations stay in the original small nullhomotopy and the finitely many small handle products it meets. They have a fixed finite number of geometrical stages; the number of intersections does not increase the control-space region of one package. Choose the initial smallness backwards through those stage bounds. In the proper case require the supplied nullhomotopies and all packages to be locally finite, either directly or because their control neighborhoods are locally finite relatively compact stars, the initial centers are proper, and the operating control map is proper. At a fixed index use disjoint extended handle neighborhoods, including their attaching collars, and transport their attachments simultaneously; there is no uncontrolled infinite sequential chain of moves.
Turn the handlebody upside down and repeat the same0/1 procedure from the outgoing face. In the original indexing this removes5/4-handles. Removing an indexk handle in the described procedure changes only indicesk,k+1,k+2; hence the outgoing operation does not reintroduce the removed0/1-handles. The result has only2- and3-handles. It still agrees with the supplied product on the protected region, since all neighborhoods were chosen outside its buffered preimage.

This specializes the source's general geometrical-connectivity induction to the only low indices needed here. It avoids its higher-index Hurewicz/excision step and does not apply the four-dimensional Whitney trick at this point.

## Actual cancellation once embedded Whitney discs have been supplied

In the intermediate four-manifold let A_i be the framed attaching2-spheres of the3-handles and B_i the belt2-spheres of the2-handles. Suppose that small handle slides and allowable identity stabilizations have already put the based intersection matrix into the identity, and suppose small pairwise disjoint framed embedded Whitney discs have been constructed for every extra A∩B pair, with interiors disjoint from all A and B.

Each Whitney disc has a product neighborhood fixed by its framing. In that neighborhood the two sheets meeting its boundary arcs can be replaced by the opposite pair of faces of the product disc; the replacement is an isotopy of the attaching sphere, keeping its boundary attachments and every B-sphere fixed. Its support lies in that product neighborhood. Disjoint Whitney discs permit these moves simultaneously. They eliminate exactly the paired intersections; the distinguished identity intersection of A_i with B_i remains. Thus each3-handle attaches across precisely one belt sphere of a2-handle, once, and misses all other belt spheres. The attaching framing extends over the Whitney move by its given normal product, so no new twist is introduced.

The new attaching spheres after these moves are only TOP: the helper supplies LOCALLY FLAT framed Whitney discs, not smooth ones. Use each supplied disc product and full boundary normal germ to conjugate the standard Whitney model into the actual manifold. The resulting attaching product for a geometrically dual2/3-handle pair likewise gives a TOP coordinate identification with the standard cancellation configuration. In the standard model the pair is removed by the familiar collar cancellation (equivalently the model u³−su plus a nondegenerate transverse quadratic form). Conjugate that model cancellation by the TOP coordinate identification just specified. Its conjugating map is not declared DIFF/PL. The result is therefore a TOP cancellation and a TOP product. Its forward and reverse tracks stay in that pair's actual product neighborhood.

Here is the basic category-defect localization in the incoming manifold M0. Before the TOP moves, the attaching region transfers to an M-regular neighborhood of a2-complex K0⊂M0, finite in the compact case and locally finite in the proper case: puncture the original attaching A-spheres where they meet the B-belt spheres, and identify their boundary edges along the original2-handle attaching circles. The helper supplies each embedded Whitney disc inside an actual M-regular neighborhood of its finite immersed tower T in the intermediate level. Transfer that neighborhood to M0 in the same surgery product charts. Away from B-tubes the transfer is the common-complement identification. Along a Whitney/tower boundary arc on B, cut off its collar at the boundary S²×S¹ of the belt tube. After undoing the2-handle this is S¹×S²⊂S¹×D³. In coordinates its old edge s↦(z(s),exp(iθ(s))) is completed by the band (s,t)↦(exp(iθ(s)),t z(s)),0≤t≤1, ending on the attaching circle at t=0. The endpoint bands attach to the already described punctured A-panels. These are2-dimensional parametrized bands in M0; after finite generic subdivision/perturbation they and the original finite tower panels give an M2-complex, not a five-dimensional trace. Their supplied product collars give an M-regular neighborhood N2. It contains the transferred attaching region and every TOP Whitney/cancellation support.

All original handle reorderings and lower/upper product transports are M operations. The ONLY category changes are inside these transferred Whitney/tower products and their TOP-conjugated cancellation charts. Consequently, in the final product coordinates M0×I→W, failure to be an M-isomorphism is confined to N2×I. In the compact operating case K0∪T is finite; in the proper case it is locally finite because the tower packages and transferred bands are locally finite. The complex K0∪T and N2 lie in the incoming FOUR-manifold; N2×I is their five-dimensional product trace. This proves the basic2-complex defect containment, finite on compact operating patches, needed for smooth1-complex displacement. No whole-tower geometric dual, regular-homotopy negligibility, or graph-neighborhood comparison is asserted by this two-file geometric argument.
The smallness estimate here is obtained from the actual supports, not from the statement that the manifolds happen to be h-cobordant. Choose a positive continuous subordinate control function so the union of each handle pair and every Whitney neighborhood it uses projects into one prescribed small ball. In a compact control patch take the minimum of the finitely many required constants; in a locally finite patch system choose a positive continuous minorant of the finite minima. Both forward and reverse product tracks lie in those same balls. The resulting product is consequently controlled in both directions. The same argument preserves the product over V when every selected control ball has closure disjoint from V.

Neither the existence of the controlled elementary matrix deformation nor the existence of these small embedded Whitney discs is asserted by this geometric stage. Those are the two independent difficult inputs, not consequences of cancellation.
