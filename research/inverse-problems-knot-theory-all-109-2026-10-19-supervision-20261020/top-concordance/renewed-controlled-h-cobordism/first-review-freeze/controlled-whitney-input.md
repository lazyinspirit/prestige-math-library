# Actual controlled Whitney input from the2/3 handle system

This is a new geometric construction for the exact helper interface in `../renewed-controlled-disk/INTERFACE.md`. It does not invoke an unprovided controlled disk theorem. It stops at framed immersed Whitney discs and framed geometric transverse spheres; replacing those discs by embedded ones is the independent new helper, which presently has a supplier-review hold.

## Small nullhomotopies in the intermediate level

Let W have a small DIFF/PL handlebody relative to M0 with only2/3-handles. Let L be the intermediate4-manifold after the2-handles. Controlled deformation retraction to M0, combined with the controlled1-connectivity of W→E, gives controlled lifting of relative2-complexes into M0: lift to W and compose with that retraction, using the sum of the two track bounds. The analogous statement holds for M1.

The same low-dimensional lifting property holds for L after a further fixed small enlargement. Here is a concrete surgery argument, rather than an inference from abstractπ1 equality. L is obtained from M0 by replacing finitely or locally finitely many S¹×D³ attaching tubes by D²×S². A loop in a new D²×S² piece can be moved to its boundary by making its D²-coordinate avoid0 and pushing radially to S¹; a generic1-dimensional map avoids a codimension2 central set. This gives a small annular homotopy, supported in that2-handle, from any new attaching loop to a loop in the old complement. Its reference image changes only within that small handle. Fill the resulting loop in M0 by the controlled lifting property. A generic2-disc in the4-manifold M0 misses the1-dimensional attaching circles, since2+1<4. Shrink the attaching tube radii inside their supplied product charts so the compact filling misses the whole tubes. The radius change is realized in each attaching product chart by an ambient isotopy; identify the changed intermediate level with the original by its inverse, carrying the prescribed boundary data through the displayed annulus rather than changing that data silently. The filling then lies in the common part of M0 and the changed L. Glue back the annular homotopy to recover the original loop exactly. All changes take place in the finitely many small handle tubes met by the input; for a locally finite input family the shrinkings are chosen locally finitely. This also applies cell by cell to relative2-complexes, keeping the already supplied subcomplex fixed.

Thus local reference-null Whitney loops in L have actual small immersed disc fillings in L. DIFF/PL general position gives generic immersions rel the fixed boundary arcs. No statement that every small loop is null is being made: it is the identity group label that supplies the reference nullhomotopy.

## Separate transverse spheres for the two collections

Write A_i for the attaching2-spheres of the3-handles and B_i for the belt2-spheres of the2-handles. The collections A and B are separately disjoint, framed, embedded spheres. The controlled matrix construction has put their based intersection numbers into λ(A_i,B_j)=δ_ij in the appropriate local group ring.

For B_i the2-handle contains a core disc D²×{point} meeting its belt sphere once. Its boundary is a small parallel of the attaching circle in M0. That circle bounds a small disc in W via the2-handle core; composing with the controlled retraction to M0 gives a small nullhomotopy in M0. Approximate this disc generically, keeping its collar fixed, and off every attaching circle. Shrinking tube radii as above lets the disc join the handle core to form an immersed sphere meeting B_i once and every other B_j zero times. Its normal framing is not needed at this intermediate stage. The dual handlebody and the retraction to M1 supply analogous unframed geometric transverse spheres for A.

This is the actual construction on FQ printed104/PDF110. Its control comes from the core disc and the given retraction, not from a new normal-bundle theorem for arbitrary surfaces.

## Framed geometric transverse spheres for A∪B

Take small framed parallel copies A' and B'. The matrix condition makes A'_i an algebraic dual to B_i and B'_i an algebraic dual to A_i, with extra intersections in opposite-sign equal-label pairs. Choose Whitney boundary arcs for those pairs and small immersed Whitney fillings V for A'∩B, W for B'∩A using the preceding lifting argument. On each original sphere the arcs can be chosen pairwise disjoint and away from the distinguished unit points: a finite embedded forest of intervals does not separate S², so add the arcs one at a time in its connected complement. Different spheres are already disjoint; local finiteness reduces each compact sphere to finitely many arcs.

Tube the interiors of V off B using the separate geometric transverse spheres of B. These are ordinary connected sums guided along arcs of B to the distinguished transverse point. Correct a possible Whitney framing defect by spinning the boundary edge lying on A'. Do the dual operation for W, using the transverse spheres of A and spinning on B'. Now V misses B and W misses A in their interiors. They may still meet the other original collection.

For an interior A∩V point choose an arc in V to its boundary edge on B. Push the A sheet along this arc and through B by a finger move. This removes that A∩V point and adds only a cancelling pair of A∩B points. Similarly push B off W by fingers through A. The guiding arcs can be chosen pairwise disjoint and away from A'∪B', by1-dimensional general position in the4-manifold; the small transverse neighborhoods are chosen after the arcs. Interpreting the inverse of a finger of B through A as a finger of A through B allows B to be fixed throughout, by applying the inverse ambient isotopy to the whole configuration. Consequently these moves are realized by an isotopy of the attaching spheres A; the handlebody itself remains DIFF/PL.

The interiors of V∪W now miss A∪B. Perform the immersed Whitney moves on A'∪B' across these discs. The resulting spheres are framed immersions, and each meets its corresponding original sphere once and every other original sphere zero times. They are therefore geometric framed transverse spheres for the total collection A∪B. No zero self/mutual intersection forms of these auxiliary spheres are asserted. Their self-intersections are permitted by the helper's linking-torus construction.

Every tubing, spin and finger is confined to the original small handle spheres and their selected small filling neighborhoods. The parallel copies and guiding arcs introduce no excursion outside those packages. There are finitely many types of operations; choose the initial positive control function backwards through those finite bounds. An unbounded number of intersection points does not create an unbounded chain of control-space excursions, since all operations associated to one small sphere package remain within its already chosen neighborhood.

## Final immersed Whitney discs for cancellation

After the finger moves the intersection matrix remains the identity: every new pair has opposite signs and the same group label. Pair all extra A∩B intersections accordingly, retaining the distinguished unit point for each A_i,B_i. Choose disjoint boundary forests on the original spheres and the corresponding small reference-null Whitney loops. The intermediate-level lifting argument gives actual small immersed fillings. Correct each framing by spinning along a boundary edge. These operations can introduce intersections of disc interiors with the original surfaces, which are allowed at the helper's initial stage; the geometric transverse spheres just constructed are exactly the data its first cleanup step uses to remove them.

The complete input packages—original spheres, their geometric transverse spheres, Whitney arcs, immersed fillings and boundary product germs—are locally finite. For the relative situation, start outside the supplied V^{2ε} product neighborhood and choose each control neighborhood with closure outside V. Every reference filling and its controlled lift stays in those chosen neighborhoods, so all subsequent geometric constructions avoid the full preimage of V. Mere starting disjointness in W would be insufficient.

These data meet the exact new Whitney helper contract. Applying that helper once its held tower suppliers are actually repaired and accepted gives the embedded discs used in `five-dimensional-handle-stage.md`. This note does not assert that the held helper is already available or that its stronger whole-tower-dual variant has been proved.
