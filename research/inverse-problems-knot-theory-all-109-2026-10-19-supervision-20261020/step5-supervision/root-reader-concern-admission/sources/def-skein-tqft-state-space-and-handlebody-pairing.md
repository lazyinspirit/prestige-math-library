## Definition

Assume full AC for the surgery-presentation and universal-construction inputs below. Fix an even integer $p\ge4$, a primitive $2p$-th root $A\in\mathbb C$, put $r=p/2$ and $\delta=-A^2-A^{-2}$, and use the selected BHMV sixth root $\kappa$ satisfying $\kappa^6=A^{-6-(p+1)p/2}$. Fix a closed oriented marked surface $\Sigma$ with its Lagrangian and weighted anomaly data. Let $I_\Sigma$ be the finite union of its marked ribbon-end bands, with each band labeled by its specified simple object $a_j\in\{0,\ldots,r-2\}$. Fix a marked handlebody $H$ with boundary $\Sigma$ and a complementary marked handlebody $H'$ so that their oriented gluing is the standard $S^3$; the boundary bands on $H$ and $H'$ are identified by the marking, with labels dualized on the oppositely oriented side.

For a banded boundary link $I$ in $\partial H$, let $\mathcal K_A(H;I)$ be the complex specialization of the relative Jones–Kauffman skein module generated by framed banded links in $H$ meeting $\partial H$ transversely in exactly $I$, modulo framed isotopy fixed on $\partial H$ and the following local relations in interior balls. In a chart where the over-strand runs southwest to northeast and the under-strand northwest to southeast, let $L_A$ join the two left endpoints and the two right endpoints, and let $L_B$ join the top endpoints and the bottom endpoints, with blackboard framing. Impose $[L_\times]=A[L_A]+A^{-1}[L_B]$. If $O$ is a zero-framed trivial circle bounding a disk disjoint from the rest of the link, impose $[L\sqcup O]=\delta[L]$. The empty link is allowed.

Let $\widetilde I_\Sigma$ be the boundary link obtained by replacing each band $I_j$ by $a_j$ parallel bands, deleting it when $a_j=0$. On $\mathcal K_A(H;\widetilde I_\Sigma)$, the identity-coefficient Jones–Wenzl projector $p_{a_j}$ acts on the corresponding parallel strands in a boundary collar. Define the colored relative skein module by $\mathcal K_A(H;I_\Sigma,\mathbf a)=P_{\mathbf a}\mathcal K_A(H;\widetilde I_\Sigma)$, where $P_{\mathbf a}$ is the product of these mutually disjoint boundary projectors. Every denominator in these projectors is among $[2],\ldots,[a_j]$, which are nonzero because $a_j\le r-2$. When $I_\Sigma$ is empty, this is the ordinary closed-link module $\mathcal K_A(H)$.

For $x\in\mathcal K_A(H;I_\Sigma,\mathbf a)$ and $y\in\mathcal K_A(H';I_\Sigma,\mathbf a^*)$, glue their matching boundary bands and idempotents. This gives a closed colored ribbon graph $\Gamma$ in the standard $S^3$. Define the handlebody pairing by $B_\Sigma(x,y)=Z_p(S^3,\Gamma,w)$, using the normalized root surgery evaluation and the weight $w$ prescribed by the fixed Lagrangian data. This is the BHMV sesquilinear pairing: it is linear in $x$ and conjugate-linear in $y$ for the coefficient involution $A\mapsto A^{-1}$, $\kappa\mapsto\kappa^{-1}$. Define

$$\operatorname{Rad}(B_\Sigma)=\{x\in\mathcal K_A(H;I_\Sigma,\mathbf a):B_\Sigma(x,y)=0\text{ for every }y\in\mathcal K_A(H';I_\Sigma,\mathbf a^*)\}, \qquad V_p(\Sigma)=\mathcal K_A(H;I_\Sigma,\mathbf a)/\operatorname{Rad}(B_\Sigma).$$

This is the radical-quotient handlebody state-space construction for the specified marking and anomaly data. Independence of the marking, finite generation, nondegeneracy, and the gluing maps are separate mathematical obligations.

**External normalization input.** In this Definition the closed marked evaluation is the BHMV TQFT normalization, not merely the raw signature-normalized closed invariant $\theta_p$ of the Kirby lemma. BHMV §2 pp.897–898 and Gilmer–Masbaum §11 pp.23–25 give
$$Z_p(M,\Gamma,w)=\eta^{b_0(M)+b_1(M)}(\kappa^3)^w\theta_p(M,\Gamma),\qquad \Delta_\pm=\eta^{-1}\kappa^{\pm3}.$$
Let $g_p=\frac12\sum_{m=1}^{2p}(-1)^mA^{m^2}$, and let the bar denote the BHMV involution $A\mapsto A^{-1}$, $\kappa\mapsto\kappa^{-1}$. The normalizing scalar is $\eta=(A\kappa)^3(A^2-A^{-2})p^{-1}\overline{g_p}\in\mathbb C^\times$. Thus $Z_p(S^3,\varnothing,0)=\eta$ and $Z_p(S^2\times S^1,\varnothing,0)=1$. These nonzero normalizations and the registered Wall-sign weight are part of the pairing. BHMV Proposition 1.9, p. 891, applies to a general prescribed banded boundary link $I$: the map from the relative skein module to the universal state space is surjective and its kernel is the left radical of the complementary pairing. For colored boundary bands, BHMV §§4.5–4.8 realize each color by the corresponding parallel cable and Jones–Wenzl projector. BHMV Theorem 4.11, p. 909, states that admissibly colored relative spines whose boundary is the prescribed colored link form an orthogonal basis. The selected $\kappa$ remains a sixth root, with one weight step acting by its cube. The source is used at these located statements and constructions, not as a claimed locally reconstructed universal-construction proof.

**Source hypotheses for this identification.** The normalized BHMV closed invariant is multiplicative and involutive, so BHMV Proposition 1.1 gives its cobordism-generated quantization functor at this fixed root; extend its coefficients to $\mathbb C$ as stated above. BHMV Theorem 1.7 supplies both hypotheses needed for Proposition 1.9: its Kauffman bracket relations let that proposition replace the free relative bordism module by the specialized relative skein module, and its surgery axioms give the surjectivity and radical-kernel description. BHMV's colored expansion in §§4.5–4.8 transports the same construction to the boundary projectors defining $\mathcal K_A(H;I_\Sigma,\mathbf a)$. The corresponding weighted Lagrangian version is the BHMV skein TQFT with integral weights and Lagrangians in place of $p_1$-structures, as described by Gilmer–Masbaum in §11; their one-weight multiplier is matched to the selected BHMV sixth root by $\kappa_{\mathrm{GM}}=\kappa^3$. In that section Lemma 11.1 states the weighted surgery axiom and Remark 11.3 applies it to the handlebody skein presentation; the source refers the Lemma 11.1 proof to the BHMV argument rather than repeating it. These are imported source inputs, not a locally reconstructed proof, and they do not add another quotient or change the state-space interface.
