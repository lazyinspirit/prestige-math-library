## Definition

Assume the Axiom of Choice. For an integral framed link diagram, **Kirby move equivalence** is the equivalence relation generated by the following operations.

1. Apply an orientation-preserving ambient isotopy to the framed link in $S^3$.

2. **Handle slide:** choose distinct components $K_i,K_j$, a parallel $K_j^+$ determined by the framing of $K_j$, and an embedded band whose ends attach to $K_i$ and $K_j^+$ and whose interior misses every link component. Replace $K_i$ by the band sum of $K_i$ with $K_j^+$ and keep the framing obtained by carrying the normal framing across the band. Orient the new component by the band-sum orientation.

3. **Stabilization or destabilization:** add or remove an unknot with framing $+1$ or $-1$ that lies in a $3$-ball disjoint from all the other components.

The integer framing of a component is measured from its preferred longitude as in the integral surgery definition. A planar link projection is not part of the move data; any drawing below is schematic.
