# A balanced sutured one-torus degeneration has zero domain

New operation, 2026-10-20. The argument below is a domain calculation. It is independent of determinant orientation choices and uses no manifold restriction.

Let $\mathcal H=(\Sigma,\alpha,\beta)$ be a balanced sutured diagram. Let $Q$ be the region chain of a disk bubble whose whole boundary lies on $\mathbb T_\alpha$, rather than on alternating alpha and beta tori. Its boundary on the Heegaard surface is a sum of full alpha circles. There is no beta boundary: each beta segment therefore has equal coefficients on its two sides. Consequently the coefficient function of $Q$ is constant on each connected component of $\Sigma\setminus\bigcup\alpha$. Every such component meets $\partial\Sigma$ by balance, and a sutured domain has zero coefficient there. Thus every coefficient of $Q$ vanishes. The identical argument with alpha and beta exchanged handles beta bubbles. Nonnegativity and admissibility are not required for this calculation.

This also describes exactly why a closed-diagram one-torus disk cannot simply be carried into the sutured count: a positive component meeting a cap or deleted boundary region is not a sutured disk domain. The argument must be applied after imposing zero multiplicity at **every** sutured boundary component, not just at one selected closed-diagram basepoint.

On any actual sutured analytic construction for which the area identity for the operator and symplectic background in use gives energy zero to the zero domain, the above calculation excludes nonconstant alpha and beta bubbles. Compatibility with the almost complex structure then makes zero energy equivalent to constancy, since the energy density is nonnegative and strictly positive wherever the derivative is nonzero. Therefore those bubbles contribute no signed boundary strata at all. No comparison of alpha-bubble and beta-bubble signs is necessary in that setting.

The scope of this last implication is deliberate. The domain calculation does not itself construct an area identity for a new symmetric-product background, control sphere classes invisible to the domain map, or prove compactness of sutured moduli spaces. An accepted zero-domain area theorem can be invoked only when its actual target, area-form, divisor and boundary hypotheses hold. It must not be extended from complete closed Heegaard data to arbitrary incomplete sutured systems merely because the same curves can be drawn on a capped surface. Signed strip breaking, triangle/rectangle compatibility, and signed move maps remain separate constructions.
