## Statement

Assume full AC and the actual marked cap, count and compactification interfaces below, including their stated analytic hypotheses. In the chosen signed degree-four quotient completion of the genus-two product, let $m$ be surface insertion and $t$ the relative-disk variable. The actual even algebra satisfies
$$m^4=4m^2+8tm\pmod {t^2}.$$
Its two simple extreme lines have surface eigenvalues
$$\lambda_+(t)=2+t\pmod {t^2},\qquad\lambda_-(t)=-2+t\pmod {t^2}.$$
The actual cap-unit projections onto them have inverse oriented pairings
$$M_+(t)=-32(1+2t)\pmod {t^2},\qquad M_-(t)=32(1-2t)\pmod {t^2}.$$
Thus the first coefficients agree with the proposed exponential extreme factors, by actual marked counts. This theorem asserts neither those exponential formulas to all orders nor a higher-genus recurrence.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The chosen quotient, rational coefficients, and the formal degree-two parameter $t$.

[F1] Actual completed cap classes $e_i(t)$, $0\le i\le3$, form the entire even module over $\mathbb Q[[t]]$. Their geometric marked pairing exists and has invertible constant term. [[lem-genus-two-marked-cap-classes-exhaust-the-even-completion]]

[F2] The ordinary even cap algebra is $\mathbb Q[m]/(m^2(m^2-4))$, with trace $\tau(m^3)=-1/2$, $\tau(m)=\tau(m^2)=\tau(1)=0$. This is the chosen quotient, not the full determinant-one theory. [[lem-genus-two-product-relations-from-ordinary-cap-counts]]

[F3] Actual mixed ruled counts give $D(\Sigma^2B)=D(\Sigma^4B)=0$, and the absolute first-charge marked count gives $D(\Sigma^6B)=-4$. [[lem-genus-two-first-marked-ruled-counts]] [[thm-genus-two-first-charge-marked-pairing-has-absolute-value-minus-four]] Full AC and the latter theorem's explicit ASD analytic input are inherited. [[def-axiom-of-choice]]

## Proof

1.1 **The marked algebra retains an actual unit.** Take the ordinary surface times pair-of-pants cobordism together with the proper marked pair-of-pants $\{q\}\times P$. Its three boundary curves are exactly the incoming and outgoing marked circles. The finite-order marked cutdown and neck-gluing construction defines a product on the completed theory. Gluing an incoming marked cap gives the marked cylinder; the all-marked collar identity is the identity continuation map. Consequently the marked cap is its unit. Moving a closed surface incidence through that collar identifies $e_i(t)$ with $m^i$ acting on the unit. Associativity and the Frobenius pairing identity follow from the two possible stretches of the four-ended marked cobordism. All these statements concern actual finite-order marked moduli before passage to the completion. [F1, given, construct]

2.1 **Only two coefficients can occur at first order.** Since the four actual even classes form the whole module, the fourth power has a unique expansion in $1,m,m^2,m^3$. Its reduction at zero is [F2]. The quotient degree modulo four gives $m$ degree two and $t$ degree two. Hence the most general first-order relation is
$$m^4=4m^2+t(am^3+bm)\pmod {t^2}.$$
Let $\tau_t(c)=\langle c,1\rangle_t$. Marked cap gluing identifies $\tau_t(m^k)$ with $D(\Sigma^k e^{tB})$. This keeps the disk insertion and has not replaced it by an ordinary coefficientwise invariant. [F1, F2, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 **The actual mixed counts fix both coefficients.** Apply the marked trace to step 2.1. Its derivative at zero is $D(\Sigma^4B)-4D(\Sigma^2B)=0$, while its right first-order contribution is $a\tau_0(m^3)+b\tau_0(m)=-a/2$. Thus $a=0$. Multiply the same relation by $m^2$ and trace. The derivative of its left difference is $D(\Sigma^6B)-4D(\Sigma^4B)=-4$. The right side is now $b\tau_0(m^3)=-b/2$. Therefore $b=8$. This coefficient is fixed by the new absolute geometric charge-one count, not by the ordinary matrix or the wall difference alone. [F2, F3, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 **The two simple extreme roots and unit projections lift.** Modulo $t^2$ write $F(x)=x^4-4x^2-8tx$. The simple roots $\epsilon=2,-2$ have derivative $F_0'(\epsilon)=8\epsilon\ne0$. Substitution of $\epsilon+ct$ gives $8\epsilon(c-1)t$, hence $c=1$. Since the actual even algebra is free and generated by its unit, its multiplication matrix is this companion matrix modulo $t^2$. The usual simple-root lifting, obtained by dividing polynomials and solving the invertible derivative equation, gives rank-one idempotent factors reducing to the two actual nonzero ordinary cap projections. This is a finite calculation in a geometrically supplied algebra, not a substitute for constructing that algebra. [F1, F2, step 1.1, step 3.1, algebra]

5.1 **Compute the first inverse-pairing coefficient.** For $\epsilon=2,-2$, the corresponding idempotent modulo $t^2$ is
$$P_\epsilon=\frac{m^3+\epsilon m^2}{8\epsilon}+t\left(-\frac{m^3}{8}-\frac{3m^2}{8\epsilon}+\frac m4\right).$$
Direct multiplication modulo the proved relation checks its idempotence and eigenvalue $\epsilon+t$. The trace derivative vanishes on the basis $1,m,m^2,m^3$: their one-ruling counts have either incompatible dimension or are the flat family with zero ruling class. Thus
$$\tau_t(P_\epsilon)=-\frac1{16\epsilon}+\frac t{16}\pmod {t^2}.$$
The Frobenius pairing on this actual idempotent line is its trace. Inverting these nonzero series gives $M_+=-32(1+2t)$ and $M_-=32(1-2t)$ modulo $t^2$. Distinct idempotent factors pair to zero by the actual pants identity. [F2, F3, step 1.1, step 3.1, step 4.1, algebra]

6.1 **No higher coefficient has been inferred.** The next coefficient of an extreme root involves a second-order coefficient of the marked fourth-power relation. The first-charge calculation and the ordinary basis do not fix all such coefficients. Likewise an inverse pairing with derivative equal to that of $\mp32e^{\pm2t}$ need not equal that exponential as a formal series. Any iteration across dual surfaces for the all-genus witness must therefore retain the uncomputed higher marked operation or supply an additional geometric identity. [step 3.1, step 4.1, step 5.1, algebra]
