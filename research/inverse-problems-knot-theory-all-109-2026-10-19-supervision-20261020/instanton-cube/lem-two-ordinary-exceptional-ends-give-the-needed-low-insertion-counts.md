## Statement

Assume full AC and the actual ordinary count and incidence construction. Blow up two disjoint ordinary interior balls using the same oriented exceptional model. Write their exceptional surfaces as $E_1,E_2$. In the sector with odd determinant on both exceptional pieces, the low geometric insertion pushforwards to the exterior are
$$E_1E_2\longmapsto1,\qquad E_1^3E_2\longmapsto-x,\qquad E_1E_2^3\longmapsto-x,$$
where $x$ is the exterior point insertion. If either exponent is zero, or is two, the corresponding minimal odd-piece pushforward is zero. For an even exceptional determinant, insertions of exceptional degree at most three give zero, and in particular $E_i^2$ gives zero. These statements are finite low-degree blowup operations, not a full blowup series.

Suppose additionally the K3 ASD seed has the actual regular isotropic moduli and universal bundle described below. Let $S=\sigma+2F$, with $\sigma^2=-2$, $F^2=0$, $\sigma F=1$, and form the twice-blown-up pair $(B,\Sigma)$ with $\Sigma=S-E_1-E_2$ and determinant $w=F$. Then its actual low closed quotient evaluations are
$$D_B^{(w,\Sigma)}(\Sigma^2)=2,\qquad D_B^{(w,\Sigma)}(\Sigma^4)=8,$$
and its odd surface evaluations vanish by degree. These give the entries $v_2=2,u_1=2,u_3=8$ needed in the genus-two normalization. They do not, without a proved relative-vector span or product-sector presentation, identify the complete relative invariant or the closed fiber-sum count.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The actual exterior regular moduli and its compact insertion cutdowns, the two disjoint ordinary blowup balls and fixed determinant data. For the K3 application additionally supply the new ordinary K3 ASD realization, not merely its algebraic moduli.

[F1] The ordinary local analytic construction and associative determinant gluing are [[thm-odd-admissible-ordinary-cobordisms-support-insertion-counts]]. Its exclusion of reducible ends is respected: the ordinary reducible three-sphere cut is calculated separately below. Geometric insertions, including their collision dimension bounds, are [[lem-adjoint-incidence-insertions-have-rational-cutdown-counts]].

[F2] The ordinary ASD index and the orientation comparison are [[thm-asd-deformation-complex-index-formula]] and [[lem-kahler-orientation-and-signed-product-quotient-gluing]]. Full AC is inherited through the analytic constructions. [[def-axiom-of-choice]]

[A1] For the K3 application the separately authored ASD seed identifies its determinant-$\sigma+F$ isotropic moduli with K3 itself, orientation-compatibly up to [F2]'s specified determinant sign, with universal K-class
$$[\mathcal U]=[\mathcal O_x(F)\otimes\mathcal O_p(\sigma-F)]+[I_\Delta\otimes\mathcal O_x(\sigma)].$$
This is the actual ASD/universal-incidence interface required from the nonvanishing lane. Its analytic construction is not proved by this item.

## Proof

1.1 **The actual ordinary exceptional solution.** Give $Z=\overline{\mathbb{CP}}^2$ the reversed Fubini--Study metric. Its self-dual Weyl curvature is zero and its scalar curvature is positive. In the determinant class $-e$ with $c_2=0$, $p_1=-1$ and the unbased ASD index is minus one. The round self-dual-form square is $2d_A^+d_A^{+*}=\nabla_A^*\nabla_A+s/3$ for any ASD connection on this metric, since $W^+=F_A^+=0$. Hence its obstruction vanishes. An irreducible solution would have no stabilizer and thus nonnegative index, impossible. Every solution is reducible. In a split rank-two bundle the Chern equations give line classes $ne,(-1-n)e$ and $c_2=n(n+1)$, so $n=0,-1$; these are the same unordered split. Its abelian curvature is the unique harmonic representative of the exceptional line class, so there is exactly one gauge class, the Chern split $\mathcal O\oplus\mathcal O(-e)$. Its harmonic stabilizer is one real axis, and its index and zero obstruction give zero first deformation space. In the even determinant charge-zero sector the energy is zero, so its solution is the unique flat connection, with stabilizer $SU(2)$ and no first or second deformation space. This is an ordinary nonsingular model; no projective-plane orbifold or singular quarter-charge solution is used. [F1, F2, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 **A genuine cylindrical puncture and its normal inverse.** Choose a small ball about an ordinary point. In normal coordinates the metric differs from the Euclidean metric by $O(r^2)$ with first derivatives $O(r)$. Replace it by the Euclidean metric on a smaller ball. The deformation operator changes by a small $H^1\to L^2$ norm; the harmonic abelian representative and its orthogonal complement vary continuously, so the previously computed zero kernel and zero obstruction on the off-axis normal factor persist. Conformally stretch this flat ball to a round cylindrical puncture. The smooth abelian connection has tangential coefficients $O(r^2)$ in radial gauge, and therefore tends exponentially to the flat three-sphere connection. Its nonzero boundary Hessian spectrum has a gap. The limiting three-sphere has $H^1=0$ and three constant gauge modes, all of which are retained. Linear point removal identifies the remaining normal kernel and cokernel with those already computed on the closed model. Thus there is an actual bounded normal inverse after the finite matching modes and the circle stabilizer are separated. Match an irreducible exterior with a framed puncture: its relative framing is $SU(2)$ and the exceptional stabilizer is its axis circle, so the matching fiber is exactly $SU(2)/S^1=\mathbb{CP}^1$. For the even flat model it is $SU(2)/SU(2)$, a point. The normal pregluing inverse is obtained by patching these inverses and the exterior inverse, retaining the flat constant gauges rather than inverting their zero eigenvalue. Cutoff errors tend to zero; contraction gives a solution at every large neck length. Conversely core compactness and the gapped end decomposition put any sequence with this broken limit in that same slice ball; uniqueness proves exhaustive collars. [F1, step 1.1, construct]

3.1 **The universal characteristic calculation on the matching family.** Over the exterior moduli, the odd matching fibers form the projective framing bundle. There need not be a global universal rank-two vector bundle. The vertical tangent of this actual two-sphere bundle has Euler class $2h$, which canonically defines a rational class $h$ restricting to the positive generator of each fiber. On the exceptional surface the universal adjoint bundle splits as a real axis and an oriented two-plane with Chern class $e+2h$, after the corresponding axis choice. Thus $p_1=(e+2h)^2$, and because $eE=-1$ its geometric insertion is $\mu(E)=h$. On a small exterior point the projective bundle identity gives
$$h^2=\frac14p_1(\mathbb E|_{\rm point})=-\mu(x).$$
For example in any local rank-two lift, if $\zeta$ is the dual tautological class and $c_1,c_2$ its classes, $h=\zeta+c_1/2$ and $\zeta^2+c_1\zeta+c_2=0$, which proves the formula; tensoring the lift by a line changes neither side. Integration over a matching fiber therefore gives $\pi_*h=1$, $\pi_*1=\pi_*h^2=0$ and $\pi_*h^3=-\mu(x)$. The even flat matching model is pulled back entirely from the exterior and has no exceptional component of $p_1$, so its exceptional insertion vanishes. These are classes on the ACTUAL glued moduli family of step 2.1, not formal replacements for a gluing theorem. [F1, step 1.1, step 2.1, construct, algebra]

4.1 **Higher charges and every exterior insertion dimension.** Let $q$ be the codimension of all exterior insertions, including insertions on any other exceptional piece not presently being stretched. The minimal odd piece raises the exterior index by two, and another local ordinary charge raises it by eight. With $m$ exceptional insertions, a zero-dimensional total cutdown has exterior dimension after its $q$ constraints equal to $2m-2-8n$, before imposing the exceptional fiber equations, if the local charge is $n$ above minimum. For $m\le3$ and $n\ge1$ this is negative; no regular exterior cutdown exists. For the even piece the corresponding dimension is $2m-8n$, which is negative for $m\le3,n\ge1$. Positive-charge cylinders on the flat three-sphere cut have the same eight-index period and satisfy the same exclusion. Interior ordinary bubbles are excluded by [F1]'s actual cutdown compactness, including when they hit the exceptional insertion surfaces. These arguments apply also to one-parameter stretching relations, on their boundary stratum, with the neck coordinate removed. Consequently only the matching families of step 2.1 contribute to all the displayed low operations. For $m=4$ on an even piece, a possible first positive-charge term has an exterior count of degree $q$; we do not compute its coefficient. In the K3 application below that exterior no-insertion count vanishes by its index parity, so this uncomputed coefficient is unnecessary. [F1, F2, step 1.1, step 2.1, step 3.1, construct, algebra]

5.1 **The orientation unit cancels in the two-piece operation.** Orient each matching two-sphere by the ordered constant-gauge quotient and its chosen complex axis plane. Relative to the fixed Donaldson determinant convention its gluing may have one orientation unit $\varepsilon\in\{\pm1\}$. Step 2.1 and finite determinant excision identify this SAME unit for the two disjoint identically oriented local models: the exterior determinant factor cancels and their ball framings and retained gauge bases are identical. No absolute single-piece sign is assumed. The class calculation in step 3.1 then gives single-piece pushforwards $E\mapsto\varepsilon$, $E^3\mapsto-\varepsilon x$, and zero for exponents zero and two. The two minimal relative indices are even, so exchanging these local factors adds no Koszul sign. Their product unit is $\varepsilon^2=1$. This proves exactly the three double-piece coefficients stated, with no leftover sign or central factor of two. These are all that the application needs. [F1, F2, step 2.1, step 3.1, step 4.1, construct, algebra]

6.1 **The low K3 evaluations from the supplied universal family.** Put $\ell=\sigma-F$, so $\ell^2=-4$. From [A1], $c_1(\mathcal U)=(\sigma+F)_x+\ell_p$ and its degree-four Chern character contains the diagonal term $-[\Delta]$. Taking $4\operatorname{ch}_2-c_1^2$ gives the mixed component of the adjoint Pontryagin class
$$p_1(\operatorname{ad}\mathcal U)_{2,2}=-2\ell_x\ell_p-4[\Delta]_{2,2}.$$
Hence the actual surface insertion on the K3 moduli is $H\mapsto H+(H\ell)\ell/2$, a rational reflection and thus an isometry of intersection forms. The point insertion is $2[\mathrm{pt}]$ from the same universal class. For $S=\sigma+2F$ both its surface square and point evaluation are therefore two. To obtain determinant $F$ as well, use the smooth twist supported in the cotangent disk neighborhood of the actual minus-two sphere $\sigma$. In unit cotangent coordinates its geodesic-flow formula with angle $\rho(r)=\pi-r$ near zero and zero near the boundary extends smoothly at the zero section and acts there by the antipodal map. It is orientation preserving, equals the identity outside, and induces $H\mapsto H+(H\sigma)\sigma$ on homology: it reverses $\sigma$, is supported in its neighborhood, and intersection preservation fixes the coefficient. This sends $\sigma+F$ to $F$ and fixes $S$, since $S\sigma=0$. It fixes an oriented maximal positive subspace orthogonal to $\sigma$, so preserves the homology orientation. Naturality of the actual counts transfers both evaluations to determinant $F$. Finally determinant $F+S=\sigma+3F$ is obtained from $\sigma+F$ by tensoring by the line of class $F$; its adjoint family is unchanged and its Donaldson orientation sign is unchanged by [F2]. These arguments use the actual ASD seed in [A1], not just a cohomological model of a putative moduli. [F1, F2, A1, construct, algebra]

7.1 **Only the needed twice-blown-up closed values.** In the first determinant sector $w=F$ both exceptional pieces are even. For $\Sigma=S-E_1-E_2$, step 4.1 and the flat-piece vanishing give $D_B^w(\Sigma^2)=D_{K3}^{F}(S^2)=2$. Its fourth surface count vanishes: the no-exceptional term has K3 index parity incompatible with degree eight; the second exceptional powers vanish; and a possible fourth exceptional power multiplies the K3 no-insertion count, whose index is also incompatible. In the second determinant sector $w+\Sigma=F+S-E_1-E_2$ both exceptional pieces are the identical odd models. Its second surface count is zero, since the only possible term $2E_1E_2$ would multiply the zero K3 no-insertion count. For its fourth surface count the nonzero terms are exactly
$$12S^2E_1E_2+4E_1^3E_2+4E_1E_2^3.$$
Step 5.1 and step 6.1 give their counts $12\cdot2-4\cdot2-4\cdot2=8$. All other terms have an exceptional exponent zero or two and vanish, or are excluded by the explicitly accounted exterior dimensions in step 4.1. Thus the quotient sum has values two and eight. Its odd surface values vanish by the relative grading modulo four. This proves the claimed finite low-degree evaluations, without importing a full exceptional series or a singular Frobenius factor. [F1, F2, A1, step 3.1, step 4.1, step 5.1, step 6.1, construct, algebra] ∎

## Exact original-facing limitation

The newly authored K3 ASD seed remains a required premise at its actual mathematical extent. These low closed evaluations determine $v_2,u_1,u_3$, but a nondegenerate four-by-four insertion pairing does not prove that the complete relative vectors lie in its span. An actual cap decomposition or genuine relevant product-sector presentation is still required before replacing the full fiber-sum count by that matrix pairing. No full genus-two gluing coefficient or original KHI closure is claimed here, and no helper-only review is requested.
