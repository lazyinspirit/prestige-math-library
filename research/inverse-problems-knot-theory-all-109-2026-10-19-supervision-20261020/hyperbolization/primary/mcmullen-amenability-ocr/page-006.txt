Examples.

1. Solvable groups are amenable, as are subgroups and quotient groups
of amenable groups.

2. The free group on 2 or more generators is nonamenable.

3. Any nonelementary Fuchsian group contains a free group on 2 generators,
and hence is nonamenable.

4. In particular, the fundamental group of hyperbolic Riemann surface of
finite type is nonamenable.

5. Subexponential growth implies amenability, but there are amenable
