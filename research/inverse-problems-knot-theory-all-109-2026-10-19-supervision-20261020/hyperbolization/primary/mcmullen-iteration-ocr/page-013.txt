In this section the spirit of our approach to the skinning map will be conveyed
by a simple test case. We will show that a geometrically finite acylindrical
hyperbolic manifold without cusps can be deformed so its convex hull has totally
geodesic boundary. Equivalently, there is a quasiconformally conjugate Kleinian
group whose domain of discontinuity is a union of circular disks.
