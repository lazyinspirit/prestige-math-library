3. 3-Manifolds

In this section we give a concise description of the skinning map and the gluing
problem for hyperbolic 3-manifolds. A more detailed account can be found in
[Mor], which also treats the Haken decomposition, pared manifolds, Andreev’s
theorem, and the overall logic of the proof of the uniformization theorem.
Thurston’s own papers on the subject [Th1; Th3-Th5], are beginning to appear.
