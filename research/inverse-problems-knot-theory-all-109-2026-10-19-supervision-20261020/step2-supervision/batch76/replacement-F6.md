[F6] **External input — actual relative normal-bundle criterion.** Freedman–Quinn, original §9.3, Theorem 9.3A, printed p.137 (scan PDF143), states: if $N$ is a closed subspace of an ANR homology four-manifold $Y$, $Y\setminus N$ is a manifold, $N\times\mathbb R$ is a manifold, the specified local complement fundamental-group conditions hold (infinite cyclic when $\dim N=2$), and an actual normal vector bundle is given near a closed set $C\subset N$ containing $\partial N$, then there is a normal vector bundle which agrees with that given bundle on a neighborhood of $C$. The relative agreement is part of the existence conclusion, not an inference from uniqueness. In the 2013 reformatted edition this is §9.5, Theorem 9.7, printed pp.144–145/PDF158–159, https://archive.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/4789/2/FreedmanQuinn-TopologyOf4Manifolds-Reformatted2013.pdf. Its p.145 footnote warns that local $\pi_2$ vanishing may also be necessary; step 8.1 satisfies that stronger condition as well. Apply the criterion to the CLOSED doubled sphere in boundaryless $S^4$, with the actual seam-annulus bundle germ constructed there, not directly to $B^4$. This supplies only a normal vector bundle, not a circle-map fiber or a Seifert hypersurface. Full AC is retained.
