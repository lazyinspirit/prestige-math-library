## Statement

For a closed connected oriented rational homology three-sphere and each Spin$^c$ structure, the pointed minus, infinity and plus Heegaard Floer complexes over $\mathbb F_2$ admit an absolute rational grading, independent of the surgery presentation, extending their relative Maslov grading, with $\deg U=-2$ and $\deg[\mathbf x_0,0]=0$ for the standard generator of $S^3$. Heegaard-move isomorphisms preserve this grading and commute with the minus/infinity/plus sequence. A smooth connected Spin$^c$ cobordism between such manifolds induces infinity and plus maps, compatible with that sequence, homogeneous of degree $(c_1(\mathfrak s)^2-2\chi(W)-3\sigma(W))/4$. These maps are obtained from the fixed-Spin$^c$ triangle counts and the one- and three-handle maps, independently of their handle presentation. Here the Chern square uses the rational relative lift of the boundary-torsion Chern class.

## Proof

**Given:** A pointed admissible Heegaard diagram, a rational homology sphere with a Spin$^c$ structure, and a smooth Spin$^c$ cobordism when a map is under consideration. Write $R=\mathbb F_2[U,U^{-1}]$. Choose strict admissible data before counting. In a connected sum of standard $S^1\times S^2$ diagrams, $\theta_+$ and $\theta_-$ denote the top and bottom exterior generators; their degree difference in each torus is one. Their tensor descriptions follow directly by separating the torus factors: the two empty bigons cancel over $\mathbb F_2$ and no mixed disk survives the neck limit.

1.1 **A grading for one actual surgery presentation.** Let an integral framed link with m components present the rational homology sphere Y as surgery on S³. Its trace W has nonsingular integral linking matrix Q. Choose a subordinate pointed Heegaard triple, the canonical-degree input generators x of S³ and Θ of the auxiliary connected sum of S¹×S², and one Whitney triangle ψ from (x,Θ) to y, representing an extension s of the specified boundary Spin-c structure. Define G_(W,s)([y,j]) = 2j − µ(ψ) + 2n_z(ψ) + (kᵀQ⁻¹k − 2m − 3 signature(Q))/4, where k is the vector of evaluations of c₁(s) on the capped handle surfaces. For another output y′ in the same boundary Spin-c structure, concatenate ψ with a disk from y to y′ and use the displayed formula. The disk relative grading interface is an accepted input. Thus the formula actually assigns a rational number to every generator in that Spin-c summand; no assertion about the holomorphic triangle count being nonzero is needed. Here is the precise fixed-presentation well-definedness argument. For fixed corner generators, two triangles defining the same Spin-c extension differ by a sum of self-disks on the three boundary pairs and a sphere class. One can see the kernel statement directly by cutting each triangle at three paths from an interior point to its boundary sides. Matching the induced Spin-c obstruction removes the interior two-cycle; the three remaining boundary cycles are exactly the self-disks. The remaining difference is the sphere obtained by identifying the boundaries of the two cut disks. This is the usual topological triangle-class/Spin-c identification, not a claim that all triangles are holomorphic. Stabilize once if necessary so that the sphere class of the symmetric product has index 2 and basepoint multiplicity 1. For a self-disk on a boundary pair, its index minus twice its basepoint multiplicity is the evaluation of the boundary c₁ on its capped periodic domain. All three boundary c₁ classes here are torsion. Consequently those differences vanish. A sphere changes the index and twice the multiplicity by the same number. Additivity now proves that −µ+2n_z is independent of the chosen triangle representing the fixed extension. Replacing x or Θ by another generator in its canonical relative degree has the same proof, using a connecting disk with µ−2n_z=0. For a disk φ from y to y′, G(y) − G(y′) = µ(φ) − 2n_z(φ). In particular the differential has degree −1 and U has degree −2. The inclusion of the negative subcomplex and the quotient to the plus complex have degree zero. These statements prove an actual rational chain grading for the fixed surgery data, rather than merely assigning a degree to maps. [F1, F2, F3]

1.2 **The rational Chern square in that formula.** The capped handle surfaces are a basis of H₂(W;Q), with intersection matrix Q. Under Poincaré–Lefschetz duality, the map from absolute to relative two-dimensional homology is represented by Q in the corresponding dual bases. Hence the rational inverse image of the cohomology class whose evaluation vector is k is Q⁻¹k. Pairing once more gives c₁(s)² = kᵀQ⁻¹k. This also proves independence of the handle basis: for a basis change P the matrix becomes PᵀQP and the evaluation vector becomes Pᵀk; substituting their inverses gives the same scalar. A handle slide is just such an integral unimodular change. The intersection form is nonsingular because the two boundary components are rational homology spheres: the long exact sequence of the pair makes H₂(W;Q) → H₂(W,∂W;Q) an isomorphism. Torsion components of c₁ vanish after tensoring with Q. Finally W is obtained from S³×I by m two-handles, so χ(W)=m. This establishes every topological term used above. [step 1.1, F1, F2, F3]

1.3 **Actual negative blow-up normalization.** Add a disjoint −1-framed unknot. Its characteristic evaluation is any odd integer a=±(2r+1), r≥0. The linking matrix gains a summand (−1), so its contribution to the topological degree term is (−a²−2+3)/4 = −r(r+1). The standard torus triple for this handle has straight attaching lines of slopes 0, infinity, and −1, each pair intersecting once on the torus. Lift to the plane. Triangles in the two families with characteristic evaluation ±(2r+1) consist of the smallest triangular region together with r further successive layers. The added layers contain respectively 1,2,…,r lifts of the basepoint. Thus n_z=r(r+1)/2. Each triangle lifts to a Euclidean triangle with its three ordered vertices fixed. Its conformal parametrization is unique modulo the automorphisms of the disk fixing these three boundary points; that automorphism group is trivial. Schwarz reflection identifies the kernel of its linearized problem with holomorphic vector fields on the doubled sphere vanishing at three points, hence with zero. The same doubling shows cokernel zero. Its Maslov index is therefore zero. Reflection gives the other family, with the same multiplicity and index. Consequently the added triangle contributes −µ+2n_z=r(r+1), which exactly cancels the topological term. The fixed-presentation rational grading is unchanged by a disjoint negative blow-up, for every Spin-c extension on the exceptional summand. This is an actual index/basepoint cancellation, not an appeal to the absolute-grading theorem. The triangle gluing interface makes the same calculation apply when it is connected-summed with the original triple. It also fixes the normalization for the empty S³ presentation at zero. [step 1.2, F1, F2, F3]

1.4 **Composition over a rational homology sphere.** For two actual traces glued along a rational homology sphere, rational Mayer–Vietoris identifies their H₂ groups as an orthogonal direct sum: the outer capped surfaces can be chosen on their own sides and have zero mutual intersection. Thus c₁² and the signature add. Euler characteristic adds because the gluing three-manifold has Euler characteristic zero. Therefore the topological degree expression Δ=(c₁²−2χ−3σ)/4 adds exactly. Gluing two triangle classes into a quadrilateral adds their indices and basepoint multiplicities. The other splitting of that quadrilateral changes them by the index and multiplicity of the auxiliary canonical triangle. The canonical-degree choice makes that triangle's index minus twice its basepoint multiplicity zero. Therefore the two expressions −µ+2n_z agree under this change of splitting. Combined with the preceding additive Δ calculation, this proves the composition identity for the candidate grading. It is the finite index operation behind the two-handle degree-shift formula. Analytic associativity remains supplied by the accepted polygon interface. For a chosen two-handle map, a counted triangle has index zero. Its output integer is j−n_z. Substituting in the definition gives, term by term, G(output) − G(input) = Δ(W,s). This is the promised degree calculation at the chain level for the chosen trace. Choice independence of the trace and the naturality of the full map are not inferred from this term-by-term calculation. [step 1.3, F1, F2, F3]

1.5 **A zero-degree calculation requiring no Floer-map assumption.** If W is a rational homology cobordism between rational homology spheres, then H₂(W;Q)=0, σ(W)=0, and χ(W)=0. The rational square of every extending c₁ is zero. Hence its required degree shift is zero in both orientations. This calculation does not prove that the infinity map is nonzero or an isomorphism. A zero map is homogeneous of every degree; replacing that missing nonvanishing statement by a degree assignment would be invalid. [step 1.4, F1, F2, F3]

2.1 **The missing Hopf index calculation.** Work in a small annular part of a complex torus with coordinates (x,y), x modulo one. Put α={y=0}, β={y=ε sin(2πx)}, and put the transverse curve γ={x=1/4}; extend the curves by their usual torus identifications. Place the basepoint outside the two small triangular regions used below. The αβ pair is the standard diagram of S¹×S². Its two intersections are θ₊ at x=0 and θ₋ at x=1/2, with relative grading gr(θ₊)−gr(θ₋)=1. Indeed, the upper small bigon has oriented boundary going from x=0 to x=1/2 on α and back on β; it is a Maslov-one disk from the former point to the latter, with n_z=0. This determines the relative grading without a Morse-sign convention. Both other pairs give S³. Use the triangle convention whose oriented boundary runs from the first input to the output on the first attaching curve. For the ordered triple (α,γ,β), the small triangle at θ₋ is positively oriented: its boundary runs from (1/4,0) right on α to (1/2,0), left along β to (1/4,ε), then down γ. It is the rigid triangle from the S³ generators to θ₋. For (α,β,γ), the small triangle at θ₊ is positively oriented: its boundary runs from (0,0) right on α to (1/4,0), up γ, and left along β back to (0,0). It is the rigid triangle from θ₊ to the S³ generator. The opposite choices of θ have the wrong orientation for those small regions. These small embedded triangles have n_z=0 and µ=0. Indeed a conformal parametrization of each curvilinear triangular disk is unique with the three corners fixed; doubling the linearized boundary problem gives a vector field on the sphere vanishing at its three corners, so there is neither kernel nor cokernel. Their fixed-vertex counts over F₂ are one. This uses only the one-dimensional local triangle model; stabilization and gluing with the old diagram use the already accepted analytic interfaces. To concatenate the two triangle classes, use θ₋ at the intermediate αβ boundary. Replace θ₊ in the second class by θ₋ using a connecting disk class. Since gr(θ₋)−gr(θ₊)=−1, this disk has µ−2n_z=−1. Consequently the two composed topological triangle classes have total µ−2n_z=−1, or raw grading contribution −µ+2n_z=+1. No holomorphic representative is claimed for the index-negative connecting class, and no nonzero composite Floer map is inferred. This is an index comparison, which is exactly what is needed for grading normalization. The associated two-handle picture is the zero-framed unknot followed by its meridian, with the meridian framing −1 chosen in the Hopf gluing. Its intersection form in capped-handle coordinates is Q_H = [[0,1],[1,−1]]. The first sphere is the zero-surgery S². Extensions torsion on the intermediate S¹×S² have evaluation zero on it. Thus the characteristic vector has form (0,b), with b odd; Q_H⁻¹=[[1,1],[1,0]], so its rational Chern square is zero for every b. The signature is zero and χ=2. Its topological term is therefore −1, exactly cancelling the raw index +1. The integer ambiguity in gluing the two Spin-c structures is measured by the periodic sphere at the intermediate boundary. Its c₁ evaluation is zero, so adding this gluing parameter changes µ−2n_z by zero. The index calculation consequently applies to every odd b, not just one extension. [step 1.5, F1, F2, F3]

2.2 **Positive blow-up normalization.** Start with a +1-framed unknot and any odd characteristic value a. Add a disjoint −1-framed unknot with characteristic value −a. In the diagonal basis (H,E) its form is diag(1,−1), with characteristic evaluations (a,−a). Slide the first component over the second. The basis (F=H+E,E), after reversing one orientation if necessary, gives precisely Q_H above, with evaluation zero on F. The Hopf calculation therefore proves the total grading contribution is zero. The disjoint negative-blow-up contribution is already zero by the preceding working proof. Subtracting it proves that a disjoint +1-framed unknot also changes the grading by zero, for every odd a. Here c₁ need not be the zero class. Only its square vanishes. This avoids the misleading sentence `c₁(s′)=0` in the old source's positive-blow-up proof: in the diagonal coordinates (a,−a) the class is usually nonzero. [step 2.1, F1, F2, F3]

2.3 **Transport under diagram and Kirby moves.** Here is the new index transport. An attaching-family isotopy supplies moving-boundary strips at the canonical inputs. Their relative-degree condition is µ−2n_z=0. Splicing them to a triangle changes neither −µ+2n_z nor its Spin-c extension. For an attaching-circle slide, glue its canonical equivalence triangle to the old surgery triangle. Split the same quadrilateral along the other diagonal into the new surgery triangle and the auxiliary canonical triangle. Additivity identifies the two index expressions, because the equivalence triangles have µ−2n_z=0. A periodic ambiguity on an auxiliary boundary has torsion c₁ and contributes zero. This covers slides among all three attaching families. Stabilization adds the small triangle with µ=n_z=0 and leaves the topology term unchanged. Move the joining paths of a bouquet past its attaching disks one at a time. Cutting the complement along these disks expresses each crossing as a slide of a surgery curve over an auxiliary curve; between crossings the change is an isotopy. Hence bouquet changes are covered too. A four-dimensional slide is represented by the surgery-curve slide and its dual-meridian slide in the subordinate triple. The preceding transport handles its index part; the unimodular calculation in the preceding phase handles c₁² and σ. The two signed blow-up calculations handle the remaining Kirby generators. [step 2.2, F1, F2, F3]

2.4 **Independence of surgery and extending Spin-c structure.** Applying this transport and the signed normalization calculations through the accepted Kirby classification shows that every surgery presentation of S³, with every extending Spin-c structure, induces degree zero there. For a fixed boundary structure, compare two extensions over surgery traces by attaching the same dual-surgery trace back to S³. At a rational homology sphere the gluing structure is unique once its restrictions are fixed, since H¹(Y;Z)=0. The index-composition identity cancels the common outgoing trace. Both composite surgery presentations of S³ have degree zero, so the original two gradings agree. This proves both extension independence and surgery-presentation independence. Extension over the traces is described by characteristic covectors modulo twice the linking lattice, which is exactly the boundary Spin-c quotient; thus every boundary structure used here has such extensions. For intermediate torsion Spin-c manifolds with positive first Betti number, use the nonsingular quotient of the linking form: k annihilates ker Q exactly when its boundary Chern class is torsion. Solve Qv=k over Q and put c₁²=kᵀv. Any other solution differs by ker Q and has the same pairing. The fixed-triangle and index-transport arguments remain valid. This is the grading needed on the connected sums with S¹×S² between handle stages. [step 2.3, F1, F2, F3]

2.5 **Chosen-handle maps and their degree.** The full map construction starts with explicit chain maps: F₂,s[x,j] = Σ_(y,ψ: µψ=0,s_zψ=s) #₂M(ψ) [y,j−n_zψ], F₁[x,j]   = [x⊗θ₊,j], F₃[x⊗θ₋,j] = [x,j],    F₃[x⊗θ₊,j]=0. Strong admissibility and the accepted compactness/gluing interfaces make the first sum finite on each generator and give its chain-map equation by the ends of one-dimensional triangle moduli. The tensor interface gives the chain equations for F₁,F₃. All n_z of counted holomorphic curves are nonnegative, so each map preserves CF⁻ and descends to CF⁺. The infinity/plus square therefore commutes already on chains. The Hopf model also fixes the S¹×S² absolute degrees once full surgery independence is established: zero surgery has topological degree −1/2 and its rigid small triangle takes the degree-zero S³ generator to θ₋. Thus gr(θ₋)=−1/2 and gr(θ₊)=+1/2. F₁ and F₃ then have degree +1/2, agreeing with χ=−1, σ=0, c₁²=0 for one- and three-handle additions. Together with the two-handle calculation this gives the desired Δ formula for a chosen whole handle presentation. [step 2.4, F1, F2, F3]

2.6 **The handle identities for the constructed maps.** Use the accepted relative handle-move completeness as a topological input, and keep the fixed global Spin-c structure attached to each moduli problem. For a two-handle slide use a subordinate quadruple with the old and new surgery curves and their dual-meridian slide. The boundary strata of its one-dimensional rectangle moduli give the homotopy between the two ways of composing the canonical equivalence triangles with F₂. The auxiliary product of the canonical top generators is the canonical top generator: in each changed torus there is exactly the small nonnegative triangle with those corners and n_z=0, counted once; all other local domains have a negative region. Gluing these local factors with the unaltered diagram is the accepted analytic operation. Thus F₂ is invariant under the link slide, and the same argument treats a bouquet or subordinate-diagram change. Retaining the actual global Spin-c label proves the equality for that structure; no equality of separately chosen summands across an intermediate positive-b₁ boundary is assumed. One-handles append the tensor θ₊ on each new torus. Reordering factors therefore commutes. A slide changes the generators of the new H¹ by a unimodular basis change; the appended top exterior generator changes by its determinant, which is 1 over F₂. This is also seen by the small canonical-slide triangles in the two involved tori. Three-handles extract the corresponding bottom factor and kill the top factor; reversing this same local diagram proves their ordering and slide identities. A change which moves a two-handle past a one-handle merely changes the bouquet and the meridian basis at that one-handle; the already described simultaneous curve-slide identity identifies its map. The reversed statement treats a two-/three-handle interchange. Disjoint critical events commute by choosing the separated local diagrams and applying the tensor/gluing interface. For a cancelling one-/two-handle pair, take the αβ pair in the explicit torus model at the beginning of this phase and its transverse γ. F₁ appends θ₊. The second of the two displayed small triangles takes θ₊ to the unique αγ intersection with coefficient one and n_z=0. The composite is therefore the ordinary stabilization equivalence on the old diagram, with no U power. A different attaching meridian which cancels the same one-handle is obtained after compressing the old meridian disks by a meridian-preserving change of basis of this punctured torus. Perform that basis change before the local calculation; the associated strong equivalence is the canonical diagram equivalence, so the composite is again the identity on the original Floer group. Turning this diagram around gives the two-/three-handle cancellation: its small triangle lands in θ₋ and F₃ extracts it, again with coefficient one and n_z=0. These calculations are the cancellation identities, not assertions based only on the existence of a topological cancelling pair. Every generating handle change has now been covered by a canonical equivalence, associativity homotopy, tensor identity, or one of the two explicit cancelling-pair counts. The accepted relative move completeness therefore makes F₃F₂F₁ independent of the chosen handle presentation. All these homotopies use nonnegative basepoint multiplicities, so they preserve the negative subcomplex as well and descend to the plus theory. It follows that the infinity/plus square, already commuting on chosen chains, is independent of all construction choices. Index transport and the preceding normalization prove its homogeneous degree is (c₁²−2χ−3σ)/4 in each flavor. [step 2.5, F1, F2, F3] ∎
