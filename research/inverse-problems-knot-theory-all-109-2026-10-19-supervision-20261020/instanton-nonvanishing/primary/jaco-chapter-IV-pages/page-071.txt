Clearly, whenever M is a Haken-manifold and F is an in-
compressible and Q-incompressible surface in M, which is not a disk
and is not parallel into OM, then the manifold, M', obtained by
splitting M open at F, is a Haken-manifold and V(M') < V(M). This

allows us to use V(M) for an inductive method of proof. (Notice that

R(M) does not have this same property.)
