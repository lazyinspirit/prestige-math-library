# Actual black-guide erasure for an unchanged parent

New author-stage lemma; no acceptance or original closure claimed. The scope of the geometric lemma below is deliberately explicit. The application still must prove that the chosen normalization has its stated tube-tail and nonincidence properties.

Let C be the actual OLD upper circle system in 9-good position. Let g1 be one unchanged geometric circle of a later upper trace system. Suppose an attempted later type7/8/9 guide a on g1 has its core in one actual open black checkerboard disk B0, has zero, one, or two tube tails, and those tails are disjoint from the WHOLE OLD F and have exactly the old legitimate-guide projected nonincidence data. Suppose all intersections of the core with old C are transverse, finite, and in the interiors of old proper black common-face arcs. The guide interior is disjoint from the unchanged g1.

Choose such a core, keeping its endpoint/tail germs fixed, with the minimum possible number of intersections with the finite proper old black-arc system. Such a minimum exists in the nonnegative integers; do not assume a minimizing metric geodesic. The minimizing core can be embedded: a generic path in the black disk can have any self-intersections removed by deleting the intervening loops, followed by a small perturbation in the open black disk; this creates no old trace intersections. The tail germs and old nonincidence data remain fixed.

The old upper circles have a sphere dual TREE. Because a misses old g1 except at its endpoints, its two endpoint germs are on the same side of g1. Therefore the word of other old-circle crossings read along a is a closed walk in that tree. If it is nonempty, there is an immediate edge backtrack: a shortest nonempty closed subwalk in a tree has length two. Accordingly some subarc b of the core has its two endpoints on the SAME OLD circle h and its interior disjoint from the WHOLE OLD trace system.

If these endpoints are on two distinct actual black traces of h, b itself is an old legitimate type7 guide. Its core is in B0 and meets no peripheral tube or book, its endpoint intervals are distinct, and the companion is the proper arc joining them in the actual upper disk F_h. Thus the endpoint nonincidence condition is exactly the distinct-trace condition, and no tube projection condition is needed. Old 9-good position forbids it.

If the endpoints are on the SAME proper black trace t, let D be the disk bounded by the actual backtrack subarc b and the intervening subarc of t in B0. The boundary b is free of the WHOLE OLD trace system and distinct old traces miss t. Thus no other old proper trace crosses this disk boundary. None lies wholly inside, because each is proper in B0 with endpoints on its boundary. A type7 guide endpoint on an old g1 trace therefore cannot lie inside D either; type8/9 core endpoints lie on the boundary of B0, outside D. There are no guide endpoints inside D.

Other portions of the minimizing guide a may still enter D, but they can enter and leave only across the t segment: they cannot cross the embedded backtrack b, and have no endpoints in D. A finite innermost such return, or b itself if there is none, gives a bigon with interior free of a and t. Its interior is also free of every other old trace by the preceding paragraph. Push across this bigon and then into a thin outside collar, fixing the guide endpoints and tails. It removes two old-t intersections and creates no other old-trace intersections. This contradicts minimality. This argument uses b, not a false assumption that the WHOLE guide a misses other old traces.

Both cases are impossible, so the minimum core misses the WHOLE OLD circle system. With its original tube tails and projected nonincidence data it is now an old legitimate type7/8/9 guide on g1. This contradicts old 9-good position. Hence no later guide satisfying these exact hypotheses exists.
