The 2-sphere X0 ∪Z0 bounds a ball Y ⊂S3 \ \νL whose interior is disjoint from
F. The minimality of |Z ∩F| implies that X0 ∩(S+ ∪S−) ̸= ∅; also, X0 must
be disjoint from all crossing bands and must contain any saddle disks that it
intersects. Therefore, it is possible to push F near X0 through Y past Z0, thus
decreasing |F \ (S+ ∪S−)|, while ensuring that the resulting positioning satisﬁes
the ﬁrst four conditions. This contradicts the minimality of |F \ (S+ ∪S−)|.
