# Actual old selected star disk

NEW elementary topology, not an assertion that a prism component Fi or the source's entire shadow U has already been constructed.

Let X be the actual old upper F disk bounded by innermost gamma. Let D_1,...,D_s be the actual transverse saddle disks incident to gamma. Use accepted 4.1(A): at each selected crossing gamma meets the upper crossing boundary in precisely its selected arc rho_i. Thus D_i intersects X precisely in the ONE complete boundary interval rho_i. Distinct selected D_i lie in distinct crossing balls and are disjoint; the rho_i have disjoint interiors and are separated by nonempty common-face intervals on gamma.

Then K=X union D_1 union ... union D_s is a disk, with its actual inherited boundary and corners. Indeed attach the D_i one at a time to X. A disk glued to a disk along one complete boundary interval is a disk. At each stage the next rho_i remains a boundary interval, because no other selected disk meets it. This proves the claim by finite induction. An intrinsic small regular neighborhood of K in the surface F, with the existing F-boundary portions retained, is again a disk after rounding corners. It contains the WHOLE selected saddle disks, rather than merely a tiny collar of gamma.

This proves a concrete old disk support without assuming any Fi properness. It does NOT make its boundary equal the fixed outer prism seams; it does NOT prove a new cap disk. Extending/trimming its boundary to the required prism wall inventory still requires the actual controlled cuts. Extra upper curtains and repeatedly encountered outer intervals cannot be added without checking their actual attachment intervals.
