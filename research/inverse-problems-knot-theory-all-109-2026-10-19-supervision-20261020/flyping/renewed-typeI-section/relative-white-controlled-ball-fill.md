# Relative white-controlled filling in an actual cut ball

NEW elementary geometric operation for the Type-I body assembly. This does not assert that the Type-I boundary cycle or its cut ball has been identified.

Let B be a specific smooth/PL 3-ball and P a properly embedded boundary-parallel white disk in B, with its actual product collar. Assume cutting B along P gives the two specified 3-balls B_minus,B_plus (this is input, not inferred from a whole white-face name). Let C be an actual embedded boundary circle on ∂B, transverse to ∂P at exactly two specified points a,b. Its two complementary arcs C_minus,C_plus lie on the respective cut spheres. Give a particular embedded arc lambda in P from a to b, together with its normal ribbon germ, and suppose no protected seam lies on that ribbon's interior. The normal germs along C must enter the designated ball and agree at its two joins with the chosen ribbon.

Then there is a properly embedded disk D in B with actual boundary C, actual white section D intersect P=lambda, and the prescribed sufficiently thin boundary/ribbon germs. The construction is relative to all protected seams and boundary faces.

Proof. On each actual cut sphere, C_sign union lambda_sign is a simple circle: C_sign lies on the original boundary face and lambda_sign in the new P face; they meet only at a,b. Choose one of its two sphere disk sides. Push its interior a small positive distance into B_sign in a boundary collar, with push function zero precisely on its boundary. This gives an embedded disk missing ALL boundary faces except its stated circle. Along C_sign and lambda_sign, replace the small graph germ by the prescribed inward germ; in a normal half-disk, both are arcs entering the same half-plane, so a small boundary-relative isotopy does this, interpolated away from a smaller collar. The two fillings are in different cut balls and have no intersection after regluing except their identical lambda boundary. Glue them there and round in the prescribed product ribbon. Two disks glued along one complete boundary interval form a disk. Its interior meets P only on lambda, because each interior filling was disjoint from its P boundary face. No additional white section is introduced. Protected boundary faces were never entered.

For Type I, lambda is the proved exterior white-core pendant. This operation becomes an actual local body proof ONLY AFTER the coupled O/U physical boundary cycle C and the actual cut-ball/P incidences are supplied. It cannot replace those incidences or make an essential peripheral meridian bound on the torus.
