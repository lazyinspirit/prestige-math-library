NEW status during first review; frozen proof is unchanged.

The central allocation contract glues PROVIDED local disks Vi with one free inner interval and specified physical retained occurrences. It does not assert that an arbitrary compatible collar strip extends to a Vi. Accordingly Step3 of the frozen root-side white-section proof has one overbroad input sentence. The page separation, full-fibre corridor, and primitive corner-order arguments do not use that sentence; the full local disk-body conclusion must wait for its replacement.

The actual Type-I channels, communicated by the original owner from the primary geometry, are:

- INNER wall: underpass U and ordinary right edge channel E1;
- OUTER wall: O_left, U, O_right, E1;
- O_left and O_right are two ends of ONE overpass channel (the front arch). The central straight channel is UNDERPASS U, with E continuing inward toward Cs.

These are not the TypeIII wall words. The proof's intrinsic U/O marking follows E and does not identify the front arch with U.

A concrete construction to complete is the following, with its unproved incidences still explicit. Start from the ACTUAL selected shadow disk Fi, not an abstract disk parametrization. Its actual boundary automatically lists the side-wall, retained outer, and tube occurrences. If its U-channel boundary interval mu is identified, the one-longitude-per-meridian property permits a root-angular rectangle on that T-channel from mu to the new underpass arc; its interior contains no other F boundary. A root-side collar of Fi is a ball; attaching a thin collar of such a rectangle along ONE boundary strip only gives a ball again. Replacing mu by the other three rectangle sides gives a simple cycle, provided the wall end connectors are the actual ones and avoid every prescribed retained occurrence. Sphere-window filling then proves disk topology, rather than central allocation being invoked as a filling theorem.

What remains to bind in this proposed route:

The accepted S-to-W+ lower-return contract is a candidate: its initial lower return at the selected saddle is actual U tube boundary, and its one-black-core continuation should stay within the selected prism before any dual white cut.

4. Relative to the pendant, choose the inward disk filling so that no additional white-core sections are created. This follows from an actual cut by the relevant W disk if that cut-ball incidence is established; it is not supplied by disk topology alone.

Until these are supplied, the complete local body is not claimed constructed. No accepted TypeI body contract has been assumed. Original partial notes, frozen first submission, and this correction are all preserved.
