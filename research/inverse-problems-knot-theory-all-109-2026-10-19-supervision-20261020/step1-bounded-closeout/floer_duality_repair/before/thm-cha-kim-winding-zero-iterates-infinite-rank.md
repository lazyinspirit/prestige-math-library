## Statement

Assume full AC. Let P⊂S¹×D² be a winding-zero pattern. Assume the axis has nonzero classical Blanchfield self-pairing in the Alexander module of P(U), with the convention that the axis defines the class paired with itself. For either the locally flat topological concordance group C_top or the smooth concordance group C_sm, let Pⁿ be the n-fold composition of the induced satellite set map, with P⁰ the identity. For every n≥0, the quotient ⟨Pⁿ(C)⟩/⟨Pⁿ⁺¹(C)⟩ has infinite rank, where ⟨S⟩ denotes the subgroup generated by S and rank means dim_Q(A⊗_Z Q) for an abelian group A. For n≥1, Pⁿ is not a group homomorphism under the same hypotheses. No pairwise-coprime Alexander-polynomial assumption is part of these Theorems A and B.

## Proof

**Given:** The complete rich Statement, with full AC as above.

1.1 In the smooth category [F1] identifies the stipulated nonzero classical axis self-value with exactly the integral surgery self-value used in [F4]. The winding and orientation conventions are the same, and AC verifies its actual geometric and analytic hypotheses. Apply [F4] to obtain infinite rational rank of $\langle P^n(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sm}})\rangle/\langle P^{n+1}(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sm}})\rangle$ for every $n\ge0$, including its separate finite-cyclic proof at $n=0$. Its finite families are chosen for each desired rank bound, so no assertion of a single explicit infinite independent sequence is needed. This establishes the entire smooth rank branch of the original Statement. [A1, F1, F2, F3, F4, given, algebra]

2.1 For $n\ge1$, [F4] also gives a knot $J$ for which $P^n(J)\#P^n(J)\#(-P^n(J\#J))$ is not smoothly slice. A homomorphism on smooth concordance would make this class zero, contradicting the actual witness. Thus the entire smooth nonhomomorphism branch follows as well. Neither [F4]'s rank proof nor this witness uses a pairwise-coprime Alexander-polynomial condition, so the original A/B scope is retained. [F3, F4, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 For the locally flat TOP category [F5] proves the algebraic coefficient survival and actual zero defect of any supplied initial lifted-half-pair manifold. Those are genuine TOP statements, not smooth replacements. However they do not identify that TOP defect with the smooth invariant of the same boundary, nor construct the locally flat normal-tube/concordance foundations listed in [L1]. A coefficient-preserving actual TOP five-bordism would preserve signatures by the first theorem in [L1], but its existence has not been proved locally. Therefore steps1.1–2.1 complete the original smooth conclusions while the literal original TOP conclusions remain explicitly unproved here; no whole-Statement completion is asserted. [F5, L1, step 1.1, step 2.1]
