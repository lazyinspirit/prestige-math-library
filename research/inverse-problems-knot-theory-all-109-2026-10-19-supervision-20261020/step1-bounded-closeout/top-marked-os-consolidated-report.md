## Actual bounded mathematical repairs

**Unlink topology.** The canonical unlink proof now constructs the
double cover using two trivial k-string tangles. The covers are
genus-(k-1) handlebodies, with matching meridian disks; doubling gives
the stated connected sum. This finite geometric part is proved for
every k>=1, including k=1.

**All reduced saddle algebra.** The canonical unlink and first-
differential proofs now state and derive all four tables. Besides
ordinary multiplication/comultiplication, marked merge is the
quotient killing the other circle's X, and marked split creates X
on the new unmarked circle. The exterior forms are respectively
quotient identifying generators, wedge with x_d+x_e after a lift,
quotient by the disappearing generator, and wedge with the new
generator. These are actual finite algebra proofs, not claimed
evaluations of Floer triangles.

**Common vertex bases and grading.** The first-differential proof
requires a single vertex identification for all incident edges.
Independent edgewise basis choices do not suffice. Conditional on
the actual surgery-to-mirror smoothing correspondence, it spells out
i=|I|-n_+(D), reduced j=sum(unmarked label degrees)+|I|+n_-(D)-2n_+(D),
and checks every marked/unmarked table raises i by1 and preserves j.
The spectral filtration p is distinguished by i=n_-(D)-p. The local
slope/smoothing comparison is still held, not assumed from the word
“convention.”

**Square-zero substitution.** The general-Y surgery proof now gives
the actual finite extra hypothesis: every top-input sum M_AB of
polygon operations along the immediate monotone paths must vanish.
Expanding the polygon boundary identity gives D^2 as insertions of
those M_AB. This proves the finite algebra implication and explains
why analytic identities alone do not evaluate M_AB.

**Cone and multi-coordinate coherence.** Once counts and top inputs
exist, n=1 is literally the cone matrix and n=0 the original complex.
Identification with the third filling still needs an actual cone
comparison. For many components those comparisons must have the
higher components compatible with all remaining coordinates; mere
vertexwise quasi-isomorphisms are insufficient. The canonical proof
now gives the finite filtered-cone implication conditional on such
coherent maps. General Y, arbitrary framed links, the stated
0/1/infinity slopes and F2 are retained.
