# Actual one-period annular strips, with arbitrary retained winding

This is new geometric admission work. No completed carrier or supplier is rechecked here. Finite panel support and finite orbit of marked tracks are different statements. The latter is unnecessary for the following actual local construction.

## The supplied geometric input

Let A be an ACTUAL neatly embedded injected PL annulus in the actual finite PL manifold Q, with its two rims in two specified disjoint injected boundary-face components F0,F1. The face labels remain distinct even when their groups have equal images. A finite product neighborhood is an embedded

    N = S1_s times I_t times I_n,

with t the long direction between its two actual horizontal annular ports; n is its normal band. The end ports lie in the specified physical faces. All other contact with the physical boundary is excluded by the neat product collar. This neighborhood is supplied by the actual annulus, not by an abstract mapping cylinder.

Give S1 circumference one. In the cyclic lift of this actual neighborhood the coordinates are R_s times I_t times I_n. If the original root word is h=c^e, with c the geometrically primitive annulus core, h acts as s -> s+e. KEEP e. Neither the source h nor its subgroup is replaced by c or by a power h^N. For |e|>1 the quotient by h is a finite cover of the embedded carrier N, not an embedded annulus with simple boundary representing h in Q. Indeed a simple essential circle in an annulus represents a primitive generator, so an embedded source boundary representing c^e would be impossible for |e|>1. The permitted alternative is the embedded carrier N together with the actual degree-e source map into it.

## An explicit finite shear

Suppose finitely many marked spanning tracks have been admitted into N, with their actual endpoints and their integer cyclic winding retained. After assigning separated normal slots n_i and the actual port coordinates, denote their lifted displacement by delta_i. Thus delta_i includes the endpoint-coordinate displacement AND the integer winding k_i. These are actual numbers attached to the original tracks, not chosen zero values.

Triangulate the (t,n) square with every horizontal slot n=n_i as an edge chain. At its bottom vertices prescribe u(0,n_i)=0, and at its top vertices prescribe u(1,n_i)=delta_i. Triangulate the intervening rectangles and interpolate affinely. This gives a finite PL function with

    u(t,n_i) = t delta_i.

Define on the ACTUAL product neighborhood

    Phi(s,t,n) = (s+u(t,n) mod 1, t, n).

This is a homeomorphism with inverse (s,t,n) -> (s-u(t,n),t,n). After subdividing at the finitely many integer-coordinate crossings it is jointly PL. Its lift commutes EXACTLY with s -> s+e. It therefore realizes an actual h-equivariant one-period strip, retaining the original degree e, however large the k_i are. No track-orbit finiteness is asserted. The long tracks at distinct n_i are disjoint because their normal slots and long coordinate are unchanged. When the original endpoints agree in circle coordinate and delta_i=k_i is integral, their actual endpoints are pointwise fixed. Unmarked port points can move, but each entire colored annular port is its original set. If all delta_i are the same integer, both full ports are pointwise fixed.

The often convenient continuous formula u(t,n)=t a(n) would be bilinear when a varies with n. It is NOT the claimed jointly PL construction. The vertex interpolation just given supplies that construction directly.

An actual path already in N with those endpoints and displacement is homotopic relative endpoints to the displayed sheared track: lift both to the contractible product R times I times I and contract their comparison loop there. Their integer displacements agree, so the homotopy projects with the same endpoints and same based word. This applies to the original marked path, not to an unrelated path selected from its endpoints. Finite collections of these homotopies need not be disjoint. Every actual source relation can retain the homotopy as part of its finite marked source complex.

If several tracks share a physical endpoint, they cannot all be distinct fibers of one I-bundle with that same endpoint. Keep the common endpoint as the basepoint, use finite disjoint normal-slot endpoints nearby, and retain the actual short port access arcs to the common endpoint. The based word includes these access arcs. Alternatively keep the paths as marked paths sharing an endpoint and do not call them distinct fibers. Neither alternative discards the fixed-end winding or changes the original h relation.

This realizes arbitrary annular Dehn-twist winding as actual shear in a finite embedded product. A Dehn-twist example is admitted, not excluded. Closing the two long ports into a mapping torus is a DIFFERENT geometric operation: it is allowed only if their actual embedded return identification in Q is supplied. A formal monodromy map does not by itself embed such a quotient in Q.

## A rigorous obstruction to the fixed-first-rim shortcut, and its actual carrier alternative

Take Q=S1_h times Sigma, where Sigma is a parametrized pair of pants. Its three boundary tori are injected. Choose actual points x0,x1 on two different boundary circles and a simple joining arc rho0. The annulus A0=S1 times rho0 is embedded, with exact first and second fiber rims. Let a,b be the free pair-of-pants generators based at x1, with a transported from the x0 boundary along rho0 and b the x1 boundary generator. Let w=[a,b], and take the actual finite path rho=rho0 followed by w. The original marked annulus map

    f(s,t)=(s,rho(t))

has those SAME two rims and original transverse path d=rho0 w. Its first fiber h is injected and its span joins the two separately labelled boundary tori.

The original d is not carried by the fixed-first-rim comparison annulus A0 with its boundary tracks in those two tori. Projection to Sigma would put w in the double coset <a><b>. Every element of that double coset has reduced form a^m b^n, whereas the commutator a b a^-1 b^-1 does not. Thus even preserving BOTH rim curves does not retain the original transverse relative class. Recovering d requires more than selecting A0 or adding an annular shear in its h direction. This is not a counterexample to a carrier theorem; it identifies the exact information the shortcut loses.

There is an actual embedded carrier preserving the original map. Take the ENTIRE finite image graph of rho, the boundary circles used to base a,b, and their actual access arcs in Sigma. Subdivide its finitely many crossings and vertices, and form its finite disk-and-band regular neighborhood R inside Sigma, relative to the prescribed boundary intervals. Then S1 times R is an ACTUAL embedded circle-piece neighborhood in Q containing the whole source image and its full relation graph by inclusion. Each vertex block is S1 times an actual polygonal disk and each edge block S1 times its actual band; their boundary parameters agree on the shared rectangular windows. No mapping-cylinder embedding is postulated. In the cyclic lift, h is the actual s translation; all period-return winding and both endpoint labels remain. The source d has not been replaced by the simpler rho0.
