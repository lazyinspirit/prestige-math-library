# Fixed-port removal of the complete inessential-circle forest

This is a mathematical donor, not an accepted library supplier. It makes the finite operation used in the current Normal working proof explicit. No canonical Statement or Definition is changed. The original hyperbolization theorem is not proved by this operation.

Let M be a compact connected orientable irreducible finite PL three-manifold with nonempty boundary, with a free PL involution tau. Let A be a finite disjoint family of proper annuli, and let tau A be its mate family. Assume transverse intersection, no triple points, and injection of the cyclic group of each annulus into pi_1(M). Each annulus has both actual rims on the physical boundary. No boundary point will move in the operation below. A and tau A are comparison systems: the two eventual motions are conjugate ambient isotopies, not one common ambient isotopy.

There is an ambient isotopy h_t of M fixed on its boundary such that h_1(A) and tau h_1(A) have no inessential intersection circles. Every originally essential circle and every intersection arc is unchanged. The second system is obtained by applying tau h_1 tau to the old tau A. An entire marked region bounded by A, and every mapped root spine in it, may be transported by h_t; its mate is transported by conjugacy. All labelled horizontal boundary tracks are constant.

The proof uses the exact-boundary PL Dehn lemma in an orientable finite PL quotient, and the elementary relative disk-system isotopy in an irreducible finite PL manifold. These are the precise supplier interfaces, not a characteristic-submanifold theorem. The former is the literal Statement of `items/thm-dehn-lemma-for-finitely-triangulated-orientable-pl-three-manifolds.md`. The latter is proved in the Normal working source's section “Proper disk systems with the same labelled ports”; a summary of its finite construction is included below.

First an intersection circle bounds a disk on one annulus exactly when it does on the other. One implication follows because the annulus that would otherwise have an essential core injects in M; the converse is symmetric. On every annulus take all maximal disks bounded by such circles. They are disjoint within that annulus and contain every smaller cap. The total cap union C is invariant. It meets neither essential-circle nor arc intersection loci: an essential circle cannot lie inside a cap, and an intersection arc cannot cross a cap boundary because triple points are excluded. Thus C meets each annulus in its chosen cap disks and possibly curves already lying inside those disks, with no extra trace outside them.

Connect all components of C by a finite polygonal tree in the ambient interior, and add its tau-image. In dimension three the tree's interiors can avoid the remaining finite intersection one-complex and meet the annuli away from C only in finitely many transverse points. A sufficiently small invariant regular neighborhood R0 of this connected invariant polyhedron therefore meets each annulus in finitely many disjoint disks. To make this relative claim precise, subdivide the finite polyhedra and their images together. On each annulus the neighborhood of the maximal cap disks is a slightly enlarged disk, and the neighborhoods of the other transverse points are small disjoint disks. Choose the ambient neighborhoods relative to those surface neighborhoods, then take a common invariant derived subdivision. The remaining compact intersection locus is disjoint and is avoided by choosing the derived neighborhood sufficiently small. R0 misses the physical boundary. Its port circles on its boundary are pairwise disjoint, including across the two systems: all old annulus intersections are either strictly inside R0 or strictly outside it. Every component of an annulus outside its R0-disk traces is connected and reaches its two physical rims.

We enlarge R0 to an invariant irreducible R without changing any disk trace or port. Suppose an interior sphere S in the current R bounds its ambient irreducibility ball B which is not wholly in R. Every connected component of M minus R meeting the interior of B lies wholly in B: a path leaving B would cross S, which lies in the interior of R. Adjoin these whole complementary components and their actual tau-mates. Their frontiers are entire boundary components of R, so the enlarged region is still a compact PL manifold. It is R union B union tau B, with redundant subsets suppressed. It has no new boundary pieces.

No outside annulus portion can meet a newly filled component. Such a portion is connected, misses R, and reaches the physical boundary outside B. If it met B, a path in that portion to a physical rim would cross S, a contradiction. The same applies to tau B. Thus every old disk trace and its entire port collar survives unchanged. Every nontrivial enlargement removes at least one component of the original finite complement and never splits or creates one. Termination is finite. In the final region every sphere's ambient ball is contained in R; hence R is irreducible. R remains invariant, connected and interior.

The free quotient Q=R/tau is a compact orientable finite PL three-manifold. The port circles form free pairs: a port from A cannot equal one from tau A because those port families are disjoint. Each port orbit therefore projects to an embedded circle in the boundary of Q. It is nullhomotopic in Q by projecting its original proper disk trace in R. Apply the exact-boundary Dehn lemma to obtain a proper embedded disk for each of these exact port circles.

Choose the quotient disks disjoint by induction, without replacing any port. Against an already disjoint subsystem, a new disk has only interior circle intersections. A cap innermost on a subsystem disk has interior disjoint from the new disk and all other subsystem disks. Replace the disk it bounds on the new disk by a slight parallel copy of that clean cap. The result is still a proper disk with the same boundary and strictly fewer intersections with the subsystem. Repetition finishes the next disk, and finite induction finishes the family. This uses no irreducibility of Q. In particular it does not assume a false clean-cap selection for an arbitrary intersecting forest.

Lift the quotient disk family to R. Its lifts are a disjoint paired disk system; each disk has two lifts because its domain is simply connected. Select the lift with the A-labelled port for each old A disk. This selected system and the old A traces are disjoint disk systems individually, with exactly the same labelled boundaries. Relative disk-system isotopy in irreducible R supplies h_t, fixed on its boundary, carrying the whole old A system to the selected target system. Extend by the identity outside R. Its conjugate carries the old tau A traces to the other half of the lifted targets. These two target halves are disjoint, so every old inessential circle disappears. Outside R the two systems were unchanged, hence no essential circle or arc was changed or introduced.

For completeness, the disk-system isotopy is a finite ball operation. Separate corresponding old and target collars at each common rim without moving the rim. Innermost target caps clean of the entire old system give embedded spheres with old caps. Their irreducibility balls contain no other old disks, since those disks reach the physical boundary and miss both sphere faces. Push the old cap across this ball to a small parallel target cap; total old/target circle count decreases. Once interiors are disjoint, each matched disk pair bounds the pinched ball between its separated rim collars. All other old and target disks are outside this pocket because they reach different physical rims. A parameterized ball push, fixed on the common rim and extended through a tapered outer collar, matches this disk pair while fixing the other disks. The finitely many pushes give the whole-system isotopy. Finite ordered-prism subdivisions supply PL time parameters; no differentiable-ball extension is asserted.

The operation moves the entire original region, not just its boundary disks. If y is any original mapped spine, its new map is h_1 composed with y; the homotopy h_t composed with y retains every based loop, relation, root degree and transverse word with their actual tracks. For a basepoint on the horizontal physical boundary the basepoint is fixed. No annulus core is replaced by a meridional cap circle, and no off-cycle branch is discarded.

This proves complete finite inessential-circle removal. It gives neither an I-bundle structure on the union of the moved regions nor a common circle fibration on its essential-circle sectors. It also gives no global mirror-color or SUPER-annulus reduction.
