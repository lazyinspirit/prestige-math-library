# Actual forget-z chain and adjacent-edge dictionary

This new construction concerns the supplied strong balanced link diagram and its actual finite marked strip counts. It supplies a local dictionary, with no general movie-invariance assertion. It uses the newly authored old-end and colored-neck operations directly. Source context is the full argument of Lemma14.12 and the elementary reductions in Section14.5 of https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.05207, read in their full text; the argument below is local.

## The same actual strip problem after forgetting z

Delete the labels z from the geometric input without changing the surface, compression systems, marked divisors, end tensors or boundary conditions. The underlying ordinary multi-pointed Heegaard problem has precisely the same target and tori. At the level of ACTUAL maps its strip set and regularity problem have not changed. Each strip formerly weighted by the product of all U_w to n_w and all V_z to n_z is now weighted only by its w intersections. Consequently its finite matrix is exactly the specialization at every V_z=1. This is a specialization of the actual finite count problem, not an assertion that arbitrary independently chosen ordinary tensors give identical counts. The artificial protected z-divisors may remain protected; no ordinary count requires them to be varied. The proven curvature specializes to zero, because each w has two adjacent intervals. Thus this gives a genuine ordinary w-based chain complex at these chosen data.

In the inserted diagram forgetting z leaves exactly the ordinary free-point stabilization diagram: the new small alpha and beta circles bound cut balls with the two w marks on their opposite bigons. The other two bigons are now unmarked. Their two contributions to the reverse off-diagonal coefficient are both one and cancel. The remaining coefficient is U_w+U_w'. Hence the actual ordinary count matrix is the V=1 matrix already constructed. The local w-top generator is theta_w by the index-one w-free bigon. Ordinary free-point insertion and deletion defined in this CHOSEN neck by that top input and its complementary extraction are therefore exactly S+ and S- of the link insertion. This proves the elementary free-point dictionary at this neck. No continuation map between independently chosen ordinary data is inferred.

## Every adjacent link arc, not just the inserted pair

Let w1,z,w2 be consecutive marks on one link component; w1 and w2 may coincide. Represent the actual oriented arc lambda from w1 to w2 as lambda1 followed by lambda2, meeting at z, using its bridge projections to the Heegaard surface. The first projected bridge avoids alpha and the second avoids beta: push each boundary-parallel bridge to the boundary of its supplied cut ball, disjoint from its compression disks, then perturb its projection transverse to the other curves and away from generators and marks except its endpoints. This is an immersed representative of the actual arc, with the same endpoints. It need not be a short local insertion arc.

For a strip domain B define a(lambda,B) as its alpha-boundary intersection with this representative, counted with the boundary orientation. Only lambda2 contributes, since lambda1 misses alpha. Across an alpha crossing on lambda2 the region multiplicity changes by precisely that alpha-boundary coefficient; there are no beta crossings on lambda2. Telescoping all its crossings therefore gives
$$a(\lambda,B)=n_{w2}(B)-n_z(B).$$
This identity holds for every actual strip domain, with multiplicity and with self-intersections of the projected arc: the successive differences telescope along the parametrized arc, so an immersion creates no correction. Likewise the beta-boundary weight satisfies b(lambda,B)=n_z(B)-n_w1(B). Signs disappear over characteristic two.

Define the actual ordinary edge operators A_lambda and B_lambda by these extra integer weights modulo two on the finite V=1 strip counts. Differentiate the independently marked polynomial differential BEFORE specialization to define Phi_w and Psi_z. Coefficient by coefficient the preceding equalities give the STRICT identities
$$A_\lambda=U_{w2}\Phi_{w2}+\left.\Psi_z\right|_{V=1},\qquad B_\lambda=U_{w1}\Phi_{w1}+\left.\Psi_z\right|_{V=1}.$$
Here every z variable is set to one, and other w variables can remain independent. The factor U_w restores the one power removed by its derivative. The derivative at V=1 weights by n_z exactly, including multiply intersecting strips. Finiteness was proved before specializing, so no sum or differentiation is interchanged with an infinite series. On identifying all w variables with U, the differentiated curvature identities make Phi_w and Psi_z chain maps. Thus these strict identities identify actual chain maps, and in particular Psi_z=A_lambda+U Phi_w2=B_lambda+U Phi_w1.

## Exact extent for original consumers

This removes the arbitrary-adjacent-arc identity from the proposed forget-z graph dictionary and proves the ordinary free-point identification at the chosen insertion. Together with the actual neck it gives the coefficient-one local cup/edge/cap cancellation S- A_lambda S+=Id. It does not prove that an arbitrary embedded H-shaped graph has operator B_lambda+U Phi_w1, that ordinary graph words are invariant under graph Cerf moves, that a decorated surface movie reduces to such a graph word, or that type-z band triangles act as the ordinary diagram comparison identity. Those are distinct geometric operations. In particular the ordinary tower generator has not yet been proved to be the image of the whole disk-w-region surface map, and no annulus concordance map or stable-slice conclusion follows from this local dictionary alone.
