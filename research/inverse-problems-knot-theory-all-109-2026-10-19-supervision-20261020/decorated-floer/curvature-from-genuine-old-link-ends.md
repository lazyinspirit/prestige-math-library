# Curvature after the actual old-link end construction

This argument records the exact remaining use of the new one-system count. It is conditional on the ACTUAL finite regular old-link strip count, index-two compactification and its exhaustive collars, and the new one-system boundary-degeneration normalization. It does not manufacture those inputs from the S3-knot or polygon theorem.

Let the old balanced multipointed link have $n$ w marks and $n$ z marks, with one of each in every alpha cut component and in every beta cut component. Work over the polynomial ring with a separate variable for EVERY mark. For each actual isolated index-one strip multiply its count by its full monomial of all marked intersection degrees, giving the finite matrix $D$.

Consider a fixed input and output and the actual compactified one-dimensional, translation-quotiented, index-two strip moduli. Broken strips contribute the corresponding entry of $D^2$, with the product monomial, since actual intersection degrees add under concatenation. A boundary degeneration has index at least two; at total index two the remaining strip must be constant by the supplied good-end exclusion. Thus it contributes only on the diagonal. Positivity and the one-system index formula force its domain to be the unit domain of one alpha or beta cut component. Its actual gluing collar uses that component's normalized moduli at the given constant generator; this is precisely the normalization/matching input, not a general assertion that any formal index-two domain has count one.

For $n>1$, a unit alpha component containing $(w_i,z_j)$ contributes exactly $U_{w_i}V_{z_j}I$ and its beta counterpart contributes the monomial for the endpoints of its beta bridge. Taken together, the alpha and beta bridges are exactly the oriented intervals between consecutive w and z marks on the actual link. Therefore the total boundary-degeneration contribution is
$$\omega I=\left(\sum_{\text{adjacent }w,z\text{ intervals}} U_wV_z\right)I.$$
The mod-two boundary of each compact one-manifold is zero, so $D^2=\omega I$. No link-connectedness assumption is used: each cut component and each adjacent interval is counted once regardless of which cycle of the global matching permutation contains it.

For $n=1$ the one-system degeneration count is zero, rather than one. The displayed curvature is nevertheless zero as well: the one w,z pair bounds two intervals, so its two identical monomials cancel in characteristic two. Thus this separate case is consistent without falsely changing its boundary-degeneration normalization. Possible interior sphere and other limiting pieces must already have been excluded by the ACTUAL old-link compactification/index argument; this conditional paragraph does not replace that analytic work with a formal expected-dimension claim.

If every w variable is sent to $U$ and every z variable to $V$, then $\omega$ becomes $2n\,UV=0$ over $\mathbb F_2$. This proves the two-color square-zero conclusion once the genuine old-link ends and the normalization have been constructed. The independent-variable identity is the one needed BEFORE applying any basepoint differentiation, so specializing early would lose necessary information.
