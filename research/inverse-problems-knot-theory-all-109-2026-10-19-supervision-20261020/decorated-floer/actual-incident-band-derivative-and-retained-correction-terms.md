# Actual incident-band derivative identity

This is one bounded new operation on the already supplied actual band triple. It is not an assertion that a whole surface admits the compatible typed tree word.

This is not a claim that ThetaW is a cycle with independent z variables.

$$D_o W+W D_i+(V_a+V_b)Z=0.$$
A one-system degeneration has index two and would leave a negative-index principal triangle; it contributes no additional boundary to this index-one triangle family. The triangle identity is the actual full-variable Leibniz identity with arbitrary fixed auxiliary input, not an application of a cycle-only chain-map assertion. All counts and the tensors ThetaW,ThetaZ are fixed, and the latter have variable-independent generator coefficients in this chosen model.

Differentiate this exact polynomial identity in Va, then set all V equal to one. Write H=(partial_Va W)|V=1. The derivative of the displayed third term is Z, because Va+Vb becomes zero after coloring. The two differential derivatives are the actual marked actions Psi_a on the physical input and output ends. The derivative of W contributes D_o H+H D_i. Consequently
$$Z=\Psi_{o,a}W+W\Psi_{i,a}+D_oH+HD_i.$$
There is no omitted derivative of ThetaW in this chosen generator model. With a polynomial auxiliary cycle instead, the corresponding derivative of that cycle must also be included; the formula cannot silently be reused after replacing the input by such a representative. Polynomial finiteness makes H an actual finite count operator.

$$\Phi_{o,b}W+W\Phi_{i,b}=D_o H_U+H_U D_i.$$

$$Z=A_{\lambda_o}W+W A_{\lambda_i}+D_o(H+U H_U)+(H+U H_U)D_i.$$

## Fixed-beta weighted triangle identity and reverse local top

There is no endpoint correction: the lambda endpoints are marks away from all attaching curves, and the actual concatenated beta-boundary chains have the same corner incidence convention. This uses the actual already finite moduli and no new universal graph-invariance assertion.
