# Actual adapted leaf-word cancellation

This new operation normalizes a chosen ordinary graph WORD at an adapted leaf. It does not prove that every decorated movie produces such a word or that arbitrary choices of graph words agree. It uses the newly constructed actual colored neck and edge counts directly.

## A free-point neck suited to the retained paths

Use the DUAL alternating insertion, obtained by literally exchanging the names w and z in the physical new neck count, and then restoring the desired old-variable names. This changes no strips, indices, regularity operators or matching maps: each actual marked intersection is simply relabeled. The dual construction is centered at an old z mark. In its two-circle disk, the NEW w is in their overlap lens and the OLD w is in the outer face; the two z marks are in the other two opposite faces. Thus both new circles enclose the new w and not any retained w. After setting z variables to one, the actual ordinary differential has two canceled empty-bigon contributions in the top-to-bottom direction and U_old+U_new in the reverse direction. After identifying the U variables it is the actual two copies of the old D. Its S+ and S- are the w-top inclusion and complementary extraction.

Choose the insertion disk disjoint from all retained edge representatives. This is an adapted choice of the auxiliary free-point data. An auxiliary point can be placed there from the outset; retained edges have finitely many one-dimensional representatives, so its small disk and connecting collar can be chosen away from them. No isotopy comparison with a previously fixed free-point map is asserted.

For a retained path gamma missing this disk, every old strip has the same alpha-boundary intersection weight as before. The top-to-bottom empty cases have only their new alpha boundary in the disk, so have zero gamma weight: in the DUAL placement the remote empty degeneration is the INNER cut disk containing the old z, whose boundary is the new alpha circle alone. The outer cut region containing the retained old w belongs to the reverse arrow. A REMOTE reverse-arrow degeneration may also have old alpha boundary outside the disk, and its gamma weight is not assumed zero. Retaining this possible contribution gives the sufficient ACTUAL triangular matrix A_gamma-plus=[[A_gamma,K_gamma],[0,A_gamma]], with K_gamma unrestricted. Its diagonal matching is the same old count with its old boundary weight retained. The top column and bottom row therefore prove STRICT commutation with the chosen S+ and S-. No full edge tensor identity or commutation with another edge action is asserted.

## An arbitrary leaf edge has coefficient one

Let lambda run from a retained branch point to the new leaf point. It may run far outside the insertion disk before reaching the leaf. Its signed total intersection with the new alpha circle is one modulo two, since its initial endpoint is outside and its final endpoint is inside. The top-to-bottom differential has TWO empty count-one cases; their alpha boundary arcs together are that full circle. Hence their edge-weighted top-to-bottom coefficient is exactly one. This uses the actual two empty cases and their actual boundary incidence, not the cancellation of their unweighted differential. Old-strip diagonal contributions, including any global part of lambda, are killed by complementary extraction. Paired reverse-arrow contributions are irrelevant on the top input. Thus on every actual old generator
$$S^-_{\mathrm{leaf}} A_\lambda S^+_{\mathrm{leaf}}=I.$$
This proves the long leaf-edge coefficient as well as the short local scalar at this chosen adapted insertion.

## Removing a leaf from an actual chosen word

Consider a chosen graph word in which an interior leaf point is introduced by S+_leaf, its single edge contributes A_lambda, and it is removed by S-_leaf. Retain the given cyclic order of all edge factors. Every OTHER edge misses the leaf disk by construction. Move only S+ and S- past those other edge factors, using the proved top-column and bottom-row commutation. Never interchange A_lambda with another edge action. The two S maps then enclose just A_lambda and cancel to the literal identity above. The remaining word has the same cyclic order with the leaf edge deleted. This proves one leaf deletion on the actual chosen chain complexes, with coefficient one and no U exponent. It can be repeated when a finite tree has been given successive adapted insertion data and the corresponding WORD decomposition.

The following interfaces remain explicit. One must construct that adapted word for the actual ribbon tree and prove that its changes of subdivision, branch decomposition and ordinary diagram choices are realized by the supplied maps; a leaf cannot be assumed already to occur as the displayed triple of factors. Bivalent vertices and the resulting end-to-end arc must also be identified with the actual ordinary arc map, using the accepted bare-arc interface only once its hypotheses are met. Finally the colored surface movie must be compared with this ordinary word, including its band triangles. Thus this proves a genuine local leaf-word normalization operation, not whole tree or whole surface tower nonvanishing.

## The chosen vertical root and its short local word

For the existence construction, first use ONLY the accepted topological proper-arc straightening on a chosen root arc of the surface, fixed in the endpoint collars. Work on the resulting surface. This root is now a vertical product arc. Its height derivative is nonzero everywhere, so a relative generic perturbation of the surface away from it makes the rest of the surface movie Morse without adding any root critical point. The supplied finite-forest construction can attach its tracks to this fixed proper arc; no graph-map invariance is used to straighten a graph after constructing its maps.

A short-root word can be normalized entirely in one original uncut region R of its endpoint ordinary Heegaard diagram. Choose nearby distinct points p and q in R and perform the actual dual free-point insertion there. At uniform U the ACTUAL source is two copies of the old count matrix. Deleting the local pair of curves and keeping p or instead q gives the same old generator tuples. For every old map its domain coefficient is constant on R, so n_p(B)=n_q(B); thus the two target ordinary count matrices are LITERALLY the same, at the same tensors. Define the chosen local point-deletion on this physical packet by theta_bottom extraction, with the retained point label chosen as needed. Both are chain maps to their actual deleted-packet target for the just-proved reason. The edge from p to q has top-to-bottom coefficient one by the same full-circle boundary-incidence calculation. Therefore the chosen local word S^-_p A_pq S^+_q sends each x to x. Identifying q back with the fixed vertical-root point p uses that literal old-domain count identification inside R; no continuation or distant point-motion assertion enters it.

This is a chosen elementary local word and its two point-deletion maps, not an identification with an arbitrary preexisting graph-map convention. To close the existence argument one must still prove that the actual surface's elementary ordinary dictionary assembles the adapted tree word, so that its valid local leaf and bivalent reductions reach this specific local root word. The topological root straightening alone supplies none of those map comparisons.
