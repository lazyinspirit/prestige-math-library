# Actual unlink sphere top cycle for a band input

This new operation supplies the second local model needed to construct a chosen band auxiliary top cycle. It is distinct from the alternating four-marked sphere already constructed. It makes no arbitrary-factor neck-transfer assertion.

Take the Riemann sphere with alpha the extended real line and beta the extended imaginary line. The two intersections are zero and infinity. Put w1 and z1 in the first quadrant, w2 and z2 in the third quadrant, and no marks in the other two quadrants. Each alpha and each beta complementary disk contains precisely one w and one z. Choose the two bridge pairs in small disjoint neighborhoods of the arcs joining w1 to z1 and w2 to z2 in their respective quadrants. Each paired union bounds a disk in its small three-ball, and these balls are disjoint. Thus this is an actual two-component unlink diagram, not the four-marked one-component diagram obtained from alternating singly marked quadrants.

The physical strip calculation is the same rank-one sector calculation as for the four-marked sphere. A positive index-one domain has nonnegative integer coefficients on the four bigons with sum one. It is therefore one of those four bigons. The sector map exp(c(s+it)+i theta) gives one strip modulo real translation in each case. Reflection across the two real boundary lines gives the linearized Fourier modes and its translation-only kernel, so each count is regular and one. Label the generators top and bottom so the two EMPTY bigons run from top to bottom. Those two contributions cancel over characteristic two. The other two bigons run from bottom to top and contain the two marked pairs. The ACTUAL independently marked matrix is consequently
$$D(\mathrm{top})=0,\qquad D(\mathrm{bottom})=(U_{w1}V_{z1}+U_{w2}V_{z2})\,\mathrm{top}.$$
In particular top is an actual cycle even before identifying variables. After uniform coloring the differential is zero. No abstract rank computation was used to produce the cycle.

The empty bigons have index one and no w or z intersections, so top exceeds bottom by one in BOTH relative Maslov gradings. Their relative Alexander degrees agree. This differs from the alternating sphere, where the w-top and z-top are different generators and their Alexander degrees differ by one. We do not infer absolute auxiliary degrees from this relative calculation alone.

For a subordinate band triple the geometric cut along the parallel-curve spheres reduces its auxiliary pair to twice-marked rank-zero spheres, one alternating four-marked sphere, and ordinary unlink/handle factors of this type. The present calculation supplies the actual local top cycle. To obtain a top cycle in the ORIGINAL chosen auxiliary diagram one still needs the actual inverse-neck transfer, preserving all marks, for this unlink placement. That transfer has not been inferred from the alternating insertion theorem. The required local return contribution would carry the PRODUCT U_w' V_z' from an old cut component containing both old marks, instead of one of the separate remote variables of the alternating model. Once that geometric count is supplied, its actual product top cycle can be used as the input to a chosen triangle count; regularity, compactification, filtration and tower normalization of that triangle operation are further obligations.
