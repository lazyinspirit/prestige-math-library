Their other two faces contain the w punctures. Thus A-prime is isotopic to A in the twice-punctured sphere; squeezing the two unmarked bigons gives a small graph over A while retaining two intersections. The other auxiliary pairs are already parallel Hamiltonian pairs. The new area construction makes the positive and negative auxiliary bigon areas equal. ## What must still be proved before this gives a count

This model alone is NOT yet the actual band triangle unit theorem. A complete proof needs a uniform patched inverse at the thin-strip/constant-strip junction after the mean rescaling, with the precise source-end norms and no unaccounted slowly decaying modes. It must bound the mixed mean/nonzero-mode error by a quantity tending to zero, solve the nonlinear equation by contraction, and prove that every actual index-zero triangle tends to one of these configurations. In particular one must exclude an escaping gradient trajectory that breaks at a lower critical point while its attachment approaches a wall of the unstable cell, and exclude unbounded winding in shrinking signed bigons. The fact that the finite OLD generator tuples avoid those walls controls the formal limiting endpoints; it is not by itself a full analytic exhaustion proof. The domain-energy argument must be uniform along the squeeze, rather than merely finite separately at each positive epsilon.

There is a second, independent comparison requirement: moving from the actual finite-angle auxiliary diagram to the small graph must carry its concrete top cycle by actual continuation, and the parameterized triangle moduli must identify the band triangle with that transported small-graph unit map. This requires the continuation and one-parameter collar construction with the fixed end data. One cannot simply replace the actual auxiliary diagram by the small model. If those two comparisons are proved, each actual old generator has one small-graph triangle of coefficient one and the V=1 band map is the ordinary chosen comparison, hence tower-normalized. At present the uniform junction/exhaustion and continuation comparison are explicitly held. No ordinary unit coefficient, absolute half-degree anchor or surface tower nonvanishing is asserted here.
