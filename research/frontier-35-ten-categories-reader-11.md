## Findings and repairs

1. In `items/lem-parabolic-mackey-biset-splitting-in-gl-n.md`, proof step 2.3
   derived a block-diagonal entry from `g=lu` after defining `g=ul`. I corrected
   the calculation to use the established order: for indices `k,j` in the same
   alpha-block `I_t`,
   `(ul)_{kj} = sum_{m in I_t} u_{km} l_{mj} = l_{kj}`, since the diagonal
   alpha-block of `u` is the identity. This proves that `l` is the alpha-block
   diagonal part. The calculation is now consistent with the Levi factorization
   fixed in the same step and with the cited block criteria.
2. In `items/thm-parabolic-mackey-formula-for-finite-gl-n.md`, proof step 8.1
   used `f(u^{-1}gv)` with coordinate parabolics `P_gamma=uP_alpha u^{-1}` and
   `P_delta=vQv^{-1}`. Its right-covariance check conjugated `q` in the wrong
   direction. I replaced the transport with
   `Upsilon(f)(g)=f(ugv^{-1})`. Then `vqv^{-1} in P_delta`, its Levi projection
   is `v pi(q) v^{-1}`, and the specified `L_delta` action on `X` gives
   `Upsilon(f)(gq)=pi(q)^{-1} Upsilon(f)(g)`. The inverse is
   `F -> (h -> F(u^{-1}hv))`; left translation satisfies
   `Upsilon(h.f)=(u^{-1}hu).Upsilon(f)`, so the map identifies the required
   unipotent invariants and Levi actions. This also gives the coordinate
   conjugations and the stated `rho'` twist.
3. In the same theorem, proof step 8.2 asserted that in the full-flag case all
   Weyl twists `{}^rho X` are isomorphic to `X`. That is false for an arbitrary
   torus module. I replaced `n!` copies of `X` by
   `direct_sum_{rho in S_n} {}^rho X`. A witness to the distinction is `n=2`,
   `q=3`, with the one-dimensional character
   `diag(a,d) -> a`: its transposition twist is `diag(a,d) -> d`, and the two
   characters differ at `diag(-1,1)`.
4. In `items/def-compositions-partial-flags-and-standard-parabolics.md`, I
   corrected “choose denominators as follows” to “choose vectors as follows”
   in the adapted-basis construction for transitivity on partial flags.
