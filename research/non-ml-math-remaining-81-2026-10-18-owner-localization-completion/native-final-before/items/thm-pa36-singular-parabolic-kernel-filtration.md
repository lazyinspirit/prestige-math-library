## Statement

Let $\kappa$ be antidominant, let
$$J=\{\alpha_i\in\Delta:\langle\kappa,\alpha_i^\vee\rangle=0\},$$
and assume its full zero-root subsystem is generated by $J$. For each
$\alpha\in J$, write $p_\alpha:X\to X_\alpha=G/P_\alpha$ for the simple
parabolic projection. Then there exists a finite sequence
$\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_N\in J$ such that for every coherent
$\mathcal D_\kappa$-module $M$ the following are equivalent:

1. $\Gamma(X,M)=0$;
2. there is a finite coherent filtration
   $$M=M_0\supseteq M_1\supseteq\cdots\supseteq M_N=0$$
   with $p_{\alpha_j*}(M_{j-1}/M_j)=0$ for every $j$.

The sequence is one choice, not asserted to be unique. The proof below gives
the sequence and proves (2)$\Rightarrow$(1); its converse is escalated
because it requires the rank-one kernel supplier
[[lem-pa36-singular-parabolic-kernel]].

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** Antidominant $\kappa$, its zero simple-root set $J$, the stated
zero-root subsystem condition, and coherent $M$.

[F1] $W_J$ is the finite Weyl group of the zero-root subsystem; it has a
longest element $w_0^J$ with a reduced expression in the simple reflections
$s_\alpha$ for $\alpha\in J$
([[def-complex-semisimple-algebraic-group-borel-and-flag-variety]]).

[F2] A B-stable filtration of the finite-dimensional highest-weight module
of highest weight $\rho$ has one-dimensional weight quotients; each weight
is below $\rho$ in the positive-root order ([[thm-borel-weil]]).

[F3] For antidominant $\kappa$, global sections is exact and the localization
unit is an isomorphism ([[thm-pa36-antidominant-singular-serre-quotient]],
[[lem-pa36-global-section-unit]]).

## Proof

1.1 Choose a reduced expression
$w_0^J=s_{\alpha_N}\cdots s_{\alpha_1}$ and define
$\mu_0=\rho$, $\mu_j=s_{\alpha_j}\mu_{j-1}$. This finite sequence is
contained in $J$. Since the word is reduced and $\rho$ pairs positively
integrally with every positive coroot of the zero-root subsystem,
$\langle\mu_{j-1},\alpha_j^\vee\rangle$ is a positive integer at each
step. The final weight $\mu_N=w_0^J\rho$ is strictly antidominant for the
zero-root subsystem. The hypotheses determine only that such a reduced word
exists; they do not single one out. [F1, choose]

2.1 Suppose (2) holds. For each quotient $Q_j=M_{j-1}/M_j$, the hypothesis
$p_{\alpha_j*}Q_j=0$ gives
$\Gamma(X,Q_j)=\Gamma(X_{\alpha_j},p_{\alpha_j*}Q_j)=0$. Exactness of
$\Gamma(X,-)$ from [F3] applied successively to the short exact sequences
$0\to M_j\to M_{j-1}\to Q_j\to0$ then gives
$\Gamma(X,M_{j-1})\cong\Gamma(X,M_j)$ for every $j$. Since $M_N=0$,
$\Gamma(X,M)=0$. This proves (2)$\Rightarrow$(1). [F3, given,
algebra]

3.1 For (1)$\Rightarrow$(2), Kashiwara's proof filters the relevant
induced module by B-weights along the sequence in [step 1.1]. The
complex-weight separation lemma
[[lem-pa36-complex-weight-central-character-separation]] isolates the
distinguished $\chi_\kappa$ summand, and every successive quotient must then
be identified as a simple-parabolic kernel using the converse in
[[lem-pa36-singular-parabolic-kernel]]. The required identification is exactly the unproved converse of the rank-one
$\mathcal D_0$-module classification recorded in
[[lem-pa36-singular-parabolic-kernel]]; neither its citation nor a proposed
filtration establishes that identification. Thus this implication remains
unresolved and is not claimed as proved. [F1, F2, F3, step 1.1]
