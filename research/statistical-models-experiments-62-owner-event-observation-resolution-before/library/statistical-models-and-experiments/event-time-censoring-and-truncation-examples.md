The examples compute exponential event contributions for exact, right-censored, interval, current-status, delayed-entry, and right-truncated observations. A finite pair of dependent censoring models has the same observed $(U,\delta)$ law but different event-time distributions, and a uniform example computes how left truncation changes the selected law.
