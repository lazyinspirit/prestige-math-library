## Summary

These examples isolate what the CLT and MCSE hypotheses do. An identity-kernel chain has finite variance but no root-$n$ normal limit without ergodicity; a stationary Gaussian AR(1) chain has an explicit $L^2$ Poisson solution whose martingale and covariance long-run variances agree; and a square-root batch sequence fails a specific Flegal–Jones sufficient-condition limit. The batch-size example establishes only inapplicability of that sufficient route, not inconsistency of the estimator.
