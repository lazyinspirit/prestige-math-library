## Examples

[[ex-ishigami-function-sobol-decomposition]] computes an independent-input
interaction, while [[ex-perfectly-correlated-inputs-and-shapley-allocation]]
shows the separate dependent-input allocation. [[ex-zero-variance-sobol-indices-undefined]]
records why zero-variance outputs have no normalized indices, and
[[ex-polynomial-chaos-coefficients-and-sobol-indices]] computes a complete
finite tensor case. [[ex-noisy-simulator-intrinsic-noise-and-input-sensitivity]]
separates input-driven variance from intrinsic randomness; the finite
Bernoulli example [[ex-ratio-pick-freeze-index-estimator-is-biased]] exhibits
bias from dividing unbiased estimates.
