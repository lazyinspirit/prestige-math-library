## Objective

The examples verify an exact degree-one expansion for a positive two-state reaction rate, uniform coercivity and the zero solution for an affine random diffusion, the Cauchy-law failure of a degree-one moment cutoff, singularity of a higher-degree Gram matrix on finite support, and the mode/node work comparison for a three-input degree-two truncation. The two counterexamples delimit finite moment construction from completeness and positive definiteness. The work comparison records the three-dimensional counts explicitly and does not claim a general sparse-grid tractability rate.
