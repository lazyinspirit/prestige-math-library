## Summary

These examples compute the exact BAOAB harmonic position variance and its separate kinetic bias, exhibit a step above the proven harmonic stability limit where no invariant law exists, and show why a constant potential on the line has no normalizable Gibbs probability. The final comparison gives the published overdamped ULA variance beside the underdamped BAOAB harmonic covariances; their different state variables and transition maps keep the conclusions separate.
