## Examples

These calculations distinguish a Hermitian-definite mass-stiffness problem, an
infinite eigenvalue caused by singular $B$ in an otherwise regular pencil, and
an identically singular determinant pencil. The mass-stiffness example also
checks the residual enclosure in the $B$-dual norm.
