### `thm-upper-and-lower-central-characterizations-of-nilpotence`

- **Location:** Proof step 1.1.
- **Severity:** nonfatal (a local quotient correction repairs it).
- **Finding:** The expression
  `H_{i+1}/Z_i(G)` need not be defined. The induction hypothesis gives
  `H_i <= Z_i(G)`, not `Z_i(G) <= H_{i+1}`.
- **Evidence:** From `[G,H_{i+1}] <= Z_i(G)` one may conclude that every coset
  `hZ_i(G)` with `h in H_{i+1}` is central, or equivalently that
  `H_{i+1}Z_i(G)/Z_i(G) <= Z(G/Z_i(G))`. That is enough to obtain
  `H_{i+1} <= Z_{i+1}(G)`, but it is not the quotient written in the proof.
- **Proposed repair:** Replace `H_{i+1}/Z_i(G)` by
  `H_{i+1}Z_i(G)/Z_i(G)` (or give the elementwise coset argument).

### `thm-free-modules-are-projective-with-choice-boundary`

- **Location:** `Given` line and the transition from the Statement to proof
  step 1.1.
- **Severity:** nonfatal (transport across the standard coordinate
  isomorphism is routine).
- **Finding:** The Statement covers every module possessing a basis, but the
  proof silently replaces such a module by the literal standard module
  `F=R^{(X)}`. No step constructs the isomorphism determined by the given
  basis or transports the lift back to the original `F`.
- **Evidence:** `def-free-module-on-a-set-and-standard-basis` distinguishes

  module has a basis. The proof's `Given` line assumes the former.
- **Proposed repair:** Begin by using the basis to identify
  `R^{(X)} ~= F`, prove the standard case, and transport the lift across this
  isomorphism.

### `lem-every-abelian-group-embeds-in-a-divisible-group`

- **Location:** proof contract, boundary row `empty`.
- **Severity:** nonfatal; the proof itself handles the zero group.
- **Finding:** The evidence says that for `A=0` the canonical free index set
  "may be empty". The underlying set of the zero group is the singleton
  `{0}`, so the canonical free module `Z^{(A)}` is indexed by one element, not
  by the empty set.
- **Evidence:** With `A=0`, the construction actually has
  `F ~= Z`, `K=F`, and `F/K=0`; that is why the proof still works.
- **Proposed repair:** State this actual zero-group specialization in the
  boundary evidence rather than invoking an empty index set.
