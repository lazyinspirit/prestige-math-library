## 2. Subject coverage

Definitions: unscaled Minkowski embedding (convention fixed, no √2 scaling);
full Euclidean lattice and covolume; successive minima for compact,
centrally-symmetric convex bodies with nonempty interior.

Geometry of numbers: half-open fundamental parallelotope of covolume volume and
finite bounded intersections; Blichfeldt; Minkowski convex-body theorem in the
strict and compact-at-equality forms; attained minima and adapted flag;
triangular Borel volume lemma; successive-minima deformation; the full
two-sided Minkowski second theorem.

Arithmetic: `O_K` and fractional ideals are full lattices; ideal covolume
`2^{-r_2}√|d_K|·N𝔞`; archimedean region with volume `2^{r_1}(π/2)^{r_2}t^n/n!`
and product bound `(t/n)^n`; small element in an ideal; Minkowski bound: every
class in `Cl(O_K)` has an integral representative of norm ≤ `M_K`; finitely many
ideals of bounded norm; `Cl(O_K)` finite; generated by prime ideals of norm
≤ `M_K`; `|d_K| > 1` for `n > 1`; a nontrivial field has a ramified finite
prime; bounded-conjugate polynomial finiteness; bounded primitive integral
element; Hermite–Minkowski finiteness.

Examples (B): `Z[i]` trivial class group via `M_K = 4/π < 2` and the published
PID criterion; `Q(√−5)` of order 2 by the unique norm-2 ramified ideal, with
non-principality; `Q(√10)` of order 2 by norm-2/norm-3 ideals and the
`(4+√10)` relation; trivial class group of the quintic `X⁵−X−1` by excluding
norms 2 and 3; signature-constant proof that `|d_K| > 1`; nontrivial field has
a ramified finite prime; a scaled-vs-unscaled covolume counterexample.

## 4. The one statement-level deviation (scope-preserving)

`lem-minkowski-successive-minima-volume-deformation`: the design says the
deformation "sends the interior of C to a centrally symmetric **convex** set
containing no nonzero lattice point", and its strategy says the upper bound
"applies the equality form of Minkowski to the volume deformation". Read in
full, Green's §3.7 proof uses convexity only of `K` (slice centroids and their
weighted average lie in `λ_kK`) and never asserts or proves convexity of the
image `φ(K)`; lattice avoidance comes from Blichfeldt applied to `φ(K)` with the
sublattice `2Λ`, plus openness of `K` and the adapted-flag property
`λ_kK ∩ Λ ⊆ span(b_1,…,b_{k−1})`. The scaffold states exactly that content
(measurable, centrally symmetric, volume `∏λᵢ vol(C)`, no two distinct points
differing by `2Λ`; convexity not asserted) and the second-theorem item depends
on Blichfeldt + the deformation, so the promised two-sided theorem is still
supplied. No consumer needs convexity of the image. Assessment: a deliberate,
documented correction of an unsupported design claim; scope is preserved. (I did
not attempt to decide whether the image is nevertheless convex; the review does
not need that.)
