## Examples

A two-point target and proposal give a bounded density ratio with an exact
acceptance rate and proposal count. A countable Pareto-type pair has normalized
masses but an unbounded target-to-proposal ratio, so no finite envelope works.
A second two-point calculation uses unnormalized masses to verify the
$Z_f/(cZ_g)$ acceptance formula and the normalized accepted law.
