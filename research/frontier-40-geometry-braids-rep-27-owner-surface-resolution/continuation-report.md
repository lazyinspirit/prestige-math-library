What is missing is a proved persistence/termination invariant for that successor when all quartic and higher terms and coordinate corrections are retained.

The source's displayed transition to its final special class is not sufficient as written. The following substitution is exact over a characteristic-zero field:

`F = z² + x³ + y⁵`, with `x = y u` and `z = y v`, gives

`F = y² (v² + y u³ + y³)`.

The strict-transform equation is therefore **`v² + y u³ + y³ = 0`**. At its origin the tangent quadratic is `v²` and its cubic restriction to the kernel plane of that quadratic is `y³`. A pure cubic cube does not become `t·a s²` with nonzero residue `a` under an invertible linear change: the first has a triple linear factor and the second two distinct factors. Higher quadratic changes in the square coordinate only add cubic terms divisible by that coordinate and do not change this restriction. Thus the immediate special-class inference cannot be justified by dropping the higher terms.
