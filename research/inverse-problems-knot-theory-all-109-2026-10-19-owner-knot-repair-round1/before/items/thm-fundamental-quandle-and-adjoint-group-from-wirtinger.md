## Statement

Let $K$ be a nonempty oriented knot and $D$ a regular oriented diagram of $K$.
Let $Q(D)$ be its diagram quandle and let
$$
W(D)=\langle g_a\ (a\in A(D))\mid g_v=g_o^{-1}g_ug_o\text{ at every crossing with source }u,\text{ target }v,\text{ and overarc }o\rangle.
$$
Assume that the designated Wirtinger constructor and correctness result for
$D$ use these same under-arcs, positive-meridian generators, and conjugation
direction, and identify $W(D)$ with $\pi_1(S^3\setminus K)$. Then the
quandle presentations $Q(D)$ from any two diagrams of $K$ are isomorphic by
the local quandle Tietze transformations for R1, R2, and R3, and
$\operatorname{Adj}(Q(D))\cong W(D)\cong\pi_1(S^3\setminus K)$. No
completeness or knot-classification assertion is made.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A nonempty oriented knot $K$, diagrams of $K$, the diagram quandle, $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$, and the stated conditional KT-01 interface.

[F1] [[def-rack-fundamental-quandle-and-adjoint-group]]: $Q(D)$ is generated by the under-arcs with crossing relations, and $\operatorname{Adj}(Q)$ has relations $e_{q*r}=e_r^{-1}e_qe_r$.

[F2] [[def-group-presentation]]: $\langle X\mid S\rangle$ is the quotient of the free group on $X$ by the normal closure of the relator words $S$.

[F3] [[thm-oriented-reidemeister-equivalence-theorem]]: assuming $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$, equivalent oriented diagrams are related by finitely many planar isotopies and oriented R1, R2, and R3 moves.

[F4] [[def-knot-planar-code]]: the finite code records the oriented knot's under-arcs, over/under data, and edge orientations; an under-arc is maximal between consecutive underpasses, and the code stores no algebraic crossing sign.

[A1] [[def-countable-choice]]: $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$ is the axiom that every sequence of nonempty sets has a choice function.

[A2] The conditional interface in the Statement is the declared but not yet verified KT-01 requirement: the actual constructor must use the same under-arcs, assign their positive meridians, and produce exactly the displayed conjugation relators; its correctness proof must identify that presented group with $\pi_1(S^3\setminus K)$ ([[alg-knot-wirtinger-presentation-from-code]], [[thm-knot-wirtinger-presentation-correctness]]). Their batch-56 carrier bodies are now present, but the chart direction and the correctness proof's transitive hypotheses do not yet verify this interface; see the unresolved checks below.

## Proof

1.1 Under R1, the new underarc generator $y$ satisfies either $y=x*x$ or $x=y*x$ for the unchanged arc color $x$. In the first case idempotency gives $y=x$; in the second, $x=x*x$ and right invertibility give the unique solution $y=x$. Thus the new generator is eliminated. Under R2, each added underarc generator is uniquely solved from its neighboring arc and the overarc by $*$ or $\bar *$; the reverse move gives the inverse substitution. Thus these moves are quandle Tietze transformations. [F1, F3, algebra]

1.2 Under R3, assigning boundary colors $x,y,z$ gives the two possible iterated labels $(x*y)*z$ and $(x*z)*(y*z)$; these agree by right self-distributivity. The operation is reversible in each over-color by right invertibility, so the reverse replacements give inverse substitutions. [F1, F3, algebra]

1.3 By [F4], the code uses the same maximal under-arcs and oriented over/under crossing data as $Q(D)$. Define $\phi:W(D)\to\operatorname{Adj}(Q(D))$ on generators by $\phi(g_a)=e_{x_a}$. At each crossing, $x_v=x_u*x_o$ in $Q(D)$, so the adjoint relation gives $e_{x_v}=e_{x_o}^{-1}e_{x_u}e_{x_o}$. Under the relator agreement required by [A2], every relator of $W(D)$ maps to the identity; [F2] makes the homomorphism from the free group on its arc generators descend to $W(D)$. [F1, F2, F4, A2, construct]

1.4 The conjugation operation on $W(D)$ is idempotent because $g*g=g^{-1}gg=g$. Its right translation by $h$ is conjugation by $h^{-1}$, with inverse conjugation by $h$. It is right self-distributive because both $(g*h)*k$ and $(g*k)*(h*k)$ reduce to $k^{-1}h^{-1}ghk$. Thus $\operatorname{Conj}(W(D))$ is a quandle. Its generators $g_a$ satisfy the crossing equations by the relators of $W(D)$, so recursive evaluation of terms gives a quandle homomorphism $f:Q(D)\to\operatorname{Conj}(W(D))$. Define $\psi$ on adjoint generators by $\psi(e_q)=f(q)$. Since $f(q*r)=f(r)^{-1}f(q)f(r)$, every adjoint relator maps to the identity; [F2] therefore makes $\psi$ a group homomorphism. [F1, F2, A2, construct]

2.1 By [F3], diagrams of $K$ are related by a finite sequence of these moves, and step 1.1 and step 1.2 identify the corresponding quandle presentations at each move. Their composites give an isomorphism between the two diagram quandles. Countable choice is used only for [F3]; these local presentation transformations use no choice. [A1, F1, F3, step 1.1, step 1.2]

3.1 The composites $\psi\phi$ fix each $g_a$. The other composite fixes each generator $e_q$: every $q\in Q(D)$ is represented by a term in the arc generators using $*$ and $\bar *$, and the adjoint relations give $e_{u*v}=e_v^{-1}e_ue_v$ and $e_{u\bar *v}=e_ve_ue_v^{-1}$, so induction on the term gives $\phi\psi(e_q)=e_q$. Hence $\phi$ and $\psi$ are inverse isomorphisms. Under the conditional interface A2, $W(D)$ is the group of the Wirtinger constructor and is $\pi_1(S^3\setminus K)$. [F1, A2, step 1.3, step 1.4, algebra] ∎
