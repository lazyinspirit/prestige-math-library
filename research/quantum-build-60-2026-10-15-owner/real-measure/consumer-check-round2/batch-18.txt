STATEMENT Assume the Axiom of Choice and Dependent Choice. Let $G$ be a locally compact Hausdorff abelian group with a fixed Haar measure $m_G$. Then there exists a Haar measure $m_{\widehat G}$ on $\widehat G$ such that for every $h$ in the complex span $E$ of $\{g*\widetilde g:g\in C_c(G;\mathbb C)\}$ (the positive core of [[lem-lca-positive-convolution-squares-form-an-inversion-core]]),
