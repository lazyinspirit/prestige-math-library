measurable probability-one event $A$ from its continuity clause. Define the
zero-repaired pathwise integral

$$X_t(\omega)=\begin{cases}\displaystyle\int_0^t B_r(\omega)\,dr,&\omega\in A,\\[4pt]0,&\omega\notin A,\end{cases}\qquad t\ge0,$$

$$\operatorname{Cov}(X_s,X_t)=\int_0^s\int_0^t\min(u,v)\,dv\,du.$$

For $0\le s\le t$, this covariance equals

FILE ex-empirical-state-frequencies-converge-to-stationary-masses

STATEMENT Assume AC ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]). Let $p$ be an irreducible
positive-recurrent transition matrix on a countable state space $E$ with
invariant probability $\pi$, let $y\in E$, and let $\mathbb P_x$ be the law of
the $p$-chain started at the deterministic state $x\in E$. Then the empirical
frequency of visits to $y$ converges,

$$\frac1n\#\{0\le k<n:X_k=y\}\ \longrightarrow\ \pi(y) \qquad\mathbb P_x\text{-almost surely},$$

and the expectation of that frequency converges to the same number,

$$\mathbb E_x\Bigl[\frac1n\#\{0\le k<n:X_k=y\}\Bigr]\ \longrightarrow\ \pi(y).$$

No aperiodicity is used, and the statements hold for every fixed pair of states
$x,y$; the second is a convergence statement about real numbers, with no
almost-sure qualifier.

LINE 55 [A1] Every family of nonempty sets has a choice function; AC is assumed and is used through the ergodic theorem supplier [F2], the Cesàro supplier [F3] and the chain-law supplier [F4]. ([[def-axiom-of-choice]])

[F1] A recurrent state $z$ is positive recurrent when $\mathbb E_zT_z^+<+\infty$ and null recurrent when $\mathbb E_zT_z^+=+\infty$; positive recurrence of the chain means that every state is positive recurrent. ([[def-positive-recurrent-and-null-recurrent-state]])

[F2] Assume AC. For an irreducible positive-recurrent countable chain with invariant probability $\pi$ and a function $f$ with $\sum_z\pi(z)|f(z)|<\infty$, $\frac1n\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}f(X_k)\to\sum_z\pi(z)f(z)$ almost surely under $\mathbb P_x$, for every starting state $x$. FILE ex-exponential-weibull-hazards-and-censored-likelihood

STATEMENT Compare an exponential lifetime with rate $\lambda=0.2$ and a Weibull lifetime
with shape $k=2$ and scale $\eta=3$. For right-censored exponential data,
assume the three event times are iid $\mathrm{Exp}(0.2)$ and use fixed censoring
times $C_i=5$ with observed records $(U_i,\delta_i)=(2,1),(5,0),(3,1)$. The
resulting subject pairs are iid because the censoring time is deterministic.
The two exact event times are density observations under the continuous model,
not point events of positive probability.
