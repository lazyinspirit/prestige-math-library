For the function operation of [[def-measurable-pointwise-extended-real-product]],
let $X=\{a,b,c\}$, let $f$ have respective values $0,-\infty,2$, and let $g$
have respective values $+\infty,-3,4$. Then $f\odot_X g$ has respective values
$0,+\infty,8$, directly by the zero, nonzero infinite, and finite clauses.

The first coordinate illustrates why the function operation has its own
symbol: the partial scalar product at that pair remains undefined. The other
two coordinates agree with the existing partial product. No limiting process is
involved in any of these evaluations.
