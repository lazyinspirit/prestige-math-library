## Definition

A **complex domain** is a nonempty, connected, open subset $U\subseteq\mathbb C$. Open means open for $d_{\mathbb C}$ of [[def-complex-metric-convergence-and-continuity]] in the metric topology of [[def-metric-topology]] (a topology by [[thm-metric-open-set-algebra]]). Connected has the meaning of [[def-connected-space]] for this topology and its subspace topology on $U$.
