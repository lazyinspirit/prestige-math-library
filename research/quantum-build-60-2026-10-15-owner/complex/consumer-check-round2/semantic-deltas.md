This compares the exact archived original Statement/Definition sections to the sealed first proposal. It is an interface-maintenance proof, not a whole-item audit. A syntactic change is not itself a change of admissible data or mathematical value.

1. `def-complex-domain`. Both predicates on *every* subset U of C are: U is nonempty, open for the modulus metric, and connected for the induced topology. The original explicitly instructs that its topology be read through Phi. The new topology theorem proves ball equality Phi[B(z,r)]=B(Phi(z),r) for every positive r, hence equality of open-set families in both directions. A separation of U transports to a separation of Phi[U] and conversely. Therefore the predicate is extensionally equal for empty, disconnected, unbounded and arbitrary subsets, not just domains already used by consumers. Removing the final unsupported explanatory equivalence from Definition removes no membership condition: the equality is now a proved supplier.

2. `def-wirtinger-derivatives`. For every modulus-open U, every function f:U→C and every point with the four original real partials, the old instruction “f=u+iv in Euclidean coordinates” means exactly f_R=Phi f Phi^{-1}, with components Re(f Phi^{-1}), Im(f Phi^{-1}). The new definition spells out those same functions on the same open coordinate domain. Thus the four partials are identical limits, their existence predicates coincide, and the two displayed half-sum/half-difference formulas are literally unchanged. No total differentiability is newly required to define them. For total differentiation, conjugating a real-linear map by the real-linear bijection Phi gives a real-linear map and |r(h)|/|h|=||Phi r(h)||₂/||Phi h||₂ for every nonzero h. Thus total-differentiability predicates and the entire real differential coincide on every original function, including nonholomorphic maps; the expanded identity has the same coefficients. The antiholomorphic predicate remains real differentiability everywhere and f_z=0 everywhere. This does not identify weak and classical derivatives: weak consumers define half-sums of their weak coordinate partials separately. Their algebraic coefficient operation is unchanged.

3. `def-complex-contours-reversal-concatenation-and-closedness`. For every continuous map gamma:[a,b]→C, all partition sums equal those of Phi gamma term by term. Their extended-real suprema coincide for finite or infinite length; a=b has the same zero convention. Consequently the class of contours and its parametrizations are identical. Closedness and reversal have literally the same endpoint predicate and map formula. The two affine concatenation pieces are the same functions of the same arbitrary endpoint-compatible maps. **One first-proposal defect was found:** that proposal unnecessarily restricted the defining concatenation sentence to factors that are contours. `items/def-complex-contours-reversal-concatenation-and-closedness.md` in this round restores arbitrary C-valued maps with matching endpoints and qualifies the extra continuity/rectifiability claims by the actual contour hypotheses. Thus the corrected proposal preserves the full original operation domain, including nonrectifiable or discontinuous maps. Continuity at the join follows from continuity of both factors and equality of endpoints. Additive length follows by inserting the join in partitions and combining factor partitions; no differentiability is needed. The new explicit piecewise-C1 paragraph is a conservative definition of the previously dictionary-supplied term: both refer to the coordinate path and the same finite subdivision with continuous derivative extensions, allowing corners and zero speeds. Orientation under increasing/decreasing bijections is unchanged. The extra nonstrict-monotone length assertion is a proved extension and imposes no condition on previous reparametrizations.

4. `def-oriented-complex-triangle-and-boundary`. Segment maps, the set of triples and convex-combination parameters, directed boundary order, perimeter, diameter, and abbreviated integral are literally the same formulas. This includes coincident/collinear vertices. For every triple, distance equality shows that the original coordinate diameter and the newly explicit modulus diameter take the supremum of the identical nonempty bounded set of real numbers. Each segment has constant coordinate speed |v-u|, including zero. On an affine rescaled concatenation piece the speed is multiplied by the reciprocal interval width and its integral is unchanged, so the total boundary length is the same sum of edge lengths. Only the supplier licensing those already asserted equalities changes.

5. `prop-triangle-integrals-give-a-primitive-on-a-star-shaped-domain`. For every U⊆C and every a∈U, the old hypothesis means U open and (1-t)a+tz∈U for all z∈U and 0≤t≤1, as explicitly stated in the old star-shaped remark. Bijectivity and the arbitrary-real affine identity give Phi((1-t)a+tz)=(1-t)Phi(a)+tPhi(z). Thus this containment is equivalent, in both directions for every z and t, to star-shapedness of Phi[U] at Phi(a). Openness is equivalent by item 1 above. The chosen centre forces nonemptiness in both versions. The continuity, all-triangles integral condition, explicit segment-integral function F and every conclusion are unchanged. No connectedness, boundary regularity or nondegeneracy is added.

6. `lem-local-holomorphic-logarithm-nonvanishing-function-on-disc`. The original “open disc D(a,r), r>0” is exactly {z:|z-a|<r}, r>0; the first proposition sentence and all h,L hypotheses and conclusions are otherwise literal. This holds for every centre and every positive radius. A disc is nonempty since a lies in it. Its openness follows by epsilon=r-|z-a|. Its segment containment follows from |(1-t)(z-a)+t(w-a)|≤(1-t)|z-a|+t|w-a|<r. The coordinate affine/topology theorem and continuous segments prove its connected-domain and star-shaped predicates without an additional condition on h. Thus the domain and conclusion are unchanged; no normalization, chosen branch or simply-connectedness hypothesis is added.

7. `def-absolute-line-integral-over-a-rectifiable-path`. In the original definition s_gamma is the coordinate arc-length function: its supplier is Euclidean and the original contour dictionary sends gamma to Phi gamma. In the new definition s_gamma is explicitly s_{Phi gamma}. For every rectifiable gamma and each t in [a,b], restriction has identical partition sums in both models, hence the same length and exactly the same real value s_gamma(t). Therefore all tagged Riemann–Stieltjes sums against it are identical for every continuous f on the same trace, not merely their limits. The absolute integral and its domain of definition agree exactly; singleton values remain zero. This is a typing clarification, not a different measure or integral.
