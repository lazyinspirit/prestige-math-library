## Axiom branch and theorem scope

- The existence, exact-solution minimum-norm, and fixed-parameter stability arguments use the ultrafilter lemma, DC, and HB, matching the actual weak-subsequence and weak-norm-lower-semicontinuity suppliers. Hilbert reflexivity invokes countable choice; the proof records DC `=>` countable choice rather than adding full AC. No argument uses full AC.
- Weak-to-weak sequential graph closedness is essential to identify the weak limit of `F(x_n)` with `F(x)`. Weak-to-strong closedness alone would not supply that identification. The fixed-`alpha` result requires strongly convergent data. The vanishing-noise result additionally requires a nonempty exact-solution set, `delta_n -> 0`, `alpha_n -> 0`, and `delta_n^2/alpha_n -> 0`; these statements remain separate.
- The existence theorem gives global minimizers but not uniqueness or algorithmic convergence. The B example exhibits two global minimizers. No unproved uniqueness or strong-convergence claim is included.
