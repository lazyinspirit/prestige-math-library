## Statement

Assuming the standard oriented h-cobordism definitions of
[[def-theta-n-group-of-oriented-h-cobordism-classes-of-homotopy-spheres]] and
[[def-b-p-n-plus-one-subgroup-of-homotopy-spheres]],
$$\Theta_7\cong\mathbb Z/28,\qquad bP_8=\Theta_7 .$$

## Remarks

This is a sourced statement whose proof is **not supplied here**. The complete
proof requires the stable sphere stems, the stable image of $J$, framed
surgery and the Kervaire-Milnor arithmetic; those inputs are absent as proved
local prerequisites, and the inspected portion of the source contains the
statement and table at printed p. 512 rather than the derivation. The claim is
deliberately non-load-bearing: the explicit construction, the homotopy-sphere
recognition and the modulo-seven detector for the examples of this page do not
use it. It is retained to locate the constructed examples inside the complete
high-dimensional classification.
