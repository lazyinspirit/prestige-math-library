## Statement

Every smooth homotopy sphere is stably parallelizable: for a smooth homotopy
$n$-sphere $\Sigma$ there is an $r\ge0$ with
$T\Sigma\oplus\varepsilon^r\cong\varepsilon^{n+r}$.

## Remarks

This statement is **recorded but not supplied here**. The complete proof uses
the stable homotopy groups of $SO$, the stable $J$-homomorphism and the
Bott/Adams obstruction input in the form given by Kervaire-Milnor Theorem 3.1;
none of those inputs has a proved local supplier in this library, and no
abbreviated substitute is offered. The statement is retained as a sourced,
non-load-bearing leaf: no construction, exoticness claim or detector in this
pair depends on it, and in particular it is never used to prove the concrete
Milnor disk-bundle calculations or the group closure results.
