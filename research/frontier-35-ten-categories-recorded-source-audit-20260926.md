# Source audit of nine published recorded remarks (2026-09-26)

Scope: the nine `published-unchecked` / `proved_here: false` subjects reported by strict `tools/depcheck.mjs` before this audit. This is an independent, bounded check of each current recorded statement, its attribution and the passages cited for it. It is not a proof of any recorded external theorem, a review of all consumers, or a certification of the local theorem closures. Exact source URLs remain in each item. Source checks below refer to the final item wording; any later content change requires a fresh check.
