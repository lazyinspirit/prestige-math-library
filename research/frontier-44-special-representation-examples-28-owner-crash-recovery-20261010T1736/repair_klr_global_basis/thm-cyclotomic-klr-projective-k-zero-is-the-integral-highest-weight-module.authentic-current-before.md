## Statement

For the standard graded cyclotomic quotient over any degree-zero field and any
real symmetrizable Cartan datum with admissible graded KLR parameters, let
$K_0(R^\Lambda)$ be the split Grothendieck group of finite graded projective
$R^\Lambda$-modules with its $A=\mathbb Z[q,q^{-1}]$-module structure and
Cartan map of [[def-graded-grothendieck-group-shift-module-and-cartan-map]].
Then the normalized functors of
[[thm-cyclotomic-klr-functors-define-an-integrable-quantum-action-on-k-zero-and-g-zero]]
equip $K_0(R^\Lambda)$ with the structure of the integral highest-weight
$U_A(\mathfrak g)$-module $V_A(\Lambda)$ with highest vector
$1_\Lambda=[k]$, via the unique $A$-linear isomorphism sending the divided
words to the divided powers of the negative half acting on the highest vector.
The identification is compatible with the $\mathbb Z[q,q^{-1}]$-bases of
indecomposable projectives and with the perfect tensor pairing.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The standard cyclotomic quotient $R^\Lambda$ with its graded
Grothendieck groups, the vacuum generator and the normalized actions.

[F1] The vacuum generation: $U_A^-1_\Lambda=K_0(R^\Lambda)$ with
$1_\Lambda$ nonzero, of weight $\Lambda$, killed by all $E_i$, and the classes
of the divided words $F_{i_r}^{(a_r)}\cdots F_{i_1}^{(a_1)}1_\Lambda$ spanning
over $A$
([[lem-the-vacuum-projective-is-a-highest-weight-generator]]).

[F2] The normalized action theorem
([[thm-cyclotomic-klr-functors-define-an-integrable-quantum-action-on-k-zero-and-g-zero]]):
the operators satisfy the full quantum relations and are locally nilpotent;
the tensor pairing is weightwise perfect in normalized projective-cover/simple
bases.

[F3] The generic negative-half categorification
([[thm-klr-categorification-of-the-negative-quantum-half]]) identifies the
generic $K_0$ with $U_A^-$ and matches divided powers with divided-power
projectives; the normalization clauses of
[[lem-cyclotomic-projective-covers-and-dualities-descend-through-the-quotient]]
and [[def-divided-power-klr-idempotents-and-projectives]].

## Proof

**Proof technique:** exhibit the highest-weight universal property using the
vacuum generation, the local nilpotence and the integrability of the two
actions, then match the divided bases and the pairing.

1.1 The vacuum is a highest-weight vector. By [F1], $1_\Lambda$ is nonzero, has weight $\Lambda$ and is killed by every $E_i$; by [F2] the operators satisfy the full quantum relations and are locally nilpotent, so the $A$-submodule $U_A^-1_\Lambda\subseteq K_0(R^\Lambda)$ is a highest-weight module generated by a highest vector of weight $\Lambda$, and [F1] says it is all of $K_0(R^\Lambda)$. [F1, F2]

2.1 The universal map from $V_A(\Lambda)$. The universal property of the integral highest-weight module ([[thm-integrable-quantum-highest-weight-module-exists-and-is-unique]], with the canonical highest vector of the simple quotient of [[def-quantum-verma-module-and-simple-highest-weight-quotient]] and the integral form $V_A(\Lambda)=U_A^-v_\Lambda$ of [[def-integral-residue-lattices-of-the-negative-half-and-highest-weight-modules]]) gives a surjective $A$-linear map $\varphi:V_A(\Lambda)\to K_0(R^\Lambda)$ sending the canonical highest vector to $1_\Lambda$ and the divided powers of the negative half to the divided-word classes; it is surjective by step 1.1. The generic negative-half categorification [F3] identifies the divided words with the divided-power projectives of the generic $K_0$, whose extensions are the cyclotomic divided functors; the divided powers are those of the divided-power integral form ([[def-lusztig-divided-power-integral-form]]), so $\varphi$ is $A$-linear on the divided bases and $\varphi$ is an isomorphism by the triangularity of the two divided bases. [step 1.1, F3]

3.1 Compatibility with the pairing and the bases. The normalized action theorem [F2] gives the weightwise perfect tensor pairing in normalized projective-cover/simple bases, and the image of the canonical pairing of $V_A(\Lambda)$ under the identification of step 2.1 is the same form ([[thm-the-negative-half-bilinear-form-is-symmetric-nondegenerate-and-integral]], [[thm-klr-cartan-pairing-agrees-with-lusztigs-bilinear-form]], [[def-the-klr-cartan-pairing]]) because both are determined by the values on the normalized generators; hence the identification is compatible with the perfect pairing and with the $\mathbb Z[q,q^{-1}]$-bases of indecomposable projectives. The construction uses the declared generic suppliers [F3] and inherits the Axiom of Choice only through the generic negative-half and integral highest-weight contracts, no other step using infinite choice. [step 2.1, F2, F3] ∎
