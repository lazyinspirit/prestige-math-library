## 6. Findings for the owner

**F1 (published-library gap, resolved in the scaffold; ledger action).** The published `def-finite-k-linear-abelian-category` requires "enough projectives: every simple object has a projective cover", but the only published definition of projective cover, `def-essential-epimorphism-and-projective-cover`, is explicitly module-scoped ("of modules"); a library-wide search found no general definition of superfluous subobject / essential epimorphism / projective cover in an abelian category. Consuming planned items: A2, A4, A5, B2 and the published definition itself. The scaffold supplies the general definition as A1 with a definitional agreement claim for module categories, so this is not an unmet scaffold prerequisite and does not block authoring. Recommended owner action: keep the observation in the canonical published-defect ledger (as surfaced by the batch notes) and, at a later owner action, add a cross-reference from the published definition to the general notion; no scaffold edit is made here.

**F2 (minor, statement-level wording; not a missing prerequisite).** `thm-projective-covers-exist-and-are-unique-for-finite-dimensional-algebras` states "Let A be a finite-dimensional algebra" without naming the base field, while its proof uses "a finite k-basis" and dim_k (steps 1.1, 3.1) — i.e. it is the finite-dimensional-over-a-field case. Consumers A3, B1 and B3 assume "finite-dimensional unital algebra over a field k", so the field case is what they need and what the proof delivers; the mismatch is presentational, not mathematical. Recommended owner action: none required for scope; Step 3b should cite the theorem over a field explicitly (or the owner may authorise a later published statement clarification).

**F3 (documented transport; uncertainty for Step 3b, not absence).** A8 and A9 rest on batch-25 lemmas stated for additive functors on *all* modules (`lem-evaluation-on-the-regular-module-has-a-commuting-right-action`, `lem-canonical-eilenberg-watts-comparison-is-balanced-and-natural`, `thm-natural-transformations-of-tensor-functors-are-bimodule-maps`) while their F is defined only on finite-dimensional modules; no separately stated finite-module variant exists in the scaffold or the published library. The design P9 explicitly intends the restriction ("the action and comparison of P2 still exist"), and both consumer strategies state that the computations are redone verbatim/restricted (A8 strategy, (ii)–(iii)). Recorded uncertainty: Step 3b must confirm that only finite-module data are used; if its reviewer wants a stated supplier, the owner may authorise a small finite-variant lemma, but the current scope does not require one and the deps edges are recorded as pattern sources with this caveat.
