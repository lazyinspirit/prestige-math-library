This page constructs the periodic square-lattice toric stabilizer code over
$\mathbb F_2$, proves its check relations, two encoded qubits, e/m string
syndromes, logical algebra, distance and recovery threshold. It also proves
exact local indistinguishability in a specified contractible disk, the exact
finite-volume Hamiltonian gap, and a constant upper bound on a logical-string
energy barrier. These are finite-model results; no positive material-phase,
small-local-perturbation-stability or thermal-lifetime conclusion is made.
