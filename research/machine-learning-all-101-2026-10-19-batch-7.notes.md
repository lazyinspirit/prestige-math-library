- `ex-finite-support-sample-compression` — On the two-point uniform domain with identity target, the first-occurrence compressor selects at most two labeled entries and reconstructs unseen inputs as zero. It is consistent; its risk is $1/2$ exactly on the all-zero sample, otherwise $0$, so at $\epsilon=1/4$ failure probability is $2^{-m}$. For $m\ge3$, this is at most the compression sum because the $i=0$ term is $(3/4)^m\ge2^{-m}$. Dependencies: scheme definition, compression theorem, population/empirical risk and its measurability; item level 4. Source: Floyd–Warmuth §3 and Theorem 5, PDF pp. 6–7 and 11 at `https://mwarmuth.bitbucket.io/pubs/J30.pdf`. Placement: B page. The finite maps and risk event are measurable.
