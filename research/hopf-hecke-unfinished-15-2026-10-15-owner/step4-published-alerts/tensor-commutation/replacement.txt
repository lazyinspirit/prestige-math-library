For positions $a,a+1$, the two $E_i$ summands at those positions have the common outside factors $1^{\otimes(a-1)}$ on the left and $K_i^{\otimes(n-a-1)}$ on the right, with two-site sum $E_i\otimes K_i+1\otimes E_i$; the two $F_i$ summands have the common outside factors $(K_i^{-1})^{\otimes(a-1)}$ on the left and $1^{\otimes(n-a-1)}$ on the right, with two-site sum $F_i\otimes1+K_i^{-1}\otimes F_i$. For an $E_i$ summand indexed by $r<a$, the checked positions carry $K_i\otimes K_i$, while for $r>a+1$ they carry the identity. For an $F_i$ summand indexed by $r<a$, the checked positions carry the identity, while for $r>a+1$ they carry $K_i^{-1}\otimes K_i^{-1}$.
