For finite Coxeter groups, the standard basis makes the Hecke algebra free over its Laurent coefficient ring, so coefficient specializations retain an explicit finite basis. This page defines the regular-trace Gram determinant and proves that its nonvanishing forces semisimplicity. Evaluating at the group-algebra parameter proves the generic determinant is nonzero in characteristic zero. For a general fiber, determinant nonvanishing is a sufficient certificate; its zero set is not asserted to be exactly the nonsemisimple locus.

The deformation argument has a separate formal branch. Over $k\llbracket h\rrbracket$, a finite free algebra with split semisimple reduction admits lifts of its complete orthogonal idempotents; the corner ranks then give exact matrix units and a product of matrix algebras of the same sizes. This yields a simple-label bijection only in that split formal setting. Generic semisimplicity alone does not assert splitness or labels.
