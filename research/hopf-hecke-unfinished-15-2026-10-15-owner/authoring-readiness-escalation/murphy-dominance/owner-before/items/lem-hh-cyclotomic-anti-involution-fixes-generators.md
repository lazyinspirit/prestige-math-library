## Statement

For $r,n\ge 1$, let $H_{r,n}$ be the multiplicative cyclotomic Hecke algebra of [[def-hh-multiplicative-cyclotomic-hecke-algebra]]. There is a unique $R$-linear involutive algebra anti-automorphism $*$ of $H_{r,n}$ fixing every $T_i$ and every $X_j^{\pm1}$. Since $L_j=X_j$ by [[lem-hh-cyclotomic-commuting-jucys-murphy-generators]], it fixes every $L_j$. Its restriction to the finite type-A Hecke subalgebra is the reversal anti-involution.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** Integers $r,n\ge1$, the coefficient ring $R=\mathbb Z[Q^{\pm1},u_1^{\pm1},\ldots,u_r^{\pm1}]$, and the generators and relations in [[def-hh-multiplicative-cyclotomic-hecke-algebra]].

[F1] The algebra is the quotient of the free associative $R$-algebra on $T_i,X_j,X_j^{-1}$ by the displayed quadratic, braid, commuting, affine, inverse, and cyclotomic relations. ([[def-hh-multiplicative-cyclotomic-hecke-algebra]], [[lem-hh-free-associative-ring-and-relations-descent]])

[F2] The involution on the free algebra obtained by reversing each word and fixing its letters descends to a quotient whenever it preserves the two-sided ideal of defining relations. ([[lem-hh-free-associative-ring-and-relations-descent]])

[F3] The recursively defined Murphy generators satisfy $L_j=X_j$ for all $j$. ([[lem-hh-cyclotomic-commuting-jucys-murphy-generators]])

## Proof

**Given:** The free presentation and relations of $H_{r,n}$.

1.1 On the free associative algebra define $*$ by $r^*=r$ for $r\in R$, $T_i^*=T_i$, $X_j^*=X_j$, $(X_j^{-1})^*=X_j^{-1}$, and $(ab)^*=b^*a^*$, extending $R$-linearly. Word reversal is an involutive anti-automorphism, so this defines an involutive anti-automorphism of the free algebra. [given, F1, F2, construct]

1.2 The quadratic relation is a polynomial in one fixed generator and is fixed by reversal. Each braid word in the $T_i$ is palindromic, each distant commutation relation reverses to the same equality with sides exchanged, each relation $X_jX_k=X_kX_j$ and $T_iX_j=X_jT_i$ reverses to its equivalent reversed equality, and $T_iX_iT_i=QX_{i+1}$ is fixed because its left word is palindromic and $Q$ is central. The inverse relations reverse to the other inverse equations, and the cyclotomic relation $p(X_1)=0$ is fixed because it is a polynomial in $X_1$. Thus reversal sends every defining relator into the defining ideal. As the ideal is two-sided, it is preserved under reversal, so $*$ descends to an involutive anti-automorphism of $H_{r,n}$. [F1, step 1.1, algebra]

2.1 The quotient is generated by the displayed letters and $R$, so any anti-homomorphism fixing them is unique. By [F3], $L_j=X_j$, hence $L_j^*=L_j$. On words in the finite generators $T_i$, reversal sends a reduced word for $w$ to the reversed reduced word for $w^{-1}$; therefore $T_w^*=T_{w^{-1}}$, as for the finite reversal anti-involution. [F1, F3, step 1.2] ∎
