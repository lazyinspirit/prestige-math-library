4. Generic Iwahori–Hecke algebras and specializations
We now consider a ﬁnite Coxeter group W with generating set S and a
weight function L: W →N, without reference to a realization of W in the
framework of groups with a BN-pair. Our aim is to develop the represen-
tation theory of the associated Iwahori–Hecke algebras over various ﬁelds
k. One of ﬁrst decisive observations is the fact that these algebras can be
deﬁned “generically” over a polynomial ring, where the parameters are pow-
ers of the indeterminate. The precise deﬁnitions are as follows. (A general
reference is [47].)
Let A = Z[v, v−1] be the ring of Laurent polynomials in an indetermi-
nate v. Then there exists an associative algebra H = HA(W, L) over A,
which is free as an A-module with basis {Tw | w ∈W} such that the multi-
