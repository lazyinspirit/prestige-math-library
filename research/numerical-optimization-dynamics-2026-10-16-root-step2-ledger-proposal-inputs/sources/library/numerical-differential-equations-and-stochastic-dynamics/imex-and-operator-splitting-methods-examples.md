The companion gives a scalar stiff-decay comparison, exact ARS modal factors for a two-cell reaction-diffusion grid, and concrete noncommuting matrices whose Lie and Strang local defects have nonzero leading terms. A two-level finite-matrix Schrödinger split preserves the Euclidean norm in every exact subflow and composition. The Dirichlet modal example proves a two-sided $h^{5/4}$ fixed-time error for its stated sequence-space model.

The examples are finite-dimensional or explicitly diagonal sequence-space calculations. They do not extend scalar absolute stability to nonnormal matrices, or establish a general unbounded-PDE splitting theorem.
