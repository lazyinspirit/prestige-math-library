4. Examples and computation
In this section, we give examples of knots which illustrate the usefulness of the new ob-
structions. The knots have very subtle behavior in the knot concordance group—these are
(n)-solvable, and not distinguished from (n.5)-solvable knots by any PTFA L2-signature
obstructions, but not (n.5)-solvable. This is detected using Theorem 3.2.
In order to construct non-PTFA amenable coeﬃcient systems lying in Strebel’s class to
which we apply Theorem 3.2, we need some reﬁned ingredients: mixed-coeﬃcient commu-
tator series and modulo p noncommutative Blanchﬁeld form. We begin by introducing the
former, and then proceed to the construction and analysis of the examples.
