Theorem 2.13.
Suppose M is a closed, oriented, connected 3-manifold
with β1(M) = 1 and φ : π1(M) −→Γ a nontrivial PTFA coeﬃcient system.
Suppose R is a ring such that ZΓ ⊆R ⊆K. Then there is a symmetric linking
form
Bℓ: H1(M; R) −→H1(M; R)#
deﬁned on the higher-order Alexander module A := H1(M; R). If either R is
a PID, or some element of the augmentation ideal of Zπ1(M) is sent (under φ)
to an invertible element of R, then Bℓis nonsingular.
Proof. Note that A is a torsion R-module by Proposition 2.11, since K is
also the quotient ﬁeld of the Ore domain R. Deﬁne Bℓas the composition of
the Poincar´e duality isomorphism to H2(M; R), the inverse of the Bockstein to
H1(M; K/R), and the usual Kronecker evaluation map to A#. The Bockstein
B : H1(M; K/R) −→H2(M; R)
associated to the short exact sequence
0 −→R −→K −→K/R −→0
is an isomorphism since H2(M; K) ∼= H1(M; K) = 0 by Proposition 2.11,
and H1(M; K) = 0 by Proposition 2.11 and Remark 2.8.3. Under the second
hypothesis on R, the Kronecker evaluation map
H1(M; K/R) −→HomR (H1(M; R), K/R)
is an isomorphism by the UCSS since H0(M; R) = 0 (see Remark 2.8.3 and
Proposition 2.9). If R is a PID then K/R is an injective R-module since it is
clearly divisible [Ste, I Prop. 6.10]. Thus

for i > 0 and therefore the Kronecker map is an isomorphism.
