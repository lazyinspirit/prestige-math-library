## Statement

Let $W(\mathbb Q)$ denote the Witt group of nonsingular symmetric bilinear forms over $\mathbb Q$, with orthogonal sum as addition and metabolic forms set to zero. For every such form $q$, four times its Witt class equals its signature times the class of the four-dimensional identity form. In particular, the kernel of the signature is killed by $4$, including its restriction to the subgroup of even-dimensional classes. The signature image is $\mathbb Z$ on the full group and $2\mathbb Z$ on the even-dimensional subgroup.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A nonsingular rational symmetric bilinear form $q$. A form is metabolic when it has a totally isotropic subspace of half its dimension. Its Witt class is denoted $[q]$.

[F1] Every nonnegative integer is a sum of four integer squares. [[thm-lagrange-four-square-theorem]]

[F2] Symmetric forms over a field of characteristic different from $2$ diagonalize. [[cor-quadratic-forms-diagonalize-in-characteristic-not-two]]

## Proof

1.1 For every positive rational $c=m/n$, with positive integers $m,n$, apply [F1] to $mn$. Dividing the resulting four integer coordinates by $n$ writes $c=x_1^2+x_2^2+x_3^2+x_4^2$ with $x_i\in\mathbb Q$. Define the matrix $$R=\begin{pmatrix}x_1&-x_2&-x_3&-x_4\\x_2&x_1&-x_4&x_3\\x_3&x_4&x_1&-x_2\\x_4&-x_3&x_2&x_1\end{pmatrix}.$$ Its columns have squared length $c$ and distinct columns have dot product zero, so $R^tR=cI_4$. Since $c\ne0$, $R$ is invertible. Thus $\langle c,c,c,c\rangle$ is isometric to $I_4$. Multiplying the equality by any scalar shows that four copies of $\langle a\rangle$ are isometric to four copies of $\langle1\rangle$ if $a>0$, and to four copies of $\langle-1\rangle$ if $a<0$. [F1, construct, algebra]

2.1 By [F2], diagonalize $q=\langle a_1,\ldots,a_n\rangle$. Suppose $p$ entries are positive and $m$ negative. Step 1.1 identifies four orthogonal copies of $q$ with $4p$ positive unit lines and $4m$ negative unit lines. Each pair $\langle1,-1\rangle$ is metabolic, its isotropic line being generated by $(1,1)$. Cancelling these pairs in the defining Witt relations gives $$4[q]=(p-m)[I_4]=\operatorname{sign}(q)[I_4].$$ Therefore a class of signature zero is killed by $4$. Signature is additive and vanishes on metabolic forms: over $\mathbb R$ a totally isotropic subspace has dimension at most the smaller of the positive and negative indices, so half dimension forces those indices equal. Consequently signature descends to the Witt group. Unit lines attain every integer signature, while even-dimensional forms have even signature and sums of copies of $\langle1,1\rangle$ or $\langle-1,-1\rangle$ attain every even integer. This proves all assertions. [F2, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
