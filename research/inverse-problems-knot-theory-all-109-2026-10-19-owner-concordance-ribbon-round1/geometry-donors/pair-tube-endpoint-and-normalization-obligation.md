# Pair-adapted spanning tube: current obligation and source history

Scope: off-canonical research for the TOP branch of `lem-knot-concordance-compatible-with-connected-sum`, especially its current Steps 2.1 and 4.1. The actual current consumer was read. No canonical item, manifest, contract, readiness record, or native state was changed.

## What is now proved

`lem-pair-chart-supported-surface-isotopies-extend-ambiently.md` supplies an explicit compactly supported extension of an intrinsic surface isotopy supported in one local-flatness pair chart. The normal-coordinate cutoff formula has an explicit continuous inverse. It assumes only that one chart, rather than a normal bundle over the annulus.

`lem-interior-annulus-core-segments-have-pair-adapted-product-tubes.md` proves a genuine restricted tube theorem. For the intrinsic annulus core and every 0<a<b<1, one pair chart contains the whole compact core segment alpha([a,b]). Finitely many intrinsic rectangle-supported flows stretch a short segment in an initial chart to that long segment; the first lemma extends each flow ambiently, preserving the annulus. The transported initial chart then yields an explicit (D³ × [a,b], D¹ × [a,b]) neighborhood. This proof avoids all 4D normal-bundle and annulus theorems. The parent independently read both proofs and reported no defect.

## Exact remaining interfaces

The restricted theorem does not give a single proper tube T:D³×[0,1]→S³×[0,1] whose intersection with the annulus is D¹×[0,1] and whose ends agree with the two prescribed ambient/annulus product germs. Taking a sequence a→0, b→1 does not produce such a tube: the different charts have no constructed compatibility or controlled limit. In particular, the compact interior chart cannot be extended to touch both boundary levels merely by an ambient isotopy fixed near those levels.

The cut-and-glue consumer also requires a normalization interface: an appropriate ambient homeomorphism, respecting the end collars, must carry the chosen whole tube onto a standard product ball B³×[0,1], or otherwise give an explicit standard product identification of its complement. Straightening only the core does not prove that assertion. A rank-three neighborhood of a straight arc need not be the particular levelwise tube used in the gluing formula simply because its core is vertical. The smooth branch can use smooth tubular-neighborhood uniqueness; the TOP branch still owes its relevant relative neighborhood argument.

Thus no TOP consumer closure is claimed. The smallest missing geometric assertion for this method is a relative proper pair-neighborhood extension and normalization along the spanning arc, with the two supplied endpoint product germs. It is an established authoring obligation; this note does not classify the consumer as an open problem or assert that this special case requires the entire general normal-bundle theorem.

## Authentic sources and a corrected historical claim

The actual complete Kirby source *Codimension-two locally flat imbeddings have normal bundles* was retrieved from https://math.berkeley.edu/~kirby/papers/Kirby%20-%20Codimension-two%20locally%20flat%20imbeddings%20have%20normal%20bundles%20-%20MR0273625.pdf to `/tmp/kirby-codim2-normal.pdf`. It is four scan pages, each with two printed pages, covering pp.416–423. I visually inspected all eight printed pages. Its displayed theorem has no dimension-four exclusion. Its main deformation proof uses a radial rescaling at printed p.419, Step 2.

The actual complete corrected Kirby–Siebenmann source *Normal bundles for codimension 2 locally flat imbeddings* was retrieved from https://math.berkeley.edu/~kirby/papers/Kirby%20and%20Siebenmann%20-%20Normal%20bundles%20for%20codimension%202%20locally%20flat%20imbeddings%20-%20MR0400237.pdf to `/tmp/pair-ks.pdf`. It has fifteen scan pages. I visually inspected PDF pp.1–3. Theorem A explicitly excludes base dimension m=2. PDF p.2 identifies an error, found by Bjorn Friberg, in the earlier proof at printed p.419, Step 2; it says that the earlier all-dimensional proof remains incomplete. The corrected paper uses handlebody, torus, and related machinery. The earlier theorem therefore cannot be used as an elementary proof of the desired 4D normal neighborhood.

Edwards–Kirby, *Deformations of Spaces of Imbeddings*, was retrieved in full from https://www.maths.gla.ac.uk/~mpowell/Edwards%20Kirby.pdf to `/tmp/pair-ek.pdf` (27 PDF pages, including its cover; printed pp.63–88). I read the actual text of the Introduction, pp.63–64, and Section 7, pp.81–83. The relative pair deformation and isotopy-extension statements themselves are not written with a dimension-four exclusion there. It would therefore be inaccurate to attribute the precise failure of Kirby's earlier normal-bundle proof merely to an absent 4D relative local-contractibility statement. This note does not supply the full Edwards–Kirby proof as a local dependency; the two new elementary modules above have direct proofs and do not invoke it.

## Livingston source versions must be distinguished

The actual published Livingston article, *Connected sums of codimension two locally flat submanifolds*, DOI https://doi.org/10.1017/prm.2022.87, was read in its full HTML at the Introduction, Section 3 including Lemmas 3.3–3.5 and their proofs, and Section 4. Its related pair-chart normalization in Lemma 3.4 invokes existence and uniqueness of normal bundles; it refers to FQ Section 9.3 in dimension four. This documents a valid general route, but does not author that missing local supplier here.

I also retrieved the complete arXiv v1, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.03930, to `/tmp/livingston-connected-sums-pairs.pdf` (ten pages, dated 8 April 2021). I read its relevant Section 4, Section 5, and surrounding introductory discussion. Its Section 5 labels a relative annulus assertion Theorem 8 and provides only an outline. The published article removes that asserted theorem and instead records a general Relative Annulus Conjecture as Problem 4.2. The old arXiv theorem label is not evidence of a completed supplier. No claim is made that the particular spanning-tube problem here is equivalent to that general conjecture.

## Disposition

The two proved interior modules are usable as restricted intermediates after ordinary owner integration. Full proper endpoint matching and whole-tube complement normalization remain unproved locally. No silent use of a full surface normal bundle, relative pair-annulus theorem, or neighborhood uniqueness is justified by the core arc-unknotting theorem alone.
