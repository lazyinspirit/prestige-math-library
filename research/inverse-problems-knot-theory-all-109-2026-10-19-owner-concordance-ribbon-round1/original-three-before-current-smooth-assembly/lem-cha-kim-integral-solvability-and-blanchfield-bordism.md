## Statement

Let P be a winding-zero pattern satisfying the nonzero Blanchfield self-pairing hypothesis of Cha–Kim, and let K be an arbitrary knot. The iterated infection cobordism used in the proof of Cha–Kim Theorems A and B is an integral (n+1/2)-solution for the coefficient system specified there; its higher-order Blanchfield pairing is nonsingular and its metabolizer is represented by the infection curves.

## Statement references

[Cha–Kim, §§4.1–4.3 and Appendix A](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.04629v2)

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The winding-zero pattern and its axis satisfy the exact hypotheses in Cha–Kim Theorems A and B; the infection cobordism and PTFA derived-series coefficient system are those constructed in their proof.

[F1] Integral (n+1/2)-solutions require lifted isotropic classes and dual classes in the equivariant intersection form ([[def-integral-half-solutions-strebel-and-l2-signatures]]).

[F2] The higher-order Blanchfield form is induced by Bockstein and Poincaré duality ([[def-blanchfield-pairing-and-higher-order-rho-invariant]]).

[F3] The relevant Blanchfield form is nonsingular when the coefficient homology vanishes ([[lem-higher-order-blanchfield-pairings-are-nonsingular]]).

## Proof

1.1 The infection cobordism is built by attaching the exterior of each infection knot along the zero-framed infection curve. The Mayer–Vietoris sequence identifies its ordinary second homology with the direct sum generated by the infection surfaces and their duals. [given, F1, algebra]

1.2 The nonzero self-pairing of the pattern axis makes the chosen infection curves nontrivial in the localized Alexander module. The Cha–Kim construction uses this to define the required PTFA coefficient maps and the Lagrangian metabolizer. [F2, F3, given]

2.1 To conclude integral (n+1/2)-solvability, one must verify that every dual class pairs as δ_ij, all Lagrangian self-pairings vanish, and each Lagrangian class lifts one derived stage further. The source’s handle and Blanchfield computations supply these checks, but they are not reproduced in steps 1.1–1.2. [F1, F2, F3, step 1.1, step 1.2]
