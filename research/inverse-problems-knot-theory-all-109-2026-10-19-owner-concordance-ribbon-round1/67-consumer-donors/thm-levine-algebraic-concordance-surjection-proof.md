## Statement

Assume full AC. Let $\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sm}}$ and $\mathcal C_{\mathrm{top}}$ denote, respectively, the smooth and locally flat topological concordance groups of smooth oriented knots in the standard oriented $3$-sphere, with connected sum as operation. Let $G_{\mathbb Z}$ be the integral algebraic concordance group generated by integral Seifert lattices modulo direct-sum and metabolic relations. In either category $\mathcal C$, the rule $[K]\mapsto[(H_1(F;\mathbb Z),V_F)]$, where $F$ is any connected oriented Seifert surface of $K$ and $V_F$ is its positive-push Seifert pairing, defines a surjective group homomorphism $\Phi_{\mathcal C}:\mathcal C\to G_{\mathbb Z}$.

## Proof

1.1 Under the full Axiom of Choice [A1], the knot-pairing convention in [F1] defines a candidate value for each knot with a chosen Seifert surface. Once the assignment is well-defined, it is onto: an arbitrary generator of $G_{\mathbb Z}$ is represented by an integral Seifert lattice, and [F3] realizes that lattice as the Seifert form of a knot. Thus every generator lies in the image, and the image of a homomorphism containing the generators is all of $G_{\mathbb Z}$. [A1, F1, F2, F3, given, algebra]

1.2 For a fixed knot, replacing its chosen Seifert surface preserves the algebraic class: [F6] gives a finite S-equivalence sequence, and [F4] preserves the class at every basis change, enlargement and reduction. The assignment to individual knot types is therefore independent of Seifert surface and basis. This conclusion uses neither the unresolved slice-to-metabolizer supplier nor the topological concordance group. [F4, F6, given, construct]

1.3 For knots represented in disjoint balls, boundary-connect-sum their Seifert surfaces along a band disjoint from the basis curves. The union of the old bases is a basis for the boundary-connected-sum surface; curves from the two separated summands have zero mutual linking, so its matrix is $V_K\oplus V_J$. The direct-sum relation in [F2] therefore gives $\Phi(K\#J)=\Phi(K)+\Phi(J)$ whenever the assignment is well-defined. [F1, F2, F5, given, construct]

2.1 To descend from knots to concordance classes, one must show a concordance difference is sent to zero. The smooth branch uses the slice-to-metabolizer conclusion [L3] and the smooth-group part of [L1]. The topological branch also needs a topological group operation; [L1] leaves connected-sum compatibility unresolved, and arc image straightening alone does not supply the pair-adapted proper tube or standard complement required by that construction. The arbitrary-surface S-equivalence required for both branches is now proved in [F6]. These obligations remain unresolved in this run, so this item does not yet prove the full two-category homomorphism. [F6, L1, L3, step 1.1, step 1.2, step 1.3, construct] ∎
