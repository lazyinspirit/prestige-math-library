## Open problem

Let P be a winding-zero pattern whose image consists of topologically slice knots, and let C_sm be the smooth knot concordance group. For n≥0, let Pⁿ be the n-fold composition of the induced satellite map, with P⁰ the identity, and let ⟨S⟩ denote the subgroup generated by S. Must every quotient ⟨Pⁿ(C_sm)⟩/⟨Pⁿ⁺¹(C_sm)⟩ have infinite rank, where rank of an abelian group A means dim_Q(A⊗_Z Q)? This is the smooth graded-quotient question recorded by Cha and Kim; the question itself supplies no premise for a proof.
