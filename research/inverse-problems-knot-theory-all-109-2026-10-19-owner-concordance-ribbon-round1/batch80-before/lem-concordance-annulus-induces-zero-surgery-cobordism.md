## Statement

Let A be a proper annulus in a compact oriented 4-manifold W, with boundary knots K₀ and K₁ in the two boundary 3-spheres. Assume A has an oriented normal disk neighborhood A×D² whose endpoint framings are the preferred zero-linking longitudes. Let R be Z or R_n=Z[1/|n|], and assume each end S³→W is an R-homology isomorphism. Removing A×D² and attaching D²×S¹×I along the annular side, with ∂D² matched to the annulus longitude and the core matched to the normal meridian, produces a cobordism V from M(K₀) to M(K₁). The statement predicts that the boundary inclusions M(K_i)→V are R-homology isomorphisms. If W is simply connected, it also predicts that π₁(V) is normally generated by either endpoint knot-group image.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** W, the proper annulus A, its relative normal disk neighborhood with endpoint zero framings, and the R-homology conditions on W.

[F1] The endpoint zero-framed surgeries fill the preferred longitudes ([[def-zero-surgery-on-a-knot]]).

[F2] The annulus is proper and has product collars at the boundary ([[def-locally-flat-proper-surface-and-concordance-collars]]).

[F3] The coefficient and ambient categories are the ones specified for C_sm, C_top, C_ex and C^{1/n} ([[def-cdr-satellite-concordance-categories]]).

## Proof

1.1 Remove the interior of A×D² from W. Its side boundary is A×S¹. Attaching D²×S¹×I with the stated slope identifications fills each endpoint exterior along its preferred longitude, so the new boundary components are M(K₀) and −M(K₁), by [F1]. [given, F1, F2]

1.2 The construction is functorial in the product collars and preserves the meridian classes. If π₁(W)=1, van Kampen applied to W=(W\int(A×D²))∪(A×D²) shows that the annulus-normal meridian normally generates π₁ of the exterior; this meridian lies in each zero-surgery boundary group. [F2, F3, step 1.1, algebra]

2.1 The required R-homology conclusion should follow by comparing the Mayer–Vietoris sequences of W=E∪(A×D²) and V=E∪(D²×S¹×I), with the endpoint decompositions of S³ and M(K_i). The annulus framing identifies the torus maps, but the degreewise chain maps and the five-lemma comparison are not supplied in this draft. [F1, F3, step 1.1]
