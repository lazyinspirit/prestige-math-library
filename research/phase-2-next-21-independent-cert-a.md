I independently confirmed a separate **published** defect in `thm-fundamental-theorem-on-flows`: proof 2.1 defines $t_0=\inf\{t\in\mathbb R:(t,p_0)\notin W\}$ with $W\subseteq\mathcal D$ and $\mathcal D_{p_0}$ an interval. If the maximal interval has a finite lower endpoint, the set contains all sufficiently negative times, so the infimum is $-\infty$, contrary to the next claim $t_0\in\mathcal D_{p_0}$. Replacing a negative bad time by its positive opposite is also unjustified. This matches the relative closedness construction in Merry's author-hosted [Theorem 9.10 proof, pp. 69–70](https://www2.math.ethz.ch/will-merry/files/Merry%20-%20Differential%20Geometry%20(2021).pdf).
