## Scope and mathematical conventions

All varieties are separated finite-type complex algebraic varieties; the KLR applications are quasiprojective. Sheaves use their ordinary analytic topology. Coefficients are finite-dimensional stalks over an arbitrary field k of characteristic zero, with AC explicitly assumed for injective replacements. This is constructible topological sheaf theory, not coherent or quasi-coherent algebraic sheaf theory. Groups are products of GL_n(C), allowing zero-dimensional factors. The quiver remains finite loopless with arbitrary arrow multiplicities. None of these contracts assumes finite orbit type, Dynkin type, algebraically closed k, or finite total equivariant Ext.

Cochains satisfy H^i(K[r])=H^{i+r}(K). Ext^n(A,B)=Hom(A,B[n]). A smooth complex d-dimensional X has normalized equivariant dualizing complex k_X[2d]. The normalized equivariant duality restricts to ordinary Verdier duality on X; it is not the unnormalized quotient-stack dualizing complex. For finite Borel approximations X_N→B_N the normalized dualizing complex is ω_XN[-2dim_C B_N]. This distinction prevents an erroneous group-dimension shift.
