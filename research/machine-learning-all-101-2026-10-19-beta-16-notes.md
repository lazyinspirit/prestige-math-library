Full-text PDFs were retrieved from the authors’ site and inspected with printed locators:

- Chapter 2, printed pp. 7–30: §§2.1–2.8. Supports the GP finite-regression convention, standard independent scalar Gaussian-noise formulas, posterior mean/covariance, multiple test inputs, marginal-likelihood expression, fitted-vector formula, explicit mean adjustment, and dense Cholesky arithmetic costs. Its finite formulas use zero mean or independent homoscedastic noise in the relevant displays; the locally proved/published supplier contract carries the broader PSD-noise and nonzero-mean hypotheses. The full relevant section was inspected at the author-hosted `RW3.pdf`: classification places a non-Gaussian logistic/probit likelihood on a GP latent function; the likelihood makes the posterior integration analytically intractable, and Laplace inference explicitly approximates the non-Gaussian posterior by a Gaussian.
