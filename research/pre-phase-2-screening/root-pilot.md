## U-P candidate

`lem-morse-smale-transversality-is-equivalent-to-surjectivity-of-the-linearized-flow-operator`
was published at baseline `52bba95d9`. Its Proof 2.1 says:

> The hyperbolic Hessians at $p$ and $q$ give exponential dichotomies at the two
> ends. The standard first-order Fredholm theorem therefore gives index
> $\lambda(p)-\lambda(q)$.

That step also invokes an adjoint solvability/range characterization. Its [F2]
supplies only the point-marked stable/unstable intersection. The declared
trajectory-manifold proposition assumes transversality; its intersection
dimension calculation does not establish Fredholmness of the decaying
first-order operator or the general surjectivity equivalence being proved.

The cited author source makes the missing analytic input identifiable:
Abbondandolo–Majer, *Lectures on the Morse Complex*, Lemma 2.21, printed
pp.80–81, invokes Proposition 1.8, pp.43–44, and equation (9). Proposition 1.8
supplies the index and kernel/cokernel description for the asymptotically
hyperbolic operator from $C^1_0$ to $C^0_0$. Those source pages were retrieved
and read; [source excerpts and PDF hash](morse-source-excerpts.md) record the
limited reading scope. No claim of full-paper reading is made.

**Supplier disposition:** no adequate local supplier for that exact analytic
result was identified. The published Phase-2
`def-fredholm-operator-cokernel-and-index` supplies vocabulary, not the theorem.
Searching the item corpus for first-order Fredholm and asymptotic-hyperbolic
operator/dichotomy terminology found no other matching proof supplier; this
does not rule out an equivalent result under different terminology.

**Later repair strategy:** supply the finite-dimensional asymptotically
hyperbolic operator theorem and its range characterization, then justify its
transport to the trajectory bundle and stable/unstable tangent spaces. This
scan does not author that prerequisite, repair the consumer, assert a false
