## Local proof repairs

- `thm-classical-erdos-hajnal-bound`: Proof 1.1 wrongly inferred $x<1/2$ from $n\ge2$, although $x=2^{-\sqrt{\log_2 n/(2C_H)}}$ can exceed $1/2$ for large $C_H$ and small $n$. Proof 6.1 invoked $\operatorname{hom}(G)\ge1$ to absorb a lower bound strictly greater than one. I restricted the Fox–Sudakov application to $n\ge N_0$ with $\log_2 n>2C_H$ and used the adjacent/nonadjacent two-vertex witness to give $\operatorname{hom}(G)\ge2$ on the remaining finite range. The Statement is unchanged. The ledger's U-P reliance on recorded `rem-fox-sudakov-quantitative-density-theorem` remains; the mapped proof supplier is `cor-fox-sudakov-quantitative-induced-density-bound`.
- `prop-free-abelian-groups-of-rank-at-least-two-are-not-hyperbolic`: The proof silently wrote $A\cong\mathbb Z^n$ with finite $n$, but the Statement includes infinite-rank free abelian groups. I added the infinite-rank case: each finite generating set uses finitely many basis coordinates, so the group is not finitely generated and is excluded by `def-hyperbolic-group`. The finite-rank square argument remains. The Statement is unchanged; the ledger U-P category/recorded-result reconciliation remains.
- `cor-operator-norm-submultiplicative-and-t-star-t-identity`: The proof took a maximum over the empty unit sphere and used a nonexistent largest singular value when the domain is zero dimensional. I applied the already declared zero-domain operator-norm convention first, then kept the existing nonzero-dimensional argument. The Statement is unchanged.

All three changed items passed targeted `precheck` together, 3 checked and 0 failing. Their old `verification` fields were removed because they predated the proof edits. I inspected their diffs; no other content file was edited by this shard.
