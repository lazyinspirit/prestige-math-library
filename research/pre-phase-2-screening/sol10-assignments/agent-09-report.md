2. **`lem-cobounded-proper-geodesic-actions-produce-finite-generating-sets` — proof-only repair.** Original proof 1.2 chose the least natural `m` with path length `ℓ≤m`. Stale audit/precheck fields were removed; targeted precheck passed.
