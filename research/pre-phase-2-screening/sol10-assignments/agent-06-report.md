which are respectively `H_n(C) ⊗ G` and `Tor_1(H_{n-1}(C),G)` by the free boundary presentation. This fills proof 2.1. The declared supplier `thm-universal-coefficient-theorem-for-homology-over-a-pid` is itself unconditional while consuming the AC-conditional boundary-freeness lemma; that separate supplier repair is outside this shard and has been reported to root.
