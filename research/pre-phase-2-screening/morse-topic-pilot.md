The consumer's hypothesis does not establish that premise. No alternative nonflat-stratum argument or adequate supplier appears in the current consumer. This is a potential missing direct prerequisite for the consumer's own proof. A later repair should supply a complete optimal finite-regularity Sard argument and preserve the published finite-$C^r$ statement.
