## Statement

The scaffold assigns the following assertion: for every generalized Cartan matrix and every choice of nonzero node parameters $q_i$, the Drinfeld–Jimbo coproduct formulas with toral action $K_iE_jK_i^{-1}=q_i^{a_{ij}}E_j$ descend to the quotient by all the Drinfeld–Jimbo relations. The calculation below proves the corresponding failure for the positive toral-action Borel quotient. That Borel counterexample does not, by itself, prove failure after imposing the mixed $E_iF_j$ relations in the full quotient; this remaining part of the assigned assertion is recorded under **Open obligation**.

For the positive Borel test, let $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\-2&2\end{pmatrix}$, set $q_1=q_2=q$ with $q$ indeterminate, and impose

$$K_1E_1K_1^{-1}=q^2E_1,\quad K_1E_2K_1^{-1}=q^{-1}E_2,\quad K_2E_1K_2^{-1}=q^{-2}E_1,\quad K_2E_2K_2^{-1}=q^2E_2.$$

Write $[2]_q=q+q^{-1}$ and $S=E_1^2E_2-[2]_qE_1E_2E_1+E_2E_1^2$. In the quotient of this positive toral-action Borel by the ideal generated by $S$, the coproduct assignments $\Delta(E_i)=E_i\otimes K_i^{-1}+1\otimes E_i$ and $\Delta(K_i)=K_i\otimes K_i$ do not preserve the Serre ideal.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The positive toral-action Borel and its normalized coproduct convention are set out in [[lem-coproduct-preserves-the-positive-and-negative-quantum-serre-ideals]]. The symmetrizable B₂ matrix has symmetrizer $\operatorname{diag}(2,1)$, so the test assignment $q_1=q_2=q$ is not its standard choice $q_1=q^2,q_2=q$ ([[def-symmetrizable-cartan-datum-for-a-quantum-group]]). The Drinfeld–Jimbo definition fixes the mixed relations in the full quotient ([[def-drinfeld-jimbo-quantized-enveloping-algebra]]).

[F1] The symmetric Gaussian convention gives $[2]_q=q+q^{-1}$ ([[def-quantum-integers-factorials-and-divided-powers-at-q-i]]).

[F2] The normalized Borel coproduct convention in [[lem-coproduct-preserves-the-positive-and-negative-quantum-serre-ideals]] is $\Delta(E_i)=E_i\otimes K_i^{-1}+1\otimes E_i$ and $\Delta(K_h)=K_h\otimes K_h$.

## Proof

**Proof technique:** Realize the toral-action Borel by a crossed word algebra, then project the coproduct expansion to bidegree $(2,1)$.

1.1 Let $W=\mathbb Q(q)\langle E_1,E_2\rangle$ and let $H=\mathbb Z^2$ act on $W$ by algebra automorphisms with $K_1(E_1,E_2)=(q^2E_1,q^{-1}E_2)$ and $K_2(E_1,E_2)=(q^{-2}E_1,q^2E_2)$. The crossed algebra has vector space basis $wK_1^aK_2^b$ for words $w$ and $(a,b)\in H$, with multiplication $$(wK_h)(vK_{h'})=w\,\chi_h(v)K_{h+h'},$$ where $\chi_h$ is the specified action. The action law gives associativity. The generators define a homomorphism from the toral-action presentation to this crossed algebra; conversely, $wK_h\mapsto w(E_1,E_2)K_h$ respects the crossed multiplication by the toral-action relations. These homomorphisms are inverse on generators, so this is the presented Borel before imposing $S$. [given, construct]

2.1 Word length in $E_1,E_2$ grades this crossed algebra because each $K_h$ acts by a scalar on every word. The element $S$ is homogeneous of word length $3$, so every element of its two-sided ideal has word length at least $3$. Consequently the quotient retains the distinct length-two words $E_1E_2$ and $E_2E_1$ as linearly independent basis vectors. [step 1.1, algebra]

2.2 The coproduct assignments preserve the toral-action relations: conjugating either tensor summand of $\Delta(E_j)$ by $K_h\otimes K_h$ multiplies it by the same scalar $q^{\langle\alpha_j,h\rangle}$, and the group-like toral relations are preserved. Thus they define an algebra map on the positive toral-action Borel before the Serre quotient. [F2, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Expanding $\Delta(S)$ and moving toral factors to the right gives the following two components with two positive letters on the left and one on the right: $$q^{-2}(1-q)(1+q^2)E_1E_2\otimes E_1K_1^{-1}K_2^{-1}-q^{-1}(1-q)(1+q^2)E_2E_1\otimes E_1K_1^{-1}K_2^{-1}.$$ For example, the coefficient of the first word is $1+q^{-2}-[2]_q=q^{-2}(1-q)(1+q^2)$: the $E_1^2E_2$ summand contributes $1+q^{-2}$, the middle Serre summand contributes $-[2]_q$, and the last summand contributes zero to that left word. The second coefficient is $1+q^2-[2]_q=-q^{-1}(1-q)(1+q^2)$ by the corresponding two contributing summands. [F1, F2, step 2.2, algebra]

4.1 Both coefficients are nonzero in $\mathbb Q(q)$, and the two left words are linearly independent by step 2.1. Every element of $J^+\otimes B^++B^+\otimes J^+$ has left or right word length at least $3$ in each homogeneous summand, whereas the displayed component has bidegree $(2,1)$. Hence this component is nonzero modulo that subspace, so the positive Serre ideal in this unsymmetrized toral-action Borel is not a coideal. [step 2.1, step 3.1, algebra] ∎

## Open obligation

The scaffold's stronger target concerns the quotient by the full Drinfeld–Jimbo ideal, including the mixed $E_iF_j$ relations. The crossed word algebra above proves nonvanishing only before those mixed relations are imposed. To establish the original full-quotient counterexample, one must prove that the displayed bidegree component remains nonzero in the full quotient tensor square (or provide a representation of that quotient detecting it). The later triangular-decomposition item cannot be used as a supplier here. This full-quotient claim remains unresolved and requires owner review.
