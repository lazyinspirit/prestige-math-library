## Statement

Let $(I,A,D,P,P^{\vee},q)$ be a finite symmetrizable Cartan datum for a quantum group, let $U_q(\mathfrak g)$ be the Drinfeld–Jimbo algebra of [[def-drinfeld-jimbo-quantized-enveloping-algebra]], and let $U_q^-,U_q^0,U_q^+$ be the subalgebras generated by the $F_i$, the $K_h$ and the $E_i$ ([[def-positive-negative-and-toral-quantum-subalgebras]]). Let $T(W)$ be the free algebra on the symbols $E_i,F_i,K_h$ and let $\widetilde U:=T(W)/\widetilde I$, where $\widetilde I$ is generated by the toral relations $K_0-1$, $K_hK_{h'}-K_{h+h'}$, by $K_hE_i-q^{\langle\alpha_i,h\rangle}E_iK_h$, $K_hF_i-q^{-\langle\alpha_i,h\rangle}F_iK_h$ and by $E_iF_j-F_jE_i-\delta_{ij}(K_i-K_i^{-1})/(q_i-q_i^{-1})$ -- that is, by the defining relations of $U_q(\mathfrak g)$ *without* the two quantum Serre families. Write $\widetilde U^{\pm},\widetilde U^{0}$ for the subalgebras of $\widetilde U$ generated by the $E_i$, the $F_i$ and the $K_h$, and let $I^{\pm}$ be the two-sided ideal of the free algebra $\widetilde U^{\pm}$ generated by the family $\mathrm{Serre}^{\pm}_{ij}$ ($i\ne j$). Let $m:U_q^-\otimes U_q^0\otimes U_q^+\to U_q(\mathfrak g)$ be the multiplication map $x_-\otimes x_0\otimes x_+\mapsto x_-x_0x_+$.

Assume the following two inputs, neither of which is proved in this item:

**(A1) Serre-free decomposition.** $\widetilde U^{0}\cong\mathbb Q(q)[P^{\vee}]$ is the group algebra of $P^{\vee}$, $\widetilde U^{\pm}$ are the free $\mathbb Q(q)$-algebras on the $E_i$ and the $F_i$, and the multiplication map $\widetilde U^-\otimes\widetilde U^{0}\otimes\widetilde U^+\to\widetilde U$ is an isomorphism of $\mathbb Q(q)$-vector spaces. In particular it is injective.

**(A2) Serre ideal identity.** Let $\langle\mathrm{Serre}\rangle\subseteq\widetilde U$ be the two-sided ideal generated by both Serre families. Then, under the identification (A1),
$$\langle\mathrm{Serre}\rangle= I^-\widetilde U^{0}\widetilde U^+ +\widetilde U^- \widetilde U^{0}I^+ .$$

Then:

(i) $m:U_q^-\otimes U_q^0\otimes U_q^+\to U_q(\mathfrak g)$ is an isomorphism of $\mathbb Q(q)$-vector spaces.

(ii) $U_q^+\cong\widetilde U^+/I^+$ and $U_q^-\cong\widetilde U^-/I^-$, so $U_q^\pm$ is the quotient of the free algebra on the $E_i$ (respectively $F_i$) by the separate Serre relations; and $U_q^0\cong\mathbb Q(q)[P^{\vee}]$ is the group algebra of $P^{\vee}$. In particular the $K_h$ are linearly independent in $U_q(\mathfrak g)$ and $K_h\ne1$ for $h\ne0$.

(iii) For every $\beta\in Q$, $m$ restricts to an isomorphism $\bigoplus_{\beta_++\beta_-=\beta}U_q^-[-\beta_-]\otimes U_q^0\otimes U_q^+[\beta_+]\to U_q(\mathfrak g)[\beta]$, and the rank of $U_q^+[\alpha]$ equals the classical PBW rank $\dim_{\mathbb C}U\mathfrak n^+[\alpha]$ for every $\alpha\in Q_+$ (the same for the negative half). Consequently $U_q(\mathfrak g)[0]=U_q^0$, and for $\beta\ne0$ the component $U_q(\mathfrak g)[\beta]$ is a free $U_q^0$-module of rank $\sum_{\beta_++\beta_-=\beta}\dim U\mathfrak n^+[\beta_+]\cdot\dim U\mathfrak n^-[-\beta_-]$.

**Open obligation.** (A1) and (A2) are not established in this item. The exact missing suppliers, in dependency order, are: the triangular decomposition of the Serre-free presentation $\widetilde U$ (its proof is a rewriting/diamond argument: the reduction rules $K_hE_i\to q^{\langle\alpha_i,h\rangle}E_iK_h$, $K_hF_i\to q^{-\langle\alpha_i,h\rangle}F_iK_h$, $E_iF_j\to F_jE_i+\delta_{ij}(K_i-K_i^{-1})/(q_i-q_i^{-1})$ have only the overlaps $KKE$, $KKF$ and $KEF$, which resolve by the q-power identity $q^{\langle\alpha_i,h\rangle}q^{-\langle\alpha_j,h\rangle}=q^{\langle\alpha_i-\alpha_j,h\rangle}$), and the annihilation statement $[F_k,\mathrm{Serre}^+_{ij}]=[E_k,\mathrm{Serre}^-_{ij}]=0$ in $\widetilde U$ (a finite q-binomial computation; it implies (A2) by absorbing the $E$- and $F$-parts of an ambient word into the ideal factor). The source prints exactly this reduction: Berkeley, Lemma 13.1.3.21 and the proof of Theorem 13.1.3.22, using the Serre-free presentation of Definition 13.1.3.12. No choice principle is used.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A finite symmetrizable Cartan datum, the Drinfeld–Jimbo algebra, and the Serre-free presentation $\widetilde U$.

[A1] $\widetilde U^{0}\cong\mathbb Q(q)[P^{\vee}]$, $\widetilde U^{\pm}$ are free on the $E_i$ and $F_i$, and $\widetilde U^-\otimes\widetilde U^{0}\otimes\widetilde U^+\to\widetilde U$ is an isomorphism; see the open obligation.

[A2] $\langle\mathrm{Serre}\rangle=I^-\widetilde U^{0}\widetilde U^++\widetilde U^-\widetilde U^{0}I^+$ under the identification [A1]; see the open obligation.

[F1] $U_q(\mathfrak g)=T(W)/I_{\mathrm{DJ}}$ with $I_{\mathrm{DJ}}=\widetilde I+\langle\mathrm{Serre}\rangle$, so $U_q(\mathfrak g)=\widetilde U/\langle\mathrm{Serre}\rangle$, and the subalgebras $U_q^\pm,U_q^0$ are the images of $\widetilde U^{\pm},\widetilde U^{0}$ ([[def-drinfeld-jimbo-quantized-enveloping-algebra]], [[def-positive-negative-and-toral-quantum-subalgebras]]).

[F2] $\widetilde U^{\pm}$ and $\widetilde U^{0}$ are the subalgebras generated by the $E_i$, $F_i$ and $K_h$; the subalgebra generated by the $E_i$ inside a quotient of $\widetilde U$ is the image of $\widetilde U^+$, and likewise for the $F_i$ and the $K_h$ ([[def-positive-negative-and-toral-quantum-subalgebras]]).

[F3] For every $\alpha\in Q_+$, $\dim_{\mathbb C(q')}U_{q'}\mathfrak n^+[\alpha]=\dim_{\mathbb C}U\mathfrak n^+[\alpha]$, and the same for the negative half; here $U_{q'}\mathfrak n^\pm$ is the $\mathbb C(q')$-algebra presented by the generators $e_i'$ (respectively $f_i'$) and the separate quantum Serre relations, with $q_i'=q'^{d_i}$ ([[thm-generic-quantum-serre-halves-have-classical-pbw-ranks-and-a-nondegenerate-hopf-pairing]]).

[F4] The tensor product of quotient maps $A\to A/J$, $B\to B/K$ has kernel $J\otimes B+A\otimes K$ and induces an isomorphism $A/J\otimes B/K\cong (A\otimes B)/(J\otimes B+A\otimes K)$; a sum of subspaces $P\otimes Q\otimes R+P'\otimes Q'\otimes R'$ meets the middle factor trivially when the first summand contributes only positive-degree elements of $I^-$ and $1\notin I^-$ ([[def-tensor-algebra-of-a-vector-space]], [[thm-universal-property-of-the-tensor-algebra]]).

[F5] $I^\pm$ is generated by the Serre elements, all of which are homogeneous of positive degree in the $E_i$ (respectively $F_i$), so $1\notin I^\pm$ and $I^+$ is contained in the direct sum of the positive-degree components of the free algebra $\widetilde U^+$.



## Proof

**Proof technique:** Reduce the triangular decomposition of $U_q(\mathfrak g)$ to the Serre-free presentation, where it is the input (A1), by comparing the two kernels in the commuting square of quotient maps; the Serre ideal identity (A2) forces the two kernels to coincide.

1.1 By [F1] the quotient map $\widetilde U\to U_q(\mathfrak g)$ has kernel $\langle\mathrm{Serre}\rangle$, and the induced maps $\widetilde U^{\pm}\to U_q^\pm$, $\widetilde U^{0}\to U_q^0$ are surjective with kernels $\widetilde U^{\pm}\cap\langle\mathrm{Serre}\rangle$, $\widetilde U^{0}\cap\langle\mathrm{Serre}\rangle$ by [F2]; indeed the image of the generated subalgebra is the subalgebra generated by the images. [F1, F2, algebra]

1.2 Under the identification [A1] the subalgebra $\widetilde U^+$ corresponds to $1\otimes1\otimes\widetilde U^+$ and $\widetilde U^-$ to $\widetilde U^-\otimes1\otimes1$. Hence by [A2] and [F4] the intersection $\widetilde U^+\cap\langle\mathrm{Serre}\rangle$ equals $(I^-\otimes\widetilde U^{0}\otimes\widetilde U^+ + \widetilde U^-\otimes\widetilde U^{0}\otimes I^+)\cap(1\otimes1\otimes\widetilde U^+)=1\otimes1\otimes I^+=I^+$, because $1\notin I^-$ by [F5]; likewise $\widetilde U^-\cap\langle\mathrm{Serre}\rangle=I^-$ and $\widetilde U^{0}\cap\langle\mathrm{Serre}\rangle=(I^-\widetilde U^{0}\widetilde U^++\widetilde U^-\widetilde U^{0}I^+)\cap(1\otimes\widetilde U^{0}\otimes1)=0$, the last because every element of either summand has a nonzero positive part in its first or third tensor factor by [F5]. Therefore, by 1.1, $U_q^+\cong\widetilde U^+/I^+$, $U_q^-\cong\widetilde U^-/I^-$ and $U_q^{0}\cong\widetilde U^{0}\cong\mathbb Q(q)[P^{\vee}]$, the last isomorphism being [A1] and the injectivity following from the vanishing of the intersection. This proves (ii) up to the linear independence of the $K_h$, which is the statement $\mathbb Q(q)[P^{\vee}]$ has the $K_h$ as a basis. [A1, A2, F2, F4, F5, algebra]

2.1 Consider the two vertical quotient maps of the commuting square whose top row is the isomorphism [A1]: the left vertical map $\widetilde U^-\otimes\widetilde U^{0}\otimes\widetilde U^+\to U_q^-\otimes U_q^{0}\otimes U_q^+$ given by the three surjections of 1.2, and the right vertical map $\widetilde U\to U_q(\mathfrak g)$. By [F4] the kernel of the left vertical map is $I^-\otimes\widetilde U^{0}\otimes\widetilde U^+ + \widetilde U^-\otimes\widetilde U^{0}\otimes I^+$, while the kernel of the right vertical map is $\langle\mathrm{Serre}\rangle$; by [A2] these two subspaces of $\widetilde U^-\otimes\widetilde U^{0}\otimes\widetilde U^+$ coincide. Hence the map induced between the quotients, which is precisely the multiplication map $m:U_q^-\otimes U_q^{0}\otimes U_q^+\to U_q(\mathfrak g)$ of the statement, is an isomorphism. [A1, A2, F4, step 1.2, algebra]

3.1 The graded statement (iii). The map $m$ is $Q$-graded for the root-lattice gradings, so it restricts to an isomorphism on each homogeneous component $\beta$, as claimed; the identifications $U_q^\pm\cong\widetilde U^\pm/I^\pm$ of 1.2 exhibit $U_q^\pm$ as the algebras presented by the separate Serre relations, so [F3] gives $\dim_{\mathbb Q(q)}U_q^+[\alpha]=\dim_{\mathbb C}U\mathfrak n^+[\alpha]$ and the corresponding negative-half equality; the $U_q^{0}$-module statement is the corresponding statement for the tensor product, and $\beta=0$ gives $U_q(\mathfrak g)[0]=U_q^-[0]\otimes U_q^{0}\otimes U_q^+[0]=U_q^{0}$. [A1, F3, step 2.1, algebra] ∎
