## Statement

Fix a symmetrizable Cartan datum $(I,A,D,P,P^{\vee},q)$, an index $i\in I$, and put $q_i=q^{d_i}$. Let $U_q(\mathfrak g)$ be the Drinfeld–Jimbo algebra and let $A_i$ be the subalgebra generated by $E_i,F_i,K_i^{\pm1}$ ([[def-drinfeld-jimbo-quantized-enveloping-algebra]], [[def-positive-negative-and-toral-quantum-subalgebras]]); write $x^{(m)}:=x^m/[m]_i!$ for the divided powers of [[def-quantum-integers-factorials-and-divided-powers-at-q-i]].

(i) The assignment $e\mapsto E_i$, $f\mapsto F_i$, $k\mapsto K_i$, $k^{-1}\mapsto K_i^{-1}$ defines an algebra isomorphism from the Drinfeld–Jimbo algebra $\mathbb A$ of the $1\times1$ Cartan matrix $(2)$ with symmetrizer $(d_i)$ onto $A_i$; the images of the monomials $F_i^aK_i^mE_i^b$ ($a,b\ge0$, $m\in\mathbb Z$) are linearly independent in $U_q(\mathfrak g)$, being images of the PBW monomials of the triangular decomposition ([[thm-triangular-decomposition-of-a-quantized-enveloping-algebra]]).

(ii) For all $a,b\ge0$, $K_iE_i^{(a)}K_i^{-1}=q_i^{2a}E_i^{(a)}$ and $K_iF_i^{(b)}K_i^{-1}=q_i^{-2b}F_i^{(b)}$, and for $b\ge1$ the divided powers satisfy the exact commutation formula
$$E_iF_i^{(b)}=F_i^{(b)}E_i+\frac{F_i^{(b-1)}}{q_i^{b}-q_i^{-b}}\sum_{s=0}^{b-1}\bigl(q_i^{-2s}K_i-q_i^{2s}K_i^{-1}\bigr);$$
iterating it computes $E_i^{(a)}F_i^{(b)}$ as a finite sum of monomials $F_i^{(b-t)}K_i^{m}E_i^{(a-t)}$ ($0\le t\le\min(a,b)$) with coefficients in $\mathbb Q(q)$.

(iii) For every $N\ge0$ there is a simple $A_i$-module $L_i(N)$ of dimension $N+1$: it is the quotient of the cyclic module $\widetilde M(N):=A_i/\bigl(A_iE_i+A_i(K_i-q_i^N)\bigr)$ by the submodule generated by $F_i^{(N+1)}v_N$, where $v_N$ is the image of $1$, with basis the images $v_t$ of $F_i^{(t)}v_N$, $0\le t\le N$, on which $K_iv_t=q_i^{N-2t}v_t$, $F_iv_t=[t+1]_iv_{t+1}$ and $E_iv_t=[N-t+1]_iv_{t-1}$ (with $v_{-1}=v_{N+1}=0$); $L_i(N)$ is the unique simple $A_i$-module generated by a vector $v\ne0$ with $E_iv=0$, $K_iv=q_i^Nv$ and $F_i^{N+1}v=0$.

**Open obligation.** Part (i) (linear independence of the monomials $F_i^aK_i^mE_i^b$ in $U_q(\mathfrak g)$) and the nonvanishing of $v_0,\dots,v_N$ and the simplicity in part (iii) use the triangular decomposition of [[thm-triangular-decomposition-of-a-quantized-enveloping-algebra]], whose decision is currently escalated because its inputs (A1), (A2) are unauthored. The consuming steps below are marked. All other claims are proved locally.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A symmetrizable Cartan datum, an index $i$, and the subalgebra $A_i$ generated by $E_i,F_i,K_i^{\pm1}$.

[F1] $E_iF_i-F_iE_i=(K_i-K_i^{-1})/(q_i-q_i^{-1})$; $K_iE_iK_i^{-1}=q_i^{2}E_i$ and $K_iF_iK_i^{-1}=q_i^{-2}F_i$; $K_i^{-1}=K_{-d_ih_i}$ is the inverse of $K_i$ ([[def-drinfeld-jimbo-quantized-enveloping-algebra]]).

[F2] $A_i$ is the subalgebra generated by $E_i,F_i,K_i^{\pm1}$; the universal property of $U_q(\mathfrak g)$ assigns a homomorphism to every assignment satisfying the defining relations ([[def-drinfeld-jimbo-quantized-enveloping-algebra]], [[def-positive-negative-and-toral-quantum-subalgebras]]).

[F3] $[m]_i=(q_i^m-q_i^{-m})/(q_i-q_i^{-1})$, $[m]_i!=\prod_{k=1}^m[k]_i$, $x^{(m)}=x^m/[m]_i!$, and $q_i-q_i^{-1}\ne0$ ([[def-quantum-integers-factorials-and-divided-powers-at-q-i]]).

[F4] Triangular decomposition: the multiplication map $U_q^-\otimes U_q^0\otimes U_q^+\to U_q(\mathfrak g)$ is an isomorphism, so the images of the monomials $F_i^aK_i^mE_i^b$ are linearly independent and span the rank-one subalgebra $A_i$ ([[thm-triangular-decomposition-of-a-quantized-enveloping-algebra]]); this input is escalated.

[F5] If $\sum_{s=0}^{b}v_s=0$ in $\mathbb Q(q)$ with $v_s=q_i^{2s}$, then $q_i^{2b}(q_i^{2}-1)=0$ after multiplying by $(q_i^2-1)$: the geometric sum identity $\sum_{s=0}^{b-1}q_i^{2s}=(q_i^{2b}-1)/(q_i^{2}-1)$ holds whenever $q_i^{2}\ne1$, which is the case here because $q$ is transcendental and $d_i>0$.



## Proof

**Proof technique:** prove the divided-power identities by a two-term induction on the exponent, then build the cyclic module $\widetilde M(N)$ and its quotient and compute the action on the string vectors; linear independence and nonvanishing are exactly the escalated input.

1.1 Weight rules. By [F1], $K_iE_i^aK_i^{-1}=q_i^{2a}E_i^a$ and $K_iF_i^bK_i^{-1}=q_i^{-2b}F_i^b$ for all $a,b\ge0$ by induction on the exponent via multiplicativity of conjugation; dividing by the nonzero scalars $[a]_i!$ and $[b]_i!$ of [F3] gives $K_iE_i^{(a)}K_i^{-1}=q_i^{2a}E_i^{(a)}$ and $K_iF_i^{(b)}K_i^{-1}=q_i^{-2b}F_i^{(b)}$. [F1, F3, algebra]

1.2 Commutation induction. Put $c:=1/(q_i-q_i^{-1})$ and $T_b:=c\sum_{s=0}^{b-1}(q_i^{-2s}K_i-q_i^{2s}K_i^{-1})$ for $b\ge1$. We prove $E_iF_i^b=F_i^bE_i+F_i^{b-1}T_b$ by induction on $b$. For $b=1$ this is [F1]. For the induction step, $E_iF_i^{b}=(E_iF_i^{b-1})F_i=(F_i^{b-1}E_i+F_i^{b-2}T_{b-1})F_i=F_i^{b-1}(F_iE_i+c(K_i-K_i^{-1}))+F_i^{b-2}T_{b-1}F_i$, and [F1] gives $T_{b-1}F_i=F_i\,c\sum_{t=1}^{b-1}(q_i^{-2t}K_i-q_i^{2t}K_i^{-1})=F_i(T_b-c(K_i-K_i^{-1}))$ after reindexing $s=t-1$; the two correction terms cancel and the identity follows. [F1, F3, algebra]

1.3 Divided-power formula. Dividing the identity of 1.2 by $[b]_i!$ gives
$$E_iF_i^{(b)}=F_i^{(b)}E_i+F_i^{(b-1)}\frac{T_b}{[b]_i},\qquad \frac{T_b}{[b]_i}=\frac{1}{q_i^{b}-q_i^{-b}}\sum_{s=0}^{b-1}\bigl(q_i^{-2s}K_i-q_i^{2s}K_i^{-1}\bigr),$$
because $F_i^{b-1}/[b]_i!=F_i^{(b-1)}/[b]_i$ and $[b]_i=(q_i^b-q_i^{-b})/(q_i-q_i^{-1})$; this is the displayed formula of (ii). Multiplying the formula for $E_i$ and for $E_i^{(a-1)}$ on the left and using $E_iF_i^{(b)}=F_i^{(b)}E_i+\dots$ repeatedly computes $E_i^{(a)}F_i^{(b)}$ as a finite sum of monomials $F_i^{(b-t)}K_i^mE_i^{(a-t)}$ with $0\le t\le\min(a,b)$, since each application of the formula moves one $E_i$ to the right at the cost of one $F_i$-power. [F3, step 1.1, step 1.2, algebra]

1.4 The cyclic module. Let $\widetilde M(N):=A_i/(A_iE_i+A_i(K_i-q_i^N))$ and $v_N:=1+\bigl(A_iE_i+A_i(K_i-q_i^N)\bigr)$. Every element of $A_i$ is a finite $\mathbb Q(q)$-linear combination of monomials $F_i^tK_i^mE_i^a$ with $t,a\ge0$ and $m\in\mathbb Z$: words are rearranged with [F1] and 1.1, and each application of the identity of 1.3 moves one $E_i$ to the right of an $F_i$-power at the cost of one $F_i$-power and finitely many $K$-factors, so the rearrangement terminates. In the left ideal $A_iE_i+A_i(K_i-q_i^N)$ all such monomials with $a>0$ or $m\ne0$ are absorbed (for $m\ne0$ because $K_i^m-q_i^{Nm}\in A_i(K_i-q_i^N)$), so the quotient is spanned by the images of $F_i^t$, $t\ge0$. Writing $v_t:=F_i^{(t)}v_N$ for $t\ge0$, the relations [F1] and 1.1 give $K_iv_t=q_i^{N-2t}v_t$ (using $K_i=K_{d_ih_i}$ and $\alpha_i(d_ih_i)=2d_i$), $F_iv_t=F_iF_i^{(t)}v_N=[t+1]_iF_i^{(t+1)}v_N=[t+1]_iv_{t+1}$ by [F3], and, using 1.3 with $a=1$,
$$E_iv_t=F_i^{(t-1)}\frac{1}{q_i^{t}-q_i^{-t}}\sum_{s=0}^{t-1}\bigl(q_i^{N-2s}-q_i^{2s-N}\bigr)v_N,$$
for $t\ge1$, and $E_iv_0=0$; the bracket equals $[N-t+1]_i$ by the geometric-sum identity [F5]. [F1, F3, F5, step 1.1, step 1.3, construct]

2.1 The quotient and its action. Let $L_i(N):=\widetilde M(N)/A_iF_i^{(N+1)}v_N$. The submodule $A_iF_i^{(N+1)}v_N$ is spanned by the $F_i^{(t)}v_N$ with $t\ge N+1$: it is contained in that span by the action formulas of 1.4, and it contains them because $F_i^{(t)}v_N=F_i^{(t-N-1)}F_i^{(N+1)}v_N$ up to the nonzero scalar $[t]_i!/[t-N-1]_i![N+1]_i!$ by [F3]. Hence in $L_i(N)$ the vectors $v_0,\dots,v_N$ satisfy the displayed action formulas with $v_{-1}=v_{N+1}=0$, and they span $L_i(N)$; they are nonzero and linearly independent by the escalated input [F4], applied to the distinct monomials $F_i^{(t)}K_i^mE_i^b$ with $t\le N$. [F3, F4, step 1.4, algebra]

3.1 Simplicity and uniqueness. Let $W\subseteq L_i(N)$ be a nonzero submodule and pick $0\ne w=\sum_{t\le T}c_tv_t$ with $T$ maximal and $c_T\ne0$. By 1.4, $E_i^Tw=[N]_i[N-1]_i\cdots[N-T+1]_ic_Tv_0$ (each application of $E_i$ lowers the index by one with the stated nonzero scalar; all displayed scalars are nonzero because $[m]_i\ne0$ for $m\ge1$ by [F3] and the nonvanishing of $q_i^{2m}-1$), so $v_0\in W$; then $F_i^tv_0=[t]_i!v_t$ puts every $v_t$ in $W$, so $W=L_i(N)$ and $L_i(N)$ is simple and generated by $v_N$ with $E_iv_N=0$, $K_iv_N=q_i^Nv_N$ and $F_i^{N+1}v_N=0$. Conversely, if $V$ is any simple $A_i$-module generated by $v\ne0$ with these three properties, then the assignment $1\mapsto v$ defines a surjection $\widetilde M(N)\to V$ whose kernel contains $F_i^{(N+1)}v_N$ by the third property, hence factors through $L_i(N)$; as $L_i(N)$ is simple and $V\ne0$, this map is an isomorphism, which gives the asserted uniqueness. [F3, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 Part (i). The universal property [F2] applied to the generators $E_i,F_i,K_i^{\pm1}$ of $A_i$, which satisfy the rank-one relations of [F1] (the rank-one datum has no Serre relations), gives a surjective algebra homomorphism $\mathbb A\to A_i$ from the rank-one Drinfeld–Jimbo algebra $\mathbb A$. Its injectivity is the escalated input [F4]: the monomials $F_i^aK_i^mE_i^b$ are linearly independent in $U_q(\mathfrak g)$, hence their preimages span $\mathbb A$ as a vector space and map bijectively onto $A_i$. [F1, F2, F4, algebra] ∎

## Remarks

The identity of step 1.2 is the exact divided-power commutation formula of the statement; it is proved here and does not use the escalated input. Steps 2.1 and 4.1 use [F4], whose proof is the open obligation recorded above.
