## Statement

Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero and let $t\ge1$. For each integer $0\le m\le t$, in the polynomial ring of a generic symmetric $t\times t$ matrix $Z$, the ideal $I_{m+1}$ generated by all $(m+1)$-minors is prime and its zero set is the locus $\operatorname{rank}Z\le m$; $k[Z]/I_{m+1}$ is normal. For each even integer $0\le m\le t$, in the polynomial ring of a generic alternating $t\times t$ matrix $A$, the ideal $J_{m+2}$ generated by the principal Pfaffians of size $m+2$ is prime and its zero set is the locus $\operatorname{rank}A\le m$; $k[A]/J_{m+2}$ is normal. When the indicated generator size exceeds $t$, the corresponding ideal is zero and the quotient is polynomial.
