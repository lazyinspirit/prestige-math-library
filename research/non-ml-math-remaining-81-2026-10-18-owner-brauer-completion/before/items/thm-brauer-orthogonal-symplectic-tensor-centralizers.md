## Statement

Let $k$ be algebraically closed of characteristic zero, and let $V$ be as in [[lem-brauer-contraction-functor-and-bending]]: dimension $m\ge1$ with a nondegenerate symmetric form and $G=O(V)$, or dimension $2n\ge2$ with a nondegenerate alternating form and $G=Sp(V)$. Use the full-group contraction functor from that item. For all nonnegative integers $a,b$, its image is $\operatorname{Hom}_G(V^{\otimes a},V^{\otimes b})$. Its kernel is the subspace obtained by bending the multilinear determinant relations of size $m+1$ in the orthogonal case, or the multilinear Pfaffian relations of size $2n+2$ in the symplectic case. In particular, the endomorphism map $B_r(\epsilon m)\to\operatorname{End}_G(V^{\otimes r})$ is surjective for every $r\ge0$ and is injective exactly when $r\le m$ for $O(V)$ or $r\le n$ for $Sp(V)$. The kernel in each fixed algebra is the span of all bent relations in that rank. The tensor ideal generated by these relations means the family of subspaces in all morphism spaces closed under composition on either side and tensoring by arbitrary diagrams. No reverse-commutant theorem is asserted here.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The full-group multilinear FFT/SFT and the Brauer contraction/bending maps.

[F1] [[thm-orthogonal-and-symplectic-multilinear-fft-sft]]: multilinear contractions span, with the exact determinant/Pfaffian relation spans and stable ranges. This supplier remains escalated.

[F2] [[lem-brauer-contraction-functor-and-bending]]: Brauer diagrams act by invariant contractions, and bending identifies intertwiners with invariant tensors.

## Proof

Escalated: once [F1] is locally proved, bend each morphism space to invariant multilinear forms using [F2]. Every matching contraction is the image of its Brauer diagram, giving surjectivity. The kernel is exactly the relation span in [F1] bent back to the original boundary type. The stable-range statement is the corresponding independence assertion in [F1]. The source's Theorem 4.8 carries out this categorical deduction but imports its FFT/SFT premises; the local algebraic premises remain unproved here.
