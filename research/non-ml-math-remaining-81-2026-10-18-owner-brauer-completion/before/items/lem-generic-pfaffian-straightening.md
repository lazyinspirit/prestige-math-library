## Statement

Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, $t\ge1$, and even $m$ with $0\le m\le t$. Let $A=(a_{ij})$ be the generic alternating $t\times t$ matrix, and let $J_{m+2}$ be the ideal generated by its principal Pfaffians of size $m+2$ (zero when $m+2>t$). For $S=(s_1<\cdots<s_h)\subseteq[t]$, let $A_S$ be the principal submatrix with rows and columns in this order, and write $P_S=\operatorname{Pf}(A_S)$, with $P_\varnothing=1$. Explicitly, for $M=\{\{i_1,j_1\},\ldots,\{i_{h/2},j_{h/2}\}\}$ a perfect matching of $\{1,\ldots,h\}$, order each pair with $i_a<j_a$ and the pairs by $i_1<\cdots<i_{h/2}$; define $\operatorname{sgn}(M)=\operatorname{sgn}(i_1,j_1,\ldots,i_{h/2},j_{h/2})$, the sign of that permutation. Then $P_S=\sum_M\operatorname{sgn}(M)\prod_a a_{s_{i_a},s_{j_a}}$. For even subsets $S=(s_1<\cdots<s_h)$ and $T=(t_1<\cdots<t_{h'})$, define $P_S\preceq P_T$ if $h'\le h$ and $s_j\le t_j$ for every $1\le j\le h'$. Let $M_m=(1,\ldots,m)$, with $M_0=\varnothing$. A product $P_{S_1}\cdots P_{S_q}$ is standard if $P_{S_1}\preceq\cdots\preceq P_{S_q}$ and $P_{M_m}\preceq P_{S_1}$. The classes of these standard products form a $k$-basis of $k[a_{ij}:i<j]/J_{m+2}$. Every nonstandard product can be rewritten using Pfaffian straightening identities, and the rewrites terminate in the order of Linshaw–Song, §1. If $m+2>t$, the ideal is zero.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The generic alternating matrix and its principal-Pfaffian ideal as defined in the Statement.
