## Statement

Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, $t\ge1$, and $0\le m<t$. Let $Z=(z_{ij})_{1\le i,j\le t}$ be the generic symmetric matrix, with $z_{ij}=z_{ji}$, and let $I_{m+1}$ be the ideal generated by its $(m+1)$-minors. For increasing subsets $A=(a_1<\cdots<a_s)$ and $C=(c_1<\cdots<c_{s'})$ of $[t]$, write $A\unrhd C$ when $s\le s'$ and $a_j\ge c_j$ for every $1\le j\le s$. Let $H_t$ consist of pairs $(A,B)$ of equal-size subsets with $A\unrhd B$, including $(\varnothing,\varnothing)$, and put $(A,B)\unrhd(C,D)$ when $B\unrhd C$. Write $p(A,B)=\det Z_{A,B}$, with $p(\varnothing,\varnothing)=1$. A product $p(A_1,B_1)\cdots p(A_q,B_q)$ is standard when $(A_1,B_1)\unrhd\cdots\unrhd(A_q,B_q)$. The classes of the standard products with $|A_i|\le m$ form a $k$-basis of $k[z_{ij}]/I_{m+1}$. Every nonstandard product can be rewritten by the minor straightening identities as a linear combination of standard products, and the rewrites terminate in the order of Lakshmibai–Raghavan–Sankaran–Shukla, §1.7. If $m\ge t$, then $I_{m+1}=0$ and the quotient is the polynomial ring.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The generic symmetric matrix and its minor ideal as defined in the Statement.
