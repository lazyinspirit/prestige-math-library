Let $(X,d)$ be a metric space ([[def-metric-space]]) and let
$x, y, z \in X$. Then

$$|d(x,z) - d(y,z)| \le d(x,y),$$

where $|\cdot|$ is the absolute value of $\mathbb{R}$ ([[def-abs-value]]).
