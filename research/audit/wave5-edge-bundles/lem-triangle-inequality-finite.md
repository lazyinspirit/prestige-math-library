#### Statement

Let $a : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}$ be a sequence of reals and $n \in \mathbb{N}$,
with finite sums as in [[def-finite-sum]] and the absolute value as in
[[def-abs-value]]. Then

$$\Big|\sum_{k<n} a_k\Big| \le \sum_{k<n} |a_k|.$$
