The hand calculation follows two failures through their risk sets, computes the Nelson–Aalen and product-limit estimates, and evaluates Greenwood's formula where its denominators are positive. Two event-time laws produce exactly the same data under a fixed censoring horizon while having different survival tails, showing why estimates beyond adequate follow-up are not identified by the observed law.
