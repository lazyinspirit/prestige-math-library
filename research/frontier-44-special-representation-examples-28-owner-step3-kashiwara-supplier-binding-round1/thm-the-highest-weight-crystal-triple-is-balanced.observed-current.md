## Statement

For every symmetrizable datum in the explicitly chosen label-weight category, the triples $(U_A^-,\mathcal L(\infty),\psi(\mathcal L(\infty)))$ and $(V_A(\lambda),\mathcal L(\lambda),\psi(\mathcal L(\lambda)))$ of [[def-balanced-triple-and-globalization-map]] are integrally balanced componentwise, and:

(i) their balanced $\mathbb Z$-intersections map isomorphically to $\mathbb Z\mathcal B(\infty)$ and $\mathbb Z\mathcal B(\lambda)$ respectively, and after rational extension these maps identify with the full $\mathbb Q$ crystal spaces;

(ii) the inverse lifts $G_\infty(b),G_\lambda(b)$ are bar invariant and form actual $A=\mathbb Z[q,q^{-1}]$ bases of the divided-power modules $U_A^-$ and $V_A(\lambda)$, with finite free root and label components whose ranks equal their field dimensions (the half ranks are the classical PBW ranks);

(iii) for every $i\in I$ and $n\ge1$,
$$I_{(i,n)}^A=\bigoplus_{b\in\mathcal B(\infty)\cap\tilde f_i^n\mathcal B(\infty)}AG_\infty(b),\qquad J_{(i,n)}^A=\bigoplus_{b\in\mathcal B(\lambda)\cap\tilde f_i^n\mathcal B(\lambda)}AG_\lambda(b),$$
and in particular the module image ideal equals $V_A(\lambda)\cap F_i^nV(\lambda)$; this saturation follows from the adapted global basis and is not an initial hypothesis;

(iv) under $\pi_\lambda$, $G_\infty(b)$ maps to $0$ exactly when its crystal image is $0$ and otherwise to $G_\lambda(\pi_\lambda(b))$;

(v) if $\tilde e_ib=0$ and $n\ge1$, then $G_\infty(\tilde f_i^nb)-F_i^{(n)}G_\infty(b)\in I_{(i,n+1)}^A$. This is the exact normalized top-string transition modulo the higher string ideal, not merely a modulo-$q$ congruence.

All assertions are integral, general symmetrizable, and proved without an integral-freeness premise.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A symmetrizable Cartan datum in the explicitly chosen label-weight category with $A=\mathbb Z[q,q^{-1}]$, the local rings $A_0,A_\infty$ of [[def-the-local-ring-at-q-zero]], the divided-power integral forms $U_A^-\subseteq U_q^-$ and $V_A(\lambda)=U_A^-v_\lambda$ of [[def-lusztig-divided-power-integral-form]] and [[def-integral-residue-lattices-of-the-negative-half-and-highest-weight-modules]], the Kashiwara lattices $\mathcal L(\infty),\mathcal L(\lambda)$, their residue sets $\mathcal B(\infty),\mathcal B(\lambda)$ and the bar lattices $\psi(\mathcal L)$ of [[def-kashiwara-lattices-lambda-and-infinity]] and [[def-the-bar-lattice-and-integral-lattice-of-a-based-module]], the string ideals $I_{(i,n)}^A,J_{(i,n)}^A$ and the projection $\pi_\lambda$ of [[lem-string-ideal-decomposition-of-the-integral-lattices]], and the height induction on root degree.

[F1] String-ideal induction ([[lem-string-ideal-triples-are-balanced]]): given integral balancedness with global lifts in every degree of height $<r$ for the half and for all highest modules, together with based-quotient compatibility and the negative-half string-ideal membership of lower lifts, every $I_{(i,n)}^A$ and $J_{(i,n)}^A$ is integrally balanced in degree $\alpha$ of height $r$ with an adapted free basis: its balanced $\mathbb Z$-intersection maps isomorphically onto $\mathbb Z(\mathcal B(\infty)\cap\tilde f_i^n\mathcal B(\infty))$, respectively $\mathbb Z(\mathcal B(\lambda)\cap\tilde f_i^n\mathcal B(\lambda))$, its Laurent extension is free over $A$, and its adapted lifts lie in both charts.

[F2] Chart algebra ([[lem-balanced-triple-linear-algebra-over-the-projective-line]]): with $M$ an $A$-submodule spanning $V$, $L_0,L_\infty$ full lattices and $E=M\cap L_0\cap L_\infty$, clause (1) gives that an isomorphism $E\to(M\cap L_0)/(M\cap qL_0)$ forces $M\cap L_0\cong\mathbb Z[q]\otimes E$, $M\cap L_\infty\cong\mathbb Z[q^{-1}]\otimes E$, $M\cong A\otimes E$ and $A_0\otimes E\cong L_0$, $A_\infty\otimes E\cong L_\infty$; clause (2) gives that a finite free $\mathbb Z$-module $F\subseteq M\cap L_0\cap L_\infty$ generating $M$ over $A$ and injecting into both rational residues satisfies $F\cong E$; clause (3) is the relative version modulo an $A$-submodule $N\subseteq M$ with $N\cong A\otimes(N\cap L_0\cap L_\infty)$, including exactness of the residue sequence and freeness conclusions.

[F3] String-ideal structure ([[lem-string-ideal-decomposition-of-the-integral-lattices]]): the integral projector $P_i$ shows that $u=\sum_lF_i^{(l)}u_l\in U_A^-$ with $\tilde e_i'$-primitive $u_l$ has all $u_l\in U_A^-$; $I_{(i,n)}^A=U_A^-\cap F_i^nU_q^-=\bigoplus_{l\ge n}F_i^{(l)}(\ker e_i'\cap U_A^-)=\sum_{l\ge n}F_i^{(l)}U_A^-$ and $J_{(i,n)}^A=\pi_\lambda(I_{(i,n)}^A)=\sum_{l\ge n}F_i^{(l)}V_A(\lambda)$; the integral residue of $I_{(i,n)}^A\cap\mathcal L(\infty)$ is $\bigoplus_{b}\mathbb Zb$ over $\mathcal B(\infty)\cap\tilde f_i^n\mathcal B(\infty)$, and on every integrable module lattice the boundary identity $(M_A)_\mu=\sum_{k\ge n}F_i^{(k)}(M_A)_{\mu+k\alpha_i}$ holds whenever $\langle h_i,\mu\rangle=-n\le-1$.

[F4] Reversal ([[lem-negative-half-reversal-preserves-integral-and-crystal-lattices]]): the anti-involution $\star$ of $U_q^-$ fixes each $F_i$, reverses words, preserves $U_A^-$, commutes with $\psi$, preserves the form $B(P^\star,Q^\star)=B(P,Q)$, and preserves $\mathcal L(\infty)$, the integral residue lattice and the bar lattice, permuting $\mathcal B(\infty)\cup(-\mathcal B(\infty))$ on the residue; it maps the one-sided piece $U_q^-F_k$ onto $F_kU_q^-$ in every degree and induces $I_{(k,n)}^A=U_A^-\cap F_k^nU_q^-$ onto $U_A^-\cap F_k^nU_q^-$ with the sides interchanged.

[F5] Rational crystals and projection ([[thm-grand-loop-existence-of-highest-weight-crystal-bases]], [[thm-crystal-basis-of-the-negative-half]], [[lem-crystal-operators-commute-with-highest-weight-projection-modulo-q]], [[def-kashiwara-lattices-lambda-and-infinity]]): $\mathcal B(\infty)$ and $\mathcal B(\lambda)$ are $\mathbb Q$-bases of the rational residues; every $\mathcal L(\infty)_{-\nu}$ is free over $A_0$ of rank $\#\mathcal B(\infty)_{-\nu}=\dim_{\mathbb Q(q)}U_q^-[-\nu]$; the partial-inverse clauses hold, so for every $b\in\mathcal B(\infty)_{-\alpha}$ with $\alpha\ne0$ there are $i\in I$ and $b'\in\mathcal B(\infty)_{-\alpha+\alpha_i}$ with $\tilde f_ib'=b$; and $\pi_\lambda$ induces a bijection $\{b\in\mathcal B(\infty):\pi_\lambda(b)\ne0\}\to\mathcal B(\lambda)$ compatible with the crystal operators modulo $q$.

[F6] Integral residues ([[lem-integral-crystal-residues-of-the-negative-half-represent-the-crystal-basis]]): $L_{\mathbb Z}(\infty)/qL_{\mathbb Z}(\infty)=\bigoplus_{b\in\mathcal B(\infty)}\mathbb Zb$ inside $\mathcal L(\infty)/q\mathcal L(\infty)$, with the module integral residue taken up in the present theorem.

[F7] Auxiliary regular data and the projection kernel ([[lem-finite-support-regularization-of-quantum-label-weight-modules]], [[thm-integrable-quantum-highest-weight-module-exists-and-is-unique]], [[lem-quantum-highest-weight-serre-quotient-is-integrable]], [[def-lusztig-divided-power-integral-form]]): every fixed finite root degree may be transported into an explicit primitive-coroot regular datum in which integral dominant labels with prescribed coordinates exist; for a dominant integral $\lambda$ the field projection $\pi_\lambda$ restricts to an $A$-linear surjection $U_A^-\to V_A(\lambda)$, and in a fixed root degree $\alpha$ the kernel of $\pi_\lambda$ is exactly the image of $U_q^-F_k$ for the colors $k$ with $\lambda(h_k)=0$, every other singular generator contributing only higher root degrees; moreover for large $\lambda$ the projection is an isomorphism of the degree components.

[F8] Half ranks ([[thm-generic-quantum-serre-halves-have-classical-pbw-ranks-and-a-nondegenerate-hopf-pairing]]): the field dimension of the Serre-presented half in each root degree equals the classical PBW rank of the enveloping algebra of the corresponding nilpotent half; this identification carries the stated Choice hypothesis of that theorem and is used only for the numerical rank clause.

[C1] Choice: the Axiom of Choice is declared via [[def-axiom-of-choice]] and enters only through the grand-loop and negative-half-form suppliers of [F4], [F5] and [F8]; the induction, chart and string computations below are choice-free.

## Proof

**Proof technique:** induction.

1.1 **Simultaneous induction and the base layer.** Replace the assertions by the following height-$r$ induction statements: X$(r)$: the triple of $U_A^-$ is integrally balanced in every degree of height $\le r$ with bar-invariant global lifts $G_\infty(b)$ forming an $A$-basis and $\mathbb Z$-intersection $\mathbb Z\mathcal B(\infty)$; Y$(r)$: the same for every $V_A(\lambda)$ with lifts $G_\lambda(b)$ and intersection $\mathbb Z\mathcal B(\lambda)$; Z$(r)$: the negative-half membership $G_\infty(b)\in I_{(i,1)}^A$ whenever $\tilde e_ib\ne0$, for every new degree of height $\le r$; and the derived clause D$(r)$: for the new degrees, the based-quotient compatibility $\pi_\lambda(G_\infty(b))=0$ if and only if $\pi_\lambda(b)=0$, and $\pi_\lambda(G_\infty(b))=G_\lambda(\pi_\lambda(b))$ otherwise. For $r=0$ the degree component is $A\cdot1$ or $Av_\lambda$, the charts meet in $\mathbb Z\cdot1$ or $\mathbb Z v_\lambda$, and the stated properties hold by inspection. Assume X, Y, Z, D for all heights $<r$ and fix a degree $\alpha$ of height $r\ge1$. [F5, F7, Given]

2.1 **The string ideals at height $r$ are balanced.** The induction hypotheses are exactly the input of [F1] at height $r$, so for every $i\in I$ and $n\ge1$ the ideals $I_{(i,n)}^A$ and $J_{(i,n)}^A$ are integrally balanced in degree $-\alpha$, respectively $\lambda-\alpha$, with adapted free bases whose balanced $\mathbb Z$-intersections map isomorphically onto $\mathbb Z(\mathcal B(\infty)\cap\tilde f_i^n\mathcal B(\infty))$ and $\mathbb Z(\mathcal B(\lambda)\cap\tilde f_i^n\mathcal B(\lambda))$. In particular, for each $b\in\mathcal B(\infty)\cap\tilde f_i^n\mathcal B(\infty)$ there is an adapted lift $g\in E_{I_{(i,n)}}:=I_{(i,n)}^A\cap\mathcal L(\infty)\cap\psi(\mathcal L(\infty))$ whose residue class in $(I_{(i,n)}^A\cap\mathcal L(\infty))/(I_{(i,n)}^A\cap q\mathcal L(\infty))$ is $b$; these are the only lifts used below. [F1, step 1.1]

3.1 **Every string lift is bar fixed.** Let $g\in E_I:=I_{(i,n)}^A\cap\mathcal L(\infty)\cap\psi(\mathcal L(\infty))$ be any adapted lift. By [F3] the ideal $I_{(i,n)}^A$ is generated over $A$ by bar-fixed divided-word vectors, so write $g=\sum_wa_ww$ with $a_w\in A$ and $\psi(w)=w$; then $g-\psi(g)=\sum_w(a_w-\psi(a_w))w$, and each antisymmetric Laurent polynomial $a_w-\psi(a_w)$ is divisible in $A$ by $q-q^{-1}$, so $h:=\bigl(g-\psi(g)\bigr)/(q-q^{-1})\in I_{(i,n)}^A$. Since $g,\psi(g)\in\mathcal L(\infty)\cap\psi(\mathcal L(\infty))$ and $(q-q^{-1})^{-1}=-q(1-q^2)^{-1}\in qA_0$, one gets $h\in q\mathcal L(\infty)$; since $(q-q^{-1})^{-1}=q^{-1}(1-q^{-2})^{-1}\in A_\infty$, one gets $h\in\psi(\mathcal L(\infty))$. Thus $h\in E_I$ and $h\in q\mathcal L(\infty)$, so its class in $(I_{(i,n)}^A\cap\mathcal L(\infty))/(I_{(i,n)}^A\cap q\mathcal L(\infty))\cong\mathbb Z(\mathcal B(\infty)\cap\tilde f_i^n\mathcal B(\infty))$ is zero; by injectivity of the balanced residue map of [F2](1) applied to $I_{(i,n)}^A$, $h=0$ and $g=\psi(g)$. The same argument applies to the module ideals $J_{(i,n)}^A$. [F2, F3, step 2.1]

3.2 **Gluing across colors (Kashiwara 7.5.1).** Let $b\in\mathcal B(\infty)_{-\alpha}$ lie in $\tilde f_i\mathcal B(\infty)\cap\tilde f_j\mathcal B(\infty)$, and let $G_i(b),G_j(b)\in\mathcal L(\infty)\cap\psi(\mathcal L(\infty))$ be the corresponding adapted lifts of $I_{(i,1)}^A$ and $I_{(j,1)}^A$ from step 2.1. Write $b$ as a lowering word with rightmost color $k$ and choose, using [F7], a dominant integral auxiliary label $\lambda$ in the regular datum with $\lambda(h_k)=0$ and all other coordinates larger than the degree bounds. By the kernel statement of [F7], $\ker\pi_\lambda$ in degree $\alpha$ equals the image of $U_q^-F_k$, and $bv_\lambda=0$ because the word ends in the color $k$ of length $0$ at $v_\lambda$. For each of the two lifts, $G_i(b)v_\lambda$ lies in $J_{(i,1)}^A$ by [F3], lies in $q\mathcal L(\lambda)$ because its crystal residue $\pi_\lambda(b)=0$, and lies in $\psi(\mathcal L(\lambda))$ by bar invariance; the balancedness of $J_{(i,1)}^A$ from step 2.1 forces $G_i(b)v_\lambda=0$, and likewise $G_j(b)v_\lambda=0$. Hence $Q:=G_i(b)-G_j(b)$ lies in $U_A^-\cap U_q^-F_k$, in $q\mathcal L(\infty)$ (both lifts have residue $b$), and in $\psi(\mathcal L(\infty))$. By the reversal [F4], $Q^\star$ lies in $U_A^-\cap F_kU_q^-\cap q\mathcal L(\infty)\cap\psi(\mathcal L(\infty))$; by [F3], $U_A^-\cap F_kU_q^-=I_{(k,1)}^A$; and then the balancedness of $I_{(k,1)}^A$ from step 2.1 again forces $Q^\star=0$, hence $Q=0$. [F1, F3, F4, F7, step 2.1]

4.1 **Global half lifts and spanning.** For $\alpha\ne0$, [F5] supplies for every $b\in\mathcal B(\infty)_{-\alpha}$ a color $i$ and a predecessor $b'\in\mathcal B(\infty)_{-\alpha+\alpha_i}$ with $b=\tilde f_ib'$, so $b\in\mathcal B(\infty)\cap\tilde f_i\mathcal B(\infty)$ and step 2.1 provides an adapted lift in $I_{(i,1)}^A$; define $G_\infty(b)$ to be this element. Step 3.2 shows the definition does not depend on the chosen color, and two lifts for the same color differ by an element of $E_I\cap q\mathcal L(\infty)=0$ by step 3.1, so $G_\infty(b)$ is well defined and bar invariant. By [F3], every divided-word generator in degree $-\alpha$ is divisible on the right by some $F_i$, hence $U_A^-=\sum_{i\in I}I_{(i,1)}^A$ in this degree; by step 2.1 and the lower-height part of the induction, each $I_{(i,1)}^A$ has the free $A$-basis $\{G_\infty(b):b\in\mathcal B(\infty)\cap\tilde f_i\mathcal B(\infty)\}$. Therefore $U_A^-$ in degree $-\alpha$ equals $\sum_{b\in\mathcal B(\infty)_{-\alpha}}AG_\infty(b)$, and X$(<r)$ supplies the lower-height components, so the family $(G_\infty(b))_b$ spans $U_A^-$ over $A$ in every degree of height $\le r$. [F3, F5, step 2.1, step 3.2]

5.1 **The half triple is integrally balanced (X$(r)$).** Put $F:=\bigoplus_{b\in\mathcal B(\infty)_{-\alpha}}\mathbb ZG_\infty(b)$; it is a finite free $\mathbb Z$-module contained in $U_A^-\cap\mathcal L(\infty)\cap\psi(\mathcal L(\infty))$ because the lifts are bar invariant and lie in the adapted integers of the string ideals. It generates $U_A^-$ over $A$ by step 4.1. Its image in $\mathcal L(\infty)/q\mathcal L(\infty)$ is $\bigoplus_b\mathbb Zb$ by [F6], and its image under $\psi$ is another free $\mathbb Z$-span of the same rank inside the infinity residue; the two images are injective. Applying the chart generation criterion [F2](2) to $M=U_A^-$, $L_0=\mathcal L(\infty)$ and $L_\infty=\psi(\mathcal L(\infty))$ gives that the half triple in degree $-\alpha$ is integrally balanced, with $E=F$ and $E\to(U_A^-\cap\mathcal L(\infty))/(U_A^-\cap q\mathcal L(\infty))$ an isomorphism onto $\bigoplus_b\mathbb Zb$; the same holds in every lower degree by X$(<r)$, so X$(r)$ holds. [F2, F6, step 3.1, step 4.1]

5.2 **String-ideal membership and the top-string transition (Z$(r)$).** Since each $G_\infty(b)$ with $b\in\tilde f_i^n\mathcal B(\infty)$ lies in $I_{(i,n)}^A$ by construction and the adapted basis of $I_{(i,n)}^A$ is free, step 2.1 and the injectivity of the balanced residue map identify $I_{(i,n)}^A=\bigoplus_{b\in\mathcal B(\infty)\cap\tilde f_i^n\mathcal B(\infty)}AG_\infty(b)$ in degree $-\alpha$; in particular $G_\infty(b)\in I_{(i,1)}^A$ whenever $\tilde e_ib\ne0$, proving Z$(r)$. Moreover, if $\tilde e_ib=0$ and $n\ge1$, then $F_i^{(n)}G_\infty(b)$ lies in $I_{(i,n)}^A$ with residue $\tilde f_i^nb$ in $I_{(i,n)}^A\cap\mathcal L(\infty)/q(\cdots)$; after subtracting from it the adapted lift $G_\infty(\tilde f_i^nb)\in E_{I_{(i,n)}}$, the difference lies in $E_{I_{(i,n)}}\cap q\mathcal L(\infty)=0$, since the two elements reduce to the same crystal class. Hence $G_\infty(\tilde f_i^nb)-F_i^{(n)}G_\infty(b)\in I_{(i,n+1)}^A$ exactly. [F1, F3, step 2.1, step 4.1]

6.1 **Killed module images vanish.** Fix $\lambda\in P^+$ and let $b\in\mathcal B(\infty)_{-\alpha}$ have $\pi_\lambda(b)=0$. By [F5] choose $i$ with $\tilde e_ib\ne0$; then $b\in\tilde f_i\mathcal B(\infty)$, so $G_\infty(b)\in I_{(i,1)}^A$ by Z$(r)$ and $G_\infty(b)v_\lambda\in J_{(i,1)}^A$ by [F3]. Since its residue $\pi_\lambda(b)$ is zero it lies in $q\mathcal L(\lambda)$, and since $G_\infty(b)$ is bar invariant and $\pi_\lambda$ commutes with $\psi$ it lies in $\psi(\mathcal L(\lambda))$. The balancedness of $J_{(i,1)}^A$ from step 2.1 forces the class of $G_\infty(b)v_\lambda$ in its balanced intersection to be zero, so $G_\infty(b)v_\lambda=0$; this is the local form of Kashiwara 7.5.2. [F1, F3, F5, step 5.2]

7.1 **The surviving module images.** For $\pi_\lambda(b)\ne0$, the element $G_\infty(b)v_\lambda$ is bar invariant, lies in $\mathcal L(\lambda)$ by the lattice projection of [F5], and its residue in $\mathcal L(\lambda)/q\mathcal L(\lambda)$ is $\pi_\lambda(b)$ by the derived clause D$(<r)$ and the operator compatibility of [F5]; the residues $\pi_\lambda(b)$ over the surviving $b$ biject onto $\mathcal B(\lambda)$ by [F5]. These images span $V_A(\lambda)$ over $A$ because $U_A^-$ is spanned by the $G_\infty(b)$ and, by [F3] and step 6.1, $\pi_\lambda$ is surjective with the killed generators contributing zero. [F3, F5, step 4.1, step 6.1]

8.1 **The module triple is integrally balanced (Y$(r)$).** Let $F_\lambda:=\bigoplus_{b:\pi_\lambda(b)\ne0}\mathbb ZG_\infty(b)v_\lambda$ inside $V_A(\lambda)\cap\mathcal L(\lambda)\cap\psi(\mathcal L(\lambda))$. It generates $V_A(\lambda)$ over $A$ by step 7.1 and its image in $\mathcal L(\lambda)/q\mathcal L(\lambda)$ is $\bigoplus_{\beta\in\mathcal B(\lambda)}\mathbb Z\beta$ by the bijection of residues, hence is a free generator group; the bar image is the corresponding free span in $\psi(\mathcal L(\lambda))/q^{-1}\psi(\mathcal L(\lambda))$, injective of the same rank. The chart generation criterion [F2](2), applied to $M=V_A(\lambda)$, $L_0=\mathcal L(\lambda)$, $L_\infty=\psi(\mathcal L(\lambda))$, gives integral balancedness in degree $\lambda-\alpha$ with $E_{\mathbb Z}=\bigoplus_{\pi_\lambda(b)\ne0}\mathbb ZG_\infty(b)v_\lambda$ and residue group $\mathbb Z\mathcal B(\lambda)$; with the induction hypothesis in lower degrees this is Y$(r)$. Uniqueness of the balanced lift identifies each $G_\infty(b)v_\lambda$ with the adapted module lift $G_\lambda(\pi_\lambda(b))$, which also proves (iv) in degree $\alpha$. [F2, step 7.1]

9.1 **Saturation and the adapted string bases.** The lifts $G_\lambda(\beta)$ for $\beta\in\mathcal B(\lambda)$ now form an $A$-basis of $V_A(\lambda)$ in every degree of height $\le r$, and by [F3] the ideals $J_{(i,n)}^A$ are generated by the images of the corresponding string ideal generators. Since each $F_i^{(n)}V(\lambda)$ is the $\mathbb Q(q)$-span of $\{\tilde f_i^n\beta:\beta\in\mathcal B(\lambda)\}$, its intersection with $V_A(\lambda)$ equals the $A$-span of the adapted lifts $\{G_\lambda(\beta):\beta\in\mathcal B(\lambda)\cap\tilde f_i^n\mathcal B(\lambda)\}$, which is exactly $J_{(i,n)}^A$; this proves the saturation identity and the displayed free decompositions of (iii) for the module, and the half decompositions were proved in step 5.2. [F1, F3, step 5.2, step 8.1]

10.1 **Conclusion.** Steps 1.1 and 2.1 set up the induction and produce the balanced string ideals; steps 3.1 and 3.2 prove bar invariance and the gluing of string lifts; steps 4.1 and 5.1 construct the global half basis, its chart injections and X$(r)$; step 5.2 proves Z$(r)$ and the exact top-string transition (v); steps 6.1, 7.1, 8.1 and 9.1 prove Y$(r)$, the projection rule (iv), the module saturation and the adapted decompositions (iii) for both halves. The ranks in (ii) are the field dimensions computed by [F5] rank by rank, with the classical PBW identification supplied by [F8] and its stated Choice hypothesis; the dimension count is finite in each root degree because only finitely many compositions occur. This closes the induction in every degree and proves (i)-(v); the Choice use is confined to [C1]. [F5, F8, step 5.1, step 5.2, step 9.1, C1] ∎
