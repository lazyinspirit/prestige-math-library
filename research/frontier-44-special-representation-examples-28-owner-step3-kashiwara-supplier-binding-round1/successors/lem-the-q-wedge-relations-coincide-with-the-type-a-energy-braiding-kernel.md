## Statement

On $V_{\mathrm{aff}}\otimes V_{\mathrm{aff}}$ put $a=Y_1/Y_2$ and let $P$ be the
flip of the two tensor factors including their loop variables. Define the
completed normalized left operator

$$R_{\mathrm{fix}}:=(q^2-a)^{-1}\bigl[(a-1)S+q^2-1\bigr],\qquad \psi_{\mathrm{fix}}:=1-q^2a^{-1},$$

where $S$ is the two-factor Hecke operator of
[[def-the-affine-hecke-algebra-action-on-finite-tensor-powers-of-the-vector-representation]]
and $(q^2-a)^{-1}$ is expanded as the geometric series in $a$ that starts with
$q^{-2}(1+a/q^2+\cdots)$. Then:

(i) $R_{\mathrm{fix}}$ swaps the two loop variables, fixes every extremal
pure tensor $u\otimes u$, and commutes with the fixed $U_q'(\mathfrak g)$-action;
the cleared identity
$\psi_{\mathrm{fix}}(R_{\mathrm{fix}}-1)=(a^{-1}-1)(S+1)$ holds in the Laurent
domain, and since multiplication by $a^{-1}-1$ is injective on the original
Laurent-loop module one has $\ker(R_{\mathrm{fix}}-1)=\ker(S+1)$ there, before
any localization.

(ii) $R_{\mathrm{fix}}$ satisfies the normalized Yang-Baxter equation
$R_{12}R_{23}R_{12}=R_{23}R_{12}R_{23}$ on the three-factor completed module.

(iii) The flipped operator $R_{\mathrm{op}}:=PR_{\mathrm{fix}}P$ with
$\psi_{\mathrm{op}}=1-q^2a$ is the opposite-channel ($\otimes_+$) normalized
braiding of
[[def-the-level-one-vector-representation-its-affinization-and-energy-function]]:
its $q^0$-leading term is
$z^{H_{\mathrm{op}}}b_1\otimes z^{-H_{\mathrm{op}}}b_2$ with the energy
$H_{\mathrm{op}}(z^ab_j\otimes z^bb_k)=\chi(j>k)+b-a$, and its kernel is
$N_{\mathrm{op}}=P\ker(S+1)$, the $U_q'(\mathfrak g)$-orbit of the extremal
diagonal generated by
$C_{j,k}=v_j\otimes z^{-\chi(j>k)}v_k+q\,z^{-\chi(j>k)}v_k\otimes v_j$ $(j\ne k)$
and $C_{j,j}=v_j\otimes v_j$. Flipping back gives the fixed-channel quotient
and the normal-ordering relations of
[[def-finite-q-wedge-space-and-normal-ordering-relations]].

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The two-factor Hecke operator $S$ with $S^2=(Q-1)S+Q$, $Q=q^2$, its loop extension $S(fw)=s(f)S(w)+(1-Q)Y_2\frac{f-s(f)}{Y_1-Y_2}w$, the loop variables $Y_1,Y_2$, the flip $P$, and the energy $H_{\mathrm{op}}$ and tensor rule of the opposite channel.

[F1] $S=PSP$ on residue tensors by the symmetric three-case formula, $S$ commutes with $Y_1Y_2$, and $S(fw)$ has the displayed divided-difference form with the left coefficient $1-Q$ ([[def-the-affine-hecke-algebra-action-on-finite-tensor-powers-of-the-vector-representation]]).

[F2] $S$ commutes with the fixed $U_q'(\mathfrak g)$-action on two factors, and the loop variables act invertibly with $Y_ju_m=u_{m-e}$; the Laurent polynomial ring $\mathbb Q(q)[Y_1^{\pm1},Y_2^{\pm1}]$ is a domain, so multiplication by a nonzero element is injective on every free module ([[def-the-affine-hecke-algebra-action-on-finite-tensor-powers-of-the-vector-representation]]).

[F3] On residue pairs $(j,k)$ the opposite-channel rule is $f$ on the first factor iff $\varepsilon_i(b_1)\ge\varphi_i(b_2)$, and $H_{\mathrm{op}}(z^ab_j\otimes z^bb_k)=\chi(j>k)+b-a$ satisfies the two loop-shift axioms and $H_{\mathrm{op}}>0\iff m_1>m_2$ ([[def-the-level-one-vector-representation-its-affinization-and-energy-function]]).

[F4] In the finite-type vector crystal, $B\otimes B$ is connected and the extremal elements are the $b_j$ on the diagonal ([[lem-the-level-one-vector-representation-has-a-perfect-crystal-with-lower-global-basis]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** clear the denominator of $R_{\mathrm{fix}}$, read the kernel off the cleared identity, verify Yang-Baxter on the Baxterized numerator, and transport the result through the flip.

1.1 The cleared identity and normalization. Since $S$ commutes with $a$ and with $Y_1Y_2$, the operator $(a-1)S+q^2-1$ is a Laurent coefficient operator; multiplying by the series $(q^2-a)^{-1}=\sum_{r\ge0}a^rq^{-2r-2}$ gives a well-defined endomorphism of the completion of $V_{\mathrm{aff}}\otimes V_{\mathrm{aff}}$ in the $a$-adic direction. Then $R_{\mathrm{fix}}-1=\frac{(a-1)S+q^2-1-(q^2-a)}{q^2-a}=\frac{(a-1)(S+1)}{q^2-a}$, and $\psi_{\mathrm{fix}}=1-q^2/a=(a-q^2)/a=-(q^2-a)/a$, so $\psi_{\mathrm{fix}}(R_{\mathrm{fix}}-1)=(a^{-1}-1)(S+1)$ exactly in the Laurent domain after clearing. Since $a^{-1}-1\ne0$ and the Laurent ring is a domain, multiplication by $a^{-1}-1$ is injective on the original free Laurent module, so no original vector is lost: $\ker(R_{\mathrm{fix}}-1)=\ker(S+1)$ there. [F1, F2, given, algebra]

2.1 Symmetries of $R_{\mathrm{fix}}$. By [F1] $S=P\,S_0\,P$ decomposes into the residue swap $P$ and the loop swap $s$ with $S_0$ a residue operator, and $a\mapsto a^{-1}$ under the factor swap; substituting in the definition shows $PR_{\mathrm{fix}}P=(q^2-a^{-1})^{-1}[(a^{-1}-1)P S_0 P+q^2-1]=R_{\mathrm{fix}}$, so $R_{\mathrm{fix}}$ swaps the loop variables. On an extremal diagonal $u\otimes u$ the operator $S$ has eigenvalue $-1$ (the $j=k$ case, transported by the loop extension), so the numerator is $(a-1)(-1)+q^2-1=(a-1)(-1)-(a-1)=0$ and $R_{\mathrm{fix}}(u\otimes u)=u\otimes u$; here $a=1$ on the diagonal, so the series is ordinary. Since $S$ commutes with $U_q'(\mathfrak g)$ by [F2] and $a$ has total $d$-degree $0$, $R_{\mathrm{fix}}$ commutes with the fixed action. [step 1.1, F1, F2, algebra]

3.1 Yang-Baxter for $R_{\mathrm{fix}}$. Write $R_{\mathrm{fix}}=R_0(a)\,s_{\mathrm{loop}}$ with $R_0(a)=\frac{(a-1)S_0+q^2-1}{q^2-a}$ and $s_{\mathrm{loop}}$ the loop swap, and Baxterize $B_i(x):=(x-1)S_{0,i}+(Q-1)$ with $Q=q^2$ and commuting external scalars $x,y$. In the three-factor window expand $B_1(x)B_2(xy)B_1(y)-B_2(y)B_1(xy)B_2(x)$: reducing $S_{0,i}^2=(Q-1)S_{0,i}+Q$ and the finite braid $S_{0,1}S_{0,2}S_{0,1}=S_{0,2}S_{0,1}S_{0,2}$ of the residue operators turns the difference into the scalar factor $(x-1)(y-1)+(x-1)+(y-1)-(xy-1)=0$ times the identity. The loop permutations supply exactly the arguments $x,y,xy$ on the three factors and the scalar denominators are the common commuting product $(q^2-x)(q^2-xy)(q^2-y)$ after clearing, so the completed operator identity $R_{12}R_{23}R_{12}=R_{23}R_{12}R_{23}$ follows. [step 2.1, F1, given, algebra]

3.2 The flipped operator. Put $R_{\mathrm{op}}=PR_{\mathrm{fix}}P$ and $\psi_{\mathrm{op}}=1-q^2a$; by [F1] and [F3] the flip exchanges the two tensor factors and the comparison rules, so $R_{\mathrm{op}}$ is the normalized braiding for the opposite coproduct with denominator $\psi_{\mathrm{op}}$, and at $q^0$ the fundamental cases $j>k$, $j=k$, $j<k$ give respectively the loop factors $a,1,1$ before the loop swap; after the swap the leading term is $z^{H_{\mathrm{op}}}b_1\otimes z^{-H_{\mathrm{op}}}b_2$ with $H_{\mathrm{op}}(z^ab_j\otimes z^bb_k)=\chi(j>k)+b-a$, which is the energy of the opposite channel. [step 2.1, F3, given, algebra]

4.1 The opposite kernel and its generators. By step 1.1 and the flip, $\ker(R_{\mathrm{op}}-1)=P\ker(S+1)$. The base kernel elements $v_j\otimes v_j$ and $v_j\otimes v_k-qv_k\otimes v_j$ $(j<k)$ of the two-factor fixed channel give after the flip $v_j\otimes v_j$ and $qv_j\otimes v_k-v_k\otimes v_j$ $(j<k)$; conjugating by loop powers and using the $l=0,\pm1$ Bernstein formulas turns these into the vectors $C_{j,k}=v_j\otimes z^{-\chi(j>k)}v_k+qz^{-\chi(j>k)}v_k\otimes v_j$ for $j\ne k$ and $C_{j,j}=v_j\otimes v_j$. The $U_q'(\mathfrak g)$-orbit of the extremal diagonal generates all $C_{j,k}$: two consecutive $F$-moves reach every diagonal, one move gives each adjacent $C_{j,k}$, and continuing around the cycle of residues with the $0$-edge loop shift gives every remaining $C$ with its loop power; common loop shifts $Y_1^rY_2^r$ and the symmetric powers $Y_1^n+Y_2^n$ extend the base cases to every gap, and subtracting the leading pair and inducting on the gap as in the straightening recursion proves generation, matching the kernel $N$ of the cited KMPY section with its $C$ generators. Flipping back identifies the fixed-channel quotient with the normal-ordering relations of the finite q-wedge space. [step 1.1, step 3.2, F3, F4, given, algebra]

5.1 Conclusion. The q-wedge relations of the fixed channel are exactly the kernel of the normalized left braiding $R_{\mathrm{fix}}$: the cleared identity $\psi_{\mathrm{fix}}(R_{\mathrm{fix}}-1)=(a^{-1}-1)(S+1)$ holds in the Laurent domain with injective multiplier $a^{-1}-1$, so the kernels agree before localization; $R_{\mathrm{fix}}$ swaps loops, fixes extremal diagonals, intertwines the fixed action and satisfies Yang-Baxter; and its flip is the opposite-channel braiding with energy $H_{\mathrm{op}}$ and kernel generated by the $C_{j,k}$. [step 1.1, step 2.1, step 3.1, step 3.2, step 4.1] ∎

## Remarks

- The formula is a **left**-coefficient fraction: $S$ and $a$ do not commute,
  and no right-fraction interpretation of $(q^2-a)^{-1}$ is asserted.

- The norm of the flip is a loop-and-factor flip only; it does not identify the
  source's positive (descending) wedge chart with the library chart, which is
  proved locally in the finite q-wedge space.
