These examples calculate the first four ranks, list and multiply the five normal forms in rank three, and show that at loop value zero a generator can be nonzero and square-zero. They use the A-page conventions and do not add general theorem suppliers.

## Examples

- [[ex-temperley-lieb-ranks-zero-through-two]]: bases in ranks zero through three and the rank-three multiplication table.
- [[ex-temperley-lieb-three-strand-normal-forms]]: the five Jones normal forms and two adjacent triple reductions in rank three.
- [[cex-temperley-lieb-zero-loop-generator-is-zero]]: the rank-two generator at $\delta=0$ is a nonzero nilpotent.

The TikZ diagram on the A page fixes the elementary cup-cap convention. The calculations use matching data and the proved local relations rather than relying on pictures as proof.
