# Gaussian MAC deterministic power selection

Confirmed native proof defect: choosing a codebook only by its decoding error does not ensure the deterministic codebook obeys the average power constraints. The Gaussian ensemble is finite-dimensional, not finite. The isolated replacement keeps the Statement, given data, and every dependency unchanged. It introduces strict power slack, a direct variance bound for uniform-message average energy (valid even for singleton message sets), and joint error/power selection. The same finite-receiver statement binds the existing strong-interference use of one common codebook ensemble at both receivers. No new theorem or axiom is needed. The operational conclusion uses deterministic realized codebooks whose design symbols were sampled from reduced-power Gaussian laws, followed by closure.
