  compact real form `𝔲_D` built from the same normalized system is
  `Θ`-stable (root planes permuted, `Θ(iH_α)=iH_{τα}`); the real form
  `𝔤_D=(𝔲_D∩𝔨_D)⊕i(𝔲_D∩𝔭_D)` with Cartan
  `𝔥_D=(𝔨_D⁰∩𝔥)⊕(𝔭_D⁰∩𝔥)` has `Θ` as Cartan involution, `𝔥_D` maximally
