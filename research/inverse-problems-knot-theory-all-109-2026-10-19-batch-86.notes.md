## Authored claims and conventions

- `def-colored-framed-knot-evaluation-from-ribbon-category` defines a finite oriented framed link with arbitrary object colors and its value in `End_C(1)`. The output is only a field scalar when the category is `k`-linear and `End_C(1)=k`. It assumes `ACω` through the verified mixed-color tangle evaluator, including the chosen left-dual/mixed-orientation dictionary.
- `thm-ribbon-evaluation-independent-of-colored-braid-representative` is the framed claim: two color-preserving braid closures of the same framed colored link have equal values in `End_C(1)`. It does not treat ordinary stabilization as framed equivalence.
- `thm-twist-correction-removes-framing` is the monochromatic unframed claim. It assumes `AC` for Markov classification and obtains `ACω` for the scalar stabilization lemma. It uses absolute simplicity to produce the unique nonzero scalar twist and does not divide by categorical dimension.
- `alg-ribbon-evaluation-from-colored-braid-word` accepts only finite-dimensional exact matrix realizations over an effective field. It requires exact braiding/inverse, duality, twist, and pivotal matrices; the color tuple must be invariant under the braid's endpoint permutation. It is not a procedure for an arbitrary abstract category.
- The qsl2 convention is fixed explicitly: Korinman `q_K=A²`; library `q_L=A⁻²=q_K⁻¹`; `K_L=K_K⁻¹`; the Kauffman-bracket ribbon element is selected. The fundamental positive braiding matrix is the one in `def-quantum-sl2-fundamental-ribbon-module`; the half-ribbon square gives `θ_V=−A³ id`, and the cup-cap loop gives `δ=−A²−A⁻²`. The bridge assumes `ACω` solely to use the published trace-to-framed-closure identification, then proves `t_n=δ⟨β̂⟩=δ^n(−A)^{w(β)} tr_n(π_n(β))` at `q=A⁻²`, `t=A⁻⁴`, `z_0=−1/(t+1)`, followed by the value-by-value normalized-bracket identity. It intentionally stops before a Jones-polynomial specialization.
- The B example uses the ordinary flip in finite-dimensional vector spaces; the trace is `d` to the number of permutation cycles. The B counterexample constructs `Rep_Q(C₂)` with twist given by its nontrivial group element and color `1⊕sgn`; the twist is invertible and nonscalar, while the one- and two-strand stabilization traces are 0 and 2.
