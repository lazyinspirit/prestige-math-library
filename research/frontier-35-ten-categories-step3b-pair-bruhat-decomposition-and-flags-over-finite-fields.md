## 8. Conventions, assumptions and sources

* Matrices are 1-indexed; $r_{i,j}(g)$ is the rank of the **southwest** submatrix
  (rows $i..n$, columns $1..j$), $r_{n+1,j}(g):=0$ (Dudas–Michel Example 4.5, p. 18).
* $W=N/T\cong S_n$ with $N$ the monomial subgroup and $\ell$ the inversion count of
  the one-line notation — correct at $q=2$ where $T=1$; the finite-group normalizer
  of $T$ is never used.
* $R_L^G=\mathrm{Ind}_P^G\circ\mathrm{Inf}_L^P$ and ${}^*\!R_L^G=(-)^U$ for a
  parabolic $P=L\ltimes U$; independence of the parabolic is item 18, not a
  definition.
* All arguments are finite or finite-dimensional and choice-free; the only average
  divides by $|U|$ in $\mathbb C$, and no incompatible-axiom branch is created.
* Sources read in full at the relevant arguments: Dudas–Michel, *Lectures on Finite
  Reductive Groups and Their Representations* (Example 4.5 and Lemma 4.7 p. 18;
  Example 8.4 p. 30; §§9.1–9.3 pp. 34–40; §§10.1–10.2 pp. 41–43) and Taylor,
  *Finite Reductive Groups* (pp. 37–39, 41–43, 51). The coverage rows carry the
  exact locators and item-level dispositions.
