Additional exact evidence and qualifications:

1. **Plaque confinement is an established prerequisite now.** Merely saying a path or rectangle image is connected in `U ∩ L` did not establish constant transverse coordinates. The new leaf-topology section proves that the intrinsic second-countable leaf meets a chart in at most countably many disjoint open plaques; a connected scalar image with two distinct values contains an interval, which contradicts `cor-interval-uncountable`. Local connectedness of intervals and squares then supplies intrinsic continuity. The maximal-leaf supplier's plaque atlas and inverse function theorem supply openness of every plaque. No assumption of embedded global leaves was made.
2. **The displayed representation previously had a false multiplication formula.** `def-based-loops-and-fundamental-group` explicitly defines `[a][b]=[a*b]`, traversing `a` first. The already-authored concatenation lemma explicitly gives `h_(a*b)=h_b ∘ h_a`. Consequently `[a] ↦ h_a` is an antihomomorphism in general. The repaired homomorphism is `rho([a])=h_(a^-1)`; its image and kernel are exactly those of the old forward map. The germ-valued isotropy map still sends a groupoid arrow represented by `a` to `h_a`, because its categorical product is `[b] ∘ [a]`. The suspension's forward-path germ stays `rho(gamma)^(-1)` while its homomorphic leaf representation is `germ rho(gamma)`.
3. **Ambient pullback components can actually combine distinct leaves.** Let `f:T²→T²` be `f([x,y])=[2x,y]`, a transverse local diffeomorphism, and let the target leaf be the irrational-slope leaf through `[0,0]`, parametrized by `[t,alpha*t]`. Its preimage consists of the two leaves parametrized by `[s,2alpha*s]` and `[s+1/2,2alpha*s]`. They are distinct: equality would require `alpha*(2m+1)` to be an integer. Each is dense and connected by the Kronecker argument. Their union is connected in the ambient subspace topology (a dense connected subset cannot be contained in one side of a nontrivial separation). Thus the old unqualified component statement and proof step 2.2's single transverse value `c_L` were unsound. The repaired statement retains the correct full pullback theorem with the essential intrinsic fibre-product qualification; original step 2.2 is current step 3.1.
4. **Several prior citations did not supply the claimed prerequisite.** The constant-rank kernel/image theorem does not prove that submersion level sets are submanifolds; `cor-local-normal-form-for-submersions` now supplies that step. `thm-composition-respects-homotopy` alone does not prove concatenation associativity, units or path-reversal inverses; the monodromy definition now provides explicit endpoint-fixed formulas and uses the opened fundamental-group proof. Equality of just the central plaque germs does not compare transports between distant endpoints; chart independence now compares actual transverse transition maps in small overlaps and composes them. The topological universal-cover existence theorem does not itself furnish a second-countable smooth total space; that prerequisite is established in the suspension definition.
5. **The holonomy-kernel cover proof had an ill-typed composition and missing subgroup argument.** Original step 4.1 called `p` a composite of `q_K:tilde L→widehat L` and `q:tilde L→L`; neither can be composed in that way, and `q_* pi_1(tilde L/K)` was undefined. The current proof instead constructs `q=p ∘ q_K`, verifies the sheets of `p`, and shows a lifted base loop closes exactly when its deck endpoint belongs to the `K`-orbit. The zero-dimensional leaf case is included explicitly as a singleton. This preserves the stated existence and uniqueness claim.
6. **False contract evidence was repaired too.** A constant path with distinct endpoint transversals gives a matching germ, not an identity on one space. The Kronecker contract had swapped proof-step locators and called a nonclosed leaf path a loop in the total suspension space; the path is closed only in the base. The finite-rotation contract used an unreduced denominator rather than the exact period and said all path germs were trivial. Boundary evidence now distinguishes loops from open paths, correct phases, intrinsic components, and actual countable-choice uses.
7. **A-page prose:** qualified pullback components by their intrinsic fibre-product topology; explained traversal order and inverse-loop representation; replaced “never a globally defined return map” by the precise statement that the germ invariant does not itself specify a global map. B-page prose remains unchanged. Its linear example says the group is generated by `A^-1`, which remains correct because a generator and its inverse generate the same cyclic group.
