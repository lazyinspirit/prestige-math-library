11.3. Analytic definition. We are now ready to give a definition of quasi-
conformality. An orientation preserving homeomorphism h : S →S′ between two
Riemann surfaces is called quasiconformal if
Q1. It has locally integrable distributional partial derivatives;
Q2. It has bounded dilatation, i.e., ¯∂h ≤k ∂h a.e. for some k ∈[0, 1).
Note that the second property makes sense because the first property implies
that h is differentiable a.e. in the classical sense (by Proposition 11.18).
We will often abbreviate “quasiconformal” as “qc”. A qc map h is called K-qc
if Dil h ≤K, where K = (k + 1)/(k −1) ∈[1, ∞) with k ∈[0, 1) as above.
