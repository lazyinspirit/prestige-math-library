These examples work through a trace eigenspace objective and a Krylov start-angle
bound, a strict saddle on the sphere-shaped Stiefel manifold, the continuum of
minimizers produced by a repeated eigenvalue, and complex finite-basis PC-VAR
optimization on the Grassmann quotient. The PC-VAR example takes its finite-basis model and energy-gradient formula
as logical prerequisites; its local complex geometry and trust-region argument
are fully proved here.
