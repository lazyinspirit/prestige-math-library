The mathematical design needs four precise corrections. First, AV-19's unqualified “very ample implies ample” is false if “very ample” means relative to nonaffine S but “ample” means absolute: `id_{P^1_k}` with `L=O_{P^1}` is relatively very ample through `P^0_{P^1}=P^1`, while O is not absolutely ample on P^1. The scaffold states H-very ample via a quasi-compact immersion into finite `P^n_S`, proves it implies **f-ampleness**, and gives absolute ampleness when S is affine. Second, the design puts affine standard opens before Proj's sheaf construction. The local prime/localization lemma now precedes the construction, and the scheme-chart affineness theorem follows it. Third, arbitrary relative Proj base change is proved without the design's unnecessary finite-generation hypothesis; the argument is an affine degree-zero localization/tensor calculation. Fourth, the design marks B proofs `not-applicable`; the manifest uses `ai-altered` proof provenance as required by `SCHEMA.md`, retaining the ten intended examples and counterexamples.

The grading convention is `M(n)_d=M_{n+d}`; O(1) is invertible only under a degree-one cover. `P(E)=Proj_S Sym(E)` parametrizes invertible **quotients** of E, and rank zero gives the empty projective bundle. Absolute ampleness requires quasi-compact X; relative f-ampleness is tested over every affine base open. The high-power theorem uses absolute ampleness, proper finite-type f and Noetherian S. It does not infer absolute ampleness from f-ampleness over a nonaffine base. The published `def-homogeneous-ideal-saturation` correctly defines `J:x_0^∞` for a single coordinate, which is too narrow for the irrelevant-ideal saturation `I:(x_0,…,x_n)^∞` needed here; the new local saturation lemma supplies that distinction. This is a scope mismatch, not a false published statement.

## Proof and dependency audit

Proj is built from homogeneous primes and degree-zero localizations; the prime correspondence is proved before affine gluing. Its local power test checks additive closure by raising a same-degree sum to twice the degree of the inverted element, then uses the leading homogeneous components of products to obtain ordinary primality. Empty Proj is treated as a nil-irrelevant condition, with nilpotence of the entire irrelevant ideal asserted only when it is finitely generated. The graded-module sheaf is checked on all standard opens, and degree-one local frames prove invertibility and tensor multiplication of twists. Veronese invariance tracks O(d) without claiming arbitrary twists invertible.

For `B=A[x_0,…,x_n]`, the saturation lemma tests a degree-d homogeneous h by the **degree-zero** image `h/x_i^d` on every chart. Local membership gives `x_i^{r_i}h∈I`; the finite monomial/pigeonhole argument gives a common power of `(x_0,…,x_n)` annihilating h modulo I. Stacks Lemma 27.13.7 then recovers every closed subscheme chartwise over an arbitrary base ring. These projective closed-subscheme and line-bundle zero-scheme constructions use batch-5 `lem-closed-immersion-affine-quotient-and-base-change` plus direct affine gluing; neither consumes the published affine-quotient or QC-ideal correspondence whose proof interface is defective. The zero-scheme ideal is quasi-coherent by batch-7 `thm-kernels-cokernels-qc-modules`; local equations differing by units glue quotient charts.

The extension lemma uses quasi-compactness **and** quasi-separatedness to clear finitely many denominators and overlap discrepancies for a quasi-coherent section. Serre's criterion is stated on Noetherian X: finite-type QC equals coherent there. In the reverse implication, the ideal of the reduced complement of an affine open is glued from radical affine ideals using published `lem-radical-commutes-with-localisation` and batch-7 affine-cover QC; it is coherent by Noetherianity. The affine-intersection lemma uses `Spec(Sym_R N/(s−1))` for a rank-one projective module N. Relative very ampleness implies f-ampleness because pulled-back positive-power section opens form a basis under an immersion; inside any affine neighbourhood the chosen section open is affine by that lemma. The high-power theorem follows the complete Stacks Lemma 29.40.3 route, including the Segre step for all d≫0; properness and separatedness turn the resulting immersion into a closed immersion.

AC is explicitly declared via `def-axiom-of-choice` wherever prime existence or the inherited affine sheaf, gluing, quotient and proper-morphism suppliers use it. The finite monomial saturation calculation, line-bundle section affine-intersection argument, positive-power ampleness equivalence, and other purely algebraic local steps retain choice-free proof strategies where applicable.
