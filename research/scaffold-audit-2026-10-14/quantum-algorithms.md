## Concrete repairs

- Expected query cost now uses the number of calls actually executed in each finite elementary-step prefix. An arbitrary finite step prefix need not determine `min(total queries,M)`. Simon’s query-indexed round truncation remains valid because its rounds each contain one query.
- Finite QPE now has the exact instrument `K_y=Σθ a_y(θ)Πθ`, including density operators entangled with a reference, positive-probability normalization and zero-probability branches. Its outcome probabilities are a spectral mixture; its conditional system state generally retains coherent contributions from multiple eigenspaces. The former exact-eigenspace-projection claim was false. Order finding, amplitude estimation, spectral sampling, HHL, walk phase detection and the adversary evaluator were checked against this distinction.
- Relative counting multiplies the rough dyadic scale by `20π²/ε`, rather than dividing by it. The final scale is rounded upward to a power of two, matching the available binary QFT. The `L=2` rough kernel is handled directly. Integer rounding uses a certified interval and a `2/3` window, following the source’s computable rounding convention; no exact half-integer decision from arbitrary real descriptions is assumed. The displayed zero-count implementation costs `O(√N/ε)` uniformly in ε; the source’s `Θ(√N)` wording treats ε as fixed. Absolute additive count error `ε_c` requires worst-case `O(N/ε_c)` accesses, whereas fraction error ε requires `O(1/ε)`.
- A median of estimates from freshly reprepared superpositions is not a high-confidence sample of one eigenvalue. The median procedure now specifies one fixed eigenstate/eigenspace; the general single-run sampler retains the full spectral law. The shared amplitude/expectation medians continue to estimate the same fixed scalar and consume the actual published independent-Bernoulli additive-tail supplier.
- QLSP input `b` belongs to the codomain/left singular space of a rectangular matrix. The Hermitian dilation has duplicated nonzero singular values and minimum-norm output `(0,A⁺b)`. QSVT inversion acts on the **adjoint** projected-unitary encoding with swapped projectors. Its success multiplier on the unnormalized matrix is `γ/2`, equivalently `δ/2` on `A/α`; the old success probability missed α.
- The Hermitian QSP signal has determinant −1, so a length-d sequence has determinant `(−1)^d`; diagonal/off-diagonal conjugation signs were repaired. A new explicit 2×2 convention-conversion lemma relates it to the Gilyén SU(2) source signal and tracks the scalar under control. QSVT now has a finite side-switching recurrence for both parities, including d=0 and real-polynomial extraction. The alleged SVD counterexample changing both singular vectors’ signs was invalid; its replacement independently changes a zero-singular left vector, demonstrating the actual nonzero-nullspace-coefficient ambiguity.
- OAA has two W calls, one W† and **two**, not four, reflections. Its exact block polynomial and controlled phase correction are explicit. A new robust lemma proves the full clean-input **isometry** error, including ancilla leakage, with a local finite-matrix argument. Taylor simulation allocates a quadratic Taylor budget, uses fresh coefficient registers, and proves approximate rather than exact auxiliary cleanup. No full-space operator error on arbitrary dirty coefficient inputs is inferred from a projected block.
- Approved higher-order simulation is preserved by three new proposed contracts: finite Suzuki recursion, its local cancellation lemma and an explicit conservative high-order error/resource theorem. Signed time coefficients, inverse access, recursion termination, actual expanded term counts and derivative-remainder constants are stated. The recursion is not left solely as a remark.
- The five-local adiabatic path now spells out unary-clock legality, each interior/boundary propagation term, all-zero input penalty, initial clock penalty, padding and the path. Propagation alone has a `2^n`-dimensional ground space; the input penalty selects the unique intended history. Clock perturbation no longer double-counts J. Path discretization has explicit q, coefficient precision and per-segment error budgets.
- Other focused repairs include the OR approximant’s grid normalization from `[−ε,1+ε]` to `[0,1]`; the exact bit-dependent tensor label needed for adversary equal-output constraints; the Johnson-walk restriction `r≥2`; angular versus fractional walk phases; unknown-marked averaged versus known-marked pure-state one-query distinguishability; rationalization thresholds `a+τ,b−τ`; an explicit F₂ pivot/elimination procedure; a charged Fredkin swap-test circuit; exact Clifford+T Toffoli compilation by its eight-case parity identity; and response preparation’s two gates in Grover accounting.

The five added suppliers are `lem-qa-21-robust-one-step-oaa-isometry`, `alg-qa-20-suzuki-recursive-product-formula`, `lem-qa-20-suzuki-order-cancellation`, `thm-qa-20-suzuki-conservative-error-and-resources`, and `lem-qa-23-hermitian-signal-convention-conversion`.
