## Mathematical route, axioms, and limitations

- Definition: alpha:Y->(0,infinity) is fixed for the operator and reconstruction family and cannot take the exact datum or a valid noise bound delta as input.
- Veto proof: for fixed y the exact observation z=y belongs to every noise ball. Since alpha(y) is independent of delta, universal convergence forces R_{alpha(y)}y=K^dagger y. Applying the same identity at nearby exact data and the convergence bound at y gives an epsilon-delta continuity proof on D(K^dagger), contradicting the stated discontinuity. No sequence-selection axiom is used in this implication.
- The Hilbert pseudoinverse supplier uses Countable Choice; its consumers retain that assumption. The source-ball theorem and SVD also use Countable Choice. No use of full AC is made or inferred. Finite-dimensional L-curve calculations are choice-free.
- The noise-underestimation witness uses Kx=(x_j/j), its finite-rank truncations, and the data-only rule alpha(z)=||z||_2^2 off zero. At exact datum zero, noise z_j=e_j/j has level 1/j, while the proxy parameter is 1/j^2; the truncated inverse returns e_j, so the error stays 1.
- The restricted-class leaf uses the power-source ball and its modulus. It demonstrates conditional stability on that class while K^dagger remains discontinuous on its full domain; it does not assert a universal data-only selection theorem.
