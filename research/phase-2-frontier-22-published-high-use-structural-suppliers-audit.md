## Topological interfaces

`def-subspace-topology-top` gives a canonical ambient open set tracing to each
relative open, so its arbitrary-union verification does not select a family of
representatives. Complements, traced bases and subbases, the initial property
of the inclusion, continuity into a subspace and restriction of maps all follow
from the displayed trace identities. Its two aliases refer to the same item.

The compactly generated chain was read through its exact used clauses.
`def-compactly-generated-conventions-for-based-homotopy` coherently fixes WH,
kification, k-products and test-image compact-open mapping spaces.
`thm-quotient-universal-property` proves descent and composite quotient maps
without a family choice: selecting a preimage of one fixed point in the
uniqueness argument is ordinary existential instantiation.

`lem-kification-compact-tests-and-finite-constructions` proves that the
k-closed sets form a topology, compact Hausdorff tests are unchanged, CG-source
maps lift through kification, and finite k-products have the categorical
property. For `X times I`, a closed interval neighbourhood and compact
projection turn k-closedness into ordinary closedness. The same compact-test
argument proves the closed-subspace and finite-coproduct clauses.

`lem-compact-test-exponential-law-and-products-of-quotients` proves evaluation
continuity using a closed compact test neighbourhood, currying by the tube
lemma, and the mapping-space homeomorphism by iterated evaluation/currying. Its
product-of-quotients proof forms the quotient by the explicit fibre relation,
then constructs the inverse by quotient descent and uncurrying; it never
chooses fibre representatives.

`lem-weak-hausdorff-diagonals-and-closed-quotients` correctly converts WH to a
closed k-diagonal. Compact test images are Hausdorff because the test map is a
closed compact quotient and disjoint fibres separate in the normal compact
domain. Product quotientness then gives the closed-relation criterion, and
point evaluations make the mapping-space diagonal closed.

`lem-compact-generation-preserves-the-cylinder-and-closed-pushouts` writes the
pushout fibre relation on the four coproduct-product pieces. The two graph
pieces and the equal-fibre piece are inverse images of the target diagonal;
closedness of the attaching subspace promotes them to closed pieces. The
closed-relation criterion proves CGWH, while the quotient universal property
proves both the pushout and the stated pullback. The closed-embedding formula
for the target summand is exact.

Finally, `lem-interval-exponential-law-and-quotient-homotopies` proves the
ordinary locally compact interval exponential law directly. It applies that
law to Sierpinski characteristic functions to prove that an arbitrary quotient
map remains quotient after product with `I`. The kified, based and relative
clauses then use precisely the preceding CG/CGWH suppliers. No WH hypothesis is
silently imported into the arbitrary-space branch.

Classification: eight new bounded no-repair-needed dispositions.

## Probability, algebra, measure and set theory interfaces

`thm-taking-out-what-is-known` first proves the conditional-expectation identity
for indicators and bounded simple factors. Dominated simple approximation gives
the bounded case. For finite unbounded `Z`, the bounded identity applied to
`R_n=|Z| wedge n`, followed by MCT, proves `E[|Z|V]=E|ZX|` for
`V=E[|X||G]`; the conditional modulus bound therefore proves `ZU` integrable.
Truncation and DCT then establish the general clause. Its explicit AC
hypothesis covers conditional-expectation existence.

`def-products-and-coproducts` is exactly the limit and colimit of a discrete
diagram and states the correct pairing and copairing equations, including the
empty and singleton consequences without asserting existence.

`thm-free-modules-are-projective-with-choice-boundary` identifies each nonempty
fibre of the surjection over a basis value, uses AC only to choose one element
from the arbitrary family, and extends the assignment by the free-module
universal property. A supplied finite enumeration reduces the choice to the
published finite-choice lemma, and the empty basis is explicit.

`thm-generated-sigma-algebra-exists-and-is-minimal` checks every sigma-algebra
axiom under a nonempty intersection. The power set supplies a canonical member
of the family of containing sigma-algebras, so the generated intersection is
defined and minimal without choice.

`thm-ultrafilter-characterisation` constructs the filter generated by one set
using the explicit family of supersets of `C intersection A`. If the complement
is absent then every such basic intersection is nonempty, so maximality forces
the set into the ultrafilter. The converse uses properness to exclude the
complement for each member of a larger filter. No ultrafilter-existence
principle is used by this characterization.

Classification: five new bounded no-repair-needed dispositions.
