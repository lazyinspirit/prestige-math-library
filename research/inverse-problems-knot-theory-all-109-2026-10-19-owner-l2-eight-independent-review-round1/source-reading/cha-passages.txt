Now we introduce a lemma which makes crucial use of Strebel’s class. Let R be a com-
mutative ring with unity. Recall that, a group G is said to be in D(R) if a homomorphism
f : M →N between projective RG-modules is injective whenever 1R ⊗f : R⊗M →R⊗N
is injective [Str74]. Reformulating this deﬁnition slightly, we have: a group G is in D(R)
if and only if for any homomorphism f : M →N between RG-modules with N projective,
the restriction of f on a projective submodule P ⊂M is injective whenever the induced
map R ⊗P →R ⊗N is injective.
Obviously a homomorphism of an R-free module, say RI, into R ⊗M lifts to a homo-
morphism of the RG-free module (RG)I →M. Thus, we have the following consequence:
Lemma 3.9. Suppose G is in D(R). Then for any homomorphism f : M →N between
RG-modules with N projective, the maximal rank of RG-free submodules in Im f is greater
than or equal to the maximal rank of R-free submodules in Im{1R ⊗f : R ⊗M →R ⊗N}.
Combining the above results obtained from amenability and Strebel’s condition, we ob-
tain the following L2-dimension estimates. (cf. [CH05, Lemma 4.4] for algebraic analogues
