## Examples

These examples compute the pseudo-arclength corrector through
$x^2-\lambda=0$, show why the natural parameter turns at the fold, exhibit a
zero-angle singular bordered matrix, and verify that a valid finite local
trace can miss a disconnected solution component. They preserve the local
correctness and global-completeness limits of the A-page results.
