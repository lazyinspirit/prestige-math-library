These examples separate three effects: resampling a rejected Gaussian
increment changes its distribution; bridge splitting couples coarse and fine
Euler paths while preserving the parent increment; and a Brownian bridge can
cross a barrier even when both observed endpoints lie above it. They illustrate
the path and selection issues covered by the A page and make no additional
convergence claim.
