## Statement

If $\mathcal A$ is an algebra on $X$, then $m_X(\mathcal A)$ is closed under
binary intersections.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** An algebra $\mathcal A$ on $X$ and $\mathcal M:=m_X(\mathcal A)$.

[L1] The family $\mathcal M$ is the smallest monotone class containing $\mathcal A$ ([[lem-generated-monotone-class-exists-and-is-minimal]]).

[L2] Every member of $\mathcal M$ intersects every member of $\mathcal A$ in a member of $\mathcal M$ ([[lem-monotone-good-sets-for-algebra-elements]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Fix $B\in\mathcal M$ and put $\mathcal C_B:=\{E\in\mathcal M:E\cap B\in\mathcal M\}$. As before, intersection with $B$ commutes with increasing unions and decreasing intersections, so $\mathcal C_B$ is a monotone class. By symmetry of intersection and [L2], every $E\in\mathcal A$ lies in $\mathcal C_B$. [L1, L2, algebra]

2.1 Minimality in [L1] gives $\mathcal M\subseteq\mathcal C_B$. Since $B$ was arbitrary, $E\cap B\in\mathcal M$ for all $E,B\in\mathcal M$. [step 1.1, L1] ∎
