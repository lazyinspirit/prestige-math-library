## Statement

Assume AC, let $h>0$ and $x,y\in\mathbb R$, and let $\mu_{x,y}^h$ be the
law on $C([0,h])$ of the bridge in
[[def-scalar-brownian-bridge-from-x-to-y]]. If $X_s=x+W_s$ is Brownian motion
started at $x$, then for every Borel path event $A\subseteq C([0,h])$ and
Borel set $D\subseteq\mathbb R$,
$$\mathbb P\{X\in A,\ X_h\in D\}= \int_D p_h(y-x)\,\mu_{x,y}^h(A)\,dy,$$
where $p_t(z)=(2\pi t)^{-1/2}e^{-z^2/(2t)}$. In particular this specifies
the endpoint-conditioned bridge law for every endpoint $y$. For every
$0\le t<h$, the restriction of $\mu_{x,y}^h$ to path events up to time $t$
has density
$$\frac{p_{h-t}(y-X_t)}{p_h(y-x)}$$
relative to Brownian motion $X$ started at $x$ on $[0,t]$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC; $h>0$; endpoints $x,y\in\mathbb R$; and the bridge process of [[def-scalar-brownian-bridge-from-x-to-y]].

[F1] Brownian motion has independent centered normal increments with variance equal to elapsed time, and its finite-dimensional vectors are Gaussian with covariance $\min(s,t)$. [[def-brownian-motion]]

[F2] Linear images of jointly Gaussian vectors are jointly Gaussian; uncorrelated Gaussian blocks are independent, as follows by factorization of their Gaussian characteristic function. [[def-multivariate-normal-law]] [[lem-characteristic-function-of-a-multivariate-normal-law]] [[lem-characteristic-functions-under-affine-maps-and-independent-sums]]

[F3] The Brownian transition density is $p_t(y-x)$ for $t>0$, and these densities satisfy the convolution identity $\int_{\mathbb R}p_s(z-x)p_t(y-z)dz=p_{s+t}(y-x)$. [[lem-brownian-transition-semigroup-property]]

[F4] On $C([0,h])$ with the uniform topology, the Borel sigma-algebra is generated by coordinate evaluations, and finite rational-coordinate cylinders form a generating pi-system. This follows from the cited result on $C([0,\infty))$ by extending each path constantly after $h$: that extension is a homeomorphism onto the closed subspace of paths constant after $h$, and coordinates after $h$ equal the coordinate at $h$. Continuity makes the rational coordinates in $[0,h]$ generate the endpoint coordinate as well. [[lem-borel-sigma-algebra-of-continuous-path-space-is-generated-by-coordinates]] [[thm-dynkin-pi-lambda]]

[A1] AC is inherited from the Brownian and Gaussian interfaces; the explicit bridge process and the countable cylinder argument add no further choice. [[def-axiom-of-choice]]

## Proof

1.1 Write $\beta_s=W_s-(s/h)W_h$. For any finite times $s_1,\ldots,s_k\in[0,h]$, [F1] makes $(\beta_{s_1},\ldots,\beta_{s_k},W_h)$ jointly Gaussian. Direct covariance calculation gives $\operatorname{Cov}(\beta_{s_i},W_h)=s_i-(s_i/h)h=0$ and $\operatorname{Cov}(\beta_s,\beta_t)=\min(s,t)-st/h$. Hence [F2] makes every finite vector of bridge residual coordinates independent of $W_h$. The countable generating coordinates in [F4] then make the entire continuous residual path independent of $W_h$. Therefore conditioning on $W_h=y-x$ gives exactly the explicit bridge process from the definition, for each $y$. [F1, F2, F4, given, algebra]

2.1 If $t=0$, the restricted bridge path is the constant initial value $x$ and the density ratio is $p_h(y-x)/p_h(y-x)=1$. Now take $0<t<h$ and fix $0=s_0<s_1<\cdots<s_k=t$. By independent increments and [F3], the joint density of $X_{s_1},\ldots,X_{s_k},X_h$ at $z_1,\ldots,z_k,y$ is $p_{s_1}(z_1-x)\prod_{i=2}^kp_{s_i-s_{i-1}}(z_i-z_{i-1}) p_{h-t}(y-z_k).$ Dividing by the strictly positive endpoint density $p_h(y-x)$ gives the conditional bridge density for these coordinates. By step 1.1 this is the finite-dimensional law of the bridge in the definition. The same expression is the Brownian path density on $[0,t]$ multiplied by $p_{h-t}(y-X_t)/p_h(y-x)$ when the coordinate at time $t$ is included; integrating out that last coordinate uses the convolution identity [F3]. [F1, F3, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Thus the restriction-density identity holds for every finite cylinder event on $[0,t]$. The density is nonnegative and integrates to one by [F3]. The class of path events for which the identity holds is a lambda-system containing the generating cylinder pi-system of [F4]; Dynkin's pi-lambda theorem therefore extends the identity to all Borel path events on $[0,t]$. This proves the restriction assertion. [F3, F4, step 2.1]

4.1 By step 1.1, the joint law of the residual bridge path and endpoint $W_h$ is the product of the bridge residual law and the $N(0,h)$ endpoint density. Applying the measurable map $(\beta,z)\mapsto(x+(s/h)z+\beta_s)_{0\le s\le h}$ and translating $z=y-x$ gives the stated full-path disintegration. The endpoint density is continuous and positive for every $y$ since $h>0$. The degenerate cases $t=0$ and a one-coordinate cylinder follow from the same formulas; $t=h$ is excluded from the restriction statement. AC is used only through [A1]. [A1, F1, step 1.1, step 3.1, given] ∎
