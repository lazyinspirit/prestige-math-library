## Statement

If $\mathcal A$ is an algebra of subsets of $X$, then the generated monotone
class $m_X(\mathcal A)$ is closed under complements relative to $X$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** An algebra $\mathcal A$ on $X$ and $\mathcal M:=m_X(\mathcal A)$.

[L1] An algebra is closed under complements ([[def-algebra-of-subsets]]).

[L2] The family $\mathcal M$ is the smallest monotone class containing $\mathcal A$ ([[lem-generated-monotone-class-exists-and-is-minimal]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Put $\mathcal C:=\{E\in\mathcal M:X\setminus E\in\mathcal M\}$. If $(E_n)$ increases in $\mathcal C$, then $X\setminus E_n$ decreases, so monotone closure of $\mathcal M$ places both $\bigcup_nE_n$ and its complement $\bigcap_n(X\setminus E_n)$ in $\mathcal M$. The decreasing case is the same with union and intersection interchanged. Hence $\mathcal C$ is a monotone class. [L2, algebra]

2.1 If $E\in\mathcal A$, then $X\setminus E\in\mathcal A\subseteq\mathcal M$ by [L1] and [L2], so $\mathcal A\subseteq\mathcal C$. Minimality in [L2] gives $\mathcal M\subseteq\mathcal C$, which is the asserted complement closure. [step 1.1, L1, L2] ∎
