## Statement

Let $\mathcal E,\mathcal F\subseteq\mathcal P(X)$. If

$$\mathcal E\subseteq\sigma_X(\mathcal F)\qquad\text{and}\qquad\mathcal F\subseteq\sigma_X(\mathcal E),$$

then $\sigma_X(\mathcal E)=\sigma_X(\mathcal F)$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** Families $\mathcal E,\mathcal F\subseteq\mathcal P(X)$ satisfying the two displayed inclusions.

[L1] Generated sigma-algebras are monotone in their generators and idempotent ([[thm-generated-sigma-algebra-calculus]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 From $\mathcal E\subseteq\sigma_X(\mathcal F)$, monotonicity and idempotence in [L1] give $\sigma_X(\mathcal E)\subseteq\sigma_X(\sigma_X(\mathcal F))=\sigma_X(\mathcal F)$. [given, L1]

2.1 Interchanging $\mathcal E$ and $\mathcal F$ gives $\sigma_X(\mathcal F)\subseteq\sigma_X(\mathcal E)$; together with step 1.1 this proves equality. [step 1.1, given, L1] ∎
