These examples work through one streaming least-squares update, a bounded-noise scalar Robbins–Monro root, and a biased simulator gradient. Together they distinguish conditional unbiasedness, almost-sure convergence with square-summable but nonsummable step sizes, and the nonzero stationarity floor caused by persistent bias.
