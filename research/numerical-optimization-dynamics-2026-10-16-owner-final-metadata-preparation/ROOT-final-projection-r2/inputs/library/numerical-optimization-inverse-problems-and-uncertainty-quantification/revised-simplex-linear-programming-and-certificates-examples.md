## Objective

The examples trace a one-pivot primal optimum and dual certificate, verify an exact degenerate six-pivot cycle and Bland's terminating path, exhibit a negative-cost unbounded ray, compute a positive Phase I optimum with an explicit Farkas vector, and compare a small residual with the error and gap at an ill-conditioned basis.
