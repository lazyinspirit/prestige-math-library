## Mathematical review and corrections made in this attempt

The draft carried a genuine convention error in the local adjoint formulas, plus incomplete axiom bookkeeping and two well-definedness gaps. All corrections were derived from the library's own published conventions and re-checked against the sources.

1. **Hodge-star convention pinned to the library's items (A: item 2).** `prop-riemannian-metrics-induce-metrics-on-dual-tensor-and-exterior-bundles` gives the determinant-normalised induced metric ($\langle d\bar z,d\bar z\rangle_g=2/\rho$ for $g=\rho(dx^2+dy^2)$) and Looijenga 3.25 fixes the conjugate-linear star on complex forms. The E-valued star $\star_E$ is therefore the conjugate-linear metric identification into $E^*$ ($\star_E:C^\infty(\Lambda^{0,q}\otimes E)\to C^\infty(\Lambda^{0,1-q}\otimes E^*)$, $s\wedge\star_Et=\langle s,t\rangle\mathrm dV_g$; $\star_E(u\,d\bar z\otimes e)=-i\psi\bar u\,dz\otimes e^*$), which is exactly Demailly's $\#$ (3.3′),(3.6); the scalar $\star$ is the $\mathbb C$-linear extension. `def-dual-and-hom-vector-bundles` was added to the deps.
2. **Corrected local formulas (A: item 5).** The draft's $\bar\partial_E^*(u\,d\bar z\otimes e)=-(1/(\rho\psi))\partial_z(\rho\psi u)e$ contradicts the library's adjointness with the induced metric: it fails the consistency test $\langle\Delta''_1u,u\rangle=\|\bar\partial_E^*u\|^2$ for nonconstant weight. The correct formula is
   $$\bar\partial_E^*(u\,d\bar z\otimes e)=-\frac{2}{\rho\psi}\,\frac{\partial(\psi u)}{\partial z}\,e,\qquad \Delta''_0f=-\frac{2}{\rho\psi}\frac{\partial}{\partial z}\Bigl(\psi\frac{\partial f}{\partial\bar z}\Bigr),\qquad \sigma_{\Delta''}=\tfrac{1}{2\rho}|\xi|^2_{\mathrm{eucl}}=\tfrac12|\xi|^2_g .$$
   It was verified twice: by direct weak adjointness ($\int\psi f_{\bar z}\bar u\,(2/\rho)\rho=$ IBP) and by the star relation $\bar\partial\star_E=-\star_E\bar\partial^*$ with $\star_E^{-1}=-\star_E$; the $\rho$-inside error would have made the item-12 duality false for nonconstant $\rho$, so this was a substantive repair, not bookkeeping. The strategy text and the A6 principal matrix ($\tfrac12\rho^{-1}\mathrm{Id}$) were synchronised.
3. **$\bar\partial$ on forms made explicit (A: item 1).** The page uses $\bar\partial$ on $E$-valued forms of type $(p,q)$ (item 5's star formula, item 12's $F=K\otimes E^*$). Item 1 now states the coefficientwise extension in holomorphic frames, its frame-independence, $\bar\partial^2=0$ and the graded Leibniz rule; divisor finiteness was corrected to "locally finite, finite when $X$ is compact" (compactness is not a standing hypothesis of that definition). The stable id and statement scope are unchanged.
4. **Item 12 stated in the page's own vocabulary.** The draft asserted a "harmonic space $H^{1,0}(E^*)$" that the page never defines. Part 2 now identifies $E^*$-valued $(1,0)$-forms with sections of $F=K\otimes E^*$, uses $\Delta''_F$ (item 4 applied to $F$ with the induced metric), states $\#\Delta''_E=\Delta''_F\#$ on smooth forms, and proves the harmonic-image statement by the local conditions ($\bar\partial_E^*u=0\iff\psi u$ anti-holomorphic $\iff\bar\partial_F(\#u)=0$). Deps extended by items 1, 4, 5; the duality, nondegeneracy ($\int s\wedge\#s=\|s\|^2$) and conjugation clauses are unchanged.
5. **Block domain of $\Delta''$ (A: item 4).** The draft's diagonal-operator domain mixed the two components; it now reads $\{u_0\in\operatorname{dom}\bar D,\ \bar Du_0\in\operatorname{dom}\bar D^*,\ u_1\in\operatorname{dom}\bar D^*,\ \bar D^*u_1\in\operatorname{dom}\bar D\}$.
6. **Axiom bookkeeping.** The page's Sobolev/compactness/regularity chain consumes published suppliers that assume the Axiom of Choice (`lem-bounded-restriction-and-cutoff-localisation-in-sobolev-spaces`, `thm-local-lp-compactness-of-w-one-p-bounded-sequences`, `thm-extension-theorem-for-bounded-smooth-domains`, `thm-higher-order-sobolev-embedding`, `thm-fredholm-alternative-for-identity-minus-compact`), while Countable Choice suffices for the completions/partitions. Accordingly items 4, 5, 6 changed from CC to AC, and items 10, 11, 12 and the five B examples — which already stated or inherited AC — now link `[[def-axiom-of-choice]]`, carry `def-axiom-of-choice` in `deps`, and name the exact use; item 1 and item 3 remain choice-free, item 2 keeps CC only. Consumers of these items (in particular batch 10's Riemann–Roch page) must carry the same assumption.
7. **B-page wording.** The flat-torus and metric-independence computations were aligned with the corrected adjoint formula (the kernel statements $\partial_zu=0$, $u=ce^{-\varphi}$ are unchanged), the coefficient of a harmonic $(0,1)$-form is correctly called anti-holomorphic, the sphere and non-harmonic examples name their AC use, and the constant-zero-mode strategy now uses the open/closed connectedness argument (a Riemann surface is connected by definition) instead of a free-standing identity-theorem citation.
