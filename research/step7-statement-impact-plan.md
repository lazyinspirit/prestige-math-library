# Statement-triggered consumer impact and Step-7 scope

## Scope and propagation contract

Frozen frontier membership in `research/<run>-step7-v2/frontier.json` governs
Step-7 repair, adjudication, rejudgment and item gates. Published frontier items
follow that protocol normally. Every outside consumer, published or draft,
uses separate maintenance. Additions never enlarge the frozen denominator.
Outside diagnostic exclusions preserve raw evidence and do not attest a
mathematical pass; global integrity, runtime and unknown errors still block.

Every actual change to an original `## Statement` or `## Definition`,
published or draft, creates a direct-consumer event. Compare section content
directly, including lemma and corollary statements. Proof-only, citation,
dependency and metadata edits do not propagate. Examine direct dependency/
reference consumers and explicit affected uses, make only necessary minimal
repairs, and leave sound consumers unchanged with evidence. Continue another
hop only when a necessary consumer repair changes its own interface. Never
expand blanket transitive closures through unchanged statements.

## Separate maintenance and certification

After frontier writers drain, three disjoint parallel maintenance lanes handle
outside consumers before central certification. Durable evidence binds each
supplier-interface event and consumer; process that pair once. Gates, judging
and unrelated context changes cannot reopen it. A genuine later interface
event creates a new obligation. Outside maintenance never becomes Step-7
repair, adjudication, rejudgment or item-gate work.

A maintenance repair identifies the exact `affected_use`,
`invalidated_claim`, `minimality` argument and before/after edit snippets.
Reconstruction against the frozen input rejects unreported changes. This
checks edit accounting rather than mathematical necessity; agents must
understand the argument, consult sources when unsure and report uncertainty.
Maintenance grants no unrelated edits or new items. Candidate records do not
establish completion.

Necessary maintenance statement changes emit further direct-consumer events.
A cascade can return to frontier items, which then receive ordinary frontier
owner work. Complete frontier repair/review and all required separate
maintenance before central certification; all writers drain before it begins.
Three frontier owner lanes remain parallel in 7.2, 7.6, 7.9 and continuations.
Use short shared-metadata locks, disjoint items and fresh frozen assignments.
Before 7.9, genuine unmet prerequisites may be fully authored and registered
with honest source and author-origin evidence. They remain outside the frozen
rejudgment/gate scope. 7.9 permits no additions.
