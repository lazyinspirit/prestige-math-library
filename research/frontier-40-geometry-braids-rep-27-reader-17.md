In the one-point counterexample the stabilizer is G_m under both linearizations, so both stable loci are empty. This is a false page claim, not a proof-step omission, and is routed as fatal under the dispatch's defect policy. Proposed repair for the Step 5b lead: say both examples change the semistable locus and quotient, and that the projective-line example also changes the stable locus.
