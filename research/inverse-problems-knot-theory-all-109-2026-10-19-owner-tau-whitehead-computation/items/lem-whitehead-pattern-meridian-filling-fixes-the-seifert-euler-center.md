## Statement

Assume Countable Choice. Use the actual positive Whitehead pattern in V=S1_theta times D2_(s,z), with ambient orientation ds wedge dtheta wedge dz, admitted by the explicit five-handle ribbon model. Retain its actual annulus 0<=s<=1,z=0 and positive band B(s,u)=(s,u cos(2pi s),C sin(pi s)-u sin(2pi s)). Fill its outer boundary by the longitude gamma, and its inner boundary by the meridian N. Use the resulting actual S3 Heegaard diagram H1, obtained by deleting lambda from the ribbon list. Its three selected matchings are x1={n,d,h,p,f}, x2={n,c,g,r,f}, x3={n,c,g,q,e}.

Take the short oriented N edge g to h. Let P be the face incident to its LEFT bank and Q the face incident to its RIGHT bank. For the marked order w in P,z in Q, and F oriented by MINUS ds wedge dtheta on its annular part, the actual negative-Morse, zero-Euler-cap Alexander values are
$$A_F(x_1)=A_F(x_2)=-1,\qquad A_F(x_3)=0.$$
For the swapped order, orient the same surface by PLUS ds wedge dtheta on its annular part; the values are +1,+1,0. In particular the zero anchor at x3 is an actual Seifert-field evaluation, independent of either a homology rank or a printed pattern label.

This selected partial-pattern value is retained under the following actual companion replacement. Keep its Morse and selected-trajectory data on a neighborhood of F compactly inside V; replace the outer longitude filling by the admitted companion exterior and its punctured-torus product compression disks, connecting the same partial-pattern trajectory ports through that outer collar. Any full glued generator obtained by this port extension has the SAME evaluation on F as the corresponding selected partial-pattern matching above. Determining which full closed-diagram states have these partial matchings, their differential and their top Alexander selector remains a separate finite gluing calculation. This lemma supplies no such count or selector.

There is also a concrete CHANGED-pairing application, not covered merely by that unchanged-port assertion. In the admitted connected-surface companion model use the actual small companion triangle with corners Q=m_comp intersect l_comp, y0=m_comp intersect delta1, x0=l_comp intersect delta1. Use the notation m for the pattern crossing on alpha1 as in the ribbon model. The actual meridian-side matching M has changing coordinates (n,e,y0), and the longitude-side matching L has (a,m,x0); both share (c,g,q) and all remaining companion coordinates. Then A(L)=A(M)=0. The proof uses the actual two-face chain F1+F6 and the companion triangle, not an assertion that the n,e tubes stayed fixed after they were replaced by a,m. No differential coefficient or count for this hexagon is claimed.

## Proof

1.1 Perform the literal cancellation rule of [F1] at alpha3/beta3=f, clearing q in direction plus; at alpha4/beta4=c, clearing h then k in directions minus,plus; at alpha2/beta2=p, clearing g then l in directions plus,minus. Retain subscripts on copied crossings. The resulting curves are G=(n), X=(d_k,d_h,e_q_g,e_q_l,r_g,r_l,m), N=(r_g,e_q_g,d_h), J=(m,n,d_k,r_l,e_q_l). Cancel G/J at n by its dual alpha slides, with no beta slide. They delete the J points and give X=(D,E,R)=(d_h,e_q_g,r_g), N=(R,E,D), with surface signs (-,+,+). The selected tracks are respectively (ndhp, ncgr, ncge_q), then (nd_hp, ngr, nge_q), then (nd_h,nr_g,ne_q_g), then (D,R,E). Here the first entries are after deleting the selected f, and each subsequent replacement is the actual band rule: a selected pivot is removed; otherwise the selected Ai point p and Bj point q are replaced by the copied q_p after the new pivot is cancelled. This is the smooth nearest trajectory transport in the product handle coordinates. The bands miss the interior of the short N edge, which becomes E to D. Its two incident faces, hence both marked points, are retained. Directly following the finite outgoing germs gives two two-edge faces and one eight-edge face; the two short cycles are (E,X-),(D,N-) and (R,X+),(D,N+). The eight-edge face contains the other bank of E to D. These lists prove the finite reduction; the accompanying independent rational/dart checker verifies them but is not a Chern argument. [F1, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 Cancel the EMPTY R-D bigon by a small N isotopy, keeping E followed and both marks fixed. Its face contains neither bank mark. Every slide and cancelling pair in step 1.1 and this empty bigon is supported off the marks. Carry the actual bridge arcs by their product coordinates. After the bigon cancellation the genus-one S3 diagram has its sole intersection E. Its complement is a disk containing both marks. In each cut ball use the supplied boundary-parallel disk to carry its one bridge arc to a thin push of one common arc in that disk: straighten its witness disk to a rectangle, carry the core to its boundary edge and use the inverse rectangle coordinates. This isotopy fixes the compression faces and endpoints. The two bridge arcs now bound the thin doubled disk. The once-intersecting handle pair cancels in a ball disjoint from this doubled disk and knot, leaving the genus-zero unknot model. Choose its negative min/max field tangent to the disk, with its disk normal a nonzero perpendicular section. Its exterior relative Euler obstruction is zero; the explicit filling cap also has Euler obstruction zero by [F2]. All selected-trajectory changes at the cancelled handle pair are supported in a ball, whose relative degree-two group H2(B3,S2;Z) is zero. The followed E and its preimage x3 therefore have A=0. This remains the value for the carried annulus-plus-band surface: both are Seifert surfaces of the same filled unknot, and [F2]'s actual exterior H2=0 calculation makes their capped evaluations equal. No assertion that the annulus-plus-band surface is a minimal surface has been made. [F1, F2, step 1.1, construct, algebra]

3.1 Determine the signs from the actual surface orientation. The face walker used in step 1.1 follows an edge and then the NEXT POSITIVE outgoing germ. At its arriving vertex the reverse incoming germ points backward; the next counterclockwise germ turns the forward motion to its right. Thus its listed cycles keep the face on the RIGHT and are CLOCKWISE, not the positive boundary orientation of the disk. Reverse the first bigon cycle. Its positive alpha boundary goes D to E, so its smooth Whitney class has input D, output E. For w in its face P and z in the eight-edge face Q it has n_w=1,n_z=0. Hence [F2] gives A(D)-A(E)=-1. The positive alpha boundary of the empty R-D bigon goes D to R, and its two degrees vanish, so A(D)=A(R). Step 2.1 now gives (-1,0,-1) for (D,E,R). This calculation uses a smooth bigon class and the finite field cochain, not a holomorphic count. Swapping the ordered marks gives the negative table by [F2]. In particular reading the clockwise face list as a positive source boundary would produce the incorrect opposite sign. [F1, F2, step 1.1, step 2.1, construct, algebra]

4.1 Check which oriented product surface goes with these labels. Write F_geom for the annulus-plus-band surface whose annular orientation is ds wedge dtheta. At theta=pi on the s=1 core tube its boundary runs in positive theta. In the actual tube coordinates (theta,phi), N runs in positive phi and Sigma=boundary(V minus H) has orientation minus dtheta wedge dphi. Thus its LEFT bank P lies at theta=pi+epsilon and its RIGHT bank Q at theta=pi-epsilon. With w in P,z in Q the alpha bridge w to z is the SHORT negative-theta bridge across this N interval. The beta bridge MUST avoid the beta compression disk bounded by N; it is the LONG negative-theta return from z to w. Their union therefore has negative core orientation, so its Seifert surface is -F_geom. This distinction is essential: identifying the short across-N arc as the beta bridge would give the wrong orientation, since that arc would cross its own compression disk. Swapping the labels gives the positive core orientation and F_geom. Global reversal of both boundary strands retains the positive clasp. Together with step3.1 this gives the stated table for each actual marked order. This local core calculation fixes the chosen port convention; comparison with a different externally supplied marked knot still requires its actual marked-bridge identification. [F1, F2, step 1.1, step 3.1, construct, algebra]

5.1 Finally perform the actual outer port replacement. Choose a closed neighborhood U of F inside the interior of V, avoiding the outer collar. The finite C compression disks and the internal legs of the selected matching have already specified the Morse cancellation field on U. Keep these coordinates and field there. Retain all internal C legs, including those leaving U but terminating at an internal critical point. Cut ONLY the legs with prescribed outer boundary ports at their outer collar section and retain those labelled ports. In [F1]'s product (T2 minus intD2) times I collar the two actual compression disks are a times I and b times I. Extend each retained leg to its corresponding disk port by product flow boxes, putting finitely many legs at distinct normal levels; the bands connecting their endpoints lie in this collar. On the companion side use its given selected trajectory tubes. The resulting matching is exactly the stated port extension; no closed-diagram matching is inferred merely from its names. All added and rerouted tubes lie outside U. Their finite difference-line cochain is consequently zero on every cell of F, and the physical negative fields on U may be chosen identical. The knot tube, boundary frame and zero-Euler cap are identical as well. Their Chern evaluation on F is therefore the SAME integer. Changing an outer gluing can change a lift on the complement of U, but cannot change this restriction; no total-map or torsion-Chern inference is needed. This proves the claimed reusable local centering operation while leaving the full glued state's identification and top-sector count to the separate actual finite construction. [F1, F2, step 1.1, step 2.1, step 3.1, step 4.1, construct, algebra]

6.1 Prove the actual changed-pairing application by its full oriented edge sum. In the positive C ribbon, F1 has CLOCKWISE boundary Q --G-minus--> n --beta1-minus--> m --alpha1-plus--> d --beta4-plus--> b --lambda-minus--> Q. F6 has CLOCKWISE boundary a --lambda-minus--> b --beta4-minus--> d --alpha1-plus--> e --beta3-plus--> a. Their beta4 edges cancel in the sum. Reversing gives the POSITIVE boundary Q --lambda-plus--> a --beta3-minus--> e --alpha1-minus--> m --beta1-plus--> n --G-plus--> Q. The two intermediate points b,d are smooth boundary points after this cancellation, not extra corners. Both face coefficients at the actual two marked faces vanish: these are full F7 containing (h,N-minus) and full F2 containing (g,N-plus). Open the Q corner and attach the actual companion triangle outside V. The full boundary becomes y0 --(lambda connected-sum m_comp)--> a --beta3--> e --alpha1--> m --beta1--> n --(gamma connected-sum l_comp)--> x0 --delta1--> y0. Thus its alpha paths are e to m, n to x0 and y0 to a: its source is exactly M=(n,e,y0) and its target L=(a,m,x0), with the stated common coordinates. This verifies the matching change rather than calling these unchanged internal tubes. Choose the meridian-side extension M by step5.1 from the actual x3 data, hence A(M)=0. On the common knot exterior inside V compare its negative trajectory field with L. By [F2]'s finite transverse-disk calculation their difference line is the signed trajectory cycle just displayed, with the determinant difference twice its winding cochain. Cap its full attaching-circle terms in the actual compression disks. The remaining boundary-port terms and the companion triangle lie in the outer collar or the companion exterior and miss F, so their evaluation on F is zero. In the interior, the explicit spanning chain is F1+F6, whose intersections with the knot are exactly its plus-z and minus-w coefficients and are BOTH zero. Hence the difference line evaluates zero on the capped F, and so does its determinant difference. The knot tube cap was identical. Therefore A(L)-A(M)=0 and A(L)=0. This proof needs no holomorphic realization, no class/domain injectivity and no equality of the two fields on F; it computes the changed-pairing Euler obstruction itself. The remaining full longitude differential and top selector are still separate. [F1, F2, step 1.1, step 3.1, step 5.1, construct, algebra] ∎
