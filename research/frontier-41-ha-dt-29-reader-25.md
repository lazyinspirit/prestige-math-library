## Mathematical checks and sources

The core formulas were checked directly: right multiplication satisfies `r_(aa') = r_(a') r_a`; the Hom action is `(a phi)(m)=phi(ma)`; the canonical comparison sends `m tensor x` to `F(ell_x)(m)` and is balanced because `ell_(ax)=ell_x r_a`. Free covers indexed by the entire underlying set give canonical presentations without choosing generators. The comparison is invertible first on the regular module, then coproducts, then cokernels. Natural transformations between tensor functors are detected at the regular module and at the maps `ell_x`; naturality at right multiplication gives precisely right A-linearity. The right-adjoint transfer equations and both triangle identities were checked. The flatness argument correctly tests the underlying abelian-group sequence and makes no left-projectivity claim. The two counterexamples separate coproduct preservation from right exactness, with countable fibre selection the only AC use.

Authoritative sources actually consulted:

## Repairs and evidence

1. **Size and category formation — material repair.** `def-additive-cocontinuous-module-functor`, Definition, formerly claimed that restricting the small-source construction formed a category of functors on the large module category. `lem-additive-cocontinuous-module-functors-form-a-category`, original Statement and Proof 3.1, then invoked replacement on an inverse with proper-class transformation families as outputs. Proper classes cannot be objects or outputs of that ZFC replacement instance, even if each family is determined by one set map. The definition now states the permitted schematic convention explicitly; the lemma proves injectivity by comparing two transformations directly in 2.1, and constructs a set of admissible regular-module-component codes by separation in 3.1. Its admissibility predicate uses only set modules, set maps and uniquely determined descents, with the defining formulas for F and G fixed. No universe axiom, global choice, or category of all definable classes is introduced.

   The size qualification was carried through `thm-eilenberg-watts-for-arbitrary-unital-rings` (Statement and F2), `cor-eilenberg-watts-is-an-equivalence-of-hom-categories` (title, Statement, F2, Proof 1.1/2.1), `cor-exact-module-tensor-functors-correspond-to-right-flat-bimodules` (Statement/F7), and `thm-natural-transformations-of-tensor-functors-are-bimodule-maps` (Statement's set-code convention). The equivalence proof now supplies the schematic quasi-inverse on transformations explicitly by conjugating with canonical comparisons and identifies it with evaluation `eta_A`; tensor-unit naturality gives the other isomorphism. The algebraic claims and all original arbitrary-ring hypotheses remain. The A-page summary now states this qualification. Other batches' carriers were not edited; owners using these statements should carry the schematic qualification when they assert an actual functor category under the same ZFC convention.

2. **Missing definition discharge link.** The characterization paragraph of `def-additive-cocontinuous-module-functor` was an unlinked reference to “the following lemma”, while its sole `justified_by` target established a different assertion. Added the exact link and discharge target `lem-additive-module-functor-preserves-colimits-iff-right-exact-and-coproduct-preserving`; that target already depends on the definition. Both characterization implications were checked in its Proof 1.1 and 2.1.

3. **Ill-typed composite transformations — material repair.** In `ex-natural-transformations-between-tensor-composites`, Verification 2.1, the old `alpha'_X eta_X alpha_X^{-1}` does not type-check because `alpha` goes from the kernel tensor functor **to** the composite. It is now `alpha'_X^{-1} eta_X alpha_X`, with inverse conjugation `alpha'_X xi_X alpha_X^{-1}`. Verification 3.1 formerly omitted X from an identity's module and equated maps on differently parenthesized tensor products. It now uses `g tensor 1_(M' tensor_A X)` composed with `1_N tensor (f tensor 1_X)`, equal to the conjugated kernel map by checking elementary tensors. The Example's displayed component and final step were synchronized. The dot-product rank-two witness was checked over an arbitrary field, including characteristic two, and remains unchanged. Evidence: the opened associativity supplier and the explicit domains of the two functors.

4. **False support computation — material repair.** In `cex-right-exact-module-functor-without-coproduct-preservation-is-not-tensor`, Counterexample 1.3, scalar sequences can have infinite supports, so the old union of their supports need not be finite. The proof now uses the finite set J of **summand indices** of an element in the source direct sum. Every output vector has support in J. Conversely, extracting coordinates from a family uniformly supported in J constructs a source element, proving the exact claimed image rather than only containment. Step 2.2 now refers to this summand-index set; step 3.1's justification includes additivity. The countable product is still exact under the explicitly assumed AC.

5. **Wrong epicness test object — material repair.** `cex-coproduct-preserving-left-exact-module-functor-is-not-tensor`, Counterexample 1.4, used the identity and zero maps of Z/2 as though they were maps out of `H=Hom(Z/2,Z/2)`. The test now uses the identity and zero **endomorphisms of H**, which are distinct since H contains the nonzero identity of Z/2. Both composites with `0 -> H` agree, so the displayed map is not epic. The finite-support Hom computation and left exactness were checked and retained.

6. **Fact wording.** `lem-canonical-free-presentation-controls-eilenberg-watts-comparison`, F4, incorrectly described tensoring as “B-linear on B-modules”, although its arguments are A-modules. It now states that the induced maps are B-linear when M is a (B,A)-bimodule. This is the exact assertion of the tensor supplier.

Boundary evidence was corrected where it claimed an identity-functor instance for unrelated A,B, that the canonical zero-module free cover was empty, that the zero-module canonical presentation had zero kernel/first term, or that one zero ring made both arbitrary module categories literally one-object categories. For X=0, the underlying set is a singleton: the canonical cover is `A -> 0`, its kernel is A, and the first free term is `A^(|A|)`.
