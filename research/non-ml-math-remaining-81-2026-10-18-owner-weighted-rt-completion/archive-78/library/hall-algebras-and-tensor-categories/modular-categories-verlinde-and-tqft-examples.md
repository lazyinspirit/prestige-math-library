This companion contains explicit finite checks and a failure case. The symmetric representation example is unconditional; the semion calculation uses a specified nontrivial associator; the root-of-unity and RT dimension calculations are stated conditionally on the corresponding A-page interfaces.

## Worked checks

- [[cex-symmetric-rep-z2-hopf-matrix-is-singular]] computes the rank-one Hopf matrix of symmetric $\operatorname{Rep}(\mathbb Z/2)$.
- [[ex-pointed-z2-semion-metric-group]] verifies the normalized abelian cocycle, both hexagons, the twist, and the nondegenerate bicharacter.
- [[ex-su2-ell-three-modular-data]] and [[ex-su2-ell-four-modular-data]] evaluate the finite sine data, twists, fusion rules, and torus dimensions for $\ell=3,4$.
- [[ex-ell-three-framing-anomaly-and-kirby-blowup]] computes $p_\pm$ and checks both weighted blow-up factors; its actual quotient/TQFT interpretation remains conditional.
- [[ex-rt-genus-two-factorization-dimension]] computes the conditional $\ell=4$ genus-two dimension $10$ from the factorization formula.

None of these B items is a supplier for the A-page theorems.
