The algebraic theory of surgery is used here to identify the Maslov
index with the Wall invariant for the non-additivity of the signature.
A lagrangian of a nonsingular symplectic form (V, φ : V × V →R)
on a ﬁnite-dimensional real vector space V is a subspace L ⊂V of half
the rank which is maximally isotropic, i.e. such that
L = {x ∈V | φ(x)(y) = 0 ∈R for all y ∈L} .

Deﬁnition 4. (i) The Maslov index of a triple Poincar´e cycle Γ is
deﬁned by
M(Γ) = M(L1, L2, L3) .
(ii) The Wall non-additivity invariant of a triple Poincar´e cycle Γ is
deﬁned by
W(Γ) = W(L1, L2, L3) .

The signature of a 4k-dimensional Poincar´e pair (C, ∂C) over R is
the signature of the singular form on H2k(C, ∂C)
σ(C) = σ(H2k(C, ∂C)) ∈Z .
For simplicity, the terminology makes no special mention of the actual
symmetric bilinear pairing
H2k(C, ∂C) × H2k(C, ∂C) →R ,
but it is understood to be the one induced by the given chain equiva-
lence C4k−∗≃C/∂C.

Lemma 5. The Wall non-additivity invariant of a triple Poincar´e cycle
Γ is such that
W(Γ) = σ(C1) + σ(C2) + σ(C3) −σ(C) ∈Z .
Proof The proof of the corresponding theorem for manifolds in Wall [5]
only uses the underlying Poincar´e triple structure. In greater detail,
consider ﬁrst the union 4k-dimensional Poincar´e pair C1 + C2 with
boundary
∂(C1 + C2) = (∂C1 ∩∂C) ⊕(C1 + C2) ∩C3 ⊕(∂C2 ∩∂C) .
A direct application of the main result of [5] gives
W(Γ) = σ(C1) + σ(C2) −σ(C1 + C2) ∈Z
and Novikov additivity for C = (C1 + C2) + C3 gives
σ(C) = σ(C1 + C2) + σ(C3) ∈Z .
Elimination of σ(C1 + C2) from these two identities gives the required
expression for W(Γ).
□
