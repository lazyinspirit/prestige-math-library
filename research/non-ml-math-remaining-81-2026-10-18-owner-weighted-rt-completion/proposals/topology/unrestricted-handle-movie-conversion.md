# Conversion of an index-grouped mixed-handle movie to relative surgery moves

2026-10-18. Scientific construction proposal. The statement below is conditional on an actual finite weak descending-field movie and its parametric index grouping. Those are separate suppliers. It is also conditional on root acceptance of the local arc-choice, one-handle-slide and duality constructions used here. This file is not a readiness assertion and does not invoke four-dimensional same-trace two-equivalence.

## 1. Movie input and the assignment to a surgery link

Fix connected compact oriented three-manifolds N,M, a specified identification of their boundaries, and the protected vertical collar. A connected four-dimensional bordism W between them is given with its complete boundary markings. Suppose two all-two-handle presentations are joined by a finite index-grouped mixed-handle movie on W: elementary framed attaching maps vary smoothly between events; events are cancelling births/deaths 0/1,1/2,2/3,3/4, same-index handle slides and critical-value interchanges of disjoint attachments. All terminal maps are retained. Positive-index-drop trajectory tangencies are permitted, since the elementary-band attaching maps remain smooth through them. This exact input is stronger than a generic function path. Its explicit supplier is `based-handle-movie-after-grouping.md` §§1–5: common index cuts and bounded-time full framed attachments; compactness isolation at the single same-index passage; reversed index1 treatment of index3; birth/value-crossing comparisons; and actual transport of every higher attaching map and endpoint marking. That construction, together with the weak field and grouping proofs, supplies this input rather than assuming it as a genericity consequence.

Assign a two-handle presentation at every ordinary time as follows. Collapse a rooted spanning tree in the initial zero/one-handle graph, eliminating every new zero-handle. Dually collapse a rooted spanning tree in the outgoing dual zero/one-handle graph, eliminating every four-handle. The two roots are the fixed nonempty connected N and M. These collapses leave a presentation with indices1,2,3. On the reversed presentation trade every one-handle (the original index3 handles) by the explicit interior circle surgery, obtaining only indices2,3 in reverse and hence indices1,2 forward. Flatten the successive two-handle attachments into the boundary of the forward one-handle stage. Trade every remaining forward one-handle by its explicit core-plus-base-arc interior circle surgery and cancellation. The result is a finite link in N. Every trade preserves the full external boundary of the four-manifold and changes its interior. Every flattening and cancellation retains its outgoing comparison.

The actual local trade, framed-circle-to-level isotopy and two-disk-factor surgery bridge are `five-dimensional-common-middle-trace-construction.md` §§1–3,5–6 and `circles-in-four-dimensional-traces.md`. The arbitrary replacement arc comparison is `replacement-arc-comparison.md`. The one-handle slide after trading is `one-handle-slide-after-trading.md`. No change of replacement arc is declared invisible: it is that comparison's actual O3/O2 sequence with its meridian correction.

## 2. Rooted tree collapses and changes of tree

The zero/one-handle graph has one old root vertex N and one vertex for every new ball; edges are the one-handles. It is connected because higher-index attaching regions are connected and cannot merge components. Choose a spanning tree T. Starting at its leaves, the union of a leaf ball and its incident tree tube is a ball with one retained attaching disk. Absorb it into its neighbor's collar using the explicit ball-plus-tube product chart. Every other foot and every higher attaching embedding is carried by this very chart. Repetition removes all new zero-handles. The support stays off the protected vertical collar. Reversing gives the outgoing tree collapse.

Two trees are compared by single-edge exchanges. If T′ differs from T, choose f∈T′−T. The unique cycle in T∪{f} contains an edge e∈T−T′; replacing e by f reduces the number of differing edges. Finite induction reaches T′. For this one exchange restrict to the cycle plus its attached tree branches; first absorb the branches by the same leaf-ball products. The cycle is now a cyclic sequence of balls and tubes attached to the root component by a tree path. Collapse all but the two edges e,f. The remaining model is two balls joined by two tubes, with its incoming root collar retained. Collapsing the first tube or the second tube gives the same ball-plus-one-handle model with different retained core paths. To see the actual comparison, cut both tubes at transverse three-disks. The cut piece is a ball with the four named disk faces e_±,f_±. Moving the chosen joining tube across the other disk face is the k=1 disk push of the actual handle-slide proof. Other incident feet are moved along the displayed tube by parallel disk patches. Each such movement is a one-handle slide over the surviving cycle handle; its thickening is the product D³ of the corresponding patch. A higher attaching strip is carried by the same product chart. Restoring the absorbed branches transports this finite collection of slides and isotopies to the original graph. Thus a tree change produces only one-handle slides and framed isotopies in the collapsed presentation, with its actual lower-boundary identification. The construction uses the cut four-face ball, not a classification of graph thickenings or a free-group basis argument alone.

At a 0/1 birth the new vertex has its cancelling edge. Choose T to contain that edge; the leaf-ball product absorbs the born pair and leaves the assigned presentation unchanged. At a death first choose T to contain the dying edge. If other feet lie on its ball, the same product absorption carries them across the joining tube; in another tree convention this is precisely the finite one-handle-slide comparison just described. Thus the 0/1 row produces only the already controlled one-handle-slide/isotopy changes. Apply the reversed argument to a 3/4 birth or death. It also proves independence of the outgoing tree choice before the dual trades.

## 3. Reversal of a two-handle slide

We need the local duality calculation, rather than an assumption that a reverse movie has the same diagram. A four-dimensional two-handle is D²_x×D²_y. In the forward presentation x is its core disk and y its cocore; in the reversed presentation their roles are interchanged. For a slide of the first core disk over the second along a band, its new core is the boundary band sum of the first core with a parallel of the second. Choose the normal disk coordinates on that band. The dual cocore disks are then the unchanged first cocore and the second cocore band-summed with the negative first cocore along the transverse rectangle. This is an actual disk construction: cut the two handle products along their cocores, follow the two opposite faces of the core band, and join their transverse disk patches by the intervening normal rectangle. On the first handle product the two parallel patches cancel across a product rectangle; on the second product the remaining patch meets its core once. No other handle is crossed after the band and normal radii are decreased. The boundary of the transverse rectangle is a genuine embedded band in the reversed attaching three-level, and its normal strip is the exchanged disk factor. Thus a forward two-handle slide becomes the opposite dual two-handle slide in reverse, together with the displayed product isotopies. Framings are transported by these disk-factor coordinates, including the sign from the reversed orientation.

This comparison is the restriction of the same core-disk-push diffeomorphism, read in the other direction. It therefore retains both external face markings when those markings are transported by the original elementary move. It does not assert that an arbitrary diffeomorphism of a two-handlebody is generated by such slides. It is only the reverse of one already supplied local slide.

## 4. Flattening choices and moves based at the opposite end

The finite geometric curve/core lemma in the decorated lifting proposal, steps1.1–3.2, is independent of its global completeness hypothesis. For an ordinary framed circle its statement is: in a three-manifold filled along an earlier link, avoid the finitely many filling cores and radially move a sufficiently small tubular thickening into the old exterior; two such choices connected by an isotopy are related by finite exterior framed isotopies and slides over the specified surgery parallels. A transverse crossing of one core gives exactly that slide, with its strip framing. Consequently successive two-handle attachments can be flattened into the original one-handle-stage boundary, and every change of flattening choice produces O2 moves over earlier two-handles. Their terminal maps are the original isotopy maps in the filled manifold. This is a local geometric input, not an appeal to global surgery completeness.

An O3 operation obtained in the reverse presentation also has a forward description. Before any cancellation it is an interior framed circle surgery in the actual four-dimensional level. Choose the forward one-handle trade circles disjoint from this circle and its small tube, perturbing one-dimensional cores in dimension four and retaining the protected boundary. These surgeries commute because their displayed product neighborhoods are disjoint. After the forward trades the four-dimensional level is a two-handle trace relative to N. The already proved π₁-surjection and four-dimensional circle-isotopy construction put the remaining framed surgery circle into N, with its rank-three framing induced by a rank-two framing there. The two-hemisphere bridge records this actual surgery by K_f and its small zero-framed meridian, hence by O3 in N. The circle is chosen off the old surgery link and its tube small enough to miss that link. All further flattening choices are controlled by the preceding local curve/core lemma. Thus an opposite-end O3 is converted to a forward O3 with finite O2/isotopy comparisons; it is not treated as an unspecified diffeomorphism of the outgoing three-manifold.

The same argument applies when other disjoint trade circles are present: choose pairwise disjoint cores and normal tubes, use their product commutation, then flatten one at a time. For a finite movie, these choices are made smoothly on each closed ordinary interval. At an isolated event compare its two endpoint choices by the explicit arc-choice or flattening lemma. No infinite choice limit or implicit naturality is used.

## 5. The remaining event rows

A same-index index1 slide becomes the O2 dual-meridian slide of `one-handle-slide-after-trading.md`; noncompatible replacement arcs are corrected by the finite arc-choice comparison. An index3 slide is that same calculation in the reversed presentation, followed by §3 for its two-handle slides and §4 for any arc-choice O3. An index2 slide is unchanged after the adjacent trades: choose the trade cores in the low forward one-handle stage and the high reversed one-handle stage, off its core-band support. When a base arc is moved in making this choice, use the arc-choice comparison. Carry all other attaching strips by the same local slide map. After flattening, it is O2 by §4. Critical-value interchanges of disjoint equal-index attachments are their commuting product gluings and produce no additional move.

For a 1/2 birth use the actual local cancelling chart, transporting all later attaching data by its cancellation map. An older two-handle may traverse the born one-handle through an allowed positive-index-drop trajectory; it is not declared disjoint merely by function genericity. Before the local conversion, slide each such traversal off the new cancelling two-handle using the very product bands used for the death calculation. There are finitely many traversals after transverse position. Each band removes one signed traversal and carries its full strip. This produces the birth-normalized attaching data, with the actual composition of slide maps followed by the cancellation map. Use the inverse slides on the other side if retaining the original convention. These finite O2 preparations are part of the birth row. Choose the other trade circles away from this small birth support. Such a choice exists: in the index-grouped presentation the other one-handle cores lie on their own lower or dual-upper stages, while an auxiliary arc can be moved off a ball by replacing its finitely many crossing subarcs with boundary-collar arcs; small distinct parallels preserve its embedding. Changing from any old choice is controlled by the arc-choice lemma. The distinguished forward one-handle replacement is then exactly the born K_f,U_0 pair of the continuous-surgery construction. At a death first slide every other traversing two-handle off the cancelling two-handle using the explicit product bands. Thus the row is O3, O2 and isotopy with the retained cancellation map. A 2/3 birth or death is the identical calculation in the reversed presentation, and §4 converts its O3 to the forward base while §3 converts its slides. A 0/1 or3/4 birth/death was handled by §2.

Positive-index-drop trajectory tangencies add no row. The supplied elementary-band theorem says their attaching embeddings and terminal maps remain smooth. After index grouping and the product trades they are therefore ordinary intervals, with auxiliary arc/core crossings already translated by the finite local geometric lemmas. This statement does not assume those tangencies are absent.

## 6. Assembly and exact conditional conclusion

The field movie has finitely many events and compact regular intervals. Choose the finite trees, arcs, thickenings and flattenings on each interval. Subdivide further only for the finite transverse auxiliary crossings. At its ends use the preceding comparisons to identify the choices. Every listed change becomes a finite O2/O3/isotopy sequence, with the actual product boundary maps. At the two endpoints the input presentations have only two-handles, so both tree collapses and every one/three-handle trade are empty; choose the flattening to be their given original links. The resulting sequence therefore connects the original links, rather than merely some unspecified links representing diffeomorphic manifolds. Its cumulative outgoing map is the given comparison induced by the marked input movie, because every event comparison was the restriction of its explicit product cancellation, slide or interior-surgery trace and the interval maps were the retained regular flows. All supports avoid the vertical boundary collar.

Combining this conversion with the actual common-middle reduction gives the desired marked O1/O2/O3 calculus **provided** the weak descending-field movie, the parametric index grouping, and all local constructions in this file are accepted as proved. The O1 operations occur only in the signature correction before the common-middle five-bordism. The proof does not assert same-trace two-equivalence: intermediate four-dimensional levels are changed by the displayed interior surgeries. The external full boundary is unchanged under its recorded parametrization. Disconnected three-manifolds are handled componentwise with a fixed matching partition; no surgery is allowed to alter that partition. Empty and closed boundary components require no new local row.
