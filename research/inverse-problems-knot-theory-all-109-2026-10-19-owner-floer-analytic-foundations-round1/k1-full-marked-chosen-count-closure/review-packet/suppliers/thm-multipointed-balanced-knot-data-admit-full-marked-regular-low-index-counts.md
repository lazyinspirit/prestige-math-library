## Statement

Assume Countable Choice. Fix an actual balanced multipointed Heegaard diagram of one oriented knot in $S^3$, with $k\ge2$ cyclically ordered mark pairs and $m=h+k-1>0$, and its positive surface area form. The two attaching systems are transverse, and their proper compression disks cut their handlebodies into $k$ balls; broad auxiliary boundary tangencies are allowed.

There is a CHOICE of orientation-compatible integrable surface structure $j$, protected diagonal/region/marked-divisor neighborhoods, common exact product generator charts, and ONE smooth nearly symmetric tame path $J_t$ constant on genuine positive endpoint time collars, such that EVERY actual nonconstant finite-energy strip of real Fredholm index one or two has onto actual $H^1\to L^2$ linearization. The same holds in every integer higher realization. The autonomous endpoint structures have regular node-fixed index-two unit-component disks for both boundary systems, with finitely many such disks modulo node-fixing positive affine maps at every generator.

There are finitely many index-one strip orbits modulo longitudinal translation, across ALL ordered generators, realized classes and ordered nonnegative $w,z$ multiplicities. In particular their full-marked or $w$-weighted index-one counts are finite sums. No $n_z=0$ restriction is made.

This is existence of auxiliary choices and low-index finite counts for $k\ge2$. It does not establish comparison with arbitrary preassigned surface complex structures or paths, any absolute grading normalization, a value for a unit boundary-disk count, square-zero or invariance of the full filtered differential, the singly pointed case, or polygon move counts.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The actual balanced $S^3$ knot diagram, $k\ge2$, area form and smooth attaching data.

[F1] One integrable background with common generator charts admits a protected autonomous structure $J_e$ regular for EVERY unit-component disk on BOTH tori at every generator, with node-fixed index two and finitely many affine orbits. It preserves the full standard structure on both tori and all protected sets. ([[thm-common-integrable-background-gives-simultaneous-balanced-boundary-disk-regularity]])

[F2] With the constant background path $J_e$, the flat-endpoint-jet theorem supplies an actual all-strip regular auxiliary path $J^*$ and smooth approximating paths $J_n$ constant on genuine endpoint collars. Their endpoint values, full boundary structures, protected spatial sets and exact generator charts agree. The auxiliary path itself need not have a collar. ([[thm-countable-choice-flat-endpoint-jet-universal-strip-regularity]])

[F3] For any fixed FINITE actual index set, every strip in a small fixed-geometry coefficient neighborhood has one common energy bound, with ALL positive $w,z$ degrees included. The signed mark-index theorem gives $\mu(u)-\mu(v)=2\sum(n_w(u)-n_w(v))$ for all realized same-end classes, across these paths. Actual holomorphic marked degrees are nonnegative. ([[lem-balanced-knot-strips-in-bounded-index-sets-have-finite-domains-and-energy]], [[lem-balanced-knot-whitney-index-differences-equal-twice-total-mark-differences]])

[F4] Actual low-index compactness with regular limiting strips gives precisely one or two unbubbled strip levels, or at index two a constant generator plus one unit-component disk. The sphere alternative is absent because $k\ge2$. At a limit of the last kind with a regular node-fixed disk, actual nearby strip operators are onto even for varying paths without a common positive time-collar width. ([[lem-balanced-low-index-strip-limits-have-only-unit-boundary-degenerations]], [[lem-regular-boundary-bubble-limits-cannot-create-strip-cokernels]]) The actual index sum holds for ANY integer sequence index. Nonconstant compact disks contribute at least two and spheres at least $2k$ by the proved compact formulas and positivity. Smoothly convergent paths have a common positive nonconstant-strip energy quantum: if all windows have sufficiently small energy the entire image lies in one exact generator chart and the whole transverse $D_0$ inverse forces constancy. [F5] The proof of the zero-marked collar-openness supplier constructs an actual squared-cutoff parametrix for ANY given unbubbled finite collection of regular strip levels: its steps1.1–4.1 use actual core convergence, common exact generator charts and collapsing gaps only. The zero-mark premise there supplies unbubbled compactness; it is NOT used as a premise for the separate construction repeated below. [F6] Actual $H^3\cap X_0$ strip map charts and their smooth section, split zero charts, higher Fredholm range agreement and the whole constant transverse-strip inverse are proved locally. ## Proof

1.1 Choose the actual integrable surface background, protected sets and common exact generator charts in [F1], including its one autonomous regular endpoint structure $J_e$. There is a uniform LOWER integer bound for the indices of ALL actual holomorphic strips with these data, across every path under consideration. For each ordered generator pair whose continuous Whitney-class set is nonempty choose one realized class $\psi_{xy}$; these are finite choices since the generator set is finite. By [F3] every actual strip with those ends has $\mu(u)=c_{xy}+2\sum n_w(u)\ge c_{xy}$, where $c_{xy}=\mu(\psi_{xy})-2\sum n_w(\psi_{xy})$ is one fixed integer. A pair with no realized class has no strip. Set $L=\min(\{0\}\cup\{c_{xy}\})$ and use the finite integer band $I=\{L,L+1,\ldots,2\}$. This does not assume connectivity of all generator pairs. Use the constant path $J_e$ in [F2] to obtain the all-strip regular auxiliary path $J^*$ and genuine collar approximants $J_n$. Their endpoint jets and full boundary structures agree with $J_e$. Shrink the coefficient neighborhood for common taming and protected bounds. Now [F3] supplies ONE energy bound $E_0$ for ALL actual strips in this entire finite band, including every possible nonconstant nonpositive-index strip, not just indices one and two. All positive marked multiplicities remain allowed. [F1, F2, F3, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 For all sufficiently late approximants there is NO nonconstant strip of index at most zero, and every index-one or index-two strip is regular. Otherwise Countable Choice selects an increasing sequence of approximation indices with either a nonconstant strip of index at most zero or a nonregular strip of index one or two. Step1.1 places all their actual indices in the finite band $I$, so a subsequence fixes the index and endpoints and has bounded-energy actual compactness for $J^*$. The entirely constant, no-bubble, no-loss alternative is impossible explicitly: energy identity would give $E(u_n)\to0$; every window would be below the common threshold of [F4]. Its small-window argument puts the whole image in one exact generator chart. Slice Poincare and finite energy put its coordinate map in $H^1$, and the actual transverse $D_0$ inverse forces that map to be zero, contradicting nonconstancy. Any remaining limit has a positive-energy strip or bubble component. Every nonconstant limiting strip is regular by [F2]; its nonzero translation kernel lies in $H^1$ by exponential ends, so its actual index is at least one. Every nonconstant disk or sphere has positive contribution at least two by [F4]. The actual bubbled index sum in [F4] therefore makes the limiting integer at least one. This excludes the entire nonpositive-index subsequence without assuming target-path negative-level regularity. For an index-one or index-two subsequence [F4]'s actual low-index classification is now applicable, because its limiting path $J^*$ is regular at ALL nonconstant strips. If it is a constant strip with one unit disk, that disk has frozen structure $J_e$ and is regular of node-fixed index two by [F1]; [F4]'s varying-cap theorem makes late strip operators onto, a contradiction. The remaining unbubbled cases are treated next. [F1, F2, F4, step 1.1, construct, contradiction]

3.1 For completeness handle the other actual limits by the explicit operator construction, without importing a zero-mark conclusion. They have one or two nonconstant regular levels $v_j$, no bubbles, smooth translated core convergence and collapsing gaps in exact common generator charts. Give each onto level its bounded right inverse $Q_j:L^2\to H^1$. On fixed translated cores use [F6]'s boundary-preserving local-addition identification $T_{j,n}$ of tangent bundles; on their matching generator ends it is the exact coordinate identity. The coefficient errors there tend to zero in $H^1\to L^2$ norm and are supported on fixed cores; off them both operators are exactly $D_0$. Choose longitudinal cutoffs $\eta_{j,n}$ with $\sum_j\eta_{j,n}^2=1$, equal one on the level cores, transitioning in gaps of widths $R_n\to\infty$, with $|\eta'_{j,n}|\le C/R_n$. For one level take its cutoff identically one. Translate $\eta_{j,n}f$ to the corresponding level, identify it and extend it by zero as an $L^2$ forcing, and call it $f_j$. Then $$Q_n^0f=\sum_j\eta_{j,n}T_{j,n}Q_jf_j$$ is an actual section with the right physical boundary trace and uniformly bounded $L^2\to H^1$ norm. The exact first-order Leibniz identity gives $$D_{u_n}Q_n^0=I+E_n,\qquad\|E_n\|\le C(\delta_n+R_n^{-1})\longrightarrow0,$$ where $\delta_n$ is the fixed-core coefficient error. The infinite tails contribute no coefficient error since their charts and operators are exact. Its Neumann-corrected right inverse contradicts nonregularity. These are precisely the actual local constructions in [F5], with unbubbled compactness here proved by [F4]. Together with step2.1 this proves that all sufficiently late constant-collar approximants have NO nonconstant strip of nonpositive index and are regular for EVERY index-one or index-two strip. The lower bound of step1.1 makes the excluded nonpositive band exhaustive; no unexamined indices below it occur. Higher range agreement in [F6] gives regularity at every integer order. Fix one such allowed path with BOTH properties. [F3, F4, F5, F6, step 1.1, step 2.1, construct, algebra]

4.1 All its unit-component boundary disks are still those for $J_e$, hence have the finiteness and regularity in [F1]. For the fixed path take any sequence of index-one strips. The energy bound [F3] gives actual compactness. The constant/no-bubble alternative is excluded by the same common energy-quantum argument in step2.1. For this FIXED chosen path, step3.1 has already excluded every nonconstant nonpositive-index strip, so every nonconstant limiting strip has integer index at least one WITHOUT needing its regularity in advance. The actual index sum and positive bubble bounds in [F4] force exactly one nonconstant strip of index one and no nonconstant bubble. That limiting strip is regular by step3.1; now the regular-limit classification in [F4] legitimately removes all ghosts as well. Thus after translations there is one unbubbled index-one strip limit, with no energy or image diameter lost in its two outer tails. This gives actual GLOBAL $H^3$ convergence, not just local smooth convergence: choose fixed sufficiently far transition bands on the two ends, where all sequence maps and the limit lie in the same exact generator charts. Their difference $w_n$ is smooth, belongs individually to $H^3$ on the tails by the actual end theory in [F6], and solves $D_0w_n=0$ there. Multiply by a fixed end cutoff $\chi$ and extend by zero to the whole strip. Then $$D_0(\chi w_n)=\chi' w_n,\qquad\|\chi w_n\|_{H^3}\le C\|\chi' w_n\|_{H^2}\longrightarrow0.$$ The whole transverse-strip inverse and higher estimate in [F6] justify the inequality with only the original real boundary trace. Smooth convergence on the fixed transition bands gives its limit zero. On the remaining compact core local smooth convergence and the local-addition charts give $H^3$ convergence. Thus after translation the sequence converges in the actual global configuration chart. [F1, F3, F4, F6, step 2.1, step 3.1, construct, algebra]

5.1 At its regular index-one limit, [F6]'s actual fixed-parameter zero set is a smooth one-dimensional manifold. The nonzero kernel vector $u_s$ is tangent to longitudinal translation, belongs to $H^3$ by its smooth exponential ends, and spans that kernel. The translation curve of this ONE fixed smooth strip is smooth as an $H^3$ curve: its successive derivatives are the longitudinal derivatives of the smooth strip, all square integrable with the same fixed generator offsets by the exact end theory. In the one-dimensional zero chart its derivative is nonzero, so the ordinary scalar inverse theorem makes its translation orbit contain a neighborhood of this solution. This does not assume differentiability of joint translation on the entire rough $H^3$ map space. Step4.1 therefore says every convergent translated index-one sequence is eventually in the limit's orbit. If there were infinitely many distinct orbits, Countable Choice selects an ordered $n$-tuple of distinct orbits for each positive integer $n$. Flattening the finite tuples and retaining first occurrences gives an infinite sequence of distinct orbits; Countable Choice then selects representatives. Step4.1 supplies a subsequence which is eventually one orbit, a contradiction. There are consequently finitely many orbits across all generators and realized classes. Their actual nonnegative marked degrees give finite weighted sums. No value of a unit-disk count or square-zero assertion follows from this finiteness argument. [F3, F4, F6, step 4.1, construct, contradiction] ∎
