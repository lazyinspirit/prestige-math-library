## Statement

Assume Countable Choice. Use compact transverse strip data with generator $x$, boundary tori $L_0,L_1$, and a smooth path $J_t$. In one fixed chart about $x$ require $J_t=i$ for every $t$, $L_0=\mathbb R^m$ and $L_1=i\mathbb R^m$. Require $J_t=J_0$ for $0\le t\le\eta$ for some $\eta>0$. Let $v$ be an actual smooth nonconstant autonomous $J_0$-holomorphic closed disk with boundary on $L_0$ and marked boundary value $x$. Suppose its actual compact node-fixed linearization is onto of real index two.

For every sufficiently small $\epsilon>0$ there is an actual smooth finite-energy $J_t$ strip $g_\epsilon:x\to x$ obtained by attaching this disk at the lower boundary point $z=s+it=0$ of the constant strip $x$. More precisely use the disk half-plane coordinate $\zeta$ with marked point infinity, the node coordinate $w=-1/\zeta$, and fixed small cut radius $r$. Put
$$T=\pi^{-1}\log(r^2/\epsilon).$$
There is an explicit preglued map $u_T$, equal to $v(z/\epsilon)$ on the inserted disk core and to $x$ on the exterior strip, joined in the exact node chart. In actual boundary-preserving local-addition coordinates and the adapted cap/cylinder Sobolev norms of the sewn linear theorem, its correction obeys
$$\|\xi_T\|_{H^3}\le C(1+T)e^{-\pi T/2},\qquad g_\epsilon=\mathcal E(u_T,\xi_T).$$
It is the unique sufficiently small correction with the two fixed disk-core phases zero, on a ball of radius $c/(1+T)$. Its actual fixed-end linearization is onto of index two, with a right inverse of adapted norm at most $C(1+T)$, also in the actual unweighted $H^1\to L^2$ regularity sense. The corrected strips depend smoothly on positive $\epsilon$ locally away from zero.

As $\epsilon\downarrow0$ they converge smoothly to $x$ away from the attaching point and, in the source scaling $z=\epsilon\zeta$, smoothly locally to $v(\zeta)$ away from its node. Their energies converge to $E_g(v)$, with no lost energy. No kernel parametrization by translation and scale, nodal collar or exhaustion of all nearby strips is asserted.

## Proof

1.1 Choose $r<1/4$ so the target disk node tail and the strip generator neighborhood lie in one smaller exact chart $q$, centered at $x$, with local addition ordinary coordinate addition. The source disk has coordinate $w=-1/\zeta$ near its marked point; its node tail has real Taylor series in $w$ by [F3]. The plumbing $zw=-\epsilon$ inserts its core by $z=\epsilon\zeta$, with the intervening logarithmic cylinder of length $T$ in [F1]. Put $q(u_T)=0$ on its left part and $q(u_T)=V$ on its right part, where $V=q(v)$ expressed in that cylinder's holomorphic coordinate. Interpolate by $q(u_T)=\chi(x-T/2)V$, where $x\in[0,T]$, a fixed real smooth $\chi$ is zero for argument at most $-1$ and one for argument at least $1$, and $u_T$ agrees with its component maps on the two caps. The reflected transverse coordinate at the right end is exactly that in [F1]. The small node tail keeps this inside the target chart. Its two cylinder edges both lie on $L_0$, so real multiplication preserves the actual lower boundary; the exterior upper boundary remains the constant $x\in L_1$. For large $T$, the entire inserted disk core has physical height at most $\epsilon/r<\eta$, where the strip path is exactly $J_0$. Thus it is genuinely the autonomous component equation there. [F1, F2, F3, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 In the adapted source coordinates the residual vanishes on both caps. On the neck the structure, target coordinates and local addition are exactly linear. Both $V$ and zero solve $D_0$, so the residual is precisely $\chi'(x-T/2)V$, with the actual holomorphic source symbol transformation on forcings. Its support has fixed cylinder width. There the disk node coordinate has longitudinal size $S+T/2+O(1)$ for a fixed $S$, so [F3] bounds every fixed source derivative by $C_ke^{-\pi T/2}$. Hence $$\|\mathcal F_T(0)\|_{H^2}\le Ce^{-\pi T/2}.$$ The actual derivative at zero is EXACTLY the sewn operator of [F1]: it is the original disk linearization on its core, the constant-strip operator on the exterior and $D_0$ throughout the collars and middle. Nonzero pregluing residual introduces no extra coefficient there because the coordinate section itself is exactly $D_0(q(u_T)+\xi)$. This identification respects the two fixed disk-core phases. Thus its phase-fixed inverse $Q_T$ has norm $M_T\le C(1+T)$. [F1, F2, F3, step 1.1, construct, algebra]

3.1 The nonlinear charts have a radius independent of $T$ and a uniform derivative Lipschitz constant $L$. On the fixed disk core this is the actual composition and product estimate of [F2], with fixed smooth reference $v$ and the autonomous structure $J_0$. On the constant-strip exterior a uniform small $C^0$ section stays in the exact generator chart, where the expression is linear. The same is true on the entire joining cylinder. Uniform $H^3\to C^1$ control follows by the fixed-cap estimates and unit cylinder/strip windows, with bounded overlap and translation-invariant constants. The latter estimates use only real physical traces and finite extensions as in [F2]. They give a uniform chart radius in which all addition inverses exist, boundary membership is preserved and all exact-chart images stay in the chosen neighborhood. Nonlinear second derivatives occur only in the fixed disk-core part; fixed collar interpolations have uniform derivatives. Consequently $$\|d\mathcal F_T(\xi)-d\mathcal F_T(\xi')\|_{H^3\to H^2}\le L\|\xi-\xi'\|_{H^3}$$ there. This uses the adapted norms, not a false uniform bound for physical derivatives in the shrinking original $z$ coordinate. [F2, step 1.1, step 2.1, construct, algebra]

4.1 Restrict to the phase-zero Hilbert source domain, where the derivative is invertible by [F1]. Choose a radius $r_T=c/(1+T)$ small enough that $M_T Lr_T\le1/2$ and that it lies in the uniform chart ball of step3.1. Its exponentially small residual eventually satisfies $2M_T\|\mathcal F_T(0)\|<r_T$. Apply the actual Newton theorem [F4] with the specified inverse $Q_T$. It gives a unique phase-zero zero with $$\|\xi_T\|_{H^3}\le2M_T\|\mathcal F_T(0)\|\le C(1+T)e^{-\pi T/2}.$$ The corrected derivative on that subspace remains invertible by the Neumann series and has inverse norm at most $2M_T$. On the full source its kernel has dimension two and it is onto, by precisely the codimension-two phase-lift argument in [F1]; hence its index is two. This proves an actual corrected zero, not merely a small residual or a conditional moduli assertion. [F1, F4, step 2.1, step 3.1, construct, algebra]

5.1 The zero is a smooth actual strip by [F2]. Its original strip ends tend uniformly to $x$: on those unchanged outer ends $u_T=x$, its section is in ordinary $H^3$, and the uniform local supremum estimate makes its tail supremum tend to zero. Its end images therefore lie eventually in the exact generator chart. There it solves $D_0$ with the two transverse lines, so the actual Fourier end argument gives exponential decay of all derivatives. For each fixed $T$ its physical-source Sobolev norms are equivalent to the adapted ones by the finite smooth rescaling on the inserted core and annulus; no equivalence constant uniform in $T$ is needed. Its actual geometric linearization has the exact transverse models at both ends. The genuine Fredholm and higher-realization agreement in [F4] now transfers the just-proved $H^3\to H^2$ onto-ness and kernel dimension two to the actual $H^1\to L^2$ operator. Thus the constructed strip is regular in that sense as well. Smooth dependence on positive $\epsilon$ is local: on any compact positive parameter interval the explicit preglued map, local-addition identifications and compact phase weights are smooth in the actual configuration topology. In fixed reference spaces their section formulas are smooth in operator norm. The invertible phase-augmented derivative and [F4] give local smooth solutions, which agree with these corrections by their specified phase uniqueness. No norm-differentiability of composition on all rough $H^3$ maps is used. [F2, F4, step 1.1, step 3.1, step 4.1, construct, algebra]

6.1 The adapted correction norm tends to zero, so the uniform local $C^1$ estimates give convergence to the component maps on every fixed component core. On the exterior strip these are compact sets away from $z=0$; on the disk they are compact sets in its actual scaled coordinate $\zeta=z/\epsilon$. The equations on those fixed core coordinates have smooth fixed coefficients, and [F2]'s actual local bootstrap upgrades this $C^1$ convergence to smooth local convergence. The preglued energies tend to $E_g(v)$: its disk part exhausts the disk as the cutoff moves to its node, the exterior is constant, and the fixed-width join has energy tending to zero by [F3]. They are uniformly bounded. Two-dimensional Dirichlet energy is unchanged by these holomorphic source coordinates. The correction changes the derivative in $L^2$ by at most $$C(\|d\xi_T\|_2+\|\xi_T\|_\infty\,\|du_T\|_2),$$ using the smooth local-addition derivative on fixed target charts. This tends to zero by step4.1 and the uniform energy bound. The target metric coefficients also change uniformly by a quantity tending to zero. Thus $E_g(g_\epsilon)\to E_g(v)$, and every strip has finite energy. The nonconstant disk limit ensures the strips are nonconstant. This proves the claimed actual attachment family and absence of energy loss, while retaining the separate obligations of kernel parametrization, collar injectivity and nodal-limit exhaustion. [F2, F3, step 1.1, step 4.1, step 5.1, construct, algebra] ∎
