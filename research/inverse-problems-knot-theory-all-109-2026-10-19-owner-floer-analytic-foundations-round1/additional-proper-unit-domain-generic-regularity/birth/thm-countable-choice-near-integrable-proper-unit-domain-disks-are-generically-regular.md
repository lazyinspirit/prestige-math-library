## Statement

Assume Countable Choice. Use the standard integrable background, protected spatial sets $D,A_x,L$ and nearly symmetric geometric class of the free-injective-site theorem, with at least two attaching-system complement components and with finitely many node values $x\in L$ inside smaller working exact charts in $\operatorname{Int}A_x$. Require the optional taming hypothesis of the disk-variation theorem whenever tameness is part of the geometric class.

There is an actual separable Hilbert ball of smooth autonomous nearly symmetric structures $J(a)$ near the standard background, preserving all values on $D\cup L\cup\bigcup_xA_x$ and the working charts, such that a dense residual subset of parameters has this property simultaneously for all the prescribed node values: EVERY actual disk of any proper unit component domain, with boundary on $L$ and that marked boundary value, has surjective actual compact node-fixed $H^3\to H^2$ linearization. It is also surjective in every actual weighted node-fixed realization of weight $0<\delta<\pi$ and every integer order. At every such disk the actual universal derivative is onto.

No dense-injective-point hypothesis on the perturbed disk is imposed: the open free injective site is proved by the compactness and integrable unit-domain suppliers. This establishes regularity only. It does not compute the node-fixed index or the count value, classify arbitrary higher-domain degenerations, or give attachment gluing and cancellation.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The actual compact target and one oriented product torus, proper unit domains, fixed geometry, protected values and finite exact working node charts. Every structure used is autonomous and fixes the full background endomorphism on $L$.

[F1] [[lem-near-integrable-proper-unit-domain-disks-have-free-injective-sites]] gives one smooth neighborhood in which every actual proper unit-domain disk has a nonempty open set of free globally unique interior immersion points, with uniqueness against the entire closed disk. It applies to all node values, without an index assumption.

[F2] [[thm-countable-choice-generic-node-fixed-disk-regularity-at-dense-injective-points]] constructs the actual separable spatial Hilbert family of smooth protected structures using rapidly convergent projected bumps and the Cayley map. Its finite parameter tangents are dense in compactly supported anti-complex-linear fields in the free region. We use this actual construction, not its conditional dense-injective conclusion. Its proof also gives the actual compact/weighted universal comparison, which is repeated at the needed level below.

[F3] [[lem-node-fixed-boundary-disk-sobolev-moduli-affine-slices-and-cover-index]] supplies actual second-countable compact node-fixed Sobolev charts and the smooth Cauchy–Riemann section. [[lem-geometric-oriented-disk-cauchy-riemann-fredholm-and-higher-regularity]] and [[lem-generator-punctured-boundary-disk-weighted-fredholm-and-node-evaluation]] give actual full compact/weighted range quotients, common smooth kernels, finite node-fixed Fredholm operators and their higher-order agreement.

[F4] [[lem-interior-regularity-of-strip-linearization-cokernels]] gives actual interior smoothness of an $L^2$ adjoint obstruction for a smooth Cauchy–Riemann principal part. [[lem-countable-choice-cauchy-riemann-similarity-and-unique-continuation]] gives its unique continuation. ## Proof

1.1 Take the actual Hilbert family in [F2], with background the integrable standard structure and free region $O=X\setminus(D\cup L\cup\bigcup A_x)$. It has the form $J(a)=J_0(I+B(a))(I-B(a))^{-1}$ with $B(a)=\sum a_nc_nB_n$, $a\in\ell^2$, and rapidly decreasing positive $c_n$. Cauchy–Schwarz bounds every $C^k$ seminorm of $B$ by a constant times $\|a\|_{\ell^2}$; the actual Cayley formulas give the same smooth continuity for $J(a)$. Shrink its parameter ball so its image lies in the neighborhood from [F1] and in the prescribed tame neighborhood if required. This is possible because a basic smooth neighborhood imposes only finitely many seminorm conditions. All protected values and exact working charts remain fixed. The derivative Cayley isomorphism in [F2] transfers the projected-bump density to compactly supported anti-$J(a)$ fields for every parameter. In particular for a nonzero tangent vector $v$ their values can approximate an arbitrary prescribed $Yv$: define it on $\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}\{v,J(a)v\}$ by anti-commutation, extend by zero on an invariant complement and apply a local bump. [F1, F2, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 At any actual smooth zero $u$ with marked value $x$, the compact universal derivative is $P\xi+Kb$, where $P$ is the actual node-fixed operator and $Kb=(d_aJ[b])(u)u_t$. Every such forcing is smooth and vanishes on a common source neighborhood of the node, because that neighborhood maps into the protected $A_x$. Consider also the full node-free operators. Their finite range quotients identify by [F3], and $Kb$ represents the same class in them; hence the full compact augmented operator is onto exactly when the full weighted augmented operator is onto. Their augmented kernels have identical pairs $(b,\xi)$: compact regularity makes a solution with this smooth forcing smooth at the node, and its homogeneous exact real-boundary expansion there is a real constant plus positive integer modes, so it belongs to every weight below $\pi$; the weighted-to-compact implication is the actual global $H^1$ comparison in [F3]. Thus their node-evaluation images coincide. For either augmented operator, imposing zero node value is onto exactly when the full operator is onto and its kernel evaluates onto the node tangent space. Indeed subtract a zero-node solution from a cutoff node lift for one direction, and correct any solution by a kernel element for the other. All relevant ranges are closed: adding $K$ to a Fredholm range changes only a subspace of its finite-dimensional quotient. This proves compact node-fixed universal surjectivity if and only if weighted node-fixed universal surjectivity, with all higher weighted equivalents, using an actual bounded finite-quotient inverse and node lifts. No point trace on ordinary $H^1$ is used. [F2, F3, step 1.1, construct, algebra]

3.1 Suppose the weighted node-fixed universal derivative at a proper unit-domain disk were not onto. Its closed range would have a nonzero weighted $L^2$ orthogonal annihilator $\eta$, by Hilbert orthogonal projection. On any compact interior patch, [F4] makes the metric-weighted representative of $\eta$ smooth and a solution of the actual homogeneous adjoint equation. Unique continuation makes it nonzero on some point of every nonempty source open set; a representative supported only at the boundary would be zero in $L^2$. By [F1], this disk has a nonempty OPEN set of free globally unique interior immersion points. Choose $z_0$ in that open set with $\eta(z_0)\ne0$. Its injective immersion is a local embedding by an invertible two-coordinate projection. Compactness of the closed source and global uniqueness give a small target neighborhood $W\Subset O$ whose full preimage lies in that embedding patch; otherwise a sequence outside that patch would limit to a second preimage of $u(z_0)$. Choose a smooth anti-$J(a)$ field $Y$ supported in $W$ such that $Y u_t(z_0)$ pairs positively with $\eta(z_0)$, using the invariant-plane construction in step1.1. Shrink the patch and the bump so that the pairing is nonnegative everywhere and positive on a source neighborhood. Its weighted integral is strictly positive. Approximate $Y$ uniformly by finite actual parameter tangents from step1.1. The pullbacks of these tangents all vanish on the same source node neighborhood. On its compact complement the weights are bounded, and Cauchy–Schwarz bounds the pairing error by a fixed multiple of the uniform field error times $\|\eta\|_{L^2_\delta}\|du\|_{L^2}$. Some actual parameter pairing is therefore nonzero, contradicting annihilation. The universal weighted derivative is onto, and step2.1 transfers this to the actual compact and every weighted higher realization. Only one OPEN injective site was used; no perturbed dense-injective assertion entered this argument. [F1, F2, F3, F4, step 1.1, step 2.1, construct, contradiction]

4.1 Use the open universal regular zero locus in the actual configuration-parameter product, without assuming an unproved open condition on disk simplicity or domain. At an onto universal derivative the fixed operator $P:E\to Y$ is Fredholm by [F3]. Let $q:Y\to C=Y/\operatorname{Ran}P$. Since $qK$ is onto, choose finitely many parameter lifts $j:C\to\ell^2$ of a basis, and let $Q$ be the bounded range inverse of $P$ on its kernel complement. Then $$f\longmapsto\bigl(jqf,Q(f-Kjqf)\bigr)$$ is a specified bounded right inverse of the universal derivative. The actual zero-chart theorem [F4] gives a split Hilbert chart, and the Neumann inverse makes universal surjectivity open nearby. Its projection to the parameter ball has kernel $\ker P$ and cokernel $C$, by its tangent equation $P\xi+Kb=0$ and the onto map $qK$; hence it is Fredholm of index $\operatorname{ind}P$. This index is locally constant: invert the infinite range block near $P$ by its Neumann series and eliminate it, leaving a finite block with fixed domain-minus-target dimension. The zero locus is second-countable by [F3], so [F4] applies on each open fixed-index locus, for each integer index and each of the finitely many node values. The countable Baire intersection of their residual regular-value sets is dense residual in the one parameter ball. At such a parameter every zero in this universal regular locus has onto fixed operator. Step3.1 places EVERY actual proper unit-domain disk there by its proved open free site. This proves generic node-fixed regularity and its weighted equivalents, without claiming a value for the index or count. [F3, F4, step 2.1, step 3.1, construct, algebra] ∎
