## Statement

Assume Countable Choice. Give a closed oriented Riemann surface $(\Sigma,j_{\mathrm{ref}})$ with positive area form and two transverse size-$m$ attaching-circle systems. Each system bounds disjoint proper compression disks cutting its connected genus-$h$ handlebody into $k\ge2$ balls, with $m=h+k-1>0$. Write $L_\alpha,L_\beta$ for their oriented product tori in the symmetric product. Fix finitely many protected region or marked points off both circle systems.

There is an orientation-preserving diffeomorphism $F:\Sigma\to\Sigma$, supported in finitely many disjoint disks about the surface crossings, such that the ACTUAL integrable complex structure $j=F^*j_{\mathrm{ref}}$ admits holomorphic crossing charts taking the alpha and beta arcs to the real and imaginary axes. Thus $J_0=\operatorname{Sym}^m(j)$ has common holomorphic generator charts taking the two tori to $\mathbb R^m,i\mathbb R^m$.

For this ONE background there is a separable Hilbert ball of smooth autonomous nearly symmetric tame structures $J(a)$ near $J_0$ which preserves its full values on BOTH tori, on fixed protected neighborhoods of the diagonal and point divisors, and on fixed generator neighborhoods. A dense residual subset of its parameters has the following property simultaneously for both systems and every generator node value: every actual disk of unit component domain has surjective compact node-fixed $H^3\to H^2$ linearization, index two, and surjective higher compact and weighted realizations, with weights $0<\delta<\pi$. For each component and generator there are finitely many such disks modulo positive affine maps fixing the boundary node. The finite set may be empty; no count value is asserted.

The surface circles need only be smooth. No simultaneous round-component model, real-oval model, or unproved integrability of an interpolated surface almost complex structure is assumed. The assertion concerns autonomous boundary disks for $k\ge2$; it does not establish strip regularity, the singly pointed sphere alternative, boundary attachment, or the full filtered differential.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The actual balanced two-system data, reference complex structure and finite protected points, with $k\ge2$.

[F1] On a smooth planar disk an orientation-preserving local interior germ extends to a disk diffeomorphism equal to the identity in a boundary collar. Its actual construction and smooth inverse use Countable Choice. ([[lem-smooth-jordan-disk-boundary-maps-extend-to-diffeomorphisms]])

[F2] Surface crossings are finite; positive smooth rectifying charts are constructed by the local inverse of the sum of the two curve parametrizations. Product generator charts, common neighborhood separation and the explicit Cayley taming formula are proved locally. ([[lem-product-integrable-generator-neighborhoods-and-relative-tame-extension]])

[F3] For ANY standard integrable symmetric-product background and smooth attaching circles with the protected real node charts, every nearby proper unit-domain disk has a nonempty OPEN set of free globally unique interior immersion sites, avoiding its own torus, diagonal/divisor neighborhoods and generator neighborhoods. Actual normalized compactness proves this assertion uniformly; the circles are not required to be analytic. ([[lem-near-integrable-proper-unit-domain-disks-have-free-injective-sites]])

[F4] The disk variation construction gives a smooth separable Hilbert Cayley family from rapidly weighted projected bumps in the free open target set. Its finite parameter tangents approximate every compactly supported anti-complex-linear field there. The proof of generic proper-unit disk regularity gives actual compact/weighted augmented-cokernel and node-evaluation comparison, interior adjoint regularity and unique continuation, Hilbert split zero charts and Countable-Choice Sard--Smale for their Fredholm parameter projections. ([[thm-countable-choice-generic-node-fixed-disk-regularity-at-dense-injective-points]], [[thm-countable-choice-near-integrable-proper-unit-domain-disks-are-generically-regular]])

[F5] All actual balanced unit-domain disks have node-fixed index two, for the nearly symmetric tame geometry and every allowed complex background. ([[lem-balanced-boundary-disk-domains-have-twice-component-sum-maslov-index]])

[F6] Actual proper unit-domain disks are globally smoothly compact modulo node-fixing positive affine maps, for coefficient limits with fixed full boundary structure. The affine action is free; its actual smooth local slices on higher zero charts identify an onto index-two disk's local quotient with a point. Compactness and isolation imply finiteness under Countable Choice. ([[lem-proper-unit-domain-boundary-disks-are-compact-modulo-node-fixing-affine-maps]], [[lem-node-fixed-boundary-disk-sobolev-moduli-affine-slices-and-cover-index]])

## Proof

1.1 Let $r$ be one of the finitely many surface crossings in [F2]. Choose an old $j_{\mathrm{ref}}$-holomorphic chart $c_r$ centered at $r$, and [F2]'s positive smooth rectifying chart $\Psi_r$, also centered there. Its coordinate axes are the actual two curve arcs. The local surface diffeomorphism germ $g_r=c_r^{-1}\Psi_r$ fixes $r$ and preserves orientation. Choose disjoint old-coordinate closed round disks about all crossings. Apply [F1] inside each such disk, with identity boundary map and collar, to extend its positive germ $g_r$ to a diffeomorphism of that disk, and extend it by the identity outside. This is legitimate even if the germ does not preserve the whole disk: only its germ is prescribed, and its images on a sufficiently small neighborhood lie in the disk interior. The finitely many supported extensions have disjoint supports; their composition $F$ is an actual positive surface diffeomorphism. Set $j=F^*j_{\mathrm{ref}}$. Its complex charts are exactly the old holomorphic charts composed with $F$, with their old holomorphic transition functions. In particular $c_rF=\Psi_r$ near $r$ is a genuine holomorphic chart for $j$ straightening both arcs. This proves integrability by an explicit atlas, without a surface almost-complex integrability theorem. The supports may miss every protected point. [F1, F2, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 Use these crossing charts in the off-diagonal product generator construction of [F2]. Now its product structure is the ACTUAL $J_0=\operatorname{Sym}^m(j)$, so no later alteration of that standard integrable background is needed. Choose pairwise disjoint compact generator neighborhoods $A_x$ inside these background holomorphic charts, with smaller working charts inside their interiors. Choose a compact protected neighborhood $D$ of the diagonal and the finitely many point divisors, disjoint from BOTH tori and from the $A_x$; this is possible since the compact tori have distinct roots and avoid all those points. All nearly symmetric and tame structures will equal $J_0$ on $$P=D\cup L_\alpha\cup L_\beta\cup\bigcup_x A_x.$$ For the beta boundary charts multiply the product coordinates by $-i$, making that torus real while preserving holomorphy. Thus [F3] applies separately to each system, and intersection of its two smooth neighborhoods gives one neighborhood of $J_0$ valid for both. Additional restrictions to the other torus only take a subspace of the same allowed coefficient class. [F2, F3, step 1.1, construct, algebra]

3.1 Every actual unit-domain disk in this common neighborhood has a free open immersion site avoiding the FULL protected set $P$. For an alpha disk [F3] first supplies a nonempty open site set $V$ avoiding $D$, the $A_x$ and $L_\alpha$. The preimage of $L_\beta$ is closed in $V$ and has empty interior there. Indeed if a source open subset mapped into $L_\beta$, its derivative would have image in $TL_\beta$; the holomorphic equation makes that image $J(a)$-invariant. Total reality of $L_\beta$ then forces the derivative zero, contradicting immersion on $V$. Total reality holds since the full structure on $L_\beta$ is fixed at $J_0$. Hence some point of $V$ misses $L_\beta$, and its nearby points do too. It retains global uniqueness against the entire closed source from [F3]. The same argument with the systems reversed proves the assertion for beta disks. This support argument uses no analytic regularity of either curve family and makes no density claim for perturbed disks beyond the actual open site. [F3, step 2.1, construct, algebra]

4.1 Apply [F4]'s actual projected-bump construction on the open set $O=X\setminus P$. It gives $B(a)=\sum_n a_nc_nB_n$, $a\in\ell^2$, with every $C^q$ norm bounded by $C_q\|a\|_{\ell^2}$ and finite tangents dense in compactly supported anti-$J_0$ fields in $O$. The Cayley formula $J(a)=J_0(I+B(a))(I-B(a))^{-1}$ is smooth in every such norm, fixes $P$ pointwise as a full endomorphism, and its tangent Cayley isomorphism gives the corresponding anti-$J(a)$ approximation. Shrink the Hilbert ball into the common neighborhood of step2.1 and into the tame neighborhood. Tameness here is open on the compact complement of the protected neighborhood; inside it the structure is unchanged. Thus EVERY actual proper unit disk for every parameter has the free open site in step3.1. This is one parameter family for both boundary problems, not two unrelated generic choices. [F2, F4, step 2.1, step 3.1, construct, algebra]

5.1 At any such disk the actual universal node-fixed derivative is $P_u\xi+K_ub$, where $K_ub=(d_aJ[b])(u)u_t$. In [F4]'s weighted realization its range is closed: $P_u$ is Fredholm and adding $K_u$ changes its range only by a subspace of its finite-dimensional quotient. If it were not onto, a nonzero Hilbert orthogonal annihilator would be smooth in the interior by [F4]'s adjoint regularity, and nonzero somewhere in every source open set by its actual unique continuation. Choose such a nonzero point in the free OPEN immersion site from step3.1. Global closed-source uniqueness and compactness give a target neighborhood compactly contained in $O$ whose whole preimage lies in that embedding patch. On the complex line generated by $u_t$ choose an anti-$J(a)$ field whose value on $u_t$ pairs positively with the annihilator, extend by zero on an invariant complement, and localize by a bump. Shrink the patch to keep the pairing positive there; its weighted integral is positive. Approximation by actual finite parameter tangents in step4.1 preserves nonzero pairing, by Cauchy--Schwarz on the compact preimage away from the protected node. This contradicts annihilation. Thus the weighted universal derivative is onto. The compact/weighted comparison in [F4] applies without change: all parameter forcings vanish on one source neighborhood of the node, the full finite cokernel quotients agree, and the augmented kernels have the same node-evaluation image. It therefore gives compact node-fixed universal surjectivity and every claimed higher realization. This argument uses the actual restricted support family, rather than inheriting genericity after shrinking an unrelated parameter family. [F4, step 3.1, step 4.1, construct, contradiction]

6.1 For each system and generator node, use the open universal regular zero locus in [F4]'s second-countable compact node-fixed map charts. An onto universal derivative has a specified split right inverse by lifting a basis of the fixed operator's finite cokernel and using its bounded range inverse. Its Hilbert zero chart and parameter projection are therefore actual smooth Fredholm objects, of fixed index two by [F5]. Countable-Choice Sard--Smale in [F4] makes the regular values residual and dense. Take the finite intersection over the two systems and the finitely many generator nodes; if chart components are separated, the same countable-base intersection in [F4] covers them. Step5.1 places EVERY actual unit-domain disk on this open regular locus, so all those disks have onto fixed compact and weighted operators at a parameter in that residual set. Their index is two by [F5]. Finally [F6] makes each affine orbit isolated and every sequence of such disks, for a fixed component and node, have a normalized smooth subsequence. Its limit is again a unit disk for this parameter and hence regular. The finiteness argument in [F6], using its corrected countable selection of distinct orbits, shows there are finitely many orbits. It allows the empty set. This proves simultaneous regularity and finiteness, while asserting neither existence nor count one, nor strip regularity or cancellation. [F4, F5, F6, step 4.1, step 5.1, algebra] ∎
