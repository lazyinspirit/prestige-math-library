## Statement

Assume Countable Choice. Fix an actual balanced multipointed Heegaard diagram of one oriented knot in $S^3$, with $k\ge2$ cyclically ordered mark pairs and $m=h+k-1>0$, and its positive surface area form. The two attaching systems are transverse, and their proper compression disks cut their handlebodies into $k$ balls; broad auxiliary boundary tangencies are allowed.

There is a CHOICE of orientation-compatible integrable surface structure $j$, protected diagonal/region/marked-divisor neighborhoods, common exact product generator charts, and ONE smooth nearly symmetric tame path $J_t$ constant on genuine positive endpoint time collars, such that EVERY actual nonconstant finite-energy strip of real Fredholm index one or two has onto actual $H^1\to L^2$ linearization. The same holds in every integer higher realization. The autonomous endpoint structures have regular node-fixed index-two unit-component disks for both boundary systems, with finitely many such disks modulo node-fixing positive affine maps at every generator.

There are finitely many index-one strip orbits modulo longitudinal translation, across ALL ordered generators, realized classes and ordered nonnegative $w,z$ multiplicities. In particular their full-marked or $w$-weighted index-one counts are finite sums. No $n_z=0$ restriction is made.

This is existence of auxiliary choices and low-index finite counts for $k\ge2$. It does not establish comparison with arbitrary preassigned surface complex structures or paths, any absolute grading normalization, a value for a unit boundary-disk count, square-zero or invariance of the full filtered differential, the singly pointed case, or polygon move counts.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The actual balanced $S^3$ knot diagram, $k\ge2$, area form and smooth attaching data.

[F1] One integrable background with common generator charts admits a protected autonomous structure $J_e$ regular for EVERY unit-component disk on BOTH tori at every generator, with node-fixed index two and finitely many affine orbits. It preserves the full standard structure on both tori and all protected sets. ([[thm-common-integrable-background-gives-simultaneous-balanced-boundary-disk-regularity]])

[F2] With the constant background path $J_e$, the flat-endpoint-jet theorem supplies an actual all-strip regular auxiliary path $J^*$ and smooth approximating paths $J_n$ constant on genuine endpoint collars. Their endpoint values, full boundary structures, protected spatial sets and exact generator charts agree. The auxiliary path itself need not have a collar. ([[thm-countable-choice-flat-endpoint-jet-universal-strip-regularity]])

[F3] In any such small fixed-geometry coefficient neighborhood, ALL actual strips in the finite index set $\{1,2\}$ have finitely many realized domains and one uniform energy bound, with ALL positive $w,z$ degrees included. ([[lem-balanced-knot-strips-in-bounded-index-sets-have-finite-domains-and-energy]])

[F4] Actual low-index compactness with regular limiting strips gives precisely one or two unbubbled strip levels, or at index two a constant generator plus one unit-component disk. The sphere alternative is absent because $k\ge2$. At a limit of the last kind with a regular node-fixed disk, actual nearby strip operators are onto even for varying paths without a common positive time-collar width. ([[lem-balanced-low-index-strip-limits-have-only-unit-boundary-degenerations]], [[lem-regular-boundary-bubble-limits-cannot-create-strip-cokernels]])

[F5] The proof of the zero-marked collar-openness supplier constructs an actual squared-cutoff parametrix for ANY given unbubbled finite collection of regular strip levels: its steps1.1–4.1 use actual core convergence, common exact generator charts and collapsing gaps only. The zero-mark premise there supplies unbubbled compactness; it is NOT used as a premise for the separate construction repeated below. ([[lem-zero-marked-bounded-energy-strip-regularity-survives-collar-approximation]])

[F6] Actual $H^3\cap X_0$ strip map charts and their smooth section, split zero charts, higher Fredholm range agreement and the whole constant transverse-strip inverse are proved locally. ([[lem-higher-sobolev-strip-map-charts-and-cauchy-riemann-section]], [[lem-countable-choice-quantitative-newton-correction-and-smooth-zero-chart]], [[lem-strip-fredholm-index-agrees-in-higher-sobolev-spaces]], [[lem-transverse-linear-strip-cauchy-riemann-isomorphism]])

## Proof

1.1 Choose the actual integrable surface background, protected sets and common exact generator charts in [F1], including its one autonomous regular endpoint structure $J_e$. Use it as the constant background path in [F2]. The protected spatial set there is just the diagonal/divisor neighborhood and compact generator neighborhoods; it need not include both entire tori for interior times. The endpoint jets are fixed, so its all-strip regular path $J^*$ and constant-collar approximants have endpoint structures exactly $J_e$ and the same full boundary structures. Shrink all coefficient neighborhoods to retain tameness and uniform protected geometry. By [F3], for the paths $J_n$ and their limit there is one energy bound $E_0$ for every strip of index one or two. This choice uses the finite ACTUAL index set, not zero-marked compactness or an assumed index-domain formula. [F1, F2, F3, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 Suppose arbitrarily late approximants had a nonregular strip of index one or two. Countable Choice selects a sequence of them with increasing approximation indices; a subsequence fixes the actual index and endpoints. By the actual compactness underlying [F4] and the bound in step1.1 there is a finite bubbled limit for $J^*$. Every nonconstant limiting strip is regular by [F2]. Thus [F4] applies. If it is a constant strip with one unit disk, that disk's frozen structure is exactly $J_e$ on its respective boundary. It is node-fixed regular of index two by [F1]. The second assertion of [F4] makes every sufficiently late operator onto, contradicting its choice. The fact that the approximating autonomous collars shrink is explicitly allowed there. [F1, F2, F4, step 1.1, construct, contradiction]

3.1 For completeness handle the other actual limits by the explicit operator construction, without importing a zero-mark conclusion. They have one or two nonconstant regular levels $v_j$, no bubbles, smooth translated core convergence and collapsing gaps in exact common generator charts. Give each onto level its bounded right inverse $Q_j:L^2\to H^1$. On fixed translated cores use [F6]'s boundary-preserving local-addition identification $T_{j,n}$ of tangent bundles; on their matching generator ends it is the exact coordinate identity. The coefficient errors there tend to zero in $H^1\to L^2$ norm and are supported on fixed cores; off them both operators are exactly $D_0$. Choose longitudinal cutoffs $\eta_{j,n}$ with $\sum_j\eta_{j,n}^2=1$, equal one on the level cores, transitioning in gaps of widths $R_n\to\infty$, with $|\eta'_{j,n}|\le C/R_n$. For one level take its cutoff identically one. Translate $\eta_{j,n}f$ to the corresponding level, identify it and extend it by zero as an $L^2$ forcing, and call it $f_j$. Then $$Q_n^0f=\sum_j\eta_{j,n}T_{j,n}Q_jf_j$$ is an actual section with the right physical boundary trace and uniformly bounded $L^2\to H^1$ norm. The exact first-order Leibniz identity gives $$D_{u_n}Q_n^0=I+E_n,\qquad\|E_n\|\le C(\delta_n+R_n^{-1})\longrightarrow0,$$ where $\delta_n$ is the fixed-core coefficient error. The infinite tails contribute no coefficient error since their charts and operators are exact. Its Neumann-corrected right inverse contradicts nonregularity. These are precisely the actual local constructions in [F5], with unbubbled compactness here proved by [F4]. Together with step2.1 this proves that all sufficiently late constant-collar approximants are regular for EVERY index-one or index-two strip. Higher range agreement in [F6] gives regularity at every integer order. Fix one such allowed path. [F4, F5, F6, step 2.1, construct, algebra]

4.1 All its unit-component boundary disks are still those for $J_e$, hence have the finiteness and regularity in [F1]. For the fixed path take any sequence of index-one strips. The energy bound [F3] and [F4] give, after translations, one nonconstant index-one strip limit, no bubbles, and no energy or image diameter lost in its two outer tails. This gives actual GLOBAL $H^3$ convergence, not just local smooth convergence: choose fixed sufficiently far transition bands on the two ends, where all sequence maps and the limit lie in the same exact generator charts. Their difference $w_n$ is smooth, belongs individually to $H^3$ on the tails by the actual end theory in [F6], and solves $D_0w_n=0$ there. Multiply by a fixed end cutoff $\chi$ and extend by zero to the whole strip. Then $$D_0(\chi w_n)=\chi' w_n,\qquad\|\chi w_n\|_{H^3}\le C\|\chi' w_n\|_{H^2}\longrightarrow0.$$ The whole transverse-strip inverse and higher estimate in [F6] justify the inequality with only the original real boundary trace. Smooth convergence on the fixed transition bands gives its limit zero. On the remaining compact core local smooth convergence and the local-addition charts give $H^3$ convergence. Thus after translation the sequence converges in the actual global configuration chart. [F1, F3, F4, F6, step 3.1, construct, algebra]

5.1 At its regular index-one limit, [F6]'s actual fixed-parameter zero set is a smooth one-dimensional manifold. The nonzero kernel vector $u_s$ is tangent to longitudinal translation, belongs to $H^3$ by its smooth exponential ends, and spans that kernel. The translation curve of this ONE fixed smooth strip is smooth as an $H^3$ curve: its successive derivatives are the longitudinal derivatives of the smooth strip, all square integrable with the same fixed generator offsets by the exact end theory. In the one-dimensional zero chart its derivative is nonzero, so the ordinary scalar inverse theorem makes its translation orbit contain a neighborhood of this solution. This does not assume differentiability of joint translation on the entire rough $H^3$ map space. Step4.1 therefore says every convergent translated index-one sequence is eventually in the limit's orbit. If there were infinitely many distinct orbits, Countable Choice selects an ordered $n$-tuple of distinct orbits for each positive integer $n$. Flattening the finite tuples and retaining first occurrences gives an infinite sequence of distinct orbits; Countable Choice then selects representatives. Step4.1 supplies a subsequence which is eventually one orbit, a contradiction. There are consequently finitely many orbits across all generators and realized classes. Their actual nonnegative marked degrees give finite weighted sums. No value of a unit-disk count or square-zero assertion follows from this finiteness argument. [F3, F4, F6, step 4.1, construct, contradiction] ∎
