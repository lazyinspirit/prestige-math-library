## Mathematical checks

- The compact-pair theorem chooses coefficient functions from sets, transfers witnesses to canonical GNS carriers, and forms a set-indexed Hilbert direct sum; it never chooses a family from a proper class. The quantitative clause uses the invariant-space complement and handles empty tests and zero vectors.
- Fell isolation is checked through the exact closure/kernel interface and separation of arbitrary C*-algebras. Injectivity of the trivial character on the intersection ideal constructs a central projection; a weakly contained trivial representation makes its image nonzero, and nondegeneracy plus the representation correspondence produces fixed vectors. No separability assumption is imported.
- Compact generation uses open compactly generated subgroups containing each compact test, an H-fixed Dirac vector in a quasi-regular summand, and finite index forced by a nonzero invariant coordinate. The quotient proof verifies openness, Hausdorffness and continuous operations before pulling representations back.
- Finite Haar volume uses disjoint compact translates and invariant L2 mass; it does not select a single representative fixed simultaneously outside an uncountable union of null sets. Haar averaging constructs uniform Borel simple approximants and checks translation invariance before taking the Bochner limit. The finite example computes the orthogonal projection directly.
- The real relative-(T) proof checks second countability, separable cyclic GNS carriers, the inverse-transpose dual action, the zero spectral projection and weak limits of projective probabilities. Normality makes the fixed-space complement invariant. The extra compact-generator clause in the normal-relative lemma uses a compact Hausdorff coefficient image, the complex C0 dual and Mazur's theorem; it does not assume a uniform bound on word lengths in an arbitrary compact set.
- The elimination supplier counts pivot repairs and row/column clearing and verifies the six-transvection diagonal identity. The SLn application embeds the required semidirect products, controls every root subgroup, telescopes over bounded generation and obtains a nonzero least-norm vector in the invariant closed convex orbit hull.
- The SL2 suppliers use the positive weighted completion, not the ordinary L2 norm. The opposite-parameter intertwiner gives exponent 1−nu in the spherical coefficient; compact-uniform convergence supplies near-invariant vectors. Nontrivial K-lines and irreducibility exclude fixed vectors. A single direct sum of a cofinal parameter sequence witnesses failure of property (T); no fixed complementary-series summand is asserted to have almost invariant vectors.
- The integer example's explicit rational circle parametrization has nonzero imaginary part, and the geometric-sum estimate treats positive, negative and zero exponents, including an empty test set.
