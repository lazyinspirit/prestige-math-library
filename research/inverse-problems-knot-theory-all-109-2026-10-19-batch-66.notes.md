- **lem-square-integer-cokernel-finiteness-and-order** (level 0, lemma; Step 1: ready). For a finite square integer matrix A, coker(A) = Z^n/AZ^n is finite if and only if det(A) is nonzero, and then its order is |det(A)|. For the empty 0 by 0 matrix, the determinant is 1 and the cokernel is the trivial group. Direct deps: none.
- **lem-cyclic-cover-linking-form-inverse-matrix** (level 0, lemma; Step 1: ready). Assume AC. Let J be an oriented n-component link in S^3 with n>=1, chosen disjoint tubes, positive meridians mu_i and zero-self-linking longitudes lambda_i oriented as J_i. Let X be the compact link exterior; Alexander duality identifies H_1(X;Z) with Z^n on the meridians, and lk(J_i,J_j), for i!=j, is the j-th coordinate of [lambda_i]. Use mu_i dot lambda_i=+1. Give J_i integral framing f_i and fill along delta_i=lambda_i+f_i mu_i. Let A be symmetric with diagonal f_i and off-diagonal entries lk(J_i,J_j), and assume det(A) is nonzero. Orient Y as the boundary of the 4-dimensional surgery handlebody with outward normal first. Then H_1(Y;Z)=Z^n/AZ^n. For torsion classes define lambda_Y([C],[D])=(F dot D)/m mod Z when partial F=mC. For meridian-coordinate representatives x,y, lambda_Y([x],[y])=-x^T A^{-1}y mod Z.
