## Mathematically sound closure strategy

Separate the **literal section edit**, the **claim actually used**, and the **review event**. For these four suppliers, the sine, complex-exponential, and chain-homotopy changes create no new downstream `Statement`/`Definition` obligation: inspect their direct uses where needed to check the repaired supplier itself, then stop at unchanged consumer statements. Heine–Cantor merits direct review of uses that assert a choice strength and of ordinary uses of its uniform-continuity conclusion. Its current proof licenses both sampled uses. Propagate beyond a direct consumer only if a necessary repair changes that consumer's own `Statement`/`Definition`; an unchanged direct theorem cannot transmit a new claim to its hundreds of descendants merely because its `deps` list names the supplier.

The existing Step-5 receipt still has 5,877 pending item-level rows and 734 changed surface hashes. These four supplier findings do **not** close those rows: an item may also be reached by another supplier with a real claim change, and the Step-5 gate still requires its recorded dispositions. For each row, record the specific changed supplier/clause and actual consumer use, identify unaffected citations, and examine every independent changed supplier that reaches the item. Do not infer a pass for the whole row from this sample, blanket-approve descendants, rewrite the receipt mechanically, or retry the held gate before the remaining obligations are honestly resolved.
