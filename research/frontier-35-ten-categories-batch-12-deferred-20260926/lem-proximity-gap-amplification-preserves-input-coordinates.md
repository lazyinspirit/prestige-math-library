## Statement

Let $\Sigma$ be a finite alphabet with $|\Sigma|\ge2$, let $K=20/7$ and $D=387$, and let $P$ be an assignment tester with alphabet $\Sigma$, rejection ratio $\rho>0$, and bounded-arity output constraints whose outputs are **input-balanced**: for every circuit $C$ with named inputs $X$, every named input coordinate occurs in exactly the same number $d_X\ge1$ of the constraints of $P(C,X)$ (the case of interest in this page is arity two; the general arity is handled by the same construction). Then there are a constant $t_1\ge1$, a finite alphabet $\Sigma_{\rm out}$ and, for every integer $t\ge t_1$, a constant $C_t<\infty$, all of them depending only on $|\Sigma|$, on the arity and output size of $P$ and, for $C_t$, on $t$ — the size bound really does grow with $t$, since the powering lists $(2D)^{2t+1}$ slots per port — such that for every integer $t\ge t_1$ there is an assignment tester $P_t$ with

- **the same named input coordinates** as $P$, labeled by bits;
- finite alphabet $\Sigma_{\rm out}$ (fixed, independent of the input circuit) and binary constraints;
- perfect completeness, and rejection ratio at least $\min\{2\rho,t^{-1}\}$;
- output size at most $C_t\cdot M$, where $M$ is the number of constraints in the output of $P$;
- an output system that is again input-balanced: its variables include the named input coordinates, and each of them occurs the same number of times in the constraints of $P_t(C,X)$;

and $P_t$ is computed from $P$'s output by a deterministic algorithm that enumerates all its random choices, in time polynomial in the size of that output and in $t$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** a finite alphabet $\Sigma$, $|\Sigma|\ge2$, an assignment tester $P$ with alphabet $\Sigma$, ratio $\rho>0$ and output system $G=P(C,X)$ for a circuit $C$ with named inputs $X=(x_1,\dots,x_n)$, an integer $t\ge4$, the degree-reduced graph $G_2=R_{\deg}(G)$ and its powering $H:=(G_2)_t$; write $M:=|E(G)|$ and $\delta:=\delta(x,\operatorname{SAT}(C))$ for the fixed input $x$.

[F1] If $E(G)\ne\varnothing$ then $G_2$ is $D$-regular over $\Sigma$ with $2M$ vertices and $DM$ edges, produced deterministically in polynomial time, and for every labeling $\tau$ of $G_2$ the decoded labeling $D\tau$ of $G$ satisfies $\operatorname{UNSAT}_{D\tau}(G)\le DK\operatorname{UNSAT}_\tau(G_2)$; if $\operatorname{val}(G)=1$ then $\operatorname{val}(G_2)=1$ ([[thm-degree-reduction-preserves-unsatisfaction]], [[def-degree-reduction-by-expander-clouds]]).

[F2] For a $D$-regular graph the powered graph $H$ of [[def-constraint-graph-powering]] has vertex set the $2M$ vertices of $G_2$, one slot for every pair (vertex, lazy pattern) of its $L=2t+1$ lazy steps, degree $d_t=(2D)^{2t+1}=O(1)$, view alphabet $\Sigma_t:=\Sigma^{\mathcal P_R}$ with $R=t+\lceil\sqrt t\rceil$, and explicit relation tables produced by enumerating patterns; the canonical lift of a labeling $\sigma$ of $G$ satisfies every constrained slot whenever $\sigma$ satisfies every edge of $G$ ([[lem-canonical-local-view-lift-preserves-perfect-satisfiability]]).

[F3] There are constants $\beta_0>0$ and $t_0$ depending only on $D$, $|\Sigma|$ and the spectral bound $\rho_2<1$ of the overlay, such that for every $t\ge t_0$ and every labeling $\varphi$ of $H$: $\operatorname{UNSAT}_\varphi(H)\ge\beta_0\sqrt t\,\min(\operatorname{UNSAT}(G_2),t^{-1})$ ([[lem-powering-amplifies-small-gaps]]).

[F4] Plurality decoding of a powered labeling $\varphi$ gives a labeling $\hat\varphi$ of $G_2$ ([[def-plurality-decoding-of-powered-local-views]]), and cloud plurality decoding of a labeling $\tau$ of $G_2$ gives a labeling $D\tau$ of $G$ with the pointwise inequality of [F1] ([[lem-cloud-consistency-forces-near-constant-labels]], [[thm-degree-reduction-preserves-unsatisfaction]]).

[F5] $P$ has the completeness and proximity-soundness clauses of [[def-assignment-tester-and-rejection-ratio]] with ratio $\rho$, where $\delta(a,\operatorname{SAT}(C))=1$ whenever $\operatorname{SAT}(C)=\varnothing$ and otherwise is the least relative Hamming distance to an accepted input; values of a constraint system are fractions of listed constraints, duplicated constraints counting with multiplicity ([[def-constraint-graph-and-labeling-value]]).

[F6] The composition of an assignment tester with the inner tester of [[lem-exponential-base-assignment-tester-from-quadratic-oracles]] is an assignment tester with alphabet $\{0,1\}$, constant arity and the same named input coordinates, whose ratio is the outer ratio times the constant $\varepsilon'/(qs)>0$ and whose output size is at most a constant $s+B$ times the outer size, where $s$ is the number of outer alphabet symbols and $B<\infty$ is the constant output bound of the inner tester ([[lem-constant-alphabet-assignment-tester-composition]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** constructive.

1.1 **Degree reduction and powering.** Assume first $E(G)\ne\varnothing$ and let $G_2=R_{\deg}(G)$ be the degree-reduced graph of [F1] and $H=(G_2)_t$ its powering as in [F2] with view alphabet $\Sigma_t=\Sigma^{\mathcal P_R}$. The powered vertices are the $2M$ ports of the cloud construction, each carrying a view; write $\varphi$ for a labeling of $H$. If $E(G)=\varnothing$ then the output is declared empty, so assume $M\ge1$. [F1, F2, construct]

2.1 **The comparison constraints.** The vertices of $H'$ will be the ports of step 1.1 together with the input coordinates. Write $\kappa:\{0,1\}\to\Sigma_t$ for the embedding of a bit as the corresponding constant view, and fix a reference pattern $\pi_0\in\mathcal P_R$ (say the all-holds pattern). For every named input coordinate $x_i$, every port $z$ of the cloud of $x_i$, and every lazy pattern $\pi\in\mathcal P_t$ read from $z$, let $w$ be the endpoint of $\pi$ and let $\pi^\ast$ be the length-$R$ reversal of $\pi$ extended by holds, so that the view at $w$ assigns the claim $\varphi(w)(\pi^\ast)$ to $z$ in the sense of [[def-plurality-decoding-of-powered-local-views]]. Add the binary **comparison constraint** on the pair of variables $(w,x_i)$ with relation
$$R_{\rm cmp}:=\bigl\{(A,B)\in\Sigma_t^2:\ A(\pi^\ast)=B(\pi_0)\bigr\}.$$
Call $H'$ the graph whose vertices are the ports together with the $n$ input coordinates and whose slots are the slots of $H$ together with these comparison slots. The input coordinates are labeled by $\kappa(0)$ and $\kappa(1)$, which the page identifies with the bits $0$ and $1$; by the input balance of the statement each named input coordinate has exactly $d_X$ ports in the cloud construction, so coordinate $x_i$ carries exactly $d_X(2D)^t$ comparison slots and the comparison family has $n_C:=nd_X(2D)^t=\bigl(\sum_i\deg(x_i)\bigr)(2D)^t\le qM(2D)^t$ slots in all, where $q$ is the arity bound of $P$ (for the binary testers of this page $q=2$ and the bounds read $\le2M(2D)^t$ and $\le4M$), while $n\le nd_X=\sum_i\deg(x_i)\le qM$ gives $|V(H')|=2M+n\le(2+q)M$. Let $n_H:=|E(H)|=2M(2D)^{2t+1}$ be the number of powered slots. If $n=0$ there are no comparison slots and $H'$ is $H$ itself; otherwise $n_C\ge(2D)^t>0$ and we reweigh the two families by whole-list replication: list every comparison slot exactly $r:=\lceil n_H/n_C\rceil\ge1$ times, so that the replicated comparison family has $r\,n_C\le n_H+n_C\le2n_H$ slots, the comparison family carries at least half of the weight of $H'$, the powered family carries at least a third, and $|E(H')|\le3n_H\le6M(2D)^{2t+1}$. Whole-list replication leaves the fraction of violated constraints inside the replicated family equal to the fraction inside the original comparison list, which is what will be used in step 3.3 below. Hence every input coordinate carries exactly the same number $r\,d_X(2D)^t$ of comparison slots and $|E(H')|\le C_tM$ for a constant $C_t$ depending only on $|\Sigma|$ and $t$; the equal multiplicity of the coordinates is what makes the counting of step 3.3 below exact. [F2, step 1.1, construct, algebra]

3.1 **Completeness.** Suppose $x\in\operatorname{SAT}(C)$ and let $\sigma$ be a labeling of $G$ with $\sigma|_X=x$ and $\operatorname{UNSAT}_\sigma(G)=0$, which exists by the completeness clause of [F5]. Lift $\sigma$ to the cloud labeling $\sigma_1$ of $G_2$ (all ports of a vertex carry the decoded label) and then to the canonical view labeling $\varphi$ of $H$; by [F2] and [F1] this labeling satisfies every powered slot, and $D\varphi$'s restriction to the input coordinates is $x$ by construction. Label the input coordinate $x_i$ by $\kappa(x_i)$; every comparison constraint is satisfied because for a pattern $\pi$ from a port $z$ of $x_i$ the canonical view at the endpoint reports $\sigma_1(z)=x_i$, so $\varphi(w)(\pi^\ast)=x_i=\kappa(x_i)(\pi_0)$ for every choice of $(\pi_0)$'s value. Hence $\operatorname{UNSAT}=0$ for this labeling of $H'$, and the padding introduces no violations. [F1, F2, F5, step 2.1, algebra]

3.2 **Soundness, close case.** Fix a labeling of the system $H'$ of step 2.1 in which the input coordinates carry the bits $x_1,\dots,x_n$ (through $\kappa$), and let $\varphi$ be its restriction to the ports. Put $\tau:=\hat\varphi$, the plurality decoding of $\varphi$ (a labeling of $G_2$), and $\sigma:=D\tau$, the cloud decoding (a labeling of $G$). Suppose first that $\operatorname{rdist}(\sigma|_X,x)\le\delta/2$. If $\operatorname{SAT}(C)\ne\varnothing$ then $\operatorname{rdist}(\sigma|_X,\operatorname{SAT}(C))\ge\delta-\delta/2=\delta/2$ by the triangle inequality, while if $\operatorname{SAT}(C)=\varnothing$ the value $\delta(\sigma|_X,\operatorname{SAT}(C))$ equals $1\ge\delta/2$ by the convention of [F5]; in both cases the proximity-soundness clause of [F5] gives $\operatorname{UNSAT}_\sigma(G)\ge\rho\,\delta/2$. By [F1] with $\tau$ in place of the labeling there, $\operatorname{UNSAT}_\tau(G_2)\ge\operatorname{UNSAT}_\sigma(G)/(DK)\ge\rho\delta/(2DK)$, so by [F3] the powered part of the labeling violates at least
$$\operatorname{UNSAT}_\varphi(H)\ \ge\ \beta_0\sqrt t\,\min\Bigl\{\frac{\rho\delta}{2DK},\,\frac1t\Bigr\}\ \ge\ \frac{\beta_0\delta}{2DK}\sqrt t\,\min\Bigl\{\rho,\frac1t\Bigr\}$$
of the powered slots, the last step because $\delta\le1$ and $\min\{a\delta,1/t\}\ge\delta\min\{a,1/t\}$ for $\delta\le1$. By step 2.1 the powered family carries at least a third of the weight of $H'$ (all of it when $n=0$), while the comparison slots contribute nonnegative violations, so the fraction of violated slots of $H'$ is at least one third of the fraction $\operatorname{UNSAT}_\varphi(H)$ of violated powered slots, that is at least $\frac{\beta_0\delta}{6DK}\sqrt t\,\min(\rho,\frac1t)$. [F1, F3, F4, F5, step 2.1, algebra]

3.3 **Soundness, far case.** Suppose instead that $\operatorname{rdist}(\sigma|_X,x)>\delta/2$, so more than $\delta n/2$ of the coordinates $x_i$ satisfy $\sigma(x_i)\ne x_i$. Fix such a coordinate $i$. Since $\sigma(x_i)$ is by construction the most frequent label of the cloud of $x_i$ and it differs from the symbol $x_i$, at most half of the $d_X$ ports $z$ of that cloud satisfy $\tau(z)=x_i$, that is, at least $d_X/2$ ports satisfy $\tau(z)\ne x_i$; and for such a port $z$, since $\tau(z)$ is the most frequent claim of the endpoints of the $(2D)^t$ lazy patterns read from $z$ and it differs from $x_i$, at least half of those patterns give an endpoint view claiming a value different from $x_i$, each such pattern being a comparison slot that the labeling violates. Hence at least $\tfrac14d_X(2D)^t$ comparison slots of coordinate $i$ are violated, and because by step 2.1 every input coordinate carries exactly the same number $r\,d_X(2D)^t$ of comparison slots, the fraction of the replicated comparison family that is violated is at least $\frac14\cdot\frac{\delta}{2}=\frac{\delta}{8}$. By step 2.1 the comparison family carries at least half of the weight of $H'$, so $\operatorname{UNSAT}_{H'}\ge\frac12\cdot\frac{\delta}{8}=\delta/16$. [F2, F4, F5, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 **Restoring a fixed alphabet, and binary arity.** By steps 3.1, 3.2 and 3.3 the system $H'$ is an assignment tester with alphabet $\Sigma_t$, arity two, the named input coordinates $X$ and rejection ratio $\min\{\frac1{16},\frac{\beta_0}{6DK}\sqrt t\min(\rho,\frac1t)\}$. Its alphabet has $s_t=|\Sigma|^{(2D)^R}$ symbols, a constant for fixed $|\Sigma|$ and $t$; compose it with the inner tester of [F6], whose alphabet is $\{0,1\}$ and whose rejection ratio and output bound are absolute constants $\varepsilon'>0$ and $B<\infty$. The composition is an assignment tester with the same input coordinates, alphabet $\{0,1\}$, arity at most $6$ and ratio at least $\frac{\varepsilon'}{qs_t}\min\{\frac1{16},\frac{\beta_0}{6DK}\sqrt t\min(\rho,\frac1t)\}$, where $q$ is the arity bound of $P$ and $q=2$ for the binary testers of this page. The raw input coordinates enter the composed system only through the comparison constraints — the powered constraints involve ports and the equality edges of the clouds, never a raw coordinate — and each of those inner systems is the tester of [[lem-exponential-base-assignment-tester-from-quadratic-oracles]] applied to a robustized circuit of one fixed canonical shape and size, whose comparison family $(\mathrm C)$ contains every named input bit with the same multiplicity and is then duplicated by the common factor $L/(n2^N)$; since coordinate $x_i$ occurs in exactly $r\,d_X(2D)^t$ comparison constraints before the composition, it occurs the same number of times as every other input coordinate afterwards. Finally convert each constraint of arity $k\le6$ over $\{0,1\}$, with relation $R\subseteq\{0,1\}^k$, into a binary gadget over the fixed alphabet $\Sigma_{\rm out}:=\bigcup_{0\le k\le6}\{0,1\}^k$ of $127$ symbols: introduce one fresh tuple vertex, one loop restricting its label to $R\subseteq\Sigma_{\rm out}$, and $k$ projection slots joining it to the $k$ variables with the relations $\{(\vec a,s):\vec a\in\{0,1\}^k,\ s=a_j\}$ for $j=1,\dots,k$. If the tuple vertex carries a label of $R$ and all projections hold then the original constraint is satisfied, so every violated original constraint makes at least one of its $k+1\le7$ gadget slots violated; the conversion therefore multiplies the number of slots by at most $7$ and divides the violation fraction by at most $7$, gives a binary system over the fixed alphabet $\Sigma_{\rm out}$, and leaves every input coordinate untouched, each occurrence of a variable in a pre-conversion constraint giving rise to exactly one projection slot on that variable and so preserving the input balance. [F5, F6, step 2.1, step 3.1, step 3.2, step 3.3, construct, algebra]

5.1 Choose $$t_1\ge\max\Bigl\{4,\ t_0,\ \frac{300\,s_t}{\varepsilon'},\ \Bigl(\frac{180\,s_tDK}{\varepsilon'\beta_0}\Bigr)^{2}\Bigr\},$$ so that $t\ge t_1$ implies $\frac{\varepsilon'}{224\,s_t}\ge\frac1t$ and $\frac{\varepsilon'\beta_0\sqrt t}{84\,s_tDK}\ge2$: indeed $t\ge300s_t/\varepsilon'$ gives $\varepsilon'/(224s_t)\ge300/(224t)>1/t$, and $t\ge(180s_tDK/(\varepsilon'\beta_0))^2$ gives $\varepsilon'\beta_0\sqrt t/(84s_tDK)\ge180/84>2$. Let $C_t$ be the constant of step 4.1 combined with the factor $s_t+B$ of the composition. Then for every $t\ge t_1$ the constructed tester $P_t$ has the same named input coordinates, alphabet $\Sigma_{\rm out}$, binary constraints, perfect completeness by step 3.1, output size at most $C_tM$, and rejection ratio at least $\min\{2\rho,t^{-1}\}$: when $\rho\le t^{-1}$ the ratio is at least $\frac{\varepsilon'\beta_0}{84s_tDK}\sqrt t\,\rho\ge2\rho$, and when $\rho>t^{-1}$ it is at least $\min\{\frac{\varepsilon'}{224s_t},\frac{\varepsilon'\beta_0}{84s_tDK\sqrt t}\}\ge\frac1t$; the far-case factor $1/16$ and the close-case factor $1/3$ of steps 3.2 and 3.3 and the factor at most $7$ of the arity conversion are all absorbed into these constants. This proves the statement with $\Sigma_{\rm out}$ and, for each $t$, $C_t$ depending only on $|\Sigma|$, on $t$ and on the arity bound of $P$. [step 2.1, step 3.1, step 3.2, step 3.3, step 4.1, discharge-construct] ∎

## Remarks

- **What each step costs.** Degree reduction costs the constant factor $DK$ of [F1] in the violation fraction and turns the undecoded cloud label into the decoded one; the powering multiplies the violation fraction by $\beta_0\sqrt t$ in the small-gap regime and saturates at $1/t$; the comparison family, replicated to carry at least half the weight of $H'$, charges $\delta/16$ in the far case, while the powered family's weight of at least a third costs a factor $3$ in the close case; the composition with the fixed inner tester costs the constant $\varepsilon'/(2s_t)$; the arity conversion costs at most a factor $7$. All factors are constants for fixed $\Sigma$ and $t$, and the only requirement on $t$ is that it is large enough that the amplified constant still beats $2\rho$ and that the saturated value exceeds $1/t$.
- **Why the input coordinates survive.** The comparison constraints attach the raw coordinate $x_i$, as a vertex of $H'$, to the powered views of the ports of its cloud, so no renaming or re-encoding of the input happens anywhere: the coordinate list $X$ and its bits are the same before and after, and the composition of [F6] uses the identity encoding on them. This is the property that lets the iteration of [[thm-constant-query-assignment-tester]] close over the same circuit inputs, and it is the reason the construction is stated for an arbitrary assignment tester rather than only for the trivial one.
- **The bounded-degree convention is not needed.** The degree reduction of [[def-degree-reduction-by-expander-clouds]] accepts a constraint graph of arbitrary degree and outputs a $D$-regular graph, so no bounded-degree hypothesis is imposed on the outer tester, and the hypotheses used are bounded arity and the input balance of the statement. The latter is needed because the far case weighs all input coordinates equally: each coordinate contributes exactly $r\,d_X(2D)^t$ comparison slots, which is what turns the pointwise estimate of step 3.3 into a bound proportional to $\delta$. A tester that is not input-balanced is made one by appending copies of its input loops.
