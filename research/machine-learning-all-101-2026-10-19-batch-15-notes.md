## Prerequisites and conventions checked

- `def-positive-semidefinite-kernels-and-reproducing-kernel-hilbert-spaces` defines real symmetric finite-Gram PSD kernels, the RKHS reproducing identity, and the quotient/completion construction. `thm-canonical-rkhs-construction-and-reproducing-property` proves the semidefinite-form Cauchy–Schwarz step, null-space quotient, completion, bounded evaluations, reproduction, and injective function identification.
- The canonical completion item explicitly assumes **countable choice**. It first uses the published metric-completion existence and compatible Banach-structure theorems, then the published inner-product completion theorem; each uses `AC_ω`. This supplies the completion object and extended Hilbert inner product without relying on full AC. The general representer theorem and SVM primal/dual results are stated conditionally on an already-given RKHS/feature Hilbert space and use no choice principle.
- `thm-orthogonal-projection-is-the-unique-nearest-point` is stated only for a finite-dimensional ambient space. The local projection lemma applies it to `V=span(M∪{f})`, which is finite-dimensional even when the ambient RKHS is not; the local lemma then supplies the residual-orthogonality and norm-contraction facts used by consumers.
- `cor-real-spectral-theorem-for-self-adjoint-endomorphisms` applies to a real finite-dimensional self-adjoint operator. The Gram-factorization proof applies it to the symmetric sample Gram matrix on `R^n`, then checks each eigenvalue is nonnegative from the PSD quadratic form.
- The SVM primal uses the unpenalized bias, `C>0`, `y_i∈{-1,+1}`, and both margin and nonnegative-slack inequalities with nonnegative multipliers. Primal attainment is proved first: an all-one-class sample has an explicit zero-objective solution; with both classes present, the finite feature span, objective sublevel bounds, two bias bounds, and the ZF Euclidean extreme-value theorem give attainment. Strict feasibility uses `w=b=0, xi_i=2`; the primal has zero equality rows. The dual bias equality has one independent row because the label vector is nonzero. Slater is invoked only after the finite attained primal value is established.
- No claim of hard-margin feasibility is made without the stated margin-separability condition. The score is a real-valued margin score; neither the definition nor the proof assigns it a calibrated probability. No numerical solver convergence claim is made.
