## Definition

Let $X$ be a set and let $\mathcal E\subseteq\mathcal P(X)$. Put

$$\Sigma_X(\mathcal E):=\{\mathcal A\subseteq\mathcal P(X):\mathcal A\text{ is a sigma-algebra on }X\text{ and }\mathcal E\subseteq\mathcal A\}.$$

The **sigma-algebra generated by $\mathcal E$** is

$$\sigma_X(\mathcal E):=\bigcap_{\mathcal A\in\Sigma_X(\mathcal E)}\mathcal A.$$

When the ambient set is clear, write $\sigma(\mathcal E)$. The existence and
minimality implicit in this terminology are proved in
[[thm-generated-sigma-algebra-exists-and-is-minimal]].
