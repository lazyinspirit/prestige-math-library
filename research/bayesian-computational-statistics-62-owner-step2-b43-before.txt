### BCS-40 — Fixed-interval Gaussian smoothing and RTS recursion
**Pages:** kalman-smoothing-and-rts-recursion / kalman-smoothing-and-rts-recursion-examples. **Scope:** Define fixed-interval smoothed law and RTS recursion $C_k=P_{k|k}F_k^TP_{k+1|k}^{-1}$, $m_{k|T}=m_{k|k}+C_k(m_{k+1|T}-m_{k+1|k})$, $P_{k|T}=P_{k|k}+C_k(P_{k+1|T}-P_{k+1|k})C_k^T$. Require forecast covariance $P_{k+1|k}\succ0$ wherever its inverse is used; singular forecasts require a separate range theorem, not a pseudoinverse substitution. **Proof route:** Markov factorization plus BCS-03 Gaussian conditioning and backward induction. **Examples:** two-time scalar model; singular forecast outside theorem. **Requires:** BCS-38, BCS-39, BCS-03 `thm-multivariate-normal-conditional-law`; `thm-cholesky-factorisation-exists-iff-hermitian-positive-definite-and-is-unique`. **Sources:** Särkkä Ch. 8 §§8.1–8.3; Law–Stuart–Zygalakis Ch. 3 §3.1.
