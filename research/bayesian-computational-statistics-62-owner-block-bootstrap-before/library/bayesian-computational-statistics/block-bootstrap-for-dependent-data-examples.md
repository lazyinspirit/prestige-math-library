## Summary

The examples calculate a conditional moving-block mean for a four-observation sample, work out a triangular taper and its scale, check a standard $n^{1/3}$ block-length sequence, and show that pointwise resampling estimates the marginal AR(1) variance instead of its long-run variance. The AR(1) calculation is a variance counterexample only; it does not claim that variance inconsistency alone settles every possible bootstrap distribution metric.
