## Statement

Assume Countable Choice. Fix a globally weakly admissible complete pointed Heegaard diagram of any closed connected oriented smooth three-manifold and a fixed positive surface area form. There exist genuine compact-transition continuation data, permitting the actual $t$-independent ordered-lifted connection forcing of the positive-domain/energy lemma, for which all zero-basepoint solutions are regular. Their actual index-zero solutions, counted WITHOUT a translation quotient, form a finite matrix $\Phi:C^0\to C^1$. This is a chain map and a chain-homotopy equivalence. Within the supplied finite-parameter family its chain-homotopy class is independent of the source profile, adapted connection and regular perturbations relative the two fixed endpoint paths. Composition is represented by actual concatenated data up to an actual finite index-minus-one continuation homotopy. The identities and inverse homotopies are obtained from actual compact one-manifolds with their exhaustive gluing collars.

Genus zero gives the identity of the one-generator complex. The comparison covers the chosen canonical-boundary endpoint complexes on this fixed globally weak diagram, while genuinely changing the surface complex structure. It does not compare arbitrary preassigned full boundary structures, sector-only admissibility, area changes without the stated common energy interface, Heegaard attaching-circle moves, integral orientations, absolute gradings or plus/infinity/cobordism packages. No assertion about a symmetric power of a nonholomorphic surface map is made.
