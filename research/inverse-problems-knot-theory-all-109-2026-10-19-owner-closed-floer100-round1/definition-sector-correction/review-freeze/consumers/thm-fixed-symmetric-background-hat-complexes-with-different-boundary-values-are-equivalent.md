## Statement

Assume Countable Choice. Fix a globally weakly admissible complete pointed Heegaard diagram of a closed connected oriented smooth three-manifold, a surface complex structure and positive area form, and common exact product generator charts. Let $J^0_t,J^1_t$ be actual nearly symmetric tame time-collar paths for this SAME background, equal to the prescribed standard structures on common protected neighborhoods of the diagonal and one point divisor in each region and equal to i in the common generator charts. Suppose each path is regular at every zero-basepoint strip. Their full values on the attaching tori at $t=0,1$ may differ. Supply one compatible reference structure near the two tori with these generator charts, as furnished by the common-boundary construction without any endpoint-preservation requirement.

The actual finite index-one counts give complexes $C^0,C^1$. There are genuine homogeneous compact-transition paths $J(s,t)$ from $J^0$ to $J^1$, with positive physical-time collars, whose zero-basepoint solutions are all regular. Their actual index-zero solutions, counted without translation, give a finite chain map $C^0\to C^1$ and a chain-homotopy equivalence. Regular choices with these fixed ends give chain-homotopic maps; concatenation represents composition up to an actual finite index-minus-one homotopy. The maps and homotopies come from actual compact one-manifolds and exhaustive transverse-strip gluing collars.

This compares arbitrary full boundary values within the stated fixed-background/common-generator/protected setting, not merely paths already sharing a torus neighborhood. Source-dependent target normalizations are proof coordinates: their force vanishes ON the two tori and need not vanish on torus neighborhoods. No change of surface complex structure, generator-chart normalization, attaching-circle move, sector-only admissibility, absolute grading or plus/infinity package is asserted. Genus zero gives the one-generator identity.
