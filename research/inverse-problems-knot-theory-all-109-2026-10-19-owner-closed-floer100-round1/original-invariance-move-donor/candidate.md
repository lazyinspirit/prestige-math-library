## Statement

Assume ACω. For every closed connected oriented smooth three-manifold $Y$, the homology of the hat Heegaard Floer complex over $\mathbb F_2$ is independent, up to isomorphism, of the pointed admissible Heegaard diagram used to define it. The assertion is invariance of the group $\widehat{HF}(Y;\mathbb F_2)$; it does not assert a diagram-independent filtered chain homotopy type.

1.2 First prove the based complete-disk comparison needed on ONE handlebody. Prepare two systems as neat auxiliary witnesses and make their intersections transverse. A handlebody is irreducible using exactly [F6], without any irreducibility assumption about Y: carry its finite graph neighborhood to the standard handlebody model in $S^3$. An interior sphere bounds the two Schoenflies balls. Remove closed disk intersections as follows. Take a circle innermost on the first family over the entire second family. Its cap misses that second family. Replace the second disk's interior cap by a parallel of the first cap and round. Their sphere bounds the just proved interior ball. No other proper disk lies inside that ball, since its boundary is on the exterior handlebody boundary. 2.1 An outermost arc on a second-family disk now bounds a cap B missing the first family. Surgery of its first-family disk $D_i$ along B gives two disks pushed disjoint from that family. Cut the other first-family disks; the remaining manifold is a solid torus. Exactly one surgery disk is an essential meridian: their oriented relative classes add to the primitive old meridian, while an essential simple disk boundary is a primitive meridian and an inessential one bounds a ball by step 1.2. Two nonzero primitive classes could sum only to zero or twice a primitive class. Replacing $D_i$ by the essential disk $D_i'$ is therefore a complete system. [F6]'s planar construction slides $D_i$ across those faces until its outer rim is precisely $\partial D_i'$, followed by the empty-annulus isotopy.
