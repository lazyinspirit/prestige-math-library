## Statement

Assume Countable Choice. Fix complete singly pointed Heegaard data of any closed oriented three-manifold, a surface complex structure $j$, protected nearly symmetric geometry and exact product generator charts. Let $J_n(s,t)$ converge smoothly with all derivatives to $J_\infty(s,t)$, with uniformly bounded coefficients in one small fixed Cayley neighborhood of that background. Suppose there are autonomous paths $J_n^\pm(t)\to J_\infty^\pm(t)$ such that $J_n(s,t)-J_n^\pm(t)$ tends to zero, with every coefficient derivative, as $s\to\pm\infty$, UNIFORMLY in $n$. This includes families which are exactly autonomous outside one common compact transition interval, and also rapidly decaying source perturbations and their compact-transition truncations. On all protected sets, including every exact generator chart, these structures agree with the common background. At $t=0,1$ their full boundary structures agree with the common background, independently of $s,n$. All source derivatives are uniformly bounded. Let $u_n$ be actual smooth finite-energy solutions of $u_s+J_n(s,t,u)u_t=0$, with the physical product-torus boundaries, generator ends, $n_z(u_n)=0$ and $E(u_n)\le E_0<\infty$.

A subsequence converges to one ANCHORED central strip $u_0$ solving the $J_\infty(s,t)$ equation, possibly constant, together with finitely many ordered nonconstant $J^-_\infty$ strip levels preceding it and finitely many ordered nonconstant $J^+_\infty$ strip levels following it. No nonconstant sphere or boundary-disk bubbles occur. All adjoining generator ends match, the complete energy is the sum of those of these components, and all intervening necks and outer tails lose neither energy nor image diameter. The central map is not divided by translation. Each nonconstant end level is specified up to its ordinary translation. Central convergence and translated end-level convergence are smooth on compact sets, including the physical boundaries.

Eventually the continuous Whitney class of $u_n$ is the concatenation of the ordered end levels, the central class and the remaining end levels. Their actual fixed-end Fredholm indices add to $\operatorname{ind}P_{u_n}$. Here the source-dependent central operator is an actual Fredholm $H^1\to L^2$ operator, with the same kernel/cokernel in its integer higher realizations; its index is the same geometric Whitney index used for the autonomous levels. No regularity, sign of a central index, finite moduli count, gluing collar or chain-map assertion is assumed or concluded.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The uniform asymptotically autonomous tails, coefficient convergence, complete cut systems with the one zero mark in each cut component, uniform energy bound, physical boundaries and generator ends. The nearly symmetric structures have one fixed integrable surface background; interpolation inside their stated small Cayley neighborhood preserves the protected values and generator charts.

The result concerns bundle-symbol operators, not only autonomous holomorphic zeros. Integer higher domains impose only the original zeroth boundary trace. ## Proof

1.1 In genus zero the target is a point: every map is constant, its section spaces are zero, and the entire assertion follows with no end levels and index zero. Assume positive genus for the remaining proof. The actual central linearization has formula $P_u\xi=\nabla_s\xi+J(s,t,u)\nabla_t\xi+(\nabla_\xi J)(s,t,u)u_t$; differentiating the map variable introduces no derivative of the prescribed source arguments. Its real principal symbol is exactly the Cauchy--Riemann symbol. Uniform generator ends put each far tail inside its exact product chart, where the equation and its coordinate linearization are exactly $D_0=\partial_s+i\partial_t$, with real and imaginary boundary planes. The transverse end expansion in [F5] applies there. The bundle-symbol assertion of [F1] therefore gives the actual Fredholm operator and higher range agreement. To identify its index, first flatten the exponential tails to their generators by scalar cutoffs inside those product charts. The index is unchanged: the completed squared boundary phase loop is homotoped only inside the same fixed end chart, where the two added cap phases cancel. Interpolate the prescribed $J(s,t)$ to the fixed background using its small Cayley coefficients; all generator charts and boundary end values stay fixed. A joint complex frame on the compact nonconstant core is constructed by the finite projection ODE in [F1]. The completed boundary phase consequently varies by a genuine loop homotopy. Its winding, hence actual index, is constant. The flattened map now has exactly the smooth relative index in [F5], so the central index is the geometric Whitney index, including for nonautonomous solutions. This proves the claimed operator assertion without importing an autonomous-solution hypothesis. [F1, F5, given, construct, algebra]

1.2 Apply [F2]'s local energy estimates and Sobolev bootstrap on each compact rectangle. Countable diagonal extraction gives smooth convergence off finitely many energy concentrations on each such rectangle, and finite total energy bounds their total number by $E_0/h$, with the positive local bubble quantum $h$. At an interior concentration use the actual interior soft-rescaling induction; at a physical edge use its boundary version. Those are local disk/half-disk arguments. They do not impose an artificial totally real condition on the curved boundary of a chosen local half-disk or on a rectangle's vertical cuts. This gives the actual finite bubble trees, their full energy identities and connecting collapsed necks at every finite concentration. The remaining anchored map solves the limiting source-dependent equation smoothly after the actual puncture-removal step in those proofs. Its energy is finite. The small-window estimate needed here and below does not require an autonomous source. Indeed [F2]'s actual source-dependent epsilon estimates bound $|du|$ by $C\sqrt\delta$ on every buffered window of energy $\delta$. Every angular slice then has small length. Compactness of the two boundaries and their finite transverse intersection force its two endpoints into one of the finitely many separated generator neighborhoods; the entire short slice remains in that chart. Connectedness forces a whole connected small-window neck into one chart. There the equation is EXACTLY $D_0v=0$, since all prescribed source-dependent structures agree with $i$ on that chart. The actual Fourier proof in [F3] then gives the same energy and diameter decay, with uniform constants. For the finite-energy central map all sufficiently far windows have arbitrarily small energy, so each whole tail lies in one chart. Slice Poincare and the positive spectral gap give its generator limit and all-order exponential expansion by [F5]. This proves the central end assertion without an autonomous-source end theorem being applied outside its hypotheses. It may be constant, and that possibility is retained as the anchored central component. No translation of this compact source core has been made. [F2, F3, F5, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 Account for every component escaping to longitudinal infinity. Fix the bad-window threshold $\delta>0$ from [F3] and retain the source interval $[-10,10]$ as an anchor. The continuous function $A_n(s)=E(u_n;[s-2,s+2]\times[0,1])$ tends to zero on both infinite tails, so $B_n=\{s:A_n(s)\ge\delta\}$ is compact. If it is nonempty, select its least point, then the least remaining point at least four to its right, and continue. The corresponding windows have disjoint interiors, so there are at most $\lfloor E_0/\delta\rfloor$ selections. Every point of $B_n$ lies between a selected point and four units to its right, since otherwise it would be an available next selection. Include the anchor at zero in the finite cluster list, whether or not it is bad. After subsequences their bounded-offset clusters either remain at a bounded distance from the anchor or escape to one of the two infinities. Bounded clusters are already part of step 1.2. Each escaping cluster, translated to its center, has on every fixed source rectangle coefficients converging with every derivative to $J^-_\infty$ or $J^+_\infty$, by the uniform tail hypothesis. Thus [F3]'s same local extraction, rescaling and end argument gives a finite-energy strip level there with its possible local bubble trees; before bubble exclusion that strip may be constant if a bubble carries the cluster energy. Different clusters have distances tending to infinity. Between clusters, beyond the outermost clusters and between the anchor and its nearest clusters every fixed-size window is below the common threshold. The actual source-dependent small-window argument proved in step 1.2 puts every buffered long gap inside one exact generator chart and makes its central energy and diameter tend to zero. The finite energy identity inside the retained cores then captures ALL energy; there is no residual escaping component or neck energy. The matching generator limits agree because each connecting image diameter tends to zero. The escaping limit levels themselves are autonomous by coefficient convergence. The finite-window extraction and source-dependent neck argument do not require exact autonomy of each approximating map, and keep the central core anchored. [F2, F3, step 1.2, construct, algebra]

3.1 Now eliminate all the bubble trees, rather than assuming their absence in the extraction. Use [F4]'s connection for the fixed divisor $E_z$. It is independent of the source and is flat near the tori and generator charts. Integrals over the smooth component cores converge. The absolute integral over any omitted neck or energy tail is bounded by its energy times one common curvature bound, and tends to zero by steps 1.2 and 2.1. The frame-Stokes formula in [F4] therefore expresses the integer $n_z(u_n)=0$ as the sum of the degrees of the central strip, every end level and every actual compact sphere or disk bubble. This argument uses a fixed TARGET line bundle and no autonomous-source or squared-determinant index connection. Every term is nonnegative by protected-divisor positivity for strips and by the compact bubble positivity in [F4]. Hence each compact bubble has zero mark degree. For a sphere its domain is $d\Sigma$; for a one-torus disk its domain is constant on each cut component. The chosen $z$ meets the sole cut component of either complete system. [F4] then makes every such zero-mark bubble constant. The local stability rules remove all remaining ghost-only trees: a finite tree of constant bubbles with no active component has a terminal component with too few distinguished points, contrary to the explicitly stabilized extraction. Thus there are no bubble trees. Each escaping cluster retains at least $\delta$ energy on a sufficiently large fixed translated core by its bad center and local energy identity; with bubbles excluded its strip level is therefore nonconstant. Only the anchored central component may remain constant. Steps 1.2 and 2.1 now give exactly the smooth central and end components, energy identity and matched necks in the Statement. [F4, step 1.2, step 2.1, construct, algebra]

4.1 Fix sufficiently far generator cuts on each of the finitely many limiting components. They and all the connecting necks lie in the same exact product charts for large $n$, by step 3.1. Multiply the coordinate maps on successive necks by real scalar cutoffs, retaining their two physical boundary planes and contracting their middle portions to the common generator. This is an actual continuous relative homotopy, with the outer ends fixed. On every retained finite core the sequence is uniformly close to its limit. The boundary-preserving local-addition straight homotopy identifies these cores with the limiting ones; the remaining finite cutoff tails are homotoped inside their product charts. This is precisely the topological construction in [F5]'s actual class-splitting proof, which uses only core convergence, physical boundary preservation and collapsed generator necks, not autonomy of the central equation. Consequently $[u_n]$ is eventually the ordered concatenation of the end classes with the anchored central class. Apply the additive continuous Whitney index from [F5] and the actual source-dependent operator identification in step 1.1. This gives the asserted equality of actual indices. A constant central component has the constant transverse $D_0$ operator and index zero, so retaining it contributes no false translation parameter or index shift. [F1, F5, step 1.1, step 3.1, construct, algebra] ∎
