## Statement

Assume ACω. For every closed connected oriented smooth three-manifold $Y$, the homology of the hat Heegaard Floer complex over $\mathbb F_2$ is independent, up to isomorphism, of the pointed admissible Heegaard diagram used to define it. The assertion is invariance of the group $\widehat{HF}(Y;\mathbb F_2)$; it does not assert a diagram-independent filtered chain homotopy type.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** ACω, a closed connected oriented three-manifold $Y$, and two pointed admissible Heegaard diagrams for $Y$.

[F1] A diagram supplies a candidate complex generated by transverse intersection points with a disk-count differential ([[def-hat-heegaard-floer-complex-of-a-closed-three-manifold]]).

[F2] The disk and polygon identities needed for maps and homotopies are the conclusion of the unresolved analytic foundations module ([[lem-os-disk-and-polygon-analytic-foundations]]).

[F3] A full invariance proof must also supply diagram-move generation and continuation, isotopy, handleslide, and stabilization chain maps with inverse chain homotopies; those maps are not defined by [F1] alone ([[def-hat-heegaard-floer-complex-of-a-closed-three-manifold]]).

## Proof

1.1 The candidate groups for two diagrams are the homologies of their respective disk-count complexes by [F1]. To identify them it is necessary to associate chain maps to a sequence of pointed Heegaard moves and prove those maps are chain homotopy equivalences using disk and polygon counts. [F1, given]

1.2 The move maps and their homotopies require [F2] and the move-generation and inverse-homotopy obligations in [F3]. The repository has no complete closed-manifold proof of those obligations; the knot-specialized pointed invariance item does not establish this statement for arbitrary closed $Y$. Hence the asserted invariance remains escalated rather than proved here. [F2, F3, step 1.1]
