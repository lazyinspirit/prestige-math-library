## Definition

Let $Y$ be a closed connected oriented smooth three-manifold. A pointed genus-$g$ Heegaard diagram for $Y$ consists of a closed genus-$g$ surface $\Sigma$, two $g$-tuples of pairwise disjoint attaching circles $\boldsymbol\alpha$ and $\boldsymbol\beta$ that specify the two handlebodies of a Heegaard splitting of $Y$, and a point $z\in\Sigma\setminus(\boldsymbol\alpha\cup\boldsymbol\beta)$. The symmetric product is $\operatorname{Sym}^g(\Sigma)=\Sigma^g/S_g$; the product tori are $T_\alpha=(\alpha_1\times\cdots\times\alpha_g)/S_g$ and $T_\beta=(\beta_1\times\cdots\times\beta_g)/S_g$ in this quotient. For a diagram with transverse finite intersection $T_\alpha\cap T_\beta$, its hat Floer chain module over $\mathbb F_2$ is freely generated by those intersection points. A Whitney disk from $x$ to $y$ is a map from a disk with two boundary punctures into $\operatorname{Sym}^g(\Sigma)$ whose two boundary arcs lie on $T_\alpha$ and $T_\beta$ and whose punctures limit to $x$ and $y$. Its Maslov index is the Fredholm index of the linearized disk equation. The candidate differential sends $x$ to the mod-two sum of $y$ over index-one holomorphic Whitney disks with multiplicity zero at $z$. The homology of this chain complex, when the analytic and move-invariance results hold, is denoted $\widehat{HF}(Y;\mathbb F_2)$.
