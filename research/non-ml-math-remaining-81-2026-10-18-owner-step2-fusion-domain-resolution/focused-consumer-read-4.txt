FILE items/def-qc-tqc-universality-notions.md
Definition

Let $\Gamma\le U(V)$ be a subgroup acting on a nonzero finite-dimensional
Hilbert space $V$. It is **exactly universal** on $V$ when $\Gamma=U(V)$,
**projectively exactly universal** when its image in $PU(V)$ is all of
$PU(V)$, and **projectively dense** when that image is dense in the metric
$d_P$ of [[def-qc-tqc-projective-gate-and-metric]].

For an encoding $J:\mathcal C\to V$ with code projector $P=JJ^*$, the action
is **approximate logical-and-leakage universal** if for every
$U\in U(\mathcal C)$ and every $\epsilon>0$ there is $W\in\Gamma$ and a
phase $z\in U(1)$ such that
$$\|PWP-zJUJ^*\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le\epsilon,\qquad \|(I-P)WP\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le\epsilon.$$
This last notion includes a leakage bound as a separate requirement; projective
density on one fusion block alone does not supply it.


FILE items/ex-fibonacci-golden-chain-projectors-and-noninvertibility.md
Example

Use the coherent Fibonacci category and pullback-R convention of [[ex-qc-tqc-fibonacci-data]]. Write $\phi=(1+\sqrt5)/2$, $u=\phi^{-1}$, and $v=\phi^{-1/2}$. The simple fusion rule is $\tau\otimes\tau=\mathbf1\oplus\tau$. For three tensor inputs $\tau,\tau,\tau$, comprising the incoming boundary $b=\tau$ and two sites ($n=2$), with output $c=\tau$, the path space $H_{\tau,2;\tau,\tau}$ has ordered basis $(\mathbf1,\tau)$ according to the intermediate charge. The supplied associator block is

$$F^{\tau\tau\tau}_{\tau}=\begin{pmatrix}u&v\\v&-u\end{pmatrix}.$$

On these three tensor inputs the two adjacent vacuum-channel projectors are $P_1=\operatorname{diag}(1,0)$ and $P_2=F^{-1}P_1F$; the first pair includes the incoming boundary charge. For the literal three-site problem $n=3$, still with $b=c=\tau$, the four tensor inputs give a three-dimensional space with path basis $(x_1,x_2)=(\mathbf1,\tau),(\tau,\mathbf1),(\tau,\tau)$. The two adjacent pairs among the three sites have projectors

$$Q_1=\begin{pmatrix}u^2&0&uv\\0&0&0\\uv&0&v^2\end{pmatrix},\qquad Q_2=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&0\\0&u^2&uv\\0&uv&v^2\end{pmatrix}.$$

Both are rank-one orthogonal projections and $Q_1Q_2Q_1=\phi^{-2}Q_1$; hence $\phi Q_i$ obey the adjacent Temperley–Lieb relation. Thus the two-site $2\times2$ block and the three-site $3\times3$ problem are both retained with their correct domains. The object $\tau$ is not tensor-invertible even though its fusion-ring class is a unit. The identity on this two-dimensional path space is an invertible operator; the local rank-one channel projection is not.



[F1] [[ex-qc-tqc-fibonacci-data]]: the two simple objects are $\mathbf1,\tau$, $\tau\otimes\tau=\mathbf1\oplus\tau$, and the stated F block is unitary with the supplied pullback R phases.

[F2] [[def-anyonic-fusion-path-space]] and [[lem-anyonic-fusion-path-basis]]: $H_{\tau,2;\tau,\tau}=\operatorname{Hom}(\tau,(\tau\otimes\tau)\otimes\tau)$ has a basis indexed by the two intermediate labels $\mathbf1,\tau$.

[F3] [[def-anyonic-local-channel-projector]]: the second adjacent channel projector is $P_2=F^{-1}P_1F$ in the fixed tree basis.

FILE items/ex-ising-periodic-path-space-and-two-sector-map.md
Example

Use the actual coherent positive-FS Ising category constructed in [[lem-ising-unitary-normal-data-and-ribbon-coherence]] at $\kappa=1$, $s=e^{\pi i/8}$ and $\beta=s$. Its simple objects are $\mathbf1,\eta,D$, with $\eta^2=\mathbf1$, $\eta D=D\eta=D$ and $D^2=\mathbf1\oplus\eta$. In the inverse-pullback convention its complete data include
$$F_D^{DDD}=2^{-1/2}\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix},\quad R_{DD}^0=s^{-1},\quad R_{DD}^\eta=i s^{-1},\quad R_{\eta D}^D=R_{D\eta}^D=-i,\quad R_{\eta\eta}^0=-1.$$
The remaining unit entries are one. Its twist is $\theta_D=s^{-1}$, positive dimension $\sqrt2$ and FS sign $+1$. This follows from the ell-four root category by the explicit associator/braiding parity conversion, rather than a vertex gauge; the original root category has negative FS and twist $s^3$.

For reference charge $D$, the full periodic path space is zero at odd length. At length $L=2N$, $N\ge1$, it is $H\oplus\bar H$, each sector of dimension $2^N$, with no total-flux or spin-parity restriction. Encode the group labels by bits, in even positions of $H$ and odd positions of $\bar H$. Under these orthonormal basis maps,
$$P_{2j-1}|_H=(I+X_j)/2,\quad P_{2j}|_H=(I+Z_jZ_{j+1})/2,$$
$$P_{2j-1}|_{\bar H}=(I+Z_jZ_{j+1})/2,\quad P_{2j}|_{\bar H}=(I+X_{j+1})/2.$$
Indices are cyclic modulo $N$. Put $C|b_1\cdots b_N\rangle=|b_2\cdots b_Nb_1\rangle$, $Q=\prod_jX_j$, and $K_{c,b}=2^{-N/2}(-1)^{\sum_jc_j(b_{j-1}+b_j)}$. The actual one-step shift and cyclic F-contracted line maps are
$$T=\begin{pmatrix}0&C\\I&0\end{pmatrix},\quad Y_\eta=\operatorname{diag}(Q,Q),\quad Y_D=\begin{pmatrix}0&K^\dagger\\K&0\end{pmatrix}.$$
They intertwine all displayed projectors, including wraparound; each line commutes with $T$ and the projectors, and $Y_D^2=I+Y_\eta$. The corresponding single-chain map instead satisfies $(K/\sqrt2)^2=(I+Q)C^{-1}/2$. At $N=1$ the bond projector is $I$ and the field projector is $(I+X_1)/2$; no three-distinct-site cyclic TL relation is asserted for length two.



[F4] [[def-anyonic-fusion-path-space]] specifies the typed path coordinates, and [[def-anyonic-local-channel-projector]] specifies F-conjugated channel projections.

FILE items/ex-multiplicity-indexed-anyonic-fusion-path.md
Example

Assume a finite semisimple fusion category $\mathcal C$ has a representative set $\operatorname{Irr}(\mathcal C)$ containing labels $x,a,y$ with $N_{x a}^y=2$. Fix representative labels and basis indices at every other vertex of a path, and suppose the remaining vertex is $x,a\to y$. Choose a basis $i_1,i_2$ of the split space $\operatorname{Hom}(y,x\otimes a)$. The two complete path labels differ only by this vertex index.

FILE items/ex-qa-37-approximate-b6-target.md
Example

Take the logical two-qubit target $U=I_4$. Its determinant is one, so the
special-unitary extensions are $T_1=I_5$ and $T_\tau=I_8$. The empty
six-strand braid has normalized block matrices $I_5,I_8$ and exponent sum
zero. It therefore certifies exact zero block error, zero logical error and
zero leakage; its physical common phase is $R_1^0=1$. This is the identity
boundary case of the shared-word B6 interface.



FILE items/ex-qa-37-fibonacci-budget.md
Example

Take the fixed SK recurrence with $\eta_0=2^{-13}$ and requested tolerance
$\varepsilon=2^{-14}$. This is a symbolic certificate example: the finite
base-net list and its maximum length $L_0$ are supplied compiler data, not
computed here. The exact recurrence gives $J=2$ and output length at most
$25L_0$. For a finite QSD factor list of size $M>0$, allocate each factor
$\delta=\min\{\varepsilon/(2M),\eta_0/2\}$; then the sum of the factor
errors is at most $\varepsilon/2$. The two-qubit B6 search, measurement
outcomes, preparation and ancilla use, Hamiltonian evolution, and phase
correction are separate ledger coordinates. The supplied base-net certificate
is fixed input data counted in $L_{\rm in}$; this example does not compute it.
No B6 search-length bound follows from the SK calculation.



FILE items/ex-qa-37-fusion-measurement-branches.md
Example

In the two-dimensional Fibonacci fusion space for three $\tau$ anyons of
total charge $\tau$, use the basis $|1\rangle,|\tau\rangle$ labelled by the
charge after fusing the first two anyons. This is the fixed convention of
[[ex-qc-tqc-fibonacci-data]]. For
$$|\psi\rangle=\sqrt{3/5}\,|1\rangle+\sqrt{2/5}\,|\tau\rangle,$$
measurement of that pair's total charge yields probabilities $3/5$ and
$2/5$ and conditional states $|1\rangle$ and $|\tau\rangle$. If either
coefficient is zero, its outcome has probability zero and there is no
normalized conditional state for that outcome.

The circuit below is schematic: the pair enters the declared fusion-channel
projective measurement, the classical outcome is the charge label, and the
post-measurement quantum branch is $P_a|\psi\rangle$. It records no physical
measurement hardware or any unproved braid diagram rule.

```tikz
\begin{tikzpicture}[x=1cm,y=1cm]
  \draw (0,1.2) -- (1.5,1.2);
  \draw (0,0.4) -- (1.5,0.4);
  \node at (0.25,1.45) {$\tau$};
  \node at (0.25,0.65) {$\tau$};
  \draw[->] (1.5,0.8) -- (2.1,0.8);
  \node[draw,minimum width=1.5cm,minimum height=0.9cm] at (3,0.8) {$\{P_1,P_\tau\}$};
  \draw[->] (3.75,0.8) -- (4.4,0.8);
  \node[draw,minimum width=1.0cm,minimum height=0.7cm] at (5,0.8) {classical $a$};
  \draw[->] (3.0,0.35) -- (3.0,-0.45);
  \node at (3.45,-0.35) {$P_a|\psi\rangle$};
\end{tikzpicture}
```

FILE items/ex-qc-tqc-b6-rank-and-leakage.md
Example

The exact B6 certificates have determinants $17$ and $22$ modulo $31$ for
the selected $24\times24$ and $63\times63$ Lie-word minors. In the
total-charge-$1$ six-anyon space there are two natural four-dimensional code
choices: a pair-charge code and the triple code of
[[def-qc-tqc-six-anyon-encoded-subspace]]. The density argument applies to
either chosen subspace; the two definitions use different fusion trees, and
no equality of their projectors is asserted. For a circuit with $G>0$ ideal
one-qubit or adjacent two-qubit gates, assigning $\delta_j=\epsilon/G$
gives total logical block error and leakage at most $\epsilon$.
