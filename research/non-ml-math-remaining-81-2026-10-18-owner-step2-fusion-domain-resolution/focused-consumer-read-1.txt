FILE items/alg-qa-37-exhaustive-b6-block-pair-compiler.md
Definition

**Inputs:** certified Cauchy names for targets $T_5\in SU(5)$ and
$T_8\in SU(8)$; a positive binary-rational tolerance $\varepsilon$; and the
fixed normalized six-strand Fibonacci B6 generators in both total-charge
blocks. Enumerate all finite Artin words over the six-strand generators and
their inverses, and at stage $s=1,2,\ldots$ inspect every word of length at
most $s$ and every integer precision $1\le p\le s$ not previously inspected,
in fixed lexicographic order. For each word $b$ and precision $p$, compute rational complex
matrix approximants $P_{5,b,p},P_{8,b,p}$ to its exact normalized block
matrices and request rational target approximants $Q_{5,p},Q_{8,p}$. Each
real and imaginary entry error in these approximants is certified at most
$2^{-p}$. For $d\in\{5,8\}$ compute the rational number
$$s_{d,b,p}=\sum_{i,j}\Bigl(|\Re(P_{d,b,p}-Q_{d,p})_{ij}| +|\Im(P_{d,b,p}-Q_{d,p})_{ij}|\Bigr).$$
Return the first word for which both rational tests
$$s_{d,b,p}+4d\,2^{-p}<\varepsilon\qquad(d=5,8)$$
hold. Output the word, its exponent sum, both rational certificates, and the
measured search/precision costs in the ledger of
[[def-qa-37-resource-cost-model]]. The braid matrices have computable
algebraic entries from the fixed Fibonacci $F/R$ data
[[lem-qc-tqc-fibonacci-b6-path-projectors-and-artin]]. This is a dovetailed
partial search definition: no cutoff or useful word-length/time bound is
asserted here. A separate theorem in this pair proves termination under certified target
access. The returned matrices are
normalized by the common $R_1$ scalar; their physical phase is recorded by
[[def-qa-37-phase-reference-cost]].

For this compiler subroutine's ledger record, $N_{\rm any}=6$,
$\epsilon_{\rm op}=\varepsilon$, $N_{\rm meas}=B_{\rm out}=0$, and
$N_{\rm phase}=t_{\rm phase}=0$ because it performs no measurement or phase
correction. Preparation, ancilla, Hamiltonian-time, transport, measurement-
implementation and physical-leakage coordinates are “not applicable” because
this mathematical search supplies no physical realization. $L_{\rm in}$,
$|b|$, $b_{\max}$, and $t_{\rm cl}$ record the encoded target/compiler inputs,
returned word, greatest precision request, and measured search/postprocessing
bit time.



FILE items/alg-qa-37-su2-solovay-kitaev-compiler.md
Definition

**Inputs:** a positive binary-rational tolerance $0<\varepsilon\le\eta_0$;
the fixed inverse-closed Fibonacci alphabet $G_{\rm Fib}$; and a fixed
certified base-net certificate $(\mathcal N_0,\eta_0,L_0)$ with
$0<\eta_0<64^{-2}$. Select exactly one target interface:

1. **Ideal exact-real interface:** supply an exact-real handle for
   $S\in SU(2)$ with exact fixed-dimensional matrix operations and exact
   comparisons. Invoke the ideal recursive procedure
   [[alg-qc-solovay-kitaev-unitary-compiler]] on $S$ and $\varepsilon$.
2. **Finite Turing interface:** supply a polynomial-time Cauchy name for
   $S\in SU(2)$ and the minimal-polynomial and rational isolating-interval
   descriptions of every real and imaginary gate coordinate required by the
   finite-precision theorem. Run the deterministic Turing implementation
   established by [[thm-qc-solovay-kitaev-bit-complexity]] on that name,
   alphabet, certificate, and tolerance. This branch uses its certified
   interval decisions and does not invoke exact-real comparisons.

In either branch, return the resulting word in the labels
$zB_1,zB_2,(zB_1)^{-1},(zB_2)^{-1}$, its expanded length, and its Artin
exponent sum $k$. The base-net list is supplied certificate data; this
algorithm does not compute it from density. A Cauchy name alone is not an
exact-real handle, and the ideal branch makes no finite bit-time claim.

**Output:** a braid word $b$ and ledger scalar $(R_1z^{-1})^k$ such that,
when the algorithm's hypotheses hold, the normalized SU(2) matrix of $b$ is
within the requested tolerance of $S$. Its physical matrix carries the
recorded scalar from [[def-qa-37-phase-reference-cost]].


FILE items/cex-qa-37-projective-is-not-controlled.md
Statement

On one target qubit, $I_2$ and $-I_2$ differ only by a global phase and
induce the same standalone ray and outcome probabilities. Controlled access
changes the comparison. With control first,
$$C(I_2)=I_4,\qquad C(-I_2)=\begin{pmatrix}I_2&0\\0&-I_2\end{pmatrix} =Z\otimes I_2.$$
On input $|+\rangle|0\rangle$, these give $|+\rangle|0\rangle$ and
$|-\rangle|0\rangle$. Measuring the control in the $X$ basis is
respectively deterministic for $+$ and $-$, so the total-variation distance
is one. Thus projective equivalence of an isolated braid gate is not
interchangeable with equality under coherent control.





FILE items/cex-qc-tqc-controlled-global-phase.md
Statement refuted

A scalar phase that is irrelevant for an uncontrolled fixed-sector braid
remains irrelevant when that braid is coherently controlled.



FILE items/def-anyonic-frobenius-schur-sign.md
Definition

Let $\mathcal C$ be a unitary spherical fusion category over $\mathbb C$, with pivotal structure and spherical dimension as in [[def-pivotal-structure]], [[def-spherical-structure]], and [[def-the-dimension-of-an-object-relative-to-a-pivotal-structure]]. Let $a$ be a self-dual simple object with $N_{aa}^{\mathbf1}=1$. Choose a unit vector $v:\mathbf1\to a\otimes a$ spanning the one-dimensional vacuum split space and let $d_a>0$ be its positive spherical dimension.

A **normalized vacuum-channel self-duality datum** is a supplied pair of maps $\operatorname{coev}:\mathbf1\to a\otimes a$ and $\operatorname{ev}:a\otimes a\to\mathbf1$ of the form

$$\operatorname{coev}=\sqrt{d_a}\,v,\qquad \operatorname{ev}=\kappa_a\sqrt{d_a}\,v^\dagger,$$

where $\kappa_a\in\{+1,-1\}$ and the two duality zig-zag identities hold after the self-duality identification, using the left-dual convention of [[def-left-dual-and-right-dual-object]]. We call $\kappa_a$ the **Frobenius–Schur sign in this normalized cup/cap convention**. It is not a braiding or $R$-symbol phase.

This item records supplied normalized duality data; it does not assert that every self-dual simple object admits the displayed gauge or prove a trace, ribbon, or category-equivalence theorem. An example must supply the datum or compute the sign from coherent normalized F-data; fusion rules alone do not specify it.


FILE items/def-anyonic-fusion-path-space.md
Definition

Let $\mathcal C$ be a fusion category over $\mathbb C$, so it is finite semisimple, rigid, monoidal, and has simple unit [[def-fusion-and-multifusion-category]]. Let $a,b,c$ be simple objects and let $n\ge0$. Set $T_0=b$ and recursively fix the left-associated parenthesization $T_{j+1}:=T_j\otimes a$. The **fixed-boundary anyonic path space** is

$$H_{a,n;b,c}:=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal C}(c,T_n).$$

For simple labels $x,y$, define the split multiplicity space $W_{x,a}^y:=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal C}(y,x\otimes a)$. A fusion path is a sequence of simple labels $x_0=b,x_1,\ldots,x_n=c$ together with, for every $1\le j\le n$, one basis index $\mu_j$ in a chosen basis $B_{x_{j-1},a}^{x_j}$ of $W_{x_{j-1},a}^{x_j}$. The basis index is retained even when distinct indices have the same adjacent labels. Fusion rules are the nonnegative integers $N_{x a}^{y}$ in $[x][a]=\sum_yN_{x a}^{y}[y]$ [[def-fusion-rules]].

If $i_j\in B_{x_{j-1},a}^{x_j}$ is the selected inclusion $x_j\to x_{j-1}\otimes a$, define the path composite by $\gamma_0:=\operatorname{id}_b$ and $\gamma_j:=(\gamma_{j-1}\otimes\operatorname{id}_a)\circ i_j:x_j\to T_j$. For $x_n=c$, $\gamma_n$ is a vector in $H_{a,n;b,c}$. The next lemma proves these composites form a basis and computes the dimension. No Hilbert-space or physical Hamiltonian structure is part of this definition.



FILE items/def-anyonic-local-channel-projector.md
Definition

Fix a fusion-path space with repeated simple site charge $a$, a position $i$ with two adjacent $a$-labels, and a simple output $s$ of $a\otimes a$. In the tree basis where this adjacent pair is fused first, let $\Pi_s$ be the projection onto the direct summand with pair output $s$, acting as the identity on every multiplicity index inside that channel and as zero on all other pair outputs. Let $F_i$ be the typed sequence of associator changes carrying the fixed path tree to this pair-first tree; each local matrix is the F-symbol of [[def-qc-tqc-f-symbol]], with rows and columns as in that Definition. Define the **local channel projector** in the fixed path basis by

$$P_{i,s}:=F_i^{-1}\Pi_sF_i.$$

This is an idempotent for any fusion category because $F_i$ is invertible. In a unitary fusion category with orthonormal tree bases, $F_i$ is unitary and $P_{i,s}$ is an orthogonal projection. The channel projection includes every multiplicity coordinate; the definition has no multiplicity-free restriction.

The coordinate convention is illustrated below: vectors move from the fixed tree to the pair-first tree by $F_i$, the channel projection acts there, and $F_i^{-1}$ returns to the fixed tree. The curved arrow denotes the defined composite, not an additional morphism.

```tikzcd
\mathcal H_{\mathrm{fixed}} \arrow[r,"F_i"] \arrow[rrr,bend right=35,"P_{i,s}"'] & \mathcal H_{\mathrm{pair}} \arrow[r,"\Pi_s"] & \mathcal H_{\mathrm{pair}} \arrow[r,"F_i^{-1}"] & \mathcal H_{\mathrm{fixed}}
```


FILE items/def-qa-37-anyon-task-and-promise.md
Definition

Work in a fixed unitary braided fusion category and fix an ordered finite list
of $n\ge1$ simple charge labels, a parenthesized fusion tree, and a total charge for
which the fusion space is nonzero. Simple charges, multiplicity bases, and
admissible fusion-tree vectors have the conventions of
[[def-qc-tqc-fusion-tree-basis]]. An encoded input subspace is specified by a
fixed-charge isometry as in [[def-qc-tqc-fixed-charge-encoding]]. Each task
instance supplies either an initial unit vector in that subspace as an abstract
quantum input, or a finite state-preparation procedure whose specified output
is that vector and whose preparation operations and ancillas are recorded.
The instance also gives a nonnegative rational error threshold and specifies
the correctness relation and metric to which it applies.

A **finite anyon decision task** is a promise pair $(Y,N)$ of disjoint sets of
binary strings with domain $D=Y\cup N$, together with a finite encoding of
admissible fusion-tree input labels; a solver must answer yes on $Y$ and no on
$N$, with no obligation outside $D$. A **finite anyon search task** is a domain
$D$ and a finite-string witness relation, with a valid witness required only
for encoded inputs in $D$. The input record states the number and order of
strands, the finite alphabet of positive and inverse Artin generators
available to the solver, any prescribed finite braid word, and the finite set
of adjacent fusion-measurement locations.
Braid action is the mathematical unitary action specified by the fixed
physical-interface record [[def-qc-tqc-physical-braid-interface]]; a
measurement location is interpreted in the declared fusion-tree basis. The
state-preparation field is an abstract task input and does not assert that a
physical device prepares the state or realizes the braid. Input length and
correctness-on-promises follow [[def-qa-task-and-promise-domain]].
