## Statement

For a unitary braided fusion category, a simple charge $x$, and a fixed total
charge $c$, the operators in
[[def-qc-tqc-braid-action-from-fusion-data]] define a unitary group
homomorphism
$$\rho_c:B_n\longrightarrow U(V(x^n\to c)).$$
In particular, the adjacent Artin relations and distant commutation relations
hold in each fixed-charge block.

**Given:** A unitary fusion category with a specified unitary braiding, the
charge $x$, the nonzero fixed-charge fusion space $V(x^n\to c)$, and the
generator matrices $T_i^{-1}R_iT_i$.

[F2] The current QC25 bodies define finite semisimple orthogonal fusion-tree
spaces, unitary associators and braidings, and unitary F/R coordinates; their
pentagon and hexagon coordinate identities match the categorical coherence
maps. The fusion-tree dimension lemma supplies complete bases.
[[def-qc-tqc-unitary-fusion-category]]
[[def-qc-tqc-fusion-tree-basis]]
[[lem-qc-tqc-fusion-tree-dimension]] [[def-braiding]]
[[thm-qc-tqc-f-symbol-unitary]] [[thm-qc-tqc-r-symbol-unitary]]
[[thm-qc-tqc-pentagon-coordinates]] [[thm-qc-tqc-hexagon-coordinates]]

2.1 By [F1], the local categorical braidings on the full tensor power satisfy the Artin relations and define a homomorphism for the fixed parenthesization. Finite semisimplicity and orthogonality in [F2] decompose that tensor power into total-charge blocks; naturality of the braiding makes each block invariant, so restriction to $V(x^n\to c)$ preserves the same relations. In the fusion-tree basis, these restricted operators are exactly $T_i^{-1}R_iT_i$ by the definition in [[def-qc-tqc-braid-action-from-fusion-data]]. The pentagon and hexagon coordinate identities in [F2] identify those matrices with the same parenthesized composites; distant generators commute because they act on disjoint tensor factors. Hence the restricted assignment is a unitary representation of the Artin presentation. [F1, F2, given, algebra] ∎

## Statement

Assume $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$, $n\ge2$, and a total charge $c$ with
$V(\tau^n\to c)\ne0$. The code in $3n$ $\tau$ anyons factors as
$\mathcal C_n\cong(\mathbb C^2)^{\otimes n}\otimes V(\tau^n\to c)$.
For any adjacent pair of encoded triples, any $U\in U(4)$, and any
$\delta>0$, one normalized six-strand braid induces an ambient operator
within $\delta$ of the code-preserving ideal extension of $U$ on the entire
fixed-charge space $V(\tau^{3n}\to c)$. Consequently its logical block error
and leakage amplitude are each at most $\delta$, uniformly over all
spectator fusion paths and states that already have a component outside the
code.

[F3] A local braid on a tensor factor acts as its braid operator tensored with
the identity on spectator factors; unitary fusion-tree reassociation changes
basis by a unitary. The physical braid phase is common to all charge and
spectator blocks; write $\widetilde\rho$ for the normalized action obtained by
removing it, which preserves the same local tensor-factor action.
[[thm-qc-tqc-gauge-covariance]]
[[lem-qc-tqc-determinant-character-phase]]

1.1 Each triple has fusion factor $V(\tau^3\to\tau)\cong\mathbb C^2$. Associating the $n$ triple charges first gives the code factorization $(\mathbb C^2)^{\otimes n}\otimes V(\tau^n\to c)$; the second factor records all spectator fusion paths. This is an orthogonal fusion-tree decomposition by [F1, F3]. [given, F1, F3, construct]

1.2 Let the chosen adjacent pair of triples occupy strands $3j-2,\ldots,3j+3$, and put $p=3(j-1)$ and $q=3(n-j-1)$. Reassociate the ordered input only, retaining the prefix of $p$ strands, the selected six-strand block, and the suffix of $q$ strands in that order; no permutation of strands is used. The fusion-tree basis gives the orthogonal decomposition $$V(\tau^{3n}\to c)\cong\bigoplus_{\alpha,a,e,d}V(\tau^p\to\alpha)\otimes V(\tau^6\to a)\otimes V_{\alpha a}^{e}\otimes V(\tau^q\to d)\otimes V_{ed}^{c},$$ where the sums run over simple charges and zero-dimensional summands are omitted (with $\tau^0=\mathbf1$). Every factor other than $V(\tau^6\to a)$ records a prefix, suffix, or fusion-multiplicity spectator. By [F3], a normalized braid word $\beta$ supported on the selected six consecutive strands acts on each summand as the identity on all other factors and $\widetilde\rho_a(\beta)$ on $V(\tau^6\to a)$. [given, F3, algebra]

2.1 For each local total charge $a$, let $T_a$ be the target extension in [F1]. On the decomposition in step 1.2 define $\mathcal W$ on each summand by the identity on every factor other than $V(\tau^6\to a)$ and by $T_a$ on that block. This is a unitary direct sum; it preserves the triple code and acts there as $U$ on the selected adjacent pair and identity on all other logical and spectator factors. Joint density [F1] gives one normalized six-strand braid word $\beta$ with $\|\widetilde\rho_a(\beta)-T_a\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le\delta$ for both $a=1,\tau$. [A1, F1, step 1.1, step 1.2, choose]
