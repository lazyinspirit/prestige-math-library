FILE items/lem-qc-tqc-fibonacci-b6-path-projectors-and-artin.md
Statement

Use the Fibonacci fusion rule $\tau\otimes\tau=1\oplus\tau$ and the
fixed-chirality data
$F^{\tau\tau\tau}_\tau=\begin{pmatrix}\phi^{-1}&\phi^{-1/2}\\
\phi^{-1/2}&-\phi^{-1}\end{pmatrix}$ and
$r=R_\tau/R_1=e^{-3\pi i/5}$. The fixed-total-charge B6 path bases are

$$\mathcal P_1=\{0101010,0101110,0110110,0111010,0111110\},$$

$$\mathcal P_\tau=\{0101011,0101101,0101111,0110101,0110111,0111011,0111101,0111111\},$$

where each path is $(x_0,\ldots,x_6)$, $x_0=0$ is the unit label,
$x_1=1$ is $\tau$, and adjacent labels obey the fusion rule. For
$d_0=1,d_1=\phi$, define $E_i$ on either path basis by
$$(E_i)_{x,y}= \begin{cases} \sqrt{d_{x_i}d_{y_i}}/d_{x_{i-1}}, &x_j=y_j\ (j\ne i),\ x_{i-1}=x_{i+1}=y_{i-1}=y_{i+1},\\ 0,&\text{otherwise,} \end{cases} \qquad P_i:=\phi^{-1}E_i.$$
Then $P_i$ are orthogonal projections, have ranks $2$ on $\mathcal P_1$
and $3$ on $\mathcal P_\tau$, satisfy
$P_iP_{i+1}P_i=\phi^{-2}P_i$ and commute at separation at least two.
Consequently $B_i=rI+(1-r)P_i$ are unitary matrices satisfying the Artin
relations of $B_6$, with
$\det B_i=r^3$ on $V(\tau^6\to1)$ and $\det B_i=r^5$ on
$V(\tau^6\to\tau)$.

FILE items/lem-qc-tqc-fibonacci-coefficient-ring-specialization.md
Statement

Let $r=e^{-3\pi i/5}$, $\phi=(1+\sqrt5)/2=1-r-r^{-1}$, and
$s=\sqrt\phi>0$. Then
$$\mathbb Z[r,s]\cong \mathbb Z[x,y]\big/\bigl(x^4-x^3+x^2-x+1,\ y^2-(1-x-x^{-1})\bigr),$$
where $x^{-1}=x^9$. The assignment
$$r\mapsto15,\qquad r^{-1}\mapsto29,\qquad \phi\mapsto19,\qquad s\mapsto9$$
defines a ring homomorphism to $\mathbb F_{31}$. The elements $r$ and
$\phi$ map to nonzero units, and conjugation of coefficients is handled by
$r\mapsto r^{-1}$ before reduction.

fixed in the Statement. The carrier states the phases
$R_1=e^{-4\pi i/5}$ and $R_\tau=e^{3\pi i/5}$.

FILE items/lem-qc-tqc-fusion-tree-dimension.md
Statement

Let $\mathcal C$ be a unitary fusion category, let $\operatorname{Irr}(\mathcal C)$
be the representative set fixed in [[def-qc-tqc-fusion-tree-basis]], and take
$a_1,\ldots,a_n,d\in\operatorname{Irr}(\mathcal C)$. Fix any parenthesized
binary tree $T$ on the ordered input list. The fusion-tree vectors of
[[def-qc-tqc-fusion-tree-basis]] form an orthonormal basis of
$\operatorname{Hom}(T,d)$. Their number is

$$m_T(d)=\sum_{\substack{\ell\text{ admissible, with every internal label in }\operatorname{Irr}(\mathcal C)}}\;\prod_{v\,\mathrm{internal}}N_{\ell(v_L)\,\ell(v_R)}^{\ell(v)},$$

where the leaves carry the inputs, the root carries $d$, and each internal edge
is labeled by a representative simple charge. For the left-associated tree on
$n$ inputs this gives $m_0(d)=1$ if $d=\mathbf1$ and $0$ otherwise,
$m_1(d)=1$ if $a_1=d$ and $0$ otherwise, and

$$m_n(d)=\sum_e m_{n-1}(e)N_{e a_n}^d\qquad(n\ge2).$$



[F3] [[def-qc-tqc-fusion-tree-basis]]: a fusion-tree vector is obtained by
composing vertex multiplicity-basis morphisms along the chosen tree, retaining
each internal charge and vertex basis index.

FILE items/lem-qc-tqc-leakage-logical-error-accumulation.md
Statement

Let $P$ be a code projection on a finite-dimensional Hilbert space $V$. Let
$W_1,\ldots,W_G$ be ideal unitaries preserving $\operatorname{ran}P$, and let
$V_1,\ldots,V_G$ be physical unitaries with
$\|V_j-W_j\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le\delta_j$. If
$V_{\mathrm{tot}}=V_G\cdots V_1$ and $W_{\mathrm{tot}}=W_G\cdots W_1$,
then, with empty products equal to $I$ and empty sums equal to $0$,
$$\|V_{\mathrm{tot}}-W_{\mathrm{tot}}\|_{\mathrm{op}} \le\sum_{j=1}^{G}\delta_j.$$
Consequently the logical block error and leakage amplitude relative to the
code action of $W_{\mathrm{tot}}$ are each at most $\sum_j\delta_j$.



FILE items/lem-qc-tqc-pu2-is-so3.md
Statement

Assume $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$. Give $PU(2)$ the quotient metric from
[[def-qc-tqc-projective-gate-and-metric]]. The map
$$q:SU(2)\longrightarrow PU(2),\qquad q(A)=[A],$$
is a two-sheeted covering with kernel $\{I,-I\}$. It induces a topological
group isomorphism $SU(2)/\{\pm I\}\cong PU(2)\cong SO(3)$.



FILE items/lem-qc-tqc-su2-determinant-lifts-dense.md
Statement

Assume $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$. Let $q:SU(2)\to PU(2)$ be the quotient by
$\{\pm I\}$, and let $\Gamma\le PU(2)$ be dense. Then the full inverse image
$q^{-1}(\Gamma)$ is dense in $SU(2)$. Thus projective density gives
determinant-one lifts arbitrarily close to any $SU(2)$ target, while the
choice of a lift can differ by the central sign.



FILE items/lem-qc-tqc-zero-logical-error-forces-zero-leakage.md
Statement

Let $J:\mathcal C\to V$ be an isometric encoding with $P=JJ^*$, let $U$ be
unitary on $\mathcal C$, and let $W$ be unitary on $V$. If the logical block
error $\|PWP-JUJ^*\|_{\mathrm{op}}$ is zero, then the leakage amplitude
$\|(I-P)WP\|_{\mathrm{op}}$ is zero.



FILE items/prop-anyonic-vacuum-channel-projectors-satisfy-temperley-lieb.md
Statement

Let $a$ be a self-dual simple site charge with one-dimensional vacuum fusion $a\otimes a\to\mathbf1$, and supply the normalized pivotal cup/cap datum of [[def-anyonic-frobenius-schur-sign]]. On a repeated-$a$ path space let $P_i:=P_{i,\mathbf1}$ and set $U_i:=\delta_aP_i$, where $\delta_a=\kappa_ad_a$ is the signed loop parameter of [[lem-anyonic-vacuum-f-symbol-frobenius-schur-normalization]]. Then, for all applicable interior indices,

$$U_i^2=\delta_aU_i,\qquad U_iU_{i+1}U_i=U_i,\qquad U_iU_j=U_jU_i\quad(|i-j|>1).$$

The sign $\kappa_a$ is retained; replacing it by $+1$ is justified only in examples whose self-duality datum has that sign.

For the diagram convention, each box $U_i$ has a cup at the top and a cap at the bottom on strands $i,i+1$; multiplication stacks diagrams from top to bottom, and a closed circle evaluates to $\delta_a$. In the left picture the upper box's cup and the lower box's cap remain on the outside, while the upper cap and lower cup form one closed circle. Thus the picture records $U_i^2=\delta_aU_i$; the proof below verifies this and the adjacent and distant relations algebraically, without relying on the drawing.

```tikz
\begin{tikzpicture}[baseline=(current bounding box.center),scale=.72]
  % The outer top cup and bottom cap are the remaining U_i diagram.
  \draw (0,3) .. controls (0,3.65) and (1,3.65) .. (1,3);
  \fill (0,3) circle (1.4pt); \fill (1,3) circle (1.4pt);
  \draw (0,2) .. controls (0,2.65) and (1,2.65) .. (1,2);
  \draw (0,2) .. controls (0,1.35) and (1,1.35) .. (1,2);
  \draw (0,1) .. controls (0,.35) and (1,.35) .. (1,1);
  \fill (0,1) circle (1.4pt); \fill (1,1) circle (1.4pt);
  \node at (1.45,2) {$=\delta_a$};
  \draw (3,3) .. controls (3,3.65) and (4,3.65) .. (4,3);
  \draw (3,1) .. controls (3,.35) and (4,.35) .. (4,1);
  \fill (3,3) circle (1.4pt); \fill (4,3) circle (1.4pt);
  \fill (3,1) circle (1.4pt); \fill (4,1) circle (1.4pt);
\end{tikzpicture}
```
