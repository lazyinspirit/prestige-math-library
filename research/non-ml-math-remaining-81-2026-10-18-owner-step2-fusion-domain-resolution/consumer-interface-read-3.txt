In the two-dimensional Fibonacci fusion space for three $\tau$ anyons of
total charge $\tau$, use the basis $|1\rangle,|\tau\rangle$ labelled by the
charge after fusing the first two anyons. This is the fixed convention of
[[ex-qc-tqc-fibonacci-data]]. For
$$|\psi\rangle=\sqrt{3/5}\,|1\rangle+\sqrt{2/5}\,|\tau\rangle,$$
measurement of that pair's total charge yields probabilities $3/5$ and
$2/5$ and conditional states $|1\rangle$ and $|\tau\rangle$. If either
coefficient is zero, its outcome has probability zero and there is no
normalized conditional state for that outcome.

[F6] The six-anyon triple code has dimension $4$ in both total-charge blocks;
in total charge $1$, the ambient dimension is $5$.

1.1 In the pairwise tree $T_p=(((\tau_1\otimes\tau_2)\otimes(\tau_3\otimes\tau_4)) \otimes(\tau_5\otimes\tau_6))$, let $q_i\in\{0,1\}$ label the charge $1$ or $\tau$ of pair $(2i-1,2i)$, respectively. Fusion to total charge $1$ allows exactly the strings $$000,\quad011,\quad101,\quad110,\quad111.$$ For $000$, the intermediate charge after the first two pair outputs is $0$. For $011,101,110,111$, it is uniquely $1,1,0,1$, respectively; each then fuses with the last pair output to total charge $1$. A string with exactly one $1$ has total charge $\tau$ and is inadmissible. Both are four-dimensional subspaces of the five-dimensional total-charge-$1$ fusion space. The argument below treats them as independent code choices and makes no projector-equality claim. ## Statement

For every Fibonacci braid word $b$ with exponent sum $k$, its physical
operator differs from the normalized operator by the common scalar
$R_1^k$ on every fixed-charge block. On the three-anyon SU(2)-lift alphabet
$zB_j$, the corresponding scalar is $(R_1z^{-1})^k$. The scalar leaves
uncontrolled fixed-sector measurement probabilities unchanged; under a
coherent control it is a relative phase and cannot be discarded without a
phase-reference correction.

FILE items/lem-qa-37-local-braid-acts-trivially-on-spectators.md

## Statement

Let $n\ge1$, $c\in\{1,\tau\}$, and consider the fixed fusion space
$V(\tau^{3n}\to c)$ with its grouped tree of $n$ consecutive triples and
outer triple-charge tree. Its orthogonal decomposition is indexed by local
triple outputs $a_i\in\{1,\tau\}$ and has factors
$$\left(\bigotimes_{i=1}^n V(\tau^3\to a_i)\right) \otimes V(a_1\otimes\cdots\otimes a_n\to c),$$
with zero-dimensional summands omitted. A braid word supported on the
internal three strands of the $i$th triple acts on this summand as
$\rho_{a_i}(b)$ on $V(\tau^3\to a_i)$ and the identity on every other local
triple factor and on the outer spectator factor. Reassociating to the fixed
left-associated $3n$-leaf tree conjugates this operator by a unitary; its
operator norm and the stated local-factor action are preserved.

[F2] A fixed-charge braid acts by precomposition with the inverse object
braid on the corresponding fusion morphism, and defines a unitary
representation. [[def-qc-tqc-braid-action-from-fusion-data]]
[[thm-qc-tqc-braid-action-unitary-and-artin]]

1.1 In the grouped tree, choose an admissible local fusion-tree basis vector $f_i\in V(\tau^3\to a_i)$ for each triple and a basis vector $g\in V(a_1\otimes\cdots\otimes a_n\to c)$ for the outer fusion. The composites obtained by inserting $f_1,\ldots,f_n$ into $g$ are exactly the grouped fusion-tree vectors. By completeness and orthonormality [F1], their orthogonal direct sum over $(a_1,\ldots,a_n)$ is the full grouped fusion space, which gives the displayed tensor/direct-sum decomposition.
