FILE items/lem-ell-four-root-ising-gauge-and-chirality-conversion.md
Statement

Set $q=e^{\pi i/4}$, $s=e^{\pi i/8}$ and $d=\sqrt2$. The specified rank-one tilting negligible quotient is ribbon-equivalent to the normal-data category of [[lem-ising-unitary-normal-data-and-ribbon-coherence]] at $\kappa=-1$, $\beta_-=-s^{-3}$, with labels $p_0=\mathbf1$, $p_1=D$, $p_2=\eta$. The full explicit normalized trivalent gauge is given in the proof using its negative-loop cup $w$, cap $b$, and $p_2=1+wb/d$. It gives $F_D^{DDD}=-2^{-1/2}\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}$, FS sign $-1$, positive dimension $\sqrt2$, and $\theta_D=s^3$.

Multiplying the associator by $\omega(x,y,z)=(-1)^{|x||y||z|}$ and the braiding by $(-i)^{|x||y|}$, with $D$ odd and group objects even, gives the distinct $\kappa=+1$, $\beta_+=s$ category. Flip the $D$ evaluation and keep its coevaluation. Its complete pullback R data are $R_{DD}^0=s^{-1}$, $R_{DD}^\eta=i s^{-1}$, $R_{\eta D}^D=R_{D\eta}^D=-i$, $R_{\eta\eta}^0=-1$, and unit entries one. Its twist is $\theta_D=s^{-1}$ in the actual pullback orientation. This is an associator/braiding conversion, not a vertex gauge equivalence or an unmodified positive-loop pivotal TL equivalence. Reversing its braiding and twist is a further explicitly different chirality operation.



FILE items/lem-ising-unitary-normal-data-and-ribbon-coherence.md
Statement

Put $G=\mathbb Z/2=\{0,1\}$, with $0=\mathbf1$, $1=\eta$, and a third simple $D$. Use $ab=a+b$, $aD=Da=D$, and $DD=0\oplus1$, with summands ordered $(0,1)$ and every nonzero elementary fusion multiplicity equal to one. Let $d=\sqrt2$, $\kappa\in\{-1,1\}$, $\tau=\kappa/d$, $\chi(a,b)=(-1)^{ab}$, $u(0)=1$, $u(1)=i$, and choose $\beta$ of modulus one with $\beta^2=\tau(1+i)$. There is a unitary skeletal spherical ribbon fusion category with canonical orthonormal summand vertices and the following complete normal data. All admissible one-dimensional F blocks are $1$, except $F_D^{\eta D\eta}=F_\eta^{D\eta D}=-1$, and
$$F_D^{DDD}=\frac\kappa{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}.$$
Rows are right-tree channels $(f,1,1)$ and columns left-tree channels $(e,1,1)$ in the inverse-precomposition convention. Unit blocks are identities and impossible channels have the unique empty matrix. The associator permutes the $DD$ summand by addition of a group input when needed; its complete formula and pentagon are proved below.

The object braiding is $c_{a,b}=\chi(a,b)$, $c_{a,D}=c_{D,a}=u(a)$, and $c_{D,D}^g=\beta/u(g)$. The pullback R symbols are their inverses, in the specified canonical vertices. Both hexagons hold in every channel. The normalized self-duality has $\operatorname{coev}_D=\sqrt d\,i_{DD}^0$, $\operatorname{ev}_D=\kappa\sqrt d\,p_{DD}^0$, FS sign $\kappa$, positive pivotal dimension $d$, and twist $\theta_D=\kappa/\beta$; $\eta$ has dimension and FS sign one and twist $-1$. The canonical left-dual tensorator is $\kappa$ on both $DD$ channels and one on all other channels; its double-dual tensorator is one on all ten admissible channels. Relative to those duals $j_D=\kappa$, $j_0=j_\eta=1$, and $j_{a\otimes b}=\nu_{a,b}(j_a\otimes j_b)$ holds channel by channel. For explicit phase specializations write $s=e^{\pi i/8}$. These assertions construct the full category; they do not assume a sector example as a supplier.

FILE items/lem-qa-37-fixed-sector-global-phase.md
Statement

For every Fibonacci braid word $b$ with exponent sum $k$, its physical
operator differs from the normalized operator by the common scalar
$R_1^k$ on every fixed-charge block. On the three-anyon SU(2)-lift alphabet
$zB_j$, the corresponding scalar is $(R_1z^{-1})^k$. The scalar leaves
uncontrolled fixed-sector measurement probabilities unchanged; under a
coherent control it is a relative phase and cannot be discarded without a
phase-reference correction.



FILE items/lem-qa-37-local-braid-acts-trivially-on-spectators.md
Statement

Let $n\ge1$, $c\in\{1,\tau\}$, and consider the fixed fusion space
$V(\tau^{3n}\to c)$ with its grouped tree of $n$ consecutive triples and
outer triple-charge tree. Its orthogonal decomposition is indexed by local
triple outputs $a_i\in\{1,\tau\}$ and has factors
$$\left(\bigotimes_{i=1}^n V(\tau^3\to a_i)\right) \otimes V(a_1\otimes\cdots\otimes a_n\to c),$$
with zero-dimensional summands omitted. A braid word supported on the
internal three strands of the $i$th triple acts on this summand as
$\rho_{a_i}(b)$ on $V(\tau^3\to a_i)$ and the identity on every other local
triple factor and on the outer spectator factor. Reassociating to the fixed
left-associated $3n$-leaf tree conjugates this operator by a unitary; its
operator norm and the stated local-factor action are preserved.



[F1] A fusion-tree basis composes orthonormal local vertex morphisms and is
complete; its basis labels include every internal charge and multiplicity
index. [[def-qc-tqc-fusion-tree-basis]]

[F2] A fixed-charge braid acts by precomposition with the inverse object
braid on the corresponding fusion morphism, and defines a unitary
representation. [[def-qc-tqc-braid-action-from-fusion-data]]
[[thm-qc-tqc-braid-action-unitary-and-artin]]

FILE items/lem-qa-37-total-error-budget.md
Statement

Let $A_0$ and $A_1$ be the initial and endpoint isometries in a finite
adiabatic braid input, let $V$ be the ideal logical unitary, and let $W_c$ be
a compiled logical unitary. Suppose
$$\|W_c-V\|_{\rm op}\le\epsilon_c,\qquad \|P(1)U_T(1)A_0-A_1V\|_{\rm op}\le\epsilon_{\rm tr},$$
and let the JRS leakage amplitude be bounded by $A_m(1)$. Fix a finite POVM $\mathsf M$ on the physical output space. If the
implemented measurement distribution differs from the distribution of this
same $\mathsf M$ on $U_T(1)A_0\rho A_0^*U_T(1)^*$ by at most $\epsilon_m$
in total variation, then for every input density operator $\rho$ the total
measured-output variation from the ideal compiled output
$A_1W_c\rho W_c^*A_1^*$ is at most
$$\epsilon_c+\epsilon_{\rm tr}+A_m(1)+\epsilon_m.$$
The transport mismatch is an explicit independent premise; the JRS estimate
bounds leakage only.

FILE items/lem-qc-tqc-determinant-character-phase.md
Statement

Fix the Fibonacci convention with common exchange scalar $R_1$. Let
$e:B_n\to\mathbb Z$ be exponent sum, sending each Artin generator $\sigma_i$
to $1$. If $\widetilde\rho$ is the generator-normalized representation
$\widetilde\rho(\sigma_i)=R_1^{-1}\rho(\sigma_i)$, then for every braid $b$
and every total-charge block,
$$\rho(b)=R_1^{e(b)}\widetilde\rho(b).$$
The scalar is the same on every total-charge block and every spectator
multiplicity. It is a global phase for an uncontrolled fixed-sector
computation; under coherent control it is a relative phase and must be
tracked.



[A1] Assume every generator has the same scalar $R_1$ on all fusion
channels; its relative channel phases define the normalized generator. The
actual Fibonacci data carrier fixes $R_1=e^{-4\pi i/5}$ and
$R_\tau=e^{3\pi i/5}$ in its stated gauge.
