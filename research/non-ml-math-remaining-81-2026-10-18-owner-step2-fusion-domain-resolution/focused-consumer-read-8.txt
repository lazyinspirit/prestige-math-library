FILE items/prop-fibonacci-fusion-ring-unit-is-not-tensor-invertible.md
Statement

In the coherent Fibonacci category [[ex-qc-tqc-fibonacci-data]], with simple objects $\mathbf1,\tau$ and $\tau\otimes\tau\cong\mathbf1\oplus\tau$, the fusion-ring class $[\tau]$ is a unit but the object $\tau$ is not tensor-invertible in the sense of [[def-categorical-object-and-operator-invertibility]].



[F1] [[ex-qc-tqc-fibonacci-data]]: the simple labels are $\mathbf1,\tau$ and $\tau\otimes\tau=\mathbf1\oplus\tau$.

FILE items/thm-anyonic-channel-projectors-are-reparenthesization-independent.md
Statement

For fixed ordered tensor inputs and fixed source and target parenthesizations, any two sequences of canonical associator F-moves between them induce the same coordinate map. Consequently, conjugating a fixed pair-channel projection by either sequence gives the same operator on the fusion-path space.



[F3] [[def-anyonic-local-channel-projector]]: a local channel projector is conjugation of the channel projection by the chosen F-move composite.

FILE items/thm-anyonic-local-channel-projectors-are-orthogonal.md
Statement

For a unitary fusion category and any fixed adjacent pair of simple site charges, the family $(P_{i,s})_s$ from [[def-anyonic-local-channel-projector]] is pairwise orthogonal, self-adjoint, and idempotent, and its sum over all simple pair outputs $s$ is the identity on the path space.



[F1] [[def-anyonic-local-channel-projector]]: $P_{i,s}=F_i^{-1}\Pi_sF_i$, where $\Pi_s$ projects onto all basis coordinates of pair output $s$.

FILE items/thm-anyonic-path-space-is-hilbert-under-unitarity.md
Statement

Let $\mathcal C$ be a unitary fusion category, and use orthonormal bases in each split space $W_{x,a}^y=\operatorname{Hom}(y,x\otimes a)$, equivalently the adjoints of orthonormal bases of $V_{x a}^y=\operatorname{Hom}(x\otimes a,y)$. Give $H_{a,n;b,c}=\operatorname{Hom}(c,T_n)$ the Hom-space inner product linear in the first variable, determined by $g^\dagger f=\langle f,g\rangle\operatorname{id}_c$. Then the path composites of [[def-anyonic-fusion-path-space]] are an orthonormal basis of $H_{a,n;b,c}$.



[F1] [[def-anyonic-fusion-path-space]]: $H_{a,n;b,c}=\operatorname{Hom}(c,T_n)$ and every path composite is formed from the inclusions $i_j:x_j\to x_{j-1}\otimes a$ by tensoring with identities and composition.

[F4] [[lem-anyonic-fusion-path-basis]]: the path composites are already a basis of $H_{a,n;b,c}$.

FILE items/thm-ising-periodic-two-sector-and-line-representation.md
Statement

Use either coherent $\kappa$ category of [[lem-ising-unitary-normal-data-and-ribbon-coherence]], with reference charge $D$ and representative set $\operatorname{Irr}(\mathcal C)=\{\mathbf1,\eta,D\}$. For $L\ge1$ define the periodic carrier

$$\mathcal H_L:=\bigoplus_{b\in\operatorname{Irr}(\mathcal C)}H_{D,L;b,b},$$

using the fixed-boundary path spaces of [[def-anyonic-fusion-path-space]] and the orthogonal Hilbert direct-sum inner product. The union of their fusion-path bases, supplied as orthonormal bases by [[thm-anyonic-path-space-is-hilbert-under-unitarity]], is the cyclic path basis: a path in the $b$ summand has $x_1=b$, $x_{L+1}=x_1$, and retains every elementary fusion index. Odd $L$ gives the zero space. For $L=2N$, $N\ge1$, this complete orthonormal carrier is $H\oplus\bar H$, each sector of dimension $2^N$, with the explicit bit identifications in the proof. These identifications intertwine every vacuum pair projector, including wraparound:
$$P_{2j-1}|_H=(1+X_j)/2,\quad P_{2j}|_H=(1+Z_jZ_{j+1})/2,$$
$$P_{2j-1}|_{\bar H}=(1+Z_jZ_{j+1})/2,\quad P_{2j}|_{\bar H}=(1+X_{j+1})/2.$$
All bit indices are modulo $N$. For $N=1$ the bond is the identity; no three-distinct-site cyclic TL assertion is made for this length-two identification.

Let $C|b_1\cdots b_N\rangle=|b_2\cdots b_Nb_1\rangle$, $Q=\prod_jX_j$, and
$$K_{c,b}=2^{-N/2}(-1)^{\sum_jc_j(b_{j-1}+b_j)}.$$
The one-step periodic path shift and the annular line maps defined by the full cyclic F contraction satisfy
$$T=\begin{pmatrix}0&C\\I&0\end{pmatrix},\quad Y_\eta=\operatorname{diag}(Q,Q),\quad Y_D=\kappa^N\begin{pmatrix}0&K^\dagger\\K&0\end{pmatrix}.$$
They obey $TP_iT^{-1}=P_{i-1}$, $Y_D^2=I+Y_\eta$, $Y_\eta^2=I$, $Y_\eta Y_D=Y_DY_\eta=Y_D$, and each line map commutes with every represented $P_i$ and with $T$. In contrast the specified normalized single-chain map obeys $(K/\sqrt2)^2=(I+Q)C^{-1}/2$. The proof supplies all wrapping, kernel, rank, Hamiltonian, and intertwining assertions on the full two-sector space, without a total-flux or global-spin-parity projection.



[F2] [[def-anyonic-fusion-path-space]] identifies each summand of $\mathcal H_L$ with the typed fixed-boundary Hom space and retains each vertex index.

[F3] [[thm-anyonic-path-space-is-hilbert-under-unitarity]] makes the path basis of each summand orthonormal; their finite direct sum is the carrier's orthonormal cyclic path basis.

FILE items/thm-qa-37-approx-encoded-two-qubit-gate.md
Statement

Assume $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$ and use the six-anyon triple-triple encoding
[[def-qc-tqc-six-anyon-encoded-subspace]]. For any $U\in U(4)$ put
$$T_1=U\oplus(\det U)^{-1}\in SU(5),\qquad T_\tau=U\oplus\operatorname{diag}((\det U)^{-1},1,1,1)\in SU(8).$$
For every $\delta>0$, one normalized six-strand braid word $b$ has both block
errors $\|B_1(b)-T_1\|_{\rm op}<\delta$ and
$\|B_\tau(b)-T_\tau\|_{\rm op}<\delta$. In either total-charge block, the
normalized braid's logical block error and leakage amplitude relative to the
encoded action of $U$ are at most $\delta$. The physical categorical braid is
$\zeta B(b)$ with $\zeta=R_1^{e(b)}$; its logical block error is at most
$\delta$ relative to the phase-matched target $\zeta U$, and its leakage
amplitude is unchanged. This common scalar is recorded under
[[def-qa-37-phase-reference-cost]] and must be handled under coherent control.
When the two determinant-corrected targets are supplied as certified Cauchy
inputs, the dovetailed B6 compiler finds such a word. No useful joint-block
word-length or search-time bound is asserted.



FILE items/thm-qa-37-arbitrary-n-encoded-circuit.md
Statement

Assume $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$. Fix the inverse-closed Fibonacci SU(2) gate
package and its supplied finite base-net certificate
$(\mathcal N_0,\eta_0,L_0)$ from
[[def-qa-37-fibonacci-braid-gate-set]]. Let $n\ge1$, let
$c\in\{1,\tau\}$ satisfy $V(\tau^n\to c)\ne0$, and let
$U\in U(2^n)$ and $\epsilon>0$. Use the arbitrary-$n$ triple code
$\mathcal C_{n,c}$, isometry $J_{n,c}$, and projector $P_{n,c}$ of
[[def-qa-37-n-triple-fusion-code]]. There exists a finite physical
Fibonacci braid word $b$ and a unit scalar $\omega$ such that
$$\|J_{n,c}^*\rho(b)J_{n,c} -\omega(U\otimes I_{V(\tau^n\to c)})\|_{\rm op}\le\epsilon, \qquad \|(I-P_{n,c})\rho(b)J_{n,c}\|_{\rm op}\le\epsilon.$$
The bounds are uniform over spectator fusion states. The proof composes exact
QSD, SK approximation of one-qubit SU(2) representatives, and joint B6
targets extended safely over spectators. The scalar $\omega$ is the product
of braid exponent-sum and one-qubit lift phases; under coherent control it
must be retained or corrected. For certified Cauchy target names the B6
dovetail terminates, but no useful joint-block word-length or search-time
bound follows. This is a mathematical existence result and does not imply a
physical realization.

FILE items/thm-qa-37-exhaustive-b6-compiler-terminates.md
Statement

Assume $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$. For every certified Cauchy input pair
$(T_5,T_8)\in SU(5)\times SU(8)$ and every positive rational
$\varepsilon$, the exhaustive compiler of
[[alg-qa-37-exhaustive-b6-block-pair-compiler]] halts with one six-strand
Fibonacci braid word whose normalized total-charge-$1$ and total-charge-$\tau$
matrices are each within operator norm $\varepsilon$ of $T_5,T_8$,
respectively. Density proves termination, but this result gives no useful
joint-block word-length or search-time bound.
