## Statement

Let $n\ge1$ and $c\in\{1,\tau\}$ with $V(\tau^n\to c)\ne0$, using the
left-associated tree for both fixed-charge spaces. In the ordered list of
$3n$ Fibonacci $\tau$ charges, group consecutive leaves into $n$ triples;
within each triple fuse its first two charges and then its third, and fuse the
$n$ triple outputs by the left-associated $n$-leaf tree to total $c$. Restrict
each triple output to $\tau$; its two local paths are indexed by the charge
$x\in\{1,\tau\}$ after the first two leaves. Fix a rebracketing sequence
of associators from this grouped tree to the fixed left-associated $3n$-leaf
tree, and let $R_{n,c}$ be the induced composite unitary. For each tensor
basis vector $e_{x_1}\otimes\cdots\otimes e_{x_n}\otimes v_\lambda$, where
$e_1,e_\tau$ are the local triple paths and $v_\lambda$ is a basis vector of
$V(\tau^n\to c)$, map it to its grouped fusion-tree basis vector, then
apply $R_{n,c}$. Extend linearly and call the resulting map $J_{n,c}$. Its
image is the code $\mathcal C_{n,c}$ and it is an isometry; set
$P_{n,c}=J_{n,c}J_{n,c}^*$ for the code projector.
The factor $V(\tau^n\to c)$ retains all spectator fusion paths and is not a
physical preparation at zero cost.

[F3] The fixed-charge code projector associated with an isometry is
$JJ^*$. [[def-qc-tqc-fixed-charge-encoding]]

2.1 Choose the fusion-tree basis of the finite spectator space $V(\tau^n\to c)$. Tensoring its basis with the $n$ local two-path bases from step 1.1 gives an orthonormal basis of the domain. By [F2], its grouped-tree images are precisely an orthonormal family in $V(\tau^{3n}\to c)$. The specified associator rebracketing is unitary by [F4], so their images in the fixed tree remain orthonormal; the linear map $J_{n,c}$ sending the tensor basis to these vectors is therefore an isometry. Applying [F3] gives the orthogonal projector $P_{n,c}=J_{n,c}J_{n,c}^*$. All choices are from fixed finite bases, and no choice axiom is used. [F2, F3, F4, step 1.1, algebra] ∎

## Definition

For a finite anyon solver instance and one run, record separately the tuple
$$\bigl(L_{\rm in},\ N_{\rm any},\ |b|,\ N_{\rm prep},\ N_{\rm anc},\ N_{\rm meas},\ B_{\rm out},\ b_{\max},\ t_{\rm cl},\ T_H,\ \epsilon_{\rm op},\epsilon_{\rm tr},\epsilon_{\rm meas},\ell_{\rm leak},\ p_{\rm leak},\ N_{\rm phase},\ t_{\rm phase}\bigr).$$
Here $L_{\rm in}$ is the complete finite classical input-description bit
length, including the declared task, state/preparation descriptions when
classically encoded, and tolerance descriptions; a directly supplied quantum
input is not assumed to have a finite classical coordinate description;
$N_{\rm any}$ is the number of ordered simple-charge strand labels in the
fusion-space instance; and $|b|$ is the number of braid-generator occurrences
(inverse generators count as one). $N_{\rm prep}$ counts elementary
operations in the declared preparation procedure, while $N_{\rm anc}$ counts
the ancillary anyons or other physical ancilla systems used by preparation or
computation. If the initial state is supplied directly as an abstract quantum
input with no preparation interface, $N_{\rm prep}$ is “not applicable”, not
silently set to zero; $N_{\rm anc}$ still records any declared solver ancillas
(zero if none, or “not applicable” if no physical ancilla model is supplied).
$N_{\rm meas}$ is the number of fusion measurements,
and $B_{\rm out}$ is the total bit length of their classical outcome record
under its declared finite encoding (zero when no measurement occurs). The
largest unary precision query to a certified matrix name is $b_{\max}$; $t_{\rm cl}$
is the classical bit time used by search and postprocessing. $T_H$ is the total
Hamiltonian evolution time over the run in the declared physical time unit;
it is “not applicable” for an abstract braid-only model without Hamiltonian
evolution. The error coordinates $\epsilon_{\rm op},\epsilon_{\rm tr},
\epsilon_{\rm meas},\ell_{\rm leak},p_{\rm leak}$ are respectively a stated
operator-norm approximation bound, an independent in-band transport bound,
an implementation-to-ideal-measurement total-variation bound, and leakage
amplitude and probability bounds. $N_{\rm phase},t_{\rm phase}$ count phase
corrections and their classical control cost. Circuit description and
elementary wire costs follow [[def-qc-circuit-cost-model]], while classical
input bit cost follows [[def-qa-classical-bit-cost]] and finite nonoracle
gates follow [[def-qa-nonoracle-gate-cost]]. An inapplicable coordinate is
marked “not applicable”; it is not assigned value zero. Unless an interface
explicitly states a bound, no braid length, preparation operation, ancilla,
matrix precision, measurement, Hamiltonian time, search step, or phase
correction is free. The tuple is a bookkeeping convention; it does not assert
any of its error or runtime bounds.

## Definition

Let $x$ be a simple charge in a unitary braided fusion category, let $n\ge2$,
and fix a total charge $c$ with nonzero multiplicity space
$V(x^n\to c)$. Use the left-associated fusion-tree basis. For the Artin
generator $\sigma_i$ that exchanges the $i$th and $(i+1)$st strands, let
$T_i$ be the specified sequence of $F$-moves that changes from the fixed tree
basis to a basis where those two charges fuse first, and let $R_i$ apply the
corresponding $R$-symbol on each pair-charge channel. Define
$$\rho_c(\sigma_i):=T_i^{-1}R_iT_i.$$
This defines the generator assignment. The next theorem proves that the
assignment descends from words to a well-defined group homomorphism. Word
products use matrix multiplication acting right-to-left on column vectors.
The abstract group in this definition is the Artin group
[[def-braid-group-by-the-artin-presentation]]; the positive crossing is the
generator convention used for the displayed $R$-symbols. If a motion is
described chronologically, its matrix product is written in reverse temporal
order.

FILE items/def-qc-tqc-fixed-charge-encoding.md
## Definition

Let $V=V(x^n\to c)\ne0$ be the finite-dimensional fixed-total-charge fusion
space for $n$ identical charges $x$, equipped with the unitary fusion-tree
inner product from [[def-qc-tqc-fusion-tree-basis]]. Let
$\mathcal C=\mathbb C^k$ with its ordered orthonormal computational basis,
where $k\ge1$. A **fixed-charge encoding** is an isometry
$J:\mathcal C\to V$, so $J^*J=I_{\mathcal C}$. Its code subspace is
$J\mathcal C$, and its code projector is
$$P:=JJ^*:V\to V.$$
An encoding records a chosen subspace of a fusion space; it does not identify
that subspace with an object of the fusion category.

FILE items/def-qc-tqc-logical-error-and-leakage.md
## Definition

Let $J:\mathcal C\to V$ be a fixed-charge encoding, $P=JJ^*$ its code
projector, $U\in U(\mathcal C)$ a logical target, and $W\in U(V)$ an ambient
unitary. Its zero-on-the-complement target extension is
$\widetilde U:=JUJ^*:V\to V$. Define the **logical block error** and
**leakage amplitude** by
$$\epsilon_{\mathrm{log}}(W,U):=\|PWP-\widetilde U\|_{\mathrm{op}}, \qquad \epsilon_{\mathrm{leak}}(W):=\|(I-P)WP\|_{\mathrm{op}}.$$
Both are finite operator norms. The first measures the compressed block
against the target; the second measures transfer from the code into its
orthogonal complement. They diagnose different errors.

[F1] $P$ is the orthogonal projection onto $J\mathcal C$.

## Definition

Assume the Fibonacci fusion rules
$1\otimes x=x\otimes1=x$ and $\tau\otimes\tau=1\oplus\tau$, with each
listed summand of multiplicity one. For six ordered $\tau$ charges, let
$W_a$ be the fixed-input, fixed-total fusion space of total charge
$a\in\{1,\tau\}$ in the tree that first fuses leaves $1,2,3$ and $4,5,6$
separately, then fuses those triple outputs. Let
$V_a$ be the same fixed-charge space in the left-associated tree.

For one triple of total charge $\tau$, its two orthonormal path vectors are
indexed by the charge $x\in\{1,\tau\}$ after the first two anyons: both
$x\otimes\tau\to\tau$ channels have multiplicity one. Write these vectors as
$|x\rangle$. For $x,y\in\{1,\tau\}$ and $a\in\{1,\tau\}$, let $|x,y;a\rangle$
be the grouped-tree vector with first triple path $x$, first triple total
$\tau$, second triple path $y$, second triple total $\tau$, and final vertex
channel $\tau\otimes\tau\to a$. The last channel has multiplicity one. Define
$$\mathcal C_a:=G_a\bigl(\operatorname{span}\{|x,y;a\rangle: x,y\in\{1,\tau\}\}\bigr)\subseteq V_a.$$
In the ordered basis
$(G_a|1,1;a\rangle,G_a|1,\tau;a\rangle,
G_a|\tau,1;a\rangle,G_a|\tau,\tau;a\rangle)$, the inclusion
$J_a:\mathbb C^4\to V_a$ is an isometry; its code projector is $P_a=J_aJ_a^*$.
The same ordered local triple paths are used for $a=1$ and $a=\tau$; only
the final pair-charge coordinate differs. The orthogonal complements are
called leakage spaces.

**Given:** The stated Fibonacci fusion rules, the multiplicity-aware
fusion-tree basis, the unitary associator, and the fixed-charge encoding
convention.

[F3] An isometric inclusion $J$ has code projector $JJ^*$ onto its image.
[[def-qc-tqc-fixed-charge-encoding]]

1.1 The fusion rule gives $\dim V(\tau^2\to1)=\dim V(\tau^2\to\tau)=1$ and therefore $\dim V(\tau^3\to1)=1$, $\dim V(\tau^3\to\tau)=2$. Applying the fusion-tree count [F2] to the grouped tree, total charge $1$ receives one basis vector from the $(1,1)$ triple-output pair and four from $(\tau,\tau)$, so $\dim W_1=1+2\cdot2=5$. Total charge $\tau$ receives two vectors from $(1,\tau)$, two from $(\tau,1)$, and four from $(\tau,\tau)$, so $\dim W_\tau=2+2+4=8$. The map $G_a$ in [F4] is unitary, hence $\dim V_1=5$ and $\dim V_\tau=8$. [A1, F2, F4, algebra]
