FILE items/thm-qc-tqc-braid-action-unitary-and-artin.md
Statement

For a unitary braided fusion category, a simple charge $x$, and a fixed total
charge $c$, the operators in
[[def-qc-tqc-braid-action-from-fusion-data]] define a unitary group
homomorphism
$$\rho_c:B_n\longrightarrow U(V(x^n\to c)).$$
In particular, the adjacent Artin relations and distant commutation relations
hold in each fixed-charge block.



[F2] The current QC25 bodies define finite semisimple orthogonal fusion-tree
spaces, unitary associators and braidings, and unitary F/R coordinates; their
pentagon and hexagon coordinate identities match the categorical coherence
maps. The fusion-tree dimension lemma supplies complete bases.

[[def-qc-tqc-fusion-tree-basis]]
[[lem-qc-tqc-fusion-tree-dimension]] [[def-braiding]]

2.1 By [F1], the local categorical braidings on the full tensor power satisfy the Artin relations and define a homomorphism for the fixed parenthesization. Finite semisimplicity and orthogonality in [F2] decompose that tensor power into total-charge blocks; naturality of the braiding makes each block invariant, so restriction to $V(x^n\to c)$ preserves the same relations. In the fusion-tree basis, these restricted operators are exactly $T_i^{-1}R_iT_i$ by the definition in [[def-qc-tqc-braid-action-from-fusion-data]]. The pentagon and hexagon coordinate identities in [F2] identify those matrices with the same parenthesized composites; distant generators commute because they act on disjoint tensor factors. Hence the restricted assignment is a unitary representation of the Artin presentation. [F1, F2, given, algebra] ∎

Assume $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$. Let $B_1,B_2$ be the projective Fibonacci generators on the two-dimensional

FILE items/thm-qc-tqc-gauge-covariance.md
Statement

Let $\mathcal C$ be a unitary braided fusion category. A finite list of simple
charges and total charge determines a fusion space in each parenthesization.
For a crossing word with underlying object map $b_\beta:P_L\to P_{L'}$, define
its action on the corresponding Hom spaces by
$f\mapsto f\circ b_\beta^{-1}$, from $\operatorname{Hom}(P_L,d)$ to
$\operatorname{Hom}(P_{L'},d)$. Let $U_\beta$ be its matrix in chosen
fusion-tree bases. If each multiplicity basis is changed by the unitary
gauge matrices of [[def-qc-tqc-gauge-transform]], and $G_L,G_{L'}$ are the
induced source and target tree-basis unitaries, then

$$U_\beta'=G_{L'}^{-1}U_\beta G_L.$$

In particular, when $n\ge2$ and all $n$ strand charges are the same simple object $a$,
the adjacent exchanges act on the same fixed-total fusion space
$V=\operatorname{Hom}(a^{\otimes n},d)$, satisfy the Artin relations, and
define a unitary representation $\rho:B_n\to U(V)$, where $U(V)$ is the group
of invertible linear maps preserving the inner product on $V$.
If $V=0$, this is the unique action on the zero-dimensional space.
In that case $\rho'(\beta)=G_L^{-1}\rho(\beta)G_L$ for every braid $\beta$.
Thus basis-dependent matrix entries change, while the braid action up to
unitary equivalence and consistently transformed expectation values do not.



[F4] [[def-qc-tqc-gauge-transform]] and
[[def-qc-tqc-fusion-tree-basis]]: local basis changes are unitary, and the
induced tree-basis change is the direct sum of the corresponding tensor
products at the vertices.

FILE items/thm-qc-tqc-hexagon-coordinates.md
Statement

Let $\mathcal C$ be a unitary braided fusion category with associator $\alpha$ and
braiding $c$. For all objects $X,Y,Z$, its two hexagons are

$$\alpha_{Y,Z,X}\circ c_{X,Y\otimes Z}\circ\alpha_{X,Y,Z} =(\operatorname{id}_Y\otimes c_{X,Z})\circ\alpha_{Y,X,Z} \circ(c_{X,Y}\otimes\operatorname{id}_Z),$$

$$\alpha_{Z,X,Y}^{-1}\circ c_{X\otimes Y,Z}\circ\alpha_{X,Y,Z}^{-1} =(c_{X,Z}\otimes\operatorname{id}_Y)\circ\alpha_{X,Z,Y}^{-1} \circ(\operatorname{id}_X\otimes c_{Y,Z}).$$

For simple input charges and a fixed simple total charge, the two route matrices
in each equation are equal in fusion-tree coordinates. Each entry is the sum
over all intermediate simple charges and multiplicity indices of the products
of the corresponding F- and R-block entries.



FILE items/thm-qc-tqc-ising-braids-clifford-only.md
Statement

Assume a Majorana–Ising exchange interface as defined in
[[def-qc-tqc-ising-majorana-interface]]. Let
$\gamma_1,\ldots,\gamma_{2m}$ be Majorana operators on the
$2^m$-dimensional fermionic Fock space, with
$\gamma_j^*=\gamma_j$, $\gamma_j^2=I$, and
$\gamma_j\gamma_k=-\gamma_k\gamma_j$ for $j\ne k$. On either fixed
eigenspace of the total parity
$$\Pi=i^m\gamma_1\gamma_2\cdots\gamma_{2m},$$
the adjacent Ising exchanges, up to their common physical scalar, are
$$U_j=\exp(-\pi\gamma_j\gamma_{j+1}/4),\qquad 1\le j<2m.$$
For $m\ge2$ they normalize the logical Pauli group on the $m-1$ encoded
qubits. Their braid-only projective image is finite and is not dense in
$PU(2^{m-1})$.



FILE items/thm-qc-tqc-pentagon-coordinates.md
Statement

Let $a,b,c,d$ be four simple input charges and $t$ a simple total charge. The
root associator identity

$$\alpha_{a,b,c\otimes d}\circ\alpha_{a\otimes b,c,d} =(\operatorname{id}_a\otimes\alpha_{b,c,d})\circ\alpha_{a,b\otimes c,d} \circ(\alpha_{a,b,c}\otimes\operatorname{id}_d)$$

has equal matrices along its two routes in the fusion-tree bases. If $A_1,A_2,A_3$
are the three successive matrices on the right and $A_4,A_5$ the two successive
matrices on the left, then

$$A_3A_2A_1=A_5A_4.$$

Each matrix coefficient sums over every intermediate simple charge and every
multiplicity-basis index.



FILE items/thm-qc-tqc-spectator-extension.md
Statement

Assume $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$, $n\ge2$, and a total charge $c$ with
$V(\tau^n\to c)\ne0$. The code in $3n$ $\tau$ anyons factors as
$\mathcal C_n\cong(\mathbb C^2)^{\otimes n}\otimes V(\tau^n\to c)$.
For any adjacent pair of encoded triples, any $U\in U(4)$, and any
$\delta>0$, one normalized six-strand braid induces an ambient operator
within $\delta$ of the code-preserving ideal extension of $U$ on the entire
fixed-charge space $V(\tau^{3n}\to c)$. Consequently its logical block error
and leakage amplitude are each at most $\delta$, uniformly over all
spectator fusion paths and states that already have a component outside the
code.
