FILE items/ex-qc-tqc-fibonacci-data.md
Example

Take the skeletal complex-linear category whose simple labels are
$\mathbf1,\tau$ and whose objects are finite direct sums of them, including
the empty direct sum $0$. For objects
$A=\bigoplus_s s^{\oplus m_s}$ and $B=\bigoplus_s s^{\oplus n_s}$, set
$\operatorname{Hom}(A,B)=\bigoplus_s\operatorname{Mat}_{n_s\times m_s}(\mathbb C)$;
composition is block-matrix multiplication, dagger is conjugate transpose,
and the norm is the maximum of the operator norms of its blocks. Thus direct
sums are orthogonal. Define tensor product on simple labels by the fusion rules
$\mathbf1\otimes a=a\otimes\mathbf1=a$ and $\tau\otimes\tau=\mathbf1\oplus\tau$,
and extend it distributively to direct sums. For each allowed simple fusion
channel $c\subset a\otimes b$, let $i_{ab}^c:c\to a\otimes b$ be the
standard unit-vector isometric inclusion. Since the multiplicity space is
$V_{ab}^c=\operatorname{Hom}(a\otimes b,c)$, its orthonormal basis vector is
the adjoint projection $(i_{ab}^c)^\dagger:a\otimes b\to c$; retain
$i_{ab}^c$ itself for the coevaluation construction below. On simple-label
blocks, tensor a pair
of morphism matrices by their Kronecker product on multiplicity coordinates
and by the identity on each simple fusion channel; extend over direct sums.
This defines a bifunctor compatible with dagger. Use the strict unit gauge:
every F or R coordinate involving $\mathbf1$ is the identity. Put

$$\phi=\frac{1+\sqrt5}{2},\qquad u=\phi^{-1},\qquad v=\phi^{-1/2}.$$

The only nontrivial associator blocks are

$$F^{\tau\tau\tau}_{\mathbf1}=[1],\qquad F^{\tau\tau\tau}_{\tau}=\begin{pmatrix}u&v\\v&-u\end{pmatrix},$$

in the intermediate-channel order $(\mathbf1,\tau)$. In the pullback R
convention of [[def-qc-tqc-r-symbol]], the exchange blocks are

$$R^{\tau\tau}_{\mathbf1}=x=e^{-4\pi i/5},\qquad R^{\tau\tau}_{\tau}=y=e^{3\pi i/5}.$$

Because these are pullback symbols, define the object-level braiding
$c_{\tau,\tau}$ on the $\mathbf1$ and $\tau$ summands by $x^{-1}$ and
$y^{-1}$, respectively. Every braiding with a unit input is the strict
identity. These fixed chiral data define the Fibonacci unitary braided fusion
category in this gauge; the verification checks every nontrivial pentagon and
hexagon sector.



FILE items/ex-qc-tqc-fibonacci-qubit-matrices.md
Example

For three $\tau$ anyons of total charge $\tau$, the fusion space is
two-dimensional. Fix the chiral convention
$$F=\begin{pmatrix}\phi^{-1}&\phi^{-1/2}\\ \phi^{-1/2}&-\phi^{-1}\end{pmatrix},\qquad r=R_\tau/R_1=e^{-3\pi i/5}.$$
After removing the common scalar $R_1$, the adjacent generators are
$$B_1=\operatorname{diag}(1,r),\qquad B_2=FB_1F.$$

The verification checks these matrices directly as a unitary $B_3$
representation. It uses the listed sector data as input and does not infer
global category coherence from this calculation.



[F1] The Fibonacci supplier states the fusion rule, the displayed $F$ block,
and the pullback phases $R_1=e^{-4\pi i/5}$ and
$R_\tau=e^{3\pi i/5}$, so $R_\tau/R_1=e^{-3\pi i/5}$.

FILE items/ex-qc-tqc-ising-data.md
Example

Use simple labels $\mathbf1,\psi,\sigma$ with unit fusion and
$\sigma\otimes\sigma=\mathbf1\oplus\psi$,
$\sigma\otimes\psi=\psi\otimes\sigma=\sigma$, and
$\psi\otimes\psi=\mathbf1$. In the three-$\sigma$, total-$\sigma$ fusion
space, the intermediate-channel basis is $(\mathbf1,\psi)$. In the pullback
R convention of [[def-qc-tqc-r-symbol]], fix

$$F^{\sigma\sigma\sigma}_{\sigma} =2^{-1/2}\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix},\qquad R^{\sigma\sigma}_{\mathbf1}=e^{-i\pi/8},\qquad R^{\sigma\sigma}_{\psi}=e^{3i\pi/8}.$$

These are sector data only. This item makes no claim about an all-label Ising
F/R table or global Ising ribbon coherence.

The phases above use the pullback convention in [[def-qc-tqc-r-symbol]]. In a
convention recording the eigenvalues of the object-level braiding itself, the
eigenvalues are the inverses of the displayed phases. This convention note
does not extend the sector data to any other Ising fusion space.



FILE items/ex-qc-tqc-ising-three-anyon-clifford.md
Example

In the three-$\sigma$, total-$\sigma$ Ising sector, use
$F=H=2^{-1/2}\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}$,
$R_1=e^{-i\pi/8}$, and $R_\psi=e^{3i\pi/8}$. After removing the common phase
$R_1$, the two projective braid generators are
$$B_1=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&i\end{pmatrix},\qquad B_2=H B_1 H.$$



FILE items/ex-qc-tqc-ising-vs-fibonacci.md
Example

In a Majorana–Ising realization satisfying
[[def-qc-tqc-ising-majorana-interface]], braid-only gates on a fixed parity
sector normalize the logical Pauli group and form a finite projective
Clifford subgroup. In the
three-$\tau$, total-$\tau$ Fibonacci sector, the two braid generators instead
generate a subgroup dense in $PU(2)$. The models therefore have different
projective gate power even though both use unitary braid matrices.



FILE items/ex-qc-tqc-small-model-matrices.md
Example

For the Fibonacci three-$\tau$, total-$\tau$ sector and the Ising
three-$\sigma$, total-$\sigma$ sector, use the F/R data of
[[ex-qc-tqc-fibonacci-data]] and [[ex-qc-tqc-ising-data]]. In each sector the
first generator is $B_1=\operatorname{diag}(R_1,R_2)$ and the second is
$B_2=FB_1F$. We verify both are unitary, satisfy the $B_3$ Artin relation,
and have projective orders $10$ (Fibonacci) and $4$ (Ising).



[F1] [[ex-qc-tqc-fibonacci-data]]: in the Fibonacci sector,
$F=\begin{pmatrix}u&v\\v&-u\end{pmatrix}$,
$u=\phi^{-1}$, $v=\phi^{-1/2}$, $R_1=e^{-4\pi i/5}$, and
$R_2=e^{3\pi i/5}$.

FILE items/lem-anyonic-fusion-path-basis.md
Statement

Let $\mathcal C$ be a complex fusion category, let $a,b,c$ be simple objects, and fix a basis $B_{x,a}^y$ of every finite-dimensional split space $W_{x,a}^y=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal C}(y,x\otimes a)$. For every admissible path $x_0=b,\ldots,x_n=c$ and every tuple of vertex indices $\mu_j\in B_{x_{j-1},a}^{x_j}$, the composite maps of [[def-anyonic-fusion-path-space]] form a basis of $H_{a,n;b,c}$. Consequently,

$$\dim H_{a,n;b,c}=\sum_{\substack{x_0=b,\ x_n=c\\x_1,\ldots,x_{n-1}\text{ simple}}}\prod_{j=1}^n N_{x_{j-1}a}^{x_j}.$$

The empty product is $1$ when $b\cong c$ and $0$ otherwise.



[F1] [[def-anyonic-fusion-path-space]]: $T_0=b$, $T_{j+1}=T_j\otimes a$, $H_{a,n;b,c}=\operatorname{Hom}(c,T_n)$, and a path composite is built from inclusions $i_j:x_j\to x_{j-1}\otimes a$.

FILE items/lem-anyonic-vacuum-f-symbol-frobenius-schur-normalization.md
Statement

Let $a$ and $(v,\operatorname{ev},\operatorname{coev},d_a,\kappa_a)$ be as in [[def-anyonic-frobenius-schur-sign]]. In the multiplicity-free vacuum-normalized gauge of [[def-qc-tqc-f-symbol]],

$$\left(F^{aaa}_a\right)_{\mathbf1,\mathbf1}=\frac{\kappa_a}{d_a},\qquad \delta_a:=\frac{1}{\left((F^{aaa}_a)^{-1}\right)_{\mathbf1,\mathbf1}}=\kappa_a d_a.$$
