FILE items/thm-qa-37-fusion-branch-validity.md
Statement

Let $(P_a)_{a\in\mathcal A}$ be the finite fusion-channel projectors of
[[def-qa-37-fusion-measurement]] on a finite-dimensional fusion space $H$,
and let $\psi\in H$ be a unit vector. The channel probabilities
$p_a=\|P_a\psi\|^2$ are nonnegative and sum to one. If $p_a>0$, the
conditional branch is the unit vector $P_a\psi/\sqrt{p_a}$. If $p_a=0$,
then $P_a\psi=0$ and no normalized conditional state is defined.



[F1] The channel family is a finite projective measurement: its projections
are pairwise orthogonal and sum to identity.

FILE items/thm-qa-37-jrs-leakage-bound.md
Statement

For a finite adiabatic braid input with rank-$m$ spectral band and schedule
$T>0$, define
$$A_m(s)=\frac1T\left[\frac{m\|H'(0)\|}{g(0)^2} +\frac{m\|H'(s)\|}{g(s)^2} +\int_0^s\left(\frac{m\|H''(r)\|}{g(r)^2} +\frac{7m\sqrt m\|H'(r)\|^2}{g(r)^3}\right)dr\right].$$
Then
$\|U_T(s)P(0)U_T(s)^*-P(s)\|\le A_m(s)$, and every unit initial band
vector has leakage probability at most $A_m(s)^2$. In particular,
$\|[I-P(1)]U_T(1)A_0\|_{\rm op}\le A_m(1)$ and leakage probability is at
most $A_m(1)^2$. This result does not prove the separate in-band transport
condition or identify band transport with Fibonacci $F/R$ data.



FILE items/thm-qa-37-su2-compiler-error-length.md
Statement

Let $G_{\rm Fib}$ be the fixed Fibonacci SU(2) alphabet with a supplied finite
base-net certificate of radius $0<\eta_0<64^{-2}$ and maximum word length
$L_0$. For every exact target $S\in SU(2)$ and rational
$0<\varepsilon\le\eta_0$, let
$$\eta_{j+1}=64\eta_j^{3/2},\qquad J=\min\{j\ge0:\eta_j\le\varepsilon\}.$$
The ideal SK recursion returns a normalized braid word $W$ with
$\|S-W\|_{\rm op}\le\varepsilon$ and length at most $L_0 5^J$; hence its
length is $O(\log^{\log_{3/2}5}(1/\varepsilon))=O(\log^{3.97}(1/\varepsilon))$.
If each real and imaginary gate coordinate is supplied by its minimal
polynomial and rational isolating interval with the endpoint/root conditions
of the published finite-precision theorem, the base certificate is fixed,
and $S$ has a polynomial-time Cauchy name with query cost at most $(b+1)^c$,
the finite implementation additionally has the bit-time bound
$O((N+s_\varepsilon)^{c+10})$ from the cited theorem, where
$N=\lceil\log_2(2/\varepsilon)\rceil$ and $s_\varepsilon$ is the binary
encoding length of the rational tolerance. The lifted physical braid carries
$(R_1z^{-1})^k$ and that phase is not part of the normalized error.



FILE items/thm-qc-tqc-approximate-encoded-two-qubit-gate.md
Statement

Assume $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$. Let $U\in U(\mathbb C^4)$ and $\delta>0$.
There is one normalized six-strand Fibonacci braid word $b$ such that its
actions $B_1(b)$ and $B_\tau(b)$ on the total-charge-$1$ and total-charge-$\tau$
blocks are simultaneously within $\delta$ of
$$T_1=U\oplus(\det U)^{-1}\in SU(5),\qquad T_\tau=U\oplus\operatorname{diag}((\det U)^{-1},1,1,1)\in SU(8).$$
For the triple-triple computational subspace in either block, the logical
block error and leakage amplitude are each at most $\delta$. The corresponding
physical categorical braid differs from the normalized word by the common
scalar phase specified by
[[lem-qc-tqc-determinant-character-phase]].



FILE items/thm-qc-tqc-arbitrary-n-register-universality.md
Statement

Assume $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$. For every $n\ge1$, every total charge $c$ with
$V(\tau^n\to c)\ne0$, every unitary $U$ on $n$ logical qubits, and every
$\epsilon>0$, the triple-$\tau$ code
$$(\mathbb C^2)^{\otimes n}\otimes V(\tau^n\to c)$$
admits a finite normalized braid word $V_b$ such that
$$\inf_{|z|=1}\|P_{\rm code}V_bP_{\rm code} -zJ(U\otimes I_S)J^*\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le\epsilon,\qquad \|(I-P_{\rm code})V_bP_{\rm code}\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le\epsilon.$$
Here $S=V(\tau^n\to c)$ is the spectator space, $J:(\mathbb C^2)^{\otimes n}\otimes S\to V(\tau^{3n}\to c)$ is the isometric triple-code inclusion, and the logical target is lifted as $U\otimes I_S$. The bounds are uniform over spectator fusion states. No efficient braid
word-length, search-time, or finite-precision bound is asserted.



[F6] The Fibonacci data and fusion-tree count give
$\dim V(\tau^3\to1)=1$ and $\dim V(\tau^3\to\tau)=2$. In an ordered
fusion-tree decomposition, a braid supported on a contiguous local block
acts on that block's fixed-charge factor and as the identity on the prefix,
suffix, and their fusion multiplicities; its fixed-charge action is unitary.
Dividing the physical action by its common $R_1$ phase retains these
properties; write $\widetilde\rho$ for the resulting normalized action.

FILE items/thm-qc-tqc-block-approximation-error.md
Statement

Let $V=P V\oplus(I-P)V$ be an orthogonal decomposition of a finite-dimensional
Hilbert space, let $U$ and $W$ be unitary operators on the two summands, and
let $T:=U\oplus W$. If an ambient unitary $G$ satisfies
$\|G-T\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le\delta$, then the logical block error of $G$ relative
to $U$ and its leakage amplitude both are at most $\delta$.
