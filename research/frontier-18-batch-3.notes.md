### 2a. Long proofs decomposed into intermediate lemmas

* **`thm-extensions-of-finite-fields-are-galois-with-cyclic-frobenius-group`.**
  The source proof (Milne 4.20, Conrad `finitefields` Thm 4.1/5.6) runs
  separability, normality, the fixed field and the order of the Frobenius
  together in one paragraph. Split into
  `def-relative-frobenius-of-a-finite-field-extension`,
  `lem-fixed-field-of-the-relative-frobenius` and
  `lem-order-of-the-relative-frobenius`, so the two facts that actually carry the
  argument — the fixed field is the base field, and the Frobenius has order
  exactly the degree — are separately verifiable, and so that both are reusable
  in `thm-the-cyclotomic-galois-group-over-a-finite-field` and
  `thm-conjugates-over-a-finite-field-are-the-frobenius-powers`, which need them
  independently of the Galois conclusion.

* **The normal basis theorem** is decomposed into five items rather than one:
  `lem-a-nonzero-polynomial-in-several-variables-does-not-vanish-on-an-infinite-subring`
  (Clark Lemma 8.21, Milne Lemma 5.19),
  `lem-a-family-is-a-basis-exactly-when-its-conjugate-matrix-is-invertible`
  (Clark Lemma 8.23, Milne Cor 5.16),
  `lem-the-galois-automorphisms-are-algebraically-independent-over-an-infinite-base-field`
  (Clark Thm 8.24), then the two cases
  `thm-normal-basis-theorem-over-an-infinite-base-field` and
  `thm-normal-basis-theorem-for-a-cyclic-extension`, and only then the combined
  `thm-normal-basis-theorem`. **The two cases have genuinely different proofs**
  — a determinant nonvanishing argument and a cyclic-vector argument — which is
  why they are separate items and not one proof with a case split; the sources
  agree on this (Conrad `linearchar` Thm 3.6/3.7, Clark Prop 8.22/Thm 8.25,
  Milne's two proofs). The combining theorem is short because the split it makes
  — infinite base field, or finite base field and hence cyclic — is exactly what
  the decomposition buys.

* **`thm-cyclotomic-polynomials-are-irreducible-over-the-rationals`.** The
  Dedekind argument is one long proof in every source. The mod-$p$ step is
  extracted as
  `lem-a-primitive-root-of-unity-and-its-prime-power-share-a-minimal-polynomial-over-the-rationals`,
  so the theorem itself is the short iteration over the prime factorisation of a
  unit modulo $n$. The separability input it needs is
  `thm-separability-of-x-n-minus-one-and-the-order-of-the-group-of-roots-of-unity`,
  a separate item rather than an inline remark, because three other items cite it.

* **`def-cyclotomic-polynomial`** carries its well-definedness as a numbered
  item, `thm-cyclotomic-polynomials-are-monic-integer-polynomials-of-degree-euler-totient`,
  linked by `justified_by`. That is the design's obligation #G2 and it is not a
  parenthesis: the division is by a monic polynomial **over $\mathbb Z$**, not
  over a field, so it needs the published `thm-monic-polynomial-division` and not
  the division algorithm.

* **`thm-intersections-of-rational-cyclotomic-fields`** is supported by two
  extracted lemmas rather than an inline computation:
  `lem-degree-of-a-compositum-with-a-galois-extension` (the Galois degree
  formula, needed twice) and
  `lem-the-totient-of-a-gcd-and-of-a-least-common-multiple` (the arithmetic step,
  which is pure number theory and does not belong inside a field-theory proof).

* **`thm-every-finite-abelian-group-is-a-galois-group-over-the-rationals`** is
  supported by `lem-a-finite-abelian-group-is-a-quotient-of-a-power-of-a-cyclic-group`
  (Clark Lemma 9.11) so the group-theoretic reduction is verifiable on its own.

* Two group-theoretic prerequisites are built rather than assumed:
  `lem-subgroup-lattice-of-a-finite-cyclic-group` and
  `lem-a-finite-cyclic-group-has-euler-totient-many-generators`. Neither exists
  in the published corpus (§9 records the greps). Both are used at least three
  times each on this page, and the first is what turns the Galois correspondence
  into the subfield lattice.

### 6c. External-dependency record for `rem-kronecker-weber`

Required by SCHEMA §3 for a future `proved_here: false` item.

* `source_url`: `https://kconrad.math.uconn.edu/blurbs/galoistheory/cyclotomic.pdf`
* `exact_statement`: "every finite abelian extension of $\mathbb{Q}$ lies inside

`external_refs` is **not** used: nothing on this page depends on
