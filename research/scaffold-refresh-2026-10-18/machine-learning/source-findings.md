Conformal Risk Control (arXiv:2208.02814v4, June 2025): all 19 extracted pages were read. The main exchangeable monotone upper bound has a clean symmetric-threshold proof. ML-90 supplies a finite-grid variant whose operational/measurability and fallback conditions are explicit. The printed Theorem 2 lower bound does not follow from its stated assumptions: take every random function identically zero, B=1, alpha=1/2 and n>3. These functions are iid, nonnegative, nonincreasing, right-continuous, have no jumps and obey the maximal-endpoint condition, yet expected selected loss is zero, below alpha−2/(n+1). A lower tightness claim needs an additional endpoint/crossing hypothesis and separate proof. No such theorem is imported. A preliminary suspicion about Proposition 6 was withdrawn: under the actual nonincreasing/right-continuous hypothesis, the supremum preserves the monotone right limit. There is no Proposition 6 defect finding and no counterexample to its monotone use in this refresh. Arbitrary-index measurability still requires a premise. The paper's empirical plots were read as extracted descriptions, not visually audited or reproduced.

DPO: selected main derivation, full Appendix A mathematical derivations and implementation sections were read, not the whole 27-page paper. The finite Gibbs optimum follows from its exact KL identity; continuous/countably infinite completions need partition-function and integrability assumptions. The Appendix A.4 preferred/dispreferred orientation is inconsistent in places with the main objective; ML-88 differentiates the preferred-minus-dispreferred log-sigmoid directly, giving −beta sigmoid(−u) times the score difference. No claim of empirical preference MLE consistency or general policy-class optimality is imported from an informal “mild assumptions” phrase.

Masked Diffusion Language Models: selected finite discrete posterior, SUBS, sampling and finite-objective algebra sections were read; the full 49-page paper and CTMC/continuous-limit proofs were not. The local design avoids assuming independent reverse tokens represent a true correlated reverse joint. Finite chain ELBO terms, endpoint reconstruction/prior terms, positive-support conditions and the distinction between model error and time discretization are explicit. “Rao-Blackwellization” is the source's loose parameterization terminology and supplies no generic conditional-expectation variance theorem.

FlashAttention: selected exact-algorithm/background and proof sections read; one larger output was truncated, so pages 5–6 are not credited as complete reading. The block-summary invariant and finite arithmetic/workspace bounds in ML-86 are independently stated and justified. The source IO model and its range/algorithm are not universal latency or fixed-memory optimality claims. LoRA: only pp. 1,3–5 read; algebraic factor rank/merging and chain-rule gradients are distinguished from measured memory/speed and the hypothesis that adaptation updates have low intrinsic rank.

The 86-page revision was not read and supplies no claim. GRPO, rejection filtering, distillation and reasoning benchmarks are procedure descriptions/empirical evidence.
