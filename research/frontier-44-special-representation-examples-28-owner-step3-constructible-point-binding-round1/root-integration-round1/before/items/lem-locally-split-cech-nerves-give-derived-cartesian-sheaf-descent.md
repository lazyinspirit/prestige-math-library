## Statement

Assume AC. For a strict augmented simplicial space $Z_\bullet\to S$, its category of simplicial analytic $k$-sheaves has explicit coinduced injective resolutions. The derived augmentation is computed by the normalized double complex with first page $R^j q_{p*}F_p$ and alternating face differential. A common internal lower bound makes every total-degree diagonal finite. The same construction works for strict bisimplicial diagrams and is compatible with composition of derived right adjoints to exact inverse image. If $Z_\bullet$ is the Čech nerve of a map $P\to S$ admitting local sections, inverse image gives an equivalence from $D^b(\operatorname{Sh}_k(S))$ to the full derived subcategory with Cartesian cohomology. For algebraic principal $\mathrm{GL}$ bundles this restricts to the algebraically constructible subcategories.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A strict augmented simplicial space $Z_\bullet\to S$: spaces $Z_p$ with continuous maps $Z_\alpha:Z_m\to Z_p$ for every monotone $\alpha:[p]\to[m]$, and an augmentation to the constant simplicial space $S$ with components $q_p:Z_p\to S$ satisfying $q_p\circ Z_\alpha=q_m$; the field $k$; the category of simplicial analytic $k$-sheaves as defined in step 1.1; the Axiom of Choice [[def-axiom-of-choice]]; the bounded derived category and derived Hom conventions of [[def-derived-category-of-an-abelian-category]], [[def-sheaf-cohomology-derived-global-sections]] and [[def-direct-sum-total-complex-of-a-double-complex]]. All sheaf categories are over the constant ringed space with structure sheaf $k$.

[F1] Sheaf categories and exactness ([[def-sheaf-on-topological-space]], [[def-presheaf-of-groups-rings-modules]], [[def-module-on-ringed-space]], [[thm-abelian-sheaves-form-abelian-category]], [[thm-exactness-of-sheaves-stalkwise]]): $\operatorname{Sh}_k(Z_p)$ is the abelian category of modules over $(Z_p,k)$ with $k$-linear morphisms; a sequence is exact exactly when it is exact on every stalk; kernels, cokernels and finite sums are formed sectionwise with sheafification for cokernels, and arbitrary products and coproducts of sheaves exist and are computed levelwise.

[F2] Inverse and direct image ([[def-inverse-image-presheaf-and-sheaf]], [[def-direct-image-sheaf]], [[lem-direct-image-is-sheaf]], [[thm-inverse-direct-image-adjunction]]): for continuous $f$, $f^{-1}$ is left adjoint to $f_*$, $f^{-1}$ is exact by stalkwise exactness, and $f_*$ preserves injectives because its left adjoint is exact; $f_*$ is precomposition with $f^{-1}$ on opens and sends sheaves to sheaves.

[F3] Injective models ([[lem-field-module-sheaves-have-injective-derived-models]], [[thm-injective-complexes-model-the-bounded-below-derived-category]], [[thm-a-bounded-below-complex-of-injectives-is-homotopically-injective]], [[def-homotopically-injective-bounded-below-complex]], [[prop-morphisms-into-a-homotopically-injective-complex-need-no-roof]]): under AC every $\operatorname{Sh}_k(Z_p)$ has a functorial injective embedding and functorial injective resolutions, bounded-below injective complexes are K-injective, and morphisms into such a complex are chain maps without roofs.

[F4] Products of injectives ([[thm-a-coproduct-of-projectives-is-projective-and-a-product-of-injectives-is-injective]]): in an abelian category with AB3 and AB3*, finite products of injectives are injective, and arbitrary small products of injectives are injective under AC.

[F5] Double complexes and totalization ([[def-cochain-complex-in-an-abelian-category]], [[def-direct-sum-total-complex-of-a-double-complex]], [[lem-the-total-differential-squares-to-zero]], [[thm-the-cohomological-filtered-complex-construction]]): a double complex with commuting square-zero differentials has a total complex with square-zero differential; a decreasing filtration of a complex with finite filtrations of each degree gives a convergent spectral sequence abutting to the cohomology; finitely many nonzero terms on each diagonal make the totalization a finite construction in each degree.

[F6] Derived conventions ([[def-canonical-truncation-of-a-complex]], [[thm-canonical-truncations-fit-a-distinguished-triangle]], [[thm-the-derived-category-inherits-a-triangulated-structure]], [[prop-bounded-derived-localizations-embed-fully-faithfully]]): canonical truncation triangles build a bounded complex from its cohomology sheaves; the derived category is triangulated with long exact cohomology sequences; the bounded derived category embeds fully faithfully in the unbounded one.

[F7] Choice ([[def-axiom-of-choice]], [[thm-choice-implies-dependent-implies-countable-choice]]): AC supplies dependent choice, hence the successive homotopy and lifting choices in the injective-model constructions.

[F8] Algebraic constructibility ([[def-algebraically-constructible-complex-of-analytic-field-sheaves]]): bounded algebraically constructible complexes are those with bounded cohomology locally constant of finite rank on the strata of a finite smooth algebraic stratification with the frontier condition.

[F9] Extension by zero ([[thm-extension-by-zero-adjunction-exactness]]): for an open subspace the functor $j_!$ is exact and left adjoint to restriction; this is the declared ambient exactness input of the design and is available if a local open-subspace model is written out.

## Proof

1.1 **The simplicial sheaf category.** A simplicial analytic $k$-sheaf on $Z_\bullet$ is a family $F=(F_p)_{p\ge0}$ with $F_p\in\operatorname{Sh}_k(Z_p)$ and structure maps $\alpha^*F_m:=Z_\alpha^{-1}F_m\to F_p$ for every monotone $\alpha:[p]\to[m]$, satisfying the identity and composition identities for composable simplicial operators; a morphism is a levelwise family of $k$-linear maps commuting with these structure maps. The category $\operatorname{Sh}_k(Z_\bullet)$ is abelian: kernels and cokernels are formed levelwise (cokernels after sheafification of the presheaf cokernel), and exactness is checked levelwise by [F1]; arbitrary products and coproducts are computed levelwise, so AB3 and AB3* hold; the category is locally small because each $\operatorname{Sh}_k(Z_p)$ is. [given, F1]

1.2 **Coinduction and explicit injective resolutions.** For $p\ge0$ and $I\in\operatorname{Sh}_k(Z_p)$ define the coinduced object $N_pI$ by $(N_pI)_m:=\prod_{\alpha:[m]\to[p]}(Z_\alpha)_*I$, the product over all monotone maps, with structure maps induced by composition of simplicial operators; then $\operatorname{Hom}(F,N_pI)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_{Z_p}(F_p,I)$ naturally, because the component at $\alpha=\mathrm{id}_{[p]}$ is a bijection onto the data and every other component is determined from it by the structure maps of $F$. Hence $N_p$ is right adjoint to evaluation at $p$, which is exact, so $N_pI$ is injective for injective $I$; explicitly, $\operatorname{Hom}(-,N_pI)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_{Z_p}((-)_p,I)$ is exact. Given $F$, choose functorial injective embeddings $F_p\rightarrowtail I_p$ by [F3]; the components give a morphism $F\to\prod_pN_pI_p$ that is a monomorphism because at level $m$ its component $p=m$, $\alpha=\mathrm{id}$ is the chosen embedding, and the product of injectives is injective by [F4] under AC. Iterating this embedding on cokernels and totalizing along the bounded-below index produces for every bounded-below complex of simplicial sheaves one explicit coinduced injective resolution. [given, F1, F2, F3, F4, F7]

2.1 **Acyclicity of coinduced injectives.** Let $J=N_pI$. For every $m$ the component at a fixed vertex $\alpha_0:Z_m\to Z_p$ of $q_{m*}J_m=\prod_{\alpha:[m]\to[p]}q_{p*}I$ is $q_{p*}I$, because $q_m\circ Z_\alpha=q_p$; thus $q_{m*}J_m$ is a product of copies of $q_{p*}I$ indexed by the monotone maps $[m]\to[p]$, that is, by the $m$-simplices of the standard $p$-simplex. On the normalized complex whose degree-$m$ term is this product and whose differential is the alternating sum of the maps that delete one vertex (the maps induced by the simplicial structure of $J$ at the face operators, which lower the number of vertices by one), inserting a fixed vertex $0$ at the start of every tuple defines a contraction: the internal deletions cancel pairwise and the top deletion composes with the insertion to the identity, so $dh+hd=\mathrm{id}$ on the normalized complex, the augmented term being $q_{p*}I$, identified with the value at the identity tuple. A general injective $J$ is a retract of a product of coinduced injectives by the embedding of step 1.2, and a retract of a contractible complex is contractible, so for every injective $J$ the normalized augmented complex is contractible: $R^jq_*J=0$ for $j>0$ and $q_*J$ is its degree-$0$ part, the equalizer of $q_{0*}J_0\rightrightarrows q_{1*}J_1$. The normalized complex computes the right derived functors of the limit functor because normalization changes no cohomology, and it is contractible, so injective simplicial sheaves are acyclic for $q_*$. [given, F1, F5, step 1.2]

3.1 **The derived augmentation model.** Let $F$ be a bounded-below complex of simplicial sheaves with $F^n=0$ for $n<a$, and let $F\to J^\bullet$ be the explicit coinduced injective resolution of step 1.2, chosen with $J^q=0$ for $q<a$. Form the double complex $D^{p,q}:=q_{p*}J^q_p$ for $p,q\ge0$, with $q$-differential the resolution differential and $p$-differential the alternating sum of the vertex-deletion maps of step 2.1 applied to $J^q$ (these are the maps $D^{p,q}\to D^{p-1,q}$ induced by the structure maps of $J^q$ at the face operators, and their squares vanish by the simplicial identities and [F5]). Since $J^q_p$ is injective in $\operatorname{Sh}_k(Z_p)$ by step 2.1, the horizontal cohomology of the double complex is $H^q(D^{p,\bullet})\cong R^qq_{p*}(F_p)$ by [F3]; since $J^q$ is injective in the simplicial category, each column is contractible by step 2.1, so the total complex is quasi-isomorphic to the augmented complex $q_*J^\bullet$, that is, to $Rq_*F$, and the spectral sequence of the filtration by $q$, whose convergence is supplied by [F5], has first page $R^qq_{p*}F_p$ with the alternating face differential. A common internal lower bound makes every diagonal finite: $J^q=0$ for $q<a$, so in total degree $n$ only $0\le p\le n-a$ occurs, each diagonal is a finite product of finite-dimensional complexes, and no completion hypothesis is needed. The bisimplicial case is the same argument with two simplicial indices: coinduction is indexed by pairs of operators, the two vertex insertions give the two contractions, finite diagonals follow from the common bound, and iterated and simultaneous totalizations agree on finite diagonals by [F5]. For a composition of augmentations $Z_\bullet\to Y_\bullet\to S$ with exact inverse images, resolving first along $Z_\bullet\to Y_\bullet$ and then along $Y_\bullet\to S$ and totalizing gives a double complex whose two spectral sequences are the two compositions; the comparison maps of the adjunctions compose by naturality of units and counits, and both totalizations have the same finite diagonals, so $R(gf)_*\cong Rg_*\circ Rf_*$ with the models compatible. [given, F3, F5, step 1.2, step 2.1]

3.2 **Locally split Čech nerves: the local contraction.** Let $q:P\to S$ admit local sections and let $Z_\bullet$ be its Čech nerve, $Z_p$ the $(p+1)$-fold fibre product of $P$ over $S$ with $q_p$ the projection. A section $s:V\to P$ over an open $V\subseteq S$ gives, for every $p$, the map $e_p:Z_p|_V\to Z_{p+1}|_V$, $(z_0,\dots,z_p)\mapsto(s(q(z_0)),z_0,\dots,z_p)$, which satisfies $d^0e_p=\mathrm{id}$, $d^{i+1}e_p=e_{p-1}d^i$, $\sigma^0e_p=e_{p+1}\sigma^0$ and $\sigma^{i+1}e_p=e_{p-1}\sigma^i$; so $Z_\bullet|_V\to V$ is a split augmented simplicial space. Fix $B\in D^b(\operatorname{Sh}_k(S))$ and put $F:=q_\bullet^*B$, so that $F_p=q_p^{-1}B$ and every map between the $Z_p$ that is compatible with the projections to $S$ induces a canonical isomorphism between the corresponding pullbacks of $B$, in particular the maps $e_p$ do. Therefore over $V$ the normalized augmented complex of step 2.1 receives contraction maps built from the $e_p$, and the vertex-insertion identity of step 2.1 applies verbatim: the complex is contractible, its degree-$0$ part is $B|_V$, and $R^jq_*(q_\bullet^*B)|_V=0$ for $j>0$. As the section domains $V$ cover $S$, this proves $Rq_*(q_\bullet^*B)\cong B$ and vanishing of the higher derived functors, with the unit of the adjunction $q_\bullet^*\dashv Rq_*$ an isomorphism. [given, F6, step 2.1]

4.1 **Full faithfulness and the essential image.** The derived adjunction between $q_\bullet^*$ and $Rq_*$ gives for bounded complexes $A,B$ on $S$ that $\operatorname{Hom}_{D}(q_\bullet^*A,q_\bullet^*B)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_{D}(A,Rq_*q_\bullet^*B)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_{D}(A,B)$, the second isomorphism by step 3.2 applied to $B$ and naturality of the comparison; by [F6] the same holds in the bounded derived categories, so $q_\bullet^*$ is fully faithful. Its essential image is the full derived subcategory of objects with Cartesian cohomology. Every $q_\bullet^*B$ with $B$ bounded has Cartesian cohomology: the structure maps of $q_\bullet^*B$ are the canonical isomorphisms $q_p^{-1}B\to Z_\alpha^{-1}q_m^{-1}B$ and the same holds after taking cohomology, shifts and cones. Conversely let $F$ be a bounded complex of simplicial sheaves whose cohomology sheaves are Cartesian. Ordinary descent: on a local section domain $V$ the Cartesian structure isomorphisms identify each $F_p|_V$ with the pullback of $F_0|_V$ along any chosen vertex projection, and the simplicial identity for the two composites over overlaps is exactly the cocycle condition, so the local sheaves $F_0|_V$ and their gluing isomorphisms descend to a sheaf $B^j$ on $S$ with $q_\bullet^*B^j\cong H^j(F)$ for each $j$; for the complex, the canonical truncation triangles of [F6] build $F$ from the shifted $H^j(F)$, and full faithfulness lifts each connecting morphism from the descended truncations; induction on the number of nonzero cohomology degrees then realizes $F$ as $q_\bullet^*$ of a bounded complex on $S$. [given, F6, step 3.2]

5.1 **Algebraic principal bundles.** When $P\to S$ is an algebraic principal $\mathrm{GL}$-bundle, the fibre products and the structure maps of the Čech nerve are algebraic, the local sections may be taken over an algebraic trivializing cover, and the descent isomorphisms of step 4.1 are algebraic transition functions. Algebraic constructibility of a bounded complex is checked after pullback to a finite algebraic cover by [F8], and the descended cohomology sheaves are glued from the finitely many local models along those transition functions, so they are locally constant of finite rank on the strata of a finite smooth algebraic stratification refining the trivializing cover; hence both directions of the equivalence restrict to the algebraically constructible subcategories. No claim is made about a naive equivariant category for a nonfree action, and no proper-descent statement is used. [given, F8, step 4.1]

6.1 **Conclusion and caveats.** Step 1.2 gives the explicit coinduced injective resolutions; step 3.1 gives the derived augmentation model with first page $R^jq_{p*}F_p$, finite diagonals, the bisimplicial case and composition compatibility; steps 3.2, 4.1 and 5.1 give the locally split Čech descent equivalence with Cartesian cohomology and its restriction to the algebraically constructible subcategories. AC enters only through the injective replacements and the products of injectives of [F3] and [F4]; the construction is otherwise formal and uses no proper-hypercover theorem. The declared extension-by-zero input [F9] is retained as ambient data; it is not visibly loaded by the present route, and no statement here depends on it beyond its availability for an explicit open-subspace model. [given, F3, F4, F9, step 1.2, step 3.1, step 3.2, step 4.1, step 5.1] ∎
