## Statement

Assume AC and the actual finite conical/triangulation and derived-incidence-model suppliers listed below. On the analytic space $X$ of a separated finite-type complex algebraic variety, constructible complexes admit the fixed-space finite models required by those suppliers. Then $\omega_X=a_X^!k$ and $D_X=R\mathcal Hom(-,\omega_X)$ preserve bounded constructible objects and the natural evaluation $A\to D_XD_XA$ is an isomorphism. On a smooth complex $d$-variety, the complex orientation normalizes $\omega_X\simeq k_X[2d]$. For constructible maps for which the geometric supplier proves the stated bounded outputs, $Rf_*D_X\simeq D_YRf_!$, $D_YRf_*\simeq Rf_!D_X$, and $f^!\simeq D_Xf^*D_Y$. Proper base change has the coherent extraordinary dual exchange $u^!Rf_*\simeq Rf'_*v^!$. The fixed-space triangulation is not an assumed globally simplicial model of $f$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC; a separated finite-type complex algebraic variety $X$ with its analytic topology; its terminal map $a_X:X\to\operatorname{pt}$; a field $k$; the fixed finite conical/triangulation and derived-incidence models supplied below; and the operations and exceptional adjoint of [[def-derived-operations-on-analytic-sheaves-over-locally-compact-spaces]] and [[thm-compactly-supported-derived-pushforward-has-exceptional-inverse-image-adjoint]].

[F1] $\omega_X=a_X^!k$ and $D_X=R\mathcal Hom(-,\omega_X)$, with the exceptional adjoint $Rf_!\dashv f^!$ representing $\operatorname{Hom}(Rf_!A,B)\simeq\operatorname{Hom}(A,f^!B)$ for bounded $A$ and bounded-below $B$ ([[def-derived-operations-on-analytic-sheaves-over-locally-compact-spaces]], [[thm-compactly-supported-derived-pushforward-has-exceptional-inverse-image-adjoint]]).

[F2] The support-and-trace calculus: closed-support localization, tensor–Hom transposition, $R\mathcal Hom(i_*k_Z,A)\cong R\Gamma_ZA$, the projection formula and canonical composition of proper-support images ([[lem-analytic-six-operation-support-and-trace-calculus]], [[lem-bounded-analytic-field-sheaves-have-derived-tensor-hom-and-pullback-pushforward-adjunctions]], [[def-sheaf-hom]]).

[F3] $D_c^b(X;k)$ is the full subcategory of $D^b(\operatorname{Sh}_k(X))$ of cohomologically bounded complexes whose cohomology is locally constant of finite rank along a finite smooth algebraic stratification ([[def-algebraically-constructible-complex-of-analytic-field-sheaves]], [[def-locally-constant-sheaf-of-finite-rank]]).

[F4] The finite models: on any separated finite-type complex variety, bounded algebraically constructible objects have bounded finite ordinary and compact-support cohomology, ordinary derived Hom and bounded constructible internal Hom, compatible with coefficient extension, by the actual conical/incidence models locally and finite affine-open descent globally ([[lem-complex-algebraic-stratifications-have-finite-conical-cochain-models]]); and the derived-incidence model identifies the bounded derived category of finite face-diagrams with the full all-sheaf-derived constructible subcategory under the stated finite triangulation hypothesis, with injective cogenerators the sheaves supported on closed cells ([[lem-finite-simplicial-constructible-sheaves-have-a-derived-incidence-model]]).

[F5] Extension by zero and its adjunction are exact ([[thm-extension-by-zero-adjunction-exactness]], [[def-extension-by-zero-abelian-sheaf]]), inverse and direct image have the stated stalk behaviour ([[def-inverse-image-presheaf-and-sheaf]], [[def-direct-image-sheaf]], [[def-sheaf-on-topological-space]]), supports of sections are closed in their domain ([[def-support-of-a-sheaf-section]]), the derived Hom conventions are those of [[def-derived-hom-in-the-bounded-setting]] and [[def-derived-category-of-an-abelian-category]], compact-support sections and the published cohomology conventions are those of [[def-compactly-supported-global-sections]] and [[def-sheaf-cohomology-derived-global-sections]], and AC is assumed ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 The dualizing object and duality transform. The operations supply $\omega_X=a_X^!k$ and $D_XA=R\mathcal Hom(A,\omega_X)$ [F1]. Dualizing transitivity is $f^!\omega_Y\simeq\omega_X$, and it is established directly by local section complexes: for open $U\subseteq X$, $\Gamma(U,f^!\omega_Y)=R\mathcal Hom_Y(Rf_!E_U,\omega_Y)\simeq R\mathcal Hom_k(R\Gamma_c(Y,Rf_!E_U),k)\simeq R\mathcal Hom_k(R\Gamma_c(U,k),k)\simeq\Gamma(U,\omega_X)$, where $E_U=j_{U!}k_U$ and the middle identifications use the constructed terminal-map Hom formula, the compact-support composition and the adjunction of [F1]; these are natural chain comparisons under restriction, so the comparison is an isomorphism on cohomology stalks and $f^!\omega_Y\simeq\omega_X$, without inferring an unrestricted adjunction from bounded tests. The local formula $R\Gamma(U,D_XA)\simeq R\mathcal Hom_k(R\Gamma_c(U,A),k)$ follows by applying the same computation to the open $U\to X$ and the terminal map: over a field the injective coefficient is $k$ itself, so this is the signed dual cochain model with no integral $\operatorname{Ext}$ correction. [given, F1, F2, F5]

1.2 Orientation. On an oriented real $n$-manifold and a small contractible ball $U$, the compact-support cochains of $k$ have a single cohomology class in degree $n$, normalized by the positive orientation generator: on a closed simplex this is the finite relative cellular complex $(\text{simplex},\text{boundary})$, whose quotient is generated by the oriented top cell, and excision identifies a ball with this model. Local models imply $\omega_X\simeq\mathrm{or}_X[n]$, and on a smooth complex $d$-variety the complex orientation trivializes $\mathrm{or}_X$, yielding $\omega_X\simeq k_X[2d]$. This fixes the positive complex generator used by the oriented diagonal Thom classes. [given, F1, F2]

1.3 Local systems on smooth strata. For a finite-rank local system $L$ on a smooth stratum the same local model gives $D_XL\simeq L^\vee[n]$ with $n$ the real dimension, and finite-dimensional vector-space evaluation gives $L\stackrel{\sim}{\to}D_XD_XL$; truncation extends this to bounded locally constant complexes. An induction on strata must not silently assume that $DD(j_!L)=j_!DD(L)$, since that involves boundary links; the fixed-space cellular construction below supplies that step. [given, F1, F4]

1.4 The incidence model locally. Cover the separated finite-type variety $X$ by finitely many affine opens. On each such open $V$, which is quasi-projective, [F4] supplies a finite regular triangulation with a deleted subcomplex, subordinate to $A|_V$. This is a model of the fixed space $V$, without a globally simplicial presentation of any algebraic map. On $V$ the derived-incidence supplier identifies bounded finite face-diagrams with the actual all-sheaf-derived constructible subcategory. Its injective cogenerators $I_\sigma$ are the constant sheaves on the closed cell intersected with $V$, pushed forward along that relatively closed subset, and every finite diagram has a finite injective bar resolution by finite sums of them. Restriction of the support-defined duality to $V$ is $D_V$, by step 1.1 and the exact open-inclusion transitivity, so these local resolutions suffice to check the global evaluation map. No single finite deleted-boundary model of an arbitrary separated variety is required. [given, F4, F3, step 1.1]

2.1 Duals of the cogenerators. The compact-support local dual formula of step 1.1 computes $D_XI_\sigma$ directly: at an interior point of $\sigma$ a small neighbourhood in $\overline\sigma$ is an oriented real $\dim\sigma$-ball, whose compact-support cochains have the single positive generator in that degree; at a proper face $\tau<\sigma$ a small neighbourhood in $\overline\sigma$ is a product $[0,\infty)^c\times\mathbb R^r$ with $c>0$, whose compact-support cochain complex is acyclic because one half-line has the relative finite cellular model $([0,1],\{1\})$ with boundary an isomorphism and tensoring that contraction with the other factors stays a contraction; at a point outside the closure the complex is zero. The positive orientation restricts to the interior coefficient, so $D_XI_\sigma=j_{\operatorname{int}\sigma!}k[\dim\sigma]$. Conversely, compact-support cochains of $j_{\operatorname{int}\sigma!}k$ on a small star of a remaining face $\tau\le\sigma$ are the cochains of an open sector $(0,\infty)^c\times\mathbb R^r\simeq\mathbb R^{\dim\sigma}$, with exactly the positive oriented generator in degree $\dim\sigma$, and restrictions between face-stars preserve it; hence $D_X(j_{\operatorname{int}\sigma!}k[\dim\sigma])=I_\sigma$. The evaluation $I_\sigma\to D_XD_XI_\sigma$ is ordinary finite-dimensional double-dual evaluation at the interior and has the same value at every face by these incidence identifications, so it is an isomorphism. These computations use finite relative cellular complexes and the actual sheaf/cochain comparison, not a guessed stalkwise duality. [given, F1, F4, step 1.2]

3.1 Biduality. On each affine open $V$ of step 1.4, $D_V$ is contravariant exact and its double is covariant exact, with evaluation a natural transformation of exact functors. Apply evaluation to the finite local injective incidence resolution of $A|_V$: it is an isomorphism on every finite sum of cogenerators by step 2.1, and induction through the finitely many resolution cones proves $A|_V\to D_VD_V(A|_V)$ is an isomorphism. These are restrictions of the support-defined global evaluation, so its cone restricts to zero on the affine cover and is zero stalkwise; hence $A\to D_XD_XA$ is an isomorphism. The same local calculations show bounded finite-rank dual cohomology; the finitely many affine opens give a uniform amplitude bound, and [F4]'s complex-stratum local models give algebraic constructibility and coefficient-extension compatibility. Refinement changes neither the support-defined duality nor evaluation, so the restriction and refinement comparisons have their identity and cocycle laws. [given, F4, step 1.4, step 2.1]

4.1 The duality exchange and the extraordinary proper exchange. After biduality, the internally localized exceptional adjunction with $B=\omega_Y$ gives $Rf_*D_XA\simeq D_YRf_!A$: both sides represent the sheaf of derived morphisms from $Rf_!A$ to $\omega_Y$, and the source-side object is $R\mathcal Hom(A,f^!\omega_Y)=D_XA$ by step 1.1. Applying this identity to $D_XA$ and applying $D_Y$ gives $D_YRf_*A\simeq Rf_!D_XA$ by the double-dual isomorphism of step 3.1. Next $\operatorname{Hom}(A,D_Xf^*D_YB)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(f^*D_YB,D_XA)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(D_YB,Rf_*D_XA)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(D_YRf_*D_XA,B)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(Rf_!A,B)$; these transpositions give a natural comparison $f^!B\to D_Xf^*D_YB$, and applying its internally localized chain formula to the generators $E_U=j_{U!}k_U$ and their shifts shows that the two derived section complexes agree, so the comparison is an isomorphism on cohomology stalks. The right side is bounded constructible by the fixed-space duality and the geometric inverse-image models, proving that $f^!$ preserves bounded constructibility; the represented Hom formula is now a genuine adjunction on that category, and inverse transposition proves its triangle identities and fixes all comparison maps. This avoids falsely equating compact-support cohomology downstairs with compact-support cohomology upstairs for a nonproper map. Applying these actual dualities to the ordinary proper base change of the support-and-trace calculus gives the coherent extraordinary exchange $u^!Rf_*\simeq Rf'_*v^!$: the duality isomorphisms and evaluations are natural and respect the sectionwise pasting identities, so the exchange has the mate and trace compatibility needed by convolution. [given, F1, F2, step 1.1, step 3.1] ∎
