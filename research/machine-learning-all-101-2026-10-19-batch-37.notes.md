## Mathematical escalations

1. **Elliptic classical regularity.** The planned item `thm-shin-elliptic-dirichlet-existence-and-residual-stability` requires a global `C^{2,alpha}(U)` solution on a bounded Lipschitz domain with an exterior sphere and only `C^0` boundary data. Under the library's global Hölder norm this is false at convex corners. The full counterexample is `cex-shin-elliptic-corner-regularity-on-exterior-sphere-domain`: on the sector `0<r<1`, `0<theta<2pi/3`, set `u=chi(r) r^(3/2) sin(3 theta/2)` with a smooth radial cutoff equal to one near the vertex and supported below `r=1/2`; let `L=Delta`, `g=0`, and `f=Delta u`. The domain is bounded Lipschitz and has an exterior sphere at every boundary point, `f` is smooth and supported away from the vertex, and `u` is a classical solution in `C^2(U) intersect C^0(closure U)`. Along `theta=pi/3`, `partial_rr u=(3/4)r^(-1/2)`, so `u` is not in the global `C^{2,alpha}(U)` class. The coefficients satisfy all stated ellipticity, sign, and smoothness assumptions. The source gives this PDE theorem as an external citation rather than a proof; the source assertion cannot override the counterexample. The theorem is therefore escalated. Owner reconciliation needs a corrected solution class (at least local `C^2(U) intersect C^0(closure U)` with the exact boundary behavior proved) and an exact placement for a local variable-coefficient solvability result. No dependent elliptic consistency claim is recorded ready.
2. **Neural-operator AP/universality proof.** Kovachki et al.'s Lemma 26 invokes external isomorphism, extension, and basis theorems for the full Lipschitz-domain `W^{m,p}` and `C^m` list. Its published-text proof does not prove those inputs locally. The planned local proof route must discharge those exact results, including the separate `p=1` route, and preserve that `C^m` receives AP only, not a basis claim. In the Appendix F proof, the use of Lemma 21 depends on a compact-union argument whose written proof says an arbitrary sequence of integer indices `alpha_n` has a convergent sequence, which is not justified as written; the compact-union claim is true and has a complete choice-free open-cover proof in `lem-neural-operator-compact-union-under-uniform-convergence`. Theorem 11 additionally requires the smooth-kernel input-functional and output-layer lemmas, finite-dimensional factorization, the `A_m` activation class, and a separate bounded-output branch. Those remaining arguments stay as assigned-local proof obligations; the source citation alone is not acceptance.
3. **Parabolic PDE existence.** Shin et al.'s Theorem F.1 states a variable-coefficient classical existence result but cites another PDE source rather than proving it. The heat-only maximum-principle supplier does not discharge that result. The local parabolic existence and its exact assumptions remain unresolved; the existence theorem and dependent consistency item stay escalated unless a complete local proof is supplied.
4. **Neural-operator BA definition.** Kovachki et al., Definition 7 (PDF p.49, lines 2777–2796 in the inspected extraction) defines `BA` using every compact `K`, every `epsilon>0`, and every `C >= diam_2(K)`, while requiring a network `f` with `|f(x)-x| <= epsilon` on `K` and `|f(x)| <= C` on all of Euclidean space. For `K={x_0}` with `x_0 != 0`, `diam(K)=0`; taking `C=0` forces `f=0`, which cannot approximate `x_0` to tolerance less than `|x_0|`. Thus the printed class is empty. The paper's adjacent assertion that ReLU belongs to `BA` (with three layers) cannot hold under the printed definition. Preserve the exact definition in the local carrier, prove emptiness, and escalate this source inconsistency; the Theorem 11 global output-bound branch is consequently vacuous as printed. No silent change from diameter to radius or sup norm is authorized.
5. **PINN minimizer proof uses an unsupported weight-comparability step.** Appendix C of the Shin source claims its two regularization weights have bounded max/min ratio from equation (3.2) and `m_r=O(m_b^{d/(d-1)})`. With the exact thresholds (3.1), equal sample counts `m_r=m_b=n` satisfy that count condition, while `\hat\lambda^R_{r,m}/\hat\lambda^R_{b,m}` is a fixed positive constant times `n^{\alpha/(d(d-1))}`, hence diverges for every `d\ge2` and `\alpha>0`. Taking `\lambda_m^R=\hat\lambda_m^R` satisfies both conditions in (3.2) but does not give a bounded ratio. The proof's asserted seminorm comparability is therefore not established by the displayed hypotheses. The complete counterexample is `cex-pinn-regularization-thresholds-need-balanced-sample-counts`. The local expected-loss inequality is proved conditional on the fill event, but the source's subsequent Arzelà–Ascoli route to uniform boundary-residual convergence needs an additional argument or a corrected sample-balance/regularization hypothesis. Keep the exact convergence claim and mark it escalated; do not add a hidden comparability assumption.
6. **The PINN sampling precondition is impossible as literally quantified.** Shin et al., Assumption 3.1 clause 3, says that for every $\varepsilon>0$ each cell of a probability-space partition has mass at least $c_r\varepsilon^d$ for a fixed $c_r>0$. Choosing $\varepsilon>c_r^{-1/d}$ makes each cell mass exceed total mass $1$, which is impossible. The local proof is `lem-shin-pinn-all-scale-cell-mass-assumption-inconsistent`; the exact source assumption remains unchanged in `thm-pinn-regularized-loss-consistency` and that item is escalated. The source's Theorem 3.1/Appendix B fill probability is $1-\sqrt m(1-1/\sqrt m)^m$, while its later minimizer theorem prints $1-\sqrt m(1-c_r/\sqrt m)^m$; the former implies the latter only when $c_r\le1$ (for sufficiently large $m$), and the source does not state that normalization. Proposed owner reconciliation: state a nonvacuous small-scale partition range and align the probability lower bound with Appendix B or an explicit constant normalization; then recheck the regularization thresholds using the same constants. Affected claims are the regularized-loss theorem and both elliptic/parabolic PINN consistency items.
