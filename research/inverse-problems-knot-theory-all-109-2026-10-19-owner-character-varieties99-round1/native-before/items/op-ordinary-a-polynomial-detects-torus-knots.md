## Open problem

For a nontrivial torus knot $T_{p,q}\subset S^3$, does equality $A_K(M,L)=A_{T_{p,q}}(M,L)$ up to multiplication by a Laurent unit force a knot $K\subset S^3$ to be a torus knot? Equivalently, does the ordinary A-polynomial distinguish torus knots from all non-torus knots?

## Open problem references

[Baldwin and Sivek, Question 1.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.19197v2)

Open. Baldwin–Sivek pose this as Question 1.5 in their 2025 paper. A web search on 2026-10-19 found no later resolution; no proof is asserted here.
