## Statement

Assume full AC and let $G=\pi_1(E_K)$ have a finite presentation. There is a finite list of words $w_1,\ldots,w_N\in G$ whose trace functions generate the coordinate ring of $X(E_K)$, so the map $[\rho]\mapsto(\operatorname{tr}\rho(w_1),\ldots,\operatorname{tr}\rho(w_N))$ is a closed embedding into $\mathbb C^N$, with defining ideal the kernel of the induced map $\mathbb C[z_1,\ldots,z_N]\to\mathbb C[\operatorname{Hom}(G,SL_2)]^{SL_2}$. On the irreducible-character locus, equal trace coordinates determine conjugate representations; on the reducible locus, the quotient records the semisimplified diagonal character, not the possibly non-diagonalizable representative.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** Full AC and the finite Wirtinger presentation of the knot group.

[F1] The character quotient coordinate ring is generated by traces of words if the SL2 trace-generation lemma holds ([[lem-sl2-character-trace-coordinate-algebra]]).

[A1] A finite trace generating list must be extracted from the trace-generation theorem and its relations must be shown to present the quotient; neither construction is supplied locally.

[A2] The irreducible-character conjugacy statement requires a proof that equal trace characters of irreducible $SL_2(\mathbb C)$ representations are conjugate. The reducible statement requires proof that the quotient identifies a reducible representation with its diagonal semisimplification. These local arguments are not supplied by the general finite-generation theorem; the proposed missing supplier is [[thm-irreducible-sl2-characters-determine-conjugacy-and-rational-descent]].

## Proof obligation

After a finite trace generating set is obtained, the closed-embedding assertion follows from the surjection from the polynomial ring in the listed coordinates to the invariant coordinate algebra. The missing work is the SL2 trace-generation theorem and the two representation-classification assertions in [A2], with exact treatment of reducible and irreducible loci. This lemma is escalated.
