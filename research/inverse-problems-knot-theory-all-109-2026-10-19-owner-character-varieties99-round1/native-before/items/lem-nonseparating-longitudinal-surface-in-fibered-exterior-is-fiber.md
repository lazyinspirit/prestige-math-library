## Statement

Let $K\subset S^3$ be a fibered knot with exterior $E_K$ and fiber surface $F$. If $S\subset E_K$ is a connected, orientable, nonseparating, incompressible properly embedded surface whose boundary is a nonempty union of longitudes, then $S$ is isotopic rel boundary to $F$.

## Statement references

[Baldwin and Sivek, Proposition 4.7](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.19197v2)

[Waldhausen, Corollary 5.5](https://doi.org/10.2307/1970594)

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A fibered knot exterior, its fiber surface, and a surface $S$ with the stated connectedness, orientability, nonseparation, incompressibility, and longitude-boundary hypotheses.

[F1] Full AC gives $H_2(E_K,\partial E_K;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z$, generated by the fiber class, and the longitude class is the boundary of that generator ([[thm-knot-exterior-homology-and-longitude]]).

[A1] The source proof uses Nielsen's theorem that an injective map of compact surfaces is homotopic to a covering, and Waldhausen's theorem that the resulting incompressible surfaces homotopic rel boundary are isotopic. Complete local proofs of these two 3-manifold inputs are not supplied; proposed missing suppliers are [[thm-nielsen-injective-surface-map-homotopic-to-covering]] and [[thm-waldhausen-homotopic-incompressible-surfaces-are-isotopic]].

## Proof obligation

The B-S argument first uses nonseparation to find a curve intersecting $S$ once, so $[S]$ is primitive and equals $\pm[F]$. It then lifts $S$ to the infinite cyclic cover $F\times\mathbb R$, uses the longitude boundary to show every loop on $S$ lies in the commutator subgroup, and applies Nielsen's covering theorem to the lifted inclusion. Equality of boundary classes forces covering degree one. The final isotopy requires Waldhausen's theorem. Since the Nielsen and Waldhausen inputs have no complete local suppliers, this lemma is escalated rather than marked proved.
