## Open problem

For a knot $K$ in a compact oriented 3-manifold $Y$ whose exterior is irreducible, meaning every embedded sphere in its interior bounds a ball in the exterior, is each of the following equivalences true: (i) the enhanced A-polynomial has Newton polygon contained in an affine line if and only if the exterior admits a Seifert fibration over a disk with at most two exceptional circle fibres; and (ii) $K$ admits infinitely many distinct rational $SL_2(\mathbb C)$-abelian surgeries if and only if the exterior admits a Seifert fibration over a disk with at most two exceptional circle fibres? An exceptional circle fibre has multiplicity greater than one.
