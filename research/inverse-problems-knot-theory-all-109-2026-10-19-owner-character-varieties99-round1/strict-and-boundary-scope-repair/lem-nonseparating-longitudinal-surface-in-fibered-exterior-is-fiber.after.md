## Statement

Let $K\subset S^3$ be a fibered knot with exterior $E_K$ and fiber surface $F$. If $S\subset E_K$ is a connected, orientable, nonseparating, incompressible properly embedded surface whose boundary is a nonempty union of longitudes, then $S$ is isotopic to $F$. After their boundary curves are identified by an isotopy in the boundary torus and this alignment is extended across a collar, the concluding isotopy may be taken relative to the aligned boundary.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A fibered knot exterior, its fiber surface, and a surface $S$ with the stated connectedness, orientability, nonseparation, incompressibility, and longitude-boundary hypotheses.

[F1] Full AC gives $H_2(E_K,\partial E_K;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z$, generated by the fiber class, and the longitude class is the boundary of that generator ([[thm-knot-exterior-homology-and-longitude]]).

[A1] The specific lifted surface map must be shown to be homotopic to a covering using its relative boundary and nonzero-degree data; a bare pi1-injective map between arbitrary compact surfaces need not be a covering map. The final step requires Waldhausen's theorem after actual boundary alignment. Complete local proofs of those precise inputs, including the choice-free branch of the present Statement, are not supplied; proposed missing suppliers are [[thm-nielsen-injective-surface-map-homotopic-to-covering]] and [[thm-waldhausen-homotopic-incompressible-surfaces-are-isotopic]].

## Proof obligation

The B-S argument first uses nonseparation to find a curve intersecting $S$ once, so $[S]$ is primitive and equals $\pm[F]$. It then lifts $S$ to the infinite cyclic cover $F\times\mathbb R$, uses the longitude boundary to show every loop on $S$ lies in the commutator subgroup, and applies Nielsen's covering theorem to the lifted inclusion. Equality of relative boundary classes forces covering degree one only after those relative-map hypotheses are verified. The boundary curves must first be aligned geometrically; the final relative isotopy then requires Waldhausen's theorem. The full-AC homology fact above is not by itself a proof of the choice-free Statement. Since the Nielsen and Waldhausen inputs have no complete local suppliers, this lemma is escalated rather than marked proved.
