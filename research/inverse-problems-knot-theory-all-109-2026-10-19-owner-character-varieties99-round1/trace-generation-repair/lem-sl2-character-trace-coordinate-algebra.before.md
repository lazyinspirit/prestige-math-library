## Statement

Assume full AC and let $G$ be a finitely presented group. Every regular function on $\operatorname{Hom}(G,SL_2(\mathbb C))$ invariant under simultaneous conjugation is a polynomial in finitely many word-trace functions $t_w(\rho)=\operatorname{tr}(\rho(w))$. Equivalently, the coordinate algebra of the affine character quotient is generated by trace functions of words in $G$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** Full AC, a finite presentation of $G$, its affine representation variety, and the simultaneous conjugation action.

[F1] The representation variety is affine and its regular functions form a coordinate algebra ([[lem-sl2-representation-variety-is-affine]]).

[F2] Every word trace is a regular conjugation-invariant function ([[lem-sl2-trace-functions-are-regular-and-invariant]]).

[F3] The invariant coordinate algebra is finitely generated and defines the affine categorical quotient ([[thm-invariant-ring-finite-generation-and-affine-categorical-quotient]]).

[A1] The SL2-specific simultaneous-conjugation invariant theorem that the invariant algebra of a finite tuple of matrices is generated by traces of words is not proved by [F3] and has no complete local supplier. The proposed missing supplier is [[thm-procesi-sl2-trace-invariant-generation]].

## Proof obligation

The required reduction is to prove the trace-generation theorem for the generic $SL_2$ matrices, then pass to the quotient representation variety. The latter passage must show that invariants of the relator ideal are the image of invariants before quotienting; reductivity supplies a Reynolds operator, but the current proof does not derive or verify the full SL2 trace-generation theorem. This lemma is escalated.
