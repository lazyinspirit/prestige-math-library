## Statement

Assume full AC and let $G$ be a finitely presented group. Every regular function on $\operatorname{Hom}(G,SL_2(\mathbb C))$ invariant under simultaneous conjugation is a polynomial in finitely many word-trace functions $t_w(\rho)=\operatorname{tr}(\rho(w))$. Equivalently, the coordinate algebra of the affine character quotient is generated by trace functions of words in $G$.

## Statement references

[Procesi, *The invariant theory of $n\times n$ matrices*, Theorem 1.3](https://doi.org/10.1016/0001-8708(76)90027-X)

[Tillmann, §§4.2–4.8](https://www.maths.usyd.edu.au/u/tillmann/papers/course.pdf)

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** Full AC, a finite presentation of $G$, its affine representation variety, and the simultaneous conjugation action.

[F1] For any finite presentation, the determinant and adjugate relator equations give the actual closed invariant representation subset in the matrix tuple space, including its reduced coordinate algebra. This general finitely presented group statement is proved directly in [[lem-sl2-matrix-invariants-by-epsilon-contractions-and-traces]], not restricted to knot groups.

[F2] Every word trace is a regular conjugation-invariant function ([[lem-sl2-trace-functions-are-regular-and-invariant]]).

[F3] The invariant coordinate algebra is finitely generated and defines the affine categorical quotient ([[thm-invariant-ring-finite-generation-and-affine-categorical-quotient]]).

[F4] [[lem-sl2-matrix-invariants-by-epsilon-contractions-and-traces]] proves trace generation for the full simultaneous matrix action by explicit epsilon tensor contractions and polarization, and proves the passage to every closed invariant reduced subset using the actual reductive quotient theorem.

## Proof

1.1 Use the finite presentation of $G$ to realize its representations as the determinant-one matrix tuples satisfying the relator equations from [F1]. This is a closed invariant reduced subset of the finite matrix space; no assumption that $G$ is a knot group is needed. [F1, given, construct]

2.1 Apply [F4] to that actual subset. Every invariant regular function is a polynomial in finitely many restricted word traces. On determinant-one matrices an adjugate in that calculation is the inverse matrix, so the words are words in $G$, and their regular invariant functions agree with [F2]. Thus the invariant coordinate algebra is generated by the stated trace functions. The full-AC quotient branch is exactly the branch of [F3] and [F4], rather than an unproved assertion of exactness for the relator ideal. [F2, F3, F4, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
