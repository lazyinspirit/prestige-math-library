## Repairs made

published dependency, plan, or other batch was edited; no item carried a stale
`verification.judge` record.

- `items/thm-primitive-gauss-sum-norm.md`: replaced an invalid
  difference-grouping argument with the finite-double-sum proof. The primitive
  twist identity changes `tau(chi) conjugate(tau(chi))` into the sum over
  `a,b`; finite additive-character orthogonality leaves only `b=1`, giving

  `research/frontier-31a-batch-6.proof-contracts.json` were updated.
- `items/cor-trace-and-norm-of-an-algebraic-integer.md`: the former proof
  incorrectly treated multiplication on the smaller module generated by powers
  of `alpha` as multiplication on all of `K`. It now takes the integral minimal
  polynomial `f`, writes `h=[K:Q(alpha)]`, uses the characteristic polynomial
  `f^h` of multiplication on `K`, and obtains the stated integral trace and
  norm. Its contract now quotes the exact minimal-polynomial criterion and
  records the corrected derivation.
- `items/ex-primitive-ancestors-of-small-characters.md`: narrowed the false
  plural statement about all characters induced from modulus 4 to modulus 8.
  The induction of `chi_4` has conductor 4, whereas the principal character
  cannot be swept into that assertion. The matching contract derivation was
  narrowed too.
- `items/ex-index-obstructs-naive-polynomial-factorization.md`: repaired two
  malformed `mathcal O_K` displays.
