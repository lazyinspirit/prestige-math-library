# Independent audit of eight auditor-created Step-3 additions

The [per-ID JSONL evidence](frontier-35-ten-categories-step3b-auditor-additions-independent-audit-20260926.jsonl) binds the current raw, guard, claim-section and proof-section hashes and the current bytes of the load-bearing supplier items. These eight IDs are the additions in `/tmp/frontier35-auditor-additions-before-renew.json` with no current owner repair. Four are accepted at the current bytes: `lem-counting-measure-on-a-discrete-group`, `lem-path-conjugation-isomorphism-of-fundamental-groups`, `lem-ag-standard-smooth-fibre-regular-parameters`, and `lem-field-is-noetherian`. Four required the repairs below. No Step-3 decision or certification was issued.

- `def-coordinate-parabolics-for-ordered-partitions`: The q>2 Remark claimed that every T-stable subspace splits into eigenspaces of **any single** torus element; the identity matrix disproves that step. For each coordinate i, the corrected argument chooses a diagonal element acting by a fixed λ≠1 at i and by 1 elsewhere. If W is T-stable and v∈W, then `(t_i−1)v=(λ−1)v_i e_i∈W`, so each coordinate component belongs to W. The q=2 discussion now names the third Borel as the stabilizer of the line spanned by `e₁+e₂`, rather than calling a matrix a Borel. The definition's block and conjugacy interfaces are unchanged.
- `lem-the-closed-disk-is-a-manifold-with-boundary`: The Möbius inverse `q(i−u)/(i+u)` is undefined at `u=−i`; the former proof asserted its inverse identity on `Im u≥−1`, which includes that point. The identity now uses `C\{−i}` and, for the manifold chart, the needed `Im u≥0`. The former `q\bar w≠0` assertion also failed at `w=0` and has been removed. The statement and chart map are unchanged.
- `lem-ag-base-change-of-standard-smooth-presentations`: The proof had typed `ψ:S→S′` as an R′-algebra map before S has any R′-algebra structure. The corrected step regards `R′→S′` and `S→S′` as compatible R-algebra maps and applies the R-algebra tensor coproduct to obtain the claimed R′-algebra map. The base-change statement and Jacobian calculation are unchanged.
- `lem-affine-module-sheaf-universal-property`: The inverse formula `M⊗_B O_X(U)→F(U)` was called the component of a sheaf morphism, although its domain is the presheaf `P_M(U)`. The statement and proof now say it is the composite through `P_M(U)→~M(U)`, and identify `M≅P_M(X)` through `B≅O_X(X)`. The right-exactness argument now cites `thm-right-exactness-of-tensor-products`, with its dependency, manifest row, fact and proof contract aligned. The only direct item consumer, `lem-sheaf-differentials-affine-compatibility`, uses the unchanged Hom/ε conclusion; its contract passes without a content edit.

For the four accepted items, the current proofs match their cited interfaces: discrete compact sets and regularity give counting Haar measure and finite-subsum integrals without choice; explicit path homotopies prove basepoint conjugation with the stated concatenation order; the Jacobian minor gives independent cotangent classes and, under the declared AC, a regular parameter system; and the field ideal list proves Noetherianity directly.
