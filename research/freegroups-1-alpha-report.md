### 2a. Confirmed fatal-class — Statement/title asserted more than the proof gave

**`ex-presentation-of-a-dihedral-group`** — the run's strongest finding. The item
defined `$D_n$` as "the symmetry group of a regular $n$-gon, of order $2n$",
asserted without proof that it is generated by `$\rho$` and `$\sigma$`, and then
proved only that the presentation is isomorphic to
`$\langle\{\rho,\sigma\}\rangle \le \operatorname{Sym}(\mathbb Z/n)$`. This
library develops no plane isometries, so the named target was not an object it
has. This is the same failure mode as decision D2: resting on unbuilt machinery.
*Repair:* `$D_n$` is now **stipulative**, `$D_n:=\langle\{\rho,\sigma\}\rangle$`,
so the order-$2n$ claim is proved in step 2.1 rather than assumed. The geometric
reading is retained as explicitly non-load-bearing motivation. Title, Given,
steps 2.1/2.2/4.1 rewritten, `[F1]` (`def-generated-subgroup`) added, proof
re-stratified to the precheck canonical form, Statement retagged `ai-altered`.

**`thm-abelianisation-of-a-free-group-is-free-abelian`** — the title said "**the**
free abelian group on $X$" while the Statement and proof give "**a** free abelian
group on $X$", and this library proves no uniqueness theorem for free abelian
groups (unlike free groups, where the published
`thm-free-groups-unique-up-to-unique-isomorphism` licenses the definite article).
*Repair:* title corrected to "The abelianisation of **a** free group on $X$ is
**a** free abelian group on $X$". The Statement was already correct and is
unchanged. Statement retagged `ai-altered`.

**`ex-presentation-of-z-squared`** — the Example's opening sentence asserted that
`$\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z$` **is** "the free abelian group on two generators", a
universal-property claim that no step proves and that becomes a precise technical
claim now that `def-free-abelian-group` sits on the same page. *Repair:* reworded
to state only what is proved, the presentation and its isomorphism to
`$\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z$`. Title was already correct and is unchanged.

## 4. The D6 spine — audited hardest, as instructed

Verified independently, in order, and confirmed sound:

1. Free equivalence is an equivalence relation and a congruence for
   concatenation, with insertions handled as well as deletions and arbitrary
   left/right contexts including empty ones.
2. `$W(X)/{\sim}$` is a group; well-definedness is derived from the congruence,
   not asserted; the inverse both reverses order and inverts each letter; `$n=0$`
   closes.
3. **The van der Waerden argument is non-circular.** `$\lambda_a$` is well
   defined on reduced words in both branches and lands in reduced words; *both*
   composition orders `$\lambda_{a^{-1}}\lambda_a$` and
   `$\lambda_a\lambda_{a^{-1}}$` are proved, not asserted; and uniqueness is
   obtained by evaluating equal permutations **at the empty word**, never by
   appeal to the normal form under proof. I traced the suffix induction of step
   4.1 myself: reducedness of `$r$` is exactly what makes `$\lambda_{a_k}$`
   prepend. Both spine refuters reached the same conclusion independently, one
   with a bounded check over 485 reduced words and 66,356 insertion cases.
4. The universal property is **earned**: the induced map is well defined because
   an elementary move changes the product by a cancelling pair, and uniqueness
   follows from the generator classes generating.
5. `$\iota$` injective, and the comparison with the published reduced-word model
   goes **through** `thm-free-groups-unique-up-to-unique-isomorphism`.

**The published `def-free-group` is not reconvention'd.** No new item redefines
"free group" to mean `$W(X)/{\sim}$`; `thm-word-quotient-model-is-free` concludes
that the model *satisfies* the published abstract definition, and
`def-relators-relations-and-finite-presentations` handles the published
relator/relation naming difference by noting both conventions give the same
quotient rather than by changing the published item.
