## Conventions and construction

For a word ν of weight β let F_ν consist of increasing I-graded complete flags 0=F^0⊂⋯⊂F^m=V with quotient at position r of color ν_r. Put E=⊕_{h:i→j}Hom(V_i,V_j), G=∏GL(V_i), X_ν={(x,F):x_hF^r_i⊂F^r_j for every h,r}, and π_ν(x,F)=x. Because there are no loops, stability is equivalent to xF^r⊂F^{r−1}. On a split flag its allowed matrix entries are Hom(D_a,D_b), a>b, repeated h_{ν_aν_b} times. Thus X_ν is a vector bundle over F_ν=∏Flag(V_i), is smooth, and has complex dimension

 d_ν=Σ_i β_i(β_i−1)/2+Σ_{a>b}h_{ν_aν_b}.

The projection π_ν is proper: X_ν is closed in E×F_ν and F_ν is projective. Define L_ν=π_ν!k[d_ν]. Define Z_{η,ν}=X_η×_E X_ν, with first coordinate categorical source, second categorical target. Use opposite categorical multiplication: α*β=β[n]∘α for α:L_η→L_ν[n] and β:L_ν→L_ρ[m], on Ext(L_η,L_ν)×Ext(L_ν,L_ρ). Its matrix convention is e_η A e_ν=Ext(L_η,L_ν). This is the convention acting on P_ν=Ext(L_ν,D_E) by precomposition and furnishing left projectives Ext(L,−). The Ext/convolution supplier identifies degree n with BM homological degree d_η+d_ν−n and carries this exact product to convolution. Ordinary categorical End would require an opposite algebra; it must not be silently substituted.

Let x_r=c_1^G(F^r/F^{r−1}) on X_ν. Since the vector bundle retracts equivariantly to F_ν, H_G^*(X_ν;k)=H_B^*(pt;k)=k[x_1,…,x_m]. Here B is the product Borel, its unipotent radical is contractible, and its torus has one character for every quotient line. Every x_r has degree 2. In the shifted polynomial module the unit in the ν summand has degree −d_ν.

Diagonal fundamental classes define e_ν. Capping the diagonal with x_r defines the dot. For s=s_r, let C_{sν,ν} be the closure of the relative-position-s incidence stratum in Z_{sν,ν}; when sν=ν this includes the diagonal boundary. All BM fundamental classes below use the standard positive complex Thom orientation. Define the explicitly VV-normalized elementary generator σ_r e_ν=(−1)^(d_{sν}−dim_C C_{sν,ν})[C_{sν,ν}]standard. For unequal colors dim C=d_{sν}−h_ij, so σ=(−1)^h_ij[C]standard; for equal colors dim C=d_ν+1, so σ=−[C]standard. This normalization concerns the selected generator only, and does not reorient BM homology, the dots, or the Yoneda/convolution product. Set τ_r e_ν=σ_r e_ν when ν_r=ν_{r+1}; otherwise set τ_r e_ν=(−1)^h_{ν_rν_{r+1}}σ_r e_ν. This unequal-only sign is essential: applying the VV §3.3(c) h=-1 sign to the equal case would violate the existing library's mixed relation.

## Local computation of the action, including the geometric sign

At a torus-fixed split flag with line characters χ_1,…,χ_m, the stable-arrow fiber U consists of the above allowed matrix entries. The quotient U_ν/(U_ν∩U_{sν}) consists of h_{ν_{r+1},ν_r} copies of Hom(D_{r+1},D_r); the opposite quotient consists of h_{ν_r,ν_{r+1}} copies of Hom(D_r,D_{r+1}). The latter's STANDARD Euler class, expressed in first-coordinate/output-word variables, is (x_r−x_{r+1})^h_ij: after swapping the colors, the arrow source i is at position r+1 and target j at r. In the standard fixed-point formula for correspondence action, the flag tangent factors cancel between different-color components (their underlying colored flags coincide), and the common-arrow factors cancel. Thus [C]standard sends f in P_ν to (x_r−x_{r+1})^h_ij s_r(f) in P_{sν}. The explicitly normalized σ is (−1)^h_ij[C]standard, so its action is (x_{r+1}−x_r)^h_ij s_r(f), exactly the VV cotangent-Euler formula. The further unequal-only factor (−1)^h_ij in τ cancels this normalization and gives

 τ_r(f)=(x_r−x_{r+1})^h_ij s_r(f), i≠j.

For i=j there is no arrow quotient. For the STANDARD elementary class the two torus-fixed points in the P^1 fiber have tangent Euler classes x_{r+1}−x_r and x_r−x_{r+1}, giving (s_r(f)−f)/(x_r−x_{r+1}). This agrees with integral fiber integration c_1(tautological line)=−1. The selected equal-color generator σ=τ=−[C]standard therefore gives

 τ_r(f)=(f−s_r(f))/(x_r−x_{r+1}).

This is a polynomial because f−s_r(f) is divisible by x_r−x_{r+1}. The elementary same-color correspondence is the pull-push along the partial flag forgetting F^r; that calculation also proves the formula without inverting any classes. The unequal-color formula is a zero-section Gysin Euler calculation for the displayed arrow quotient. The derivation therefore descends from torus localization to G equivariance integrally over k.

For unequal colors d_{sν}−d_ν=h_ij−h_ji. The action raises unshifted polynomial degree by 2h_ij, so on shifted summands its degree is 2h_ij+d_ν−d_{sν}=h_ij+h_ji=−a_ij. The equal-color divided difference has degree −2=−a_ii. Idempotents have degree 0 and dots degree 2. The resulting Ext identification is graded, not merely ungraded. The signed presentation in this packet is the finite specialized presentation explicitly verified below; comparison with the existing10 generic KLR item is a separate local10 bridge, preventing a page dependency cycle.

## All relations, with the native three-strand sign

Projection idempotents and polynomial multiplications give the unit, orthogonality, dot commutativity and word-intertwining identities. For different colors, T=M_{(x−y)^h_ij}s and the reverse crossing give T_reverse T=(x−y)^h_ji(y−x)^h_ij=Q_ij(x,y); dot sliding follows from sM_f=M_{sf}s. For equal colors D=(1−s)/(x−y) maps to s-invariant polynomials, so D²=0; directly D(xf)−yD(f)=f and D(yf)−xD(f)=−f. Other dots commute. Disjoint crossings commute because they involve disjoint variables and their colors are unaffected by the other crossing.

For the three-strand relation write x=x_r,y=x_{r+1},z=x_{r+2}. If all colors are distinct, either reduced product crosses the same three labeled line pairs once, with the same factor for each oriented pair; permutation of the variables gives identical coefficients of the longest permutation. If colors are i,i,j, both products are M_C s_1s_2D_1 with C=(x−y)^h_ij(x−z)^h_ij: C is symmetric in y,z, so D_2M_C=M_CD_2, and D_2s_1s_2=s_1s_2D_1. The case j,i,i is the reversed-variable identity. If all three colors are equal, expand D_1D_2D_1 and D_2D_1D_2 in the skew group algebra: both equal Σ_{w∈S_3}(−1)^ℓ(w)w/((x−y)(x−z)(y−z)).

For outer equal colors i,j,i with i≠j put a=h_ij,b=h_ji,N=a+b, w_0=s_2s_1s_2=s_1s_2s_1. Expanding the middle equal-color divided difference gives

 T_2T_1T_2=[(y−z)^a(z−y)^b−(y−z)^a(x−y)^b w_0]/(x−z),

 T_1T_2T_1=[(x−y)^b(y−x)^a−(x−y)^b(y−z)^a w_0]/(x−z).

Their permutation terms cancel, and their difference is

 [Q_ij(x,y)−Q_ij(z,y)]/(z−x).

This includes arbitrary edge multiplicities and both arrow directions. If a=b=0 the correction is zero. If i=j, Q_ii=0 and the nilHecke braid just proved supplies the zero correction. Thus every relation of the existing positive mixed-sign KLR presentation holds, including its exact correction denominator. These are operator identities; their transfer to convolution uses the faithfulness established next, not a claim that agreement on a nonfaithful module suffices.

## Relative-position PBW, faithfulness, and generation

For fixed ν and w∈S_m the only possible target word is wν. The corresponding flag-pair orbit O_{w,ν} is one G-orbit; after fixing a split flag its stabilizer is B∩wB'w^−1. It contains a maximal torus and its remaining part is unipotent. Its projection to the first flag has an affine Schubert-cell fiber (one matrix entry for each appropriate same-color inversion). Over O_{w,ν}, Z is the associated vector bundle with fiber U_F∩U_F'. This proves an incidence-stratum decomposition; it says nothing about fibers of π_ν over an arbitrary representation.

The equivariant BM module of this stratum is a shift of H_G^*(O)=H_{B∩wB'w^−1}^*(pt)=k[x_1,…,x_m], where the variables come from the first flag and the restriction map from its Borel is an isomorphism. In particular it has only even degrees. Order relative positions by a linear extension of Bruhat order, so successive unions are closed. The closed/open BM exact sequence has zero odd terms; it is therefore short exact in each even degree and injects the preceding closed union. The fundamental class of the closure of each stratum restricts to its fundamental class and generates this rank-one polynomial quotient. Multiplication by arbitrary first-flag polynomials gives explicit lifts. Induction yields a free left polynomial-module basis of closure fundamental classes, one for each compatible (w,ν). This is the complete argument filling VV Lemma 2.27's omitted cell proof.

For faithfulness, refine the flag orbit decomposition using the torus-stable Schubert cells of the first flag. These are affine spaces, the orbit projection and arrow bundle are equivariantly trivial over each cell, and their fibers are affine spaces. Thus the relative-position incidence varieties have a filtration by torus-stable affine cells with one fixed point each and nonzero line-character differences as tangent weights. No zero arrow weights occur because there are no loops. Closed/open exact sequences and Thom isomorphism show H_T^BM(Z) and H_T^BM(X) are free over S_T. Inverting the product of the finitely many nonzero weights makes fixed-point pushforward an isomorphism, proved cell by cell from the Euler-class self-intersection identity. In that localization the classes supported at fixed pairs are matrix units up to the nonzero Euler class of the middle X point: [p,q]*[q,t]=eu(T_qX)[p,t]. Hence the localized action is faithful. Freeness makes the original action faithful. Restriction H_G^BM→H_T^BM is injective: G/B has an even Schubert-cell filtration, giving the Leray-Hirsch freeness over H_G^*(pt), and the unit summand gives an injection. Consequently the G-equivariant polynomial action is faithful as well. These arguments require the BM exact sequence, vector-bundle Thom and refined Gysin self-intersection suppliers, recorded in the inventory; they do not import a geometric KLR theorem.

Finally consider a reduced word w=s_{r_1}⋯s_{r_l}. Convolution of STANDARD elementary fundamental classes is supported in positions ≤w. Its restriction to the open position-w stratum is the standard fundamental class with coefficient 1. Normalized σ and native τ change this coefficient only by explicitly specified scalar signs. To check this, at a fixed split flag pair use the Euler formula: for length-additive uv, the inversion sets obey Inv(uv)=Inv(u)⊔uInv(v). Same-color flag tangent weights therefore occur once on each side of

 eu(C_u)eu(C_v)=eu(C_{uv})eu(X_middle).

For each arrow matrix weight λ, allowedness in U_F∩U_F' is the indicator that its source follows its target in both orders. The length-additive inversion-set identity says the multiset of weights in (U_first∩U_last)⊕U_middle equals the multiset in (U_first∩U_middle)⊕(U_middle∩U_last). Take duals and Euler products to obtain the arrow part of the same equality. Every occurrence of λ is repeated h_ij times; no multiplicity-one claim is made. The fixed-point coefficient of the top stratum is thus 1. The selected generator signs (including the equal-color negative standard class) make the coefficient ±1, always a unit. Induction on the Bruhat filtration and the closure-class basis shows dots and elementary crossings generate all convolution homology. Moreover the products τ_w e_ν form a polynomial basis, since their change-of-basis matrix from closure classes is triangular with diagonal ±1.

The generator map from the signed KLR presentation to convolution is therefore surjective, and injective by the following intrinsic presentation argument, independently of current10. ## Internal equivariant topology calculations (not attributed to six operations)

The torus calculation can be built from finite approximations: for T=(C×)^m use ((C^N−0)^m)/T=(CP^{N−1})^m. The cellular complex of each CP^{N−1} has one cell in each even dimension0,…,2N−2, zero differential, and hyperplane intersection shows H*=k[t]/(t^N). Products give the polynomial ring in the stable degree range. The complex unipotent group of upper unitriangular matrices has its coordinate contraction and is contractible. Thus BT→BB is a homotopy equivalence and H_B*=k[t_1,…,t_m]. The associated-space equivalence EG×_G(G/B)≃EG/B identifies the same polynomial algebra with H_G*(G/B); its line characters are precisely the Chern classes used here. For B∩wB'w^−1, its unipotent radical again has strictly triangular coordinates and is contractible, giving the same calculation without a reductivity assumption on the stabilizer.

Flag-bundle freeness does not need an unproved equivariant formality claim. The flag bundle of a rank n complex bundle is a tower of projective bundles of ranks n,n−1,…,2. For a rank r bundle W, the tautological class t restricts on every CP^{r−1} fiber to a generator and 1,t,…,t^{r−1} form its cohomology basis by the preceding cellular computation. Cellular induction on the base proves that the sum of cup-product maps by these classes is an isomorphism; the same proof on each finite skeleton applies to Borel bases. The relation is t^r−c_1(W)t^{r−1}+⋯+(−1)^r c_r(W)=0, obtained from the exact tautological-line/quotient sequence and c_r(quotient)=0. Iterating produces explicit monomial bases and a summand generated by1. The map from G-equivariant cohomology or BM homology to T-equivariant theory is the pullback to this flag bundle: BB≃BT over BG, with fiber G/B. The same relative cellular argument with BM coefficients proves Leray–Hirsch and in particular injectivity. This is enough for the restriction used above; no general invariant-theory assertion is required.

For the incidence cell calculation, choose coordinate flags in every V_i. Row echelon matrices give each Schubert cell a unique unipotent representative with one coordinate for each inversion; conjugation by T scales that coordinate by its nonzero difference of line characters. Over such a first-flag cell, a relative-position orbit is trivialized by transporting the second flag with that representative. The remaining second-flag coordinates again have row echelon representatives, and the stabilizer intersection is upper triangular in both orders. The common stable-arrow entries are transported by these representatives and give an actual vector-bundle trivialization. The resulting cell has coordinates consisting of these two sets of same-color inversion entries and the allowed common-arrow entries. Every arrow coordinate has weight χ_target−χ_source with distinct colors, so none has zero weight. Thus each incidence piece has one fixed point and is a T-equivariant affine space with explicitly nonzero weights. Refining the Bruhat closed/open filtration by these Schubert filtrations supplies the required cellular induction; no assertion about π fibers occurs.

For a cell W of complex dimension r with weights λ_1,…,λ_r, its BM Thom generator has degree2r. The zero-section self-intersection multiplies by its top Chern/Euler class ∏λ_i: each one-dimensional factor is the first Chern class of its associated line bundle, and the product follows by the direct-sum Thom product. Therefore fixed-point pushforward becomes an isomorphism after inverting these weights. The closed/open sequence and induction over the finite cell filtration make this a localization isomorphism for X and Z, and also prove polynomial freeness.
