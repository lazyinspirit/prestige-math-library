## Statement

Let $(N\subseteq M,\tau,E_N,\mathcal X,p,k,\lambda)$ be the finite-index extremal II1 data of [[def-extremal-finite-index-ii1-subfactor-data]], and let $(u_j)_{j=1}^k$ be the coordinate frame of [[lem-finite-right-n-module-frame-from-projection-model]]. On $H=L^2(M,\tau)$ let $e_N$ be the orthogonal projection onto $L^2(N,\tau_N)$, and set $M_1=\operatorname{End}_N(\mathcal X)\cong pM_k(N)p$ in its natural representation on $H$. Then $M_1=W^*(M,e_N)$, $e_Nxe_N=E_N(x)e_N$ for $x\in M$, and the faithful normalized trace
$$
\tau_1(T)=\lambda^{-1}(\operatorname{Tr}_k\otimes\tau_N)(T),\qquad T\in pM_k(N)p,
$$
satisfies the Markov formula $\tau_1(xe_Ny)=\lambda^{-1}\tau(yx)$ for $x,y\in M$. Equivalently, its unnormalized value is $\sum_j\tau_N(\langle u_j,T(u_j)\rangle_N)$ in the specified coordinate frame. This proves the first basic-construction stage under the stated bounded-coordinate data. Iteration and the adjacent Jones-projection relations require the same verified framed-extremal data at the subsequent stages; they are recorded as an owner escalation rather than inferred here.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A faithful normal trace-preserving expectation $E_N$, the finite projection model $\mathcal X\cong pN^k$, its bounded coordinate vectors $u_j\in M$, and $\lambda=(\operatorname{Tr}_k\otimes\tau_N)(p)>0$.

[F1] $E_N$ is a normal faithful positive $N$-bimodule projection fixing $N$ and preserving $\tau$. ([[def-extremal-finite-index-ii1-subfactor-data]])

[F2] The coordinate frame satisfies $x=\sum_j u_jE_N(u_j^*x)$ on $M$, and $\sum_j\tau_N(E_N(u_j^*u_j))=\lambda$. ([[lem-finite-right-n-module-frame-from-projection-model]])

[F3] $\operatorname{End}_N(\mathcal X)$ is identified by the finite projection model with the corner $pM_k(N)p$, whose unnormalized trace is the restriction of $\operatorname{Tr}_k\otimes\tau_N$. ([[def-extremal-finite-index-ii1-subfactor-data]])

## Proof

**Given:** The finite-index data and facts [F1]–[F3].

1.1 For $x\in M$ and $n\in N$, trace preservation and the $N$-bimodule identity give $\langle n,E_N(x)\rangle_{L^2}=\tau(n^*E_N(x))=\tau(E_N(n^*x))=\tau(n^*x)$. Thus $E_N$ agrees on the dense subspace $M\subset L^2(M)$ with the orthogonal projection onto $L^2(N)$; denote this projection by $e_N$. [F1, given, algebra]

2.1 For $x,z\in M$, $e_Nxe_N(z)=E_N(xE_N(z))=E_N(x)E_N(z)$ by right $N$-bimodularity. Hence $e_Nxe_N=E_N(x)e_N$. Each $x\in M$ acts by left multiplication on $H$ and commutes with the right $N$ action, while $e_N$ is right $N$-linear; therefore $W^*(M,e_N)\subseteq\operatorname{End}_N(\mathcal X)$. [F1, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 For $T\in\operatorname{End}_N(\mathcal X)$ and $x\in M$, right $N$-linearity and the frame identity give $T(x)=\sum_jT(u_j)E_N(u_j^*x)$. Applying the frame identity once more to each $T(u_j)\in\mathcal X=M$ writes $T(u_j)=\sum_i u_iE_N(u_i^*T(u_j))\in M$. The corner model makes $T$ adjointable and $T^*T\le\|T\|^2 1$, so $\|T x\|_2^2=\tau_N(\langle Tx,Tx\rangle_N)=\tau_N(\langle x,T^*Tx\rangle_N)\le\|T\|^2\|x\|_2^2$. Thus it extends to a bounded operator on $H$. As operators on the dense subspace $M\subset H$, $T=\sum_j T(u_j)e_Nu_j^*$, a finite sum in the algebra generated by $M$ and $e_N$. Thus every module endomorphism belongs to $W^*(M,e_N)$, proving equality. [F2, F3, step 2.1, construct]

4.1 By [F3], the corner trace is faithful and positive; its value at the identity $p$ is $\lambda$, so division by $\lambda$ makes $\tau_1$ a faithful normalized trace. In the coordinate frame its unnormalized value on $T$ is $\sum_j\tau_N(E_N(u_j^*T(u_j)))$, since the frame vectors are precisely the projected coordinate vectors and this sum is the diagonal matrix trace of $pTp$. [F2, F3, step 3.1, algebra]

5.1 For $x,y\in M$, apply the frame trace formula to $T=xe_Ny$, which acts by $z\mapsto xE_N(yz)$. It gives $\lambda\tau_1(xe_Ny)=\sum_j\tau(u_j^*xE_N(yu_j))=\tau((\sum_jE_N(yu_j)u_j^*)x)$. Taking the adjoint of the frame identity for $y^*$ gives $y=\sum_jE_N(yu_j)u_j^*$, so the right side is $\tau(yx)$. This proves the Markov formula and completes the first-stage trace construction. [F1, F2, step 4.1, algebra] ∎

## Escalation

The full assigned tower claim still needs an explicit proof that every iterated inclusion retains the required finite bounded frame and extremality, and a derivation of both adjacent Jones relations $e_i e_{i+1}e_i=\lambda^{-1}e_i$ and $e_{i+1}e_i e_{i+1}=\lambda^{-1}e_{i+1}$. The current local frame proof establishes only the first stage. Do not identify the full diagram algebra with $M_i$ or a relative commutant on this evidence.
