## Statement

Let $\ell\ge3$, $q=e^{\pi i/\ell}$, $\delta=q+q^{-1}>0$, $k=\ell-2$, and let $[a]=\sin(a\pi/\ell)/\sin(\pi/\ell)$. For the normalized closure trace $\tau_n$, let $J_n$ be its trace-pairing radical and put $Q_n=\mathrm{TL}_n(\mathbb C,\delta)/J_n$. Then $J_n$ is exactly the endomorphism component of the negligible tensor ideal generated by the boundary Jones–Wenzl projector $p_{\ell-1}$. The $Q_n$ form the actual $A_{\ell-1}$ path-space matrix tower of [[def-temperley-lieb-type-a-path-spaces-and-matrix-unit-tower]], with paths starting at $0$ and staying in $\{0,\ldots,k\}$. Their simple modules are precisely the nonzero endpoint path spaces, and restriction branches with multiplicity one over adjacent predecessor vertices.

Reflection gives a finite-dimensional $C^*$-structure on $Q_n$ in which the normalized closure trace is faithful positive. The strand inclusions are injective unital $*$-maps and normalized right closure descends to the positive trace-preserving conditional expectation $\bar E_n:Q_{n+1}\to Q_n$ for $n\ge1$. More explicitly, choose the normalized path matrix units $F_{p,q}$ whose prefix inclusions satisfy $F_{p,q}\mapsto\sum_c F_{pc,qc}$. If $pc,qc$ end at $c$, with predecessor endpoints $a,b$, then
$$\bar E_n(F_{pc,qc})=\begin{cases}\dfrac{[c+1]}{\delta[a+1]}F_{p,q},&a=b,\\0,&a\ne b.\end{cases}$$
Here the extensions and matrix units exist only for valid adjacent retained labels. The diagonal trace is $\tau_n(F_{p,p})=\delta^{-n}[a+1]$ when $p$ ends at $a$. These assertions hold at every finite rank, including the $\ell=3$ boundary; no extra quotient/path identification is assumed.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The stated positive root parameters and the closure/reflection convention.

[F1] Inclusion and normalized right closure preserve the trace radicals, are bimodular and trace-preserving, and satisfy $E_n\iota_n=\mathrm{id}$. ([[lem-temperley-lieb-right-closure-preserves-trace-radical]], [[lem-temperley-lieb-diagram-tower-conditional-expectation-identities]])

[F2] The retained path inclusions and retractions are recursively constructed by the JW up/down splitting, with complete matrix units and diagonal unnormalized trace $[a+1]$. The proof's down maps are a cup inclusion $\alpha_a$ and $\lambda_a^{-1}\alpha_a^*$, where $\lambda_a=[a+1]/[a]>0$; the up term is its orthogonal complement. ([[lem-temperley-lieb-root-fusion-paths-have-nonzero-pairings]], [[lem-temperley-lieb-jones-wenzl-partial-trace-and-uniqueness]])

[F3] The fixed embedded boundary ideal is exactly $J_n$, its quotient is the full retained path matrix algebra, and categorical negligibility has these exact endomorphism components. ([[thm-temperley-lieb-root-negligible-quotient-is-semisimple]])

[F4] The abstract endpoint path tower uses prefix extension, and its restricted endpoint module branches once over each adjacent predecessor. ([[def-temperley-lieb-type-a-path-spaces-and-matrix-unit-tower]], [[thm-temperley-lieb-type-a-path-space-tower-branching]])

[F5] At this positive root, $[a]>0$ for $1\le a<\ell$, $[\ell]=0$, and $[a]+[a+2]=\delta[a+1]$. ([[def-temperley-lieb-quantum-integers-and-quantum-characteristic]])

## Proof

**Given:** The hypotheses and [F1]–[F5].

1.1 By [F3], the radical is the specified boundary ideal and its quotient has the complete path matrix units of [F2]. In the up/down splitting replace each down inclusion $\alpha_a$ by $\lambda_a^{-1/2}\alpha_a$ and its retraction by $\lambda_a^{-1/2}\alpha_a^*$. Their composite is the endpoint identity, since $\alpha_a^*\alpha_a=\lambda_a p_{a-1}$; their reversed composite is the unchanged down projection. Keep the up inclusion/retraction unchanged. Every $\lambda_a$ is positive by [F5], so these rescalings exist. Compose these isometric splittings along each retained path to obtain $I_p$ with $I_p^*I_q=0$ for distinct paths, $I_p^*I_p=p_a$ for endpoint $a$, and $\sum_p I_pI_p^*=1_n$. Thus $F_{p,q}=I_pI_q^*$ are complete matrix units with $F_{p,q}^*=F_{q,p}$. This identifies the actual quotient and its reflection with the direct sum of ordinary complex matrix algebras. Closing across the two path maps gives $\operatorname{Tr}_n(F_{p,q})=\delta_{p,q}[a+1]$, so its normalized trace is faithful positive by [F5]. [F2, F3, F5, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 Tensoring an endpoint path map with a strand and resolving its endpoint identity by the same isometric up/down splitting gives $F_{p,q}\otimes1=\sum_c F_{pc,qc}$. The coefficients are exactly one because the inclusion and retraction at each common endpoint use reciprocal normalizations. At $a=k$ delete $p_{k+1}$ before splitting; only its down term remains, as in [F2]. Thus the actual quotient inclusions are exactly the prefix maps of [F4]; no compatibility is imposed as an additional hypothesis. Transporting the endpoint action along these matrix units and applying [F4] gives the asserted multiplicity-one branching, omitting zero path spaces and invalid boundary labels. [F2, F3, F4, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 [F1] defines $\bar\iota_n([x])=[\iota_n(x)]$ and $\bar E_n([z])=[E_n(z)]$ independently of representatives. Bimodularity, trace preservation and $\bar E_n\bar\iota_n=\mathrm{id}$ pass to these quotients, so inclusion is injective. Its compatibility with reflection follows from real $\delta$. Closure also commutes with reflection: the two finite matching graphs obtained by reflecting before or after closing have the same components and the real factor $\delta^{-1}$. Thus $\bar E_n$ is star-preserving. For $z\ge0$ and $b\in Q_n$, bimodularity gives $\tau_n(b^*\bar E_n(z)b)=\tau_{n+1}(\bar\iota_n(b)^*z\bar\iota_n(b))\ge0$. If the self-adjoint $\bar E_n(z)$ had a negative spectral subspace in one of the finite matrix blocks from step 1.1, its nonzero spectral projection would contradict this inequality and faithfulness. Hence $\bar E_n$ is positive, unital, idempotent, bimodular and trace-preserving, the claimed conditional expectation. [F1, step 1.1, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 Bimodularity with the endpoint central projections shows that $\bar E_n(F_{pc,qc})=0$ if the predecessor endpoints $a,b$ differ. If they agree, bimodularity with the individual predecessor matrix units shows that its value is a single scalar $t_{a,c}F_{p,q}$ independent of $p,q$: multiplying on the left and right by predecessor diagonal units isolates that matrix entry, and multiplying by predecessor off-diagonal units identifies their coefficients. Trace preservation on a diagonal unit now gives $t_{a,c}\delta^{-n}[a+1]=\delta^{-(n+1)}[c+1]$, so $t_{a,c}=[c+1]/(\delta[a+1])$. The coefficients are positive and sum to one over valid neighbours by [F5]; at the upper boundary the omitted term is $[\ell]=0$, and at $a=0$ the nonexistent down term is $[0]=0$. For $\ell=3$ every vertex has one retained neighbour and this coefficient is one. Rank zero and rank one have their unique scalar path algebra and the unital inclusion between them. This proves every assertion at the stated finite ranks. [F1, F4, F5, step 1.1, step 2.1, step 3.1, algebra] ∎
