## Uneditable findings and closure needed

- **`lie-algebras-and-infinitesimal-group-schemes-examples` — false-claim, fatal.** library/scheme-theory/lie-algebras-and-infinitesimal-group-schemes-examples.md:22-24, second prose paragraph, "the generators being the classes of t, t-1 and their images". Lie(G) is Hom_k(m_e/m_e^2,k), so [t], [t-1] and [s] are cotangent vectors, not elements generating the Lie algebra. The repaired GL lemma and Lie-algebra example identify the actual generators as the dual functionals taking those classes to 1. Change the B-page summary to say "generators dual to the cotangent classes"; B-page prose is outside this reader's edit authority.

- **`lem-ag-separable-residue-cotangent-sequence` — false-claim, fatal.** items/lem-ag-separable-residue-cotangent-sequence.md:66, Proof step 4.1, theta(1 tensor dx)=x mod m^2 "for x in R". The displayed formula is asserted for every x in R, but theta has target m/m^2. For x=1 its left side is theta(d1)=0, while 1 mod m^2 is nonzero and not in that target. Step 3.1 actually constructs D=id-s*pi on R/m^2, whose value on a general x is x-s(pi(x)); it equals x mod m^2 only for x in m. Restrict the displayed equality to x in m (or use the correct general formula). The short-exact-sequence statement is unchanged by this correction; assigned consumer F6/Proof 1.1 uses this supplier.

- **`thm-ag-perfect-field-jacobian-regularity` — false-claim, fatal.** items/thm-ag-perfect-field-jacobian-regularity.md:100, Proof step 1.2, "the classes of the y_j span pR/p^2". F6 supplies an initial part of a regular parameter system generating I', not a basis of the entire ambient cotangent space. Take P=k[x,y], I=(x), q=(x,y)/(x), R=P_(x,y): d=2, dim(A_q)=1 and c=1, so y_1=x spans only a one-dimensional subspace of the two-dimensional pR/(pR)^2. Replace the assertion by "their classes form a basis of (I'+(pR)^2)/(pR)^2". This is exactly the subspace supplied by the opened F5/F6 targets and suffices for the ensuing Jacobian independence argument. Assigned consumer F3/Proof 2.1 uses the local-chart conclusion.

These are false authored assertions, rather than merely unstated routine proof steps, so they are routed as fatal under the dispatch's classification rule.
