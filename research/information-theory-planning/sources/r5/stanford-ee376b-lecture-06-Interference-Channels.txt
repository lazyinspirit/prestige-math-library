Summary

∙Discrete memoryless interference channel (DM-IC)

∙Simultaneous nonunique decoding is optimal under strong interference

∙Han–Kobayashi coding scheme:

㶳Rate splitting and superposition coding

㶳Fourier–Motzkin elimination

㶳Optimal for injective deterministic ICs

∙Gaussian interference channel:

㶳Capacity region under strong interference achieved via simultaneous decoding

㶳Sum-capacity under weak interference achieved by treating interference as noise

㶳Han–Kobayashi coding scheme achieves within half a bit of the capacity region
