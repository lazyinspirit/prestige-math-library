Summary

∙Feedback can simplify coding and improve reliability

∙Iterative refinement:

㶳Schalkwijk–Kailath scheme for the Gaussian channel

㶳Block feedback scheme for the DMC

∙Feedback can enlarge the capacity region of multiuser channels:

㶳Can induce cooperation between the senders

㶳Cover–Leung inner bound

㶳Can provide channel information to be broadcast to multiple receivers

㶳The cutset bound is achievable for the relay channel with feedback
