Summary

∙General network models that include graphical networks, RC, and single-hop
networks with feedback as special cases

∙Cutset bounds for the DMN and Gaussian networks

∙Sliding window decoding for network decode–forward

∙Noisy network coding:

㶳Same message is sent multiple times using independent codebooks

㶳No binning

㶳Simultaneous nonunique decoding without requiring the recovery of compression bin
indices

㶳Includes CF for the relay channel and network coding for graphical networks

㶳Achieves within constant gap of cutset bound for Gaussian multimessage multicast
