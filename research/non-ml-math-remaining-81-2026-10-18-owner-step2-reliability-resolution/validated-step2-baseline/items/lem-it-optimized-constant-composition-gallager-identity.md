## Statement

Let $W$ be a finite DMC and fix $\rho>0$. For each input law $P$, define
$$E_{0,\rm cc}(\rho,P):=\inf_{V:\,V(\cdot\mid x)\ll W(\cdot\mid x)}\left\{\sum_xP(x)D(V(\cdot\mid x)\|W(\cdot\mid x))+\rho I_P(V)\right\},$$
where $I_P(V)$ is the mutual information of $P(x)V(y\mid x)$ and terms with $P(x)=0$ are omitted. At $\rho=0$, set $E_{0,\rm cc}(0,P)=0$. Then
$$\max_P E_{0,\rm cc}(\rho,P)=\max_P E_0(\rho,P),$$
with $E_0$ from [[def-it-gallager-and-expurgated-exponent-functions]]. The equality is after optimizing over $P$; it is not asserted for each fixed $P$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A finite DMC $W$, a parameter $\rho>0$, and the finite KL and mutual-information conventions.

[F1] [[def-it-one-letter-gallager-exponent-functions]]: $E_0(\rho,P)=-\ln\sum_y(\sum_xP(x)W(y\mid x)^{1/(1+\rho)})^{1+\rho}$.

[F2] [[def-it-finite-mutual-information]]: $I_P(V)$ is $D(PV_{XY}\|P\otimes PV_Y)$.

[F3] [[def-finite-kullback-leibler-divergence]] and [[thm-it-finite-kl-nonnegative-equality]]: finite KL is support-aware, nonnegative, and vanishes exactly at equal probability vectors.

## Proof

1.1 Put $\alpha=(1+\rho)^{-1}\in(0,1)$ and, for an output law $Q$, set $Z_x(Q)=\sum_yW(y\mid x)^\alpha Q(y)^{1-\alpha}$. If $Z_x(Q)>0$, define $R_x(y)=W(y\mid x)^\alpha Q(y)^{1-\alpha}/Z_x(Q)$. Expanding the finite sums gives for every $V_x\ll W_x$ with finite $D(V_x\|Q)$ $D(V_x\|W_x)+\rho D(V_x\|Q)=(1+\rho)D(V_x\|R_x)-(1+\rho)\ln Z_x(Q).$ By [F3], the minimum over $V_x$ is $-(1+\rho)\ln Z_x(Q)=-\alpha^{-1}\ln Z_x(Q)$. If $Z_x(Q)=0$, every such objective with positive $P(x)$ is infinite, matching the extended value of this expression. [F1, F3, given, algebra, cases]

1.2 Define $S_y(P)=\sum_xP(x)W(y\mid x)^\alpha$ and $\Psi(P)=\sum_yS_y(P)^{1/\alpha}$. Then [F1] gives $E_0(\rho,P)=-\ln\Psi(P)$. The function $\Psi$ is continuous and convex on the compact input simplex, so it has a minimizer $P^*$. Write $S_y=S_y(P^*)$, $\Psi_* =\Psi(P^*)>0$, $T=\{y:S_y>0\}$, and $q^*(y)=S_y^{1/\alpha}/\Psi_*$ on $T$, with $q^*(y)=0$ outside $T$. [F1, given, algebra]

2.1 For any $V$ and output law $Q$, expansion of finite KL gives $\sum_xP(x)D(V_x\|Q)=I_P(V)+D(PV_Y\|Q)\ge I_P(V),$ with equality at $Q=PV_Y$ by [F2–F3]. Taking the joint infimum over $V,Q$ and applying step 1.1 row by row therefore yields $E_{0,\rm cc}(\rho,P)=\inf_Q\sum_{x:P(x)>0}P(x)f_x(Q),\quad f_x(Q):=-\alpha^{-1}\ln Z_x(Q).$ Coordinates with $P(x)=0$ are omitted, so no $0\cdot\infty$ term is used. [F2, F3, step 1.1, algebra]

2.2 Differentiation of the finite sum gives $\partial_x\Psi(P^*)=\alpha^{-1}T_x$, where $T_x=\sum_yW(y\mid x)^\alpha S_y^\rho$. Since $P^*$ minimizes $\Psi$ on the simplex, the one-sided derivative toward the point mass at $x$ is nonnegative; hence $T_x\ge\Psi_*$. The weighted average $\sum_xP^*(x)T_x=\Psi_*$, by homogeneity of $\Psi$, so equality holds whenever $P^*(x)>0$. Substitution into $Z_x(q^*)=\Psi_*^{-(1-\alpha)}T_x$ gives $Z_x(q^*)\ge\Psi_*^\alpha$, with equality for every active input. Thus $f_x(q^*)\le-\ln\Psi_*$ for all $x$, with equality on $\operatorname{supp}P^*$. [step 1.2, algebra]

3.1 Applying step 2.1 with the particular output law $q^*$ gives, for every $P$, $E_{0,\rm cc}(\rho,P)\le\sum_xP(x)f_x(q^*)\le-\ln\Psi_* =\max_{P'}E_0(\rho,P').$ Therefore $\max_PE_{0,\rm cc}(\rho,P)\le\max_PE_0(\rho,P)$. [step 2.1, step 2.2, algebra]

3.2 For $P^*$, the function $F(Q)=\sum_{x:P^*(x)>0}P^*(x)f_x(Q)$ is convex: $Z_x(Q)$ is concave because $t^{1-\alpha}$ is concave, and $-\ln$ is convex decreasing. On the relative interior of the simplex supported on $T$, differentiation gives $\frac{\partial F}{\partial Q(y)}(q^*)=-\rho\frac{\sum_xP^*(x)W(y\mid x)^\alpha}{S_y}=-\rho\qquad(y\in T),$ using $T_x=\Psi_*$ on active inputs from step 2.2. The convex supporting inequality and the fact that $Q$ and $q^*$ both sum to one on $T$ imply $F(Q)\ge F(q^*)$ for every $Q$ supported on $T$. [step 1.2, step 2.2]

4.1 For a general output law $Q$, let $c=Q(T)$. If $c=0$, every active row has $Z_x(Q)=0$ and $F(Q)=+\infty$. If $c>0$, normalize $Q_T=Q(\cdot\mid T)$. Every active row is supported on $T$, so $Z_x(Q)=c^{1-\alpha}Z_x(Q_T)$ and $f_x(Q)=f_x(Q_T)-\rho\ln c\ge f_x(Q_T)$. Step 3.2 then gives $F(Q)\ge F(q^*)$. Since active inputs satisfy $f_x(q^*)=-\ln\Psi_*$, step 2.1 yields $E_{0,\rm cc}(\rho,P^*)=F(q^*)=-\ln\Psi_*=\max_PE_0(\rho,P)$. [step 2.2, step 3.2, cases, algebra]

5.1 Step 4.1 gives $\max_PE_{0,\rm cc}(\rho,P)\ge E_{0,\rm cc}(\rho,P^*)=\max_PE_0(\rho,P)$, while step 3.1 gives the reverse inequality. This proves the globally optimized identity and establishes no pointwise-in-$P$ equality. [step 3.1, step 4.1, algebra] ∎
