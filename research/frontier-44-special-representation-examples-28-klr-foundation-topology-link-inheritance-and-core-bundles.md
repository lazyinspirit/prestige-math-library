# Controlled link inheritance and compact core bundle construction

This is the same topology author's expanded construction for integration into `lem-compact-semialgebraic-families-admit-compatible-finite-triangulations`. It is research proof text, not a new accepted supplier or a new metadata node. The compact primitive still needs its full finite interior induction and descending gluing. The outer contract remains compact Whitney spaces; its induction uses the stronger category of finite smooth controlled spaces with commuting radial collars, since a smoothly normalized link need not itself be semialgebraic or Whitney.

## Inherited compact controlled links

Let `W` be compact, with finite compatible tube data and the actual commuting radial collars supplied in the new radial lemma. Retain outer maps while using inner normalized domains. Fix a stratum `X` and a level `0<a<1`. Write `A={p in T_X : rho_X(p)=a}` and `L=closure_W(A)`. This is compact. A limit of points of `A` that leaves `T_X` must land on the frontier of `X`: in tube parameters either the bases have an interior limit, which retains the tube because the normalized radius is the fixed `a<1`, or the bases approach the frontier, where the imposed displacement bound forces the point and its base to have the same limit. Consequently `L` meets only upper incident strata, where it is the smooth hypersurface `rho_X=a`, and lower frontier strata of `X`; it misses `X` itself and incomparable strata.

Membership in `L` on a lower frontier stratum `Y<X` is locally constant along that stratum. Here is the required transport, rather than an appeal to a Whitney property of `L`. On a small `Y`-base coordinate box, construct smooth lifts of each base coordinate field in increasing dimension, with the maximal incident tube controller as in the radial lemma. The field on `Y` is the given coordinate field; upper fields satisfy `d pi_Z V=V_Z pi_Z`, `d rho_Z V=0` on the retained tube of every lower `Z`. On the `X` tube, therefore, the lift preserves `rho_X=a`. The compact-trapping proof of the radial lemma gives a jointly continuous short flow, even if ambient boundedness on an entire outer tube is unavailable. It maps `A` to itself wherever defined, hence maps its closure `L` to itself, as does the inverse flow. Ordered coordinate transports along `Y` prove local constancy of membership. Thus `L∩Y` is an open and closed union of components of `Y`.

The same horizontal-lift construction, with the level constraints imposed in the chain of controllers, shows that the closure-incidence label of an upper level piece is locally constant on each lower level piece. If a disconnected level piece has different incidence labels, split it by the finitely many Boolean labels recording membership in the closures of the finitely many upper pieces. These subsets are open and closed smooth pieces. There are at most finitely many labels, so this makes a finite frontier stratification; it does not require a finite number of connected components by assumption. The maps and collars below restrict to these pieces, since the transports preserve each closure and hence its incidence labels.

For a lower frontier `Y<X`, the restricted `Y` tube retracts `L` into `L∩Y`. Indeed, radial `Y` transport preserves `rho_X` on `A` by the commuting collar construction, and its limit as `rho_Y→0` is `pi_Y`. Hence `pi_Y(A∩T_Y)⊂L`; continuity gives the same inclusion for `L∩T_Y`. For an upper `Y>X`, the old identity `rho_X pi_Y=rho_X` shows directly that `pi_Y` preserves the level hypersurface. Therefore all inherited projections have their actual target in the corresponding stratum of `L`.

The inherited radius/projection maps are submersive on the smooth strata of `L`. There are two cases. If `Y<X<Z`, on `Z` the map `(pi_X,rho_X)` is submersive and `(pi_Y,rho_Y)` factors through `pi_X`; the latter pair is submersive on `X`. The joint map `(pi_Y,rho_Y,rho_X)` is therefore submersive on `Z`. Restricting to `rho_X=a` gives the full rank of `(pi_Y,rho_Y)` there. If `X<Y<Z`, the map `(pi_Y,rho_Y)` is submersive on `Z` and `rho_X` factors through `pi_Y`. Its restriction to the kernel of `d rho_X` surjects onto the tangent space of `(Y∩{rho_X=a})×(0,1)`: lift a target vector with `d rho_X=0` through the original pair, and the factorization forces its lift to satisfy the same equation. This is precisely the needed level-pair submersion.

All control equations and incomparable-domain disjointness restrict. Local properness restricts because `L` is closed in compact `W`. The radial families restrict too: they preserve `A` where defined, preserve its closure by continuity, and preserve the new incidence labels. Their semigroup, commutation and projection equations are the original equations on actual retained domains. Their zero-end convergence still follows from the compact tube estimate. Thus `L` is a compact finite smooth controlled stratified space with commuting collars. Its maximal stratum dimension is at most `dim(W)-1`: upper level strata lose one dimension, and lower frontier strata have dimension strictly less than `dim(X)≤dim(W)`. This is the exact dimension drop needed by the interior triangulation induction. Nothing in this argument identifies `L` with an algebraic or semialgebraic set.

## Compact cores and corner faces

For each stratum `Z` define its core by removing the small open tubes of lower-dimensional strata. Compactness is literal. Any sequence in this core that tends to the frontier of `Z` would eventually lie in the open tube of its limiting lower stratum, contradicting its definition. Thus the core is a closed subset of `W` contained in `Z`, hence compact. The same argument applies to a level link after lower-stratum tubes are removed.

At a core point the active lower controllers form a chain. The differentials of their radius functions are independent on the stratum. For a chain `Y_1<...<Y_k<Z`, use the submersion `(pi_Yk,rho_Yk)` on `Z`; the earlier radius functions factor through `pi_Yk`, and inductively their differentials are independent on `Y_k`. Lifting first through the earlier joint radius map and then through the maximal pair proves surjectivity of the joint radius map on `Z`. In constant-rank coordinates, inequalities `rho_Yj≥d_j` are independent half-space coordinates. The core is therefore a smooth compact manifold with corners, and every designated face or face intersection is a genuine corner face. This checks the relative smooth author's corner-face compatibility premise.

The normal level-core map to a fixed base core, `pi_X: Z∩S_X(a)→X`, is a proper corner submersion with compatible faces. For `Y<X`, the boundary coordinate `rho_Y-d_Y` is the pullback of the same base boundary coordinate by `pi_X`. For `X<Y<Z`, it is an extra source face coordinate. The pair-map factorization proves surjectivity of the joint differential `(d pi_X, d rho_Y1,...,d rho_Yk)` for the extra source faces. The preceding kernel argument applies also to the fixed equation `rho_X=a`. Thus local smooth constant-rank charts put the map and all active faces in the form `(b,u)→b`, with base inequalities in coordinates `b`, the corresponding source inequalities equal to those base inequalities, and extra source inequalities in coordinates `u`. Properness follows from the already proved compactness of the truncated cores.

## Actual face-compatible product charts

To obtain bundle charts, choose a sufficiently small corner box in the base. For each base coordinate `b_i`, choose local smooth horizontal fields satisfying `d pi_X V_i=∂/∂b_i` and `d h_j V_i=0` for every active extra source face-defining coordinate `h_j`. The joint differential just proved has a smooth right inverse, giving these fields. Choose patches avoiding every inactive closed face and retaining every active one. A smooth subordinate partition preserves all these linear equations. No derivative of a partition coefficient enters. For a corresponding source face pulled back from the base, its derivative is automatically the prescribed derivative of that base coordinate; this face must not incorrectly be required tangent to an outward base field.

On a compact inverse image, finitely many extended smooth corner charts give a common short flow time. Flow each coordinate in a fixed order from a selected base point (possibly on a boundary face). The coordinate segment remains in the base orthant. Extra source faces are invariant; corresponding source faces follow the nonnegative base coordinates, so the source path remains in the corner manifold. The ordinary smooth field is temporarily extended across the coordinate hyperplanes in its chart to define the one-sided boundary flow; only the part with nonnegative base and fibre face coordinates is retained. Compactness and the face constraints exclude finite-time exit. Reversing the coordinate flows returns to the selected fibre and supplies the smooth inverse. These products preserve every named face and the projection exactly. A finite base cover gives genuine finite smooth bundle product trivializations, which are the additional hypothesis required by the relative-smooth bundle-fitting author.

This product construction uses the actual constant-rank, smooth partition and smooth ODE suppliers already declared in the compact primitive header. It does not import Ehresmann's theorem or presume that a proper map without the joint corner rank conditions is a bundle.
