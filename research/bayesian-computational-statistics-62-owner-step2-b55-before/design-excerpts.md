### BCS-52 — Studentized permutation tests under weak parameter nulls
**Pages:** asymptotically-robust-permutation-tests / asymptotically-robust-permutation-tests-examples. **Scope:** Under $P=Q$, exchangeability gives finite-sample exactness (BCS-51). For the two-sample order-$(r,r)$ U-statistic route, take independent IID $X_1,\ldots,X_m\sim P$ and $Y_1,\ldots,Y_n\sim Q$, fixed $r\le\min(m,n)$, $m/(m+n)\to p\in(0,1)$, and a measurable kernel $h$ symmetric within each block and antisymmetric when the $X$ and $Y$ blocks are exchanged. Define $U_{m,n}$ as the average of $h$ over all $\binom mr\binom nr$ index pairs and $\theta(P,Q)=E_{P^r\otimes Q^r}h$. Under weak null $\theta(P,Q)=0$, require $0<E[h^2]<\infty$ for every assignment of $P$- and $Q$-distributed arguments to the kernel positions, as in Chung–Romano Theorem 3.1. For $\bar P=pP+(1-p)Q$, define $h_{P,Q}^X(x)=\int h(x,x_2,\ldots,x_r,y_1,\ldots,y_r)P^{r-1}(dx_{2:r})Q^r(dy_{1:r})-\theta$ and $h_{P,Q}^Y(y)=\int h(x_1,\ldots,x_r,y,y_2,\ldots,y_r)P^r(dx_{1:r})Q^{r-1}(dy_{2:r})-\theta$, with the corresponding pooled projection $h_{\bar P}(z)=\int h(z,z_2,\ldots,z_r,z_{r+1},\ldots,z_{2r})\bar P^{2r-1}(dz_{2:2r})$; antisymmetry and equal pooled blocks give $E_{\bar P}h_{\bar P}=0$. Require positive finite $L^2(P)$, $L^2(Q)$ and $L^2(\bar P)$ norms for the respective first projections. Prove the Hoeffding decomposition, show its degenerate remainder is $o_P((m+n)^{-1/2})$ under the fixed-order second-moment premises, and derive the sampling CLT from the projected IID sums. For permutation studentization use consistent estimators $\hat\sigma_m^2\to E_P[(h_{P,Q}^X)^2]$, $\hat\sigma_n^2\to E_Q[(h_{P,Q}^Y)^2]$, and permutation consistency for the pooled targets $E_{\bar P_N}[h_{\bar P_N}^2]$, where $\bar P_N=(m/N)P+(n/N)Q$. For separate-group estimators, consistency under IID triangular $\bar P_N$ samples is sufficient; joint estimators must establish permutation consistency directly; define $V_{m,n}=r\{\hat\sigma_m^2/m+\hat\sigma_n^2/n\}^{1/2}$ and $S_{m,n}=U_{m,n}/V_{m,n}$. The standard-error pivot is $S_{m,n}$ itself: its permutation cdf converges uniformly in probability to $\Phi$ (the report instead writes $\sqrt m(U/V^{\rm CR})$, where $V^{\rm CR}=\sqrt m V_{m,n}$), and the same studentized true-sampling limit follows from its projection CLT and consistent variance estimates. Mere positive kernel second moment does not exclude a degenerate first projection. Unstudentized Wilcoxon is not licensed under a weak mean/median null by the exact $P=Q$ result. **Proof route:** The 2011 report supplies the mixture-coupling architecture, but its all-assignment centering and fixed-mixture fraction-rate assumptions are not inferred from the weak null. A local successive-revelation permutation variance bound strengthens the antisymmetric-kernel coupling using the exact triangular mixture, without those extra hypotheses. Combine this coupling with Hoeffding/Hájek projections, second-moment remainder bounds, the finite-population CLT and the stated estimator consistency conditions. Do not claim the broader 2013 asymptotically-linear/k-sample theorem's proof: its main text delegates proofs to an unavailable supplement. **Examples:** same mean/unequal variance, exact versus weak null, studentized U-statistic, degenerate first projection. **Requires:** BCS-51; `MSF-02` `def-statistic-and-estimator`; `def-identically-distributed-and-iid-random-variables`, `thm-lindeberg-levy-iid-central-limit-theorem`, `thm-slutsky-for-real-random-variables`; `def-two-sample-u-statistic-parameter`, `def-two-sample-order-r-u-statistic-and-first-projections`, `lem-two-sample-u-statistic-hoeffding-decomposition`, `lem-two-sample-u-statistic-degenerate-remainder-order`, `lem-permutation-mixture-coupling-for-two-sample-u-statistics`, `lem-pooled-mixture-projection-and-variance`, and `thm-two-sample-u-statistic-projection-clt`. The listed pair-local definitions and lemmas provide the estimand, estimator, first projections, degenerate-remainder bound, mixture coupling, pooled-mixture variance and projected sampling CLT; the planned general z-estimator theorem is not the proof supplier for this route. **Sources:** Chung & Romano (2011), Stanford Technical Report 2011-09, §3.2 Theorems 3.1–3.2 and Appendix A Lemmas A.1–A.2 with proofs, full PDF pp. 1–47; Chung & Romano (2013), *Annals of Statistics* 41(2), 484–507, full main article read but proof supplement unavailable and not a proof supplier.
