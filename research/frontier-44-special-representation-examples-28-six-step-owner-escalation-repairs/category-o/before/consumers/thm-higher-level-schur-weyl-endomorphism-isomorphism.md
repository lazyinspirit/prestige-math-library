## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $\lambda$ be a pyramid with columns
$q_1\ge\cdots\ge q_l$, let $c$ be an admissible origin, let $P_c$ be the ground
simple projective-injective of the relevant summand of the parabolic category
$\mathcal O$ of
[[def-integral-parabolic-category-o-block-for-higher-level-schur-weyl-duality]],
and let $T=P_c\otimes V^{\otimes d}$ with $V=\mathbb C^N$. Write
$B_d=\psi_d(H_d(\Lambda))$ for the image of the right cyclotomic Hecke action of
[[def-degenerate-cyclotomic-hecke-algebra]] and $B_\alpha$ for its central
components. Then the right Hecke action gives an algebra isomorphism
$$\operatorname{End}_{\mathfrak g}(T)^{\mathrm{op}}=B_d,$$
and on each central component, cut by the central idempotents $f_\alpha$ of
$B_d$ acting on $T=\bigoplus_\alpha T_\alpha$,
$$\operatorname{End}_{\mathfrak g}(T_\alpha)^{\mathrm{op}}=B_\alpha =f_\alpha H_\alpha(\mathbf a)f_\alpha,$$
where $H_\alpha(\mathbf a)$ is the corresponding block of the cyclotomic Hecke
algebra. For a nonempty pyramid, in the stable range $q_l\ge d$ the image is the full cyclotomic
algebra, $B_d=H_d(\mathbf a)$ and $B_\alpha=H_\alpha(\mathbf a)$; a single
component is not identified with the entire degree-$d$ algebra. Outside the
stable range the central-corner identification uses the admissible-origin
corner of [[def-stable-range-and-block-idempotent-for-higher-level-schur-weyl-duality]].
The maps $H_d(\mathbf a)\twoheadrightarrow B_d$ are surjective in all cases,
and the full action $H_d(\mathbf a)\to\operatorname{End}(V_c^{\otimes d})$ is
injective precisely when $q_l\ge d$ for a nonempty pyramid. For the empty pyramid $l=N=0$, the degree-zero objects and algebras are $T_0=U_0=H_0=\mathbb C$, and for $d>0$ they are $T_d=U_d=H_d=0$. All endomorphism identities hold there, and the raw action is faithful in every degree, including the zero-algebra action; $q_l$ is not defined in this branch.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The pyramid $\lambda$, an admissible origin $c$, the ground object $P_c$, the tensor object $T=P_c\otimes V^{\otimes d}$ with its central decomposition, and the Hecke image $B_d$.

[F1] The right Hecke action on $T$ exists and its generators $x_j,s_i$ are natural endomorphisms of the tensor functor, commuting with $\mathfrak g$; the image is $B_d=\psi_d(H_d(\Lambda))$, and the Whittaker functor sends $T$ to $V_c^{\otimes d}$ ([[def-arakawa-suzuki-casimir-operators-on-tensor-functors]], [[def-degenerate-cyclotomic-hecke-algebra]], [[def-the-higher-level-schur-weyl-projective-generator-and-schur-functor]]).

[F2] The Whittaker functor is exact on $\mathcal O_c^d$, its kernel is the Serre subcategory generated by the off-standard simples, $T$ is a self-dual prinjective detector whose simple socle constituents are standard-labelled and whose indecomposable summands are precisely the standard-labelled projective-injective covers, each occurring with positive multiplicity, and $V(T)=V_c^{\otimes d}$ ([[thm-the-whittaker-functor-is-exact-and-fully-faithful-on-the-required-projectives]], [[def-the-higher-level-schur-weyl-projective-generator-and-schur-functor]]).

[F3] The filtered double centralizer identifies the two image algebras on $V_c^{\otimes d}$ for every complex origin: $W_d$ and $B_d$ are mutual centralizers, and in particular $\operatorname{End}_{W_d}(V_c^{\otimes d})^{\mathrm{op}}=B_d$; for a nonempty pyramid, if $q_l\ge d$ the full action of $H_d(\mathbf a)$ is faithful ([[thm-the-finite-w-and-degenerate-cyclotomic-hecke-actions-satisfy-double-centralizer]]).

[F4] Outside the stable range the image of the Hecke action is a central block corner $fH_d(\mathbf a)f$ for admissible origins, and in the stable range $B_d=H_d(\mathbf a)$ ([[def-stable-range-and-block-idempotent-for-higher-level-schur-weyl-duality]]).



## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

**Given:** The objects $P_c$, $T$, the Hecke image $B_d\subseteq\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb C}(T)^{\mathrm{op}}$, and the admissible-origin hypotheses.

1.1 The Hecke action lies in the g-endomorphisms. For the empty pyramid, the empty tensor in degree zero and its ground object give $T_0=U_0=H_0=\mathbb C$. In positive degree $V=0$ gives $T_d=U_d=0$, and the level-zero cyclotomic relation is the empty product $1$, so $H_d=0$. The stated endomorphism identities and faithfulness follow directly, with the unique zero-algebra map injective; the remaining argument assumes a nonempty pyramid. Each generator $x_j$ of the truncated tensor action is built from the trace Casimir and place permutations and commutes with the diagonal $\mathfrak g$-action; the place permutations are natural in the module variable, so the resulting right $H_d(\mathbf a)$-action on $T$ consists of $\mathfrak g$-endomorphisms, and its image is $B_d$ by definition. Hence there is a surjection $H_d(\mathbf a)\twoheadrightarrow B_d$ and an inclusion $B_d\subseteq\operatorname{End}_{\mathfrak g}(T)^{\mathrm{op}}$, with the opposite convention fixed by the right action. [F1, algebra]

1.2 The Whittaker comparison is injective. Let $\varphi:T\to T$ be nonzero and put $X=\operatorname{im}\varphi$, a nonzero submodule of the finite-length object $T$. Choose a simple submodule $L(A)\subseteq X$; because $\operatorname{soc}X\subseteq\operatorname{soc}T$, [F2] gives $A\in\mathrm{Std}$. Exactness gives an injection $V(L(A))\hookrightarrow V(X)$, and the first module is nonzero, so $V(X)\ne0$. Exactness also identifies $V(X)$ with the image of $V(\varphi)$, hence $V(\varphi)\ne0$. This proves injectivity of $\operatorname{End}_{\mathfrak g}(T)^{\mathrm{op}}\to\operatorname{End}_{W_d}(V_c^{\otimes d})^{\mathrm{op}}$. No restriction on all composition factors of $T$ is used. [F2, algebra]

2.1 The image of the comparison. On $V(T)=V_c^{\otimes d}$ the induced map lands in the centralizer of the finite-W action, $\operatorname{End}_{W_d}(V_c^{\otimes d})^{\mathrm{op}}$; by the filtered double centralizer [F3] this centralizer equals $B_d$; and the induced map contains $B_d$ because every Hecke generator on $V_c^{\otimes d}$ arises from the corresponding endomorphism of $T$ by [F1]. Since $B_d$ is the whole target, the injective map of step 1.2 is also surjective, so $\operatorname{End}_{\mathfrak g}(T)^{\mathrm{op}}=B_d$. [F1, F3, step 1.2, algebra]

2.2 Stable range. For a nonempty pyramid, if $q_l\ge d$ then the action of $H_d(\mathbf a)$ is faithful by [F3] and the image is the full algebra $B_d=H_d(\mathbf a)$; a single central component is correspondingly a block $H_\alpha(\mathbf a)$, not the whole algebra. Outside the stable range the corner identification of [F4] applies for admissible origins, while the map $H_d(\mathbf a)\twoheadrightarrow B_d$ remains surjective by step 1.1. [F3, F4, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Central components. The central idempotents $f_\alpha$ of the finite-dimensional algebra $B_d$ decompose $T=\bigoplus_\alpha T_\alpha$ and $B_d=\bigoplus_\alpha B_\alpha$; applying the isomorphism of step 2.1 componentwise gives $\operatorname{End}_{\mathfrak g}(T_\alpha)^{\mathrm{op}}=f_\alpha B_df_\alpha=B_\alpha$. The component $B_\alpha$ sits inside the cyclotomic block as the corner $f_\alpha H_\alpha(\mathbf a)f_\alpha$ by [F4]; it is not the entire degree-$d$ algebra. [F4, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 Failure of raw faithfulness below the stable range. For a nonempty pyramid, suppose $d>q_l$ and use an enlargement by full ground rows with smallest height at least $d$. In its last column, raise the first $q_l+1$ entries above their ground values by $1$; raise its top entry further by $d-q_l-1$, and leave every other entry ground. This is column strict, has total degree $d$, and is standard: for columns with the same charge, the changed rightmost entry is at least every unchanged entry to its left in that row; different charge cosets are incomparable. The first $q_l$ rows of this column are retained, but its next row is deleted, so this standard label is noncontractible. Its nonzero large finite-W simple belongs to a complementary central block. Hence the supporting idempotent $f$ of [F4] is proper; the faithful large tensor progenerator makes $1-f$ nonzero also in the large Hecke algebra. The small raw action has the nonzero kernel $(1-f)H_d(\mathbf a)$ and is not faithful. Together with step 2.2 this proves the asserted precise stable-range criterion. [F2, F4, step 2.2, construct, algebra] ∎

## Remarks

- The key point is that the Whittaker functor is faithful on endomorphisms of $T$ although it is not faithful on the whole category: every nonzero image has a standard composition factor, and the kernel consists precisely of the off-standard simples.

- The opposite algebra convention in $\operatorname{End}_{\mathfrak g}(T)^{\mathrm{op}}$ is the one fixed in [[def-the-higher-level-schur-weyl-projective-generator-and-schur-functor]]: the right Hecke action composed left-to-right matches the left action of $B_\alpha$ on the modules of the Schur functor.
