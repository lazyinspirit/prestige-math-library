## Definition

Fix a symmetrizable Cartan datum with real simple roots (so $a_{ii}=2$ for
every color $i$), a field $k$ placed in degree zero, and weighted-homogeneous
KLR polynomials $Q_{jk}$ satisfying (Q1)-(Q4) as in
[[def-quiver-hecke-klr-algebra-for-a-symmetrizable-cartan-datum]]; write
$b_{jk}=d_j a_{jk}=b_{kj}$. Fix a color $i$. The **minimal-parabolic KLR
diagram proof category** $\mathcal P_i$ is the additive graded $k$-linear
2-category of weighted oriented diagrams described by the generators, objects
and equations below.

**Generators and composition.** The objects are the weights
$\mu\in P$. The generating $1$-morphisms are the downward generators
$G_j:\mu\to\mu-\alpha_j$, one for every color $j$, and the single upward
generator $H_i:\mu\to\mu+\alpha_i$. Horizontal composition reads chronological
negative words from the vacuum outward. Take additive sums of objects and
grading shifts, and use idempotent completion when speaking of word
projectives. The generator $H_i$ denotes **normalized projective
restriction**: in the polynomial flag realization at input weight $h=\langle
h_i,\mu\rangle$ it is basic reversed flag restriction shifted by
$-d_i(1+h)$; it is distinct from unshifted basic restriction.

**The graded 2-morphism spaces.** The weights are 0-objects. A 1-morphism
is a finite composable word in the $G_j,H_i$, with its specified source and
target weights; identities are empty words. For parallel words $u,v$, define
$\operatorname{Hom}^n(u,v)$ as the degree-$n$ part of the $k$-span of formal
oriented string diagrams generated by the dots, downward crossings and four
units/counits below, modulo the displayed local relations. Every strand is
labeled by its color, and its direction is downward for $G_j$ and upward for
$H_i$. The top boundary is the source word and the bottom boundary the target
word. Regions carry the unique weights obtained from the outside source
weight by the indicated simple-root changes. Diagrams are generic: transverse
double crossings, isolated dots and nondegenerate cups/caps, with no triple
crossing or dot at a crossing/extremum. Oppositely oriented crossings denote
the specified adjunction mates of a downward crossing, not new generators.
Use planar isotopies preserving the ordered oriented local events; moving
a crossing past an extremum uses the mate relations below. Vertical
composition is stacking and horizontal composition is juxtaposition at a
common weight; degrees add under either operation. The interchange relation
and bilinearity are imposed. A degree-$n$ map from $u$ to $v$ is a degree-zero
map $u\{n\}\to v$ with the internal-shift convention of the grading supplier.
Additive sums, shifts and idempotent completion have their usual graded
2-category meanings applied to these Hom spaces.

**Degrees and the native crossing.** Dots have degree $2d_j$ and downward
crossings have degree $-b_{jk}$, matching the grading of
[[def-klr-grading-and-diagrammatic-composition]]. The native crossing is
$\tau$, with native equal-color divided difference
$(f-sf)/(x_1-x_2)$; for expressing the source diagram equations put
$\kappa=-\tau$ for **every** color pair, retaining dots, idempotents and $Q$.
Thus the native cubic braid numerator is globally opposite to the source
numerator, while the quadratic $Q$ is unchanged. Impose the full downward KLR
relations of the cited Definition.

**Adjunctions and the rank-one matrix.** At input $i$-weight $h$, the first
unit $1\to H_iG_i$ and the second counit $H_iG_i\to1$ have degree
$d_i(1-h)$; the first counit $G_iH_i\to1$ and the second unit
$1\to G_iH_i$ have degree $d_i(1+h)$. Impose both adjunction triangles with
their adjacent input-weight shifts and cyclicity of the dot. For $h\ge0$
prescribe the degree-zero rank-one matrix
$$H_iG_i\cong G_iH_i\oplus\bigoplus_{r=0}^{h-1}1\{d_i(1-h+2r)\};$$
for $h<0$ prescribe
$$G_iH_i\cong H_iG_i\oplus\bigoplus_{r=0}^{-h-1}1\{d_i(1+h+2r)\}.$$
The off-diagonal crossing component is the cup--source-crossing--cap
transfer, and the identity columns and rows are the indicated dotted
units/counits. Adjoin its actual inverse blocks, requiring both matrix inverse
equations; the dotted dual rows and columns are the finite triangular
coefficient convolution determined by bubble inversion, and the crossing
block satisfies the local cross-and-cap equations. In particular, at $h<0$,
if $r_h:G_iH_i\to H_iG_i\oplus D_{-h}$ is this matrix, then $r_h^{-1}j_H$ and
$p_Hr_h$ are the specified natural inclusion and projection with product
$1_{H_iG_i}$.

**Explicit rank-one inverse blocks.** Put $P=G_iH_i$, $V=H_iG_i$
at the common outside weight $\mu$, and let $T:P\to V$ and
$S:V\to P$ be the two native opposite-crossing mates. The source-crossing
component in the preceding rank-one matrix is $-T$. For $h\ge0$, its
identity columns are $C_k=x^k\eta_\mu$, and its identity rows are
$$R_k=\sum_{a+j=h-1-k}A_{\mu,j}\,\varepsilon'_\mu x^a,\qquad 0\le k<h,$$
where $a,j\ge0$. The matrix is $\rho=(-T,(C_k)_k):P\oplus D_h\to V$
and its specified inverse is $\sigma=(S,(R_k)_k):V\to P\oplus D_h$.
Impose both equations $\sigma\rho=1$ and $\rho\sigma=1$; in components
these say $-ST=1_P$, $R_kT=0$, $SC_k=0$, $R_kC_\ell=\delta_{k\ell}$
and $-TS+\sum_kC_kR_k=1_V$. For $h<0$, take
$C^-_k=x^k\eta'_\mu$ and
$$R^-_k=\sum_{a+j=-h-1-k}\mathsf H_{\mu,j}\,\varepsilon_\mu x^a,\qquad 0\le k<-h,$$
again with $a,j\ge0$. Now
$\rho=(-T,(R^-_k)_k):P\to V\oplus D_{-h}$ and
$\sigma=(S,(C^-_k)_k):V\oplus D_{-h}\to P$; impose
$\rho\sigma=1$, $\sigma\rho=1$. Equivalently $-TS=1_V$,
$TC^-_k=0$, $R^-_kS=0$, $R^-_kC^-_\ell=\delta_{k\ell}$ and
$-ST+\sum_kC^-_kR^-_k=1_P$. The shift summands $D_h,D_{-h}$ are
exactly those displayed above. The row degrees are respectively
$d_i(h-1-2k)$ and $-d_i(1+h+2k)$, opposite the column degrees, so every
matrix component is degree zero after the indicated shifts. Empty identity
sums at $h=0$ give the same inverse equations. These are imposed relations
in the presentation; a claim that the concrete flag bimodules satisfy them
still requires their local realization proof.

**Bubble series and slides.** The normalized far-left bubble series
$A_\mu(t),\mathsf H_\mu(t)$ have constant coefficient $1$ and satisfy
$A_\mu(t)\mathsf H_\mu(t)=1$. Genuine negative-degree bubbles vanish. The $A$
coefficient with index $m$ is the clockwise bubble with $-h-1+m$ dots when
this count is nonnegative, extended by formal inverse coefficients otherwise;
its degree is $2d_im$. The other family has dot count $h-1+\ell$ and degree
$2d_i\ell$. Impose the finite-coefficient slides verified on the monic flag
model: adjoining an $i$ root $z$ divides $A$ by $(1-zt)^2$ and multiplies
$\mathsf H$ by that factor; adjoining a spectator root $y$ of color $j\ne i$
multiplies $A$ by
$q_{ij}^{\mathrm{rev}}(y,t)=t^{-a_{ij}}Q_{ij}(t^{-1},y)/t_{ij}$, where $t_{ij}$
is the nonzero extremal coefficient, and divides $\mathsf H$ by it. Every
reciprocal coefficient is defined by the finite recursion with constant
coefficient $1$.

**Four oriented same-color equations.** The following notation specifies
actual local diagrams, all with color $i$. The two-crossing equations have
common outside weight $\mu$; the single curls have the adjacent region
weights specified individually below.
Read tangent directions in the page. Let $C_L,C_R$ be the cap arch
(two bottom endpoints and no top endpoints), whose tangent at its peak
points left or right, respectively. Their bottom arrows, from left to
right, are $(\downarrow,\uparrow)$ and
$(\uparrow,\downarrow)$. Let $U_L,U_R$ be the cup valley
(two top endpoints and no bottom endpoints), whose tangent at its trough
points left or right; their top arrows are
$(\uparrow,\downarrow)$ and $(\downarrow,\uparrow)$.
An exponent on $C$ or $U$ counts dots on its open arc. Let $B_{\mathrm{ccw}}^b$
and $B_{\mathrm{cw}}^b$ be the counterclockwise and clockwise bubbles
with literal dot count $b$, located to the right of the cap/cup in the outside
region of weight $\mu$. Their negative dot counts are exactly the fake
bubbles defined by the coefficient conventions above, not zero by convention.
Write $\eta_\mu:1_\mu\to H_iG_i$ and
$\varepsilon'_\mu:H_iG_i\to1_\mu$ for the first unit and second counit,
and $\eta'_\mu:1_\mu\to G_iH_i$, $\varepsilon_\mu:G_iH_i\to1_\mu$
for the second unit and first counit. Let $\tau^{HH}$ be the twofold
adjunction mate of the native equal-color downward crossing. Define
$$L_\mu=(H_i\varepsilon'_\mu)(\tau^{HH}G_i)(H_i\eta_\mu),\qquad R_\mu=(\varepsilon_\mu H_i)(G_i\tau^{HH})(\eta'_\mu H_i).$$
The first formula starts and ends at $H_i$ with input $\mu$; the second
starts and ends at $H_i$ with input $\mu-\alpha_i$. Thus both units and
counits in $L_\mu$ have degree $d_i(1-h)$, while those in $R_\mu$ have
degree $d_i(1+h)$. The crossing degree $-2d_i$ makes their degrees
$-2d_ih$ and $2d_ih$ respectively. Products in these formulas apply the
rightmost map first, with adjacent weights determined by composition.
Geometrically $L_\mu$ is an upward-strand curl with its loop bulging LEFT and
its left-adjacent region of weight $\mu$. This is an endomorphism of
$H_i$ at input $\mu$, and its bubble on the right-hand side is in the
left region $\mu$. Let $R_\mu$ be the upward-strand curl with its loop
bulging RIGHT and its right-adjacent region of weight $\mu$. Its left
region is $\mu-\alpha_i$, so it is an endomorphism of $H_i$ at input
$\mu-\alpha_i$, and its bubble is in the right region $\mu$.
Both have one native crossing and the two extremal turns of the loop.
Let $D_{\downarrow\uparrow}$ and $D_{\uparrow\downarrow}$ be the two
opposite crossing mates composed in the two orders on their indicated
straight boundaries, with common outside region $\mu$. Impose
$$L_\mu=\sum_{a+b=-1}x^aB_{\mathrm{ccw},\mu}^b,\qquad R_\mu=-\sum_{a+b=-1}x^aB_{\mathrm{cw},\mu}^b,$$
$$D_{\downarrow\uparrow}=-1_{\downarrow\uparrow}+\sum_{a+b+c=-2}C_L^aB_{\mathrm{cw},\mu}^bU_R^c,$$
$$D_{\uparrow\downarrow}=-1_{\uparrow\downarrow}+\sum_{a+b+c=-2}C_R^aB_{\mathrm{ccw},\mu}^bU_L^c.$$
Composition in the last two displays is top-to-bottom: $U$ first, then
the outside bubble, then $C$. The single-curl equations are upward-strand
endomorphism equations; their cup/cap forms are their specified adjunction
mates, not cap drawings with unspecified endpoint arrows. In every sum,
$a,c$ on open strands are nonnegative. The clockwise bubble with literal
$b$ dots is the $A_\mu$ coefficient of index $b+h+1$, and the
counterclockwise bubble is the $\mathsf H_\mu$ coefficient of index
$b-h+1$, where $h=\langle h_i,\mu\rangle$. Coefficients of negative index
are zero and the index-zero coefficient is one. The nonnegative coefficient
and open-dot indices make each sum finite. The opposite-crossing equations
have degree zero: their correction degrees are
$2d_i(1-h)+2d_i(a+b+c+h+1)=0$ and
$2d_i(1+h)+2d_i(a+b+c-h+1)=0$. The two curls have degrees
$-2d_ih$ and $2d_ih$ at their respective, different input weights.

The native single-crossing signs follow from $\kappa=-\tau$; two-crossing
signs are unchanged. The literal triple-dot sum is $-2$, not the compact
$-1$ printed in Webster's Figure 2: in the full positive-weight equation,
coefficient indices satisfy $f_1+f_2+f_3=h-1$ and the bubble has literal
count $b=-h-1+f_2$, giving $a+b+c=-2$; the negative-weight equation uses
$g_1+g_2+g_3=-h-1$ and $b=h-1+g_2$, giving the same value. Reflection
exchanges the two bubble orientations and preserves region weights. The
single-curl coefficient sums instead have total $h$ or $-h$, yielding
literal $a+b=-1$. At $h=0$ the triple sums are empty and the single
curls are respectively $+1$ and $-1$ on their specified shifted input
weights. At the $h=1$, $p(u)=u$ vacuum, $H_iG_i=k$, $G_iH_i=0$,
$A_\mu=1$ and the only surviving clockwise bubble has $b=-2$; thus the
first cancellation is $0=-1_k+1_k$, with no degree or vacuum contradiction.

**Distinct-color crossing mates.** For $j\ne i$, the two sideways crossings, defined as the two
adjunction mates of the downward crossing, cancel in the two orders with the
nonzero scalars $t_{ij}$ and $t_{ji}$; scale the reverse mate by the
corresponding inverse to obtain the mixed isomorphism
$H_iG_j\cong G_jH_i$, commuting with the $j$ dot.

**Status of the relations.** These equations are relations of this proof-tool
category. Their consistency and homogeneous realization are established by
the polynomial flag model
([[lem-minimal-parabolic-polynomial-flag-model-realizes-the-graded-diagram-relations]]),
rather than assumed for the actual cyclotomic induction/restriction action; no
claim about the latter is made here. This definition uses no choice: it is a
presentation by generators and homogeneous equations, and every sl(2) infinite
family appearing in the matrix and bubble clauses is a finite coefficient
recursion from a constant coefficient $1$.
