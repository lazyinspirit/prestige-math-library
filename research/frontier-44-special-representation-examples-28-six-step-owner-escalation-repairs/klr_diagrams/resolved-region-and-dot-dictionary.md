Fix the OUTSIDE region weight mu and h=<h_i,mu>. All open down/up caps/cups
have outside mu; their inside region is mu-alpha_i. Up/down caps/cups instead
have inside mu+alpha_i. The boundary words are chronological: down/up means
apply G_i then H_i, hence composite H_iG_i; up/down means G_iH_i. Thus C_L
and U_R are respectively the first unit and second counit of H_iG_i, of degree
d_i(1-h) each. C_R and U_L are the second unit and first counit of G_iH_i,
of degree d_i(1+h) each. Bubble weight is the outside mu, not the cap's inside
mu+-alpha_i. The vertical reflection exchanges clockwise/counterclockwise
bubbles without changing mu or h.

Cautis–Lauda's (2.19)/(2.20) index their clockwise bubble by literal
n-1+ell and counterclockwise by -n-1+ell. Reflection gives precisely the

index, and ell=0 is the formal degree-zero coefficient.

    a+b+c = f1+f2+f3-n-1 = -2.
