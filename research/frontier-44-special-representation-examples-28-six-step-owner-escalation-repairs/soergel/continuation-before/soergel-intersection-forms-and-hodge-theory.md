This page develops the Hodge theory of Soergel bimodules for type $A$ in the real category of [[def-real-type-a-soergel-category-and-scalar-extension]]: intersection forms on Bott-Samelson bimodules, the deformed Lefschetz operator and the local intersection forms, then the linearity of positive Rouquier complexes, the three-case hard Lefschetz theorem and, finally, Soergel's character conjecture together with the positivity of Kazhdan-Lusztig polynomials.

The running normalization is the coordinate root convention $\alpha_i=\beta_i=x_i-x_{i+1}$ with the dominant chamber $c_1>\dots>c_n$; the diagrammatic roots of the published pages are not used for positivity, traces or descent signs. The statements use the suppliers listed in each item. The character and positivity conclusions require complete local proofs of the prerequisite Hom and support-filtration results; the Kazhdan-Lusztig basis is used through its existence and triangularity only until the character theorem of this page proves the character identity, and positivity is then deduced as a corollary. Finite computations for $S_2$ and $S_3$ are collected on [[soergel-intersection-forms-and-hodge-theory-examples]].
