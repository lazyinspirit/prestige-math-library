## Definition

Let $Q$ be a KLR datum as in [[def-quiver-hecke-klr-algebra-for-a-symmetrizable-cartan-datum]]. For $i\in I$ and $n\ge1$ the subalgebra $R(n\alpha_i)$ is generated by the polynomial subalgebra $k[x_1,\dots,x_n]$ and the crossings $\tau_1,\dots,\tau_{n-1}$ with $\tau_a^2=0$ (because $Q_{ii}=0$), the braid and far commutation relations, and the nil-Hecke dot moves, i.e. it is the nil Hecke algebra of rank $n$; its PBW basis is $\{x_1^{a_1}\cdots x_n^{a_n}\tau_w\}_{a\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}^n,w\in S_n}$ ([[thm-klr-pbw-basis-theorem]]). Fix a reduced expression $w_0=s_{i_1}\cdots s_{i_{\binom n2}}$ of the longest element and put $\partial_{w_0}:=\tau_{i_1}\cdots\tau_{i_{\binom n2}}$ (independent of the reduced expression). The **divided-power idempotent** is
$$e_{i,n}:=x_1^{n-1}x_2^{n-2}\cdots x_{n-1}\,\partial_{w_0}\in R(n\alpha_i),$$
and its horizontal reflection is $\psi(e_{i,n}):=\partial_{w_0}x_1^{n-1}\cdots x_{n-1}$. Both are nonzero idempotents of degree $0$, and $e_{i,n}\ne0$ because the PBW basis separates the monomials involved. The **divided-power projective** is
$$P_{i,n}:=R(n\alpha_i)\,\psi(e_{i,n})\{-d_i\tbinom n2\},$$
where the shift is the one fixed by the source normalization: as a graded left module $R(n\alpha_i)\psi(e_{i,n})$ is the polynomial representation, it is indecomposable projective, and with this shift $R(n\alpha_i)$ is a finite direct sum of $[n]_i!$ shifted copies of $P_{i,n}$, where $[n]_i!=\prod_{a=1}^n\frac{q_i^{a}-q_i^{-a}}{q_i-q_i^{-1}}$ is the $q_i$-factorial of [[def-quantum-integers-factorials-and-divided-powers-at-q-i]] (at $q_i=1$ it is $n!$); hence $P_{i,n}$ is the unique indecomposable projective summand up to shift and $\mathrm{End}_{R(n\alpha_i)}(P_{i,n})_0=k$. For a divided-power sequence $\theta=i_1^{(n_1)}\cdots i_r^{(n_r)}$ put $\beta=\sum_an_a\alpha_{i_a}$ and $P_\theta:=\mathrm{Ind}(P_{i_1,n_1}\boxtimes\cdots\boxtimes P_{i_r,n_r})$ along the ordered concatenation of the blocks of [[def-klr-induction-and-restriction-bimodules]]; then $[P_\theta]$ represents the ordered product of these divided powers; one may reorder only by the proved commuting/Serre relations, adjacent colours need not commute, and for a single colour $\theta=i^{(n)}$ one has the indecomposability above.

**Projective duality.** For a finite graded projective left $R(\beta)$-module $P$ put $P^\#:=\operatorname{HOM}_{R(\beta)}(P,R(\beta))^\psi$, the $\psi$-twisted graded Hom into the rank-one free module, where $\psi$ is the horizontal-reflection anti-involution. Evaluation $P^\#\to$ (dual of $P$) identifies $(P^\#)^\#\cong P$ for finite shifted free modules, and hence for every finite graded projective; the duality reverses internal shifts and fixes each normalized $P_{i,n}$ after the displayed shift.

## Remark

**Nil-Hecke matrix calculation.** Put $V=k[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$, $C=k[e_1,\ldots,e_n]=V^{S_n}$ and $N=\binom n2$. The elementary symmetric functions are algebraically independent and generate the symmetric ring: subtracting the leading symmetric monomial orbit successively expresses every invariant as a polynomial in them, and their distinct leading monomials give independence. Adjoin $x_1$ as a root of the monic polynomial $T^n-e_1T^{n-1}+\cdots+(-1)^ne_n$, then $x_2$ as a root of its quotient by $T-x_1$, and continue. Eliminating coefficients recovers the polynomial ring in the roots; each monic extension is free on powers below its degree. Hence $V$ is free over $C$ on $x^a$ with $0\le a_r\le n-r$.

Write $\delta=(n-1,n-2,\ldots,0)$. Expanding the divided differences gives $\partial_{w_0}(f)=\sum_{w\in S_n}\operatorname{sgn}(w)w(f)/\prod_{a<b}(x_a-x_b)$: the braid relations make this independent of the reduced word, and the numerator of $x^\delta$ is the Vandermonde determinant. Thus $\partial_{w_0}(x^\delta)=1$, its values are symmetric, and it kills every other Artin basis monomial by degree. It is therefore the coefficient-of-$x^\delta$ trace of the monic tower. This trace has a perfect $C$-bilinear pairing $\langle f,g\rangle=\partial_{w_0}(fg)$: for a monic extension of degree $m$, the coefficient-of-$T^{m-1}$ trace has matrix zero below the antidiagonal and ones on it in the power basis, hence determinant $\pm1$; composing these perfect trace pairings through the tower preserves perfection. Choose the finite trace-dual Artin basis $(b_a^*)$ to $(b_a)$. The operators $f\mapsto b_a\partial_{w_0}(b_b^*f)$ are matrix units, all lying in $NH_n$; faithfulness of [[thm-polynomial-representation-of-a-klr-algebra]] therefore identifies $NH_n=\operatorname{End}_C(V)$ in every characteristic.

**Idempotents, column and duality.** In this faithful action, $E=\partial_{w_0}x^\delta$ sends $f$ to $\partial_{w_0}(x^\delta f)\in C$ and fixes $C$, so $E^2=E$. Reflection $\psi$ fixes dots and crossings and reverses products, giving $e_{i,n}=\psi(E)$ also idempotent and nonzero. Both have degree zero. The map $NH_nE\to V$, $aE\mapsto a(1)$, is an isomorphism with inverse $f\mapsto m_fE$: $E$ is the rank-one projection onto $C\cdot1$, so $aE=m_{a(1)}E$. Its endomorphism ring is $C$ and its degree-zero part is $k$, proving graded indecomposability. The trace pairing has degree $-2d_iN$ and satisfies $\langle af,g\rangle=\langle f,\psi(a)g\rangle$: this is immediate for dots and follows for $\partial_r$ by expanding $(1-s_r)/(x_r-x_{r+1})$ in the trace formula. Consequently $V^\#\cong V\{-2d_iN\}$, and $P_{i,n}=V\{-d_iN\}$ is self-dual. The Artin basis degrees yield $NH_n\cong\bigoplus_a V\{-2d_i|a|\}$, since $\operatorname{End}_C(V)=V\otimes_C V^*$; centering gives multiplicity $q_i^N\prod_{m=1}^n(1+q_i^{-2}+\cdots+q_i^{-2(m-1)})=[n]_i!$. This also proves uniqueness of the indecomposable projective shift orbit: a finite graded projective over the connected positively graded ring $C$ is shifted-free, by lifting a basis modulo $C^+$ and using its projective splitting and the least-degree argument on kernel and cokernel, and the matrix equivalence transports that assertion to $NH_n$.

**Duality and induction.** For a finite graded projective $B=R(\beta)\otimes R(\gamma)$-module $M$, finite-projective evaluation gives $\operatorname{HOM}_R(Re\otimes_BM,R)\cong\operatorname{HOM}_B(M,eR)\cong\operatorname{HOM}_B(M,B)\otimes_BeR$. Twisting by reflection yields $(\operatorname{Ind}M)^\#\cong\operatorname{Ind}(M^\#)$ with the same block order and grading. The identities are checked on finite shifted-free $M$ and then its summands. Thus ordered inductions of the normalized one-color columns are self-dual, as required for bar compatibility.

**Two colours and order.** For several colours $P_\theta$ is defined by induction along the ordered blocks, so the class is the ordered induction product; blocks of orthogonal colors commute by the invertible degree-zero block shuffle, since their $Q_{ij}$ are nonzero constants, while adjacent colours are related only by the Serre relations proved later on this page, so no silent symmetrization of the word $\theta$ is made. The construction selects no representatives beyond the fixed reduced-expression system of [[thm-klr-pbw-basis-theorem]] and uses no choice principle.
