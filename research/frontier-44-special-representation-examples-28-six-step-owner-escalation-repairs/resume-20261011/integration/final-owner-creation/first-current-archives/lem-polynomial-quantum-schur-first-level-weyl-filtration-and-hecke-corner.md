## Statement

Put $A=\mathbb Z[v,v^{-1}]$, $q=v^2$, and use the actual integral tensor action and the finite normalized-cell constructions of [[lem-integral-divided-power-tensor-action-surjects-onto-type-a-q-schur-algebra]] and [[lem-integral-quantum-highest-weight-modules-identify-with-normalized-q-schur-weyl-cells]]. For $N>d\ge1$, $\lambda\vdash d$, and $W_N(\lambda)$ its Weyl module, the **first level** is $W_N(\lambda)^1=\bigoplus_{\alpha_N=1}W_N(\lambda)_\alpha$. It has an integral lower-Schur Weyl filtration with one factor $W_{N-1}(\lambda(i))$ for each removable row $i$, ordered by decreasing dominance of $\lambda(i)$. This filtration and its quotient identifications commute with arbitrary coefficient-ring base change.

Over every field with a chosen unit $v$, write $\nabla=W^\tau$ and $L=W/\operatorname{rad}B_\tau$, with the suppliers' abstract highest norm-one form. Then $\operatorname{Hom}(W_{N-1}(\mu),\nabla_N(\lambda)^1)$ is one dimensional exactly for $\mu=\lambda(i)$, and zero otherwise. The same Hom-space with target $L_N(\lambda)^1$ has dimension at most one and vanishes off these labels. Equivalently every lower-Levi primitive line of $L_N(\lambda)^1$ has one of these weights, with dimension at most one; primitive means annihilated by **all** positive divided generators.

For every commutative ring $R$, unit $q$, and left $S_R(N,d)$-module $V$, its first level carries the actual lower algebra $S_R(N-1,d-1)$ action induced on permutation modules, and there is a natural isomorphism
$$e_{\rm lower}V^1\cong\operatorname{Res}^{H_d}_{H_{d-1}}eV.$$
The quantum Levi action, when a square root is chosen, agrees with this action under the actual tensor identification and the suppliers' weight-unit conjugation. This statement neither equates first level with a simple-root layer nor asserts that an exact corner functor preserves socles.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The rings, ranks, actual permutation-module Schur algebras, weight projections, and normalized Weyl modules of the Statement.

[F1] The actual integral divided tensor action surjects onto $S$, its weight blocks identify tensor space with the permutation-summand realization, and this survives arbitrary base change ([[lem-integral-divided-power-tensor-action-surjects-onto-type-a-q-schur-algebra]]).

[F2] The finite cellular construction and weight-unit transport are the explicit arguments in the cellular-highest-induction, polynomial-universality and direct-cell-form paragraphs of [[lem-integral-quantum-highest-weight-modules-identify-with-normalized-q-schur-weyl-cells]]. The integral highest-lattice quotient and its global-basis isomorphism are not used. Step 1.1 below proves the exact needed cell interfaces without their inherited Choice. Its universal assertion concerns actual Schur modules; the lower modules used in steps 3.1 and 5.1 carry that action by steps 1.2 and 7.1. The broader finite-label-support highest universality is a separate assertion of the supplier and is not used here.

[F3] The Weyl cell is free with the actual semistandard-tableau basis, weight ranks are its tableau counts, and all weights are dominated by $\lambda$ ([[def-type-a-q-schur-algebra-and-weyl-modules]]). Permuting composition contents preserves their tableau counts by the finite bijections of [[lem-bender-knuth-involutions-on-semistandard-tableaux]]. The divided-power reordering establishes triangular spanning integrally; it does not require the freeness or full PBW-rank theorem ([[lem-divided-power-and-toral-quantum-binomial-reordering-identities]]).

## Proof

1.1 Establish the finite cell interfaces directly. Give the actual right Weyl cell the left action $u\cdot w=w\rho(\tau(u))$. Its canonical vector $h$ is highest: the semistandard weight bounds exclude $\lambda+a\alpha_i$, and its toral character is $\lambda$; the canonical cell form has $B_{\rm cell}(h,h)=1$. The cell law gives $h\varphi_{T^\lambda T}=w_T$, and [F1]'s integral surjection makes it cyclic highest. For an actual polynomial Schur module $X=U^-x$ cyclic highest of weight $\lambda$, triangular spanning gives weights in $\lambda-Q_+$, so every partition projection $1_\eta$ with $\eta>\lambda$ annihilates it. The higher cellular ideal is generated by these projections: in decreasing shape order $\varphi_{S,T^\eta}1_\eta\varphi_{T^\eta,T}\equiv\varphi_{ST}$ modulo higher shapes, with coefficient one. Hence that ideal kills $X$. Thus $S1_\lambda\to X$, $s1_\lambda\mapsto sx$, factors through the actual left Weyl cell $S1_\lambda/I^{>\lambda}1_\lambda$; its basis is exactly the shape-$\lambda$ columns because semistandard type $\lambda$ requires shape at least $\lambda$. The unit diagonal $C=\sum_\mu c_\mu1_\mu$, $c_\mu=v^{-\sum_j\binom{\mu_j}{2}}$, of [F1] transports this left-star cell to the displayed left quantum action, giving its universal highest property. On its actual weight-$\mu$ vectors define $B=(c_\lambda/c_\mu)B_{\rm cell}$ and different-weight pairings zero. The exact identity $\rho(\tau(u))=C^{-1}\rho(u)^*C$ makes this symmetric integral form $\tau$-contravariant: the right-action weight factor $c_\nu/c_\mu$ is canceled by $(c_\lambda/c_\nu)(c_\nu/c_\mu)=c_\lambda/c_\mu$. Its highest value is one. All factors are units, so its radical is precisely the actual cell radical and its simple quotient is self-dual. These are finite cellular and matrix arguments, valid after arbitrary base change and with no use of the free global highest lattice. [F1, F2, F3, algebra, construct]

1.2 Construct the corner over the original $R,q$. Put $M_0=\bigoplus_{\alpha\in\Lambda(N-1,d-1)}m_\alpha H_{d-1}$. Length-additive parabolic coset bases identify $M_1=\bigoplus_\alpha m_{(\alpha,1)}H_d$ with $M_0\otimes_{H_{d-1}}H_d$, by $m_\alpha h\otimes a\mapsto m_\alpha ha$. Hence $f\mapsto f\otimes1$, extended by zero on the other summands, embeds the lower Schur algebra with identity $p=\sum_{\alpha_N=1}1_\alpha$. It is injective because the unit parabolic coset supplies a direct summand. The lower regular composition induces the upper regular type $\omega_*=(1^{d-1},0^{N-d},1)$, so $e_{\rm lower}pV=e_*V$. On that summand the lower Hecke corner acts by left multiplication by exactly $h\in H_{d-1}$ on $H_d$, since $h\otimes a\mapsto ha$. [given, F3, algebra, construct]

2.1 Use the root convention $F_{i,i+1}=F_i$ and $F_{i,j}=vF_{i+1,j}F_i-F_iF_{i+1,j}$. In the negative algebra, its last-simple-root-degree-one part is spanned by $xF_{i,N}$ with $x$ in the negative lower Levi. Here is a direct spanning proof, requiring no specialized PBW independence. Every divided-simple-generator word of this degree has exactly one $F_{N-1}$, exponent one; all other generators belong to the lower Levi. Move each factor to its right past the current root using $$F_{i,N}F_{i-1}^{(s)}=v^{-s}F_{i-1}^{(s)}F_{i,N}+v^{-1}F_{i-1}^{(s-1)}F_{i-1,N},\qquad F_{i,N}F_i^{(s)}=v^sF_i^{(s)}F_{i,N}.$$ Other lower simple divided generators commute with $F_{i,N}$. The first identity follows from $vF_{i,N}F_{i-1}=F_{i-1}F_{i,N}+F_{i-1,N}$ and $F_{i-1,N}F_{i-1}=vF_{i-1}F_{i-1,N}$: induction on ordinary powers gives coefficient $v^{-1}[s]$, canceled with $[s]!$ in the generic algebra, leaving the displayed integral identity. The second and remaining commutations follow by inserting the root recurrence into the rank-two Serre identity and then inducting on the interval length; the only noncommuting lower adjacent endpoint is $i-1$, already treated. Each move removes one right factor, so it terminates. Triangular spanning and the highest-vector relations now show $W^1=\sum_iU^-_{N-1}F_{i,N}z_\lambda$. If $\lambda_i=\lambda_{i+1}$, the weight $\lambda-\epsilon_i+\epsilon_{i+1}$ is absent: its sorted weight strictly dominates $\lambda$, contrary to the tableau support. Thus $F_i z_\lambda=0$ and the root recurrence gives $F_{i,N}z_\lambda=-F_iF_{i+1,N}z_\lambda$. Iteration removes all nonremovable rows. Rows beyond the height contribute zero by nonnegative weight support. Therefore the claimed removable-row generators span integrally. [F2, F3, step 1.1, algebra, construct]

3.1 Order these rows as $i_1>\cdots>i_t$, so $\mu_j=\lambda(i_j)$ decreases in dominance, and let $W_j$ be the negative lower-Levi span of the first $j$ generators, with $W_0=0$. Inductively suppose $W_{j-1}$ is invariant. In $W^1/W_{j-1}$ every weight lies below a remaining generator weight by step 2.1, and $\mu_j$ is their maximum. Every positive divided generator kills the image of $F_{i_j,N}z_\lambda$, since its target weight is strictly above $\mu_j$. Triangular spanning makes its negative span invariant. The actual universal highest property gives a surjection $W_{N-1}(\mu_j)\to W_j/W_{j-1}$. This proves invariance and these integral surjections at each stage, without dividing a quantum integer. [F2, F3, step 1.1, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 Exactly one entry $N$ in a semistandard $\lambda$-tableau lies at a removable corner, and deleting it gives a semistandard $\lambda(i)$-tableau on $1,\ldots,N-1$. Conversely appending $N$ at that corner preserves semistandardness. Consequently the actual free ranks satisfy $\operatorname{rank}W^1=\sum_j\operatorname{rank}W_{N-1}(\mu_j)$. Over $\mathbb Q(v)$ the surjections of step 3.1 and this dimension equality force equality of every quotient dimension. The kernel of each integral surjection lies in the free $A$-module $W_{N-1}(\mu_j)$ and has generic rank zero; injectivity into its fraction-field extension therefore makes that kernel zero. Each integral quotient is thus the actual free lower Weyl module. Its freeness makes the successive short exact sequences split as $A$-module sequences, so arbitrary base change preserves the filtration injections, quotients and surjections. Generic dimensions were used only to prove kernels of explicit integral surjections vanish, not to replace a specialized construction. [F3, step 3.1, algebra]

5.1 Over a field let $B^-$ denote the actual lower Borel image. A $B^-$-map $W^1\to F_\mu$ is determined by its values at the generators of step 2.1; their weights are distinct, so its dimension is at most one and is zero off $\mu_j$. At $\mu_j$, quotient by $W_{j-1}$. This quotient has no weight above $\mu_j$, its $\mu_j$-space is the one-dimensional generator line by step 4.1, and later generator weights lie strictly below it. Taking that generator's coefficient, and zero on every other weight, is a well-defined nonzero $B^-$-map, since a nonconstant negative operator cannot reach $\mu_j$. This proves dimension exactly one. The universal highest property gives $\operatorname{Hom}(W_{N-1}(\mu),X)=\operatorname{Hom}_{B^+}(F_\mu,X)$ for every polynomial $X$: maps are exactly primitive vectors of that weight. Taking contravariant duals identifies this with $\operatorname{Hom}_{B^-}(X^\tau,F_\mu)$. With $X=(W^1)^\tau$ this proves the first Hom assertion. With $X=L^1$, self-duality and the injection $L^1\hookrightarrow(W^1)^\tau$ give the upper bound and label restriction. For a primitive vector $x$ of any composition weight, the functional $y\mapsto B(x,y)$ on $L^1$, pulled back to $W^1$, is nonzero by the nondegenerate homogeneous form and kills every nonconstant negative lower operator by contravariance. Thus it is a $B^-$-map to the character of that weight. The same generator bound excludes all other primitive weights, without presupposing their dominance. The form's toral weight orthogonality ensures first-level projection commutes with this duality. [F2, step 2.1, step 4.1, algebra]

6.1 Record the precise coefficient test needed by lowering arguments. If $L^1\to\nabla_{N-1}(\lambda(i))$ is nonzero, compose with $W^1\to L^1$ and with the highest-weight coefficient in $\nabla$. The resulting $B^-$-functional is nonzero, hence nonzero at $F_{i,N}z_\lambda$ by step 5.1. Every nonempty cut product $F_{i,b_1}F_{b_1,b_2}\cdots F_{b_s,N}z_\lambda$ has a nonconstant lower-Levi factor to the left and is killed by this functional. Multiplying factors by the unit toral normalization used for $\widehat F$ changes only their nonzero scalar factors on these fixed weight vectors. Thus the same zero/nonzero test applies to the normalized cut products. This argument needs neither an inherited tensor norm nor a claimed full-Hom surjection at singular parameters. [F2, step 5.1, algebra]

7.1 The summands of types $\omega_*=(1^{d-1},0^{N-d},1)$ and $\omega=(1^d,0^{N-d})$ are both the actual regular module $H_d$. Their identity isomorphisms give block matrix units $u,v$ in $S$ with $uv=e_*$ and $vu=e$. Multiplication by $u$ and $v$ consequently identifies $eV$ with $e_*V$, transporting every lower Hecke operator to its actual upper corner inclusion. This proves the natural restriction isomorphism, including $d=1$, whose lower algebra and corner are $R$. In the tensor realization of [F1], on words containing exactly one $N$ every lower divided generator leaves that position untouched and applies its subset-sum formula to the remaining ordered positions. These are exactly the induced operators of step 1.2 under the actual tensor/permutation intertwiner. Weight projections agree as well, so [F1]'s lower surjectivity proves equality of the full lower actions. The weight-unit diagonal conjugation of [F2] restricts to this subspace and intertwines the same actions after left/right conversion. This verifies the quantum/cellular comparison at every specialization by explicit generators. [F1, F2, step 1.2, algebra] ∎
