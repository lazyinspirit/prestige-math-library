102: 3.1 **The rank-one boundary.** Take $q=(1,1)$, $c=(0,0)$ and $d=1$. The ground $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ highest weight is $-1$, and its natural tensor gives the regular-block detector $T=P(-2)$. The rank-one Verma formula $ef^kv=k(\lambda-k+1)f^{k-1}v$ gives the nonsplit sequence $0\to L(-2)\to\Delta(0)\to L(0)\to0$ and $\Delta(-2)=L(-2)$. BGG reciprocity in [F1] gives $P(0)=\Delta(0)$ and the nonsplit flag $0\to\Delta(0)\to T\to L(-2)\to0$; the flag cannot have $\Delta(0)$ on top because the cover has head $L(-2)$. Thus $T$ has head and socle $L(-2)$ and middle factor $L(0)$. Let $x$ be its head-to-socle endomorphism. Then $x^2=0$, and the level-two $d=1$ endomorphism algebra is $R=\mathbb C[x]/x^2$ by the supplied Hecke identification. Any map $T\to P(0)$ has image in $\operatorname{soc}P(0)=L(-2)$: its composite to $L(0)$ is zero. Hence $\pi P(0)=R/(x)$, and $\pi^*\pi P(0)=T/xT$ has head $L(-2)$, whereas $P(0)$ has head $L(0)$, so recovery fails. Moreover tensoring the injection $(x)\hookrightarrow R$ with $T$ gives $T/xT\xrightarrow{x}T$, with nonzero kernel $\ker x/xT\cong L(0)$. Thus $\pi^*$ is not exact.
