65: 4.1 The second centralizer. Writing an endomorphism as a $2\times2$ block matrix indexed by the basis of $M$, commutation with every $1\otimes E_{ij}$ forces each block to be a scalar matrix. Thus the $\mathfrak{gl}_2$-endomorphisms are $M_2(\mathbb C)\otimes1$, of dimension $4$, while $\psi(H_1)=\mathbb C\cdot\mathrm{id}$ has dimension $1$; hence the second equality also fails. The missing hypothesis is the truncated/deformed setup: the double centralizer theorem applies to the finite-W-algebra tensor space $V_c^{\otimes d}$ over a pyramid (equivalently, to $U(\mathfrak g^e)$ with the truncated polynomial algebra $\mathbb C_l[x_1,\dots,x_d]\rtimes\mathbb C[S_d]$), where the Hecke action is the cyclotomic quotient action and the algebra on the opposite side is the finite W-algebra, not $U(\mathfrak g)$ itself ([[thm-the-finite-w-and-degenerate-cyclotomic-hecke-actions-satisfy-double-centralizer]], [[def-the-type-a-finite-w-algebra-and-whittaker-category]]).
