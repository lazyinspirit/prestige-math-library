72: 1.1 **Ext-self-orthogonality and finite projective dimension.** Each $T(A)$ has a standard flag and each $T(B)$ has a costandard flag. All positive $\operatorname{Ext}$ groups between these two kinds of flags vanish by the locally proved criterion of [F1], giving $\operatorname{Ext}^i(T(A),T(B))=0$ for every $i>0$. The actual projective sequence onto each standard has a standard-filtered kernel with strictly higher labels; starting at maximal labels, where the standard is projective, downward induction on the finite poset gives finite projective dimension for every standard. Long exact sequences along a finite standard flag then give finite projective dimension for every $T(A)$ and their finite sum $T_\Gamma$. The second flag in the Ext argument is costandard, and no standard/standard Ext vanishing is assumed. [F1, F2, F3]
78: 4.1 **The highest-weight structure of the dual.** The quotient $\operatorname{Hom}(T_\Gamma,T(A))\to\Delta_R(A)=\operatorname{Hom}(T_\Gamma,\nabla(A))$ has a kernel filtered by standards with labels strictly higher in the reversed order, and its endomorphism ring is $\mathbb C$ with head labelled $A$; this follows from full faithfulness in step 3.1 and the maximal tilting support of [F1]. Hence the Ringel-dual category is highest weight with the reversed poset and the displayed standards, and its filtered image is $\mathcal F(\Delta_R)$, not $\mathcal F(\nabla_R)$. [F1, step 3.1]
80: 5.1 **Conclusion.** $T_\Gamma$ is a characteristic tilting generator with Ext-self-orthogonal summands and finite projective dimension, the Ringel-dual category has the reversed poset with standards $\Delta_R(A)=\operatorname{Hom}(T_\Gamma,\nabla(A))$, and $\operatorname{Hom}(T_\Gamma,-)$ is an exact fully faithful equivalence from costandard-filtered objects to standard-filtered dual objects, exchanging tiltings with projectives; the algebraic realization is proved in the next theorem.
