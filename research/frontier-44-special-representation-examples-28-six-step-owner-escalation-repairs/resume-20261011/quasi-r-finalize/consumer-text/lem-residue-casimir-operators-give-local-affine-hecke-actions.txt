83: 1.2 **The Casimir scalars on flag sections.** Write $C=\sum_{a,b}E_{ab}E_{ba}$. The matrix-unit brackets show that $C$ is central and that its highest-weight scalar is $c(\lambda)=\sum_j\lambda_j(\lambda_j+n+1-2j)$: commute each positive root factor past its negative partner and apply it to the highest vector. Moreover $\Delta(C)=C\otimes1+1\otimes C+2x$ and $c(V)=c(V^{*})=n$. On a valid generalized-standard section of highest weight $\lambda+\varepsilon_j$, $x$ therefore has scalar $(c(\lambda+\varepsilon_j)-c(\lambda)-n)/2=\lambda_j+1-j=a_j$; the section $\lambda-\varepsilon_j$ has scalar $-a_j-n+1$. Thus $i\to i+1$ selects scalar $i$, and $i+1\to i$ selects scalar $-n-i$. The generalized-standard tensor flags will be constructed below; these formulas concern their sections and do not assert scalar action on arbitrary weight vectors. [F2, F3, algebra]

87: 2.2 **Multiplicity-free generalized-standard flags.** Filter $V|_{\mathfrak p}$ by its successive Levi blocks and $V^{*}|_{\mathfrak p}$ by the reverse blocks; each section is a natural or dual natural Levi module with radical acting by zero. For a finite Levi simple $E(\lambda)$, the Weyl numerator identity $A(\lambda+\rho_{\mathfrak l})\sum_j e^{\varepsilon_j}=\sum_j A(\lambda+\rho_{\mathfrak l}+\varepsilon_j)$ gives $E(\lambda)\otimes V_b=\bigoplus_jE(\lambda+\varepsilon_j)$ over the dominant valid moves in block $b$: an invalid move makes two shifted coordinates equal and its alternating numerator zero. The same identity with $-\varepsilon_j$ treats the dual. Levi complete reducibility, part of the parabolic structure [F3], turns these character identities into multiplicity-one decompositions. The tensor identity for induction maps $u\otimes(e\otimes v)$ to $\sum(u_{(1)}\otimes e)\otimes u_{(2)}v$, with inverse given by the antipode; the coproduct/antipode identities verify balancing and inverse composition. PBW makes induction exact, so the resulting filtration of $\Delta(A)\otimes V$ or $\Delta(A)\otimes V^{*}$ has precisely the generalized-standard sections for these valid moves. Their central characters are the changed entry multisets, and step 1.2 gives the Casimir scalar on each section. Exact block or generalized-eigenspace projection therefore retains precisely the specified moves, each once. [F2, F3, F5, step 1.2, algebra]
