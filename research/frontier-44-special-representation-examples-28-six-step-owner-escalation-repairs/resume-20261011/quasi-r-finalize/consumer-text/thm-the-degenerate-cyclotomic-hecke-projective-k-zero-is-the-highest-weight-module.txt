126: 1.3 **The standard lattice and its ground generation.** The standard lattice $V(\Lambda)_{\mathbb Z}$ has $\mathbb Z$-basis $\{P_A:A\in\mathrm{Std}^\Lambda\}$, the source vector $P_A$ lies in the highest constituent with $\pi_{\mathrm{Fock}}P_A=P_A$, and at parameter one $P_A=T_{A^\uparrow}=G_{\mathrm{low}}(A)$, so $V(\Lambda)_{\mathbb Z}$ is integrally generated by its ground vector $P_{A_\Lambda}$ under the divided-power operators: the actual lower lattice is $V_{\mathcal A}(\Lambda)=U^-_{\mathcal A}v_\Lambda$ by [[def-lower-global-basis-of-a-quantum-highest-weight-module]]. 130: 3.1 **The inclusion is the lattice inclusion.** The embedding of projectives into all finite-dimensional modules induces a map on Grothendieck groups; in the category of finite-dimensional $H^\Lambda$-modules a projective class $[Y(A)]$ maps to the class of the same module, which under the identification $\Phi$ of [[thm-the-degenerate-cyclotomic-hecke-g-zero-is-the-dual-highest-weight-module]] is the costandard coordinate vector of the module $Y(A)$. By step 1.2, $\pi\pi^*(Y(A))=Y(A)$, so this vector equals the image of $[P(A)]$ under the exact quotient, namely $P_A$ by step 1.3. Hence the composite $\Phi\circ(\text{inclusion})\circ\Psi^{-1}$ is the identity on the basis $\{P_A\}$, i.e. the natural inclusion $V(\Lambda)_{\mathbb Z}\hookrightarrow V(\Lambda)^*_{\mathbb Z}$. It is injective because the vectors $P_A$ are linearly independent in the rational highest module, and hence also as elements of its full costandard lattice. The normalized highest form is nondegenerate on each finite weight space; no claim that the embedded standard and costandard lattices coincide is used. This proves (ii). 132: 3.2 **The Cartan pairing is the normalized highest pairing.** By [F2] the Cartan pairing descends to $K_0\times G_0$ and pairs the two bases dually; under the identifications of step 2.1 and of [[thm-the-degenerate-cyclotomic-hecke-g-zero-is-the-dual-highest-weight-module]] the value on basis vectors is computed on the Fock side, where the pairing between the standard and costandard families at parameter one is the normalized highest pairing $C_0$ with $C_0(P_A,D_B)=\delta_{AB}$ by [[lem-the-fock-pairing-matches-projective-simple-and-standard-costandard-pairings]]. This proves (iii); in particular the costandard vectors entering the pairing are the projected simples $\pi_{\mathrm{Fock}}L_B=D_B$, not unprojected monomials. [step 2.1, F2, given, algebra]
134: 4.1 **Chevalley operators on $K_0$.** Use the actual exact residue biadjunction from the tower supplier. For projective $P$ over the source, $\operatorname{Hom}(f_iP,-)=\operatorname{Hom}(P,e_i-)$ is exact, so $f_iP$ is projective; for projective $Q$ over the target, $\operatorname{Hom}(e_iQ,-)=\operatorname{Hom}(Q,f_i-)$ is exact, so $e_iQ$ is projective. Applying an idempotent by itself would not prove projectivity over a different algebra. These functors induce the adjoint operators of [F3] under the Hom pairing. Their $G_0$ counterparts are the already identified Chevalley operators, and the normalized highest pairing makes $e_i,f_i$ adjoint, so the same identification holds on $K_0$.
