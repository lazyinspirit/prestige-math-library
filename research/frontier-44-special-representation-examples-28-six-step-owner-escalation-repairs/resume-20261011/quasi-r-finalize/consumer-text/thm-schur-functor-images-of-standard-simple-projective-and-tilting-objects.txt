117: 6.2 Simple images and heads. The finite-W simple-image classification and the genuine Morita equivalence in [F2] and [F3] imply that $D(A)=\pi(L(A))\cong\operatorname{Hom}_{W_d}(V_c^{\otimes d},\mathsf L(A))$ is simple for $A\in\mathrm{Std}$, that these are distinct and exhaustive, and that $\pi(L(A))=0$ otherwise.
