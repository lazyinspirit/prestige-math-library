79: 1.3 **The actual simple classes.** Here $\mathfrak g=\mathfrak{gl}_2$ and the parabolic is the Borel. The tuple formula gives highest weights $(0,1),(0,2),(1,1),(1,2)$ for $00,01,10,11$, with root highest weights $-1,-2,0,-1$. On a Verma vector of root highest weight $m$, induction using [F3] gives $ef^kv=k(m-k+1)f^{k-1}v$. For $m=-1,-2$ every coefficient for $k>0$ is nonzero, so a nonzero weight submodule contains $v$ and the Verma is simple.
