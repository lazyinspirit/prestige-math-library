100: 1.2 **Rectification of the ground components.** On a column cancel the simultaneous $i,i+1$ occurrences; concatenate the column words and cancel $-+$ pairs. In reverse tensor order this is the lower rule with ordinary column reading. Weakly increasing rows are stable: a changed entry violating the neighboring row inequality would have an equal neighboring entry giving a later surviving $+$ or earlier surviving $-$, contrary to the selected extremum; if that neighboring sign is cancelled, column strictness gives its matching mate in the same intervening strip and cancels the selected sign too. Apply this matching successively across neighboring columns, including unequal heights. The only highest tableau in this closed set is ground: starting at the rightmost column, absence of unmatched minus forces consecutive bottom entries from the alphabet minimum; induct leftward using row inequalities on the overlap, while a gap above it gives an unmatched minus with no right-hand mate. Raising decreases the entry sum to its finite lower bound, so the whole closed standard set is the reverse lower ground component. For the original lower order read columns from the right and align their tops; the same argument gives the reverse-standard set. Insert its reverse-column word by bumping the smallest larger entry. Adjacent insertions differ by the Knuth moves $acb\leftrightarrow cab$ for $a\le b<c$ and $bac\leftrightarrow bca$ for $a<b\le c$. Cancelling the three-letter signatures for each adjacent alphabet pair preserves their unmatched counts and selected sign, and the changed entry follows its bump path, so insertion commutes with crystal edges. Reverse insertion, bumping the largest smaller entry in the backward column word, reverses each successive bump path and preserves the row and column inequalities.
