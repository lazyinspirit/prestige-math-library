## Universal rings and the legitimate lifting argument

Let P=k[X_1,...,X_N], S=P^(S_N), and T_r=P^(S_r x S_(N-r)). The full
splitting tower is successive monic extensions. Its Artin basis has bounds
0<=a_j<=N-j. The corresponding block splitting tower makes P free over
T_r on the two block Artin bases, of rank r!(N-r)!.

There is an S-linear retraction P->T_r: multiply by the product of the two
block staircase monomials and apply their normalized longest divided
differences. Each block operator is the coefficient trace of its monic
tower, so it takes the staircase to 1 and is T_r-linear. Consequently T_r
is finite projective over S. Graded projectivity and a homogeneous basis of
T_r/S_+T_r imply graded freeness: lift that basis; graded Nakayama gives a
surjection from the corresponding free module; its projective kernel has
zero S_+ fiber and vanishes by the same least-degree argument. The rank is
binom(N,r), obtained by comparing the Artin ranks of P/S and P/T_r.

The full top-coefficient form P->S factors through the two-block
top-coefficient form P->T_r. This defines the parabolic form I_r:T_r->S.
Its perfection has an elementary module proof. Choose the block Artin
basis of P/T_r. Under P~=T_r^q, Hom_S(P,S)~=Hom_S(T_r,S)^q. The P/S
pairing map factors as q copies of the T_r/S pairing map, composed with
the invertible block Gram matrix over T_r. Since both the full and block
monic pairings are perfect, this direct sum of q copies is invertible;
therefore the T_r/S pairing map itself is invertible. This avoids an
unexplained claim that tensoring a pairing with a splitting ring proves
perfection.

The Artin matrix-unit calculation identifies NH_r with End_(k[e_1,...,e_r])
k[X_1,...,X_r]. For a monic p, the two-sided matrix ideal generated by
p(X_1) is the ideal of the coefficients of the remainder of p modulo
c_r. Indeed, first use the monic X_1 extension and then its residual
splitting tower. In the power basis the first column of multiplication by
the remainder contains every remainder coefficient; every other entry is
in their ideal. Matrix units identify a two-sided matrix ideal with the
ideal of its entries. The remainder coefficients are a unit triangular
change of generators from the excessive coefficients of p/c_r. Hence the
monic factor-ring matrix realization is justified without factorials.

An actual universal map between finite graded free S-modules that reduces
to an isomorphism modulo S_+ is an isomorphism. Graded Nakayama first kills
its cokernel. A projective splitting then kills the graded kernel by the
same argument. Alternatively, equal graded basis degrees make the
determinant degree zero; its value at S_+=0 is a nonzero scalar, so its
adjugate gives a polynomial inverse. This conclusion requires an *actual*
universal map and finite freeness of both its actual source and target.
Generic-root equality alone is not such a lifting argument.

## Integral relative-cap reduction

finite matrix trace. Define Res at infinity by coefficient extraction of
X_1^(-1)...X_r^(-1) after monic formal expansion. Each required coefficient
is a finite integral polynomial in the coefficients of p; this definition
does not use a root, analytic residue, or 1/r!.

The retained proposed full form is

    t_r^p(z)=(-1)^(r(N-r)) Res(Delta_r b_r Tr_r(z)/product_a p(X_a)),
    Delta_r=product_(a<b)(X_a-X_b),
    b_r=X_1^(r-1) X_2^(r-2)...X_(r-1).

To identify it with the normalized parabolic form, the signed
parabolic-divided-difference expansion must be installed. The cancellation
method in the retained evidence is appropriate: over universal integral
coefficients and their characteristic-zero generic-root fraction field,
group ordered tuples into r-subsets. Repeated tuples vanish. The
alternating determinant sums Delta_r b_r to Delta_r^2, whose internal root
denominator cancels. There is no factorial division. Equality of the two
polynomial forms over that fraction field then implies equality over the
integral universal coefficient ring. However, the orientation/staircase
normalization still has to be matched to the *actual* model maps.

After that identification and perfection, there is a unique bilinear
relative cap epsilon_r^p with t_r^p epsilon_r^p=t_(r+1)^p. Let p'=pq,
deg(q)=A, Q=tq, t a specified unit. The central multiplier
U_r=product_a Q(X_a,y) gives the well-defined map j_r:H_r(p)->H_r(p'),
and pi_r is the quotient in the other direction. Residue cancellation
gives

    t_r^(p')(j_r z)=(-1)^(rA)t^r t_r^p(z).

Testing the relative cap against j_r(a) and using bilinearity, the form
identity at r+1, and perfection at r yields

    pi_r epsilon_r^(p')(Q(X_(r+1),y) lift(z))
        =(-1)^A t epsilon_r^p(z).

This also proves independence of the lift. Normalizing epsilon by
(-1)^(N+1), and its dual unit by the inverse scalar, changes the p'
normalization by (-1)^A and yields the desired constant t. At r=0 the
formula is checked directly by tau_(pq)(Qf)=t tau_p(f).
