## Complete local argument and downstream proof routes

Use the existing row-coroot convention alpha_j(h_i)=a_ij. For an indecomposable
affine GCM A, the published trichotomy theorem gives rank n-1 and a unique
strictly positive null ray for A^t. Since A has integer entries, rational
Gaussian elimination gives a nonzero rational generator of ker(A^t): the
real nullspace is its real span. Change sign to make all coordinates positive,
clear the finitely many denominators and divide by their positive gcd. This
gives the unique primitive positive integer vector b with A^t b=0. Uniqueness
follows because any two such integer vectors on the same ray are rational
multiples and primitivity forces the multiple to be one.

Define c_A=sum_i b_i h_i. Independence of the h_i makes c_A nonzero. The
published generator relations give [c_A,h]=0,
[c_A,e_j]=(sum_i b_i a_ij)e_j=0 and
[c_A,f_j]=-(sum_i b_i a_ij)f_j=0. These generators generate g(A), so c_A is
central, and c_A=sum_i b_i[e_i,f_i] lies in the derived algebra. The level
of a weight lambda is lambda(c_A). In a cyclic highest-weight module the
central element acts as lambda(c_A) times the identity, by commuting it
through every enveloping-algebra word applied to the highest vector.

For dominant integral lambda, lambda(c_A)=sum_i b_i lambda(h_i) is a
nonnegative integer. If this is positive and a nonzero highest-weight module
were finite dimensional, the preceding scalar action would give
trace(c_A)=lambda(c_A) dim(V) nonzero. But each trace([e_i,f_i]) is zero:
in any finite basis the two traces are sum_{r,s} E_rs F_sr and
sum_{r,s} F_rs E_sr, equal by interchanging finite indices. Contradiction.
This proves the positive-level infinite-dimensional clause without a loop
model, Heisenberg algebra, or Weyl--Kac character formula.

If the level is zero and lambda is dominant integral, positivity of every
b_i forces lambda(h_i)=0 for all i. In an integrable highest-weight module,
the published/simple-root sl2 argument on the selected page gives f_i v=0
when the highest sl2 label is zero; e_i v=0 by highest weight. The line Cv
is therefore invariant under every e_i, f_i and h (h acts by lambda(h));
since the module is generated by v it is that line. Its derived algebra
acts trivially. Complementary Cartan directions may act by nonzero scalars;
“one dimensional” never means the whole full affine algebra acts by zero.
For affine A1, A=[[2,-2],[-2,2]], b=(1,1), c_A=h_0+h_1. A weight with
labels (1,0) exists by independence of h_0,h_1 and has level one. This also
preserves the assigned basic-level-one example without RL-13.

No choice principle beyond finite algebra is used in this local argument.
The general complete-reducibility theorem retains its own stated assumptions.
