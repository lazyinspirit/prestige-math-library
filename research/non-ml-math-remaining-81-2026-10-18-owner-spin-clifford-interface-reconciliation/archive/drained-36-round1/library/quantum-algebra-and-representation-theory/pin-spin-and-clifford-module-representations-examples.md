Worked examples verify the A-page conventions in small dimensions and at the indefinite boundary. They distinguish the squares of Pin$^+$ and Pin$^-$ reflection lifts, compute the double-angle map for Spin$(2)$, exhibit the two real spin modules from the split even Clifford algebra in signature $(1,1)$, and show why full-even Spin in mixed signature cannot be replaced by its norm-one subgroup.

## Examples

- [[ex-pin-plus-minus-and-spin-two-double-angle]] computes the one-dimensional lift squares and Spin$(2)$ angle map.
- [[ex-pin-reflection-and-scalar-kernel-in-the-plane]] checks the twisted reflection action and scalar kernel in a concrete positive plane.
- [[ex-split-even-clifford-algebra-gives-two-spin-modules]] computes the split even algebra and its two real Spin modules in signature $(1,1)$.
- [[ex-mixed-signature-full-spin-and-norm-one-spin]] checks the norm-$-1$ even lift and component distinction.
