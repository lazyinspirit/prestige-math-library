# Frontier 17 scope amendment

Recorded 2026-08-21 after the owner directed the blocked run to continue.

Step 1's independent drift review found that
`trigonometric-and-oscillatory-examples-in-several-variables` uses the surface
area of a sine-generated surface of revolution and therefore requires
`regular-surfaces-and-surface-integrals`. The latter was lower in reading order
but absent from both the published library and the original Frontier 17 scope.

The conservative resolution is to retain every originally requested pair, add
the missing prerequisite A/B pair as batch 10, and add the explicit backward
edge to the dependent page. Existing batches 1–9 remain in place with their
scaffolds intact. Only the new batch and the run-wide drift review are re-armed.
No gate is waived and no mathematical item is removed.

The first drift report and its dispatch evidence are preserved under
`research/frontier-17-scope-repair-history/` before the replacement review is
dispatched.

## Batch-seam repair

The first amended Step 1 gate run showed that the new surface pair could not
remain isolated in batch 10: its local-parametrization theorem directly uses
two not-yet-published constant-rank items. The two bounded automatic repair
rounds correctly failed to alter mathematical dependencies and then stopped.

With the run drained and paused, batches 4 and 10 were mechanically
repartitioned without changing their union: batch 4 now contains constant-rank
plus regular surfaces, while batch 10 contains the trigonometric pair moved
intact from batch 4. The pre-rebalance manifests, coverage records, proof
contracts, and full notes are preserved under
`research/frontier-17-scope-repair-history/batch-rebalance/`. Set-preservation
checks ran before the live files were atomically replaced; all six pages, all
74 items, all three coverage records, and all 61 proof contracts were retained.

## Assignment-cap rebalance

After Step 1 closed, the Step 2 Alpha correctly refused to assign ten batches:
the owner-approved limits are at most three Alpha groups, at most three batches
per Alpha, and at most two A/B pairs per Beta. The prerequisite-preserving scope
addition had exposed a planner-capacity mismatch rather than a mathematical
defect.

The run was restored to nine batches without changing any cap. The two small
algebra singletons — sums of two squares and Frattini/Burnside — were combined
as the two pairs of batch 1, and the trigonometric pair moved intact from batch
10 to batch 2. Batches 3–9 were untouched. Before replacement, all batch 1, 2,
and 10 artifacts plus the refusing Alpha's first assignment record were archived
under `research/frontier-17-scope-repair-history/assignment-cap-rebalance/`.
Mechanical set checks preserved all six affected pages and all 65 affected proof
contracts; the run-wide ledger still contains 26 unique pages. No mathematical
content or source disposition was dropped.
