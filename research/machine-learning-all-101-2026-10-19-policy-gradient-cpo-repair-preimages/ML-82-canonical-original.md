### ML-82 — Constrained MDPs and policy safety

**Pages:** constrained-mdps-and-policy-safety / constrained-mdps-and-policy-safety-examples. **Scope:** Define a finite discounted CMDP with bounded reward and cost, discount $\gamma\in[0,1)$, expected discounted return/cost, feasible policies, and a trust-region update. State Achiam et al.’s Theorem 1 in its average-divergence form: for $f:S\to\mathbb R$, define $\delta_f(s,a,s')=r(s,a,s')+\gamma f(s')-f(s)$, $\epsilon_f=\max_s|\mathbb E_{a\sim\pi',s'\sim P}[\delta_f(s,a,s')]|$, $L_{\pi,f}(\pi')=(1-\gamma)^{-1}\mathbb E_{s\sim d^\pi,a\sim\pi',s'\sim P}[\delta_f(s,a,s')]$, and $D^\pm_{\pi,f}(\pi')=L_{\pi,f}(\pi')\pm\frac{2\gamma\epsilon_f}{(1-\gamma)^2}\mathbb E_{s\sim d^\pi}[D_{\rm TV}(\pi'\|\pi)[s]]$, where $d^\pi$ is the normalized discounted occupancy and $D_{\rm TV}(p\|q)=\frac12\sum_a|p(a)-q(a)|$. The theorem states $D^-_{\pi,f}(\pi')\le J(\pi')-J(\pi)\le D^+_{\pi,f}(\pi')$; the cost analogue follows by applying it to each cost return. The main paper sends Theorem 1’s proof to its supplement, which is not included in the cached main paper; retain the full local derivation obligation or acquire/read that supplement before calling the result proved. Separate the idealized trust-region bound from CPO’s sampled linearization, conjugate-gradient solve, line search, and cost shaping. Expected discounted-cost control is not pathwise safety or guaranteed safe exploration. Proposed interfaces: def-constrained-markov-decision-process, def-discounted-cost-constraint, alg-constrained-policy-optimization, thm-policy-performance-bound-by-average-divergence, rem-cpo-implementation-is-approximate. **Proof route:** derive the performance-difference identity, bound the state-distribution change by expected TV and then average KL using Pinsker/Jensen, and apply the same calculation to each cost. Reuse published `thm-pinsker` with event-supremum TV<=sqrt(KL/2), natural logarithms and AC for the finite-action TV/KL inequality; finite-sample and policy-linearization errors remain separate local estimates. **Examples:** an expected-cost constraint with rare violations, ideal update versus sampled line-search update, and empirical near-satisfaction. **Requires:** ML-72 — Finite MDPs and Bellman operators; ML-76 — Policy gradients, REINFORCE, and actor–critic; def-measure-kernel-and-probability-kernel, thm-measurability-of-integration-against-a-kernel, and def-conditional-expectation-given-a-sigma-algebra; prove finite-action Pinsker/Jensen locally. **Sources:** Achiam et al., *Constrained Policy Optimization*, complete cached ICML/PMLR 70 main-paper PDF pp. 1–10: Theorem 1 and average-divergence definitions, pp. 3–4; TV-to-KL comparison and trust-region Proposition 1, pp. 4–5; CPO constrained update and practical approximation, pp. 5–8.
