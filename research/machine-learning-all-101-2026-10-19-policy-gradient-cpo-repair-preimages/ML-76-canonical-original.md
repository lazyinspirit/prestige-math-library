### ML-76 — Policy gradients, REINFORCE, and actor–critic

**Pages:** policy-gradients-reinforce-and-actor-critic / policy-gradients-reinforce-and-actor-critic-examples. **Scope:** Define a differentiable episodic stochastic policy $\pi_\theta$, fixed start-state objective $J(\theta)=v^{\pi_\theta}(s_0)$, and trajectory score. Under episode termination, common-support differentiability, finite expected return/score products, and justified differentiation through the trajectory law, derive the episodic score-function identity $\nabla J(\theta)=\mathbb E_{\pi_\theta}[\sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\nabla_\theta\log\pi_\theta(A_t\mid S_t)\,G_t]$. An integrable baseline $b(S_t)$ independent of the sampled action preserves the expected direction because its score term has conditional mean zero. Define REINFORCE and one-step actor–critic separately; the bootstrapped critic target can introduce approximation bias, and an expected update identity is not convergence of a nonlinear/deep optimizer. Continuing average-reward variants require the source's stationary ergodic state-distribution premise. Proposed interfaces: def-differentiable-stochastic-policy, thm-policy-gradient-theorem, alg-reinforce, alg-reinforce-with-baseline, alg-one-step-actor-critic, lem-action-independent-baseline-does-not-bias-policy-gradient. **Proof route:** differentiate the episodic trajectory likelihood under the stated integrability/dominated-differentiation assumptions, apply the log-derivative identity, telescope rewards-to-go, and show $\mathbb E[\nabla_\theta\log\pi_\theta(A_t\mid S_t)b(S_t)\mid S_t]=0$. **Examples:** episodic REINFORCE, an action-independent variance-reducing baseline, a biased bootstrapped target. **Requires:** ML-72 — Finite MDPs and Bellman operators; ML-73 — Tabular prediction and control by interaction; thm-chain-rule-for-total-derivatives and def-conditional-expectation-given-a-sigma-algebra. **Sources:** Sutton & Barto PDF pp. 343–361 (printed pp. 323–341), Chapter 13: episodic objective and theorem proof, pp. 343–347; score-function REINFORCE and baseline, pp. 347–352; actor–critic algorithms, pp. 357–359. The displayed episodic theorem proof is source-backed; the explicit differentiation/interchange conditions remain to be stated and proved locally. No deep actor–critic convergence theorem is imported.
