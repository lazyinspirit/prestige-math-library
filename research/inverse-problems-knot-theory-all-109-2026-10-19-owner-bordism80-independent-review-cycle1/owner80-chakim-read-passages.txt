Lemma 4.12. Suppose rankR H1(M; R) = 1 for each component M of ∂W. Then,
dimKG Coker{H2(∂W; KG) →H2(W; KG)} ≤rankR Coker{H2(∂W; R) →H2(W; R)}.
Proof. In [CHL09, Lemma 5.10 (1)], it was shown that the equality
dimKG Coker{H2(∂W; KG) →H2(W; KG)} = rankR Coker{H2(∂W; R) →H2(W; R)}.
holds under a stronger hypothesis. Examining the proof of [CHL09, Lemma 5.10 (1)], which
analyzes the long exact sequences of (W, ∂W) with coefficients in R and KG, one sees that the
same argument shows the desired inequality under our weaker hypothesis. For the reader’s
convenience, we describe details below. We remark that we do not use that W is an R-
coefficient Lagrangian bordism in this proof.
If ψ: π1(W) →G is trivial, then one can readily obtain the desired inequality (actually
equality in this case). Suppose that ψ is nontrivial. For brevity, let
r = rankR Coker{H2(∂W; R) →H2(W; R)}
r′ = dimKG Coker{H2(∂W; KG) →H2(W; KG)}
and denote by βi and bi be the ith Betti numbers with R-coefficients and KG-coefficients,
respectively. Using the homology long exact sequence for (W, ∂W) with R-coefficients and
the Poincar´e duality βi(W) = β4−i(W, ∂W), one can readily see that
r = χ(W) + 2β1(W) −2 −β1(∂W) + β0(∂W),
where χ is the Euler characteristic. For the KG-coefficient case, since ψ is nontrivial, b0(W) =
0 by [COT03, Propositions 2.9], and the the same argument gives
r′ = χ(W) + 2b1(W) −b1(∂W) + b0(∂W).
Therefore,
r −r′ = 2(β1(W) −1 −b1(W)) −(β1(∂W) −b1(∂W)) + (β0(∂W) −b0(∂W)).
Since ψ is nontrivial, we have β1(W) −1 −b1(W) ≥0 by [COT03, Proposition 2.11]. So, to
complete the proof, it suffices to show that for each component M of ∂W
β1(M) −b1(M) = β0(M) −b0(M).
It holds if ψ induces a trivial homomorphism of π1(M), since βi(M) = bi(M) in this case.
Otherwise, our hypothesis β1(M) = 1 implies that b1(M) = 0 by [COT03, Propositions 2.11].
Also b0(M) = 0 by [COT03, Propositions 2.9]. Since β0(M) = 1, the promised equality
holds.
□
