**Unresolved source/plan scope issue:** Arjovsky et al.'s Assumption 1 (PDF p. 19, lines 667–672) gives pointwise local Lipschitz constants $L(\theta,z)$ with $\mathbb E L(\theta,Z)<\infty$ at each fixed $\theta$. Appendix C (PDF pp. 19–20, lines 710–746) chooses a neighborhood of $(\theta,z)$, then integrates its parameter-Lipschitz inequality as though one parameter neighborhood worked for almost every $z$; the neighborhoods may depend on $z$. The stated assumption does not itself supply that common neighborhood. A witness is obtained with compact $X=[0,1]$, latent atoms $n$ of masses $p_n=C2^{-n/2}$, and locally finite triangular parameter bumps centered at $\theta_n=2^{-n}$ with widths $w_n=2^{-3n}$, supported in disjoint latent-coordinate neighborhoods of $n$. At every fixed parameter only one atom has a nonzero local Lipschitz constant, so the expected local constant is finite; at $\theta_n$, the generator law moves mass $p_n$ from $0$ to $1$, giving $W_1(\delta_0,P_{\theta_n})=p_n$, while $W_1(\delta_0,P_0)=0$ and $p_n/\theta_n\to\infty$. Thus the source assumptions as written do not imply local Lipschitzness of the Wasserstein value. The local theorem will prove continuity under pointwise parameter continuity and compactness, and prove the local-Lipschitz/a.e.-differentiability branch under an explicit common-neighborhood integrable envelope. This additional uniform-envelope hypothesis needs owner reconciliation against the approved scope; the affected WGAN local-Lipschitz and a.e.-differentiability claims will be marked `escalated`, not ready.
