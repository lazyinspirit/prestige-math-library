## Mathematical dependency and axiom check

The module sheaf is constructed by a finite distinguished-open Čech equalizer. The extra affine-open restriction lemma maps `C⊗_A M` into sections on any affine open `Spec C⊂Spec A` and proves an isomorphism on stalks using `C_q≅A_p`; thus the affine equivalence and affine-cover criterion do not import their own conclusions. Kernels, tensor and pullback are checked on affine charts and stalks. The qcqs pushforward uses finite affine source and overlap covers, the direct-image sheaf definition, the affine ring map and a finite sheaf equalizer. Its non-quasi-separated B example uses the exact infinite-variable monomial ring from Stacks and verifies that `A→Γ(U,O)` is injective while localization at `t` creates an extra glued section.

Coherence uses Noetherian finite-presentation and submodule finiteness; no arbitrary-ring finite-type/coherent equivalence is claimed. The locally free locus proof uses finite presentation to make the relation kernel finite type, then Nakayama to kill it near a free stalk. Fitting ideals use `I_r=A` for `r≤0`, zero for minors larger than matrix dimensions, and separate `Fitt_{-1}=0`; the rank-locus theorem distinguishes constant fibre rank from local freeness. `V(E)=Spec_X Sym(E^vee)` is built from a graded QC algebra and batch-5 relative Spec; local frames prove its section-sheaf equivalence. The closed-subscheme correspondence uses the batch-5 affine-quotient lemma, not either published theorem it replaces.
