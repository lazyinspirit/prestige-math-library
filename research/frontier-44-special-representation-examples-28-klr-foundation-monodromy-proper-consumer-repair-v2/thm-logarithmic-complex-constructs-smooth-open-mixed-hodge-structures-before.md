## Statement

Assume AC. Let $U$ be smooth quasiprojective over $\mathbb C$, and choose an SNC smooth projective compactification $j:U\hookrightarrow X$, boundary $D$. The logarithmic de Rham complex with Hodge filtration by degree and pole-count weight filtration constructs a functorial rational mixed Hodge structure on $H^n(U,\mathbb Q)$, independent of the compactification. Its weights are at least $n$, and
$$W_nH^n(U,\mathbb Q)=\operatorname{im}(H^n(X,\mathbb Q)\longrightarrow H^n(U,\mathbb Q)).$$

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** Sheaf/singular comparison on $U$ and the smooth strata uses [[lem-locally-contractible-metric-spaces-have-singular-constant-sheaf-cohomology]]: the quasiprojective analytic topology is metrizable and smooth coordinate balls give its contractible-open basis. The compactification from [[lem-smooth-quasiprojective-varieties-admit-snc-projective-compactifications]], AC from [[lem-smooth-projective-modification-preserves-open-cohomology-images, thm-cohomological-kunneth-isomorphism-under-finite-free-hypotheses, cor-homology-of-spheres, cor-cohomology-over-a-field-is-dual-to-homology-over-that-field, lem-locally-contractible-metric-spaces-have-singular-constant-sheaf-cohomology, def-axiom-of-choice]], and compact Kähler Hodge decomposition from [[thm-compact-kahler-hodge-decomposition-via-elliptic-harmonic-forms]]. Rational singular/cohomological comparison and functorial pure Hodge filtration on smooth projective cohomology are required clauses of that last supplier; its currently announced de Rham-only contract does not yet supply them.

## Proof

1.1 In coordinates with $D=\{z_1\cdots z_r=0\}$, define $\Omega_X^\bullet(\log D)$ to be the exterior algebra of $dz_1/z_1,\ldots,dz_r/z_r,dz_{r+1},\ldots,dz_d$ with holomorphic coefficients. Its differential preserves pole count. Let $W_a$ be the subcomplex generated by terms with at most $a$ logarithmic factors, with $W_a=0$ for $a<0$, and let $F^p$ be forms of degree at least $p$. For the disjoint union $D^{[a]}$ of $a$-fold component intersections, residues give $\operatorname{Gr}^W_a\Omega_X^\bullet(\log D)\simeq\Omega_{D^{[a]}}^{\bullet}[-a].$ More precisely the right side means degree-$k$ forms $\Omega_{D^{[a]}}^{k-a}$ with the shifted differential sign; the cohomology is $H^{k-a}(D^{[a]},\mathbb C)$. The residue records the ordered logarithmic factors, with their alternating sign. Coordinate changes preserve it because a changed local equation is a unit times the old equation, and $d\log(uz)=d\log z+d\log u$ changes only the lower pole-count term. [given, construct, algebra]

2.1 On a polydisc, the complement retracts to $(S^1)^r$. Its rational cohomology is the exterior algebra on the coordinate meridians. The logarithmic complex has exactly the corresponding cohomology classes, represented by $(2\pi i)^{-1}dz_i/z_i$. This can be checked analytically by expanding a holomorphic coefficient in convergent power series and integrating monomials: the operator $z_i\partial/\partial z_i$ is invertible on a nonzero exponent, with division by its positive integer exponent preserving local convergence; ordinary integration contracts the remaining coordinates. Only the zero-exponent logarithmic wedges remain. Thus $\Omega_X^\bullet(\log D)$ computes $Rj_*\mathbb C_U$. Pole count on its cohomology sheaves is the canonical truncation filtration $\tau_{\le a}Rj_*\mathbb Q_U$ after complexification. Indeed its weight graded local cohomology sheaf is concentrated in degree $a$, with basis the meridian wedges. Integrating a meridian gives $2\pi i$, so rational residue comparison is the Tate-twisted complex $\mathbb Q_{D^{[a]}}(-a)[-a]$. These stalk calculations give the filtered comparison; they are not merely an unfiltered de Rham citation. [step 1.1, construct, algebra]

3.1 Derived global sections of the graded complex have degree-$k$ cohomology $H^{k-a}(D^{[a]},\mathbb Q)(-a)$, pure of weight $k+a$, and their $F$ spectral sequence degenerates at $E_1$ by compact Kähler theory. Thus the rational truncation/logarithmic comparison is a mixed Hodge complex with graded Hodge-complex weight $a$. Its cohomology is a mixed Hodge structure by [[lem-mixed-hodge-complexes-give-functorial-cohomology-and-simplicial-weights]]. In the weight spectral sequence, $E_1^{-a,q}=H^{q-2a}(D^{[a]},\mathbb Q)(-a)\Longrightarrow H^{q-a}(U,\mathbb Q),$ only $a\ge0$ occurs. The degree-$n$ weights $q=n+a$ are therefore at least $n$. The column $a=0$ is $H^n(X)$; no positive-column outgoing differential exists, so its quotient by incoming differentials is the lowest-weight piece of $H^n(U)$. Equivalently the shifted weight definition gives $W_nH^n(U)=\operatorname{im}H^n(W_0\Omega_X^\bullet(\log D))$, and $W_0=\Omega_X^\bullet$. This proves the image formula. [step 1.1, step 2.1, given, algebra]

4.1 Pullback for a morphism of compactified pairs preserves logarithmic forms, degree and pole count: a boundary equation pulls back to a unit times a product of boundary equations with nonnegative exponents, so its logarithmic differential is the corresponding linear combination modulo a regular form. It also commutes with rational comparison, hence induces a mixed Hodge morphism on open cohomology. Common graph resolutions from [[lem-smooth-quasiprojective-varieties-admit-snc-projective-compactifications]] extend arbitrary open morphisms to compactified pairs. In particular two compactifications are dominated by a third; the comparison map on open cohomology is the identity and an isomorphism of mixed Hodge structures by strictness. The resulting filtrations are independent of choices and functorial. For a smooth projective compactification whose boundary is not SNC, dominate it by an SNC modification; [[lem-smooth-projective-modification-preserves-open-cohomology-images]] identifies the two restriction images and proves the same $W_n$ formula for the original compactification. [step 1.1, step 2.1, step 3.1, construct] ∎
