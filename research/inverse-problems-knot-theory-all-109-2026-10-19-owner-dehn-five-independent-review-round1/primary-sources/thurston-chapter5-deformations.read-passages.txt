5.9. A Proof of Mostow’s Theorem.
This section is devoted to a proof of Mostow’s Theorem for closed hyperbolic
n-manifolds, n ≥3. The proof will be sketchy where it seems to require analysis.
With a knowledge of the structure of the ends in the noncompact, complete case, this
proof extends to the case of a manifold of ﬁnite total volume; we omit details. The
outline of this proof is Mostow’s.
Given two closed hyperbolic manifolds M1 and M2, together with an isomorphism
of their fundamental groups, there is a homotopy equivalence inducing the isomor-
phism since M1 and M2 are K(π, 1)’s. In other words, there are maps f1 : M1 →M2
and f2 : M2 →M1 such that f1 ◦f2 and f2 ◦f1 are homotopic to the identity. Denote
lifts of f1, f2 to the universal cover Hn by ˜f1, ˜f2 and assume ˜f1 ◦˜f2 and ˜f2 ◦˜f1 are
equivariantly homotopic to the identity.

at inﬁnity of Hn which extends ˜f1 and ˜f2.
