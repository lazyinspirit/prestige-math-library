Resolvent Estimates in L∞for the Stokes Operator
in Nonsmooth Domains
Jun Geng ∗
Zhongwei Shen †
Abstract
We establish resolvent estimates in spaces of bounded solenoidal functions for the
Stokes operator in a bounded domain Ωin Rd under the assumptions that Ωis C1
for d ≥3 and Lipschitz for d = 2. As a corollary, it follows that the Stokes operator
generates a uniformly bounded analytic semigroup in the spaces of bounded solenoidal
functions in Ω.
The smoothness conditions on Ωare sharp.
The case of exterior
domains with nonsmooth boundaries is also studied. The key step in our proof involves
new estimates that connect the pressure to the gradient of the velocity in the Lq average,
but only on scales above certain level.
Keywords: Resolvent Estimate; Stokes Operator; Bounded Function; Nonsmooth Do-
mains.

We now describe our approach to Theorem 1.1, which is based on the method used by
K. Abe, Y. Giga, and M. Hieber in [3] for L∞resolvent estimates in smooth domains. This
method was inspired by the works of K. Masuda [26] and H.B. Stewart [30] for general elliptic
operators in the space of continuous functions. The basic idea is to localize the Lq resolvent
estimates for some q > d and use the estimate (1.8) to control the error terms involving
the pressure. The major issue with this method for a nonsmooth domain is the lack of L∞
estimates for ∇u, as both ∇u and p are not expected to be bounded up to the boundary in
a C1 domain. Moreover, the main set-up form for a priori estimate,
Mq(u, p)(λ)
= |λ||u(x)| + |λ|1/2|∇u(x)| + |λ|d/2q∥∇2u∥Lq(Ω∩B(x,|λ|−1/2)) + |λ|d/2q∥∇p∥Lq(Ω∩B(x,|λ|−1/2)),
used in [3], involves ∇2u and ∇p, which cannot be controlled even for C1,α domains.
We manage to resolve these issues by replacing (1.8) with several new and stronger
estimates that relate the pressure to the gradient of the velocity in the Lq average, but only
on scales above a given level. A key observation is that such estimates are only needed on
scales above |λ|−1/2 when it is used to bound the L∞norm of u by an interpolation inequality.
With the Lq resolvent estimates we obtained recently in [18] for 1 < q < ∞, this allows us to
treat the case of C1 domains without the Lipschitz bound for the velocity. In the case d = 2,
the approach, together with a perturbation argument, which upgrades the L2 estimates for
∇u to Lq estimates for some q > 2, yields the L∞resolvent estimate for Lipschitz domains.
We mention that our new estimates for the pressure, which are of independent interest, are
proved for Lipschitz domains. By taking limits in the scale, it follows that the estimate (1.8)
used in [1, 2, 3] in fact holds for Lipschitz domains. However, as we pointed out above, it is
not strong enough for treating nonsmooth domains.
