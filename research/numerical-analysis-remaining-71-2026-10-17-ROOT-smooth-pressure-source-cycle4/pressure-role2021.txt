THE ROLE OF THE PRESSURE IN THE REGULARITY THEORY
FOR THE NAVIER-STOKES EQUATIONS
HYUNJU KWON
Abstract. We ﬁrst show the equivalence of two classes of generalized suitable weak solutions
to the 3D incompressible Navier-Stokes equations allowing distributional pressure, the class of
dissipative weak solutions and local suitable weak solutions. Then, an ε-regularity criterion
for dissipative weak solutions follows from that for local suitable weak solutions. We relax the
ε-regularity criterion with a new approach using a local version of Leray projection operator. As
an application of the approach, we obtain the short-time regularity result on a bounded domain
for dissipative solutions.

Our approach, inspired by [6], is based on taking a local version of Leray projection operator,
−curl ∆−1(ϕ curl ·) for some spatial cut-oﬀϕ, to (NS). In this way, one can remove the pressure
term ∇p from the discussion. Then, a dissipative solution can be decomposed into the principal
part v = −curl ∆−1(ϕ curl u) and harmonic part h = u−v. where v is a suitable weak solution to
perturbed Navier-Stokes equations with smooth perturbation h and some smooth external force.
Theorem 1.4 then follows from the suitable modiﬁcation of the proof of ε-regularity criteria for
suitable weak solutions. We note that in [6], the perturbation terms (v · ∇)h + (h · ∇)v are
considered as a part of the external force, which deteriorates the regularity of force.
