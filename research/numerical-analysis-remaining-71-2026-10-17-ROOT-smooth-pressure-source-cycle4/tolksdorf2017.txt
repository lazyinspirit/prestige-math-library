not even known that the Stokes operator generates a strongly continuous semigroup on Lp

in [27], that for each bounded Lipschitz domain, there exists ε > 0 such that the Stokes operator
generates an analytic semigroup on Lp

answered to the aﬃrmative by Shen in the seminal paper [23].
