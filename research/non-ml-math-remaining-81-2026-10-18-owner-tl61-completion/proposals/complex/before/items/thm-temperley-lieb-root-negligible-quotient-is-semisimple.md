## Statement

Let $q=e^{\pi i/\ell}$, $\ell\ge3$, $\delta=q+q^{-1}$, and let $\tau_n=\delta^{-n}\operatorname{Tr}_n$. The trace radical is $\operatorname{Rad}(\tau_n)=\{x\in\mathrm{TL}_n:\tau_n(xy)=0\text{ for every }y\in\mathrm{TL}_n\}$. For each $n$, let $I_n$ be the two-sided ideal in $\mathrm{TL}_n(\mathbb C,\delta)$ generated by the embedded boundary projector $p_{\ell-1}$ when $n\ge\ell-1$, and put $I_n=0$ for $n<\ell-1$. The quotient algebra is $\mathrm{TL}_n/I_n$. Then $I_n=\operatorname{Rad}(\tau_n)$; the quotient is a direct sum of full matrix algebras indexed by retained paths of height at most $\ell-2$. A label $a$ denotes the image of $p_a$ in the quotient, for $0\le a\le\ell-2$, and tensor products obey the truncated rule

$$a\otimes b\cong\bigoplus_{c=|a-b|,\,|a-b|+2,\ldots}^{\min(a+b,\,2\ell-4-a-b)}c.$$

At rank $n=\ell-1$, the unreduced algebra is semisimple but $\tau_n$ is degenerate; the trace radical is the block generated by $p_{\ell-1}$. The boundary trace and the inclusion of the generated ideal in the trace radical are proved below. Equality of the radical with that ideal, the path-block spanning/counting argument, and the general fusion rule require recoupling identities not yet derived locally and remain escalated.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The root parameter, unnormalized closure trace, and Jones–Wenzl projectors up to rank $\ell-1$.

[F1] The closure trace is cyclic and factors through the specified partial trace, without division by $\delta$ ([[lem-temperley-lieb-closure-trace-component-identities]]).

[F2] If $[2],\ldots,[n]$ are nonzero, $p_n$ is a nonzero idempotent with one-dimensional corner and $\operatorname{Tr}_n(p_n)=[n+1]_q$ ([[lem-temperley-lieb-jones-wenzl-partial-trace-and-uniqueness]]).

[F3] Negligible morphisms form a tensor ideal under the pivotal trace pairing ([[lem-negligible-morphisms-form-a-tensor-ideal-by-partial-trace]]).

[F4] The Hecke Markov trace at $z_0=-1/(q^2+1)$ descends to the full diagram quotient and equals the normalized closure trace ([[thm-temperley-lieb-hecke-markov-trace-descends-at-jones-specialization]]).

[F5] At finite order $\ell\ge3$, $\mathrm{TL}_{\ell-1}(\mathbb C,\delta)$ is semisimple ([[thm-temperley-lieb-complex-semisimplicity-classification]]).

[F6] $[r]_q\ne0$ for $1\le r<\ell$ and $[\ell]_q=0$ ([[def-temperley-lieb-quantum-integers-and-quantum-characteristic]]).

[L1] The root path matrix-unit and theta-pairing statements are obligations in [[lem-temperley-lieb-root-fusion-paths-have-nonzero-pairings]]; the current carrier records the unresolved recoupling induction.

## Proof

**Proof technique:** isolate the boundary idempotent in the trace radical, then use the retained path blocks.

1.1 Since $[2],\ldots,[\ell-1]_q$ are nonzero, [F2] constructs $p_{\ell-1}$ and gives $\operatorname{Tr}_{\ell-1}(p_{\ell-1})=[\ell]_q=0$ by [F6]. The idempotent is nonzero. For any $x$, cyclicity gives $\operatorname{Tr}(p x)=\operatorname{Tr}(p x p)$; the one-dimensional corner in [F2] writes $p x p=\lambda_xp$, so $\operatorname{Tr}(px)=0$. Thus $p_{\ell-1}$ is negligible. [F1, F2, F6, given]

1.2 By tensor-ideal closure [F3], every morphism in the two-sided tensor ideal generated by $p_{\ell-1}$ is negligible; hence $I_n$ lies in the trace radical at every rank where it is embedded. The scalar normalization $\delta^{-n}$ is defined since $q^2$ has order at least $3$, so $\delta\ne0$; multiplying the form by this nonzero scalar does not change its radical. The trace used here is precisely the descended Jones trace by [F4]. [F3, F4, step 1.1, algebra]

2.1 At rank $\ell-1$, [F5] gives semisimplicity of the unreduced algebra, while step 1.1 exhibits a nonzero trace-radical idempotent. This proves the semisimple-but-trace-degenerate boundary without identifying trace radical with Jacobson radical. For general $n$, the remaining equality $\operatorname{rad}(\tau_n)=I_n$ and the full matrix-block/fusion decomposition require the path matrix units and dimension count promised by [L1]. That derivation is not supplied; these conclusions remain escalated. [F5, L1, step 1.1, step 1.2]
