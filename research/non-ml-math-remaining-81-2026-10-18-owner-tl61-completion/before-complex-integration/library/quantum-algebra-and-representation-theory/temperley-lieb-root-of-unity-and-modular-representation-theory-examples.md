These examples check one noncritical Gram radical, one critical simple standard module, and one Spencer digit-support calculation. Each verification states its exact parameter and label convention.

The first example’s TikZ half-diagrams specify the two noncrossing pairings. Their labels are the mathematical objects; the loop-count proof computes the Gram entries independently of the picture.

## Examples

- [[ex-temperley-lieb-order-three-noncritical-gram-radical]] computes the 2-by-2 Gram matrix at rank $n=4$ and its one-dimensional radical at $q^2$ of order three; its label is noncritical.
- [[ex-temperley-lieb-order-three-critical-standard-module]] checks a rank-seven, five-defect critical standard module and its nonzero determinant at the same root.
- [[ex-temperley-lieb-spencer-support-digits-eleven]] expands $12$ in the $(\ell,P)=(3,3)$ scales and obtains $\{11,5\}$; it makes no composition-factor claim without Spencer’s separate theorem.

These are finite verifications, not suppliers for the unresolved noncritical classification, Spencer decomposition theorem, or TLJ fusion and tilting-centralizer claims.
