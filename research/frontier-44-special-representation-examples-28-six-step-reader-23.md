### 1. `thm-parabolic-kl-polynomials-give-the-dual-canonical-transition-matrix`

**Location:** Proof 1.1 (line 95), both the sorting biconditional and the description of J.

**Class / severity:** false-claim / fatal.

Being minimal-right modulo K does not characterize sorting a fixed tuple: for a=(1,0) with distinct entries, K is trivial, every permutation is minimal-right, and only the transposition sorts a. Also the column Levi J permutes positions within each column, rather than permuting columns. State that the sorting permutations form one right K-coset and choose its unique minimum; correct the J action.
