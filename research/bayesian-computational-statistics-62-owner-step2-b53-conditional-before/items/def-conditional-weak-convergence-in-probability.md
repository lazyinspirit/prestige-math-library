## Definition

Let $E$ be a separable complete metric space, let $\mathcal D_n$ be the sigma-algebra generated by the observed data at sample size $n$, and let $Z_n^*$ be a bootstrap random element whose remaining randomness is the resampling randomization. Write $\mathcal L^*(Z_n^*)$ for its conditional Borel probability law given $\mathcal D_n$. Choose a metric $d_w$ on Borel probability measures on $E$ such that $d_w(\mu_n,\mu)\to0$ exactly when $\mu_n\Rightarrow\mu$ in the sense of [[def-weak-convergence-of-borel-probability-measures]]. For a fixed Borel probability measure $\mu$ on $E$, we say that $Z_n^*$ converges conditionally weakly in probability to $\mu$ when

$$d_w\!\left(\mathcal L^*(Z_n^*),\mu\right)\longrightarrow 0\quad\hbox{in probability}.$$

We use this definition only on separable Hilbert spaces and their finite products.

## Remarks

The conditioning fixes the observations and averages only over bootstrap randomization. A conditional limit in probability is not an assertion of almost-sure conditional convergence.
