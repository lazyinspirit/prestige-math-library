## Dependency and mathematical audit

Conventions used throughout: the library's descending convention (negative Riemannian gradient, index = number of negative Hessian squares); a `k`-handle has attaching sphere `S^{k-1}` and belt sphere `S^{n-k-1}`; in the middle level after the `k`-handles the two families have complementary dimension `k + (n-k-1) = n-1`, and the endpoint cases `k = 0` and `k = n-1` are the ones of the DT-7 cancelling-pair and matrix definitions. Coefficients are a field `F` for the polynomial identity and its corollaries; the Euler identity is field independent. `AC_ω` is stated on every proved item that uses in-run handle theory, with `def-countable-choice` in `deps`; definitions and the coefficient-dependence remark carry no proof obligation.

The load-bearing mathematical checks:

- **Item 3** is the rank decomposition `dim A_k − dim B_k + dim C_k = α_k + α_{k-1} ≥ 0` for a long exact sequence of finite-dimensional vector spaces, with `α_k = dim ker(A_k→B_k)`, and it also propagates finite-dimensionality from `A,C` to `B`. This is Nicolaescu Lemma 2.3.2 and Remark 2.3.1, and the same bookkeeping Wall and Ritter use.
- **Item 4(a)** is the handle-attachment form of the published single-handle corollary (collar excision plus the core retraction), including the identification of the relative generator with the core class and of the connecting map with the attaching-sphere class. **Item 4(b)** uses DT-5's simultaneous attachment plus excision and the published direct-sum splitting of a disjoint union of relative complexes; the core classes form a basis and dimensions add. No orientation or Morse–Smale hypothesis is needed.
- **Item 5** slices the compact manifold between consecutive critical values. Each slab is compact and its critical points share one value, so item 4(b) gives the slabs' graded dimensions; the pair long exact sequence with item 3 gives `P_{i-1} + P_{i,i-1} = P_i + (1+t)Q_i` with nonnegative coefficients and propagates finiteness from `M_0 = ∅`; summing and telescoping yields the identity with `Q = Σ Q_i`, and the left side is the Morse polynomial. Uniqueness of `Q` follows because `Z[t]` is a domain. Compactness is used exactly for finiteness of the critical set and of the bands.
- **Item 12** is the same argument relative to `M_0`: the separation lemma makes the critical values distinct with perturbations supported near interior critical points (so adaptedness is preserved), the first slab is a product collar with vanishing relative homology, each later slab is one handle by DT-6's interior-slab lemma, and the triple sequence (local lemma) keeps the bookkeeping relative to `M_0`. The top collar identifies `(W,M_0)` with `(W^{1-ε},M_0)`, and boundary critical points would break the count, which the statement excludes.
- **Item 11** proves the equality case in both required directions: `M_f = P_{M,F} + (1+t)Q` with nonnegative coefficients makes perfectness equivalent to `Q = 0`, and item 3 applied to a handle chain complex (whose `k`-th group has dimension `m_k`) makes perfectness equivalent to the vanishing of all handle boundary maps. No identification of the two correction polynomials is asserted.
- **Item 14** defines the handle chain complex by the triple connecting maps and proves `H_*(C) ≅ H_*(M;F)` by transposing the library's proof that cellular homology computes singular homology to the finite handle filtration, using item 4 for the relative groups and the higher-index lemma for stabilization. The index-ordered presentation comes from DT-6's correspondence and rearrangement, applied to the closed manifold through the empty-face convention of the triad definition (ledger rows 1, 3–6 below).
- **Item 13** is Milnor's Corollary 7.3: the triple boundary is the composite of the excision isomorphism, the inclusion, and the pair boundary, and Lemma 7.2 expands the inclusion image of a closed submanifold of the middle level in the left-hand disk basis with coefficients the intersection numbers with the right-hand spheres. In the library's language the right-hand sphere is the belt sphere of the lower handle and the left-hand sphere the attaching sphere of the upper handle; the local sign is the local degree of the collapse map, and the product-chart lemma plus the dual retraction supply the local model. Reducing mod two gives the mod-two statement. The formula's convention (framings and the induced boundary orientation of the middle level) is the one of the DT-7 matrix definition.
