The examples compute the sharp $M/\alpha$ factor for a nonsymmetric coercive
form, verify the symmetric energy-best property on a two-cell Poisson mesh,
and construct an $L^2$-forced weak solution on an L-shaped domain that is not
in $H^2$. The corner example shows why the extra Aubin–Nitsche order needs an
actual adjoint-regularity hypothesis; it does not assert a specific lower
convergence rate for every reentrant-corner mesh.
