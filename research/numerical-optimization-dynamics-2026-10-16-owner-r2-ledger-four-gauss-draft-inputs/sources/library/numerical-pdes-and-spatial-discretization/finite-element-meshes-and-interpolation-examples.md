These examples compute an affine triangle and a parallelogram image, compare a shape-regular equilateral mesh family with a degenerating sliver family on a fixed square, and write the oriented boundaries of two triangles to verify shared-edge cancellation. A smooth logarithmic cutoff sequence has a fixed interior point value while its two-dimensional H1 norm tends to zero, showing why the point-evaluation threshold is strict.
