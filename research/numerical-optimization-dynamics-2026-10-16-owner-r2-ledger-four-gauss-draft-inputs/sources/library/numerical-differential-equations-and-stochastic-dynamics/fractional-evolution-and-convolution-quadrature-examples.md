## Objective

These examples compute first steps for scalar fractional relaxation, expose
the initial singularity in $u(t)=t^{1/2}$, instantiate the graded-grid formula,
and compare full-history work with an exactly computed truncated-memory bias.
They make no optimal graded-mesh or accelerated-convolution claim.
