The narrow route that preserves every load-bearing mathematical assertion is:

1. Rehome `lem-compact-cw-images-have-finite-cell-support-without-choice` from the Hurewicz page to `cw-complexes-and-cellular-homology`, immediately after `def-skeleta-cw-subcomplex-and-relative-cw-complex` and before `thm-relative-homology-of-consecutive-cw-skeleta`. Its actual dependencies are CW definitions and earlier compactness theorems. This also places agent 04's uniqueness supplier before the homology-axioms page.
2. Rehome `thm-relative-cellular-homology-computes-relative-singular-homology` from the CW page to the Hurewicz page, immediately after `lem-cw-quotient-induces-relative-singular-homology-isomorphisms`. Its dependency on that quotient theorem is essential, and it has no direct item consumers in the current plan.
3. Add `simplicial-subdivision-and-simplicial-approximation` to `homology-axioms-degree-and-classical-applications.requires`. It precedes the latter page and supplies the published common-linear-refinement lemma.
4. The remaining two backward dependencies cannot be removed by a small page move. The shard-09 simplicial/singular theorem needs a focused proof repair: for finite simplicial subcomplex inclusions, barycentric open-star normalization explicitly gives a neighborhood deformation retract, so the earlier `cor-homology-of-good-pairs-is-reduced-homology-of-the-quotient` proves the two relative-simplex/attachment excisions. For arbitrary weak realizations, select one point canonically in each occupied open simplex: take the least rational interior threshold with nonempty compact intersection with the compact singular image, then its finite-dimensional lexicographic minimum. All subsets of these points are weakly closed because a closed simplex has finitely many faces; the selected set is compact discrete and therefore finite. This proves the needed choice-free finite-support fact locally, replacing the later Hurewicz supplier rather than silently dropping it. Add `simplicial-subdivision-and-simplicial-approximation` to the relative-homology page's `requires` if its barycentric-star construction is cited there. The theorem's Statement need not change.
5. The current `thm-relative-homology-of-consecutive-cw-skeleta` likewise needs a focused earlier proof of the quotient step. The radial collar `X^{n-1}\cup\bigcup_e\chi_e\{\|x\|>1/2\}` is an open neighborhood of the lower skeleton in `X^n` and retracts to it by radial projection, fixing the boundary. Thus the earlier good-pair quotient corollary gives `H_*(X^n,X^{n-1})\cong\widetilde H_*(\bigvee_e S^n)`. For a finite subwedge, the complementary-sphere basepoint collar gives the same good-pair comparison used in the existing finite-wedge induction. The rehomed choice-free compact-support lemma justifies passage to arbitrary wedges. This replaces the later general CW quotient supplier with a proof specific to skeletal pairs; the theorem's Statement need not change. Sync the actual resulting dependency list into the plan.

After these focused proof replacements, the intended page order remains relative homology → CW cellular homology → homology axioms → higher homotopy → fibrations → Hurewicz, with only the two item rehomes and two earlier `requires` additions above. A page-only solution without proof replacements would require splitting the relative-homology, CW, homology-axioms, higher-homotopy and Hurewicz pages into early and late fragments, because each contains consumers and suppliers on both sides of the cycle. The focused replacements are the smaller reviewable change.
