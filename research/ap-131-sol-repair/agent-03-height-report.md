The central correction is `lem-polynomial-ring-dimension-upper-bound`. Under the Axiom of Choice, its exact relative-height formula follows after localizing at a prime of finite height, using the localized converse height theorem to choose generators for the maximal ideal, and applying the height theorem to the extended generators and one fibre polynomial. The height-zero case is explicit. This now supplies a genuine proof of `thm-dimension-formula-for-affine-domains`: induction proves the height/transcendence-degree formula for polynomial rings, then Noether normalization and height preservation under going down transfer it to any affine domain. The old proof's [F1] had simply asserted the target dimension formula, so it was removed.

The general polynomial-ring height and affine height-formula Statements now explicitly assume Choice, as do load-bearing ring-theoretic descendants. The classical-variety consumers already had an Axiom of Choice convention in their public Statements; their Facts now quote the qualified algebra suppliers exactly. This prevents a large unrelated branch from acquiring an artificial Choice premise. The affine normalization lower bound now uses the new `lem-module-finite-domain-extension-lifts-finite-prime-chains`. Its determinant/localization proof constructs a nonzero finite-dimensional residue algebra, chooses a maximal proper ideal by its bounded vector-space dimension, and repeats this finite construction along the supplied chain. It requires no general maximal-ideal theorem or choice principle. Consequently `thm-affine-domain-dimension-transcendence-degree` and the normalization upper bound keep their original unconditional Statements and proofs. The cusp, quotient-height, localization, plane-curve, sharp-height, and zero-ring examples were similarly recomputed directly, removing unnecessary conditional citations.
