## Statement

For a finite group $G$, $C_G(F^*(G))\le F^*(G)$.

## Source status

Smith, *CFSG—A User’s Manual*, printed p. 23, states this property and cites
Aschbacher, §31.13. That page does not prove the property. The earlier local
argument only restated the same theorem as an assumption, so this item records
an external result without a local proof.
