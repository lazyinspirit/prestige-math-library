The library proves elementary family results and records
[[thm-generalized-fitting-subgroup-contains-its-centralizer]] from Smith, but
does not give an independent proof of that theorem: its published proof quotes
Smith's equivalent self-centralizing assertion as a premise. The library also
does not prove classification, recognition, order formulae, character tables,
or sporadic constructions.
