Explicit coordinate and power-series rings illustrate regularity and completion. The cusp, split node, and dual numbers distinguish Krull dimension from embedding dimension, while Koszul and periodic resolutions make finite and infinite projective dimension concrete. The finite regular-base example proves the Cohen–Macaulay/freeness criterion, and the final flat map exhibits a singular closed fibre between regular local rings.
