
ITEM alg-qa-37-exhaustive-b6-block-pair-compiler


## Definition

**Inputs:** certified Cauchy names for targets $T_5\in SU(5)$ and
$T_8\in SU(8)$; a positive binary-rational tolerance $\varepsilon$; and the
fixed normalized six-strand Fibonacci B6 generators in both total-charge
blocks. Enumerate all finite Artin words over the six-strand generators and
their inverses, and at stage $s=1,2,\ldots$ inspect every word of length at
most $s$ and every integer precision $1\le p\le s$ not previously inspected,
in fixed lexicographic order. For each word $b$ and precision $p$, compute rational complex
matrix approximants $P_{5,b,p},P_{8,b,p}$ to its exact normalized block
matrices and request rational target approximants $Q_{5,p},Q_{8,p}$. Each
real and imaginary entry error in these approximants is certified at most
$2^{-p}$. For $d\in\{5,8\}$ compute the rational number
$$s_{d,b,p}=\sum_{i,j}\Bigl(|\Re(P_{d,b,p}-Q_{d,p})_{ij}| +|\Im(P_{d,b,p}-Q_{d,p})_{ij}|\Bigr).$$
Return the first word for which both rational tests
$$s_{d,b,p}+4d\,2^{-p}<\varepsilon\qquad(d=5,8)$$
hold. Output the word, its exponent sum, both rational certificates, and the
measured search/precision costs in the ledger of
[[def-qa-37-resource-cost-model]]. The braid matrices have computable
algebraic entries from the fixed Fibonacci $F/R$ data
[[lem-qc-tqc-fibonacci-b6-path-projectors-and-artin]]. This is a dovetailed
partial search definition: no cutoff or useful word-length/time bound is
asserted here. A separate theorem in this pair proves termination under certified target
access. The returned matrices are
normalized by the common $R_1$ scalar; their physical phase is recorded by
[[def-qa-37-phase-reference-cost]].

For this compiler subroutine's ledger record, $N_{\rm any}=6$,
$\epsilon_{\rm op}=\varepsilon$, $N_{\rm meas}=B_{\rm out}=0$, and
$N_{\rm phase}=t_{\rm phase}=0$ because it performs no measurement or phase
correction. Preparation, ancilla, Hamiltonian-time, transport, measurement-
implementation and physical-leakage coordinates are “not applicable” because
this mathematical search supplies no physical realization. $L_{\rm in}$,
$|b|$, $b_{\max}$, and $t_{\rm cl}$ record the encoded target/compiler inputs,
returned word, greatest precision request, and measured search/postprocessing
bit time.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The exact normalized braid matrices, rational approximants with
entrywise coordinate error at most $2^{-p}$, and the two accepted rational
tests.

[F1] The certified fixed-dimensional input supplies rational complex matrix
approximants whose real and imaginary coordinates each have error at most
$2^{-p}$. [[def-qa-37-certified-matrix-input]]

[F2] For a finite matrix, the operator norm is at most the Frobenius norm.
[[thm-spectral-and-frobenius-norms-are-unitarily-invariant-with-singular-value-formulas-and-rank-comparison]]

[F3] The normalized B6 matrices are fixed finite-dimensional algebraic
matrices in the declared total-charge blocks.
[[lem-qc-tqc-fibonacci-b6-path-projectors-and-artin]]

## Proof

1.1 For any $d\times d$ complex matrix whose real and imaginary entry coordinates have errors at most $2^{-p}$, the Frobenius norm of the error is at most $d\sqrt2\,2^{-p}$. Apply this to the word approximant and target approximant using [F1]; their two errors total at most $2d\sqrt2\,2^{-p}<3d\,2^{-p}$. The triangle inequality for the Euclidean entry norm therefore gives $\|B_d(b)-T_d\|_F\le\|P_{d,b,p}-Q_{d,p}\|_F+3d2^{-p}$. [F1, F3, given, algebra]

2.1 The sum of the absolute real and imaginary coordinates in $s_{d,b,p}$ dominates the Frobenius norm of $P_{d,b,p}-Q_{d,p}$. By [F2], $\|B_d(b)-T_d\|_{\rm op}\le\|B_d(b)-T_d\|_F\le s_{d,b,p}+3d2^{-p}<s_{d,b,p}+4d2^{-p}<\varepsilon$. Thus each accepted rational test certifies the strict operator-norm bound in its block. [F2, step 1.1, given, algebra] ∎

ITEM def-qa-37-finite-adiabatic-braid-input


## Definition

A **finite adiabatic braid input** consists of a finite-dimensional complex
Hilbert space $V$, a $C^2$ Hermitian path $H(s)$ on $0\le s\le1$, a
norm-continuous constant-rank orthogonal spectral projection $P(s)$ onto a
coherently followed band with uniform positive full gap, a slow-time schedule
$T>0$, and an explicit initial isometry $A_0:\mathbb C^m\to\operatorname{ran}P(0)$.
These are the path and propagator hypotheses of
[[def-qa-36-finite-adiabatic-path-model]]; the output propagator $U_T$ solves
$iU_T'(s)=TH(s)U_T(s)$. Also supply an endpoint isometry
$A_1:\mathbb C^m\to\operatorname{ran}P(1)$ and a specified Fibonacci braid
word $b$ with physical action $\rho(b)$ from
[[def-qc-tqc-physical-braid-interface]] in the fixed convention of
[[ex-qc-tqc-fibonacci-data]], and a unitary target
$\rho_{\rm log}(b)\in U(\mathbb C^m)$ in the declared logical input/output
frames. The target $\rho_{\rm log}(b)$ is the selected categorical $F/R$
action written in those frames, including the phase convention recorded by
[[def-qa-37-phase-reference-cost]]; specifying it does not assert that the
physical path realizes it. The **in-band transport condition** is the separate
hypothesis
$$\|P(1)U_T(1)A_0-A_1\rho_{\rm log}(b)\|_{\rm op}\le\epsilon_{\rm tr}.$$
This condition is not a consequence of a projector-leakage estimate. The
model also specifies a finite output measurement and, if needed, its own
implementation-variation bound. The JRS expression and leakage consequence
are handled as a separate result.

ITEM lem-qa-37-fixed-sector-global-phase


## Statement

For every Fibonacci braid word $b$ with exponent sum $k$, its physical
operator differs from the normalized operator by the common scalar
$R_1^k$ on every fixed-charge block. On the three-anyon SU(2)-lift alphabet
$zB_j$, the corresponding scalar is $(R_1z^{-1})^k$. The scalar leaves
uncontrolled fixed-sector measurement probabilities unchanged; under a
coherent control it is a relative phase and cannot be discarded without a
phase-reference correction.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The fixed Fibonacci $F/R$ convention, a finite Artin word $b$, its
exponent sum $k$, and either an uncontrolled fixed-sector use or a controlled
use.

[F1] The Fibonacci determinant-character supplier proves that exponent sum
records the same $R_1$ scalar on every charge block and that a controlled
scalar becomes relative. [[lem-qc-tqc-determinant-character-phase]]

[F2] An isolated unit-modulus scalar does not change measurement
probabilities. [[thm-qc-standalone-global-phase-invariance]]

[F3] A scalar applied only on the active coherent-control branch can change
measurement probabilities. [[thm-qc-controlled-global-phase-is-relative-phase]]

## Proof

1.1 Let $b=\sigma_{i_1}^{\varepsilon_1}\cdots\sigma_{i_m}^{\varepsilon_m}$, where $\varepsilon_j\in\{1,-1\}$. Each generator contributes $R_1$ and its inverse contributes $R_1^{-1}$, so their scalar product is $R_1^{\sum_j\varepsilon_j}=R_1^k$. The Artin relations preserve exponent sum, hence this factor is independent of the word representative by [F1]. For $\widehat B_j=zB_j$, the normalized product contributes $z^k$; solving for the physical matrix gives $(R_1z^{-1})^k\widehat B(b)$. [given, F1, algebra]

2.1 On a standalone fixed sector the factor $R_1^k$ (or $(R_1z^{-1})^k$ for the lifted alphabet) has modulus one, so [F2] gives identical outcome probabilities. Under coherent control only the active branch receives this scalar, so [F3] shows that the relative phase can change outcomes; the exact scalar and correction cost must therefore be recorded as in the phase-reference definition. [F1, F2, F3, step 1.1] ∎

ITEM thm-qa-37-exhaustive-b6-compiler-terminates


## Statement

Assume $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$. For every certified Cauchy input pair
$(T_5,T_8)\in SU(5)\times SU(8)$ and every positive rational
$\varepsilon$, the exhaustive compiler of
[[alg-qa-37-exhaustive-b6-block-pair-compiler]] halts with one six-strand
Fibonacci braid word whose normalized total-charge-$1$ and total-charge-$\tau$
matrices are each within operator norm $\varepsilon$ of $T_5,T_8$,
respectively. Density proves termination, but this result gives no useful
joint-block word-length or search-time bound.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The normalized B6 representation, $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$, certified
Cauchy names for $T_5,T_8$, and rational $\varepsilon>0$.

[F1] The joint closure of the normalized B6 image contains
$SU(5)\times SU(8)$ under ACω. [[lem-qc-tqc-b6-joint-su5-su8-density]]

[F2] Each target name returns rational complex matrices converging entrywise
with the stated certified precision. [[def-qa-37-certified-matrix-input]]

[F3] The compiler enumerates every finite B6 word and dovetails all
precision levels; an accepted certificate implies both operator errors are
strictly below $\varepsilon$.
[[alg-qa-37-exhaustive-b6-block-pair-compiler]]

[F4] For a $d\times d$ matrix, $\|A\|_F\le\sqrt d\|A\|_{\rm op}$, and $\|A\|_{\rm op}\le\|A\|_F$.
[[thm-spectral-and-frobenius-norms-are-unitarily-invariant-with-singular-value-formulas-and-rank-comparison]]

[A1] Assume exactly $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$ as used by [F1].
[[def-countable-choice]]

## Proof

1.1 The product of open operator-norm $\varepsilon/128$-balls around $T_5,T_8$ is a neighborhood in $SU(5)\times SU(8)$. By [A1] and [F1], it meets the normalized B6 image, so there is one finite braid word $b_*$ with both block operator errors less than $\varepsilon/128$. For dimension $d\le8$, the real-coordinate $\ell^1$ norm of a complex matrix is at most $\sqrt2 d\|A\|_F\le\sqrt2 d^{3/2}\|A\|_{\rm op}\le32\|A\|_{\rm op}$ by [F4]. Hence the exact coordinate-sum distance in either block is strictly less than $\varepsilon/4$. [A1, F1, F4, given, choose, algebra]

2.1 Fix this finite word. Its exact block matrices and the targets have coordinate approximants converging to their entries by [F2]–[F3]. Therefore $s_{d,b_*,p}$ converges to the real-coordinate $\ell^1$ distance between the exact word block and target; by step 1.1 that limit is less than $\varepsilon/4$. Since $4d2^{-p}\to0$, some finite $p$ makes both certificates strictly below $\varepsilon$. Dovetailing eventually examines $(b_*,p)$, and certificate soundness [F3] ensures acceptance. Thus the procedure halts. ACω enters only through [A1] and [F1]; density gives no search-index bound. [F2, F3, A1, step 1.1, algebra, F1] ∎

ITEM thm-qa-37-jrs-leakage-bound


## Statement

For a finite adiabatic braid input with rank-$m$ spectral band and schedule
$T>0$, define
$$A_m(s)=\frac1T\left[\frac{m\|H'(0)\|}{g(0)^2} +\frac{m\|H'(s)\|}{g(s)^2} +\int_0^s\left(\frac{m\|H''(r)\|}{g(r)^2} +\frac{7m\sqrt m\|H'(r)\|^2}{g(r)^3}\right)dr\right].$$
Then
$\|U_T(s)P(0)U_T(s)^*-P(s)\|\le A_m(s)$, and every unit initial band
vector has leakage probability at most $A_m(s)^2$. In particular,
$\|[I-P(1)]U_T(1)A_0\|_{\rm op}\le A_m(1)$ and leakage probability is at
most $A_m(1)^2$. This result does not prove the separate in-band transport
condition or identify band transport with Fibonacci $F/R$ data.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The finite-dimensional $C^2$ Hermitian path, its constant-rank
uniformly gapped band, preparation isometry, schedule $T>0$, and propagator.

[F1] The JRS theorem gives the displayed rank-dependent projector bound and
transition-probability bound. [[thm-qa-36-jrs-finite-adiabatic-bound]]

[F2] The preparation isometry maps every unit vector in $\mathbb C^m$ to a
unit vector in $\operatorname{ran}P(0)$.
[[def-qa-37-finite-adiabatic-braid-input]]

## Proof

1.1 The hypotheses of [F1] are exactly the finite-path, $C^2$, constant-rank, uniform-gap and $T>0$ clauses in the input definition. Substituting its $H,P,g,m,T$ into the JRS expression gives the displayed $A_m(s)$ and $\|U_T(s)P(0)U_T(s)^*-P(s)\|\le A_m(s)$. [F1, F2, given, algebra]

2.1 Let $v\in\mathbb C^m$ be unit and set $\psi=A_0v$. By [F2], $\psi$ is a unit vector in the initial band, so [F1] gives $\|[I-P(1)]U_T(1)\psi\|^2\le A_m(1)^2$. Taking square roots and then the supremum over unit $v$ gives $\|[I-P(1)]U_T(1)A_0\|_{\rm op}\le A_m(1)$. The squared bound is its leakage probability. No statement in [F1] supplies the in-band comparison $\epsilon_{\rm tr}$; that remains an independent input hypothesis. [F1, F2, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
