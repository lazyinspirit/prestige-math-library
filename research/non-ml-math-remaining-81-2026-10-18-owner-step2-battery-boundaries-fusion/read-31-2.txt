
ITEM def-qa-37-fusion-measurement


## Definition

Let $H$ be a fixed finite fusion space with an orthonormal fusion-tree basis
in the category and simple-representative domain of
[[def-qa-37-fusion-input-encoding]]. For a colored task, $H$ is the
currently occupied ordered-list summand tracked by the task record.
For an adjacent pair of leaves or a declared adjacent pair of subtrees, recouple
the tree by its specified unitary $F$-moves so that the pair fuses first. For
each allowed simple total charge $a$ of that pair, let $P_a$ be the orthogonal
projection onto the span of basis vectors whose pair label is $a$, transported
back by the inverse recoupling unitary. Distinct channel subspaces are
orthogonal and their direct sum is $H$, so $(P_a)_a$ is a finite projective
measurement in the sense of [[def-qc-projective-measurement]]. Its outcome is
$a$, its unnormalized branch vector on input $\psi$ is $P_a\psi$, and the
recoupling follows the inverse-precomposition F convention of
[[def-qc-tqc-f-symbol]], whose unitarity is proved in
[[thm-qc-tqc-f-symbol-unitary]]. The branch vector is unnormalized until its probability is checked.

ITEM def-qa-37-n-triple-fusion-code

## Statement

Let $n\ge1$ and $c\in\{1,\tau\}$ with $V(\tau^n\to c)\ne0$, using the
left-associated tree for both fixed-charge spaces. In the ordered list of
$3n$ Fibonacci $\tau$ charges, group consecutive leaves into $n$ triples;
within each triple fuse its first two charges and then its third, and fuse the
$n$ triple outputs by the left-associated $n$-leaf tree to total $c$. Restrict
each triple output to $\tau$; its two local paths are indexed by the charge
$x\in\{1,\tau\}$ after the first two leaves. Fix a rebracketing sequence
of associators from this grouped tree to the fixed left-associated $3n$-leaf
tree, and let $R_{n,c}$ be the induced composite unitary. For each tensor
basis vector $e_{x_1}\otimes\cdots\otimes e_{x_n}\otimes v_\lambda$, where
$e_1,e_\tau$ are the local triple paths and $v_\lambda$ is a basis vector of
$V(\tau^n\to c)$, map it to its grouped fusion-tree basis vector, then
apply $R_{n,c}$. Extend linearly and call the resulting map $J_{n,c}$. Its
image is the code $\mathcal C_{n,c}$ and it is an isometry; set
$P_{n,c}=J_{n,c}J_{n,c}^*$ for the code projector.
The factor $V(\tau^n\to c)$ retains all spectator fusion paths and is not a
physical preparation at zero cost.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The Fibonacci fusion rule $\tau\otimes\tau=1\oplus\tau$ with
multiplicity one, the orthonormal fusion-tree basis and dimension recurrence,
and unitary associator moves.

[F1] The Fibonacci rule has the only nontrivial fusion channels
$\tau\otimes\tau=1\oplus\tau$, each of multiplicity one.
[[ex-qc-tqc-fibonacci-data]]

[F2] Fusion-tree vectors form an orthonormal basis, with dimension computed
by the finite multiplicity recurrence. [[lem-qc-tqc-fusion-tree-dimension]]

[F3] The fixed-charge code projector associated with an isometry is
$JJ^*$. [[def-qc-tqc-fixed-charge-encoding]]

[F4] The associator and its induced F-matrices are unitary, so rebracketing
preserves the fusion-space inner product. [[thm-qc-tqc-f-symbol-unitary]]

## Proof

1.1 Write $m_k(a)=\dim V(\tau^k\to a)$. The Fibonacci rule [F1] and the fusion-tree recurrence [F2] give $m_2(1)=m_2(\tau)=1$ from the two channels of $\tau\otimes\tau$. Applying the same recurrence once more gives $m_3(1)=m_2(\tau)=1$ and $m_3(\tau)=m_2(1)+m_2(\tau)=2$. Thus the local total-$\tau$ triple has exactly the two orthogonal paths labelled $x=1,\tau$ used as one logical basis. [F1, F2, given, algebra]

2.1 Choose the fusion-tree basis of the finite spectator space $V(\tau^n\to c)$. Tensoring its basis with the $n$ local two-path bases from step 1.1 gives an orthonormal basis of the domain. By [F2], its grouped-tree images are precisely an orthonormal family in $V(\tau^{3n}\to c)$. The specified associator rebracketing is unitary by [F4], so their images in the fixed tree remain orthonormal; the linear map $J_{n,c}$ sending the tensor basis to these vectors is therefore an isometry. Applying [F3] gives the orthogonal projector $P_{n,c}=J_{n,c}J_{n,c}^*$. All choices are from fixed finite bases, and no choice axiom is used. [F2, F3, F4, step 1.1, algebra] ∎

ITEM def-qa-37-phase-reference-cost


## Definition

For the fixed Fibonacci convention, write $\rho(b)$ for the physical braid
matrix and $B(b)$ for the normalized word matrix obtained by removing the
common exchange scalar $R_1$ from each Artin generator, as in
[[lem-qc-tqc-determinant-character-phase]]. If $e(b)$ is exponent sum, then
$$\rho(b)=R_1^{e(b)}B(b).$$
On the three-anyon total-$\tau$ block the SK alphabet instead uses the
special-unitary lifts $zB_j$ with $z=e^{3\pi i/10}$, so a word of exponent sum
$k$ satisfies
$$\rho(b)=(R_1z^{-1})^k\widehat B(b),\qquad \widehat B(b)=z^kB(b).$$
A **phase reference record** stores the exact generator scalar, exponent sum,
which normalized convention was compiled, the resulting scalar on each
coherently used branch, and any correction operation and its declared cost
under [[def-qc-circuit-cost-model]]. A standalone fixed-sector computation
may report its output projectively using the metric of
[[def-qc-tqc-projective-gate-and-metric]]. In coherent control the active
branch scalar is relative to the inactive branch and must remain in the
record. No phase correction is assigned zero cost unless its interface says
so. This ledger distinguishes the common $R_1^{e(b)}$ phase from the
additional $z^{-e(b)}$ needed when the compiled alphabet is the SU(2) lift.

ITEM lem-qa-37-local-braid-acts-trivially-on-spectators

## Statement

Let $n\ge1$, $c\in\{1,\tau\}$, and consider the fixed fusion space
$V(\tau^{3n}\to c)$ with its grouped tree of $n$ consecutive triples and
outer triple-charge tree. Its orthogonal decomposition is indexed by local
triple outputs $a_i\in\{1,\tau\}$ and has factors
$$\left(\bigotimes_{i=1}^n V(\tau^3\to a_i)\right) \otimes V(a_1\otimes\cdots\otimes a_n\to c),$$
with zero-dimensional summands omitted. A braid word supported on the
internal three strands of the $i$th triple acts on this summand as
$\rho_{a_i}(b)$ on $V(\tau^3\to a_i)$ and the identity on every other local
triple factor and on the outer spectator factor. Reassociating to the fixed
left-associated $3n$-leaf tree conjugates this operator by a unitary; its
operator norm and the stated local-factor action are preserved.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The multiplicity-aware fusion-tree basis, the fixed grouped tree,
and a braid word acting only on the strands internal to one triple.

[F1] A fusion-tree basis composes orthonormal local vertex morphisms and is
complete; its basis labels include every internal charge and multiplicity
index. [[def-qc-tqc-fusion-tree-basis]]

[F2] A fixed-charge braid acts by precomposition with the inverse object
braid on the corresponding fusion morphism, and defines a unitary
representation. [[def-qc-tqc-braid-action-from-fusion-data]]
[[thm-qc-tqc-braid-action-unitary-and-artin]]

[F3] Rebracketing and its F-matrix are unitary. [[thm-qc-tqc-f-symbol-unitary]]

## Proof

1.1 In the grouped tree, choose an admissible local fusion-tree basis vector $f_i\in V(\tau^3\to a_i)$ for each triple and a basis vector $g\in V(a_1\otimes\cdots\otimes a_n\to c)$ for the outer fusion. The composites obtained by inserting $f_1,\ldots,f_n$ into $g$ are exactly the grouped fusion-tree vectors. By completeness and orthonormality [F1], their orthogonal direct sum over $(a_1,\ldots,a_n)$ is the full grouped fusion space, which gives the displayed tensor/direct-sum decomposition. [F1, given, algebra]

2.1 A braid word supported inside triple $i$ changes only the local morphism $f_i$, by $f_i\mapsto f_i\circ b^{-1}$ under [F2]; every $f_j$ with $j\ne i$ and the outer morphism $g$ are unchanged. Thus on the grouped basis its matrix is $\rho_{a_i}(b)$ tensored with identities. Rebracketing to the fixed tree conjugates this matrix by the unitary in [F3], proving the same operator statement in that convention. Tensoring an operator $A$ with an identity does not increase its norm: expand a vector in an orthonormal basis of the spectator factor and sum $\|A\xi_j\|^2\le\|A\|^2\sum\|\xi_j\|^2$. The norm of a finite orthogonal direct sum is at most the maximum of its block norms by the same squared-norm decomposition. These finite identities justify uniform spectator bounds. [F1, F2, F3, step 1.1, algebra] ∎

ITEM thm-qa-37-fusion-branch-validity


## Statement

Let $(P_a)_{a\in\mathcal A}$ be the finite fusion-channel projectors of
[[def-qa-37-fusion-measurement]] on a finite-dimensional fusion space $H$,
and let $\psi\in H$ be a unit vector. The channel probabilities
$p_a=\|P_a\psi\|^2$ are nonnegative and sum to one. If $p_a>0$, the
conditional branch is the unit vector $P_a\psi/\sqrt{p_a}$. If $p_a=0$,
then $P_a\psi=0$ and no normalized conditional state is defined.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A finite orthogonal resolution $(P_a)$ of the identity and a unit
input vector $\psi$.

[F1] The channel family is a finite projective measurement: its projections
are pairwise orthogonal and sum to identity.
[[def-qa-37-fusion-measurement]]

[F2] The projective measurement outcome law assigns probability
$p_a=\operatorname{tr}(|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|P_a)$ to outcome $a$.
[[thm-qc-projective-measurement-outcome-law]]

[F3] A unit vector defines a pure-state ray whose rank-one projector is a
density operator. [[def-qc-pure-state-and-ray]]
[[thm-qc-rank-one-density-operator]]

[F4] On an outcome of positive probability, the conditional density operator
is $P_a\rho P_a/p_a$. [[thm-qc-projective-conditional-state]]

[F5] The Dirac outer product acts by $|x\rangle\langle y|z=
\langle z,y\rangle_{\rm root}x$; trace is the sum of diagonal entries, and
$\|x\|^2=\langle x,x\rangle_{\rm root}$.
[[def-qc-dirac-notation-conversion]]
[[def-trace-of-an-endomorphism]] [[def-inner-product-norm]]

[F6] In orthonormal coordinates, $|x\rangle\langle y|$ has matrix
$[x][y]^\dagger$, so its trace is $\langle x,y\rangle_{\rm root}$.
[[thm-qc-dirac-notation-coordinate-identity]]

## Proof

1.1 Set $\rho=|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|$, a density operator by [F3]. The Dirac rule and $P_a=P_a^*=P_a^2$ give $|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|P_a=|\psi\rangle\langle P_a\psi|$, whose trace is $\langle\psi,P_a\psi\rangle_{\rm root}$ by [F6]. By self-adjointness and idempotence this equals $\langle P_a\psi,P_a\psi\rangle_{\rm root}=\|P_a\psi\|^2$ by [F5]. Thus [F2] identifies $p_a=\|P_a\psi\|^2\ge0$ as the outcome probability. The resolution identity gives $\sum_a p_a=\langle\psi,(\sum_aP_a)\psi\rangle_{\rm root}=\|\psi\|^2=1$. [F1, F2, F3, F5, F6, given, algebra]

2.1 If $p_a>0$, then $\rho_a=P_a\rho P_a/p_a$ is the conditional density operator by [F4]. The Dirac rule and self-adjointness give $P_a\rho P_a=|P_a\psi\rangle\langle P_a\psi|$; using step 1.1, $\rho_a$ is the rank-one projector of the unit vector $P_a\psi/\sqrt{p_a}$. If $p_a=0$, step 1.1 gives $\|P_a\psi\|=0$, hence $P_a\psi=0$. The zero-probability convention is that this branch is omitted and no normalized conditional vector or state is assigned; division by $\sqrt{p_a}=0$ is undefined. [F1, F4, F5, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
