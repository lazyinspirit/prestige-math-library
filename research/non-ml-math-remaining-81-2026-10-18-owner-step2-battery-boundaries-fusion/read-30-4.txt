
ITEM thm-qc-tqc-toric-local-indistinguishability


## Statement

Let $\mathcal C$ be the periodic toric code and let $\Pi$ be its orthogonal
code projector. Let $D$ be a compact embedded disk in the torus with
connected complement. Let $S\subseteq E_L$ be a set of edge qubits such that
$D$ contains both the primal edge segment and its crossing dual edge segment
for every $e\in S$. Then every operator $O$ supported on $S$ has scalar compression
to the code:

$$\Pi O\Pi=c(O)\Pi$$

for some $c(O)\in\mathbb C$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The periodic toric checks, a disk region $D$ as in the Statement, and an operator supported on its edge set $S$.

[F1] [[def-qc-tqc-toric-checks]]: stars are X on the four edges incident to a vertex and plaquettes are Z on the four edges in a face boundary.

[F2] [[lem-qc-tqc-toric-check-commutation]]: the check operators commute.

[F3] [[def-qc-stabilizer-code-space]]: the code is their common $+1$ eigenspace.

[F4] [[thm-qc-stabilizer-projector-and-dimension]]: $\Pi$ is the orthogonal projector onto the stabilizer code space. Together with [F3], this means every check $g$ satisfies $g\Pi=\Pi g=\Pi$.

[F5] [[def-qc-local-operator-support-and-weight]]: support on $S$ means, after the declared wire permutation, the operator is $O_S\otimes I_{S^c}$.

[F6] [[def-qc-pauli-matrices]]: the one-qubit matrix units are $|0\rangle\langle0|=(I+Z)/2$, $|1\rangle\langle1|=(I-Z)/2$, $|0\rangle\langle1|=(X+iY)/2$, and $|1\rangle\langle0|=(X-iY)/2$, by direct substitution.

[F7] [[lem-qc-tensor-product-basis-and-dimension]]: ordered elementary computational-basis tensors form a basis of the finite register.

[F8] [[def-qc-tensor-map]]: tensor products of one-qubit maps act factor by factor on elementary tensors and extend linearly.

[F9] [[thm-qc-pauli-commutator-is-symplectic-pairing]]: a Pauli commutes with every check exactly when its binary symplectic pairing with each check label is zero.

[F10] [[def-qc-stabilizer-syndrome-and-logical-operators]]: a phase-aware Pauli label records its X and Z supports separately; equal binary labels differ only by a scalar phase.

[F11] [[lem-qc-tqc-finite-square-cycle-is-boundary]]: every finite primal or dual cycle in the infinite square tiling is a sum of face boundaries.

[F12] [[lem-qc-tqc-contractible-disk-lifts-to-plane]]: primal and dual cycles supported in $D$ lift to finite cycles in the infinite square tiling.

[F13] [[def-qc-tqc-periodic-square-cellulation]]: the quotient sends each unit face to the correspondingly indexed torus face and sends its four boundary edges to the displayed periodic edge boundary; the same incidence identity holds for translated dual squares and star supports. By linearity the quotient therefore sends finite primal and dual boundaries to torus boundaries.

[F14] [[thm-qc-tqc-toric-code-dimension]]: the code projector is nonzero.

## Proof

1.1 The basis matrix units $|x\rangle\langle y|$ span all operators on the finite register by [F7]. Each is the tensor product of one-qubit matrix units by [F8]. The four identities in [F6] therefore express every global matrix unit as a linear combination of tensor Pauli operators. Hence tensor Paulis span the full operator space. If $O$ is supported on $S$, apply the same expansion to $O_S$ in [F5] and tensor with identity outside $S$; every resulting Pauli term is supported on a subset of $S$. [F5, F6, F7, F8, algebra]

2.1 Consider one tensor Pauli $E$ from this expansion. If it anticommutes with a check $g$, then the commutation fact [F2] and the common-$+1$ code definition [F3] make the projector relation [F4] applicable. It gives $\Pi E\Pi=\Pi gE\Pi=-\Pi Eg\Pi=-\Pi E\Pi$, so $\Pi E\Pi=0$. If $E$ commutes with all checks, write it as a phase times $X^xZ^z$. By [F1] and [F9], $z$ has even incidence at every primal vertex and $x$ has even incidence at every dual vertex. They are therefore primal and dual cycles. Their supports lie among the qubits in $S$; by [F12] both cycles lift to finite square-grid cycles. By [F11], the lifted Z cycle is a sum of square face boundaries and the lifted X cycle is a sum of dual face boundaries. Projecting these finite face chains to the torus gives products of plaquette checks and star checks with exactly the original support labels. Indeed, the quotient is a chain map because the boundary of each unit face maps to the stated four-edge torus boundary by [F13]; repeated image faces cancel in pairs over $\mathbb F_2$. Thus $E$ differs by a scalar phase from a product of checks, by [F10], and $\Pi E\Pi$ is a scalar multiple of $\Pi$ by [F3] and [F4]. [F1, F2, F3, F4, F9, F10, F11, F12, F13, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Every term in the Pauli expansion of $O$ from step 1.1 has either zero compression or scalar compression by step 2.1. Summing these finitely many terms gives $\Pi O\Pi=c(O)\Pi$. The code projector is nonzero by [F14], so the scalar can be chosen as the sum of the scalar coefficients of those terms. [F14, step 1.1, step 2.1, algebra] ∎

## Remarks

The disk and support hypotheses are finite-cellulation statements. This theorem proves exact local indistinguishability for the specified toric stabilizer model; it makes no perturbation-stability or finite-temperature lifetime claim.


ITEM thm-qc-tqc-toric-mutual-sign


## Statement

Let $P$ be a primal path and $Q$ a dual path whose edge supports share
exactly one physical qubit. Then their string Paulis anticommute:
$Z(P)X(Q)=-X(Q)Z(P)$. This is the algebraic mutual e/m string sign.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A primal Z string and a dual X string with exactly one shared edge-qubit.

[F1] [[lem-qc-tqc-toric-string-syndrome]]: $Z(P)$ is Z on the primal path edges and $X(Q)$ is X on the primal edge-qubits crossed by the dual path.

[F2] [[thm-qc-pauli-commutator-is-symplectic-pairing]]: two Pauli strings commute with sign $(-1)^{\omega}$, where $\omega$ is the parity of their X/Z support overlap.

## Proof

1.1 The X and Z supports meet at exactly one qubit, so their binary symplectic pairing is $1\in\mathbb F_2$. By [F2], $Z(P)X(Q)=(-1)^1X(Q)Z(P)=-X(Q)Z(P)$. [given, F1, F2, algebra]

2.1 The sign is obtained from the finite Pauli commutator alone. No trajectory, Hamiltonian evolution, or dynamical exchange is part of the hypotheses, so the result asserts the mutual string algebra and no braiding experiment. [given, step 1.1] ∎

## Remarks

The sign matches the conventional e/m mutual-statistics label for the toric code. The theorem proves the finite operator identity only.


ITEM def-qc-tqc-toric-energy-barrier


## Definition

Fix $L\ge3$, $J_e,J_m>0$, a unit vector $\psi$ in the toric code space, and
a specified nontrivial logical Pauli representative $Q$. An **elementary
Pauli path to $Q$** is a finite sequence $E_0,\ldots,E_T$ with $E_0=I$,
$E_T$ a scalar-phase multiple of $Q$, and
$E_{r+1}=P_rE_r$, where each $P_r$ is $X$, $Y$, or $Z$ on a single edge and
the identity on every other edge.

For any prefix Pauli $E$, define its energy excess
$\varepsilon(E):=\langle E\psi,H_LE\psi\rangle-E_{\rm code}$, where
$E_{\rm code}:=-L^2(J_e+J_m)$ is the energy of every code vector. The
**Pauli-path energy barrier** of $Q$ is

$$\mathcal B_L(Q):=\min_{(E_0,\ldots,E_T)}\max_{0\le r\le T}\varepsilon(E_r),$$

where the minimum ranges over elementary Pauli paths to $Q$. This is a
finite-code energy quantity; it is not a thermal lifetime.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The finite Hamiltonian, a nonzero toric code space, a unit code vector, and a specified logical Pauli representative.

[F1] [[def-qc-tqc-toric-hamiltonian]]: $H_L$ is the stated finite sum of star and plaquette checks.

[F2] [[thm-qc-tqc-toric-code-dimension]]: the code space has dimension four and every code vector has every check eigenvalue $+1$.

[F3] [[def-qc-stabilizer-syndrome-and-logical-operators]]: every Pauli is a phase-aware tensor product of one-qubit Pauli factors and its logical class is defined modulo scalar phase and stabilizers.

[F4] [[def-qc-pauli-matrices]]: the one-qubit Pauli factors are $I,X,Y,Z$, all unitary.

[F5] [[thm-qc-pauli-commutator-is-symplectic-pairing]]: conjugating a check by a Pauli changes its sign by a value in $\{+1,-1\}$ determined by their binary supports.

## Verification

1.1 For any unit code vector $\psi$, each check has eigenvalue $+1$ by [F2], so [F1] gives $\langle\psi,H_L\psi\rangle=-L^2(J_e+J_m)=E_{\rm code}$. For any Pauli prefix $E$ and any check $g$, [F5] gives $gE\psi=\pm Eg\psi=\pm E\psi$. Thus $E\psi$ has a joint check syndrome, and each term of $H_L$ has a fixed expectation on it. The sign pattern depends on $E$ and the checks, not on the choice of code vector $\psi$. Therefore $\varepsilon(E)$ is well-defined and independent of that choice. [F1, F2, F5, given, algebra]

1.2 Every $Q$ in [F3] is a finite tensor product of one-edge Pauli factors $I,X,Y,Z$ from [F4], up to scalar phase. Applying its nonidentity factors one edge at a time gives at least one elementary path, so the set of paths in the Definition is nonempty. [F3, F4, construct]

2.1 By [F1] and [F2], the energy excess of any prefix is $2J_ea+2J_mb$, where $a,b\in\{0,\ldots,L^2\}$ count its violated stars and plaquettes. This is a finite set of possible real values. Hence the set of peak excesses over the nonempty path family is a nonempty subset of a finite set, and its minimum exists. [F1, F2, step 1.2, algebra] ∎


ITEM thm-qc-tqc-toric-distance


## Statement

For the periodic $L\times L$ toric stabilizer code with $L\ge3$, the
minimum weight of a normalizer Pauli outside the scalar-phase stabilizer is
$d(S)=L$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** $L\ge3$, the toric checks on $2L^2$ edge qubits, their binary Pauli support convention, and their logical Pauli quotient.

[F1] [[def-qc-tqc-toric-checks]]: stars are X on incident primal edges and plaquettes are Z on face-boundary edges.

[F2] [[lem-qc-tqc-toric-cycle-quotient]]: a primal or dual cycle is a boundary exactly when both winding parities vanish; every nonboundary cycle has nonzero winding in one of the two coordinate directions.

[F3] [[def-qc-stabilizer-syndrome-and-logical-operators]]: the binary label of a Pauli records its X and Z supports, and normalizer Paulis are those commuting with all checks; logical classes are modulo phases and stabilizers.

[F4] [[thm-qc-pauli-commutator-is-symplectic-pairing]]: a Pauli commutes with a check exactly when their binary symplectic pairing is zero.

[F5] [[def-qc-tqc-toric-logical-loops]]: $\bar Z_1$ is a nontrivial logical Pauli supported on the $L$ horizontal edges $h_{i,0}$.

[F6] [[def-qc-stabilizer-distance]]: when the code encodes at least one qubit, distance is the minimum weight in $N(S)\setminus Z_nS$.

[F7] [[def-qc-local-operator-support-and-weight]]: the weight of a Pauli is the number of edge qubits on which it is nonidentity.

[F8] [[thm-qc-tqc-toric-code-dimension]]: the code has dimension $4$ and encodes two qubits.

## Proof

1.1 Let $E=i^aX^xZ^z$ be a Pauli in the normalizer. Commuting with every star forces the Z-support $z$ to have even incidence at every primal vertex; commuting with every plaquette forces the X-support $x$ to have even incidence at every dual vertex, by [F1] and [F4]. Thus $z$ is a primal cycle and $x$ is a dual cycle. [given, F1, F3, F4, construct]

2.1 If both cycles were boundaries, their supports would be products of plaquette and star checks respectively, so $E$ would be a scalar-phase stabilizer. Hence a nontrivial logical Pauli has at least one nonboundary cycle by [F2] and [F3]. That cycle has a nonzero winding parity. For a horizontal winding, each of the $L$ disjoint vertical cuts is crossed an odd number of times; for a vertical winding, each of the $L$ disjoint horizontal cuts is crossed an odd number of times. In either case its support contains at least one distinct edge from each cut, hence at least $L$ edges. The Pauli support contains the support of each nonzero X or Z chain, even when both bits occur on one qubit, so [F7] gives $\operatorname{wt}(E)\ge L$. [F1, F2, F3, F7, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 The operator $\bar Z_1$ from [F5] has exactly $L$ distinct edge factors and is logically nontrivial. Thus the set defining [F6] is nonempty and contains a weight-$L$ element. The code encodes two qubits by [F8], so [F6] applies; combining this upper bound with step 2.1 gives $d(S)=L$. [F5, F6, F8, step 2.1, algebra] ∎
