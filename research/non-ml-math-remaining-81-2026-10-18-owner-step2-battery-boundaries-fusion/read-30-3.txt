
ITEM lem-qc-tqc-toric-string-syndrome


## Statement

Let $P$ be a primal edge trail with no repeated edge and distinct endpoints
$q_0,q_1$. The Pauli string $Z(P)$ has commutation syndrome $1$ at exactly
the two endpoint stars and $0$ at every other star or plaquette. Let $Q$ be
a dual edge trail with no repeated edge and distinct endpoint faces
$f_0,f_1$. The string $X(Q)$ has commutation syndrome $1$ at exactly those
two endpoint plaquettes and $0$ at every other plaquette or star.

If either kind of edge-simple trail is closed, its corresponding string has
zero syndrome at every check.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The toric checks, an edge-simple primal or dual trail, and the finite check-eigenvalue convention for syndromes.

[F1] [[def-qc-tqc-toric-checks]]: stars are $X$ on incident primal edges, and plaquettes are $Z$ on boundary edges.

[F2] [[def-qc-tqc-toric-excitations]]: an e or m defect is a check with eigenvalue $-1$; the corresponding commutation sign specifies whether a string creates that defect when applied to a common $+1$ check eigenvector.

[F3] [[def-qc-pauli-matrices]]: on one qubit, $XZ=-ZX$, while $X$ commutes with $X$ and $Z$ commutes with $Z$.

## Proof

1.1 In the mod-two support of a primal trail, each internal visit to a vertex contributes one incoming and one outgoing edge, hence even incidence. After pairing arrivals and departures at every visit, the distinct endpoints have odd incidence and every other vertex has even incidence. By [F3], a $Z$ string anticommutes with a star exactly when the number of shared edges is odd. Thus it anticommutes with exactly $A_{q_0}$ and $A_{q_1}$. It commutes with every plaquette because both use only $Z$ factors. [given, F1, F3, algebra]

1.2 In the dual graph, the same incidence count gives odd overlap exactly at the two endpoint faces. A dual edge crosses the primal edge carrying the same qubit, so [F3] makes the $X$ string anticommute with exactly the two endpoint plaquettes. It commutes with all stars because both use only $X$ factors. By [F2], these signs are precisely the stated e and m endpoint syndromes. [given, F1, F2, F3, algebra]

2.1 For a closed edge-simple primal trail, every vertex has even degree in its mod-two edge support: arrivals and departures pair at every visit, including the common initial and final vertex. Thus every star overlap is even, and every plaquette overlap is even because both strings use only $Z$. For a closed dual trail, the same incidence count gives even overlap with every plaquette, and its X string commutes with all stars. Hence both closed strings have zero syndrome. [F1, F3, construct, algebra] ∎


ITEM thm-qc-tqc-toric-code-dimension


## Statement

For the periodic $L\times L$ toric checks with $L\ge3$, the common $+1$
eigenspace is a valid stabilizer code on $n=2L^2$ qubits. Its independent
check rank is $r=2L^2-2$, so its dimension is
$2^{n-r}=4=2^2$ and it encodes two logical qubits.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** An integer $L\ge3$, the periodic toric checks, and the common $+1$ eigenspace convention.

[F1] [[lem-qc-tqc-toric-check-commutation]]: the checks are commuting Hermitian involutions; the product of all stars and the product of all plaquettes are both $I$.

[F2] [[lem-qc-tqc-toric-incidence-ranks]]: the star supports have rank $L^2-1$ and their only relation is the total star sum; the face boundaries have rank $L^2-1$ and their only relation is the total face sum.

[F3] [[def-qc-stabilizer-code-space]]: a stabilizer code is the common $+1$ eigenspace of an abelian subgroup of Hermitian Pauli operators that does not contain $-I$.

[F4] [[def-qc-stabilizer-syndrome-and-logical-operators]]: the binary Pauli label records the X and Z support vectors separately.

[F5] [[thm-qc-stabilizer-projector-and-dimension]]: an $n$-qubit stabilizer subgroup with $r$ independent generators has a nonzero code space of dimension $2^{n-r}$.

[F6] [[def-qc-tqc-periodic-square-cellulation]]: the periodic lattice has $L^2$ vertices and faces and $2L^2$ edges.

## Proof

1.1 Omit the fixed star $A_{0,0}$ and fixed plaquette $B_{0,0}$. By [F2], the remaining $L^2-1$ stars are independent, and the remaining $L^2-1$ plaquettes are independent; [F1] expresses each omitted check as the product of the retained checks of its type. Their combined binary supports are independent because an X-support and a Z-support occupy separate coordinates by [F4]. Thus these checks generate all checks with $r=2L^2-2$ independent generators. [F1, F2, F4, given, algebra]

2.1 Let $S$ be their generated subgroup. By [F1] it is abelian and generated by Hermitian Paulis. If a product of selected generators had zero binary label, its X-support and Z-support would each be zero by [F4]. The two independent families in [F2] then force both selected subsets to be empty, so that product is $I$. In particular, no product is $-I$, and $-I\notin S$. Hence $S$ satisfies the stabilizer hypotheses in [F3]. [F1, F2, F3, F4, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 There are $n=2L^2$ edge qubits by [F6]. The dimension theorem [F5] therefore gives $\dim\mathcal C(S)=2^{2L^2-(2L^2-2)}=4=2^2$; [F3] and step 2.1 ensure that its hypotheses hold, and step 1.1 supplies the independent rank. The code is nonzero and encodes exactly two qubits. [F3, F5, F6, step 1.1, step 2.1, algebra] ∎


ITEM def-qc-tqc-toric-logical-loops


## Definition

On the periodic torus, define four Pauli representatives

$$\bar Z_1=\prod_{i\in\mathbb Z_L}Z_{h_{i,0}},\qquad \bar Z_2=\prod_{j\in\mathbb Z_L}Z_{v_{0,j}},\qquad \bar X_1=\prod_{j\in\mathbb Z_L}X_{h_{0,j}},\qquad \bar X_2=\prod_{i\in\mathbb Z_L}X_{v_{i,0}}.$$

The $\bar Z$ supports are the two fixed fundamental primal cycles. The
$\bar X$ supports are the dual cycles crossing them. These operators are
called the toric logical loops. As verified below, each is a nontrivial
logical Pauli class in the periodic toric code.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The periodic toric checks and the four specified Pauli loop representatives.

[F1] [[def-qc-tqc-toric-checks]]: stars are X on the four incident edges and plaquettes are Z on the four boundary edges.

[F2] [[thm-qc-pauli-commutator-is-symplectic-pairing]]: a Pauli commutes with a check exactly when its binary symplectic pairing with that check is zero; for these pure X/Z checks this is the parity of the shared support.

[F3] [[lem-qc-tqc-toric-cycle-quotient]]: the two primal fundamental loops have winding pairs $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$; the two dual loops have the corresponding nonzero dual winding classes; a cycle is a boundary exactly when both winding parities vanish.

[F4] [[lem-qc-tqc-toric-incidence-ranks]]: the only star-support relation and the only face-boundary relation are their respective total sums.

[F5] [[thm-qc-tqc-toric-code-dimension]]: the common check eigenspace is nonzero and has dimension four.

[F6] [[def-qc-stabilizer-syndrome-and-logical-operators]]: logical Pauli classes are normalizer elements modulo scalar phases and stabilizers.

[F7] [[def-qc-tqc-periodic-square-cellulation]]: indices are modulo $L$; horizontal edges join consecutive vertices in their first coordinate and vertical edges join consecutive vertices in their second coordinate. The dual edges cross the corresponding primal edges and join the adjacent faces.

[F8] [[lem-qc-tqc-toric-check-commutation]]: the product of all stars and the product of all plaquettes are identity, and all checks commute.

## Verification

1.1 The supports of $\bar Z_1$ and $\bar Z_2$ close because their successive horizontal and vertical edges meet at vertices modulo $L$, by [F7]. The supports of $\bar X_1$ and $\bar X_2$ close on the dual graph because the crossed edges join successive adjacent faces modulo $L$, also by [F7]. By [F1] and [F2], a primal Z cycle commutes with every star and plaquette, and a dual X cycle commutes with every plaquette and star. Their winding pairs are nonzero by [F3], so none is a boundary. If a pure Z loop were in the stabilizer, its X support would be zero; by [F4] the selected star factors would either be empty or the full star family, and [F8] makes that full product $I$. The loop would therefore be a product of plaquettes, contrary to its nonzero winding. Interchanging X and Z gives the same conclusion for the dual loops. Hence all four lie in $N(S)\setminus Z_nS$ and define nontrivial logical classes by [F6]. [given, F1, F2, F3, F4, F6, F7, F8, algebra]

2.1 The code is nonzero by [F5], so these normalizer classes act on its four-dimensional code space. The four formulas fix the representatives whose pairings are calculated in the subsequent logical-algebra result. [F5, step 1.1] ∎

## Remarks

The commutation pairings for these fixed representatives are listed in
[[thm-qc-tqc-toric-logical-algebra]]. This is an orientation-only forward
reference; the verification above does not depend on that theorem.


ITEM thm-qc-tqc-toric-gap


## Statement

For the finite toric-code Hamiltonian
$H_L=-J_e\sum_q A_q-J_m\sum_f B_f$ with $J_e,J_m>0$, the ground space is
the four-dimensional toric code space and the ground energy is
$E_g=-L^2(J_e+J_m)$. The exact spectral gap above it is
$\Delta_L=4\min(J_e,J_m)$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** $L\ge3$, fixed couplings $J_e,J_m>0$, the Hamiltonian, the toric code space, and the joint-check syndrome labels.

[F1] [[def-qc-tqc-toric-hamiltonian]]: $H_L$ is the finite sum $-J_e\sum_qA_q-J_m\sum_fB_f$.

[F2] [[lem-qc-tqc-toric-joint-check-eigenspaces]]: the check eigenvalue sectors are an orthogonal decomposition of the Hilbert space; each defect parity is even.

[F3] [[thm-qc-tqc-toric-code-dimension]]: the common $+1$ eigenspace has dimension four.

[F4] [[lem-qc-tqc-toric-string-syndrome]]: a one-edge primal Z string creates exactly two e defects, and a one-edge dual X string creates exactly two m defects.

[F5] [[def-qc-tqc-toric-excitations]]: e and m defect counts are the numbers of star and plaquette checks with eigenvalue $-1$.

## Proof

1.1 On a joint-check eigenspace with $N_e$ negative star signs and $N_m$ negative plaquette signs, each star sum is $L^2-2N_e$ and each plaquette sum is $L^2-2N_m$. By [F1] the Hamiltonian acts there by the scalar $-L^2(J_e+J_m)+2J_eN_e+2J_mN_m$. [given, F1, F2, F5, algebra]

2.1 The all-positive sector is the code space by definition, is nonzero by [F3], and has energy $E_g=-L^2(J_e+J_m)$. Every other nonzero sector has at least one defect species present; by [F2] its defect count is even, hence at least two. Step 1.1 then places its energy at least $4\min(J_e,J_m)$ above $E_g$. [F2, F3, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 If $J_e\le J_m$, choose a one-edge primal path and apply its Z string to a nonzero code vector. By [F4] the result is a nonzero joint eigenvector with exactly two e defects, so its energy is $E_g+4J_e$. If $J_m<J_e$, use a one-edge dual path and its X string instead; it yields a nonzero joint eigenvector with exactly two m defects and energy $E_g+4J_m$. These states attain the lower bound from step 2.1, proving $\Delta_L=4\min(J_e,J_m)$. [F1, F4, step 2.1, algebra] ∎
