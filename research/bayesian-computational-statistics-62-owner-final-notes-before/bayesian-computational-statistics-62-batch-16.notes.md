## Mathematical route and unresolved fixed-scale condition

- Log-Gaussian densities require a jointly measurable path evaluation and normalizer map, finite positive normalizers prior-almost surely and at the truth, and IID sampling. For sigma-finite data measure, boundedness alone is not enough to make the normalizer finite; the theorem states the normalizer condition. For the sieve tests, the valid-normalizer subset is measurable by Tonelli; finite covers are recentered within it, which changes a radius-3 supremum cover to radius 6 and a Hellinger cover to radius 7 for sufficiently large n.
- Binary regression uses the derivative and derivative-to-Bernoulli-variance bounds on the link, a jointly measurable evaluation version for the L2(G) option, and the local L2 likelihood comparisons. The Bernoulli joint density is bounded by one, which is the bounded-density condition for the IID density-L2 test branch.
- Fixed-design Gaussian regression is independent but non-IID. Batch 16 supplies the normal KL/log-square calculation, a Gaussian linear/quadratic tail bound, finite-cover score tests for mean separation, residual-energy tests for scale separation, and an independent-observation denominator/splitting argument. The test route is local and does not treat fixed design as IID.
- The design's fixed-scale assumptions give only `Pi_sigma(|sigma-sigma0|<epsilon_n) >= c epsilon_n`. The local denominator argument needs a lower bound `exp(-C_sigma n epsilon_n^2)`. A nonzero Gaussian direction plus the concentration condition implies `log(1/epsilon_n)=O(n epsilon_n^2)`, which supplies it. The allowed degenerate prior `W=0` has concentration function zero and admits `epsilon_n=sqrt(log log n/n)`, for which the uniform scale local mass has negative log asymptotic to `(1/2)log n`, not `O(log log n)`. This is a denominator proof gap, not a posterior counterexample. The owner must resolve the degenerate unknown-scale scope or provide another verified joint local-mass argument; the theorem and dependent examples remain escalated.
