## 2. Per-pair richness report

Both passes were run on the single A/B pair in this batch, and both found work.

**Proof-decomposition pass.**

- The norm/trace block in the design was split into four proof-bearing items: the
  embedding formulas, the basic-law/tower theorem, the trace-form criterion, and
  the surjectivity corollary. The split is not cosmetic: the inseparable exponent,
  the transitivity law, and nondegeneracy of the trace form are distinct audit
  obligations and should not be buried inside one omnibus theorem.
- The cyclic/Kummer/solvability block was decomposed around the actual proof
  pivots: multiplicative Hilbert 90, additive Hilbert 90, the cyclic Kummer
  characterization, Artin-Schreier, the perfect pairing, the correspondence, the
  radical normal-closure lemma, and the abelian-kernel lemma. Without those
  intermediate statements, the two solvability theorems turn into a single long
  proof that quietly assumes exactly the conventions the design warns against.
- `thm-a-transitive-subgroup-of-s-p-containing-a-transposition-is-s-p` is kept as a
  standalone theorem even though GA-4 itself does not use it. The design
  explicitly says X-1's companion page needs it, and proving it here now is far
  cheaper than rediscovering or re-proving it later.

**Corollary pass.**

- Added `cor-trace-is-surjective-for-finite-separable-extensions`. It is immediate
  from the trace-form theorem, but the additive Hilbert 90 proof wants it as a
  named fact.
- Added `cor-degree-of-f-adjoin-an-nth-root-by-coset-order`. The design names the
  degree formula as part of the Kummer block and it is genuinely useful
  immediately on the B page.
- Added `cor-polynomials-of-degree-at-most-four-are-solvable-by-radicals`. This is
  the honest “low-degree formulas exist” summary after the group-theoretic
  solvability theorems.
- Added `cor-abel-ruffini-for-the-general-polynomial`. The design's Abel–Ruffini
  endpoint is not a restatement of the general-polynomial theorem; it is the
  actual reader-facing consequence.

No page approaches the 60-item ceiling, so no split is proposed.

### Finding 2 — the solvability proof needs two auxiliary lemmas kept separate

**What is wrong.**
If the solvability-by-radicals proof is scaffolded only as “adjoin roots of unity,
then use Kummer theory along a composition series”, the two load-bearing moves
that the design explicitly warns about disappear:

1. the normal closure of a radical extension is still radical; and
2. adjoining roots of unity changes the Galois group only by an abelian kernel.

**What I propose.**
Keep both `lem-normal-closure-of-a-radical-extension-is-radical` and
`lem-adjoining-roots-of-unity-to-a-galois-extension-adds-an-abelian-kernel`
as separate A-page items.

**If declined.**
`thm-solvable-by-radicals-implies-a-solvable-galois-group` and
`thm-solvable-galois-group-implies-solvable-by-radicals` become monoliths with
the page’s hardest convention-sensitive steps hidden inline.

### Finding 3 — the concrete insoluble quintic stays deferred to X-1’s companion

**What is wrong.**
The standard $x^5-6x+3$ argument requires the splitting field to sit inside
$\mathbb C$, so complex conjugation really is a transposition. GA-4 sits below
X-1 and may not use “$\mathbb C$ is algebraically closed”.

**What I propose.**

**If declined.**

## 8. Web research ledger and dependency-closure statement

### Source ledger

- `https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/FT.pdf`
  Supports Hilbert 90, cyclic extensions with roots of unity, Kummer theory,
  Galois’s solvability theorem, the general polynomial, and norms/traces.
  Convention flags:
  the norm formula includes the inseparable exponent; the solvability theorem is
  stated in characteristic `0`; the general polynomial endpoint is about the
  general polynomial, not every quintic over `\mathbb Q`.
- `https://virtualmath1.stanford.edu/~conrad/248APage/handouts/normtrace.pdf`
  Supports the determinant/trace dictionary, trace and norm embedding formulas,
  the tower laws, and the trace-form route to nondegeneracy/nonvanishing.
  Convention flags:
  trace zero in the inseparable case is explicit; the trace-form argument is
  algebraic, not analytic.
- `https://palmer.wellesley.edu/~aschultz/w20/math306/lectureoutlines.html`
  Supports the weakest-definition route for radical extensions and solvability by
  radicals, the two-direction solvability theorem, and a Kummer-theory-based proof
  outline of Galois’s theorem.
  Convention flags:
  this source works over characteristic `0` in the solvability theorem and treats
  solvability-by-radicals as a statement about splitting fields inside radical
  extensions.
- `https://archive.nptel.ac.in/content/storage2/courses/111101001/modules/lec20/20.2.html`
  Supports the Artin-Schreier theorem as the characteristic-`p` cyclic
  replacement for Kummer theory.
  Convention flags:
  the additive map is `x\mapsto x^p-x`; the theorem is specific to degree `p`.
- `https://www.math.iitb.ac.in/~srg/Lecnotes/isant.pdf`
  Supports the cyclic/abelian-extension block as a second lecture-note treatment,
  especially the adjacency of Hilbert 90, Artin-Schreier and Kummer material in
  one course-note source.
  Convention flags:
  the note separates cyclic from abelian extensions and treats Artin-Schreier as
  the characteristic-`p` cyclic story.
- Secondary source-check only for theorem 25:
  `https://proofwiki.org/wiki/Transitive_Subgroup_of_Prime_containing_Transposition`
  and the mirrored Clark-text snippet at `turn1search2` both match the same exact
  statement. I did not add them to the coverage file because the page does not
  need theorem 25 as an internal dependency, but the step-5 author should still
  keep a literature citation on that item if it remains intended for later reuse.

### Convention disagreements found

- **Radical extension.**
  Sources disagree on whether the tower must already be Galois or already contain
  the relevant roots of unity. The scaffold adopts the weakest definition and
  proves the two strengthenings as lemmas, exactly as the design requires.
- **Kummer extension.**
  The scaffold follows Conrad’s convention rather than Ash’s broader
  “abelian Galois” usage, so the definition is source-backed but library-adapted.
- **Norm formula.**
  The inseparable exponent is explicit in Milne and in the design. It is the
  whole reason for `fs-the-norm-is-always-the-product-of-the-embeddings}`.
- **Solvability theorem scope.**
  Both directions are scaffolded with `characteristic 0` in the title and
  statement, not as a caveat in remarks.
