A scalar positive principal symbol permits componentwise weak interior estimates on a finite bundle atlas. Compact Sobolev inclusions and a shifted coercive variational problem then produce a selfadjoint operator with the exact $H^2$ domain, a finite smooth kernel, and a smooth Green inverse. Applied to the de Rham and Dolbeault Laplacians, these results give harmonic representatives in every degree and bidegree. This supplies compact analysis for subsequent Kähler Hodge theory.
