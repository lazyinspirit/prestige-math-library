### Resolve these two cross-package interfaces first

**Canonical-order formula.** The constructibility package proposes a local
history formula χ(x,y) defining the actual published canonical order and
agreeing with that order inside weak limit levels. The diamond package
requires that the formula transferred through the collapse be exactly the
one with proved weak-level agreement. Reconcile them as follows:

1. Prove in the new supplier that χ defines the actual published relation
   in ambient ZF and that its interpretation in each required limit Lλ
   agrees with that relation there.
2. In the diamond proof use χ explicitly in the two least-pair assertions
   interpreted in Lθ and Lγ. The global least-pair recursion still uses
   the actual published relation, now identified with χ.
3. Do not infer weak-level equivalence of χ with another formula merely
   from their equivalence in full ZF. If a different fixed syntactic formula
   is used inside the collapse, prove the required equivalence there.

This permits preservation of the published order and its formula/arity-first
definition-code convention without editing published content or silently
changing the order. The canonical hull's actual least-witness functions use
the same actual order; a documented alternate formula for it does not change
those functions.

**Ambient versus internal subset bounds.** The original subset-bound item
is stronger than a V=L-only statement. Preserve its ambient-ZF claims for
constructible x⊆κ and constructible x⊆Lκ, with ambient κ⁺. Its GCH
consumer then applies that theorem **inside L**, using L's cardinals and
successors. The constructibility package's internally-in-L discussion is
the GCH application, not permission to restrict the original supplier.
