## Mathematical review and source evidence

The conventions are `k_theta=[[cos theta,sin theta],[-sin theta,cos theta]]`, `a_t=diag(exp(t/2),exp(-t/2))`, left P-covariance and right translation. The parabolic character is `delta_P(a_t)=exp(t)` and the group modular function is its reciprocal. I checked the AN multiplication and modulus, KAN/ANK inverses, angular Jacobians, Haar-density cancellation, adjoint determinant, inversion/NAK conversion and the example integral `2 eta (cosh eta - 1)`.

I checked Fourier parity, density and finite-support detection; the derived directions and ladder zeros; the exceptional submodule/quotient orientations and Casimir normalization; the integral's convergence and differentiated integrable bounds; both parity base constants, the odd reflection sign, Gamma pole-zero cancellations and exact exceptional kernels. The operator continuation establishes common local pole removal, polynomial bounds for both index tails, smooth-seminorm power series, Fourier density and group intertwining. The unitary arguments separately establish the odd-zero Hardy-space summands and the weighted complementary Hilbert completion. Sign equivalence has continuous inverses off the exceptional lattice and fails at nonzero exceptional points. The spherical coefficient uses the opposite-parameter intertwiner; its compact-uniform limit, Fell neighborhoods and direct-sum almost invariant vectors are justified. The finite tables, sign examples and counterexample at nu=3 (with the separate degenerate endpoint nu=1) agree with their suppliers.
