## Finite smoothing of actual embedded carriers

A compact embedded cooriented C2 leaf L has finitely many ambient smooth neighborhoods with C2 defining functions Fi, vanishing exactly on L there and with coherently positive normal derivative. Take a finite ambient smooth partition of unity ρi subordinate to those neighborhoods. On their common neighborhood set F=ΣρiFi. On L the terms involving dρi vanish, and dF=ΣρidFi is nonzero with the common positive normal. After shrinking a compact neighborhood, F has no other zeros. Approximate F in C2 on that compact neighborhood by a smooth ambient function G, using finitely many chart convolutions and a fixed smooth partition. Choose a smooth ambient positively transverse field V by finite positive chart fields and smooth weights. The equation G(Φt(x))=0 has a unique small C2 root t(x) for x∈L, uniformly by compactness and the positive V derivative. The resulting map to the nearby smooth level L' is C2 and has a C2 inverse obtained by solving F=0 along the same V orbit. This proves a C2 diffeomorphism to a smooth surface by a finite construction, without importing global atlas smoothing or full AC.

For a compact disk region in a leaf, first extend it across its regular boundary by a thin actual surface collar and apply that ambient defining-function construction on a compact neighborhood of the enlarged surface region. The projected disk has C2 boundary on a smooth surface. On that surface, construct a C2 signed defining function for its boundary circle with nonzero differential by finite local curve charts and smooth weights. Smooth that function and use a smooth boundary-transverse surface field and its unique short roots. A cutoff extends this boundary displacement over its collar and is the identity deeper inside; its normal derivative stays positive when the approximation is sufficiently small. The resulting disk has smooth boundary and is C2 diffeomorphic to the actual disk. This two-stage construction provides the same content as simultaneous two-root projection, while explicitly separating surface and boundary regularity.

## Smooth finite handles and genus/disk normal forms

Use thm-adapted-excellent-morse-functions-exist-on-compact-cobordisms (AC_omega), not cor-every-compact-smooth-manifold-admits-an-excellent-morse-function (full AC). For a closed smooth surface both triad faces are empty. For a smooth disk use its boundary as an outgoing face with a fixed collar. thm-morse-rearrangement-by-index orders the finite indices under AC_omega, and thm-morse-functions-and-handle-decompositions-correspond supplies their actual smooth handles.

In a connected closed surface, the graph of zero-handles and one-handles is connected: two-handles attach along connected circles and cannot connect different components. A primal spanning tree merges the zero-handles by zero/one cancellation. In the dual handle decomposition, a dual spanning tree merges all maxima by one/two cancellation. Applying those finitely many geometric cancellations removes handles without changing the surface diffeomorphism type. The local tree edge must meet the chosen endpoint zero-handle belt once; other incident bands are transported by collar straightening/handle slides. The dual process has the same exact attaching/belt condition. These attaching transports must be included in the proof; χ alone does not produce the cancellations.

After those moves, a closed oriented χ=0 surface has one zero-handle, two one-handles and one two-handle. Its zero-handle is a disk. The first orientable band gives an annulus. Because the final two-handle has one attaching boundary circle, the second band joins the two annulus boundary components and gives a once-punctured torus. Collar-preserving disk/band straightening gives the standard model, and the final disk closes it. Thus the smooth L' is diffeomorphic to T2, and the finite C2 carrier projection gives L diffeomorphic to T2.

For a connected oriented smooth topological disk, cancel primal minima and dual maxima relative to its nonempty boundary so that only one zero handle remains. Equivalently its finite disk-and-band spine has free rank zero, so it is a tree, and successive leaf disk/band absorption gives one disk. Again the disk/band absorption is an actual smooth collar move, not a deduction from homotopy equivalence alone. Composing with the C2 carrier projection yields an actual C2 parametrization of each Jordan cap.

If a prescribed orientation-preserving C2 boundary parametrization differs from the obtained disk parametrization by ψ∈DiffC2(S1), choose an increasing angle lift ψ̃ and its isotopy ψ̃s=(1−s)θ+sψ̃(θ). Its angular derivative remains positive. Apply this isotopy with a smooth radial cutoff zero near the center and one near the boundary; the resulting polar map is C2, is the identity near the center and realizes ψ at the boundary. Product collars then preserve the prescribed seam coordinates. Orientation reversing boundary data are handled by one fixed reflection before the positive lift construction.

## Cap quotient and inherited ambient seam charts

At a paired seam the two regular C2 half-sweeps map to opposite sides of the same actual disk. Use an ambient smooth transverse field V and the C2 disk parametrization to define one common signed normal-flow collar of that disk in the target. On each half-sweep its C2 local inverse pulls that collar back to a source face collar. These face collars agree after the supplied base-disk identification because they use the same target coordinates. Thus the seam atlas is C2 and its map to the embedded target is a C2 local diffeomorphism. This explicitly controls the compatible charts; local openness alone would not do so.

The actual disk cylinder, with product collars and rounded corners, is a three-ball. Its two paired boundary disks are parametrized C2 disks. Straighten those attaching disks and their collar data before identifying them: the quotient is the standard three-ball with one one-handle, hence D2×S1. Compare any chosen corner rounding by common-transverse-profile interpolation; adjoining the compact outer torus product collar leaves that diffeomorphism type unchanged. This is an ordinary manifold diffeomorphism, not a smooth conjugacy of contracting holonomy.
