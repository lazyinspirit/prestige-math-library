## Bounded dispositions

Atomic equality and membership have the same actual meanings in nested
transitive sets. Boolean constructors preserve agreement. A bounded witness is
an element of a parameter from the smaller domain, so transitivity keeps the
witness in that domain and transfers the matrix in both directions. Structural
formula induction proves Delta-zero absoluteness without internal set-theory
axioms or choice.

The empty, subset, unordered-pair, singleton, union, intersection, difference,
ordered-pair, product, relation, function, evaluation and injection formulas all
bound their quantifiers through supplied sets. The nonempty intersection formula
uses a member of the family only inside its proof, while the empty family is
explicitly assigned the empty set. Kuratowski coordinates are bounded by two
nested membership quantifiers through the candidate pair, so no union object
must already belong to the transitive domain. Domain/range formulas include both
no-surplus and no-missing coordinate directions. The result asserts graph
agreement only and correctly avoids claiming that an arbitrary transitive set
is closed under these operations.
