The first example gives crossing population hazards and survival curves with a positive-probability sample pattern whose two logrank contributions cancel, leaving a zero score and positive plug-in variance. The second enumerates a three-assignment design with unequal probabilities, calculates each absolute logrank score and exact p-value, and checks the full superuniform distribution. These examples distinguish an observed score from a survival-law equality claim and distinguish finite Fisher randomization from Gaussian calibration.
