## Independent mathematical basis

The precise claim remains: assuming AC, an auxiliary equation
L X-sharp q_j Y R = q in the embedded rule group, for a special positive
spelling, implies X q_j Y = q in the positive semigroup. Sharp changes signs
in place; it does not reverse word order. Empty tape contexts are allowed,
but every semigroup word still contains its state letter. Reverse semigroup
replacement does not mean a reversed deterministic machine transition.

I independently checked the following complete chain, rather than treating
Sol's earlier risk review as proof.

1. The no-rule-pinches supplier compares reduced auxiliary words and gives the
common count p and central pinch. Its proof uses the embedded infinite cyclic
x subgroup and the trivial intersection with either edge subgroup; its
multiple-letter comparison is derived in the base-groups supplier. No unseen
cyclic pinch is required. At p=0 the free state factor forces q_j=q and both
tape coefficients to be identity. Retraction to the free tape group forces
empty reduced tapes, and infinite order then forces both x exponents to vanish.
2. For the sign test, an expanded reduced word in the sx basis has no internal
tape pinch: a pair of opposite tape letters with the same label would have
come from inverse adjacent basis letters. The reduced word Z has no internal
pinch either. If Z begins negatively, the only possible seam pinch is
s x s-inverse; with s-inverse x s=x^2 it requires x in the even-power subgroup,
which is impossible. The empty-basis case also cannot cancel a nonempty Z.
The cyclic rearrangement x^a u-inverse Z=1 follows by conjugating the original
Z x^a u-inverse=1, so it is legitimate. Theta gives the other subgroup sign;
inversion gives the mirrored final-negative-letter test.
3. In the edge subgroup expression, minimize occurrences of the twisted
state generator. Free-product cancellation of opposite adjacent state
occurrences would force the intervening T_eta element to be identity and
remove two inverse occurrences. Minimality excludes that event. Thus exactly
one positive state occurrence accounts for the single positive state in the
given coefficient. Its two tape coefficients give the equations of step 2.2.
Equivalently, the supplier's state-twisting automorphism reduces this to the
normal form in the subgroup generated by the one state and T_eta.

would make its reduced product with Y begin negatively; a surviving letter of

exclude both, giving literal prefix/suffix divisibility, not merely equality
in the tape group. They also determine the signs of nonempty remainders.

basis. It therefore negates m,n and fixes the signed tape strings. The
replacement equation in 4.1 follows exactly with L3 x^(-m) on the left and
x^(-n) L4 on the right. F3 supplies the map for epsilon=-1 and its inverse
for epsilon=+1. There is no assumption that sharp acts on arbitrary group
