These examples evaluate the one-shot dependence-testing expression and meta-converse on a binary symmetric channel, then give a finite noiseless-channel counterexample separating small average error from maximal error. The calculations retain the null/alternative convention for $\beta_\epsilon$ and distinguish average-error bounds from per-message guarantees.
