## Objective

The examples verify a compact scale hyperprior, balance the theorem's
bandwidth terms explicitly, and distinguish failure of a prior-tail premise
from failure of posterior contraction. A polynomial scale tail falls outside
the stated theorem; the calculations assert no posterior inconsistency or
failure result.
