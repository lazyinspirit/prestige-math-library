## Trivial real principal bundle

[[ex-gauge-group-and-curvature-of-a-trivial-real-principal-bundle]] computes
the full gauge group of $\mathbb R^2\times\mathbb R$ and a nonconstant gauge
actions on its principal connection and an associated line bundle. It computes
the induced multiplier $e^{u(x,y)}$ on the associated line and verifies
directly that the horizontal distribution changes while its vertical curvature
is carried by the bundle automorphism.
