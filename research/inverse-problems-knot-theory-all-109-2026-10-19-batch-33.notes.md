## Authoring checkpoints

- `def-ip-nullspace-property` is level 0. It defines the matrix space and row action, the zero-based to one-based coordinate map, kernel, zero-extended restrictions, support complement, and strict order-s NSP for `1≤s≤n`; it explicitly excludes nonzero residuals. Direct dependencies are `def-matrix-space`, `def-matrix-product-and-identity-matrix`, and `def-p-norms-on-rn`. Sources inspected: Foucart–Rauhut §4.1, Definition 4.1, printed pp. 78–79; MIT notes §6.3, printed pp. 93–94 / PDF pp. 105–106. The MIT notes use supports of exactly size `s`; the definition follows the book’s `|S|≤s` convention. It has no phase proof (`verification.precheck: n/a`).
- `thm-ip-basis-pursuit-exact-recovery-under-nsp` is level 1. It proves strict objective increase for every distinct feasible vector by a row-wise matrix difference calculation, the strict NSP, the one-norm triangle inequality, and disjoint support decomposition. Direct dependencies include `def-ip-nullspace-property`, the finite-dimensional norm and norm property, the norm triangle axiom, and `lem-finite-sum-laws`. The constraint is exact/noiseless; the proof is choice-free and claims no noisy recovery. Sources inspected: Foucart–Rauhut §4.1, Theorems 4.4–4.5, pp. 79–80; MIT §6.3, pp. 93–94.
- `lem-ip-nullspace-property-stability-cone` is level 1. It obtains one uniform `rho<1` by normalizing the nonzero kernel to the Euclidean unit sphere, proving that sphere closed and bounded, maximizing the continuous head-to-tail ratio for each of finitely many supports, then rescaling. The closedness argument uses explicit row-functional and norm neighborhoods, so it does not assume sequential closure or Countable Choice. Direct dependencies include the NSP definition, Euclidean inner product/norm, norm continuity, finite-sum and finite-maximum facts, square monotonicity, Cauchy–Schwarz, and the choice-free Heine–Borel/extreme-value theorem. Source inspected: Foucart–Rauhut §4.2, Definition 4.11 and Remark 4.16, pp. 82–84. The statement gives no nonzero-residual bound.
- `ex-ip-failed-nsp-two-column-nonunique-basis-pursuit` is level 1. The 1-by-2 matrix has a nonzero kernel vector with equality at the singleton support; the exact datum has minimum one-norm one attained by two distinct feasible vectors. The row product formula, one-norm definition, and real absolute-value triangle inequality supply the complete finite verification.
- `ex-ip-nontrivial-order-one-nsp-matrix` is level 2. The 2-by-3 kernel is computed explicitly; the empty support and all three singleton supports are checked for every nonzero kernel multiple, then the local exact-recovery theorem applies. No finite-smoke registry fixture covers NSP/basis pursuit, so the finite arithmetic is proved directly.
- `ex-ip-gaussian-identity-design-minimax-lower-bound` is level 2. For `A=I_d`, the matrix identity gives zero kernel and vacuous strict NSP, while the noisy independent-coordinate law exactly matches the existing Gaussian sparse-sequence minimax theorem. It carries AC and the theorem’s sample-size, dimension, sparsity, noise, estimator, and squared-norm hypotheses; it makes no new noisy basis-pursuit claim.
