## Additional argument checks

The finite-product completeness and reflexivity argument in `lem-w-one-p-is-reflexive` was checked: coordinate Cauchy limits give product completeness, finite successive weak extractions give a product weak subsequence, and a functional on the finite product is the sum of its coordinate restrictions. The bidual transfer is injective because the pullback by the inverse isometry is the inverse of the pullback by the isometry. These short elementary closures do not require an unstated new theorem. The printed Sobolev-completeness proof expressly uses only Countable Choice after AC supplies it, licensing its reuse under DC here.

The Gateaux-versus-Frechet caveat was independently checked with the explicit function F(x,y)=x³y/(x⁶+y²) off zero and F(0,0)=0: along every fixed line the quotient tends to zero, while F(x,x³)=1/2 for x!=0. The preserved caveat is therefore sound. For second variation, the opened Taylor remainder supplier covers the C2 one-variable restriction at order one. For the regularity corollary, subtraction of the smooth extension gives forcing f+Delta G and zero trace; the smooth and Schauder statements have exactly the hypotheses consumed. Trace-lifting boundedness was checked against the actual elliptic-form lemma, which supplies an H1 bound, not just an H1_0 bound.
