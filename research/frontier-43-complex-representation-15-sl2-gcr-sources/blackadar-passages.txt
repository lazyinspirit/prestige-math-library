IV.1.2.
Elementary C*-Algebras
IV.1.2.1.
Definition.
A C*-algebra A is elementary if A ∼= K(H) for
some Hilbert space H.
The separable elementary C*-algebras are the ﬁnite-dimensional matrix al-

An elementary C*-algebra has an obvious “identity representation,” which
is irreducible. It is often convenient to identify the C*-algebra with its image
under this representation.

IV.1.3.1.
Definition. Let A be a C*-algebra, π an irreducible represen-
