## Mathematical and dependency audit

- The total differential is balanced because both bimodule differentials are middle-algebra-linear. Its mixed square terms cancel with signs `(-1)^(p+1)` and `(-1)^p`. Tensoring a homotopy in the second factor requires `(-1)^p`; tensoring one in the first factor requires no extra sign. These identities also verify naturality and composition before localization.
- Reassociation of finite tensor diagonals has differential signs `1`, `(-1)^p`, and `(-1)^(p+q)` on both sides. The comparison `F⊗Cone(u) → Cone(F⊗u)` multiplies the shifted-source component by `(-1)^p`; the comparison for `Cone(v)⊗X` is identity on both components. The connecting maps therefore match, so the full standard cone triangles are preserved.
- Left finite graded projectivity of each bimodule term and the published `thm-bimodule-tensor-exactness-and-projective-preservation` give finite graded projective output terms. Underlying right projectivity gives right flatness by `lem-projective-modules-are-flat-over-an-arbitrary-ring`; the published bounded-above flat-tensor lemma then preserves quasi-isomorphisms. These prove the homotopy-category and derived-category claims separately. There is no finite-dimensional-output assertion.
- The inverse theorem uses the associator, unit maps, and tensoring of the **supplied bimodule-linear** homotopies. It does not apply the preceding projective-bimodule proposition directly to `F⊗G` or `G⊗F`, since right projectivity of those composite bimodules is not among the hypotheses and is unnecessary for the homotopy transfer. The two individual tensor functors have the right-flat hypothesis and hence descend. No coherence of a family of group-action isomorphisms follows.
- The diagonal `k[x]` example defines its special-case enveloping action inline. The kernel of `k[x,y] → k[x]` is `(x-y)`; a splitting would make that nonzero proper ideal idempotent-generated in a domain, a contradiction. It has no forward dependency on the later general enveloping-algebra page. The two-term sign and contractible-complex examples are direct computations.
