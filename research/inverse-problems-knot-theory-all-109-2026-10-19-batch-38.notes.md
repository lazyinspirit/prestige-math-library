## Mathematical conventions and proof route

- `M` is compact, connected, nonempty, and two-dimensional; no orientability assumption is added to the theorem. The orientation cover is defined and proved for manifolds with boundary on this A page; the separate published orientation-cover result is not used because its local-homology definition is stated for boundaryless manifolds. Boundary convexity uses the outward normal convention `II(w,w)=g(∇_w ν_out,w)>0`; incoming vectors use the inward normal.
- The scalar transform is over unit-speed boundary-to-boundary geodesics, with `f ∈ C_c^∞(M°)`. No arbitrary weights or higher-dimensional theorem are claimed.
- The local proof route is: construct the transport primitive from zero data; establish the chartwise geodesic flow, Liouville density, integration by parts and Santaló formula; derive the `X,V,X_perp` commutators; prove fixed-endpoint index-form positivity by the scalar Jacobi factorization; define the tangent orientation double cover and prove it preserves simplicity and zero scalar data; prove the two-dimensional Pestov identity; apply it componentwise on the oriented cover. The final implication is `VXu=0`, so the Pestov right-hand side is a sum of nonnegative terms including `||Xu||²=||f||²`.
- No arbitrary-choice axiom is used. Local ODE existence/smooth dependence is applied in individual charts, and every cover used along a compact geodesic segment is finite. The proof does not invoke the library's countable-choice global geodesic-spray construction.
