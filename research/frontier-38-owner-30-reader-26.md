## Uneditable findings and concurrent resolutions

Remaining: `lem-projection-formula-invertible-twist` is a draft supplier in batch 2, outside this reader's edit scope. In its Statement, the final Euler-characteristic clause says only that X and Y are proper over k and f is a morphism of schemes. It must also require f to respect those k-structures; Proof 3.1 takes k-dimensions of a cohomology isomorphism without establishing k-linearity.

Counterexample: set k=Q(t). Let X and Y have the same underlying scheme Spec Q(u), give X the k-structure t↦u² and Y the k-structure t↦u, and let f be the identity of the underlying scheme. X and Y are finite, hence proper, over k, with degrees 2 and 1 respectively. Take F=O_X and L=O_Y. The identity scheme morphism has f_*O_X=O_Y and all higher direct images zero, while chi_k(X,f*L)=2 and chi_k(Y,L)=1. Thus the advertised clause is false without a k-morphism hypothesis. This is a fatal missing-hypothesis finding for the supplier; the assigned consumer `lem-blowup-intersection-matrix-at-smooth-point`, Facts [F5] and Proof 4.2, uses the actual blowup map over k, so its inference remains valid. Proposed supplier repair: state “f is a k-morphism” in the final clause and explicitly identify the cohomology comparison as k-linear. I did not edit that supplier.
