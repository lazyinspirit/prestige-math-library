## Conventions and source-wide hypotheses

Macdonald uses English diagrams: λ is a weakly decreasing finite sequence, `λ ⊇ μ` means diagram containment, and tableaux are weakly increasing in rows and strictly increasing in columns.  Write `Λ` for the graded ring over `Z`, `Λ_Q = Λ ⊗_Z Q`, `z_λ = ∏_i i^{m_i(λ)}m_i(λ)!`, and `ε_λ=(-1)^{|λ|-ℓ(λ)}`.  The characteristic-map portion takes ordinary complex (more generally characteristic-zero splitting-field) character theory for granted.  Appendix A explicitly assumes an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero.  These restrictions must appear at every proposed symmetric-group item; integral statements about `Λ` must not be silently changed into power-sum statements over `Z`.
