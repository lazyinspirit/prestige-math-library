With $q(t)=q_0(1+t)$, the rational function $\delta(t)=q(t)+q(t)^{-1}$ has a pole at $t=-1$; a nonzero degree-$d$ polynomial in $\delta(t)$ has a pole of order $d$, while a nonzero constant stays nonzero. Its Laurent expansion at $t=0$ is therefore nonzero. This establishes injectivity of polynomial substitution and the nonvanishing needed for the quantum-integer product over every characteristic.
