## Item checkpoints

- `lem-ip-gronwall-with-integrable-coefficient` — Proves both forward and reflected estimates for continuous nonnegative $u$ and measurable $\ell\ge0$ with finite L1 integral. The proof sets $C(t)=\int\ell$, $V=A+\int\ell u$, and $W=e^{-C}V$, proves $W$ is AC, computes $W'\le0$ almost everywhere using CC+DC FTC and the ordinary chain/product rules, then reflects time for the backward form. Explicit degenerate interval and $A=0$ cases are recorded. Direct deps: `def-countable-choice`, `def-dependent-choice`, `def-l-one-of-a-measure`, `cor-the-indefinite-integral-of-an-l-one-function-is-absolutely-continuous`, `thm-first-fundamental-theorem-of-calculus-for-l-one`, `thm-fundamental-theorem-of-calculus-for-absolutely-continuous-functions`, `thm-a-lipschitz-function-after-an-absolutely-continuous-function-is-absolutely-continuous`, `lem-the-product-of-two-absolutely-continuous-functions-is-absolutely-continuous`, `thm-chain-rule`, `lem-derivative-algebra-and-chain-rules`, `thm-derivative-of-exponential`, `lem-mean-value-theorem-on-closed-interval`, `cor-c-one-change-of-variables-for-l-one-functions`. Sources: Teschl §2.4 Lemma 2.7; CMU 2026 ODE notes Lemma 9; both full texts inspected.
- `lem-ip-lipschitz-ode-triangular-flow-and-c1-density` — For jointly continuous drift and uniform-in-space L1 growth/Lipschitz envelopes on compact parameter sets, proves finite-cell Banach contraction construction, global uniqueness, `exp(∫L)` forward and reverse Lipschitz bounds, and joint parameter/initial-state continuity. Its separate C1 branch proves the variational matrix ODE, invertibility from the reverse C1 flow, Jacobi/Liouville determinant formula, and pushforward density by change of variables. It explicitly says global Lipschitzness alone supplies no Jacobian formula. Direct in-run supplier: `lem-ip-gronwall-with-integrable-coefficient`. Sources: Teschl §§2.3–2.6; CMU ODE notes; Folland Theorem 2.47. All relevant passages were inspected.
- `thm-ip-exact-triangular-transport-disintegrates-to-posterior` — Under AC, finite-dimensional Borel joint law, reference probability, triangular Borel map with Borel-bijective fibers, and exact joint pushforward, constructs $\kappa_y=T_y{}_{\#}\eta$ as a probability kernel, proves its rectangle disintegration by Tonelli, and applies simultaneous uniqueness outside one $\pi_Y$-null set. The statement includes the Bayesian posterior interpretation only when $\pi$ is the proper prior/data joint. It assumes no density or C1 formula. Direct in-run suppliers: flow lemma; IP-16 posterior definition. Sources: full Durrett Chapter 4 §4.1.3 and Varadhan Chapter 4 §4.3; local proof does not rely on citation alone.
- `alg-ip-conditional-flow-matching` — Defines independent samples $t\sim U[0,1]$, $X_0\sim\eta$, $(X_1,Y)\sim\pi$, linear interpolation, target $X_1-X_0$, finite empirical squared loss, a named optimizer stopping statistic/tolerance and finite step budget, field-regularity status, and numerical ODE success/failure output. It states that the population minimizer is an almost-sure conditional-expectation class and does not provide an off-support representative. Exact posterior status requires the separate joint pushforward identity; finite training/solver success do not prove it. The IP-23 six-term ledger lacks a learned-flow term, which is separately reported. Direct in-run suppliers: flow lemma; exact transport theorem; IP-16 posterior definition; IP-23 error-budget definition. Sources: Blechschmidt et al. §§3.1–3.2 and Appendix B; Tong et al. Theorem 3.2 and §3.2; Zhai et al. §§3.1–3.2.
- `ex-ip-cfm-low-imh-acceptance-with-accurate-means` — Reports the Blechschmidt et al. v1 preprint's $m=256$, $\gamma^2=10^{-5}$, $n_{KL}=128$, MLP-800 rates $0.66\%,11.21\%,5.97\%,5.47\%$, the $0.8\%$–$1.8\%$ relative-L2 mean/95%-band comparison to DRAM, and the first observation's empirical prior-predictive tail radii. This is a bounded empirical report, not an asymptotic or full-law accuracy claim. Direct deps: the published MH density-ratio lemma and AC. Source: full Blechschmidt §§4.2.1–4.2.2, Fig. 6/Table 1; Roberts–Rosenthal §2.1/Proposition 2.
- `ex-ip-likelihood-free-conditional-flow-matching` — Specifies an interface with prior-predictive samples but no evaluable likelihood or unbiased nonnegative likelihood estimator. It shows that a density-based independent-MH ratio cannot be evaluated from those inputs even when the C1 flow proposal density is available, while keeping exact triangular transport as a logically separate route and qualifying pseudo-marginal alternatives. Direct in-run suppliers: flow density lemma, exact transport theorem, IP-16 posterior definition. Sources: Blechschmidt et al. §§3.1–3.4 and §4.4; Owen Chapter 11 §11.4.
- `op-ip-function-space-flow-matching-error-theory` — Formulates a uniform-on-compact-data W2 error question in a separable Hilbert limit, with posterior model/potential, conditional target, statistical training, optimizer, and numerical solver terms. The status review records finite-dimensional posterior-CFM guarantees and generic Hilbert functional-flow error theorems, then identifies the still-uncovered combined Bayesian-potential/function-space/uniform-conditional result. The review is bounded and explicitly not an exhaustive absence proof. Direct suppliers: IP-16 posterior definition/propriety, IP-17 Hellinger/discretization, IP-23 budget/functional error, flow lemma, exact theorem, algorithm. Sources: Blechschmidt, Zhai–Jeong–Ročková, Shikhman, and Chemseddine et al.; locators and harvested dispositions are in the coverage/source-evidence files.
