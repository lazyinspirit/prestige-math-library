### `lem-regular-value-choice-does-not-change-the-framed-cobordism-class`

- Subject: in-run-dependency; assigned consumer: `lem-mod-two-degree-is-well-defined-and-homotopy-invariant`.
- Location: Statement (unrestricted k and value independence), Fact F6, Proof 4.1 and 5.1, observed pre-reader carrier.
- Class/severity: false-claim / fatal.
Regularity of a smooth homotopy does not imply regularity of its endpoint restrictions: F(x,t)=exp(it), from S^1 x I to S^1, has full differential surjective at every point, but F(-,0) is constant and its image value is critical. The original F6 and step 4.1 make this false inference.
