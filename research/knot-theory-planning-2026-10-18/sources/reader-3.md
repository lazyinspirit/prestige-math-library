# Reader 3: polynomial, skein, quantum and finite-type knot invariants

Read and status snapshot: 2026-10-18. Lane: Jones/Kauffman bracket, HOMFLY-PT and Kauffman polynomials, skein modules, ribbon/Reshetikhin–Turaev invariants, and Vassiliev/Kontsevich theory.

## Findings and exact local coverage

The root library already has a substantial published braid/Hecke route to HOMFLYPT and a generic ribbon-category evaluation of framed colored links, plus a separate writhe-normalized ribbon trace for unframed links. Reuse these only at their written hypotheses. The smallest knot-specific gaps are a direct bracket state sum with its local Reidemeister proof and a locally proved bracket/Temperley–Lieb/Hecke identification. The local Jones specialization states the Hecke-trace specialization and explicitly quotes the Jones trace identification; its companion theorem that the specialized trace descends through the Temperley–Lieb quotient is draft. The Kauffman/Dubrovnik BMW and tangle-basis chain is also draft. No local finite-type/Kontsevich integration chain or general 3-manifold Kauffman bracket skein-module definition was found.

Do not conflate different uses of “skein module.” Joshua Chen’s thesis concerns Temperley–Lieb fusion and Turaev–Viro surface state spaces in a restricted scope; it is not a proof of the general Kauffman bracket skein module of a 3-manifold. Sean Pohorence’s 2016 note defines the latter and sketches a root-of-unity TQFT bridge, but imports the hard Blanchet–Habegger–Masbaum–Vogel results. Keep a root-of-unity surface TQFT pair separate from a general 3-manifold skein-module pair and from ribbon-category evaluation of framed links.

## Exact conventions and equivalences

**Repository HOMFLYPT normalization.** The published coefficient ring is
\[
R=\mathbb Z[v^{\pm1},z,z^{-1},u^{\pm1},s]\big/(s^2-v,\;vzu^2-(z+1-v)).
\]
Put \(z_-:=v^{-1}(z+1-v)\), \(l:=us\), \(m:=s-s^{-1}\), and \(\alpha:=(uz)^{-1}\). Then \(l^{-1}-l=m\alpha\). No inverse of \(m\) is imposed in \(R\). For a braid \(\beta\in B_n\), with exponent sum \(e(\beta)\), Hecke representation \(\pi_n\), and Ocneanu trace \(\operatorname{tr}_n\), the library definition is
\[
P(\widehat\beta)=u^{e(\beta)}\alpha^{n-1}\operatorname{tr}_n(\pi_n(\beta)),
\]
with \(P(\text{unknot})=1\) and empty link separately set to \(uz\). Its published Markov proof uses cyclicity and both Hecke stabilization scalars \(z,z_-\), with \(u\alpha z=1=u^{-1}\alpha z_-\). Its oriented skein convention is \(l^{-1}P_+-lP_-=mP_0\), with positive crossing calibrated to the positive Artin generator. In \(R[m^{-1}]\) only, \(\alpha=(l^{-1}-l)/m\). The Johnson–Freyd notes use a different parameterization \((q,\zeta,\lambda,\mu,\alpha,z)\), with trace-normalizing equations \(\mu\lambda\zeta=1=\mu\lambda^{-1}(q^{-1}\zeta+q^{-1}-1)\); their \(\alpha,z\) must not be silently identified with the repository’s \((uz)^{-1},z\).

**Published Jones specialization in the repository.** Set \(s^2=v=t\), \(z=-1/(v+1)\), \(u=s\); the coefficient-ring map exists after inverting \(v+1\). The resulting nonempty-link invariant satisfies
\[
t^{-1}V_+-tV_-=(t^{1/2}-t^{-1/2})V_0,\qquad V(\text{unknot})=1,
\]
and the positive closure \(\widehat{\sigma_1^3}\) has value \(t+t^3-t^4\). The published definition specializes the Hecke-trace link invariant, not a trace on the Temperley–Lieb quotient. The six-term Hecke parabolic quotient and normalized TL generators are defined, but trace descent is only draft.

**Kauffman bracket normalization.** For an unoriented regular link diagram, each crossing has the two smoothings shown in Kauffman’s bracket convention; name them A and B and attach coefficients \(A\) and \(A^{-1}\), respectively. For the crossing as drawn in Kauffman 1990, printed p. 430, the A smoothing is the horizontal pairing and the B smoothing is the vertical pairing; switching the crossing interchanges the coefficients. A state \(S\) chooses one smoothing at every crossing; if \(a(S),b(S)\) count its A and B choices and \(|S|\) is the number of resulting circles, then
\[
\langle D\rangle=\sum_S A^{a(S)-b(S)}(-A^2-A^{-2})^{|S|-1}.
\]
Thus \(\langle\bigcirc\rangle=1\) and the extra-circle factor is \(\delta=-A^2-A^{-2}\). With Kauffman’s positive/negative curl convention, the curls multiply the bracket by \(-A^3,-A^{-3}\). For an oriented diagram of writhe \(w(D)\),
\[
f_D(A)=(-A^3)^{-w(D)}\langle D\rangle,\qquad V_D(t)=f_D(t^{-1/4}).
\]
A local definition must include crossing and A/B smoothing diagrams and calibrate its oriented positive crossing against the existing positive Artin generator. Kauffman 1990, Proposition 3.2 gives
\[
\langle D\rangle=L_D(-A^3,A+A^{-1}),\quad f_D(A)=F_D(-A^3,A+A^{-1}),\quad
V_D(t)=F_D(-t^{-3/4},t^{-1/4}+t^{1/4}).
\]
This agrees with the repository’s \(t+t^3-t^4\) test. Kauffman 1990 §VI proves regular-isotopy invariance of its two-variable \(L\)-polynomial; §III cites earlier bracket work for part of the bracket RII/RIII verification, so that paper alone is not a complete state-sum proof.

**Kauffman/Dubrovnik convention.** Kauffman’s regular-isotopy polynomial \(L\) has positive/negative curl factors \(a,a^{-1}\); its oriented writhe normalization is \(F_D=a^{-w(D)}L_D\) (Definition 2.4). Its Dubrovnik reparameterization satisfies
\[
D_+-D_-=z(D_0-D_\infty),\quad D_{\text{positive curl}}=aD,\quad
D_{\text{negative curl}}=a^{-1}D,\quad D_{\bigcirc}=1.
\]
The printed Lickorish conversion is \(D_K(a,z)=-i^{-w(K)}(-1)^{c(K)}L_K(ia,-iz)\), \(i^2=-1\), where \(c(K)\) is component count; Kauffman explicitly omits proof of this proposition. The local BMW draft uses \(g_i-g_i^{-1}=z(1-e_i)\), \(g_ie_i=\rho^{-1}e_i\), \(e_i^2=\delta e_i\), and \(z(\delta-1)=\rho-\rho^{-1}\). This is a different algebra presentation; match it to Dubrovnik by an explicit dictionary, including the skein sign.

**Framing and ribbon evaluation.** Regular isotopy uses Reidemeister II and III but not I. With blackboard framing, Reidemeister I changes band framing by one, so framed-link evaluation does not imply invariance under unframed Markov stabilization. The published local framed oriented tangle category fixes collars, orientations, framing homotopy classes and crossing/twist generators. A ribbon object defines a functor on that category; the ribbon trace equals the blackboard-framed braid-closure evaluation. Only under the additional scalar-twist hypothesis (absolutely simple \(X\) and \(\theta_X=\lambda\,\mathrm{id}\)) does multiplying by \(\lambda^{-w(\beta)}\) correct both Markov stabilizations and produce an unframed oriented link invariant. Do not apply that normalization to an object with non-scalar twist.

## Source-derived proof recipes and local obligations

**Bracket and Jones.** The finite state-sum algorithm takes a finite regular link diagram, resolves each of \(n\) crossings in two ways, and sums the \(2^n\) states with the displayed edge weights and circle factor. Termination follows from finiteness. For correctness, include the local pictures in the contract and verify that the loop parameter makes RII cancel; then expand both local RIII diagrams and pair terms using that RII relation. R1 is not a bracket invariance: calculate its curl factors and prove the writhe-normalized \(f_D\) is unchanged when a curl is added or removed. Derive the Jones substitution from that normalized bracket and check both the skein relation and positive trefoil calibration. Kauffman 1990 §III leaves part of the bracket argument to cited earlier work; Kauffman 2022 gives an accessible diagrammatic proof map. A local pair should make each finite case comparison explicit.

**HOMFLYPT/Hecke.** Reuse the published ring, trace construction, braid representation and Markov theorem at their exact hypotheses. The proof route is: verify the braid-to-Hecke representation from the quadratic relation; compute positive stabilization using \(z\), negative stabilization using \(z_-\); use trace cyclicity for conjugation; use Markov for independence of braid representative; derive skein from the Hecke quadratic relation and coefficient map. Keep empty-link value \(uz\) separate because usual link normalization is for nonempty closures. Original HOMFLY and Jones/Hecke papers were not available as complete texts here; the recipe uses complete lecture notes and actual local theorem bodies, not inaccessible abstracts.

**Temperley–Lieb and bracket bridge.** The local published Jones specialization factors the coefficient ring through \(z=-1/(v+1)\), \(u=s\), \(v=s^2\), and proves a TL generator quotient after inverting \(v+1\). It does not prove that the specialized trace descends to the quotient; that theorem exists only as draft item thm-temperley-lieb-hecke-markov-trace-descends-at-jones-specialization. The draft route checks \(T_i\mapsto qU_i-1\), kills the six-term parabolic ideal, identifies normalized closure trace, and uses the free Hecke tower for uniqueness. A separate local diagrammatic comparison is needed to identify this trace with the bracket state sum under the exact \(A,t\) dictionary. Do not call the external Jones trace statement a completed local proof.

**Kauffman/BMW.** Do not reuse drafts as proved suppliers. The local candidate chain (def-bmw-algebra-with-fixed-kauffman-skein-convention, lem-framed-kauffman-link-evaluation, thm-bmw-kauffman-tangle-basis) has detailed conventions and a descending-tangle proof route, but all are draft. Reconcile BMW \(q,\rho,\delta,z\) with Dubrovnik \(a,s,d\), including skein sign, curl direction, isolated circle value, coefficient ring and inversions. For the basis theorem separately prove spanning by crossing-switch descent, uniqueness of descending classes indexed by perfect matchings, and independence (the draft uses a closure-pairing determinant followed by base change). Kauffman 1990 proves \(L\) regular-isotopy invariant by induction but explicitly omits the Dubrovnik conversion proof.

**Ribbon/Reshetikhin–Turaev.** Existing local theorem bodies give a proof skeleton: present the framed tangle category with boundary collars; prove braid, dual, snake, bent-inverse and twist relations from braiding/ribbon axioms; extend uniquely as a monoidal functor. Prove nested cup-cap closure equals categorical trace before calling the trace a link evaluation. Framed isotopy follows from the functor; ordinary Markov stabilization adds a full twist and is not framed isotopy. Unframed invariance requires the separate scalar-twist lemma and exponent/writhe correction. To connect this generic result to Jones/HOMFLYPT, the local track still needs an explicit representation/object, quantum trace and normalization, a proof the trace agrees with existing Hecke/Ocneanu or TL closure trace, and exact coefficient substitution. A citation to RT or quantum-group theory is not that bridge.

**3-manifold bracket skein modules and TQFT.** Define the module as the free coefficient module on framed-link isotopy classes in the specified oriented 3-manifold modulo the two local bracket relations, with framing convention and parameter ring fixed. Prove quotient relations are local/isotopy-compatible and give generators only where established; the module is not generally free. For a root-of-unity TQFT separately prove the negligible/Jones–Wenzl quotient and simple-object/fusion data, finite-dimensional surface state spaces, handlebody skein basis, nondegenerate gluing pairing, and surgery/Kirby-color invariance. Chen gives a detailed route for certain surface TV state spaces and spine moves but imports recoupling identities; Pohorence gives an overview only. State BHMW/Morita hypotheses and each local proof dependency explicitly.

**Finite type and Kontsevich.** Define the singular-knot extension by \(V(K_\times)=V(K_+)-V(K_-)\), order by vanishing on sufficiently high-codimension singular knots, and symbol on chord diagrams. Derive 4T from a codimension-two crossing event; include FI exactly when forgetting framing. To integrate a weight system via the Kontsevich integral, local proof modules must establish: (i) completed chord-diagram algebra and quotient relations; (ii) infinitesimal braid/4T relations and flatness of the KZ connection using Arnold’s identity; (iii) convergence/finiteness of configuration-space sum, including endpoint/critical-point regularization and sharp-needle estimates; (iv) braid-isotopy invariance by flat holonomy; (v) invariance through knot Morse critical events using correction \(\widetilde Z(K)=Z(K)/Z(\infty)^{c/2}\) with exact critical-point/framing convention; and (vi) the associated-graded leading-term identity making the integral universal. Bar-Natan–Stoimenow §2 is a proof map, not a complete analytic proof. Bar-Natan’s braid result separates pure braids but does not establish knot-pair separation. The 2026 Jones claim, if independently validated, implies Jones finite-type coefficients detect the unknot; it does not assert finite-type invariants distinguish every pair of nonisotopic knots.
