# Knot Theory category and pathway design

Category `knot-theory`, title **Knot Theory**, 54 A/B pairs / 108 candidate pages. Orders are provisional rebasing inputs; no registry is changed.

## Category overview

Knot Theory studies oriented classical knots and links in the standard three-sphere through diagrams, peripheral topology, surfaces and algebraic invariants. This category reuses the existing smooth link/Reidemeister/braid/Hecke/ribbon interfaces, adds precise classical and computational constructions, and separates an algorithm from the theorem that makes its output invariant. Its pathways lead from Wirtinger groups and Seifert matrices through link nilpotent invariants, geometry of exteriors, concordance and four-dimensional obstructions, skein/quantum/finite-type invariants and categorification. Smooth, PL and locally-flat categories have distinct definitions. Framed, unframed and decorated-cobordism theories retain their own conventions. Deep established theorems with missing local proofs remain explicit future authoring and source-recovery obligations. Dated open questions and the separate Jones claimed-solution status record are terminal nonlogical context. Each A page has a companion of concrete calculations and boundary examples; no companion item is a logical supplier. This is a research scaffold, not a proof-complete or publication-ready library frontier.
