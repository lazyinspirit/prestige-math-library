Summary. In this paper the authors show that the largest eigenvalue of 
the sample covariance matrix tends to a limit under certain conditions when 
both the number of variables and the sample size tend to infinity. The above 
result is proved under the mild restriction that the fourth moment of the 
elements of the sample sums of squares and cross products (SP) matrix exist.

In many situations of multivariate statistical inference, the statistics are 
involved with the eigenvalues of sample covarianee matrices and/or those of 
multivariate F matrices. However, when the dimension and degrees of freedom 
are both large, the computation of such statistics directly from the covariance 
matrices or the multivariate F matrices is usually not easy. Since such statistics 
are usually an integral of certain functions with respect to the spectral distribu- 
tion of the covariance matrices or that of the multivariate F matrices, it is 
of interest to find the limiting distribution of such spectral distributions and 
then to use the integral with respect to the limit approximating the real one. 
In recent years, the study in this area has received a considerable amount of 
attention. For some works of this area, the reader is referred to Bai, Yin and 
Krishnaiah (1986, 1987), Grenander and Silverstein (1977), Jonsson (1982), 
Wachter (1978, 1980), Yin (1986), Yin et al. (1983) and Yin and Krishnaiah 
(1983, 1986a, b).
