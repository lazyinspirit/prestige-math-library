The Bernoulli calculation gives an exact binomial-weight formula for typical-set size, including a finite block check and the fair and degenerate endpoints. Two finite-alphabet source sequences isolate the IID hypotheses: perfect dependence keeps identical marginals but loses the marginal-entropy limit, while independent non-identical block laws can have oscillating normalized information and no constant information-rate limit.
