The examples calculate binary KL, Rényi, and total-variation divergences,
then check how zero coordinates determine singular divergence values. The
testing example uses P as the null, Q as the alternative, and a rejection
function whose value is the probability of rejecting P. It computes the
type-I budget, the alternative acceptance error, and the likelihood-ratio
boundary randomization exactly.
