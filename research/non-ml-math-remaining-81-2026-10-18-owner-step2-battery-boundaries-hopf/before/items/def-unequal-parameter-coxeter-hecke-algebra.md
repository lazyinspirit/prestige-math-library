## Definition

Let $(W,S)$, $\Gamma$, $A=\mathbb Z[\Gamma]$, and $L:W\to\Gamma$ be as in [[def-arbitrary-rank-coxeter-system-and-bruhat-order]] and [[def-unequal-parameter-weight-function-and-coefficient-order]]. For $s\in S$, write $q_s=v^{L(s)}\in A^\times$. Let $F=A\langle T_s:s\in S\rangle$ be the free associative $A$-algebra and let $I$ be the two-sided ideal generated by
$$
(T_s-q_s)(T_s+q_s^{-1})\quad(s\in S)
$$
and, for each distinct $s,t\in S$ with finite $m(s,t)$, the equality of the two alternating products of $T_s,T_t$ having $m(s,t)$ factors. Define the **unequal-parameter Coxeter Hecke algebra** by $H_A(W,L)=F/I$, and use $T_s$ for the image of a generator. For $m(s,t)=\infty$ there is no braid relation between $T_s$ and $T_t$.

The quadratic relation is equivalently
$$
T_s^2=(q_s-q_s^{-1})T_s+1,
$$
so each $T_s$ is invertible with $T_s^{-1}=T_s-(q_s-q_s^{-1})$. The basis, bar, and Bruhat support properties are not part of this definition; they are proved in [[lem-unequal-parameter-hecke-standard-basis-and-bar-triangularity]].
