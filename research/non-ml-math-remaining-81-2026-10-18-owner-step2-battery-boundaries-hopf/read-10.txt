## Statement

Assume the inherited Axiom of Choice. Use the simply-laced full-weight datum and root parameter of [[def-full-toral-weight-root-of-unity-module-category]], with $\ell=\operatorname{ord}(\zeta^2)$ odd and $\ell>h$. Write $L=\operatorname{ord}(\zeta)$, so $L=\ell$ or $2\ell$. In the toral quotient $\overline U=U^L_\zeta/(K_i^L-1:i\in I)$ let $u$ be the ordinary-generator image generated by all $E_i,F_i,K_i^{\pm1}$. This quotient acts on every full type-1 module; it is not an assertion that $K_i^\ell=1$ in the unmodified algebra.

For every reduced convex root ordering, $u^\pm$ have the ordinary root monomial bases with every exponent in $\{0,\ldots,\ell-1\}$, and multiplication gives $u^-\otimes\mathbb C[(\mathbb Z/L\mathbb Z)^I]\otimes u^+\cong u$. Thus $\dim u=L^{|I|}\ell^{2|\Phi^+|}$. The braid symmetries specialize to automorphisms of $u$ and transport these triangular decompositions to every transformed Borel. For $p_i=\langle u^0,u^+,F_i\rangle$, $u$ is free on both sides over $p_i$; complementary negative-root monomials are right-module basis elements when the negative convex ordering ends in $\alpha_i$. The same freeness holds after braid transport. All these assertions retain the formal $P$-grading on modules.

The ordinary negative Borel $u^-u^0$ is a finite Hopf algebra; no claim is made that a general braid-transformed Borel is a Hopf subalgebra.


## Proof

**Given:** These data.

1.1 For each root vector the actual integral identity $X_\beta^\ell=[\ell]_\zeta!X_\beta^{(\ell)}=0$ follows from [F1]; its divided root vector exists before specializing. Ordinary monomial spanning therefore truncates every exponent to less than $\ell$. Each remaining ordinary monomial is a nonzero scalar multiple of a distinct specialized divided basis vector, since every $[r]_\zeta!$ with $r<\ell$ is nonzero. They are consequently independent and form the asserted residue bases of the two image halves. This proves both spanning and independence without assuming a presentation of the small algebra. [F1, algebra]

2.1 Every $K_i^L$ is central at the root. By [F2], its central ideal has triangular vector-space form $U^-\otimes\langle K_i^L-1\rangle U^0\otimes U^+$. In the toral quotient the powers $\prod_iK_i^{b_i}$, $0\le b_i<L$, are independent: the formal integral labels have independently varying simple-coroot coordinates, and evaluating a linear relation on these toral characters gives every character of $(\mathbb Z/L\mathbb Z)^I$. Multiply by each inverse character and sum; nontrivial character sums vanish by translation and the trivial sum is $L^{|I|}$, so every coefficient is zero. Mixed ordinary reordering uses only $(\zeta-\zeta^{-1})^{-1}$ and puts all words in negative-torus-positive order. Combining the toral independence with [F2] and step 1.1 proves the stated full triangular basis and dimension. Integral type-1 labels satisfy $K_i^L=1$, so passing to this quotient loses none of the selected module actions. [F1, F2, F3, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 The symmetries preserve the ordinary form and its inverse before specializing and preserve $U_A$ itself by [F1]. They therefore induce inverse maps on the actual image, rather than merely on an abstract presentation. Their toral images are $K_i^{-1}$ on color $i$ and $K_jK_i$ on a neighbor, so they preserve the ideal generated by $K_j^L-1$. Consequently they are automorphisms of $u$. Applying any such automorphism to the triangular basis gives the asserted transformed basis. A module transported by such a symmetry has its formal labels reflected along with the torus; formal labels are retained even where torus characters coincide. [F1, F3, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 Choose a negative convex ordering ending in $\alpha_i$. Mixed ordinary reordering shows that $p_i$ has basis $F_i^rK^bE^{\mathbf c}$ with $r<\ell$, $0\le b_j<L$, and all positive residue exponents $\mathbf c$. Indeed other $E_j$ commute with $F_i$ and the repeated-color commutator introduces only a toral element, so these words span; they are part of the basis of step 2.1, hence independent. The full triangular basis is the product of a monomial in the complementary negative root factors and one such $p_i$ basis vector. This is precisely a free right-module decomposition of $u$ over $p_i$. Its inverse antipode preserves $u$ and $p_i$ by the ordinary formulas in [F2], converting it into a free left-module decomposition as well. Applying the algebra automorphisms of step 3.1 proves the transformed parabolic statements. No inference from equality of characters to exact induction has been used. [F1, F2, step 2.1, step 3.1, algebra]

5.1 The ordinary coproduct, counit and antipode preserve the negative Borel $u^-u^0$: $\Delta(F_i)=F_i\otimes1+K_i\otimes F_i$, $\Delta(K_i)=K_i\otimes K_i$, $S(F_i)=-K_i^{-1}F_i$, and $S(K_i)=K_i^{-1}$. The toral relations are Hopf relations, so these descend from [F2]. The Borel is finite by step 2.1, hence is a finite Hopf algebra. Its negative augmentation ideal times the Borel is nilpotent by positive root height and has semisimple finite-torus quotient. These facts are available for the finite-Hopf Frobenius socle argument. General transformed Borels are obtained as algebras, which is all the parabolic induction argument requires. This proves all assertions. [F2, step 2.1, step 3.1, step 4.1, algebra] ∎
