## Verification

**Given:** The proposed $B_2$ root datum and affine Hecke parameters.

1.1 The vectors $e_1$ and $\tfrac12(e_1+e_2)$ form a basis of $X$, while $e_1-e_2$ and $2e_2$ form a basis of $Y$: an integer pair $(a,b)$ has even coordinate sum exactly when $(a,b)=a(e_1-e_2)+\frac{a+b}{2}(2e_2)$. The dot-product matrix between these two bases is the identity, so the pairing is perfect and integral. The roots lie in $X$, the coroots lie in $Y$, and direct pairings on $\alpha_1,\alpha_2$ give the Cartan matrix $\begin{pmatrix}2&-2\\-1&2\end{pmatrix}$. The signed-permutation group generated by $s_1,s_2$ preserves both displayed root sets and is finite. Thus [F1] applies and these data are a reduced based $B_2$ root datum. [F1, given, algebra]

1.2 The simple walls of $\mathcal A_0$ are $x_1=x_2$, $x_2=0$, and $x_1+x_2=1$; the other positive-root functions $x_1$ and $x_1-x_2$ lie strictly between $0$ and $1$ in this region, so no other integer root wall cuts it. Their reflections are respectively the displayed $s_1,s_2,s_0$. The product $s_1s_2$ is $(x_1,x_2)\mapsto(-x_2,x_1)$, a quarter-turn of order $4$. The product $s_2s_0$ is $(x_1,x_2)\mapsto(1-x_2,x_1-1)$; it fixes $(1,0)$ and its linear part about that point is the same quarter-turn, so it has order $4$. The product $s_0s_1$ is $(x_1,x_2)\mapsto(1-x_1,1-x_2)$, a half-turn of order $2$. Hence the affine Coxeter labels are $m(s_1,s_2)=m(s_2,s_0)=4$ and $m(s_1,s_0)=2$. [F3, given, algebra]

2.1 The affine roots defining $s_0$ and $s_1$ are long and the one defining $s_2$ is short. The Coxeter labels just computed are all even, so the odd-edge graph has three singleton components. Therefore $q_{s_0}=2$, $q_{s_1}=2$, and $q_{s_2}=3$ are constant on every required component by [F4]. In the symmetric convention the corresponding parameters are $v_{s_0}=v_{s_1}=\sqrt2$ and $v_{s_2}=\sqrt3$, with the quadratic relations of [F2]. [F2, F4, step 1.2, algebra]

3.1 Here $Y=Q^\vee$, so the extended group has trivial length-zero quotient and $\Omega=\{1\}$. Applying [L1] to the affine Coxeter system identified in step 1.2, with the valid affine Coxeter parameters from step 2.1, shows that $\{T_w:w\in W_{\mathrm{aff}}\}$ is a $\mathbb C$-basis. This verifies the claimed unequal-parameter affine $B_2$ example. [F2, L1, step 1.1, step 1.2, step 2.1] ∎
