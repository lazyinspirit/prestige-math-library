## Example

Let $E=\mathbb R^2$ with the dot product and standard vectors $e_1,e_2$. Set
$$
Y=\mathbb Z(e_1-e_2)+\mathbb Z(2e_2)=\{(a,b)\in\mathbb Z^2:a+b\text{ is even}\},\qquad X=\mathbb Z e_1+\mathbb Z\tfrac12(e_1+e_2).
$$
The displayed bases of $X$ and $Y$ are dual under the dot product. Let
$$
\Phi=\{\pm e_1,\pm e_2,\pm(e_1+e_2),\pm(e_1-e_2)\},
$$
with coroot $\alpha^\vee=2\alpha$ for the short roots $\alpha=\pm e_i$ and $\alpha^\vee=\alpha$ for the long roots $\alpha=\pm e_1\pm e_2$. Choose $\Delta=\{\alpha_1=e_1-e_2,\alpha_2=e_2\}$. The affine alcove is
$$
\mathcal A_0=\{(x_1,x_2):0<x_2<x_1,\ x_1+x_2<1\}.
$$
Its simple reflections are $s_1(x_1,x_2)=(x_2,x_1)$, $s_2(x_1,x_2)=(x_1,-x_2)$, and $s_0(x_1,x_2)=(1-x_2,1-x_1)$. The affine Iwahori–Matsumoto parameters are $q_{s_0}=q_{s_1}=2$ on the long-root walls and $q_{s_2}=3$ on the short-root wall.
