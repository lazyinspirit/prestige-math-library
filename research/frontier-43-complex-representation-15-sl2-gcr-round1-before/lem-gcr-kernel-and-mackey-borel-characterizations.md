## Statement

Assume AC. For a separable C*-algebra $A$, the following are equivalent: every irreducible image contains nonzero compacts (GCR); the primitive-kernel map is injective, hence a homeomorphism onto $\operatorname{Prim}(A)$; the Mackey dual ([[def-mackey-borel-structure-and-countable-separation]]) is countably separated; the Mackey dual is standard Borel. In these cases the Mackey Borel structure equals the topology-generated Borel structure. Moreover GCR implies every factor representation has a type-I generated von Neumann algebra. The converse factor-type-I implies GCR is not asserted or used in this lemma.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC; a separable C*-algebra $A$; the primitive-kernel map from unitary equivalence classes of irreducible representations to primitive ideals; and the Mackey Borel structure on the unitary dual.

[F1] The run-local H2 suppliers: bounded self-adjoint density and finite-vector transitivity for concrete C*-algebras; faithful essential pure-state orbits obstruct countable separation; primitive ideals carry standard Borel quotient-norm codings, and the kernel map is continuous and open onto its image; saturated Borel images of the pure-state space are Borel by local analytic separation ([[lem-bounded-density-and-finite-vector-transitivity-for-c-star-representations]], [[lem-faithful-essential-pure-state-orbits-obstruct-countable-separation]], [[lem-primitive-ideals-have-standard-borel-quotient-norm-codings]], [[lem-local-analytic-separation-and-saturated-borel-quotients]]).

[F2] The Mackey Borel structure is the quotient Borel structure on the unitary dual induced by the representation spaces on fixed carriers, and countable separation and standardness are the usual Borel-space notions ([[def-mackey-borel-structure-and-countable-separation]]).

[F3] Every positive compact self-adjoint operator has a nonzero eigenvalue with finite-dimensional spectral projection, which lies in the C*-algebra generated by the operator; finite-rank operators are norm dense in the compacts, and the compact operators form a closed two-sided *-ideal of $\mathcal B(H)$ ([[thm-spectral-theorem-for-compact-self-adjoint-operators]], [[cor-finite-rank-operators-are-norm-dense-in-compact-hilbert-space-operators]], [[def-compact-linear-operator]]).

[F4] Matrix units of an irreducible C*-algebra: a nonzero *-ideal of an irreducible concrete C*-algebra contains the compacts; the closed span of a system of matrix units is an elementary C*-algebra whose nondegenerate irreducible representations are all unitarily equivalent to the defining one; and a representation that is nondegenerate on such an essential ideal extends uniquely to the ambient algebra by strong limits along an approximate unit ([[def-matrix-units]], [[lem-c-star-algebras-and-closed-ideals-have-positive-contractive-approximate-units]], [[def-von-neumann-algebra-and-commutant]]).

[F5] A separable type-I factor is a multiple of an irreducible representation; in particular a factor generated by the image of an elementary C*-algebra is type I ([[lem-separable-type-i-factors-are-multiples-of-irreducible-representations]], [[lem-polar-decomposition-and-nonzero-partial-isometries-in-factors]]).

[F6] AC supplies the approximate units, finite-rank witnesses and maximal orthogonal families used below, and is inherited by all cited suppliers ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]).

## Proof

**Given:** AC; a separable C*-algebra $A$; the primitive-kernel map from unitary equivalence classes of irreducible representations to primitive ideals; and the Mackey Borel structure on the unitary dual.

[F1] The run-local H2 suppliers: bounded self-adjoint density and finite-vector transitivity for concrete C*-algebras; faithful essential pure-state orbits obstruct countable separation; primitive ideals carry standard Borel quotient-norm codings, and the kernel map is continuous and open onto its image; saturated Borel images of the pure-state space are Borel by local analytic separation ([[lem-bounded-density-and-finite-vector-transitivity-for-c-star-representations]], [[lem-faithful-essential-pure-state-orbits-obstruct-countable-separation]], [[lem-primitive-ideals-have-standard-borel-quotient-norm-codings]], [[lem-local-analytic-separation-and-saturated-borel-quotients]]).

[F2] The Mackey Borel structure is the quotient Borel structure on the unitary dual induced by the representation spaces on fixed carriers, and countable separation and standardness are the usual Borel-space notions ([[def-mackey-borel-structure-and-countable-separation]]).

[F3] Every positive compact self-adjoint operator has a nonzero eigenvalue with finite-dimensional spectral projection, which lies in the C*-algebra generated by the operator; finite-rank operators are norm dense in the compacts, and the compact operators form a closed two-sided *-ideal of $\mathcal B(H)$ ([[thm-spectral-theorem-for-compact-self-adjoint-operators]], [[cor-finite-rank-operators-are-norm-dense-in-compact-hilbert-space-operators]], [[def-compact-linear-operator]]).

[F4] Matrix units of an irreducible C*-algebra: a nonzero *-ideal of an irreducible concrete C*-algebra contains the compacts; the closed span of a system of matrix units is an elementary C*-algebra whose nondegenerate irreducible representations are all unitarily equivalent to the defining one; and a representation that is nondegenerate on such an essential ideal extends uniquely to the ambient algebra by strong limits along an approximate unit ([[def-matrix-units]], [[lem-c-star-algebras-and-closed-ideals-have-positive-contractive-approximate-units]], [[def-von-neumann-algebra-and-commutant]]).

[F5] A separable type-I factor is a multiple of an irreducible representation; in particular a factor generated by the image of an elementary C*-algebra is type I ([[lem-separable-type-i-factors-are-multiples-of-irreducible-representations]], [[lem-polar-decomposition-and-nonzero-partial-isometries-in-factors]]).

[F6] AC supplies the approximate units, finite-rank witnesses and maximal orthogonal families used below, and is inherited by all cited suppliers ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]).



**Proof technique:** a compact-ideal/amplification analysis of irreducible images, the kernel map, and the run-local category and coding lemmas.

**Given:** AC; the separable C*-algebra $A$; an irreducible representation $\rho$ with image $\rho(A)$.

1.1 Compact-ideal lemma: if $\rho(A)\cap K(H)\neq\{0\}$, then $K(H)\subseteq\rho(A)$. Indeed $K(H)\cap\rho(A)$ is a nonzero closed two-sided $*$-ideal of $\rho(A)$; choosing a nonzero positive $T$ in it, [F3] gives a nonzero finite-rank spectral projection $P\in K(H)\cap\rho(A)$, and the corner $P\rho(A)P$ is an irreducible C*-subalgebra of the finite-dimensional algebra $\mathcal B(PH)$, hence equals $\mathcal B(PH)$ by the finite-dimensional double commutant theorem; the operators $\theta_{\xi,\eta}$ with $\xi\in PH$ are therefore approximated in norm by elements of the ideal, and since $\{b^*\xi_0:b\in\rho(A)\}$ is dense in $H$ for a unit $\xi_0\in PH$ by irreducibility, all rank-one operators, and with them $K(H)$ by [F3], lie in $\rho(A)$. [F3, F5, algebra]

2.1 Elementary-ideal uniqueness: if $\rho$ is irreducible with $\rho(A)\cap K(H)\neq\{0\}$, then $I_\rho:=\rho^{-1}(K(H))$ is a nonzero closed two-sided $*$-ideal of $A$ with $\rho(I_\rho)=K(H)$ by step 1.1, so $I_\rho/\ker\rho\cong K(H)$ is an elementary C*-algebra; every nondegenerate irreducible representation of $I_\rho$ is unitarily equivalent to the defining one by [F4]. [F4, step 1.1]

3.1 Kernel injectivity under GCR: assume every irreducible image of $A$ contains nonzero compacts, and let $\rho,\sigma$ be irreducible representations with $\ker\rho=\ker\sigma=:J$. Then $I:=I_\rho$ and $I_\sigma$ both contain $J$, and passing to $A/J$ the restrictions of $\rho$ and $\sigma$ to $I$ are nonzero and irreducible: nonzero because $I\not\subseteq J$ (as $\rho(I)=K(H)\neq0$), and irreducible because any closed $\sigma(I)$-invariant subspace is invariant under $\sigma(I)+\sigma(A)=\sigma(A)$. Since $I$ is elementary, these two restrictions are unitarily equivalent by step 2.1; a unitary $u$ intertwining them intertwines the full representations as well, because both are determined by their restrictions to the essential ideal $I$ through strong limits along an approximate unit of $I$ by [F4]. Hence $\rho\cong\sigma$, so the kernel map is injective. [F4, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 Under GCR the kernel map is also a homeomorphism: it is continuous and open by the coding supplier of [F1], and injective by step 3.1, and a continuous open bijection between Borel spaces is a Borel isomorphism; combining with the saturated-image lemma of [F1] shows that the Mackey Borel structure and the topology-generated Borel structure of $\operatorname{Prim}(A)$ coincide, so the Mackey dual is standard Borel and, standardness providing a countable separating family, countably separated. [F1, step 3.1, algebra]

4.2 GCR implies factor representations are type I: let $\rho$ be a factor representation with $M:=\rho(A)''$ a factor, and let $J=\ker\rho$. If $J$ were not primitive, then by the primitive-coding supplier of [F1] applied to the separable algebra $A/J$ there would be two nonzero ideals of $A/J$ with zero product; their images under the faithful extension of $\rho$ to $A/J$ would be nonzero orthogonal ideals of the prime C*-algebra $\rho(A)$, whose support projections in the factor $M$ would be nonzero orthogonal central projections, impossible. Hence $J$ is primitive, so $\rho$, viewed through $A/J$, is an irreducible representation with image containing nonzero compacts by GCR; let $I=\rho^{-1}(K(H))$ be the corresponding elementary ideal. [F1, F5, step 3.1]

5.1 Conversely, if the Mackey dual is countably separated, then $A$ is GCR: for a separable algebra that is not GCR, the category lemma of [F1] produces a primitive quotient with a faithful irreducible essential representation whose faithful pure-state orbits are dense and meager, and consequently the faithful irreducible classes are not countably separated; restriction of a hypothetical countable separating family contradicts this. [F1, step 4.1]

6.1 Finally, standard Borel implies countably separated, so the four conditions are equivalent, and under them the Borel structures agree by step 4.1. [F2, step 4.1, step 5.1]

7.1 The image $\rho(I)$ is an elementary essential ideal of $\rho(A)$ acting irreducibly on $H$, so $\rho(I)''=\rho(A)''$: indeed $\rho(I)$ is nonzero and $\rho(I)$-invariant subspaces are $\rho(A)$-invariant, and the identity is obtained as a strong limit of $\rho(e_\lambda)$ along an approximate unit of $I$ by [F4]. Since $\rho(I)\cong K(E)$ is elementary, its generated von Neumann algebra is a type-I factor: by [F5] it is isomorphic to $\mathcal B(E)$ because a nonzero irreducible representation of $K(E)$ generates $\mathcal B(E)$ and the double commutant of the matrix units is the full algebra. Hence $M=\rho(A)''=\rho(I)''$ is a type-I factor, proving that every factor representation of a GCR algebra has type-I generated von Neumann algebra. [F4, F5, step 4.2, algebra] ∎



The zero ideal case: if $A$ is simple and elementary, $\operatorname{Prim}(A)$ is a point and every irreducible is a multiple of one class, so injectivity, standardness and countable separation are immediate; the compact-ideal argument is unaffected. One-dimensional Hilbert spaces reduce the compact ideal to $\mathbb C$ and the matrix-unit argument to scalars. If $A$ is GCR but not unital, approximate units of the ideals replace the identity in steps 3.1, 4.2 and 7.1, and no unit is assumed. If the Mackey dual is empty the countable-separation condition is vacuous and the implications hold trivially for the zero algebra. The factor-type-I implication is proved only in the forward direction; the converse is explicitly not asserted, by the Statement. Choice is used exactly as recorded in [F6] and the axiom-use field.



Blackadar, Part IV §IV.1.2-IV.1.3, printed pp. 346-349, contains the compact-ideal amplification, the uniqueness of irreducible representations of elementary algebras and the GCR-to-factor-type-I route; the component arguments are expanded above in steps 1.1-3.1 and 7.1-8.1 rather than cited as complete. Farah, Theorem 3.7.1, printed p. 107, refers the classification out and is not used as a proof. The category, coding, separation and countable-separation obstruction are supplied by the four run-local H2 lemmas of [F1]; the present item adds the elementary-ideal, kernel-injectivity and factor-type-I components and assembles the equivalences. The converse implication factor-type-I $\Rightarrow$ GCR is deliberately absent: it is the owner-authorized Glimm citation branch of `lem-separable-group-c-star-type-i-and-smooth-dual-criteria`.
