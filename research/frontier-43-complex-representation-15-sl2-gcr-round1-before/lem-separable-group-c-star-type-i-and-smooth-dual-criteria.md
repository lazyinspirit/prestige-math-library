## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $G$ be a second-countable locally compact group with separable full group C\*-algebra $C^*(G)$, primitive ideal space $\operatorname{Prim}(C^*(G))$ with the Jacobson topology, unitary dual $\widehat G$ with the Mackey Borel structure ([[def-mackey-borel-structure-and-countable-separation]]) and Fell topology ([[def-unitary-dual-of-a-locally-compact-group]], [[def-fell-topology-on-the-unitary-dual]], [[def-primitive-ideal-space-of-a-group-c-star-algebra]]). Then the following are equivalent: (i) $G$ is type I (every factor representation is a multiple of an irreducible); (ii) the Mackey Borel structure on $\widehat G$ and the Borel structure generated by the Fell topology coincide and $\widehat G$ is a standard Borel space; (iii) $\widehat G$ is countably separated; (iv) the canonical map $\kappa:\widehat G\to\operatorname{Prim}(C^*(G))$ is a homeomorphism onto its image in the hull-kernel/Fell conventions, i.e. the type I, smooth-dual and primitive-ideal criteria agree.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC; the second-countable LCH group $G$; the structures of the Statement; and the conventions of the cited definitions.

[F1] Strongly continuous unitary representations of $G$ correspond bijectively to nondegenerate star-representations of $C^*(G)$, preserving unitary equivalence, generated von Neumann algebras and kernels; weak containment corresponds to kernel inclusion ([[thm-nondegenerate-representations-of-c-star-g-are-unitary-representations-of-g]], [[thm-weak-containment-is-equivalent-to-kernel-inclusion]]).

[F2] The full group C*-algebra $C^*(G)$ of a second-countable group is separable with a countable dense star-subalgebra ([[lem-second-countable-group-c-star-algebra-is-separable-with-a-countable-dense-star-subalgebra]], [[def-full-group-c-star-algebra]]).

[F3] GCR characterization lemma: for a separable C*-algebra the conditions GCR, injective primitive-kernel map, countably separated Mackey dual and standard Mackey dual are equivalent, the Mackey Borel structure then equals the topology-generated one, and GCR implies that every factor representation has a type-I generated von Neumann algebra; the converse factor-type-I implies GCR is not asserted there ([[lem-gcr-kernel-and-mackey-borel-characterizations]], [[def-mackey-borel-structure-and-countable-separation]]).

[F4] The unitary dual carries the Mackey Borel structure and the Fell topology; $\operatorname{Prim}(C^*(G))$ carries the Jacobson topology and the kernel map $\kappa$ is the canonical map from irreducible classes to primitive ideals ([[def-unitary-dual-of-a-locally-compact-group]], [[def-fell-topology-on-the-unitary-dual]], [[def-primitive-ideal-space-of-a-group-c-star-algebra]]).

[F5] A factor representation is type I when its generated von Neumann algebra is a type-I factor, equivalently when the representation is a multiple of an irreducible one; a group is type I when every factor representation on a separable carrier is of type I; a separable type-I factor is a multiple of an irreducible representation ([[def-type-i-factor-representation-and-type-i-group]], [[lem-separable-type-i-factors-are-multiples-of-irreducible-representations]]).

[F6] AC is the stated hypothesis ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]).

[F7] Owner-authorized cited fact: for a separable C*-algebra $A$, if every factor representation of $A$ is type I then $A$ is GCR. This is cited to Glimm 1961 exactly as recorded in this item's `proof_scope`; no local proof and no original full-text reading is claimed for it. All other directions are proved locally ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]).

## Proof

**Given:** AC; the second-countable LCH group $G$; the structures of the Statement; and the conventions of the cited definitions.

[F1] Strongly continuous unitary representations of $G$ correspond bijectively to nondegenerate star-representations of $C^*(G)$, preserving unitary equivalence, generated von Neumann algebras and kernels; weak containment corresponds to kernel inclusion ([[thm-nondegenerate-representations-of-c-star-g-are-unitary-representations-of-g]], [[thm-weak-containment-is-equivalent-to-kernel-inclusion]]).

[F2] The full group C*-algebra $C^*(G)$ of a second-countable group is separable with a countable dense star-subalgebra ([[lem-second-countable-group-c-star-algebra-is-separable-with-a-countable-dense-star-subalgebra]], [[def-full-group-c-star-algebra]]).

[F3] GCR characterization lemma: for a separable C*-algebra the conditions GCR, injective primitive-kernel map, countably separated Mackey dual and standard Mackey dual are equivalent, the Mackey Borel structure then equals the topology-generated one, and GCR implies that every factor representation has a type-I generated von Neumann algebra; the converse factor-type-I implies GCR is not asserted there ([[lem-gcr-kernel-and-mackey-borel-characterizations]], [[def-mackey-borel-structure-and-countable-separation]]).

[F4] The unitary dual carries the Mackey Borel structure and the Fell topology; $\operatorname{Prim}(C^*(G))$ carries the Jacobson topology and the kernel map $\kappa$ is the canonical map from irreducible classes to primitive ideals ([[def-unitary-dual-of-a-locally-compact-group]], [[def-fell-topology-on-the-unitary-dual]], [[def-primitive-ideal-space-of-a-group-c-star-algebra]]).

[F5] A factor representation is type I when its generated von Neumann algebra is a type-I factor, equivalently when the representation is a multiple of an irreducible one; a group is type I when every factor representation on a separable carrier is of type I; a separable type-I factor is a multiple of an irreducible representation ([[def-type-i-factor-representation-and-type-i-group]], [[lem-separable-type-i-factors-are-multiples-of-irreducible-representations]]).

[F6] AC is the stated hypothesis ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]).

[F7] Owner-authorized cited fact: for a separable C*-algebra $A$, if every factor representation of $A$ is type I then $A$ is GCR. This is cited to Glimm 1961 exactly as recorded in this item's `proof_scope`; no local proof and no original full-text reading is claimed for it. All other directions are proved locally ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]).



**Proof technique:** the group/C*-algebra dictionary, a cyclic-subrepresentation reduction for the separable-carrier convention, the local GCR characterization lemma, and the single owner-authorized cited Glimm implication.

**Given:** AC; the second-countable LCH group $G$; the structures of the Statement.

1.1 By [F1] the unitary dual $\widehat G$ is identified with the set of unitary equivalence classes of irreducible nondegenerate star-representations of $C^*(G)$, the kernel map of [F4] is the primitive-kernel map, and the generated von Neumann algebra of a representation of $G$ equals that of the corresponding representation of $C^*(G)$; by [F2] the algebra $C^*(G)$ is separable. [F1, F2, F4]

1.2 Reduction of the convention: let $\pi$ be a factor representation of $G$ on a Hilbert space $H$ with $M:=\pi(G)''$ a factor, and choose $\xi\neq0$; set $K:=\overline{\pi(C^*(G))\xi}$, a closed $\pi(G)$-invariant subspace that is separable by [F2]. Its projection $p$ lies in $\pi(G)'=M'$, and the restriction $\pi_K(g):=p\pi(g)|_K$ has $(\pi_K(G))''=pMp$. The kernel of the surjective star-homomorphism $M\to pMp$, $x\mapsto x|_K$, is the weakly closed two-sided ideal $\{x\in M:xp=0\}$, which equals $Mz$ for a central projection $z\in Z(M)=\mathbb C I$; the homomorphism is nonzero because $I$ maps to $I_K\neq0$, so $z=0$ and the restriction $M\to pMp$ is injective. Hence $M\cong pMp$ is a factor acting on the separable space $K$, and if every factor representation of $G$ on a separable carrier is type I then $M$ is a type-I factor. [F5, algebra]

2.1 (i) implies C^*(G) is GCR: assume (i) and let $\rho$ be a factor representation of $C^*(G)$ corresponding to a factor representation $\pi$ of $G$ with $M=\pi(G)''$ a factor; by step 1.2, $M\cong pMp$ is a type-I factor because the representation $\pi_K$ on the separable carrier $K$ is a factor representation of $G$, and by [F5] being a multiple of an irreducible is the same as having type-I generated von Neumann algebra; hence every factor representation of the separable C*-algebra $C^*(G)$ is type I by the dictionary [F1], and the cited fact [F7] gives that $C^*(G)$ is GCR. This is the only step using the cited implication; no local justification of it is claimed. [F1, F5, F7, step 1.2]

3.1 GCR implies (ii), (iii) and (iv): by [F3], GCR for $C^*(G)$ gives that the primitive-kernel map is injective, hence a homeomorphism onto $\operatorname{Prim}(C^*(G))$, that the Mackey dual is standard Borel with Borel structure equal to the topology-generated one, and that it is countably separated; under the identifications of [F4] these are exactly clauses (ii), (iii) and (iv), with the topology-generated Borel structure of the dual being that of the Fell topology. [F3, F4, step 2.1]

4.1 Converse directions: if (iii) holds, then the Mackey dual of $C^*(G)$ is countably separated and [F3] gives GCR; if (iv) holds, the kernel map is injective and [F3] gives GCR; if (ii) holds, standardness implies countable separation, so (iii) holds and again GCR follows from [F3]. Combining with step 3.1, clauses (ii), (iii) and (iv) are mutually equivalent and each is equivalent to GCR for $C^*(G)$. [F3, step 3.1]

5.1 GCR implies (i): [F3] gives that every factor representation of the separable algebra $C^*(G)$ has type-I generated von Neumann algebra; by the dictionary [F1] the same holds for every factor representation of $G$, on a separable carrier by [F5] and on an arbitrary carrier by the reduction of step 1.2; hence every factor representation of $G$ is a multiple of an irreducible by [F5], which is clause (i). Together with steps 2.1-5.1 the four clauses are equivalent, and the criterion directions are assembled from the locally proved characterization lemma except for the single cited implication identified in [F7]. [F1, F3, F5, step 4.1] ∎



If $G$ is trivial, $C^*(G)=\mathbb C$ is GCR, the dual is a point, and all four clauses hold; the cited fact is not needed there but applies. If $G$ is compact and second countable, $C^*(G)$ is a c0-direct sum of matrix algebras, hence GCR, and the dual is discrete and countable, so the equivalences hold with an atomic standard dual; the criteria do not assert that the dual is closed or finite. If $G$ is discrete and countable, $C^*(G)$ is separable unital and the statements reduce to the C*-algebraic equivalences. The type-I convention is the one of [F5], restricted a priori to separable carriers; step 1.2 shows the restriction is immaterial for factor representations of a second-countable group, since the cyclic carrier of any nonzero vector is separable. The zero representation and the zero algebra are excluded by the nonzero-carrier conventions. The single cited element is used exactly once, in step 2.1, and no consumer may enlarge its scope; the original Glimm text was not read and no local proof of that implication is claimed.



The four proved implications other than the cited one rest on the locally proved GCR/kernel/Mackey characterization lemma of [F3], whose own inputs are the run-local H2 lemmas (GNS purity, excision, essential orbit density, primitive coding and analytic separation) together with the compact-ideal and amplification arguments recorded there. Blackadar, Part IV §IV.1.5, printed pp. 358-361, states the corresponding equivalences but explicitly omits the deep antiliminal construction (IV.1.5.2) and provides no proof of IV.1.5.12; those omissions are why the exact residual implication factor-type-I $\Rightarrow$ GCR is cited to Glimm 1961 under the owner's last-resort authorization. Bekka-de la Harpe, Chapter 8 §8.F, printed pp. 256-259, states the Glimm characterization of type I groups; the present item proves the group-level equivalences locally from the C*-algebraic statement except for the one cited implication.
