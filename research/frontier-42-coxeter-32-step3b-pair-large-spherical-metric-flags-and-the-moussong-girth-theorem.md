## Scaffold repairs (with evidence)

1. **`lem-cg-metric-flag-links-and-local-cat-one`(ii) was false as scaffolded.** The scaffold asserted that the closed star $\operatorname{St}(F)$ of a face $F$ *is isometric* to the spherical join $F*\operatorname{Lk}_X(F)$. Counterexample: the single spherical $2$-simplex with Gram matrix $C=\begin{pmatrix}1&-\frac12&-\frac25\\-\frac12&1&-\frac25\\-\frac25&-\frac25&1\end{pmatrix}$ (det $\tfrac{27}{100}>0$, so it is a large metric flag complex; edge lengths $120^\circ,113.58^\circ,113.58^\circ$). For $F$ the edge of length $120^\circ$, $x$ the opposite vertex and $p$ the midpoint of $F$: $\langle x,f\rangle\le-\tfrac25<0$ for every $f\in F$, so there is **no** radial decomposition $x=\cos\theta f+\sin\theta l$ with $\theta\le \pi/2$, and $\operatorname{Lk}_X(F)$ is a one-point space, so the join $F*\operatorname{Lk}_X(F)$ is a right triangle, not isometric to $\sigma$. (The scaffold's own source report only claims the vertex case, "the radial spherical cone of radius $\pi/2$ on its angular link embeds in its star".) The repaired clause proves the correct local model at $p\in\operatorname{relint}F$: the tangent cone splits as $T_pF\oplus N$ and the ball $B(p,\varepsilon)$ is isometric to the ball about the pole of the spherical cone $\{p\}*(S^{\dim F-1}*\operatorname{Lk}_X(F))$, which is CAT(1) when $\operatorname{Lk}_X(F)$ is. All consumers of clause (ii) (the vertex radial cones of `lem-cg-minimum-...`(ii) and the local CAT(1) induction) are served by the repaired clause; the promised claim (links stay large metric flag; conditional local CAT(1)) is unchanged.
2. **`lem-cg-minimum-nonshrinkable-loop-and-radial-vertex-insertion`(iii)** ended with "every local geodesic of length $<r$ is uniquely minimizing". The general local-geodesic statement is not needed by (iv) (the used subarcs of the minimum circle are minimizing because $\gamma$ is isometric to $S^1_m$), and the scaffold gave no proof; it is replaced by the directly proved statement that every subarc of $\gamma$ of length at most $r=m/2$ is a minimizing geodesic and that pairs of points of $\gamma$ at distance $<r$ have a unique geodesic of $X$, namely the shorter subarc.
3. **`ex-cg-a-tilde-2-nerve-...`(i)** asserted the link of a vertex $s_i$ in the affine $\widetilde A_2$ nerve is a two-point space with $d=2\pi/3$. The Schur complement of $C_{\{s_i\}}$ in $C_S$ has off-diagonal $-1$, and the pair $\{s_j,s_k\}$ spans no edge of the link (the full set is not a simplex), so the truncated angular distance is $\pi$. The item is corrected to $d=\pi$ (which is exactly the "angle $\pi$ at every vertex" that makes the boundary cycle a closed geodesic).

4. **`def-cg-large-spherical-metric-flag-and-almost-negative-matrix`(2)** identified the global chain distance $d(s,t)$ with the prescribed length of the edge cell without supplying that local/global equality. The direct supplier `def-cg-abstract-isometric-polyhedral-gluing-and-chain-metric` warns that a chain can leave a cell and return with shorter length in general (Bridson–Haefliger I.7.6). A 3-cycle with edge lengths $0.5,0.2,0.2$ radians witnesses the general phenomenon, but it is outside this item's large-edge hypotheses and therefore does not refute the claim as originally qualified. Clause (2) now defines $c_{st}$ directly from the unique prescribed edge Gram entry and labels $\ell_{st}=\arccos c_{st}$ as the one-cell length; the matrix definition no longer relies on the unproved local/global equality.
