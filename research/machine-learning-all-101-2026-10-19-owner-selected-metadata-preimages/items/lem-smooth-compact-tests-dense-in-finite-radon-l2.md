## Statement

Assume Dependent Choice. Let $M$ be a finite Radon Borel measure on $\mathbb R^n$, where $n\ge1$. Then $C_c^\infty(\mathbb R^n)$ is dense in $L^2(M)$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** DC, a finite Radon measure $M$ on $\mathbb R^n$, and $f\in L^2(M)$.

[F1] Under DC, $C_c(\mathbb R^n)$ is dense in $L^2(M)$ for a Radon measure. [[def-dependent-choice]] [[def-radon-measure-on-an-lch-space]] [[thm-c-c-is-dense-in-l-p-for-radon-measures]]

[F2] DC implies Countable Choice, as required by the mollifier smoothness supplier. [[def-dependent-choice]] [[lem-dependent-choice-implies-countable-choice]] [[def-countable-choice]]

[F3] There exists a smooth compactly supported function of unit Lebesgue integral; its rescalings form a mollifier family, and convolution of a continuous compactly supported function with a mollifier is smooth. [[lem-smooth-unit-mass-compact-bump-on-euclidean-space]] [[def-mollifier-family-generated-by-a-unit-mass-smooth-bump]] [[thm-convolution-with-a-mollifier-is-smooth-and-differentiates-under-the-integral-sign]]

## Proof
1.1 By [F1], given $\epsilon>0$ choose $g\in C_c(\mathbb R^n)$ with $\|f-g\|_{L^2(M)}<\epsilon/2$. Extend g by zero on $\mathbb R^n$; it is bounded and uniformly continuous. Let $\rho_\delta$ be the mollifier generated by [F3]. Since $\rho$ has compact support, for some $R<\infty$ its support is contained in $B_R(0)$, and $\sup_x|(g*\rho_\delta)(x)-g(x)|\le\sup_{|z|\le R\delta,x}|g(x-z)-g(x)|\longrightarrow0$ by uniform continuity. [given, F1, F2, F3]

2.1 The function $g*\rho_\delta$ is smooth by [F2], and its support lies in the sum of the compact supports of g and $\rho_\delta$, hence is compact. Since M is finite, $\|g*\rho_\delta-g\|_{L^2(M)}\le M(\mathbb R^n)^{1/2}\|g*\rho_\delta-g\|_\infty.$ Choose $\delta$ small enough that this is below $\epsilon/2$ (if $M$ is zero, every $L^2(M)$ class is zero and the conclusion is immediate). The triangle inequality gives $\|f-g*\rho_\delta\|_{L^2(M)}<\epsilon$, proving density. [F1, F2, F3, step 1.1] ∎
