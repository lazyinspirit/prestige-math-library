   maximizing `(rho,x)`; uniqueness by minimal-length cancellation; stabilizer
   generated by zero-label simple reflections; longest element `w_0^2=1`.

    and regular-point argument; `w.Delta=det(w)Delta`.
11. `lem-weyl-anti-invariants-are-divisible-by-the-discriminant` — accepted.
12. `lem-finite-reflection-invariant-generators-are-algebraically-independent`
    — accepted. Reynolds degree induction; relation lemma via a generating
    reflection; Euler/derivative contradiction with irredundancy. The `G={1}`
    case of the relation lemma was checked separately: the averaged term
    `R(g_1)` is a positive-degree invariant, hence in `I=S_+` there.
13. `lem-reflection-basic-invariants-form-a-regular-sequence` — accepted.
    Orbit monic equations, growth counts `m=n`, local CM at the origin, graded
    descent, Hilbert-series free basis. The published depth/CM/parameter
    statements (F3–F7) were read and their hypotheses match this use.
14. `lem-weyl-coinvariant-hilbert-series-has-order-w-dimension` — accepted.
    Molien trace and the `t->1` limit isolating `prod d_i=|W|` via faithfulness.
15. `thm-chevalley-shephard-todd-for-finite-weyl-groups` — accepted.
16. `def-finite-semisimple-lie-algebra-and-symmetric-adjoint-action` — accepted.
17. `lem-finite-lie-engel-trace-criterion-and-killing-nondegeneracy` — accepted.
    Nilpotence of `ad_a`; spectral interpolation `R` with `R(0)=0`; Engel by
    maximal-subalgebra induction; trace criterion via
    `tr(bx)=sum dim(V_lambda)|lambda|^2`; Killing radical argument. F1–F3 read.
18. `lem-finite-lie-triangularization-and-rank-one-complete-reducibility` —
    accepted. Lie's theorem; rank-one string via a maximal eigenvalue; central
    `Omega` separating blocks; complete reducibility by highest-vector lifting.
19. `lem-finite-semisimple-cartan-root-and-string-structure` — accepted. Inner
    derivations via the trace form; self-centralizing maximal toral subalgebra;
    perfect opposite pairing; `c_alpha != 0`; `dim g_alpha = 1` and string
    structure; `h_R` positive definite; simple triples generate.
20. `lem-finite-semisimple-pbw-and-highest-weight-construction` — accepted.
    PBW by the swap rule with the Jacobi confluence check; Verma basis and
    universal property; finite weight spaces; dominant finite-dimensionality;
    finite-dimensional simples are exactly the `L(lambda)`.
21. `def-kostant-harmonic-subspace-of-the-symmetric-algebra` — accepted.
    `H_d = I_d^\perp` for the Fischer pairing and adjoint stability verified;
    the complement is explicitly left to item 23.
22. `lem-highest-weight-characters-are-unitriangular-in-weyl-orbit-sums` —
    accepted. Finite unitriangular change of basis and its integral inverse.
23. `lem-kostant-harmonics-give-an-invariant-polynomial-complement` — accepted.
    This was the earlier escalated gap. The auxiliary free Lie algebra with the
    conjugate-linear involution `sigma` descends to `g`; the Hermitian form
    `<x,y> = -B(x,sigma y)` is positive definite on `h` and inductively on all
    root spaces; the Fischer form `(-1)^d(p,sigma q)_B` is positive definite on
    `S_d`; since `sigma` preserves `I_d`, its orthogonal complement is exactly
    `H_d`. The descent `K=K_*` and the positivity induction were checked in
    detail, including `g=0`.
24. `lem-local-chevalley-restriction-for-kostant-freeness` — accepted. The
    earlier escalated gap is closed by the local argument: invariance fixed by
    root exponentials, powers of dominant integral forms spanning, the element
    `N_i` implementing the simple reflection on `h`, trace polynomials giving
    orbit moments, and injectivity by an explicit local polynomial isomorphism.
25. `thm-kostant-harmonic-decomposition-of-the-symmetric-algebra` — accepted.
    Weight filtration, leading-part spanning/independence, degreewise dimension
    comparison and the degree induction for `R \otimes H -> S` all checked.
26. `lem-filtered-freeness-lifts-from-associated-graded-algebras` — accepted.
    Self-contained conditional lemma; finite choices only.
