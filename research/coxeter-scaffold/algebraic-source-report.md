## Coxeter-native contracts recommended for the enriched scaffold

These contracts should be explicitly named in page prose and ordered before their consumers. Suggested names here are descriptive contracts, not new canonical IDs.

### Real roots and the crystallographic seam

1. Start with finite-rank (W,S), the group-presentation contract and elementary real finite-dimensional linear algebra. Construct the basis e_s and symmetric bilinear form B(e_s,e_t)=−cos(π/m_st), diagonal 1, with B(e_s,e_t)=−1 for m=∞. Define σ_s(v)=v−2B(v,e_s)e_s. Verify σ_s²=1 and finite rank-two relators by actual 2×2 computation; the degenerate m=∞ block must not be treated as an ordinary finite-order rotation.
2. Define roots Φ=W·{e_s}; prove B(α,α)=1, hence roots are nonzero even when B is degenerate. Root **sign coherence**, simple sign change, length/sign equivalence and faithfulness are additional theorems. Supply their induction explicitly; do not infer them merely because rank-two relators hold. The positive cone uses nonnegative **real** coordinates, not nonnegative integers.
3. A finite real reflection group gives a finite reflection root configuration; identify its chamber simple generators with the abstract Coxeter generators only after the chamber/presentation theorem. Crystallographic root-system theory requires a separate integrality condition. In particular I₂(5), H₃ and H₄ are legitimate finite Coxeter systems without being finite crystallographic root systems; general Coxeter classification is not Dynkin classification.
4. Existing Lie proof homes supply Weyl/root-lattice/Cartan classification in the crystallographic specialization. The Coxeter page supplies the matrix/diagram comparison: Cartan integer products give 4cos²(π/m_st). A labeled Coxeter diagram forgets root-length orientation and cannot distinguish B_n and C_n root data. Do not infer weight/coweight lattices or a semisimple Lie algebra from an arbitrary Coxeter diagram.

### Elementary affine Weyl theory, independent of loop algebras

For a supplied finite reduced crystallographic Φ⊂E, define H_{α,k}={x:(x,α)=k}, k∈Z, α∨=2α/(α,α), and r_{α,k}(x)=x−((x,α)−k)α∨. This convention uses **root pairings**, so translations lie in Q∨. A convention using (x,α∨)=k instead changes the lattice and must not be mixed in.

Prove r_{α,k}=t_{kα∨}s_α, r_{α,k}²=1, r_{α,1}r_{α,0}=t_{α∨}, and wt_γw⁻¹=t_{wγ}. Crystallographic integrality proves W preserves Q∨ and that translations permute the wall arrangement: t_γ(H_{α,k})=H_{α,k+(γ,α)}. These formulas prove that the group generated by **all affine root reflections** is exactly Q∨⋊W: one containment follows from r=t s; the other from the translation difference and generation of Q∨. Unique decomposition follows by evaluating t_γw at 0. This argument needs no highest-root/orbit-generation theorem.

After that, prove wall arrangement **local finiteness**: on a compact K, each (x,α) is bounded and only finitely many integers k occur, with Φ finite. Prove the closed fundamental alcove/facet theorem and simple affine generators independently, using a supplied irreducible highest root plus geometric reflection descent or the already proved chamber/alcove theorem. The all-root-reflection semidirect-product computation alone does not establish simple affine presentation, alcove transitivity or stabilizers. Reducible Φ uses products of irreducible alcoves; rank zero is trivial. Extended affine groups P∨⋊W require the finite quotient P∨/Q∨, and are not automatically the same Coxeter group. Noncrystallographic groups and twisted affine Lie types are excluded from an unqualified Q∨⋊W theorem.

Prerequisite closure: elementary real reflection contract → finite crystallographic root/coroot contract → Q∨ lattice/action proof → published external semidirect-product proof → affine-reflection computation → local finiteness → separately proved alcove theorem → affine simple presentation. Compare the resulting action to the existing published untwisted loop theorem afterward; no dependence in reverse.

### Finite invariant and coinvariant interface

Complexify a finite faithful real reflection representation E to V=E⊗R C by choosing an explicit finite basis; prove the same matrices act faithfully and each reflection has complex fixed hyperplane. Apply the existing general finite-complex-reflection theorem above, carrying AC explicitly. This covers finite noncrystallographic groups without inventing a second invariant-theory proof home. The Coxeter category can own the comparison and concrete I₂(m), H₃/H₄ examples; retain abstract commutative algebra and Lie harmonics in their existing homes.

An instructive **direct I₂(m) invariant calculation** avoids the deep general regularity chain: over C choose coordinates u,z with a rotation acting by (u,z)↦(ζu,ζ⁻¹z) and a reflection swapping u,z, ζ a primitive mth root. Rotation invariance forces exponent difference divisible by m; reflection invariance symmetrizes monomials. Factor equal powers into a=uz; the recurrence p_k=(u^m+z^m)p_{k−1}−a^m p_{k−2} expresses all symmetric sums in a and b=u^m+z^m. Prove algebraic independence directly (e.g. distinct leading monomials under lexicographic order), obtaining C[u,z]^W=C[a,b] with degrees 2,m. Compute quotient basis/Hilbert series by explicit reductions if claiming it choice-free; citing the general Hilbert-series item reintroduces its AC premise. For m=2 the reducible example still works.

Do not silently claim the coinvariant algebra is the **regular representation** from its dimension alone; the inspected theorem supplies dimension/freeness, not the representation-character assertion. That strengthening requires an exact-character or generic-free-orbit deformation supplier. Do not identify this quotient with Kostant harmonics on a Lie algebra, or with flag-variety cohomology outside crystallographic geometry.

### Artin and Hecke/KL interface

Given the completed Coxeter presentation/Matsumoto contract, construct the positive Artin monoid from the free monoid quotient by finite braid relations and the Artin group from the free group quotient by the same relations. Check well-defined multiplication/congruence and universal properties locally. Generator images give surjections onto W after imposing squares. Reduced Coxeter words lift to one positive Artin element by Matsumoto; the section is injective because its composite with the projection is identity. It is generally **not a homomorphism** (already a simple generator squares nontrivially upstairs while s²=1 downstairs). This proves neither monoid-to-group embedding, general Artin torsion-freeness, nor K(π,1); no topology is needed for this interface. Type-A Braid Group pages retain all geometric/configuration-space/Garside proof homes.

Generic Hecke construction/basis/base-change remains in **Hopf Algebras & Hecke Algebras**. Coxeter-native pages can explain why braid connectivity is needed to index its standard basis and how odd-edge conjugacy classes constrain unequal parameters; those facts precede the algebra consumer. General Bruhat finite intervals come from subword existence (at most 2^ℓ(w) subexpressions), so triangular recursion works even if W is infinite. Algebra generators, Laurent coefficients, bar, basis and standard multiplication must come from the Hecke scaffold's actual proved contracts, not its currently empty items list.

KL polynomial/canonical-basis construction and cell modules retain **Special Topics in Representation Theory** or the already designated algebra proof home. The Coxeter scaffold may provide a clearly gated comparison, not duplicate an advanced application chain. For Lusztig normalization T_s²=(v−v⁻¹)T_s+1, set H_s=T_s with parameter replaced by v⁻¹ to match Elias–Williamson H_s²=(v⁻¹−v)H_s+1. State this conversion before coefficients are compared. In equal parameter, coefficient h_{y,w}(v)=v^{ℓ(w)−ℓ(y)}P_{y,w}(v⁻²) uses the Elias–Williamson convention. Do not copy a garbled extracted normalization formula.

Canonical-basis uniqueness needs finite triangular support, bar involution and Laurent coefficient splitting; basis existence alone does not imply coefficient positivity. Cell definitions need reflexive-transitive closure and quotient-module stability proof, not just terminology; unequal weights change cells. Elias–Williamson's advanced positivity uses a real realization with independent simple roots/coroots and reflection-faithfulness, polynomial grading degree 2, a strictly dominant form, Hodge forms and hard-Lefschetz induction. An arbitrary faithful geometric Coxeter representation, especially affine with degenerate Gram form, must not be silently substituted for that realization. Positive-characteristic categorification and p-canonical bases are different contracts.

## Exit condition and unresolved coverage

The enriched scaffold is honest when each construction above has either an exact inspected published supplier (with its hypotheses and AC carried) or an earlier named local proof obligation with the full route and prerequisites. It is not yet a completed mathematical build. Geometry/root-sign/general word proofs are for the other Coxeter team reports; this report supplies algebraic seams. Advanced Soergel positivity, general Artin K(π,1), reflection-group converse CST, regular-representation coinvariant structure, and arbitrary twisted affine lattice claims are **not supplied by this reading** and must not be promoted to ungated theorems.
