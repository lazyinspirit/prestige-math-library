The first example writes the A1 Hecke action as matrices. The second gives
different Hall-module structures on the same one-dimensional vector space,
showing that an action is separate data. The third compares the ordinary Hall
form with the transported Jordan Green pairing and checks the positive-mode
normalization degree by degree.
