## Ordered helper packet

### 1. lem-arith-strict-henselian-etale-sections

Statement: for a separated étale finite-type scheme H over a strictly henselian local ring R with separably closed residue field k, H(R)→H(k) is bijective. The finite part of H is a finite étale disjoint union of copies of Spec R containing its whole closed fibre; the complement has no R-sections.

Exact earlier suppliers: thm-etale-locally-standard-etale; cor-henselian-local-simple-root-criterion; cor-idempotents-lift-uniquely-in-a-henselian-pair; thm-quasi-finite-algebra-open-finite-factorization for affine finite-part construction. Scheme finite-part construction may instead be bundled in this proof by gluing the section images below.

Complete local proof: for x∈H(k), choose its standard étale chart R[T]/(P) localized at g. The base neighbourhood contains the closed point of Spec R and therefore equals all of Spec R. The x-value a mod m is a simple root of the monic P; Hensel lifts it to a∈R, with g(a) a unit, giving a section. If two sections have equal reduction, their equalizer is open because an étale diagonal is open and closed because H is separated. It contains the closed point, hence is all of Spec R. A section image is open (base change of an étale diagonal) and closed (separatedness). Finitely many closed-fibre points give disjoint section images. Their finite disjoint union is the finite part, and its complement has empty closed fibre. Each R-section meets the closed fibre, so belongs to the finite part. This proves the finite-part assertion without a scheme-level Zariski Main Theorem prerequisite. Source: BLR7.3/3; expanded section argument supplies the omitted local details.

### 2. lem-arith-prime-to-characteristic-multiplication-etale

Statement: if G/R is smooth, separated, commutative and finite type and n is invertible in R, [n]:G→G and G[n]→Spec R are étale. Consequently G[n](Rsh)→G[n](ksep) is bijective.

Proof: on tangent spaces at the identity, the differential of [n] is n times the identity, by differentiating the sum of n copies of the identity group morphism. Translation identifies every other tangent differential with this one. On equal-dimensional smooth source/target charts the relative Jacobian criterion makes [n] étale wherever its differential is invertible. This fibrewise assertion plus the standard smooth local coordinates gives the assertion over R as well: the relative coordinate Jacobian has determinant a unit, so the graph equations present an étale morphism. Pull back along the identity to obtain G[n]; apply helper1. Negative n is handled by inversion. Source: BLR7.3/2(b), with its differential computation explicitly reproduced. Earlier supplier names for smooth Jacobian/base change: thm-jacobian-criterion-smooth-morphism; thm-ag-standard-smooth-base-change-composition; thm-etale-equivalent-flat-unramified-fp; lem-etale-residue-extensions-finite-separable. ### 3. lem-arith-field-prime-to-characteristic-torsion-and-tate-module

Statement: for A/F abelian dimension g and prime ell≠char F, A[ell^v](Fsep)≅(Z/ell^v)^(2g), multiplication by ell gives surjective transition maps, and T_ell A≅Z_ell^(2g). The natural projections give T_ell A/ell^v T_ell A≅A[ell^v](Fsep). Inertia acts trivially on T iff it acts trivially on every finite torsion group.

Exact supplier: thm-nonaffine-abelian-multiplication-finite-faithfully-flat supplies finite locally free [n] and rank |n|^(2g) over arbitrary fields, including surjectivity on algebraically closed points. Helper2 gives étaleness, so separable geometric points have cardinal ell^(2gv). For a finite abelian ell-group H=A[ell^v], H[ell]=A[ell] has ell^(2g) elements; elementary cyclic classification says H has 2g cyclic factors, each exponent at most v. Since the sum of their exponents is 2gv, every exponent is v. Roots for each torsion point exist by field multiplication, and roots of ell^v-torsion are ell^(v+1)-torsion. Lift a basis recursively; the lifts are independent because reducing any relation by ell gives divisibility of every coefficient by ell^v, after which cardinality/order settles independence. Compatible coordinates yield the Z_ell basis of the inverse limit. For the quotient assertion, if a compatible sequence has vth coordinate zero, define b_r=a_(r+v), viewed in A[ell^r] since ell^r a_(r+v)=a_v=0; then ell^v b=a. Projection is onto by recursive root lifting. Thus the kernel is ell^v T. Inertia equivalence follows from these quotients and coordinatewise equality. This is an elementary group argument after the exact multiplication supplier; no H1 duality assertion is made. All necessary choice assumptions from that supplier persist.

### 4. lem-arith-affine-commutative-prime-to-characteristic-torsion-bound

Statement: over algebraically closed k, a smooth connected commutative affine group N of dimension a satisfies |N[ell^v](k)|≤ell^(va) for ell≠char k.

Exact supplier: lem-nonaffine-affine-group-faithful-representation (arbitrary-field). Do NOT use lem-affine-algebraic-group-faithful-rational-representation, whose Statement is C-only. Split character algebra terminology may reuse lem-diagonalizable-character-antiequivalence.

Complete matrix proof: embed N in GL_m. The commuting point matrices generate a finite-dimensional commutative algebra of matrices. They have a common eigenvector: inductively take an eigenspace of one generator, stable under all others, and refine using a finite generating list until all act scalarly. Induct on the quotient dimension to obtain a common invariant flag. Matrix entries below the diagonal vanish at every k-point; reducedness and the Nullstellensatz make them zero as functions, so the group scheme embeds in upper triangular matrices. Let chi_i be its diagonal characters. The subalgebra of k[N] generated by chi_i and chi_i^-1 is a Hopf algebra k[M], with M=Z^m/L, L the relations among these characters. Distinct group-like elements are linearly independent: a shortest relation Σc_i h_i=0, coproduct minus its tensor product with h_1, gives Σ_(i>1)c_i h_i⊗(h_i-h_1)=0; independence of a proper sublist forces h_i=h_1, a contradiction. Thus no further linear relations occur. k[N] is a domain because N is smooth connected over algebraically closed k. Hence M is torsion-free: a nontrivial finite-order monomial yields nontrivial zero divisors or a nonzero nilpotent in k[M] from x^d-1. Therefore M≅Z^t, the diagonal image has torus coordinate ring k[M], and t≤a since its fraction field embeds in k(N). An element in the diagonal kernel is upper unitriangular. In characteristic zero, a unitriangular matrix of finite order d has minimal polynomial dividing both (X-1)^m and X^d-1; the latter is squarefree, so it is identity. In characteristic p, (I+U)^(p^e)=I for p^e≥m; an element also killed by ell^v must be identity by Bezout. Thus N[ell^v] injects into the diagonal torus's ell^v-torsion, of cardinal ell^(vt)≤ell^(va). No closed-image/splitting/unipotent-filtration result is needed.

### 5. lem-arith-special-fibre-torsion-growth-detects-properness

Statement: for smooth commutative finite-type G/k, dim G=g, if |G[ell^v](kbar)|=ell^(2gv) for every v≥1, then G^0 is an abelian variety.

Exact supplier: thm-barsotti-chevalley-perfect-field-group-variety applied over kbar; helper3 for abelian torsion count; helper4 for affine torsion bound. Write 0→N→G^0→B→0 with dimN=a and dimB=b, so g=a+b. Every torsion fibre over B[ell^v] contains at most |N[ell^v]| points, by subtracting any point in that fibre. Therefore |G^0[ell^v]|≤ell^(v(2b+a)). There are C finitely many components; subtracting a torsion point in a component injects that component's torsion into G^0[ell^v], hence |G[ell^v]|≤C ell^(v(2g-a)). The assumed cardinal forces ell^(va)≤C for all v, so a=0. The affine smooth connected N is then trivial, hence G^0=B. A purely inseparable ksep→kbar extension changes no points of the étale finite torsion scheme: its residue extensions are finite separable, and ksep is separably closed. Properness descends along the algebraic closure, giving abelian G^0/k. Exact geometric properness descent supplier: lem-nonaffine-geometric-properness-field-descent. This bound suffices for NOS and avoids asserting the stronger exact exponent 2b+t.

### 6. lem-arith-connected-smooth-quasiprojective-model-proper-special-fibre

Statement: let R be a DVR and G/R a smooth separated finite-type quasi-projective scheme with generic fibre an abelian variety. If the special fibre is proper and geometrically connected, G is proper over R. Applied to the connected identity model, it becomes an abelian scheme.

Selected complete proof using published coherent perfect-complex result, not full formal GAGA: properness descends under R→Rhat by thm-properness-descent-fpqc and completion faithful flatness. Over complete R embed G as an open subscheme of its projective schematic closure X in P^m_R (a locally closed immersion makes it open in its closure). Closure is t-torsion-free on coordinate charts because its ideal is contracted from the generic fibre, so X is R-flat. The proper generic fibre G_K is closed in projective space; hence X_K=G_K. The finite R-module H0(X,O_X) injects into H0(X_K,O)=K by flatness. Every member is integral over R, and R is integrally closed; the scalar inclusion gives H0(X,O)=R.

If X_k were disconnected, its nontrivial clopen partition defines compatible idempotents e_n in H0(X_n,O), X_n=X×R R/t^(n+1), since these nilpotent thickenings all have the same underlying topological space and the unique functions 0/1 on that partition glue. Invoke the EXACT Statement of lem-proper-flat-fp-cohomology-perfect-complex for the already projective flat X and O_X: its bounded finite projective complex C is concentrated in degrees ≥0, and naturally H0(X_n,O)=ker(C0/t^(n+1)→C1/t^(n+1)). Finite projective modules over complete R are complete, and inverse limits preserve kernels. Thus lim H0(X_n,O)=ker(C0→C1)=H0(X,O)=R. The compatible e_n would give a nontrivial idempotent in R, impossible. Hence X_k is connected. The open immersion G_k→X_k is also proper because its source is proper and target separated, so its image is closed and open, nonempty, and therefore all X_k. It is an isomorphism. Generic fibres already coincide; the complement X\G is empty, so G=X. Descend properness to R.

Exact required earlier statements: quasi-projective Neron identity model from the Neron branch's BLR6.4 packet; lem-proper-flat-fp-cohomology-perfect-complex; thm-properness-descent-fpqc; thm-flatness-of-noetherian-completion; lem-projective-coherent-cohomology-finite-and-vanishing; thm-global-functions-proper-integral-variety; thm-over-a-pid-flat-is-equivalent-to-torsion-free and cor-dvr-is-a-pid; lem-schematic-closure-and-dense-agreement for contracted generic-fibre schematic closure. The closure argument uses that smooth G is flat, hence schematically dense in its generic fibre closure; on each chart the contraction ideal satisfies t f in I implies f in I. ### 7. lem-arith-abelian-scheme-torsion-specialization-unramified

Statement: for an abelian scheme A/R of relative dimension g, ell≠char k and every v, A[ell^v] is finite étale of rank ell^(2gv). Over Rsh, specialization identifies its generic geometric points with special separable points, and inertia acts trivially.

Proof: helper2 gives étaleness of [ell^v]; properness of [ell^v] and finite geometric fibres from field multiplication make it finite (thm-proper-quasi-finite-is-finite). Its kernel is finite étale. The rank is locally constant and equals ell^(2gv) on the generic fibre by helper3, hence everywhere on connected SpecR. Helper1 decomposes it over Rsh as copies of SpecRsh, so its geometric generic points are already rational over Ksh and specialization is a bijection. Its Galois action therefore factors through the residue Galois action, and inertia is trivial. This is the precise finite-étale smooth-proper specialization claimed here; it is not general smooth proper base change for H^i.

### 8. thm-arith-neron-ogg-shafarevich-prime-to-residue-characteristic

Statement: for an abelian variety A/K of dimension g with finite-type Neron model N/R and prime ell≠char k, the following are equivalent: good reduction by an abelian scheme; N is an abelian scheme; all A[ell^v](Ksep) are inertia-fixed; T_ell A is inertia-unramified.

Proof: good reduction identifies the abelian scheme with the Neron model by the Neron branch's abelian-scheme mapping property and uniqueness; helper7 gives inertia invariance; helper3 gives equivalence finite torsion/Tate invariance. Conversely, Neron sections over Rsh are exactly A(Ksh), and helper2+1 identify N[ell^v](Rsh) with N_k[ell^v](ksep). Invariance means all ell^v-torsion is over Ksh, so helper3 gives cardinal ell^(2gv) on the special fibre for every v. Helper5 makes N_k^0 abelian. The Neron identity component N^0 is smooth separated finite type, its generic fibre is A, and its geometric special fibre is connected/proper. Neron-branch quasi-projectivity plus helper6 makes N^0 an abelian scheme. Its Neron property makes N=N^0 by uniqueness. Source BLR7.4/5; full local detail expanded above.
