# Step-3 published-audit triage (2026-09-26)

Snapshot before the nine recorded-source checks and while other Step-3 writers were active: strict `node tools/depcheck.mjs --json` reported 343 published-status errors: 334 `published-unaudited` proved-here items and nine `published-unchecked` `proved_here: false` remarks. Counts and hashes must be recomputed at the final stable certification boundary. `SCHEMA.md` §Verification defines `audited` as a scalar owner audit record, `verified` as a delegated `model`/`verdict`/`date`/`scope`/`delegated_by` record, and `sources_checked` as a `date`/`scope`/`by` record for unproved items. `tools/depcheck.mjs` checks the presence of these fields. A field is an audit-status record, not a proof theorem or an independent judge verdict. Do not use `--pending-audit-ok` to stand in for the strict Step-3 gate.

HEAD comparison of the 334 proved-here files found 124 Statement/Definition/Example edits, 184 argument edits with unchanged interface, 14 other body-prose edits, eight frontmatter-only edits, three verification-only edits, and one newly published item. The nine recorded remarks had eight body changes and one metadata-only change. Thus restoring historical stamps mechanically is not sound.

Search of all 65 `research/**/*receipts*.jsonl` files (4,153 rows) found 147 of the 334 with item-ID-bound evidence carrying the exact current byte SHA-256 in a current-state field or explicit evidence. Of these, 135 rows had accept/repair decisions, explicit scopes and empty or null unresolved lists; 12 need case review. Among the 135, 125 are writer-repair/self-review receipts and ten are unchanged-item acceptances by another reviewer, two of the ten after an earlier repair. Five same-hash accept candidates also have a defer record and require reconciliation. An exact hash match identifies possible reusable evidence; it is not automatically an independent audit. For example, `cex-finite-additivity-alone-does-not-prove-infinite-cw-uniqueness` matches `research/up-1630-review/agent-03-receipts.jsonl` line 150, but that same agent made the repair, so owner review remains necessary.

The broader scan of all 532 `research/**/*.jsonl` files (240,165 lines) found current hashes for 326 of 343 subjects somewhere, but the 179 additional proved-here hash sightings outside item-specific receipts were chiefly edge-impact traces, assignment inventories and writer event logs. They do not establish a fresh whole-item audit: one explicit example, `research/ap-131-sol-repair/agent-08-impact-rem-riemann-integral-choice-ledger.jsonl`, calls its scope a “transitive edge trace; no fresh whole-proof audit.” Seventeen subjects had no current hash in any JSONL at the snapshot. The nine unproved remarks had no matching recorded-source receipt then; a separate current source audit is in `research/frontier-35-ten-categories-recorded-source-audit-20260926.md`.

Recommended final-boundary workflow: freeze writers; rerun strict depcheck; enumerate exact current IDs and SHA-256; join item-specific current-hash receipts and classify each receipt by provenance, scope and unresolved issues; give priority to the 39 proved-here IDs in the selected 682-manifest transitive closure (`research/frontier-35-ten-categories-selected-published-audit-queue-20260926.md`, hashes there are diagnostic and may drift); independently read changed interfaces and proofs, relevant source passages and load-bearing suppliers; record any defects in the canonical published-consumer ledger, repair under owner authority, then re-audit final bytes. Apply `verified` only for a genuine delegated positive review with the exact scope, or scalar `audited` only for a genuine owner audit. The four published bull-free A-P proofs remain pending Phase-3 repair; a current defect-focused `audited` record can coexist with their explicit A-P pending status but must not claim that their proofs or SPGT dependence have closed. Finally rerun strict depcheck and all Step-3 checks on the stable content.
