### Local different: Jacobian, Koszul diagonal, and trace

The corrected local proof fixes the distinction between a generator of the dual module and the trace functional. For a completed local extension `A -> B`, it chooses a generator `lambda` of `W = Hom_A(B,A)` and writes the trace functional as `tau = h lambda`; it does not assert that `tau` generates `W`. It also uses nonvanishing of the fibre trace **functional**, not nondegeneracy of the fibre trace pairing.

The proof route is now explicit. The graph of the curve morphism is a section of the smooth projection `C x_k D -> C`, hence a regular immersion (Stacks tag `067R`); composition with the smooth projection to `D` is lci (tag `069J`). Finite flatness over the target DVR and the quasi-finite flat-lci presentation give a square complete-intersection presentation. Its conormal matrix gives `Fitt_0(Omega_{B/A}) = (det J)`. For the diagonal ideal `I = ker(B tensor_A B -> B)`, the proof exhibits the diagonal sequence `g_j = x_j tensor 1 - 1 tensor x_j` as regular by writing the ambient ring as a polynomial ring over `B`, then localizing. Polynomial differences express each `f_i tensor 1` as a linear combination of the `g_j`; the map of Koszul resolutions has top component the determinant, and the top Tor group on the diagonal side is `Ann(I)`. Thus the Noether different is generated by `det J` (tag `0BWD`).

For the trace bridge, the finite-free basis calculation gives `Ann(I) ~= Hom_B(W,B)` (tag `0BVS`), and multiplication on `Ann(I)` equals evaluation at the trace element (tag `0BVT`). The socle of `B/(s^e)` proves that `W` is free of rank one over `B`; it does not identify its generator with the trace. If `tau = h lambda`, evaluation has image `(h)`. The different/Noether-different comparison and the Kähler-different formula identify `(h) = Fitt_0(Omega_{B/A}) = (det J)` (tags `0BW6`, `0BWG`, and `0BVZ`). Finally the fibre trace functional is

`tau_0(c) = e * Tr_{L/K}(c_bar)`.

It is nonzero exactly when `L/K` is separable and `e` is invertible in `K` (tag `0C13`). In that case `h mod s^e` is a nonzero socle element, so `ord(h)=e-1`; otherwise `h` lies in `(s^e)`, so `ord(h)>=e`. The source-coverage carrier records the full Stacks tag pages whose statements and proofs were read; the proof does not use a citation as a substitute for the displayed local calculation.

This bridge is complete under the stated finite-flat-lci hypotheses. The **claim-level closure** still waits on the live internal ramification-index/Weil-definition inputs named above and the root-owned escalation decision; I did not certify that changed dependency chain.

### Normalization and function fields

`thm-normalization-glues-integral-finite-type-curves` now constructs overlap maps using a common principal-open refinement chosen from both affine charts. On a small overlap, each chart represents the other chart's localizing function; the resulting common principal open has the same coordinate ring inside `K`, so the integral closures agree after localization. This avoids the false assumption that every principal open from one chart is itself principal in the other chart. The proof uses a single identified generic point to establish global integrality, proves both containments for the initiality chart rings, and uses the universal-property maps in their correct directions.

`thm-curves-function-fields-equivalence` now uses the actual denominator opens `D(g_i)` as sources of the affine morphisms. Equality of morphisms is proved by the closed equalizer containing the generic point, not by treating the generic point as a closed subscheme. The projective model is built explicitly: homogenize the prime ideal of the affine chart in `S[x_0^{-1}]`, contract to a homogeneous prime `J` in `S`, and take `Proj(S/J)`. The statement and proof give the unique model isomorphism in the contravariant direction: an isomorphism `phi' phi^{-1}: k(C) -> k(C')` gives `psi:C' -> C` with `psi^*=phi' phi^{-1}`. Unused schematic-closure dependencies were removed from this item's metadata and contract.
