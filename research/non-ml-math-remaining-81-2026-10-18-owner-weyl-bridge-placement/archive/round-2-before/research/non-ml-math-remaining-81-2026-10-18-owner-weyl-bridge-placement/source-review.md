## Exact repaired interface

Added `items/lem-common-domain-commutator-integrates-to-weyl-relations.md` on the PA-07 A page. For a finite family of self-adjoint A_a and real skew matrix c, it defines the eligible common domain inline: D is a graph core for every generator, invariant under all generators and their functional-calculus groups, scalar commutators [A_a,A_b]=i c_ab I hold on D, and every orbit e^{isA_a}xi is differentiable in every A_b graph norm with explicit derivative i A_a e^{isA_a}xi.

The last condition is a sufficient analytic regularity condition on individual orbits. It assumes neither the desired conjugation formula nor any two-group Weyl commutation relation. It is stronger than mere common invariant density, precisely because the omitted passage through an unbounded factor cannot be justified by density alone. The approved claim specified no weaker optimal hypothesis, analytic-vector theorem, or necessary-and-sufficient classification to preserve. The repair retains its full finite-mode exponentiation conclusion under explicit sound sufficient regularity, including arbitrary real central parameter; only conversion to A_n requires nonzero parameter. It does not replace the analytic claim by a finite-dimensional algebra example or by the desired conclusion as a hypothesis.

The proof has five substantive steps:

1. With every intermediate product on D defined by invariance, split the difference quotient of U_a(s)A_bU_a(-s)xi into the moving-vector term and fixed-vector unitary derivative. The explicit graph derivative controls the unbounded A_b term. The derivative is -c_ab xi, so conjugation equals A_b-c_ab s on D.
2. Unitarly transport the graph core of A_b to prove D is a core for the conjugated operator. D is also a core for A_b-c_ab s because the two graph norms are equivalent. Both operators are closed and agree on their core, giving equality of full operators and domains. In particular invariance of the full domain D(A_b) under U_a(s) is proved, not silently assumed.
3. Compute the exact derivative domains of the conjugated group and scalar-shifted group in both directions. Their generators agree by step 2. Invoke actual local Stone uniqueness to obtain U_a(s)U_b(t)=exp(-i c_ab st)U_b(t)U_a(s) on all H. Functional calculus supplies the individual exponentials; the new proof supplies the previously absent integration.
4. The explicitly phased ordered finite product W(f) yields the full Weyl cocycle exp(-i sigma(f,g)/2), involution, unitarity and joint strong continuity. The scalar reordering calculation is displayed in full.
5. For the canonical Heisenberg block c=kappa[[0,I],[-I,0]], establish the standard n-mode Weyl relations and identify x_j=Q_j, p_j=(i/kappa)P_j on D as a unital algebraic A_n(C)-module. The sign agrees with PA-07's [p_j,x_k]=delta_jk convention.

The theorem carries AC through the actual spectral-calculus and Stone suppliers, and CC through the self-adjoint/graph supplier closure. It makes no claim that those hypotheses follow from an arbitrary algebraic module, or that a bare invariant commutator domain suffices.
