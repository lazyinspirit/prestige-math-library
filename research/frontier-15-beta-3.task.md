## Decisions already made — do not relitigate

- **Owner-authorised 2026-08-14: GA-1 retains the algebraic closure.** The
  ambient-normal-extension fallback is retired. Do not "save" ~12 items by
  cutting it.
- **The one-step route** (corrected 2026-08-13 against the harvest): the
  ω-iteration `F ⊆ F₁ ⊆ F₂ ⊆ ⋯` is NOT built. Milne Prop. 6.5, Conrad
  Thm. 2, Clark Thm. 4.9 all give the one-step theorem — if `L/F` is
  algebraic and every nonconstant `f ∈ F[x]` has a root in `L`, then `L` is
  algebraically closed — so `F₁` is already closed. Its proof splits into the
  perfect case (primitive element theorem) and the characteristic-p case
  (perfect subfield first), as **separate numbered items**.

## Traps the design states

1. **The one-step lemma is the construction.** The properness of the ideal
   generated by `{f(x_f)}` is one honest computation (contradiction inside a
   splitting field of `f₁⋯fₙ`); do not compress it into "quotient by a
   maximal ideal".
2. **Uniqueness of the closure is up to a NON-unique isomorphism.** Writing
   "unique up to unique isomorphism" — the phrasing every other universal
   property teaches — is a false statement here.
3. **Id namespace:** the group-theoretic `def-normal-closure` is taken; use
   `def-normal-closure-of-an-algebraic-extension`.
4. Zorn appears twice (root extension, embedding extension); the choice cost
   is stated in the Statements, not hidden.
