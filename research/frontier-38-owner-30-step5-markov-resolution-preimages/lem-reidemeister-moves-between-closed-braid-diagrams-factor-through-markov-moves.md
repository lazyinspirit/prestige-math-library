## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $B$ and $C$ be closed braid diagrams
representing the same oriented link. Then $B$ can be transformed into $C$ by a
sequence of braid isotopies of closed braid diagrams, Markov stabilizations and
destabilizations, and their inverses; equivalently, the braids read from $B$
and $C$ are Markov equivalent.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC, closed braid diagrams $B,C$ for the same oriented link, and the Reidemeister equivalence theorem ([[thm-oriented-reidemeister-equivalence-theorem]]).

[F1] Every non-braid-like R2 and R3 is generated by type I moves, braid-like R2 and R3 moves, reducing moves and inverses; this is the generating lemma ([[lem-non-braid-like-reidemeister-moves-are-generated-by-braid-like-moves-and-reductions]]).

[F2] Braid-like moves and braid isotopies may be moved to height zero, where they are braid isotopies of closed braids or stabilizations ([[lem-braid-like-moves-can-be-moved-to-height-zero]]).

[F3] Braid-like R2 and R3 moves on closed braids and planar isotopies are braid isotopies, hence conjugations in the braid group ([[lem-braid-like-reidemeister-moves-on-closed-braids-are-braid-isotopies]]).

[F4] Reducing-move peaks can be lowered or reduced to the four-band configuration ([[lem-reducing-move-peaks-can-be-lowered-to-the-four-band-case]]), and the four-band case is a Markov sequence ([[lem-the-four-band-d-pair-case-is-a-markov-sequence]]).

[F5] The regular-projection existence used in the hypothesis is $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$-stated and is discharged from AC by the choice-implication bridge ([[thm-choice-implies-dependent-implies-countable-choice]]).


[F6] A reducing move lowers height by exactly one and preserves the oriented link; its inverse raises height by one ([[lem-yamada-vogel-reducing-moves-lower-bad-seifert-circle-complexity]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 **Reidemeister sequence and phase one.** By the oriented Reidemeister equivalence theorem there is a finite sequence of planar isotopies and oriented R1, R2, R3 moves from $B$ to $C$; by [F5] this uses $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$, discharged from AC. Apply [F1] to every non-braid-like R2 and R3 in the sequence: the sequence is replaced by one using only type I moves, braid-like R2 and R3 moves, and reducing moves and their inverses. [F1, F5, given]

2.1 **Phase two: braid-like content at height zero.** Apply [F2] to replace each type I and braid-like II or III move. The II and III moves occur at height zero and are braid isotopies by [F3], hence conjugations. The type I replacement has a stabilization or destabilization at height zero and, when the kink must return across other strands, includes braid-like III moves and inverse reductions as specified by [F2]. Group the resulting transformation into height-zero Markov portions interleaved with reducing sequences. Split each reducing sequence at its height-zero diagrams, so its intermediate diagrams have strictly positive height and its endpoints have height zero. [F2, F3, step 1.1]

3.1 **Phase three: eliminate a whole height level.** Consider one of the reducing portions split at height zero in step 2.1. It has height-zero endpoints and strictly positive interiors, precisely the endpoint conditions of [F4]; let $H$ be its maximum height. If $H=0$, there are no reducing moves. Otherwise each occurrence of height $H$ is a peak, since reducing moves change height by exactly one. The peak-lowering argument of [F4] replaces each such peak by finitely many commuting valleys or irreducible four-band peaks: first the intersections of its reducing arcs are eliminated, then compatible insertions produce commuting valleys, leaving only the four-band case. A valley has height at most $H-1$; in the four-band case, reduce the two neighbours of the peak to height zero and join the resulting braids by the Markov sequence supplied by [F4]. These reductions start at height $H-1$ and only decrease height, so the entire replacement avoids height $H$. Carry this out for every one of the finitely many original height-$H$ peaks. The replacement paths introduce no height-$H$ diagrams, and hence the maximum height of the remaining reducing content is strictly smaller. Induction on $H$ eliminates all reducing content, leaving a finite Markov sequence. [F4, F6, step 2.1]

4.1 **Conclusion.** Steps 1.1-3.1 factor the chosen Reidemeister sequence between $B$ and $C$ through Markov moves, so the braids read from $B$ and $C$ are Markov equivalent. AC is inherited through the AC-stated generating, height and four-band items. ∎ [F1, F2, F4, step 3.1]
