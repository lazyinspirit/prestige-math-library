These worked examples compute self-information entropy and differential entropy for the uniform law, mutual information for a deterministic finite copy, conditional mutual information before and after coarsening the conditioning variable, and the effect of changing a regular conditional kernel on a null fiber. Each example states its probability law and computes the relevant divergence with the same support conventions as the A page.
