# Monic-coefficient transfer without assuming End(v)=symmetric polynomials

First-pass algebraic construction by `/root/repair_klr_global_basis/cyclotomic_action_construction`, run `frontier-44-special-representation-examples-28`. Inputs: the actual iterated canonical-rho normal form (including the endpoint rank check), the first adjunction G⊣H, and the trace helper's universal monic nil-Hecke model and candidate-map identities. This proves the specific endomorphism comparison needed to transfer the model; it does not assume a universal category is already its polynomial model.

Let v be the identity vacuum of the formal rank-one representation of weight −N, with Hv=0. Put A=End(v). Here A is commutative: v is the image of an identity 1-arrow, and horizontal and vertical composition on its 2-endomorphisms have the same unit and satisfy interchange, so f g=g f. This claim is not made for the endomorphism ring of an arbitrary annihilated object in an unrelated category. Such objects are obtained later by evaluation of the formal representation.

The canonical rho on the vacuum gives

$$HGv\cong\bigoplus_{j=0}^{N-1}v\{\text{the prescribed dot-unit degree}\},$$

with actual basis maps Hx^j∘η. Under this basis the dot x on HGv sends the j-th basis vector to the (j+1)-st for j<N−1. Its final column is a vector of coefficients in A. Hence it is the companion matrix of a unique monic polynomial

$$p(t)=t^N+c_{N-1}t^{N-1}+\cdots+c_0\in A[t].$$

Companion matrix multiplication directly gives p(Hx)=0. The first adjunction identifies End(Gv) with Hom(v,HGv), sending p(x) to p(Hx)η. That image vanishes; hence p(x)Gv=0. Applying G^(a−1) and using naturality of the coefficients gives p(x_1)G^av=0. Thus the known nil-Hecke action factors through the standard monic quotient NH_a(p), over A.

The full normal form at (m,n,λ)=(a,a,−N), on v, leaves only its i=a component. Its coefficient basis has (a!)² binom(N,a) elements. All corresponding maps v→H^aG^av are decorated iterated **ordinary** units: nil-Hecke elements act on the G and H blocks, and H-block elements are ordinary adjunction mates of the corresponding G-block elements. Applying the ordinary iterated adjunction therefore sends these basis maps to the nil-Hecke endomorphisms of G^av. This is an explicit adjoint correspondence, not an inference from dimensions: for an endomorphism z of G^av its adjoint is H^a(z)η_a, and conversely a decorated unit gives exactly that z by the first triangle identities. Multiplying these maps by scalar endomorphisms of v accounts for their A coefficients. Consequently the map

$$NH_a(p)\longrightarrow\operatorname{End}(G^av)$$

is surjective as an A-module and hence as an A-algebra.

The universal monic nil-Hecke quotient is free over its coefficient ring with rank (a!)² binom(N,a), by the matrix/monic-remainder model proved in the trace construction. Base change to the coefficients c_j∈A preserves that finite free basis. The full actual normal form gives the same finite free A-rank for End(G^av), by ordinary adjunction. The displayed surjection is therefore an isomorphism: choose preimages of the finite target basis to obtain a right inverse matrix. Its determinant gives a multiplicative inverse for the determinant of the original square matrix over commutative A; the adjugate then gives a two-sided inverse. This proof permits zero divisors in A and uses only finite choice. For a>N, both sides vanish; a=0 is A.

The comparison is compatible with the inclusions NH_a(p)→NH_{a+1}(p), because applying G to a nil-Hecke endomorphism preserves its generator action and its A coefficients. Thus on the additive, idempotent-complete subcategories generated by each G^av, the identification of endomorphism algebras identifies G with the model's induction. Its supplied first right adjoint H is then identified with the model's restriction by the explicit first-adjunction Hom correspondence. The crossing and dot generators match, and the original unit/counit match under these adjunction identifications. Hence the canonical rho inverses and all four candidate second maps, which are expressions in exactly these generators and the first adjunction, are carried to the model's candidate maps.

Every word-generated object in the formal rank-one representation is a summand of a finite sum of G^av: use the now-proved normal form to move positive H powers to the right, where they kill v. Therefore the identification covers the entire formal rank-one representation needed for detection. The model's already-constructed dual-basis candidate triangle identities transfer to it. Evaluation at any H-annihilated object in an integrable representation transfers these same identities to the generated strings, without requiring its possibly noncommutative endomorphism ring to satisfy the determinant argument. The first-adjunction integrable detection lemma then proves the candidate triangle composites invertible on all objects. Its opposite-representation version handles the other triangle, exactly as in the trace construction.

This removes the separate unproved claim End(v)=k[c_1,…,c_N] from the model-transfer route. The argument uses the actual normal form, explicit companion relation, actual nil-Hecke coefficient basis and equal finite free ranks. It does not declare a split polynomial-ring surjection to be an isomorphism without injectivity. Different-color crossing mates remain a distinct obligation.
