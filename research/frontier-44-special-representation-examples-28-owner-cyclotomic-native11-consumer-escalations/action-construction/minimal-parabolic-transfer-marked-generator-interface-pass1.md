## Vacuum companion and the monic coefficient dictionary

Write a_i(u)=u^λ+c_{λ−1}u^{λ−1}+…+c_0, λ≥0. Once H_i v=0, the canonical rho supplies the actual basis e_k=H_i(x^k)η:v→H_iG_i v, 0≤k<λ. The dot maps e_k to e_{k+1} for k<λ−1; write its last column Σα_k e_k. The unique companion polynomial is p(u)=u^λ−Σα_k u^k. No Frobenius argument is needed to construct this matrix.

In the original horizontal ideal, the marked generator a_i(x)G_i v is zero and its H_i whisker is therefore zero. Applying that whisker to e_0 gives

$$e_\lambda+\sum_{k<\lambda}c_ke_k=0.$$

Basis uniqueness forces α_k=−c_k and p=a_i. Equivalently, closing the marked relation with dot powers gives the closed-dot recurrence H(t)a_i^{rev}(t)=1, where H(t)=Σ_{ℓ≥0}h_ℓt^ℓ, h_0=1 and h_ℓ=0 for ℓ<0; this is the trace helper's explicit computation. Thus the boundary bubble coefficient dictionary is a consequence of the **marked ideal**, not an extra unexplained assumption.

Conversely, first impose H_i v=0 and this coefficient dictionary. The same companion matrix then has polynomial a_i. It follows that a_i(H_i x)=0 on H_iG_i v, so H_i(a_i(x)G_i v)=0. Ordinary adjunction identifies End(G_i v) with Hom(v,H_iG_i v), sending a_i(x) to a_i(H_i x)η=0. Hence a_i(x)G_i v itself vanishes. This establishes equivalence of the monic i-marker and the indicated coefficient dictionary after killing H_i v. Standard powers are the case c_k=0; arbitrary monic coefficients give the stated reciprocal series instead of zeroing every positive bubble. At λ=0, canonical rho says H_iG_i v=0; ordinary adjunction gives G_i v=0 and the degree-zero polynomial a_i=1 marker also vanishes.

For j≠i, the formal mixed commutator H_iG_j≅G_jH_i intertwines the j dot by the mixed KLR dot relation. Thus H_i(a_j(x)G_j v) identifies with a_j(x)G_jH_i v=0. This uses the actual dot-intertwining formula, not a statement that a nonzero mixed Hom space is one dimensional.

## Finite reduction of arbitrary marked whiskers

Consider a horizontal whisker A(a_j(x)G_j v), where A is any word in the downward G_k and upward H_i. Induct on the number of upward symbols in A and then on the number of downward symbols to the right of its first upward symbol. A has the form B H_i D, where D is entirely downward. Apply the sign-correct canonical rho and the mixed commutator to H_iD. In either rank-one weight sign, H_iG_i is a summand of G_iH_i plus a finite sum of shifted identities; at different colors H_iG_k≅G_kH_i. Repetition therefore expresses H_iD as a summand of a finite sum of functors of the two forms D' H_i and D'', with D',D'' downward and D'' shorter than D. Every inclusion, projection and comparison here is a natural transformation of functors in its input object.

Apply this decomposition to the endomorphism f=a_j(x)G_j v. Naturality means the corresponding matrix is diagonal with entries D'(H_i f) or D''f; no dot on the marked input is moved through a KLR crossing by an unstated isotopy. The entries containing H_i f vanish by the explicit companion/dot-intertwining calculations above. The remaining entries are ordinary downward whiskers of the same first-strand marked relation. An endomorphism of a summand is reconstructed by its finite inclusion/projection maps, so the whole H_iD(f) belongs to the ideal generated by those downward marked entries. Now continue through B by the induction on upward symbols. All reductions are finite.

Thus arbitrary upward whiskers of a_j(x)G_j v create no relation outside the ideal generated by downward cyclotomic markers and the unmarked H_i-vacuum generators, once the equivalent boundary coefficient dictionary is in force. This is an ideal statement about all marked generators, not merely a normal form for unmarked diagrams. It is precisely what is needed before the cap-off constructor reduces the remaining unmarked H_i-vacuum factors.

There is no dependency on the generic cyclotomic symmetric/Frobenius theorem. The source signs only change the scalar entries in the rho inclusions/projections and are already bound; dot companion coefficients themselves are unaffected.
