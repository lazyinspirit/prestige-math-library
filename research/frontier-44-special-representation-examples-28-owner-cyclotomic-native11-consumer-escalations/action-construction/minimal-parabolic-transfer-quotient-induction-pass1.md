## Purely algebraic comparison for arbitrary monic polynomials

Let a_j(u)∈k[u] be monic of degree λ_j≥0. Set a(x_b)=Σ_ν a_{ν_b}(x_b)e(ν), I_β=R(β)a(x_1)R(β), A_β=R(β)/I_β. For S=R(β+α_i), put e_R=e(β,i), e_L=e(i,β),

$$K_0=Se_R/Sa(x_1)R(\beta)e_R,\qquad K_1=Se_L/Sa(x_2)R_1(\beta)e_L.$$

Their right A_β[t] actions and finite projectivity follow from the actual PBW block coset bases, exactly as in the earlier comparison construction; this does not require a_i to be a power, A_β to be graded, or A_β to be finite dimensional. Define

$$P(u)=u\,a(x_1)\kappa_1\cdots\kappa_n,\qquad Q(v)=v\,g_n\cdots g_1,$$

where g is the source intertwiner, and therefore has the native expression (y−x)+(y−x)²τ at equal colors and −τ at unequal colors. The generator-linearity calculation uses only the KLR relations; arbitrary polynomial a_j is permitted. The P/Q polynomial induction likewise uses a_j(x_1)s=s a_j(x_2), which holds for every polynomial, not just a monomial. Thus

$$QP=\sum_{\nu\in I^\beta}a_i(t)\prod_{\nu_a\ne i}Q_{i,\nu_a}(t,x_a)e(\nu).$$

Its degree in t is λ_i−⟨h_i,β⟩+2β_i=λ_i+Σ_{j≠i}(−a_ij)β_j, and its leading coefficient is the same nonzero scalar c=∏_{j≠i}t_{i,j;−a_ij,0}^{β_j}. Multiplication is injective on the finite A_β[t]-projective K_1 by the leading-coefficient argument. Hence P is injective.

The PBW left-end cosets give, with no category-theoretic input,

$$I_{\beta+\alpha_i}e_R=Sa(x_1)R(\beta)e_R+Sa(x_1)\kappa_1\cdots\kappa_ne_R.$$

Here the shorter cosets a=0,…,n−1 are in the first term, and the full-transfer coset a=n has new first color i. Therefore P has image exactly the kernel of the ordinary cyclotomic projection K_0→A_{β+α_i}e_R. We obtain the actual exact sequence

$$0\to K_1\xrightarrow{P}K_0\to A_{\beta+\alpha_i}e_R\to0.$$

This comparison already holds for arbitrary monic a_j in **ungraded** modules. For standard powers, or over a universal graded coefficient ring making each a_j homogeneous, P has the previously proved shift (α_i|2Λ−β); after specializing coefficients to a field with inhomogeneous a_j, no graded shift or graded K_0 claim is made.

## Monic annihilators: no flat-specialization inference

The following direct argument also supplies finite dimension and root-string termination for the ungraded branch. The first dot has the common monic annihilator g_1(u)=∏_j a_j(u). Suppose g(x_a)=0, with g monic of degree d. At equal colors the annihilator's divided-difference/exchange closure gives g(x_{a+1})=0 on that word. At unequal colors j,l, with x=x_a,y=x_{a+1}, the mixed and quadratic relations give g(y)Q_{jl}(x,y)=0.

Let C=k[x]/(g(x)), free over k on 1,…,x^{d−1}. Multiplication by f(y)=g(y)Q_{jl}(x,y) on C[y] has a d×d matrix over k[y], whose leading term is c y^{d−a_lj} times the identity, c=t_{j,l;0,−a_lj}≠0. Its determinant is a monic polynomial after dividing by the scalar c^d. The adjugate identity applied to the coordinate vector of1 shows that this determinant belongs to the ideal generated by f in C[y]. Lifting back to k[x,y] therefore supplies a scalar-coefficient monic h(y) in (g(x),g(y)Q_{jl}(x,y)), hence h(x_{a+1}) annihilates the word. If d=0, the module is already zero. Multiply the finitely many word annihilators to get a common monic polynomial at the next position. PBW and reduction by these monic polynomials give a finite spanning basis, so each A_β is finite dimensional.

For an all-i tail starting at position ℓ+1, take its common monic dot annihilator g of degree d from A_{ℓ+1}. The repeated divided difference ∂_{ℓ+d}…∂_{ℓ+1}g(x_{ℓ+1}) is the unit (−1)^d in source convention (the lower terms vanish after d operators). Thus a tail of d+1 equal-color entries is zero. The previously proved induction on the number of non-i entries applies with these degrees, rather than nilpotence exponents, and gives A_{β+kα_i}=0 for sufficiently large k. No deformation-flatness theorem or graded specialization is used.

A common scalar-coefficient monic annihilator of the new terminal dot also makes the comparison's cokernel projective over A_β by the explicit central-monic projective-dimension-one splitting lemma and its choice-free addendum. Consequently the ungraded projectivity/exactness needed for the monic transfer is a direct algebraic theorem, not a consequence of a supposed unchanged graded quotient dimension.
