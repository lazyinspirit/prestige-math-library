## Exact formal category and model data

Fix i. Let P_i be the diagram category generated by every downward G_j and upward H_i, with the ordinary G_i⊣H_i maps, the actual canonical rho_i and its inverse, the rank-one second maps constructed from those inverses, and the downward KLR dots/crossings. For the formal minimal-parabolic category impose the rank-one relations, dot cyclicity, and the mixed cup/cap cancellation relations that the trace helper has verified **on its explicit model**. In this formal category the desired mixed relation is a defining relation, not yet a theorem about the actual cyclotomic action. Its model verification is lawful; transporting it to the actual action still requires the quotient identification below.

The vertex with spectator counts m_j and i-root count r uses

$$C=\bigotimes_{j\ne i}k[Y_j]^{S_{m_j}},\qquad p(u)=a_i(u)\prod_{j\ne i}\prod_{y\in Y_j}\frac{Q_{ij}(u,y)}{t_{ij}},$$

with N=deg_u p and h=N−2r. Its commutative algebra is the monic factor ring

$$L_m=C[e_1,\ldots,e_r]/\left(\text{coefficients above }N-r\text{ of }p(u)/\prod_{a=1}^r(u-x_a)\right).$$

An i flag adjoins a distinguished z with monic relation b_m(z)=0, where b_m(u)=p(u)/∏(u−x_a); the target symmetric root alphabet is the old alphabet plus z. A spectator j flag adjoins y, with the j alphabet enlarged and p replaced by p Q_{ij}(u,y)/t_{ij}. The two orders of these flags yield the explicit rings /(b) and /(bq), linked by quotient π and multiplication by Q. All Q coefficients are retained. Rank-one cups/caps are the dual bases of the two specified flag coefficient traces, with the primitive-idempotent shifts bound in the trace note. These are concrete operators, not a postulated representation of a named 2-category.

## Degree separation in the downward sector

Consider a fixed finite linear combination of diagrams with all boundary strands downward and only i-bubbles at the far left. By the downward PBW theorem, its untruncated action has the form

$$\sum_{w,\nu}f_{w,\nu}(z_1,\ldots,z_L;b_1,b_2,\ldots)\,\tau_we(\nu),$$

with finitely many bubble coefficients b_a and dot powers. Choose the standard polynomial realization with P_{ij}=Q_{ij}, P_{ji}=1 for one chosen order of each distinct-color pair; equal-color crossings act by the signed divided difference already bound above. In the fraction field every τ_w has a nonzero coefficient on its own permutation w, and shorter correction terms have smaller permutation length. A maximal nonzero PBW coefficient cannot cancel against those terms. Thus the generic action over the independent coefficient polynomial ring is faithful. This is the same algebraic PBW leading-permutation argument as the existing local KLR supplier, extended by independent bubble variables.

The bubble coefficients in the flag model are the coefficients of p(u)/∏(u−x_a) (or its reciprocal according to the orientation). The first D such coefficients are a unit triangular polynomial change from the first D elementary symmetric functions in the old i alphabet, with the spectator symmetric functions and framing coefficients as parameters: expand the inverse of 1−e_1t+e_2t²−… and compare coefficients in increasing t degree. The coefficient of each newly introduced e_a is ±1. If r≥D, these elementary symmetric functions are independent, so a fixed nonzero polynomial in the bubble parameters remains nonzero before the truncation relations. The spectator symmetric coefficients are independent in the same way once each relevant spectator alphabet is large enough.

Choose a polynomial test vector witnessing the nonzero generic operator; it has finite degree, as do all coefficients in the result. Let D now bound those degrees and the finitely many dot/bubble indices. If N−r is larger than D plus the number of i flags in the word, every monic flag relation and every root-factor coefficient relation has degree above the witness and its output. Those rings are graded with positive dot degrees and nonnegative coefficient degrees, so the ideal generated by such relations has zero intersection with the degrees in question. Consequently the nonzero generic output survives in the finite flag model. The same argument works with nonzero spectator Q coefficients and nonsimply-laced dot degrees: measure D in actual internal degree, not an assumed common dot degree.

For a fixed formal source weight μ, one may increase r while retaining μ by choosing the auxiliary framing weight λ=μ+Σm_jα_j; then N−r=μ_i+r after including the spectator Cartan contribution. Only the i-framing/string must be nonnegative for this minimal-parabolic model; the auxiliary framing need not be dominant at the spectator vertices, since no H_j is adjoined there. Thus the separation does not require finite Dynkin type. Alternatively use the universal graded monic a_i coefficients, assigning each coefficient its missing polynomial degree, and choose an arbitrarily large framing degree. This proves joint faithfulness of the model family in the **downward PBW plus independent i-bubble sector**.

## What bending does and does not prove

In the formal category whose mixed relations are already imposed, ordinary and second adjunction give mutually inverse Hom correspondences that bend each upward i boundary strand to a downward i boundary strand on the other side. A perfect matching then becomes a matching between two all-downward boundary words, hence a permutation together with dot positions and far-left i-bubbles. Straighten its chosen reduced expression by the KLR braid/quadratic relations; the leading permutation coefficient is a product of the imposed nonzero mixed-cancellation constants and is a unit, while correction terms have fewer crossings. In a finite crossing filtration this is a triangular change to the downward PBW/bubble basis. The preceding degree separation therefore proves independence of the formal matching/dot/bubble family. This is the nondegeneracy argument for P_i's model family.

This argument may **not** be repeated in the actual cyclotomic action until the mixed cancellations there are established. Bending Hom spaces is an isomorphism after the already-proved second adjunction, but identifying the bent diagrams with the expected downward PBW elements uses the very mixed cup/cap relation at issue. No proof of that identification follows merely from rank-one detection or from the fact that the ordinary sideways crossing is invertible.

## Negative word generators after taking the formal cyclotomic quotient

Let v_Λ be the formal source vacuum. Let J be the horizontal/vertical ideal generated by H_i v_Λ=0 and the chosen first-strand relations a_j(x_1)G_j v_Λ=0. Every object in the additive idempotent completion of P_i(Λ)/J is a summand of a finite sum of negative words G_νv_Λ. Indeed all such words generate from v_Λ under the G_j. To see closure under H_i, induct on word length. For j≠i use the formal mixed commutator H_iG_j≅G_jH_i. For j=i use the sign-correct canonical rho: in either sign H_iG_i is a summand of G_iH_i plus finitely many shifted identities. The remaining H_i on the shorter word is handled by induction; H_i v_Λ=0 starts it. This uses actual finite direct sums and summands, not an equality in K_0.

For standard powers a_j(u)=u^{λ_j}, far-left positive i-bubbles vanish, negative-degree bubbles already vanish and the degree-zero bubble is1. For a general monic a_i, the far-left bubble coefficients instead specialize to the coefficients of a_i, with the reciprocal bubble series determined by monic inversion. Setting all positive bubbles to zero in that branch would change its algebra and is forbidden. Under the correct bubble dictionary, the downward KLR corner gives a surjective map

$$e(\nu)R^\Lambda(\beta)e(\mu)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Hom}_{P_i(\Lambda)/J}(G_\mu v_\Lambda,G_\nu v_\Lambda).$$

Surjectivity follows from the formal matching normal form and the specialized bubbles. The first-strand relations are in its kernel by the definition of J. What is still needed is **injectivity**, not more word generators.

## Smallest remaining transfer lemma

The necessary local supplier is:

`lem-minimal-parabolic-cyclotomic-word-corner-ideal-intersection`.

Typed contract: in the just-defined formal minimal-parabolic category, for all negative words ν,μ of the same β,

$$J\cap e(\nu)R(\beta)e(\mu)=e(\nu)\Bigl(\sum_jR(\beta)a_j(x_1)e(j,\beta-\alpha_j)R(\beta)\Bigr)e(\mu).$$

The intersection uses the included generic downward KLR copy, whose injection is established by the formal model faithfulness above. With standard powers the bubble dictionary is zero positive-degree coefficients; with arbitrary monic a_j it is the prescribed coefficient evaluation. This result must be proved before the formal model's mixed-mate equality transfers to actual R^Λ word modules.

The only potentially extra generators on the left are diagrams factoring through A H_i v_Λ. Bend that initial H_i using the first/second adjunction. They become diagrams on an added leftmost i strand followed by a **cap-off operation at the vacuum**. A precise sufficient calculation is: expand each such diagram in PBW cosets for the added leftmost strand, then prove its cap-off lies in the displayed ordinary first-strand ideal, including the leftmost bubble specialization terms. In the standard branch, the source proof distinguishes a far-left bubble from a left curl; the curl must be expanded using the actual rank-one cup/cap polynomial formula. In the monic branch, that same expansion must use the coefficients of a_i rather than declaring those bubbles zero.

I have not supplied that complete cap-off calculation here. Generic model faithfulness, the negative generator induction, and a surjective corner map do not prove the ideal-intersection assertion. A faithful action of the **unquotiented** formal category does not make a quotient by J faithful automatically. Nor does a model quotient at large highest parameters detect this specific ideal intersection without an explicit kernel calculation. Webster Proposition1.7 is an authoritative source for the result, but its compressed “capping off/loop-de-loop” paragraph is not adopted as a complete local supplier.

Thus the remaining obstruction is this exact ideal-intersection/cap-off lemma (standard and monic dictionaries), rather than a Hom-one-dimensionality assumption, a generic Frobenius theorem, or a rank-one candidate-map identity. If that lemma is proved, the word generators above give the desired equivalence to actual projective R^Λ modules. First-adjunction uniqueness identifies the formal H_i with the actual restriction, and the canonical-rho formulas identify its second maps with the already-constructed actual maps; then the model's verified mixed-mate relations transfer on every word projective.
