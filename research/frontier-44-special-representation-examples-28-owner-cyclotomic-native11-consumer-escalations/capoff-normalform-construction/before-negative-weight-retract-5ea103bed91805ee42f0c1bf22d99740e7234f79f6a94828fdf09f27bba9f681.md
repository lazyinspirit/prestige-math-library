## Bringing every bubble coefficient to the vacuum

After bending, a coefficient may initially be a bubble at source Lambda+alpha_i, immediately to the left of an added first i strand with dot z. It may not be replaced directly by the Lambda vacuum value. The required rank-one bubble-slide equation in coefficient normalization is

$$A_{\Lambda+\alpha_i}(t)=(1-zt)^2A_\Lambda(t),\qquad H_{\Lambda+\alpha_i}(t)=(1-zt)^{-2}H_\Lambda(t).$$

The weight indices differ by 2; these are normalized power series with constant coefficient 1. For each fixed coefficient this is a finite polynomial equation in z and the vacuum coefficients. Negative exponents on (1-zt) denote formal coefficient recursion, not inversion of z or an analytic infinite sum.

Here is an algebraic verification, independent of quotient faithfulness. In the unquotiented rank-one polynomial flag model, with total monic polynomial p and existing-root factor c_r, the normalized bubble series is p^{rev}/(c_r^{rev})^2; this is the coefficient/cap identity for the two monic flag pairings (equivalently complement factor divided by root factor). Adding the distinguished root z replaces c_r^{rev} by c_r^{rev}(1-zt). The target series is therefore the source series divided by (1-zt)^2. Multiplying proves the displayed equation; inversion gives the H equation. The two sides are all-downward one-strand bubble/dot morphisms, so prerequisite 2's faithful **unquotiented** polynomial/flag family proves this formal diagram equality. It is also the usual rank-one bubble-slide consequence of dot cyclicity and cross-and-cap. This legitimate use of generic faithfulness proves an equation before quotienting, not the intersection after quotienting.

Thus a finite diagram has, after finitely many coefficient slides, only polynomial dots on the added strand and bubble coefficients at Lambda. In P_i/S these coefficients are scalars. No source-weight bubble specialization or collision of two distinguished roots is assumed.

## Why arbitrary whiskers of the other monic generators add no relations

It is necessary to handle A a_j(x) id(F_j v) when A itself contains E_i; cap-off of unmarked E_i-vacuum factors alone does not establish this. The following finite normalization supplies that check.

$$xv_r=v_{r+1}\ (r<\lambda_i-1),\qquad xv_{\lambda_i-1}=-\sum_{r=0}^{\lambda_i-1}c_{i,r}v_r.$$
