## Construction-time mathematical repairs

The scaffold inherited from the previous worker contained statements that are false or mis-typed. They were repaired in place before any readiness record was written; the repairs are part of the mathematical record, not cosmetic edits.

1. **`lem-sublevel-bands-of-a-proper-morse-exhaustion-can-be-chosen-capped-free` (removed; false).** Counterexample: $M=\mathbb R^2$ with $f=r^2+\text{bump}$ having a local maximum of value 26 (a mountain in a valley), otherwise radial. Any increasing sequence of regular values $b_j\to\infty$ must cross 26, and for $b_j<26<b_{j+1}$ the component of the band $f^{-1}([b_j,b_{j+1}])$ containing the peak is a compact cap that misses the upper level; so no sequence with the claimed property exists, although the theorem the item was built to serve is true. Replaced by `lem-open-manifolds-admit-exhaustions-with-no-caps`, which *absorbs* the caps instead of trying to avoid them: for a regular sublevel $K$ there are only finitely many compact components ("caps") of $M\setminus\operatorname{int}K$ (each cap has nonempty boundary, a union of components of the compact regular level, and distinct caps have disjoint boundaries), and adjoining them all to $K$ yields a compact manifold with boundary whose complement has no compact component. Iterating this at the stages of a countable exhaustion gives a cap-free sequence, and a final compactness/clopen argument shows every band component meets the outgoing boundary. This is the route that matches §12.5's "prove the no-top-index handle exhaustion" and it uses only $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$.
2. **`lem-critical-values-of-a-proper-morse-function-form-a-closed-discrete-set` (removed; false as stated).** The claim "the set of critical values exceeding $b$ has a least element for every real $b$" fails for $f(x)=x^2$ on $\mathbb R$ (critical values $\{0\}$, empty above $b>0$). The item was also unnecessary on the repaired route. Replaced by `lem-regular-sublevels-are-compact-manifolds-with-boundary`, the standard fact actually used (regular sublevels are compact manifolds with boundary): it also carries the empty-level caveat.
3. **`lem-smooth-families-and-path-components-in-the-weak-topology` (statement narrowed).** As previously stated for an arbitrary source $M$ it was false: the immersion condition is *not* open in the weak topology on a noncompact source (a map can be made non-immersive arbitrarily far away inside a basic neighborhood). It is now stated for compact $M$, where the weak topology is the strong Whitney topology and the openness argument is valid; the definition of regular homotopy was qualified accordingly. This is exactly the compact-source form used by the corollary and by the band-by-band induction.
4. **`lem-parametric-immersion-extension-on-a-disk` (absolute case added).** The item now separates Francis's absolute statement (all $0\le k\le n$; needed for the equidimensional free-boundary base case) from the relative parametric Hirsch–Smale form ($k<n$; needed to attach handles of index $k\le m-1$). The previous statement carried only the relative form and cited the absolute case in its strategy.
5. **`lem-positive-codimension-thickening-reduces-closed-sources-to-the-open-case` (typo/type repair).** The statement "Let $m<n\le n$" was replaced by "$m<n$"; `Imm_ν(M,N)` was corrected from a subspace of formal data to the subspace of genuine immersions whose normal bundle is identified with $\nu_F$, and the formal restriction map `FImm(D(ν_F),N) → FImm_ν(M,N)` was added.
6. **B-page repairs.** (i) `cex-a-closed-manifold-with-formally-plausible-rank-data-needs-positive-codimension` used $S^n$ and claimed its tangent bundle is trivial; that is true only for $n=1,3,7$, and for other $n$ there is not even a formal immersion. Replaced by the $n$-torus $T^n=(S^1)^n$, which is always parallelizable. (ii) `ex-an-open-parallelizable-manifold-immerses-in-euclidean-space-of-equal-dimension` listed the open Möbius band as an example; its tangent bundle is non-trivial, so it satisfies neither the trivial-tangent-bundle hypothesis nor admits any formal immersion into $\mathbb R^2$. Replaced by the open annulus, with the frame items added as dependencies. (iii) `ex-the-standard-sphere-immersion-and-its-normal-line` asserted the Euler-characteristic obstruction to framing $TS^2$ as proved; it is now cited from the recorded unproved remark `rem-the-hairy-ball-theorem-for-even-dimensional-spheres` through `external_refs`, as SCHEMA requires for mentions of recorded-not-proved results. (iv) `cex-a-bundle-map-with-rank-drop-is-not-a-formal-immersion` had `provenance.proof: not-applicable`, which SCHEMA reserves for definitions and remarks; changed to `literature-derived`.
7. **Wording repairs.** `def-space-of-immersions-and-space-of-formal-immersions` now names the two weak topologies directly instead of "the two preceding items"; "no closed component" was replaced by "no compact component" where it described open manifolds; the subcritical-handle statement's confusing boundary-connected-sum parenthetical was rewritten; the smooth-families lemma's compactness hypothesis is now explicit.

## Second-pass repairs (2026-10-05, before closing the batch)

A final read of the assembled manifest found three further mathematical defects and one precision defect in the statements (items 8–11 below) plus one typo (item 12), all repaired before the batch was closed. The affected items and their in-run consumers were re-recorded: 15 readiness records were refreshed after re-reading the corrected text (deps unchanged except where noted), and the whole-run checks below were re-run on the repaired manifest.

8. **`lem-formal-immersion-gives-the-tangent-normal-bundle-identity` (false display).** The old statement displayed a "canonical" isomorphism $TM\oplus\nu_F\to f^*TN$, $(v,w)\mapsto F(v)+w$. The formula is not well defined: $w$ is a coset modulo $F(TM)$, so changing the representative changes $F(v)+w$ by an element of $F(TM)$; the quotient sequence does not split canonically. The statement now defines $\nu_F$ as the quotient, records the short exact sequence, derives a splitting from a smooth complement (the orthogonal complement once a metric is chosen), and states explicitly that no splitting is canonical in general. This was a genuine falsehood, not a wording issue; it was found by checking well-definedness of the displayed map, and the corrected proof route uses the published complement theorem already in `deps`.
9. **`lem-positive-codimension-thickening-…` (dimension bookkeeping) and the closed-source theorem strategy.** The normal disk bundle $D(\nu_F)$ of $M^m$ in $N^n$ has rank-$(n-m)$ fibres, hence dimension $m+(n-m)=n=\dim N$; the previous text called it "the open $m$-manifold $D(\nu_F)$" and invoked the open-source theorem "since $m\le n$", and the closed-source theorem's strategy said the thickening has "dimension $m$" and is mapped "with $m<n$". Corrected: $D(\nu_F)$ is an $n$-manifold with boundary, its interior is an open $n$-manifold, so the open-source theorem applies in the equidimensional case; the transfer between the interior and the closed disk bundle is made by the collar item, which was added to the thickening lemma's `deps` (levels unchanged: the lemma stays at level 10, since its level comes from the open-source theorem's 9).
10. **`lem-smooth-families-and-path-components-in-the-weak-topology` (logical slip).** The old statement claimed every continuous $\Phi$ is homotopic *rel $Q$* to a smooth-adjoint map and then conditioned the rel-$Q$ form on smoothness over $Q$. The unconditional rel-$Q$ claim fails when $\Phi|_Q$ has no smooth adjoint, since a homotopy rel $Q$ cannot change the restriction. The statement now asserts: homotopic to a smooth-adjoint map, rel $Q$ when the adjoint over $Q$ is already smooth; the strategy approximates relative to the closed set $Q\times M$.
11. **`lem-parametric-immersion-extension-on-a-disk` (relative clause).** The relative parametric conclusion now reads "constant on $Q$ and on $A$ and relative to the parameter boundary $\partial P$" instead of the redundant older phrasing; content unchanged.
12. **`cex-a-bundle-map-with-rank-drop-…` (typo).** $F_0\mapsto F_x$.
