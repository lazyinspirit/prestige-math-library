### PE25 — Overlapping Schwarz and stable subspace decompositions

**A:** `overlapping-schwarz-and-stable-subspace-decompositions`.

**Disposition:** genuinely-new PDE preconditioner; exact CG, matrix norms and SPD preconditioning are existing-covered.

**Scope/results:** Define local restriction/prolongation, additive and multiplicative Schwarz, exact/inexact local solves and a coarse space for SPD conforming diffusion. Prove the abstract additive Schwarz condition bound from a stable decomposition constant C0 and a strengthened Cauchy–Schwarz/overlap constant C1; give the precise norm and normalization so the bound is C0*C1. Prove multiplicative contraction from the associated subspace correction estimate with its actual constants. Then prove a two-level overlapping P1 Poisson specialization on quasiuniform shape-regular meshes, shape-regular subdomains, coarse diameter H and overlap delta, with explicit H/delta dependence and bounded overlap. A one-level method does not receive a uniform-in-subdomain-count bound. High coefficient contrast and anisotropy require separately constructed robust coarse spaces; the ordinary partition proof does not imply robustness.

**Proof route:** Local energy minimization expresses B inverse as the minimum summed local energy over decompositions. Lower/upper Rayleigh bounds follow from stable decomposition and bounded synthesis, using existing SPD/CG suppliers. Construct a boundary-preserving coarse quasi-interpolant (NA28 lower-regularity interface) and partition-of-unity theta_i with ||grad theta_i||≤C/delta; decompose u into coarse part plus interpolated theta_i(u-u0), then prove local interpolation/overlap bounds and stable energy estimates. Multiplicative correction uses exact projection error products, telescoped energy decreases and a proved stable-decomposition estimate; noncommuting projections cannot simply multiply scalar contraction factors. Inexact local solvers require uniform spectral equivalence and resulting modified constants.

**Dependencies/smallest suppliers:** NA06/07/28/29, local stable-decomposition variational identity, partition construction/coarse quasi-interpolation/overlap-count proofs. **B:** one- versus two-level 1D Poisson, overlap shrinking, coefficient-channel failure of a naive coarse space, exact/inexact local solves. **Sources:** Toselli–Widlund, *Domain Decomposition Methods—Algorithms and Theory* (2005), abstract Schwarz and overlapping methods chapters, https://doi.org/10.1007/b137868; Xu, *Iterative Methods by Space Decomposition and Subspace Correction*, SIAM Review 34 (1992), 581–613, https://doi.org/10.1137/1034116.

### PE26 — Substructuring, FETI-DP and BDDC

**A:** `substructuring-feti-dp-and-bddc-preconditioners`.

**Disposition:** genuinely-new interface preconditioner; block LU/Schur complement/CG are reused.

**Scope/results:** Define discrete harmonic extension, interface Schur complement, primal assembled versus partially assembled interface spaces, primal constraints (corners/edge averages as appropriate), scaled averaging and a concrete BDDC method. Define corresponding FETI-DP dual multipliers/jump operator with explicit redundant-constraint convention. Prove well-definedness after removing local floating-subdomain nullspaces and establishing primal constraints; prove exact interface equivalence and the appropriate common nontrivial-spectrum comparison. Prove the classical bound C(1+log(H/h))² only for a stated scalar second-order diffusion family on shape-regular polygonal subdomains in 2D with matching quasiuniform triangulations and the selected corner/edge constraints/scaling; coefficient jumps need precisely stated subdomain-constant aligned coefficient/scaling assumptions. A 3D claim must add its edge/face constraint and trace proof. Do not claim arbitrary graph/mesh/high-contrast scalability.

**Proof route:** Reuse block elimination for Schur algebra. Prove harmonic extension minimizes energy, then characterize the BDDC preconditioner by constrained local minimization and averaging. Prove averaging energy bound using the exact discrete trace/H1/2 norm and vertex-cutoff logarithmic estimate; this logarithmic estimate, local trace lifting and stable coarse constraint construction are same-page analytic prerequisites with full proofs, not assumed universal inequalities. Show dual jump/averaging decomposition identities and map eigenvectors between primal and dual operators, explicitly excluding null/unit modes where required. Work/storage counts are separate from conditioning and depend on local/coarse solver costs.

**Dependencies/smallest suppliers:** NA06/28/29, PE25 abstract subspace energy interface; `thm-block-lu-factorisation-via-an-invertible-leading-block`; local interface fractional trace/lifting/logarithmic estimates and floating-nullspace constraints. **B:** two floating Poisson subdomains, missing corner singularity, matching primal/dual spectrum, coarse-solve work bottleneck. **Sources:** Toselli–Widlund 2005 substructuring/FETI-DP/BDDC chapters, https://doi.org/10.1007/b137868; Mandel–Dohrmann–Tezaur, *An algebraic theory for primal and dual substructuring methods by constraints*, Applied Numerical Mathematics 54 (2005), 167–193, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apnum.2004.09.022. ### PE27 — Nonlinear full-approximation multigrid

**A:** `nonlinear-full-approximation-multigrid`.

**Disposition:** genuinely-new nonlinear multilevel method; NA07 linear multigrid and NA09 local nonlinear solver are reused.

**Scope/results:** Define FAS for F_h(u_h)=f_h with iterate restriction I_h^H, residual restriction R_h^H, prolongation P_H^h, coarse operator F_H and tau correction; specify nonlinear pre/post smoothing and finite budgets/branch failure. Prove fine-root fixed-point consistency for arbitrary consistent transfers when the coarse right side is F_H(Iu)+R(f-F_h(u)); require the coarse solver return the selected root Iu at zero defect. Prove exact reduction to linear correction multigrid when operators/smoothers/coarse solves are linear. Prove local convergence near a root from a bound q<1 on the derivative of the actual FAS cycle plus a proved Lipschitz derivative bound and invariant radius. Give a semilinear monotone diffusion example with actually verified smooth branch/smoother/coarse assumptions. Mesh-independent basin/rate requires those constants uniformly in h; no arbitrary nonlinear FAS convergence claim.

**Proof route:** Substitute the exact root and telescope the coarse correction. Differentiate every smoother, restriction, implicit coarse branch and prolongation at the root; use the local implicit theorem for the coarse Jacobian inverse and derive the linearized two-grid error operator exactly. NA07 contraction applies only when this derivative equals a linear cycle satisfying its actual smoothing/approximation hypotheses. Mean-value integral yields ||E(u)-u*||≤(q+Lr/2)||u-u*|| on the stated ball; choose r with q+Lr/2<1, prove invariance and iteration contraction. Coarse inexact solves add a quantified residual-to-correction defect with its own inverse bound. Cost recurrence includes nonlinear smoothing/coarse work, not a blanket O(N).

**Dependencies/smallest suppliers:** NA07/09, PE12 monotone diffusion example; existing Euclidean/Banach implicit theorem; local derivative-of-cycle/branch-admissibility and uniform-constant verification. **B:** nonlinear reaction Poisson tau cancellation, zero-tau inconsistent fixed point, linear FAS equivalence, loss of local basin near a bifurcation. **Sources:** Briggs–Henson–McCormick, *A Multigrid Tutorial*, 2e (2000), nonlinear problems/FAS chapter, https://doi.org/10.1137/1.9780898719505; Hackbusch, *Multi-Grid Methods and Applications* (1985), nonlinear multigrid convergence sections, https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-662-02427-0. Source reading must match the actual local theorem; fixed-point consistency alone is not convergence.

### PE28 — hp finite elements and spectral elements

**A:** `hp-finite-elements-and-spectral-element-methods`.

**Disposition:** genuinely-new elementwise degree-adaptive numerical guarantees; NA10/12/28/29/30/35/38 primitives are reused.

**Scope/results:** Define conforming variable-degree element spaces on an explicitly admissible matching mesh and polynomial-degree assignment, hierarchical basis, p/h/hp refinement, and a tensor-product Gauss–Lobatto spectral element on affine quadrilaterals/hexahedra. Prove p-explicit local approximation and inverse/trace inequalities, including degree factors in penalties/preconditioners. Derive energy error via NA29 with actual h,p,regularity dependence. Prove exponential p convergence for analytic solutions on a fixed regular mesh using a stated analytic derivative bound. For polygonal corner singularities prove exponential-in-a-power-of-DOF hp convergence only on the explicitly geometrically graded mesh and weighted-analytic regularity class, with the actual grading/degree law. Spectral-element GLL quadrature error is a Strang crime: mass/stiffness norms must be proved equivalent with p-uniform constants in the stated branch; do not equate collocation with exact Galerkin without checking quadrature.

**Proof route:** Construct reference interval polynomial projection using integrated orthogonal bases and derivative coefficient estimates; tensorize and transport with p-explicit constants. Prove face lifting/conforming assembly for varying degrees. Combine analytic derivative growth and factorial estimates to optimize truncation; for corners sum weighted layer approximation errors and count all DOFs to derive the claimed exponent. GLL norm equivalence and quadrature defects follow exactness plus explicit top-mode correction; variable coefficients require bounded smoothness and aliasing control. Root may select a 2D theorem first, but cannot infer its dimension exponent in 3D.

**Dependencies/smallest suppliers:** NA10/12/28–30/35/38, PE17 for curved extension; local p-explicit polynomial projection/trace/lifting, weighted corner regularity and graded-mesh counting proofs. **B:** analytic p refinement, corner singularity h versus hp, GLL mass top mode, p-dependent DG penalty failure. **Sources:** Schwab, *p- and hp-Finite Element Methods* (1998), polynomial approximation/analytic regularity/geometric mesh chapters, https://global.oup.com/academic/product/p-and-hp-finite-element-methods-9780198503903; Canuto–Hussaini–Quarteroni–Zang, *Spectral Methods: Fundamentals in Single Domains* (2006), GLL/quadrature and spectral approximation, https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-540-30726-6.

### PE30 — First-order least-squares FEM and practical DPG

**A:** `least-squares-fem-and-practical-dpg`.

**Disposition:** extend-existing least-squares/Riesz/projection; genuinely new PDE residual norm equivalence, broken-trace formulation and practical Fortin construction.

**Scope/results:** Define first-order-system least-squares Poisson on H(div)×H1_0 with residual `(sigma+grad u, div sigma-f)` and the correct norm. Prove residual norm equivalent to H(div)×H1_0 error and Galerkin best approximation; boundary conditions are encoded rather than omitted. Define ultraweak Poisson DPG on a stated simplicial mesh with skeleton H1/2 and H-1/2 quotient trace spaces, optimal trial-to-test map via the existing Riesz theorem, and a practical enriched broken test space. Prove continuous inf-sup, ideal energy best approximation, practical quasi-optimality and residual estimator equivalence using a concrete uniformly bounded Fortin operator for the chosen polynomial family/enrichment degree. State the exact degree enrichment from its proved construction. An arbitrary finite test space receives no stability claim.

**Proof route:** For least-squares recover grad u and sigma from the residual using weak Poisson energy, integration by parts and Poincare; triangle/divergence bounds give both inequalities. Prove injectivity/adjoint graph stability and BNB (NA34), then Riesz maps residual to optimal tests. Construct the practical Fortin map locally by moment constraints/bubble corrections, prove polynomial solvability/scaled norm bounds and face trace preservation, then transfer inf-sup. ### PE31 — Semi-Lagrangian characteristic transport

**A:** `semi-lagrangian-characteristic-transport`.

**Disposition:** genuinely-new transport time/space composition error; characteristic ODE and interpolation methods are existing-covered.

**Scope/results:** Define characteristic backtracking plus multilinear/barycentric interpolation for linear nonconservative transport on a periodic box or an explicitly resolved inflow/exit branch. Prove maximum/sup-norm stability for nonnegative partition-of-unity interpolation regardless of displacement-to-cell-size ratio. Prove a smooth-solution global error bound distinguishing characteristic foot error, interpolation defect per step and initial/boundary error: for fixed T, a typical bound is C(dt^q+h^(r+1)/dt) when each backtracking step has O(dt^(q+1)) foot error and interpolation has O(h^(r+1)) defect. State the actual joint mesh/time condition; absence of a classical advective CFL does not eliminate consistency constraints. Define a conservative characteristic-cell overlap method and prove mass conservation/positivity separately; pointwise interpolation is not inherently conservative. High-order interpolation receives its actual operator norm/limiter bound, not automatic L∞ contractivity.

**Proof route:** Characteristic chain rule plus bounded velocity/state derivatives; prove backtracking map stays admissible and handle periodic wrapping/inflow first-hit. Existing ODE local error controls foot error via solution Lipschitz norm. Interpolation convexity proves sup stability; telescope one-step consistency defects with discrete Gronwall. For conservative remapping use Reynolds/Jacobian mass factors for divergence-form transport, orientation/invertibility and partitioned departure-cell measures, then PE17 overlap conservation. Boundary hitting error requires transversal events or exact exit oracle with declared error; no hidden root solve.

**Dependencies/smallest suppliers:** `def-linear-transport-equation-and-its-characteristic-flow`, root ODE flow regularity, NA10, P-NUM-ODE-02, PE17, time-agent event/exit contracts; local interpolation operator norm and divergence Jacobian transport. **B:** rotation across many cells per step, fixed h/dt consistency loss, nonconservative mass drift, conservative deforming-cell remap. **Sources:** Staniforth–Cote, *Semi-Lagrangian Integration Schemes for Atmospheric Models—A Review*, Monthly Weather Review 119 (1991), 2206–2223, https://doi.org/10.1175/1520-0493(1991)119%3C2206:SLISFA%3E2.0.CO;2 (algorithm context); Falcone–Ferretti, *Semi-Lagrangian Approximation Schemes for Linear and Hamilton–Jacobi Equations* (2014), transport interpolation/trajectory convergence chapters, https://doi.org/10.1137/1.9781611973051. ### PE32 — Monotone Hamilton–Jacobi–Bellman schemes and policy iteration

**A:** `monotone-hjb-schemes-and-policy-iteration`.

**Disposition:** genuinely-new spatial viscosity PDE discretization; existing first-order viscosity definitions/comparison/Hopf–Lax and generic finite linear solvers are reused. CTRL owner keeps direct trajectory/collocation/Pontryagin methods.

**Scope/results:** Define monotone upwind and positive-interpolation semi-Lagrangian schemes for a specified first-order HJ/HJB Cauchy problem, and a discounted stationary Bellman equation on a periodic domain with compact or finite controls and bounded Lipschitz coefficients. Prove monotonicity, L∞ stability, consistency and numerical-scheme-to-viscosity convergence by a full half-relaxed-limit argument using the exact existing first-order comparison class. A control minimization over infinite controls is an explicit oracle or a finite control discretization with its own uniform Hamiltonian error. Define finite-control Howard/policy iteration for the assembled proper M-matrix Bellman system; prove monotone improvement and finite termination with deterministic tie handling/no switch on equal value, or state a separately proved contraction stopping bound. Include a concrete second-order Bellman controlled-diffusion scheme on a monotone diagonal-dominant/wide-stencil branch; prove its comparison/convergence with new second-order viscosity suppliers. No generic central stencil monotonicity for arbitrary anisotropic diffusion, no unproved universal convergence rate.

**Proof route:** Upwind/positive interpolation makes nodal update order preserving; positive discount gives stationary sup contraction and discrete comparison/unique solution. Finite policy matrices have verified M-matrix inverse positivity; compare successive value solutions, show each strict switch strictly improves at a component and eliminate repeated policies using tie rule, so finite policy space terminates. Derive consistency on smooth test functions including h²/dt interpolation and control-grid error where relevant. To pass numerical limits, choose strict touching test functions, extract near-maximal mesh nodes, apply discrete monotonicity replacing states by test-function values plus contact error, then take limsup/liminf; prove boundary/initial barriers and use continuous comparison to squeeze both limits. The published half-relaxed theorem applies to approximate PDE solutions, so this mesh-node consistency proof is genuinely new. For second order locally define semijets/degenerate ellipticity, prove the required theorem of sums via sup/inf convolution and twice-differentiability arguments (with actual local analytic suppliers), then doubling variables and discount produce comparison; prove Perron/barrier existence or obtain it from convergent discrete approximants. These large but established proofs belong here before their consumers, not a citation to Barles–Souganidis. Restrict a second-order theorem to the coefficient/boundary class for which all those estimates are supplied.

**Dependencies/smallest suppliers:** NA23/25, PE31, existing first-order viscosity definitions/half-relaxed limits/comparison, canonical linear solves; local mesh touching lemma, discrete comparison/M-matrix, finite-policy monotonicity, control discretization, second-order semijet/theorem-of-sums/comparison/barriers. Choice assumptions follow actual extraction/projection proofs. **B:** eikonal/HJ kink, discounted control switching, monotone versus nonmonotone stencil, diagonal diffusion Bellman manufactured value, infinite-control quantization. **Sources:** Barles–Souganidis, *Convergence of approximation schemes for fully nonlinear second order equations*, Asymptotic Analysis 4 (1991), 271–283, https://doi.org/10.3233/ASY-1991-4305 (full proof must be locally supplied); Crandall–Ishii–Lions, *User's guide to viscosity solutions of second order partial differential equations*, Bull. AMS 27 (1992), 1–67, https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9207212, second-order theorem of sums/comparison; Falcone–Ferretti 2014, https://doi.org/10.1137/1.9781611973051, numerical HJB/policy chapters; Bokanowski–Maroso–Zidani, *Some convergence results for Howard's algorithm*, SIAM JNA 47 (2009), 3001–3026, https://doi.org/10.1137/08073041X, precise finite-policy and convergence hypotheses to verify.
