Direct dependencies: `def-finite-pomdp`, `def-belief-state-and-bayes-filter`, `alg-pomdp-witness-backup`, `lem-finite-pomdp-belief-update-is-probability-kernel`, `thm-belief-mdp-sufficient-statistic`, `def-sum-over-a-finite-index-set`, `lem-finite-sum-laws`, `lem-finite-set-has-max`, `def-finite-cardinality`, `def-nat-power`, `thm-product-rule`, `thm-cardinality-of-a-set-of-functions`, `thm-induction-principle`. - **`ex-pomdp-two-state-bayes-update`, level 4.** Uses self-loop states, prior `(3/4,1/4)` and likelihoods `(3/4,1/4)` to compute `q(x)=5/8`, posterior `(9/10,1/10)`. - **`ex-pomdp-zero-likelihood-observation-is-not-updated`, level 4.** A point prior gives `q(y)=0`, `q(x)=1`; `τ(y)` is undefined and only the positive branch appears in the transition.
