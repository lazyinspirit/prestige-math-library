The examples verify exact quadratic interpolation at three nodes, equality in the pointwise cubic remainder formula, and linear lower growth of equispaced cardinal basis amplification under unit-bounded nodal data. The last example concerns data that vary with degree; polynomial density remains a separate existence statement and the example does not claim divergence for a fixed function.
