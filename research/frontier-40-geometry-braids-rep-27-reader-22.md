## Repairs and evidence

### `def-algebraic-cycle-and-cycle-group`

**Location:** Definition, total group and final conventions.

Restricted the direct-sum total grading to finite type; for general locally finite type schemes the total cycle group permits locally finite sums with unbounded component dimensions.

**Evidence:** The disjoint union of affine spaces of all dimensions has a locally finite fundamental cycle with infinitely many nonzero dimensional parts; it is not in a direct sum over degrees.

### `def-chow-group-of-cycles-mod-rational-equivalence`

**Location:** Definition, divisor support and total Chow group.

Replaced unconditional finite divisor support by locally finite support, with finiteness on finite type schemes; made the total locally finite quotient convention explicit.

**Evidence:** An integral line with infinitely many origins, obtained by gluing copies of A^1 along G_m, has a locally finite but infinite divisor of the coordinate. The existing affine minimal-prime argument proves local finiteness, not global finiteness without quasi-compactness.

### `def-grothendieck-group-of-coherent-sheaves-and-vector-bundles-on-a-scheme`

**Location:** Definition, opening premise and generator sets.

Carried AC from the coherent-category supplier into the opening hypothesis. Indexed generators by isomorphism classes and supplied their set-sized affine presentation/gluing argument.

**Evidence:** The opened thm-coherent-sheaves-abelian-noetherian-scheme explicitly assumes AC. The published Grothendieck-category definition indexes a set of isomorphism classes, rather than the class of all sheaf objects.

### `lem-cycle-of-a-closed-subscheme`

**Location:** Statement; Given; L5; proof 2.1, 3.1, 4.1.

Required X' to be locally of finite type over the same field, inherited AC for going down, and distinguished flatness from the separate pure-fibre hypothesis. Explained the stalk tensor formula using the closed-subscheme quotient; chose a smooth curve for O_C(-p); used quotient lengths rather than the domain order function at possibly nonreduced Cartier local rings.

**Evidence:** Without local finite type, even a zero-dimensional flat fibre may be non-Noetherian and have infinite length (a square-zero algebra k plus an infinite-dimensional k-space). def-flat-morphism-schemes supplies flatness only. The order-function supplier is explicitly for domains; the fundamental Cartier coefficient is instead the finite quotient length.

### `def-pushforward-in-algebraic-k-theory`

**Location:** Definition, functoriality well-definedness argument.

Supplied the composition spectral sequence from the Grothendieck spectral sequence and checked its module-injective/flasque acyclicity hypotheses.

**Evidence:** The cited Leray statement concerns global sections and one direct image, not the displayed two-direct-image E_2 page. The newly opened Grothendieck spectral-sequence statement, module-injectives-flasque lemma, flasque acyclicity theorem and existing Leray module comparison supply exactly this extension.

### `lem-k-theory-of-projective-space-and-projections`

### `lem-two-dimensional-tame-symbol-reciprocity`

Replaced the unsupported Cartier consequence with the rational-section key formula, its coefficientwise proof by unit rescaling and local reciprocity, first-Chern descent, Cartier descent in the supported target, and commutation on the common zero scheme.

### `lem-relative-projective-bundle-k-theory-generators`

**Evidence:** Exterior powers of the pulled-back E need not be line bundles, and alternating negative coefficients are not the graded pieces of an extension. A line-quotient exact sequence gives two exterior filtration pieces; solving its recurrence gives the claimed alternating sum.
