## Mathematical review results

The sheafification proof uses the filtered preorder of covering supports, rather than assuming that the category retaining all refinement maps is filtered. Its finite-limit calculation first commutes matching families with limits, then invokes the opened filtered-colimit supplier. The descent proof establishes saturated quasi-compact quasi-affine pieces, flat base change of their finite equalizers, effective algebra descent, invariant-open descent, and gluing; its final steps separately descend separatedness and local quasi-finiteness.

The algebraic-space quotient chain does not assume that an arbitrary quotient is already algebraic: the affine lemma first descends the quotient-map pullbacks, then separately descends the diagonal. Open subquotients and disjoint-union gluing pass to arbitrary schemes. The repaired presentation definition is consumed only for its kernel-pair and quotient clauses; no assigned consumer uses the removed quasi-compactness-invariance assertion.

The normalization and Dold–Kan arguments preserve the last-face sign. The standard resolution contraction is a set-level extra degeneracy, with the additive homology passage provided by the separate prism/normalization lemma. The cotangent comparison uses the opposite polynomial category and products of augmentation fibres; its base-change isomorphism retains the derived-Tor-independence hypothesis and does not assert arbitrary ordinary base-change invariance. The boundary/horn product proof specifies pivots, attachment order and new omitted faces; the variable-base model construction uses cotensors and the path/retract argument, not a presumed simplicial-set model structure. Fixed-base derivations and ordinary specialization are distinguished from weak coefficient replacement and global descent.

For BG, finite-etale descent is applied on affine fpqc refinements, including arbitrary test schemes. The diagonal is its equivariant Isom sheaf, the atlas pullback is the torsor, and the product inertia formula is restricted to abelian G. The counterexample uses the trivial torsor's nontrivial automorphisms, without assuming that every torsor over the field is trivial. The batch-15 supplier supplies precisely the trivial-action quotient-sheaf comparison. The inertia equivalence now has its explicit choice-free inverse.
