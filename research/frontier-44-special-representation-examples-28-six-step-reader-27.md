## Uneditable downstream observation

`items/lem-kahler-commutators-and-laplacian-type-preservation.md`, Proof **4.1**, line **92**, in current-run **batch 31**, contains the false intermediate operator equalities `*dd*=dd**` and `*d*d=d*d*` (where the leading star is Hodge star and the stars on d are adjoints). Hodge star interchanges the two summands: the needed identities are `*dd*=d*d*` and `*d*d=dd**`.

On the flat two-torus, let `f(x,y)=cos(2πx)`. Then `dd* f=0`, so `*dd* f=0`, but `dd*(*f)=4π² *f`, since `d(*f)=0` and the coefficientwise Laplacian is `-∂x²-∂y²`. Thus the first printed equality is false. The intended conclusion `*Δ=Δ*` is true, but needs the two correctly swapped summands and their adjacent-degree signs established from the star/adjoint formulas. This is a confirmed false proof claim (fatal under the reader rule for false claims), not grounds to withdraw the true commutation conclusion. The supplier interfaces used for this witness were opened above.
