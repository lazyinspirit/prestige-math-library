## Uneditable finding

Subject: `split-reductive-root-systems-bruhat-cells-and-parabolics-examples`, prose lines 32–33, GLn summary.

The phrase “minimal parabolics are the stabilizers of a single partial flag” gives no flag type and cannot characterize minimal parabolics as written. On the unrestricted partial-flag reading, every standard parabolic is such a stabilizer. If “single” means a flag with one proper subspace, these are the **maximal** proper parabolics. For GL4, the stabilizer of a line is P_{ {2,3} }; it strictly contains P_{ {2} } above B, so it is not minimal above B. The opened example’s Example paragraph and Verification 3.1 correctly identify the single-subspace stabilizers as P_{Δ\{i}}, whereas minimal parabolics above B have singleton I and preserve a flag with one omitted stage. Milne21.93 and the standard subset classification give the same distinction. The lead should specify the flag type and distinguish minimal-above-B from maximal proper parabolics, retaining the n≤2 caveat. This is a suspected overstrong/ambiguous page classification, not a claim that every possible weak reading of that sentence is false. B-page prose was not edited.
