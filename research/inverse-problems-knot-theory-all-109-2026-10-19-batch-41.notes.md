## Mathematical discrepancy escalated to owner

The IP-41 plan's printed boundary conjugacy is inconsistent with the standard full physical flux convention. For $\rho=\sqrt\gamma$, $u=\rho v$, $\Lambda_\gamma f=\gamma\partial_\nu v|_{\partial\Omega}$, and $\Lambda_q g=\partial_\nu u|_{\partial M}$, direct differentiation at the boundary gives
$$\Lambda_\gamma f=\rho\,\Lambda_q(\rho f)-\rho(\partial_\nu\rho)f.$$
The plan prints the final term without the factor $\rho$. Indeed, $\partial_\nu u=(\partial_\nu\rho)f+\rho\partial_\nu v$ and $\gamma=\rho^2$, so multiplying by $\rho$ gives the displayed corrected relation. The source passage in Salo's notes is consistent with the corrected formula when its boundary argument $f$ is tracked through $\Lambda_\gamma(\rho^{-1}f)$.

- **Affected claims:** `lem-ip-conductivity-liouville-transform-and-dn-conjugacy` and `cor-ip-euclidean-cta-conductivity-uniqueness`.
- **Disposition:** preserve the standard physical flux convention and prove the corrected relation locally; do not reproduce the algebraically false printed formula. Owner reconciliation is required for the shared plan/design line. No shared plan was edited.
- **Proposed placement:** correct the boundary formula in the IP-41 design under the existing Liouville/DN-conjugacy item, then retain the standard conductivity flux contract in `def-ip-conductivity-dirichlet-to-neumann-map`.
