## Confirmed defects repaired

1. **Circumcenter lemma, proof 3.1:** the intersection of finitely many decreasing sublevel sets was called the set with the smallest index. Changed to the set with the largest index. Immediate nonfatal correction; the nested-set FIP argument and choice-free proof are otherwise valid.
2. **Angular-link lemma, proof 4.1:** the Euclidean normal link at the relative interior of a Coxeter face was asserted to equal a spherical face link without deriving that identification. Added the calculation: the face direction space is V_U; the containing facets are precisely those indexed outside U; the tangent cone is V_U plus the cone on the negative simple vectors outside U; orthogonal projection leaves the negative projected vectors, with the stated normalized Schur-complement Gram matrix. Explicit face-map derivatives and the cocycle justify gluing these links. This closes an essential omitted geometric prerequisite without changing the statement.

Clause (iv) and proof 4.1 excluded pi/m without its m=2 equality exception; stated the exact exception.
