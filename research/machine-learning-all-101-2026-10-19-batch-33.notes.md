## Mathematical conventions and obligations

- Feature maps are row vectors of finitely many channels. A filter value is a `C_in × C_out` matrix, so the planned scalar expression `f(h) ψ(g^{-1}h)` extends without changing order.
- For a left action on sites, `L_u f(x)=f(u^{-1}·x)`; on group-indexed features `L_uF(g)=F(u^{-1}g)`. The group-to-group proof reindexes with the bijection `h=uk`; the first image-to-group proof reindexes the lattice with `x=u·y`. No finiteness of G is used.
- The image-to-group layer uses filters on `Z^2`; later layers use maps and filters on G. Group sums and lattice sums are finite-support or absolutely convergent.
- Pointwise functions act componentwise. Non-strided pooling uses a fixed finite right neighborhood. Restricting the feature domain to a subgroup retains only that subgroup action; pooling over finite right cosets is defined on `G/H` and yields a well-defined quotient-indexed map. Pooling over all of a finite group is invariant.
- A finite grid is equivariant only when the group action and boundary/output convention are compatible. The B-page zero-padding witness records a concrete failure; no universal approximation, benchmark superiority, continuous-rotation, or CNN-training claim is made.
