## Mathematical content decisions (checkpoint per item)

- **Derivative conventions.** Real scalars throughout; Fréchet derivative with the uniform
  $\varepsilon$-$\delta$ remainder; the definition records that the limit is over a punctured
  neighbourhood and that differentiability implies continuity (remark with a complete two-line
  estimate), and delegates uniqueness to item 2.
- **Rules.** Sum rule, bounded-bilinear product rule and chain rule are proved pointwise; the product
  rule expands the increment so that the only surviving term is $B(h,k)$, bounded by
  $C\|h\|\|k\|$; the chain rule splits the two remainders with explicit thresholds.
- **$C^k$.** Recursive operator-norm definition through the Banach spaces
  $\mathcal B_k=\mathcal B(X,\mathcal B_{k-1})$; the identification with bounded multilinear maps is
  recorded as a reading convention, and only the $k=1$ composition/chain rule is asserted (the
  $k\ge2$ composition rule is explicitly not used).
- **Mean value estimate.** Proved by the Hahn–Banach norming functional and the scalar mean value
  theorem; AC is stated and declared, and its single use (step 1.2) is identified. The degenerate
  cases $x=y$ and $f(x)=f(y)$ are separated.
- **Inverse theorem.** Normalisation by $A^{-1}$ and a translation (with a proved affine-changes
  lemma), contraction on a closed ball, Lipschitz inverse, derivative $D\Phi(g(y))^{-1}$, $C^1$ from
  continuity of inversion, and $C^k$ by the $C^\infty$ regularity of inversion on the open set of
  invertible operators proved from the Neumann series. AC is declared and propagated.
- **Implicit theorem.** Reduction to $(x,y)\mapsto(x,F(x,y))$, explicit triangular inverse of the
  block derivative, both directions of the local "exactly when" statement, and
  $Dg=-D_YF^{-1}D_XF$ along the graph with the Neumann perturbation keeping $D_YF$ invertible.
- **Manifolds.** Hausdorff, second countable atlas with $C^k$ transitions; chart independence of the
  class of a map proved for $k=1$ (chain rule) and explicitly not asserted for $k\ge2$; tangents as
  chart-classes modulo the transition derivative; well-definedness, the vector-space structure and
  functoriality proved in item 10.
- **Split submanifolds and regular values.** Splitness is the slice condition with a complemented
  model-space splitting; the regular value theorem prints "onto with complemented kernel" and is
  proved by the implicit theorem in a complement of the kernel, with the tangent identified with the
  kernel; the closing remark explains why surjectivity alone cannot replace the split-kernel clause
  and records the automatic cases.
- **Bundle sections and transversality.** The vertical derivative is defined through a
  trivialisation, and its independence is proved in the definition item from the cocycle identity
  $\sigma_\Psi=g\sigma_\Phi$ at a zero; the transverse-section theorem applies the regular value
  theorem locally and then shows the conclusion and tangent description are trivialisation-invariant.
- **Fredholm maps.** Definition with pointwise index and a separate constant-index phrase; index
  invariance under chart conjugation via additivity (AC declared); local reduction to
  $(u,v)\mapsto(u,g(u,v))$ with $u$ in a complement of the range, $v$ in the finite-dimensional
  kernel, and $g$ finite-dimensional; local constancy of the index; the projection examples and the
  $c_0\subset\ell^\infty$ counterexample, which declares AC_ω and proves non-complementation of
  $c_0$ inline from A-homed quotient and countability suppliers (see "Local suppliers added").
