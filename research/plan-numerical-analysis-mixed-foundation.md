## Domain, norms and final contract

Let d∈{2,3} and D⊂R^d be a nonempty bounded connected open Lipschitz domain. Here Lipschitz means a finite boundary atlas after rigid motions in which D is locally the strict epigraph of a Lipschitz function, with interior side and chart dimensions specified. No boundary smoothness, convexity or global star-shapedness is required. Connectedness is essential: on disconnected domains the compatible data have zero mean on each component, not merely global zero mean.

Set Q=L²₀(D)={q∈L²(D;R):∫D q=0} and V=H¹₀(D;R^d), the closure of C∞c(D;R^d). Use the Frobenius gradient norm |v|₁=||∇v||₂ and the ordinary L² pressure norm. Bounded-domain Poincaré makes |·|₁ a Hilbert norm on V. The final theorem constructs a **linear** bounded operator B_D:Q→V such that div B_Dq=q in distributions (hence in L²) and

$$||\nabla B_Dq||_2\le C_{\mathrm{div}}(D)||q||_2.$$

With the chosen Poincaré constant C_P(D), also ||B_Dq||H¹≤sqrt(1+C_P(D)²) C_div(D)||q||₂. Constants depend on d and the explicitly chosen finite Lipschitz/star-shaped cover, balls, cutoffs and overlap bumps; they are finite, may deteriorate with domain geometry, and are independent of FEM mesh h. No domain-uniform or optimal constant is promised. Inherited choice assumptions of the actual Fourier/Sobolev/density suppliers must be carried explicitly; finite covers/tree choices do not erase those assumptions.

For the exact conforming mini-element theorem, D is additionally polygonal/polyhedral and admits the stated conforming affine simplicial meshes; this is the natural domain for that already approved exact-mesh claim. Continuous lifting remains valid on the full Lipschitz class. Curved-domain approximation needs a separately declared lift/geometry estimate, never an implicit claim that straight simplices exactly tile a curved domain. Preserve d=2 or 3, a connected shape-regular conforming family, homogeneous velocity trace, continuous piecewise-affine zero-mean pressure, and continuous affine velocities enriched by one scalar interior simplex bubble per vector component. The mesh-shape constant, viscosity and C_div(D) remain in all pressure constants.

## Proposed typed local carriers in dependency order

1. `def-na-stokes-zero-mean-pressure-and-velocity-spaces` (definition): D,d,V,Q, norms, symmetric gradient ε(v), and b(v,q)=−∫D q div v. Declare real spaces, ν>0, and the exact mini-element domain/mesh subclass separately.

2. `lem-na-lipschitz-domain-finite-star-shaped-cover` (lemma): construct a finite cover D=⋃ᵢUᵢ by bounded open sets Uᵢ⊂D, each star-shaped with respect to an interior ball Bᵢ of radius ρᵢ>0, with connected intersection graph. Boundary patches meet ∂D in their closure. Prove the geometry locally: shrink a Lipschitz graph chart to a convex horizontal cylinder; choose an interior ball high above the graph; choose its height/radius so every segment from every point of the lower patch to the ball remains above the graph, using φ(x′)−φ(y′)≤L|x′−y′|. Keep the horizontal cylinder and upper cap convex along those segments. Cover the remaining compact interior by balls. Compactness gives finitely many patches, and connected D forces a connected intersection graph. Supply a subordinate nonnegative bounded partition χᵢ with Σχᵢ=1 on D. Only bounded measurability and 0≤χᵢ≤1 are needed for the L² decomposition: one may partition into measurable disjoint sets Eᵢ⊂Uᵢ and take χᵢ=1Eᵢ, avoiding unnecessary cutoff differentiability claims. For each tree edge pick ψ_e∈C∞c(Uᵢ∩Uⱼ), ∫ψ_e=1. Prove existence using a ball in that nonempty open intersection and a scaled nonnegative smooth bump. Record all chosen balls, patch diameters Rᵢ, and bump norms. An unsupported appeal to “Lipschitz localization” is insufficient.

3. `lem-na-finite-cover-zero-mean-data-decomposition` (lemma): root a spanning tree of that finite graph. Put gᵢ=χᵢq, mᵢ=∫gᵢ and Mᵢ=Σmⱼ over the subtree at i. If ψᵢ is the bump on edge i→parent(i), define qᵢ=gᵢ+Σchildren j Mⱼψⱼ−Mᵢψᵢ for nonroot i, and omit the last term at the root. Zero-extend all terms to D. Prove ∫qᵢ=0, support in Uᵢ, Σqᵢ=q and linear dependence on q. Since Σχᵢ=1 and χᵢ≥0, |Mᵢ|≤|D|¹ᐟ²||q||₂. In particular ||qᵢ||L²(Uᵢ)≤Dᵢ||q||₂ with the explicit finite bound Dᵢ=1+|D|¹ᐟ²Σe incident at i ||ψ_e||₂. The root uses Σmᵢ=0. No derivative of χᵢ is used. This finite transfer proof supplies localization; a mean-subtraction on each patch without compensating overlaps would fail.

4. `def-na-star-shaped-bogovskii-integral` (definition): for a bounded U star-shaped with respect to B(a,ρ), choose ω∈C∞c(B), ∫ω=1. Initially for smooth compactly supported f of zero mean, set

$$B_Uf(x)=\int_U f(y)(x-y)\int_0^1\omega\!\left(y+\frac{x-y}{t}\right)\frac{dt}{t^{d+1}}\,dy.$$

This is a mathematical integral operator, not a finite executable procedure or quadrature oracle. Translate a to zero before coordinate estimates; U then lies in |y|≤R=diam U.

5. `lem-na-bogovskii-kernel-support-and-divergence` (lemma): establish |G(x,y)|≤C|x−y|¹⁻ᵈ on bounded support; the radial singularity is integrable locally. Bound the integral operator in L² by the two Schur integrals on a bounded containing ball (prove the elementary Schur/Cauchy–Schwarz estimate locally if absent). For compactly supported smooth f, nonzero kernel points lie on segments joining supp f to supp ω; star-shapedness puts their compact union inside U. Prove smoothness/distributional interpretation by truncated t-integrals and test functions. Direct change of variables z=y+(x−y)/t and integration in t gives div B_Uf=f−ω∫f, hence f for zero mean. Check boundary contributions at t=0 and t=1 in the test-function pairing; do not differentiate the singular kernel absolutely. Establish C∞c(U) output for smooth compact input, or alternatively prove compact support plus global H¹ and mollification inside its positive boundary margin; either route must actually establish H¹₀ membership.

6. `lem-na-bogovskii-truncated-derivative-fourier-formula` (lemma): for φ=ω or x_kω define Tφ,j by the distributional limit of ∫ε¹∫∂xⱼ[φ(y+(x−y)/t)] f(y)dy dt/t^d. Justify the limit against Schwartz tests by the O(ε)||φ||₁||f||₁ estimate and truncated Fubini/change of variables. Splitting at t=1/2, derive

$$\widehat{T_{\phi,j}^{<}f}(\xi)=2\pi i\xi_j\int_0^{1/2}\widehat\phi(t\xi)\widehat f((1-t)\xi)\,dt.$$

The actual derivative is ∂ⱼ(B_Uf)_k=T_(x_kω),j f−T_ω,j(y_kf); retain this minus sign from the defining difference. Signs do not affect the subsequent norm bound. Prove or consume the actual d-dimensional Plancherel, scaling, Fourier differentiation and smooth-density interfaces; a one-dimensional result without a tensor-product proof is insufficient.

7. `lem-na-bogovskii-fourier-ray-and-derivative-ltwo-bound` (lemma): define A_j(φ)=2^((d−1)/2)(ρ⁻¹||φ||₁+ρ||∂ⱼ²φ||₁)+2^d|U|||∂ⱼφ||∞. Prove 2π|ξⱼ|∫₀∞|φ̂(tξ)|dt≤ρ⁻¹||φ||₁+ρ||∂ⱼ²φ||₁ by splitting at (2πρ|ξⱼ|)⁻¹ and using φ̂ bounded by ||φ||₁ and the Fourier transform of ∂ⱼ²φ; ξⱼ=0 contributes zero and is handled separately. Apply weighted Cauchy–Schwarz to the Fourier formula, Tonelli, η=(1−t)ξ and s=t/(1−t), obtaining ||T< f||₂≤2^((d−1)/2)(ρ⁻¹||φ||₁+ρ||∂ⱼ²φ||₁)||f||₂. For 1/2≤t≤1 use the bounded kernel on U×U and Cauchy–Schwarz to obtain the second A_j term. Conclude ||Tφ,j f||L²(U)≤A_j(φ)||f||₂. Supply every interchange/limit by truncation, density and distributional convergence; a singular-integral theorem quoted without its local proof is not substituted.

8. `thm-na-star-shaped-bogovskii-bounded-right-inverse` (theorem): combine the preceding coordinate bounds, |y_k|≤R, and Frobenius summation to take

$$C_U=\left(\sum_{k,j=1}^d[A_j(x_k\omega)+R A_j(\omega)]^2\right)^{1/2}.$$

Prove ||∇B_Uf||₂≤C_U||f||₂ and H¹₀ output. Approximate any f∈L²₀(U) by f_n=h_n−ω∫h_n, h_n∈C∞c(U), h_n→f in L²; prove bounded-domain integral convergence. The H¹₀ Poincaré estimate makes B_Uf_n Cauchy in H¹; its limit is independent of approximants, linear, and has divergence f. Zero extension of H¹₀(U) into H¹₀(D) is proved from C∞c approximants, rather than a boundary regularity assertion. Retain the available Sobolev/choice premises.

9. `thm-na-bounded-lipschitz-domain-divergence-right-inverse` (theorem): put B_Dq=Σᵢ extension₀(B_Uᵢqᵢ). Finite summation, the decomposition and the local theorem give the promised linear right inverse, with the concrete valid bound C_div(D)=Σᵢ C_UᵢDᵢ. State dependence on this chosen cover, never on mesh h. Prove ∫div v=0 for v∈H¹₀(D)^d by compact smooth approximation, establishing compatibility as well as sufficiency.

10. `lem-na-homogeneous-dirichlet-korn-identity` (lemma): for v∈C∞c(D)^d, two integrations by parts show 2||ε(v)||₂²=||∇v||₂²+||div v||₂². Extend by H¹₀ density. Thus ||∇v||₂≤sqrt(2)||ε(v)||₂ with no rigid-motion quotient or unproved general Korn theorem. With a(u,v)=2ν∫ε(u):ε(v), this yields a(v,v)≥ν|v|₁². Reuse Poincaré to pass to full H¹ norm only with its explicit constant.

11. `thm-na-continuous-stokes-divergence-infsup` (theorem): for q≠0 take v=−B_Dq; b(v,q)=||q||₂² and |v|₁≤C_div(D)||q||₂, hence infq supv b(v,q)/(|v|₁||q||₂)≥1/C_div(D). Prove boundedness |b(v,q)|≤sqrt(d)|v|₁||q||₂. These are premises of the locally proved mixed/BNB result, not consequences inferred circularly from Stokes solvability.

12. `thm-na-mini-element-fortin-and-uniform-infsup` (theorem): after the approved NA-28 interpolation/trace interfaces, construct the boundary-preserving stable quasi-interpolant and element bubble corrections that preserve each cell's vector mean. Prove the chosen quasi-interpolant's local L² error and H¹ stability on shape-regular vertex patches, including boundary patches; point interpolation of an arbitrary H¹ function is forbidden. A bubble b_K vanishes on ∂K and has ∫K b_K≠0. For each component choose its coefficient to restore ∫K(v−Π_hv)=0. Since ∇q_h is constant on K and v−Π_hv has zero boundary trace globally, element integration by parts gives ∫D q_h div(v−Π_hv)=0. Reference-cell scaling, finite patch overlap and interpolation estimates yield |Π_hv|₁≤C_F|v|₁, uniformly in h; state dependence on d and shape regularity. Transfer β_h≥1/(C_F C_div(D)); then prove the mixed Galerkin pressure/velocity estimates with β_h retained.
