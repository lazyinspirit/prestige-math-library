These examples check the hypotheses and conventions of the A page at concrete
boundaries. Each calculation is carried by its own proof and uses the exact
dependencies recorded in the batch manifest.

- [[cex-dirac-on-euclidean-space-is-not-fredholm]] shows why compactness
  matters: low Fourier frequencies make the Euclidean Dirac range dense but
  not closed.
- [[ex-complex-projective-plane-has-nonspin-tangent-bundle]] computes a
  nonzero $w_2$ and rules out a Spin lift.
- [[ex-flat-even-torus-spin-dirac-has-index-zero]] computes the periodic
  flat-torus kernel by Fourier modes and checks equality of chiral dimensions.
- [[ex-positive-scalar-curvature-sphere-has-zero-spin-dirac-kernel]] applies
  the untwisted Lichnerowicz vanishing result to the round two-sphere.
- [[ex-twisted-sphere-dirac-index-and-curvature-term]] computes a nonzero
  twist curvature term and the corresponding integer index.

The flat-torus and sphere examples use the completed complex Clifford-module
and half-spin interfaces on the A page. The negative cotangent action is
$c_0(v)=i c_+(v)$, with the corresponding volume-chirality sign recorded there.

native readiness and final owner acceptance remain separate workflow records.
