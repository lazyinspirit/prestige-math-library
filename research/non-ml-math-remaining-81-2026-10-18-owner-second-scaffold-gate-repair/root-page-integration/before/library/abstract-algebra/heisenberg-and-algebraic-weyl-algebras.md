This page defines the finite-dimensional Heisenberg Lie algebra and the algebraic polynomial Weyl algebra with its central parameter. It proves the central-quotient presentation and the basis $x^\alpha p^\beta$ over every field, then separates the characteristic-zero trace obstruction from the positive-characteristic module examples on the companion page.

## Scope boundary

The algebra here is the tensor-algebra quotient on polynomial generators. It is distinct from exponentiated Weyl relations or a Weyl $C^*$-algebra. The common-domain, self-adjointness, regularity, and integration requirements for an infinitesimal-to-exponentiated theorem are recorded in [[rem-algebraic-weyl-modules-do-not-automatically-exponentiate]]; [[lem-common-domain-commutator-integrates-to-weyl-relations]] proves the bridge for a common invariant graph core with graph-differentiable unitary orbits. It derives the bounded Weyl relations by closing the integrated conjugation identity and applying Stone generator uniqueness. The algebraic results do not depend on this analytic lemma. The later [[regular-weyl-representations-and-stone-von-neumann]] page retains its separate universal-completion and representation-uniqueness obligations.

## Source and supplier limits

The proof uses the current local statement and proof of [[thm-poincare-birkhoff-witt]] for the ordered basis of the Heisenberg enveloping algebra. The Dumas and Etingof treatments provide independent Weyl-algebra presentations and context; the characteristic-independent normal-order proof is supplied locally. The physics Fock construction and the published uncertainty inequality remain context only; neither is a universal-algebra or analytic-representation dependency here.
