Lie Algebra Homology and Cohomology
Lie algebras were introduced by Sophus Lie in connection with his studies of
Lie groups; Lie groups are not only groups but also smooth manifolds, the
group operations being smooth. If G is a Lie group, the tangent space g of G
at the identity e e G is a Lie algebra over IR. The vector space of left invariant
vector fields on G is canonically isomorphic to g, and the Lie bracket [X, Y]
of vector fields X and Y may be defined as a vector field:
[X, Y]f = X(Yf) - Y(Xf), 
f a smooth function on G.
This rich interplay with Differential Geometry forms the original motivation
for the abstract study of Lie algebras. More history is given in 7.8.14 below.
