These worked calculations accompany [[frobenius-algebras-and-two-dimensional-sewing]]. They show that the dual numbers are symmetric Frobenius but nonsemisimple in every characteristic, and that a nonabelian group algebra can be symmetric Frobenius without being commutative. The latter excludes it as the algebra assigned to a circle by a closed oriented two-dimensional TQFT; it makes no claim about an open-closed extension with a separate closed algebra.

The examples use the actual all-field statement of the published coefficient-of-identity proposition, despite its narrower title. Full computations and the exact supplier limits are in the individual example items. These carriers remain drafts; the calculations and local checks do not constitute independent approval.
