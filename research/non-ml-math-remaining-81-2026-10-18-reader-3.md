## Findings that could not be edited

1. `lem-c1-local-diffeomorphisms-preserve-null-sets-locally`, Statement opening
   assumption and Proof 1.1: the item says “$C^1$ local diffeomorphism,” while
   its cited definition defines local diffeomorphisms only for smooth maps.
   If the intended premise is a $C^1$ diffeomorphism with $C^1$ local inverse,
   that notion and the local inverse result are not stated in this item’s
   declared prerequisites. Define/cite that finite-regularity notion and its
   local inverse theorem, or state the smooth premise. This is a suspected
   citation/definition mismatch, not a counterexample to the intended claim.

2. `prop-an-equidimensional-c1-map-sends-null-sets-to-null-sets`, Proof 3.1:
   the proof takes a countable union of Euclidean null sets and says
   “Countable subadditivity makes it null.” The item’s dependencies and
   displayed facts do not supply or prove countable-union closure for Euclidean
   nullity. Under its stated $mathrm{AC}_\omega$ hypothesis, close this with
   the cube-cover argument using budgets $\varepsilon 2^{-i-1}$, or cite/add
   `lem-null-sets-in-rn-closed-under-subsets-and-countable-unions`.

3. `thm-transverse-preimage-theorem`, Proof 2.1: surjectivity of the map on
   $T_yN/T_yZ$ is converted to surjectivity of $dh_y\circ dF_p$ after asserting
   $\ker dh_y=T_yZ$. The local-defining-map source and regular-level tangent
   result are available in the item’s Facts, but Step 2.1 does not cite/use the
   latter. Add that tangent-kernel step and identify the induced quotient map
   $T_yN/T_yZ\to\mathbb R^c$ as an isomorphism.

4. `thm-morse-sard-for-euclidean-maps`, Proof 2.2: the slicing lemma requires
   its compact set to lie in $[a,b]\times\mathbb R^{n-1}$. The proof applies it
   directly to $f(C_0\cap\overline B)$ without choosing such an interval. For
   a nonempty compact image, bound its first-coordinate projection in a compact
   interval (handle the empty image separately) before applying the lemma.

5. `thm-parametric-transversality`, Proof 1.1 and 4.1: Sard is applied to
   $\pi_S:W\to S$, but the proof only establishes that $W$ is embedded and
   does not establish that this restricted projection is smooth. State that
   the inclusion of the embedded submanifold $W$ is smooth and compose it with
   the smooth product projection, or verify smoothness in slice charts.

6. `ex-a-fibre-product-of-submersions`, Verification 1.1: the text inserts
   `[L1, given, algebra]` between prose and the displayed matrix, then repeats
   it at the end of the step. Move the tags to the step end so the step has one
   valid terminal tag clause.

7. `cor-outside-a-null-set-every-point-is-a-transverse-zero-after-translation`,
