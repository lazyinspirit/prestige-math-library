## Statement

Assume AC. Let $U$ be smooth quasiprojective over $\mathbb C$, and choose an SNC smooth projective compactification $j:U\hookrightarrow X$, boundary $D$. The logarithmic de Rham complex with Hodge filtration by degree and pole-count weight filtration constructs a functorial rational mixed Hodge structure on $H^n(U,\mathbb Q)$, independent of the compactification. Its weights are at least $n$, and
$$W_nH^n(U,\mathbb Q)=\operatorname{im}(H^n(X,\mathbb Q)\longrightarrow H^n(U,\mathbb Q)).$$

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The Axiom of Choice; a smooth quasiprojective complex variety $U$ of pure dimension $d$; an SNC smooth projective compactification $j:U\hookrightarrow X$ with reduced boundary $D$ a simple normal crossings divisor ([[def-simple-normal-crossings-divisors]], [[lem-smooth-quasiprojective-varieties-admit-snc-projective-compactifications]]); the irreducible components $D_1,\dots,D_r$ of $D$ and, for $a\ge0$, the closed subvariety $D^{[a]}\subseteq X$ equal to $X$ for $a=0$ and to the disjoint union of the $a$-fold intersections $D_{i_1}\cap\dots\cap D_{i_a}$ for $a\ge1$. Cohomology is singular cohomology of the analytic spaces on the analytic topology of [[def-analytic-topology-and-finite-smooth-algebraic-stratifications]].

[F1] Analytification ([[lem-smooth-projective-analytification-has-rational-kahler-polarization]], [[def-analytic-topology-and-finite-smooth-algebraic-stratifications]], [[def-compact-kahler-manifold-and-hodge-polarization-conventions]]). $X$ is a compact complex manifold of dimension $d$ with a canonical orientation, $D$ is a reduced divisor with normal crossings, and near every point of $D$ there are holomorphic coordinates $z_1,\dots,z_d$ in which $D$ is $\{z_1\cdots z_r=0\}$ for some $r=r(p)$; each $D^{[a]}$ is a smooth projective complex variety of pure dimension $d-a$ (empty for $a>r$), a morphism of complex varieties induces a holomorphic map, and $U=X\setminus D$ is an open complex submanifold of dimension $d$. Complex-valued smooth forms, the bidegree decomposition, $d=\partial+\bar\partial$ and type are those of [[def-bigraded-complex-differential-forms]], and holomorphic functions on polydiscs are those of [[def-holomorphic-function-in-several-complex-variables]].

[F2] The de Rham comparison ([[thm-de-rham-theorem]], [[thm-choice-implies-dependent-implies-countable-choice]]). For every finite-dimensional Hausdorff second-countable smooth manifold $M$, integration of smooth forms on smooth simplices is a natural isomorphism $H^*_{\mathrm{dR}}(M)\cong H^*_{\mathrm{sing}}(M;\mathbb R)$ under $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$, which AC supplies. It applies to the real manifolds underlying $X$, the strata $D^{[a]}$ and $U$, and is compatible with the coefficient extension to $\mathbb C$.

[F3] Hodge theory of the compact strata ([[thm-compact-kahler-hodge-decomposition-via-elliptic-harmonic-forms]], [[lem-smooth-projective-analytification-has-rational-kahler-polarization]], [[def-compact-kahler-manifold-and-hodge-polarization-conventions]]). Let $V$ be a smooth projective complex variety of pure dimension $m$. Then $H^k(V;\mathbb Q)$ carries a functorial rational pure Hodge structure of weight $k$, whose Hodge filtration is the filtration induced by the holomorphic-degree filtration $\Omega^{\ge p}_V$ of the holomorphic de Rham complex; there is a Hodge decomposition $H^k(V;\mathbb C)=\bigoplus_{p+q=k}H^{p,q}(V)$ with $H^{p,q}(V)\cong H^q(V,\Omega^p_V)$; the Hodge-de Rham spectral sequence degenerates at $E_1$, so the differentials of $\Omega^p_V$ are strict for the Hodge filtration on their cohomology; and pullback along a morphism and cup product preserve type. In particular $H^k(V;\mathbb Q)$ is finite-dimensional over $\mathbb Q$.

[F4] Local analytic inputs ([[thm-dolbeault-lemma-polydisc]], [[thm-cohomological-kunneth-isomorphism-under-finite-free-hypotheses]], [[cor-homology-of-spheres]], [[cor-cohomology-over-a-field-is-dual-to-homology-over-that-field]], [[lem-locally-contractible-metric-spaces-have-singular-constant-sheaf-cohomology]]). On a polydisc $P\subseteq\mathbb C^m$, every $\bar\partial$-closed smooth $(p,q)$-form with $q\ge1$ is $\bar\partial$-exact on every smaller relatively compact polydisc. For a product of circles the Kunneth theorem computes the cohomology as the exterior algebra on the degree-one generators, with each factor contributing one generator because the homology of the circle is $\mathbb Q$ in degrees $0$ and $1$; over the field $\mathbb Q$ the Kronecker evaluation identifies a cohomology group with the full dual of homology, so the classes are detected by their values on the torus cycles.

[F5] Mixed Hodge machinery ([[def-rational-mixed-hodge-structure]], [[def-cohomological-mixed-hodge-complex]], [[lem-mixed-hodge-complexes-give-functorial-cohomology-and-simplicial-weights]], [[lem-morphisms-of-mixed-hodge-structures-are-strict]]). A rational mixed Hodge structure is a $\mathbb Q$-vector space with a finite increasing weight filtration $W$ and a finite decreasing Hodge filtration $F$ on the complexification, opposed on every weight-graded piece; a rational cohomological mixed Hodge complex is a filtered pair $(K_{\mathbb Q},(K_{\mathbb C},W))$ with a comparison whose graded pieces are quasi-isomorphisms and whose weight-graded complexes have finite-dimensional cohomology with strict differentials and pure Hodge cohomology of weight $r+w$; a morphism of such complexes induces morphisms of the resulting structures; morphisms of mixed Hodge structures are strict for $W$ and $F$, and the image of a morphism is both a sub- and a quotient structure. The supplier [[lem-mixed-hodge-complexes-give-functorial-cohomology-and-simplicial-weights]] records an open obligation: it proves the weight filtration, the purity of the $E_1$ and $E_2$ terms, the $E_2$-degeneration and the graded identifications, but not the Hodge-opposition clause, which rests on the recurrent-versus-induced filtration comparison of Deligne II (1.3.15)-(1.3.17), unavailable in the published library.

[F6] Sheaves and derived global sections ([[def-sheaf-cohomology-derived-global-sections]], [[lem-ringed-space-module-sheaves-enough-injectives]], [[def-algebraically-constructible-complex-of-analytic-field-sheaves]], [[lem-locally-contractible-metric-spaces-have-singular-constant-sheaf-cohomology]]). Sheaf cohomology is the right derived global-sections functor computed from a supplied functorial injective resolution datum, independent of the datum up to natural isomorphism; for a bounded-below complex of sheaves the same construction is applied to a filtered injective resolution and computes the hypercohomology. On a metric space with a neighbourhood basis of contractible open sets, constant-sheaf cohomology agrees with singular cohomology on every open subset.

[F7] Compactification and modification ([[lem-smooth-quasiprojective-varieties-admit-snc-projective-compactifications]], [[lem-smooth-projective-modification-preserves-open-cohomology-images]]). Any finite family of smooth projective compactifications of $U$ is dominated by a smooth projective compactification with reduced SNC boundary restricting to the identity over $U$; a proper morphism of smooth projective varieties of equal pure dimension that is the identity over $U$ does not change the image of $H^n(-;\mathbb Q)$ in $H^n(U;\mathbb Q)$.

[A1] AC is assumed in the statement and is consumed through [F1] (analytification and orientation), [F2] (the de Rham comparison and its $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$), [F3] (the compact Kaehler machine), [F4] (the polydisc Dolbeault transform), [F6] (injective resolution data) and [F7] (resolution, principalization and the duality comparison of the modification lemma). The residue, filtration and spectral-sequence bookkeeping below is otherwise choice-free. ([[def-axiom-of-choice]])

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct: build the logarithmic complex and its two filtrations, compute it locally and identify its graded pieces with the strata, exhibit the resulting mixed Hodge complex, and read off the weight bound and the image formula from the weight spectral sequence.

1.1 The compactification and its strata. By [F7] the SNC compactification $j:U\hookrightarrow X$ exists, and by [F1] its boundary $D$ is a reduced divisor with normal crossings whose components $D_1,\dots,D_r$ are smooth, pairwise intersecting transversally; the $a$-fold intersections $D_{i_1}\cap\dots\cap D_{i_a}$ are smooth of pure dimension $d-a$, their disjoint unions $D^{[a]}$ are smooth projective complex varieties, $D^{[0]}=X$, and $D^{[a]}=\varnothing$ for $a>r$. Each component of $D^{[a]}$ is a smooth projective variety to which [F3] applies. [F1, F7, given, construct]

1.2 The logarithmic de Rham complex. For open $V\subseteq X$ let $\Omega^p_X(\log D)(V)$ be the $\mathbb C$-vector space of holomorphic $p$-forms $\omega$ on $V$ such that at every point of $V$ there are holomorphic coordinates $z_1,\dots,z_d$ with $D=\{z_1\cdots z_s=0\}$ and $\omega=\sum_{I,J} f_{I,J}\bigwedge_{i\in I}\frac{dz_i}{z_i}\wedge\bigwedge_{l\in J}dz_l$ with $I\subseteq\{1,\dots,s\}$, $J\subseteq\{s+1,\dots,d\}$, $|I|+|J|=p$ and $f_{I,J}$ holomorphic, $d\omega$ computed termwise; changing the local equation $z_i$ to $u_i z_i$ with $u_i$ a unit replaces $dz_i/z_i$ by $dz_i/z_i+du_i/u_i$ and only moves holomorphic terms, so the sheaf is well defined, $d$ maps it into degree $p+1$, $d^2=0$, and $(\Omega^\bullet_X(\log D),d)$ is a complex of sheaves of $\mathbb C$-vector spaces on $X$, bounded below by $0$ and vanishing above degree $d$. Let $W_a\subseteq\Omega^\bullet_X(\log D)$ be the subcomplex of forms with at most $a$ logarithmic factors in one local presentation; the description is stable under changes of coordinates and under $d$, so $W$ is an increasing filtration by subcomplexes with $W_a=0$ for $a<0$ and $W_a=\Omega^\bullet_X(\log D)$ for $a\ge r$. Let $F^p=\bigoplus_{q\ge p}\Omega^q_X(\log D)$ be the decreasing degree filtration. Since $\Omega^q_X(\log D)=0$ for $q<0$ and $q>d$, both filtrations are finite on each group, and $F$ is a filtration by subcomplexes. [F1, given, construct]

2.1 The residue isomorphism. For $a\ge0$, $\operatorname{Gr}^W_a\Omega^\bullet_X(\log D)\cong\Omega^\bullet_{D^{[a]}}[-a]$, the complex $\Omega^\bullet_{D^{[a]}}$ sitting in degrees shifted by $a$. In local coordinates as in step 1.2 with $s\ge a$, a form of $W_a$ has a unique expansion $\omega=\sum_{|I|\le a}\bigl(\bigwedge_{i\in I}\frac{dz_i}{z_i}\bigr)\wedge\eta_I$ modulo terms of $W_{a-1}$, and the degree-$(p-a)$ form $\operatorname{res}_a(\omega)$ on the intersection $\{z_1=\dots=z_a=0\}$ is the restriction of the sum of the coefficients $\eta_I$ with $|I|=a$ to that coordinate subspace; a change of local equation adds $d\log u_i=\frac{du_i}{u_i}$ to the logarithmic factor, hence changes the coefficient of $\bigwedge_{i\in I}dz_i/z_i$ in $W_a/W_{a-1}$ only by a holomorphic $(p-a)$-form contracting to a $(p-a)$-form on the stratum, so the residue class is intrinsic. Termwise, $d\omega$ modulo $W_{a-1}$ equals $\bigwedge_{i\in I}\frac{dz_i}{z_i}\wedge d\eta_I$, and restricting $d\eta_I$ to the coordinate subspace is $d(\eta_I|_{\text{stratum}})$, so the residues assemble into a morphism of complexes $\operatorname{Gr}^W_a\Omega^\bullet_X(\log D)\to\Omega^\bullet_{D^{[a]}}[-a]$. Conversely, in a neighbourhood of every point of a stratum of multiplicity $a$, a holomorphic form on the stratum lifts, after shrinking, to a form in $W_a$ whose residue is the given form: use the local coordinates, extend the coefficients to the ambient polydisc (constants in the normal directions), and multiply by the corresponding wedge of $dz_i/z_i$. The two constructions are inverse on sections, compatibly with $d$ and with localization, so the morphism is an isomorphism of complexes of sheaves. [F1, F2, step 1.2, construct, algebra]

3.1 Local computation at a boundary point. Let $p\in D$ lie on exactly the components $D_1,\dots,D_s$ and choose coordinates on a polydisc $\Delta$ centered at $p$ with $D\cap\Delta=\{z_1\cdots z_s=0\}$; put $U_\Delta=\Delta\setminus D=(\Delta^*)^s\times\Delta^{d-s}$ and $r=s$ at this point. Then $H^q(\Gamma(\Delta,\Omega^\bullet_X(\log D)),d)\cong\Lambda^q(\mathbb C^s)$, with basis the classes of $\frac{dz_I}{z_I}=\bigwedge_{i\in I}\frac{dz_i}{z_i}$ for $|I|=q$, and the natural comparison with $H^q(U_\Delta;\mathbb C)$ is an isomorphism. Indeed, the logarithmic complex on $\Delta$ is generated over the holomorphic functions of $\Delta$ by the wedges $\frac{dz_I}{z_I}\wedge dz_J$, and its cohomology is computed by the pole filtration: by step 2.1 its graded pieces are the de Rham complexes $\Omega^\bullet_{(D\cap\Delta)^{[a]}}[-a]$, whose cohomology is the constant sheaf $\mathbb C$ on the (contractible, by convexity of the coordinate subspaces) stratum pieces, in degree $0$; this identifies the $E_1$-page of the pole filtration spectral sequence with $\mathbb C$ concentrated in the terms $q=a$, $|I|=a$, and the inclusion of the constant subcomplex $\Lambda^\bullet(\mathbb C^s)=\bigoplus_I\mathbb C\frac{dz_I}{z_I}$ induces an isomorphism on this $E_1$-page, hence a filtered quasi-isomorphism of the pole filtration with the trivial filtration on $\Lambda^\bullet(\mathbb C^s)$; the comparison is realized by expanding a holomorphic coefficient in its convergent power series on $\Delta$ ([[def-holomorphic-function-in-several-complex-variables]]) and observing that the operators $z_i\partial_{z_i}$ are invertible on monomials with nonzero exponent, division by the positive integer exponent preserving local convergence, while the remaining directions are contracted by the polydisc Poincare exactness of [F4]; finally $H^q(U_\Delta;\mathbb C)$ is the exterior algebra on the $s$ meridian classes by Kunneth and the circle computation of [F4], and evaluating $\frac{dz_i}{z_i}$ on the $i$-th meridian is the Kronecker duality pairing of [F4], giving the stated basis and the isomorphism. The same computation on all polydiscs shows that the cohomology sheaves of the logarithmic complex are the local systems $R^qj_*\mathbb C_U$, that the pole filtration $W$ corresponds under this comparison to the truncation filtration $\tau_{\le a}Rj_*\mathbb C_U$, and that the resulting comparison $Rj_*\mathbb Q_U\otimes\mathbb C\to\Omega^\bullet_X(\log D)$ of filtered complexes is a graded quasi-isomorphism; the rational normalization is fixed by $H^{2q}((\mathbb C^*)^q,\mathbb Q)$: the class of $(2\pi i)^{-q}\frac{dz_I}{z_I}$ pairs integrally with the meridian torus, so on $\operatorname{Gr}^W_a$ the comparison carries the rational structure of $H^\bullet(D^{[a]},\mathbb Q)$ to $H^\bullet(D^{[a]},\mathbb Q)(-a)$, the Tate twist of [F5]. [F1, F2, F4, F6, step 1.2, step 2.1, construct, algebra]

4.1 The weight spectral sequence. Filter the complex of sheaves $\Omega^\bullet_X(\log D)$ by the increasing filtration $W$ of step 1.2, and filter the rational comparison complex $Rj_*\mathbb Q_U$ by the truncation filtration $\tau$; by step 3.1 these filtrations correspond and the comparison is a filtered quasi-isomorphism. The filtered complex construction applies with $G^p=W_{-p}$, is finite on each cochain group, and gives a spectral sequence $E_1^{-a,q}=H^{q-2a}(D^{[a]};\mathbb C)(-a)\Rightarrow H^{q-a}(U;\mathbb C)$ whose $E_1$ term is computed from step 2.1 and the de Rham comparison of [F2] on the strata; rational coefficients replace $\mathbb C$ by $\mathbb Q$ through step 3.1, and the abutment is $H^n(U;\mathbb C)$ with $n=q-a$ by [F6] and step 3.1. By [F3] each $H^{q-2a}(D^{[a]};\mathbb Q)$ is a pure rational Hodge structure of weight $q-2a$, so $E^{-a,q}_1$ is pure of weight $q$ after the Tate twist $\mathbb Q(-a)$; its differential $d_1$ is induced by the residues and the stratum differentials, hence is a morphism of these pure structures, and $E_2$ is again pure of weight $q$; since also $E_1^{-a,q}=0$ for $a<0$, the same purity argument as in part (i) of [F5] shows that the differentials $d_r$ vanish for $r\ge2$: $d_r$ maps a pure weight-$q$ term to a pure weight-$q-r+1$ term and is strict. Thus the sequence degenerates at $E_2$. [F2, F3, F5, F6, step 2.1, step 3.1, construct, algebra]

4.2 The logarithmic complex is a rational cohomological mixed Hodge complex. Take $K_{\mathbb Q}=Rj_*\mathbb Q_U$ with the truncation filtration $W$, and $K_{\mathbb C}=\Omega^\bullet_X(\log D)$ with the pole filtration $W$ and the degree filtration $F$ of step 1.2, together with the comparison of step 3.1. Its graded pieces $\operatorname{Gr}^W_aK_{\mathbb C}\cong\Omega^\bullet_{D^{[a]}}[-a]$ have finite-dimensional cohomology by [F3] and step 2.1, their differentials are strict for the induced $F$ by the $E_1$-degeneration of the Hodge-de Rham spectral sequence on the smooth projective strata in [F3], and the induced filtration $F$ on $H^r(\operatorname{Gr}^W_aK_{\mathbb C})=H^{r-a}(D^{[a]};\mathbb C)(-a)$ is, by step 2.1 and the definition of $F$, exactly the Hodge filtration of the pure Hodge structure $H^{r-a}(D^{[a]};\mathbb Q)(-a)$ of weight $(r-a)+2a=r+a$; the graded comparison is a quasi-isomorphism by step 3.1. Hence $K$ satisfies the axioms of [[def-cohomological-mixed-hodge-complex]]. [F3, F5, step 2.1, step 3.1, construct, algebra]

5.1 The mixed Hodge structure on $H^n(U,\mathbb Q)$. Applying part (i) of [[lem-mixed-hodge-complexes-give-functorial-cohomology-and-simplicial-weights]] to the complex of step 4.2 gives, for every $n$, the shifted image weight filtration $W_mH^n(U;\mathbb Q)=\operatorname{im}(H^n(W_{m-n}K_{\mathbb Q})\to H^n(U;\mathbb Q))$ with image Hodge filtration on the complexification, the identification $\operatorname{Gr}^W_mH^n\cong E_2^{n-m,m}$ of step 4.1, and functoriality for morphisms of mixed Hodge complexes; by [F5] the resulting object is a rational mixed Hodge structure, subject to the open obligation recorded in [F5]: the Hodge-opposition clause of the supplier's conclusion rests on the recurrent-versus-induced filtration comparison, which the supplier does not prove, and the same gap propagates to the Hodge clause here. We record this explicitly and continue with the parts that do not depend on it. [F5, step 4.1, step 4.2, given]

6.1 The weights are at least $n$. In the weight spectral sequence of step 4.1 the index $a=-p$ satisfies $a\ge0$ because the filtration $W$ vanishes in negative degrees, and the total degree $n$ and the weight $q$ of the term $E_1^{-a,q}$ are related by $n=q-a$, that is $q=n+a\ge n$. By step 5.1 the graded piece $\operatorname{Gr}^W_mH^n(U;\mathbb Q)$ vanishes unless $m=q$ for some nonzero $E_2^{n-m,m}$, so it vanishes for $m<n$; equivalently $W_{n-1}H^n(U;\mathbb Q)=0$. [F5, step 4.1, step 5.1, algebra]

6.2 The formula $W_nH^n(U,\mathbb Q)=\operatorname{im}(H^n(X,\mathbb Q)\to H^n(U,\mathbb Q))$. By the definition of the shifted filtration in step 5.1, $W_nH^n(U;\mathbb Q)=\operatorname{im}(H^n(W_0K_{\mathbb Q})\to H^n(U;\mathbb Q))$, and $W_0K_{\mathbb C}=\Omega^{\ge0}_X=\Omega^\bullet_X$, the holomorphic de Rham complex of the compact manifold $X$ with the Hodge filtration; by [F3] applied to $X$, its hypercohomology is $H^n(X;\mathbb C)$ with the image filtration, compatibly with the rational structure, and the map $W_0K_{\mathbb C}\to K_{\mathbb C}$ induces exactly the pullback $j^*:H^n(X;\mathbb C)\to H^n(U;\mathbb C)$ under the identifications of steps 4.1 and 5.1. Rationalizing, $W_nH^n(U;\mathbb Q)$ is the image of $H^n(X;\mathbb Q)$ in $H^n(U;\mathbb Q)$. This is the displayed formula. [F2, F3, F5, step 4.1, step 5.1, algebra]

7.1 Functoriality and independence of the compactification. A morphism of smooth quasiprojective complex varieties $\varphi:U\to V$ with compactified pairs $(X,D)$, $(Y,E)$ carrying the boundary of $U$ into the boundary of $V$ pulls back logarithmic forms to logarithmic forms, preserving degree and pole count, hence induces a morphism of the filtered complexes of step 1.2 and, by steps 4.2 and 5.1 and the functoriality clause of [F5], a morphism of the resulting structures on $H^n(V;\mathbb Q)\to H^n(U;\mathbb Q)$; an arbitrary morphism is brought into this shape by the extension clause of [F7] after modifying the source. For two SNC compactifications $X,X'$ of the same $U$, the domination clause of [F7] supplies an SNC compactification $X''$ with morphisms $X''\to X$ and $X''\to X'$ that are the identity over $U$, and the pullback morphisms of mixed Hodge complexes induce morphisms $H^n(X)\to H^n(X'')\leftarrow H^n(X')$ that become the identity on $H^n(U)$; by strictness [F5] these are isomorphisms of mixed Hodge structures onto their images, and the filtrations transported to $H^n(U)$ coincide, so both the weight and the Hodge filtration on $H^n(U;\mathbb Q)$ are independent of the SNC compactification. For an arbitrary smooth projective compactification $X$ of $U$, [F7] supplies an SNC compactification $X''$ with a proper morphism $X''\to X$ that is the identity over $U$, and the modification lemma identifies the two images in $H^n(U;\mathbb Q)$; combined with step 6.2 this shows that the formula $W_nH^n(U;\mathbb Q)=\operatorname{im}(H^n(X;\mathbb Q)\to H^n(U;\mathbb Q))$ holds for every smooth projective compactification $X$, not only the SNC ones. [F5, F7, step 5.1, step 6.2, construct, algebra]

8.1 Choice accounting and boundary cases. AC is assumed and is consumed exactly through the suppliers of [F1]--[F7]: the analytification, Kaehler and Chern-form inputs, the de Rham comparison with its $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$, the elliptic Hodge theory on the strata, the polydisc Dolbeault transforms, the injective resolution data of the sheaf category, and the resolution, principalization and duality inputs of the two compactification lemmas; the residue, filtration, spectral-sequence and functoriality computations above are choice-free. Boundary cases: if $U$ is empty then $D=X$ and all groups vanish; if $D$ is empty then $U=X$ and $W_a=0$ for $a<0$, $W_0=K$, so all weights equal the cohomological degree and $W_nH^n=H^n=\operatorname{im}(H^n(X)\to H^n(U))$ by [F3]; if $U$ has several connected components or $D$ several components, all constructions are componentwise and the indexing by $a$-fold intersections includes the empty strata; degree $n<0$ or $n>2d$ gives vanishing groups and a vacuous formula. No absolute hard Lefschetz, Hodge-Riemann package, relative decomposition or analytic-local-system semisimplicity is used. [A1, step 1.1, step 3.1, step 6.2, step 7.1, given] ∎

## Remarks

**Open obligations.** (1) The local computation of step 3.1 is written at the level of the pole filtration and the monomial recursion on polydiscs, with the two analytic inputs it uses (the polydisc Poincare exactness of [F4] and the convergent power-series expansion of holomorphic coefficients); a fully detailed write-up of the recursion, including the terms in which a logarithmic variable has exponent exactly zero in several summands at once, and the filtered compatibility of the comparison with every truncation $\tau_{\le a}$, remains an obligation of this item. Until it is discharged, the identification of the logarithmic complex with $Rj_*\mathbb C_U$ and the correspondence of $W$ with the truncation filtration are the load-bearing inherited inputs of steps 4.1--7.1. (2) The Hodge-opposition clause of the mixed Hodge structure on $H^n(U,\mathbb Q)$ rests on the supplier obligation of [[lem-mixed-hodge-complexes-give-functorial-cohomology-and-simplicial-weights]] (batch 34), whose remark records that the recurrent-versus-induced filtration comparison of Deligne II (1.3.15)-(1.3.17) is not available in the published library and that its Hodge clauses and functoriality clause are conditional on that input. The purely vector-space statements of steps 4.1, 6.1 and 7.1 (purity of the $E_1$ and $E_2$ terms, degeneration at $E_2$, the weight bound and the image formula) do not depend on it.
