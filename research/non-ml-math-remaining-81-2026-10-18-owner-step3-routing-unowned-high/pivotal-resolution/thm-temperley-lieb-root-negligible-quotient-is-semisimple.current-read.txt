## Statement

Let $q=e^{\pi i/\ell}$, $\ell\ge3$, $\delta=q+q^{-1}$ and $\tau_n=\delta^{-n}\operatorname{Tr}_n$. Put

$$\operatorname{Rad}(\tau_n)=\{x\in\mathrm{TL}_n:\tau_n(xy)=0\text{ for every }y\in\mathrm{TL}_n\}.$$

Let $I_n$ be the two-sided ideal generated by $p_{\ell-1}\otimes1_{n-\ell+1}$ for $n\ge\ell-1$, and $I_n=0$ below that rank. Then $I_n=\operatorname{Rad}(\tau_n)$, and $\mathrm{TL}_n/I_n$ is the direct sum of full matrix algebras with multiplicities given by length-$n$ paths from $0$ of heights $0,\ldots,\ell-2$. The additive idempotent-complete negligible quotient has simple labels $0,\ldots,\ell-2$, represented by $p_a$, and

$$a\otimes b\cong\bigoplus_{\substack{|a-b|\le c\le\min(a+b,2\ell-4-a-b)\\c\equiv a+b\ (2)}}c.$$

At $n=\ell-1$ the unreduced algebra is semisimple, while its trace radical is the nonzero one-dimensional block generated by $p_{\ell-1}$. Trace radical and Jacobson radical are therefore distinct at this first boundary.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The positive root parameter, unnormalized closure and the fixed embedded boundary ideals.

[F1] [[lem-temperley-lieb-root-fusion-paths-have-nonzero-pairings]] proves the complete retained-path decompositions and matrix units, with diagonal trace weights $[a+1]$.

[F2] [[lem-temperley-lieb-boundary-tensor-ideal-has-the-embedded-components]] proves that the boundary tensor ideal has exactly these endomorphism components, including zero below the boundary.

[F3] [[lem-temperley-lieb-jones-wenzl-partial-trace-and-uniqueness]] gives the projector corner, annihilation and trace formulas. [[lem-temperley-lieb-closure-trace-component-identities]] gives the closure trace rules.

[F4] [[lem-negligible-morphisms-form-a-tensor-ideal-by-partial-trace]] proves negligibility is a tensor ideal. [[def-temperley-lieb-quantum-integers-and-quantum-characteristic]] gives $[a+1]>0$ on retained labels and $[\ell]=0$.

[F5] [[lem-temperley-lieb-general-theta-net-evaluation]] gives the unique bundled trivalent matching, its theta pairing, exact generic/unit domain, and positive/zero root level alternatives.

## Proof

1.1 Write $m=\ell-1$ and $p=p_m$. It is a nonzero idempotent with one-dimensional corner and trace $[\ell]=0$ by [F3,F4]. For every $x$, cyclic closure gives $\operatorname{Tr}(px)=\operatorname{Tr}(pxp)$; the latter is a scalar multiple of $\operatorname{Tr}(p)=0$. Thus $p$ is negligible. By [F4], the categorical tensor ideal it generates is negligible, and [F2] identifies its endomorphism component as exactly $I_n$. Consequently $I_n\subseteq\operatorname{Rad}(\operatorname{Tr}_n)$ at every rank. The scalar $\delta^{-n}$ exists and does not change this radical. [F2, F3, F4, given, algebra]

2.1 In the quotient by $I_n$, [F1] gives matrix units $E_{a,b}$ for paths ending at each retained $c$, with trace $\operatorname{Tr}(E_{a,b})=\delta_{a,b}[c+1]$. If an element has a nonzero coefficient at $E_{a,b}$, multiplying by $E_{b,a}$ detects it in this nonzero weighted matrix-trace pairing. The quotient trace pairing is therefore nondegenerate. Together with step 1.1 this proves $I_n=\operatorname{Rad}(\tau_n)$ and the full matrix-block description. Inserting the path decompositions on both ends describes every unequal-degree Hom space as the corresponding rectangular matrix blocks; its pairing with the reverse Hom space is also nondegenerate. Thus the categorical negligible ideal is precisely the boundary tensor ideal, not a larger ideal known only on endomorphisms. The endpoint corners are $\mathbb Cp_c$, so its additive Karoubi quotient is semisimple with exactly the stated simple labels. [F1, F2, F3, F4, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Sandwich a basis matching from $c$ to $a+b$ between $p_c$ and $p_a\otimes p_b$. Any arc joining two points of the same labelled arm is killed by its innermost adjacent turnback. A remaining matching must have the three bundle sizes $(a+b-c)/2$, $(b+c-a)/2$, $(c+a-b)/2$, so it exists only for a triangle/parity triple and is unique. Thus this projected Hom space is spanned by the vertex of [F5], and its reverse by the reflected vertex. Their trace pairing is the exact theta scalar. When the labels are retained, [F5] makes it positive exactly for $a+b+c\le2\ell-4$ and zero above that level. Step 2.1 identifies quotient Hom with this pairing modulo its radical, so its dimension is respectively one or zero. In a semisimple category these are precisely the simple multiplicities in $p_a\otimes p_b$. The triangle, parity and level inequalities yield the displayed truncated rule. No theta value is divided at a forbidden level. [F1, F3, F5, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 Finally work at rank $m=\ell-1$ **before** quotienting. The same up/down path proof as [F1] applies without deleting its final up endpoint $p_m$: a length-$m$ path cannot exceed height $m$, and every split before its final step uses only $[1],\ldots,[m]$, which are nonzero. All endpoint projectors up to $p_m$ are nonzero by their identity coefficient. The matching-turnback corner calculation gives full matrix units just as before, now including the endpoint $m$; their completeness is the unreduced JW split. Hence $\mathrm{TL}_m$ is semisimple. The endpoint-$m$ block has the unique all-up path and is $\mathbb Cp_m$. Its trace weight is $[m+1]=0$, whereas every other endpoint weight is positive. Thus precisely this one-dimensional block is the trace radical, while semisimplicity makes the Jacobson radical zero. This proves the boundary assertion directly, without assuming a later ordinary semisimplicity criterion. [F1, F3, F4, step 2.1, algebra] ∎
