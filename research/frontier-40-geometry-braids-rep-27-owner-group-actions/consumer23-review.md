## Final fibred-category supplier correction

omitting their cartesian universal property. A base category with one arrow
0->1, one object over each endpoint and two distinct arrows upstairs over that
arrow satisfies those weakened conditions but is not fibred. The corrected
`def-category-fibred-in-groupoids` requires cartesian lifts explicitly and gives
their full universal property. Vistoli3.5/3.21/3.22/3.23 full definitions/proof
