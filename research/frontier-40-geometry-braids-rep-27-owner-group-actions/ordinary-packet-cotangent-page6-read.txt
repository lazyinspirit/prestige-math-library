5. Constructing a resolution
08PV
In the Noetherian finite type case we can construct a “small” simplicial resolution
for finite type ring maps.
Lemma 5.1.
08PW
Let A be a Noetherian ring. Let A →B be a finite type ring map.
Let A be the category of A-algebra maps C →B.
Let n ≥0 and let P• be a
simplicial object of A such that
(1) P• →B is a trivial Kan fibration of simplicial sets,
(2) Pk is finite type over A for k ≤n,
(3) P• = cosknsknP• as simplicial objects of A.
Then Pn+1 is a finite type A-algebra.

the ring map P0 →B is surjective, this is of finite type over A by More on Algebra,
