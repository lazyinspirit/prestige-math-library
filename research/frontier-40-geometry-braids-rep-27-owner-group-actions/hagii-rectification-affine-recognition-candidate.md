## 3. Explicit truncations and bounded derived sections

## 4. Justified Postnikov reconstruction for these QC ring sheaves

Put F=holim_n O_n. Derived Γ preserves this limit. On each principal affine U⊂X₀ we have RΓ(U,F)=holim_nA_n(U). 0→lim¹π_{i+1}A_n(U)→π_i holim_nA_n(U)→lim_nπ_iA_n(U)→0.

Here each homotopy-group tower is **eventually constant with isomorphic transition maps** by Section3. Its lim¹ vanishes: the difference map1−shift on the product is surjective; choose a value at a sufficiently large stable stage,solve recursively forward using those isomorphisms,and solve the finitely many earlier coordinates backward. Hence

π_i RΓ(U,F)=Γ(U,π_iO)

for every i≥0. The projection F→O_n induces the canonical iso on π_i homotopy sheaves for n≥i: on the principal affine basis the preceding formulas identify both sides and the projection with the identity.
