## 1. Represented free cells and stalk behavior

Write h_U for the represented open presheaf. For simplicial A-modules, let F_A(h_U×K) be the free A-module on that simplicial presheaf; for unital A-algebras, let it be the free polynomial A-algebra. Nonunital algebras use the free nonunital commutative A-algebra. At V⊂X in degree n the generators are h_U(V)×K_n; h_U(V) is one element if V⊂U and empty otherwise. The usual operators act on coefficients and generator labels. There is the exact enriched free/evaluation adjunction

Hom(F_A(h_U×K),M)=Hom_sSet(K,underlying M(U)).

The affine variable-base action on cotensors is constant precomposition, just as in the accepted packet; restrictions respect it. Tensor/cotensor/enrichment in presheaves are pointwise with those actions.

Generate Cof by I={F_A(h_U×∂Δ[n])->F_A(h_U×Δ[n]): U∈C,n≥0}. Here the initial object is **A for unital A-algebras**, zero for modules/nonunital algebras. It is not the zero ring in the unital source-cofibration statements.

Every stalk of an I-cell is an affine cell (if x∈U) or an identity(if x∉U). Polynomial/free module constructions commute with stalks because their expressions and relations are finite. Stalks also preserve coproducts,pushouts and transfinite colimits. Consequently every Cof map is stalkwise an affine cofibration, by the cell/retract description. In particular it is stalkwise injective; at the presheaf level it is also sectionwise injective since evaluation of the same cells is affine. The Cof class here is **projective represented free cells**, not all monomorphisms.

Presheaf generating domains are finitely presentable relative to A: the displayed adjunction reduces Hom to a finite simplicial-set map into sections at U. Thus an ω-small-object construction gives the I-factorization, with I-inj exactly sectionwise boundary lifting. Sectionwise boundary lifting is an affine trivial fibration, hence a sectionwise quasi-isomorphism. Filtered stalk colimits preserve its normalized homology, so I-inj is a local weak equivalence. The free relative I-factorization is strictly natural under coefficient squares,by carrying every indexed represented lifting square to its coefficient square; it commutes with filtered colimits of presheaf arrows because its represented finite boundary data do. These are actual finitary presheaf properties. They must not be attributed to all sheaf factorizations.

## 2. Explicit local recognition for presheaves

Each of the three presheaf categories has all limits and colimits; these are degreewise the ordinary algebraic/module ones. Each object is small for sufficiently large regular filtered colimits: its total set of section elements and the simplicial/restriction/operation equations are a set, and a compatible map out of it stabilizes after a cardinal larger than that data. This substitutes the only smallness role of local presentability in the recognition argument.

Let W be stalk normalized quasi-isomorphisms. It has retract and two-out-of-three closure. I-inj⊂W was proved in Section1. A pushout of a Cof map in W remains in W: at each stalk it is a pushout of an affine acyclic cofibration, which is weak by the affine model axioms/left properness. Transfinite composites of such maps remain weak because filtered module colimits are exact and normalization commutes with them. No general SSet left-properness theorem enters.

Here is the required solution set at I. Fix an I-square into a local weak map f:F->G. Choose a cardinal bound above the site,its point set,the total coefficient presheaf A,and the generating-square data. Begin bounded A-subalgebras(or submodules)F0⊂F,G0⊂G containing that data and closed under f,restrictions,simplicial operators and finite operations. Closure remains bounded: every polynomial/linear expression has finitely many arguments and coefficients.

At each stage,for every x,n and normalized cycle c in the current G-stalk,choose a normalized cycle a in the full F-stalk and a primitive b in the full G-stalk with f(a)-c=∂b; they exist by surjectivity of H_n(f_x). Adjoin their finitely many representing section simplices on a common neighborhood; shrink so their normalized equations hold. For every current normalized F-cycle a whose image is a boundary in the current G-stalk, choose a primitive of a in the full F-stalk by injectivity of H_n(f_x), and adjoin its finite section data. Include n=0 in both steps. There are only boundedly many such finite data over the set of points and dimensions. Close again under all algebraic/module/restriction operations and f; repeat countably. Use a larger bound closed under the fixed finite operations if necessary. Any normalized cycle or primitive in the resulting stalk union is represented at one finite stage, since the normalization and boundary equations are finite. The next stage supplied the required witness. Hence the union subarrow f∞ is weak at every stalk. Up to isomorphism its data are bounded and form a set. The original I-square factors through it. This proves the solution-set condition without Ex∞,homotopy-group accessibility,or general KS.

For clarity,the rest of Smith recognition is actual proof. For every I-square into one of those bounded weak subarrows,push out its generating map,then I-factor the resulting corner as an I-cell followed by I-inj. The composite from the chosen weak map's source to the intermediate object is Cof and W by two-out-of-three. Let J be the set of all these composites. Every I-square into a W-map factors through J. Given a W-map,successively attach these interpolating J-maps for all I-squares into its remaining map. The initial composite stays Cof∩W by the just-proved pushout/transfinite closure,so the remaining map stays W. At a large regular smallness ordinal the remaining map is I-inj. A Cof∩W-map is therefore a retract of a J-cell map. Conversely every J-cell is Cof∩W. Thus cof(J)=Cof∩W. Small-object constructions for I and J give both factorizations; lifting,retract and two-out-of-three arguments give all Quillen model axioms. J's domains may need a large regular ordinal; an ω-factorization for them is not asserted.

The same proof with sectionwise normalized quasi-isomorphisms instead of stalkwise ones gives the **objectwise projective algebra/module model**,with the same Cof class and sectionwise affine fibrations. For its solution set the cycle/primitive witnesses are chosen over the set of site objects rather than stalk points. All its objects are fibrant because their additive section groups are Kan. All objects of the local model are **not** necessarily fibrant.

## 3. Literal simplicial corner and sheaf models,including smallness

The represented free generators satisfy the corner argument without arbitrary SSet model theory. Their tensor pushout product with a boundary monomorphism is the free A-object of the corresponding product monomorphism,over the same represented open; its boundary-cell decomposition gives Cof. Its product with a horn is the free A-object of the product-horn attachment from the accepted finite matching proof; at every stalk it is an affine acyclic cofibration,so it is Cof∩W. Extend this by colimits and retracts to all Cof maps. If j is already Cof∩W,then j□boundary is stalkwise an affine acyclic cofibration by the affine corner axiom,so again Cof∩W. Stalks commute with tensors and the relevant pushouts.

Transposing a horn mapping-corner problem against j to the corresponding tensor corner now gives a lift against every local fibration. A boundary mapping-corner problem has a lift when j or the other map is acyclic. Therefore the enriched mapping corners have actual horn lifting and actual boundary lifting in the acyclic case. The latter implies a simplicial homotopy equivalence by the already proved section/prism argument. Replacement-invariant Kan mapping spaces and enriched derived adjunctions follow from the accepted cofib/fib homotopy argument. This proof does not assume a generic weak-equivalence theorem for arbitrary simplicial presheaves. Cotensors retain the constant coefficient action; slices/augmentations use the prescribed relative cotensor.

For sheaves use degreewise sheafification of these free generators, I_sh=aI, and Cof_sh=cof(I_sh). Sheaf stalks of cells are the same affine cells. Limits are the ordinary sheaf limits; colimits are sheafification of presheaf colimits. Stalk weak maps have exactly the same retract,pushout and transfinite properties. The cardinal audit is important:

Choose an infinite ρ above the site/covering-arrow/point cardinalities and the coefficient sheaf's total section data. Put θ=2^ρ,so θ^ρ=θ, and choose a regular λ>θ. Sheafifying a presheaf with at most θ total section elements produces at most θ total elements: each matching family uses at most ρ entries and a cover from the fixed site,so the plus constructions have at most θ^ρ=θ choices. The bounded presheaf cycle-witness construction above therefore sheafifies to bounded A-subsheaves of F,G; repeat it and sheafify the union. The stalk argument proves the resulting bounded subarrow weak. These provide a sheaf solution set.

For smallness, λ-filtered colimits of sheaves are computed sectionwise: each covering equalizer uses fewer than λ entries/equations,and λ-filtered colimits of sets commute with such limits by choosing a common stage for their fewer-than-λ witnesses. Consequently Hom from a sheafified represented finite generator commutes with these colimits by the free/evaluation adjunction. More general bounded J-domains are also small after taking λ larger than their total section/equation data. Thus the necessary small-object factorizations may be **λ-long rather than ω-long**. Their existence is proved; arbitrary finite/ω presentability for sheaf generators is not claimed. Apply the same explicit Smith argument,now with I_sh and the sheaf solution set,to obtain the local sheaf ring/module/nonunital models. Cof_sh is not asserted to be all sheaf monomorphisms.

Sheafification a and inclusion i are enriched adjoint. a takes I to I_sh,takes cell/retract Cof maps to Cof_sh,and preserves W because stalks are unchanged. Thus it is left Quillen. The unit M->iaM is stalkwise an isomorphism in every degree,the counit aiN=N is identity,and inclusion detects W. For a cofibrant presheaf P and fibrant sheaf N,a map aP->N is weak iff its adjoint P->iN is weak,by two-out-of-three with that unit. This proves the Quillen equivalence and its actual enriched derived mapping comparison.

For modules the local model is also monoidal. F_A(h_U×K)⊗_A F_A(h_V×L)=F_A(h_{U∩V}×(K×L)); the same generator product argument gives the module pushout-product axiom. The unit A is cofibrant using the represented open X. Tensor is colimit-preserving; retract/cell induction extends the corner tests. A cofibrant module is stalkwise affine cofibrant and tensor-flat,so tensoring it preserves local weak maps. These assertions survive sheafification and use the intersection-closed site hypothesis explicitly.

## 4. Hypercover fibrancy for the algebraic local models

For an object F,the enriched free/evaluation identity gives

Map(F_A(h_W),F)=underlying F(W).

Let U•->h_W be a **split** hypercover by coproducts of represented opens. Its underlying augmented stalk is boundary-trivial by the preceding hypercover proof,with an actual simplicial contraction on every nonempty fiber. Applying the free polynomial/module/nonunital functor preserves that homotopy: at an n-simplex and homotopy parameter,send each generator to the specified homotopy image and keep A_n fixed. Polynomial evaluation or linear extension gives the compatible maps. Therefore

F_A(U)->F_A(h_W)

is stalkwise a homotopy equivalence and hence W,by the additive prism argument. Both sources are Cof objects: the split represented nondegenerate summands attach successively in dimension along the represented free boundaries. This also proves their Reedy-free realization comparison. No claim that free commutative algebra preserves every arbitrary SSet weak map is needed.

If F is locally fibrant,mapping this W between Cof objects into F gives an equivalence. By tensor/coend adjunction this is exactly

F(W) ≃ Tot([n]↦∏_α F(U_{n,α})).

The cosimplicial mapping object is Reedy fibrant: its matching maps are the enriched maps from the free latching inclusion into the fibrant target,and have horn lifting by Section3. Tot here is the literal tensor/coend end computing derived maps from the Cof free hypercover realization into the fibrant target;this is the homotopy-limit convention in this explicit mapping enhancement. Its comparison with an independently specified infinity-categorical space-valued limit belongs to the external enhancement comparison kept open below. No general arbitrary-simplicial-set model is used in defining or checking this end. The dense bounded split hypercover set suffices; the matching/cardinal construction was proved in the previous candidate and uses only finite stalk boundary lifting.

Conversely suppose F satisfies this descent for that dense split set. Factor F->terminal in the local algebraic model as a local acyclic cofibration F->HF followed by a local fibration. HF has descent by the first argument. Factor F->HF in the **objectwise projective algebraic model** as an objectwise acyclic cofibration F->Z followed by an objectwise fibration p:Z->HF. All objects of that objectwise model are fibrant. Its derived mapping invariance with the free Cof hypercover objects proves Z has descent whenever F does. Also p is locally weak by two-out-of-three.

At every stalk p is a horn fibration (objectwise horn lifts pass through finite stalk data) and W,so it is locally boundary-trivial by the additive E1/E2 criterion. Any represented boundary square for p acquires an actual compatible lift after a split hypercover refinement,by the finite matching construction of the previous candidate. Refine further into the fixed dense set. Form the cotensor corner p^{∂Δ[n]}. Its source and target are objectwise fibrant and have descent: finite cotensors preserve the normalized weak comparisons by the affine mapping argument,and the displayed pullback is a homotopy pullback since p is objectwise a fibration. Pullback comparisons of the relevant additive Kan mapping spaces are weak by the proved additive right-properness argument. Thus mapping h_W and this refining hypercover into the cotensor corner gives horizontal equivalences and vertical Kan fibrations. The lift on the hypercover shows the prescribed lower-left vertex's component lifts on the left; lifting edges through that fibration gives a lift of the specified vertex itself. This fills every represented boundary square,so p is objectwise boundary-trivial.

Consequently F->HF is an objectwise weak Cof map. All objectwise algebraic objects are fibrant,so lifting against that objectwise acyclic Cof map gives a retraction HF->F. F is therefore a retract of the locally fibrant HF and is locally fibrant. This proves the hypercover characterization **inside the actual algebraic local models**,without an imported general KS/Jardine model.

For modules and free-ring mapping objects here,the underlying displayed mapping diagrams are additive simplicial groups(the image of the free variable determines the map); their weak comparisons are normalized homology comparisons and the earlier additive corner/right-properness proofs apply. Ordinary sheafification alone does not give this fibrancy. A sectionwise Kan algebra/module without hypercover descent is not locally fibrant.

## 5. Actual coefficient,square-zero and space pullback transfers

For a strict coefficient morphism A->B of simplicial ring presheaves/sheaves,extension of algebra/module scalars is left adjoint to restriction. It takes represented free A-cells to represented free B-cells,hence preserves Cof. For a Cof∩W-map,its stalk is affine Cof∩W and the affine extension is left Quillen,so its extended stalk is acyclic. Thus extension preserves Cof∩W. This proves the actual **local** Quillen adjunction; preserving the underlying section map alone was insufficient and is not used as a global-fibration argument. The strict adjunction is enriched through constant-precomposition cotensors. Weak coefficient maps induce the same Quillen equivalences as the affine units: Cof objects are stalkwise affine tensor-flat,so their extension units are weak; restriction detects W. Module extension is treated identically.

Let NU_B be nonunital B-algebras and Aug_B augmented B-algebras with a specified section. Unitalization I↦B⊕I and augmentation kernel are an actual enriched equivalence of presheaf/sheaf categories. They preserve W by the split additive decomposition. They identify free nonunital represented generators with free augmented represented generators,hence identify Cof,Cof∩W and Fib exactly. For the slice generating cell with a nonzero assigned augmentation,translate each variable by its assigned simplex in B;this is a simplicial B-algebra isomorphism to the zero-augmentation free cell,compatible with its boundary. Thus zero-augmentation generators generate the entire slice Cof class,not just a selected subclass. The augmented model is the corresponding slice of the constructed B-algebra model; Cof is the same represented free-cell class with assigned augmentation. This proves its local model compatibility.

Indecomposables Q:NU_B->Mod_B takes free nonunital represented cells to free module cells,so preserves Cof. Acyclic Cof maps remain acyclic after Q at every stalk because the affine(Q,Z) adjunction is Quillen and stalks commute with the cokernel defining Q. Hence Q is locally left Quillen and zero multiplication Z is locally right Quillen. In particular if M is **locally fibrant** as a module,then B⊕ZM->B is locally fibrant in the augmented model. This is the correct square-zero target; sectionwise Kan fibrancy of M would not have established it.

Extension B⊗_A(-):A-alg/B->Aug_B is likewise locally left Quillen: it preserves the represented free Cof class and is acyclic at each stalk on acyclic Cof maps by the affine adjunction. Consequently all three global cotangent adjunctions now have actual local model and enriched derived interpretations. They are not conditional on a generic SSet model transfer.

For a continuous map f:X->Y,ordinary inverse image of sheaves preserves stalk weak maps since (f^-1N)_x=N_{f(x)}. It takes a represented-open free generator on V⊂Y to the corresponding free generator on f^-1V⊂X; finite free algebra/module operations commute with the inverse image,as do their simplicial structures. It therefore preserves Cof and Cof∩W,so is left Quillen between these sheaf models. Compose it with derived coefficient extension along f^-1O_Y->O_X to obtain the actual local derived sheaf-module pullback. The canonical inverse-image/tensor associativity maps give their ordinary composition coherence.
