# Strict simplicial cotangent adjunctions

## Statement

Let A->B be a morphism of simplicial commutative unital rings ([[def-simplicial-object-and-simplicial-commutative-ring]]). Write sAlg_A/B for simplicial A-algebras C with augmentation C->B, sAugAlg_B for diagrams B->D->B with composite identity, sNUAlg_B for simplicial B-modules with associative commutative B-bilinear multiplication without a required unit, and sMod_B for simplicial B-modules. There are adjunctions (i) C |-> B tensor_A C from sAlg_A/B to sAugAlg_B, left adjoint to restriction of scalars; (ii) K(I)=B direct_sum I, with (b,x)(c,y)=(bc,by+cx+xy), from sNUAlg_B to sAugAlg_B, left adjoint to I(D)=ker(D->B); (iii) Q(I)=I/(submodule generated by xy) from sNUAlg_B to sMod_B, left adjoint to Z(M), the zero-multiplication nonunital algebra. Adjunction (ii) is an equivalence of ordinary categories, with canonical natural isomorphism K I(D)->D, (b,x)|->s(b)+x, and I K(I)=I. It preserves and reflects weak equivalences defined by homology of the associated underlying simplicial abelian groups. If model structures with fibrations and weak equivalences created in underlying simplicial sets have been separately proved on these categories, then all three adjunctions are Quillen adjunctions and (ii) is a Quillen equivalence. This conditional assertion does not assert that those model structures exist. No AC is needed for these strict constructions.

## Proof

Proof. All constructions are degreewise and their face/degeneracy maps are induced by the corresponding maps of A,B,C,I,M. Those maps preserve addition, action and multiplication, so the constructions are simplicial. For (i), an augmented B-algebra map B tensor_A C->D is uniquely of the form b tensor c |-> b f(c) for an augmented A-algebra map f:C->D. The balanced tensor relation is respected because f is A-linear; multiplicativity, unit and augmentation compatibility hold exactly when they do for f. These formulas are inverse natural bijections on hom sets. For (ii), ker(D->B) is a B-module through the specified section s:B->D and an ideal closed under multiplication. Every d has the unique expression s(p(d))+(d-s(p(d))); its second summand lies in the kernel. This proves that (b,x)|->s(b)+x is a bijective B-module map in each degree, compatible with simplicial operators. Multiplying the two expressions gives bc in the base and by+cx+xy in the kernel, proving the ring formula and associativity, commutativity and unit (1,0). A nonunital B-algebra map I->ker(D->B) extends uniquely by that formula to an augmented B-algebra map K(I)->D; restriction to the kernel is inverse. The displayed natural isomorphisms prove an actual categorical equivalence, stronger than a homotopy-category assertion. For a morphism of augmented B-algebras, the associated underlying abelian complexes split naturally as s(B) direct_sum s(I). Its map is identity on s(B) and the induced map on s(I). The same holds on homology, proving preservation and reflection of weak equivalences. For (iii), the B-submodule generated by products is a simplicial submodule because every simplicial operator preserves products and action. A B-linear map I->M is multiplicative into Z(M) exactly when it kills every product, exactly when it factors uniquely through Q(I). This supplies the inverse natural hom-set bijections. Finally, under the stated separately established model structures, the right adjoints in (i) and (iii) preserve fibrations and trivial fibrations because they leave the underlying simplicial-set map unchanged. For (ii), the underlying simplicial set of K(I) is B times I. Product with identity on B preserves every prescribed lifting property, by projecting a lifting square to I. Conversely lifting problems for I are obtained by using the zero simplex in B. Thus I preserves and reflects the underlying fibration class; weak equivalences are preserved/reflected by the direct-sum argument. The strict equivalence is therefore a Quillen equivalence if those model structures exist. These are strict algebraic adjunctions and a conditional model assertion; the proof imports no model-category existence, derived mapping-space or descent theorem. Each category is locally small: morphisms are compatible degreewise functions between fixed sets. Thus the proved natural hom-set bijections give adjunction data by [[thm-equivalent-encodings-of-an-adjunction]]. The conditional Quillen terminology is the precise lifting definition in [[def-model-category-and-quillen-adjunction]].
