## Local interfaces and proof contents

* `lem-etale-specialization-trait-through-a-specialization`: any selected underlying-point specialization on a locally Noetherian scheme is represented by a DVR; completion preserves both point images. The proof gives the valuation blowup chart, a complete one-dimensional normalization argument including the finite-module length bound and its extension to arbitrary submodules, and the DVR completion argument. It constructs an algebraically closed residue field by successive monic lifts of algebraic residue extensions, keeping the same uniformizer at successor and union stages, then completing. This avoids assuming excellence or finite normalization of a nonnormal Noetherian local domain.
* `lem-etale-specialization-proper-geometric-finite-etale-invariance`: algebraically closed field extension preserves finite étale covers for the exact smooth proper hypotheses needed by the consumer. Full faithfulness is proved independently for finite type schemes via integral-component invariance, an elementary coefficient-specialization proof, incidence graphs, and internal Hom descent. Essential surjectivity spreads finite algebra/gluing/separability data, chooses a smooth rational coefficient point, constructs explicitly algebraically independent lacunary power series, proves injectivity of the étale-chart arc into the complete DVR with residue **exactly the original algebraically closed field**, applies existing smooth proper complete-DVR lifting, and reflects a common-overfield isomorphism by full faithfulness. The proof imports neither general henselian approximation nor a homotopy/product theorem. It also proves finite purely inseparable field invariance for arbitrary schemes using the nilpotent self-fibre-product diagonal and fpqc descent, supplying removal of purely inseparable generic coefficients.
* `lem-etale-specialization-geometric-basepoint-interface`: on a chosen complete DVR with algebraically closed residue, inverse special restriction followed by generic pullback is the cover functor. A fibre-functor path yields the explicit generic-to-special homomorphism; the proof supplies path existence by finite Galois trivialization and compactness, continuity, path conjugacy, field transport and compatibility with further complete DVR extensions having the same residue field.

All items retain AC explicitly and list its uses. The field item uses the exact existing local A911 complete-DVR lifting and descent suppliers; other listed dependencies are published. These are local proofs and mechanical checks, not an independent mathematical audit. The consumer and its aggregate reduction remain the active author's integration responsibility. The trait item does not assert that arbitrary independently specified strict-henselization specialization data are identical; compatible geometric fields and paths must be selected and transported by the integrating reduction.
