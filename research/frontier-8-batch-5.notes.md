## 1. Dependency-closed scope decision

The published `uniform-spaces` page already supplies the general filter-based
completion construction, its extension and uniqueness theorem, total
boundedness, compactness versus completeness plus total boundedness, compatible
compact-Hausdorff uniformities, controlled pseudometrics, and the exact DC/UFL
choice seams. Repeating that theory here would create a second completion spine.
The new A page therefore has one narrow task: construct and characterize the
Samuel precompact reflection using the existing completion spine.

The A-page spine is:

1. define the **Samuel uniformity** by the gauge
   `p_f(x,y)=|f(x)-f(y)|` over uniformly continuous `f:X->[0,1]`;
2. prove that it is coarser than the original uniformity and is totally bounded
   in ZF;
3. under dependent choice, prove that it induces the original topology by the
   already-published controlled-pseudometric construction;
4. define the **Samuel completion** as the Hausdorff completion of this weak
   uniformity, reserving **Samuel compactification** for the cases in which the
   canonical map is a genuine compactification;
5. prove that total boundedness passes to a dense uniformly continuous image,
   so the Samuel completion is compact under the ultrafilter lemma and is a
   compactification of a separated uniform space under DC;
6. prove the compact-Hausdorff target extension property locally: a compact
   Hausdorff target has its unique compatible uniformity, and under DC that
   uniformity is generated by its continuous unit-interval coordinates;
7. prove uniqueness, agreement with ordinary uniform completion for totally
   bounded spaces, and the canonical continuous surjection from Stone–Čech to
   Samuel; and
8. place the example and counterexample material on the companion leaf page.

The full Samuel universal property is admitted only in the precise
choice-qualified form that the local proof closes: DC is used to recover the
topology from continuous pseudometrics, and the ultrafilter lemma is used to
turn the complete totally bounded Samuel completion into a compact space. The
page must not silently state the ZFC textbook theorem without those seams.

## 2. Convention and proof lock

1. **Bounded functions.** Sources commonly use all bounded real-valued
   uniformly continuous functions. This scaffold uses `[0,1]`-valued functions.
   Authoring must include the affine-rescaling argument showing that a bounded
   real-valued function contributes no additional weak uniformity. A constant
   function and a zero-length range are harmless.
2. **Precompact means totally bounded.** External sources use both words. The
   library term is `totally bounded`, exactly as in
   `def-totally-bounded-uniform-space`.
3. **Direction of coarsening.** The Samuel uniformity is coarser than the given
   uniformity. Equivalently, the identity map from the original uniform space
   to its Samuel reflection is uniformly continuous. Do not reverse this.
4. **Topology recovery costs DC.** The proof must use a normal sequence with
   `E_1 subset U`, the controlled pseudometric inclusion
   `{p<=1/4} subset E_1`, and `f(y)=min(1,4p(x,y))`. It must not cite complete
   regularity as an unqualified ZF result when the local route uses DC.
5. **Completion versus compactification.** The completion exists for every
   uniform space and may identify points. Under DC, a separated original
   uniformity has the same topology as its separated Samuel uniformity, so the
   completion map is a topological embedding. Compactness additionally uses the
   ultrafilter lemma.
6. **Total boundedness proof.** For a basic finite-coordinate Samuel entourage,
   form finitely many coordinate cells and select representatives only after
   discarding empty cells. `lem-finite-choice` is sufficient; there is no
   arbitrary choice over `X`. Split the empty coordinate family and `X=empty`.
7. **Compact-target extension.** A continuous coordinate `h:K->[0,1]` is
   uniformly continuous on compact Hausdorff `K`; hence `h compose f` is one of
   the Samuel coordinates. The gauge of all such `h` induces the target
   topology and therefore, by uniqueness, is the target's compatible
   uniformity. This is the load-bearing factorization step.
8. **Totally bounded equality.** Given `U`, the controlled pseudometric must
   satisfy `{p<=1/4} subset U`. A finite `p`-net and the finitely many functions
   `y mapsto p(z,y)` then produce a Samuel entourage contained in `U`. This is
   the only planned theorem identifying the original and Samuel uniformities.
9. **Stone–Čech comparison.** The map is from `beta X` onto `S(X)`, not in the
   opposite direction. Surjectivity comes from compact image, Hausdorff
   closedness, and density. No injectivity is asserted.
10. **Non-Hausdorff spaces.** For them the object is a Hausdorff completion or
    reflection, not automatically a compactification of the original topology.

## 4. Explicit deferrals and rescopings

These are deliberate exclusions from this dependency-closed pair.

- **Metric sequential completeness versus filter completeness.** The published
  uniform page defines completeness by Cauchy filters, while the earlier metric
  spine uses Cauchy sequences. Proving their equivalence requires a careful
  countable-choice audit. It is not needed for the Samuel construction and is
  deferred rather than asserted in a remark.
- **Metric completion automatically supplies a uniform completion.** This
  bridge is likewise deferred. The `(0,1)` example verifies the uniform
  completion axioms directly using compactness of `[0,1]`; it does not rely on
  an unstated general bridge.
- **The broad Samuel equivalence catalogue.** No equivalence with Gelfand
  spectra, maximal ideals, proximity/Smirnov compactifications, uniformly
  continuous function algebras, or every published definition of
  precompactification is planned.
- **An unrestricted claim that Samuel completion equals ordinary completion.**
  The page proves equality only for totally bounded uniform spaces under DC.
  The discrete-natural counterexample records why the unrestricted claim is
  false.
- **An unrestricted uniform-embedding claim.** Under DC the Samuel map is a
  topological embedding for separated spaces. It need not preserve the
  original uniformity; the B page makes the distinction explicit.
- **A choice-free full compact-target universal property.** The planned local
  proof carries DC and the ultrafilter lemma. Removing or weakening those
  hypotheses is a separate foundational project.
- **Algebraic compactification constructions.** No Banach-algebra, character
  space, or product-closure construction is imported merely to give another
  model of the same compactification.
- **Unopened or later-page dependencies.** There are no forward references and
  no external-fallback items. If authoring discovers a missing theorem, the
  default action is to narrow or defer the clause, not to cite a later page or
  place a load-bearing fact in remarks.
