The one-dimensional conformal vertex algebra verifies the chosen admissible-module definition of rationality directly. The dual-number example shows why a finite-label constant-field subcategory of vertex-algebra modules need not be semisimple. It has no conformal vector, so it is not a counterexample to any rationality theorem for conformal vertex operator algebras.
