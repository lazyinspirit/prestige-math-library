Canonical Bases of q-Deformed Fock Spaces
Bernard Leclerc∗and Jean-Yves Thibon†
Abstract
We deﬁne a canonical basis of the q-deformed Fock space representation of the aﬃne Lie
algebra bgln. We conjecture that the entries of the transition matrix between this basis and
the natural basis of the Fock space are q-analogues of decomposition numbers of the v-Schur
algebras for v specialized to a nth root of unity.

computed explicitely. It would be interesting to compare it with the canonical basis obtained
