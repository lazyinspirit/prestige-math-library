# Finite cyclotomic Hecke algebras and spectral propagation

Research construction for the native24 first repair. This is an actual algebraic route for the finite/split-spectral input of the CRT definition. It does not claim the stronger bounded ordered-monomial basis or content-idempotent primitivity. Those retained claims have separate construction obligations. No native readiness or independent audit is claimed.

Fix the native affine presentations, an arbitrary field F, finite quantum characteristic e≥2, dominant Λ and the standard split FIRST cyclotomic polynomial. Put ℓ=Σ_i λ_i. The DG branch has q=1 and charF=e, the NG branch q≠1 and q has order e. At d=0 use HΛ_0=F. At d>0 with ℓ=0 the first relation is1=0, so the algebra is zero; below assumeℓ>0.

1. **Normal spanning, without a cyclotomic basis assumption.** The DG mixed relation lets each polynomial generator pass each simple reflection, with its permutation term and a scalar lower term. In NG the relations give
   X_r T_r=T_r X_(r+1)+(1-q)X_(r+1),
   X_(r+1) T_r=T_r X_r+(q-1)X_(r+1),
   and the inverse-variable versions printed immediately after BK(4.5). Solve these equations in the opposite direction to move every Laurent variable left. All correction terms have fewer misplaced variable/crossing pairs, so induction terminates. The image of the finite Hecke subalgebra is spanned by the images of T_w, by its existing standard-basis supplier (only its spanning clause is consumed, no injectivity in the affine algebra is assumed). Thus affine ordered polynomial/Laurent monomials times T_w span. For DG use the group-algebra image and its finite permutation basis. This is enough here; independence is not used.

2. **Global intertwiners from the presentation.** For neighboring polynomial variables x,y, the DG element θ=s(x-y)+1 satisfies θx=yθ and θ²=1-(x-y)². For NG commuting units x,y, Θ=T(1-xy^(-1))+1-q satisfies Θx=yΘ and Θ²=(x-qy)(y-qx)/(xy). These are identities in the WHOLE affine algebra, without spectral idempotents or unequal-color inverse differences. Substitute the mixed equation s y=x s+1 and s²=1 for DG. For NG substitute T²=(q-1)T+q and TxT=qy, using T^(-1)=q^(-1)T+q^(-1)-1. Expanding the two squares and moving variables gives exactly the displayed products. Hence they remain valid in the quotient. These are the global identities BK(3.10)–(3.12), (4.10)–(4.12), whose full proof-bearing presentation and uses were read; the algebraic substitutions are reproduced here rather than importing the foreign spectral lemma.

3. **Monic propagation via a finite power-basis norm.** Suppose a split monic f(x) annihilates the old variable. Then θ f(x) θ=f(y) θ²=0, or Θ f(x) Θ=f(y) Θ²=0. In DG this says f(y)[1-(x-y)²]=0. In NG multiply by the commuting unitxy to get f(y)(x-qy)(y-qx)=0. Let C=F[z]/f(z), with its explicit basis1,z,...,z^(D-1), D=degf. Multiplication by the respective quadratic r(z,y) on C[y] has matrix M(y) with leading coefficient -Id (DG) or -qId (NG), hence detM(y) has invertible leading coefficient. The adjugate identity, applied to the basis vector1, writes r(z,y)R(z,y)=detM(y) in C[y]. Lift back to F[x,y], multiply by f(y), and use f(x)=0 and f(y)r(x,y)=0. It follows that f(y)detM(y)=0. Divide by its leading scalar to obtain a monic next-variable annihilator. No flatness, algebraic closure, generic faithfulness or denominator difference is assumed.

4. **Spectral inclusion.** If f(z)=∏_a(z-a) with multiplicities, the same power-basis determinant has product ∏_a r(a,y), since its multiplication matrix is triangular after the finite decomposition into factors (or by the companion-matrix resultant identity). In DG its new roots are a+1,a-1; in NG they are qa,q^(-1)a. Thus starting from ∏_i(x_1-i)^λ_i gives roots entirely in the prime-field residue set; starting from ∏_i(X_1-q^i)^λ_i gives roots in{q^i}. Both sets are closed under the indicated moves. Multiplicities are retained; no square-free assumption is made. In NG all roots remain nonzero, so every annihilator has nonzero constant coefficient. Its monic relation expresses the inverse variable as a polynomial in that variable.

5. **Finiteness and canonical CRT projectors.** Apply the construction successively along the positions. Each polynomial variable has a finite-degree monic annihilator, so normal spanning reduces its exponents below this degree, with negative exponents eliminated in NG. Finitely many exponent tuples and T_w span HΛ_d. This proves finite dimension without the exact ℓ^d d! basis theorem. The minimal polynomial on its regular module divides the constructed split annihilator; consequently it has only the required eigenvalues. The usual finite polynomial Euclidean/CRT construction gives the canonical projectors ε_r(i), including zero factors. Their commuting products e(i) sum to1 and act on every finite module as the corresponding simultaneous generalized-eigenspace projectors. This proves precisely the needed spectral/finite input of the native residue definition over the actual original field. Centrality follows from the local Hecke cross/intertwiner identities, independently of primitivity: the only possible image of an i-weight under the neighboring Hecke generator has weightsi ands_r i, so fixed-content sums are invariant. Primitivity is NOT inferred from this argument.
