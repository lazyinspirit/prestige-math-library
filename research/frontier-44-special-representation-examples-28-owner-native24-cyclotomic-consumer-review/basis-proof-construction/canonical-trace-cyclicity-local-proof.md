## Full local proof

Write q=q_H and f(t)=t^ℓ+c_(ℓ−1)t^(ℓ−1)+…+c_0=∏_(a=1)^ℓ(t−Q_a), with ℓ≥1. Assume the independently proved universal ordered PBW basis; no trace assertion is part of this input. For n≥1 put H_n for the rank-n cyclotomic algebra, H_0=R. PBW identifies H_(n−1) with the subalgebra on X_1,…,X_(n−1),T_1,…,T_(n−2): its bounded words are a subset of the H_n basis. Let v_n=1 and v_k=s_(n−1)…s_k for 1≤k<n. The finite Hecke left-coset decomposition w=u v_k with u∈S_(n−1) is length additive. Since X_n commutes with all generators of H_(n−1), PBW gives the left H_(n−1)-basis {X_n^a T_(v_k):0≤a<ℓ,1≤k≤n}. Define the left-linear projection E_n:H_n→H_(n−1) by E_n(X_n^aT_(v_k))=1 for a=0,k=n and zero otherwise. Its PBW coefficient interpretation gives τ_n=τ_(n−1)∘E_n, where τ_0 is the identity R→R.

For n>1 prove E_n(hX_1)=E_n(h)X_1 on this left basis. If k=n, then X_n commutes with X_1, giving the claim. If k>1 but k<n, each factor of T_(v_k) has index at least two and commutes with X_1, giving the claim. Only k=1 remains. Write V=T_(n−1)…T_1 and U=(T_(n−1)−q+1)…(T_1−q+1). The mixed relation T_jX_j=X_(j+1)(T_j−q+1) gives VX_1=X_nU. Thus, if 0≤a<ℓ−1, X_n^aVX_1=X_n^(a+1)U has positive bounded X_n-degree. Expansion of U in finite Hecke words and their left-coset decomposition shows E_n of this expression is zero.

At a=ℓ−1 the same expression is X_n^ℓU, so a separate cyclotomic argument is necessary. For every m≥1, Bernstein straightening gives
T_jX_j^m=X_(j+1)^m(T_j−q+1)−(q−1)Σ_(b=1)^(m−1)X_j^b X_(j+1)^(m−b).
Applying this successively along V proves VX_1^m=X_n^m U+J_m, where J_m is a linear combination of polynomial–finite-Hecke terms of total dot degree m, each with X_n-degree between 1 and m−1 and no variable above n. Here J_1=0. To see the bound inductively, at each step the active variable has a positive exponent c≤m. The leading branch transfers c to the next variable; every correction transfers a positive exponent strictly below c. Earlier variables commute with the next crossing. Once a correction has occurred, the final X_n-exponent remains below m; no branch transfers exponent zero. This also proves that the leading branch is exactly X_n^mU. For m≤ℓ, every lower exponent in J_m is individually below ℓ because X_n-degree is positive and total degree is at most ℓ. Hence these are already bounded polynomials, and finite Hecke left-coset straightening preserves positive bounded X_n-degree. Therefore E_n(J_m)=0 for m≤ℓ. Also E_n(X_n^mU)=0 for 1≤m<ℓ, and E_n(V)=0 because v_1 is a nonidentity left coset.

Now the actual first-dot relation, multiplied by V, is 0=Vf(X_1)=X_n^ℓU+J_ℓ+Σ_(m=1)^(ℓ−1)c_m(X_n^mU+J_m)+c_0V. Applying E_n gives E_n(X_n^ℓU)=0. This completes the missing a=ℓ−1 case, including ℓ=1 (then every J_m and the positive lower-power sum is empty). Thus E_n(hX_1)=E_n(h)X_1 for all h. By left linearity E_n(X_1h)=X_1E_n(h), and induction using τ_n=τ_(n−1)E_n proves τ_n(X_1h)=τ_n(hX_1). The base n=1 is the commutative algebra R[X_1]/f(X_1); n=0 is R. This uses neither individual relations f(X_r)=0 nor assumed trace invariance.

It remains to check finite generators T_j directly on every PBW element pT_w with p=X^a bounded. Bernstein gives T_jp=(s_jp)T_j+(1−q)X_(j+1)(p−s_jp)/(X_j−X_(j+1)). For p=X_j^aX_(j+1)^b times the other variables, the correction monomials have the same total dot degree, and both displayed-variable exponents are at most max(a,b)<ℓ: when a>b the correction exponents are (a−1−k,b+1+k),0≤k<a−b, and when a<b they are (a+k,b−k),0≤k<b−a, with the corresponding sign. Thus, when the exponent profile is positive, every term on either side of τ(T_jpT_w)=τ(pT_wT_j) has a positive bounded profile and value zero; no cyclotomic reduction occurs. When p=1, the finite Hecke multiplication formula gives coefficient of 1 in T_jT_w or T_wT_j equal to q if w=s_j, and zero otherwise. This proves finite-generator cyclicity without using the sought trace.

Finally X_1 and T_1,…,T_(n−1) generate H_n as an R-algebra. Indeed c_0=(−1)^ℓ∏Q_a is a unit, so f(X_1)=0 makes X_1^(−1) a polynomial in X_1; each T_j^(−1)=q^(−1)(T_j−q+1), and X_r=q^(1−r)T_(r−1)…T_1X_1T_1…T_(r−1), with inverses expressed likewise. Move the generators of a word one at a time around τ, using the identities proved above, to obtain τ(ab)=τ(ba) for every pair of words and hence all a,b by R-bilinearity. No generic-faithfulness, reduced-basis trace, or imported Bremke–Malle cyclicity is assumed.
