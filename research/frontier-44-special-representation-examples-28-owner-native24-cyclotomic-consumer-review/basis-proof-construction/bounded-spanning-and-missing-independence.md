## NG transported relations and terminating reduction

R_r=q^{r−1}X_r^ℓ+Σ_{a,w} c_{a,w}X_1^{a_1}⋯X_r^{a_r}T_w,

where w∈S_r, Σ_j a_j≤ℓ, and a_r<ℓ. No variable with index above r occurs. Prove this by induction. For a positive integer m, direct summation of the mixed relation gives

T_r X_r^m T_r=q X_{r+1}^m−(q−1)Σ_{k=1}^{m−1}X_r^k X_{r+1}^{m−k}T_r.

For an old remainder monomial, its coefficient has X_r-degree a_r<ℓ. All variables below r commute with T_r, while its T_w lies in the finite H_r and commutes with X_{r+1}. ## DG transported relations and terminating reduction

R_r=x_r^ℓ+Σ_{a,w}c_{a,w}x^a w,
