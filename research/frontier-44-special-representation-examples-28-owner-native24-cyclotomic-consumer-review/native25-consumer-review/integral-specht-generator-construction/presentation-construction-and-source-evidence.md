This is precisely the quotient by the initial-generator weight,dot,and horizontal-crossing relations, not a heuristic assertion that dot nilpotence implies rationality of a particular vector.

For each Garnir node A=(a,b), take the belt consisting of the row-a suffix beginning in column b and row-(a+1) prefix ending in b. Fill its Garnir tableau G^A from lower left to upper right. Partition it into residue-A bricks of length e, ignoring the shorter endpoint fragments. Let k be the number of bricks and f the number in the upper row; let T^A be the maximal brick tableau.
