# Universal cyclotomic Hecke symmetrizing trace: actual local route

SAME native24 pending first repair construction; no native/canonical/readiness/gate mutation. This closes the universal trace input once the named universal PBW and the local cyclicity lemma bind. It does not close Q-S1 or the Schur Jantzen weight Gram identity. The Hecke parameter is q_H throughout, unrelated to grading v.

Let R=Z[q_H^(±1),Q_1^(±1),...,Q_ell^(±1)], ell≥1, and H_n be the nondegenerate monic cyclotomic affine-Hecke quotient. Assume the independently constructed universal PBW basis B={X_1^a_1...X_n^a_n T_w:0≤a_i<ell,w∈S_n}. Define τ(B)=1 exactly at all a_i=0,w=1 and zero elsewhere. The complete local tower proof in basis-proof-construction/canonical-trace-cyclicity-local-proof.md supplies τ(ab)=τ(ba): its E_n projection, top monic-degree case and finite Bernstein calculation were read and checked. No Bremke–Malle trace statement is imported. Prove perfectness as follows.

1. Set J_1=1,J_i=q_H^(-1)T_(i-1)J_(i-1)T_(i-1) in the finite Hecke subalgebra. These are units, since all T_i and q_H are units. Define δ(0)=0,δ(c)=−1 for c>0 and the second family
C={T_v J_1^δ(c_1)...J_n^δ(c_n) X_1^c_1...X_n^c_n:0≤c_i<ell,v∈S_n}.
The standard anti-involution fixing the presentation generators fixes each X_i. Thus B*= {T_v X^c} is a basis. At each fixed profilec, multiplying the finite Hecke factor by the unit J_1^δ(c_1)...J_n^δ(c_n) is an invertible R-linear change of the finite Hecke basis. Hence C is an R-basis. This uses actual universal PBW, not flatness or generic semisimplicity.

2. Let V_j be the span of X_1^a_1...X_j^a_j T_w,0≤a_i<ell,w∈S_n. It is stable under left multiplication by lower-rank polynomial generators and right multiplication by finite-Hecke elements: the lower-rank algebra embeds by PBW and its monic reductions stay in that rank. For h∈V_j and 0<s<ell, τ(X_(j+1)^s h)=0 by the defining coefficient rule. This is immediate bounded ordered PBW, not a per-dot cyclotomic relation.

3. The exact Bernstein identity (obtained by its product rule) is, for k≥m,
X_i^k X_(i-1)^m T_(i-1)=T_(i-1)X_i^m X_(i-1)^k+(q_H−1)Σ_(c=m+1)^k X_i^c X_(i-1)^(k+m−c),
with the reversed version obtained by the presentation anti-involution. It gives the telescoping identity
X_i^k=q_H^(-1)T_(i-1)X_(i-1)^kT_(i-1)+q_H^(-1)(q_H−1)Σ_(c=1)^(k−1) X_i^c X_(i-1)^(k−c)T_(i-1).
For 1≤i≤j+1 and 1≤k≤ell it follows that
τ(X_(j+1)^k h)=q_H^(i-j-1)τ(T_j...T_i X_i^k T_i...T_j h), h∈V_j.
Here is the source-adapted downward induction, with its boundedness premise explicit. The base i=j+1 is identity. Substitute the preceding telescoping formula in the induction term. The leading summand is the next i−1 term. In every correction0<c<k, move one adjacent X through the inverse string T_i^(-1)...T_j^(-1), using T_a^(-1)=q_H^(-1)(T_a+1−q_H) and the displayed Bernstein rule. It becomes a sum of finite-Hecke factors followed by dot monomials, with X_(j+1)-exponent b≤max(c−1,k−c−1)≤k−2. The original moved dot supplies one further X_(j+1), so the resulting exponent is in1,...,k−1<ell. All other factors belong to V_j after lower-rank monic reduction. Cyclicity rotates only the explicit finite-Hecke factors, and the coefficient-zero rule in2 kills each correction. Thus the downward induction holds. This reproduces the actual complete calculation of Malle–Mathas Lemma3.7; its trace premise is now our local tower lemma. No foreign center/semisimplicity claim is used.

4. Put α=(−1)^(ell+1)Q_1...Q_ell. Take i=1,k=ell in3 and reduce the FIRST variable X_1^ell with its actual polynomial ∏(X_1−Q_a)=0. The terms of degrees1,...,ell−1 vanish by3 and2; the constant coefficient is (−1)^ell productQ_a and moves to the other side with a minus. Therefore
τ(X_(j+1)^ell h)=α τ(h J_(j+1)).
For j=0 the same equation is the direct first-variable polynomial calculation with J_1=1. The acquired author-preprint Proposition3.8 prints the opposite sign. Our coefficient is forced by the monic relation, confirmed at ell2,n2, and is a UNIT either way. We do not silently import the literal erroneous sign.

5. Let G_j be the pairing matrix between B,C restricted to profiles whose entries abovej are zero, with all finite permutations still included. Its size N_j=ell^j n!. Order new last profiles by b_(j+1)=0,1,...,ell−1 and c_(j+1)=0,ell−1,ell−2,...,1. Cyclicity rotates the rightmost dot monomial to the front, combining its exponents with the left dot monomial. If both new exponents are0, the block is G_j. If0<b+c<ell, it is zero by2. If b+c=ell, formula4 applies; the right factor J_(j+1)^(-1) occurs because c>0, and cancels the J_(j+1) in4. The block is αG_j. Terms with b+c>ell need not be computed. After the indicated column permutation the whole matrix is block lower triangular, with one diagonal G_j and ell−1 diagonals αG_j. Consequently
 det G_(j+1)=±α^((ell−1)N_j)(det G_j)^ell.
The block-size exponent is recorded correctly; only invertibility is needed. G_0=(τ(T_w T_v)) has entries q_H^length(w) δ_(v,w^-1), by the ordinary finite-Hecke basis multiplication. Thus its determinant is a unit. Induction makes detG_n a unit. Since B and C are actual R-bases, τ identifies H_n with its R-dual: it is a perfect symmetric associative pairing. No fraction-field rank is substituted for a unit determinant.
