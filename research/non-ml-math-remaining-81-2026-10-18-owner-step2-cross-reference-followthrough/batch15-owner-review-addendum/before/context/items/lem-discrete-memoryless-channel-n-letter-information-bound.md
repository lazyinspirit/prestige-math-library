## Statement

For every input probability vector on $\mathcal X^n$ and output $Y^n$ generated by a finite DMC $W^n$, one has $I(X^n;Y^n)\le nC(W)$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A finite DMC $W$, $n\ge1$, and any probability vector for the input word $X^n$.

[F1] [[thm-it-finite-entropy-chain-rule]]: $I(U;V)=H(V)-H(V\mid U)$ and conditional entropy has its finite chain rule.

[F2] [[thm-it-finite-conditional-mutual-information-identities-and-equality]]: finite conditional mutual information is nonnegative; in particular, conditioning on a finite variable cannot increase conditional entropy.

[F3] [[def-discrete-memoryless-channel]] and [[lem-discrete-memoryless-channel-block-kernels-are-stochastic]]: $P(Y^n=y^n\mid X^n=x^n)=\prod_{i=1}^nW(y_i\mid x_i)$.

[F4] [[def-discrete-memoryless-channel-information-capacity]]: $I(X_i;Y_i)\le C(W)$ for every marginal input law of $X_i$.

## Proof

1.1 By [F1], $H(Y^n)=\sum_{i=1}^nH(Y_i\mid Y^{i-1})$. For each $i$, [F2] gives $H(Y_i\mid Y^{i-1})\le H(Y_i)$, so $H(Y^n)\le\sum_iH(Y_i)$. [F1, F2, given, algebra]

1.2 The conditional chain rule in [F1] gives $H(Y^n\mid X^n)=\sum_iH(Y_i\mid X^n,Y^{i-1})$. For every positive-probability value $(x^n,y^{i-1})$, factorization [F3] and summing the unused future coordinates show that the conditional law of $Y_i$ is $W(\cdot\mid x_i)$. Thus $H(Y_i\mid X^n,Y^{i-1})=H(Y_i\mid X_i)$, including after averaging over conditioning values. [F1, F3, given, algebra]

2.1 By [F1], $I(X^n;Y^n)=H(Y^n)-H(Y^n\mid X^n)$. Steps 1.1 and 1.2 therefore give $I(X^n;Y^n)\le\sum_{i=1}^n(H(Y_i)-H(Y_i\mid X_i))=\sum_{i=1}^nI(X_i;Y_i)\le nC(W)$ by [F4]. [F1, F4, step 1.1, step 1.2, algebra] ∎
