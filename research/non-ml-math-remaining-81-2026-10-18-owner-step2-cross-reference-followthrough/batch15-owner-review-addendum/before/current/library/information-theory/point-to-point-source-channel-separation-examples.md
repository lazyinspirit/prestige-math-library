These examples compute a strict entropy-capacity budget for a finite IID source and a BSC, then show the limit of that asymptotic result. A full-support noisy channel has positive error at every finite blocklength for any nontrivial source, even when the asymptotic separation inequality is strict. The examples distinguish asymptotic reliability from finite-block zero-error performance.
