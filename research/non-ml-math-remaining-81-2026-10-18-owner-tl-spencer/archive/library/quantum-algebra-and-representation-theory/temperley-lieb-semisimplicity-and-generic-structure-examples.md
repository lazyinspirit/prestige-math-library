These examples keep algebra semisimplicity, trace nondegeneracy, and projector recursion as separate calculations. At order three, rank two is semisimple even though the normalized Jones trace is degenerate. At zero loop value, rank three and rank four have different semisimplicity behavior. At loop value two, the rank-two Jones–Wenzl recursion and its normalized trace can be checked directly.

Each example uses only the definitions and proved complex results on [[temperley-lieb-semisimplicity-and-generic-structure]]. The examples are not theorem suppliers. Spencer's arbitrary-characteristic projector criterion remains an A-page proof obligation and no modular example here assumes it.

## Examples

- [[ex-temperley-lieb-order-three-trace-boundary]] computes the rank-two semisimple algebra and its degenerate trace form at $n=\ell-1$.
- [[ex-temperley-lieb-zero-loop-parity-ranks-three-four]] evaluates the odd/even split at zero loop value in ranks three and four.
- [[ex-temperley-lieb-jones-wenzl-at-loop-two]] verifies the projector and trace at $q=1$.
