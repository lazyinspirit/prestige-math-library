## Defect outside edit scope

`thm-symmetric-elliptic-form-operator-is-self-adjoint-with-compact-resolvent`, current-run batch 11, consumed directly by `ex-dirichlet-heat-semigroup-from-the-laplacian`. Fatal historical missing-hypothesis defect in the initially observed complex compact-resolvent sentence in `## Statement`; its `## Proof`, step 4.1, supplies compactness only for bounded Omega under AC.

The initial statement said “For K=C, L has compact resolvent” following a separate conditional compact-inverse sentence, with Omega merely open. A counterexample is Omega=R and L=-d²/dx²: for nonzero separated translates phi_n of a smooth compactly supported function, the bounded L2 sequence f_n=(L+1)phi_n satisfies K_1 f_n=phi_n, whose mutual distances are sqrt(2)||phi||.
