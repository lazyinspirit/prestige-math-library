# Prospective elementary SL2 K-type/type-I route

Status: the K-corner and ladder route was included in the bounded Codex SL2 source/proof audit, then integrated with the corrected run Casimir normalization into the current batch-5 manifest and owner direction. This note remains proof-route context, not an authored-item acceptance or gate record.
No source reading or provenance is asserted by this note.

Let G=SL2(R), K=SO2, A=C*(G), and chi_m(k_theta)=exp(i m theta).
The outline proves dim H_m<=1 for every irreducible unitary representation,
and that each irreducible image of A contains a nonzero rank-one operator.
It therefore supplies a direct GCR route rather than importing general
Harish-Chandra admissibility. It does not prove Plancherel inversion.

## Elementary geometry and measure

1. G=K Aplus K: diagonalize g^Tg by Q in SO2 with eigenvalues
   exp(2t),exp(-2t), t>=0. Then gQ diag(exp(-t),exp(t)) lies in SO2.
   Thus g=k1 diag(exp(t),exp(-t)) k2.
2. G is generated by u(x)=[[1,x],[0,1]] and v(y)=[[1,0],[y,1]].
   If g_11!=0 use the LDU factorization and
   diag(a,a^-1)=u(a-1)v(1)u(a^-1-1)v(-a).
   If g_11=0, left multiplication by u(1) makes that entry nonzero.
3. Each u(x),v(y) is a commutator with diag(s,s^-1), s>1, since
   conjugation scales their parameters by s^2 and s^-2 respectively.
   Hence the modular homomorphism is one on generators: G is unimodular.
4. Set J=diag(1,-1) and sigma(g)=J g^T J. This is an involutive
   anti-automorphism, fixes every element of K and every positive diagonal
   a_t, and preserves Haar measure: anti-automorphism sends left Haar to
   right Haar; unimodularity and sigma^2=id force its positive scale to one.

## The character corners are commutative

For f in Cc(G), form the double K average

    Q_m f(g)= integral_(KxK) chi_m(k)^-1 chi_m(l)^-1
                                      f(k^-1 g l^-1) dk dl.

Under the left integrated convention, its represented operator is
P_m pi(f) P_m, where P_m=integral_K chi_m(k)^-1 pi(k) dk.
The formulas use pi(f)=integral f(g)pi(g)dg. Substituting g=k h l
in the represented integral gives P_m pi(f) P_m exactly. To compute
covariance at k0 g l0, substitute k=k0 kprime and l=lprime l0
in Q_m; the character factors yield
Q_m f(k0 g l0)=chi_m(k0)^-1 chi_m(l0)^-1 Q_m f(g).
Both sides therefore use the same inverse character. A second averaging
cancels those factors, proving Q_m^2=Q_m.

Every such bi-character function h satisfies h(sigma(g))=h(g): write
 g=k1 a_t k2; sigma(g)=k2 a_t k1 and the two scalar character factors
commute. Also convolution preserves this bi-character space, and for Haar-
preserving sigma,

    (h1*h2) composed with sigma = (h2 composed with sigma)*(h1 composed with sigma).

Thus h1*h2=h2*h1. K averaging is a norm contraction for the universal
C*-norm (each integrated unitary operator has the corresponding corner
norm bound); Cc is dense in A. Its extension is the norm-closed corner
p_m A p_m in the multiplier notation. Products and adjoints remain in this
corner by the averaging/projector identities. Therefore this C*-corner is
commutative. If avoiding multiplier theory, define it as the closed image
of Q_m and prove the identities first on Cc by K integration; no p_m in A
is being asserted.

## Irreducible multiplicity one and compact operators

Let pi be irreducible and nonzero. Nondegenerate group/C*-correspondence and
the bicommutant theorem give strong closure pi(A)=B(H). K Fourier projection
P_m is an orthogonal projection onto the m-character space H_m; Fourier
completeness on the compact circle supplies some m with P_m!=0.

Compression of a strongly dense algebra is strongly dense in B(H_m):
for T in B(H_m), extend it by zero on H_m perp, approximate strongly by
pi(a), and compress. The compressed algebra is exactly the represented
commutative corner above. A commutative algebra strongly dense in B(H_m)
forces dim H_m<=1. Indeed each fixed corner element commutes with its whole
strong closure, so is scalar; the strong closure of scalars is scalars.
This avoids multiplying two unbounded approximating nets.

Since dim H_m=1, strong density supplies a with
P_m pi(a) P_m !=0. The element Q_m a belongs to A and its represented
operator is this nonzero rank-one operator. Thus pi(A) intersects K(H)
nontrivially; divide by its nonzero scalar to get P_m in pi(A). For a unit vector
e spanning H_m, products pi(a)P_m pi(b) are rank-one operators between
pi(a)e and pi(b*)e. Irreducibility makes pi(A)e dense in H, so norm
limits give all rank-one operators, then all K(H). All irreducibles are GCR. G is second-countable,
so the already supplied separable GCR=>factor-type-I direction applies.
No use of the owner-cited factor-type-I=>GCR implication is needed here.

## Toward classification (still a separate authoring obligation)

The finite-dimensional K-type conclusion makes K-finite vectors smooth
without a general admissibility theorem: convolution with Cc-infinity
smooths any strongly continuous unitary representation by differentiating
translated test functions in L1. K projection preserves smoothness, by
compact K averaging and adjoint-coordinate differentiation. The resulting
smooth vectors are dense in each H_m; its dimension<=1 makes all of H_m
smooth. Prove the smoothing/approximate-identity interfaces locally from
actual earlier group/manifold suppliers.

The sl2 ladder relations on one-dimensional K-types, skew-adjointness and
nonnegative squared ladder norms determine the possible consecutive weight
supports, Casimir value and unitary parameters. Compare to the actually
constructed principal, complementary, discrete and limit models; do not
substitute a parameter list for exhaustion. Phase-gauge the basis along
the connected weight string. Linear growth of ladder coefficients gives
factorial bounds on repeated generators, so finite K-type vectors are
analytic for each generator. The unitary Taylor integral remainder shows
that two such representations with matching derived actions agree on
small exponentials, then on all exponentials by subdivision and on G by
Iwasawa generation. This is a potential direct uniqueness route; exact
coefficients, endpoint cases and model existence remain to be supplied
from the current batch3/5 contracts.

For Borel classification/type-I via parameters, actual Borel invariant
extraction and all normalization choices must still be proved. The rank-
one GCR argument above already bypasses that need for the type-I claim.
Plancherel density, formal-degree calibration and inversion/completeness
are untouched by this note. Any genuinely missing part must be held or
