## Sources consulted

- MIT 18.966 notes, `https://math.mit.edu/classes/18.966/2014SP/notes.pdf`:
  Definition 1.1 gives foliation transitions with transverse coordinates
  depending only on transverse coordinates; its torus example gives translated
  affine leaves and density in the irrational case; Proposition 1.6 and
  Theorem 1.7 give integrable-implies-involutive and Frobenius.
- Etingof, *Introduction to Representation Theory / Lie Groups and Lie
  Algebras*, `https://math.mit.edu/~etingof/lnlg.pdf`, §8.3: a distribution is a
  constant-rank subbundle, integral sheets are tangent-curve reachability
  classes, and involutivity is equivalent to integrability.
- Tataru, *Partial Differential Equations*,
  `https://math.berkeley.edu/~tataru/papers/book.pdf`, §1.1: principal symbols
  live on the cotangent bundle and their Hamilton fields generate
  bicharacteristics, supporting the repaired characteristic remark.
- Melrose, MIT 18.155 notes,
  `https://math.mit.edu/~rbm/18.155-F16/L20-pre.pdf`, Definition 1: strict
  hyperbolicity relative to a covector includes that the covector is
  noncharacteristic, supporting the added (p_2(x,\tau)\ne0) condition.
- Opened library dependencies supplied the exact local conventions used in the
  remaining decisions: `thm-zorn` explicitly assumes Choice;
  `thm-the-tangent-bundle-has-a-canonical-smooth-2n-manifold-structure`
  explicitly assumes (\mathrm{AC}_\omega); and
  `def-calligraphic-l-p-on-a-measure-space` explicitly uses real-valued
  functions, which resolves the (L^p) phase objection.
