## Finite component authoring plan with real source proofs

The following proposed local IDs are proof obligations, not newly existing suppliers. Collision-check before authoring. They can be consolidated into the previously commissioned `lem-separable-group-c-star-type-i-and-smooth-dual-criteria` only if the resulting item supplies every used argument.

1. `lem-irreducible-c-star-representations-containing-compacts-have-an-elementary-ideal`. Use Blackadar IV.1.2.4–5 and IV.1.3.5, printed 347–349: a nonzero compact in an irreducible image gives all compact operators; its preimage is an ideal with elementary quotient. Spell out the nonzero finite-rank spectral projection, minimal rank projection, irreducibility of the hereditary corner and approximation of rank-one operators. Needed local ingredients are compact spectral calculus, hereditary-corner irreducibility (II.6.1.9), and irreducible restriction to a nonzero ideal (II.6.1.6–8).

2. `lem-nondegenerate-representations-of-compact-operator-algebras-are-amplifications`. Blackadar IV.1.2.2, printed 346: explicit matrix-unit basis construction gives a unitary H_rep ≅ K tensor L and rho(a)=a tensor I. This supplies uniqueness of the irreducible representation of an elementary algebra. Handle the finite-dimensional case and separable infinite case; record AC where bases are chosen.

3. `lem-gcr-irreducible-kernels-determine-equivalence`. Combine items 1–2 and the unique extension of nondegenerate ideal representations (II.6.1.6) as in IV.1.3.6. Two irreducibles with the same primitive kernel share the same elementary essential ideal and equivalent restrictions, hence equivalent extensions. This proves the GCR ⇒ injective-kernel direction with complete short arguments.

4. `lem-separable-gcr-algebras-have-type-i-factor-representations`. Use IV.1.3.8(ii): a factor kernel is prime (II.6.1.11 gives a genuine approximate-unit proof); for a separable algebra it is primitive (II.6.5.15). GCR yields an elementary ideal of the primitive quotient; the factor restriction to that ideal is essential and an amplification by item 2. Its double commutant agrees with the whole representation's double commutant by II.6.1.6, proving it type I. Supply the primitive-from-prime/Baire argument and the Baire theorem it invokes, rather than citing Choquet without a local proof. Alternatively prove the required faithful irreducible representation of a separable prime algebra by a full direct construction. This component does not prove type I ⇒ GCR.

5. `lem-internally-type-i-algebras-are-postliminal-and-gcr`. The local abelian-element ideal series in IV.1.1.7–12 and IV.1.3.2/8(i) yields the forward implication; transfinite construction and stabilization use AC and must be stated. This is optional as a decomposition of the final equivalence proof, not an extra claim added to the pair. It still does not prove bidual type I ⇒ internal type I.

6. `lem-noncommutative-hereditary-c-star-algebras-contain-square-zero-elements`. Blackadar II.6.4.14 supplies a full elementary argument: choose a non-scalar self-adjoint element in an irreducible image, disjoint spectral cutoffs, and a linking element from primeness; their product is nonzero and squares to zero. This is a genuine initial ingredient of the missing construction. It is not a proof of the CAR subquotient or the Type-II factor extension; approximate matrix units alone do not settle the central support/type assertion.
