It is appropriate only when the owner actually read the item at its current content. `verification.verified` is a **delegated** record with exact `model`, `verdict`, `date`, `scope`, and `delegated_by`, appropriate only for an independent reader's positive current-item review. Neither record is a judge verdict, a complete transitive supplier audit, or an assertion that a pending theorem has a finished proof. `tools/depcheck.mjs` checks the presence of one field on each published proved-here item, while the canonical ledger and bounded receipts retain the mathematical disposition.
