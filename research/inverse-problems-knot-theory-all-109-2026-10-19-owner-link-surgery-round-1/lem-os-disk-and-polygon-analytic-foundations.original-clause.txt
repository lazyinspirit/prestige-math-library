
Assume ACω. For every admissible pointed Heegaard multi-diagram for a closed oriented three-manifold, generic perturbations within the admissible class make every Maslov-index-one disk moduli space and every polygon moduli space used for the hat differential, continuation maps, handleslide maps, stabilization maps, and surgery maps regular and finite after quotient by translation. Their compactifications have exactly the broken-disk or broken-polygon codimension-one ends, together with the stated boundary-degeneration strata; gluing gives collars at those ends, and the boundary-degeneration terms cancel over $\mathbb F_2$. No sphere bubble, boundary escape, or uncontrolled periodic domain contributes an additional codimension-one end. In particular, the disk differential squares to zero and the polygon maps satisfy the chain-map, composition, and homotopy identities required in Heegaard Floer theory.
