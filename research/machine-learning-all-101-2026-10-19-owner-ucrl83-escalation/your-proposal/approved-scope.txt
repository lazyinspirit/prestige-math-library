### ML-75 — Average-reward regret and UCRL2

**Pages:** average-reward-regret-and-ucrl2 / average-reward-regret-and-ucrl2-examples. **Scope:** Assume the Axiom of Choice required by the path-law and martingale suppliers. Define a finite communicating MDP with finite state/action sets, a joint next-state/reward probability kernel permitting every Borel reward law on $[0,1]$, raw diameter $D_0$, and normalized diameter $D=\max\{1,D_0\}$. Retain the finite-MDP supplier's finite state/action skeleton through the local bounded-reward extension; no global reward-alphabet rewrite is required. Prove that optimal finite-horizon mean values have a common limit $\rho^*$, independent of initial state. Distinguish pathwise regret $\Delta_T=T\rho^*-\sum_{t=1}^T r_t$ from its expectation. Define UCRL2's confidence sets, count-doubling episodes and extended value iteration; prove finite inner termination for all strictly positive transition radii by a local span contraction. For every prescribed initial state and fixed integer $T\ge2$, prove $\Pr(\Delta_T\le34DS\sqrt{AT\log(T/\delta)})\ge1-\delta$; for every $T\ge1$ prove the same coefficient with $\log(2T/\delta)$. Preserve and refute the original all-horizon zero-offset formula at $T=1$. For integers $S,A\ge10$, real $D\ge\max\{1,20\log_A S\}$, and integer $T\ge DSA$, prove that for every learning algorithm and prescribed initial state label there exists an exactly $S$-state, exactly $A$-action communicating MDP of normalized diameter at most $D$ with expected regret at least $0.015\sqrt{DSAT}$. Retain and refute the original infeasible/reversed-quantifier lower statement; give the strengthened exact construction, padding, small-diameter and KL/Pinsker argument locally. This is not discounted-return RL, and the theorem does not extend to uncommunicating/transient-state models without changes. Proposed interfaces: def-communicating-mdp-and-diameter, def-average-reward-and-regret, alg-ucrl2, thm-ucrl2-high-probability-regret, thm-ucrl2-regret-lower-bound. **Proof route:** establish reward and categorical-$L^1$ confidence events, span-of-bias $\le D$, extended-value-iteration approximation, failed-confidence episode bounds, and the doubling-count sum; derive the lower bound from the source's hard-MDP construction. **Examples:** noncommunicating model, large diameter, discounted/average-reward target mismatch. **Requires:** ML-72 — Finite MDPs and Bellman operators; def-time-homogeneous-markov-chain-with-transition-kernel, def-filtration-and-filtered-probability-space, def-martingale-difference-sequence, def-martingale-submartingale-and-supermartingale, lem-conditional-hoeffding-bound-for-bounded-martingale-differences, and thm-azuma-hoeffding-inequality; carry the Axiom of Choice required by the Markov-chain supplier. **Sources:** Jaksch–Ortner–Auer, *Near-optimal Regret Bounds for Reinforcement Learning*, complete JMLR PDF pp. 1–38 (printed pp. 1563–1600): model/diameter and Theorem 2, pp. 2–5; UCRL2 and confidence/episode construction, pp. 7–12; regret proof, pp. 12–21; Theorem 5 and hard-instance proof, pp. 22–27. The theorem's probability and initial-state quantifiers are retained.
