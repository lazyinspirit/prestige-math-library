### A page: vc-dimension-growth-and-pac-classification

- **def-shattered-set-and-vc-dimension** (dependency level 0). Defines binary traces and shattering on indexed tuples; repeated points remain separate indices, the empty tuple is shattered, and VC dimension is the supremum of shattered lengths.
  Direct dependencies: def-shattering-and-vc-dimension. Sources: Mohri, Definition 3.10; Bartlett–Mendelson, Theorem 6.
- **def-growth-function** (dependency level 1). Defines $\Pi_H(m)$ as the attained maximum number of distinct binary traces on an indexed $m$-tuple; establishes $\Pi_H(0)=1$.
  Direct dependencies: def-shattered-set-and-vc-dimension. Sources: Mohri, Definition 3.6 and equation (3.19); Bartlett–Mendelson, Theorem 6.
- **lem-vc-loss-rademacher-bound** (dependency level 1). Proves the explicit finite-sample empirical Rademacher bound $C_{\rm VC}\sqrt{(d+1)/m}$ by VC-subgraph covering and a finite chaining argument; no choice principle is used.
  Direct dependencies: def-shattered-set-and-vc-dimension, def-vc-subgraph-class-and-index, def-product-sigma-algebra-and-finite-product-sigma-algebras, thm-vc-covering-entropy, lem-bounded-variable-hoeffding-mgf, thm-geometric-series, def-natural-logarithm, thm-natural-logarithm-laws. Sources: Mohri, Theorems 3.3, 3.5, 3.7 and Corollary 3.8; Bartlett–Mendelson, Theorem 6.
- **thm-sauer-shelah-growth-bound** (dependency level 2). Transports the finite-set-family Sauer bound to indexed samples and proves $\Pi_H(m)\le(em/d)^d$ for $1\le d\le m$, with $m=0$ and $d=0$ handled.
  Direct dependencies: def-shattered-set-and-vc-dimension, def-growth-function, thm-sauer-shelah, def-binomial-coefficient, thm-binomial-theorem, lem-exponential-dominates-one-plus-x, thm-exponential-addition-formula, def-real-exponential-function-and-e. Sources: Mohri, Theorem 3.17 and Corollary 3.18 plus official errata; Bartlett–Mendelson, Theorems 5–6 for VC context.
- **def-vc-pac-learning-criterion** (dependency level 3). Defines risk-measurable learning rules and realizable/agnostic PAC events for possibly infinite measurable binary classes; does not imply measurable-selector or ERM existence.
  Direct dependencies: def-realizable-and-agnostic-pac, def-population-and-empirical-risk. Sources: Mohri, Chapter 2; local extension is limited to explicit risk measurability.
- **thm-vc-uniform-convergence** (dependency level 3). Provides the choice-free finite-class absolute-deviation branch and, under AC, the exact one-sided VC bound $\sqrt{2d\log(em/d)/m}+\sqrt{\log(1/\delta)/(2m)}$ for $1\le d\le m$.
  Direct dependencies: def-shattered-set-and-vc-dimension, def-growth-function, thm-sauer-shelah-growth-bound, def-population-and-empirical-risk, lem-population-and-empirical-risk-are-measurable, def-identically-distributed-and-iid-random-variables, thm-independent-random-elements-have-product-joint-law, thm-sigma-finite-product-measure-exists-is-rectangular-and-is-unique, thm-fubini-theorem-for-l-one-on-sigma-finite-product-spaces, thm-mcdiarmid-bounded-differences, def-axiom-of-choice, thm-finite-class-uniform-deviation, lem-bounded-variable-hoeffding-mgf, def-natural-logarithm, thm-natural-logarithm-laws. Sources: Mohri, Corollary 3.19; Bartlett–Mendelson, Theorem 5 in a different normalization.
- **thm-vc-entropy-uniform-convergence** (dependency level 3). Proves a two-sided uniform deviation rate $2C_{\rm VC}\sqrt{(d+1)/m}+\sqrt{\log(2/\delta)/(2m)}$ with explicit constant and the imported AC condition for concentration.
  Direct dependencies: lem-vc-loss-rademacher-bound, def-axiom-of-choice, def-natural-logarithm, def-population-and-empirical-risk, lem-population-and-empirical-risk-are-measurable, def-identically-distributed-and-iid-random-variables, thm-sigma-finite-product-measure-exists-is-rectangular-and-is-unique, thm-independent-random-elements-have-product-joint-law, thm-fubini-theorem-for-l-one-on-sigma-finite-product-spaces, thm-mcdiarmid-bounded-differences, lem-probability-measure-basic-identities, thm-natural-logarithm-laws, def-real-exponential-function-and-e, cor-exponential-reciprocal-and-positivity. Sources: Mohri, Theorem 3.3 and full proof; Bartlett–Mendelson, Theorem 5 and Appendix B.
- **thm-vc-realizable-sample-complexity** (dependency level 4). Proves a conditional realizable guarantee for measurable consistent rules: if $d\ge1$, $m\ge64[ d\log(64e/\epsilon)+\log(4/\delta)]/\epsilon$ suffices; $d=0$ has zero risk.   Direct dependencies: def-shattered-set-and-vc-dimension, def-growth-function. Sources: AI-generated, with complete local verification.

## Mathematical route and conventions

- Traces are indexed vectors. Repeated instance points remain distinct coordinates, but a classifier must assign equal labels to equal instances. The empty tuple is shattered and has one trace.
- The finite trace family on `[m]` is the exact interface to published Sauer–Shelah. The polynomial bound adds the explicit binomial/exponential argument; no finite-dimensional trace pattern is counted as distinct unless its binary vector differs.
- The finite-sample VC Rademacher lemma uses the published choice-free VC-subgraph entropy bound and finite trace nets. The absolute-deviation theorem names AC only on the McDiarmid branch. Its separate finite-class branch is choice-free.
- The entropy uniform-deviation theorem assumes measurable suprema and AC exactly where the published McDiarmid supplier requires it. The agnostic PAC item carries that axiom and does not assert existence of an exact or approximate ERM.
- The realizable theorem is conditional on a risk-measurable consistent rule and explicitly measurable bad-consistent/split-witness events. Its double-sample proof uses pointwise witnesses without selecting a sample-indexed witness function; the proof itself is choice-free.
- The affine halfspace example uses the convention $w\cdot x+b\ge0$ for label 1 and strict negativity for label 0. Its linear-dependence step is supported by the published finite-dimensional independent-set bound, the list/image independence lemma, and the explicit dependence definition.
- The B examples include TikZ sketches of threshold cuts, interval contiguity, an affine halfspace labeling, and a singleton indicator; each diagram is explained in the statement, and the verifications prove every claim independently of the figures.
- No minimax lower-bound claims were added. No ERM existence, measurability of an arbitrary selector, or computational-efficiency claims are inferred.
