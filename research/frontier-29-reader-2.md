## Confirmed defects and edits

### Repaired in-scope item defects

1. `items/thm-module-radical-is-jacobson-radical-times-the-module.md`
   - Confirmed defect on current bytes: step `1.1` tried to deduce
     `j(M/N)=0` from `aj\in J` by an invalid contradiction involving maximal
     left ideals. The argument did not actually construct the relevant maximal
     left ideal annihilating the simple quotient.
   - Repair made: replaced that step with the standard annihilator argument.
     For a maximal submodule `N<M` and any `m`, the surjective map
     `A -> M/N`, `a |-> am+N`, has maximal-left-ideal kernel whenever
     `m \notin N`; since `J(A)` lies in every maximal left ideal, `jm\in N`
     for all `j\in J(A)`, giving `J(A)M \subseteq rad(M)` cleanly.

2. `items/prop-additive-group-of-zp-is-torsion-free.md`
   - Confirmed defect on current bytes: the least-index argument referred to
     `x_{t-1}` when `t=1`, so the proof's exact-divisibility step was not
     well-formed at the first nonzero coordinate.
   - Repair made: rewrote the step to extract exact `p`-divisibility directly
     from compatibility and minimality of `t`, which handles `t=1` and the
     general case uniformly. I also tightened the item's direct dependency to
     the compatible-tuple definition it actually uses.

3. `items/prop-zp-is-topologically-generated-by-one.md`
   - Confirmed defect on current bytes: the witness
     `(1,1+p,1+p+p^2,\ldots)` fails for `p=2` because it is exactly `-1` in
     `\mathbb Z_2`, so it does not prove that the additive group is not
     abstractly cyclic. The printed congruence subtraction also did not yield a
     contradiction.
   - Repair made: replaced the witness with the even-digit tuple
     `\beta_n = \sum_{0 \le 2i < n} p^{2i} \bmod p^n`, proved it is
     compatible, and showed that an integer preimage would force
     `(1-p^2)m=1`, impossible for every prime `p`.
