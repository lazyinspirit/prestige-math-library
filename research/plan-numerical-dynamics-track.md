### P-NUM-ODE-01 — One-step ODE consistency and local error

**Pages:** one-step-ode-consistency-and-local-error / one-step-ode-consistency-and-local-error-examples. **Scope:** Define a one-step method $\Psi_h(t,y)$, local defect $\|\Psi_h(t,y(t))-y(t+h)\|$, and stability. For a fixed interval $[0,T]$ and exact solutions staying in a compact tube, state the order-$p$ hypothesis as a uniform local defect $\le Ch^{p+1}$ and the one-step stability bound $\|\Psi_h(t,u)-\Psi_h(t,v)\|\le(1+Lh)\|u-v\|$. Derive these premises for Euler when $f$ is $C^1$ in time and state with bounded derivatives on that tube; any named Runge–Kutta method must state its order conditions and required derivatives separately. **Proof route:** Taylor-expand the exact flow and the selected method increment, bound remainders uniformly on the tube, and prove the stated stability estimate; do not infer an order from the method label alone. **Examples:** Euler on a scalar linear field; a nonsmooth field where its local order drops. **Requires:** thm-existence-and-uniqueness-of-a-maximal-ode-solution, thm-chain-rule-for-total-derivatives, and a proved Taylor remainder estimate; the local error calculation is explicit, but a numerical-analysis source remains unaudited. **Sources:** Chen et al., neural-ODE Appendix B/C and numerical issues, PDF pp. 13–16, and Papamakarios et al., continuous-flow section pp. 31–33, discuss solver use but do not prove the error theorem. Acquire/read a matching numerical-ODE source before publication. This pair precedes P-NUM-ODE-02.

### P-NUM-ODE-02 — Global ODE and log-density integration error

**Pages:** global-ode-and-density-error / global-ode-and-density-error-examples. **Scope:** Under P-NUM-ODE-01’s uniform order-$p$ defect/stability bounds and a fixed finite interval, derive $\max_n\|y_n-y(t_n)\|\le C_T h^p$ by the discrete Grönwall recurrence. For a continuous normalizing flow augment the state by $\ell'=-\operatorname{div}f(x,t)$ and bound the log-density error separately. For Euler, $f\in C^2$ with bounded derivatives on a compact tube makes the augmented vector field locally Lipschitz and supports a local $O(h^2)$ defect; for order $p>1$, require the method-specific order conditions and enough bounded derivatives of the augmented field, or state a separate quadrature theorem with matching smoothness. $C^2$ regularity alone does not justify arbitrary order-$p$ convergence. State solver, quadrature, and state-error contributions separately. A state trajectory error alone does not control log-density or adjoint-gradient error. **Proof route:** derive $e_{n+1}\le(1+Lh)e_n+Ch^{p+1}$, iterate it, then apply a second bound to the augmented/log-density component under its own local defect and stability estimates. **Examples:** Euler global error for a linear flow; same state solver with a separately refined density quadrature. **Requires:** P-NUM-ODE-01 — One-step ODE consistency and local error; discrete Grönwall and the divergence-Lipschitz estimate, proved locally if no exact supplier exists. **Sources:** Chen et al. pp. 13–16 and Papamakarios et al. pp. 31–33 discuss solver use and numerical issues but do not prove these error theorems; the local defect/stability/Grönwall proof remains a clearly specified authoring obligation. Independence of finite outputs requires disjoint independent bit blocks; recycling bits changes dependence.

Give a counter-based stream interface as a deterministic keyed permutation of finite counters; a random permutation/key model or computational pseudorandomness assumption must be explicit, never an IID theorem. No cryptographic-quality claim or empirical-test proof of independence. Non-overlapping blocks avoid exact reuse only within certified period; do not imply probabilistic independence.
