## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]). Let $k$ be a field and let $R,S$ be finite-dimensional unital $k$-algebras, with finite-dimensional left module categories $R\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$, $S\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ ([[def-algebra-over-a-commutative-ring]], [[def-dimension]], [[def-left-and-right-modules]]). Put $T=R\otimes_kS$ ([[thm-tensor-product-of-algebras-over-a-commutative-ring]]). (i) The functor $\boxtimes:R\text{-}\mathrm{mod}\times S\text{-}\mathrm{mod}\to T\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ ([[def-product-category]]), $(X,Y)\mapsto X\otimes_kY$ with $(r\otimes s)(x\otimes y)=rx\otimes sy$ ([[thm-universal-property-of-module-tensor-products]], [[thm-bimodule-actions-induced-on-tensor-products]], [[prop-functoriality-of-module-tensor-products]]), is $k$-linear in each variable ([[def-k-linear-category-and-k-linear-functor]]) and right exact in each variable ([[def-left-exact-and-right-exact-functor]]). (ii) For every $k$-linear abelian category $\mathcal E$ ([[def-abelian-category]]), restriction along $\boxtimes$ is an equivalence of categories from $k$-linear right exact functors $T\text{-}\mathrm{mod}\to\mathcal E$ with all natural transformations to $k$-linear functors $R\text{-}\mathrm{mod}\times S\text{-}\mathrm{mod}\to\mathcal E$ right exact in each variable with all natural transformations ([[def-functor-category]], [[def-natural-transformation]], [[def-equivalence-and-adjoint-equivalence-of-categories]]); a quasi-inverse sends $H$ to the functor $\bar H$ built from $W=H(R,S)$ and its right $T$-action as in [[lem-bilinear-right-exact-functors-are-determined-by-the-pair-of-regular-modules]]. (iii) Consequently $T\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ together with $\boxtimes$ is a Deligne product of $R\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ and $S\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ in the sense of [[def-deligne-product-of-finite-linear-categories]]: it is finite $k$-linear abelian ([[def-finite-k-linear-abelian-category]], [[prop-finite-dimensional-module-categories-are-intrinsically-finite]], [[thm-dimension-formula]]), and the universal property holds. For abstract finite $k$-linear abelian categories, transporting along chosen module models ([[thm-finite-abelian-categories-are-finite-dimensional-module-categories]]) yields a Deligne product, well defined up to an equivalence respecting the universal bifunctors. No commutativity of $R$ or $S$ is assumed. AC is used for the set-indexed presentation and universal-object selections, as in the determination lemma.

All module sources and functor categories use chosen small representatives; transport along supplied module equivalences is understood.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The Axiom of Choice ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]), a field $k$ and finite-dimensional unital $k$-algebras $R,S$, with $T=R\otimes_kS$; $R\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$, $S\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ and $T\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ denote the finite-dimensional left module categories, and $\mathcal E$ is a $k$-linear abelian category.

[F1] The tensor product $X\otimes_kY$ of $X\in R\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ and $Y\in S\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ carries the outer actions induced from the two factors, and the two commute, giving the left action $(r\otimes s)(x\otimes y)=rx\otimes sy$ of the $k$-algebra $T=R\otimes_kS$ ([[thm-bimodule-actions-induced-on-tensor-products]], [[thm-tensor-product-of-algebras-over-a-commutative-ring]]).

[F2] The tensor product is functorial in both variables: $(f,g)\mapsto f\otimes g$ is the unique homomorphism with $(f\otimes g)(m\otimes n)=f(m)\otimes g(n)$, and it satisfies $\operatorname{id}\otimes\operatorname{id}=\operatorname{id}$ and $(f'f)\otimes(g'g)=(f'\otimes g')(f\otimes g)$ ([[prop-functoriality-of-module-tensor-products]]). For every abelian group $A$ and balanced map $b$ there is a unique homomorphism out of the tensor product factoring $b$ ([[thm-universal-property-of-module-tensor-products]]).

[F3] The determination lemma: for a $k$-linear bifunctor $H$ right exact in each variable with $W=H(R,S)$, the finite presentations of $T\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ construct a functor $\bar H$ with a natural isomorphism $\bar H(X\otimes_kY)\cong H(X,Y)$, and every natural transformation of such bifunctors is determined by its component at $(R,S)$, the assignment being a bijection onto the compatible maps of right $T$-modules ([[lem-bilinear-right-exact-functors-are-determined-by-the-pair-of-regular-modules]]).

[F4] For every ring $A$ the category of left $A$-modules is abelian ([[thm-modules-over-a-ring-form-an-abelian-category]]); for a finite-dimensional $k$-algebra $A$ the category $A\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ of finite-dimensional left modules is a finite $k$-linear abelian category in the intrinsic sense ([[prop-finite-dimensional-module-categories-are-intrinsically-finite]], [[def-finite-k-linear-abelian-category]]).

[F5] For an intrinsic finite category, the module-model theorem constructs a $k$-linear fully faithful, exact, essentially surjective functor $H:\mathcal C\to A\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$, and makes it an equivalence when a splitting of essential surjectivity is supplied ([[thm-finite-abelian-categories-are-finite-dimensional-module-categories]]). On a chosen small module source, the stated AC assumption selects that splitting: collection bounds a set of preimage objects and isomorphisms, and AC chooses one per target module. Full faithfulness then gives the quasi-inverse uniquely on morphisms ([[def-axiom-of-choice]], [[thm-fully-faithful-split-essentially-surjective-characterises-equivalence]]).

[F6] Dimension: with a finite basis $(x_1,\dots,x_m)$ of $X$ the universal property of [F2] identifies $X\otimes_kY$ with the finite direct sum of $m$ copies of $Y$, so $\dim_k(X\otimes_kY)=\dim_kX\cdot\dim_kY$, and likewise $\dim_kT=\dim_kR\cdot\dim_kS$, using the dimension formula and its boundary case $\dim_F(U\oplus W)=\dim_FU+\dim_FW$ ([[thm-dimension-formula]], [[def-dimension]]).

[F7] The cokernel $q:B\to C$ of $f:A\to B$ is universal with $qf=0$: every $h$ with $hf=0$ factors uniquely through $q$, and dually for kernels ([[def-kernels-and-cokernels-as-equalizers-and-coequalizers]]); in an abelian category every morphism has a kernel and a cokernel ([[def-abelian-category]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** constructive.

1.1 By [F1] the tensor product $X\otimes_kY$ carries commuting left actions of $R$ and $S$, hence the left $T$-action $(r\otimes s)(x\otimes y)=rx\otimes sy$ of the $k$-algebra $T=R\otimes_kS$ ([[def-algebra-over-a-commutative-ring]]), and it is functorial in both variables with the identity and composition laws [F2]; choosing a finite basis of $X$ identifies $X\otimes_kY$ with a finite direct sum of copies of $Y$ by the universal property of [F2], so $\dim_k(X\otimes_kY)=\dim_kX\cdot\dim_kY$ [F6] and $\boxtimes$ lands in the finite-dimensional category $T\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ ([[def-dimension]]). Each partial functor is $k$-linear because additivity and scalar multiplication are read off on generators, where $(f+f')\otimes1=f\otimes1+f'\otimes1$ and $(\lambda f)\otimes1=\lambda(f\otimes1)$, and the maps are determined by their values on generators [F2]; hence $(X,Y)\mapsto X\otimes_kY$ is $k$-linear in each variable ([[def-k-linear-category-and-k-linear-functor]], [[def-product-category]]). [given, F1, F2, F6]

2.1 For fixed $Y$ the functor $X\mapsto X\otimes_kY$ preserves cokernels: if $q:X\to X'$ is the cokernel of $f:X''\to X$, so that $\ker q=\operatorname{im}f$ ([[def-exact-and-short-exact-sequences-of-modules]], [[def-module-homomorphism-kernel-image-and-cokernel]]), then $q\otimes1$ is surjective with $\operatorname{im}(f\otimes1)\subseteq\ker(q\otimes1)$, and the balanced map sending $(x',y)$ with $q(x)=x'$ to the class of $x\otimes y$ is well defined because representatives differ by an element of $\ker q=\operatorname{im}f$, and it induces a two-sided inverse of the map on generators [F2]; hence $\ker(q\otimes1)=\operatorname{im}(f\otimes1)$. The functor is additive by the same generator computation, so it preserves finite coproducts and cokernels and therefore every finite colimit, that is, it is right exact ([[def-left-exact-and-right-exact-functor]], [[thm-an-additive-functor-preserves-finite-biproducts]]); the argument in the first variable is identical, so $\boxtimes$ is right exact in each variable. [step 1.1, F2, F7]

3.1 Let $G,G':T\text{-}\mathrm{mod}\to\mathcal E$ be $k$-linear and right exact. Both $G\circ\boxtimes$ and $G'\circ\boxtimes$ are $k$-linear and right exact in each variable by step 2.1, so [F3] applies to the pair $(G\circ\boxtimes,G'\circ\boxtimes)$ and gives a bijection $\operatorname{Nat}(G\circ\boxtimes,G'\circ\boxtimes)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_T(G(T),G'(T))$ onto right $T$-module maps, by evaluation at $(R,S)$. Applying [F3] with the second algebra $k$ to the bifunctors $(Z,V)\mapsto G(Z\otimes_kV)$ and $(Z,V)\mapsto G'(Z\otimes_kV)$, whose value at $(T,k)$ is $G(T\otimes_kk)\cong G(T)$, gives in the same way a bijection $\operatorname{Nat}(G,G')\cong\operatorname{Hom}_T(G(T),G'(T))$: injectivity because the component at $(Z,k)$ recovers $\eta_Z$ under the natural isomorphism $Z\otimes_kk\cong Z$, and surjectivity because the extension of a right $T$-module map constructed by [F3] is natural and has the prescribed component. Since $(\eta\circ\boxtimes)_{R,S}=\eta_T$, composition with $\boxtimes$ identifies these two bijections, so restriction along $\boxtimes$ is fully faithful. [step 2.1, F3]

4.1 Let $H$ be a $k$-linear bifunctor right exact in each variable and let $\bar H$ be the functor constructed from $W=H(R,S)$ in [F3]. Write $K$ for the functor on finite free $T$-modules used in that construction. Then $\bar H$ is $k$-linear: if $g_u,g_v$ are lifts of $u,v:Z\to Z'$ between the chosen presentations, then $g_u+g_v$ lifts $u+v$ and the induced map on cokernels is additive in the lift by the uniqueness in [F7], so $\bar H(u+v)=\bar H(u)+\bar H(v)$, and $g_{\lambda u}=\lambda g_u$ gives $\bar H(\lambda u)=\lambda\bar H(u)$ similarly. It is right exact: for a presentation $T^m\xrightarrow{\delta}T^n\to Z\to0$ the universal property of the cokernel [F7] gives a bijection $\mathcal E(\bar H(Z),E')\cong\{h:W^n\to E':hK(\delta)=0\}$ natural in $E'$, and the right-hand side is the set of $T$-linear maps $Z\to\mathcal E(W,E')$ under the left $T$-action $(t\cdot\varphi)(w)=\varphi(w\cdot t)$ on the hom-object, a map $h$ corresponding to the family of its components $h\,i_j:W\to E'$ and the condition $hK(\delta)=0$ saying exactly that this family annihilates $\delta(T^m)$; hence a cokernel sequence $Z''\to Z\to Z'\to0$ in $T\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ gives exact sequences $0\to\mathcal E(\bar H(Z'),E')\to\mathcal E(\bar H(Z),E')\to\mathcal E(\bar H(Z''),E')$ for all $E'$, which by the characterization in [F7] says that $\bar H(Z'')\to\bar H(Z)\to\bar H(Z')$ is again a cokernel sequence. So $\bar H$ preserves cokernels and, being additive, all finite colimits, that is, it is right exact ([[def-left-exact-and-right-exact-functor]], [[thm-an-additive-functor-preserves-finite-biproducts]], [[def-abelian-category]]). By [F3] there is a natural isomorphism $\bar H\circ\boxtimes\cong H$. Fix presentation data once on the small source. The construction on compatible action maps in the determination lemma then makes $H\mapsto\bar H$ a functor, with $\bar H\circ\boxtimes\cong H$ natural in $H$. Full faithfulness from step 3.1 lifts this comparison uniquely to $\overline{G\circ\boxtimes}\cong G$, natural in $G$, so restriction and this functor are quasi-inverse, giving an equivalence of categories ([[def-equivalence-and-adjoint-equivalence-of-categories]], [[def-functor-category]], [[def-natural-transformation]], [[rem-category-theory-class-and-size-conventions]]). [step 3.1, F3, F7, construct]

5.1 By [F4] $T\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ is an abelian $k$-linear category, and because $\dim_kT=\dim_kR\cdot\dim_kS$ is finite [F6] while the module category of a finite-dimensional $k$-algebra is intrinsically finite [F4], $T\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ is a finite $k$-linear abelian category in the sense of [[def-finite-k-linear-abelian-category]]; the equivalence of step 4.1, required for every $k$-linear abelian $\mathcal E$, is exactly the universal property of [[def-deligne-product-of-finite-linear-categories]], so $T\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ with $\boxtimes$ is a Deligne product of $R\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ and $S\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$. For abstract finite $k$-linear abelian categories $\mathcal A,\mathcal B$, [F5] provides equivalences $\mathcal A\simeq R\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$, $\mathcal B\simeq S\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ with $R,S$ finite-dimensional, and transporting $\boxtimes$, the finite direct sums, the algebra $T$ and the universal property along them yields a Deligne product of $\mathcal A$ and $\mathcal B$, well defined up to an equivalence respecting the universal bifunctors as follows directly from the universal property: for two products $P,Q$, extend their universal bifunctors to right exact functors $P\to Q$ and $Q\to P$. The restrictions of both composites are isomorphic to the corresponding universal bifunctors, so full faithfulness of restriction lifts these isomorphisms to composites isomorphic to the identities. No commutativity of $R$ or $S$ was used. The construction inherits the set-indexed choices of [F3], made under the stated AC assumption. [step 4.1, F4, F5, F6, discharge-construct] ∎
