## Statement

The graded $\mathbb C$-algebra $M_*=\bigoplus_{k\ even}M_k$ is freely generated by $E_4$ and $E_6$: the substitution $X\mapsto E_4$, $Y\mapsto E_6$ defines an isomorphism of graded $\mathbb C$-algebras $\mathbb C[X,Y]\to M_*$, where $\deg X=4$ and $\deg Y=6$. Equivalently, for every even $k\ge0$ the forms $E_4^aE_6^b$ with $4a+6b=k$, $a,b\ge0$, form a basis of $M_k$, and the cusp forms form the principal ideal $S_*=\Delta M_*$; explicitly $S_k=\Delta M_{k-12}$ for all $k\ge0$ (with $M_{k-12}=0$ for $k<12$).

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The graded spaces $M_k,S_k$ and the Eisenstein forms $E_4,E_6$ with $E_4(\infty)=E_6(\infty)=1$, together with $\Delta=(E_4^3-E_6^2)/1728\in S_{12}$ of order one at the cusp and without zeros on $\mathfrak H$ ([[def-level-one-modular-form-and-cusp-form]], [[thm-eisenstein-series-are-modular-forms]], [[lem-discriminant-is-a-nonvanishing-cusp-form]], [[def-modular-discriminant-and-j-invariant]]).

[F1] For even $k$ the monomials $E_4^aE_6^b$ with $4a+6b=k$ are linearly independent in $M_k$, and their number equals $\dim M_k$ from the dimension formula; every such monomial has value $1$ at the cusp ([[cor-dimension-of-level-one-modular-forms]], [[thm-eisenstein-series-are-modular-forms]]).

[F2] A nonzero weight-$k$ form has a well-defined constant term $f(\infty)$ at the cusp, and $S_k$ is exactly the kernel of $f\mapsto f(\infty)$ ([[def-level-one-modular-form-and-cusp-form]]).

[F3] $\Delta$ has vanishing order $1$ at the cusp and no zeros on $\mathfrak H$; therefore $f\mapsto f/\Delta$ maps $S_k$ bijectively onto $M_{k-12}$ for every even $k$: it is holomorphic on $\mathfrak H$, has the correct weight, and at the cusp the order drops by exactly $1$; conversely $\Delta h\in S_k$ for $h\in M_{k-12}$ ([[lem-discriminant-is-a-nonvanishing-cusp-form]], [[def-modular-discriminant-and-j-invariant]], [[thm-level-one-valence-formula]], [[cor-zeros-of-e4-and-e6-at-the-elliptic-points]]).

[F4] For every even $k\ge4$ there is a monomial of weight $k$ (and for $k=0$ the empty monomial $1$); $M_2=0$ and $M_{k}$ is one-dimensional for $k=0,4,6,8,10$ ([[cor-dimension-of-level-one-modular-forms]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** induction on the weight $k$ in steps of $12$.

1.1 Base cases: for $k=0$, $M_0=\mathbb C$ consists of constants and the empty monomial $1$ has weight $0$; for $k=2$, $M_2=0$; for $k=4,6,8,10$ the space $M_k$ is one-dimensional and contains the monomial $E_4,E_6,E_4^2,E_4E_6$ respectively, whose value at the cusp is $1$, so every $f\in M_k$ equals $f(\infty)$ times that monomial. Hence in these degrees the monomials span $M_k$. [base, F1, F2, F4, given, algebra]

1.2 Assume, as induction hypothesis, that for some even $k\ge12$ the weight-$(k-12)$ monomials span $M_{k-12}$; equivalently every element of $M_{k-12}$ is a polynomial in $E_4,E_6$. [ih, given]

2.1 Let $f\in M_k$ and choose a monomial $P=E_4^aE_6^b$ of weight $k$ [F4]; then $P(\infty)=1$ and $f-f(\infty)P\in M_k$ has zero constant term at the cusp, so $f-f(\infty)P\in S_k$ [F2]. By [F3] there is $h\in M_{k-12}$ with $f-f(\infty)P=\Delta h$; by the induction hypothesis 1.2 the form $h$ is a polynomial in $E_4,E_6$, so $f=f(\infty)P+\Delta h$ is a polynomial in $E_4,E_6$ (using $\Delta=\frac{1}{1728}(E_4^3-E_6^2)$). Hence the monomials span $M_k$ for every even $k\ge0$. [F2, F3, F4, step 1.1, step 1.2, given, algebra]

3.1 The graded algebra map $\varphi:\mathbb C[X,Y]\to M_*$ with $\varphi(X)=E_4$, $\varphi(Y)=E_6$ is well defined because $E_4^aE_6^b\in M_{4a+6b}$ and multiplication of modular forms adds weights. In degree $k$ the source is spanned by the monomials of weight $k$, whose images are linearly independent and as numerous as $\dim M_k$ [F1]; since they also span $M_k$ by the induction result, $\varphi$ is an isomorphism in each degree and hence an isomorphism of graded algebras. Therefore the monomials form a basis and $E_4,E_6$ are algebraically independent. Finally [F3] gives $S_k=\Delta M_{k-12}$ for every $k$, that is $S_*=\Delta M_*$. [F1, F3, step 2.1, discharge-induction] ∎
