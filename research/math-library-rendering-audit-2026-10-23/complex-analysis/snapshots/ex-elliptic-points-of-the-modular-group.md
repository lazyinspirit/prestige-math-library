## Example

In $PSL_2(\mathbb Z)\backslash\mathfrak H$ there are exactly two elliptic classes: the class of $i$, with stabiliser of order $2$ generated by $S$, and the class of $\omega=e^{2\pi i/3}$, with stabiliser of order $3$ generated by $ST$; every other stabiliser is trivial. The corresponding orbifold points have orders $2$ and $3$; the quotient map $\mathfrak H\to X(1)$ has local degrees $2$ at $i$ and $3$ at $\omega$, and $j(i)=1728$, $j(\omega)=0$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The action of $G=PSL_2(\mathbb Z)$ on $\mathfrak H$ with $S\cdot z=-1/z$, $T\cdot z=z+1$ and the closure $\overline D=\{\tau:|\Re\tau|\le1/2,\ |\tau|\ge1\}$ of the standard fundamental domain ([[thm-standard-fundamental-domain-for-the-modular-group]]); the quotient map $\pi:\mathfrak H\to Y(1)\subset X(1)$ with its local charts at the elliptic points, where it is $z\mapsto z^\nu$ in a centred coordinate ([[lem-modular-quotient-local-charts]], [[thm-j-uniformizes-the-level-one-modular-curve]]); the modular function $j=E_4^3/\Delta$ with $\Delta=(E_4^3-E_6^2)/1728$ nonvanishing on $\mathfrak H$ ([[def-modular-discriminant-and-j-invariant]]).

[F1] Every $G$-orbit meets $\overline D$; the only points of $\overline D$ with nontrivial $G$-stabiliser are $i$, $\omega$ and $\omega+1$, with $\operatorname{Stab}(i)=\langle S\rangle$ of order $2$, $\operatorname{Stab}(\omega)=\langle ST\rangle$ of order $3$ and $\operatorname{Stab}(\omega+1)=\langle TS\rangle$ of order $3$, while every other point of $\overline D$ has trivial stabiliser; $T\cdot\omega=\omega+1$ ([[thm-standard-fundamental-domain-for-the-modular-group]], [[lem-modular-quotient-local-charts]]).

[F2] In the quotient chart at an elliptic point a generator of the stabiliser acts by $z\mapsto e^{2\pi i/\nu}z$ and $\pi$ becomes the map $z\mapsto z^\nu$; for $G$ this is $\nu=2$ at $i$ and $\nu=3$ at $\omega$ and $\omega+1$, so $\pi$ has local degree $2$ at $i$ and $3$ at $\omega$ and $\omega+1$, representatives of the two elliptic classes; the same local degrees hold at all their modular translates ([[lem-modular-quotient-local-charts]], [[thm-j-uniformizes-the-level-one-modular-curve]]).

[F3] $E_4$ has a simple zero at the class of $\omega$ and no other zeros, and $E_6$ has a simple zero at the class of $i$ and no other zeros; in particular $E_6(i)=0$, $E_4(i)\ne0$, $E_4(\omega)=0$ and $E_6(\omega)\ne0$ ([[cor-zeros-of-e4-and-e6-at-the-elliptic-points]]).

[F4] $\Delta=(E_4^3-E_6^2)/1728$ has no zeros on $\mathfrak H$ and $j=E_4^3/\Delta$ is a holomorphic $G$-invariant function with $j(i)=1728$ and $j(\omega)=0$ ([[def-modular-discriminant-and-j-invariant]]).

## Verification

1.1 Exactly two elliptic classes. Let $\tau\in\mathfrak H$ have nontrivial stabiliser in $G$. By [F1] there is $\gamma\in G$ with $\gamma\cdot\tau\in\overline D$, and $\operatorname{Stab}(\gamma\cdot\tau)=\gamma\operatorname{Stab}(\tau)\gamma^{-1}$ is then nontrivial, so $\gamma\cdot\tau\in\{i,\omega,\omega+1\}$ by [F1]. Since $T\cdot\omega=\omega+1$, every point with nontrivial stabiliser is $G$-equivalent to $i$ or to $\omega$. The classes of $i$ and $\omega$ are distinct: if $\omega=\gamma\cdot i$, then conjugation would give $\operatorname{Stab}(\omega)=\gamma\operatorname{Stab}(i)\gamma^{-1}$, a group of order $2$, whereas $\operatorname{Stab}(\omega)=\langle ST\rangle$ has order $3$ by [F1]. So there are exactly two elliptic classes, the classes of $i$ and $\omega$, and their stabilisers are of orders $2$ and $3$ generated by $S$ and $ST$; every point outside these two classes has trivial stabiliser, since a point with nontrivial stabiliser is equivalent to $i$ or $\omega$ and all other points of $\overline D$ have trivial stabiliser [F1]. [F1, given, algebra]

2.1 By [F2], the quotient map has local degree $2$ at $i$ and $3$ at $\omega$ and $\omega+1$. For every $\gamma\in G$, $\pi\circ\gamma=\pi$ and $\gamma$ is a biholomorphism, so the local degree is unchanged at $\gamma i$ or $\gamma\omega$. Thus its ramification locus is exactly $G\cdot i\cup G\cdot\omega$, with local degrees $2$ and $3$ respectively; outside these orbits the stabiliser is trivial by 1.1 and the quotient chart is a local inverse of $\pi$, giving degree $1$. [F2, step 1.1, given, algebra]

3.1 Special values. At $\tau=i$, [F3] gives $E_6(i)=0$ and $E_4(i)\ne0$, so $\Delta(i)=(E_4(i)^3-0)/1728=E_4(i)^3/1728\ne0$ and $j(i)=E_4(i)^3/\Delta(i)=1728$. At $\tau=\omega$, [F3] gives $E_4(\omega)=0$, so $j(\omega)=0/\Delta(\omega)=0$, the denominator being nonzero by [F4]; the same two values are recorded in [F4]. [F3, F4, given, algebra] ∎
