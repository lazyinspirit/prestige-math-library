The corollary [[cor-single-valued-continuation-on-simply-connected-domains]]
repackages the earlier logarithm existence theorem on simply connected plane
domains in continuation language. A nonvanishing holomorphic function determines
an initial local logarithm germ, and once that germ continues along every path,
monodromy promotes it to a single-valued holomorphic logarithm.

This does not widen the earlier result
[[thm-holomorphic-logarithms-homologically-simply-connected-domains]]. Instead
it gives a second route to the same conclusion under the simply connected
hypothesis and agrees with the earlier CA-17 synthesis recorded in
[[rem-analytic-equivalences-from-global-cauchy-theory]] and
[[thm-grand-equivalence-for-simply-connected-plane-domains]].
