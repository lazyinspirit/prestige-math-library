## Definition

Let $\Phi$ be the root system from [[thm-the-root-set-is-a-reduced-crystallographic-root-system]]. For a root $\alpha$, define the corresponding coroot by

$$\alpha^\vee:=\frac{2H_\alpha}{\alpha(H_\alpha)} \in \mathfrak h,$$

where $H_\alpha$ is the vector from [[def-killing-dual-vector-attached-to-a-root]]. The associated **root reflection** is the linear map

$$s_\alpha(\lambda):=\lambda-\lambda(\alpha^\vee)\alpha \qquad (\lambda\in \mathfrak h^*).$$

The subgroup of $\operatorname{GL}(\mathfrak h^*)$ generated by these reflections is the **Weyl group** $W$, acting on $\mathfrak h^*$ in the usual way.
