## Definition

Let $V$ be a representation of the Virasoro algebra [[def-virasoro-algebra]]. A nonzero vector $v\in V$ is a **highest-weight vector of weight $h\in\mathbb C$ and central charge $c\in\mathbb C$** if $L_n v=0$ for every $n>0$, $L_0v=hv$, and $Cv=cv$. The **highest-weight module generated by $v$** is the submodule spanned by all finite words in the Virasoro generators applied to $v$. This definition asserts neither irreducibility nor unitarity; quotienting the generated module by a proper submodule is an additional choice.
