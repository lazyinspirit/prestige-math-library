## Definition

Let $(V,B)$ be a finite-dimensional nondegenerate real quadratic space with $Q(v)=B(v,v)$, and let $\mathrm{Cl}(V,Q)$ use $v^2=Q(v)1$. Use the grading involution $\alpha$ and Clifford conjugation $\bar x$ from [[lem-real-clifford-involutions-and-norm]]. Let $\mathrm{Cl}^0$ and $\mathrm{Cl}^1$ be the even and odd parts for word-length parity. Define the **unrestricted twisted Clifford normalizer** as the set

$$\Gamma(V,B)=\{x\in\mathrm{Cl}(V,Q)^\times:\alpha(x)v x^{-1}\in V\text{ for every }v\in V\},\qquad \rho_x(v)=\alpha(x)v x^{-1}.$$

Define the normalized anisotropic vectors $U(V,Q)=\{u\in V:|Q(u)|=1\}$. For $\dim V>0$, **Pin$(V,Q)$** is the subgroup of $\mathrm{Cl}(V,Q)^\times$ generated by $U(V,Q)$; for $V=0$, set Pin$(0)=\{1,-1\}$. Define

$$\operatorname{Spin}_{\mathrm{full}}(V,Q)=\operatorname{Pin}(V,Q)\cap\mathrm{Cl}^0,\qquad \operatorname{Spin}_{N=1}(V,Q)=\{x\in\operatorname{Spin}_{\mathrm{full}}(V,Q):N(x)=1\}.$$

For definite signature, the conventional names Pin$^+(n)$ and Pin$^-(n)$ mean the groups in $\mathrm{Cl}_{n,0}$ and $\mathrm{Cl}_{0,n}$, respectively. In mixed signature the subscript “full” is retained because the full even subgroup and the norm-one subgroup can have different images.

## Remarks

The unrestricted normalizer's closure and homogeneity are local proof obligations in this pair. The scalar $-1$ is included explicitly in dimension zero.
