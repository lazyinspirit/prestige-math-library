## Statement

For the reduced based root datum and wall definitions of [[def-hh-affine-hecke-algebra-from-root-datum]], $W_{\mathrm{aff}}=Q^\vee\rtimes W_0$ is the Coxeter group on the base alcove's wall reflections, acts simply transitively on alcoves, and has length equal to the number of separating walls. The extended group $Y\rtimes W_0$ preserves the wall arrangement and equals $W_{\mathrm{aff}}\rtimes\Omega$, where $\Omega$ is the alcove stabilizer. These claims include empty roots and central lattice directions. Every affine wall reflection is conjugate to a simple affine wall reflection. If the positive parameters on simple walls are constant on extended conjugacy classes, they therefore give a well-defined parameter $v(H)>0$ on every wall. For simple $\alpha$, put $v_\alpha=v(H_{\alpha,0})$. The parameter of $H_{\alpha,j}$ is $v_\alpha$ for even $j$ and $v'_\alpha:=v(H_{\alpha,1})$ for odd $j$; if $\alpha\notin2X$, these two values agree.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A reduced based root datum $R$, its positive affine parameters, and the canonical walls.

[F1] [[def-abstract-root-datum-and-its-weyl-group]] gives perfect lattice duality and finite Weyl action by the stated root reflections.

[F2] [[lem-root-datum-combinatorics]] identifies the essential root/coroot data with reduced crystallographic Euclidean root systems, and finite simple reflections generate $W_0$.

[F3] [[def-cg-affine-root-hyperplane-reflection-and-alcove]] fixes the Euclidean affine reflection and coroot translation conventions.

[F4] [[thm-cg-affine-alcove-transitivity-presentation-and-length]] proves the canonical affine Coxeter presentation, simple transitivity, wall conjugacy, and separating-wall length for a supplied essential Euclidean root system.

[F5] [[def-hh-affine-hecke-algebra-from-root-datum]] uses conjugacy-invariant positive simple-wall parameters.

## Proof

1.1 Average any inner product on $V=Y\otimes\mathbb R$ over the finite group $W_0$. Each reflection is then orthogonal, has normal line $\mathbb R\alpha^\vee$, and fixes $\ker\alpha$, so $\langle\alpha,v\rangle=2(v,\alpha^\vee)/(\alpha^\vee,\alpha^\vee)$. Put $a_\alpha=2\alpha^\vee/(\alpha^\vee,\alpha^\vee)$. Its metric coroot is exactly $\alpha^\vee$, and $(v,a_\alpha)=\langle\alpha,v\rangle$. Thus [F2] identifies the $a_\alpha$ as a reduced crystallographic root system on $E=\mathbb R\Phi^\vee$, with the chosen positive system. The orthogonal complement $C=E^\perp$ is exactly the common root kernel and is fixed by $W_0$. Its presence adds a Cartesian factor $C$ to every alcove and changes neither the reflecting walls nor their combinatorics on $E$. Apply [F3]–[F4] on $E$ to obtain the asserted Coxeter system, simple transitivity and length for $Q^\vee\rtimes W_0$ on $V=E\oplus C$. The base alcove is equivalently $0<\langle\alpha,v\rangle<1$ for all positive $\alpha$: no integer wall cuts this region; every alcove in the positive chamber touching $0$ must satisfy these inequalities, and the supplied fundamental-alcove theorem identifies it with this region. [F1, F2, F3, F4, given, algebra]

2.1 Every translation by $y\in Y$ sends $H_{\alpha,j}$ to $H_{\alpha,j+\langle\alpha,y\rangle}$ and every Weyl element permutes root directions, so $W_{\mathrm e}$ preserves the arrangement. Its conjugation preserves the group generated by all wall reflections, namely $W_{\mathrm{aff}}$, hence that subgroup is normal. For $h\in W_{\mathrm e}$, simple transitivity gives a unique $w\in W_{\mathrm{aff}}$ with $wA=hA$; then $w^{-1}h\in\Omega$. The intersection is trivial by simple transitivity, proving the semidirect decomposition. Separating-wall length agrees with Coxeter length on the affine subgroup and vanishes exactly on $\Omega$. If the roots are empty, $E=0$, $A=V$, $W_0=W_{\mathrm{aff}}=1$, and $\Omega=Y$, so every assertion still holds. [F1, F4, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Every wall is a facet wall of some alcove by [F4]. Write that alcove as $wA$ with $w\in W_{\mathrm{aff}}$; its reflection is $wsw^{-1}$ for a unique wall type $s$ of $A$. If two such descriptions give $wsw^{-1}=utu^{-1}$, then $s$ and $t$ are conjugate in $W_{\mathrm e}$, so [F5] gives the same parameter. This defines $v(H)$ independently of the alcove used and makes it invariant under $W_{\mathrm e}$. Translation by $\alpha^\vee$ changes the integer level by $2$, so only parity can matter for fixed $\alpha$. If $\alpha\notin2X$, perfect duality supplies $y\in Y$ with $\langle\alpha,y\rangle$ odd: otherwise evaluating on a lattice basis would put $\alpha/2$ in $X$. Translation by that $y$ identifies odd and even walls, forcing equal parameters. If $\alpha\in2X$, all pairings are even, and the formulas asserted are exactly the two potentially distinct parity values. The argument uses finitely many basis vectors and a finite Weyl average, without a choice axiom. [F1, F4, F5, step 1.1, step 2.1, algebra] ∎
