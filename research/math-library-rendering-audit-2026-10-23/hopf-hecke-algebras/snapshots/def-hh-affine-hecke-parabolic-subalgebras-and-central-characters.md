## Definition

Let $H$ be the complex affine Hecke algebra of a reduced based root datum with positive real parameters and Bernstein PBW as in [[thm-hh-affine-hecke-general-root-data-bernstein-pbw]]. To keep the page's dual convention explicit, put $L=Y$, $B=\Delta^\vee\subset L$, and write $\beta^\vee=\alpha\in X$ when $\beta=\alpha^\vee\in B$. Thus $A=\mathbb C[L]$ is the Bernstein subalgebra, $T=\operatorname{Hom}(L,\mathbb C^\times)$, and $E=\operatorname{Hom}(L,\mathbb R)=X\otimes\mathbb R$. The Weyl group $W=W_0$ acts on all these objects. Index parabolic subsets by $P\subseteq B$; they correspond bijectively to subsets of $\Delta$ by $\alpha\leftrightarrow\alpha^\vee$.

Write $W_P=\langle s_\beta:\beta\in P\rangle$, and let $H_P$ denote the subalgebra of $H$ generated by $A$ and $N_{s_\beta}$ for $\beta\in P$. In particular $H_\varnothing=A$ and $H_B=H$. The notation $H_P$ here always retains the full lattice $L$. For an $H_P$-module $V$, its **parabolic induction** is $I_P(V)=H\otimes_{H_P}V$ with $H$ acting on the first factor.

All modules in the classification below are nonzero, unital, complex and finite dimensional. For an $A$-module $V$ and $t\in T$, the **generalized weight space** is
$$
V_t=\{v\in V:(\theta_\ell-t(\ell))^{\dim V}v=0\text{ for every }\ell\in L\}.
$$
The **weight set** is $\operatorname{Wt}(V)=\{t:V_t\ne0\}$. The logarithmic absolute value $\lambda(t)\in E$ is $\lambda(t)(\ell)=\log|t(\ell)|$. For an irreducible $H_P$-module, the **central weight character** $\operatorname{cc}_P(V)$ is its $W_P$-orbit of $A$-weights; the fact that there is exactly one such orbit is proved in [[lem-hh-affine-hecke-parabolic-weight-filtration]]. This terminology specifies the character of $A^{W_P}$; no equality with a larger center is needed.
