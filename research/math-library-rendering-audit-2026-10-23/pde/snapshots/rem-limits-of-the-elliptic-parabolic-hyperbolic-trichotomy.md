## Remark

The names on this page combine related but differently formulated pointwise
notions: ellipticity and strict hyperbolicity are conditions on real scalar
second-order principal symbols, whereas the stated parabolic condition is for a
space-time operator with a first-order time derivative and a semidefinite
spatial quadratic form. These notions do not by themselves give a global
taxonomy of equations that change type from point to point, systems with
matrix-valued principal symbols, higher-order equations, or fully nonlinear
equations whose leading behavior is not a single quadratic form.
