## Remarks

The projective hierarchy quantifies over reals with arithmetic matrices. The set-theoretic Lévy hierarchy quantifies over sets with bounded membership matrices. A mention of Shoenfield absoluteness is therefore not an application of this page's $\Sigma_1$ upward theorem.

Compare [[def-set-theoretic-levy-hierarchy-and-absoluteness]] and [[thm-sigma-one-upward-and-pi-one-downward-absoluteness]]. Kamensky, §5.2, pp.45–47, explains the distinct real-coding problem; its printed Shoenfield sketch omits a coding argument. Additional descriptive-set-theoretic coding and absoluteness machinery is required before that result could be a supplier. No Shoenfield theorem is asserted or used here.
