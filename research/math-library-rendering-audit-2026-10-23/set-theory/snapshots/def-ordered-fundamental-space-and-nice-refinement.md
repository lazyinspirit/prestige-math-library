## Definition

A **fundamental space** is a Hausdorff space $(X,\mathcal T)$ of cardinality
$\aleph_1$ that is zero-dimensional, separable, first countable, and
$\aleph_1$-dense (every nonempty open set has size $\aleph_1$), together with
strictly decreasing clopen local bases

$$X=W_0^x\supsetneq W_1^x\supsetneq W_2^x\supsetneq\cdots,\qquad\bigcap_{n<\omega}W_n^x=\{x\}.$$

It is **ordered** when $X=\omega_1$ and $\omega$ is dense.  The adjective does
not say that $W_n^\alpha\subseteq\alpha$: indeed $W_0^\alpha=\omega_1$.

Fix an ordered fundamental space.  Suppose $V_n^\xi\subseteq W_n^\xi$ and
$V_n^\xi\searrow\{\xi\}$.  For $\alpha\leq\omega_1$, set

$$R_n^\xi(\alpha)=\begin{cases}V_n^\xi,&\xi<\alpha,\\W_n^\xi,&\alpha\leq\xi,\end{cases}$$

and let $\mathring{\mathcal T}_\alpha$ be the topology generated by all these
$R_n^\xi(\alpha)$; put
$\widetilde{\mathcal T}=\mathring{\mathcal T}_{\omega_1}$.  A **nice
refinement** requires:

- $\max V_n^\alpha=\alpha$, and $V_n^\alpha$ is closed while
  $V_n^\alpha\cap\alpha$ is open in $\mathring{\mathcal T}_\alpha$;
- $V_n^\alpha=\{\alpha\}$ for $\alpha<\omega$;
- for $\omega\leq\alpha<\omega_1$, there are nonempty, pairwise-disjoint,
  compact clopen sets
  $H_\ell^\alpha\subseteq\alpha\cap(W_\ell^\alpha\setminus
  W_{\ell+1}^\alpha)$ such that
  $$V_m^\alpha=\{\alpha\}\cup\bigcup_{\ell\geq m}H_\ell^\alpha,$$
  and $\{V_m^\alpha:m<\omega\}$ is a clopen local base at $\alpha$ in
  $\widetilde{\mathcal T}$.

For the model-guided version, fix a continuous suitable chain
$\langle M_\alpha:\alpha<\omega_1\rangle$ of countable elementary submodels,
put the global assignment $(\alpha,n)\mapsto W_n^\alpha$ in $M_1$, and require
the earlier $V$-sequence and final topology below $\alpha$ to belong to
$M_{\alpha+1}$.  On $\alpha+1$, choose
a metric $d_\alpha$ for
$\mathring{\mathcal T}_\alpha\restriction(\alpha+1)$ whose ring
$(W_\ell^\alpha\setminus W_{\ell+1}^\alpha)\cap(\alpha+1)$ lies at
radial value $2^{-\ell}$ from $\alpha$ and has internal diameter at most
$2^{-\ell-3}$ in $d_\alpha$.

For $\omega\leq\alpha<\omega_1$, if $S\in M_{\alpha+1}$ is countable and
$S\subseteq K(\alpha,\widetilde{\mathcal T}\restriction\alpha)$, define, for
$\nu\leq\omega$,

$$\Gamma_\alpha(S,\nu)=\{K\in S:(\forall\ell<\nu)\ K\cap(W_\ell^\alpha\setminus W_{\ell+1}^\alpha)\subseteq H_\ell^\alpha\}.$$

Here $K(Y)$ denotes the nonempty compact subsets of $Y$, with the **Vietoris
topology** generated by the subbasic conditions $K\subseteq U$ and
$K\cap U\neq\varnothing$ for open $U\subseteq Y$.  Its standard basic
neighbourhoods are
$N(U_0,\ldots,U_r)=\{K:K\subseteq\bigcup_{i\leq r}U_i\text{ and }
K\cap U_i\neq\varnothing\text{ for every }i\leq r\}$; in a zero-dimensional
space the $U_i$ may be taken nonempty, pairwise-disjoint and clopen.  Distances
between compacta use the Hausdorff metric induced by $d_\alpha$.  For the
same range $\omega\leq\alpha<\omega_1$, the approximation condition
$(\star_\alpha)$ is

$$\forall S\left[\begin{array}{c}S\in M_{\alpha+1},\ |S|\leq\aleph_0,\\S\subseteq K(\alpha,\widetilde{\mathcal T}\restriction\alpha)\end{array}\Longrightarrow\exists^\infty\nu<\omega\ \forall K\in\Gamma_\alpha(S,\nu)\ \exists J\in\Gamma_\alpha(S,\omega)\ d_\alpha(K,J)\leq2^{-\nu}\right].$$

The condition permits $S$ and either $\Gamma$-set to be empty; its implication
then has the literal vacuous meaning.  The model chain, compatible metrics,
and recursive selections are ZFC choices and account for the declared AC
dependency.  They are auxiliary construction data, not part of the raw
definition of a fundamental space.
