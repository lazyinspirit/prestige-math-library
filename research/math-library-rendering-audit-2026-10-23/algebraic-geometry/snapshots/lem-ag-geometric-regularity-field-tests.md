## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]). Let $k$ be a field and let
$A$ be a finite-type $k$-algebra
([[def-ag-geometrically-regular-algebra-and-fibre]]). Then:

1. $A$ is geometrically regular over $k$ if and only if $A$ is a regular ring
   ([[def-regular-noetherian-ring]]) and $A\otimes_kk'$ is regular for every
   finite purely inseparable field extension $k'/k$;
2. if $A$ is geometrically regular over $k$, then $A\otimes_kK$ is a regular
   ring for **every** field extension $K/k$, not only for the finitely generated
   ones appearing in the definition;
3. conversely, if $K/k$ is a field extension such that $A\otimes_kK$ is
   geometrically regular over $K$, then $A$ is geometrically regular over $k$.

Clause 1 is the finite purely inseparable test, clause 2 removes the finite
generation from the scalar extension, and clause 3 is descent of geometric
regularity along the faithfully flat field extension $k\to K$. The zero algebra
is regular vacuously and all clauses hold for it.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A field $k$, a finite-type $k$-algebra $A$, and the Axiom of Choice.

[F1] [[def-ag-geometrically-regular-algebra-and-fibre]]: $A$ is geometrically regular over $k$ when $A\otimes_kK$ is a regular Noetherian ring for every finitely generated field extension $K/k$; the condition on a single scalar extension is tested at primes, and geometric regularity of $A$ over $k$ does not presuppose that $A$ is regular, which is why clause 1 of the statement carries regularity of $A$ as a separate hypothesis.

[F2] [[def-regular-noetherian-ring]]: a commutative Noetherian ring is regular when every prime localisation is a regular local ring, the zero ring being regular vacuously; the maximal-ideal test is proved with the localisation and polynomial-extension theorem.

[F3] [[lem-ag-finite-field-extension-separable-factorization]]: under the Axiom of Choice, for a finitely generated field extension $K/k$ of characteristic $p>0$ there are finite purely inseparable extensions $k'/k$ and $K'/K$ with $K'/k'$ separably generated, and in characteristic $0$ one may take $k'=k$, $K'=K$.

[F4] [[thm-localisation-and-polynomial-extension-of-regular-rings]]: under the Axiom of Choice, localisations and finite polynomial extensions of a commutative regular Noetherian ring are regular, and regularity may be tested at maximal ideals.

[F5] [[def-ag-separating-transcendence-basis]]: $K/k$ is separably generated when it has a finite transcendence basis $t_1,\dots,t_s$ with $K/k(t_1,\dots,t_s)$ finite separable.

[F6] [[thm-primitive-element-theorem-for-finite-separable-extensions]]: a finite extension generated by elements all but at most one of which are separable is simple; in particular a finite separable extension is simple.

[F7] [[lem-ag-flat-local-regularity-ascent-descent]]: under the Axiom of Choice, for a flat local homomorphism $(R,\mathfrak m)\to(S,\mathfrak n)$ of Noetherian local rings: if $R$ and $S/\mathfrak mS$ are regular then $S$ is regular, and if $S$ is regular then $R$ is regular.

[F8] [[thm-polynomial-is-separable-iff-coprime-to-its-derivative]]: $0\ne f\in F[x]$ is separable if and only if $\gcd(f,f')=1$.

[F9] [[lem-polynomial-factorisation-into-irreducibles]] and [[thm-polynomial-quotient-is-a-field-iff-irreducible]]: a nonzero nonunit polynomial over a field is a product of irreducibles, and the quotient by a nonconstant polynomial is a field exactly when that polynomial is irreducible.

[F10] [[thm-right-exactness-of-tensor-products]]: $-\otimes_RM$ is right exact, so $F[T]/(f)\otimes_F\kappa\cong\kappa[T]/(\bar f)$ for a field extension $\kappa/F$.

[F11] [[thm-auslander-buchsbaum-serre-regularity-criterion]]: under the Axiom of Choice, a nonzero Noetherian local ring is regular exactly when every finite module over it has finite projective dimension, and then its global dimension equals its dimension.

[F12] [[cor-noether-normalisation-module-finiteness]]: a nonzero finite-type algebra over a field $k$ is module-finite over a polynomial ring $k[z_1,\dots,z_d]$.

[F13] [[cor-dimension-preserved-by-integral-extensions]] and [[cor-dimension-of-a-finite-polynomial-ring-over-a-field]]: an injective integral extension of nonzero commutative rings preserves dimension, and $\dim k[z_1,\dots,z_d]=d$.

[F14] [[cor-localisation-dimension-does-not-increase]]: localising a commutative ring does not increase its dimension.

[F15] [[def-projective-dimension-of-an-object]] and [[def-left-and-right-global-dimension-of-a-ring]]: $\operatorname{pd}(M)$ is the infimum of the lengths of projective resolutions of $M$, and the global dimension of a ring is the supremum of the projective dimensions of its modules.

[F16] [[prop-extension-of-scalars-preserves-flat-modules]] and [[thm-localisations-are-flat]]: extension of scalars along a flat ring map preserves flatness, and localisations are flat.



## Proof

1.1 The forward implication of clause 1 is immediate: taking $K=k$ in [F1] exhibits $A$ itself as regular, and every finite purely inseparable $k'/k$ is a finitely generated field extension, so [F1] makes $A\otimes_kk'$ regular. [F1, F2]

1.2 A base-change fact used below: if $B$ is a regular Noetherian $k$-algebra and $K/k$ is a separably generated field extension, then $B\otimes_kK$ is regular. Indeed, choose a separating transcendence basis $t_1,\dots,t_s$ and put $F:=k(t_1,\dots,t_s)$, so that $K/F$ is finite separable by [F5] and $K=F(\gamma)$ by [F6] with minimal polynomial $P\in F[T]$ satisfying $\gcd(P,P')=1$ by [F8]. The ring $B_1:=B\otimes_kF$ is a localisation of the polynomial extension $B[t_1,\dots,t_s]$, hence regular by [F4]; let $q$ be a prime of $B_2:=B\otimes_kK=B_1\otimes_FK$ and $p:=q\cap B_1$, so that $(B_1)_p\to(B_2)_q$ is a flat local homomorphism of Noetherian local rings whose closed fibre is a localisation of $\kappa(p)\otimes_FK\cong\kappa(p)[T]/(\bar P)$ by [F10]. Reducing the Bezout identity for $\gcd(P,P')=1$ modulo $p$ shows $\gcd(\bar P,\bar P')=1$, so $\bar P$ is separable by [F8] and factors into pairwise distinct irreducibles by [F9], whence $\kappa(p)[T]/(\bar P)$ is a product of fields and its further localisation is a field, in particular regular; [F7] then makes $(B_2)_q$ regular. As $q$ was arbitrary, $B_2$ is regular by [F2]. [F2, F4, F5, F6, F7, F8, F9, F10]

2.1 The finite purely inseparable test implies the finitely generated case of clause 1: assume $A$ is regular and $A\otimes_kk'$ is regular for every finite purely inseparable $k'/k$, and let $K/k$ be finitely generated. By [F3] there are finite purely inseparable extensions $k'/k$ and $K'/K$ with $K'/k'$ separably generated (in characteristic $0$ take both extensions trivial). The ring $A\otimes_kk'$ is regular by hypothesis, so step 1.2 applied to $B:=A\otimes_kk'$ and the separably generated extension $K'/k'$ makes $A\otimes_kK'=(A\otimes_kk')\otimes_{k'}K'$ regular. Since $K'/K$ is finite purely inseparable, the field extension $K\to K'$ is faithfully flat and local, and for every prime $\mathfrak q$ of $A\otimes_kK$ and every prime $\mathfrak q'$ of $A\otimes_kK'$ over it the induced map $(A\otimes_kK)_{\mathfrak q}\to(A\otimes_kK')_{\mathfrak q'}$ is a flat local homomorphism of Noetherian local rings with regular target, so [F7] descends regularity; as $\mathfrak q$ was arbitrary, $A\otimes_kK$ is regular by [F2]. With step 1.1 this proves clause 1, since [F1] defines geometric regularity by the regular rings $A\otimes_kK$ over finitely generated $K/k$. [F1, F2, F3, F7, step 1.1, step 1.2]

3.1 Arbitrary field extensions. Assume $A$ is geometrically regular over $k$ and let $K/k$ be any field extension, written as the filtered union of its finitely generated subextensions $K_i$. Then $A\otimes_kK=\bigcup_iA\otimes_kK_i$ is a filtered union, and each $A\otimes_kK_i$ is regular by clause 1 as proved in step 2.1. Let $\mathfrak q$ be a prime of $A\otimes_kK$ and put $R:=(A\otimes_kK)_{\mathfrak q}$ and $R_i:=(A\otimes_kK_i)_{\mathfrak q_i}$ with $\mathfrak q_i:=\mathfrak q\cap(A\otimes_kK_i)$, so that $R=\bigcup_iR_i$ is a filtered union of regular local rings. There is a uniform bound on dimensions: for $A\ne0$, if $A$ is module-finite over $k[z_1,\dots,z_d]$ by [F12], then base change along $k\to K_i$ presents $A\otimes_kK_i$ as a quotient of a finite free $K_i[z_1,\dots,z_d]$-module by [F10], so it is integral over $K_i[z_1,\dots,z_d]$ and has dimension $d$ by [F13]; hence $\dim R_i\le d$ by [F14]. For $A=0$ the ring is the zero ring, regular by [F2]. [F2, F10, F12, F13, F14, step 2.1]

4.1 The filtered union is regular. In the notation of step 3.1 let $M$ be a finitely generated $R$-module; presenting $M$ by finitely many generators and relations, all structure constants lie in some stage, so $M\cong M_i\otimes_{R_i}R$ for a finitely generated $R_i$-module $M_i$ and some $i$. By [F11] the regular local ring $R_i$ of dimension at most $d$ has $\operatorname{gldim}R_i=\dim R_i\le d$, so $M_i$ has a projective resolution of length at most $d$ by [F15]; tensoring it with $R$, which is flat over $R_i$ because it is a localisation of the flat base change $R_i\to R_i\otimes_{K_i}K$ by [F16], gives an exact sequence of projective $R$-modules of length at most $d$ (a direct summand of a free module stays such after tensoring), hence $\operatorname{pd}_RM\le d$ by [F15]. As every finitely generated $R$-module has finite projective dimension, [F11] makes $R$ regular; therefore $A\otimes_kK$ is regular by [F2], which is clause 2. [F2, F11, F15, F16, step 3.1]

5.1 Descent, clause 3. Let $K/k$ be a field extension with $A\otimes_kK$ geometrically regular over $K$, and let $E/k$ be a finitely generated field extension; we show $A\otimes_kE$ is regular. The $k$-algebra $E\otimes_kK$ is nonzero, so choose a prime of it and let $L$ be its residue field, a field receiving both $E$ and $K$ for which $L/K$ is a finitely generated field extension. Then $A\otimes_kL=(A\otimes_kK)\otimes_KL=(A\otimes_kE)\otimes_EL$ is regular by clause 2, applied over $K$ to the geometrically regular $K$-algebra $A\otimes_kK$; and $A\otimes_kE\to A\otimes_kL$ is faithfully flat and local at corresponding primes because $E\to L$ is a field extension, so [F7] descends regularity to every local ring of $A\otimes_kE$, making it regular by [F2]. As $E$ was arbitrary, $A$ is geometrically regular over $k$ by [F1]. [F1, F2, F7, step 4.1]

6.1 Clause 1 is step 2.1, clause 2 is step 4.1 and clause 3 is step 5.1; the zero algebra is covered by the vacuous regularity of [F2]. ∎
