## Statement

Let $(\mathcal I,\mu)$ be a marked ideal on a smooth $K$-scheme, $C\subseteq\operatorname{supp}(\mathcal I,\mu)$ a regular center with SNC with $E$, $\sigma\colon X'\to X$ the blowup with exceptional divisor $D$, and $0\le r\le\mu$ ([[def-multiple-test-blowup-and-controlled-transform]]).
Then
$$\sigma^{\mathrm{c}}\bigl(\mathcal D^r(\mathcal I),\mu-r\bigr)\subseteq\mathcal D^r\bigl(\sigma^{\mathrm{c}}(\mathcal I,\mu)\bigr),$$
i.e. the controlled transform of the $r$-th derivative ideal is contained in the $r$-th derivative ideal of the controlled transform ([[def-ideal-of-derivatives]]).

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A marked ideal $(\mathcal I,\mu)$ on a smooth $K$-scheme $X$, a regular center $C\subseteq\operatorname{supp}(\mathcal I,\mu)$ with SNC with $E$, the blowup $\sigma\colon X'\to X$ with exceptional divisor $D$ and local equation $y$, and $0\le r\le\mu$.

[F1] [[def-ideal-of-derivatives]]: $\mathcal D(\mathcal A)$ is generated by the local sections of $\mathcal A$ and their first derivatives with respect to local coordinates; equivalently by the sections $f\in\mathcal A$ together with $D(f)$ for $K$-derivations $D$ of $\mathcal O_X$; $\mathcal D^r$ is the iterate and $\mathcal D^r(\mathcal I,\mu)=(\mathcal D^r(\mathcal I),\mu-r)$.

[F2] [[def-multiple-test-blowup-and-controlled-transform]], [[lem-controlled-transform-is-well-defined]]: the controlled transform is $\sigma^{\mathrm c}(\mathcal A,\nu)=(\mathcal I(D)^{-\nu}\sigma^*\mathcal A,\nu)$, computed by $f\mapsto y^{-\nu}\sigma^*(f)$ on sections; it is an ideal sheaf, well defined up to units.

[F3] [[def-embedding-dimension-and-regular-local-ring]], [[def-blowup-scheme-along-ideal]]: at a point of $C$ choose local parameters $u_1,\dots,u_n$ with $C$ described by $u_1=\dots=u_m=0$ and $y=u_m$; the corresponding chart of the blowup has coordinates $u'_i=u_i/y$ $(i<m)$, $u'_i=u_i$ $(i>m)$, $u'_m=u_m=y$.

[F4] [[def-derivation-algebra]], [[cor-derivations-represented-by-differentials]]: the $K$-derivations of the structure sheaf form a locally free sheaf; a derivation is determined by its values on local coordinates, and $y\sigma^*(D)$ for a derivation $D$ on $X$ acts on $\sigma^*(f)$ by $y\sigma^*(Df)$.

[F5] [[lem-derivative-ideals-have-the-same-support]]: for $0\le j\le\mu-1$, $\operatorname{supp}(\mathcal I,\mu)\subseteq\operatorname{supp}(\mathcal D^j(\mathcal I),\mu-j)$ in every characteristic, so the center hypothesis transfers to the lower derivative ideal.

## Proof

1.1 Use the adapted blowup coordinates of [F3]. The chain rule gives $\delta:=y\sigma^*D\in\operatorname{Der}_K(\mathcal O_{X'})$ for each coordinate derivation $D$ on $X$, and $\delta(y)/y$ is regular: for normal directions different from the chart index $\delta(y)=0$, for the chart-index direction $\delta(y)=y$, and for the tangent directions $\delta(y)=0$. Linear combinations have the same properties. [F3, F4]

2.1 Put $I'=y^{-\mu}\sigma^*I$. For a generator $f\in I$, write $\sigma^*f=y^\mu g$ with $g\in I'$. Leibniz gives $y^{1-\mu}\sigma^*(Df)=\delta(g)+\mu(\delta(y)/y)g\in\mathcal D(I')$. The undifferentiated generator of $\mathcal D(I)$ transforms to $y^{1-\mu}\sigma^*f=yg\in I'$. These two calculations, including the original ideal generators, prove $\sigma^{\mathrm c}(\mathcal D(I),\mu-1)\subseteq\mathcal D(I')$. No division by the characteristic is used. [F1, F2, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 The case $r=0$ is equality. For $1\le r\le\mu$, [F5] makes the center admissible for $(\mathcal D^{r-1}(I),\mu-r+1)$. Apply step 2.1 to this marked ideal and then use monotonicity of $\mathcal D$ and the induction hypothesis: $\sigma^{\mathrm c}(\mathcal D^r(I),\mu-r)\subseteq\mathcal D(\sigma^{\mathrm c}(\mathcal D^{r-1}(I),\mu-r+1))\subseteq\mathcal D^r(I')$. This completes the induction. [F1, F5, step 2.1] ∎
