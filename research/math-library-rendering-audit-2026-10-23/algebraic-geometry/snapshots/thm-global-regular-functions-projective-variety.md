## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Every global regular function on a classical projective variety $X$ is constant.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC, algebraically closed $k$, a nonempty irreducible projective algebraic set $X\subseteq\mathbf P_k^n$, and a global regular function f.

[F1] A global regular function gives a degree-zero element of the fraction field of the graded domain $A=S(X)$, locally represented by equal-degree homogeneous fractions with nonvanishing denominator ([[def-regular-function-projective-variety]], [[lem-projective-irreducibility-homogeneous-prime]]).

[F2] Under AC, $I(V(J))=\sqrt J$ for every polynomial ideal J ([[cor-strong-nullstellensatz-two-inclusions]]).

[F3] Every finite-dimensional endomorphism satisfies its monic characteristic polynomial ([[thm-cayley-hamilton]]).

## Proof

1.1 In A let D be the homogeneous ideal generated by all homogeneous denominators h for which $fh\in A_{\deg h}$. Global regularity says that at each point of X at least one such h is nonzero. This uses the set of all admissible denominators, not a simultaneous choice of local representatives. Let $\widetilde D$ be the inverse image of D in $k[x_0,\ldots,x_n]$. Its affine zero set is contained in the cone over X, and has no nonzero point: any such point represents a point of X where all admissible denominators would vanish. [F1, given, algebra]

2.1 Each coordinate x_i therefore vanishes on $V(\widetilde D)$. F2 implies $x_i^{N_i}\in\widetilde D$ for some $N_i\ge1$, so its class lies in D. This is the precise inherited AC use in the argument. Expressing that homogeneous class as a finite sum of homogeneous generators of D and taking the component of degree N_i shows $f x_i^{N_i}\in A_{N_i}$: multiply each generator by f in its prescribed degree. This also covers coordinates zero in A. [F1, F2, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Choose $N\ge1$ at least every N_i. Then $fx_i^N\in A_N$ for all i. Choose an integer $d>(n+1)(N-1)$. Every monomial of degree d contains some x_i to exponent at least N, so multiplying by f carries its class into A_d. The finitely many monomials of degree d span A_d, hence multiplication by f defines a k-linear endomorphism of this finite-dimensional space. It is nonzero as a space: at any point of X some coordinate x_i is nonzero, and then $x_i^d$ is nonzero in A_d. [step 2.1, given, algebra]

4.1 F3 supplies a monic polynomial $P\in k[T]$ with $P(f)a=0$ for every $a\in A_d$. Choose nonzero $a\in A_d$; cancellation in $\operatorname{Frac}(A)$ gives $P(f)=0$. As k is algebraically closed, P splits into linear factors, and the field has no zero divisors. Thus $f=c$ in the fraction field for some $c\in k$. Its local evaluations are consequently that same constant at every point of X. [F1, F3, step 3.1, algebra] ∎
