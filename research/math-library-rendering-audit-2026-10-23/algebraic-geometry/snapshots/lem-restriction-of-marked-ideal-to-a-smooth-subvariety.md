## Statement

Assume AC ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]) for the regular-parameter and blowup suppliers.

Let $(\mathcal I,E,\mu)$ be a marked ideal of maximal order on the smooth $K$-scheme $X$, and let $S\subseteq X$ be a regular closed subscheme having SNC with $E$ and not contained in $\operatorname{supp}(\mathcal I,E,\mu)$ ([[def-marked-ideal]], [[def-simple-normal-crossings-divisors]]).
For restriction to $S$, work componentwise and omit from the restricted boundary the members containing that component of $S$; retain the restrictions of the other members in their original order. Here SNC position for $S$ means that its ideal is generated by a subset of parameters compatible with the boundary equations. This convention is required, for example, when $S$ is itself a boundary stratum: a divisor containing $S$ does not restrict to a Cartier divisor on it.

Then $\operatorname{supp}(\mathcal I,\mu)\cap S\subseteq\operatorname{supp}(\mathcal I|_S,\mu)$, where $\mathcal I|_S$ is the pullback ideal on $S$ ([[def-coherent-module-scheme]]).
If $C\subseteq\operatorname{supp}(\mathcal I,\mu)\cap S$ is a regular center with SNC with $E$, $\sigma\colon X'\to X$ is the blowup and $S'\subseteq X'$ is the strict transform of $S$ ([[def-strict-transform-closed-subscheme]]), then
$$\sigma^{\mathrm{c}}\bigl((\mathcal I,\mu)|_S\bigr)=\bigl(\sigma^{\mathrm{c}}(\mathcal I,\mu)\bigr)|_{S'}.$$
Moreover, for any multiple test blow-up $(X_i)$ of $(\mathcal I,\mu)$ all of whose centers lie in the strict transforms $S_i$ of $S$, the restrictions $\sigma_i|_{S_i}$ define a multiple test blow-up $(S_i)$ of $(\mathcal I,\mu)|_S$ and $[(\mathcal I,\mu)|_S]_i=(\mathcal I_i,\mu)|_{S_i}$ for every $i$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A marked ideal $(\mathcal I,E,\mu)$ on a smooth $K$-scheme $X$ whose support does not contain a smooth subvariety $S\subseteq X$ that has SNC with $E$; a blowup $\sigma\colon X'\to X$ with center $C\subseteq\operatorname{supp}(\mathcal I,E,\mu)\cap S$; the strict transform $S'\subseteq X'$ of $S$.

[F1] [[def-marked-ideal]]: the restriction of the marked ideal is $(\mathcal I,\mu)|_S=(\mathcal I\cdot\mathcal O_S,\mu)$, with support $\{x\in S:\operatorname{ord}_x(\mathcal I\mathcal O_S)\ge\mu\}$.

[F2] [[def-order-of-an-ideal-sheaf-at-a-point]]: order is defined by containment of stalks in powers of the maximal ideal; for $x\in S$ the maximal ideal of $\mathcal O_{S,x}$ is the image of $\mathfrak m_{X,x}$, so $\mathcal I_x\subseteq\mathfrak m_{X,x}^\mu$ implies $\mathcal I_x\mathcal O_{S,x}\subseteq\mathfrak m_{S,x}^\mu$; restriction can only raise the order.

[F3] [[def-multiple-test-blowup-and-controlled-transform]], [[def-exceptional-divisor-blowup]]: the controlled transform of a section $f\in\mathcal I(U)$ is $f'=y^{-\mu}\sigma^*(f)$ for a local equation $y$ of the exceptional divisor $D$; the controlled transform of the marked ideal is generated by the $f'$.

[F4] [[def-strict-transform-closed-subscheme]], [[def-blowup-scheme-along-ideal]]: in coordinates $x_1,\dots,x_k$ defining $S$ and $y_1,\dots,y_{n-k}$ along it at a point of $C$, with the center described by $x_1,\dots,x_k,y_1,\dots,y_m$, the chart of the blowup has coordinates $x'_i=x_i/y_m$ $(i\le k)$, $y'_j=y_j/y_m$ $(j<m)$, $y'_m=y_m$, $y'_j=y_j$ $(j>m)$, and the strict transform $S'$ is described by $x'_1=\dots=x'_k=0$ with $y'_m$ a local equation of the exceptional divisor of $S'\to S$.

[F5] [[def-embedding-dimension-and-regular-local-ring]], [[def-simple-normal-crossings-divisors]]: $S$ is smooth with SNC with $E$, so the coordinates can be chosen adapted both to $S$ and to $E$; after omitting the members containing a component of $S$, the remaining restrictions are again a family in simultaneous SNC position. The same omission convention applies at every stage.


## Proof

1.1 The first inclusion. Let $x\in\operatorname{supp}(\mathcal I,\mu)\cap S$, so $\mathcal I_x\subseteq\mathfrak m_{X,x}^\mu$. Applying the ring map $\mathcal O_{X,x}\to\mathcal O_{S,x}$ gives $\mathcal I_x\mathcal O_{S,x}\subseteq\mathfrak m_{X,x}^\mu\mathcal O_{S,x}=\mathfrak m_{S,x}^\mu$, so $\operatorname{ord}_x(\mathcal I\cdot\mathcal O_S)\ge\mu$ and $x\in\operatorname{supp}((\mathcal I,\mu)|_S)$. Hence $\operatorname{supp}(\mathcal I,\mu)\cap S\subseteq\operatorname{supp}((\mathcal I,\mu)|_S)$. [F1, F2]

1.2 Work at a point of $S'$ over $C$. By the parameter-generation theorem, the center ideal is $(x_1,\ldots,x_k,y_1,\ldots,y_m)$, where $S$ is defined by the $x$'s. On a chart indexed by an $x_i$, saturation makes the strict transform of $S$ empty. On a chart indexed by $a=y_j$, it is defined by $x_i/a=0$, and its chart is precisely the corresponding chart of $\operatorname{Bl}_C S$. The exceptional equation on $S'$ is $a|_{S'}$, a nonzerodivisor. Thus for every generator $f$ of $I$, restriction of $a^{-\mu}\sigma^*f$ to $S'$ equals $(a|_{S'})^{-\mu}(\sigma|_{S'})^*(f|_S)$. This proves the transform identity on every nonempty chart without assuming a power-series expansion; the empty charts have no stalk to check. These identities glue, and the remaining restricted boundary has SNC by [F5]. [F3, F4, F5, algebra]


2.1 Iteration along a multiple test blow-up. Suppose $(X_i)_{0\le i\le r}$ is a multiple test blow-up of $(\mathcal I,\mu)$ with every center $C_i$ contained in the strict transform $S_i$ of $S$. By induction on $i$, step 1.2 applied to the restricted marked ideal $(\mathcal I_i,\mu)|_{S_i}$ and the blowup $\sigma_{i+1}$ with center $C_i\subseteq S_i$ gives $[(\mathcal I,\mu)|_S]_{i+1}=\sigma_{i+1}^{\mathrm c}((\mathcal I_i,\mu)|_{S_i})=((\mathcal I_{i+1},\mu)|_{S_{i+1}})$; moreover $C_i$, being also a center for the restricted marked ideal with SNC with $E_i|_{S_i}$ by [F5], makes $(S_i)$ a multiple test blow-up of $(\mathcal I,\mu)|_S$ with the same transform rule. The base case $i=0$ is the identity. This proves the final assertion for every length, and in particular the equality $[(\mathcal I,\mu)|_S]_i=(\mathcal I_i,\mu)|_{S_i}$ stated in the lemma. [F1, F5, step 1.2] ∎

## Remarks

- The hypothesis that $S$ is not contained in $\operatorname{supp}(\mathcal I,\mu)$ is used only to keep the two suppressed-locus readings apart; the computation of step 1.2 uses no such hypothesis, and the first inclusion of step 1.1 is unconditional. The empty case $S=\varnothing$ makes all statements vacuous.
- The identity is the source's Lemma 2.10.3 and is the restriction calculus on which the coefficient-ideal lemmas below are built.
