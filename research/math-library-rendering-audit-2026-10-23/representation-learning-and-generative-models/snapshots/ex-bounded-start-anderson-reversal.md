## Example

Assume AC. Let $X_0$ have a compactly supported $C^\infty$ probability density $\rho$ on $\mathbb R^d$, let $W$ be standard $d$-dimensional Brownian motion independent of $X_0$, and use the usual augmentation of the filtration generated by $X_0$ and $W$. Set $X_t=X_0+W_t$ on $[0,T]$. For $t>0$ its density is
$$p_t(x)=\int_{\mathbb R^d}(2\pi t)^{-d/2}e^{-|x-z|^2/(2t)}\rho(z)\,dz.$$
Assume that $p_t$ is the unique smooth forward-density solution on $[0,T]$ and, in addition, Anderson's unique smooth joint-density and conditional-density hypotheses for $(X_t,\widetilde W_t)$, where $\widetilde W_0=0$ and, for $t>0$, $d\widetilde W_t=dW_t+\nabla\log p_t(X_t)dt$. Then on reverse times $0\le u<T$, the reverse drift is $\nabla\log p_{T-u}(Y_u)$ with unit diffusion covariance. The value of $\nabla\log p_0$ is not used in the time integral; the statement makes no score assertion at the data endpoint $u=T$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC, the initial law and Brownian motion in the Example, and Anderson's unique smooth joint and conditional density premises.

[A1] Anderson's theorem requires bounded initial state independent of the forward Brownian process, a unique smooth forward density, unique smooth augmented joint density, and unique smooth conditional augmented density; strong SDE well-posedness alone does not supply these density hypotheses. [[thm-anderson-reverse-time-diffusion]]

[F1] In the additive Gaussian setup with $g\equiv1$, the positive-time marginal density is the displayed convolution, is positive, and solves $\partial_t p_t=\tfrac12\Delta p_t$. [[def-gaussian-smoothed-data-law-and-transition-density]] [[thm-gaussian-smoothing-density-and-forward-equation]]

## Verification
1.1 The compact support of $\rho$ makes $X_0$ almost surely bounded, and its independence from $W$ is part of the Example. The restart map is explicit: for $s\le r\le T$, $X_r=X_s+(W_r-W_s)$. The SDE has $b=0$ and $\sigma=I_d$, so it is a globally Lipschitz strong SDE; the convolution and heat equation in [F1] give the stated positive-time marginal density. The separate augmented joint and conditional uniqueness requirements are explicitly assumed, exactly as listed in [A1]. [A1, F1, given]

2.1 Apply Anderson's theorem with $t_0=0$, $b=0$, $a=I_d$, $\nabla\!\cdot a=0$, and $p_t$ from [F1]. Its reverse drift is $-b+\nabla\!\cdot a+a\nabla\log p_t=\nabla\log p_t$, and the reverse diffusion covariance is $I_d$. Since $p_t$ is only used for $t>0$, this gives the stated equation for $u<T$ and asserts nothing about a score at $u=T$. [A1, F1, step 1.1] ∎
