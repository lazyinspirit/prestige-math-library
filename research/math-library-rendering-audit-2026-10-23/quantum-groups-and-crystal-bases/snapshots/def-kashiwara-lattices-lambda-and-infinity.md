## Definition

Let $\lambda\in P^+$ and let $L(\lambda)$ be the simple integrable highest weight module with highest weight vector $v_\lambda$ ([[thm-integrable-quantum-highest-weight-module-exists-and-is-unique]]); in this page we write $V(\lambda):=L(\lambda)$, following the notation of the crystal sources. Let $U_q^-$ be the negative half with its Kashiwara operators $\tilde e_i,\tilde f_i$ ([[thm-the-i-string-decomposition-of-the-negative-half]]), and let $A_0$ be the crystal limiting ring ([[def-the-local-ring-at-q-zero]]).

Define $\mathcal L(\lambda)$ to be the $A_0$-submodule of $V(\lambda)$ generated by all vectors $\tilde f_{i_1}\cdots\tilde f_{i_r}v_\lambda$ ($r\ge0$, $i_1,\dots,i_r\in I$), where for $r=0$ the vector is $v_\lambda$; and $\mathcal L(\infty)$ to be the $A_0$-submodule of $U_q^-$ generated by all $\tilde f_{i_1}\cdots\tilde f_{i_r}\mathbf 1$ ($r\ge0$), where $\mathbf 1$ is the unit of $U_q^-$. Here $\tilde f_i$ on $V(\lambda)$ are the module Kashiwara operators and $\tilde f_i$ on $U_q^-$ are the negative-half operators. Define
$$\mathcal B(\lambda):=\{\tilde f_{i_1}\cdots\tilde f_{i_r}v_\lambda+q\mathcal L(\lambda):r\ge0\}\setminus\{0\}\subseteq\mathcal L(\lambda)/q\mathcal L(\lambda),$$
$$\mathcal B(\infty):=\{\tilde f_{i_1}\cdots\tilde f_{i_r}\mathbf 1+q\mathcal L(\infty):r\ge0\}\setminus\{0\}\subseteq\mathcal L(\infty)/q\mathcal L(\infty).$$

Then, with $\pi_\lambda:U_q^-\to V(\lambda)$, $\pi_\lambda(P)=Pv_\lambda$:

(a) $\mathcal L(\lambda)=\bigoplus_{\mu\in P}\mathcal L(\lambda)_\mu$ with $\mathcal L(\lambda)_\mu=\mathcal L(\lambda)\cap V(\lambda)_\mu$, and $\mathcal L(\infty)=\bigoplus_{\nu\in Q_+}\mathcal L(\infty)_{-\nu}$ with $\mathcal L(\infty)_{-\nu}=\mathcal L(\infty)\cap U_q^-[-\nu]$; correspondingly $\mathcal B(\lambda)=\bigsqcup_\mu\mathcal B(\lambda)_\mu$ and $\mathcal B(\infty)=\bigsqcup_\nu\mathcal B(\infty)_{-\nu}$.

(b) $\tilde f_i\mathcal L(\lambda)\subseteq\mathcal L(\lambda)$ and $\tilde f_i\mathcal L(\infty)\subseteq\mathcal L(\infty)$.

(c) (Recursive spanning.) For $\alpha\in Q_+\setminus\{0\}$,
$$\mathcal L(\lambda)_{\lambda-\alpha}=\sum_{i\in I}\tilde f_i\mathcal L(\lambda)_{\lambda-\alpha+\alpha_i},\qquad \mathcal B(\lambda)_{\lambda-\alpha}=\Bigl(\bigcup_{i\in I}\tilde f_i\mathcal B(\lambda)_{\lambda-\alpha+\alpha_i}\Bigr)\setminus\{0\},$$
and the same formulas with $\mathcal L(\infty),\mathcal B(\infty)$. Each $\mathcal L(\lambda)_{\lambda-\alpha}$ and each $\mathcal L(\infty)_{-\alpha}$ is generated over $A_0$ by finitely many monomials of total length at most $\operatorname{ht}(\alpha)$, hence is a finitely generated $A_0$-module.

No basis property, scalar-extension spanning or crystal-lattice property of $\mathcal L(\lambda)$ or $\mathcal L(\infty)$ is asserted here; these are conclusions of the grand loop. The script letters avoid the collision with the simple module $L(\lambda)$. No choice principle is used. Each fixed component is torsionfree inside a field vector space and generated by finitely many words of its fixed height, so it is free over the DVR $A_0$; its full field-span is proved later. The field map $\pi_\lambda$ is surjective, but exact equality on Kashiwara monomial generators is not asserted.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** $\lambda\in P^+$, the simple integrable highest weight module $V(\lambda)=L(\lambda)$ with highest vector $v_\lambda$, the negative half $U_q^-$, the $A_0$-spans $\mathcal L(\lambda),\mathcal L(\infty)$ generated by the $\tilde f$-words, and the residue sets $\mathcal B(\lambda),\mathcal B(\infty)$.

[F1] $\tilde f_i$ on $V(\lambda)$ and on $U_q^-$ is $\mathbb Q(q)$-linear, preserves the lattice $\mathcal L(\lambda)$, respectively $\mathcal L(\infty)$, once (b) is known, and lowers the weight, respectively the root degree, by $\alpha_i$; on $U_q^-$ it is injective with $\tilde e_i\tilde f_i=\mathrm{id}$ ([[def-kashiwara-crystal-operators]], [[thm-the-i-string-decomposition-of-the-negative-half]]).

[F2] $V(\lambda)=\bigoplus_{\mu\in P}V(\lambda)_\mu$ and $U_q^-=\bigoplus_{\nu\in Q_+}U_q^-[-\nu]$ are direct sums of weight, respectively root, spaces ([[def-quantum-weight-module-and-integrability]], [[thm-the-i-string-decomposition-of-the-negative-half]]).

[F3] Every word $\tilde f_{i_1}\cdots\tilde f_{i_r}v_\lambda$ is homogeneous of weight $\lambda-\alpha_{i_1}-\cdots-\alpha_{i_r}$, and every word $\tilde f_{i_1}\cdots\tilde f_{i_r}\mathbf 1$ is homogeneous of root degree $-\alpha_{i_1}-\cdots-\alpha_{i_r}$ ([[def-kac-moody-integral-and-dominant-integral-weights]], [F1], [F2]).

[F4] A finitely generated torsionfree module over the PID $A_0$ is free ([[cor-finitely-generated-torsion-free-modules-over-a-pid-are-free]], [[def-the-local-ring-at-q-zero]]).

[F5] $V(\lambda)$ is universal among integrable cyclic highest weight modules of highest weight $\lambda$: every such module receives a unique map of $U_q(\mathfrak g)$-modules sending its highest vector to a prescribed highest vector, and $\pi_\lambda:U_q^-\to V(\lambda)$, $P\mapsto Pv_\lambda$, is a surjective $\mathbb Q(q)$-linear map sending $\mathbf 1$ to $v_\lambda$ ([[thm-integrable-quantum-highest-weight-module-exists-and-is-unique]]).

## Verification

**Proof technique:** direct. Homogeneity gives the graded decompositions, prepending a color gives the recursive spanning and stability, and finite generation plus torsionfreeness gives freeness of the components.

1.1 Grading (a). Each generator $\tilde f_{i_1}\cdots\tilde f_{i_r}v_\lambda$ is homogeneous of weight $\lambda-\alpha_{i_1}-\cdots-\alpha_{i_r}$, and each generator of $\mathcal L(\infty)$ is homogeneous of root degree $-\alpha_{i_1}-\cdots-\alpha_{i_r}$, by [F3]. Taking $A_0$-linear combinations and using [F2], every element of $\mathcal L(\lambda)$ lies in $\bigoplus_\mu V(\lambda)_\mu$ with only finitely many nonzero components, and its weight components lie in $\mathcal L(\lambda)$; hence $\mathcal L(\lambda)=\bigoplus_\mu\mathcal L(\lambda)_\mu$ with $\mathcal L(\lambda)_\mu=\mathcal L(\lambda)\cap V(\lambda)_\mu$, and similarly $\mathcal L(\infty)=\bigoplus_\nu\mathcal L(\infty)_{-\nu}$. The residue sets decompose accordingly by their definition. [F2, F3, given]

1.2 Stability (b). A generator is carried by $\tilde f_i$ to the generator with the color $i$ prepended; since $\tilde f_i$ is $\mathbb Q(q)$-linear, it commutes with $A_0$-coefficients, so $\tilde f_i\mathcal L(\lambda)\subseteq\mathcal L(\lambda)$ and $\tilde f_i\mathcal L(\infty)\subseteq\mathcal L(\infty)$. [F1, given]

2.1 Recursive spanning (c). Let $\alpha\in Q_+\setminus\{0\}$ and let $w$ be a word of total degree $\alpha$; writing $w=\tilde f_{i_1}w'$ exhibits the class of $w$ as $\tilde f_{i_1}$ of the class of the shorter word $w'$, which is a generator of degree $\alpha-\alpha_{i_1}$. This gives $\subseteq$; the reverse inclusion holds because $\tilde f_i$ preserves $\mathcal L$ by step 1.2 and lowers the degree by $\alpha_i$. For the residue sets, a word class that is nonzero remains nonzero when shortened (if $w'\in q\mathcal L$ then $w=\tilde f_{i_1}w'\in q\mathcal L$), and the displayed union minus zero therefore equals $\mathcal B(\lambda)_{\lambda-\alpha}$; the same argument applies to $\mathcal B(\infty)$. [F1, step 1.1, step 1.2, given]

3.1 Finite generation and freeness. Iterating (c) from degree $\alpha$ down to degree $0$ expresses every $\mathcal L(\lambda)_{\lambda-\alpha}$ as the sum of the images of $A_0v_\lambda$ under the words of length exactly $\operatorname{ht}(\alpha)$; there are finitely many such words because $I$ is finite, and for $\alpha=0$ the component is $A_0v_\lambda$. Hence each component is finitely generated over $A_0$ by monomials of length at most $\operatorname{ht}(\alpha)$. It is torsionfree because it is contained in the $\mathbb Q(q)$-vector space $V(\lambda)$, respectively $U_q^-$, so [F4] makes it a free $A_0$-module. [F4, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 Choice and the field map. The words are indexed by finite sequences in the finite set $I$, so no choice is needed to name the generators. The map $\pi_\lambda$ is surjective by [F5], because $U_q^-$ generates $V(\lambda)$ over $U_q(\mathfrak g)$ from $v_\lambda$; no equality between $\pi_\lambda$ and the lattice projections, and no spanning over $A_0$ of the field-spanning property, is asserted here. [F5, step 3.1]

5.1 No crystal-lattice claim yet. The statements proved above are the graded decompositions, stability, recursive spanning, finite generation and freeness of the components; whether $\mathcal B(\lambda)$ and $\mathcal B(\infty)$ are crystal bases, and whether $\mathcal L(\lambda)\otimes_{A_0}\mathbb Q(q)\to V(\lambda)$ is an isomorphism, is not asserted and is deferred to the grand loop. [given, step 3.1] ∎
