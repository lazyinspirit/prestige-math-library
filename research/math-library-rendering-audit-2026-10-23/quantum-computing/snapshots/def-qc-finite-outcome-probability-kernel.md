## Definition

Let $H$ be a nonzero finite-dimensional complex-inner-product space and fix an ordered basis $\mathcal B$ of $H$ ([[def-dimension]], [[def-real-and-complex-inner-product-space]]). Represent each endomorphism by its matrix in $\mathcal B$ ([[def-coordinate-column-and-matrix-of-a-linear-map]]), and identify its real and imaginary entries with a point of $\mathbb R^{2(\dim H)^2}$. Give the density set $\mathcal D(H)$ (nonnegative operators of trace one, [[def-qc-density-operator]]) the topology whose open sets are intersections of $\mathcal D(H)$ with open subsets of that Euclidean coordinate space. Its Borel $\sigma$-algebra is the smallest $\sigma$-algebra containing those open sets; a $\sigma$-algebra is a family of subsets closed under complements and countable unions.

Let $\Omega$ be the finite outcome set of a POVM $E$ on $H$. A **finite probability kernel** from $\mathcal D(H)$ to $\Omega$ assigns to each $\rho\in\mathcal D(H)$ a finite probability space on $\Omega$, and requires that $\rho\mapsto K(\rho,S)$ be Borel measurable for every event $S\subseteq\Omega$ ([[def-finite-probability-space-and-event]]). The Borel $\sigma$-algebra on $\mathbb R$ is generated by its open sets; a real-valued function is Borel measurable when the preimage of every such Borel set belongs to the domain's Borel $\sigma$-algebra.

For the POVM outcome weights from [[def-qc-povm-and-outcome-law]], define the candidate kernel by

$$K_E(\rho,S):=\sum_{a\in S}\operatorname{tr}(\rho E_a),\qquad S\subseteq\Omega.$$

Here $\operatorname{tr}$ is the finite-dimensional endomorphism trace ([[def-trace-of-an-endomorphism]]).

That this assignment is a probability kernel and is affine in $\rho$ is proved in [[thm-qc-finite-outcome-law-is-a-probability-kernel]]. The matrix-coordinate $\sigma$-algebra is fixed in the definition; no unmentioned topology on the state set is used.
