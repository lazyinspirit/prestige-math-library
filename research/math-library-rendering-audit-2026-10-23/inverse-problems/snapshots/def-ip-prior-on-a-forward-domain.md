## Definition

Let $X$ be a specified real separable Banach or Hilbert parameter space with metric $d_X(x,x')=\|x-x'\|_X$ and its norm topology. Let $(Y,d_Y)$ be a metric data space equipped with a sigma-algebra $\Sigma_Y$, let $U\subseteq X$ be a nonempty admissible truth set, and let $F:U\to Y$ be a forward map. Choose a Borel set $D\in\mathcal B(X)$ with $D\subseteq U$ such that the restriction $G:=F|_D:D\to Y$ is measurable for the trace Borel sigma-algebra $\mathcal B(D):=\{D\cap A:A\in\mathcal B(X)\}$. A prior on this forward domain is a Borel probability measure $\mu$ on $X$ such that $\mu(D)=1$. Its restriction $\mu|_D$, defined by $\mu|_D(A)=\mu(A)$ for $A\in\mathcal B(D)$, is then a probability measure on $D$, and the induced data law is $G_\#(\mu|_D)$, defined by $G_\#(\mu|_D)(E)=(\mu|_D)(G^{-1}(E))$ for $E\in\Sigma_Y$. A Borel probability on $X$ with $\mu(D)<1$ is not a prior on this declared forward domain.
