## Definition

Let $\Theta\subset S^1$ be a nonempty proper open set of line normals, assume $\theta\in\Theta$ if and only if $-\theta\in\Theta$, and assume $S^1\setminus\overline{\Theta}$ contains a nonempty open arc. The measured line with normal $\theta$ and signed offset $s$ is $\ell_{\theta,s}=\{y\in\mathbb R^2:y\cdot\theta=s\}$. Identify the nonzero cotangent vectors of the Euclidean plane with pairs $(x,\xi)\in\mathbb R^2\times(\mathbb R^2\setminus\{0\})$. Such a covector is visible from $\Theta$ when $\xi/|\xi|\in\Theta$, and invisible when $\xi/|\xi|\notin\Theta$. The associated measured Fourier wedge is

$$W_\Theta=\{\sigma\theta:\theta\in\Theta,\ \sigma\in\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}\}.$$
