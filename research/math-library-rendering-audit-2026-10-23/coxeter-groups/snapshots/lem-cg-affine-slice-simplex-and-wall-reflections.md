## Statement

Let $(W,S,m,V,B,\rho,\Gamma)$ be of affine form type, and let $\delta=\sum_{s\in S}\delta_se_s$ be positive with $\operatorname{rad}(B)=\mathbb R\delta$ ([[def-cg-irreducible-affine-coxeter-type]] (1)-(4), [[lem-cg-positive-radical-and-affine-gram-exclusions]] (1)). Put $U=V/\operatorname{rad}(B)$ with its quotient Euclidean form $b$, and let $E=E_\delta$, $H_s$, $A$, and $\bar A$ be as in [[def-cg-irreducible-affine-coxeter-type]] (3)-(4). Let $\rho^*(w)\varphi=\varphi\circ\rho(w)^{-1}$ be the dual action ([[def-cg-dual-chambers-and-reflection-hyperplanes]] (1)); give the direction space $K_\delta$ of $E$ its dual Euclidean form $b^*$ from (4). For each $s\in S$ let $\bar e_s$ be the image of $e_s$ in $U$.

**(1) The action.** Every $\rho^*(w)$ preserves $E$ and acts there by an affine isometry. It sends $H_s$ to $\{\varphi\in E:\varphi(\rho(w)e_s)=0\}$ and permutes the family of alcove translates $\{\rho^*(w)A:w\in W\}$. The induced homomorphism $W\to\operatorname{Isom}(E)$ is faithful.

**(2) The affine relation.** For every $\varphi\in E$, $$\sum_{s\in S}\delta_s\varphi(e_s)=\varphi(\delta)=1.$$

**(3) The alcove is a simplex.** For each $s\in S$ there is exactly one point $v_s\in E$ with $v_s(e_t)=0$ for $t\ne s$, and it satisfies $v_s(e_s)=1/\delta_s$ and $v_s\in\bar A$. The points $(v_s)_{s\in S}$ are affinely independent and $$\bar A=\operatorname{conv}\{v_s:s\in S\}$$ is a Euclidean simplex of dimension $|S|-1$ ([[def-geometric-simplex-spanned-by-affinely-independent-vertices]]). Its facets are $\bar A\cap H_s$. The inward unit normal to the facet $\bar A\cap H_s$ is $b^\flat(\bar e_s)\in K_\delta$, equivalently $\bar e_s$ under the identification $K_\delta\cong U$; its Gram matrix is $$b^*(b^\flat(\bar e_s),b^\flat(\bar e_t))=b(\bar e_s,\bar e_t)=B(e_s,e_t).$$ In particular, $A$ is nonempty and $\bar A$ is compact.

**(4) Facet reflections.** For every $s\in S$, $\rho^*(s)|_E$ is the reflection in $H_s$; thus the action on $E$ is generated by the $|S|$ facet reflections of $\bar A$. Every point of $A$ has trivial stabilizer.

**(5) Alcove intersections.** If $w\ne u$, then $\rho^*(w)A\cap\rho^*(u)A=\varnothing$. For every $w\in W$, $$\rho^*(w)\bar A\cap\bar A=\{\varphi\in\bar A:\varphi(e_s)=0\text{ for all }s\in S(w)\},$$ the closed face of $\bar A$ indexed by $S(w)$ (allowing the empty face when $S(w)=S$), where $S(w)$ is the support of $w$ ([[thm-hh-parabolic-minimal-representatives-and-length-additivity]] (1)). This intersection has nonempty interior in $E$ only when $w=1$.

**(6) Abstention.** This lemma does not assert that $E=\bigcup_{w\in W}\rho^*(w)\bar A$, or that the action is properly discontinuous or cocompact. No choice principle is used.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A finite Coxeter system of affine form type, its real Coxeter space $(V,B)$, a positive radical vector $\delta$, the radical quotient $(U,b)$, the affine slice $E$, walls $H_s$, strict alcove $A$, and its closure $\bar A$ as in the cited definition.

[F1] $\operatorname{rad}(B)=\mathbb R\delta$ and every $\delta_s>0$ ([[def-cg-irreducible-affine-coxeter-type]] (2)-(4), [[lem-cg-positive-radical-and-affine-gram-exclusions]] (1)).

[F2] The quotient form $b$ is well-defined and positive definite, so $U$ is a Euclidean vector space of dimension $|S|-1$ ([[def-cg-irreducible-affine-coxeter-type]] (3)).

[F3] The direction space of $E$ is $K_\delta\cong U^*$; $b^\flat:U\to U^*$ is an isomorphism, and $b^*$ is transported to $K_\delta$ through it. Also $A=\{\varphi\in E:\varphi(e_s)>0\ \forall s\}$ while $\bar A$ is its closure ([[def-cg-irreducible-affine-coxeter-type]] (4)).

[F4] $V=\mathbb R^S$ has basis $(e_s)$, $B$ is symmetric, $B(e_s,e_s)=1$, and $r_{e_s}(v)=v-2B(v,e_s)e_s$ ([[def-cg-real-coxeter-form-and-reflection]] (1)-(3)).

[F5] The homomorphism $\rho$ satisfies $\rho(s)=r_{e_s}$ and preserves $B$ ([[lem-cg-reflection-representation-descends-and-root-norms]] (1)-(2)).

[F6] The dual action $w\cdot f=f\circ\rho(w)^{-1}$ is a left action by linear bijections; $C=\{f\in V^*:f(e_s)\ge0\ \forall s\}$ and $C^\circ=\{f:f(e_s)>0\ \forall s\}$ ([[def-cg-dual-chambers-and-reflection-hyperplanes]] (1)-(2)).

[F7] Both $\rho$ and its dual action are injective ([[thm-cg-root-length-criterion-and-faithfulness]] (3)).

[F8] For $f\in C$, $\operatorname{Stab}_W(f)=W_{S(f)}$; $wC\cap C=\{f\in C:w\in W_{S(f)}\}$; and the open chambers $wC^\circ$ are pairwise disjoint ([[thm-cg-dual-chamber-intersections-and-point-stabilizers]] (4)-(6)).

[F9] $S(w)$ is independent of the reduced expression, and $w\in W_J$ iff $S(w)\subseteq J$ ([[thm-hh-parabolic-minimal-representatives-and-length-additivity]] (1)); $W_\varnothing=\langle\varnothing\rangle=\{1\}$ ([[def-generated-subgroup]]).

[F10] Cauchy-Schwarz holds for the real inner product $b$: $|b(x,y)|\le\|x\|_b\|y\|_b$ ([[cor-inner-product-induces-a-norm]], [[thm-cauchy-schwarz-for-real-and-complex-inner-product-spaces]]).

[F11] A finite convex hull of points in a real topological vector space is compact ([[lem-locally-convex-closures-and-finite-compact-convex-hulls]]).

[F12] The topology on $E$ is that of the metric induced by the norm from $b^*$, and an isometry preserves that metric ([[cor-inner-product-induces-a-norm]], [[def-metric-topology]], [[def-isometry-and-metric-embedding]]).

[F13] A geometric simplex is the convex hull of affinely independent vertices ([[def-geometric-simplex-spanned-by-affinely-independent-vertices]]).
## Proof

**Proof technique:** describe the slice using its coordinate evaluations, then identify its vertices, faces, and Euclidean normals.

1.1 (The action on the slice.) Since every generator fixes $\operatorname{rad}(B)$ pointwise by the reflection formula [F4], $\rho(w)\delta=\delta$ for all $w$; hence $(\rho^*(w)\varphi)(\delta)=\varphi(\delta)$ and $E$ is invariant. The induced map $T$ on $U$ preserves $b$ by [F5]. For $\alpha=b^\flat(z)$ and $\beta=b^\flat(z')$ in $K_\delta$, the contragredient action sends them to $b^\flat(Tz)$ and $b^\flat(Tz')$, so their $b^*$-pairing remains $b(Tz,Tz')=b(z,z')$; hence the restriction to $E$ is an affine isometry. For each $s$, $\varphi(e_s)=0$ iff $(\rho^*(w)\varphi)(\rho(w)e_s)=0$, so walls are sent to the stated walls and the family of translates of $A$ is permuted. Since affine form type has corank one, $S$ is nonempty. Put $D=\sum_t\delta_t>0$ and define $\varphi_0$ by $\varphi_0(e_s)=1/D$ for every $s$; then $\varphi_0\in E$. If $\rho^*(w)$ fixes $E$ pointwise, it fixes $\varphi_0$ and every direction vector (a difference of two points of $E$). Every $f\in V^*$ decomposes as $f=(f-f(\delta)\varphi_0)+f(\delta)\varphi_0$, where the first term is a direction vector, so $\rho^*(w)$ fixes all of $V^*$. Injectivity in [F7] gives $w=1$. [F1, F2, F3, F4, F5, F6, F7, F12, algebra]

1.2 (The affine relation.) For $\varphi\in E$, linearity and $\delta=\sum_s\delta_se_s$ give $\sum_s\delta_s\varphi(e_s)=\varphi(\delta)=1$. Since $\delta_s>0$, the point $\varphi_0$ with $\varphi_0(e_s)=1/(\sum_t\delta_t)$ for all $s$ is in $E$ and in $A$. Also $|S|\ge2$: the nonzero radical rules out $S=\varnothing$, and for $|S|=1$ the matrix $B=(1)$ has zero radical. [F1, F3, F4, algebra]

1.3 (The coordinate vertices.) For fixed $s$, a functional vanishing on every $e_t$ with $t\ne s$ is a multiple of the coordinate functional $q_s$ defined by $q_s(e_s)=1$, $q_s(e_t)=0$ for $t\ne s$; since $q_s(\delta)=\delta_s\ne0$, at most one such functional lies in $E$. The functional $v_s$ with these zero values and $v_s(e_s)=1/\delta_s$ is well-defined on the basis $(e_t)$ and satisfies $v_s(\delta)=1$, so it is that unique point of $E$. If $\sum_s\mu_sv_s=0$ and $\sum_s\mu_s=0$, evaluation at $e_t$ gives $\mu_t/\delta_t=0$; hence the vertices are affinely independent. For every $\varphi\in E$, both sides of $\varphi=\sum_s\delta_s\varphi(e_s)v_s$ agree on each basis vector $e_t$, so this barycentric identity holds. [F1, F4, algebra]

2.1 (The closure is the coordinate simplex.) For $\psi\in K_\delta$, let $z\in U$ be the vector corresponding to $\psi$ under $b^\flat$; then $\psi(e_s)=b(z,\bar e_s)$ and $\|\bar e_s\|_b^2=B(e_s,e_s)=1$. By [F10], $|\psi(e_s)|\le\|z\|_b=\|\psi\|_{b^*}$, so each affine coordinate map $\varphi\mapsto\varphi(e_s)$ is continuous in the metric topology [F12]. It follows that $\bar A\subseteq\{\varphi\in E:\varphi(e_s)\ge0\ \forall s\}$. Conversely, if all these coordinates are nonnegative, then for $0<\epsilon<1$ the point $\varphi_\epsilon=(1-\epsilon)\varphi+\epsilon\varphi_0$ lies in $E$ and has every coordinate strictly positive; as $\epsilon\to0$, $\|\varphi_\epsilon-\varphi\|_{b^*}=\epsilon\|\varphi_0-\varphi\|_{b^*}\to0$. Thus $$\bar A=E\cap C=\{\varphi\in E:\varphi(e_s)\ge0\ \forall s\}.$$ In particular each $v_s$ belongs to $\bar A$. [F3, F4, F10, F12, step 1.2, algebra]

3.1 (Simplex, facets, normals, and compactness.) By the barycentric identity in step 1.3 and the closure description in step 2.1, each $\varphi\in\bar A$ has nonnegative coefficients $\lambda_s=\delta_s\varphi(e_s)$ summing to $1$, and conversely, for any coefficients $\lambda_s\ge0$ with $\sum_s\lambda_s=1$, the combination $\sum_s\lambda_sv_s$ lies in $E$ and has coordinate $\lambda_t/\delta_t\ge0$ at each $e_t$, so it lies in $\bar A=E\cap C$. Hence $\bar A=\operatorname{conv}\{v_s:s\in S\}$ is a simplex of dimension $|S|-1$. The face where $\varphi(e_s)=0$ is exactly the convex hull of the vertices $v_t$ with $t\ne s$, so these are its facets; in the barycentric model $\{(\lambda_s):\lambda_s\ge0,\sum_s\lambda_s=1\}$, the relative interior is exactly where every $\lambda_s>0$, so $\operatorname{int}_E(\bar A)=A$. The linear part of $\varphi\mapsto\varphi(e_s)$ on $K_\delta$ is represented under $b^*$ by $b^\flat(\bar e_s)$, whose squared norm is $b(\bar e_s,\bar e_s)=B(e_s,e_s)=1$; it points inward because the coordinate is nonnegative on $\bar A$ and positive on $A$. Its pairings with the other normals are $b(\bar e_s,\bar e_t)=B(e_s,e_t)$. A finite convex hull of points is compact by [F11], hence $\bar A$ is compact. [F2, F4, F11, F13, step 1.3, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 (Facet reflections and interior stabilizers.) Put $n_s:=B(e_s,-)\in K_\delta$; under the quotient identification this is $b^\flat(\bar e_s)$, the unit normal from step 3.1. For $v\in V$, [F4] gives $B(r_{e_s}v,e_s)=B(v,e_s)-2B(v,e_s)B(e_s,e_s)=-B(v,e_s)$, hence $r_{e_s}^2(v)=v$ and $\rho(s)^{-1}=r_{e_s}$ by [F5]. Thus for $\varphi\in E$, using symmetry of $B$, $$(\rho^*(s)\varphi)(v)=\varphi(v-2B(v,e_s)e_s)=\varphi(v)-2\varphi(e_s)B(e_s,v),$$ so $\rho^*(s)\varphi=\varphi-2\varphi(e_s)n_s$. Since $n_s$ is unit and $n_s(e_s)=1$, the affine coordinate $\varphi(e_s)$ is signed distance from $H_s$, and this is the Euclidean reflection in $H_s$. If $\varphi\in A$ is fixed by $w$, then $\varphi\in C$ and $S(\varphi)=\varnothing$; [F8] gives $w\in W_\varnothing=\{1\}$ by [F9]. [F4, F5, F8, F9, step 3.1, algebra]

4.2 (Alcove intersections.) Since $E$ is invariant and $\bar A=E\cap C$, one has $\rho^*(w)\bar A=E\cap wC$ and $\rho^*(w)A=E\cap wC^\circ$. For $\varphi\in\bar A$, the chamber intersection rule [F8] and the support property [F9] give $$\varphi\in\rho^*(w)\bar A\cap\bar A\iff w\in W_{S(\varphi)}\iff S(w)\subseteq S(\varphi),$$ which is equivalent to $\varphi(e_s)=0$ for all $s\in S(w)$. This coordinate condition is the face opposite the vertices indexed by $S(w)$, interpreted as empty when $S(w)=S$. If $w\ne u$, then $w^{-1}u\ne1$, so $wC^\circ\cap uC^\circ=\varnothing$ by [F8], and therefore $\rho^*(w)A\cap\rho^*(u)A=\varnothing$. Finally, for $w\ne1$, $S(w)\ne\varnothing$ by [F9], so the displayed face is proper and has empty interior in $E$; for $w=1$ it is $\bar A$, whose interior is the nonempty set $A$. [F3, F6, F8, F9, step 2.1, step 3.1, algebra]

5.1 (Scope and Choice.) The proof establishes only the action, simplex, wall-reflection, stabilizer, and intersection claims stated above; it does not establish that these translates cover $E$, or that the action is properly discontinuous or cocompact. All constructions use finite coordinates and explicit convex interpolation, so no choice principle is used. [given] ∎
