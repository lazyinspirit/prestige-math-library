## Definition

Assume the Axiom of Choice for the link-invariance assertion below, via
[[thm-the-hecke-trace-construction-is-an-oriented-link-invariant]] and its
Markov-equivalence supplier. Let $H(1)\subset H(2)\subset\cdots$ be the Hecke tower over
$\Lambda=\mathbb Z[v^{\pm1},z]$ of
[[def-markov-trace-on-the-type-a-hecke-tower]], with $H(n)$ generated by
$T_1,\dots,T_{n-1}$ ([[def-generic-type-a-hecke-algebra]]). For $1\le i\le
n-2$ let
$$E^{(i)}_n:=\sum_{w\in\langle s_i,s_{i+1}\rangle}T_w$$
be the sum of the six standard-basis elements over the parabolic subgroup
$\langle s_i,s_{i+1}\rangle\cong S_3$ inside $H(n)$, and let $J(n)\subset H(n)$
be the two-sided ideal generated by the elements $E^{(i)}_n$ for
$1\le i\le n-2$ ([[def-left-right-and-two-sided-ideal]]). The **Temperley--Lieb
quotient** of the Hecke tower is the quotient algebra
$$\mathrm{TL}(n):=H(n)/J(n)$$
([[def-quotient-ring]]). Put $D:=\Lambda[(v+1)^{-1}]$ and form
$H_D(n):=D\otimes_\Lambda H(n)$ and
$\mathrm{TL}_D(n):=D\otimes_\Lambda\mathrm{TL}(n)$. The normalized generators
below are defined in these scalar extensions, where $v+1$ is a unit.

**The Temperley--Lieb relations.** In $H_D(n)$ put
$$e_i:=\frac{T_i+1}{v+1}\qquad(1\le i\le n-1),\qquad \lambda:=\frac{v}{(v+1)^2}.$$
Then $e_i^2=e_i$ and $e_ie_j=e_je_i$ for $|i-j|>1$, while in the quotient
$\mathrm{TL}_D(n)$ one has
$$e_ie_{i+1}e_i=\lambda e_i,\qquad e_{i+1}e_ie_{i+1}=\lambda e_{i+1} \qquad(1\le i\le n-2).$$
These are Jones' Temperley--Lieb relations
$e_i^2=e_i$, $e_ie_{i\pm1}e_i=\lambda e_i$, $e_ie_j=e_je_i$ for $|i-j|>1$
with loop parameter $\lambda$. After adjoining $s$ with $s^2=v$ to $D$, the
elements $f_i:=(v+1)s^{-1}e_i=\lambda^{-1/2}e_i$ satisfy
$$f_i^2=\delta f_i,\qquad f_if_{i+1}f_i=f_i,\qquad f_{i+1}f_if_{i+1}=f_{i+1},\qquad f_if_j=f_jf_i\ (|i-j|>1),$$
with $\delta:=\lambda^{-1/2}=\frac{v+1}{v^{1/2}}=s+s^{-1}$ when $s^2=v$.

**The Jones specialization.** Let
$z_0:=-\frac{1}{v+1}$ and let
$$T:=\mathbb Z[v^{\pm1},(v+1)^{-1},s]\Big/\bigl(s^2-v\bigr)$$
be the ring in which $v+1$ is inverted and a square root $s$ of $v$ is
adjoined. By the universal property of the coefficient ring
$R$ of [[def-the-homflypt-coefficient-ring]] there is a unique unital ring
homomorphism $\varphi:R\to T$ with
$$v\mapsto v,\qquad z\mapsto z_0,\qquad s\mapsto s,\qquad u\mapsto s ;$$
it sends $\alpha=(uz)^{-1}\mapsto-\frac{s^2+1}{s}$. The **Jones
specialization** of the invariant $P$ of
[[def-homflypt-polynomial-from-the-hecke-markov-trace]] is
$$V(\widehat\beta):=\varphi\bigl(P(\widehat\beta)\bigr) =s^{e(\beta)}\Bigl(-\frac{s^2+1}{s}\Bigr)^{n-1} \operatorname{tr}_n\bigl(\pi_n(\beta)\bigr)\Big|_{z=z_0}\in T$$
for $\beta\in B_n$ with $n\ge1$ and closure $\widehat\beta$
([[def-closure-of-a-geometric-braid]]). Then $V$ is an invariant of nonempty oriented links with $V(\text{unknot})=1$ and
$$s^{-2}V(L_+)-s^{2}V(L_-)=(s-s^{-1})V(L_0)$$
on every skein triple; in the variable $t:=s^2$ this is the Jones skein
relation $t^{-1}V_+-tV_-=(t^{1/2}-t^{-1/2})V_0$, and $V$ is the Jones
polynomial in the convention of Birman--Brendle §4.3 property 6: positive $\sigma_1^3$ has value $t+t^3-t^4$. Mirroring a link replaces $t$ by $t^{-1}$; the convention is fixed, rather than chosen separately for each computation. The separate empty-link extension of $P$ specializes to $V(\varnothing)=-s/(s^2+1)$ in $T$; that formal extension is outside the classical Jones-polynomial identification for nonempty links.

**Caveats.** The quotient $\mathrm{TL}(n)$ is defined over $\Lambda$, but the
normalized generators and their displayed relations are in the base change
$\mathrm{TL}_D(n)$, since $v+1$ need not be a unit in $\Lambda$. No claim is
made here that the Ocneanu trace on $H(n)$ factors through the quotient map
$H(n)\to\mathrm{TL}(n)$; the quotient is recorded for the Temperley--Lieb
relations (1), and the Jones invariant is defined as the specialization of the
link invariant $P$, not as a trace on the quotient. What
is proved below is the Markov normalization after scalar extension, namely
$\operatorname{tr}_{n+1}^+(xe_n)=\frac{z+1}{v+1}\operatorname{tr}_n^+(x)$ for
$x\in H_D(n)$, whose value at $z_0$ is $\lambda$; this is Jones' Markov trace
normalization for the Temperley--Lieb parameter $\tau=\lambda$. The
identification of $V$ with the Jones polynomial uses the explicit Hecke-trace specialization in Birman--Brendle §4.3 property 6, quoted in [F6]. That source also supplies the relation on arbitrary oriented skein triples; the local skein supplier proves the braided triples.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]), the Hecke tower over $\Lambda=\mathbb Z[v^{\pm1},z]$, its scalar extension to $D=\Lambda[(v+1)^{-1}]$, the elements $e_i$, the ideals $J(n)$, the quotients $\mathrm{TL}(n)$ and $\mathrm{TL}_D(n)$, the coefficient ring $R$ and its specialization $\varphi:R\to T$.

[F1] $H(n)$ is the $\Lambda$-algebra with generators $T_1,\dots,T_{n-1}$ and relations $T_i^2=(v-1)T_i+v$, $T_iT_{i+1}T_i=T_{i+1}T_iT_{i+1}$ and $T_iT_j=T_jT_i$ for $|i-j|>1$, with standard basis $\{T_w:w\in S_n\}$ ([[def-generic-type-a-hecke-algebra]], [[def-markov-trace-on-the-type-a-hecke-tower]]).

[F2] A quotient ring is the universal ring in which the ideal is killed, and a two-sided ideal is closed under left and right multiplication ([[def-quotient-ring]], [[def-left-right-and-two-sided-ideal]]).

[F3] The $\Lambda$-linear Ocneanu trace extends by scalar extension to $\operatorname{tr}_n^+:H_D(n)\to D$; for $x\in H_D(n)$ it satisfies $\operatorname{tr}_{n+1}^+(xT_n)=z\operatorname{tr}_n^+(x)$ and $\operatorname{tr}_{n+1}^+(x)=\operatorname{tr}_n^+(x)$ ([[thm-the-ocneanu-markov-trace-exists-and-is-unique]]). Hence $\operatorname{tr}_{n+1}^+(xe_n)=\frac{z+1}{v+1}\operatorname{tr}_n^+(x)$; at $z_0=-1/(v+1)$ the factor is $\lambda=v/(v+1)^2$.

[F4] $R$ has the universal property that unital ring homomorphisms $R\to T$ correspond to units $v_0,z_0,u_0,s_0\in T^\times$ with $s_0^2=v_0$ and $v_0z_0u_0^2=z_0+1-v_0$, and $\alpha=(uz)^{-1}$ in $R$ ([[def-the-homflypt-coefficient-ring]]).

[F5] $P$ is the well-defined link invariant of [[thm-the-hecke-trace-construction-is-an-oriented-link-invariant]], with $P(\text{unknot})=1$, and it satisfies $l^{-1}P_+-lP_-=mP_0$ for $l=us$, $m=s-s^{-1}$ ([[thm-the-homflypt-skein-relation]], [[def-homflypt-polynomial-from-the-hecke-markov-trace]]).

[F6] **Literature input (quoted).** The algebra $\mathrm{TL}(n,\tau)$ with generators $e_1,\dots,e_{n-1}$ and relations $e_i^2=e_i$, $e_ie_{i\pm1}e_i=\tau e_i$, $e_ie_j=e_je_i$ for $|i-j|\ge2$ carries a Markov trace normalized by $\operatorname{tr}(1)=1$ and $\operatorname{tr}(xe_{n+1})=\tau\operatorname{tr}(x)$ for $x\in \mathrm{TL}(n+1,\tau)$ (Jones, The Jones Polynomial, printed pp. 7-9). Birman--Brendle §4.3 defines the normalized Hecke-trace invariant $P(l,m)$ (their equations preceding (18)) and asserts in property 6 that $P(t,t^{1/2}-t^{-1/2})$ is the Jones polynomial. Their equation (18) holds for arbitrary oriented skein triples. These are quoted source results for that specific trace construction, rather than an extension of the local braided-triple theorem or reliance on the inconsistent skein formula printed in Jones’ survey, p. 2.

## Proof

1.1 **Idempotents.** In $H_D(n)$, $v+1$ is a unit. From $T_i^2=(v-1)T_i+v$ of [F1], $(T_i+1)^2=(v+1)(T_i+1)$; dividing by $(v+1)^2$ gives $e_i^2=e_i$. The far-commutation $e_ie_j=e_je_i$ for $|i-j|>1$ is inherited from $T_iT_j=T_jT_i$ of [F1], since $e_i$ is a polynomial in $T_i$ with coefficients in $D$. [F1, algebra]

1.2 **The three-strand relation.** In $H_D(n)$, expanding $(T_i+1)(T_{i+1}+1)(T_i+1)$ with [F1] gives $E^{(i)}_n+v(T_i+1)=E^{(i)}_n+v(v+1)e_i$: the six standard-basis elements of the parabolic subgroup are $1,T_i,T_{i+1},T_iT_{i+1},T_{i+1}T_i,T_iT_{i+1}T_i$, and substituting $T_i^2=(v-1)T_i+v$ leaves exactly $v(T_i+1)$ in addition. Since $(T_i+1)(T_{i+1}+1)(T_i+1)=(v+1)^3e_ie_{i+1}e_i$, this reads $(v+1)^3e_ie_{i+1}e_i=E^{(i)}_n+v(v+1)e_i$; in $\mathrm{TL}_D(n)$, where the class of $E^{(i)}_n$ is zero by [F2], it becomes $e_ie_{i+1}e_i=\frac{v}{(v+1)^2}e_i=\lambda e_i$. The mirrored computation gives $e_{i+1}e_ie_{i+1}=\lambda e_{i+1}$. [F1, F2, algebra]

1.3 **The specialization homomorphism.** Take $T$ as in the definition and put $v_0:=v$, $z_0:=-1/(v+1)$, $u_0:=s$, $s_0:=s$ in $T$. These are units by construction, $s_0^2=v=s^2=v_0$, and $v_0z_0u_0^2=v\cdot(-1/(v+1))\cdot v=-v^2/(v+1)$ while $z_0+1-v_0=(-1+(v+1)(1-v))/(v+1)=-v^2/(v+1)$; by the universal property of [F4] there is a unique unital ring homomorphism $\varphi:R\to T$ with these values, and it sends $\alpha=(uz)^{-1}$ to $(s\cdot(-1/(v+1)))^{-1}=-(v+1)/s=-(s^2+1)/s$. [F4, algebra]

2.1 **Normalized generators.** In the scalar extension of $\mathrm{TL}_D(n)$ adjoining $s$ with $s^2=v$, put $f_i=(v+1)s^{-1}e_i=\lambda^{-1/2}e_i$. Then $f_i^2=\lambda^{-1}e_i=\lambda^{-1/2}f_i=\delta f_i$ with $\delta=\lambda^{-1/2}=(v+1)s^{-1}=s+s^{-1}$, and $f_if_{i+1}f_i=\lambda^{-3/2}e_ie_{i+1}e_i=\lambda^{-3/2}\lambda e_i=\lambda^{-1/2}e_i=f_i$, with the symmetric relation for $f_{i+1}f_if_{i+1}$; far-commutation is inherited from step 1.1. [F1, step 1.1, step 1.2, algebra]

2.2 **The Jones invariant.** For $\beta\in B_n$ with $n\ge1$ put $V(\widehat\beta):=\varphi(P(\widehat\beta))$; this is exactly the displayed specialization because $\varphi(u)=s$ and $\varphi(v)=v=s^2$, and $\alpha$ to $-(s^2+1)/s$. Since $\varphi$ is a ring homomorphism and $P$ is an invariant of oriented links by [F5], $V$ is an invariant of oriented links with $V(\text{unknot})=\varphi(1)=1$. Applying $\varphi$ to the skein relation of [F5] and using $\varphi(l)=s^2$, $\varphi(m)=s-s^{-1}$ gives $s^{-2}V_+-s^2V_-=(s-s^{-1})V_0$ on the braided triples supplied there, in the variable $t=s^2$. [F4, F5, step 1.3, algebra]

2.3 **The Markov normalization on the tower.** Extend the trace by scalar extension as in [F3]. For $x\in H_D(n)$, $e_n=(T_n+1)/(v+1)$, so $\operatorname{tr}_{n+1}^+(xe_n)=\frac{1}{v+1}\bigl(\operatorname{tr}_{n+1}^+(xT_n)+\operatorname{tr}_{n+1}^+(x)\bigr)=\frac{z+1}{v+1}\operatorname{tr}_n^+(x)$, and at $z=z_0$ the factor is $\frac{v}{(v+1)^2}=\lambda$. This matches the Markov normalization with parameter $\tau=\lambda$ in [F6]; it does not assert descent of the Ocneanu trace to $\mathrm{TL}_D(n)$. [F3, F6, step 1.2, algebra]

3.1 **Identification and arbitrary skein triples.** The ring $T$ is $\mathbb Z[s^{\pm1},(s^2+1)^{-1}]$ by eliminating $v=s^2$, hence injects into $\mathbb Q(s)$. In the source normalization of [F6], set its Hecke parameter to $v=s^2$ and its rescaling parameter to $\kappa=s^2$; then its generator rescaling is $\sqrt\kappa=s$ and its trace parameter is $-(1-v)/(1-\kappa v)=-1/(v+1)=z_0$. Its strand factor is $-\kappa^{-1/2}(1-\kappa v)/(1-v)=-(v+1)/s$, exactly step 1.3. Thus our $V$ is the image of the same source trace construction with $l=\sqrt{\kappa v}=s^2$ and $m=s-s^{-1}$. Property 6 identifies this with the Jones polynomial, and source equation (18) gives the stated relation for every oriented skein triple. The identities hold in $T$ since both sides lie in $T$ and its embedding into $\mathbb Q(s)$ is injective. The mirror substitution is the source chirality rule $P(l^{-1},-m)$, which here is $s\mapsto s^{-1}$. [F4, F5, F6, step 1.3, step 2.2, algebra] ∎

## Remarks

- The rescaling in the definition is the correct one: with $f_i=\lambda^{-1} e_i$ the three-strand relation would give $f_if_{i+1}f_i=\lambda^{-1}f_i$, not $f_i$; the square root $\lambda^{-1/2}$ (equivalently $v^{1/2}$) is necessary, and it exists in the Jones specialization ring $T$.
- The classical normalization of the Temperley--Lieb loop value is $\delta=s+s^{-1}$ with $s^2=v$, in agreement with $\lambda^{-1/2}=(v+1)/v^{1/2}$.
- The two-strand example [[ex-the-jones-specialization-of-a-two-strand-closure]] and the three-crossing skein example [[ex-the-hecke-trace-skein-calculation-for-a-three-crossing-braid]] compute $V$ explicitly from this definition.
