## Definition

Let $G=(V,\Sigma,P,S)$ be a context-free grammar. The **language generated by
$G$** is
$$ L(G):=\{w\in\Sigma^*: S\Rightarrow_G^* w\}. $$

A language $L$ over $\Sigma$ is **context-free** when $L=L(G)$ for some
context-free grammar $G$ over $\Sigma$.
