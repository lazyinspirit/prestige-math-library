## Remarks

The A-page table concerns strong invariants of the finite-dimensional stable Dirac-mass extension problem. Weak invariants require additional lattice or torus hypotheses, and the free-fermion table does not assert general interaction stability or a classification of every disordered system.

Proof scope: only the finite-dimensional stable free Dirac-mass model specified on the A page is in scope. The item makes no theorem claim about weak lattice invariants, disordered Hamiltonians, or interacting systems.
