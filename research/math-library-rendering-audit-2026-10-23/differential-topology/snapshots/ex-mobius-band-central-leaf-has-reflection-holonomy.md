## Example

Assume Countable Choice $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$ ([[def-countable-choice]]). Let
$\mathrm{Mob}=(\mathbb R\times(-1,1))/\mathbb Z$ be the smooth Möbius band, with
the action $k\cdot(t,x)=(t+k,(-1)^kx)$, and let $F$ be the foliation whose
leaves are the images of the horizontal segments $\mathbb R\times\{x\}$; the
projection $[t,x]\mapsto[t]$ is a bundle over the circle with fibre the open
interval $(-1,1)$, to which $F$ is transverse.
Then the middle leaf $L_{\mathrm{mid}}=\pi(\mathbb R\times\{0\})$ is
diffeomorphic to $S^1$ and its holonomy group at every point is $\mathbb Z/2$:
the generator of $\pi_1(L_{\mathrm{mid}})\cong\mathbb Z$ has holonomy germ the
reflection $x\mapsto-x$ of the transversal, and its square has trivial germ.
Every other leaf is diffeomorphic to $S^1$ (it wraps twice around the band) and
has trivial holonomy group.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The quotient $\mathrm{Mob}=(\mathbb R\times(-1,1))/\mathbb Z$ under $k\cdot(t,x)=(t+k,(-1)^kx)$, the foliation $F$ by images of the horizontal segments, the projection $\pi(t,x)=[t,x]$ and the circle quotient map $t\mapsto[t]$ of $\mathbb R/\mathbb Z$ ([[def-circle-as-real-line-mod-integers]]).

[F1] The band is the suspension of the representation $\rho:\mathbb Z\to\operatorname{Diff}((-1,1))$, $\rho(k)(x)=(-1)^kx$, over $B=S^1$: the diagonal action $k\cdot(t,x)=(t+k,\rho(k)x)$ is the displayed action, so the quotient and its descended foliation are those of the suspension construction ([[def-suspension-foliation-of-a-group-action]], [[def-leaf-of-a-regular-foliation]]).

[F2] In a suspension, the leaf through $y$ is $\widetilde B/K_y$ with $K_y=\{\gamma:\rho(\gamma)y=y\}$, the isomorphism $\pi_1(L_y)\cong K_y$ holds, and the holonomy representation is $\gamma\mapsto\operatorname{germ}_y(\rho(\gamma))$; forward-path holonomy is its inverse ([[prop-suspension-holonomy-is-the-germ-of-the-monodromy-action]]).

[F3] The holonomy group of a leaf is the image of the holonomy representation, i.e. the isotropy of the holonomy groupoid at a point of the leaf ([[prop-isotropy-of-the-holonomy-groupoid-is-the-leaf-holonomy-group]], [[def-holonomy-representation-and-holonomy-group-of-a-leaf]]).

[F4] The diffeomorphisms $x\mapsto x$ and $x\mapsto-x$ of $(-1,1)$ generate a group isomorphic to $\mathbb Z/2$, and $(-1)^k$ equals $1$ for even $k$ and $-1$ for odd $k$ ([[def-holonomy-representation-and-holonomy-group-of-a-leaf]]).

## Verification

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 **The middle leaf.** By [F1] the band is the suspension of $\rho$ over $S^1$ with the displayed action. Let $y=0$. Then $\rho(k)(0)=(-1)^k0=0$ for every $k$, so $K_0=\mathbb Z$ and by [F2] the leaf through $[0,0]$ is $\mathbb R/\mathbb Z\cong S^1$ with $\pi_1(L_{\mathrm{mid}})\cong\mathbb Z$. Its holonomy representation is $k\mapsto\operatorname{germ}_0(\rho(k))$. The generator $k=1$ accordingly has holonomy germ the reflection $x\mapsto-x$: the map $\rho(1)$ is not the identity near $0$ (it sends $x$ to $-x$), so its germ is a nonidentity involution, and $k=2$ gives $\rho(2)=\mathrm{id}$. Hence the holonomy group is the two-element group generated by this reflection, isomorphic to $\mathbb Z/2$ by [F4]. [F1, F2, F3, F4]

1.2 **The other leaves.** Let $y\neq0$. Then $\rho(k)y=(-1)^ky=y$ holds exactly when $k$ is even, so $K_y=2\mathbb Z$ and by [F2] the leaf through $[0,y]$ is $\mathbb R/2\mathbb Z\cong S^1$, the leaf wrapping twice around the band. Its holonomy representation sends $k\in2\mathbb Z$ to the identity germ of $\rho(k)=\mathrm{id}$, so the holonomy group is trivial. [F2, F3, F4]

2.1 **Conclusion.** The middle leaf is a circle whose holonomy group is $\mathbb Z/2$, generated by the reflection germ $x\mapsto-x$, while every other leaf is a circle with trivial holonomy group; the projection to the base circle exhibits the band as an interval bundle over the circle transverse to $F$. [step 1.1, step 1.2] ∎
