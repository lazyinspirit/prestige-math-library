## Example

Assume the Axiom of Choice. For $(h,j)=(2,-1)$ the disk bundle
$W=W_{2,-1}=D(\xi_{2,-1})$ has Euler number $\varepsilon=h+j=1$ and
$k=h-j=3$. The middle form of
[[lem-disk-bundle-intersection-form-and-signature-for-xi-h-j]] is generated by
the zero section $S^4\subseteq W$, whose normal bundle is $\xi_{2,-1}$ with
Euler number $1$; hence the one-by-one matrix of the form is $[1]$ and the
boundary signature is $\sigma(W_{2,-1})=1$. The normalized Thom evaluation
$\langle U\smile x,[W,M]\rangle=1$ gives the same sign in the rank-one
computation.
