## Statement

Assume AC, inherited from the signature form and L-genus suppliers. For every
$k\ge0$, with the complex orientation of $\mathbb{CP}^{2k}$,
$$\sigma(\mathbb{CP}^{2k})=1=L[\mathbb{CP}^{2k}].$$

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC, the integer $k\ge0$, the complex orientation of $\mathbb{CP}^{2k}$, and the generator $y=c_1(\gamma^*)$.

[F1] $\sigma(M)=p-q$ is the difference of the positive and negative inertia indices of the nondegenerate symmetric form $Q_M$ on the middle cohomology ([[def-signature-of-a-closed-oriented-four-k-manifold]], [[lem-middle-dimensional-intersection-form-is-symmetric-and-nondegenerate]]).

[F2] $Q_M(x,y)=\langle x\smile y,[M]\rangle$ ([[def-middle-dimensional-intersection-form]], [[def-kronecker-evaluation-pairing]]).

[F3] The in-run supplier gives $H^*(\mathbb{CP}^n;\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z[y]/(y^{n+1})$, and the coefficient bridge of [[lem-signature-is-independent-of-basis-and-field-extension-from-rationals-to-reals]] gives $H^{2k}(\mathbb{CP}^{2k};\mathbb R)=\mathbb R\,y^k$, and $\langle y^{2k},[\mathbb{CP}^{2k}]\rangle=1$ ([[lem-tangent-bundle-of-complex-projective-space-and-its-pontryagin-classes]]).

[F4] The L-genus satisfies $L[\mathbb{CP}^{2k}]=1$ for every $k\ge0$ ([[lem-l-genus-of-complex-projective-space-is-one]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct; the middle cohomology is one-dimensional with value one.

1.1 By [F3], $H^{2k}(\mathbb{CP}^{2k};\mathbb R)$ is the one-dimensional space spanned by $y^k$, and $Q_{\mathbb{CP}^{2k}}(y^k,y^k)=\langle y^{2k},[\mathbb{CP}^{2k}]\rangle=1$ by [F2]. Hence the matrix of $Q$ in the basis $\{y^k\}$ is the $1\times1$ matrix $(1)$, its inertia is $(1,0,0)$, and [F1] gives $\sigma(\mathbb{CP}^{2k})=1-0=1$. [given, F1, F2, F3]

1.2 The L-genus value is $L[\mathbb{CP}^{2k}]=1$ by [F4]. [given, F4]

2.1 Steps 1.1 and 1.2 give $\sigma(\mathbb{CP}^{2k})=1=L[\mathbb{CP}^{2k}]$ for every $k\ge0$; for $k=0$ the manifold is a point, the middle group is $H^0=\mathbb R$ with $Q(1,1)=1$, and both values are $1$. [step 1.1, step 1.2] ∎
