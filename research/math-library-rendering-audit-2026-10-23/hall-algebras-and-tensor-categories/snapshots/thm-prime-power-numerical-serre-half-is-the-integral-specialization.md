## Statement

Assume the inherited Choice hypothesis of the integral/global-basis suppliers. Let $A=(a_{ij})$ be a symmetric generalized Cartan matrix on a finite index set; finite, affine and indefinite type are all allowed. Let $\nu=\sqrt{p^a}>1$, $K=\mathbb Q(\nu)\subset\mathbb R$, and $\epsilon\in\{1,-1\}$. The algebra over $K$ on generators $E_i$ with only the symmetric numerical quantum Serre relations at parameter $\nu^\epsilon$ is canonically isomorphic, by $E_i\mapsto E_i$, to the actual Lusztig integral-half specialization $K\otimes_{\mathbb Z[t,t^{-1}],t\mapsto\nu^\epsilon}U_A^+$. For both signs, its finite homogeneous components have the classical PBW ranks and the nondegenerate specialized half form. The Green-form assertion is specifically for $\epsilon=-1$: on $H_-:=K\otimes_{\mathbb Z[t,t^{-1}],t\mapsto\nu^{-1}}U_A^-$, relabeling its generators $F_i$ as $E_i$, the primitive-generator coproduct has tensor multiplication
$$ (x\otimes y)(x'\otimes y')=\nu^{(\deg y,\deg x')}xx'\otimes yy', $$
and the rescaled form $G(x,y)=(p^a-1)^{-\operatorname{ht}\alpha}B_{\nu^{-1}}(x,y)$ for $x,y\in H_-[-\alpha]$, zero in different degrees, has $G(F_i,F_j)=\delta_{ij}/(p^a-1)$ and satisfies both Hopf adjunctions.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The finite symmetric datum, the prime-power parameter, and for each $t\in\{\nu,\nu^{-1}\}$ the numerical Serre half at $t$ and actual integral specialization $H_t^+$.

[F1] The actual integral specialization has nondegenerate homogeneous half forms at $\nu$ and $\nu^{-1}$, with classical PBW component ranks and the stated Green normalization at the inverse parameter ([[thm-prime-power-specializations-of-the-integral-quantum-half-have-a-nondegenerate-green-form]]).

[F2] For the kernel of the map from the free negative tensor algebra to the actual half, modulo the numerical Serre ideal, a nonzero least-height residual degree $-\beta$ satisfies $(\beta,\beta)=2\operatorname{ht}\beta>0$ ([[lem-a-minimal-numerical-serre-kernel-obeys-the-quantum-casimir-constraint]]). Its quantum Casimir is constructed on the actual nondegenerate integral specialization only ([[lem-the-nondegenerate-integral-specialization-has-a-quantum-casimir]]).

[F3] In the numerical Serre half at any real parameter $q>0$, $q\ne1$, a nonzero homogeneous element $z$ killed by both $i$-skew derivatives has $\sum_j a_{ij}\deg_j z\leq0$ ([[lem-numerical-serre-rank-one-skew-kernel-is-integrable]]).

[F4] The negative and positive separate Serre presentations are identical after renaming generators. The two skew derivatives on words can be read from the mixed commutator, and its expression has distinct toral factors ([[lem-the-generic-borel-half-and-opposite-half-form-a-dj-crossed-double]]). The actual specialized torus and triangular factors are independent by [[lem-the-nondegenerate-integral-specialization-has-a-quantum-casimir]], step 1.1.

## Proof

1.1 For an empty index set interpret both empty-generator halves as their coefficient ring; the assertion is then the identity map. Otherwise choose a regular Cartan datum without changing the matrix: take $P=P^\vee=\mathbb Z^{2I}$ with evaluation pairing, $h_i=(e_i,0)$, and $\alpha_j=((a_{ij})_i,e_j)$. The simple roots and coroots are independent and their pairings are $a_{ij}$, as required by [[def-symmetrizable-cartan-datum-for-a-quantum-group]]. The separate generic halves and their divided-power subalgebras depend only on these matrix relations, so this introduces no restriction. There is a canonical surjection $\pi:S_\nu\to H_\nu^+$. The actual half satisfies every numerical Serre relation by evaluation of their generic polynomial identities. It is generated by the images of the $E_i$, since the divided-power generators equal $E_i^n/[n]_\nu!$ and the denominators are nonzero. Suppose for contradiction that $\ker\pi\ne0$, and choose a nonzero homogeneous $z\in\ker\pi$ of least positive height, of degree $\beta=\sum_i n_i\alpha_i$. The degree-zero map is injective, so this height is positive. Renaming $E_i$ as $F_i$ identifies this kernel with the residual kernel of [F2]; hence $(\beta,\beta)=2\operatorname{ht}\beta>0$. [given, F1, F2, F4, assume-contra, choose, algebra]

2.1 Both skew derivatives of $z$ lie in $\ker\pi$. Here is a direct justification that requires no numerical presentation comparison. For homogeneous $x$ in the actual positive half, with $i$-weight $m$, define the two word deletions $D_i,D_i'$ with product rules from [F3]. The source mixed relations give $[x,F_i]=(D_i'(x)K_i-K_i^{-1}D_i(x))/(\nu-\nu^{-1})$; it is true on a simple generator and its extension to a product follows by expanding the commutator and crossing the torus. The deletion formulas also define $D_i,D_i'$ on $S_\nu$ by [F3]. Apply the mixed identity to the word expression $z$ whose image in the actual half is zero. Independence of the toral factors $K_i,K_i^{-1}$ in [F4], after moving them to one side, forces $\pi(D_i z)=\pi(D_i'z)=0$. Each derivative lowers height, so minimality of $z$ gives $D_i z=D_i'z=0$ in $S_\nu$, for every $i$. [F3, F4, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Extend scalars from $K$ to $\mathbb C$; the nonzero vector stays nonzero because this is a field extension. Apply [F3] for every $i$ to the common-kernel vector of step 2.1. It gives $\beta(h_i)=\sum_j a_{ij}n_j\leq0$. Symmetry of the Cartan matrix and its normalization $(\alpha_i,\alpha_j)=a_{ij}$ yield $(\beta,\beta)=\sum_i n_i\beta(h_i)\leq0$. This contradicts the strictly positive value in step 1.1. Therefore the assumed kernel vanishes, and the canonical surjection is an isomorphism. [F3, step 1.1, step 2.1, contradiction]

4.1 Now set $t=\nu^{-1}$ and repeat steps 1.1–3.1 with $t$ in place of $\nu$. The same proof applies: $t>0$, $t\ne1$, the symmetric quantum integers and Serre coefficients are invariant under parameter inversion, [F1] supplies the nondegenerate pairing and PBW ranks at both specializations, [F2] gives the Casimir constraint for $U_t$, [F3] applies at $q=t$, and [F4] supplies the specialized toral and triangular factors. Thus the least-height contradiction proves injectivity of the canonical map at $t=\nu^{-1}$ directly. The remaining form and Green-pairing assertions are exactly the inverse-parameter assertions in [F1]; under the generator renaming $F_i\leftrightarrow E_i$, they have the displayed values and coproduct twist. No semilinear map between coefficient-field fibers is used. [F1, F2, F3, F4, step 1.1, step 2.1, step 3.1, algebra, discharge-contradiction] ∎
