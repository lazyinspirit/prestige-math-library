## Definition

Let $(X_n)_{n\in\mathbb N}$ be random elements on a common probability space.
For $m\in\mathbb N$, write

$$\sigma(X_n:n\ge m):=\sigma_\Omega\left(\bigcup_{n\ge m}\sigma(X_n)\right),$$

the sigma-algebra generated by all coordinate sigma-algebras from time $m$
onward.

The **tail sigma-algebra** of the sequence is

$$\mathcal T(X_n:n\in\mathbb N):=\bigcap_{m\in\mathbb N}\sigma(X_n:n\ge m).$$

Thus a tail event is one whose membership is unchanged by altering only finitely
many initial coordinates.
