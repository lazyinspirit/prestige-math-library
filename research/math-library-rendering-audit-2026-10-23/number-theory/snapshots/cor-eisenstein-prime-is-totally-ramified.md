## Statement

If $L=K(\alpha)$ is generated by a monic Eisenstein polynomial of degree $n$
at $\mathfrak p$, then $\mathfrak p\mathcal O_L=\mathfrak P^n$ for one prime
$\mathfrak P$; thus it is totally ramified.

## Proof

**Given:** the Eisenstein polynomial at $\mathfrak p$.

1.1 Let $\mathfrak P\mid\mathfrak p$ have ramification index $e$. Comparing the valuations of the terms in the Eisenstein equation forces $n v_{\mathfrak P}(\alpha)=e$; in particular $e\le[L:K]\le n$. [given, algebra]

2.1 The inequalities in step 1.1 are therefore equalities, so $v_{\mathfrak P}(\alpha)=1$ and $e=n$. The resulting factorisation is $\mathfrak p\mathcal O_L=(\mathfrak p,\alpha)^n$; hence this is the unique prime factor and the prime is totally ramified. [step 1.1, algebra] ∎
