## Definition

Let $M$ be a smooth manifold and $k\ge 0$. A **smooth differential $k$-form**
on $M$ is a smooth section of $\bigwedge^kT^*M\to M$. The space of such forms
is denoted $\Omega^k(M)$, and $\Omega^0(M)=C^\infty(M)$.
