## Example

Use factors $A,B,C,D,E$ and the regular $2^{5-2}$ fraction generated by

$$x_D=x_Ax_B,\qquad x_E=x_Cx_D.$$

Writing $I$ for the identity word, its defining relations are $I=ABD$ and $I=CDE$, so $H=\{I,ABD,CDE,ABCE\}$ and the initial resolution is III. A complete mirror foldover yields the regular $2^{5-1}$ fraction $I=ABCE$ with $H_{[5]}=\{I,ABCE\}$: each subset $S$ remains paired with $S\triangle\{A,B,C,E\}$, so $A$ remains aliased with $BCE$ while $C$ and $DE$ separate. Reversing only factor $A$ yields the regular $2^{5-1}$ fraction $I=CDE$ with $H_{\{A\}}=\{I,CDE\}$: each $S$ remains paired with $S\triangle\{C,D,E\}$, so $A$ separates from $BD$ but $C$ remains aliased with $DE$. The targeted follow-up therefore leaves resolution III and a specific coefficient ambiguity; this is an exact residual alias list, not a claim that all main effects are clear.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The five factors and generator equations in the Example, the complete mirror foldover $J=\{A,B,C,D,E\}$, the partial foldover $J=\{A\}$, and the unrestricted fixed-design linear model with all factorial columns on the augmented runs.

[F1] [[def-regular-fractional-factorial-defining-relation-and-resolution]]: signed words multiply by symmetric difference, and resolution is the shortest nonidentity defining-word length.

[F2] [[thm-regular-fractional-factorial-aliasing-criterion]]: $H$ is generated by the independent signed generators and gives all equal- or opposite-column alias relations.

[F3] [[thm-factorial-foldover-separates-opposite-parity-aliases]]: after reversing factor set $J$, the residual relation subgroup is $H_J=\{(\epsilon,S)\in H:|S\cap J|\text{ is even}\}$.

[F4] [[thm-factorial-column-aliases-prevent-separate-identification]]: equal columns in an unrestricted fixed-design model contribute only through the sum of their coefficients, and the two coefficients are not separately identifiable.

## Verification

**Proof technique:** multiply the generators, then apply the exact parity criterion to each follow-up.

1.1 The equations give $x_Ax_Bx_D=+1$ and $x_Cx_Dx_E=+1$, so the defining words are $ABD$ and $CDE$. Their product is $ABCE$ because the repeated $D$ cancels in symmetric difference. The two incidence vectors are nonzero and distinct; over $\mathbb F_2$, two nonzero vectors are dependent only if equal, so these generators are independent. By [F2] these and the identity are exactly the four words in $H$. Their nonidentity lengths are $3,3,4$, hence the initial resolution is III. [given, F1, F2, algebra]

2.1 For the complete mirror, [F3] changes the signs of the length-three relations ABD and CDE but preserves ABCE, giving $H_{[5]}=\{I,ABCE\}$. The two eight-run blocks are disjoint because ABD has opposite signs on them, and both lie in $Q=\{x:x_Ax_Bx_Cx_E=+1\}$; choosing $x_A,x_B,x_C,x_D$ freely determines $x_E$, so $|Q|=16$ and their union is exactly this regular $2^{5-1}$ fraction. Its aliases are precisely $S$ with $S\triangle\{A,B,C,E\}$, so $A$ remains paired with $BCE$, while $C\triangle DE=CDE$ is absent and that pair separates. The shortest residual word has length $4$. [given, F1, F2, F3, step 1.1, choose, construct, algebra]

2.2 For the partial foldover on $J=\{A\}$, [F3] changes ABD and ABCE but preserves CDE, giving $H_J=\{I,CDE\}$. The two eight-run blocks are disjoint because ABD has opposite signs on them, and both lie in $Q'=\{x:x_Cx_Dx_E=+1\}$; choosing $x_A,x_B,x_C,x_D$ freely determines $x_E$, so their union is all 16 runs in this regular $2^{5-1}$ fraction. The complete residual pairs are precisely $S$ and $S\triangle\{C,D,E\}$: this includes $C=DE$ and excludes the original relation $A=BD$. The shortest residual word has length $3$. [given, F1, F2, F3, step 1.1, choose, construct, algebra]

3.1 The retained columns $c_C=c_{DE}$ in the partial design contribute as $(\beta_C+\beta_{DE})c_C$. By [F4], only this combination enters the fitted vector for this pair, so the partial follow-up does not separately identify $\beta_C$ and $\beta_{DE}$ without a restriction or further runs. [given, F4, step 2.2, algebra] ∎
