## Statement

Let $\kappa$ be antidominant, let
$$J=\{\alpha_i\in\Delta:\langle\kappa,\alpha_i^\vee\rangle=0\},$$
and assume its full zero-root subsystem is generated by $J$. For each
$\alpha\in J$, write $p_\alpha:X\to X_\alpha=G/P_\alpha$ for the simple
parabolic projection. Then there exists a finite sequence
$\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_N\in J$ such that for every coherent
$\mathcal D_\kappa$-module $M$ the following are equivalent:

1. $\Gamma(X,M)=0$;
2. there is a finite coherent filtration
   $$M=M_0\supseteq M_1\supseteq\cdots\supseteq M_N=0$$
   with $p_{\alpha_j*}(M_{j-1}/M_j)=0$ for every $j$.

The sequence is one choice, not asserted to be unique; zero successive
quotients are permitted.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** Antidominant $\kappa$, its zero simple-root set $J$, the stated
zero-root subsystem condition, and coherent $M$.

[F1] A reduced expression for $w_J$ gives a fixed coherent chain
$\mathcal E_N\subseteq\cdots\subseteq\mathcal E_0=E$,
where $E=\mathcal D_\kappa\otimes_{\mathcal O_X}\mathcal O_X(\rho)$,
$\mathcal E_N$ is generated by finitely many global differential sections,
and
$$\mathcal E_{j-1}/\mathcal E_j=\mathcal O_X(\rho)\otimes p_{\alpha_j}^\dagger N_j$$
for coherent partial-TDO modules $N_j$. This includes the empty-word case
([[lem-pa36-singular-verma-chain-and-generation]]).

[F2] The simple-parabolic direct-image functors have no positive higher
images on coherent modules and are exact there; the displayed quotients in
[F1] lie in their respective kernels
([[lem-pa36-singular-parabolic-kernel]]).

[F3] For antidominant $\kappa$, global sections is exact
([[thm-pa36-antidominant-singular-serre-quotient]]).

[F4] The regular antidominant equivalence applies to all quasicoherent
modules, restricts to coherent modules, and expresses any coherent module
as a quotient of a finite sum of its TDO by finitely many global sections
([[thm-pa36-regular-beilinson-bernstein-localization]]).
Tensoring by an integral line bundle is the TDO-shifting equivalence
([[lem-pa36-flag-tdo-pbw-and-twist]]).



## Proof

1.1 Choose once and for all the reduced expression and its simple-root sequence from [F1]. The explicit proof there constructs the Verma embeddings, their rational simple-parabolic descent, and the central-character projector proving global generation of the final term. Thus this finite chain is an actual local supplier, independent of $M$; it is not an unproved instruction to filter a localization functor. By [F2], each quotient belongs to $\ker p_{\alpha_j*}$. [F1, F2, choose]

1.2 Suppose the asserted filtration of $M$ exists. Every factor $Q_j$ satisfies $\Gamma(X,Q_j)=\Gamma(X_{\alpha_j},p_{\alpha_j*}Q_j)=0$. Applying exactness [F3] to $0\to M_j\to M_{j-1}\to Q_j\to0$ gives $\Gamma M_{j-1}=\Gamma M_j$. Starting with $M_N=0$ proves $\Gamma M=0$. [F3, given, algebra]

1.3 Now suppose $\Gamma M=0$. The weight $\kappa-\rho$ is regular antidominant. As proved by the simple-reflection and dual-positive-root calculation in [F1], for every positive root $\beta$, $\langle\rho,\beta^\vee\rangle$ is a positive integer: subtracting it from a nonintegral complex pairing leaves a nonintegral pairing, while subtracting it from a permitted integral pairing, which is nonpositive, gives a strictly negative integer. Thus no positive integral or zero pairing occurs. By the integral twisting equivalence and [F4], $\mathcal O_X(-\rho)\otimes M$ is generated by finitely many global differential sections over $\mathcal D_{\kappa-\rho}$. Tensor back with $\mathcal O_X(\rho)$; the twist bimodule identity gives a surjection $E^n\twoheadrightarrow M$ for some finite $n$, with $E$ as in [F1]. Locally this identity is the equality of line-twisted differential operators from the PBW/twist construction in [F4]. [F1, F4, construct, algebra]

2.1 Put $T=\mathcal D_\kappa\Gamma(X,E)\subseteq E$ and $Q=E/T$. The submodule $T$ is the image of the global-section evaluation map and is quasi-coherent; the local filtered-noetherian PBW argument for a TDO makes it coherent as a submodule of the coherent module $E$. Every component $E\to M$ in step 1.3 kills $\Gamma(X,E)$ because $\Gamma M=0$, hence kills $T$, giving a surjection $Q^n\twoheadrightarrow M$. The globally generated submodule $\mathcal E_N\subseteq E$ from [F1] is contained in $T$: each of its global generators is a section of $E$. Therefore the images $Q_j$ of $\mathcal E_j$ in $Q$ give $Q=Q_0\supseteq\cdots\supseteq Q_N=0$. Their factors are coherent quotients of $\mathcal E_{j-1}/\mathcal E_j$. Exactness of the corresponding $p_{\alpha_j*}$ in [F2] puts each factor in its kernel. [F1, F2, step 1.3, construct]

3.1 Take the direct sum of this chain $n$ times and its image under $Q^n\twoheadrightarrow M$. This gives the required finite coherent filtration of $M$ with the same root sequence; every factor is a coherent quotient of a finite sum of factors from step 2.1, and hence has zero corresponding direct image by [F2]. If the sequence is empty, [F1] gives $E=\mathcal E_N$ globally generated, so $T=E$, $Q=0$ and $M=0$, exactly the length-zero filtration. The argument and step 1.2 prove both directions for all the stated complex parameters, with no unproved singular-kernel or derived-localization premise. [F1, F2, step 1.2, step 2.1, algebra] ∎
