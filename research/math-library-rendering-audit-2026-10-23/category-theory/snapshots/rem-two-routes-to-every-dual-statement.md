Every dual statement on the next page may be reached in two honest ways. One
may dualize a member argument through [[thm-exactness-is-self-dual]], or one may
run the comember calculus directly via [[def-comember-and-the-dual-calculus]].
The page keeps both routes visible because Mac Lane's subtraction surrogate
[[thm-chasing-rule-the-subtraction-surrogate]] is used directly in some member
arguments while other proofs are cleaner after formal dualization.
