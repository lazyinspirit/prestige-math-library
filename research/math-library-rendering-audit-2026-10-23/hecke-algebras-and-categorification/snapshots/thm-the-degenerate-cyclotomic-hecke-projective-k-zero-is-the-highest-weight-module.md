## Statement

Assume inherited Axiom of Choice ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]) for the category-O, Fock/global and quotient suppliers invoked below.

Assume the bounded-below setting, the tower $H^\Lambda$ and its residue functors of
[[def-degenerate-cyclotomic-hecke-tower-and-residue-functors]], and the Fock data
with the four bases of [[def-dual-canonical-canonical-and-quasi-canonical-fock-bases]].
For $A\in\mathrm{Std}^\Lambda$ let $D(A)$ be the simple module of the
identification of [[thm-the-degenerate-cyclotomic-hecke-g-zero-is-the-dual-highest-weight-module]]
and let $Y(A)\twoheadrightarrow D(A)$ be its projective cover in
$\mathrm{Rep}(H^\Lambda)$. Work at source parameter $s=1$.

**(i)** The assignment $[Y(A)]\mapsto P_A$ is an isomorphism from the split
Grothendieck group $K_0(\mathrm{Proj}(H^\Lambda))$ of finite-dimensional
projective modules onto the standard highest weight lattice
$V(\Lambda)_{\mathbb Z}=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb Z}\{P_A:A\in\mathrm{Std}^\Lambda\}$,
so $\{P_A\}$ is a $\mathbb Z$-basis; at parameter one the source vector $P_A$
coincides with $T_{A^\uparrow}$, and the lattice is integrally generated by its
ground vector under divided powers.

**(ii)** Under the identifications (i) and
[[thm-the-degenerate-cyclotomic-hecke-g-zero-is-the-dual-highest-weight-module]]
the map $K_0(\mathrm{Proj}(H^\Lambda))\to G_0(\mathrm{Rep}(H^\Lambda))$ induced
by the embedding of projectives into all finite-dimensional modules is the
natural injective inclusion $V(\Lambda)_{\mathbb Z}\hookrightarrow V(\Lambda)^*_{\mathbb Z}$
of the standard lattice into the full costandard lattice.

**(iii)** The Cartan Hom pairing becomes the normalized highest pairing: with
the projected simple vectors $D_B=\pi_{\mathrm{Fock}}L_B$ one has
$C_0(P_A,D_B)=\delta_{AB}$; these are projected simple vectors, not an
unqualified identification of monomials with simples.

**(iv)** Both residue functors $e_i$ and $f_i$ on projective modules induce
operators on $K_0$ that are the Chevalley operators adjoint to their $G_0$
operators of [[thm-the-degenerate-cyclotomic-hecke-g-zero-is-the-dual-highest-weight-module]].

**(v)** The left adjoint $\pi^*$ of the Schur quotient is right exact on all
modules, sends $H$-projectives to category-O projectives and recovers the
standard-labelled prinjectives; exactness of $\pi^*$ on arbitrary modules is
neither asserted nor needed.

For an arbitrary admitted consecutive $I$, use the compatible finite cuts and the intrinsic Hecke identity of the $G_0$ theorem. They identify the projective covers and the lower integral basis $P_A$ on every selected content. Consequently (i)–(iv) hold for the full supported tower, including unbounded intervals; clause (v) concerns the Schur functor in each actual stable realization.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The inherited Axiom of Choice and the tower $H^\Lambda$ and its block-wise Grothendieck groups as in
[[def-degenerate-cyclotomic-hecke-tower-and-residue-functors]], the exact Schur
quotient $\pi$ with right-exact left adjoint $\pi^*$ of
[[def-the-higher-level-schur-weyl-projective-generator-and-schur-functor]], the
Schur-image identifications $\pi(N(A))=S(A)$, $\pi(L(A))=D(A)$,
$\pi(P(A))=Y(A)$ for $A\in\mathrm{Std}^\Lambda$ and
$\pi^*\pi(P(A))=P(A)$ of
[[thm-schur-functor-images-of-standard-simple-projective-and-tilting-objects]],
and the Fock dictionary $\pi_{\mathrm{Fock}}(P_A)=P_A$, $\pi_{\mathrm{Fock}}L_B=D_B$
at parameter one. We prove (i)-(v).

[F1] Finite-dimensional projective modules over a finite-dimensional algebra
have a free split Grothendieck group on the classes of the projective covers
([[thm-finite-dimensional-algebra-projective-classes-form-a-split-k-zero-basis]]).

[F2] On object pairs the Cartan pairing is
$\langle[P],[M]\rangle=\dim_k\operatorname{Hom}(P,M)$, its descent to
$K_0\times G_0$ and the duality of the two bases are proved in
[[thm-split-simple-projective-hom-pairing-has-dual-bases]] (definition:
[[def-projective-simple-hom-pairing-on-grothendieck-groups]]).

[F3] Exact adjoint functors acting compatibly on projectives induce adjoint maps
of Grothendieck groups for the Hom pairing
([[thm-adjoint-exact-functors-induce-adjoint-grothendieck-operators]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 **The split $K_0$ basis of covers.** Every simple finite-dimensional $H^\Lambda$-module has a projective cover, unique up to isomorphism, and the classes $[Y(A)]$, $A\in\mathrm{Std}^\Lambda$, of the covers of the simples $D(A)$ of [[thm-the-degenerate-cyclotomic-hecke-g-zero-is-the-dual-highest-weight-module]] form a $\mathbb Z$-basis of the split Grothendieck group of finite-dimensional projective modules; this is [F1] applied block by block and summed over $\alpha\in Q^+$. [F1, given, construct]

1.2 **The left adjoint on projectives.** The Schur quotient $\pi$ has a left adjoint $\pi^*$ which is right exact on all modules and sends $H$-projectives to category-O projectives; for $A\in\mathrm{Std}^\Lambda$ one has $\pi^*(Y(A))=P(A)$ and $\pi\pi^*(Y(A))=Y(A)$, so $\pi^*$ recovers the standard-labelled prinjectives, and its exactness on arbitrary modules is not needed. These are the statements of [[def-the-higher-level-schur-weyl-projective-generator-and-schur-functor]] and [[thm-schur-functor-images-of-standard-simple-projective-and-tilting-objects]]. [given, construct]

1.3 **The standard lattice and its ground generation.** The standard lattice $V(\Lambda)_{\mathbb Z}$ has $\mathbb Z$-basis $\{P_A:A\in\mathrm{Std}^\Lambda\}$, the source vector $P_A$ lies in the highest constituent with $\pi_{\mathrm{Fock}}P_A=P_A$, and at parameter one $P_A=T_{A^\uparrow}=G_{\mathrm{low}}(A)$, so $V(\Lambda)_{\mathbb Z}$ is integrally generated by its ground vector $P_{A_\Lambda}$ under the divided-power operators: the actual lower lattice is $V_{\mathcal A}(\Lambda)=U^-_{\mathcal A}v_\Lambda$ by [[def-lower-global-basis-of-a-quantum-highest-weight-module]]. This is the parameter-one specialization of [[thm-the-highest-weight-fock-submodule-has-the-qg-global-bases]], together with the basis statements of [[def-dual-canonical-canonical-and-quasi-canonical-fock-bases]] and [[thm-simples-projectives-and-tiltings-map-to-the-three-distinguished-fock-bases]]. [given, algebra]

2.1 **The identification $K_0\cong V(\Lambda)_{\mathbb Z}$.** Define $\Psi$ on the basis of step 1.1 by $\Psi([Y(A)])=P_A$; by step 1.3 both sides are free $\mathbb Z$-modules on the same index set $\mathrm{Std}^\Lambda$, so $\Psi$ is a group isomorphism onto $V(\Lambda)_{\mathbb Z}$, giving (i). The identification is realized functorially by $\pi^*$: step 1.2 shows that on projective classes the composite of $\pi^*$ with the category-O identification $[P(A)]\mapsto P_A$ at parameter one (the projective half of the tableau $K_0$/Fock identification of [[thm-the-parabolic-o-grothendieck-group-is-the-integral-fock-module]], proved in [[thm-simples-projectives-and-tiltings-map-to-the-three-distinguished-fock-bases]]) is $\Psi$, so no exactness of $\pi^*$ on non-projectives is used. [step 1.1, step 1.2, step 1.3, given, construct]

3.1 **The inclusion is the lattice inclusion.** The embedding of projectives into all finite-dimensional modules induces a map on Grothendieck groups; in the category of finite-dimensional $H^\Lambda$-modules a projective class $[Y(A)]$ maps to the class of the same module, which under the identification $\Phi$ of [[thm-the-degenerate-cyclotomic-hecke-g-zero-is-the-dual-highest-weight-module]] is the costandard coordinate vector of the module $Y(A)$. By step 1.2, $\pi\pi^*(Y(A))=Y(A)$, so this vector equals the image of $[P(A)]$ under the exact quotient, namely $P_A$ by step 1.3. Hence the composite $\Phi\circ(\text{inclusion})\circ\Psi^{-1}$ is the identity on the basis $\{P_A\}$, i.e. the natural inclusion $V(\Lambda)_{\mathbb Z}\hookrightarrow V(\Lambda)^*_{\mathbb Z}$. It is injective because the vectors $P_A$ are linearly independent in the rational highest module, and hence also as elements of its full costandard lattice. The normalized highest form is nondegenerate on each finite weight space; no claim that the embedded standard and costandard lattices coincide is used. This proves (ii). [step 1.3, step 2.1, given, algebra]

3.2 **The Cartan pairing is the normalized highest pairing.** By [F2] the Cartan pairing descends to $K_0\times G_0$ and pairs the two bases dually; under the identifications of step 2.1 and of [[thm-the-degenerate-cyclotomic-hecke-g-zero-is-the-dual-highest-weight-module]] the value on basis vectors is computed on the Fock side, where the pairing between the standard and costandard families at parameter one is the normalized highest pairing $C_0$ with $C_0(P_A,D_B)=\delta_{AB}$ by [[lem-the-fock-pairing-matches-projective-simple-and-standard-costandard-pairings]]. This proves (iii); in particular the costandard vectors entering the pairing are the projected simples $\pi_{\mathrm{Fock}}L_B=D_B$, not unprojected monomials. [step 2.1, F2, given, algebra]

4.1 **Chevalley operators on $K_0$.** Use the actual exact residue biadjunction from the tower supplier. For projective $P$ over the source, $\operatorname{Hom}(f_iP,-)=\operatorname{Hom}(P,e_i-)$ is exact, so $f_iP$ is projective; for projective $Q$ over the target, $\operatorname{Hom}(e_iQ,-)=\operatorname{Hom}(Q,f_i-)$ is exact, so $e_iQ$ is projective. Applying an idempotent by itself would not prove projectivity over a different algebra. These functors induce the adjoint operators of [F3] under the Hom pairing. Their $G_0$ counterparts are the already identified Chevalley operators, and the normalized highest pairing makes $e_i,f_i$ adjoint, so the same identification holds on $K_0$. [step 1.2, step 3.2, F3, given, algebra]

5.1 **The full integral extension.** The arbitrary-$I$ construction of [[thm-the-degenerate-cyclotomic-hecke-g-zero-is-the-dual-highest-weight-module]] fixes each selected finite-dimensional algebra $H_\alpha^\Lambda$, its simple labels and residue bimodules. An algebra identity fixes projective covers of the identified simples, uniquely up to isomorphism, so it fixes their split-$K_0$ basis. The lower lattice map is $P_A\mapsto P_{\jmath A}$ under the same common-ground-tail label map; the actual lower/global cut compatibility makes this an integral basis identification commuting with divided powers and with the pairing against the full upper basis. It therefore transports $\Psi$, the Cartan map and its pairing on each content; overlaps agree by the intrinsic algebra identity and the composable tail maps. Their weightwise direct sum proves (i)–(iv) for every admitted consecutive $I$, without claiming integral ground cyclicity for the costandard lattice. [step 2.1, step 3.1, step 3.2, step 4.1, given, construct, algebra]

6.1 **Conclusion.** Step 2.1 proves (i), step 3.1 proves the lattice-inclusion statement (ii), step 3.2 proves the pairing statement (iii) with its projected-simple qualification, step 4.1 proves (iv), step 5.1 gives their full integral arbitrary-$I$ extension, and the properties of the right-exact left adjoint stated in step 1.2 are exactly the assertion (v); at no point is exactness of $\pi^*$ on arbitrary modules, or a recovery of all category-O projectives, assumed. [step 2.1, step 3.1, step 3.2, step 4.1, step 5.1, given] ∎
