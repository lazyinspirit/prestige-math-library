## Definition

Fix positive integers $n$ and $k$, write $[k]=\{1,\ldots,k\}$, and let $(\Omega,\mathcal F,\mathbb P)$ be a probability space. A finite-horizon $k$-armed stochastic bandit in **reward-stack form** consists of probability measures $P_i$ on $(\mathbb R,\mathcal B(\mathbb R))$ with finite first absolute moments, and an independent table of integrable real random variables $(X_{s,i})_{1\le s\le n,\ i\in[k]}$ such that the table entries are mutually independent and each $X_{s,i}$ has law $P_i$. Its mean for arm $i$ is $\mu_i=\int_{\mathbb R}x\,P_i(dx)=\mathbb E[X_{s,i}]$, which does not depend on $s$. The learner may also use a random element $U$ for private randomization, provided $\sigma(U)$ is independent of the sigma-algebra generated by the entire table; a deterministic learner uses a constant $U$.

A non-anticipating policy chooses a measurable arm $A_t\in[k]$ at each round $t\in[n]$ using only $U$ and the previously observed history $(A_1,Y_1,\ldots,A_{t-1},Y_{t-1})$. Write $\mathcal H_{t-1}=\sigma(U,A_1,Y_1,\ldots,A_{t-1},Y_{t-1})$ for that history sigma-algebra. Define the pull count $T_i(0)=0$ and
$$
T_i(t)=\sum_{r=1}^{t}\mathbf 1_{\{A_r=i\}},\qquad Y_t=X_{T_{A_t}(t),A_t}.
$$
Thus each time arm $i$ is selected the learner receives the next unused entry in arm $i$'s reward stack. Set
$$
\mu^*=\max_{i\in[k]}\mu_i,\qquad \Delta_i=\mu^*-\mu_i\ge0,
$$
define the pseudo-regret $\bar R_n=\sum_{t=1}^n(\mu^*-\mu_{A_t})$, and define the realized reward regret $R_n^{\rm rew}=n\mu^*-\sum_{t=1}^nY_t$ and expected reward regret $R_n=\mathbb E[R_n^{\rm rew}]$.

Each $Y_t$ is integrable because it selects one entry of the finite table, so $|Y_t|\le\sum_{s=1}^n\sum_{i=1}^k|X_{s,i}|$ and the right-hand side is integrable. Hence $R_n^{\rm rew}$ is integrable as well.

## Definition references

[[def-online-decision-protocol]]: the learner chooses before the current round's feedback and uses only earlier revealed history.

[[def-adversary-and-feedback-model]]: a stochastic finite-arm environment has a fixed probability law for each arm, finite first moments, and the stated conditional reward-law convention.

[[def-static-dynamic-and-strongly-adaptive-regret]]: pseudo-regret is the sum of mean gaps along the action sequence; realized reward regret subtracts the observed rewards; expected regret integrates realized regret.

[[def-random-element-and-real-random-variable]]: table entries and learner randomization are measurable random elements, with the table entries real-valued.

[[def-law-or-distribution-of-a-random-element]]: the law of a random element is the pushforward probability measure on its measurable target space.

[[thm-change-of-variables-for-expectation]]: expectation of an integrable measurable function of a random element equals its integral against the law, in particular for the identity function on the real line.

[[def-expectation-of-a-nonnegative-or-integrable-random-variable]]: expectation of an integrable real random variable is its Lebesgue integral.

[[cor-expectation-linearity-monotonicity-and-modulus-bound]]: finite linear combinations of integrable random variables are integrable and expectation is linear.

[[def-independent-random-elements]]: the table entries are mutually independent as random elements, and learner randomization is independent of the table.
