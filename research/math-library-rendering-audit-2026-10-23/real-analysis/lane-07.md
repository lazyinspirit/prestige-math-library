## Diagram candidates

These are lane07-retained-scope explanatory aids for dense geometry; neither is a correctness defect and neither proof depends on a picture. Helper findings remain in their linked reports.

[Elementary solid regions: one boundary presentation adapted in all three coordinate directions](../../../../items/def-elementary-solid-region.md), lines 33, 35, 37, and 43, supplies a single patch list with three different partitions into upper, lower, and lateral patches. A six-face box gives concrete maps: `φx−(y,z)=(a,y,z)`, `φx+(y,z)=(b,y,z)`, `φy−(x,z)=(x,c,z)`, `φy+(x,z)=(x,d,z)`, `φz−(x,y)=(x,y,e)`, and `φz+(x,y)=(x,y,f)`. For the x-direction, the two x-faces are upper/lower and the other four patches lateral; the roles rotate for y and z. Three aligned views coloring this same patch list would clarify the shared-presentation relation.

[The solid generated by rotating `y=sin x` on `[0,π]` has volume `π²/2`](../../../../items/ex-volume-of-the-sine-solid-of-revolution.md), lines 46–56 and 60–62, identifies the planar region, axis, disc formula, and zero-radius endpoint sections. Rotation maps `(x,r,θ)` to `(x,r cos θ,r sin θ)` for `0≤r≤sin x`, `0≤θ<2π`; the boundary profile is `(x,sin x,θ)`, and the endpoint fibers collapse at `x=0,π`. A profile/solid pair with one disc section labeled `sin x` and `π sin²x` would show the geometric relation behind the calculation.

Among the other 144 lane07-reviewed items, none presented a comparably strong diagram case.
