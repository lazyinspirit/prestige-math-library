## Definition

The ideal generated by a subset and principal ideals.

For $S\subseteq R$, define $(S)$ to be the intersection of all two-sided ideals
of $R$ containing $S$.  This family is nonempty because it contains $R$, and
[[lem-ideal-criterion-and-intersections]] shows that $(S)$ is an ideal.  For
$a\in R$, the ideal $(\{a\})$ is written $(a)$ and is called **principal**.
