## Statement

If $F\subseteq K\subseteq L$ and both $K/F$ and $L/K$ are purely inseparable,
then $L/F$ is purely inseparable. If $K_1/F$ and $K_2/F$ are purely inseparable
subextensions of a common algebraic extension, then their compositum $K_1K_2/F$
is purely inseparable.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** Purely inseparable extensions in one of the configurations of the Statement.

[L1] In characteristic $p$, pure inseparability is equivalent to the elementwise condition that a suitable $p$-power lies in the base ([[thm-purely-inseparable-extension-characterizations]]).

[L2] Frobenius respects addition, multiplication, and nonzero inverses in characteristic $p$ ([[thm-frobenius-endomorphism-and-finite-field-automorphism]]).

[L3] A compositum is the subfield generated by the two subextensions, so each of its elements lies in a subfield generated by finitely many elements from them ([[def-field-extension-generated-subfields-and-simple-extension]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 For $a\in L$, choose $m$ with $a^{p^m}\in K$ and then $n$ with $(a^{p^m})^{p^n}\in F$ using [L1]. Thus $a^{p^{m+n}}\in F$, so $L/F$ is purely inseparable. [L1, algebra]

1.2 For $a\in K_1K_2$, [L3] places $a$ in $F(u_1,\ldots,u_r,v_1,\ldots,v_s)$ with $u_i\in K_1$ and $v_j\in K_2$. Choose one exponent $N$ whose $p^N$th power sends every generator into $F$. Applying Frobenius to a rational expression for $a$ and using [L2] gives $a^{p^N}\in F$. [L1, L2, L3]

2.1 Hence the compositum is purely inseparable by [L1]. In characteristic zero all extensions in the hypotheses are trivial, so both conclusions hold there as well. [step 1.1, step 1.2, L1] ∎
