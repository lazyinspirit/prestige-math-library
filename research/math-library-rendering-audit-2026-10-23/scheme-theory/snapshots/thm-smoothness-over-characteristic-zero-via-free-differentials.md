## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $k$ be a field of characteristic $0$ and let $X$ be a $k$-scheme locally of finite type ([[def-locally-finite-type-and-finite-type-morphism]]). If $\Omega_{X/k}$ is locally free ([[def-sheaf-relative-differentials]]), then $X$ is smooth over $k$ ([[def-smooth-morphism-schemes]]). Conversely, if $X$ is smooth over $k$ then $\Omega_{X/k}$ is locally free of finite rank ([[thm-differentials-smooth-locally-free]]); over a characteristic-zero field the two conditions are therefore equivalent. The Axiom of Choice is used through the cited regularity and Jacobian results.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A field $k$ of characteristic $0$, a $k$-scheme $X$ locally of finite type, and a point $x\in X$.

[F1] [[def-smooth-morphism-schemes]]: $f:X\to S$ is smooth at $x$ when it is locally of finite presentation at $x$, flat at $x$, and the fibre over $f(x)$ is geometrically regular at $x$; $f$ is smooth when this holds at every point.

[F2] [[lem-free-differentials-imply-regular-in-characteristic-zero]]: if $A$ is a finite-type $k$-algebra and $\Omega_{A/k,\mathfrak q}$ is free over $A_{\mathfrak q}$, then $A_{\mathfrak q}$ is a regular local ring.

[F3] [[thm-ag-perfect-field-jacobian-regularity]]: if $A=P/I$ is a finite-type algebra over a perfect field, $\mathfrak q\in\operatorname{Spec}A$ and $A_{\mathfrak q}$ is regular, then there are $g_1,\dots,g_c\in I$ and $t\in A\smallsetminus\mathfrak q$ such that $I$ is generated by the $g_j$ after inverting $t$ and some $c\times c$ minor of the Jacobian is a unit of $A_t$, so that $A_t$ is standard smooth over $k$.

[F4] [[def-ag-standard-smooth-algebra]] and [[thm-ag-standard-smooth-geometric-regularity]]: $A_t$ standard smooth over $k$ means it has a presentation with an invertible Jacobian minor; and for a finite presentation ring map $R\to S$, standard smoothness at a prime $\mathfrak q$ is equivalent to flatness of $R_{\mathfrak p}\to S_{\mathfrak q}$ together with geometric regularity of the fibre $S\otimes_R\kappa(\mathfrak p)$ at $\mathfrak q$.

[F5] [[cor-finite-type-algebra-over-noetherian-ring-is-finitely-presented]] and [[lem-field-is-noetherian]]: $k$ is Noetherian and every finite-type $k$-algebra is finitely presented over $k$.

[F6] [[lem-sheaf-differentials-affine-compatibility]] and [[def-locally-finite-type-and-finite-type-morphism]]: every point of $X$ lies in an affine open $\operatorname{Spec}A$ with $A$ a finite-type $k$-algebra, and on such an open $\Omega_{X/k}$ restricts to the sheaf attached to $\Omega_{A/k}$, so local freeness of $\Omega_{X/k}$ makes $\Omega_{A/k,\mathfrak q}$ a free $A_{\mathfrak q}$-module at the prime $\mathfrak q$ corresponding to $x$.

[F7] [[cor-fields-of-characteristic-zero-and-finite-fields-are-perfect]] and [[def-perfect-field]]: a field of characteristic $0$ is perfect.

[F8] [[thm-differentials-smooth-locally-free]]: a morphism smooth at a point has locally free relative differentials of finite rank near that point.

[F9] [[def-locally-finite-presentation-morphism]]: locally of finite presentation means that each point admits affine source and target charts whose algebra map is finitely presented.

## Proof

1.1 Regularity of the local ring. Fix $x\in X$ and choose an affine open $\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq X$ containing $x$, with $A$ a finite-type $k$-algebra and $\mathfrak q\in\operatorname{Spec}A$ the prime corresponding to $x$; such a chart exists by [F6]. Local freeness of $\Omega_{X/k}$ means that on some neighbourhood of $x$ the sheaf restricts to a free module, so $\Omega_{A/k,\mathfrak q}$ is a free $A_{\mathfrak q}$-module; the hypothesis of [F2] is therefore satisfied and $A_{\mathfrak q}$ is a regular local ring. [F2, F6, given]

2.1 A standard smooth chart. The field $k$ has characteristic $0$, hence is perfect by [F7]. As $A$ is a finite-type $k$-algebra and $A_{\mathfrak q}$ is regular, [F3] supplies $t\in A\smallsetminus\mathfrak q$ with $A_t$ standard smooth over $k$. [F3, F7, step 1.1]

3.1 Smoothness at $x$. The algebra $A_t$ is finite type over the Noetherian field $k$, hence finitely presented by [F5]. Applying the pointwise criterion [F4] to $k\to A_t$ at $\mathfrak qA_t$ shows that $k\to A_{\mathfrak q}$ is flat and that the fibre $A_t\otimes_k\kappa(0)=A_t$ is geometrically regular at $\mathfrak qA_t$. This is precisely geometric regularity of the fibre of the chart $D(t)\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ at $x$. Its finite presentation also gives local finite presentation at $x$ by [F9]. These three conditions make $X\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ smooth at $x$ by [F1], since smoothness is unchanged on restricting to an open neighbourhood. As $x$ was arbitrary, $X$ is smooth over $k$. [F1, F4, F5, F9, step 2.1]

4.1 The converse and the equivalence. Conversely, if $X$ is smooth over $k$, then [F8] makes $\Omega_{X/k}$ locally free of finite rank near every point, hence locally free; this is the stated converse. Combining it with the implication from a locally free $\Omega_{X/k}$ to smoothness proved above, the two conditions are equivalent over a field of characteristic $0$, and the Axiom of Choice is inherited through [F2], [F3], [F4] and [F8]. [F8, step 3.1] ∎

## Remarks

The characteristic-zero hypothesis enters through perfectness of $k$ in the Jacobian chart of [F3]; in characteristic $p$ the theorem fails, the standard example being $X=\operatorname{Spec}k[t]/(t^p)$ with free $\Omega_{X/k}$ and a nonreduced, nonregular point.
