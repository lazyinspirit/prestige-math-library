## Statement

Flatness is not the same as "all fibres isomorphic", and it does not force
smoothness. The standard witness over $\mathbb Q$ is the family
$$f:\operatorname{Spec}\frac{\mathbb Q[t,x]}{(x^2-t)}\longrightarrow\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Q[t]=\mathbb A^1_{\mathbb Q},\qquad B=\frac{\mathbb Q[t,x]}{(x^2-t)}.$$

The equation $x^2=t$ is monic in $x$ of degree $2$, so by monic division
every class in $B$ has a unique representative $a(t)+b(t)x$ with
$a,b\in\mathbb Q[t]$; hence $B$ is free with basis $1,x$ over $A=\mathbb Q[t]$,
therefore flat over $A$ ([[cor-free-modules-are-projective-and-flat]]), so $f$
is a flat morphism ([[def-flat-morphism-schemes]]).

The fibres are computed from the tensor product
([[def-scheme-theoretic-fibre]], [[def-geometric-fibre]]). At $t=0$,
$$B\otimes_A A/(t)\cong\frac{\mathbb Q[x]}{(x^2)}$$ in which $x$ is a nonzero
nilpotent: it is nonzero because $x=x^2q$ in $\mathbb Q[x]$ would give
$1=xq(x)$ by cancellation in the domain $\mathbb Q[x]$, contradicting degrees.
At $t=1$,
$$B\otimes_A A/(t-1)\cong\frac{\mathbb Q[x]}{(x^2-1)}\cong\mathbb Q\times\mathbb Q,$$ the isomorphism being evaluation $g\mapsto(g(1),g(-1))$ with
inverse $(a,b)\mapsto\frac{a+b}{2}+\frac{a-b}{2}x$ and kernel generated by the
comaximal factorisation $x^2-1=(x-1)(x+1)$
([[thm-chinese-remainder-theorem-for-comaximal-ideals]]).

So the fibre over $t=0$ has one point (the local ring $\mathbb Q[x]/(x^2)$ has
the single prime $(x)$, [[def-prime-and-maximal-ideals]]), while the fibre over
$t=1$ has two points. The fibres are therefore not isomorphic as schemes, and
the special fibre is not even reduced. Since a morphism that is smooth at a
point has geometrically regular, hence reduced, fibre there
([[def-smooth-morphism-schemes]]), $f$ is flat but not smooth at the origin;
the visual phenomenon is the collision of the two branches $x=\pm\sqrt t$ as
$t\to0$, which flatness permits because flatness constrains only the
$A$-module structure, not the geometry of the special fibre. No fibre-dimension
theorem and no choice principle are used.
