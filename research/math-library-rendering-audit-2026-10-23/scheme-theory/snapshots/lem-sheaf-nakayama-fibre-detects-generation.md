## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice, inherited from the construction of associated
sheaves. Let $X$ be a scheme, let $\mathcal F$ be a quasi-coherent
$\mathcal O_X$-module of finite type
([[def-finite-type-finite-presentation-module-sheaf]]) and let $x\in X$, with
fibre $\mathcal F(x)=\mathcal F_x\otimes_{\mathcal O_{X,x}}\kappa(x)$
([[def-fibre-of-module-at-point]]).

1. $\mathcal F(x)=0$ if and only if $\mathcal F$ vanishes on some open
   neighbourhood of $x$.
2. If $W$ is a neighbourhood of $x$ and $s_1,\dots,s_n\in\mathcal F(W)$ are
   sections whose images in $\mathcal F(x)$ span $\mathcal F(x)$ over
   $\kappa(x)$, then there is an affine open $U\subseteq W$ containing $x$ such
   that $s_1|_U,\dots,s_n|_U$ generate $\mathcal F|_U$, that is, the induced
   morphism $\mathcal O_U^{\,n}\to\mathcal F|_U$ is an epimorphism.

No new arbitrary-index choice is made: the argument uses only finitely many
generators and finitely many denominators.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The Axiom of Choice; a scheme $X$; a finite-type quasi-coherent
$\mathcal O_X$-module $\mathcal F$; a point $x\in X$; a neighbourhood $W$ of
$x$ and sections $s_1,\dots,s_n\in\mathcal F(W)$.

[F1] $\mathcal F(x)=\mathcal F_x/\mathfrak m_x\mathcal F_x$ is a vector space
over $\kappa(x)=\mathcal O_{X,x}/\mathfrak m_x$, restriction to an open
$U\ni x$ gives $(\mathcal F|_U)(x)=\mathcal F(x)$, and $\mathcal F(x)=0$ if
$\mathcal F$ vanishes near $x$ ([[def-fibre-of-module-at-point]],
[[def-residue-field-scheme-point]]).

[F2] For an affine scheme $\operatorname{Spec}A$ with associated sheaf
$\widetilde M$ and a prime $\mathfrak p$, the stalk is
$(\widetilde M)_{\mathfrak p}\cong M_{\mathfrak p}$ over
$A_{\mathfrak p}\cong\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Spec}A,\mathfrak p}$
([[lem-associated-sheaf-stalk-localization]]).

[F3] $\mathcal F$ is of finite type: at each point there is an affine open
$U=\operatorname{Spec}A$ with $\mathcal F|_U\cong\widetilde M$ for a finitely
generated $A$-module $M$, and equivalently $\mathcal F|_U$ is generated by
finitely many sections over $U$; on an affine open, global sections are
$\Gamma(U,\widetilde M)\cong M$ ([[def-finite-type-finite-presentation-module-sheaf]],
[[thm-associated-module-sheaf-exists]],
[[def-generated-cyclic-finitely-generated-and-free-modules]]).

[F4] Determinant trick: if $R$ is a commutative ring, $I\trianglelefteq R$ an
ideal and $N$ a finitely generated $R$-module with $IN=N$, then there is
$a\in I$ with $(1-a)N=0$ ([[lem-determinant-trick-for-nakayama]]).

[F5] Localisation is exact and commutes with kernels, images and cokernels: for
an $R$-linear map $\psi$, $S^{-1}(\operatorname{coker}\psi)\cong\operatorname{coker}(S^{-1}\psi)$
([[thm-localisation-of-modules-is-exact]],
[[cor-localisation-commutes-with-kernels-images-and-cokernels]]).

[F6] A fraction vanishes: $m/s=0$ in $S^{-1}M$ exactly when $um=0$ for some
$u\in S$ ([[lem-zero-in-a-localised-module]]).

[F7] Axiom of Choice: every family of nonempty sets has a choice function
([[def-axiom-of-choice]]).



**Proof technique:** direct; pass to an affine chart, express the fibre as
$M_{\mathfrak p}/\mathfrak pM_{\mathfrak p}$, apply the determinant trick at
the local ring, and spread the vanishing of a finitely generated localised
module to a basic open.

## Proof

1.1 Let $U=\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq W$ be an affine open neighbourhood of $x$, let $\mathfrak p\subseteq A$ be the prime corresponding to $x$, and write $\mathcal F|_U\cong\widetilde M$ with $M$ a finitely generated $A$-module by [F3]; then $\mathcal O_{X,x}\cong A_{\mathfrak p}$, $\kappa(x)\cong A_{\mathfrak p}/\mathfrak pA_{\mathfrak p}$, and by [F2] the fibre of the Statement is $\mathcal F(x)\cong M_{\mathfrak p}/\mathfrak pM_{\mathfrak p}$, while global sections satisfy $\Gamma(U,\widetilde M)\cong M$ so the restrictions $s_i|_U$ correspond to elements $g_i\in M$. [F1, F2, F3]

1.2 Nakayama at the point: for a finitely generated $A$-module $N$ and a prime $\mathfrak p$, one has $N_{\mathfrak p}=0$ if and only if $N_{\mathfrak p}=\mathfrak pN_{\mathfrak p}$. The implication from left to right is immediate; conversely, if $N_{\mathfrak p}=\mathfrak pN_{\mathfrak p}$, apply [F4] over the local ring $A_{\mathfrak p}$ with $I=\mathfrak pA_{\mathfrak p}$ to the finitely generated $A_{\mathfrak p}$-module $N_{\mathfrak p}$ to obtain $a\in\mathfrak pA_{\mathfrak p}$ with $(1-a)N_{\mathfrak p}=0$, and since $1-a\notin\mathfrak pA_{\mathfrak p}$ is a unit of the local ring, $N_{\mathfrak p}=0$. [F4]

1.3 Vanishing spreads to a basic open: if $N$ is a finitely generated $A$-module with $N_{\mathfrak p}=0$, then $N_t=0$ for some $t\notin\mathfrak p$. Choose finitely many generators $n_1,\dots,n_r$ of $N$; since $n_i/1=0$ in $N_{\mathfrak p}$, [F6] gives $t_i\notin\mathfrak p$ with $t_in_i=0$, and with $t=t_1\cdots t_r\notin\mathfrak p$ one has $tn_i=0$ for all $i$, so $t$ annihilates every element of $N$ and therefore every element of $N_t$ is zero; only finitely many existential instantiations occur, so no arbitrary-index choice is used. [F6, F7]

2.1 Let $\psi:A^n\to M$ be the $A$-linear map with $\psi(e_i)=g_i$, and let $C=\operatorname{coker}\psi=M/\psi(A^n)$, a finitely generated $A$-module; by [F5] its localisation is $C_{\mathfrak p}\cong M_{\mathfrak p}/\psi_{\mathfrak p}(A_{\mathfrak p}^n)$, and $C_{\mathfrak p}/\mathfrak pC_{\mathfrak p}\cong M_{\mathfrak p}/(\psi_{\mathfrak p}(A_{\mathfrak p}^n)+\mathfrak pM_{\mathfrak p})$. The images of $s_1,\dots,s_n$ span $\mathcal F(x)$ over $\kappa(x)$ precisely when $M_{\mathfrak p}=\psi_{\mathfrak p}(A_{\mathfrak p}^n)+\mathfrak pM_{\mathfrak p}$, that is, when $C_{\mathfrak p}=\mathfrak pC_{\mathfrak p}$; by step 1.2 applied to the finitely generated module $C$, this is equivalent to $C_{\mathfrak p}=0$. [F5, step 1.1, step 1.2]

2.2 Proof of claim 1: $\mathcal F(x)=0$ if and only if $M_{\mathfrak p}=\mathfrak pM_{\mathfrak p}$ by step 1.1, if and only if $M_{\mathfrak p}=0$ by step 1.2, if and only if $M_t=0$ for some $t\notin\mathfrak p$ by step 1.3 together with the trivial implication; for such $t$ the open $D(t)$ contains $x$ and $\mathcal F|_{D(t)}\cong\widetilde{M_t}=0$, so $\mathcal F$ vanishes on a neighbourhood of $x$, while conversely vanishing near $x$ gives $\mathcal F_x=0$ and hence $\mathcal F(x)=0$ by [F1]. [F1, step 1.1, step 1.2, step 1.3]

3.1 Proof of claim 2: if the images of $s_1,\dots,s_n$ span $\mathcal F(x)$, then $C_{\mathfrak p}=0$ by step 2.1, so step 1.3 applied to the finitely generated module $C$ gives $t\notin\mathfrak p$ with $C_t=0$, that is, $M_t=\psi_t(A_t^n)$; the restrictions of $g_1,\dots,g_n$ to $D(t)$ therefore generate $\Gamma(D(t),\widetilde M)\cong M_t$, so the induced morphism $\mathcal O_{D(t)}^n\to\mathcal F|_{D(t)}$ is an epimorphism on the affine open $D(t)\subseteq U\subseteq W$ containing $x$. [step 1.1, step 1.3, step 2.1]

4.1 The Axiom of Choice is inherited from [F2] and the associated-sheaf construction behind [F3]; the argument itself instantiates finitely many generators and denominators, so it makes no new arbitrary-index choice, and both claims are proved in steps 2.2 and 3.1. [F2, F3, F7, step 2.2, step 3.1] ∎
