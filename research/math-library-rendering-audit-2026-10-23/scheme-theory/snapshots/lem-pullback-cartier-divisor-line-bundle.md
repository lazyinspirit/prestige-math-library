## Statement

Let $f:X\to Y$ be a morphism of schemes and let $D$ be a Cartier divisor on
$Y$ whose pullback $f^*D$ is defined
([[def-pullback-cartier-divisor]]). Then there is a canonical isomorphism of
$\mathcal O_X$-modules
$$\mathcal O_X(f^*D)\;\cong\;f^*\mathcal O_Y(D),$$
where $f^*$ is the pullback of modules ([[def-pullback-module-ringed-spaces]])
and $\mathcal O_Y(D)$, $\mathcal O_X(f^*D)$ are the invertible sheaves of
[[def-invertible-sheaf-of-cartier-divisor]]. If $D$ is effective, then the
constant section $1\in\Gamma(Y,\mathcal O_Y(D))$ corresponds under this
isomorphism to the constant section $1\in\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X(f^*D))$, and
the isomorphism is independent of the admissible local-equation datum used to
define $f^*D$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A morphism $f:X\to Y$, a Cartier divisor $D$ on $Y$, and an
$f$-admissible local-equation datum $\{(U_i,a_i/s_i)\}$ representing $D$, with
pulled-back equations $g_i'=f^{\#}(a_i)/f^{\#}(s_i)$ on $f^{-1}U_i$
([[def-pullback-cartier-divisor]]).

[F1] $f^*D$ is the Cartier divisor on $X$ represented by the local-equation
datum $\{(f^{-1}U_i,g_i')\}$; it is independent of the admissible datum, and
for effective $D$ with regular equations $f_i\in S_Y(U_i)$ one may take
$g_i'=f^{\#}(f_i)$, the defined pullback then being effective
([[def-pullback-cartier-divisor]]).

[F2] For the datum $\{g_i\}$ of $D$ one has
$\mathcal O_Y(D)|_{U_i}=g_i^{-1}\mathcal O_{U_i}$, and
$\mathcal O_X(f^*D)|_{f^{-1}U_i}=g_i'^{-1}\mathcal O_{f^{-1}U_i}$; the sheaves
are well defined and independent of the datum
([[def-invertible-sheaf-of-cartier-divisor]]).

[F3] On the overlap $U_i\cap U_j$ one has $g_i/g_j\in
\mathcal O_Y^{\times}(U_i\cap U_j)$; consequently
$g_i'/g_j'=f^{\#}(g_i/g_j)\in\mathcal O_X^{\times}(f^{-1}(U_i\cap U_j))$
([[def-cartier-divisor]], [[def-pullback-cartier-divisor]]).

[F4] The pullback of modules is
$f^*\mathcal G=\mathcal O_X\otimes_{f^{-1}\mathcal O_Y}f^{-1}\mathcal G$
([[def-pullback-module-ringed-spaces]]), its stalks satisfy
$(f^*\mathcal G)_x\cong\mathcal O_{X,x}\otimes_{\mathcal O_{Y,f(x)}}\mathcal G_{f(x)}$
([[lem-stalk-tensor-product]], [[lem-stalk-inverse-image-sheaf]]), and it is a
functor. If $\mathcal M$ is an invertible $\mathcal O_Y$-module with generator
$e$ over an open $V$, then $f^*\mathcal M$ is an invertible
$\mathcal O_X$-module with generator $f^*(e)$ over $f^{-1}V$: at $x$ the
stalk $\mathcal M_{f(x)}=\mathcal O_{Y,f(x)}e$ is free of rank one, so
$\mathcal O_{X,x}\otimes_{\mathcal O_{Y,f(x)}}\mathcal M_{f(x)}\cong
\mathcal O_{X,x}\cdot(1\otimes e)$ by the tensor unit isomorphism
([[thm-unit-isomorphisms-for-module-tensor-products]]), and the module map
$\mathcal O_{f^{-1}V}\to f^*\mathcal M|_{f^{-1}V}$ sending $1$ to $f^*(e)$ is
an isomorphism on stalks, hence an isomorphism of sheaves
([[thm-sheaf-morphism-isomorphism-stalkwise]]).

[F5] An isomorphism of sheaves of modules may be presented by a gluing datum
of local isomorphisms on a common cover; if two local isomorphisms agree on
overlaps, they glue to a global isomorphism
([[thm-gluing-sheaves]], [[def-gluing-datum-sheaves]],
[[def-sheaf-on-topological-space]]).

## Proof

1.1 **Generators and transitions.** On $U_i$ the sheaf $\mathcal O_Y(D)$ is freely generated by $e_i=g_i^{-1}$, and on $U_j$ by $e_j=g_j^{-1}$; on the overlap $e_i=u_{ij}^{-1}e_j$ with $u_{ij}=g_i/g_j$, a unit of $\mathcal O_Y(U_i\cap U_j)$. On $X$, the sheaf $\mathcal O_X(f^*D)$ is freely generated over $f^{-1}U_i$ by $e_i'=g_i'^{-1}$, and $e_i'=v_{ij}^{-1}e_j'$ with $v_{ij}=g_i'/g_j'=f^{\#}(u_{ij})$, a unit of $\mathcal O_X(f^{-1}(U_i\cap U_j))$. [F2, F3]

2.1 **Local isomorphisms.** By [F4] the pullback $f^*\mathcal O_Y(D)$ is freely generated over $f^{-1}U_i$ by $f^*(e_i)$. Define an $\mathcal O_{f^{-1}U_i}$-linear map $\varphi_i:f^*\mathcal O_Y(D)|_{f^{-1}U_i}\to\mathcal O_X(f^*D)|_{f^{-1}U_i}$ by $\varphi_i(f^*(e_i))=e_i'$. Since source and target are freely generated of rank one by these sections, $\varphi_i$ is an isomorphism. [F4, step 1.1]

3.1 **Compatibility on overlaps.** Over $f^{-1}(U_i\cap U_j)$ one has $f^*(e_i)=f^*(u_{ij}^{-1}e_j)=f^{\#}(u_{ij})^{-1}f^*(e_j)$ by the functoriality of $f^*$ and step 1.1, while $e_i'=f^{\#}(u_{ij})^{-1}e_j'$ by step 1.1. Hence $\varphi_i(f^*(e_i))=f^{\#}(u_{ij})^{-1}\varphi_j(f^*(e_j))=\varphi_j(f^*(e_i))$, so $\varphi_i$ and $\varphi_j$ agree on the overlap. [F4, step 1.1, step 2.1]

4.1 **Gluing.** The local isomorphisms $\varphi_i$ cover $X$ on the opens $f^{-1}U_i$ and agree on all overlaps by step 3.1, so they glue to an isomorphism of $\mathcal O_X$-modules $\Theta:f^*\mathcal O_Y(D)\to\mathcal O_X(f^*D)$. [F5, step 3.1]

5.1 **Canonical sections for effective divisors.** Suppose $D$ is effective, with regular equations $f_i$ on a refined cover, and put $f_i'=f^{\#}(f_i)$ and $e_i=f_i^{-1}$, $e_i'=f_i'^{-1}$. The constant section $1\in\Gamma(Y,\mathcal O_Y(D))$ satisfies $1=f_i e_i$ over $U_i$, and the constant section $1\in\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X(f^*D))$ satisfies $1=f_i' e_i'$ over $f^{-1}U_i$. Since $f^*$ is a functor and $\Theta(f^*(e_i))=e_i'$, one has $\Theta(f^*(1))=\Theta(f_i' f^*(e_i))=f_i' e_i'=1$, so the constant sections correspond. [F1, F2, F4, step 4.1]

6.1 **Conclusion.** $\Theta$ is a canonical isomorphism $\mathcal O_X(f^*D)\cong f^*\mathcal O_Y(D)$, and it matches the constant sections in the effective case; replacing the admissible datum by another one changes $e_i$ and $e_i'$ by the same units and hence leaves $\Theta$ unchanged, so the isomorphism is independent of the datum. [step 4.1, step 5.1] ∎

No choice principle is used: on each chart the isomorphism is determined by
the given generators, and the local maps glue because they agree on overlaps.
If $X$ or $Y$ is empty both sheaves are the zero sheaf and the isomorphism is
the unique one.
