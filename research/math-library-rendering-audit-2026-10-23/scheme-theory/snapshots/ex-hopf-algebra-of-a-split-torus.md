## Example

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $k$ be a field, let $n\ge1$ and let $T=\mathbf G_m^n=\operatorname{Spec}k[t_1^{\pm1},\dots,t_n^{\pm1}]$ be the split torus of rank $n$, a product of copies of the multiplicative group scheme of [[lem-general-linear-group-scheme-and-its-coordinate-ring]]. Then the coordinate Hopf algebra of $T$ ([[def-coordinate-hopf-algebra-of-affine-group-scheme]]) is given on the Laurent generators by
$$\Delta(t_i)=t_i\otimes t_i,\qquad\varepsilon(t_i)=1,\qquad S(t_i)=t_i^{-1}\qquad(1\le i\le n),$$
extended by multiplicativity; the $t_i$ are units, and $T(R)=(R^\times)^n$ with pointwise multiplication for every commutative unital $k$-algebra $R$. For integers $r_1,\dots,r_n\ge1$ the ideal generated by $t_1^{r_1}-1,\dots,t_n^{r_n}-1$ is a Hopf ideal ([[lem-hopf-ideal-kernels-and-quotients]]) and defines the closed subgroup scheme $\mu_{r_1}\times\cdots\times\mu_{r_n}\subseteq T$, where $\mu_r(R)=\{a\in R^\times:a^r=1\}$. In characteristic $p>0$ the scheme $\mu_p$ has coordinate ring $k[t]/(t-1)^p$, which is nonreduced, so it is not determined by its $k$-points.

## Verification

**Given:** AC, a field $k$, an integer $n\ge1$, the torus $T=\mathbf G_m^n$ with coordinate ring $A=k[t_1^{\pm1},\dots,t_n^{\pm1}]$, integers $r_1,\dots,r_n\ge1$, and the prime $p=\operatorname{char}k$ when $p>0$.

[F1] For $\mathbf G_m=\operatorname{Spec}k[t,t^{-1}]$ the comorphisms are $\Delta(t)=t\otimes t$, $\varepsilon(t)=1$, $S(t)=t^{-1}$, and $\mathbf G_m(R)=R^\times$. ([[lem-general-linear-group-scheme-and-its-coordinate-ring]])

[F2] Products of affine schemes correspond to tensor products of coordinate rings, $\operatorname{Spec}(B\otimes_kC)\cong\operatorname{Spec}B\times_k\operatorname{Spec}C$, and a product of group schemes is a group scheme with the componentwise group law. ([[thm-affine-fibre-product-tensor-ring]], [[def-group-scheme-over-a-field]])

[F3] A quotient by a Hopf ideal carries a Hopf algebra structure with the quotient map a Hopf morphism, and the induced map of spectra is a closed immersion. For a finitely generated commutative Hopf algebra $B$, [F5] supplies finite type under the assumed AC; by [F2] and the affine anti-equivalence, the Hopf identities transpose to the group identities, making $\operatorname{Spec}B$ a group scheme with structure comorphisms $B\to B\otimes_kB$, $B\to k$, $B\to B$. ([[lem-hopf-ideal-kernels-and-quotients]], [[lem-quotient-spectrum-map-is-a-closed-immersion]], [[def-commutative-hopf-algebra-over-a-field]], [[thm-affine-scheme-ring-anti-equivalence]])

[F5] Under AC, affine schemes are quasi-compact, so the spectrum of a finitely generated $k$-algebra is of finite type by its single affine chart. Both $A$ and $A/I$ are finitely generated, the latter by the images of the Laurent generators. ([[cor-affine-scheme-quasi-compact]], [[def-finite-type-and-module-finite-algebras]], [[def-locally-finite-type-and-finite-type-morphism]], [[def-axiom-of-choice]])

[F4] In characteristic $p>0$ one has $t^p-1=(t-1)^p$ in $K[t]$, and $\mu_p(K)=\{1\}$. ([[prop-p-power-roots-of-unity-in-characteristic-p]])

1.1 The coordinate ring of $T$ is the $n$-fold tensor product $\bigotimes_{i=1}^nk[t_i,t_i^{-1}]=k[t_1^{\pm1},\dots,t_n^{\pm1}]$ by [F2]. Its componentwise operations satisfy the group identities, and [F5] supplies finite type; hence $T$ is a group scheme and $A$ is a commutative Hopf algebra with structure maps transposed from the group operations ([[lem-coordinate-ring-of-affine-group-scheme-is-a-hopf-algebra]]); the product of the $\mathbf G_m$ structures has comorphisms $\Delta(t_i)=t_i\otimes t_i$, $\varepsilon(t_i)=1$ and $S(t_i)=t_i^{-1}$ on each Laurent generator, extended multiplicatively. These images are units, so they define $k$-algebra homomorphisms out of $A$, and the Hopf axioms hold because they hold componentwise for $k[t_i,t_i^{-1}]$ by [F1]. Points satisfy $T(R)=\mathbf G_m(R)^n=(R^\times)^n$ with pointwise multiplication. [F1, F2, F5]

2.1 Put $I=(t_1^{r_1}-1,\dots,t_n^{r_n}-1)\subseteq A$. For each $i$ one has $\Delta(t_i^{r_i}-1)=t_i^{r_i}\otimes t_i^{r_i}-1=(t_i^{r_i}-1)\otimes t_i^{r_i}+1\otimes(t_i^{r_i}-1)\in I\otimes_kA+A\otimes_kI$, also $\varepsilon(t_i^{r_i}-1)=1-1=0$ and $S(t_i^{r_i}-1)=t_i^{-r_i}-1=-t_i^{-r_i}(t_i^{r_i}-1)\in I$; hence $I$ is a Hopf ideal by [F3], and $A/I$ carries the quotient Hopf algebra structure with $A\to A/I$ a Hopf morphism. [F3, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 By [F3] the quotient map gives a closed immersion $\mu_{r_1}\times\cdots\times\mu_{r_n}:=\operatorname{Spec}(A/I)\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Spec}A=T$, and the quotient Hopf structure gives its group operations. By [F5] it is of finite type, and the inclusion preserves the group operations, hence is a closed subgroup scheme. Its $R$-points are the algebra maps $A/I\to R$, equivalently the tuples $(a_1,\dots,a_n)$ with $a_i\in R^\times$ and $a_i^{r_i}=1$, and the group law is the restriction of the pointwise product of $T(R)$; this is exactly $\mu_{r_1}(R)\times\cdots\times\mu_{r_n}(R)$ with $\mu_r(R)=\{a\in R^\times:a^r=1\}$. [F3, F5, step 1.1, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 Suppose $\operatorname{char}k=p>0$ and take $n=1$, $r_1=p$. By [F4], $t^p-1=(t-1)^p$ in $k[t]$, so $k[t,t^{-1}]/(t^p-1)\cong k[t]/(t^p-1)\cong k[s]/(s^p)$ with $s=t-1$; the class $s$ is a nonzero nilpotent, so $\mu_p$ is nonreduced. By [F4] again, $\mu_p(k)=\{1\}$, so the $k$-points are trivial while the coordinate ring $k[s]/(s^p)$ is not the coordinate ring $k$ of the trivial group scheme; in particular the scheme, and with it the group scheme, is not determined by its $k$-points. Every computation used the explicit Laurent generators and finitely many relations. [F4, step 3.1, algebra] ∎
