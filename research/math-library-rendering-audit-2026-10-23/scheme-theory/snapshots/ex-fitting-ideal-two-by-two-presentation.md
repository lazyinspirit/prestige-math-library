## Example

Assume the Axiom of Choice, inherited from the existence theorem for the
associated sheaf. Let $k$ be a field, let $A=k[x,y]$ be the polynomial ring on
the two indeterminates $x,y$
([[def-polynomial-ring-on-a-family-of-indeterminates]]), and let
$$\varphi:A^2\longrightarrow A^2$$
be the $A$-linear map whose matrix in the standard bases is
$\operatorname{diag}(x,y)$, so that $\varphi(e_1)=xe_1$ and
$\varphi(e_2)=ye_2$
([[def-generated-cyclic-finitely-generated-and-free-modules]]). Put
$$M=\operatorname{coker}\varphi=A^2/\operatorname{im}\varphi$$
([[def-module-homomorphism-kernel-image-and-cokernel]]), let
$X=\operatorname{Spec}A$, and let $\mathcal F=\widetilde M$ be the associated
sheaf ([[thm-associated-module-sheaf-exists]],
[[def-associated-sheaf-module-affine-scheme]]).

Then $M$ is finitely generated and $\mathcal F$ is a quasi-coherent
$\mathcal O_X$-module of finite type
([[def-finite-type-finite-presentation-module-sheaf]]), and:

1. The Fitting ideals of $M$ ([[def-fitting-ideal-sheaf]]) are
   $$\operatorname{Fitt}_0(M)=(xy),\qquad\operatorname{Fitt}_1(M)=(x,y),\qquad\operatorname{Fitt}_2(M)=A,$$
   and correspondingly $\operatorname{Fitt}_0(\mathcal F)=(xy)^\sim$,
   $\operatorname{Fitt}_1(\mathcal F)=(x,y)^\sim$ and
   $\operatorname{Fitt}_2(\mathcal F)=\mathcal O_X$, with
   $\operatorname{Fitt}_k(\mathcal F)=\mathcal O_X$ for every $k\ge2$ and
   $\operatorname{Fitt}_{-1}(\mathcal F)=0$.
2. The fibre of $\mathcal F$ at a prime
   $\mathfrak p\in X$ ([[def-fibre-of-module-at-point]]) has dimension
   $$\dim_{\kappa(\mathfrak p)}\mathcal F(\mathfrak p)=[x\in\mathfrak p]+[y\in\mathfrak p],$$
   where $[x\in\mathfrak p]$ is $1$ if $x\in\mathfrak p$ and $0$ otherwise.
   Thus the fibre dimension is $2$ at the origin
   $\mathfrak m_0=(x,y)\in X$, it is $1$ at every point of the two punctured
   coordinate axes $V(x)\setminus\{\mathfrak m_0\}$ and
   $V(y)\setminus\{\mathfrak m_0\}$, and it is $0$ at every point of
   $X\setminus(V(x)\cup V(y))$, in particular at the generic point of $X$.
3. Consequently the rank-locus equalities
   $$V(\operatorname{Fitt}_0(\mathcal F))=V(xy),\qquad V(\operatorname{Fitt}_1(\mathcal F))=V(x,y),\qquad V(\operatorname{Fitt}_2(\mathcal F))=\varnothing$$
   hold, matching the fibre-dimension strata of (2), and $\mathcal F$ is not
   finite locally free of any rank: the ideal sheaf
   $\operatorname{Fitt}_0(\mathcal F)=(xy)^\sim$ is neither the zero sheaf nor
   $\mathcal O_X$, because $(xy)\subseteq(x,y)\subsetneq A$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A field $k$; the polynomial ring $A=k[x,y]$; the $A$-linear map
$\varphi:A^2\to A^2$ with matrix $\operatorname{diag}(x,y)$ in the standard
bases; $M=\operatorname{coker}\varphi$; $X=\operatorname{Spec}A$;
$\mathcal F=\widetilde M$.

[F1] Fitting ideals on an affine chart: for $U=\operatorname{Spec}R$ with
$\mathcal F|_U\cong\widetilde N$ and a presentation
$R^{(J)}\xrightarrow{\psi}R^n\to N\to0$ with $n$ finite, one has
$\operatorname{Fitt}_k(\mathcal F)|_U=\widetilde{I_{n-k}(\psi)}$, where
$I_r(\psi)$ is generated by the $r\times r$ minors of a matrix of $\psi$, with
$I_r(\psi)=R$ for $r\le0$ and $I_r(\psi)=0$ for $r>n$; the ideal
$I_{n-k}(\psi)$ depends only on $N$ and $k$, the Fitting ideals are nested as
$\operatorname{Fitt}_{k-1}\subseteq\operatorname{Fitt}_k$, and
$\operatorname{Fitt}_{-1}=0$
([[def-fitting-ideal-sheaf]]).

[F2] Rank-locus theorem: for a quasi-coherent $\mathcal O_X$-module
$\mathcal G$ of finite type and $r\ge0$ one has
$V(\operatorname{Fitt}_r(\mathcal G))=\{x:\dim_{\kappa(x)}\mathcal G(x)>r\}$;
if $\mathcal G$ is finite locally free of rank $r$, then
$\operatorname{Fitt}_{r-1}(\mathcal G)=0$ and
$\operatorname{Fitt}_r(\mathcal G)=\mathcal O_X$
([[thm-fitting-ideals-control-rank-loci]]).

[F3] The fibre at a point is
$\mathcal G(x)=\mathcal G_x\otimes_{\mathcal O_{X,x}}\kappa(x)\cong\mathcal G_x/\mathfrak m_x\mathcal G_x$
([[def-fibre-of-module-at-point]]).

[F4] Stalk of an associated sheaf:
$(\widetilde N)_{\mathfrak p}\cong N_{\mathfrak p}$ for an $R$-module $N$ and a
prime $\mathfrak p$ of $R$, canonically under the inherited Axiom of Choice
([[lem-associated-sheaf-stalk-localization]]).

[F5] Residue field: for a prime $\mathfrak p$ of $A$ one has
$\kappa(\mathfrak p)\cong A_{\mathfrak p}/\mathfrak pA_{\mathfrak p}\cong
\operatorname{Frac}(A/\mathfrak p)$, and the class of an element $a\in A$ in
$\kappa(\mathfrak p)$ is zero if and only if $a\in\mathfrak p$
([[def-residue-field-scheme-point]],
[[cor-residue-field-of-a-localisation-at-a-prime]]).

[F6] Localisation of modules is exact, so localising a presentation at a prime
again gives a presentation, with the localised matrix
([[thm-localisation-of-modules-is-exact]]).

[F7] The tensor product is right exact, so tensoring a presentation preserves
the cokernel and the matrix is reduced by the coefficient change
([[thm-right-exactness-of-tensor-products]]).

[F8] Rank-nullity over a field: for a linear map $T:V\to V$ of a
finite-dimensional vector space, $\dim_FV=\dim_F\ker T+\operatorname{rank}T$;
applied also to the quotient map $V\to\operatorname{coker}T$ it gives
$\dim_F\operatorname{coker}T=\dim_FV-\operatorname{rank}T$
([[thm-rank-nullity]]).

[F9] Polynomial ring and universal property: $A=k[x,y]$ is a commutative ring
with $1$ containing $x,y$, and for a commutative ring $S$, a specified
ring homomorphism $\alpha:k\to S$, and elements $s_1,s_2\in S$, there is a
unique ring homomorphism $A\to S$ extending $\alpha$ with $x\mapsto s_1$
and $y\mapsto s_2$ ([[def-polynomial-ring-on-a-family-of-indeterminates]],
[[thm-universal-property-of-a-polynomial-ring-on-a-family]]).

[F10] For an ideal $M\subseteq R$ the quotient $R/M$ is a field if and only if
$M$ is maximal, and a prime ideal containing a maximal ideal equals it
([[thm-quotient-is-field-iff-ideal-maximal]],
[[def-prime-and-maximal-ideals]]).

[F11] The associated sheaf $\widetilde M$ on $X=\operatorname{Spec}A$ exists,
has $\Gamma(X,\widetilde M)=M$ and sections $\widetilde M(D(f))=M_f$ on
distinguished opens, and its construction uses the Axiom of Choice
([[thm-associated-module-sheaf-exists]],
[[def-associated-sheaf-module-affine-scheme]], [[def-axiom-of-choice]]).

[F12] On the affine scheme $X=\operatorname{Spec}A$, the sheaf
$\widetilde M$ is quasi-coherent, and it is of finite type as soon as $M$ is a
finitely generated $A$-module
([[def-finite-type-finite-presentation-module-sheaf]],
[[def-quasi-coherent-module-scheme]]).

[F13] Vanishing sets: $V(I)=\{\mathfrak p:I\subseteq\mathfrak p\}$ for an ideal
$I$, and $V(xy)=V(x)\cup V(y)$, $V(x,y)=V(x)\cap V(y)$, $V(A)=\varnothing$
([[def-prime-spectrum-and-vanishing-sets]],
[[lem-vanishing-set-identities]]).

[F14] $A=k[x,y]$ is a domain, so $x\neq0$, $y\neq0$ and $xy\neq0$
([[cor-multivariate-polynomial-ring-over-a-domain-is-a-domain]]).

[F15] $A^2$ is the free $A$-module on the standard basis $e_1,e_2$, and the
cokernel of an $A$-linear map is the quotient by its image
([[def-generated-cyclic-finitely-generated-and-free-modules]],
[[def-module-homomorphism-kernel-image-and-cokernel]]).

**Proof technique:** direct; read the Fitting ideals off the diagonal matrix,
localise the presentation at a prime and reduce the diagonal matrix over the
residue field, then identify the rank strata with the vanishing sets.



## Proof

1.1 Setup: the map $\varphi$ is $A$-linear with $\varphi(e_1)=xe_1$, $\varphi(e_2)=ye_2$ on the standard basis of $A^2$ [F15], so $$A^2\xrightarrow{\ \varphi\ }A^2\xrightarrow{\ \pi\ }M\longrightarrow0$$ is a presentation of $M=\operatorname{coker}\varphi$ with $n=2$ generators and two relations; in particular $M$ is generated by $\pi(e_1),\pi(e_2)$, hence is finitely generated, and $\mathcal F=\widetilde M$ on $X=\operatorname{Spec}A$ is quasi-coherent of finite type [F11, F12]. The Axiom of Choice enters only through the associated-sheaf construction of [F11]. [F9, F11, F12, F15]

2.1 The Fitting ideals: apply [F1] to the presentation of step 1.1 with $R=A$, $N=M$, $n=2$ and matrix $\operatorname{diag}(x,y)$. The only $2\times2$ minor is the determinant $\det\operatorname{diag}(x,y)=xy$, so $I_2(\varphi)=(xy)$; the $1\times1$ minors are the entries $x,y,0,0$, so $I_1(\varphi)=(x,y)$; and $I_0(\varphi)=A$ by the convention for $r\le0$. With $\operatorname{Fitt}_k(M)=I_{2-k}(\varphi)$ this gives $\operatorname{Fitt}_0(M)=(xy)$, $\operatorname{Fitt}_1(M)=(x,y)$ and $\operatorname{Fitt}_2(M)=A$, and hence on $X=\operatorname{Spec}A$ $$\operatorname{Fitt}_0(\mathcal F)=(xy)^\sim,\qquad \operatorname{Fitt}_1(\mathcal F)=(x,y)^\sim,\qquad \operatorname{Fitt}_2(\mathcal F)=\mathcal O_X.$$ The same convention gives $I_r(\varphi)=A$ for every $r\le0$, so $\operatorname{Fitt}_k(\mathcal F)=\mathcal O_X$ for every $k\ge2$, and $\operatorname{Fitt}_{-1}(\mathcal F)=0$; the inclusion $(xy)\subseteq(x,y)\subseteq A$ is the nestedness $\operatorname{Fitt}_0\subseteq\operatorname{Fitt}_1\subseteq\operatorname{Fitt}_2$ of [F1]. [F1, F15, step 1.1]

2.2 The fibre at a prime: fix $\mathfrak p\in X$. Localising the presentation of step 1.1 at the multiplicative set $A\setminus\mathfrak p$ gives the exact sequence $$A_{\mathfrak p}^2\xrightarrow{\ \varphi_{\mathfrak p}\ }A_{\mathfrak p}^2\longrightarrow M_{\mathfrak p}\longrightarrow0,$$ whose matrix is again $\operatorname{diag}(x,y)$ over $A_{\mathfrak p}$ [F6]. The residue field $\kappa(\mathfrak p)=A_{\mathfrak p}/\mathfrak pA_{\mathfrak p}$ is an $A_{\mathfrak p}$-algebra [F5], and tensoring the sequence over $A_{\mathfrak p}$ is right exact [F7], so $$\kappa(\mathfrak p)^2\xrightarrow{\ \operatorname{diag}(\bar x,\bar y)\ }\kappa(\mathfrak p)^2\longrightarrow M_{\mathfrak p}\otimes_{A_{\mathfrak p}}\kappa(\mathfrak p)\longrightarrow0$$ is exact, where $\bar x,\bar y$ are the images of $x,y$ in $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$. Since $\mathcal F=\widetilde M$, its stalk at $\mathfrak p$ is $M_{\mathfrak p}$ [F4], so by [F3] $$\mathcal F(\mathfrak p)\cong M_{\mathfrak p}\otimes_{A_{\mathfrak p}}\kappa(\mathfrak p)\cong\operatorname{coker}\bigl(\operatorname{diag}(\bar x,\bar y):\kappa(\mathfrak p)^2\to\kappa(\mathfrak p)^2\bigr).$$ [F3, F4, F5, F6, F7, step 1.1]

3.1 The dimension formula: over the field $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$ the matrix $\operatorname{diag}(\bar x,\bar y)$ has rank $[\bar x\neq0]+[\bar y\neq0]$, because its image is spanned by $\bar x\,e_1$ and $\bar y\,e_2$, each of which is a nonzero multiple of a basis vector or is zero. Applying rank-nullity [F8] to this map and to the quotient map onto its cokernel gives $$\dim_{\kappa(\mathfrak p)}\mathcal F(\mathfrak p)=2-\operatorname{rank}\operatorname{diag}(\bar x,\bar y)=[\bar x=0]+[\bar y=0].$$ By [F5] one has $\bar x=0$ exactly when $x\in\mathfrak p$, and likewise for $y$, so $$\dim_{\kappa(\mathfrak p)}\mathcal F(\mathfrak p)=[x\in\mathfrak p]+[y\in\mathfrak p]:$$ the dimension is $2$ when both $x,y\in\mathfrak p$, it is $1$ when exactly one of them lies in $\mathfrak p$, and it is $0$ when neither does. [F5, F8, step 2.2]

4.1 The rank strata: the homomorphism $\operatorname{ev}:A\to k$, $f\mapsto f(0,0)$, extending $\operatorname{id}_k$ and sending $x$ and $y$ to $0$ exists by [F9]; it is surjective because it is the identity on constants, and its kernel is $(x,y)$: a polynomial with zero constant term is a sum of monomials $x^iy^j$ with $(i,j)\neq(0,0)$, each divisible by $x$ or by $y$, while conversely $x,y\in\ker\operatorname{ev}$. Hence $A/(x,y)\cong k$ is a field and $\mathfrak m_0=(x,y)$ is a maximal ideal [F10]; a prime contains both $x$ and $y$ exactly when it contains $\mathfrak m_0$, hence exactly when it equals $\mathfrak m_0$. By step 3.1 and [F13] the fibre-dimension strata are therefore: $\{\mathfrak m_0\}$, where the dimension is $2$ (the origin of $X$); the points of $$\bigl(V(x)\setminus V(y)\bigr)\cup\bigl(V(y)\setminus V(x)\bigr)=\bigl(V(x)\cup V(y)\bigr)\setminus V(x,y),$$ where the dimension is $1$ (the two punctured coordinate axes); and the complement of $V(x)\cup V(y)=V(xy)$, where the dimension is $0$. The generic point $(0)$ contains neither $x$ nor $y$ by [F14], so it lies in the last stratum. [F9, F10, F13, F14, step 3.1]

5.1 The rank-locus equalities: the stalk of $(xy)^\sim$ at $\mathfrak p$ is $(xy)_{\mathfrak p}$ [F4], and $(xy)_{\mathfrak p}\subseteq\mathfrak pA_{\mathfrak p}$ holds exactly when $xy\in\mathfrak p$, by [F5] applied to $a=xy$; hence $V(\operatorname{Fitt}_0(\mathcal F))=V(xy)$. Similarly $V(\operatorname{Fitt}_1(\mathcal F))=V(x,y)$, and $V(\operatorname{Fitt}_2(\mathcal F))=V(A)=\varnothing$. By [F13] and step 4.1 these are exactly the loci $\{\dim_{\kappa(\mathfrak p)}\mathcal F(\mathfrak p)>0\}=V(xy)$, $\{\dim>1\}=V(x,y)$ and $\{\dim>2\}=\varnothing$, in agreement with the rank-locus theorem [F2] applied to the finite type module $\mathcal F$; the equalities display the strata of step 4.1 as the vanishing sets of the three Fitting ideals computed in step 2.1. [F2, F4, F5, F13, step 2.1, step 3.1, step 4.1]

6.1 Conclusion and choice accounting: suppose $\mathcal F$ were finite locally free of some rank $r\ge0$. By [F2] one would have $\operatorname{Fitt}_{r-1}(\mathcal F)=0$ and $\operatorname{Fitt}_r(\mathcal F)=\mathcal O_X$. For $r=0$ this would give $(xy)^\sim=\mathcal O_X$, impossible because $(xy)\subseteq(x,y)\subsetneq A$ and $(x,y)$ is proper [F10]; for $r=1$ it would give $(xy)^\sim=0$, impossible because $\Gamma(X,(xy)^\sim)=(xy)\neq0$ by [F11] and $xy\neq0$ [F14]; and for $r\ge2$ it would give $\operatorname{Fitt}_{r-1}(\mathcal F)=0$, impossible because $\operatorname{Fitt}_{r-1}\supseteq\operatorname{Fitt}_1=(x,y)^\sim$ by the nestedness of [F1] while $(x,y)\neq0$, as $x\in(x,y)$ with $x\neq0$ [F14]. Even allowing varying local ranks cannot help: at $\mathfrak m_0$ the module $M_{\mathfrak m_0}$ has nonzero fibre, but is annihilated by $xy$, which remains nonzero in the domain $A_{\mathfrak m_0}$. A nonzero free module over that ring has zero annihilator. Thus the stalk at the origin is not free of any rank, and $\mathcal F$ is of finite type but not finite locally free. All computations used the given presentation, the canonical localisations and the canonical comparisons of [F4] and [F5]; no selection of presentations, points or covers is made, so the only use of the Axiom of Choice is the inherited one recorded in [F11]. [F1, F2, F10, F11, F14, step 2.1, step 5.1] ∎
