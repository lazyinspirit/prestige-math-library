## Statement refuted

**False claim:** for every scheme $X$, every $\mathcal O_X$-module $\mathcal F$
and every point $x\in X$, the canonical surjection from the stalk to the fibre,
$$\mathcal F_x\longrightarrow\mathcal F(x)=\mathcal F_x/\mathfrak m_x\mathcal F_x,$$
is an isomorphism, so that the stalk of $\mathcal F$ at $x$ is already a vector
space over the residue field $\kappa(x)$
([[def-fibre-of-module-at-point]]).

The structure sheaf of the affine line at its origin refutes this. Let
$k$ be a field, $A=k[t]$, $X=\operatorname{Spec}A$ and
$\mathfrak p=(t)\in X$, the origin of the affine line
([[def-affine-scheme-spectrum]]). For $\mathcal F=\mathcal O_X$ the stalk at
$\mathfrak p$ is the local ring
$$\mathcal F_{\mathfrak p}=\mathcal O_{X,\mathfrak p}\cong A_{\mathfrak p}=k[t]_{(t)},$$
with nonzero maximal ideal $\mathfrak pA_{\mathfrak p}=(t)A_{\mathfrak p}$,
while the fibre is
$$\mathcal F(\mathfrak p)=\mathcal F_{\mathfrak p}/\mathfrak p\mathcal F_{\mathfrak p}\cong k[t]_{(t)}/(t)k[t]_{(t)}\cong k,$$
a one-dimensional vector space over $\kappa(\mathfrak p)=k$. The reduction
$k[t]_{(t)}\to k$ is surjective and its kernel $(t)k[t]_{(t)}$ contains the
nonzero element $t$; it is therefore not injective, and it is not an
isomorphism of $\mathcal O_{X,\mathfrak p}$-modules or of rings. So the stalk
and the fibre of a module sheaf must be distinguished.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The Axiom of Choice, a field $k$, the polynomial ring $A=k[t]$, the
scheme $X=\operatorname{Spec}A$, its point $\mathfrak p=(t)$, the structure
sheaf $\mathcal O_X$, and the $\mathcal O_X$-modules $\mathcal F=\mathcal O_X$
with stalk $\mathcal F_{\mathfrak p}$ and fibre $\mathcal F(\mathfrak p)$.

[L1] $\mathcal F(\mathfrak p)=\mathcal F_{\mathfrak p}\otimes_{\mathcal O_{X,\mathfrak p}}\kappa(\mathfrak p)\cong\mathcal F_{\mathfrak p}/\mathfrak p\mathcal F_{\mathfrak p}$
and the fibre is a vector space over
$\kappa(\mathfrak p)=\mathcal O_{X,\mathfrak p}/\mathfrak p\mathcal O_{X,\mathfrak p}$
([[def-fibre-of-module-at-point]], [[def-residue-field-scheme-point]]).

[L2] For $\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Spec}A$ the stalk of the structure sheaf
is $\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Spec}A,\mathfrak p}\cong A_{\mathfrak p}$
([[thm-stalk-structure-sheaf-prime-localization]]); for any $A$-module $M$ the
stalk of the associated sheaf is $(\widetilde M)_{\mathfrak p}\cong M_{\mathfrak p}$
([[lem-associated-sheaf-stalk-localization]]).

[L3] In $X=\operatorname{Spec}A$ the point $\mathfrak p=(t)$ is the prime
generated by $t\in k[t]$, and the local ring $A_{\mathfrak p}$ has maximal
ideal $\mathfrak pA_{\mathfrak p}$ ([[def-residue-field-scheme-point]]);
$\mathfrak p$ is prime since $k[t]/(t)=k$ is a field.

[L5] Localisation commutes with quotients: $S^{-1}(A/I)\cong S^{-1}A/S^{-1}I$,
so $A_{\mathfrak p}/IA_{\mathfrak p}\cong(A/I)_{\mathfrak p}$
([[thm-localisation-of-modules-commutes-with-quotients-and-sums]]).

[L6] The refuted claim: the canonical map $\mathcal F_x\to\mathcal F(x)$ is an
isomorphism for every $\mathcal O_X$-module $\mathcal F$ and every point $x$.



**Proof technique:** direct; compute stalk, maximal ideal and fibre for
$\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Spec}k[t]}$ at $\mathfrak p=(t)$, and show the
reduction map has nonzero kernel.

## Proof

1.1 With $A=k[t]$ and $\mathfrak p=(t)$, the stalk of $\mathcal F=\mathcal O_X$ at $\mathfrak p$ is $\mathcal F_{\mathfrak p}\cong A_{\mathfrak p}=k[t]_{(t)}$ by [L2], and by [L3] its maximal ideal is $\mathfrak pA_{\mathfrak p}=(t)A_{\mathfrak p}$, which contains the nonzero element $t$: if $t/1=0$, some $s\notin(t)$ would satisfy $st=0$ in the domain $k[t]$, a contradiction. [L2, L3, algebra]

1.2 The fibre is $\mathcal F(\mathfrak p)\cong\mathcal F_{\mathfrak p}/\mathfrak p\mathcal F_{\mathfrak p}\cong A_{\mathfrak p}/(t)A_{\mathfrak p}$ by [L1], and by [L5] applied to the principal ideal $(t)\subseteq A$ this is $\cong(A/(t))_{\mathfrak p}=k_{\mathfrak p}=k$, the residue field $\kappa(\mathfrak p)=A_{\mathfrak p}/\mathfrak pA_{\mathfrak p}\cong k$ being one-dimensional over itself; so the fibre is a one-dimensional $k$-vector space. [L1, L5]

2.1 The canonical map of [L1] is the quotient map $A_{\mathfrak p}\to A_{\mathfrak p}/(t)A_{\mathfrak p}\cong k$, which is surjective, and it is not injective: the element $t\in A_{\mathfrak p}$ is nonzero and is mapped to zero; equivalently its kernel is the nonzero ideal $(t)A_{\mathfrak p}$. [L1, step 1.1, step 1.2]

2.2 The stalk is not isomorphic to the fibre even as a ring: if $t$ were a unit of $A_{\mathfrak p}$, there would be $a\in A$ and $s\in A\setminus(t)$ with $at/s=1$, hence $u(at-s)=0$ in $A$ for some $u\in A\setminus(t)$, which is impossible in the polynomial ring $k[t]$ because the right-hand side of $at=s$ is not divisible by $t$; so $t$ is a nonunit, $A_{\mathfrak p}$ is a local ring that is not a field, while the fibre is the field $k$, and the two are not isomorphic. [step 1.1, step 1.2]

3.1 By steps 2.1 and 2.2 the canonical surjection $\mathcal F_{\mathfrak p}\to\mathcal F(\mathfrak p)$ of [L1] is surjective but not injective, hence not an isomorphism, and the stalk $k[t]_{(t)}$ and the fibre $k$ are different objects; the Axiom of Choice is inherited through [L2] only, no new choice being made in this computation. [L1, L2, step 2.1, step 2.2] ∎
