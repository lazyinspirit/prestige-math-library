## Open problem

Let $P$ be a winding-zero pattern whose induced satellite map $P:\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sm}}\to\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sm}}$ is nonconstant and whose image consists only of topologically slice knots. For $n\ge0$, let $P^n$ be the $n$-fold composition of this map, with $P^0$ the identity, and let $\langle S\rangle$ denote the subgroup generated by $S$. Must every quotient $\langle P^n(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sm}})\rangle/\langle P^{n+1}(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sm}})\rangle$ have infinite rank, where the rank of an abelian group $A$ is $\dim_{\mathbb Q}(A\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb Q)$? This is the smooth graded-quotient question recorded by Cha and Kim; the question itself supplies no premise for a proof.
