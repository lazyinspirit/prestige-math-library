## Definition

Let $A$ be an indeterminate, put $q=A^4$, $q_K=A^2$, and $q_L=A^{-2}=q_K^{-1}$, and work over $F=\mathbb Q(A)$. An oriented knot in $S^3$ means a smooth embedded circle with an orientation, considered up to orientation-preserving ambient isotopy. For an integer $r$, put
$$[r]=\frac{q_K^r-q_K^{-r}}{q_K-q_K^{-1}},\qquad [m]!=\prod_{r=1}^{m}[r]\quad(m\ge1),\qquad [0]!=1.$$
For each $N\ge1$, put $q_N=\exp(2\pi i/N)$.
For each integer $N\ge1$, let $V_N$ have basis $v_0,\ldots,v_{N-1}$ and define the $U_{q_L}(\mathfrak{sl}_2)$ action in the library coproduct convention by
$$K_Lv_j=q_L^{N-1-2j}v_j,\qquad Ev_j=[N-j]v_{j-1},\qquad Fv_j=[j+1]v_{j+1},$$
where $v_{-1}=v_N=0$. In the universal $R$-matrix formula use $q_K$ and $H v_j=(N-1-2j)v_j$; on $V_N\otimes V_N$ its action is the finite sum
$$\mathcal R_N=q_K^{H\otimes H/2}\sum_{r=0}^{N-1}q_K^{r(r-1)/2}\frac{(q_K-q_K^{-1})^r}{[r]!}\,E^r\otimes F^r,$$
and the positive crossing is $c_N=P\mathcal R_N$, where $P(x\otimes y)=y\otimes x$. This is an irreducible module of dimension $N$. Equip these matrices with the Kauffman-bracket half-ribbon data: the twist matrix is the scalar $\theta_N=\lambda_N I$ with $\lambda_N=(-1)^{N-1}A^{N^2-1}$, and the matrix $G_N=(-1)^{N-1}\operatorname{diag}(A^{2(N-1-2j)})_{j=0}^{N-1}$ is the pivotal comparison used in the closure trace. The resulting generic quantum dimension is
$$d_N=\operatorname{Tr}(G_N)=(-1)^{N-1}\frac{A^{2N}-A^{-2N}}{A^2-A^{-2}}.$$
Use the contragredient left-dual action $(x\cdot f)(v)=f(S(x)v)$ on $V_N^*$, with evaluation $\operatorname{ev}(f\otimes v)=f(v)$ and coevaluation $1\mapsto\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}v_j\otimes v_j^*$. The ambient category is the $F$-linear category generated by the modules $V_N$ for all $N\ge1$ under finite tensor products, contragredient duals, finite direct sums and images of module-idempotents, whose morphisms are module homomorphisms, and whose tensor-product action is induced by the stated Hopf coproduct; its tensor unit is $F$ with the trivial action, so its tensor-unit endomorphism ring is $F$. These matrix data satisfy the ribbon-category axioms: braiding and inverse braiding, both duality snakes, monoidal pivotal comparison, balancing and dual compatibility of twist. Assume the Axiom of Choice and Countable Choice for the framed-tangle and Markov constructions. An oriented knot $K\subset S^3$ presented as the closure of an $n$-strand braid $\beta$ has generic normalized value
$$J_N^{\mathrm{gen}}(K;q)=\lambda_N^{-w(\beta)}\frac{\operatorname{Tr}(G_N^{\otimes n}\rho_N(\beta))}{d_N}\in F,$$
where, for a word $\beta=\sigma_{i_1}^{\varepsilon_1}\cdots\sigma_{i_m}^{\varepsilon_m}$ with $\varepsilon_j\in\{1,-1\}$, $w(\beta):=\sum_{j=1}^m\varepsilon_j$, and $\rho_N(\beta)$ is obtained by replacing each positive braid generator by $c_N$ on the corresponding adjacent tensor factors and each inverse generator by $c_N^{-1}$, then composing in word order. This quotient is an integral Laurent polynomial in $A^4$. Define the **normalized colored Jones sequence** by the unique $J_N(K;q)\in\mathbb Z[q,q^{-1}]$ satisfying $J_N(K;A^4)=J_N^{\mathrm{gen}}(K;A^4)$. Its root value $J_N(K;q_N)$ is obtained by substituting into this Laurent polynomial. The variable transport to the ordinary Jones convention is $t=q^{-1}=A^{-4}$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** An integer $N\ge1$, the Axiom of Choice and Countable Choice, the generic module and matrices from the Definition, an oriented knot $K$, and a braid word $\beta$ whose closure represents $K$.

[F1] The library presentation has $K_L E K_L^{-1}=q_L^2E$, $K_L F K_L^{-1}=q_L^{-2}F$, and $EF-FE=(K_L-K_L^{-1})/(q_L-q_L^{-1})$ ([[def-drinfeld-jimbo-quantized-enveloping-algebra]]).

[F2] The library coproduct is $\Delta(E)=E\otimes K_L^{-1}+1\otimes E$ and $\Delta(F)=F\otimes1+K_L\otimes F$ ([[thm-the-drinfeld-jimbo-formulas-define-a-hopf-algebra]]).

[F3] A ribbon category has a braiding, chosen duals, and a twist satisfying the balancing and dual-compatibility identities ([[def-braiding]], [[def-left-dual-and-right-dual-object]], [[def-twist-and-ribbon-structure]]).

[F4] For an absolutely simple color with scalar twist $\lambda I$, the generic framed trace corrected by $\lambda^{-w(\beta)}$ is invariant under oriented unframed Markov equivalence, under the stated Axiom of Choice ([[thm-twist-correction-removes-framing]]).

[F5] The framed colored-link value is the ribbon-tangle evaluation in $\operatorname{End}_{\mathcal C}(\mathbf1)$ ([[def-colored-framed-knot-evaluation-from-ribbon-category]]). For an $X$-colored closed braid, its categorical value is $t_n(\beta)=\operatorname{Tr}_L(j_{X^{\otimes n}}\rho_n(\beta))$ ([[def-ribbon-evaluation-of-an-x-colored-closed-braid]]) and equals the tangle evaluation of its blackboard-framed closure ([[lem-ribbon-trace-equals-the-framed-closure-evaluation]]).

[F6] Absolute simplicity means $\operatorname{End}(V_N)=F I$ ([[def-absolutely-simple-object]]).

[F7] The finite matrices satisfy all ribbon identities for every $N\ge1$ and their pivotal trace of the identity is $d_N$ ([[lem-colored-jones-generic-ribbon-data]]).

[F8] The normalized generic quotient for every knot and color belongs to $\mathbb Z[A^4,A^{-4}]$ ([[lem-colored-jones-generic-quotient-is-laurent]]).

[A2] The Axiom of Choice supplies a choice function for every family of nonempty sets ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]).

[A3] Countable choice supplies a choice function for every sequence of nonempty sets ([[def-countable-choice]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

**Given:** The hypotheses in the Definition.

1.1 The diagonal weights give $K_L E K_L^{-1}v_j=q_L^2Ev_j$ and $K_L F K_L^{-1}v_j=q_L^{-2}Fv_j$. For the commutator, $EFv_j-FEv_j=([j+1][N-j-1]-[N-j][j])v_j=[N-1-2j]v_j$; the last equality follows by expanding the two quantum-number products over $q_K-q_K^{-1}$. Since $[r]_{q_K}=[r]_{q_L}$, this is $(K_L-K_L^{-1})(q_L-q_L^{-1})^{-1}v_j$. The generators therefore satisfy the defining $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ relations. [F1, given, algebra]

1.2 The eigenvalues $q_L^{N-1-2j}$ of $K_L$ are pairwise distinct in $F$. Any module endomorphism is therefore diagonal in the given basis; commuting with $E$, whose successive coefficients $[N-j]$ are nonzero in $F$, forces all diagonal entries to be equal. Thus $\operatorname{End}_{U_{q_L}(\mathfrak{sl}_2)}(V_N)=F I$ and the color is absolutely simple by [F6]. [F6, given, algebra]

1.3 If $N=1$, $V_N$ is one-dimensional and irreducible. For $N>1$, let $W$ be a nonzero submodule and choose $x=\sum_j c_jv_j\in W$ nonzero. The eigenvalues of $K_L$ are distinct, so the polynomial spectral projection for any $j$ with $c_j\ne0$ maps $x$ to $c_jv_j$; hence $v_j\in W$. Repeated application of $E$ gives $v_0\in W$, since every coefficient $[N-k]$ encountered is nonzero. Repeated application of $F$ to $v_0$ gives each $v_k$ because the coefficients $[k]$ are nonzero. Thus $W=V_N$, proving irreducibility. [given, algebra]

1.4 The displayed sum for $d_N$ is a geometric sum, so $d_N=(-1)^{N-1}[N]$. Its numerator $A^{2N}-A^{-2N}$ is a nonzero Laurent polynomial and its denominator $A^2-A^{-2}$ is nonzero in $F$; hence the generic normalization is defined. The Hopf coproduct [F2] supplies the tensor-power action used in $\rho_N(\beta)$. By the braiding, dual, and ribbon axioms [F3] and the all-color ribbon identities [F7], the comparison on $V_N^{\otimes n}$ is $G_N^{\otimes n}$. The basis coevaluation and evaluation in the left categorical trace [F5] then give the ordinary matrix trace $\operatorname{Tr}(G_N^{\otimes n}\rho_N(\beta))$, which is the scalar of the framed closure. [F2, F3, F5, F7, given, algebra]

2.1 By [F4], [F7], and [A2], $\lambda_N^{-w(\beta)}t_N(\beta)$ is unchanged by every conjugation and Markov stabilization, since $V_N$ is absolutely simple and its specified twist scalar is nonzero. Countable choice [A3] supplies the evaluator's hypothesis. Dividing by the fixed nonzero $d_N$ preserves that equality, so the displayed generic value is independent of the braid representative. [A2, A3, F4, F6, F7, step 1.2, step 1.4, given]

2.2 The unknot is represented by the one-strand identity braid, whose exponent sum is zero and whose pivotal trace is $\operatorname{Tr}(G_N)=d_N$. Hence $J_N^{\mathrm{gen}}(\bigcirc;q)=1$. For $N\ge2$, at $q_N=e^{2\pi i/N}$ choose $A_N=e^{\pi i/(2N)}$; then $A_N^4=q_N$ and $A_N^2-A_N^{-2}\ne0$, while $A_N^{2N}-A_N^{-2N}=e^{\pi i}-e^{-\pi i}=0$. Thus $d_N(q_N)=0$: the generic quotient cannot be evaluated there by dividing by $d_N$. [F5, step 1.4, given, algebra]

3.1 Apply [F8] to the given knot-closing braid and color under [A2] and [A3]. The generic quotient therefore has a unique integral Laurent representative in $q=A^4$. Substitution into that finite Laurent sum is defined for every nonzero complex $q$, including $q_N$; it does not divide by the specialized quantum dimension. Together with steps 2.1 and 2.2, this proves the stated representative independence, unknot normalization and root-evaluation convention. [A2, A3, F8, step 2.1, step 2.2, given, algebra] ∎

## Remarks

The all-color ribbon identities are proved by the finite-series interpolation argument in the first well-definedness supplier. The second proves integral Laurentness by cutting the knot into an integral scalar endomorphism and then proving invariance under $A\mapsto iA$. Thus root evaluation uses the resulting Laurent polynomial, even though the generic quantum dimension vanishes at the specified root for every $N\ge2$.
