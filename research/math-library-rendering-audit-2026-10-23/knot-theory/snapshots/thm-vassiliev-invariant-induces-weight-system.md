## Statement

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $n\ge0$. For an unframed $R$-valued invariant $V$ of type at most $n$, the degree-$n$ derivative induces an $R$-linear functional on degree-$n$ chord diagrams modulo 4T and 1T. For a framed $R$-valued invariant $V$ of type at most $n$, assume $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$; its degree-$n$ derivative induces an $R$-linear functional on degree-$n$ chord diagrams modulo 4T. Framed derivatives are not subject to a universal 1T vanishing law: integer-valued framed self-linking has value $2$ on the one-chord diagram with blackboard framing.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A commutative ring $R$, an integer $n\ge0$, and an $R$-valued invariant $V$ of type at most $n$. The 4T and unframed 1T conclusions are choice-free. The framed case and its non-1T witness assume $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$ as in [A1].

[F1] [[def-singular-knot-and-vassiliev-derivative]]: the $n$-fold derivative is the alternating sum over independent resolutions; framed type bounds also count framing zeroes as singularities.

[F2] [[def-framed-chord-diagram-and-four-term-relations]]: both framed and unframed symbol modules are generated by ordinary degree-$n$ chord diagrams; 4T is imposed in both and 1T only in the unframed module.

[F3] [[lem-vassiliev-4t-from-singular-reidemeister-move]]: in both categories the derivative value is representative-independent and annihilates every 4T generator.

[F4] [[lem-vassiliev-framing-one-term-relation]]: unframed derivatives vanish on diagrams with an isolated chord; under $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$, framed self-linking has type at most $1$ and value $2$ on the one-chord blackboard-framed diagram.

[A1] [[def-countable-choice]]: $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$ selects one element from each set in a sequence of nonempty sets; it is assumed for the framed normal-bundle branch and the framed witness inherited from [F4], not for the unframed 4T or 1T arguments.

## Proof

**Given:** As above, with $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$ added when working in the framed category and when invoking the framed witness clause of [F4].

1.1 For each degree-$n$ diagram $D$, choose an $n$-singular representative $K_D$ and set $w_V(D)=D_nV(K_D)$. By [F3] the value is independent of the representative and, in the framed case, of its zero-free framing class. Thus this defines a function on degree-$n$ diagrams. Extending on the free $R$-module by $R$-linearity defines a linear functional. [given, A1, F1, F3, choose, construct]

2.1 By [F3], $w_V(D_1)-w_V(D_2)+w_V(D_3)-w_V(D_4)=0$ for every 4T generator. Thus the functional annihilates the 4T submodule and factors through the framed quotient by the definition in [F2]. [A1, F2, F3, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 If $V$ is unframed, [F4] makes the functional vanish on every 1T generator as well, so it factors through the unframed quotient in [F2]. In the framed branch under $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$, [F4] and [A1] give a type-at-most-one framed self-linking example with one-chord value $2$; since $2\ne0$ in $\mathbb Z$, no universal framed 1T relation can be added. [F2, F4, A1, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
