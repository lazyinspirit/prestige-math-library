## Statement

Fix even $p\ge4$, a primitive $2p$-th root $A$, and $r=p/2$. In the Temperley–Lieb diagram category over $\mathbb C$ with loop value $\delta=-A^2-A^{-2}$, call $f:m\to n$ negligible when $\operatorname{Tr}_m(g\circ f)=0$ for every reverse morphism $g:n\to m$, where $\operatorname{Tr}$ is the unnormalized diagram-closure trace. A tensor ideal is a family of linear subspaces of the morphism spaces closed under composition on either side and tensor product on either side by arbitrary morphisms. The negligible morphisms form a tensor ideal. This ideal is generated, as a tensor ideal, by the Jones–Wenzl projector $p_{r-1}$, defined by the standard recursion through its first zero quantum integer; in particular the quotient has precisely the surviving projector labels $0,\ldots,r-2$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The specialized Temperley–Lieb category, its unnormalized closure trace, and the root-order projectors.

[F1] [[lem-jones-wenzl-idempotents-exist-below-root-order]]: $p_{r-1}$ is a nonzero idempotent with zero closure trace.

[F2] [[def-temperley-lieb-diagram-category]]: each morphism is a finite $\mathbb C$-linear combination of noncrossing matching diagrams; cups and caps bend strands, and composition is stacking with each closed component weighted by $\delta$.

[F3] [[def-temperley-lieb-negligible-morphisms]]: $f:m\to n$ is negligible exactly when $\operatorname{Tr}_m(g\circ f)=0$ for every $g:n\to m$.

[F4] [[lem-temperley-lieb-negligible-morphisms-form-tensor-ideal]]: the negligible subspaces form a two-sided tensor ideal in the planar Temperley–Lieb category for every complex loop value.

[F5] [[lem-temperley-lieb-closure-trace-component-identities]]: the unnormalized closure trace is cyclic and its identities do not divide by $\delta$.

[F6] [[lem-temperley-lieb-jones-wenzl-partial-trace-and-uniqueness]]: the corner of each defined $p_n$ is $\mathbb C p_n$.

[F7] [[thm-temperley-lieb-rank-one-pivotal-ribbon-conversion]]: at the canonical root with order parameter $r\ge3$, the negative-loop quotient has kernel equal to the embedded ideal generated by $p_{r-1}$ and its ribbon quotient is equivalent to the specified rank-one tilting quotient.

[F8] [[thm-temperley-lieb-root-negligible-quotient-is-semisimple]]: for the positive-loop root $q=e^{\pi i/r}$, the boundary projector ideal is the exact negligible ideal and the quotient has simple labels $0,\ldots,r-2$ with the truncated fusion rule.

## Proof

**Given:** The data in the Statement.

1.1 By the boundary-projector result, $p_{r-1}$ is an idempotent with trace zero. The diagram category [F2] supplies the endomorphism algebra $\mathrm{TL}_{r-1}$ and its closure trace. For any $g\in\mathrm{TL}_{r-1}$, [F6] gives $p_{r-1}gp_{r-1}=\lambda p_{r-1}$ for some $\lambda\in\mathbb C$. Cyclicity [F5] gives $\operatorname{Tr}_{r-1}(g\circ p_{r-1})=\operatorname{Tr}_{r-1}(p_{r-1}gp_{r-1})=\lambda\operatorname{Tr}_{r-1}(p_{r-1})=0$. Thus $p_{r-1}$ itself is negligible by [F3]. [F1, F2, F3, F5, F6, given, algebra]

2.1 By [F4], the negligible morphisms form a tensor ideal. Since [F3] and step 1.1 place $p_{r-1}$ in that ideal, the tensor ideal it generates is contained in the negligible ideal. [F3, F4, step 1.1, algebra]

2.2 Put $A_0=e^{\pi i/(2r)}$. For $r\ge3$, $A_0$ is a primitive $4r$-th root, $q_0=A_0^2=e^{\pi i/r}$, and $-A_0^2-A_0^{-2}=-(q_0+q_0^{-1})$. Thus the negative-loop category in [F7] is exactly the BHMV Temperley–Lieb category at $A_0$; its quotient kernel is the ideal generated by $p_{r-1}$, and [F8] gives the complete label list. [F1, F7, F8, step 1.1, algebra]

2.3 If $r=2$, the loop value is zero and $p_1=1_1$ has zero trace by step 1.1. The tensor ideal it generates contains every identity $1_n$ for $n\ge1$, while the object $0$ has endomorphism algebra $\mathbb C$ with trace $1$. Hence the quotient is equivalent to finite-dimensional vector spaces with the single simple label $0$; its negligible ideal is exactly the ideal generated by $p_1$. [F1, F3, F4, step 1.1, algebra, cases]

3.1 For arbitrary primitive $A$, write $A=A_0^k$ with $\gcd(k,4r)=1$. The cyclotomic automorphism $\sigma_k$ of $\mathbb Q(A_0)$ sends $A_0$ to $A$, sends $\delta(A_0)$ to $\delta(A)$, and sends every coefficient of each recursively defined projector and every closure-trace pairing matrix to its specialized coefficient at $A$. For each fixed pair of object labels, the matching set is finite by [F2], so the Hom spaces and trace-pairing matrices are finite-dimensional. The field automorphism preserves the ranks and kernels of all these matrices, the exact boundary ideal, and the quotient's simple labels. This completes the canonical-to-arbitrary-root transport and the proof. [F1, F2, step 2.2, step 2.3, algebra] ∎
