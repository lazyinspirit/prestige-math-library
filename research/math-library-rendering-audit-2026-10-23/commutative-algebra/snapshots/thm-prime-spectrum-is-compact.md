## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice.

For every commutative ring $R$, the topological space $\operatorname{Spec}(R)$ is compact.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A commutative ring $R$, an open cover $\mathcal U$ of $\operatorname{Spec}(R)$, and the Axiom of Choice.

[L1] A topological space is compact when every open cover has a finite subcover ([[def-compact-space]]).

[L2] Every point of a Zariski-open set has a distinguished-open neighbourhood inside it ([[lem-distinguished-open-refinement-at-a-point]]).

[L3] A distinguished-open cover of the spectrum forces the covering ideal to be the unit ideal ([[lem-spectrum-compactness-open-cover-to-unit-ideal]]).

[L4] A finite unit expression $1=\sum a_if_i$ yields the finite cover $\operatorname{Spec}(R)=\bigcup D(f_i)$ ([[lem-spectrum-compactness-unit-expression-finite-subcover]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 If $\operatorname{Spec}(R)=\varnothing$, then the empty subfamily of $\mathcal U$ already covers it. By [L1], the spectrum is compact in this case. [L1, given]

1.2 Assume now that $\operatorname{Spec}(R)\neq\varnothing$. For each $\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Spec}(R)$, choose $U_{\mathfrak p}\in\mathcal U$ with $\mathfrak p\in U_{\mathfrak p}$. By [L2], choose $f_{\mathfrak p}\in R$ with $\mathfrak p\in D(f_{\mathfrak p})\subseteq U_{\mathfrak p}$. Then $\{D(f_{\mathfrak p})\}_{\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Spec}(R)}$ is a distinguished-open cover of the spectrum. [L2, given, choose]

2.1 By [L3], the ideal generated by the family $\{f_{\mathfrak p}\}$ is $R$. Hence there exist finitely many primes $\mathfrak p_1,\dots,\mathfrak p_n\in\operatorname{Spec}(R)$ and coefficients $a_1,\dots,a_n\in R$ such that $1=a_1f_{\mathfrak p_1}+\cdots+a_nf_{\mathfrak p_n}$. [L3, step 1.2, choose]

3.1 Applying [L4] to this identity gives $\operatorname{Spec}(R)=D(f_{\mathfrak p_1})\cup\cdots\cup D(f_{\mathfrak p_n})\subseteq U_{\mathfrak p_1}\cup\cdots\cup U_{\mathfrak p_n}$. Thus $\mathcal U$ has a finite subcover. [L4, step 1.2, step 2.1]

4.1 Steps 1.1 and 3.1 show that every open cover of $\operatorname{Spec}(R)$ has a finite subcover. Therefore $\operatorname{Spec}(R)$ is compact by [L1]. [L1, step 1.1, step 3.1] ∎
