## Statement

Let $\varphi:R\to A$ be a ring homomorphism of commutative rings. Then contraction along $\varphi$ defines a map
$$ \operatorname{Spec}(\varphi):\operatorname{Spec}(A)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Spec}(R),\qquad \mathfrak q\longmapsto\varphi^{-1}(\mathfrak q). $$
For every ideal $I\trianglelefteq R$, if $IA$ denotes the ideal generated by $\varphi(I)$, then
$$ \operatorname{Spec}(\varphi)^{-1}(V(I))=V(IA). $$

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A ring homomorphism $\varphi:R\to A$ of commutative rings.

[L1] Contraction of prime ideals is well-defined and respects identities and composition ([[lem-spectrum-map-respects-composition-and-identities]]).

[L2] Contraction pulls back vanishing sets by the rule $\operatorname{Spec}(\varphi)^{-1}(V(I))=V(IA)$ ([[lem-spectrum-map-preimage-of-vanishing-set]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 The first displayed assignment is well-defined on prime ideals by [L1]. [L1]

1.2 The stated pullback formula for vanishing sets is exactly [L2]. [L2]

2.1 Together, steps 1.1 and 1.2 give the spectrum map induced by $\varphi$ and its basic effect on vanishing sets. [step 1.1, step 1.2] ∎
