## 1. Exact inverse translation in a fixed nonintegral coset pattern

Work in type A, first with sl_N; split off the scalar centre for gl_N. Write a=lambda+rho. The integral reflection group permutes indices inside each C/Z coset of the coordinates a_j. Assume a is regular and decreasing inside each coset in its integer order: differences of coordinates in one coset are positive integers in index order. Coordinates in different cosets need not be real or comparable. Such lambda is maximal in its integral-reflection linkage class; strong linkage and the actual maximal-Verma projectivity supplier make Delta_lambda projective in its entire linkage block and, by block projection, in O.

Let nu be a full dominant integral finite-dimensional highest weight, and suppose b=a+nu is also regular and decreasing within the same coset pattern. Let E=L(nu). The ordinary finite tensor Verma flag of E⊗Delta_lambda has factors Delta_(lambda+xi), xi a weight of E. If one lies in the central character of mu=lambda+nu, then lambda+xi+rho=w b for some full Weyl permutation w. All weights of E differ from nu by the root lattice, so their coordinate fractional shifts are the same common scalar; coordinatewise congruence forces w to preserve the coset pattern of a. Thus w belongs to the product permutation group of these cosets.

For such w the vector wa−a is real and has sum zero inside every coset. Common complex scalars therefore cancel in the bilinear norm calculation. Since a is decreasing inside each coset, a−w^−1 a is a nonnegative combination of the positive difference roots inside those cosets. The exact identity is

||w(a+nu)−a||²−||nu||² = ||wa−a||² + 2〈nu,a−w^−1 a〉.

The second term is nonnegative by full dominance of nu, and the first is strictly positive for w≠1 by regularity. These comparisons are real after removing the common scalar on each coset; no ordering of C is asserted. The finite-dimensional weight norm bound then excludes every w≠1. The remaining constituent is Delta_mu, with multiplicity one because nu is the extremal highest weight of E. Hence the central projection of E⊗Delta_lambda is exactly Delta_mu.

Similarly the projected E*⊗Delta_mu has possible weights w a−b. Its norm excess is

||wa−(a+nu)||²−||nu||² = ||wa−a||² + 2〈nu,a−wa〉,

again strictly positive except w=1. The weight −nu occurs in E* once, so the inverse projected tensor functor sends Delta_mu to Delta_lambda. For every v in the integral reflection group, apply v^−1 to a candidate tensor weight in the Verma flag of E⊗Delta_(v dot lambda). Weyl invariance of the weight set of E reduces uniqueness to the preceding norm inequality. The unique extremal weight is v nu, again of multiplicity one. Thus translation sends every Delta_(v dot lambda) to Delta_(v dot mu), and the inverse sends it back. Tensor biadjunction and exact block projections are the actual published general interfaces. Its unit and counit on these Vermas are nonzero by the adjunction triangle identities and hence isomorphisms because End Delta=C. On finite Verma flags they remain isomorphisms by short-exact-sequence induction; on projective presentations and their cokernels they remain isomorphisms. This gives exact inverse translations on the two linkage blocks once their projective objects have finite Verma flags, supplied by the general O projective/BGG theory. It is a local proof with explicit nonintegral hypotheses, not a citation to the narrower actual14 Statement.

## 2. Fixed-source tensor projectives and the actual HC equivalence

Fix regular coset-dominant lambda. For any simple L(eta) with eta−lambda in the weight lattice, choose a sufficiently large even integer n. The published arbitrary-weight reach construction makes eta'=eta+n rho maximal and Delta_eta' projective, with L(n rho)⊗Delta_eta' mapping onto L(eta). Enlarge n so nu=eta'−lambda is full dominant integral and eta' is regular/decreasing in every original coset. The preceding argument makes Delta_eta' a central direct summand of L(nu)⊗Delta_lambda. Thus a finite tensor of Delta_lambda is projective and maps onto every such simple. Projective covers, finite length and projective presentations imply that the additive summands of these tensor objects generate the whole weight-coset category O_(lambda+P).

Let HC_chi be the category of finitely generated U-bimodules B, annihilated on the right by ker chi_lambda, whose adjoint semisimple-g action is a direct sum of finite-dimensional simple modules. Put Phi_E=E⊗U_chi with diagonal left and ordinary right action. Finite adjoint-stable generators give an epimorphism Phi_E→B. The kernel is again finitely generated because U⊗U^op is noetherian by PBW, and remains adjoint algebraic by complete reducibility. Therefore every B has a finite presentation by Phi_E's. The identity

Hom_HC(Phi_E,B)=Hom_g(E,B_ad)

makes Phi_E projective, since taking a finite-dimensional isotypic multiplicity is exact in a semisimple direct-sum category. This does not require importing HC finite-length or primitive-bimodule classification.

The functor T_lambda(B)=B⊗_U Delta_lambda is right exact and sends Phi_E to E⊗Delta_lambda. The genuine all-weight Duflo result in actual13 identifies U_chi with the adjoint-finite End of Delta_lambda. For finite E,F, tensor-Hom adjunction therefore identifies

Hom_HC(Phi_E,Phi_F) ≅ Hom_g(E⊗Delta_lambda,F⊗Delta_lambda).

Finite adjoint type permits commuting the finite tensor factors with the adjoint-finite Hom; the explicit isotypic identity is the repaired13 universal Hom interface. The projective-presentation criterion now proves equivalence: presentations by Phi_E map to presentations by the tensor projectives; every target presentation lifts because maps between these generators lift uniquely. To prove full faithfulness first apply Hom from a generator to a presentation (both generator images are projective), then apply Hom from the cokernel presentation. Kernels/cokernels of the resulting finite diagrams give the required isomorphisms. No assertion that right exactness alone is exactness is used; exactness follows after the equivalence of abelian categories is obtained.

For any starting weight z, regularize by z+n rho with a sufficiently large even n in the root lattice, leaving its weight-coset category unchanged. For a full Weyl-conjugate weight wz, use w(z+n rho) and make it coset-dominant by an integral-reflection permutation; this also changes it only by the root lattice. The two resulting regular weights have the same central character. Applying the preceding HC equivalence twice gives the Z-linear equivalence asserted by BG5.12, without importing BG5.9 or its classification theorem.
