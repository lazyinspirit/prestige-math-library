Two finite examples isolate common boundaries of the estimators on the A page.
Rectangular windows can have diverging volume while their relative boundary
does not vanish. A pair-count sample covariance can also violate a necessary
positive-definiteness condition, even though it remains a defined finite-sample
summary statistic.
