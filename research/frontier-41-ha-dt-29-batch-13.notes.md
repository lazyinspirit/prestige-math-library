### Conflicts and sharpenings, resolved in favour of the plan (or recorded)

1. **Prerequisite range.** The design's DT-21 header says "Requires: DT-5--DT-7, DT-11--DT-12, DT-15--DT-16; DG tubes and orientations; AT relative homology/homotopy interfaces". The current plan's exact §12.4 `requires` array (and the dispatch) is broader: it also names `whitney-embedding-tubular-neighbourhoods-and-approximation`, `relative-homology-excision-and-mayer-vietoris`, `orientations-poincare-lefschetz-and-alexander-duality`, `higher-homotopy-groups-and-cofiber-sequences`, `hurewicz-whitehead-freudenthal-and-cw-approximation`, `obstruction-theory-postnikov-towers-and-classifying-spaces` and `the-fundamental-group`. The plan controls; the manifest carries the plan's array verbatim. The items actually consume published items of DT-5, DT-11, DT-12, DT-15, DT-16, DG (Whitney/collars/tubular) and AT (relative homology, higher homotopy, Hurewicz/Whitehead, orientations); no item of this pair consumes `obstruction-theory-postnikov-towers-and-classifying-spaces` or `the-fundamental-group`, which are present as reading-order prerequisites of the plan.
2. **The §12.5 binding repair is implemented.** Items 1--2 require `0 <= p <= m-1` and `q = m-p >= 1`; the dual sphere has dimension `q-1`; the trace handle has index `p+1`; no `S^{-1}` surgery is silently included (the endpoint cases are printed in the definitions, including `p = m-1`, `q = 1`, and the `q = 1` dual 0-sphere).
3. **The below-middle inequality is printed.** The design's item 8 says only "when p is below the middle". The scaffold states the exact hypothesis `p <= q-2`, equivalently `2p+2 <= m`, which is the condition under which the trace kills the class in the *surgered* manifold (the dual handle has index `q >= p+2`). This matches Lück's `2(k+1) <= n`, Ranicki's `2n+2 <= m` (Corollary 10.3) and the `2n+1 <= m` normal-map form (Propositions 10.24, 10.25(i)). Outside the range only the trace statement is made and the middle case is excluded explicitly (item 15 = design item 13).
4. **The framing slogan is sharpened.** The design's item 10 reads "a homotopy class can be killed smoothly only when represented by an embedded sphere with trivialised normal bundle". The scaffold separates the exact content: for *the given sphere*, framed representability is equivalent to triviality of the normal bundle (tubular-neighbourhood theorem); a class is killable by the construction only through a framed embedded representative, and the converse that a framed representative is actually killed holds in the printed below-middle range. The slogan version would overclaim outside that range, where the dual handle can retain a Poincaré-dual class.
5. **The normal-map surgery item needs a null-homotopy.** The design's item 12 says "when the framed kernel element is chosen". In both sources the datum is an element `x` of `pi_{p+1}(f)` — a null-homotopy of `f` restricted to the surgery sphere — represented by the framed embedding (Lück Theorem 3.59(1); Ranicki Definition 10.6). The scaffold states the null-homotopy as part of the data; it is what produces both the map and the bundle extension on the handle.
6. **Source line.** The design's DT-21 source line names "MH §3, pp. 20--36" in addition to Lück, Ranicki and Wall. The available Milnor h-cobordism file (Auckland mirror) is a 122-page image scan with no text layer, and no OCR tool exists in this environment, so it could not be inspected as text and is **not** claimed as read. The two independent treatments required per A page are Lück (lecture-notes treatment, read in full over the stated ranges) and Ranicki (book, read in full over Chapter 10 §§10.1--10.4 and Chapter 7 §§7.2--7.3), with Wall Chapter 7 §§7.1--7.2 as a third full treatment. This is a limitation recorded honestly, not a retrieval failure of a required source.
7. **Wall URL recovery.** The plan's source register points to `https://people.math.ethz.ch/~dkosanovic/24-FS/Wall-Differential-Topology.pdf`, which answers 404 (attempt recorded 2026-10-05). The same book is reachable at the Auckland mirror `https://www.math.auckland.ac.nz/~hekmati/Books/Wall.pdf` (the URL already used by batch 1; identical hash evidence), and that mirror is the citation in the coverage and items.
8. **B item 3 stays inside the read sources.** The design asks to "identify the slope dependence without entering knot classification". The scaffold verifies framing dependence on the unknot in `S^3` with the two extreme framings (`S^2 x S^1` versus `S^3`, separated by first homology), computed from the two solid-torus decompositions. The `L(n,1)` classification of lens surgeries is **not** claimed, because the read ranges do not contain that computation; entering it would import a source outside the pair's verified evidence.
