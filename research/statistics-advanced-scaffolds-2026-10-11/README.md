## Assignments

- **bayesian-01**: Bayesian foundations: posterior existence/propriety, conjugacy, decisions/prediction, hierarchical models, empirical Bayes, marginal likelihood and Bayes factors.
- **bayesian-02**: Asymptotic/nonparametric Bayes: consistency/contraction, Bernstein–von Mises and failures, misspecification/robustness, Gaussian processes and nonparametric priors, uncertainty calibration.
- **bayesian-03**: Monte Carlo/MCMC: rejection/importance/control variates, error/CLTs, MH/Gibbs, ergodicity/mixing, HMC/Langevin numerical bias, adaptive MCMC conditions and diagnostic limitations.
- **bayesian-04**: Sequential computation: Kalman filtering/smoothing, state-space, SMC/particle filters, Feynman–Kac, error/degeneracy, particle MCMC, data assimilation.
- **bayesian-05**: Computational inference: latent-variable EM, variational inference/ELBO, Laplace and integration, ABC/simulation-based inference, identifiability/approximation bias. Generic factorization/optimization prerequisites must be here or earlier, never Category 4.
- **bayesian-06**: Resampling/computational uncertainty: bootstrap validity/failure/refinements, jackknife, permutation inference, cross-validation/model comparison, numerical versus statistical error, multilevel/quasi Monte Carlo.
- **learning-01**: Learning foundations: PAC/agnostic/ERM, VC/Rademacher reuse, stability/compression/PAC-Bayes, calibration/minimax/sample complexity, distribution shift/domain adaptation, adversarial robustness and fairness limitations.
- **learning-02**: Classical/structured ML: linear/kernel/SVM/RKHS, GP learning, trees/forests/boosting, clustering/mixtures, spectral/manifold/dimension reduction/metric learning, graphical/latent models, sparse prediction, causal discovery, privacy/federated/distributed learning.
- **learning-03**: Neural architectures/approximation: universal approximation exact topology/rates, depth-width separation/ReLU regions, CNN/group equivariance, GNN/WL limits, RNN/LSTM/state-space, attention/transformer definitions/expressivity, neural operators/physics-informed approximation.
- **learning-04**: Neural training/statistics: autodiff/backprop correctness, nonconvex GD/SGD/momentum/adaptive optimization, initialization, implicit bias, overparameterization/interpolation/double descent, NTK/mean-field/feature learning, norm/margin/compression/stability generalization and limits.
- **learning-05**: Advanced representation/generative learning: self-supervised/contrastive, autoencoders/VAE, GAN/IPM/optimal transport, normalizing flows, score/diffusion/flow matching, energy models, neural ODE/SDE, multimodal/transfer/foundation models, uncertainty/calibration; distinguish rigorous regimes from empirical claims.
- **learning-06**: Online/adaptive decisions: OCO/regret, bandits/contextual bandits, MDP/Bellman, RL exploration/sample complexity, policy gradient/actor-critic, offline/model-based/deep RL limits, partial observation, imitation, safe/constrained decisions and adaptive inference.
