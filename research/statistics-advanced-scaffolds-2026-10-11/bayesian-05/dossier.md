## Integration constraints and recommendation

The canonical working scaffold was inspected read-only. It currently assigns BCS-13 to numerical optimization, BCS-14 to EM, BCS-15 to VI, BCS-16 to Laplace, and BCS-17 to ABC. Preserve that sequence. Extend BCS-13 itself to publish the generic prerequisites all following computational methods need: a dominated latent/observed-data factorization and marginalization contract, KL/relative-entropy definition and nonnegativity, and the finite-dimensional optimization interfaces. This keeps generic factorization and optimization within Category 3, with no dependency on Category 4. If those foundations are instead split into a new earlier BCS pair, renumber all dependent pairs consistently before authoring; do not leave implicit prerequisites in BCS-14/15.

The current plan has no generic numerical-analysis or deterministic quadrature supplier. BCS-18 (assigned to Bayesian-03) supplies IID Monte Carlo, not deterministic quadrature. Put a proved composite trapezoid rule and explicit error bound in the BCS-16 A-page, or add a separately numbered earlier Category 3 numerical-integration pair. The simple theorem needed here is local: for `f ∈ C²[a,b]`, a uniform mesh of width `h=(b-a)/N`, and composite trapezoid rule `T_N`, prove `|∫_a^b f(t)dt - T_N| ≤ (b-a)h² ||f''||∞/12` by integrating the second-order interpolation remainder on each subinterval. Do not cite the FNC `O(h²)` assertion as a library proof: its displayed derivation points back to a section not read here.

BCS-17 must separate parameter identifiability from summary sufficiency. Distinct parameters inducing distinct full-data laws do not make an arbitrary summary sufficient or injective. Exact posterior recovery from zero-tolerance summary ABC needs sufficiency (or a stated information-preserving/injective summary with measurable inverse); the shrinking-tolerance limit additionally needs local support and density/kernel regularity. The planned MSF-05 sufficiency interface is unpublished, and the inspected statistical-model draft limits its countable factorization criterion to countable models while leaving the broader standard-Borel proof as an obligation. Scope ABC's sufficiency theorem to whatever MSF-05 actually proves when reconciled.

Add two post-core interfaces after BCS-18 and the existing numerical/statistical-error pair, if the author has room in the coverage-driven sequence:

1. **Synthetic likelihood and indirect inference.** Define the Gaussian synthetic likelihood for a declared summary vector and distinguish its ideal population mean/covariance target from finite-simulation plug-in estimates. Define the indirect-inference binding map and pseudo-true optimizer, and require identification of the target by that map for parameter recovery. Keep BSL asymptotic coverage/calibration out until the finite-simulation covariance condition and the supplement-dependent proof route are supplied locally.
2. **Identifiability and computational approximation bias.** Define the exact inferential target and each approximation target separately (summary/tolerance ABC, reverse-KL variational family, Laplace truncation, synthetic likelihood, numerical optimizer/integrator). State error for a named target/metric and decompose exact-target error from sampling/model error by triangle inequality. Use MSF-01 observational equivalence and estimand-identifiability interfaces, not Hessian rank alone. A nonsingular local Hessian is local curvature; it is not a global identifiability theorem. Add explicit counterexamples for a non-sufficient ABC summary, a restricted mean-field family, and singular/nonidentified modes where a one-mode Gaussian Laplace approximation does not describe the full posterior.

These additions are proposed as BCS-40 and BCS-41, respectively, unless the author folds their complete scope into another pair. A separate later ABC-MCMC/ABC-SMC algorithm pair is also sensible after the generic MH and SMC pairs (currently BCS-21 and BCS-27); it should name the augmented simulator/tolerance target and inherit those exact kernels, not imply that ordinary MH or SMC removes ABC tolerance bias. None of these additions may depend on Category 4.

### BCS-13 — Computational foundations: factorization, relative entropy, and optimization

**Pages:** `numerical-optimization-for-statistical-computation` / `numerical-optimization-for-statistical-computation-examples` (existing working slugs; consider title broadening without changing the path). **Scope:** (i) Define a latent-variable model by a joint complete-data density/kernel and an observation map or marginal, with a common reference measure where a density is used. State the positive-finite marginal-fiber restriction before defining a pointwise conditional latent density. (ii) Define KL for probability laws with absolute continuity, set it to `+∞` when absolute continuity fails, and prove nonnegativity by Jensen/`u log u`; equality means equality of probability laws a.e., not equality of model parameters. Add a chain/variational identity only under the explicit common-support and finite-integrability premises. (iii) Define gradient descent with stated step selection and backtracking, and damped Newton with a positive-definite Hessian solve and line search; distinguish stopping rules from convergence theorems. Give a strongly convex/smooth convergence theorem in its exact step/line-search regime and a local damped-Newton theorem under nonsingular positive curvature and Lipschitz Hessian. Do not claim that a nonconvex statistical objective has a global optimum or that a numerical stationary point is a global MAP. **Proof route:** Tonelli for density marginalization; Jensen/RN for KL; descent lemma and line-search argument; strong-convexity/smoothness estimate; local Taylor/Hessian-Lipschitz contraction for Newton. ### BCS-14 — Latent-variable EM and likelihood ascent

**Pages:** `em-algorithm-and-latent-variable-likelihood-ascent` / `...-examples`. **Scope:** Define the E-step conditional law at current `θ_t`, `Q(θ|θ_t)=E_{θ_t}[log p_θ(X,Z)|X=x]`, and an exact M-step; permit a GEM step that increases `Q`. Require `0<p_{θ_t}(x)<∞` and, for each candidate `θ` where a pointwise identity is used, `0<p_θ(x)<∞`; define the update domain accordingly or treat zero-likelihood candidates separately. Require a proper conditional latent law and finite or carefully controlled extended expectations/KL. When `q_t≪p_θ(·|x)` and log terms are integrable, prove `log p_θ(x)-log p_{θ_t}(x) = Q(θ|θ_t)-Q(θ_t|θ_t)+KL(q_t || p_θ(·|x))`. For support changes, prove the Jensen lower-bound inequality directly and require `p_θ(x,z)>0` `q_t`-a.s. whenever Q-ascent is invoked; a candidate with `Q=-∞` cannot certify ascent. Thus an exact M-step with finite Q or a finite Q-ascent GEM step cannot decrease observed log likelihood under the declared update domain. State only this ascent result by default. Any stationarity claim needs differentiability/interchange and update-limit hypotheses; any sequence convergence claim needs a separate theorem. Monotonicity does not imply uniqueness, global optimality, finite convergence, or a well-defined update at a zero likelihood. **Proof route:** Bayes factorization and KL identity from BCS-13; conditional expectation/change-of-measure; compare `Q` values. **Examples:** finite mixture responsibility update; GEM; two local maxima; Gaussian-mixture covariance collapse/unbounded likelihood as a failure of a global maximizer. **Requires:** BCS-13; planned `BCS-02` and `MSF-04 def-likelihood-density`; existing `thm-conditional-density-formula`, `thm-bayes-formula-for-dominated-kernels`, `thm-conditional-expectation-exists-by-radon-nikodym`, `thm-conditional-jensen-inequality`, `thm-change-of-variables-for-expectation`. **Sources:** [S01] PDF pp.1–7/printed pp.1–7, §§1–4; [S02] only as optimization background. **Proposed items:** `alg-expectation-maximization`, `thm-em-observed-log-likelihood-is-nondecreasing`, `thm-generalized-em-is-nondecreasing-under-q-ascent`, `rem-em-ascent-does-not-imply-global-optimality-or-convergence`.

### BCS-15 — Variational inference and the evidence lower bound

**Pages:** `variational-inference-and-evidence-lower-bound` / `...-examples`. **Scope:** For an unnormalized posterior density `γ(z)` with `0<Z=∫γ<∞`, define a variational density `q` and `ELBO(q)=E_q[log γ(Z)-log q(Z)]`. If `q≪π=γ/Z` and the displayed log terms are integrable, prove `log Z=ELBO(q)+KL(q||π)`. Define mean-field `q(z)=∏_j q_j(z_j)` and derive coordinate update `q_j^*(z_j)∝exp(E_{q_{-j}} log γ(z))` only when the conditional expectation exists and its normalizer is positive finite. Coordinate ascent is nondecreasing only when each coordinate maximizer exists and is exact. Record reverse-KL direction, local optima, and that the approximation family may suppress dependence and understate marginal variance. An ELBO value by itself is not a universal calibration or downstream functional-error guarantee; the gap requires `log Z` or a separate bound. **Proof route:** BCS-13 KL identity and product-density algebra; integrate out coordinate terms; exact coordinate maximization. **Examples:** exact finite discrete family; correlated bivariate Gaussian under a factorized family; coordinate update with no finite normalizer. **Requires:** BCS-13, planned BCS-02 / `MSF-04 def-likelihood-density`; existing `thm-conditional-density-formula`, `thm-bayes-formula-for-dominated-kernels`, `thm-change-of-variables-for-expectation`, Tonelli/Fubini only where justified. **Sources:** [S03] PDF pp.1–11, §§1, 2.1–2.5; independent derivation [S04] PDF pp.31–38/printed pp.213–220, §§6.1–6.3. **Proposed items:** `def-variational-family-and-elbo`, `thm-elbo-kl-decomposition`, `alg-coordinate-ascent-variational-inference`, `thm-exact-coordinate-ascent-does-not-decrease-elbo`, `cex-mean-field-variational-inference-underestimates-correlated-gaussian-variance`, `rem-high-elbo-alone-does-not-certify-posterior-calibration`.

### BCS-16 — Laplace approximation, deterministic quadrature, and marginal integration

**Pages:** `laplace-approximation-and-marginal-integration` / `...-examples`. **Scope:** State a leading multivariate Laplace theorem for `I_n=∫_K a(θ)exp(nh(θ))dθ` on compact `K⊂R^d`, where `h` is continuous with unique interior maximizer `θ̂`, is `C³` near it, `-∇²h(θ̂)=H` is positive definite, `a` is continuous with `a(θ̂)>0`, and local quadratic domination plus a strict outside-neighborhood gap hold. Then `I_n ∼ a(θ̂)e^{nh(θ̂)}(2π/n)^{d/2}/√det(H)`. Give the posterior evidence instance only when the log likelihood has the declared `n h` regime and the prior is a positive continuous amplitude at the interior mode. Do not assert an `O(n⁻¹)` relative remainder unless fourth-order smoothness and a full error proof are provided. For general noncompact domains, state tail localization as an extra hypothesis; do not silently replace it by local curvature. Add composite trapezoid algorithm and the `C²[a,b]` error theorem stated above. **Proof route:** Taylor expansion and rescaling `u=√n(θ-θ̂)`; dominated convergence on the local rescaled integral and exponential control off the mode; Cholesky/spectral theorem and Gaussian integral for determinant normalization. Composite trapezoid error follows by summing integrated local second-derivative interpolation bounds. **Examples:** normal quadratic exact integral; boundary mode (different scale); two equal modes (sum contributions); singular Hessian; one-dimensional smooth quadrature mesh. **Requires:** BCS-13; existing `thm-multivariable-taylor-formula-with-lagrange-remainder`, `cor-second-order-taylor-expansion-with-the-hessian`, `thm-cholesky-factorisation-exists-iff-hermitian-positive-definite-and-is-unique`, `cor-real-spectral-theorem-for-self-adjoint-endomorphisms`, `thm-gaussian-integral`, `cor-real-gamma-one-half-is-root-pi`, and (for posterior evidence) BCS-02. **Sources:** heuristic/tutorial [S05] entire PDF pp.1–8; conditions/asymptotic expansion [S06] DLMF §2.3(iii), equations 2.3.13–2.3.18 and conditions (a)–(c); quadrature definition/error order [S07] §5.6.1, equations 5.6.1–5.6.9. The DLMF refers the detailed proof/error bound to external works; the local leading theorem and quadrature remainder still need their stated local proofs. **Proposed items:** `alg-laplace-approximation`, `thm-multivariate-laplace-leading-term-at-interior-nondegenerate-mode`, `cex-single-mode-laplace-fails-under-boundary-or-singular-curvature`, `alg-composite-trapezoid-rule`, `thm-composite-trapezoid-error-for-c2-integrands`.

### BCS-17 — Approximate Bayesian computation and summary-based inference

**Pages:** `approximate-bayesian-computation-and-simulation-based-inference` / `...-examples`. **Scope:** Define prior predictive simulator `θ∼Π`, `Y∼P_θ`, summary `S(Y)`, metric/kernel `K_ε(S(y_obs),S(Y))`, and rejection target with density proportional to `Π(dθ)∫K_ε(S(y_obs),S(y))P_θ(dy)`. For an indicator kernel this is the exact posterior under the augmented event `d(S(Y),S(y_obs))≤ε`, conditional on positive event probability. In the zero-tolerance/density limit, recover the posterior based on `S` under explicitly stated local density regularity; recover the full-data posterior only if `S` is sufficient or information-preserving in the stated sense. Parameter identifiability of the full model does not imply summary sufficiency. Keep finite simulation/acceptance Monte Carlo error separate from summary-information loss and tolerance bias. Do not state generic frequentist coverage or universal error rates. **Proof route:** disintegrate the prior-simulator joint law; normalize on positive-finite fibers; use the local kernel/density limit; use the sufficiency factorization interface for full-posterior equality. ## Open proof obligations and claims intentionally withheld

1. Prove BCS-13 generic latent/observed factorization and KL interfaces with common-reference, support, and finiteness premises. The current library supplies Tonelli and conditional densities but not these statistical definitions/results.
2. In BCS-13, expose the precise line-search conditions used for gradient-descent convergence and the exact local Newton neighborhood/contraction hypotheses. Boyd’s cited results are for convex/smooth or locally regular regimes; they do not license a global result for nonconvex likelihoods.
3. In EM, prove the fixed-data ELBO/KL identity only on a valid positive-finite observed-data fiber. If adding stationary-point or sequence convergence results, separately prove all differentiation, compactness, continuity, and limit-point conditions.
4. In VI, show coordinate normalizers exist and keep the exact coordinate maximization premise. An ELBO is an evidence lower bound, not automatic calibration.
5. In Laplace, do the local rescaling/DCT and off-mode tail bound; choose and prove one clean domain/tail contract. DLMF gives hypotheses/asymptotic statement but points elsewhere for full proofs/error bounds; UConn is heuristic. Boundary, multiple, or singular modes need their own expansions and are not corollaries of the single-mode theorem.
6. Prove local trapezoid error directly; FNC’s exact `O(h²)` rationale points to an unread section. Never substitute IID Monte Carlo convergence for deterministic quadrature.
