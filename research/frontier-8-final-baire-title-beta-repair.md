# Frontier 8 final Baire-title Beta repair

- **Item:** `thm-baire-category-for-complete-metric-spaces`
- **Old title:** “A complete metric space is not a countable union of closed sets with empty interior”
- **New title:** “A nonempty complete metric space is not a countable union of closed sets with empty interior”

The item's Statement already assumes that the metric space is nonempty, while the old title omitted that hypothesis. Since the empty metric space is complete and is the union of the empty-interior closed sequence, the unqualified title asserted too much. This repair changes only the reader-facing item title; its containing page derives item titles mechanically and therefore needs no source edit.
