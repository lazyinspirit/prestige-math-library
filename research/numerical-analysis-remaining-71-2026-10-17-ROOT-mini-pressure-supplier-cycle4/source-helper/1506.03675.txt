A note on the pressure of strong solutions to the Stokes system
in bounded and exterior domains
J¨org Wolf
Department of Mathematics
Humboldt University Berlin
joerg@math.hu-berlin.de
Abstract
We consider the Stokes problem in an exterior domain Ω⊂Rn with an external force
f ∈Ls(0, T ; W k, r(Ω)) (k ∈N, 1 < r < ∞). In the present paper we show that in contrast to
u the boundary regularity of the pressure can be improved according to the diﬀerentiability
of f up to order k. In particular, this implies that the pressure is smooth with respect to
x ∈Ωif f is smooth with respect to x ∈Ω.
Keywords Stokes equations, exterior domain, boundary regularity
Mathematics subject classiﬁcation 35Q30, 76D03.
