## Exact distinction and minimal correction

The definition presently says that **both** global Ext and sheaf Ext “agree with local computations from finite locally free resolutions” for coherent `F`. This is false for global Ext if local computation means taking cohomology of the local Hom complex. On `Y=P^1_k`, put `F=O_Y` and `G=O_Y(-2)`. The length-zero locally free resolution of `F` makes `\mathcal E xt^q_Y(F,G)=0` for `q>0`; nevertheless `\operatorname{Ext}^1_Y(F,G)=H^1(Y,O_Y(-2))\cong k` because `\operatorname{Hom}_{O_Y}(O_Y,-)=\Gamma(Y,-)`. Stacks, *Cohomology of Sheaves* §43, tag 0BQP, explicitly distinguishes sheaf Ext from global Ext and gives the local-to-global spectral sequence `H^p(Y,\mathcal E xt^q(F,G))⇒\operatorname{Ext}^{p+q}_Y(F,G)` in the applicable bounded cases.

The **smallest ready-safe edit** is to delete the sentence beginning “for coherent F these agree…” from this definition. Keep the two displayed constructions, write `\mathcal E xt` for the sheaf-valued one to distinguish it from `\operatorname{Ext}`, and state that comparison maps and homotopies make each independent of the chosen injective resolution. If local finite-free computation is wanted now, state it **only for sheaf Ext**, on an open `U` where `F|_U` has a finite locally free resolution `P^\bullet`: `\mathcal E xt^q_Y(F,G)|_U\cong\mathcal H^q(\mathcal H om_{O_U}(P^\bullet,G|_U))`. That additional assertion needs the local exactness of `\mathcal H om(P^i,-)`, restriction of injectives/comparison or a bounded double-complex argument, and a real finite locally free supplier; the unbuilt AV-18 page cannot be assumed. It is cleaner to prove it in a later lemma where the Koszul calculation consumes it. For global Ext, a finite locally free resolution generally requires **hypercohomology** of its Hom complex, not ordinary cohomology of global Hom terms without acyclicity.

## What the current suppliers actually prove

Stacks, *Cohomology of Sheaves* Lemmas 8.1 and 12.2–12.3 (printed PDF pp. 8–9, 18–19), confirms the first item's relevant route: an injective `O_Y`-module has surjective restrictions via `j_!O_U`, hence is flasque and acyclic on every open. The published module enough-injectives theorem supplies functorial stalk injective targets for **commutative** stalk rings, and the product of injective skyscrapers gives an `O_Y`-module injective embedding. The first item's stated use is on ordinary commutative ringed spaces of schemes; if “every ringed space” is intended to include noncommutative structure sheaves, its functoriality claim needs an additional construction or should be qualified. Its AC hypothesis covers the choice used by products, Zorn, and the comparison theorem's DC hypothesis. The published acyclic-resolution theorem and injective-comparison theorems have separately recorded missing **direct DC dependency metadata** in the canonical published ledger; that published metadata debt remains, but the local contracts explicitly assume AC and do not invoke the inapplicable enough-projectives balanced-Ext theorem.

After removal of the false final clause, the definition is sound for arbitrary `O_Y`-modules: choose an `O_Y`-injective resolution of `G`; `H^q\operatorname{Hom}_{O_Y}(F,I^\bullet)` is global Ext, while `\mathcal H^q\mathcal H om_{O_Y}(F,I^\bullet)` is sheaf Ext. Published injective comparison maps and uniqueness up to homotopy make both constructions resolution-independent; the internal Hom sheaf functor is additive and carries cochain homotopies to cochain homotopies. No coherent-sheaf or finite-free resolution theorem is needed to **define** them.

The first-variable lemma then follows independently of projective sheaves. For each `p`, injectivity of `I^p` sends `0→F'→F→F''→0` to the short exact sequence `0→Hom(F'',I^p)→Hom(F,I^p)→Hom(F',I^p)→0`. The published natural long exact cohomology sequence yields the displayed **global** Ext sequence, with `Ext^0=Hom` by left exactness. Any comparison map between two injective resolutions of `G` induces a map of these short exact sequences of complexes; uniqueness up to homotopy gives a resolution-independent natural connecting morphism. This proves the lemma under its explicit AC assumption, with no general sheaf-Ext/local-to-global claim.
