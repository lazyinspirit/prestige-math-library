## Decisions

### `the-ergodic-theorems-of-von-neumann-and-birkhoff`: sufficient

The 26 A items and 8 B items cover the whole MT-23 programme: time averages and
their quotient well-definedness, the maximal ergodic theorem, sigma-finite
nonergodic Birkhoff convergence, finite-measure invariant-sigma-algebra
identification, finite-measure $L^p$ convergence for $1\le p<\infty$, the
ergodic finite-measure specialization, and von Neumann's $L^2$ theorem. The
topological half includes unique ergodicity, irrational rotations, Weyl
equidistribution by continuous approximation, canonical base-$b$ expansions,
simultaneous all-base Borel normality, and the fair-coin frequency theorem. The
B page supplies every designed computation and boundary example, including a
nonconstant nonergodic limit, the $L^2$ projection, an uncountable null set of
nonnormal numbers, and failure without integrability.

Walkden's complete relevant sections and Del Vigna's complete six-page note
cover the mean, maximal and pointwise ergodic spine, unique ergodicity, normal
numbers and the main examples; derived $L^p$ and digit consequences have the
needed earlier interfaces in the manifest. The pair is the intended full
ergodic-theorem supplier for `stationary-markov-chains-and-ergodic-limits`.
Entropy, joinings and Ornstein theory are expressly a later ergodic-theory
track, not omissions here. No merger or enrichment is needed.

### `banach-alaoglu-goldstine-and-krein-milman`: sufficient

The 16 A items and 7 B items implement FA-9 and its amendment. They include the
product model and Banach--Alaoglu theorem, the added absolute-polar definition
and polar compactness, bounded-ball weak-star metrizability and the separable
sequential corollary, Goldstine and finite-data approximation,
Banach--Dieudonne, extreme points and faces, both Krein--Milman forms, Bauer's
maximum principle, Milman's converse, and the dual-ball extreme-point

Dirac extreme points, the $\ell^\infty$/$c_0$ contrast, the isometric nondual
consequence, the nonseparable weak-star sequential counterexample, and the
choice/sequentiality boundary.

The 37 coverage rows draw on complete treatments by Buhler--Salamon, Teschl,
Ball and Hanche-Olsen and explicitly dispose the nearby invariant-set,
ordinal, locally compact point-mass, Hilbert-cube and extra extreme-point
examples. Those exclusions do not weaken this pair's compactness and compact
convex-geometry role. The RMK prerequisite now supplies the measure examples,
and the page exports the compactness/extreme-point interfaces required by
reflexivity and several later analysis consumers. No merger or enrichment is
needed.
