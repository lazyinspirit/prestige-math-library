# Batch 98 source reading record

All local PDFs and extracted text files in this directory are retained as read-only evidence. The relevant full-text passages below were inspected; a citation documents context and never replaces the local proof.

## IMEX and additive Runge–Kutta sources

- Uri M. Ascher, Steven J. Ruuth, and Raymond J. Spiteri, “Implicit-explicit Runge-Kutta methods for time-dependent partial differential equations,” *Applied Numerical Mathematics* 25 (1997), full 17-page author PDF: <https://chaosbook.org/library/AscherANM97.pdf>. Local copies: ascher-anm97.pdf and ascher-anm97.txt. Inspected §2 stage equations, additive tableaus, and order discussion (PDF pp. 2–5), then §3 scalar stability (PDF pp. 6–8). Supports the standard IMEX stage setup, ARS tableau context, and scalar test equation for alg-imex-euler-step, def-ars222-additive-tableau-and-stages, prop-ars222-bivariate-stability-region, and the stiff partition example. The branch, residual, colored-order, and global-error arguments are proved locally.
- Lorenzo Pareschi and Giovanni Russo, “Implicit-explicit Runge-Kutta schemes and applications to hyperbolic systems with relaxation,” full 20-page arXiv author text: <https://arxiv.org/pdf/1009.2757>. Local copies: pareschi-russo-arxiv.pdf and pareschi-russo-arxiv.txt. Inspected §2.1 (PDF pp. 2–3), Table 2 (p. 6), and §3 stability discussion (PDF pp. 3–5). Supports the four coupled second-order conditions, the ARS coefficient/tableau context, and scalar additive stability for lem-ars222-coefficient-identities, thm-ars222-additive-order-two-and-tolerance, prop-ars222-bivariate-stability-region, and ex-reaction-diffusion-ars222-mode-amplification. The theorem derives the colored conditions from the local stage equations; the general single-field RK proposition from batch 35 is not used as an additive-order proof.
- Pareschi and Russo, published 2005 treatment, full 20-page author copy: <https://www.math.ntnu.no/conservation/2004/063.pdf>. Local copies: pareschi-russo-jsc2005.pdf and pareschi-russo-jsc2005.txt. Read the matching additive-stage and order-condition sections. Disposition: duplicate treatment by the same authors; retained as source history and not counted as an independent treatment.

## Exact splitting

- Ernst Hairer, *Geometric Numerical Integration*, Lecture 2: “Symplectic Integrators,” full 18-page lecture note: <https://www.unige.ch/~hairer/poly_geoint/week2.pdf>. Local copies: hairer-gni-week2.pdf and hairer-gni-week2.txt. Read §5 (PDF pp. 12–14), covering Lie–Trotter and Strang flow compositions, their order comparison, and the stated scope. Supports def-exact-split-flows-lie-strang-and-bracket, lem-lie-strang-local-error-expansions, and thm-lie-strang-global-orders-and-subflow-error. The commutator coefficients, common-tube hypotheses, stability bounds, and subflow error budgets are proved locally.
- Gilbert Strang, “On the Construction and Comparison of Difference Schemes,” *SIAM Journal on Numerical Analysis* 5(3) (1968), 506–517, publisher PDF: <https://epubs.siam.org/doi/pdf/10.1137/0705041>. The complete 12-page article was inspected through the full-text web PDF reader, including the smooth-solution half-step symmetry/order discussion on printed pp. 509–513 (PDF pp. 3–7). It supports the historical symmetric-composition context for the same three A-page items. CLI full-body retrieval failures and the local proof alternatives are recorded in the batch source-coverage file; no quote or fetched-byte stamp is claimed for this source here.

## Dirichlet sine modes

- Richard S. Laugesen, *Spectral Theory of Partial Differential Equations*, full 120-page graduate lecture notes: <https://arxiv.org/pdf/1203.2344>. Local copies: laugesen-spectral-pde-notes.pdf and laugesen-spectral-pde-notes.txt. Inspected the complete relevant printed p. 15, which gives interval Dirichlet sine eigenfunctions and eigenvalues. Supports the modal heat interpretation in def-dirichlet-sine-mode-heat-splitting-model and the companion boundary example. The generator-domain proof and the exact $h^{5/4}$ modal error calculation are supplied locally; no PDE completeness or general unbounded-operator convergence result is imported.
