Assume countable choice and fix a generic movie \(M\) of a smooth oriented surface cobordism \(\Sigma\) from an oriented link \(L_0\) to an oriented link \(L_1\). For each regular frame \(D\), let \(Kh(D)\) be the normalized integral Khovanov homology group. Associate to a birth the unit map \(\eta:\mathbb Z\to\mathcal A\), \(\eta(1)=1\); associate to a death the counit \(\epsilon:\mathcal A\to\mathbb Z\), \(\epsilon(1)=0,\epsilon(X)=1\); and associate to a saddle the multiplication \(m:\mathcal A\otimes\mathcal A\to\mathcal A\) when two resolution circles merge, or the comultiplication \(\Delta:\mathcal A\to\mathcal A\otimes\mathcal A\) when one splits. Each local map is tensored with the identity on unaffected circles. Associate to planar isotopy the induced identification of the cube complexes, and to a Reidemeister move a chain homotopy equivalence between its normalized cube complexes. Compose the induced maps on homology in movie order and denote the result by \(Kh(M):Kh(L_0)\to Kh(L_1)\), considered modulo multiplication by \(-1\). This definition fixes a movie; independence from changing the movie is not included in the definition.
