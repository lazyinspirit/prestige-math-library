Let \(\mathcal A=\mathbb Z[X]/(X^2)\) with unit \(\eta(1)=1\) and counit \(\epsilon(1)=0,\epsilon(X)=1\). On a movie with one unaffected circle, birth a disjoint circle and then cap that same circle by a death. The composite operator is
\[
\mathcal A\xrightarrow{\operatorname{id}_{\mathcal A}\otimes\eta}
\mathcal A\otimes\mathcal A\xrightarrow{\operatorname{id}_{\mathcal A}\otimes\epsilon}
\mathcal A,
\]
which is zero and therefore is not the identity, even after allowing multiplication by an overall sign.

```tikz
\begin{tikzcd}
\mathcal A
\arrow[r, "{\operatorname{id}\otimes\eta}"]
& \mathcal A\otimes\mathcal A
\arrow[r, "{\operatorname{id}\otimes\epsilon}"]
& \mathcal A
\end{tikzcd}
```

The movie is a cylinder on the original circle disjoint union a born-and-capped sphere; the diagram specifies the local birth and death sequence and is not used as a proof.
