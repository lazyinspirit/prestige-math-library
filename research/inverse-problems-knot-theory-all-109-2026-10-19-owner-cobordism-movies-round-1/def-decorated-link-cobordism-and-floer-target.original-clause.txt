Let \(Y_0,Y_1\) be closed smooth oriented three-manifolds, let \(W\) be a compact smooth oriented four-manifold with boundary \(-Y_0\sqcup Y_1\), where \(-Y_0\) has the reversed orientation, and let \(K_i\subset Y_i\) be images of smooth oriented embeddings of the circle. A **decorated knot cobordism** from \((Y_0,K_0)\) to \((Y_1,K_1)\) is a properly embedded smooth oriented surface \(\Sigma\subset W\) with boundary \((-K_0)\sqcup K_1\), together with one \(w\)-basepoint and one \(z\)-basepoint on each end knot and a finite union \(\mathcal A\subset\Sigma\) of properly embedded arcs. The arcs divide \(\Sigma\) into subsurfaces \(\Sigma_w,\Sigma_z\), with each \(w\)-basepoint in the closure of \(\Sigma_w\), each \(z\)-basepoint in the closure of \(\Sigma_z\), and \(\mathcal A\) meeting the boundary transversely away from the marked points. The basepoint labels and the two regions are part of the data.

For an end pair \((Y,K)\), a doubly pointed Heegaard diagram consists of a closed genus-\(g\) oriented surface \(S\), two \(g\)-tuples \(\boldsymbol\alpha,\boldsymbol\beta\) of pairwise disjoint simple closed curves giving a Heegaard splitting of \(Y\), and points \(w,z\) in the complement of those curves such that arcs in the two handlebodies from \(w\) to \(z\) form \(K\). Each intersection point \(\mathbf x\in\mathbb T_{\boldsymbol\alpha}\cap\mathbb T_{\boldsymbol\beta}\) determines a Spin-c structure \(\mathfrak s_w(\mathbf x)\) on \(Y\) from the homology class of the nonvanishing gradient vector field specified by \(\mathbf x\) and \(w\). For \(\mathfrak t\in\operatorname{Spin}^c(Y)\), let \(CFL^-_{UV}(Y,K,\mathfrak t)\) be generated over \(R=\mathbb F_2[U,V]\) by those intersection points with \(\mathfrak s_w(\mathbf x)=\mathfrak t\). A periodic domain is an integral two-chain in the regions of \(S\setminus(\boldsymbol\alpha\cup\boldsymbol\beta)\) whose boundary is an integral sum of the attaching curves; its capped homology class in \(Y\) is denoted \([P]\), and \(n_w(P)\) is its multiplicity at \(w\). Restrict to diagrams strongly admissible for \(\mathfrak t\): there is a generator \(\mathbf x\) with \(\mathfrak s_w(\mathbf x)=\mathfrak t\), and for every nonzero periodic domain \(P\) with \(n_w(P)=0\) and \(\langle c_1(\mathfrak t),[P]\rangle=2N>0\), some region of \(P\) has multiplicity at least \(N\). Here \(c_1(\mathfrak t)\) is the first Chern class of its determinant line bundle. The symmetric product \(\operatorname{Sym}^g(S)\) is the quotient of \(S^g\) by permutations of the factors. The associated product tori \(\mathbb T_{\boldsymbol\alpha},\mathbb T_{\boldsymbol\beta}\subset\operatorname{Sym}^g(S)\) determine the differential on each Spin-c summand by
\[
\partial \mathbf x=\sum_{\mathbf y}\ \sum_{\substack{\phi\in\pi_2(\mathbf x,\mathbf y)\\ \mu(\phi)=1}}
#_2\bigl(\mathcal M(\phi)/\mathbb R\bigr)\,U^{n_w(\phi)}V^{n_z(\phi)}\mathbf y.
\]
Here \(\pi_2(\mathbf x,\mathbf y)\) is the set of homotopy classes of Whitney disks, \(\mu\) is the Maslov index, \(\mathcal M(\phi)/\mathbb R\) is the moduli space of index-one disks modulo translation for chosen generic almost-complex data, \(n_w,n_z\) are their local multiplicities at the marked points, and \(\#_2\) is the finite count modulo two. This is the two-variable specialization of the colored link Floer complex. Its curvature vanishes: for a knot with one \(w\)- and one \(z\)-basepoint the curvature formula is \(UV+VU=0\) in \(\mathbb F_2[U,V]\), so \(\partial^2=0\). The complex has the two-coordinate filtration induced by the \(w\)- and \(z\)-multiplicities.

For a chosen \(\mathfrak s\in\operatorname{Spin}^c(W)\), the Floer map indexed by \(\mathfrak s\) has the complexes for \((Y_0,K_0,\mathfrak s|_{Y_0})\) and \((Y_1,K_1,\mathfrak s|_{Y_1})\) as source and target; \(\operatorname{Spin}^c(W)\) denotes the set of Spin-c structures on \(W\).
