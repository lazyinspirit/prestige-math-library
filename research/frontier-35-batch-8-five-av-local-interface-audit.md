The relative $\mathbf P^1$ assertion for an arbitrary complex base also needs a local Picard/projective-bundle rule: on each trivialization $\mathbf P^1_U$, a line bundle of constant fibre degree $m$ must be identified as $\mathcal O(m)\otimes\pi^*M$, with its change-of-trivialization law. Fibre degree alone is not the construction of this identification. The Stacks projective-bundle calculation below starts with $\mathcal O(d)$; it does not by itself classify an arbitrary $L$.

## A dependency-ordered local proof obligation list

These are *interfaces*, not a claim that ten new item records suffice. Existing batch-8 items 2, 5, 10–14 and 26 would need full proofs that absorb some of them; the rest could use the 33 free slots. Every arrow has to be proved in the A page or point to an actually published result.

1. Affine module sheafification: $M\mapsto\widetilde M$, $\Gamma(D(f),\widetilde M)=M_f$, exactness, and quasi-coherent restrictions; Noetherian coherence and closure of kernels.
2. $\operatorname{Proj}$ charts, intersections and $\mathcal O(d)$, including finite affine covers and closed projective embeddings. This supports the existing twist-cohomology item and defines the word “projective” in the smooth-projective theorem.
3. Affine quasi-coherent acyclicity: contract the localized augmented Čech complex after every prime localization, then pass from cofinal standard covers to derived sheaf cohomology. Only then does the standard $\mathbf P^N$ affine cover compute sheaf cohomology. Stacks, *Cohomology of Schemes* Lemmas 2.1–2.2 (tags 01X9, 01XB), prove exactly these separate steps.
4. Laurent-monomial $H^q(\mathbf P^N,\mathcal O(d))$, with multiplication/base-change compatibility and residue pairing. The existing item 2 states the answer, but its proof must use interfaces 1–3. Stacks Lemmas 8.1–8.2 (01XT, 01XV) provide a detailed source proof.
5. A specialized Serre theorem for $\mathbf P^N_k$: every coherent $F$ has a finite sum-of-twists surjection with coherent kernel; $H^q(F)$ is finite dimensional, positive $H^q(F(d))$ eventually vanishes, and the truncated section module $\bigoplus_{d\ge d_0}H^0(F(d))$ is finitely generated and sheafifies back to $F$. Stacks Lemma 14.1 (01YS) obtains these using ampleness, coherent-kernel closure and induction; Proposition 14.4 (0BXD) uses the sheafification equivalence. A local replacement must prove the specialized cases, not cite either result as an already published supplier.
6. Finite **graded free** resolution of that finitely generated $k[x_0,\ldots,x_N]$-module, followed by exact sheafification, to prove the existing item 5 for every coherent $F$. Local regularity/Auslander–Buchsbaum alone yields local finite free resolutions, not a global resolution by direct sums of twists. A graded Hilbert-syzygy argument, or a proved equivalent, is needed.
7. Cup/evaluation and Yoneda-Ext pairings compatible with connecting morphisms for finite twist resolutions. This is necessary to promote the twist residue duality to the existing coherent-$F$ theorem and then use the ambient $\mathbf P^N$ theorem. Vakil, Class 53, pp. 90–91, expressly omits the connecting-square check because it needs a cup-product construction. A citation to that proof does not close the gap.
8. Smoothness-to-regularity/cotangent facts; regular-embedding Koszul resolution; adjunction determinant; local sheaf-Ext concentration; and transfer from sheaf Ext to global Ext. The published regular-local parameter-ideal and Koszul theorems supply ring-level substeps, not the scheme-level assembly. A smaller transfer proof is available: for injective $\mathcal O$-module $I$, internal $\mathcal Hom(F,I)$ is flasque (extend maps via the monomorphism $j_!(F|_U)\hookrightarrow F|_V$); Grothendieck's spectral sequence then gives $H^p(Y,\mathcal Ext^q(F,G))\Rightarrow\operatorname{Ext}^{p+q}_Y(F,G)$. The Koszul calculation leaves one row $q=c$. Closed-immersion exact pushforward identifies $H^p(Y,i_*M)=H^p(X,M)$. Thus **this** transfer need not invoke affine quasi-coherent acyclicity, although items 2 and 26 still do.
9. For smooth projective $X\hookrightarrow\mathbf P^N$, prove that the resulting Ext isomorphism is induced by cup/evaluation and an embedding-independent normalized trace, not just an abstract isomorphism of vector spaces. Stacks, *Duality for Schemes* §27 Lemma 27.1(5),(7), Lemma 27.4, and Remark 27.6 state this via the $f^!$ dualizing-complex machine from preceding sections. That machine is not a local supplier in this run; the elementary ambient/Koszul route must establish its own trace/naturality comparison.
10. On $\mathbf P^1_S$, prove the relative line-bundle description, $R^q\pi_*\mathcal O(d)$ by affine-base Čech plus base-change/gluing, and the $GL_2$-equivariant apolarity identification. Stacks Lemmas 8.2–8.4 (01XV–01XX) contain the projective-bundle formulas; Lemma 8.3's proof is explicitly omitted and Lemma 8.4 uses an additional Koszul spectral sequence. Lurie's three-page Borel–Weil–Bott note states the degree-shift mechanism but assumes its family-level descent. After the direct-image isomorphism is actually constructed, the now-published Leray spectral sequence collapses to the existing cohomology-shift theorem. The $m=-1$ case has both direct images zero and needs no nonzero apolarity map.

The algebraic-group quotient/Borel/minimal-parabolic supplier proposed by the worker is a **separate** gap. None of the five AV replacements proves it. Even if all ten interfaces above are carried out, readiness of all 27 A items still requires that group geometry and the three B examples to close.

## Current verdict

The full AV-15 and AV-17 predecessor pages are mathematically broader than this pair needs, and AV-18/19/22 can likewise be narrowed to the listed interfaces. That establishes a *possible shape* for local repair, not a complete 33-slot proof. In particular, the graded finite-resolution theorem, connecting-square/cup compatibility, embedding-independent trace comparison, relative Picard description, and arbitrary-base $GL_2$-equivariant direct-image descent remain unverified locally. There is no sound numerical lower bound on item records: one item can contain a long proof, so neither `33 free slots` nor `five missing pages` proves feasibility or impossibility. Keep dependent claims escalated until their exact interfaces have written proofs and published prerequisites.
