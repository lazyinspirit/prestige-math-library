## Unresolved supplier interfaces and affected claims

The claims remain in the carriers with explicit assumptions; no absent proof excerpt or acceptance is asserted.

1. **Fractional weak Green/conormal identity.** The published fractional face-trace lemma on this page proves a unique $H^r(K)\to L^2(F)$ trace for $r>1/2$. It does not identify that trace with the weak conormal trace in the elementwise Green identity. The missing exact supplier is currently a page/interface, not an item ID: `finite-element-meshes-and-interpolation` (NA-28). It must prove, for a matching affine simplex mesh, $u\in H^{1+r}(\Omega)$ with $r>1/2$ and $-\Delta u=f\in L^2$, the elementwise Green formula with $L^2$ normal traces and cancellation of the interior-facet fluxes. This affects [A1] of `thm-nitsche-consistency-and-energy-quasioptimality` and the Green identities used by both L2 theorems. The current authored statements are conditional on the exact identity; those three theorem items are escalated.

2. **Nitsche comparison-norm interpolation.** The exact unresolved interface is a zero-trace interpolant $I_{h,0}\zeta\in V_h\cap H_0^1$ with $\|\zeta-I_{h,0}\zeta\|_{\#,h}\le C h^s\ell_\Omega^{1-s}\|\zeta\|_{H^{1+s}}$ for $s>1/2$, including the weighted boundary normal-derivative error. The current published conforming Aubin–Nitsche supplier only assumes an H1 best-approximation bound; Juntunen–Stenberg Lemma 3.4 states a related fractional interpolation result but does not give its proof. The planned/external pages are `finite-element-meshes-and-interpolation` (NA-28) and `ceas-lemma-and-aubin-nitsche-error-estimates` (NA-29); the current plan has no exact new item ID for this Nitsche interface. This affects [A2] and the regularity-only conclusion of `thm-symmetric-nitsche-l2-error-under-dual-regularity`; the theorem is escalated and its approximation bound remains explicit.

3. **Nonsymmetric weighted adjoint flux trace.** To recover the source's half-order bound, one needs a mesh-uniform estimate $\bigl(\sum_Fh_F\|\partial_n\zeta_e\|_{L^2(F)}^2\bigr)^{1/2}\le C h^{1/2}\ell_\Omega^{1/2}\|\zeta_e\|_{H^{1+s}}$ for $s>1/2$, together with the fractional Green identity above. The current face trace lemma proves existence of individual traces, not this global mesh-summed estimate. The exact missing page interface is again `finite-element-meshes-and-interpolation` (NA-28); no exact item ID is yet declared. This affects [A1] and the half-order conclusion of `thm-nonsymmetric-nitsche-l2-error-with-boundary-defect`; that theorem and its dependent B example are escalated.

The minimal proposed local suppliers are (i) a fractional H(div)/Green identity on affine simplex facets under the exact $H^{1+r}$ and $L^2$ Laplacian hypotheses, (ii) a zero-trace Nitsche comparison-norm adjoint interpolation theorem, and (iii) its mesh-summed weighted flux-trace estimate.
