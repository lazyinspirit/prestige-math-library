Added the complete two-colored recursion and its finite-label vanishing requirement, then proved the canonical condition locally: `x=y=-2cos(π/m)` gives `p_j=q_j=(-1)^(j−1)sin(jπ/m)/sin(π/m)` by the addition formulas; the denominator is nonzero and the numerator vanishes at `j=m`. Infinite labels impose no condition. Added the three exact local trigonometric suppliers. Replaced the ambiguous collection of subspaces “fixed by some element” by the collection of **full** fixed spaces `Fix(w)` having codimension one; otherwise the identity would make every hyperplane eligible. Preserved the source-reported scope remarks about Soergel theory without treating them as a newly proved existence theorem. Added the new derivation to the proof contract. Precheck requested moving the independent new step from 2.4 to 1.5; that suggestion was adopted without dropping its mathematics, and the contract locator was updated.
