## Design, plan, and source conflicts (recorded as required)

1. **Rank-two interval count — design phrase versus sources (resolved).** The
   design's RL-6 rows say `lem-bruhat-rank-two-intervals-are-diamonds` has "two
   saturated paths" and that `prop-the-bgg-differential-squares-to-zero` covers
   "including the absent-path cases". The fetched sources state the count as
   "zero or two" (Zhou, Columbia thesis, Lemma (10.3,10.4), p. 10; this lane
   read the sentence verbatim: `there are either zero or two vertices that fit
   in arrows between`) and conditionally (Hemelsoet–Voorhaar Prop. 2.2, p. 3:
   if a middle exists, there are exactly two). The manifest states the sharp
   form **exactly two** and the item now records a complete route: (i) existence
   is elementary from the subword characterisation of Bruhat order plus parity
   of word length (a reduced subword for y omits exactly two positions; deleting
   only one gives a length-(n-1) word whose product lies between y and x and has
   length n-1 because n-2 and n have the same parity); (ii) the published BGG
   count supplies the upper bound (exactly two whenever one exists). Hence the
   "absent-path" branch is empty; the design phrase is stale. Residual caveat:

## Mathematical verification, corrections and residual uncertainty

- **Rank-two diamonds.** Beyond the source statements, the scaffolder ran
  exhaustive reflection-chain computations: all rank-two intervals have exactly
  two intermediate elements in A4/S4, A5/S5, dihedral I₂(m) for m = 3..8, B3
  (48 elements, 9 reflections), B4 (384, 16) and D4 (192, 12); a free-product
  Coxeter sanity check (three involutions, words to length 5) also found only
  diamonds. An earlier internal D4 run used a wrong extra generator (the
  π-rotation `diag(1,1,-1,-1)` instead of the reflection `s_{ε₃+ε₄}`); after
  correcting the reflection set the D4 check passes, so the recorded
  computation is the corrected one.
- **Statement correction history.** The first Step-1 record for
  `lem-bruhat-rank-two-intervals-are-diamonds` (sha `7c465baf…`) asserted the
  zero case was impossible for a graded interval. That justification was not
  sound (gradedness alone gives at least one middle, not exactly two, and the
  sources say 0-or-2). The item's statement and route were rewritten to the
  two-part proof above and the record was reissued (current sha
  `f38890557ecbc23d86148a7bf59a8c84049af192d8524cf85abb99f2867bb7de`). Fourteen
  downstream records were reissued unchanged after the hash change, so all 35
  records are current.
- **`cex-unsigned-bruhat-edge-sums-need-not-square-to-zero`** (provenance
  `ai-generated`, `generation.role: counterexample`, no dependency may cite it):
  the scaffold's coefficient was corrected from 2 to 4. In A2 there are exactly
  four length-three paths `w₀ ⊳ m ⊳ n ⊳ e`; each composite equals the common
  inclusion `ι_{w₀→e}` by path-independence in the fixed ambient `M(λ)`, so the
  unsigned `(w₀,e)`-entry is `4ι_{w₀→e} ≠ 0`. Record reissued (sha
  `13127266089d71a2b79c8d63d67951f0005e7bb286047f8d049b899292775416`).
- **Residual uncertainty — BGG §11 induction (main).** The upper bound "0 or 2
  / if one exists then exactly two" is quoted by Zhou (p. 10) and
  Hemelsoet–Voorhaar (Prop. 2.2) from BGG, with the proof deferred to BGG
  section 11. This scaffold did not independently reconstruct that induction.
  The elementary existence half is complete and recorded in the item; the
  author should reproduce the induction from one of the fetched treatments
  during authoring. This is the single most important item-side risk remaining.
- **Residual uncertainty — compatible signs.** Three published treatments were
  located (`lem-compatible-signs-exist-on-the-bruhat-graph`): Zhou p. 10 with
  footnote p. 17, Rocha-Caridi Lemma 10.4 pp. 354–355 (real-form normalisation
  through real structure constants; the published
  `lem-chevalley-basis-and-real-structure-constants` is a declared dependency),
  and Hemelsoet–Voorhaar Sec. 4.2 pp. 8–9. The scaffolder did **not**
  independently reproduce a closed-form reflection-ordering signing; the item's
  route records both published routes and the author must reproduce one of them.
- All proof-bearing items declare the Axiom of Choice exactly where their proof
  or construction invokes it; the combinatorial/definitional items do not. A
  recursive audit of the 142-item transitive closure of the batch's external
  dependencies found no path to `deferred-set-theory-beyond-choice`.
