The proposed directed splice and loop deletion do not establish that collider activity and distinct-vertex path simplicity are preserved. 2. `thm-dag-factorization-implies-d-separation-conditional-independence`, step 3.1: the proposed component kernels omit likelihood contributions from the kernels indexed by conditioned vertices in `C`. Such kernels can depend on parent coordinates in a moral component; fixing `x_C` does not make them disappear or turn the remaining component laws into normalized probability kernels. The claimed conditional law `Q` therefore has not been derived for the stated arbitrary measurable spaces. Needed supplier/repair: a complete measure-kernel separator factorization proof retaining the `C`-node factors, or a different local factorization/elimination proof that proves the conditional-product identity without an unavailable regular conditional kernel. This affects `lem-backdoor-criterion-implies-counterfactual-exchangeability` and both adjustment theorems.

In addition, its global-Markov supplier is unresolved under item 2. Needed supplier/repair: a finite parallel-world graph d-separation lemma with an explicit path correspondence, including exogenous-root switches, or another complete proof of the response-family conditional-independence interface. This affects `thm-backdoor-adjustment-sufficiency`.
4. `lem-frontdoor-interventional-identities` and `thm-frontdoor-adjustment-sufficiency`: the helper uses disjoint exogenous-coordinate sets and a conditional global-Markov argument, so the continuous-mediator diagonal evaluation is not used as a premise.
