The general smooth-projective duality route is projective-space Laurent Čech/residue duality → finite twisted locally free resolutions → coherent Ext duality → regular immersion/Koszul Ext collapse → adjunction and the trace. In the resolution lemma, a final syzygy that is merely locally free would **not** meet the stated direct-sum-of-twists claim. Its strategy instead passes to a finite graded polynomial module, uses the published regular-local projective-dimension bound at the homogeneous maximal ideal, and proves the final graded syzygy is graded free by graded Nakayama and localization; sheafification then yields actual sums of twists. The required graded-module realization still belongs to the unbuilt Proj supplier. The trace definition is a **datum**; its existence and embedding independence are deferred to `thm-serre-duality-smooth-projective-variety-locally-free-sheaves`, avoiding circular proof. Stacks *Duality for Schemes* §27 proves a stronger result by general Grothendieck duality, which is not a local prerequisite; its theorem was not substituted for the proposed projective-space argument. Vakil's Classes 53–54 explicitly leaves connecting-morphism compatibility and the determinant-of-differentials step as exercises/omissions, so those are local proof obligations, not silently cited facts. The zero-dimensional projective case is handled by `P⁰=Spec k` and ordinary multiplication.

On the flag side, published `thm-quotient-manifold-by-a-closed-lie-subgroup` constructs a smooth real manifold quotient and uses countable choice. It does **not** construct an algebraic quotient, a principal B torsor, root subgroups, Bruhat cells, or an algebraic projective embedding. The published Lie-algebra/root-system items establish abstract weights and Weyl combinatorics but not the passage to a complex semisimple algebraic group. Brion's inspected proof develops the `GL_n` flag model and says the results extend; that sentence is not a proof of the arbitrary-G algebraic-group bridge. Milne's inspected general-group arguments show the needed route but are not local library suppliers. All flag claims remain escalated until the earlier pair proposed below is approved and built.

The relative `P¹` shift uses both direct images and the Leray sequence.
