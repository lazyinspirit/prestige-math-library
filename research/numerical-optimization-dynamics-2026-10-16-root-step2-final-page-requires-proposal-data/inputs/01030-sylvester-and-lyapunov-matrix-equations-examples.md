The examples compute the separation and solution of a Sylvester equation with nearly colliding eigenvalue sums, the positive matrix solving a stable two-dimensional Lyapunov covariance balance, and a one-step low-rank ADI approximation whose residual factor is exactly zero. The covariance example checks the algebraic balance and positivity; it makes no separate stochastic-realization claim.
