These worked cases calculate a Gaussian tail-tilt contribution, compare
independent splitting allocations at equal work for a two-period queue, and
show that a cross-entropy pilot can miss a positive-probability event. The
counterexamples separate correct level marginals from independent level
samples and distinguish a zero SIR particle prefix from a zero event
probability. A deterministic path shows a finite grid can miss a crossing.
The discrete importance-sampling example has finite mean and infinite second
moment.
