## Conventions and proof refinements


- Complex inner products are linear in the first variable. Polarization uses i^k E_(x+i^k y)/4.
- R_A(z)=(A-z)^(-1). Williams uses the opposite sign. Stone's integrated jump has positive sign in this convention; Riesz contour integral uses (zI-A)^(-1). Teschl Problem 4.1 lacks the displayed normalization; it is supplied explicitly.
- Cayley U=(T-i)(T+i)^(-1), inverse T(I-U)y=i(I+U)y. Only ker(I-U)=0 is required. The deficiency extension uses D(T_V)=D(T)+{u+Vu} and action Tx+iu-iVu; its Cayley extension uses -V.
- U(t)=exp(itT), G=iT, T=-iG. Teschl's exp(-itA) convention is translated.
- Truncation convergence for unbounded integrals needs domination; products and sums have the exact restricted domains. Spectral mapping is essential range, not the literal image of arbitrary Borel functions.
- Cyclicity uses T and T*, not powers of T alone. General cyclic decomposition and arbitrary Hilbert dimension use AC; separable constructions use a supplied countable dense set. Inherited Hilbert projection/Riesz completeness costs must remain in the final axiom ledger. No ZF claim is made for unrestricted existence.
- Separable multiplicity has countable or infinite fiber dimension, not point-eigenspace dimension at nonatoms. General direct integrals are orientation only. Common scalar spectral-type support sets are asserted only for separable H.
- Strong resolvent convergence needs a special argument to pass to the adjoint resolvent; SOT does not generally commute with adjoints. Norm calculus convergence is limited to C_0(R)+constants, while strong convergence permits every bounded continuous function.
- Min–max assumes a self-adjoint operator bounded below, uses its spectral form domain and n-dimensional trial spaces. Teschl's printed trial-dimension typo is corrected. Exhaustion and empty-class conventions are explicit. No general closed-form representation/Friedrichs theorem is assumed.


Additional source reconciliation: Teschl Lemma 2.28 is Cayley closedness, Theorem 2.29 is the conjugation-real equal-deficiency criterion, Lemma 2.30 is the resolvent-difference formula for extensions, Corollary 4.13 is min–max monotonicity, and Corollary 5.4 is the dense invariant-core criterion. Final coverage uses those exact results, not the provisional mislabeled headings. Their useful claims are now explicit inline proof obligations. Lemma 6.21 includes both parameter invariance and compact C_0-calculus differences; both are retained, the latter after the resolvent-algebra lemma. The source's C_infinity notation means vanishing at infinity here. Norm-continuity iff bounded generator is separately proved in the unitary-group counterexample context.
