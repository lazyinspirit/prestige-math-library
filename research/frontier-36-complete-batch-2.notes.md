## Mathematical and dependency audit

- The field definition allows zero fibres and a noncomplete measure. Its
  countable Gram coefficients and fibrewise density yield a rational-complex
  fundamental span. For measurable sections, the norm is a countable
  supremum; least-index approximants from that span prove inner-product
  measurability and pointwise-limit closure. The quotient inner product uses
  published scalar L² Cauchy–Schwarz and the nonnegative-zero-integral
  theorem, with representative independence checked explicitly.
- Completeness uses a summable L² subsequence, countably chosen measurable
  representatives, a pointwise convergent vector series outside one
  measurable null set, and dominated convergence for its L² tail. Separability
  uses the **proved** real coding
  `thm-standard-borel-spaces-admit-bimeasurable-real-codings` and algebra
  `cor-standard-borel-spaces-have-countable-generating-and-measure-determining-algebras`,
  a finite-measure exhaustion, a finite-measure pi-lambda density argument,
  measurable fibrewise Gram–Schmidt, and published Parseval. It does not infer
  a countable algebra from the page title.
- Matrix coefficients on the rational fundamental span make the fibre norm
  measurable. Pointwise approximation of measurable sections proves the
  operator action, product and adjoint are measurable. Localizing a rational
  test vector to a positive-measure finite-measure set proves the exact
  essential-supremum operator norm; zero fibres contribute zero.
- The commutant theorem reconstructs an arbitrary commuting bounded operator
  from its images of localized normalized fundamental sections. Finite-measure
  exhaustion, characteristic multipliers and one common conull set make the
  pointwise rational-span inequality valid before extension to the fibres.
  This supplies a genuine measurability and boundedness proof. The extra
  diagonal-closure lemma then uses countably many measurable rank-one fields
  to show `D''=D`, including the nonfaithful representation caused by zero
  fibres.
- The generator lemma uses WOT metrizability on the unit ball, a countable
  dense family and its spectral projections, then encodes commuting
  projections with a ternary self-adjoint series. This avoids a binary-expansion
  endpoint ambiguity. The spectral theorem uses the published cyclic
  representation `thm-cyclic-spectral-representation`, countable cyclic
  decomposition `lem-maximal-orthogonal-family-of-cyclic-reducing-subspaces`,
  and the constructed active-coordinate part of
  `def-spectral-multiplicity-function-in-the-separable-case`. RN densities and
  measurable rank enumeration give the model. WOT closure and bounded
  continuous approximation give the full diagonal algebra. Uniqueness for a
  **fixed generator** uses its spectral projection measure class, puts both
  model fields into one pointwise direct-sum field, and applies the new
  general-field commutant theorem to an off-diagonal block to obtain pointwise
  unitary intertwiners;
  it does not assume uniqueness for unrelated coordinates.
- The B examples check the zero-dimensional constant field by padding an
  empty basis with zero sections, prove multiplicity two with two cyclic
  copies and noncommuting matrix units, and compute the two-by-two field's
  norm, adjoint, square and nonscalar action directly.

## Published proof-gap candidate for the canonical ledger

`lem-unitary-intertwiners-preserve-direct-integral-fiber-dimension` is
**published**. In its proof step 4.1, it passes from the scalar coordinate
intertwiners `V_sr=M_g` to an infinite fibrewise matrix and asserts that
`V*V=VV*=I` forces both pointwise unitary identities. The written step does
not establish convergence of the infinite matrix action or a single conull
set on which all countably many coordinate identities hold. This is a
potential proof gap, not a counterexample to its claim. The published
`def-spectral-multiplicity-function-in-the-separable-case` cites that lemma
for its uniqueness clause; this scaffold uses only the definition's explicit
construction and proves uniqueness separately. Neither item is used here as
an unchecked uniqueness supplier, so this candidate does not block the new
FA pair. A repair should add a countable localization/Parseval argument to
the published lemma. Alternatively, after the planned
`thm-decomposable-operators-are-the-commutant-of-diagonal-multiplication`
is authored, reviewed and published (it is currently **scaffolded, not
published**), the lemma can derive a fibrewise unitary field from that
theorem and then take dimensions. The definition's uniqueness citation
should be rechecked with that repair. No published file was edited here.
