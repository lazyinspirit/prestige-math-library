## Deep mathematical obligations within the 17 designed A items

Rows 2–5 require an explicit scheme-level construction of `P^n`, `O(d)`, affine quasi-coherent Čech acyclicity and the Laurent-monomial cohomology calculation. A perfect pairing on twists then uses the residue coefficient in `H^n(P^n,O(-n-1))`. The finite twisted locally free resolution of **every coherent sheaf** on `P^n_C` additionally needs Serre eventual global generation and a finite graded free-resolution/Hilbert-syzygy argument; no published item supplies either whole interface. Keep the base field/regularity hypothesis explicit, since the same assertion over a singular base is not automatic.

Rows 1, 6 and 7 need more than the conormal determinant formula. For a smooth closed embedding `i:X→P^N` of codimension `c`, prove that `i` is a regular immersion, compute the Koszul local Ext sheaves (`Ext^q_{P^N}(i_*E,ω_{P^N})=0` for `q≠c` and `Ext^c≅i_*(E^∨⊗ω_X)`), and pass from local to global Ext. Then projective-space coherent duality yields `H^j(X,E)^∨≅H^{dim X-j}(X,E^∨⊗ω_X)`. Construct the trace from that argument rather than assuming the desired normalized trace in row 1. Stacks *Duality for Schemes* §27, Lemma 27.1(5),(7), Lemma 27.4 and Remark 27.6 state the general result, but Lemma 27.1's proof uses the preceding `f^!` right-adjoint and dualizing-complex theory. Those prerequisites are absent from the selected/published library, so citing §27 is source support, not a local proof.

Rows 8–16 require an **algebraic** connected simply connected semisimple group and Borel/torus, existence and projectivity of `G/B`, Bruhat cells for arbitrary type, root-subgroup/Levi `SL_2` reduction, the algebraic principal `B`-bundle and associated character line bundles, and `P_α/B≅P^1`. The published Lie-subgroup page proves smooth manifold quotients and smooth principal bundles; it does not prove these algebraic claims. Brion, *Lectures on the Geometry of Flag Varieties*, PDF p. 2 expressly presents the results for `GL_n(C)` and leaves extension to arbitrary reductive groups to algebraic-group structure theory; §2.1 likewise cites another source for arbitrary reductive Bruhat relations. Lurie, *A Proof of the Borel–Weil–Bott Theorem*, pp. 1–3, states the general reductive case but assumes the quotient, Bruhat big cell, rank-one Levi reduction, minimal parabolic projection and family descent. Restricting rows 8–16 to type A would fail the downstream RL-9 general-semisimple Borel–Weil–Bott contract.

Row 17's “declared Leray supplier” is not the batch-7 Leray acyclic-cover theorem. The published abstract Grothendieck spectral sequence can be instantiated only after defining derived `f_*`, proving its injective/acyclic hypotheses, and computing relative direct images of degree-`m` line bundles on the `P^1` bundle with the required base-change/descent. Fibrewise Serre duality by itself gives a dual vector space, not the natural family-level shift; Lurie pp. 2–3 adds `SL_2`-equivariant self-duality and descent.

With the fixed convention `L_λ=G×_B C_{-λ}`, `m=⟨λ,α^∨⟩`, and `π:G/B→G/P_α`, the signed formula to prove is `ω_π≅L_{-α}` and, for `m≥−1`, `H^i(G/B,L_λ)≅H^{i+1}(G/B,L_{λ-(m+1)α})=H^{i+1}(G/B,L_{s_α·λ})`. For `m=-1`, both sides vanish. This uses `⟨ρ,α^∨⟩=1`; reversing the relative-canonical sign breaks the dot-action shift.
