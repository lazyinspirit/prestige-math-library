**Objective.** This companion page works the smallest oriented computation of
the A page's duality theory. On the complex line $\mathbb C$, regarded as a
smooth complex $1$-fold, the complex orientation computes the dualizing object
$\omega_{\mathbb C}=k_{\mathbb C}[2]$ from the single positive generator of the
compact-support cochains of a positively oriented disk. The closed inclusion
$i:\{0\}\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$ then exposes the two closed-support shifts:
the exceptional inverse image $i^!k_{\mathbb C}=k[-2]$ is computed both through
the relative cohomology of the punctured disk and through the duality identity
$i^!\simeq D_{\{0\}}i^*D_{\mathbb C}$, and the shift $[2]$ applied to
$\omega_{\mathbb C}$ gives $i^!\omega_{\mathbb C}=k$, consistently with
dualizing transitivity. The example consumes the A page's AC-assumed Verdier
supplier, while the displayed relative-cellular computation itself is
choice-free.
