**Objective.** This companion page works the degenerate Ariki categorification
theorem of [[the-degenerate-ariki-categorification-theorem]] on explicit small
windows. The decomposition example computes the full transition and projection
dictionary on the two-factor Fock space with $I=\{0\}$, including the inverse
chart, the projected simple vector and the height-two block decomposition. The
projective/simple example contrasts composition multiplicities with the Cartan
Hom pairing in the same block, verifies cover self-duality through the symmetric
trace, and exhibits the index-two ground-generated integral lattice inside the
full costandard lattice. The charge-reversal example displays the complete
height-at-most-two window for $I=\mathbb Z$: the eight ambient monomials, the
genuine standard and reverse-standard labels, the height-two algebra
$M_2(\mathbb C)\oplus B_+\oplus B_-$, the projected half-sums, the sign action
and the full tables of Young and signed Young images. The counterexample
records why the degenerate theorem may not be transported to nondegenerate Hecke
algebras at roots of unity, using the level-one specialization at $\xi=-1$
against the semisimple degenerate quotient. Every item here is a dependency
leaf: it consumes the parent page and its established prerequisites, and nothing
outside this pair depends on anything homed here. No equality of a supported
cut with the whole cyclotomic algebra, and no unprojected monomial used as a
simple class, appears anywhere on this page.
