**Objective.** This companion page works the smallest geometric instance of the
semisimplicity theorem of
[[geometric-fixed-parts-and-gauss-manin-semisimplicity]]: the finite étale
cyclic cover $z\mapsto z^r$ of the punctured line. The example exhibits the
rank-$r$ weight-zero local system $R^0f_*\mathbb Q$, its sheet-permuting
monodromy and its explicit decomposition over $\mathbb C$ into distinct
root-of-unity characters, and it shows why the geometric semisimplicity theorem
yields the conclusion over coefficient fields that do not contain the relevant
roots of unity. It is a leaf page: it consumes only its companion A page, and no
other page depends on it.
