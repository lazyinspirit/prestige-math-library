**Objective.** This companion page checks the decomposition statement of
[[thm-projective-pushforward-of-smooth-constant-sheaves-has-semisimple-perverse-cohomology]]
on the smallest projective family: the constant map
$f:\mathbb P^n\to\mathrm{pt}$. On a point the middle-perverse heart is the
category of finite-dimensional vector spaces in ordinary degree zero, and the
cellular decomposition of $\mathbb P^n$ with one cell in each dimension
$0,2,\ldots,2n$ computes $H^{2j}(\mathbb P^n;k)=k$ and no other cohomology, so
the perverse splitting of $Rf_*k[n]$ reads
$Rf_*k[n]\cong\bigoplus_{j=0}^n k[n-2j]$, with one copy of $k$ in each degree
$-n,-n+2,\ldots,n$ and the case $n=0$ giving the single summand $k$.
