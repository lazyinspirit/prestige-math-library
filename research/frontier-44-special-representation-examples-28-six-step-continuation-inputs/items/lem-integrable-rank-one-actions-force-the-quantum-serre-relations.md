## Statement

Work over $\mathbb Q(q)$ with a symmetrizable Cartan datum of type $A$ as in
[[def-cyclic-affine-type-a-cartan-datum]], put $q_i=q^{d_i}$ and let
$B$ be a unital $\mathbb Q(q)$-algebra equipped with elements
$E_i,F_i,K_h^{\pm1}$ ($i\in I$, $h\in P^\vee$) satisfying the toral and
toral-action relations and the mixed commutators
$E_iF_j-F_jE_i=\delta_{ij}(K_i-K_i^{-1})/(q_i-q_i^{-1})$ of
[[def-drinfeld-jimbo-quantized-enveloping-algebra]]; assume that every element of
every $B$-module $W$ on which the toral elements act diagonally generates a
finite-dimensional $\{E_i,F_i,K_i^{\pm1}\}$-stable subspace for each $i$
(rank-one integrability). Then the negative quantum Serre sums

$$\sum_{r=0}^{1-a_{ij}}(-1)^r\binom{1-a_{ij}}{r}_iF_i^{1-a_{ij}-r}F_jF_i^r=0\qquad(i\ne j)$$

vanish as operators on $W$, and, by the Chevalley interchange
$E_i\leftrightarrow F_i$, $K_h\leftrightarrow K_{-h}$, so do the positive sums
$\sum_{r}(-1)^r\binom{1-a_{ij}}{r}_iE_i^{1-a_{ij}-r}E_jE_i^r$. In particular the
$e=2$ cubic sums with $a_{01}=a_{10}=-2$ hold. No Serre relation is assumed.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A symmetrizable Cartan datum whose symmetrizer is $D=\mathrm{diag}(1,\dots,1)$ with $q_i=q$, a $\mathbb Q(q)$-algebra $B$ with elements $E_i,F_i,K_h^{\pm1}$ satisfying the toral, toral-action and mixed relations, and the rank-one integrability hypothesis on every weight module $W$.

[F1] $K_iF_jK_i^{-1}=q^{-a_{ij}}F_j$ and $K_iE_jK_i^{-1}=q^{a_{ij}}E_j$; hence $F_j$ has weight $m:=-a_{ij}\ge0$ under conjugation by $K_i$, and $[E_i,F_j]=0$ for $i\ne j$ ([[def-drinfeld-jimbo-quantized-enveloping-algebra]], [[def-cyclic-affine-type-a-cartan-datum]]).

[F2] In the rank-one subalgebra generated by $E_i,F_i,K_i^{\pm1}$ the commutator formula $E_iF_i^{(b)}=F_i^{(b)}E_i+F_i^{(b-1)}(q_i^{b}-q_i^{-b})^{-1}\sum_{s=0}^{b-1}(q_i^{-2s}K_i-q_i^{2s}K_i^{-1})$ holds, together with the explicit string modules $L_i(N)$ of dimension $N+1$ on which $E_i,F_i$ are injective/surjective string maps ([[thm-quantized-sl-two-string-formulas]]).

[F3] Every finite-dimensional integrable $U_i$-module is a direct sum of the string modules $L_i(N)$; equivalently, every element of such a module is a sum of highest and lowest string vectors, and the $i$-string decomposition is unique ([[thm-unique-kashiwara-i-string-decomposition]]).

[F4] $(x+y)^N=\sum_{r=0}^Nq^{r(N-r)}\binom{N}{r}_i x^ry^{N-r}$ whenever $yx=q^2xy$, and the Gaussian binomials satisfy the Pascal recurrences $\binom{n}{r}_i=q^{-r}\binom{n-1}{r}_i+q^{n-r}\binom{n-1}{r-1}_i$ ([[lem-q-binomial-expansion-for-q-commuting-elements]], [[lem-quantum-pascal-recurrence-and-gaussian-integrality]]).

[F5] The assignment $E_i\mapsto F_i$, $F_i\mapsto E_i$, $K_h\mapsto K_{-h}$ preserves the toral, toral-action and mixed relations and interchanges the two Serre families, so a proof of one family gives the other ([[def-drinfeld-jimbo-quantized-enveloping-algebra]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** compute the iterated adjoint action of $F_i$ on $F_j$ by the Gaussian binomial formula, show it vanishes beyond the rank-one string bound, and read the Serre sum off the surviving coefficient.

1.1 The adjoint action of $F_i$ on operators is $\operatorname{ad}(T)=F_iT-K_iTK_i^{-1}F_i$, and on $T=F_j$ one has $\operatorname{ad}^n(F_j)=\sum_{r=0}^{n}(-1)^rq^{r(m-n+1)}\binom{n}{r}_iF_i^{n-r}F_jF_i^r$ for every $n\ge0$, where $m=-a_{ij}$ is the $K_i$-weight of $F_j$ from [F1]. The case $n=0$ is the definition; the induction step multiplies the formula for $n$ on the left by $F_i$ and conjugates the rightmost factor, using $K_iF_jK_i^{-1}=q^mF_j$ and $K_iF_i^rK_i^{-1}=q^{-2r}F_i^r$ to move the toral elements, and the coefficient identity $\binom{n+1}{r}_i=q^{r}\binom{n}{r}_i+q^{n+1-r}\binom{n}{r-1}_i$ of [F4] (with $q$ replaced by $q_i=q$); the powers of $q$ combine to $q^{r(m-n)}$. [F1, F4, given, algebra]

1.2 Vanishing beyond the string bound. Let $w\in W$ be any vector and let $W_i\subseteq W$ be the finite-dimensional $\{E_i,F_i,K_i^{\pm1}\}$-stable subspace generated by $w$, which exists by the rank-one integrability hypothesis; enlarge it if necessary to a finite-dimensional integrable $U_i$-submodule containing the vectors $\{F_jw,F_iF_jw,\dots\}$ that occur, using the commutation formula of [F2] to reorder words $F_i^aE_i^bK_i^c$ and the finiteness of the nilpotence exponents on the finitely many weight spaces. On this finite-dimensional integrable module the operator $T=F_j$ is a weight vector of weight $m$ for the adjoint action, and $\operatorname{ad}(E_i)(T)=E_iT-K_iTK_i^{-1}E_i$: conjugating with $K_i$ gives $K_i(E_iF_j-F_jE_i)K_i^{-1}=[E_i,F_j]=0$ for $i\ne j$ by [F1], so $T$ is a highest weight vector of weight $m$ for the adjoint $U_i$-action. By the finite rank-one string decomposition [F3] applied to the finite-dimensional module generated by $T$, the string through a highest vector of weight $m$ has length at most $m+1$, so $\operatorname{ad}^{m+1}(F_j)=0$ on $W_i$. [F1, F2, F3, given, algebra]

2.1 The negative Serre sum vanishes. Put $n:=m+1=1-a_{ij}$. At $n=m+1$ the exponent in the expansion of step 1.1 is $r(m-n+1)=0$, so step 1.2 gives $0=\operatorname{ad}^{n}(F_j)=\sum_{r=0}^{n}(-1)^r\binom{n}{r}_iF_i^{n-r}F_jF_i^r$; as an operator identity on $W_i$ and hence on every vector $w\in W$ this is exactly the negative Serre sum. [step 1.1, step 1.2, given, algebra]

3.1 The positive Serre sum vanishes. The Chevalley interchange of [F5] preserves all toral, toral-action and mixed relations used above and exchanges the roles of the $E$'s and $F$'s, so applying the argument of steps 1.1--2.1 to the image operators gives $\sum_{r=0}^{1-a_{ij}}(-1)^r\binom{1-a_{ij}}{r}_iE_i^{1-a_{ij}-r}E_jE_i^r=0$ on every weight vector, hence as operators. [step 2.1, F5, given, algebra]

4.1 Conclusion. Under only the toral, toral-action, mixed-commutator and rank-one-integrability hypotheses, both quantum Serre sums of order $1-a_{ij}$ vanish, for every pair $i\ne j$ of the cyclic affine type $A$ datum, the cubic case included; no Serre relation was assumed. [step 2.1, step 3.1] ∎
