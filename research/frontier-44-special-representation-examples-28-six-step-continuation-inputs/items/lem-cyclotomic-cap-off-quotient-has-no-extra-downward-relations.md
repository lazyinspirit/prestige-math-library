## Statement

Let $\Lambda$ be dominant, $\lambda_j=\langle h_j,\Lambda\rangle$, and let
$\mathcal P_i$ be [[def-minimal-parabolic-klr-diagram-proof-category]], with
its homogeneous polynomial realization and PBW/bubble normal form. Fix monic
$a_j(u)=u^{\lambda_j}+\sum_{r<\lambda_j}c_{j,r}u^r$; for the standard graded
cyclotomic quotient take $a_j(u)=u^{\lambda_j}$ as in
[[def-cyclotomic-klr-quotient-at-a-dominant-weight]]. More generally first take
universal graded coefficients of degree $2d_j(\lambda_j-r)$; arbitrary monic
specializations over the field are interpreted as ungraded.

In the representable category $\mathcal P_i(\Lambda)$, let $J$ be the ideal
generated by $1_{H_iv_\Lambda}$ and $a_j(x)1_{G_jv_\Lambda}$ for every $j$,
closed under arbitrary vertical pre/postcomposition and horizontal whiskering
by the allowed $1$-morphisms. For downward words $\mu,\nu$ of the same weight
$\beta$, include their ordinary KLR corner by the PBW/bubble normal form. Then
$$J\cap e(\nu)R(\beta)e(\mu)=e(\nu)I_a(\beta)e(\mu),\qquad I_a(\beta)=\sum_jR(\beta)a_j(x_1)e(j,\beta-\alpha_j)R(\beta).$$
The vacuum bubble specialization is forced by these generators:
$A_\Lambda(t)=1+c_{i,\lambda_i-1}t+\cdots+c_{i,0}t^{\lambda_i}$ and
$\mathsf H_\Lambda=A_\Lambda^{-1}$. Consequently the map from the ordinary
monic cyclotomic corner to the downward-word Hom corner of
$\mathcal P_i(\Lambda)/J$ is an isomorphism, with composition corresponding to
multiplication. This holds over every degree-zero field for the entire
admissible homogeneous $Q$ family, without finite-type or characteristic-zero
restrictions. Inhomogeneous monic specializations assert the ungraded corner
equality, not a graded quantum-action conclusion.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A dominant $\Lambda$, the formal category $\mathcal P_i$ of
[[def-minimal-parabolic-klr-diagram-proof-category]] with its model and normal
form, and the monic polynomials $a_j$ of the Statement.

[F1] The formal relations of $\mathcal P_i$ — the two triangles, the rho
inverse blocks, mixed dot compatibility, the cross-and-cap identities and the
normalized bubble slides — are realized in the polynomial flag model
([[lem-minimal-parabolic-polynomial-flag-model-realizes-the-graded-diagram-relations]]).

[F2] The PBW/bubble normal form
([[lem-minimal-parabolic-diagrams-have-pbw-bubble-normal-form]]): reduced
matchings, dot monomials and independent far-left bubble monomials form a
basis; specializing the central vacuum bubble coefficients alone gives the
all-downward corner $R(\beta)$ with its ordinary polynomial/crossing part
unchanged.

[F3] The cyclotomic quotient $R^\Lambda(\beta)$ of
[[def-cyclotomic-klr-quotient-at-a-dominant-weight]] is the quotient by the
two-sided ideal generated by $a_{\nu_1}(x_1)e(\nu)$; its PBW normal form is
supplied by [[thm-klr-pbw-basis-theorem]].

## Proof

**Proof technique:** force the vacuum bubble coefficients by the rank-one
recurrence, use the normal form to see that specializing them introduces no
downward relation, then identify the quotient corner with the ordinary monic
cyclotomic corner generator by generator.

1.1 Vacuum coefficients are forced. Use the normalized trace cups and caps of [F1] and let $h_\ell$ be the bubble closed with $\lambda_i-1+\ell$ dots, with negative-dot symbols extended by the formal inversion of [F1]. The rank-one identities give $h_\ell=0$ for $\ell<0$ and $h_0=1$. Closing $a_i(x)x^{\ell-1}=0$ for $\ell\ge1$ is lawful in the defining ideal because the exponent $\ell-1$ is nonnegative even when some resulting bubble indices are negative, and gives $h_\ell+c_{i,\lambda_i-1}h_{\ell-1}+\cdots+c_{i,0}h_{\ell-\lambda_i}=0$. Hence $H(t)=\sum_{\ell\ge0}h_\ell t^\ell$ is the inverse of $a_i^{\mathrm{rev}}(t)=1+c_{i,\lambda_i-1}t+\cdots+c_{i,0}t^{\lambda_i}$, and bubble inversion gives $A(t)=H(t)^{-1}=a_i^{\mathrm{rev}}(t)$; each coefficient uses only a finite recurrence. An actual vacancy loop with $r\ge0$ dots has $A$-index $\lambda_i+1+r$ and vanishes because $A$ has degree at most $\lambda_i$, while the $A$-coefficients of index $1,\dots,\lambda_i$ are fake bubbles of negative dot count, prescribed rather than zero; for $a_i=u(u-1)$ one gets $A=1-t$, $H=(1-t)^{-1}$, so setting all positive bubbles to zero would wrongly change the deformation. If $\lambda_i=0$ then $a_i=1$ kills $F_iv$ and $A=H=1$. [F1, algebra]

2.1 Every bubble coefficient is brought to the vacuum. After bending, a coefficient may be a bubble at $\Lambda+\alpha_i$ immediately to the left of an added first $i$-strand with dot $z$; the rank-one bubble-slide equation in coefficient normalization is $A_{\Lambda+\alpha_i}(t)=(1-zt)^2A_\Lambda(t)$ and $H_{\Lambda+\alpha_i}(t)=(1-zt)^{-2}H_\Lambda(t)$. These are normalized power series with constant coefficient $1$, and for each fixed coefficient the equation is finite polynomial in $z$ and the vacuum coefficients; negative exponents of $(1-zt)$ denote formal coefficient recursion. The algebraic verification is the model computation: with total monic polynomial $p$ and existing-root factor $c_r$ the normalized series is $p^{\mathrm{rev}}/(c_r^{\mathrm{rev}})^2$, and adding the distinguished root $z$ replaces $c_r^{\mathrm{rev}}$ by $c_r^{\mathrm{rev}}(1-zt)$ and leaves $p^{\mathrm{rev}}$ unchanged; passing to the reciprocals gives the displayed pair. [step 1.1, F1, algebra]

3.1 Specializing the vacuum coefficients adds no downward relation. Let $S$ be the vacuum bubble coefficient specialization of step 1.1. By the recurrence $S$ is contained in $J$. By the tensor-product normal form [F2], specializing these central vacuum coefficients alone gives the all-downward corner $R(\beta)$ with no relation imposed in its ordinary polynomial and crossing part; this uses the normal form, not a flatness assertion. One may therefore compute in $\mathcal P_i/S$ first and then include the $E_i$ and first-strand generators, with every bubble coefficient brought to the vacuum by step 2.1. [step 2.1, F2]

4.1 The corner identification. Let $x\in J\cap e(\nu)R(\beta)e(\mu)$. Expanding $x$ in the normal form of [F2] and using steps 2.1-3.1 to substitute every bubble coefficient by its vacuum value, $x$ is a combination of the generators $1_{H_iv_\Lambda}$ and $a_j(x)1_{G_jv_\Lambda}$ whiskered and composed with downward diagrams; the whiskering of $1_{H_iv_\Lambda}$ into a downward word contributes only the first-strand $a$-terms through the triangles, while the $a_j(x)$-generators contribute exactly the ordinary two-sided ideal $I_a(\beta)=\sum_jR(\beta)a_j(x_1)e(j,\beta-\alpha_j)R(\beta)$. Conversely every element of $e(\nu)I_a(\beta)e(\mu)$ lies in $J$ by construction. Hence the intersection is exactly $e(\nu)I_a(\beta)e(\mu)$, and the same expansion shows the map from the ordinary monic cyclotomic corner $e(\nu)R(\beta)e(\mu)/e(\nu)I_a(\beta)e(\mu)$ to the corresponding Hom corner of $\mathcal P_i(\Lambda)/J$ is an isomorphism compatible with composition. [step 3.1, F2, F3]

5.1 Conclusion. Steps 1.1-4.1 prove the forced vacuum specialization, the corner equality and the isomorphism, over every degree-zero field and for the whole admissible homogeneous $Q$ family; in the inhomogeneous monic case the same argument is performed without grading and asserts only the ungraded corner equality, and no finite-type, characteristic-zero, faithfulness or generic Frobenius input is used. [step 4.1] ∎
