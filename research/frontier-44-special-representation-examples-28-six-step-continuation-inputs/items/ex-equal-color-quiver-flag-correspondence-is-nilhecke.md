## Example

Assume the Axiom of Choice as inherited from the definition and relation suppliers. Take the
one-vertex loopless quiver with no arrows, $\beta=2\alpha_i$ and the unique word $\nu=(i,i)$.
Then $E=\{0\}$, $F_\nu=X_\nu=\operatorname{Flag}(\mathbb C^2)=\mathbb P^1$, $d_\nu=1$,
$Z_{\nu,\nu}=\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1$, and the elementary correspondence $\tau_1$ acts on
the shifted polynomial summand $P_\nu=k[x_1,x_2]$ (unit in degree $-1$) by the divided
difference $$\tau_1(f)=\frac{f-s_1f}{x_1-x_2}.$$ In particular $\tau_1$ is homogeneous of degree
$-2=-a_{ii}$, and $\tau_1^2=0$, $\tau_1x_1-x_2\tau_1=1$, $\tau_1x_2-x_1\tau_1=-1$; the
one-corner convolution algebra is the rank-two nil-Hecke algebra.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** the Axiom of Choice; the one-vertex quiver with no arrows; the dimension vector
$\beta=2\alpha_i$; the unique word $\nu=(i,i)$; the incidence data of
[[def-quiver-full-flag-incidence-varieties-and-geometric-klr-generators]].

[F1] Assume AC. [[def-quiver-full-flag-incidence-varieties-and-geometric-klr-generators]]
defines $X_\nu=\{(x,F)\in E\times F_\nu:x_h(F^r_i)\subseteq F^r_j\}$, $d_\nu$, $Z_{\nu,\nu}$,
and the normalized elementary generator $\sigma_1=\tau_1=-\left[C_{\nu,\nu}\right]_{\mathrm{standard}}$
in the equal-color case, with $Q_{ii}=0$ and the equal-color mixed relations.

[F2] Assume AC. [[thm-quiver-flag-convolution-generators-satisfy-the-signed-klr-relations]]
supplies, for every finite loopless quiver and every $\beta$, the equal-color operator formula
$\tau_r(f)=(f-s_rf)/(x_r-x_{r+1})$, the degree $\deg\tau_re_\nu=-a_{ii}=-2$, the square
relation $\tau_r^2e_\nu=Q_{ii}e_\nu$ and the mixed relations $\tau_rx_r-x_{r+1}\tau_r=1$,
$\tau_rx_{r+1}-x_r\tau_r=-1$.

[A1] The Axiom of Choice is present only because the consumed definition and relation suppliers
inherit it; the explicit $\mathbb P^1$ cell and localization computations below are finite and
choice-free ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]).

## Verification

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 The data. For this quiver $E=\bigoplus_{h:i\to j}\operatorname{Hom}(V_i,V_j)=\{0\}$, so $X_\nu=F_\nu=\operatorname{Flag}(\mathbb C^2)=\mathbb P^1$ with $d_\nu=\dim_{\mathbb C}X_\nu=1$ and $Z_{\nu,\nu}=X_\nu\times_EX_\nu=\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1$. The convolution algebra is therefore the single corner $H^{\mathrm{BM},G}(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1;k)$, a free module over the polynomial ring $k[x_1,x_2]$ generated by the diagonal class and the elementary class. [F1, given]

2.1 The normalized elementary action. The relative-position-$s_1$ stratum in $\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1$ is the complement of the diagonal, and it is dense, so its closure is all of $\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1$; at the two torus-fixed points of a $\mathbb P^1$ fiber the tangent Euler classes are $x_1-x_2$ and $x_2-x_1$. Hence the standard fundamental class acts by $(s_1f-f)/(x_1-x_2)$, and the normalized class $\tau_1=-\left[C\right]_{\mathrm{standard}}$ acts by the displayed divided difference $(f-s_1f)/(x_1-x_2)$, which is polynomial because $f-s_1f$ is antisymmetric in $x_1,x_2$; this is the finite cellwise localization computation over a characteristic-zero field, using the cellular model of $\mathbb P^1$. [F1, F2, step 1.1]

3.1 The nil-Hecke identities. Direct evaluation on polynomials gives $\tau_1^2=0$ because $(f-s_1f)/(x_1-x_2)$ is symmetric in $x_1,x_2$ and hence killed by one more divided difference, and gives $\tau_1x_1-x_2\tau_1=1$ and $\tau_1x_2-x_1\tau_1=-1$; the degree is $-2=-a_{ii}$. Equivalently, the one-corner convolution algebra with the operators $x_1,x_2,\tau_1$ satisfies the nil-Hecke presentation of rank two, in agreement with [[thm-quiver-flag-convolution-generators-satisfy-the-signed-klr-relations]] in the all-equal color case. [A1, F2, step 2.1] ∎
