## Example

Work in the rank-one subalgebra $A_i$ generated by $E=E_i$, $F=F_i$,
$K^{\pm1}=K_i^{\pm1}$ inside $U_q(\mathfrak g)$
([[thm-quantized-sl-two-string-formulas]]), with $q:=q_i$ and
$[m]:=[m]_i$ ([[def-quantum-integers-factorials-and-divided-powers-at-q-i]]),
and let $x^{(a)}:=x^a/[a]!$ denote divided powers.

(a) **Divided powers multiply.**
$E^{(2)}E^{(1)}=[3]E^{(3)}$ and
$F^{(2)}F^{(2)}=\binom{4}{2}_iF^{(4)}=[4]!/\bigl([2]!\bigr)^2\,F^{(4)}$, since
$x^{(a)}x^{(b)}=\binom{a+b}{a}_ix^{(a+b)}$; numerically
$\binom{4}{2}_i=q^{-4}+q^{-2}+2+q^{2}+q^{4}$, a nonzero element of
$A=\mathbb Z[q,q^{-1}]$ because $q$ is transcendental.

(b) **The commutator is the first toral binomial.**
$E^{(1)}F^{(1)}-F^{(1)}E^{(1)}=[E,F]=\frac{K-K^{-1}}{q-q^{-1}}=T_{i,0,1}$, and
for $b\ge1$ the published reordering gives
$EF^{(b)}=F^{(b)}E+F^{(b-1)}\frac{1}{q^{b}-q^{-b}}\sum_{s=0}^{b-1}\bigl(q^{-2s}K-q^{2s}K^{-1}\bigr)$;
for $b=2$ the toral factor is
$\frac{K-K^{-1}+q^{-2}K-q^{2}K^{-1}}{q^{2}-q^{-2}}$, a combination of the
integral toral binomials $T_{i,c,t}$.

(c) **Reordering at $a=b=2$.**
$E^{(2)}F^{(2)}=F^{(2)}E^{(2)}+F^{(1)}T_{i,-2,1}E^{(1)}+T_{i,0,2}$, where
$T_{i,-2,1}=\frac{Kq^{-2}-K^{-1}q^{2}}{q-q^{-1}}$ and
$T_{i,0,2}=\frac{(K-K^{-1})(Kq^{-1}-K^{-1}q)}{(q-q^{-1})(q^{2}-q^{-2})}$; all
three toral binomials are elements of the integral form $U_A$
([[def-lusztig-divided-power-integral-form]]), and on a weight vector of weight
$\mu$ their $q=1$ values are $\mu(h_i)$, $\mu(h_i)-2$ and
$\binom{\mu(h_i)}{2}$
([[lem-q-one-toral-specialization-identifies-the-classical-cartan-generators]]).

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The rank-one algebra $A_i$ with its presentation, the divided powers
and the toral binomials.

[F1] $[a]!\ne0$ in $\mathbb Q(q)$ and $x^{(a)}x^{(b)}=\binom{a+b}{a}_ix^{(a+b)}$
for all $a,b\ge0$
([[def-quantum-integers-factorials-and-divided-powers-at-q-i]],
[[thm-quantized-sl-two-string-formulas]] (ii)).

[F2] $EF-FE=(K-K^{-1})/(q-q^{-1})$ and, for $b\ge1$,
$EF^{(b)}=F^{(b)}E+\frac{F^{(b-1)}}{q^{b}-q^{-b}}\sum_{s=0}^{b-1}(q^{-2s}K-q^{2s}K^{-1})$
([[thm-quantized-sl-two-string-formulas]] (ii)).

[F3] The general divided-power reordering identity
$E^{(a)}F^{(b)}=\sum_{t=0}^{\min(a,b)}F^{(b-t)}T_{i,2t-a-b,t}E^{(a-t)}$ with
$T_{i,c,t}=\prod_{s=1}^{t}\frac{Kq^{c-s+1}-K^{-1}q^{-c+s-1}}{q^{s}-q^{-s}}$
([[lem-divided-power-and-toral-quantum-binomial-reordering-identities]] (iii),
[[def-lusztig-divided-power-integral-form]]).

[F4] The toral binomials lie in $U_A$, commute with one another and with the
$K_h$, and on a weight vector of weight $\mu$ act by the Gaussian value
$\binom{\mu(h_i)+c}{t}_i$, whose value at $q=1$ is
$\binom{\mu(h_i)+c}{t}$
([[def-lusztig-divided-power-integral-form]],
[[lem-q-one-toral-specialization-identifies-the-classical-cartan-generators]] (b)).

[F5] $\binom{4}{2}_i=[4][3]/[2]=q^{-4}+q^{-2}+2+q^{2}+q^{4}$, a nonzero Laurent
polynomial, and $q$ is transcendental, so this element of $A$ is not zero
([[def-quantum-integers-factorials-and-divided-powers-at-q-i]]).



## Verification

**Proof technique:** expand the divided powers by definition, apply the published rank-one commutation and reordering formulas at a=b=2, and evaluate the toral binomials on weights.

1.1 Divided powers multiply. By definition $E^{(2)}E^{(1)}=\frac{E^{2}}{[2]!}\cdot E=\frac{E^{3}}{[2]!}=\frac{[3]!}{[2]!}E^{(3)}=[3]E^{(3)}$ and $F^{(2)}F^{(2)}=\frac{F^{4}}{([2]!)^{2}}=\frac{[4]!}{([2]!)^{2}}F^{(4)}=\binom{4}{2}_iF^{(4)}$, using $[a]!\ne0$ and $x^{(a)}=x^{a}/[a]!$ from [F1]; the general identity $x^{(a)}x^{(b)}=\binom{a+b}{a}_ix^{(a+b)}$ is the same computation. [F1, algebra]

1.2 The commutator and the first binomial. By [F2] and [F3], $EF-FE=\frac{K-K^{-1}}{q-q^{-1}}$, which is exactly $T_{i,0,1}$ by the definition of the toral binomial; more generally the displayed formula for $EF^{(b)}$ is [F2], and at $b=2$ the toral factor is $\frac{(K-K^{-1})+(q^{-2}K-q^{2}K^{-1})}{q^{2}-q^{-2}}$, i.e. $T_{i,0,1}+T_{i,-2,1}$ (compare the two displayed formulas of [F3] at $c=0$ and $c=-2$), an integral combination of toral binomials. [F2, F3, algebra]

1.3 Reordering at $a=b=2$. Setting $a=b=2$ in the general identity of [F3] gives $E^{(2)}F^{(2)}=\sum_{t=0}^{2}F^{(2-t)}T_{i,2t-4,t}E^{(2-t)}=F^{(2)}E^{(2)}+F^{(1)}T_{i,-2,1}E^{(1)}+T_{i,0,2}$ with $T_{i,-2,1}=\frac{Kq^{-2}-K^{-1}q^{2}}{q-q^{-1}}$ and $T_{i,0,2}=\frac{(K-K^{-1})(Kq^{-1}-K^{-1}q)}{(q-q^{-1})(q^{2}-q^{-2})}$ by the product definition. All three toral factors are the generators $T_{i,c,t}$ of the integral form, hence lie in $U_A$. [F3, algebra]

2.1 Values on weights and integrality. On a weight vector of weight $\mu$, $T_{i,0,1}$ acts by $[\mu(h_i)]_i$, $T_{i,-2,1}$ by $[\mu(h_i)-2]_i$ and $T_{i,0,2}$ by $\binom{\mu(h_i)}{2}_i$ by [F4]; at $q=1$ these are the integers $\mu(h_i)$, $\mu(h_i)-2$ and $\binom{\mu(h_i)}{2}$. The numerical value $\binom{4}{2}_i=q^{-4}+q^{-2}+2+q^{2}+q^{4}$ is nonzero in $A$ by [F5], so the product formula of step 1.1 exhibits a nonzero integral coefficient, and no division by a vanishing denominator occurs: every expression used is an element of $U_A$ or a polynomial identity valid in $\mathbb Q(q)$. No choice is used; the computation is a finite expansion in $A_i$, independently re-checked symbolically in a rank-one representation for $a=b=2$. [F3, F4, F5, step 1.1, step 1.3, algebra] ∎
