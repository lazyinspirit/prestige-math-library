## Definition

Fix $e\ge2$ and let $(I,A,D,P,P^\vee,\alpha_i,h_i,\delta,d,c,q)$ be the cyclic
affine type $A$ Cartan datum of
[[def-cyclic-affine-type-a-cartan-datum]], so $I=\mathbb Z/e\mathbb Z$ and
$q_i=q$ for all $i$. Choose once and for all the representatives
$j\in\{1,\dots,e\}$ of the residues of $I$, so that $j=e$ represents the residue
$0$; all indices $j-1$, $j+1$ below are read in this representative range.

**The derived vector module.** Put $P^\vee_{\mathrm{cl}}:=\bigoplus_{i\in I}\mathbb Z h_i$, and let $U_q'(\mathfrak g)$ denote the subalgebra of $U_q(\mathfrak g)$ generated by $E_i,F_i,K_h$ with $h\in P^\vee_{\mathrm{cl}}$. Let $V:=\bigoplus_{j=1}^{e}\mathbb Q(q)v_j$ and
define $\mathbb Q(q)$-linear operators $K_h$ ($h\in P^\vee_{\mathrm{cl}}$), $E_i$, $F_i$
($i\in I$) on $V$ by

$$K_hv_j:=q^{\langle\Lambda_j-\Lambda_{j-1},h\rangle}v_j,\qquad E_iv_j:=\delta_{j\equiv i+1}\,v_{j-1},\qquad F_iv_j:=\delta_{j\equiv i}\,v_{j+1},$$

where $v_0:=v_e$ and $v_{e+1}:=v_1$ are read cyclically, and $\equiv$ refers to
the residues of $I$. The vector $v_j$ has **classical weight**
$\lambda_j^{\mathrm{cl}}\in P_{\mathrm{cl}}$, where $\lambda_j:=\Lambda_j-\Lambda_{j-1}\in P$ is the fixed representative used in the affinization, and **residue** $j\in I$. These operators give the derived $U_q'(\mathfrak g)$-action; no degree operator $K_d$ is part of this finite action.

**Affinization.** The **affinization** $V_{\mathrm{aff}}$ is the
$\mathbb Q(q)$-vector space with basis $\{u_m\}_{m\in\mathbb Z}$, identified
with $V\otimes\mathbb Q[z,z^{-1}]$ by $u_m:=z^av_j$ whenever
$m=j-ae$ with $j\in\{1,\dots,e\}$ and $a\in\mathbb Z$; this representation of
$m$ is unique. On this space the full affine operators $K_h$ ($h\in P^\vee$), $E_i,F_i$ are

$$K_hu_m:=q^{\langle\lambda_j+a\delta,h\rangle}u_m,\qquad E_iu_m:=\delta_{m\equiv i+1}\,u_{m-1},\qquad F_iu_m:=\delta_{m\equiv i}\,u_{m+1},$$

so that in the $z$-picture
$E_i(z^av_j)=\delta_{j\equiv i+1}z^{a+\delta_{i0}}v_{j-1}$ and
$F_i(z^av_j)=\delta_{j\equiv i}z^{a-\delta_{i0}}v_{j+1}$. We write
$\mathrm{wt}(u_m):=\lambda_j+a\delta\in P$ and $l(u_m):=ae-j=-m$; the
**ordinary order** is $u_m>u_n\iff m>n$.

**Crystal data.** Put $B:=\{b_1,\dots,b_e\}$, $B_{\mathrm{aff}}:=\{u_m\}_{m\in\mathbb Z}$,
and define partial operators $f_i,e_i$ on $B_{\mathrm{aff}}$ by
$f_iu_m:=u_{m+1}$ if $m\equiv i$ and $f_iu_m:=0$ otherwise, and
$e_iu_m:=u_{m-1}$ if $m\equiv i+1$ and $e_iu_m:=0$ otherwise. Let

$$\varepsilon_i(u_m):=\delta_{m\equiv i+1},\qquad \varphi_i(u_m):=\delta_{m\equiv i},\qquad \varepsilon(u_m):=\sum_i\varepsilon_i(u_m)\Lambda_i^{\mathrm{cl}},\qquad \varphi(u_m):=\sum_i\varphi_i(u_m)\Lambda_i^{\mathrm{cl}},$$

in $P_{\mathrm{cl}}$. On the finite set $B$ the same formulas apply through
$b_j\leftrightarrow v_j$, so that $f_ib_j=b_{j+1}$ if $j\equiv i$ and $0$
otherwise, and $e_ib_j=b_{j-1}$ if $j\equiv i+1$ and $0$ otherwise.

**The opposite channel and its energy.** On $B_{\mathrm{aff}}\otimes B_{\mathrm{aff}}$
use the tensor rule $\otimes_+$

$$f_i(x\otimes y):=\begin{cases}f_ix\otimes y,&\varepsilon_i(x)\ge\varphi_i(y),\\x\otimes f_iy,&\varepsilon_i(x)<\varphi_i(y),\end{cases}\qquad e_i(x\otimes y):=\begin{cases}e_ix\otimes y,&\varepsilon_i(x)>\varphi_i(y),\\x\otimes e_iy,&\varepsilon_i(x)\le\varphi_i(y),\end{cases}$$

and the **energy function**

$$H_{\mathrm{op}}(z^ab_j\otimes z^bb_k):=\chi(j>k)+b-a,\qquad \chi(j>k):=\begin{cases}1,&j>k,\\0,&j\le k,\end{cases}$$

for $a,b\in\mathbb Z$ and $j,k\in\{1,\dots,e\}$. For a fixed **charge**
$M\in\mathbb Z$ the **ground sequence** is $b^\circ_s:=u_{M-s}$ ($s\ge0$).

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The cyclic affine type $A$ Cartan datum of [[def-cyclic-affine-type-a-cartan-datum]] with residues $I=\mathbb Z/e\mathbb Z$, $e\ge2$; the representatives $j\in\{1,\dots,e\}$; the spaces $V$ and $V_{\mathrm{aff}}$ with the displayed operators, crystal data, tensor rule and energy function; a charge $M\in\mathbb Z$ with ground sequence $b^\circ_s=u_{M-s}$.

[F1] $U_q(\mathfrak g)$ is presented by generators $E_i,F_i,K_h$ ($i\in I$, $h\in P^\vee$) with $K_0=1$, $K_hK_{h'}=K_{h+h'}$, $K_hE_iK_h^{-1}=q^{\langle\alpha_i,h\rangle}E_i$, $K_hF_iK_h^{-1}=q^{-\langle\alpha_i,h\rangle}F_i$, $E_iF_j-F_jE_i=\delta_{ij}(K_i-K_i^{-1})/(q-q^{-1})$ and the two quantum Serre sums, $q_i=q$; any assignment satisfying these relations extends uniquely to an algebra homomorphism ([[def-drinfeld-jimbo-quantized-enveloping-algebra]], [[def-positive-negative-and-toral-quantum-subalgebras]]).

[F2] The datum has $\langle\alpha_j,h_i\rangle=a_{ij}$, $\langle\alpha_i,d\rangle=\delta_{i0}$, $\langle\Lambda_j,h_i\rangle=\delta_{ij}$, $\langle\delta,h_i\rangle=0$, $\langle\Lambda_j,d\rangle=0$ and $\langle\delta,d\rangle=1$ ([[def-cyclic-affine-type-a-cartan-datum]]).

[F3] For $e\ge3$, $\alpha_i=-\Lambda_{i-1}+2\Lambda_i-\Lambda_{i+1}+\delta_{i0}\delta$ and for $e=2$, $\alpha_0=2\Lambda_0-2\Lambda_1+\delta$, $\alpha_1=-2\Lambda_0+2\Lambda_1$; in all cases $\lambda_{j-1}-\lambda_j=\alpha_{j-1}$ for $2\le j\le e$, and $\lambda_e+\delta-\lambda_1=\alpha_0$ with $\lambda_j:=\Lambda_j-\Lambda_{j-1}$ ([[def-cyclic-affine-type-a-cartan-datum]]).

[F4] The level of $\lambda\in P$ is $\langle c,\lambda\rangle$; $\lambda_j$ has level $0$ and $\Lambda_i$, as an element of $P_{\mathrm{cl}}=P/\mathbb Z\delta$, has level $1$; $q\neq q^{-1}$ in $\mathbb Q(q)$, and $q^m\ne1$ for $m\ne0$ ([[def-cyclic-affine-type-a-cartan-datum]], [[def-kac-moody-integral-and-dominant-integral-weights]]).

[F5] Writing $j(m)\in\{1,\dots,e\}$ for the representative of $m\bmod e$ and $a(m):=(j(m)-m)/e$, every $m\in\mathbb Z$ has the unique expression $m=j(m)-a(m)e$; hence $u_m=z^{a(m)}v_{j(m)}$ and the assignment $m\mapsto(j(m),a(m))$ is a bijection $\mathbb Z\to\{1,\dots,e\}\times\mathbb Z$.

[F6] For all $m\in\mathbb Z$ the identities $\langle\lambda_{j(m)},h_i\rangle=\delta_{i,j(m)}-\delta_{i,j(m)-1}$ and $\langle\lambda_{j(m)}+a(m)\delta,d\rangle=a(m)$ hold ([[def-cyclic-affine-type-a-cartan-datum]]).

## Verification

**Proof technique:** check the defining relations of the quantized affine algebra on the explicit cyclic shifts, compute the weights and crystal data, then verify the energy axioms on the opposite channel by a two-factor case analysis.

1.1 The derived operators on $V$ are well defined and give a $\mathbb Q(q)$-linear action of the generators. Each $E_i,F_i$ formula assigns to each $v_j$ either $0$ or a unique basis vector, and each $K_h$ multiplies $v_j$ by the nonzero scalar $q^{\langle\lambda_j,h\rangle}$, so $E_i,F_i,K_h$ are well-defined linear endomorphisms of the finite-dimensional space $V$; $K_0=\mathrm{id}$ and $K_{h+h'}=K_hK_{h'}$ hold because $\langle\lambda_j,\cdot\rangle$ is additive, so the toral assignment is a homomorphism $P^\vee_{\mathrm{cl}}\to\mathrm{GL}(V)$. [F1, F2, given, algebra]

1.2 The derived defining relations hold on $V$. For $h\in P^\vee_{\mathrm{cl}}$ and $j\equiv i+1$, conjugation gives $K_hE_iK_h^{-1}v_j=q^{\langle\lambda_{j-1}-\lambda_j,h\rangle}v_{j-1}$ with cyclic representatives; the weight difference is $\alpha_i$ for $j\ge2$ and $\alpha_0-\delta$ for $j=1$, and $\langle\delta,h\rangle=0$, so the coefficient is $q^{\langle\alpha_i,h\rangle}$ in both cases. Reversing the arrow gives $K_hF_iK_h^{-1}=q^{-\langle\alpha_i,h\rangle}F_i$. For distinct colors $i,t$, $E_iF_tv_j$ can be nonzero only if $j\equiv t$ and $j+1\equiv i+1$, which forces $t=i$; $F_tE_iv_j$ can be nonzero only if $j\equiv i+1$ and $j-1\equiv t$, again forcing $t=i$, so both vanish when $t\ne i$. For equal colors, $E_iF_iv_j=\delta_{j\equiv i}v_j$ and $F_iE_iv_j=\delta_{j\equiv i+1}v_j$, and $K_iv_j=q^{\delta_{i,j}-\delta_{i,j-1}}v_j$; thus $(K_i-K_i^{-1})/(q-q^{-1})$ acts by $1,-1,0$ respectively when $j\equiv i$, $j\equiv i+1$, or neither. Finally $E_i^2=F_i^2=0$ because two successive steps of one color would require $i\equiv i+1$. For $e\ge3$ and $a_{it}=0$, $E_iE_t$ requires $t=i+1$ and $E_tE_i$ requires $t=i-1$, both excluded by nonadjacency; the corresponding $F$ paths require $t=i-1$ or $t=i+1$ and also vanish. For $a_{it}=-1$, the outside terms of $E_i^2E_t-(q+q^{-1})E_iE_tE_i+E_tE_i^2$ vanish by $E_i^2=0$; its middle path would require, from right to left, residues $i+1\to i\to i-1$ and then $i-1\equiv i+1$, impossible for $e\ge3$. The middle $F_iF_tF_i$ path requires $i\to i+1\to i+2$ and then $i+2\equiv i$, also impossible. For $e=2$, $a_{it}=-2$ and every cubic Serre term $E_i^{3-r}E_tE_i^r$ ($0\le r\le3$) has at least one side exponent at least $2$, so it vanishes; the same holds for $F$. These arguments check all derived relations, including the double edge. [F1, F2, F3, algebra]

1.3 The affinized space is well defined and carries the displayed operators. By [F5] each $u_m$ is $z^{a(m)}v_{j(m)}$ for a unique pair, so $\{u_m\}$ is a $\mathbb Q(q)$-basis of $V_{\mathrm{aff}}$. The formulas $E_iu_m=\delta_{m\equiv i+1}u_{m-1}$ and $F_iu_m=\delta_{m\equiv i}u_{m+1}$ define linear endomorphisms, and they agree with the $z$-picture: if $m=j-ae$ with $j\equiv i+1$ and $j\ne1$, then $m-1=(j-1)-ae$ and $u_{m-1}=z^av_{j-1}$, while for $j=1$ one has $m=1-ae$, $m-1=e-(a+1)e$ and $u_{m-1}=z^{a+1}v_e$, matching $E_i(z^av_1)=z^{a+\delta_{i0}}v_e$ because $j=1\equiv i+1$ forces $i=0$; the $F_i$ computation is the mirror image. [F5, given, algebra]

2.1 $V_{\mathrm{aff}}$ is a full $U_q(\mathfrak g)$-module with the stated weights and integrable strings. If $E_iu_m\ne0$, then $\mathrm{wt}(u_{m-1})-\mathrm{wt}(u_m)=\alpha_i$: for $j(m)\ge2$ this is $\lambda_{j(m)-1}-\lambda_{j(m)}$; for $j(m)=1$ the new pair is $(e,a(m)+1)$, giving $\lambda_e+\delta-\lambda_1=\alpha_0$ by [F3]. Reversing these arrows gives weight difference $-\alpha_i$ for $F_i$. Hence the full toral conjugation relations hold for every $h\in P^\vee$, and the additive weight pairing gives $K_0=1$ and $K_hK_{h'}=K_{h+h'}$. The mixed and Serre computations of step 1.2 apply to the same integer residue paths on $u_m$; when a same-color mixed composition is nonzero, its two opposite steps return to $u_m$, so their coefficients and the $K_i$ commutator are unchanged. Thus [F1] gives the full module structure, and $K_du_m=q^{a(m)}u_m$ by [F6]. Each color acts on disjoint pairs $\{u_{i+ke},u_{i+ke+1}\}$ with $K_i$ eigenvalues $q,q^{-1}$ and one-step raising and lowering maps, and on singleton vectors of other residues with $K_i$ eigenvalue $1$ and both maps zero. Every basis vector therefore lies in a finite-dimensional rank-one stable space, so the module is integrable and its displayed weight decomposition has level $0$. Finally multiplication by $z$ commutes with $E_i,F_i$ and every derived $K_h$, since $\langle\delta,h\rangle=0$ there. The subspace $(z-1)V_{\mathrm{aff}}$ is consequently stable under the genuine subalgebra $U_q'(\mathfrak g)$, and its quotient, identified with $V$ by $z^av_j\mapsto v_j$, carries exactly the finite operators of step 1.2. This proves the claimed derived module action without assigning $K_d$ to finite $V$. [step 1.2, step 1.3, F1, F2, F3, F4, F6, algebra]

3.1 The crystal data of $u_m$ and $b_j$ are as displayed. By step 2.1, $F_iu_m\ne0$ exactly when $m\equiv i$, and then the image is $u_{m+1}$, so the $i$-string through $u_m$ has length $\varphi_i(u_m)=\delta_{m\equiv i}$; dually the $e_i$-string has length $\varepsilon_i(u_m)=\delta_{m\equiv i+1}$. Hence $\varepsilon(u_m)=\sum_i\delta_{m\equiv i+1}\Lambda_i^{\mathrm{cl}}=\Lambda^{\mathrm{cl}}_{j(m)-1}$ and $\varphi(u_m)=\Lambda^{\mathrm{cl}}_{j(m)}$, which lie in $P_{\mathrm{cl}}$ and have level $1$ by [F4]; the consistency identity $\varphi_i(u_m)=\varepsilon_i(u_m)+\langle h_i,\mathrm{wt}(u_m)\rangle$ holds because $\langle h_i,\mathrm{wt}(u_m)\rangle=\delta_{i,j(m)}-\delta_{i,j(m)-1}$ by [F6]. On $B$ the same computation with $\mathrm{wt}(b_j)=\lambda_j^{\mathrm{cl}}$ gives $f_ib_j=b_{j+1}$ for $j\equiv i$, $e_ib_j=b_{j-1}$ for $j\equiv i+1$, $\varepsilon(b_j)=\Lambda_{j-1}^{\mathrm{cl}}$, $\varphi(b_j)=\Lambda_j^{\mathrm{cl}}$, and the two maps $j\mapsto\Lambda_{j-1}^{\mathrm{cl}}$ and $j\mapsto\Lambda_j^{\mathrm{cl}}$ are bijections from $\{1,\dots,e\}$ onto the $e$ level-one fundamental classes $\{\Lambda_0^{\mathrm{cl}},\dots,\Lambda_{e-1}^{\mathrm{cl}}\}$. [step 2.1, F4, F6, given, algebra]

4.1 The opposite-channel tensor rule is a crystal rule on $B_{\mathrm{aff}}\otimes B_{\mathrm{aff}}$. For one fixed color write $+$ for a top vector with $(\varepsilon_i,\varphi_i)=(0,1)$, $-$ for its bottom vector with $(1,0)$, and $o$ for a singleton with $(0,0)$. The displayed rule gives exactly the strings $++\xrightarrow{f_i}+-\xrightarrow{f_i}--$, $+o\xrightarrow{f_i}-o$, and $o+\xrightarrow{f_i}o-$, with $-+$ and $oo$ isolated; the displayed $e_i$ reverses each arrow, including the ties. Thus the string lengths $(\varepsilon_i,\varphi_i)$ are respectively $(0,2),(1,1),(2,0)$ on the first string, $(0,1),(1,0)$ on the next two strings, and $(0,0)$ on the isolated pairs. These nine cases give $\varepsilon_i(x\otimes y)=\max(\varepsilon_i(x)-\langle h_i,\mathrm{wt}(y)\rangle,\varepsilon_i(y))$ and $\varphi_i(x\otimes y)=\max(\varphi_i(x),\varphi_i(y)+\langle h_i,\mathrm{wt}(x)\rangle)$, using step 3.1 on the factors. They imply $\varphi_i-\varepsilon_i=\langle h_i,\mathrm{wt}(x)+\mathrm{wt}(y)\rangle$; each arrow changes the additive weight by $-\alpha_i$ and its inverse by $\alpha_i$. This verifies the claimed rule, which is the opposite-coproduct $\otimes_+$ rule in the cited KMPY section. [step 2.1, step 3.1, given, algebra]

5.1 The energy function is constant on every connected component of the crystal graph $B_{\mathrm{aff}}\otimes B_{\mathrm{aff}}$ for the rule of step 4.1. Write $x=z^ab_j$ with $m=j-ae$ and $y=z^bb_k$ with $n=k-be$, so $H_{\mathrm{op}}(x\otimes y)=\chi(j>k)+b-a$. By step 4.1 the arrow $f_i$ acts on the first factor precisely when $\varepsilon_i(x)\ge\varphi_i(y)$ and on the second factor when $\varepsilon_i(x)<\varphi_i(y)$, and an arrow is nonzero only when the selected factor lies in the domain of $f_i$; explicitly, the first-factor case is nonzero exactly for $j\equiv i$ (whence $\varepsilon_i(x)=0$) and $k\not\equiv i$ (whence $\varphi_i(y)=0$), and the second-factor case is nonzero exactly for $k\equiv i$ and $j\not\equiv i+1$. In the first case the new pair is $(z^{a'}b_{j'},z^bb_k)$ with $j'=j+1,a'=a$ if $j\ne e$, and with $j'=1,a'=a-1$ if $j=e$ (then $i=0$): for $j\ne e$ one has $k\ne j$, so $\chi(j'>k)=\chi(j>k)$, and for $j=e$ one has $k\le e-1$, so $\chi(j'>k)-\chi(j>k)=[1>k]-1=-1$ while $a'-a=-1$, giving $\Delta H=(b-a')-(b-a)+(-1)=1-1=0$. In the second case the new pair is $(z^ab_j,z^{b'}b_{k'})$ with $k'=k+1,b'=b$ if $k\ne e$, and $k'=1,b'=b-1$ if $k=e$ (then $i=0$): for $k\ne e$ one has $j\ne k+1$, so $\chi(j>k')=\chi(j>k)$, and for $k=e$ one has $j\ne1$, so $\chi(j>1)-\chi(j>e)=1-0=1$ while $b'-b=-1$, giving $\Delta H=1-1=0$. The defining rule is reversible by $e_i$, so every edge of the graph is covered by these two cases and $H_{\mathrm{op}}$ is constant on components. [step 4.1, given, algebra]

6.1 The energy function has the two loop-shift axioms, vanishes on extremal diagonals and has the stated positivity criterion. For $x=z^ab_j$ and $y=z^bb_k$: $H_{\mathrm{op}}(zx\otimes y)=\chi(j>k)+b-(a+1)=H_{\mathrm{op}}(x\otimes y)-1$ and $H_{\mathrm{op}}(x\otimes zy)=\chi(j>k)+(b+1)-a=H_{\mathrm{op}}(x\otimes y)+1$, which are the two axioms (E1) and (E2); $H_{\mathrm{op}}(z^ab_j\otimes z^ab_j)=0$ for every $j,a$, which includes every extremal element $z^ab_j$ because every color string through any $b_j$ is a singleton or a one-arrow string: at positive label the raising map vanishes and the Weyl reflection follows the lowering arrow, at negative label the lowering map vanishes and the reflection follows the raising arrow, and at zero label both vanish; these reflections stay in $B$, and the same argument with the loop shifts stays in $B_{\mathrm{aff}}$, so the normalization (E4) holds. For $m_1=j-ae$ and $m_2=k-be$: if $j>k$ then $m_1-m_2=(j-k)+(b-a)e$ and $H_{\mathrm{op}}=\chi+ b-a$; the case $b\ge a$ gives $H_{\mathrm{op}}\ge1$ and $m_1-m_2\ge1$; the case $b<a$ gives $H_{\mathrm{op}}\le0$ and $m_1-m_2\le(j-k)-e\le e-1-e<0$. If $j\le k$ then $H_{\mathrm{op}}=b-a$, and $m_1>m_2$ forces $(b-a)e>k-j\ge0$, so $b>a$ and $H_{\mathrm{op}}>0$; conversely $b>a$ gives $H_{\mathrm{op}}>0$ and $m_1-m_2=(j-k)+(b-a)e\ge e-(k-j)\ge1$. Hence in all cases $H_{\mathrm{op}}(x\otimes y)>0\iff m_1>m_2$, and this criterion includes $e=2$. [step 3.1, step 5.1, given, algebra]

7.1 The ground sequence satisfies the two ground identities. Let $b^\circ_s=u_{M-s}$ and write $M-s=j-ae$ and $M-s-1=k-be$ with $j,k\in\{1,\dots,e\}$. If $j\ne1$ then $k=j-1$ and $b=a$; if $j=1$ then $k=e$ and $b=a+1$. In the first case $\chi(j>k)=1$ and $b-a=0$, in the second $\chi(1>e)=0$ and $b-a=1$; hence $H_{\mathrm{op}}(b^\circ_s\otimes b^\circ_{s+1})=1$ in both cases, for every $M$ and every $s\ge0$. For the $\varepsilon$-$\varphi$ identity: $b^\circ_s=u_{M-s}$ has $\varepsilon(b^\circ_s)=\Lambda^{\mathrm{cl}}_{j-1}$ and $b^\circ_{s+1}=u_{M-s-1}$ has $\varphi(b^\circ_{s+1})=\Lambda^{\mathrm{cl}}_k$, and $k\equiv M-s-1\equiv j-1$ in $\mathbb Z/e\mathbb Z$ with $k=j-1$ when $j\ne1$ and $k=e$ representing $0=j-1$ when $j=1$; hence $\varepsilon(b^\circ_s)=\varphi(b^\circ_{s+1})$. [step 3.1, step 6.1, given, algebra]

8.1 Conclusion. The derived module $V$ is a well-defined finite-dimensional $U_q'(\mathfrak g)$-module of level $0$ with basis $v_j$ of classical weights $(\Lambda_j-\Lambda_{j-1})^{\mathrm{cl}}$ and crystal operators $f_ib_j=b_{j+1}$ for $j\equiv i$, $e_ib_j=b_{j-1}$ for $j\equiv i+1$, with $\varepsilon(b_j)=\Lambda^{\mathrm{cl}}_{j-1}$, $\varphi(b_j)=\Lambda^{\mathrm{cl}}_{j}$, both of level $1$, by steps 1.1, 1.2 and 3.1; its affinization $V_{\mathrm{aff}}$ has basis $u_m=z^{a}v_j$ with $m=j-ae$, weights $\Lambda_j-\Lambda_{j-1}+a\delta$ and $K_d$ eigenvalue $q^a$, carries the integrable action $E_iu_m=\delta_{m\equiv i+1}u_{m-1}$, $F_iu_m=\delta_{m\equiv i}u_{m+1}$ by step 2.1, and has ordinary order $u_m>u_n\iff m>n$; the opposite-channel tensor rule and energy $H_{\mathrm{op}}(z^ab_j\otimes z^bb_k)=\chi(j>k)+b-a$ with $l(z^ab_j)=ae-j$ satisfy the two loop-shift axioms, constancy on components, the extremal normalization and the positivity criterion by steps 5.1 and 6.1; and the charge-$M$ ground sequence $b^\circ_s=u_{M-s}$ satisfies $\varepsilon(b^\circ_s)=\varphi(b^\circ_{s+1})$ and $H_{\mathrm{op}}(b^\circ_s\otimes b^\circ_{s+1})=1$ by step 7.1. All statements hold for every $e\ge2$. [step 2.1, step 3.1, step 5.1, step 6.1, step 7.1] ∎

## Remarks

- The residue convention is fixed once and for all: $v_j$ represents the residue
  $j\in\mathbb Z/e\mathbb Z$ with $j=e$ representing $0$, and $E_i$ raises the weight by $\alpha_i$ (it lowers $j$ by $1$ when $j\equiv i+1$), while $F_i$ lowers the weight by $\alpha_i$. In the partition model, these correspond respectively to removing and adding a node of residue $i$. This matches the node convention
  $c-r$ of the partition Fock space below.

- The energy function is defined in the channel opposite to the fixed coproduct
  of the library: its tensor rule compares $\varepsilon_i$ of the first factor
  with $\varphi_i$ of the second, and it is $H_{\mathrm{op}}$ and not $H$ that
  the normal-ordering rule uses. Passing between the two channels is the content
  of [[lem-the-q-wedge-relations-coincide-with-the-type-a-energy-braiding-kernel]].

- After the ground state is fixed, every element of the ground sequence is
  determined; an overall loop shift changes the charge and the phase of
  $b^\circ_0$, so the sequence is not determined independently of that choice.

- The finite module $V$ is a level-$0$ $U_q'(\mathfrak g)$-module whose crystal
  $\varepsilon,\varphi$ take the level-$1$ values; the level refers to the target
  of $\varepsilon,\varphi$ and not to the weights of $V$. The vector
  representation itself never carries level-$1$ weights.
