## Statement

Let $R^\Lambda$ be the cyclotomic KLR algebra of
[[def-cyclotomic-klr-quotient-at-a-dominant-weight]], and let
$E^\Lambda_i,F^\Lambda_i$ be the functors of
[[def-cyclotomic-klr-induction-and-restriction-functors]]. For every $i\in I$
and $\beta\in Q_+$:

(a) the bimodule $R^\Lambda(\beta+\alpha_i)e(\beta,i)$ is a projective right
$R^\Lambda(\beta)$-module, and $e(\beta,i)R^\Lambda(\beta+\alpha_i)$ is a
projective left $R^\Lambda(\beta)$-module;

(b) consequently $E^\Lambda_i$ and $F^\Lambda_i$ are exact and send finitely
generated graded projectives to finitely generated graded projectives;
moreover $F^\Lambda_i$ preserves all projectives and $E^\Lambda_i$ preserves
finitely generated ones;

(c) there is a functorial exact sequence in
$\mathrm{Mod}(R(\beta+\alpha_i))$
$$0\longrightarrow q^{(\alpha_i\mid 2\Lambda-\beta)}\bar F_iM\longrightarrow F_iM\longrightarrow F^\Lambda_iM\longrightarrow 0,\qquad M\in\mathrm{Mod}(R^\Lambda(\beta)),$$
where $F_i$ is the right-append and $\bar F_i$ the left-append generic
induction of
[[lem-generic-klr-induction-and-restriction-mixed-relations]], regarded on
$R^\Lambda$-modules through the quotient, and the exponent is the Cartan
pairing $(\alpha_i\mid 2\Lambda-\beta)=2(\alpha_i\mid\Lambda)
-(\alpha_i\mid\beta)$
([[def-quantum-integers-factorials-and-divided-powers-at-q-i]] records the
normalization of the powers of $q$).

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The cyclotomic quotient $R^\Lambda$ of
[[def-cyclotomic-klr-quotient-at-a-dominant-weight]]; put
$S=R(\beta+\alpha_i)$, $A=R^\Lambda(\beta)$, $e_R=e(\beta,i)$, $e_L=e(i,\beta)$.

[F1] $S e_R$ is a free right $R(\beta)$-module and $e_LS$ a free left
$R(\beta)$-module by the PBW/coset decomposition
([[thm-klr-pbw-basis-theorem]],
[[def-klr-induction-and-restriction-bimodules]]); the generic functors
$F_i,\bar F_i,E_i$ and their definitions and exactness are
[[lem-generic-klr-induction-and-restriction-mixed-relations]].

[F2] The cyclotomic ideal is the two-sided ideal generated by
$a_{\nu_1}(x_1)e(\nu)$; in the standard graded case it is homogeneous and the
quotient is graded. Multiplication by any dot $x_a$ on $R^\Lambda(\beta)$ is
killed by a monic polynomial with scalar coefficients
([[lem-cyclotomic-klr-dot-annihilators-and-finite-root-strings]]).

[F3] A finite projective module is a direct summand of a finite free module. For a central monic polynomial $f\in Z(A)[t]$ of degree $m$, polynomial division gives an $A$-linear decomposition $A[t]=fA[t]\oplus A[t]_{<m}$, with $A[t]_{<m}\cong A^m$. The resolution splitting needed below is proved explicitly in step 1.2.

## Proof

**Proof technique:** compare the cyclotomic towers over the generic ones with an
explicit monic comparison map, then use the monic-annihilator projective
splitting to deduce projectivity, exactness and the exact sequence with its
shift.

1.1 The comparison maps. By the PBW basis [F1] the right $A[t]$-modules $K_0=Se_R/Sa(x_1)R(\beta)e_R$ and $K_1=Se_L/Sa(x_2)R_1(\beta)e_L$ are finite projectives, where $t$ records the appended dot. With the source substitution $\kappa=-\tau_{\mathrm{native}}$, define $P(u)=ua(x_1)\kappa_1\cdots\kappa_n$ and $Q(v)=vg_n\cdots g_1$, where $g=\kappa$ at unequal colours and $g=(y-x)-(y-x)^2\kappa$ at equal colours. Moving each dot and crossing across the appended strand shows block bilinearity, and expanding the cyclotomic ideal along the left cosets shows $\operatorname{im}P=\ker(K_0\to R^\Lambda(\beta+\alpha_i)e_R)$. [F1, algebra]

1.2 Monic annihilators and projectivity, locally. Let $0\to P_1\xrightarrow{j}P_0\to M\to0$ be an exact sequence of right $A[t]$-modules with finite projective $P_0,P_1$, and suppose a central monic $f$ annihilates $M$. Multiplication by $f$ is injective on a free $A[t]$-module by its unit leading coefficient, hence on its projective summands. On a finite free module monic division gives an $A$-linear operator $p_{\mathrm{free}}$ taking a polynomial to its quotient by $f$, with $p_{\mathrm{free}}(xf)=x$. Choose split inclusion $u:P_1\to A[t]^N$ and projection $v:A[t]^N\to P_1$; then $p=vp_{\mathrm{free}}u$ satisfies $p(xf)=x$ on $P_1$, since $u$ is $A[t]$-linear and $vu=1$. Since $fM=0$, multiplication by $f$ on $P_0$ has image in $j(P_1)$, defining $h:P_0\to P_1$ by $jh=f$. Injectivity of $j$ gives $hj=f$ on $P_1$, so $phj=1$. Thus $j$ has the $A$-linear retraction $ph$, and $M$ is a direct summand of the $A$-projective module $P_0$ and is $A$-projective. Equivalently, $P_r/fP_r$ is a summand of $(A[t]/fA[t])^N\cong A^{mN}$ and is $A$-projective, which is the monic-quotient reason for the splitting. This argument uses finite projective presentations and division and does not assume $f$ invertible or the target $M$ projective. [F3, algebra]

2.1 The monic comparison and its quotient. The local quadratic and braid calculation gives $QP=a_i(t)\prod_{\nu_a\ne i}Q_{i\nu_a}(t,x_a)$ on each word corner of $K_1$, with a uniform unit leading coefficient. This is a polynomial endomorphism of the finite projective $A[t]$-module $K_1$; the injectivity argument of step 1.2 applies, so $QP$ and therefore $P$ are injective. Together with the image-kernel computation of step 1.1, this gives the actual projective resolution $0\to q^sK_1\xrightarrow{P}K_0\to Be_R\to0$, where $B=R^\Lambda(\beta+\alpha_i)$ and $s=(\alpha_i\mid 2\Lambda-\beta)$. The target is the cokernel $Be_R$, whereas $K_0,K_1$ are the projective comparison modules over $A[t]$. No cyclicity of $K_0$ or $K_1$ follows or is required. [step 1.1, step 1.2, algebra]

3.1 Projectivity of the cyclotomic bimodules. The appended dot on $M=Be_R$ is annihilated by a central monic scalar-coefficient polynomial by [F2]. Apply step 1.2 to the projective $A[t]$-resolution in step 2.1, with $M=Be_R$, to conclude that $Be_R$ is right $A$-projective. This module is finitely generated over $A$: its finite $A[t]$-generators together with dot powers below the degree of the annihilator generate it over $A$. In the homogeneous standard case the resolution and $M$ are graded; decompose the resulting $A$-linear splitting into homogeneous components on finite homogeneous generators, and take its degree-zero component to get a graded splitting. The generator-fixing anti-involution sends $Be_R$ to $e_RB$ and converts this right projectivity into left $A$-projectivity. The same monic argument is valid for arbitrary ungraded monic cyclotomic deformations. [F1, F2, step 1.2, step 2.1]

4.1 Exactness and preservation. Tensoring by the right $A$-projective module $Be_R$ is exact, so $F_i^\Lambda$ is exact, and idempotent restriction $E_i^\Lambda$ is exact. The left $B$-module $Be_R$ is a direct summand of $B$, hence induction sends any projective $A$-module, presented as a summand of a free module, to a projective $B$-module; finite generators remain finite under induction. A finitely generated projective $B$-module is a summand of a finite direct sum of shifts of $B$, and its restriction is consequently a summand of the corresponding finite direct sum of shifts of $e_RB$. Left $A$-projectivity and finite generation of $e_RB$ from step 3.1 therefore show that restriction preserves finitely generated projectives. These arguments apply ungraded to monic deformations as well. [step 3.1, algebra]

4.2 The exact sequence (c). Tensor the bimodule resolution $0\to q^sK_1\xrightarrow{P}K_0\to Be_R\to0$ of step 2.1 over $A$ with a left $A$-module $M$. It remains exact because its cokernel $Be_R$ is right $A$-projective by step 3.1, so the original sequence is split as a sequence of right $A$-modules. By their definitions in step 1.1, $K_1\otimes_A M$ is the left-append generic induction $\bar F_iM$ and $K_0\otimes_A M$ is the right-append generic induction $F_iM$, while $Be_R\otimes_A M=F_i^\Lambda M$. The map $P$ is left $R(\beta+\alpha_i)$-linear and has homogeneous degree $s=(\alpha_i\mid 2\Lambda-\beta)$, giving exactly the functorial sequence in (c) in the declared target category. On the ungraded monic-deformation branch the same sequence is interpreted without a grading shift. [F1, step 1.1, step 2.1, step 3.1]

5.1 Conclusion. Steps 3.1-4.2 prove (a), (b) and (c) with the displayed shift, in the graded standard case with graded projectives and verbatim ungraded for a monic deformation; the comparison is monic and no filtered-colon citation is used. [step 3.1, step 4.2] ∎
