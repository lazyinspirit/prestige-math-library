## Example

In the two-dimensional module $V(\omega)=L_1(1)$ let $v$ be highest and $w=Fv$, with $Ev=0$, $Fv=w$, $Ew=v$, $Fw=0$, $Kv=qv$, $Kw=q^{-1}w$. Then $V_A=Av\oplus Aw$, $L_0=A_0v\oplus A_0w$, $L_\infty=A_\infty v\oplus A_\infty w$ and $E_{\mathbb Z}=\mathbb Zv\oplus\mathbb Zw$. The integral globalization maps $E_{\mathbb Z}$ isomorphically onto the $\mathbb Z$-span of its two crystal classes, and its rational extension gives the full $\mathbb Q$ residue. The lower global basis is $v,w$. The field string image $FV$ is $\mathbb Q(q)w$, its integral image ideal inside $V_A$ is $Aw$, and its balanced part is $\mathbb Zw$.

On $V\otimes V$ the exact quasi-$R$ is $\Theta=1+(q-q^{-1})E\otimes F$, so
$$\Psi(vv)=vv,\quad\Psi(vw)=vw,\quad\Psi(wv)=wv+(q-q^{-1})vw,\quad\Psi(ww)=ww,$$
with concatenation for tensor notation. This preserves total weight and the $A$-tensor lattice and is a semilinear involution. The actual tensor global basis is $vv,\,vw,\,wv+qvw,\,ww$; the raw vector $wv$ is not bar fixed. Its zero crystal lattice is the $A_0$ tensor lattice, and its balanced $\mathbb Z$-part is the $\mathbb Z$-span of the corrected vectors.

For the mixed free lattice $L'=A_0(v+w)\oplus qA_0v$ with actual infinity chart $\psi(L')=A_\infty(v+w)\oplus q^{-1}A_\infty v$, the integral balanced part is only $\mathbb Z(v+w)$; it misses the valid residue class of $qv$. Thus $L'$ is genuinely nonbalanced even though $v+w$ is bar fixed, and $L'$ is not claimed to be a crystal lattice.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The rank-one module and string formulas of [[thm-quantized-sl-two-string-formulas]] and the $i$-string decomposition [[thm-unique-kashiwara-i-string-decomposition]]; the balanced triple definitions [[def-balanced-triple-and-globalization-map]], [[lem-balanced-triple-linear-algebra-over-the-projective-line]]; the integral globalization theorem and lower global basis [[thm-the-highest-weight-crystal-triple-is-balanced]], [[thm-existence-bar-invariance-and-uniqueness-of-the-lower-global-basis]], [[def-lower-global-basis-of-a-quantum-highest-weight-module]]; and the tensor bar results [[thm-the-weightwise-quasi-r-intertwiner-exists-and-is-unique]], [[thm-quasi-r-tensor-bars-on-finite-products-of-based-integrable-modules]].

[L1] Module structure: $V=L_1(1)$ has weights $1$ and $-1$ with one-dimensional weight spaces, $Fv=w$, $Ew=v$, $Ev=0=Fw$, $Kv=qv$, $Kw=q^{-1}w$ ([[thm-quantized-sl-two-string-formulas]]).

[L2] Divided powers: in this module $F^{(n)}=0$ for $n\ge2$, $Fv=w$, so the integral lattice generated by divided powers is exactly $V_A=Av\oplus Aw$ ([[thm-unique-kashiwara-i-string-decomposition]]).

[L3] Charts and balanced intersection: the crystal lattice is $L_0=A_0v\oplus A_0w$, its bar image is $L_\infty=A_\infty v\oplus A_\infty w$, and $A\cap A_0=\mathbb Z[q]$, $A\cap A_\infty=\mathbb Z[q^{-1}]$, $A\cap A_0\cap A_\infty=\mathbb Z$, with $A/qA=0$ since $q$ is a unit; the chart residues are computed through $\mathbb Z[q]/q\mathbb Z[q]=\mathbb Z$ ([[def-balanced-triple-and-globalization-map]], [[lem-balanced-triple-linear-algebra-over-the-projective-line]], [[def-the-local-ring-at-q-zero]]).

[L4] Quasi-$R$: $\Theta_\alpha=(q-q^{-1})E\otimes F$ in degree $\alpha$, and the tensor bar is $\Psi=\Theta(\psi\otimes\psi)$ with $\psi$ fixing $v,w$ and inverting $q$ ([[thm-the-weightwise-quasi-r-intertwiner-exists-and-is-unique]], [[thm-quasi-r-tensor-bars-on-finite-products-of-based-integrable-modules]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 **The module triple.** By [L1] and [L3], $V_A\cap L_0=\mathbb Z[q]v\oplus\mathbb Z[q]w$, whose quotient by $q(V_A\cap L_0)$ has $\mathbb Z$-basis the classes of $v$ and $w$; the same two vectors are exactly $E_{\mathbb Z}=\mathbb Zv\oplus\mathbb Zw$, so the integral globalization map is an isomorphism and the triple is balanced. The rational extension has basis the two classes over $\mathbb Q$. The lower global basis is $v,w$, congruent to the crystal classes. [L2, L3]

1.2 **The string image.** The field string image is $FV=\mathbb Q(q)w$; the integral image ideal is $FV\cap V_A=Aw$, whose balanced part is $Aw\cap\psi(Aw)=\mathbb Zw$. The three spaces are genuinely different, as displayed. [L1, L3]

1.3 **The tensor bar.** Applying [L4]: $E\otimes F$ kills $vv,vw,ww$ since $Ev=0$ and $Fw=0$, and sends $wv$ to $Ew\otimes Fv=v\otimes w=vw$; hence the four displayed formulas. $\Psi$ preserves total weight because $E\otimes F$ does, preserves the $A$-tensor lattice because $\Theta=1+(q-q^{-1})E\otimes F$ has $A$-coefficients, and is involutive because $(q-q^{-1})+\overline{(q-q^{-1})}=0$ and $(E\otimes F)^2=0$ on this module. [L4]

2.1 **The corrected tensor global basis.** The bar-fixed combinations are $vv,vw,ww$ and $wv+cvw$ with $c$ satisfying $c+q^{-1}(q-q^{-1})=c$, i.e. $c=q$; the transition matrix from the raw basis is unitriangular with off-diagonal $q$, so the corrected family $vv,vw,wv+qvw,ww$ is an $A$-basis congruent to the raw tensor basis modulo $q$, with zero crystal lattice the $A_0$-tensor lattice and balanced $\mathbb Z$-part its $\mathbb Z$-span; the raw $wv$ is not bar fixed. [L4, step 1.3]

2.2 **The nonbalanced mixed lattice.** For $L'=A_0(v+w)\oplus qA_0v$, an element $\alpha(v+w)+\beta v$ with $\alpha\in A_0$, $\beta\in qA_0$ lies in $V_A$ exactly when $\alpha\in\mathbb Z[q]$ and $\beta\in q\mathbb Z[q]$; lying in $\psi(L')=A_\infty(v+w)\oplus q^{-1}A_\infty v$ forces in addition $\alpha\in\mathbb Z[q^{-1}]$ and $\beta\in q^{-1}\mathbb Z[q^{-1}]$ with Laurent degree bounds, so $\alpha\in\mathbb Z$ and $\beta=0$: the balanced part is exactly $\mathbb Z(v+w)$. The integral zero residue contains the independent class of $qv$, which is not in the image of $\mathbb Z(v+w)$, so $L'$ is not balanced; the bar-fixed vector $v+w$ alone does not repair this, and since $\tilde e(v+w)=v\notin L'$ the lattice is not crystal stable either. [L3, step 1.2]

3.1 **Conclusion.** Steps 1.1–1.2 compute the rank-one balanced triple and its string-image strata; steps 1.3–2.1 compute the exact quasi-$R$ action, the corrected tensor global basis and its lattice; step 2.2 exhibits the nonbalanced mixed lattice while distinguishing $v+w$ from the undefined class of $v$. All computations are finite, and the zero divisors $A/qA=0$ are never used for residues. [step 1.2, step 2.1, step 2.2] ∎
