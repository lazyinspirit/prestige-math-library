## Definition

Let $V$ be an FI-module over $k$ ([[def-fi-category-and-fi-module]]) and let
$\Sigma$ be a set of elements, each lying in $V_S$ for some finite set $S$. The
**sub-FI-module generated by $\Sigma$**, written
$\operatorname{span}_V(\Sigma)$, is the intersection of all sub-FI-modules of
$V$ that are subfunctors and contain $\Sigma$
([[def-submodule]], [[lem-submodule-criterion-sums-and-intersections]] for the
objectwise intersection of submodules). The family is nonempty because $V$
itself qualifies, and the intersection is a sub-FI-module, since each
transition map $f_*$ preserves every member and hence their intersection;
thus $\operatorname{span}_V(\Sigma)$ is the smallest sub-FI-module of $V$
containing $\Sigma$.

Explicitly, $\operatorname{span}_V(\Sigma)_T$ is the $k$-span of all elements
$f_*(v)$ with $v\in\Sigma\cap V_S$ and $f\colon S\to T$ a morphism of $FI$: the
set of all finite $k$-linear combinations of such elements is a sub-FI-module
of $V$ containing $\Sigma$, because $g_*(f_*(v))=(g\circ f)_*(v)$, and every
sub-FI-module containing $\Sigma$ contains each $f_*(v)$; hence it equals the
intersection. Call $V$ **finitely generated** when
$\operatorname{span}_V(\Sigma)=V$ for some finite $\Sigma$, and **generated in
degree $\le d$** when $\operatorname{span}_V(V_{\le d})=V$, where
$V_{\le d}=\bigsqcup_{|S|\le d}V_S$ is the set of all elements lying in the
values of $V$ at finite sets $S$ with $|S|\le d$. The **generation degree** of
$V$ is the least $d\ge0$ such that $V$ is generated in degree $\le d$, and
$\infty$ when no such $d$ exists
([[def-generated-cyclic-finitely-generated-and-free-modules]]).

Finite generation of $V$ and pointwise finite generation of the individual
$k$-modules $V_S$ are different conditions: the pointwise property is implied
by finite generation (as shown below by
[[lem-generated-in-degree-d-is-a-quotient-of-free-fi-modules]]) but does not
imply it, and no pointwise finite generation or finite-dimensionality is built
into either notion. A degree bound on a generating set is an additional
structure on $V$, not a consequence of finite generation. No choice principle
is used: $\operatorname{span}_V(\Sigma)$ is defined by an intersection of
subfunctors, and its explicit description involves only finite $k$-linear
combinations.
