## Definition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, let $n\ge0$ and $\ell\ge1$, and let $z,U_1,\dots,U_\ell$
be units of $R$ ([[def-algebra-over-a-commutative-ring]]). The **arbitrary-level
cyclotomic Hecke algebra** $H_n=H_n(R;z;U_1,\dots,U_\ell)$ is the $R$-algebra
presented by invertible commuting elements $X_1,\dots,X_n$ and generators
$T_1,\dots,T_{n-1}$ subject to the relations
$$(T_i-z)(T_i+1)=0,\qquad T_iT_{i+1}T_i=T_{i+1}T_iT_{i+1},\qquad T_iT_j=T_jT_i\quad(|i-j|>1),$$
$$T_iX_iT_i=zX_{i+1},\qquad X_jT_i=T_iX_j\quad(j\notin\{i,i+1\}),\qquad \prod_{a=1}^{\ell}(X_1-U_a)=0 .$$
For $n=0$ set $H_0=R$. The **grading variable** of this page is $v$, not the
unit $z$: the two are identified only where a statement says so explicitly.

**Multipartitions and Murphy data.** An $\ell$-**multipartition** of $n$ is an
ordered tuple $\lambda=(\lambda^{(1)},\dots,\lambda^{(\ell)})$ of partitions
with $\sum_{b}|\lambda^{(b)}|=n$ ([[def-young-tableau-standard-tableau-and-shape]]).
Multipartitions are partially ordered by dominance, $\lambda\unrhd\mu$ meaning
that $\sum_{b\le k}|\lambda^{(b)}|+\sum_{j\le m}\lambda^{(k)}_j\ge
\sum_{b\le k}|\mu^{(b)}|+\sum_{j\le m}\mu^{(k)}_j$ for all $k,m$. Fill the
diagram $t^\lambda$ with $1,\dots,n$ in component order, then row order, then
column order. Put $a_k:=\sum_{b<k}|\lambda^{(b)}|$,
$u_\lambda:=\prod_{k=1}^{\ell}\prod_{j\le a_k}(X_j-U_k)$,
$x_\lambda:=\sum_{w\in S_\lambda}T_w$, and $m_\lambda:=x_\lambda u_\lambda$,
where $S_\lambda$ is the row subgroup: the subgroup of $S_n$ stabilizing the
rows of $t^\lambda$
([[def-finite-symmetric-group-and-permutation-notation]],
[[def-generic-type-a-hecke-algebra]]). Write $M_\lambda:=m_\lambda H_n$ for
the right $H_n$-submodule generated by $m_\lambda$
([[def-left-and-right-modules]]). The **presentation involution** $*$ of $H_n$
fixes every $X_j$ and every $T_i$ and reverses products. For standard
tableaux $s,t$ of shape $\lambda$ put $m_{st}:=T_{d(s)}^{*}m_\lambda T_{d(t)}$,
where $d(s)$ is the permutation determined by $t^\lambda d(s)=s$.
Denote by $H^{>\lambda}$ the span of the higher Murphy shapes once their
basis and cell law is established by
[[thm-arbitrary-level-cyclotomic-hecke-murphy-basis-is-cellular]], and by
$S_\lambda$ the right cell (**Specht**) module with basis $m_t$ and the
action given by that cell law; its cellular form defines
$D_\lambda:=S_\lambda/\operatorname{rad}S_\lambda$, which may vanish.

**Types.** The types used below are **exactly** all $\ell$-multipartitions of
$n$, including tuples with empty components — not all multicompositions. A
**semistandard tableau** $S$ has entries the labels $(i,k)$ (row $i$ in
component $k$) ordered by component first and then by row, weakly increasing
along rows, strictly increasing down columns, and a label of component $a$
may occur only in physical component $b\le a$
([[def-semistandard-tableau-and-kostka-number]]); the **type** of $S$ is the
multipartition counting each label with the multiplicity of the corresponding
row. For standard $s$ let $\mu(s)$ be the tableau obtained from $s$ by
replacing each entry by its row-and-component in $t^{\mu}$. Put
$$m_{(S,t)}:=\sum_{s:\,\mu(s)=S}m_{st},\qquad m_{(S,T)}:=\sum_{s,t:\,\mu(s)=S,\ \nu(t)=T}m_{st}.$$
Let
$$M:=\bigoplus_{\lambda\ \text{multipartition of}\ n}M_\lambda,\qquad C_n:=\operatorname{End}_{H_n}(M),$$
with ordinary composition ([[def-direct-sum-of-a-family-of-modules]]). For
$S,T$ of shape $\lambda$ and types $\mu,\nu$, the map $\varphi_{ST}$ — once
justified by [[lem-cyclotomic-semistandard-sums-give-schur-endomorphism-basis]] —
sends $m_\alpha h$ to $\delta_{\alpha,\nu}m_{(S,T)}h$. Its right cell module
$\Delta_\lambda$ has basis $v_T$ over the semistandard $T$ of multipartition
types, with contravariant cell form and quotient $L_\lambda$. Let $T^\lambda$
be the unique tableau of shape and type $\lambda$, and let $e_\lambda$ be the
identity projection onto $M_\lambda$. Put $\omega:=(\varnothing,\dots,\varnothing,(1^n))$
and $e:=e_\omega$; then $m_\omega=1$, so $M_\omega=H_n$, and $H_n$ is an
actual direct summand of $M$. The weight projections used by the Jantzen
theory below are the $e_\nu$ with $\nu$ a multipartition. Write
$K_{(\lambda,\nu)}:=\#\operatorname{SStd}_\nu(\lambda)$ for the number of
semistandard tableaux of shape $\lambda$ and type $\nu$.

**Boundary of the definition.** All asserted module, form and map laws are
discharged by the linked local suppliers
[[thm-arbitrary-level-cyclotomic-hecke-murphy-basis-is-cellular]],
[[lem-cyclotomic-semistandard-sums-give-schur-endomorphism-basis]] and
[[thm-cyclotomic-schur-standards-and-schur-functor-control-block-linkage]];
the definition itself claims no proof of them and no primitive
residue-content classification. No choice principle is used: only finite
bases, finite subgroup and coset decompositions, finite filtrations and
explicit polynomial identities occur.
