## Definition

Let $\mathfrak g$ be a finite-dimensional complex semisimple Lie algebra with
Cartan subalgebra $\mathfrak h$ and Weyl group $W$, acting on $\mathfrak h^*$
by the root reflections of [[def-root-reflections-and-the-weyl-group-action]]
([[def-finite-weyl-root-system-lattice-and-chamber-conventions]]), hence on the
symmetric algebra $S=S(\mathfrak h^*)$ by graded algebra automorphisms; the
dual action identifies $S$ with $S(\mathfrak h)$ as a graded $W$-algebra, and
the page writes $S=S(\mathfrak h)$ for either. Write
$$S^W_+=\bigoplus_{d>0}(S^W)_d,\qquad I:=S\,S^W_+,$$
so that $I$ is the homogeneous ideal of $S$ generated by the invariants of
positive degree; this is the specialization to the reflection group case of
the quotient construction of
[[def-finite-linear-invariant-and-coinvariant-polynomial-algebras]], because
$S^W_+$ is exactly the space of positive-degree elements of the invariant
algebra $S^W$.

The **coinvariant algebra** of the pair $(\mathfrak h,W)$ is the graded
quotient
$$C=C(\mathfrak h,W):=S/I.$$
It is a commutative graded $\mathbb C$-algebra with $C_0=\mathbb C$: the ideal
$I$ is homogeneous of positive degree, so constants survive and every
homogeneous piece of $S$ maps onto its image. The quotient map
$$\rho:S\longrightarrow C,\qquad \rho(f)=f+I,$$
is a surjective graded algebra homomorphism, and the action of $W$ on $S$
descends to a $\mathbb C$-linear action on $C$ by graded algebra
automorphisms, because $I$ is $W$-stable. The algebra $C$ is finite
dimensional: $W$ is generated by its root reflections, so
[[thm-chevalley-shephard-todd-for-finite-weyl-groups]] writes $S$ as a free
module of finite rank over the polynomial algebra $S^W$, and
[[lem-weyl-coinvariant-hilbert-series-has-order-w-dimension]] gives
$\dim_{\mathbb C}C=|W|<\infty$ with the graded Hilbert series recorded there.

**Degree normalization.** Throughout the Soergel material on this page the
Cartan generators are given degree $2$, that is $\deg\mathfrak h=2$; the
symmetric algebra $S$ is then graded with $\deg S_d=2d$ for homogeneous
polynomials of ordinary degree $d$, and $C$ inherits this doubled grading. In
particular a basic invariant of ordinary degree $d_i$ has page degree $2d_i$,
and the top nonzero degree of $C$ is $2N$ with
$N=\sum_i(d_i-1)$ the number of positive roots.

**Auxiliary permutation model for type $A$.** For $\mathfrak g=\mathfrak{sl}_n$
one may instead work with the place-permuting $S_n$-action on the auxiliary
polynomial ring $S_{\mathrm{perm}}=\mathbb C[x_1,\dots,x_n]$, where a
permutation $\sigma$ sends $x_i$ to $x_{\sigma(i)}$, and with the quotient
$$C_{\mathrm{perm}}:=\mathbb C[x_1,\dots,x_n]/(e_1,\dots,e_n),$$
where $e_1,\dots,e_n$ are the elementary symmetric polynomials. Splitting the
permutation representation $\mathbb C^n=\mathfrak h\oplus\mathbb C\cdot(1,\dots,1)$
into its sum-zero Cartan part and its fixed average coordinate exhibits
$\mathbb C[x_1,\dots,x_n]\cong S(\mathfrak h)\otimes\mathbb C[e_1]$ with
$e_1=x_1+\cdots+x_n$ the invariant coordinate, and restriction to the hyperplane
$e_1=0$ has kernel $(e_1)$. Since $e_1,\dots,e_n$ generate the positive-degree
invariants of the permutation action, while $e_2,\dots,e_n$ restrict to basic
invariants of $S(\mathfrak h)^W$, dividing out by the positive-degree invariants
commutes with this presentation and yields a canonical isomorphism of graded
algebras
$$C(\mathfrak h,W)\cong C_{\mathrm{perm}}=\mathbb C[x_1,\dots,x_n]/(e_1,\dots,e_n),$$
in which $x_i$ has degree $2$ and $e_i$ has degree $2i$. Elements of $C$ are
written as classes $\rho(f)$; the auxiliary model is used only for explicit
rank computations and never replaces the intrinsic definition.

All constructions above use only the finite Weyl group, its finite-dimensional
reflection representation and finite coordinate choices; no choice principle is
used.
