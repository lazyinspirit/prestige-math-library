## Statement

Let $(I,A,D,P,P^{\vee},q)$ be a finite symmetrizable Cartan datum
([[def-symmetrizable-cartan-datum-for-a-quantum-group]]), $A=\mathbb Z[q,q^{-1}]$
([[def-the-laurent-polynomial-ring]]), and
$R:=\mathbb Q[q]_{(q-1)}\subset\mathbb Q(q)$, so that $R$ is a DVR with
uniformizer $q-1$ ([[def-discrete-valuation-ring]]). Let
$U_R:=U_A\otimes_AR$ be the base
change of the integral form $U_A$ of
[[def-lusztig-divided-power-integral-form]], identified with its image in
$U_q(\mathfrak g)$.

(a) **Halves.** Each root component $U_R^\pm[\pm\nu]$ is a finite free
$R$-module, generated as an $R$-module by ordinary words
$E_{i_1}\cdots E_{i_k}$ (respectively $F_{i_1}\cdots F_{i_k}$) of degree
$\pm\nu$, and its $q=1$ fibre $U_R^\pm[\pm\nu]\otimes_RR/(q-1)$ is the classical
half $U(\mathfrak n^\pm)_{\mathbb Q}[\pm\nu]$ of the Kac-Moody algebra,
presented by the separate classical Serre relations
([[thm-generic-quantum-serre-halves-have-classical-pbw-ranks-and-a-nondegenerate-hopf-pairing]](i),
[[thm-serre-presentation-of-a-kac-moody-algebra]]).

(b) **Torus.** Write $H_i:=T_{i,0,1}$ and let $L=\sum_i\mathbb Z(d_ih_i)$.
Then $U_R^0$ has the $R$-basis
$\{K_c\prod_iK_i^{\delta_i}H_i^{t_i}\}$ with $\delta_i\in\{0,1\}$,
$t_i\ge0$ and $c$ running through the explicit representatives of
$P^{\vee}/L$ of
[[lem-unmodified-integral-torus-has-a-quantum-binomial-basis]](b); and
multiplication
$U_R^-\otimes_RU_R^0\otimes_RU_R^+\to U_R$ is an isomorphism of
$R$-modules.

(c) **Positive quotient.** For every characteristic-zero field $k$ (an
$A$-algebra via $q\mapsto1$), the positive toral quotient
$U_A^0\otimes_Ak/(K_h-1:h\in P^{\vee})$ is the polynomial algebra $k[H_i:i\in I]$
with, in it, $\bar T_{i,c,t}=\binom{H_i+c}{t}$; and the positive full quotient
$(U_A\otimes_Ak)/(K_h-1:h\in P^{\vee})$ is isomorphic to $U(\mathfrak g_{\mathrm{der}})_k$,
where $\mathfrak g_{\mathrm{der}}$ is the classical Lie algebra presented by the
generators $e_i,f_i,h_i$ with the Cartan and Serre relations, its Cartan being
the span of the independent simple coroots. This is an assertion about the
stated quotient only: it is not claimed that $K_h=1$ in the unquotiented
specialization $U_A\otimes_Ak$, where genuine toral characters such as
$K=-1$ for $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ survive. Direct substitution $q=1$ into the
vanishing denominators $q_i^s-q_i^{-s}$ is never performed.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The Cartan datum, the Laurent ring $A$, the local DVR $R$ with
uniformizer $q-1$, the integral form $U_A$ with its toral generators
$K_h^{\pm1}$, $T_{i,c,t}$, and the classical Lie algebra conventions of the
Kac-Moody page.

[F1] $R=\mathbb Q[q]_{(q-1)}$ is a PID and a DVR with uniformizer $q-1$ and
residue field $\mathbb Q$: $\mathbb Q[q]$ is Euclidean, hence a PID with ideals
$(f)$; the ideals of the localization $R$ are the ideals of $\mathbb Q[q]$
contained in the maximal ideal $(q-1)$, namely $0$ and $((q-1)^m)$, because
$(f)\subseteq(q-1)$ exactly when $(q-1)\mid f$; so $R$ is local with maximal
ideal $(q-1)R$ and residue field $\mathbb Q[q]/(q-1)\cong\mathbb Q$, every
nonzero element of the fraction field $\mathbb Q(q)$ is $(q-1)^m$ times a unit
of $R$ for a unique $m\in\mathbb Z$, and that exponent is a discrete valuation
with valuation ring $R$ ([[def-discrete-valuation-ring]]). Finitely generated
torsion-free $R$-modules are free
([[cor-finitely-generated-torsion-free-modules-over-a-pid-are-free]]).
$q_i^{\pm1}\in R^{\times}$, and $q_i-q_i^{-1}=(q-1)w$ with
$w\in R^{\times}$; for $r\ge1$, $r!\ne0$ in characteristic zero, so
$[r]_i!\in R^{\times}$
([[def-quantum-integers-factorials-and-divided-powers-at-q-i]]).

[F2] $U_A$ is the $A$-subalgebra generated by the divided powers
$E_i^{(r)},F_i^{(r)}$, the $K_h^{\pm1}$ and the $T_{i,c,t}$; its toral part
$U_A^0$ is generated by the $K_h^{\pm1}$ and the $T_{i,c,t}$; $A\to R$ is flat,
so $U_R$ is the $R$-subalgebra generated by the same elements, and
$U_A\otimes_AR\cong U_R$ as $R$-algebras
([[def-lusztig-divided-power-integral-form]]).

[F3] $U_q^-$ (respectively $U_q^+$) is the quotient of the free algebra on the
$F_i$ (respectively $E_i$) by the separate quantum Serre ideal, and the
Drinfeld-Jimbo Serre elements specialize at $q=1$ to the classical Serre
elements, with Gaussian binomials reducing to ordinary binomials; words of
degree $\pm\nu$ span the components; the halves have the classical PBW ranks
equal to their generic dimensions by the published rank comparison, which
assumes AC
([[def-drinfeld-jimbo-quantized-enveloping-algebra]],
[[def-positive-negative-and-toral-quantum-subalgebras]],
[[thm-generic-quantum-serre-halves-have-classical-pbw-ranks-and-a-nondegenerate-hopf-pairing]](i),
[[def-axiom-of-choice]]).

[F4] The classical enveloping algebra of the Kac-Moody algebra has the
presentation by the Cartan and Serre relations, $U(\mathfrak g)=U(\mathfrak n^-)\otimes U(\mathfrak h)\otimes U(\mathfrak n^+)$
as vector spaces, and $U(\mathfrak g_{\mathrm{der}})=U(\mathfrak n^-)\otimes k[h_i]\otimes U(\mathfrak n^+)$
for the derived algebra of the statement; the classical PBW theorem supplies
the ordered-monomial bases
([[thm-serre-presentation-of-a-kac-moody-algebra]],
[[lem-pbw-for-countably-presented-kac-moody-lie-algebras]]).

[F5] Over the field $\mathbb Q(q)$, multiplication
$U_q^-\otimes_{\mathbb Q(q)}U_q^0\otimes_{\mathbb Q(q)}U_q^+ \to U_q(\mathfrak g)$
is an isomorphism, and $U_q^0\cong\mathbb Q(q)[P^{\vee}]$
([[thm-triangular-decomposition-of-a-quantized-enveloping-algebra]](i),(ii)).

[F6] The product and shift identities of the toral binomials hold on the nose:
$T_rT_s=\sum_{j=0}^{\min(r,s)}z^{rs}K_i^{-j}\binom{r+s-j}{r}_z\binom{r}{j}_zT_{r+s-j}$
and $T_{c+1,t}=z^tT_{c,t}+K_i^{-1}z^{-c+t-1}T_{c,t-1}$ with $z=q_i$, and
$E_jT_{i,c,t}=T_{i,c-a_{ij},t}E_j$, $F_jT_{i,c,t}=T_{i,c+a_{ij},t}F_j$,
$E_iF_j-F_jE_i=\delta_{ij}T_{i,0,1}$
([[lem-divided-power-and-toral-quantum-binomial-reordering-identities]]).

[F7] $L=\sum_i\mathbb Z(d_ih_i)$ has rank $|I|$, the $K_i=K_{d_ih_i}$ are
algebraically independent, and the $A$-basis
$\{K_c\prod_iK_i^{\delta_i}T_{i,0,t_i}\}$ of $U_A^0$ over the explicit
representatives $c$ of $P^{\vee}/L$ exists
([[lem-unmodified-integral-torus-has-a-quantum-binomial-basis]](b),
[[def-free-abelian-group]]).



## Verification

**Proof technique:** localize to the DVR, generate the halves by ordinary generators with unit denominators, compare generic dimensions with classical PBW ranks to identify the fibres, rewrite the toral basis through $H_i=T_{i,0,1}$, and reduce the positive quotient via the integral shift recursion.

1.1 The localized form. $A\to R$ is a localization (inverting $q-1$ and the elements of $\mathbb Q[q]$ nonzero at $q=1$), hence flat, and $R$ is a DVR and a PID by [F1]; $U_R$ is generated by the images of the generators of $U_A$ and is the actual image inside $U_q$, which is the content of (a)'s first sentence ([[def-lusztig-divided-power-integral-form]]). [F1, F2, algebra]

1.2 The halves are finite free and specialize to the classical halves. Fix a root $\nu$. By [F2] and [F1], $E_i^{(r)}=E_i^r/[r]_i!$ and $F_i^{(r)}=F_i^r/[r]_i!$ with $[r]_i!\in R^{\times}$, so $U_R^+[\nu]$ is generated as an $R$-module by the ordinary words $E_{i_1}\cdots E_{i_k}$ of degree $\nu$ (finitely many for fixed $\nu$); it is torsion-free inside the $\mathbb Q(q)$-vector space $U_q^+[\nu]$, hence finitely generated torsion-free, hence free over the PID $R$ by [F1], of rank $\dim_{\mathbb Q(q)}U_q^+[\nu]$; the same holds on the negative side. The $q=1$ fibre is generated by the same words, and the quantum Serre relations of the free algebra specialize coefficientwise to the classical Serre relations, so there is a surjection from the classical half $U(\mathfrak n^+)[\nu]$ onto the fibre $U_R^+[\nu]\otimes_RR/(q-1)$; both are finite-dimensional over the residue field $\mathbb Q$, with dimensions equal to the generic dimension by [F3]'s rank comparison (the only AC use, inherited here). A surjection of finite-dimensional spaces of equal dimension is an isomorphism, so the fibre is $U(\mathfrak n^+)_{\mathbb Q}[\nu]$, and likewise for $U^-$. This proves (a). [F1, F2, F3, algebra]

1.3 The toral $R$-basis through $H_i$. In the one-node case with $z=q_i$, $K=K_i$, the identity $K^{-1}=K-(q_i-q_i^{-1})H$ with $H=T_{i,0,1}$, together with $H^i$-powers, spans all $K$-powers: $K^{2}=(q_i-q_i^{-1})KH+1$, and inductively every $K^m$ lies in the $R$-span of $\{K^{\delta}H^{t}\}$; each factor $(Kz^{c-s+1}-K^{-1}z^{-c+s-1})/(z^{s}-z^{-s})$ of $T_{c,t}$ equals $[c-s+1]_iK+z^{c-s+1}(z-z^{-1})/(z^{s}-z^{-s})H$ times a unit of $R$, because $q_i-q_i^{-1}$ and $z^{s}-z^{-s}=(q-1)\cdot\text{unit}$ differ by an $R$-unit; hence every $T_{c,t}$ lies in the span of $\{K^{\delta}H^{t}\}$ generated by $K^{\pm1}$ and $H$, and conversely $K^{\delta}H^{t}$ is a product of generators. So in one node the simple toral part is $R$-spanned by $\{K^{\delta}H^{t}:\delta\in\{0,1\},t\ge0\}$, and the extremal-exponent argument of [[lem-unmodified-integral-torus-has-a-quantum-binomial-basis]] (top exponent $t+1$ isolates $KH^t$, then exponent $-t$ isolates $H^t$) proves $R$-independence. Tensoring the finitely many nodes and then multiplying by the coset representatives of [F7], and using that $R$ contains the coefficients $(q_i-q_i^{-1})^{\pm1}$ replaced by $R$-units, gives the $R$-basis of (b). [F1, F6, F7, algebra]

1.4 Triangular decomposition over $R$. By [F5] the multiplication map is an isomorphism over $\mathbb Q(q)$; restricted to the $R$-lattices it is injective because it is injective after tensoring with the flat field $\mathbb Q(q)$ and the source is torsion-free. Its image contains every generator of $U_R$: the $E_i^{(r)},F_i^{(r)}$ through the half factors, and $K_h^{\pm1},T_{i,c,t}$ through the toral factor; since these generate $U_R$ as an $R$-algebra, the image is all of $U_R$. Hence the multiplication map is an isomorphism, completing (b). [F5, F7, algebra]

2.1 The positive toral quotient. Work in $W:=U_A^0\otimes_Ak$ with $k$ a characteristic-zero field, $q\mapsto1$. By step 1.3 and [F7], $W$ has the $k$-basis $\{K_c\prod_iK_i^{\delta_i}H_i^{t_i}\}$; quotienting by all $K_h-1$ (in particular by $K_c-1$ and $K_i-1$ for every $i$) leaves the span of the monomials $\prod_iH_i^{t_i}$, and the relation $K_i^{2}\equiv1$ forced by $K_i^{2}=(q_i-q_i^{-1})K_iH_i+1$ at $q=1$ is compatible with $K_i=1$; the elements $\prod_iH_i^{t_i}$ remain linearly independent, since a relation $\sum a_t\prod H_i^{t_i}\in(K_h-1)W$ would, after unique expansion in the basis and use of $(K_h-1)W=k\text{-span}\{(K_i-1)\prod H_i^{t}\}$ for each node (the $R$-units absorbed), force all $a_t=0$. Hence $U_A^0\otimes_Ak/(K_h-1)\cong k[H_i:i\in I]$. Reducing the integral shift recursion of [F6] gives $\bar T_{c+1,t}=\bar T_{c,t}+\bar T_{c,t-1}$ with $\bar T_{c,0}=1$ and $\bar T_{c,1}=\bar H_i+c$ (from $T_{c,1}=K_i[c]_i+T_{i,0,1}$ and $[c]_i\mapsto c$), whose unique solution is $\bar T_{i,c,t}=\binom{H_i+c}{t}$; alternatively, each factor of $T_{i,c,t}$ equals $[c-s+1]_iK_i+u_sH_i$ with $u_s\mapsto1/s$ at $q=1$, and $\prod_{s=1}^{t}((c-s+1)+H_i/s)=\binom{H_i+c}{t}$. [F6, F7, step 1.3, algebra]

3.1 The positive full quotient. Let $Q:=(U_A\otimes_Ak)/(K_h-1:h\in P^{\vee})$. By step 1.4 and step 2.1, $Q\cong U(\mathfrak n^-)_k\otimes_kk[H_i]\otimes_kU(\mathfrak n^+)_k$ as vector spaces, with the half factors from step 1.2 and the toral factor from step 2.1. The mixed relation $E_iF_j-F_jE_i=\delta_{ij}T_{i,0,1}$ of [F6] and the shift relations $T_{i,0,1}E_j=E_jT_{i,a_{ij},1}$, $T_{i,0,1}F_j=F_jT_{i,-a_{ij},1}$ specialize to $[e_i,f_j]=\delta_{ij}h_i$, $[h_i,e_j]=a_{ij}e_j$, $[h_i,f_j]=-a_{ij}f_j$, and the half relations specialize to the classical Serre relations, so there is a surjection $U(\mathfrak g_{\mathrm{der}})_k\twoheadrightarrow Q$ mapping $e_i\mapsto\bar E_i$, $f_i\mapsto\bar F_i$, $h_i\mapsto\bar H_i$. By [F4] the source has the vector-space decomposition $U(\mathfrak n^-)\otimes k[h_i]\otimes U(\mathfrak n^+)$; comparing with the decomposition of $Q$ and the isomorphisms of steps 1.2 and 2.1 degree by degree, the surjection is an isomorphism. This proves (c). [F4, step 1.2, step 1.4, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 Honest boundaries. Nothing above asserts $K_h=1$ before quotienting: for $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ the assignment $E=F=0$, $K=-1$ is an integral representation (with all divided powers acting by zero) whose toral binomials act by $(-1)^{t}\binom{c}{t}$-type scalars and whose $K$ image at $q=1$ is $-1$, so the unquotiented specialization retains toral characters; only the stated quotient can be identified with the classical positive algebra. All divisions performed were by $[r]_i!$ and by $(q_i-q_i^{-1})$-type elements inside the DVR $R$ where they are units, i.e. before reduction, and never by a vanishing denominator after $q=1$. [given, F1, algebra] ∎
