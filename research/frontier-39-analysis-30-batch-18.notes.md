## Design, plan and published-content reconciliation

1. **Plan requires versus the design prose.** `research/plan-spec.json`
   (orders 458.045/458.046) declares exactly one A-page prerequisite,
   `strongly-continuous-semigroups-and-hille-yosida` (in-run draft, batch 17),
   and the B page requires its A companion. The design prose instead names
   PDE-16–PDE-19, PDE-23, FA-17, FA-21 and the complex-analysis scalar contour
   pages, with the note “FA-17, not complex analysis, supplies the
   Banach-algebra holomorphic functional calculus”. The plan controls; every
   additional input is consumed at item level (published complex-analysis,
   functional-analysis and PDE items, or in-run draft suppliers of batches
   10–12 and 17). Of the item-level edges, 80 land on in-run drafts of batches
   10, 11, 12 and 17 and are declared as cross-batch rows; the rest are
   published items on disk. **Conflict recorded.**

2. **Item 3 of the design needs a prerequisite the design does not list.**
   The equivalence theorem
   `thm-sectorial-resolvent-characterisation-of-bounded-analytic-semigroups`
   cannot be stated without knowing that the contour family is generated by
   the sectorial operator; EN Proposition 4.4 proves exactly this, and the
   design's inventory had no home for it. Minted locally:
   `lem-generator-of-the-contour-semigroup-is-the-sectorial-operator`
   (position 6 of the A page), with a direct proof through the strong
   derivative `d/dt T(t)x = AT(t)x` and equality of the resolvents
   `R(lambda,B) = R(lambda,A)`. **Design gap recorded.**

3. **The Dunford contour and its complex analysis were minted locally.** The
   design names FA-17 for the Banach-algebra holomorphic calculus, but the
   library's FA-17 contour machinery assumes the Axiom of Choice and its
   admissible cycles are compact, whereas the analytic-semigroup contour is an
   unbounded keyhole whose independence is proved by contour deformation. The
   following local prerequisites were therefore minted, each before its
   consumers:
   - `lem-resolvent-identity-and-holomorphy-for-closed-operators` — the
     resolvent identity, openness of the resolvent set and norm-holomorphy for
     a closed (possibly unbounded) operator, from the Neumann series in
     B(X); needed by the contour construction and the semigroup law.
   - `lem-banach-valued-cauchy-theorem-on-star-shaped-domains` — primitive,
     Cauchy theorem and contour independence for Banach-valued holomorphic
     maps on star-shaped domains, proved directly by the segment primitive and
     the batch-17 Banach-valued fundamental theorem of calculus. This is the
     choice-free replacement for the scalarisation-plus-Hahn-Banach route of
     FA-17/SN (2.14).
   - `thm-cauchy-integral-formula-and-cauchy-estimates-for-banach-valued-holomorphic-functions`
     — Cauchy formula, power-series expansion and Cauchy estimates for
     Banach-valued holomorphic functions, proved by the slit-annulus
     triangulation and the geometric series; supplies the quantitative
     complex-analysis step behind the smoothing estimates without AC.
   - `lem-power-series-coefficients-are-determined-by-real-values` and
     `lem-taylor-expansion-with-integral-remainder-for-banach-valued-curves` —
     the two calculus facts EN uses in the implication (c)⇒(a) of Theorem 4.6
     (their (4.14) and the identity theorem step); the identity problem is
     reduced to comparing power-series coefficients along the real axis.
   **Design conflict recorded**: the design's count of A-page items (14 + 7
   additions) is preserved, but six additional prerequisite items are required
   for mathematical closure; the page remains far below the 100-item cap.

4. **A- and B-page counterexamples were made self-contained.** The B-page
   items `cex-the-translation-semigroup-is-not-analytic`,
   `cex-an-analytic-semigroup-need-not-be-norm-continuous-at-zero` and
   `ex-sectorial-multiplication-operator` initially reached into batch-17's
   *examples* page for translation, multiplication and unbounded-generator
   facts. `content-policy --manifest-only` correctly rejects a B-leaf
   dependency (`batch-b-leaf-target`), and each of the three items was
   rewritten to define and compute its own witness from published or A-page
   suppliers (weak-derivative domain computed inline for translation; heat
   eigenbasis for the norm-discontinuity; pointwise multiplication resolvent
   for the sector). No claim was weakened; the batch-17 examples page is no
   longer on any dependency path.

5. **Sign convention.** `def-sectorial-operator-with-the-semigroup-sign-convention`
   fixes the `u'=Au`, `e^{tA}` convention of batch PDE-23: the resolvent sector
   `Sigma_{pi/2+delta}` opens around the positive real direction, the spectrum
   lies in the complementary left sector, and the contour encircles the
   spectrum. The definition carries the algebraic dictionary
   `R(mu,-A+omega I) = -R(omega-mu,A)` for sources (Pazy, Lunardi) that state
   sectoriality for `-A` and write `e^{-t(-A)}`. This implements the plan's
   convention note (L2669: “PDE-24 follows the `u'=Au` convention and locates
   the sector for `-A` explicitly”) and the drift constraint to fix the sign,
   sector and orientation before the contour integral.

6. **Choice-strength audit.** The design's primary route quotes FA-17 and FA-21
   (Banach-algebra holomorphic calculus; unbounded spectral calculus). The
   scaffold deliberately strengthens the core to a choice-free development and
   declares axioms only where they are actually inherited:
   - Items 1–14 (contour construction, semigroup law, generator
     identification, smoothing, differentiability, the (a)⇔(e)
     characterisation and its form-generated version) are **choice-free**: the
     Banach-valued Cauchy theory is proved locally by primitives, the
     self-adjoint resolvent estimate is proved from the numerical-range
     identity, and both the semigroup law and the complex-time extension are
     proved by power-series comparison instead of the operator-valued identity
     theorem plus Hahn-Banach separation.
   - `thm-self-adjoint-nonpositive-operators-generate-bounded-analytic-semigroups`
     is choice-free apart from the vocabulary of
     `def-symmetric-self-adjoint-and-essentially-self-adjoint`; it does not
     consume `thm-self-adjoint-resolvent-estimate` (which states Countable
     Choice).
   - `cor-spectral-gap-gives-exponential-decay-of-a-self-adjoint-parabolic-semigroup`
     is proved through dissipativity and Lumer-Phillips; the Axiom of Choice is
     declared **only** for the clause passing from the spectral hypothesis
     `sigma(A) subset (-infinity,-lambda_1]` to the quadratic hypothesis
     `(Au,u) <= -lambda_1||u||^2` through the PVM. That use is identified in
     the record and in the item contract.
   - `cor-dirichlet-laplacian-generates-an-analytic-heat-semigroup` and
     `ex-analytic-dirichlet-heat-semigroup` carry the Axiom of Choice and
     Countable Choice exactly as they are declared by the in-run PDE-17/18
     suppliers they consume (compact-resolvent spectral decomposition, trace
     and Sobolev machinery). No new choice principle is introduced.
   - The plan's choice ledger entry for PDE-24 (“ZF relative to Bochner
     integration, completeness and FA-17”) is therefore met in a stronger form
     for the abstract core; the PDE realisation inherits the batch-10–12 costs
     of its suppliers, as those items declare them. **Deviation recorded for
     Alpha review:** the design's named “FA spectral calculus” is not consumed,
     because the needed resolvent bounds and generation statements are proved
     without it.

7. **Design citations adjusted.** The additions row for the Duhamel
   cancellation and the classical-regularity theorem cites `[EN] II.4.b`; EN's
   Section II.4.b treats differentiable semigroups, not Hölder forcing. The
   mathematical support actually read for these items is `[SN]` Section 2.3
   (Theorem 2.31 with its proof, and Remark 2.32) together with `[T]` Chapter
   11 Section 11.3 (Lemma 11.12) and Section 11.5. The manifest records the
   correct locators; the statement, hypotheses and conclusions are unchanged.
   **Design citation conflict recorded.**

8. **Bounded-generator example.** `ex-analytic-semigroup-generated-by-a-bounded-operator`
   is stated for a general Banach space (not only a Hilbert space) because the
   batch-17 exponential series item is proved there; the sector criterion is
   read through `sigma(A) cap closed(Sigma_{pi/2+delta}) = empty`, which for
   bounded operators is equivalent to sectoriality by the Neumann series plus
   compactness of the spectrum.
