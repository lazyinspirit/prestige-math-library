This page proves first- and second-order delta limits for finite-dimensional random vectors and the asymptotic linearity and normality of iid Z-estimators. The first-order result uses a pointwise derivative expansion; the second-order result requires a zero first derivative and a quadratic Peano expansion. The Z-estimator result assumes consistency, a root residual of order $o_P(n^{-1/2})$, a nonsingular mean derivative, finite score covariance, and local stochastic equicontinuity. It does not infer equicontinuity from consistency alone. Its normal-limit branch assumes AC through the library's multivariate iid central limit theorem.
