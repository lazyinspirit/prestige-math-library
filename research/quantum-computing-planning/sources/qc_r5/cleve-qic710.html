Weighting of tests and assignments in final mark
Let T ∈ {0,1,2,...,100} be the test portion of your grade and A ∈ {0,1,2,...,100} be the assignment portion of your grade. Your final mark will the following (rounded to the nearest integer):
  • If T ≥ 50 then your final mark will be 0.64T + 0.36A
  • If T < 50 then your final mark will be 0.64T + 0.18A
(Note that the above implies that T ≥ 50 is needed in order to obtain a final grade ≥ 50)
