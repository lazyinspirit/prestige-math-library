                  Measurement-based quantum computation on cluster states

                               Robert Raussendorf, Daniel E. Browne,∗and Hans J. Briegel
                                   Theoretische Physik, Ludwig-Maximilians-Universit¨at M¨unchen, Germany
                                                        (Dated: February 1, 2008)

                 We give a detailed account of the one-way quantum computer, a scheme of quantum computation
                       that consists entirely of one-qubit measurements on a particular class of entangled states, the cluster
                           states. We prove its universality, describe why its underlying computational model is diﬀerent from
                       the network model of quantum computation and relate quantum algorithms to mathematical graphs.
                      Further we investigate the scaling of required resources and give a number of examples for circuits
                           of practical interest such as the circuit for quantum Fourier transformation and for the quantum
                       adder. Finally, we describe computation with clusters of ﬁnite size.
