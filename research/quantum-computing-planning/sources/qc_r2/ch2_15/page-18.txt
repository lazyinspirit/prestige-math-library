Because ⟨M A⟩has the form eq.(2.56) for any observable M A acting

possible quantum states, each occurring with a specified probability. That
is, we would obtain precisely the same result for ⟨M A⟩if we stipulated
that qubit A is in one of two quantum states. With probability p0 = |a|2

it is in the quantum state |0⟩, and with probability p1 = |b|2 it is in
the state |1⟩. To describe the measurement of an observable which acts

corresponding eigenstate of that observable. Then

Prob(an) = p0⟨0|EA(an)|0⟩+ p1⟨1|EA(an)|1⟩,
(2.57)

which is the probability of outcome an summed over the ensemble, and
weighted by the probability of each state in the ensemble.
