To summarize the geometrical interpretation of a qubit: we may think
of a qubit as a spin-1

by a unit vector ˆn in three dimensions, the spin’s direction. A unitary

two possible outcomes: the spin is either up or down along a specified
axis.
It should be emphasized that, while this formal equivalence with a spin-

system transforms as a spinor under spatial rotations!

2.2.2 Photon polarizations

Another important two-state system is provided by a photon, which can
have two independent polarizations. These photon polarization states also
transform under rotations, but photons differ from our spin-1

2 objects in
two important ways: (1) Photons are massless. (2) Photons have spin-1
(they are not spinors).
