Furthermore, we can imagine performing an orthogonal measurement in

the purification |Φ1⟩AB of ρA, there is a measurement we can perform in
system B that realizes the |ϕi⟩A ensemble interpretation of ρA. When
the measurement outcome in B is known, we have successfully extracted
one of the pure states |ϕi⟩A from the mixture ρA.
