Eq.(2.23) is a conservation law. It says, for example, that if we pre-
pare an eigenstate of Q, then time evolution governed by the Schr¨odinger
equation will preserve the eigenstate. Thus we see that symmetries imply
conservation laws. Conversely, given a conserved quantity Q satisfying
eq.(2.23) we can construct the corresponding symmetry transformations.
