4.4 Teleportation with mixed states.
An operational way to define entanglement is that an entangled
state can be used to teleport an unknown quantum state with better
fidelity than could be achieved with local operations and classical
communication only. In this exercise, you will show that there are
mixed states that are entangled in this sense, yet do not violate any
Bell inequality. Hence, for mixed states (in contrast to pure states)
“entangled” and “Bell-inequality-violating” are not equivalent.
