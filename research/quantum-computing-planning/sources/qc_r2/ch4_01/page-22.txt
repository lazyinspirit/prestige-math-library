4.3.4 All entangled pure states violate Bell inequalities

Separable states do not violate Bell inequalities. For example, in the case
of a separable pure state, if a is an observable acting on Alice’s qubit, and
b is an observable acting on Bob’s, then

⟨ab⟩= ⟨a⟩⟨b⟩.
