Suppose that Charlie generates the input bits at random. Then there is
a very simple strategy that enables Alice and Bob to win the game three
times our of four: they always choose the output a = b = 0 so that they
lose only if the input is x = y = 1. The CHSH inequality can be regarded
as the statement that, if Alice and Bob share no quantum entanglement,
then there is no better strategy.
