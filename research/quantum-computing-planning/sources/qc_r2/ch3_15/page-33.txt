If we detect a photon (and so project out a single-photon state of the
environment), then we have prepared the state |0⟩A of the atom. Not only
that, we have prepared a state in which we know with certainty that the
initial atomic state was the excited state |1⟩A; if the atom had started out
in the ground state than it could not have decayed and no photon could
have been detected.
