3.6 Non-Markovian noise

3.6.1 Gaussian phase noise

The master equation describes the evolution of a quantum system subject
to Markovian noise, but in some experimental systems the Markovian
approximation is not very accurate. In this section we will discuss some
of the features of decoherence for a system subjected to non-Markovian
noise.
As a simple example, consider a single qubit with energy eigenstates |0⟩
and |1⟩, where the energy splitting between the two states fluctuates. For
example, the qubit could be a spin-1
