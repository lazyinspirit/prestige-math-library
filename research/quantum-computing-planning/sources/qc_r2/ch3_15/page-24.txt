3 Foundations II: Measurement and Evolution

3.4 Three quantum channels

The best way to familiarize ourselves with the concept of a quantum
channel is to study a few examples. We will now consider three examples
(all interesting and useful) of channels acting on a single qubit. If we
wish we may imagine that the channel E describes the fate of quantum
information that is transmitted with some loss of fidelity from a sender
to a receiver. Or, if we prefer, we may imagine that the transmission is
in time rather than space; that is, E describes the time evolution of a
quantum system that interacts with its environment.

3.4.1 Depolarizing channel
