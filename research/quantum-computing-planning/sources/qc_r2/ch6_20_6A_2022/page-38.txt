6.7 The Grover Algorithm Is Optimal

Grover’s quadratic quantum speedup for exhaustive search is already interesting and
potentially important, but surely with more cleverness we can do better, can’t we?
No, it turns out that we can’t. Grover’s algorithm provides the fastest possible quantum
search of an unsorted database, if “time” is measured according to the number of queries
of the oracle.
