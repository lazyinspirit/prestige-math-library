The computation of ax(mod N) is carried out by executing a routine:

INPUT 1
For j = 0 to m −1, if xj = 1, MULTIPLY a2j.

This routine requires at most m mod N multiplications, each requiring of order
(log N)2 elementary operations. (Using tricks for performing efficient multiplication
of very large numbers, the number of elementary operations can be reduced to
O(log N log log N log log log N); thus, asymptotically for large N, a circuit family with
size O(log2 N log log N log log log N) can compute fN,a.) Since r < N, we will have a
reasonable chance of success at extracting the period if we choose m ∼2 log N. Hence,
the computation of fN,a can be carried out by a circuit family of size O((log N)3).
