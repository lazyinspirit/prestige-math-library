so the phase gate is triggered by |00..0⟩rather than |11..1⟩. Thus Us is realized by a
circuit of size O(log N).
What does UGrover do? It preserves the plane spanned by |s⟩and |w⟩, so we may
confine our attention to that plane. First, Uw reflects |s⟩about the axis |w⊥⟩(the vector
⊥to |w⟩in the span of |s⟩and |w⟩). Then Us reflects Uw|s⟩about the axis |s⟩. The net
effect of UGrover, then, is a counterclockwise rotation in the plane by the angle 2θ, where
θ is initial angle between |s⟩and |w⟩. Each time we repeat the Grover iteration the state
vector rotates further in the counterclockwise direction by 2θ.
