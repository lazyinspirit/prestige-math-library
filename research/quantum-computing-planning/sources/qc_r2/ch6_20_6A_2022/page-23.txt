Suppose that a is coprime to N (which is overwhelmingly likely if p and q are very
large – anyway Alice can check before she encodes). Then

aϕ(N) ≡1 (mod N)
(6.77)

(Euler’s theorem). This is so because the numbers less than N and coprime to N form a
group (of order ϕ(N)) under mod N multiplication. The order of any group element must
divide the order of the group (the powers of a form a subgroup). Since GCD(e, ϕ(N) = 1,
we know that e has a multiplicative inverse d = e−1 mod ϕ(N):

ed ≡1 (mod ϕ(N)).
(6.78)
