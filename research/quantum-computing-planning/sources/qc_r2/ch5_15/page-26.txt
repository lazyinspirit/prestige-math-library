It seems likely, then, that the classification of complexity will be dif-
ferent depending on whether we use a classical computer or a quantum
computer to solve problems. If such a separation really holds, it is the
quantum classification that should be regarded as the more fundamental,
for it is better founded on the physical laws that govern the universe.
