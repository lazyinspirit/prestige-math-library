5.3.3 Most unitary transformations require large quantum circuits

We saw that any Boolean function can be computed by an exponential-
size classical circuit, and also that exponential-size circuits are needed to
compute most functions. What are the corresponding statements about
unitary transformations and quantum circuits? We will postpone for now
consideration of how large a quantum circuit suffices to reach any uni-
tary transformation, focusing instead on showing that exponential-size
quantum circuits are required to reach most unitaries.
