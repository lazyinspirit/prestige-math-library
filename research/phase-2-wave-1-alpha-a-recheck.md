## BR: repaired

The current first lemma proves finite simultaneous density using complements
in a **finite** sum of simples, finite basis extraction and the scalar form of

coefficients zero. This contradiction gives the required action-map
surjectivity. The finite dimension bound also bounds the number of distinct
simple types. Neither Jacobson radical structure nor a composition-series
existence theorem is used. The exact published splitting-field definition
