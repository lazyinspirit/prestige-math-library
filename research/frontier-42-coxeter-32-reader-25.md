## Mathematical reading and edits

1. `def-cg-length-series-descent-generating-polynomial`: the finite-rank finite-fiber argument supplies the coefficients, including rank zero. The unit characterization over Z and the marked-variable specialization are correct. No circular justifier is needed for these definitions.

2. `lem-cg-fundamental-weight-orbit-and-schreier-distance`: the coordinate functional lies in the chamber and vanishes exactly on T. The cited point-stabilizer theorem gives W_T. The orbit fiber is the left coset wW_T, and uniqueness/additivity from the parabolic supplier gives its minimum. Both walk inequalities, the omission of self-loops, and the coefficientwise quotient factorization are justified. Infinite W_T is permitted. No item edit; corrected the contract's stale “right-coset theorem” wording to “left-coset theorem.”

3. `thm-cg-parabolic-growth-factorization-and-rationality`, Fact F12 and Proof 4.1: the statement defines rationality for a series over Z, but the old induction only preserved nonzero denominator constants over a field. A nonzero integer constant need not be a unit of Z. Repaired the proof to preserve integer polynomial presentations with denominator constant ±1. Since every parabolic series has constant 1, its numerator also has constant ±1, licensing its reciprocal. Finite common denominators retain unit constants; the infinite reciprocal has constant 1 and the finite recursion denominator has constant ±1 by Boolean cancellation. This proves the promised integral formal rationality and hence the Q(t) conclusion. Also corrected Proof 1.3's reversed arrow for the component-product isomorphism: its displayed map sends tuples to their product in W. The descent transfer, outside-parabolic exchange argument, Boolean sum and two-case Steinberg formulas were checked without altering their mathematical statements.

4. `lem-cg-classical-type-poincare-products`: the old orbit steps used the full permutation/signed-permutation/dihedral model groups “by step 1.1,” although that step proved only the Gram isometry; the group identification occurred later. Moved the identification ahead of the orbit computations and cited it explicitly. Adopted precheck's canonical numbering: 1.1 Gram isometry, 2.1 conjugation/faithfulness, 3.1 model groups and inversion length, 4.1–4.4 orbits, 5.1 recurrences/low-rank bases, 6.1 products, 7.1 orders. Updated exact internal locators and the insertion example's locators accordingly. Added Fact F17 and five existing trigonometric/square-root dependencies to establish cos(pi/3)=1/2 and cos(pi/4)=1/sqrt(2), instead of silently using these constants in the Gram comparison. The derivation uses the opened double-angle, quarter-turn, parity, monotonicity and square-root statements. The A/B paths, D graph with a four-cycle and two attached tails, dihedral path, attaining words, lower bounds, D2/D3 bases and telescoping identities were checked.
