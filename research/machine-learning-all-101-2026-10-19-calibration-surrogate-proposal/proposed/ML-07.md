### ML-07 — Calibration, proper losses, and surrogate classification

**Pages:** calibration-proper-losses-and-surrogate-classification / calibration-proper-losses-and-surrogate-classification-examples. **Scope:** For finite labels $Y\in\{1,\ldots,K\}$, define full reliability by $P(Y\in\cdot\mid g(X))=g(X)$, classwise calibration by $P(Y=y\mid g_y(X))=g_y(X)$, and top-confidence calibration by $P(Y=\arg\max g(X)\mid\max g(X))=\max g(X)$; resolve argmax ties by the smallest class index. For $K\ge3$ these notions differ. Any measurable feature $h(X)$ induces the calibrated forecast $P(Y\in\cdot\mid h(X))$, including an uninformative constant forecast. Define strictly proper scoring rules on the finite probability simplex; log and Brier scores are strictly proper. For the binary surrogate theorem, take $Y\in\{-1,+1\}$, $\eta(x)=P(Y=1\mid X=x)$, convex nondecreasing margin loss $\Phi$, conditional risk $L_\Phi(x,u)=\eta(x)\Phi(-u)+(1-\eta(x))\Phi(u)$, and a measurable extended-real conditional minimizer $h_\Phi^*(x)\in\arg\min_{u\in[-\infty,+\infty]}L_\Phi(x,u)$. Define $R_{01}(h)=P(Yh(X)\le0)$ (zero score counts as error), $h^*(x)=\eta(x)-1/2$, and $L_\Phi^*=E[L_\Phi(X,h_\Phi^*(X))]$. If some $s\ge1,c>0$ satisfy $|h^*(x)|^s\le c[L_\Phi(x,0)-L_\Phi(x,h_\Phi^*(x))]$ for all $x$, then every measurable real-valued score $h$ with finite surrogate risk satisfies $R_{01}(h)-R_{01}^*\le2c^{1/s}[L_\Phi(h)-L_\Phi^*]^{1/s}+E[\min\{\eta(X),1-\eta(X)\}\mathbf1_{\{h(X)=0\}}]$. The correction vanishes for scores nonzero almost surely. For the label classifier predicting $+1$ at $h\ge0$ and $-1$ at $h<0$, its zero-one classification excess is bounded by $2c^{1/s}[L_\Phi(h)-L_\Phi^*]^{1/s}$ without a tie correction. Equivalently parameterizing the margin premise by $a^s$ gives coefficient $2a$. Preserve the original unadjusted implication as the demonstrated false statement `fs-unadjusted-surrogate-regret-bound`; its scale defect and zero-score defect have separate finite counterexamples. For calibration estimation, state Vaicenavicius et al.'s exact conditions: fixed-bin underestimation uses a finite data-independent partition and continuous convex discrepancy; the refining-partition a.s. consistency theorem requires Lipschitz calibration function, discrepancy continuous and uniformly continuous in its first argument, and finite data-independent partitions whose mesh tends to zero, with $n\to\infty$ taken before mesh refinement. Fixed-bin ECE is not universally consistent. Proposed interfaces: `def-probability-calibration`, `def-proper-scoring-rule`, `thm-log-and-brier-strict-propriety`, `def-classification-calibration-of-a-surrogate`, `thm-surrogate-regret-controls-classification-regret`. **Proof route:** conditional expectations establish calibration; strict propriety follows by pointwise minimization of expected score; surrogate regret follows from the pointwise margin inequality and Jensen; calibration estimator claims use their partition limits in the stated order. **Examples:** distinct multiclass calibration tables, constant prior forecast, fixed-bin underestimation, a calibrated risk score with low informativeness. **Requires:** ML-01 risk/conditional-law hypotheses; MSF-03 risk and Bayes-rule interfaces; `thm-existence-of-regular-conditional-distributions-for-standard-borel-targets` (AC) if an RCD is used rather than finite-label conditional expectations. **Sources:** Vaicenavicius et al., “Evaluating Model Calibration in Classification,” PMLR 89 (2019), complete 9-page paper and 10-page supplement, Definitions 1–3, Propositions 1–2, Theorems 1–2 and Appendix A.2 proofs; Gneiting and Raftery, “Strictly Proper Scoring Rules, Prediction, and Estimation,” JASA 102(477) (2007), selected §§1, 2.1–2.2, 3.1–3.2 and Theorems 1–3, PDF pp. 1–7 of 20; Mohri et al., Chapter 4, Theorem 4.7 and proof, physical PDF pp. 93–94.
