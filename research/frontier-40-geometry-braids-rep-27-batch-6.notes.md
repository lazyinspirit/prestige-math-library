## 2. Conflicts and deviations recorded

1. **Parameter naming (design `q` vs supplier `v`).** The design writes the Markov trace over `A = Z[q^{±1}, z]` with quadratic relation `(T_i-q)(T_i+1)=0`. The in-run RG-13 supplier `def-generic-type-a-hecke-algebra` (batch 2) freezes the generic parameter as `v` over `A = Z[v^{±1}]`, with the finite-field parameter `q` reserved for the `GL_n(F_q)` specialization. This page uses `v` for the generic Hecke parameter and `z` for the trace parameter, matching the supplier; the design's `q` is read as `v` throughout. Recorded as a naming reconciliation, not a scope change.
2. **Design dependency removed from `def-markov-trace-on-the-type-a-hecke-tower`.** The design lists `[def-generic-type-a-hecke-algebra, thm-type-a-iwahori-hecke-presentation, def-trace-of-an-endomorphism]`. The Iwahori–Hecke presentation is about the *finite-field* specialization and is not needed to define the abstract trace tower, and `def-trace-of-an-endomorphism` (basis-independent trace of a linear map) is a different notion from a linear functional with the trace property; neither is used. The dependency list is `[def-generic-type-a-hecke-algebra, def-algebra-over-a-commutative-ring, def-polynomial-ring-over-a-commutative-ring, def-the-laurent-polynomial-ring, def-ring-homomorphism]` (the last four for the scalar-extension ring). The RG-13 normalization is still frozen by the supplier's statement.
3. **HOMFLYPT normalization and variable names.** The design's `P(\widehat\beta)=u^{e(\beta)}v^{n-1}\operatorname{tr}_n` uses `v` for the HOMFLYPT variable and `q` for the Hecke parameter, which cannot be combined with the supplier's names. This page defines `P=u^{e}\alpha^{n-1}\operatorname{tr}_n` with `\alpha=(uz)^{-1}` and `u^2=z_-/z`, `z_-=v^{-1}(z+1-v)`, and records the standard variables `l=us`, `m=s-s^{-1}` (`s^2=v`), in which the skein relation is `l^{-1}P_+-lP_-=mP_0`. The relation between this normalization and BB's (l,m) dictionary is exactly the one derived from BB §4.3; the literature's alternative parametrizations (BB Remark 4.2) are recorded as a convention caveat rather than as a load-bearing claim.
4. **`def-temperley-lieb-quotient-and-jones-specialization` reinterpreted.** The design asks to "define the extra three-strand ideal, quotient, and one-variable trace normalization yielding the Jones polynomial". The quotient is defined as the design asks (ideal generated by the six-term parabolic sums, TL relations verified in the quotient, dimension not claimed). However, the Ocneanu trace does **not** descend to that quotient: `tr_3(E^{(1)}_3T_1)=v+(3v-1)z+(v^2+2v-1)z^2`, whose value at `z_0=-1/(v+1)` is `v(v^2+1)/(v+1)^2≠0`, while `tr_3(E^{(1)}_3)=1+z(v+2)+z^2(v+1)` does vanish at `z_0`. The item therefore defines the quotient for the algebraic relation and realizes the "one-variable normalization" as the specialization `z=z_0`, `u=s` of the *link invariant* `P`, whose skein relation is the Jones skein relation. The non-descent computation is recorded in the item so that no author asserts it.
5. **Markov theorem removed from the Burau–Alexander proposition.** The design lists `thm-markovs-closed-braid-equivalence-theorem` among the deps of `prop-burau-determinant-recovers-the-alexander-polynomial-of-a-closed-braid`. The formula is proved for each braid representative directly by the Fox-calculus computation (Morton + Conway) and the invariance of the right-hand side is not used; Markov's theorem is used only by the *HOMFLYPT* invariance theorem, where the design also lists it. Recorded as an unused-dependency removal, not a scope change (the proposition still states the unit-normalized invariant formula).

## 4. Mathematical verification performed at scaffold time

- **Trace recursion and values.** With the RG-13 relation `T_i^2=(v-1)T_i+v` and the recursion `tr_{n+1}(xT_ny)=z\,tr_n(xy)`: `tr_2(T_1)=z`, `tr_2(T_1^2)=(v-1)z+v`, `tr_3(T_1T_2T_1)=z^2(v-1)+zv`, `tr_3(T_1T_2)=z^2`, `tr_3(E^{(1)}_3)=1+z(v+2)+z^2(v+1)`; the last vanishes exactly at `z=-1/(v+1)` or `z=-1`.
- **Skein relation.** From `T_i=vT_i^{-1}+(v-1)` I derived `u^{-1}P_+-vuP_-=(v-1)P_0` and hence, with `l=us`, `m=s-s^{-1}`, the standard `l^{-1}P_+-lP_-=mP_0`. For the closed-braid triple at the middle crossing of `σ_1σ_2σ_1` (`x=y=σ_1`), with `A=tr_3(T_1T_2T_1)=z^2(v-1)+zv`, `B=tr_3(T_1T_2^{-1}T_1)=(1-v^{-1})z^2+(3-v-v^{-1})z+(1-v)` and `C=tr_3(T_1^2)=(v-1)z+v`, the relation reduces after cancelling `u^2α^2` and multiplying by `s` to the polynomial identity `A-vB=(v-1)C` (both sides equal `(v-1)^2z+v(v-1)`); since both sides carry the same factor `u^2α^2`, the check is independent of `u`. Verified symbolically by expansion and on exact rational samples. One exact instance with all normalisation relations satisfied: `v=4`, `s=2`, `u=3/4`, `z=-12/5` (so `z_-/z=(z-3)/(4z)=9/16=u^2` and `α=(uz)^{-1}=-5/9`), `l=m=3/2`: `P_+=P_-=1`, `P_0=-5/9`, and `l^{-1}P_+-lP_-=-5/6=mP_0`.
- **Jones specialization.** At `z_0=-1/(v+1)` one gets `z_-/z=v`, so `u=s` is admissible, `α=-(s^2+1)/s`, `l=s^2`, `m=s-s^{-1}`. For the trefoil `σ_1^3∈B_2`: `tr_2=(v^3-v^2-1)/(v+1)`, `V=-s^8+s^6+s^2=-t^4+t^3+t` for `t=s^2`, which is the standard right-trefoil table value `-t^{-4}+t^{-3}+t^{-1}` under the mirror substitution `t↦t^{-1}` (the page's recorded convention caveat). For `β=σ_1σ_2σ_1∈B_3`: `tr_3=-(v^2+1)/(v+1)^2`, `V=-s(s^4+1)=-t^{1/2}(t^2+1)`. **This braid is not a second representative of the trefoil**: `σ_1σ_2σ_1=σ_1^{-1}(σ_1^2σ_2)σ_1` is conjugate to `σ_1^2σ_2`, the positive stabilisation of `σ_1^2∈B_2`, so its closure is the `(2,2)`-torus link, i.e. the Hopf link (2 components, consistent with the half-integral powers of `t`); the `2`-braid representative `σ_1^2` of the same link gives the same value `V=-s(s^4+1)`, as Markov invariance requires.
- **Repair made before recording (2026-10-04).** The first written draft of the B examples contained three defects, all corrected before the readiness records were (re)written: (i) the 3-braid example's statement carried a literal `?` placeholder in its skein triple and identified the closure of `σ_1σ_2σ_1` with the trefoil; the closure is the Hopf link (conjugation to `σ_1^2σ_2` plus destabilisation to `σ_1^2`), and the two values `-s^5-s` and `-s^8+s^6+s^2` are different, so the identification was replaced by the correct one and a legitimate skein triple was supplied and verified; (ii) the same false cross-check sentence in `ex-the-jones-specialization-of-a-two-strand-closure` was replaced by the explicit warning that `σ_1σ_2σ_1` is not a trefoil representative; (iii) `cex-an-unnormalized-hecke-trace-is-not-markov-invariant` carried a discarded fragment (`·?`) in its strategy and an ambiguous hypothesis in its refuted statement, both replaced by the exact computation. The three affected readiness records were re-recorded with the updated dependency lists; the other 24 batch-6 records were verified unchanged (`step1-decisions check` shows no unresolved batch-6 item afterwards).

- **Non-descent of the trace to the TL quotient.** `tr_3(E^{(1)}_3T_1)=v+(3v-1)z+(v^2+2v-1)z^2`, which is `v(v^2+1)/(v+1)^2` at `z_0`, so the Ocneanu trace does not kill the TL ideal even though `tr_3(E^{(1)}_3)` vanishes; the item records this to prevent the false claim.
- **Burau–Alexander formula.** With the batch-5 convention `\bar\rho_2(σ_1)=-t` and `\bar\rho_3(σ_1)=(-t,t;0,1)`, `\bar\rho_3(σ_2)=(1,0;1,-t)`: I verified `(\bar\rho_3(σ_1σ_2))^3=t^3I_2`, `det(I-\bar\rho_3(σ_1σ_2)^2)=1+t^2+t^4`, and `(1-t)(1+t^2+t^4)/(1-t^3)=t^2-t+1`, i.e. the trefoil; and for `σ_1^m∈B_2` the values of the formula for `m=1,3,-3`.
