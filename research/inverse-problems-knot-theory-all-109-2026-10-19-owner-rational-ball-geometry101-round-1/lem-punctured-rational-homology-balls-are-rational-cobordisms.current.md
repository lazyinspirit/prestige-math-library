## Statement

Let $B$ be a compact connected oriented smooth four-manifold with $H_*(B;\mathbb Q)\cong H_*(\mathrm{point};\mathbb Q)$ and connected rational homology sphere boundary $Y$. Choose a smoothly embedded closed four-ball $D$ in its interior and put $W=B\setminus\operatorname{int}D$. With the induced orientation,
$$\partial W=-S^3\sqcup Y.$$
The manifold $W$ is connected, $H_j(W;\mathbb Q)$ is $\mathbb Q$ for $j=0,3$ and zero otherwise, and both inclusions $S^3\to W$, $Y\to W$ induce isomorphisms on rational homology in every degree. Its rational homological Euler characteristic is zero and its rational intersection module $H_2(W;\mathbb Q)$ is zero. Consequently its signature is zero, and the square of every covector with respect to that zero intersection module is zero. These assertions hold also for the reversed cobordism $-W$ from $Y$ to $S^3$, and use no choice principle.

The last square assertion refers to the intersection-form covector convention; the lemma supplies no Floer cobordism map, absolute grading formula, or Spin-c existence theorem.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The oriented compact $B$, its connected rational homology sphere boundary, and the embedded interior ball. Orient its $S^3$ boundary by the outward orientation of $D$, so the inner boundary of $W$ has its negative orientation.

[F1] [[thm-mayer-vietoris-sequence-in-singular-homology]] gives the exact homology sequence for a two-open-set cover with any abelian coefficient group, in particular $\mathbb Q$.

[F2] [[lem-fundamental-class-of-a-boundary-pushes-forward-to-zero]] gives $i_*[\partial W]=0$ in $H_3(W;\mathbb Q)$, with the actual induced boundary orientations and no choice assumption.

## Proof

1.1 Take collar thickenings of $W$ and $D$ inside $B$, so they form an open cover whose pieces retract onto $W,D$ and whose intersection retracts onto $S^3$. The collar here comes from the explicitly embedded smooth ball and its radial annulus. The radial contractions give these retractions and fix the common sphere. Apply [F1]. Since $D$ has the homology of a point and $B$ has the same rational homology, the sequence gives $H_j(W;\mathbb Q)=0$ for every $j>0$, $j\ne3$, and the actual inclusion $H_3(S^3;\mathbb Q)\to H_3(W;\mathbb Q)$ is an isomorphism. In detail the degree-three window is $0=H_4(B)\to H_3(S^3)\to H_3(W)\to H_3(B)=0$; degree four and all higher windows have zero neighboring terms, and degrees one and two do as well. [F1, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 The degree-zero window is $0\to\mathbb Q\to H_0(W;\mathbb Q)\oplus\mathbb Q\to\mathbb Q\to0$. Exactness implies $H_0(W;\mathbb Q)$ has dimension one: the last surjection is split by one nonzero component generator, and its kernel has dimension one by the first injection. Thus $W$ is connected, since a smooth manifold is locally path connected and $H_0$ is freely generated by its path components. Each boundary inclusion induces the identity between one-dimensional component groups in degree zero. Together with step 1.1 this proves the inclusion-isomorphism assertion for the inner $S^3$. [F1, step 1.1, construct, algebra]

3.1 The oriented boundary of $W$ is $-S^3\sqcup Y$, because the normal on its interior-ball boundary points into the deleted ball. By [F2], $i_{Y*}[Y]-i_{S^3*}[S^3]=0$. The second term is nonzero by step 1.1. Hence $H_3(Y;\mathbb Q)\to H_3(W;\mathbb Q)$ is nonzero between one-dimensional spaces and is an isomorphism. All other positive-degree groups of $Y$ vanish, and those of $W$ do as well by step 1.1. Degree zero was checked in step 2.1. Thus $Y\to W$ is a rational homology isomorphism in every degree, with its actual boundary sign accounted for. [F2, step 1.1, step 2.1, construct, algebra]

4.1 The rational Betti numbers are $b_0=b_3=1$ and all others zero, so their alternating sum is $1-1=0$. The rational intersection form is on the zero vector space $H_2(W;\mathbb Q)$; its signature is therefore zero. Its dual space also has only its zero covector, and that covector's square is zero under the unique zero-dimensional form inverse. Reversing orientation exchanges the incoming and outgoing boundaries, negates the intersection form and the oriented boundary relation, and leaves all rational homology groups and inclusion isomorphisms unchanged. This proves the reverse-cobordism assertions too. There is no nonempty zero-mode selection or infinite algebraic splitting here: every exact window has finite-dimensional stated terms and uses only finite linear algebra. No Spin-c or Floer assertion has entered the proof. [given, step 1.1, step 2.1, step 3.1, construct, algebra] ∎
