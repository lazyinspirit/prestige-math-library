## Cross-track evidence and exact boundaries

* **r1 finite Dirichlet-L verification.** Platt, *Numerical computations concerning the GRH*, arXiv:1305.3087v1, https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.3087. r1 read the full paper’s §§3–7; the local source snapshots are `analytic/r1-sources/platt-numerical-computations-grh-2016.pdf/.txt`. Theorem 7.1: primitive (qle400{,}000); even (q) use (T_q=max(10^8/q,75cdot10^6/q+200)), odd (q) use (T_q=max(10^8/q,37.5cdot10^6/q+200)). Helfgott’s q-ranges are subranges; for odd q≤300,000, (10^8/q) dominates (37.5cdot10^6/q+200). This is a finite verification input, not an unproved GRH premise, but r1 reports the machine-readable certificate/replay output is still absent. The generic certificate A page and the finite-verification A page are distinct planned suppliers.
* **r2 prime distribution.** Planned A page `prime-distribution-in-arithmetic-progressions`, item `thm-siegel-walfisz-prime-distribution`, supplies the uniform progression mean for the slowly growing Green–Tao W-trick and the finite-complexity prime-pattern argument. Its planned Bombieri–Vinogradov theorem is not a dependency of the Green–Tao 2008 AP proof. Helfgott’s major arcs use his explicit formula for the smoothed Dirichlet-twisted von Mangoldt sum plus Platt’s finite Dirichlet-L zero verification (Helfgott, *Major arcs*, §1.2, equation (1.9), and §4.6, especially pp. 5–6 and 56–57). They do not invoke Siegel–Walfisz, Bombieri–Vinogradov, or the fixed-modulus PNT-AP result. Do not create any of those as a Helfgott page dependency.
* **ant_r4 finite-field proof prerequisites.** Planned pages/items from the algebraic/étale track are `constructible-etale-sheaves-and-lisse-sheaves` (`def-constructible-l-adic-sheaf-on-a-scheme`, `def-lisse-l-adic-sheaf-on-a-scheme`, `def-middle-extension-l-adic-sheaf-on-a-curve`, `thm-lisse-l-adic-sheaves-and-continuous-pi1-representations`, `def-frobenius-trace-function-of-a-lisse-sheaf`); `compactly-supported-etale-cohomology-and-trace-formula` (`def-compactly-supported-etale-cohomology`, `thm-grothendieck-lefschetz-trace-formula-for-constructible-l-adic-sheaves`); `swan-conductors-and-grothendieck-ogg-shafarevich` (`def-swan-conductor-of-an-l-adic-inertia-representation`, `def-conductor-of-a-middle-extension-sheaf-on-a-curve`, `thm-grothendieck-ogg-shafarevich-euler-characteristic-formula`, `lem-gos-conductor-complexity-bound-on-the-affine-line`); and `deligne-weights-and-finite-field-trace-bounds` (`thm-deligne-weight-bound-for-compactly-supported-cohomology`, `thm-deligne-square-root-trace-bound-for-curves`, `thm-geometric-invariants-control-compact-support-degree-two`). They are planned interfaces and not yet local proof closure. Global Galois-representation suppliers do not replace these sheaf/cohomology hypotheses.

## Source-reading limits that affect the build

1. The prime-AP and prime-pattern proofs import finite Szemerédi, inverse Gowers, nilmanifold factorization, Möbius/Type I–II cancellation, and a Goldston–Yıldırım sieve. The source papers are complete locally, but not every long technical proof has been reconstructed into local items.
2. The BDG decoupling proof needs real Fourier/weighted-Lp theory, multilinear Kakeya/Brascamp–Lieb, ball inflation, rescaling, and an induction-on-scales iteration. The finite DFT pages do not supply these.
3. The Deligne/FKM trace theorem needs generic étale sheaves, Frobenius trace formula, the (H_c^2) geometric-invariant criterion, Deligne’s weight estimate, and the GOS conductor bound. All edges are explicit in the fragment; a current Galois representation page is not a substitute.
4. Helfgott’s ternary Goldbach endpoint needs two distinct finite inputs: replayable Platt character-L zero certificates for the major-arc estimates and a prime-gap/exhaustive certificate below (10^{27}). Neither is present as local replay evidence. The source’s analytic range may be stated as a source result, but the full theorem is not a locally proof-closed supplier until both finite inputs are represented.
5. The binary Goldbach conjecture remains an `op-` item and has no consumer. Neither the ternary theorem nor any finite verification supplies it.
