### Milne (2017)

- Read completely, include: Ch. 2, “Elliptic Modular Curves as Riemann
  Surfaces,” printed pp. 25–39; Ch. 4, “Modular Functions and Modular Forms,”
  pp. 48–66; Ch. 5, “Hecke Operators,” pp. 67–86.
- Read completely, partial include: §9, “Modular Forms, Dirichlet Series, and
  Functional Equations,” pp. 101–104; use the stated Hecke–Weil L-function
  contracts only. Its converse theorem proof is explicitly omitted, so defer
  it.
- Defer/not claimed: Introduction/Ch. 1 (general preliminaries), Ch. 3
  (elliptic functions), Chs. 6–8 (modular equation, canonical model, moduli),
  Ch. 10 (Eichler–Shimura), Ch. 11 (curve zeta functions), and Ch. 12 (CM).
  Ch. 5's cited Ogg newform details §§5.33–5.37 are not suppliers here.

  cusp forms, Hecke operators, adelic forms); §§5.1–5.8 (induced
  representations, Eisenstein definitions, Fourier expansion/meromorphic
  continuation); §§6.1–6.4 (spectral decomposition/kernel); §§7.1–7.7
