## Heading map and extraction

- **§1, printed pp. 391–393:** Dedekind zeta functional equation and the relation of values at positive integers to K-groups/regulators. Borel’s rank statement is summarized: for (i>1), (K_i(F)) and (K_i(\mathcal O_F)) are finite up to the rank (n_+=r_1+r_2) when (i=2m-1) and ((-1)^m=+1), and rank (n_-=r_2) when ((-1)^m=-1); the Borel regulator lattice covolume is rationally proportional to a normalized Dedekind-zeta value. The section motivates explicit polylogarithm formulas as a refinement of Borel’s theorem.
- **§2, printed pp. 394–396:** The Bloch–Wigner dilogarithm (D(x)) is defined by (\operatorname{Im}(\operatorname{Li}_2(x)+\log|x|\log(1-x))) for (|x|\le1) and inversion elsewhere. It satisfies the six-term symmetries and Spence–Abel five-term relation. The Bloch group is (B_2(F)=A(F)/C(F)), where (A(F)=\ker([x]\mapsto[x]\wedge[1-x])) in the free group on (\mathbb P^1(F)) with ([0]=[\infty]=0), and (C(F)) is generated by five-term relations. The map induced by (D) on (B_2(F)) is the Borel-regulator map for (K_3(F)) up to finite kernel/cokernel (Bloch–Suslin).
- **§§3–6, printed pp. 396–410:** The modified trilogarithm, its functional equations, the successive algebraic and functional-equation conditions on the trilogarithm Bloch group, and the numerical examples are read as context for the general construction. The source states that Goncharov’s weight-three construction relates the Borel regulator/K-groups to the trilogarithm for number fields. The specific §6 formulas give the clean functional-equation relations used to define the quotient in higher weights.
- **§7, printed pp. 410–414:** Higher modified polylogarithms are defined by the source’s (D_m), regularized ~(D_m), and (P_m) combinations of \(\operatorname{Li}_j\), powers of \(\log|x|\), and Bernoulli numbers. The section proves a specialization-invariance proposition: a formal combination satisfying the stated tensor relation has constant sum of the modified polylogarithm values. This supplies the functional-equation mechanism for the higher Bloch quotient.
- **§8, printed pp. 415–418:** The higher Bloch groups are recursively defined from the free group on \(\mathbb P^1(F)\), the maps \(\beta_m([x])=[x]^{m-2}\otimes([x]\wedge[1-x])\), and the subgroup generated by specializations of the modified-polylogarithm functional equations; the final quotient is \(\mathcal B_m(F)=\mathcal A_m(F)/\mathcal C_m(F)\). The higher regulator is the sum of the modified \(\mathcal L_m\) values over the prescribed real/complex embeddings. The exact source main-conjecture formulation asks for a canonical map from \(\mathcal B_m(F)\) to \(K_{2m-1}(F)\) with finite kernel and cokernel, compatible with the Borel regulator, and predicts a lattice with covolume a rational multiple of \(\sqrt{|D_F|}\,\zeta_F(m)/\pi^{m n_\pm}\). This is conjectural, not a theorem supplier.
- **§10C, printed p. 427:** The Artin-\(L\)-function extension is described only as expected; the article explicitly does not give the correct detailed formulation and refers to unpublished Gross. This branch is excluded from R5’s theorem inventory and recorded as deferred pending a published, precise formulation and source.
- **§11, printed pp. 428–429:** The historical status at publication is recorded: Goncharov’s trilogarithm result proves the weight-three case; Deligne–Beilinson give a motivic refinement; Beilinson constructs maps compatible with regulator; general higher weights remain a conjectural program. The source’s historical discussion is not used as a 2026 status claim; current weight-four/weight-five status is instead checked against the full Charlton–Gangl–Radchenko author manuscript below.

## Exact formulas used in the scaffold

For (m\ge2), (d_m=r_2) if (m) is even and (d_m=r_1+r_2) if (m) is odd. The single-valued classical polylogarithm in the later full-text formulation is

\[
\mathcal L_m(z)=\operatorname{Re}_m\!\left(\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}\frac{2^jB_j}{j!}\operatorname{Li}_{m-j}(z)\log^j|z|\right),
\]

where the real/imaginary part is selected by parity. The actual group and normalization used in the theorem plan must follow Zagier §8's recursively defined \(\mathcal A_m/\mathcal C_m\) and main-conjecture convention; the displayed formula is not a substitute for those relations.
