## Access and proof gates

- Milne CFT Ch. VI §2, Theorem 2.4(a), reduces the Dedekind-zeta simple-pole proof to the partial-zeta lattice count in VI Proposition 2.8 and cites Serge Lang, *Algebraic Number Theory* (1970), Ch. VI §3, Theorem 3. The Internet Archive/Open Library record `algebraicnumbert00lang` returned HTTP 403 (“Item not available”) for the full text on 2026-10-23, so Lang was not read. R1 independently read the complete proof in Neukirch, *Algebraic Number Theory*, VII §5, printed pp. 457–468, Thm. 5.9 and Cor. 5.10–5.11; it proves meromorphic continuation, functional equation, simple pole/residue, and the analytic class-number formula from earlier lattice/Poisson results without citing Lang. Thus the zeta-pole proof gate is closed by this separately attributed supplier; the Lang acquisition limitation remains factual provenance, not an outstanding proof gap.
- Milne CFT VI §2, Theorem 2.4(b), says the general ray-class Hecke L(1, chi) nonvanishing proof uses the global existence theorem; Theorem 2.5 says the number-field proof is “the same analysis as in the case K=Q” and omits the details. R1 read the full alternative proof in Neukirch VII §13.3 Lemma 13.3 (nonvanishing) and §13.2 Thm. 13.2 (ray-prime density). AN's one-way edges are global CFT existence → Hecke nonvanishing → ray-prime density. R2 global CFT uses Milne VII §§4 and 6 and has no edge back to AN analytics.
- Milne CFT V.3 Theorem 3.27 states the conductor–discriminant formula and omits its proof. The full proof is read in Neukirch VII §11 Thm. 11.9; the planned item must reproduce it with the existing Brauer-induction result and exact ramification/different prerequisites.
- Milne CFT I §4 cites Serre, *Local Fields* (1962), IV.3 Prop. 14 for upper-numbering quotient compatibility and V.7 for Hasse–Arf. Their proofs were read in Neukirch II §10 Prop. 10.9 and V §6 Prop. 6.3, so Serre is not a remaining proof gate for these results.
- Milne CFT III restricts its local existence proof to characteristic zero; ADT Appendix A.6 and Yoon §2 Thm. 3 state/cite the equal-characteristic local axiom without its proof. The full finite-residue proof is now read in Fesenko–Vostokov IV §6 Thm. 6.2, printed pp. 155–156, with internal prerequisites in Ch. I §§1–9, Ch. III §§1–2, and Ch. IV §§1–6 read in full. It covers complete discretely valued fields with finite residue field in characteristic p. Fesenko Ch. V §4 does not prove general open-subgroup existence for infinite perfect residue fields (it refers to [Fe6]); Ch. V §5.1 proves only its stated cyclic totally ramified p-extension case and leaves the general norm-kernel question open.
- The global function-field existence gate remains separate: ADT proves global reciprocity/class formation but omits existence; Yoon §3.3.1 cites rather than proves nonsplitting-place infinitude and §3.2 assumes a rational place. The local norm-subgroup supplier is no longer gated. The remaining proof obligations are the global nonsplitting-place lemma, the first/second norm-index proofs, and the open-subgroup existence theorem, with the degree-one virtual divisor supplied by the Pic1/Lang/finite-field Brauer chain.
- Fesenko–Vostokov VII.4 states the corrected p=2 Vostokov formula, but the pairing properties and uniformizer-independence proof are assigned to Exercises VII.2.2–4. Keep that p=2 formula as an established-result proof gate; the odd-p formula is fully proved in VII §§2–4.
- Fesenko–Vostokov VII.5.5 displays a higher-dimensional symbol but does not prove the higher reciprocity identification; IX.4.13 only sketches higher local CFT and cites Parshin, Kato, and [FK]. The source's Ch. I, III–VII and IX read ranges are not enough to claim general d-local CFT or higher-dimensional duality, and no claim about open versus solved status is made.
- Milne ANT VIII Prop. 8.2 supports nonarchimedean finite-place completion decomposition only after its local extension-of-absolute-value input. Milne VII Thm. 7.38 treats discrete bases; Remark 7.39 says the broader extension result needed in VIII is omitted. The shared adelic tensor item must route number/function-field finite places through the exact discrete-place suppliers, and the archimedean R/C factor decomposition must be proved separately. The R2 read does not attribute that archimedean proof to Milne.
- Milne ANT VII Thm. 7.33 and Remark 7.37 are the read Hensel-factor lifting source. The proposed R2 algorithm fixes finite input modulo p^N, exactly N-1 correction stages, and a separate internal correctness proof; it does not use an arbitrary Qp oracle.
