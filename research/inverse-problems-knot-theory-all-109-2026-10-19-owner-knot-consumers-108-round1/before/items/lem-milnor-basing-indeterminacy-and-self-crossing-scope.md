## Statement

Assume AC. Let $L$ be a nonempty labeled oriented link in $S^3$, let
$I=(i_1,\ldots,i_k,j)$ have length at least two, and let $b,b'$ be any two
based meridian-longitude word systems used in the preceding definition. Then
the based residue candidates $\bar\mu_L^b(I)$ and $\bar\mu_L^{b'}(I)$ agree
under the canonical identification of their residue groups. If $L$ and $L'$ are
related by a finite sequence of self-crossing changes, the same residue is
preserved whenever the entries of $I$ are pairwise distinct. No invariance for
repeated-index sequences under self-crossing changes is asserted.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** Full AC, the closed-link peripheral system, and finite based word-lift data through the length of $I$.

[A1] Full AC, written AC, is [[def-axiom-of-choice]].

[F1] Integer coefficients, based residue candidates, and the gcd of proper subsequences and their cyclic permutations are defined by the preceding definition. [[def-milnor-integer-and-residue-invariants]]

[F2] Conjugating one longitude changes a degree-$m$ coefficient by an integer combination of coefficients on proper contiguous subwords. [[lem-magnus-conjugation-coefficients-use-shorter-subwords]]

[F3] The side-based meridian relation at a crossing is fixed by the local crossing chart. [[lem-link-wirtinger-local-meridian-conjugacy]]

[F4] Closed-link meridians lift to every finite nilpotent quotient, and the link group is computed by the crossing relations. [[lem-link-nilpotent-quotient-generated-by-component-meridians]], [[thm-link-wirtinger-presentation-correctness]]

## Proof

**Given:** The chosen link, based systems, and multi-index $I$.

1.1 Consider a change that conjugates the chosen longitude lift while keeping the meridian variables fixed. By [F2], its degree-$k$ coefficient changes by a sum of products of integer Magnus coefficients indexed by proper subsequences of $(i_1,\ldots,i_k,j)$. Every such coefficient is among the generators of the integer ideal whose nonnegative generator is $\Delta_I^b$ in [F1]. Hence this type of conjugation preserves the residue modulo $\Delta_I^b$. [F1, F2, given]

1.2 A general change of basing also changes the chosen meridian generators by simultaneous conjugations and changes the free word lifts by elements in the kernel of $F_r/\Gamma_{|I|}F_r\to G/\Gamma_{|I|}G$. The current local facts [F3]–[F4] do not determine the coefficient changes of these two operations, nor prove that their combined changes lie in the ideal generated by every proper-subsequence coefficient and its cyclic permutations. This is the unresolved part of the closed-link indeterminacy claim under the stated axiom branch [A1]. [A1, F1, F3, F4, step 1.1]

2.1 For a self-crossing change, [F3] describes the meridian conjugation at the changed crossing. To infer invariance for pairwise-distinct indices one must also derive the exact change in each preferred longitude word and show that every resulting Magnus monomial repeats the crossing component's index. That derivation is not supplied by [F3] alone; it remains unresolved here. [F1, F3, given]
