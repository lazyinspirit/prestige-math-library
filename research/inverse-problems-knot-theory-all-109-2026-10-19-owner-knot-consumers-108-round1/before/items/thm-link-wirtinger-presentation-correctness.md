## Statement

Assume countable choice.

Let $D$ be a finite regular oriented diagram of a nonempty labeled link, after
inserting a small type-I curl on any component that has no underpassing point.
Let $x_1,\ldots,x_n$ be the positive meridians of the strands between
successive underpassing points. At a crossing $c$, write $o(c)$ for the
over-strand and $s(c),t(c)$ for the source and target under-sides defined using
the positive in-plane normal to the over-strand. Set
$$r_c=x_{o(c)}^{-1}x_{s(c)}x_{o(c)}x_{t(c)}^{-1}.$$
Then the assignment of each generator to its based positive meridian induces
an isomorphism
$$
\langle x_1,\ldots,x_n\mid r_c\ (c\text{ a crossing})\rangle
\cong \pi_1(E_L).
$$
Any one crossing relator may be omitted without changing the represented
group. The theorem makes no claim that all relators are independent.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The link diagram, its labels, crossing conventions, and common based meridian paths.

[A1] Countable choice, written $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$, is [[def-countable-choice]].

[F1] The complement has an open cover whose upper piece is generated freely by the strand meridians, whose lower piece is contractible, and whose overlap is a punctured sphere with crossing circles normally generating its fundamental group even after one is omitted. [[lem-link-wirtinger-crossing-ball-cover]], [[lem-link-diagram-upper-region-free-meridians]]

[F2] The Seifert–van Kampen theorem identifies the fundamental group of a union with the pushout of the two inclusion maps from a path-connected overlap. [[thm-seifert-van-kampen]], [[thm-group-pushout-as-an-amalgamated-quotient]]

[F3] Under the upper-region meridian basis, the image of a small puncture circle at crossing $c$ is conjugate to $r_c$ or $r_c^{-1}$; these have the same normal closure. [[lem-link-wirtinger-local-meridian-conjugacy]]

[F4] A group presentation is the quotient of the free group on the stated generators by the normal closure of the stated relators. [[def-group-presentation]], [[def-free-group]]

[F5] A regular link diagram whose components have underpasses has an ambient-isotopic height realization with every underpass at height $-1$ and all other points above $-1$. [[lem-regular-link-diagram-admits-normalized-height-realization]]

## Proof

1.1 If a component has no underpassing point, insert a small type-I curl in a disk meeting only that component. The local curl is the boundary of a disk pushed into a ball disjoint from the rest of the link, so removing it by the reverse isotopy identifies the resulting link with the original. After this finite operation each component has an underpass. Apply [F5] to obtain the normalized height model required by the cover supplier; the ambient isotopy transports the based exterior group and meridians. [F5, given, construct]

1.2 By [A1, F1], the upper-region group is free on $x_1,\ldots,x_n$, the lower-region group is trivial, and the overlap group is normally generated by the crossing circles $c_1,\ldots,c_n$. Applying [F2] to the open cover, the pushout with a trivial lower group is the quotient of the upper group by the normal closure of the images of these circles. [A1, F1, F2, step 1.1]

1.3 By [F3], the normal closure in the upper group of the images of the crossing circles is the normal closure of the relators $r_c$. By [F4], the pushout quotient is therefore the stated Wirtinger presentation, and its generator map is the based positive-meridian map. [F3, F4, step 1.2]

2.1 The cover lemma also states that any $n-1$ of the crossing circles normally generate the overlap group. Replacing the full list by any list omitting one circle therefore gives the same normal closure in the upper group and the same quotient from step 1.3. [F1, step 1.3] ∎
