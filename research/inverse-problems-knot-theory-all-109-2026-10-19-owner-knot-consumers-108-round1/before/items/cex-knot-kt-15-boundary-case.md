## Statement refuted

Assume the Axiom of Choice. The false assertion is that the abelianized peripheral system $\bigl(H_1(E_K;\mathbb Z),[\mu],[\lambda]\bigr)$ determines the ambient-equivalence class of every smooth knot $K\subset S^3$, where $[\mu]$ is the positive meridian class and $[\lambda]$ is the preferred longitude class.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The crossing-free unknot diagram, the standard three-crossing trefoil, their compact exteriors and peripheral curves.

[A1] Full AC is the assertion that every set-indexed family of nonempty sets has a choice function. [[def-axiom-of-choice]]

[F1] The Wirtinger presentation uses one positive-meridian generator for each under-arc and one crossing conjugacy relation for each crossing, and identifies the presented group with the complement fundamental group. [[thm-knot-wirtinger-presentation-correctness]]

[F2] The trefoil group is nonabelian and its abelianization is $\mathbb Z$. [[lem-trefoil-knot-group-is-nonabelian-with-abelianization-z]]

[F3] For every smooth knot, $H_1(E_K;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z$ is generated by $[\mu]$, and the preferred longitude satisfies $[\lambda]=0$. [[thm-knot-exterior-homology-and-longitude]]

[F4] Ambient equivalence is given by an ambient isotopy of $S^3$, whose final diffeomorphism restricts to a homeomorphism of knot complements. [[def-oriented-link-in-s-three-and-ambient-isotopy]]

[F5] Based loop classes form a group under concatenation. A homeomorphism sends loops and endpoint-fixed homotopies to loops and endpoint-fixed homotopies, preserves concatenation, and its inverse induces the inverse group map. [[def-based-loops-and-fundamental-group]] [[thm-fundamental-group-laws]]

## Counterexample

1.1 The crossing-free unknot diagram has one under-arc and no crossings. By [F1] its group presentation has one generator and no relators, so every reduced word is a power of that generator and $\pi_1(S^3\setminus U)\cong\mathbb Z$. By [F2] the trefoil group is nonabelian, hence it is not isomorphic to the unknot group. If $U$ and $T$ were ambient equivalent, [F4] and [F5] would give an isomorphism of their complement groups. Therefore they are not ambient equivalent. [given, F1, F2, F4, F5, algebra]

2.1 Under [A1], the homology supplier [F3] applies to both knots. Each abelianized peripheral system has $H_1\cong\mathbb Z$ with the meridian as generator and preferred longitude zero. The isomorphism sending $[\mu_U]$ to $[\mu_T]$ therefore sends the complete decorated triple $(H_1,[\mu],[\lambda])$ for $U$ to that for $T$. The systems agree although the knots do not, refuting the assertion. [A1, F3, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
