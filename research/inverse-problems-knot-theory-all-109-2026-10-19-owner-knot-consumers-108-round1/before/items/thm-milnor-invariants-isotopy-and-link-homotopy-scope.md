## Statement

Assume AC. Every integer Milnor coefficient of a based string link is invariant
under smooth isotopy through string links fixing the endpoint collars. For
closed labeled oriented links, the residue candidate $\bar\mu_L(I)$ is invariant
under ambient isotopy. A link-homotopy is a finite sequence of ambient isotopies
and local crossing changes in which the two branches at every crossing change
belong to the same component. Under link-homotopy, $\bar\mu_L(I)$ is invariant
when the entries of $I$ are pairwise distinct. No invariance for repeated-index
sequences under link-homotopy is asserted.

## Proof

**Given:** The isotopy or link-homotopy described in the statement.

1.1 Under the stated axiom branch [A1], a string-link isotopy induces a homeomorphism of exteriors carrying each bottom meridian and based longitude to its corresponding class. By naturality of the end inclusion, it carries the unique preimage of the longitude in $F_r/\Gamma_{k+1}$ to the corresponding preimage for the isotopic string link. The finite Magnus expansion is a group homomorphism on that quotient, so every coefficient of degree at most $k$ agrees. This proves the string-link assertion. [A1, F1, given]

2.1 Under [A1], an ambient isotopy of closed links induces the peripheral correspondence in [F3]. Applying that correspondence to the based word-lift data gives the transported based residue candidate. If [F2] holds, the candidate is independent of the basing and lift choices, so the two closed-link residues agree. The supplied item [F2] is not yet proof-closed; this step therefore remains conditional. [A1, F2, F3, step 1.1]

3.1 Under [A1], a link-homotopy is generated by ambient isotopies and self-crossing changes. For pairwise-distinct indices, ambient isotopy is handled by step 2.1 and each self-crossing change is the second assertion of [F2]. Since [F2] is not yet proof-closed, this implication remains conditional and no repeated-index conclusion is drawn. [A1, F2, step 2.1]
