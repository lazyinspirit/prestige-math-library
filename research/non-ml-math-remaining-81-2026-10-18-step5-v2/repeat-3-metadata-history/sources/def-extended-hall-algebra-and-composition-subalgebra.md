## Definition

Let $\mathcal A$ be a finite-field hereditary Hall category and let
$K_0=K_0(\mathcal A)$. Put
$([\alpha],[\beta])=\langle\alpha,\beta\rangle+
\langle\beta,\alpha\rangle$. The **extended Hall algebra** is the vector
space $\widetilde H=\mathbb Q(\nu)[K_0]\otimes H_{\mathcal A,\nu}$ with
basis $k_\alpha[M]$ and multiplication
$$ (k_\alpha[M])(k_\beta[N]) =\nu^{-(\beta,[M])}k_{\alpha+\beta}([M]\star[N]). $$
The notation is left-torus normal order: $k_\alpha[M]$ is the actual product of the torus and Hall elements. Thus moving $k_\beta$ past $[M]$ contributes $\nu^{-(\beta,[M])}$. Equivalently, $k_\alpha k_\beta=k_{\alpha+\beta}$ and
$k_\alpha[M]k_\alpha^{-1}=\nu^{(\alpha,[M])}[M]$. Associativity follows
from the bilinearity of the symmetric Euler form and the associative Hall
product; the toral action is checked in
[[lem-extended-hall-torus-action-is-well-defined]].

If $\mathcal A$ has a specified set of simple objects $\{S_i\}$, the
**composition subalgebra** is the subalgebra of $H_{\mathcal A,\nu}$
generated by $[S_i]$. Its extension by all $k_\alpha$ is the **extended
composition subalgebra**.
