## Remark

This page proves the type-A1 DAHA presentation and its polynomial representation, but it proves no homomorphism from a specified skein algebra into DAHA, no knot invariant parameter map, and no physical knot-model operator theorem. A future interface claim must name the source algebra, coefficient and parameter map, generator images, representation space or completion, and verify every defining relation. A physical application also needs its state space, operators, boundary conditions, and model hypotheses. The type-A affine Bernstein PBW theorem and this polynomial representation do not supply those data.
