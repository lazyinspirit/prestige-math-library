## Statement

Let $n\ge2$ and let $V=\mathbb R^n$ have its positive-definite inner product. Give $S^{n-1}=\{v:Q(v)=1\}$ the subspace topology and let

$$p:\operatorname{Spin}_{\mathrm{full}}(n)\to S^{n-1},\qquad p(x)=\rho_x(e_n).$$

Then $p$ is onto, the stabilizer of $e_n$ is the embedded $\operatorname{Spin}_{\mathrm{full}}(n-1)$ generated by even products from $e_n^\perp$, and $p$ is a locally trivial right $\operatorname{Spin}_{\mathrm{full}}(n-1)$ bundle. For unit $a$, write $U_a=\{v\in S^{n-1}:B(v,a)>-1\}$. For $v\in U_a$, the rotor

$$s_a(v)=\frac{1+va}{\sqrt{2(1+B(v,a))}}$$

satisfies $\rho_{s_a(v)}(a)=v$; in particular, $s_{e_n}$ is a local section of $p$ on $U_{e_n}$. Translating this section by Spin gives local sections of $p$ on the caps $U_a$. The bundle admits a finite partition of unity whose functions have closed supports contained in the corresponding local chart domains.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** Positive-definite $V=\mathbb R^n$, $n\ge2$, the Clifford convention $v^2=Q(v)$, and the group maps already defined.

[F1] The twisted action sends normalized vectors to reflections, is a group homomorphism on Pin, and full-even Spin maps onto $SO(n)$ ([[lem-twisted-clifford-adjoint-reflection-and-scalar-kernel]], [[thm-real-pin-spin-image-kernel-and-compactness]]).

[F2] Full-even Spin consists of even products of unit vectors and has scalar kernel $\{1,-1\}$ ([[lem-normalized-pin-spin-product-and-norm-laws]], [[thm-real-pin-spin-image-kernel-and-compactness]]).

[F3] Orthogonal maps of a finite-dimensional real quadratic space factor into reflections ([[lem-cartan-dieudonne-real-nondegenerate-reflection-factorization]]).

## Proof


**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 The group $SO(n)$ acts transitively on $S^{n-1}$: for $a,v$ unit, a rotation in their plane sends $a$ to $v$; when $v=-a$, a half-turn in a plane containing $a$ does so, which exists because $n\ge2$. By [F1], each such rotation has a Spin lift, so $p$ is onto. [F1, construct]

2.1 If $x\in\operatorname{Spin}_{\mathrm{full}}(n)$ fixes $e_n$, then $\rho_x$ restricts to an element of $SO(e_n^\perp)$. By [F3] it is a product of an even number of reflections with normals in $e_n^\perp$; lifting these gives $y\in\operatorname{Spin}_{\mathrm{full}}(n-1)$ with $\rho_y=\rho_x$. Then $xy^{-1}$ lies in the scalar kernel [F2], so $x=\pm y$. Since $n\ge2$, choose a unit vector $u\in e_n^\perp$; the even product $u(-u)=-1$ lies in the embedded $\operatorname{Spin}_{\mathrm{full}}(n-1)$. Therefore both $y$ and $-y$ belong to that subgroup. Conversely each vector in $e_n^\perp$ fixes $e_n$ under its reflection, so each even product from that subspace fixes $e_n$. This identifies the stabilizer. [F1, F2, F3, step 1.1, construct]

2.2 For unit $a,v$ with $B(v,a)>-1$, put $u=(v+a)/\sqrt{2(1+B(v,a))}$. Then $Q(u)=1$ and $s_a(v)=ua=(1+va)/\sqrt{2(1+B(v,a))}$ is a product of two unit vectors, so lies in Spin. Directly, $s_a(v)a=(a+v)/\sqrt{2(1+B(v,a))}=v s_a(v)$, giving $\rho_{s_a(v)}(a)=v$; the formula is continuous on $U_a$. For the bundle map $p=p_{e_n}$, this gives the section $s_{e_n}$ on $U_{e_n}$. For any unit $a$, choose $g_a\in Spin(n)$ with $\rho_{g_a}(e_n)=a$, which exists by step 1.1 and [F1]. Since $\rho_{g_a^{-1}}v\in U_{e_n}$ exactly when $v\in U_a$, the translated formula $\sigma_a(v)=g_a s_{e_n}(\rho_{g_a^{-1}}v)$ is defined continuously on $U_a$ and satisfies $p(\sigma_a(v))=v$. Thus every cap $U_a$ is a chart domain for $p$. [F1, F2, step 1.1, construct]

3.1 For a section $s:U\to Spin(n)$, the map $p^{-1}(U)\to U\times Spin(n-1)$ given by $x\mapsto(p(x),s(p(x))^{-1}x)$ is continuous, and its second coordinate fixes $e_n$ by the group action. Its inverse is $(v,h)\mapsto s(v)h$, so the chart is a homeomorphism commuting with projection. Right multiplication by the stabilizer is free and transitive on each fiber, proving the asserted local bundle structure. [step 2.1, step 2.2, algebra]

4.1 The caps $U_a$ cover the sphere. For each point choose a smaller metric ball of radius $r$ whose closed ball of radius $2r$ lies in one such cap; compactness of $S^{n-1}$ gives finitely many smaller balls covering it. For each selected center $c_j$, define $\psi_j(v)=\max(0,1-d(v,c_j)/(2r_j))$. These continuous functions have supports in their chart caps and their sum is positive because the smaller balls cover. Then $\phi_j=\psi_j/(\sum_i\psi_i)$ form a finite partition of unity with closed supports inside the corresponding charts. This is the numerability data used in the universal-cover proof. [step 2.2, step 3.1, construct] ∎

## Remarks

The local rotor formula is checked by multiplication; the bundle and its explicit charts are not inferred from a diagram or from a citation-only homogeneous-space assertion.
