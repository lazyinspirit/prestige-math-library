## Example

Let $\mathfrak a=\mathbb C e$ be the one-dimensional odd subalgebra spanned by $e$ inside the $\mathfrak{gl}(1|1)$ of [[thm-gl-1-1-is-a-lie-superalgebra]]. Its bracket is zero because $[e,e]=0$. Then $U(\mathfrak a)$ is the two-dimensional exterior algebra $\mathbb C\oplus\mathbb C e$, with $e^2=0$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The one-dimensional graded subspace $\mathfrak a=\mathbb C e$ and the enveloping-superalgebra convention.

[F1] [[def-gl-1-1-bracket-data]]: $e$ is odd and $[e,e]=0$.

[F2] [[thm-gl-1-1-is-a-lie-superalgebra]]: the bracketed graded space is a Lie superalgebra.

[F3] [[def-universal-enveloping-superalgebra]]: the quotient is $T(\mathfrak a)/I$, with the relation $\iota_{\mathfrak a}(e)^2=\iota_{\mathfrak a}([e,e])/2$ for odd $e$.

[F4] [[thm-super-poincare-birkhoff-witt-ordered-monomial-basis]]: for an ordered homogeneous basis with one odd basis vector $e$, the ordered monomials $1,e$ form a basis.

## Verification

**Proof technique:** direct.


1.1 By [F3] and [F1], the defining relation is $\iota_{\mathfrak a}(e)^2=0$; the tensor quotient is generated by $1$ and $\iota_{\mathfrak a}(e)$, so every word reduces to a linear combination of them. [F1, F2, F3, algebra]


2.1 By [F4], $1,e$ are linearly independent in the quotient and span it, so $U(\mathfrak a)=\mathbb C\oplus\mathbb C e$ as a vector space with the stated multiplication. [F3, F4, step 1.1] ∎
