## Definition

Assume the Axiom of Choice, inherited from the relative Proj construction used
by the blowup ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]). Let $X$ be an integral scheme
([[def-integral-scheme]]), let $\mathcal I\subseteq\mathcal O_X$ be a
quasi-coherent ideal sheaf of finite type, and let $J\subseteq\mathcal K_X$ be
an invertible ideal sheaf ([[def-invertible-sheaf]]): for example the
invertible ideal sheaf $\mathcal O_X(-D)$ of an effective Cartier divisor $D$
([[def-cartier-divisor]]), or more generally an invertible fractional ideal,
i.e. an invertible subsheaf of the sheaf of rational functions on $X$
([[def-rational-section-line-bundle]]). Write $J\mathcal I\subseteq\mathcal
O_X$ for the product ideal sheaf, the image of the multiplication
$J\otimes_{\mathcal O_X}\mathcal I\to\mathcal O_X$.

The **canonical rescaling identification**
$$\rho_{\mathcal I,J}\colon\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}X\longrightarrow\operatorname{Bl}_{J\mathcal I}X$$
of the blowups of [[def-blowup-scheme-along-ideal]] is defined by the
following local data. Let $U=\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq X$ be an affine
open on which $J|_U=(g)$ is generated by one element. Since $X$ is integral,
$A$ is a domain; $g\ne0$ (an invertible sheaf on a nonempty scheme is nonzero)
and $g$ is a nonzerodivisor of $A$, and
$J\mathcal I|_U=gI$ for the ideal $I=\Gamma(U,\mathcal I)\subseteq A$. For
every $n\ge0$ multiplication by $g^n$ is an isomorphism of $A$-modules
$$I^n\longrightarrow g^nI^n=(gI)^n,\qquad x\longmapsto g^nx,$$
compatible with the multiplications $I^m\times I^n\to I^{m+n}$ and
$(gI)^m\times(gI)^n\to(gI)^{m+n}$, hence defines a graded $A$-algebra
isomorphism
$$\varphi_U\colon R(I)=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}I^n\;\longrightarrow\;R(gI)=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}(gI)^n,\qquad x\longmapsto g^nx\ \ (x\in I^n),$$
and on relative Proj over $U$ an isomorphism of $U$-schemes
$\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}U\to\operatorname{Bl}_{J\mathcal I}U$
([[def-rees-algebra-ideal-sheaf]]).

The definition is independent of the chosen equation: if $g'$ generates
$J|_U$ then $g'=ug$ for a unit $u\in A^\times$, and the corresponding graded
isomorphism $\varphi_U'$ differs from $\varphi_U$ by the graded automorphism of
$R(gI)$ that multiplies the degree-$n$ piece by $u^n$; that automorphism is
induced by the unit $u$ of the degree-zero part and acts as the identity on
$\operatorname{Proj}$, so $\varphi_U$ and $\varphi_U'$ induce the same
isomorphism of blowups. On an overlap $U\cap V$ with $g_V=ug_U$ the two local
isomorphisms likewise induce the same isomorphism after restriction, since the
degree-zero identification is multiplication by the unit $u^n$ on localizations
at homogeneous elements of positive degree. The local isomorphisms therefore
glue over an affine cover of $X$ to the single canonical isomorphism
$\rho_{\mathcal I,J}$; it is an isomorphism of $X$-schemes and is compatible
with the structural morphisms and with restriction to open subschemes of $X$
([[lem-blowup-local-on-base-scheme]]).

In particular the blowup of $X$ along an ideal sheaf depends on the ideal only
up to multiplication by an invertible fractional ideal: replacing $\mathcal I$
by $J\mathcal I$ changes the blowup by the canonical isomorphism
$\rho_{\mathcal I,J}$, whose inverse is $\rho_{J\mathcal I,J^{-1}}$.

## Remarks

- The integrality of $X$ is used only to know that a local equation of $J$ is
  a nonzerodivisor, so that multiplication by its powers is injective; it also
  makes the notion of an invertible fractional ideal literal. No regularity,
  Noetherianity or separatedness of $X$ is assumed.
- The identification is the reason a blowup may be computed after replacing
  $\mathcal I$ by a twist $\mathcal I\otimes L^m$ with $L$ ample, a
  globally generated ideal sheaf of finite type; the canonical isomorphism
  $\rho_{\mathcal I,L^m\mathcal I}$ is the one used for that replacement.
- The construction selects no data beyond the local equations of $J$ used to
  present the maps, and all choices give the same isomorphism, so the Axiom of
  Choice is used only through the relative Proj construction already present
  in [[def-blowup-scheme-along-ideal]].
