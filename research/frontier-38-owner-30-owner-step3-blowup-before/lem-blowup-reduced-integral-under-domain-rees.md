## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice, inherited from the relative Proj construction
([[def-axiom-of-choice]]). Let $X$ be an integral scheme
([[def-integral-scheme]]) and let $\mathcal I$ be a nonzero quasi-coherent
ideal sheaf of finite type on $X$ ([[def-quasi-coherent-ideal-sheaf]]). Then
the blowup $\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}X$ of
[[def-blowup-scheme-along-ideal]] is integral: the affine blowup algebras
$A[I/a]$ are domains because $A$ is a domain and $a$ is nonzero, and they glue
along localisations. More generally, if $X$ is reduced then
$\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}X$ is reduced, because the affine blowup
algebras of a reduced ring are reduced.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** An integral (respectively reduced) scheme $X$, a nonzero
quasi-coherent ideal sheaf $\mathcal I$ of finite type, and for an affine open
$U=\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq X$ with $I=\Gamma(U,\mathcal I)$ and $a\in I$
the affine blowup algebra $A[I/a]=(R(I))_{(a)}\subseteq A_a$, the
degree-zero part of the localisation of $R(I)=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}I^nt^n$
([[lem-affine-blowup-algebra-properties]],
[[def-rees-algebra-ideal-sheaf]]).

[F1] [[def-integral-scheme]]: $X$ is integral exactly when it is nonempty and
every nonempty affine open of $X$ is the spectrum of a domain; equivalently $X$
is reduced and its underlying space is irreducible.

[F2] [[lem-affine-blowup-algebra-properties]]: For $a\in I$ the affine blowup
algebra $A[I/a]$ has $IA[I/a]=aA[I/a]$ with $a$ a nonzerodivisor and
$\bigl(A[I/a]\bigr)_a=A_a$. Its construction as the degree-zero part of the
localisation of $R(I)\subseteq A[t]$ at the degree-one element $at$ embeds
$A[I/a]$ into $A_a$ as the subring generated by $A$ and the fractions $i/a$,
$i\in I$; if $A$ is a domain and $a\ne0$ then $A[I/a]$ is a domain, and if $A$
is reduced then $A[I/a]$ is reduced.

[F3] [[thm-affine-blowup-standard-charts]]: For $I=(f_0,\dots,f_r)$ the
standard opens $D_+(f_it)=\operatorname{Spec}A[I/f_i]$ cover
$\operatorname{Bl}_I\operatorname{Spec}A$ and the presentation is independent
of the chosen generating family.

[F4] [[def-affine-scheme-spectrum]]: For $a\in A$ the principal open $D(a)$ is
$\operatorname{Spec}A_a$ and the localisation morphism is an open immersion
$D(a)\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Spec}A$ with image the complement of
$V(a)$.

[F5] [[lem-blowup-local-on-base-scheme]]: For an open subscheme
$U\hookrightarrow X$ there is a canonical isomorphism
$\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I|_U}U\to\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}X\times_XU$;
the blowup is covered by the restrictions over an affine cover of $X$.

[F6] [[def-reduction-of-scheme]]: A scheme is reduced exactly when all its
affine open subschemes have reduced coordinate rings; reducedness is therefore
an affine-local property, and a subring of a reduced ring is reduced.

## Proof

1.1 Let $U=\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq X$ be a nonempty affine open and let $a\in I=\Gamma(U,\mathcal I)$ be nonzero. Then $A$ is a domain by [F1] and $A[I/a]$ embeds into $A_a$ as a subring by [F2]; $A_a$ is a domain because $a\ne0$ and $A$ is a domain, and a subring of a domain is a domain, so $A[I/a]$ is a domain. In the reduced case $A$ is reduced, so $A_a$ and its subring $A[I/a]$ are reduced by [F6]. [F1, F2, F6]

1.2 For a finite family $f_0,\dots,f_r$ of generators of $I$, the charts $U_i=\operatorname{Spec}A[I/f_i]$ cover $\operatorname{Bl}_I U=\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}X\times_XU$ by [F3] and [F5], and for each $i$ the image of $f_i$ in $A[I/f_i]$ is a nonzerodivisor with $\bigl(A[I/f_i]\bigr)_{f_i}=A_{f_i}$ by [F2], so by [F4] the principal open $D(f_i)\subseteq U_i$ is identified by $\pi$ with the open subscheme $U\smallsetminus V(f_i)$ of $U$. [F2, F3, F4, F5]

2.1 Suppose now that $X$ is integral and $\mathcal I\ne0$, so some affine open $U$ as above contains a nonzero $a\in I$. The open subscheme $W:=\pi^{-1}(X\smallsetminus V(\mathcal I))\subseteq\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}X$ contains $D(f_i)\cong U\smallsetminus V(f_i)$ for every nonzero generator $f_i$ of every chart, hence is nonempty, and $\pi$ restricts to an isomorphism $W\cong X\smallsetminus V(\mathcal I)$; here $X\smallsetminus V(\mathcal I)$ is a nonempty open subset of the irreducible space $X$ because $\mathcal I\ne0$, so it is irreducible and dense, and therefore $W$ is nonempty and irreducible. [F1, F4, F5, step 1.2]

3.1 Every chart $U_i$ of step 1.2 is irreducible, since its coordinate ring $A[I/f_i]$ is a domain by step 1.1, and the open $D(f_i)\subseteq U_i$ is nonempty and contained in $W$; moreover $D(f_i)$ is dense in $U_i$, because $A[I/f_i]\hookrightarrow A_{f_i}$ is injective by [F2] with $f_i\ne0$ a nonzerodivisor. Hence every chart is contained in the closure of the irreducible set $W$, and since the charts cover $\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}X$ by [F3], the blowup equals that closure and is irreducible. [F1, F3, step 1.1, step 1.2, step 2.1]

4.1 Each chart $U_i$ of step 1.2 is reduced: in the integral case its coordinate ring is a domain by step 1.1, and in the reduced case it is reduced by step 1.1; reducedness is affine-local by [F6], so $\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}X$ is reduced in both cases. In the integral case this combines with the irreducibility of step 3.1 and the nonemptiness of $W$ to show that $\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}X$ is integral, as claimed. [F6, step 1.1, step 3.1] ∎

## Remarks

- The argument does not need $X$ to be Noetherian or $\mathcal I$ to be
  principal anywhere; it only uses that the affine blowup algebra sits inside
  the localisation $A_a$.
- If $\mathcal I=0$ the blowup is empty and hence reduced, while
  irreducibility and nonemptiness fail; this is why the integral statement
  assumes $\mathcal I\ne0$.
