## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]). Let $k$ be a field, let
$X$ be an integral regular projective surface over $k$
([[def-divisor-intersection-number-on-smooth-projective-surface]]), let
$p\in X$ be a closed point with residue field $\kappa(p)$ and
$r:=[\kappa(p):k]$, let $\pi:X'=\operatorname{Bl}_pX\to X$ be the blowup of $X$
at $p$ ([[def-blowup-scheme-along-ideal]]) and let
$E:=\pi^{-1}(p)$ be the exceptional curve
([[def-exceptional-divisor-blowup]]). Then:

1. $X'$ is an integral regular projective surface over $k$, $E$ is an effective
   Cartier divisor, $E$ is isomorphic to $\mathbb P^1_{\kappa(p)}$, and
   $\mathcal O_E(E)\cong\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1_{\kappa(p)}}(-1)$; consequently
   $E\cdot E=-r$.
2. For all Cartier divisors $D,D'$ on $X$
   ([[def-cartier-divisor]]): $E\cdot\pi^*D=0$ and
   $\pi^*D\cdot\pi^*D'=D\cdot D'$; in particular $(\pi^*D)^2=D^2$.
3. If $C$ is a reduced effective Cartier divisor on $X$ through $p$ with
   multiplicity $m:=\operatorname{mult}_p(C)\ge1$
   ([[def-effective-cartier-divisor]]) and strict transform $C'$
   ([[def-strict-transform-closed-subscheme]]), then $\pi^*C=C'+mE$,
   $C'\cdot E=mr$ and $C'\cdot C'=C\cdot C-m^2r$. If $p\notin C$ then
   $\pi^*C=C'$ and $(C')^2=C^2$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** a field $k$, an integral regular projective surface $X$ over $k$, a closed point $p\in X$ with residue field $\kappa(p)$ and residue degree $r=[\kappa(p):k]$, the blowup $\pi:X'=\operatorname{Bl}_pX\to X$ and the exceptional curve $E=\pi^{-1}(p)$, and the Axiom of Choice ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]).

[F1] Blowup interfaces: $X'=\operatorname{Bl}_I X=\operatorname{Proj}_X R(I)$ for the ideal sheaf $I$ of the closed point $p$, with structural morphism $\pi$ and relative twists ([[def-blowup-scheme-along-ideal]]); $E=\pi^{-1}(p)$ is the scheme-theoretic inverse image of the center, a closed subscheme with ideal $I\mathcal O_{X'}$ ([[def-exceptional-divisor-blowup]]); $\pi$ is an isomorphism over $X\setminus p$ and $E$ is the complement of that open subscheme ([[lem-blowup-isomorphism-off-center]]); and $\pi$ is proper and locally H-projective, and globally H-projective as soon as the (possibly rescaled) ideal is generated by finitely many global sections ([[thm-blowup-projective]]).

[F2] Integrality and regularity of $X'$: since $X$ is integral and $I$ is a nonzero ideal of finite type, $X'$ is integral and $\pi$ is birational ([[cor-blowup-birational-integral-scheme]]); and since $X$ is a regular finite-type $k$-scheme of pure dimension two and $p$ is a closed point, $X'$ is regular of pure dimension two, $E$ is an effective Cartier divisor canonically isomorphic to $\mathbb P^1_{\kappa(p)}$, and $\mathcal O_E(E)\cong\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1_{\kappa(p)}}(-1)$ ([[thm-blowup-regular-surface-closed-point-regular]]); alternatively $E\cong\mathbb P(I/I^2)=\mathbb P^1_{\kappa(p)}$ for the regular immersion $p\hookrightarrow X$ ([[cor-exceptional-divisor-smooth-center-normal-bundle]], [[lem-exceptional-curve-normal-bundle-minus-one]]).

[F3] Projectivity of $X'$ over $k$: $X$ is projective over $k$ in the H-projective convention, so there is a closed immersion $X\hookrightarrow\mathbb P^n_k$ and the pullback $A=\mathcal O_X(1)$ of the twisting sheaf is H-very ample relative to $\operatorname{Spec}k$, hence ample ([[def-very-ample-invertible-sheaf-relative]], [[lem-very-ample-implies-ample]], [[def-ample-invertible-sheaf]]). The coherent ideal sheaf $I$ of the closed point has a twist $I\otimes A^{\otimes m}$ that is globally generated for some $m\ge0$, and since $I$ is of finite type this twist is generated by finitely many global sections ([[lem-eventual-global-generation-coherent-twists]], [[def-globally-generated-sheaf]]). Multiplication by a local equation of the invertible sheaf $A^{\otimes m}$ identifies the blowups along $I$ and along $I\otimes A^{\otimes m}$ ([[def-blowup-fractional-ideal]]), so $X'$ is a closed subscheme of a relative projective space over $X$ ([[thm-blowup-projective]]); composing with the closed immersion $X\hookrightarrow\mathbb P^n_k$ exhibits $X'$ as projective over $k$, and $\pi$ is proper.

[F4] Cohomology of line bundles on the exceptional curve: for $E\cong\mathbb P^1_{\kappa(p)}$ over $\kappa(p)$ and an invertible sheaf $\mathcal M$ on $E$ of degree $d$ over $\kappa(p)$, $\chi_k(E,\mathcal M)=r(1+d)$, where $r=[\kappa(p):k]$; in particular $\chi_k(E,\mathcal O_E)=r$, and for $\mathcal O_E(E)\cong\mathcal O(-1)$ of degree $-1$ one has $\chi_k(E,\mathcal O_E(E))=0$ and $\deg_E(\mathcal O_E(E))=\chi_k(E,\mathcal O_E(E))-\chi_k(E,\mathcal O_E)=-r$ ([[lem-exceptional-fiber-line-bundle-euler-characteristic]], [[def-degree-invertible-sheaf-proper-dimension-one]]).

[F5] Pushforward and projection formula: $\pi_*\mathcal O_{X'}=\mathcal O_X$ and $R^q\pi_*\mathcal O_{X'}=0$ for every $q>0$ ([[lem-blowup-point-pushforward-vanishing]]); consequently $\chi(X',\pi^*\mathcal N)=\chi(X,\mathcal N)$ for every invertible $\mathcal O_X$-module $\mathcal N$ ([[lem-projection-formula-invertible-twist]], [[def-euler-characteristic-coherent-sheaf]]).

[F6] Effective divisors, twists and transforms: for an effective Cartier divisor $H$ on a surface, the sequence $0\to\mathcal N(-H)\to\mathcal N\to i_*(\mathcal N|_H)\to0$ is exact for invertible $\mathcal N$ ([[lem-effective-cartier-divisor-exact-sequence]], [[cor-twist-exact-sequence-effective-divisor]]); the total transform $\pi^*D$ of a Cartier divisor is the pullback Cartier divisor with $\mathcal O_{X'}(\pi^*D)\cong\pi^*\mathcal O_X(D)$ ([[def-total-transform-divisor]], [[def-pullback-cartier-divisor]], [[lem-pullback-cartier-divisor-line-bundle]]); and for a reduced effective Cartier divisor $C$ on the regular surface $X$ through $p$ with multiplicity $m\ge1$, the total transform decomposes as $\pi^*C=C'+mE$ with $C'$ the strict transform, which is reduced ([[lem-total-transform-strict-plus-exceptional-multiplicity]], [[def-strict-transform-closed-subscheme]], [[def-reduction-of-scheme]]).

[F7] The intersection product of [[def-divisor-intersection-number-on-smooth-projective-surface]] is symmetric and $\mathbb Z$-bilinear on the Picard group of an integral regular projective surface, and the restriction theorem identifies $H\cdot F=\deg_H(\mathcal O(F)|_H)$ for effective $H$ ([[thm-surface-intersection-product-bilinear-and-symmetric]], [[thm-intersection-with-curve-as-degree-of-restriction]]); degrees, and with them intersection numbers, are additive on the proper curve $H$ ([[cor-degree-additive-proper-curve]]).

[F8] The Axiom of Choice enters through the blowup, sheaf-cohomology, global-generation and Euler-characteristic suppliers above; the points and divisors appearing below are given data.

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct; compute $E^2$ from the restriction theorem and the cohomology of $\mathcal O(-1)$ on $\mathbb P^1_{\kappa(p)}$, transport intersection numbers along $\pi$ with the projection formula, and expand $\pi^*C=C'+mE$ using bilinearity.

1.1 Set-up. By [F1]–[F3] the scheme $X'$ is an integral regular projective surface over $k$, $\pi$ is proper, $E=\pi^{-1}(p)$ is an effective Cartier divisor isomorphic to $\mathbb P^1_{\kappa(p)}$ with $\mathcal O_E(E)\cong\mathcal O(-1)$, and all intersection numbers below are those of [F7] on the surfaces $X$ and $X'$, whose hypotheses are satisfied. [F1, F2, F3, F7]

1.2 The self-intersection input from the exceptional curve. By [F4] the Euler characteristics on $E$ are $\chi_k(E,\mathcal O_E)=r$ and $\chi_k(E,\mathcal O_E(E))=\chi_k(E,\mathcal O(-1))=0$, so $\deg_E(\mathcal O_E(E))=-r$; the sheaf $\mathcal O_E(E)$ is invertible on the proper curve $E$. [F4]

1.3 Transporting Euler characteristics along $\pi$. By [F5], $\pi_*\mathcal O_{X'}=\mathcal O_X$, higher direct images vanish, and $\chi(X',\pi^*\mathcal N)=\chi(X,\mathcal N)$ for every invertible $\mathcal O_X$-module $\mathcal N$; moreover $\pi^*(\mathcal N^{\vee})\cong(\pi^*\mathcal N)^{\vee}$ and $\pi^*$ commutes with tensor products, so the four Euler characteristics defining $\pi^*D\cdot\pi^*D'$ are those of the pulled-back line bundles. [F5]

1.4 The transform decomposition. For a reduced effective Cartier divisor $C$ on the regular surface $X$ through $p$ with multiplicity $m\ge1$, the total transform satisfies $\pi^*C=C'+mE$ as effective Cartier divisors on $X'$, with $C'$ the strict transform; if $p\notin C$ then $E\cap C'=\varnothing$ and $\pi^*C=C'$ because $\pi$ is an isomorphism over $X\setminus p$ and the total transform meets $E$ only over the center. [F1, F6]

1.5 Part 2, first identity: $E\cdot\pi^*D=0$. The morphism $\pi|_E:E\to X$ has image the closed point $p$ and the pullback of the ideal sheaf $I$ of $p$ to $E$ is $I\mathcal O_{X'}\cdot\mathcal O_E=0$ by [F1]; hence $\pi|_E$ factors through the closed subscheme $\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(p)\hookrightarrow X$, and $\pi^*D|_E$ is pulled back from the spectrum of the field $\kappa(p)$. Every invertible sheaf on $\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(p)$ is trivial, so $\mathcal O_{X'}(\pi^*D)|_E\cong\mathcal O_E$ as a line bundle on $E$, of degree $0$ over $\kappa(p)$. Since $\pi^*D$ is an effective-free Cartier divisor, the restriction theorem on the surface $X'$ gives $E\cdot\pi^*D=\deg_E(\mathcal O_{X'}(\pi^*D)|_E)=\deg_E(\mathcal O_E)=0$ by [F4]. [F1, F4, F6, F7]

2.1 Part 1: $E\cdot E=-r$. Since $E$ is an effective Cartier divisor and $\mathcal O_{X'}(E)$ restricts to $\mathcal O_E(E)$ on $E$, the restriction theorem gives $E\cdot E=\deg_E(\mathcal O_E(E))$, and step 1.2 computes this as $-r$. [F7, step 1.2]

2.2 Part 2, second identity: $\pi^*D\cdot\pi^*D'=D\cdot D'$. By [F6] the associated line bundles satisfy $\mathcal O_{X'}(\pi^*D)\cong\pi^*\mathcal O_X(D)$ and $\mathcal O_{X'}(\pi^*D')\cong\pi^*\mathcal O_X(D')$. Substituting into the defining alternating sum and applying step 1.3 to the four invertible sheaves $\mathcal O_X$, $\mathcal O_X(D)^{\vee}$, $\mathcal O_X(D')^{\vee}$ and $\mathcal O_X(D)^{\vee}\otimes\mathcal O_X(D')^{\vee}$ gives $\pi^*D\cdot\pi^*D'=D\cdot D'$; taking $D=D'$ gives $(\pi^*D)^2=D^2$. [F6, step 1.3]

3.1 Part 3. Let $C$ be reduced and effective with $p\in C$ and multiplicity $m\ge1$. By step 1.4, $\pi^*C=C'+mE$; applying step 1.5 with $D=C$ gives $0=E\cdot\pi^*C=E\cdot C'+m\,E\cdot E$, so $C'\cdot E=-m\,E\cdot E=mr$ by step 2.1 and symmetry of the product. Moreover $\pi^*C\cdot\pi^*C=C\cdot C$ by step 2.2, while bilinearity on $X'$ gives $\pi^*C\cdot\pi^*C=C'\cdot C'+2m\,C'\cdot E+m^2E\cdot E=C'\cdot C'+2m^2r-m^2r=C'\cdot C'+m^2r$; hence $C'\cdot C'=C\cdot C-m^2r$. If $p\notin C$, then the multiplicity is $0$ and step 1.4 gives $\pi^*C=C'$, so $C'\cdot C'=(\pi^*C)^2=C^2$ by step 2.2. [F6, F7, step 1.4, step 2.1, step 1.5, step 2.2]

4.1 Conclusion and choice accounting. Step 2.1 gives $E\cdot E=-r$; steps 1.5 and 2.2 give $E\cdot\pi^*D=0$ and $\pi^*D\cdot\pi^*D'=D\cdot D'$, hence $(\pi^*D)^2=D^2$; step 3.1 gives $\pi^*C=C'+mE$, $C'\cdot E=mr$, $C'\cdot C'=C\cdot C-m^2r$ and the off-center case. The Axiom of Choice enters through the suppliers recorded in [F8]; the point, the divisors and the blowup are given data and no selection is made in the computations.
