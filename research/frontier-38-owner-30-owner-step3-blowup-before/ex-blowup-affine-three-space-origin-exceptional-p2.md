## Example

Let $k$ be a field. The blowup of $\mathbb A^3_k$ at the origin for $I=(x,y,z)$ is the subscheme of $\mathbb A^3\times\mathbb P^2$ cut out by the $2\times2$ minors $x_iT_j-x_jT_i$ of the matrix with rows $(x,y,z)$ and $(T_0,T_1,T_2)$; its three standard charts are $\operatorname{Spec}k[x,y/x,z/x]$, $\operatorname{Spec}k[x/y,y,z/y]$ and $\operatorname{Spec}k[x/z,y/z,z]$, each isomorphic to $\mathbb A^3_k$. The exceptional divisor is cut by $x$ in the first chart, by $y$ in the second and by $z$ in the third, and is isomorphic to $\mathbb P^2_k$, with $\mathcal O_E(E)=\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^2_k}(-1)$ in the quotient convention.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A field $k$, the affine space $\mathbb A^3_k=\operatorname{Spec}k[x,y,z]$, the ideal $I=(x,y,z)$ of the origin, and the blowup of the origin.

[A1] **Choice.** The Axiom of Choice is assumed as inherited from the blowup and Proj constructions used below.

[F1] [[thm-affine-blowup-standard-charts]]: For $I=(f_0,\dots,f_r)\subseteq A$ the charts $\operatorname{Spec}A[I/f_i]$ cover $\operatorname{Bl}_I\operatorname{Spec}A$, glued by the displayed transition functions.

[F2] [[lem-affine-blowup-algebra-properties]]: $A[I/a]$ has $I\,A[I/a]=a\,A[I/a]$ and $(A[I/a])_a=A_a$, and for $I=(a_0,\dots,a_r)$, $a=a_0$, it is generated by the ratios $a_i/a$.

[F3] [[lem-blowup-independent-ideal-generators]]: Different finite local generating families of $I$ give canonically isomorphic chart presentations of the same blowup; the affine blowup presentations $A[I/a]$ are canonically the standard charts.

[F4] [[def-exceptional-divisor-blowup]]: $E=\pi^{-1}(Z)$ is the zero scheme of the inverse-image ideal $\mathcal I\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Bl}}$.

[F5] [[thm-exceptional-divisor-normal-cone-proj]]: $E\cong\operatorname{Proj}_Z(\operatorname{gr}_{\mathcal I}\mathcal O_X)$ canonically.

[F6] [[cor-exceptional-divisor-smooth-center-normal-bundle]]: For a regular center, $E\cong\mathbb P_Z(\mathcal I/\mathcal I^2)=\operatorname{Proj}_Z\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal I/\mathcal I^2)$ in the quotient convention; for the origin of $\mathbb A^3$ the conormal space is free of rank three, so this is $\mathbb P^2_k$.

[F7] [[thm-pullback-center-ideal-invertible]]: $\mathcal I\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Bl}}=\mathcal O(1)=\mathcal O(-E)$ and $\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Bl}}(E)=\mathcal O(-1)$.

## Verification

1.1 The three charts of the blowup are $\operatorname{Spec}A[I/x]$, $\operatorname{Spec}A[I/y]$, $\operatorname{Spec}A[I/z]$ by [F1]; by [F2] the first is $A$-generated by $y/x$ and $z/x$, with $I\,A[I/x]=x\,A[I/x]$, so it is the polynomial ring $k[x,y/x,z/x]\cong k[x,s,t]=\mathbb A^3_k$; symmetrically the other two charts are $k[x/y,y,z/y]$ and $k[x/z,y/z,z]$, each isomorphic to $\mathbb A^3_k$, glued by the transition ratios of [F1] and [F3]. [F1, F2, F3]

2.1 The same blowup is presented inside $\mathbb A^3\times\mathbb P^2$ by the $2\times2$ minors of $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}x&y&z\\T_0&T_1&T_2\end{smallmatrix}\right)$: writing $T_i$ for the degree-one Rees generators, the relations $xT_1-yT_0$, $xT_2-zT_0$, $yT_2-zT_1$ cut the Rees algebra of $I$, and on the chart $T_0=1$ they become $xT_1=y$, $xT_2=z$, which is exactly the first chart of step 1.1, with the other charts obtained symmetrically; by [F3] the presentations agree. [F1, F3, step 1.1]

3.1 The exceptional divisor is cut by $x$ in the first chart, by $y$ in the second and by $z$ in the third: on each chart $I\mathcal O$ is generated by the corresponding variable, by [F2] and [F4]. By [F5] and [F6] it is $\operatorname{Proj}_k(\operatorname{gr}_{(x,y,z)}k[x,y,z]_0)=\operatorname{Proj}_kk[X,Y,Z]=\mathbb P^2_k$, the projectivized cotangent space in the quotient convention, since the conormal space $\mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2$ is free of rank three over $k$. [F2, F4, F5, F6, step 2.1]

4.1 Finally [F7] gives $\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Bl}}(E)=\mathcal O(-1)$ and $\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Bl}}(-E)=I\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Bl}}=\mathcal O(1)$; restricting the first identity to $E$ and using the identification $E=\operatorname{Proj}_k\operatorname{gr}_{\mathfrak m}k[x,y,z]_0=\mathbb P^2_k$ of step 3.1, the restricted twist is the standard $\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^2_k}(-1)$, so $\mathcal O_E(E)=\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^2_k}(-1)$ in the quotient convention. [F7, step 3.1] ∎
