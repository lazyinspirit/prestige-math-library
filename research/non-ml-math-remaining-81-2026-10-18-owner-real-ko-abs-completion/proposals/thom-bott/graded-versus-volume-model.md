# Exact group-model construction: graded ABS and ungraded Clifford–Thom

Isolated mathematical proposal; no canonical file is changed. This is a complete elementary comparison lemma, not proof of the still-missing family connectivity/Thom theorem.

Fix an oriented Euclidean bundle V over a finite CW complex X of positive rank n=4m and the negative-square algebra bundle C(V)=Cl(V,-g). Write omega for the oriented Clifford volume section. Its square is 1: reversing n odd factors contributes (-1)^(n(n-1)/2), their squares contribute (-1)^n, and the sum exponent n(n+1)/2=2m(4m+1) is even. The determinant-one change-of-frame identity for the alternating Clifford product makes omega global. It anticommutes with c(v) for v in V.

Define U(V) to be the additive category of finite-rank real **ungraded** left C(V)-module bundles and C(V)-linear continuous bundle maps. Define K_C(V)(X) as the Grothendieck group of isomorphism classes under direct sum. This is the domain of EH Definition10.1/Theorem10.3 in the approved rank4m route. A module admits an invariant Euclidean metric locally/globally by averaging a chosen metric over the finite group generated by signed products of an orthonormal frame in each local trivialization, then gluing these invariant metrics by a partition of unity. Each such metric is C(V)-invariant in the needed sense (unit Clifford vectors act orthogonally); convex sums preserve that identity. An underlying bundle embedding into a finite sum of local regular modules can be made C(V)-linear using a partition of unity and local regular-module summands; the orthogonal complement is then C(V)-invariant. These complement facts require their actual finite-cover/partition hypotheses and supplied real bundle lemma, rather than being an assumed K-theory theorem.

Let G(V) be the category of Z/2-graded module bundles. A grading is represented by a parity involution Gamma with Gamma²=1 and Gamma c(v)=-c(v)Gamma. Since omega is a product of n even-many odd generators, Gamma commutes with omega. Put T=Gamma omega. Then T²=1. For every v,

T c(v)=Gamma omega c(v)=-Gamma c(v)omega=c(v)Gamma omega=c(v)T.

Thus T is C(V)-linear. The continuous idempotents (1±T)/2 split the bundle into ungraded C(V)-submodules L_+ and L_-. Their image ranks are locally constant: a continuous idempotent has eigenvalues0,1, so its trace is an integer-valued continuous rank function; the usual image-of-projection local frames establish subbundle trivializations. On L_+, Gamma=omega, and on L_-, Gamma=-omega.

Conversely, for an ungraded module L, either choice Gamma=±omega is a grading, since omega anticommutes with the odd generators. A pair (L_+,L_-) therefore gives the graded module with Gamma=omega on its first summand and Gamma=-omega on its second. A graded C(V)-linear map commutes with Gamma and omega, hence T, so it preserves this splitting; conversely a pair of ungraded module maps commutes with the stated gradings. These constructions are inverse additive equivalences

G(V) ≅ U(V) × U(V).

Consequently M(V)=K_0(G(V)) ≅ K_C(V)(X) ⊕ K_C(V)(X), and forgetting grading is (a,b)↦a+b. Its kernel is exactly {(a,-a)}: the equation a+b=0 implies b=-a, and every such pair is killed. This is independent of any Thom/Bott theorem. Over X=pt and rank8, C_8=M_16(R), so the two groups are Z² and Z. An isomorphism from raw M(V) to KO_c(V) via the volume construction is impossible.

The **volume** clutching construction uses eigenspaces of omega on the underlying ungraded bundle. It does not use Gamma. Hence its class factors through the forgetful sum map. If the family theorem later proves the standard volume-symbol map Phi:K_C(V)(X)→KO_c(V) is an isomorphism, the kernel of the corresponding raw graded volume map is precisely the anti-diagonal above, not zero.

By contrast, the ABS construction uses the parity spaces M^0,M^1. For Gamma=omega, its triple is the volume triple (L^+,c(v),L^-). For Gamma=-omega the parity spaces are swapped, so its triple is (L^-,c(v),L^+). On the unit sphere c(v)^-1=-c(v). The inverse relative class of the first triple is (L^-,-c(v),L^+); multiplying one of its two bundle factors by the constant automorphism -I identifies that triple with (L^-,c(v),L^+). Thus the parity symbol changes sign under parity reversal. On the pair decomposition its class is Phi(a)-Phi(b), when the same orientation/convention is used for Phi. The volume symbol instead is Phi(a)+Phi(b). These maps must not be identified by merely renaming M(V).

For n=0 the same abstract category comparison reduces to parity splitting of real bundles, giving M_0(X)=KO^0(X)⊕KO^0(X). The ABS degree-zero parity symbol is rank/bundle difference. The real Thom rank-zero isomorphism is the identity on KO^0(X), handled directly with Th(0_X)=X_+; it is not an isomorphism from M_0(X). The positive-rank4m Morse/Hopf theorem is neither needed nor applied in this boundary.

## North/south sign conversion, required before integration

Use relative triples (E,F,sigma:E|S→F|S) with absolute class E−F. EH uses H=(Lambda+ on the north half, Lambda− on the south half, c(v)) and chi(H)=H−pi*Lambda+ with the north section collapsed. On the remaining south disk its difference is Lambda−−Lambda+, and its boundary identification into Lambda+ is c(v)^−1=−c(v). Therefore EH Psi is represented by (Lambda−,Lambda+,−c), the negative of the standard symbol (Lambda+,Lambda−,c). Our multiplicative volume-symbol map Phi is **−EH Psi**. This sign change preserves isomorphism and is explicit. It is not legitimate to call both maps identical. Choose the rank-eight generator beta=Phi8(Sigma) so that the graded ABS symbol with even=volume+ satisfies alpha8=beta. In positive ranks divisible4, the graded tensor action has volume omega_(V⊕W)=omega_V⊗omega_W and parity Gamma_V⊗Gamma_W=omega_V⊗omega_W, so this choice is multiplicative. Choosing even=volume− uniformly would fail that tensor compatibility. Rankzero Phi is the direct KO identity, not an unjustified EH limiting assertion.
