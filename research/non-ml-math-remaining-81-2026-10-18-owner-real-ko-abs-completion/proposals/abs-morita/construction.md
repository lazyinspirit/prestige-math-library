# Isolated ABS/Morita proof construction

These are proposed replacements, not integrated items, native execution, readiness,
independent review, or gate evidence. Only this proposal directory is writable by
this worker. Canonical graded $M_n$ and $A_n$ remain unchanged. The exact pending
real geometric interface is the **ungraded** isomorphism
$K_{\mathrm{Cl}(V)}(X)\to KO_c^0(V)$ for oriented positive rank $4m$.
There is no claim that this interface is already proved. At rank zero its
replacement is the ordinary identity $KO(X)\to KO(X)$, not a graded-module
isomorphism: graded $M_0(\mathrm{pt})=\mathbb Z^2$.

**Sign contract.** These drafts use triples $(E,F,a:E\to F)$ with absolute
class $[E]-[F]$, and volume-positive spinors in even degree. Hence the geometric
supplier must use $\Phi(\Lambda)=[\Lambda^+,\Lambda^-,c(v)]$. With EH's
hemispheres and the north section collapsed, this is **minus** EH's map
$\Psi_{EH}(\Lambda)=[H(\Lambda)]-\pi^*[\Lambda^+]$: restriction to the
south disk gives $[\Lambda^-]-[\Lambda^+]$ with boundary inverse $-c(v)$.
Negating an isomorphism preserves its isomorphism property, but the sign
must be accounted for before calling $\alpha_8(B)$ the same $\beta$.
The Bott normalization required here is $\beta=\Phi_{\mathbb R^8}(B)$,
not $\Psi_{EH}(B)$. The direct-sum product is proved for $\Phi$'s symbols.
Uniformly choosing even equal to volume-negative in every rank is not a
replacement: tensoring two such gradings gives volume-positive grading.

## Corrections requiring owner integration

- The Morita item must say **ungraded** module bundle with its canonical volume
  grading when rank is positive $4m$. Arbitrarily graded modules have extra
  parity data and the proposed evaluation statement for all such modules is
  false.
- The rank-$8m+4$ map is $[E]\mapsto\Phi(E\otimes_{\mathbb H}\Sigma)$.
  It is not ordinary multiplication of the real class of $E$ by a real
  spinor class. A trivial quaternionic line is the unit in this formula.
- The all-ranks shifted target is $KO^{j+r}(\operatorname{Th}(V))$, not
  $KO^{j-r}(\operatorname{Th}(V))$. Equivalently the theorem can be written
  $KO^{j-r}(X,A)\cong\widetilde{KO}^{j}(\operatorname{Th}(V|_X)/
  \operatorname{Th}(V|_A))$. For $r=1$, the old target at a point is
  $\widetilde{KO}^{-1}(S^1)=\mathbb Z/2$, whereas the source is $\mathbb Z$.
  This is a substantive sign repair, not notation.
- EH's north-section subtraction and the plus-even relative symbol have
  the opposite signs under the stated relative-triple convention. Apply
  the sign contract above consistently to the real Thom, Bott, coefficient,
  ABS and all-ranks proposals.
