## Statement

For a given Spin^c bundle V of even rank over a finite CW pair, the complex graded spinor bundle gives the relative symbol (S^+,S^−,c(v)) and a class in reduced/relative complex K-theory. Multiplication by this class defines a natural KU Thom map in every degree. Prove equivariance for Spin^c(n)=(Spin(n)×U(1))/⟨(−1,−1)⟩, including the U(1) weight, identify the fiber symbol with the complex Bott generator using the graded tensor model, and give the complete finite-CW induction and direct-sum proof.
