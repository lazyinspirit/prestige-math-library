## Statement

For n=0,…,7, the groups KO^(−n)(pt) are ℤ,ℤ/2,ℤ/2,0,ℤ,0,0,0 in that order. Multiplication by the rank-eight class β gives KO^(−n)(pt)≅KO^(−n−8)(pt) in every degree. Prove the low rows from the local clutching computation and the rank-four Spin/KSp boundary; extend all remaining degrees only through the locally proved rank-eight real Bott theorem.

## Authoring status

The eight residue groups and multiplication by the rank-eight class depend on unresolved Spin/KSp and real Bott results; the table cannot be asserted from the cited statements alone.
