## Statement

For finite CW pairs, relative KO classes can be represented by triples of real bundles and an isomorphism over the subspace, modulo stabilization and homotopy. Pullback along homotopic maps gives the same KO class, with the exact finite-CW hypotheses and any reused axiom assumptions stated explicitly.
