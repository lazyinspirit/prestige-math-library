## Statement

For the specified finite-dimensional stable Dirac-mass extension problem with d fixed negative-square gamma generators and one flattened mass, the strong d-dimensional group at real index q is KO^(−(q−d))(pt); the complex analogue for indices 0 and 1 is KU^(−(q−d))(pt). Prove the explicit sign-changing Morita equivalence Cl_(p+1,q+1)≅M₂(Cl_(p,q)), identify all real residues q=0,…,7 and complex residues q=0,1, and state the class-by-dimension table. The result concerns only this finite stable free mass model; it gives no weak lattice, arbitrary-disorder, or interaction-stable classification.
