## Statement

Write O=colim_n O(n) and Sp=colim_r Sp(r) under standard block inclusions. Clutching over S^n identifies \widetilde{KO}^0(S^n) with π_(n−1)(O) and \widetilde{KSp}^0(S^n) with π_(n−1)(Sp) in the stable range. Compute KO^(−1)(pt)=π₀(O)=ℤ/2, KO^(−2)(pt)=π₁(O)=ℤ/2, KO^(−3)(pt)=π₂(O)=0 and KSp^(−j)(pt)=π_(j−1)(Sp)=0 for j=1,2,3 from the orthogonal and symplectic sphere fibrations, the SO(3) double cover, and the stated low homotopy suppliers.

## Authoring status

The clutching identification is available, but the low stable homotopy computation needs a complete proof of π0(O), π1(SO), π2(SO), and π0–π2(Sp), including the SO(3) cover and stability fibration maps. The current local argument has not been completed.
