## Definition

Let V→X be a finite-rank Euclidean real vector bundle and put C(V)=Cl(V,−⟨·,·⟩), so each vector v acts with square −‖v‖². A real graded C(V)-module bundle is a finite-rank real vector bundle Λ=Λ^0⊕Λ^1 with a continuous fiberwise unital action preserving the grading and satisfying the Clifford relations. Let M(V) be the Grothendieck group of their isomorphism classes under direct sum.
