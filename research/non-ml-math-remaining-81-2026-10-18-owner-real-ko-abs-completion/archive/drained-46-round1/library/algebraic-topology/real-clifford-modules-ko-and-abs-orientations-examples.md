This companion checks the conventions and boundary cases on [[real-clifford-modules-ko-and-abs-orientations]]. The worked calculations include the degree-zero parity cokernel and a finite class-A Hamiltonian; the sign-convention counterexample is proved directly. Examples that depend on the still-unavailable real Clifford classification, KO coefficient table, spin Thom supplier, or characteristic-class interface remain explicitly escalated and do not supply general results.

## Checks carried by the companion

- The positive-square and negative-square one-generator algebras differ; this prevents an index shift from being hidden by notation.
- The zero-dimensional graded-module cokernel keeps even and odd parity types distinct.
- The low-signature, KO coefficient, and rank-four quaternionic calculations are read from the A-page only after their full local suppliers are ready.
- The non-Spin example requires its exact Stiefel–Whitney input; the current characteristic-class definition carrier is unavailable and is recorded as an unresolved dependency.
- The free-fermion examples retain the strong/weak and free/interacting limits of the A-page statement.
