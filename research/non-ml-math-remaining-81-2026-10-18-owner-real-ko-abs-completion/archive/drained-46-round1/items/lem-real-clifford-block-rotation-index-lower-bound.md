## Statement

Let J₁,…,J_l be the family in [[def-orthogonal-clifford-families-and-centrioles]], let γ be a nonminimal geodesic in P_l, and let ind(γ) be the Morse index of its energy on endpoint-fixed variation fields. Decompose ℝ^p into minimal mutually orthogonal subspaces invariant under the Clifford family and the geodesic generator; each such block is a finite-dimensional Clifford module. Write s_q for the real dimension of an irreducible C_q-module, fixed by the exact real Clifford classification. A two-block rotation is the variation generated by the skew operator with matrix blocks (0,−I; I,0) on two isomorphic invariant blocks and zero on the others; different counted pairs must yield linearly independent directions for the index form. If l is not congruent to 2 modulo 4, prove the safe lower bound ind(γ)≥p/s_(l+1)−1, retaining one unit of slack when using it to prove ind(γ)>d. If l=4m−2, the loop γ(t)J_l⁻¹ lies in U(p/2); define its winding w as the winding number of its complex determinant. In this residue class prove that p−4|w|≥4d s_l implies ind(γ)>d. The strictness gap in the source's Proposition 5.4 remains unresolved.
