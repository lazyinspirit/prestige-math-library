## Statement

For finite CW pairs (X,A), the relative KO groups defined by the quotient satisfy the cofiber long exact sequence, are compatible with suspension, and have the external and internal products induced by bundle tensor product. State and prove the pair and basepoint conventions used by the Clifford–Thom map.
