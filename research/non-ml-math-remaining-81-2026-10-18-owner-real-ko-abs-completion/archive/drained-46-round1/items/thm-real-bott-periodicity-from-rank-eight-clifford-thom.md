## Statement

Let β∈\widetilde{KO}^0(S^8) be the rank-eight real spinor Hopf class of the trivial bundle. For every finite CW complex X, external product with β gives KO^0(X)→\widetilde{KO}^0(X_+∧S^8). Prove this map is an isomorphism, derive reduced and relative forms via the section/wedge split exact sequence, extend to every suspended degree, and prove multiplicativity. This is the local real Bott theorem and is not inferred from complexification.
