## Example

In the declared BDI finite Dirac-mass convention, take q=1 and d=1. Exhibit the real gamma and symmetry generators, verify their squares and anticommutation relations, and compute an explicit one-parameter integer-valued family whose class is the generator of KO^0(pt)=ℤ under the extension theorem. The family must be written as finite matrices and its invariant evaluated directly.

A complete explicit BDI generator/family and integer invariant is not supplied. It depends on the unresolved extension table and the exact real Clifford convention.
