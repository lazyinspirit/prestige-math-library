## Example

Assume AC. The coefficients $KO^{-n}(\mathrm{pt})$ in residues $n=0,\ldots,7$ are $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Z/2,\mathbb Z/2,0,\mathbb Z,0,0,0$. The degree-four integer generator is quaternionic spinor clutching, and degree eight is generated by the fixed real Bott symbol $\beta$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC and the suspension convention for negative KO degrees.

[F1] The coefficient theorem proves the eight groups from independent low clutching, the rank-four $KSp$ Thom map and rank-eight real Bott, with their actual generators ([[thm-ko-coefficient-table-and-eightfold-periodicity]]).

## Verification

1.1 In the first four residues [F1] identifies the integer unit with the real line, the first order-two generator with the Möbius clutching and the second with the stable one-turn orthogonal clutching. Degree $-3$ is zero. Thus the first four entries are $\mathbb Z,\mathbb Z/2,\mathbb Z/2,0$, including their geometrically specified generators. [F1]


2.1 The rank-four quaternionic spinor map in [F1] identifies $KO^{-4-j}(\mathrm{pt})$ with $KSp^{-j}(\mathrm{pt})$ for $0\le j\le3$. The latter are $\mathbb Z,0,0,0$, supplying exactly the remaining entries; the quaternionic line gives the integer generator. Multiplication by the actual rank-eight symbol then repeats these residues, so the example does not infer a real theorem from complex Bott or from a bare list of abstract groups. [F1, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
