- `def-ip-2d-radon-transform`: Definition fixes oriented line normals, signed offsets, `theta_perp`, the compact-support Riemann Fourier realization, the ramp multiplier `2 pi |sigma|`, and the Riesz-potential normalization. The definition's only direct supplier is the published LCA Fourier-transform convention. The item is at level 0 because it has no in-run supplier. - `thm-ip-fourier-slice-identity`: Statement is `widehat{Rf}(theta,sigma)=widehat f(sigma theta)` for every compactly supported smooth complex `f`, all unit normals, and all real frequencies. The proof is the compact-support Riemann/Fubini coordinate calculation with the orientation-preserving orthogonal map `(s,t) -> s theta + t theta_perp`; it is choice-free. Direct suppliers: `def-ip-2d-radon-transform`, `thm-continuous-functions-on-compact-jordan-sets-are-integrable`, `thm-riemann-fubini-on-product-rectangles`, and `cor-change-of-variables-for-compactly-supported-functions`. Sources: Salo §2.1 pp. 4–7; Natterer §2.1, Theorem 2.1, pp. 18–20. Supplier bodies and exact excerpts are recorded; strict proof-contract validation, precheck, and proof-layout pass.
- `thm-ip-radon-normal-operator`: Pointwise claim is `R*Rf=4 pi |D|^{-1}f` together with `f=(4 pi)^(-1)R*|D_s|Rf`; the statement defines the backprojection using the polar surface measure.
