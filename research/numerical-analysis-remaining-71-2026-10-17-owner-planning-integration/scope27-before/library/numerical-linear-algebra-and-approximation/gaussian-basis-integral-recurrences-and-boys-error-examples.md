These examples evaluate the same-center s-orbital overlap, kinetic, attraction, and electron-repulsion integrals; exhibit the small-$T$ cancellation in the upward Boys recurrence; and attain the explicit $n,r$ tensor-error bounds with an aligned finite perturbation. A two-function overlap matrix shows how nearly duplicate exponents produce a small eigenvalue. The screening example accumulates every skipped-block bound and stops screening before the total budget is exceeded.
