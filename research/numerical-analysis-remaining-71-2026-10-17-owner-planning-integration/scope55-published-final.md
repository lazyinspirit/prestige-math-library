## Approved scope and actual defects

Both original Statements and all frontmatter are byte-for-byte preserved. No choice axiom, domain restriction, or algebra restriction is introduced. The lattice proof formerly selected countable approximant families and a diagonal without an axiom. Stone–Weierstrass formerly selected interpolants for all pairs and lower envelopes for all points, which is not justified by the stated premises. These were real proof gaps, not false Statements.

The lattice proof now establishes closure under algebra operations by two witnesses for a fixed tolerance, then approximates absolute value by one algebra element and one polynomial on the explicitly bounded interval. Polynomial constants are allowed because the algebra is unital; no artificial p(0)=0 condition is needed. Max/min follow from the original identities. The existing interval approximation supplier provides a polynomial at each prescribed tolerance, so no sequence of its witnesses is selected.

The theorem uses the entire family of interpolants matching f at a fixed x. Compactness supplies a finite cover and finitely many representatives, establishing existence of a finite maximum matching f(x) and globally exceeding f-delta. The second cover is indexed by ALL finite maxima with that global lower bound. Pointwise existence establishes coverage without choosing a g_x for each x. A finite subcover and minimum yield h in the closure with sup error at most delta; a single final algebra approximant gives error <2delta<epsilon for delta=epsilon/3. This avoids both arbitrary-index choices and the incorrect implication from pointwise strict bounds to a strictly smaller supremum. Finite nonempty covers are justified by nonemptiness of K, including singleton K. Finite existential witness extraction needs no choice axiom.
