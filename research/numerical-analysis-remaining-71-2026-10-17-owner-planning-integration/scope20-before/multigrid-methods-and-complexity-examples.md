The examples calculate a coarse correction that removes a piecewise-linear
error exactly, expand the V- and W-cycle work recurrences for four-to-one
coarsening, and check a finite Poisson mode against the uniform two-grid
estimate. Every convergence claim uses the A-page hypotheses; arithmetic work
and convergence remain separate.
