## Statement

Let $u_0\in\mathrm{BV}_b(\mathbb R)$ and put $V:=\operatorname{TV}(u_0)$.
Let $U^n=(U_j^n)_{j\in\mathbb Z}$ be generated by
[[def-monotone-finite-volume-entropy-scheme]] under its full invariant-interval
CFL bound. Define the update map
$$H(a,b,c):=b-\lambda\bigl(F(b,c)-F(a,b)\bigr).$$
Then $H$ is nondecreasing in each coordinate on $I^3$ and $H(s,s,s)=s$ for
$s\in I$. Consequently,
$$a_-\le U_j^n\le a_+\quad(j\in\mathbb Z,\ n\ge0),\qquad \sum_{j\in\mathbb Z}|U_{j+1}^n-U_j^n|\le V\quad(n\ge0).$$
Moreover, for every $n\ge0$,
$$\frac h\tau\sum_{j\in\mathbb Z}|U_j^{n+1}-U_j^n|\le (L_1+L_2)V.$$
For a sequence $x=(x_j)_{j\in\mathbb Z}$, the notation
$\|x\|_1:=\sum_{j\in\mathbb Z}|x_j|$ is used only when this sum is finite;
$x\vee y$ and $x\wedge y$ mean the coordinatewise maximum and minimum.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** $u_0$, $V$, the cells and cell-average initialization, a locally Lipschitz monotone consistent flux $F$ on $I=[a_-,a_+]$, coordinate bounds $L_1,L_2$, $\lambda=\tau/h$ with $\lambda(L_1+L_2)\le1$, and the flux-difference update.

[F1] $\operatorname{TV}(u_0)<\infty$ means $\int_{\mathbb R}|u_0(x+h)-u_0(x)|\,dx\le hV$ for every $h>0$ ([[def-bounded-bv-data-on-line]]).

[F2] Each updated coordinate is $H(U_{j-1}^n,U_j^n,U_{j+1}^n)$ and $H$ is defined from the flux-difference rule ([[def-monotone-finite-volume-entropy-scheme]]).

## Proof

1.1 For a finite set $J\subset\mathbb Z$, the cell-average formula gives $\sum_{j\in J}|U_{j+1}^0-U_j^0|\le\frac1h\int_{\cup_{j\in J}I_j}|u_0(x+h)-u_0(x)|\,dx\le V.$ Taking the supremum over finite $J$ proves the initial discrete variation bound. Every initial cell average lies in $I$, since $a_-\le u_0\le a_+$ almost everywhere on its cell. [F1, given, algebra]

1.2 If $a$ or $c$ increases, monotonicity of $F$ in its first or second coordinate makes $H$ nondecreasing in that variable. If $b_2\ge b_1$, coordinate Lipschitz bounds give $H(a,b_2,c)-H(a,b_1,c)\ge(b_2-b_1)\bigl(1-\lambda(L_1+L_2)\bigr)\ge0.$ Thus $H$ is nondecreasing in all coordinates. Consistency gives $H(s,s,s)=s$. [F2, given, algebra]

2.1 Suppose $U^n$ takes values in $I$. Coordinatewise comparison with the constant triples $(a_-,a_-,a_-)$ and $(a_+,a_+,a_+)$, followed by step 1.2, yields $a_-=H(a_-,a_-,a_-)\le U_j^{n+1}\le H(a_+,a_+,a_+)=a_+$. Induction from step 1.1 proves the range bound at every time. [step 1.2, step 1.1, algebra]

2.2 Let $S$ denote the sequence update. If $x,y\in I^{\mathbb Z}$ and $\sum_j|x_j-y_j|<\infty$, monotonicity gives $|Sx-Sy|\le S(x\vee y)-S(x\wedge y)$ coordinatewise. The difference on the right is summable: the update formula and the coordinate Lipschitz bounds make its flux differences summable. For any $z,w$ with $\sum_j|z_j-w_j|<\infty$, summing the update over $-N\le j\le N$ yields $\sum_{j=-N}^N(Sz_j-Sw_j)=\sum_{j=-N}^N(z_j-w_j)-\lambda(d_N-d_{-N-1}),\quad d_j:=F(z_j,z_{j+1})-F(w_j,w_{j+1}).$ The coordinate Lipschitz bounds make $\sum_j|d_j|<\infty$, so both boundary terms tend to zero. The update difference is absolutely summable, and hence $\sum_j(Sz_j-Sw_j)=\sum_j(z_j-w_j)$. Applying this to $z=x\vee y$ and $w=x\wedge y$ proves $\|Sx-Sy\|_1\le\|x-y\|_1$. [F2, step 1.2, algebra]

3.1 The initial neighbor difference is summable by step 1.1. The update commutes with integer shifts because the same $H$ is applied at every index. Apply step 2.2 to $x=U^n$ and its shift $y_j=U_{j+1}^n$ to obtain $\sum_j|U_{j+1}^{n+1}-U_j^{n+1}|\le\sum_j|U_{j+1}^{n}-U_j^{n}|.$ Induction proves the stated TVD bound. [step 1.1, step 2.2, algebra]

4.1 Adding and subtracting $F(U_j^n,U_j^n)$ in the update and using the two coordinate Lipschitz bounds gives $|U_j^{n+1}-U_j^n|\le\lambda\bigl(L_2|U_{j+1}^n-U_j^n|+L_1|U_j^n-U_{j-1}^n|\bigr).$ Multiply by $h/\tau=1/\lambda$, sum over $j$, and use step 3.1 to obtain the time-increment estimate. [F2, step 3.1, algebra] ∎
