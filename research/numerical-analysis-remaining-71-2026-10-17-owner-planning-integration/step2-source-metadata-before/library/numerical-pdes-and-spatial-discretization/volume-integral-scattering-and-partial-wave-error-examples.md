These examples give a strict small-norm ball bound with an explicit Born tail, an exact conditional amplification factor for a near-unit eigenmode, exact zeroth-mode matching for a radial square well, and a forward-direction witness showing why a fixed angular cutoff fails for high-frequency plane waves. The eigenmode and exact square-well conclusions remain conditional calculations. For supplied positive-real parameter enclosure interfaces, the square-well verification additionally constructs the two generally non-dyadic spherical arguments and certifies the matching coefficients to any positive rational absolute tolerance using sufficiently large finite caps. Zero-containing coefficient enclosures carry no relative certificate. No physical resonance is asserted.
