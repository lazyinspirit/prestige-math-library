# Evolutionary incompressible flow discretization

This draft treats Newtonian incompressible Navier–Stokes on bounded connected Lipschitz polygonal or polyhedral domains in dimensions two and three, with viscosity $\nu>0$, homogeneous Dirichlet velocity data, and pressure in the zero-mean gauge $L^2_0(D)$. The bilinear viscosity convention is $a_\nu(u,v)=2\nu(\varepsilon(u),\varepsilon(v))$ and $b(v,p)=-(p,\operatorname{div}v)$. The nonlinear term is the real skew form in [[def-na-evolutionary-skew-convective-form]]; its cancellation is independent of pointwise discrete incompressibility.

## Evolution problem and discrete step

The regular-pressure energy weak formulation and its time-space integrability are stated in [[def-na-evolutionary-incompressible-ns-weak-solution]] and [[lem-na-evolutionary-ns-weak-form-is-well-defined]]. That definition specifies a regular-pressure solution class; it does not assert existence in that class for all energy data. The weak MINI kernel $Z_h$ is distinguished from the exact divergence-free kernel; the scheme uses the stable continuous P1-bubble/P1 pair supplied by [[mixed-finite-elements-and-stokes-stability]].

[[def-na-mini-navier-stokes-backward-euler-scheme]] defines the mixed backward Euler equations with cell-average forcing. [[thm-na-mini-be-step-existence-energy]] proves finite-step existence by an outward-pointing Brouwer argument and records both the work-form energy inequality and its mesh-independent consequence. The coercivity, pressure reconstruction at each finite step, skew cancellation, and forcing estimate are given in the local proof.

## Velocity compactness, limits, and uniqueness

The Fortin map estimates in [[lem-na-mini-fortin-l2-approximation]], the second-order $L^2$ estimate on $H^2$ data in [[lem-na-mini-fortin-h2-approximation]], and the finite-probe argument in [[lem-na-mini-weak-solenoidal-compactness]] give strong $L^2_tL^2_x$ compactness without treating weakly divergence-free MINI velocities as pointwise divergence-free. The local [[lem-na-solenoidal-scalar-equations-give-weak-continuity]] turns the scalar test identities into a weakly continuous $H_\sigma$ representative. [[thm-na-mini-navier-stokes-be-subsequential-limit]] proves the subsequential solenoidal velocity equation, weak initial trace, and work-form energy inequality for arbitrary vanishing mesh and time-step sizes. The additive [[lem-na-solenoidal-evolution-integrated-pressure-recovery]] reconstructs a unique mean-zero pressure distribution with a bounded weakly continuous primitive for that limit, and proves bounded discrete pressure primitives and convergence of the discrete pressures as time distributions. The original design additionally required every such limit to have pressure in $L^{4/3}_tL^2_0$. That universal conclusion is false under its original hypotheses: the exact nonlinear square counterexample on the companion page has $L^2_tL^2_x$ forcing and an $H_\sigma$ initial datum, but no pressure in that class. The primitive result retains the exact criterion: regular pressure exists if and only if the actual full distributional derivative $u_t$ belongs to $L^{4/3}(0,T;V^*)$.

The pressure-free [[lem-na-two-dimensional-solenoidal-energy-velocity-uniqueness]] proves strong initial trace and uniqueness for two-dimensional solenoidal energy velocities. [[thm-na-ns-weak-solution-uniqueness-2d-and-weak-strong-3d]] proves unconditional uniqueness in two dimensions and weak–strong uniqueness in three dimensions only under its explicit strong-solution regularity and equation hypotheses.

## Smooth branch and instantaneous pressure refutations

[[thm-na-mini-solenoidal-stokes-projection-error]] supplies the finite-dimensional energy projection and its MINI approximation rate. [[thm-na-mini-be-smooth-velocity-error]] proves a fully discrete velocity estimate on a smooth no-slip branch, including the required time-step restriction and explicit viscosity dependence. Its Gronwall factor contains $\nu^{-3}$; it makes no Reynolds-uniform claim. The additive [[thm-na-mini-be-smooth-integrated-pressure-error]] proves the corresponding first-order estimate for the pressure primitive and a precise negative time norm under the same initialization.

The PE06 design originally asked for both universal instantaneous pressure regularity and an instantaneous smooth pressure-error rate. The first clause is refuted by the square example; the second is refuted by the independent smooth graded-mesh construction on the companion page, under its original data, arbitrary shape-regular grading, mass initialization and time-partition hypotheses. [[rem-na-pe06-pressure-recovery-escalation]] retains the original formulation, the exact generic derivative criterion, and the distinction between full-test derivative estimates and restricted solenoidal bounds. The proposed uniform $H^1$ stability of the $L^2$-orthogonal projector onto $Z_h$ is false on the square MINI family and is not used as a supplier. The square construction supplies only the generic refutation. The smooth construction supplies its own actual pressure observation and nonlinear backward Euler realization; it preserves the positive velocity and primitive-pressure theorems.

## Worked cases

The square energy-data pressure counterexample, the smooth graded-mesh pressure-rate counterexample, the periodic Taylor–Green solution, the no-forcing discrete energy budget, the zero-mean pressure gauge, a matching unit-square mesh family, and a conditional three-dimensional computation are on [[energy-stable-incompressible-navier-stokes-discretization-examples]]. The periodic vortex uses a torus and is explicitly separate from the no-slip theorem branch.
