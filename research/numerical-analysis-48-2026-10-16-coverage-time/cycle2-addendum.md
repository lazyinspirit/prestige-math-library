### T10/T11 hard-sphere addendum

For finitely many spherical particles with masses m_i>0, radii r_i>0 and smooth external forces on a compact separated configuration tube, define pair guard ||q_i-q_j||-(r_i+r_j). An isolated binary impact has unit normal n=(q_i-q_j)/||q_i-q_j|| and preimpact relative normal velocity v_n=(v_i-v_j) dot n<0. For prescribed restitution e in [0,1], impulse J=-(1+e)v_n/(1/m_i+1/m_j) gives v_i^+=v_i^-+J n/m_i, v_j^+=v_j^--J n/m_j. Prove total linear momentum conservation, normal relative velocity -e v_n and kinetic energy change -(1-e²)v_n²/[2(1/m_i+1/m_j)]. Tangential velocities are unchanged in this frictionless model. These are conditional mathematical collision laws, not a claim that restitution is constant in real materials. Event-driven integration invokes T10 transversality, dense trajectory/guard certificates, event separation, finite event count and restart residual error. Include collision search cost O(P²) naive and any spatial neighbor structure's actual assumptions. Simultaneous collisions, grazing v_n=0, dense frictional multi-contact and inelastic collapse/Zeno violate the isolated binary theorem; return explicit unresolved-event/budget status, or invoke a separately specified complementarity model. T11's scalar complementarity result is not advertised as uniqueness of arbitrary granular frictional contact. A DEM soft-contact penalty is a different ODE model; convergence of penalty to rigid impacts requires its own stiffness/restitution calibration limit and is not inferred from timestep convergence.

## T19 — `parallel-in-time-parareal-and-mgrit`

**Disposition:** genuinely-new. No canonical Parareal/MGRIT carrier located by filename/content searches. **Methods:** for fine propagator F and coarse G over macro H, U0^k=u0, U_(n+1)^(k+1)=G(U_n^(k+1))+F(U_n^k)-G(U_n^k); initialize by serial G. Parallel F evaluations followed by serial coarse correction; finite sweep/time budgets. Target is the sequential fine trajectory, not the exact ODE unless F's error is added.

**Proof:** consistency induction proves U_n^k=F^n(u0) for n<=k, hence exact fine solution after at most N sweeps in exact arithmetic, independent of a contraction assertion. For useful convergence, assume on a common invariant tube ||G(x)-G(y)||<=a||x-y|| and ||(F-G)(x)-(F-G)(y)||<=b||x-y||, with a<=1+CH, b<=C H^(q+1). Error recurrence e_(n+1)^(k+1)<=a e_n^(k+1)+b e_n^k yields by induction binomial(n,k)b^k max(1,a)^n times a bounded initial-error envelope, with endpoint conventions and all iterates remaining in tube; fixed T then gives iteration-dependent acceleration. Initial and fine/coarse algebraic errors are additive forcing, not hidden exactness. For oscillatory/nonnormal or unstable coarse maps, a,b may make the bound useless.

**MGRIT:** define linear all-at-once bidiagonal time operator A_t for u_n-Phi u_(n-1)=r_n, F/C splitting, ideal interpolation/elimination Schur complement with exact coarse propagator Phi^m, practical coarse propagator Psi, F/FCF relaxation and coarse correction. Multilevel recursive cycle requires separately bounded level operators and recursion, not transfer of two-grid contraction.

**Same-frontier prerequisites:** all-at-once block elimination, forced-binomial error lemma, norm contraction and fine-error decomposition owned T19; time propagators consume NA15/T01. **Examples:** dissipative heat modes, oscillatory coarse phase error, nonlinear tube escape. **Cost:** N fine macro solves parallel over processors plus N serial coarse solves per iteration, synchronization/communication, iterations and coarse levels included; speedup only under actual processor/work model. **Sources, unread candidates:** Lions/Maday/Turinici, C R Acad Sci Paris 332 (2001), original Parareal, https://doi.org/10.1016/S0764-4442(00)01793-6 ; Falgout et al., parallel time integration with MGRIT, author portal https://computing.llnl.gov/projects/parallel-time-integration-multigrid . ## T21 — `finite-bit-random-streams-and-variate-generation`

**Disposition:** extend-existing substantial algorithm/error guarantee. `ex-quantile-coupling-on-the-real-line` already proves generalized inverse Q(U) has the target law; `lem-randomized-probability-integral-transform` treats the reverse randomized CDF transform. The new guarantee is actual bit/stream implementation and finite-distribution error plus Box-Muller sampling, absent from inspected canonical methods.

**Randomness model:** independent ideal fair bits B_j on an explicitly constructed countable product probability space; U=sum B_j 2^-j has uniform law by dyadic cylinder intervals and continuity of measure. Disjoint bit blocks/streams preserve independence. A b-bit K uniform on {0,...,2^b-1} gives U_b=(K+1/2)2^-b, coupling |U-U_b|<=2^(-b-1), hence W1 at most that and Kolmogorov discrepancy at most 2^-b. Finite-support versus continuous law has total variation distance1, so no TV-small assertion. Independence of finite outputs requires disjoint independent bit blocks; recycling bits changes dependence.

**Practical finite generators:** define an actual full-period LCG modulo 2^w with odd increment and multiplier a congruent1 mod4, w>=2; prove full period by induction modulo powers of2 using affine iterate arithmetic, and state finite state/period bound for every deterministic generator. With random uniform initial state each single state is uniform, but adjacent states satisfy the recurrence and are not independent. Give a counter-based stream interface as a deterministic keyed permutation of finite counters; a random permutation/key model or computational pseudorandomness assumption must be explicit, never an IID theorem. No cryptographic-quality claim or empirical-test proof of independence. Non-overlapping blocks avoid exact reuse only within certified period; do not imply probabilistic independence.

**Variates:** inverse CDF already canonical; add implemented bracketed inverse with certified |F(xhat)-u|<=epsilon on a monotone continuous CDF, finite-bit U discrepancy adds to Kolmogorov error (plus any actual CDF approximation error). Spatial error requires density f>=m>0 on the inversion bracket, and tails handled separately. Discrete CDF binary search gives exact categorical law under ideal U, finite-bit count discrepancy bounded per threshold; Bernoulli rational sampling via unbiased integer rejection has geometric expected bit/block cost and budget failure; finite budget discarded separately.

Box-Muller uses independent ideal U1,U2 in (0,1), Z1=sqrt(-2 log U1)cos(2pi U2), Z2=sqrt(-2 log U1)sin(2pi U2). Prove independent standard-normal density by polar change of variables and product density normalization; same-pair polar Jacobian/measure change prerequisite must be proved if not published. Finite bits imply bounded normal tails and discrete output, never exact Gaussian; couple midpoint U_b to U, restrict U1>=delta and guard angular/endpoint arithmetic, derivative bounds give explicit Wasserstein/bounded-Lipschitz output error on that event, while discarded event probability <=delta+2^-b. Tail expectations use proved Gaussian/Rayleigh moments and Cauchy-Schwarz; elementary log/sqrt/trig evaluation errors supplied by precision owner add through those derivative bounds. No Ziggurat implementation required if not fully specified/proved; rejection algorithm may be a later optional genuinely new optimization only with envelope/area proof. ## NA15 nonlinear RKC construction (bind instead of stopping at linear example)

Preserve the simple linear proof above as the stability model, and include this actual autonomous nonlinear second-order stabilized explicit RK recurrence. For s>=2 set w1=3/(s²-1), b0=b1=b2=1/4 and b_j=(j²-1)/(3j²) for j>=2, a_j=1-b_j. Let Y0=y, Y1=y+b1 w1 h f(y). For j=2,...,s define mu_j=2 b_j/b_(j-1), nu_j=-b_j/b_(j-2), mt_j=2 b_j w1/b_(j-1), gt_j=-a_(j-1) mt_j, and

Yj=(1-mu_j-nu_j)y+mu_j Y_(j-1)+nu_j Y_(j-2)+mt_j h f(Y_(j-1))+gt_j h f(y).

Output Ys. It is an explicit RK method because every stage is an affine combination of y and earlier RHS evaluations. At constant f its stage abscissae follow c1=b1 w1 and c_j=b_j w1 j² for j>=2, hence c_s=1. Derive the coefficient of f'f by expanding the recurrence through h², or equivalently use the one order2 rooted tree plus actual scalar stability polynomial a_s+b_s T_s(1+w1 z), to prove final coefficient1/2. This equivalence is justified only through order2; higher-order linear matching does not establish nonlinear order. For f in C2 with bounded derivatives on a controlled tube and all stages admitted, Taylor remainder plus one-step Lipschitz propagation proves global order2 for fixed s on fixed T. The long real stability interval remains a scalar linear/normal-matrix guarantee; nonlinear dissipation, positivity, SSP and stiffness-uniform accuracy do not follow. Time-dependent f is handled by augmenting t'=1 and using the actual derived c_j, not an arbitrary stage-time guess. Include an explicit finite-stage domain check and s-dependent roundoff amplification. Damped w0>1 variants are optional only with separately verified coefficients; the undamped scheme already has the proved interval and actual nonlinear method.
