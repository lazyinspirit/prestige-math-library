## Common contract for all proposals

A page gives algorithms with concrete access to f, derivatives/Jacobian-vector products, noise/Poisson samples, linear/nonlinear solves and history interpolation. State arithmetic model and charge each solve/action/sample; factorization reuse is conditional on an unchanged matrix. Distinguish truncation, algebraic solve, action/history error, statistical error and scientific model approximation. Every rate is finite horizon with constants and norms identified; stiffness-uniform, mesh-uniform, long-time and invariant-measure rates require separate theorems. Finite iteration/depth budgets return a failure status, not a fabricated converged value. An implicit method requires a selected admissible branch. A scalar stability calculation does not imply nonlinear dissipativity or nonnormal-matrix power bounds.

Residual solve error rho_n contributes <= sum propagated ||rho_n||/(1+hmu). Do not impose hL small as the only stiff existence proof. **Proof route/prereqs.** Same-pair lemmas: finite-dimensional strongly monotone continuous onto map via coercivity/Brouwer (or minimization only for gradient f); contraction branch; SDIRK Taylor order2; two-stage Radau quadrature/stage order3 and rational-function A/L stability.
