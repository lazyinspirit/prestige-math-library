## Definition

Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional real vector space, and let $A,B\le V$ be linear
subspaces. They are **transverse**, written $A\pitchfork B$, when

$$A+B=V.$$

Equivalently,

$$\dim(A\cap B)=\dim A+\dim B-\dim V$$

by [[thm-dimension-formula]].
