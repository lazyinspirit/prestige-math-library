## Mathematical audit and proof route

- **Binary resultant:** `def-sylvester-resultant-of-binary-forms` fixes nominated positive degrees, including zero forms and zero leading coefficients, and ordered Sylvester bases. Scaling, specialization, and the infinity chart are proved before the root criterion. When one leading coefficient is nonzero, the Sylvester map becomes a block triangular map whose lower block is multiplication by the other polynomial on `K[T]/(f)`; a filtration by linear factors, valid with repeated roots, identifies that determinant with the published monic root product. Both-zero leading coefficients give the infinity root and a direct kernel. The criterion is conditional on a supplied algebraic closure `K` and asserts a geometric point of `P^1(K)`, with no rational-root assertion or AC spent on constructing `K`. `def-algebraic-closure` and `def-projective-space-points` are explicit well-definedness dependencies. If a later consumer needs existence of `K` for every field, it must invoke published `thm-existence-of-algebraic-closures` and its AC assumption.
- **Complete intersection:** Gauss induction supplies the actual three-variable UFD; coprimality makes `(F,G)` regular. Two graded multiplication exact sequences give `(1-t^d)(1-t^e)/(1-t)^3` and eventual coefficient `de`. The homogeneous quotient is never called Artinian.
- **Dimension and nonemptiness:** Under AC, the published polynomial-ring height bound gives `dim k[x_0,x_1,x_2]=3`; height-one primes in this UFD are principal and cannot contain both coprime forms. Thus every prime over `(F,G)` has height at least two. Krull height gives a minimal prime of height two, and the irrelevant maximal ideal gives a quotient chain of length one; an ambient chain of length four rules out a quotient chain of length two. A chain of two homogeneous Proj primes would likewise give ambient length four. To prove `Proj` nonempty without assuming the chosen minimal prime is homogeneous, an empty standard cover would make every coordinate nilpotent; the quotient would then be finite-dimensional over `k`, contradicting its ring dimension one. Hence `Proj` is nonempty and zero-dimensional.
- **Finite length bridge:** Noether normalization, lying over, going up, and the Artinian product theorem turn each zero-dimensional standard chart into finite local Artinian factors, with finite residue degrees. For finite `k`, base change to `k(t)`: for each finite residue field `κ/k`, `κ[t]` is a domain and its localization `κ⊗_k k(t)` is a finite-dimensional domain over `k(t)`, hence a field. Tensoring the local composition series preserves length and Hilbert-piece dimensions. Over the resulting infinite field, choose one degree-one form avoiding all support, dehomogenize to a finite algebra `A`, and prove `S_m→A` eventually surjective by bounded denominators. Its kernel is the degree-`m` part of `L`-torsion; this torsion vanishes on all standard charts, is finitely generated, and is killed by a power of the irrelevant ideal, so it vanishes in high degrees. Thus the eventual Hilbert value is the intrinsic local sum, even when the homogeneous ideal is unsaturated.
- **Result:** The total length is `de`, weighted by `[κ(x):k]` over arbitrary `k`; the tangent line/conic example computes `k[u]/(u²)` directly. The algebraic length corollary supplies planned AV-8 `plane-curves-local-intersection-multiplicity-and-bezout` at order 366.063. That consumer remains planned with an empty plan inventory and is **not** treated as published. The later local-equation invariance and geometric intersection formulation remain its obligations.
