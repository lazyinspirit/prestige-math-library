**`lem-piecewise-smooth-embeddings-are-stable-under-fine-c-one-approximation`**

**`lem-relative-shape-controlled-secants-approximate-smooth-simplex-maps`**

Let $K$ be a finite geometric complex and $f:K\to\mathbb R^q$ piecewise smooth. There are subdivisions $K_m$ agreeing on every original face, with mesh tending to zero and a single lower thickness bound $\theta>0$ for every positive-dimensional simplex. Their secant maps $S_m$, defined by affine interpolation of the values of $f$ at vertices, converge strongly in piecewise $C^1$ to $f$.

If $L\subset K$ is a subcomplex and $U$ an open neighbourhood of $|L|$, the approximation can instead be chosen pointwise equal to $f$ on a neighbourhood of $|L|$ and equal to a secant map outside $U$, on shape-controlled subdivisions. Subdivision of $L$ is permitted; it does not alter $f$ there. All affine target-plane constraints already satisfied by $f$ on original simplices are preserved.

There is also a local extension statement: given a finite subcomplex $A\subset K$, a sufficiently small strong approximation $g$ to $f|_A$ on any subdivision extends to a prescribed small strong approximation $G$ to $f$ on a subdivision of $K$, with $G=f$ and with the original simplices unchanged outside the closed star of $A$. The permitted error of $g$ depends on the requested error and the finite original simplices, not on the shape of the subdivision of $A$. If the correction $g-f$ has zero component under an affine linear target map $p$ on $A$, then the extended correction has zero $p$ component everywhere. This last extension does not assert a shape bound on its coned simplices.

**`lem-relative-smooth-triangulations-can-be-fitted-while-preserving-a-bundle-projection`**

Let $F:K\to M$ and $G:L\to M$ be whole-star smooth embeddings of finite complexes into a smooth manifold without boundary. Given any positive strong $C^1$ tolerance, there are approximations on finite subdivisions fitting in a full subcomplex whose union is a whole-star smooth embedding.

The same conclusion holds for bundle diagrams $\pi F=hr_K$, $\pi G=hr_L$ over a fixed whole-star smooth base embedding $h:D\to B$, when all relevant images are in a smooth bundle with product trivializations. The final approximations satisfy these **same** diagrams with $h$ and $r_K,r_L$ unchanged as point maps, so the bundle projection is preserved exactly. The union map has the induced affine map to $D$.

The construction may be supported in a prescribed open neighbourhood $V$ of the new piece being fitted, including only the old pieces whose images meet that neighbourhood. In particular it fixes a closed protected region $R$ pointwise whenever all newly processed pieces and their required closed stars have images in $V$, with $\overline V\cap R=\varnothing$, and the maps already fit on the pieces outside this support. This is the fixed-collar relative interface: it does not assert arbitrary pointwise fixed-side fitting without the neighbourhood/separation hypotheses. Additional local affine plane constraints are preserved if the chosen charts make them coordinate planes and the already supplied diagrams respect them.

**`thm-elliptic-differential-operators-on-compact-vector-bundles-have-parametrices-and-regularity`**

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $M$ be a compact smooth manifold without boundary of real dimension $n\ge2$, equipped with a smooth positive density, and $E\to M$ a finite-rank real Euclidean or complex Hermitian smooth vector bundle. Let $P$ be a smooth-coefficient second-order differential operator on $E$, formally symmetric for the density and metric, whose positive-frequency principal symbol is
$$\sigma_2(P)(x,\xi)=a_x(\xi,\xi)I_{E_x},$$
where $a$ is a smooth positive-definite real quadratic form on cotangent vectors. Thus in any local frame $P=-a^{ij}\partial_i\partial_j+B^j\partial_j+C$, with scalar real positive-definite $a^{ij}$ and matrix-valued lower coefficients. Nonnegativity of $P$ is not needed here.

For nonnegative integers $k$, define $H^k(M,E)$ by requiring the coefficients of each member of a finite smooth partition of unity times the section, in precompact bundle charts and extended by zero, to lie in $H^k(\mathbb R^n)$; the norm is the square root of the sum of these component Sobolev norms squared. Different such choices give equivalent norms. The following hold.

1. These are Hilbert spaces, smooth sections are dense, $H^0=L^2$ with equivalent norms, $H^{k+1}\hookrightarrow H^k$ is compact, and $H^k\hookrightarrow C^r$ continuously whenever $k>r+n/2$.
2. If $u\in H^1$ and $Pu=f\in H^k$ distributionally, then $u\in H^{k+2}$ and
$$\|u\|_{H^{k+2}}\le C_k(\|f\|_{H^k}+\|u\|_{L^2}).$$
In particular smooth data give smooth weak solutions. The same estimate holds for all $u\in H^{k+2}$.
3. For sufficiently large real $\lambda>0$, $P+\lambda:H^2\to L^2$ is a bounded bijection with bounded inverse $B:L^2\to H^2$. As an operator on $L^2$, $B$ is compact, injective and selfadjoint, has dense range $H^2$, and the unbounded operator $P$ with domain exactly $H^2$ is selfadjoint. The map $B:H^k\to H^{k+2}$ is bounded for every nonnegative integer $k$ and preserves smooth sections.
4. It supplies two-sided parametrices **modulo compact operators**:
$$PB=I-\lambda B\quad\hbox{on }L^2,\qquad BP=I-\lambda B\quad\hbox{on }H^2.$$
Here on the second space $B$ denotes its restriction after inclusion $H^2\hookrightarrow L^2$; it is compact on $H^2$. No assertion concerning arbitrary-order elliptic operators, or pseudodifferential symbol classes, is part of this theorem.

**`thm-sobolev-compactness-and-elliptic-estimates-give-fredholm-hodge-decomposition`**

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $M,E,P$ have exactly the compact, boundaryless, scalar-positive-symbol and formal symmetry hypotheses of [[thm-elliptic-differential-operators-on-compact-vector-bundles-have-parametrices-and-regularity]], with $\operatorname{dom}P=H^2(M,E)$. Write $\mathcal H=\ker P$.

Then $\mathcal H$ is finite dimensional and consists of smooth sections, $\operatorname{ran}P=\mathcal H^\perp$ is closed in $L^2$, and
$$L^2(M,E)=\mathcal H\mathbin{\oplus^\perp}\operatorname{ran}P.$$
Let $\Pi$ be the orthogonal projection onto $\mathcal H$. There is a unique linear operator $G:L^2\to H^2\cap\mathcal H^\perp$ with
$$PG=I-\Pi\quad\hbox{on }L^2,\qquad GP=I-\Pi\quad\hbox{on }H^2,\qquad G\Pi=\Pi G=0.$$
It is bounded from $H^k$ to $H^{k+2}$ for every integer $k\ge0$, compact and selfadjoint on $L^2$, and preserves smooth sections. For all such $k$,
$$H^k=\mathcal H\oplus P(H^{k+2}),\qquad C^\infty(M,E)=\mathcal H\oplus P(C^\infty(M,E)),$$
where the second summands are the corresponding intersections with $\mathcal H^\perp$, and the decompositions are topological. The projection is smoothing: for every integer $r\ge0$, $\|\Pi f\|_{C^r}\le C_r\|f\|_2$. Consequently $G$ is a two-sided parametrix with finite-rank smoothing remainders. If additionally $(Pu,u)_2\ge0$ for smooth $u$, then $P$ and $G$ are nonnegative on their respective domains.

**`thm-hodge-laplacian-kernel-represents-de-rham-and-dolbeault-cohomology`**

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $M$ be a compact smooth Riemannian manifold without boundary, of real dimension at least two. Its metric defines the $L^2$ pairing on forms by the Riemannian density; orientation is unnecessary. Let $d^*$ denote the formal adjoint of $d$, and $\Delta=dd^*+d^*d$ on total real forms, or on their complexification. With domain $H^2$ the operator is nonnegative and selfadjoint. Its degreewise smooth kernels $\mathcal H^k$ are finite dimensional, and there is an orthogonal smooth decomposition
$$\Omega^k(M)=\mathcal H^k\mathbin{\oplus^\perp}d\Omega^{k-1}(M)\mathbin{\oplus^\perp}d^*\Omega^{k+1}(M).$$
The map $h\mapsto[h]$ is an isomorphism $\mathcal H^k\to H^k_{\mathrm{dR}}(M)$, over $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$ as appropriate.

More generally let $X$ be a compact complex manifold of complex dimension $m\ge1$ with a smooth Hermitian metric. Let $E$ be a holomorphic bundle of finite rank with a smooth Hermitian metric (meaning its local frames have holomorphic invertible transition matrices). On $E$-valued $(p,q)$ forms, define $\bar\partial_E$ by the coefficientwise Dolbeault operator in holomorphic bundle charts and let $\bar\partial_E^*$ be its formal adjoint. For each fixed $p$, $\Delta_{\bar\partial_E}=\bar\partial_E\bar\partial_E^*+\bar\partial_E^*\bar\partial_E$ on the sum over $q$ is nonnegative and selfadjoint on $H^2$; its smooth harmonic spaces $\mathcal H_E^{p,q}$ are finite dimensional and
$$\Omega^{p,q}(X,E)=\mathcal H_E^{p,q}\mathbin{\oplus^\perp}\bar\partial_E\Omega^{p,q-1}(X,E)\mathbin{\oplus^\perp}\bar\partial_E^*\Omega^{p,q+1}(X,E).$$
In particular harmonic forms represent the smooth Dolbeault quotient
$$H_{\bar\partial}^{p,q}(X,E):=\ker\bar\partial_E/\operatorname{im}\bar\partial_E$$
uniquely. Terms outside the degree range are zero. This asserts smooth Dolbeault cohomology; an identification with sheaf cohomology requires a separate Dolbeault-resolution comparison theorem.

For either complex write $D=d$ or $D=\bar\partial_E$, $P=DD^*+D^*D$, and let $\Pi,G$ be the preceding Green projection and inverse. They preserve degree, $G$ preserves smooth forms, and on smooth forms
$$DG=GD,\qquad D^*G=GD^*,\qquad D\Pi=\Pi D=D^*\Pi=\Pi D^*=0.$$
Every closed smooth $u$ satisfies $u=\Pi u+D(D^*Gu)$.

**`lem-quiver-full-flag-incidence-has-a-smooth-projective-compactification`**

Assume the Axiom of Choice ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]) as inherited from the relative Proj and scheme-properness suppliers. Let $\Gamma$ be a finite loopless quiver, with arbitrary finitely many arrows between two distinct vertices. Let $V=\bigoplus_iV_i$ be a finite-dimensional complex graded vector space, $\beta_i=\dim V_i$, $E_V=\bigoplus_{h:i\to j}\operatorname{Hom}(V_i,V_j)$ and $G_V=\prod_i\operatorname{GL}(V_i)$. Let $\nu=(\nu_1,\ldots,\nu_m)$ be a word with $\beta_i$ occurrences of $i$. Let $\mathcal F_\nu$ be the variety of increasing full graded flags $0=F^0\subset F^1\subset\cdots\subset F^m=V$ with $F^r/F^{r-1}$ of color $\nu_r$. Then $X_\nu=\{(x,F):x_hF_i^r\subset F_j^r\text{ for all }h:i\to j,r\}$ is a $G_V$-equivariant vector subbundle $W_\nu$ of $E_V\times\mathcal F_\nu$. Its rank is $\sum_{h:i\to j}\#\{(p,q):\nu_p=i,\nu_q=j,q<p\}$ and its dimension is that rank plus $\sum_i\beta_i(\beta_i-1)/2$. The map $\pi_\nu:X_\nu\to E_V$ is projective.

Put $\mathbb P_{\rm lines}(U)=\operatorname{Proj}\operatorname{Sym}(U^\vee)$. Then the bundle inclusion defines a $G_V$-equivariant projective map $\bar\pi_\nu:\mathbb P_{\rm lines}(W_\nu\oplus\mathcal O)\to\mathbb P_{\rm lines}(E_V\oplus\mathbb C)$ whose source is smooth projective and whose restriction over the affine chart $t\ne0$ is exactly $\pi_\nu$. The analogous associated-bundle construction over any finite product of complex Grassmannians formed from tautological bundles is a projective map between smooth projective varieties, restricting over its affine chart to the associated incidence map.
