## Statement

Assume the flag-variety setup of
[[def-pa36-flag-tdo-and-module]]. If $\kappa$ is antidominant, then every
quasi-coherent left $\mathcal D_\kappa$-module $M$ satisfies
$$H^i(X,M)=0\qquad(i>0).$$
Moreover, $\Gamma(X,-)$ is exact on the category of quasi-coherent left
$\mathcal D_\kappa$-modules.
If $\kappa$ is also regular, then $\Gamma(X,M)=0$ implies $M=0$. The
antidominance and regularity conditions are independent hypotheses; regularity
alone does not imply the vanishing assertion.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The Axiom of Choice, $X=G/B$, an ample line bundle
$\mathcal O_X(-\eta)$ for sufficiently dominant integral $\eta$, an
antidominant (and, for conservativity, regular) $\kappa$, and a quasi-coherent
left $\mathcal D_\kappa$-module $M$.

[F1] Every quasi-coherent $\mathcal O_X$-module is a filtered union of
coherent submodules, and cohomology commutes with those filtered colimits
([[lem-pa36-qcoh-filtered-union-and-cech-colimits]]).

[F2] For coherent $\mathcal F$ and ample $L$, there is an integer $n_0$
such that $H^i(X,\mathcal F\otimes L^{\otimes n})=0$ for every $i>0$ and
$n\ge n_0$ ([[thm-serre-vanishing]]).

[F3] For coherent $\mathcal F$ and ample $L$, the twist
$\mathcal F\otimes L^{\otimes n}$ is globally generated for all sufficiently
large $n$ ([[lem-eventual-global-generation-coherent-twists]],
[[def-globally-generated-sheaf]]).

[F4] The natural injection
$M\to M\otimes L\otimes V$ splits for antidominant $\kappa$, and the natural
surjection $M\otimes V^*\to M\otimes L$ splits for regular antidominant
$\kappa$, as sheaves of complex vector spaces; this is enough for the split maps on sheaf cohomology. Here $L=\mathcal O_X(-\eta)$ and
$V=\Gamma(X,L)^*$ ([[lem-pa36-ample-translation-splittings]]).

[F5] For a sheaf of rings, the right-derived global sections on its module
category agree with ordinary sheaf cohomology of the underlying module sheaf
([[lem-pa36-module-global-sections-derived-cohomology]]).

## Proof

1.1 Let $i>0$ and write $M=\varinjlim_j\mathcal F_j$ as the filtered union of coherent $\mathcal O_X$-submodules from [F1]. Fix one $\mathcal F_j$. Choose a sufficiently dominant integral $\eta$ so that $H^i(X,\mathcal F_j\otimes L)=0$ by [F2], with $L=\mathcal O_X(-\eta)$. The composite $$H^i(X,\mathcal F_j)\to H^i(X,M)\to H^i(X,M\otimes L\otimes V)$$ factors through $H^i(X,\mathcal F_j\otimes L\otimes V)$ by naturality of the coevaluation map. This last group is $H^i(X,\mathcal F_j\otimes L)\otimes V=0$ because $V$ is finite-dimensional. By [F4], the map $H^i(X,M)\to H^i(X,M\otimes L\otimes V)$ is injective. Thus $H^i(X,\mathcal F_j)\to H^i(X,M)$ is zero for every $j$. Taking the filtered colimit and using [F1] gives $H^i(X,M)=0$. [F1, F2, F4]

1.2 For every quasi-coherent left $\mathcal D_\kappa$-module $M$, [F5] and step 1.1 give $R^q\Gamma_{\operatorname{Mod}(\mathcal D_\kappa)}(X,M)\cong H^q(X,M)=0$ for each $q>0$. Given a short exact sequence $0\to M'\to M\to M''\to0$ of quasi-coherent $\mathcal D_\kappa$-modules, view it in the full module-sheaf category. Its derived-functor long exact sequence and the vanishing of $R^1\Gamma(M')$ show that $\Gamma(X,M)\to\Gamma(X,M'')$ is surjective; left exactness supplies exactness at the other terms. Hence $\Gamma$ is exact on quasi-coherent $\mathcal D_\kappa$-modules for antidominant $\kappa$. [F5, step 1.1, algebra]

2.1 Now assume $\kappa$ is regular antidominant and $\Gamma(X,M)=0$. For any coherent $\mathcal F_j\subseteq M$, choose a sufficiently dominant integral $\eta$ so that $\mathcal F_j\otimes L$ is globally generated by [F3]. By the split surjection in [F4], $\Gamma(X,M\otimes L)$ is a direct summand of $\Gamma(X,M\otimes V^*)=\Gamma(X,M)\otimes V^*=0$; hence $\Gamma(X,M\otimes L)=0$. The inclusion $\mathcal F_j\otimes L\hookrightarrow M\otimes L$ remains injective on global sections because $\Gamma$ is left exact, so $\Gamma(X,\mathcal F_j\otimes L)=0$. A globally generated sheaf with no global sections is zero, hence $\mathcal F_j=0$. All coherent subsheaves in [F1] vanish, so $M=0$. This applies the splitting to $M$, not to the arbitrary coherent $\mathcal O_X$-submodule. [F1, F3, F4, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
