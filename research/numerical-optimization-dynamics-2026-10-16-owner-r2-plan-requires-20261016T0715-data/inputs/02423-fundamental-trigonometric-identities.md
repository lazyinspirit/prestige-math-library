The sine--cosine addition formulas and the quotient-function definitions supply the basic algebra on this page. The development keeps every tangent, cotangent, secant, and cosecant denominator visible, and uses the real polynomial and finite-counting prerequisites for the Chebyshev and binomial arguments.

The page derives subtraction, double- and triple-angle, signed half-angle, product-to-sum, and rational half-angle identities before connecting the unit circle with amplitude--phase form. It then develops real polynomial roots and Chebyshev recurrences, multiple-angle identities, and the minimax normalization of $T_n$.
