This page fixes the branch-sensitive conventions that later conformal arguments
depend on. It distinguishes the holomorphic principal logarithm on the slit
plane from the pointwise principal value on the negative axis, defines
branch-based complex powers only after a holomorphic logarithm has been chosen,
and records exactly where branch discrepancies obstruct naive product and power
laws.

From there the page moves through the disc and upper half-plane models of
conformal geometry. Blaschke factors, Schwarz and Schwarz-Pick rigidity,
automorphism classifications, and the normalized Poincare metric are assembled
before the elementary branch-driven maps: sectors, slit planes, the Joukowski
map, and the sine half-strip map. The closing theorem keeps the three simply
connected plane models distinct by ruling out biholomorphisms among
$\widehat{\mathbb C}$, $\mathbb C$, and $\mathbb D$.
