## Examples

These examples verify a circular double-well MEP and show why its straight chord is wrong, exhibit corner and cusp failures in tangent construction, and reject an index-two maximum as the desired saddle. A two-dimensional matrix calculation shows mode sensitivity as the spectral gap closes, while a quartic potential calculates dimer truncation bias and force-noise error exactly. A zero potential gives both infinitely many continuum MEPs and distinct zero-residual finite NEB chains, illustrating why the stable inverse hypotheses are needed.
