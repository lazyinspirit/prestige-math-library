The examples compute the two-node Gauss–Legendre rule from its moments and
Jacobi matrix, derive positive two-node weights from cardinal-polynomial
squares, and count the nodes and exactness range of a four-coordinate tensor
rule. The Legendre example states its countable-choice assumption where the
library's Lebesgue measure construction requires it.
