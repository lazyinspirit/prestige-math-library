## Worked examples

The companion page checks the Euler twist, Green coefficient normalization,
and simple-generator relations in small finite-field representation
categories.

- [[ex-green-hall-square-and-coproduct-for-finite-vector-spaces]] counts the
  $q+1$ lines in $\mathbb F_q^2$ and obtains the mixed Green coefficient
  $\nu^{-1}$.
- [[ex-a2-hall-products-and-quantum-serre]] computes both simple products for
  $1\to2$ and verifies the degree-$(2,1)$ divided-power Serre relation by
  classifying rank-zero and rank-one arrow maps.
- [[cex-kronecker-composition-subalgebra-is-proper]] counts $q+2$ isoclasses
  in degree $(1,1)$ for the Kronecker quiver, while the simple-generated
  component is spanned by at most two products.

These examples distinguish the simple-generated composition subalgebra from
the full Hall algebra. The Kronecker count is a counterexample to any claim
that all loopless-quiver Hall algebras are generated by their simple classes.
