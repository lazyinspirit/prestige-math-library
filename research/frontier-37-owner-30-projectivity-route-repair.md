## Independent proof route in the consumer

1. A strict length-one chain of nonempty irreducible closed subsets in the
   integral curve has the form `Z0 ⊊ C`: otherwise adjoining `C` gives a
   length-two chain. A point `p` of `Z0` is therefore nongeneric and, by the
   curve closed-point result, closed.
2. For an affine chart `U = Spec A` containing `p`, the smooth-curve local-ring
   theorem gives `dim A_m = dim O_(C,p) = 1`, hence `dim A >= 1`. Chains in
   `Spec A` remain strict after taking their closures in `C` (intersecting back
   with `U` recovers the original closed subsets), so `dim A <= dim C = 1`.
   Thus `dim A = 1`. The affine-domain dimension/transcendence-degree theorem
   and the function-field identification yield `trdeg_k k(C) = 1`.
3. The generic local ring is the function field, hence a field. Every other
   point is closed, and its local ring is a DVR by smoothness; DVRs are
   integrally closed. This verifies the normality condition in the proper
   normal-curve rational-function-map supplier.
4. A transcendental `f in k(C)` gives a finite map `C -> P^1_k`. The sheaf
   `O_(P^1)(1)` is ample, and its pullback along this finite map is ample.
   The ample-powers theorem over the Noetherian base `Spec k` gives a power
   that is closed H-very ample. Unpacking that definition gives the required
   closed immersion and projective factorization.

## Live supplier defect and repair route

In `items/lem-proper-normal-curve-rational-function-map.md`, step 3.1 claims
that both `f` and `f^{-1}` are regular at every closed point by applying the
monic-polynomial valuation argument from step 1.2. That argument is only in
the algebraic-over-`k` case, so it does not prove the transcendental branch.
The claim itself is false there: on `C = P^1_k` with `f = t`, the valuation at
infinity is `v_infinity(t) = -1`.

The finite-map conclusion has this repair route. At each closed point the DVR
property implies `f` or `f^{-1}` is regular. The loci where each is regular
are open, contain the generic point, and cover all closed points. On their
overlap `f` is a unit, so the maps to the two standard affine charts glue to a
map to `P^1`. The injection `k(t) -> k(C)` makes this map dominant; properness
of the source and separatedness of the target make it proper. Its closed-point
fibres are proper closed subsets of a one-dimensional integral curve, hence
finite, and its generic fibre is a single point because `k(C)/k(f)` is finite.
Thus it is quasi-finite and hence finite. On either standard chart its finite
coordinate algebra is a finite torsion-free module over the PID `k[t]` or
`k[t^{-1}]`, so it is free; its generic rank is `[k(C):k(f)]`. This proves
finite local freeness without the false simultaneous-regularity claim.

There is also a false, unused extra clause in the supplier Statement: it says
the chart algebras are `k[f]` and `k[f^{-1}]`. For `C = P^1_k` and `f=t^2`,
the preimage of the target chart `A^1` has coordinate ring `k[t]`, not
`k[t^2]`. The proper finite-map interface need not assert chart algebras are
generated by `f`; the consumer uses only existence and finiteness of the map.
The batch-5 repair should correct that interface as well as its proof.
