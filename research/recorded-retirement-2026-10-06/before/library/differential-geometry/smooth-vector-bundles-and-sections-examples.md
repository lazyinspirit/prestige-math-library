The companion page collects the standard witnesses and failure modes for the
bundle constructions. It includes the Mobius bundle, the tautological line
bundle, trivial tangent and normal bundles with explicit frames, the pullback of
the tautological line bundle along the antipodal cover, and the graph and
rank-jump models that explain exactly where the structural theorems apply and
where they fail.
