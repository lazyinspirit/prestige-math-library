This page isolates the exact regularity class for the Lebesgue fundamental
theorem of calculus.  It distinguishes image-null property $(N)$ from a
preimage claim, keeps the nonmonotone substitution theorem conditional, and
records the Henstock--Kurzweil comparison as source-backed but not locally
proved.
