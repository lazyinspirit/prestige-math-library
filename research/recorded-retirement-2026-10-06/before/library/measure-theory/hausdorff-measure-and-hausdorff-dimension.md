Diameter-power covers lead to Hausdorff measure, its critical dimension, Lipschitz comparisons, Euclidean volume comparison, and exact Cantor and digit-set computations. All exponents are finite and nonnegative; covers have nonempty members and may be empty only for the empty set. Countable Choice is assumed for the measure-theoretic results. The exact higher-dimensional normalising factor is recorded separately without a local proof.
