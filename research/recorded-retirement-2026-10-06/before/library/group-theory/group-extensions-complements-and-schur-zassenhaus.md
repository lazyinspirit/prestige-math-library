This page records the basic dictionary for group extensions: equivalence and
morphisms of short exact sequences, complements, retractions, and the outer
action carried by an extension. It then proves the existence half of
Schur-Zassenhaus for normal Hall subgroups, proves the classical conjugacy
statement under a solvability hypothesis on the kernel or quotient, and records
the deeper clean conjugacy theorem honestly as a cited boundary item.

The final items isolate two standard consequences. Coprime finite extensions
split, and a complete kernel forces a split over its centralizer. The examples
page then turns these abstract criteria into concrete witnesses in small finite
groups.
