## Definition

Let $G$ be a finite abelian group, written additively. A normalized **abelian 3-cocycle** is a pair of functions $\omega:G^3\to\mathbb C^\times$ and $r:G^2\to\mathbb C^\times$, equal to $1$ whenever one of their arguments is $0$, satisfying the pentagon equation

$$\omega(a,b,c)\omega(a,b+c,d)\omega(b,c,d)=\omega(a+b,c,d)\omega(a,b,c+d)$$

and the two braided hexagon equations

$$\omega(b,c,a)\omega(a,b,c)r(a,b+c)=r(a,b)r(a,c)\omega(b,a,c),$$

$$r(a+b,c)=\omega(a,b,c)\omega(c,a,b)\omega(a,c,b)^{-1}r(a,c)r(b,c).$$

The associated pointed category has one simple object $g$ in each grade $g\in G$, tensor product $g\otimes h=g+h$, associator $\alpha_{g,h,k}:(g\otimes h)\otimes k\to g\otimes(h\otimes k)$ given by scalar $\omega(g,h,k)$, and braiding $c_{g,h}:g\otimes h\to h\otimes g$ given by scalar $r(g,h)$. For the left dual $g^\vee=-g$, take evaluation $g^\vee\otimes g\to0$ to have scalar $1$ and coevaluation $0\to g\otimes g^\vee$ to have scalar $\omega(g,-g,g)^{-1}$. The first snake is the identity by this choice; the second is the identity because the normalized pentagon at $(g,-g,g,-g)$ gives $\omega(g,-g,g)\omega(-g,g,-g)=1$. A ribbon twist is a function $q:G\to\mathbb C^\times$ with $q(0)=1$, $q(-g)=q(g)$, and

$$q(g+h)=r(g,h)r(h,g)q(g)q(h).$$

Write $b(g,h)=r(g,h)r(h,g)$ for the double-braiding scalar. The pointed ribbon category data include a compatible pivotal structure whose twist is $q$ and whose simple dimensions are $1$; this convention is an explicit input, not a consequence of the quadratic function alone. [[thm-pointed-metric-group-is-modular-iff-radical-trivial]] proves from the two hexagons that $b$ is a symmetric bicharacter and that the Hopf-link matrix has entries $s_{gh}=b(g,h)$. A quadratic function alone is not the category data: the associator, both hexagons, duality maps, and the stated pivotal convention are part of the definition.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A finite abelian group $G$, normalized functions $\omega,r,q$ satisfying the displayed equations, and the chosen pivotal convention of dimension $1$ on each grade.

[F1] A monoidal category requires a unit, associator natural isomorphism satisfying the pentagon, and unit constraints satisfying the triangle ([[def-fusion-and-multifusion-category]]).

[F2] A braiding is a natural isomorphism satisfying the two hexagon equations; a ribbon twist satisfies balancing and dual compatibility ([[def-braided-monoidal-category]], [[def-twist-and-ribbon-structure]]).

[F3] A left dual consists of evaluation and coevaluation satisfying the two snake identities ([[def-rigid-object-and-rigid-monoidal-category]]).

## Proof

**Given:** The data in the Statement.

1.1 On homogeneous simple grades, the associator and braiding are multiplication by the scalars $\omega$ and $r$. Therefore the two paths around the monoidal pentagon agree exactly by the displayed pentagon equation, and each braided hexagon diagram agrees exactly by its corresponding displayed equation. These equalities extend to finite direct sums by linearity and to all homogeneous morphisms by naturality of scalar maps. The grade $0$ acts as the tensor unit because normalization makes every unit associator and braiding scalar equal to $1$. [F1, F2, given, algebra]

2.1 The chosen evaluation and coevaluation have the stated sources and targets. The first snake has scalar $\omega(g,-g,g)^{-1}\omega(g,-g,g)=1$. The second has scalar $\omega(g,-g,g)^{-1}\omega(-g,g,-g)^{-1}=1$, because the pentagon at $(g,-g,g,-g)$ gives $\omega(g,-g,g)\omega(-g,g,-g)=1$. Hence each grade has the specified left dual. [F3, given, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 The scalar $b(g,h)=r(g,h)r(h,g)$ is the double-braiding value. The displayed formula for $q(g+h)$ is exactly the balancing identity on two homogeneous simples, while $q(-g)=q(g)$ is the dual-compatibility condition. Thus the stated scalar twist is ribbon-compatible; the pivotal dimension-one convention is an explicit part of the input data. [F2, given, algebra] ∎
