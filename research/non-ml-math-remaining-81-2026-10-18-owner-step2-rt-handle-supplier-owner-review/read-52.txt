## Statement

For the actual $\ell=3$ ribbon category and weighted surgery construction of [[thm-su2-root-unity-ribbon-category-is-modular]] and [[thm-weighted-rt-tqft-and-factorization]], the normalized Gauss sums are $p_+=1+i$, $p_-=1-i$, and $\kappa=e^{\pi i/4}$. The weighted normalization is unchanged by either Kirby blow-up, while omitting the surgery-signature correction $\kappa^{-\sigma(B)}$ changes the normalized link sum by $\kappa^{\pm1}\ne1$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** $\ell=3$, $q=e^{\pi i/3}$, the positive-trace convention in the sine data, and the surgery normalization of [[lem-modular-gauss-sums-and-kirby-color]].

[F1] The $\ell$-level data give labels $a=0,\ldots,\ell-2$, $d_a=\sin((a+1)\pi/\ell)/\sin(\pi/\ell)$, $\theta_a=q^{a(a+2)/2}$, and the sine Hopf matrix ([[lem-su2-hopf-link-sine-data-from-truncated-fusion]]).

[F2] $p_\pm=\sum_a\theta_a^{\pm1}d_a^2$, $p_+p_-=D^2$, and the normalized blow-up factors are $D^{-1}\kappa^{-1}p_+$ and $D^{-1}\kappa p_-$ ([[lem-modular-gauss-sums-and-kirby-color]]).

[A1] The actual tilting quotient and full weighted RT functor are established by their exact suppliers ([[thm-su2-root-unity-ribbon-category-is-modular]], [[thm-weighted-rt-tqft-and-factorization]]).

## Proof

1.1 The labels are $0,1$. The dimension formula gives $d_0=d_1=1$. The twist formula gives $\theta_0=1$ and $\theta_1=q^{3/2}=e^{\pi i/2}=i$. Thus $D^2=1+1=2$ and choose $D=\sqrt2$. The Gauss sums are $p_+=1+i,\qquad p_-=1-i,\qquad \kappa=\frac{1+i}{\sqrt2}=e^{\pi i/4}.$ In particular $\kappa\ne1$. [F1, given, algebra]

2.1 A positive blow-up multiplies the surgery link value by $p_+$ and changes $(m,\sigma)$ to $(m+1,\sigma+1)$, so its normalized ratio is $D^{-1}\kappa^{-1}p_+=D^{-1}\frac{D}{p_+}p_+=1.$ A negative blow-up has ratio $D^{-1}\kappa p_-=D^{-1}\frac{p_+p_-}{D}=1$ by [F2]. If the factor $\kappa^{-\sigma}$ is omitted, the same two blow-ups multiply the value by $p_+/D=\kappa$ and $p_-/D=\kappa^{-1}$, both nontrivial. Thus an unweighted surgery link sum is not invariant under these moves for this actual category; this is a failure of omitting the signature correction, not an assertion that the weight-zero arrows form a subcategory. [F2, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 The actual modular quotient is supplied by [A1], so the preceding values are realized category data. The full weighted functor uses $\kappa^{n+a-\sigma(B)}$ together with its exact outgoing cap factors. A Kirby blow-up changes neither the marked bordism, its weight nor its cap-reference correction, and step 2.1 cancels its complete surgery ratio. A unit increase in bordism weight instead multiplies the map by $\kappa=e^{\pi i/4}\ne1$. Thus the actual category has a nontrivial framing anomaly, and the proved weighted functor includes it with the positive weight convention. [A1, step 2.1, algebra] ∎
