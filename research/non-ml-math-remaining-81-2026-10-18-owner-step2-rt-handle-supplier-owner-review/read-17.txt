## Statement

Let $\mathcal C$ be modular with $p_\pm=\sum_i\theta_i^{\pm1}d_i^2$, $D^2=\sum_i d_i^2$, and $\kappa=p_+/D$. Then

$$p_+p_-=D^2,\qquad \kappa^{-1}=p_-/D.$$

The formal Kirby color is $\Omega=\sum_i d_i\,i$. A zero-framed $\Omega$ meridian around a simple strand $j$ acts by $D^2\delta_{j,0}$ times the identity, and a $\pm1$-framed $\Omega$ unknot evaluates to $p_\pm$. Hence the surgery normalization

$$\tau(M_L)=D^{-m-1}\kappa^{-\sigma(B)}F(L_\Omega)$$

is invariant under adding/removing a disjoint $\pm1$-framed $\Omega$ unknot; it gives $\tau(S^3)=D^{-1}$ and $\tau(S^2\times S^1)=1$. The same normalized graph evaluation is invariant under full framed surgery-component handle slides and colored-strand slides over surgery components, with all arbitrary exterior graph coupons retained. A topological theorem asserting completeness of these and relative moves is a separate supplier.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A complex modular ribbon fusion category, a framed colored link evaluation $F$, and a surgery diagram with $m$ components and symmetric linking matrix $B$.

[F1] The balancing character identity is $s_{xi}s_{xj}=d_x\sum_kN_{ij}^ks_{xk}$; also $s_{0i}=d_i$, $s_{i^*j^*}=s_{ij}$, $d_{i^*}=d_i$, and $\theta_{i^*}=\theta_i$ ([[lem-balancing-and-hopf-link-character-identity]]).

[F2] $s^2=D^2C$, where $C_{ij}=\delta_{i,j^*}$, and $D^2\ne0$ ([[lem-modular-hopf-matrix-squared]]).

[F3] All $d_i$ are nonzero; trace is additive and multiplicative on tensor products in this complex braided category; fusion multiplicities satisfy Frobenius reciprocity ([[lem-simple-ribbon-fusion-dimensions-are-nonzero]], [[thm-basic-properties-of-the-categorical-trace]], [[def-fusion-rules]]).

[F4] The framed tangle functor evaluates a colored $\pm1$-framed unknot of color $i$ as $\theta_i^{\pm1}d_i$; closed colored links are invariant under framed isotopy ([[thm-a-ribbon-object-defines-a-unique-framed-tangle-evaluation-functor]], [[lem-ribbon-trace-equals-the-framed-closure-evaluation]], [[thm-ribbon-evaluation-is-an-invariant-of-framed-colored-links]], [[def-ribbon-evaluation-of-an-x-colored-closed-braid]]).

[F5] For a simple $X$ and simple $Z$, the partial trace over $Z$ of the double braiding on $X\otimes Z$ is $(s_{XZ}/d_X)1_X$; the trace of the zero-framed $i$-colored unknot is $d_i$ ([[lem-balancing-and-hopf-link-character-identity]], [[lem-ribbon-trace-equals-the-framed-closure-evaluation]]).

[F6] [[lem-kirby-color-full-band-slide-and-coupon-identity]] proves the complete two-$\Omega$ and open colored-strand band-slide identities with fixed arbitrary graph coupons, by explicit finite coend/channel resolution and injective bending. It has no Gauss-sum or surgery-normalization premise.

[A1] The link-evaluation suppliers use $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$ and no full AC ([[def-countable-choice]]).

## Proof

**Given:** The data in the Statement.

1.1 Pivotal trace additivity and tensor multiplicativity give $d_i d_j=\sum_kN_{ij}^kd_k$. Frobenius reciprocity and the change of variable in the fusion sum then give $\sum_i d_iN_{ij}^k=\sum_i d_iN_{k,j^*}^{i}=d_kd_{j^*}=d_kd_j.$ Using the balancing formula from [F1] and summing over $i$ yields $\sum_i\theta_i d_i s_{ij} =\theta_j^{-1}\sum_{i,k}d_iN_{ij}^k\theta_kd_k =\theta_j^{-1}d_jp_+.$ [F1, F3, given, algebra]

1.2 A simple strand $j$ encircled by a zero-framed simple loop $i$ is the scalar $s_{ij}/d_j$ on $j$ by [F5]. Hence the $\Omega$-meridian acts by $\frac1{d_j}\sum_i d_is_{ij} =\frac{(s^2)_{0j}}{d_j} =D^2\delta_{j,0},$ where $d_j\ne0$ by [F3]. Thus $D^{-2}$ times this meridian is the projector onto the unit channel. This proves the projector identity from modular inversion. [F2, F3, F5, given, algebra]

2.1 Multiply the last identity by $s_{j^*,k}$ and sum over $j$. By [F1]–[F2], $\sum_j s_{ij}s_{j^*,k} =\sum_x s_{i^*,x}s_{xk} =(s^2)_{i^*,k} =D^2\delta_{i,k}.$ Therefore $p_+\sum_j\theta_j^{-1}d_js_{j^*,k}=D^2\theta_kd_k$. Reindexing $j$ by $j^*$ and taking $k=0$ gives $p_+p_-=D^2$; since $p_+p_-=D^2\ne0$, both Gauss sums are nonzero and $\kappa^{-1}=p_-/D$. [F1, F2, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 By [F4], a $\pm1$-framed $\Omega$ unknot evaluates to $\sum_i d_i\theta_i^{\pm1}d_i=p_\pm$. For a $+1$ blow-up, the normalization changes by $D^{-1}\kappa^{-1}p_+=1$; for a $-1$ blow-up it changes by $D^{-1}\kappa p_-=p_+p_-/D^2=1$. The empty surgery diagram gives $D^{-1}$. By [F5], a zero-framed unknot colored by $\Omega$ evaluates to $\sum_i d_i^2=D^2$, so its one-component normalization is $D^{-2}D^2=1$, the $S^2\times S^1$ value. [F2, F4, F5, A1, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 Apply [F6] to the full coupon-bearing graph. Its finite coend identity preserves the entire band-slide morphism with every boundary factor and exterior coupon fixed, for either two surgery components or an open colored strand slid over a surgery component. It is independent of the simple-meridian computation of step 1.2. A surgery-component slide changes the linking matrix by the integral basis congruence $B\mapsto P^{\mathsf t}BP$, preserving signature even when $B$ is singular, and preserves the number of surgery components. An open graph-strand slide changes neither surgery matrix nor component count. Therefore every normalization factor is unchanged, proving the full normalized slide identities. Together with step 3.1 this proves all the stated local move identities; it imports no relative topological move-completeness theorem. [F6, step 1.2, step 3.1, algebra] ∎
