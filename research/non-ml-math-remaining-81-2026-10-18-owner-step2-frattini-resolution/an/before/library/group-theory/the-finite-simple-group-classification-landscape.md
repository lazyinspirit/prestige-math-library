This page develops the local finite-group theory of components, the layer and
the generalized Fitting subgroup $F^*(G)$. It proves that distinct components
commute and that $F^*(G)$ contains its centralizer, with the normal-subgroup
arguments needed for those conclusions. Composition factors and elementary
cyclic and alternating simple groups provide context. The examples $C_4$ and
$C_2\times C_2$ show why a list of composition factors does not determine a
finite group.
