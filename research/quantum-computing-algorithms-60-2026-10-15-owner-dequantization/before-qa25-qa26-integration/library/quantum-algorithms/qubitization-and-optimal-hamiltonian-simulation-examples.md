The two-dimensional example computes the clean block and signal complement at $x=0$ and $x=\pm1$. The degree-two Chebyshev example separates the real-even and imaginary-odd terms and applies the certified Bessel-tail bound. The two-bit path example lists all four input paths, checks their endpoint parity, computes the sparse normalization, and verifies the time-$\pi$ transfer by a finite matrix calculation.
