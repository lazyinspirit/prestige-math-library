The examples show how a tolerance changes the numerical rank of a nearly collinear design, why an absolute error can erase a tiny singular value while a componentwise-relative perturbation preserves it, and how ordinary column pivoting can violate the strong coefficient threshold. The last example gives an exact finite matrix and proves the pivot order and offending coefficient.
