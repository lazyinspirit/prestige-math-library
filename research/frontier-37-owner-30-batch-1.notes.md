## Mathematical dependency audit

- **Return cycles and recurrence.** The published `def-hitting-return-and-visit-times` counts a strictly positive return, and `thm-recurrence-and-transience-are-class-properties` supplies almost-sure hitting of any state in an irreducible recurrent class. The new occupation lemma uses the killed-prefix recursion, nonnegative Tonelli, and the return-time tail sum; minimality against `π/π(b)` proves positive recurrence from a stationary probability without invoking Kac. Kac then obtains `μ_b(y)=π(y)/π(b)` by subtracting two finite invariant measures, using irreducibility to kill the nonnegative difference. Thus the existence, Kac, and uniqueness arguments have no cycle.
- **Reversal and set Kac.** The reversed kernel is defined only on positive-π states; stationarity excludes flow from that support to its complement. Finite path reversal identifies the reverse law. The set-return formula uses a first *backward* hit of a positive-mass set and a nonnegative tail sum; `T_A^+` is defined explicitly in its statement. No value at an infinite stopping time is evaluated.
- **Ordinary convergence.** `def-aperiodic-chain` and `lem-matrix-chapman-kolmogorov-equations` support the finite-residue argument that all sufficiently large return times have positive probability. This makes the independent product chain irreducible. Its invariant `π⊗π` and the published recurrent-class hitting theorem give an almost-sure diagonal meeting. The proof strategy first gets coordinatewise convergence and then uses the countable half-ℓ1/Scheffé identity with a finite high-π-mass set to obtain total variation; it does not assume that pointwise convergence alone is enough.
- **Ergodic limits.** `thm-renewal-decomposition-at-successive-return-times`, Kac, and `thm-kolmogorov-iid-l1-strong-law` supply integrable iid return lengths and signed rewards. The strategy controls a partial final excursion by the next integrable absolute-reward block and handles the finite initial segment from every deterministic start. Cesaro convergence uses the indicator observable and bounded convergence. For a general stationary process, the shift is on the *canonical path space*, and the published Birkhoff identification plus finite-measure L1 result gives the invariant-sigma-algebra conditional expectation. The constant limit is asserted only after shift ergodicity. For the countable stationary irreducible chain, a shift-invariant event gives a bounded harmonic hitting martingale; bounded optional sampling and Levy upward convergence show that event has probability zero or one, without aperiodicity.
- **Axiom strength.** The matrix identities, finite-simplex Cesaro construction, detailed balance, half-ℓ1 formula, eventual-positive-return arithmetic, and canonical pushforward definition use no Choice in their arguments. Some published ZF proofs cite `def-axiom-of-choice` to name the axiom they avoid (for example `lem-finite-choice`), which is not an AC use. AC is declared in the new items that consume the published AC-qualified canonical-chain, conditional-expectation, strong-Markov, or Levy-upward interfaces; the manifest strategies identify those uses. PT-17 §11 calls the countable direct route ZF *after path laws are supplied*. The new occupation lemma records its finite-time choice-free calculation conditional on that supplied law; the canonical-law route in this scaffold retains the actual AC cost of its published suppliers. This is an explicit axiom-route tension for Step 3, not a claim that a ZF path-law construction was proved here.
