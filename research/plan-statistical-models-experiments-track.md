## Scope and dependency boundary

This category develops scientific statistical model contracts, analytical finite-sample and design-based inference, model-specific likelihood and estimating-equation consequences, dependent-data and event-process methods, and classical multiple/sequential inference. A theorem gives a consequence of a stated law or assignment design. It does not establish that the model, treatment version, censoring law, measurement process, graph, exposure mapping, or target population describes a scientific setting. Each A page names observation and sampling/assignment units, fixed/random quantities, observation scheme, support, and empirical assumptions.

Dependencies may be existing independent mathematics, planned earlier Mathematical Statistics and Inference Foundations (MSF) A pages, and earlier A pages in this category. No direct or transitive dependency may reach Bayesian/computational statistics or machine learning/adaptive decision methods. Classical randomization, analytical inference, discrete-time martingale validity, group-sequential designs, and two-stage conditional-error methods are in scope. Priors, posterior inference, bootstrap calibration, EM/variational optimization, numerical filtering, learned allocation, bandit regret, and reinforcement learning are excluded. B pages are examples/counterexamples companions and never suppliers.

Proposed category directory: statistical-models-and-experiments; proposed foundations directory: mathematical-statistics. Dossiers distinguish fully read, partial, extracted-but-unread, and unused source material.

### SME-02 — Fixed-design regression, estimability, and Gauss–Markov
**Pages:** fixed-design-linear-regression-and-estimability / fixed-design-linear-regression-and-estimability-examples. **A:** For \(Y=X\beta+\epsilon\), fitted vector unique and coefficient minimizers differ by \(\ker X\); \(c^T\beta\) estimable iff \(c\in row(X)\). Under fixed \(X\), full rank, \(E\epsilon=0\), \(Cov(\epsilon)=\sigma^2I\), prove OLS BLUE for estimable contrasts; Gaussianity unnecessary. **Proof:** normal equations and covariance cross-term cancellation against any linear unbiased competitor. **B:** collinearity, estimable contrast, heteroskedasticity, leverage/diagnostics. **Requires:** SME-01; MSF-01/02; existing LS/rank/projection/moments. **Sources:** MIT 18.443 regression pp. 1–4; Wickham pp. 2–6, proof pp. 5–6; diagnostics pp. 8, 11–12, 17.

### SME-03 — Exact Gaussian regression inference and prediction
**Pages:** exact-gaussian-linear-regression-inference / exact-gaussian-linear-regression-inference-examples. **A:** Fixed full-rank \(X\in R^{n\times p}\), \(1\le p<n\), iid \(N(0,\sigma^2)\) errors with \(\sigma^2>0\). QR and MSF-35 give independent coefficient/residual coordinates, \(RSS/\sigma^2\sim\chi^2_{n-p}\), exact nonzero-contrast \(t_{n-p}\) and nested rank-difference \(F_{q,n-p}\) for \(1\le q\le p\); both denominator degrees of freedom are positive. Distinguish MLE \(RSS/n\) from \(s^2=RSS/(n-p)\); future independent response adds its noise variance. **Proof/B:** QR specialization and inversion; mean-response versus prediction interval, Rademacher-error counterexample. **Requires:** SME-02; MSF-08/10/35; QR/normal/product law. **Sources:** MIT 18.443 regression pp. 2–7 and MVN pp. 3–4; Wickham pp. 6–7 states, not derives, independence.

### SME-04 — Generalized linear models and Fisher scoring
**Pages:** generalized-linear-models-and-fisher-scoring / generalized-linear-models-and-fisher-scoring-examples. **A:** State independent exponential-family responses, base measure, dispersion, link and variance. Canonical score \(X^T(y-\mu)/a(\phi)\), Hessian \(-X^Tdiag(b''(\eta_i))X/a(\phi)\); positive \(b''\)+full rank gives at most one stationary maximizer, not existence. Define IRLS \(z_i=\eta_i+(y_i-\mu_i)/(d\mu_i/d\eta_i)\), \(w_i=(d\mu_i/d\eta_i)^2/(\phi V(\mu_i))\), weighted-LS update; prove one Fisher-scoring step only when information invertible. Deviance definition from saturated-vs-fit likelihood is nonnegative by nested suprema. Under common support, differentiability and the integrable derivative-envelope conditions, derive the score/information identities. Regular fixed-dimensional nested comparison may use MSF-15 only for iid random-design \((X_i,Y_i)\) under its exact interior/regularity premises. For deterministic independent nonidentically distributed responses, either omit asymptotic calibration or prove local triangular-array score/Hessian theorem with Lindeberg, normalized design-information convergence, max-leverage/no-dominating-observation and uniform Taylor remainder conditions. No generic saturated-model chi-square or deviance-residual law. **B:** logistic separation, Poisson overdispersion, identity-link boundary. **Requires:** SME-01; MSF-04/05/07; MSF-15 only for the matching iid random-design route. **Sources:** MIT 18.650 pp. 10–21, 23–33, 40–52; CMU Tibshirani pp. 1–5. Deviance diagnostics source gap remains.

### SME-05 — Multivariate normal, Wishart, and Hotelling \(T^2\)
**Pages:** multivariate-normal-wishart-hotelling / multivariate-normal-wishart-hotelling-examples. **A:** For iid \(N_p(\mu,\Sigma)\) with \(\Sigma\succ0\) and \(1\le p<n\), sample mean/scatter independent, \((n-1)S\sim W_p(\Sigma,n-1)\). Under \(n>p\), \(T^2=n(\bar X-\mu_0)^TS^{-1}(\bar X-\mu_0)\) has \((n-p)T^2/[p(n-1)]\sim F_{p,n-p}\). Keep trace chi-square and covariance LR distinct. **Proof:** orthogonal row decomposition and normal-squares; local inverse quadratic proof: full rank Gaussian columns a.s.; rotate \(Z\) to \(e_1\); Schur complement \(1/(e_1^TW^{-1}e_1)=g_1^T(I-P_{G_2})g_1\sim\chi^2_{\nu-p+1}\), independent of \(\|Z\|^2\sim\chi_p^2\), giving F.
