`ex-five-finite-proofs-of-am-gm-and-equality` (level 5) compares:

1. The published Cauchy doubling followed by arithmetic-mean backward descent.

   `u<1<v` by `1,u+v-1`: same sum, strictly larger product.

   by `1,uv`: same product, strictly smaller sum.
4. Ordinary induction using the independently proved *integer* Bernoulli
   supplier. If `B` is the first `n-1` entries' mean and `t` is the last entry

   side. This does not consume the AM-GM-derived rational Bernoulli extension.
5. Binary averaging followed by a single *geometric*-mean padding argument,
   with no backward descent. Padding by `G` to a doubling natural length
   keeps the padded geometric mean `G` and gives the desired sum bound.
