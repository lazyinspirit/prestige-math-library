## Definition

Let $\mathfrak g$ be a complex Lie algebra, and let $T(\mathfrak g)=\bigoplus_{n\ge 0}\mathfrak g^{\otimes n}$ be its tensor algebra. Let $I\subseteq T(\mathfrak g)$ be the two-sided ideal generated by all elements

$$x\otimes y-y\otimes x-[x,y] \qquad (x,y\in \mathfrak g).$$

The **universal enveloping algebra** of $\mathfrak g$ is the quotient algebra

$$U(\mathfrak g):=T(\mathfrak g)/I.$$

The image of $x\in \mathfrak g$ in $U(\mathfrak g)$ is again denoted by $x$. The defining relation is therefore

$$xy-yx=[x,y] \qquad (x,y\in \mathfrak g).$$

This is the quotient algebra used throughout the page for PBW, the Casimir element, and the Harish-Chandra projection.
