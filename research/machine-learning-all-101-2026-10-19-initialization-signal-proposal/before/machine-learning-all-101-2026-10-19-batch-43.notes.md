The task's IID layer-input and independent-row-weight model admits this case (for example, an independent Gaussian first affine layer on a deterministic nonzero input). Thus the local backward theorem and PReLU result are drafted only as fixed-layer conditional formulas; multi-layer iteration needs owner reconciliation or stronger/different hypotheses. Exact evidence: `lem-iid-relu-layer-forces-zero-atom-next`, proof steps 1.1–2.1, and `ex-finite-width-iid-relu-stack-has-zero-atom`, steps 1.1–1.2.
