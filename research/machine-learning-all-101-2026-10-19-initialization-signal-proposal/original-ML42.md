### ML-42 — Initialization and signal propagation

**Pages:** initialization-and-signal-propagation / initialization-and-signal-propagation-examples. **Scope:** Define rectifier variance-preserving initialization and derive the ReLU moment recurrence under layerwise independent identically distributed weights whose law is symmetric about zero, independent identically distributed layer inputs independent of the weights, symmetric preactivations with no atom at zero, and zero biases. For the backward recurrence additionally assume each layer weight is independent of its incoming gradient and the ReLU gate is independent of the downstream gradient; zero mean alone does not imply the required symmetry. At fan-in n_l, Var(y_l)=½n_l Var(w_l)Var(y_{l−1}); choosing Var(w_l)=2/n_l prevents this simplified forward second moment from gaining an exponential layer factor. The backward recurrence uses fan-out; the two choices need not both hold in arbitrary architectures. For PReLU slope $a$, the forward factor is $2/((1+a^2)n_l)$ and the corresponding backward factor uses fan-out $\hat n_l$, namely $2/((1+a^2)\hat n_l)$. This is a moment calculation, not a theorem that training succeeds or generalizes. Proposed items: alg-rectifier-variance-preserving-initialization; thm-relu-forward-variance-recurrence; thm-relu-backward-variance-recurrence; cor-prelu-variance-preserving-scale. **Proof route:** condition on the previous layer, use symmetry and independence to compute second moments and the ReLU half-moment; for backpropagation separately use independence of weights from incoming gradients and of gates from downstream gradients. Propagate the forward/backward recurrences and state finite-moment and independence assumptions at every layer. **Examples:** fan-in versus fan-out choice; nonzero bias or correlated weights violating recurrence; PReLU slope. ImageNet depth and accuracy comparisons remain experiments. **Requires:** ML-30 — Feedforward networks and single-hidden-layer universality for layer notation; published `def-independent-random-elements`, `thm-independent-random-elements-have-product-joint-law`, `def-expectation-of-a-nonnegative-or-integrable-random-variable`, and `def-moments-variance-and-covariance` for the exact independence and second-moment clauses. The layerwise ReLU/PReLU moment calculations are local; the moment identities do not justify independence or symmetry by themselves. **Sources:** He, Zhang, Ren, and Sun, “Delving Deep into Rectifiers,” §2.2 and forward/backward variance recurrences, pp. 3–5. Report its empirical depth/accuracy results only as experiments.
