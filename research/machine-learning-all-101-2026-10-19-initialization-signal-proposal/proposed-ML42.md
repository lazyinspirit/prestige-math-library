### ML-42 — Initialization and signal propagation

**Pages:** initialization-and-signal-propagation / initialization-and-signal-propagation-examples. **Scope:** Define rectifier variance-preserving initialization and derive the ReLU moment recurrence under layerwise independent identically distributed weights whose law is symmetric about zero, layer inputs independent of the weights with a common finite coordinate second moment (coordinate dependence is allowed), symmetric preactivations (zero atoms are allowed in the forward calculation), and zero biases. Retain the source's backward half-factor and finite-depth product as conditional formulas under atomless symmetric preactivations, each layer weight matrix independent of its gated incoming gradient, and the ReLU gate independent of the downstream gradient. Prove that these full independence hypotheses force zero downstream second moment at a nondegenerate actual ReLU hidden layer. Separately give the exact finite-network covariance-sum identity for zero-at-kink backpropagation, distinguishing specified kink values from ordinary derivatives; zero mean alone does not imply symmetry. At fan-in n_l, Var(y_l)=½n_l Var(w_l)Var(y_{l−1}); choosing Var(w_l)=2/n_l prevents this simplified forward second moment from gaining an exponential layer factor. The backward recurrence uses fan-out; the two choices need not both hold in arbitrary architectures. For PReLU slope $a$, the forward factor is $2/((1+a^2)n_l)$ and the corresponding backward factor uses fan-out $\hat n_l$, namely $2/((1+a^2)\hat n_l)$. This is a moment calculation, not a theorem that training succeeds or generalizes. Proposed items: alg-rectifier-variance-preserving-initialization; thm-relu-forward-variance-recurrence; thm-relu-backward-variance-recurrence; cor-prelu-variance-preserving-scale. **Proof route:** condition on the previous layer, use symmetry and independence to compute second moments and the ReLU half-moment; for backpropagation separately use independence of weights from incoming gradients and of gates from downstream gradients. Give the full finite-depth forward product allowing zero atoms; retain and prove the conditional backward product with every finite-moment and independence assumption stated at every layer. Include the atom-aware ReLU/PReLU gate factor, its specified kink convention, and the finite-width zero-event and independence-feasibility obstructions. Do not infer nonzero actual-gradient preservation from incompatible hypotheses. **Examples:** fan-in versus fan-out choice; nonzero bias or correlated weights violating recurrence; PReLU slope. ImageNet depth and accuracy comparisons remain experiments. **Requires:** ML-30 — Feedforward networks and single-hidden-layer universality for layer notation; published `def-independent-random-elements`, `thm-independent-random-elements-have-product-joint-law`, `def-expectation-of-a-nonnegative-or-integrable-random-variable`, and `def-moments-variance-and-covariance` for the exact independence and second-moment clauses. The layerwise ReLU/PReLU moment calculations are local; the moment identities do not justify independence or symmetry by themselves. **Sources:** He, Zhang, Ren, and Sun, “Delving Deep into Rectifiers,” §2.2 and forward/backward variance recurrences, pp. 3–5. Report its empirical depth/accuracy results only as experiments.
