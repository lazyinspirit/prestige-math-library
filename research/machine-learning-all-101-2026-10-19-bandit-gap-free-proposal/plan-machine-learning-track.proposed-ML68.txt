**Pages:** stochastic-finite-armed-bandits / stochastic-finite-armed-bandits-examples. **Scope:** Define a finite $k$-armed stationary bandit with finite reward means, conditional reward laws, gaps $\Delta_i=\mu^*-\mu_i$, pseudo-regret and expected regret. For the source model, assume a jointly independent reward table $(X_{s,i})_{s\le n,i\le k}$ with each $X_{s,i}\sim P_i$, and centered 1-sub-Gaussian rewards in the exact MGF sense $\mathbb E e^{\lambda(X_{s,i}-\mu_i)}\le e^{\lambda^2/2}$ for every $\lambda\in\mathbb R$. For ETC with $1\le m\le n/k$, state $R_n\le m\sum_i\Delta_i+(n-mk)\sum_i\Delta_i e^{-m\Delta_i^2/4}$. For UCB with horizon $n\ge k$, initialize by pulling each arm once; thereafter use $\hat\mu_i+\sqrt{2\log(1/\delta)/T_i}$ and set the unobserved-arm index to $+\infty$. For $n\ge2$, with $\delta=1/n^2$, state expected gap-dependent regret $R_n\le\sum_i3\Delta_i+\sum_{i:\Delta_i>0}16\log(n)/\Delta_i$ and gap-free regret $R_n\le\sqrt{8nk\log n}+3\sum_i\Delta_i$. These are expected-regret results; do not present them as high-probability bounds. The source Theorem 7.2 proves $8\sqrt{nk\log n}+3\sum_i\Delta_i$ for this same UCB rule. The retained $\sqrt{8nk\log n}+3\sum_i\Delta_i$ bound is a local sharpening, not the source theorem’s stated constant; it requires the fixed-prefix counting, integrated tail and Cauchy–Schwarz argument below. Proposed interfaces: `def-stochastic-bandit-and-pseudo-regret`, `def-centered-subgaussian-reward`, `lem-independent-subgaussian-prefix-tail`, `alg-explore-then-commit`, `thm-explore-then-commit-expected-regret`, `alg-upper-confidence-bound-bandit`, `thm-ucb-gap-dependent-regret`, `thm-ucb-gap-free-regret`. **Proof route:** decompose regret into pulls of suboptimal arms. Locally prove the fixed-prefix sub-Gaussian tail from the MGF definition by product joint law, Tonelli, Markov's inequality and exponential optimization; apply it at each deterministic reward-table prefix and prove the finite union bound before bounding expected pulls. Do not apply a fixed-$n$ tail bound at a random pull count. For the local gap-free sharpening, prove `lem-ucb-prefix-envelope-sum-bound` and `lem-ucb-sharp-suboptimal-arm-expected-pulls` locally. Charge each selected suboptimal-arm prefix at most once on the event that every optimal-arm prefix index is optimistic. Its complement contributes at most one expected pull. Bound the remaining deterministic-prefix tail sum by the decreasing-envelope integral $(4L+2+\sqrt{8\pi L})/\Delta_i^2$, where $L=\log n$, and separately check horizons $n\le4$, $5\le n\le11$ and $n\ge12$ to prove $\mathbb E T_i(n)\le3+8L/\Delta_i^2$. Apply finite Cauchy–Schwarz to $q_i=(\mathbb E T_i(n)-3)_+$, using $\Delta_i^2q_i\le8L$ and $\sum_iq_i\le n$, to obtain the retained constant without changing the confidence radius. **Examples:** two-arm gap, tied optimal arms, heavy-tailed rewards outside the stated model. **Requires:** ML-65 — Online protocols and regret benchmarks; `def-independent-random-elements`, `thm-independent-random-elements-have-product-joint-law`, `def-expectation-of-a-nonnegative-or-integrable-random-variable`, `cor-markov-inequality-for-random-variables`, `thm-tonelli-theorem-for-sigma-finite-product-spaces`, `prop-order-and-scalar-rules-for-the-nonnegative-integral`, `cor-additivity-of-the-nonnegative-lebesgue-integral`, and `thm-exponential-is-strictly-increasing`. The published Hoeffding/Azuma items for bounded variables are not used for unbounded 1-sub-Gaussian rewards. **Sources:** Lattimore–Szepesvári PDF pp. 65–82 (printed pp. 56–73), concentration definitions/inequalities pp. 83–97 (printed pp. 74–88), explore-then-commit pp. 100–110 (printed pp. 91–101), and UCB pp. 111–125 (printed pp. 102–116).
