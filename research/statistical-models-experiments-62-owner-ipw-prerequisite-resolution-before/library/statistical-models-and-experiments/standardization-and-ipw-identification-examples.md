## Objective

These examples compute both population functionals in a finite-stratum model, show how a positive propensity near zero creates an extreme weight, and calculate how capping weights changes the population target. A final pair of compatible full-data laws shows why structural nonpositivity can prevent identification even when consistency and conditional exchangeability hold.
