Worked examples substitute the five-percent false-positive and false-negative
targets and ten-percent CV criterion into the known-blank normal formulas.
They then change the model by adding estimated-blank variance or
concentration-dependent variance. The final example treats a below-threshold
report as a censored event and computes its probability under the declared
response law.
