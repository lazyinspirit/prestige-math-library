  - ex-atomic-spectrum-versus-continuous-spectral-density

An explicit weakly stationary sinusoid has an atomic spectrum, while a
causal AR(1) process with $|\rho|<1$ has a continuous spectral density. Their
expected periodograms are the corresponding Fejér averages: two shifted
kernels in the atomic case and a convolution against a continuous density in
the AR(1) case.
