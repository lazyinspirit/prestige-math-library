The worked example compares classical and Berkson models under their distinct
assumptions. It then gives binary misclassification mechanisms whose observed
associations reverse direction, one nondifferential relative to the named
variables and one differential. A finite pair of latent-error decompositions
has the same measured law but different variance splits; observing a validation
variable can distinguish those two candidate laws.
