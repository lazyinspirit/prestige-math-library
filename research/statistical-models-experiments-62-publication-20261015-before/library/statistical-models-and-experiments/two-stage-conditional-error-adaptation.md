For one fixed null law and interim sigma-algebra, the page proves that an
adapted design keeps its null rejection probability within the original
conditional-error budget whenever its conditional probability of any eventual
rejection is bounded almost surely by the original conditional error. A
finite-size stratification lemma makes the selected-sample-size null law
explicit. The stage-2 p-value corollary handles the random threshold
$p_2\le A$ by finite dyadic approximations, so conditional superuniformity
is required at each fixed threshold, not through an unproved jointly chosen
conditional distribution. Fresh independent stage-2 data provide one route
only when the null law at each selected size is stated and calibrated.

The scope is single-hypothesis two-stage sample-size or continuation
adaptation. Conditional power is distinct from type-I control. Treatment
allocation learning, endpoint selection, multiarm selection, and multi-stage
closed testing are outside this page.
