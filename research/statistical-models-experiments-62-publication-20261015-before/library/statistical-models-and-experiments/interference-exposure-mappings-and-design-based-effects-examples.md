## Objective

Work through a finite two-stage strategy comparison and a unit-level exposure-weighting calculation. Counterexamples show why cross-group contamination breaks partial interference, why own-treatment-only exposure maps can miss peer effects, and how nonconstant direct effects create the precise positive bias term in a source variance estimator even when stratified interference holds.
