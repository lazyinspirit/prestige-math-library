This page develops design-based variance for fixed finite populations. It
derives the Horvitz–Thompson total variance and the pair-inclusion estimate,
then proves the fixed-size Sen–Yates–Grundy identity and the SRSWOR
finite-population correction. Zero pair support limits the stated inverse
pair formula, while the sign condition on pair covariances is used only as a
sufficient guarantee for nonnegative sample estimates. These design moments
do not supply exact normal or t inference.
