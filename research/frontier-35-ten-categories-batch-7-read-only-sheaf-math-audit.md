### Typed replacement route

Write `T(A,B)=Tot(A⊗_ℤ B)`, `P A` for the canonical bounded-above flat replacement, and `e_A=α_A:P A→A`. All objects and arrows in this paragraph are in `D^−(Ab(X))` after applying `Q`, and `T` on actual chain maps is the ordinary total-tensor functor. Stacks Project, *Cohomology of Sheaves*, Lemma 26.9 (tag 06YD) establishes bounded-above flat implies K-flat; Lemma 26.13 (tag 06YG) supplies comparison under quasi-isomorphisms between K-flat complexes; Definition 26.14 (tag 06YH) descends tensor to the derived category. Stacks Project, *More on Algebra*, §74 items (6), (8), (9) give respectively the total differential `d(x⊗y)=dx⊗y+(−1)^{deg x}x⊗dy`, the sign-free associator, and the symmetry `x⊗y↦(−1)^{deg x deg y}y⊗x`. *Cohomology of Sheaves* §31, Lemma 31.1 (tag 0FP2), uses the same shifted-unit cup-product convention. These source passages were retrieved directly from the current Stacks PDFs for this update.

First repair the upstream derived-tensor item so that `T^L(A,B):=Q T(PA,PB)` is a bifunctor on `D^−`, including equality for homotopic representatives before roof descent. Its explicit object-level comparisons may then be used safely. For three complexes `A,B,C`, put `L=T(PA,PB)` and `R=T(PB,PC)`. Define the following **typed isomorphisms**:

`Θ_{A,B,C}: T^L(T^L(A,B),C)=Q T(P L,PC) → Q T(L,PC)` by `Q T(e_L,id_{PC})`, and

`Ξ_{A,B,C}: T^L(A,T^L(B,C))=Q T(PA,P R) → Q T(PA,R)` by `Q T(id_{PA},e_R)`.

K-flatness of `PC` and `PA` makes both arrows invertible. The derived associator is exactly `a^L_{A,B,C}=Ξ_{A,B,C}^{−1}∘Q A(PA,PB,PC)∘Θ_{A,B,C}`. The derived symmetry is `s^L_{A,B}=Q S(PA,PB)`. With `Z=ℤ_X[0]`, the left unitor is `l^L_A=Q(e_A)∘QΛ(PA)∘Q T(e_Z,id_{PA})`, and similarly `r^L_A=Q(e_A)∘Q P(PA)∘Q T(id_{PA},e_Z)`, where `P(PA)` in this final expression denotes the **ordinary right-unitor map named P in the item**, not the replacement functor; renaming that map `Ρ` in the proof would remove this collision.

For chain maps `f:A→A'`, `g:B→B'`, `h:C→C'`, set `u=T(Pf,Pg):L→L'` and `v=T(Pg,Ph):R→R'`. Naturality of the augmentation gives the actual commuting squares

`Θ' ∘ Q T(Pu,Ph) = Q T(u,Ph) ∘ Θ`,

`Ξ' ∘ Q T(Pf,Pv) = Q T(Pf,v) ∘ Ξ`,

while ordinary associator naturality gives

`Q A(PA',PB',PC') ∘ Q T(u,Ph) = Q T(Pf,v) ∘ Q A(PA,PB,PC)`.

Composing these three equalities proves `a^{L}_{A',B',C'}∘T^L(T^L(f,g),h)=T^L(f,T^L(g,h))∘a^{L}_{A,B,C}` with every intermediate domain matching. Ordinary naturality of `S,Λ,Ρ` and `e` proves the symmetry and unitor squares. For an arbitrary derived morphism represented by a roof `(A←^s V→^f A')`, naturality for `Qf` and `Qs` implies naturality for `Qf∘Qs^{−1}` by inverting the `Qs` square. Apply this in each argument. No numerator is assumed invertible.

For coherence, each parenthesized iterated derived tensor of `A,B,C,D` has a canonical isomorphism to the corresponding parenthesized ordinary total tensor of `PA,PB,PC,PD`, obtained recursively from `Θ` and `Ξ`. Every strict total of these flat complexes is again K-flat (Stacks *Cohomology of Sheaves* Lemma 26.5, tag 079R), so each intermediate augmentation comparison is a quasi-isomorphism after tensoring. Naturality of `e` makes the comparison squares commute. Under these isomorphisms the derived pentagon, triangle, and hexagons become precisely the ordinary total-complex identities from `lem-koszul-structure-of-the-abelian-sheaf-tensor-product` and *More on Algebra* §74 items (8)–(9), so both routes in each diagram agree. This is the needed coherence of the **transported derived maps**, not only of `Q(A),Q(S),Q(Λ),Q(P)` on strict total replacements.

For shifts, `Z[k]^n=Z^{n+k}`, hence `Z[−p]` has its sole term in degree `p`. Let `m_{p,q}:T(Z[−p],Z[−q])→Z[−p−q]` send `1⊗1` to `1`, and let `b_{p,q}=Q T(e_{Z[−p]},e_{Z[−q]})`, an isomorphism. Then the shifted-unit map is `κ(p,q)=b_{p,q}^{−1}∘Q(m_{p,q}^{−1})`. The **typed** symmetry square is

`Q S(Z[−p],Z[−q]) ∘ b_{p,q} = b_{q,p} ∘ s^L_{Z[−p],Z[−q]}`.

On the sole degree-`p+q` term, the left `S` is `(−1)^{pq}` times factor exchange and `m_{q,p}∘S=(−1)^{pq}m_{p,q}`. Thus `s^L_{Z[−p],Z[−q]}∘κ(p,q)=κ(q,p)∘((−1)^{pq}id_{Z[−p−q]})`. For `p=0` or `q=0`, the ordinary unitor equals `m_{0,q}` or `m_{q,0}`, yielding `l^L_{Z[−q]}∘κ(0,q)=id` and `r^L_{Z[−q]}∘κ(q,0)=id`. The degree-zero comparison square is `c_{G,F}∘s^L_{F,G}=Qσ_{F,G}∘c_{F,G}`; the two paths of the associator/comparison square both send a pure degree-zero replacement tensor to the same sheaf-level reassociation, and vanish on the other degrees because the target is concentrated in degree zero.
