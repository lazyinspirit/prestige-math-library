# Exact boundary tensor-ideal components: partial trace and an explicit factorization

This is an isolated precision supplement to construction.md §3. It replaces the sentence asserting that every low-rank consequence vanishes merely by an innermost cup/cap against the projector. A seam contraction can instead be a partial trace, and that missing case is essential.

Write m=ell−1>=2, f=p_m, B_n=TL_n(C,delta), delta=q+q^-1>0, and let E_n:B_n→B_(n−1) be the **unnormalized** right partial trace. Put

    J_n=0                                      (n<m),
    J_n=B_n (f tensor 1_(n−m)) B_n             (n>=m).

Let I be the categorical tensor ideal generated by f in the planar TL category. We prove I(n,n)=J_n for EVERY n, with arbitrary additional strands. The argument uses actual partial trace at m, not just turnback annihilation. It does not presuppose trace-radical equality, fusion semisimplicity or the quantum-group comparison.

## 1. Diagram identities and the critical base

The following three identities follow by composition/closure of planar matchings; expanding a matching proves each identity on a basis, so they hold for arbitrary linear combinations:

(i) B_n is spanned by B_(n−1) and B_(n−1) U_(n−1) B_(n−1). Indeed B_n is generated by the lower algebra and U_(n−1). In an alternating word containing two occurrences of U_(n−1), identity (iii) replaces a subword U_(n−1) x U_(n−1) by a lower-algebra coefficient times one U_(n−1). Repetition leaves at most one occurrence. This proves the spanning assertion without claiming a direct sum.

(ii) E_n is B_(n−1)-bilinear, E_n(x tensor1)=delta x, and E_n(U_(n−1))=1. Here bilinearity pulls diagrams on the first n−1 strands past the last closure.

(iii) For x in B_(n−1),

    U_(n−1) (x tensor1) U_(n−1)
      =(E_(n−1)(x) tensor1 tensor1) U_(n−1).

The two adjacent U's join the last input/output strand of x into its right partial trace; the other n−2 strands pass unchanged. The normalization is fixed by x=1, giving U²=delta U, and by x=U_(n−2), giving the adjacent triple relation. Thus there is no hidden division by delta in (iii).

JW turnback annihilation gives U_i f=f U_i=0 for 1<=i<m. Since B_m is generated by 1,U_1,...,U_(m−1), every word other than 1 kills f on the appropriate side. Consequently J_m=C f. The exact JW partial-trace formula gives

    E_m(f)=([m+1]/[m])p_(m−1)=([ell]/[ell−1])p_(ell−2)=0.

The denominator is nonzero; no [ell] is divided out. This establishes E_m(J_m)=0=J_(m−1). For example, closing one exterior strand of f is zero by this identity even when there is no cap joining two consecutive input endpoints of f.

## 2. The tower ideals are closed under every right partial trace

Inclusion J_n tensor1⊂J_(n+1) is immediate. We prove E_n(J_n)⊂J_(n−1) inductively. The cases n<m are zero and n=m is §1. Fix n>m; its embedded generator f tensor1_(n−m) belongs to the lower algebra B_(n−1). Write a typical element as a f b, suppressing identity strands. Expand a and b using §1(i). There are four types, with all displayed a_i,b_i in B_(n−1):

    a0 f b0,
    a1 U_(n−1) a2 f b0,
    a0 f b1 U_(n−1) b2,
    a1 U_(n−1) a2 f b1 U_(n−1) b2.

For the first type, §1(ii) gives delta a0 f b0, in J_(n−1). For the second it gives a1 a2 f b0, and for the third a0 f b1 b2, also in J_(n−1). For the fourth use §1(iii) followed by §1(ii); its partial trace is

    a1 E_(n−1)(a2 f b1) b2.

Here a2 f b1 is in J_(n−1), so the induction hypothesis puts its E_(n−1) image in J_(n−2). Its inclusion into B_(n−1) lies in J_(n−1), or is zero at the boundary. Multiplying by a1,b2 preserves that ideal. This proves the assertion for all four types, hence for every element of J_n.

In particular every iterated right partial trace from J_N to B_n lies in J_n and is zero when n<m. This is the precise closure needed for arbitrary bends; a turnback-only argument would not establish it.

## 3. An auxiliary skein braiding for the factorization

We now give an explicit formula reducing an arbitrary categorical consequence to the tower operations just proved. A convenient device is an auxiliary braiding on **positive-loop** TL. It is used only for this planar factorization and is not asserted to be the quantum crossing from construction.md.

Choose A=i s (so A!=0 and delta=−A²−A^-2) and put

    C=A 1+A^-1 U,        C^-1=A^-1 1+A U.

All these are actual elements of the ordinary diagram algebras. Their product is 1 because the U coefficient is A²+A^-2+delta=0. Expanding the adjacent triple gives Yang–Baxter: its only remaining coefficient is A+A^-1 delta+A^-3=0. For a cup W1=cup tensor id and W2=id tensor cup, the snakes give U1 W1=delta W1, U2 W1=W2 and U1 W2=W1. Therefore

    C1 C2 W1=(A²+delta+A^-2)W1+W2=W2,
    C2 C1 W2=W1.

The reflected equations give the two cap slides. These four identities prove naturality with the generators of every planar matching. Defining C_(r,t) as the ordered block crossing gives the two hexagons by partitioning that block; the triple relation identifies any two resulting crossing words. Thus these formulas define a braided rigid structure on the very same diagram category. Every use can alternatively be expanded into a finite linear combination of ordinary planar matchings.

Let theta_t be its planar curl on t strands. The generator curl is

    theta_1=E_2(C)=A delta+A^-1=−A³.

Sliding the cups/caps through the block crossings by the four displayed equations gives, successively on t strands,

    theta_(r+t)=C_(t,r) C_(r,t) (theta_r tensor theta_t),
    E_(2t) E_(2t−1) ... E_(t+1)(C_(t,t))=theta_t.

The second formula means partial trace of the last t output/input strands. It is the explicit curl identity: close these strands from right to left, move each cap through its block crossings using the cap-slide equations, and remove its matching cup using the snake; the remaining strand makes one curl, and the unclosed strands retain exactly the full-twist word in the first formula. Starting from the computed theta_1 proves both formulas by induction. In particular theta_t is a product of the invertible scalar (−A³)^t and invertible crossings, so theta_t^-1 exists in B_t. No quantum-group or unproved universal R input enters this auxiliary construction.

## 4. Explicit arbitrary-size factorization

Take ANY a:t→n, b:n→t and F=f tensor1_(t−m) with t>=m. Let N=n+t. Although a and b separately change strand number, a tensor b is an endomorphism of N: its source has t+n strands and its target n+t strands, the same object N in the strict diagram category. Define the following actual element of B_N:

    X=(a tensor b) (F theta_t^-1 tensor 1_n) C_(n,t).

The middle factor is

    (f tensor1_(N−m)) (theta_t^-1 tensor1_n),

so X belongs to J_N. We claim that tracing its last t strands gives exactly a F b. This is an algebraic factorization, with no isotopy assumption about where the extra strands lie. Naturality, already proved from the four cup/cap slides, gives

    (1_t tensor b) C_(n,t)=C_(t,t)(b tensor1_t).

Hence

    X=(a F theta_t^-1 tensor1_t) C_(t,t)(b tensor1_t).

Partial-trace naturality (pull maps on the untraced wires outside the closure), followed by the curl identity in §3, now yields

    ptr_t(X)=a F theta_t^-1 ptr_t(C_(t,t)) b
            =a F theta_t^-1 theta_t b
            =a F b.

Here ptr_t is E_(n+1) ... E_N, applied in descending domain order. By §2, ptr_t(X) lies in J_n. Thus **every** such a F b belongs to J_n, including n<m, where it is zero. This handles arbitrary external strands, arbitrary numbers of cups/caps, and seam contractions. It neither needs a cap wholly inside f nor discards a possibly nonzero low-rank partial trace without proof.

## 5. Equality with the tensor-ideal component

A tensor-ideal word containing f is a finite sum of terms a (1_r tensor f tensor1_u) b, possibly with other occurrences of f. Expand every other occurrence of f in its finite diagram basis and absorb it into a or b; it suffices to retain one distinguished occurrence. The auxiliary block braidings move 1_r tensor f tensor1_u to f tensor1_(r+u): naturality with f (a diagram linear combination) gives an invertible conjugation, which is absorbed into a,b. Each term therefore has precisely the form in §4. Thus I(n,n)⊂J_n.

Conversely the tensor ideal contains f tensor1_(n−m) for n>=m and is closed under left/right composition, so J_n⊂I(n,n). For n<m the reverse inclusion is automatic because J_n=0. We conclude

    I(n,n)=J_n

at every rank. Below m the component vanishes by the proved iterated-partial-trace closure, not an incomplete innermost-turnback slogan. At and above m it is exactly the two-sided ideal generated by the single fixed left-embedded projector, not an unproved enlargement by arbitrary annular consequences.

This closes the precision gap in construction.md §3. The later retained-path matrix-unit and trace-radical proofs may now use this exact J_n. The separate general theta-net formula claim in native62 remains its own obligation; this appendix's auxiliary curl theta_t is not that trivalent-net scalar theorem.
