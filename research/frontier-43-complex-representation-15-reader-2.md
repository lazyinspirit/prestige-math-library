## Mathematical checks and source evidence

Thus the locally detectable support value and the topological-mean restriction are essential.

The full L1 dual argument was checked on sigma-compact clopen cosets. Summable approximation errors give convergence on each coset, the glued continuous sequence gives one globally Borel bounded representative, and Cc density extends its pairing to all L1 without an uncountable null-union assertion. The smoothing adjoint formulas were checked with the stated inversion/right-translation modular factors.
