## Definition

Let $M$ be a smooth oriented $3$-manifold, and put
$R=\mathbb Z[A^{\pm1}]$ and $\delta=-A^2-A^{-2}$. A **framed unoriented
link in $M$** is a finite disjoint union of smoothly embedded annuli
$S^1\times[-1,1]$ in the interior of $M$, considered up to ambient isotopy
through such framed annuli; the empty union is allowed. Let
$\mathcal L_{\mathrm{fr}}(M)$ be the set of these isotopy classes and let
$\mathcal F_R(M)$ be the free $R$-module with basis
$\mathcal L_{\mathrm{fr}}(M)$.

For the local crossing relation, choose an orientation-preserving chart from a
closed $3$-ball in $M$ to a standard ball in $\mathbb R^3$, and project to the
first two coordinates. In the displayed chart the over-strand runs from the
southwest endpoint to the northeast endpoint, and the under-strand runs from
the northwest endpoint to the southeast endpoint. The $A$-smoothing pairs the
two left endpoints and the two right endpoints, and the $B$-smoothing pairs
the two top endpoints and the two bottom endpoints. The smoothed strips carry
blackboard framing.

```tikz
  \begin{scope}
    \draw[thick] (-1,1)--(-0.14,0.14);
    \draw[thick] (0.14,-0.14)--(1,-1);
    \draw[very thick] (-1,-1)--(1,1);
    \node at (0,-1.38) {crossing};
  \end{scope}
  \begin{scope}[xshift=3.0cm]
    \draw[thick] (-1,1) .. controls (-0.45,0.35) and (-0.45,-0.35) .. (-1,-1);
    \draw[thick] (1,1) .. controls (0.45,0.35) and (0.45,-0.35) .. (1,-1);
    \node at (0,-1.38) {$A$-smoothing, weight $A$};
  \end{scope}
  \begin{scope}[xshift=6.0cm]
    \draw[thick] (-1,1) .. controls (-0.35,0.45) and (0.35,0.45) .. (1,1);
    \draw[thick] (-1,-1) .. controls (-0.35,-0.45) and (0.35,-0.45) .. (1,-1);
    \node at (0,-1.38) {$B$-smoothing, weight $A^{-1}$};
  \end{scope}
\end{tikzpicture}
```

For each such local triple, the three framed links agree outside the chart.
Let $\mathcal R_A(M)$ be the submodule of $\mathcal F_R(M)$ generated by
$[L_\times]-A[L_A]-A^{-1}[L_B]$ for all crossing triples, and by
$[L\sqcup O]-\delta[L]$ for every framed link $L$, where $O$ is a
zero-framed trivial circle bounding a disk in a ball disjoint from $L$. The
**Kauffman bracket skein module** of $M$ is
$$
\mathcal K_A(M):=\mathcal F_R(M)/\mathcal R_A(M).
$$
The relations are imposed on framed link classes; no unframed type-I
identification is added.
