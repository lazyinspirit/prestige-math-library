## Statement

Let $A$ be an indeterminate, $R_A=\mathbb Z[A^{\pm1},(A^{-4}+1)^{-1}]$,
$q=A^{-2}$, $t=q^2=A^{-4}$, $\delta=-(q+q^{-1})=-A^2-A^{-2}$, and
$z_0=-1/(t+1)$. For a nonempty regular diagram $D$, define
$\langle D\rangle$ by summing its crossing smoothings with weights $A$ and
$A^{-1}$ and the factor $\delta^{k-1}$ for a state with $k$ circles. Let
$B_n$ be the $n$-strand Artin braid group with positive geometric generators
$\sigma_i$ (label $i$ passes below the midpoint and label $i+1$ passes above),
drawn with braid parameter increasing from bottom to top; let
$e:B_n\to\mathbb Z$ be the homomorphism $e(\sigma_i)=1$. Let $H(n)$ be
the type-A Hecke algebra with generators $T_i$, quadratic relation
$T_i^2=(v-1)T_i+v$, braid relations, and distant commutation; let $\pi_n$
send $\sigma_i$ to $T_i$. Its normalized Markov trace $\operatorname{tr}_n$
satisfies $\operatorname{tr}_n(1)=1$, cyclicity, tower restriction, and
$\operatorname{tr}_{n+1}(xT_n)=z\operatorname{tr}_n(x)$. Let
$\mathrm{TL}_n$ be the noncrossing pairing algebra with loop value $\delta$,
and let $\operatorname{Tr}_n(D)=\delta^{c(D)}$ be the unnormalized trace,
where $c(D)$ is the number of circles after joining corresponding top and
bottom endpoints outside the rectangle; set
$\tau_n=\delta^{-n}\operatorname{Tr}_n$. For $\beta\in B_n$,
$n\ge1$, orient its closure in the braid direction. Under the specialization
$v\mapsto t$, $z\mapsto z_0$ of the Hecke tower, one has
$$\langle\widehat\beta\rangle
 =\delta^{n-1}(-A)^{e(\beta)}\operatorname{tr}_n(\pi_n(\beta))\big|_{v=t,\,z=z_0}.$$
The identity gives bracket value $1$ on the one-strand identity closure. Its
positive and negative Markov stabilizations multiply the raw bracket by
$-A^3$ and $-A^{-3}$, respectively.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** $n\ge1$, an $n$-strand braid $\beta$, the Laurent coefficient ring
$R_A$, and the definitions and relations named below.

[F1] A crossing in the source chart is the A-smoothing with coefficient $A$
plus the B-smoothing with coefficient $A^{-1}$, and the loop value is
$\delta=-A^2-A^{-2}$
([[def-kauffman-bracket-smoothing-and-loop-value]]).

[F2] The positive geometric half twist has the explicit diamond path with label
$i$ passing below the midpoint and label $i+1$ above it; the reflected path is
its inverse ([[def-elementary-geometric-half-twist]]).

[F3] $B_n$ is generated by $\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_{n-1}$ with the Artin
braid and distant-commutation relations, and the closure joins corresponding
top and bottom endpoints with the strand orientations respected
([[def-braid-group-by-the-artin-presentation]],
[[def-closure-of-a-geometric-braid]]).

[F4] The vertical identity diagram has $n$ through strands; $e_i$ has a top
cap and bottom cup at positions $i,i+1$; stacking removes each closed loop
with coefficient $\delta$
([[def-temperley-lieb-diagram-category]]).

[F5] The generators satisfy $e_i^2=\delta e_i$,
$e_ie_{i+1}e_i=e_i$, $e_{i+1}e_ie_{i+1}=e_{i+1}$, and distant commutation
([[lem-temperley-lieb-generators-satisfy-local-relations]]).

[F6] The local crossing operators satisfy R2 cancellation and the R3 braid
identity in the declared source chart
([[lem-kauffman-bracket-r2-r3-identities]]).

[F7] The unnormalized diagram closure trace is cyclic,
$\operatorname{Tr}_{n+1}(x\otimes1)=\delta\operatorname{Tr}_n(x)$, and
$\operatorname{Tr}_{n+1}((x\otimes1)e_n)=\operatorname{Tr}_n(x)$;
when $\delta$ is a unit, $\tau_n=\delta^{-n}\operatorname{Tr}_n$
([[def-temperley-lieb-closure-trace-and-partial-trace]],
[[lem-temperley-lieb-closure-trace-component-identities]]).

[F8] The Hecke tower has quadratic relation
$T_i^2=(v-1)T_i+v$, and its Ocneanu trace satisfies the tower restriction,
cyclicity, and $\operatorname{tr}_{n+1}(xT_n)=z\operatorname{tr}_n(x)$
([[def-markov-trace-on-the-type-a-hecke-tower]],
[[thm-the-ocneanu-markov-trace-exists-and-is-unique]]).

[F9] The assignment $\sigma_i\mapsto T_i$ is a group homomorphism to Hecke
units, and $T_i^{-1}=v^{-1}T_i+(v^{-1}-1)$
([[lem-the-hecke-generators-satisfy-the-artin-relations-and-are-units]]).

[F10] The Hecke tower has the decomposition
$H(n+1)=H(n)\oplus H(n)T_nH(n)$ as an $H(n)$-bimodule
([[lem-the-hecke-tower-is-free-over-the-previous-level]]).

[F11] The exponent sum is a homomorphism $e:B_n\to\mathbb Z$ with
$e(\sigma_i)=1$ ([[def-exponent-sum-of-a-braid]]).

## Proof

1.1 Draw the positive half twist from [F2] in a vertical braid box with its parameter increasing from bottom to top and its first disk coordinate $x$ increasing to the right. At the crossing time, label $i+1$ is above label $i$; their projected tangent vectors in the ordered coordinates $(t,x)$ are $(1,-2h)$ and $(1,2h)$. Thus the over-strand runs northwest to southeast in this box. Rotating its source crossing chart through a positive quarter-turn is orientation preserving and puts the over-strand in the source chart's southwest-to-northeast position. The top and bottom pairs of that chart pull back to the two vertical through arcs, and its left and right pairs pull back to the top cap and bottom cup. Thus its two smoothings are $I$ with weight $A$ and $e_i$ with weight $A^{-1}$. For the opposite crossing the pairings exchange. Hence the local operators are $$C_i=AI+A^{-1}e_i,\qquad C_i^{-}=A^{-1}I+Ae_i.$$ The relations [F5] and [F6] show these operators satisfy the Artin relations and are mutual inverses; distant generators commute by [F5]. [F1, F2, F3, F4, F5, F6, construct]

2.1 Define $\rho_n(\sigma_i)=C_i$ and $S_i:=-A^{-1}C_i=-I-qe_i$. With $t=q^2$ and $\delta=-(q+q^{-1})$, direct multiplication gives $$S_i^2=I+(2q+q^2\delta)e_i=I+(q-q^3)e_i =(t-1)S_i+t.$$ The braid and distant-commutation relations for $S_i$ follow from [F5] and step 1.1; thus $T_i\mapsto S_i$ defines a homomorphism from the Hecke tower specialized at $v=t$ into the diagram algebra. For a braid word, this map sends $\pi_n(\beta)$ to the product of the corresponding $S_i^{\pm1}$. [F5, F8, F9, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 The element $A^{-4}+1$ is a unit in $R_A$ and $\delta=-A^2(A^{-4}+1)$ is therefore a unit. Set $\tau_n=\delta^{-n}\operatorname{Tr}_n$. By [F7], $\tau_n(1)=1$, $\tau_{n+1}(x\otimes1)=\tau_n(x)$, and $\tau_n$ is cyclic. Since $S_n=-I-qe_n$, the last closure rule of [F7] gives $$\tau_{n+1}((x\otimes1)S_n) =-\tau_n(x)-q\delta^{-1}\tau_n(x) =-\frac{1}{q^2+1}\tau_n(x)=z_0\tau_n(x).$$ Thus $\tau\circ\rho_H$ satisfies all specialized Markov-trace rules, where $\rho_H(T_i)=S_i$. [F7, F8, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 Any specialized Markov trace is determined by its restriction to $H(n)$: on $H(n+1)=H(n)\oplus H(n)T_nH(n)$, restriction determines the first summand, while cyclicity and the Markov rule force $\operatorname{tr}_{n+1}(xT_ny)=z_0\operatorname{tr}_n(yx)$ on every product in the second summand. Starting at $H(1)$, induction proves uniqueness, without dividing by any specialized parameter. The scalar extension of the Ocneanu trace exists by [F8], so the trace in step 3.1 equals that specialized trace. [F8, F10, step 3.1, induction, algebra]

5.1 Expand each crossing of $\beta$ by $C_i$ or $C_i^{-}$ and close the resulting Temperley–Lieb diagrams. The unnormalized trace counts each closed state circle with a factor $\delta$, whereas the bracket state sum counts it with $\delta^{|S|-1}$; hence $$\langle\widehat\beta\rangle =\delta^{-1}\operatorname{Tr}_n(\rho_n(\beta)) =\delta^{n-1}\tau_n(\rho_n(\beta)).$$ Since $C_i=-A S_i$ and $C_i^{-}=(-A)^{-1}S_i^{-1}$, multiplying along an Artin word gives $\rho_n(\beta)=(-A)^{e(\beta)}\rho_H(\pi_n(\beta))$. Step 4.1 now gives the displayed trace identity. [F1, F3, F7, F9, F11, step 4.1, algebra]

6.1 The closure of the identity in $B_1$ has one circle; the state sum gives $\delta^{1-1}=1$, and its trace expression gives $\delta^0(-A)^0\operatorname{tr}_1(1)=1$. For positive stabilization, the Markov trace factor is $z_0=-1/(q^2+1)$, so the bracket formula changes by $$(-A)\delta z_0=-A^3.$$ For negative stabilization, [F9] and [F8] give trace factor $$z_-:=t^{-1}(z_0+1-t)=-\frac{q^2}{q^2+1};$$ the bracket formula changes by $$\delta(-A)^{-1}z_-=-A^{-3}.$$ This proves the unknot value and both stabilization normalizations. [F7, F8, F9, F11, step 5.1, algebra] ∎
