The retained scope is: define every finite-matrix phi function by its everywhere-convergent series; prove its recurrence and augmented-exponential block identity including at zero; define the Euclidean logarithmic norm and prove its matrix-exponential bound; derive an exact finite-matrix defect identity; give a finite-budget scaled Taylor action with truncation, certified local roundoff, finite call count, and inconclusive outcomes; give Arnoldi exponential and phi actions with exact happy breakdown separated from numerical breakdown; prove a semigroup-weighted residual certificate; and work three examples (phi_1(0), a stiff diagonal action missing a slow mode, and a nonnormal transient action).
