This also covers a neighbourhood `V` that is not itself open. The contract derivation, empty-case boundary record and risk review were updated. The Statement and its direct consumer are unchanged by this proof repair. This is closed row `frontier-36-complete-5a-d-alpha-26-barrier-empty`; the scope had no separate ordinary obligation for the risk-review finding, so it is recorded by supplemental decision `gate:frontier-36-complete-5a-d-alpha-26-barrier-empty`.
