The examples compute the degree-one root and Lobatto operator norms, transfer
an explicit factorial best-error rate for the exponential, and prove divergence
for equispaced interpolation of Runge's function. The last example shows why
polynomial density alone does not imply convergence for a fixed interpolation
array.
