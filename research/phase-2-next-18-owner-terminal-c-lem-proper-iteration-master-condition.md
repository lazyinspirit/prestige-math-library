# Owner terminal review: `lem-proper-iteration-master-condition`

**Decision:** `repaired`.

Terra correctly rejected the claim that the naive projection of $D\cap M$ to a shorter stage is automatically predense.  The repair defines, below the current condition in the intermediate extension, the dense set of tails that either extend into $D$ or are incompatible with the current tail.  Elementarity and the lower-stage master then produce the required compatible member, after which properness of the next iterand supplies the extension.

This is the dense-set localization in Jech’s Proper Iteration Lemma, *Set Theory*, Lemmas 31.16–31.18 on printed pages 604–606, bibliographic page https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/3-540-44761-X.  The revised proof no longer assumes an invalid projection theorem.
