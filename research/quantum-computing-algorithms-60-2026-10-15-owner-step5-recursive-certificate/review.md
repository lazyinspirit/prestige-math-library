## Scalar recovery and syndrome coordinates without assuming unitary recovery

Start with the finite phase-free Pauli basis $P_a$ of weight at most four. Each is unitary; the family $P_a/\sqrt M$ is therefore a normalized physical channel. Its correction by the fixed decoded recovery with Kraus operators $R_j$ implies $R_jP_aW=c_{ja}I$: these are Kraus operators of the identity logical channel, whose Choi rank is one. Taking linear combinations gives this identity on the whole error span. Do not normalize an arbitrary nonunitary error basis as if all its elements were unitary.

Summing $W^*P_a^*R_j^*R_jP_bW$ over $j$ gives $W^*P_a^*P_bW=c_{ab}I$. Diagonalizing the finite positive matrix $(c_{ab})$ produces an isometry $J$ from logical register times syndrome space onto the correctable range. This constructs syndrome coordinates; the physical correction itself remains its listed nonunitary instrument. If an actual branch operator $A$ maps every erroneous encoded input to $\sum_c E'_cWg$ with coefficients independent of the logical factor, linearity on the orthogonalized error basis gives $AJ_{in}=J_{out}(g\otimes B_A)$ on its admitted range. For a measurement replace $g$ by its outcome-resolved logical Kraus operator and keep the public result port. Sum $A^*A=I$ on that range to obtain trace preservation there, including coherent incoming syndromes.

The same proof works recursively. Apply the child recovery isometries first. Each unmarked child factor is logical identity with an attached syndrome coefficient; each unrestricted child factor becomes an arbitrary operator on that one outer coordinate. At most $s$ such coordinates occur, so the outer fixed recovery corrects $s\le4$. This proves recursive recovery and supplies $J_{r;s}$ without inserting a decoder into the noisy circuit. Cross terms are covered because all these are pairwise operator identities. Syndrome systems may be entangled between wires, but each system stays attached to the physical edge which carries it; no extra operation on a global syndrome store is introduced.

## The local contraction argument

Fix the lower implementation and its complete invariant. Consider one finite upper circuit with at most one bad lower child $L$. There is no assumption on $L$'s output: regard its full channel on the listed input/output ports as arbitrary CPTP. The listed supplier schedules serialize their active gates and place an idle on every other live register. For every output wire of $L$, its next good use is therefore either an idle, or a protected gate whose first operation on that input is a block-local correction; if it is discarded no cleanup is needed. That initial correction/idle attracts the whole arbitrary lower port into $K_{r-1;1}$. It operates before the first interaction on that port.

Move this *first-use* block-local cleanup backward along that unused wire to the output of $L$. Every intervening operation has disjoint ports, so the CP maps commute even when their inputs are entangled. A discarded output can be traced instead. The product of these local cleanup instruments and $L$ is CPTP after all private outcomes are summed. It changes only $L$'s listed output ports, so it is one arbitrary faulty upper location on the original location support. It is not one fault plus an independently counted refresh fault. Its retained subnormalized branches are left in the contraction. On an already-admitted predecessor output the same cleanup is decoded identity.

After cleanup each subsequent good child is contracted by its admitted logical/syndrome identity. Leading cleanups and remainders must be contracted as their actual consecutive branch operators: an individual normalized postselected branch is never declared CPTP. The resulting network is exactly the original finite upper circuit with one arbitrary local replacement, with private syndrome maps as coefficients and all public records retained. If a final output of $L$ is never used again, keep it as an unrestricted lower factor instead of presuming it encoded. The following finite case analysis shows that these unrefreshed output factors and any remaining coarse error have the same coordinate label. That is the missing support certificate.
