## Edits (all in-flight items of this batch; reflow and precheck run on each)

1. `items/prop-first-stiefel-whitney-class-classifies-orientability.md`
   - **Defect (fatal, repaired).** The statement claimed the bijection
     `Vect_1(B) ≅ H^1(B;F_2)`, `L ↦ w_1(L)`, for every
     *classification-scope* base (paracompact Hausdorff CGWH, not necessarily of
     CW type), while its proof used the representability theorem, which the
     cited item states only for based CW complexes. The claim is false in that
     generality: for the Warsaw circle `W` (compact Hausdorff, hence
     paracompact Hausdorff and CGWH; Schon, Remark 4(a), p. 166, records
     `π̌_1(W) ≅ Z` with contractible fibre and total space in Example 2.4.8) the
     nontrivial double cover is a numerable real line bundle, so
     `[W, RP^∞] ≅ Z/2`, while `W` is weakly contractible, so
     `H^1_sing(W;F_2) = 0`; moreover `w_1` of that bundle lies in that zero
     group. Hatcher's Proposition 3.10 confines the statement to spaces of the
     homotopy type of a CW complex, and Proposition 3.11 does the same for the
     orientability criterion, so the scope was narrowed to CW complexes and
     admissible bases.
   - **Defect (fatal, repaired).** Step 1.2 derived the tensor identity by
     applying it to `Q ≅ γ^* ⊗ π^*(L⊗M)`, i.e. it assumed the very identity
     being proved, and step 2.2 and step 3.1 depended on that step.
   - **Repair.** Statement now covers CW complexes and admissible
     (paracompact Hausdorff, CW homotopy type) bases; the bijection and
     orientability clauses are proved over a CW complex first (steps 1.1, 2.1)
     and transported to admissible bases along a homotopy equivalence from a CW
     model (new step 3.1, using pullback bijections on line bundles and on
     `H^1` and naturality of `w_1`). Tensor additivity is now proved the way
     Hatcher proves Proposition 3.10: on `RP^∞×RP^∞`, with the universal
     product bundle `N = q_1^*γ_1 ⊗ q_2^*γ_1`, whose class is read off on the
     two axes using the Künneth basis `a_1, a_2` (new [F7], [F8]); the general
     case follows by classifying maps and naturality. The determinant step 4.1
     then transfers the tensor identity to the flag bundle, and step 5.1 gives
     the orientability equivalence.
   - **Contract update.** Added dependencies
     `lem-mod-two-cohomology-ring-of-infinite-real-projective-space`,
     `lem-tautological-degree-one-class-is-well-defined-and-fiber-generating`,
