## Defect outside edit authority

`items/thm-smooth-dependence-of-ode-solutions-on-parameters.md`, Statement lines 30–38, with proof invocation at 2.1: **fatal missing hypothesis in the stated supplier**, reached by assigned consumer `thm-stable-normal-bundle-is-independent-of-the-embedding`. The Statement requires smoothness only in (x,λ) and asserts local existence on a common time interval, without continuity in time or joint continuity of state/parameter derivatives. Its Facts/Given instead calls the vector field smooth, a stronger hypothesis, and its proof uses that stronger reading.

Explicit counterexample to the Statement as written: F(t,x,λ)=1 when t=0 and 0 otherwise, on the open domain R³, is smooth in (x,λ) for each t. A classical solution with t₀=0 would have derivative zero for every t≠0 and derivative one at zero. The mean value theorem makes it constant on both sides of zero; continuity then makes it constant across zero, forcing derivative zero there, a contradiction. The opened published IVP definition requires the differential equation at every time, and its local interval is nondegenerate. The opened initial-data supplier does not supply local existence either: it assumes a common local solution map. A safe supplier repair would explicitly require joint smoothness, as the cited Wang argument and the assigned normal-transport use do, or state the precise weaker joint continuity assumptions needed. Published content was left untouched.
