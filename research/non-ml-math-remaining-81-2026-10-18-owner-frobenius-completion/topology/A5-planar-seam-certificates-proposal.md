# A5 replacement: finite planar seam certificates

This is a proposed replacement for the Proof of A5, with its existing Facts and assumptions. It does not assert completeness of the relation list. Canonical associators, unitors and slot permutations are understood as in A2. Their surface versions are product cylinders, so they add no internal identifications to the certificates.

**Given:** The ten oriented generator patches, their marked black collars, and all free-edge colors.

1.1 We first give the elementary cut procedure used in every certificate below. Let $P(B_0;B_1,\ldots,B_h)$ be a round disk with $h$ disjoint smaller round disks removed. Each $B_j$ is the cyclic word of marked black intervals and colored free arcs on that component, or a single marked black circle. The orientation on $B_0$ is opposite to that on the interior holes, as dictated by the surface orientation. A word is always read in the induced surface-boundary direction; $X^-$ and $U^+$ denote an incoming and outgoing black slot. To cut $P$ into a disk, pick a nonblack basepoint on each component with such a point, or a basepoint on a black circle otherwise, and draw disjoint arcs $s_j$ from $B_0$ to $B_j$. Arrange the arcs at the root in the order $1,\ldots,h$. After cutting them the disk boundary word is
$$
B_0s_1B_1s_1^{-1}s_2B_2s_2^{-1}\cdots s_hB_hs_h^{-1}.
$$
Black circle basepoints are auxiliary, not extra markings. A desired circle parameterization is respected on its whole collar, including this point. If two cut patches have this same word, match the corresponding black collars by their parameterizations and match each colored free arc by an increasing smooth map. Match the two sides of every $s_j$ by identical product maps. This gives a disk-boundary map extending to the collars. After rounding the nonblack corners, lift the remaining angular map $h$ to a strictly increasing map of $\mathbb R$ with $h(\theta+2\pi)=h(\theta)+2\pi$. The interpolation $(1-u)\theta+uh(\theta)$ has positive derivative for $0\le u\le1$. Use $u=0$ near the center and $u=1$ near the boundary; its polar extension is smooth and orientation preserving, and agrees with the prescribed collar map after the collar is attached. Regluing the paired $s_j$ edges gives the desired diffeomorphism. This proves the cut criterion directly for these planar models; no surface normal-form theorem is used. [given, F1, construct]

1.2 Here are the three gluing rules for computing the certificates. A disk generator with cyclic word $EX$ glued to another disk with word $X^{-1}D$ along an interval seam $X$ becomes the disk with word $ED$; this follows by placing the two collars along the two sides of the same straight segment in a disk. If either disk has holes, carry their disjoint removed disks through this construction; the holes are simply retained. Gluing a boundary circle of one planar patch to one of a different planar patch joins their annular collars and gives a planar patch with the unglued boundary components; this can be seen by placing one punctured disk in the deleted disk of the other, using inversion of the annular radial coordinate on the collar. Finally, two disks glued along two disjoint interval seams give an annulus when the boundary splicing yields two boundary cycles. To check this without a classification theorem, first glue one seam to obtain a disk; the second seam identifies two boundary intervals with opposite induced orientations. Their standard rectangular collar is a strip joining those intervals outside the disk. The two complementary boundary arcs are distinct, and a cut through that strip returns a disk. Its regluing is precisely $P(B_0;B_1)$. All these constructions respect the marked portions of the boundary. The rule for one interval seam can be applied repeatedly to any tree of disk/annulus patches. [step 1.1, F1, construct]

1.3 A product generator is a disk with cyclic boundary word $U_{ac}^{+}F_cY_{bc}^{-}F_bX_{ab}^{-}F_a$. A coproduct generator is the same disk read with reversed heights, with word $U_{ab}^{+}F_bV_{bc}^{+}F_cX_{ac}^{-}F_a$. The unit and counit are disks with a single black interval and its complementary free arc. A zipper is an annulus with words $(U_{aa}^{+}F_a;X_C^{-})$, and a cozipper is an annulus with words $(X_{aa}^{-}F_a;U_C^{+})$. The closed product and coproduct are punctured disks with three marked circle boundaries; the closed unit and counit have one. These are concrete choices of the elementary patches. The gluing rules in step 1.2 give the following exhaustive table for the defining equations of A2. 2.1 Every row is a finite application of the gluing rules to specified disks or punctured disks. The rules prove planarity by construction; planarity is not inferred merely from an Euler characteristic or an appeal to the classification under proof. For an open row with one boundary component the displayed word is already the cut disk. For the other rows cut the indicated root-to-hole arcs, and use step 1.1. The identical words and paired seam sides give orientation-preserving diffeomorphisms fixed on every external black collar and preserving each free-edge label. In particular the Cardy row retains separate $a$- and $b$-labeled boundary components, and the open Frobenius rows retain their different domains and codomains. The cyclic trace row allows rotation of the disk boundary word but fixes the actual black slots after their specified symmetry; it gives no open commutativity assertion. [step 1.1, step 1.2, step 1.3, F1, F2, construct]

2.2 The rows give the closed Frobenius, open indexed Frobenius, cyclic trace, zipper algebra, centrality, pairing adjunction and Cardy equations of A2. A strong symmetric monoidal functor preserves composition, tensor, identities and the symmetry cylinders, and carries the product-cylinder associators and unitors to its tensor comparison maps coherently. Applying it to these actual surface equalities therefore gives exactly the typed equations in A2. No relation-completeness assertion has been used. [step 2.1, F2, F3] ∎

## Integration note

The right closed-Frobenius row can alternatively be obtained by swapping the two inputs and two outputs of the preceding row with the closed commutativity/cocommutativity diffeomorphisms. If using the explicit seam partition description, retain the actual four marked circle slots throughout; the common punctured-disk certificate, not a planar drawing order of its holes, is what fixes them.
