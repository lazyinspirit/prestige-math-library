## Preserved scope and exact conventions

Preserve arbitrary fields, nonsemisimple finite-dimensional Frobenius algebras, the functional/object equivalence, the closed oriented 2D TQFT equivalence, and the knowledgeable open/closed sewing equivalence. Symmetric does not mean commutative. Finite Hopf Frobenius is an example supplier, not a surface classification theorem. Neither `C=Z(A)` nor semisimplicity is imposed. No 3D modular TQFT or fully extended classification at points is being inferred.

Use compact oriented smooth 2-manifolds with faces `(∂0M,∂1M)`: their black boundary `∂0M` is parametrized incoming/outgoing circles and intervals; their free boundary `∂1M` is colored by a fixed set S; intersections are corners and collars respect faces. Morphisms are orientation-preserving diffeomorphism classes relative to all black parametrizations and preserving free labels. Objects are finite ordered words in circles and oriented intervals with ordered endpoint colors; composition glues collars only with matching colors and opposite induced orientations. Tensor is ordered disjoint union and symmetry permutes parametrized components. The singleton S case is the knowledgeable pair `(A,C,i,i*)`; retaining general S is legitimate and prevents accidental erasure of boundary labels. For the closed subcategory, **there is no free boundary**: it is not merely the full subcategory on circle objects, which would admit windows.

Target is `Vect_fd(k)` with its symmetric tensor product. A more general symmetric monoidal target may be stated after its coherence conventions are supplied, but is unnecessary for the finite-dimensional claim. For general S the correct open data are `A_ab`, not one algebra for every interval: `μ_abc:A_ab⊗A_bc→A_ac`, `Δ_abc:A_ac→A_ab⊗A_bc`, units `η_a:k→A_aa`, counits `ε_a:A_aa→k`, common commutative Frobenius C, `i_a:C→A_aa`, and `i_a*:A_aa→C`. Use LP (5.2)–(5.14), explicitly typed, including

`i_a i_b* = μ_aba τ_(A_ba,A_ab) Δ_bab : A_bb→A_aa`.

The pairings identify `A_ab` with the dual of `A_ba`. Arbitrary S does not introduce an infinite sum: any one compact surface uses finitely many generators and labels.

## Derived-move proof route: supply calculations, not pictures alone

For either Frobenius object X define `b_X=ε_X μ_X` and `d_X=Δ_X η_X`. The calculation
`(1⊗b_X)(d_X⊗1)=(1⊗ε_X) Δ_X=id_X`
uses Frobenius followed by unit/counit; derive the other snake too. Associativity gives invariance of b; symmetry of b and the snakes give the corresponding symmetry of d. These prove LP (3.47)–(3.51) and the rotation formulae (3.58)–(3.61), by composing a leg of b or d and canceling a snake.

Pairing duality is `b_C(1⊗i*)=b_A(i⊗1)`. Swapping the pairing legs gives its reflected version; insert `d_A` or `d_C` and cancel snakes to obtain **both** copairing transports (3.54), (3.55). Transpose `i μ_C=μ_A(i⊗i)` under the nondegenerate pairings to obtain `Δ_C i*=(i*⊗i*) Δ_A`; transpose the unit equation to obtain `ε_C i*=ε_A`. This supplies (3.64) used in stage I.

Set `W=i* i:C→C`, `H=μ_CΔ_C:C→C`, and `O=μ_AΔ_A:A→A`. Derive the C-module relation `i*(i(c)a)=c i*(a)` by pairing both sides against arbitrary closed input, using pairing duality, associativity and centrality of i. Thus W is C-linear; it slides through μ_C, and by transposition through Δ_C. H slides by Frobenius and associativity; O is an A-bimodule map by the same calculation. These give (3.65)–(3.71).
