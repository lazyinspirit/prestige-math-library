## 1. The doubled pyramid grading really satisfies the required hypotheses

Use e=ΣE_(L(i),i), acting leftwards. If the original column degree of E_ij is col(j)−col(i), set the Gan–Ginzburg degree equal to twice this. Thus e has degree2, the degree−1 space is zero, the Lagrangian is zero, and m is precisely the original strictly negative column subalgebra. The native symplectic-form assertion on the original degree−1 space is false; it is not needed. The trace form is nondegenerate also on gl_N, so no Killing-form degeneracy is silently imported.

For a row of length p take h with entries p+1−2c at column c and f the usual right-shift sl2 operator. The grading element H differs from h by a scalar on each row and commutes with this sl2 triple. On Hom(R_b,R_a), the elementary tensor-product sl2 decomposition has strings with highest h-weight p_a+p_b−2k, 1≤k≤min(p_a,p_b). Their highest and lowest H-weights are respectively 2(p_b−k)≥0 and −2(p_a−k)≤0. Hence ad e is injective in every negative H-degree and surjective onto every strictly positive H-degree. The lowest vectors are exactly ker ad f; they lie in nonpositive H-degrees. Since trace pairs opposite degrees, m^perp is the sum of nonpositive H-degrees. String by string,

m^perp=[m,e] ⊕ ker ad f.

The character condition is immediate: [m,m] has degree≤−4 and pairs trivially with e of degree2. The case e=0 has m=0 and all subsequent arguments reduce to identities.

Let M=exp(m), with polynomial exponential and logarithm (finite nilpotent series). Consider α:M×(e+ker ad f)→e+m^perp, (u,z)↦uzu^−1. Its differential at (1,e) is the preceding direct-sum isomorphism. Both sides are affine spaces with positive contracting weights: on m use −H-weight, and on the slice/target use 2−H-weight. Choose homogeneous coordinates ordered by increasing positive weight. In a coordinate of weight w, an equivariant polynomial has a linear combination of variables of weight w plus a polynomial in variables of smaller weight; a nonlinear monomial involving a weight-w variable has larger weight. The differential makes each equal-weight linear block invertible. Successive substitution therefore constructs a polynomial inverse to α. This proves the affine-space isomorphism without a contracting-variety theorem or an unproved formal-character assertion.

## 2. PBW, cohomology and Skryabin equivalence

Put Q=U(g)/U(g)I_chi and use the doubled Kazhdan degree H-degree+2. PBW orders p before m and replaces the rightmost m generators by their character; thus gr Q=C[e+m^perp], with a nonnegative exhaustive separated filtration. The preceding α identifies it, as an m-module, with C[M]⊗C[e+ker ad f]. The Chevalley–Eilenberg complex on C[M] is the algebraic de Rham complex: invariant vector fields trivialize the tangent bundle and their brackets give exactly the CE differential. Exponential coordinates identify M with affine space. On a polynomial differential form of positive total degree, contraction with the Euler vector field divided by total degree is a homotopy; constants are the only degree-zero cohomology. Therefore H^0(m,gr Q)=C[e+ker ad f] and H^j(m,gr Q)=0 for j>0.

Give the CE exterior dual of m the positive weights opposite to its H-degrees, so that the differential preserves filtration. Vanishing of the graded positive cohomology lifts cocycles explicitly: subtract a coboundary with the same highest filtration term and repeat, decreasing an integer bounded below. The same procedure lifts every graded invariant. Consequently gr W=C[e+ker ad f], H^j(m,Q)=0 for j>0, and H^0(m,Q)=W (with the eta twist restored in its p realization).

Choose polynomial coordinates on M homogeneous for the contracting action and lifts to Q. Their ordered monomials form a right W-basis: spanning and independence follow by highest filtration terms from gr Q=C[M]⊗gr W, with finite descending induction. In particular Q is right W-free. For a finitely generated W-module L give it the filtration from finite generators. This basis proves gr(Q⊗_W L)=gr Q⊗_(gr W)gr L. The same CE homotopy/descending induction gives H^0(m,Q⊗_W L)=L and H^j(m,Q⊗_W L)=0 for j>0. Arbitrary L is a filtered union of finitely generated modules; tensor products and the finite-term CE complex commute with these unions, so the identities extend to arbitrary L.

Whittaker vectors detect a nonzero locally nilpotent module independently of this equivalence. The shifted m representation is a nilpotent Lie algebra whose individual operators are locally nilpotent. Choose a central element, take its nonzero kernel, and induct on dim m; the central kernel is stable and the quotient acts with the same local nilpotence. This gives a nonzero common kernel. The same proof applies to right modules by reversing the signs of the Lie action.

For an object E in the Whittaker category, the canonical counit Q⊗_W Wh(E)→E has kernel with zero Whittaker vectors, because the unit on Wh(E) is the preceding H^0 identity. Its kernel is therefore zero. The long exact CE sequence, H^1(Q⊗Wh(E))=0 and H^0 of the counit being an isomorphism then give zero Whittaker vectors in its cokernel; the cokernel is zero. This proves Skryabin equivalence and exactness, including the right-module version needed below. It does not assert exactness of the genuine Whittaker functor on unrestricted ordinary category O.

## 3. Continuous dual injectivity with a local Artin–Rees proof

Let U=U(m), I=I_chi. Replace x by y=x−chi(x). These shifted generators have the original brackets, because chi vanishes on brackets. Choose a basis adapted to the lower-central filtration, with weight w_i the largest lower-central index containing its basis vector. PBW reordering and the commutator formula show that I^n is spanned by ordered monomials of total lower-central weight≥n: one inclusion follows from reordering n factors, while the reverse follows because each basis vector of weight w lies in I^w, being a linear combination of iterated brackets of length w.

The Rees algebra R=⊕_(n≥0) I^n t^n is generated by y_i t^r, 0≤r≤w_i. Their span is a finite-dimensional Lie algebra: a nonzero bracket component y_k has w_k≥w_i+w_j, so r+s≤w_k. PBW gives its enveloping algebra a polynomial associated graded. For any left ideal, Hilbert basis gives finitely many generators of its homogeneous leading ideal; lift them and subtract matching highest-degree terms until the nonnegative degree decreases to zero. The lifts generate the original ideal. Thus the enveloping algebra is left noetherian, and its surjection onto R makes R left noetherian. For a left ideal J⊂U, ⊕(J∩I^n)t^n is a graded left ideal of R and has finitely many homogeneous generators. For fixed k, choosing n greater than k plus their largest degrees gives J∩I^n⊂I^k J. This is the precise Artin–Rees consequence needed here.

Set E_chi=∪_(n≥0)(U/I^n)^*, with left action (x f)(r)=f(r x). To prove injectivity, take a U-linear map phi:J→E_chi. U is noetherian, so choose finitely many generators of J. A common I^k kills their images and hence all images, since I is two-sided. The scalar functional lambda(j)=phi(j)(1) kills I^k J. Choose n with J∩I^n⊂I^k J, extend lambda from J/(J∩I^n) to U/I^n, and call the extension a∈E_chi. The map U→E_chi, u↦u a, extends phi, because at r its value on j is a(rj)=phi(rj)(1)=phi(j)(r). Baer's criterion gives injectivity. This supplies the injective-continuous-dual assertion cited without proof in BK Lemma8.20.

## 4. Exactness on genuine parabolic O and the action convention

For a U(m)-finitely generated g-module X, evaluation at1 identifies Hom_m(X,E_chi) with the scalar functionals lambda:X→C that kill I^n X for some n. Indeed, reconstruct the map by F_lambda(v)(r)=lambda(rv); finitely many generators give a common n. The right U(g)-action is (lambda·u)(v)=lambda(uv). On E-valued maps its formula is therefore

(F·u)(v)(r)=lambda(u r v),

not the generally non-m-linear expression F(uv)(r)=lambda(r u v). This distinguishes the compressed scalar-functional notation in the source from a literal E-valued composition.

The right action preserves continuity. For a fixed u∈U(g), the finite PBW subspace generated by all iterated ad m on u is finite-dimensional and its negative-column action is nilpotent; sufficiently long products of ad m vanish. Moving u past a product of sufficiently many shifted m generators therefore leaves at least n of them on the left. Equivalently u I^(n+C)⊂I^n U(g) for some C=C(u). Thus lambda u kills I^(n+C)X. On this continuous-dual module the right shifted m-action is locally nilpotent. Injectivity of E_chi makes Hom_m(−,E_chi) exact, and right Skryabin makes its Whittaker vectors exact. These Whittaker vectors are precisely Hom_m(X,C_chi). Dualizing this finite-dimensional vector space gives an exact covariant functor.

The restricted dual uses the row-sum torus a=span{Σ_(row(j)=r)E_jj}, not the full Cartan and not the column-origin vector c. Every e-adjacent pair lies in one row, so chi has a-weight0. If X is generated over U(m) by finitely many a-weight spaces, an m-linear functional into C_chi vanishes outside those finitely many weights: lambda(uv)=chi(u)lambda(v), and chi(u)=0 in every nonzero a-weight. Thus the restricted-dual definition agrees with the entire finite-dimensional Hom space. Generalized parabolic Vermas are U(m)-free on a finite-dimensional Levi module by PBW; their finite projective flags/presentations from the repaired parabolic highest-weight supplier make every genuine parabolic O object finitely generated over U(m). This establishes exactness on precisely that category.

For X=U(g)⊗_(U(p))X_0, the Hom space is canonically X_0^*. The finite-W action on its covariant dual uses the right-model head twist eta_head(E_jj)=E_jj+n−Σ_(k≤col(j))q_k. The left-model twist is eta_tail(E_jj)=E_jj+n−Σ_(k≥col(j))q_k. Their numerical difference is −tr_m(ad E_jj), since the trace counts earlier minus later column heights. This numerical identity does not by itself prove equality of the two W models. The actual identification of the left/right W models and compatibility on all shifted-Yangian generators is the explicit matrix-generator check in the companion finite-W matrix note; the vector-space duality alone does not supply it.
