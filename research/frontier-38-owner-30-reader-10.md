## Uneditable defect and lead action

`character-groups-and-elementary-lca-duals-examples`, summary lines 21–23: the map described for lifting is `chi composed with epsilon: R/Z -> T`. Such a map need not lift to the universal cover; taking `chi=id` already gives the degree-one circle isomorphism. Elementarily, if it lifted to a continuous `ell: R/Z -> R`, then `a(t)=ell([t])` would be continuous, `a(t)-t` integer-valued and therefore constant on the connected line, but `a(1)=a(0)`, a contradiction. The valid argument in the assigned real-line and circle items first precomposes with `p: R -> R/Z`. The lead should describe lifting that real-line composite, or say the result follows from the real-line character classification after precomposition with `p`. Class: false-claim; severity: fatal as a literal mathematical lifting assertion. The character classification itself is sound. No published-dependency defect was confirmed in the used interfaces.
