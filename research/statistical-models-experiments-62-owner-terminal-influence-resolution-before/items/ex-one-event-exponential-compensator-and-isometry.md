## Example

Let $W\sim\operatorname{Exp}(\lambda)$ with $\lambda>0$, $N_t=\mathbf1_{\{W\le t\}}$, and
$Y_t=\mathbf1_{\{t\le W\}}=1-N_{t-}$. On $[0,T]$ the compensator and
martingale are
$$A_t=\int_0^t\lambda Y_s\,ds=\lambda(t\wedge W),\qquad M_t=N_t-A_t.$$
They satisfy the finite-horizon model contract, and the constant step
integrand $H=1$ gives
$$\mathbb E[M_T^2]=\mathbb E[A_T]=1-e^{-\lambda T}.$$

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** $\lambda>0$, a waiting time $W$ with density $f_W(w)=\lambda e^{-\lambda w}$ on $[0,\infty)$ and tail $P(W>w)=e^{-\lambda w}$, the natural filtration $\mathcal F_t=\sigma(N_s:0\le s\le t)$, and a finite horizon $T>0$.

[F1] Predictability is measurability for the sigma-algebra generated by $(s,t]\times B$, $B\in\mathcal F_s$, and time-zero sets; it is preserved under pointwise limits and products, and $1_{[0,\tau]}$ is predictable for a stopping time $\tau$. ([[def-progressively-measurable-and-predictable-process]])

[F2] The model contract requires $M=N-A$ to be a square-integrable martingale and $\mathbb E A_T<\infty$. ([[def-counting-process-intensity-and-compensator-contract]])

[F3] For bounded predictable step $H$, the elementary integral isometry gives $\mathbb E(\int_0^T H\,dM)^2=\mathbb E\int_0^T H^2dA$. ([[lem-elementary-counting-process-integral-isometry]])

[F4] Under the stated contract, $[M]_t=N_t$ and $\langle M\rangle_t=A_t$. ([[thm-compensated-counting-process-brackets]])

[A1] AC is inherited through conditional expectation in the martingale interface. ([[def-axiom-of-choice]])

## Verification

1.1 The process $N$ has one unit jump and $Y_t=1-N_{t-}$ is predictable by dyadic left-step approximation. Therefore $A_t=\lambda(t\wedge W)$ is continuous, increasing, starts at zero, and is adapted by left Riemann sums; continuous adapted left-step approximations make it predictable by [F1]. The exponential density gives $\mathbb E A_T=\lambda(\int_0^T u\lambda e^{-\lambda u}du+Te^{-\lambda T})=1-e^{-\lambda T}<\infty$, verifying the compensator hypotheses in [F2]. [F1, F2, given, construct]

1.2 Fix $0\le s<t\le T$. On $\{W\le s\}$ both $N$ and $A$ have stopped changing, so $M_t-M_s=0$. On $\{W>s\}$, $\mathbb P(W-s>u\mid W>s)=\mathbb P(W>s+u)/\mathbb P(W>s)=e^{-\lambda u}$; with $h=t-s$, the conditional event increment is $\mathbf1_{\{W>s\}}(1-e^{-\lambda h})$, and the residual exponential density gives the same compensator increment $\mathbf1_{\{W>s\}}\lambda(\int_0^h u\lambda e^{-\lambda u}du+he^{-\lambda h})=\mathbf1_{\{W>s\}}(1-e^{-\lambda h})$. Thus $\mathbb E[M_t\mid\mathcal F_s]=M_s$ for every pair, and $|M_t|\le1+\lambda T$ makes $M$ square-integrable and verifies [F2]. [A1, F2, given, algebra]

2.1 The process $H=1$ on $(0,T]$ and $H_0=0$ is a bounded predictable step integrand with elementary integral $M_T-M_0=M_T$. By [F3], $\mathbb E M_T^2=\mathbb E A_T=1-e^{-\lambda T}$; by [F4], its optional and predictable brackets are $N$ and $A$. [F2, F3, F4, given, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
