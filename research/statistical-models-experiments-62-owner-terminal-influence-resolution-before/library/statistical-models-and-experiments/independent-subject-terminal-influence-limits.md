This page proves finite-dimensional terminal normal limits from a separately established subject-level influence expansion. The IID result uses the multivariate central limit theorem and vector Slutsky; the fixed finite-group result proves the covariance sum $\sum_g\rho_g^{-1}\Sigma_g$ while allowing different group laws. Both results require centered finite-variance contributions, and the grouped result also requires deterministic sample sizes with positive limiting proportions. They do not prove a consumer's risk-set or random-denominator remainder, and they assert no process-level or functional martingale limit.
