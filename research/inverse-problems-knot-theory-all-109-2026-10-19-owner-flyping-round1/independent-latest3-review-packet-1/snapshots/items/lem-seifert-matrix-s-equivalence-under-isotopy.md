## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $(K,F)$ and $(K',F')$ be oriented knot and spanning-surface pairs in the oriented standard three-sphere, and let $h_t:S^3\to S^3$ be a smooth ambient isotopy with $h_0$ the identity, $h_1(K)=K'$, and $h_1(F)=F'$, preserving the given orientations. If $V$ and $V'$ are their Seifert matrices in any integral bases, then the basis transported by $h_1$ has matrix $V$, and any other target basis with transition matrix $P$ has matrix $V'=P^TVP$. Hence $V$ and $V'$ are S-equivalent, meaning they are related by a finite sequence of integral unimodular congruences and the two elementary rank-two enlargement moves or their reverses.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** Full AC, two oriented knot-surface pairs and an orientation-preserving smooth ambient isotopy carrying the first pair to the second.

[A1] Full AC supplies the Alexander-duality linking convention used by the Seifert pairing. [[def-axiom-of-choice]]

[F1] The Seifert pairing is bilinear on homology classes and is computed by linking positive normal push-offs. [[def-knot-seifert-pairing-and-matrix]], [[lem-seifert-pairing-well-defined]]

[F2] An orientation-preserving ambient isotopy induces homology isomorphisms on complements and carries positive meridians to positive meridians; naturality of Alexander duality preserves the total-linking coefficient. [[def-oriented-link-in-s-three-and-ambient-isotopy]], [[prop-singular-chains-and-homology-are-covariantly-functorial]], [[def-one-variable-alexander-module-of-an-oriented-link]]

[F3] The positive normal is fixed by the ambient and surface orientations, so an orientation-preserving diffeomorphism carrying the oriented surface carries positive normal levels to positive normal levels. [[def-knot-seifert-pairing-and-matrix]]

[F4] S-equivalence is generated by integral unimodular congruences, the two elementary rank-two enlargements, and their reverses. [[def-seifert-matrix-s-equivalence]]

## Proof

1.1 Put $h=h_1$. Because $h$ preserves the ambient orientation and the orientations of $K,F$, [F3] sends each positive normal push-off of a curve $a_i$ to a positive normal push-off of $h(a_i)$, up to a homotopy within the positive collar. By [F2] the induced map on the complement preserves the positive-meridian coefficient, so
$$
\operatorname{lk}(a_i^+,a_j)=\operatorname{lk}(h(a_i)^+,h(a_j)).
$$
Thus the transported basis $b_i=h_*[a_i]$ of $H_1(F';\mathbb Z)$ has exactly the same matrix $V$. [A1, F1, F2, F3, given, construct]

2.1 Let $c_1,\ldots,c_n$ be any chosen integral basis on $F'$. There is a unique integer matrix $P$ with $[c_j]=\sum_iP_{ij}b_i$; since both lists are integral bases, $P\in\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb Z)$. Bilinearity [F1] gives the matrix in the $c$ basis as $V'=P^TVP$. By [F4], this unimodular congruence is one of the defining S-equivalence moves, so the two matrices are S-equivalent. [F1, F4, step 1.1, given, algebra] ∎

## Remarks

The conclusion uses only the congruence move in the stated S-equivalence relation; the elementary enlargements are included to fix the relation's full convention for later consumers.
