3.2. Fair position, flyping circles, and push-through moves.
Definition 3.2. F is in fair position if:32
(a) F ∩W is comprised entirely of standard arcs;
(b) F is transverse in S3 to B, W, ∂C, and v \ vF ;
(c) ∂F is transverse on ∂νL to each meridian;
(d) whenever ∂F ∩Ct ̸= ∅, F ∩Ct is a crossing band;
(e) no arc of F ∩∂C ∩S± is parallel in ∂C into ∂C ∩∂S0;
(f) B ∪W cuts each component of F ∩C into disks;
(g) each crossing band in F is disjoint from S+; and
(h) S+ ∪S−cuts F into disks.
Lemma 3.3. F can be isotoped into fair position.
The proof of Lemma 3.16 appears in §7, as do the proofs of all
