Greene used Theorem 1.1 of [Gr17] and lattice flows to give a
geometric proof of part of Tait’s conjectures:
Theorem 1.2 of [Gr17]. All reduced alternating diagrams of a given
link have the same crossing number and writhe.
We will give alternate proofs of both parts of this theorem. The
part about crossing number will follow from Theorem 4.5 and will
serve as an intermediate step in our proof of the flyping theorem.
Later, we will deduce the part about writhe as a corollary of the
flyping theorem, since flypes preserve writhe.
Remark 2.16. Theorem 1.2 of [Gr17] does not imply, a priori, that
a reduced alternating diagram realizes the underlying link’s cross-
ing number, since it does not rule out the possibility that a non-
alternating diagram could have fewer crossings. All existing proofs
of this fact [Ka87, Mu87, Th87, Tu87] use the Jones polynomial.
