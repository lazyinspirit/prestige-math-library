2.5. Generalized plumbing.
2.5.1. Basic definitions. Let F be a spanning surface for a nonsplit
link L. A plumbing cap for F is an embedded disk V ⊂S3 \
◦νL with
V ∩(F ∪∂νL) = ∂V such that:
• ∂V bounds a disk bU ⊂F ∪νL,
• bU ∩F is a disk U called the shadow of V , and
• denoting the 3-balls of S3\\(bU ∪V ) by Y1, Y2, neither subsur-
face Fi = F ∩Yi is a disk.
If the first two properties hold but the third fails, we call V a fake
plumbing cap for F; we still call U the shadow of V .
