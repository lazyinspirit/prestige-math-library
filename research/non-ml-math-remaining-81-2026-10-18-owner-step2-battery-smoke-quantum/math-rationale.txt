Item-by-item computational rationale (suffix of frontier81-quantum-*):

1. gentle: rho=1, Lambda=1 or 0. Compute the unnormalized operation directly;
   disturbance is absolute scalar difference, 0 or 1; bound is 0 or 2.
2. sqrt-order: A=0, B=1. Recompute square roots and their squares, positivity
   and ordered difference. This is precisely the authored singular endpoint.
3. hayashi-nagaoka: (S,T)=(0,0),(1,0),(0,1). Support inverse is zero at zero;
   independently evaluate both sides to obtain (1,2),(0,0),(1,6).
4. entropy-zero: spectral entropy sum at (1) and (1,0) uses explicit zero-log
   extension. Both sums vanish, without calling a continuity theorem.
5. packing-one: M=1, d=D=1, u=0,1/4,1. Generate the message-pair index sum;
   there is no competing index. Direct square-root POVM for a sole scalar
   output has error 0, and the computed remaining bound is 2(u+2 sqrt u).
6. schmidt-pure: spectrum (1,0,0), M=1. Realize a physical encoder by E_j=<j|
   and decoder D=|0>. Composite K_j=|0><j| gives fidelity sum 1. Independently
   sum the top M eigenvalues, obtaining 1. This realizes the padded pure case.
7. typical-pure: rho=[1], delta=1/4, n=1..8. Enumerate its only positive
   eigenword; surprisal 0 lies in the typical window, and mass=rank=1.
8. schumacher-pure: rho=diag(1,0), delta=1/4, n=1..8. Enumerate all 2^n
   eigenvalues, omit zero probabilities from surprisal, select rank-one
   support. The authored construction uses one extra flag: M=2 and rate
   ln(2)/n. The retained branch has fidelity contribution mass^2=1. The
   check does not falsely assert that the construction uses M=1, or infer
   an asymptotic convergence theorem from eight block lengths.
9. hsw-single: sole alphabet symbol at four spectra (1),(1,0),(1/2,1/2),
   (1/4,3/4). Directly form the ensemble average and entropy difference,
   which vanishes. One-message decoding by effect I has outcome mass 1.
10. qubit-rates: spectra (p,1-p), p=0,1/2,1. Recompute the spectral entropy
    and divide by ln 2 for qubit units: (0,ln2,0)/(0,1,0).
11. fixed-povm: form X basis vectors and Z basis inputs; square inner products
    and multiply by input probability 1/2. All four joint entries are 1/4.
    Marginals and mutual-information sum are independently recomputed; I=0
    strictly below the average-state spectral Holevo value ln2.
12. binary-pure: c=0,1. Construct the output vector (c,sqrt(1-c^2)), form its
    pure projector and average with |0><0|, then compute eigenvalues through
    the 2x2 characteristic discriminant, yielding Holevo ln2 or 0.
13. toric-inventory: generate 18 edges from modulo-3 endpoints, derive star
    supports by searching actual endpoints, and face supports from the
    oriented square formula. Verify 9 vertices/stars, 9 faces, 18 edges,
    four distinct edges per support, and distinct edge endpoints.
14. toric-ranks: Gaussian elimination over F2 gives each 9x18 rank 8. Enumerate
    all 512 row subsets of each family: only empty/full sums vanish.
15. toric-commutation: all 81 star-face intersection sizes are 0 or 2; for
    each of 18 coordinates count exactly two star and two face occurrences.
16. toric-dimension: recompute ranks and commutation, use separate X and Z
    coordinates, and compute 2^(18-(8+8))=4. No huge Hilbert matrix is built.
17. toric-loops: generate all four declared coordinate supports, verify exact
    intersection counts I2, commutation with relevant checks, and exclusion
    from explicitly generated opposite-type stabilizer support spans.
18. toric-gap: enumerate every even-parity 9-bit star and face sign pattern:
    256^2-1=65,535 nonzero pairs, at couplings (1,1),(1,2),(3,1),(1/2,3/4).
    Minimum of directly computed 2 Je ne+2 Jm nm is 4 min(Je,Jm). For every
    single edge, calculate its star/face syndrome to verify the two-defect
    attainability witnesses. No claim of checking every real coupling.
19. toric-worked: repeat actual inventory/rank/total-relation/logical tests,
    then XOR successive h00,h10,h20 edges and compute star syndromes from
    dot products. Endpoints are {q00,q10}, {q00,q20}, then empty.
20. planar-toric: independently construct the planar d=3 relative chain
    maps from the published supplier definition, deleting rough vertical
    edges and rough vertices. Enumerate all 262,144 supports for each
    18-qubit CSS model. Among commuting supports outside stabilizer spans,
    minimum pure Z/X weights are (3,4) planar and (3,3) toric. Computed
    check ranks yield code dimensions 2 and 4, respectively. This checks
    actual relative/periodic code geometry, not only theorem substitution.
21. fusion-measurement: apply two basis projections to the authored state
    (sqrt(3/5),sqrt(2/5)) and endpoints (1,0),(0,1). Compute branch norms,
    probabilities, normalized nonzero states, and zero projected vectors.
    The zero outcome receives no normalized state.
22. controlled-phase: multiply diagonal controlled-I and controlled-(-I)
    matrices by |+0>, apply X-basis measurement on control, and recompute
    laws (1,0),(0,1), whose total variation distance is 1.
23. b6-identity: start empty word products as identity in dimensions 5,8,
    compute determinant-corrected identity target, restrictions via explicit
    first-four-coordinate embeddings, and complementary projection times
    output. Block/logical error and leakage vanish; exponent zero has phase 1.
    Identity is basis independent, so standard embedding coordinates are a
    faithful finite model for this identity-only boundary case.
24. sk-budget: compute log2 exponents exactly by a rational recurrence:
    (a/b) -> (12b+3a)/(2b). Levels are -13,-27/2,-57/4; first <=-14 is J=2,
    and 5^J=25. Check the authored min allocation at M=1,2,10 independently.
25. multiplicity-two: conditional on the authored split-space basis, enumerate
    the two complete labels with fixed surrounding labels and distinct local
    mu indices. Their coordinate basis Gram matrix is identity and rank 2.
    This does not assert existence of a fusion category with a chosen rule.
26. ising-periodic: apply D fusion adjacency to all nine length-two cyclic
    label pairs from {D,1,eta}. Four satisfy both transitions, two per sector.
    Compute cyclic-shift permutation, which exchanges sectors and squares
    to identity. No categorical F/R coherence inference is made.
27. fib-projector: form numerical phi,u,v and the actual F matrix; compute
    F^T F, F^T P1 F, square, determinant, trace, and P1 P2 P1. This checks
    unitarity, displayed P2, idempotence, rank 1, and factor phi^-2 on the
    authored two-site 2x2 block. The source's richer three-site 3x3 block
    is preserved; this authored finite selector asks for the two-channel block.
