The Euler-twisted Hall product uses a supplied small finite-field hereditary category and the subobject convention $0\to N\to R\to M\to0$. The Green coproduct lands in a coefficientwise completed tensor space; its image products converge and its compatibility is proved by the full cross-square fiber count. The extended algebra uses left-torus normal order and the DJ-compatible coproduct. The ordinary tensor target is asserted when every basis coproduct has finite support.

Assuming the Axiom of Choice inherited through the quantum global-basis and PBW-rank suppliers, for every finite loopless quiver, using finite-dimensional nilpotent representations when oriented cycles occur, the fixed-parameter Ringel map is an injective extended-positive-Borel map with composition image. The numerical Serre-only presentation is proved equal to the actual integral specialization at the prime-power parameter; generic nondegeneracy alone is not used as specialization. Reflection, finite-field Schur descent and explicit affine flag families prove that composition equals full Hall exactly when every connected graph component is finite ADE. Disconnected and empty quivers are included.
