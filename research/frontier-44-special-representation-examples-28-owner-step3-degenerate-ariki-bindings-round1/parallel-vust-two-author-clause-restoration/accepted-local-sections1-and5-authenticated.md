## Authenticated accepted local Section 1
 First centralizer and stable faithfulness, independent of normality

Let V be finite-dimensional over C, e nilpotent with largest string length l≥1, E=V^⊗d and B=C[x_1,...,x_d]/(x_1^l,...,x_d^l)⋊C[S_d], acting by individual e operators and places. Put A=End_{C[e]}V=g^e as an associative vector algebra. Under End(E)=End(V)^⊗d, commuting with each e in its individual position gives A^⊗d: choose complements to the kernels of [e,-] in the individual End(V) factors and compare the direct tensor summands. Commuting with place permutations then gives (A^⊗d)^{S_d}, the span of a^⊗d for a∈A by ordinary characteristic-zero polarization.

This span equals the image of U(g^e). One inclusion is immediate from commuting individual e/operators and places. For the other put a_i=1⊗...⊗a⊗...⊗1; these d operators commute. Newton identities express a_1...a_d as a polynomial over Q in their power sums p_k=sum_i a_i^k=Delta(a^k), 1≤k≤d. Since a^k∈A, each p_k belongs to the enveloping image. Thus a^⊗d belongs to that image, proving the first equality without a density, distribution or graded-Schur black box. The same argument shows that an operator commutes with U(g^e) iff it commutes with the diagonal tensor action of the invertible elements G_e=A^×; the reverse direction follows by differentiating (1+t a)^⊗d, and the forward direction by Newton identities.

If e has at least d distinct strings of length l, take the tensor of their top vectors. The l^d d! vectors obtained by individual powers 0≤r_i<l and all place permutations are distinct members of the Jordan tensor basis (string labels recover the permutation, and positions recover the powers). They give a copy of the regular right B-module, so the right action is injective. For d=0 both identities and injectivity are scalar identities. This stable argument is not used as a replacement for the full converse below.


## Authenticated accepted local Section 5
 Extension and polarized tensor covariants

Let X be this normal orbit closure and O its dense orbit. Regular functions on O extend to X. Here is the needed Hartogs argument without an imported intersection theorem. For f∈Frac A regular off codimension≥2, put J={a∈A:af∈A}. At every height-one prime J is the unit ideal. If J were proper choose0≠a∈J. In the normal S2 domain every associated prime of A/(a) has height1, by localization, the actual depth-zero criterion and the regular-element depth shift. Finite prime avoidance supplies b∈J avoiding those primes. Since b(af)=a(bf), regularity of b modulo a implies af∈aA. Cancellation gives f∈A, contradiction. Apply coordinatewise to maps into a finite-dimensional vector representation.

For B∈End(V^⊗d) fixed by G_e, define on O the equivariant map g e g^{-1}↦g^⊗d B(g^{-1})^⊗d. It is well-defined. It is regular: on rank-minor charts of the constant-rank powers choose bases of kerX^k, their image/quotient complements and hence homogeneous Jordan top vectors by Gaussian elimination. The resulting Jordan-basis conjugator g(X) and its inverse are regular where the finitely many chosen minors/determinant do not vanish. These charts coverO and the expressions agree by G_e-invariance. Hartogs extends the map equivariantly to X (equality on the dense orbit gives equality everywhere). Finite-degree GL complete reducibility then lifts it to an equivariant polynomial map Phi:End(V)→End(V^⊗d).

To determine Phi, polarize its degree-k matrix input and pair its output with d independent matrices Y_1,...,Y_d by the nondegenerate trace form. This is a multilinear invariant on End(V)^{k+d}; the actual Schur–Weyl theorem identifies all such invariants with permutation contractions. Each permutation gives products of traces along its cycles, by expansion of matrix indices. Depolarizing the k identical X variables leaves cycles consisting only of X (scalar invariant coefficients) and cycles through the Y's, of the form Tr(X^{a_1}Y_{i_1}...X^{a_r}Y_{i_r}). These latter cycles are exactly the trace pairing of Y_1⊗...⊗Y_d with a place permutation followed by X^{h_1}⊗...⊗X^{h_d}. Nondegeneracy of trace therefore proves that Phi is a sum of these operator covariants with scalar invariant polynomial coefficients. Evaluating at e gives only permutation×individual e powers; powers≥l vanish. This proves the FULL converse centralizer identity for every nilpotent type and every d, independently of stable faithfulness.
