# Batch17 ROUND1 — typed coset sorting by localization and Levi induction

Assume AC. This is local evidence for the same first repair round, using the actual13 all-weight Duflo/Hom interface and the HC projective-presentation reconstruction in the companion note. It does not import an inverse-twisting theorem, BG5.12 or a nonintegral KL theorem. Exact finite-W presentation/simple-image and projective-cover consumers still require their separate proof handoff.

## 1. Exact adjacent swap of two different cosets

Let alpha be an adjacent simple root whose two shifted coordinates lie in different C/Z cosets. Every H_alpha-weight in the entire relevant weight-coset category is nonintegral. Write E,F,H for its sl2 triple. F is injective: if Fv=0, choose r maximal with E^r v≠0. The identity F E^(r+1)v=−(r+1)(h+r)E^r v, for Hv=hv, contradicts nonintegrality. This argument applies to every object and every subquotient.

Powers of F admit Ore localization of U(g). For each u, ad F is locally nilpotent; the finite binomial commutation formula moves any sufficiently large F power past u and proves both Ore conditions. PBW makes U(g) a domain, so denominators do not annihilate nonzero elements. U_F is the union of the right-free modules F^−k U, hence is right flat: tensor with it is the filtered union of these exact free-module tensor functors. Since M→M_F is injective on the category above, M↦M_F/M is exact by the diagram of two short exact sequences. Twist this quotient by phi=exp(ad E)exp(ad(−F))exp(ad E), which sends E→−F, F→−E and H→−H. These adjoint exponentials are finite on each enveloping element.

For Delta_eta with highest vector v, F^−1 v modulo Delta_eta is a highest vector after this twist, of weight s dot eta. The new E kills it since the old F sends it to v. Each other new positive root is an old positive root different from alpha; commuting it through F^−1 gives only positive-root terms, which kill v. The new F sends F^−r v to r(eta(H)+r+1)F^−(r+1)v, with nonzero coefficient. PBW orders F before the other negative roots; the reflection permutes those other positive roots. Thus these strings and reflected negative-root monomials generate the entire quotient. Its character is

s(e^eta Σ_(r≥1)e^(r alpha) Π_(beta>0,beta≠alpha)(1−e^−beta)^−1)
=ch Delta_(s dot eta).

The resulting surjection from Delta_(s dot eta) is therefore an isomorphism, weight by weight. This proves the actual standard-label transport.

Localization commutes with a finite tensor X. On M_F⊗X, the total F=F_M+F_X has inverse F_M^−1 Σ_(k≥0)(−F_M^−1 F_X)^k; the sum is finite because F_X is nilpotent. This proves (M⊗X)_F=M_F⊗X by the localization universal property, and it identifies the embedded M⊗X before passing to the quotient. The finite Weyl matrix exp(E_X)exp(−F_X)exp(E_X) identifies phi(X) with X, naturally and compatibly with tensor products. Hence the swap functor commutes with finite tensoring.

To prove equivalence rather than merely exactness, choose regular coset-dominant lambda. Its swapped weight is coset-dominant in the swapped pattern because the swap crosses different cosets and preserves each coset's internal order. The functor sends every tensor projective X⊗Delta_lambda to X⊗Delta_(s dot lambda). Maps between such generators are exactly the actual13 HC matrices (X*⊗Y⊗U_chi)^g. Under localization they extend uniquely; under the twist and the finite Weyl matrices they undergo simultaneous Weyl conjugation. This fixes every invariant matrix, since its g-action is zero and exponentiating that action is the identity. Thus maps between the projective generators are identified with the common HC Hom matrices. The projective-presentation criterion in the companion note proves equivalence; no assertion that the square of the twist is abstractly the identity is needed.

Apply adjacent different-coset swaps to stably sort the indices by coset. Internal orders are preserved throughout. Exactness and standard-label transport imply corresponding simple heads and projective covers are transported as well.

## 2. Parabolic restriction is made only after the final sorting

Each original column Levi block lies entirely in one coset. Stable sorting therefore keeps each such consecutive block consecutive and preserves its internal order. At the final permutation, every original Levi simple root is again a positive simple root. A highest-weight simple belongs to parabolic O precisely when its highest weight has nonnegative integral pairings on these Levi simple roots: necessity is the finite sl2 string condition, and sufficiency is the finite-dimensional highest-weight Levi module induced into g and then quotiented. The sorting equivalence transports exactly these simple labels.

Parabolic locally finite objects form the Serre subcategory generated by these simples. Subquotients preserve local finiteness. For extensions, the negative Levi root operators are locally nilpotent on each end term, hence on the middle term; the Cartan is semisimple and the ambient O positive operators already act locally finitely. The finite-Levi highest-weight/PBW argument then makes every vector generate a finite-dimensional Levi module; the positive parabolic radical has bounded finite-degree action as in the actual15 construction. Thus extensions remain parabolic locally finite. Restricting the exact sorting equivalence to these Serre subcategories is legitimate; no assertion is made that each intermediate twist preserves the intermediate Levi-finite category.

The generalized Verma is the largest parabolic-finite quotient of its ordinary Verma: every Levi lowering generator must satisfy F_j^(eta(H_j)+1)v=0, and the finite-Levi Weyl/highest-weight presentation gives exactly the induced Levi simple module quotient. Consequently the restricted equivalence carries the corresponding generalized standards, their simple heads and projective covers. This closes the typed label boundary left open by a merely Z-linear HC equivalence.

## 3. Separated contiguous cosets give the actual product model

After sorting, let q have Levi l=⊕_k gl_(N_k), one contiguous block for each coset. Fix a linkage block with highest labels the integral-reflection orbit, which is a product of permutations inside these blocks. Every highest label has the same blockwise centre weights z_k. All weights of objects have each initial block-centre prefix sum at most its highest-label value; an outside negative root strictly lowers some prefix sum. Here the comparison means that bound minus weight is a nonnegative integer; it is not an order on C.

For an object M, q-radical invariants equal its top block-centre weight section. The top section is annihilated by the positive outside radical, because that would raise a prefix beyond its bound. Conversely, a nonzero invariant vector in a lower section lies in a Levi O module; applying positive Levi roots finitely often produces a Levi highest vector. It remains radical-invariant and is therefore a full g highest vector. Its generated subquotient has a simple highest label with a strictly lower prefix sum, contradicting the actual strong-linkage restriction to the block's integral-reflection orbit. Hence no lower invariant section exists. The top section is exact because the block centres act semisimply; this proves exactness of the relevant parabolic restriction rather than assuming arbitrary radical invariants are exact.

Let R=U(g)⊗_(U(q))−, with radical acting zero. PBW makes it exact, and its top section is the original Levi module. Its simple head has the expected highest label. Any additional simple composition factor would have nonzero top section, but that would contradict the top section of R(L_l)=L_l; hence R(L_l) is simple. More generally R and the exact top-section functor are inverse: the unit is PBW's top-section identity; the counit's kernel and cokernel have zero top section, while that section detects every nonzero finite-length object through its simple factors. They therefore vanish. This proves the product-block equivalence and transports ordinary and generalized standards with their labels.

For a coset kappa with column heights q_j, put n_kappa=max q_j and shift every source entry by a↦a−c_kappa+n_global−n_kappa. The integral charges are m_j=q_j−n_kappa, nonincreasing in the retained column order. Taking inf I_kappa=1−n_kappa gives actual16's column size m_j+1−inf I_kappa=q_j. Thus the genuine integral models from actual16 are consumed with their exact hypotheses. The original complex origin is retained, no order on C is used, and original parabolic structural scope is not replaced by a single integral block. Arbitrary Hecke root multisets are realized separately by taking the integer maximum in each coset and q_j=n+a_j−c_kappa sufficiently large; that construction concerns the original17 admissible charged-pyramid category, not a silent extension of the actual16 Definition.
