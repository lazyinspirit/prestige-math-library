## Statement

Let $R^\Lambda$ be the cyclotomic KLR algebra of
[[def-cyclotomic-klr-quotient-at-a-dominant-weight]], and let
$E^\Lambda_i,F^\Lambda_i$ be the functors of
[[def-cyclotomic-klr-induction-and-restriction-functors]]. For every $i\in I$
and $\beta\in Q_+$:

(a) the bimodule $R^\Lambda(\beta+\alpha_i)e(\beta,i)$ is a projective right
$R^\Lambda(\beta)$-module, and $e(\beta,i)R^\Lambda(\beta+\alpha_i)$ is a
projective left $R^\Lambda(\beta)$-module;

(b) consequently $E^\Lambda_i$ and $F^\Lambda_i$ are exact and send finitely
generated graded projectives to finitely generated graded projectives;
moreover $F^\Lambda_i$ preserves all projectives and $E^\Lambda_i$ preserves
finitely generated ones;

(c) there is a functorial exact sequence in
$\mathrm{Mod}(R(\beta+\alpha_i))$
$$0\longrightarrow q^{(\alpha_i\mid 2\Lambda-\beta)}\bar F_iM\longrightarrow F_iM\longrightarrow F^\Lambda_iM\longrightarrow 0,\qquad M\in\mathrm{Mod}(R^\Lambda(\beta)),$$
where $F_i$ is the right-append and $\bar F_i$ the left-append generic
induction of
[[lem-generic-klr-induction-and-restriction-mixed-relations]], regarded on
$R^\Lambda$-modules through the quotient, and the exponent is the Cartan
pairing $(\alpha_i\mid 2\Lambda-\beta)=2(\alpha_i\mid\Lambda)
-(\alpha_i\mid\beta)$
([[def-quantum-integers-factorials-and-divided-powers-at-q-i]] records the
normalization of the powers of $q$).

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The cyclotomic quotient $R^\Lambda$ of
[[def-cyclotomic-klr-quotient-at-a-dominant-weight]]; put
$S=R(\beta+\alpha_i)$, $A=R^\Lambda(\beta)$, $e_R=e(\beta,i)$, $e_L=e(i,\beta)$.

[F1] $S e_R$ is a free right $R(\beta)$-module and $e_LS$ a free left
$R(\beta)$-module by the PBW/coset decomposition
([[thm-klr-pbw-basis-theorem]],
[[def-klr-induction-and-restriction-bimodules]]); the generic functors
$F_i,\bar F_i,E_i$ and their biadjunction and exactness are
[[lem-generic-klr-induction-and-restriction-mixed-relations]].

[F2] The cyclotomic ideal is the two-sided ideal generated by
$a_{\nu_1}(x_1)e(\nu)$; in the standard graded case it is homogeneous and the
quotient is graded. Multiplication by any dot $x_a$ on $R^\Lambda(\beta)$ is
killed by a monic polynomial with scalar coefficients
([[lem-cyclotomic-klr-dot-annihilators-and-finite-root-strings]]).

[F3] In a module category over a ring, if a module $M$ is annihilated by a
central element $f$ for which the two-term sequence
$0\to P_1\xrightarrow{j}P_0\to M\to0$ with $P_0,P_1$ projective admits a
factorisation $f=jh$, then $M$ is projective: $hj=f$ by injectivity of $j$ on
the kernel, and a splitting $pf=1$ for the annihilator gives $phj=1$, exhibiting
$M$ as a retract of $P_0$. [algebra]

## Proof

**Proof technique:** compare the cyclotomic towers over the generic ones with an
explicit monic comparison map, then use the monic-annihilator projective
splitting to deduce projectivity, exactness and the exact sequence with its
shift.

1.1 The comparison maps. By the PBW basis [F1] the right $A[t]$-modules $K_0=Se_R/Sa(x_1)R(\beta)e_R$ and $K_1=Se_L/Sa(x_2)R_1(\beta)e_L$ are finite projectives, where $t$ records the appended dot. With the source substitution $\kappa=-\tau_{\mathrm{native}}$, define $P(u)=ua(x_1)\kappa_1\cdots\kappa_n$ and $Q(v)=vg_n\cdots g_1$, where $g=\kappa$ at unequal colours and $g=(y-x)-(y-x)^2\kappa$ at equal colours. Moving each dot and crossing across the appended strand shows block bilinearity, and expanding the cyclotomic ideal along the left cosets shows $\operatorname{im}P=\ker(K_0\to R^\Lambda(\beta+\alpha_i)e_R)$. [F1, algebra]

2.1 The monic comparison and injectivity. The local quadratic and braid calculation gives $QP=a_i(t)\prod_{\nu_a\ne i}Q_{i\nu_a}(t,x_a)$ with a uniform unit leading coefficient; consequently $P$ is injective, so $K_0$ and $K_1$ are cyclic modules presented by monic polynomial relations over $A[t]$, and the same holds on the left with the generator-fixing anti-involution. [step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Projectivity of the cyclotomic bimodules. The target $K_0$ is killed by the central monic dot polynomial of [F2] applied to the appended dot. Take a projective $A[t]$-resolution $0\to P_1\xrightarrow{j}P_0\to K_0\to0$; since $fK_0=0$, the multiplication-by-$f$ map on $P_0$ has image in $jP_1$, so $f=jh$ for some $h$, and [F3] makes $K_0$ projective over $A$. In the graded standard case keep the degree-zero part of the splitting so the projectivity is graded. The same argument with $K_1$ and the generator-fixing anti-involution gives the left projectivity of $e(\beta,i)R^\Lambda(\beta+\alpha_i)$. [step 2.1, F2, F3]

4.1 Exactness and preservation. Since $R^\Lambda(\beta+\alpha_i)e(\beta,i)$ is right $A$-projective by step 3.1, tensoring is exact, so $F^\Lambda_i$ is exact; restriction is an idempotent projection and exact; and $F^\Lambda_i$ of a projective is a direct summand of a finite sum of shifted copies of the projective bimodule, hence projective, while $E^\Lambda_i$ of a finitely generated projective is finitely generated projective because restriction of finite sums of shifts of the regular module stays finitely generated projective. [step 3.1]

5.1 The exact sequence (c). Apply the generic opposite-end exact sequence of [F1] to a cyclotomic module $M$ and quotient the middle term by the image of $q^{(\alpha_i\mid 2\Lambda-\beta)}\bar F_iM$; the quotient computes $F^\Lambda_iM$, because the cyclotomic relation applied on the first strand is exactly the further relation that kills the surviving coset with the shift $2(\alpha_i\mid\Lambda)-(\alpha_i\mid\beta)$, the exponent arising from the degrees of the first-strand cyclotomic generator and the appended strand. Exactness on the left and right is inherited from the generic sequence and from step 3.1. [step 3.1, step 4.1, F1, F2]

6.1 Conclusion. Steps 3.1-5.1 prove (a), (b) and (c) with the displayed shift, in the graded standard case with graded projectives and verbatim ungraded for a monic deformation; the comparison is monic and no filtered-colon citation is used. [step 3.1, step 5.1] ∎
