## Example

For $I=\{0\}$ and $\Lambda=2\Lambda_0$, the degree-one supported block
$B=\mathbb C[x]/x^2$ ([[def-degenerate-cyclotomic-hecke-tower-and-residue-functors]])
has simple $D_{01}=\mathbb C$ with $x\mapsto0$ and projective cover $Y_{01}=B$.
Therefore $[Y_{01}]=2[D_{01}]$ in $G_0$, whereas
$\dim\operatorname{Hom}(Y_{01},D_{01})=1$. At parameter one the categorification
images of [[thm-degenerate-ariki-categorification]] and
[[thm-the-degenerate-cyclotomic-hecke-projective-k-zero-is-the-highest-weight-module]]
are

$$P_{00}=D_{00}=M_{00},\qquad P_{01}=M_{01}+M_{10},\qquad D_{01}=\tfrac12(M_{01}+M_{10}),\qquad P_{11}=D_{11}=M_{11}.$$

The simple image is the PROJECTED vector $\pi L_{01}$, not the unprojected
$L_{01}=M_{01}$. With the normalized highest pairing $C_0$ one has
$C_0(P_A,D_B)=\delta_{AB}$ across these three weights. In the degree-two block
$M_2(\mathbb C)$, the simple $D_{11}$ is two-dimensional and is itself its
projective cover; the abstract Fock weight is one-dimensional, not the
underlying module. Thus the composition Cartan values are $1,2,1$, while the
projective/simple Hom pairing is the identity. Projective covers are self-dual
here by the actual symmetric block forms, not merely because their heads are
self-dual.

In the costandard basis $(D_{00},D_{01},D_{11})$,

$$fD_{00}=2D_{01},\quad fD_{01}=D_{11},\quad fD_{11}=0;\qquad eD_{00}=0,\quad eD_{01}=D_{00},\quad eD_{11}=2D_{01}.$$

The divided powers give $f^{(2)}D_{00}=D_{11}$ and $e^{(2)}D_{11}=D_{00}$, with
all other second divided moves $0$. Hence the integral ground-generated lattice
is EXACTLY $\mathbb ZD_{00}\oplus2\mathbb ZD_{01}\oplus\mathbb ZD_{11}$, of index
$2$ in the full costandard lattice
$\mathbb ZD_{00}\oplus\mathbb ZD_{01}\oplus\mathbb ZD_{11}$. The standard lattice
has basis $(P_{00},P_{01},P_{11})$, is integrally divided-power generated by its
ground, and pairs perfectly with the full costandard lattice. Costandard ground
cyclicity holds over $\mathbb Q$, not over $\mathbb Z$; crystal and
composition-factor reachability do not imply integral vector generation.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The data $I=\{0\}$, $\Lambda=2\Lambda_0$ with charge $(0,0)$, the
degree-one and degree-two supported blocks, and the categorification
identifications of [[thm-the-degenerate-cyclotomic-hecke-g-zero-is-the-dual-highest-weight-module]]
and [[thm-the-degenerate-cyclotomic-hecke-projective-k-zero-is-the-highest-weight-module]]
with the Fock dictionary of [[def-dual-canonical-canonical-and-quasi-canonical-fock-bases]].
We verify every displayed pairing, self-duality, operator and lattice statement.

[F1] The classes of the covers $Y(A)$ and of the simples $D(A)$ are dual bases
of $K_0(\mathrm{Proj})\times G_0(\mathrm{Rep})$ under the Cartan Hom pairing, and
the identification with the standard and costandard lattices is realized at
parameter one by $[Y(A)]\mapsto P_A$ and $[D(A)]\mapsto D_A=\pi L_A$
([[thm-the-degenerate-cyclotomic-hecke-projective-k-zero-is-the-highest-weight-module]]).

[F2] The Cartan pairing descends to the two Grothendieck groups and pairs the
cover and simple bases dually
([[thm-split-simple-projective-hom-pairing-has-dual-bases]],
[[def-projective-simple-hom-pairing-on-grothendieck-groups]]).

## Verification

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 **The degree-one block.** For $I=\{0\}$, level $2$ and charge $(0,0)$ the degree-one cyclotomic quotient is $H^{(0,0)}_1=\mathbb C[x_1]/(x_1^2)$ by the level-two instance of [[def-degenerate-cyclotomic-hecke-algebra]], and it is already supported, so $B=\mathbb C[x]/x^2$ with $x=x_1$. Its radical is $(x)$ and its quotient is $\mathbb C$, so $B$ is local; the unique simple is $D_{01}=\mathbb C$ with $x\mapsto0$, the regular module $Y_{01}=B$ is its projective cover, and the regular composition series $0\subset(x)\subset B$ has successive quotients $D_{01},D_{01}$, giving $[Y_{01}]=2[D_{01}]$ in $G_0$. Every $B$-linear map $B\to D_{01}$ is determined by the image of $1$, so $\dim\operatorname{Hom}(Y_{01},D_{01})=1$. [given, algebra]

2.1 **The symmetric form on $B$ and self-duality.** The coefficient functional $\tau(a+bx)=b$ is a symmetrizing trace on $B$: it is linear, $\tau(uv)=\tau(vu)$ for all $u,v$ (both sides pick out the $x$-coefficient of the product), and its radical is zero because $\tau(x)\ne0$, so the associated form $\langle u,v\rangle=\tau(uv)$ has matrix $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ in the basis $(1,x)$, which is nonsingular. The anti-involution $\ast$ of [[def-degenerate-cyclotomic-hecke-algebra]] that fixes $x$ is the identity of $B$ here, so the form is generator-fixing, and it exhibits an isomorphism $Y_{01}\cong Y_{01}^{\circledast}$: the dual of the regular module with respect to the trace. This is the actual symmetric-algebra proof of cover self-duality, not an inference from self-duality of the head. [step 1.1, given, algebra]

2.2 **The degree-two block and its simple.** With $x=x_1$, $y=x_2$, $u=s_1$ the relations give $u^2=1$, $[x,y]=0$, $ux=yu-1$, $uy=xu+1$, $x^2=0$, and from $ux^2=0$ one gets $y^2=(x+y)u$, so the eight words $1,x,y,xy,u,xu,yu,xyu$ span the degree-two algebra; the models $(N,-N,-J)$ on $M_2(\mathbb C)$ with $N=E_{12}$ and $J$ the swap matrix, $(\epsilon,1+\epsilon,1)$ and $(\epsilon,-1+\epsilon,-1)$ on $\mathbb C[\epsilon]/\epsilon^2$ satisfy all relations, and the central Hermite projectors of the element $z=x+y$ separate the three factors, which proves that the span of the eight words is isomorphic to $M_2(\mathbb C)\oplus B_+\oplus B_-$ with $B_\pm=\mathbb C[\epsilon]/\epsilon^2$. The supported content cut at $2\alpha_0$ keeps the factor $M_2(\mathbb C)$; its simple module $\mathbb C^2$ has Gram matrix $J$, since $N^{t}J=JN$ and $(-J)^{t}J=J(-J)$, and it is self-dual and projective, so its projective cover is itself and its endomorphism ring is $\mathbb C$; degree $0$ contributes $\mathbb C$, and Hom-spaces between distinct content blocks vanish because they are different blocks. In particular the one-dimensional Fock weight vector $M_{11}$ is not the two-dimensional module $D_{11}$, and the composition length of the degree-two projective cover is $2$, not $1$. [step 1.1, given, algebra]

3.1 **The projected dual bases at parameter one.** By [F1] the categorification images are the projected vectors; on the mixed weight the normalized highest vector is $P_{01}=M_{01}+M_{10}$ of $C_0$-norm $2$, so the correct field dual is $D_{01}=\tfrac12(M_{01}+M_{10})$, and the endpoint vectors satisfy $P_{00}=D_{00}=M_{00}$ and $P_{11}=D_{11}=M_{11}$. The Gram matrix in the $D$-basis is $\operatorname{diag}(1,\tfrac12,1)$ and the change of basis $\operatorname{diag}(1,2,1)$ converts it into the identity, which is the pairing $C_0(P_A,D_B)=\delta_{AB}$; this is the parameter-one projected-simple dictionary of [[def-dual-canonical-canonical-and-quasi-canonical-fock-bases]] and [[thm-simples-projectives-and-tiltings-map-to-the-three-distinguished-fock-bases]], not an identification of $L_{01}=M_{01}$ with the simple. [step 2.2, F1, given, algebra]

4.1 **The categorified residue operators.** Induction from degree $0$ to the degree-one block gives the regular module of $B$; the supported induction from $B$ to the degree-two block $M_2(\mathbb C)$ sends the simple $\mathbb C$ to $\mathbb C^2$ and the regular module $B$ to $M_2(\mathbb C)$, which is two copies of the simple $\mathbb C^2$; restriction of $\mathbb C^2$ to $B$ is the regular Jordan module $B$ itself (the generator $x$ of $B$ acts on $\mathbb C^2$ as $N$ with $N^2=0$ and $\dim\ker N=1$), and restriction of $D_{01}=\mathbb C$ is the simple $B$-module. Using the exact residue functors of [[def-degenerate-cyclotomic-hecke-tower-and-residue-functors]] and the categorification identification of [F1], this yields the displayed columns $fD_{00}=2D_{01}$, $fD_{01}=D_{11}$, $fD_{11}=0$ and $eD_{00}=0$, $eD_{01}=D_{00}$, $eD_{11}=2D_{01}$; equivalently, in the $D$-basis, $f$ has columns $(0,2,0)^{\mathsf T},(0,0,1)^{\mathsf T},0$ and $e$ has columns $0,(1,0,0)^{\mathsf T},(0,2,0)^{\mathsf T}$, so the commutator is $\operatorname{diag}(2,0,-2)$ in the $sl_2$-normalization of [[thm-quantized-sl-two-string-formulas]]. [step 2.2, step 3.1, F1, given, algebra]

5.1 **The index-two lattice and its cyclicity.** The divided powers $f^{(2)}=f^2/2$ and $e^{(2)}=e^2/2$ give $f^{(2)}D_{00}=D_{11}$ and $e^{(2)}D_{11}=D_{00}$, and the displayed operators multiply $D_{01}$-coordinates by even integers, so the lattice $L_{\mathbb Z}=\mathbb ZD_{00}\oplus2\mathbb ZD_{01}\oplus\mathbb ZD_{11}$ is stable under $e,f$, their divided powers and the toral operators at weights $2,0,-2$. It contains the ground vector $D_{00}$, and its elements have $D_{01}$-coordinate even, so it does not contain $D_{01}$; hence it is a proper integral submodule containing the ground, of index $2$ in the full costandard lattice, and $D_{01}$ is not in the integral ground-generated span. Over $\mathbb Q$ division by $2$ is available, so the ground vector generates the whole three-dimensional costandard space; the standard lattice is by [F1] the lattice with basis $(P_{00},P_{01},P_{11})$, which is integrally generated by its ground under the divided powers and pairs perfectly with the full costandard lattice by step 3.1. This proves that integral costandard generation fails while the full integral duality and the standard divided-power statement survive. [step 3.1, step 4.1, F1, F2, given, algebra]

6.1 **Conclusion.** Steps 1.1 and 2.1 verify the degree-one cover, its composition length against its Hom dimension, and the symmetric-form self-duality; step 2.2 and step 3.1 verify the degree-two block, the projected dual bases and the pairing identity; step 4.1 verifies the residue operators, their commutator and the categorified $[Y_{01}]=2[D_{01}]$; step 5.1 verifies the exact index-two lattice, its stability, its failure to contain $D_{01}$ and the rational cyclicity. No step identifies the simple image with the unprojected monomial $L_{01}$, and no self-duality is inferred from the head alone. [step 2.1, step 3.1, step 4.1, step 5.1, given] ∎
