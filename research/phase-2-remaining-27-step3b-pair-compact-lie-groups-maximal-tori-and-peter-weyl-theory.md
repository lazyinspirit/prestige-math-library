## Controlling conventions

- Haar measure is the normalized (probability) regular Borel measure of
  `cor-normalized-haar-measure-on-a-compact-lie-group`; every proof-bearing item
  that uses it states "Assume the Axiom of Choice" and declares
  `def-axiom-of-choice`, with the exact use identified in the closing step (for
  most items through the Haar/metric/covering theory). The two choice-free
  items are `fs-every-element-of-a-disconnected-compact-lie-group-...` (ZF) and
  the B-page `cex-a-disconnected-compact-group-element-outside-every-identity-component-torus`
  (ZF), matching the manifest axiom bases.
- Matrix coefficients use `pi_ij(g) = <pi(g)e_j, e_i>`; Schur orthogonality is
  stated and proved in that convention (`∫ pi_ij conj(sigma_kl) = delta_ik
  delta_jl / d_pi`), checked against the independent `SU(2)` computation
  `∫ |a|^2 = 1/2` and used consistently by Peter–Weyl and the Fourier example.
- `S^1 = R/Z`; roots are the honest characters of `T`, identified with their
  differentials; `Q ⊆ X*(T) ⊆ P` is kept at the *actual* character lattice.
- The Weyl Jacobian is `J(t) = ∏_{alpha>0} |1 - alpha(t)^{-1}|^2`; the
  independence of the positive system is proved by the pair-invariance
  `|1 - alpha^{-1}| = |1 - alpha|` rather than imported.
- The character chain follows the owner-mandated local order (denominator and
  anti-invariant basis → Weyl integration/orthogonality numerator
  identification → theorem), with descent through
  `Z(G)^0 × G_der^sc → G`, lift independence, and continuous extension from the
  regular set; neither `rho` nor any half-root is asserted to lie in `X*(T)`.
