## Published proof finding for the canonical ledger

`thm-indecomposable-projective-kg-modules-correspond-to-simple-kg-modules` is **published**. Its proof step 2.1 at `items/thm-indecomposable-projective-kg-modules-correspond-to-simple-kg-modules.md:51` says that the direct sum of projective covers of simple head summands is again a projective cover, without establishing superfluity of the direct-sum kernel. The theorem is true; this is a missing proof step, not evidence for changing its statement. Its repair can use the **published** `thm-module-radical-is-jacobson-radical-times-the-module` and `lem-radical-of-a-finite-length-module-is-superfluous`: each cover kernel lies in every maximal submodule and hence in `rad(P_i)`; the direct-sum kernel lies in `rad(⊕P_i)=J(A)(⊕P_i)=⊕rad(P_i)`, which is superfluous. The currently **draft-scaffold, ready** suppliers `thm-finite-dimensional-algebra-projective-classes-form-a-split-k-zero-basis` and `thm-split-simple-projective-hom-pairing-has-dual-bases` independently establish the needed cover/simple correspondence in their own strategies, so this published proof gap is not a load-bearing prerequisite for batch 21.
