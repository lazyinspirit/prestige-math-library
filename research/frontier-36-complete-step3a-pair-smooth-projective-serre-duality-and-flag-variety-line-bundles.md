# Step 3a scope review: smooth-projective Serre duality and flag-variety line bundles

**Decision: sufficient.** The current A and B scopes cover the intended role of this pair as the algebraic-geometric supplier for the planned Borel–Weil–Bott page.

The plan identifies the need as higher-dimensional smooth-projective Serre duality together with the algebraic flag quotient, homogeneous line bundles, minimal-parabolic projective-line fibrations, and their relative cohomology shift. Its 17 binding A targets are all present in batch 16: duality on projective-space twists and coherent sheaves, duality for locally free sheaves on smooth projective varieties, the semisimple group/Borel setup and projective flag variety, Bruhat cells, Borel-character bundles and their classification, the canonical weight \(-2\rho\), minimal-parabolic fibres and signed coroot degrees, the relative canonical weight, and the relative \(\mathbf P^1\) cohomology shift. The manifest has 38 A items; the other 21 provide the locally scoped Ext, immersion, root-subgroup, rank-one, rational-representation, orbit-quotient, and chart arguments required by the owner’s 2026-09-27 direction. This expansion stays below the stated 100-item ceiling and does not replace general semisimple claims with special-linear cases.

The B page has the three planned examples: \(\mathrm{SL}_2\) and the line-bundle sign convention, the two \(\mathrm{SL}_3\) minimal-parabolic projections and their degree pairs, and the explicit Laurent-monomial pairing on \(\mathbf P^2\). Together they give concrete checks of both strands of the A page.

The scope boundary is coherent. Borel–Weil global sections and the Borel–Weil–Bott theorem are deferred to their planned consumer; general Cohen–Macaulay duality and Schubert intersection/singularity theory are explicitly out of scope. The general smooth-projective theorem is stated for finite locally free sheaves, while coherent-sheaf duality is included on projective space. That is the duality scope required for the planned line-bundle wall-crossing use; the design does not promise arbitrary coherent-sheaf duality on every smooth projective variety.

The page requires 13 prerequisite pages. The three in-run scheme/cohomology suppliers are represented in batches 7–9: coherent modules and finite locally free sheaves; Proj, twists, projective embeddings and ampleness; and affine/projective quasi-coherent cohomology, projective-space twist cohomology, finiteness, and cohomology/base-change tools. The batch-16 dependency record marks these three page edges open pending supplier authoring and review. They are live proof/dependency obligations, not missing topics from this pair’s declared scope. No item or supplier proof is approved by this scope decision.
