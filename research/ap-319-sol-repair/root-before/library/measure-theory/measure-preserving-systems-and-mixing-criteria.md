Measure preservation is defined by inverse images and then characterized by invariant integrals. Pullback gives an isometry on real and complex Lp spaces. Strict and modulo-null invariance lead to equivalent probability-space criteria for ergodicity. Strong mixing implies weak mixing, which implies ergodicity; generating-family approximation and complex L2 correlations give two ways to check mixing. Completeness of the measure space is unnecessary. The completion clause alone states its countable-choice assumption.
