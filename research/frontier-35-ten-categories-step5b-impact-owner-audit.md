## What the receipt measures

`tools/impact-audit.mjs` compares the latest snapshots bearing the named labels. Its surface hash includes frontmatter (apart from verification) and body sections other than Scratch, Proof, Refutation, Counterexample, and Verification. Thus a dependency-list, title, Fact, or Remark change counts even when the Statement/Definition claim is unchanged. For each changed surface the tool walks the **full transitive reverse `deps` graph**, adds direct `deps`, `justified_by`, `forward_refs`, `external_refs`, and wikilink citations, then deduplicates consumers into one receipt row per item. The gate checks the exact changed and required sets, reviewer, one `still-licensed` / `repaired` / `not-load-bearing` status and a nonempty note per row. It does not verify the mathematics in those notes.

The frozen snapshots contain 20,129 and 20,806 items. All 677 additions are counted as changed interfaces; another 57 pre-existing items changed, making 734 sources. The required set has 5,877 items; all 5,877 receipt rows remain `pending`. In the current dependency graph, 5,852 are in a transitive `deps` closure and 25 enter only by direct citation. Of the 5,852, exactly 770 are one dependency hop from at least one changed source; the other 5,082 are two to 18 hops away. Those 770 direct items have 2,247 changed-supplier dependency pairs. The large count therefore does not mean 5,877 independently changed mathematical statements.

## Efficient, honest closure method

1. Freeze the two snapshot labels as the audit window. For each of the 734 changed sources, identify the exact effective public clause: Statement/Definition, hypotheses and conventions in Facts, and any load-bearing Remark or logical metadata. Keep the 677 new sources distinct from edits to the 57 pre-existing sources. Prioritize substantive restrictions or convention changes, then the high-fan-out existing sources. Use hash-matched old text when asserting an unchanged clause.
2. Review **each direct changed-supplier/consumer use** against the current supplier and consumer. Record the consumer location, precise invoked result or definition, relevant hypotheses (including AC and boundary cases), and whether the source change affects it. Check every changed supplier for a consumer with several such dependencies. Consult an authoritative source when the mathematics is unfamiliar. Route a genuinely broken published item to the canonical ledger and the authorized repair owner; leave it pending until repaired and recertified.
3. Propagate a material change one hop at a time. If a checked direct consumer's own exported Statement/Definition and load-bearing assumptions remain valid and unchanged, its descendants continue to consume that stable claim; no separate proof re-review of each distant descendant is mathematically induced by that path. If a necessary repair changes that consumer's outgoing claim or assumptions, review its direct consumers next. Treat a consumer that is itself one of the 734 changed sources as its own source event. The Step-7 protocol states an explicit Statement/Definition stopping rule; applying a narrower computed scope to this Step-5b gate would require a deliberate tool/workflow change, because today's `impact-audit.mjs` still requires all 5,877 rows.
4. Under the existing gate, keep all 5,877 item rows. For a distant row, an individualized note can identify the changed source(s), a checked path or cut node, the exact unchanged clause that the distant item actually consumes, and the hash-bound direct-use evidence that stops propagation. A graph-generated path is a locator, never mathematical approval. If multiple independent changed paths reach an item, cover each material path or a proved cut set; do not select one convenient path and ignore the others. The 25 citation-only rows need their citation channel and whether the cited clause is load-bearing.
5. Fill a row only after the underlying source/use evidence is complete. A useful review record is `{consumer_id, consumer_sha256, supplier_id, supplier_sha256, source_window, changed_clause_before_after_or_new, consumer_location, consumed_clause, hypothesis_check, path_or_direct_channel, evidence_paths, disposition, reason, reviewer}`. `still-licensed` means the actual use satisfies the current clause; `not-load-bearing` means the changed clause is not used, with the exact citation or path explained; `repaired` needs the closed defect and current carrier evidence. The receipt's `notes` field should carry the essential source, use, and reason plus a durable evidence pointer. “All dependencies remain valid” or a repeated generic sentence is not evidence.
6. Finish authoring and repairs first, then refresh invalidated contracts, manifests, decisions, impact evidence, and certifications on stable carriers together. A proof-only or metadata repair may alter the current surface/receipt calculation, so rederive both impact windows before the Step-5b gate. Do not recertify intermediate carrier versions. The separate six unresolved forward-reference verdicts presently hold `5b-cross`; this note does not resolve them or authorize a controller retry.

This method reduces the **mathematical reading** to actual changed uses and necessary propagation. It does not silently reduce the engine's current 5,877-row receipt scope; that requires a reviewed mechanism change or evidence-bearing completion of every required row.
