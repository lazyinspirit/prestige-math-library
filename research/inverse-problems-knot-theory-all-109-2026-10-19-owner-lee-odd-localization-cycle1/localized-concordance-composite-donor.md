# Conditional algebraic donor: localized BN concordance composite

This is an off-canonical argument, not a claimed completion of the missing integral based movie maps. Its explicit input is a finite based oriented movie of a connected oriented concordance, module-linear BN chain maps for its elementary events, and Reidemeister equivalences that transport the localized canonical orientation lines by units. The ordinary Morse maps are the displayed BN Frobenius operations. The missing supplier must establish these actual based Reidemeister maps and mod-two comparison.

Put $S=\mathbb Z[h,h^{-1}]$ and $R_h=S[X]/(X^2-hX)$. Write $A=X$, $B=h-X$. Then $A^2=hA$, $B^2=hB$, $AB=0$, $\Delta(A)=A\otimes A$, $\Delta(B)=-B\otimes B$, $\eta(1)=h^{-1}(A+B)$, and $\epsilon(A)=1$, $\epsilon(B)=-1$. Every surviving elementary Morse coefficient is therefore a unit of $S$.

The localized state-cube contraction in the foundations lemma can be taken $R_h$-linear. Indeed, each enhanced-state Boolean summand has a fixed label on every original segment, so its marked label is fixed. On that entire summand $X$ acts by the same scalar, either $h$ or $0$. Rescalings by edge units and contractions of an identity factor commute with that scalar. Consequently the full localized complex retracts $R_h$-linearly onto its two singleton orientation states. For a knot these form one regular rank-one $R_h$ module, since $R_h\simeq S\oplus S$ by the idempotents $e_A=X/h$, $e_B=1-X/h$.

Fix either incoming orientation of the concordance. At each regular movie level, sum over orientations of partial surface components that extend it. A newly born disk contributes the two orientations, each with coefficient $h^{-1}$. A saddle within a partial component extends its orientation uniquely. A saddle joining distinct partial components retains exactly the compatible pairs; every resulting orientation has exactly one compatible predecessor, so no two coefficients cancel. A death preserves nonzero coefficients: if it made a partial component closed, that component could not later attach and would be a closed component of the final surface, contrary to connectedness with incoming boundary. Thus each final surviving orientation has a single unit coefficient. The stipulated Reidemeister transport multiplies these coefficients by units. Connectedness of the final concordance implies that either incoming orientation extends to exactly one outgoing orientation. Hence the induced map on singleton states is diagonal on the two marked eigensummands with unit diagonal entries $u_A,u_B\in S^\times$.

The corresponding endomorphism between the two regular rank-one $R_h$ modules is multiplication by $u_Ae_A+u_Be_B$, whose inverse is $u_A^{-1}e_A+u_B^{-1}e_B$. Compose this inverse with the explicit source and target retracts. It is a chain homotopy inverse to the localized movie composite, because the complementary Boolean summands have the preceding $R_h$-linear contractions. For a concordance the movie degree is its Euler characteristic $0$, so the equivalence has the required grading. This proves the composite-localization conclusion from the explicit stated movie-map input; no localized equivalence of an individual birth, saddle or death is asserted.

The same orientation argument applies to connected genus-$g$ knot cobordisms, with the corresponding quantum degree $-2g$. It does not supply the absent odd edge-sign census, signed based RIII maps, mod-two movie square, or movie existence preserving a marked arc.
