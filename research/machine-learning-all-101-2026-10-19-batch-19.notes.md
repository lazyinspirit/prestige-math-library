### Item completion log

- `def-negative-gradient-pseudo-residual` — A, level 0. Defines the finite-sample empirical score loss, its coordinate gradient convention, and the per-observation negative derivative called a pseudo-residual; it makes no parameter-gradient claim. Sources: ESL §10.10.3, printed p. 361; Natekin–Knoll §2.4, PDF p. 3. Dependencies: none.
- `alg-adaboost-m1` — A, level 0. Defines finite normalized reweighting, the half-log coefficient, explicit $e_m=0$ perfect-stop and $e_m\ge1/2$ failure branches, and the $+1$ tie rule. Sources: ESL §10.1, printed pp. 337–338; Schapire §2, PDF pp. 2–4. Dependencies: none. The positive-error normalizer is explicitly $2\sqrt{e_m(1-e_m)}$; the following theorem proves that it is the exact sum normalizer.
- `lem-pseudo-residuals-are-negative-score-gradient` — A, level 1. Proves that the empirical score loss on a finite sample is totally differentiable and has coordinate gradient $(\partial_2\ell(y_i,z_i))_i$, so its pseudo-residual vector is the negative score-space gradient. Sources: ESL §§10.10.1–10.10.3, printed pp. 358–361; Natekin–Knoll §§2.3–2.4, PDF pp. 2–3. Dependencies: `def-negative-gradient-pseudo-residual` plus the published total-derivative definition, chain rule, sum rule, partial-derivative convention, and gradient definition. The coordinate convention follows the published Euclidean definitions.
- `thm-adaboost-exponential-training-error-bound` — A, level 1. Proves the normalized exponential-loss identity and the empirical nonpositive-margin bound for every finite completed sequence with $0<e_m<1/2$; the declared tie rule also bounds training misclassification. Sources: ESL §10.4, printed pp. 343–344; Schapire §3, PDF pp. 4–5. Dependency: `alg-adaboost-m1`. The proof derives the half-log coefficient by minimizing the round exponential normalizer and treats only completed weak rounds; `e_m=0` is handled by the algorithm's perfect-stop branch.
+- `ex-adaboost-perfect-round-stops` — B, level 1. On two observations the base classifier has $e_1=0$, so the algorithm returns it with zero error and no coefficient. Generated finite example, verified directly. Dependency: `alg-adaboost-m1`.
- `ex-adaboost-two-rounds-and-margin-bound` — B, level 2. For four binary labels and two explicit classifiers, computes $e_1=1/4$, $e_2=1/6$, both half-log coefficients, both normalizers, every normalized weight vector, the four margins, and $Z_1Z_2=\sqrt{15}/6$. The computed nonpositive-margin fraction is $1/4$. Generated finite example, all arithmetic verified locally. Dependencies: `alg-adaboost-m1`, `thm-adaboost-exponential-training-error-bound`.
- `alg-gradient-boosting` — A, level 4. Defines finite-sample gradient tree boosting with attained constant initialization, score pseudo-residuals, a finite-depth squared-error CART fit, and separate exact attained loss-line minimizers in each leaf. It explicitly distinguishes the CART leaf means from the later update offsets and excludes shrinkage, subsampling, convergence, and generalization claims. Sources: ESL §§10.9–10.10.3, printed pp. 353–361; Natekin–Knoll §§2.3–2.4, PDF pp. 2–3. Dependencies: local pseudo-residual definition/gradient lemma and batch-17 tree definition, CART procedure, and finite-termination theorem. The actual draft suppliers and their relevant proofs were inspected; no approval is claimed. No axiom of choice is needed for the finite sequence of attained minima.
- `thm-gradient-boosting-empirical-loss-nonincreasing` — A, level 5. Proves round-by-round $L_m\le L_{m-1}$ by partitioning the finite training sample into nonempty leaves and comparing each attained leaf minimizer with the feasible zero offset. Sources: ESL Algorithm 10.3, printed p. 361; full algebra is local. Dependencies: alg-gradient-boosting, tree partition definition, and the CART finite-output theorem. The proof is choice-free and asserts no convergence or generalization.
- `ex-gradient-boosting-zero-tree-direction` — B, level 5. With squared loss and labels $(+1,-1)$ at zero scores, residual targets $(1,-1)$ have zero one-leaf least-squares fit, so the fitted vector is not a strict descent direction despite the nonzero unrestricted gradient; its exact leaf offset is also $0$. Generated finite example, all calculations verified locally. Dependencies: `alg-gradient-boosting`, `def-negative-gradient-pseudo-residual`, `lem-pseudo-residuals-are-negative-score-gradient`, `def-empirical-decision-tree`, `alg-cart-greedy-split`, and published `def-directional-and-partial-derivatives`.
