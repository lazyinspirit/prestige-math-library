## Subject coverage (definitions, results, examples)

- Outer product: $\circ$ is bilinear, associative, commutative and unital, so $R_S=\bigoplus_n R(S_n)$ is a commutative graded ring; the outer LR rule $\operatorname{Ind}_{S_m\times S_n}^{S_{m+n}}(S^\mu\boxtimes S^\nu)\cong\bigoplus_\lambda (S^\lambda)^{\oplus c^\lambda_{\mu\nu}}$; outer Pieri rules (horizontal strip for $S^{(r)}$, vertical strip for $S^{(1^r)}$).
- Restriction coproduct: the finite graded $\Delta(f)=\sum_{a+b=n}\Delta_{a,b}(f)$ with no completion; restriction is Schur skewing under ch; restriction multiplicities are the LR coefficients; the coproduct is cocommutative.
- Hopf structure: local graded coalgebra/bialgebra/Hopf definition over a commutative ring; connected graded bialgebras have a unique graded antipode by the reduced-coproduct recursion; Mackey over the $2\times2$ matrix double cosets gives bialgebra compatibility; ch is an isomorphism onto the usual Hopf algebra $\Lambda$ with the diagonal coproduct.
- LR coefficients: the Littlewood–Robinson proof of $s_\mu s_\nu=\sum_\lambda c^\lambda_{\mu\nu}s_\lambda$ and $s_{\lambda/\mu}=\sum_\nu c^\lambda_{\mu\nu}s_\nu$, including (9.3)–(9.7) joints; exchange and conjugation symmetries $c^\lambda_{\mu\nu}=c^\lambda_{\nu\mu}=c^{\lambda'}_{\mu'\nu'}$.
- Skew modules: $K^{\lambda/\mu}=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb C[S_m]}(S^\mu,\operatorname{Res}_{S_m\times S_r}^{S_{m+r}}S^\lambda)$ with commuting $S_r$-action, and $K^{\lambda/\mu}\cong\bigoplus_\nu(S^\nu)^{\oplus c^\lambda_{\mu\nu}}$ with Frobenius characteristic $s_{\lambda/\mu}$ over $\mathbb C$.
- Companion computations: $[3,2][2]$ decomposition by horizontal strips with dimension check $105$; the multiplicity-two example $c^{(4,2,1)}_{(2,1),(3,1)}=2$ with dimension check $210$; the semistandard-vs-LR failure; all five bidegree components of $\Delta(\chi^{(3,1)})$ matching the skew Schur expansion.

Design limits that are honoured, not gaps: no skew-polytabloid filtration or arbitrary-field skew-Specht construction is claimed (`def-skew-multiplicity-module-over-c` explicitly says so — this is the reviewed "preferred minimal closure" of the SYMR-3 planning review); internal/Kronecker products are deferred to SYMR-13; Macdonald Appendix A §6 (polynomial functors) and James §16 Lemmas 16.1–16.3 (the $A_c/R_c$ operator proof) are marked out-of-scope with reasons in the coverage file.
