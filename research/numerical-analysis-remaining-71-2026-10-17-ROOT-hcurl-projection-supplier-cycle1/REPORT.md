## Construction and exact boundary interface

Choose continuous piecewise affine local mesh size `g_h`, a smooth inward field `b`, a fixed radius `r`, and use `F_y^+(x)=x+epsilon*g_h(x)*(b(x)+r*y)` for unrestricted fields. Average the pullback integral over every oriented mesh simplex, then reconstruct the Whitney field. All samples are inside the domain; no commuting extension is imported. For homogeneous fields use `F_y^-(x)=x+epsilon*g_h(x)*(-b(x)+r*y)` and zero extension. The image of every boundary simplex lies strictly outside the domain, so all its moments vanish. Relative finite-element forms have globally graph-conforming zero extensions; this is proved through cancellation, not assumed from unbounded edge moments.

The relative projection is defined on ambient L2, with target the relative finite-element space. It commutes on `H_0 Lambda`. In particular its degree zero maps `H01` to the zero-boundary nodal space; degree one maps `H0curl` to the zero-tangential edge space; `grad Pi0 = Pi1 grad`; `curl Pi1 = Pi2 curl`; degree two fixes every finite-element curl. Both full and relative families converge strongly on ambient L2, uniformly on compact L2 subsets.

The phrase in the original Statement, “The degree-one construction preserves the zero tangential boundary subspace after its boundary correction,” must be made explicit. The proposal supplies **two families**: unrestricted and boundary-corrected. A single unrestricted projection that is L2-bounded and maps H0curl into the relative edge space is impossible: H0curl is L2 dense, the relative target is finite-dimensional and closed, so continuity would force the entire range into that target. The original theorem only asserted graph boundedness; a literal demand for one single graph-bounded unrestricted family preserving H0curl is not established by these two families. The proposed text explicitly distinguishes the operators and does not silently claim that stronger single-family property. The controlling PE09 design requires a commuting projection and boundary correction, and the separate relative construction supplies the exact downstream mathematical need.
