## Definition

Assume the Axiom of Choice ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]), inherited from the
published block theory. Work in category $\mathcal O$ of a finite-dimensional
complex semisimple Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$ with Cartan $\mathfrak h$,
positive system $\Phi^+$, simple roots $\alpha_1,\dots,\alpha_r$, Weyl group
$W$, Weyl vector $\rho$ and dot action $w\cdot\lambda=w(\lambda+\rho)-\rho$, as
in [[def-bgg-category-o]] and
[[def-dot-action-facets-and-single-wall-translation-data]].

**(1) Dot-antidominant integral weights.** A weight $\mu$ is **dot-antidominant**
if $\langle\mu+\rho,\alpha^\vee\rangle\le0$ for every $\alpha\in\Phi^+$, and
**integral** if $\langle\mu,\alpha_i^\vee\rangle\in\mathbb Z$ for all $i$
([[def-integral-weyl-group-of-a-weight]],
[[def-integral-dominant-and-strictly-dominant-weights]]).

**(2) The dot stabilizer.** Fix such a weight $\mu$ and put
$$J=J(\mu):=\{i:\langle\mu+\rho,\alpha_i^\vee\rangle=0\},\qquad W_\mu:=W_J=\langle s_i:i\in J\rangle.$$
Then the dot stabilizer $\{v\in W:v\cdot\mu=\mu\}$ equals $W_J$. Indeed, for
$i\in J$ the reflection $s_i$ fixes $\mu+\rho$, so $W_J$ lies in the
stabilizer; conversely the stabilizer of an integral dot-antidominant weight
is generated by the reflections it contains and is a standard parabolic
([[lem-finite-weyl-closed-chambers-and-stabilizers]], applied to the dominant
vector $-\mu-\rho$), and by
[[def-cg-parabolic-quotient-and-two-sided-minima]] a standard parabolic
subgroup is $W_I$ for the set $I$ of simple reflections it contains, which is
exactly $J$. In particular $w'\cdot\mu=w\cdot\mu$ if and only if
$w^{-1}w'\in W_J$, that is, $w'\in wW_J$.

**(3) The singular integral block.** The **singular integral block**
$\mathcal O_\mu$ is the block of
[[thm-central-character-summands-split-into-linkage-blocks]] of the central
character $\chi_\mu$ of $\mu$; by
[[cor-central-characters-are-dot-weyl-orbits]] two central characters agree
exactly when the highest weights lie in one dot orbit, and by
[[thm-category-o-decomposes-by-generalized-central-character]] the block is a
summand of $\mathcal O$. Its simple objects $L(w\cdot\mu)$, standard objects
$\Delta(w\cdot\mu)=M(w\cdot\mu)$, costandard objects $\nabla(w\cdot\mu)$ and
projective covers $P(w\cdot\mu)$ are indexed by $w\in W$
([[def-standard-and-costandard-objects-in-category-o]],
[[def-verma-module]]); by (2) the label $w\cdot\mu$ depends only on the coset
$wW_J$, so the labels can be normalized by the minimal right coset
representatives

$$W^J=\{w\in W:\ell(ws)>\ell(w)\ \text{for all }s\in J\},$$

which are exactly the elements of minimal length in the cosets $wW_J$
([[thm-hh-parabolic-minimal-representatives-and-length-additivity]] (3));
inversion carries $W^J$ onto the minimal left coset representatives ${}^JW$,
and the two families are the label sets of the parabolic Hecke modules of
[[def-parabolic-hecke-module-and-antispherical-standard-basis]].

**(4) Highest-weight structure and multiplicities.** The block is a
highest-weight category: it has enough projectives
([[thm-category-o-has-enough-projectives]]), projective covers have finite
Verma flags ([[thm-projectives-in-category-o-have-verma-flags]]), the standard
classes and simple classes are bases of the Grothendieck group
([[def-grothendieck-group-and-character-of-category-o]],
[[prop-the-grothendieck-group-of-o-has-simple-and-standard-bases]]), and it
satisfies BGG reciprocity
$$(P(w\cdot\mu):\Delta(y\cdot\mu))=[M(y\cdot\mu):L(w\cdot\mu)]$$
([[thm-bgg-reciprocity]], [[def-verma-flag-and-its-multiplicities]]). The
multiplicity matrix is unitriangular for the refinement of the dominance order
on the dot orbit: $(P(w\cdot\mu):\Delta(w\cdot\mu))=1$, and
$(P(w\cdot\mu):\Delta(y\cdot\mu))\neq0$ only if $y\cdot\mu\ge w\cdot\mu$
([[cor-projective-standard-labels-lie-above-the-head]]); the ordinary
standard/costandard Hom and $\mathrm{Ext}^1$ vanishing used on this page is
[[lem-standard-costandard-hom-and-ext-vanishing]].

**(5) Regular versus singular.** The block is **regular** when $J=\varnothing$
(the weight lies on no wall) and **singular** when $J\neq\varnothing$; the
singular translation statements of this page use this notation, and
$J=\varnothing$ recovers the regular case. The genuine regular-parabolic
multiplicity statements below have their own separate explicit dominant
regular base and are not consequences of the notation here.

## Remarks

- **Choice.** The Axiom of Choice is inherited with the published block theory:
  restricted duality, enough projectives, Verma flags of projectives and the
  Grothendieck-basis theorem are quoted from items that assume it
  ([[def-axiom-of-choice]], [[def-standard-and-costandard-objects-in-category-o]],
  [[thm-category-o-has-enough-projectives]],
  [[thm-projectives-in-category-o-have-verma-flags]],
  [[prop-the-grothendieck-group-of-o-has-simple-and-standard-bases]]), and no
  new choice principle is introduced here.
- **No new multiplicity assertion.** Everything in (4) is quoted structure of
  the published block theory; the first genuinely new multiplicity statements
  are the translation, singular and parabolic multiplicities proved later on
  this page. In particular no claim is made here that the singular matrix
  equals an ordinary Kazhdan--Lusztig matrix or a parabolic one.
- **Label conventions.** $W^J$ (right minima) is used for the singular block,
  ${}^JW$ (left minima) for the genuine regular parabolic category later; the
  two families are exchanged by inversion
  ([[def-cg-parabolic-quotient-and-two-sided-minima]] (2)), and the small
  $S_3/S_2$ matrices of the companion examples exhibit both label orders.
