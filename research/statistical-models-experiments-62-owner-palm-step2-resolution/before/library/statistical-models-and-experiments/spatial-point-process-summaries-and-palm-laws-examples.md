A uniformly translated one-dimensional lattice is stationary but has an atomic reduced second factorial measure, so a pair-correlation density need not exist. Doubling each lattice site makes one-occurrence Palm deletion visible at radius zero. A finite-window calculation shows which anchors the border procedure retains and how its ordered-pair sum is normalized; it does not establish estimator unbiasedness.
