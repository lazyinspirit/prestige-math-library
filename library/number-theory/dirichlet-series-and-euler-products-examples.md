These examples show how the abstract half-plane statements behave in practice:
the alternating and ordinary zeta series give different boundary phenomena, and
the Möbius, divisor, totient, and Liouville identities can be read prime by
prime or coefficient by coefficient once the page's absolute-convergence
hypotheses are in place.
