These examples keep the page finite and concrete. They tabulate divisor sums,
run the Dirichlet-inverse recursion by hand, cash out the prime-power formulas
for $\tau$ and $\sigma_k$, and show how the published totient and Möbius items
interact on a single line of inversion.

The last two examples separate nearby notions and record one historical warning:
multiplicative does not mean completely multiplicative, and the Mertens
conjecture is a sourced false statement rather than a usable theorem.
