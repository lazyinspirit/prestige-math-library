This page classifies the rank-one absolute values on $\mathbb Q$, records the
place language and the rational product formula, and then builds $\mathbb Q_p$
as a completion before comparing it with the integral compatible-residue model.

The p-adic half keeps the model boundaries explicit. The completion field is
constructed first, the integral subring is then identified with the $(p)$-adic
completion of $\mathbb Z$, and only after that do the digit expansion,
compactness, Hensel lifting, Newton iteration, and square criteria appear.
