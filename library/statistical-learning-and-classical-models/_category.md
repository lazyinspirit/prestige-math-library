Published pages establish prediction problems, risks, and empirical minimization, then develop finite-class PAC, VC and Rademacher bounds, stability, compression, PAC-Bayes, and minimax lower bounds. The modeling pages cover linear and sparse methods, classifiers, kernels and Gaussian processes, trees and boosting, clustering, spectral methods, dimension reduction, latent representations, graphical and causal models, metric learning, and semi-supervised methods. Further work treats active querying, shift, robustness, calibration, fairness, privacy, federated learning, and deployment guarantees, keeping distinct objectives and assumptions explicit.

Probability and Mathematical Statistics ground risk and finite-sample guarantees; Statistical Models and Experiments specifies data-generating settings. Information Theory supports lower bounds. Linear Algebra supports model fitting, and Numerical Linear Algebra and Approximation supports kernel methods. Neural Networks and Learning Dynamics, Online and Reinforcement Learning, and Representation Learning and Generative Models use these bounds and prediction tools in generalization, decisions, and model evaluation. Each applies the bounds to its own prediction or decision setting, retaining the relevant sampling and model assumptions.
