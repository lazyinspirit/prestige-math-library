The companion page carries the witnesses that make the implication diagram on
the main page concrete. The typewriter sequence separates convergence in
measure and in $L^1$ from pointwise almost-everywhere convergence; the
translated unit intervals show exactly where finite measure enters; the spike
family separates convergence in measure from convergence in $L^1$; and the
Dirichlet-function and disjoint-spike examples keep the Lusin and uniform
integrability traps visible instead of leaving them as prose.
