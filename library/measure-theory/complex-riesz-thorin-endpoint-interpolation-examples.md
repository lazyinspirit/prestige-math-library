A two-by-two matrix and a probability averaging operator give explicit endpoint estimates and their interpolated consequences. The parameter p=4/3 illustrates the conjugate exponent and constant. Unit-L1 spikes on the Lebesgue interval show that finite-target hypotheses do not imply an unasserted infinity endpoint.
