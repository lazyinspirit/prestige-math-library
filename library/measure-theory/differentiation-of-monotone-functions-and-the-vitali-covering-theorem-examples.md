These examples keep the page’s boundary phenomena local. The Cantor function
shows both the strict integral inequality and an uncountable nondifferentiability
set. The rational-jump constructions isolate the atomic part. The dense Cantor
series gives a strictly increasing singular function. The Dini example makes the
four one-sided quantities visibly different, and the last two items show exactly
where the fine-cover and continuity hypotheses matter.
