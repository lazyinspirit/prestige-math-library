This page keeps the three classical seams visible. The first block packages the
four Dini derivatives and the finite derivative convention. The second proves
the rising-sun route, first for continuous monotone functions and then for
general monotone functions after the jump-part reduction. The third proves the
interval Vitali theorem and the null-set characterisation that later measure
arguments use.

The remaining items compare that route with the Lebesgue-Stieltjes route,
record the sharp integral inequality for increasing functions, and state the
atomic, absolutely continuous, and singular-continuous decompositions that
probability and harmonic-analysis pages cite later.
