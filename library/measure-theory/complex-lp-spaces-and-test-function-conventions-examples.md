Three calculations illustrate the complex conventions: conjugate phase tests recover a norm that real tests miss; two step functions distinguish bilinear integration from the first-variable-linear L2 pairing; and mollification has a quantitative finite-p error while retaining an infinity-norm obstruction. The Euclidean examples retain countable choice from their measure and mollifier prerequisites.
