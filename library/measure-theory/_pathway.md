## sigma-algebras-and-borel-sets

A sigma-algebra is the closure needed for countable additivity, and outer measure plus
Caratheodory turn covering costs into complete measures such as Lebesgue measure. The added
pages build the main analytic layers above that base. Measurable functions are
characterised by generators, threshold sets and stability under arithmetic and sequential
limits, then approximated from below or in dominated form by simple functions. Those
approximations define the Lebesgue integral, yielding monotone and dominated convergence,
Fatou, and $L^1$ consequences. On the line, right-continuous increasing functions and
Borel measures finite on compact sets become equivalent through the Lebesgue-Stieltjes and
distribution-function correspondence.
