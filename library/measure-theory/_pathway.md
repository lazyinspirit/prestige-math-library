## sigma-algebras-and-borel-sets

A sigma-algebra is the family of sets on which countable operations stay available, which
is the closure property a measure needs and a topology does not have. The Borel sets are
the smallest such family containing the open sets, generated countably in Euclidean space
by rational boxes, and the generated sigma-algebra, the trace on a subspace and
measurability of a function are the notions the rest of measure theory is stated in.
