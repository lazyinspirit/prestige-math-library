These examples distinguish dimension from critical measure and from topology. They compute lengths and fractal dimensions using the preceding cover estimates and mass distribution principle, and exhibit the failures of countability, union-sum, and continuous-invariance assertions. Countable Choice is assumed; the Vitali refutation explicitly assumes full Choice.
