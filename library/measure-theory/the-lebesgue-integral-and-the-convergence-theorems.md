This page builds the Lebesgue integral in the three-stage route fixed by the
measure-theory design notes. First come nonnegative simple functions and the
simple integral, then the nonnegative integral together with monotone
convergence, Fatou, and the density construction, and only after that the
signed and complex theories.

The page's false statements record the exact hypotheses the convergence
theorems spend: monotonicity for monotone convergence, a dominating integrable
majorant for dominated convergence, almost-everywhere rather than everywhere
equality for zero-integral criteria, and probability normalization for Jensen's
inequality.
