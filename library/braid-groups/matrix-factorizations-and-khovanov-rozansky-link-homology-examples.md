These four entries make the construction of the companion page concrete. The
first writes out the positive crossing complex with its two resolutions, the
presentation matrices $P_0,P_1,Q_0,Q_1$ and the two matrices defining
$\chi_0$, and displays the negative crossing side by side with its
$\{0,-2\}$ normalization, using the corrected $\chi_1$-cone. The second
computes the factorization of the one-mark circle, obtains
$H(\Gamma)\cong\mathbb Q[x]\{-1,1\}$ and reads off the unknot Euler
characteristic $t^{-1}/(q^{-1}-q)$. The third iterates the construction for a
two-crossing closed braid, exhibits the four resolutions and verifies that the
total potential of a closed diagram vanishes. The fourth is a scope
illustration: it tabulates the moves actually used in the invariance proof —
IIa, coherent-orientation III, conjugation, the oriented stabilizations and
Markov's theorem — and records why the oriented IIb move is neither used nor
claimed.
