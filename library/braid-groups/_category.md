Braid groups are built from configuration spaces as well as presentations. The published pages compare ordered and unordered configurations, derive pure braid groups and Fadell-Neuwirth sequences, study punctured-disc mapping classes, and recover Artin presentations, braid combing, Garside normal forms, and the center. They also develop the Artin action on free groups, Burau representations, and Lawrence-Krammer-Bigelow linearity. Further pages connect braids to link closures, Markov equivalence, Hecke-Markov traces, and polynomial link invariants. A categorical branch treats braid actions through graded quiver algebras, derived tensor functors, Soergel bimodules, Rouquier complexes, matrix factorizations, Hochschild homology, and Yang-Baxter operators.

Topology and Algebraic Topology provide homotopy, covering-space, and configuration-space tools; Abstract Algebra and Category Theory support presentations and monoidal constructions. Categorification and Geometric Representation Theory draws on the Soergel material, braid actions, categorical braid complexes, and diagram constructions. Quantum Algebra and Representation Theory and Knot Theory also use the collection's representation and closure results.
