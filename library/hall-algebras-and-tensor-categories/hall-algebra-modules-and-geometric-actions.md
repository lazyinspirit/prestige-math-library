Hall modules use the stated untwisted Hall multiplication. The generic nilpotent Jordan regular module is transported by the exact normalized Hall–Littlewood map; its Green adjoints give the normalized Heisenberg Fock action over $\mathbb Q(t)$. The geometric route is the full arbitrary-framing no-arrow $A_1$ family $T^*\mathrm{Gr}(v,W)$: stable framed data, projective conormal Hecke correspondences, refined excess coefficients and associative supported Chow convolution produce the highest-weight-$\dim W$ $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ action. The coefficient theory is ordinary rational top Chow of the zero fiber; all geometry and maps are $\mathrm{GL}(W)$-equivariant. The original point construction is its $\dim W=1$ example.
