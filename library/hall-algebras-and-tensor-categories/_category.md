Hall algebra pages turn extension data in finitary, derived, motivic, or geometric settings into algebraic operations, while tracking the hypotheses each construction needs. Published topics include Ringel-Hall multiplication, Green's bialgebra and quantum-group realizations, derived Hall algebras, Hall modules and geometric actions, motivic integration, and cohomological Hall algebras from quiver correspondences. Stability pages connect Hall integration to Donaldson-Thomas and BPS invariants and wall crossing. Related tensor-category topics cover Frobenius algebras and two-dimensional sewing, modular categories, Verlinde formulas and TQFT, noninvertible symmetries and anyonic chains, and higher-categorical defects. Their pages compare counting, motivic, and cohomological realizations.

Category Theory and Homological Algebra provide categorical and derived settings; Algebraic Geometry supports the quiver-correspondence pages; Hopf Algebras & Hecke Algebras and Quantum Groups and Crystal Bases supply bialgebra and quantum-group context. Published Knot Theory material uses Hall-algebra constructions. The tensor-category topics connect with modular, TQFT, and anyonic applications. These theories carry separate coefficient systems, finiteness conditions, and geometric assumptions.
