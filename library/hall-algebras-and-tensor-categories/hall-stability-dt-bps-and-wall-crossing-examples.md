This companion contains finite calculations that use the A-page conventions.
Its examples test the groupoid weight, Euler normalization, phase order, and
the noncommutative target. They do not supply general theorem premises.

## Worked examples

- [[ex-one-vertex-finite-quiver-hall-integration-is-quantum-dilogarithm]]
  counts $\operatorname{GL}_n(\mathbb F_q)$ and recovers the quantum
  dilogarithm series with the exact Euler normalization.
- [[ex-a2-quiver-hall-wall-crossing-pentagon]] classifies all representations
  of $1\to2$, checks every subobject of the indecomposable extension, and
  derives the completed A₂ pentagon in the skew torus.
- [[cex-omitting-strictly-semistable-objects-breaks-hn-factorization]] shows
  in dimension $(1,1)$ why the wall factor must include the strictly
  semistable extension.
- [[cex-commutative-target-erases-the-a2-wall-crossing]] compares the
  bidegree-$(1,1)$ coefficients and shows that ordinary commutative
  multiplication loses the pentagon.

The finite-field coefficients are arithmetic groupoid masses. None of these
computations identifies them with physical BPS state counts or with motivic
and cohomological Hall theories.
