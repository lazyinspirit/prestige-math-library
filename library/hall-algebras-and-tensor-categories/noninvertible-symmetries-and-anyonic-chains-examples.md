These examples compute channel projectors in the coherent Fibonacci model, count the two alternating Ising periodic-path sectors, and show why each multiplicity index contributes a distinct path state. The Ising example instantiates the complete A-homed coherent positive-FS category, with explicit inverse-pullback chirality. Its two-sector basis maps intertwine the actual wraparound projectors, translation and annular lines; the single-chain map retains its distinct translated square.

The Fibonacci calculation keeps the complex pullback-R convention and the actual positive Frobenius–Schur sign. It also separates three meanings of invertibility: the Fibonacci class is a unit in the Grothendieck ring, its simple object has no tensor inverse, and the identity map on any path Hilbert space is an invertible operator. None of these finite categorical computations asserts a physical symmetry for an unspecified Hamiltonian.
