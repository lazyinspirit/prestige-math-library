This page assembles the absolute Hodge-theoretic package for smooth complex
projective varieties. It begins by building the analytification of a smooth
projective complex scheme as a compact Hausdorff second-countable complex
manifold and by representing every ample class by a positive closed real
$(1,1)$-form whose de Rham class is the real image of the topological first
Chern class. The Kähler identities then transport the pointwise Lefschetz
$\mathfrak{sl}_2$-action through harmonic representatives, yielding hard
Lefschetz, the primitive decomposition and its rationality at rational Kähler
classes.

The remaining items record the polarization theory: morphisms, direct sums and
polarizability of rational pure Hodge structures; the orthogonal splitting of
polarized structures and their semisimplicity; the Hodge–Riemann bilinear
relations with their positivity, sign-adjusted full-cohomology polarization and
continuous variation of primitive kernels; and the combined package for smooth
projective varieties, including functoriality, rationality, orthogonal
splitting and persistence under extension of scalars to any characteristic-zero
field. The package is deliberately absolute: weak hyperplane Lefschetz,
relative hard Lefschetz, singular intersection-cohomology purity and
semisimplicity of arbitrary local systems belong to other pages. The Axiom of
Choice is assumed throughout and propagated from the analytic suppliers; the
orthogonal-splitting lemma and the accompanying example are choice-free.
