This page follows the classical one-variable route fixed by the design. The
first block proves Bloch's theorem by the elementary maximizing-point
normalization, extracts the corresponding Landau radius bound, and then uses
branch constructions for functions omitting $0$ and $1$ to obtain Schottky's
theorem.

From Schottky the page derives the normal-family theorem for two-value-omitting
families, Little Picard, the repaired fixed-annulus lemma ruling out an
essential singularity when two finite values are omitted, and then Great
Picard. The final remark records the agreement with the later Nevanlinna route
without turning that later page into a load-bearing dependency here.
