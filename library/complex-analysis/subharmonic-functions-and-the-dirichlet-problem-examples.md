The companion page computes the standard model examples and records the
counterexamples that keep the Perron and upper-envelope theorems honest:
$|z|^2$, $\log|z|$, a Poisson modification that flattens a radial quadratic on
an inner disc, an explicit annulus solution, an explicit polygonal barrier, the
punctured-disc obstruction to solvability, and the false statements that fail
once local boundedness above, regularity, or the maximum principle is dropped.
