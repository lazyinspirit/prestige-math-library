The examples isolate the two obstructions that the A page has to name honestly.
Polynomial approximation fails as soon as a compact set carries a hole, and the
naive sum of principal parts usually diverges unless the Mittag-Leffler
correction terms are built in.

The positive examples stay close to the source route: an explicit three-disc
pole push, a direct summation from the cotangent expansion, and a concrete
Mittag-Leffler construction with double poles at the integers.
