The example performs the smallest nontrivial calculation behind the
Hodge–Riemann positivity identity. On an oriented Euclidean two-plane it
verifies that the type-$(1,0)$ form $u=e+if$ is primitive and that the Weil-star
identity gives $*u=-iu$, matching the pointwise primitive-star formula at
$n=k=1$, and it computes the norm density $u\wedge*\bar u=2\,e\wedge f$ that
underlies positivity.
