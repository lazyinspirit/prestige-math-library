Worked examples for the normal Moore space pair. The page computes the stars of
the standard metric covers by balls of radius $2^{-n}$ and verifies the two
inclusions that make them a development, so that every metric space is a Moore
space; and it computes the three-quarter event estimate in the PMEA separation
argument, where two good events of measure above three quarters and a
two-coordinate difference event of measure one half overlap in a point that
separates the two chosen neighbourhoods. The false statement records the
relative-consistency refutation of "$\mathrm{ZFC}$ proves NMSC": CH yields a
normal nonmetrizable Moore space, so assuming $\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC})$,
$\mathrm{ZFC}$ does not prove the normal Moore space conjecture.
