These pages develop the ZFC language and axioms, set constructions, relations and quotients,
choice principles, filters and ultrafilters, well-founded ranks, ordinals, and cardinal
arithmetic. They continue through formal syntax and completeness, constructible and inner
models, forcing and iterations, symmetric extensions and choice failure, large cardinals,
and consistency results about descriptive set theory and topology, trees, stationary sets,
cardinal invariants, and the continuum. Choice-strength results compare Baire category,
ultrafilter, and compactness principles; forcing and symmetric models isolate consequences
independent of ZF or ZFC.

Topology and Real Analysis supply metric and analytic tools; Measure Theory and Probability
supply measurable constructions. Combinatorics, Computability Theory, Number Theory, Linear
Algebra, and Abstract Algebra contribute specific results used in the subject. Constructions
of functions and products, Zorn's lemma, ordinals, and transfinite recursion supply tools
for Topology, Real Analysis, Measure Theory, Abstract Algebra, Category Theory,
Computability Theory, and Combinatorics. Set constructions and choice principles also
underpin pages in Algebraic Geometry, Scheme Theory, Representation Theory of Groups, and
Numerical Pdes and Spatial Discretization.
