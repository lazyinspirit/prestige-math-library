These examples keep the page's asymptotic claims concrete. One turns a specific
quadratic time bound directly into a quadratic space bound, another changes
between two explicitly translated tuple encodings, and the counterexample
returns to the standard single-tape versus multitape slowdown to show why model
invariance is only asymptotic.
