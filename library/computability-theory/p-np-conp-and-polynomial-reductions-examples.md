These examples keep the class containments concrete. One easy parity language
already sits in $P$, hence in both $NP$ and $coNP$; another turns the abstract
$NP\subseteq PSPACE$ proof into an explicit lexicographic certificate search;
and the counterexample shows why "NP" cannot mean "not polynomial."
