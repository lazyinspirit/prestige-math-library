These examples work through three of the page's recurring patterns on small
instances. The first writes down an explicit clause-cluster graph and clique,
the second traces the NAE-to-colouring construction on a toy formula, and the
counterexample shows why a yes-preserving map can still fail to be a valid
many-one reduction.
