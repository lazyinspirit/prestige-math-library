These examples unpack the page's two most concrete constructions. One decodes a
single finite-sequence code far enough to read off its length and a chosen
coordinate, and the other shows the equivalence with lambda-definability on the
familiar factorial function. The counterexample page item keeps the main growth
separation visible: Ackermann is total and computable, but still not primitive
recursive.
