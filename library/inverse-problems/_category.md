---
title: Inverse Problems
---

The Inverse Problems pages study what can be recovered from indirect, noisy, incomplete, or discretized observations. Foundational results address identifiability, stability, compact operators, Picard criteria, and linear and nonlinear regularization, including Tikhonov, Landweber, Levenberg–Marquardt, spectral methods, and variational source conditions. Applied settings include Radon and limited-angle tomography, inverse scattering, electrical impedance tomography, and wave observations. Statistical and Bayesian pages cover Gaussian sequence models, minimax rates, posterior propriety and contraction, MAP estimators, function-space priors, and posterior computation. Other pages analyze sparse recovery, total variation, plug-and-play and diffusion methods, learned reconstruction, experimental design, and benchmark shift, including limits and unresolved uniqueness questions. Some pages also separate data, operator, discretization, and solver errors.

Functional Analysis supplies Hilbert-space geometry, Riesz representation, compact operators, and Banach differentiability; Partial Differential Equations supplies Sobolev traces, weak solutions, and elliptic regularity. Fourier Analysis provides transforms for tomography. Mathematical Statistics frames experiments, risk, and detection; Bayesian Computational Statistics supports priors and posterior computation. Numerical Optimization Inverse Problems and Uncertainty Quantification supplies spectral regularization, parameter choice, and iterative solvers. Together they frame recovery and computation.
