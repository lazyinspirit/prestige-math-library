Existing published reflection invariant suppliers already cover arbitrary finite complex reflection groups, including complexified H3, H4 and dihedral groups. This page adapts their actual hypotheses to Coxeter geometry and derives new Coxeter-specific information without duplicating the proof home.

This is a prose scaffold for future local item authoring. Its empty item lists do not assert proof completion. Every construction below is a named supplier contract; definitions are justified by the separately named existence, descent or uniqueness proofs before any application consumes their properties. Source reading supports the selected proof route and is not a substitute for a library proof.

## Ordered construction and proof contracts

**lem-cg-complexification-satisfies-reflection-invariant-hypotheses.** For finite W prove the faithful real representation complexifies faithfully, each generator has one-dimensional (-1)-eigenspace and fixes a complex hyperplane, and coefficients have characteristic zero. Apply the published general complex-reflection direction of thm-chevalley-shephard-todd-for-finite-weyl-groups, including its explicit AC hypothesis; its title does not restrict its statement to crystallographic W.

**def-cg-coxeter-basic-degrees-and-graded-coinvariants.** Define degrees d_i of a homogeneous algebraically independent generating family of C[V]^W, exponents e_i=d_i-1, and the coinvariant quotient by positive-degree invariants using the published invariant definition. Do not assert degree multiset independence merely from the chosen generators.

Definition justification: `lem-cg-basic-degrees-independent-and-coinvariant-series`.

**lem-cg-basic-degrees-independent-and-coinvariant-series.** Compare Hilbert series products ∏(1-t^d_i)^-1; recover smallest degrees successively, proving multiset independence. Apply the existing regular-sequence and order-W Hilbert suppliers to get Hilb coinvariants=∏(1+t+...+t^(d_i-1)) and product d_i=|W|, carrying the supplier AC scope.

**lem-cg-formal-rational-differentials-and-invariant-jacobian.** Prove the exact characteristic-zero differential bridge. Finite invariant-module freeness or the coordinate orbit monic polynomials make C(x_1,...,x_n) finite algebraic over C(p_1,...,p_n). A nonconstant irreducible minimal polynomial has nonzero derivative in characteristic zero and gcd(P,P′)=1 since degree P′<degree P; Bezout implies P′(x_j)≠0. Differentiate its rational-coefficient relation by the explicitly verified quotient/chain rules to solve dx_j as a linear combination of dp_i. Hence the square invariant derivative matrix has full rational rank and J=det(dp_i) is a nonzero polynomial. State all rational denominators as nonzero field elements and prove algebraic differentiation on generators before using it; no étale-quotient or transcendental Jacobian criterion is imported.

**thm-cg-coinvariant-top-degree-and-discriminant.** Use the full Molien identity actually proved in the published coinvariant Hilbert supplier: near t=1 write δ=1−t. Identity term has poleδ^-n; each reflection contributes (1/2)δ^-(n−1), while all other orthogonal elements have fixed codimension≥2. Prove rank-one orthogonal elements are exactly root reflections by the generic hyperplane-point chamber stabilizer. Comparing with ∏(1−t^d_i)^-1 gives Σ(d_i−1)=|Φ_+|, independently of growth. The prior formal-differential lemma makes J nonzero; the chain rule makes it anti-invariant. Each hyperplane equation divides J, distinct linear primes divide their product, and equal total degrees give J=cΔ≠0. Likewise every anti-invariant polynomial is Δ times an invariant, by divisibility and generator signs. Existing regular-sequence Hilbert series gives top coinvariant degree Σ(d_i−1) and a one-dimensional top component; prove the top Δ class nonzero by the coefficient-factorial Hermitian pairing on complex polynomials in B-orthonormal REAL coordinates: multiplication by an invariant p is adjoint to conjugate-p formal differentiation; conjugate p remains invariant because W has real matrices in these coordinates, and conjugate-p(∂)Δ is anti-invariant of degree below the number of positive roots, hence zero by Δ divisibility. Thus Δ is orthogonal to the whole positive-invariant ideal in its degree but has positive norm, so its quotient class is nonzero and spans the sign line. Scalar/ordering dependence of Δ is explicit; no regular-representation decomposition is imported.

**thm-cg-regular-coxeter-eigenvectors-determine-basic-degrees.** Work irreducibly with the already proved bipartite Coxeter plane and root enumeration, independent of invariants. Its c-eigenvector x with cx=ζ_h^-1 x is regular because real and imaginary parts span the plane and no root hyperplane contains it, so J(x)≠0. Homogeneity/invariance gives dp_i(x)∘c=ζ_h^(d_i−1)dp_i(x), and these covectors form a basis. Prove c has no eigenvalue1 from (c−1)v=-2ΣB(v,α_i)ρ_i with triangular basisρ_i. Inverse-paired real orthogonal eigen residues r_i∈{1,...,h−1} sum to nh/2; root enumeration gives the same |Φ_+|. Essentiality gives d_i≥2. Thus positive d_i−1 differ from r_i by nonnegative multiples h whose sum is zero, proving d_i−1=r_i. Handle A1 directly and products componentwise. Obtain A/B/D/I spectra from explicit permutation/signed-coordinate models; the six exceptional rank≤8 exact characteristic matrices and cyclotomic factor identities are fully recorded in math-checks/finite-degree-poincare-certificates.json. Prove the matrix-trace coefficient recurrence by differentiating det(I−tC) with cofactors and comparing formal coefficients, then verify every recorded polynomial identity in Q[z]/Φ_h. No invariant-degree table or approximate spectral calculation is assumed.
