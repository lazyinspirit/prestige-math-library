An affine Weyl group is first built directly as a Euclidean isometry group. This construction closes the geometric and lattice prerequisites before comparison with the separately proved loop-algebra model.

This is a prose scaffold for future local item authoring. Its empty item lists do not assert proof completion. ## Ordered construction and proof contracts

**def-cg-affine-root-hyperplane-reflection-and-alcove.** In a supplied finite reduced crystallographic root system in Euclidean E, define H_(a,k)={x:B(x,a)=k}, r_(a,k)(x)=x-(B(x,a)-k)a∨ for integer k, and alcoves as components of the complement of all such hyperplanes. Define affine reflection group and Q∨⋊W with explicit multiplication.

Definition justification: `lem-cg-affine-reflection-identities-and-local-finiteness`.

**lem-cg-affine-reflection-identities-and-local-finiteness.** Verify r_(a,k)=t_(k a∨)r_a, its square one and hyperplane fixed pointwise. Since roots are finite and integer k bounded on compact sets, prove local finiteness and openness of complement. Prove translations by simple coroots arise as products r_(a,1)r_(a,0), giving affine group=Q∨⋊W with unique Euclidean decomposition.

**lem-cg-highest-root-and-fundamental-alcove.** Supply highest-root dominance from the finite crystallographic root poset, proving every positive root bounded by highest root θ and positive simple-root coefficients. Then A={x:B(x,a_s)>0, B(x,θ)<1} is a nonempty bounded simplex; exhibit a sufficiently small sum of dual fundamental coordinates. A1 and reducible factors have separate conventions.

**lem-cg-affine-generic-gallery-paths-and-disk-moves.** Prove Q and Q∨ discrete full-rank lattices from simple-root/coroot bases. For a locally finite root arrangement, a compact polygonal path meets a finite list of walls; perturb intermediate vertices outside finitely many proper affine subspaces so crossings are transverse and avoid codimension-two faces. A null-homotopy disk is triangulated into finitely many cells and perturbed to avoid codimension≥3 strata; its crossings change galleries by immediate backtracks and rank-two polygon circuits. Prove finite bad-set avoidance, subdivision compatibility and boundary fixation using the supplied finite convex triangulation/relative homotopy tools. This is the exact presentation bridge, not an appeal to pictures.

**thm-cg-affine-alcove-transitivity-presentation-and-length.** Start with the proved fundamental alcove A and locally finite reflection-invariant arrangement. A generic finite gallery reaches every alcove. Inductively reflect across each facet of the current alcove; it is the conjugate of a facet reflection of A, so the subgroup generated by A walls is transitive. Every arrangement wall is a facet somewhere (choose a generic wall point), hence its reflection belongs to this subgroup, which therefore equals the all-root-reflection group Q∨⋊W. Rank-two wall angles give the affine Coxeter relators. For injectivity and trivial alcove stabilizer, an abstract simple word mapping A back to A gives a closed gallery. Fill its path by a boundary-fixed generic Euclidean disk using the earlier finite general-position supplier; its gallery changes only by backtracks or codimension-two dihedral circuits, precisely the abstract square/braid relators. Thus the word is trivial in the abstract presentation. This establishes the presentation and simple transitivity without assuming that bounded inequalities or translation uniqueness alone exclude stabilizers. Any gallery must cross every wall separating its endpoints; a straight generic path crosses each separator once, so minimum word length equals the number of separating affine hyperplanes. Rank one uses the line-gallery argument; reducible systems use products.
