The noncrossing lattice proof needs an explicit spherical root complex and its convexity. This page supplies the root ordering, every imported orthogonal identity and the facet induction suppressed by the original article.

This is a prose scaffold for future local item authoring. Its empty item lists do not assert proof completion. Every construction below is a named supplier contract; definitions are justified by the separately named existence, descent or uniqueness proofs before any application consumes their properties. Source reading supports the selected proof route and is not a substitute for a library proof.

## Ordered construction and proof contracts

**def-cg-bipartite-coxeter-element-and-root-recursion.** For an irreducible finite Coxeter diagram, its proved tree classification gives a two-coloring. Order the commuting color classes J,K and set c=s1...sn. Define cyclic α_i,s_i, prefix roots ρ_i=s1...s_(i-1)α_i, dual β_i with B(β_i,α_j)=δ_ij, μ_i=s1...s_(i-1)β_i and h=ord(c). Define μ(a)=-2(c-1)^-1a only conditionally on invertibility, proved next. Treat rank one explicitly and reducible systems componentwise, using lcm for the global order instead of a uniform Coxeter number.

Definition justification: `lem-cg-steinberg-bipartite-root-enumeration`.

**lem-cg-steinberg-bipartite-root-enumeration.** For irreducible finite rank n≥2 order the two nonempty color classes J,K, and put a=∏J s,b=∏K s,c=ab. On simple coordinates maximize the quadratic form of A=2(I-B) on the compact Euclidean unit sphere; absolute values and connectedness give Ap=λp with p>0 and 0<λ<2. For u=ΣJ p_sα_s,v=ΣK p_sα_s, prove B(u,u)=B(v,v)=d>0,B(u,v)=-λd/2 and a(u)=-u,a(v)=v+λu,b(v)=-v,b(u)=u+λv. Their invariant plane meets C in a sector of angle θ=arccos(λ/2), whose interior lies in C°. Its regular point has trivial W stabilizer, so the color-dihedral action on the plane is faithful. If h=ord(c), θ=kπ/h; its 2h chamber sectors have disjoint interiors, giving θ≤π/h and k=1. Every root wall meets this plane in a boundary line. At a fundamental boundary ray the zero set is exactly J or K; W_J or W_K is an independent commuting-reflection group, whose only rank-one reflections are its simple generators. Transport to every ray: no extra root walls occur. A half-turn crosses every positive wall once; expanding alternating color blocks yields a reduced word by the root-sign length criterion and precisely the prefix roots ρ_i without repeats. If h even, there are h/2 blocks of each color. If h odd, a,b are dihedrally conjugate, so their (-1)-eigenspace dimensions |J|,|K| agree; the total letter count is again nh/2. Hence Φ_+=(ρ_1,...,ρ_(nh/2)); the word is w0, equal to c^(h/2) for even h and c^((h-1)/2)a for odd h. Prove c-1 invertible separately from (c-1)β_i=-2ρ_i and triangular independence for 1≤i≤n. Handle A1 directly and reducible systems componentwise; never claim one common nh/2 formula for unequal component Coxeter numbers.

**lem-cg-ordered-root-pairings-and-simple-systems.** Prove (c-1)μ_i=-2ρ_i, μ_i·ρ_i=1, μ_i·ρ_j≥0 for i≤j and ≤0 for j<i, and μ_i·ρ_(i-t)=0 for 1≤t<n, by the dual-basis recursion. For σ≤_Tc construct its induced root set Pσ and simple system by the reverse-selection procedure of Brady–Watt §5; prove existence, independence, spanning and σ factorization using the Wall restriction and the triangular dot-product criterion. Expand all rank-two positivity comparisons.

**def-cg-brady-watt-ordered-spherical-root-complex.** Define X(c) vertices the ordered positive unit roots; an increasing subset {a1,...,ak} spans a face when r_ak...r_a1≤_Tc. Define X(σ) by its full vertex set Pσ and induced faces, and positive cone realization. A face is spherical only after linear independence and common open hemisphere have been proved.

Definition justification: `thm-cg-root-complex-convex-cones-and-facet-induction`.

**lem-cg-ordered-root-complex-is-geometric-simplicial.** Use upper triangular μ(a_i)·a_j with diagonal one to prove independence and the factorization criterion. Add vertices in order; new simplices cone over the old μ(a)^⊥ subcomplex. Prove pairwise geometric intersections are common faces and that induced X(σ) has dimension ℓ_T(σ)-1, using the published realization conventions rather than identifying abstract and geometric intersections without proof.

**thm-cg-root-complex-convex-cones-and-facet-induction.** Write K_i=M(σ)∩all μ(θ_j) positive halfspaces∩all μ(τ_j) negative halfspaces for j>i. Prove cone X(σ,τ_i)=K_i by nested rank and root-index induction. For apex a=τ_(i+1), Wall restriction gives L=M(σ)∩μ(a)^⊥=M(R(a)σ). The base F is the previous complex intersect L; inner rank induction identifies it as a convex full-dimensional complex with every facet supported by a restricted μ(b), b a root in L. For each base facet extend its normal through a as ν=μ(b)−(μ(b)·a)μ(a). Prove μ+μ*=2I; since μ(a)·b=0, ν=μ(b−2(a,b)a)=μ(R(a)b). This is a signed root in M(σ), hence a signed Pσ root by the reflection-normal criterion. Convert to its positive root r. If r<a and r is not among θ_j, the explicit separating-root lemma below yields two earlier roots in L on opposite sides of μ(r)^⊥, both vertices of F, contradicting support. Therefore every new facet normal is θ_j, a or later τ_j. The signs are μ(θ_j)≥0 on Pσ, μ(a) zero on F/positive at a, and later μ(τ_j)≤0 on every current vertex; these are the proved ordered-root inequalities. The same calculation with a=θ_k supplies the initial cone over its lower-rank base. Thus the exact facet halfspaces bound the whole added region K_(i+1)∩μ(a)^+, establishing both inclusions rather than an unexpanded similar argument. Separating-root proof: for earlier r not θ and μ(r)·a=0, take b=R(a)r (positive and earlier than a by the finite dihedral simple-system rule), with μ(a)·b=0, μ(r)·b=1; take d=σ^-1r, positive because the inverse inversion roots are exactly the reordered θ, and prove μ(a)·d=0, μ(r)·d=-1 by Wall restriction/dual-basis identity. Ordered-root signs give d<r<a. Treat rank-one, zero-base and facet redundancy explicitly. At final index this identifies cone X(σ) with cone(Pσ) and Mσ intersect the μθ positive halfspaces, hence spherical convexity; it never asserts arbitrary moved-space intersections are meets.
