Boolean algebras encode finite logical operations, while their Stone spaces express those operations as clopen sets. This page develops ideals, filters, quotients, finite atoms, the Boolean prime ideal principle and its equivalent compactness principles. The Stone representation and duality arguments state BPI explicitly; the arbitrary-language compactness proof constructs its Henkin theory without enumerating the language, and the compact Hausdorff product proof establishes product nonemptiness before using coordinate points.

Regular-open sets give complete Boolean algebras in ZF. Under BPI they provide the order-dense Stone completion, its uniqueness, and the characterization of complete Boolean algebras by extremally disconnected Stone spaces. The extension theorem for order-continuous homomorphisms carries an explicit AC assumption and proves its one-element extension construction before applying maximality. Finally, separative quotients and the downward-open topology give forcing completions in ZF. Throughout, trivial Boolean algebras and empty Stone spaces are allowed, while forcing preorders and forcing filters are nonempty.
