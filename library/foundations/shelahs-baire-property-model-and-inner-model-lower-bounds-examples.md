The examples display the concrete computations behind the two branches. The
universal-meagre example grafts the finitely many sections of an old
nowhere-dense tree onto distinct sections of a stronger witness tree and shows
that the resulting direct extension forces the old body into a finite subunion
of the countable envelope of finite-prefix rearrangements of the generic tree,
including the level-by-level display for the singleton $\{0^\omega\}$. The
Raisonnier example produces the cofinite tails as members of $F(x)$ from the
cylinder covers of the constructible reals, and the capture example computes the
tail intersections $N_f$ for the constant block function and verifies that the
capture indices $0\in\varphi_U(n)$ are eventual rather than pointwise. The
sweet-amalgam example instantiates the amalgamation, modulus, class and complete
embedding data for two sweet models over a common complete subalgebra.

The false statement records the exact contrast between the two regularity
properties: the all-Baire-property model is equiconsistent with ZFC, while
universal measurability requires an inaccessible, and a single relative model
satisfies the first without the second.
