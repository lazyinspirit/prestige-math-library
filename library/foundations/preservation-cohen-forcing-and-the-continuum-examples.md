The examples calculate a nice name for one Cohen coordinate, the dense sets producing a Levy-collapse surjection, and the restriction/union isomorphism that makes two Cohen coordinates mutually generic.

The false statement separates the two main preservation mechanisms: Cohen forcing is ccc, but its descending sequence of longer finite strings has no common lower bound, so ccc does not imply countable closure.
