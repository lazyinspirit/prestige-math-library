The real example calculates the countable collapse of the hull generated by $\omega\cup\{\omega,x\}$ and obtains an explicit countable stage containing a constructible real. The diamond example concerns the precise antichain guessed at a sealing stage of the Suslin-tree construction. It proves its conditional stage claim directly and computes a binary-tree instance with eventually-zero branches; the separate $V=L$ theorem supplies global diamond existence.
