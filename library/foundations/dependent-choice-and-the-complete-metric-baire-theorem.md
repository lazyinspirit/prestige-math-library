Over ZF, Dependent Choice is equivalent to the Baire theorem for arbitrary complete metric spaces. The proof first reconciles the two serial-relation formulations of DC and the four category formulations of Baire. Tagged centre/radius states give the forward implication. A complete discrete sequence space, open dense successor-occurrence sets, and least-index recursion give the converse. The local arguments identify every choice use and include the empty-space and singleton cases.
