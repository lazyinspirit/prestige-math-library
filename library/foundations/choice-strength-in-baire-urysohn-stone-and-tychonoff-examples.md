Worked examples and counterexamples for the choice-strength pair. The page
computes the canonical least-ball selection in the separable Baire proof, the
finite-menu intersection in the DMC Urysohn construction, the failure of
closedness for the cofinite coordinate set, and the isolated-point repair that
recovers a choice function from a compact T1 product; it also records the false
statement that the Boolean prime ideal principle proves Stone's theorem for
metric spaces, refuted by the transferred Corson model.
