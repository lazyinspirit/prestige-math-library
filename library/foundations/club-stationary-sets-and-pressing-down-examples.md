Tails and diagonal intersections distinguish different notions of largeness. Cofinality strata supply stationary costationary sets and a direct trace computation; successor ordinals show why an unbounded domain is insufficient for pressing down. The subway argument and normal-function iteration give applications, while the countable-intersection counterexample checks the strict cofinality bound.
