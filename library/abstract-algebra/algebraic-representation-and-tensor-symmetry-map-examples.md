These worked cases exercise the A-page interfaces. The standard two-dimensional
$\mathfrak{sl}_2(\mathbb C)$ action extends uniquely to an enveloping-algebra
module. For $\mathbb C[C_2]$, the coproduct makes the tensor product of the
trivial and sign modules a sign module, and the ordinary flip intertwines this
particular pair. The counterexample shows that applying an arbitrary algebra
element separately to both tensor factors need not be additive in that
element, even for the scalar algebra $\mathbb C$. This B page verifies only
these explicit computations; general module, Lie, and bialgebra results remain
at their canonical supplier pages.
