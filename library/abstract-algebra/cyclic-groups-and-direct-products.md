The earlier development of groups, subgroups, cosets, Lagrange's theorem, and group homomorphisms provides the language used here. In particular, cyclic subgroups are already available, as are the quotient and isomorphism principles that identify familiar groups by their structure rather than by a chosen presentation.

This page will classify cyclic groups, describe their subgroups and generators, and develop external and internal direct products. It will then relate element orders to greatest common divisors and least common multiples, leading to the coprime cyclic-product criterion and the additive form of the Chinese remainder theorem.
