The symmetric group acts on its finite underlying set, and the orbit-partition theorem applies to the cyclic subgroup generated by a permutation. Finite cardinality and the count of bijections supply the size of $S_n$, while kernels of group homomorphisms supply normal subgroups. These results make cycle structure and parity available without introducing polynomial machinery.

Support and cycle type lead to the unique disjoint-cycle decomposition, the order formula, and generation by transpositions. Inversion number then defines sign; the transposition lemma proves factorisation parity is well defined and makes sign a homomorphism. The cycle-sign formula identifies even permutations, after which the alternating group is defined as the sign kernel and its normality, cardinality, and homomorphism characterisation follow.
