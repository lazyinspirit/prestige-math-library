This companion checks the defining relations on two concrete polynomial modules. The truncated quotient has dimension $p$ in characteristic $p$, while the untruncated polynomial representation exists in every characteristic and becomes nonfaithful in characteristic $p$.

## Reading prerequisite

The examples use the definitions and normal-order theorem on [[heisenberg-and-algebraic-weyl-algebras]]. They do not supply general theorems about exponentiation, unitary representations, or infinite-dimensional CCR systems.
