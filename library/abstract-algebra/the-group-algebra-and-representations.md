This page builds the finite-group representation language from the group algebra
upward. It starts at the arbitrary commutative-ring seam: the free module
$R[G]$, the augmentation map, and the dictionary between $R$-linear $G$-actions
and left $R[G]$-modules. That bridge is load-bearing for later pages, so the
representation and module viewpoints are identified explicitly rather than mixed
informally.

Once the dictionary is in place, the page turns to finite-dimensional
representations over fields: subrepresentations, intertwiners, the regular and
permutation representations, Schur's lemma, splitting fields, and the abelian
case. The endpoint is the classification of equivalence classes of degree-one
representations by homomorphisms into $k^\times$, with the abelianisation
factor visible at the statement level.
