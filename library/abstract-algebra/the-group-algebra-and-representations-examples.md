These examples keep the page concrete. They show what the dictionary and Schur
machinery look like on familiar groups: cyclic groups, $C_2$, $S_3$, coset
actions, and the quaternion group.

The false statements mark the exact boundaries the A page keeps visible. The
trivial representation shows that faithfulness is extra structure, finite group
algebras can have zero divisors, and Schur's scalar conclusion really does need
the splitting-field or algebraically closed hypotheses.
