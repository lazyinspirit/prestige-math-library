These worked examples check the A-page conventions in explicit low-dimensional
models. The symmetrized product on $M_2(\mathbb R)$ is Jordan but not
associative, and the self-adjoint part of $M_2(\mathbb C)$ need not be closed
under the original multiplication. The standard-plane spin factor gives a
three-element multiplication table and a direct square formula. The named
octonion units witness a nonzero associator, while an off-diagonal product
shows how octonion multiplication enters the Albert algebra.

[[cex-special-jordan-product-need-not-be-associative]] and
[[cex-self-adjoint-part-need-not-be-associative-subalgebra]] distinguish the
Jordan product from the original associative product. [[ex-real-two-dimensional-spin-factor]]
calculates the products and squares for $S(\mathbb R^2)$.
[[ex-octonions-are-alternative-but-not-associative]] checks the associator
$[i,j,\ell]=2k\ell$, and [[ex-albert-offdiagonal-octonion-product]] computes one
Hermitian matrix product with $k/2$ and $-k/2$ in transposed off-diagonal
entries. The induced-norm supplier gives homogeneity and continuity in the
spin-factor cone proof; the real square-root theorem supplies roots in the cone
construction. Each example is proved from its listed suppliers and coordinate
conventions.
