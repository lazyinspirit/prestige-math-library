The examples compute an exact strict decrease under qubit dephasing, a strict strong-subadditivity instance for a pure GHZ state, and a nonlinear map on a specified family of diagonal qubit states. The counterexample preserves root fidelity and contracts trace distance on that family while making a finite relative entropy infinite; it is explicitly not a quantum channel.
