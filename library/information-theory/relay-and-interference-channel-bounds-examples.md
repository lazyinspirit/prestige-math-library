These examples test the cut-dependent relay calculation, the equality case of the finite strong-interference theorem, the failure of that formula outside its regime, and a numerical complex Gaussian strong-interference region. The Gaussian relay example uses the proved continuous-alphabet NNC construction on the A page with its stated fixed quantizer. The binary examples convert the finite suppliers’ nats convention explicitly to bits; the counterexample excludes the entire time-sharing union of the wrongly generalized formula.
