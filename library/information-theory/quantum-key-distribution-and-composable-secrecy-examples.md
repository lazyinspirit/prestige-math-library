The first calculation keeps a $1/4$ abort probability and shows that the real-ideal trace distance and secrecy error are both $3/8$ when the accepted key is always $0$ but the parties agree. The second compares a uniformly secret but always mismatched key with a perfectly matched but deterministic key, showing that secrecy and correctness are distinct conditions.
