This companion computes binary type-class sizes with their polynomial factors visible, then works a finite Bernoulli upper-tail event and its asymptotic relative-entropy rate. The Bernoulli calculation includes the threshold ceiling, the all-ones endpoint, and a one-sided Hoeffding comparison under its stated Axiom of Choice assumption. The final counterexample shows why an entropy exponential alone is not an exact finite type-class count.
