These explicit rank-one calculations bind the rho shift, central character, and regular-versus-singular boundary of the localization theorems. In particular singular localization has a nonzero kernel; it is described by the proved parabolic classification rather than a claimed regular equivalence.

- [[cex-pa36-positive-twist-global-sections-fail]]: A positive integral SL2 twist is regular but not D-affine.
- [[ex-pa36-sl2-twisted-differential-operators]]: The SL2 flag charts realize the stated rho shift.
- [[ex-pa36-integral-line-bundle-translation]]: Integral line-bundle translation changes the infinitesimal character.
- [[ex-pa36-verma-delta-rho-shift]]: A point-supported SL2 module gives the shifted Verma module.
- [[cex-pa36-singular-localization-and-parabolic-kernel]]: At the singular SL2 twist global sections is not conservative.
- [[ex-pa36-finite-dimensional-integral-central-character]]: Finite-dimensional irreducible sl2 modules have exact integral central characters.
