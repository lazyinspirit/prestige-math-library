This companion collects finite computations for [[soergel-intersection-forms-and-hodge-theory]]. The rank-one example computes the trace form, the reduced module and the Lefschetz map of $B_s$ for $n=2$; the rank-two examples compute the character of $B_sB_t$ and the braid decomposition $B_sB_tB_s\cong B_{sts}\oplus B_s$, and identify the coinvariant algebra of $S_3$ with the longest-element bimodule, including its Kazhdan-Lusztig expansion.

The final example works the hard Lefschetz and Hodge-Riemann signs of the coinvariant algebra of $S_3$ in an explicit two-variable model, with the normalized top trace $\operatorname{Tr}(x^2y)=1$, and then verifies the same signs for every element of the dominant chamber.
