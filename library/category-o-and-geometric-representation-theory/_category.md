Published pages connect highest-weight representation theory with Hecke combinatorics and geometry. The collection develops Kazhdan-Lusztig bases, polynomials and cells, Soergel intersection forms and Hodge theory, projective functors and the Soergel functor, and the character and multiplicity theorem with singular and parabolic extensions. Parabolic category O supplies standard, simple, projective and tilting bases for Fock-space comparisons; Arakawa-Suzuki functors connect to higher-level Schur-Weyl duality. Other pages cover Beilinson-Bernstein localization, rational Cherednik algebras, proper cohomological descent, constructible hypercovers, and equivariant Borel-Moore convolution with constructible Ext.

Lie Theory and Representation Theory provide highest-weight and module settings; Coxeter Groups, Homological Algebra, and Algebraic Geometry supply combinatorial, derived, and geometric inputs. Hecke Algebras and Categorification uses the degenerate Ariki and KLR route developed here. Published pages also support work in Representation Theory, Hopf Algebras & Hecke Algebras, Algebraic Geometry, and Hall Algebras and Tensor Categories. Basic category O remains in Lie Theory, while braid-group Soergel applications remain in Braid Groups.
