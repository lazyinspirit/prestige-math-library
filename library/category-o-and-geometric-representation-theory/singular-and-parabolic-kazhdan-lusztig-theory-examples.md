This companion collects the finite computations for
[[singular-and-parabolic-kazhdan-lusztig-theory]]. The first two items compute
both parabolic Kazhdan–Lusztig families for the smallest nontrivial parabolic,
$S_3$ modulo $S_2$: the antispherical and spherical coefficient tables are
written out completely, and the counterexample records that they genuinely
differ ($n_{e,s_2s_1}=0$ against $m_{e,s_2s_1}=v^2$), so the two
normalizations may not be substituted for one another in a multiplicity
statement.

The remaining two items illustrate the two categories. The wall-translation
example works through $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ and $\mathfrak{sl}_3$: Vermas and
simples translate as predicted, non-minimal simple labels are killed, and the
big projective $\Xi_\mu$ together with the singular composition and
projective-standard matrices are computed explicitly in type $A_2$. The final
example inverts both the antispherical matrix of the regular parabolic block
and the spherical matrix of the singular block, writing the corresponding
character formulas term by term in $\mathfrak{sl}_3$ and showing that the
singular inversion is not the generalized-Verma character formula of the
parabolic category.
