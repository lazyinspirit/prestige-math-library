This page builds smooth partitions of unity from explicit flat and step functions, Euclidean and manifold bumps, and locally finite normalization. It then uses that machinery for smooth Urysohn separation, extension by zero, gluing of real-valued local data, compact exhaustions, and proper exhaustion functions.
