The preceding integration page supplies partitions, Darboux and tagged Riemann sums, the algebra and order estimates for proper integrals, the working fundamental theorem, integration by parts, substitution, and the integral test. Earlier real-analysis pages supply monotone functions and their one-sided limits, compactness and uniform continuity, rational powers, and complete-real convergence. Those declared dependencies are used here to pass from finite variation sums to suprema, decompose BV functions into monotone parts, and compare Stieltjes sums without importing measure theory.

The page defines total variation, its canonical positive and negative parts, and absolute continuity, then proves the Jordan decomposition and the $C^1$–Lipschitz–AC–BV hierarchy. It next defines the Riemann–Stieltjes integral and develops its criterion, estimates, algebra, integration by parts, and successively broader existence theorems through regulated approximation and the no-common-jump condition. Ordinary integration, differentiable integrators, and monotone reparametrization appear as reductions. A partition estimate finally yields Young integration for rational Hölder exponents whose sum exceeds one, followed by an explicit scope ledger.
