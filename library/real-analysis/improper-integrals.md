The proper-integration page provides oriented integrals, additivity, linearity, order and absolute-value estimates, the working FTC, integration by parts, substitution, Bonnet’s second mean value theorem, and the series integral test. Earlier pages supply one-sided and infinite limits, completeness and Cauchy convergence, monotone convergence, derivatives, rational powers, and geometric and $p$-series. These declared dependencies let compact truncations carry the argument until a finite real limit is taken at each singular end.

The page defines one-ended and mixed improper integrals, proves split-point invariance, linearity, and the Cauchy criterion, then distinguishes absolute from conditional convergence and develops bounded-primitive, comparison, limit-comparison, integral-test, and rational $p$-test machinery. Principal value is kept separate from ordinary convergence. Dirichlet and Abel tests lead to two divergence criteria. A monotone proper change-of-variable theorem is lifted to singular intervals, and Frullani’s formula is proved with a proper $1/t$ factor. The final ledger records the separate-end convention and the rational-exponent scope.
