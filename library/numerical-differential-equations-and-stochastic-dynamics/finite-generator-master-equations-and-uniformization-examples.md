These examples check the column convention in a two-state gate, compute a finite birth-death Poisson prefix with a geometric tail certificate, and track escaped mass for a countable immigration-death chain projected to two states. A final pair of finite-state laws shows that equal deterministic mean trajectories need not specify equal probability vectors.
