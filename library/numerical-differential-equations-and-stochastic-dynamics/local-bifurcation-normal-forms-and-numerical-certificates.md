This page develops the local equilibrium geometry for scalar folds, transcritical points, and symmetry-preserving pitchforks, then proves the simple Hopf branch and its stability from a parameterized center manifold and a fixed multilinear normalization. The interval procedure accepts only proof-carrying enclosures and strict nondegeneracy bounds. Its conclusions are local: an eigenvalue plot or a finite continuation trace does not establish global dynamics or branch completeness.
