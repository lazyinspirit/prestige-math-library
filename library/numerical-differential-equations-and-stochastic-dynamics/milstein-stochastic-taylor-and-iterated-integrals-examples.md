These examples verify the Milstein update for geometric Brownian motion and a bounded scalar multiplicative-noise coefficient. A two-noise system shows that a noncommuting Milstein term contains Brownian area not determined by endpoint increments, so its sampling cost and approximation error require separate treatment.
