These examples compute a diagonal stiff diffusion step with reaction, show transient growth under an exact exponential for a nonnormal matrix, evaluate the $\varphi$ functions at zero without matrix division, exhibit decay under imaginary-time propagation, and verify norm preservation for real-time skew-Hermitian evolution. The exact-action examples distinguish properties of the mathematical step from errors of a certified numerical action implementation.
