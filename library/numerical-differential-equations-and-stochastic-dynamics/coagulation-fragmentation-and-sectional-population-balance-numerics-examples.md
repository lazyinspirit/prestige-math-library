## Summary

These worked examples calculate constant-kernel number decay with conserved mass, a concrete barycentric pivot split, a binary fragmentation event, and an above-cutoff event routed to the reported reservoir. The final counterexample combines the explicit product-kernel pre-gelation moment formula with mass-one finite-grid states to show why finite-grid mass conservation cannot supply a uniform continuum second-moment bound.
