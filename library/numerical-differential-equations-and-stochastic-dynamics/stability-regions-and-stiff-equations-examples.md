These examples compute forward Euler's frequency-dependent step limit, a fast/slow diagonal system's shared step restriction, and the contrasting stiff-mode damping of backward Euler, BDF2, and trapezoidal. The counterexample shows why a scalar A-stability result does not by itself prove matrix contractivity.
