## Summary

These computations verify the fixed kick-then-drift convention for symplectic Euler, exhibit the exactly preserved positive modified quadratic form for Störmer–Verlet when $|h|<2$, and give backward Euler as an A-stable but non-symplectic method. The identities are derived from the actual step matrices. The Verlet estimate is specific to the harmonic oscillator and does not substitute for the finite-order theorem on the A page.
