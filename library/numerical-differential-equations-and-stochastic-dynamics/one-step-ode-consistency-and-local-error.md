## Summary

Define the local defect and uniform order of a one-step method, together with the one-step Lipschitz bound used to compare inputs in a compact time-state set. Prove the uniform $O(h^2)$ local defect for every admitted start time and step, and the $1+Lh$ stability bound for forward Euler under explicit $C^1$ component and Jacobian bounds. This page makes no global convergence claim and does not infer higher order from a method's name.
