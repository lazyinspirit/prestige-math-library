This page develops the scalar Milstein method from its iterated It\^o integral and proves strong order one using conditional local-defect bounds and a stochastic local-to-global recurrence. It also proves the commutative-noise reduction to Brownian increments, states where noncommuting noise needs separately sampled areas, and establishes weak order two for stochastic Heun under additive noise and bounded backward-solution derivatives. The new Milstein MLMC variance proof supplies the rate assumptions for the existing complexity theorem.
