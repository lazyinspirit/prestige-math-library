The examples distinguish simple unit roots, repeated roots strictly inside the unit disk, and repeated unit roots that create parasitic growth. They verify the constant-step BDF2 root and order conditions, show the variable-step BDF2 failure under unrestricted ratios, and compare one-pass PECE with a fully implicit Adams corrector on the scalar test equation.
