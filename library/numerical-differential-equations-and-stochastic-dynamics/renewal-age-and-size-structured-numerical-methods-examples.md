These examples show how a normalized triangular maturation profile approximates a point delay while retaining a nonzero finite-width discrepancy, how half-size division balances the discrete first size moment, and how localized fertility produces births at an incompatible initial corner without breaking positivity of the upwind update. The cutoff example uses exact cell masses and a one-cell-per-step shift to separate accumulated outflow and omitted initial tail from time-discretization error.
