These examples calculate a transverse bouncing-ball impact and its reset error, exhibit square-root guard-root location sensitivity at a grazing contact, and compare the continuations produced by two explicit priority orders for simultaneous guards. An inelastic ball has infinitely many impacts in finite time, so the finite-event hypothesis cannot be dropped. Finally, a guard with two roots between equal-sign step endpoints shows why endpoint sign screening cannot certify that no enabled event occurred.
