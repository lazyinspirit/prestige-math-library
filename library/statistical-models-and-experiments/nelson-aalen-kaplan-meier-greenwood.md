This page defines the pooled risk-set estimators with a fixed event and censoring endpoint convention. Under finite event-time support and positive event and censoring survival, a risk-set proportion argument proves pointwise consistency by induction; the Nelson–Aalen limit is the sum of discrete hazards. Under the stated continuous-time counting-process model contract, the page proves the pointwise influence expansion, its terminal normal limit, and Greenwood's first-order variance consistency. Random risk-set denominators, finite compensator energy, and positive risk support are proved or stated explicitly. Greenwood is not an exact finite-sample variance identity, and no process limit or confidence band is claimed.
