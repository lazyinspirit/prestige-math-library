## Objective

Model fixed finite-population outcomes that may respond to assignments inside a group while stating partial interference across groups as a separate design assumption. Supply the finite conditional-expectation formula and finite-partition averaging identity used by the design proofs. Define exposure mappings and the outcome-consistency condition they need; under unitwise positive exposure probabilities, prove the Horvitz–Thompson exposure mean is design-unbiased. For a fully specified two-stage complete-randomization law, define direct, indirect, total, and overall strategy effects and prove the displayed estimators unbiased without a stratified-interference premise. Treat stratified interference as an extra premise for the source variance estimators, give their heterogeneity corrections, and state the minimum sample sizes needed to make those sample-variance formulas defined. These are conditional finite-design guarantees for fixed outcomes and the declared assignment law; they do not establish that a real assignment protocol was implemented as specified or that a proposed exposure map is substantively adequate.
