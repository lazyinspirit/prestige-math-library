Work a two-point calibration example with correlated uncertain reference
standards and a separate repeatability contribution from a later reading. A
second stipulated calibration batch shows how a changed curve can affect the
reported estimate; an explicit equal-weight pooled least-squares fit shows a
separate discrepancy. The calculation is illustrative and makes no universal
process or coverage claim.
