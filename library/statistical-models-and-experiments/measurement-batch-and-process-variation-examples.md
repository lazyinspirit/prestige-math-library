Three stable reference standards are measured on two days under stipulated
linear response curves. The worked calculations recover the day-specific
slopes, fit the equally weighted pooled line, and compare its check-standard
residuals and inverse readings with the separate curves. The example is
deterministic and illustrates how a shared curve can conceal day-specific
behavior; it makes no empirical, inferential, or general stability claim.
