These examples distinguish complete-block support from incomplete blocks,
show why a Latin-square randomization restricts the assignments allowed by an
RCBD, calculate a paired contrast with heterogeneous unit effects, and exhibit
two interaction/error decompositions of the same two-by-two residual array.
The paired counterexample shows why the observed contrast's design variance
also depends on baseline outcomes.
