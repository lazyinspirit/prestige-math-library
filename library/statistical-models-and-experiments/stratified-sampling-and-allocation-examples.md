These worked finite populations compare proportional and disproportionate
allocations, show why the positive-variance hypothesis matters for the
continuous Neyman formula, and calculate how nearest-integer rounding can
violate a stratum's minimum sample size. The valid designs are checked with
the design-weighted estimator and its exact variance formula. A corrected
integer allocation must still respect each stratum's sample-size bounds; the
continuous optimum alone does not solve that constrained problem.
