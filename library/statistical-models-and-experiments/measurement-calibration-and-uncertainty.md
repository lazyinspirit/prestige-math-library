Specify the measurand and measurement model, carry correction and reference
standard uncertainty through the full input covariance matrix, and distinguish
Type A from Type B evaluation by how the information is evaluated. The
combined standard uncertainty is the standard deviation of a first-order
linearization. It does not by itself give an interval or establish that the
chosen model describes the physical measurement process.
