These worked finite designs calculate inclusion probabilities and indicator
covariance for SRSWOR, show how a certainty unit and census behave under
Horvitz–Thompson weighting, and exhibit two different totals with one identical
observed record when a unit is never sampled. Each probability is over its
declared design law, with the population values held fixed.
