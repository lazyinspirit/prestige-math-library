This page defines locally finite counting measures with multiplicities, Poisson
and Cox processes, and the scalar count law. It proves the finite mixed-binomial
construction, sigma-finite existence under countable choice and dependent
choice, Campbell and Laplace formulas, both add-one and deletion forms of
Mecke's identity with its converse, and Cox mean, Laplace, and second-moment
identities. A supplied regular conditional kernel is part of the Cox model.
Diffuse-intensity simplicity, directing-law uniqueness, and compensator theory
are outside this page's claims.
