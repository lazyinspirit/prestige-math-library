Finite examples compute a confounded observational association and the
backdoor-adjusted target, show how conditioning on a collider creates
dependence, verify a front-door formula with overlapping mediator support,
and exhibit two Markovian SCMs with the same observed law but different
intervention laws. Each conclusion is conditional on the stated SCM and its
graph; none treats the model assumptions as empirical facts.
