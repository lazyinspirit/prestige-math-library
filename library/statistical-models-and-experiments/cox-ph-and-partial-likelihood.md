This page specifies the Cox conditional event-intensity model, risk-set partial likelihood, and hazard-ratio interpretation. Its asymptotic result is conditional on independent subjects, bounded predictable covariates, positive limiting risk support, a nonsingular information matrix, a consistent score root. Integrated risk-set control is proved for arbitrary bounded predictable paths; stronger uniform-in-time control is separately qualified by its exact empirical-process hypotheses.

The estimator expansion is proved from local risk-set, Hessian, and score-martingale arguments. The counting-process martingale property is stated as a model contract. A terminal normal limit follows only after the expansion through the separate independent-subject result. Neither the model nor its fitted hazard ratio establishes empirical adequacy or causality.
