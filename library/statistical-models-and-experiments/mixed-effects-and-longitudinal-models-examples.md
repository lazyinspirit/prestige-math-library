## Examples

A random intercept yields an intraclass correlation when the total variance is positive. Two visits can leave random-slope and residual covariance parameters aliased even with a full-rank fixed design. A finite random-intercept model can also have an outcome-dependent dropout mechanism, showing that the complete-data covariance structure alone does not make dropout MAR.
