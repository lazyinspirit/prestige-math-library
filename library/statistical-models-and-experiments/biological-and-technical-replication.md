This page distinguishes biological, assignment, and technical measurement
units, then proves the mean and variance of a balanced average under a
two-level random-intercept model. Its variance identity separates variation
between independent biological units from technical measurement error. The
formula requires the stated common variances and mutual-independence
assumptions; the names of replicate types do not imply those assumptions. The
result is a design-model calculation, not a normal approximation, a guarantee
of representativeness, or evidence that the model fits an empirical study.
