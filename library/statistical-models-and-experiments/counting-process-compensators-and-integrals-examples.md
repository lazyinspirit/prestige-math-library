These examples check the one-event exponential compensator and its step
integral isometry, use the left-open entry convention for a delayed-entry
risk process, and show that a shared event jump creates a nonzero cross
bracket. The delayed-entry calculation verifies the stated exponential model;
it does not claim informative entry is ignorable. The tied-jump example
identifies the precise hypothesis needed for orthogonality.
