## Examples

The first example computes both tails of a two-sided test under a specified positive shift. The second uses exact normal quantiles to obtain a least sample size and then shows how halving the effect or increasing the standard deviation changes power.

The oracle counterexample distinguishes an extensional empty-set answer from what finitely many exact power queries can establish, even under monotone feasibility. Model-specific analytic formulas and valid global certificates remain available.
