This page defines the record needed to interpret a scientific statistical
analysis: its observation law, units and indices, target, observation
mechanism, support, conditioning convention, and the status of its adequacy
assumptions. The contract separates what follows mathematically from a
specified model from the empirical question of whether that model describes
the scientific setting.
