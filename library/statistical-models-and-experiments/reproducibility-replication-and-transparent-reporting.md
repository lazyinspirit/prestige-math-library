This page states the National Academies' convention for computational
reproducibility, study replicability, and generalizability, and defines
transparent reporting as an explicit record of relevant data, methods,
curation, analysis decisions, computational conditions, and uncertainty.
These distinctions are definitions and reporting recommendations; they do not
guarantee correctness or future replication. The companion example separates a
same-data grouping change from a second study using new data.
