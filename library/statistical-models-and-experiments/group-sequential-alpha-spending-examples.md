These worked two-look designs use the Pocock-type and O’Brien–Fleming-type
spending curves under the same canonical Gaussian covariance. They calculate
the first boundary from its marginal null law and define the final boundary
as the unique root of the joint crossing equation. Both examples calibrate
their symmetric two-sided absolute-value crossings directly.
