This page distinguishes censoring after sampling from truncation before sample inclusion. It defines right, left, interval, and current-status observations and gives likelihood factors only under explicit observation laws. Independent censoring, noninformative inspection, and entry positivity remain assumptions of the stated model; the proofs do not establish that an empirical study satisfies them. The continuous event-time likelihood is separated from the general interval-mass and atom formulas. Informative censoring or inspection requires a different joint model, and no general interval-censored NPMLE is asserted.
