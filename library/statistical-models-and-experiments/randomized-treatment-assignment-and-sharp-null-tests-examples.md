The examples calculate a two-sided test under unequal assignment
probabilities with tied scores, showing the conservative weighted p-value and
the exact uniform value obtained by randomizing within the atom. A separate
two-unit schedule has zero [[def-finite-population-average-treatment-effect-and-weak-null]]
while each unit's outcome changes, demonstrating why the Fisher sharp null
is a stronger hypothesis.
