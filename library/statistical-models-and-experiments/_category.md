---
title: Statistical Models and Experiments
---
These pages formalize statistical models for design, regression, causal inference,
measurement, survival, time series, spatial data, and sequential tests. The scope includes
fixed and generalized linear models, mixed models, factorial and randomized designs,
finite-population estimators, missingness and measurement error, potential outcomes and
instrumental variables, censoring and point processes, calibration and reproducibility.
Multiple-testing control, selective inference, confidence procedures, and dependence-aware
summaries connect the models to practical study design.

Probability, Measure Theory, Real Analysis, Linear Algebra, Mathematical Statistics, and
Combinatorics provide foundations for model laws, estimands, and design calculations.
Bayesian Computational Statistics uses the experiment and distribution models; Numerical
Differential Equations and Stochastic Dynamics uses survival and point-process models;
Numerical Linear Algebra and Approximation uses time-series prediction. Causal and design
results serve Inverse Problems and Numerical Optimization Inverse Problems and Uncertainty
Quantification. Distribution results also appear in Information Theory, Neural Networks and
Learning Dynamics, Machine Learning Algorithms, and Statistical Learning and Classical Models; ceiling and integer-threshold facts
are used in Quantum Algorithms and Numerical Pdes and Spatial Discretization.
