Finite laws show that strict response positivity can coexist with arbitrarily
large weighted-contribution variance as an observation probability approaches
zero. At a positivity failure, two full-data laws can give the same observed
records but different means. A location-shift sensitivity curve likewise
keeps the observed law fixed while changing the full-data mean; the resulting
range depends on the stated sensitivity model and is not a confidence interval.
