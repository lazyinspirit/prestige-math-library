The examples compare constant exponential and increasing Weibull hazards, work
the finite-atom survival product, and calculate an exponential likelihood from
event and censored exposure times. A second calculation separates the
no-failure, positive-exposure boundary supremum from a zero-exposure data set,
where the likelihood is constant. These are model calculations, not evidence
that a physical failure process follows either parametric family or censoring
assumption.
