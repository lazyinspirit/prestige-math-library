Method agreement compares paired measurements of the same target under a
declared population of measurement conditions. The signed difference has a
population bias and spread; under an exact normal-difference model, its central
95% quantiles are the bias plus or minus the standard-normal 97.5th percentile
times the spread. The customary 1.96 display is rounded. Sample plug-in limits
are point estimates, not endpoint confidence intervals. Repeated measurements
need their within-target dependence stated, and correlation alone does not
establish agreement or calibration.
