The examples show the deterministic rank obstruction when the sample size does not exceed the dimension, a numerical distinction between the exact Wishart trace and the covariance likelihood ratio, and a two-dimensional Hotelling calculation with its exact $F$ cutoff. The likelihood-ratio example explicitly avoids inferring a finite-sample null law from an observed statistic; the Hotelling example keeps the test decision conditional on the selected significance level.
