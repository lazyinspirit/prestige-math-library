This page defines a prescheduled group-sequential design under a stipulated
canonical Gaussian simple-null law and proves the recursive boundaries exist
uniquely. The cumulative rejection probability is calibrated to the chosen
nondecreasing alpha-spending function at each planned information fraction.
The proof treats one-sided upper boundaries and separately calibrated
symmetric two-sided crossing events. Exactness depends on the specified joint
law and planned looks; data-dependent timing, estimated variance, and
composite-null guarantees require additional calibration arguments.
