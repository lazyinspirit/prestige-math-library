The examples compute a two-factor interaction on the raw and logarithmic response scales, then add one unequal replicate to show how the weighted design matrix loses orthogonality while still sending only the zero coefficient vector to zero. These calculations distinguish the balanced sign-design identities from assumptions about response additivity or error distributions.
