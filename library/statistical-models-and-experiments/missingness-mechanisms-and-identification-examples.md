Finite response mechanisms make the MCAR/MAR/MNAR distinctions concrete.
Two full-data laws can agree on the response rate, observed success mass, and
entire observed-record distribution while having different population means.
A separate example distinguishes an unrecorded outcome from an outcome that
has no value after a competing event such as death.
