The examples keep participant, visit, and technical-reading indices distinct
and state the probability law used for inference. A second example shows
algebraically how an unvalidated measurement offset can make the scientific
target nonidentifiable from the observed law. Neither example treats a
mathematical model specification as evidence that its assumptions hold in a
physical setting.
