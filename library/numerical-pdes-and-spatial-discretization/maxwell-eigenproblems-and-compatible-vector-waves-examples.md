cluster. The spherical-shell example exhibits a topology-dependent zero mode
that must be separated from positive eigenvalues by the full kernel gauge.

[[ex-na-nodal-curlcurl-spurious-gradient-mode]] on the prerequisite
[[discrete-de-rham-complexes-and-edge-elements-examples]] page; that example
is linked as context and is not duplicated here.
