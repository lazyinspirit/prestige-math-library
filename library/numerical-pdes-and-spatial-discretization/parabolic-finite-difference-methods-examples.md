The examples isolate the forward-Euler endpoint and instability above its CFL bound, contrast backward-Euler damping with Crank–Nicolson's weak damping of the highest mode, and compare the continuous and discrete maximum bounds on a smooth heat solution. Fourier conclusions are stated on periodic grids; the continuous and discrete boundary bounds use their own hypotheses.
