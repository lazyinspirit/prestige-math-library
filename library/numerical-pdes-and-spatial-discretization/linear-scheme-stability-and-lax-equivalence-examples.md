The examples use a diagonal heat semigroup on $c_0$ to compare stable and
unstable explicit spectral-grid steps, then exhibit a mesh-dependent grid norm
that keeps grid powers bounded while the reconstructed common-space operators
are unbounded. Each example checks the declared maps, norms, and local defect
directly.
