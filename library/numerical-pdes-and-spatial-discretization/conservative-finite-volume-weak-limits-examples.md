These examples verify the stationary Burgers shock speed under the Godunov
update, exhibit uniformly bounded cell reconstructions with no strongly
convergent subsequence, and show a bounded strongly convergent conservative
Godunov sequence whose weak limit violates a Kruzhkov entropy inequality. The
last scheme uses $\tau/h=2$, outside the usual CFL regime; it does not contradict
entropy-convergence results with their stability hypotheses.
