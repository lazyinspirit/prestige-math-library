The examples compute one oriented Nedelec edge circulation and its face curl flux, exhibit a zero-curl nodal vector mode outside the compatible nodal-gradient image, and give an azimuthal curl-free field with nonzero circulation on a solid torus. The nodal example diagnoses a mismatch in that chosen finite element pair; the handle example shows why a cohomology or harmonic component must be retained on noncontractible domains.
