## Examples

The finite calculations verify a rest lake with a dry crest, a first HLL step that wets a neighboring dry cell while preserving water, and a concrete central-flux/centered-source update that creates a spurious momentum from a discrete lake. The counterexample distinguishes exact well-balancedness of the specified hydrostatic reconstruction from any general claim about centered source approximations.
