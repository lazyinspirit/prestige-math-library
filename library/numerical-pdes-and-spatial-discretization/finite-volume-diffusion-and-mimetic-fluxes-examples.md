These examples calculate an exact shared flux on a two-cell rectangular Voronoï mesh, exhibit a nonorthogonal face where the naive two-point formula produces a spurious transverse flux, and check the affine-exact mimetic reconstruction on a skew parallelogram with an anisotropic tensor. A final one-dimensional interface calculation compares harmonic and arithmetic coefficient averages and verifies the exact resistance-weighted flux across a discontinuous positive coefficient.
