These examples exercise the local scope and its failure boundaries. The
acoustic system gives an exact two-wave composition, and the ideal-gas contact
has equal pressure and velocity but different densities. The Sod data have a
unique pressure root bracketed by their initial pressures, with a left
rarefaction and right shock. Two exact velocity choices demonstrate the
vacuum-threshold equality and strict-vacuum cases. The Burgers counterexample
shows that an exact Roe flux-jump identity and weak Rankine–Hugoniot balance do
not imply the convex entropy inequality.
