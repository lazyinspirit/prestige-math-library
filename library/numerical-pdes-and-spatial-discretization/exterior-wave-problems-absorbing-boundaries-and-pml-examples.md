These examples evaluate the exact outgoing monopole and spherical DtN symbol, then show the normal-to-grazing loss of impedance and PML attenuation. The final one-dimensional Dirichlet cavity gives an explicit near-resonant response as accumulated damping vanishes. It is a finite continuous one-mode calculation and carries no general PML or discretization stability claim.
