These examples calculate the exact effective tensor for a layered scalar medium, show that its condition number can grow with contrast, evaluate a conditional FE-HMM error ledger with rational bounds, and calculate a nonzero one-dimensional resonance error at ratio $3/2$. Each example states the precise coefficient, ratio, and hypotheses it uses. The ledger’s bounds are conditional certificates and do not assert a general FE-HMM rate.
