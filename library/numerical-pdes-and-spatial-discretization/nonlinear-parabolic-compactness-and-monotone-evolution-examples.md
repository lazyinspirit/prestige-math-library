These examples derive a finite-propagation porous-medium profile, compute a
one-dimensional failure of linear Ritz commutation with the nonlinear
$3$-Laplacian, and show that temporal oscillations prevent strong
$L^2(0,T;H)$ compactness without derivative control. The $p$-Laplacian
example verifies growth, coercivity, monotonicity and continuity of the
induced flux, and instantiates convergence on the explicit interval or
unit-cube mesh family from the A page. Its dimension and strict $p$ range
are unchanged.

Every calculation is proved in its carrier. The profile example makes no
initial Dirac-mass or mass-conservation claim. The Ritz counterexample tests
only the displayed one-dimensional residual, while the oscillation example
separates the $L^p(V)$ bound from time compactness.
