---
title: Numerical Pdes and Spatial Discretization
---
These pages analyze spatial discretizations for elliptic, parabolic, hyperbolic, wave, flow,
elasticity, Maxwell, transport, reaction-diffusion, and Hamilton–Jacobi problems. Methods
include finite differences, finite elements and spectral elements, discontinuous Galerkin,
finite volumes, mixed and mimetic schemes, boundary and volume integrals, meshfree methods,
and particle-in-cell models. Results address interpolation and mesh transfer, stability,
conservation, entropy, adaptivity, multigrid and domain decomposition, multiscale
homogenization, and convergence under stated regularity and geometry assumptions.
Finite-element, finite-volume, and DG analyses make their interpolation, stability, and mesh
hypotheses explicit.

Partial Differential Equations, Real Analysis, Functional Analysis, Linear Algebra, Measure
Theory, and Numerical Linear Algebra and Approximation supply weak-form, operator, and
discrete-analysis foundations. Numerical Differential Equations and Stochastic Dynamics
contributes grid and time-stepping results; Numerical Optimization Inverse Problems and
Uncertainty Quantification uses PDE-constrained optimization and spectral bounds.
Specialized results also connect these pages to Complex Analysis, Differential Geometry,
Fourier Analysis, and Probability. A ceiling bound from Statistical Models and Experiments
enters monotone Hamilton–Jacobi–Bellman convergence. Variational and evolution estimates
support Inverse Problems, Neural Networks and Learning Dynamics, and Representation Learning
and Generative Models.
