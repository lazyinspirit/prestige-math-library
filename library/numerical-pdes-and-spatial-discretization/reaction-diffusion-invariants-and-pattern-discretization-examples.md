The examples calculate a Brusselator instability on a continuous torus, show how a three-node periodic grid suppresses that unstable mode, verify the Fisher–KPP forward-Euler interval bound before exact TPFA diffusion, and give a three-node consistent-mass P1 counterexample to nodal positivity. The Turing examples make linear mode claims only; the finite-element calculation does not assert failure of mass-lumped methods.
