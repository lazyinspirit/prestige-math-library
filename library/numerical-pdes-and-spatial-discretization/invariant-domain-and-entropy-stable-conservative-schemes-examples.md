These worked cases exercise the exact assumptions of the companion page:
near-vacuum Euler states on the symmetric HLL speed branch, a two-point
Burgers entropy residual, a finite-node conservative positivity rescaling, and
a coarse Burgers shock update that shows why semidiscrete entropy stability
does not promise sharp shock resolution. The last example reports its
forward-Euler state calculation without asserting a fully discrete entropy
inequality.
