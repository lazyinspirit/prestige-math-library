The examples compute a four-triangle heat-mesh case where the $L^2$ and Ritz
initial projections differ, verify the semidiscrete Ritz residual directly
on the same one-dimensional trial space, and exhibit a scalar solution with
$H^1$ but not $H^2$ time regularity whose first Crank–Nicolson nodal error is
of order $\tau^{7/4}$. These are explicit checks of the separate initial-data,
Ritz-residual, and time-regularity conditions in the main page.
