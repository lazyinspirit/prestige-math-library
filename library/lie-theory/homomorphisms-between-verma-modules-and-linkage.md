Singular vectors control maps out of Verma modules. The PBW and Jantzen arguments here supply the ingredients for the directed strong-linkage criterion proved after finite length on the Category O page. Central-character coincidence remains only a necessary coarse condition.

The general strong-linkage and BGG homomorphism statements assume AC where
they use the central-character and finite-length results. Direct PBW
calculations retain the hypotheses stated in their individual items.
