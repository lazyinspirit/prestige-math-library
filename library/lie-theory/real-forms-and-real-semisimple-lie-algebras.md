This page develops the real forms of complex semisimple Lie algebras:
complexification and the canonical conjugation, the bijection between real forms
and conjugate-linear involutions, compact and split real forms, Cartan
involutions and Cartan decompositions, the maximal compact subgroups, restricted
roots and restricted Weyl groups, Iwasawa decompositions, theta-stable Cartan
subalgebras with Cayley transforms, Vogan and Satake diagrams, and the resulting
classification of real semisimple Lie algebras together with the classical
real-form list. The normalized real structure constants the existence theorems
use are supplied locally by
[[lem-chevalley-basis-and-real-structure-constants]].
