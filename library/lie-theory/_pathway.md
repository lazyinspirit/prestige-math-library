## foundations-and-applications

The published foundations begin with groups and their infinitesimal structure, then develop Lie-algebra structure and highest weights. Compact groups and real forms connect the algebraic and geometric branches. The advanced track develops central characters, category O and Kac–Moody representation theory; draft pages retain their status.
