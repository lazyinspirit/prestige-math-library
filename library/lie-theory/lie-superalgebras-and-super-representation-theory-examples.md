This companion records worked examples whose proofs use the A-page definitions. Its examples test the super-PBW relation, a model-specific highest-weight boundary, failure of general semisimplicity, and the N=1 supercharge bracket.

## Examples

- [[ex-odd-subalgebra-of-gl-1-1-has-exterior-enveloping-algebra]] verifies that the odd generator with zero self-bracket squares to zero and that the resulting quotient has basis $1,e$, using the local super-PBW theorem.
- [[ex-typicality-boundary-for-gl-1-1-highest-weight-modules]] proves that the fixed two-dimensional $\mathfrak{gl}(1|1)$ module is simple exactly when $a+b\ne0$, relative to the declared graded-submodule convention.
- [[cex-finite-dimensional-lie-supermodules-need-not-be-semisimple]] gives a finite-dimensional nonsplit example. It refutes universal semisimplicity in the graded supermodule category; it makes no claim about ordinary Lie-algebra modules.
- [[ex-complexified-four-dimensional-n1-super-poincare-spinor-bracket]] writes the odd bracket in a Pauli basis and identifies translations. Dotted indices label the second chiral summand in the complexification, with no conjugation or reality condition implied.

The N=1 example is purely algebraic. The physics dossier notes that a Wess–Zumino field realization closes on shell; no field-theory or off-shell closure claim is made here. Ordinary PBW and ordinary highest-weight classification are not used to establish any of these examples.
