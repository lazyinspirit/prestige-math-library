Starting from the Verma-module construction and PBW model on the Harish–Chandra page, this page proves injectivity and uniqueness statements for homomorphisms, then uses the normalized bilinear contravariant form to detect the unique maximal submodule.  The determinant is a block invariant, so its equality is always
understood up to a nonzero basis scalar.
