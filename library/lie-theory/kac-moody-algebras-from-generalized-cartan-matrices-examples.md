These calculations test the Cartan–Serre construction at its boundaries: rank one, the six roots of $A_2$, a singular affine realization, and a negative-norm imaginary root. A small asymmetric-zero matrix isolates the GCM zero-pattern hypothesis. The loop-$\mathfrak{sl}_3$ counterexample computes every nonzero imaginary multiple of $\delta$ and its two-dimensional root space directly, without using a later affine-algebra theorem.
