The examples fix every Fourier sign and normalization on delta, constants,
plane waves, derivatives, monomials, principal value, and the lattice comb.
The elementary fundamental solution shows exactly what division by a
nonvanishing Fourier symbol can accomplish without asserting a general PDE
existence theorem.

Two closing obstructions mark the boundary of the calculus: no compatible
associative differential algebra can multiply all distributions in the naive
way, and even the two constant tempered distributions have no ordinary
convolution.  Paley–Wiener theory and microlocal analysis are identified as
later subjects, not imported as unproved prerequisites.
