The worked example computes two state quotient models on $M_n(\mathbb C)$:
the first-coordinate vector state gives the defining representation on
$\mathbb C^n$, while normalized trace gives left multiplication on the
normalized Hilbert-Schmidt space $M_n(\mathbb C)$. For $n\ge2$, the trace
state is non-extreme and its GNS representation has a proper invariant
subspace; $n=1$ is recorded as the boundary case.

[[ex-vector-and-tracial-states-on-matrix-algebras]] proves the state bounds,
quotient identifications, cyclicity, and reducibility directly from the matrix
formulas using the first-variable-linear convention fixed on the A page.
