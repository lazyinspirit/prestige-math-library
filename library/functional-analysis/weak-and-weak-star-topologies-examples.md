These examples separate nearby convergence notions by explicit calculations. Coordinate vectors in ell-p for finite p greater than one are weakly null, while summation detects their failure at p equal to one. The specified predual changes weak-star convergence: coordinate vectors distinguish it from weak convergence on the dual of c0.

The square-root coordinate set has a weak closure point that no sequence in the set approaches weakly. Shift powers distinguish weak operator, strong operator and operator norm convergence. Finally, finite-support coordinate maps expose the completeness hypothesis in uniform boundedness, and a net carrying its own witness point converges weakly while its norms tend to infinity. The latter construction requires no neighborhood-indexed choice function.
