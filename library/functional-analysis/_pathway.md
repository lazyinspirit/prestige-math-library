## foundations

The track separates linear normed-space structure from Banach completeness. Closed
subspaces, products, series, and completion provide ambient constructions; bounded maps,
quotient norms, openness, quotients, and complemented kernels or ranges develop operator
theory. In finite dimension all norms are equivalent, subspaces are closed, and maps are
bounded, while Riesz's lemma witnesses infinite-dimensional noncompactness. Hahn--Banach
extends sublinearly dominated functionals, including over complex scalars through real
parts, and yields norming functionals and dual separation. Its geometric form uses a
Minkowski gauge: one open convex set gives strict separation, and a closed convex set and a
disjoint compact convex set are strongly separated. Annihilators detect closure and density,
complement finite directions, identify closed hyperplanes, and show that convex weak and
norm closures agree.
