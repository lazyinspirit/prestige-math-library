## foundations

The track separates linear normed-space structure from Banach completeness. Closed subspaces,
products, series, completion, bounded maps, and quotient norms develop operator theory.
Finite-dimensional norm equivalence, Riesz's lemma, and, under DC, Baire category lead to the
open-mapping, bounded-inverse, and closed-graph theorems. Under the stated Hahn--Banach
principle, norm-preserving extension gives norming functionals and an isometric canonical map
into the bidual. Its geometric branch uses the Minkowski gauge for separation, with separate
hypotheses for strict and uniform conclusions. The dual-spaces page treats annihilators,
transpose and bidual maps, closed range, and norm versus weak-star closure for $c_0$ and
$\ell^1$.
