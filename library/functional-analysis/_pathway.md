## foundations

The track separates linear normed-space structure from Banach completeness. Closed subspaces,
products, series, completion, bounded maps, and quotient norms develop operator theory.
Finite-dimensional norm equivalence, Riesz's lemma, and Baire category under DC lead to the
open-mapping, bounded-inverse, and closed-graph theorems. Sequential uniform boundedness instead
uses $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$: near-norming vectors and a geometric-tail argument turn unbounded
operator norms into a pointwise contradiction. Hahn--Banach supplies norming functionals, bidual
maps, and geometric separation; the final page treats annihilators, transpose, closed range, and
norm versus weak-star closure for $c_0$ and $\ell^1$.
