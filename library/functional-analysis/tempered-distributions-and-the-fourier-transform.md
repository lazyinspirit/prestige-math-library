Tempered distributions are the continuous bilinear dual of Schwartz space.
The finite-seminorm criterion makes that continuity usable, while restriction
to compactly supported tests and extension of compactly supported
distributions relate the theory precisely to ordinary distributions.  Weak
and strong dual topologies are kept distinct throughout.

With the negative-sign, $2\pi$-normalized Fourier convention, transposition
extends the Schwartz transform to a topological automorphism of
$\mathcal S'$.  The resulting calculus includes derivatives, polynomial and
slowly increasing multipliers, elementary transforms, and the unit-lattice
Dirac comb.  The final results prove smoothness of $\mathcal S'*\mathcal S$,
handle convolution by a compactly supported distribution, and state only the
Fourier product and convolution identities for which every operation is
defined.
