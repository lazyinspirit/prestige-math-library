This page builds on [[complexification-realification-and-real-structures]] and [[rn-as-a-normed-space]]. It defines complex norms with absolute homogeneity for every complex scalar, identifies complex modulus on the real embedding, and proves that realification preserves the norm and its induced metric. It also verifies the finite coordinate sum norm, defines restricted complex norms on subspaces, and fixes the real-valued comparison convention for equivalent complex norms.
