Explicit group-algebra calculations determine the blocks and Brauer pairs of $A_4$ in characteristic three and the blocks of $S_3$ in characteristics two and three. A characteristic-two example in $C_3\rtimes D_8$ computes three local blocks at $C_4$ and shows why only the stable one lies below the indicated $D_8$-pair. Matrix units and local truncated-polynomial algebras verify primitivity and the defect-zero and full-defect boundaries.
