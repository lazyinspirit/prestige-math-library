For a finite group $G$ and a normal subgroup $N$, translation organizes the restriction of an irreducible complex representation into one orbit of normal isotypical components. Selecting one component replaces $G$ by its inertia group, and induction reconstructs the original module. The two directions are established separately before the character correspondence and its ramification identities.

All modules are finite-dimensional complex left modules. Conjugation is ${}^g\theta(n)=\theta(g^{-1}ng)$ and cosets are left cosets. The final correspondence assumes an actual extension to inertia and proves the quotient parametrization through multiplicity spaces.
