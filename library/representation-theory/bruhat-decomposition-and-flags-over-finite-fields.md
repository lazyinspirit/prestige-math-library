This page develops the structure of the finite general linear group
$\operatorname{GL}_n(\mathbb F_q)$ through its standard subgroups and its
action on flags. It fixes the standard Borel subgroup $B$, the diagonal torus
$T$ and the maximal unipotent subgroup $U$, identifies the complete flags of
$\mathbb F_q^n$ with the coset space $G/B$, and introduces compositions,
partial flags and the standard parabolics $P_\alpha=L_\alpha\ltimes U_\alpha$
with their Levi decompositions.

On that base it treats the Weyl group $W=N/T\cong S_n$ with its inversion
length, proves that Gaussian elimination produces a pivot permutation, and
derives the Bruhat decomposition $G=\bigsqcup_{\sigma\in S_n}BP_\sigma B$
together with the classification of pairs of complete flags by their relative
position and the cardinality $q^{\ell(\sigma)}$ of a Bruhat cell. The
southwest rank matrices determine both the double coset and the relative
position.

The second half passes to the Harish-Chandra functors $R_L^G$ and
${}^*\!R_L^G$, the exactness of the unipotent invariants over $\mathbb C$, and
the adjunction between induction and restriction. It classifies the
double cosets of two standard parabolics, records the balanced-product form of
the parabolic Mackey formula, proves that formula for finite $\operatorname{GL}_n$,
establishes transitivity of Harish-Chandra induction together with its
independence of the chosen parabolic, and closes with cuspidal support and the
Harish-Chandra series whose existence and uniqueness follow from a
Levi with least block statistic and the parabolic Mackey formula.
