These examples accompany
[[sl2-r-principal-and-complementary-series]]. They compute explicit Iwasawa
coordinates and Haar density, tabulate the first even and odd $K$-types and
their ladder arrows, and evaluate initial intertwiner eigenvalue ratios in the
spherical complementary range. The counterexample exhibits the loss of
positivity beyond the unitary interval.
