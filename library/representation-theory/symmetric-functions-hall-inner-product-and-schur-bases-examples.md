These examples calculate the distinctions among integral and rational symmetric-function bases in degree two and degree three. They also expand the Cauchy kernel through bidegree three and contract its degree-three component against a Schur function.

The final example computes the skew Jacobi–Trudi determinant for $(3,1)/(1)$ and checks directly that its two edge-disconnected components contribute the product $h_2h_1$.
