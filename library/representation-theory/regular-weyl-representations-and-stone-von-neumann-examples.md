This companion page records boundary examples for
[[regular-weyl-representations-and-stone-von-neumann]]. Its examples verify
the one-mode phase, show why the nonzero central parameter and regularity
hypotheses matter, and exhibit two inequivalent irreducible line-regular
representations in infinitely many modes.

## Examples

- [[ex-zero-central-character-has-many-regular-characters]] gives inequivalent
  one-dimensional regular Weyl systems at central parameter zero.
- [[ex-one-mode-schrodinger-weyl-relations]] checks the translation and
  modulation formulas against the fixed Weyl phase convention.
- [[cex-universal-weyl-c-star-algebra-has-nonregular-representations]]
  extends the twisted left-regular representation to the universal completion
  and proves failure of strong continuity directly.
- [[cex-infinite-mode-regular-weyl-representations-are-inequivalent]]
  assumes Countable Choice, constructs two infinite tensor product
  representations, proves each is irreducible and regular on every
  finite-support line, and distinguishes them by local Gaussian projections.

## Scope

Under Countable Choice, the infinite-mode example uses the algebraic direct sum
$c_{00}(\mathbb N;\mathbb R^2)$ and proves only that the two displayed
representations are inequivalent. It makes no classification claim. The
nonregular example concerns the universal C*-algebra built from a discrete
phase-space group. No example identifies an unbounded commutator on a whole
Hilbert space or removes its domain requirements.

The canonical physics-tree examples of nonregular Weyl states and unbounded
CCR operators remain cross-references only: this batch has no accepted
math-domain dependency route to those carriers. The examples here have
self-contained mathematical proofs and do not depend on them.
