**Objective.** This companion page works the smallest nontrivial instance of
the equivariant constructible calculus of
[[equivariant-constructible-perverse-foundations]]: the multiplicative group
$\mathbb C^\times$ acting trivially on a point. The finite Stiefel
approximations identify the equivariant Ext groups with the cohomology of the
complex projective spaces $\mathbb P^{N-1}$ in the stable range, so
$\operatorname{Ext}_G^m(k,k)$ is $k$ in every nonnegative even degree and zero
otherwise: degreewise finite, with infinite-dimensional total Ext, and the
forgetful restriction to the ordinary point is not derived fully faithful.
