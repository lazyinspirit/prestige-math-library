For a finite group in characteristic zero, character values need not determine a
field in which matrices can be chosen.  The Schur index measures precisely this
failure of descent: it is the common multiplicity in the Galois orbit after
scalar extension, the index of an endomorphism division algebra, and the least
multiplicity that becomes realizable over the character field.
