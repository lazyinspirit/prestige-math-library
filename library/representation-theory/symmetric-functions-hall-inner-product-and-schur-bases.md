This page develops the stable graded ring of symmetric functions from compatible finite-rank specializations. It establishes the integral monomial, elementary/complete, and Schur bases, while identifying the power sums as a basis only over $\mathbb Q$. The Hall form is defined through the dual $h_\lambda,m_\mu$ bases; the Cauchy kernel then gives power-sum orthogonality and Schur orthonormality.

The page also proves Jacobi–Trudi and dual Jacobi–Trudi, describes the involution exchanging elementary and complete functions, and defines skew Schur functions by Hall adjointness. Skew determinants and semistandard tableau expansions lead to the dominance-unitriangular Kostka change of basis.
