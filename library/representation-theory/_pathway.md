## representation-theory

Finite-group methods lead from characters and induced characters through Brauer's modular theory to blocks, defect groups and the Green correspondence, while the modular function and $L^1$ group algebras open the analytic half. There, unitary representations, positive type and GNS, complete reducibility and Peter–Weyl, induced representations and Mackey imprimitivity, group C*-algebras and the Fell dual develop the compact and locally compact theory; amenability, direct integral decomposition into factor and type-I representations, and Kazhdan's property (T) supply structural results. The $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)$ pages compute the principal, complementary and discrete series and the irreducible unitary dual. Geometrically, equivariant constructible perverse foundations and quiver full-flag incidence bundles with their compactifications support correspondence convolution realizing the signed KLR algebra as an equivariant Ext algebra.
