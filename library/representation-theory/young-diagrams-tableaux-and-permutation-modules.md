This page sets up the combinatorial and permutation-module apparatus of the
symmetric group. It fixes the English Young diagram convention, conjugation of
partitions, tableaux and standard tableaux, removable and addable nodes, the
dominance order with the conjugation-reversal equivalence, and the row and
column stabilizers of a tableau.

On that base it proves the basic row-column incidence lemma and introduces
Young subgroups, tabloids and the Young permutation module $M^\lambda$, which
is identified with the permutation module on the coset space
$S_n/S_\lambda$ and hence with the induced trivial module, together with the
conjugation formula for stabilizers. The page closes with semistandard
tableaux and Kostka numbers, the input for Young's rule. Specht modules,
irreducibility, branching and the hook length formula are later pages.
