These examples accompany [[kazhdans-property-t-and-spectral-gap]]. A compact group has property (T) with an explicit compact Kazhdan set, and every finite group is a compact special case. In contrast, $\mathbb Z$ has no property (T): characters arbitrarily close to the trivial character on any prescribed finite test set have no invariant vector ([[cex-z-does-not-have-property-t]]).

For $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)$, the spherical complementary series approaches the trivial class in the Fell topology as its parameter tends to the endpoint, while positive weights preserve a nonzero even K-mode with a nontrivial character in each irreducible Hilbert completion, excluding invariant vectors. The direct-sum construction on the A page turns these near-invariant vectors into a single witness to failure of property (T) ([[cex-sl2-r-complementary-series-destroys-property-t]]).
