This page builds the unitary positive-energy representations of connected covers of the proper orthochronous Poincaré group. It fixes the mostly-plus metric, translation-character and spectral-PVM convention, proves the full Lorentz orbit and cover-stabilizer data, and supplies the measurable Mackey induction classification for the entire Euclidean translation dual. The resulting theorem treats future timelike, nonzero future null and zero momentum in every spacetime dimension $D\ge2$, including the full $D=3$ cover family, the two $D=2$ future null rays, and all Lorentz-cover representations at zero momentum. Central-kernel descent is stated separately. The page assumes AC through its Mackey, spectral and induction suppliers. It makes no claim about parity, time reversal, spin-statistics, or quantum-field-theoretic particle interpretation.
