These examples compute characteristic formulas in explicit transport models,
show how characteristic data can fail on a tangent surface, and illustrate the
second-order classification on the Laplace, heat, wave, and Tricomi operators.
The closing counterexample records both failure modes of the naive global
trichotomy: mixed-type equations and higher-order equations.
