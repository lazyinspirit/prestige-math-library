Starting with a quasilinear Cauchy problem, this page separates ODE solvability
from the inverse-projection step that actually constructs a PDE graph.  It
then makes projection failure precise through Burgers caustics, develops both
Charpit invariants for fully nonlinear equations, and records the analytic
Cauchy–Kovalevskaya theorem exactly without using its omitted proof.
