These examples calculate Hochschild chains over the ground field, the one-variable polynomial algebra with regular and twisted coefficients, and the two-variable diagonal Koszul complex. The computations show how commutator maps determine homology, how the regular coefficient case has zero Koszul differential, and how the ordered exterior generators fix the two-variable signs.
