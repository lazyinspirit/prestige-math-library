These examples keep the companion page concrete without turning it into an Ext
or Tor page too early. They show the degree-zero and vanishing profiles of exact
and Hom-based functors, make change-of-resolution and lift-independence
visible, and separate acyclic resolutions from injective ones by a simple
abelian-group computation.
