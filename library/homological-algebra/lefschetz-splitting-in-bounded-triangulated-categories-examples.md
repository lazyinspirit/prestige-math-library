**Objective.** This companion page runs the abstract Hard Lefschetz splitting
of [[lefschetz-splitting-in-bounded-triangulated-categories]] on the smallest
complex that has two Lefschetz steps. In the bounded derived category of
finite-dimensional vector spaces over a field, with its canonical t-structure,
the object $k[2]\oplus k\oplus k[-2]$ carries a degree-two map that shifts the
three shifted copies of $k$ one step at a time. Its t-cohomology is
concentrated in degrees $-2,0,2$, and the map and its iterate induce the
identities on the two Lefschetz steps while all remaining induced maps are
maps between zero objects; the splitting lemma therefore returns exactly the
three shifted copies it started from. The example checks the splitting
interface only, and explicitly does not test any primitive-lift compatibility.
