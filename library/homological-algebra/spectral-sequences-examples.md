These computations use explicit finite filtered complexes of abelian groups. Multiplication by two illustrates both a nonzero first differential and collapse at the first page, depending on the filtration. A second example delays an identity differential until page two. Each calculation lists the surviving terms and checks the filtration on the homology abutment.

The edge-map calculation uses the first-quadrant filtration endpoints. The two four-element filtered groups then exhibit equal graded pieces with distinct underlying groups, and the cyclic example retains a nonsplit extension after collapse. Finally, negating both grading indices turns the nonzero homological second differential into its cohomological counterpart.
