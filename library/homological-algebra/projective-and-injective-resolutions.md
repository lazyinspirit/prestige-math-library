This page introduces projective and injective resolutions as augmented or coaugmented exact complexes and then keeps the major structural results separate: existence, comparison, uniqueness up to homotopy, horseshoe constructions, Schanuel-type stable comparison, and the Grothendieck injective-embedding theorem.

The separation is deliberate. Existence never silently becomes functoriality, comparison existence never silently becomes uniqueness, higher syzygies remain relative to displayed resolutions, and the Grothendieck injective theorem keeps its generator, pushout, transfinite, and AB5 steps visible.
