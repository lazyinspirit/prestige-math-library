This page develops Hochschild homology of unital algebras over a field. It constructs the two-sided bar resolution, identifies its tensor product over the enveloping algebra with Hochschild chains, and obtains the Tor description, coefficient functoriality, long exact sequences, and degree-zero coinvariants. For polynomial algebras, it proves that the diagonal Koszul complex resolves the regular bimodule and computes Hochschild homology with central bimodule coefficients under AC. The regular coefficient case gives the exterior-power formula and its internal grading.
