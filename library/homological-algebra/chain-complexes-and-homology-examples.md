These examples specialise the categorical constructions on the main page to
abelian groups and modules, where kernels, images, quotients, and induced maps
can be computed explicitly. They also supply the promised counterexamples to
the two false converses kept on the A page: a quasi-isomorphism need not be an
isomorphism of complexes, and homology does not determine a chain map.
