These examples calculate the signs in a two-term tensor totalization, show
that the diagonal bimodule $k[x]$ is projective on each side but not over its
enveloping algebra, and exhibit the contractible two-term regular bimodule
complex whose tensor functor is naturally zero on the bounded projective
homotopy and bounded derived categories.
