These examples keep the abstract delta-functor axioms tied to the concrete
derived-functor constructions already on disk. They show how long exact
sequences, dimension shifting, and universality are used in practice, and they
also isolate the main failure mode: exactness alone is not enough without
naturality of the connecting maps.
