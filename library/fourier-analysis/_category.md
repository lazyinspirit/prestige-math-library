---
title: Fourier Analysis
---

The Fourier Analysis pages cover Fourier series and transforms, harmonic analysis on locally compact abelian groups, and the estimates that connect them to function spaces. They treat convergence, summability, pointwise divergence, the Carleson–Hunt theorem, uncertainty principles, restriction estimates, and Poisson summation. Further topics include Fourier multipliers, Sobolev characterisations, Riesz potentials, singular integrals, Calderón–Zygmund decomposition, and Littlewood–Paley square functions. The collection also develops maximal-function methods, real Hardy spaces, BMO and H1 duality, weighted estimates, lacunary series and Sidon sets, and sampling and lattice duality. Examples also show where hypotheses in these estimates matter in practice.

Measure Theory, Real Analysis, Topology, Functional Analysis, Abstract Algebra, and Linear Algebra provide the integration, function-space, and group structure used here. Numerical Linear Algebra and Approximation, Numerical PDEs and Spatial Discretization, Partial Differential Equations, Representation Theory of Groups, and Inverse Problems draw on the collection's transforms and estimates for spectral, approximation, and recovery questions and related applications.
