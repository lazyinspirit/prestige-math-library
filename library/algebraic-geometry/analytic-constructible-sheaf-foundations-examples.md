**Objective.** This companion page works the two smallest instances of the
constructible calculus introduced on [[analytic-constructible-sheaf-foundations]]:
the point, where the constructible category is the bounded derived category of
finite-dimensional vector spaces and the identity map realizes all six
operations as the identity, tensor, Hom and the dualizing object $k$ in degree
zero; and the open closed pair $j:\mathbb C^*\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$,
$i:\{0\}\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$, where the extension-by-zero sequence
$0\to j_!k_{\mathbb C^*}\to k_{\mathbb C}\to i_*k\to0$ produces the ordinary
localization triangle of the constructible derived category. The point-operation
clauses rest on the declared later interfaces of
`analytic-derived-six-operations-and-duality`; the line computation is closed
locally from published extension-by-zero and mapping-cone material, and the item
carries that forward obligation openly rather than assuming it.
