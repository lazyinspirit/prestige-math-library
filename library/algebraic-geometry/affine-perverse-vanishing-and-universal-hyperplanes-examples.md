**Objective.** This companion page checks the defect statement of
[[lem-projective-hyperplane-sections-decrease-the-defect-of-semismallness]] on
the smallest projective family: the constant map $\mathbb P^n\to\mathrm{pt}$,
whose defect is $n$, together with its universal hyperplane incidence over the
dual projective space, whose fibres are $\mathbb P^{n-1}$ and whose defect
$n-1$ is exactly the predicted decrease; the case $n=1$ degenerates to the
graph of an isomorphism onto the dual projective line with defect zero.
