This companion page carries the low-dimensional computations behind the
theory of [[higher-dimensional-resolution-of-singularities]]. The tight
calculations are deliberately kept out of the main development: the main page
cites none of them except through the standard blowup interfaces it states
locally.

[[lem-blowup-charts-of-the-quadric-cone]] computes the blowup of the origin of
$\mathbb A^3$ along the quadric cone $xy=z^2$ in the three standard charts,
identifies the strict transform as a smooth surface and the fibre over the
vertex as a smooth conic, and proves the transversality that makes the
exceptional divisor a divisor on the strict transform.
[[ex-resolution-of-a-surface-singularity]] exhibits the resulting one-blowup
resolution and compares it with the surface and higher-dimensional resolution
theorems, and
[[cex-no-claim-of-resolution-in-positive-characteristic]] shows that the
maximal-contact mechanism underlying the main page fails in characteristic two,
which is why no positive-characteristic claim is made there.
