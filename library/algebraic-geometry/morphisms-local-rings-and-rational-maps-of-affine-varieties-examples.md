These examples keep the page’s affine dictionary concrete. They compute a
coordinate-ring pullback, identify a basic principal-open isomorphism, exhibit a
classical birational parametrization, and isolate three warnings: rational maps
need not extend everywhere, birational need not mean isomorphic, and bijective
morphisms need not be isomorphisms.
