This page develops the birational geometry of surfaces: exceptional curves and
their contractions, the negativity of curves contracted by a birational
morphism, factorization of proper birational morphisms of regular surfaces into
point blowups, and resolution of normal surface singularities over an arbitrary
field.

The starting point is the intrinsic notion of an exceptional curve of the first
kind and its contraction ([[def-exceptional-curve-and-contraction]]), together
with the universal property of a contraction
([[lem-universal-property-of-a-contraction]]) and the fact that blowing up a
regular point of a surface is a contraction
([[lem-blowing-up-a-regular-point-is-a-contraction]]). The dictionary on a
regular projective surface identifies exceptional curves by their
self-intersection, and the negativity theorem
([[thm-negativity-for-exceptional-curves-on-smooth-surfaces]]) shows that a birational morphism of these regular surfaces
can never contract a curve of nonnegative self-intersection. Uniqueness of a
contraction follows from its universal property. Iterating the
local factorization lemma
([[lem-birational-surface-morphism-factors-through-blowup-at-a-non-isomorphism-point]])
and the one-step count drop for contracted curves
([[lem-contracted-curve-count-decreases-under-a-point-blowup-factorization]])
proves that every proper birational morphism of regular projective surfaces is
a composition of point blowups followed by an isomorphism
([[thm-factorization-of-birational-morphisms-of-smooth-surfaces]]).

Resolution is proved for an arbitrary normal finite-type surface
([[thm-resolution-of-normal-surface-singularities]]). The local theory reduces
a normal local surface to its completion, resolves complete equicharacteristic
normal local domains by normalized point blowups
([[thm-complete-equicharacteristic-normal-surface-resolution-by-normalized-point-blowups]]),
and descends the resolution. The heart of the complete case is the theory of
rational singularities: a rational Gorenstein normal surface point is resolved
by ordinary point blowups
([[thm-rational-gorenstein-normal-surface-singularity-resolved-by-point-blowups]]),
the tangent conic of such a point is computed by its Hilbert function
([[lem-rational-gorenstein-surface-tangent-conic-and-hilbert-function]]), and
the classification-free analysis of the nonsquare, double-plus-simple and
triple-cubic branches terminates the singular-successor tree
([[lem-nonsquare-tangent-conic-rational-surface-blowups-terminate]],
[[lem-square-tangent-conic-blowup-singularities-controlled-by-a-cubic]],
[[lem-double-plus-simple-cubic-rational-surface-branch-terminates]],
[[lem-triple-cubic-rational-surface-branch-reduces-in-two-steps]]). The scope
note [[rem-surface-contraction-and-resolution-scope-boundaries]] records what
is deliberately not claimed: Castelnuovo's existence criterion, negative
definiteness of reducible exceptional configurations, and resolution beyond
dimension two.
