These examples keep the classical affine dictionary concrete. They compute the
coordinate rings of affine space and a parabola, exhibit a reducible algebraic
set with zero divisors, identify the punctured affine line as a principal open,
and isolate the two main warnings of the page: the affine-line Zariski topology
is coarse, and a point set does not remember nilpotent structure.
