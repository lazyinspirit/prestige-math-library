**Objective.** This page develops complex algebraic stratified topology: finite
smooth complex algebraic Whitney stratifications of reduced quasi-projective
varieties and of a single morphism, compatible with prescribed closed and
locally closed algebraic subsets; normalized curve arcs at boundary points;
generic Whitney regularity; ordinary normal tubes and relative tubular
neighbourhoods whose projection and radius pairs stay submersive along a chain
of strata; compatible Whitney control data with commuting radial collars and
continuous local trivializations of controlled stratified submersions; and
finite compatible triangulations of the compact controlled Whitney spaces and
links that arise. It culminates in the conical neighbourhood theorem, in which
a neighbourhood of a stratum point is the product of a Euclidean ball with the
cone on a compact Whitney-stratified normal link whose stratified
homeomorphism type is locally constant along the stratum.

The page closes with the finite conical cochain models for a bounded complex of
analytic sheaves that is algebraically constructible: a finite deleted-boundary
triangulation of the ambient quasi-projective variety, bounded
finite-dimensional cochain models for ordinary and compact-support cohomology,
ordinary and internal derived Hom and local cohomology with prescribed
algebraic support, constructibility of internal Hom on a finite complex Whitney
refinement, local constancy of the link and support models along the complex
strata, and compatibility of every model with extension of the coefficient
field, obtained by local construction and finite affine-open descent on
separated finite-type varieties. The existence theorems are stated under AC,
and each item records the exact point at which choice is consumed. The
companion page exhibits the deleted-boundary convention on the smallest
noncompact example, the complex affine line.
