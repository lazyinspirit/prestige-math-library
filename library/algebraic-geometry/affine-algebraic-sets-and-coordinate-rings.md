This page sets up the classical affine dictionary over an algebraically closed
field. It starts with zero loci and vanishing ideals, packages the
Nullstellensatz into the algebraic-set and prime/radical correspondences, and
then moves to coordinate rings, principal opens, and the reduced affine
$k$-algebra object correspondence that the next page will upgrade to a genuine
anti-equivalence on morphisms.

The scope stays intentionally classical. The empty algebraic set is retained in
the lattice of closed sets, but the word "variety" is reserved for the nonempty
irreducible objects. Nilpotents and non-rational points are recorded only as a
boundary remark at the end; the full scheme-theoretic repair belongs later in
the track.
