**Objective.** This companion page works the smallest failure of naive finite
triangulation. The complex affine line $\mathbb C$ is not compact, so it is not
homeomorphic to the realization of any finite simplicial complex; it is
nevertheless homeomorphic to the realization of a finite complex with one
boundary vertex deleted, via the octahedral boundary complex and stereographic
projection. The example fixes the deleted-boundary convention under which the
finite triangulation statements of the companion page apply to noncompact
quasi-projective varieties.
