This page develops the character dual of a finite abelian group, the normalized negative-character Fourier transform, and its orthogonality, inversion, Parseval, convolution, quotient, annihilator, and coset identities. It uses the published prerequisites on [[cosets-and-lagranges-theorem]], [[cyclic-groups-and-direct-products]], [[finite-abelian-characters-for-combinatorics]], [[group-homomorphisms-and-the-isomorphism-theorems]], [[monoids-groups-and-subgroups]], [[normal-subgroups-and-quotient-groups]], [[rings-subrings-and-integral-domains]], [[roots-and-rational-powers]], [[the-complex-exponential-and-eulers-formula]], [[the-structure-of-finite-abelian-groups]], and [[vector-spaces-and-subspaces]]. The proofs distinguish the group-index row orthogonality from the dual-index column orthogonality. Convolution is unnormalized, so its transform has an explicit $\sqrt{|G|}$ factor.
