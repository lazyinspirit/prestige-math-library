These finite examples check the boundaries of the definitions and spectral results: a stationary law with a zero coordinate does not support a full-state inverse-similarity formula; a stationary directed cycle need not have the reversible discriminant similarity; period two forces absolute gap zero; and the two-state reversible walk displays the distinct endpoint eigenvalue multisets of $U$ and $W=U^2$.
