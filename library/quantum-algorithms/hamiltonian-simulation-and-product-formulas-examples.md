These examples check the commuting identity, compute the first-order commutator estimate on two Pauli terms, and show why its certified step count can grow while the total Hamiltonian norm stays fixed. A sparse diagonal oracle family also shows why row and value access does not itself provide an explicit term list or charge term exponentials for free.
