The reconstruction example uses $N=2$, $Q=17$, and $y=9$ to show that the exact weak inequality accepts a boundary tie, while the finite-rational Legendre step separately uses a strict approximation hypothesis. The order-finding example takes $N=5$, $x=2$, and order $4$: Fourier index $k=2$ reconstructs the proper divisor $2$ but fails the exact power test, while a coprime index reconstructs and retains $4$.
