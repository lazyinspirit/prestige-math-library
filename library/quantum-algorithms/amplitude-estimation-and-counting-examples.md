The worked case $N=16$, $M=4$ evaluates the generic count-error expression at zero, one, half, and all marked addresses, showing why the exact endpoint clauses sharpen the uniform bound. The counterexample compares $I$ and $-I$: uncontrolled calls differ by a global phase, while their controlled blocks are distinguished on a control superposition. It makes explicit why controlled-power query counts require separately declared controlled access.
