title: Quantum Algorithms
---

Published pages build mathematical quantum algorithms from explicit finite-dimensional registers, circuit and oracle access, probability, and resource models. They cover reversible computation, Fourier sampling and hidden-subgroup methods, Simon and Shor algorithms, phase estimation, search and query lower bounds, quantum walks, Hamiltonian simulation, amplitude estimation, block encodings, signal processing, and singular-value transformation. Further pages analyze linear systems, sampling, optimization, communication, adiabatic computation, complexity classes, and algorithm composition. Correctness, success probability, queries, gates, precision, state preparation, and classical post-processing are tracked separately so speedup claims retain their input and cost models.

Quantum Computing provides circuit and state foundations; Linear Algebra, Probability, and Computability Theory support algorithm semantics, success guarantees, and complexity. Number Theory and Fourier Analysis underpin factoring, period-finding, and hidden-subgroup methods. These tools lead to applications in Quantum Computing and Information Theory, where algorithms yield circuit constructions and query and communication bounds; Statistical Learning and Classical Models uses quantum-optimization results, and Numerical Partial Differential Equations and Spatial Discretization informs simulation analysis. The branches have distinct prerequisites and can be read selectively.
