The rank-one example verifies the unitary dilation and degree-one QSVT block at every singular value $\sigma\in[0,1]$, including both endpoints. The counterexample takes the zero map and shows that assigning nonzero values to zero-singular-vector pairs makes a cross-space rank-one sum depend on independent kernel-basis choices. The stated odd and even conventions remove that ambiguity.
