The companion computes the zero-query $n=1$ boundary and an explicit $n=2$ oracle with hidden mask $11$. It writes both conditional coset states, verifies the uniform rows $00$ and $11$, computes the exact rank-rise probability and performs the row reduction by hand. A four-sample cap at $\delta=1/2$ has exact failure probability $1/16$.
