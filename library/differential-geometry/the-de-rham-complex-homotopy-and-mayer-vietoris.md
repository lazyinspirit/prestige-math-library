The de Rham complex records closed forms modulo exact forms. Pullbacks induce contravariant maps of graded real algebras. The interval homotopy operator gives an explicit cochain homotopy and the Poincaré primitive. The continuous-homotopy result includes a smoothing argument through a Euclidean embedding and tubular retraction, with its countable-choice hypothesis stated explicitly.

For the two-open-set Mayer–Vietoris sequence the difference convention is second restriction minus first. A partition-of-unity lift supplies surjectivity, and the connecting class is computed with the same sign. The sphere and punctured-space calculations include the degree-zero and low-dimensional cases. Choice assumptions are stated at the results that use them; smooth homotopy invariance itself is choice-free.
