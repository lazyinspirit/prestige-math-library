These examples accompany
[[real-forms-and-real-semisimple-lie-algebras]]. They compute the compact and
split real forms of $\mathfrak{sl}_2(\mathbb{C})$, the Cartan involution and
Cartan decomposition of $\mathfrak{sl}_n(\mathbb{R})$, polar and Iwasawa
decompositions, the compact and split Cartan subalgebras of
$\mathfrak{sl}_2(\mathbb{R})$, restricted roots of
$\mathfrak{sl}_n(\mathbb{R})$, a nonreduced $BC$ restricted root system,
Vogan diagrams for the real forms of $\mathfrak{sl}_3(\mathbb{C})$, a complex
simple algebra viewed as a real one, two nonconjugate real Cartan subalgebras,
two real forms with the same complexification but different Killing-form
signatures, and hyperbolic space as $SO(n,1)/SO(n)$.
