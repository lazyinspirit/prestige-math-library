This page defines tensors intrinsically as multilinear maps, builds the
finite-dimensional exterior algebra from alternating covectors and duality, and
then globalizes the constructions to tensor bundles, tensor fields, and
differential forms. It stops before exterior derivative, Lie derivative,
Cartan calculus, and de Rham theory.
