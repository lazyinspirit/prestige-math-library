## foundations-and-applications

Begin with smooth manifolds, tangent spaces, submanifolds, vector bundles and flows. Differential forms lead to Stokes and de Rham theory, while metrics and connections lead to geodesics, curvature and comparison. Symplectic geometry and characteristic forms complete the available pathway. Lie groups and Lie-algebra structure theory have their own category, Lie Theory.
