A scalar positive principal symbol permits componentwise weak interior estimates on a finite bundle atlas. Compact Sobolev inclusions and a shifted coercive variational problem then produce a selfadjoint operator with the exact $H^2$ domain, a finite smooth kernel, and a smooth Green inverse. Applied to the de Rham and Dolbeault Laplacians, these results give harmonic representatives in every degree and bidegree.

The page fixes its own conventions first: complex manifolds, holomorphic vector bundles and Hermitian metrics; second-order Laplace-type operators on metric vector bundles with their principal symbol and formal symmetry; the formal adjoint of a smooth differential operator; and the $E$-valued Dolbeault operator with its $L^2$ structure. The three theorems then carry the compact analysis: elliptic regularity and shifted parametrices, the smooth kernel and Green inverse, and the identification of harmonic forms with de Rham and Dolbeault cohomology. This supplies compact analysis for subsequent Kähler Hodge theory; the Dolbeault-resolution comparison with sheaf cohomology is deliberately left to that later material.
