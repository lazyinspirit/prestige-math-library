This page builds the weak Whitney embedding and immersion theorems in honest
finite-dimensional Euclidean terms, then turns the Euclidean normal-bundle
picture into intrinsic tubular neighbourhoods and neighbourhood retractions.
With that geometry in place, it proves Euclidean and manifold-valued Whitney
approximation, smooths continuous homotopies without invoking boundary theory,
and finishes with global transverse approximation and the zero-set consequence
for sections.
