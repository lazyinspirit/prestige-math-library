These pages develop smooth manifolds and maps, tangent and cotangent bundles, vector fields, flows, differential forms and integration. Local constructions are globalized with partitions of unity and exhaustions; ordinary differential equation results, rank theorems and transversality support submanifold and embedding theory. The geometric strands include de Rham theory, Stokes' theorem, Riemannian metrics, curvature, geodesics and comparison, as well as symplectic and Poisson geometry, Hamiltonian systems and reduction. Further pages treat characteristic forms, compact Laplace-type operators, harmonic representatives, Clifford bundles and Dirac operators, and formal deformation quantization. Together these topics provide tools for studying global geometry and analysis on manifolds.

Topology and Real Analysis supply manifold, convergence and calculus foundations; Measure Theory supports integration and volume, while Lie Theory and Algebraic Topology contribute group and cohomological tools. Differential Topology uses these manifolds, orientations and characteristic classes; Knot Theory and Lie Theory draw on the same geometric language. Inverse Problems also uses tools for analysis on manifolds.
