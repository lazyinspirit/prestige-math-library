This page treats a smooth distribution as a constant-rank smooth vector
subbundle of the tangent bundle, separates integrability from involutivity, and
proves the Frobenius theorem in both its local-coordinate and maximal-leaf
forms. The local half runs through framed distributions, bracket closure, and a
commuting-frame coordinate lemma; the global half builds leaves from flat-chart
plaques instead of from the ambient subspace topology.
