**Objective.** This page develops the Ascoli–Arzelà theorem for continuous maps from a locally compact Hausdorff space into a metric space. It first extends the compact-open topology and equicontinuity to the required topological-domain setting, proves the pointwise compactness and closure lemmas, and then identifies compact compact-open closure exactly with equicontinuity and pointwise relative compactness under the Axiom of Choice.

**Specialisations and boundaries.** For nonempty compact metric domains the compact-open topology becomes the uniform topology. Proper targets turn pointwise relative compactness into pointwise boundedness, which yields compact-family and subsequence criteria for real and finite-dimensional Euclidean targets. Compact targets need only equicontinuity for the sufficient direction, while the companion examples show why boundedness alone fails for a general metric target.
