Covering-space lifting supplies unique path and map lifts, injective induced maps, and the subgroup criterion ([[thm-path-lifting-for-covering-maps]], [[thm-covering-space-lifting-criterion]]). Right monodromy records lifted endpoints ([[def-monodromy-action-on-a-covering-fibre]]), while [[thm-universal-cover-existence]] and [[thm-deck-group-of-a-universal-cover-is-the-fundamental-group]] provide the universal cover and its traversal-order deck action. Subgroup index measures covering sheets through [[thm-sheets-equal-fundamental-group-index]].

Local homeomorphisms and compactness first give a finite-covering criterion. The lifting criterion then controls covering morphisms, and quotients of a universal cover realize arbitrary subgroups. Basepoint change produces conjugation, yielding [[thm-classification-of-connected-covering-spaces]]. For [[def-regular-covering]], the same conjugation calculation identifies regularity with subgroup normality; normalizer cosets then give [[thm-deck-group-as-normalizer-quotient]]. The quotient circle specializes the classification to $n\mathbb Z$, including its universal cover and the regularity of every connected circle covering.
