Based loops and induced homomorphisms turn continuous maps into group maps ([[def-based-loops-and-fundamental-group]], [[def-induced-homomorphism-on-fundamental-groups]]). The arbitrary-map pushout of [[def-pushout-of-group-homomorphisms]] combines homomorphisms with a common source without assuming injectivity. Compactness and the Lebesgue-number theorem ([[thm-heine-borel-rn]], [[thm-lebesgue-number-lemma]]) supply finite subdivisions subordinate to open covers, while [[thm-fundamental-group-of-the-circle]] supplies the basic nontrivial calculation.

Loop subdivision gives [[lem-open-cover-loop-generation]], and a subordinate homotopy grid makes its pushout value invariant in [[lem-van-kampen-factorization-invariance]]. These results yield [[thm-seifert-van-kampen]] and its quotient and free-product consequences. Explicit wedge neighbourhoods lead to [[thm-fundamental-group-of-finite-wedge-of-circles]], while componentwise loops give [[thm-fundamental-group-of-a-product]]. The same machinery proves simple connectedness of higher-dimensional spheres and computes the fundamental group of [[def-two-dimensional-torus]].
