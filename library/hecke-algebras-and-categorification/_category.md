Hecke representation theory and categorification connect deformed group algebras with graded modules, quantum groups and highest-weight structures. This collection develops ordinary Hecke Specht polytabloids, their comparison with Murphy constructions, and type-A cellular bases. The KLR branch studies induction and restriction, quantum Serre relations on Grothendieck groups, and cyclotomic highest-weight categorification. Cyclotomic Hecke and quiver Hecke algebras provide the bridge that transports grading and categorical information to Hecke modules.

Ariki categorification relates these constructions to decomposition numbers. The degenerate Ariki theorem retains its separate parameter regime and draws on category O and higher-level Schur–Weyl duality. Category O and Geometric Representation Theory supplies that route and the geometric convolution foundations; Quantum Groups and Crystal Bases supplies global bases, highest weights and Fock spaces. Symmetric Groups and Symmetric Functions contains the classical and modular symmetric-group applications. Foundational presentations, bases and structural constructions remain in Hopf Algebras & Hecke Algebras. Every topic keeps its examples companion, and publication statuses distinguish available material from draft work.
