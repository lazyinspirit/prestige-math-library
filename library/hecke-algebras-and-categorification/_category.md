Published pages develop ordinary Hecke Specht polytabloids and signed-Murphy comparisons, type-A Specht modules and cellular bases, and unequal-parameter cells. The categorification branch treats KLR algebras, induction and quantum Serre relations on Grothendieck groups, cyclotomic Hecke and quiver-Hecke algebras, and highest-weight categorification. Ariki's theorem links these constructions to decomposition numbers, with a separate degenerate-parameter theorem using higher-level Schur-Weyl duality. Example companions distinguish worked cases and counterexamples across the topics.

Coxeter Groups, Abstract Algebra, Hopf Algebras & Hecke Algebras, Quantum Groups and Crystal Bases, Symmetric Groups and Symmetric Functions, and Category O and Geometric Representation Theory provide the relevant group, algebra, crystal, and module settings. Category O and Geometric Representation Theory uses the degenerate Ariki route developed here; Hopf Algebras & Hecke Algebras, Quantum Algebra and Representation Theory, and Symmetric Groups and Symmetric Functions use related Hecke and decomposition-number results. Classical symmetric-group modules remain on their subject pages, while these pages focus on Hecke deformation and categorification routes.
