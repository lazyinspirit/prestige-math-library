**Objective.** This page records the degenerate analogue of Ariki's
categorification theorem: the finite-dimensional representation theory of the
degenerate cyclotomic Hecke tower $H^\Lambda$ at parameter one is the integral
higher-weight theory of the Fock space of $\mathfrak{gl}_{I^+}$, with simple
modules corresponding to dual-canonical vectors, Specht classes to standard
monomials and projective covers to quasi-canonical vectors. The page keeps the
full integral statements: the module Grothendieck group is the entire projected
costandard lattice and the split projective group is the standard
divided-power lattice, paired by the normalized highest pairing; the failure of
integral costandard ground-cyclicity is recorded honestly rather than absorbed
into an over-strong generation claim. The main theorem assembles the two
Grothendieck identifications, the decomposition formula, the Chevalley action of
the residue functors and the head/socle rule for residue translates of simples,
and extends the statement from bounded-below to arbitrary consecutive residue
sets by compatible finite double cuts containing every charge and required
support.

The tower itself is built from the supported central cuts, not from the whole
cyclotomic algebra, and the page states explicitly why the two differ. The final
two items transfer the same circle of ideas to the Fock-space involutions: sign
duality and multipartition transpose govern the reverse-standard labels, and the
full families of Young and signed Young images realize the quasihereditary
Schur-cover algebra and its Ringel dual on the opposite-parabolic side. The
page is deliberately ungraded: no Koszul grading, no root-of-unity theory and no
nondegenerate Hecke categorification is asserted or imported, and the remark on
parameter ranges explains why the degenerate theorem does not transport to
roots of unity. Its example companion works the smallest blocks, the projected
dual bases and the index-two lattice explicitly.
