**Objective.** This companion page works the smallest nontrivial instances of
the cyclotomic Hecke/KLR dictionary of
[[cyclotomic-hecke-and-quiver-hecke-algebras]] and records its boundary
phenomena: the homogeneous degree check for the corrected KLR braid relation
(including the $e=2$ double-edge case), the degenerate level-one map for
$S_3$, the rank-two level-one cyclotomic KLR algebra at $e=3$, and the
counterexample showing that the whole affine Hecke algebra is not one
cyclotomic KLR block. All computations are finite and explicit over the stated
fields.
