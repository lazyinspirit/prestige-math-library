## representation-theory

Specht polytabloids and cellular bases develop Hecke module constructions. KLR induction and restriction categorify quantum Serre relations; cyclotomic quotients give highest-weight categorification. The Hecke–KLR bridge leads to graded decomposition numbers. The degenerate Ariki theorem follows its distinct category-O route, using the functors and Fock-space dictionary in Category O and Geometric Representation Theory.
