These examples keep the abstract decision-problem language concrete: free
reduction in a free group, coordinate reduction in a cyclic presentation, one
free-group conjugacy calculation, one partial coset-enumeration warning, and a
small relator-area computation. The counterexample isolates the gap between
enumerating positive instances and deciding all instances.
