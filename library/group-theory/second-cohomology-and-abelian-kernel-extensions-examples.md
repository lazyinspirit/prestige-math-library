These examples compute factor sets and extension classes directly: the
nonsplit $C_{p^2}$ extension, the zero cocycle for a split extension, cyclic,
dihedral, and quaternion central extensions, a visible section change by a
cochain, a Baer-sum calculation, and a witness that the middle group alone does
not determine extension equivalence.
