These examples make the abstract constructions visible: the free-product tree,
an amalgam tree, the Bass-Serre tree of a Baumslag-Solitar group, one quotient
graph basis for a free action, one explicit Kurosh decomposition, and one
virtually free example from finite vertex groups. The counterexample shows why
quotient graph data without stabilizers is not enough.
