This page builds inverse limits concretely, adds the finite-discrete topology,
characterises profinite groups through compact totally disconnected topology,
and then constructs profinite completion, residual finiteness, and free-group
subgroup separability. It keeps the Malcev class as a recorded source-backed
remark rather than pretending that the surrounding commutative-algebra proof is
already in library scope.
