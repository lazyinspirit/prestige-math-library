This page develops the concrete factor-set model of degree-two group
cohomology. Starting from normalized $2$-cocycles and coboundaries, it first
compares that normalized quotient with the explicit inhomogeneous degree-two
cochain complex, then builds extension classes, twisted products, and the
$H^2$ classification of abelian kernel extensions with fixed action.

The second half packages Baer sum, the trivial-action central-extension case,
and the low-degree exact-sequence interpretation for the standard five-term
sequence. The final remark marks the boundary where the nonabelian obstruction
theory leaves degree two and enters $H^3$.
