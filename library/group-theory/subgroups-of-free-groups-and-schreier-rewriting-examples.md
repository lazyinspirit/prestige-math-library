These examples compute Schreier generators in small subgroups of free groups,
show how the index-rank formula appears in explicit coset graphs, and run one
Reidemeister-Schreier calculation to a familiar surface-group presentation.
They also isolate the two routine pitfalls of the method: infinite-index
subgroups can have infinite bases, and a transversal that is not a Schreier
system need not produce the reduced basis promised by Nielsen-Schreier.
