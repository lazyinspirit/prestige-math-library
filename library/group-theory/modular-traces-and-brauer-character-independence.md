Traces of simple modules are separated by explicitly prescribed endomorphisms. The argument then passes from modular traces to lifted traces on elements of order prime to the characteristic, using unique root lifting in a complete discrete valuation ring. Scaling and reduction prove linear independence of irreducible Brauer characters. The construction fixes its lifting convention and treats extensions of the value field separately.
