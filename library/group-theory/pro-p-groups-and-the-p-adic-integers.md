This page specializes profinite inverse limits to finite $p$-group quotients,
builds $\mathbb Z_p$ as compatible residue classes, identifies its metric and
inverse-limit topologies, and then carries the finite Frattini and Burnside
generation principles into the finitely generated pro-$p$ setting.
