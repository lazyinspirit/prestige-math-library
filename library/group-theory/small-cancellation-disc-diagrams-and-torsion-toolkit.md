We establish the disc-diagram tools behind the small-cancellation torsion theorem. The presentation uses distinct symmetrised words as relators, so proper powers are retained without counting equal rotations as pieces. The argument proceeds through van Kampen diagrams, minimality and cut vertices, an explicit curvature count, and Greendlinger shells. Literal periodic-word comparisons then identify the origin of every nonidentity torsion element. No finiteness assumption on the presentation is needed.
