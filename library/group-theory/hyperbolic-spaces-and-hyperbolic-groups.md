This page develops the standard first pass through Gromov hyperbolicity:
slim triangles, equivalent formulations, quasi-geodesic stability,
quasi-isometry invariance, hyperbolic groups, algorithmic consequences,
elementary subgroup structure, and the boundary of a proper geodesic
hyperbolic space. The small-cancellation bridge is included only as a sourced
agreement result and is not used as a hidden later dependency spine.
