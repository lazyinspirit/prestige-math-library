This page fixes a splitting $p$-modular system, records reduction of
$\mathcal OG$-lattices, replaces the semisimple Maschke picture with radicals
and Nakayama, develops projective covers and indecomposable projectives for
$kG$, and ends with relative projectivity together with the vertex/source
package that stays below full block theory.
