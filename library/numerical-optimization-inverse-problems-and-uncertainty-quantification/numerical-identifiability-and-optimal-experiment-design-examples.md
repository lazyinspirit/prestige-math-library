These examples separate nonlinear identifiability from linearized rank and from global injectivity, derive a parallel-decay alias, and compute a finite three-point D-optimal design together with the reported status of a one-update multiplicative run. A scalar sensitivity example shows that a computed ranking can reverse even when the conditional log-determinant gap bound applies. Each calculation states the design domain and verifies its own finite arithmetic.
