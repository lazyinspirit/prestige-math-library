These calculations separate what the certificates prove from what they do
not prove: an active-set quadratic has a unique global minimizer, a
constraint-qualification failure can remove all multipliers at a true
minimizer, a KKT stationary point can be a saddle, and a closed convex
problem without Slater can have a positive Lagrangian duality gap.
