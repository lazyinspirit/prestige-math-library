---
title: Numerical Optimization Inverse Problems and Uncertainty Quantification
---
These pages develop numerical methods for nonlinear equations, smooth and nonsmooth
optimization, linear and constrained programming, and global search. They cover Newton,
quasi-Newton, gradient, proximal, bundle, splitting, interior-point, trust-region,
stochastic-gradient, variance-reduced, and manifold methods, with explicit globalization and
certificate conditions. Further topics treat inverse problems and regularization, automatic
and numerical differentiation, adjoint sensitivities, optimal design and identifiability,
rare-event and simulation-gradient estimation, polynomial-chaos uncertainty quantification,
and variational electronic-structure calculations. Claims distinguish descent, stationarity,
local convergence, global optimality, and statistical identifiability.

Real Analysis, Linear Algebra, and Functional Analysis support analytic methods;
Probability, Mathematical Statistics, Bayesian Computational Statistics, and Statistical
Models and Experiments provide probabilistic and inferential foundations. Numerical Linear
Algebra and Approximation and Numerical Differential Equations and Stochastic Dynamics
supply computational tools. Regularization results serve Inverse Problems; Newton and
sensitivity methods support Numerical Differential Equations and Stochastic Dynamics and
Numerical Pdes and Spatial Discretization. Reverse-mode differentiation and
simulation-gradient methods recur in Neural Networks and Learning Dynamics, Online and
Reinforcement Learning, and Representation Learning and Generative Models; convex-duality
results also serve Statistical Learning and Classical Models.
