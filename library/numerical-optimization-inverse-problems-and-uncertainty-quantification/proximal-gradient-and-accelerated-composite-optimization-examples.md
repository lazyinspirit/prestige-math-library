These examples calculate a two-pixel total-variation proximal map, an exact first step for a nonorthogonal Lasso design, and a projected-gradient KKT fixed point. The scalar FISTA example shows by rational interval bounds that the accelerated objective can increase from one iterate to the next, despite the $O(1/k^2)$ bound relative to the optimum.
