These calculations trace exact derivatives of finite models and selected implicit branches, compare discrete and continuous sensitivities, and verify an elliptic adjoint gradient. The counterexamples isolate the terminal adjoint seed and the distinction between uniform state accuracy and derivative accuracy. The QR and tent-map examples are finite-horizon calculations only.
