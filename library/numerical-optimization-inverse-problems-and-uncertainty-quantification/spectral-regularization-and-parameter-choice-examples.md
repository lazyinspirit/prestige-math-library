Worked examples show the Tikhonov source-condition balance for $0<\nu\le1$
and the $\lambda/(1+\lambda)$ bias saturation for $\mu>1$ on a compact
diagonal operator, failure of Morozov when the noise level is understated,
and a finite-sample GCV tie that selects the worse solution estimate. Each
calculation uses the hypotheses and finite procedures from the companion A
page.
