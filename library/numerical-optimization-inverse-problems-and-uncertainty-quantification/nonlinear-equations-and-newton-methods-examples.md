These computations contrast fixed-point residual stopping with nonlinear-root
stopping, verify the local Newton error recurrence at a simple root, and show
reduced order at a double root. They also exhibit a singular starting Jacobian
outside the stated local neighborhood and calculate how a frozen inverse differs
from the updated-Jacobian Newton map.
