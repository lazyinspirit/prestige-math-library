These examples calculate noise amplification in an ill-conditioned diagonal
problem, compare Tikhonov bias and data response at two parameters, retain a
quadrature residual before inversion, and apply the exact normal-equation
residual bound. The residual example uses an exact residual and does not by
itself certify its floating-point evaluation.
