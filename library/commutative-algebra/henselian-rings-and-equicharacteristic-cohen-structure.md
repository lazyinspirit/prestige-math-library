This page organizes three linked themes. Henselian pairs are introduced through
coprime factor lifting, simple-root lifting, and idempotent lifting; complete,
nilpotent, Artinian, and quotient cases then show where the abstract mechanism
actually applies. The second half switches to complete equicharacteristic local
rings, where maximal residue-injective subfields become coefficient fields and
formal power-series substitution becomes the engine behind Cohen presentations.

The endpoint is the equicharacteristic Cohen structure theorem and its
parameter-subring refinement for complete local domains. Mixed characteristic is
recorded honestly as the next theorem beyond this page, not folded into the
proved-here route without the missing Cohen-ring machinery.
