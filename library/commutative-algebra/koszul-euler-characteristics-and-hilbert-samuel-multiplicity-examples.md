These calculations isolate three conventions in the Euler/multiplicity bridge: an empty sequence computes length, a nonzero annihilator contributes a necessary subtraction, and a redundant zero generator changes the coefficient index even when the generated ideal stays the same. The two DVR examples display every differential and homology module and compute the Hilbert–Samuel polynomial directly. No general dimension theorem or power-series-ring construction is needed.
