This page upgrades the set-theoretic prime-spectrum material to the Zariski
topology. It identifies vanishing sets as the closed sets, shows distinguished
opens form the local basis, proves quotient and localization spectra as the
expected subspaces, and establishes compactness in the library's non-Hausdorff
sense.

It then computes closures and specialization, identifies closed points,
irreducible closed subsets, irreducible components, and Noetherianity, and
records the connectedness criterion via idempotents together with two common
geometric payoffs: support is specialization-closed, and affine finite-type
spectra have dense closed points.
