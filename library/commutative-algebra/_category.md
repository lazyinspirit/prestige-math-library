name: commutative-algebra
title: Commutative Algebra
---
Commutative algebra studies commutative rings and modules through ideals, localization
and finiteness. Its topics include Noetherian and Artinian conditions, Hilbert basis,
integral extensions, normalization, Dedekind domains and ideal classes, prime spectra
and primary decomposition. Local algebra develops support and localization of modules,
depth, Cohen–Macaulay modules, regular sequences, Koszul complexes, completion, flatness
and regular local rings. Dimension and height theorems connect these tools to geometry,
while Hilbert–Samuel multiplicity and Koszul Euler characteristics give numerical
invariants. Further topics include valuation rings, Henselian rings, resultants and
quasi-finite morphisms.

The subject builds on ring, ideal, polynomial and module foundations from Abstract
Algebra, with selected inputs from Algebraic Geometry, Scheme Theory, Homological
Algebra and Combinatorics. Its results are used in later work in Algebraic Geometry,
Scheme Theory, Homological Algebra, Representation Theory of Groups and Symmetric Groups
and Symmetric Functions, where localization, finiteness, dimension and module structure
recur. Geometric links appear in coordinate rings, projective constructions and sheaf
cohomology.
