Embedding dimension and associated graded rings lead to regular parameters, minimal resolutions, the Auslander–Buchsbaum formula, and the homological criterion for regularity. Localization and completion connect these descriptions; Serre’s criterion establishes normality, including the case of rings with zero divisors. Local rings are nonzero and commutative Noetherian; finite modules are finitely generated. The proofs retain the choice hypotheses of their dependencies. Fields occupy dimension zero, and DVRs exclude fields.
