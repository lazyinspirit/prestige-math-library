These examples work through the standard concrete consequences of the Dedekind package: PIDs and semilocal Dedekind domains, explicit fractional-ideal arithmetic in $\mathbb Z$, localizations at a prime, the two-generator construction, and the divisor/class translation. The final example also records a singular one-dimensional domain where local principality fails, so invertibility fails with it.
