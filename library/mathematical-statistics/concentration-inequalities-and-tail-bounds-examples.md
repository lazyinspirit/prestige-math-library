These examples apply the A-page bounds to a Rademacher sum, a perfectly dependent Gaussian maximum, and a sub-Gaussian vector. A symmetric countable distribution shows why finite variance alone cannot give a sub-Gaussian tail. Each calculation retains the parameter and degenerate-case assumptions of the theorem it uses.
