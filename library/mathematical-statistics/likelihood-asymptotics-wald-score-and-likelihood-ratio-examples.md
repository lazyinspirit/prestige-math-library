In the known-variance normal location model, the MLE and all three regular
one-dimensional statistics coincide exactly, so this model has an exact
$\chi_1^2$ law in finite samples. At the one-sided null boundary $\mu=0$,
the unrestricted normal model is still smooth, but the local null is a
half-line. The likelihood-ratio limit then has a point mass at zero and is not
the ordinary $\chi_1^2$ law.
