## Objective

Work out the mean and median influence and replacement breakdown calculations, then give a fully specified functional whose gross-error sensitivity is finite while its empirical plug-in statistic has a replacement breakdown fraction tending to zero. The examples use the definitions and proved results on [[robust-influence-and-breakdown]] and add no dependency for another page.
