An asymptotically linear estimator has a Gaussian limit under the iid central limit theorem. In a pathwise differentiable model, regularity forces its influence function to represent the path derivative; orthogonal decomposition then gives the covariance bound of the canonical gradient. The separate convolution route treats general regular estimators under explicit LAN, regularity, limit-law, and dense-submodel hypotheses. None of these lower bounds establishes that an attaining estimator exists.
