## Objective

Define statistical functionals and pointwise gross-error influence, then distinguish their local sensitivity from finite-sample replacement breakdown. Prove the M-location influence formula under explicit differentiation and local-root hypotheses, derive the median formula with its pointwise tie convention, and establish exact mean and odd-sample median replacement breakdown fractions. The earlier A-page requirements are [[contamination-models-and-robust-risk]] for the gross-error path and [[pathwise-differentiability-influence-functions-and-efficient-scores]] for the distinction between robust and semiparametric influence notions.
