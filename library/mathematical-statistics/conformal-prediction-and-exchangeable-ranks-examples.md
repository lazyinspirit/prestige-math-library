These examples compute a tied split quantile and a full-conformal candidate rank, then exhibit the limits of the guarantees. A rare subgroup can have poor conditional coverage even while marginal coverage exceeds its target, and a calibration/test distribution shift can produce zero coverage when exchangeability fails.
