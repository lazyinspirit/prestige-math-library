This page develops exact finite-sample inference by conditioning and by finite group actions. It defines conditional rejection probabilities, shows that a common sufficiency kernel makes a test's conditional rejection probability nuisance-free, and proves that conditional level controls unconditional size. A local measurability lemma establishes conditional-kernel tail and atom evaluations at random arguments. It defines randomized upper-tail p-values and proves their conditional uniformity with a tie randomizer. For finite group actions, it defines orbit ranks and proves validity under invariant null laws, counting transformation multiplicities so nonfree actions are covered. The group results concern invariance of the null distribution and do not assume treatment assignment was randomized.
