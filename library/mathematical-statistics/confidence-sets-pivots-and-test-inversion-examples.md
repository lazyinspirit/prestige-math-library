These examples calculate exact normal and uniform-maximum pivot intervals,
invert a binomial lower-tail test to obtain a conservative upper confidence
limit, and show directly how atoms can make a nominal discrete pivot interval
overcover. The unknown-variance normal interval uses the actual MSF-35 exact
t-pivot and quantile suppliers; its proof records the common full-measure
sample space, nuisance variance, and calibrated inversion.
