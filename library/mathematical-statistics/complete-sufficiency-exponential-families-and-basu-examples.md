These finite and parametric examples test the general criteria: Bernoulli likelihood classes, an endpoint mixture that dominates an entire family, a countable likelihood-ratio vector, and normal mean/residual independence. A curved normal submodel supplies an explicit zero-mean nonzero function of a sufficient statistic, while a finite counterexample isolates why a sufficiency reference must share the model’s conditional law.
