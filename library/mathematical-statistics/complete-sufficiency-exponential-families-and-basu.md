This page develops sufficiency through parameter-independent conditional laws and likelihood factorization, then treats minimal sufficiency, complete statistics, exponential families, and Basu’s theorem. The reference-measure criterion is stated with an in-model reference law; an arbitrary external dominator does not preserve a statistic’s conditional law. The proofs identify the exact countability, standard Borel, domination, and open natural-parameter assumptions needed in each result.
