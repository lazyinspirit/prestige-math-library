This page separates three models: a local gross-error path, an iid Huber mixture, and adversarial replacement after clean iid sampling. It defines expected robust risk separately from tail risk and proves a Huber lower bound from the contamination modulus. The lower-bound statement makes its loss-separation condition explicit; its expected-risk and tail-risk conclusions are not interchangeable.
