This page defines the causal Bernoulli-shift approximation convention in
$H=L^2([0,1])$ and proves the local ingredients needed for its sample-mean
central limit theorem. The finite-dependent vector CLT is proved by truncation
and independent blocks. A local basis-projection lemma proves norm convergence
and Parseval from basis completeness. A separate Hilbert-valued expectation
lemma supplies the orthogonal-projection step. The long-run covariance is shown to be a positive
trace-class operator and an $L^2([0,1]^2)$ kernel equivalence class; a local
kernel-operator lemma makes that identification precise. A positive trace-class
covariance is realized by a Borel Hilbert-valued Gaussian. The final theorem
proves weak convergence of the normalized sums in $H$ under AC. It does not
assert convergence of a partial-sum path process.
