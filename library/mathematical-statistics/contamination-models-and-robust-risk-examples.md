These examples compute the mean's directional response along a gross-error path, the exact modulus and equalizing contaminations in a Bernoulli Huber model, and a finite-sample replacement bound. The bounded replacement calculation uses values in $[0,1]$; it does not imply a robust mean rate for unbounded observations.
