Orthogonal fixed-design regression reduces the Lasso to coordinatewise soft
thresholding. A collinear design shows why sparsity alone does not imply
incoherence, coefficient identifiability or a fast coefficient-error bound.
The sample-covariance example records the unavoidable rank deficiency when
the dimension exceeds the sample size. The super-exponential covariance
rate obstruction is retained on the main page beside its corrected capped
minimax theorem.
