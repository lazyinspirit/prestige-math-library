---
title: Mathematical Statistics
---
These pages develop statistical inference from probability models and experiments through
estimation, testing, decision theory, and asymptotics. They cover likelihood and
sufficiency, finite-sample optimality, information bounds, exact and confidence-set
procedures, concentration and empirical-process methods, minimax bounds, sparse and robust
estimation, M- and Z-estimation, semiparametric efficiency, and sequential validity.
Nonparametric regression and density estimation, functional time-series limits, and
conformal prediction extend the treatment beyond classical parametric settings. Exact tests,
valid p-values, confidence inversion, and minimax bounds distinguish finite-sample
guarantees from asymptotic limits.

Probability, Measure Theory, Real Analysis, Linear Algebra, Functional Analysis, and
Topology supply probability, convergence, operator, and geometric foundations. Statistical
Models and Experiments uses the statistical-experiment, estimand, and risk framework;
Bayesian Computational Statistics uses the model and likelihood results. Inverse Problems,
Information Theory, Numerical Optimization Inverse Problems and Uncertainty Quantification,
Numerical Linear Algebra and Approximation, Numerical Differential Equations and Stochastic
Dynamics, Numerical Pdes and Spatial Discretization, Statistical Learning and Classical
Models, and Neural Networks and Learning Dynamics draw on these estimation and inference
results.
