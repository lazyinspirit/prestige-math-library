This page builds semiparametric score geometry from common-dominated paths that are differentiable in quadratic mean. It proves score mean-zero and uniqueness, defines the tangent set and its closed linear span separately, and fixes the nuisance tangent space by designated paths with zero right derivative of the target. It also records the nuisance orthogonal complement in the mean-zero $L^2(P)$ Hilbert space. Countable Choice is the exact assumption used for $L^2$ completeness; the broader likelihood-density convention retains its separate Axiom-of-Choice dependency. One-sided boundary score cones remain distinct from their linear tangent spaces.
