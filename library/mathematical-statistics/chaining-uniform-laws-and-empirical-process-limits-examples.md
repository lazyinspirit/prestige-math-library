The finite-subset counterexample separates pointwise strong laws from a uniform law by choosing the realized finite sample as the witnessing set. Under Countable Choice, deterministic interval brackets show that the Uniform$[0,1]$ threshold class is strongly Glivenko–Cantelli. Under AC, the same brackets have integrable square-root entropy and give a functional empirical-CDF limit; direct covariance calculation identifies its tight Gaussian bridge as the standard Brownian bridge.
