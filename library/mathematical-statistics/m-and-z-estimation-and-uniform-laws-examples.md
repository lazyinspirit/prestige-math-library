These examples distinguish criterion optimization from solving an estimating equation. The quadratic criterion selects the sample mean, while the absolute criterion selects the sample median or median interval. A finite Bernoulli criterion class has a uniform law by a direct strong-law argument. The counterexample has exact empirical maximizers and zero uniform error but no consistency for a fixed population maximizer because population separation fails.
