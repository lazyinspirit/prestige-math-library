The finite Bernoulli example computes a one-sided empirical Rademacher complexity and its uniform deviation directly. The threshold counterexample uses finite uniform sample spaces to show why the VC entropy growth must retain its dependence on $r$. The uncountable-class example states its Countable Choice assumption and shows that separate measurability of every function need not make the empirical supremum measurable.
