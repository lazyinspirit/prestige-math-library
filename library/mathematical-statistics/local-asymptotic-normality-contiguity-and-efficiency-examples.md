The known-variance normal location model has an exact Gaussian local
likelihood ratio, an exact normal central sequence, and a regular sample
mean. A two-point sequence with a shrinking base probability and fixed
alternative probability shows that a likelihood-ratio limit with the wrong
mean can fail contiguity despite every finite likelihood ratio having mean
one.
