This page turns moment-generating-function bounds into finite-sample tail guarantees. It defines scalar and vector sub-Gaussian proxies and the restricted moment-generating-function range for sub-exponential variables. It proves independent bounded-sum Hoeffding and Bernstein bounds, a finite maximum inequality, and an epsilon-net reduction with a local volume proof. McDiarmid's inequality is proved from a Doob martingale whose conditional endpoint widths are checked by product integration. The page keeps independence, boundedness, variance, measurability, and the Axiom of Choice assumptions attached to the results that use them.
