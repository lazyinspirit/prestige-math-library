This page defines bias, mean squared error and uniformly minimum-variance
unbiased estimation, proves the bias–variance identity, and establishes the
Rao–Blackwell and Lehmann–Scheffé theorems with finite-moment hypotheses. It
keeps squared-error risk distinct from unbiasedness and states the common
conditional-law, completeness and choice assumptions needed by the optimality
results.
