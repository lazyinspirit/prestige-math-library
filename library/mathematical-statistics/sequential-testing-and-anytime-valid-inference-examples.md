## Examples

A finite Bernoulli likelihood ratio gives a concrete null test martingale.
Its two-observation version shows an exact data-dependent first-crossing
probability below Ville's bound. The counterexample separates validity under
one null law from the required supermartingale condition under every law in a
composite null family.
