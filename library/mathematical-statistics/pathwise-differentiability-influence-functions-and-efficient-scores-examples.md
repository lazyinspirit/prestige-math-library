The mean examples use explicitly designated one-sided bounded-score paths; they derive the mean's parameter influence curve and the sample mean's exact estimator influence function, and show that a finite mean alone does not guarantee pathwise differentiability. A finite four-point model verifies nuisance projection and the efficient-score canonical-gradient formula under its full score-space decomposition.
