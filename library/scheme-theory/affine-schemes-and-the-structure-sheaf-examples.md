These calculations keep the structure sheaf visible. Field and zero-ring
spectra establish the two extreme cases, while $\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z$
separates generic from closed points. Dual numbers then show why the topology
alone does not recover a scheme. The affine-line examples distinguish its
localized open, its relative functor of points, and the generic point from a
classical $k$-valued point.
