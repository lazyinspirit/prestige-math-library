These calculations use the tensor-product and fibre formulas from the theory page. The families $xy=t$ and $x^2=t$ illustrate reducible, nonreduced, empty and field-valued fibres. Scalar extension can split an integral scheme or introduce nilpotents; the examples distinguish actual scheme points from points valued in a specified field.

Polynomial graphs and quadratic self fibre products give explicit equations. The multiplicity comparison uses nilradical powers to distinguish schemes with the same one-point topology and residue field. All calculations retain the full coordinate rings, in every characteristic stated.
