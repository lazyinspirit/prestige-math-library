The split torus $T=\mathbf G_m^n$ is displayed with its Laurent coordinate
Hopf algebra and its explicit comultiplication, counit and antipode on the
generators; its points over any commutative $k$-algebra $R$ are the tuples of
units. The ideals generated by the Laurent binomials $t_i^{r_i}-1$ are checked
to be Hopf ideals, so they cut out the closed subgroup schemes
$\mu_{r_1}\times\cdots\times\mu_{r_n}$, and in characteristic $p>0$ the scheme
$\mu_p$ is shown to have coordinate ring $k[t]/(t-1)^p$: it is nonreduced, with
trivial $k$-points, so it is not determined by its rational points.

The second example works out the comodule dictionary in the simplest graded
case: a weight decomposition $V=\bigoplus_mV_m$ of a finite-dimensional vector
space is exactly a comodule structure over $k[t,t^{-1}]$, the associated
representation acts on $V_m$ by the scalar $g^m$, and in a basis of weight
vectors the comorphism is $x_{ij}\mapsto\delta_{ij}t^{m_i}$. The construction
is verified directly from the coassociativity and counit identities.
