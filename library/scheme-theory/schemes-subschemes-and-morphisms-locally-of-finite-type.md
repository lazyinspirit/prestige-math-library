This page builds the affine-local language of schemes: open charts, gluing,
morphisms, reductions, closed subschemes, and the two finiteness conditions.
It deliberately keeps the finite-presentation distinction visible over
non-Noetherian bases.

The affine quotient description supplies the local proof of the
quasi-coherent-ideal/closed-subscheme correspondence. Quasi-compact morphisms
then have a scheme-theoretic image obtained from the kernel ideal sheaf.
