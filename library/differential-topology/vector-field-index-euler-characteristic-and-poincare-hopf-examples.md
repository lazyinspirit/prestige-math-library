The examples test the sign conventions and the hypotheses of the index theory.
The planar source, sink and saddle compute the local index in the simplest
chart, the outward radial field on a disk verifies the boundary form of
Poincare-Hopf in its basic case, and the odd sphere carries an explicit
nowhere-zero field.

The counterexamples mark the load-bearing hypotheses: the inward radial field
on an odd-dimensional ball shows that "nonzero on the boundary" cannot replace
"strictly outward" in the boundary formula, and the closed interval shows that
the odd-dimensional vanishing of the Euler characteristic needs closedness.
