This companion works the simplest instance of the fitting programme of
[[relative-smooth-triangulation-and-bundle-fitting]]. For the trivial bundle
$\pi:B\times N\to B$ over two supplied finite corner-adapted triangulations, the
product cells $\sigma\times\tau$ form a finite convex cell complex whose
compatible triangulation gives an explicit global triangulation
([[ex-a-trivial-smooth-bundle-has-a-projection-preserving-product-triangulation]]).
The product map $F=(h,g)$ is a homeomorphism with tangent-star differential
$(u,v)\mapsto(dh(u),dg(v))$, so the whole-star hypotheses transfer, and the
first-coordinate projection $r=\operatorname{pr}_1$ witnesses $\pi F=hr$ with
the base triangulation $h$ unchanged. The example is proved directly and does
not invoke the general bundle theorem.
