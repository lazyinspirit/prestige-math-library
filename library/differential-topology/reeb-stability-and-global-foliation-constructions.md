Local Reeb stability describes a compact finite-holonomy leaf by its holonomy cover and a finite transverse action. The trivial-holonomy specialization gives a product neighborhood. The supporting constructions keep deck and transverse-action conventions explicit. The separate C¹ Reeb–Thurston block derives trivial holonomy from vanishing first real cohomology; its product-neighborhood construction uses a transverse collar and compatible chart first integrals on compact overlaps.

For a closed connected smooth ambient manifold, a cooriented codimension-one foliation containing a compact finite-fundamental-group leaf is globally a fibre bundle over a circle. Closedness is proved by finite rational-homology control and one-sheeted collar graphs; full AC is stated for its finite-CW/homology inputs. The local finite-holonomy block uses only the separately stated countable-choice principle.
