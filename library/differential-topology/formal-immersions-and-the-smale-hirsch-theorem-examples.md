These examples test the definitions and the main theorem on the smallest
nontrivial cases. The circle in the plane identifies formal immersions with
nowhere-zero direction fields and hence with the rotation number; the standard
sphere computes the normal bundle and the tangent-normal identity in the first
even-dimensional case; an open parallelizable manifold applies the
equidimensional open-source theorem; the torus shows that formally plausible
rank data can fail to be holonomic for closed sources of codimension zero; and
a constant bundle map of rank one on the plane exhibits the pointwise nature of
fibrewise injectivity.
