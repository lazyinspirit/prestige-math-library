title: Differential Topology
---

These pages study smooth maps and manifolds through transversality, intersections and their global consequences. They develop regular homotopy, immersion and embedding obstructions, vector-field indices, Morse functions, trajectories, homology and handle decompositions. Cobordism topics include framed bordism, characteristic numbers, surgery, handle trading, the smooth h-cobordism and s-cobordism theorems, and Whitehead torsion. Other pages address foliations, holonomy, fixed-point index and Lefschetz theory, exotic smooth structures and Milnor spheres, vanishing cycles and taut foliations. The subject moves between local critical-point analysis and global classification and surgery, with dimension and orientability hypotheses shaping particular results.

These pages draw on Differential Geometry for smooth manifolds, bundles and characteristic classes; Algebraic Topology and Homological Algebra provide homology, cohomology and torsion tools, while Topology and Real Analysis support local and global arguments. Transversality and isotopy extension support knot-complement results; Morse theory, cobordism and surgery describe how smooth manifolds change. These methods also underpin three-manifold and bordism constructions in Hall Algebras and Tensor Categories.
