This page develops projective functors on a regular integral category-$\mathcal O$ block and Soergel's functor $V=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal O}(P(w_0\cdot\lambda),-)$ with values in the coinvariant algebra. The coinvariant and invariant-theory prerequisites ([[def-the-coinvariant-algebra-of-a-finite-weyl-group]], [[lem-the-harmonic-complement-of-the-coinvariant-algebra]], [[lem-the-coinvariant-algebra-is-a-gorenstein-algebra-with-one-dimensional-socle]]) and the block conventions ([[def-regular-integral-category-o-block-and-its-antidominant-weight]]) come first, followed by the definition of projective functors ([[def-projective-functors-on-a-regular-integral-category-o-block]]) and of the deformed family ([[def-the-deformed-category-o-family-and-its-specializations]]). Wall-crossing is introduced in [[def-wall-crossing-functor-and-unit-counit]], its action on standards and on the Grothendieck group in [[lem-wall-crossing-functors-act-on-standard-modules-by-the-two-step-extension]] and [[lem-wall-crossing-functors-act-on-the-grothendieck-group-by-right-multiplication-by-one-plus-s]], and the projective generation theorem in [[lem-wall-crossing-compositions-produce-the-projective-with-the-top-standard-label]] and [[thm-indecomposable-projective-functors-are-indexed-by-the-weyl-group]]. The deformed lifting and central-order theory ([[thm-lifting-of-indecomposable-projectives-to-the-deformed-family]], [[lem-the-central-action-on-the-deformed-antidominant-projective]]) route Soergel's Endomorphismensatz and Struktursatz ([[thm-endomorphisms-of-the-antidominant-projective-are-the-coinvariant-algebra]], [[thm-soergels-struktursatz-full-faithfulness-on-projectives]]), and the dictionary is recorded in [[thm-wall-crossing-under-the-soergel-functor-is-tensoring-with-the-coinvariant-soergel-bimodule]], [[thm-indecomposable-projectives-correspond-to-indecomposable-soergel-modules]] and [[prop-verma-flag-multiplicities-are-standard-filtration-ranks-under-v]]. The page warns explicitly where no graded lift of category $\mathcal O$ is asserted and where graph supports coalesce at the origin.
