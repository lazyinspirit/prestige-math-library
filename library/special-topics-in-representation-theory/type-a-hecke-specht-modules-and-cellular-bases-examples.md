These examples compute the machinery of
[[type-a-hecke-specht-modules-and-cellular-bases]] in small rank and record
two counterexamples that delimit its hypotheses. The Jucys-Murphy elements and
their q-content eigenvalues are computed through rank four, and the six-element
Murphy cellular basis of $H(S_3)$ is written out explicitly. Two
counterexamples show that the separation hypothesis $e>n$ cannot be weakened
and that cellularity alone does not force cell-form quotients to be nonzero,
cell modules to be simple, or the algebra to be semisimple; the q-Schaper
calculation for the shape $(3,2)$ at $e=2$ exhibits a case where the virtual
sum formula alone yields only a bound and an extra dimension argument is needed
to determine the decomposition number.
