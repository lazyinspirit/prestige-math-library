## representation-theory

Partitions, tableaux and permutation modules lead to Specht modules; branching, the hook-length formula and Robinson–Schensted prepare the character dictionary through symmetric functions; rim hooks and Murnaghan–Nakayama compute character values. Jucys–Murphy elements, seminormal forms and cellular bases organise Hecke and q-Schur Specht modules; cores and abacus calculus classify blocks, and modular branching through the good-node crystal governs restriction. Soergel intersection forms, projective functors and parabolic Kazhdan–Lusztig theory connect Hecke algebras with category $\mathcal O$ and higher-level Schur–Weyl duality; quantized enveloping algebras, divided-power forms, Kashiwara operators, crystal lattices, global and tensor-product bases, level-one Fock spaces, cyclotomic Hecke and KLR algebras and Ariki categorification form the quantum and KLR tracks; Kronecker coefficients, FI-modules and Murnaghan stability close the arc with equivariant Borel–Moore convolution.
