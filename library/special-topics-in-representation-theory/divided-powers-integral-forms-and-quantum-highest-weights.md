This page develops the integral structure of the Drinfeld–Jimbo algebra and the
highest weight representation theory of its integrable modules over the label
lattice. The algebra, its Serre presentation and its triangular decomposition
come from [[quantized-enveloping-algebras-and-quantum-serre-relations]], and
the classical comparison targets live on
[[integrable-highest-weight-kac-moody-modules]].

## Divided powers, skew derivations and the integral form

The rank-one divided powers and Gaussian binomials of
[[def-quantum-integers-factorials-and-divided-powers-at-q-i]] are the local
calculus; the skew derivations $e_i',e_i''$ obtained from the commutator with
$E_i$ are constructed on the negative half in
[[def-kashiwara-skew-derivations-on-the-negative-quantum-half]], with their
twisted Leibniz rules, height drop and bar compatibility. The Lusztig integral
form $U_A$ over $A=\mathbb Z[q,q^{-1}]$, generated by the $E_i^{(r)}$,
$F_i^{(r)}$, $K_h^{\pm1}$ and the toral binomials $T_{i,c,t}$, is set up in
[[def-lusztig-divided-power-integral-form]] together with the warning that $A$
is not a PID, so no freeness is inferred from torsion-freeness. The reordering
and integrality of products, the ordinary-power projector on skew-string
coefficients, and the stability of $U_A$ under the bar and contravariant
dualities are proved in
[[lem-divided-power-and-toral-quantum-binomial-reordering-identities]],
[[lem-divided-power-half-has-integral-skew-string-coefficients]] and
[[lem-the-lusztig-integral-form-is-stable-under-bar-and-contravariant-duality]];
[[lem-unmodified-integral-torus-has-a-quantum-binomial-basis]] gives the exact
$A$-basis of the unmodified toral part by the Gaussian product, shift and
independence argument, over the non-PID ring and for possibly nonseparating
toral pairings.

## The half form and separation

The normalized Lusztig–Kashiwara form on $U_q^-$ is specified by its recursion,
normalization and adjunction in
[[def-the-lusztig-kashiwara-bilinear-form-on-the-negative-quantum-half]], and
its existence, symmetry, nondegeneracy and integrality properties are proved in
[[lem-the-negative-half-is-separated-by-iterated-skew-derivations]] and
[[thm-the-negative-half-bilinear-form-is-symmetric-nondegenerate-and-integral]]:
the matching-permutation Gram formula and the scaled generic braided pairing
give symmetry and the $e_i'$-adjunction, iterated skew derivations separate the
negative half, $(W,W)\subseteq A$ on the word lattice, and the rank-one values
$1/[a]_{q_i^2}!$ show that the divided-power lattice carries exactly the
Gaussian denominators. AC is inherited only through the published generic
pairing.

## Highest weight modules and their forms

Quantum weight modules, the chosen label grading and integrability are defined
in [[def-quantum-weight-module-and-integrability]]; the Verma module and its
unique simple graded quotient in
[[def-quantum-verma-module-and-simple-highest-weight-quotient]]; and the
normalized contravariant form on an integrable highest weight module, with its
Cartan-projection construction, radical and ordinary simplicity, in
[[def-contravariant-form-on-an-integrable-quantum-highest-weight-module]] and
[[lem-cartan-projection-constructs-the-quantum-verma-contravariant-form]]. The
dominant Serre quotient is constructed, proved integrable and universal in
[[lem-quantum-highest-weight-serre-quotient-is-integrable]], and
[[thm-integrable-quantum-highest-weight-module-exists-and-is-unique]] identifies
it with the canonical simple module $L(\lambda)$. The local $q=1$ DVR
deformation proves the character and the universality without global
freeness in
[[lem-local-q-one-deformation-proves-integrable-highest-weight-universality]],
and the finite-support regularization
[[lem-finite-support-regularization-of-quantum-label-weight-modules]] handles
non-separating toral pairings.

## Specialization at $q=1$

Localizing at $q=1$ inside the DVR $\mathbb Q[q]_{(q-1)}$, the halves and the
torus admit explicit bases and specialize to the classical halves, the positive
toral quotient becomes $k[H_i]$, and the positive quotient is the derived
classical enveloping algebra in
[[lem-rational-local-quantum-halves-and-torus-specialize-at-q-one]];
[[lem-q-one-toral-specialization-identifies-the-classical-cartan-generators]]
identifies the classical Cartan generators on graded lattices and
[[prop-specialization-at-q-one-recovers-the-classical-enveloping-relations]]
packages the half isomorphisms, the full positive quotient and the boundaries
($K=-1$ in $\mathfrak{sl}_2$, no direct substitution). Complete reducibility of
bounded-above integrable modules with finite-dimensional label-weight spaces
follows with AC declared in
[[prop-integrable-quantum-highest-weight-modules-are-semisimple]].

The companion [[divided-powers-integral-forms-and-quantum-highest-weights-examples]]
works through the rank-one divided powers and toral reordering, the
$\mathfrak{sl}_2$ Verma and simple modules including the negative-weight
boundary, an explicit lattice and its classical specialization, and a
counterexample to naive $q=1$ substitution.

The auxiliary type-A Hecke material fixes the triangular bar-invariant basis and its elementary coefficient identities. These constructions precede the locally proved Soergel character argument that establishes coefficient positivity on [[soergel-intersection-forms-and-hodge-theory]] and the full theorem on [[kazhdan-lusztig-bases-polynomials-and-cells]].
