These examples compute the Drinfeld–Jimbo formulas of
[[quantized-enveloping-algebras-and-quantum-serre-relations]] in small rank
and record one counterexample about the parameter normalization.

[[ex-quantum-serre-calculation-in-type-a-two]] expands the two type-$A_2$
quantum Serre polynomials, lists every mixed bidegree of their coproducts, and
checks the classical specialization at $q=1$.
[[ex-the-double-edge-quantum-serre-relation-for-affine-a-one]] performs the
same four-letter calculation for the double edge of affine type $A_1^{(1)}$,
where the coefficients $[3]_q=q^{2}+1+q^{-2}$ produce the alternating Gaussian
identity.
[[ex-quantized-sl-two-relations-coproduct-and-antipode]] collects the
rank-one formulas: the coproduct, counit and antipode on the generators, the
$q$-binomial expansion of $\Delta(E^{n})$, the failure of involutivity of the
antipode, and the non-cocommutativity of the coproduct, with the nonvanishing
of $E$ and $K-1$ proved by an explicit oscillator representation.

[[cex-unsymmetrized-q-parameters-break-the-cartan-normalization]] shows that
using one and the same parameter at every node of $B_2$ destroys the coideal
property of the Serre ideal, so that the Cartan normalization $q_i^{a_{ij}}=q_j^{a_{ji}}$ is essential. An explicit four-dimensional representation detects the failure in the full quotient, including its mixed relations.
