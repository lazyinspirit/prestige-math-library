These examples compute an interior vibration mode with shift-invert, show a block trial space resolving two close modes with one exact Rayleigh-Ritz update, and exhibit a restart shift that geometrically suppresses a wanted eigenvector component. The finite models illustrate the assumptions and failure boundaries; they do not assert general convergence.
