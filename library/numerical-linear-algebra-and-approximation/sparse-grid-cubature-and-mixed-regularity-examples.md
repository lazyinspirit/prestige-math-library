These examples verify a four-dimensional Clenshaw–Curtis sparse grid with 48 distinct nodes, show that a diagonal ridge fails the fourth-derivative hypothesis, and compute a nonnegative polynomial chaos coefficient whose level-two Simpson-Smolyak estimate is negative. Positive tensor Gaussian weights preserve nonnegativity, without an exactness claim for that polynomial.
