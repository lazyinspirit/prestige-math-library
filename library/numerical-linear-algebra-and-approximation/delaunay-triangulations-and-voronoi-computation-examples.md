## Examples

These examples verify the stable diagonal selected for four cocircular sites, the duplicate-collapse and lower-dimensional branch for collinear data, and an exact barycentric interpolation value on one Delaunay cell. They use the definitions and procedures on the companion page; the calculations are carried out in the item proofs.
