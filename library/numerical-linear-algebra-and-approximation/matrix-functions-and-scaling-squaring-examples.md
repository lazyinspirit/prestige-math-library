The examples compute a positive diagonal Gram matrix and its polar factors,
show how a stable spectrum can coexist with a large transient norm for a
nonnormal exponential, and locate the planar half-turn on the principal
logarithm branch cut. Each calculation states the matrix identities and
spectral facts it uses.
