## Examples

These examples compute a neutral dipole structure factor, expose the zero-mode obstruction for nonneutral data, show the local $1/r$ potential and $1/r^2$ field singularities at a source, compare the opposing real and reciprocal cutoff trends as $\alpha$ changes, and verify when the declared mesh force is the gradient of the mesh energy. The $P=2$ knot convention and distinction between RMS estimates and deterministic bounds are retained explicitly.
