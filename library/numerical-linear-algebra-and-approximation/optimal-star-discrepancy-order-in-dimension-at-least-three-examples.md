## Summary

The example verifies the exact finite discrepancy of the center point in dimensions two and three from [[def-star-discrepancy-and-digital-net]]. For literature context, Owen reports the planar order $n^{-1}\log n$ and, for fixed $d\ge3$, a classical Roth lower baseline $n^{-1}(\log n)^{(d-1)/2}$ alongside a construction upper order $n^{-1}(\log n)^{d-1}$. The lower rate is only a classical baseline here, not a claim of best-known status. Those exponents differ for $d\ge3$, so these bounds do not establish the conjectured order. Dick's version 3 result concerns uniform growing-dimension tractability; its Remark 1.4 distinguishes fixed $d$. This page makes no resolution or counterexample claim. [Owen, Chapter 15, printed pp. 17–19 and Theorem 15.7](https://artowen.su.domains/mc/practicalqmc.pdf); [Dick, arXiv:2607.23571v3, §1 and Remark 1.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.23571v3).
