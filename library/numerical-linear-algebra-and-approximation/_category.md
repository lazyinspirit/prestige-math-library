---
title: Numerical Linear Algebra and Approximation
---
These pages cover finite-precision matrix computation and approximation: direct and sparse
factorizations, iterative methods and preconditioners, eigensolvers, Schur and generalized
eigenvalue problems, matrix functions, and Sylvester and Lyapunov equations. Other themes
include structured and low-rank matrices, randomized and tensor methods, transforms,
interpolation, splines, quadrature and cubature, kernel approximation, geometric predicates,
verified arithmetic, and certified root and spectral enclosures. Error analysis tracks
rounding, residuals, approximation, conditioning, and algorithmic cost. Gaussian basis
integral recurrences and special-function error estimates extend these calculations to
computational chemistry.

Linear Algebra, Real Analysis, Functional Analysis, Complex Analysis, Measure Theory,
Fourier Analysis, and Probability provide algebraic, analytic, and spectral foundations.
Stable factorizations and residual estimates support time integrators in Numerical
Differential Equations and Stochastic Dynamics, optimization and least-squares methods in
Numerical Optimization Inverse Problems and Uncertainty Quantification, and finite-element
solvers in Numerical Pdes and Spatial Discretization. Certified enclosures and kernel
approximations also serve Information Theory, Inverse Problems, Neural Networks and Learning
Dynamics, Machine Learning Algorithms,
and Statistical Learning and Classical Models.
