## Objective

For convex data with a defined Riemann integral, convexity brackets the integral
between midpoint and trapezoid values. The page also verifies exact Simpson
values on a quadratic and a cubic over a complete two-panel block. The examples
use the exact-arithmetic conventions of the A page.
