These examples make the abstract bounds concrete: a separable grid has TT rank one; a small tensor realizes equality in the two-cut root-sum-square error estimate; and a diagonal order-$d$ tensor has every exact TT bond rank equal to its mode size. The final counterexample expands a convergent rank-two CP sequence and proves by a matrix-pencil determinant argument that its limit has CP rank three, so a best rank-two approximation need not be attained.
