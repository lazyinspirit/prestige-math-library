## Summary

Using the published half-open, equal-weight point-multiset definition of star discrepancy, this page asks for the exact minimum order when the integer dimension $d\ge3$ is fixed before $n\to\infty$. The candidate $n^{-1}(\log n)^{d-1}$ remains conjectural in this scope. Configurations are ordered $n$-point lists, and repeated points count with multiplicity. The companion page is contextual and does not supply a proof.
