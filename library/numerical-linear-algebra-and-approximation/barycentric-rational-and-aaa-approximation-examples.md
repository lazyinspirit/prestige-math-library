The examples verify an exact AAA recovery of a scalar transfer-function pole,
a zero sampled residual with arbitrarily large error at an unsampled point,
and the distinct defects of a singular Padé system and a solved Padé
approximant with a small-residue pole-zero pair. Each calculation uses exact
algebra and stays within the finite-sample or local Taylor guarantees proved
on the companion page.
