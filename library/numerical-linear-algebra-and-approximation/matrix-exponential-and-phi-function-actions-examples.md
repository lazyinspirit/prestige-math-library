The examples verify the phi-one augmented action at zero without a removable-singularity quotient, show how a one-vector Arnoldi projection can miss the slow mode of a stiff diagonal matrix, and compute an exact transient-growth example with a valid logarithmic-norm residual certificate. Their calculations use exact arithmetic; the theorem's separate finite-precision terms remain conditional on certified relation and reduced-action data.
