The examples compute the logarithmic endpoint integral, an interior-pole principal value, and a weakly singular reference-triangle integral. The Gaussian example gives the sinh–sinh transformed identity and a rigorous finite-sum tail bound. Its transformed complex extension fails the uniform strip-decay hypothesis, so no total quadrature error bound is asserted for that example; this records the exact boundary of the analytic-strip theorem.
