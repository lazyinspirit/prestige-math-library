The examples show exact recovery of a rank-$k$ matrix almost surely, a slowly
decaying diagonal spectrum for which the tail-Frobenius term can dominate the
singular-value term, and a fully specified thresholded arithmetic model where
powering loses a small mode unless QR is inserted between applications of
$A$ and $A^*$.
