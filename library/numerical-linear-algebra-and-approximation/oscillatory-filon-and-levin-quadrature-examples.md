## Examples

These examples compute exact Fourier moments and compare an oscillatory integral with one sample, exhibit the $\omega^{-1/2}$ contribution of an interior stationary point alongside its endpoint term, and show with an explicit collocation matrix why a small Levin nodal residual needs an inverse-condition factor before it can bound the endpoint output error.
