## Examples

These examples check the scalar CARE branch, a singular-state DARE pencil with an infinite eigenvalue, the effect of near-boundary conditioning on residual certification, and failures when stabilizability or detectability is absent. The iteration examples contrast exact Newton-Kleinman descent with an unchecked inexact step and show how exact SDA PSD invariants differ from a rounded block with a measurable symmetry defect.
