The examples compute both roots and companion eigenvectors for a damped scalar oscillator, distinguish algebraic multiplicity at infinity from the nullity of a singular leading matrix, and show a rational transfer matrix whose pole becomes a false polynomial root after clearing its denominator. Each calculation uses the domain and reversal conventions from the A page.
