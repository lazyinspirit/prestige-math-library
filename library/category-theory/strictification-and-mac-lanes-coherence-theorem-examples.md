These examples make the coherence theorem concrete. They write out canonical
comparison maps, display the free word category at small length, and compute the
strictification functor in a familiar cartesian case.

The counterexample on this page is the page's guardrail: formal vertices can
collapse to one object in a specific monoidal category, and once that happens a
noncanonical endomorphism can spoil commutativity.
