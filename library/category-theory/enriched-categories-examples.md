These examples and false statements make the enriched dictionary concrete. The
positive side shows how strict 2-categories, preorders, Lawvere metric spaces,
rings, powers in `Set`, and underlying-set change of base fit the abstract
definitions. The negative side isolates the traps that motivated the A page:
underlying hom-set bijections need not detect cotensors, conical limits are not
just ordinary limits, constant enriched functors can fail, the underlying
ordinary category can forget structure, large-source Yoneda does not construct
an entire enriched functor category, and a monoidal category can carry more
than one symmetry.
