These worked cases use the A-page state–field and formal-distribution conventions. A commutative differential algebra gives a vertex algebra by exponentiating its derivation. At critical level, the universal affine $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ vertex algebra still exists and its current OPE can be computed, while the normalized Sugawara denominator vanishes. Constant matrix fields give a direct counterexample to the claim that lower truncation alone implies locality.
