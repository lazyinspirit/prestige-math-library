These worked calculations test the normalization and parameter boundary in [[affine-current-algebras-virasoro-and-sugawara]]. The level-one central-charge example is conditional on a module satisfying the A-page theorem’s restricted-module hypotheses; it does not infer unitarity for arbitrary affine modules. The level-zero example verifies the trivial representation directly. The critical-level counterexample shows that the displayed normalized quotient is undefined when its denominator vanishes; it does not rule out other critical-level constructions.

## Examples

- [[ex-sl2-level-one-sugawara-central-charge]] computes $h^\vee=2$ and $c=1$ for normalized $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ at level one.
- [[ex-sl2-level-zero-trivial-sugawara]] verifies the one-dimensional trivial level-zero representation and its zero Sugawara modes.
- [[cex-critical-level-sugawara-formula-undefined]] computes the critical level $k=-2$ for normalized $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ and checks the zero denominator.

Each item gives its hypotheses and calculation. These examples clarify the A-page formulas; they are not theorem suppliers for broader module classes.
