These worked cases test the conventions and formulas on the A page. The matrix-amplification example computes a concrete framed inclusion and its first-stage trace; the XXZ example gives the $q=1$ three-site blocks; the Potts case separates the $Q=2,3,4$ values of $q$ and counts its open generators; and the rank-three case evaluates right closure on all five diagrams. The counterexample checks self-adjointness in the declared basis. The scaling remark preserves the source's cautious status and separates open chains from other boundary algebras.

Each example depends on the exact A-page definition or theorem listed in its item contract. The matrix-amplification example uses the proved first-stage basic-construction formula. The full ordinary finite-index tower is proved separately on the A page; the matrix example verifies its right-action extremality convention and first-stage normalization directly.
