These examples check the A-page conventions at the zero-loop, denominator, first-root, and low-level fusion boundaries. Each computation states the exact parameter and uses the A-page result it illustrates; none supplies a general theorem.

## Examples

- [[ex-temperley-lieb-zero-loop-normalized-trace-fails]] distinguishes the unnormalized diagram closure from the normalized trace when $\delta=0$.
- [[ex-temperley-lieb-loop-two-jones-wenzl-projector]] computes the first nontrivial projector at $\delta=2$.
- [[ex-temperley-lieb-localization-denominator-v-plus-one]] records where the normalized Hecke generators cease to be defined.
- [[ex-temperley-lieb-order-three-root-boundary]] works out the semisimple-but-trace-degenerate $\ell=3$ boundary and its quotient.
- [[ex-temperley-lieb-ell-four-and-five-fusion]] evaluates the truncated rule at $\ell=4$ and $\ell=5$.
