Assuming the Axiom of Choice, the one-dimensional conformal vertex algebra verifies the chosen admissible-module definition of rationality directly. The dual-number example shows why a finite-label constant-field subcategory of vertex-algebra modules need not be semisimple. It has no conformal vector, so it is not a counterexample to any rationality theorem for conformal vertex operator algebras.

## Example boundary

The A-page module-construction and grading proofs supply [[ex-rank-one-heisenberg-jordan-module-is-logarithmic]]. It uses a genuine conformal rank-one Heisenberg VOA, with $a_0=I+N$ and $L_F(0)|_E=I/2+N$ on its lowest two-dimensional space. The full Jacobi identity, lower truncation and finite lower-bounded generalized weights are proved by its A-page suppliers; its Jordan block is calculated here. The unrelated dual-number example remains explicitly nonconformal and is not used to discharge this construction. Triplet/W-algebra classification and modularity remain separately bounded research context.
