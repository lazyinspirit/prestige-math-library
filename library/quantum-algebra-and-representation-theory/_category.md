Published pages treat explicit algebras and their representations through Clifford and CAR algebras, Majorana generators, PBW bases, real and complex Clifford classification, graded products, and Pin and Spin modules. A diagram-algebra strand builds Temperley-Lieb algebras from noncrossing diagrams and Catalan bases, then covers cellularity, semisimplicity, Jones-Wenzl traces and quotients, root-of-unity behavior, and towers; Brauer, BMW, and partition algebras extend the diagrammatic setting. Other pages develop affine current algebras, Virasoro and Sugawara operators, vertex-algebra state-field foundations, and rational and logarithmic vertex modules.

These pages build on Abstract Algebra, Lie Theory, Representation Theory, Category Theory, Hopf Algebras & Hecke Algebras, and Quantum Groups and Crystal Bases. Braid Groups supplies constructions required by the braid-representation pages, while quantum representations draw on Quantum Groups and Crystal Bases. Hall Algebras and Tensor Categories uses related algebra and representation results. Clifford applications appear in Differential Geometry and Algebraic Topology, and braid-link pages connect to Knot Theory.
