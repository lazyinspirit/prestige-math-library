These examples check the positive-square-first table at low signatures, show how the even subalgebra determines graded-module types, and exhibit the failure of the ordinary tensor product for orthogonal Clifford sums. Each calculation uses the definitions and proved results on [[real-complex-clifford-classification-and-graded-products]].

## Worked checks

- [[ex-real-clifford-low-signature-models]] compares the first real matrix and division-algebra cases with the classification table.
- [[ex-graded-clifford-module-types-in-low-dimensions]] checks split and nonsplit even-algebra rows and the resulting graded-simple counts.
- [[cex-ordinary-tensor-product-fails-for-clifford-orthogonal-sums]] shows that commuting factor generators cannot model the anticommuting generators from orthogonal summands.
