Published pages define online decision protocols and compare regret to explicit benchmarks, then analyze Hedge, online gradient and mirror descent, adaptive regularization, confidence sequences, and stochastic, adversarial, and contextual bandits. Reinforcement-learning pages develop finite MDP Bellman operators, tabular prediction and control, PAC exploration, policy gradients, function approximation, planning, and average-reward regret. Further topics cover partial observability, offline evaluation, imitation, constrained policies, preference optimization, and verifiable generation. Guarantees preserve each problem’s feedback, sampling, stopping, approximation, and cost assumptions.

Probability and martingale theory underpin confidence sequences and bandit guarantees. Statistical Learning and Classical Models supplies prediction models; Linear Algebra and Functional Analysis support function approximation. Methods from Numerical Optimization, Inverse Problems and Uncertainty Quantification combine with differentiation from Neural Networks and Learning Dynamics for policy-gradient and preference methods. The category brings these foundations together to study decisions under sequential feedback, including bandits and finite-MDP control, with guarantees tied to the information each protocol reveals and feedback timing.
