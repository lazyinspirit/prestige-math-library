The page defines the embedded parabolic subalgebra, induction, restriction, and the coefficient-at-identity trace over the universal Hecke coefficient ring. It then proves both left and right coset-basis decompositions, the induced-module basis and the induction-restriction adjunction. For finite $W$, the coordinate-pairing argument proves $\tau(T_xT_y)=\delta_{x^{-1},y}$, so the trace is symmetric and perfect over the Laurent ring and after every commutative base change. No field semisimplicity is assumed.

The reading order places both definitions before their justifying theorems. Prerequisites: [[tensor-coherence-and-algebraic-descent]], [[coxeter-presentations-exchange-and-reduced-word-theorems]], and [[generic-coxeter-hecke-algebras-and-the-standard-basis]]. Worked calculations and the nonsemisimple specialization appear on [[hecke-parabolic-induction-and-symmetrizing-traces-examples]].
