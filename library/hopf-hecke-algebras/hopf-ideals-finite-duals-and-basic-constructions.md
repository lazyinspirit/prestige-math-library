This page separates the three descents needed for a Hopf quotient: multiplication, coproduct and counit, and antipode. It proves kernel and tensor-product constructions, finite-dimensional duality without assuming the antipode is bijective, and the finite dual of an arbitrary associative algebra by finite quotient and representation arguments. The final construction gives a PBW model and Hopf structure for Taft algebras.

The finite dual uses only functionals factoring through finite-dimensional quotient algebras, so its transposed multiplication is proved to land in an algebraic tensor product before it is used as a coproduct. The quotient and tensor-product branches are choice-free; AC is used only for the arbitrary-vector-space tensor-kernel assertion in the kernel branch.
