The page first defines left-left Yetter–Drinfeld modules and the coaction action of $H^{*\mathrm{op}}$. It then fixes a normal order for the finite Drinfeld double, proves the cross-relations associative, checks its coalgebra and antipode, and identifies its modules with Yetter–Drinfeld modules. The canonical tensor is finally verified as a quasitriangular structure.
