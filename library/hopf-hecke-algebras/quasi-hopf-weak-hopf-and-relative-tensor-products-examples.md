The examples compute a quasi-Hopf algebra from a normalized $3$-cocycle and a weak Hopf algebra from a two-object discrete groupoid. The counterexample shows that the quasi-bialgebra axioms alone do not supply a quasi-antipode. Each verification checks the displayed algebraic structure directly.

Companion: [[quasi-hopf-weak-hopf-and-relative-tensor-products]].

## Boundary counterexample

[[cex-a-quasi-bialgebra-need-not-be-quasi-hopf]] constructs a bialgebra with $\Phi=1$ whose nontrivial idempotent group-like element prevents any quasi-antipode. This keeps existence of an antipode separate from the quasi-bialgebra axioms.

## Weak-Hopf example

[[ex-two-object-discrete-groupoid-is-a-weak-hopf-algebra]] checks the weak unit, weak counit, and antipode equations for the two-object discrete groupoid algebra. It computes both the target base and the relative module tensor product.

## Quasi-Hopf example

[[ex-quasi-hopf-algebra-from-the-nontrivial-3-cocycle-on-c2]] verifies all quasi-bialgebra and quasi-antipode equations for the function algebra of $C_2$, including the pentagon and the action of the associator on a triple tensor product.
