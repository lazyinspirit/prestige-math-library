## foundations

Establish tensor descent and coherence before coalgebras, finite coefficient constructions, comodules, convolution and antipodes. Prove every quotient and finite-dual formula is well-defined before using it.

## hopf-structures

Construct tensor and finite dual representations, prove the Hopf-module inverse, then derive integrals and finite antipode bijectivity. Quasitriangular structures and finite doubles build on these suppliers.

## hecke-foundations

Prove Coxeter exchange and reduced-word independence before the generic Hecke basis. Parabolic freeness, trace perfection and carefully qualified specialization follow in dependency order.

## affine-cyclotomic-and-tensor-actions

Prove affine Laurent divisibility and PBW, then cyclotomic spanning and independence before integral cellular structure. The concrete tensor operator gives a Hecke action; full generic quantum centralizers remain a separately gated proof target.
