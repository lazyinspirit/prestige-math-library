The companion gives three fully worked applications of the definitions and results on [[comodules-matrix-coefficients-and-coalgebra-duality]]: group gradings, a two-dimensional coefficient matrix, and a regular module over an infinite-dimensional dual algebra that is not rational. The matrix example uses the $n=2$ instance of the finite comatrix coalgebra proved on the preceding page.
