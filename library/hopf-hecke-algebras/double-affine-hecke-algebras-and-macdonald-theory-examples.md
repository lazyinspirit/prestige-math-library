These calculations use the A-page type-$A_1$ normalization and test its parameter boundary.

## Worked computations

- [[ex-daha-a1-first-symmetric-macdonald-eigenfunctions]] verifies $P_0=1$ and $P_1=z+z^{-1}$ directly from the difference operator and its eigenvalue theorem.
- [[cex-daha-a1-eigenvalue-separation-fails-at-q-one]] shows that $q=1$ collapses the operator to a scalar and destroys pairwise eigenvalue separation.

The B items are checks of the A-page formulas; they do not supply general DAHA or Macdonald results.
