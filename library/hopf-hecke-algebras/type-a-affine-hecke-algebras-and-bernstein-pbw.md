For $n\ge2$, the universal coefficient ring is $R_0=\mathbb Z[Q,Q^{-1}]$, the weight lattice is $\mathbb Z^n$, and its Laurent algebra is $A=R_0[X_1^{\pm1},\ldots,X_n^{\pm1}]$. The page fixes the multiplicative normalization $(T_i-Q)(T_i+1)=0$ and proves the Laurent divisibility needed for the Bernstein divided differences before using them.

The proof chain supplies the finite Hecke basis in this normalization, constructs the rational skew group algebra and its Demazure–Lusztig operators, proves the affine relations directly, and derives the $R_0$-basis $\{X^\lambda T_w\}$. The center calculation is made over the universal ring. Base change transports the proved basis to every commutative specialization; no specialization claim about centers is made.

The construction is algebraic in the weight lattice and finite Weyl group. It does not require a p-adic field or an Iwahori decomposition. The companion page works out the rank-two relation, a negative-exponent divided difference, the polynomial action, and central symmetric polynomials.
