These worked calculations use the universal multiplicative presentation of [[type-a-affine-hecke-algebras-and-bernstein-pbw]]. They cover a negative Laurent exponent, the rank-two Bernstein relation, the polynomial-module action, and central symmetric polynomials.

The p-adic Iwahori–Hecke algebra is a separate construction: after specifying a nonarchimedean local field, a reductive group, and an Iwahori subgroup, one forms the convolution algebra of compactly supported, Iwahori-bi-invariant functions with a chosen Haar measure. The algebraic presentation on the A page supplies none of that data and proves no identification with that convolution algebra.

The degenerate affine Hecke algebra uses polynomial variables $x_j$, symmetric-group generators $s_i$, and an additive relation such as $s_ix_{i+1}=x_is_i+1$. This page instead uses invertible Laurent variables $X_j$ and the multiplicative relation $T_iX_iT_i=QX_{i+1}$. At $Q=1$, the latter presentation has $T_i^2=1$ and $T_if=s_i(f)T_i$; the additive relation belongs to the separate degenerate presentation.
