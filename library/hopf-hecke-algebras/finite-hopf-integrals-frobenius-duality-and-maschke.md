Nonzero finite-dimensional Hopf algebras have one-dimensional spaces of left and right integrals, a bijective antipode, and a nondegenerate associative Frobenius pairing. Normalized left integrals also characterize semisimplicity through an explicit averaging operator. The proofs keep left and right conventions distinct, derive the dual regular Hopf module before using the Hopf-module structure theorem, and use finite-dimensional splitting arguments for the semisimplicity criterion.

## Scope

This page proves the finite-dimensional integral theorem, the Frobenius pairing and dual-basis identities, and the Hopf–Maschke criterion. It does not assert semisimplicity of the dual Hopf algebra or involutivity of the antipode in arbitrary characteristic.
