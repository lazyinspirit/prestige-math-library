The examples check a scalar linear drift with nondegenerate constant diffusion
against every strong-solution hypothesis, then use a deterministic initial
value to show the limit of that conclusion: strong well-posedness permits an
initial law concentrated on a Lebesgue-null singleton and does not itself
assert a density at the initial endpoint.
