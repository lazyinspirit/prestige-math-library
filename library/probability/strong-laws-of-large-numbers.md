Truncation and variance summability establish the IID integrable strong law. The converse identifies integrability as necessary for finite almost-sure limits; Etemadi uses only pairwise independence. A separate maximal-ergodic argument proves the probability-space Birkhoff theorem and recovers the IID result on coordinate shifts. Finite variance gives the stated logarithmic normalization. Product constructions explicitly assume AC.
