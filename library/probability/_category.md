---
title: Probability
---

The Probability pages develop probability spaces, random elements, laws, expectation, moments, and inequalities, then build limit theory for independent variables and weak convergence. They cover characteristic functions, central limit theorems, Borel–Cantelli and zero–one laws, conditional expectation, and regular conditional distributions. Further pages develop discrete-time martingales, stopping and optional stopping, convergence theorems, Markov kernels and chains, recurrence, and stationary ergodic limits. The collection constructs Brownian motion, studies its path and Markov properties, and develops stochastic integration, Itô’s formula, and well-posedness for forward stochastic differential equations. Product measures and Kolmogorov extension provide the framework for random processes.

Measure Theory, Real Analysis, Set Theory, Topology, and Combinatorics provide the integration, convergence, and construction tools used here. Mathematical Statistics, Bayesian Computational Statistics, Online and Reinforcement Learning, Numerical Optimization Inverse Problems and Uncertainty Quantification, Information Theory, and Representation Learning and Generative Models build on these results about probability laws, conditional expectation, limits, and stochastic processes.
