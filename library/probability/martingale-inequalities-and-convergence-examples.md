Centered random walks illustrate both Doob maximal bounds, and dyadic and reverse conditional-expectation martingales identify their limiting sigma-algebras. A nonnegative martingale is shown to converge almost surely without an unjustified equality of expectations.

The nested-interval martingale supplies two explicit boundary failures: $L^1$ boundedness need not give $L^1$ convergence or preserve expectation, and its finite closed versions have maximal $L^1$ norm $n/2+1$, ruling out a horizon-independent $p=1$ analogue. The final random-walk calculation specializes Azuma to the Gaussian-scale bound $2e^{-t^2/(2n)}$.
