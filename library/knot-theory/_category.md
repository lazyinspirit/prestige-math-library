name: knot-theory
title: Knot Theory
---

These pages study knots and links through their complements, diagrams, algebraic presentations and geometric structures. Classical invariants include crossing data, colorings, Seifert matrices, Alexander modules and polynomials, signatures and branched-cover homology. Quantum and categorified directions include skein modules, Jones-type polynomials, Khovanov homology, knot Floer homology, the Kontsevich integral and finite-type invariants. Three-manifold topics cover surgery, satellite constructions, hyperbolic exteriors, JSJ tori and exceptional fillings; concordance pages treat slice obstructions, four-ball genus, ribbon knots, cobordism maps and filtrations. The collection also includes terminating computations, unknot recognition and a page of open questions, alongside conjectural targets such as the volume and slice-ribbon conjectures.

Algebraic Topology supplies homology and cohomology; Differential Geometry and Differential Topology provide smooth manifolds, surfaces and cobordisms. Abstract Algebra, Braid Groups, Group Theory, Quantum Algebra and Representation Theory, and Homological Algebra contribute algebraic tools for invariant constructions. Together, these subjects provide the tools to study knot complements, three-manifold surgery and concordance.
