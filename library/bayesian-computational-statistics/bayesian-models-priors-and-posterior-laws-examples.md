The examples compute a finite posterior and show why a null-data posterior version is not unique; realize a posterior RCD as a sample-point-indexed random probability measure; construct a standard-Borel posterior kernel for countable data; and verify the dominated-density formula on a nonstandard-Borel observation space. The last example uses a separate, choice-free construction and does not rely on standard-Borel factorization.
