The examples separate the exact $P=Q$ null from a weak parameter null,
demonstrate studentization for a mean difference and a discrete rank
U-statistic, and verify that positive kernel second moment does not guarantee
nondegenerate first projections. A counterexample shows that weak-null
centering of the two-sample kernel does not imply centering under every mixed
assignment. Another records the scale error caused by multiplying a
standard-error-studentized pivot by an extra $\sqrt m$.
