These finite-state examples compute acceptance probabilities for a symmetric
random walk, an independence proposal, a proposal with disconnected support,
and a rejected self-transition. They verify the detailed-balance flow in each
case and show concretely that invariance does not by itself ensure
irreducibility or uniqueness.
