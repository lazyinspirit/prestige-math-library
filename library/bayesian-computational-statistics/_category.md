---
title: Bayesian Computational Statistics
---
These pages connect Bayesian modeling to computation and uncertainty calibration. They cover
prior and posterior laws, propriety, conjugate and hierarchical models, prediction and Bayes
actions, marginal likelihood, consistency and contraction, Bernstein–von Mises limits,
Gaussian-process and Dirichlet-process priors, and nonparametric inference. Computational
topics include EM, variational and Laplace approximations, approximate Bayesian computation,
Monte Carlo and quasi-Monte Carlo, importance and rejection sampling, MCMC and diagnostics,
filtering and particle methods, bootstrap and permutation procedures, cross-validation, and
error and cost accounting. Guarantees separate sampling error from approximation, model
bias, and data uncertainty.

Probability, Measure Theory, Real Analysis, Mathematical Statistics, Statistical Models and
Experiments, and Linear Algebra supply model and inference foundations; Numerical Linear
Algebra and Approximation provides quadrature and discrepancy estimates. Inverse Problems
uses prior and posterior laws for Gaussian inverse models; Monte Carlo and multilevel bounds
support Numerical Optimization Inverse Problems and Uncertainty Quantification and Numerical
Differential Equations and Stochastic Dynamics. Leapfrog and interpolation results appear in
Numerical Pdes and Spatial Discretization. Gaussian-process results serve Statistical
Learning and Classical Models; variational inference serves Machine Learning Algorithms.
