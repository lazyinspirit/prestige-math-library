## Summary

These examples separate point count from replicate count, compare a digital shift with nested scrambling, and show both the smooth-integrand rate and its limits. Under the stated AC assumption for the Student $t_1$ calibration, the skewed two-point replicate law has an unbiased variance estimate but very poor coverage for a two-replicate Student interval.
