These finite and continuous examples calculate the augmented stationary target,
show how a positive tolerance and a summary can differ from the exact-data
posterior, derive an ABC-SMC mixture weight by hand, and exhibit vanishing
acceptance and finite-particle collapse. Each calculation checks only its
stated model and does not establish general accuracy or convergence.
