## Summary

On the ambient product $\{0,1\}^2$, a target supported only on the diagonal makes every single-site update stay at its current supported state; the resulting two-state restricted chain is reducible. The remaining examples compute both full conditionals for a correlated bivariate Gaussian, then use cross-time covariance to show a fixed systematic sweep can be nonreversible despite preserving the Gaussian target.
