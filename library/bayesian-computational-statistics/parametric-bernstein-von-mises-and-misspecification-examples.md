## Objective

The examples compute an exact normal-normal posterior in a correctly specified regular model, show how a unit-variance normal working model can produce credible intervals with nonnominal sampling coverage under a truth with variance $\sigma^2\ne1$, and calculate the truncated posterior that arises when the normal mean is at the boundary. The computations separate posterior width from sampling width and show why the interior-point hypothesis matters.
