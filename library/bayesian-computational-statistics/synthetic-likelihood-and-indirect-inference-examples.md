## Examples

A Bernoulli summary has a non-Gaussian two-point law even though its mean and variance define a positive-variance Gaussian synthetic likelihood. A finite model shows how a non-injective summary binding map can lose parameter identification and how the declared tie rule still selects a target. A two-dimensional Rademacher summary has a positive-definite population covariance, while its two-simulation covariance is random and always singular.
