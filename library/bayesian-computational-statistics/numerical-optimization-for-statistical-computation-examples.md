These examples instantiate the complete-data and observed-data factorization,
show why a zero evidence fibre does not define a pointwise conditional density,
and compute a Gaussian KL value. A quadratic verifies the strongly convex
gradient-descent rate. A quartic objective exhibits two distinct global
minima and an intervening local maximum; this landscape makes no claim about
which point a local algorithm will reach.
