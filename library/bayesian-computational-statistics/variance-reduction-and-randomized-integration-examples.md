These finite and elementary examples calculate when a known-mean control removes variance, when a control has no effect, how a monotone antithetic pair cancels exactly, and how proportional stratification compares with an ordinary sample mean. A two-observation regression example also verifies that fitting a control coefficient on the production sample need not preserve unbiasedness; the pilot example checks conditional unbiasedness when the coefficient is independent of production data.
