## Objective

These examples verify a smooth white-noise functional, show a nonlinear remainder that survives at the root-n scale, and instantiate the declared type-S and type-H prior conditions at a uniform truth. The final counterexample separates posterior credibility from frequentist coverage when no BvM condition is available.
