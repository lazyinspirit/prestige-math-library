## Examples

- [[ex-mlmc-finite-level-telescoping-and-bias]] computes the finite-level telescope and shows the remaining discretization bias separately from sampling variance.
- [[ex-mlmc-euler-strong-and-weak-cost-regimes]] compares the finite-$L^2$ strong, conditional weak-order-one, and autonomous all-moment Euler contracts.
- [[ex-mlmc-discontinuous-payoff-half-rate]] gives an exact coupled discontinuous-payoff hierarchy with bias exponent one and correction-variance exponent one-half.
- [[cex-mlmc-no-bias-rate-no-accuracy]] shows that finite correction variances and bounded work do not control target bias.
- [[cex-mlmc-no-positive-variance-decay]] gives a hierarchy whose nondecaying correction variance forces at least cubic work despite bias order one.
