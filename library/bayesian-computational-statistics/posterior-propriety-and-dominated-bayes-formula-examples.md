A finite model illustrates a valid positive evidence normalizer. A separate
finite dominated model has a prior-predictive null fiber, where the quotient is
undefined and a conditional-law version supplies only a filler value. With a
flat Lebesgue prior, at least one known-variance normal observation yields a
proper normal posterior; the improper Beta(0,0) prior instead has an infinite
binomial posterior normalizer at either all-failure or all-success data. The
last two examples are fiberwise updates under sigma-finite improper priors and
make no prior-predictive probability claim.
