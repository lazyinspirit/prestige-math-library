## Summary

These examples check the Koksma–Hlawka bound on a smooth integrand, show an integrable inverse-CDF transform whose endpoint singularity has infinite variation, and separate good one-coordinate projections from joint net quality. A final example distinguishes the arbitrary point counts allowed for quadrature and lattice rules from the power-of-base sizes required of digital nets.
