A polynomial-tail truth satisfies the polished-tail block-energy condition
and has conservative frequentist coverage after sufficiently large inflation.
The sparse-gap truth lies in a polynomial hyperrectangle but has coverage
tending to zero along a subsequence, when its regularity cap exceeds the
smoothness index and its scale and inflation satisfy the stated bounds.
