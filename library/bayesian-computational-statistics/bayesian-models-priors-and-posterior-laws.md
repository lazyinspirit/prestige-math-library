This page defines a proper Bayesian model as a prior probability paired with a parameter-measurable sampling kernel. It constructs the prior-predictive joint law, distinguishes the sample-point-indexed posterior RCD from a data-indexed posterior kernel, and states the exact standard-Borel and Axiom-of-Choice conditions for their existence and factorization. Null-data versions and the separate dominated-density route are kept explicit; fixed-data posterior propriety is developed on the next A page.
