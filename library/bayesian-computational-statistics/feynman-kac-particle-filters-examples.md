## Objective

Finite calculations compare bootstrap and informed proposals, show how missing proposal support loses positive target mass, and compute multinomial versus residual offspring counts for one weight vector. The examples illustrate the scope of the general identities without claiming general finite-particle accuracy or a uniform variance ordering for the resampling schemes.
