## Objective

The examples verify the global-entropy theorem in a regular Bernoulli density model, calculate the source-covered Hölder entropy balance under its explicit prior-mass premise, and show that entropy is sufficient rather than necessary by using a point prior whose posterior remains exactly at the truth while a deliberately broad sieve violates the entropy inequality. The Bernoulli rate includes the logarithmic overhead of the global entropy condition; none of the examples asserts a generic minimax or almost-sure guarantee.
