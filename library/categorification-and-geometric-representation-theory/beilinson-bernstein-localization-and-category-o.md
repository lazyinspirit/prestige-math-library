The weight parameter is complex; antidominance excludes positive integral positive-coroot pairings. The TDO has isotropy character $\kappa+\rho$, so $\mathcal D_{-\rho}$ is ordinary differential operators. Global TDO sections are the exact central quotient for every parameter. Global sections is exact for antidominant parameters and gives the regular antidominant equivalence, including coherent modules. At singular antidominant parameters the proved replacement is the Serre quotient and the complete simple-parabolic kernel filtration under the stated full zero-subsystem condition.
