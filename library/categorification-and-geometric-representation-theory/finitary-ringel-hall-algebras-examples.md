This companion checks the general definition in three settings: finite
vector spaces over $\mathbb F_q$, finite abelian $p$-groups, and nilpotent
representations of the one-loop Jordan quiver. The first and third exhibit the
$q+1$ lines in a two-dimensional vector space; the $p$-group calculation
also includes the nonsplit middle group $C_{p^2}$. All coefficients use the
subobject-counting normalization from the A page.

These worked calculations test the definitions and boundary cases. They do not
supply general Hall algebra theorems to other pages. The Hall multiplication
remains distinct from the Hall bilinear form on symmetric functions.

The Hall–Littlewood calculation checks the partition images and all three
pairing conventions through degree two, using the proved A-page
identification rather than assuming a ring isomorphism preserves a pairing.
