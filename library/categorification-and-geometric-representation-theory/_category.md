Categorification and geometric representation theory studies algebraic structures through categories, functors, and geometry. These pages develop Hopf and Hecke algebras, quantum enveloping algebras, integral forms, highest-weight modules, crystal lattices, tensor-product crystals, and global bases. Quantum affine representations and Fock spaces connect these constructions to KLR algebras, highest-weight categorification, and Ariki decomposition-number theorems. Kazhdan–Lusztig theory, Soergel methods, projective functors, category O, and localization provide categorical and geometric approaches to characters and multiplicities. Hall algebras organize extension data in finitary, derived, motivic, and cohomological settings; further topics include Yangians, root-of-unity quantum groups, tilting quotients, braided tensor structures, stability, wall crossing, sewing, and defects.

Abstract Algebra, Category Theory, and Homological Algebra supply ring, module, tensor, and derived foundations. Coxeter Groups, Lie Theory, and Representation Theory of Groups provide reflection and highest-weight methods; Algebraic Geometry supports descent and convolution. Symmetric Groups and Symmetric Functions supplies branching and partition combinatorics, while Braid Groups contributes categorical actions and diagram constructions. The reading pathway follows these prerequisite chains through quantum groups and crystals to categorification, and through Hall integration and tensor categories to geometric and topological applications. Related Clifford, diagram-algebra, and vertex-algebra topics remain in Quantum Algebra and Representation Theory. Example companions accompany the theory pages, with each construction retaining its own hypotheses and conventions.
