## algebraic-and-geometric-foundations

Begin with tensor coherence, ordinary Hecke Specht constructions, Kazhdan–Lusztig bases, proper descent, and equivariant convolution. These are independent entry points within this collection; follow each page’s prerequisites in other categories.

## coalgebras-hecke-bases-and-categorification

Develop coalgebras and generic Hecke bases alongside Specht modules, Soergel Hodge theory, and KLR induction. The quantum-symmetry interface connects the algebraic branches.

## affine-hecke-hopf-and-highest-weight-structures

Continue to comodules, bialgebras, Hecke induction and traces, affine Hecke algebras, and tensor actions. Cyclotomic KLR categorification and projective functors prepare the highest-weight branches; noninvertible symmetries begin the tensor-category applications.

## quotients-characters-and-tensor-categories

Study cyclotomic quotients, the Hecke–KLR bridge, specialization, unequal parameters, and double-affine algebras. Hopf modules and duality proceed alongside character formulas, rational Cherednik algebras, finitary Hall algebras, and modular categories.

## hall-realizations-and-finite-structures

Build Green’s coproduct, derived and cohomological Hall constructions after their counting and categorical prerequisites. Cellular Murphy bases, finite Hopf integrals, quasitriangular structures, singular and parabolic multiplicities, and higher defects extend the parallel branches.

## fock-spaces-doubles-and-hall-integration

Parabolic category O supplies the Fock-space bases used later. Drinfeld doubles, Frobenius sewing, Hall modules and geometric actions, and motivic integration follow their structural prerequisites.

## schur-weyl-duality-and-wall-crossing

Higher-level Schur–Weyl duality provides the functorial input for the degenerate Ariki theorem. The Hall branch reaches stability, DT/BPS invariants, and wall crossing after its integration constructions.

## decomposition-numbers-and-geometric-routes

Finish with Ariki categorification and graded decomposition numbers, the separate degenerate Ariki theorem, and the category-O/KLR/Hecke route. Beilinson–Bernstein localization follows the prerequisites declared on its page.
