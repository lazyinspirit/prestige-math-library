These finite calculations check the route conventions on the A page:
[[category-o-klr-and-hecke-categorification-route]]. They do not supply a
general Schur–Weyl, Ariki, or KLR categorification theorem.

## Worked computations

- [[ex-category-o-degenerate-tensor-functor-on-trivial-sl2-module]] checks the
  normalized one-strand degenerate generator on a finite-dimensional object
  of the BGG category.
- [[ex-level-one-e2-hecke-klr-block]] computes both rank-one algebras and
  checks the nonzero-block hypothesis for the finite $e=2$ bridge.
- [[cex-place-permutation-is-not-a-nondegenerate-hecke-generator]] shows that
  the degenerate place permutation cannot simply be relabeled as the
  nondegenerate Hecke generator at $\xi=-1$.

The proofs use explicit finite-dimensional vectors, one-strand quotients, and
the displayed quadratic relation. Their parameter and grading conventions
remain those of the A page.
