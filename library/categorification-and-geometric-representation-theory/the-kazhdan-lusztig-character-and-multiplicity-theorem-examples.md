This companion collects the finite computations for
[[the-kazhdan-lusztig-character-and-multiplicity-theorem]]. The principal
$\mathfrak{sl}_3$ block is computed in full: the Kazhdan–Lusztig basis of the
normalized Hecke algebra $H_v(3)$, the multiplicity matrix, which is the Bruhat
zeta matrix, and its integer inverse, whose signed rows give the simple
characters. A counterexample on the ten-element interval $[1324,3412]$ of $S_4$
shows that the leading coefficient of a Kazhdan–Lusztig polynomial is not the
ordinary multiplicity, which is the value at $q=1$. This numerical witness
does not rule out a graded realization; constructions and proofs of Koszulity
and $\mathrm{Ext}$ refinements remain outside this page. The final
example works the rank-one $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ case: the two blocks $C_0$ and
$C_1$ have identical multiplicity data, and the actual tensor/projection
equivalences transport the impossible-to-split Verma sequence
$0\to M_{-2}\to M_0\to L_0\to0$ to $0\to M_{-3}\to M_1\to L_1\to0$.
