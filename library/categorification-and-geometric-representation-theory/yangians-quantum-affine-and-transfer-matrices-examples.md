This companion contains a concrete two-site trace calculation and a counterexample to inferring completeness from a Bethe equation. Both examples refer to the exact type-A RTT and periodic-chain conventions on [[yangians-quantum-affine-and-transfer-matrices]].

The two-site example reuses the single finite $\mathbb C^2$ rational family [[def-hh-rational-spectral-r-matrix]]. The counterexample uses a direct sum of counit modules to show that a scalar equation can hold while every positive-root Bethe vector vanishes.
