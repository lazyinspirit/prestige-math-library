A sequential prespectrum records spaces together with coherent suspension
maps. Its stable homotopy groups are explicit colimits of ordinary homotopy
groups, so strict maps, homotopies, tail independence, and products can all be
checked on representatives without invoking a stable model category.

The sphere prespectrum recovers the classical stable stems because
Freudenthal makes its suspension system eventually constant in every
nonnegative degree. Shift bookkeeping follows the displayed colimit grading:
moving the levels left lowers the stable index, while the right-shifted
sequential suspension raises it. Pairings descend to a graded product, and
the sphere-coordinate permutations account for the Koszul sign.

This page is intentionally elementary. It does not calculate a positive
stable stem or construct Brown representability, replacement functors,
localizations, or the stable homotopy category.
