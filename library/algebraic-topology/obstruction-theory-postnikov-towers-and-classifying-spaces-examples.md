These examples locate the first section obstruction for a sphere fibration,
identify $\mathbb{CP}^{\infty}$ as $K(\mathbb Z,2)$ and
$\mathbb{RP}^{\infty}$ as $K(\mathbb Z/2,1)$, and recognize the first
nontrivial Postnikov section of a simply connected space. The trivial
principal bundle illustrates the basepoint of the classification bijection.

The two boundary examples keep their quantifiers visible. Separately chosen
maps on a common $1$-cell need not combine into one extension problem, even
though vanishing cell obstructions for one fixed skeleton map do glue. The
smooth long line supplies a locally trivial but nonnumerable tangent-frame
bundle, showing why Milnor's theorem classifies numerable bundles rather than
all locally trivial bundles over arbitrary bases.
