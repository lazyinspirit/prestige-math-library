This companion works through the three-bit repetition code, the binary $[7,4,3]$ Hamming code, and a short $[4,2,3]_5$ Reed–Solomon evaluation code. It also computes a quantum repetition-code projector and exhibits a weight-one undetectable operator at distance one. The finite syndrome and projector calculations are explicit; the page remains a logical leaf and supplies no prerequisites to the theory page.
