## Objective

Develop the finite phase-aware stabilizer formalism for ordered qubit registers. Prove the stabilizer average and code-dimension formula, characterize deterministic and fair Pauli measurement branches, and show that exact signed-tableau updates reproduce the projective instrument, including impossible outcomes. The final simulation result gives explicit bit and workspace bounds for adaptive circuits generated by $H$, $S$, $S^\dagger$, and CNOT from computational-basis inputs. The current QC09 syntax, unitary-denotation, and resource bodies provide the circuit interfaces; this page states the Pauli-specific finite-history tree used by the theorem. The proof uses instrument branch laws directly and does not require uniqueness of a POVM induced by a general instrument.
