This page builds multiplicity-aware fusion spaces in a unitary fusion category, defines F and pullback R coordinates, and proves their unitarity and the pentagon and hexagon equations with every intermediate charge and multiplicity index. It also records gauge covariance, modular nondegeneracy, topological spin, framed ribbon evaluation, and the distinction between algebraic braid or Hecke relations and positive unitary structure. The Fibonacci carrier verifies one complete chiral model; the Ising carrier is limited to its stated three-anyon sector.
