The one-edge graph gives a two-qubit state with one negative computational-basis amplitude and generators $X\otimes Z$ and $Z\otimes X$. A four-vertex open-graph pattern separately checks all four outcomes of the CZ gadget, including its branch probability and exact Pauli/Hadamard correction convention.
