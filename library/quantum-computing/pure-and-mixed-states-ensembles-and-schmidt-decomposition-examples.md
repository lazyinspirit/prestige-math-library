The Bell vector is already a two-term Schmidt form: its positive coefficients are both $1/\sqrt2$ and each factor family is an ordered orthonormal basis. Expanding its outer product and contracting either factor gives $I_2/2$, checking the tensor order and partial-trace index convention directly. The example also shows that this density operator has two distinct equal-weight ensemble decompositions, in the computational and $\{|+\rangle,|-\rangle\}$ bases; see [[ex-qc-bell-state-schmidt-reduction]].
