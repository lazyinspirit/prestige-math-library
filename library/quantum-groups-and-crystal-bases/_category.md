Quantum groups deform enveloping algebras while retaining structures that make highest-weight theory, tensor products, and combinatorial bases accessible. The published sequence develops quantized enveloping algebras and quantum Serre relations, divided powers and integral forms, quantum highest-weight modules, Kashiwara operators and crystal lattices, tensor-product crystals, and highest-weight uniqueness. Balanced triples yield global crystal bases with fixed lattice and normalization conventions. Further pages treat quantum affine type A, level-one Fock space, residue crystals, Yangians and transfer matrices, and root-of-unity quantum groups with tilting quotients.

Abstract Algebra, Lie Theory, and Categorification and Geometric Representation Theory provide classical, root-data, and structural inputs. Symmetric Groups and Symmetric Functions supplies modular-branching context for residue crystals. Categorification and Geometric Representation Theory uses global bases, Fock spaces, and quantum-group realizations in its KLR, highest-weight, category-O, and Hall constructions. Quantum Algebra and Representation Theory draws on the same quantum structures.
