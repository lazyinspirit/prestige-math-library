This page collects pure-pair results for path–antipath and forest classes, together with the leaf–co-leaf extension construction.

The forest argument constructs support-regular blockades and embeds rooted trees before deducing the strong Erdős–Hajnal property. The viral extension argument deletes a leaf from one forbidden graph and a co-leaf from the other; its copy-count and sparse-blockade lemmas supply the proof.
