These examples spend the two finite checks that the A-page proofs actually use.
The first isolates the one-subdivided-star model behind the $E$-graph route and
the pendant-leaf deletion back to $P_5$. The second records an explicit
six-vertex witness graph for Bird, checks the homogeneous pair that gives it the
Erdős-Hajnal property by substitution, and verifies the induced co-Bird models
inside its $H^+$ and $H^-$ extensions.
