These examples pin the page to explicit small graphs: the bull is
self-complementary, $C_5$ is the standard bull-free non-perfect witness, and
$K_4$ exhibits a visible nontrivial module. The two false statements record the
two temptations the A-page theorems do not justify: bull-free does not imply
perfect, and two-narrow does not collapse to one-narrow.
