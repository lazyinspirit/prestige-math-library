These examples separate four easy-to-conflate ideas: mixedness itself is not
transitive, the quotient merges exactly the mixed-chain components, mixing can
appear only after unioning a quotient block, and the abstract witness descent
of Lemma 6.2 can be checked on a concrete finite configuration.
