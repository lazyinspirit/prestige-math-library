This page closes the generalized-niceness route. The preceding page produced a
constant-scale restricted theorem; the present page adds the Rödl
initialization that removes the starting restriction and then isolates the
blockade hypothesis needed for the published blockade-to-restricted theorem.

The final theorem converts those local restricted or blockade outcomes into a
polynomial clique or stable set and then invokes complement invariance to pass
from the $\overline{\mathcal F}$-free formulation used in generalized niceness
back to the ordinary Erdős-Hajnal property of $\mathcal F$.
