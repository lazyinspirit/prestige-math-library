This page is intentionally narrow. It fixes the blockade vocabulary used in the
iterative Erdős–Hajnal literature, isolates the pattern-graph viewpoint for pure
blockades, and records the two gateway arguments that convert either
complete/anticomplete blockade hypotheses or large sparse-pair hypotheses into
restricted induced subgraphs or sparse blockades.

The later cograph and iterative-comb pages own the deeper structure theory. Here
the pattern graph is used only as much as the gateway theorem needs: a
$P_4$-free pattern yields a large homogeneous set of blocks, which in turn yields
a complete or anticomplete subblockade. The second theorem is even more local:
it is the maximal-blockade extraction argument that turns repeated large sparse
pairs into a long ordered blockade.
