This companion collects finite computations, boundary cases and
counterexamples for [[roots-and-rational-powers]]. It computes rational powers
of rational bases from the definition, including the negative-exponent and
non-coprime-representative clauses, and works through odd roots of negative
reals, where $x \mapsto x^{n}$ is a bijection but the symbol $a^{1/n}$ is not
extended to negative bases. Equality cases are tracked: the two-term
arithmetic-geometric mean, its reciprocal form $x + x^{-1} \ge 2$, the
two-term Lagrange identity for Cauchy–Schwarz, Young at $(2,2)$ and
$(3,3/2)$, Hölder at $(3,3/2)$, and two-entry Minkowski, whose strict and
equality instances match the determinant criterion. Two comparison examples
verify the finite routes to Bernoulli's inequality and to AM-GM, including the
rational-exponent forms and the quadratic refinement. The counterexamples mark
each load-bearing hypothesis: nonnegative AM-GM entries, normalised
nonnegative weights, $p \ge 1$ in Minkowski, $x \ge -1$ in Bernoulli, and a
positive base for the iterated rational-power law.
