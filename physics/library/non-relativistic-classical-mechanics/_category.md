These pages formulate non-relativistic Newtonian mechanics on affine spacetime with absolute time, Euclidean spatial slices, and Galilean inertial frames. They specify quantities, particle worldlines and configuration spaces; adopt force and initial-data models; and derive conditional motion, momentum, angular-momentum and energy balances, constrained and rigid-body dynamics, central-force and scattering results, oscillations, variational and Hamiltonian formulations, symmetry and conservation laws, stability, approximation, and continuum balances. Each conclusion is relative to its declared force law, frame, regularity, constraint, and constitutive assumptions. Mathematical results establish consequences of those assumptions; they do not establish that a model describes nature, and measurement-related claims retain the stated sampling and uncertainty conditions.

The companion Non-relativistic Classical Mechanics Mathematics category supplies affine-time geometry, Galilean transformations, variations, canonical methods, balance, stability, and continuum results, with real-analysis, linear-algebra, geometry, topology, and measure-theory prerequisites. Classical Statistical Mechanics uses this subject’s framework, units, and Hamiltonian constructions in its phase-space and stochastic-response models. The physics pages also draw on real and functional analysis, PDE, and probability for specific results.
