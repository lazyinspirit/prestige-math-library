Worked checks for [[cm13-symplectic-geometry-and-canonical-methods]].
[[ex-cm13-fundamental-brackets-and-evolution-checks]] evaluates the bracket
and the evolution law on one-label data of the force branch, where
$\{x^{a},H\}=m^{-1}p_{a}$ and $\{p_{a},H\}=-U_{x^{a}}$, and checks the
oscillator case of the derived bracket algebra.
[[ex-cm13-isotropic-oscillator-flow-symplectic-and-volume-checks]] carries
the isotropic oscillator through its explicit flow, verifying the matrix
and determinant tests of canonicity on each two-dimensional block and the
area and energy checks of the motion.
[[ex-cm13-free-particle-flow-generating-function]] exhibits the free-particle
flow $Q=q+(t/m)p$ as generated by the first-kind action function
$S_{t}(q,Q)=-m\lvert Q-q\rvert^{2}/(2t)$, with the projection check and the
exact change of the canonical one-form.
[[ex-cm13-point-transformation-generating-function-and-projection]]
generates the point-transformation chart transition
$(q,\pi)\mapsto(X(q),DX(q)^{-\mathsf T}\pi)$, from generalized momentum $\pi$
to Cartesian momentum, using the second-kind action function $S(q,P)=P\cdot
X(q)$. It shows that the $(q,Q)$-projection is nowhere invertible, so the
local converse's invertible-projection hypothesis cannot be dropped; the
momentum shift supplies the contrast. [[ex-cm13-determinant-tests-for-linear-canonical-maps]]
proves on explicit linear maps that $\det A=1$ is equivalent to canonicity
in dimension two but strictly weaker in dimension four, by the
volume-preserving shear with $\{Q^{2},P_{1}\}=1$.
[[ex-cm13-verlet-map-of-the-mechanical-splitting-is-canonical]] verifies that
the velocity-Verlet step of the model's mechanical splitting is a canonical
map of the presentation: the drift and kick are exact flows with unit
determinant, their composite preserves the two-form, and the oscillator
instance is computed explicitly.
