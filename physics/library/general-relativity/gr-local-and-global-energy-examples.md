The flat examples use the standard coordinate orientation for any
finite-chain flux. In the SI dust reading the coefficient $\rho$ of
$T=\rho u\otimes u$, with $g(u,u)=-1$, is rest energy density; the
rest-mass density is $\rho/c^2$.

This page collects the worked computations of
[[gr-local-and-global-energy]] on the flat model. The measured fields of a
translating dust, together with the local balance reduced to the dust
continuity equation, are
[[ex-gr-translating-dust-measured-energy-and-balance]]; the accelerated
boost-Killing congruence in the wedge, where the measured current has the
nonvanishing source $-\Phi^aa_a$ while the redshift-weighted current is
divergence-free, is
[[ex-gr-boost-congruence-energy-balance-in-flat-space]]; and the perfect-fluid
energy equation $D\varepsilon+(\varepsilon+p)\theta=0$ recovered from the
balance is
[[ex-gr-perfect-fluid-energy-equation-from-the-local-balance]]. The Killing
energy of a constant static dust, its current three-form, its value on slice
chains and its conservation up to the side flux are
[[ex-gr-killing-energy-charge-of-a-static-dust]]; its conserved charge uses
the prescribed divergence-free tensor and Killing symmetry, without
asserting that nonzero dust on the flat metric solves the field law. Two counterexamples separate
the notions: measured energy density depends on the observer congruence
([[cex-gr-measured-energy-density-depends-on-the-observer]]), and
divergence-freedom does not force the measured energy density to be
nonnegative
([[cex-gr-conservation-does-not-imply-nonnegative-measured-energy-density]]).
