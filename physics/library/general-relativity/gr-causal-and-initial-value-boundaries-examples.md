This page collects the worked computations and countermodels of
[[gr-causal-and-initial-value-boundaries]] on the flat models.

**Flat computations.** [[ex-gr-minkowski-level-sets-and-causal-double-cones]]
verifies the Minkowski Cauchy property, the causal convexity of the double
cones, the compactness of a causal diamond and the domain of dependence of an
inertial slice by explicit coordinate inequalities.
[[ex-gr-flat-slab-vertical-line-proper-time]] records the affine parameter and
length of the vertical geodesics of the open slab, their maximal domain and
the absent boundary point. [[ex-gr-radial-focusing-in-flat-minkowski]]
computes the radial congruence converging to one event, shows
$\sigma=\omega=0$, $\theta=-3/\rho$, and exhibits equality in the
Raychaudhuri focusing bound at the focal event even though the curvature
vanishes and every geodesic extends.

**Countermodels.** [[cex-gr-flat-cylinder-has-a-closed-timelike-curve]]
exhibits the closed future timelike curve of the flat cylinder, refuting
chronology for flat time-oriented models.
[[cex-gr-spacelike-hypersurface-need-not-be-cauchy]] shows that a bounded
spacelike fragment of an inertial slice of globally hyperbolic Minkowski
space is missed by an inextendible causal curve, so spacelike is not Cauchy.
[[cex-gr-achronal-set-need-not-be-acausal]] separates the two set conditions
with two null-related events: achronal but not acausal.

The general causal-curve conclusions use the Countable Choice and Dependent
Choice premises recorded in their suppliers and consuming items. All six
items are mathematics: conditional computations and
counterexamples inside the explicitly displayed models. None reports a
measurement, and none asserts anything about nature.
