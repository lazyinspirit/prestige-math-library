These examples work the vocabulary of [[gr-experimental-interface]] on the
framework's own cosmological redshift prediction. The first constructs an
explicit family of future null geodesics in an exponentially expanding flat
FLRW datum, turns the emitted-to-received scale-factor ratio into an
operational model whose redshift prediction is $z=a_R/a_E-1$, and solves a declared
illustrative one-point record for its exact consistency interval, including
the endpoint values and the positive length of the window. The second refutes,
under the same explicitly declared comparison rule, the uniqueness claim that
a bounded one-point record determines the parameter of the operational model:
two exact rational parameter witnesses — in fact a whole positive-length
interval of them — are consistent with the illustrative record.

Neither item asserts a measurement, an apparatus, a calibration, a sampling
model or any empirical conclusion about the model or about a postulate. Every
number is a declared illustrative value; the model itself is a hypothetical
configuration satisfying the framework's postulates, and no field-law or
source-observation claim is made.
