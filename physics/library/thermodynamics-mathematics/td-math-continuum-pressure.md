This page defines the stable power-tempered pair-potential class and proves a
finite free-cube grand-pressure limit for it. Stability bounds each finite
partition sum; an explicit particle cutoff controls its grand tail; separated
cubes and a summable lattice interaction bound then give the lower limit
estimate. The result concerns free-boundary cubes and the displayed power-law
tail. It does not assert canonical fixed-density convergence or general
boundary independence.
