This page develops positional continuum statistical mechanics for stable regular pair interactions. It states the configuration, boundary, Poisson-reference, canonical and grand-canonical conventions explicitly, then proves free-cube pressure and interior exact-particle limits before deriving their thermodynamic duality. A superstable branch supplies the stationary entropy-energy variational principle and spatially averaged local-observable equivalence under a singleton minimizer.

The page keeps the hypotheses separated. Canonical limits are asserted at interior attainable densities; close-packing and other endpoints are not supplied by an interior limit. Arbitrary van Hove shapes use the superstable cell-occupation bound. Uniform exterior-pressure comparison uses a genuine positive divergent core and the stated uniformly tempered exterior class. The controlled energy shell is a positional shrinking energy-density window selected by a diagonal argument, not an exact energy surface or a phase-space shell. Normalized Poisson pressure differs from the physical $1/N!$-normalized grand rate by the explicit unit-volume term.

The general decreasing-integrable-tail results assume a monotone integrable envelope. The page does not infer boundary universality from stability alone, does not infer the genuine core from superstability, and does not identify variational minimizers with DLR laws. All thermodynamic statements are conditional mathematical deductions in fixed numerical units; no experimental observation is claimed. The current prerequisite [[td-math-convex-contact-and-mixing]] supplies the convex contact and calibration context. The local [[lem-tdmath-periodic-law-local-tame-transfer]] makes the periodic-preparation interpretation explicit and transfers entropy-controlled empirical-field limits to periodically continued local-tame laws.
