This page fixes a simple locally finite configuration model on $\mathbb R^d$ and a unit-intensity Poisson reference. It proves the finite-region relative-entropy rules, count and translation properties, existence and cube-supremum formula for specific relative entropy, compactness of finite-entropy levels for local observables with at most linear count growth, and an $L^1$ spatial information-density limit under full-translation ergodicity and finite specific entropy.

The configuration model and entropy are mathematical definitions; they do not identify a physical preparation or measured entropy. The spatial limit is a conditional mathematical deduction, not an empirical law. All local claims include their finite-intensity, standard-Borel, absolute-continuity, and choice hypotheses. The page stays within the reserved inventory of 10 A items (cap 32); the examples companion has 3 B items (cap 8).

Georgii–Zessin's continuum point-field treatment is used for the local topology, Poisson reference, relative entropy and compactness context. Nguyen–Zessin is used for the spatial conditional-density and information argument. Georgii's book chapter is a finite-state lattice comparison only; it is not cited as proving the continuum configuration claims, which are proved locally here.
