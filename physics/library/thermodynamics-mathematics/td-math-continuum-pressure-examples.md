These examples verify that a singular nonnegative repulsion and a hard-core
model with a negative algebraic tail both satisfy the stable power-tempered
hypotheses. The second proof gives a uniform lattice-shell bound on the total
attraction. The
companion A page's free-cube theorem does not extend by itself to arbitrary
boundaries or to canonical fixed-density limits.
