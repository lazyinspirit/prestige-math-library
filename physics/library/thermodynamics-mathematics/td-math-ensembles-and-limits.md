This page develops conditional mathematical identities for countable and measurable canonical laws, positive trace-class Gibbs operators, finite probability models, and finite-range lattice limits. The countable and measurable canonical differentiation results require their stated local summable or integrable moment envelopes; a lower energy bound alone does not ensure a finite countable partition sum. The finite-model partition results require no such envelopes. The operator result assumes the Axiom of Choice and a positive trace-class Gibbs operator, with a local summable moment envelope for its parameter-derivative identities.

The finite-range pressure theorem is restricted to finite alphabets, bounded translation-invariant interactions of finite range, and cubic boxes with free boundary. Boundary changes are covered only under the stated surface-order energy bound. Concentration and the limiting mean are asserted only at differentiability points. The transfer-matrix result is specific to the stated periodic two-state chain. No unrestricted ensemble-equivalence or general thermodynamic-limit claim is made.

The entropy, mutual-information, and Bose/Fermi results concern explicitly specified finite or countable probability laws; the response-covariance identity concerns the stated measurable density on a measure space. None asserts that a physical preparation or measured fluctuation has been established.
