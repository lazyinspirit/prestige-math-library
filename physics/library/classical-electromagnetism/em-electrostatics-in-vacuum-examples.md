This companion page collects the two explicit electrostatic computations of the
pair. [[ex-em-infinite-line-normalization]] treats the idealised infinite
straight line charge: it verifies the radial field, the logarithmic potential and
the classical zero-source equations off the axis, computes the lateral cylinder
flux $\lambda L/\varepsilon_0$, proves the distributional identity
$\operatorname{div}E=\rho_\lambda/\varepsilon_0$ on $\mathbb R^3$ by
superposition of the point-charge identities along the axis, records the failure
of the infinity normalization and of the star-shaped hypothesis, and shows that
the energy per unit length diverges logarithmically in both cutoffs. The line
source is an explicitly specified distribution outside the framework's declared
source types, so the example is a mathematical idealisation with no physical
realisation claim. [[ex-em-uniform-ball-coulomb]] treats the uniformly charged
ball: it verifies the piecewise candidate field and continuous potential, the
pointwise equations off the sphere, the distributional equations with zero
surface charge, the exterior Coulomb identification of total charge $Q$, and the
exact finite energy $3Q^2/(20\pi\varepsilon_0a)$ equal to the assembly integral,
without any uniqueness or empirical claim.
