This page closes the framework's empirical dimension with a declared record
vocabulary and three primary-report accounts, and then states exactly what such
evidence can and cannot do. The definition fixes the finite vocabulary of
primary sources with declared reading limits, comparison quantities and
measurands, apparatus-and-preparation descriptions, recorded data and reported
observations, calibration models with declared error bounds, uncertainty
records with source labels, analysis-assumption lists and qualified
interpretations, and it asserts no measurement. The three experiments record
Faraday's 1831 induction transients, Hertz's 1888 finite-velocity spark and
wire-node indicators, and Cavendish's enclosed-globe inverse-square nulls with
its sensitivity control, each kept qualitative with its missing calibration,
datasets, sampling laws and corrupted numerics retained as missing. On that
basis the comparison theorem isolates two exact framework reference
predictions — a zero enclosed electric flux in the smooth charge-free branch
and the exact averaged plane-wave intensity — and proves the deterministic
confinement $|\theta-\theta_0|\le(s+\delta)/m$ under a declared
apparatus-and-calibration dictionary and declared bounds, with no sampling
law, coverage, $p$-value or numerical application asserted. The closing remark
records the framework's material, point-source, mechanical and quantum
validity limits. Every item is conditional on its stated hypotheses and on the
qualifications carried by the reports it cites.
