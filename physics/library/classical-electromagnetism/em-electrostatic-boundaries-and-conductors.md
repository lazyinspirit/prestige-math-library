This page develops the ideal electrostatic conductor model and the boundary
value problems it induces in one named inertial laboratory frame.
[[post-em-electrostatic-conductor-model]] adopts the equilibrium idealisation:
finitely many compact smooth field-free conductor bodies strictly inside an
ambient region, a connected vacuum region, a continuous stationary surface
charge, the distributional vacuum Maxwell equations, and a continuous
equilibrium potential that is constant on each conductor body, with the two
data types of prescribed component potentials or prescribed component charges.
Three local mathematical prerequisites supply the geometry and the uniqueness
tool: [[lem-em-boundary-of-a-bounded-smooth-domain-is-a-compact-embedded-hypersurface]]
converts the one-sided graph definition of a bounded smooth domain into a
compact embedded hypersurface with finitely many boundary components;
[[lem-em-vacuum-region-of-conductors-is-a-bounded-smooth-domain]] shows that the
complement of the conductor bodies is again a bounded smooth domain with the
expected outer and conductor boundary pieces and normal signs; and
[[lem-em-half-space-harmonic-uniqueness-with-decay]] gives the decay-class
uniqueness used for the unbounded image problem.
[[pthm-em-conductor-surface-charge]] proves the pointwise surface-charge and
normal-field relations, the constancy of the potential trace on each conductor
component, and the total-charge flux identities.
[[pthm-em-electrostatic-bvp-uniqueness]] and
[[pthm-em-electrostatic-bvp-existence]] establish the classical, weak and
transmission uniqueness, flux compatibility and smooth solvability statements on
the bounded vacuum region. [[pthm-em-image-method-certificate]] verifies the
classic grounded-plane image solution as an explicitly declared idealisation of
the half-space, and [[pthm-em-capacitance-energy]] derives the linear
charge-potential map, the symmetric positive definite capacitance matrix and
the stored-energy identities for the specified conductor geometry.

The companion page [[em-electrostatic-boundaries-and-conductors-examples]]
carries the worked grounded-plane force, the ideal parallel-plate capacitor and
the incompatible-Neumann-data impossibility example.
