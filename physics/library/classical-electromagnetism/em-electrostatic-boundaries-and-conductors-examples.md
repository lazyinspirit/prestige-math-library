This companion page collects three worked computations of the conductor pair.
[[ex-em-grounded-plane-image-force]] computes the external mirror field at a
charge held above an ideal grounded plane and the resulting attractive force
$q^2/(16\pi\varepsilon_0a^2)$ under the adopted external coupling, with the
self-field and all dynamics explicitly excluded.
[[ex-em-parallel-plate-capacitor]] declares and verifies the ideal
two-infinite-plane model directly: the piecewise field and potential, the
distributional divergence and vanishing curl with the two plane densities, the
potential difference $V=\sigma d/\varepsilon_0$, and the ideal-model relations
$Q=(\varepsilon_0A/d)V$ and $U=\frac12QV=Q^2/(2C)=\frac12CV^2$ for a chosen area
$A$, while recording that infinite planes lie outside the bounded-conductor and
declared-sheet hypotheses and that fringing is not computed.
[[ex-em-neumann-data-failure]] shows by the necessary flux compatibility that
the unit normal-derivative datum on the unit ball with zero volume source
admits neither a classical nor a weak solution, the obstruction being the
strictly positive total boundary flux $4\pi$; the item reports no measurement
or material claim.
