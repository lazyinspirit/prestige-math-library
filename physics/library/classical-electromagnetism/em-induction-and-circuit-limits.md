This page develops induction and the lumped circuit approximation from the
framework's adopted equations. Two local mathematical lemmas provide the
Newtonian linkage pairing and the curl-field energy identity for smooth compact
conserved currents, and a moving-circuit definition fixes the geometry of a
$C^2$ embedded material sheet with a single-valued $C^1$ velocity, its magnetic
flux and its electromotive force line integral. The moving-loop theorem then
derives transport by differentiating the parametrized flux and sums the
elementary-piece Faraday identities of the integral Maxwell theorem to prove
$\mathrm{emf}=-\dot\Phi_B$ under explicit no-singularity-crossing hypotheses,
and the lumped circuit model is adopted as a separate postulate with declared
branch relations, constant element matrices and a qualitative quasistatic
regime. Conditional on that model, the page proves the reciprocal linkage
coefficients and frozen magnetic energy of smooth compact conserved profiles,
the fixed-geometry inductive energy bookkeeping and its identification with
the frozen field energy, and the scalar RL and LC dynamics with their
bookkeeping-energy statements. The geometric capacitor identification invoked
for the LC loop is supplied by the electrostatic-conductors page and remains an
explicit conditional clause. Ohmic power is retained in the adopted RL model; no additional material-loss
mechanism, radiation, distributed-element, thin-wire or empirical claim is made.
