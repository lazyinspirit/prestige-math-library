The companion collects the three leaf items of the pair in their dependency
order. The sliding-bar thought experiment fixes an ideal sliding-contact model
with separate segment velocities: the segmentwise electromotive force
$\mathrm{emf}=-B_0l x'(t)$ and the rectangular flux derivative are computed
directly, a finite-volume bar force integral is evaluated from the adopted
local matter coupling, and the signed instantaneous mechanical/electrical power
balance is exhibited; self-fields, contacts, friction and bar dynamics are
excluded. The two-circuit example proves the reciprocal inductive energy and
power identity, the positive-semidefinite bound $|M|\le\sqrt{L_1L_2}$ for a
smooth compact conserved-profile realization together with the null direction
at perfect linkage, the explicitly adopted common-flux ideal-transformer
relations and their reversed-winding sign, and it proves by a
derivative-vector argument that the perfect common-flux voltage ratio is not a
universal identity under strict linkage. The RL example verifies the
constant-voltage exponential transient, its time constant $L/R$, the unique
stationary solution and the qualified limit statement, with uniqueness
inherited from the RL theorem. Each computation is exact and conditional on
its declared idealisation, and no measurement is involved.
