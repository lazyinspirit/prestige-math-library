This page develops magnetostatics in vacuum from the framework's adopted
equations: the steady-current regime, the compact smooth conserved-current
class, and three explicitly declared ideal source models (the closed thin loop,
the infinite straight wire and the infinite cylindrical sheet). Two local
mathematical lemmas compute the distributional curl of the azimuthal line field
and of the piecewise constant cylinder field, and a third differentiates the
Newtonian kernel; these are the exact distributional ingredients of the source
equations for the ideal models. The Coulomb-gauge Newtonian vector potential is
constructed for compact smooth conserved currents, the Biot-Savart
representation and the controlled far-field dipole estimate with its explicit
uniform remainder follow, and the force and torque on a declared thin-wire loop
in a prescribed external field are computed under an explicitly adopted
neutral-wire coupling. The ideal noncompact models are declared idealisations
with stated symmetry and decay data, adopted physically by a separate
postulate; no measurement, apparatus or empirical claim is involved on this
page, and no finite-energy, self-energy, uniqueness or apparatus-accuracy claim
is made for them.
