This page builds the medium-response layer of the classical electromagnetic
framework from an explicitly declared coarse-graining convention: a fixed smooth
unit-mass averaging kernel and averaging length, the exact interior footprint
domain, the every-time versus almost-every-time input qualification, and the
compatible-extension and boundary-layer conventions, together with the explicit
statements that averaging supplies neither a constitutive law nor a field
equation. On that basis the polarization $P$, magnetization $M$, bound sources
and the fields $D$ and $H$ are defined, and the macroscopic Maxwell equations
$\operatorname{div}D=\rho_f$, $\operatorname{div}B=0$,
$\operatorname{curl}E=-\partial_tB$, $\operatorname{curl}H=J_f+\partial_tD$ are
derived as an exact rewriting of the adopted smooth vacuum equations. The
declared material-closure branches add the constant symmetric linear Ohmic
response and a causal memory response, with their own hypotheses, and the
stationary interface jumps, the causal dispersion relations with the
monochromatic passivity criterion, and the restricted nondispersive energy
balance with Ohmic dissipation are established conditionally on those
assumptions. Each item states its own regularity, units, frame conventions and
exclusions; no microscopic mechanism, material data or empirical claim is
asserted.
