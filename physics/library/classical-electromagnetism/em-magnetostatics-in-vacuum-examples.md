The examples verify the declared ideal magnetostatic models explicitly. The
infinite straight wire and the infinite cylindrical current sheet are exhibited
with their exact fields, circulations, jumps and distributional source
equations, and checked against the admissibility predicate of the
steady-current definition; the axis field of the declared ideal circular loop
is evaluated from the Biot-Savart line integral; and the force per unit length
between two declared ideal parallel wires is computed from the external field
of one wire under the adopted thin-wire coupling. Each computation is exact and
conditional on its declared idealisation, with self-fields, support forces,
dynamics and any measurement excluded.
