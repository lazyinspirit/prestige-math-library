The examples compute the integral and counit for a finite group algebra, a Taft algebra, and the four-dimensional Sweedler algebra. They recover the characteristic condition in classical Maschke's theorem, show a concrete failure of semisimplicity when the integral counit vanishes, and exhibit the Frobenius pairing by a finite matrix calculation.
