## Scope

This page defines a nonlinear electromagnetic model with a $C^2$ two-invariant Lagrangian on a fixed Lorentzian geometry and connection sector, then derives its source-free field equation from compact variations. It also specifies an abstract contract for smooth stress and current source maps and proves the divergence identities forced by the coupled Einstein–Maxwell equations. These are conditional mathematical results; the page supplies no quantum-state construction, renormalized-source existence, QED coefficient, evolution theorem, or empirical conclusion.
