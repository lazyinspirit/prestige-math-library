## Scope

This mathematical page supplies the smooth compact torus geometry needed by
its companion example, fixes the Lorentzian Hodge-star sign for a smooth
sourced two-form equation, derives current conservation conditionally, and
records finite-chain charge and flux identities. It also derives the free
Maxwell action's field and metric variations under compact-support hypotheses
and computes the resulting tensor in an observer tetrad.

All results are deductions about smooth geometric fields with the stated
equations and variation assumptions. They do not adopt a physical field law,
assert that a field describes nature, or use experimental data. The fixed-bundle
action variation is conditional on the precise connection-gluing interface
reserved on [[emg-mg02]]; it does not assume a global potential or include
source/matter stress.
