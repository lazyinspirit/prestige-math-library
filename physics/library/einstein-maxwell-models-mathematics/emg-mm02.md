## Scope

This page fixes a global length-coordinate chart for the metric $g_H=-H^{-2}(dx^0)^2+H^2\delta_{ij}dx^i dx^j$, its coordinate orientation, and the time direction selected by $H\partial_0$. It proves the Lorentzian frame and derives the Ricci, scalar, and Einstein components for every positive $C^3$ function $H$, without imposing harmonicity. For smooth $H>0$ with $\Delta H\le0$, it then verifies an exact formal Einstein–Maxwell–charged-dust family and derives the regular spherical profile, proper mass and charge, and dilute bounds. These are mathematical deductions under the stated formal equations, signature, charge sign, and SI bookkeeping; they do not assert physical realization, stability, or general initial-value existence.
