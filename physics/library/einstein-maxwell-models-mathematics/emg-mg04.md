## Scope

This page develops the compact-variation Einstein equation, the gauge and
diffeomorphism Noether identity for matter, and the classical charged-scalar
current and stress tensor. It then derives the electromagnetic stress exchange
identity under the sourced Maxwell equations. Each result is conditional on its
stated smoothness, bundle, field-equation and compact-support hypotheses.

The mathematical items do not adopt Einstein, Maxwell or matter equations as
physical laws, claim existence of a coupled solution, or use experimental data.
The current M02 and M03 supplier carriers are drafts, and their inspected
interfaces are used conditionally. The seven typed M01 replacement carriers
referenced by this batch are authored drafts on [[emg-mg02]]; their review and
independent acceptance remain pending. The historical research-only `GR-G3`
token is reconciled at its actual contracted-Bianchi use to the published
[[thm-gr-second-bianchi-and-divergence-free-einstein]]; no broader `GR-G3`
result is asserted.
