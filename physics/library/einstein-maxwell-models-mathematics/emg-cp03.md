This page defines the strict interior charged barotropic model and develops its current balance, positive symmetric fluid block, local coupled development, and compact-slice charge compatibility. Its local development uses the explicit classical Maxwell-stress identity on CP02 at C1 metric/field regularity and the positive field-strength principal block. The periodic metric jet is initialized by direct Christoffel contraction with p00=-2 tr_h K, giving H0=0 in the stated sign convention. The finite-Sobolev and smooth geometric branches retain their exact strict admissibility conditions; draft authoring status is separate from the mathematical proofs. No shocks, vacuum boundaries, finite-conductivity law, global existence, or empirical validation are asserted.
