## Scope

This page develops exact smooth mathematical data for three source-free Einstein-Maxwell families in the geometric convention fixed by emg-ms01: harmonic conformastatic data, constant-radius Bertotti-Robinson products, and null plane-wave data. The arbitrary-positive-function conformastatic Ricci table from emg-mm02 is the input to the harmonic verification and to the nonharmonic counterexample on the examples page. For each family, definitions specify the manifold or coordinate domain, metric, two-form, regularity, orientation, time direction, and units before the component calculation.

The harmonic family is verified by direct Maxwell and Einstein tensor calculations. The finite multicenter result gives fluxes only through regular finite spheres in the punctured exterior. The single-center coordinate map identifies the open MP exterior with the extremal RN exterior, and a separate coordinate scaling proves a local smooth RN-to-Bertotti-Robinson limit on the positive-radius patch. The product and null-wave equations are checked from their full factor or coordinate components.

## Conventions and limits

All entries are mathematical deductions from their displayed hypotheses. They adopt no physical postulate and report no measurement. The curvature sign, Lorentzian signature, Hodge orientation, geometric field normalization, and zero cosmological constant are those of emg-ms01; the conformastatic metric and its arbitrary-positive-H Ricci identities are those of emg-mm02. The solutions do not assert global horizon extensions, completeness, asymptotic flatness for the null-wave family, finite total wave energy, stability, uniqueness, or physical realization. A harmonic pole is excluded from the manifold; its regular-sphere flux is not a proof of regular matter at that point.
