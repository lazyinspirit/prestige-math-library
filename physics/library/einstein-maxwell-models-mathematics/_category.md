These mathematical pages provide constructions used in Einstein–Maxwell models: oriented Lorentzian fields, observers and tetrads; Hodge duality, local potentials and current identities; unit conversions; matter tensors; coupled evolution systems; conformal constraints; exact electrovacuum and charged-wave calculations; and selected numerical or effective-model results. Further pages treat horizon behavior, scattering and stationary uniqueness under stated restrictions. Conformal initial-data and local constraint results expose their analytic hypotheses; exact electrovac examples retain their chart, extension and parameter conditions. Statements specify their manifold, smoothness, coordinate and boundary assumptions. They do not adopt a physical field law or claim that a model describes nature. Examples include conformastatic geometries and Reissner–Nordström extensions.

The constructions build on general-relativistic geometry and differential geometry, with analysis, PDE and topology results where needed. Einstein–Maxwell models use them for field and solution calculations; selected results also support Quantum Field Theory mathematics and Thermodynamics and General Relativity mathematics. Physical meanings come from those consuming model pages.
