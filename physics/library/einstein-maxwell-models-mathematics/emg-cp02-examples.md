This companion checks a nontrivial constrained torus datum and records the exact scope of maximality. [[ex-emg-nonzero-parallel-field-data]] takes flat $\mathbb T^3$, $\Lambda=0$, nonzero parallel constant electric and magnetic data, and $K=Hh$ with $6H^2=\kappa\mu_0^{-1}(|e|^2+|b|^2)$. It verifies every Einstein and Maxwell constraint and then invokes the conditional local development theorem. For each nonzero component $b_i$, the oriented coordinate two-torus orthogonal to that axis has magnetic period $(2\pi L_0)^2b_i$, where $L_0>0$ is the fixed length scale of the example; hence a nonzero component rules out a global potential on a neighborhood of the full initial torus. The example makes no later-time isotropy claim.

[[rem-emg-maximal-is-not-complete]] states the logical boundary of the smooth maximal globally hyperbolic development result: its universal embedding property, using the affine geodesic equation and maximal-interval convention, does not assert geodesic completeness. No concrete incomplete Einstein–Maxwell maximal development is claimed here. Neither item reports an observation or an experiment.
