This companion records exact parameter substitutions and counterexamples for
the geometric electrovac page. The zero-rotation limits identify coordinate
forms only; equal metric coefficients do not determine the sign of electric
flux, and zero scalar curvature does not force zero Ricci curvature. Every
entry is a mathematical model deduction, not a reported observation.
