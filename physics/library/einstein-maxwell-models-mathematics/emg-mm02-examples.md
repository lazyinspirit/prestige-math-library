## Scope

These examples specialize the exact smooth family to a regular sphere and to a quantified dilute parameter range. The first checks the center values, proper mass and charge, and everywhere-positive density. The second records uniform metric and density comparison bounds and shows that, away from the origin, the static dust's nonzero electromagnetic force per unit mass equals its nonzero acceleration; at the origin both vanish and the central static worldline is geodesic. Both are exact mathematical calculations, not measurements or stability claims.
