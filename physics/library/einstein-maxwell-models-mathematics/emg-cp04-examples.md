The scalar-zero branch with $V(0)=0$ has zero current and stress and reduces exactly to the source-free Einstein–Maxwell equations when the electromagnetic two-form has a global potential. The scope remark records why this example does not extend to nontrivial bundles, and why $\lambda=0$ alone does not force the scalar stress to vanish.
