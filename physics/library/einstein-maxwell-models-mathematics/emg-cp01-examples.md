These examples check three separate interfaces of the tensor formulation. Zero field on Minkowski spacetime is an exact coupled solution; a nonzero Maxwell test field on a fixed flat metric generally fails the Einstein constraint; and a closed magnetic field on a torus can have a nonzero period, allowing local potentials but obstructing a single-valued global potential. The magnetic-period example is explicitly a Maxwell-sector example on a prescribed background, not a coupled flat solution.
