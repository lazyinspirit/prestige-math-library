## Scope

These examples calculate an advected but spatially varying charged-dust ratio without Maxwell closure, local Ohmic heating in an imposed field, anisotropic magnetic stress and a future-null observer energy-current vector, and a static electrostatic test-particle first integral. They are exact mathematical calculations under their displayed fields, domains, and supplier hypotheses. They report no measurements and do not assert a coupled Einstein–Maxwell solution, matter evolution, or a singular point-particle model.
