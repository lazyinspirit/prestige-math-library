## Scope

The counterexample uses a conformally rescaled Minkowski metric with zero electromagnetic field to separate source-free Maxwell covariance from Einstein-equation covariance. The Coulomb calculation checks the SI charge coefficient against the geometric length parameter in the exact field normalization.

Both are conditional mathematical calculations. The source point is excluded from the Coulomb field domain; no singular source model, observation, or measured charge is asserted.
