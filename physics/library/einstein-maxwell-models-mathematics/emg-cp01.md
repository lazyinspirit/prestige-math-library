This page defines the smooth source-free Einstein-Maxwell tensor system and its periodic high-Sobolev state interface, then records exact mathematical consequences used in Cauchy analysis. The Maxwell stress tensor is symmetric and trace-free, its divergence has the source term fixed by the stated sign convention, and the Lorenz-gauge potential wave operator contains a Ricci coupling. Closed fields have local potentials; no global potential is assumed. The Gauss-Codazzi and Maxwell constraints are necessary conditions on induced data, and the fixed smooth background construction makes the harmonic initial gauge compatible with finite Sobolev regularity. The symmetric-hyperbolic theorem is a general periodic PDE supplier only. This page does not claim that the coupled Einstein-Maxwell equations have been reduced to such a system or prove a local coupled development.

The causal vocabulary fixes global causal and Cauchy conventions, signed Killing-horizon normalization, section area, selected-model escape and approached metric extensions.
