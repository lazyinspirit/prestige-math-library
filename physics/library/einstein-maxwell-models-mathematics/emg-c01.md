## Scope

This page gives an explicit SI-to-geometric parameter dictionary for the smooth oriented Lorentzian four-manifold, metric, two-form and coordinate conventions on [[emg-mg01]]. It distinguishes positive input parameters from derived conversion factors and records the units of the electromagnetic two-form and geometric mass, charge, spin and magnetic-flux parameters.

The tensor normalization is an exact algebraic equivalence for the same metric and field. The Maxwell equations have a four-dimensional conformal covariance with an explicit current scaling, while the Einstein tensor transforms by derivative terms and is not conformally invariant. A separate constant rescaling preserves the displayed coupled electrovac equations when all listed fields and the cosmological parameter are transformed together. The fixed-metric small-field proposition gives only an exact quadratic Einstein residual; it is not a nearby-solution theorem or a point-source limit.

These are mathematical identities conditional on their stated smoothness, orientation, signature and parameter hypotheses. They do not adopt a physical field equation, establish empirical values, or imply that conformally related geometries solve the same Einstein equation.
