## Scope

This companion gives an explicit smooth Maxwell field and current on a flat
product with compact spatial three-torus. The equations hold pointwise, while
the total charge on the compact boundaryless spatial cycle vanishes. The
construction is a mathematical model example, not a reported experiment or a
claim about a physical system.
