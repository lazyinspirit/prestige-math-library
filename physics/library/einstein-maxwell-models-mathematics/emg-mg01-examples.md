## Scope

This companion computes the Lorentzian Hodge star on one constant two-form in oriented Minkowski four-space. It checks the two-form sign $\star_g^2=-\operatorname{id}$ and the electric/magnetic scalar contractions using the conventions proved on [[emg-mg01]]. The example is a mathematical calculation; it records no observation and assumes no Maxwell model.
