## Scope

This companion checks the charged-scalar equation and sourced Maxwell sign on
an exact flat-background plane wave, and computes a nonzero Einstein–Hilbert
normal-derivative flux when only the boundary value of the metric variation is
fixed. These are mathematical examples with the stated smooth fields and
cutoffs, not reports of experiments or claims about observed systems.
