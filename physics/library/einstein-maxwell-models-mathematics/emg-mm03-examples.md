## Scope

The first example constructs an exact transverse plane Maxwell wave by taking the real part of a finite WKB potential in Minkowski space. It checks the field components and source-free equations directly and uses the A-page residual result to keep the test-field claim separate from the coupled Einstein equation.

The second example solves the constant-magnetic-field relativistic orbit exactly and compares it with the dimensionless Newton–Lorentz orbit on a fixed finite scaled-time interval. Its error bound is conditional on the stated slow initial speed and remains a test-motion calculation; it does not assert that a uniform magnetic field with an unchanged Minkowski metric is a coupled Einstein–Maxwell solution.
