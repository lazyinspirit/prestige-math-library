## Scope

The counterexample on this page gives a family of smooth exact two-forms on Minkowski space whose two scalar invariant corrections tend uniformly to zero while a first coordinate derivative grows without bound. It separates small invariant corrections from a bound on derivatives in an effective model. The fields are mathematical configurations; no field-equation solution, QED coefficient, or empirical observation is asserted.
