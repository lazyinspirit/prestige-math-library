This page fixes $e(x)=\exp(2\pi i x)$ and
$\widehat f(\xi)=\int_{\mathbb R}f(x)e(-x\xi)\,dx$. It develops primitive
characters, their Gauss sums, and the parity-sensitive theta argument leading
to the completed Dirichlet $L$-function and its functional equation. The
primitive principal character modulo $1$ remains the zeta exception.
