For a finite extension $K/\mathbb Q$, this page separates its maximal order
$\mathcal O_K$ from arbitrary full-rank orders. Discriminants are first
attached to ordered bases; the change-of-basis formula then makes the order
and field invariants well defined. The embedding determinant always uses all
$[K:\mathbb Q]$ embeddings.
