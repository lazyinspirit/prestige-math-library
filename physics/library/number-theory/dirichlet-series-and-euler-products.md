This page fixes Dirichlet-series notation, proves the half-plane and abscissa
geometry, and then turns multiplicativity into Euler products only in regions
where absolute convergence genuinely licenses the regrouping.

The closing identities stay inside the initial half-plane of the zeta series.
They use already published arithmetic-function identities together with the
Euler-product machinery from this page, without importing later continuation or
line-one analysis.
