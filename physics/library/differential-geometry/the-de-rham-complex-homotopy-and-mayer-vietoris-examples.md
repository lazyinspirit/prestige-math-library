These examples calculate de Rham groups and products for points, finite discrete spaces, Euclidean spaces, circles, spheres, and punctured spaces. Explicit angular and volume forms represent generators, with nonzero periods proving nonexactness. The annulus retraction illustrates smooth homotopy invariance, and a circle connecting form displays the second-minus-first Mayer–Vietoris sign. Counterexamples show why closedness and the contravariant order of pullback matter.
