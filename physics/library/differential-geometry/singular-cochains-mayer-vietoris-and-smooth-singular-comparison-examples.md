The calculations compare subdivision depths within one finite chain, evaluate a canonical overlap cochain extension, and construct smooth affine simplices in a coordinate ball with an actual common extension neighbourhood. The Takagi path supplies a continuous simplex with no finite derivative anywhere, including its endpoints.

Endpoint-relative path smoothing is illustrated by an explicit homotopy, and the point calculation retains all unnormalized degenerate simplices and computes the alternating differentials. The final remark distinguishes the kernel/image definition of real singular cohomology from its natural evaluation isomorphism under AC.
