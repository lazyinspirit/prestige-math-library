The counterexample $\sum_{n\ge0}n!g^n$ is a valid formal coefficient sequence but diverges at every nonzero complex $g$, so its numerical radius of convergence is zero. This shows why formal coefficient manipulations alone do not imply an analytic function or a truncation error bound. It makes no claim about convergence or summability of any particular QFT expansion.
