The example chooses nonnegative compactly supported tests in separated
spacetime regions for the massless scalar representation. Their supports are
spacelike separated, so the fields and local Weyl algebras commute, while the
vacuum two-point pairing is strictly positive. The calculation is a
mathematical correlation in the model and makes no statement about
measurement, signaling, or empirical data.
