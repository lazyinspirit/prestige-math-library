For finite $N$, this page defines a Borel Gaussian reference law on $\mathbb R^N$ with finite covariance and its normalized tilt by a nonnegative homogeneous quartic polynomial. It proves all polynomial moments are finite; the partition function and polynomial insertions have derivatives of every finite order for $\lambda>0$, extending continuously by right derivatives at $\lambda=0$. It also establishes the finite-dimensional Wick pairing formula and gives finite-order asymptotic expansions with explicit order-dependent remainders. Wick-normal powers are defined by a covariance generating function. These are exact finite-dimensional mathematical constructions under the stated normal-law and Choice hypotheses; no continuum Euclidean field measure, reflection-positive reconstruction, or cutoff removal is asserted.
