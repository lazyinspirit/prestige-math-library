These examples give an explicit nonzero state in a fixed-background Gauss sector, a one-fermion counterexample to arbitrary total charge on a closed compact graph, and a charged CAR operator whose expectation vanishes in a physical state. Each verification is conditional on the exact finite CAR supplier stated on the A page; none asserts a continuum limit or an empirical claim.
