The worked scalar mode has time factor $e^{-i\omega|t|}/(2\omega)$. Its first derivative jumps by $-i$ at the origin, so the fixed $(-,+,+,+)$ wave operator produces the $i\delta$ Green source after spatial Fourier inversion. The massless shell apex is omitted as a measure-zero, locally integrable point; the example makes no pole-prescription or interacting-theory claim.
