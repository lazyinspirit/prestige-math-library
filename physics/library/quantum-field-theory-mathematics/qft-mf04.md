This page constructs the free scalar time-ordered two-point function directly as a tempered partial spatial Fourier distribution in the fixed $(-,+,+,+)$ convention. It proves the derivative-jump normalization $(\Box-\mu^2)H_\mu=i\delta_0$ without inserting an undefined product of a step function and a singular distribution. It also derives the classical scalar and commuting-spinor free equations from their normalized actions and records the exact operator domains and point-smearing obstructions for the supplied free Fock models. QFT-MF01, QFT-MF02, and QFT-MF03 carriers are used at their actual current draft statements and proof status; none of these mathematical deductions asserts a physical measurement or interacting-field construction.
