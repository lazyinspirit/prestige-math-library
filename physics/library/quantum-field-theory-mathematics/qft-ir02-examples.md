The one-coordinate quartic probability measure exists for every nonnegative coupling even though its finite-order asymptotic perturbation series has zero radius of convergence. The quadratic Wick pairing is computed exactly. The Wick-subtracted quartic satisfies an explicit quadratic coercivity bound; its form-domain conclusion is stated conditionally on the current QFT-IR01 coercive-form draft, whose interface is checked here and whose independent review remains pending.
