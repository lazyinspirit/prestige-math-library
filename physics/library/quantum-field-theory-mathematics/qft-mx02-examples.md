These worked calculations specialize the A-page formal coefficients to the massless annihilation limit, massless scalar phase space, and the spin term in a uniform magnetic field. They verify angular and unit factors without introducing observations, exact interacting evolution, lifetime laws, higher-order QED corrections, or a full magnetic spectrum. The annihilation example remains conditional on its missing QFT-MG03 formal Ward input.
