The Ising example checks the level-two null vector in the simple highest-weight quotient, reduces the four-spin BPZ system to its cross-ratio ODE, verifies both chiral solutions, and computes their monodromy. The diagonal pairing is single-valued, while each chiral block remains multivalued; this calculation does not construct a full local CFT.
