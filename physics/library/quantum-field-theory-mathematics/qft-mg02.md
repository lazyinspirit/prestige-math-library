This page defines a finite oriented-graph regulator with one compact $U(1)$ rotor on each link and finite CAR matter at each vertex. It proves the exact Gauss projection and nonempty constrained sector, self-adjointness of the rotor Hamiltonian on its maximal electric-energy domain, and Stone evolution preserving that sector. It then derives the finite-graph regional charge/flux identity, operator selection rules, the time-ordered contact relation, and a conditional continuum charged-localization obstruction. All results are mathematical statements about the declared finite regulator or, for the final clause, conditional deductions from its explicit continuum hypotheses. The CAR construction remains conditional on the current batch-37 draft Fock/CAR carriers: their actual interfaces match, but no Step-1 readiness records are current for those supplier items.
