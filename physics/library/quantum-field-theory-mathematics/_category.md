This collection supplies mathematical constructions for quantum field models: algebraic states, distributions and Fock spaces, free fields, regulated interactions, microlocal products, causal renormalization, local algebras, scattering, and effective expansions. Other branches develop BRST complexes, anomaly obstructions, KZ connections and chiral interfaces, topological sewing, and Hall-algebra wall-crossing data. Finite constructions, formal expansions, and conditional interfaces retain their stated domains and limits. The expansion adds non-Abelian gauge geometry and BRST/BV, super-Poincaré representations, instanton moduli, equivariant localization and index theory, finite symmetry gauging, defect fusion, and EFT operator and positivity methods. The added material distinguishes finite localization theorems from continuum field-theory assumptions.

Functional Analysis, Measure Theory, Complex Analysis, and Partial Differential Equations provide analytic foundations; Lie Theory, Differential Geometry, Homological Algebra, and Category Theory supply algebraic and geometric tools. Quantum Field Theory and Thermodynamics and General Relativity already use selected constructions. The expanded material also supplies mathematical prerequisites for Advanced QFT and Supersymmetry, with physical models and interpretations developed in that separate collection.
