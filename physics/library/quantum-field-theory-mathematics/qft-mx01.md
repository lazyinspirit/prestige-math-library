This page fixes the sign, metric, SI, natural-unit, gauge, and inner-product conventions used by the constructed and formal QFT examples. The metric for spacetime geometry is $\eta=(-,+,+,+)$; Clifford and momentum contractions use the separately declared $h=-\eta$. Energy vectors use joules and $s$ uses joules squared, with every natural-coordinate conversion stated explicitly. The coupling and gauge symbols have defined SI units, and the first-linear mathematical Hilbert pairing is distinguished from the usual second-linear coordinate contraction.

The mathematical results remain conditional finite or one-mode calculations. They prove a norm-convergent Dyson expansion and its finite-time remainder for bounded matrices, exact unitarity and coefficient identities in that model, an explicit finite-time kernel limit, and the oscillator's distributional boundary prescription. The approximate-identity limit concerns a specified integral coefficient, not an exact long-time probability or exponential law. The oscillator calculation has one mode and a fixed core; it does not establish spatial causality or interacting-field existence.

Tong's lecture notes and Srednicki's author-hosted draft provide independent QFT treatments; Fitzpatrick supplies a separate quantum-mechanics treatment of finite-time perturbation. BIPM supplies the SI reference. The draft arguments address the displayed claims under their explicit mathematical hypotheses and conventions. Their local review does not establish publication readiness or empirical applicability.
