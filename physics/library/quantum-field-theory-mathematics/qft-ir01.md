This mathematical page fixes one finite-mode space, a coercive real polynomial,
and its smooth compactly supported differential expression. It constructs the
closed positive form, proves compactness of its inclusion by cube means and
coercive tails, and obtains one explicit self-adjoint form realization with
compact resolvent. Its domain is specified spectrally, and the proof does not
claim essential self-adjointness of the initial differential expression or
uniqueness among other extensions. A scaled Hermite basis gives an exact
quadratic-form comparison; the resulting eigenbasis yields global unitary
evolution, a finite Gibbs trace, and the ground-state variational principle.
All claims concern fixed finite $N$ and mathematical parameters; no measurement,
continuum limit, or infinite-volume conclusion is included.
