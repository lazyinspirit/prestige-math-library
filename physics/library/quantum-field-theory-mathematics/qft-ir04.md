This page defines scaling degree for distributions dilated about a point, proves a same-degree extension theorem for arbitrary punctured distributions of finite real scaling degree, and classifies the finite delta-jet freedom at that point. The proof uses a fixed-support Frechet space and Baire category under the explicitly stated Axiom of Choice, which supplies the dependent-choice hypothesis of the published Baire theorem. The four-dimensional Euclidean example computes an exact logarithmic scale term for one specified kernel. The results do not assert that arbitrary distribution products exist or that an extension is selected by a physical renormalization prescription.
