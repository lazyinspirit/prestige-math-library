The counterexample checks the radial power printed for the Yukawa expression
in the cited source. Its $r^{-2}e^{-\mu r}$ term has nonzero screened
Laplacian at every $r>0$, while $r^{-1}e^{-\mu r}$ solves the homogeneous
equation off the origin. No delta normalization or full Green-function claim
is made.
