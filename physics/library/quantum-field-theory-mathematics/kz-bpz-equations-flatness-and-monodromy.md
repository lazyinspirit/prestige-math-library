This page derives the genus-zero WZW KZ equations from the affine current Ward identity and the Sugawara $L_{-1}$ mode, then proves flatness and braid monodromy on the finite top-space KZ system. For unequal labels the unordered system uses a direct sum over label assignments. A separate horizontal identification is required before calling its full fiber a conformal-block space.

The fixed-coordinate current and Virasoro interfaces allow restricted affine modules and fractional conformal weights. The generic integral-$L_0$ coinvariant carrier is used only under its actual hypotheses; no coordinate-independent fractional-weight extension, local-freeness result, or full CFT is asserted.
