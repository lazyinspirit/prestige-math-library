This page constructs the selected massless scalar Fock field on the prescribed external metric $g=\Omega^2\eta$ on $\mathbb R^4$. It proves the exact curvature-coupled wave identity, the weak distribution continuity of the compact-test smearing map, the massless field equation, vacuum positivity, the two-point kernel relative to curved volume, causal commutativity, and formal symmetry. The construction has five current draft direct suppliers: the QFT Fock/test-space definition, the mass-shell definition, the massless Minkowski field, its causal commutator, and the conformal-curvature result. It does not assert a unique curved-spacetime state, a generic Hadamard theorem, renormalized stress-tensor covariance, or backreaction.
