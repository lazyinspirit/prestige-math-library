The companion example compares one-mode bosonic CCR and fermionic CAR with the exact domains of creation, annihilation and the number operator. Its square-summable coefficient sequence belongs to both creation and annihilation domains but lies outside the number-operator domain; the displayed convergence and divergence arguments establish this distinction locally.

Use the Fock pairing, sector conventions and operator domains from [[qft-mf01]]. The example is a mathematical boundary witness for unbounded domains and does not assign particle statistics, model a measurement or report an empirical result.
