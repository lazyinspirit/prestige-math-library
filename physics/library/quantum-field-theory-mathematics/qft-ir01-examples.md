The one-mode quartic example bounds the change of the ground eigenvalue by a
Gaussian trial state and monotonicity of the quadratic form. Its parameters are
mathematical model data. The scope note records that results for one fixed
finite regulator imply neither existence nor nonexistence of a continuum
limit; such a limit needs a separately specified family and convergence
structure.
