Assume Countable Choice. For the finite-support Weyl algebra on $S=\mathbb R^2$ defined in [[cex-qft-nonregular-weyl-state]], the Weyl relations and positivity of a normalized state do not ensure that every GNS orbit map $t\mapsto\pi(W(tv))\xi$ is norm-continuous for every $v\in S$ and every vector $\xi$ in the GNS Hilbert space. The counterexample constructs a state whose cyclic unit vector $\Omega$ satisfies $\|\pi(W(tv))\Omega-\Omega\|=\sqrt2$ whenever $v\ne0$ and $t\ne0$, so the orbit is discontinuous at $t=0$ in every nonzero direction. The construction is mathematical and makes no claim about a physical vacuum or preparation.
