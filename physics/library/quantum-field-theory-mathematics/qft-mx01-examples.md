The companion page gives two worked checks against the A-page suppliers. The finite two-level example chooses times where the exact unitary matrix element square and the first-order squared coefficient separate visibly; it also compares the exact survival value with a positive-rate exponential. This makes the finite-time truncation warning numerical without claiming that a continuum unstable particle has periodic survival.

The oscillator example evaluates the one-mode time-ordered correlator at a negative time and compares it with the explicitly defined kernel $G_R$. At the chosen time the correlator is nonzero and $G_R$ vanishes; this is a comparison of two functions in the stated model and makes no claim about response to an external source. Both examples are exact calculations in the stated mathematical models. No experimental datum is added, and no probabilistic interpretation is assigned to either matrix-element square.
