This page defines finite bounded Kraus instrument data and proves their outcome effects, normalized probabilities, and conditional density-state updates for trace-class states on any nonzero complex Hilbert space, assuming Countable Choice. These are mathematical identities and do not establish physical apparatus realization or a measurement postulate. The companion page records the separate boundary between formal coefficients and analytic convergence.

[[def-qft-finite-bounded-kraus-instrument]] fixes the finite operator family and normalization convention. [[lem-qft-finite-instrument-normalization]] proves the probability and state-update identities from the exact density-expansion and bounded trace-cyclicity suppliers.
