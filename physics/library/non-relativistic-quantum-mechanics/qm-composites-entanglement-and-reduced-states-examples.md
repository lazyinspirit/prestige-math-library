The companion page to [[qm-composites-entanglement-and-reduced-states]]
collects exact model computations and one thought experiment.
[[texp-nrqm-composites-singlet-correlations]] computes the spin singlet of two
declared spin-half internal factors: the reduced preparations, the joint
directional outcome laws, the correlation function $\mathbb E(a,b)=-a\cdot b$
with perfect anti-correlation for equal directions, and the
setting-independent marginals, all as conditions inside the declared
framework.
[[ex-nrqm-composites-marker-overlap-visibility]] evaluates the marked
two-component model on flat one-dimensional profiles and a two-dimensional
marker, where the detection law is
$p(\varphi)(B)=\frac12(1+\cos\vartheta\cos\varphi)$, the visibility equals the
marker overlap $\cos\vartheta$ exactly, and the extremes $\vartheta=0$ and
$\vartheta=\pi/2$ give full contrast and the pure which-path law.
[[ex-nrqm-composites-improper-versus-proper-mixtures]] contrasts the reduced
preparation $\frac12I$ of the singlet with a fair random mixture of factor
preparations that assigns the same density operator, and shows that the two
cases are distinguished by the joint directional correlations, not by any
local statistic of the factor.
[[rem-nrqm-composites-examples-scope-limits]] records what these computations
establish, their lack of empirical content, and the comparisons and
mechanisms they leave to later pages.
