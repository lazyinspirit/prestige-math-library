This companion page works the driven-dynamics statements of
`qm-driven-dynamics-and-adiabatic-limits` through four conditional
computations and a scope record.

[[ex-nrqm-driven-dynamics-circular-drive-exact-populations]] checks the exact
Rabi populations of the declared circular two-level model at the
quarter-inversion value $1/2$, the pi-pulse value $1$, the return value $0$
and a detuned value $1/4$, all at rational multiples of the Rabi period.
[[ex-nrqm-driven-dynamics-linear-drive-rotating-wave-budget]] evaluates the
rotating-wave error contract of the linear drive at declared dimensionless
times, including an exact rational averaged population and the observation
that the contract is conservative at the initial time, and contrasts it with
the error-free circular case.
[[ex-nrqm-driven-dynamics-ammonia-two-level-resonance]] matches the declared
linear model onto a two-level inversion family driven by an oscillating
field, computes the averaged inversion probability and the pulse time
$\pi\hbar/(2\mu E_{0})$ at resonance and evaluates the error contract there
for a declared coupling ratio.
[[ex-nrqm-driven-dynamics-driven-oscillator-coherent-response]] shows that a
coherent preparation of the driven oscillator
$H_{\mathrm{osc}}-f(t)Q$ follows exactly the classical forced response in its
mean position and momentum while keeping its position and momentum spreads.
[[rem-nrqm-driven-dynamics-examples-scope-limits]] records what the examples
establish and what they do not contain.
