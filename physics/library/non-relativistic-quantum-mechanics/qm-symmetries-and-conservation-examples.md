The companion page to [[qm-symmetries-and-conservation]] collects exact model
computations, one selection-rule derivation, one counterexample and one
conditional instance of the invariance theorems.
[[ex-nrqm-symmetries-free-translation-and-momentum]] computes the free model
$V\equiv0$: every translation is a symmetry, the free flow and the
translations act in the momentum representation by unimodular multipliers that
commute, the momentum components are conserved sharp observables with the law,
moment and generator statements of the translation theorem, and the modulus
of the momentum representative is invariant in time almost everywhere.
[[cex-nrqm-symmetries-nonconstant-potential-breaks-translation]] refutes the
universal claim that every bounded measurable nonconstant potential has all
nonzero translations as symmetries: for the indicator of the unit ball no
nonzero translation is a symmetry, because the shifted potential differs from
the original on a set of positive measure.
[[ex-nrqm-symmetries-parity-selection-rule]] shows that in an even-potential
model every energy eigenspace is invariant under the parity action, that a
nondegenerate eigenstate has definite parity, and that the position matrix
element between two states of equal parity vanishes.
[[ex-nrqm-symmetries-central-potential-rotation-invariance]] verifies the
almost-everywhere rotational and parity invariance of a bounded radial
potential and instantiates the rotational-and-parity theorem: all rotations
are symmetries, the rotation action is a strongly continuous unitary symmetry
group, and the coordinate-rotation generators are conserved sharp observables.
None of the items is an experiment; no counts, calibration, resolution,
efficiency, background, sampling or uncertainty appears, and no measured
value is asserted.
