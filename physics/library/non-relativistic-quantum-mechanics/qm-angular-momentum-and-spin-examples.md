The companion page to [[qm-angular-momentum-and-spin]] collects exact model
computations of the spin and orbital structure and one conditional thought
experiment.
[[ex-nrqm-angular-momentum-spin-half-directional-probabilities]] evaluates
the Born probabilities of the directional spin components on product
preparations in the Bloch parametrization, with the coordinate and worked
special cases.
[[ex-nrqm-angular-momentum-l-eigenfunctions]] writes the explicit harmonic
polynomial representatives of the low spherical harmonics, checks their
orthonormality on the sphere, computes
$L_{3}^{\mathrm{sph}}Y_{1m}=\hbar mY_{1m}$ and the sphere Casimir value
$2\hbar^{2}$ on the degree-one block, and records the general $(l,m)$
identities.
[[ex-nrqm-angular-momentum-zeeman-toy-model]] computes a uniform-field
Zeeman model: the sector operator and expectation identities on product
vectors in $D(H_{0})$, the factorized flow on all product states, an
explicitly labelled two-level truncation with gap $\Delta E=\hbar\omega$,
and the precession matrices of the transverse means.
[[texp-nrqm-angular-momentum-stern-gerlach-splitting]] is a conditional
thought experiment: under the explicit hypothetical containment premise it
derives the separation of the two spin-sector means in a linear field window
and the conservation of the sector weights and of the total mean; it asserts
no apparatus, no trajectory and no measured record.
[[rem-nrqm-angular-momentum-examples-scope-limits]] records what these
computations establish and the measurement and empirical questions they leave
open.
