The companion page to [[qm-central-potentials-and-hydrogen]] collects exact
model computations and one empirical-status record.
[[ex-nrqm-central-hydrogen-bound-energy-units-and-reduced-mass]] verifies the
bound-energy identity in the declared $Z=1$ hydrogen branch,
$E_{n}=-\frac{m\kappa^{2}}{2\hbar^{2}n^{2}}=-\frac{\hbar^{2}}{2ma_{\kappa}^{2}n^{2}}=-\frac{m\alpha^{2}c^{2}}{2n^{2}}$,
with $\alpha=q^{2}/(4\pi\varepsilon_{0}\hbar c)$ on the equal-magnitude
charge branch $q_{-}=q_{+}=q=e$. It also verifies the reduced-mass factor
$\mu/m_{1}=1/(1+m_{1}/m_{2})$ between the relative and fixed-nucleus
energies, checks the dimensional consistency of each identity, and identifies
the continuum threshold of the two-body operator; no numerical value is
assigned.
[[ex-nrqm-central-hydrogen-radial-distributions-and-moments]] computes the
normalised reduced radial functions of the states with $n\le2$, their radial
densities and the moments $\langle r\rangle_{10}=3a/2$,
$\langle r\rangle_{20}=6a$, $\langle r\rangle_{21}=5a$ and
$\langle r^{-1}\rangle_{20}=\langle r^{-1}\rangle_{21}=1/(4a)$, and checks
that reduced radial functions of different angular momentum are not
orthogonal.
[[rem-nrqm-central-hydrogen-spectroscopy-status]] records that no experiment
item is built on this pair, that transition-frequency and line-intensity
statements belong to the later radiation and light-matter items, and what a
comparison with measured spectra would presuppose.
[[rem-nrqm-central-examples-scope-limits]] records the scope of the examples
page and its dependence on the declared model.
