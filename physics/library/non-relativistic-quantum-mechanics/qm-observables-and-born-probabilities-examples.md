The companion page to [[qm-observables-and-born-probabilities]] collects
finite worked readouts of the outcome law and one refutation of an informal
claim about the state assignment. The exact two-level energy readout
[[ex-nrqm-observables-two-level-energy-readout]] computes the spectral measure
of $E_{1}P_{1}+E_{2}P_{2}$ on $\mathbb C^{2}$, the outcome probabilities of a
pure and of a mixed preparation, and the mean, second moment and variance of
the readout. [[ex-nrqm-observables-position-bin-probabilities]] computes the
first-coordinate law of a normalized Gaussian packet in the massive-particle
model, shows that exact coordinate values carry zero probability while bins of
positive length carry positive probability, and gives explicit elementary
bounds for the symmetric bin. The counterexample
[[cex-nrqm-observables-plane-wave-is-not-a-preparation]] refutes the claim that
a free plane wave or a point-localized delta symbol is a preparation: neither
is an element of the state space, and the model's exact-position statement is
atomlessness. Finally [[ex-nrqm-observables-position-mean-without-variance]]
exhibits a legitimate preparation whose position law has a finite mean and
infinite second moment, showing that the domain hypotheses of the moment
statements are genuine. The scope remark
[[rem-nrqm-observables-examples-scope-limits]] records that none of the items
is an experiment: no counts, calibration, resolution, uncertainty or data
comparison is asserted anywhere on the page.
