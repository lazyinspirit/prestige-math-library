This page develops the isolated-system evolution adopted by the framework's
autonomous-dynamics postulate P4. The vocabulary is fixed in
[[def-nrqm-autonomous-dynamics-framework-evolution]]: an assigned energy
observable is an assigned sharp observable whose declared unit is an energy
unit, the flow is the strongly continuous unitary group
$U(t)=\exp(-itH_{\mathrm{op}}/\hbar)$ generated by it in the spectral calculus,
and the definition records the Schroedinger-picture evolutions
$\psi(t)=U(t)\psi(0)$ and $\rho(t)=U(t)\rho(0)U(t)^{*}$, the Heisenberg
representative $A_H(t)=U(t)^{*}AU(t)$ of a bounded assigned sharp observable,
the notions of stationary preparation and energy eigenstate, and strong
spectral compatibility with the energy. No assumption beyond P1, P2 and P4 is
adopted.

The mathematical layer is the flow calculus of a self-adjoint generator.
[[lem-nrqm-autonomous-dynamics-flow-calculus]] records the group, unitarity and
domain properties of $U(t)=e^{itT}$, the commutation of the flow with every
bounded Borel function of $T$, and the fact that the flow is norm-differentiable
exactly on $D(T)$, with derivative $iTU(t)x$. The Heisenberg flow
[[lem-nrqm-autonomous-dynamics-heisenberg-flow]] conjugates a bounded operator
by the flow, proves boundedness, norm and spectrum invariance, the transported
spectral measure $E_{A(t)}(B)=U(-t)E_A(B)U(t)$, the covariance law, and the
domain-qualified equation of motion $-i[T,A(t)]$. The stationary-ray lemma
[[lem-nrqm-autonomous-dynamics-stationary-ray-characterization]] proves that
the invariance of a ray under the flow is equivalent to the vector being an
eigenvector of the generator, by restricting the flow to the closed invariant
span of the vector and applying Stone's theorem there.

The physics theorems read these results as conditional consequences of P4.
[[pthm-nrqm-autonomous-dynamics-schrodinger-picture-flow]] draws the unitary
flow, the invariance of the energy domain, the domain-qualified Schroedinger
equation $i\hbar\frac{d}{dt}\psi=H_{\mathrm{op}}\psi$ and the evolution of
density-operator preparations.
[[pthm-nrqm-autonomous-dynamics-heisenberg-picture]] identifies the Heisenberg
representative as an assigned sharp observable with the same unit, norm and
spectrum, proves that the two pictures assign identical outcome laws to every
preparation, and gives the domain-qualified Heisenberg equation together with
the freezing of energy-compatible bounded observables.
[[pthm-nrqm-autonomous-dynamics-stationary-states-and-energy-conservation]]
computes the conservation of the energy law in every preparation, the
conservation of finite first and second moments for pure preparations
represented by nonzero vectors in the energy domain (using unit
representatives), the conservation of bounded energy-compatible observables,
and the exact equivalence between stationary pure preparations and energy eigenrays, with the
eigenray evolution $U(t)\psi=e^{-it\lambda/\hbar}\psi$. No item asserts
equilibration, relaxation, a measurement model, a driven or open-system
evolution, an apparatus realization or an empirical result; the scope remark
[[rem-nrqm-autonomous-dynamics-scope-and-empirical-status]] records these
limits.
