The companion page to [[qm-photons-and-radiation-modes]] collects three
conditional comparisons inside the declared radiation models and a scope
remark.
[[ex-nrqm-photons-malus-and-jones-analyzer-ratios]] computes the transmitted
Born probability $\cos^{2}\theta$ of the declared ideal analyser on the
preparation along $e_{1}$ and the dimensionless relative-intensity fraction of
a declared elementary classical transverse-amplitude model whose coordinates
are normalized to a fixed reference amplitude and whose intensity is squared
amplitude norm, and shows that the two declared models agree on the
ratio, so that this number alone does not establish field quantisation.
[[ex-nrqm-photons-single-mode-number-statistics-coherent-and-thermal]] compares
the count statistics of three declared preparations of one mode: the sharp
count of the $n$-quanta state, the Poisson law of dimensionless coherent-state
label $z$ with mean and variance $|z|^{2}$, and the geometric law with mean
$1/(e^{\beta E_{0}}-1)$ and variance $m(m+1)$ of a declared
effective-mode-energy preparation, with declared parameters
$E_{0}>0$ joules and $\beta>0$ in $\mathrm J^{-1}$, not derived from
the mode profile.
[[texp-nrqm-photons-classical-field-and-quantized-detection-comparison]]
proves that the declared classical and quantised models assign the same
normalised two-bin law, with the same interference cross-term, to every
declared amplitude datum, so that a fringe pattern of that form is not a
discriminator between the two declared models; a discriminating test must
instead use a prediction on which the models differ and must declare the
detector, preparation and sampling model. The page records no experiment, no
measured count and no statistical inference; single-photon polarisation and
counting comparisons remain research candidates for a separate reported
experiment, not observations recorded here.
[[rem-nrqm-photons-examples-scope-limits]] records what the three computations
establish, the idealisations and declared parameters they rest on and what
they do not claim.
