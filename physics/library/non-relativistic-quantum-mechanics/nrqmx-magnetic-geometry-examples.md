This companion page works out two consequences inside the ideal models fixed
on [[nrqmx-magnetic-geometry]]. [[ex-nrqmx-landau-3d-continuum]] adds free
motion parallel to a uniform field and derives absolutely continuous bands
from the planar Landau levels. [[ex-nrqmx-flux-half-crossing]] compares the
periodic ring levels at zero and half a flux quantum and identifies the
twofold ground-state crossing. Both are conditional model deductions; neither
is an experimental report or a finite-sample prediction. In the three-
dimensional example, $A=(0,Bx,0):\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^3$ and the signed
scalar $B$ is the $z$-component of the field vector
$\mathbf B=(0,0,B)=\nabla\times A:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^3$.
