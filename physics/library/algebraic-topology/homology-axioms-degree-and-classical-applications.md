Ordinary homology is characterized on CW pairs by its axioms and a specified coefficient group. The comparison proceeds from oriented simplices to finite CW pairs, cellular complexes, and the skeletal telescope. Sphere degree then supplies local multiplicity formulas and classical applications: no retraction, Brouwer fixed points, tangent fields, and invariance of Euclidean dimension.
