title: Algebraic Topology
---

Algebraic topology assigns algebraic invariants to spaces and maps. The pages develop singular, simplicial and cellular homology; singular cohomology, products, coefficient theorems, local coefficients and duality; and the homotopy theory of CW complexes, fibrations, cofiber sequences and higher homotopy groups. Spectral sequences and obstruction theory support calculations and classification, including Postnikov towers and classifying spaces. Further topics include characteristic classes, Thom and Gysin constructions, vector-bundle classification, complex and real K-theory, spectra, stable homotopy groups, bordism and simple-homotopy torsion. The range joins foundational chain models to generalized cohomology and stable phenomena, with hypotheses on spaces, coefficients and bundles varying by result.

Topology supplies continuity and homotopy foundations; Homological Algebra contributes chain-complex methods, while Abstract Algebra and Category Theory provide coefficient and functorial tools. These constructions support Differential Topology, Differential Geometry and Knot Theory, where homology, cohomology and spectral sequences drive geometric arguments. They also provide local-system and homotopy tools for Algebraic Geometry, Complex Analysis and Representation Theory of Groups.
