This page defines Glivenko–Cantelli and Donsker classes with explicit pointwise measurability and outer-probability conventions. Finite $L^1(P)$ bracketing entropy yields a uniform law, with separate convergence-in-probability and strong branches; the strong branch declares Countable Choice. A deterministic comparison converts uniform empirical-risk deviation into an oracle inequality. The page also proves a sub-Gaussian chaining bound, a local bracketing maximal inequality, asymptotic equicontinuity under the fixed-$P$ normalized $L^2$ bracketing entropy integral, and the resulting functional central limit theorem. The functional theorem states its finite-variance, measurability, and Axiom of Choice hypotheses, then proves tightness of the Gaussian limit and process-level convergence locally.
