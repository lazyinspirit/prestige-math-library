The two-point Bernoulli pair gives an everywhere positive likelihood ratio
and verifies the change-of-measure identity by direct summation. Two
disjoint point masses show why an arbitrary pair of laws need not have a
full likelihood-ratio density: the denominator gives zero mass to the
numerator's support.
