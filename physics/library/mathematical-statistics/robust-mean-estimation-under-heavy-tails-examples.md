These examples use a continuous polynomial-tail law with finite variance, an exact five-block calculation showing rejection of one arbitrarily displaced block, and indistinguishable point-mass samples at a replacement fraction of one half. They keep the clean finite-variance guarantee, sample-replacement model, and iid Huber-mixture model distinct.
