This page develops the finite-dimensional Gaussian route from orthogonal changes of coordinates to independent chi-square components. It defines the positive-degree Student t and Fisher F laws, proves their CDF regularity, and derives the exact unknown-variance one-sample t pivot and fixed-design Gaussian regression t/F pivots. Choice assumptions are stated where the characteristic-function and Fourier-uniqueness route uses them; t and F denominators always have positive degrees of freedom.
