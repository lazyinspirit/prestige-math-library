Four examples separate the main hypotheses and conclusions: weak convergence to a constant can coexist with persistent expectations; multiplying a vanishing deterministic rate by a tight random factor preserves convergence in probability and gives a root-$n$ bounded sequence; observationally equivalent Bernoulli laws can carry different targets; and the Bernoulli sample proportion has an explicit finite-sample consistency bound, including at the parameter endpoints.
