The examples compute the physical-dependence errors and long-run covariance
for a finite moving average and a geometrically weighted Rademacher shift. A
square-integrable linear filter shows that the theorem's summability condition
is substantive, while a deterministic path sequence separates convergence of
terminal values from weak convergence of path laws.
