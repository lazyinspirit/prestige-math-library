The companion page keeps the examples concrete: sequence spaces, bounded and
continuous function spaces, a standard incomplete dense subspace, and the
remetrisation warning that the topology of a vector space is weaker than its
norm geometry. Each example depends only on the A-page completion and closed
subspace results, plus earlier published theorems already in closure.
