This companion checks an anisotropic norm on $\mathbb C_{\mathbb R}$ and tests the same function on the original complex vector space $\mathbb C$. Its values at $1$ and $i$ show that it is real-homogeneous on the realification but fails complex absolute homogeneity on the original complex structure.
