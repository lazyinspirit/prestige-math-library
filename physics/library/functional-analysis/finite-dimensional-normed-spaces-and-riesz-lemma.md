This page packages the first finite-dimensional rigidity results for normed
spaces: coordinate-space models, norm equivalence, completeness, closedness of
finite-dimensional subspaces, and boundedness of linear maps from finite
dimension. It then turns to Riesz's lemma and the compactness consequences that
separate finite from infinite dimension, keeping the closed-unit-ball direction
choice free and isolating the sharper Baire-cost remark for the countable-Hamel
basis theorem. The page closes with the based Kuratowski embedding and the
Wojdyslawski convex-hull closedness refinement.
