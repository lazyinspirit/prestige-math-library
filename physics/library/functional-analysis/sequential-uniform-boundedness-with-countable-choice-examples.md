Coordinate truncations on $c_0$ have exact operator norm one and converge to every input. A local proof of completeness assembles unique scalar coordinate limits without choice. The optional application of the sequential theorem explicitly assumes $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$.

On the incomplete space $c_{00}$, the operators $T_nx=nx_n$ have norm $n$, while each orbit is eventually zero. Reciprocal truncations give a Cauchy sequence without a limit in the domain, isolating the missing completeness hypothesis.
