Paths, endpoint-fixed homotopies, finite closed pasting and product continuity
supply the topological input. The earlier group material supplies the algebraic
language for operations on loop classes and homomorphisms induced by pointed
continuous maps.

This page constructs $\pi_1(X,x_0)$, proves its group laws, and establishes
well-definedness, functoriality and based-homotopy invariance of induced maps.
It defines simple connectedness without assuming change of basepoint and proves
that every nonempty convex Euclidean subset is simply connected.
