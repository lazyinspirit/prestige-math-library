This mathematics page develops finite-mode bosonic Fock and Gaussian structure. The Fock space remains infinite-dimensional at every positive mode count, and a finite number cutoff cannot preserve exact canonical commutation relations. The page fixes an explicit action scale, quadrature order, Weyl-label convention, covariance, and density-operator moment definition.

The Fock-to-Schrödinger identification, singular-block symplectic factorization, and explicit chirp, coordinate, and Fourier implementers are proved locally. The Fourier convention is the imported $2\pi$-frequency transform, with its dilation to the angular-frequency unitary used in the coordinate formulas stated explicitly. The finite metaplectic group is then constructed from normalized homogeneous quadratic evolutions; its Maslov phase has exactly two choices over each finite symplectic matrix. Williamson reduction and a product of one-mode thermal states give the finite-mode Gaussian covariance criterion. No infinite-mode implementability theorem or experimental claim is asserted.

The page uses three independent full-text treatments: the mathematical lecture notes of Phan Thành Nam, the Gaussian review of Weedbrook et al., and the symplectic and Maslov arguments of Robbin and Salamon. Convention differences and the excluded source assertion about general Hermiticity of $i\Omega V$ are recorded in the batch coverage evidence.
