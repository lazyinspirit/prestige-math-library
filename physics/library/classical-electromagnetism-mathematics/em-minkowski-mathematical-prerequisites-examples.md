The companion page records the hypothesis boundaries of the main page in one
worked example. [[ex-em-minkowski-mathematical-prerequisites-hypothesis-boundaries]]
checks that the vector $e_0$ is future timelike and unit; that the boost sends
it to $(\gamma,-\gamma\beta,0,0)$ with quadratic value
$\eta(\Lambda_\beta e_0,\Lambda_\beta e_0)=-1$ and positive time component
while the factor $\gamma$ is undefined at $\beta=1$; that the null line
$(s,s,0,0)$ makes the proper-time construction identically zero and is therefore
not a parameter; that the symmetric form $F=\eta$ has $\Phi=\mathrm{id}$ and
violates $\eta(u,\Phi(u))=0$, exhibiting the antisymmetry hypothesis as genuine;
and that the skew witness $F_{01}=1$ sends $e_0$ to $-e_1$ with the invariant
pairing and the raised-index formula agreeing in sign. No physical clock premise
and no experimental claim is used.
