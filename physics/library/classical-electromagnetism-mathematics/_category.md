The mathematical prerequisites and supporting suppliers for the classical
electromagnetism collection: elementary vector, derivative and measure
conventions; the Fourier, Sobolev, elliptic and spectral machinery used by the
field equations; contour and causal-dispersion analysis; the wave-kernel and
retarded-potential estimates; the variational and boundary-form prerequisites
of the electromagnetic action and its interface problems; and the deterministic
bounded-error comparison used to read confined parameter statements from
calibrated indicator data. Items here are mathematics: they carry `domain:
mathematics`, depend only on mathematical suppliers, and are excluded from
physical conclusions. Their physical consumers are the classical
electromagnetism and special relativity pages, which use them as stated
mathematical premises under the physics schema's dependency roles. No physical
postulate, observation or empirical claim is asserted on this page, and the
statistical qualifications of any physical use remain with the consuming
physical item.
