This mathematical category develops tools used by the electromagnetic pages: vector, derivative and measure conventions; complex Fourier representations and period averages; Sobolev, elliptic and spectral results; causal-dispersion and wave-kernel estimates; retarded-potential bounds; surface distributions and interface formulas; variational and boundary forms; and deterministic bounded-error comparisons. Some estimates cover point-supported currents or compact sources; interface identities track distributional jumps on smooth surfaces. A comparison result confines parameter error using declared sensitivity and calibration bounds, without a sampling or coverage claim. Contour analysis treats causal dispersion under its declared response assumptions. Statements specify domains and regularity, including smooth-field or weak formulations where needed.

The results draw on real and functional analysis, differential geometry, PDE and topology, with selected spacetime definitions from special relativity. They support Classical Electromagnetism directly, and selected results also support relativistic particle mechanics and Thermodynamics and General Relativity mathematics. Physical interpretations belong to those consuming frameworks and retain their model assumptions.
