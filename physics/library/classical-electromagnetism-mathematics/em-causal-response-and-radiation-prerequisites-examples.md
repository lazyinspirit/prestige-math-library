The companion page records two hypothesis boundaries of the main page. The
first clause of [[ex-em-causal-response-and-radiation-prerequisites-hypothesis-boundaries]]
exhibits the causal kernel $K(s)=-e^{-s}$, whose integrable-branch transfer
function $\chi(\omega)=-1/(1-i\omega)$ has strictly negative imaginary part for
every $\omega>0$: the response is causal and satisfies the dispersion
relations, yet its monochromatic period-averaged work is strictly negative, so
nonnegative work is an additional passivity assumption on the model. The
second clause shows that in the compact-source radiation setting the flux
integral at fixed observation time vanishes identically once the sphere lies
beyond the light cone of the source's compact spacetime support, while the
limit of [[thm-em-radiation-asymptotic-flux]] is taken at fixed retarded time:
the two formulations differ, and no decay assumption at spatial infinity is
available to replace the retarded-time statement.
