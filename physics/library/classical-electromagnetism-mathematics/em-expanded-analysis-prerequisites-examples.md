The companion page records explicit mathematical checks of the hypotheses and conventions of two items on [[em-expanded-analysis-prerequisites]]; it carries `domain: mathematics` and uses only the mathematical premises of those items.

[[ex-em-outgoing-dtn-and-pec-zero-mode-boundaries]] works two exact examples: the degree-zero outgoing Dirichlet-to-Neumann coefficient $t_0=ik-1/R$ obtained by logarithmic differentiation of the explicit spherical Hankel function, matching the exact DtN bounds of the spherical Hankel lemma; and the smooth shell field $\nabla(1/|x|)=-x/|x|^3$, which is an admissible divergence-free, curl-free member of the PEC graph class and therefore an explicit topology zero mode that a complete spectral description must retain.
