Explicit mathematical boundary cases for the bounded-error comparison of the
companion page. A linear prediction with slope $m>0$ attains the confined bound
$\delta/m$ at both endpoints of the compatible interval, so the constant in the
conclusion cannot be improved for that instance; and the identically zero
prediction shows that the positive lower bound on the response is not
removable, since with the same indicator value and the same two bounds every
real parameter value remains compatible. Both constructions are mathematical
observations about a hypothetical bounded-error model; no experimental data,
sampling law or statistical inference is asserted.
