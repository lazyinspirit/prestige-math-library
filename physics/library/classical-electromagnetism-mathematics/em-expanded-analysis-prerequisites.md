This page is the mathematical analysis toolkit of the classical-electromagnetism track: the variational, Sobolev, elliptic and spectral prerequisites used by the field equations and their interface problems, together with the retarded, causal-dispersion, multipole, spherical-harmonic and cavity-analysis suppliers. Items here are mathematics: they carry `domain: mathematics`, depend only on mathematical suppliers, and are stated as conditional mathematical results. No physical postulate, observation or empirical claim is asserted on this page; the physical consumers are the classical-electromagnetism and special-relativity pages, which use these items as stated mathematical premises. The Axiom of Choice or Countable Choice is declared exactly where the cited completeness, trace, extension, harmonic and measure interfaces require it, and the arguments that are choice-free are marked as such.
