title: Algebraic Geometry
---

These pages treat algebraic geometry from affine and projective varieties through schemes and higher-dimensional geometry. They develop coordinate rings, morphisms and rational maps; dimension, constructibility, tangent spaces, smoothness and Bertini; embeddings and projective quotients; divisors, intersection products, Chow groups and Riemann–Roch. Birational topics include contractions, blowups, normalization and resolution of singularities. Other strands cover algebraic groups and representations, invariant theory and geometric invariant theory, abelian varieties and arithmetic models, étale covers, and the cohomological geometry of constructible sheaves, mixed Hodge structures and perverse decompositions. The collection connects concrete geometry of curves and surfaces with questions that use scheme and cohomological methods.

Scheme Theory and Commutative Algebra supply scheme and local-ring language; Lie Theory, Representation Theory of Groups and Homological Algebra support group, quotient and cohomology topics. Complex Analysis and Algebraic Topology add analytic and cohomological tools. These methods support Scheme Theory, Lie Theory, Representation Theory of Groups, Hall Algebras and Tensor Categories, and Knot Theory, where they inform geometric quotients, group representations and character varieties.
