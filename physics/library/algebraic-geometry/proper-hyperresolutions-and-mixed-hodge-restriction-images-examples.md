- ex-a-nodal-projective-curve-has-a-weight-zero-cohomology-class

The example computes the mixed Hodge structure of the first cohomology of a
rational projective curve with one ordinary node. Its normalization is the
projective line, and the Cech nerve of the normalization has as its degree-$p$
term the diagonal copy of $\mathbb P^1$ together with $2^{p+1}-2$ reduced
isolated points, one for each nonconstant tuple of the two node preimages. On
the degree-zero cohomology row of the descent spectral sequence the alternating
face differential forces the diagonal coordinate to vanish and the two
off-diagonal coordinates to be opposite, so the $(1,0)$ entry of $E_2$ is
one-dimensional, while the $(0,1)$ entry vanishes because $H^1(\mathbb P^1)=0$.
The weight identification of the proper-weights theorem then shows that this
single class has weight $0$: proper singular cohomology need not be pure of its
degree, and in this case the total degree-one cohomology is concentrated in the
lowest possible weight.
