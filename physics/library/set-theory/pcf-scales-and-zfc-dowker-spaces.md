The construction begins with countable paracompactness, shrinking criteria,
and the interval-product characterization of Dowker spaces. Reduced ordinal
products, exact upper bounds, and PCF scales provide the set-theoretic tools
for the Rudin and Kojman–Shelah constructions. The general PCF development
and the direct countable-product scale argument are presented separately.

The second part develops Balogh's continuum-sized construction from its
combinatorial map and neighborhood bases. The final part states the precise
additional hypotheses for the small-Dowker constructions and develops their
coloring, guessing, and ladder-topology arguments. Conditional results are
kept distinct from the ZFC constructions; the concluding remark records a
dated literature status, not an additional theorem.

Uses of the Axiom of Choice are identified in individual items. The recorded
Set Theory catalogue supplies no proof prerequisite. Worked instances and
the interval-product counterexample are on the companion examples page.
