The examples compute the distinctions among common-level products, full
products, dense matrices, and unequal cone heights.  The dimension-two word
calculation displays every legal rearrangement, while the finite--cofinite
algebra makes the compactness-tree prime ideal concrete.

The final countermodel explains why BPI does not well-order every set.  In the
basic Cohen model its infinite Dedekind-finite set of reals cannot be
well-ordered, since repeatedly taking the least unused member would enumerate
infinitely many distinct elements.
