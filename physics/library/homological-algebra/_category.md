---
title: Homological Algebra
---

Homological algebra studies chain complexes and the standard derived constructions built from them. Its published pages develop homology, chain homotopies, mapping cones, long exact sequences, projective and injective resolutions, Ext and Tor, derived functors and categories, and spectral sequences. Other pages treat triangulated categories, delta functors, universal coefficients and Künneth theorems, group cohomology, Hochschild homology, bounded bimodule complexes and derived tensor products, and Grothendieck groups. The collection extends into dg methods, A-infinity and L-infinity deformation complexes, categorical Eilenberg-Watts results, Morita bicategories, and cyclic-tensor invariance.

Category Theory supplies additive and abelian settings, universal constructions, exactness, and diagram methods; Abstract Algebra and Commutative Algebra supply module and ring contexts. Linear Algebra and Differential Geometry also provide inputs for particular pages. Algebraic Geometry, Algebraic Topology, Braid Groups, Category O and Geometric Representation Theory, Hall Algebras and Tensor Categories, and Hecke Algebras and Categorification use these homological tools, including derived categories and spectral sequences.
