These examples solve the specified finite Gibbs-jump model for a thermal qubit and for its selected energy-resolved record. The first gives exact finite correlation and response coefficients, including the difference between a fixed-dissipator open response and a static Gibbs derivative. The second computes the record displacement variance. The record is a model output of the chosen instrument, not a measured position or a reported experiment.
