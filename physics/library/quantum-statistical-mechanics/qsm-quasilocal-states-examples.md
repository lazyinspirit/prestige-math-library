The first example multiplies two explicit Pauli matrices and checks disjoint-site commutation. The second sums norm-one one-site observables with summable coefficients, proves norm convergence, and detects every finite localization by a nonzero commutator. The third checks the partial-trace consistency equations for maximally mixed finite-region densities and derives their product expectation formula. The state construction in the third example is conditional on the finite-dimensional density/state interface described on the A page.
