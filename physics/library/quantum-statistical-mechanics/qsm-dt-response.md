This page separates finite-matrix response, trace-class normal-state response, and response on the quasi-local algebra of finite-range quantum spin systems. It proves the bounded-observable Kubo formula with an explicit finite-time remainder, records the precise graph-domain conditions needed for a strong Schrödinger solution, and derives the finite spectral fluctuation–dissipation relation with its zero-frequency qualification.

The infinite-Hilbert-space results use trace-class densities and bounded observables only; they do not use an unbounded trace product or assume finite energy or entropy moments. The infinite-spin result instead uses an invariant algebraic state on the actual finite-range automorphism group, not a global trace Hamiltonian. Neither a spatial finite-range bound nor isolated pure-point dynamics supplies temporal decay or a dc transport coefficient. The two physical postulates are adopted model assumptions, and the prepared-control physics theorem is a conditional prediction rather than an empirical observation.
