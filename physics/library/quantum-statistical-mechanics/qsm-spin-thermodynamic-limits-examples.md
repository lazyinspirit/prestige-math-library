The exactly solvable uniform onsite interaction $\Phi(\{x\})=cI_{\{x\}}$ has $H_\Lambda=c|\Lambda|I_\Lambda$ and pressure $\log q-\beta c$ for every finite nonempty region. This calculation is purely mathematical; it does not assign a material-specific value to $c$ or report a measurement.
