This page fixes finite ordered tensor registers, density matrices, bounded
observable trace pairings, finite POVMs and their subsystem restrictions. The
positive-temperature canonical preparation and a nonempty uniform energy
band are adopted separately as physical assumptions. The finite independent
model proves fixed-subsystem trace-norm convergence only under its explicit
width condition; it does not assert global trace convergence or a generic
interacting equivalence. A classical consistent family embeds as one
quasilocal C-star state, without identifying general quantum states as
classical. The regulated QFT branch uses a selected finite-mode coercive
polynomial form realization and retains unresolved QSM normal-state and
trace-Gibbs prerequisites. All probabilities remain model predictions, not
reported frequencies; neither thermalization nor regulator removal is
claimed.
