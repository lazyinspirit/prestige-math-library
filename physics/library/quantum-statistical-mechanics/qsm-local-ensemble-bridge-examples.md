These mathematical counterexamples establish three separate limits on
ensemble language: an exact mean-energy band can be empty at zero width;
agreement of entropy density does not force a one-site state to converge;
and equality of every proper fixed-size marginal does not force global
trace-norm convergence. Their states and measurements are finite matrix
constructions, not claims about laboratory preparations or empirical
frequencies.
