This page defines the bounded-strip KMS condition with imaginary-time width $\beta\hbar$ in physical seconds. It proves strip norm control and uniqueness, derives invariance from the KMS condition, characterizes finite-dimensional Gibbs states exactly, and constructs infinite-volume spin KMS states from subsequential limits of finite Gibbs states. The infinite-volume conclusions retain their explicit finite-matrix, countability, finite-range, and local-state hypotheses; they do not imply uniqueness, phase classification, a global trace-class Gibbs density, or physical relaxation.
