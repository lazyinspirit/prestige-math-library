These examples separate stationarity from the KMS condition and show that an infinite-volume KMS state need not come from a normal density in the finite-excitation vacuum representation. The zero-field tracial state is unique and KMS on the quasi-local algebra, while the vacuum-representation Gibbs trace diverges in the explicit positive-energy example. These are mathematical model results, not empirical claims.
