This page fixes the mathematical types, SI units, and primitive or derived roles for finite-dimensional quantum spins on an infinite lattice. It adopts local Born probabilities and the physical-time KMS criterion as model assumptions, then derives conditional predictions for thermodynamic dynamics, KMS-state existence, pressure limits, and diagonal Ising regimes. The dimensionless log-partition pressure per site is kept distinct from mechanical pressure; no relaxation, material fit, or experimental result is claimed.
