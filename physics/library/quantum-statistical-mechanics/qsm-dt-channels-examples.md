These exact finite calculations separate positivity from complete positivity, show a channel that lowers von Neumann entropy, and exhibit correlated joint preparations with the same system marginal but different outputs. A final two-qubit product-preparation model gives channel maps at every time while violating the semigroup law through a coherence revival. The counterexamples report no measurements and make no universal claim about every correlated preparation or every finite bath.
