This companion computes the one-down-spin sector of the periodic exchange
Hamiltonian. Each basis state is fixed by the $N-2$ bonds not touching the
down spin, while the two incident bonds move it to a neighboring site. The
discrete Fourier vectors therefore have eigenvalues
$J(N-2+2\cos(2\pi m/N))$. This finite calculation checks the periodic shift
orientation and coupling normalization; it is a mathematical example, not a
reported measurement.
