The orthogonal pure qubit states $|0\rangle$ and $|1\rangle$ have trace distance one, and their computational-basis PVM achieves total variation one. The calculation checks the factor $1/2$ in the state-distance convention against a finite measurement that distinguishes the states perfectly.
