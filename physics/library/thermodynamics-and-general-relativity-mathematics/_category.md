These pages provide mathematical definitions and conditional results used by the thermodynamics and general relativity models. The category develops finite spacelike slabs, geometric currents and flux balances, reference normalizations and stationary thermal data. It also contains restricted evolution constructions, independent-cell Gibbs states, static spherical equations and finite regulated gravitational preparations. Further sections specify smooth null screens, parameter families, smeared detector expressions and photon trace or flux calculations in the exact settings required by their consumers.

Every construction identifies its spaces, maps, domains, regularity and boundary assumptions. A current identity is a statement about the specified fields and geometry; assigning it a physical energy or entropy meaning belongs to a separately adopted model. Similarly, a finite trace, a chosen state or a restricted spectral calculation is used within its stated mathematical scope. Examples and counterexamples clarify normalization freedom, boundary exchange and the hypotheses needed for a limiting argument. The category supplies precise prerequisites for physical deductions while keeping mathematical proof separate from empirical interpretation and from additional constitutive or thermodynamic postulates.
