[[ex-tgr-chiral-generator-rescaling]] defines the length-flow change $u'=u/b$ for dimensionless $b>0$, the current-density pullback through compact-test pairings, and the resulting covariance law $C'=b^2C_\kappa(b\,\cdot)=C_{\kappa'}$ with $\kappa'=b\kappa$. Its spectral density is the angular Fourier transform with kernel $e^{-i\omega u}$, so its “generator frequency” is the model's Fourier-dual parameter in inverse metres, not an asserted operator eigenvalue. With $E$ in joules and the formal scale $\sigma$ in joule-metres, it sets $\omega=E/\sigma$ and $E'=bE$ to preserve the phase. For the strip widths $\beta=2\pi/\kappa$ and $\beta'=2\pi/\kappa'$, the example proves $S_{\kappa'}(\omega')=bS_\kappa(\omega)$ for $\omega'=b\omega$ and leaves $\beta'\omega'=\beta\omega$; the calculation assigns no physical clock normalization or temperature.
