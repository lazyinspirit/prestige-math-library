This page derives the energy and spatial projections of a perfect stress balance,
the corresponding particle and entropy identities, and the local
Carter–Lichnerowicz circulation relation. It then proves an exact entropy
production formula for a frame-decomposed dissipative stress and particle
current. The final lemma gives a nonnegative first-order entropy residual and
two conditional model limitations: instantaneous tails for the Fourier branch
and a growing homogeneous Eckart mode. Each conclusion is mathematical and
conditional on its stated smooth fields, constitutive equations, and supplier
interfaces; no nonlinear evolution or empirical applicability is inferred.
