This page constructs exact Gibbs states for finitely many independent cells assigned by a cell-location map to points of one stationary Lorentzian domain, conditional on the charged whole-future-mass-shell stationarity and global-compensator hypotheses supplied by TGR-ET02. It proves KMS inverse-temperature scaling along a metric-defined observer orbit with constant lapse, then derives finite-time response and spectral-frequency scaling for neutral controls on a specified finite cell. A one-mode occupation model gives the separate trace obstruction when the rotating generator loses strict positivity.

All conclusions are mathematical and conditional on the stated finite-dimensional, smooth-field, gauge-compensation, and supplier hypotheses. The page does not construct a generic curved-spacetime quantum field state, infer thermalization, or turn a finite response into a dc transport coefficient. The companion page supplies an exact two-level proper-clock calculation.
