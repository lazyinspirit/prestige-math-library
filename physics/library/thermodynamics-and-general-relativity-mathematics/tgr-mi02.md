This page proves that the smooth one-form identity $dE=C\kappa\,dA$, under the stated positivity and smoothness hypotheses, admits a family of temperature and entropy functions with the same product and arbitrary additive entropy offset. It is a mathematical underdetermination result; it does not identify these functions with operationally measured thermodynamic quantities or derive a quantum calibration.
