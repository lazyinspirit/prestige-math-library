This leaf gives a direct parcel calculation from the A-page state function. Adding a constant rest-energy term per marker leaves the temperature and pressure derivatives unchanged, shifts $H$ and $\mu$ by that constant and $\varepsilon$ and $h$ by number density times the constant, and gives $E_m=E_0+\mathsf Nmc^2$ for parcel totals. No material measurement is asserted.
