This page first constructs the global smooth evolution for constant matrix systems with a positive symmetrizer and nonnegative damping, including its weighted energy estimate and finite propagation bound. It then applies that theorem to a frozen adiabatic sound system and a corrected bulk-relaxation system. Every conclusion is conditional on the displayed mathematical hypotheses; the linear analysis does not assert a nonlinear fluid evolution theorem.
