This page develops conditional mathematical results for a charged massive
particle in a stationary Lorentzian domain. It fixes the Lorentz-force and
gauge-compensation conventions, proves conservation of the gauge-compensated
Killing energy, and derives the charged electrochemical redshift from a whole
future-mass-shell Liouville condition. A separate finite-dimensional theorem
proves redshifted temperature and chemical-potential relations from independent
entropy-exchange variations and concavity. The global charged conclusion
requires a globally defined potential symmetry compensator; it asserts no
existence of an equilibrium state or physical realization.
