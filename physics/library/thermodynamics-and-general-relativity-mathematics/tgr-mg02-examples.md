These examples and counterexamples use only the mathematical definitions and results on [[tgr-mg02]]. [[ex-tgr-regular-central-pressure-family]] instantiates local existence and continuous dependence with the smooth positive relation $\epsilon(p)=\epsilon_0+\lambda p$. [[ex-tgr-constant-density-positive-branch]] specializes the exact solution to $u=1/2$ and computes its positive center values.

[[cex-tgr-constant-density-critical-center]] shows that this branch has no finite-pressure, positive-lapse endpoint at $u=8/9$. [[cex-tgr-incompressible-causal-transport]] separates a static constant-density solution from a finite, single-valued barotropic transport law. The counterexample uses no fluid evolution equation and infers no observed signal speed.
