The example specializes the results on [[tgr-et01]] to the explicitly given spatially flat FLRW metric with $a(s)=s/L_0$. It computes the curvature scalar and uses its local isometry invariance to rule out timelike Killing fields on every nonempty open subset. It then excludes normalizable stationary massive Jüttner ansätze under the theorem's own hypotheses and verifies a separate future-null conformal-Killing exponential branch. The example distinguishes a mathematical null-shell construction from a claim about physical radiation or an observed equilibrium.
