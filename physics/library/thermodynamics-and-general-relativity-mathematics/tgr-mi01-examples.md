The example is a smooth, positive scalar density carried by a constant future
unit timelike vector in flat Minkowski coordinates. Its divergence is exactly
zero, while its slice charge decreases across a finite open box. The complete
outward side flux is positive and equals the lost charge. It includes the
slab duration $T$; the instantaneous flux rate does not. This is a mathematical
current example, not a solution of the Einstein–fluid equations or a physical
entropy postulate.
