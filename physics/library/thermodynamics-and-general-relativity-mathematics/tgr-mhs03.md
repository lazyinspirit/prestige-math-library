This page constructs the massless scalar shell restriction along a smooth
future timelike curve by proving rapid decay of its on-shell test amplitude.
It defines the curve-smeared finite-particle field, its Segal closure, and its
tested covariance without assuming a point field. For the proper-time
hyperbola it derives the damped covariance boundary directly. Its accelerated
spectrum and detailed balance are conditional on the actual batch-74 draft
single-pole contour item and its draft exponential-current prerequisite. Both
carriers are present in the batch-74 manifest, and their current run Step-1
records report `ready`; this is authoring-readiness evidence, not proof
acceptance. The local proof uses the contour item's length-coordinate interface
with $\alpha=a/c^2$ and $u=cs$; it does not treat either draft label as proof
acceptance. The second lemma proves a quantitative finite-switch coefficient
bound and solves a separately defined two-state Markov model. Neither result
is an all-order detector-probability theorem or an empirical observation.
