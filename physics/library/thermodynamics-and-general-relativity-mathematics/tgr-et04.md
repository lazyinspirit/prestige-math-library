This page first proves the spatial shear's symmetry, trace-free property and nonnegative norm, then derives a conditional entropy-production identity for smooth relativistic fluid fields with zero particle diffusion and specified nonnegative first-order heat, shear, and bulk coefficients. It also constructs one supported static-box heat solution for the special conductivity $\kappa_{\rm th}=A_0/N$ and insulated rectangular faces. The construction proves state positivity, energy conservation, entropy increase, uniqueness, and exponential $L^2$ relaxation to the Tolman-normalized profile $T=\overline\vartheta/N$. Its hypotheses include a fixed static metric, bounded positive lapse, an explicit material state function, and compatible even-reflected periodic initial data. Neither the entropy identity nor this example proves a causal, stable, general dissipative-fluid initial-value theorem or a full Einstein-fluid solution.
