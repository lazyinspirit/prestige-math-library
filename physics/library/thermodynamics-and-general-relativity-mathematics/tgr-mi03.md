This page derives the finite matter-current charge associated with a Killing symmetry, conditionally applies the batch-50 draft finite-slab balance theorem, and computes the restricted static expression $H_\xi=\int_\Omega \mathcal N_\xi\epsilon\sqrt{\det h}\,d^3x$. It keeps the units of mechanical, wave, and energy-valued covectors distinct. Equal endpoint charges require zero net outward side flux. The horizon rescaling statement is conditional on the explicitly supplied equation $\nabla_\xi\xi=\kappa_\xi\xi$; no global energy, equilibrium, or horizon theorem is inferred. The MI01 carriers are present on disk and in batch 50’s manifest, but remain draft; dependent results retain that proof-status qualification.
