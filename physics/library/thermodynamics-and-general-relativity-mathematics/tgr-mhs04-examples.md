The counterexample shows why the discrete zero-energy oscillator cannot be
retained in the occupation space merely because the zero wavevector has
Lebesgue measure zero in a continuum integral: its infinitely many
zero-energy occupations make the actual Gibbs multiplier noncompact and
non-trace-class. The half-mass example specializes the positive-cutoff
effective-area model to $M_{\min}=M_0/2$, giving an exact $7/8$ lifetime
fraction and a finite entropy budget. It stops at positive mass and makes no
claim about a zero-mass endpoint or a physical generalized second law.
