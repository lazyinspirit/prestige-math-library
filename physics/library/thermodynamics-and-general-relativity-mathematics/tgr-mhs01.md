This page defines the positive two-dimensional screen quotient of a smooth null hypersurface, its deformation form, and the strictly subextremal Kerr–Newman parameter functions. It proves the null focusing equation and an area monotonicity theorem only for a smooth future-affine generator flow whose compact spacelike cuts remain embedded for every future parameter and satisfy null convergence. It also proves the exact first-law differential and Smarr identities for the selected outer Kerr–Newman family, with the zero-rotation, zero-charge Schwarzschild slice checked separately. Its fixed-$(a,q)$ restriction retains the angular-momentum work term through the factor $r_+^2/D$; the work-free calibration instead restricts to connected subfamilies with fixed angular momentum $j$ and charge $q$. The family identities are mathematical deductions and do not assert a physical process, a thermodynamic temperature or entropy, a general event-horizon theorem, or extremal attainability.
