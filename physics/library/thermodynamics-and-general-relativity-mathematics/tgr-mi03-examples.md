The example chooses a finite cavity wall point as the reference for a future timelike Killing field. Rescaling the generator sets the reference lapse to one and divides the finite Killing charge, normalized temperature, and particle Killing energy by the same factor. The local proper-temperature and proper-energy ratios remain fixed by algebra; no asymptotic flatness or global equilibrium assumption is needed.
