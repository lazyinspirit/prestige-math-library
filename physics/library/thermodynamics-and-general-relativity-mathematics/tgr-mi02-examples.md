The first example instantiates two distinct positive calibrations on a smooth one-dimensional state family and checks that both reproduce the same energy differential. The counterexample uses two explicit density matrices with the same fixed positive real scalar label and zero energy but distinct finite spectral entropies; it makes no claim about a coupled gravitational model.
