This mathematical page fixes a selected future timelike Killing congruence and a positive proper-temperature field, with signature $(-,+,+,+)$ and explicit length-coordinate and SI unit conventions. The carrier definition supplies geometric data only: it does not assert a global flow, a KMS state, material thermalization, or an empirical thermometer law. The normalization lemma proves the connected-domain Tolman relation from the Killing equation. The kinematic equivalence gives all four tensor conditions needed for an inverse-temperature vector to be Killing; the acceleration/temperature-gradient relation alone is insufficient.

The local-flow lemma proves that a nowhere-zero Killing field generates local isometries and preserves the metric connection, curvature and its finite covariant derivatives. It assumes no complete flow or global isometry group. The massive-shell algebra lemma proves the exact rigidity statement for affine-quadratic polynomials on both massive and null cones; its null conclusion is the multiple-of-metric conformal exception.

The future-shell measure is defined in a local future tetrad and proved tetrad-independent by the change-of-variables lemma. The Jüttner partition theorem then derives the $K_1$ and $K_2$ integrals, the observer-rest probability law and the number-current and stress moments from the specified exponential density. Its constants include a declared reference action unit; this is not asserted to be Planck's constant or a measured parameter.

Finally, the massive stationarity theorem proves the Liouville condition and classifies shell normalizability: the normalizer is finite exactly for future timelike inverse temperature. These are conditional mathematical deductions about the stated ansatz, not evidence that any physical system realizes it. The only choice assumption inherited by the geometric and integration suppliers is countable choice, recorded explicitly; no unrestricted axiom of choice is used.
