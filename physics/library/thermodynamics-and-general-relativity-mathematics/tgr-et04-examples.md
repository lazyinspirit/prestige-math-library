The example substitutes one positive cosine mode into the supported static heat theorem. Direct differentiation verifies its decay rate, reflected Neumann faces, positive proper temperature, and exact exponential $L^2$ relaxation. The lapse enters through $T=\vartheta/N$; the proper-temperature limit varies spatially when the lapse is nonconstant, while the redshifted temperature $NT$ approaches a spatial constant.
