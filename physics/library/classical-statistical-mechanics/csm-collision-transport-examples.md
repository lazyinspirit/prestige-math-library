This companion gives a normalized pair of anisotropic Gaussian velocity laws with equal number density, mean velocity, and scalar internal energy but unequal covariance stress. It is a mathematical counterexample to scalar-moment closure, not an experimental observation or a hydrodynamic-limit claim.
