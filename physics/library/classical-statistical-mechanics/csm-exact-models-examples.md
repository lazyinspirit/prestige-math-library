These worked examples display the translation zero-mode divergence of an unpinned harmonic chain, evaluate a Maxwell speed law and a Poisson particle-count case including the vacuum sector, specialize the exact Ising-ring correlation length, check a hard-rod virial remainder at half packing, and compare finite shell support with canonical Gaussian tails. The shell marginal comparison is conditional on Countable Choice through its Lebesgue change-of-variables supplier. These are exact calculations under the named models, not measurements or evidence that any system realizes a preparation.
