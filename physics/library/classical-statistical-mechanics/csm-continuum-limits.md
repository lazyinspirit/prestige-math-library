This page derives the exact unit-Poisson sector density on bounded Borel regions, defines finite-range continuum Gibbs conditional kernels and the DLR fixed-kernel condition, then proves finite positive normalization, kernel measurability, nested-region consistency and the conditional-law interpretation under explicit stability and finite-range assumptions. It then records distinct, conditional thermodynamic and positional-limit results for stable power-tempered, stable regular and superstable regular models. Each conclusion retains its own tail, positive-core, boundary, density, differentiability and choice hypotheses. It makes no global continuum DLR existence or uniqueness claim and does not identify variational minimizers with DLR laws.
