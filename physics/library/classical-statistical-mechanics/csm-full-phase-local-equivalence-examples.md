These finite Bernoulli countermodels separate energy-density or relative-entropy
density agreement from convergence at a selected coordinate. They compute the
first-coordinate total-variation discrepancy and the conditional relative
entropy exactly, then verify concentration and entropy-density limits directly.
They are mathematical counterexamples, not experimental observations or claims
about a physical ensemble.
