These examples distinguish conditional Gibbs specifications from free-boundary marginals and work through the exactly solvable interacting Ising chain. A final regime comparison keeps the sufficient high-temperature uniqueness and low-temperature coexistence hypotheses separate and leaves their intervening parameters unclassified. The examples are exact calculations within stated classical models, not reported experiments or evidence about a particular material.
