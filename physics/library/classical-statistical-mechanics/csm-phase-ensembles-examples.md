These worked examples check finite classical ensemble formulas under the
preparations and reference conventions of [[csm-phase-ensembles]]. They keep
the finite indistinguishable-particle mixing correction, compare shell and
cumulative-volume entropy conventions without selecting a preferred one,
derive the exact ideal-gas canonical and grand laws, and compute finite
isothermal-isobaric volume fluctuations for the declared $dV/V_0$ prior.
They are conditional mathematical-model calculations, not measured
observations, and do not establish macroscopic ensemble equivalence.
