These examples separate interaction range from stability, and stability from the stronger cell-superstability bound. The final calculation shows why a spatially uniform one-body energy can change grand pressure at fixed activity: it is a volume contribution, not a tempered exterior boundary. The examples establish only their stated conditional model calculations; they do not report empirical measurements.
