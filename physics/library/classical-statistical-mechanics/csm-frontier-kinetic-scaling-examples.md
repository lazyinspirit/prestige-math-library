This companion works through the dimensional and conservation checks that distinguish the smooth periodic $N^{-1}$ mean-field family, periodic hard spheres under Boltzmann–Grad scaling, and the two-state diffusive model. The hard-sphere checks are conditional on an admissible piecewise trajectory with isolated binary contacts; they do not assert global hard-sphere flow existence. The comparison makes no cross-family implication claim among the three scaling prescriptions and contains no reported experiment or derivation of a nonlinear Boltzmann equation.
