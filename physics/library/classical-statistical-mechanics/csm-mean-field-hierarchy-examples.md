The two-particle example starts from uniform positions and independent
centered Gaussian momenta. Its one-particle marginal has zero initial time
derivative, while the joint density has a nonzero derivative on an open set.
It therefore exhibits finite-$N$ correlation production without claiming
that propagation of chaos fails in the large-$N$ regime.
