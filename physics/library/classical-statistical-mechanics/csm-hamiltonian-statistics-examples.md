The examples separate recurrence from ergodicity, weak mixing from strong
mixing, and fine reference-measure entropy from entropy of a chosen finite
partition. The Chacon example is a discrete probability-preserving system and
claims no Hamiltonian realization. The oscillator and rotor calculations are
explicit mathematical models; neither is presented as an observation.
