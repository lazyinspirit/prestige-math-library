This page defines bounded finite-range interactions on finite-alphabet lattices and distinguishes finite-volume free energies from conditional energies. It develops the finite Gibbs entropy identity, the product measure framework (with its exact Countable Choice assumption), Gibbs specifications and DLR states, and local proofs of existence, stationarity, uniqueness bounds and low-temperature coexistence for specified models. The pressure variational result is one-way: DLR states are variational equilibria, with no converse asserted. Continuum stability terminology is defined separately and does not imply that finite range alone gives stability. Physical interpretations are explicitly adopted model assumptions; all consequences remain conditional, with no empirical measurements or critical point claims.
