The S3 calculation lists every reduced expression and checks both the subword and saturated reflection-chain conventions directly. Its inversion sets give an explicit failure of containment to characterize strong Bruhat order.

For the A2 plane, symmetrizing a polynomial lift proves invariant generation. An explicit Jacobian proves independence, and monic division in the coinvariant quotient gives its six basis classes and Hilbert series.

For sl2, the Killing form and invariant ring are computed from matrices and polynomial derivations. The exact Laplacian formula gives a unique harmonic decomposition in each degree, including the constant and linear cases. Each example is proved locally: the A2 calculation consumes only the finite-reflection Chevalley--Shephard--Todd theorem, and none assumes the general Bruhat order or Kostant decomposition theorem.
