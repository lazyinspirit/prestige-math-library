The companion computes a one-bit phase query and a two-bit reversible dot product explicitly. It compares clean uncomputation with a circuit that leaves address-dependent garbage, then checks how a constant phase changes when its call is controlled. Each example includes the resulting basis-state calculation and its measurement consequence.
