The exact-grid example computes the complete output law for phase $5/8$ with eight phase labels. The phase $3/16$ example computes both tied nearest-label probabilities, lists the four circular-tail labels at threshold $1/4$, and gives their exact total as $\frac1{32}(\csc^2(5\pi/16)+\csc^2(7\pi/16))$, below the proved bound $1/2$.
