The OR_N calculation gives a balanced dual-vector certificate of cost √N and a matching adversary matrix. The four-bit sorted function then separates the nonnegative and signed optimizations: the nonnegative value is exactly 5/2, while an explicit rational signed matrix has ratio greater than 2.511486. Coordinate-distribution certificates give the nonnegative upper bound, and product distributions preserve that upper bound under iterated composition.
