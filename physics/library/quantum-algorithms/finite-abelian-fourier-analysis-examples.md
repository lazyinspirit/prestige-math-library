Character tables for $C_2$, $C_3$, $C_4$, $C_2\times C_2$, and $C_2\times C_3$ verify dual cardinalities, both finite orthogonality directions, inversion, Parseval, the unnormalized-convolution factor, and annihilators of explicit subgroups. The calculations are exact finite sums; the order-four cyclic and noncyclic groups are distinguished directly.
