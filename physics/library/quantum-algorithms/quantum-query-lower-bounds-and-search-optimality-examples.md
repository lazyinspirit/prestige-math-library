These worked items make two parts of the lower-bound discussion concrete. The four-address example constructs a legal second-query state by an explicit input-independent reflection and computes every query magnitude and hybrid-distance allowance. The one-phase-query counterexample computes trace distance for both a fixed marked address and a uniformly unknown mark. It is limited to the specified uniform initial state and one-query experiments; the A-page hybrid theorem supplies the general lower bound.
