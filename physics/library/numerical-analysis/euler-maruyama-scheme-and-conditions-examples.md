## Objective

The first example computes the grid recursion and continuous interpolation
for a scalar additive-noise equation with an exact path and finite moments.
The second checks a superlinear drift against the global Lipschitz and
linear-growth hypotheses, showing precisely why the uniform-moment theorem
does not apply.
