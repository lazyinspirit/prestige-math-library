These examples work the rim-hook machinery of
[[rim-hooks-and-the-murnaghan-nakayama-rule]] on small symmetric groups and on
one extended shape, and exhibit the boundary between removable border strips
and removable unions of skew hooks.

[[ex-s5-character-table-by-fixed-tabloid-reconstruction]] runs the fixed-tabloid
reconstruction for $S_5$, solving the Gram equation for the triangular matrix
$B$ and recovering the full character table with $\det C=2880$.
[[ex-the-s4-character-table-by-rim-hook-recursion]] computes the $S_4$ table
directly from the recursion and checks the orthogonality relations, and
[[ex-murnaghan-nakayama-for-shape-five-four-four]] evaluates the character of
$(5,4,4)$ on $(5,4,3,1)$ and $(7,3,3)$ and records the forced zeros.

[[ex-r-cycle-filter-for-nu-three-two-and-r-three]] lists the four ways to add a
$3$-border strip to $(3,2)$ to obtain a partition of $8$, forms the filtered virtual character and checks
its vanishing on classes without a $3$-cycle, while
[[cex-a-disconnected-rim-set-is-not-a-border-strip]] shows that a removable
union of two disjoint skew hooks need not be a border strip and cancels only in
the alternating sum.
