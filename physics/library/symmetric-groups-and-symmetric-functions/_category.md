Representations of symmetric groups connect finite-group symmetry with partitions, tableaux and symmetric functions. This collection begins with Young diagrams, permutation modules and Specht modules, then develops the branching rule, the Young graph, hook lengths and the Robinson–Schensted correspondence. Symmetric functions and the Frobenius characteristic translate characters into Schur bases. Outer products and skew Specht modules lead to Littlewood–Richardson coefficients, while rim hooks and the Murnaghan–Nakayama rule compute character values.

Jucys–Murphy elements and integral Specht lattices prepare the modular branch. Cores, quotients and blocks organize characteristic-dependent behavior; the good-node crystal describes modular branching. Internal tensor products lead to Kronecker coefficients, FI-modules and Murnaghan stability. Plancherel measure supplies the asymptotic branch through random Young diagrams. Quantum Groups and Crystal Bases provides the crystal and Fock-space machinery, and Hecke Algebras and Categorification develops the deformed and categorical applications. General finite-group methods remain in Representation Theory of Groups. Each topic has an examples companion, and page and item statuses identify the available content.
