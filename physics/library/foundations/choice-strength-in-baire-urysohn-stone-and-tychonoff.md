This page compares locally proved forms of the Baire category theorem,
Urysohn separation, metric paracompactness and compact-product principles.
Separable complete metric spaces are Baire in ZF. The unrestricted
complete-metric form is equivalent to dependent choice, and the compact
Hausdorff form is equivalent to dependent multiple choice. Products of compact
Hausdorff spaces are Baire exactly under dependent choice.

Dependent multiple choice implies Urysohn separation. A given failure of
Urysohn separation yields a failure of bounded Tietze extension and of dependent
multiple choice. The nonprovability corollaries explicitly assume consistency
of an extending theory containing a counterexample; no countable-choice or
Boolean-prime-ideal countermodel is constructed here. The Brunner and Corson
local constructions in ZFA remain available with their stated ambient theory.

Stone's theorem follows from AC; its effective per-cover strengthening implies
AC. The separately constructed dependent-choice countermodel retains its own
contract. Products of cofinite spaces are compact exactly under BPI, while the
compact T1 and unrestricted compact-product principles are equivalent to AC.
The final ledger distinguishes these positive implications and exact ambient
theories from conditional metatheorems. Questions about converses are presented
as questions without a claim about current research status.
