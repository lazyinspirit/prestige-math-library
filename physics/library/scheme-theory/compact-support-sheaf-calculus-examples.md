These examples pin down the definitions of the companion page
[[compact-support-sheaf-calculus]] on the smallest nondegenerate space, a
finite set with the discrete topology. On it a sheaf of vector spaces is just a
family of stalks, every sheaf is c-soft by a literal extension by zero, the
compactly supported global sections are the direct sum of the stalks while
global sections are their product, and all higher compactly supported
cohomology vanishes, so that the derived object is concentrated in degree
zero. The same computation verifies the discrete case of the exceptional
inverse image along the terminal map: the constant-sheaf functor is right
adjoint to $\Gamma_c$, with the Hom bijection read off from the universal
property of the direct sum. A closing remark explains why the finiteness of
the space is used twice and why the acyclicity here also admits a choice-free
proof, the statement assuming the Axiom of Choice only to match the general
acyclicity lemma it cites.
