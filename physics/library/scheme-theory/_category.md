name: scheme-theory
title: Scheme Theory
---

These pages begin with affine spectra and structure sheaves, then treat general morphisms and relative geometry. They cover locally finite type maps, fibre products and base change, flat, smooth and étale morphisms, and separated, proper, finite and projective behavior. Sheaf topics include presheaves and sheafification, quasi-coherent and coherent modules, cohomology, exact operations and comparison results. Further pages develop divisors and line bundles, Proj and ampleness, blowups, Hilbert schemes, deformation theory, group schemes and algebraic group actions. The collection also reaches algebraic spaces, stacks, derived geometry, compact-support sheaf calculus, six operations and duality, while keeping curve results such as residue pairings and Riemann–Roch alongside general constructions.

Commutative Algebra and Abstract Algebra supply ring and module foundations; Homological Algebra and Category Theory support cohomology and derived constructions, while Topology and Algebraic Geometry provide geometric and sheaf-theoretic tools. These foundations support pages in Algebraic Geometry, Complex Analysis, Lie Theory, and Hall Algebras and Tensor Categories, where morphisms, group schemes and sheaves matter.
