The companion page fixes the standard witnesses that the main page's theorem
statements point at: counting measure turns the integral into a series, the
Dirichlet function has zero integral without vanishing everywhere, and the
canonical spike and travelling-mass sequences show exactly where Fatou,
monotone convergence, and dominated convergence can fail when their hypotheses
are weakened.
