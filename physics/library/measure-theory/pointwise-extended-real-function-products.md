This page defines a total pointwise product of functions from an arbitrary set
into the extended real line [[def-extended-reals]]. Its zero rule includes all
four ordered zero-infinity pairs. On finite inputs it is ordinary real
multiplication, and on nonzero inputs with an infinite factor it follows the
signs. The function-level operation leaves the existing partial scalar product
unchanged and makes no measurability claim. Pointwise addition remains partial
at opposite infinities, and no other extended-real scalar operation is added.
