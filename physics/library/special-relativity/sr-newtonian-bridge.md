The controlled low-speed bridge of the special-relativity framework. A named
inertial frame supplies comparison functionals directly from its spatial
Euclidean structure; a uniform second-order Taylor remainder for
$(1-z)^{-1/2}$, with an explicit constant $K(z_0)$ depending only on the speed
bound, is transported through the model's observer identities to the measured
energy, momentum and kinetic energy of a massive particle state. A separate
family of affine Galilean limiting chart maps, together with bounded chart
regions, has an explicit $O(C^{-2})$ limit on each bounded region and a proved
failure on the whole chart domain for nonzero boost velocity; at zero velocity
both maps are the identity everywhere. The correspondence theorem then states the exact
remainders and their uniform bounds in the bounded domain $|w|^2\le z_0c^2$; a
scope remark records that no speed-independent constant exists, that the ideal
massless case is excluded rather than obtained as a limit, and that the
comparison is conditional and not a framework identity. No measurement or
empirical precision is asserted anywhere on this page.
