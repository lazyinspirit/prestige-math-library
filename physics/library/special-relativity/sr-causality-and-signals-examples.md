These examples and counterexamples work the causality page on explicit
integers and rationals. The boost family on the spacelike pair $(11,13,0,0)$
reaches simultaneity at $b=11/13$ and produces both time orders at $b=3/5$ and
$b=12/13$, with all three charts verified inertial
([[ex-sr-boost-makes-spacelike-events-simultaneous]]); the four displacements
$(5,3,0,0)$, $(5,5,0,0)$, $(5,7,0,0)$ and $(5,-3,0,0)$ are classified by the
strict, equality and reversed inequalities of the coordinate criterion
([[ex-sr-causal-classification-of-explicit-displacements]]). The causal
diamond $J^+(p)\cap J^-(q)$ for $p=x^{-1}(0,0,0,0)$, $q=x^{-1}(2,0,0,0)$ is
computed with its interior; its $1+1$ section $x^2=x^3=0$ has coordinate
area $2$ m$^2$, invariant under boosts in the $(0,1)$ plane ([[ex-sr-causal-diamond-in-one-plus-one]]), and the parallel future
null displacements $(1,1,0,0)$, $(2,2,0,0)$ compose to the future null
$(3,3,0,0)$, with the full characterization that a sum of future causal
vectors is null exactly for parallel future null summands
([[ex-sr-parallel-null-displacements-compose-to-null]]). Two further examples
isolate the non-causal side: a prearranged sweep of coordinate speed $w>c$
whose successive events are spacelike separated and can be made simultaneous
([[ex-sr-superluminal-sweep-has-no-causal-connection]]), and the exact relay
arithmetic at $u=2c$, $v=(9/10)c$ arriving at $t=-50/(121c)$ s
([[ex-sr-antitelephone-relay-arrival-time]]).

The counterexamples refute two specific displayed claims and nothing wider: a
causally related pair need not be joinable by a future timelike worldline, as
the null-separated pair $(0,0,0,0)$, $(3,3,0,0)$ shows
([[cex-sr-null-separated-events-are-not-timelike-joinable]]); and the sign of
a time-coordinate difference is not frame independent, as two explicit boost
charts give opposite signs for the same spacelike-separated pair
([[cex-sr-spacelike-time-order-is-not-absolute]]). The final example shows
that two nonparallel future null legs compose to a future timelike
displacement while every tangent stays null, so the strict clause of the
causality theorem is not an equivalence
([[ex-sr-null-legs-compose-to-a-timelike-displacement]]). Every computation is
exact and no item asserts a measurement or an empirical conclusion.
