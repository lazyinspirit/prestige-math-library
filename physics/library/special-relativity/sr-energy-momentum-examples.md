These worked computations accompany [[sr-energy-momentum]].
[[ex-sr-four-momentum-at-beta-three-fifths]] evaluates the four-momentum at
coordinate speed $3c/5$, obtaining $\gamma_w=5/4$, components
$(5mc/4,3mc/4,0,0)$, kinetic energy $mc^2/4$ and the mass shell
$E^2-c^2|\mathbf p|^2=m^2c^4$, and compares the exact kinetic energy with the
Newtonian expression $9mc^2/50$. [[ex-sr-four-momentum-boost-to-rest]] applies
the passive boost with parameter $3/5$ along $e_1$ to the same particle,
finding $P'=(mc,0,0,0)$ and exhibiting the frame in which the particle is at
rest while the observer energy changes and the invariant mass does not.

[[ex-sr-equal-mass-head-on-collision-mass]] adds the four-momenta of two
equal masses in head-on motion, finding total $(5mc/2,0,0,0)$, invariant mass
$5m/2>2m$ and a mass excess equal to the total kinetic energy divided by $c^2$; the strictness
is checked against the equality case of the collision necessary conditions.
[[ex-sr-perfectly-inelastic-collision-rest-mass]] exhibits a conserving
two-into-one datum with the same total and shows that the same incoming side
also admits an outgoing side of two particles at rest with masses $m$ and
$3m/2$, so conservation fixes only the total.
[[ex-sr-null-pair-invariant-mass]] computes the collective mass
$2\sqrt{E_1E_2}/c^2$ of two oppositely directed massless momenta, the equal
special case $2E/c^2$, and the parallel case in which the total is future null
with $M=0$ and no center-of-momentum frame.
[[ex-sr-stationary-target-threshold]] substitutes into the threshold
inequality for a projectile on a co-moving target, obtaining
$E\ge mc^2+2\mu c^2+\mu^2c^2/(2m)$ and, at $\mu=m$, $E\ge7mc^2/2$.

The two counterexamples delimit the definitions:
[[cex-sr-massless-momentum-not-mu-at-zero-mass]] shows that setting $m=0$ in
$P=mU$ yields the zero vector, not a nonzero future null four-momentum, so the
this substitution cannot produce a nonzero future null momentum; and
[[cex-sr-kinetic-energy-is-observer-dependent]] exhibits one massive particle
with kinetic energy $mc^2/4$ for one future unit observer and $0$ for another,
while the invariant mass stays $m$. All items are exact computations or
refutations in the model; no measurement is asserted.
