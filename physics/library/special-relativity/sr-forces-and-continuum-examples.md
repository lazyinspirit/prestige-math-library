Worked computations for the forces and continuum page. The examples compute the boosted components of a dust tensor and the exact factorization of its boosted energy coefficient, the static uniaxial stress balance in which the stress but not the energy density is constrained, the finite-box flux identity checked on an explicit field with opposite-face cancellations and explicit transverse-area factors, a family of exact constant-velocity dust solutions with transverse density profile and finite-box constancy of the slice integrals, the null plane-wave scalar field with its conserved Hilbert tensor and the zero/nonzero branch of the profile derivative, and the perfect-fluid components in the rest and boosted frames with the exact energy-density shift. The counterexamples show that a prescribed covariant symmetric tensor of the form $F\eta^{-1}$ need not be conserved, and that zero local divergence alone does not conserve a finite slice integral when the side flux does not vanish. Every item is a mathematical computation or witness; none asserts a measurement, a material model or empirical evidence.
