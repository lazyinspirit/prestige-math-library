This page supplies the flat-spacetime force and continuum layer of the framework. It defines the symmetric stress-energy field class, its inertial component and scaling conventions, its four-force density $f^b=\partial_aT^{ab}$ and the exact conservation terminology, and it proves the pointwise observer decomposition of a symmetric field into energy density, momentum density and rest stress together with its uniqueness. The compact-support machinery (the fundamental lemma, integration by parts and differentiation under the integral sign) supplies the first variation of the adopted scalar-field action, its Euler–Lagrange equation and the Hilbert stress-energy tensor defined by compactly supported metric variations, including the full off-shell divergence identity. Dust and perfect-fluid models are analysed through exact conservation equivalences, and the finite-box balance theorem gives the flux form of conservation by the fundamental theorem of calculus, with explicit side-flux terms and the vanishing-side-flux corollary. The Hodge decomposition of an alternating two-form for an observer, its quadratic invariants and the optional electromagnetic identification are also supplied here for later use. On the physical side, the page adopts the scalar-field, dust and perfect-fluid models with their SI units and observer readings, the total-tensor isolation premise, and then proves conditional local conservation for the three models and integrated conservation of the slice four-momentum of an isolated system on finite boxes. Nothing here asserts gravity, electromagnetic coupling, a force law, an equation of state, energy conditions, an angular-momentum theorem, a four-vector transformation of slice integrals or any empirical fact.
