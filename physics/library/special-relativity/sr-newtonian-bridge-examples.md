Worked instances of the Newtonian bridge. The first evaluates the low-speed
correspondence at the single state $|w|=c/5$, with exact radical values and
ten-place rounded decimals checking every displayed bound of the uniform
expansion and of the inversion estimate. The second works the Galilean chart
bounds at $C=10$, $C=30$ and $C=100$ on the bounded region $D_{1,2}$, including
the explicit tolerance threshold of the limit lemma. The third is a
counterexample: the family of states with Lorentz factor $\gamma$ refutes any
speed-independent uniform bound on the kinetic-energy remainder, so the
constants of the correspondence necessarily depend on the admissible speed
domain. All decimals are rounded values of exact expressions and no empirical
claim is made.
