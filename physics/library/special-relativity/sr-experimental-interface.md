This page fixes the vocabulary in which the framework's empirical layer is stated
and carries it into one reported experiment. It defines run descriptions,
operational models with their interpretation obligation, declared data records
with closed-box bounds, calibration declarations, and the vocabulary of declared
uncertainty and total declared bounds, all as mathematical structures of the
framework rather than claims about apparatus; a comparison claim is kept separate
and requires its own declared rule. The research queue records the report
families whose primary sources remain to be retrieved and read, together with the
qualifications any future experiment record must carry. The stored-lithium-ion
Doppler report is recorded with its apparatus, procedure, frequency estimates and
reported uncertainty scales, and the comparison theorem supplies conditional
hard-bound and reported-scale analyses of explicitly specified prediction
hypotheses. An alternative hypothesis is compared as an explicitly specified
competitor; no statistical significance, coverage, or empirical proof of the
framework is asserted, and every conclusion inherits the source's conditions,
calibration dependence and uncertainty limitations.

The companion examples page [[sr-experimental-interface-examples]] instantiates
the vocabulary on the framework's own longitudinal Doppler prediction with
declared illustrative values, and exhibits two distinct parameters consistent
with one bounded record.
