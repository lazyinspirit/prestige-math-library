These examples work the vocabulary of [[sr-experimental-interface]] on the
framework's longitudinal Doppler prediction. The first instantiates an
operational model, computes the inverse of the Doppler ratio, and solves a
declared bounded record for its consistency interval, including the
zero-uncertainty singleton and the exact positive length of the interval when the
declared uncertainty is positive; every number is a declared illustrative value.
The second refutes, under an explicitly declared comparison rule, the uniqueness
claim that a bounded one-point record determines the parameter of an operational
model: two exact rational parameter witnesses, and in fact a whole
positive-length interval of them, are consistent with one illustrative record.
Neither item asserts a measurement, an apparatus, a sampling model, or any
empirical conclusion about a model or postulate.
