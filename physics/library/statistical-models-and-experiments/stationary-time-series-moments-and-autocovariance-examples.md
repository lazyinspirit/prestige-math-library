The finite examples separate pairwise uncorrelated data from mutual
independence and compare the two sample-ACVF denominator conventions. A
three-observation vector gives an exact negative Toeplitz quadratic form for
the pair-count-normalized version. No asymptotic or central-limit claim is
attached to either calculation.
