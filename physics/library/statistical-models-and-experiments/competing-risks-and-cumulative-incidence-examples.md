The finite counterexample exhibits distinct latent joint failure-time laws
with the same observed first-time/cause law. The two-group mortality example
computes all-cause risk-set updates and cause-specific product limits exactly;
it shows how higher competing mortality lowers observed cancer incidence while
the competing-cause-as-censoring complement remains a different quantity.
