## Example scope

The Bernoulli example uses success probabilities $1/4$ under $H_0$ and $3/4$ under $H_1$, with both thresholds equal to $1/4$. It computes the exact terminal error probabilities, each $1/10$, and verifies almost-sure stopping from the log-likelihood drift conditions and directly from the independent two-observation blocks.
