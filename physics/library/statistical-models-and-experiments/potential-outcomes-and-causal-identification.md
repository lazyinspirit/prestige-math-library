This page fixes a target population and treatment versions before defining potential outcomes, consistency, the ATE/CATE, conditional exchangeability, and positivity. For countably discrete treatments and standard-Borel covariates, it proves the standardization identity from the full-data assumptions and identifies the almost-everywhere RCD version used for general covariates. Identification means that all compatible full-data laws give the same target value. Exchangeability remains a substantive, generally untestable assumption; positivity failure leaves target strata unsupported. The theorem does not assert pointwise identification at null values of a continuous treatment.
