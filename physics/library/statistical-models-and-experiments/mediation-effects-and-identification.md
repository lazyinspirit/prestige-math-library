This page defines controlled and natural direct and indirect effects before
stating their identification assumptions. The mediation formula is proved for
a simple Markovian structural model with independent exogenous variables,
treatment and mediator support, and no post-treatment mediator–outcome
confounder. Its cross-world independence is an additional structural
assumption; it does not follow from single-world conditional independences
alone. Regular conditional kernels make the formula precise for a standard-
Borel mediator and baseline covariates. Separable interventionist effects
are defined separately because their component interventions target different
quantities. The proof establishes a population identification functional; it
supplies no regression-coefficient shortcut or empirical validation of model
adequacy.
