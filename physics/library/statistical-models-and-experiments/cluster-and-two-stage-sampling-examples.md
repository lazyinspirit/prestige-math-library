The worked village-and-field example enumerates every outcome of a small
two-stage SRSWOR design and computes its exact expansion estimate and design
variance directly. The counterexample uses unequal PSU sizes and an explicit
common within-PSU correlation model to show why the equal-size design-effect
shortcut cannot use the arithmetic mean cluster size as a universal
substitute.
