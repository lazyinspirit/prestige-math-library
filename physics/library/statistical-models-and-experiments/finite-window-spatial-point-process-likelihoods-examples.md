On a fixed square window, the examples compute the homogeneous Poisson rate
estimate at positive and zero counts, then compare the exact and pseudo
likelihood criteria for the Poisson and soft Strauss models. In the Strauss
case the Poisson reference, empty exterior boundary, and interaction range are
fixed explicitly; the pseudo criterion differs from the joint likelihood for
nontrivial interaction.
