## Overview

The examples verify the homogeneous Poisson likelihood and its maximum
likelihood estimate, including the zero-count boundary; work out the
finite-window likelihood for a Hawkes conditional-intensity specification
without asserting existence or stability; and construct a finite-window
Poisson process whose intensity stops at time one, leaving positive mass on
no further event.
