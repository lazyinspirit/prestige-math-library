This companion verifies an explicit underdamped periodic Cattaneo mode by direct substitution. Its temperature-departure envelope decays and a sufficiently small nonzero amplitude keeps the forward-time model state inside the selected near-equilibrium domain with positive temperature. The construction is a model calculation, not an experiment or a claim about measured transport coefficients.
