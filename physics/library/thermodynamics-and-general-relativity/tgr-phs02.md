This page adopts a one-dimensional exponential-coordinate chiral-current model and a separate massless Minkowski-vacuum acceleration model with explicit two-state rate rules. The reduced reference temperature follows conditionally from the contour spectrum and the adopted rate equation; the local proper-temperature comparison requires a separately selected stationary bath and a matched reference. The acceleration theorem uses the constructed temporal field smearing, states the finite-switch coefficient bound, and keeps its effective temperature distinct from an exact all-order detector probability or an observed reading.
