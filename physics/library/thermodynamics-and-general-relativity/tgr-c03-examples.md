The example verifies explicit perfect-branch sound waves and spatially constant neutral modes. It shows by direct substitution why energy contraction does not imply that every perturbation decays. The comparison is conditional on the perfect-branch theorem and its stated $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$ hypothesis; it contains no empirical data.
