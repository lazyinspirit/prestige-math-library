This companion computes the entropy difference between two positive-mass
Schwarzschild members of the selected model family. The calculation is
conditional on the area-calibration theorem with its adopted reversible-heat
and pointwise reduced-temperature assumptions, and cancels its undetermined
additive entropy constant; it is neither a measurement nor a microscopic
state-count argument.
