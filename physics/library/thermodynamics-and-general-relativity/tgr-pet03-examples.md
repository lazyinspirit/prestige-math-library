The cosine example gives a closed-form decaying mode in the supported parabolic heat model. Its redshifted temperature tends to a constant, while its proper-temperature limit varies with a nonconstant lapse. The formula and insulated-face conditions are verified directly; this constructed solution is not an experimental observation.
