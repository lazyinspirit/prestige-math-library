This page adopts one fixed-background, supported static heat branch with a positive lapse, constant heat capacity, conductivity proportional to the inverse lapse, insulated rectangular faces, and compatible positive data. It specifies the state function, heat flux, energy balance, no-local-power support, and entropy current. A conditional physics theorem consumes the exact mathematical static-heat solution and derives energy conservation, entropy increase, and relaxation of redshifted temperature to a constant. These statements remain conditional on the model assumptions; they do not establish empirical realization, a full Einstein-fluid solution, causal relativistic-fluid dynamics, or finite propagation speed.
