This page develops a static, isotropic, spherically symmetric $\Lambda=0$ Einstein-fluid model with a supplied smooth positive equation of state. The metric, areal radius, stress tensor, signature, curvature convention and SI quantities are stated explicitly. The local TOV result uses the current mathematical center-construction and Einstein-reduction suppliers conditionally; it claims no global stellar surface or generic stability.

The constant-density branch is a separate constitutive and boundary preparation. Its explicit regular solution and vacuum match hold only for $0<u<8/9$; this does not assert a general Buchdahl theorem or a causal finite-sound-speed EOS. The entropy item uses a separately supplied compatible local state chart and fixed mass-energy/particle constraints. It proves first-variation criticality only, not an entropy maximum or existence theorem. The finite-bath result is another selected effective model: a negative-capacity finite subsystem coupled to a logarithmic bath by a scalar exchange ODE. None of these model deductions is an observation or a claim that nature realizes the preparation.
