The examples specialize the A-page results to half compactness and to a half-capacity finite bath, and exhibit the divergence of central pressure as the constant-density compactness approaches its family endpoint. The star calculations remain within the specified constant-density model; the bath calculation remains within the adopted scalar exchange law. They report no measurement, generic compactness bound, causal fluid dynamics, or stability property of a relativistic star.
