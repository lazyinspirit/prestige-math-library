This page adopts two adiabatic first-variation systems about a uniform rest state on prescribed Minkowski spacetime: a perfect-material branch and a bulk-relaxation branch. It proves global linear stability and finite propagation by applying the exact current mathematical suppliers conditionally, including their stated $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$ assumption. The causal parameter ranges, exact supplier hypotheses, SI conventions, and lack of a nonlinear error or existence claim are stated explicitly. No empirical observations are used.
