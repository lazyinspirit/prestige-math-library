The companion considers one ideal finite-region case: when the entropy-current flux vanishes on every side face, the conditional balance reduces to nondecreasing proper slice entropy, with equality when local production also vanishes. It is a thought experiment within the stated model, not an observation, horizon-area law, or global generalized second law.
