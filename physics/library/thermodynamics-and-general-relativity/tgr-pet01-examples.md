These examples compute the exact lapse, local temperature, and observer acceleration in the Schwarzschild exterior, the Rindler right wedge, and a rigidly rotating Minkowski domain. Each uses the selected Killing flow and stays inside its timelike domain. The rotating quantum trace check is restricted to the cited one-mode model; it is not a continuum-field theorem. No horizon, Hawking, Unruh, or reported experimental temperature is inferred.
