This page develops three explicitly different finite model preparations: a softly regulated finite Newtonian canonical gas, a confined relative Kepler model under canonical and Gibbs-volume microcanonical conventions, and a finite ideal gas on a prescribed static relativistic background. The model assumptions are kept separate from the mathematical normalization and response results. Every response claim retains its preparation, entropy convention, regulator, and physical applicability limits; no result here asserts thermalization, an observed gravitational system, or generic self-consistent gravitational stability.
