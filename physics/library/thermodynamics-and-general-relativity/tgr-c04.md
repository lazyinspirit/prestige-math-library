This page adopts two distinct effective branches. The supported-rest Cattaneo subsystem uses constant positive heat capacity, conductivity, and relaxation time, together with a specified quadratic entropy; its entropy production, characteristic speed, and finite propagation are conditional model deductions, and relativistic causality requires the derived speed bound. The separate entropy-only radiation model adopts its own equation of state and perfect-stress balance; it yields local entropy-current conservation and a frozen Minkowski-rest sound speed of $c/\sqrt3$. Neither branch asserts a measured material realization, a universal constitutive law, or a microscopic quantum derivation.
