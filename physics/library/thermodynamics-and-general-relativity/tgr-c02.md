This page adopts two restricted constitutive branches: first-order gradient dissipation and a bulk-only relaxation model with a corrected extended entropy current. The first theorem derives the quadratic local production identity for sufficiently regular solutions of the first-order branch. The bulk theorem consumes the exact conditional identity of [[lem-tgr-bulk-relaxation-entropy]] and verifies one homogeneous solution within a declared near-equilibrium regime. Both arguments retain their mathematical supplier assumptions; neither proves general solution existence, causal or stable evolution, or universal material behavior. No experiment is reported.
