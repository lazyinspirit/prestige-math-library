This page adopts a restricted reversible-heat identification for the selected
strictly subextremal stationary family. At each family point, its reduced
exponential-current branch has a constant parameter equal to that point's
positive geometric surface-gravity coefficient; this gives a smooth
reference-temperature field for the family. The conditional theorem combines
that field with the exact SI family differential. The result fixes the area
coefficient within the adopted model while leaving the entropy zero free; it
asserts no general Hawking law or microscopic state count.
