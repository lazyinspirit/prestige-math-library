This page adopts one specified finite independent-cell Gibbs/KMS state in the selected stationary bath, derives the local proper-time KMS width orbit by orbit, and applies the exact finite-cell response theorem to neutral observables and finite controls. The state is an actual finite construction within the model assumptions; it does not assert a generic curved-spacetime Gibbs trace, equilibration dynamics, or an experimental realization. The response predictions keep the finite-time remainder, spectral lines, and zero-frequency qualification.
