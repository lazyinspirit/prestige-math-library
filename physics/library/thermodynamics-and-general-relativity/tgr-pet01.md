This page adopts one selected comoving stationary bath, derives its conditional Tolman–Ehrenfest temperature relation, and records two separate particle-exchange protocols before deriving the conditional Tolman–Klein relation. It also defines one finite, normalized classical Jüttner preparation from the exact mass-shell measure and partition supplier. The conclusions carry their stated connected-domain, positive-temperature, full-mass-shell or finite-cell hypotheses. They do not assert universal equilibrium, a generic curved-spacetime Gibbs/KMS state, collisional equilibration, or empirical material validity.
