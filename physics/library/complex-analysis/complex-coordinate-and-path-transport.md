This page makes the identification $\mathbb C\cong\mathbb R^2$ precise for
real-linear maps, metric topology, and total derivatives. It defines polygonal
length directly for complex-valued paths, then transfers length, bounded
variation, piecewise-$C^1$ speed, and monotone reparametrization through the
coordinate isometry. The proofs retain arbitrary real affine parameters,
singleton intervals, corners, zero-speed pieces, pauses, and reversal.

The prerequisite interfaces are the complex metric and real coordinate model,
the Euclidean total derivative, the Euclidean path-length results, connectedness,
and arc length. The A-page items distinguish facts inherited from these
published suppliers from the type-specific transport arguments proved here.
