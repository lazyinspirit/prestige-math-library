The worked example computes an explicit real-coordinate map and affine segment,
checks a real total derivative, and follows a finite complex path through its
coordinate length, a pause, a reversal, and the singleton-interval convention.
It also verifies a continuous bounded complex path whose polygonal sums are
unbounded, so bounded trace does not suffice for rectifiability.
