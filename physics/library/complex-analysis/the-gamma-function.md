The page keeps the complex theory only, exactly as the seam amendment requires.
It starts from Euler's integral on the right half-plane, proves holomorphy
there, bridges that definition back to the earlier real Gamma function, and
then extends meromorphically to the whole plane by the functional equation.

The second half packages the classical function theory: Euler's limit formula,
the reciprocal-Gamma Weierstrass product, zero-freeness, reflection, the
Beta-Gamma identity, multiplication and duplication, Stirling asymptotics on
closed sectors away from the negative axis, and the Hankel contour formula for
$1/\Gamma$.
