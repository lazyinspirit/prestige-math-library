This page closes the first several-variable account of natural domains of
holomorphic existence. It starts from holomorphic hulls and the Cartan-Thullen
theorem, which identifies domains of holomorphy with holomorphic convexity and
puts convex domains on the safe side of the several-variable extension
phenomena.

The second half builds the plurisubharmonic language that geometric
pseudoconvexity requires. After the line-test definition, the Levi-form
criterion, and the basic closure properties, the page records Hartogs
pseudoconvexity as a source of continuous plurisubharmonic exhaustions, turns
the continuity principle into Hartogs pseudoconvexity, and proves the
smooth-boundary implication from Hartogs to Levi pseudoconvexity. The
deliberately omitted direction is the full Levi problem
`pseudoconvex => domain of holomorphy`, which belongs to the later
$\bar\partial$ page rather than this one.
