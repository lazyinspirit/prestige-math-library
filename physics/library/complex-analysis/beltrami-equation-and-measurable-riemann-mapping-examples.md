These examples work out the constant-coefficient affine model, approximate measurable coefficients by piecewise-affine data, and compute the Möbius normalization of a solution. The pullback example tracks the coefficient phase, ellipse direction, sphere-chart transition, weak solutions, and invariant dilatation under conformal coordinates. The counterexample with the identity and inversion shows why a Beltrami equation alone does not determine a unique sphere map without normalization.
