These examples make the Picard package concrete. The first records the explicit
numerical bound produced by the elementary Bloch proof actually written on this
page, the next specializes Schottky's theorem at a fixed center value, and the
exponential examples show the sharpness of both Picard theorems.

The counterexample and false statements keep the holomorphic and meromorphic
versions distinct: on the plane, a nonconstant meromorphic function may omit
two sphere values, and Little Picard does not need any extra boundedness
hypothesis.
