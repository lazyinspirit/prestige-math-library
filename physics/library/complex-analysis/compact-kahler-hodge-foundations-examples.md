The example records the dimension-zero instance of the Hodge decomposition. On
a single point the complex of complex-valued forms is $\mathbb C$ in degree
zero with zero differential, so the cohomology is purely of type $(0,0)$ and
vanishes in positive degrees, and the rational comparison identifies
$H^0(\mathrm{pt};\mathbb Q)$ with $\mathbb Q$. The pairing $Q(x,y)=xy$ has
Weil operator the identity and positive-definite Hermitian form
$h(x,y)=x\bar y$, so it is a rational polarization of the weight-zero
structure, and the degree-zero hard-Lefschetz map $L^0$ is the identity.
