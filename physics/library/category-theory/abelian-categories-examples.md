These examples keep the page-level abstractions concrete. The positive examples
show how kernels, cokernels, quotients, pullbacks, and exact functors reduce to
the familiar algebra of groups and modules. The counterexamples isolate the two
main failure modes the A page warns about: additive structure with no AB2, and
topological or filtered settings where the underlying algebra looks exact but
the categorical isomorphism fails.
