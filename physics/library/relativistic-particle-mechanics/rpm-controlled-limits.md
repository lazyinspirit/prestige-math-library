## Scope

This page proves a finite-time ODE comparison bound under common Lipschitz and compact-containment hypotheses, uniform pointwise and family-level low-speed remainder estimates, and a compact-continuation criterion for smooth force motion on the future unit timelike shell. It then applies the comparison estimate to a formal prescribed-field relativistic-to-Newtonian family and records the separate weak-field, slow-motion geodesic comparison under the exact hypotheses of the GR mathematics theorem. The flat inertial kinematic estimates and the curved weak-field geodesic estimate are distinct model branches; no flat-frame momentum formula is silently transferred to curved coordinates.

The trajectory results hold only for existing solutions on specified finite intervals with the stated field bounds and compact containment. The weak-field result does not construct a metric family from arbitrary Newtonian data or assert large-time convergence at fixed parameter. The prescribed electromagnetic result holds fields fixed in a formal model-parameter limit and excludes self-fields, radiation reaction, and empirical claims.
