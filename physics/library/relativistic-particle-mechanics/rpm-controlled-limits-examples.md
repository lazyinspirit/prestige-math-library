## Scope

This companion evaluates the exact low-speed kinetic-energy remainder at a stipulated speed cutoff of one tenth of the speed parameter $c$. The speed condition is a model hypothesis, not a measurement or an experimental uncertainty. The example uses the uniform kinematic lemma and the mass-shell energy definition; it does not assert that an apparatus prepared such a state or that any relativistic trajectory is close to a Newtonian one over time.
