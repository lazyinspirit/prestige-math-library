The pure-gauge example uses the polynomial potential $A=d\chi$ in flat spacetime to separate kinetic momentum from the gauge-dependent canonical covector. Its field strength and Lorentz force vanish, and its fixed-endpoint stationary curves remain free massive straight timelike curves. A position-dependent canonical component can nevertheless vary along a curve. All conclusions are conditional on the adopted test-body action and its exact mathematical hypotheses; no self-field, radiation reaction, force measurement or energy measurement is inferred.
