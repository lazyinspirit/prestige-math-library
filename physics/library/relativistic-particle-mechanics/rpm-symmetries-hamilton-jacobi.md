This page fixes the proper orthochronous Poincare semidirect product, the cotangent and dual-charge sign conventions, and the translation and Lorentz moment components. It derives their algebra and conditional shell descent, identifies the massive spinless and positive classical-spin KKS orbits, and develops local Hamilton-Jacobi solutions and complete-integral coordinates. For these Hamilton-Jacobi families, a caustic is a point where the characteristic projection loses local invertibility. Physical consequences are conditional on the separately stated free-action or optional spinning-orbit model postulates. Sources using natural units or the opposite metric signature are translated explicitly. The page asserts neither quantum spin classification nor global Hamilton-Jacobi continuation through such points.
