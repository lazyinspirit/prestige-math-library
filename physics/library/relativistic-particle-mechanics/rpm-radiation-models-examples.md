These examples keep their model equations separate. The nonrelativistic Abraham–Lorentz example solves a scalar future-boundary problem and verifies pre-acceleration for a nonnegative compact pulse. The force-free LAD counterexample is a smooth relativistic rapidity solution with exponentially increasing proper acceleration. The latter contrasts with the force-free reduced LL equation on [[rpm-radiation-models]]. Neither calculation is an experimental report or a conclusion about finite-body electrodynamics.
