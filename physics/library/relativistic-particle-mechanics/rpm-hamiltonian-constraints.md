This page fixes the cotangent symplectic sign, observable domain, and action-unit conventions; states the regularity package for Legendre maps and constraints; and proves the local first- and second-class reduction results under their exact hypotheses. It keeps local Frobenius reduction distinct from the non-Hausdorff global counterexample, and keeps the explicit flat-shell global reduction distinct from the local curved lapse-shift reduction. The massive square-root and massless-shell actions are separate adopted models. Hamiltonian and observer-energy identities are conditional deductions; no experiment, global curved quotient, or universal constraint-algorithm termination is asserted.
