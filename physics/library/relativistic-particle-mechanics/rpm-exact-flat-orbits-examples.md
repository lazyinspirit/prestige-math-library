These symbolic examples specialize the page's conditional results: a pair of stationary Rindler clocks and a radial ray; a particle released from rest in a uniform electric field; a planar circular orbit in a uniform magnetic field; and the exact constant drift solution in subcritical crossed fields. Parameters are chosen for calculation and do not represent reported measurements. The instantaneous Rindler frequency ratio uses the theorem's additional adopted local phase-reception rule and retains its local ray-regime and finite-apparatus qualifications.
