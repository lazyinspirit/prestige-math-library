This page develops a conditional flat and local-curved framework for classical polarization transport, finite-size charge estimates, observer-dependent centroids, and neutral pole-dipole dynamics. The mathematical carriers prove the restricted BMT transport algebra, positive-energy spin–size inequality, componentwise charge-multipole remainder, flat centroid disk, differentiated SSC identity, and local TD closure only on its explicitly admissible region. The physical items separately define body moments and adopt MPD and BMT models before deriving their conditional consequences.

The charged-spin definition and BMT transport law are current batch-24 suppliers on [[rpm-external-charge]]; their inspected interface fixes the $(-,+,+,+)$ signature, $F_{0i}=-E_i/c$, $F_{ij}=\epsilon_{ijk}B_k$, the magnetic-moment parameter, and the curved BMT law as an adopted model extension. These assumptions do not prove quantum-spin ontology, empirical validity, or a curved Maxwell theory. The MPD equations are finite-dimensional neutral test-body truncations, not arbitrary stress-energy moment theorems. The TD closure is local and can fail when its matrix becomes singular or its algebraically determined tangent ceases to be timelike.

The body angular-momentum vector $\mathbf s_*$ and the BMT polarization $\mathbf s$ remain distinct unless an additional identification is adopted. The positive-energy bound requires $e\ge0$ and $|j|\le e/c$ in the body rest frame. The Coulomb-exterior support-radius bound additionally assumes an exact Coulomb exterior and nonnegative remaining energy; it does not infer the field profile from spherical symmetry alone. Multipole estimates are componentwise on a fixed smooth trivialization and do not establish a global MPD approximation error.
