This page develops the local variational description of a massive charged test body on a prescribed smooth, time-oriented Lorentzian background. It states the SI field and worldline conventions, adopts a local external-potential action and a separate classical polarization transport model, and proves the fixed-endpoint Euler–Lagrange equation, local ODE well-posedness and mass-shell preservation. The flat inertial specialization recovers the coordinate Lorentz force and power under the stated EM and SR conventions.

The electromagnetic input on a curved background is an explicitly prescribed $C^1$ two-form with a local $C^2$ potential where needed. The flat EM supplier fixes the sign dictionary; it does not derive curved Maxwell dynamics. The action is local to a potential domain, so no global potential, self-field, radiation reaction, or global trajectory completeness is asserted. The BMT curved transport equation is an additional model postulate, not a consequence of the charged action or of the flat homogeneous-field source.

Prerequisite frameworks: [[em-lorentz-covariant-formulation]] and [[rpm-geometry-actions]].
