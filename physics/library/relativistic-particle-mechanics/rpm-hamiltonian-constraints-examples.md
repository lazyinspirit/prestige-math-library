These examples show that a regular first-class system can have a non-Hausdorff global orbit space despite its local symplectic leaf spaces, and that a flat temporal chart can have negative coordinate Hamiltonian while its normal-observer energy stays positive. Both are exact conditional calculations; they make no empirical claim and preserve the distinction between chart velocity and local observer velocity.
