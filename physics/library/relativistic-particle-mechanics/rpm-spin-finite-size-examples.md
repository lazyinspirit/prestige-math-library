These worked examples specialize the adopted BMT law to its Fermi–Walker and $g_s=2$ shared-generator branches, compare fixed-charge and scaled-body size limits, solve a uniform-rest-magnetic-field polarization rotation alongside an independent centroid disk, and recover the spinless geodesic branch of the local TD model. All field values, body data and parameter values are model assumptions; none is a reported measurement.

The BMT polarization vector and body angular-momentum moment stay separate throughout. The examples inherit the conditional energy, support, electromagnetic-field, and TD admissibility assumptions of [[rpm-spin-finite-size]]. They assert no material-family existence, no universal point-particle limit, and no global existence beyond the stated local model domains.
