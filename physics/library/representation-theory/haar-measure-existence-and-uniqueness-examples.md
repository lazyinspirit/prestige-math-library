These calculations identify Haar measure on discrete groups, Euclidean spaces, finite groups and arbitrary products of compact groups. The discrete example includes uncountable groups, where compact sets remain finite. Euclidean box volumes and finite-group weights make normalization explicit. The product example calculates finite-coordinate cylinder probabilities while retaining the uncountable-index compactness and choice assumptions.
