This page develops the representation theory of the group
$G=\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)$: the discrete series, the limits of discrete
series, and the classification of the irreducible unitary dual. It begins with
the smooth and $K$-finite vectors of a unitary representation and the derived
$(\mathfrak g,K)$-module $V=H^{\infty,K}$, whose density and stability are
proved in [[lem-k-finite-vectors-are-dense-and-stable-under-the-derived-action]]
under the fixed Casimir normalization of
[[def-k-finite-and-smooth-vectors-for-sl2-r]].

At the exceptional principal-series parameters the compact picture splits into
highest- and lowest-weight submodules
([[lem-highest-and-lowest-weight-submodules-at-principal-series-reducibility-points]]).
The holomorphic and antiholomorphic models
[[def-holomorphic-and-antiholomorphic-discrete-series-models]] realize the
discrete series as weighted Hilbert spaces of holomorphic functions
([[lem-the-weighted-discrete-series-space-is-a-hilbert-space]]), with an
$\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)$-invariant action and norm
([[lem-the-weighted-area-form-is-sl2-r-invariant]]) and multiplicity-one
$K$-type chains ([[thm-irreducibility-and-k-types-of-the-discrete-series]]).
The two limits of discrete series are the irreducible summands of the reducible
unitary principal series $I_{1,0}$
([[def-limits-of-discrete-series-for-sl2-r]]).

The native Haar integral is calibrated by the KAK integration formula
[[lem-kak-integration-formula-for-k-bi-invariant-functions-on-sl2-r]], which
turns the extremal $K$-type coefficients
([[lem-k-type-coefficient-formulas-for-the-sl2-discrete-and-principal-series]])
into explicit radial integrals; the discrete-series coefficients are
square-integrable ([[thm-square-integrability-of-sl2-r-discrete-series-matrix-coefficients]])
while the limits are not ([[thm-the-limits-of-discrete-series-are-not-square-integrable]]).
Unitarity and irreducibility of the limits
([[thm-unitarity-and-irreducibility-of-the-limits-of-discrete-series]]) combine
with Fell continuity of the principal-series parameter
([[lem-fell-continuity-in-the-parameter-of-the-unitary-principal-series]]) to
produce the non-discreteness and non-Hausdorffness of the dual at the stated
limits
([[cor-the-unitary-dual-of-sl2-r-is-non-discrete-and-non-hausdorff-at-the-stated-limits]]).

Temperedness is defined by weak containment in the regular representation
([[def-tempered-unitary-representation]]); the tempered boundary of the unitary
series is determined in [[thm-tempered-status-of-the-sl2-r-unitary-series]]
through the Plancherel support
[[thm-plancherel-support-for-sl2-r]]. The classification of all irreducible
unitary representations is completed in
[[thm-classification-of-the-irreducible-unitary-dual-of-sl2-r]]. Concrete
computations accompany the main page on
[[sl2-r-discrete-series-and-unitary-dual-examples]].
