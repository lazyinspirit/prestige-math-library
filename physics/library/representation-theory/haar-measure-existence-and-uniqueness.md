A locally compact Hausdorff group admits a nonzero left-invariant Radon measure, unique up to a positive scalar. The construction starts with finite covers by translates of compactly supported test functions. Uniform translation estimates give asymptotic additivity, and compactness of the candidate space produces a positive linear functional. Riesz–Markov–Kakutani representation then supplies the measure with the stated regularity.

The uniqueness argument compares compactly supported kernels using finite tensor approximation. It does not require global sigma-compactness or an identification of product Borel sigma algebras. On a compact group, normalization gives a probability measure invariant under left translation, right translation and inversion. The use of the axiom of choice in arbitrary-product compactness is stated in the construction and its consumers.
