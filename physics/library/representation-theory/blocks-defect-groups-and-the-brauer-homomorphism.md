A block is viewed as an indecomposable module for the double group, with defect groups defined by diagonal vertices. Relative traces connect this definition to Brauer coefficient projection, giving maximal support and the defect-zero criterion. The coefficient arguments work over fields of characteristic $p$; matrix-algebra conclusions state their splitting-field hypothesis.

Brauer pairs are ordered by normal descent. Primitive-idempotent lifting and a normalizer trace argument prove unique descent, independence of the chain, and all six equivalent criteria. Maximal pairs exist, are conjugate, and detect defect groups. A two-stage Mackey restriction argument retains the diagonal vertex and yields the intersection of two Sylow subgroups, followed by the normal-core and radical properties.
