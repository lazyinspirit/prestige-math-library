---
title: Partial Differential Equations
---

The Partial Differential Equations pages develop classical and weak methods for elliptic, parabolic, and hyperbolic equations. They introduce characteristics and analytic existence, then build Sobolev spaces through weak derivatives, approximation, extension, traces, compactness, and embedding inequalities. Elliptic topics include weak solutions, maximum principles, harmonic estimates, regularity, and Fredholm spectra. Evolution pages treat semigroups, heat kernels, Duhamel formulas, wave energy, finite propagation, and representation formulas. The collection also covers fundamental solutions, Green identities, variational methods, constrained problems, scalar conservation laws with entropy solutions, and Hamilton–Jacobi equations with viscosity solutions. Examples show the force of the hypotheses and standard counterexamples.

Real Analysis, Measure Theory, Functional Analysis, Topology, Set Theory, and Differential Geometry supply tools for the weak, elliptic, and evolution theories here. Numerical PDEs and Spatial Discretization, Fourier Analysis, Complex Analysis, Neural Networks and Learning Dynamics, and Differential Geometry use these Sobolev, regularity, and evolution results in their own problems and estimates.
