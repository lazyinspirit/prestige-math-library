Starting with a quasilinear Cauchy problem, this page separates ODE solvability
from the inverse-projection step that actually constructs a PDE graph.  It
then makes projection failure precise through Burgers caustics, develops both
Charpit invariants for fully nonlinear equations. The closing scope remark
links the locally proved
[[thm-cauchy-kovalevskaya-for-a-noncharacteristic-analytic-cauchy-problem]]:
analytic Cauchy data on a noncharacteristic analytic hypersurface yield a unique
local analytic solution germ. For an implicit nonlinear equation the theorem
fixes a compatible jet and its selected analytic branch.
