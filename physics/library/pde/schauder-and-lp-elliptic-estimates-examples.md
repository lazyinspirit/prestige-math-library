The examples check the radius bookkeeping of the Schauder estimate, exhibit
the logarithmic loss that makes the endpoint $\alpha=1$ fail, record the
non-Dini and jump-coefficient obstructions to Schauder regularity, separate
weak boundary regularity on Lipschitz domains from the a priori estimates,
and reduce the method of continuity to a one-dimensional eigenvalue model.
