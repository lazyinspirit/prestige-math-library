The mathematical prerequisites and supporting suppliers for the non-relativistic
classical mechanics collection: the affine-time geometry and Galilean chart
transformations of the kinematical model; the rotation group and its
configuration-space facts; the stability, hyperbolicity and stroboscopic-map
definitions used by the dynamical statements; the constrained mass metric and
the material-region, density, traction and stress definitions used by the
continuum balances; and, on the companion example pages, worked finite
verifications. Items here are mathematics: they carry `domain: mathematics`,
depend only on mathematical suppliers, and are excluded from physical
conclusions by themselves. Their physical consumers are the non-relativistic
classical mechanics pages, which use them as stated mathematical premises under
the physics schema's dependency roles and add their own postulates,
interpretations and empirical qualifications. No physical postulate,
observation or empirical claim is asserted on this page.
