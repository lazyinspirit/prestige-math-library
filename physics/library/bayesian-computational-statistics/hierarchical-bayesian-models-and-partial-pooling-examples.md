The examples identify the complete-pooling boundary and the no-pooling limit
of the conditional normal update, with finite positive variance giving partial
pooling. A two-point proper hyperprior shows by direct calculation that
integrating hyperparameter uncertainty can produce a posterior different from
the posterior at a plug-in mode. A separate one-group calculation shows that
an improper log-scale variance hyperprior can leave the posterior
unnormalizable even with a finite observation and a proper conditional normal
model.
