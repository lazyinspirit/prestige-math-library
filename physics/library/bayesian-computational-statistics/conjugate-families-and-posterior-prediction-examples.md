The examples compute one Beta–Binomial evidence and its endpoint posteriors,
show a zero-exposure Gamma–Poisson update and its impossible-data boundary,
pool two Normal observations by precision, evaluate a three-category
Dirichlet–Multinomial evidence exactly, and apply the Gaussian conditioner to
a singular state covariance with a positive-definite observed block.
