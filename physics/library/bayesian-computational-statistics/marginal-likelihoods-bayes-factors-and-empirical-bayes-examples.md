These worked cases compare point-null mass under atomic and continuous priors,
show why a Bayes factor can change with the spread of a proper prior, and why an
arbitrarily normalized improper prior cannot give a generic model evidence.
They also compute a negative balanced-ANOVA moment estimate and exhibit a
finite hierarchy in which full Bayes retains hyperparameter uncertainty and
induces group dependence, while a Type-II plug-in posterior conditions on one
selected hyperparameter value.
