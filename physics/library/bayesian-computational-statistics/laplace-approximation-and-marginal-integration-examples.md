The full-line Gaussian integral is an exact noncompact special case because its
tails are evaluated explicitly. A boundary mode has an $n^{-1}$ contribution
rather than the interior $n^{-1/2}$ scale; a singular interior mode also falls
outside the nondegenerate formula, and two equal modes contribute two Gaussian
terms. For quadrature, the one-panel rule on $x^2$ attains the second-derivative
error bound exactly.
