Finite symmetric groups, one-line notation, inversions, cycle decomposition,
binomial coefficients, factorials, and the symbolic-method page provide the
background for this development. Those items fix the inversion convention, give
the product counts behind Lehmer codes, supply the cycle-form seam used by
Foata's fundamental transformation, and provide the formal power-series language
used when the Eulerian-polynomial exponential generating function is written.

The page defines descents, major index, excedances, Eulerian numbers,
$q$-factorials, Lehmer codes, and the weak and Bruhat orders. It then proves the
Lehmer-code bijection and MacMahon's inversion enumerator, constructs Foata's
transformations to compare major index, descents, and excedances with inversions,
and closes with the Eulerian recurrence, Worpitzky's identity, and the
Eulerian-polynomial exponential generating function.
