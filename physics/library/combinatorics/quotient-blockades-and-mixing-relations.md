This page isolates the quotient construction used at the start of Section 6 of
the six-vertex extension paper. It defines the mixed-block reachability
relation, passes to its quotient blockade, records the three local consequences
collected as Lemma 6.1, and ends at the exact descent statement of Lemma 6.2.

Nothing from the later co-$E$ or co-Bird structure theory is pulled forward
here. The point of the page is the quotient mechanism itself, not the later
graph-specific applications.
