This page develops good functions, $\alpha$-narrowness, modular decomposition,
and the basic bull-free structural lemmas. Vertex replication, Lovász's
numerical criterion and complement invariance, and substitution of perfect
graphs have full local proofs.

The narrowness lemmas prove that $\alpha$-narrow graphs contain large perfect
induced subgraphs and hence large cliques or stable sets. The perfect-vertex-deletion lemma also gives an independent two-narrowness proof for the five-cycle
example. Substitution preserves $\alpha$-narrowness.

The bull-free decomposition and hole lemmas are retained, but this page
establishes no general two-narrowness theorem for bull-free graphs and no
Erdős–Hajnal exponent $1/4$. Those conclusions require a locally proved
perfection theorem for bull-free Berge graphs, which is not supplied.
