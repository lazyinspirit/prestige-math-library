The example constructs the product $E\times_{\mathbb C}\mathbb P^3_{\mathbb C}$ from a supplied smooth projective genus-one curve $E$ with identity point $O$. It verifies that the projection is a proper flat genus-one fibration with regular section $b\mapsto(O,b)$ and a smooth projective fourfold as total space. The construction is mathematical; it asserts no Calabi–Yau property or physical compactification.
