This page develops flat genus-one fibrations over smooth complex bases and the elliptic structures supplied by a regular section. It builds the small étale site, sheaves, sheafification, and torsors used to describe relative group actions, then introduces line-bundle Weierstrass models and computes their analytic dualizing sheaves. The statements keep the generic-fiber group law separate from any group scheme or torsor extension over the base, and do not infer smoothness of special fibers or the broad Calabi–Yau manifold conditions from the Weierstrass equation alone.
