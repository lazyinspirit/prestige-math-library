This page fixes the free flat RNS matter formulation, NS/R spin sectors, and the Type II/0 GSO sector conventions, then defines the reparametrization and superconformal ghost systems and their local operator products. It derives the local central-charge cancellation condition $c_{\mathrm{tot}}=0$ exactly at $D=10$ and, under the stated nonzero-$p^+$ chart and zeta prescription, obtains the open-string intercepts and the closed sector-by-sector mass shell and level matching. The Type 0 projection retains the NS–NS vacuum; the closed mass-shell result [[pthm-rns-closed-lightcone-level-matching]] gives $\alpha'M^2/4=-1/2$, hence $M^2=-2/\alpha'<0$ for that state. The BRST cohomology entry remains conditional on nilpotence and relative-subspace stability; no no-ghost theorem or global string measure is asserted.
