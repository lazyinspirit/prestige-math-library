The example [[ex-rns-open-ns-vacuum-before-gso]] checks the vacuum and first transverse excitation in the flat ten-dimensional open NN RNS oscillator model. The vacuum has negative mass squared and is removed by the supersymmetric chiral GSO projection; the first NS excitation is massless and passes that projection. These conditional level and parity calculations do not establish BRST closure, a complete physical-state classification, spacetime supersymmetry, or experimental evidence.
