# GKP Flux Potentials And Throat Hierarchies

This page records the conditional leading calculation that links quantized three-form fluxes, the logarithmic conifold period, and the redshift at a Klebanov–Strassler cap. The proof fixes the flux signs, cycle orientations, Einstein-frame warp convention, weak-coupling assumption, compact tadpole condition, and the limits of the leading approximation.

The result is [[pthm-r2-gkp-conifold-flux-warp-hierarchy]]. Its exponent is a model deduction within the tree-level supergravity regime. It does not establish a compact vacuum, an unbounded flux family at fixed tadpole, or stabilization of all Kähler moduli.

The matching coefficient is fixed relative to the large $K/(g_s M)$ exponent at fixed remaining data. The KS tip contributes a factor proportional to $(g_s M)^{-1/2}$, and an explicit LVS gluing adds flux-, topology-, and volume-dependent prefactors. No uniform numerical order-one bound across varying compactifications is asserted.
