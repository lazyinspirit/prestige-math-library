This page records two selected QED conventions: finite/formal diagram
coefficients and a fixed finite-graph compact $U(1)$ regulator. The formal
coefficient postulate fixes the signature, charge normalization, vertex and
free-line symbols, off-shell denominators, and optional distributional kernel.
Its formal and tree deductions apply only to coefficientwise finite matrices
and the specified conserved external currents.

The regulator postulate adopts a finite graph with compact rotor links and
finite CAR matter, with an exact Hamiltonian domain and assigned energy units.
The accompanying results establish a nonzero physical Gauss sector, restricted
self-adjoint dynamics, and finite-regulator Ward/Gauss consequences under
their stated mathematical suppliers. Full Axiom of Choice is required for
the dynamics and Ward results. A separate continuum implication is conditional
on explicit common-domain, locality, Gauss, and limit hypotheses.

The page does not assert convergence of formal series, all-loop gauge
independence, a continuum limit, interacting continuum QED, or empirical
confirmation. These scopes remain conditional on the named draft suppliers;
page membership does not certify independent proof acceptance.
