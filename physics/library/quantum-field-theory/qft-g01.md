This page specifies a positive transverse free-photon model in the stated
flat-spacetime and SI/natural-unit conventions. The physical one-photon space
has two transverse degrees of freedom almost everywhere, and the adopted
free Fock model has a nonnegative Hamiltonian and smeared local field-strength
observables. The charged Dirac/Maxwell coupling is a separate smooth classical
model; its local equations and the neutral periodic Coulomb branch are
conditional deductions. The photon Hilbert-space and local-observable
arguments cite current QFT-MG01 carriers at their inspected conditional
interfaces. The transverse-space, null-quotient, and covariance-spectrum
carriers have current ready records; the QED gauge-data, field-strength, Weyl,
Dirac-current, Coulomb, and finite-mode constraint carriers are escalated.
Readiness is not independent mathematical approval, and dependent items remain
conditional. No interacting continuum QED construction or empirical outcome
is asserted.
