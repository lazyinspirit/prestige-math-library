Quantum Field Theory specifies selected physical models: free scalar, Dirac and transverse-photon systems; formal QED conventions; soft coherent photon-count predictions; and finite-regulator interacting models. Postulates fix fields, units and observable meanings, while theorem pages derive consequences conditional on those assumptions and the mathematical constructions. Predictions retain their smearing, observable-domain and regulator conditions. The photon sector selects a positive-transverse free model; QED pages distinguish finite-graph compact U(1) results from formal coefficient rules. Finite-lattice and finite-coordinate results concern their stated regulators, and soft-count or thermal predictions use specified profiles, cutoffs and observables. The finite-regulator bosonic pages derive evolution and positive-temperature predictions for bounded observables without removing the regulator; no page presents a measurement as empirical validation.

The mathematics companion supplies algebraic states, test spaces, Fock carriers, regulators and finite measurement constructions; selected pages also use special-relativistic and electromagnetic conventions. QFT results support selected Thermodynamics and General Relativity pages. Mathematical constructions, physical adoption and measurement remain distinct.
