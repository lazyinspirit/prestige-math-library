[[ex-qft-mode-occupations]] calculates bosonic occupation and fermionic one-mode
exclusion for normalizable wave-packet modes in the selected free-field
representations. It also constructs a normalized bosonic Fock vector outside
the maximal annihilation-operator domain. These are conditional algebraic
model results, not observed data or a general spin-statistics theorem.
