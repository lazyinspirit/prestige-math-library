[[texp-qft-two-level-measurement]] constructs a two-dimensional Hilbert space,
a normalized state, and a two-outcome projective Kraus instrument. It computes
the conditional model probabilities and state updates, including the
zero-probability boundary. A separate factorial-coefficient example shows
that formal coefficients exist even when the evaluated power series has
radius zero; the coefficient alone is not assigned a measurement
probability. No observed outcome is reported.
