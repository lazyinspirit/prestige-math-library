The companion page specializes the formal pair-production coefficient to
massless particles, displays the scalar identical-particle factor and the
extra assumption needed for exponential mean and half-life formulas, and
evaluates the minimal Dirac spin coefficient in a uniform magnetic field.
These are conditional model calculations. The spin coefficient is compared
with a separately defined orbital coefficient; no full Landau spectrum,
radiative anomaly, apparatus, or measurement is claimed.
