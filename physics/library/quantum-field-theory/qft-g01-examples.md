[[texp-qft-photon-auxiliary-comparison]] constructs a normalized negative-norm
wavepacket in an indefinite auxiliary space and contrasts it with a positive
transverse physical state. [[texp-qft-qed-neutral-cell]] integrates the
periodic Gauss constraint to show why a smooth single-species electron source
with nonzero total charge cannot fit on a boundaryless torus. Both are
hypothetical calculations under stated model and boundary assumptions; neither
reports an experiment or assigns a probability to an auxiliary vector.
