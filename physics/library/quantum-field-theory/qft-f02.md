This page fixes a single real scalar species as an exact bosonic Fock model,
including its mass-shell normalization, smeared field, vacuum-subtracted
Hamiltonian and domains. It derives covariance, the Klein–Gordon equation,
positive-energy spectrum, vacuum moments, causal commutativity and the
time-ordered two-point distribution conditionally on the adopted QFT-F01
free-field model. These deductions describe the selected model; they do not
establish empirical universality.
