[[cex-qft-point-operator-assumption]] refutes a finite vacuum-vector norm
limit for the specified normalized Gaussian spacetime smearings of the free
scalar field. The obstruction applies to that representation and topology;
it does not rule out distributional or renormalized point-field constructions.
