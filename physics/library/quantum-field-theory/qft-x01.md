This page distinguishes exact observables in an actually constructed finite
regulator, formal perturbative coefficient records, and source-reported
empirical estimates. Its finite-dimensional probability and truncation bound
are conditional on an explicit pure-state preparation, adoption of the
constructed unitary as that model's state update, and the constructed-model
Born postulate. It also records exact unitarity and finite-cutoff warnings;
none is a continuum-QFT or experimental conclusion.
