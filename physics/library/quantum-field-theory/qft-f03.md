This page defines the selected free Dirac CAR model and derives its smeared
field relations, graded locality, spin-lift covariance and positive Fock
energy. The negative-energy branch of the first-quantized Dirac operator is
represented by antiparticle creation in the Fock model; it is not a negative
energy Fock excitation. The species/statistics assignment is adopted as a
physical postulate, and none of these conditional deductions is an empirical
confirmation or a general spin-statistics theorem.
