This page gives the exact photon-count distribution for a coherent state
prepared in the positive transverse free-photon model, with a fixed massive
charge-flow current and finite energy cutoffs. The local mathematical
carriers construct the number operator and its count PVM, so each count event
can be assigned through a finite projective partition under the adopted Born
interpretation. The soft coefficient, normalization, energy expectation, and
zero-cutoff behavior are conditional on the planned QFT-MX03 supplier at its
inspected interface. The result concerns a prepared free state and supplies no
prediction for a full interacting-QED scattering event or detector response.
