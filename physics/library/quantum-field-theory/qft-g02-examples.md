The infrared thought experiment instantiates the transverse free-photon
coherent-tail result for one explicit angular profile. It gives a logarithmic
number expectation, bounded free-energy expectation, and weak convergence to
zero with no Fock-norm limit, conditional on the current free-photon and
mathematical interfaces.

The lattice thought experiment applies the exact regional Gauss/flux relation:
an operator that preserves the physical sector and commutes with every
boundary electric operator has zero regional-charge commutator on the common
domain. Both are model deductions, not observations. Neither constructs a
continuum charged sector or asserts a continuum QED limit.
