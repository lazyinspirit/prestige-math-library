This page adopts specified formal QED and scalar tree coefficients as
model-specific leading readings through an explicit two-body flux and
phase-space map. It derives the full-mass unpolarized pair-production
coefficient, a bounded incoming-electron-mass comparison, scalar symmetry
factors and a decay-rate coefficient, plus the minimal Dirac magnetic
benchmark from the adopted local action. Formal rules, mathematical
coefficients and reported measurements remain separate: the page constructs
no continuum scattering operator or LSZ limit, gives no loop or
soft-radiation error budget, and reports no empirical estimate.
