This companion applies the regulated coherent-state count law to one
prescribed velocity change, then records the authors' 2022 electron
magnetic-moment determination and an exact affine subtraction of its reported
ratio. The first result is a calculation for a prepared free state. The
experiment entry preserves the source's apparatus, correction procedure,
weighted field determinations, stated uncertainty, and correlation limits.
The final theorem carries those qualifications through subtraction and makes
no theoretical comparison.
