This page adopts one specified flat, noninteracting real scalar and Dirac
field model, including its unit conventions, field representations, vacuum,
and observable interpretation in [[post-qft-free-field-model]] and
[[def-qft-free-field-observables]]. The scalar is assigned to bosonic
symmetric Fock space and the Dirac field to CAR Fock space. These assignments
are postulates of the selected model. The CCR/CAR and mode-occupation result
in [[pthm-qft-free-fock-statistics]] is a conditional mathematical deduction
from the named constructions; it does not prove a general spin-statistics
theorem or establish that the model describes nature. The page claims no
experimental observation, point-field operator, coincident product, or
interacting continuum QFT construction.
