This page fixes a four-dimensional Minkowski length-coordinate convention and
records the compatible SI and natural-unit normalizations for selected QFT
model data. It distinguishes exact SI defining constants from the measured
magnetic constant, mass and wave-number conversions from physical energy and
momentum, and canonical engineering dimensions from anomalous dimensions.

[[def-qft-framework-units]] is a unit and domain dictionary. The SR suppliers
set the coordinate and metric convention; the EM suppliers are used only for
SI units and tensor components. No classical Maxwell law or Lorentz-force law
is imported as a QFT premise. Fields are smeared distributions on common dense
domains; the page supplies no pointwise fields or field products. Further
operator properties, local observable algebras, and continuum field
representations require separate model constructions.

[[post-qft-constructed-model-and-measurement]] adopts the Born interpretation
only when the state, finite instrument, and any invoked dynamics have actually
been constructed. Its normalization input is the finite-instrument result on
[[qft-mc03]], under the exact Countable Choice hypothesis stated there. A
formal coefficient, experiment, or agreement with data does not fill a missing
model construction.
