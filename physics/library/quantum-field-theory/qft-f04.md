[[pthm-qft-free-observable-domain-limits]] records the exact domains and
moment conditions for the selected free observables, the spacelike
commutation of the specified bounded scalar and even Dirac algebras, and the
limits of normalized nonnegative Schwartz point-smearing families. It does
not supply pointwise field operators or coincident products. The Born rule is
invoked only for a constructed state and finite bounded instrument; no
apparatus or outcome is claimed.

[[pthm-qft-classical-quantum-free-correspondence]] checks that the classical
equations from compactly supported variations and the tested equations of the separately
adopted scalar and Dirac free representations have matching differential
expressions in the declared conventions. The Fock representations and
species/statistics assignments remain physical postulates, not deductions
from the classical actions.
