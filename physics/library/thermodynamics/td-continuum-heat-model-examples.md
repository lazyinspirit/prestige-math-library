These are constructed examples inside the adopted constant-coefficient Fourier model, not measured temperature records. The first computes a compatible nonuniform profile's mean and variance exactly, then applies the conditional relaxation theorem. The second shows that the model's positive Gaussian kernel produces an immediate nonzero perturbation throughout the rectangle from compactly supported initial data. That is a mathematical property of the diffusion equation, not an experimental assertion about signal speeds.
