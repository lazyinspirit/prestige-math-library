This page fixes the nonrelativistic macroscopic state and process conventions in SI units. Each equilibrium state's internal energy is evaluated in its statewise instantaneous center-of-mass rest chart, while each process model selects one fixed Galilean chart for bulk kinetic and external-field energy bookkeeping and transfer records. The energy balance and separated-system additivity are adopted physical postulates with explicit boundaries and energy exclusions; they are not proofs that the model describes every material system. Fixed-composition state charts are specified open and convex where the model uses them, while process records may include nonequilibrium intermediate configurations. Entropy, temperature state functions, equilibrium maximum principles, and reversible-work identities belong to their later assigned pages.
