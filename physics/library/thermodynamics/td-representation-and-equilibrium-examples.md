The formal charts calculate response derivatives and entropy-maximizing exchange conditions under explicitly stipulated functions. They are not measurements or empirical equations of state. The retained-volume example shows that equal temperature does not supply pressure equality when volume exchange is constrained. The finite-dimensional tag model checks that tagged scaling and additivity alone do not establish homogeneity for one fixed tag.
