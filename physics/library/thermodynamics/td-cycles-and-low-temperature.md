This page derives ideal-bath engine and refrigerator bounds from the adopted first- and second-law bookkeeping. It fixes heat and work signs, books the ideal entropy-neutral work store's energy gain or loss, and states the isolated compound boundary; finite reservoirs and unlisted transfers are outside the result.

The two low-temperature statements are separate, explicitly scoped physical postulates. The uniform Nernst heat-theorem formulation concerns entropy differences on a declared compact family with a specified topology. Operational unattainability concerns the full declared family of finite-duration protocols, with nonempty positive-temperature states and positive-start protocols, and must not be made vacuous by excluding the zero-temperature boundary by construction. Neither postulate is derived from the other or from the ideal-bath cycle bounds.

The companion page gives symbolic cycle calculations, a constructed response chart showing that entropy-integral convergence alone does not force a heat-capacity bound $O((T/T_*)^q)$ for any real $q\ge1$, a finite canonical residual-entropy calculation, and a restricted geometric-cooling protocol. These are model calculations, not experimental reports.
