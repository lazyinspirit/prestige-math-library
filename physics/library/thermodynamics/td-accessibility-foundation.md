This page defines adiabatic accessibility and the tagged product/contact operations, then states the adopted accessibility, simple-system, and thermal-contact assumptions. Its product-comparison and entropy results are conditional deductions. The contact theorem uses A1–A7, S1–S3, T1–T5, and the additional equilibrium-join coherence condition C1; the bare printed source theorem does not supply that exact interface. The mixing result additionally requires a declared cross-type transition network, finite catalyst-chain costs, Choice, and an explicit no-sinks condition. None of these assumptions is inferred from an experiment, and the dimensionless order entropy is not yet calibrated to $\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$.
