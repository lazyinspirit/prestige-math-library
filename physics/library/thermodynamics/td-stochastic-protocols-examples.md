These worked examples calculate a finite quench with below-free-energy work on some paths, a disconnected detailed-balance chain that fails to converge to the full reference law, and a biased bath in which Shannon entropy decreases. The Ornstein–Uhlenbeck and memory-reset examples remain calculations within their stated models. None is an experiment or an empirical measurement.
