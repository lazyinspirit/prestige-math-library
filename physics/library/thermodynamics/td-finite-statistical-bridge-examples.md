These examples calculate one-mode bosonic and fermionic weights, a two-site product law, divergence of the unrenormalized trace for countably many identical bosonic modes, and two frozen finite models that separate canonical preparation from dynamical equilibration. Each example states its own model and derives the claimed probabilities or state invariance from its declared prerequisites, including the assigned A-page items and, for the isolated quantum dynamics, the NRQM autonomous-evolution assumption and definition. The NRQM B-leaf examples are not proof premises. The examples are hypothetical calculations, not experimental observations.
