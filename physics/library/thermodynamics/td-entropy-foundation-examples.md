These conditional examples distinguish a model deduction from an empirical report. The idealized compression calculation uses an adopted gas model and work protocol; it does not report a measurement or prove the second-law postulate. The two feasible-set examples show why global attainment and full feasible-set scope must be stated explicitly. The local-maximum example does not assert that any real material is metastable; applying that term to a material requires a separately specified kinetic model.
