These leaf cases show that the adopted contact assumptions have a nondegenerate logarithmic consistency witness and that a degenerate thermometer can satisfy a simple one-system order while failing T4 and product comparison. Both are constructed mathematical models; neither is an experimental observation or a universal claim about real materials.
