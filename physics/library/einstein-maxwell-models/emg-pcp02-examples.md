## Scope

The example selects the neutral $p=w\epsilon$ sector, evaluates its positive
EOS number density and its normalized enthalpy coordinate, and constructs compatible flat-torus
Einstein-Maxwell initial data. The resulting local development is conditional
on [[lem-emg-fieldstrength-positive-symmetric-reduction]] and
[[lem-emg-maxwell-stress-conservation]], whose current carriers are draft with
pending prechecks. No global or empirical claim is made.
