## Scope

The example checks the exact zero-scalar branch when the scalar self-potential
vanishes at zero. It verifies the scalar equation, current, and Hilbert stress
directly, then reduces the coupled field equations to source-free
Einstein–Maxwell. The calculation is conditional on the model statement and
does not report an observation or establish empirical validity.

The zero-scalar sector also covers the declared classical finite-regularity branch. Its Cauchy conclusion follows by zero scalar wave energy with bounded classical coefficients; smooth geometric uniqueness is used only for smooth data. The potential condition is $V(0)=0$, with no requirement that $V\prime(0)$ vanish.
