For the punctured-space field $F_b=b\sin\theta\,d\theta\wedge d\phi$, the example compares the integer-normalized case $b=\kappa_0/2$, a noninteger normalized flux, and the zero-field case. It then distinguishes a flat connection with nontrivial holonomy from an integer flat connection removed by a single-valued $U(1)$ gauge transformation. These are exact classical calculations, not reported observations.
