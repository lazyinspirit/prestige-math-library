Einstein–Maxwell models bring electromagnetic fields, charged matter and spacetime geometry into a common conditional physical framework. This category specifies primitive field quantities, units, source and observer conventions, and the adopted relations between electromagnetic and gravitational dynamics. Its pages address electrovacuum evolution, selected charged fluid and scalar models, observer measurements, geometric-optics approximations and particular exact solution families. Mathematical constructions and geometric calculations are supplied by the companion mathematics category and the earlier relativity and electromagnetism prerequisites.

Each model states whether the spacetime geometry is prescribed or is part of a coupled solution. A calculation for a charged probe on a fixed background therefore carries its own interpretation and assumptions. Exact static and charged solution examples retain their parameter ranges, coordinate domains and normalization choices, while local evolution results retain the regularity and formulation conditions of their suppliers. The category presents deductions within these specified models; it does not replace the separate empirical justification of their physical adoption. Consult the assumptions and units alongside each observable or limiting statement.
