The Einstein–Maxwell pages specify field quantities, source and observer conventions, and SI or geometrized units, then adopt distinct curved-vacuum, charged-matter and fixed-background models. Conditional results cover electrovac evolution, observer Maxwell equations and conserved source charge, selected scalar and fluid systems, charged probes, geometric optics and exact coupled solutions. The matter pages treat specified branches, not a universal matter model; probe calculations on prescribed backgrounds remain distinct from coupled Einstein solutions. A charged-dust construction gives an exact coupled example, while the fixed-background branch studies separate probes; multicenter electrovac examples retain their harmonic data. Evolution and exact-solution conclusions keep their regularity, domain, parameter and normalization hypotheses.

The models use the Einstein–Maxwell mathematics companion, general-relativistic geometry and classical electromagnetic fields and couplings; selected charged-particle results supply further prerequisites. Their electrovac and matter results also support pages in Thermodynamics and General Relativity. Every conclusion remains conditional on the stated model assumptions. These model connections are not empirical evidence about nature.
