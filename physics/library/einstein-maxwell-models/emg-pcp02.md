## Scope

This page adopts a smooth strict-interior charged barotropic perfect-fluid
closure for the equation-based Einstein-Maxwell model. It distinguishes the
material postulate from the conditional local development theorem. The theorem
states periodic Sobolev and smooth geometric local-development branches for
compatible data. Its use remains conditional on the current draft inputs
[[lem-emg-fieldstrength-positive-symmetric-reduction]] and
[[lem-emg-maxwell-stress-conservation]], both with pending prechecks. No
experimental validation or global evolution is claimed.
