## Scope

These examples calculate two limits of the specified smooth coupling. A
classical scalar plane wave can solve its matter equation on a prescribed flat
background while failing the coupled Einstein equation; with nonzero
electromagnetic coupling, the same zero-potential background also fails the
sourced Maxwell equation. A separate constant electric field solves the
source-free Maxwell equations on Minkowski space but has nonzero Hilbert
stress, so the same flat metric fails the coupled Einstein equation. These
are exact conditional model calculations, not observations or claims about
particle ontology.
