## Scope

This page adopts Maxwell fields and massive charged probes on a separately prescribed smooth Lorentzian background. It makes explicit that the unchanged metric need not satisfy Einstein's equation for the test field, and that no probe self-field or self-gravity is evaluated. The conditional theorems then establish finite-order curved-spacetime wave residuals, null rays and polarization, exact stress-residual scaling for source-free fields and jointly scaled smooth source/current pairs, and a finite-duration weak/slow charged-trajectory comparison.

The geometric-optics claim is limited to its stated smooth phase and amplitude hypotheses, pointwise constraints and compact-chart component bounds. The weak/slow result requires its explicit stationary metric and field scalings, a parameter cutoff sufficiently small in terms of the uniform bounds, a speed bound, existing trajectories, common compact containment and finite scaled duration. The sourced stress residual omits any source-matter stress and is not a full Einstein–Maxwell–matter equation. None supplies a convergent WKB series, a nearby coupled solution, point-charge source, global evolution, or empirical validation. Fixed-background test status is an adopted model choice, not a theorem that the omitted backreaction is small.
