## Scope

This examples page works out the selected electromagnetic stress in two observer frames. A pure magnetic field has unequal principal stresses, while crossed equal-normalized electric and magnetic fields give a null energy current and saturate the dominant-energy causal-flux bound. These are exact pointwise calculations under the A-page definitions. They are ideal test-background statements, not measurements or full coupled Einstein–Maxwell solutions.
