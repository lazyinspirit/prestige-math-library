## Scope

This page fixes a model-local conversion between selected SI mass,
angular-momentum, electric-charge and magnetic-flux inputs and the geometric
length parameters of the chosen RN and KN solution families. It then states
conditional source-free equation-based model deductions for those data and
converts selected RN/KN horizon, escape, redshift and duality results to SI
readings. The identified parameters are not automatically ADM or Komar
quantities, and mathematical draft suppliers are used only through their exact
current interfaces and proof status.

The page distinguishes adopted clock and geometric-optics assumptions from
derived model predictions. It supplies no measured black-hole values,
observational report, empirical precision, charge quantization, or claim that
the selected solution families describe an observed object. Claims about
global potentials, horizons and escape are restricted to their explicitly
specified domains and conventions.
