## Scope

This page translates checked geometric electrovac families into the adopted
smooth SI Einstein–Maxwell equation model, then treats the harmonic
Majumdar–Papapetrou exterior, its single-center extremal RN correspondence,
the constant-radius Bertotti–Robinson product and local RN scaling limit, and
an exact null-wave family. The common conversion fixes
$\alpha=\sqrt{4\pi G_N/(\mu_0c^4)}$ and is proved once before its consumers.
The mass, charge, flux and length parameters use the conventions of emg-s01.

The claims are conditional deductions from the adopted zero-cosmological-
constant, source-free classical equations. The mathematical suppliers from
emg-ms03 are used at their current statements and hypotheses; their draft
status and lack of independent proof approval are not converted into
acceptance. The sources provide convention and solution context; the exact
local equations, coordinate identifications and convergence are supplied by
the cited mathematical proofs.

## Limits

A harmonic center is removed from the manifold. Its finite-sphere flux is
not a proof of matter at that point, horizon regularity, completeness,
stability, or a black-hole realization. The Robinson–Bertotti limit is
proved only on compact subsets with positive scaled radius. The null-wave
family makes no asymptotic-flatness or finite-total-energy claim. No item
reports an observation or claims empirical validity.
