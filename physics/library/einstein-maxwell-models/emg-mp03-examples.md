## Scope

These examples give a stationary electrostatic energy first integral for a prescribed-field charged probe, an exact transverse plane-wave Maxwell solution on Minkowski space whose nonzero stress prevents the unchanged metric from being a coupled vacuum solution, and a constant-magnetic-field finite-time comparison of a relativistic test orbit with its Newton–Lorentz limit. They are ideal conditional calculations with their A-page model assumptions and limitations; none reports measurements or proves the physical model describes nature.
