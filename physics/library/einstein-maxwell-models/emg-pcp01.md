## Scope

This page develops local and maximal Cauchy evolution in the selected smooth
source-free Einstein-Maxwell action branch. It derives the field-strength
equations from the adopted compact-variation action, then applies the
conditional CP02 local and global mathematical suppliers. Two local
mathematical inputs make the normal collar and fixed bundle-sector arguments
explicit. Initial constraints, SI signs, periodic Sobolev regularity, and the
compatible normalized circle-bundle sector remain explicit. These model
deductions neither prove that the adopted action describes nature nor assert
geodesic completeness.

Geodesic completeness here means that every maximal solution of $\ddot x^a+\Gamma^a{}_{bc}\dot x^b\dot x^c=0$ has affine-parameter domain all of $\mathbb R$; maximality forbids same-parameter extension to a larger interval. The development results make no such affine-domain assertion.
