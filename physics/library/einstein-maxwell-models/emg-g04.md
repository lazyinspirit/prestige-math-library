## Scope

This page develops two explicitly separate smooth Einstein–Maxwell
formulations in the selected SI and Lorentzian conventions. The first adopts
the coupled equations directly, with current and stress supplied by a named
matter model whose fields, equations, constraints, regularity, and current and
stress maps are stated at each use. The second adopts a compact interior action
for one selected invariant matter functional, then derives the metric,
connection, and matter
Euler–Lagrange equations from stationarity. Neither formulation is derived
from the other, and neither claims existence or uniqueness of coupled
solutions.

The page then specifies a smooth charged-dust closure and a classical charged
complex-scalar branch. Their balance identities are conditional deductions on
their smooth solution domains. The external charged-particle result supplies
test-body sign and normalization context only; it does not construct
continuum matter or a coupled PDE solution. Singular sources, boundary
variational principles, empirical validity, and quantum interpretations are
outside scope.
