## Scope

This page verifies one exact compatible initial state with nonzero parallel
electromagnetic fields on a flat three-torus, including a normalized circle
bundle with one flux quantum in the adopted scale. Its local development is a
conditional consequence of the A-page theorem. The initial stress is
anisotropic, so the example makes no later-time isotropy claim and reports no
empirical observation.
