## Scope

This page fixes the smooth SI matter, current, stress and observer quantities used by the selected equation-based Einstein–Maxwell framework. It then adopts two restricted matter branches: transported charged dust and a smooth charged perfect fluid with an optional Ohmic current. Their consequences are conditional deductions from the adopted closures and the listed mathematical suppliers. The page distinguishes local matter exchange, comoving mechanical work, finite-domain Killing flux and pointwise energy conditions.

The models are classical smooth continua. The dust branch makes no coupled PDE existence claim and asserts neither a global smooth continuation nor single-velocity validity beyond its stated smooth domain. The fluid branch does not supply an equation of state, global conducting-fluid theory or entropy-production law. A local symmetry charge includes its finite side-flux term, and the observer energy conditions apply only to electromagnetic stress, nonnegative dust, or fluids satisfying their exact inequalities. No measurements, empirical validation, global potential, singular source, local gravitational-energy tensor or unconditional matter-positivity premise is asserted.
