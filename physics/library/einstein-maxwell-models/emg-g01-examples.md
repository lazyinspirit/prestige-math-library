## Scope

This companion computes the observer electric and magnetic fields of one constant two-form and verifies that it satisfies the smooth vacuum Maxwell equations on a prescribed flat Minkowski background. It is a model calculation, not a measured field and not a verification of the coupled Einstein equation.
