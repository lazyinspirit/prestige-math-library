This worked pointwise calculation evaluates the electromagnetic stress tensor
for pure electric and pure magnetic observer fields. The component formulas
are conditional on the current draft SI stress-component lemma, whose proof
closure remains unresolved; the example reports no measurement and asserts
no global field solution.
