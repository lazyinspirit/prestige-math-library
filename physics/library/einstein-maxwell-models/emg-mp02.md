This page checks a smooth, static, fully coupled Einstein–Maxwell solution family for the selected pressureless charged-dust model, then specializes it to a globally regular spherical density profile. It records the induced-volume proper mass and charge, observer field, curvature, and exact dilute metric and density bounds. The charge-to-mass ratio is finely fixed for this family alone. These are conditional model deductions; they establish neither empirical realization nor dynamical existence, formation, or stability.
