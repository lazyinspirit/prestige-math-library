## Scope

This page fixes the selected Einstein–Maxwell model's SI conventions, the primitive and derived roles of its electromagnetic quantities, and the observer-dependent electric and magnetic fields on an oriented Lorentzian spacetime. It adopts the smooth curved-vacuum Maxwell system as a physical postulate and checks, by local component calculations, that its flat specialization matches the inherited SI Maxwell equations and Lorentz force/power density.

The flat component calculation is conditional on the stated Maxwell and Lorentz-coupling postulates. It does not derive the curved field law from special relativity, turn a nonzero prescribed field on Minkowski space into a coupled Einstein solution, or make an empirical claim. The current SI status of $\mu_0$ as experimentally determined is retained without assigning it an exact numerical value.
