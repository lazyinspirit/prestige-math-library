## representation-theory

Partitions, tableaux and permutation modules open the way to Specht modules; branching and the hook-length formula with Robinson–Schensted prepare the character dictionary through symmetric functions, the Hall inner product and Schur bases; Jucys–Murphy elements and seminormal forms lead to integral Specht modules and their modular simples, while Plancherel measure governs asymptotic Young diagrams. Outer products and skew Specht modules with Littlewood–Richardson coefficients organise induction and restriction; Kazhdan–Lusztig bases, polynomials and cells carry the combinatorics into Hecke algebras; quantized enveloping algebras and quantum Serre relations open the quantum-group track.
