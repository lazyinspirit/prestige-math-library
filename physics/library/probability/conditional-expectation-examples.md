Finite partition and discrete fibre calculations make the defining event identity concrete. Trivial and full conditioning, an independent sum, least-squares prediction and total variance are evaluated on explicit finite probability spaces.

Two null-atom counterexamples distinguish arbitrary pointwise versions from almost-sure classes; strict almost-sure order remains valid. A countable atomic example shows why an unbounded known factor needs an integrable product: both factors are integrable, while the product has infinite positive and negative integrals.
