These examples distinguish three claims: residual divergence at one point in a space of continuous functions, almost-everywhere divergence of an integrable function, and divergence in the $L^1$ norm. Uniform Fejer convergence can coexist with unbounded ordinary sums at zero. Fejer kernels also give a local refutation of strong $(1,1)$ boundedness, independently of the recorded Kolmogorov theorem.
