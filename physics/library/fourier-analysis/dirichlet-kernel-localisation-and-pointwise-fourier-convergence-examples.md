The companion page records the concrete leaves behind the A-page criteria: the
Dirichlet kernel's removable value and sign changes, logarithmic growth of its
$L^1$ norm, the explicit sawtooth Fourier series, a localisation calculation
with compactly supported data away from the evaluation point, and a continuous
logarithmic-modulus counterexample showing that continuity alone does not force
the Dini condition.
