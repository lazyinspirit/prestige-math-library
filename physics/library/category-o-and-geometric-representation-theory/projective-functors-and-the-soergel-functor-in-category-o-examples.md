These examples and counterexamples illustrate the projective-functor and Soergel-functor constructions of [[projective-functors-and-the-soergel-functor-in-category-o]] in the smallest blocks. [[ex-the-coinvariant-algebra-of-s-two-and-s-three-as-graded-regular-representations]] computes the graded regular representation in ranks one and two; [[ex-the-wall-crossing-functor-on-verma-modules-in-rank-one]] and [[ex-wall-crossing-on-projectives-in-the-sl-two-block]] compute wall-crossing on the $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ block, on standards and on projectives; [[ex-the-class-map-and-wall-crossing-action-on-the-grothendieck-group-in-rank-two]] and [[cex-wall-crossing-functors-do-not-commute]] exhibit the non-commutativity of the wall-crossing action in type $A_2$; [[ex-the-soergel-functor-on-a-small-regular-block]] computes the full projective Hom table and the pre-reduction graph ranks; and [[cex-soergel-full-faithfulness-does-not-hold-on-all-category-o-objects]] shows that full faithfulness fails outside the projective-target hypothesis of the Struktursatz.
