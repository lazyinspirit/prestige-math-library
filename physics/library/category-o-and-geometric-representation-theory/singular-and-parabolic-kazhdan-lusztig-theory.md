This page develops the two parabolic Hecke modules attached to a standard
parabolic subgroup and the two categories in which their canonical bases
appear. The spherical and antispherical modules are introduced together with
their standard bases, right actions and bar involutions; the parabolic
Kazhdan–Lusztig bases are then constructed by the same triangular induction as
the ordinary basis, and their coefficients are identified with the ordinary
Kazhdan–Lusztig polynomials: an alternating sum over the Levi Weyl group in the
antispherical case, and the longest-element reduction
$m_{y,x}=p_{w_0^Jy,w_0^Jx}$ in the spherical case.

The second half separates two constructions that are often conflated. The
singular integral block of category $\mathcal O$ is treated through the facet
tensor-weight exclusion lemma and exact translation functors: standards and
costandards translate to standards and costandards, a simple survives exactly
when its label is a minimal coset representative, and singular multiplicities
are spherical parabolic values at one, with the big antidominant projective
carrying every distinct singular standard once. Separately, the genuine
locally finite parabolic category is built from a standard parabolic and its
Levi subalgebra, proved to be a highest weight category with generalized Verma
standards and generalized-standard flags, and its multiplicities are the
antispherical values $n_{y,x}(1)$, with the weighted inverse character
formula. The finite computations are collected on
[[singular-and-parabolic-kazhdan-lusztig-theory-examples]].
