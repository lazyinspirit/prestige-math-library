This page develops the elementary Hecke, Bruhat, bar and triangular-basis constructions, followed by polynomial recursion, inversion and cell theory. The elementary triangular-bar basis theorem supplies every algebraic clause used here without coefficientwise positivity. The full basis theorem including positivity follows from the local Soergel character and filtration-multiplicity argument on [[soergel-intersection-forms-and-hodge-theory]]. The recursion and inverse-basis identities provide the algebraic tools used below.

The final part identifies the type-A cells through Robinson–Schensted. Knuth and dual Knuth moves preserve the insertion and recording tableaux; rank-two star operations transport the Kazhdan–Lusztig graph and left-cell relations. For the two-sided forward implication, Geck’s shape-invariance result is used after identifying the algebra and coefficient-step conventions. The resulting classification states that left cells are the $Q$-fibers, right cells are the $P$-fibers, and two-sided cells are the common-shape fibers. The concrete calculations are collected on [[kazhdan-lusztig-bases-polynomials-and-cells-examples]].
