These examples compute concrete Folner families in abelian groups, isolate a
standard extension argument for the lamplighter group, and show by direct
computation that amenability and subexponential growth are not equivalent. The
free-group examples keep the two standard witnesses of nonamenability visible:
large boundary and paradoxical decomposition.
