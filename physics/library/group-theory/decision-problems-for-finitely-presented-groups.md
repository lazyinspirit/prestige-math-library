This page fixes the algorithmic vocabulary before speaking about solvability.
It keeps the word problem for one fixed finite presentation separate from the
uniform problem and proves positive decision results for free groups,
finitely generated abelian groups, and the specified amalgamated constructions.
The abelian algorithm computes exponent vectors and searches for a Smith
normal form of the finite integer relation matrix. The page also defines
conjugacy, isomorphism and Markov-property problems without asserting general
undecidability theorems.

The final seam defines algebraic relator area and the Dehn function in a way
that stays inside presentations and normal closures. Van Kampen diagrams, their finite enumeration, and the resulting decision
algorithm from a recursive Dehn bound are developed later on
[[asymptotic-cones-and-the-sublinear-triangle-criterion]].
