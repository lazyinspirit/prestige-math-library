These examples keep the degree-one formulas concrete. Trivial actions recover
ordinary homomorphisms, a cyclic source is determined by the value on one
generator, and complements in affine or semidirect-product models can be
written down explicitly.

The final two examples show the genuinely nonabelian and genuinely
non-homomorphic behavior that the A page warns about.
