## Definition

Left, right and two-sided ideals.

Let $R$ be a ring. An additive subgroup $I\le(R,+)$ is a **left ideal** when
$r i\in I$ for every $r\in R$ and $i\in I$, and a **right ideal** when
$i r\in I$ for every such $r,i$.  A **two-sided ideal**, written
$I\mathrel{\trianglelefteq}R$, is both a left and a right ideal.  In a
commutative ring these three notions agree.
