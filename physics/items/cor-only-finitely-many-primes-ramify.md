## Statement

Only finitely many rational primes ramify in a number field.

## Proof

**Given:** the discriminant criterion.

1.1 The field discriminant is a fixed nonzero integer. [given]

2.1 It has only finitely many prime divisors, and the criterion identifies these exactly with the ramified rational primes. [step 1.1, algebra] ∎
