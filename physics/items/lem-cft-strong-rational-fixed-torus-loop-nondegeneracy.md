## Statement

Let $V$ be a simple, self-contragredient conformal vertex algebra of CFT type over $\mathbb C$, admissibly rational and $C_2$-cofinite. Its category of all grading-restricted generalized modules admits the ordinary-intertwiner $P(z)$ braided tensor structure, is finite semisimple and rigid with restricted contragredient duals, and has ribbon twist $\theta_M=\exp(2\pi iL_M(0))$. Its categorical Hopf-link matrix in the convention of [[def-modular-ribbon-category-and-hopf-link-data]] is invertible. Conformal weights may lie in finitely many complex integral cosets; no real-weight assumption is added. If its conformal-block modular functor has been constructed, the category induced by its genus-zero sewing data is equivalent, with these structures, to this category. No numerical identification of the Hopf matrix with character $S$ or character Verlinde formula is asserted.

Consequently every actual coefficient module generated by one generalized-weight vector in the corrected HLZ construction is admitted into C and is semisimple. This supplies the corrected no-log ambient-membership premise, rather than inferring it from a relative tensor-product universal property. Every weak module is the sum of its cyclic submodules and therefore a sum of ordinary simple submodules. By AC choose a maximal independent family of such simple submodules.
