## Definition

For a separated Deligne–Mumford stack $X$ of finite type over a field, let $Z_d(X)$ be the rational vector space generated by integral closed substacks of dimension $d$. The **rational Chow group** $A_d(X)_{\mathbb Q}$ is $Z_d(X)$ modulo rational equivalence in the stack intersection theory of Vistoli; $A_*(X)_{\mathbb Q}=\bigoplus_d A_d(X)_{\mathbb Q}$. This fixes the rational Chow convention used in the following virtual-class construction.

If $E\to X$ is a rank-$r$ vector bundle and $0_E:X\to E$ its zero section, the **zero-section Gysin map** is the refined intersection operation
$
0_E^!:A_d(E)_{\mathbb Q}\longrightarrow A_{d-r}(X)_{\mathbb Q}.
$
It is the bivariant pullback associated with the regular zero section; it lowers cycle dimension by the rank and is compatible with smooth pullback. The Behrend–Fantechi virtual class uses this map only after the intrinsic cone has been represented as a cycle in a vector-bundle presentation.

For a regular closed immersion $i:Z\hookrightarrow Y$ of codimension $c$, the **refined Gysin pullback** $i^!$ is the bivariant Chow operation on any base change of $Y$ along a morphism $Y'\to Y$; it sends a class on $Y'$ to a class on $Z'=Z\times_Y Y'$ and lowers dimension by $c$. For a smooth finite-type scheme $B$ over the field and a closed point $b\in B$, the embedding $b:\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(b)\hookrightarrow B$ is regular of codimension $\dim\mathcal O_{B,b}$. For a smooth family $Y\to B$, its fibre inclusion $Y_b\hookrightarrow Y$ is regular by flat base change. More generally, for any family $M\to B$ in the stated category, the fibre operation used in the PT deformation statement is the refinement $b_M^!$ of this regular base embedding; $M_b\hookrightarrow M$ need not itself be regular. No regular-closed-immersion fibre operation is asserted for nonclosed points of $B$.

## Construction and well-definedness

Under the Axiom of Choice inherited from this supplier, the construction in [[lem-gts-rational-dm-chow-and-refined-gysin-construction]] specifies the rational-equivalence relation here as the rational span of the principal divisor cycles $\operatorname{div}_W(h)$, where $W$ is an integral closed substack of one higher dimension and $h\in k(W)^*$. It proves finite-scheme-cover descent of this quotient, including generic inertia degree factors, and proves vector-bundle homotopy invariance on this quotient. Thus the unrefined zero-section operation is the inverse of $p^*:A_{d-r}(X)_{\mathbb Q}\to A_d(E)_{\mathbb Q}$.

For every base map $T\to Y$ in the separated finite-type DM category, the same supplier constructs the deformation algebra $\mathcal O_T[t,I_T/t]$, its special normal-cone fiber, the well-defined specialization $\sigma_{Z_T/T}$, and the cone inclusion $a_T:C_{Z_T}T\hookrightarrow N_{Z/Y}|_{Z_T}$. It then defines the refined operation by $(p_T^*)^{-1}(a_T)_*\sigma_{Z_T/T}$ and proves its proper, flat and Cartier compatibilities. Restriction along arbitrary further base maps means reindexing this family; it requires neither a general Chow pullback along arbitrary maps nor regularity of the base-changed immersion. For the closed-point assertion in the Definition, the regular local ring $\mathcal O_{B,b}$ has a regular system of parameters generating its maximal ideal. These parameters define the point immersion near $b$, and flat pullback preserves their regularity for a smooth family. For a possibly nonflat moduli family, the operation is instead the supplier's refinement of that base immersion.
