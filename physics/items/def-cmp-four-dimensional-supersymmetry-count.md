## Definition

Let $M_4=\mathbb R^4$ with coordinates $x^0,x^1,x^2,x^3$, Lorentzian metric $\eta=-(dx^0)^2+\sum_{j=1}^3(dx^j)^2$, orientation $dx^0\wedge dx^1\wedge dx^2\wedge dx^3$, and time orientation $\partial_{x^0}$. Its orthonormal frame transformations preserving both orientations form $SO^+(1,3)$; write $\operatorname{Spin}^+(1,3)$ for its connected double cover.

For this signature, let $\operatorname{Cl}_{1,3}^{\mathbb C}$ be the complex algebra generated by $\gamma_0,\ldots,\gamma_3$ subject to $\gamma_\mu\gamma_\nu+\gamma_\nu\gamma_\mu=2\eta_{\mu\nu}I$. A complex Dirac module $S_4^{\mathbb C}$ is a four-dimensional irreducible module for this algebra, with the induced $\operatorname{Spin}^+(1,3)$ action. Supply a $\operatorname{Spin}^+(1,3)$-equivariant antilinear involution $J:S_4^{\mathbb C}\to S_4^{\mathbb C}$, meaning $J(\lambda s)=\overline\lambda J(s)$ and $J^2=I$. Define the real Majorana module $S_4^{\mathrm M}:=\{s\in S_4^{\mathbb C}:J(s)=s\}$. It has real dimension $4$: for every $s\in S_4^{\mathbb C}$, both $(s+J(s))/2$ and $(s-J(s))/(2i)$ lie in $S_4^{\mathrm M}$ and $s$ is the first plus $i$ times the second. A four-dimensional Majorana supersymmetry parameter is a vector in $S_4^{\mathrm M}$; over $M_4$ its spacetime-dependent version is a section of the associated bundle for a supplied spin structure.

A **four-dimensional supersymmetry count** $N\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}$ is the number of independent copies of $S_4^{\mathrm M}$ in a specified parameter space $\mathcal Q\cong(S_4^{\mathrm M})^{\oplus N}$ as real $\operatorname{Spin}^+(1,3)$-modules. Equivalently, $\dim_{\mathbb R}\mathcal Q=4N$ for this specified direct-sum module. In this convention the theory is called $N$-extended; in particular, $32$ real dimensions of such parameters correspond to $N=8$. This is a naming convention for a stated supersymmetry-parameter space; it does not assert that such a space exists in a given compactification.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The counting convention in the Definition.

[A1] The symbol $N$ counts copies of the supplied real four-dimensional Majorana module by definition; the convention makes no dynamical or existence assertion.

## Proof

**Given:** The real dimension of a four-dimensional supersymmetry-parameter space is $4N$ under [A1].

2.1 Dividing by $4$ yields $N=8$. This verifies the numerical translation used on the examples page. [A1, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
