## Example

Assume AC. Let $T=\mathbb R/\mathbb Z$ act on $M=\mathbb{CP}^1$ by $t\cdot[z_0:z_1]=[z_0:e^{2\pi it}z_1]$. For $k\in\{\mathbb Q,\mathbb R\}$, use the product Milnor Borel model and let $u=c_1(L_\chi)\in H^2(BT;k)$ for $\chi(t)=e^{2\pi it}$. The fixed set is $F=\{p_+,p_-\}$, where $p_+=[1:0]$ and $p_-=[0:1]$; the tangent weights are $+1$ at $p_+$ and $-1$ at $p_-$. If $\mathscr S_T(k)$ is generated by nontrivial character classes and $A_T(k)=H^*(BT;k)$, then restriction identifies
$$
\mathscr S_T(k)^{-1}H_T^*(\mathbb{CP}^1;k)\cong \mathscr S_T(k)^{-1}A_T(k)\oplus\mathscr S_T(k)^{-1}A_T(k),
$$
with the two summands given by evaluation at $p_+$ and $p_-$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC, the circle action on $\mathbb{CP}^1$, the coefficient field $k$, and the product Milnor Borel model.

[A1] Assume AC, as required by the character-localization and Borel localization suppliers. [[def-axiom-of-choice]]

[F1] The character localization definition gives $A_T(k)=k[u]$, with $u$ the degree-two class of the weight-one character, and $-u$ is the class of the inverse character. Both coefficient cases are supplied under [A1]. [[def-qftx-character-localization]]

[F2] The fixed-point and normal-weight conventions are those of the compact torus fixed-locus lemma. [[lem-qftx-compact-torus-fixed-locus-normal-weights]]

[F3] Under AC, for a smooth action of a product torus on a compact finite-dimensional Hausdorff second-countable smooth manifold without boundary, restriction to the fixed set becomes an isomorphism after inverting the nontrivial character classes. [[thm-borel-localization-for-compact-torus-actions]]

## Verification

**Given:** AC and the action and conventions in the Example.

1.1 A point $[z_0:z_1]$ is fixed by all $t\in T$ only if one homogeneous coordinate is zero: if both were nonzero, equality $[z_0:e^{2\pi it}z_1]=[z_0:z_1]$ would force $e^{2\pi it}=1$ for every $t$. Thus the fixed points are $p_+$ and $p_-$. In the local coordinate $w=z_1/z_0$ at $p_+$, the action is $w\mapsto e^{2\pi it}w$. In $v=z_0/z_1$ at $p_-$, it is $v\mapsto e^{-2\pi it}v$. These give the stated weights and character classes $u$ and $-u$, both inverted by $\mathscr S_T(k)$. [F1, F2, algebra]

2.1 The standard projective line is a compact real two-dimensional Hausdorff second-countable smooth manifold without boundary, and the displayed chart actions are jointly smooth. Thus the hypotheses of [F3] hold under [A1]. Since $F$ is a pair of fixed points, $ET\times_TF=(BT\times\{p_+\})\amalg(BT\times\{p_-\})$, so $H_T^*(F;k)=A_T(k)\oplus A_T(k)$. Localizing this finite sum gives the displayed target, and [F3] identifies the restriction map with the asserted isomorphism. [A1, F1, F3, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
