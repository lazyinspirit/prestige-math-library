## Statement

Assume AC. Let $P$ be any probability law on the simple locally finite positional configuration space $\Omega$ of [[def-tdmath-poisson-configuration-reference]], and let $\nu$ be a probability on the momentum mark space $\mathbb R^d$. Use the marked configuration space $\Omega^{\rm mark}$ and local restrictions $\xi_\Delta$ of [[def-csm-marked-periodic-continuation]]. Define translations by $T_yq=\{x+y:x\in q\}$ and $T_y^{\rm mark}\xi=\{(x+y,p):(x,p)\in\xi\}$. Then there is a unique probability law $P^{\rm mark}$ on $\Omega^{\rm mark}$ whose positional projection is $P$ and whose marks, conditional on the entire positional configuration, are independent with common law $\nu$. If $P$ is translation invariant, meaning $(T_y)_\#P=P$ for every $y\in\mathbb R^d$, then $(T_y^{\rm mark})_\#P^{\rm mark}=P^{\rm mark}$ for every $y$. For the Gaussian $\nu_b$ of [[def-csm-full-marked-model]], this is the independent Gaussian-marked positional lift. This is a mathematical marking construction, not a physical preparation or a convergence result.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A probability law P on Ω and a probability mark law ν on R^d.

[A1] AC is required by the standard-Borel coordinate extension used below ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]).

[F1] [[def-tdmath-poisson-configuration-reference]]: Ω has countably many unit-cell coordinates; each cell is a finite configuration represented by its count and lexicographically ordered point list, and its sigma-algebra is generated by Borel count maps.

[F2] [[def-csm-marked-periodic-continuation]]: Ω^mark has the marked-count sigma-algebra, positional projection, and finite-region restrictions ξ_Δ used in the statement.

[F3] [[lem-csm-gaussian-mark-moments]]: in the marked model, the normalized Gaussian kernel ν_b is a probability on R^d.

[F4] [[thm-kolmogorov-extension-for-standard-borel-coordinate-spaces]]: the consistent finite-dimensional product laws ν^{⊗J}, for finite J in a countable index set, extend uniquely to a probability on the countable product.

[F5] [[thm-sigma-finite-product-measure-exists-is-rectangular-and-is-unique]] and [[thm-tonelli-theorem-for-sigma-finite-product-spaces]]: product with P exists, and section integrals of nonnegative measurable functions are measurable.

## Proof

**Given:** The positional probability P and mark probability ν above.

1.1 Let I=Z^d×N index one potential mark slot for each cell and each within-cell rank. For finite J⊂I prescribe the mark law ν^{⊗J}. These laws are consistent under coordinate restriction and are probabilities because ν is a probability by the Given hypothesis, so [F4] gives a probability ν^I under [A1]. The product probability P⊗ν^I exists by [F5]. [given, A1, F4, F5, construct]

2.1 For each cell i, write its finite positional list as x_{i,1},...,x_{i,N_i}, in the measurable lexicographic code of [F1]. Define Ψ(q,p) by pairing x_{i,k} with p_{i,k} for every i and 1≤k≤N_i(q). Every bounded spatial region meets finitely many unit cells, and each list is finite, so Ψ(q,p) is locally finite and simple in its positions. By [F2], it belongs to Ω^mark. For bounded Borel spatial A and Borel momentum set C, its marked count is the finite sum
$$N_{A\times C}(\Psi(q,p))=\sum_{i:C_i\cap A\ne\varnothing}\sum_{k=1}^{N_i(q)}1_{\{x_{i,k}\in A\}}1_{\{p_{i,k}\in C\}}.$$
Each summand is measurable in the cell code and mark coordinate by [F1, F4], so Ψ is measurable for the sigma-algebra generated by these counts from [F2]. Its pushforward of P⊗ν^I is the required law P^mark. [F1, F2, F4, F5, step 1.1, construct]

3.1 Given q, each occupied point uses a distinct coordinate of the product ν^I, so its finite mark families have product law ν^{⊗J}, independent of q. This proves the conditional independence property and fixes every finite marked-count cylinder probability. Those cylinders generate the marked configuration sigma-algebra by [F2], so any law with the same position marginal and conditional product kernel agrees with the pushforward in step 2.1 on a generating class and is identical. Thus the lift exists and is unique. By [F3], the Gaussian kernel ν_b of the model is an admissible probability choice for ν. For each y, the map T_y^mark is measurable because it sends the count N_{A×C} to N_{(A-y)×C}. If P is translation invariant, translating the constructed law by y leaves its positional marginal P and its conditional independent mark kernel ν unchanged; uniqueness therefore gives (T_y^mark)_#P^mark=P^mark. [F1, F2, F3, F4, given, step 1.1, step 2.1] ∎
