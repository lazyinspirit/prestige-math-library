## Postulate

Adopt the following Euclidean source model in the conventions stated here. Let $r>0$ have units of length and let
$$S^4_r=\{y\in\mathbb R^5:\sum_{j=1}^5 y_j^2=r^2\}$$
have its round metric, standard orientation, and scalar curvature $12/r^2$. In stereographic coordinates $x^\mu$ of length, $\mu=1,\dots,4$, the metric is
$$ds^2=\frac{\sum_{\mu=1}^4(dx^\mu)^2}{(1+x^2/(4r^2))^2},\qquad x^2=\sum_{\mu=1}^4(x^\mu)^2.$$
Use the standard spin structure and the spin connection induced by the Levi-Civita connection of the round metric. Identify the four real scalar directions $5,6,7,8$ with $\mathbb H$ in the ordered basis $(1,i,\mathbf j,i\mathbf j)$. The source's internal $SU(2)_R^L\times SU(2)_R^R$ is the two-factor cover of $SO(4)$ acting on these scalar directions by $q\mapsto u_Rqu_L^{-1}$ and fixing scalar directions $0,9$; the labels $L,R$ follow the source's spinor representations. Give the internal four-plane its Euclidean metric and the orientation of the ordered basis above. If $f^5,\ldots,f^8$ is its dual basis, identify a skew matrix $M$ with $\alpha_M=\tfrac12\sum_{A,B=5}^8M_{AB}f^A\wedge f^B$ and define its Hodge star on two-forms by $*(f^A\wedge f^B)=\tfrac12\sum_{C,D=5}^8\varepsilon_{ABCD}f^C\wedge f^D$, extended linearly, where $\varepsilon$ is the fully antisymmetric symbol with $\varepsilon_{5678}=1$. The $SU(2)_R^L$ infinitesimal action $q\mapsto-qa$, $a\in\operatorname{Im}\mathbb H$, has $*\alpha_M=-\alpha_M$ (anti-self-duality), whereas $SU(2)_R^R$ acts by $q\mapsto aq$ with $*\alpha_M=\alpha_M$ (self-duality). For example, $a=i$ in the first action gives $\alpha_M=f^5\wedge f^6-f^7\wedge f^8$. Thus the source's anti-self-dual $R^Q$ belongs to $\operatorname{Lie}(SU(2)_R^L)$ in these conventions. Set $SU(2)_R:=SU(2)_R^L$, take $P_R=S^4_r\times SU(2)_R$ with zero connection, and use the selected $SU(2)_R$ generator below. The global gauge group is $G=SU(2)$, not $SO(3)$; its Lie algebra consists of anti-Hermitian $2\times2$ traceless matrices and has positive invariant form $\kappa(X,Y)=-2\operatorname{Tr}_2(XY)$, normalized by $\kappa(-i\sigma_a/2,-i\sigma_b/2)=\delta_{ab}$. Let $\Sigma$ be the declared set of bundle-sector codes for smooth right principal $SU(2)$-bundles over $S^4_r$, using the nonempty minimum-rank set code of each smooth equivariant isomorphism type. As part of the sectorwise model data, supply a set-indexed family of representative bundles $P_s\to S^4_r$ with $[P_s]=s$ for $s\in\Sigma$; each $s$ is a separate gauge sector. The family is supplied data, not a choice asserted to exist for an arbitrary declared set $\Sigma$. The sector set and any sum over it are formal model inputs, not a classification or measure construction. For a chosen sector $s$, abbreviate its supplied representative $P_s$ by $P$ and write $\operatorname{ad}P=P\times_{\operatorname{Ad}}\mathfrak{su}(2)$ with $\operatorname{Ad}_uX=uXu^{-1}$. Set $E_{\mathrm{aux}}=S^4_r\times\mathbb R^7$ with its standard Euclidean inner product and fixed global orthonormal frame $e_1,\ldots,e_7$. The auxiliary field in sector $P$ is $K=\sum_iK_i e_i$, where each $K_i$ is a smooth section of $\operatorname{ad}P$; all contractions over $i$ use this fixed fiber metric.

The matter content is the massless four-dimensional $\mathcal N=4$ vector multiplet: one gauge connection $A_\mu$, six real adjoint scalar fields $\Phi_A$ with $A\in\{0,5,6,7,8,9\}$, and four adjoint Weyl fermions. Equivalently, its flat-space component content is one $\mathcal N=1$ vector multiplet and three adjoint $\mathcal N=1$ chiral multiplets. Set the topological $\theta$-term to zero in this selected model. Use the ordinary trace $\operatorname{Tr}_2$ in the defining representation of $SU(2)$ and a positive dimensionless coupling $g_{\mathrm{YM}}$. The current N=4 model carrier uses the same global group, $\kappa(X,Y)=-2\operatorname{Tr}_2(XY)$, the same dimensionless coupling, and connection-normalized adjoint chiral fields, with one vector and three adjoint chiral multiplets; this is an interface match for flat-space component content and normalization. This postulate separately adopts the source’s curved $S^4$ action, curvature coupling, selected-spinor algebra, gauge fixing, contour, and formal regulator inputs; it does not identify that curved action with the supplier’s flat Minkowski superspace action. For the dimensional-reduction notation, in a local gauge trivialization define the Lie-algebra-valued components $F_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu A_\nu-\partial_\nu A_\mu+[A_\mu,A_\nu]$, $F_{\mu A}=D_\mu\Phi_A$, and $F_{AB}=[\Phi_A,\Phi_B]$, extended antisymmetrically to $F_{MN}$. Here $D_\mu X=\nabla_\mu^{\mathrm{LC}}X+[A_\mu,X]$ on adjoint tensor fields, with the supplied spin connection on spinor indices, and $D_A X=[\Phi_A,X]$; in the fermion term $D_\mu\Psi$ includes the spin and gauge connections. These $F_{MN}$ are Lie-algebra-valued matrices, so $\operatorname{Tr}_2$ is taken in the defining representation. Use $\eta_{MN}=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,\dots,1)$ for $M,N=0,\dots,9$. With the seven localization auxiliaries included, the adopted classical action input is
$$S_{\mathrm{off}}=-\frac{1}{g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2}\int_{S^4_r}d^4x\,\sqrt g\,\operatorname{Tr}_2\left(\frac12\sum_{M,N}F_{MN}F^{MN}-\Psi\Gamma^M D_M\Psi+\frac{2}{r^2}\sum_A\Phi_A\Phi^A-\sum_{i=1}^7K_iK_i\right).$$
Here $\Psi$ is the adjoint ten-dimensional Majorana–Weyl fermion used to package the four-dimensional fields; after Euclidean continuation it is complex and has no independent conjugate field. The chiral gamma matrices obey $\Gamma_M\widetilde\Gamma_N+\Gamma_N\widetilde\Gamma_M=2\eta_{MN}I_{16}$, with $\widetilde\Gamma_0=-\Gamma_0$ and $\widetilde\Gamma_M=\Gamma_M$ for $M=1,\dots,9$; $\Gamma_{MN}:=\widetilde\Gamma_{[M}\Gamma_{N]}$ acts on $S^+$ and $\widetilde\Gamma_{MN}:=\Gamma_{[M}\widetilde\Gamma_{N]}$ acts on $S^-$. Brackets on two indices have weight $1/2$. The blocks are symmetric and $(\Gamma_{MN})^{\mathsf T}=-\widetilde\Gamma_{MN}$ in the source basis. A spinor bilinear $\zeta B\chi$ means $\zeta^{\mathsf T}B\chi$, with no Hermitian conjugation; the background spinors commute, while fermion components anticommute. The four sphere directions have positive metric. The term with $2/r^2$ is the conformal curvature coupling of the six scalars. The Yang–Mills coupling is dimensionless, $[A_\mu]=[\Phi_A]=\mathrm{length}^{-1}$, $[K_i]=[F_{MN}]=\mathrm{length}^{-2}$, and $[\Psi]=\mathrm{length}^{-3/2}$.

Let $\widehat\epsilon_s=(0_8,1,0_7)^{\mathsf T}$ be the normalized sixteen-component chiral spinor in the source gamma convention, with $\Gamma_{5678}\widehat\epsilon_s=\widehat\epsilon_s$ and $\Gamma_{1234}\widehat\epsilon_s=-\widehat\epsilon_s$, and set $\widehat\epsilon_c=(2r)^{-1}\Gamma_{012}\widehat\epsilon_s$. With $\Omega(x)=1+x^2/(4r^2)$, adopt
$$\epsilon(x)=\Omega(x)^{-1/2}\bigl(\widehat\epsilon_s+x^i\widetilde\Gamma_i\widehat\epsilon_c\bigr),\qquad \widetilde\epsilon(x)=\Omega(x)^{-1/2}\left(\widehat\epsilon_c-\frac{x^i}{4r^2}\Gamma_i\widehat\epsilon_s\right),\quad i=1,\dots,4.$$
They solve the conformal Killing-spinor system
$$\nabla_\mu\epsilon=\widetilde\Gamma_\mu\widetilde\epsilon,\qquad \nabla_\mu\widetilde\epsilon=-\frac{1}{4r^2}\Gamma_\mu\epsilon.$$
The normalized spinor $\widehat\epsilon_s$ and the resulting $\epsilon$ are dimensionless, while $\widehat\epsilon_c$ and $\widetilde\epsilon$ have mass dimension one. Here $Q_\epsilon$ denotes the supercharge operator of mass dimension $1/2$ selected by this normalized spinor; an infinitesimal physical transformation uses a separate odd coefficient of mass dimension $-1/2$. Accordingly, $Q_\epsilon^2$, $v^\mu\partial_\mu$, $R^Q$, and the selected generator $\omega$ have mass dimension one, and the flow parameter for $v$ has mass dimension $-1$.
After Euclidean continuation, put $\Delta_\pm=\mathbb C^2$ with the defining $\operatorname{Spin}(4)$ representations $\pi_\pm$, let $V_R=\mathbb C^2$ with the defining representation $\tau$ of $SU(2)_R$, and let $V_0=\mathbb C^2$ with the trivial $SU(2)_R$ representation. Define the sixteen-dimensional complex chiral module and its smooth representation by
$$W_\epsilon=(\Delta_+\oplus\Delta_-)\otimes(V_R\oplus V_0),\qquad \rho_\epsilon(s,k)=(\pi_+(s)\oplus\pi_-(s))\otimes(\tau(k)\oplus I_{V_0}).$$
Thus $\rho_\epsilon:\operatorname{Spin}(4)\times SU(2)_R\to\operatorname{GL}_{\mathbb C}(W_\epsilon)$ is the restriction of the continued source chiral module: each four-dimensional spin chirality occurs once with the defining $SU(2)_R^L$ doublet and once with a two-dimensional multiplicity space trivial under $SU(2)_R^L$. The spinor/R field bundle is $(\widetilde F\times_{S^4}P_R)\times_{\rho_\epsilon}W_\epsilon$ with quotient relation $[(\widetilde p,p_R)h,z]=[(\widetilde p,p_R),\rho_\epsilon(h)z]$ for $h\in\operatorname{Spin}(4)\times SU(2)_R$. In local product sections its derivative is $d\epsilon_i+(d\rho_\epsilon)_e(A^S_i,A^R_i)\epsilon_i$, where $(d\rho_\epsilon)_e$ is the differential at the identity. The spin connection in $A_i^S$ is induced by the round metric's Levi-Civita connection; because the R bundle is trivial with zero connection, the $\nabla_\mu$ in this system has zero R-connection term.
The selected spinors obey the source's same-chirality conditions, in particular $\widetilde\epsilon\epsilon=0$, so the dilation term in the supercharge square vanishes. Their bilinear $v^\mu=\epsilon\Gamma^\mu\epsilon$ is the source-normalized infinitesimal generator of the $U(1)$ isometry rotating the $(x^1,x^2)$ and $(x^3,x^4)$ planes equally; no further rescaling of this generator is made below. The corresponding internal R-symmetry generator is the skew matrix $R^Q_{AB}=2\epsilon\widetilde\Gamma_{AB}\widetilde\epsilon$ on the six scalar indices and acts by $(\delta_R\Phi)_A=R^Q_{AB}\Phi^B$. The fermion action is $\delta_R\Psi=\tfrac14R^Q_{AB}\Gamma^{AB}\Psi$ with the source reduction metric used to raise indices. These formulas adopt the ordering in Pestun (2.9); no interchange of the two commuting background spinors is assumed. In particular the transpose identity would give $2\widetilde\epsilon\Gamma_{AB}\epsilon=-R^Q_{AB}$, so that expression is not substituted with a plus sign. The gauge connection and Faddeev--Popov ghosts are R-singlets.

In the fixed global frame $e_i$ of $E_{\mathrm{aux}}$ above, take seven smooth spinor sections $\nu_i$; equivalently, $\sum_i\nu_i\otimes e^i$ is a section of the spinor bundle tensored with $E_{\mathrm{aux}}^*$. They satisfy

Adopt the off-shell transformations
$$Q_\epsilon A_M=\Psi\Gamma_M\epsilon,\qquad Q_\epsilon\Psi=\frac12\Gamma^{MN}F_{MN}\epsilon+\frac12\Gamma^{\mu A}\Phi_A\nabla_\mu\epsilon+\sum_iK_i\nu_i,\qquad Q_\epsilon K_i=-\nu_i\Gamma^M D_M\Psi.$$
With $v^M=\epsilon\Gamma^M\epsilon$, the source writes the off-shell square as $Q_\epsilon^2=-\mathscr L^{\mathrm{cov}}_v-\delta_R-\delta_\Omega$. On adjoint scalars $\mathscr L^{\mathrm{cov}}_vX=v^\mu D_\mu X+[v^A\Phi_A,X]$; on the connection it is $(\mathscr L^{\mathrm{cov}}_vA)_\mu=v^\nu F_{\nu\mu}-D_\mu(v^A\Phi_A)$. On spinors it includes the geometric spin lift, and on the seven auxiliary components it is the exact operator in Pestun (2.30), with its auxiliary-index rotation. Thus this source operator already contains the gauge connection; it must not be added to a second gauge action. The equivalent split into a separately chosen geometric lift and a gauge transformation is specified next. The dilation parameter is $2\widetilde\epsilon\epsilon=0$. This off-shell closure is for the selected generator; it does not assert finite-auxiliary closure of the full hypermultiplet algebra.

Write $\alpha_\theta=\mathrm{Fl}_v^\theta$ for the base circle action, parameterized so $\left.\frac{d}{d\theta}\right|_{0}\alpha_\theta=v$. For every declared gauge sector $s\in\Sigma$, with supplied representative $P=P_s$, adopt as additional formal model data a smooth lift $\widehat\alpha_{\theta,P}:P\to P$ of the circle flow generated by $v$, together with compatible lifts to the spin, R, and auxiliary bundle $E_{\mathrm{aux}}$; the auxiliary lift is orthogonal and has infinitesimal index action fixed by $\Xi_{ij}$ below; each $\widehat\alpha_{\theta,P}$ is a principal-bundle automorphism covering the same base isometry. Gauge-chart background connections below are chosen invariant under this lift. This postulate assumes these sectorwise lifts as inputs; it does not prove their existence for every smooth $SU(2)$ bundle. For a field bundle with induced action $\widehat\alpha_\theta^*$, define $\mathcal L_v^{\mathrm{lift}}\xi=\left.\frac{d}{d\theta}\right|_{\theta=0}\widehat\alpha_\theta^*\xi$. In a local gauge frame let $\beta_{v,P}$ be the even infinitesimal vertical coefficient of this fixed lift, characterized on adjoint scalar fields by $\mathcal L_v^{\mathrm{lift}}X=v^\mu\partial_\mu X+[\beta_{v,P},X]$ and on connections by $\mathcal L_v^{\mathrm{lift}}A=\mathcal L_v A-D_A\beta_{v,P}$. Here the unadorned $\mathcal L_v$ is the ordinary Lie derivative of a local one-form. Let $\mu_{v,A}=v^\mu A_\mu-\beta_{v,P}$; this difference is a globally defined adjoint section, equivalently the contraction of the principal connection with the chosen lifted generator in these conventions. Define $\kappa_v=\mu_{v,A}+v^A\Phi_A$ and $\phi=-\kappa_v$. With $\delta_{\mathrm{gauge}}(u)X=[u,X]$ and $\delta_{\mathrm{gauge}}(u)A=-D_Au$, the source operator decomposes as
$$\mathscr L_v^{\mathrm{cov}}=\mathcal L_v^{\mathrm{lift}}+\delta_{\mathrm{gauge}}(\kappa_v),\qquad Q_\epsilon^2=\delta_{\mathrm{gauge}}(\phi)-\mathcal L_v^{\mathrm{lift}}-\delta_R.$$
Indeed, on scalars the vertical coefficients add to $v^\mu A_\mu+v^A\Phi_A$; on a connection the right side is $\mathcal L_v A-D_A(v^\mu A_\mu+v^A\Phi_A)=\iota_vF_A-D_A(v^A\Phi_A)$. Thus no gauge contribution is counted twice. A lift-adapted local frame has $\beta_{v,P}=0$ and recovers the source's local $v^MA_M$ parameter. On spinors the spacetime spin lift is used separately from $\delta_R$. On the auxiliary bundle set
$$\Xi_{ij}=\nu_{[i}\Gamma^\mu\nabla_\mu\nu_{j]},\qquad (\mathcal L_v^{\mathrm{lift}}K)_i=v^\mu\partial_\mu K_i+[\beta_{v,P},K_i]+\Xi_{ij}K_j.$$
The background $\nu_i$ are gauge singlets, so their derivative here is the supplied spin derivative; the internal derivatives vanish in this massless model. The auxiliary-index contribution in Pestun (2.30) is included in this lift and there is no further independent $R$ matrix on $K_i$. The antisymmetrization is $\nu_{[i}\Gamma^\mu\nabla_\mu\nu_{j]}=\tfrac12(\nu_i\Gamma^\mu\nabla_\mu\nu_j-\nu_j\Gamma^\mu\nabla_\mu\nu_i)$.

Choose an invariant background connection $A_*$ as fixed sectorwise data, inert under $s$ and $\delta$, and set $a=A-A_*\in\Omega^1(S^4,\operatorname{ad}P)$; this difference is a globally defined adjoint-valued one-form. In the trivial bundle with $A_*=0$, $\mathfrak h_P$ is the space of constant $\mathfrak{su}(2)$-valued sections and these definitions reduce to $d^*A=0$ and $d^*D_A$.

In particular the factor $i$ in the first term follows from the chosen gauge fermion and $\psi=sA$. For nontrivial $P$, the lift, background-gauge chart, zero-mode complex and this completion are additional formal model inputs; Pestun's trivialization-chart calculation does not construct them.

$$Z_{\mathrm{formal}}=\sum_{[P]\in\Sigma}\frac{1}{\operatorname{vol}(\mathcal G(P),g_{\mathrm{YM}})} \int_{\mathcal C_P}[DA\,D\Phi\,D\Psi\,DK\,Dc\,D\widetilde c\,Db\,Da_0\,D\widetilde a_0\,Db_0\,Dc_0\,D\widetilde c_0]\, e^{-S_{\mathrm{off}}[P]-S_{\mathrm{gf}}^Q[P]}.$$
Here $D\Phi=\prod_{A\in\{0,5,6,7,8,9\}}D\Phi_A$ and $DK=\prod_{i=1}^7DK_i$. The composite $\phi=-\kappa_v$ and the background spinors are not additional independent integration variables, and there is no independent conjugate of $\Psi$. This notation remains formal and constructs no continuum measure. In this sum the index is the sector code $s=[P]\in\Sigma$ and $P$ denotes its supplied representative $P_s$. Here $\mathcal G(P)$ is the smooth gauge-automorphism group of $P$, $\mathcal C_P$ is the declared contour in that sector, and $\operatorname{vol}(\mathcal G(P),g_{\mathrm{YM}})$ is the source's formal gauge-group volume with its invariant measure scaled by the gauge coupling; it is not an actual infinite-dimensional Haar measure. In the Euclidean contour, gauge fields and $\Phi_{5},\dots,\Phi_9$ lie in the real Lie algebra, $\Phi_0=i\Phi_0^E$ with $\Phi_0^E$ real, the seven localization auxiliaries satisfy $K_i=iK_i^E$ with $K_i^E$ real, and $a_0=i a_0^E$ with $a_0^E$ real.
