## Definition

Fix a finite-dimensional complex semisimple Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$, a Cartan subalgebra $\mathfrak h$, and a positive Borel $\mathfrak b=\mathfrak h\oplus\mathfrak n^+$. Let $\{\alpha_i\}_{i\in I}$ be the simple roots of the chosen positive system and write $Q^+=\sum_{i\in I}\mathbb Z_{\geq0}\alpha_i$ and $\mu\leq\lambda$ when $\lambda-\mu\in Q^+$. Let $W$ be the Weyl group of [[def-root-reflections-and-the-weyl-group-action]] and let $\rho$ be the Weyl vector of [[def-weyl-vector-rho-for-a-chosen-positive-system]]; for $w\in W$ set $w\cdot\lambda=w(\lambda+\rho)-\rho$.

The **BGG category $\mathcal O$** is the full subcategory of left $U(\mathfrak g)$-modules $M$ satisfying all three conditions: $M$ is finitely generated; $M=\bigoplus_{\mu\in\mathfrak h^*}M_\mu$ where $M_\mu=\{v:hv=\mu(h)v\text{ for all }h\in\mathfrak h\}$; and $U(\mathfrak n^+)v$ is finite dimensional for each $v\in M$. Its morphisms are all $\mathfrak g$-linear maps. The zero module is included, generated by the empty set. The enveloping and triangular conventions are those of [[thm-pbw-ordered-monomial-basis-for-the-enveloping-algebra]] and [[thm-triangular-decomposition-from-a-chosen-positive-root-system]].
