## Remark

For a $C_2$-cofinite vertex operator algebra $V$ with no nonzero negative-weight states, Huang's genus-one modular-invariance theorem concerns spaces of analytic extensions of pseudo-$q$-traces, shifted by $-c/24$, of products of geometrically modified intertwining operators among grading-restricted generalized modules; the intertwining operators may be logarithmic. Its inputs include a finite-dimensional associative algebra $P$ with a symmetric linear functional; a grading-restricted generalized $V$-$P$ bimodule that is projective as a right $P$-module; and $P$-compatible intertwining operators. These are pseudo-trace correlation functions, not ordinary characters obtained from an ordinary trace on a semisimple $L(0)$-grading.

The 2026 quasi-lisse result has different data. It starts with a charged conformal vertex algebra $(V,\omega,h)$, where $Y(h,z)=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}h_nz^{-n-1}$, $h\in V_1$, $h_0$ acts semisimply on $V$ with integer eigenvalues, $L(1)h=0$, and $h_nh=0$ for $n\geq2$ while $h_1h=2\kappa\mathbf1$ for some $\kappa\in\mathbb C$, and assumes finite strong generation (generation by finitely many states using derivatives and normally ordered products) and stable quasi-lissity. The charge grading defines the charged conformal-block sheaf for a degree-zero line bundle $L$ on an elliptic curve $E$. Here the Zhu Poisson algebra is $R_V=V/C_2(V)$ with its induced commutative product and Poisson bracket; the charge-zero quotient is obtained by killing the ideal generated by nonzero $h_0$-charge subspaces. Stable quasi-lisse means the spectrum of that quotient has finitely many symplectic leaves. The theorem gives a holonomic D-module with regular singularities along a lattice $N\alpha\in\mathbb Z+\mathbb Z\tau$ and an integrable connection away from it. Stable rationality means that the category of finitely generated stable $V$-modules is semisimple; under this additional condition, trace functions of irreducible stable modules span the charged blocks and are flat on $0<-N\operatorname{Im}(\alpha)<\operatorname{Im}(\tau)$, with covariance under the Jacobi group $SL_2(\mathbb Z)\ltimes\mathbb Z^2$.

These are not the finite semisimple character/Verlinde theorem for a strongly rational VOA. Strong rationality here means CFT type, $C_2$-cofiniteness, rationality, and self-contragredience (with simplicity in the workspace convention). The hypotheses and outputs above do not identify a logarithmic pseudo-trace space or a charged elliptic family with that finite semisimple modular-functor statement.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The $C_2$-cofinite and grading-restricted generalized-module conventions of the linked definitions, together with the exact source theorem hypotheses recorded above. No physical or path-integral interpretation is assumed.

[F1] [[def-c2-cofinite-vertex-algebra]]: $C_2(V)=\operatorname{span}\{u_{(-2)}v:u,v\in V\}$ and $C_2$-cofiniteness means $V/C_2(V)$ is finite dimensional.

[F2] [[def-logarithmic-vertex-module]]: logarithmic modules are grading-restricted generalized modules; $L_0$ may have nontrivial Jordan blocks on finite-dimensional generalized weight spaces.

[A1] Huang, Theorem 5.5: assume $V$ is $C_2$-cofinite with no nonzero negative-weight elements; use grading-restricted generalized modules, finite-dimensional associative $P$ with symmetric functional $\varphi$, a grading-restricted generalized $V$-$P$ bimodule projective over $P$, and $P$-compatible intertwining operators. The modular transform of the corresponding genus-one correlation function lies in the same correlation-function space, whose elements have the stated shifted pseudo-$q$-trace expansions and analytic extensions.

[A2] Arakawa–van Ekeren–Li, Theorems 1.1–1.3: use a charged conformal vertex algebra with the current, quasi-primary and integral-charge conditions explicitly stated in the Remark (source §3.1 and Definition 3.4), finite strong generation, and stable quasi-lissity; stable rationality is additionally required for the trace-spanning and Jacobi-equivariance conclusions. The family is over degree-zero line bundles on elliptic curves and excludes the source's singular charge lattice from the vector-bundle locus.

## Proof

1.1 By [F1], the C$_2$-cofinite condition is the stated finite quotient condition, while [A1] imposes the additional generalized-module and symmetric pseudo-trace inputs, including projectivity and intertwining-operator compatibility. Huang's theorem transforms these correlation functions, so its conclusion concerns analytic extensions of shifted pseudo-$q$-traces and does not reduce to ordinary irreducible characters. [given, F1, F2, A1, algebra]

2.1 Under [A2], the charge current, integral charge grading, line bundle on the elliptic curve, stable quasi-lisse condition, and (for trace spanning) stable rationality are explicit hypotheses. Its outputs are charged conformal-block D-modules and Jacobi-group covariance on the stated domain. The line-bundle and charge data are part of this conclusion and cannot be removed while retaining the same theorem. [given, A2, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 The conclusions in steps 1.1 and 2.1 have different inputs and different output spaces from the finite semisimple character/Verlinde theorem under strong rationality. Neither [A1] nor [A2] asserts that theorem or supplies its finite semisimple module category. The remark records this scope separation and makes no implication from one regime to the other. [given, A1, A2, step 1.1, step 2.1, algebra] ∎
