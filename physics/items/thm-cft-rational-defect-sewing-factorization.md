## Statement

In the oriented FRS construction, for a complex modular ribbon fusion category $\mathcal C$ and the simple symmetric special Frobenius algebra, boundary-module, and defect-bimodule data of the preceding definition, the assigned full-CFT correlators factorize across a bulk circle cut crossed by a topological defect $X$. The factorization is the finite sum over simple chiral labels and defect-field morphism bases weighted by the quantum dimensions and the inverse defect two-point matrix. The corresponding boundary-defect factorization identity holds for a cut crossing a defect between the declared boundary conditions, with its reduction to defect-free boundary factorization as stated in the source.

Assume full AC and its inherited countable-choice consequence. Use the exact weighted RT/TFT construction, including its Lagrangian/framing weight and Maslov correction; no anomaly-free functor is asserted without separate anomaly-cancellation data. Every region is labeled by a simple symmetric special Frobenius algebra; boundaries carry left modules; an oriented internal defect from an $A$-region to a $B$-region is an $A$-$B$-bimodule. All worldsheets and cuts are oriented, every defect crossed by a cut is topological, and the sphere, disk, and defect two-point pairings that occur are nondegenerate.

For an oriented FRS worldsheet $\Sigma$, let $\widehat\Sigma$ be its oriented double, $M_\Sigma$ its connecting weighted RT cobordism with the labeled ribbon graph, and $C(\Sigma)=Z_{\mathcal C}(M_\Sigma)(1)\in V_{\mathcal C}(\widehat\Sigma)$ its assigned correlator, where $Z_{\mathcal C}(M_\Sigma):\mathbb C\to V_{\mathcal C}(\widehat\Sigma)$ and $1\in\mathbb C=V_{\mathcal C}(\varnothing)$. For either cut, let $n_\Sigma$ and $n_{\Sigma'}$ be the weights of its original and factorized connecting cobordisms, and let $\mu_W$ be the actual Maslov correction for the gluing from [F3]. The gluing cobordism is assigned the unique integer weight $n_G$ satisfying $n_G+n_{\Sigma'}+\mu_W=n_\Sigma$. This prescription matches the integer weight of every sewn term with that of the original connecting cobordism; equality of the correlators is the finite local ribbon-graph resolution asserted below, not equality of each individual decorated sewn cobordism with the original one. In the bulk case the cut circle has a collar crossed by one topological $A$-$A$ defect $X$. For $p,q$ in the finite simple-label set, $\Sigma_{p,q,\alpha,\beta}$ is obtained by cutting and capping with the dual defect fields $\alpha,\beta$, and $G_{\ell,pq}:V_{\mathcal C}(\widehat\Sigma_{p,q})\to V_{\mathcal C}(\widehat\Sigma)$ is the map assigned to this weighted RT gluing cobordism. The sphere defect two-point matrix $c^{\mathrm{def}}_{X,\bar p,\bar q}$ is the matrix of the RT correlator on the sphere with the paired fields specified by the oriented FRS data. Then
$$ C(\Sigma)=\sum_{p,q\in I}\sum_{\alpha,\beta} d_p d_q\bigl(c^{\mathrm{def}}_{X,\bar p,\bar q}\bigr)^{-1}_{\alpha\beta} \,G_{\ell,pq}\!\left(C(\Sigma_{p,q,\alpha,\beta})\right), $$
where $d_p=\operatorname{tr}_j(\operatorname{id}_{U_p})$, $\alpha,\beta$ range over bases of the paired defect-field spaces, and the inverse matrix uses the FRS source ordering.

In the boundary case, the cut arc is crossed by one topological $A$-$B$ defect $X$, and the adjacent boundary conditions are a left $B$-module $M_\ell$ and a left $A$-module $M_r$. The factorized worldsheet $\Sigma_{i,\alpha,\beta}$ is obtained by cutting along the arc and inserting paired fields from the boundary defect-field spaces; $G_{\ell,i}^{\mathrm{bnd}}$ is the map assigned to this weighted boundary gluing cobordism. If $c^{\mathrm{bdef}}_{M_\ell,M_r,X,i}$ is the disk boundary-defect two-point matrix on those fields, then
$$ C(\Sigma)=\sum_{i\in I}\sum_{\alpha,\beta} d_i\bigl(c^{\mathrm{bdef}}_{M_\ell,M_r,X,i}\bigr)^{-1}_{\beta\alpha} \,G_{\ell,i}^{\mathrm{bnd}}\!\left(C(\Sigma_{i,\alpha,\beta})\right). $$
This boundary-defect identity reduces by local fusion of $X$ into the boundary module to the defect-free boundary factorization formula. These are statements about the abstract oriented rational FRS construction; they assert neither analytic VOA sewing nor a physical path-integral measure.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The two cut geometries and the algebra, module, bimodule, field-space, pivotal-trace, and weighted RT/TFT conventions in the Statement. The sphere, disk, and defect two-point matrices that are inverted are nondegenerate on the precise field spaces just defined.

[L1] [[def-cft-mtc-symmetric-special-frobenius-defect-data]]: each region is labeled by a simple symmetric special Frobenius algebra $A$; a boundary label is a left $A$-module; an internal defect is an $A$-$B$-bimodule; defect fields and two-point matrices are the specified module/bimodule Hom spaces and RT correlators. The special normalization is $m\Delta=\operatorname{id}_A$, $\epsilon\eta=d_A\operatorname{id}_{\mathbf1}$ with $d_A\ne0$, and the pivotal duality is fixed.

[F1] [[def-modular-ribbon-category-and-hopf-link-data]]: $\mathcal C$ is finite semisimple with a finite simple-label set; the pivotal map is $j=u\theta$, the trace is $\operatorname{tr}_j(f)=\operatorname{Tr}_L(jf)$, $d_i=\operatorname{tr}_j(\operatorname{id}_{U_i})$, and the Hopf-link matrix is invertible. No positivity of $d_i$ is assumed.

[F2] [[lem-simple-ribbon-fusion-dimensions-are-nonzero]]: $d_i\ne0$ for every simple $U_i$. The proof uses the simple unit summand in $U_i\otimes U_i^\vee$, the nonzero evaluation and coevaluation from the duality snakes, and the pivotal trace formula.

[F4] [[lem-cft-separability-projector-realizes-relative-tensor-product]]: for a special Frobenius algebra $B$, the separability projector on $X\otimes M$ splits with image $X\otimes_B M$ and carries the descended left $A$-action.

[F3] [[thm-weighted-rt-tqft-and-factorization]]: with full AC and its countable-choice consequence, the exact RT state spaces and weighted ribbon-bordism category define a symmetric monoidal functor; the composition law includes the Maslov/signature correction, the normalized tube maps give cutting/sewing inverses, and a unit change of bordism weight acts by $\kappa$. This supplies the gluing maps here but does not cancel the weight anomaly.

[F5] [[lem-cft-defect-sewing-idempotent-trace-resolution]] proves nondegeneracy of the trace pairing on the fixed Hom spaces of any idempotent $P$ and its complete resolution $P=\sum_{i,a,b}d_i(C^{(i)}{}^{-1})_{ba}f_{i,b}e_{i,a}$. It does not identify a connecting-network projector or an RT two-point coefficient.

[F6] [[lem-cft-defect-sewing-bimodule-trace-pairing]] constructs commuting left/right separability averages on ordinary Hom spaces, proves their pivotal trace adjointness and nondegeneracy on bimodule morphisms, and gives explicit inverse bending maps between the dual defect-field space and the reversed bimodule Hom space. Its pairing is $\operatorname{tr}_j(\alpha T(\beta))$ with unrescaled categorical dualities; the RT two-point coefficient and the actual cap normalizations are computed below with the fixed source cup and opposite-cap words.

[A1] Full AC is adopted for the weighted RT construction, and countable choice is carried with it: [[def-axiom-of-choice]] defines AC and [[def-countable-choice]] defines $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$, which the imported weighted supplier lists among its premises. In this proof the choice assumptions are used only through that supplier's surgery-presentation and state-space construction; they do not select the finite simple-label or finite Hom-space bases.

[A2] The sphere defect-field, defect-free disk boundary-field, and disk boundary-defect two-point pairings used in the displayed sewings are nondegenerate. This is an explicit hypothesis of the theorem; no inverse is taken if the corresponding pairing fails to be nondegenerate.
