## Statement

At $R=\sqrt2$, so $R^2=2/1$, the rational finite gauging subgroup has order two. The source's self-duality identification labels its nontrivial symmetry line by $g$, which shifts the compact scalar by $\pi$ and acts as $gV_{n,w}=(-1)^nV_{n,w}$. Assume the line subcategory generated by $\mathbf1$ and $g$ is finite semisimple and that these are non-isomorphic simple objects. Use the rationally normalized defect $N_R=U_R\circ W_{\mathbb Z_2}$, where $W_{\mathbb Z_2}:C_R\to C_R/\mathbb Z_2$ is the endpoint-framed finite gauging wall and $U_R:C_R/\mathbb Z_2\to C_R$ is the selected invertible self-duality interface. With this normalization,
$$N_R\otimes\bar N_R\cong\mathbf1\oplus g.$$
On the algebraic primary-label space $\mathcal V_R=\bigoplus_{(n,w)\in\mathbb Z^2}\mathbb C V_{n,w}$, let $\rho(g)V_{n,w}=(-1)^nV_{n,w}$ and $P=(I_{\mathcal V_R}+\rho(g))/2$. The sum of the two line actions is $I_{\mathcal V_R}+\rho(g)=2P$.
The projection $P$ annihilates the genuine field $V_{1,0}$, and the charge action sends that field to a non-genuine endpoint. An invertible endodefect acts bijectively on genuine local fields, so this rational-radius duality line is noninvertible. In general its charge action is $V_{n,w}\mapsto V_{2w,n/2}$ when $n$ is even and to a non-genuine endpoint when $n$ is odd.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The compact-boson CFT at dimensionless radius $R=\sqrt2$, its genuine primary fields $V_{n,w}$, $n,w\in\mathbb Z$, and the adopted two-step flat-gauging construction.

[F1] [[def-cft-r5-compact-boson-full-theory]]: with $R=\sqrt2r$, the primary weights are $h=\frac14(n/R+wR)^2$ and $\bar h=\frac14(n/R-wR)^2$.

[F2] [[def-cft-r5-compact-boson-charge-lattice-and-cocycle]]: genuine primary labels are integral pairs $(n,w)\in\mathbb Z^2$.

[F3] [[def-cft-r5-topological-defect-interface-conventions]]: an invertible endodefect has a two-sided fusion inverse. Dragging a genuine local field across an invertible endodefect and then across its inverse returns the original field; consequently, the endodefect acts bijectively on the genuine local-field space. A non-genuine endpoint is not an element of that space.

[F4] [[def-cft-r5-finite-gauging-wall-conventions]]: the finite gauging is untwisted and the framed flat-bundle groupoid uses weight $1/|\operatorname{Aut}|$; each relative-holonomy sector on the fused interval has trivial endpoint-preserving automorphism group and weight one.

[F5] [[pthm-cft-r5-finite-gauging-wall-fusion]]: under its finite semisimple line-sector and pairwise-distinct-simple-line hypotheses, the fusion of a gauging wall with its reverse is one copy of each group line.

[A1] [[post-cft-r5-compact-boson-flat-gauging-model]]: adopt the source's two-step flat gauging followed by T-duality and its field-label action $(n,w)\mapsto(R^2w,n/R^2)$ when the output is integral; otherwise the line ends on a non-genuine sector. This is a conditional model construction, not a derivation of a continuum functional measure.

[A2] [[post-cft-r5-compact-boson-flat-gauging-model]]: At $R^2=2$, the source's finite subgroup gauging and selected self-duality interface $U_R$ identify its nontrivial gauging line with $gV_{n,w}=(-1)^nV_{n,w}$. Normalize the rational defect exactly as $N_R=U_R\circ W_{\mathbb Z_2}$, with no extra scalar, using [[def-cft-r5-finite-gauging-wall-conventions]]. Assume the line category generated by $\mathbf1,g$ is finite semisimple and these two lines are non-isomorphic simple objects. Argurio et al. §3.1 identifies the continuous and finite constructions only up to normalization; §4.2 discusses fixing the rational normalization by modular-bootstrap/locality. The exact finite-wall normalization here is adopted as the rational model input and is not claimed to be proved by those source passages.

## Proof

1.1 At $R=\sqrt2$, [F1] gives $$h_{n,w}=\frac{(n+2w)^2}{8},\qquad \bar h_{n,w}=\frac{(n-2w)^2}{8}.$$ For the genuine input $V_{1,0}$, these weights are both $1/8$. [given, F1, F2, algebra]

1.2 The induced generator satisfies $\rho(g)^2V_{n,w}=(-1)^{2n}V_{n,w}=V_{n,w}$. Thus $P=(I+\rho(g))/2$ satisfies $P^2=(I+2\rho(g)+\rho(g)^2)/4=(I+\rho(g))/2=P$. Its action is $PV_{n,w}=V_{n,w}$ for even $n$ and $PV_{n,w}=0$ for odd $n$, so in particular $PV_{1,0}=0$. [given, A2, F2, algebra]

2.1 Applying [A1] at $R^2=2$ sends $V_{n,w}$ to $V_{2w,n/2}$ exactly when $n$ is even: $2w$ is integral for every $w\in\mathbb Z$, and $n/2$ is integral precisely for even $n$. When $n$ is odd the winding endpoint is not genuine. For integral outputs, substitution into the weights of step 1.1 gives unchanged $h$ and $\bar h$, because $n'/R+w'R=n/R+wR$ and $n'/R-w'R=-(n/R-wR)$. [given, A1, F1, F2, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 By [A2], fusing $N_R$ with its orientation reverse cancels the adjacent inverse T-duality interfaces and leaves $W_{\mathbb Z_2}$ fused with its orientation reverse. Applying [F4] and [F5] gives $N_R\otimes\bar N_R\cong\mathbf1\oplus g$; the sum of these line actions on $\mathcal V_R$ is $2P$. More directly, [A1] at $(n,w)=(1,0)$ sends the nonzero genuine local field $V_{1,0}$ to the non-genuine endpoint with charge label $(0,1/2)$. Thus the defect action does not preserve the genuine local-field space. If $N_R$ were invertible, [F3] says its action on that space would be bijective, which is impossible. Therefore $N_R$ is noninvertible. [given, A1, A2, F2, F3, F4, F5, algebra] ∎
