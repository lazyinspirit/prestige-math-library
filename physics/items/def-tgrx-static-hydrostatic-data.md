## Definition

Let $\Sigma$ be a nonempty compact smooth three-manifold without boundary, let $h$ be a smooth Riemannian metric on $\Sigma$, and give $\mathbb R$ its usual topology and the standard smooth structure generated by the global identity chart $\operatorname{id}_{\mathbb R}:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$. Give $M=\mathbb R\times\Sigma$ the canonical product smooth structure. Adopt a constant $c>0$ in metres per second as the length/time conversion, write $x^0=ct_{\rm phys}$ for the standard length coordinate on $\mathbb R$, where $t_{\rm phys}$ is time in seconds, and use spatial coordinates measured in metres. For a smooth function $N:\Sigma\to(0,\infty)$, extend $N$ constantly along $\mathbb R$ and define the metric on $M$ by $g=-N^2\,dx^0\otimes dx^0+h$, where $h$ is pulled back from $\Sigma$. The metric components and lapse $N$ are dimensionless in these length coordinates. This is a static spacetime model: the coefficients are independent of $x^0$, $\partial_{x^0}$ is the selected future timelike Killing field, and its lapse is defined here by $N_\xi:=\sqrt{-g(\xi,\xi)}$ for $\xi=\partial_{x^0}$, so $g(\partial_{x^0},\partial_{x^0})=-N^2$ and $N_\xi=N$.

The Riemannian volume density of $h$ is denoted by $dV_h$; in length coordinates it has units of cubic metres and finite total volume, and no orientation of $\Sigma$ is required. In a fixed SI gauge, let $\Phi:\Sigma\to\mathbb R$ be a smooth electrostatic scalar potential in volts, let $q\in\mathbb R$ be a fixed charge in coulombs, let $T_0>0$ be a reference temperature in kelvins, and let $\mu_0\in\mathbb R$ be a reference chemical-potential parameter in joules. The sign convention is that the local electrochemical parameter is $\mu_0-q\Phi$. For the static Killing field $\xi=\partial_{x^0}$, its lapse is $N_\xi=N$; fix the Killing-normalized temperature $T_K:=N_\xi T$ to be the constant $T_0$, so the local temperature is $T(x)=T_0/N(x)$. Define the local chemical-potential field by $\mu(x)=(\mu_0-q\Phi(x))/N(x)$ for $x\in\Sigma$; then $q\Phi$ and $\mu$ have units of joules.

Let $D\subset(0,\infty)\times\mathbb R$ be an open domain for the pair $(T,\mu)$, measured in kelvins and joules, and let $p:D\to\mathbb R$ be a supplied $C^2$ pressure function with values in pascals. Require $(T(x),\mu(x))\in D$ at every $x\in\Sigma$. The function $p$ is model input; this definition does not derive an equation of state or assert that the prescribed data have a material realization.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The data in the Definition, including a nonempty compact smooth $\Sigma$, a smooth positive-definite metric $h$, a smooth positive lapse $N$, and the stated SI fields and parameters.

[F1] [[def-smooth-manifold]]: a smooth manifold is a topological manifold equipped with a maximal smooth atlas.

[F2] [[def-cartesian-product]]: $\mathbb R\times\Sigma$ is the set of ordered pairs with first entry in $\mathbb R$ and second in $\Sigma$.

[F3] [[prop-products-of-smooth-manifolds-have-a-canonical-product-smooth-structure]]: the product of smooth manifolds has the canonical product smooth structure and dimension equal to the sum of the dimensions.

[F4] [[def-riemannian-metric-and-riemannian-manifold]]: $h$ is a smooth symmetric tensor and $h_y(v,v)>0$ for each nonzero $v\in T_y\Sigma$.

[F5] [[def-gr-smooth-lorentzian-objects]]: a smooth symmetric nondegenerate metric with inertia $(3,1,0)$ is Lorentzian of signature $(-,+,+,+)$; a smooth timelike field supplies a time orientation.

[F6] [[def-gr-killing-field]]: a smooth vector field is Killing exactly when its metric Lie derivative vanishes, $\mathcal L_\xi g=0$.

[F7] [[def-riemannian-volume-density]] and [[lem-the-riemannian-volume-density-is-coordinate-independent]]: $dV_h=\sqrt{\det(h_{ij})}\,|dx^1dx^2dx^3|$ is a globally defined positive smooth density. [[prop-riemannian-volume-is-the-radon-measure-of-the-riemannian-density]] associates to it the compact-finite Borel measure $\operatorname{vol}_h$, and [[thm-density-measure-integration-agrees-with-smooth-density-integration]] defines integration of Borel scalar functions against this measure. In this item and its consumers, $\int_\Sigma f\,dV_h$ means the measure integral $\int_\Sigma f\,d\operatorname{vol}_h$; compactness gives $\operatorname{vol}_h(\Sigma)<\infty$.

[A1] Adopt the local SI conventions in the Definition: $c>0$ is in metres per second, $x^0=ct_{\rm phys}$ is a length coordinate, and spatial coordinates are in metres. These conventions require no orientation of $M$ or $\Sigma$.

[A2] Adopt the specified scalar-potential convention in the Definition: $q$ is in coulombs and $\Phi$ is in volts, so $q\Phi$ has units of joules.

[A3] The local lapse definition is $N_\xi:=\sqrt{-g(\xi,\xi)}$ for the selected timelike field $\xi=\partial_{x^0}$.

[F11] [[def-countable-choice]]: $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$ says every sequence $(X_n)_{n\in\mathbb N}$ of nonempty sets has a choice function $f$ satisfying $f(n)\in X_n$; this is the countable-choice assumption used to construct $\operatorname{vol}_h$ and its integration in [F7].

## Proof

1.1 By [F1] and [F2], $M=\mathbb R\times\Sigma$ is the Cartesian product equipped with the smooth product structure of [F3], hence is a smooth four-manifold. The tensor $g=-N^2 dx^0\otimes dx^0+h$ is smooth and symmetric. At each $(x^0,y)$ the tangent space splits as $\mathbb R\partial_{x^0}\oplus T_y\Sigma$, and for $a\in\mathbb R$ and $v\in T_y\Sigma$ its quadratic form is $-N(y)^2a^2+h_y(v,v)$. Since $N(y)>0$ and $h_y$ is positive definite by [F4], this form has one negative direction and three positive directions, and its direct-sum matrix is nondegenerate; hence it has signature $(-,+,+,+)$ by [F5]. [given, F1, F2, F3, F4, F5, algebra]

1.2 The field $\partial_{x^0}$ is smooth and has squared norm $-N^2<0$, so it is future timelike for the selected time orientation. Its local flow translates $x^0$ and fixes $y\in\Sigma$; since $N$ and $h$ are independent of $x^0$, this flow pulls $g$ back to itself and hence $\mathcal L_{\partial_{x^0}}g=0$ by [F6]. Thus the field is Killing, and its lapse equals the prescribed $N$ by [A3] and $N>0$. [given, F5, F6, A3, algebra]

2.1 By [F7], $dV_h$ is a globally defined positive smooth density with associated Borel measure $\operatorname{vol}_h$. This measure is finite on compact $\Sigma$, and the notation $\int_\Sigma f\,dV_h$ denotes its integral for Borel $f$. The profiles $T=T_0/N$ and $\mu=(\mu_0-q\Phi)/N$ are smooth because $N$ is smooth and strictly positive. Their length-coordinate conventions follow from [A1], while $q\Phi$ has energy units by [A2]. The measure construction and integration use the stated $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$ premise [F11]. [given, F7, F11, A1, A2, algebra] ∎
