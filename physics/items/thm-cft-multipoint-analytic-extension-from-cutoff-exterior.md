## Statement

Let $V$ be a strongly graded Möbius or conformal vertex algebra with trivial auxiliary grading. All modules considered below are strongly graded generalized $V$-modules with that trivial auxiliary grading, including the explicit three-operator Möbius structure when applicable. Consider an arbitrary finite composable chain of $n\geq1$ logarithmic intertwining operators, with leaf modules $W_1,\ldots,W_{n+1}$ and intermediate modules $M_1,\ldots,M_{n-1}$. Assume these leaves and intermediate modules are C1-cofinite and quasi-finite-dimensional in the real-weight convention. Assume the output $W$ is quasi-finite-dimensional. Then for every $f\in W'$ and leaf vectors the scalar product coefficient converges absolutely for $|z_1|>\cdots>|z_n|>0$, continues multivalued analytically to distinct nonzero insertion points, and has a finite power/log convergent regular-singular expansion in every finite nested-cluster boundary chart and its simultaneous boundary intersections, including infinity. More precisely, in boundary coordinates $q_1,\ldots,q_r$ and nonsingular shape parameters it is a finite sum of products $\prod_jq_j^{\alpha_j}(\log q_j)^{k_j}$ times convergent Laurent series with a finite lower bound in each coordinate; finite integral shifts can be absorbed into the powers. Powers and logarithms carry the continued branches. No C1 condition on $W'$, tensor-product existence, category closure, or global uniform nilpotence assumption on $W$ is made.
