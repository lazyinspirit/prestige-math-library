## Statement

Let $V=\bigoplus_{n\geq0}V_n$ be a positive-graded chiral VOA with $V_0=\mathbb C\mathbf1$. Let $F_c,F_o$ carry the bulk, boundary and bulk-to-boundary OPE maps, vacuum and Virasoro data, and ordinary full-VOA and boundary-algebra axioms in [[def-cft-locally-c1-cofinite-open-closed-data]]. Assume its exact source-category hypotheses: locally finite $L(0)$, finite-dimensional generalized weight spaces, finite support in finitely many $\Delta+\mathbb Z_{\geq0}$ cosets, $C_1$-cofiniteness, finite generation of the restricted contragredient, countable bulk and boundary decompositions, and the stated finite OPE-channel supports. Assume also all five genus-zero bulk-boundary bootstrap channel conditions in that definition.

For every binary bulk tree $A$ and every two-coloured bulk/boundary tree $E$, the iterated OPE is absolutely locally uniformly convergent on its specified complexified tree domain, and its restriction to the real configuration domain extends to a single-valued real-analytic correlation function on $X_r(\mathbb C)$ or $X_{r,s}(\mathbb H)$, respectively. This is a genus-zero statement; it asserts no annulus/Cardy condition, higher-genus sewing, modular invariance, or path-integral existence.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** Use the ordinary non-super VOA convention, the source's restricted graded dual, the tree domains $U_A,\widetilde U_E,U_E$ from the local data definition, and the stated ordering of open leaves.

[F1] [[def-cft-locally-c1-cofinite-open-closed-data]]: each module has the source's locally finite generalized $L(0)$ action, finite-dimensional generalized weight spaces, finite $\Delta+\mathbb Z_{\geq0}$ support, finite-dimensional $W/C_1(W)$, and finitely generated restricted dual. Bulk and boundary OPEs have finite intermediate-label support; each bulk summand has finite bulk-to-boundary support; the five channel identities and exact domains are part of the data.

[F2] [[def-cft-c1-cofinite-module]]: $C_1(W)$ is generated by $u_{-1}w$ for positive-weight $u\in V$ and the quotient $W/C_1(W)$ is the local finite-dimensionality condition. By itself this condition does not include the finite-coset support, dual finite generation, or finite-fusion-support clauses in [F1].

[F3] [[def-cft-r5-full-local-cft-data]]: the R5 package specifies state spaces, local fields, radial OPE data and crossed-channel equalities; it is a separate axiom package and does not by itself prove its equivalence to Moriwaki's full-VOA/open-closed package in [F1].

[F4] [[def-cft-bordered-worldsheet-with-collars]]: the boundary charts and their double use the declared analytic half-disk and reflection conventions. The present conclusion concerns the genus-zero plane and upper-half-plane configuration spaces, not general compact-surface sewing.

[F5] [[def-conformal-vertex-algebra]]: a chiral VOA has the declared conformal vector and $L(-1)$ translation; the two chiral actions in [F1] must preserve these exact conformal vectors and lie in the kernels of the corresponding full translation modes.

[F6] [[def-vertex-algebra-module]]: every module action obeys the module Jacobi identity and lower truncation. These formal identities do not alone imply analytic convergence.

[F7] [[def-cft-contragredient-module]]: the restricted graded dual uses the opposite-field action; that definition explicitly leaves the module axioms to a separate theorem. The finite-generation hypothesis in [F1] is therefore not supplied merely by writing down the dual vector space.

[F8] [[def-cft-intertwining-operator-space]]: an intertwining-operator space is the vector space of ordinary, logarithm-free intertwining operators of the declared module type. Its definition does not itself prove finite dimensionality.

[F9] [[lem-cft-source-category-tree-blocks-and-insertion-continuation]] proves finite Ward quotients using C1-cofinite inputs and a finitely generated outer dual, convergence of the actual finite tree projection sums on the full admissible tree domains, and compatibility of actual contractions with path continuation. It allows complex finite-coset weights and proves the required local finite-log estimates without assuming a tensor category, real weights, or ambient admission. No logarithm-free continuation of an individual chiral block is needed here.

[F10] [[thm-cft-contragredient-module-well-defined]] supplies the restricted-dual module axioms for these grading-restricted generalized modules. Its opposite-field convention is exactly F7's candidate action. Finite generation of these duals remains the explicit hypothesis in F1.

## Proof

**Given:** The ordinary non-super conventions, all the exact hypotheses of the unchanged Statement, the source restricted duals, and the recursively doubled tree domains in F1. The five bootstrap equations are hypotheses; no R5-to-full-VOA equivalence is used.

1.1 Every module that actually occurs in a fixed OPE tree meets F9's finite-coset and finite-weight-space conditions. Its C1 quotient gives finite generation: choose finite homogeneous quotient representatives and repeatedly replace a nonrepresentative by positive-state (-1)-modes, lowering the argument's integer degree within its fixed coset until the lower bound is reached. Thus the nilpotent part of L(0) has a uniform finite exponent on that module, bounded by the maximum on these finitely many generators, because the nilpotent parts commute with all modes between shifted weight blocks. The outer restricted dual is an actual module by F10 and is finitely generated by the explicit hypothesis F1. This matches the outer-dual hypothesis of F9, which requires finite generation, not C1-cofiniteness, on that dual. It does not apply the different HLZ category hypothesis or its ordinary no-log admission condition. Complex coset origins cause no problem: F9's reduction uses nonnegative integer degrees inside a coset. [given, F1, F2, F7, F9, F10, algebra]

2.1 A fixed input state has finite support in the declared direct sums. A fixed restricted-dual functional also has only finitely many relevant output labels: it has finite weight support, each such full weight space is finite dimensional, and the module decompositions are direct sums. At each vertex F1 leaves only finitely many possible intermediate labels for each already chosen input pair. Induction through the finite tree therefore gives a finite number of channel assignments. For each assignment the OPE coefficient is a finite sum of tensor products of the specified intertwining operators; membership in the algebraic tensor product in F1 means a finite tensor sum and does not require a separate assertion that the whole intertwining space is finite dimensional. F9 now proves absolute locally uniform convergence on the entire admissible cut tree domain for each of these scalar channel series. Their finite sum has the same convergence. The sums over weights are the actual finite-block projection sums in F9, not merely a normally convergent function after an unproved resummation. [F1, F8, F9, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 For a closed tree apply step2.1 independently in its left and right copies. This gives a holomorphic function on $U_A\times U_A$. For an open-output coloured tree, each open multiplication is its boundary intertwiner, and a closed subtree entering the bulk-to-boundary intertwiner produces its two separate chiral subtrees. The recursion in F1 consequently gives exactly the ordinary chiral tree $\widetilde E$: for example the two bootstrap channels have trees $((1,2),(\bar1,\bar2))$ and $((1,\bar1),(2,\bar2))$, not one common parenthesization. Step2.1 gives its holomorphic function on $U_{\widetilde E}=\widetilde U_E$. The root translations are the source's $e^{z_A L(-1)}$ or its left/right pair, already included in F9's anchored contraction. The exterior opposite field is the one in F10; no extra sign is attached to a reflected leaf. [F1, F4, F5, F9, F10, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 We specify the geometric compositions rather than assuming a two-coloured analytic action. Closed-closed insertion is the independent grafting in both chiral copies. Open-open insertion is a single grafting in the doubled tree. Insertion of a closed tree at a closed leaf of an open tree is two disjoint graftings at that leaf and its barred mate. Along an actual configuration path their coordinates are $c+q w$ and $\bar c+\bar q\bar w$, so doubling commutes with the insertion literally. For an open insertion use real anchor c and positive real q; it preserves the upper half-plane and the boundary order. Along a compact path choose q small enough that all inserted configurations remain disjoint. The space of smaller positive scales is contractible, so changing that choice adds no path monodromy. F9's convergent contraction-continuation identity now proves all three gluing/continuation squares, with the actual transported logarithm branches. This uses the source-category Ward quotient constructed in F9, not tensor-product representability, a conditional braided-category theorem, or compact-surface sewing. [F1, F4, F9, step 3.1, algebra]

4.2 The elementary comparison germs follow from the stated algebra axioms and bootstrap data. Unfold the ordinary full-VOA axiom: its three-input product and iterate channels are absolutely convergent representatives of the same two-variable real-analytic three-input coefficient, and its skew/exchange identity uses the integral-spin single-valued two-input coefficient. Unfold the ordinary boundary-algebra axiom: its two parenthesizations of three boundary inputs are restrictions of the same analytic function in the ordered real chamber. These are precisely the two associativity comparisons and the bulk exchange; they are part of F1, not consequences inferred from module Jacobi alone. F1's bulk-bulk bootstrap identifies the product of two bulk-to-boundary fields with the bulk product followed by bulk-to-boundary insertion. Its bulk-boundary bootstrap identifies the two orders of a boundary field and a bulk-to-boundary field. The vacuum bootstrap and creation give the unit comparisons. The explicit domains in F1 contain the required separated configurations for each comparison. Equality on their real configuration germs identifies their complexified holomorphic germs: a holomorphic power series vanishing at $(z,\bar z,x)$ on an open set has every coefficient zero, since the monomials in z, $\bar z$ and real x are linearly independent as real-analytic Taylor series. Thus these are valid analytic-continuation identities of the blocks from step3.1, with the branches fixed by those configurations. [given, F1, F5, F6, step 3.1, algebra]

5.1 An elementary comparison may be performed inside any larger tree. First take every exterior subtree much smaller than its separation from the comparison's marked inputs. The input states and output weight blocks in its formal grafting are finite at each coefficient. Step4.1 gives an absolutely locally uniform contraction there and commutation with continuation along the elementary path; hence the identity of step4.2 remains an identity after grafting. Shrinking scales uniformly along a compact path, then continuing the grafted identity, removes this auxiliary scale restriction. This proves the comparison in every context. It does not insert an infinite completed state into an identity asserted only for actual vectors without controlling the resulting sum. [F9, step 4.1, step 4.2, algebra]

6.1 Every coloured binary tree is connected to a fixed normal tree by these comparisons. At a bulk-to-boundary vertex use the bulk-bulk bootstrap to replace a closed multiplication below that vertex by the open product of the two bulk-to-boundary subtrees. Repeating decreases the number of closed multiplication vertices below bulk-to-boundary vertices, so terminates. Boundary associativity then turns the open tree into a right comb whose entries are individual bulk-to-boundary fields and the ordered open leaves. Use the bulk-boundary comparison to move a bulk entry past an adjacent open entry. Two adjacent bulk entries may be exchanged by reversing the bulk-bulk comparison, applying bulk exchange, and reapplying that comparison. Sort the bulk labels into one fixed order; open labels are never exchanged. Closed-output trees are similarly connected by binary rotations and bulk exchanges. Record the actual comparison path at each move, rather than resetting a principal logarithm at its endpoint. Step5.1 gives equality of the continued germs along these recorded paths. This establishes compatibility of all tree objects and all required compositions without importing a presentation of PaB or PaPB as a premise. [F1, step 4.2, step 5.1, algebra]

7.1 We give the remaining topological generation argument explicitly. In a fixed boundary-order chamber the mixed configuration space is exactly $X_r(\mathbb H)$ times the convex chamber $x_1>\cdots>x_s$: the upper-half-plane condition already prevents a bulk point from meeting a real boundary point. Thus its fundamental group is that of the labelled bulk configurations. For either the plane or the upper half-plane, forgetting the last bulk point is locally a product with the surface minus the other points. Local product maps are obtained by moving the other points in pairwise disjoint small discs with cutoff translations, leaving the complement fixed. A loop can be reduced inductively by first expressing its projected loop in the generators for one fewer points. Lift those paths using these local products. The remaining projected loop bounds a homotopy, which lifts by a finite subdivision into product charts; its residual loop lies in the punctured-surface fiber. Cutting that surface along disjoint arcs from the punctures to an outer boundary leaves a simply connected region, so its loops are generated by meridians about the punctures. Induction proves that every pure loop is a product of conjugates of two-bulk meridians. Relabelling paths for different planar leaf orders are obtained by adjacent exchanges; these are already among step6.1's comparisons. The zero- and one-bulk cases have no pure loop generator. [given, step 6.1, construct]

8.1 Each such meridian fixes the correlation germ. Bring its two bulk points into a small common disc disjoint from the other marked points, connecting to a tree in which they form one subtree by the paths in step4.1. For a closed output, the full-VOA two-input function is single-valued: its radial monomials have integral left-minus-right spin, and the ordinary exchange axiom identifies its two orderings. For an open output, F1's bulk-bulk bootstrap explicitly gives one single-valued real-analytic function on the whole $X_{2,0}(\mathbb H)$, so in particular its two-bulk meridian fixes the germ. Graft this two-input coefficient into the remaining tree. Step4.1 and step5.1 prove that continuing the grafted coefficient round the meridian is the grafting of that unchanged two-input germ, hence is unchanged. Conjugating by the connecting tree paths gives the assertion for a meridian based at any normal configuration. Step7.1 therefore makes every loop fix the germ. No individual chiral block is asserted to have trivial monodromy; the invariant germs are the full left/right or doubled bulk-boundary combinations selected by the hypotheses. [F1, F9, step 4.1, step 5.1, step 6.1, step 7.1, algebra]

9.1 Restrict the holomorphic functions of step3.1 by $\Phi_c$ or $\Phi$. These maps are real analytic and locally maximally totally real, so the restrictions are real analytic on the specified real tree domains. The flat continuations in F9 and the path identities of steps6.1–8.1 make the continuation from any tree independent of every path on each configuration component. They therefore define a single-valued real-analytic function on the whole plane or ordered upper-half-plane configuration space, whose restriction recovers every tree expansion by analytic uniqueness. Treat the other boundary-order components with their declared open-leaf order; no commutativity of the boundary algebra is used. For zero or one leaf the vacuum, creation and translation maps give the same assertions without an internal projection sum; one bulk leaf at an open output is its declared bulk-to-boundary intertwiner, whose scalar pairing is the two-chiral-leaf base case of F9. This proves the full unchanged Statement, including the exact convergence domains and finite-support hypotheses. The argument neither assumes the R5 package equivalent to F1 nor asserts any annulus, higher-genus or path-integral conclusion. [F1, F3, F4, F9, step 2.1, step 3.1, step 6.1, step 8.1, algebra] ∎

## Remarks
