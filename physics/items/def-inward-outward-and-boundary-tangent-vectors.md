## Definition

At $p\in\partial M$, use a boundary chart and the full $n$-dimensional tangent space. A vector is inward, outward, or boundary-tangent if its last coordinate is respectively positive, negative, or zero. The boundary-preserving differential calculation makes these alternatives chart independent.
