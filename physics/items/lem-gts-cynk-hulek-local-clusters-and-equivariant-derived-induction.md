## Statement

Assume AC. Let E1,...,En be the source complex elliptic curves with origin-preserving automorphisms gi of order $m=2$ or3, having the same primitive eigenvalue on their one-forms. Put $G_n=\{(a_i)\in(\mathbb Z/m)^n:\sum a_i=0\}$. Start $X1=E1$; recursively take Xn to be the distinguished cluster resolution for the even action $(h_{n-1},gn^{-1})$ on $X_{n-1}×En$, with odd residual hn induced by (1,gn). Then Xn is smooth connected projective, crepant over (product Ei)/Gn, has trivial canonical line and $H0(Xn,\Omega^p)=0$ for $0<p<n$. Its actual universal-cluster kernels give a residual-equivariant Fourier–Mukai equivalence $D^b_{G_n}(\operatorname{product} E_i)\simeq D^b(X_n)$. No arbitrary-dimensional derived McKay theorem is a premise.
