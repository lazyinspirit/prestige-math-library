## Definition

Let $M$ and $Q$ be smooth manifolds with $M$ second countable (all smooth manifolds here are Hausdorff and second countable). The **weak (compact-open) $C^\infty$ topology** on $C^\infty(M,Q)$ is generated by the following subbasis. A basic set is determined by finitely many data: charts $(U_i,\varphi_i)$ of $M$ and $(V_i,\psi_i)$ of $Q$, compact sets $K_i\subseteq U_i$ with $f(K_i)\subseteq V_i$ for the reference map $f$, an integer $r_i\ge0$ and a tolerance $\varepsilon_i>0$; it consists of all smooth $g:M\to Q$ with $g(K_i)\subseteq V_i$ and $$\max_{|\alpha|\le r_i}\ \sup_{x\in\varphi_i(K_i)}\bigl|D^\alpha(\psi_i\circ g\circ\varphi_i^{-1})(x)-D^\alpha(\psi_i\circ f\circ\varphi_i^{-1})(x)\bigr|<\varepsilon_i $$ for every $i$. The weak topology is the topology generated by all these basic sets, as the reference map $f$ and the finitely many data vary; at each point of a finite intersection, shrinking the tolerances around that point gives a basic neighbourhood contained in the intersection, so these sets form a basis. Empty compact pieces impose no condition and are omitted from the displayed supremum. This is the only topology on $C^\infty(M,Q)$ used on this page. It does not depend on the chosen atlases of $M$ and $Q$ ([[lem-the-weak-smooth-topology-is-independent-of-the-chosen-atlas]]), and for compact $M$ the immersion condition is open in it ([[lem-the-immersion-condition-is-open-in-the-weak-topology-for-compact-sources]]). Under $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$ the same construction applies to the total spaces $TM$ and $TN$, with their canonical smooth structures ([[thm-the-tangent-bundle-has-a-canonical-smooth-2n-manifold-structure]]).
