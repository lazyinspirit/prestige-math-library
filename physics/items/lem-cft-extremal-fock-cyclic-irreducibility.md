## Statement

Use the oscillator spaces, modes and screening coefficients of the rank-one oscillator data. Fix integers $p\ge2$ and $0\le i\le p-2$, and set
$$d=p-1-i,\quad \beta=i/(2p),\quad \delta=(2p-2-i)/(2p),\quad c=1-6(p-1)^2/p,$$
$$a_n=((2pn+d)^2-(p-1)^2)/(4p),\qquad b_n=((2pn-d)^2-(p-1)^2)/(4p).$$
Then the actual cyclic Virasoro modules generated by the indicated Fock vacua satisfy
$$U(\mathrm{Vir})e^{(\beta-n)\alpha}\cong L(c,a_n),\qquad n\ge0,$$
$$U(\mathrm{Vir})e^{(\delta-n)\alpha}\cong L(c,b_n),\qquad n\ge1.$$
Their $L_0$-characters are respectively
$$\frac{q^{a_n}-q^{b_{n+1}}}{\prod_{m\ge1}(1-q^m)},\qquad \frac{q^{b_n}-q^{a_n}}{\prod_{m\ge1}(1-q^m)}.$$
Here $L(c,h)$ is the unique simple highest-weight quotient of the ordinary Verma module. These are full algebraic modules at all grades; no truncation is asserted.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The parameters, oscillator spaces, modes and actual cyclic modules in the Statement.

[F1] [[def-cft-feigin-fuchs-oscillator-data]] gives the polynomial charge spaces. Their degree-$N$ monomial basis has the partition dimension $P(N)$.

[F2] [[lem-cft-feigin-fuchs-virasoro-screening]] proves the actual Virasoro action, vacuum highest weights and integral-charge screening commutation for every integer $p\ge2$ used here.

[F3] [[lem-cft-fock-oscillator-duality]] proves the degreewise nondegenerate pairing at reflected charge $t^*=(p-1)/p-t$, with $L_m^*=L_{-m}$ and the exact screening transpose.

[F4] [[lem-cft-long-screening-string-lengths]] proves the exact nonzero maximal powers $Q^{2n}e^{(\beta-n)\alpha}$ and $Q^{2n-1}e^{(\delta-n)\alpha}$ and their next-power vanishing.

[F5] [[lem-cft-boundary-virasoro-verma-chain]] identifies the first embedded Verma as the maximal proper submodule. For $M(c,a_n)$ its first grade is $(i+1)(2n+1)$ and highest weight $b_{n+1}$; for $M(c,b_n)$ they are $2nd$ and $a_n$.

[F6] [[lem-cft-virasoro-verma-pbw-basis]] gives the partition dimension and negative PBW word basis at every Verma grade.

## Proof

Here a singular vector means a nonzero $L_0$-eigenvector annihilated by every $L_m$ with $m>0$. This is exactly the highest-weight-vector condition stated in [F2].

1.1 The oscillator monomials $\prod_m x_m^{k_m}e^{t\alpha}$, where $k_m\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}$ is finitely supported, have degree $N=\sum m k_m$ and are indexed by partitions of $N$, so $\dim(\mathcal F_t)_N=P(N)$. Substitution in the current vacuum-weight formula [F2] gives $h(\beta-n)=a_n$ and $h(\delta-n)=b_n$. The charges are integral for the screening hypotheses: $2p(\beta-n)=i-2pn$ and $2p(\delta-n)=2p-2-i-2pn$ are integers. Thus each composition used in [F4] is defined under [F2]. [F1, F2, F4, given, algebra]

2.1 Fix $n\ge0$, let $t=\beta-n$, so $t^*=\delta+n$, and set $w=Q^{2n+1}e^{(\delta-(n+1))\alpha}$. By [F4] this vector is nonzero in $\mathcal F_{\delta+n}$; by [F2] it is singular. Screening commutation preserves its weight, which is $b_{n+1}$, while the target vacuum in $\mathcal F_{\delta+n}$ has weight $a_n$. Its relative oscillator level is
$$g=b_{n+1}-a_n=(i+1)(2n+1)>0.$$
[F2, F4, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 The highest-vector property defines a Verma map $A:M(c,a_n)\to\mathcal F_t$ sending its top to $e^{t\alpha}$; its image is the stated cyclic module. For a level-$g$ PBW word $X$, the adjoint identity in [F3] gives $\langle AXv_{a_n},w\rangle=\langle e^{t\alpha},X^*w\rangle=0$, because $X^*$ is a nonempty product of positive modes and each positive mode kills the singular vector $w$. Nondegeneracy of the pairing makes $u\mapsto\langle u,w\rangle$ a nonzero functional on $(\mathcal F_t)_g$, so the level-$g$ image is proper. Its domain and target both have dimension $P(g)$ by [F1] and [F6]; hence $\ker A$ has a nonzero vector at level $g$. [F1, F3, F6, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 The kernel of $A$ is a proper graded submodule because its highest vector maps to a nonzero vacuum. By [F5], it lies in the maximal proper submodule $J$ of $M(c,a_n)$, whose first nonzero level is $g$ and whose grade-$g$ space is one-dimensional. Step 3.1 then implies $\ker A$ contains that first singular vector; since this vector generates $J$, while $\ker A\subseteq J$, equality follows. Thus the actual cyclic image is $L(c,a_n)$, with the first character in the Statement by [F5]. [F5, step 3.1, algebra]

5.1 For $n\ge1$, put $t=\delta-n$, so $t^*=\beta+n$. The vector $w=Q^{2n}e^{(\beta-n)\alpha}\in\mathcal F_{\beta+n}$ is nonzero by [F4], singular by [F2], and has weight $a_n$; the target vacuum weight is $b_n$, so its positive relative level is $g=a_n-b_n=2nd$. The same adjoint calculation as in step 3.1 makes the level-$g$ Verma-map image proper. Equal level dimensions [F1, F6] give a nonzero kernel vector there. By [F5], the kernel is contained in the first maximal submodule, meets its one-dimensional first grade, and therefore equals it. This proves the second cyclic irreducibility and character formula. [F1, F2, F3, F4, F5, F6, step 3.1, algebra] ∎
