## Statement

Let $X$ be a smooth projective toric variety over $\mathbb C$ with toric prime divisors $D_1,\ldots,D_N$. Let $L_1,\ldots,L_l$ be line bundles whose first Chern classes have nonnegative degree on every effective curve class of $X$ and whose direct sum is generated by global sections. Let $Y\subset X$ be the smooth common zero locus of a regular section of $\bigoplus_aL_a$. Write $i:Y\hookrightarrow X$ for inclusion and define the ambient anticanonical class $\kappa_{X,L}=\sum_{j=1}^N c_1(\mathcal O_X(D_j))-\sum_{a=1}^l c_1(L_a)\in H^2(X;\mathbb Q)$. Adjunction identifies $i^*\kappa_{X,L}=c_1(TY)$. Assume $\kappa_{X,L}\cdot\beta\ge0$ for every $\beta\in\operatorname{NE}(X)$, and choose an ample divisor $A_X$ on $X$. In the quantum-cohomology interface, take its ample divisor on $Y$ to be $A_Y=i^*A_X$, and let $\Lambda_Y$ and $\Lambda_X$ be the completions of the effective curve rings by $A_Y$-degree and $A_X$-degree, respectively. The continuous coefficient map $\rho_i:\Lambda_Y\to\Lambda_X$ is defined by $Q^\beta\mapsto q^{i_*\beta}$; it respects the filtrations since $A_Y\cdot\beta=A_X\cdot i_*\beta$, and every coefficient sum is finite because a completed series has finite support below each filtration cutoff. Apply $\rho_i$ coefficientwise to the full cohomology-valued descendant function and write the resulting $X$-monoid series as $J_Y^X$. Put $H_{\rm amb}(Y)=\operatorname{im}(H^*(X;\mathbb Q)\to H^*(Y;\mathbb Q))$. Since the cohomology of a smooth projective toric variety is generated by its toric divisor classes, this is the subalgebra generated by $c_1(\mathcal O_X(D_j))|_Y$, the source's ambient subalgebra. Use the nondegenerate restriction of the Poincaré pairing to define $\operatorname{pr}_{\rm amb}:H^*(Y;\mathbb Q)\to H_{\rm amb}(Y)$ as orthogonal projection. Put $J_Y^{X,\rm amb}=\operatorname{pr}_{\rm amb}(J_Y^X)$. Use the effective curve monoid and formal $I_Y,J_Y$ functions of the quantum-cohomology interface, with the source's ambient cohomology subspace and grading $\deg q^\beta=\kappa_{X,L}\cdot\beta$ for $\beta\in\operatorname{NE}(X)$. For $\gamma\in\operatorname{NE}(Y)$, the identity $\kappa_{X,L}\cdot i_*\gamma=c_1(TY)\cdot\gamma$ makes the coefficient map $\rho_i$ homogeneous.

Under the source's equivariant graph-space localization and convex quantum-Lefschetz inputs stated in Facts & Assumptions, the formal vector functions $I_Y$ and $J_Y^{X,\rm amb}$ agree after the weighted-homogeneous triangular substitution
$$t_0\longmapsto t_0+f_0(q)z+h(q),\qquad \log q_i\longmapsto\log q_i+f_i(q).$$
The change-of-coordinate series $f_0(q)$, $h(q)$, and $f_i(q)$ are supported on nonzero effective curve classes; the $I$- and $J$-functions retain their curve-class-zero classical terms. The source grading has $\deg z=\deg t_0=1$, $\deg f_0=\deg f_i=0$, and $\deg h=1$. This is the genus-zero quantum-cohomology theorem, not an HMS equivalence or an all-genus statement.

**Proof technique:** direct; construct equivariant Chow localization and graph/toric recursion, construct the convex zero-locus comparison, then normalize and apply coefficientwise uniqueness.

1.1 **Equivariant Chow operations and degenerate targets.** If $Y$ is empty its cohomology-valued series are zero. If $X$ has dimension zero, every effective curve class is zero and the asserted identity is the common classical term with zero corrections. Hence suppose $\dim X>0$ and $Y\ne\varnothing$. Fix a proper finite-type complex DM stack $M$ with torus $T=(\mathbb C^*)^r$ action and a global equivariant two-term POT, with fixed virtual rank on the component under discussion. In any specified Chow degree use $U_N=(\mathbb C^N\setminus0)^r$ and the mixed quotient $(M\times U_N)/T$, shifted by $r(N-1)$. For a GL framing use the product with the full-rank matrix good pair and the explicit common affine-torsor replacements of [F3]. Thus the actual scheme proper pushforward, operational Chern and cone/zero-section Gysin operations define compatible equivariant rational-stack operations. 2.1 **The actual fixed substack.** An equivariant Hilbert-graph presentation embeds each stable-map stack used below into a smooth finite-type DM quotient: its framed quasi-projective scheme embeds equivariantly in a Grassmannian, and the open set with finite stabilizers is smooth; remove the invariant boundary so the graph locus is closed there. At a fixed geometric point a finite torus isogeny lifts the action to its étale formal chart. Exactness of torus weights chooses compatible homogeneous regular parameters; the zero scheme of its infinitesimal vector fields is cut out by the parameters of nonzero weights and is formally smooth. These intrinsic vector fields define the same closed fixed locus on overlapping charts. Intersecting with $M$ therefore represents its geometric fixed substack $F$, closed and hence proper. The finite-isogeny construction of [F5] makes the fixed coherence unique after one integer divisible by all geometric inertia orders: the connected group of pairs $(t,x\simeq tx)$ is a torus mapping isogenously to $T$, and the indicated power map lifts through it. Unramified inertia makes the geometric coherence and cocycle unique in families. Thus it is the saturated fixed convention of [F5]. ## Remarks

Givental's original v2 proof explicitly recorded the missing equivariant-foundation verification for general toric targets. The proof above reconstructs that verification with local cone/Gysin operations and the fixed/moving construction underlying Graber–Pandharipande, rather than silently treating the historical source limitation as resolved by citation. The nef defining bundles and the ambient nef anticanonical-minus-bundle class are separate hypotheses. The two-point descendant normalization is the source's covector $(a,J)=(s_a,1)$; its transposed column is not the same function. The comparison is coefficientwise formal and ambient only.
