## Statement

Let $E/F$ be a field extension, $G$ a group, and $V,W$ finite-dimensional
$F$-representations of $G$.  The map
$$E\otimes_F\operatorname{Hom}_G(V,W)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Hom}_G(E\otimes_FV,E\otimes_FW),\qquad a\otimes f\longmapsto ((b\otimes v)\mapsto ab\otimes f(v))$$
is an $E$-linear isomorphism.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** $E/F$, $G$, $V$, and $W$ as in the statement.

[L1] Extension of scalars sends an $F$-linear map $f$ to $1_E\otimes f$ ([[def-restriction-and-extension-of-scalars]]).

[L2] An intertwiner is exactly a linear map satisfying $f\rho_V(g)=\rho_W(g)f$ for every $g\in G$ ([[def-intertwiner-equivalent-and-faithful-representations]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Choose $F$-bases of $V$ and $W$, and put $H=\operatorname{Hom}_F(V,W)$. For $g\in G$, let $T_g:H\to H$ send $f$ to $f\rho_V(g)-\rho_W(g)f$. By [L2], $\operatorname{Hom}_G(V,W)=\bigcap_{g\in G}\ker T_g$. Since $H$ is finite-dimensional, so is $\operatorname{End}_F(H)$. Therefore finitely many $T_{g_1},\ldots,T_{g_r}$ span the subspace generated by all the $T_g$, and $\operatorname{Hom}_G(V,W)=\bigcap_{i=1}^r\ker T_{g_i}$. [L2, choose, algebra]

2.1 The chosen bases identify $E\otimes_FH$ with $\operatorname{Hom}_E(E\otimes_FV,E\otimes_FW)$ and identify each $E\otimes_FT_g$ with the corresponding intertwining equation over $E$. Gaussian elimination over $F$ gives a basis of the finite simultaneous kernel in step 1.1 and extends it to a basis of $H$; the same row operations remain valid over $E$. Hence scalar extension preserves this finite kernel. Every $T_g$ is an $F$-linear combination of the selected $T_{g_i}$, so the simultaneous kernel over $E$ for all $g$ equals that finite kernel and, by [L2], is $\operatorname{Hom}_G(E\otimes_FV,E\otimes_FW)$. [L2, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 The resulting identification sends $a\otimes f$ to the displayed map, which agrees with [L1].  Therefore it is the asserted $E$-linear isomorphism. [L1, step 2.1] ∎
