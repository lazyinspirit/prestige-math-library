## Definition

Fix a symmetrizable Cartan datum with finite color set $I$ and no imaginary
simple roots, a KLR datum $Q=(Q_{ij})_{i\ne j}$ satisfying (Q1)-(Q4), and a
field $k$ placed in degree zero
([[def-quiver-hecke-klr-algebra-for-a-symmetrizable-cartan-datum]],
[[def-symmetrizable-cartan-datum-for-a-quantum-group]]); let
$R=\bigoplus_{\beta\in Q_+}R(\beta)$ be the resulting graded KLR algebra, with
its idempotents $e(\nu)$, dots $x_a$, crossings $\tau_a$ and degrees
$\deg x_a=2d_{\nu_a}$, $\deg\tau_a=-b_{\nu_a\nu_{a+1}}$
([[def-klr-grading-and-diagrammatic-composition]]). Let $\Lambda$ be a
dominant integral weight and put $\lambda_i:=\langle h_i,\Lambda\rangle\ge0$
([[def-kac-moody-integral-and-dominant-integral-weights]]).

**Cyclotomic data.** For every color $i$ choose a monic polynomial
$a_i(u)\in k[u]$ of degree exactly $\lambda_i$
([[def-polynomial-ring-over-a-commutative-ring]]); the standard choice made
below is $a_i(u)=u^{\lambda_i}$. For $n\ge1$ write
$$a(x_s)=\sum_{\nu\in I^n}a_{\nu_s}(x_s)e(\nu)\qquad(1\le s\le n),$$
the element of $R_n(Q)$ that acts on the $\nu$-component by the monic
polynomial $a_{\nu_s}$ in the dot at position $s$.

**The cyclotomic quotient.** For $\beta\in Q_+$ define $J_a(\beta)$ to be the
two-sided ideal of $R(\beta)$ generated by the first-strand elements
$a_{\nu_1}(x_1)e(\nu)$, $\nu\in I^\beta$; equivalently
$$J_a(\beta)=\sum_{\nu\in I^\beta}R(\beta)\,a_{\nu_1}(x_1)e(\nu)\,R(\beta),$$
and put
$$R_a^\Lambda(\beta):=R(\beta)/J_a(\beta),\qquad R_a^\Lambda(0):=k .$$
The **cyclotomic KLR algebra** is the direct sum
$R_a^\Lambda:=\bigoplus_{\beta\in Q_+}R_a^\Lambda(\beta)$; it carries the
local units $e(\beta)=\sum_{\nu\in I^\beta}e(\nu)$, which act as the identity
on $R_a^\Lambda(\beta)$ and annihilate the other summands, and it is a
$k$-algebra with these orthogonal idempotents.

**Standard choice and grading.** The STANDARD choice $a_i(u)=u^{\lambda_i}$ is
used throughout the graded categorification, duality-normalization and based
statements below; for it we write $R^\Lambda:=R^\Lambda_a$. Under the standard
choice each individual first-strand generator
$x_1^{\lambda_{\nu_1}}e(\nu)$ is homogeneous of degree
$2d_{\nu_1}\lambda_{\nu_1}$ in the grading of
[[def-klr-grading-and-diagrammatic-composition]], so the ideal $J_a(\beta)$
and the quotient $R^\Lambda(\beta)$ are graded, and $R^\Lambda$ is a
$\mathbb Z$-graded algebra with local units. The sum $a(x_1)$ itself need not
have one common degree across different colors, and no single degree is
assigned to it.

**Boundary of the definition.** Arbitrary monic polynomials with lower
coefficients in degree-zero $k$ give an UNGRADED deformation $R_a^\Lambda$;
nilpotent dots, homogeneous trace degree and the highest-weight graded $K_0$
identification are not asserted for that branch, and the ungraded branch is
used only where a statement explicitly says so. Finite dimension, finite root
strings, ordinary projectivity and symmetry of the standard quotient have the
separately stated monic conclusions below. The cyclotomic relation is imposed
on the FIRST strand, and cyclotomic induction appends strands on the RIGHT
([[def-cyclotomic-klr-induction-and-restriction-functors]]); this convention
fixes the side on which the quotient map acts throughout the page.

This definition uses no choice.
