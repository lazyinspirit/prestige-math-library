## Example

Define $\mathcal A_D^{\mathrm{even}}$ as the norm closure of finite linear combinations of the identity and even-degree products of $\Psi(F)$ and $\Psi(F)^\dagger$, for Schwartz spinor tests $F$. In the selected free Dirac model, let $U_{2\pi}$ implement a spatial rotation by $2\pi$ and let $U_{4\pi}$ implement a rotation by $4\pi$. On the $n$-particle sector of the adopted antisymmetric Fock decomposition, indexed by $n\in\mathbb N_0=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$, $U_{2\pi}$ acts as $(-1)^n$ times the identity; $U_{4\pi}=I$. Every finite even polynomial in bounded spacetime-smeared Dirac fields and their adjoints is invariant under conjugation by $U_{2\pi}$, and therefore so is $\mathcal A_D^{\mathrm{even}}$. This is an exact model calculation; it does not infer a general spin-statistics theorem.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The physical model and mathematical conventions of [[pthm-qft-dirac-free-properties]].

[F1] [[pthm-qft-dirac-free-properties]] proves that the $2\pi$ spin matrix is $-I_4$, the test pullback sends $F$ to $-F$, the field and its adjoint change sign, $U_{2\pi}$ acts on the $n$-particle sector as $(-1)^n$ for every $n\in\mathbb N_0$, and the $4\pi$ action is the identity.

[F2] [[pthm-qft-dirac-free-properties]] supplies the strongly continuous unitary Fock representation $U$ and its covariance action on the smeared field.

[F3] [[pthm-qft-dirac-free-properties]] states that the bounded smeared field $\Psi$ is antilinear in every Schwartz spinor test $F$.

## Verification

1.1 By [F1] and the covariance action in [F2], $U_{2\pi}\Psi(F)U_{2\pi}^{-1}=\Psi(-F)$. Antilinearity from [F3] gives $\Psi(-F)=-\Psi(F)$; taking adjoints gives the same sign for $\Psi(F)^\dagger$. The $4\pi$ action is the identity by [F1]. [F1, F2, F3, given, algebra]

2.1 If $M=A_1\cdots A_r$ is a monomial whose factors are smeared fields or their adjoints, repeated use of step 1.1 and multiplicativity of unitary conjugation gives $U_{2\pi}MU_{2\pi}^{-1}=(-1)^rM$. Thus every even-degree monomial is fixed. Products of even-degree monomials and adjoints of even-degree monomials still have even degree, so finite linear combinations of them and the identity form a unital *-algebra; linearity fixes every such polynomial. [step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Conjugation by the unitary in [F2] is norm-continuous on bounded operators: $\|UOU^{-1}-UO'U^{-1}\|=\|O-O'\|$. Therefore every norm limit of finite even polynomials is also invariant. The conclusion concerns the algebra defined in the Example; it does not assert that pointwise field products exist. [F2, F3, step 2.1, algebra] ∎
