## Definition

Let \(V=\bigoplus_{r\ge0}V_r\) be a conformal vertex algebra of CFT type over \(\mathbb C\), let \(A(V)=V/O(V)\) carry the associative product of the preceding Zhu-algebra lemma, and let \(U\) be any unital left \(A(V)\)-module. For homogeneous \(a\in V_r\), call \(\deg(a_{(m)})=r-m-1\) the operator degree; on an admissible module this is the grade shift. Form the current Lie algebra from symbols \(a(m)\), linear in \(a\), with translation relation \((Ta)(m)=-m\,a(m-1)\) and bracket
\[
[a(m),b(n)]=\sum_{j\ge0}\binom mj(a_{(j)}b)(m+n-j).
\]
Let \(\mathfrak p\) be its subalgebra of nonpositive operator degree. Give \(U\) the \(\mathfrak p\)-action in which every negative-degree mode is zero and the degree-zero mode \(a_{(r-1)}\) acts by \([a]\in A(V)\). The induced graded mode module is \(E(U)=U(\widehat V)\otimes_{U(\mathfrak p)}U\). Define \(M(U)\) as the quotient of \(E(U)\) by the vacuum and translation relations and every coefficient of the full module-Jacobi identity, together with the current-mode commutator relations.
For the direct realization proof, use the concrete weak-associativity defects
\[
(z_0+z_2)^{r+p}\bigl(Y_E(a,z_0+z_2)Y_E(b,z_2)v-Y_E(Y(a,z_0)b,z_2)v\bigr)
\]
for homogeneous \(a\in V_r\), \(b\in V\), and homogeneous \(v\in E(U)(p)\). The displayed power makes each substitution coefficientwise finite by the induced grading and mode truncation. The realization lemma proves that the submodule generated by these defects equals the full Jacobi relation submodule: the current-mode commutator and the defects imply Jacobi, while Jacobi yields these defects by a residue calculation. This is a proof obligation, not an equivalence assumed in the definition. The presentation remains a formal graded quotient until the lemma proves its top is not collapsed and its fields satisfy all module axioms.

For any graded quotient \(M(U)=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}M(U)(n)\), define
\[
\Omega_0(M(U))=\{v:\ a_{(m)}v=0\text{ for every homogeneous }a\in V_r\text{ and }m\ge r\}.
\]
For \(n\ge0\), let \(\mathcal W_{-n}\) be the set of finite words in actual state modes whose total operator degree is \(-n\), including the empty word when \(n=0\). The degree-\(n\) top-return data are the trilinear evaluations
\[
\beta_n(v,w,\lambda)=\lambda\!\left(\pi_0(wv)\right),\qquad v\in M(U)(n),\quad w\in\mathcal W_{-n},\quad\lambda\in U^*,
\]
where \(U^*=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb C}(U,\mathbb C)\) and \(\pi_0\) is the grade-zero projection. These definitions do not assert \(M(U)(0)=U\), nondegeneracy of any return, or well-definedness of the induced presentation.

## Remarks

The next lemma proves that \(M(U)\) is an admissible module, that \(M(U)(0)\cong U\), and that these mode-word returns are well-defined on the resulting module. It does not assert that the positive-degree return maps separate every induced grade.
