## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** Let $V=\mathbb C^{1|1}$ have even basis vector $e_0$ and odd basis vector $e_1$. Write $h$ for the $L_0$ eigenvalue. Give $V$ the Hermitian inner product for which $(e_0,e_1)$ is orthonormal, and let the parity operator be $P=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$.

[F1] [[def-cft-r5-n1-superconformal-algebra|$N=1$ superconformal mode algebra in NS and Ramond sectors]]: In the Ramond sector the defining odd bracket is $\{G_r,G_s\}=2L_{r+s}+\frac{r^2-1/4}{3}\delta_{r+s,0}C$.

[F2] [[def-cft-r5-n1-superconformal-algebra|$N=1$ superconformal mode algebra in NS and Ramond sectors]]: The adjoint convention is $L_m^*=L_{-m}$, $G_r^*=G_{-r}$ and $C^*=C$.

[F3] [[thm-complex-numbers-form-a-field]]: $\mathbb C$ is a field, so the specified matrices act on the complex vector space $V$.

## Example

Consider only the Ramond zero-mode subalgebra generated by $L_0,G_0,C$. Define
$
\rho(C)=\frac32 I_V,\qquad
\rho(L_0)=\frac{17}{16}I_V,\qquad
\rho(G_0)=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
$
This is an example of a positive finite-dimensional representation of that zero-mode subalgebra. It is not asserted to extend to a representation of all $N=1$ modes.

## Proof
 1.1 Since $\rho(L_0)$ is scalar, $[\rho(L_0),\rho(G_0)]=0$. Also $\rho(G_0)^2=I_V$, so $\{\rho(G_0),\rho(G_0)\}=2I_V$. The Ramond relation in [F1] at $r=s=0$ has right-hand side $2\rho(L_0)-\rho(C)/12=\frac{17}{8}I_V-\frac18I_V=2I_V$, which agrees. The image of $C$ is scalar and hence central. Thus all brackets of this zero-mode subalgebra are satisfied. [given, F1, F3, algebra]

2.1 The matrices $\rho(L_0),\rho(C),\rho(G_0)$ are self-adjoint, matching [F2]. The parity operator commutes with $\rho(L_0)$ and $\rho(C)$ and anticommutes with $\rho(G_0)$, so the even/odd grading is respected. The standard Hermitian form is positive definite because $\|a e_0+b e_1\|^2=|a|^2+|b|^2>0$ for every nonzero vector. This verifies positivity for this finite-dimensional zero-mode representation only; it is not a unitarity or module-classification result. [given, F2, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
