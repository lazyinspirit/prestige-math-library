## Definition

Let $S$ be a complex supermanifold of dimension $1|1$; its reduced space $C=S_{\mathrm{red}}$ is a compact connected Riemann surface equipped with finitely many ordered distinct marks $p_1,\ldots,p_N$, where $N\geq0$. For every mark $p_i$, specify an open neighborhood $U_i\subset C$, a radius $r_i>0$, and a biholomorphism $z_i:U_i\to\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<r_i\}$ with $z_i(p_i)=0$; require the $U_i$ to be pairwise disjoint. These are the pointed compact analytic worldsheet data. A (non-Ramond) super Riemann surface is such an $S$ together with an odd locally free rank-$0|1$ direct summand $\mathcal D\subset T_S$ for which the bracket map $\mathcal D^{\otimes2}\to T_S/\mathcal D$, $Q\otimes Q\mapsto[Q,Q]/2\bmod\mathcal D$, is an isomorphism. In a local superconformal coordinate $z|\theta$, $\mathcal D$ is generated by $Q=\partial_\theta+\theta\partial_z$; with left odd derivatives, $Q^2=\partial_z$ and $[Q,Q]=2\partial_z$. A superconformal coordinate change is a parity-preserving holomorphic change of coordinates whose push-forward preserves the line $\mathcal D$; in local coordinates this is $Q\widetilde z-\widetilde\theta Q\widetilde\theta=0$.

An NS (Neveu–Schwarz) puncture is a marked superpoint with local coordinates $(z,\theta)=(0,0)$ and the nondegenerate local generator $Q$. A Ramond puncture is recorded as a Ramond divisor $F=\{z=0\}$ in a local superdomain: the distribution is generated by $Q_R=\partial_\theta+z\theta\partial_z$, so $Q_R^2=z\partial_z$ and its bracket has a simple zero exactly on $F$. Thus the bracket is an isomorphism off $F$, and the Ramond extension satisfies $\mathcal D^{\otimes2}\cong(T_S/\mathcal D)\otimes\mathcal O(-F)$, where $\mathcal O(-F)$ is the ideal line locally generated by the even coordinate $z$ vanishing on $F$. On a simply connected sector of the punctured disk, $\psi=\sqrt z\,\theta$ gives $Q_R=\sqrt z(\partial_\psi+\psi\partial_z)$; continuation once around $F$ changes $\psi$ to $-\psi$. This sign is the local Ramond spin monodromy. For a closed surface without Ramond divisors, a spin structure is a holomorphic line $S$ with $S^{\otimes2}\cong K_C$, and the reduced odd line of $\mathcal D$ is $S^*$.

## Remarks

Here “SRS with Ramond data” means the nondegenerate distribution on the punctured complement together with the specified simple-degeneracy extension along each Ramond divisor. It does not assert that the bracket is invertible at a Ramond divisor. A supermoduli object parameterizing these data is supergeometric and is not identified with the ordinary moduli space of reduced Riemann surfaces; no global compactification is asserted.
