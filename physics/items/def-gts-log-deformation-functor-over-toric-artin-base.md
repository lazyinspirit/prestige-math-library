## Definition

Let $k$ be a field, $Q$ a sharp toric monoid, $\Lambda=k\llbracket Q\rrbracket$ the completion of $k[Q]$ at the ideal generated by all monomials $z^q$ with $q\ne0$, and $\mathfrak m$ its maximal ideal. The category $\mathbf{Art}_\Lambda$ has local Artinian $\Lambda$-algebras $A$ with identified residue field $A/\mathfrak m_A\cong k$ as objects and local $\Lambda$-algebra maps as arrows. The log base $\operatorname{Spec}(Q\to A)$ has underlying scheme $\operatorname{Spec}A$ and the log structure associated to the monoid chart induced by $\Lambda\to A$.

Fix $S_0=\operatorname{Spec}(Q\to k)$ and a proper log-smooth saturated morphism $f_0:X_0\to S_0$ of relative dimension $d$. For $A\in\mathbf{Art}_\Lambda$, write $S_A=\operatorname{Spec}(Q\to A)$ and let $S_0\to S_A$ be the base change induced by $A\to k$. A log-smooth deformation of $f_0$ over $A$ is a proper log-smooth morphism $f_A:X_A\to S_A$ of relative dimension $d$, together with a specified isomorphism of log spaces over $S_0$
$$\iota_A:X_A\times_{S_A}S_0\xrightarrow{\sim}X_0.$$
Two such deformations $(X_A,f_A,\iota_A)$ and $(X'_A,f'_A,\iota'_A)$ are isomorphic when an isomorphism $h:X_A\to X'_A$ over $S_A$ preserves the log structures and satisfies $\iota'_A\circ(h\times_{S_A}S_0)=\iota_A$. Thus the reduction includes a chosen identification, and isomorphisms preserve it. Its log-smooth deformation functor
$$\mathrm{LD}_{X_0/S_0}:\mathbf{Art}_\Lambda\longrightarrow\mathbf{Set}$$
assigns to $A$ the set of isomorphism classes of log-smooth deformations $f:X\to\operatorname{Spec}(Q\to A)$ reducing to $f_0$. Smoothness of this functor means the lifting property: for every surjection $B\to A$ in $\mathbf{Art}_\Lambda$, every deformation over $A$ lifts to a deformation over $B$. These definitions assert neither that lifts exist nor that the functor is smooth.
