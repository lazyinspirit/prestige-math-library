## Definition

An abelian category satisfies **AB4** when it satisfies AB3 and every small
coproduct of monomorphisms is again a monomorphism.

Dually, it satisfies **AB4*** when it satisfies AB3* and every small product of
epimorphisms is again an epimorphism.
