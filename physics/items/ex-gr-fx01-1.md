## Statement

Take $M=\mathbb R\times\mathbb R^3$ with length-valued coordinates
$(t,\mathbf x)$, Lorentzian metric $g=-dt^2+d\mathbf x^2$, and time
orientation $N=\partial_t$. For $\mathbf x\ne0$, set
$r:=|\mathbf x|$ and
$\partial_r:=\sum_{i=1}^3(x^i/r)\partial_{x^i}$. For $R>0$, let
$$S=\{(0,R\omega):\omega\in\mathbb S^2\}.$$
The future null normals
$\ell=\partial_t+\partial_r$ and $k=\partial_t-\partial_r$ satisfy
$g(\ell,k)=-2$ and have expansions
$$\theta_\ell=\frac2R,\qquad \theta_k=-\frac2R.$$
Along the inward congruence generated by $k$, the screen Jacobi map is
$A_\lambda=(1-\lambda/R)\operatorname{id}$, so its first focal instant is
$\lambda=R$. The affine focusing bound from
[[prop-gr-fx-null-raychaudhuri]] is attained, and every point
$\gamma_\omega(\lambda)=(\lambda,(R-\lambda)\omega)$ with
$R<\lambda<3R$ lies in $I^+(S)$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The Minkowski metric, sphere radius $R>0$, null normals and congruence specified in the Statement.

[F1] On the positive screen, $\chi_\ell(X,Y)=g(\nabla_X\ell,Y)$, $\theta=\operatorname{tr}_q\chi$, and the screen Jacobi map detects focal instants ([[def-gr-fx-null-screen-geometry]]).

[F2] Under Countable Choice for its Lorentzian supplier conventions, an affine twist-free null congruence satisfies $\theta'=-\theta^2/2-|\sigma|^2-\operatorname{Ric}(\ell,\ell)$; under $\operatorname{Ric}(\ell,\ell)\ge0$ and $\theta(0)<0$, the focal bound is $2/|\theta(0)|$, provided the smooth congruence and generator exist through that comparison parameter ([[prop-gr-fx-null-raychaudhuri]]).

[F3] Under Countable Choice and Dependent Choice, a focal point on a future null normal generator is followed immediately by points of its unique geodesic extension in $I^+(S)$ ([[lem-gr-fx-null-focal-boundary-exclusion]]).

[A1] Coordinates are length-valued, $N=\partial_t$ fixes the future time orientation, $R$ and $\lambda$ are measured in metres, and no field equation or physical energy condition is assumed.

## Proof

**Given:** [F1]–[F3] and [A1].

1.1 On $S$, $q=R^2d\Omega^2$. For tangent $X,Y$, flat-space differentiation gives $\nabla_X\partial_r=X/R$ and $\nabla_X\partial_t=0$. Therefore $\chi_\ell=q/R$ and $\chi_k=-q/R$, and since the screen dimension is two, $\theta_\ell=2/R$ and $\theta_k=-2/R$. Direct inner products give $g(\ell,\ell)=g(k,k)=0$ and $g(\ell,k)=-1-1=-2$; both fields are future because their $t$ components are $1$. [A1, F1, construct, algebra]

2.1 In Cartesian coordinates the inward geodesic is $\gamma_\omega(\lambda)=(\lambda,(R-\lambda)\omega)$. Varying $\omega$ in a tangent direction $X\in T_{R\omega}(R\mathbb S^2)$ gives the Jacobi field $J_X(\lambda)=(1-\lambda/R)X$. Its screen map is therefore $A_\lambda=(1-\lambda/R)\operatorname{id}$ for $0\le\lambda<R$, with first rank loss at $R$. Hence $B=-\operatorname{id}/(R-\lambda)$, $\theta=-2/(R-\lambda)$, $\sigma=0$, and $\operatorname{Ric}=0$. Differentiating $\theta$ verifies the displayed equation directly, and $2/|\theta(0)|=2/(2/R)=R$. Thus the explicit focal parameter equals the comparison value; under its Countable Choice convention, [F2] gives the same sharp bound. [A1, F1, F2, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Fix $R<\lambda<3R$. The point $p_-= (0,-R\omega)\in S$ and
$q_\lambda=\gamma_\omega(\lambda)$ have time separation $\lambda$ and spatial
separation
$$|(R-\lambda)\omega-(-R\omega)|=|2R-\lambda|<\lambda.$$
The straight segment from $p_-$ to $q_\lambda$ is future timelike because
its time component is positive and its squared interval is
$-\lambda^2+(2R-\lambda)^2<0$. Thus $q_\lambda\in I^+(S)$. Under Countable Choice and Dependent Choice this also agrees with
[F3] for parameters sufficiently close to $R$; the displayed segment proves
the stronger stated range $R<\lambda<3R$ directly, without either choice
assumption. [A1, F3, step 2.1, algebra] ∎

## Remarks

The two signs distinguish the outward and inward future null directions; the negative expansion corresponds to the inward congruence and its focal radius $R$. The example is a flat-space calculation, not an empirical claim.
