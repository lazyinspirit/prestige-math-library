## Statement

Work over C, coefficientwise in a complete positive curve-class filtration with finitely many types at each cutoff. Suppose an actual resolved nodal logarithmic degeneration of a smooth projective surface with smooth anticanonical boundary has smooth toric and standard boundary-point-blowup local pieces, retaining every boundary-component contact vector and every zero-boundary-degree piece. Use the actual proper-supported logarithmic map cone, normalization/diagonal gluing, Cartier specialization, and finite exhaustive type refinement supplied by the projective finite-DF and adapted supported-map constructions. This hypothesis supplies an actual algebraic family and operations, rather than a subdivision of a drawing.

Then the following local-to-scattering comparison holds for genus-zero maps. A toric root with d>=2 labelled balanced contacts, one torus point condition and insertion psi^(d-2) has virtual degree one. A rigid labelled transverse graph has degree |coker Phi| times the product of compact-edge contact orders and its attached exceptional virtual degrees, where Phi is the integral edge/incidence/root matching map. The connected degree-w exceptional relative-line factor is (-1)^(w+1)/w²; exceptional pieces with more than one relative contact have zero evaluation pushforward. The local toric invariant with n-1 fixed incoming boundary points equals the sum of weighted trivalent determinant products divided by the product of incoming external orders. The actual boundary-blowup cut supplies precisely those missing external orders, with only the actual partition automorphism divisors.

The resulting connected disk weights are the coefficients of the unique consistent Hamiltonian scattering completion of the computed initial relative data, with log wall function equal to the sum of outgoing order times connected disk degree. For a primitive exceptional slab this wall function is 1+z^u. Completed broken-line coefficients are the corresponding disk-tree sums. After the root descendant and point incidence, every surviving root branch has exactly one primitive boundary label; zero-boundary disk corrections remain on its spine. Thus the constrained virtual tree sum equals the labelled ordered primitive-boundary theta-product sum, with root factor one and the genuine finite relabelling groupoid factors. This assertion does not include ordinary/relative descendant comparison, multi-end period factorial normalization, or construction of the supplied resolved target.
