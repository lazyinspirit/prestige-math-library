## Example

Assume the full Axiom of Choice, let $N\ge1$, and on a neighborhood $U$ of
$0\in\mathbb C^N$ define the coherent quotient
$\mathcal Q_U=\mathcal O_U/(z_1,\ldots,z_N)\mathcal O_U$. Let $W$ be a
coordinate polydisc supplied by the local coherent-acyclicity theorem for
$\mathcal Q_U$. For any nonempty coordinate polydisc $D\subseteq W$ containing
$0$, set
$$\mathcal Q=\mathcal O_D/(z_1,\ldots,z_N)\mathcal O_D.$$
Then $\mathcal Q$ is coherent and the evaluation morphism identifies it with
the skyscraper sheaf $i_*\mathbb C$ at $i:\{0\}\hookrightarrow D$. Consequently
$$\Gamma(D,\mathcal Q)\cong\mathbb C,\qquad H^q(D,\mathcal Q)=0\quad(q>0).$$

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** Full AC, a polydisc $D\subseteq W\subset\mathbb C^N$ with $N\ge1$ and $0\in D$, and the ideal sheaf generated by the coordinate functions.

[A1] [[def-axiom-of-choice]]: full AC is assumed, as in the local coherent-acyclicity theorem.

[F1] [[def-qftx-analytic-coherent-module]]: a coherent module is locally of finite type and has locally finite-type kernels for maps from finite-rank free modules.

[F2] [[thm-qftx-gaga-analytic-oka-coherence]]: under full AC, $\mathcal O_D$ is coherent, finite-rank free analytic modules are coherent, and cokernels of morphisms of coherent analytic modules are coherent.

[F3] [[def-holomorphic-function-in-several-complex-variables]] defines holomorphicity by complex differentiability at every point; [[cor-holomorphic-functions-in-several-variables-are-smooth]] shows that a holomorphic representative is continuous and separately holomorphic. Hence [[thm-power-series-expansion-in-several-complex-variables]] applies and gives its convergent multivariable power series on a smaller polydisc.

[F4] [[def-skyscraper-sheaf-abelian-group]]: the skyscraper sheaf $i_*\mathbb C$ has sections $\mathbb C$ on opens containing $0$ and zero on opens not containing $0$; its stalk is $\mathbb C$ at $0$ and zero away from $0$.

[F5] [[thm-sheaf-morphism-isomorphism-stalkwise]]: a morphism of sheaves is an isomorphism if its induced map on every stalk is an isomorphism.

[F6] [[thm-qftx-gaga-analytic-local-coherent-polydisc-acyclicity]]: choose $W$ for the particular coherent module $\mathcal Q_U$ of this example. Then $H^q(D,\mathcal Q_U|_D)=0$ for $q>0$ on every nonempty coordinate polydisc $D\subseteq W$. This choice of $W$ depends on $\mathcal Q_U$.

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 For each $D\subseteq W$, restriction of $\mathcal Q_U$ is the cokernel of the finite free map $\mathcal O_D^{\oplus N}\to\mathcal O_D$ given by $(a_1,\ldots,a_N)\mapsto\sum_j a_jz_j$. Coherence of $\mathcal O_D$ and closure under cokernels in [F2] make $\mathcal Q$ coherent. Define evaluation $\mathcal O_D\to i_*\mathbb C$ by $f\mapsto f(0)$ on opens containing $0$ and by the unique zero section on opens disjoint from $0$. Each $z_j$ maps to zero, so the morphism factors through $\mathcal Q$. [A1, F2, F4, given, construct]

1.2 On the stalk at $0$, evaluation $\mathcal O_{D,0}\to\mathbb C$ is surjective. Its kernel consists exactly of germs with zero constant coefficient. By [F3], write such a germ as $f(z)=\sum_{\alpha\ne0}c_\alpha z^\alpha$ on a smaller polydisc. Assign each multi-index to its least $j$ with $\alpha_j>0$ and set $g_j(z)=\sum_{\alpha:\,j=\min\{k:\alpha_k>0\}}c_\alpha z^{\alpha-e_j}$. These subseries converge on every still smaller polydisc, and termwise regrouping gives $f=\sum_{j=1}^N z_jg_j$. Thus the kernel is precisely $(z_1,\ldots,z_N)\mathcal O_{D,0}$, and the quotient stalk maps isomorphically to $\mathbb C$. [F3, given, algebra]

2.1 If $y\in D$ and $y\ne0$, choose $j$ with $y_j\ne0$. The germ of $z_j$ is a unit at $y$, so the quotient stalk $\mathcal Q_y$ is zero; [F4] says the target stalk is also zero. At $0$, step 1.2 gives an isomorphism on stalks. Hence [F5] makes the induced morphism $\mathcal Q\to i_*\mathbb C$ an isomorphism. [F4, F5, step 1.2, given]

3.1 By [F4], $\Gamma(D,i_*\mathbb C)=\mathbb C$, so step 2.1 gives $\Gamma(D,\mathcal Q)\cong\mathbb C$. By step 1.1, $\mathcal Q=\mathcal Q_U|_D$; apply [F6] with the $W$ chosen for $\mathcal Q_U$ to obtain $H^q(D,\mathcal Q)=0$ for every $q>0$. [F1, F2, F4, F6, step 1.1, step 2.1] ∎
