## Statement

For $m,w\ge1$, the exact sum of $m$ nonnegative $w$-bit integers has constant-depth polynomial-size circuits over AND, OR, NOT and MAJ, with $w+\lceil\log_2m\rceil$ output bits. The circuits are DLOGTIME-uniform under the following explicit array convention: the input bit $x_{i,j}$ has tuple address $(i,j)$; array dimensions and gate-coordinate domains may be padded to powers of two, with padded inputs zero. Gate addresses use a fixed number of binary coordinate fields, and the uniformity machine receives the binary dimensions and gate addresses. Equivalently, the input is a bit matrix with both dimensions padded to powers of two. Constants and output truncation are included in the family. The claim does not apply to arbitrary effectively decodable encodings.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** $m,w\ge1$, the bit array in the Statement, and random-access Turing-machine DLOGTIME uniformity. All logarithms in the proof are base two.

## Proof


1.1 Two-word addition has the following constant-depth Boolean construction. For $d$-bit words $a,b$, put $g_j=a_j\wedge b_j$ and $p_j=a_j\vee b_j$. The carry entering bit $k>0$ is $$c_k=\bigvee_{0\le j<k}\left(g_j\wedge\bigwedge_{j<t<k}p_t\right),\qquad c_0=0.$$ A carry is generated by two ones, propagated through positions with at least one one, and killed by two zeros; this proves the formula. The output bit is the XOR of $a_k,b_k,c_k$, computed by its constant-size Boolean truth table, with the final carry appended. These formulas have constant depth and polynomial size, and predecessor tests use equality and order on their bit-position addresses. [given, construct]

1.2 For $m\ge2$, let $\ell=\lceil\log_2m\rceil$, let $b$ be the least power of two at least $\ell$, and put $B=2^b$. Then $m\le B\le m^2$. Split each input into consecutive $b$-bit blocks and pad the last block with zeros. For block $q$, define $$S_q=\sum_{i=0}^{m-1}\sum_{r=0}^{b-1}2^r x_{i,qb+r}.$$ Its upper bound is $C=m(B-1)$, polynomial in $mw$. [given, construct, algebra]

2.1 To compute all bits of $S_q$, pad the row domain to a power of two $M_0\ge m$ with $M_0<2m$. Create a rectangular set of distinct copy gates $(i,r,t)$ with $0\le i<M_0$, $0\le r<b$, and $0\le t<B$. Gates with $i\ge m$ or $t\ge2^r$ are zero; otherwise they copy $x_{i,qb+r}$. There are $D=M_0bB$ slots, a power of two polynomial in $mw$, and exactly $S_q$ slots are true. For each $1\le h\le D$, feed these slots and $D+1$ distinct constant slots to a MAJ gate, with exactly $D-h+1$ constant slots equal to one. Its odd fan-in is $2D+1$, so under either convention for majority it tests $S_q+(D-h+1)\ge D+1$, equivalently $S_q\ge h$. Write this output as $T_{q,h}$ and set $T_{q,0}=1$, $T_{q,D+1}=0$. Then $E_{q,h}=T_{q,h}\wedge\neg T_{q,h+1}$ tests $S_q=h$, and bit $u$ of $S_q$ is the OR of $E_{q,h}$ over the indices $h$ having bit $u$ equal to one. The depth is constant and the size polynomial. [step 1.2, construct, algebra]

3.1 Write $S_q=r_q+Bv_q$. Since $r_q<B$ and $v_q<m\le B$, form two words: $R$ has block $q$ equal to $r_q$, and $V$ has block $q+1$ equal to $v_q$ and block zero zero. These bits are direct selections from the bits computed in step 2.1. The blocks do not overlap within either word, and $$\sum_{i=0}^{m-1}x_i=\sum_q S_q B^q=R+V.$$ Apply step 1.1 once to $R,V$. This is a constant number of constant-depth stages of polynomial total size. [step 1.1, step 2.1, algebra, construct]

4.1 We verify uniformity explicitly. Use fixed-length tuples with a constant number of $O(\log(mw+2))$-bit fields for gate type, block, row, within-block bit, copy, threshold, output-bit and constant-slot indices. All predecessor predicates in step 1.1 and step 1.2 and step 2.1 and step 3.1 use equality, order, the bit predicate on $h$, $t<2^r$, the constant-slot test $u<D-h+1$, and $j=qb+r$. Since $b$ is a power of two, the last relation is a shift and concatenation. The exponent of the power of two $D=M_0bB$ is the sum of the corresponding bit-length exponents. Testing $t<2^r$ compares $r$ with the highest nonzero bit position of $t$. Determine the field widths and $b$ from the binary dimensions and perform these tests by a constant number of linear scans, additions, subtractions, shifts and comparisons of $O(\log(mw+2))$-bit words. Each gate-type or predecessor query takes $O(\log(mw+2))$ time on the random-access uniformity machine; no general multiplication of two varying address fields is used. Dimensions and coordinate domains are padded to powers of two, and positions outside the actual input bounds are constant zero. For $m=1$, copy the input word. Finally $\sum_i x_i<m2^w\le2^{w+\lceil\log_2m\rceil}$, so the retained output width is exactly the one in the Statement. [step 1.1, step 1.2, step 2.1, step 3.1, algebra, construct] ∎
