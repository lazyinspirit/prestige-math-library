## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice as carried by [[lem-em-exterior-vector-helmholtz-mixed-boundary-existence]], whose variational, Sobolev-compactness and compact-spectral steps use it; the algebraic computations below use no further choice. Let $O\subset\mathbb R^3$ be a bounded open set with $C^\infty$ boundary, $D:=\mathbb R^3\setminus\overline O$ connected, $k>0$, $\omega:=ck$ with $c$ the vacuum speed of [[def-em-si-conventions]], and $R>0$ with $\overline O\subset B_R(0)$; let $V\subseteq\mathbb R^3$ be open with $\overline D\subseteq V$, and let $(\widetilde E_{\rm inc},\widetilde B_{\rm inc})$ be a $C^\infty$ frequency-$\omega$ source-free vacuum phasor pair on $V$ ([[def-em-monochromatic-source-free-phasors]], [[def-em-lab-geometry]], [[def-em-fields]], [[def-em-si-conventions]]). Plane-wave instances are provided by [[pthm-em-plane-wave-maxwell]]: if $\kappa\cdot e=0$, $b=\kappa\times e/\omega$ and $|\kappa|=\omega/c$, then $(\widetilde E_{\rm inc},\widetilde B_{\rm inc})(x)=(e\,e^{i\kappa\cdot x},b\,e^{i\kappa\cdot x})$ satisfies the four phasor equations on $\mathbb R^3$ ([[def-em-plane-wave-definition]]). Adopt the ideal perfectly conducting (PEC) boundary model: the tangential component of the total electric field vanishes on $\partial O$. This is a declared idealization of the stationary interface conditions of [[pthm-em-material-stationary-jumps]] with vanishing fields inside the conductor; it is not one of the finite-conductivity branches of [[post-em-material-closure]], and the induced surface charge and current are neither prescribed nor determined here. Let $e_T$ be the tangential projection of $-\widetilde E_{\rm inc}$ on $\partial O$, and let $\widetilde E_{\rm scat}$ be the unique $C^\infty$ field on $\overline D$ of [[lem-em-exterior-vector-helmholtz-mixed-boundary-existence]] with $(\Delta+k^2)\widetilde E_{\rm scat}=0$ and $\operatorname{div}\widetilde E_{\rm scat}=0$ on $D$ ([[def-laplacian-of-a-c2-function]], [[def-divergence-and-curl-of-a-c1-vector-field]]), tangential trace $e_T$ on $\partial O$ and that lemma's outgoing modal condition on $S_R$. Set $\widetilde B_{\rm scat}:=(i\omega)^{-1}\operatorname{curl}\widetilde E_{\rm scat}$ and, on $D$, $\widetilde E_{\rm tot}:=\widetilde E_{\rm inc}+\widetilde E_{\rm scat}$, $\widetilde B_{\rm tot}:=\widetilde B_{\rm inc}+\widetilde B_{\rm scat}$. Then: (a) [Phasor Maxwell] $(\widetilde E_{\rm scat},\widetilde B_{\rm scat})$ and $(\widetilde E_{\rm tot},\widetilde B_{\rm tot})$ are $C^\infty$ frequency-$\omega$ source-free vacuum phasor pairs on $D$; and for every $C^\infty$ frequency-$\omega$ source-free vacuum phasor pair $(\widetilde E,\widetilde B)$ on an open set $O'\subseteq\mathbb R^3$, the associated real fields of [[def-em-monochromatic-source-free-phasors]] satisfy on $O'\times\mathbb R$ the smooth-branch vacuum Maxwell equations of [[post-em-vacuum-maxwell]] with $\rho\equiv0$ and $J\equiv0$. Consequently the associated real total fields $E_{\rm tot},B_{\rm tot}$ satisfy those source-free equations on $D\times\mathbb R$. (b) [PEC boundary condition] the tangential trace of $\widetilde E_{\rm tot}$ on $\partial O$ vanishes; hence for every unit normal field $n$ of $\partial O$ the associated real total electric field $E_{\rm tot}(x,t)=\operatorname{Re}(\widetilde E_{\rm tot}(x)e^{-i\omega t})$ ([[def-complex-conjugate-real-imaginary-part-and-modulus]]) satisfies $n\times E_{\rm tot}=0$ on $\partial O\times\mathbb R$. (c) [Uniqueness] if $(\widetilde E',\widetilde B')$ is a frequency-$\omega$ source-free vacuum phasor pair on $D$ whose fields belong to $C^\infty(\overline D;\mathbb C^3)$ that is outgoing on $S_R$ in the modal sense of the vector Helmholtz lemma and has tangential trace $e_T$ on $\partial O$, then $(\widetilde E',\widetilde B')=(\widetilde E_{\rm scat},\widetilde B_{\rm scat})$ on $D$; equivalently $(\widetilde E_{\rm tot},\widetilde B_{\rm tot})$ is the unique total source-free phasor pair in the corresponding $C^\infty$ closure class with that prescribed incident phasor, vanishing tangential electric trace, and outgoing scattered correction $\widetilde E_{\rm tot}-\widetilde E_{\rm inc}$ in the lemma's modal class. The total field itself is not required to be outgoing; an incident plane wave is generally not outgoing. (d) [Scope] one positive frequency $\omega=ck$, one smooth bounded stationary PEC obstacle with connected exterior, and $C^\infty$ incident and scattered classes only; no lossy, impedance, nonsmooth or dispersive obstacle, no determination of the induced surface charge or current, no radiation-zone amplitude or far-field asymptotics beyond the lemma's outgoing modal class, no point or singular source, no finite-energy or empirical claim.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The Axiom of Choice; the bounded $C^\infty$ obstacle $O$ with connected exterior $D$; $k>0$, $\omega=ck$, $R>0$ with $\overline O\subset B_R(0)$; the open $V\supseteq\overline D$ and the $C^\infty$ frequency-$\omega$ source-free vacuum phasor pair $(\widetilde E_{\rm inc},\widetilde B_{\rm inc})$ on $V$; the tangential projection $e_T$ of $-\widetilde E_{\rm inc}$ on $\partial O$; the field $\widetilde E_{\rm scat}$ and $\widetilde B_{\rm scat}=(i\omega)^{-1}\operatorname{curl}\widetilde E_{\rm scat}$ of the Statement; the totals $\widetilde E_{\rm tot}=\widetilde E_{\rm inc}+\widetilde E_{\rm scat}$ and $\widetilde B_{\rm tot}=\widetilde B_{\rm inc}+\widetilde B_{\rm scat}$ on $D$.

[F1] Exterior vector Helmholtz lemma: for the geometry, $k$ and $R$ above and every smooth $\mathbb C$-valued tangential field $e_T$ on $\partial O$, there is exactly one $E\in C^\infty(\overline D;\mathbb C^3)$ with $(\Delta+k^2)E=0$ on $D$, $\operatorname{div}E=0$ on $D$, tangential trace $E_{\rm tangential}=e_T$ on $\partial O$, and the modal outgoing condition on $S_R$ (its trace lies in $H^{1/2}(S_R;\mathbb C^3)$ and its normal derivative on $S_R$ equals the sphere Dirichlet-to-Neumann image of that trace componentwise in the weak trace sense); its normal boundary values satisfy the closure relation; the lemma assumes the Axiom of Choice ([[lem-em-exterior-vector-helmholtz-mixed-boundary-existence]]).

[F2] Phasor conventions: a frequency-$\omega$ source-free vacuum phasor pair on an open set is a $C^\infty$ pair $(\widetilde E,\widetilde B)$ satisfying $\operatorname{div}\widetilde E=0$, $\operatorname{div}\widetilde B=0$, $\operatorname{curl}\widetilde E=i\omega\widetilde B$ and $\operatorname{curl}\widetilde B=-i\omega c^{-2}\widetilde E$ there, with divergence and curl acting componentwise on the complex fields; the associated real fields are $E=\operatorname{Re}(\widetilde Ee^{-i\omega t})$, $B=\operatorname{Re}(\widetilde Be^{-i\omega t})$ ([[def-em-monochromatic-source-free-phasors]], [[def-complex-conjugate-real-imaginary-part-and-modulus]]).

[F3] Phasor calculus and equivalence: for $C^1$ complex fields the displayed partial-derivative and divergence/curl formulas hold; the real-part evaluation is injective; and the phasor system holds on an open set if and only if the associated real fields satisfy $\operatorname{div}E=0$, $\operatorname{div}B=0$, $\operatorname{curl}E=-\partial_tB$, $\operatorname{curl}B=c^{-2}\partial_tE$ there ([[lem-em-phasor-real-part-calculus-and-equivalence]]).

[F4] Smooth vacuum Maxwell branch at zero sources: for jointly $C^1$ fields with the additional derivatives required by each equation existing and continuous and continuous sources, the adopted vacuum Maxwell postulate consists of the four equations $\operatorname{div}E=\rho/\varepsilon_0$, $\operatorname{div}B=0$, $\operatorname{curl}E=-\partial_tB$, $\operatorname{curl}B=\mu_0J+c^{-2}\partial_tE$; with $\rho\equiv0$ and $J\equiv0$ this is exactly the source-free system of [F3] ([[post-em-vacuum-maxwell]], [[def-em-si-conventions]]).

[F5] Plane-wave amplitude equations: for $k\in\mathbb R^3\setminus\{0\}$, $\omega>0$ and $e,b\in\mathbb C^3$, the plane-wave fields satisfy the source-free smooth Maxwell equations if and only if $\kappa\cdot e=0$, $\kappa\cdot b=0$, $\kappa\times e=\omega b$ and $\kappa\times b=-(\omega/c^2)e$; on the dispersion relation $|\kappa|=\omega/c$ the solutions are exactly the pairs with $\kappa\cdot e=0$ and $b=\kappa\times e/\omega$ ([[pthm-em-plane-wave-maxwell]], [[def-em-plane-wave-definition]]).

[F6] Vector-calculus identities: for a $C^2$ real field on an open subset of $\mathbb R^3$, $\operatorname{curl}\operatorname{curl}F=\nabla\operatorname{div}F-\Delta F$ and $\operatorname{div}(\operatorname{curl}F)=0$; divergence and curl are linear over the reals, and both identities and both operators extend to complex fields by applying them to real and imaginary parts componentwise ([[cor-the-curl-of-a-curl-of-a-c2-field]], [[thm-the-divergence-of-a-curl-vanishes]], [[lem-divergence-and-curl-are-linear-and-obey-the-scalar-product-rules]], [[def-laplacian-of-a-c2-function]], [[def-divergence-and-curl-of-a-c1-vector-field]]).

[F7] Tangential projection and cross product: for a unit normal field $n$ of $\partial O$ and a field $w$, the tangential projection is $w_{\rm tang}:=w-(w\cdot n)n$ with the Euclidean inner product of [[def-euclidean-inner-product]], and $n\times w=n\times w_{\rm tang}$ because $n\times n=0$; the cross product is bilinear and alternating ([[def-cross-product-in-r3]], [[lem-cross-product-is-bilinear-alternating-and-orthogonal]]).

[F8] Material context: the PEC condition is the declared idealization of the stationary interface conditions of [[pthm-em-material-stationary-jumps]] with vanishing fields inside the conductor; the material-closure branches of [[post-em-material-closure]] contain no $\sigma\to\infty$ ideal-conductor branch, and the fields of [[def-em-fields]] with the units of [[def-em-si-conventions]] on the laboratory chart of [[def-em-lab-geometry]] are the physical reading adopted by [[def-em-monochromatic-source-free-phasors]].

[F9] Choice accounting: the Axiom of Choice of the Statement is used exactly through [F1]; all other computations are pointwise algebra and finite sums, and no further selection from infinite families is made ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct; instantiate the exterior vector Helmholtz lemma for the scattered field, verify the four phasor equations algebraically, transfer the real-field Maxwell statement through the phasor equivalence, read the PEC condition off the tangential traces, and reduce uniqueness of any candidate to the lemma's uniqueness clause.

1.1 [Setting and existence of the scattered field] The hypotheses of [F1] are met by the geometry, $k$ and $R$, and $e_T$ is a smooth $\mathbb C$-valued tangential field on $\partial O$ by [F7] applied to the $C^\infty$ incident field; hence [F1] supplies a unique $\widetilde E_{\rm scat}\in C^\infty(\overline D;\mathbb C^3)$ with $(\Delta+k^2)\widetilde E_{\rm scat}=0$, $\operatorname{div}\widetilde E_{\rm scat}=0$ on $D$, tangential trace $e_T$ on $\partial O$, and the outgoing modal condition on $S_R$. The field $\widetilde B_{\rm scat}:=(i\omega)^{-1}\operatorname{curl}\widetilde E_{\rm scat}$ is $C^\infty$ on $\overline D$ by [F6] and the smooth closure regularity of $\widetilde E_{\rm scat}$, and the incident pair together with $e_T$ and the geometry is the data of the Statement. [given, F1, F6, F7]

2.1 [The scattered pair is source-free] On $D$: $\operatorname{div}\widetilde E_{\rm scat}=0$ by step 1.1; $\operatorname{curl}\widetilde E_{\rm scat}=i\omega\widetilde B_{\rm scat}$ by the definition of $\widetilde B_{\rm scat}$; $\operatorname{div}\widetilde B_{\rm scat}=(i\omega)^{-1}\operatorname{div}(\operatorname{curl}\widetilde E_{\rm scat})=0$ by [F6]; and $\operatorname{curl}\widetilde B_{\rm scat}=(i\omega)^{-1}\operatorname{curl}\operatorname{curl}\widetilde E_{\rm scat}=(i\omega)^{-1}(\nabla\operatorname{div}\widetilde E_{\rm scat}-\Delta\widetilde E_{\rm scat})=(i\omega)^{-1}k^2\widetilde E_{\rm scat}$, which equals $-i\omega c^{-2}\widetilde E_{\rm scat}$ because $k=\omega/c$ gives $k^2/(i\omega)=-ik^2/\omega=-i\omega c^{-2}$. All four equations of [F2] hold on the open set $D$ for a $C^\infty$ pair, so $(\widetilde E_{\rm scat},\widetilde B_{\rm scat})$ is a frequency-$\omega$ source-free vacuum phasor pair on $D$. [step 1.1, F1, F2, F6, algebra]

2.2 [Uniqueness of the outgoing scattered correction] Let $(\widetilde E',\widetilde B')$ be a frequency-$\omega$ source-free vacuum phasor pair on $D$ whose fields belong to $C^\infty(\overline D;\mathbb C^3)$, outgoing on $S_R$ in the modal sense of [F1], with tangential trace $e_T$ on $\partial O$. From the phasor equations and [F6], $\operatorname{curl}\operatorname{curl}\widetilde E'=i\omega\operatorname{curl}\widetilde B'=i\omega(-i\omega c^{-2}\widetilde E')=\omega^2c^{-2}\widetilde E'=k^2\widetilde E'$ and $\operatorname{div}\widetilde E'=0$, so [F6] gives $\nabla\operatorname{div}\widetilde E'-\Delta\widetilde E'=-\Delta\widetilde E'=k^2\widetilde E'$, that is $(\Delta+k^2)\widetilde E'=0$ on $D$. The field $\widetilde E'$ is $C^\infty$ on $\overline D$, has tangential trace $e_T$, divergence zero and the same modal outgoing condition on $S_R$; all hypotheses of the uniqueness clause of [F1] are therefore satisfied, and $\widetilde E'=\widetilde E_{\rm scat}$ on $D$. Then $\widetilde B'=(i\omega)^{-1}\operatorname{curl}\widetilde E'=(i\omega)^{-1}\operatorname{curl}\widetilde E_{\rm scat}=\widetilde B_{\rm scat}$ on $D$ by the third phasor equation and the definition of $\widetilde B_{\rm scat}$. [step 1.1, F1, F2, F6, algebra]

3.1 [The total pair is source-free] On $D$ the incident pair satisfies the four equations of [F2] because $D\subseteq V$ and the pair is source-free on $V$; the scattered pair satisfies them by step 2.1; the totals $\widetilde E_{\rm tot}=\widetilde E_{\rm inc}+\widetilde E_{\rm scat}$ and $\widetilde B_{\rm tot}=\widetilde B_{\rm inc}+\widetilde B_{\rm scat}$ are $C^\infty$ on $D$ as sums, and each of the four equations is linear over the complex scalars by [F6] applied to real and imaginary parts, so they hold for the totals; hence $(\widetilde E_{\rm tot},\widetilde B_{\rm tot})$ is as well a frequency-$\omega$ source-free vacuum phasor pair on $D$. [step 2.1, F2, F6, algebra]

4.1 [Real-field Maxwell statement] Let $(\widetilde E,\widetilde B)$ be any $C^\infty$ frequency-$\omega$ source-free vacuum phasor pair on an open $O'\subseteq\mathbb R^3$. By [F3] its associated real fields satisfy the real system of [F3] on $O'\times\mathbb R$, and by [F4] that system with zero sources is exactly the smooth branch of the adopted vacuum Maxwell equations at $\rho\equiv0$, $J\equiv0$; the required regularity is the joint $C^1$ (indeed $C^\infty$) calculus supplied by [F3]. Applying this to $(\widetilde E_{\rm tot},\widetilde B_{\rm tot})$ on $O'=D$, which is admissible by step 3.1, gives the asserted source-free equations for $E_{\rm tot},B_{\rm tot}$ on $D\times\mathbb R$. This proves the two statements of (a), the scattered and total clauses following from steps 2.1 and 3.1. [step 2.1, step 3.1, F3, F4]

4.2 [PEC boundary condition] On $\partial O$ one has $\widetilde E_{\rm tot,tang}=\widetilde E_{\rm inc,tang}+\widetilde E_{\rm scat,tang}=\widetilde E_{\rm inc,tang}+e_T=0$, because the tangential projection is the real-linear map of [F7] and $e_T$ is the tangential projection of $-\widetilde E_{\rm inc}$; thus the tangential trace of $\widetilde E_{\rm tot}$ vanishes. For the associated real field, the tangential projection of $E_{\rm tot}(x,t)=\operatorname{Re}(\widetilde E_{\rm tot}(x)e^{-i\omega t})$ is $\operatorname{Re}(\widetilde E_{\rm tot,tang}(x)e^{-i\omega t})=0$ by the real-linearity of the projection [F7] and of $\operatorname{Re}$; hence $n\times E_{\rm tot}=n\times E_{\rm tot,tang}=0$ on $\partial O\times\mathbb R$ by [F7]. This is (b). [step 3.1, F2, F7, algebra]

5.1 [Equivalent total formulation] Write a candidate total pair as $(\widetilde E_{\rm inc}+\widetilde E'',\widetilde B_{\rm inc}+\widetilde B'')$ on $D$, with both fields smooth up to its boundary, where $(\widetilde E'',\widetilde B'')$ is the scattered correction, required to be a $C^\infty$ frequency-$\omega$ source-free vacuum phasor pair and outgoing on $S_R$ in the modal sense, with the total tangential electric trace vanishing. By linearity [F6] the total tangential trace is $\widetilde E_{\rm inc,tang}+\widetilde E''_{\rm tang}$, so the vanishing of the total trace is equivalent to $\widetilde E''_{\rm tang}=e_T$; by step 2.2 applied to $(\widetilde E'',\widetilde B'')$, the correction equals $(\widetilde E_{\rm scat},\widetilde B_{\rm scat})$ on $D$, hence the total equals $(\widetilde E_{\rm tot},\widetilde B_{\rm tot})$; conversely the constructed total has these properties by steps 3.1, 4.2 and the outgoing property of $\widetilde E_{\rm scat}$ from step 1.1. This is the equivalent formulation of (c). [step 3.1, step 4.2, step 2.2, F6, algebra]

6.1 [Scope and choice accounting] Clause (d) records the exact hypotheses: one positive frequency, one smooth bounded stationary PEC obstacle with connected exterior, and $C^\infty$ incident and scattered classes; no lossy, impedance, nonsmooth or dispersive obstacle, no induced surface charge or current, no radiation-zone asymptotics beyond the modal class, no singular source and no empirical claim enters the argument. The PEC condition is adopted as an idealization by [F8] and is not derived from the material closures; the total field is not asserted to be outgoing, as an incident plane wave generally is not, and the uniqueness statement is exactly about the total-minus-incident correction. The plane-wave instance clause of the Statement is the specialization of the equivalence of [F5] to $|\kappa|=\omega/c$ and $b=\kappa\times e/\omega$, so it introduces no commitment beyond that cited statement. The only choice principle used is the Axiom of Choice assumed in the Statement through [F1], with the accounting of [F9]; all remaining steps are pointwise algebraic identities and finite sums. [step 4.2, step 2.2, step 5.1, F5, F8, F9, given] ∎

## Remarks

- The phrase "outgoing" applies to the scattered correction only. The constructed total field $\widetilde E_{\rm tot}=\widetilde E_{\rm inc}+\widetilde E_{\rm scat}$ is source-free on $D$ but need not be outgoing, and no decay or far-field asymptotic beyond the lemma's modal class is claimed.
- The ideal PEC condition is a boundary model, not a theorem about any particular conductor; the induced surface charge and current that would accompany it in a microscopic model are neither prescribed nor derived here.
