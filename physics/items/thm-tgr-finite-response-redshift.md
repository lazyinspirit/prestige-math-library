## Statement

Fix a cell label $i\in\{1,\ldots,r\}$ in [[thm-tgr-finite-cell-gibbs-factorization]], let $x_i=\chi(i)$ be its assigned point, and write $N:=N_i=N(x_i)>0$, $H_{\mathrm{loc}}:=H_i$, $Q:=Q_i$, and $\mu_{\mathrm{loc}}:=\mu_i$. Let $\beta_K>0$ in joules$^{-1}$ and $\mu_K$ in joules be the global parameters supplied there. Put $G_{\mathrm{loc}}:=H_{\mathrm{loc}}-\mu_{\mathrm{loc}}Q$ and $G_K:=NG_{\mathrm{loc}}$. Set $\beta_{\mathrm{loc}}:=N\beta_K$ and define the cell normalizer $Z_{\mathrm{loc}}:=Z_i=\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathcal H_i}e^{-\beta_{\mathrm{loc}}G_{\mathrm{loc}}}=\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathcal H_i}e^{-\beta_KG_K}>0$. Let $\sigma$ be the cell Gibbs density supplied by that theorem, so $\sigma=Z_{\mathrm{loc}}^{-1}e^{-\beta_{\mathrm{loc}}G_{\mathrm{loc}}}=Z_{\mathrm{loc}}^{-1}e^{-\beta_KG_K}$. For neutral Hermitian matrices, define
$$\alpha^{\mathrm{loc}}_\tau(C):=e^{iG_{\mathrm{loc}}\tau/\hbar}Ce^{-iG_{\mathrm{loc}}\tau/\hbar},\qquad \alpha^K_t(C):=e^{iG_Kt/\hbar}Ce^{-iG_Kt/\hbar}.$$
For this selected cell, let $q$ be the common charge in coulombs and $\Phi_i:=\Phi_\xi(x_i)$ its compensated potential in volts from the supplier. Define $H_{K,i}:=NH_{\mathrm{loc}}+q\Phi_iQ$ in joules, and write $H_K:=H_{K,i}$ in the physical control below. The supplier normalization gives $N\mu_{\mathrm{loc}}+q\Phi_i=\mu_K$, hence $H_K=G_K+\mu_KQ$.

Let $A=A^*$ and $B=B^*$ satisfy $[A,Q]=[B,Q]=0$. Then their local proper-time and Killing-time dynamics agree after $\tau=Nt$.

Let $h:[0,S]\to\mathbb R$ be continuous, with $S>0$ seconds, and let $h(\tau)B$ have units joules. For dimensionless $\epsilon\in\mathbb R$, the proper-time grand-generator control $G_{\mathrm{loc}}-\epsilon h(\tau)B$ corresponds in Killing time to $G_K-\epsilon N h(Nt)B$. Define $E_{\mathrm{loc}}(\tau)$ and $E_K(t)$ as the finite-time expectations of $A$ from the same initial density $\sigma$ under these controls. Because $A$ and $B$ commute with $Q$, replacing each grand generator by its physical Hamiltonian changes the propagator only by a unitary generated by a constant multiple of $Q$ and leaves the expectation of $A$ unchanged. Thus these expectations also equal those obtained from the respective physical controls $H_{\mathrm{loc}}-\epsilon h(\tau)B$ and $H_K-\epsilon Nh(Nt)B$. In particular,
$$E_K(t)=E_{\mathrm{loc}}(Nt)\qquad(0\le t\le S/N).$$
The finite-time Kubo expansions have identical first-order terms under this rescaling and the same quadratic remainder bound. With
$$R_{\mathrm{loc}}(u):=\frac{i}{\hbar}\operatorname{Tr}\!\left(\sigma[\alpha^{\mathrm{loc}}_u(A),B]\right),\qquad C_S:=\frac{2}{\hbar}\int_0^S|h(s)|\,\|B\|\,ds,$$
the local expansion is
$$E_{\mathrm{loc}}(\tau)=\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma\alpha^{\mathrm{loc}}_\tau(A))+\epsilon\int_0^\tau h(s)R_{\mathrm{loc}}(\tau-s)\,ds+r_{\mathrm{loc}}(\tau),\qquad |r_{\mathrm{loc}}(\tau)|\le\|A\|\frac{(|\epsilon|C_S)^2e^{|\epsilon|C_S}}2.$$
Use $\hbar>0$ in joule-seconds, so $C_S$ is dimensionless and $R_{\mathrm{loc}}$ has units $[A][B]/(\mathrm{J\,s})$. The Killing-time response kernel is $R_K(t)=R_{\mathrm{loc}}(Nt)$ and its control amplitude is $Nh(Nt)$.

For the equilibrium correlation spectral measures, use the Fourier convention of [[thm-qsm-dt-spectral-fdt]]. If $D_N(\omega):=N\omega$, then
$$S_K=(D_N)_*S_{\mathrm{loc}},\qquad S_K(\omega)=N^{-1}S_{\mathrm{loc}}(\omega/N)$$
in distribution notation; the measure form preserves the weights of delta-function spectral lines. Since $\beta_K\hbar(N\omega)=\beta_{\mathrm{loc}}\hbar\omega$, detailed balance is unchanged. Any zero-frequency atom remains an atom at zero and is not assigned a value by the coth formula, which remains restricted to nonzero frequencies.

For a static local perturbation $G_{\mathrm{loc}}-\lambda B$, where $\lambda B$ has units joules, the re-prepared Gibbs family satisfies
$$\left.\frac{d}{d\lambda}\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma_\lambda B)\right|_{\lambda=0}=\beta_{\mathrm{loc}}\operatorname{Cov}_{\mathrm{KM},\sigma}(B,B)=N\beta_K\operatorname{Cov}_{\mathrm{KM},\sigma}(B,B)\ge0.$$
Here $\delta B:=B-\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma B)I$ and
$$\operatorname{Cov}_{\mathrm{KM},\sigma}(B,B):=\int_0^1\operatorname{Tr}\!\left(\sigma^{1-u}\delta B\,\sigma^u\delta B\right)\,du.$$
It reduces to the ordinary variance when $B$ commutes with $G_{\mathrm{loc}}$; no such reduction is asserted otherwise. These are exact finite-dimensional deductions for the constructed neutral cell. They imply no infinite-time relaxation, dc transport coefficient, or response law for arbitrary interacting reduced states.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** One finite cell of the stated independent-cell construction, its positive lapse and Gibbs density, neutral Hermitian matrices $A,B$, a continuous control on a finite proper-time interval, and the stated units.

[F1] [[thm-tgr-finite-cell-gibbs-factorization]]: for label $i$ at $x_i=\chi(i)$, $N=N_i=N(x_i)$ and the local data are $H_{\mathrm{loc}}=H_i$, $Q=Q_i$, and $\mu_{\mathrm{loc}}=\mu_i$; under the stated whole-shell stationarity, global-compensator, and $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$ hypotheses it supplies $\beta_{\mathrm{loc}}=N\beta_K$, $G_K=NG_{\mathrm{loc}}$, $H_{K,i}=NH_{\mathrm{loc}}+q\Phi_iQ=G_K+\mu_KQ$, and $\sigma=Z_{\mathrm{loc}}^{-1}e^{-\beta_{\mathrm{loc}}G_{\mathrm{loc}}}$. Here $Z_{\mathrm{loc}}=Z_i$ is the cell trace normalizer, whereas the supplier's total partition sum is $Z=\prod_j Z_j$. On observables commuting with number, grand-generator and physical dynamics agree. Its $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$ assumption is [[def-countable-choice]]: every countable family of nonempty sets has a choice function; no stronger choice principle is used. This current draft supplier has a complete conditional argument; its ready record is authoring readiness, not independent proof approval.

[F2] [[thm-qsm-dt-finite-kubo]]: for a finite Hermitian generator $G$, fixed Gibbs initial density $\sigma=e^{-\beta G}/Z$, Hermitian $A,B$, continuous $h$, and finite interval, the control $G-\epsilon hB$ has first-order kernel $i\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma[\alpha_u(A),B])/\hbar$ and the explicit quadratic remainder in its Statement. The current QSM item is draft with a current ready Step 1 record, not independent proof approval.

[F3] [[thm-qsm-dt-spectral-fdt]]: for a finite Gibbs matrix and Hermitian $A$, the correlation has a finite positive spectral measure with delta lines and detailed balance; its zero-frequency atom is retained separately and the coth identity is restricted to nonzero frequencies. The current QSM item is draft with a current ready Step 1 record, not independent proof approval.

[F4] [[lem-qsm-matrix-exponential-response]]: for finite Hermitian $G_{\mathrm{loc}}$, $\beta_{\mathrm{loc}}>0$, and Hermitian $B$, the derivative of the re-prepared Gibbs expectation is $\beta_{\mathrm{loc}}\operatorname{Cov}_{\mathrm{KM},\sigma}(B,B)\ge0$; this equals $\beta_{\mathrm{loc}}\operatorname{Var}_\sigma(B)$ only if $B$ commutes with the Gibbs density.

## Proof

1.1 By [F1], $\beta_{\mathrm{loc}}=N\beta_K$ and $\sigma$ is the same normalized density for $G_{\mathrm{loc}}$ and $G_K=NG_{\mathrm{loc}}$. For neutral matrices the chemical term proportional to $Q$ does not change conjugation, so $\alpha^K_t(A)=\alpha^{\mathrm{loc}}_{Nt}(A)$ and the same holds for $B$. [given, F1, algebra]

2.1 If $U_{\mathrm{loc}}(\tau)$ solves $i\hbar U'_{\mathrm{loc}}=(G_{\mathrm{loc}}-\epsilon h(\tau)B)U_{\mathrm{loc}}$, then the chain rule gives $$i\hbar\frac{d}{dt}U_{\mathrm{loc}}(Nt)=\bigl(G_K-\epsilon N h(Nt)B\bigr)U_{\mathrm{loc}}(Nt).$$ Both grand-generator propagators have the same identity initial condition. Uniqueness of the finite-dimensional controlled evolution in [F2] gives $U_K(t)=U_{\mathrm{loc}}(Nt)$ and equality of the grand-generator expectations. To compare with the physical controls, note that $H_{\mathrm{loc}}=G_{\mathrm{loc}}+\mu_{\mathrm{loc}}Q$ and $H_K=G_K+\mu_KQ$. Each grand controlled generator commutes with $Q$ at every time because $[G_{\mathrm{loc}},Q]=[B,Q]=0$. Therefore the corresponding physical propagator equals its grand propagator multiplied by $e^{-i\mu_{\mathrm{loc}}Q\tau/\hbar}$ locally or $e^{-i\mu_KQt/\hbar}$ in Killing time; differentiating these products verifies their evolution equations and identity initial data. Since $[A,Q]=0$, conjugation by these extra factors leaves $A$ fixed, so physical and grand expectations agree. The two exact expectation equalities imply $E_K(t)=E_{\mathrm{loc}}(Nt)$. [given, F1, F2, step 1.1, algebra]

2.2 Apply [F2] on $[0,S]$ locally and on $[0,S/N]$ in Killing time. Its Killing kernel is $R_K(t)=R_{\mathrm{loc}}(Nt)$ by step 1.1. The first-order terms agree because $$\epsilon\int_0^tNh(Ns)R_{\mathrm{loc}}(N(t-s))\,ds=\epsilon\int_0^{Nt}h(v)R_{\mathrm{loc}}(Nt-v)\,dv$$ after $v=Ns$. Also $$\frac2\hbar\int_0^{S/N}|Nh(Ns)|\|B\|\,ds=C_S,$$ so the two Kubo remainder bounds agree at corresponding times. [given, F2, step 1.1, algebra]

2.3 Diagonalize $G_{\mathrm{loc}}$ with eigenvalues $E_j$ and eigenvectors $u_j$. The density weights $p_j=Z_{\mathrm{loc}}^{-1}e^{-\beta_{\mathrm{loc}}E_j}$ are also the weights for $G_K$, whose eigenvalues are $NE_j$; hence each transition frequency is multiplied by $N$, while each weight and matrix element of $A$ is unchanged. By [F3], $S_K=(D_N)_*S_{\mathrm{loc}}$, equivalently the displayed distribution scaling. Since $\beta_K\hbar N\omega=\beta_{\mathrm{loc}}\hbar\omega$, detailed balance is preserved; $D_N(0)=0$ carries any zero-frequency atom to itself, where [F3] gives no coth formula. [given, F3, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Apply [F4] to the local re-prepared density $\sigma_\lambda\propto e^{-\beta_{\mathrm{loc}}(G_{\mathrm{loc}}-\lambda B)}$ to obtain the stated Kubo–Mori derivative. Since $G_{\mathrm{loc}}$ is finite Hermitian and $x\mapsto e^{-\beta_{\mathrm{loc}}x}$ is injective on its real spectrum, $B$ commutes with $\sigma$ exactly when it commutes with $G_{\mathrm{loc}}$ (both preserve the same eigenspace decomposition). Thus the variance reduction in [F4] has precisely the condition stated here; the finite-time expansion in step 2.2 instead keeps the initial $\sigma$ fixed. [given, F4, step 1.1, step 2.2, algebra] ∎

## Remarks

The response variables are finite matrices and finite-time controls, not measured apparatus data. The exact Kubo expansion and the static derivative concern different preparations: the former fixes $\sigma$, while the latter re-normalizes the Gibbs family for each $\lambda$.
