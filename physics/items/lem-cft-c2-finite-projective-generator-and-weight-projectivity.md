## Statement

Let V be a C2-cofinite vertex operator algebra with finite-dimensional nonnegative integral state grades over C; V0 need not equal C1. The category C of all grading-restricted generalized V-modules has a projective generator P which is itself grading-restricted and finitely generated. The algebra A=End_V(P)^op is finite dimensional. The mutually inverse exact equivalences
 H(M)=Hom_V(P,M),  E(U)=P tensor_A U
identify C with the category of finite-dimensional unital left A-modules. Every generalized-weight space P_[h] is a finite projective right A-module. Hence P itself is projective as a right A-module, and if U is a finite (A,R)-bimodule projective on the right over a finite-dimensional algebra R, every weight space of P tensor_A U, and that whole module, is projective on the right over R.

For every k>=1 the joint generalized-module category is the finite external product of k copies of C: external products of the constructed projectives represent the exact joint-weight functors and form a projective generator. In particular the two-component category is equivalent to finite A tensor A-modules, without assuming its objects split as tensor products. Contragredience gives also an exact equivalence with finite A-bimodules, normalized on external objects by
 Phi(M tensor N')=H(M) tensor_C H(N)^*.
Here the star on the right is ordinary finite-dimensional vector-space duality.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The stated C2-cofinite algebra and actual weak-module convention; all category maps commute with the fields.

[F1] [[lem-cft-nonnegative-c2-mode-window-finiteness]] and [[lem-cft-higher-zhu-action-on-weak-omega-spaces]] supply the finite-dimensional universal zero-mode image algebra Z_N and its action on Omega_N of ANY weak module. This image algebra is used in place of assuming a CFT-only higher-Zhu quotient theorem covers a larger vacuum space.

[F2] [[lem-cft-nonnegative-c2-mode-window-finiteness]] proves finite degree spans and a lower degree cutoff for every cyclic weak module. The finite-generation and grading-restriction facts needed here are derived below from that bound and the uniform Z_N identities; the broader vacuum scope is not obtained by silently invoking a CFT-only module theorem.

[F3] The VOA/module Jacobi axioms supply all coefficient, translation and conformal weight commutators. In this full nonnegative scope contragredience is also proved by the same explicit opposite substitution: A(x)=exp(xL1)(-x^-2)^L0 is finite on each state, transposing Jacobi and substituting inverse variables gives module Jacobi on the restricted dual, and A(x^-1)A(x)=1 proves double evaluation intertwines fields. Positive L1 is locally nilpotent by the lower bounds; integral state weights remove the branch on A(x). Exactness is weightwise finite-dimensional duality. These calculations use nonnegative state grades, not V0=C1.

## Proof


1.1 Choose a monic annihilating polynomial p_0(t) of o(omega) in the finite zero-mode image algebra Z_0, and let E be its finite root set. Every nonzero weight coset of a grading-restricted module has a lowest weight, since its real parts are bounded below and its integer shifts are discrete. Its lowest space is contained in Omega_0, so p_0(L0)=0 there by F1. Thus its least weight lies in E. Since every occupied coset contains a root in the finite set E, there are only finitely many cosets. Consequently EVERY object has support in E+N, with the uniform real-part lower bound b=min_{e in E} Re(e). If E is empty the algebra has no nonzero unital module, contradicting its own regular representation and the vacuum module; equivalently its monic annihilating polynomial cannot be a nonzero constant. [F1, F2, algebra]

2.1 Put B=max_{e in E} Re(e) and Lambda=(E+N) intersect {z:Re(z)<=B}. This is finite. Every object W is generated by its Lambda weight spaces. Indeed the submodule U they generate is grading-restricted; its quotient is grading-restricted as well, by finite weight-space quotients and the same lower bound. If W/U were nonzero, one of its cosets would have a lowest weight in E, hence in Lambda, whereas all its Lambda spaces are zero. This is impossible. Choose an integer N>=B-b. A vector of any Lambda weight belongs to Omega_N(W), because a_m lowers its real weight below b whenever m>=wt(a)+N. A monic annihilating polynomial p_N(t) of o(omega) in the finite zero-mode image algebra Z_N therefore annihilates L0 on all these weight spaces, UNIFORMLY over all W. Discard lambda in Lambda which are not roots of p_N and let K_lambda be the multiplicity of each remaining root. Then (L0-lambda)^{K_lambda}=0 on W_[lambda] for every W. This is a uniform polynomial identity, not the object-dependent local nilpotence in a generalized-module definition. [F1, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 We construct a representing object P_lambda rather than assuming projective covers exist. Start with finite words in symbols a(m), linear in states a, on a generator e of formal weight lambda; give a(m) integer degree wt(a)-m-1. Impose the conformal commutator [L0,a(m)]=(wt(a)-m-1)a(m), the vacuum and translation identities, and (L0-lambda)^{K_lambda}e=0. Quotient by the submodule generated by all weight spaces of real part below b. Modes are then lower truncated on EVERY vector, since a sufficiently large mode index lowers its real weight below b. Impose all coefficients of the weak-module Jacobi identity. These are actual finite relations on each fixed word: in the iterate formula its two sums terminate because the rightmost positive-index mode lowers the word below b, and the state commutator sums terminate by state truncation. Equivalently impose the usual Borcherds coefficient identity in this already lower-truncated graded word module. The quotient therefore is a weak module with generator e and algebraic generalized weights in lambda+Z, all with real part at least b. This construction is a free presentation by explicitly valid weak-module relations; it is allowed to give zero when no such vector can occur. The imposed nilpotence propagates to every word by the conformal commutator. It is generated by one vector, and its degree-d span is finite dimensional by F2. Distinct degrees have distinct generalized weights lambda+d, so it is grading-restricted. Denote it by P_lambda. [F2, F3, step 2.1, construct]

4.1 For every W in C, every w in W_[lambda] satisfies exactly the universal relations just imposed: the lower bound is step 1.1, the nilpotence is step 2.1, and the remaining relations are the actual module identities. It consequently defines a unique module map P_lambda->W taking e to w. Conversely every module map has such an image. Thus
 Hom_V(P_lambda,W)=W_[lambda]
naturally and linearly. The generalized-weight functor on C is exact. To see surjectivity, lift a generalized-weight vector through an epimorphism; its preimage has finite weight support, and polynomial projections in L0 isolate its lambda component while preserving its image. Kernels are computed weightwise. Therefore P_lambda is projective. A map from a finite sum of these objects surjects onto any W by step 2.1 and finite dimensionality of its Lambda spaces. Put P=sum_lambda P_lambda. It is a finite direct sum of grading-restricted finitely generated modules and is the desired projective generator. [F2, step 3.1, step 2.1, algebra]

5.1 A module map P->W is determined by its finitely many generating vector images in the finite spaces W_[lambda]. Hence H(W)=Hom(P,W) is finite dimensional, and A=End(P)^op is finite dimensional. P has the right A-action p dot f^op=f(p), and H(W) has the left action f^op dot g=g composed with f. The evaluation map P tensor_A H(W)->W is balanced. It is an isomorphism on finite direct sums of P. Every W has a finite presentation P^r->P^s->W->0: its kernel is a grading-restricted submodule and hence finitely generated by F2, so another finite sum of P surjects onto it. Since H is exact by projectivity, applying H and then the right-exact tensor construction to this presentation proves evaluation is an isomorphism for W. Conversely a finite presentation of any finite-dimensional A-module by finite free A-modules shows H(P tensor_A U)=U; P tensor_A U is a quotient of a finite sum of P and belongs to C. These calculations prove both equivalences and their exactness. No semisimplicity of A or C was assumed. [F2, step 4.1, algebra]

6.1 For every weight h, the functor U -> (P tensor_A U)_[h]=P_[h] tensor_A U is exact, since both E and the generalized-weight functor are exact. The finite right A-module P_[h] is therefore projective. Here is an elementary finite-dimensional proof of the last implication. Exactness of that tensor functor makes its vector-space dual Hom_A(-,P_[h]^*) exact, so P_[h]^* is injective among finite left A-modules. Dualize a finite free right-module epimorphism A^d->P_[h]; the resulting embedding into (A^*)^d splits by this injectivity. Dualizing the splitting makes P_[h] a summand of A^d. Direct sums over h show that P is projective as a right A-module. If U is projective right R, tensoring a chosen summand decomposition P_[h] direct-summand A^d with U makes P_[h] tensor_A U a summand of U^d, hence projective right R. Direct sums prove the whole-module assertion. [step 5.1, step 4.1, algebra]

6.2 For a joint module, fix all generalized weights except one. The resulting component fiber is a grading-restricted one-component module, so step 1.1 gives the same coordinatewise lower bound b and support E+N. The all-weak Omega_N identity applies to a component even when its other-coordinate sum is infinite; hence step 2.1 gives the same nilpotence K_lambda on every coordinate-window space. Because the component modes commute, the external product P_lambda1 tensor ... tensor P_lambdak represents the functor taking a joint module to W_[lambda1,...,lambdak]. A homomorphism is determined by the image of the tensor of its universal generators; the independent component universal relations and their commutation prove existence for every such vector. That functor is exact by joint polynomial projections. For the joint generating-window assertion, a nonzero quotient outside that generated window has a weight of least total real part, since it has finitely many integer cosets and coordinatewise lower bounds. Its space is killed by every negative-degree component mode, so every coordinate is a root in E; this contradicts vanishing of the joint window. These external projectives thus give a projective generator. Homomorphisms between two external products split as tensor products of component Hom spaces: images of finitely many generators involve finitely many vectors in the other factors, and coefficientwise component linearity gives the asserted factorization. The same finite-presentation argument as step 5.1 proves the joint equivalence with finite A^{tensor k}-modules. Objects themselves were never assumed external tensor products. [F2, F3, step 1.1, step 2.1, step 4.1, step 5.1, algebra]

7.1 Exact contragredience identifies C^op with C. On the finite module category it can be applied in the second factor of the preceding external-product equivalence. More explicitly, use the projective generator P in the first factor and an injective cogenerator I in the underlying second copy of C (the equivalence E sends the finite left A-module A^* to one: Hom_A(-,A^*) is ordinary vector-space duality); I' is a projective generator because duality is exact and involutive. External objects M tensor N', with M projective and N injective, form projective generators of the joint category. Send them to H(M) tensor H(N)^*, which are projective generators of the A-bimodule category. Their Hom spaces factor as in step 6.2 and are identical under this assignment. Extend through finite projective presentations. This constructs the claimed exact equivalence Phi and its stated formula on all external objects, by exactness. It does not require identifying the contragredient of an arbitrary joint module with a partial vector-space dual or presuming P self-dual. [F3, step 5.1, step 6.2, algebra] ∎
