## Statement

For a compact relatively spin Lagrangian in the geometrically tame regime and admissible $J$ of the geometrically tame disk-atlas supplier, there is a coherent rational chain-valued disk perturbation system on its actual collared atlas, with class/energy compatibility, supported evaluation/Morse matching, and relative branch extensions over all tree faces. Its finite Morse model $A$ has energy-filtered degree-one suspended operations $m_k$, $k\ge0$, with $m_{1,0}$ its actual Morse differential, $m_{0,0}=0$, $m_0$ of positive valuation, and curved coderivation $D^2=0$. Coefficients are finite rational weighted counts at each energy/arity cutoff. The codimension-one boundary is precisely the signed nested-operation sum; sphere-node strata have codimension two. The geometric evaluation correspondence and Morse model have the explicit external-edge chain comparisons constructed below. A prescribed finite symmetry of the chosen disk atlas, intertwining its Cauchy–Riemann equation and product collars, is exactly respected when it also permutes the selected transverse Morse/edge model and preserves the chosen orientation and relative-spin datum through a specified lift; no action of a symplectomorphism failing to preserve these analytic data, or arbitrary fixed-model strictification, is asserted.

The integer grading uses the coefficient ring $\Lambda^0_{\mathbb Q,e}$ of formal sums $\sum_{\lambda,j}c_{\lambda,j}T^\lambda e^j$, with $c_{\lambda,j}\in\mathbb Q$, $\lambda\in\mathbb R_{\ge0}$, $j\in\mathbb Z$, and only finitely many nonzero pairs with $\lambda<R$ for every real $R$. Multiplication is convolution, $\deg T^\lambda=0$, $\deg e=2$, and completion is in the energy filtration $F^R$ defined by $\lambda\ge R$. Extend the finite rational Morse module to this ring and complete in energy. For a smooth relative disk representative of $\beta$, let $\mu(\beta)$ be the first Chern number of the conjugate double of its complex tangent pullback with real boundary bundle $TL$; this is the Maslov index in the bordered index convention. It is even because $L$ is oriented. The bare class operation $m_{k,\beta}$ has suspended degree $1-\mu(\beta)$, and
$$m_k=\sum_\beta T^{\omega(\beta)}e^{\mu(\beta)/2}m_{k,\beta}.$$
Degrees on the energy completion mean the completed grading. Specializing $e=1$ gives the same geometric counts, chain comparisons and curved identities over the rational energy-only Novikov ring, with degree one interpreted modulo two. No integer degree-one assertion is made for the energy-only specialization unless all contributing Maslov indices vanish.
