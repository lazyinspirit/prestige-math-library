## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]) inherited from the finite
coherent-resolution and cohomological suppliers. Let $k$ be an algebraically
closed field and let $f:X\to Y$ be a projective morphism of nonsingular
irreducible quasi-projective $k$-schemes of finite type
([[def-projective-morphism-pre-proj]], [[def-smooth-morphism-schemes]],
[[def-proper-morphism]]); projectivity is exactly the hypothesis that $f$ factors
as a closed immersion $i:X\hookrightarrow\mathbb P^n_k\times_kY$ followed by the
projection $p:\mathbb P^n_k\times_kY\to Y$ for some $n\ge0$
([[lem-projective-morphism-relative-proj-presentation]]). Then for every
$\alpha\in K_0(X)$
([[def-grothendieck-group-of-coherent-sheaves-and-vector-bundles-on-a-scheme]],
[[lem-k-zero-vector-bundles-versus-coherent-sheaves]]):
$$\operatorname{ch}\bigl(f_!(\alpha)\bigr)\cdot\operatorname{td}(T_Y)\;=\;f_*\Bigl(\operatorname{ch}(\alpha)\cdot\operatorname{td}(T_X)\Bigr)\qquad\text{in }A^*(Y)_{\mathbb Q},$$
where $\operatorname{ch},\operatorname{td}$ are the Chern character and Todd
class of [[def-chern-character-and-todd-class]], $f_!$ is the $K$-theory
pushforward ([[def-pushforward-in-algebraic-k-theory]]) and $f_*$ is the proper
Chow pushforward ([[lem-proper-pushforward-of-cycles-well-defined]]).
Equivalently $\tau_Y\circ f_!=f_*\circ\tau_X$ for
$\tau_S(\alpha)=\operatorname{ch}(\alpha)\operatorname{td}(T_S)$. For
$Y=\operatorname{Spec}k$ this is Hirzebruch-Riemann-Roch
$\chi(X,\mathcal F)=\deg(\operatorname{ch}(\mathcal F)\operatorname{td}(T_X))$
([[def-euler-characteristic-coherent-sheaf]]).

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** the Axiom of Choice; an algebraically closed field $k$; a projective morphism $f:X\to Y$ of nonsingular irreducible quasi-projective finite type $k$-schemes; a factorization $f=p\circ i$ with $i:X\hookrightarrow\mathbb P^n_k\times_kY$ a closed immersion and $p$ the projection.

[L1] Riemann-Roch holds for the closed immersion $i$: $\operatorname{ch}(i_*E)=i_*(\operatorname{ch}(E)\operatorname{td}(N)^{-1})$, equivalently $\tau\circ i_*=i_*\circ\tau$ ([[thm-rr-for-regular-embeddings]]).

[L2] Riemann-Roch holds for the projection $p$: $\tau_Y\circ p_!=p_*\circ\tau_{\mathbb P^n\times_kY}$ ([[thm-rr-for-projective-space-projections]]).

[L3] $K$-theory pushforward is functorial under composition, $(p\circ i)_!=p_!\circ i_!$, and Chow proper pushforward is functorial, $(p\circ i)_*=p_*i_*$; $K_0$ on the smooth quasi-projective source is generated by vector bundles ([[def-pushforward-in-algebraic-k-theory]], [[lem-proper-pushforward-of-cycles-well-defined]], [[lem-k-zero-vector-bundles-versus-coherent-sheaves]]).

[L4] The projection formula and naturality of the Chern character and Todd class for flat morphisms identify the two composites; the target of $\tau$ is the codimension-graded Chow group of a smooth scheme ([[lem-chow-ring-naturality-and-projection-formula]], [[lem-chern-character-and-todd-class-multiplicativity]], [[lem-projection-formula-in-algebraic-k-theory]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct; factor the projective morphism into a closed immersion followed by a projective-space projection and compose the two Riemann-Roch squares.

1.1 The factorization. By the relative Proj presentation of a projective morphism, $f$ factors as $f=p\circ i$ with $i:X\hookrightarrow\mathbb P^n_k\times_kY$ a closed immersion of nonsingular varieties and $p$ the projection; both factors are morphisms of nonsingular irreducible quasi-projective $k$-schemes, and $p$ is the projection to the second factor. [L3, given, algebra]

2.1 Composition of the two squares. Consider the two commutative squares: $\tau\circ i_*=i_*\circ\tau$ on $X\to\mathbb P^n\times_kY$ by [L1], and $\tau\circ p_!=p_*\circ\tau$ on $\mathbb P^n\times_kY\to Y$ by [L2]. Composing them gives $\tau_Y\circ p_!\circ i_!=p_*\circ i_*\circ\tau_X$, and by the functoriality of both pushforwards, $p_!\circ i_!=f_!$ and $p_*i_*=f_*$, this is exactly $\tau_Y\circ f_!=f_*\circ\tau_X$, the displayed formula. [L1, L2, L3, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Coherent classes and Hirzebruch-Riemann-Roch. Since $X$ is smooth and quasi-projective, $K_0(X)=K^0(X)$ and the identity proved for vector bundles extends to all coherent classes by additivity of $\operatorname{ch}$, of $f_!$ and of $f_*$ along finite resolutions [L3]. For $Y=\operatorname{Spec}k$, the Chow pushforward is the degree and the $K$-theory pushforward is the Euler characteristic, so the formula becomes Hirzebruch-Riemann-Roch $\chi(X,\mathcal F)=\deg(\operatorname{ch}(\mathcal F)\operatorname{td}(T_X))$; the transport of $\operatorname{ch}$ and $\operatorname{td}$ to coherent classes uses the naturality and multiplicativity of [L4]. [L3, L4, step 2.1, given, algebra] ∎
