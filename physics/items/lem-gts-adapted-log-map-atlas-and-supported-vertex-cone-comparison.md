## Statement

Assume AC. Let R be a characteristic-zero discrete valuation ring, with uniformizer t. Let Q/R be the proper separated regular logarithmically smooth simple-normal-crossing algebraic-space target supplied by a divisorial toric-pushout family model. Its entire generic pair is the specified smooth projective pair (X,D). Assume the model has finitely many global Cartier divisor line-and-section generators and their tensor and base identifications. Fix a nonzero genus-zero curve class and finitely many labelled contacts and markings. Use the actual logarithmic domain and basic ghost base B; its objects specify the source curve, ghost homomorphisms and base line-and-section identifications. Assume the supplied central projective pieces and their double curves admit the projective relative map constructions in the projective foundations. For the torus-translation assertion below, each relevant double curve is a projective line with its two boundary points.

Then the actual stable basic Q-map functor M is an algebraic stack locally of finite presentation, with Deligne-Mumford stable charts. It has an adapted smooth atlas on arbitrary scheme bases, formed by finite target-lattice diagrams and positive whole-source canonical resolution roofs. The universal source differential and nodal trace give the actual relative perfect obstruction theory E=(Rπ_*g^*T_Q^log)^∨→L_(M/B). Its restriction to the entire generic pair is canonically the original generic obstruction theory and intrinsic cone, including scheme multiplicities.

Let H be the supplied proper projective-support stack of lifted maps and their pulled-back Q logarithmic enhancements, with generic restriction the original generic map stack. Q-stabilization defines an actual proper h:H→M, with E_H canonically h^*E; no perfect obstruction theory on H is asserted. Proper pushforward of the closure of the original generic cone, followed by the zero-section and trait-fiber refined Gysin operations on proper supports, equals specialization of that original generic virtual class. The finite supported type inventory gives the same class as the full compactified vertex operation on the proper aligned enhancement spaces. At a double-curve corner the matching immersion is the logarithmic diagonal, with normal T_E(−S), rather than the ordinary diagonal. Its full operation retains nontransverse types, boundary components, Q-constant projective lifts and nilpotents. Enhancement and primitive-base multiplicities give the compact-edge factors, with the usual finite automorphism quotients. Independent torus translations of the edge logarithmic diagonals preserve this proper cycle operation. Interior-root cotangent insertions agree after choosing the root outside the projective exceptional image. Consequently full local projective vertex expansions may be substituted in this finite supported operation before restricting to a rigid transverse incidence sector.
