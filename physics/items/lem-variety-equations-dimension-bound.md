## Statement

Let $X$ be an irreducible classical variety of dimension $n$, and let $f_1,\ldots,f_r$ be global regular functions, with $r\ge0$. Every nonempty irreducible component $Z$ of their common zero set has $\dim Z\ge n-r$.

Work over a fixed algebraically closed field $k$, with the Axiom of Choice. Classical varieties are separated and admit finite affine covers; they may be reducible or empty unless irreducibility is specified. Irreducible means nonempty. All fibres and points below are classical closed-point fibres and points.

## Facts & Assumptions

[F2] If $U$ is a nonempty open of an irreducible classical variety $X$, then $\dim U=\dim X$. Every proper closed subvariety $Z\subsetneq X$ has $\dim Z<\dim X$. [F3] Under the Axiom of Choice, if $R$ is Noetherian, $I=(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$ has $n\ge1$ generators, and $\mathfrak p$ is minimal over $I$, then $\operatorname{ht}(\mathfrak p)\le n$ ([[thm-krull-height-theorem]]).

[F4] Under the Axiom of Choice, if $k$ is a field, $A$ is a finite-type $k$-domain, and $\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Spec}(A)$, then $\operatorname{ht}(\mathfrak p)+\dim(A/\mathfrak p)=\dim A$ ([[cor-height-plus-quotient-dimension-affine-domain]]).

## Proof

1.1 For $r=0$ the zero set is $X$ and the bound is equality. Suppose $r\ge1$ and fix a nonempty component $Z$. Choose a nonempty affine chart $U$ meeting $Z$ away from the other finitely many components of the zero set. Then $Z\cap U$ is a component of the affine zero locus. Both $U$ and $Z\cap U$ have the dimensions of $X$ and $Z$ respectively. [F2]

2.1 The prime $\mathfrak p$ defining $Z\cap U$ is minimal over the ideal generated by the restrictions of the $r$ functions in the Noetherian domain $k[U]$. Hence $\operatorname{ht}\mathfrak p\le r$. The height formula and geometric/ring comparison give $\dim Z=\dim k[U]/\mathfrak p=n-\operatorname{ht}\mathfrak p\ge n-r$. Zero or redundant equations cause no problem; if the zero set is empty there is no component to test. [F1, F3, F4, step 1.1] ∎
