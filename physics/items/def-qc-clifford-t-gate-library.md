## Definition

Let $\omega=e^{i\pi/4}$. The one-qubit generators and exact matrices in the
ordered computational basis are

$$H=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix},\qquad T=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&\omega\end{pmatrix},\qquad T^\dagger=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&\omega^{-1}\end{pmatrix}.$$

On basis vectors, $H|q\rangle=(|0\rangle+(-1)^q|1\rangle)/\sqrt2$ and
$T^k|q\rangle=\omega^{kq}|q\rangle$ for $q\in\{0,1\}$.

Write $S=T^2=\operatorname{diag}(1,i)$ and
$Z=T^4=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$. Pauli $X,Y,Z$ are as in
[[def-qc-pauli-matrices]]. On two ordered wires,
$\operatorname{CNOT}|a,b\rangle=|a,b\mathbin\oplus a\rangle$; the first wire
is control and the second target. A circuit is a finite chronological word of
these gates at stated wire positions; its matrix is the product with later
gates on the left. No extra wire or controlled-$T$ operation is implicit.

The scalar phase $\omega I$ is an allowed Clifford phase in the one-qubit
matrix-word convention. Its scalar factor is retained in exact matrix products;
the convention that an overall scalar does not affect a resource count is
stated separately from matrix equality. It does not grant a controlled-$T$
operation or allow a scalar on a target wire to be discarded under control.

The exact one-qubit H/T algebra is generated by $H,T,T^\dagger$; $S$ and $Z$
are aliases for the displayed powers. The distinction between exact matrix
equality and [[def-qc-global-phase-equivalence|global-phase equivalence]] is
maintained. Resource costs are supplied by
[[def-qc-elementary-circuit-resources]] and remain separate from this matrix
definition.

## Unresolved supplier checks

The actual QC07 Pauli/gate bodies and QC08 controlled-unitary body were
inspected; their displayed matrices and control-first phase convention agree
with this definition. The actual QC09 resource-vector body was inspected: it
defines size, depth, width, and separately declared weighted/query costs.
Scalar phase remains a matrix-word convention and is not an extra QC09 gate
call. The actual QC07/08 gate interfaces and QC09 resource interface agree;
this is reconciliation, not acceptance of their draft content.
