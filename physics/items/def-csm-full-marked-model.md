## Definition

Assume AC. Fix $d\geq1$ and let $\phi:\mathbb R^d\to\mathbb R\cup\{+\infty\}$ be the numerical even Borel pair potential. Assume the stability, cell-superstability, lower-regularity, and decreasing-integrable-tail conditions of [[def-csm-stable-superstable-continuum-interaction]], with its fixed cell side $\ell$, and the superstable regular interaction, unit-coordinate, and periodic-image conventions of [[def-tdmath-superstable-regular-interaction]]. These are model hypotheses; the definitions do not prove that a particular material satisfies them. Use the finite-region phase-space and statistical-state conventions of [[def-csm-statistical-state-observable]].

Let $E_0>0$ joules be the energy unit, $\ell_0>0$ metres the length unit, $m>0$ kilograms the particle mass, and $p_*>0$ kg m s$^{-1}$ the momentum reference. A physical position vector $\mathbf q_{\rm phys}\in\mathbb R^d$ in metres has dimensionless numerical coordinate $x=\mathbf q_{\rm phys}/\ell_0$. From here on, $q\subseteq\Lambda_L=[0,L)^d$ denotes a finite configuration of these numerical coordinates, so the physical cube has side $\ell_0L$ and its points are $\ell_0x$. Each momentum mark $p_x\in\mathbb R^d$ is an SI momentum vector in kg m s$^{-1}$. Write $V_L=L^d$ for numerical volume. Fix also a primitive positive scalar conversion parameter $k_B$ with units J K$^{-1}$, called the Boltzmann constant: the thermal-energy scale assigned to temperature $T$ is $k_BT$ joules. Its value is fixed as part of the model data, not derived by this mathematical definition. If $T>0$ kelvin, put $\beta_{\rm phys}=1/(k_BT)$ in J$^{-1}$ and $b=\beta_{\rm phys}E_0=E_0/(k_BT)>0$.

The potential $\phi$ in the two interaction suppliers is numerical in units of $E_0$ and takes dimensionless displacements as input. For a physical displacement $\mathbf r\in\mathbb R^d$ measured in metres, its pair-potential map is $V_{\rm pair}(\mathbf r):=E_0\phi(\mathbf r/\ell_0)$ joules. For a finite positional configuration $q\subseteq\Lambda_L$, set $U_{L,\mathrm{free}}(q):=H_\phi(q)$ using [[def-tdmath-superstable-regular-interaction]], and set $U_{L,\mathrm{per}}(q):=H_{L,\mathrm{per}}(q)$ using that item's periodic-image sum. The periodic definition is used only for periods for which that sum is defined; the large-$L$ results below use sufficiently large periods as supplied there. Thus $E_0U_{L,bc}$ is the dimensional pair energy in either boundary convention.

Let $Q_L$ be the unit-intensity Poisson probability on positional configurations in $\Lambda_L=[0,L)^d$, defined by [[def-tdmath-poisson-configuration-reference]]. Its $N$-particle conditional law $Q_{L,N}$ is uniform on the unordered simple $N$-point configurations; in labelled coordinates it is $L^{-dN}dx_1\cdots dx_N$ on the no-collision set. A finite marked configuration in this sector is a simple set $\xi=\{(x,p_x):x\in q\}$ with $q\subseteq\Lambda_L$, $N(q)=N$, and $p_x\in\mathbb R^d$. Equip the finite marked sector with the sigma-algebra generated by the counts $N_{A\times C}(\xi)=\#\{(x,p_x)\in\xi:x\in A,\ p_x\in C\}$ for bounded Borel $A\subseteq\mathbb R^d$ and Borel $C\subseteq\mathbb R^d$. Let $\lambda_d$ be Lebesgue measure on the SI numerical momentum coordinates and set $r:=p_*^{-d}\lambda_d$. For each fixed $q=\{x_1,\ldots,x_N\}$, define $r^{\otimes q}$ as the image of $r^{\otimes N}$ under $(p_1,\ldots,p_N)\mapsto\{(x_j,p_j):1\le j\le N\}$; this is independent of the enumeration of $q$ because $r^{\otimes N}$ is permutation-invariant. The cell-code representation in [[def-tdmath-poisson-configuration-reference]] supplies a measurable enumeration on each finite sector, so $q\mapsto r^{\otimes q}$ is a measurable kernel for the marked-count sigma-algebra. The finite phase reference is
$$R_{L,N}(dq,dp):=Q_L(dq)\,r^{\otimes q}(dp)\,1_{\{N(q)=N\}}.$$
It is sigma-finite, meaning the marked sector is covered by countably many measurable sets of finite reference measure: for each integer $k\geq1$, restrict to marks satisfying $|p_x|\leq kp_*$ for every one of the $N$ points. These sets exhaust the sector, and their reference measures are at most $Q_L(N(q)=N)r(B_k)^N<\infty$, where $B_k=\{p:|p|\leq kp_*\}$ satisfies $r(B_k)\leq(2k)^d$ because it lies in the box $[-kp_*,kp_*]^d$ of Lebesgue volume $(2kp_*)^d$ by the box-volume supplier in the dependency closure of [[def-tdmath-poisson-configuration-reference]]. The factor $e^{-V_L}/N!$ from the Poisson sector is retained. In physical coordinates the corresponding one-particle product scale is $h_{\rm ref}^d=\ell_0^dp_*^d$, an action to the power $d$; it is a reference convention, not a derived quantum constant.

For a marked configuration define the total dimensionless energy
$$\mathcal H_{L,bc}(q,p)=U_{L,bc}(q)+\sum_{x\in q}K(p_x),\qquad K(p)=\frac{|p|^2}{2mE_0}.$$
The corresponding physical Hamiltonian is $H^{\rm phys}_{L,bc}:=E_0\mathcal H_{L,bc}$ joules, so $\beta_{\rm phys}H^{\rm phys}_{L,bc}=b\mathcal H_{L,bc}$.
For $b>0$, [[lem-csm-gaussian-mark-moments]] gives $\kappa_b=\int e^{-bK}r(dp)=(2\pi mE_0/(bp_*^2))^{d/2}$ and the probability kernel $\nu_b(dp)=\kappa_b^{-1}e^{-bK(p)}r(dp)$. Whenever the positional canonical partition is finite and positive, nonnegative product integration [[thm-tonelli-theorem-for-sigma-finite-product-spaces]] gives exact identities
$$Z^{\rm ph}_{Q,L,N,bc}(b)=\int e^{-b\mathcal H_{L,bc}}dR_{L,N}=\kappa_b^N Z^{\rm pos}_{Q,L,N,bc}(b),\qquad C^{\rm ph}_{L,N,bc}=C^{\rm pos}_{L,N,bc}\,\nu_b^{\otimes q}.$$
Here $Z^{\rm pos}_{Q,L,N,bc}=E_{Q_L}[1_{\{N(q)=N\}}e^{-bU_{L,bc}}]$ and the canonical probabilities are the normalized respective integrands. Under a full grand preparation of activity $z_{\rm ph}$, integrating each momentum changes the positional activity to $z=z_{\rm ph}\kappa_b$; thus a positional activity $z=e^\alpha$ corresponds to $z_{\rm ph}=e^\alpha/\kappa_b$. These are exact definitions and normalizations; they do not assert dynamical equilibration or finite-volume grand convergence. Write $\mathbb T_L^d:=\mathbb R^d/L\mathbb Z^d$ for the periodic position space, represented by $\Lambda_L$; translations act modulo $L$, and the translation-average is Lebesgue measure on $\Lambda_L$ divided by $V_L$. For periodic boundary conditions, simultaneous translation of all positions modulo the period preserves $R_{L,N}$ and $\mathcal H_{L,\mathrm{per}}$: the conditional position law is uniform on $\mathbb T_L^d$, the mark reference is invariant under relabelling points, and the image energy depends only on pair displacements. Hence normalized canonical and finite band restrictions are invariant under every such translation.
