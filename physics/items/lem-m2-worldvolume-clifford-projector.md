## Statement

Let $(V,\eta)$ be an 11-dimensional real vector space with nondegenerate bilinear form of signature $(-,+,\ldots,+)$, let $W\subset V$ be an oriented Lorentzian three-plane, and let $S$ be a 32-dimensional real vector space with a unital $\mathbb R$-algebra representation $\rho:\mathrm{Cl}^+(V,\eta)\to\mathrm{End}_{\mathbb R}(S)$ of the plus-$\eta$ Clifford algebra. For an oriented basis $u_0,u_1,u_2$ of $W$, write $\gamma_i=\rho(\bar u_i)$, $g_{ij}=\eta(u_i,u_j)$, $\gamma_{ij}=\frac12[\gamma_i,\gamma_j]$, $\gamma_{ijk}=\gamma_{[i}\gamma_j\gamma_{k]}$, and $\gamma^i=g^{ij}\gamma_j$. Let $\varepsilon^{012}=+1$ be the oriented alternating symbol and define
$$
\Gamma_W=\frac{\varepsilon^{ijk}\gamma_{ijk}}{3!\sqrt{-\det g}}.
$$
Then $\Gamma_W^2=I$, $\Gamma_W$ commutes with every $\gamma_i$, and
$$
\varepsilon^{ijk}\gamma_{jk}=2\sqrt{-\det g}\,\gamma^i\Gamma_W.
$$
Moreover $\operatorname{tr}_S\Gamma_W=0$, so $P_\pm=(I\pm\Gamma_W)/2$ are complementary projections of rank $16$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** $(V,\eta)$, the oriented Lorentzian three-plane $W$, an oriented basis, and a real 32-dimensional representation $S$ with the plus-$\eta$ Clifford convention.

[F1] The Clifford algebra is the quotient algebra generated by vectors with $\bar v^2=\eta(v,v)1$ ([[def-clifford-algebra-of-a-quadratic-space]]).

[F2] Polarization gives $\bar v\bar w+\bar w\bar v=2\eta(v,w)1$ ([[lem-clifford-polarized-relations]]).

[A1] The chosen representation maps the unit to $I_S$, and $\dim_{\mathbb R}S=32$.

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Choose an oriented $\eta$-orthonormal basis $(e_0,e_1,e_2)$ of $W$ with $\eta(e_0,e_0)=-1$ and $\eta(e_1,e_1)=\eta(e_2,e_2)=1$. By [F1] and [F2], the associated matrices satisfy $\gamma_0^2=-I$, $\gamma_1^2=\gamma_2^2=I$, and distinct matrices anticommute. The normalized oriented volume element is $\Gamma_W=\gamma_0\gamma_1\gamma_2$ in this basis. Reordering the six factors requires three transpositions and contributes $-1$, while the three squares multiply to $-I$; hence $\Gamma_W^2=I$. Moving any one tangent generator past the other two contributes $(-1)^2=+1$, so $\Gamma_W$ commutes with each $\gamma_i$. [given, F1, F2, algebra]

2.1 In the same orthonormal basis, $\sqrt{-\det g}=1$. For $i=0$, $\varepsilon^{0jk}\gamma_{jk}=2\gamma_1\gamma_2=2\gamma^0\Gamma_W$ because $\gamma^0=-\gamma_0$. For $i=1$, the left side is $-2\gamma_0\gamma_2$, and $\gamma^1\Gamma_W=\gamma_1\gamma_0\gamma_1\gamma_2=-\gamma_0\gamma_2$. For $i=2$, the left side is $2\gamma_0\gamma_1$, and $\gamma^2\Gamma_W=\gamma_2\gamma_0\gamma_1\gamma_2=\gamma_0\gamma_1$. Thus the displayed identity holds in this basis. Both sides transform as vector densities under oriented changes of worldvolume coordinates, so the identity holds for every oriented basis; its coordinate-independent content uses the induced volume density. [given, F1, F2, step 1.1, algebra]

2.2 The orthogonal complement $W^\perp$ is positive definite of dimension eight, so choose a unit vector $n\in W^\perp$. By [F1], $\rho(\bar n)^2=I$, hence $\rho(\bar n)^{-1}=\rho(\bar n)$. By [F2], $\rho(\bar n)$ anticommutes with each of the three tangent generators and therefore with their product $\Gamma_W$. Thus conjugation by $\rho(\bar n)$ sends $\Gamma_W$ to $-\Gamma_W$. Trace is invariant under conjugation, so $\operatorname{tr}\Gamma_W=-\operatorname{tr}\Gamma_W$, and the real trace is zero. [given, F1, F2, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 [step 1.1] gives $\Gamma_W^2=I$, so direct multiplication yields $P_\pm^2=P_\pm$, $P_+P_-=0$, and $P_++P_-=I$. The eigenspace decomposition of an idempotent gives $\operatorname{rank}P_\pm=\operatorname{tr}P_\pm=(32\pm0)/2=16$, using [A1] and step 2.2. Thus the projections are complementary and each has rank $16$. [given, A1, step 1.1, step 2.2, algebra] ∎
