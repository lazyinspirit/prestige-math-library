## Definition

Assume the Axiom of Countable Choice, the exact choice strength required by
[[def-tautological-one-form-on-a-cotangent-bundle]] to construct the smooth
cotangent manifold. Let $d,N\ge1$, let $Q$ be a smooth $d$-manifold, and
choose a countable smooth-chart subcover of its atlas. Such a subcover is
obtained from a countable basis of the second-countable topology by choosing,
for each basis element contained in a chart domain, one such chart domain; this
countable selection is covered by the stated Axiom of Countable Choice. Give
$Q_N:=Q^N$ the product topology and the product smooth structure generated by
the charts
$$\left(U_1\times\cdots\times U_N,\ (q_1,\ldots,q_N)\mapsto(\phi_1(q_1),\ldots,\phi_N(q_N))\right),$$
where each $(U_i,\phi_i)$ belongs to the chosen countable subcover of charts of
$Q$. These images are open in $\mathbb R^{dN}$, and their transition maps are
componentwise smooth, so they form a smooth atlas of dimension $n=dN$; the
finite product of the countable chart family is countable. Its generated
maximal atlas is the product smooth structure in the convention of
[[def-smooth-manifold]]. The induced cotangent charts are indexed by these
base charts and form a countable atlas of $\Gamma_N$. Its
**configuration space** is $Q_N$. Its **phase space** is the cotangent bundle
$$\Gamma_N:=T^*Q_N,$$
whose points are pairs $(q,p)$ with $p\in T_q^*Q_N$. Let
$\pi_N:T^*Q_N\to Q_N$ be the cotangent-bundle projection. The **tautological
one-form** and **canonical symplectic form** are
$$\lambda_{(q,p)}(\xi):=p(d\pi_N\xi),\qquad \omega_N:=-d\lambda.$$
Thus in an induced cotangent chart $(q^1,\ldots,q^n,p_1,\ldots,p_n)$,
$$\lambda=\sum_{j=1}^n p_j\,dq^j,\qquad \omega_N=\sum_{j=1}^n dq^j\wedge dp_j.$$
The cotangent supplier proves $\omega_N$ closed and nondegenerate under the
same Countable Choice premise. On an open phase chart this is exactly the
standard form of [[def-cm-phase-domain-and-standard-symplectic-two-form]];
[[lem-cm-standard-symplectic-form-is-closed-and-nondegenerate]] gives its
closedness, contraction and nondegeneracy coordinate calculations.

Let $\mathcal F_N:=\mathcal B(\Gamma_N)$ be the Borel sigma-algebra generated
by the topology of $\Gamma_N$ ([[def-borel-sigma-algebra]]). The **Liouville
density**, denoted
$$d\Lambda_N:=|\omega_N^{\wedge n}|/n!,$$
is the positive density whose coordinate density in every induced cotangent
chart is $dq^1\cdots dq^n\,dp_1\cdots dp_n$. Define its Borel measure
$\Lambda_N$ explicitly as follows. If $\Gamma_N=\varnothing$,
set $\Lambda_N(\varnothing)=0$. Otherwise choose an at-most-countable induced
cotangent atlas $(V_k,\psi_k)_{k\in J}$ indexed by $J\subseteq\mathbb N$, order
$J$ by the usual order, and set
$$E_k:=V_k\setminus\bigcup_{j\in J,\,j<k}V_j\quad(k\in J).$$
For $A\in\mathcal F_N$, put
$$\Lambda_N(A):=\sum_{k\in J}\lambda_{2n}\bigl(\psi_k(A\cap E_k)\bigr),$$
where $\lambda_{2n}$ is Lebesgue measure on the chart image. Each $E_k$ is
Borel, and each chart map is a homeomorphism, so the terms are defined on
Borel sets. The coordinate transitions preserve $\omega_N$ because
$\omega_N=-d\lambda$ is intrinsic; hence they preserve $\omega_N^n/n!$
and have absolute Jacobian one. For two atlases, the intersections of their
disjoint chart pieces form a countable common Borel refinement. On each
nonempty refinement piece the coordinate transition is a $C^1$ diffeomorphism
with absolute Jacobian one, so the change-of-variables theorem applied to its
indicator [[thm-c-one-change-of-variables-for-nonnegative-lebesgue-measurable-functions]]
gives the same measure from either atlas. Summing over the refinement proves
atlas independence. Countable additivity follows because the chart images of
disjoint Borel sets remain disjoint within each $E_k$ and Lebesgue measure is
countably additive. For the resulting nonnegative array $a_{k\ell}$, both
$\sum_k\sum_\ell a_{k\ell}$ and $\sum_\ell\sum_k a_{k\ell}$ are the
supremum of the same finite subsums of $\{a_{k\ell}\}$, using the
nonnegative extended-sum convention of [[def-measure]]. Hence the sums
reorder and $\Lambda_N$ is a measure with the displayed coordinate density.
Given any finite constant $c_N>0$, define the **labelled reference measure**
$$m_N:=c_N\Lambda_N$$
on $(\Gamma_N,\mathcal F_N)$. It is sigma-finite: $Q_N$ is
countable-atlased as just constructed, so it is covered by countably many
cotangent chart images. Each chart image is open in $\mathbb R^{2n}$ and is
covered by the countable family of bounded open boxes with rational endpoints
whose closures lie in that chart image: every point of an open set lies in one
such box. Each box has finite Euclidean Lebesgue measure, and its chart
preimage therefore has finite $m_N$-measure because the Liouville coordinate
density is the constant $c_N$. The countably many chart-box preimages cover
$\Gamma_N$ and establish sigma-finiteness directly, without a sigma-compactness
supplier.
The constant $c_N$ is mathematical normalization data; this definition assigns
no physical unit to it.

For a partition of the particle labels into species classes of sizes
$N_1,\ldots,N_s$, let $G:=S_{N_1}\times\cdots\times S_{N_s}$ act on
$Q_N=Q^N$ by permuting factors within each class and on $T^*Q_N$ by the
cotangent lift. In canonical coordinates each lift permutes corresponding
$q$ and $p$ coordinates, so it preserves $\omega_N$, $\Lambda_N$ and $m_N$.
Let $\Pi_N:\Gamma_N\to\Gamma_N/G$ be
the orbit map, equip the orbit set with the quotient sigma-algebra
$$\mathcal F_{\mathrm{quot}}:=\{B\subseteq\Gamma_N/G:\Pi_N^{-1}(B) \text{ is Borel in }\Gamma_N\},$$
and define the **quotient reference measure** by
$$\bar m_N(B):=\frac{1}{\prod_{a=1}^s N_a!}\,m_N(\Pi_N^{-1}(B)).$$
This is a measure because inverse images preserve complements and countable
disjoint unions and $m_N$ is countably additive. The stated invariance makes
it the normalized quotient pushforward. It is a measurable quotient;
no smooth quotient structure at configurations with stabilizers is asserted.
For one species the factor is $1/N!$.

The quotient factor here is mathematical counting data. A physical postulate
determines when labels are operationally indistinguishable and how the
reference scale is interpreted; neither follows from the cotangent geometry.
