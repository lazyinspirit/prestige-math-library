## Definition

Let $(C,m_0,m_1,\ldots)$ be a filtered curved $A_\infty$ algebra over a complete Novikov ring $\Lambda_0$, with positive filtration ideal $\Lambda_+=\{a:\nu(a)>0\}$, and let $e$ be a specified strict or canonical cohomological unit. The filtration and continuity hypotheses must make the following series converge. A **weak bounding cochain** is an element $b\in F^{>0}C[1]^0$ for which

$$m_0^b:=\sum_{k\geq 0}m_k(b,\ldots,b)=\lambda_b e$$

for some scalar $\lambda_b\in\Lambda_+$. Equivalently, the filtered $A_\infty$ Maurer–Cartan equation holds modulo the line generated by the unit. In the geometric convention of [[def-gts-r4-09b-multi-object-fukaya-category-of-branes-and-polygon-morphisms]], this is a brane together with a positive-valuation degree-one bounding datum; the source’s canonical model writes the unit as the Poincaré dual of the fundamental class.

A brane admitting such a $b$ is **weakly unobstructed**, and $\lambda_b$ is its potential. A **fixed-potential Fukaya category datum at $\lambda$** consists of chosen weakly unobstructed branes with $\lambda_b=\lambda$ together with specified deformed morphism modules and multi-object operations satisfying the $A_\infty$ identities. Equality of potentials is required for the source’s pairwise deformed Floer differential. The definition does not assert that bounding cochains exist, that all objects share a potential, or that coherent multi-object operations have been constructed. The batch-39 supplier remains escalated for that last construction.
