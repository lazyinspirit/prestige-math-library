## Statement

Let $V$ be a nonnegatively integer-graded conformal vertex algebra with finite-dimensional grades, generated by finite mode words of homogeneous quasi-primary states. Suppose it has a positive normalized Hermitian form with orthogonal grades and $L(j)^\dagger=L(-j)$ for $j=-1,0,1$. Suppose the generating states are Hermitian in energy modes: for $a$ of weight $d$, $a_n=a_{(n+d-1)}$ obeys $a_n^\dagger=a_{-n}$. Then $V$ has a well-defined antiunitary involutive vertex-algebra automorphism $\Theta$ fixing its conformal vector, with $\Theta a=(-1)^d a$ on these generators, and its given Hilbert form is invariant. Explicitly, $B(u,v)=\langle u,\Theta v\rangle$ is symmetric bilinear and $B(Y(a,z)b,c)=B(b,Y(e^{zL(1)}(-z^{-2})^{L(0)}a,z^{-1})c)$. For homogeneous $a\in V_d$, $(a_{(n)})^\dagger=(-1)^d\sum_{j\ge0}(L(1)^j\Theta a)_{(2d-j-n-2)}/j!$. All adjoints here are algebraic adjoints between finite-dimensional graded blocks; Hilbert closure of smearings is a separate analytic assertion. Here a conformal vertex algebra means a vertex algebra with a specified conformal vector whose modes satisfy the Virasoro relations, whose zero mode grades the space as stated, and whose $L(-1)$ is the translation operator; the extra CFT-type condition $V_0=\mathbb C\mathbf1$ is not imposed. The conclusion does not require that condition or simplicity.
