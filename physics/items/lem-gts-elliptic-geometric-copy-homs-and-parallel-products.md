## Statement

Assume full AC. Let $T=\mathbb C/\Lambda$ be a flat complex torus with its positive area form and complexified area parameter $\rho$ with $\operatorname{Im}\rho>0$. Its objects are graded primitive straight rational circles with the given coherent brane orientations and finite complex local systems whose monodromy eigenvalues have modulus one, including finite Jordan blocks. There is an actual unital geometric category obtained from distinct genuine Hamiltonian graph copies, their holomorphic-polygon operations and algebraic localization at the constructed continuation cocycles. The all-arity map from each ordered finite polygon chart to its dg envelope is a quasi-isomorphism on every strictly forward Hom complex. Its Homs and binary products therefore represent the actual copy-localized Floer cohomology, including repeated and parallel objects.

For the same physical circle with local-system fibres $V_i$ and monodromies $M_i$, its Hom complex is quasi-isomorphic to the two-term complex $\operatorname{Hom}(V_i,V_j)\xrightarrow{D_{ij}-1}\operatorname{Hom}(V_i,V_j)$ in degrees zero and one, where $D_{ij}(T)=M_jTM_i^{-1}$. For distinct parallel fluxes the Hom is acyclic. The equal-circle degree-zero products are composition of flat intertwiners; their actions on degree-one coinvariants are the induced left and right compositions, and two degree-one classes multiply to zero. The kernel/coinvariant trace pairing agrees with the geometric cyclic triangle pairing. Flat intertwiners act on transverse morphisms by coefficient composition; all remaining binary products with a parallel endpoint are determined by this pairing and cyclic rotation of the actual triangles. These identifications commute with finite direct sums, grading shifts and finite covers. All fixed-word polygon sums converge absolutely at the stated complex parameter. No coherent-sheaf or theta-comparison theorem is assumed.

8.2 **All degree-compatible continuation triangles.** Take three distinct parameter values $r<s<t$. Put $a=α_{rs}, b=α_{rt}, c=α_{st}, A=a+π, B=b+π, C=c+π$. Then $a<b<c<A<B<C<a+2π$.
