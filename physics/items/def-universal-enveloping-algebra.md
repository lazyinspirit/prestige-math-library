## Definition

Let $\mathfrak g$ be a Lie algebra over $k$. In $T(\mathfrak g)$, regard
$[x,y]\in\mathfrak g$ as an element of tensor degree one, and let $I$ be the
two-sided ideal generated by

$$x\otimes y-y\otimes x-[x,y]\qquad(x,y\in\mathfrak g).$$

The **universal enveloping algebra** is

$$U(\mathfrak g)=T(\mathfrak g)/I.$$

Write

$$\iota_{\mathfrak g}:\mathfrak g\longrightarrow U(\mathfrak g)$$

for the composite of the degree-one inclusion with the quotient map. This
definition makes $U(\mathfrak g)$ a unital associative algebra and $\iota_{\mathfrak g}$ a
linear map. It does not assert that $\iota_{\mathfrak g}$ is injective; that conclusion will
come from PBW.
