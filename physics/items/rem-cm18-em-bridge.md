## What the framework already supplies

The particle side of the framework is complete on its own terms: labels with
$C^{2}$ worldlines parametrized by frame time and fixed positive masses
([[post-cm01-worldlines]]); a force branch in which the force map is declared
model input with standing continuity and state-local-Lipschitz hypotheses
([[post-cm02-force-superposition]]); motions, unique maximal motions and the
continuation criterion ([[def-cm02-newtonian-flow]], [[pthm-cm02-ivp]],
[[pthm-cm02-continuation]]); the mechanical quantities and their balance
theorems, conditional on the declared interaction structure
([[def-cm03-centre-of-mass-and-momenta]], [[pthm-cm03-frame-transformations]],
[[rem-cm03-conservation-scope]]); and the operational observable model
through which predictions at observation times are read
([[def-cm18-observable-model]]). The framework contains no charge, no field
and no coupling law.

## What an electromagnetic coupling must add

A charged-particle coupling is a **new physical assumption**, not a
consequence of the framework, and it must supply at least:

1. **Charge attributes.** To each label an attribute with a declared
   dimension and unit. The dimension group of the framework is generated by
   length, duration and mass alone ([[def-cm01-units]] clause (a)); the
   dimension and unit of charge, and of the components of any field used,
   must therefore be declared by the coupling and cannot be inferred from
   this framework.
2. **Field maps.** Electric and magnetic fields (or the corresponding
   potentials) as maps with declared domains, regularity, sources and
   boundary or radiation conditions, supplied by an independently developed
   electromagnetic model; the particle framework constrains none of these.
3. **A coupling law.** An added force map $F^{\mathrm{EM}}$ per label, for
   instance a declared function of the fields, the charge and the label's
   velocity in the chosen frame, as in the Lorentz force of the cited
   electromagnetic treatment. The coupling law is adopted: the framework
   neither derives it nor tests it.
4. **Field-side bookkeeping.** If statements about the energy or momentum of
   the combined system are wanted, the field contributions and their balance
   with the particles must be declared as separate objects; this framework
   contains no field energy or field momentum.

## What the force branch yields conditionally

Once a coupling has been declared, the mechanical theorems apply to the
total force map $F+F^{\mathrm{EM}}$ provided it satisfies the standing
hypotheses of the force branch (continuity and state-local Lipschitz,
locally uniformly in time) on the declared domain: then
[[pthm-cm02-ivp]] and [[pthm-cm02-continuation]] give the local, unique and
maximal motions of the coupled model, and the balance laws of the
[[def-cm03-centre-of-mass-and-momenta|balance page]] apply with the coupling
force among the external contributions.

No field-momentum theorem is imported. With the electromagnetic force
treated as external to the declared particle labels, the particle mechanical
momentum alone need not be conserved; the balance laws record the exchange
through the external force, and the open-system boundary of
[[rem-cm02-open-system-warning]] applies. A conservation statement for
matter plus field requires the field contributions of item 4 above as
additional declared data, and is not a consequence of this framework.

## What is not claimed

No field equation, no value or dimension of any charge or coupling constant,
no derivation or validation of a coupling law, no prediction of any
electromagnetic phenomenon and no measured electromagnetic quantity is
asserted. The observable models of [[def-cm18-observable-model]] acquire an
electromagnetic readout only after a coupling has been declared; until then
this framework is silent about electric and magnetic effects.
