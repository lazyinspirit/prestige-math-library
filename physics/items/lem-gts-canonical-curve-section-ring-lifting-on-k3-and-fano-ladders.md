## Statement

For a proper complex variety Z and line bundle M, write $R(Z,M)=\oplus _{n\ge 0}H⁰(Z,Mⁿ)$, with multiplication induced by tensor product. Say M is normally generated when every multiplication SymⁿH⁰(Z,M)→H⁰(Z,Mⁿ) is onto. The conclusion below asserts normal generation in this precise sense.

(K3) Let S be a smooth integral projective complex surface with $K_{S}\cong O_{S}$ and $H¹(S,O_{S})=0$. Let L be an ample line bundle and C∈|L| a smooth integral curve. Assume the complete canonical morphism of C is a closed immersion, so the separate Max Noether construction supplies that $R(C,K_{C})$ is generated by degree one. Then $H¹(S,Lⁿ)=0$ for every $n\ge 0$, L is normally generated, and L is very ample. No very-ampleness of L is needed as a hypothesis for this implication.

(Ambient lift) Let Y be a smooth integral projective complex variety with $H¹(Y,O_{Y})=0$. Let A be ample, and suppose a smooth integral member S∈|A| has normally generated restriction $L=A|S$ and $H¹(S,Lⁿ)=0$ for all $n\ge 0$. Then $H¹(Y,Aⁿ)=0$ for all $n\ge 0$, A is normally generated, and A is very ample. In particular this applies to a threefold with smooth anticanonical K3 member and $A=-K_{Y}$, provided the stated $H¹(Y,O_{Y})=0$ is supplied, and can be repeated for a smooth index-two Fano fourfold with an actual smooth member Y∈|A|. The ambient H¹ hypothesis is explicit in this general algebraic interface. In a smooth ample ladder of dimensions at least three above the stated K3 it follows from the relative-handle and compact-Kähler Hodge suppliers, as proved below; the word Fano is not used as an implicit invocation of Kodaira vanishing.

## Facts & Assumptions

[A1] Assume the Axiom of Choice as the ambient convention of the named geometric and cohomological suppliers. No classification, Kodaira vanishing, or canonical-embedding criterion is inferred from terminology. In particular the general ambient $H¹(O)=0$ hypotheses remain explicit; the smooth ample ladder case is discharged by the named relative-handle and Hodge suppliers.

[F1] The exact suppliers are [[lem-gts-canonical-curve-section-ring-generation-by-inner-projection]], [[thm-serre-duality-smooth-projective-variety-locally-free-sheaves]], [[thm-serre-duality-curves-line-bundles]], [[lem-adjunction-formula-for-effective-divisors-on-smooth-surfaces]], [[thm-ample-powers-very-ample-proper-base]], [[lem-gts-ample-divisor-relative-handles-and-integral-restriction]], [[thm-compact-kahler-hodge-decomposition-via-elliptic-harmonic-forms]], [[def-axiom-of-choice]]. ## Proof

**Given:** Exactly the Statement objects, hypotheses and conventions.

1.1 Let Z be integral proper with $H⁰(O_{Z})=C$, let M be a line bundle, and let the nonzero section s∈H⁰(Z,M) have an effective Cartier zero divisor W with $H⁰(W,O_{W})=C$. Suppose $H¹(Z,M^{n-1})=0$ for every $n\ge 1$. Multiplication by s and restriction give exact sequences $0\to H⁰(Z,M^{n-1})$ --s→ H⁰(Z,Mⁿ) → H⁰(W,(M|W)ⁿ)→0. [given, A1, F1, algebra]

2.1 The left injection follows because s is a nonzerodivisor. In degree zero restriction is the isomorphism C→C by the additional $H⁰(W,O_{W})=C$ hypothesis; in every positive degree it is onto by step 1.1. Thus the restriction of graded rings is onto, its kernel is the ideal generated by the single degree-one element s, and R(Z,M)/(s)≅R(W,M|W). [given, A1, F1, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 If the latter ring is generated in degree one, lift its degree-one generators to H⁰(Z,M). For a homogeneous u of degree n, its restriction is a linear combination of products of n degree-one restricted sections. Subtract the same linear combination of products of their chosen lifts. The remainder is s v with v of degree n−1. Starting with $n=0$, induction writes v, and hence u, as a polynomial in degree-one sections. Therefore M is normally generated. This argument proves the actual symmetric-power multiplication assertion, not merely finite generation in unspecified degrees. [given, A1, F1, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 Put $g=g(C)$. For the actual smooth Cartier divisor C in S, a local defining equation f gives the conormal sequence $0\to L⁻¹|C\to Ω¹_{S}|C\to Ω¹_{C}\to 0$, with first map f mod f²↦df. Smoothness of both C and S makes df nonzero in the normal direction, so this sequence is locally an injection of a rank-one summand with locally free quotient. If $f_{i}=u_{ij}$ $f_{j}$, then $df_{i}=u_{ij}$ $df_{j}$ on C; these are precisely the transitions of L⁻¹|C. Taking determinants therefore gives $K_{S}|C=(L⁻¹|C)\otimes K_{C}$, hence $K_{C}=(K_{S}\otimes L)|C=L|C$. The named numerical adjunction supplier gives $2g-2=L²>0$, so $g\ge 2$. On S, Serre duality gives $H²(S,L)=H⁰(S,L⁻¹)*=0$: a nonzero section of the inverse ample line bundle would have an effective zero divisor of negative L degree, impossible; a nowhere-zero section would trivialize L, also impossible since $L²>0$. Also $H²(S,O_{S})=C$ because $K_{S}=O_{S}$ and S is integral proper. [given, A1, F1, step 3.1, algebra]

5.1 For $0\to O_{S}\to L\to K_{C}\to 0$, the cohomology sequence includes $0=H¹(O_{S})\to H¹(L)\to H¹(K_{C})\to H²(O_{S})\to H²(L)=0$. [given, A1, F1, step 4.1, algebra]

6.1 Curve Serre duality gives $H¹(K_{C})=H⁰(O_{C})*=C$. The displayed map between these one-dimensional spaces is onto, hence an isomorphism. Consequently $H¹(L)=0$. This is the $n=1$ step that must not be omitted from an inductive vanishing argument. [given, A1, F1, step 5.1, algebra]

7.1 For $n\ge 2$, the exact sequence $0\to L^{n-1}\to Lⁿ\to K_{C}ⁿ\to 0$ and curve Serre duality give $H¹(C,K_{C}ⁿ)=H⁰(C,K_{C}^{1-n})*=0$, since that line bundle has negative degree. Inductively $H¹(S,L^{n-1})=0$, and the long exact sequence then forces $H¹(S,Lⁿ)=0$. The case $n=0$ is the K3 hypothesis. Thus every restriction in Section 1 is onto, including $n=1$. [given, A1, F1, step 6.1, algebra]

8.1 The separately proved Max Noether output says $R(C,K_{C})$ is generated by $H⁰(K_{C})$. Since C is smooth integral proper over C, $H⁰(C,O_{C})=C$. Apply Section 1 to S,L,C: the full K3 section ring is generated by H⁰(S,L). This establishes all multiplication maps in every degree, not just the quadratic multiplication map. [given, A1, F1, step 7.1, algebra]

9.1 For the ambient data, tensor the divisor sequence by Aⁿ to obtain $0\to A^{n-1}\to Aⁿ\to Lⁿ\to 0$. [given, A1, F1, step 8.1, algebra]

10.1 The base $H¹(Y,O_{Y})=0$ and $H¹(S,Lⁿ)=0$ imply inductively $H¹(Y,Aⁿ)=0$: the relevant segment is $H¹(Y,A^{n-1})\to H¹(Y,Aⁿ)\to H¹(S,Lⁿ)$. Both outside groups vanish. Since S is smooth integral projective over C, $H⁰(S,O_{S})=C$. Section 1 then applies at every degree and lifts normal generation from L to A. [given, A1, F1, step 9.1, algebra]

11.1 The same local equation and conormal determinant argument in any smooth ambient Z gives $K_{D}=(K_{Z}\otimes O_{Z}(D))|D$ for each actual smooth Cartier divisor D. Thus for the smooth threefold with $S\\in  |-K_{Y}|$ one obtains $K_{S}=O_{S}$, but $H¹(S,O_{S})=0$ is a further K3 input, not a consequence of this line-bundle identity alone. For the smooth fourfold V with $-K_{V}=2A$ and actual smooth Y∈|A| it gives $K_{Y}=(K_{V}\otimes A)|Y=-A|Y$, equivalently $-K_{Y}=A|Y$. If the explicit H¹(O) hypotheses hold at each level, the preceding algebra applies twice. No new cohomological vanishing theorem enters at the second lift. [given, A1, F1, step 10.1, algebra]

12.1 For an actual smooth ample divisor S in Y with dim $Y\ge 3$, [[lem-gts-ample-divisor-relative-handles-and-integral-restriction]] gives H₁(S,Z)≅H₁(Y,Z). By [[thm-compact-kahler-hodge-decomposition-via-elliptic-harmonic-forms]], $H¹(S,O_{S})=0$ implies $H¹(S,C)=0$, because H¹ splits into the conjugate (1,0) and (0,1) parts. The homology isomorphism gives $H¹(Y,C)=0$ and hence $H¹(Y,O_{Y})=0$. Repeat the same argument for any next smooth ample ambient inclusion of dimension at least three. Thus the needed ambient vanishing is actually supplied throughout the smooth K3/Fano ladder, without Kodaira vanishing or simple-connectivity assumptions. [given, A1, F1, step 11.1, algebra]

13.1 Let M be any ample line bundle on a projective variety and suppose all its section-ring multiplications are onto. Choose $N>0$ with Mᴺ very ample. At a point where every degree-one section vanished, every product of N such sections would vanish; surjectivity would make all sections of Mᴺ vanish, contradicting its global generation. Therefore M is globally generated and defines a morphism $φ_{M}$. [given, A1, F1, step 12.1, algebra]

14.1 The degree-N multiplication surjection identifies the complete Mᴺ linear system with the restrictions to $φ_{M}(Z)$ of all degree-N homogeneous polynomials in its degree-one coordinates, modulo those vanishing identically. Consequently the morphism given by Mᴺ is the restriction of the Nth Veronese of $φ_{M}$ to its linear span. Since Mᴺ gives a closed immersion and the Veronese is a closed immersion, $φ_{M}$ is itself a closed immersion: identifying the Veronese image with its source identifies $φ_{M}(Z)$, with its closed subscheme structure, with the Mᴺ image. Thus M is very ample. [given, A1, F1, step 13.1, algebra] ∎
