## Statement

Adopt the state and composition postulate [[post-nrqm-framework-state-and-composition]] (P1), the observable and probability postulate in the reading of [[def-nrqm-observables-framework-observable-and-outcome-law]], and the autonomous-dynamics postulate [[post-nrqm-framework-autonomous-dynamics]] (P4), and assume the Axiom of Countable Choice and the Axiom of Choice ([[def-countable-choice]], [[def-axiom-of-choice]]), the choice hypotheses of the cited spectral suppliers. Let $H$ be the state space assigned to a declared isolated system of the massive-matter sector ([[def-nrqm-state-geometry-framework-state-space]]), let $(H_{\mathrm{op}},u_H)$ be its assigned energy observable with spectral projection-valued measure $E_H$ ([[def-nrqm-autonomous-dynamics-framework-evolution]] clause (a)), and let
$$U(t)=\exp\!\Bigl(-\frac{i}{\hbar}tH_{\mathrm{op}}\Bigr)=\int_{\mathbb R}e^{-it\lambda/\hbar}\,dE_H(\lambda),\qquad \psi(t):=U(t)\psi(0),\qquad \rho(t):=U(t)\rho(0)U(t)^{*}$$
be the flow and the state evolutions of clauses (b) and (c) of [[def-nrqm-autonomous-dynamics-framework-evolution]], with $\hbar>0$ and the energy unit in the sense of [[def-nrqm-framework-quantities-and-units]]. Then:

(a) **Unitary flow.** $U$ is a strongly continuous one-parameter unitary group ([[def-strongly-continuous-one-parameter-unitary-group]]) with $U(0)=I$, $U(t+s)=U(t)U(s)$ and $U(t)^{*}=U(t)^{-1}=U(-t)$; every orbit has constant norm, $\|\psi(t)\|=\|\psi(0)\|$, so for $\psi(0)\ne0$ the ray $[\psi(t)]$ is a pure preparation of the model for every $t$ ([[def-nrqm-state-geometry-pure-mixed-states-and-purity]]).

(b) **Domain invariance.** $U(t)D(H_{\mathrm{op}})=D(H_{\mathrm{op}})$ for every $t$ and $H_{\mathrm{op}}U(t)=U(t)H_{\mathrm{op}}$ on $D(H_{\mathrm{op}})$: the flow preserves the energy domain and commutes with the energy there.

(c) **Schroedinger equation on the domain.** For $\psi(0)\in D(H_{\mathrm{op}})$ the curve $t\mapsto\psi(t)$ is norm-differentiable at every $t\in\mathbb R$, it stays in $D(H_{\mathrm{op}})$, and
$$i\hbar\,\frac{d}{dt}\psi(t)=H_{\mathrm{op}}\psi(t)=H_{\mathrm{op}}U(t)\psi(0);$$
for $\psi(0)\in H\setminus D(H_{\mathrm{op}})$ the curve is norm-differentiable at no $t\in\mathbb R$, so the equation is not asserted there, although the evolution $t\mapsto\psi(t)$ remains continuous.

(d) **Density-operator evolution.** For every preparation $\rho$ ([[def-nrqm-state-geometry-framework-state-space]]) and every $t$ the operator $\rho(t)=U(t)\rho(0)U(t)^{*}$ is again a preparation: self-adjoint, positive, trace class with $\operatorname{tr}\rho(t)=1$ ([[def-trace-class-operator]], [[def-self-adjoint-positive-unitary-and-normal-operator]]). Moreover for every bounded operator $B$ the identity $\operatorname{tr}\bigl(\rho(t)B\bigr)=\operatorname{tr}\bigl(\rho(0)U(t)^{*}BU(t)\bigr)$ holds.

(e) **Status.** Clauses (a)-(d) are conditional consequences of P1 and P4 and the cited mathematics. They assert no evolution for a prescribed time-dependent Hamiltonian, no non-unitary reduced dynamics, no measurement, apparatus or detector model, no claim that any laboratory system is isolated, and no empirical result.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The adopted postulates P1 and P4 ([[post-nrqm-framework-state-and-composition]], [[post-nrqm-framework-autonomous-dynamics]]); the Axiom of Countable Choice and the Axiom of Choice; the assigned energy observable $(H_{\mathrm{op}},u_H)$ with PVM $E_H$ and flow $U(t)$ of [[def-nrqm-autonomous-dynamics-framework-evolution]]; the state space $D(H)$ of [[def-nrqm-state-geometry-framework-state-space]]; a vector $\psi(0)\in H$; a preparation $\rho$; a bounded operator $B$.

[F1] **P4, read through the definition.** Clause (i) of P4 assigns the energy operator: $H_{\mathrm{op}}$ is self-adjoint with a declared energy unit; clause (ii) fixes $U(t)=\exp(-itH_{\mathrm{op}}/\hbar)$ as the strongly continuous unitary group generated by it, with $D(T)=D(H_{\mathrm{op}})$ for $T=-H_{\mathrm{op}}/\hbar$; clause (iii) fixes $\psi(t)=U(t)\psi(0)$ and $\rho(t)=U(t)\rho(0)U(t)^{*}$ and states the Schroedinger equation as holding exactly for initial vectors in $D(H_{\mathrm{op}})$ ([[post-nrqm-framework-autonomous-dynamics]] clauses (i)-(iii), [[def-nrqm-autonomous-dynamics-framework-evolution]] clauses (a)-(c)).

[F2] **Flow calculus.** For the self-adjoint $T=-H_{\mathrm{op}}/\hbar$ with $U(t)=e^{itT}$: $U$ is a strongly continuous one-parameter unitary group with $U(t)D(T)=D(T)$ and $TU(t)=U(t)T$ on $D(T)$; for $u\in D(T)$ the curve $t\mapsto U(t)u$ is differentiable at every $t$ with derivative $iU(t)Tu=iTU(t)u$; for $u\notin D(T)$ it is differentiable at no point ([[lem-nrqm-autonomous-dynamics-flow-calculus]] clauses (a) and (c), [[lem-self-adjoint-operator-generates-a-strongly-continuous-unitary-group]]).

[F3] **P1, read through the state-space vocabulary.** Pure preparations are the unit rays of the assigned Hilbert space and general preparations are exactly the states $\rho\in D(H)$: self-adjoint positive trace-class operators with trace one ([[post-nrqm-framework-state-and-composition]] clauses (ii)-(iii), [[def-nrqm-state-geometry-framework-state-space]], [[def-nrqm-state-geometry-pure-mixed-states-and-purity]], [[lem-nrqm-composites-density-and-scalar-spectral-probability]] clause (a)).

[F4] **Trace-class calculus.** The trace-class operators form a two-sided operator ideal, so $U\rho U^{*}$ is trace class for a preparation $\rho$ and a unitary $U$; the trace is linear and cyclic, $\operatorname{tr}(SA)=\operatorname{tr}(AS)$ for trace-class $S$ and bounded $A$ ([[thm-trace-class-is-a-two-sided-banach-operator-ideal]], [[lem-nrqm-composites-nuclear-bounded-cyclicity]] clause (b)); a self-adjoint operator $B$ is positive exactly when $\langle Bx,x\rangle\ge0$ for all $x$ ([[def-self-adjoint-positive-unitary-and-normal-operator]]).



## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct; translate P4 into the mathematical generator $T=-H_{\mathrm{op}}/\hbar$, apply the flow calculus of [[lem-nrqm-autonomous-dynamics-flow-calculus]], and verify the density-operator claims with the trace-class calculus.

**Given:** P1, P4, the assigned energy observable $(H_{\mathrm{op}},u_H)$, its flow $U(t)$, a vector $\psi(0)$, a preparation $\rho$, a bounded operator $B$, and [F1]-[F4].

1.1 [The flow and the orbits] Put $T:=-H_{\mathrm{op}}/\hbar$, a self-adjoint operator with $D(T)=D(H_{\mathrm{op}})$; by clause (b) of [[def-nrqm-autonomous-dynamics-framework-evolution]] the flow is $U(t)=e^{itT}$. By [F2], $U$ is a strongly continuous one-parameter unitary group with the displayed inverse and group laws, and $\|\psi(t)\|=\|U(t)\psi(0)\|=\|\psi(0)\|$ because $U(t)$ is unitary. If $\psi(0)\ne0$ then $\psi(t)\ne0$, and by the normalization clause (ii) of P1 ([[post-nrqm-framework-state-and-composition]]) the unit ray $[\psi(t)]$ is a pure preparation; this is (a). [given, F1, F2, F3, algebra]

1.2 [Domain invariance] $D(H_{\mathrm{op}})=D(T)$ and $H_{\mathrm{op}}=-\hbar T$, so the invariance $U(t)D(T)=D(T)$ and the commutation $TU(t)=U(t)T$ on $D(T)$ of [F2] read as $U(t)D(H_{\mathrm{op}})=D(H_{\mathrm{op}})$ and $H_{\mathrm{op}}U(t)=U(t)H_{\mathrm{op}}$ on $D(H_{\mathrm{op}})$; this is (b). [given, F1, F2, algebra]

1.3 [Preparations evolve to preparations] Let $\rho$ be a preparation, so $\rho$ is self-adjoint, positive and trace class with $\operatorname{tr}\rho=1$ by [F3]. Then $\rho(t)=U(t)\rho U(t)^{*}$ is trace class by the ideal property of [F4]. It is self-adjoint because $\rho(t)^{*}=U(t)\rho^{*}U(t)^{*}=\rho(t)$; it is positive because $\langle\rho(t)x,x\rangle=\langle\rho\,U(t)^{*}x,U(t)^{*}x\rangle\ge0$ for every $x$, using positivity of $\rho$ and unitarity of $U(t)$; and its trace is $\operatorname{tr}\rho(t)=\operatorname{tr}(U(t)\rho U(t)^{*})=\operatorname{tr}(\rho U(t)^{*}U(t))=\operatorname{tr}\rho=1$, where the second equality is trace cyclicity from [F4] applied to the trace-class operator $\rho U(t)^{*}$ and the bounded operator $U(t)$. Hence $\rho(t)$ is again a preparation. [given, F3, F4, algebra]

2.1 [Schroedinger equation] Let $\psi(0)\in D(H_{\mathrm{op}})=D(T)$. By [F2] the curve is differentiable at every $t$ with $\frac{d}{dt}\psi(t)=iU(t)T\psi(0)$, and $\psi(t)=U(t)\psi(0)\in D(T)=D(H_{\mathrm{op}})$ by step 1.2. Substituting $T=-H_{\mathrm{op}}/\hbar$ and using the commutation of step 1.2 gives $i\hbar\frac{d}{dt}\psi(t)=i\hbar\cdot iU(t)T\psi(0)=-\hbar U(t)(-\frac1\hbar H_{\mathrm{op}})\psi(0)=U(t)H_{\mathrm{op}}\psi(0)=H_{\mathrm{op}}U(t)\psi(0)=H_{\mathrm{op}}\psi(t)$. [given, step 1.2, F2, algebra]

2.2 [Two-picture statistics identity] For bounded $B$, cyclicity from [F4] applied to the trace-class operator $\rho U(t)^{*}B$ and the bounded operator $U(t)$ gives $\operatorname{tr}\bigl(\rho(t)B\bigr)=\operatorname{tr}\bigl(U(t)\rho U(t)^{*}B\bigr)=\operatorname{tr}\bigl(\rho U(t)^{*}BU(t)\bigr)$, which is the displayed identity of (d). [given, step 1.3, F4, algebra]

3.1 [Outside the domain] If $\psi(0)\in H\setminus D(H_{\mathrm{op}})=H\setminus D(T)$, then by [F2] the curve $t\mapsto U(t)\psi(0)$ is differentiable at no point of $\mathbb R$; this completes (c). [given, step 2.1, F2]

4.1 Steps 1.1, 1.2, 2.1, 3.1, 1.3 and 2.2 establish (a)-(d); clause (e) records what the statement does not assert, in agreement with clauses (iv) and (v) of P4. [step 1.1, step 1.2, step 2.1, step 3.1, step 1.3, step 2.2] ∎

## Remarks

- **Isolated idealization.** P4 is adopted only for an isolated autonomous system; the model equation (c) is a statement about the model's evolution operator, not evidence that a given laboratory system is isolated.
- **No state reduction.** Nothing in this item is a measurement postulate: the evolution $\rho\mapsto U\rho U^{*}$ is applied to a preparation, and no outcome, apparatus or conditioning is introduced.
