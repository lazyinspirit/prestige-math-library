## Statement

Let $\mathfrak H=\{\tau\in\mathbb C:\operatorname{Im}\tau>0\}$ and, for real $\alpha$, use $q^\alpha:=e^{2\pi i\tau\alpha}$ with $q=e^{2\pi i\tau}$. Set $\eta(\tau)=q^{1/24}\prod_{n\ge1}(1-q^n)$. A **formal $c=1$ modular-bootstrap datum** is an integer-valued function $d:\mathbb R_{\ge0}^2\to\mathbb Z$ together with its null-subtracted multiplicity function defined as follows. Put
$$C(P)=\begin{cases}\{P-j:j=0,1,\ldots,\lfloor P\rfloor\},&P\in\tfrac12\mathbb Z_{\ge0},\\\{P\},&P\notin\tfrac12\mathbb Z_{\ge0},\end{cases}\qquad D_{P,\bar P}:=\sum_{P'\in C(P),\,\bar P'\in C(\bar P)}d_{P',\bar P'}.$$ 
Call $D$ the formal primary-multiplicity array; this name does not posit a Hilbert-space realization.
Define $\operatorname{supp}d:=\{(P,\bar P):d_{P,\bar P}\ne0\}$ and $\operatorname{supp}D:=\{(P,\bar P):D_{P,\bar P}\ne0\}$. Require both supports to be locally finite in $\mathbb R_{\ge0}^2$, meaning every compact subset meets each support in finitely many points. For $\chi_P(\tau):=q^{P^2}/\eta(\tau)$ define
$$Z(\tau,\bar\tau):=\sum_{P,\bar P\ge0}d_{P,\bar P}\,\chi_P(\tau)\chi_{\bar P}(-\bar\tau),$$
and require this series to converge absolutely for every $\tau\in\mathfrak H$. In this item, **unitary** means exactly the nonnegative-primary-multiplicity axiom $D_{P,\bar P}\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}$; it does not assert a Hilbert space, operator products, or existence of a full local CFT. Assume the modular-bootstrap axioms [A1]–[A6] in **Facts & Assumptions** below, including the Axiom of Choice in [A6].

For $R>0$ define the Narain lattice and its atomic measure by
$$\Lambda_R=\left\{\left(\frac{mR+n/R}{2},\frac{mR-n/R}{2}\right):m,n\in\mathbb Z\right\},\qquad \rho_R=\sum_{x\in\Lambda_R}\delta_x.$$
Choose the source's asymmetric density representatives with $R\ge1$; the characters are even in each momentum, so densities related by sign symmetrization give the same partition function. Then the A1–A5 partition-function densities are exactly:

1. $\rho_R$ for $R\ge1$;
2. $\rho_R^{\rm orb}=\tfrac12(-\rho_1+2\rho_2+\rho_R)$ for $R>1$, with $\rho_1^{\rm orb}=\rho_2$;
3. $\rho_{\rm tet}=\tfrac12(-\rho_1+\rho_2+2\rho_3)$;
4. $\rho_{\rm oct}=\tfrac12(-\rho_1+\rho_2+\rho_3+\rho_4)$;
5. $\rho_{\rm ico}=\tfrac12(-\rho_1+\rho_2+\rho_3+\rho_5)$;
6. $\tfrac12(\rho_R+\rho_{R'})$ for distinct $R,R'\ge1$.

This classifies only the formal partition-function data just defined. In particular, A1–A5 admit the spurious average in item 6. The theorem does not construct a full local CFT for each listed density and does not prove full-CFT uniqueness or physical realizability.
