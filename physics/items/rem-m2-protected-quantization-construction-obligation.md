## Statement

**HELD OBLIGATION, not an existence theorem, physical postulate, or
open-problem classification:** Construct the regulated physical
protected sector and curve comparison for the original $F_4$
background/charge scope. Prove the actual vacuum/twist index class and
its relation to four-dimensional chirality, and control regulator
removal and additional light constituents. A net-index objective
requires all local net contributions but does not require full-state
equivalence or a calculation of every vectorlike multiplicity.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The exact input contracts in
[[def-m2-regulated-protected-sector-data]] and
[[def-m2-curve-comparison-realization-data]]. The physical realization
remains a separately held authoring obligation.

[F1] [[def-m2-regulated-protected-sector-data]]: the required regulated
physical Hilbert quotient, preserved charge, domains, and regulator
removal are named as data inputs, with no physical existence assertion.

[F2] [[def-m2-curve-comparison-realization-data]]: the actual curve
comparison, physical bundle/twist, comparison domains, and relative
zero-mode contributions are required data; no index equality or
rank-one assumption is asserted.

## Proof

1.1 By [F1], a physical protected-sector realization would require the
actual Hilbert quotient, preserved charge, and regulator-removal data.
By [F2], a curve reduction would additionally require its actual
physical comparison, bundle/twist, and relative sector. These are
distinct input obligations, not consequences of the mathematical
definition of a data package. [F1, F2, given]

2.1 The source item [F1] defines only the required data package and
explicitly asserts no physical existence. Therefore the obligation in
the statement records work needed to establish the physical realization
and index relation; it asserts neither their existence nor their
nonexistence. It is not a logical supplier for an index theorem.
[F1, F2, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
