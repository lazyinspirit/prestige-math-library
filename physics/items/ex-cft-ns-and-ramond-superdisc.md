## Example

Let $U=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}$ and give $U$ the complex superdomain sheaf $\mathcal O_U\otimes\Lambda(\theta)$ with one odd coordinate $\theta$ and left odd derivatives. The two local generators $Q_{NS}=\partial_\theta+\theta\partial_z$ and $Q_R=\partial_\theta+z\theta\partial_z$ realize the NS and Ramond puncture models, respectively.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The disk $U$, its structure sheaf $\mathcal O_U\otimes\Lambda(\theta)$, the local left-derivation convention, and the two odd vector fields specified in the Example.

[F1] [[def-cft-complex-supermanifold]]: the odd generator obeys $\theta^2=0$, and tangent sections act by left graded derivations.

[F2] [[def-cft-super-riemann-surface]]: the non-Ramond bracket map must be an isomorphism; at a Ramond divisor it may have a simple zero, with $\mathcal D^{\otimes2}\cong(T_S/\mathcal D)\otimes\mathcal O(-F)$.

## Verification

1.1 For a local superfunction $f(z,\theta)=f_0(z)+\theta f_1(z)$, direct application gives $Q_{NS}f=f_1+\theta f_0'$ and $Q_{NS}^2f=f_0'+\theta f_1'=\partial_zf$. Thus $[Q_{NS},Q_{NS}]=2\partial_z$ and the rank-$0|1$ distribution is locally free with quotient tangent line generated by $\partial_z\bmod Q_{NS}$. [given, F1, algebra]

1.2 The same calculation for $Q_R$ gives $Q_R^2f=z\partial_zf$ and $[Q_R,Q_R]=2z\partial_z$. Its bracket map is an isomorphism on $U\setminus\{0\}$ and has a simple zero along $F=\{0\}$. Since $z\partial_z$ generates the ideal line $T_S/\mathcal D\otimes\mathcal O(-F)$, the Ramond divisor relation in [F2] holds locally. [given, F1, F2, algebra]

2.1 The induced map $\mathcal D^{\otimes2}\to T_S/\mathcal D$ sends $Q_{NS}\otimes Q_{NS}$ to $\partial_z\bmod Q_{NS}$, a nowhere-zero frame, so it is an isomorphism on $U$. Marking $(z,\theta)=(0,0)$ therefore gives an NS puncture in the stated local model. [given, F2, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 On any simply connected sector of $U\setminus\{0\}$ choose a branch $\sqrt z$ and set $\psi=\sqrt z\,\theta$. Holding $\theta$ fixed, $\partial_z=\partial_z|_\psi+\frac{\psi}{2z}\partial_\psi$, while $\partial_\theta=\sqrt z\,\partial_\psi$. Hence $z\theta\partial_z=\sqrt z\,\psi\partial_z+\frac{\psi^2}{2\sqrt z}\partial_\psi=\sqrt z\,\psi\partial_z$, since $\psi^2=0$, and therefore $Q_R=\sqrt z(\partial_\psi+\psi\partial_z)$. After one circuit around $0$, the branch changes sign and so does $\psi$; this is the Ramond monodromy. [given, F1, step 1.2, algebra] ∎

## Remarks

The two local models share the same reduced disk. The diagram records only the branch continuation in step 3.1; the verification above establishes the sign directly.

```tikzcd
(z|\theta) \arrow[r,"\psi=\sqrt z\,\theta"] \arrow[d,"one\ circuit"'] & (z|\psi) \arrow[d,"\psi\mapsto-\psi"] \\
(z|\theta) \arrow[r,"\psi'=-\psi"'] & (z|-\psi)
```

This is a local mathematical model only. It asserts no compact global SRS, worldsheet SCFT, superstring measure, or amplitude.
