## Statement

Assume full AC and work over $\mathbb C$. For every finite ordered product $X=E_1\times\cdots\times E_n$ of smooth projective elliptic curves, choose degree-one lines $L_i$ and put $H=\sum_i\operatorname{pr}_i^*c_1(L_i)$. There exists a rational, locally finite Bridgeland stability condition on $D^b\operatorname{Coh}(X)$ with support property on an explicitly constructed finite-rank numerical quotient lattice and central charge
$$Z_X(F)=-\int_X e^{-iH}\operatorname{ch}(F).$$
Its heart is constructed by the ordered AP constant-heart and rational Liu-tilt induction with $s=t=1$; its skyscraper sheaves are stable of phase $1$.

The condition is invariant as a slicing and charge under every factorwise product of elliptic-curve automorphisms and under tensoring by every line in $\operatorname{Pic}^0(X)$. For every finite subgroup of those symmetries it admits an explicitly equivariant numerical support lattice. In particular it supplies the invariant product stability required for the diagonal order-two and order-three Cynk–Hulek groups. This statement does not infer block-permutation invariance or equality of differently ordered tilted hearts merely from their symmetric central charge.

1.1 **Categorical Hilbert basis calculation.** For a noetherian C-linear abelian category A, define graded $S=Sym(V)$ objects by components $M_{n}$ in A with associative S action and finite-type objects as quotients of finite sums $S \otimes A_{i}$ shifted in degree. Every subobject of S tensor A is finite type. Here is the actual induction: write $S=B[x]$, filter a submodule by x-degree, and project its ith associated piece to B tensor A to obtain leading-coefficient modules $L_{i}$. Multiplication by x gives $L_{i}$ subset $L_{i+1}$. By induction in dim V, B tensor A is noetherian, so this chain stabilizes at $i=N$. Each $L_{i}$ for $i\le N$ is finitely generated over B, in degrees bounded by M. The multiplication $S_{1} \otimes M_{n}\to M_{n+1}$ is onto for $n\ge N+M$: on x-degree $i\ge N$ use $L_{i}=L_{i+1}$; for $i<N$ use generation of $L_{i+1}$ by $B_{1}$. Lifting through the finite x-degree filtration proves surjectivity. Thus the submodule is generated in finitely many degrees. For an increasing chain $M_{i}$ in S tensor A, take the degreewise union; it exists because each finite-degree component is noetherian. The union is finite type by the previous paragraph, so its finitely many generating components already lie in one $M_{i}$. The chain stabilizes. Every finite-type object is a quotient of a finite-type free object, hence noetherian; kernels and images are finite type by the submodule result. This proves an abelian noetherian category of finite-type graded A-valued S modules. No projective generator of A is required. [F1, F2, F3, F4, F5, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 **The projective moving windows.** On $P^{r}$ the diagonal is the zero locus of the universal tautological map $O(−1)\to Q$; it is a regular rank-r zero locus. Cech cohomology of O(m) (group monomials by their negative exponents) gives $H^{>0}(O(m))=0$ for $m\ge 0$ and all cohomology zero for $−r\le m<0$. Tensoring/pushing the diagonal complex therefore gives the relative decomposition into p*DbCoh(X)(−r),...,p*DbCoh(X), including its projection triangles. For each integer n glue the base t-structures using those finite projection triangles. Its window is exactly `E in D_n^[a,b] iff Rp_*E(n),...,Rp_*E(n+r) in D_X^[a,b].` Indeed apply Rp_*(−(j)) to a projection triangle: earlier summands contribute only H0(O(j−i)) tensor the corresponding base object. Induction through the r+1 triangles gives both orthogonality and truncations. Boundedness is finite gluing of bounded t-structures. The required resolution has variable multiplicities at EVERY term, including the first: `0->V0 tensor O(m)->V1 tensor O(m+1)->...->Vr tensor O(m+r)->O(n+r)->0` for $n\ge m$. Put $k=n−m+r$. The dual form of the diagonal Koszul resolution above, applied to O(k), gives these terms, with $dim(V_{j})=binom(k,j) binom(k−j−1,r−j)$. To compute them, resolve each exterior power of the tautological quotient by its exterior Euler sequence and group the resulting homogeneous monomials by degree; the Koszul complex of the coordinate variables is exact in every positive total degree. This also shows that no positive cohomology remains in those coefficient complexes for $k\ge r$, so the finite diagonal convolution is the displayed exact vector-bundle resolution, rather than a complex with omitted higher terms. Here binom(a,b)=0 for $0\le a<b$ and binom(a,0)=1. In particular $k=r$ gives $V_{r}=C$ and $V_{j}=0$ for $j<r$, so for $n=m$ the augmentation is the identity.
