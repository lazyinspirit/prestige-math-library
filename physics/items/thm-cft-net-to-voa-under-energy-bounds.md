## Statement

For an irreducible conformal net generated by a family $F$ of quasi-primary vectors invariant under the stated anti-linear involution, assume the Fredenhagen–Jörß fields for $F$ satisfy energy bounds and every $L_0$ eigenspace is finite-dimensional. Then $H_{\mathrm{fin}}$ carries a simple unitary strongly local VOA structure whose associated net is the original net.

5.1 For two generating fields, their polynomial mode energy estimates bound every commutator coefficient on $H^\infty$ by a fixed polynomial in the two mode numbers times one Sobolev norm of the input. 7.1 The creative translation-covariant local generating fields satisfy every hypothesis of F4, so they reconstruct the unique graded vertex algebra on $D$ and its full Jacobi identity. 8.1 On an input $v\in H_h$, the first product vanishes when its rightmost mode index $k+j+d_A-n-1$ exceeds $h$, and the second vanishes when its rightmost index $j-d_A+1$ exceeds $h$, because there are no negative-energy output spaces. For fixed integer $n$, $|\binom nj|$ grows at most polynomially in $j$: for $n\ge0$ it is zero for $j>n$, and for $n<0$ its absolute value is $\binom{j-n-1}{-n-1}$. Suppose the fields of $A$ and $B$ have polynomial mode bounds. In each surviving product, all mode indices and the intermediate nonnegative energy are at most a constant times $1+h+|k|$ in absolute value. Apply the two mode bounds successively, then sum the polynomially many terms with their polynomial binomial coefficients. 17.1 For a nonempty interval $I$ let $\mathcal P_F(I)\Omega$ be the linear span of finite products of smooth F-smearings compactly supported there, applied to $\Omega$. This space is Hilbert dense.
