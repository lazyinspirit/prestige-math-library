## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Countable Choice ([[def-countable-choice]]). Work in the
notation of
[[thm-nrqm-orbital-and-spin-orbital-self-adjoint-generators]] and
[[thm-nrqm-orbital-and-spin-sphere-laplacian-diagonal-domain]]: $S^2$ with its
polar measure $\sigma$, the Hilbert space $L^{2}(S^2,\sigma)$ with inner
product linear in the first argument
([[def-l-p-space-as-a-quotient-by-null-functions]],
[[def-complex-lp-and-euclidean-test-function-conventions]],
[[def-hilbert-space]], [[def-inner-product-space]]), the complete orthonormal
family $(Y_{lm})_{l\ge0,\,-l\le m\le l}$
([[thm-nrqm-orbital-and-spin-sphere-onb]]), the blocks
$V_{l}:=\operatorname{span}\{Y_{l,-l},\dots,Y_{ll}\}$, the finite span $H$,
the self-adjoint operators $L_{1},L_{2},L_{3}$ with $L_{3}=L_{z}$ and
$L_{i}Y_{lm}\in V_{l}$ for all $l,m,i$, and the diagonal sphere Laplacian $A$
with $AY_{lm}=l(l+1)Y_{lm}$. Then for every $l\ge0$:

(a) each $L_{i}$ leaves $V_{l}$ invariant, and the restriction
$L_{i}|_{V_{l}}$ is a self-adjoint endomorphism of the finite-dimensional
inner product space $V_{l}$
([[def-self-adjoint-and-normal-endomorphism]]), with
$L_{3}Y_{lm}=\hbar mY_{lm}$ for $-l\le m\le l$;

(b) the triple $\bigl((1/\hbar)L_{1}|_{V_{l}},(1/\hbar)L_{2}|_{V_{l}},(1/\hbar)L_{3}|_{V_{l}}\bigr)$
is unitarily equivalent to the spin-$l$ matrices of
[[prop-nrqm-orbital-and-spin-finite-spin-matrices]]; in particular
$$\sum_{i=1}^{3}L_{i}^{2}F=\hbar^{2}l(l+1)F\qquad(F\in V_{l});$$

(c) for every $F$ in the finite span $H$ one has
$\sum_{i=1}^{3}L_{i}^{2}F=\hbar^{2}AF$.

The statement is a mathematical statement about the operators on
$L^{2}(S^2,\sigma)$; no physical interpretation as angular momentum of a
particle is asserted.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The Axiom of Countable Choice; the sphere $S^2$ with polar measure
$\sigma$; the family $(Y_{lm})$, the blocks $V_l$, the finite span $H$; the
operators $L_1,L_2,L_3$; the diagonal operator $A$; a degree $l\ge0$; indices
$m,i$; a vector $F\in H$.

[F1] **Orbital generators.** Each $L_{i}$ is self-adjoint on
$L^{2}(S^{2},\sigma)$; for $F\in V_{l}$ with polynomial representative $p_{F}$
one has $L_{i}F=\mathrm{res}_{l}(-i\hbar D_{i}p_{F})$ with
$D_{i}p=(x\times\nabla p)_{i}$, so $L_{i}V_{l}\subseteq V_{l}$ and
$L_{i}H\subseteq H$; on $H$ one has $L_{3}=L_{z}$ with
$L_{3}Y_{lm}=\hbar mY_{lm}$, and
$L_{i}L_{j}-L_{j}L_{i}=i\hbar\sum_{k}\varepsilon_{ijk}L_{k}$ for all $i,j$
([[thm-nrqm-orbital-and-spin-orbital-self-adjoint-generators]] clauses
(a),(b),(c)).

[F2] **Sphere Laplacian and the basis of a block.** $A$ is self-adjoint with
$AY_{lm}=l(l+1)Y_{lm}$, the finite span $H$ is a core of $A$, and the
$Y_{l,-l},\dots,Y_{ll}$ are orthonormal in $L^{2}(S^{2},\sigma)$
([[thm-nrqm-orbital-and-spin-sphere-laplacian-diagonal-domain]] clauses
(a),(b), [[thm-nrqm-orbital-and-spin-sphere-onb]]); hence
$\dim V_{l}=2l+1$ ([[def-dimension]],
[[def-orthonormal-family-complete-orthonormal-system-and-hilbert-basis]]).

[F3] **Finite restrictions are self-adjoint.** If a self-adjoint operator $T$
on a Hilbert space leaves a finite-dimensional subspace $W$ invariant, then
$T|_{W}$ is a self-adjoint endomorphism of $W$: for $x,y\in W$ one has
$\langle T|_{W}x,y\rangle=\langle Tx,y\rangle=\langle x,Ty\rangle=\langle x,T|_{W}y\rangle$
because $Ty\in W$ and $T$ is symmetric on its domain
([[def-self-adjoint-and-normal-endomorphism]],
[[def-symmetric-self-adjoint-and-essentially-self-adjoint]],
[[def-linear-subspace]]).

[F4] **Block decomposition of a finite angular momentum triple.** If a
nonzero finite-dimensional complex inner product space $V$ carries
self-adjoint endomorphisms $J_{1},J_{2},J_{3}$ with
$[J_{1},J_{2}]=iJ_{3}$ and its cyclic companions, then there are
$r\ge1$ and $j_{1},\dots,j_{r}\in\frac12\mathbb N$ with
$\sum_{\nu}(2j_{\nu}+1)=\dim V$ and an orthonormal basis
$(v_{\nu,k})$ of $V$ such that, with $J_{\pm}:=J_{1}\pm iJ_{2}$ and
$J^{2}:=J_{1}^{2}+J_{2}^{2}+J_{3}^{2}$,
$$J_{3}v_{\nu,k}=(j_{\nu}-k)v_{\nu,k},\quad J_{+}v_{\nu,k}=\sqrt{k(2j_{\nu}-k+1)}\,v_{\nu,k-1},\quad J_{-}v_{\nu,k}=\sqrt{(k+1)(2j_{\nu}-k)}\,v_{\nu,k+1},\quad J^{2}v_{\nu,k}=j_{\nu}(j_{\nu}+1)v_{\nu,k};$$
moreover $\dim\ker(J_{3}-MI)$ equals the number of blocks with
$j_{\nu}\in M+\mathbb Z$ and $j_{\nu}\ge M$, and the number of blocks with
$j_{\nu}=M$ equals $\dim\ker(J_{3}-MI)-\dim\ker(J_{3}-(M+1)I)$, for
$M\in\frac12\mathbb N$; on each block the map $v_{\nu,k}\mapsto e_{j_{\nu}-k}$
is a unitary equivalence of $(J_{1},J_{2},J_{3})$ with the spin-$j_{\nu}$
matrices, and $J^{2}$ is self-adjoint and commutes with $J_{1},J_{2},J_{3}$
([[thm-nrqm-orbital-and-spin-finite-spin-block-decomposition]] clauses
(a),(b),(d), [[prop-nrqm-orbital-and-spin-finite-spin-matrices]] clause (b)).

[F5] **Countable sums of weights.** The spectrum of $L_{3}$ on the finite
space $V_{l}$ consists of the numbers $\hbar m$, $m=-l,\dots,l$, each with
multiplicity one, because $(Y_{lm})_{m}$ is an orthonormal basis of $V_{l}$
consisting of eigenvectors by [F1], [F2]
([[def-dimension]], [[cor-inner-product-induces-a-norm]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct; restrict the self-adjoint generators to the
finite blocks, invoke the finite-dimensional classification of angular
momentum triples, and identify the single block and its parameter from the
simple weight spectrum of $L_{3}$.

**Given:** The data of the statement and [F1]-[F5].

1.1 [Invariance and finite self-adjointness] Fix $l$; by [F1] $L_{i}V_{l}\subseteq V_{l}$, and by [F3] applied to the invariant finite-dimensional subspace $V_{l}\subset L^{2}(S^{2},\sigma)$ the restriction $L_{i}|_{V_{l}}$ is a self-adjoint endomorphism of $V_{l}$; by [F1] and [F2], $L_{3}Y_{lm}=\hbar mY_{lm}$ for every $m$ with $|m|\le l$, which is (a). [F1, F2, F3, algebra]

2.1 [Angular momentum relations on the block] Put $J_{i}:=(1/\hbar)L_{i}|_{V_{l}}$; by step 1.1 the $J_{i}$ are self-adjoint endomorphisms of $V_{l}$ and by [F1] they satisfy $[J_{i},J_{j}]=i\sum_{k}\varepsilon_{ijk}J_{k}$ on $V_{l}$; moreover $J^{2}:=\sum_{i}J_{i}^{2}$ commutes with each $J_{i}$ by [F4] clause (a), and the eigenvalues of $J_{3}$ on $V_{l}$, read in units of $\hbar$, are $-l,-l+1,\dots,l$, each simple by [F5]. [step 1.1, F1, F4, F5, algebra]

3.1 [Single block of parameter $l$] By step 2.1 and [F4] applied to the nonzero finite-dimensional space $V_{l}$ with the triple $(J_{1},J_{2},J_{3})$, there are $r\ge1$ and parameters $j_{\nu}\in\frac12\mathbb N$ whose weights $j_{\nu}-k$, $0\le k\le2j_{\nu}$, make up the spectrum of $J_{3}$; since every weight of an integer parameter is an integer and every weight of $J_{3}$ is an integer by step 2.1, no parameter is a non-integer half-integer, and [F4] applied with $M=0$ gives $r=\dim\ker J_{3}=1$ because $0$ is a simple eigenvalue of $J_{3}$; applying [F4] with $M=l$ gives $1=\dim\ker(J_{3}-lI)-\dim\ker(J_{3}-(l+1)I)$, so the unique parameter equals $l$; hence $J^{2}=l(l+1)\mathrm{id}_{V_{l}}$ by [F4], that is $\sum_{i}L_{i}^{2}F=\hbar^{2}l(l+1)F$ for every $F\in V_{l}$, and the block basis of [F4] exhibits the unitary equivalence to the spin-$l$ matrices, which is (b). [step 2.1, F4, algebra]

4.1 [Finite span] Let $F\in H$; as a finite linear combination $F=\sum_{l,m}c_{lm}Y_{lm}$, the identities of step 3.1 apply to each term because $Y_{lm}\in V_{l}$, and $\sum_{i}L_{i}^{2}F=\sum_{l,m}c_{lm}\hbar^{2}l(l+1)Y_{lm}=\hbar^{2}AF$ by the diagonal definition of $A$ in [F2] and the linearity of the finite sums. [step 3.1, F2, algebra] ∎

## Remarks

- **Why the block argument is not circular.** The classification theorem
  [[thm-nrqm-orbital-and-spin-finite-spin-block-decomposition]] is applied to
  the finite restrictions $(1/\hbar)L_{i}|_{V_{l}}$; the Casimir eigenvalue is
  then identified from the weight spectrum already present in
  [[thm-nrqm-orbital-and-spin-orbital-self-adjoint-generators]], and no
  differential identity for $\sum_{i}D_{i}^{2}$ is assumed.
- **Scope.** The identity concerns the operators on $L^{2}(S^{2},\sigma)$ and
  their finite harmonic span; no domain statement for a maximal operator
  $\sum_{i}L_{i}^{2}$ on $L^{2}(\mathbb R^{3})$ is made here.
