## Definition

Let $\mathcal D$ be an essentially small triangulated category, meaning that it is equivalent to a category whose objects form a set. Write $K(\mathcal D)$ for its triangulated Grothendieck group, with the distinguished-triangle relation $[B]=[A]+[C]$ for every triangle $A\to B\to C\to A[1]$. Fix a finite-rank free abelian group $\Gamma$, a group homomorphism $v:K(\mathcal D)\to\Gamma$, and a norm $\|\cdot\|$ on $\Gamma_{\mathbb R}=\Gamma\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb R$.

A slicing is a family of full additive subcategories $\mathcal P(\phi)\subseteq\mathcal D$, indexed by $\phi\in\mathbb R$, such that $\mathcal P(\phi+1)=\mathcal P(\phi)[1]$, $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal D}(A_1,A_2)=0$ when $A_i\in\mathcal P(\phi_i)$ and $\phi_1>\phi_2$, and each nonzero object $E$ has a finite Harder–Narasimhan filtration by distinguished triangles whose nonzero factors belong to $\mathcal P(\phi_1),\ldots,\mathcal P(\phi_k)$ with $\phi_1>\cdots>\phi_k$. For $I\subseteq\mathbb R$, $\mathcal P(I)$ denotes the extension-closed full subcategory generated by the $\mathcal P(\phi)$ with $\phi\in I$.

A locally finite Bridgeland stability condition with respect to $v$ is a pair $\sigma=(Z,\mathcal P)$, where $Z:\Gamma\to\mathbb C$ is a group homomorphism and $\mathcal P$ is a slicing, satisfying $Z(v(E))=m(E)e^{i\pi\phi}$ for every nonzero $E\in\mathcal P(\phi)$, with $m(E)>0$, and the source local-finiteness condition: for some $\eta>0$, each $\mathcal P((\phi-\eta,\phi+\eta))$ has finite length in its induced strict exact structure, uniformly in $\phi$. A nonzero object in some $\mathcal P(\phi)$ is semistable of phase $\phi$; it is stable when it is simple in that phase subcategory.

The support property means that there is a constant $C>0$ such that $\|v(E)\|\le C|Z(v(E))|$ for every nonzero semistable object $E$. In finite rank this condition is independent of the chosen norm. The stability space $\operatorname{Stab}_{\Gamma}(\mathcal D)$ consists of these locally finite stability conditions satisfying the support property; no existence of such a condition on an arbitrary category is asserted by this definition.

For a stability condition $\sigma=(Z,\mathcal P)$, let $\phi^+_\sigma(E)$ and $\phi^-_\sigma(E)$ be the largest and smallest phases in its finite Harder–Narasimhan filtration. For two slicings $\mathcal P,\mathcal Q$, set
$
d(\mathcal P,\mathcal Q)=\sup_{0\ne E\in\mathcal D}\max\{|\phi^+_{\mathcal P}(E)-\phi^+_{\mathcal Q}(E)|,\ |\phi^-_{\mathcal P}(E)-\phi^-_{\mathcal Q}(E)|\}.
$
For $U\in\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb Z}(\Gamma,\mathbb C)$, set $\|U\|_\sigma=\sup_{E\ \sigma\text{-semistable},\ E\ne0}|U(v(E))|/|Z(v(E))|$, allowing $+\infty$. Both suprema are taken in the nonnegative extended real numbers $[0,+\infty]$, so the supremum of an empty family is $0$. In particular the zero category has the unique zero slicing, $d(\mathcal P,\mathcal P)=0$, and $\|U\|_\sigma=0$ for every charge perturbation $U$; its support condition is vacuous. The source topology on the stability space is generated by
$
B_\epsilon(\sigma)=\{(W,\mathcal Q):\|W-Z\|_\sigma<\sin(\pi\epsilon),\ d(\mathcal P,\mathcal Q)<\epsilon\},\qquad 0<\epsilon<1/4.
$
