## Postulate

Let $T>0$ be the prescribed temperature control in kelvins,
$\beta=(k_BT)^{-1}$, and let the chemical-potential control $\mu$ have
units J per particle under the SI convention
[[def-td-si-quantities]]. In this adopted law, $\mu$ is the energy
coefficient conjugate to the dimensionless sector count $N$ in the weight
$e^{-\beta(H_N-\mu N)}$; no derivative with respect to integer $N$ is used.
For $N\ge1$, let $\Gamma_N$ and its reference
measure be those of [[post-csm-counting-and-physical-observables]], and let
$$H_N:\Gamma_N\to\mathbb R\cup\{+\infty\}$$
be Borel measurable for the sector sigma-algebra
$\mathcal F_N$ generated by the open sets of $\Gamma_N$, with $+\infty$
marking forbidden states, and use the convention
$e^{-\beta(+\infty)}:=0$. In each sector require
$H_N(gz)=H_N(z)$ for $m_N^{\rm phys}$-almost every $z$ and every
within-species permutation $g\in G_N$, with $G_N$ and its action as in
[[post-csm-counting-and-physical-observables]]. Hence each sector Gibbs
density admits an invariant representative; the factor $e^{\beta\mu N}$
is constant on that sector and preserves this invariance. Operationally
distinguishable labels have trivial permutation factors as in that convention.
Define
the vacuum sector by $\Gamma_0=\{*\}$ with sigma-algebra
$\mathcal F_0=\{\varnothing,\{*\}\}$, reference mass
$m_0^{\mathrm{phys}}(\{*\})=1$, particle number $N=0$, and vacuum Hamiltonian
$H_0(*)=0$. On the disjoint union $\mathcal G:=\bigsqcup_{N=0}^\infty\Gamma_N$,
use the sigma-algebra
$$\mathcal F_{\mathcal G}:=\{A\subseteq\mathcal G:A\cap\Gamma_N\in\mathcal F_N\text{ for every }N\ge0\},$$
where $\mathcal F_N$ is the declared measurable structure on sector $N$, and
the reference measure
$$m^{\mathrm{phys}}(A):=\sum_{N=0}^\infty m_N^{\mathrm{phys}}(A\cap\Gamma_N).$$
This disjoint-union reference is sigma-finite because it is a countable sum
of the sigma-finite sector references, including the finite vacuum measure.
Let
$$\Xi(\beta,\mu):=\sum_{N=0}^\infty\int_{\Gamma_N} e^{-\beta(H_N-\mu N)}\,dm_N^{\rm phys}$$
satisfy $0<\Xi<\infty$. Adopt the probability measure on
$(\mathcal G,\mathcal F_{\mathcal G})$ with density
$$\rho(N,z):=\Xi^{-1}e^{-\beta(H_N(z)-\mu N)}$$
relative to $m^{\mathrm{phys}}$. The finiteness condition is
part of the model; it requires control of the sector sum and is not implied
by each finite-sector partition function being finite. Particle-exchange
preparation is an assumption, not a deduction from fixed-$N$ dynamics. Under
the vacuum convention above, the $N=0$ contribution to $\Xi$ is exactly one.
