## Statement

Take the finite-chain data of the current definition on $\mathcal H_L$ and identify $\mathcal H:=\mathcal H_L$. Let $H=H^*$ have energy unit joules, let $\hbar>0$ have unit joule-seconds, and let $\rho$ be a positive-definite trace-one matrix satisfying $[\rho,H]=0$. Define $\omega(A):=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho A)$ and $\alpha_t(A):=e^{iHt/\hbar}Ae^{-iHt/\hbar}$. Let $J_L$ be the total lattice current of the finite-volume current definition and center it with respect to this same $\rho$, setting $J:=J_L-\omega(J_L)I$. With no identification of $H$ and $H_L$, the estimate below is an abstract bound for this observable under $\alpha_t$. Its lattice-transport specialization further takes $H=H_L$; then the Gibbs density $\rho_{\beta,L}$ in that definition is stationary and is an admissible choice of $\rho$. More generally, $\rho_{\beta,L}$ can be used with another $H$ exactly when it also satisfies $[\rho_{\beta,L},H]=0$. Then $J=J^*$, as established by the local continuity result. For $m\ge1$, let $Q_1,\ldots,Q_m$ be linearly independent conserved matrices, $[H,Q_a]=0$. Put
$$G_{ab}:=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho Q_a^*Q_b),\qquad v_a:=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho Q_a^*J).$$
Then $G$ is positive definite, the finite Cesàro limit exists, and
$$\lim_{T\to\infty}\frac1T\int_0^T\operatorname{Re}\,\omega\!\left(\alpha_t(J)J\right)\,dt\;\ge\;v^*G^{-1}v\;\ge0.$$
This is a finite-dimensional persistent-correlation bound. It does not identify a thermodynamic Drude weight or assert that either volume/time order of limits exists.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The finite-dimensional Hilbert space, self-adjoint $H$, positive-definite stationary density $\rho$, centered self-adjoint current $J$, conserved matrices $Q_a$, and $\hbar>0$ in the Statement.

[A1] Faithfulness and stationarity: $\rho>0$, $\operatorname{Tr}\rho=1$, and $[\rho,H]=0$.

[F1] [[def-qsmx-lattice-current-and-limits]]: the lattice total current is $J_L=\sum_{(j,j+1)} j_{j,j+1}$, with $j_{j,j+1}=(i/\hbar)[h_{j,j+1},q_{j+1}]$; the same definition specifies the connected finite-volume correlation convention.

[F2] [[lem-qsmx-lattice-current-continuity]]: each such bond current is self-adjoint and obeys the local continuity equation under the stated bond-locality and charge-conservation hypotheses.

[F3] [[thm-qsm-dt-cesaro-dephasing]]: for finite-dimensional unitary evolution generated by self-adjoint $H$, Cesàro averages of every density matrix converge in trace norm to its equal-energy block-diagonal part $\sum_E P_E\rho P_E$; blocks inside degenerate eigenspaces remain.

[F4] [[lem-qsm-matrix-state-calculus]]: finite Hermitian matrices have orthogonal spectral projections, and the finite trace is cyclic.

## Proof

**Proof technique:** orthogonal projection onto the conserved subspace.

**Given:** [A1], [F1]–[F4], a centered self-adjoint $J$, and linearly independent $Q_a$ with $[H,Q_a]=0$.

1.1 The lattice-current definition fixes $J_L=\sum_jj_{j,j+1}$, and the local-current lemma gives $J_L=J_L^*$. Subtracting its real mean $\omega(J_L)$ preserves self-adjointness, so $J$ is a matrix observable in $\mathcal B(\mathcal H)$. [given, F1, F2, algebra]

1.2 On $\mathcal B(\mathcal H)$ define $(A,B)_\rho:=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho A^*B)$, linear in its second argument. It is positive definite: if $(A,A)_\rho=0$, then $\rho\ge cI$ for some $c>0$ in finite dimension, so $0=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho A^*A)\ge c\operatorname{Tr}(A^*A)$ and hence $A=0$. The invariance $[\rho,H]=0$ gives $(\alpha_t(A),\alpha_t(B))_\rho=(A,B)_\rho$ by cyclicity of the finite trace, so $\alpha_t$ is unitary on this finite-dimensional Hilbert space of matrices. [given, A1, F4, algebra]

2.1 Write $H=\sum_E E P_E$ over its distinct eigenvalues. Then $\alpha_t(J)=\sum_{E,F}e^{it(E-F)/\hbar}P_EJP_F$. For $E\ne F$,
$$\left|\frac1T\int_0^T e^{it(E-F)/\hbar}\,dt\right|\le\frac{2\hbar}{T|E-F|}\longrightarrow0,$$
while for $E=F$ the coefficient is $1$. The sum is finite, so $M_TJ:=T^{-1}\int_0^T\alpha_t(J)\,dt$ converges in matrix norm to $PJ:=\sum_E P_EJP_E$. This block map is the orthogonal projection onto $\mathcal N:=\{A:[H,A]=0\}$ in $(\cdot,\cdot)_\rho$: $PJ\in\mathcal N$, and each off-diagonal block of $J-PJ$ has zero weighted trace pairing with a block-diagonal matrix because $\rho$ is block diagonal. It is the same energy-block projection as the density-matrix dephasing in [F3]; [F3] states the density-matrix version, while the explicit finite expansion here proves the observable version used. [given, A1, F3, F4, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Since $J=J^*$, $(\alpha_t(J),J)_\rho=\omega(\alpha_t(J)J)$. Norm convergence in step 2.1 therefore gives
$$\lim_{T\to\infty}\frac1T\int_0^T\operatorname{Re}\,\omega(\alpha_t(J)J)\,dt=\operatorname{Re}(PJ,J)_\rho=\|PJ\|_\rho^2,$$
because $J-PJ$ is orthogonal to $\mathcal N$ and $PJ\in\mathcal N$. [step 1.2, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 Set $S:=\operatorname{span}\{Q_1,\ldots,Q_m\}\subseteq\mathcal N$, and let $P_S$ be its orthogonal projection. Then $P_SJ=P_SPJ$, so $\|PJ\|_\rho^2\ge\|P_SJ\|_\rho^2$. The Gram matrix $G$ is positive definite by linear independence and the positive-definite inner product. Writing $P_SJ=\sum_a c_aQ_a$, orthogonality gives $Gc=v$, so $c=G^{-1}v$ and $\|P_SJ\|_\rho^2=c^*Gc=v^*G^{-1}v$. Together with step 3.1 this proves the bound. [given, step 1.1, step 1.2, step 3.1, algebra] ∎
