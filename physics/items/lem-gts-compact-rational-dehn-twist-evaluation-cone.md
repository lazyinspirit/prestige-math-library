## Statement

For this Statement, the integral-prequantum convention means that $[\omega]$ is integral in the source normalization, and an unobstructed brane is supplied with a chosen bounding cochain $b$ whose deformed curvature is $\mu_0^b=0$. Let $n\in\{2,3\}$ and let $X$ be a compact Calabi–Yau symplectic manifold in those conventions. Let $S\subset X$ be an actual framed graded Lagrangian sphere with its quadratic-thimble Spin lift and actual zero-curvature bounding object. Let $\tau_S$ be the positive compactly supported twist in that framed cotangent tube, with canonical lift $\tau_S(S)=S[1-n]$.

For a finite absolute probe collection, an action-coset line at a Floer generator $x$ is a one-dimensional complex vector space $\ell_x$ with a chosen basis and a real action label $A_x+\Gamma$, where $\Gamma=m^{-1}\mathbb Z$ for some integer $m\ge1$. The action label is formal data: $q^{A_x}$ is not assumed to be a scalar in the coefficient field. A polygon or section $u$ induces a map from the tensor of its input lines to its output line; after these line identifications, its scalar energy/action exponent is a real number $a(u)$. Call the finite collection rational when all such closed scalar exponents lie in $\Gamma$. Its scalar coefficients then lie in the complete Laurent/Puiseux field $\Lambda_m=\mathbb C((q^{1/m}))$. Every finite probe collection used below, including its Hamiltonian copies and twist data, has its own such finite $m$.

For every admissible compact unobstructed brane $K$ in such a rational collection, the actual right Yoneda modules satisfy

$$\operatorname{Cone}\bigl(CF(S,K)\otimes Y_S\xrightarrow{\mathrm{ev}}Y_K\bigr)\simeq Y_{\tau_S K},$$

where the first arrow is the full geometric Yoneda evaluation. The equivalence is induced by actual all-arity polygon/critical-section maps and a full module nullhomotopy, not only by an associated-graded acyclicity statement. It is natural on both module sides and compatible with coherent continuation and the framed grading.

For the relative clause, let $D=\sum_iD_i$ be a reduced effective divisor with simple normal crossings (its smooth components meet as coordinate hyperplanes in local complex charts), disjoint from the twist tube and with all branes in $X\setminus D$. Require a fixed integrable complex structure on a neighborhood of $D$ for which these are holomorphic normal-crossing charts. Every auxiliary almost-complex structure used for the relative raw disk and section moduli agrees there with this structure (with its product extension in the section fibrations), and the Hamiltonian and other inhomogeneous terms in those raw map equations vanish there. Assume $[\omega]=\kappa\,\operatorname{PD}[D]$ for $\kappa>0$, and choose the source connection primitive $\lambda$ on $X\setminus D$ with $d\lambda=\omega$ and integral $-\kappa$ on a positively oriented small meridian of each component. The branes are exact for $\lambda$. Let $s_D$ be the canonical section of $\mathcal O_X(D)$, which is nonvanishing on every brane in $X\setminus D$. For a relative disk or section class $\beta$ with boundary on those branes, put $d=\langle c_1(\mathcal O_X(D),s_D|_{\cup L_j}),\beta\rangle$, the relative topological intersection with the total divisor. The relative supplier proves that these holomorphic component intersections are nonnegative and additive. Use coefficients $\mathbb C[\![q]\!]$ with weight $q^d$ and $\deg q=0$; all positive-$q$ disk and sphere strata are retained.

For an absolute probe collection that may cross $D$, use its action-coset lines rather than the relative divisor exponent: $\beta\cdot D$ need not be invariant for such a probe. For each finite collection, choose the local cap action rational and small compared with its finitely many action gaps, enlarging $m$ finitely to include that cap action. The compatible finite-collection comparisons extend to $\Lambda_{\mathbb Q}=\bigcup_{m\ge1}\mathbb C((q^{1/m}))$. Thus no arbitrary real action origin is asserted to be a scalar in $\Lambda_m$ or $\Lambda_{\mathbb Q}$. Full Axiom of Choice is the ambient convention. No dimension-one compact extension, arbitrary obstructed brane, or independently constructed wrapped category is asserted.
