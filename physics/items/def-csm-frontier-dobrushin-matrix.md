## Definition

Let $d\geq1$, let $S$ be a finite nonempty set, and put $X=S^{\mathbb Z^d}$ with the $\sigma$-algebra generated by finite-coordinate cylinders. A specification on $X$ is a family $\gamma=(\gamma_\Lambda)$ of probability kernels indexed by finite $\Lambda\subset\mathbb Z^d$. It is proper when each kernel leaves every exterior-measurable event fixed, and consistent when $\gamma_\Lambda\gamma_\Delta=\gamma_\Lambda$ for all finite $\Delta\subseteq\Lambda$. For $i\in\mathbb Z^d$, write $\gamma_i(\cdot\mid\eta)$ for its one-site conditional probability vector on $S$, where $\eta\in S^{\mathbb Z^d\setminus\{i\}}$. For probability vectors $p,q$ on $S$, define their total-variation distance by $\lVert p-q\rVert_{\rm TV}:=\frac12\sum_{s\in S}|p(s)-q(s)|$. The influence coefficient of site $j$ on the conditional law at site $i$ is

$$c^\gamma_{ij}:=\begin{cases}0,&i=j,\\[2pt]\displaystyle\sup_{\substack{\eta,\eta'\in S^{\mathbb Z^d\setminus\{i\}}\\ \eta_k=\eta'_k\ \text{for every }k\notin\{i,j\}}}\lVert\gamma_i(\cdot\mid\eta)-\gamma_i(\cdot\mid\eta')\rVert_{\rm TV},&i\ne j.\end{cases}$$

The matrix $C^\gamma=(c^\gamma_{ij})_{i,j\in\mathbb Z^d}$ is the Dobrushin influence matrix of $\gamma$. Its coefficients lie in $[0,1]$; a supremum need not be attained. The Dobrushin row constant is $c_\gamma:=\sup_i\sum_j c^\gamma_{ij}$, with value $+\infty$ allowed. A common uniform finite influence radius $R$ means $c^\gamma_{ij}=0$ whenever $\lVert i-j\rVert_\infty>R$.

## Remarks

The arrow $j\to i$ below means that changing spin $j$ can change the conditional law at $i$; its weight is $C_{ij}$. A length-$n$ path from $j$ to $i$ contributes the product of its edge weights to $(C^n)_{ij}$. The picture fixes this index convention only; proofs use the matrix definitions above.

```tikz
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth]
  \node (j) at (0,0) {$j$};
  \node (k) at (2,0) {$k$};
  \node (i) at (4,0) {$i$};
  \draw[->] (j) -- node[above] {$C_{kj}$} (k);
  \draw[->] (k) -- node[above] {$C_{ik}$} (i);
  \draw[->,bend left=25] (j) to node[below] {$C_{ij}$} (i);
\end{tikzpicture}
```
