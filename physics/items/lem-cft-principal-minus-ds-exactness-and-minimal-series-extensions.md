## Statement

Assume Choice and noncritical k with pinned principal data. The actual minus-DS functor on affine category O is exact, with all nonzero-degree cohomology zero. It maps a simple L(lambda) to the simple highest W module of top $\gamma_{-w_0\bar\lambda}$ when lambdabar has no positive integral shifted positive-root pairing, and to zero otherwise.

At the original nondegenerate admissible thresholds, let $S_{\gamma }$ range over the minimal-series simples with characters $\gamma_{-w_0\bar\lambda}$, lambda nondegenerate admissible. A universal W Verma with such a top has no different minimal-series simple as a local composition factor. Consequently, if A is a CFT-type conformal quotient of $W^{k}$ whose simple list is precisely these ordinary modules and whose actual Zhu algebra is semisimple, every extension between two such simples in the lower-bounded generalized finite-grade module category splits. The last assertion does not itself assume A rational or simple.
