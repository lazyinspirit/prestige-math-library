## Definition

Let $M$ be a finite-dimensional complex manifold, let $\mathcal O_M$ be its sheaf of holomorphic functions, and let $\mathcal F$ be a coherent $\mathcal O_M$-module sheaf. On an open set $V$ contained in a coordinate chart and in a neighborhood with a finite presentation $\mathcal O_V^{p_1}\to\mathcal O_V^{p_0}\xrightarrow{\pi}\mathcal F|_V\to0$, where $p_0,p_1$ are finite nonnegative ranks, require that the section map $\Gamma(V,\mathcal O_V^{p_0})\to\Gamma(V,\mathcal F)$ is surjective. Give $\mathcal O(V)^{p_0}$ the compact-convergence topology generated by the seminorms $f\mapsto\sup_{z\in K}\lVert f(z)\rVert$ for compact $K\subset V$, and give $\Gamma(V,\mathcal F)$ the quotient topology induced by that surjection. On any open subset covered by countably many such sets, use the topology of the closed subspace of the product of their local section spaces consisting of compatible families. For a finite ordered open cover $\mathcal U=(U_i)$ whose nonempty finite intersections have these section topologies, give each Čech cochain space

$$C^q(\mathcal U,\mathcal F):=\prod_{i_0<\cdots<i_q}\Gamma(U_{i_0}\cap\cdots\cap U_{i_q},\mathcal F),\qquad (\delta c)_{i_0\cdots i_{q+1}}=\sum_{\nu=0}^{q+1}(-1)^\nu c_{i_0\cdots\widehat{i_\nu}\cdots i_{q+1}}\big|_{U_{i_0}\cap\cdots\cap U_{i_{q+1}}}.$$

the finite-product topology, give its cocycle space the subspace topology, and give the quotient of cocycles by coboundaries the Čech quotient topology. The accompanying Fréchet-quotient lemma proves local chart and presentation independence; the compact projective-space finiteness theorem proves independence of its admissible finite acyclic cover topology.
