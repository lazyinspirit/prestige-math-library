## Definition

Assume the Axiom of Choice as inherited through the cited Hall supplier. Let
$V=\mathbb C^2$ with ordered orthonormal basis $|\uparrow\rangle,|\downarrow\rangle$.
On $V$ define
$$ E=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad F=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad H=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}. $$
They satisfy $[H,E]=2E$, $[H,F]=-2F$, and $[E,F]=H$, and therefore define
the standard two-dimensional representation of $\mathfrak{sl}_2(\mathbb C)$.
Write $S^+=E$, $S^-=F$, and $S^z=H/2$.

For each integer $N\geq1$, the spin space is
$\mathcal H_N=V^{\otimes N}$ with its tensor-product inner product. If $A$ is
an endomorphism of $V$, let $A_j$ act as $A$ on factor $j$ and as the identity
on the other factors. Define the diagonal generators
$$ S_N^+=\sum_{j=1}^N E_j,\qquad S_N^-=\sum_{j=1}^N F_j,\qquad S_N^z=\frac12\sum_{j=1}^N H_j. $$
For $m\in\{-N/2,-N/2+1,\ldots,N/2\}$, its weight space is
$\mathcal H_{N,m}=\ker(S_N^z-mI)$. These are dimensionless matrices in the
algebraic spin convention.

For each integer $r\geq0$, define $V(r)$ to be the vector space
$\mathbb C[x,y]_r$ of homogeneous polynomials of degree $r$, with
$\mathfrak{sl}_2$ action
$$ E=x\partial_y,\qquad F=y\partial_x,\qquad H=x\partial_x-y\partial_y. $$
The ordered monomials $x^{r-k}y^k$ for $0\leq k\leq r$ form its basis.
Under $x\mapsto|\uparrow\rangle$ and $y\mapsto|\downarrow\rangle$,
$V(1)$ is the representation on $V$ above. A tensor power $V(1)^{\otimes N}$
uses the diagonal action defined by the sums $S_N^+,S_N^-,2S_N^z$.

Let $q_H>1$ be a prime power, fix a finite field $\mathbb F_{q_H}$, and put
$\nu=\sqrt{q_H}>1$ and $K=\mathbb Q(\nu)$. The representation category
$\mathcal A=\mathrm{Vect}^{\mathrm{fd}}_{\mathbb F_{q_H}}$ of the one-vertex,
no-arrow quiver has one simple isomorphism class, represented by
$S=\mathbb F_{q_H}$. For objects $M,N$, write
$\chi_0(M,N)=\dim_{\mathbb F_{q_H}}\operatorname{Hom}(M,N)-
\dim_{\mathbb F_{q_H}}\operatorname{YExt}^1(M,N)$. In the Hall convention of
[[def-euler-twisted-hall-algebra]], the Hall space is
$H_{\mathcal A,\nu}=\bigoplus_{[M]}K[M]$, with basis $[M]$ indexed by
isomorphism classes and product
$$[M]\star[N]=\nu^{\chi_0(M,N)}\sum_{[L]}g^L_{M,N}[L],$$
where $g^L_{M,N}$ counts subspaces $N'\subseteq L$ with $N'\cong N$ and
$L/N'\cong M$, or equivalently short exact sequences
$0\to N\xrightarrow{i}L\xrightarrow{p}M\to0$ modulo changes of the
endpoint labels: $(i,p)\sim(i\circ a,b\circ p)$ for
$a\in\operatorname{Aut}(N)$ and $b\in\operatorname{Aut}(M)$, with $L$
fixed pointwise. Each orbit is determined by $\operatorname{im}i=N'$;
without this quotient, each such subspace contributes
$|\operatorname{Aut}(N)|\,|\operatorname{Aut}(M)|$ labelled sequences.
Thus the twist is part of the multiplication.
For $M\cong\mathbb F_{q_H}^{m}$ and $N\cong\mathbb F_{q_H}^{n}$,
$\dim\operatorname{Hom}(M,N)=mn$ and every short exact sequence splits, so
$\operatorname{YExt}^1(M,N)=0$ and $\chi_0(M,N)=mn$.

By [[def-extended-hall-algebra-and-composition-subalgebra]], the composition
subalgebra is the subalgebra of this Hall algebra generated by the classes of
simple objects; its torus extension is the distinct extended composition
subalgebra. Denote the former by $\mathcal C$ here, so
$\mathcal C=K\langle[S]\rangle\subseteq H_{\mathcal A,\nu}$. The rank-one
positive quantum half is $U_\nu^+(\mathfrak{sl}_2)=K[E]$, the polynomial
algebra on one generator. The Ringel map $E\mapsto[S]$ embeds this positive
half with image $\mathcal C$; since the one-vertex graph is finite Dynkin type
$A_1$, the finite-Dynkin criterion identifies $\mathcal C$ with the full Hall
algebra in this case. The simple object is unique up to isomorphism, not as a
chosen representative. The Axiom of Choice is inherited through the cited
Ringel supplier.

## Remarks

The formal Hall parameter $q_H$ and its square root $\nu$ are mathematical
parameters; $q_H$ is not a physical Potts-state parameter. The field-valued
Ringel identification is stated for $\nu>1$; a specialization to $\nu=1$
requires an integral form. The positive half contains no negative generator or
compact star structure. No categorical action on $V(1)^{\otimes N}$ is part of
this definition.
