## Remark

Every cited conditional result retains its stated hypotheses. In particular, the point-source and causal-wave comparisons below assume Countable Choice ($\mathrm{AC}_\omega$, [[def-countable-choice]]) through their source suppliers; this qualification supplies no additional field or self-interaction model.

Record the established scope limits of the classical electromagnetic results
assembled in this framework, with no speculative content. Each clause names
what is and is not supplied; the remark asserts no theorem and adds no result.

(a) [Continuum and regularity regime] The fields of the framework are the maps
of [[def-em-fields]] on the laboratory region, with the regularity class
carried by each statement; the solved branches are the smooth and stated weak
branches of the adopted equations, and every conclusion holds only under its
own stated regularity, domain and boundary hypotheses. No discrete, atomic or
finite-resolution structure of matter or charge is assumed, and none is
derived.

(b) [Material-model regime] The material results rest on the declared
constitutive branches of [[post-em-material-closure]] (the elementary
inertial-rest linear nondispersive Ohmic branch and the causal memory branch
with its declared kernel or transfer-function hypotheses); arbitrary
nonlinear, nonlocal, hysteretic, or beyond-branch anisotropic and strongly
dispersive response is not established by this framework, and each declared
branch carries its own regime of validity, including the finite-band
qualification of the constant-coefficient branch.

(c) [Singular sources and self-interaction]
For a nonzero point charge $q\ne0$,
[[pthm-em-point-self-energy-divergence]](a)-(b) gives divergence of the
self-field energy as the inner cutoff is removed; for $q=0$ its displayed
truncated energy is identically zero and this divergence does not occur.
[[rem-em-radiation-reaction-limits]] records that products of a singular
self-field with the charge's own distributional current are not defined by
the adopted source model, and that the emitted-power results concern
prescribed sources and determine no back-reaction. No self-force dynamics, runaway
analysis, finite-size regularization or electromagnetic derivation of rest
mass is supplied by this framework.

(d) [External-force and mechanical coupling] Particle motion enters only
through the adopted Lorentz coupling [[post-em-lorentz-coupling]] with a
prescribed external field; the framework determines the motion of a specified
test charge in a specified field and supplies no material law, no self-field
dynamics and no derivation of the particle parameters. Any coupling to matter
beyond the declared branches is an additional model assumption.

(e) [Quantum and ontological exclusions] The framework adopts no quantisation
of charge, no spin or magnetic-moment degree of freedom, no measurement
postulates and no photon model. Its interference results, such as the coherent
cross term of [[pthm-em-classical-interference]], are deterministic statements
about classical wave fields; classical electromagnetic waves interfere, so an
interference pattern alone neither establishes a quantum description nor
falsifies classical electromagnetism, and the framework's field predictions
do not single out an ontology of the underlying field or of a particle.

(f) [Empirical status] The empirical content of this page is report-backed and
qualitative: the records of the three primary reports, in their stated read
extents, carry no calibrated response functions, trial-level datasets or
declared sampling laws, so they support and constrain the specified models
only within their declared apparatus, calibration and analysis assumptions;
agreement does not prove the framework and disagreement does not deductively
falsify it. No measurement, precision, probability or confidence level is
invented or inferred by this remark.

(g) [Status] This is a scope remark, not an established theorem; it records the
limits of the results assembled here and asserts no new result, no measurement
and no proof.
