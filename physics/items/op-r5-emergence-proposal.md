## Open problem

The Wilsonian cutoff is a chosen positive matching scale below the ultraviolet completion scale. For a declared tower, let $N(E)$ count its species below energy $E$ with a stated degeneracy convention; the species estimate is $\Lambda_{\rm sp}^{d-2}\sim M_{P,d}^{d-2}/N(\Lambda_{\rm sp})$, used only parametrically in its stated regime. Here “emergence” means a specified contribution to a Wilsonian kinetic coefficient produced by integrating out declared fields with a declared regulator and matching prescription. That prescription fixes the renormalization scheme and finite counterterms, while ultraviolet boundary data can alter the coefficient.

With those conventions, the open proposal asks whether, in a specified controlled quantum-gravity limit with a known tower, some scalar, gauge and gravitational kinetic data are generated by integrating out that tower, with the cutoff tied to the species scale. The tower, scalar dependence of its masses, number of species, loop order, counterterms, and range of validity must be supplied for any controlled conditional calculation. The proposal does not assert that all kinetic terms vanish at the cutoff or that every coupling is regulator independent.
