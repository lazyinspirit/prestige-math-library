## Statement

Let $X$ be a proper scheme over $\mathbb C$ with a symmetric perfect obstruction theory of virtual dimension zero and associated virtual count $\#^{\rm vir}(X)=\deg[X]^{\rm vir}$. Let $c_X$ be Behrend's canonical constructible cycle and $\nu_X=\operatorname{Eu}(c_X)$ its canonical constructible function. Then, in the scheme case of Behrend Theorem 4.18,
$
\#^{\rm vir}(X)=\chi_c(X,\nu_X).
$
The broader source statement is restricted to smooth Deligne–Mumford stacks, global finite-group quotients, or gerbes over schemes; no identity for arbitrary proper Deligne–Mumford stacks is asserted.
