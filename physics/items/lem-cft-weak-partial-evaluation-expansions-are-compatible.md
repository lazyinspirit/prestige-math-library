## Statement

Let $V$ be strongly graded Möbius or conformal and let the strongly graded generalized modules have real weights and objectwise uniform nilpotence. Set $a=z_0$, $b=z_2$, $c=a+b=z_1$, with $|c|>|b|>|a|>0$. Assume the actual scalar products and iterates of the given two intertwining maps converge and define analytic branches on their sewing domains. For the product fix its output-dual vector and last input and take the partial functional on the first two inputs. For the iterate fix its output-dual vector and first input and take the partial functional on the last two inputs. Suppose each partial functional has a weak sum of real-weight generalized eigenvectors of its explicitly defined two-point $D=L'_{P(a)}(0)$ or $D_1=L'_{P(b)}(0)$, with finite auxiliary support in each term, one finite nilpotence bound, and absolute convergence of its grouped $e^{sD}$ expansion on one complex neighborhood of zero independent of its inner test vectors. Require the single space generated by all its expansion terms under all transported modes and the three Möbius operators to have finite-dimensional doubly homogeneous components and lower truncation in every fixed auxiliary-degree/integer-weight string. These hypotheses are required for every algebraic exterior pair.

Then every spectral term satisfies full two-point compatibility, including lower truncation as a functional and the complete transported delta identity. The conclusion holds in both partial-evaluation directions with their specified branches, without discreteness or finite exponent classes. If each cyclic generated term additionally has the stipulated injected transported-module realization in an object of the category, the local strong-grading and cyclic-admission lemma gives an actual strongly graded generalized module on the space generated by all terms; category membership of an entire infinite sum is not inferred.
