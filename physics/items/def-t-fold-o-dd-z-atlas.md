## Definition

Let $N$ be a smooth Hausdorff second-countable base manifold with open cover $\{U_\alpha\}$, and let $\mathbb T^d:=\mathbb R^d/(2\pi\mathbb Z)^d$ be the real $d$-torus defined by the displayed translation equivalence. A local torus patch is $U_\alpha\times\mathbb T^d$ with dimensionless fibre coordinates of period $2\pi$, a smooth positive-definite dimensionless fibre metric $g_\alpha$, a smooth antisymmetric dimensionless two-form $B_\alpha$, and modulus matrix $E_\alpha=g_\alpha+B_\alpha$. The integral momentum-winding lattice is $\mathbb Z^{2d}$ with bilinear form $\eta=\begin{pmatrix}0&I_d\\I_d&0\end{pmatrix}$. Define $O(d,d;\mathbb Z):=\{M\in GL(2d,\mathbb Z):M^T\eta M=\eta\}$. On each overlap, specify a base-coordinate diffeomorphism, a local two-form gauge transformation $B\mapsto B+d\Lambda_1$, and a locally constant matrix $M_{\alpha\beta}\in O(d,d;\mathbb Z)$ acting on the integral momentum-winding lattice. If $M_{\alpha\beta}=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}$, its local action on torus moduli is $E\mapsto(aE+b)(cE+d)^{-1}$ wherever the denominator is invertible. Require the base, gauge, and lattice transitions to compose consistently on triple overlaps; in particular $M_{\alpha\beta}M_{\beta\gamma}=M_{\alpha\gamma}$. The geometric subgroup is generated by $GL(d,\mathbb Z)$ changes of torus basis and integral $B$-field shifts. If an overlap matrix lies outside that subgroup, the local $g_\alpha$ and $B_\alpha$ need not assemble into one ordinary global metric and two-form. On the doubled torus $\widehat{\mathbb T}^{2d}:=\mathbb R^{2d}/(2\pi\mathbb Z)^{2d}$ the same integral matrix acts as a torus diffeomorphism. These data define a candidate T-fold atlas only. A global quantum string background additionally requires an exact fibre-CFT isomorphism on each overlap, including momentum-winding and operator-cocycle data, and separate modular-invariance, worldsheet-anomaly, beta-function, and global-defect checks.

## Remarks

On a triple overlap the following diagram states the lattice cocycle condition. Its arrows are the induced integral coordinate transitions; commutativity checks only the doubled-lattice atlas and does not assert that the corresponding fibre conformal field theories have already been identified.

& \mathbb T^{2d}_\alpha
