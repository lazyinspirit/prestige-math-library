## Example

As defined in [[def-td-system-and-permitted-exchanges]], choose the gas inside a sealed cylinder with a conducting wall and movable piston as the system. If the outer boundary allows energy transfer but no matter transfer, it is closed. Choose instead a vessel whose outlet allows water vapour to cross the boundary; it is open because that named matter species crosses. An ideal rigid, impermeable, thermally insulating enclosure with no other external energy channel is isolated. These classifications depend on the selected boundary and stipulated channels; they do not claim that a real apparatus is perfectly sealed or insulated.
