## Statement

For $N\in\{4,5\}$ and $n=N-2$, take the compact relative category and source symplectic scaling of the preceding named construction. In the real hyperplane $H_{\mathbb R}=\{\sum x_i=0\}$ let $S=\{\sum x_i^2=1\}$, let $f=-\sum x_i^3$, and let $L_\epsilon$ be its small exact graph immersion into the pair of pants. Its finite embedded lifts define the actual finite geometric category, not merely an abstract exterior algebra.

Its minimal central binary algebra is the graded exterior/smash model $Q_N=\Lambda(V)\rtimes\widetilde\Gamma$, where $\widetilde\Gamma=\{(\zeta_i)\in\mu_N^N:\prod\zeta_i=1\}$ and a singleton generator has symmetric downstairs rational degree $n/N$. The operations of arities $3,\ldots,N-1$ vanish, and the primary arity-$N$ Hochschild class is a nonzero scalar multiple of $w=u_1\cdots u_N$. The all-distinct rigid coefficient is $\pm1$ in its transported orientation line.

After the actual compact CY object regrading, every fixed nontrivial primitive-idempotent Hom is concentrated in one integer degree; self Homs are in degrees $0,n$. Relative cohomology is therefore finite free over $\mathbb C[\![q]\!]$, with a strictly unital finite-group equivariant minimal model. The first-order geometric compact relative deformation class is the nonzero Fermat line $\mathbb C[\sum_j u_j^N]$, with $\deg q=0$. In the multivariable first-order model each coefficient of $q_j[u_j^N]$ is $\pm1$ with the fixed brane/deck labels; specializing all $q_j=q$ gives a nonzero class. The one-divisor contact-power comparison, including its confluent jets and ramified roots, is part of this conclusion. No identification of these graph objects with a named pencil collection, no split-generation, and no full HMS equivalence is assumed. Full Axiom of Choice is the ambient convention.
