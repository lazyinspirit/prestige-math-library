## Definition

Fix an integer $d\ge1$, a finite nonempty alphabet $S$ with the discrete topology, and a bijective enumeration $x_1,x_2,\ldots$ of $\mathbb Z^d$. Let $\Omega=S^{\mathbb Z^d}$ be the set of all configurations $\sigma:\mathbb Z^d\to S$ with the product topology, the smallest topology that makes each coordinate map $\sigma\mapsto\sigma_x$ continuous. For a finite set $F\subseteq\mathbb Z^d$ and assignment $a\in S^F$, the cylinder set determined by $(F,a)$ is $[F,a]=\{\sigma\in\Omega:\sigma|_F=a\}$. The cylinder sigma-algebra $\mathcal C$ is generated by these sets.

For each finite $F$, let $\pi_F:\Omega\to S^F$ be restriction to $F$. A probability measure $\rho$ on $(\Omega,\mathcal C)$ is a countably additive map $\mathcal C\to[0,1]$ with $\rho(\varnothing)=0$ and $\rho(\Omega)=1$. Its finite-coordinate pushforward is the measure on all subsets of $S^F$ defined by $(\pi_F)_*\rho(B):=\rho(\pi_F^{-1}(B))$; the preimage is a finite union of cylinders and belongs to $\mathcal C$. For probability measures $\mu_n,\mu$ on this space, local weak convergence $\mu_n\to_{\mathrm{loc}}\mu$ means that $(\pi_F)_*\mu_n(B)\to(\pi_F)_*\mu(B)$ for every finite $F\subseteq\mathbb Z^d$ and every $B\subseteq S^F$. Equivalently, all finite-coordinate cylinder probabilities converge. The cylinder sigma-algebra is shown below in a local result to equal the Borel sigma-algebra, with local weak convergence equal to ordinary weak convergence on this space.

## Remarks

This finite-spin product topology and cylinder convergence are distinct from the vague topology on locally finite counting measures. Vague convergence tests compactly supported spatial functions and may lose mass at infinity; it does not by itself imply weak convergence of probability measures.
