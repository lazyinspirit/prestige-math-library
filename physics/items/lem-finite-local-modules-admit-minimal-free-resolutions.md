## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Every finite module $M$ over a nonzero Noetherian local ring $(R,\mathfrak m,k)$ has an augmented resolution $\cdots\to F_1\to F_0\to M\to0$ by finite-rank free modules, with $d_i(F_i)\subseteq\mathfrak mF_{i-1}$ for $i>0$. Such a resolution is called minimal; it need not be bounded. This extends the bounded terminology without changing it.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The objects and hypotheses in the statement. The Axiom of Choice ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]) supplies dependent choice ([[def-dependent-choice]]) for the infinite sequence of successive finite generator/lift selections, and the choice assumptions of the cited Nakayama inputs.

[F1] [[def-minimal-free-resolution-over-a-local-ring]]: A finite free resolution $F_\bullet\to N$ over local $(R,\mathfrak m)$ is minimal when $d_i(F_i)\subseteq\mathfrak mF_{i-1}$ for every $i>0$.

[F2] [[cor-minimal-generators-over-a-local-ring]]: Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $(R,\mathfrak m)$ be a local ring with residue field $k=R/\mathfrak m$, and let $M$ be a finitely generated left $R$-module. A finite generating set $x_1,\dots,x_r$ of $M$ is minimal if and only if the images of $x_1,\dots,x_r$ in $M/\mathfrak mM$ form a $k$-basis. In particular every minimal generating set of $M$ has the same cardinality.

[F3] [[thm-nakayama-lemma]]: Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $R$ be a commutative ring, let $I \trianglelefteq R$ satisfy $I \subseteq J(R)$, and let $M$ be a finitely generated left $R$-module. If $IM=M$, then $M=0$.

[F4] [[thm-equivalent-characterizations-of-noetherian-modules]]: For a left $R$-module $M$, write (1) every submodule is finitely generated, (2) ACC, and (3) every nonempty set of submodules has a maximal member. The implications $1\Rightarrow2$ and $3\Rightarrow1$ are choice-free; $2\Rightarrow3$ assumes DC, under which the three conditions are equivalent. See def-noetherian-module.

## Proof

**Proof technique:** Explicit algebraic derivation.

1.1 Choose a basis of $M/\mathfrak mM$ and lift it to $x_1,\ldots,x_r\in M$. If $N=\sum_iRx_i$, then $M=N+\mathfrak mM$, so the finite module $M/N$ satisfies $\mathfrak m(M/N)=M/N$. Nakayama gives $M=N$. Thus the corresponding map $F_0=R^r\to M$ is onto. A relation among the $x_i$ has all coefficients in $\mathfrak m$, because their residue classes are independent. Hence $K_0=\ker(F_0\to M)\subseteq\mathfrak mF_0$. [F2, F3, algebra]

2.1 Each finite-rank free module is finitely generated, hence Noetherian by [[thm-finitely-generated-modules-over-noetherian-rings-are-noetherian]]. Its kernel submodule is therefore finitely generated by the Noetherian condition in [F4]. Repeating step 1.1 on $K_0$ and on each successive kernel gives an exact augmented complex: the next map surjects onto precisely the preceding kernel, then includes it in the preceding free module. Its image lies in the required maximal-ideal multiple by step 1.1 at that stage. DC, supplied by AC, makes these successive finite generator/lift selections into one infinite sequence; the Nakayama inputs also use the assumed AC. If a kernel is zero, choose zero modules thereafter, making the selection relation serial; for $M=0$ use the zero complex. The bounded case agrees with the prior definition. [F4, F3, F1, step 1.1, given, choose] ∎
