## Definition

Let $(\mathcal A,U,\mathcal H,\Omega)$ be a conformal net with the interval, Hilbert-space, commutant, and projective covariance conventions fixed earlier on this page. Write $L_0\geq0$ for the rotation generator, $H^\infty=\bigcap_{k\geq0}D((1+L_0)^k)$ for its smooth finite-energy domain, and $H_{\mathrm{fin}}$ for the algebraic span of all eigenspaces of $L_0$. A vector $a\in\mathcal H$ is **quasi-primary of weight $d$** when $L_0a=da$, $d\in\mathbb Z_{\geq0}$, and $L_1a=0$. The field data include an antiunitary involution $\theta$ with $\theta^2=1$ and $\theta\Omega=\Omega$; it is the stated anti-linear involution used in the theorem.

For an interval $I$, the vacuum is required to be cyclic and separating for $\mathcal A(I)$: cyclic means $\overline{\mathcal A(I)\Omega}=\mathcal H$, and separating means $X\Omega=0$ for $X\in\mathcal A(I)$ implies $X=0$. The Tomita operator $S_I$ is the closure of the antilinear map $\mathcal A(I)\Omega\to\mathcal H$, $X\Omega\mapsto X^*\Omega$, and $\mathcal A(I)'\Omega=\{X\Omega:X\in\mathcal A(I)'\}$ is the dense commutant-vacuum domain. For a quasi-primary $a$ of weight $d$ and $f\in C^\infty_c(I)$, with $\widehat f_m=\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}f(e^{i\vartheta})e^{-im\vartheta}\,d\vartheta/(2\pi)$, define the Fredenhagen–Jörß vector
$$a(f)=\sum_{n\geq0}\widehat f_{-n-d}\frac{L_{-1}^n a}{n!}.$$
When $a(f)\in D(S_I)$ and $S_Ia(f)=(-1)^d(\theta a)(\bar f)$, the densely defined operator $T^0_{I,a,f}$ on $\mathcal A(I)'\Omega$ given by $T^0_{I,a,f}(X\Omega)=Xa(f)$ is closable; its closure is the Fredenhagen–Jörß smeared field $Y_I(a,f)$. The local gluing lemma below uses only the linearity and interval-compatibility properties explicitly recorded at the end.

For a closed densely defined operator $T$, say $T$ is **affiliated** with a von Neumann algebra $M$ when every unitary $u\in M'$ maps $D(T)$ onto itself and $uT\xi=Tu\xi$ for every $\xi\in D(T)$. The supplied polar decomposition gives $T=V_T|T|$, where $V_T$ is a bounded partial isometry and $|T|=(T^*T)^{1/2}$ is positive self-adjoint with $D(|T|)=D(T)$. Let $E_{|T|}(B)$ be the spectral projection of $|T|$ for a Borel set $B\subseteq[0,\infty)$. For a family $\mathcal T$ of such operators, define $W^*(\mathcal T)=W^*(\{V_T:T\in\mathcal T\}\cup\{E_{|T|}(B):T\in\mathcal T,\ B\subseteq[0,\infty)\text{ Borel}\})$, where the outer $W^*$ is the weak-operator closure of the bounded unital $*$-algebra generated by this set. Every $T\in\mathcal T$ is affiliated with this algebra: a unitary in its commutant commutes with $V_T$ and all spectral projections of $|T|$, hence preserves $D(|T|)$ and commutes with $|T|$ by the spectral calculus, and therefore preserves $D(T)$ and commutes with $T=V_T|T|$. This is the convention for generating local algebras from closed, possibly unbounded, fields. A family $F\subset H_{\mathrm{fin}}$ is a **generating family of quasi-primary vectors** when every $a\in F$ is quasi-primary and, for every $I\in\mathcal I$,
$$
\mathcal A(I)=W^*\!\left(\{Y_{I_1}(a,f): I_1\in\mathcal I,\ I_1\subset I,\ a\in F,\ f\in C^\infty_c(I_1)\}\right).
$$
The family is PCT-invariant when $\theta F=F$. Its Fredenhagen–Jörß fields satisfy **polynomial energy bounds** when for each $a\in F$ there are $M_a>0$ and integers $s_a,k_a\geq0$ such that
$$\|Y_I(a,f)b\|\leq M_a\|f\|_{s_a}\|(1+L_0)^{k_a}b\|,\qquad \|f\|_s=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}(1+|n|)^s|\widehat f_n|,$$
for every interval $I$, $f\in C^\infty_c(I)$, and $b\in \mathcal A(I)'\Omega\cap H^\infty$. The finite-dimensional-energy condition means every eigenspace $\ker(L_0-\lambda)$ is finite-dimensional for each eigenvalue $\lambda\geq0$. All these conditions are data or hypotheses; no statement that every conformal net admits such fields is made.

The fields are linear in test functions on the common invariant core $H^\infty$ and satisfy interval compatibility: if $I_1\subset I_2$ and $\operatorname{supp}f\subset I_1$, then $Y_{I_2}(a,f)b=Y_{I_1}(a,f)b$ for every $b\in H^\infty$. These are the exact properties used by the local gluing lemma below.
