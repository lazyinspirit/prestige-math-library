## Definition

Use units $\hbar=c=1$. For target dimension $D\ge3$, a fixed target momentum is a contravariant vector $p=(p^\mu)\in\mathbb R^{1,D-1}$, with $p_\mu=\eta_{\mu\nu}p^\nu$ and dimension $L^{-1}$. The parameter $\alpha'>0$ has dimension $L^2$. Thus the zero modes and oscillator modes below are dimensionless.

For fixed $p$, take the algebraic open-string matter Fock space generated by $\alpha^\mu_{-n}$, $n>0$, from $|0;p\rangle$, with $[\alpha_m^\mu,\alpha_n^\nu]=m\eta^{\mu\nu}\delta_{m+n,0}$, adjoint $(\alpha_n^\mu)^\dagger=\alpha_{-n}^\mu$, and normal ordering relative to $\alpha_n|0;p\rangle=0$ for $n>0$. Set $\alpha_0^\mu=\sqrt{2\alpha'}\,p^\mu$ for open strings. For closed strings use two independent commuting left/right oscillator families: $[\alpha_m^\mu,\alpha_n^\nu]=m\eta^{\mu\nu}\delta_{m+n,0}$, $[\widetilde\alpha_m^\mu,\widetilde\alpha_n^\nu]=m\eta^{\mu\nu}\delta_{m+n,0}$, $[\alpha_m^\mu,\widetilde\alpha_n^\nu]=0$, $(\widetilde\alpha_n^\mu)^\dagger=\widetilde\alpha_{-n}^\mu$, and $\alpha_0^\mu=\widetilde\alpha_0^\mu=\sqrt{\alpha'/2}\,p^\mu$. On the finite-particle domain define $L_n^m=\frac12\sum_k :\alpha_{n-k}\cdot\alpha_k:$ and the independent tilde copy for closed strings; the dot is contraction by $\eta$, and each sum acts finitely on each state.

Define the weighted matter levels on the algebraic Fock domains by $N_{\rm lev}=\sum_{n>0}\alpha_{-n}\cdot\alpha_n$ and, for closed strings, $\widetilde N_{\rm lev}=\sum_{n>0}\widetilde\alpha_{-n}\cdot\widetilde\alpha_n$. These sums act finitely on every finite-particle vector. The oscillator commutators give $[N_{\rm lev},\alpha_{-n}^\mu]=n\alpha_{-n}^\mu$ and $N_{\rm lev}|0;p\rangle=0$, so a monomial $\alpha_{-n_1}^{\mu_1}\cdots\alpha_{-n_r}^{\mu_r}|0;p\rangle$ has level $n_1+\cdots+n_r$; the right-moving closed level is defined in the same way. The per-mode occupation number is $n^{-1}\alpha_{-n}\cdot\alpha_n$ (and its tilde copy); its unweighted sum counts quanta and is not the level. In a transverse sector, the dot in these level formulas is the Euclidean contraction over transverse indices.

## Remarks

[[def-st-flat-bosonic-string-model]] fixes the target as flat Minkowski space with signature $(-,+,\ldots,+)$ and excludes background fields and interactions. [[def-st-string-tension-alpha-prime]] fixes $\alpha'>0$, $[\alpha']=L^2$, and $T=(2\pi\alpha')^{-1}$. [[pthm-st-canonical-phase-space-constraints]] gives only the classical constraint normalization used as formulation context. The oscillator commutators, Fock representation, adjoints, and normal ordering above are adopted algebraic quantization data; they are not consequences of that classical theorem. Open and closed zero-mode factors are kept distinct throughout.
