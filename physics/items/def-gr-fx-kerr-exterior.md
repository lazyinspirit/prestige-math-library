## Definition

Assume $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$ for the tangent-bundle smooth structure of the
Lorentzian conventions.

Let $M>0$ and $a\in\mathbb R$ satisfy $|a|<M$, and put
$r_\pm:=M\pm\sqrt{M^2-a^2}$.

Let $\mathcal M_{\mathrm K}:=\mathbb R\times(r_+,\infty)\times S^2$ carry the
product smooth structure, where $S^2=\{(x,y,z)\in\mathbb R^3:x^2+y^2+z^2=1\}$
has its usual smooth structure and round metric $\gamma_{S^2}$. Let
$\pi:\mathcal M_{\mathrm K}\to S^2$ be the third projection, let $\xi$ be the
smooth rotation field on $S^2$ generated by $(x,y,z)\mapsto(-y,x,0)$, and put
$\gamma:=\pi^*\gamma_{S^2}$ and
$\xi^\flat:=\pi^*(\gamma_{S^2}(\xi,\cdot))$. Let $t,r$ be the first and
second coordinate functions on $\mathcal M_{\mathrm K}$, and let
$\zeta:=z\circ\pi$ be the third Euclidean coordinate on the sphere factor.
Define the global smooth functions

$$\Sigma:=r^2+a^2\zeta^2,\qquad \Delta:=r^2-2Mr+a^2.$$

On the axis-avoiding polar coordinate domain, let $0<\theta<\pi$ and regard
$\phi$ as the circle-valued azimuth in $\mathbb R/(2\pi\mathbb Z)$. Each
choice of an open real interval of length less than $2\pi$ lifting $\phi$
gives an ordinary local chart. In any such chart,
$\zeta=\cos\theta$, $\Sigma=r^2+a^2\cos^2\theta$,
$\gamma=d\theta^2+\sin^2\theta\,d\phi^2$, and
$\xi^\flat=\sin^2\theta\,d\phi$.

Define the symmetric covariant $2$-tensor $g$ globally on $\mathcal M_{\mathrm K}$
by

$$g=-\left(1-\frac{2Mr}{\Sigma}\right)dt\otimes dt+\frac{\Sigma}{\Delta}\,dr\otimes dr+\Sigma\gamma+\frac{a^2(\Sigma+2Mr)}{\Sigma}\,\xi^\flat\otimes\xi^\flat-\frac{2aMr}{\Sigma}\left(dt\otimes\xi^\flat+\xi^\flat\otimes dt\right).$$

On any such local polar chart, its Boyer-Lindquist line element is

$$ds^2=-\left(1-\frac{2Mr}{\Sigma}\right)dt^2-\frac{4Mar\sin^2\theta}{\Sigma}\,dt\,d\phi+\frac{\Sigma}{\Delta}\,dr^2+\Sigma\,d\theta^2+\frac{(r^2+a^2)^2-a^2\Delta\sin^2\theta}{\Sigma}\sin^2\theta\,d\phi^2.$$

The displayed cross term is $2g_{t\phi}\,dt\,d\phi$. This definition makes
no nondegeneracy or signature claim; Lorentzian signature and a time orientation
are established separately.

## Remarks

The Boyer-Lindquist chart excludes the rotation axis, while the global tensor
definition uses the smooth round metric and the smooth rotation field on $S^2$.
The domain has $r>r_+$; the hypersurface $r=r_+$ is not part of this definition
and is included only after the regular ingoing extension is constructed.

The Lorentzian signature and time-orientation properties are proved by
[[lem-gr-fx-kerr-exterior-properties]], not assumed by this tensor definition.

The smooth tangent-bundle convention assumes only countable choice
$\mathrm{AC}_\omega$; no stronger choice principle is used.

The parameters correspond to $M=G_Nm_{\mathrm{phys}}/c^2$ and
$a=J_{\mathrm{phys}}/(m_{\mathrm{phys}}c)$ when a physical interpretation is
adopted. No claim that this datum describes a measured rotating object is made.
