## Statement

Assume AC. Let $\pi:T→Y$ be a finite étale torsor under a finite abelian group H between smooth projective complex varieties, with actual character lines Lχ in $\pi_*O_T$. If a locally finite support-property Bridgeland condition (P,Z) on Db(Y) is invariant under tensoring by every Lχ, then $P_T(\phi)=\{E:\pi_*E\in P(\phi)\}, Z_T=Z \pi_*$ define such a condition on Db(T), with coimage support lattice. For a smooth connected projective complex surface S and $n\ge 2$, every finite-order line on $S^{[n]}$ is the determinant norm Nn(M) of a finite-order line M on S. More generally, if S is a finite disjoint union of smooth connected projective complex surfaces $S_a$, then $S^{[n]}$ is the disjoint union, over all nonnegative tuples $(n_a)$ with sum n, of the products of $S_a^{[n_a]}$. On each such component every finite-order line is an external product of norms $N_{n_a}(M_{a,(n_b)})$ of finite-order lines on the factors $S_a$, omitting $n_a=0$ and using $N_1(M)=M$. These factor lines may depend on the component tuple. A single global norm $N_n(M)$ exists exactly when one can choose a tuple of finite-order lines $M_a$ on $S_a$, independent of $(n_b)$, realizing all these componentwise factors. One has $Nn(M)=\rho^*M^{n}$ and $p^*Nn(M)=q^*(M^{\boxtimes n})$ on the actual isospectral Hilbert correspondence. Its Fourier–Mukai kernel therefore intertwines those tensor functors, whether or not equivalence has yet been proved. The line norm is det $p_*(O_U \otimes q^*M)$ $\otimes(det p_*O_U)^{-1}$ for the universal length-n family U.
