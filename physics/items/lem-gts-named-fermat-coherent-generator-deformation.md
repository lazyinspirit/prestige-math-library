## Statement

Assume AC. Let $N\in\{4,5\}$, $R=\mathbb C[\![q]\!]$, $Y_q=\{y_1\cdots y_N+q\sum_i y_i^N=0\}\subset\mathbb P_R^{N-1}$ and $\Gamma=\widetilde\Gamma/\mu_N$. Put $A_p=\Omega^p_{\mathbb P_R^{N-1}}(p)$ and $s_j=\lfloor2j/N\rfloor$ for $1\le j\le N$. The restricted bundles $A_{N-j}[N-j+s_j]$ with every character twist form an actual generator, with $N^{N-1}$ objects, of the perfect quotient-stack category. Their Čech dg algebra has a bounded finite-free minimal replacement with the source exterior/trivial-extension binary product, nonzero central primary class $w$, and nonzero even-coordinate invariant first-order Fermat class. Its actual scalar extension computes the generic quotient-stack coherent category over $\Lambda_{\mathbb Q}$. For $N=4$ the quotient has exactly six $A_3$ singularities, and its explicit projective minimal crepant resolution has the same derived category by the declared algebraic surface-derived-McKay supplier. For $N=5$ the quotient stack itself is the source target.
