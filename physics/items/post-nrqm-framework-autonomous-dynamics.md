## Postulate

Adopt as physical assumptions of the framework the following statement of isolated autonomous dynamics. Assume the Axiom of Countable Choice and the Axiom of Choice ([[def-countable-choice]], [[def-axiom-of-choice]]), which are the choice hypotheses of the cited spectral and group suppliers.

**(i) Energy operator.** An **isolated autonomous system** of the sector has a declared **energy operator** $H_{\mathrm{op}}:D(H_{\mathrm{op}})\subset H\to H$ that is self-adjoint ([[def-adjoint-of-a-densely-defined-unbounded-operator]], [[def-symmetric-self-adjoint-and-essentially-self-adjoint]]) and a declared energy unit in which its spectral coordinate is read. The energy operator is part of the model specification of the system; for a massive particle it is fixed by the kinematics postulate P5, and singular or unbounded model Hamiltonians require their own self-adjointness statements.

**(ii) Evolution.** The time evolution of the model is the strongly continuous one-parameter unitary group
$$U(t):=\exp\!\Bigl(-\frac{i}{\hbar}tH_{\mathrm{op}}\Bigr)\qquad(t\in\mathbb R),\qquad U(t+s)=U(t)U(s),\quad U(t)^{*}=U(t)^{-1},$$
defined by the spectral calculus of the self-adjoint generator ([[thm-spectral-theorem-for-unbounded-self-adjoint-operators]], [[def-unbounded-integral-against-a-pvm]], [[lem-self-adjoint-operator-generates-a-strongly-continuous-unitary-group]], [[def-strongly-continuous-one-parameter-unitary-group]]). The energy unit enters the argument of the exponential so that $H_{\mathrm{op}}t/\hbar$ acts in the dimensionless spectral variable.

**(iii) State evolution.** A vector state evolves continuously, $\psi(t):=U(t)\psi(0)$ for every $\psi(0)\in H$, and the Schroedinger equation
$$i\hbar\,\frac{d}{dt}\psi(t)=H_{\mathrm{op}}\psi(t)$$
holds in the norm-differentiable sense exactly for initial vectors $\psi(0)\in D(H_{\mathrm{op}})$, where the derivative is the norm limit of the difference quotients; for other vectors the equation is not asserted, although the evolution remains continuous. A density-operator preparation evolves as $\rho(t)=U(t)\rho(0)U(t)^{*}$, which is a density operator for every $t$ and satisfies $\operatorname{tr}\rho(t)=1$ ([[lem-nrqm-composites-density-and-scalar-spectral-probability]]).

**(iv) Time-dependent families.** A prescribed time-dependent family $t\mapsto H(t)$ of self-adjoint operators is a different model assumption: a norm-continuous finite-dimensional Hermitian family is used only through the unitary two-time propagator that its family defines, and the exponential of the integral of $H(t)$ is not identified with that propagator unless the family commutes with itself at different times. The general unbounded time-dependent case requires a separate existence and domain theorem of its own and is not adopted by this postulate; the driven-dynamics page states its own assumptions for the models it treats.

**(v) Open systems.** A non-unitary evolution of the reduced description of a system coupled to an environment is an extension obtained from an explicitly chosen environment model and a declared reduction, not a replacement for clause (ii); no environment model is adopted by this postulate.

**(vi) Status.** Clauses (i)-(v) are adopted assumptions of the isolated-system idealization. They assert nothing about the origin of the energy operator of a concrete system, about the accuracy of the idealization for a laboratory preparation, or about which laboratory interactions realize an isolated evolution; those are tested identifications in the sense of P7.

## Status and scope

- **Domain matters.** The Schroedinger equation is a statement about vectors in $D(H_{\mathrm{op}})$; for a vector outside the domain only the unitary group acts, and the expression $H_{\mathrm{op}}\psi$ is undefined. Statements about the derivative of expected values require the common-domain hypotheses stated with them.
- **Autonomous only.** An isolated system evolves autonomously; driven evolution is an additional model assumption of the pages that treat it.
- **Units.** The energy unit is declared with the observable; energies and times enter the phase $H_{\mathrm{op}}t/\hbar$ so that the combination is dimensionless in the declared units.
