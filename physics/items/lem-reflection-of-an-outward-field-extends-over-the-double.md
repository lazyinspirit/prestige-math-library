## Statement

Assume countable choice $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$
([[def-countable-choice]]). Let $M$ be a compact smooth $n$-manifold with
boundary, $n\ge1$, and let $X$ be a smooth vector field that is nonzero and points
strictly outward along $\partial M$
([[def-inward-outward-and-boundary-tangent-vectors]]). Choose the collar generated by the inward field $-X$. Let $DM$ be the
smooth double defined by this collar, with seam involution $\tau$
interchanging the two labelled halves
([[def-double-of-a-smooth-manifold-with-boundary]],
[[thm-the-double-has-a-well-defined-smooth-structure]]). Then
$$X^+(y)=X(y)\ \ (y\in M_+),\qquad X^+(\tau y)=-d\tau_y\bigl(X(y)\bigr)\ \ (y\in M_+)$$
defines a smooth vector field on this double (an arbitrary fixed collar
need not give a smooth field); its zeros are exactly the two copies of
the zeros of $X$, and for every isolated zero $p\in\operatorname{int}M$
$$\operatorname{ind}_{\tau p}(X^+)=(-1)^n\operatorname{ind}_pX.$$

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A compact smooth $n$-manifold $M$ with boundary, a smooth field $X$ nonzero and strictly outward along $\partial M$, and the labelled double $(DM,\tau)$.

[F1] $DM$ is the quotient of $M_+\sqcup M_-$ identifying the two copies of $\partial M$, with $\tau$ the involution interchanging the labelled halves; smooth collar data near the seam give it the structure of a smooth boundaryless manifold, and two collar choices give structures related by a diffeomorphism fixing the seam pointwise and preserving the halves ([[def-double-of-a-smooth-manifold-with-boundary]], [[thm-the-double-has-a-well-defined-smooth-structure]]).



[F3] Because $X$ is strictly outward and nonzero on the compact boundary, there is $\delta>0$ such that $X$ has no zero in the $\delta$-neighbourhood of $\partial M$; the zeros of $X$ therefore lie in the interior at positive distance from $\partial M$ ([[def-inward-outward-and-boundary-tangent-vectors]]).

[F4] The inward field $-X$ has smooth local forward semiflows at boundary points, using smooth coordinate extensions across the face ([[thm-inward-pointing-vector-fields-have-local-forward-semiflows-at-the-boundary]]). Their differentials at time zero are invertible because $-X$ is transverse to the boundary. Compactness supplies a uniform short time. Uniqueness and strict inwardness make the map $c(x,u)=\Phi^{\,-X}_u(x)$ injective: a trajectory cannot return to the boundary, since its boundary defining coordinate has positive derivative at any putative return. Thus $c$ is a global collar for short time, and $X=-\partial_u$ in its coordinates.

[F5] The index is independent of the chart and of a trivialization with matching base and fibre orientations, and negation scales it by $(-1)^n$ ([[lem-vector-field-index-is-independent-of-chart-ball-and-trivialization]], [[lem-negation-scales-the-local-index-by-minus-one-to-the-dimension]]).

## Proof

1.1 If $\partial M=\varnothing$, the double is the disjoint union of two copies, and the two fields are $X$ and $-X$. Otherwise use the single global flow collar $c(x,u)$ of [F4] to define the double's smooth structure. Its seam charts have signed coordinate $u$, with $\tau(x,u)=(x,-u)$. This is the collar-defined double of [F1], rather than a replacement of an already fixed smooth structure while keeping $X$ unchanged. [F1, F4, given, construct]

2.1 In these charts $X=-\partial_u$ on the first half. Reflection followed by negation gives $-d\tau(-\partial_u)=-\partial_u$ on the second half. The prescriptions therefore agree at the seam and give one smooth nonzero expression there. Off the seam they are $X$ and the push-forward of $-X$, so they are smooth globally. [F1, F4, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 There are no seam zeros, and off the seam the zero set consists precisely of the two copies of $X^{-1}(0)$. For any isolated zero $p$, a chart at $p$ transported by $\tau$ identifies the second field with $-X$. Chart invariance and [F5] give $\operatorname{ind}_{\tau p}X^+=\operatorname{ind}_p(-X)=(-1)^n\operatorname{ind}_pX$. The same computation applies to the two disjoint copies when the boundary is empty. [F3, F5, step 1.1, step 2.1, algebra] ∎
