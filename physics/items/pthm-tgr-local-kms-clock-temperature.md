## Statement

Fix one cell $i$ of the finite preparation in [[post-tgr-selected-gibbs-kms-preparation]], at its assigned event $p_i\in O$, with $N_i=N_\xi(p_i)$, $T_i=T(p_i)$, and $\mu_i=\mu(p_i)$. Set $\beta_K:=(k_BT_K)^{-1}>0$ in joules$^{-1}$. Let $\mathcal A_i=\mathcal B(\mathcal H_i)$, let $G_i:=H_i-\mu_iQ_i$ be its proper grand generator in joules, let $G_{K,i}:=N_iG_i$ be its Killing-time grand generator, and let $\omega_i(X):=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_iX)$. Use length coordinates $x^0=ct$, with $c>0$ the speed of light in metres per second and the reference-normalized $\xi$ dimensionless in these coordinates. Choose an open interval $I_{K,i}\subseteq\mathbb R$ containing $0$ on which the integral curve through $p_i$ remains in $O$. Define the unique smooth curve $\gamma_i:I_{K,i}\to O$ by
$$\gamma_i(0)=p_i,\qquad \frac{d\gamma_i}{dt_K}(t_K)=c\,\xi_{\gamma_i(t_K)},$$
where $t_K$ is physical Killing time in seconds; the equation states its parameterization by the reference-normalized Killing field. This curve is future-directed timelike. The stationary normalization used in [[thm-tgr-kms-orbit-time-rescaling]] gives $\xi(N_\xi)=0$, so $N_\xi(\gamma_i(t_K))=N_i$ throughout this interval. Its proper-time parametrization is
$$\Gamma_i:N_iI_{K,i}\to O,\qquad \Gamma_i(\tau):=\gamma_i(\tau/N_i),$$
which satisfies $g(d\Gamma_i/d\tau,d\Gamma_i/d\tau)=-c^2$. Thus $\tau$ is proper time in seconds and $\tau=N_it_K$ on the geometric orbit. No completeness of $\xi$ is assumed: the geometric curve is defined on its flow interval, while the finite-matrix dynamics below are defined for all real parameters by their generators and use the same constant rescaling.

Define

$$\alpha^{K,i}_{t_K}(X):=e^{it_KG_{K,i}/\hbar}Xe^{-it_KG_{K,i}/\hbar},\qquad \alpha^{\mathrm{loc},i}_{\tau}(X):=e^{i\tau G_i/\hbar}Xe^{-i\tau G_i/\hbar},$$

where both $t_K$ and $\tau$ are measured in seconds, and $N_i>0$ is dimensionless. Write $\mathcal A_i^0:=\{X\in\mathcal A_i:[X,Q_i]=0\}$ for the neutral observable algebra. Then $\omega_i$ is $\beta_K$-KMS for $\alpha^{K,i}$ and is $\beta_{\mathrm{loc},i}$-KMS for $\alpha^{\mathrm{loc},i}$, where

$$\beta_{\mathrm{loc},i}=N_i\beta_K=\frac{1}{k_BT_i},\qquad \hbar\beta_{\mathrm{loc},i}\text{ is the strip width in seconds}.$$

On $\mathcal A_i^0$, the same proper-time automorphism is generated by the physical Hamiltonian $H_i$, because its chemical-potential term commutes with every observable in that subalgebra. Thus the local temperature is $T_i=T_K/N_i$ for this constructed state and selected orbit. The conclusion is only orbitwise; it does not make a position-dependent lapse into one global time-rescaled dynamics.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The selected stationary bath and one actual finite-cell Gibbs/KMS preparation, with $T_K>0$, $N_i>0$, and $\hbar>0$ in joule-seconds.

[A1] [[post-tgr-stationary-equilibrium]] adopts one connected stationary bath with smooth future timelike Killing field $\xi$, lapse $N_\xi$, observer $u=\xi/N_\xi$, positive proper-temperature field $T$, and Killing inverse-temperature vector $\beta=u/(k_BT)$. It does not assert a KMS state.

[F1] [[post-tgr-selected-gibbs-kms-preparation]] adopts the finite density $\rho=\bigotimes_j\rho_j$ and its Killing-time grand-generator dynamics; the mathematical construction is conditional on $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$ through the cited finite-cell supplier. That postulate makes no generic curved-QFT state claim.

[F2] [[def-qsm-kms-state]]: a $\beta$-KMS state has, for every observable pair, a bounded function continuous on the closed strip of width $\beta\hbar$, holomorphic inside, and with boundary values $\omega(A\alpha_t(B))$ and $\omega(\alpha_t(B)A)$.

[F3] [[thm-tgr-kms-orbit-time-rescaling]]: if $\omega$ is $\beta_K$-KMS for $\alpha$, then for fixed dimensionless $\lambda>0$, $\omega$ is $\lambda\beta_K$-KMS for $\alpha^\lambda_\tau=\alpha_{\tau/\lambda}$. On one stationary orbit with $\tau=N t_K$, it gives $\beta_{\mathrm{loc}}=N\beta_K$ and $T_{\mathrm{loc}}=T_K/N$; a spatially varying lapse is not one global rescaling.

[F4] [[def-countable-choice]]: $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$ means every countable family of nonempty sets has a choice function. It is carried from the finite-cell construction; no stronger choice principle is used.

## Proof

1.1 By [F1], the full product state is $\beta_K$-KMS for the grand-generator dynamics. The embedded cell algebra $\mathcal A_i$ is invariant under that dynamics: all lifted cell generators commute, and conjugation by the other tensor factors cancels, leaving $\alpha^{K,i}_{t_K}$. Restricting the full-state strip functions to observables in this invariant subalgebra gives the KMS boundary values for $\omega_i$, since the product density restricts to $\rho_i$. Thus $\omega_i$ is $\beta_K$-KMS for $\alpha^{K,i}$. [given, A1, F1, F2, algebra]

2.1 Since $G_{K,i}=N_iG_i$ and $N_i$ is constant along the selected orbit, $\alpha^{\mathrm{loc},i}_{\tau}=\alpha^{K,i}_{\tau/N_i}$. Apply [F3] with $\lambda=N_i$ to obtain the $N_i\beta_K$-KMS strip and width $\hbar N_i\beta_K$. The stationary normalization in [F3] gives $T_i=T_K/N_i$, so $N_i\beta_K=N_i/(k_BT_K)=1/(k_BT_i)$. If $X\in\mathcal A_i^0$, then conjugation by $e^{i\tau\mu_iQ_i/\hbar}$ fixes $X$, so the local grand and physical $H_i$ automorphisms agree there. [F1, F2, F3, F4, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
