## Definition

Use a Virasoro representation generated by a highest-weight vector $v_h$ with $L_nv_h=0$ for every $n>0$ and $L_0v_h=hv_h$, as in the cited highest-weight module definition. For Liouville, $c_L=1+6Q^2$ and $h=\Delta_\alpha=\frac{\alpha}{2}(Q-\frac{\alpha}{2})$ for a charge $\alpha\in\mathbb C$. Fix four external charge parameters $\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3,\alpha_4\in\mathbb C$ and write $\Delta_{\alpha_j}=\frac{\alpha_j}{2}(Q-\frac{\alpha_j}{2})$; this formal block definition imposes no Seiberg or reality conditions on these external parameters. A **partition** of $n\in\mathbb N$ is a finite nonincreasing sequence $\nu=(\nu_1,\ldots,\nu_k)$ of positive integers with $|\nu|=\sum_j\nu_j=n$; set $T_0=\{\varnothing\}$ and let $T_n$ denote the partitions of $n$ for $n>0$. For the block data below, require that the descendant words indexed by $T_n$ form a basis at each level, and choose the real form generated over $\mathbb R$ by $v_h$ and the Virasoro modes when $c_L,h\in\mathbb R$; the complex module is its complexification. Write $L_{-\nu}=L_{-\nu_k}\cdots L_{-\nu_1}$ and $L_{-\varnothing}=I$.

The Young diagram for $\nu=(3,1)$ uses the English convention: its first and second rows have respectively $3$ and $1$ left-aligned unit boxes. The boxes record the parts of the partition only; the order of the corresponding Virasoro operators is fixed separately by $L_{-\nu}$ above.

For a highest-weight vector $v_h$, use the real-valued contravariant bilinear form normalized by $\langle v_h,v_h\rangle=1$ and $\langle L_{-m}u,v\rangle=\langle u,L_mv\rangle$ for $m>0$, then extend it complex-bilinearly. At level $n$, let $F_{c,h}^{(n)}(\nu,\nu')=\langle L_{-\nu}v_h,L_{-\nu'}v_h\rangle$ for $\nu,\nu'\in T_n$. For real $c,h$ every matrix entry is real because it is the value of the form on vectors in the chosen real module; therefore an inverse, when it exists, is real as well. Only when this finite matrix is invertible is $F_{c,h}^{(n)}{}^{-1}$ used. This definition does not assert nondegeneracy at every weight.

For three weights $\Delta,\Delta',\Delta''$ and a partition $\nu=(\nu_1,\ldots,\nu_k)$, put

$$v(\Delta,\Delta',\Delta'',\nu)=\prod_{j=1}^k\left(\nu_j\Delta'-\Delta+\Delta''+\sum_{u<j}\nu_u\right).$$

For $P>0$ such that every required internal Gram matrix at $c=c_L$ and $h=\Delta_{Q+iP}$ is invertible, define the formal spherical block

$$F_P(z)=\sum_{n=0}^\infty\beta_n(P)z^n,\qquad \beta_n(P)=\sum_{\nu,\nu'\in T_n}v(\Delta_{\alpha_1},\Delta_{\alpha_2},\Delta_{Q+iP},\nu)\,[F_{c_L,\Delta_{Q+iP}}^{(n)}{}^{-1}]_{\nu,\nu'}\,v(\Delta_{\alpha_4},\Delta_{\alpha_3},\Delta_{Q+iP},\nu').$$

The series is initially formal. Analytic convergence on $|z|<1$, nondegeneracy of all matrices for $P>0$, and convergence of the spectral integral are theorem obligations, not consequences of this definition.
