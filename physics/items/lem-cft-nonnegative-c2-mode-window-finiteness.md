## Statement

Let V be a vertex operator algebra with finite-dimensional nonnegative integral state grades, vacuum, translation and conformal Jacobi axioms, and suppose V/C2(V) is finite dimensional. The space V0 is allowed to be larger than C1. Choose a finite homogeneous set A, together with the vacuum, representing V/C2(V), including its nonvacuum weight-zero representatives. If a weak module is generated by w and $a_m w=0$ for a in A and $m\ge N$, it is spanned, in every total operator degree d, by
 $a1_{(i1)}...al_{(il)}w,  i1<...<il<N$,
and these strings satisfy $d\ge l(l-1)/2-Nl$. Hence every degree span is finite dimensional and vanishes for sufficiently negative d; no L0 grading of the weak module is assumed.

For every $N\ge 0$, the vector quotient $V/O_N(V)$, with the usual higher-circle and T+L0 relations, is finite dimensional. The common zero-mode operators on $\Omega_N$ of all weak modules form a finite-dimensional unital associative algebra $Z_N$; every $\Omega_N$ is a module for $Z_N$ and o(omega)=L0. This image algebra suffices here; no identification with a higher-Zhu bimodule or reverse rationality criterion is asserted.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** Work over $\mathbb C$ in the non-super state-field convention. In this item the broader phrase “vertex operator algebra” means a complex vertex algebra with vacuum $\mathbf1\in V_0$ and conformal vector $\omega\in V_2$, with modes $Y(\omega,z)=\sum_{j\in\mathbb Z}L(j)z^{-j-2}$, central charge $c\in\mathbb C$, and $L(-1)=T$, where $[L(m),L(n)]=(m-n)L(m+n)+\frac{c}{12}(m^3-m)\delta_{m+n,0}\operatorname{id}_V$ for all $m,n\in\mathbb Z$, and for which $L(0)$ is diagonalizable and $V=\bigoplus_{r\geq0}V_r$ with each $V_r$ finite dimensional. No assumption $V_0=\mathbb C\mathbf1$ is made. Each weak module has the lower-truncated fields, vacuum, and full module Jacobi identity specified below, with no grading assumed.

[F1] [[def-vertex-algebra-state-field]] defines the state-field data $(V,\mathbf1,T,Y)$ by truncation, vacuum/creation, translation covariance, and locality. The conformal-vector data above adds the Virasoro action and the grading; for homogeneous $a\in V_r$, $\mathrm{wt}(a)=r$.

[F2] [[def-vertex-algebra-module]] gives the weak-module Jacobi identity. Its coefficient residues give, with generalized binomial coefficients for every integer $m$,
$$[a^M_{(m)},b^M_{(n)}]=\sum_{j\geq0}\binom mj(a_{(j)}b)^M_{(m+n-j)},\qquad (a_{(m)}b)^M_{(n)}=\sum_{t\geq0}(-1)^t\binom mt\left(a^M_{(m-t)}b^M_{(n+t)}-(-1)^m b^M_{(m+n-t)}a^M_{(t)}\right).$$
The commutator sum is finite by state truncation; each iterate sum is finite on a fixed vector by rightmost-mode truncation. Translation gives $(T^j a)^M_{(m)}=(-1)^jm(m-1)\cdots(m-j+1)a^M_{(m-j)}$, and conformal weight covariance gives $\mathrm{wt}(a_{(m)}b)=\mathrm{wt}(a)+\mathrm{wt}(b)-m-1$ whenever the product is nonzero.

[F3] [[def-c2-cofinite-vertex-algebra]] defines $C_2(V)=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb C}\{a_{(-2)}b:a,b\in V\}$; the hypothesis in the Statement is that the vector quotient $V/C_2(V)$ is finite dimensional.

[F4] [[lem-cft-higher-zhu-action-on-weak-omega-spaces]] proves the preservation, $O_N(V)$-annihilation, and $o(a*_N b)=o(a)o(b)$ identities in precisely this full nonnegative-grade scope for arbitrary weak modules. Its exact formulas are, for homogeneous $a\in V_r$,
$$a\circ_N b=\sum_{j=0}^{r+N}\binom{r+N}{j}a_{(j-2N-2)}b,\qquad a*_N b=\sum_{p=0}^{N}(-1)^p\binom{N+p}{N}\sum_{j=0}^{r+N}\binom{r+N}{j}a_{(j-N-p-1)}b,$$
$$D a=(T+L(0))a=Ta+ra,\qquad O_N(V)=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb C}\{a\circ_N b,\;Da:a,b\in V\}.$$
These are the “higher-circle” and $T+L(0)$ relations used in Step 5.1; no CFT-type or associative-quotient assertion is imported for this broader case.

## Proof


1.1 Fix the total operator degree d, and induct on total state weight $p\ge 0$, then on word length l. Replacing a homogeneous state by a representative plus C2-products lowers p in every iterate term, since $\operatorname{wt}(a_{-2} b)=\operatorname{wt}(a)+\operatorname{wt}(b)+1$ while the two replacement states have total wt(a)+wt(b). The replacement is finite on its fixed tail by the mode truncations in F1 and F2. A vacuum mode either vanishes or removes a letter, lowering length. Nonvacuum weight-zero representatives remain in A; they are NOT silently discarded. At $p=0$ there are no C2 replacement terms, since those would have positive weight, and the same length and tuple induction below applies. [given, F2, F3, algebra]

2.1 Sort adjacent indices by the commutator. Each commutator state has smaller total state weight by j+1, so the error is already reduced by the p induction. After sorting, a last index $\ge N$ kills the cyclic vector. For fixed p,l,d the surviving sorted tuples have fixed sum p-l-d and every index $\le N-1$, hence every index is also at least p-l-d-(l-1)(N-1). There are only finitely many tuples, with a finite lexicographic order. [F2, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 At a repeated adjacent index i use $(a_{-1} b)_{(2i+1)}=\sum_{t\ge 0}(a_{(-1-t)}b_{(2i+1+t)}+b_{(2i-t)}a_t)$. For $i<0$ the first sum contains exactly one desired repeated pair at $t=-i-1$; for $i\ge 0$ the second contains exactly one reversed pair at $t=i$. Commute that reversed pair, whose error has smaller p. Every other pair has unequal indices summing to 2i, so after sorting it has a smaller first index and a lexicographically smaller full tuple, or dies by the N cutoff. The collapsed state $a_{-1} b$ has the same p and shorter word length. Its C2 replacement lowers p as before. Induction on this finite tuple set, after the outer p and length inductions, therefore removes all equal indices with coefficient ONE. The argument applies at $p=0$ as well: commutator states of negative weight are zero, and the collapsed weight-zero representative is handled by shorter length. [F2, step 1.1, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 These reductions cover every finite p and l, not a fixed truncated filtration only. In a resulting strict word, $i_j\le N-1-(l-j)$, while $\operatorname{wt}(a_j)\ge 0$. Consequently $d=\sum_j(\operatorname{wt}(a_j)-i_j-1)\ge l(l-1)/2-Nl$. This quadratic is bounded below and tends to positive infinity. At fixed d it bounds l. For each such l there are finitely many states and their fixed total weight fixes the index sum; step 2.1 bounds all indices. Hence the full unbounded-filtration degree span is finite dimensional and is zero below the quadratic minimum. [step 2.1, step 3.1, algebra]

5.1 For $k\ge 2$ let $C_k(V)=\operatorname{span}\{a_{-k} b\}$. Apply the spanning result to V generated by its vacuum. Nonnegative modes kill the vacuum, so all surviving indices are negative. A word whose smallest index is -m with $m\ge k$ belongs to $C_k$, because $(T^{(m-k)}a)_{(-k)}=k(k+1)...(m-1)a_{(-m)}$. Otherwise all distinct indices lie in the finite interval -k+1,...,-1; finitely many such words remain. Thus $C_k(V)$ is homogeneous, has finite codimension in V, and contains all sufficiently high state-weight spaces. Modulo $O_N(V)$, the circle identity reduces $a_{(-2N-2)}b$ to $-\sum_{j\ge 1}\operatorname{binom}(\operatorname{wt}(a)+N,j)a_{(j-2N-2)}b$. Each term has smaller state weight. Induction on nonnegative state weight therefore reduces $V/O_N$ to finitely many low grades. This proves its finite dimension, including nonvacuum weight-zero states. No ideal property of $O_N$ is used. [F3, F4, step 4.1, algebra]

6.1 Let $F$ be the free complex vector space on the set $\bigcup_{\ell\geq0}(V\times\mathbb Z)^\ell$ of finite mode words, including the empty word, and let $P_{a,m}$ prepend the letter $(a,m)$. Let $\mathcal K$ consist of all subspaces $K\subseteq F$ stable under every $P_{a,m}$ for which the induced operators on $F/K$ are linear in $a$ and satisfy the weak-module axioms. This is a set, as a subset of the power set of $F$; it involves no basis or isomorphism-representative choices. For every cyclic weak module generated by $w$, evaluation of a word on $w$ gives a surjection $F\to Vw$ intertwining all prepend operators. Its kernel belongs to $\mathcal K$, and the induced isomorphism identifies $Vw$ with that quotient. For fixed $N\geq0$, map $a\in V$ to the family of its zero-mode operators in $\prod_{K\in\mathcal K}\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb C}(\Omega_N(F/K))$. By [F4] these operators preserve the indicated spaces, kill $O_N(V)$, take the vacuum to the identity family, and multiply according to $a*_N b$. The image $Z_N$ is therefore an associative unital algebra of dimension at most $\dim V/O_N(V)$, hence finite. Every $w\in\Omega_N(M)$ belongs to its cyclic weak submodule, and $w\in\Omega_N(Vw)$, so this family detects exactly the state zero-mode identities on all weak $\Omega_N$ spaces. Thus the action on every $\Omega_N(M)$ factors through $Z_N$. In particular a polynomial annihilating $o(\omega)$ in this finite-dimensional algebra annihilates $L_M(0)$ on every weak $\Omega_N(M)$, uniformly in $M$. [F4, step 5.1, algebra] ∎
