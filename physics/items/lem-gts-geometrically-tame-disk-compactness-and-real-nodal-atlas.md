## Statement

Let $L$ be a compact embedded Lagrangian in a boundaryless geometrically tame symplectic manifold $(M,\omega)$, with an admissible smooth almost-complex structure $J$. Here geometric tameness means a complete metric $g$ with bounded sectional curvature and positive injectivity-radius bound, and uniform constants $c,C>0$ with $|\omega|_g,|J|_g\le C$ and $\omega(v,Jv)\ge c|v|_g^2$. For each $N\ge1$ and energy cutoff, the stable disk moduli with $N$ ordered boundary marks (including an output), allowing interior sphere trees, has compact image region and compact stable-map topology. Every nonconstant component has energy at least a common $h>0$, only finitely many global free relative homotopy classes occur below each cutoff, and smoothing preserves their rooted-tree collapse class, with additive integral homology image including torsion. Fixed-type component homotopy annotations are retained.

These moduli admit actual finite supported obstruction charts with finite tuple groups, evaluation-submersive thickenings, coordinate-change normal/obstruction isomorphisms and compatible smooth collars. Boundary nodes have a real shared constant mode and one nonnegative smoothing parameter; interior nodes have a complex shared mode and two real smoothing parameters. Real-node residual, uniformly bounded inverse, quadratic and inverse-gluing estimates are constructed below. A common double-exponential gluing profile gives genuine smooth corner transitions and smooth core/evaluation maps. There is no assertion of differentiability in the original naive plumbing parameter. Finite symmetries act by their actual tuple maps. Orientation and chain-valued perturbation are separate results.
