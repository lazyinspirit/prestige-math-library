## Statement

Let $(I,A,D,P,P^{\vee},q)$ be a symmetrizable Cartan datum and let $U_q(\mathfrak g)$ be the Drinfeld–Jimbo algebra of [[def-drinfeld-jimbo-quantized-enveloping-algebra]], with $q_i=q^{d_i}$, $K_i:=K_{d_ih_i}$, coproduct $\Delta$, counit $\varepsilon$ and antipode $S$ as in [[thm-the-drinfeld-jimbo-formulas-define-a-hopf-algebra]]. Write $A=\mathbb Z[q,q^{-1}]$ and let $U_A$, with generators $E_i^{(r)},F_i^{(r)},K_h^{\pm1},T_{i,c,t}$, be the Lusztig divided-power form of the receiving page. On the source side take Lusztig's presentation over $\mathbb Q(v)$, where $v$ is an indeterminate and $v_i=v^{d_i}$; the transport below sends $v_i$ to $q_i^{-1}$: generators $E_i^{L},F_i^{L},K_h^{L}$, the toral relations $K_h^{L}K_{h'}^{L}=K_{h+h'}^{L}$, $K_0^{L}=1$, the toral-action relations
$$K_h^{L}E_i^{L}=v^{\langle\alpha_i,h\rangle}E_i^{L}K_h^{L},\qquad K_h^{L}F_i^{L}=v^{-\langle\alpha_i,h\rangle}F_i^{L}K_h^{L},$$
the mixed relations $E_i^{L}F_j^{L}-F_j^{L}E_i^{L}=\delta_{ij}(K_i^{L}-(K_i^{L})^{-1})/(v_i-v_i^{-1})$, and the Serre relations with $v_i$-Gaussian binomial coefficients; let $\Delta_L$ be the source coproduct on generators
$$\Delta_L E_i^{L}=E_i^{L}\otimes1+K_i^{L}\otimes E_i^{L},\qquad \Delta_L F_i^{L}=F_i^{L}\otimes(K_i^{L})^{-1}+1\otimes F_i^{L},\qquad \Delta_L K_h^{L}=K_h^{L}\otimes K_h^{L}.$$
Let $B_K$ be the normalized Kashiwara half form on $U_q^-$ of [[thm-the-negative-half-bilinear-form-is-symmetric-nondegenerate-and-integral]], with skew derivations $e_i',e_i''$ of [[def-kashiwara-skew-derivations-on-the-negative-quantum-half]], and let $\psi$ be the bar involution of [[lem-the-lusztig-integral-form-is-stable-under-bar-and-contravariant-duality]]. Then:

(i) **Parameter transport.** The assignment $\varphi(v)=q^{-1}$, $\varphi(E_i^L)=E_i$, $\varphi(F_i^L)=F_i$, $\varphi(K_h^L)=K_{-h}$ defines an algebra isomorphism semilinear for the field isomorphism $\mathbb Q(v)\to\mathbb Q(q)$, $v\mapsto q^{-1}$. Its inverse sends $q\mapsto v^{-1}$ and $K_h\mapsto K_{-h}^L$, fixing the root generators. After the separate coefficient-renaming isomorphism $\rho:v\mapsto q$, $K_h^L\mapsto K_h$, this transport is $\varphi=\psi\rho$, with $\psi$ the library bar involution. It carries the source integral form onto $U_A$, fixes divided powers, inverts toral generators and fixes $T_{i,c,t}$. One must not apply the library bar a second time to the already transported parameter $v=q^{-1}$.

(ii) **Coproduct transport.** On all of the source algebra, $(\varphi\otimes\varphi)\circ\Delta_L=\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}\circ\varphi$, where $\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}=\mathrm{flip}\circ\Delta$. Equivalently the source coproduct is the opposite of the fixed library coproduct after parameter transport: the flip of the transported source coproduct is $\Delta$. In particular, with the barred coproduct $\bar\Delta(u):=(\psi\otimes\psi)\Delta(\psi(u))$, an element of the completed tensor square satisfying the source intertwining convention for $\Delta_L$ has a transport-flip that satisfies the library convention $\Delta(u)\Theta=\Theta\bar\Delta(u)$; this is the convention under which the normalized family of (vi) is read.

(iii) **The abstract half and its twisted product.** Transporting $\theta_i\mapsto F_i$ sends the source half $\mathbf f$ to $U_q^-$, the divided powers $\theta_i^{(n)}$ to $F_i^{(n)}$, and the source twisted coproduct multiplication $(x_1\otimes x_2)(y_1\otimes y_2)=v^{\langle\deg x_2,\deg y_1\rangle}x_1y_1\otimes x_2y_2$ to
$$(x_1\otimes x_2)(y_1\otimes y_2)=q^{-\langle\deg x_2,\deg y_1\rangle}x_1y_1\otimes x_2y_2$$
on $U_q^-\otimes U_q^-$; the coefficient extraction $e_i'(F_jQ)=\delta_{ij}Q+q_i^{-a_{ij}}F_je_i'(Q)$ of the transported twisted coproduct agrees with the library skew derivation $e_i'$.

(iv) **The half form up to the root-color factor.** On the homogeneous degree $\nu=\sum_i\nu_i\alpha_i$ the source form and the normalized Kashiwara form are related by
$$B_L=C_\nu\,B_K,\qquad C_\nu=\prod_i(1-q_i^2)^{-\nu_i},$$
a color-degree scaling which is a unit congruent to $1$ modulo $q$ and is not a single scalar on the whole half; for $P,Q$ of different degrees both sides vanish. In the rank-one subalgebra $B_L(F_i^{(n)},F_i^{(n)})=\prod_{r=1}^n(1-q_i^{2r})^{-1}$.

(v) **The normalized Drinfeld pairing.** Let $\iota:U_q^+\to U_q^-$ be the algebra isomorphism $E_i\mapsto F_i$. On homogeneous elements of opposite degrees set
$$D(x,y)=d_\nu^{-1}B_K(\iota x,y),\qquad d_\nu=\prod_i(q_i-q_i^{-1})^{\nu_i}\ \ (\deg x=\nu,\ \deg y=-\nu),$$
and set $D(x,y)=0$ for degrees not opposite. Then $D$ is a well-defined homogeneous pairing, perfect on every finite graded piece, and $D(E_i,F_j)=\delta_{ij}/(q_i-q_i^{-1})$; the pairing with generator value $1$ is the $B_K$-transport $B_K(\iota x,y)$. Its divided-power values are
$$D(E_i^{(n)},F_i^{(n)})=\frac{q_i^{-n(n-1)/2}}{[n]_i!\,(q_i-q_i^{-1})^n}.$$

(vi) **Quasi-R normalization.** In each degree $\nu$ let $(x_a),(y_b)$ be bases of $U_q^+[\nu]$ and $U_q^-[-\nu]$ and let $\Theta_\nu=\sum_a x_a\otimes y^a$ be the $D$-coevaluation, $y^a$ the $D$-dual basis. Then $\Theta_\nu$ is the transported, tensor-flipped source quasi-R component: the source coefficient of Lusztig's printed $\Theta$ is $(-1)^{\mathrm{ht}\,\nu}\prod_iv_i^{\nu_i}$ times the $B_L$-coevaluation, and after parameter transport and flip this scalar equals $d_\nu$, the scalar by which the $D$-coevaluation differs from the $B_K$-coevaluation. In particular, writing $[n]_{q_i^2}!=\prod_{r=1}^n(1+q_i^2+\cdots+q_i^{2(r-1)})$ (the geometric-sum factorial of the receiving page),
$$\Theta_{n\alpha_i}=(q_i-q_i^{-1})^n[n]_{q_i^2}!\,E_i^{(n)}\otimes F_i^{(n)},\qquad\text{so}\qquad \Theta_{\alpha_i}=(q_i-q_i^{-1})\,E_i\otimes F_i.$$

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A symmetrizable Cartan datum, the library algebra $U_q(\mathfrak g)$ with its presentation and Hopf structure, the source presentation with parameter $v=q^{-1}$ as displayed above, and the half form $(B_K)$ of [[thm-the-negative-half-bilinear-form-is-symmetric-nondegenerate-and-integral]] with skew derivations of [[def-kashiwara-skew-derivations-on-the-negative-quantum-half]].

[F1] Presentation and grading ([[def-drinfeld-jimbo-quantized-enveloping-algebra]]): $U_q(\mathfrak g)$ is generated by $E_i,F_i,K_h$ subject to the toral relations $K_hK_{h'}=K_{h+h'}$, $K_0=1$, the toral-action relations $K_hE_i=q^{\langle\alpha_i,h\rangle}E_iK_h$, $K_hF_i=q^{-\langle\alpha_i,h\rangle}F_iK_h$, the mixed relations $E_iF_j-F_jE_i=\delta_{ij}(K_i-K_i^{-1})/(q_i-q_i^{-1})$, and the positive and negative Serre relations with $q_i$-Gaussian binomial coefficients; the algebra is graded by $Q$ with $\deg E_i=\alpha_i$, $\deg F_i=-\alpha_i$, $\deg K_h=0$, and any generator assignment satisfying the relations extends uniquely to an algebra homomorphism.

[F2] Hopf structure ([[thm-the-drinfeld-jimbo-formulas-define-a-hopf-algebra]]): $\Delta$ is the algebra homomorphism with $\Delta E_i=E_i\otimes K_i^{-1}+1\otimes E_i$, $\Delta F_i=F_i\otimes1+K_i\otimes F_i$, $\Delta K_h=K_h\otimes K_h$; $\varepsilon(E_i)=\varepsilon(F_i)=0$, $\varepsilon(K_h)=1$; $S(K_h)=K_{-h}$, $S(E_i)=-E_iK_i$, $S(F_i)=-K_i^{-1}F_i$; these maps descend from the free algebra and make $U_q(\mathfrak g)$ a Hopf algebra.

[F3] Bar involution ([[lem-the-lusztig-integral-form-is-stable-under-bar-and-contravariant-duality]]): $\psi$ is the semilinear algebra involution of $U_q(\mathfrak g)$ with $\psi(q)=q^{-1}$, $\psi(E_i)=E_i$, $\psi(F_i)=F_i$, $\psi(K_h)=K_h^{-1}$, and it restricts to a bijection of $U_A$ fixing the divided powers $E_i^{(r)},F_i^{(r)}$, inverting $K_h$, and fixing the toral binomials $T_{i,c,t}$. No choice principle is used here.

[F4] The half form ([[thm-the-negative-half-bilinear-form-is-symmetric-nondegenerate-and-integral]]): there is exactly one homogeneous bilinear form $B_K$ on $U_q^-$ with $B_K(1,1)=1$ and $B_K(F_iP,Q)=B_K(P,e_i'Q)$; it is symmetric, satisfies $B_K(U_q^-[\nu],U_q^-[\mu])=0$ for $\nu\ne\mu$, is nondegenerate on each finite-dimensional graded piece $U_q^-[\nu]$, and in the rank-one subalgebra
$$B_K(F_i^{(a)},F_i^{(b)})=\delta_{ab}\,[a]_{q_i^2}!^{\,-1},\qquad [a]_{q_i^2}!=\prod_{m=1}^a(1+q_i^2+\cdots+q_i^{2(m-1)}).$$

[F5] Skew derivations ([[def-kashiwara-skew-derivations-on-the-negative-quantum-half]]): $e_i',e_i'':U_q^-\to U_q^-$ are the $\mathbb Q(q)$-linear maps with $e_i'(F_j)=\delta_{ij}$, $e_i'(1)=0$, and twisted Leibniz rules $e_i'(xy)=e_i'(x)y+q_i^{\langle\operatorname{wt}x,h_i\rangle}x\,e_i'(y)$ and $e_i''(xy)=e_i''(x)y+q_i^{-\langle\operatorname{wt}x,h_i\rangle}x\,e_i''(y)$; consequently $e_i'F_j=q_i^{-a_{ij}}F_je_i'+\delta_{ij}$ and $e_i''F_j=q_i^{a_{ij}}F_je_i''+\delta_{ij}$, and both maps decrease the positive height by one and are locally nilpotent. They satisfy $e_i''=\psi e_i'\psi$.

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 **The parameter transport respects every defining relation.** Apply the assignment $v\mapsto q^{-1}$, $E_i^L\mapsto E_i$, $F_i^L\mapsto F_i$, $K_h^L\mapsto K_h^{-1}$ to the source relations. The toral relations become $K_h^{-1}K_{h'}^{-1}=K_{h+h'}^{-1}$ and $K_0^{-1}=1$, which are the library toral relations. For the toral action, $v^{\langle\alpha_i,h\rangle}=(q^{-1})^{\langle\alpha_i,h\rangle}=q^{-\langle\alpha_i,h\rangle}$ and the library relation $K_h^{-1}E_i=q^{\langle\alpha_i,-h\rangle}E_iK_h^{-1}=q^{-\langle\alpha_i,h\rangle}E_iK_h^{-1}$ is exactly the image; the $F$-relation is the same computation with the sign of the exponent reversed. The mixed relation maps to $E_iF_j-F_jE_i=\delta_{ij}(K_i^{-1}-K_i)/(q_i^{-1}-q_i)=\delta_{ij}(K_i-K_i^{-1})/(q_i-q_i^{-1})$, because numerator and denominator both change sign. In the Serre relations the coefficients are $v_i$-Gaussian binomials, the generators map to $E_i$ or $F_i$, and $[m]_{v_i}=(v_i^m-v_i^{-m})/(v_i-v_i^{-1})=(q_i^{-m}-q_i^{m})/(q_i^{-1}-q_i)=[m]_{q_i}$ together with the symmetry $\binom{m}{r}_i=\binom{m}{m-r}_i$ makes each Serre polynomial identical term by term to the library one; the inverse assignment $q\mapsto v^{-1}$, $E_i\mapsto E_i^L$, $F_i\mapsto F_i^L$, $K_h\mapsto K_{-h}^L$ reverses the transport. [F1, algebra]

2.1 **The transport and the integral forms.** The source quotient universal property and step 1.1 give the semilinear isomorphism $\varphi:U_L\to U_q$, with the displayed inverse. The independent coefficient-renaming map $\rho$ identifies the two generator-and-relation algebras without changing toral generators. On generators and scalars $\varphi=\psi\rho$, so the already-proved integral bar stability [F3] gives the asserted transport of the divided-power forms. Only $\psi$ is an involution of the single library algebra; $\varphi$ and its inverse have different source and target coefficient fields. [F1, F3, step 1.1, algebra]

2.2 **The abstract half and the twisted product.** Under the transport $\theta_i\mapsto F_i$ one has $\theta_i^{(n)}\mapsto\theta_i^n/[n]_{v_i}!=F_i^n/[n]_{q_i}!=F_i^{(n)}$, since $[n]_{v_i}=[n]_{q_i}$ by step 1.1; the half is generated by the $\theta_i$ with the source Serre relations, and those map to the $F_i$-Serre relations of [F1], so the transported half is $U_q^-$ and the divided powers match. The source twisted multiplication carries the coefficient $v^{\langle\deg x_2,\deg y_1\rangle}$; under $v=q^{-1}$ and the degree sign reversal $\deg\theta_i=\alpha_i\mapsto\deg F_i=-\alpha_i$ this coefficient becomes $q^{-\langle\deg x_2,\deg y_1\rangle}$ in the transported degrees, which is the displayed formula. Extracting from the transported twisted coproduct the coefficient of $\theta_i\otimes(\text{remaining word})$, i.e. of $F_i\otimes\cdot$, yields $e_i'(F_jQ)=\delta_{ij}Q+q_i^{-a_{ij}}F_je_i'(Q)$: this is exactly the twisted Leibniz rule of [F5] evaluated on $F_j$, since $\langle\operatorname{wt}F_j,h_i\rangle=-a_{ij}$, and the recursion determines the extracted coefficient map uniquely from $e_i'(F_j)=\delta_{ij}$. [F1, F5, step 1.1]

3.1 **Coproduct transport.** The source coproduct $\Delta_L$ and the library coproducts are algebra homomorphisms, so it suffices to compare them on generators. One computes $(\varphi\otimes\varphi)\Delta_L(K_h^L)=K_h^{-1}\otimes K_h^{-1}=\Delta(K_h^{-1})=\Delta(\varphi K_h^L)$; $(\varphi\otimes\varphi)\Delta_L(E_i^L)=E_i\otimes1+K_i^{-1}\otimes E_i=K_i^{-1}\otimes E_i+E_i\otimes1=\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}(E_i)=\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}(\varphi E_i^L)$; and $(\varphi\otimes\varphi)\Delta_L(F_i^L)=F_i\otimes K_i+1\otimes F_i=1\otimes F_i+F_i\otimes K_i=\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}(F_i)=\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}(\varphi F_i^L)$, using $\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}(E_i)=K_i^{-1}\otimes E_i+E_i\otimes1$ and $\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}(F_i)=1\otimes F_i+F_i\otimes K_i$ from [F2]. Both sides are semilinear algebra homomorphisms out of the source presentation, so $(\varphi\otimes\varphi)\circ\Delta_L=\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}\circ\varphi$ holds on all of $U_q(\mathfrak g)$; equivalently $\Delta\circ\varphi=\mathrm{flip}\circ(\varphi\otimes\varphi)\circ\Delta_L$, so the flip of the transported source coproduct is the library coproduct $\Delta$. The transport commutes with the respective source and library bars, as is checked on scalars and generators. Defining $\bar\Delta(u)=(\psi\otimes\psi)\Delta(\psi(u))$, the transport of the intertwining convention therefore follows: applying $\varphi\otimes\varphi$ and flip to the source identity for $\Delta_L$ produces an element $\Theta$ of the completed tensor square satisfying $\Delta(u)\Theta=\Theta\bar\Delta(u)$, because the same transport and tensor flip carry the source barred coproduct to $\bar\Delta$. [F2, step 2.1, algebra]

3.2 **The form dictionary.** Define $B_L(P,Q):=C_\nu B_K(P,Q)$ for homogeneous $P,Q$ of degree $\nu$, where $C_\nu=\prod_i(1-q_i^2)^{-\nu_i}$ and $C_0=1$, and set $B_L(P,Q)=0$ when the degrees differ. Then $B_L(1,1)=1$, $B_L$ is homogeneous and symmetric because $B_K$ is, and for homogeneous $P$ of degree $\nu$ and $Q$ of degree $\nu+\alpha_i$ one has $B_L(F_iP,Q)=C_{\nu+\alpha_i}B_K(F_iP,Q)=C_{\nu+\alpha_i}B_K(P,e_i'Q)=\frac{C_{\nu+\alpha_i}}{C_\nu}B_L(P,e_i'Q)=(1-q_i^2)^{-1}B_L(P,e_i'Q)$, using [F4] and $C_{\nu+\alpha_i}/C_\nu=(1-q_i^2)^{-1}$; here $e_i'Q=0$ whenever $Q$ has a degree not admitting a deletion. This is the source recursion with generator norm $(1-v_i^{-2})^{-1}=(1-q_i^2)^{-1}$ and transported derivative $e_i'$ from step 2.2, and a bilinear form satisfying it, vanishing on unequal degrees and normalized by $B(1,1)=1$, is determined uniquely by induction on the word length of the first variable: peeling the first letter of a word $P$ expresses $B(P,Q)$ through $B$ of a strictly shorter word. Hence $B_L$ is exactly the source form, and $B_L=C_\nu B_K$ is a color-degree scaling rather than a single scalar, because $C_\nu$ depends on all exponents $\nu_i$. In the rank-one subalgebra, [F4] gives $B_L(F_i^{(n)},F_i^{(n)})=(1-q_i^2)^{-n}\prod_{m=1}^n(1+q_i^2+\cdots+q_i^{2(m-1)})^{-1}=\prod_{r=1}^n(1-q_i^{2r})^{-1}$, since $1+q_i^2+\cdots+q_i^{2(m-1)}=(1-q_i^{2m})/(1-q_i^2)$. [F4, F5, step 2.2, algebra]

4.1 **The normalized Drinfeld pairing.** For homogeneous $x$ of degree $\nu$ and $y$ of degree $-\nu$ the expression $D(x,y)=d_\nu^{-1}B_K(\iota x,y)$ is a well-defined element of $\mathbb Q(q)$, because $d_\nu=\prod_i(q_i-q_i^{-1})^{\nu_i}\ne0$ and $B_K$ is graded; it is bilinear in each variable and vanishes when the degrees are not opposite. It is perfect on the finite-dimensional piece $U_q^+[\nu]\times U_q^-[ -\nu]$ because $d_\nu^{-1}$ is a nonzero field scalar, $\iota$ is an isomorphism and $B_K$ is nondegenerate there by [F4]; the pairing with generator value $1$ is the $B_K$-transport $B_K(\iota x,y)$. For simple roots, $B_K(\iota E_i,F_j)=B_K(F_i,F_j)=\delta_{ij}$ by the adjunction $B_K(F_iP,Q)=B_K(P,e_i'Q)$ with $P=1$ and $e_i'(F_j)=\delta_{ij}$ of [F4] and [F5]; hence $D(E_i,F_j)=\delta_{ij}(q_i-q_i^{-1})^{-1}$. For divided powers, with $\nu=n\alpha_i$, $D(E_i^{(n)},F_i^{(n)})=(q_i-q_i^{-1})^{-n}B_K(F_i^{(n)},F_i^{(n)})=q_i^{-n(n-1)/2}/([n]_i!(q_i-q_i^{-1})^n)$ by the rank-one form of [F4] and step 3.2. No choice principle is used: all bases below are finite bases of finite-dimensional graded pieces. [F4, F5, step 3.2]

5.1 **The coevaluation scalar.** Fix $\nu$ and bases $(x_a)$ of $U_q^+[\nu]$, $(y_b)$ of $U_q^-[ -\nu]$. Let $\tilde B_K(x,y):=B_K(\iota x,y)$; its Gram matrix $G$ in these bases is invertible by step 4.1, and its coevaluation is $\sum_a x_a\otimes y^a$ with $y^a$ the $\tilde B_K$-dual basis. Replacing the pairing by $D=d_\nu^{-1}\tilde B_K$ multiplies the inverse Gram matrix, hence every dual vector, by $d_\nu$, so the $D$-coevaluation $\Theta_\nu$ equals $d_\nu$ times the $\tilde B_K$-coevaluation. Likewise, since $B_L=C_\nu B_K$ with $C_\nu$ a nonzero scalar on the degree-$\nu$ piece by step 3.2, the $B_L$-coevaluation equals $C_\nu^{-1}$ times the $B_K$-coevaluation. The printed source coefficient of its quasi-R component is $(-1)^{\mathrm{ht}\,\nu}\prod_iv_i^{\nu_i}$ times the $B_L$-coevaluation, so after the parameter transport $v_i=q_i^{-1}$ its scalar relative to the $\tilde B_K$-coevaluation is $(-1)^{\mathrm{ht}\,\nu}\prod_iq_i^{-\nu_i}C_\nu^{-1}=(-1)^{\mathrm{ht}\,\nu}\prod_iq_i^{-\nu_i}(1-q_i^2)^{\nu_i}=\prod_i(q_i^{-1}(q_i^2-1))^{\nu_i}=\prod_i(q_i-q_i^{-1})^{\nu_i}=d_\nu$, the sign $(-1)^{\nu_i}$ being absorbed by $q_i^2-1=-(1-q_i^2)$ in the $i$-th color. Hence the transported and flipped source component is exactly the $D$-coevaluation $\Theta_\nu$. [step 3.2, step 4.1, algebra]

6.1 **The rank-one quasi-R coefficients.** Take $\nu=n\alpha_i$. Since $(F_i^{(n)})$ spans $U_q^-[ -\nu]$ and $D(E_i^{(n)},F_i^{(n)})$ is a nonzero scalar by step 4.1, the $D$-dual of $E_i^{(n)}$ is $D(E_i^{(n)},F_i^{(n)})^{-1}F_i^{(n)}=(q_i-q_i^{-1})^n[n]_{q_i^2}!F_i^{(n)}$, so $\Theta_{n\alpha_i}=(q_i-q_i^{-1})^n[n]_{q_i^2}!E_i^{(n)}\otimes F_i^{(n)}$ with $[n]_{q_i^2}!=\prod_{r=1}^n(1+q_i^2+\cdots+q_i^{2(r-1)})$, because that geometric product is the inverse of $q_i^{-n(n-1)/2}/[n]_i!$ by the identity used in step 3.2; in particular $\Theta_{\alpha_i}=(q_i-q_i^{-1})E_i\otimes F_i$. This proves the stated general identification and the $n=1$ coefficient $q_i-q_i^{-1}$; no lower-integral self-duality or unitarity is asserted. [step 3.2, step 4.1, step 5.1, algebra]

7.1 **Conclusion.** Steps 1.1 and 2.1 prove (i), step 3.1 proves (ii), step 2.2 proves (iii), step 3.2 proves (iv), step 4.1 proves (v), and steps 5.1 and 6.1 prove (vi). Therefore the parameter, coproduct, half, form, pairing and quasi-R-coefficient dictionaries hold with the stated color-degree factors, and no choice principle is used. [step 1.1, step 2.1, step 2.2, step 3.1, step 3.2, step 4.1, step 5.1, step 6.1] ∎
## Remarks

- The two normalizations are genuinely different: $B_K$ has rank-one norms $1/[n]_{q^2}!$ and the source form has $\prod_{r=1}^n(1-q^{2r})^{-1}$, and they agree only up to the color factor $C_\nu$, which is a nonconstant unit. The quasi-R coefficient multiplying the $D$-coevaluation is the reciprocal $d_\nu$ of the pairing normalization, equivalently $(q_i-q_i^{-1})^n[n]_{q_i^2}!$ on the $i$-th axis.
- Clause (ii) is a statement about conventions only; the existence and uniqueness of the completed family satisfying $\Delta(u)\Theta=\Theta\bar\Delta(u)$ is proved independently in the quasi-R block of this page, and clause (vi) pins only its normalizing scalar, exactly as the owner direction requires.
