## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]).

Let $(R,\mathfrak m)$ be Noetherian local, let $M\ne0$ be finite, and let
$I\subseteq\mathfrak m$ be generated by $n$ elements. Then
$\operatorname{depth}_I(M)\le n$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** the Axiom of Choice and generators $\mathbf x=(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$ of $I$.

[L1] Under Choice, depth is the first nonzero degree of the Koszul cochain complex ([[lem-koszul-depth-first-nonzero-cohomology]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Nakayama gives $M/IM\ne0$, so the Koszul homology in degree $0$ is nonzero and the depth is finite. [given]

2.1 By [L1], the depth is the first nonzero degree of a cochain complex concentrated in degrees $0,\ldots,n$. Therefore it is at most $n$. [L1, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
