## Definition

Let $M$ be a finite-dimensional Hausdorff second-countable smooth manifold without boundary. For $k\ge 0$, let $C_k^\infty(M;\mathbb Z)$ be the free abelian group generated by the smooth singular $k$-simplices of $M$, with finite support, and put $C_k^\infty(M;\mathbb Z)=0$ for $k<0$. For $k\ge1$, define $\partial_k:C_k^\infty(M;\mathbb Z)\to C_{k-1}^\infty(M;\mathbb Z)$ by the alternating sum of affine face restrictions $\partial_k\sigma=\sum_{i=0}^k(-1)^i\sigma\circ\delta_i$, using the face maps of [[def-smooth-singular-simplex]]. Set $\partial_0=0$ (and $\partial_k=0$ for $k<0$). The smoothness convention is the neighbourhood-extension convention for smooth singular simplices. Set $Z_k^\infty(M;\mathbb Z)=\ker\partial_k$, $B_k^\infty(M;\mathbb Z)=\operatorname{im}\partial_{k+1}$, and $H_k^\infty(M;\mathbb Z)=Z_k^\infty/B_k^\infty$.

## Remarks

Every face of a smooth singular simplex is smooth by composition with its affine face map. For $k\ge2$, each affine codimension-two face in $\partial_{k-1}\partial_k\sigma$ occurs in the two orders of deleting its vertices, with opposite alternating signs; hence $\partial_{k-1}\partial_k=0$. In degree $1$ this follows from $\partial_0=0$. Thus $B_k^\infty\subseteq Z_k^\infty$, the displayed quotient is defined, and the smooth chains form a subcomplex of the ordinary integral singular chain complex. Constant and degenerate simplices are retained.
