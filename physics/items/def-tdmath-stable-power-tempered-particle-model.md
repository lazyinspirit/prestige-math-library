## Definition

Fix a positive integer $d$ and use the Euclidean space $\mathbb R^d$ with norm
$\|x\|_2=\sqrt{\langle x,x\rangle}$ of [[def-euclidean-inner-product]]. A
pair potential is an even Borel-measurable function
$\phi:\mathbb R^d\to\overline{\mathbb R}$ whose image is contained in
$\mathbb R\cup\{+\infty\}$. Here $\overline{\mathbb R}$ is the extended real
line ([[def-extended-reals]]). Borel measurability means the inverse image of
each Borel subset of $\overline{\mathbb R}$ belongs to the Borel sigma-algebra
generated by open sets in the Euclidean topology on $\mathbb R^d$
([[def-borel-sigma-algebra-on-the-extended-real-line]],
[[def-borel-sigma-algebra]]). Even means $\phi(-x)=\phi(x)$. The value
$+\infty$ represents a forbidden pair separation. Coordinates and
potential values are numerical values in fixed reference length and energy
units.

For $N\ge1$ and an ordered configuration $(q_1,\ldots,q_N)\in(\mathbb R^d)^N$,
define its pair energy by
$$U_N(q_1,\ldots,q_N):=\sum_{1\le i<j\le N}\phi(q_i-q_j),$$
with an empty sum equal to $0$; also put $U_0=0$. The finite sum is in
$\mathbb R\cup\{+\infty\}$, since each pair term is real or $+\infty$.

The pair potential is **stable** if there is a finite constant $B\ge0$ such
that $U_N(q_1,\ldots,q_N)\ge-BN$ for every $N\ge1$ and every configuration.
It is **power-tempered** if there are constants $C,R,\epsilon>0$ such that
$\phi(x)\in\mathbb R$ whenever $\|x\|_2\ge R$, and, on that set, the ordinary
real absolute value $|\phi(x)|$ satisfies
$$|\phi(x)|\le C\|x\|_2^{-d-\epsilon}.$$
The combined model class consists of pair potentials that are both stable and
power-tempered. No condition is imposed on the positive core, which may be
unbounded or infinite, and the tail may have either sign.
