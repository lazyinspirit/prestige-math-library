## Example

With the conventions of [[thm-verdier-duality-on-oriented-stratified-complex-varieties]] and coefficient field $k$: on the complex line $\mathbb C$ (an oriented smooth complex 1-fold) the dualizing object is $\omega_{\mathbb C}=k_{\mathbb C}[2]$. For the closed inclusion $i:\{0\}\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$ one has $i^!k_{\mathbb C}=k[-2]$ and $i^!\omega_{\mathbb C}=k$. The example consumes the AC-assumed Verdier supplier; the displayed computation itself is choice-free.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The complex line $\mathbb C$ with its analytic topology, the closed inclusion $i:\{0\}\hookrightarrow\mathbb C$, a coefficient field $k$, and the conventions of [[thm-verdier-duality-on-oriented-stratified-complex-varieties]] and [[def-derived-operations-on-analytic-sheaves-over-locally-compact-spaces]]; AC is consumed through the Verdier supplier.

[F1] The Verdier supplier: $\omega_X=a_X^!k$ and $D_X=R\mathcal Hom(-,\omega_X)$; on a smooth complex $d$-fold the complex orientation gives $\omega_X\simeq k_X[2d]$; $f^!\simeq D_Xf^*D_Y$; and dualizing transitivity holds ([[thm-verdier-duality-on-oriented-stratified-complex-varieties]]).

[F2] Closed-support localization and the exceptional adjoint: for a closed inclusion $i:Z\to X$ the object $i^!$ is the derived functor of $i^{-1}\Gamma_Z$, both localization triangles hold and $R\mathcal Hom(i_*k_Z,A)\cong R\Gamma_ZA$ ([[lem-analytic-six-operation-support-and-trace-calculus]], [[thm-compactly-supported-derived-pushforward-has-exceptional-inverse-image-adjoint]]).

[F3] Compactly supported global sections and their derived functors $H^q_c$ are defined for locally compact Hausdorff spaces ([[def-compactly-supported-global-sections]]).

[F4] Conventions: shifts satisfy $H^p(A[r])=H^{p+r}(A)$ and the cochain cone and derived-Hom conventions are those of [[def-derived-category-of-an-abelian-category]] and [[def-derived-hom-in-the-bounded-setting]] ([[def-derived-operations-on-analytic-sheaves-over-locally-compact-spaces]], [[def-inverse-image-presheaf-and-sheaf]], [[def-direct-image-sheaf]], [[def-extension-by-zero-abelian-sheaf]], [[def-sheaf-on-topological-space]]).

## Verification

1.1 A positively complex-oriented open disk $U\subset\mathbb C$, with closed-disk compactification $D=\overline U$, has compact-support cochain complex with a single cohomology generator in degree $2$ over $k$: the closed disk with its boundary is the finite relative cellular model $(D,\partial D)$ whose quotient is generated by the oriented $2$-cell, which computes compact supports on $U$ by extension by zero to $D$. Hence the dual local model has $k$ concentrated in degree $-2$, and the orientation normalization of [F1] at $d=1$ gives $\omega_{\mathbb C}=k_{\mathbb C}[2]$. This uses the same positive complex orientation generator as the diagonal Thom calculations. [given, F1, F3]

2.1 Closed-support cohomology at the origin. By [F2] the object $i^!k_{\mathbb C}$ is $R\Gamma_{\{0\}}(\mathbb C,k_{\mathbb C})$, the derived functor of $i^{-1}\Gamma_{\{0\}}$, and the localization triangle of [F2] computes it as the relative cohomology of the pair $(U,U\setminus\{0\})$ for a small open disk $U$ around the origin; radial retraction of the punctured disk to the circle gives the relative cellular model with a single generator in degree $2$, so $i^!k_{\mathbb C}=k[-2]$. Applying the shift $[2]$ of [F4] gives $i^!\omega_{\mathbb C}=i^!(k_{\mathbb C}[2])=k[-2][2]=k$, consistently with dualizing transitivity of [F1]. The duality route produces the same answer: $D_{\mathbb C}k_{\mathbb C}=k_{\mathbb C}[2]$, $i^*(k_{\mathbb C}[2])=k[2]$ and $D_{\{0\}}(k[2])=k[-2]$, with the same positive orientation generator used in both computations. The displayed computation involves only the finite relative cellular model of a disk and is choice-free; AC is present only because the consumed Verdier supplier assumes it. [given, F1, F2, F4, step 1.1] ∎
