## Statement

Assume AC. Let $N\in\{4,5\}$, $n=N-2$, and let $Q_N=\Lambda V\rtimes\widetilde\Gamma$ be the source object-graded exterior smash algebra, where $\dim V=N$ and $\widetilde\Gamma=\{(\lambda_i)\in\mu_N^N:\prod\lambda_i=1\}$. Use exterior weight $n/N$ with the actual object shifts, so shifts cancel around Hochschild words. Index the primitive object idempotents by characters $\chi_m$ of $\widetilde\Gamma$, with $m\in(\mathbb Z/N)^N/\langle(1,\ldots,1)\rangle$, and put $\theta_m=\operatorname{frac}(2\sum_i m_i/N)$. Give $v_i e_{\chi_m}$ degree $n/N+\theta_{m+e_i}-\theta_m$; it is integral and the shifts telescope on a closed word. Normalized Hochschild cochains are taken over this semisimple object ring, completed in arity; arity $s$ and internal degree $t$ have total degree $s+t$, and the truncation means $t\le0$. For a minimal uncurved central structure with binary algebra $Q_N$, no operations below arity $N$ and primary class $w=y_1\cdots y_N$, its truncated degree-two Hochschild group has a binary complete-graph cycle subquotient and the pure-power subquotient spanned by $y_i^N$. Under the actual even-coordinate action, its invariant part is exactly $\mathbb C[\sum_i y_i^N]$. No unrestricted one-dimensional cyclic claim is made. Two such central structures with the same primary class are isomorphic by an object-preserving strictly unital all-arity gauge. A strict finite-group action on a bounded finite-free filtered complex over $\mathbb C[\![q]\!]$ with finite-free cohomology admits an equivariant contraction and equivariant minimal transfer, as explicitly constructed below.
