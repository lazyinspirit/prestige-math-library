## Definition

Fix a four-dimensional smooth spacetime region $U\subseteq\mathbb R^{1,3}$ with a stated orientation, spin structure, boundary conditions, and unit system. A selected model specifies a continuous global symmetry group $H$, its action on the fields and observables, the nondynamical background connections, the field and jet domains, a regulator and measure or formal perturbative generating functional, and the admissible local counterterms. A background connection is nondynamical model data: it transforms under local changes of background trivialization but is not integrated over.

Let $\mathcal A_{\mathrm{bg},U}^{g,p}$ be the finite-jet horizontal $p$-forms of ghost number $g$ generated by the nondynamical background connections and their BRST ghost jets, without matter-field jets. Fix their coefficient field $\mathbb K$ as part of the model and restrict its nilpotent BRST differential $s$ and horizontal exterior derivative $d_H$ to this background subalgebra, with $s^2=d_H^2=sd_H+d_Hs=0$. The model supplies two $\mathbb K$-linear subspaces

$$\mathcal C\subseteq\mathcal A_{\mathrm{bg},U}^{0,4},\qquad\mathcal N\subseteq\mathcal A_{\mathrm{bg},U}^{1,3}.$$

Here $\mathcal C$ is exactly the space of admissible changes of local background counterterm density, with coefficients and units giving dimensionless integrated action changes. The space $\mathcal N$ is exactly the space of boundary primitives whose $d_H$-derivatives are discarded in the infinitesimal variation under the stated support or boundary conditions. This convention must hold on every admitted background and ghost input; a surface contribution that is not discarded is not an element of $d_H\mathcal N$. Linearity of these two spaces is explicit model input, not a consequence of locality or nilpotence. No linear span of a restricted admissibility set is taken implicitly. If quantization or other restrictions instead make the admissible changes nonlinear, this vector-space quotient does not apply to them without a separately specified equivalence structure.

The space of compatible Wess–Zumino cocycles and the space of admissible changes are

$$Z^{1,4}:=\{a\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{bg},U}^{1,4}:sa+d_Hb=0\text{ for some }b\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{bg},U}^{2,3}\},\qquad B_{\mathcal C,\mathcal N}:=s\mathcal C+d_H\mathcal N.$$

Both are linear spaces, and $B_{\mathcal C,\mathcal N}\subseteq Z^{1,4}$: for $a=sc+d_Hn$ choose the ghost-number-two three-form $b=sn$, giving $sa+d_Hb=s^2c+sd_Hn+d_Hsn=0$. More generally, if $sa+d_Hb=0$, then $a'=a+sc+d_Hn$ obeys $sa'+d_H(b+sn)=0$, so every admitted representative change preserves consistency. The witness space for $b$ has bidegree $(2,3)$ and is distinct from the boundary-ambiguity space $\mathcal N$ of bidegree $(1,3)$.

An infinitesimal local anomaly representative is a specified $a_4\in Z^{1,4}$ for the model's quantum background variation. The **local perturbative ’t Hooft anomaly** is its class in

$$H^{1,4}_{\mathcal C,\mathcal N}(s\mid d_H):=Z^{1,4}/B_{\mathcal C,\mathcal N},\qquad a'_4\sim a_4\ \Longleftrightarrow\ a'_4-a_4=sc+d_Hn\text{ for some }c\in\mathcal C,\ n\in\mathcal N.$$

The subspace property makes this an equivalence relation and a quotient vector space. Its class is zero exactly when $a_4=sc+d_Hn$ for some such $c,n$; adding the admissible counterterm density $-c$ then leaves only the discarded term $d_Hn$. Conversely, if adding an admissible density $c'$ leaves a discarded term, $a_4+sc'=d_Hn$, then $a_4=s(-c')+d_Hn$ has zero class. This is precisely cancellation of the infinitesimal local background variation in the declared convention. The ordinary unrestricted relative cohomology $H^{1,4}(s\mid d_H)$ is the special case $\mathcal C=\mathcal A_{\mathrm{bg},U}^{0,4}$ and $\mathcal N=\mathcal A_{\mathrm{bg},U}^{1,3}$ with a boundary convention discarding all these total derivatives; it is not substituted for a restricted model quotient.

Any prescribed noninvariance of the classical action or selected couplings is an explicit-symmetry-breaking insertion and is separated before assigning this quantum anomaly class. It is not, by itself, a measure anomaly. A claim of anomaly matching therefore assumes the stated classical symmetry, or carries such breaking insertions separately.

This definition applies to a global symmetry whose background is nondynamical. If the same local class concerns a field integrated over as a gauge redundancy, it is a local gauge anomaly, not a ’t Hooft anomaly; an uncancelled gauge anomaly obstructs the proposed gauging unless a separately specified cancellation mechanism is included. The definition addresses only infinitesimal transformations and makes no claim about large or disconnected transformations, determinant-line holonomy, torsion phases, or a physical continuum limit.
