## Definition

Assume $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$. Let $(M,g,N,o)$ be an oriented and
time-oriented smooth Lorentzian four-manifold, and let
$P^+_g(M)$ be its smooth oriented time-oriented orthonormal frame bundle from
the oriented-frame-bundle proposition. Set
$\mathrm{Spin}^+(1,3):=SL(2,\mathbb C)$ and let
$\rho:SL(2,\mathbb C)\to SO^+(1,3)$ be the Hermitian-matrix covering
homomorphism of the Hermitian-matrix double-cover proposition. A **Lorentzian spin
structure** is a smooth right principal $SL(2,\mathbb C)$-bundle
$\widetilde P\to M$ together with a smooth map $\lambda:\widetilde P\to
P^+_g(M)$ over the identity of $M$ such that

$$\lambda(ua)=\lambda(u)\rho(a)\qquad (u\in\widetilde P,\ a\in SL(2,\mathbb C)),$$

and the restriction of $\lambda$ to each fiber is a surjective two-sheeted
covering map. The map is over the identity, meaning $\pi^+\circ\lambda=\widetilde\pi$; the square below displays this base condition and is not used as a proof.

```tikzcd
\begin{tikzcd}
\widetilde P \arrow[r,"\lambda"] \arrow[d,"\widetilde\pi"'] & P^+_g(M) \arrow[d,"\pi^+"] \\
M \arrow[r,"\mathrm{id}_M"'] & M
\end{tikzcd}
```

A smooth finite-dimensional complex representation here consists of a
finite-dimensional complex vector space $V_\sigma$ and a smooth group
homomorphism $\sigma:SL(2,\mathbb C)\to GL(V_\sigma)$, where
$GL(V_\sigma)$ is the group of invertible complex-linear maps on
$V_\sigma$, with the smooth structure inherited as an open subset of the
real vector space of complex-linear endomorphisms of $V_\sigma$. Its
**associated vector bundle** is the quotient
$$\widetilde P\times_\sigma V_\sigma:=(\widetilde P\times V_\sigma)/\sim,$$
where $\sim$ is the equivalence relation generated by
$(ua,v)\sim(u,\sigma(a)v)$ for $u\in\widetilde P$,
$a\in SL(2,\mathbb C)$ and $v\in V_\sigma$; equivalently
$(u,v)\sim(ua,\sigma(a)^{-1}v)$, using
the right-principal associated-bundle convention. Write $[u,v]$ for
the class of $(u,v)$. Since every $\sigma(a)$ is complex-linear, the
fiberwise vector-space operations
$[u,v]+[u,w]=[u,v+w]$ and $z[u,v]=[u,zv]$ for $z\in\mathbb C$ are
independent of the representative. The associated-bundle local-triviality proposition
gives local triviality; on an overlap with local sections satisfying
$s_j(x)=s_i(x)a_{ij}(x)$, the smooth principal charts give transition maps
$(x,v)\mapsto(x,\sigma(a_{ij}(x))v)$. These are smooth because $\sigma$ is
smooth and define the smooth vector-bundle structure. The **defining spinor representation** here is $V_\sigma=\mathbb C^2$ with the action $\sigma(a)v=av$ for $a\in SL(2,\mathbb C)$ and column vectors $v\in\mathbb C^2$. It is a smooth group homomorphism because matrix multiplication is smooth and $(ab)v=a(bv)$. Its associated bundle is called the **rank-two complex spinor bundle** in this convention; a **spinor field** is a smooth section of that bundle.

To define a **lift of a Lorentz connection**, first supply a principal
connection $H^+\subset TP^+_g(M)$ on the frame bundle: it is a smooth vector
subbundle complementary to $VP^+:=\ker(d\pi^+)$ and invariant under the
right $SO^+(1,3)$ action, as in
the principal-connection definition. This $H^+$ is additional
global data; the local affine connection and local matrix $\omega$ in
the local tetrad-connection definition do not determine it unless they are
specified as restrictions of a chosen global metric-compatible affine
connection whose induced principal connection is the supplied $H^+$. A lift
is a supplied principal connection $\widetilde H\subset T\widetilde P$,
likewise a smooth
right-invariant horizontal complement to
$V\widetilde P:=\ker(d\widetilde\pi)$, such that
$d\lambda_u(\widetilde H_u)=H^+_{\lambda(u)}$ at each
$u\in\widetilde P$. It induces a covariant derivative on the associated
vector bundle by horizontal differentiation: a section is represented by a
smooth equivariant map $f:\widetilde P\to V_\sigma$ with
$f(ua)=\sigma(a)^{-1}f(u)$, and its derivative along a smooth base vector
field $X$ at $x$ is $\nabla_Xs(x):=[u,df_u(\widetilde X^H_u)]$ for
$u\in\widetilde P_x$. Here $s(x)=[u,f(u)]$, and the **horizontal lift** is the smooth field
$\widetilde X^H_u:=(d\widetilde\pi_u|_{\widetilde H_u})^{-1}X_x$; the restriction is a linear isomorphism because $\widetilde H$ complements the vertical kernel. Right invariance and uniqueness give
$\widetilde X^H_{ua}=dR_a(\widetilde X^H_u)$. Differentiating $f\circ R_a=\sigma(a)^{-1}f$ therefore gives
$df_{ua}(\widetilde X^H_{ua})=\sigma(a)^{-1}df_u(\widetilde X^H_u)$, and the associated-bundle relation makes $[u,df_u(\widetilde X^H_u)]$ independent of $u$. In principal charts this formula is smooth, is real $C^\infty(M)$-linear in $X$ and complex-linear in $s$. For $h\in C^\infty(M,\mathbb C)$, the product rule applied to $(h\circ\widetilde\pi)f$ gives $\nabla_X(hs)=X(h)s+h\nabla_Xs$. These are the covariant-derivative properties used here. The local
Clifford-compatibility calculation is a separate proposition about supplied
gamma matrices and does not follow from the existence of $\sigma$ alone.
A spin structure, a global spinor field, and a lifted connection are extra data: this definition asserts
neither existence nor uniqueness of a spin lift, global tetrad, or connection
lift. The matrix group and covering homomorphism are the Lorentzian ones specified above.
