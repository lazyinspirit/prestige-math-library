## Statement

Let $\mathfrak g$ be a finite-dimensional complex simple Lie algebra, with long roots normalized to squared length $2$, Coxeter number $h$, dual Coxeter number $h^\vee$, and lacing number $r\in\{1,2,3\}$. Let $p,q\in\mathbb Z_{>0}$, $\gcd(p,q)=1$, and $k+h^\vee=p/q$. Assume either $\gcd(q,r)=1$ and $p\ge h^\vee$, or $\gcd(q,r)=r$ and $p\ge h$. These are the admissible numerator/gcd inequalities used here; no additional nondegeneracy bound on $q$ is assumed. Let $L(k\Lambda_0)$ be the simple affine vacuum. For a finite minimal parabolic with simple Levi root $\alpha_i$, set $c_i=2/(\alpha_i,\alpha_i)$ and $k_i+2=c_i(k+h^\vee)$. The explicitly defined semi-infinite nilradical BRST cohomology of every $L(k\Lambda_0)$-module carries an actual module action of the SIMPLE affine rank-one quotient $L_{\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_2}(k_i\Lambda_0)$, with the spectator Heisenberg factor at level $k+h^\vee$.

For a positively loop-graded highest module with top $E$, its lowest-depth groups are $H^r(\mathfrak m_i,E)$ for $r\ge0$ and zero for $r<0$. In particular its highest vector gives a nonzero rank-one highest subquotient. The proof constructs faithful parabolic BRST induction on the relevant Levi highest-weight category, identifies restriction on actual Wakimoto modules, and exhibits the rank-one vacuum singular state as a genuine boundary. A universal Levi current action alone is not the asserted conclusion.
