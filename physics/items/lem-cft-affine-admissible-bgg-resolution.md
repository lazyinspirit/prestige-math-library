## Statement

Let $k+h^\vee>0$, and let $\lambda$ be regular dominant with full-rank affine integral real-root system, with positive roots inherited from the original affine Borel. Let $W_\lambda$ be its integral Coxeter group. There are compatible nonzero Verma inclusions and incidence signs making
$$B_j(\lambda)=\bigoplus_{w\in W_\lambda,\ \ell(w)=j}M(w\mathbin\cdot\lambda)$$
a complex with $H_0=L(\lambda)$ and $H_j=0$ for $j>0$. Hom spaces between the orbit Vermas have dimension at most one. The proof constructs exact semiregular tilting on finite Verma flags, taking $M(\mu)$ to $M(-\mu-2\widehat\rho)$, and the opposite-polarization semiregular bimodule whose left central level is its right level minus $2h^\vee$. Integrability in the proof is always transported to an ORIGINAL simple-root $\mathfrak{sl}_2$, not asserted for an unshifted rescaled-loop root subalgebra.

33.1 **Simple images and highest labels.** For any simple highest subquotient $L(\mu)$ of an orbit Verma sum, $T L(\mu)$ is nonzero by the unit, and is simple as an actual $U$-module: any of its $U$-submodules belongs to the full subquotient category $\mathcal C'$, and the exact inverse would produce a proper nonzero submodule of $L(\mu)$. Apply $T$ to $M(\mu)\twoheadrightarrow L(\mu)$. Its source is $M(s_i\mathbin\cdot\mu)$ by step 27.1, so its nonzero simple quotient is precisely $L(s_i\mathbin\cdot\mu)$. Thus every simple subquotient, rather than just a formal character or a highest line in a localization, has the asserted image. [step 27.1, step 32.1, algebra]

34.1 At each gallery wall, all weights in the relevant integral orbit have nonintegral $h_i$ eigenvalues, since the wall is outside that integral root system and root-lattice shifts change $h_i$ by integers. Iterating this construction through the finite gallery therefore gives exactly the simple-subquotient transport and integral-root labels needed below, with a restricted-Hom inverse at every step. No external equivalence theorem is used. [F1, F2, F3, F4, step 26.1, step 33.1, algebra]

35.1 Pair its Weyl labels in left cosets of ${1,s_{i}}$. Filter by the length of the minimal coset representative. The Bruhat subword property shows this filtration is preserved by the differential. 36.1 The cokernel is locally $sl2_{i}$-integrable: $f_{i}$ has the stated finite relation on its highest vector, $ad(f_{i})$ is locally nilpotent on every enveloping word, and $e_{i}$ is locally nilpotent by highest support. 37.1 Now suppose some original homology has a simple highest subquotient L(mu). Linkage for the orbit Verma terms forces mu in the integral dot orbit of lambda. 38.1 For $j>0$ the complex term consists of M(v dot lambda) with $length(v)=j$; all their weights are strictly below lambda. No such homology can contain L(lambda), so it is zero. 40.1 **Opposite polarization.** Reverse the principal grading and replace $2\widehat\rho$ by $-2\widehat\rho$ in the same construction. Every finite normal-order and PBW calculation remains valid.
