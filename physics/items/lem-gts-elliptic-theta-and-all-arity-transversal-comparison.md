## Statement

Assume AC. Fix $\tau\in\mathbb H$, $q=e^{2\pi i\tau}$, the complex elliptic curve $E_\tau$, and its symplectic mirror torus with complexified area $\rho=\tau$. Use the source gradings, finite local systems whose monodromy eigenvalues have modulus one (including nilpotent Jordan parts), and the coherent parallel-framing/orientation conventions. Then the explicit theta correspondence covers every indecomposable coherent sheaf by rational graded straight circles and their local systems, including vertical torsion jets, and identifies every graded Hom space and binary composition. On generic transversal collections (distinct circle labels, pairwise transverse or disjoint, and no triple concurrence), it extends to a fully faithful all-arity $A_\infty$ comparison between the nonvertical polygon category and the specified Dolbeault/coherent enhancement. The comparison is compatible with finite nilpotent parameter jets, finite isogeny descent and direct sums. Its generic collections are cofinal under adjoining finitely many fresh line objects/resolutions. This lemma makes no binary-only claim of a derived equivalence; nontransversal/derived completion is the separate attached supplier.
