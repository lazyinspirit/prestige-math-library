## Statement

Assume full AC. Let $X$ be a smooth manifold with a one-form $\lambda$ such that $d\lambda$ is symplectic. Its Liouville vector field $Z$ is the unique vector field satisfying $\iota_Zd\lambda=\lambda$. Assume there is a compact domain $K\subset X$ with smooth boundary such that $Z$ points strictly outward along $\partial K$ and its positive flow is complete on $X\setminus K$. Let $L_0,\ldots,L_r$ be a finite collection of compact exact graded relatively spin Lagrangians, meaning $\lambda|_{L_i}=df_i$ and the grading data consist of a chosen infinite Maslov covering $\widetilde{\operatorname{Lag}}(TX,\omega)\to\operatorname{Lag}(TX,\omega)$ and a lift of each Gauss map $x\mapsto T_xL_i$. The cover restricts in every tangent fiber to the universal cover of its Lagrangian Grassmannian. Choose an auxiliary compatible complex structure to express the convention: in a unitary frame the squared phase of a plane $P=U\mathbb R^n$ is $\det_{\mathbb C}(U)^2\in S^1$, and a lift has real coordinate $\theta$ with phase $e^{2\pi i\theta}$. Frame transitions use the chosen Maslov cover; simultaneous changes shift both lifted phases at an intersection equally. Thus degrees are computed in one common lifted fiber, not from unspecified independent phase branches. A global squared-phase trivialization, when used, is additional chosen data realizing this same cover. Use transverse intersection generators; if a Hamiltonian-chord version is used, require every chord in each relevant path-space component to be nondegenerate. Assume admissible domain-dependent perturbation data with no escape to infinity. Require the linearized operator at every solution in each zero- and one-dimensional polygon moduli space, and at every component of every broken configuration in their compactifications, to be onto in the declared weighted Sobolev spaces; in particular, a negative-index regular component is absent. Also assume coherent determinant orientations compatible with gluing, or use $\mathbb F_2$ coefficients. Count rigid polygons. The target is that the compactified boundary of each one-dimensional moduli space consists precisely of the broken-polygon configurations; with coherent orientations, their signed count is the boundary sum and gives the A-infinity identities. This is a conditional branch and does not assert existence of regular perturbation data or a gluing theorem.
