## Example

Let $\mathfrak g=\mathfrak{su}(2)$ with basis $T_a=-\tfrac{i}{2}\sigma_a$, where

$$
\sigma_1=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad \sigma_2=\begin{pmatrix}0&-i\\i&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad \sigma_3=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Then $[T_a,T_b]=\epsilon_{ab}{}^cT_c$. Use the defining representation on $V=\mathbb C^2$, so $\rho(T_a)=T_a$, and let $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ be a nonempty open set, where $n$ is a nonnegative integer. Place $V$ wholly in either even or odd parity. For $n\geq1$, use the finite-jet algebra of the earlier BRST definition. For $n=0$, $U=\mathbb R^0$ is a point: take the graded-commutative algebra over $\mathbb C$ freely generated by the two matter components of the chosen parity and three odd ghosts, with no gauge-potential components or positive-order jets. On these algebras, with even gauge potential $A_\mu=A_\mu^aT_a$, odd ghosts $c^a$, and homogeneous matter components $\psi^\alpha$, define the odd left derivation $s$ by $sf=0$ on coefficient functions, by prolongation $s(D_I\Phi)=D_I(s\Phi)$ on jets, and by

$$sA_\mu^a=\partial_\mu c^a+\epsilon^a{}_{bc}A_\mu^bc^c,\qquad sc^a=-\frac12\epsilon^a{}_{bc}c^bc^c,\qquad s\psi=-c^aT_a\psi.$$

The operator $s$ squares to zero on these generators and their formal spacetime jets.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The displayed basis, representation, fields, and local BRST formulas.

[A1] $\epsilon^a{}_{bc}$ is the totally antisymmetric symbol with $\epsilon^1{}_{23}=1$.

[F1] [[def-qftx-nonabelian-brst-complex]]: for $n\geq1$, an even gauge potential, odd ghost, and parity-preserving Lie-algebra representation, $sA_\mu=\partial_\mu c+[A_\mu,c]$, $sc=-\tfrac12[c,c]$, $s\psi=-\rho(c)\psi$, and $s(D_I\Phi)=D_I(s\Phi)$, with $s(FG)=(sF)G+(-1)^{|F|}F(sG)$ for homogeneous $F$.

[F2] [[lem-qftx-nonabelian-brst-nilpotence]]: for $n\geq1$, these formulas square to zero on all finite-jet polynomials whenever the Lie-algebra Jacobi and representation identities hold.

## Verification

1.1 Direct multiplication gives $\sigma_1\sigma_2=i\sigma_3$, $\sigma_2\sigma_1=-i\sigma_3$, $\sigma_2\sigma_3=i\sigma_1$, $\sigma_3\sigma_2=-i\sigma_1$, $\sigma_3\sigma_1=i\sigma_2$, and $\sigma_1\sigma_3=-i\sigma_2$, while $\sigma_a^2=I$. Hence $[T_a,T_b]=(-i/2)^2[\sigma_a,\sigma_b]=\epsilon_{ab}{}^cT_c$ by [A1]. Matrix commutators satisfy Jacobi because the Jacobiator expands to $XYZ-YXZ-ZXY+ZYX+YZX-ZYX-XYZ+XZY+ZXY-XZY-YZX+YXZ=0$, so these structure constants obey Jacobi. Since $\rho(T_a)=T_a$, the same commutator identity is the representation identity; it preserves either chosen pure grading of $V$. For $n\geq1$, substitution into [F1] gives exactly the three component formulas. [A1, F1, algebra]

2.1 If $n\geq1$, the hypotheses of [F2] hold for $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ and its defining representation with either pure grading. Applying that lemma gives $s^2=0$ on the generators, every total-derivative jet, and their finite graded-polynomial algebra. [given, F2, step 1.1]

3.1 If $n=0$, the displayed formulas reduce to $sc^1=-c^2c^3$, $sc^2=-c^3c^1$, $sc^3=-c^1c^2$, and $s\psi=-c^aT_a\psi$. The odd left-derivation rule gives $s^2c^1=c^3c^1c^3-c^2c^1c^2=0$, since each odd ghost has square zero; cyclic permutation proves $s^2c^2=s^2c^3=0$. Put $C=c^aT_a$. Anticommutation and step 1.1 give $C^2=\tfrac12c^ac^b[T_a,T_b]=\tfrac12\epsilon^d{}_{ab}c^ac^bT_d=-sC$, so $s^2\psi=-(sC)\psi+C(s\psi)=C^2\psi-C^2\psi=0$ for either matter parity. The square of an odd derivation is an even derivation, as the cross terms in $s^2(FG)$ cancel. It therefore vanishes on this entire freely generated graded-commutative algebra. There are no remaining gauge components or positive-order jets. Together with step 2.1, this proves the assertion for every allowed $n$. [given, A1, step 1.1, step 2.1, algebra] ∎
