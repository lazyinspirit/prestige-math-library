## Statement

Assume AC. For $n\ge 1,l\ge 0$ over C, let $W_{f}\subset (A²)^{n}\times (A²)^{l}$ be the graph $a_{i}=x_{f(i)},b_{i}=y_{f(i)}$ of f:[l]→[n], and $R(n,l)=C[x,y,a,b]/\bigcap _{f}$ $I(W_{f})$. For $0\le r\le n,0\le k\le l$ and integer m, let I(m,r,k) be the ideal of the reduced union of $V(x_{j}:j\\in  T)\cap W_{f}$ over pairs with |T∩[r] minus $f([k])|\ge m$. Then R(n,l) has a homogeneous $C[y_{1},…,y_{n}]$-basis with a subset spanning each I(m,r,k). In particular it is free over C[y]. If A is the space of $S_{n}$-alternating polynomials in C[x,y] and $J=C[x,y]A$, then every $J^{d}$, $d\ge 0$, is free over C[y]. No Hilbert-scheme Cohen–Macaulayness or n! theorem is a premise.
