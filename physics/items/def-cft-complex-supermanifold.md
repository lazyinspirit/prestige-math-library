## Definition

Write a complex superalgebra as a unital associative $\mathbb Z_2$-graded algebra $A=A_0\oplus A_1$ satisfying $ab=(-1)^{|a||b|}ba$ for homogeneous $a,b$. A locally ringed superspace is a topological space $X$ with a sheaf $\mathcal O_X$ of such algebras whose stalk at each point has a unique maximal homogeneous ideal. A morphism $(f,f^\#):(X,\mathcal O_X)\to(Y,\mathcal O_Y)$ consists of a continuous map $f:X\to Y$ and a parity-preserving unital sheaf morphism $f^\#: \mathcal O_Y\to f_*\mathcal O_X$ whose stalk maps $\mathcal O_{Y,f(x)}\to\mathcal O_{X,x}$ are local: the inverse image of the maximal homogeneous ideal of $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ is the maximal homogeneous ideal of $\mathcal O_{Y,f(x)}$. An isomorphism is a morphism with a two-sided inverse.

For $m\geq1$ and open $U\subseteq\mathbb C^m$, let $\mathcal O_U(V)$ be the algebra of functions $f:V\to\mathbb C$ that are complex Fréchet differentiable at every point of the open set $V\subseteq U$. For $m=0$, $\mathbb C^0$ is a point; on that point $\mathcal O_U(U)=\mathbb C$, the algebra of all functions from the point to $\mathbb C$. For integers $m,n\geq0$, the complex superdomain $U^{m|n}$ has underlying topological space $U$ and sheaf
$$\mathcal O_{U^{m|n}}(V)=\mathcal O_U(V)\otimes_{\mathbb C}\Lambda_{\mathbb C}(\theta_1,\ldots,\theta_n),$$
where the generators $\theta_i$ are odd, so $\theta_i\theta_j=-\theta_j\theta_i$ and $\theta_i^2=0$. The complex supermanifold of dimension $m|n$ is a Hausdorff, second-countable locally ringed superspace locally isomorphic to these superdomains by the morphisms just defined. Its reduced space $S_{\mathrm{red}}$ is the ringed space obtained by quotienting $\mathcal O_S$ by the ideal locally generated by odd sections; on $U^{m|n}$ this is $(U,\mathcal O_U)$.

Use left odd derivatives. A homogeneous derivation $X$ of parity $|X|\in\{0,1\}$ satisfies the graded Leibniz rule $X(fg)=X(f)g+(-1)^{|X||f|}fX(g)$. The tangent sheaf $T_S$ is the locally free sheaf of such derivations. A distribution of rank $r|s$ is a locally free direct summand of $T_S$ with even rank $r$ and odd rank $s$.

For an even invertible coordinate Jacobian written in blocks $J=\begin{pmatrix}A&B\\ C&D\end{pmatrix}$, with $A,D$ even square blocks and $B,C$ odd blocks, its Berezinian is $\operatorname{Ber}(J)=\det(A-BD^{-1}C)/\det D$. The Berezinian line is the line bundle whose transition functions are these Berezinians. All uses below have invertible $D$ blocks; the alternative formula using invertible $A$ is equivalent on the common domain.

## Remarks

This is local complex-analytic supergeometry. It does not assert that a global supermoduli space, a compactification, an integration cycle, or a physical functional measure exists.
