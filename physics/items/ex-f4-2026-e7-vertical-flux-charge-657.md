## Example

Li reports a fixed four-dimensional F-theory model with a toric Calabi–Yau fourfold over a toric threefold base. In the source's model, the base divisor $D^{19}$ has local surface geometry $\mathbb F_0=\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1$ and supports a rigid $G_2$ gauge factor. A vertical flux is a source-normalized four-form class in the source's integral span of products of divisor classes. Under the source's flux quantization, transversality, primitivity, and tadpole conditions, the choice $(a,b)=(329,657)$ with flux parameters $(n_S,n_F)=(1,-1)$ breaks this $G_2$ factor to the abelian gauge group generated by $T=329T_1+657T_2$.

In the source's charge normalization, the adjoint charge set is $q=1,327,328,329,330,657$ together with the conjugate charges. The thesis reports that charge $657$ occurs in a nonzero vectorlike sector of this model; the reported multiplicity uses a separate source calculation. Thus $657$ is the largest charge in this model's displayed charge set, not a bound for all F-theory compactifications. The thesis treats any landscape-wide upper-bound interpretation as heuristic.

The model retains other gauge factors; the source adds flux to break the adjacent $SU(2)$ when decoupling it from the constructed $U(1)$. The thesis also identifies codimension-two $(4,6)$ sectors on other divisors as probably conformal and not fully understood. This example does not claim a complete realistic vacuum.

The flux and tadpole equations here use Li's own $G_4$ notation and normalization. They are not identified with the R3-normalized class $g_4=[G_4/(2\pi\ell_p)^3]$ of the contextual local tadpole carrier without the separate physical field identification and source crosswalk.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** Li's fixed toric fourfold and base, its source-normalized vertical flux and charge conventions, the source-reported flux conditions, and the source data below. The local verification checks the source's stated arithmetic; it does not establish the physical field/source crosswalk.

[A1] Li's source data: $\widehat Y_4$ is its fixed resolved fourfold, the base divisor is denoted $D^{19}$ and is locally $\mathbb F_0$ with a $G_2$ factor, and vertical flux lies in the source's integral span of products of divisor classes in $H^{2,2}_{\mathrm{vert}}(\widehat Y_4)$. The flux-breaking ansatz uses coprime integers $(a,b)$ and parameters $n_S,n_F$. For $b$ divisible by $3$, the source permits integer $n_S,n_F$ and gives $\phi_{1S}=(a-2b/3)n_S$, $\phi_{2S}=(2a-b)n_S$. The source's generator is $T=aT_1+bT_2$, and its adjoint charges are $a$, $b$, $3a-b$, $2a-b$, $a-b$, and $3a-2b$, with conjugates. [Li, §2.2.8 and §§5.4.1–5.4.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.14844v1)

[A2] In Li's own conventions, Eq. (2.62) is $N_{D3}+\tfrac12\int_{\widehat Y_4}G_4\wedge G_4=\chi(\widehat Y_4)/24$ with $N_{D3}\ge0$. For the selected model the thesis gives $\chi(\widehat Y_4)=1{,}737{,}888$. Eq. (5.20) gives $\tfrac12\int_{\widehat Y_4}G_4\wedge G_4=-2(a^2-ab+b^2/3)n_Sn_F$, and Eq. (5.21) imposes $a^2-ab+b^2/3\le 36206$ for the selected model. These are the thesis's source conventions; no physical identification with the R3-normalized class is assumed. [Li, §2.2.8 and §5.4.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.14844v1)

[A3] The thesis separately reports nonzero vectorlike multiplicity at charge $657$ in this model, based on a distinct source calculation; that multiplicity is not derived by the charge arithmetic below. [Li, §5.4.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.14844v1)

## Verification

1.1 Since $657=2\cdot329-1$, $\gcd(329,657)=1$, and $657=3\cdot219$. Thus the source's coprimality and $3\mid b$ conditions hold. With $(n_S,n_F)=(1,-1)$, its flux formula gives $\phi_{1S}=329-2(657/3)=-109$ and $\phi_{2S}=2\cdot329-657=1$, agreeing with the source's displayed choice. [A1, given, algebra]

2.1 The source's quadratic expression is $329^2-329\cdot657+657^2/3=108241-216153+143883=35971$, which is at most $36206$. Its source-normalized flux contribution is therefore $2\cdot35971=71942$. The same thesis gives $\chi(\widehat Y_4)=1737888$, so $\chi/24=72412$ and the source's integrated equation leaves $72412-71942=470$ units of its residual space-filling-brane charge. This verifies only the thesis's arithmetic in its own conventions. [A2, step 1.1, algebra]

2.2 Substituting $(a,b)=(329,657)$ into the six source charges gives $329$, $657$, $987-657=330$, $658-657=1$, $329-657=-328$, and $987-1314=-327$. Taking absolute values yields exactly $1,327,328,329,330,657$, whose maximum is $657$. The thesis separately reports the nonzero vectorlike multiplicity in [A3]; this arithmetic does not prove that multiplicity or any universal upper bound. [A1, A3, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 The locally verified content is the fixed model and source-unit arithmetic in [A1]–[A2]. These source data do not identify Li's $G_4$ with an independently normalized R3 class. The calculation establishes no universal charge bound or complete vacuum. [A1, A2, step 2.1, step 2.2] ∎
