## Statement

Assume $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$ for the cotangent supplier. Let $(M,g)$ be a smooth Lorentzian manifold with canonical cotangent coordinates $(x^a,p_a)$ and $C_m(x,p)=\tfrac12(g^{ab}p_ap_b+m^2c^2)$ for fixed $m\ge0$. For any smooth vector field $\xi$, set $F_\xi=p_a\xi^a(x)$. With canonical bracket $\{F,G\}=F_xG_p-F_pG_x$,

$$\{F_\xi,C_m\}=-\tfrac12(\mathcal L_\xi g^{-1})^{ab}p_ap_b.$$

If $\xi$ is Killing, this bracket vanishes for all cotangent covectors, and $F_\xi$ is constant along every constrained free-shell trajectory generated by $eC_m$ with any smooth multiplier $e$ on its actual interval. No global existence or empirical conservation is asserted.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A smooth nondegenerate Lorentzian metric, cotangent coordinates, constants $m\ge0$, $c>0$, and a smooth vector field $\xi:M\to TM$ with $\xi(x)\in T_xM$. A trajectory generated by $eC_m$ means a smooth curve $z:I\to T^*M$ on an interval $I$, with $C_m(z)=0$ and $z'(\lambda)=e(\lambda)X_{C_m}(z(\lambda))$ for a smooth parameter-only multiplier $e:I\to\mathbb R$.

[A1] [[def-countable-choice]]: assume countable choice $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$ only for the cotangent supplier; no full axiom of choice is used.

[L2] [[thm-rpm-h-canonical-poisson-and-flow]]: the current H0 draft fixes the canonical bracket $F_xG_p-F_pG_x$ and $dF/dt=F_t+\{F,H\}$ on actual solution intervals, with no completeness claim. Its H25 Step 1 record is ready/hash-current, but the carrier remains draft/unbound with ai-altered proof provenance and no independent proof approval.

[F1] Here the Lie derivative of the metric is $\mathcal L_\xi g=\left.\frac d{ds}\right|_{s=0}\Phi_s^*g$, where $\Phi_s$ is the local flow of $\xi$. In coordinates its components are $K_{ab}:=(\mathcal L_\xi g)_{ab}=\xi^c\partial_cg_{ab}+g_{cb}\partial_a\xi^c+g_{ac}\partial_b\xi^c$. A **Killing field** means $K_{ab}=0$ in every chart. This definition requires neither orientation nor time orientation nor dimension four. The inverse metric has Lie derivative $(\mathcal L_\xi g^{-1})^{ab}=\xi^c\partial_cg^{ab}-g^{cb}\partial_c\xi^a-g^{ac}\partial_c\xi^b$.

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct tensor differentiation.

**Given:** The smooth metric, canonical bracket [L2], shell function and generated trajectories specified above.

1.1 In coordinates, $\partial_{x^c}F_\xi=p_a\partial_c\xi^a$, $\partial_{p_c}F_\xi=\xi^c$, $\partial_{p_c}C_m=g^{cb}p_b$, and $\partial_{x^c}C_m=\tfrac12\partial_cg^{ab}p_ap_b$. Therefore [L2] gives $\{F_\xi,C_m\}=p_a(\partial_c\xi^a)g^{cb}p_b-\tfrac12\xi^c(\partial_cg^{ab})p_ap_b=-\tfrac12(\mathcal L_\xi g^{-1})^{ab}p_ap_b$, by [F1] and symmetry of $p_ap_b$. [given, L2, F1, algebra]

2.1 Differentiating $g^{ac}g_{cb}=\delta^a_b$ gives $\partial_e g^{ab}=-g^{ac}g^{bd}\partial_e g_{cd}$. Substitution in the coordinate formula of [F1] gives $(\mathcal L_\xi g^{-1})^{ab}=-g^{ac}g^{bd}K_{cd}$. If $\xi$ is Killing, $K_{cd}=0$ by definition, so $\mathcal L_\xi g^{-1}=0$. Step 1.1 then gives $\{F_\xi,C_m\}=0$ at every cotangent covector. [F1, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Along any generated trajectory in the stated sense, the chain rule and [L2] give $\frac d{d\lambda}F_\xi=dF_\xi(eX_{C_m})=e\{F_\xi,C_m\}$ because $F_\xi$ has no explicit parameter dependence. This vanishes by step 2.1 throughout the solution's actual interval, and hence $F_\xi$ is constant there. The calculation is local in charts and thus applies along the whole interval, with no completeness assumption. [given, L2, A1, step 2.1] ∎
