## Example

Assume countable choice $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$, meaning that every countable family of nonempty sets has a choice function, for the supplied gauge datum. Use $M=\mathbb R^{1,3}$, coordinates $x^0=ct$, metric $\eta=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,1,1)$, and natural units $c=\hbar=1$. Let $SU(2)=\{u\in GL_2(\mathbb C):u^\dagger u=I,\det u=1\}$ act on a fixed trivial right principal gauge bundle, and let $\phi\in C_c^\infty(M,\mathbb R)$ be an even real Lorentz-scalar of engineering weight one in the trivial $SU(2)$ representation. Let the global group $\mathbb Z_2=\{1,-1\}$ act by $\phi\mapsto-\phi$. The finite-jet module is the complexification of this real scalar field with conjugation fixing $\phi$.

For the finite-jet quotient use $G_{\mathrm{alg}}=SO(\eta,\mathbb C)\times SL(2,\mathbb C)\times\mu_2$, where $SO(\eta,\mathbb C)=\{L\in GL_4(\mathbb C):L^T\eta L=\eta,\det L=1\}$, $SL(2,\mathbb C)=\{g\in GL_2(\mathbb C):\det g=1\}$, and $\mu_2=\{z\in\mathbb C:z^2=1\}$. The Lorentz factor acts on derivative indices, $SL(2,\mathbb C)$ acts trivially on $\phi$, and $\mu_2$ acts by sign. Here $SO^+(1,3)=\{L\in GL_4(\mathbb R):L^T\eta L=\eta,\det L=1,L^0{}_0\ge1\}$. At the finite field-representation level, the physical group $SO^+(1,3)\times SU(2)\times\mathbb Z_2$ maps to $G_{\mathrm{alg}}$ by $(L,u,\sigma)\mapsto(L,I_2,\sigma)$, where $L$ is included as the same real matrix in $SO(\eta,\mathbb C)$, the gauge factor maps trivially, and $\sigma=\pm1$ is included as the same element of $\mu_2$. Thus each scalar jet transforms by the Lorentz action on its derivative indices, trivially under $SU(2)$, and by sign under $\mathbb Z_2$.

Let $m\ge0$ be a fixed mass spurion of engineering weight one, let $C\in\mathbb R$ be a fixed dimensionless scalar parameter, and let $\Lambda_{\mathrm{op}}>0$ be a fixed coefficient-suppression scale of engineering weight one. Treat $m,C,\Lambda_{\mathrm{op}}$ as constant Lorentz scalars and $SU(2)$ gauge singlets that transform trivially under the physical $\mathbb Z_2$ and under the full $G_{\mathrm{alg}}$-action. Define $\Box=\eta^{\mu\nu}\partial_\mu\partial_\nu$ and adopt the base action

$$S_0[\phi]=-\frac12\int_M\bigl(\partial_\mu\phi\,\partial^\mu\phi+m^2\phi^2\bigr)\,d^4x$$

and add the dimension-six correction

$$S_6[\phi]=\frac{C}{\Lambda_{\mathrm{op}}^2}\int_M\phi^2\partial_\mu\phi\,\partial^\mu\phi\,d^4x.$$

For compactly supported $h$, define the leading Euler derivative by $DS_0[\phi](h)=\int_Mh\mathcal E_0(\phi)\,d^4x$; the calculation below verifies $\mathcal E_0(\phi)=(\Box-m^2)\phi$. Take derivative cutoff $r=2$, maximum field degree $q=4$, retained engineering dimension $D_{\max}=6$, and loop order $L_{\max}=0$. Let the exact declared dimension-six relation subspace be the $G_{\mathrm{alg}}$-invariant span of $\partial_\mu(\phi^3\partial^\mu\phi)$ and $\phi^3\mathcal E_0(\phi)$. All fields and variations have compact support, so the displayed integrations by parts have no boundary term. Retain terms through formal order $\Lambda_{\mathrm{op}}^{-2}$, with $O(\Lambda_{\mathrm{op}}^{-4})$ denoting terms at least quadratic in the formal bookkeeping parameter $\Lambda_{\mathrm{op}}^{-2}$, not a numerical error bound. Then $\phi^2\partial_\mu\phi\,\partial^\mu\phi$ represents $-\frac{m^2}{3}\phi^4$ in this quotient, and the local field substitution $\phi_{\mathrm{old}}=\phi+\frac{C}{3\Lambda_{\mathrm{op}}^2}\phi^3$ changes $S_0+S_6$ to $S_0-\frac{Cm^2}{3\Lambda_{\mathrm{op}}^2}\int_M\phi^4\,d^4x+O(\Lambda_{\mathrm{op}}^{-4})$. This is an action-level statement at the retained order only.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The compact-support scalar model, its stated $S_0$, the formal $\Lambda_{\mathrm{op}}^{-2}$ truncation, and the declared dimension-six relation subspace.

[A1] The action $S_0$ is an adopted classical model functional for this example. No quantum functional measure or physical realization is assumed.

[A2] Every admitted field, variation, and current in the displayed integrations has compact support in $M$; thus the integral of the divergence of the current $\phi^3\partial^\mu\phi$ is zero.

[A3] The bookkeeping is formal through first order in $\epsilon=\Lambda_{\mathrm{op}}^{-2}$. Terms quadratic in $\epsilon$ are denoted $O(\Lambda_{\mathrm{op}}^{-4})$; this notation asserts no analytic convergence or numerical error estimate.

[A4] $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$ is countable choice inherited through the supplied non-Abelian gauge-data construction; no full Axiom of Choice is assumed. [[def-countable-choice]]

[F1] [[def-aqft-operator-basis-physical-data]]: the contract fixes field weights, symmetry representations, coefficient units, the finite retained operator quotient, and the rule that a field-redefinition relation must retain induced higher-order terms.

[F2] [[def-aqft-local-operator-and-dimension]]: local operators are homogeneous finite-jet polynomials with assigned engineering weights; derivatives have weight one and four-dimensional coefficient factors follow the declared power convention.

[F3] [[def-aqft-operator-basis-quotient]]: the quotient is by the explicitly declared invariant relation subspace and makes no claim about undeclared relations.

[F4] [[def-qftx-nonabelian-gauge-data]]: the fixed gauge group is compact $SU(2)$ and the scalar field uses its trivial representation; the finite scalar computation invokes no gauge-field equation.

[A5] The fixed parameters $m,C,\Lambda_{\mathrm{op}}$ have engineering weights $1,0,1$, respectively, and transform trivially under the physical Lorentz, gauge, and $\mathbb Z_2$ actions and under $G_{\mathrm{alg}}$.

## Verification

**Given:** The facts and assumptions above.

1.1 The transformation $\phi\mapsto-\phi$ sends $\partial_\mu\phi\mapsto-\partial_\mu\phi$, so $S_0$, $S_6$, the current $J^\mu:=\phi^3\partial^\mu\phi$, and the density $\phi^3(\Box-m^2)\phi$ are even. The current $J^\mu$ is a Lorentz vector, and its divergence $\partial_\mu J^\mu$ is a Lorentz scalar. Every other spacetime index in the action densities and relation generators is contracted with the invariant metric $\eta$, so these are Lorentz scalars under $SO(\eta,\mathbb C)$. The parameters are invariant, $SL(2,\mathbb C)$ acts trivially on the scalar field and parameters, and the $SU(2)$ action is trivial; hence all listed expressions are also gauge- and algebraic-group invariant. The density $\phi^2\partial_\mu\phi\,\partial^\mu\phi$ has weight $2+2+2=6$, as does $m^2\phi^4$. The divergence generator has weight six because its current has weight five, and $\phi^3\mathcal E_0$ has weight six because $\mathcal E_0=(\Box-m^2)\phi$ has weight three. Thus the declared relation generators are invariant and homogeneous in the dimension-six sector; this step does not yet equate the two operator classes. [given, A1, A4, A5, F1, F2, F3, F4, algebra]

1.2 For a compactly supported variation $h$, differentiating $S_0[\phi+\tau h]$ at $\tau=0$ gives $DS_0[\phi](h)=-\int_M(\partial_\mu\phi\,\partial^\mu h+m^2\phi h)\,d^4x$. Integration by parts, with no boundary term by A2, yields $DS_0[\phi](h)=\int_M h(\Box-m^2)\phi\,d^4x$, so the leading Euler derivative is $\mathcal E_0=(\Box-m^2)\phi$. [given, A1, A2, algebra]

1.3 Put $a=C/(3\Lambda_{\mathrm{op}}^2)$, define $F(\phi)=\phi+a\phi^3$, and define $G(\Phi)=\Phi-a\Phi^3$. These local polynomial maps send $C_c^\infty(M,\mathbb R)$ to itself because they vanish when the field vanishes, and preserve $\mathbb Z_2$. Expanding the cubic terms through first order in $a$ gives $F(G(\Phi))=\Phi-a\Phi^3+a(\Phi-a\Phi^3)^3=\Phi+O(\Lambda_{\mathrm{op}}^{-4})$; the same expansion gives $G(F(\phi))=\phi+O(\Lambda_{\mathrm{op}}^{-4})$. Thus $G$ is a two-sided inverse through the retained order. [given, A3, F1, algebra]

1.4 The product rule gives the local polynomial identity $\partial_\mu(\phi^3\partial^\mu\phi)=3\phi^2\partial_\mu\phi\,\partial^\mu\phi+\phi^3\Box\phi$. Integrating it over $M$ and using compact support from A2 makes the divergence integral zero, so $\int_M\phi^2\partial_\mu\phi\,\partial^\mu\phi\,d^4x=-\frac13\int_M\phi^3\Box\phi\,d^4x$. [given, A2, algebra]

2.1 Taylor expansion of the quadratic functional $S_0$ gives $S_0[F(\phi)]=S_0[\phi]+\frac{C}{3\Lambda_{\mathrm{op}}^2}\int_M\phi^3\mathcal E_0\,d^4x+O(\Lambda_{\mathrm{op}}^{-4})$. The second variation is quadratic in the displacement $F(\phi)-\phi=O(\Lambda_{\mathrm{op}}^{-2})$, so it contributes only at the displayed remainder order. Substituting $F(\phi)$ into $S_6$ changes its integrand first at order $\Lambda_{\mathrm{op}}^{-2}$ inside a term already multiplied by $\Lambda_{\mathrm{op}}^{-2}$; hence $S_6[F(\phi)]=S_6[\phi]+O(\Lambda_{\mathrm{op}}^{-4})$. [given, A3, step 1.2, algebra]

2.2 Since $\Box\phi=m^2\phi+\mathcal E_0$, substituting this identity into the local polynomial identity of step 1.4 gives $3\phi^2\partial_\mu\phi\,\partial^\mu\phi=\partial_\mu(\phi^3\partial^\mu\phi)-m^2\phi^4-\phi^3\mathcal E_0$. The divergence and leading-equation terms are exactly the declared generators, so F3 yields $[\phi^2\partial_\mu\phi\,\partial^\mu\phi]=[-m^2\phi^4/3]$ in the polynomial quotient. This is equality of classes, not pointwise equality of the representatives for arbitrary fields; A2 separately gives the corresponding action-integral identity. [given, A2, F1, F2, F3, step 1.4, step 1.2, algebra]

3.1 By step 2.2, the sum of the order-$\Lambda_{\mathrm{op}}^{-2}$ contributions in step 2.1 is $\frac{C}{3\Lambda_{\mathrm{op}}^2}\int_M\phi^3\mathcal E_0\,d^4x+\frac{C}{\Lambda_{\mathrm{op}}^2}\int_M\phi^2\partial_\mu\phi\,\partial^\mu\phi\,d^4x=-\frac{Cm^2}{3\Lambda_{\mathrm{op}}^2}\int_M\phi^4\,d^4x$. The derivative operator cancels, leaving the stated quartic coefficient shift. If $C=0$, the map is the identity; if $m=0$, the induced quartic shift vanishes; no division by $m$ is used. This establishes only the finite-order action relation and implies no off-shell, quantum-measure, or scattering equivalence. [given, A3, step 2.2, step 1.3, step 2.1, algebra] ∎
