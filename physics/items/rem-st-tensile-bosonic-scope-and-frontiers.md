## Remark

The $D=26$, $a=1$ spectrum and no-ghost claims in this lane concern the standard tensile flat bosonic string. Noncritical/Liouville strings, curved-target sigma models, tensionless or Carrollian strings, ambitwistor strings, and worldsheet-emergence constructions use distinct action or constraint systems and are not logical consequences of this flat tensile model.
