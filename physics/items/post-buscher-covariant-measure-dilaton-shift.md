## Postulate

Adopt the standard covariantly regularized local measure for gauging one free spacelike circle isometry in the Euclidean NS–NS sigma model on a compact closed worldsheet. Here a free circle isometry means a smooth $S^1$ action with trivial stabilizer at every target point (if $u\cdot p=p$ for $u\in S^1$, then $u=1$), generated by a nowhere-vanishing vector field $k$ satisfying $\mathcal L_kG^{(s)}=0$. Choose an adapted dimensionless angular target-space coordinate $y\sim y+2\pi$ in which $k=\partial_y$; require $\partial_yG^{(s)}=0$ and $\partial_y\Phi=0$, and choose a local gauge for the two-form potential $B_2$ in which $\partial_yB_2=0$. In this chart let $G_{yy}>0$ be the length-squared component of the string-frame metric and set $\widehat G_{yy}=G_{yy}/\alpha'$. Integrating out the worldsheet gauge field contributes
$$
-\frac1{8\pi}\int_\Sigma\sqrt h\,R^{(2)}\log\widehat G_{yy}
$$
to the effective curvature coupling. With the Fradkin–Tseytlin coefficient $\frac1{4\pi}\int_\Sigma\sqrt h\,\Phi R^{(2)}$, this measure convention gives the local rule $\Phi'=\Phi-\frac12\log\widehat G_{yy}$. This is adopted leading-order measure data, not a proof of global path-integral or exact conformal-field-theory equivalence.

## Remarks

The determinant is included as adopted physical measure data, not proved locally. Alvarez–Alvarez-Gaumé–Lozano define the regularized determinant by a ratio of differential-operator determinants in equation (2.1.7), identify the gauge-field measure factor in (4.0.9), and evaluate its curvature contribution with the heat-kernel expansion in (4.0.10)–(4.0.16). Their determinant formulas use the sigma-model-normalized, dimensionless isometry metric coefficient: in an $\alpha'=1$ string-unit notation it is $g_{00}$, while in this item's dimensionful string-frame chart it is $\widehat G_{yy}=G^{(s)}_{yy}/\alpha'$. Accordingly the logarithm is of the dimensionless ratio $G^{(s)}_{yy}/\alpha'$, not of a dimensionful metric component. The source's alternative complex-coordinate normalization is stated separately in its §2.1; neither convention changes this conversion. No global gauge-bundle sum, higher-derivative term, string-loop correction, or exact CFT statement is supplied by this postulate.
