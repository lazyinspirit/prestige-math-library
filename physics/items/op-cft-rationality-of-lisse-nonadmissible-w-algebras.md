## Open problem

Let $\mathfrak g$ be a finite-dimensional complex simple Lie algebra, let $B$ be its normalized invariant form, and let $k\in\mathbb C$. Let $\widehat{\mathfrak g}$ be the untwisted affine central extension of $\mathfrak g[t,t^{-1}]$ defined using $B$, with central generator $K$. The universal affine vacuum vertex algebra is
$$V^k(\mathfrak g)=U(\widehat{\mathfrak g})\otimes_{U(\mathfrak g[t]\oplus\mathbb CK)}\mathbb C_k,$$
where $\mathfrak g[t]$ acts trivially on $\mathbb C_k$ and $K$ acts as $k$; let $L_k(\mathfrak g)$ denote its unique simple graded quotient. For a vertex algebra $V$, define
$$C_2(V)=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb C}\{u_{(-2)}v:u,v\in V\},\qquad R_V=V/C_2(V),$$
with the commutative product on $R_V$ induced by $u\cdot v=u_{(-1)}v$, and set $X_V=\operatorname{Specm}(R_V)$, the set of maximal ideals of $R_V$. For the simple affine quotient $L_k(\mathfrak g)$, the current states define the surjective algebra homomorphism
$$\rho_k:S(\mathfrak g)\twoheadrightarrow R_{L_k(\mathfrak g)},\qquad x\longmapsto [x_{-1}\mathbf1].$$
Here $S(\mathfrak g)$ is the symmetric algebra and the bracket denotes the class in $R_{L_k(\mathfrak g)}$. Surjectivity follows because $V^k(\mathfrak g)$ and its quotient $L_k(\mathfrak g)$ are generated by current states. Identify $\operatorname{Specm}S(\mathfrak g)$ with $\mathfrak g^*$. The induced map on maximal spectra identifies $X_{L_k(\mathfrak g)}=\operatorname{Specm}(R_{L_k(\mathfrak g)})$ with the closed subset $V(\ker\rho_k)\subseteq\mathfrak g^*$. Use $B$ to identify $\mathfrak g$ with $\mathfrak g^*$ when viewing nilpotent adjoint orbits there.

Let $G$ be the connected adjoint group of $\mathfrak g$ and let $f\in\mathfrak g$ be nilpotent, meaning $(\operatorname{ad}f)^m=0$ for some integer $m\ge1$. Choose a semisimple grading element inducing a decomposition $\mathfrak g=\bigoplus_{j\in\frac12\mathbb Z}\mathfrak g_j$ with $[\mathfrak g_i,\mathfrak g_j]\subseteq\mathfrak g_{i+j}$ and $B(\mathfrak g_i,\mathfrak g_j)=0$ unless $i+j=0$. Require $f\in\mathfrak g_{-1}$ and that $\operatorname{ad}f:\mathfrak g_j\to\mathfrak g_{j-1}$ is injective for $j\ge\frac12$ and surjective for $j\le\frac12$. On $\mathfrak g_{1/2}$ set $\omega_f(u,v)=B(f,[u,v])$, choose a maximal isotropic subspace $\ell$ for $\omega_f$, and put
$$\mathfrak m=\ell\oplus\bigoplus_{j\ge1}\mathfrak g_j,\qquad \chi=B(f,\cdot)|_{\mathfrak m}.$$
Choose a basis $u_1,\ldots,u_d$ of $\mathfrak m$ with $[u_i,u_j]=\sum_s c_{ij}^s u_s$. Use the charged-ghost Fock superalgebra $F(\mathfrak m)$ generated by odd fields
$$\psi_i(z)=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}\psi_{i,n}z^{-n-1},\qquad \psi_i^*(z)=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}\psi^*_{i,n}z^{-n},$$
whose modes satisfy $\{\psi_{i,m},\psi_{j,n}\}=\{\psi^*_{i,m},\psi^*_{j,n}\}=0$ and $\{\psi_{i,m},\psi^*_{j,n}\}=\delta_{ij}\delta_{m+n,0}$; its vacuum is killed by $\psi_{i,n}$ for $n\ge0$ and $\psi^*_{i,n}$ for $n\ge1$. Give currents degree $0$, $\psi_i$ degree $-1$, and $\psi_i^*$ degree $+1$. Write $J^{u_i}(z)=\sum_n (u_i)_n z^{-n-1}$ for the affine current of $u_i$. With the charged-ghost normal ordering, the BRST state is
$$Q(z)=\sum_i (J^{u_i}(z)-\chi(u_i))\psi_i^*(z)-\frac12\sum_{i,j,s}c_{ij}^s:\psi_i^*(z)\psi_j^*(z)\psi_s(z):.$$
The charged-ghost BRST construction proves $Q_{(0)}^2=0$. Thus $C^k(\mathfrak g,f)=V^k(\mathfrak g)\otimes F(\mathfrak m)$ is a cochain complex with degree-zero cohomology $W^k(\mathfrak g,f)=H^0(C^k(\mathfrak g,f),Q_{(0)})$.

Assume $H^0(C^k(\mathfrak g,f),Q_{(0)})$ is nonzero and a separate conformal-structure construction supplies a specified conformal vector $\omega_{\mathrm{DS}}$; write $Y(\omega_{\mathrm{DS}},z)=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}L(n)z^{-n-2}$, require $[L(m),L(n)]=(m-n)L(m+n)+(m^3-m)\delta_{m+n,0}c_{\mathrm{DS}}I/12$ for some $c_{\mathrm{DS}}\in\mathbb C$, and require $L(-1)=T$. Let $J_k$ be the specified homogeneous maximal proper vertex ideal defining the source's simple quotient, and set $q_k:H^0(C^k(\mathfrak g,f),Q_{(0)})\twoheadrightarrow W_k(\mathfrak g,f)=H^0(C^k(\mathfrak g,f),Q_{(0)})/J_k$. Require that $q_k$ descend $\omega_{\mathrm{DS}}$. Give $W_k$ the induced conformal grading and assume it is of CFT type: $W_k=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}(W_k)_n$, where $(W_k)_n=\{w:L(0)w=nw\}$, each weight space is finite dimensional, and $(W_k)_0=\mathbb C\mathbf1$. This makes lisse mean that $R_{W_k}$ is finite dimensional and uses rationality in the admissible-module convention where every admissible module is an internal direct sum of irreducible admissible submodules with its specified grading inherited by the summands.

Let $\mathcal O=G\cdot f$ be the nilpotent adjoint orbit, viewed in $\mathfrak g^*$ through $B$, and let $\overline{\mathcal O}$ denote its Zariski closure. Suppose $X_{L_k(\mathfrak g)}=\overline{\mathcal O}$ and $W_k(\mathfrak g,f)$ is lisse. Must $W_k(\mathfrak g,f)$ be rational?
