## Open problem

Let $X$ be a smooth projective complex variety of complex dimension $n$, and let $p$ be an integer with $0\le p\le n$. Let $Z^p(X)$ be the free abelian group on irreducible closed subvarieties of codimension $p$, with rational equivalence generated by divisors of nonzero rational functions on irreducible codimension-$(p-1)$ subvarieties. Set $\mathrm{CH}^p(X)_{\mathbb Q}:=(Z^p(X)/{\sim_{\mathrm{rat}}})\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb Q$. For each irreducible subvariety, take the Poincaré-dual cohomology class of its fundamental homology class, and extend linearly to the cycle-class map $\mathrm{cl}:\mathrm{CH}^p(X)_{\mathbb Q}\to H^{2p}(X;\mathbb Q)$. Write $H^{2p}(X;\mathbb C)=\bigoplus_{a+b=2p}H^{a,b}(X)$ for the Hodge decomposition and $H^{p,p}(X)_{\mathbb Q}:=\{\alpha\in H^{2p}(X;\mathbb Q):\alpha\otimes1\in H^{p,p}(X)\}$. Is every $\alpha\in H^{p,p}(X)_{\mathbb Q}$ in the image of $\mathrm{cl}$?

## Status

As of 2026-11-04 the Clay Mathematics Institute lists the Hodge conjecture as unsolved. The official description records known special cases, including the low-dimensional cases, and states that the general problem remains open. This item is a precise conjectural question, not a proof premise; the proved algebraicity theorem for Hodge loci is a separate claim.
