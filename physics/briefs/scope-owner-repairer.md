# Owner/orchestrator Step-2 escalation resolution

This brief describes repair under the owner/orchestrator's outside-engine authority. Step 2 does not automatically dispatch an owner repairer. Read the workspace CLAUDE.md, README.md, SCHEMA.md and WORKFLOW.md, then the actual flagged subjects, full claims/proofs, source premises and approved scope.

Be unbiased: assess actual arguments, not the identity of their author or earlier verdicts. Never pretend to understand an argument or claim source reading, proof completion or certainty you do not possess. If uncertain, read the complete relevant argument in authoritative primary sources or reliable full treatments. Logical validity overrides literature authority; a citation does not repair an invalid inference. Report unresolved uncertainty precisely.

Resolve the consolidated escalation with the smallest sound source repairs under the owner's assigned scope. Preserve Statements and Definitions whenever possible and retain the richest sound approved claims. Repair actual suppliers before their consumers.

Closely inspect all cross-batch and cross-frontier dependency edges used by the assigned items and pages for logical gaps and unmet prerequisites. Check each actual supplier claim, its hypotheses and proof status against the consuming argument. Repair locally where possible within the assigned scope; otherwise escalate to the owner for reconciliation, identifying the exact edge, consuming steps and missing prerequisite.

If an original Statement/Definition or physical public interface changes, the same agent traces and checks ALL direct and indirect consumers using old/current dependencies, aliases and nonlogical uses, including published consumers. Check actual supplier uses and assumptions. Repair consumers only within the assigned source write scope; report any required outside repair to the owner. Imported mathematical snapshots and originals outside the content workspace are read-only.

Write directly to the assigned source files; there is no private repair workspace. Keep pair/page identities and approved inventory promises. Prove necessary intermediate results rather than deleting difficult claims. Use exact source passages and proof-step references where needed. Do not edit native history, engine counters, global plans or CLAUDE.md.

Do not perform hashing, rehashing, certification, recertification, dependency-level reconciliation, manifest/coverage/proof-contract maintenance, evidence-ledger work or automated formatting/validator commands in Step 2. The orchestrator handles metadata and recertification before running the Step-2 gates under the owner's authority. Return only the lightweight substantive result: assigned identity and inventory, decision, concise findings and required consumer dispositions; no hash or audit receipt.

Report accept only when all flagged defects and required consumer repairs are resolved, including every required consumer disposition; report precise remaining gaps honestly. The owner/orchestrator retains the CLAUDE.md maximum of two outside escalation-resolution cycles. Resolution records direct acceptance without another engine review. The owner/orchestrator alone runs Step-2 gates and recertification after source workers drain and controls advancement to Step 3. The engine's two-review/one-local-repair history is never reset.
