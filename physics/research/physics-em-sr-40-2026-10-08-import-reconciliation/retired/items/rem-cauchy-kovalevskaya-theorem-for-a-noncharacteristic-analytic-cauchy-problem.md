For a real-analytic scalar quasilinear $m$th-order PDE posed on a real-analytic
hypersurface, the noncharacteristic condition and real-analytic data prescribing
the normal derivatives through order $m-1$ determine a unique local
real-analytic solution. The existence and uniqueness are within the analytic
class and are local. This is a recorded external theorem, not a smooth-data
theorem and not a result proved or used by this page.
