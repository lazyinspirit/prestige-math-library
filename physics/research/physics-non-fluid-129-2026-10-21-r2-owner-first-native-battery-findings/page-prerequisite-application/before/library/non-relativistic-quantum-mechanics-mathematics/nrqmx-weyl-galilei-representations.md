This page gives finite-dimensional regular Weyl uniqueness with its exact finite-phase-space, nonzero-parameter, irreducibility, and regularity hypotheses. It derives the translation/boost cocycle and its central extension, then treats the spin-cover action and the trace-functional identity relative to a declared block-diagonal algebra. It asserts neither infinite-mode uniqueness nor a universal physical mass-superselection rule.
