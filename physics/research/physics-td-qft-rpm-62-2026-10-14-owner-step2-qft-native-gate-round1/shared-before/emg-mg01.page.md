## Scope

This mathematical page fixes the oriented, time-oriented Lorentzian four-manifold and the local field, observer, worldline, coordinate, and tetrad conventions used in the Einstein–Maxwell mathematics sequence. The preceding [[gr-connections-and-free-fall]] page supplies the metric-selected connection and the precise timelike-curve and proper-time definitions; the items here use only its mathematical geometry, not its physical clock or free-fall postulate.

The observer congruence, an adapted coordinate chart, and an orthonormal tetrad are defined as different geometric objects. Local tetrads exist, but the page assumes no global tetrad and no hypersurface-orthogonality of an observer congruence. The Lorentzian Hodge star is built from the metric and the independent manifold orientation. Its sign is proved with the one negative direction of the metric. The final two lemmas derive the all-degree conformal multiplier and reconstruct the electric-like and magnetic-like observer covectors and their scalar invariants from an arbitrary smooth two-form.

All results on this page are mathematical statements. No Maxwell equation, physical postulate, measurement, or empirical claim is used.
