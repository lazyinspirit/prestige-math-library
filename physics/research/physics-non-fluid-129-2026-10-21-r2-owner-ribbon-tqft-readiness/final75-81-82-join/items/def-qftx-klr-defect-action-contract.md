## Definition

Fix a field $\Bbbk$, a finite color set $I$, a symmetrizable Cartan matrix $A=(a_{ij})_{i,j\in I}$, and positive integers $d_i$ satisfying $d_i a_{ij}=d_j a_{ji}$. Here $a_{ii}=2$, $a_{ij}\le0$ for $i\ne j$, and $a_{ij}=0$ exactly when $a_{ji}=0$. Fix a weight lattice $X$ (a free abelian group), simple roots $\alpha_i\in X$, and integer-valued pairings $\langle\lambda,\alpha_i^\vee\rangle$ with $\langle\alpha_j,\alpha_i^\vee\rangle=a_{ij}$. Choose polynomials $Q_{ij}(u,v)\in\Bbbk[u,v]$ with $Q_{ii}=0$, $Q_{ij}(u,v)=Q_{ji}(v,u)$, and $Q_{ij}\ne0$ for $i\ne j$; each $Q_{ij}$ is homogeneous for $\deg u=2d_i$, $\deg v=2d_j$, of weighted degree $-2d_i a_{ij}$.

For $\nu=\sum_{i\in I}m_i i\in\mathbb N[I]$ of height $r=\sum_i m_i$, a **quiver Hecke algebra** $R_Q(\nu)$ is the graded $\Bbbk$-algebra generated by idempotents $e(\mathbf i)$ for sequences $\mathbf i=(i_1,\ldots,i_r)$ with content $\nu$, dots $y_1,\ldots,y_r$, and crossings $\psi_1,\ldots,\psi_{r-1}$. Put $s_k\mathbf i=(i_1,\ldots,i_{k+1},i_k,\ldots,i_r)$. The defining relations are

$$
e(\mathbf i)e(\mathbf j)=\delta_{\mathbf i,\mathbf j}e(\mathbf i),\qquad \sum_{\mathbf i}e(\mathbf i)=1,\qquad y_a e(\mathbf i)=e(\mathbf i)y_a,\qquad y_a y_b=y_b y_a,
$$

$$
\psi_k e(\mathbf i)=e(s_k\mathbf i)\psi_k,\qquad \psi_k y_a=y_a\psi_k\ \ (a\ne k,k+1),
$$

$$
(\psi_k y_{k+1}-y_k\psi_k)e(\mathbf i)=(y_{k+1}\psi_k-\psi_k y_k)e(\mathbf i)=\delta_{i_k,i_{k+1}}e(\mathbf i),\qquad \psi_k^2e(\mathbf i)=Q_{i_k,i_{k+1}}(y_k,y_{k+1})e(\mathbf i),
$$

$$
\psi_k\psi_l=\psi_l\psi_k\ \ (|k-l|>1),\qquad (\psi_{k+1}\psi_k\psi_{k+1}-\psi_k\psi_{k+1}\psi_k)e(\mathbf i)=\begin{cases}\displaystyle\frac{Q_{i_k,i_{k+1}}(y_{k+2},y_{k+1})-Q_{i_k,i_{k+1}}(y_k,y_{k+1})}{y_{k+2}-y_k}e(\mathbf i),&i_k=i_{k+2}\ne i_{k+1},\\0,&\text{otherwise.}\end{cases}
$$

The displayed quotient is a polynomial because its numerator vanishes when $y_{k+2}=y_k$. The degrees are $\deg e(\mathbf i)=0$, $\deg(y_k e(\mathbf i))=2d_{i_k}$, and $\deg(\psi_k e(\mathbf i))=-d_{i_k}a_{i_k i_{k+1}}$.

For this definition, a small additive $\Bbbk$-linear graded abelian category has a set of objects and morphisms, finite biproducts, kernels and cokernels with every monomorphism a kernel and every epimorphism a cokernel, $\Bbbk$-vector-space Hom groups with bilinear composition, and a decomposition of each Hom space into integer degrees with composition adding degrees. An exact functor is a $\Bbbk$-linear functor preserving short exact sequences. An adjunction $F\dashv E$ is specified by natural unit and counit maps satisfying the two triangle identities. A natural transformation is a family of morphisms commuting with every source morphism; homogeneous degree $s$ means each component has internal degree $s$. For an abelian category $\mathcal C$, its exact Grothendieck group $K_0(\mathcal C)$ is generated by isomorphism classes $[M]$ with relations $[B]=[A]+[C]$ for each short exact sequence $0\to A\to B\to C\to0$.

A **KLR categorical action contract** specifies, for each $\lambda\in X$, such a small additive $\Bbbk$-linear graded abelian category $\mathcal C_\lambda$, exact functors $F_i:\mathcal C_\lambda\to\mathcal C_{\lambda-\alpha_i}$ with specified exact right adjoints $E_i$, and homogeneous natural transformations $x_i:F_i\Rightarrow F_i$ of degree $2d_i$ and $\tau_{ij}:F_iF_j\Rightarrow F_jF_i$ of degree $-d_i a_{ij}$. For each $\nu$ and $\lambda$, the direct sum of composite functors $F_{i_1}\cdots F_{i_r}$ over sequences of content $\nu$ must carry an action of $R_Q(\nu)$: $e(\mathbf i)$ is projection to the indicated sequence summand, $y_k$ acts by the dot on the $k$th factor, and $\psi_k$ acts by the crossing on adjacent factors, all with the relations displayed above. Functor-composition order and grading shifts must be fixed in the proposed action and used consistently.

This contract records required data and checks; it does not assert that any such categories, functors, or transformations exist. A claim of an **integrable** action additionally states and proves local nilpotence: for every object $M$ and color $i$, some powers $E_i^N(M)$ and $F_i^N(M)$ vanish. A claimed highest-weight action additionally specifies a weight $\lambda$, a chosen object in $\mathcal C_\lambda$ killed by every $E_i$, and the precise subcategory generated from it by the $F_i$, shifts, finite sums, and summands. To assert that the induced operators on $K_0(\mathcal C):=\bigoplus_{\lambda\in X}K_0(\mathcal C_\lambda)$ form a representation of $U_q(\mathfrak g)$, the contract must identify the exact Grothendieck-group convention, establish every required quantum-group relation, and cite a decategorification theorem whose hypotheses match the specified categories and functors. Merely satisfying the positive KLR relations does not establish those extra conclusions.

A physical interpretation as line-changing defects requires a separately specified defect 2-category, whose objects are declared defect sectors, whose 1-morphisms are declared defects changing sectors, and whose 2-morphisms are declared junction maps. A 2-functor here assigns objects, functors, and natural transformations while preserving identities and both compositions; a weak target additionally requires its coherence isomorphisms and equations. No physical defect realization is part of this mathematical contract.

## Diagrammatic convention

The diagram depicts the generating 2-morphism that exchanges adjacent functor factors. The lower labels are the source order $(i,j)$ and the upper labels the target order $(j,i)$; time/composition is read upward. The crossing is the formal natural transformation $\tau_{ij}$, not an invertible topological braid crossing. The overpass convention is only a drawing convention and carries no extra algebraic relation; the displayed KLR relations, not the picture, define the action.

## Remarks

The Khovanov–Lauda and Rouquier presentations use convention choices for root data, grading shifts, and polynomial parameters. The data above fixes one symmetrizable Cartan convention explicitly. A proposed action in another convention must provide a checked translation to these formulas before it can be used as a supplier.
