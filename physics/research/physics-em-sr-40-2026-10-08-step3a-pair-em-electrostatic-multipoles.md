- `pthm-em-moment-origin-change` (physics-theorem, level 17): reference-point moments; `Q^(b)=Q`, `p^(b)=p−Qb` (invariance exactly when `Q=0`), the full subset expansion of every Cartesian moment (with the displayed `l=2` instance), and `Q^(b)_ij = Q_ij − 3(b_ip_j+b_jp_i) + 2(b·p)δ_ij + (3b_ib_j−|b|²δ_ij)Q`, with reference-point independence of the traceless quadrupole when `Q=p=0`; covers density and finite-point branches.
- `pthm-em-electric-dipole-expansion` (physics-theorem, level 17): uniform monopole-dipole potential expansion `φ(x)=Q/(4πε₀r)+p·x/(4πε₀r³)+R(x)` with `|R(x)| ≤ 16a²‖ρ‖₁/(4πε₀r³)` for `r ≥ 2a`; leading-term form; physical reading through the `L^1` branch of the Coulomb solution; explicit exclusions (no derivative estimate for `R`, no field expansion, nothing inside `r<2a`).
- `pthm-em-electric-general-multipoles` (physics-theorem, level 18): spherical moments `q_lm = ∫|y|^lY_lm(y/|y|)ρ`; the exterior all-order series with absolute/uniform convergence on `|x| ≥ r_0 > a` and the geometric tail bound; degree-0/1 terms identified with the monopole and dipole terms plus the supplier remainder; the degree-2 term identified with `n_iQ_ijn_j/(8πε₀r³)`; `C^∞` and termwise differentiation with the field series on `{r>a}`; separate source-free ball boundary branch (L² data, Poisson kernel for continuous data, uniqueness in the bounded-radial-L² class).
- `pthm-em-electric-dipole-torque` (physics-theorem, level 18): prescribed external field datum Lipschitz on the support; explicitly adopted, declared extension of the smooth continuum coupling `f_ext=ρE_ext` (self-field, mechanical support and back-reaction excluded); `F = QE_ext(0)+R_F`, `|R_F| ≤ La‖ρ‖₁`; `τ = p×E_ext(0)+R_τ`, `|R_τ| ≤ La²‖ρ‖₁`; neutral case; reference-point shift `τ_o=τ−o×F`; ideal pair of test charges at rest with the explicitly adopted finite force assignment and bounds `2|q|La`, `2|q|La²`; conditional covariant at-rest specialization with the exact tensor sign check `q'F^{i0}U_0=q'E_i`.
