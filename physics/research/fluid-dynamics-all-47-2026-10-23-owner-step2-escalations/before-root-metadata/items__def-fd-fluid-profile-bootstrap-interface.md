## Definition

Let $H=L^2([0,1],\mathcal B,\lambda;\mathbb R)$ be the real Hilbert space of almost-everywhere classes of square-integrable real profiles, with $\langle u,v\rangle_H=\int_0^1u(\xi)v(\xi)\,d\xi$, where $\mathcal B$ is generated by intervals with rational endpoints and $\lambda$ is Lebesgue measure restricted to $\mathcal B$. Let $(\Omega,\mathcal F,\mathbb P)$ and $(S,\mathcal S,\nu)$ be probability spaces, let $(\delta_t)_{t\in\mathbb Z}$ be independent identically distributed measurable $S$-valued random elements on $\Omega$ with law $\nu$, and let $f:S^{\mathbb N_0}\to H$ be measurable from the product sigma-algebra $\mathcal S^{\otimes\mathbb N_0}$ to the Borel sigma-algebra of $H$. The associated causal profile process is $X_t=f(\delta_t,\delta_{t-1},\ldots)$. Assume $X_0$ is Bochner integrable and has Bochner mean $\mathbb E_BX_0:=\int_\Omega X_0(\omega)\,d\mathbb P(\omega)=0$, with $\mathbb E\|X_0\|_H^2<\infty$. Assume Countable Choice and Dependent Choice and take an independent product extension carrying the countable replacement-innovation family.

For every $t\in\mathbb Z$, $m\ge1$, and $k\le t-m$, let $\delta'_{t,m,k}$ have law $\nu$, with all these copies mutually independent and independent of the original innovations. Set

$$X_{t,m}=f(\delta_t,\ldots,\delta_{t-m+1},\delta'_{t,m,t-m},\delta'_{t,m,t-m-1},\ldots),\qquad \delta_m=\bigl(\mathbb E\|X_0-X_{0,m}\|_H^2\bigr)^{1/2}.$$

The profile process is $L^2$-$m$-approximable when $\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}\delta_m<\infty$. For data $X_1,\ldots,X_n\in H$, $n\ge1$, and an integer block length $1\le b\le n$, moving-block resampling independently draws starts uniformly from $\{1,\ldots,n-b+1\}$, concatenates the corresponding ordered length-$b$ blocks, and truncates the last block to obtain exactly $n$ resampled elements $X_1^*,\ldots,X_n^*$. Define $\bar X_n^*=n^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^nX_j^*$, and let $\mathbb E^*$ denote expectation over these draws with the observations fixed. For any bounded real-linear functional $L:H\to\mathbb R$, let $\|L\|=\sup_{\|u\|_H\le1}|L(u)|$; the centered resampled scalar profile summary is $\sqrt n\,L(\bar X_n^*-\mathbb E^*\bar X_n^*)$. This definition specifies the data space and resampling law; it does not assert asymptotic validity.

## Remarks

When a measured or simulated profile $q(z)$ on a physical segment $z\in(a,a+\ell)$ is represented in $H$, use $X(\xi)=\sqrt{\ell}\,q(a+\ell\xi)$, so $\int_a^{a+\ell}|q(z)|^2\,dz=\int_0^1|X(\xi)|^2\,d\xi$. If $z$ has units of length and $q$ has units $Q$, then $X$ has units $Q\sqrt{\mathrm{length}}$, since $\xi$ is dimensionless. Any physical scalar sensor must be transformed with the profile and carry its corresponding units. Resolution, instrument averaging, shared calibration error, and finite-data uncertainty are not removed by this mathematical representation.
