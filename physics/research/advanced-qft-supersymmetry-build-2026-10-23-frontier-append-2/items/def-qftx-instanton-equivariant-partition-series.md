## Definition

Fix integers $N\geq2$ and $k\geq0$ and a real stability parameter $\zeta>0$. Let $V=\mathbb C^k$ and $W=\mathbb C^N$ with Hermitian structures. The positive-FI stable complex ADHM quotient $X_{k,N}^\zeta$ is the geometric quotient by $GL(V)$ of quadruples $(B_1,B_2,I,J)$ with $B_i\in\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb C}(V)$, $I:W\to V$, and $J:V\to W$, satisfying $[B_1,B_2]+IJ=0$ and the condition that the vectors $B_{i_1}\cdots B_{i_m}I(w)$ span $V$ for all words of length $m\geq0$ and $w\in W$. This is the positive stability chamber; it is not, by definition, the zero-FI regular quotient or the moduli space of anti-self-dual connections.

Let $T_{\mathbb C}=(\mathbb C^\times)^2\times(\mathbb C^\times)^N$ act algebraically, and let $K=(S^1)^2\times(S^1)^N$ be its maximal compact subgroup. The action is $(t_1,t_2,h)\cdot(B_1,B_2,I,J)=(t_1B_1,t_2B_2,Ih^{-1},t_1t_2hJ)$, where $h$ acts on $W$ with weights $e_\alpha^{-1}$. Thus $\mathbf W=\sum_{\alpha=1}^N e_\alpha^{-1}$ and $\mathbf W^\vee=\sum_{\alpha=1}^N e_\alpha$. Write $\epsilon_i=c_1^K(t_i)$ and $a_\alpha=c_1^K(e_\alpha)$; each has mass dimension one in units $c=\hbar=1$. Let $q$ be a dimensionless formal charge-counting variable.

Assume the complexification of this action gives each $X_{k,N}^\zeta$ a smooth separated finite-type complex structure of dimension $2kN$, a finite invariant affine atlas, and a finite proper fixed locus $F_{k,N}$. Extend the complex torus to $T_m=T_{\mathbb C}\times\mathbb C_m^\times$ with maximal compact subgroup $K_m=K\times S_m^1$; the flavor factor acts trivially on $X_{k,N}^\zeta$. Let $\widetilde m$ be the degree-two equivariant first Chern class of its defining flavor character, let $L_{-1}$ be the associated line of inverse flavor character, and set
$$\mathcal E_{\widetilde m}=c_{2kN}^{T_m}(TX_{k,N}^\zeta\otimes L_{-1})\in H_{T_m}^{4kN}(X_{k,N}^\zeta;\mathbb Q).$$
At a fixed point with tangent weights $w_1,\ldots,w_{2kN}$, its restriction is $\prod_{j=1}^{2kN}(w_j-\widetilde m)$. Let $S_{T_m}$ be generated by the first Chern classes of nontrivial integral characters of $T_m$, put $R_{T_m}=H^*(BK_m;\mathbb Q)=\mathbb Q[\epsilon_1,\epsilon_2,a_1,\ldots,a_N,\widetilde m]$, and let $K_{N,m}=\operatorname{Frac}(R_{T_m})$. When the character-localized Borel–Moore concentration map and proper fixed-locus trace define $p_{k,*}^{\mathrm{loc}}$, set
$$I_{k,N}^{\mathrm{adj}}(\widetilde m)=p_{k,*}^{\mathrm{loc}}\bigl(\mathcal E_{\widetilde m}\cap[X_{k,N}^\zeta]_{K_m}\bigr)\in S_{T_m}^{-1}R_{T_m}\subset K_{N,m}.$$
This is a localized pushforward, not an ordinary pushforward along a nonproper map. The concentration isomorphism and trace are required inputs and are not asserted to exist by this definition.

When these data are supplied for every $k\geq0$, define the coefficientwise formal series
$$Z_{N,\mathrm{adj}}^{\mathrm{loc}}(q;\widetilde m)=\sum_{k\geq0}q^k I_{k,N}^{\mathrm{adj}}(\widetilde m)\in K_{N,m}\langle\!\langle q\rangle\!\rangle.$$
It has no asserted analytic convergence. In the source's Euclidean convention the real mass $m_E$ has mass dimension one, $m=i m_E$, and the equivariant mass is $\widetilde m=m+(\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2)/2$. For the adopted round $S^4_r$ model, $N=2$, $\epsilon_1=\epsilon_2=r^{-1}$, and hence $\widetilde m=i m_E+r^{-1}$. This mass shift is a source-qualified convention. The S4 postulate assumes a supersymmetry-compatible noncommutative regulator agreeing near each pole with the flat-space regulator; it does not construct that regulator or a continuum path-integral measure.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** $N\geq2$, $k\geq0$, $\zeta>0$, the smooth positive-FI ADHM quotient, the compact torus action, and the localized Borel–Moore data stipulated in the Definition.

[F1] [[def-qftx-localized-equivariant-borel-moore-data]]: normalized equivariant Borel–Moore groups use the ordinary dualizing object without a quotient-stack shift, the smooth complex-oriented fundamental class has degree $2d$, and equivariant cohomology acts by cap product.

[F2] [[def-qftx-proper-fixed-locus-localized-pushforward]]: an allowed insertion is a homogeneous equivariant cohomology class capped with the complex-oriented Borel–Moore fundamental class, and the localized pushforward is the proper trace after the fixed-inclusion concentration inverse.

[A1] The source-qualified mass convention $m=i m_E$ and $\widetilde m=m+(\epsilon_1+\epsilon_2)/2$ is used for the N=2* insertion. This states the formal equivariant mass convention and does not assert a continuum hypermultiplet determinant identity.

[L1] [[post-qftx-s4-n2-localization-model]]: the current Statement fixes one adjoint hypermultiplet, gauge group $U(2)$, real Euclidean mass $m_E$, dimensionless $q=e^{2\pi i\tau}$, $\epsilon_1=\epsilon_2=r^{-1}$, and the assumed noncommutative S4 regulator. Its formal regulator and contour remain source-qualified model inputs.

## Verification

1.1 The complex tangent bundle has rank $2kN$, so its top Chern class after tensoring by the flavor line has cohomological degree $2(2kN)=4kN$. At a fixed point, tensoring a tangent character of additive weight $w_j$ by the inverse flavor character changes its weight to $w_j-\widetilde m$, giving the stated restriction of $\mathcal E_{\widetilde m}$. [given, algebra]

2.1 The complex-oriented fundamental class has normalized Borel–Moore degree $4kN$. Capping it with $\mathcal E_{\widetilde m}$ of degree $4kN$ gives a degree-zero Borel–Moore class, so the stipulated localized proper trace has scalar coefficients in $S_{T_m}^{-1}R_{T_m}$. [F1, F2, given, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 In the current S4 postulate the gauge group is $U(2)$, the mass is real Euclidean $m_E$, and the two pole rotation weights are $r^{-1}$. Applying the source convention from the Definition gives $\widetilde m=i m_E+(r^{-1}+r^{-1})/2=i m_E+r^{-1}$, so this is the specialization used in the displayed U(2) series. [L1, A1, step 2.1, algebra] ∎
