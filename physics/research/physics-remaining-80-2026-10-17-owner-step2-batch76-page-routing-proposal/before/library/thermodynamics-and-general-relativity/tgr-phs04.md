This page combines a zero-mode-excluded ideal photon Gibbs/ray model with a restricted Schwarzschild stationary-family entropy input and an adopted fixed-asymptotic-reference mass balance. The positive-cutoff mass ODE, coarse entropy export, and internal quasistatic diagnostic are exact conditional model results. The diagnostic is not an error bound against an exact Einstein–quantum backreaction solution; the effective area is not a material patch on the null horizon, and no Hawking-collapse theorem, measured flux, or universal generalized second law is claimed.
