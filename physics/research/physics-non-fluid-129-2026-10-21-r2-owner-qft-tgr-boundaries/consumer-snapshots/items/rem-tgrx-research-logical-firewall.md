## Remark

A label such as “research frontier,” a reported source result, and an adopted physical postulate have different logical roles. The fixed-coupling equation is an effective model below its cutoff; it does not assert existence, uniqueness, or stability of a solution. Wall's GSL proof states horizon-algebra, semiclassical, and renormalized-entropy hypotheses.

Kirklin proves an exact modified inequality for von Neumann entropies of idealized dynamical quantum-reference-frame cuts in perturbative gravity; the free energy of the later cut remains in the bound. Its usual-GSL reduction is approximate, at leading order in a UV-cutoff semiclassical limit, and requires additional conditions that make that free energy nonnegative. This does not establish regulator independence for the local finite-regulator entropy definition in the open problem.

The other crossed-product papers treat their stated Killing-horizon or stationary-algebra settings; the cited dynamical-horizon result is first-order and identifies renormalized generalized entropy only at leading local area-law order. Island computations retain replica-continuation and gravitational-saddle assumptions, while the cited JT matrix-integral completion is model-specific and ensemble-dependent.

These sources do not by themselves establish that every specified interacting four-dimensional curved-spacetime matter theory and nonstationary collapse solution satisfies the local fixed-scheme regulator-independent limit asked for in the open problem, or that generic evaporation is unitary. This is a bounded summary of the cited material through 22 October 2026, not an exhaustive literature census or a theorem that no such result exists elsewhere. It neither refutes those broader claims nor supplies them as premises.
