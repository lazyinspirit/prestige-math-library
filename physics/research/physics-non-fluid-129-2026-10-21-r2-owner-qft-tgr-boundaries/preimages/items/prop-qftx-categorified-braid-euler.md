## Statement

Fix $n\ge2$ and the type-A Rouquier categorical braid-line model. Let $\chi(C)=\sum_m(-1)^m[C^m]$ be the alternating class of a bounded cochain complex in the split Grothendieck ring $K_0^{\mathrm{split}}(\mathrm{SBim}_n)$, and use the shift convention $v[X]=[X(1)]$. Let $\Phi:K_0^{\mathrm{split}}(\mathrm{SBim}_n)\to H_{S_n}$ be the algebra isomorphism with $\Phi([B_i])=H_i=v(T_i+1)$ and Hecke parameter $q=v^{-2}$. For $\beta\in B_n$, let $e(\beta)$ be its exponent sum, the number of positive letters minus the number of negative letters in any signed word for $\beta$, and let $T_\beta$ be the image of $\beta$ under the group homomorphism $B_n\to H_{S_n}^{\times}$ sending $\sigma_i\mapsto T_i$. Then

$$
\Phi\!\left(\chi(F(\beta))\right)=v^{e(\beta)}T_\beta.
$$

Consequently the normalized map $\beta\mapsto v^{-e(\beta)}\Phi(\chi(F(\beta)))$ is the standard Hecke braid homomorphism, and its normalization is multiplicative under braid stacking. Homotopy-equivalent complexes have the same Euler class, but need not be isomorphic as graded complexes.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** $n\ge2$, a braid $\beta\in B_n$, and the Rouquier complexes and grading convention of [[def-qftx-categorified-line-model]].

[F1] [[prop-decategorification-of-a-rouquier-complex-is-the-hecke-braid-generator]]: in the stated shift convention, $\Phi(\chi(F_i))=vT_i$ and $\Phi(\chi(F_i^{-1}))=v^{-1}T_i^{-1}$ for each simple generator.

[F2] [[lem-euler-class-of-a-rouquier-complex-is-homotopy-invariant-and-multiplicative]]: $\chi$ is invariant under homotopy equivalence and satisfies $\chi(C\otimes_R D)=\chi(C)\chi(D)$ for the signed tensor totalization of bounded complexes with terms in $\mathrm{SBim}_n$.

[F3] [[thm-rouquier-complex-is-well-defined-up-to-canonical-homotopy-equivalence]]: signed words representing the same braid determine canonically homotopy-equivalent Rouquier complexes.

[F4] [[def-braid-group-by-the-artin-presentation]]: $B_n$ is generated by $\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_{n-1}$ with adjacent braid relations and distant commutation relations; inserting or deleting an inverse pair is also a word equality.

[F5] [[def-complex-homotopy-and-contractibility-in-an-additive-category]]: a cochain complex is contractible when there are maps $h^m:C^m\to C^{m-1}$ satisfying $1_{C^m}=d^{m-1}h^m+h^{m+1}d^m$ for every $m$.

[F6] [[def-qftx-categorified-line-model]]: a signed word $\sigma=\sigma_{i_1}^{\epsilon_1}\cdots\sigma_{i_r}^{\epsilon_r}$ is assigned the signed tensor totalization $F(\sigma)=F_{i_1}^{\epsilon_1}\otimes_R\cdots\otimes_R F_{i_r}^{\epsilon_r}$, and its braid has this complex up to canonical homotopy equivalence.

[A1] Use $T_\beta$ for the standard homomorphism image specified in the Statement; no physical interpretation of $v$ is assumed.

## Proof

1.1 Choose a signed word $\sigma=\sigma_{i_1}^{\epsilon_1}\cdots\sigma_{i_r}^{\epsilon_r}$ representing $\beta$. By the word definition and multiplicativity in [F2], $\chi(F(\sigma))=\prod_{k=1}^r\chi(F_{i_k}^{\epsilon_k})$. Applying the algebra homomorphism $\Phi$ and the generator values in [F1] gives $\Phi(\chi(F(\sigma)))=v^{\sum_k\epsilon_k}T_{i_1}^{\epsilon_1}\cdots T_{i_r}^{\epsilon_r}$. [A1, F1, F2, F6, construct]

2.1 The exponent sum is unchanged by each defining relation: the two sides of an adjacent braid relation each have sum $3$, the two sides of a distant commutation relation each have sum $2$, and an inverse pair has sum $0$. Thus $e(\sigma)$ descends to $e(\beta)\in\mathbb Z$ and satisfies $e(\beta\gamma)=e(\beta)+e(\gamma)$. The product of the $T_i^{\epsilon_k}$ is $T_\beta$ by the specified group homomorphism, so step 1.1 gives the displayed formula independent of the chosen word. [F4, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 The comparison in [F3] identifies $F(\beta\gamma)$ in the homotopy category with the signed tensor totalization $F(\beta)\otimes_R F(\gamma)$. Applying homotopy invariance and multiplicativity from [F2], then applying the algebra homomorphism $\Phi$, gives $\Phi(\chi(F(\beta\gamma)))=\Phi(\chi(F(\beta)))\Phi(\chi(F(\gamma)))$. Dividing by $v^{e(\beta\gamma)}=v^{e(\beta)}v^{e(\gamma)}$ proves multiplicativity of the normalized map. [F2, F3, step 2.1, algebra]

3.2 For a positive generator the formula is $vT_i$ and for its inverse it is $v^{-1}T_i^{-1}$ by [F1]. Since $v$ is an indeterminate in the Laurent polynomial coefficient ring, these factors cannot be discarded for nonzero exponent sum. The normalized value is exactly $T_\beta$ by step 2.1. [F1, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 Let $D$ be the two-term complex with $D^0=D^1=R$, differential $d^0=1_R$, and all other terms zero. The map $h^1=1_R:D^1\to D^0$ (all other $h^m=0$) satisfies the contracting-homotopy equation in [F5], so $D$ is homotopy equivalent to the zero complex. But its graded terms are $R$ in degrees $0$ and $1$, whereas the zero complex has zero terms; since $R=\mathbb Q[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$ has $1\ne0$, the complexes are not isomorphic as graded complexes. Their Euler classes are both zero by [F2] and $[R]-[R]=0$. [F2, F5, F6, step 1.1] ∎
