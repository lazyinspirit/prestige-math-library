## Statement

Assume $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$. In the selected model of
[[post-qft-free-field-model]], let $H_\mu$ be the scalar one-particle Hilbert
space and let $\mathcal K=\mathcal H_+\oplus\overline{\mathcal H_-}$ be the
Dirac particle/antiparticle one-particle space. Let $\Omega_s$ and $\Omega_D$
be their zero-particle Fock vectors. The assignments of $H_\mu$ to bosonic
Fock space and $\mathcal K$ to CAR Fock space are the physical assumptions
of that postulate.

For every unit scalar wave-packet mode $h\in H_\mu$, define
$$|n\rangle_h=\frac{(a^\dagger(h))^n}{\sqrt{n!}}\Omega_s,\qquad n\ge0.$$
These vectors are normalized and mutually orthogonal, belong to the
finite-particle domain, and
$$a(h)|n\rangle_h=\sqrt n\,|n-1\rangle_h,\qquad
a^\dagger(h)|n\rangle_h=\sqrt{n+1}\,|n+1\rangle_h,$$
where $a(h)|0\rangle_h=0$. The one-mode number operator
$N_h=a^\dagger(h)a(h)$ therefore satisfies
$N_h|n\rangle_h=n|n\rangle_h$. The maximal annihilation domain is the
square-summability domain of the sector images, as in
[[lem-qft-fock-ccr-car]].

For every unit Dirac particle mode $u\in\mathcal H_+$ or antiparticle mode
$u\in\overline{\mathcal H_-}$, regarded as a vector of $\mathcal K$, the
bounded operator $N_u=c^\dagger(u)c(u)$ is an orthogonal projection. The
one-mode subspace generated by $u$ is the closed span of
$\{(c^\dagger(u))^n\Omega_D:n\ge0\}$. It is spanned by $\Omega_D$ and
$c^\dagger(u)\Omega_D$; the latter is normalized and
$c^\dagger(u)^2=0$. Thus a single Dirac CAR mode has occupation $0$ or $1$.
All statements are conditional on the adopted representations and
$\mathrm{AC}_\omega$. They do not derive which statistics nature assigns to
all fields and do not assert an experimental observation.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The physical species assignments in [A1], Countable Choice in
[A2], and the mathematical Fock operator construction in [F1].

[A1] [[post-qft-free-field-model]] adopts the real scalar bosonic
symmetric-Fock model and the Dirac particle/antiparticle CAR-Fock model. This
is a physical model assumption, not a universal statistics theorem.

[A2] [[def-countable-choice]] states $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$: every sequence of
nonempty sets has a choice function. The Fock supplier assumes this principle,
which is carried into this consumer.

[F1] [[lem-qft-fock-ccr-car]] assumes $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$ and a separable
complex Hilbert space. It constructs the symmetric and antisymmetric Fock
spaces and states that on $\mathcal D_{\rm fin}$ the bosonic operators are
mutual adjoints with
$[a(h),a^\dagger(k)]=\langle h,k\rangle_2 I$, while the fermionic operators
are mutual adjoints, bounded on the whole Fock space, and satisfy
$\{c(h),c^\dagger(k)\}=\langle h,k\rangle_2 I$ and
$\{c^\dagger(h),c^\dagger(k)\}=0$.

[F2] [[lem-qft-fock-ccr-car]] gives the exact bosonic maximal domain
$D(a(h))=\{\Psi=(\Psi_n):\sum_{n\ge1}\|a_n(h)\Psi_n\|^2<\infty\}$.

## Proof

**Given:** [A1], [A2], and [F1]. All bosonic vectors constructed below lie in
$\mathcal D_{\rm fin}$, so every displayed composition is defined.

1.1 Fix a unit $h\in H_\mu$ and put $v_n=(a^\dagger(h))^n\Omega_s$. The
vacuum relation is $a(h)\Omega_s=0$ and the CCR give
$a(h)a^\dagger(h)=a^\dagger(h)a(h)+I$. The base case is
$a(h)v_1=\Omega_s$. If $a(h)v_n=nv_{n-1}$, then
$a(h)v_{n+1}=a(h)a^\dagger(h)v_n
=a^\dagger(h)a(h)v_n+v_n
=n a^\dagger(h)v_{n-1}+v_n=(n+1)v_n$.
Thus $a(h)v_n=nv_{n-1}$ for every $n\ge1$. [given, A1, F1, algebra]

1.2 Since $a^\dagger(h)$ and $a(h)$ are mutual adjoints on the finite-sector
vectors, $\|v_{n+1}\|^2=\langle v_n,a(h)a^\dagger(h)v_n\rangle_2
=(n+1)\|v_n\|^2$. Starting from $\|v_0\|=1$ gives $\|v_n\|^2=n!$.
The vectors $v_n$ lie in distinct Fock sectors and are orthogonal.
Consequently the displayed $|n\rangle_h$ are orthonormal, and dividing
$a(h)v_n=n v_{n-1}$ by the factorial normalizations gives
$a(h)|n\rangle_h=\sqrt n|n-1\rangle_h$. The creation formula gives
$a^\dagger(h)|n\rangle_h=\sqrt{n+1}|n+1\rangle_h$, and applying $a^\dagger$
to the annihilation formula gives $N_h|n\rangle_h=n|n\rangle_h$.
[F1, step 1.1, algebra]

2.1 Fix a unit $u\in\mathcal K$. By [F1],
$c(u)c^\dagger(u)+c^\dagger(u)c(u)=I$,
$c^\dagger(u)^2=0$, and $c(u)\Omega_D=0$. Hence
$\|c^\dagger(u)\Omega_D\|^2
=\langle\Omega_D,c(u)c^\dagger(u)\Omega_D\rangle_2
=\langle\Omega_D,(I-c^\dagger(u)c(u))\Omega_D\rangle_2=1$.
A second creation vanishes. Since vacuum and one-particle sectors are
orthogonal, the cyclic subspace generated by $u$ is exactly
$\operatorname{span}\{\Omega_D,c^\dagger(u)\Omega_D\}$. Moreover,
$N_u^*=N_u$ and
$N_u^2=c^\dagger(u)(I-c^\dagger(u)c(u))c(u)=N_u$ because
$c^\dagger(u)^2=0$. Thus $N_u$ is a projection, so its only possible
eigenvalues are $0$ and $1$; it acts as $0$ on $\Omega_D$ and as $1$ on
$c^\dagger(u)\Omega_D$. [A1, A2, F1, algebra]

2.2 The scalar ladder and Dirac one-mode exclusion above follow only after
the species-to-Fock assignments in [A1] are made. The argument uses the
specified representations and proves no relation assigning statistics to
every relativistic field. [A1, step 1.2, step 2.1, given] ∎
