## Definition

Use the fields, Fock spaces, vacua, coordinate conventions, and natural/SI
normalizations adopted in [[post-qft-free-field-model]]. For an open spacetime
region $O\subseteq\mathbb R^4$, a scalar test is a real
$f\in C_c^\infty(O;\mathbb R)$ and a Dirac test is a
$F\in C_c^\infty(O;\mathbb C^4)$. These are compactly supported smooth test
functions on the fixed inertial Minkowski chart.

For scalar tests use the bounded Weyl operators
$$W_\mu(f)=W_{\rm Segal}(\sqrt2 K_\mu f),$$
where $K_\mu$ is the future-shell restriction and
$W_{\rm Segal}$ is the unitary generated by the closed Segal field, with the
normalization and phase convention of
[[lem-qft-smeared-free-scalar]]. Define the scalar bounded observable algebra
$\mathfrak A_s(O)$ to be the norm closure of the unital $*$-algebra generated
by these $W_\mu(f)$. The real smeared field is symmetric on
$\mathcal D_{\rm fin}$ and its self-adjoint closure has the exact multiplier
domain supplied by [[lem-qft-free-domain-observables]] and
[[lem-qft-segal-weyl-domain]].

For Dirac tests, $\Psi(F)$ is the bounded smeared CAR field of
[[lem-qft-free-dirac-car-locality]]. Define $\mathfrak A_D(O)$ as the norm
closure of the unital algebra generated by products containing an even number
of these field operators and their adjoints, with all test supports in $O$.
“Even” here means even field parity, or equivalently an even number of odd
CAR field generators in each product. The even algebra is the selected local
Dirac observable algebra; an odd charged field is not included as a commuting
local observable.

The selected vacuum states on bounded operators are the vector states
$\omega_s(A)=\langle\Omega_s,A\Omega_s\rangle$ and
$\omega_D(B)=\langle\Omega_D,B\Omega_D\rangle$. For the scalar Fock space,
define the total number, Hamiltonian, and translation momentum components by
$$N_s=d\Gamma_s(1),\qquad H_s=\hbar c\,d\Gamma_s(\omega_\mu),\qquad
P_{s,j}=\hbar\,d\Gamma_s(-k_j).$$
Here $d\Gamma_s$ acts on the $n$-particle sector by summing the displayed
one-particle multiplier over its $n$ arguments. Their maximal domains are
the square-integrability domains of those sector sums; in particular,
$$D(N_s)=\left\{\Psi=(\Psi_n):\sum_{n\ge1}n^2\|\Psi_n\|^2<\infty\right\}.$$
The sign in $P_{s,j}$ matches the field translation phase
$\omega_\mu x^0-\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf x$. For the Dirac Fock space, define
$H_D$ and $P_{D,j}$ by the maximal sector multipliers of
[[lem-qft-dirac-fermionic-translation-spectrum]]: the particle and
antiparticle one-particle four-vectors are respectively
$(\omega_\mu,\mathbf k)$ and $(\omega_\mu,-\mathbf k)$, and the sector
multiplier is their sum. The physical energy and momentum scales are $\hbar c$
and $\hbar$, respectively, as fixed by [[def-qft-framework-units]].

For any of these self-adjoint operators $A$, a normalized state
$\Psi\in D(A)$ has model mean $\langle\Psi,A\Psi\rangle$ and variance
$\operatorname{Var}_\Psi(A)=\|(A-\langle\Psi,A\Psi\rangle)\Psi\|^2$.
This requires the second spectral moment but does not require
$\Psi\in D(A^2)$. Products of unbounded operators use their composition
domains.

If a signed SI charge parameter $q_{\rm SI}$ is supplied, the intended
Dirac charge-number operator is $Q_{\rm SI}=q_{\rm SI}(N_p-N_{\bar p})$.
Here $\Pi_p$ and $\Pi_{\bar p}$ are the orthogonal projections onto
$\mathcal H_+$ and $\overline{\mathcal H_-}$; on the $n$-particle sector,
$N_p$ and $N_{\bar p}$ are the sums of the corresponding projections over
the $n$ tensor factors. Its candidate maximal direct-sum domain is
$$D(Q_{\rm SI})=\left\{\Psi=(\Psi_n):\sum_{n\ge0}\left\|q_{\rm SI}\sum_{r=1}^n(\Pi_p-\Pi_{\bar p})^{(r)}\Psi_n\right\|^2<\infty\right\}.$$
The current mathematical carrier does not establish this charged-number
domain or its self-adjointness; its exact unresolved interface is recorded as
[L2] below, so this part remains conditional.

Measurements in this model are assigned only when a state and a normalized
finite bounded Kraus instrument have actually been constructed. For such an
instrument, the adopted outcome probabilities and positive-probability
conditional states are exactly those of
[[post-qft-constructed-model-and-measurement]]. They are model probabilities,
not observed outcomes. The field definitions are test-smeared: neither
$\Phi_\mu(x)$ nor $\Psi(x)$ is a pointwise operator supplied here, and the
smearing results do not define coincident products or a diagonal restriction.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The adopted free-field model, its specified mathematical
representations, and the measurement interpretation only for constructed
states and instruments.

[A1] [[post-qft-free-field-model]] adopts a flat real scalar bosonic Fock model
and a Dirac particle/antiparticle CAR Fock model with selected zero-particle
vacua; these are model assumptions.

[F1] [[post-qft-constructed-model-and-measurement]] adopts
$p_a=\operatorname{tr}(\rho E_a)$ only for a supplied density state and
normalized finite bounded instrument. It supplies no apparatus realization
or measurement result.

[F2] [[lem-qft-free-domain-observables]] currently states that real scalar
smearings have self-adjoint closures, that $N_s,H_s,P_{s,j}$ have the
displayed maximal domains, that Dirac smearings are bounded, that $H_D,P_{D,j}$
have the domains of the Dirac translation supplier, that finite means and
variances follow on $D(A)$, and that no point-field norm limit or coincident
product is supplied. This is an actual draft carrier with a complete local
proof conditional on its listed mathematical suppliers; it has no current
Step 1 readiness receipt.

[F3] [[lem-qft-smeared-free-scalar]] defines the scalar test restriction,
the common finite-particle domain, real-field symmetry, and the bounded Weyl
operators with phase
$\exp(-i\,\operatorname{Im}\langle K_\mu f,K_\mu g\rangle_2)$.
Its current Step 1 decision is escalated through QFT-MF01 dependencies.

[F4] [[lem-qft-free-dirac-car-locality]] defines the bounded spacetime-smeared
Dirac field and its CAR and even graded-locality conclusions. Its current
carrier is draft; the QFT-MF01 and QFT-MF02 prerequisite edges remain open.

[F5] [[lem-qft-dirac-fermionic-translation-spectrum]] gives the particle and
antiparticle translation vectors, their sector-sum joint spectral measure,
and the exact maximal domains for the Dirac generators. Its actual carrier is
draft and its QFT-MF01/QFT-MF02 edges remain open.

[F6] [[lem-qft-second-quantized-spectrum]] gives the scalar sector-sum
multipliers, their joint PVM, and their exact square-integrability domains.

[F7] [[def-countable-choice]] states $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$: every sequence of
nonempty sets has a choice function. It is an explicit mathematical
assumption of the Fock and free-field supplier arguments used here.
