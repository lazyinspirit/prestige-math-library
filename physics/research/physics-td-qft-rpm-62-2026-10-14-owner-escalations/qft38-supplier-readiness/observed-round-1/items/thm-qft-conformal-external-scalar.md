## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Countable Choice $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$ of [[def-countable-choice]]. On $M=\mathbb R^4$ with global coordinates $x=(x^0,x^1,x^2,x^3)$, set $\eta_{ab}=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,1,1)$ and use $\partial_0$ to select the standard future cone. Let $\Omega\in C^\infty(\mathbb R^4;(0,\infty))$ be real and dimensionless, and define the prescribed external Lorentzian metric $g_{ab}=\Omega^2\eta_{ab}$. No field equation for $g$ is assumed. Write $d\mathrm{vol}_g=\Omega^4d^4x$ for its positive integration density and $\Box_\eta=\eta^{ab}\partial_a\partial_b$. Let $R_g$ be the scalar curvature in the convention of [[thm-emg-conformal-einstein-transformation]], and define
$$\Box_g h:=\Omega^{-4}\partial_a\!\left(\Omega^2\eta^{ab}\partial_bh\right),\qquad P_g:=\Box_g-\frac16R_g.$$
The test space is $\mathcal D=C_c^\infty(\mathbb R^4;\mathbb C)$ with its usual test-function topology. Let $\Phi_0$ be the massless Minkowski Fock field and $|0\rangle$ its normalized vacuum from [[lem-qft-smeared-free-scalar]] and [[lem-qft-scalar-causal-commutator]], on their stated common dense invariant domain $\mathcal D_{\rm fin}$. For $f\in\mathcal D$, define the curved-background smeared field by
$$\Phi_g(f):=\Phi_0(\Omega^3f).$$

Then, with all equalities understood on $\mathcal D_{\rm fin}$ or as distributional equalities as appropriate:

1. The exact curvature and scalar wave identities are
$$R_g=-6\Omega^{-3}\Box_\eta\Omega,\qquad P_g(\Omega^{-1}u)=\Omega^{-3}\Box_\eta u\quad(u\in C^\infty(\mathbb R^4;\mathbb C)).$$

2. The map $f\mapsto\Phi_g(f)$ is a weak operator-valued distribution on $\mathcal D_{\rm fin}$ and satisfies $\Phi_g(P_gf)=0$ for every $f\in\mathcal D$. The normalized Minkowski vacuum defines a positive state on the finite polynomial $*$-algebra generated by these fields.

3. If $\operatorname{supp}f$ and $\operatorname{supp}h$ are spacelike separated (every pair of points in the two supports has positive $\eta$-squared separation), then $[\Phi_g(f),\Phi_g(h)]=0$ on $\mathcal D_{\rm fin}$.

4. The two-point distribution relative to $d\mathrm{vol}_g(x)d\mathrm{vol}_g(y)$ is
$$W_g(x,y)=\Omega(x)^{-1}\Omega(y)^{-1}W_0(x,y),$$
where $W_0$ is the massless Minkowski Fock-vacuum two-point distribution relative to $d^4x\,d^4y$. On compact tests, $P_g$ is formally symmetric for $d\mathrm{vol}_g$.

This is a selected free-field representation obtained from one Minkowski Fock vacuum. It asserts no uniqueness of a curved-spacetime state, generic Hadamard-state existence, thermal-state result, renormalized stress-tensor transformation, massive or minimally coupled result, Einstein equation, or backreaction claim. The metric is prescribed background data, not a dynamical solution.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The smooth positive conformal factor and definitions in the Statement, $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$, and the exact planned supplier interfaces below. The supplier items marked planned have research proof modules but no current authored carrier; the proof is conditional on their stated interfaces.

[A1] $\Omega$ is smooth, real, and strictly positive on all of $\mathbb R^4$, and $g=\Omega^2\eta$ is prescribed. In particular, multiplication by $\Omega^3$ and $\Omega^{-1}$ is smooth on every compact set.

[A2] Adopt $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$ as defined by [[def-countable-choice]]: every countable family of nonempty sets has a choice function. The exact planned QFT Fock suppliers and the planned EMG geometry supplier carry this assumption in their declared dependency closures; no stronger choice principle is used here.

[F1] Planned interface of [[thm-emg-conformal-einstein-transformation]] (its carrier is absent): for $u_a=\Omega^{-1}\partial_a\Omega$, $H_{ab}=(\nabla_a u)_b$, $\theta=g^{ab}H_{ab}$, and $s=g^{ab}u_au_b$, the fixed GR curvature convention gives $\widetilde S=\Omega^{-2}(S-6\theta-6s)$ for $\widetilde g=\Omega^2g$.

[F2] Planned interface of [[lem-qft-smeared-free-scalar]] from research proof module FF3 (its carrier is absent): for $\mu\ge0$, $Kf(p)=\int f(x)e^{ip\circ x}\,d^4x$ on the future mass shell, and $\Phi_0(f)=a(K\overline f)+a^\dagger(Kf)$ on the invariant dense domain $\mathcal D_{\rm fin}$. For $\mu=0$ this field is a weak operator-valued tempered distribution, obeys $\Phi_0(\Box_\eta f)=0$, and its normalized Fock vacuum has positive polynomial moments, including $W_0$.

[F3] Planned interface of [[lem-qft-scalar-causal-commutator]] from research proof module FF4 (its carrier is absent): at $\mu=0$, the commutator kernel is the causal Klein–Gordon distribution $\Delta_0$ with $\operatorname{supp}\Delta_0\subseteq\{(t,\mathbf x):|\mathbf x|\le|t|\}$, and the compactly smeared fields commute when their supports are spacelike separated.

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Put $u_a=\Omega^{-1}\partial_a\Omega$. The flat metric has zero scalar curvature, and its connection is the coordinate-flat connection, so the planned transformation [F1] gives $R_g=-6\Omega^{-2}(\eta^{ab}\partial_a u_b+\eta^{ab}u_au_b)$. Since $\partial_a u_b=\Omega^{-1}\partial_a\partial_b\Omega-\Omega^{-2}(\partial_a\Omega)(\partial_b\Omega)$, the gradient-square terms cancel and $R_g=-6\Omega^{-3}\Box_\eta\Omega$. [given, A1, A2, F1, algebra]

1.2 Matrix inversion and $\det(\Omega^2\eta)=-\Omega^8$ give $g^{ab}=\Omega^{-2}\eta^{ab}$ and the density $\Omega^4d^4x$. Thus $\Box_gh=\Omega^{-4}\partial_a(\Omega^2\partial^ah)$, where $\partial^a=\eta^{ab}\partial_b$. For $h=\Omega^{-1}u$, the differentiated numerator is $\partial_a(\Omega\partial^au-u\partial^a\Omega)=\Omega\Box_\eta u-u\Box_\eta\Omega$, because the two cross-gradient terms cancel. Combining with step 1.1 yields $P_g(\Omega^{-1}u)=\Omega^{-3}\Box_\eta u$. [given, A1, step 1.1, algebra]

2.1 For every compact $K\subset\mathbb R^4$ and integer $m\ge0$, the product rule gives $p_{K,m}(\Omega^3f)\le C_{K,m}p_{K,m}(f)$, where $p_{K,m}(f)=\max_{|\alpha|\le m}\sup_K|\partial^\alpha f|$ and $C_{K,m}<\infty$ is the finite sum of the corresponding suprema of derivatives of $\Omega^3$ on $K$ with their multinomial coefficients. Also $\operatorname{supp}(\Omega^3f)=\operatorname{supp}f$. Therefore $\Omega^3f\in\mathcal D$, the multiplier is continuous on each fixed-support test space, and [F2] defines $\Phi_g(f)$ on the same invariant $\mathcal D_{\rm fin}$. [given, A1, F2, construct]

2.2 Substituting $u=\Omega f$ in the wave identity of step 1.2 gives $\Omega^3P_gf=\Box_\eta(\Omega f)$. Hence $\Phi_g(P_gf)=\Phi_0(\Box_\eta(\Omega f))=0$ by the massless field equation in [F2]; the smooth-coefficient operator $P_g$ preserves compact smooth tests. [given, F2, step 1.2, algebra]

2.3 For a finite polynomial $B$ in fields $\Phi_g(f_j)$, replace each factor by $\Phi_0(\Omega^3f_j)$. The common invariant domain in [F2] makes the product on $|0\rangle$ well-defined, and the vacuum functional satisfies $\omega_g(B^*B)=\|B(\Phi_0(\Omega^3f_j))|0\rangle\|^2\ge0$ and $\omega_g(1)=1$. Thus it is a positive normalized state on the stated polynomial algebra. [given, A2, F2, construct]

3.1 At every point $g(v,v)=\Omega^2\eta(v,v)$, so the nonzero future-causal tangent vectors for $g$ and $\eta$ coincide; the same $\partial_0$ time orientation is used. Define causal reachability by piecewise $C^1$ curves with nonzero future-causal tangent on each smooth piece. Such a curve is admissible for $g$ exactly when it is admissible for $\eta$, so the two causal-reachability relations and their spacelike-separated compact support pairs agree. [given, A1, algebra]

3.2 Multiplication by $\Omega^3$ preserves each test support by step 2.1. For spacelike-separated supports, [F3] therefore gives $[\Phi_g(f),\Phi_g(h)]=[\Phi_0(\Omega^3f),\Phi_0(\Omega^3h)]=0$ on $\mathcal D_{\rm fin}$. [given, F3, step 2.1, step 3.1]

4.1 Relative to $d\mathrm{vol}_g(x)d\mathrm{vol}_g(y)$, the distribution $W_g(x,y)=\Omega(x)^{-1}\Omega(y)^{-1}W_0(x,y)$ pairs with compact tests by the two factors $\Omega^4\Omega^{-1}=\Omega^3$. Thus its smeared pairing is exactly $W_0(\Omega^3f,\Omega^3h)$, the covariance of the field defined in step 2.1; multiplication by the positive smooth reciprocal factors preserves distributional well-definedness and support locally. [given, A1, F2, step 2.1, algebra]

4.2 For compact $f,h$, integrate inside a finite coordinate box containing both supports. Using $\Box_gh=\Omega^{-4}\partial_a(\Omega^2\partial^ah)$ and the vanishing boundary terms gives $\int\overline f\,\Box_gh\,d\mathrm{vol}_g=-\int\Omega^2\eta^{ab}(\partial_a\overline f)(\partial_bh)\,d^4x=\int\overline{\Box_gf}\,h\,d\mathrm{vol}_g$. Since $R_g$ is real by step 1.1, the multiplication term $R_g/6$ is symmetric too, so $P_g$ is formally symmetric. [given, A1, step 1.1, algebra] ∎

## Remarks

- The local multiplier estimates require only smoothness on each compact set. No boundedness or decay condition on $\Omega$ or its derivatives at infinity is assumed.
- The result concerns the chosen free Fock-field representation and its state. It does not assert that arbitrary quantum observables, especially a renormalized stress tensor, transform without anomaly.
