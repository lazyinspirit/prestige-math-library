## Statement

Let $O\subseteq\mathbb R^4$ be open. For real
$f\in C_c^\infty(O)$ define
$$W_\mu(f):=W_{\rm Segal}(\sqrt2K_\mu f),$$
where the Segal normalization is exactly
$\Phi_{\rm Segal}(h)=(a^\dagger(h)+a(h))/\sqrt2$ on the finite-particle
domain, as in [[lem-qft-segal-weyl-domain]]. This scale makes the generator
of $t\mapsto W_\mu(tf)$ equal to the real scalar field
$\Phi_\mu(f)=a(K_\mu f)+a^\dagger(K_\mu f)$ of
[[lem-qft-smeared-free-scalar]]. Define the unital norm-closed operator
algebra
$$\mathfrak A_\mu(O)=
\overline{\operatorname{alg}}^{\|\cdot\|}
\{I,W_\mu(f),W_\mu(f)^*:f\in C_c^\infty(O;\mathbb R)\}.$$
Then $O_1\subseteq O_2$ implies
$\mathfrak A_\mu(O_1)\subseteq\mathfrak A_\mu(O_2)$. If every difference
$x-y$, with $x\in O_1$ and $y\in O_2$, is spacelike, then every element of
$\mathfrak A_\mu(O_1)$ commutes with every element of
$\mathfrak A_\mu(O_2)$. These are algebraic properties of the constructed
representation; they are not empirical signaling or measurement claims.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The scalar shell, Fock and Segal conventions of
[[def-qft-scalar-mass-shell]], [[lem-qft-smeared-free-scalar]] and
[[lem-qft-segal-weyl-domain]], and the causal commutator result.

[A1] $\mathfrak A_\mu(O)$ is by definition the operator-norm closure of the
unital algebra generated by the listed local Weyl unitaries and their
adjoints; $C_c^\infty(O)$ embeds into $C_c^\infty(O')$ when $O\subseteq O'$.

[F1] With the scale $\sqrt2$, the shell Weyl operators obey
$W_\mu(f)W_\mu(g)=
e^{-i\operatorname{Im}\langle K_\mu f,K_\mu g\rangle_2}W_\mu(f+g)$
for real tests.
[[lem-qft-smeared-free-scalar]]

[F2] The field commutator is
$[\Phi_\mu(f),\Phi_\mu(g)]=-i\Delta_\mu(f,g)I$, and
$\Delta_\mu(f,g)=0$ for spacelike-separated supports.
[[lem-qft-scalar-causal-commutator]]

## Proof

1.1 If $O_1\subseteq O_2$, each real test function supported in $O_1$ is
also a real test function supported in $O_2$. Thus the generators and their
adjoints for $\mathfrak A_\mu(O_1)$ belong to the generating set for
$\mathfrak A_\mu(O_2)$; finite sums and products do too, and norm closure
preserves inclusion. This proves isotony. [A1, given, algebra]

2.1 Let $f,g$ be real tests supported in the two spacelike-separated
regions. By [F2] their field commutator is zero. Expanding the two fields
using the CCR gives
$2i\,\operatorname{Im}\langle K_\mu f,K_\mu g\rangle_2I$. Hence the
imaginary part vanishes. Substituting it in [F1] gives
$W_\mu(f)W_\mu(g)=W_\mu(f+g)=W_\mu(g)W_\mu(f)$. Taking adjoints shows each
generator from the first region commutes with each generator and adjoint
from the second. Commutation is preserved under finite linear combinations
and products; if bounded sequences $A_n\to A$ and $B_n\to B$ in operator
norm satisfy $A_nB_n=B_nA_n$, then continuity of multiplication gives
$AB=BA$. Taking the two norm closures proves the assertion for the full
local algebras. [F1, F2, step 1.1, given, algebra] ∎
