These leaf examples check the vertical-separator boundary in biconjugation, a whole flat face of Fenchel minimizers, a singular two-phase equation map where equation counting fails, and a water-formation extent at the boundary of its feasible interval. The reaction example verifies stoichiometric feasibility and atom conservation for the displayed finite model only; its chosen objective is mathematical data and is not an empirical or thermodynamic claim.
