This page adopts the macroscopic concave entropy representation for the declared thermodynamic systems. Entropy, equilibrium maximum, the macroscopic second law, and the zeroth law are physical postulates, not mathematical theorems or conclusions from observations. Their statements name the allowed systems, contact protocols, process boundaries, units, regularity charts, and feasible sets. In particular, an equilibrium maximum is global and assumed attained on its exact feasible set; concavity alone supplies neither existence nor uniqueness. The thermal-contact equivalence relation does not itself provide a thermometer scale. Lieb–Yngvason's accessibility-based entropy construction is a distinct route with additional operational hypotheses; it is not used to prove these postulates.

A separately designated common-constitutive-law bulk branch adopts coordinate-preserving entropy transport from scaled systems to one material-law chart. Its open-cone domain and common reference conventions supply same-function homogeneity for conditional Euler, Gibbs–Duhem and radial-curvature deductions. The general tagged family remains broader; coordinate additivity and tagged scaling alone do not supply this branch assumption.
