This page develops the local geometry of a nonsingular Pfaff one-form $\omega$ on an open finite-dimensional Euclidean domain. It defines horizontal paths and gives complete local contraction and variational-flow arguments before proving the codimension-one Frobenius criterion: $\omega\wedge d\omega=0$ exactly when a local nonzero factor $\mu$ makes $\mu\omega=dS$. The local Carathéodory result uses a precise two-sided horizontal inaccessibility hypothesis and carries the exact implicit-rank input it consumes. Neither result supplies a global entropy, a global potential, a positive-temperature scale, or a physical identification of mathematical horizontal curves with adiabatic processes. The rank and inaccessibility conclusions remain conditional on the planned local implicit-rank supplier until its actual batch-4 carrier is available and inspected. A global period example on the companion page records why local exactness does not imply a global potential; physical sign, units, and calibration require separate model assumptions.
