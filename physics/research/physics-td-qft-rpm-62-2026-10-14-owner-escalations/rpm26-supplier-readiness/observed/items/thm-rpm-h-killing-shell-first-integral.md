## Statement

Let $(M,g)$ be a smooth Lorentzian manifold with canonical cotangent coordinates $(x^a,p_a)$ and $C_m(x,p)=\tfrac12(g^{ab}p_ap_b+m^2c^2)$ for fixed $m\ge0$. For any smooth vector field $\xi$, set $F_\xi=p_a\xi^a(x)$. With canonical bracket $\{F,G\}=F_xG_p-F_pG_x$,

$$\{F_\xi,C_m\}=-\tfrac12(\mathcal L_\xi g^{-1})^{ab}p_ap_b.$$

If $\xi$ is Killing, this bracket vanishes for all cotangent covectors, and $F_\xi$ is constant along every constrained free-shell trajectory generated by $eC_m$ with any smooth multiplier $e$ on its actual interval. No global existence or empirical conservation is asserted.

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct tensor differentiation.

**Given:** The smooth metric, canonical bracket [L2], shell function and multiplier equations [L1].

1.1 In coordinates, $\partial_{x^c}F_\xi=p_a\partial_c\xi^a$, $\partial_{p_c}F_\xi=\xi^c$, $\partial_{p_c}C_m=g^{cb}p_b$, and $\partial_{x^c}C_m=\tfrac12\partial_cg^{ab}p_ap_b$. Therefore [L2] gives

$$\{F_\xi,C_m\}=p_a(\partial_c\xi^a)g^{cb}p_b-\tfrac12\xi^c(\partial_cg^{ab})p_ap_b=-\tfrac12(\mathcal L_\xi g^{-1})^{ab}p_ap_b,$$

by the coordinate definition $(\mathcal L_\xi g^{-1})^{ab}=\xi^c\partial_cg^{ab}-g^{cb}\partial_c\xi^a-g^{ac}\partial_c\xi^b$ and symmetry of $p_ap_b$. [L1, L2, algebra]

1.2 If $\xi$ is Killing, [F1] gives $\mathcal L_\xi g=0$. Apply $\mathcal L_\xi$ to $g^{ac}g_{cb}=\delta^a_b$ and use the product rule; it follows that $(\mathcal L_\xi g^{-1})^{ac}g_{cb}=0$, hence $\mathcal L_\xi g^{-1}=0$ by nondegeneracy. Step 1.1 then gives $\{F_\xi,C_m\}=0$. [F1, step 1.1, algebra]

1.3 Along the H7 constrained equation, $\frac d{d\lambda}F_\xi=e\{F_\xi,C_m\}$ because $F_\xi$ has no explicit parameter dependence. This vanishes by step 1.2 throughout the solution's actual interval. [L1, L2, A1, step 1.2] ∎
