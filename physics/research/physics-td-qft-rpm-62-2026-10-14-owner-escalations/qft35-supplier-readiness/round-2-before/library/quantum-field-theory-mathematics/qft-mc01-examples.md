The algebraic Weyl relations and positivity of a state do not force regularity. The counterexample constructs a normalized positive state on the finite-support Weyl algebra of the standard symplectic plane; in its GNS representation, every nonzero one-parameter direction has a unitary orbit that jumps at the identity. The construction is mathematical and makes no claim about a physical vacuum or preparation.
