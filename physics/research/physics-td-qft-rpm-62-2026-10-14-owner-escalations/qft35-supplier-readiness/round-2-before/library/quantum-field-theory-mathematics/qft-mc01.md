This page develops the algebraic notion of a positive normalized state without assuming a norm or a C*-completion. Positivity gives a Hermitian semidefinite form, Cauchy–Schwarz including its zero-norm case, and a left-radical quotient. Under the explicitly stated Countable Choice assumption used by the Hilbert-completion supplier, the quotient yields a common-domain GNS representation whose operators are closable, whose cyclic vector recovers the state, and which is unique up to an intertwining unitary. State-preserving group actions are unitarily implemented; strong continuity follows when their matrix coefficients are continuous at the identity. Symmetry of a represented self-adjoint algebra element does not imply essential self-adjointness.
