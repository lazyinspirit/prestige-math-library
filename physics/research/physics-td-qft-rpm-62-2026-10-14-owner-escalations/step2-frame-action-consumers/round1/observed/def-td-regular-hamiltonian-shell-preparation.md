## Definition

Assume the Axiom of Choice ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]). Fix an integer $n\ge1$ and an open phase-coordinate domain $O_{\rm phys}\subseteq\mathbb R^{2n}$ with physical canonical coordinates $q_i$ in metres and $p_i$ in $\mathrm{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$. The canonical symplectic form on this chart is $\omega_{\rm phys}=\sum_{i=1}^n dq_i\wedge dp_i$. Let $\hat q_i=q_i/(1\,\mathrm m)$ and $\hat p_i=p_i/(1\,\mathrm{kg\,m\,s^{-1}})$, and let $O\subseteq\mathbb R^{2n}$ be the open image of the chart in these dimensionless coordinates. The corresponding dimensionless canonical form is $\hat\omega=\sum_{i=1}^n d\hat q_i\wedge d\hat p_i$, so $\omega_{\rm phys}=(1\,\mathrm{J\,s})\hat\omega$.

Let $\hat H:O\to\mathbb R$ be a numerical energy function with continuous partial derivatives through order two, and let $H_{\rm phys}:O_{\rm phys}\to\mathbb R\,\mathrm J$ be $H_{\rm phys}(q,p)=(1\,\mathrm J)\hat H(\hat q,\hat p)$. Where defined, its physical Hamiltonian vector field $X_{H_{\rm phys}}$ is determined by $\iota_{X_{H_{\rm phys}}}\omega_{\rm phys}=dH_{\rm phys}$. For each elapsed time $t$ in its domain, the physical flow map is a function $\phi_t:D_t\to O_{\rm phys}$, where $D_t\subseteq O_{\rm phys}$ is the set of initial data whose trajectories exist through time $t$, and it satisfies $d\phi_t/dt=X_{H_{\rm phys}}\circ\phi_t$. Equivalently, in the dimensionless chart, the flow map $\hat\phi_{\hat t}:\hat D_{\hat t}\to O$ is generated by $X_{\hat H}$ satisfying $\iota_{X_{\hat H}}\hat\omega=d\hat H$, at dimensionless time $\hat t=t/(1\,\mathrm s)$; here $\hat D_{\hat t}$ is the set of initial coordinates whose trajectories exist through $\hat t$. These definitions specify the flow maps when they exist but impose no global-existence or completeness hypothesis on the shell preparation.

A specified open interval $J_E\subseteq\mathbb R$ is a regular compact-shell band when $\hat H^{-1}(K)$ is compact in $O$ for every compact $K\subset J_E$ and $\nabla\hat H$ is nonzero at every point of $\hat H^{-1}(J_E)$.

For $\hat E\in J_E$, the energy shell is $\Sigma_{\hat E}:=\{x\in O:\hat H(x)=\hat E\}$. The graph-chart definition of the weighted shell integral is [[def-tdmath-regular-energy-shell]]: in a local graph chart $x_i=h(z,\hat E)$, its coarea measure is $d\mu_{\hat E}=dA_{\hat E}/|\nabla\hat H|$, with graph-area factor and gradient in the dimensionless Euclidean coordinates. Set $D(\hat E):=\int_{\Sigma_{\hat E}}d\mu_{\hat E}$ and admit the preparation only when $\Sigma_{\hat E}\ne\varnothing$ and $0<D(\hat E)<\infty$. Its normalized shell law is $\nu_{\hat E}(A):=D(\hat E)^{-1}\mu_{\hat E}(A\cap\Sigma_{\hat E})$. The current draft theorem [[thm-tdmath-regular-energy-shell-measure]] proves the coarea identity, chart independence and positive finite shell mass under its stated $C^2$, compact-regular-band hypotheses; its statement and proof are present, while its acceptance remains unresolved. Multiplying the unnormalized shell measure by a fixed positive phase-volume normalization does not change $\nu_{\hat E}$.
