This page distinguishes exact observables in an actually constructed finite
regulator, formal perturbative coefficient records, and source-reported
empirical estimates. Its finite-dimensional result assigns Born probabilities
only under the stated constructed-model postulate and supplies an explicit
truncation error bound. It also records exact unitarity and finite-cutoff
warnings; none is a continuum-QFT or experimental conclusion.
