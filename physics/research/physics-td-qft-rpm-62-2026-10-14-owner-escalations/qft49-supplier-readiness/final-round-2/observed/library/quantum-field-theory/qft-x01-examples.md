The companion page works through the finite two-level transition and the
one-mode time-ordered correlator at a negative time. The squared first-order
coefficient is not treated as a probability, and the oscillator correlation
is distinguished from its retarded response. These are exact calculations in
the stated constructed models, with no simulated or reported data.
