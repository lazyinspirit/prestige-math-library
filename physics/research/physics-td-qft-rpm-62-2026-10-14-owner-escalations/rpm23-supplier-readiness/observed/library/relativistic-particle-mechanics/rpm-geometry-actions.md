This page fixes the flat and curved Lorentzian geometry, distinguishes observers, congruences, charts and tetrads, and states the proper-time and test-particle conventions. Mathematical results establish the local shell-force IVP and the arbitrary-parameter massive and einbein variations; separate postulates adopt ideal-clock, massive, null and named-force models. Conditional consequences cover free geodesics, Killing charges, the mass shell, observer energy and the flat inertial work relation. No completeness, empirical force law, or general energy conservation is assumed.
