These examples separate three cases that a coordinate-root calculation
alone does not settle: the extremal RN ingoing chart is regular with zero
surface gravity, superextremal RN has no positive radial root and every
point in the selected model escapes, and rotating KN stationary-limit and
Killing-horizon surfaces differ away from the axis.

The topology example gives an RN sphere with nonzero magnetic flux and local
potentials but no global real potential, by the direct local-gauge calculation
and finite-chain Stokes argument. Its additional KN finite-annulus comparison
is conditional on the exact current batch-21 dyonic-flux draft, whose own
current-form and Gauss inputs remain unaccepted. The extremal, superextremal
and rotating examples are conditional on the exact selected-model carriers
and the planned causal-vocabulary interface where cited. These mathematical
examples report no observations and establish no cosmic-censorship claim.
