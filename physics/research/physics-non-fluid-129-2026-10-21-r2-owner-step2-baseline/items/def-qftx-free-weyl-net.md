## Definition

For a Green-data background of [[def-qftx-green-hyperbolic-interface]], let $(V(M),\sigma_M)$ be the real symplectic vector space of [[lem-qftx-green-propagator-symplectic-quotient]]. Define its **algebraic Weyl $*$-algebra** $\mathfrak W(M)$ to be the complex vector space of finite formal sums of basis symbols $W_M(v)$, $v\in V(M)$, with bilinear product and conjugate-linear involution specified on generators by

$$W_M(v)W_M(w):=e^{-i\sigma_M(v,w)/2}W_M(v+w),\qquad W_M(v)^*:=W_M(-v).$$

The unit is $W_M(0)$. This is the algebraic Weyl algebra: this definition assigns no norm, Hilbert-space representation, or $C^*$-completion. For an open set $O\subseteq M$, define the local algebra $\mathfrak W_M(O)$ as the unital $*$-subalgebra generated by the symbols $W_M([f])$ with real $f\in\mathcal D_g(M)$ and $\operatorname{supp}f\subseteq O$. No norm closure is taken.

An **admissible Green-background morphism** $\iota:(U,g_U,N_U,P_U,E^U_+,E^U_-)\to(M,g_M,N_M,P_M,E^M_+,E^M_-)$ is an orientation-preserving, time-orientation-preserving isometric smooth embedding with open causally convex image, for which $P_M\iota_*f=\iota_*P_Uf$ on compact smooth real tests. Here $\iota_*f$ is extension by zero from the open image, which is smooth and compactly supported because $\operatorname{supp}f$ is compact in $U$. The morphism is **Cauchy** when its image contains a Cauchy hypersurface of $(M,g_M,N_M)$, meaning a spacelike embedded hypersurface met exactly once by every inextendible causal curve, as in [[def-gr-spacelike-hypersurfaces-and-cauchy-sets]]. The associated quotient map, when well-defined, sends $[f]\in V(U)$ to $[\iota_*f]\in V(M)$, and the Weyl map is required to send $W_U([f])$ to $W_M([\iota_*f])$.

## Remarks

Only the real symplectic form and the algebraic Weyl relations are part of this definition. In particular, no assertion about Reeh–Schlieder, local factor type, existence of a state, or a $C^*$-completion is made.
