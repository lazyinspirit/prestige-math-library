## Definition

Let the laboratory frame and SI conventions be those of the framework, and let $\rho,J$ be a jointly smooth conserved volume source supported in $\overline B_a(0)$ in space at every time, for some $a>0$. Let $E,B$ be the retarded fields generated by that source. Write $S^2=\{n\in\mathbb R^3:|n|=1\}$, $r=|x|$, and $t=u+r/c$. If maps $E_{\mathrm{rad}},B_{\mathrm{rad}}:S^2\times\mathbb R\to\mathbb R^3$ satisfy

$$rE(rn,u+r/c)\longrightarrow E_{\mathrm{rad}}(n,u),\qquad rB(rn,u+r/c)\longrightarrow B_{\mathrm{rad}}(n,u)$$

uniformly in $n\in S^2$ and $u$ in every compact interval as $r\to\infty$, then $E_{\mathrm{rad}}$ and $B_{\mathrm{rad}}$ are the electric and magnetic radiation data at fixed retarded time. The scaled fields carry units $\mathrm V$ and $\mathrm T\,\mathrm m$, respectively. This definition by itself does not assert that the limits exist.
