## Definition

Use the smooth off-shell coordinate model $\mathbb M=\mathbb R^4$, with $x^0=c_{\mathrm{light}}t$ in SI coordinates, metric $\eta=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,1,1)$, increasing $x^0$ as the future direction, and standard orientation. The scalar time-ordering convention sets $c_{\mathrm{light}}=1$, so $x^0$ is the length coordinate in that convention as well. Here off shell means that no field equation is imposed. Take smooth real components $a_\mu\in C^\infty(\mathbb M;\mathbb R)$ and a smooth commuting matter field $\psi\in C^\infty(\mathbb M;\mathbb C^4)$. Use the normalized real potential $a=a_\mu dx^\mu$ and dimensionless coupling $g=q\sqrt{\mu_0c_{\mathrm{light}}/\hbar}$ of the charged-gauge-data convention; $a_\mu$ has units $\mathrm m^{-1}$, $g$ is dimensionless, and $\psi$ has units $\mathrm m^{-3/2}$. The exact supplier convention is $a'_\mu=a_\mu+\partial_\mu\alpha$, $\psi'=e^{ig\alpha}\psi$, for a smooth dimensionless real infinitesimal gauge parameter $\alpha$.

Let $\mathcal P$ be the free graded-commutative $\mathbb C$-algebra over $C^\infty(\mathbb M;\mathbb C)$ generated by the finite jets of $a_\mu$, $\psi$, an odd ghost $c_{\mathrm{gh}}$, an odd antighost $\bar c$, and an even auxiliary variable $B$. Every element of $\mathcal P$ is a polynomial in finitely many jet coordinates. The fields $a_\mu$ and $\psi$ have Grassmann parity $0$ and ghost number $0$; $c_{\mathrm{gh}}$ has parity $1$ and ghost number $1$; $\bar c$ has parity $1$ and ghost number $-1$; and $B$ has parity $0$ and ghost number $0$. The ghost and nonminimal variables are formal algebraic generators; no state-space or positivity claim is made. The generators $c_{\mathrm{gh}},\bar c,B$ carry no physical unit assignment, and the differential below is dimensionless. The subscript distinguishes the ghost from the speed of light $c_{\mathrm{light}}$.

Define the odd derivation $s:\mathcal P\to\mathcal P$ of ghost number $1$ by
$$
sa_\mu=\partial_\mu c_{\mathrm{gh}},\qquad sc_{\mathrm{gh}}=0,\qquad s\bar c=B,\qquad sB=0,\qquad s\psi=igc_{\mathrm{gh}}\psi .
$$
Extend $s$ by the graded Leibniz rule $s(XY)=(sX)Y+(-1)^{|X|}X(sY)$ for homogeneous $X$, where $|X|$ is Grassmann parity, and by $s(\partial^\beta X)=\partial^\beta(sX)$ for every multi-index $\beta$. This is an off-shell local algebraic differential for the Abelian gauge transformations; it is not a BRST charge on a Hilbert space and it does not assert existence of a gauge-invariant quantum theory.
