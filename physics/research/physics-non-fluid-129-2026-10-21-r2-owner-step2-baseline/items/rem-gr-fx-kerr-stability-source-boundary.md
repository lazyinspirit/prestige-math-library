## Remark

The accessed 2021 Klainerman–Szeftel manuscript describes a nonlinear stability result for the Kerr family at sufficiently small angular momentum, in asymptotically flat solutions of the vacuum Einstein equations, and presents it as a multi-paper proof. This records the authors' stated scope; it is not a local proof or an independent acceptance of every companion paper. The 2008 review's discussion of then-known global constructions of dynamical vacuum black-hole spacetimes is historical and is not used here as current status. This remark makes no claim about whether the full subextremal or extremal Kerr stability problem is open or solved.
