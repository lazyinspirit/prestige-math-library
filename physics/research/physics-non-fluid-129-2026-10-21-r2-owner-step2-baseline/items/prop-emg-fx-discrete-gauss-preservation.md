## Statement

Use the finite cochain data and exact update specified in the definition and algorithm items cited immediately below, with step size $\tau>0$. Every update preserves both constraints $D_2b=0$ and $D_0^{\mathsf T}d=0$. Every continuously differentiable semidiscrete solution conserves $\mathcal E(b,d)$. For the staggered sequence, define

$$Q_n:=b_n^{\mathsf T}M_2b_n+d_{n+1/2}^{\mathsf T}M_1^{-1}d_{n+1/2}-\tau b_n^{\mathsf T}M_2D_1M_1^{-1}d_{n+1/2}.$$

Then $Q_n$ is exactly invariant under the update. If $S:=M_2^{1/2}D_1M_1^{-1/2}$, set $\sigma:=0$ when the domain of $S$ is zero and otherwise set $\sigma:=\max_{\lVert y\rVert_2=1}\lVert Sy\rVert_2$; for a nonzero domain this is the largest singular value of $S$. Then $Q_n$ is positive definite whenever $\tau\sigma<2$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** Finite-dimensional real spaces and matrices as in [[def-emg-fx-cochain-maxwell]], and either a continuously differentiable solution of its semidiscrete equations or an exact-arithmetic state sequence generated by [[alg-emg-fx-leapfrog-cochains]].

[F1] [[def-emg-fx-cochain-maxwell]]: $D_1D_0=0$, $D_2D_1=0$, $M_1,M_2$ are symmetric positive definite, the semidiscrete equations are $\dot b=-D_1M_1^{-1}d$, $\dot d=D_1^{\mathsf T}M_2b$, the constraints are $D_2b=0$ and $D_0^{\mathsf T}d=0$, and $\mathcal E=(b^{\mathsf T}M_2b+d^{\mathsf T}M_1^{-1}d)/2$.

[F2] [[alg-emg-fx-leapfrog-cochains]]: $b_{n+1}=b_n-\tau D_1M_1^{-1}d_{n+1/2}$ and $d_{n+3/2}=d_{n+1/2}+\tau D_1^{\mathsf T}M_2b_{n+1}$.

[F3] [[lem-cm-positive-definite-matrix-square-roots]]: a symmetric positive-definite matrix $M$ has a symmetric positive-definite square root and inverse square root, with $M^{-1/2}MM^{-1/2}=I$; the assertion applies to positive dimensions, and on the zero-dimensional space the unique map is handled directly.

[F4] [[thm-operator-norm-is-the-largest-singular-value]]: for a linear map between finite-dimensional inner-product spaces, the maximum of $\lVert Sy\rVert_2$ over unit domain vectors equals its largest singular value when the domain is nonzero; the norm is $0$ when the domain is zero.

[F5] [[thm-cauchy-schwarz-for-real-and-complex-inner-product-spaces]]: in a real Euclidean space, $|x^{\mathsf T}y|\leq\lVert x\rVert_2\lVert y\rVert_2$.

## Proof

1.1 For one update, the complex identities give
$D_2b_{n+1}=D_2b_n-\tau D_2D_1M_1^{-1}d_{n+1/2}=D_2b_n$ and
$D_0^{\mathsf T}d_{n+3/2}=D_0^{\mathsf T}d_{n+1/2}+\tau(D_1D_0)^{\mathsf T}M_2b_{n+1}=D_0^{\mathsf T}d_{n+1/2}$.
Thus both constraints are preserved, with no restriction on $\tau$. [given, F1, F2, algebra]

1.2 For a semidiscrete solution, symmetry of $M_2$ and $M_1^{-1}$ gives
$\frac{d}{dt}\mathcal E=\dot b^{\mathsf T}M_2b+\dot d^{\mathsf T}M_1^{-1}d
=-d^{\mathsf T}M_1^{-1}D_1^{\mathsf T}M_2b+d^{\mathsf T}M_1^{-1}D_1^{\mathsf T}M_2b=0$.
Therefore $\mathcal E$ is constant on the solution interval. [given, F1, algebra]

2.1 In positive dimensions set $x_n=M_2^{1/2}b_n$, $y_{n+1/2}=M_1^{-1/2}d_{n+1/2}$, and $S=M_2^{1/2}D_1M_1^{-1/2}$. By [F3], the update becomes $x_{n+1}=x_n-\tau Sy_{n+1/2}$ and $y_{n+3/2}=y_{n+1/2}+\tau S^{\mathsf T}x_{n+1}$. Write $x=x_n$, $y=y_{n+1/2}$, and $x'=x_{n+1}=x-\tau Sy$. Then
$\lVert y+\tau S^{\mathsf T}x'\rVert_2^2
=\lVert y\rVert_2^2+2\tau x'^{\mathsf T}Sy+\tau^2\lVert S^{\mathsf T}x'\rVert_2^2$
and
$-\tau x'^{\mathsf T}S(y+\tau S^{\mathsf T}x')
=-\tau x'^{\mathsf T}Sy-\tau^2\lVert S^{\mathsf T}x'\rVert_2^2$.
Adding these to $\lVert x'\rVert_2^2$ cancels the squared terms and leaves $\lVert x'\rVert_2^2+\lVert y\rVert_2^2+\tau x'^{\mathsf T}Sy$. Finally,
$\lVert x'\rVert_2^2+\tau x'^{\mathsf T}Sy
=\lVert x-\tau Sy\rVert_2^2+\tau(x-\tau Sy)^{\mathsf T}Sy
=\lVert x\rVert_2^2-\tau x^{\mathsf T}Sy$,
so $Q_{n+1}=Q_n$. If either coordinate space is zero-dimensional, the cross term vanishes and the update leaves the squared Euclidean norm on the remaining state space unchanged. [given, F1, F2, F3, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 By [F4] and [F5],
$|x^{\mathsf T}Sy|\leq\lVert x\rVert_2\lVert Sy\rVert_2\leq\sigma\lVert x\rVert_2\lVert y\rVert_2
\leq\frac{\sigma}{2}(\lVert x\rVert_2^2+\lVert y\rVert_2^2)$.
Hence $Q_n\geq(1-\tau\sigma/2)(\lVert x_n\rVert_2^2+\lVert y_{n+1/2}\rVert_2^2)$, which is strictly positive for every nonzero state when $\tau\sigma<2$. If $\sigma=0$ or a coordinate space is zero, the cross term vanishes and the same conclusion holds for every $\tau>0$. [F3, F4, F5, step 2.1, algebra] ∎

## Remarks

The sign in $Q_n$ follows from the specified order $b_n,d_{n+1/2}$. This is a finite algebraic result; the cited finite-element sources use their own field spaces, material operators and boundary conditions. No mesh-uniform stability or continuum-convergence theorem is claimed.
