## Statement

For each integer $N\geq0$, let $P_N(\mathbb C,2)$ be the quotient of the free
unital associative complex algebra on $u_1,\ldots,u_{N-1}$ by the relations
$$u_i^2=2u_i,\qquad u_iu_j=u_ju_i\ (|i-j|>1),\qquad u_iu_{i+1}u_i=u_i,\quad u_{i+1}u_iu_{i+1}=u_{i+1}.$$
This complex Temperley–Lieb algebra has a
conjugate-linear anti-involution $*$ determined by
$$c^*=\overline c\quad(c\in\mathbb C),\qquad u_i^*=u_i\quad(1\leq i<N),\qquad (xy)^*=y^*x^*.$$
For $N=0$ or $N=1$ this is complex conjugation on $\mathbb C$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** $N\geq0$ and the presentation $P_N(\mathbb C,2)$.

[F1] [[def-temperley-lieb-generator-presentation]]: the algebra is the quotient
of the free unital associative $\mathbb C$-algebra by the two-sided ideal
generated by $u_i^2-2u_i$, $u_i u_j-u_j u_i$ for $|i-j|>1$, and the two
relations $u_i u_{i+1}u_i-u_i$, $u_{i+1}u_i u_{i+1}-u_{i+1}$.

[F2] [[def-complex-numbers-and-arithmetic]],
[[lem-complex-conjugation-and-modulus-laws]]: complex conjugation is additive,
multiplicative, and involutive, and fixes the real scalar $2$.

## Proof

1.1 On a word $w=u_{i_1}\cdots u_{i_k}$ define
$w^*=u_{i_k}\cdots u_{i_1}$, extend by
$(\sum_w c_w w)^*=\sum_w\overline{c_w}w^*$, and set $1^*=1$. Reversal of
words and [F2] give $(xy)^*=y^*x^*$ and $(x^*)^*=x$ in the free algebra.
Thus this is a conjugate-linear anti-involution before taking the quotient.
[F2, construct, algebra]

2.1 The star fixes each relation $u_i^2-2u_i$ and each palindromic relation
$u_i u_{i+1}u_i-u_i$ and $u_{i+1}u_i u_{i+1}-u_{i+1}$. It sends a distant
commutator $u_i u_j-u_j u_i$ to its negative. Hence the star sends every
generator of the defining ideal into that ideal. [F1, F2, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Every element of the ideal is a finite sum of terms $x r y$ with $r$ a
displayed defining relation; its star is $y^*r^*x^*$, which is again in the
ideal by step 2.1. The ideal is therefore star-invariant, so the operation
descends to the quotient and remains conjugate-linear, anti-multiplicative,
and involutive there. If $N\leq1$ there are no generators or relations, and
the same construction is just coefficient conjugation. [F1, step 2.1, algebra] ∎
