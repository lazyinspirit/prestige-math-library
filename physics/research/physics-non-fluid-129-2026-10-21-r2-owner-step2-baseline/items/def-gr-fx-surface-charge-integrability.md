## Definition

Let $(\mathcal S,\Omega)$ be a specified smooth presymplectic solution space
and let $X_\xi$ be an admissible vector field generated by a specified
diffeomorphism parameter $\xi$ that preserves the boundary conditions. Define
the **charge-variation one-form**
$\alpha_\xi:=\iota_{X_\xi}\Omega$ on $\mathcal S$. It is **integrable on
$\mathcal S$** when there is a globally defined smooth function
$H_\xi:\mathcal S\to\mathbb R$ with $d_{\mathcal S}H_\xi=\alpha_\xi$; this
is the convention $\iota_{X_\xi}\Omega=d_{\mathcal S}H_\xi$. When a
finite-dimensional smooth parameter family is specified, a closed pullback
of $\alpha_\xi$ has local primitives on star-shaped coordinate patches;
global integrability on $\mathcal S$ is the stronger exactness condition.

## Remarks

A diffeomorphism direction is null exactly when
$\iota_{X_\xi}\Omega=0$. If an admissible boundary charge has nonzero
variation, that direction is not null and survives a reduction that quotients
only by null directions. Neither covariance nor on-shell closure alone
guarantees integrability. The potential ambiguity can change boundary terms
in $\Omega$, so integrability must be checked after the boundary conditions
and the choice of $\mu$ and $Y$ in the potential ambiguity are specified.
