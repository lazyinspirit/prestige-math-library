These mathematical examples use finite hypothetical data only. The all-plus
Bernoulli-spin sample has a boundary empirical mean and no finite field MLE.
The two-spin example computes the exact joint likelihood and the product of
one-site conditional probabilities separately; after normalization over the
same finite prior, they give different posterior distributions. No empirical
observation or statistical estimate is reported.
