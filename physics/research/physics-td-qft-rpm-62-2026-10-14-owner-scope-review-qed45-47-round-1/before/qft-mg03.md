This page develops finite-dimensional matrix kernels over $\mathbb C\llbracket z\rrbracket$, their coefficientwise formal inverses and $U(1)$ covariance, the finite Grassmann Gaussian determinant, explicit free-Dirac tree identities, and a free transverse-photon energy multiplier with an exact coherent cutoff family. Formal series are interpreted coefficientwise and the tree kernels are rational matrices away from their mass-shell poles. The coherent family is a mathematical free-photon construction. No loop-distribution product, all-orders Ward theorem, continuum QED construction, or physical charged-sector result is asserted. Draft QFT-MG01 gauge-data and transverse-photon definitions are present, but their page manifest still has an empty item inventory and no Step 1 readiness records; items using those interfaces remain conditional and their readiness is escalated.
