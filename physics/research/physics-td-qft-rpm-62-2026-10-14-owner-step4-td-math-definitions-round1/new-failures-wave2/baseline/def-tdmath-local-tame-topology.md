## Definition

Let $\mathcal P^1_{\rm stat}(\Omega)$ be the probability laws $P$ on the configuration space of [[def-tdmath-poisson-configuration-reference]] that are invariant under every $T_x$ and satisfy $E_PN_{C_0}<\infty$. Stationarity then makes every bounded-region count integrable, because a bounded set meets finitely many unit cells.

A real measurable function $f$ on $\Omega$ is local if there is a bounded Borel set $A$ such that $f$ is measurable with respect to the restriction $\omega\mapsto\omega|_A$. It is tame if there is a finite $b\geq0$ such that $|f(\omega)|\leq b(1+N_A(\omega))$ for every $\omega$ and one such bounded $A$. Write $\mathcal T$ for the family of all local tame functions. Each $f\in\mathcal T$ is integrable under every $P\in\mathcal P^1_{\rm stat}(\Omega)$.

The local-tame topology $\tau_L$ on $\mathcal P^1_{\rm stat}(\Omega)$ is the initial topology generated by all maps $P\mapsto E_P f$, $f\in\mathcal T$. Equivalently, a net $(P_\alpha)$ converges to $P$ in $\tau_L$ exactly when $E_{P_\alpha}f\to E_Pf$ for every measurable local $f$ with at most linear growth in the count of a bounded support. Bounded local indicators are among these tests, so this topology separates distinct probability laws. The domain restriction to finite-intensity stationary laws ensures every displayed expectation is a finite real number.
