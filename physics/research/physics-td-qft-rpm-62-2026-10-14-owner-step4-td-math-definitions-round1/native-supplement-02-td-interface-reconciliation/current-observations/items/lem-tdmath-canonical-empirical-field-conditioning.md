## Statement

Assume AC, the ultrafilter extension principle, DC, and HB, and let $\phi$ be superstable and regular as in [[def-tdmath-superstable-regular-interaction]]. Let $\Lambda_L=[0,L)^d$, $\beta,z>0$, and $b\in\{\mathrm{free},\mathrm{per}\}$. On the simple labelled sector $\mathcal S_{L,N}=\{q\in\Lambda_L^N:q_i\neq q_j\ (i\neq j)\}$ use $H_{L,\mathrm{free}}(q)=\sum_{i<j}\phi(q_i-q_j)$ and the periodic energy $H_{L,\mathrm{per}}$ from [[def-tdmath-superstable-regular-interaction]]. For $N\geq1$ define
$$Z_b(L,N;\beta)=\frac1{N!}\int_{\mathcal S_{L,N}}e^{-\beta H_{L,b}(q)}\,dq_1\cdots dq_N,\qquad Z_b(L,0;\beta)=1,$$
with $e^{-\beta(+\infty)}=0$, and put $\Xi_b(L,z,\beta)=\sum_{N\geq0}z^NZ_b(L,N;\beta)$. The unit-intensity Poisson reference $Q_{\Lambda_L}$ has sector density $e^{-L^d}/N!$ relative to labelled Lebesgue measure, so its normalized grand partition is
$$Z_{Q,b}(L;z,\beta)=E_{Q_{\Lambda_L}}[z^{N_{\Lambda_L}}e^{-\beta H_{L,b}}]=e^{-L^d}\Xi_b(L,z,\beta).$$
The exact-$N$ canonical law $C^b_{L,N}$ has density $e^{-\beta H_{L,b}}/(N!Z_b(L,N;\beta))$ on $\mathcal S_{L,N}$, and the grand-canonical law $G^b_{L,z,\beta}$ has density $z^{N_{\Lambda_L}}e^{-\beta H_{L,b}}/Z_{Q,b}(L;z,\beta)$ relative to $Q_{\Lambda_L}$. Let $\rho>0$ be an interior attainable density, choose $\alpha=\log z$ supporting the free canonical rate $F_\beta$ at $\rho$, and take $N_L/L^d\to\rho$. Assume
$$L^{-d}\log Z_b(L,N_L;\beta)\longrightarrow F_\beta(\rho).$$
Let $R_{L,\omega}$ be the periodized empirical field of [[def-tdmath-periodized-empirical-field]], and let
$$\mathcal M_{z,\beta}:=\operatorname*{argmin}_{P\in\mathcal P^1_{\rm stat}}\{I(P)+\beta\Phi(P)-\rho(P)\log z\}.$$
For any finite-volume law $C$ and arbitrary event $E$, write $C^*(E)=\inf\{C(A):E\subseteq A,\ A\text{ measurable}\}$ for its outer probability. Here measurability uses the configuration sigma-algebra; on law space the evaluation sigma-algebra is generated by $P\mapsto P(B)$ for measurable configuration events $B$. Convergence in probability in $\tau_L$ means that the outer probability of leaving each open neighborhood tends to zero; for measurable events this is ordinary probability. Then for every open $\tau_L$ neighborhood $U$ of $\mathcal M_{z,\beta}$ there is $c_U>0$ such that
$$\limsup_{L\to\infty}L^{-d}\log (C^b_{L,N_L})^*\{\,\omega:R_{L,\omega}\notin U\,\}\leq-c_U.$$
If $\mathcal M_{z,\beta}=\{P_*\}$, then $R_{L,\omega}\to P_*$ in probability in $\tau_L$ under $C^b_{L,N_L}$. This is a statement about spatially averaged local observables through the empirical field, not about the raw finite-box law at a fixed origin.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC, the ultrafilter extension principle, DC, HB, a superstable regular interaction, $\beta,z>0$, an interior density $\rho$, exact particle numbers $N_L/L^d\to\rho$, the displayed canonical rate limit, and the same finite-volume boundary condition in the canonical and grand-canonical laws.

[A1] [[def-axiom-of-choice]], [[def-ultrafilter-extension-principle]], [[def-dependent-choice]], and [[def-hahn-banach-extension-principle-relative]] are assumed, carrying the explicit compactness hypotheses of [F3].

[F1] [[def-tdmath-superstable-regular-interaction]]: the periodic image energy is absolutely defined under the stated tail convention, and the unit-intensity Poisson reference has sector density $e^{-L^d}/N!$; together with the Statement's local finite-volume definitions this gives $Z_{Q,b}=e^{-L^d}\Xi_b$ for either boundary condition.

[F2] [[thm-tdmath-canonical-grand-duality]]: a supporting $\alpha=\log z$ gives physical pressure $P(\alpha,\beta)=F_\beta(\rho)+\alpha\rho$.

[F3] [[thm-tdmath-continuum-variational-pressure]]: the normalized grand pressure is $p_Q(z,\beta)=P(\alpha,\beta)-1=-\min_P\{I(P)+\beta\Phi(P)-\rho(P)\log z\}$, and its minimizer set is nonempty and compact in $\tau_L$.

[F4] [[def-tdmath-local-tame-topology]], [[def-tdmath-periodized-empirical-field]] and [[lem-tdmath-periodized-empirical-field-stationary-density]]: $R_{L,\omega}$ is the stationary empirical field used by the grand empirical-field large-deviation principle.

[L1] Georgii's Theorem 2 (1994, Sections 3–5) gives, for superstable regular interactions, the large-deviation upper and lower bounds for evaluation-measurable sets in $\tau_L$ for these periodized empirical fields under both free and periodic grand-canonical Gibbs laws, with rate $I(P)+\beta\Phi(P)-\rho(P)\log z+p_Q(z,\beta)$. The evaluation sigma-algebra is distinguished from the topology on printed p.172; Theorem 2 requires measurable functionals, and its specialization to sets on pp.175–176 requires measurable sets. The complete Sections 3–5 proof was read; the chosen $b$ must be one of the two stated boundary conditions.

## Proof
**Given:** The hypotheses and suppliers in Facts & Assumptions.

1.1 The canonical law is the grand law conditioned on $N=N_L$, since the grand weight on the $N_L$ sector differs from the canonical density only by the constant activity factor $z^{N_L}$. Its conditioning probability is $$G^b_{L,z,\beta}(N=N_L)=\frac{z^{N_L}e^{-L^d}Z_b(L,N_L;\beta)}{Z_{Q,b}(L;z,\beta)}.$$ Divide the logarithm by $L^d$. The numerator terms tend to $\rho\log z+F_\beta(\rho)-1$ by the assumed canonical rate and [F1]; the denominator tends to $p_Q(z,\beta)$. By [F2]–[F3], $p_Q(z,\beta)=F_\beta(\rho)+\rho\log z-1$, so $$L^{-d}\log G^b_{L,z,\beta}(N=N_L)\longrightarrow0.$$ Thus the exact-number conditioning event has subexponential, positive grand probability for all sufficiently large $L$. [given, F1, F2, F3, algebra]

1.2 Fix an open neighborhood $U$ of $\mathcal M_{z,\beta}$. Each $P$ in this compact set has a basic neighborhood contained in $U$, described by finitely many strict inequalities in tame expectations from [F4]. These expectations are evaluation-measurable, so each such basic neighborhood is measurable. A finite subcover of $\mathcal M_{z,\beta}$ gives a measurable open $V$ with $\mathcal M_{z,\beta}\subseteq V\subseteq U$. Set $F=V^c$, which is closed and measurable. The good rate $J$ in [L1] has zero set exactly $\mathcal M_{z,\beta}$. Its infimum on $F$ is positive: otherwise the nonempty nested closed sets $F\cap\{J\leq1/n\}$ in the compact sublevel $\{J\leq1\}$ would have a common point, a zero outside $\mathcal M_{z,\beta}$. If $F$ is empty the estimate is immediate. The measurable-set upper bound in [L1] gives some $c>0$ with $\limsup L^{-d}\log G^b_{L,z,\beta}\{R_{L,\omega}\in F\}\leq-c$. Since $\{R\notin U\}\subseteq\{R\in F\}$, this also bounds its outer probability. [A1, F3, F4, L1, algebra]

2.1 The measurable event $\{R\in F\}$ contains $\{R\notin U\}$, so conditioning on $N=N_L$ yields $$(C^b_{L,N_L})^*\{R_{L,\omega}\notin U\}\leq \frac{G^b_{L,z,\beta}\{R_{L,\omega}\in F\}}{G^b_{L,z,\beta}(N=N_L)}.$$ The numerator decays exponentially by step 1.2 and the denominator has zero exponential rate by step 1.1, so the normalized logarithm of the left side has limsup at most $-c$. Taking $c_U=c$ proves the stated exponential bound, and the ratio tends to zero. This proves concentration on every neighborhood of $\mathcal M_{z,\beta}$. If the minimizer is unique, these neighborhood bounds are exactly convergence in probability in $\tau_L$. [step 1.1, step 1.2, algebra] ∎
