The Statement promises “a sufficiently small nonzero amount of transfer in both directions.” On its singular literal reading, this allows only one positive and one negative feasible transfer away from zero. Step 1.1 instead asserts feasibility of every sufficiently small t of either sign. The Given's shorthand “bilateral local feasibility” supplies no independent explicit quantifier to repair that mismatch. The intended ordinary local-exchange interpretation is plausible, but the existing sources do not give an operational definition forcing it.

A compatible local thermodynamic countermodel makes the gap concrete. Fix dimensionless delta >0 and use numerical energy E=U/(1 J), volume w=V/(1 m^3)>0 and amount z=n/(1 mol)>0. On the convex open domain E in (-2 delta,3 delta), w,z>0 adopt

    S(U,V,n) = S0 + [4 E - E^2/(2 delta) + log w + log z] (1 J K^-1).

This entropy is C2 and concave, and S_U=(4-E/delta) K^-1 is strictly positive everywhere on that domain. Its T and p maps have valid local charts: T/K=1/(4-E/delta) and p/Pa=(T/K)/w, so (E,w,z) can be locally recovered from (T,p,n) for positive T,p,n. Take two named copies/branches with central energies E_alpha=0 and E_beta=delta, volumes 1 m^3 and amounts 1 mol. Keep volume, amounts, all auxiliary states, and total energy fixed. Declare only the three complete partition tuples with transfer u in {0,+delta,-delta} feasible, so E_alpha=u and E_beta=delta-u. Supply the total entropy of this retained separated split as the sum; these partition tuples need not be states of the simple aggregate composition map, as the feasible-set definition expressly permits. Every named auxiliary is absent or fixed.

The entropy change from the central tuple is [u-u^2/delta] (1 J K^-1): zero at +delta and negative at -delta. Thus u=0 is an attained global maximum on this F; nonzero transfers of both signs are feasible and delta can be chosen as small as desired. Nevertheless S_alpha,U=4 K^-1 and S_beta,U=3 K^-1, so T_alpha=1/4 K and T_beta=1/3 K. The derivative in the energy-transfer direction is nonzero. This is a formal countermodel to the weak reading, not a material claim and not an added production item.

The smallest sufficiently rich replacement is two-sided accumulation, not an interval: for every epsilon>0 there exist feasible pure increments a,b in the channel's fixed SI numerical coordinate with 0<a<epsilon and -epsilon<b<0. Add the dimensioned increment to the selected coordinate of one phase and subtract it from the corresponding coordinate of the other; hold every nonselected coordinate of those phases, every other phase, and every auxiliary state fixed. Open charts provide an ambient interval on which the sum of the two changing phase entropies plus constant contributions is differentiable. At each positive feasible increment, its difference quotient is <=0; at each negative feasible increment it is >=0. Differentiability gives a unique limit, so the derivative is both <=0 and >=0 and equals zero. The chain rule identifies that derivative with h(S_alpha,U-S_beta,U), k(S_alpha,V-S_beta,V), or q(S_alpha,nj-S_beta,nj), respectively. The fixed nonzero h,k,q have units J, m^3, mol; parameter t is dimensionless. The stated derivative identities then give all original temperature, pressure/temperature, and chemical-potential/temperature equalities. Positive finite temperatures justify the subsequent cancellations. P=1 remains vacuous.
