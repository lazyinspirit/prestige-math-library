## Statement

For $n=2$ or $3$, let $X=\mathbb P^n_{\mathbb C}$ and let $X^{an}$ be its complex analytic space. Define analytification by $\mathcal F^{an}=\mathcal F\otimes_{\mathcal O_X}\mathcal O_{X^{an}}$. It is an exact tensor-compatible equivalence from algebraic coherent sheaves (locally finitely presented $\mathcal O_X$-modules) to holomorphic coherent sheaves. It identifies algebraic and holomorphic morphisms, preserves cohomology and the derived morphism groups $\operatorname{Ext}^q$, and commutes with restriction to the fixed hyperplane $L_\infty=\{z_0=0\}$. For an algebraic coherent sheaf $\mathcal E$ flat over a finite-type complex parameter scheme $S$, let $p:X\times S\to S$ be projection. Analytification commutes with the algebraic family structure and fiber restrictions. Whenever the algebraic and analytic higher direct images $R^qp_*\mathcal E$ are locally free and the natural maps from their residue-field fibers to $H^q(X_s,\mathcal E_s)$ are isomorphisms, the comparison identifies those direct images and base-change maps. No assertion is made that an arbitrary holomorphic family over a nonproper algebraic base algebraizes.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** $n\in\{2,3\}$, the algebraic projective space $X=\mathbb P^n_{\mathbb C}$, and its analytic space $X^{an}$.

[A1] For this conditional derivation, assume the projective analytic comparison package: local algebraic-to-holomorphic local-ring maps are flat and form flat pairs; coherent analytic sheaves on projective space and its hyperplanes satisfy Cartan Theorems A and B; positive twists of coherent analytic sheaves are generated by finitely many global sections as in Serre's §16; and analytic cohomology of twisting line bundles is the Dolbeault cohomology of projective space. These are the analytic inputs in Serre's proof, not consequences of the five listed algebraic suppliers. No current local carrier for this package was found.

[A2] For the family clause only, assume the relative analytic/algebraic direct-image comparison for the given algebraic flat family in the stated locally-free and base-change range. This clause makes no assertion for analytic families that do not arise by analytification.

[F1] Full AC is available through the finite-resolution and projective-cohomology suppliers. [[def-axiom-of-choice]]

[F2] Every coherent algebraic sheaf on projective space has a finite resolution by finite sums of twisting line bundles. [[lem-coherent-sheaf-finite-twisted-locally-free-resolution-projective-space]]

[F3] The algebraic cohomology of each twisting sheaf on $\mathbb P^n$ is finite-dimensional and has the standard projective-space values; sufficiently positive twists of a coherent sheaf have no higher cohomology. [[thm-cohomology-projective-space-twisting-sheaves]], [[thm-serre-vanishing]]

[F4] In a morphism of long exact sequences, if the four neighboring vertical maps are isomorphisms, then the middle vertical map is an isomorphism. [[thm-five-lemma-for-a-morphism-of-long-exact-sequences]]

[L1] The packages [A1] and [A2] are unresolved prerequisites: this item has not supplied the full local analytic proof or the relative family comparison carrier. The reference above contains the comparison argument but is not treated as a substitute for establishing those inputs locally.

## Proof

**Given:** The statement and [A1] for the conditional proof below.

1.1 Flatness of the local analytic-ring extension in [A1] makes analytification exact: tensoring an exact coherent stalk sequence with the holomorphic local ring preserves its kernels and cokernels. It also gives $(\mathcal F\otimes\mathcal G)^{an}\cong\mathcal F^{an}\otimes\mathcal G^{an}$ by associativity of stalkwise tensor product. Restriction to $L_\infty$ is the same stalkwise tensor construction along its local defining ideal, so analytification commutes with that restriction. [A1, algebra]

2.1 For $\mathcal O_X$, both zeroth cohomology groups are $\mathbb C$ and higher algebraic cohomology vanishes by [F3]; higher analytic cohomology vanishes by the Dolbeault clause of [A1]. For every integer $m$, the hyperplane sequence $0\to\mathcal O_X(m-1)\to\mathcal O_X(m)\to\mathcal O_{L}(m)\to0$ is exact on both sides by step 1.1. Induction on dimension for $L\cong\mathbb P^{n-1}$, followed by the long exact sequences and [F4], propagates the algebraic-to-analytic cohomology isomorphism from $m=0$ in both directions to every integer $m$. This proves comparison for every twisting line bundle. [A1, F3, F4, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Resolve a coherent algebraic $\mathcal F$ by the finite line-bundle resolution of [F2]. AC is retained through this finite-resolution supplier [F1]. By step 1.1 its analytification remains exact. Apply the cohomology comparison from step 2.1 to every term. Starting at the rightmost nonzero term and using the long exact cohomology sequences, induction on the finite resolution length proves $H^q(X,\mathcal F)\cong H^q(X^{an},\mathcal F^{an})$ for every $q$. Thus the analytification functor is fully cohomology-compatible on coherent objects. [A1, F1, F2, step 1.1, step 2.1, induction]

4.1 For coherent algebraic $\mathcal F,\mathcal G$, the sheaf $\mathcal H om(\mathcal F,\mathcal G)$ is coherent. Flatness in [A1] identifies its analytification with $\mathcal H om(\mathcal F^{an},\mathcal G^{an})$ stalkwise. Step 3.1 in degree zero therefore identifies algebraic and holomorphic morphisms. A finite locally free resolution of $\mathcal F$ computes $\operatorname{Ext}^q(\mathcal F,\mathcal G)$ by the hypercohomology of its Hom complex; exact analytification and termwise cohomology comparison identify the two finite Ext groups. [A1, F2, step 1.1, step 3.1]

5.1 To see essential surjectivity, let $\mathcal M$ be holomorphic coherent. The uniform positive-twist generation assertion included in [A1] supplies one integer $a$ and finitely many global generators of $\mathcal M(a)$, hence a surjection $\mathcal O(-a)^r\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal M$. Its kernel is coherent. Applying the same assertion to the kernel gives a surjection from a finite sum of twisting sheaves; composing it with the kernel inclusion yields a finite presentation by twisting sheaves. The matrix entries are holomorphic sections of line bundles; step 2.1 identifies each such section with an algebraic section, so the presentation matrix is algebraic. Its algebraic cokernel $\mathcal F$ analytifies to $\mathcal M$ by exactness in step 1.1. If two algebraic sheaves analytify to isomorphic holomorphic sheaves, step 4.1 algebraizes that isomorphism and its inverse; their composites remain identities by faithfulness. This proves essential surjectivity and uniqueness up to algebraic isomorphism. [A1, step 1.1, step 2.1, step 4.1, construct]

6.1 For an algebraic flat family on $X\times S$, the fiber-restriction map is algebraic tensor product with $\mathcal O_s$ before analytification and holomorphic tensor product afterward; step 1.1 identifies these constructions. AC is retained by the direct-image and base-change suppliers [F1]. Under [A2] and the statement's locally-free higher-direct-image and base-change hypotheses, the relative comparison map is defined and its residue-field fibers are the cohomology isomorphisms of step 3.1. A map of locally free sheaves that is an isomorphism on every residue-field fiber is an isomorphism, since its determinant is a unit in every local ring. This proves precisely the conditional family/base-change compatibility stated and makes no algebraization claim for arbitrary analytic families. [A1, A2, F1, step 1.1, step 3.1, algebra] ∎
