## Statement

Let $T=(\mathbb C^\times)^r$ act algebraically on a separated finite-type complex variety $X$ that admits a finite cover by $T$-invariant affine open subvarieties. Let $F=X^T$ be the fixed-point subscheme, $i:F\hookrightarrow X$ its closed inclusion, and let $S_T$ be the character multiplicative set in the localized equivariant Borel–Moore definition. Then in every homological degree $j$, proper pushforward induces an isomorphism
$$
i_*:S_T^{-1}H^{\mathrm{BM},T}_j(F;\mathbb Q)\xrightarrow{\ \cong\ }S_T^{-1}H^{\mathrm{BM},T}_j(X;\mathbb Q).
$$
No compactness of $X$, normality hypothesis, or compact-orbit-type nilpotence hypothesis is required.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The torus action, the finite invariant affine cover, rational coefficients, and the localized Borel–Moore data.

[A1] AC is inherited through the normalized equivariant Borel–Moore construction; no additional choice principle is used in the finite affine-cover argument. [[def-qftx-localized-equivariant-borel-moore-data]]

[F1] The normalized equivariant Borel–Moore groups are modules over $R_T$, with proper pushforwards and invariant-open restrictions. [[def-qftx-localized-equivariant-borel-moore-data]]

[F2] On an invariant affine principal open $D(f)$ cut out by a nonzero-weight eigenfunction, some nontrivial character class annihilates every equivariant Borel–Moore class, including after any further invariant open restriction. [[lem-qftx-eigenfunction-open-bm-annihilation]]

[F3] A closed subset and its open complement give the equivariant Borel–Moore long exact sequence through support localization. [[thm-constructible-six-functors-proper-base-change-and-verdier-duality]]

[F4] Localization of $R_T$-modules by $S_T$ preserves short and long exact sequences. [[lem-qftx-module-localization-preserves-exactness]]

## Proof

**Given:** The data in the Statement.

1.1 Fix a member $U=\operatorname{Spec}A$ of the invariant affine cover. Choose finitely many algebra generators of $A$. The algebraic $T$-action makes $A$ a rational $T$-comodule, so the coaction of each generator is a finite Laurent sum and the generator is a finite sum of character eigenfunctions. Replacing the generators by these finitely many eigencomponents still generates $A$, since their sums recover the original generators. The fixed ideal in $A$ is generated by its nontrivial-weight subspace: the coaction sends a weight-$\chi$ element $f$ to $f\otimes\chi$, so the equalizer with the trivial coaction kills $f$ when $\chi\ne1$, and the quotient by all such elements has only weight-zero functions and hence trivial action. The ideal generated by the nontrivial-weight algebra generators contains every nonzero-weight polynomial in the generators, since each monomial of nonzero total weight has a nonzero-weight factor. Thus $U\setminus F$ is covered by the finitely many invariant principal opens $D(f)$ for those generators. [given, construct]

2.1 Taking these principal opens over the finitely many affine charts gives a finite invariant open cover $\{D_a\}$ of $X\setminus F$. Every nonempty finite intersection is a $T$-invariant open subset of at least one $D_a$; [F2] therefore makes its localized Borel–Moore groups zero in every degree. [F1, F2, step 1.1]

3.1 For a finite invariant open cover of $X\setminus F$, the augmented Čech complex with terms $j_{I!}\mathbb Q_{D_I}$, where $D_I$ is an intersection and $j_I:D_I\hookrightarrow X\setminus F$ is open, resolves the constant equivariant sheaf. At a point the indices containing it form a nonempty simplex, whose augmented cochain complex is contracted by its least index. Applying derived Hom into the normalized dualizing object gives a finite Čech spectral sequence for Borel–Moore homology: open base change identifies each term with the Borel–Moore group of an intersection $D_I$. Those groups vanish after localization by [F2]. Since the Čech filtration is finite and localization is exact by [F4], the abutment $S_T^{-1}H^{\mathrm{BM},T}_j(X\setminus F;\mathbb Q)$ is zero for every $j$. [F1, F2, F4, step 2.1, construct]

4.1 The closed/open localization sequence for $i:F\hookrightarrow X$ and $X\setminus F$ contains $S_T^{-1}H^{\mathrm{BM},T}_{j+1}(X\setminus F)\to S_T^{-1}H^{\mathrm{BM},T}_j(F)\xrightarrow{i_*}S_T^{-1}H^{\mathrm{BM},T}_j(X)\to S_T^{-1}H^{\mathrm{BM},T}_j(X\setminus F)$. Both outside groups vanish by step 3.1, so $i_*$ is an isomorphism. If $F=\varnothing$, the same argument gives zero localized Borel–Moore homology for $X$; if $F=X$, $i_*$ is the identity. The proof used only the finite invariant affine cover and not Sumihiro's theorem or compactness. [A1, F1, F3, F4, step 3.1] ∎
