A finite collection of holonomies witnesses every connection in the compact irreducible boundary family uniformly, using Grassmannian compactness. Give those loops small disjoint control arcs, unused by the other loops, by sufficiently small detours preserving the uniform irreducibility margin; add extra loops as needed. Bump one-forms along the arcs, with Lie-algebra coefficients transported by the original connection, make the derivative of the r-holonomy map onto SU(N)^r surjective in finite perturbation parameters. This is an explicit ODE variation calculation. The forms may depend smoothly on interior disk parameters; local normal extensions supply genuine global adjoint-valued forms.

Choose r so that this is smaller than perturbation dimension for every k. Sard then makes the union of projected bad parameters null, so arbitrarily small good parameters exist. A cutoff fixes the boundary collar. Where the cutoff is positive the normalized holonomy derivative must remain invertible uniformly; where it vanishes the original family was already irreducible. Relative simplicial induction handles general finite polyhedra.
