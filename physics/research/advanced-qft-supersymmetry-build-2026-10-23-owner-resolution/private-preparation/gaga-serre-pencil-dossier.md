## 1. Both K_s and Q_s are needed

For each nonzero linear section s, let H_s={s=0} and

K_s=ker(F(-1) --s-->F), I_s=im(F(-1) --s-->F), Q_s=coker(F(-1) --s-->F).

K_s and Q_s are annihilated by the ideal of H_s: locally multiplication by its equation t kills the kernel and quotient. Thus they are coherent modules on H_s, including when F has torsion or s is a zero divisor. Twisting gives exact sequences

(1) 0→K_s(n)→F(n-1)→I_s(n)→0,
(2) 0→I_s(n)→F(n)→Q_s(n)→0.

Inductive GAGA and algebraic Serre make every positive cohomology of K_s(n),Q_s(n) vanish eventually. For q≥1, the segment

H^qK_s(n)→H^qF(n-1)→H^qI_s(n)→H^(q+1)K_s(n)

makes the middle arrow an eventual isomorphism. The segment

H^(q-1)Q_s(n)→H^qI_s(n)→H^qF(n)→H^qQ_s(n)

makes multiplication by s eventually an isomorphism for q≥2, but only eventually surjective for q1. The H⁰Q_s term prevents an initial assertion of q1 injectivity.

## 2. Stabilization and the two-section pencil

Fix s0. For q1 its eventual surjections make the finite dimensions h¹(F(n)) a nonincreasing sequence of integers; they stabilize. After increasing N, multiplication by s0 is an isomorphism for all n≥N. For q≥2 choose N directly from Section1.

Fix q and V=H^qF(N). Define a_N=id and a_(n+1)=m_(s0,n)a_n, giving isomorphisms V→H^qF(n). Choose s1 independent of s0 (r≥1) and set

T_n=a_(n+1)^-1 m_(s1,n)a_n.

Commutativity m_(s1,n+1)m_(s0,n)=m_(s0,n+1)m_(s1,n) gives T_(n+1)=T_n=:T. If V≠0, choose an eigenvalue lambda of T. The section s_lambda=s1-lambda*s0 is nonzero, while its multiplication under these identifications is always T-lambda*id, singular.

Apply Section1 to THIS fixed s_lambda: for q≥2 its multiplication is eventually an isomorphism, a contradiction. For q1 it is eventually surjective between spaces of the same stabilized dimension, hence also an isomorphism, again a contradiction. Therefore V0 and H^qF(n)=0 for n≥N.

The order of quantifiers is essential: choose lambda first, then pass beyond the threshold for s_lambda. No twist bound uniform over an infinite pencil is asserted.

## 4. Generation and a uniform twist

Fix p and choose a hyperplane H_s through p. Inductive GAGA and algebraic Serre generation give finitely many global generators of Q_s(n) for sufficiently large n. Also choose n so H¹F(n-1)=H²K_s(n)=0. Sequence(1) gives H¹I_s(n)=0; sequence(2) then makes H⁰F(n)→H⁰Q_s(n) surjective. Lift the finite generators.

Let C be their coherent cokernel. At p, C_p/tC_p=0 because their reductions generate F(n)_p/tF(n)_p. Since t is in the maximal ideal, Nakayama gives C_p0. Coherence/finite local generation yields an open U_p where these sections generate.

Choose a homogeneous coordinate ell_p nonzero at p and shrink U_p into {ell_p≠0}. Compactness gives finitely many such U_j covering X, with sections generating F(n_j). For every n≥max n_j multiply the j-th set by ell_j^(n-n_j). The resulting GLOBAL sections of F(n) still generate on U_j, because ell_j is a unit there. Their finite union generates globally. This proves a uniform eventual generation bound, not just pointwise generation at varying twists.
