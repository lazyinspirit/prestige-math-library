## Statement

Assume ZF together with the Axiom of Choice. Let $M$ be $S^3$ or $S^4$ with its compact, connected, oriented, boundaryless smooth structure and a smooth Riemannian metric. Let $N\ge2$, let $P\to M$ be a smooth principal right $SU(N)$-bundle, choose a smooth reference connection $A_0$, a basepoint $m\in M$, and a frame of the fiber $P_m$. Put $\mathcal A_\infty=A_0+\Omega^1(M;\operatorname{ad}P)$ with its $C^\infty$ section topology. For a smooth connection $A$, let $\operatorname{Hol}_A\subseteq SU(N)$ be the group generated by parallel transports along based piecewise-smooth loops at $m$, expressed in the chosen frame. Call $A$ irreducible when the standard action of $\operatorname{Hol}_A$ on $\mathbb C^N$ has no nonzero proper invariant complex subspace, and write $\mathcal A_\infty^*$ for this locus. For each integer $q>\dim(M)/2+1$, let $\mathcal G_{q+1}$ be the $H^{q+1}$ gauge group of sections of $\operatorname{Ad}P$, acting on $H^q$ connections by the local formula $A^u=u^{-1}Au+u^{-1}du$, and let $Z_N=\{\zeta I_N:\zeta^N=1\}$. Measure $C^1$ closeness by coefficient and first-derivative suprema in a fixed finite bundle atlas. For every $A\in\mathcal A_\infty^*$, a finite tuple of based piecewise-smooth loops has holonomies with no common nonzero proper invariant subspace; the same tuple continues to witness irreducibility on a $C^1$ neighborhood of $A$, so $\mathcal A_\infty^*$ is open in the $C^\infty$ topology. The stabilizer of $A$ in $\mathcal G_{q+1}$ is exactly $Z_N$. Irreducibility here does not assert $\operatorname{Hol}_A=SU(N)$.

[F4] A continuous real-valued function on a nonempty compact space attains its minimum ([[thm-compactness-under-continuous-maps]], claim 2).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 By [F1], $\mathcal A_\infty^*$ is exactly the set of connections whose holonomy action has no common proper nonzero invariant subspace. By [F2], for each such connection the stabilizer in the stated Sobolev gauge group is $Z_N$. The latter is the conditional stabilizer conclusion recorded separately from the holonomy criterion. [A1, given, F1, F2]

1.2 For each $V\in\mathscr G_N$, irreducibility supplies a based piecewise-smooth loop $\ell_V$ whose holonomy $g_V=\operatorname{Hol}_A(\ell_V)$ does not preserve $V$. If $P_V$ is the orthogonal projection onto $V$, then $(I-P_V)g_VP_V\ne0$. For each fixed loop $\ell$, the set of $V$ with $(I-P_V)\operatorname{Hol}_A(\ell)P_V\ne0$ is open, since the projector topology and matrix multiplication are continuous. These open sets cover $\mathscr G_N$. [F1, F3, given, construct]

2.1 Compactness in [F3] gives a finite subcover, hence finitely many loops $\ell_1,\ldots,\ell_r$ for which no proper nonzero subspace is invariant under every $g_j=\operatorname{Hol}_A(\ell_j)$. The case $r=0$ cannot cover the nonempty space $\mathscr G_N$, so $r\ge1$. [F3, step 1.2, choose]

3.1 Define the continuous function
$$h_A(P)=\sum_{j=1}^r\|(I-P)g_jP\|_F$$
on $\mathscr G_N$, where $\|\cdot\|_F$ is the Frobenius norm. It is positive at every $P$ by step 2.1. By [F4] it has a minimum $\delta>0$, and $h_A(P)\ge\delta$ for every $P\in\mathscr G_N$. [F3, F4, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 On a smooth segment of any chosen loop, write parallel transport as $U'=-B(t)U$, where $B(t)\in\mathfrak{su}(N)$ is the connection form contracted with the path tangent; [F5] gives the unique solution on the whole segment. For a nearby connection with coefficient $B+b$, let $\widetilde U$ be its solution and put $W=U^{-1}\widetilde U$. Differentiation gives $W'=-U^{-1}bU\,W$, $W(0)=I$, and skew-Hermiticity makes both $U$ and $W$ unitary. The integral equation therefore gives $\|W(1)-I\|_F\le\int\|b(t)\|_F\,dt$. A $C^1$ perturbation of the connection makes each of the finitely many segment integrals arbitrarily small, so finite composition shows all $g_j$ vary continuously in the stated $C^1$ topology. Choose a neighborhood with $\|g_j(A')-g_j(A)\|_F<\delta/(2r)$ for every $j$. [F5, step 2.1, step 3.1, algebra, choose]

5.1 For any orthogonal projection $P$, both $\|P\|_{\mathrm{op}}$ and $\|I-P\|_{\mathrm{op}}$ are at most $1$. Thus $$\left|\|(I-P)\operatorname{Hol}_{A'}(\ell_j)P\|_F-\|(I-P)g_jP\|_F\right|\le\|\operatorname{Hol}_{A'}(\ell_j)-g_j\|_F.$$ Summing over $j$ and using step 4.1 together with the uniform positive margin from step 3.1 gives $h_{A'}(P)>\delta/2$ uniformly in $P$. Hence the same finite tuple has no common invariant proper nonzero subspace for every such $A'$. [step 3.1, step 4.1, algebra]

6.1 A $C^1$ neighborhood on the compact base is specified by finitely many continuous section seminorms; each is continuous in the $C^\infty$ section topology. Therefore the neighborhood in step 4.1 is also a $C^\infty$ neighborhood, and $\mathcal A_\infty^*$ is open. step 2.1 gives the finite holonomy witness at $A$, and step 1.1 gives the stated stabilizer. [F1, step 1.1, step 2.1, step 4.1, step 5.1] ∎
