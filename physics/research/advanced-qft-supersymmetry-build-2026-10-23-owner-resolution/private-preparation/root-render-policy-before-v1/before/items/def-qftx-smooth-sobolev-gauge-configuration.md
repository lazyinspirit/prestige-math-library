## Definition

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $M$ be a compact, connected, oriented, boundaryless smooth manifold of dimension $d\in\{3,4\}$ with a smooth Riemannian metric, let $N\ge2$, and fix a smooth right principal $SU(N)$-bundle $\pi:P\to M$, a smooth connection $A_0$, and a point $p_m\in P$ over $x_m\in M$. Use the standard unitary representation of $SU(N)$ on $\mathbb C^N$ and the invariant positive inner product $\langle X,Y\rangle=-\operatorname{tr}(XY)$ on $\mathfrak{su}(N)$. Define the adjoint vector bundle $\operatorname{ad}P=P\times_{\operatorname{Ad}}\mathfrak{su}(N)$ and the adjoint group bundle $\operatorname{Ad}P=P\times_{\operatorname{Ad}}SU(N)$ with associated-bundle relation $[pg,z]=[p,\operatorname{Ad}_g z]$.

Choose one finite smooth bundle atlas with relatively compact coordinate domains and a subordinate partition of unity. For each integer $k>d/2+1$, let $H^k(T^*M\otimes\operatorname{ad}P)$ and $H^{k+1}(\operatorname{ad}P)$ be the Sobolev completions of smooth sections in the resulting finite-atlas norms. Define the affine configuration space $\mathcal A_k:=A_0+H^k(T^*M\otimes\operatorname{ad}P)$ and the set $\mathcal G_{k+1}:=H^{k+1}(\operatorname{Ad}P)$ of Sobolev gauge sections. The next lemma proves that the pointwise product and inverse close on this set and make it a Hilbert Lie group. Its based subset is $\mathcal G_{k+1,m}:=\{u\in\mathcal G_{k+1}:u(p_m)=e\}$. Let $Z_N=\{zI_N:z^N=1\}$ be the center of $SU(N)$, viewed as constant gauge sections. Indeed, if $Z=(z_{ij})$ is central and $D=\operatorname{diag}(t_1,\ldots,t_N)\in SU(N)$, then $(t_j-t_i)z_{ij}=0$. For each $i\ne j$ one can choose such a diagonal matrix with $t_i\ne t_j$, so $Z$ is diagonal. Commuting with the determinant-one unitary rotation whose block on the $(i,j)$ coordinates is $\begin{pmatrix}\cos t&\sin t\\-\sin t&\cos t\end{pmatrix}$ for $t\notin\pi\mathbb Z$ forces the $i$th and $j$th diagonal entries equal. Hence $Z=zI_N$; determinant one gives $z^N=1$, and conversely every such scalar is central. Write $\bar{\mathcal G}_{k+1}$ for the set of $Z_N$-cosets with quotient topology; the next lemma also verifies that the induced pointwise operation makes it a Hilbert Lie group.

Let $\mathcal A_\infty:=A_0+\Omega^1(M,\operatorname{ad}P)$ and let $\mathcal G_\infty$ be the smooth gauge group, with the $C^\infty$ section topology generated by all finite-atlas derivative seminorms. Define $\bar{\mathcal G}_\infty:=\mathcal G_\infty/Z_N$. In a local section of $P$, write a connection as a matrix-valued one-form $A$ and a gauge section as a map $u$ into $SU(N)$; the right gauge action is $A^u:=u^{-1}Au+u^{-1}du$, equivalently pullback by the gauge automorphism associated with $u$. The calculus lemma proves that this formula defines a smooth action on the Sobolev spaces above. For an adjoint section $\xi$, set $d_A\xi:=d\xi+[A,\xi]$ using the degree-one Lie-algebra-valued-form bracket. In local coordinates and an orthonormal adjoint frame, define the formal adjoint on an adjoint-valued one-form $a$ by $(d_A^*a)=-g^{ij}(\nabla_i^{\mathrm{LC}}a_j+[A_i,a_j])$, where $\nabla^{\mathrm{LC}}$ differentiates the covector index. This is the $L^2$ adjoint for the Riemannian volume and stated fiber metric; its local coefficient formula defines a distribution at Sobolev regularity.

For $A\in\mathcal A_k$, parallel transport of its $C^1$ connection form around piecewise smooth loops based at $p_m$ defines a subgroup $\operatorname{Hol}_{p_m}(A)\subseteq SU(N)$. Call $A$ irreducible when this subgroup leaves no nonzero proper complex subspace of $\mathbb C^N$ invariant; changing the chosen frame conjugates the subgroup and leaves this condition unchanged. Write $\mathcal A_k^*$ and $\mathcal A_\infty^*$ for the irreducible loci. Once the calculus lemma verifies the group action, define orbit equivalence by $A\sim B$ exactly when $B=A^u$ for some gauge section $u$; all resulting quotients carry the quotient topology, whose open sets are those with open inverse image under the orbit projection.
