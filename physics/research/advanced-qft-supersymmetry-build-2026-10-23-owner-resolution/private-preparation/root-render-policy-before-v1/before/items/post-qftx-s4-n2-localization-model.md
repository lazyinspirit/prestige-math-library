## Postulate

Use natural units $c=\hbar=1$. For $r>0$, let

$$S^4_r=\{y\in\mathbb R^5:\sum_{j=1}^5 y_j^2=r^2\}$$

with its standard orientation, round metric, scalar curvature $12/r^2$, and standard spin structure. In stereographic length coordinates $x^\mu$, $\mu=1,\ldots,4$, use

$$ds^2=\frac{\sum_{\mu=1}^4 (dx^\mu)^2}{(1+x^2/(4r^2))^2},\qquad x^2=\sum_{\mu=1}^4(x^\mu)^2.$$

Let $G=U(2)$, with Lie algebra $\mathfrak u(2)=\{X\in M_2(\mathbb C):X^\dagger=-X\}$ and positive invariant form $(X,Y)=-\operatorname{tr}_{\mathbf 2}(XY)$. A gauge sector is a smooth right principal $U(2)$ bundle $P\to S^4_r$ up to smooth bundle isomorphism, with a smooth connection $A$ and curvature $F_A=dA+A\wedge A$ in a local trivialization. Fields in the adjoint representation are sections of the associated adjoint bundle. This postulate does not classify the bundle sectors. 

The microscopic field content is one four-dimensional rigid $\mathcal N=2$ vector multiplet and one adjoint hypermultiplet: the vector multiplet has the connection, one complex adjoint scalar, two adjoint Weyl fermions, and an $SU(2)_R$ auxiliary triplet; the hypermultiplet has two complex adjoint scalars and one adjoint Dirac fermion. There is no other matter. 

The hypermultiplet has real Euclidean mass $m_E$ of mass dimension one, supplied by a background flavor-vector multiplet. 

In the source's orthonormal basis for its four scalar directions, write its mass matrix as $M_{ij}=i m_EJ_{ij}$, where $J$ is the fixed real antisymmetric self-dual complex structure with $J^2=-I$ and source normalization $J_{ij}J^{ij}=4$. This is the $\mathcal N=2^*$ model; $m_E=0$ is its $\mathcal N=4$ limit. 

Adopt the source's Euclidean curvature-coupled action and Scherk–Schwarz mass deformation, with gauge kinetic normalization

$$S_{\mathrm{YM}}=\frac{1}{2g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2} \int_{S^4_r}d^4x\sqrt g\,(F_{\mu\nu},F^{\mu\nu}),$$

where $g_{\mathrm{YM}}>0$ is dimensionless and the trace is in the defining representation. 

The theta angle $\theta$ is real and dimensionless. Set

$$\tau=\frac{4\pi i}{g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2}+\frac{\theta}{2\pi},\qquad q=e^{2\pi i\tau}.$$

The rigid background data are a fixed metric, spin structure and connection, trivial $SU(2)_R$ background bundle with zero connection, the mass background, and a nonzero conformal Killing-spinor pair $(\epsilon,\widetilde\epsilon)$ in the source's gamma-matrix convention satisfying

$$\nabla_\mu\epsilon=\widetilde\Gamma_\mu\widetilde\epsilon,\qquad \nabla_\mu\widetilde\epsilon=-\frac{1}{4r^2}\Gamma_\mu\epsilon.$$

Choose the source's $\mathcal N=2$ projection $\Gamma_{5678}\epsilon=\epsilon$ and spinors with $\widetilde\epsilon\epsilon=0$. The selected off-shell supercharge $Q$ uses the source's seven auxiliary scalars and transformations (2.26)–(2.30). With $v^\mu=\epsilon\Gamma^\mu\epsilon$, its square on the vector multiplet is the lifted symmetry

$$Q^2=-\mathcal L_v-\delta_{\mathrm{gauge}}(v^M A_M)-\delta_R-\delta_\Omega,$$

 where $\mathcal L_v$ is the lifted Lie derivative, $\delta_R$ is the rotation generated by $R_{AB}=2\widetilde\epsilon\widetilde\Gamma_{AB} \epsilon$, and $\delta_\Omega$ is the dilation proportional to $2\widetilde\epsilon\epsilon=0$. On hypermultiplet fields the mass deformation adds the source's flavor rotation: $-v^0M_{ij}\Phi_j$ on its four scalar components and $-\tfrac14v^0M_{ij}\Gamma^{ij}\chi$ on its fermion, where $M_{ij}$ is the specified mass matrix. The vector $v$ is the round-sphere isometry rotating the two orthogonal coordinate planes with equal angular magnitudes $r^{-1}$, so the pole equivariant parameters are $\epsilon_1=\epsilon_2=r^{-1}$. 

Adopt the source's formal gauge-fixing and ghost complex. On its gauge-fixing slice $d^*A=0$, the Faddeev–Popov operator is $d^*\nabla_A$ and the determinant $\det'(d^*\nabla_A)$ omits its zero modes; constant gauge transformations are factored by the gauge-group volume. The BRST-like differential $\delta$ acts by $\delta X=-[c,X]$, $\delta c=-a_0-\tfrac12[c,c]$, $\delta\widetilde c=b$, and $\delta a_0=0$, where $c,\widetilde c$ are the ghost and antighost, $b$ is the multiplier, and $a_0$ is the constant ghost for ghost. The source adds constant variables $\widetilde a_0,b_0,c_0,\widetilde c_0$ to handle the zero-mode sector and uses $Q=s+\delta$; its square is the constant gauge transformation together with the selected $U(1)$ rotation. This is a formal source prescription, not a construction of a global gauge-fixing slice or measure on every bundle. 

In the Euclidean contour, gauge and hypermultiplet fields take values in the real Lie algebra; the scalar descending from the time component is continued as $\Phi_0=i\Phi_0^E$, the seven localization auxiliaries as $K_i=iK_i^E$, and $\Phi_0^E,K_i^E$ are real Lie-algebra-valued fields. The finite-dimensional variable $a$ in the matrix integral uses the source's continued Cartan contour. Let $\Sigma$ denote the chosen noncommutative $\mathbb R^4$ regulator for the instanton sectors, with its $U(1)^2$ rotations and the above $\epsilon_i$. Adopt the additional source assumption that a supersymmetry-compatible noncommutative deformation on $S^4_r$ exists and agrees near each pole with Nekrasov's noncommutative $\mathbb R^4$ deformation. Pestun explicitly does not construct this $S^4$ deformation. The gauge-fixed functional measure, contour, and this regulator extension remain formal model inputs; this postulate proves no measure existence, anomaly cancellation, regulator-removal limit, continuum Q-exact identity, or empirical claim.
