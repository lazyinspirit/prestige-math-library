## 967.15 — Oka coherence and local coherent free resolutions

Proposed page: `qftx-gaga-analytic-coherence`; theorem: `thm-qftx-gaga-analytic-oka-coherence`.

Statement: Assume AC. On every finite-dimensional complex manifold M the holomorphic structure sheaf O_M is coherent: the relation sheaf of any finite tuple of local holomorphic sections is locally finitely generated. Coherent O_M-modules are closed under kernels, cokernels and finite extensions. For every coherent F, point x and finite integer l≥0, there is a chart neighborhood W of x on which F has an exact partial free resolution O_W^{p_l}→…→O_W^{p_0}→F|_W→0 by finite ranks. Exactness at the leftmost term is not asserted. No global analytic free resolution is claimed.

Proof obligation: Prove the Weierstrass-induction relation lemma with generators uniform on one neighborhood, following Demailly II3.19–3.20. Handle zero/unit tuple entries and coordinate changes. Prove the elementary coherent-kernel calculus and obtain each finite partial resolution by successive coherent kernels and finite shrinking. Stalk Noetherianity alone is insufficient.

Existing/new dependencies: `def-axiom-of-choice`, `thm-holomorphic-germ-ring-is-noetherian`, `thm-weierstrass-preparation-theorem`, `thm-weierstrass-division-theorem`, `lem-generic-linear-coordinate-makes-a-holomorphic-germ-regular`, `thm-finite-generation-and-finite-presentation-over-a-noetherian-ring`, `thm-exactness-of-sheaves-stalkwise`.

Source: https://www-fourier.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/~demailly/manuscripts/agbook.pdf — II §3.3, Theorem3.19 and Lemma3.20, printed/PDF pp.89–90; complete proof read.

Examples:

* `ex-qftx-gaga-analytic-relation-kernel`: For (z_1,z_2):O^2→O on a bidisc, its relation sheaf is generated by (-z_2,z_1); show local exactness including the origin by power-series division. Verification obligation: Prove divisibility and gluing, not just away-from-origin rank.

## 967.16 — Faithful flatness of finite-type algebraic-to-analytic local rings

Proposed page: `qftx-gaga-local-analytic-flatness`; theorem: `thm-qftx-gaga-analytic-local-ring-flatness`.

Statement: Assume AC. Let X be a finite-type complex scheme, x a complex closed point, and define X^an locally by extending a finite polynomial defining ideal to convergent holomorphic functions, retaining nilpotents. The canonical local map A=O_{X,x}→B=O_{X^an,x} is injective and faithfully flat; its induced map on maximal-ideal completions is an isomorphism. In Serre’s flat-pair convention, B/A is flat over A and M→M⊗_A B is injective for every A-module M. On an affine ambient chart at x, C[t]_(t) and C{t} have common completion C[[t]], and the quotient comparison is compatible with every finitely generated defining ideal. This is a local-ring assertion, not a relative direct-image theorem.

Proof obligation: Identify finite jets on polynomial local and convergent rings by truncation and finite degree-k monomial grouping of convergent tails. Apply existing completion flatness: T is flat over A and faithfully flat over B. For each injection of A-modules tensor its B-kernel with T, use A-flatness of T and faithful reflection to get zero kernel. Prove local faithfulness and unit injection, then Tor gives B/A flat. Pass to the same extended defining ideal for finite-type quotients; do not invoke reduced-set Nullstellensatz.

Existing/new dependencies: `def-axiom-of-choice`, `thm-holomorphic-germs-at-a-point-form-a-local-ring`, `thm-power-series-expansion-in-several-complex-variables`, `thm-holomorphic-germ-ring-is-noetherian`, `thm-hilbert-basis-theorem`, `thm-noetherian-ring-quotients-and-localisations`, `thm-completion-of-a-noetherian-local-ring`, `thm-flatness-of-noetherian-completion`, `thm-flatness-criteria-by-injections-and-ideals`, `thm-faithfully-flat-ring-map-characterisations`, `thm-long-exact-tor-sequence-in-the-right-module-variable`.

Source: https://www.college-de-france.fr/media/jean-pierre-serre/UPL1698785858825941763_Serre_GAGA.pdf — §6 Proposition3/Corollary1 printed pp.9–11; Annex Propositions22–23,27–29 printed pp.36–40; read.

Examples:

* `ex-qftx-gaga-analytic-equal-completions`: C[t]_(t)→C{t} is faithfully flat with common completion C[[t]], but is not surjective. Verification obligation: Use a convergent nonrational germ, e.g. exp(t), and prove its nonrationality.

* `ex-qftx-gaga-analytic-nonreduced-quotient`: C[x,y]_(x,y)/(x^2)→C{x,y}/(x^2) is faithfully flat and retains the nonzero nilpotent class x. Verification obligation: Apply quotient flatness and verify x≠0; do not replace by reduced vanishing sets.

## 967.17 — Local coherent acyclicity on sufficiently small polydiscs

Proposed page: `qftx-gaga-local-coherent-acyclicity`; theorem: `thm-qftx-gaga-analytic-local-coherent-polydisc-acyclicity`.

Statement: Assume AC. Let F be coherent analytic on a neighborhood of a point x in C^N. There is a coordinate polydisc W about x such that for every nonempty coordinate polydisc D⊂W and every q>0, H^q(D,F|_D)=0. Scalar sheaf cohomology on such D is computed by its smooth Dolbeault resolution. One can choose W subordinate to a partial finite-rank free resolution of F of length exceeding 2N. The assertion is local in the supplied resolution neighborhood; no untwisted projective Cartan A/B or global Stein generation theorem is asserted.

Proof obligation: Write scalar Dolbeault sheaf exactness from the local lemma and smooth-module acyclicity, then global scalar polydisc vanishing. Establish the ordinary sheaf cohomological-dimension bound H^j(D,G)=0 for j>2N for arbitrary abelian sheaves on a polydisc, by explicit finite-order refinements/paracompact Čech comparison, not by misusing a compact-support bound. Choose a partial free resolution longer than 2N on W; dimension shift along its coherent kernels on every D⊂W. No global Cartan A/B.

Existing/new dependencies: `def-axiom-of-choice`, `thm-qftx-gaga-analytic-oka-coherence`, `thm-dolbeault-lemma-polydisc`, `thm-dolbeault-cohomology-polydisc-vanishes-positive-q`, `thm-smooth-function-module-sheaves-are-acyclic`, `thm-acyclic-resolution-theorem-for-right-derived-functors`, `thm-long-exact-sequence-sheaf-cohomology`, `thm-exactness-of-sheaves-stalkwise`.

Source: https://www-fourier.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/~demailly/manuscripts/agbook.pdf — IX §4.B, (4.3)–Lemma4.4, printed/PDF p.421; proof read. Adapt to polydiscs using actual scalar suppliers and a separately proved 2N ordinary cohomological-dimension bound.

Examples:

* `ex-qftx-gaga-analytic-point-sheaf-acyclicity`: On a sufficiently small polydisc, the coherent quotient O/(z_1,…,z_N) has H^0=C and zero higher cohomology. Verification obligation: Apply the theorem and compute global quotient explicitly.

* `ex-qftx-gaga-analytic-hyperplane-quotient`: For a polydisc D through the origin, O_D/(z_1) has zero positive cohomology and global sections equal holomorphic functions on D∩{z_1=0}. Verification obligation: Use exact multiplication sequence and scalar acyclicity; distinguish coherent quotient from a vector bundle.

## 967.18 — Finite-dimensional coherent cohomology on compact projective space

Proposed page: `qftx-gaga-compact-coherent-finiteness`; theorem: `thm-qftx-gaga-analytic-compact-projective-coherent-finiteness`.

Statement: Assume AC. For 0≤r≤3 and any coherent analytic O_{P^r(C)}-module F, every H^q(P^r(C),F), q≥0, is finite-dimensional. With the locally defined coherent-section Fréchet topology and the induced Čech quotient topology these cohomology groups are Hausdorff. The proof includes the required Fréchet open-mapping and Schwartz compact-complex finiteness results, local coherent-section topology, and compactness of restriction; none is presumed to follow from a Banach-only or one-variable theorem.

Proof obligation: Develop metrizable complete locally convex section spaces and prove the Fréchet open-mapping theorem by Baire and successive approximation; Banach open mapping is only an analogy. Prove Schwartz compact-perturbation and its compact morphism of Fréchet complexes finite-dimensional/Hausdorff cohomology corollary. Define coherent section topology as a closed-relation quotient and show chart/generator independence using Artin–Rees/Krull. Prove several-variable Montel from polydisc Cauchy estimates, Ascoli and diagonal compact exhaustion (the imported named Montel theorem is plane-only). Prove compact coherent restriction maps. Construct finite nested Leray covers of P^r subordinate to distinguished resolution neighborhoods and justify all finite-intersection acyclicity. If intersections are not coordinate polydiscs, explicitly prove their pseudoconvex chart geometry and scalar Dolbeault vanishing, then coherent dimension shifting; do not apply pair3 to arbitrary intersections. Apply the proved compact-complex theorem to the resulting restriction of Čech complexes. This contains substantial internal prerequisites and remains unproved.

Existing/new dependencies: `def-axiom-of-choice`, `thm-qftx-gaga-analytic-oka-coherence`, `thm-qftx-gaga-analytic-local-coherent-polydisc-acyclicity`, `thm-artin-rees-lemma`, `thm-krull-intersection-theorem`, `thm-cauchy-estimates-on-a-polydisc`, `thm-locally-uniform-limit-of-holomorphic-functions-in-several-variables`, `cor-ascoli-arzela-for-compact-metric-domains`, `thm-leray-acyclic-cover-theorem`, `thm-cech-to-sheaf-cohomology-comparison`, `cor-dolbeault-vanishing-pseudoconvex-domain`.

Source: https://www-fourier.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/~demailly/manuscripts/agbook.pdf — IX1.8–1.9 pp.406–407 and IX4.3–4.8 pp.421–424; complete displayed proofs read, including functional-analytic and local-resolution prerequisites. No whole-book reconstruction claimed.

## 967.19 — Projective Dolbeault comparison and scalar structure-sheaf cohomology

Proposed page: `qftx-gaga-projective-scalar-dolbeault`; theorem: `thm-qftx-gaga-analytic-projective-dolbeault-scalar-cohomology`.

Statement: Assume AC. For 0≤r≤3, any holomorphic vector bundle E on P^r(C) has its analytic sheaf cohomology naturally identified with the cohomology of the global smooth E-valued Dolbeault complex. In particular H^0(P^r,O)=C and H^q(P^r,O)=0 for q>0. The scalar vanishing is proved by a direct Laurent/Čech calculation on the standard affine cover, including its acyclic intersections and convergent cochain contractions. This statement does not assume GAGA, higher-dimensional Kähler Hodge theory, or arbitrary-coherent projective vanishing.

Proof obligation: Generalize the written curve Dolbeault resolution pattern directly in all dimensions ≤3 using local polydisc exactness and smooth-module acyclicity. In the standard projective cover identify finite intersections with products of C and C*, prove scalar acyclicity using explicit pseudoconvex exhaustions and scalar Dolbeault solvability (or direct Laurent solution). Expand holomorphic functions on polyannuli, prove parameter holomorphy, normal convergence and uniqueness by iterated Cauchy integrals. Decompose homogeneous degree-zero Laurent modes and give a sign-correct Čech contracting homotopy for positive degree, verifying subseries extend to the claimed charts and converge locally uniformly. Global degree-zero functions are constants. This source-guided Laurent reconstruction is not supplied by the algebraic O(m) theorem and not proved by Serre’s footnote.
