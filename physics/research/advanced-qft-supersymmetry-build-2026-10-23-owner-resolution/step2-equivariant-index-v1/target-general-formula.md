# Target general formula and downstream specialization

This is the precise construction target, not a completed theorem carrier or acceptance record. The old simple-point theorem remains unchanged in scope until its general prerequisites are actually proved.

Let T=(R/Z)^r act smoothly and isometrically on a compact boundaryless smooth manifold M. Let (E^bullet,d) be the existing first-order equivariant elliptic complex, with explicit smooth bundle linearizations and invariant metrics. Write D=d+d*, and let s be the compact-support class of its even-to-odd positive-frequency symbol. Assume the stated Hodge/cohomology identification when using the old spectral presentation. The new analytic prerequisites use full AC; preserve the existing countable-choice simple-point conclusion separately rather than silently replacing its foundation assumptions.

For g in T let F range over the connected components of M^g; write nu_F for the real normal bundle and i_F:T*F -> T*M for cotangent inclusion using the invariant metric. The intended formula is

    chi_Ind(g) = sum_F integral_(T*F)
      ch_(g,c)(i_F^*s) Td(TF tensor C)
      / pi_F^* ch_g(lambda_-1(nu_F,C^*)).

Here the cotangent orientation is dtheta=sum dxi_j wedge dx_j. The integral is the compact-support cohomological pairing, as constructed in the RR supplier. The symbol character is a compact-support relative Chern character, not the ordinary character of the virtual bundle restricted to the zero section. The absolute normal denominator is the trace-weighted Chern character of the full exterior-power virtual normal bundle. Its degree-zero term is the nonzero real determinant det_R(1-dg|nu_F), so its inverse exists by a finite nilpotent series in positive degrees. The trace-weighted degree-zero operation is tr(g), distinct from Borel ch_T's degree-zero rank.

This formula may be evaluated at every g using its actual fixed set. At an element where the fixed set enlarges, recompute F and nu_F for that element: retaining a smaller generic fixed set would give vanishing normal denominators. No transversally elliptic distribution formula is asserted.

## Exact proof chain

1. The new finite-chart calculus and controlled-realization theorems construct an additive representation-valued analytic index on compact symbols, invariant under symbol homotopy/stabilization and compatible with restriction of the acting group. The arbitrary continuous topological triples must be identified with smooth homogeneous operator-symbol representatives by an actual smoothing/radial argument.
2. The associated-bundle product and compact O(n)-Bott normalization give analytic Gysin compatibility for a real equivariant embedding. Use its normal sphere compactification as the compact associated bundle. Controlled-kernel excision identifies its normal neighborhood with the neighborhood in the target manifold. This proves Ind(i_!b)=Ind(b) without assuming a full equivariant representation Bott isomorphism.
3. Apply the proved K fixed-inclusion reconstruction s=sum i_F! b_F, where b_F=(pi_F^*lambda_-1(nu_F,C^*))^-1 i_F^*s, in representation localization at functions nonzero at g. Additivity and Gysin compatibility reduce the character to the index on F of b_F.
4. The closure of the cyclic group generated by g fixes F pointwise. Its finitely many fiber-weight subbundles split the symbol. The index character at g is therefore the weighted sum of ordinary analytic indices of those subbundles. The ordinary embedding argument, ordinary Bott theorem and the same analytic Gysin compatibility identify those ordinary indices with the integer pushforward in the RR supplier.
5. The RR supplier now yields the displayed characteristic-class expression. The original complex's Hodge operator identifies its analytic index representation with the alternating cohomology representation. No source citation replaces steps 1 or 2.

## Existing branch retained

When each fixed component is a point, its cotangent space is zero, the Todd factor is 1, and the relative symbol restricts to the virtual fiber. The general formula reduces to the existing isolated-point fiber-supertrace divided by the real determinant. Independently, the original rescaled heat-kernel lemma already proves that special case under its existing countable-choice/spectral hypotheses. Keep that proof and its consumers valid while adding the general branch.

The zero-manifold and rotating two-sphere examples calculate their characters directly. They need no new logical dependency on the general theorem. The sphere's real-to-complex cohomology and inverse-pullback bundle action were repaired explicitly.
