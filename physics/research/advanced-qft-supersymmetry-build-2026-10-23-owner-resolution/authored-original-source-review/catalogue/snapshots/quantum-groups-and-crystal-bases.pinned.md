Quantum groups deform enveloping algebras while retaining structures that make highest weights, tensor products and combinatorial bases accessible. This collection starts with quantized enveloping algebras and quantum Serre relations. Divided powers and integral forms prepare quantum highest-weight modules; Kashiwara operators and crystal lattices then describe their combinatorial structure. Tensor-product crystals and highest-weight uniqueness organize how these structures combine. Balanced triples construct global crystal bases with explicit lattice and normalization conventions.

The affine type-A branch develops the level-one Fock-space action and its partition crystal. The residue-crystal page connects these conventions with the symmetric-group applications. Symmetric Groups and Symmetric Functions contains modular branching and its good-node rule; Hecke Algebras and Categorification contains KLR realizations and decomposition-number consequences. Classical root data and Kac–Moody theory remain in Lie Theory, while foundational Hopf-algebra constructions remain in Hopf Algebras & Hecke Algebras. Each topic retains its examples companion and its existing publication status, with prerequisites available through the page links.
