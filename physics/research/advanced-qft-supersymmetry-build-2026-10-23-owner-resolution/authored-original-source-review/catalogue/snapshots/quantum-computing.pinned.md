This proposed category develops quantum computing as finite-dimensional
mathematics. It begins with complex inner-product spaces, ordered registers,
tensor products and finite outcome laws, then defines pure and mixed states,
measurements, instruments and completely positive trace-preserving maps. It
builds qubit and gate conventions before giving circuits a precise syntax and
denotational semantics, with their wire order, classical control, inverse
access and ancilla costs made explicit. Reversible computation, exact and
approximate gate synthesis, uniform circuit families, promise problems and
bounded-error models then establish the interfaces used by quantum algorithms.
Separate branches develop Pauli and stabilizer methods, teleportation, classical
and quantum error correction, fault-tolerant computation, measurement-based
computation, circuit resource limits and state-preparation costs. The scope
retains every one of the twenty-four commissioned QC topics while keeping
unrelated branches separate in the dependency graph. Existing root linear
algebra, finite probability, algebra, Boolean-circuit and computation items
are reused by exact ID. Physics postulates, experiments and observations are
never logical suppliers: physical motivations may appear only in explicit
nonlogical remarks, and no physical-realization claim is needed. This file is
a draft overview for a prose-planning proposal; it registers no page or item
carrier and makes no proof-acceptance claim.
