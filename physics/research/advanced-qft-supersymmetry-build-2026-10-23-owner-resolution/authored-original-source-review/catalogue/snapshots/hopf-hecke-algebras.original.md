Published Hopf pages develop coalgebras and comodules, matrix coefficients and duality, bialgebras, convolution, and antipode identities, followed by Hopf ideals, finite duals, Hopf modules, integrals, Frobenius duality, Drinfeld doubles, Yetter-Drinfeld modules, and braided structures. They also cover quasi- and weak Hopf algebras, relative tensor products, and tensor coherence. The Hecke sequence builds generic Coxeter Hecke algebras and standard bases, then treats traces, parabolic induction, deformation and semisimplicity, affine and double-affine algebras, cyclotomic quotients, Murphy bases, and cellular modules. Hecke symmetries give a quantum Schur-Weyl interface between the strands.

Abstract Algebra and Category Theory supply basic algebraic and monoidal constructions; Coxeter Groups and Representation Theory support Hecke and module theory. Hecke Algebras and Categorification uses Hecke structures for categorification, while Quantum Groups and Crystal Bases draws on Hopf constructions for quantum-group theory. Category O and Geometric Representation Theory uses the affine and cyclotomic Hecke foundations, and Hall Algebras and Tensor Categories draws on the bialgebra and tensor structures. Specialized representation applications retain their homes in those subject areas.
