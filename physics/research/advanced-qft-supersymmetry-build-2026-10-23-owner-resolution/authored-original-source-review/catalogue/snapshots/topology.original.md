The topology pages cover both metric and general spaces. In metric spaces they treat convergence, completeness and completion, uniform continuity, compactness and discrete or countable bases. General topology develops open-set continuity, subspaces, products and quotients; connectedness, compactness and separation axioms; nets and filters; paracompactness, partitions of unity and metrization. Uniform spaces and compactifications extend these ideas, while function-space topology, Ascoli–Arzelà and Stone–Weierstrass study spaces of maps. The homotopy strand introduces the fundamental group, van Kampen, covering-space classification, applications and compact connected surfaces. The pages connect point-set methods with algebraic and geometric topics, while retaining tools beyond sequential convergence.

Real Analysis supplies convergence and compactness tools, while Set Theory supports choice and cardinal arguments; Combinatorics and Differential Geometry contribute specialized inputs. The resulting compactness, separation and covering-space methods support Algebraic Topology, Complex Analysis, Differential Geometry and Differential Topology. They also underpin Scheme Theory, Functional Analysis, Partial Differential Equations, Measure Theory and Probability as these subjects study spaces and maps.
