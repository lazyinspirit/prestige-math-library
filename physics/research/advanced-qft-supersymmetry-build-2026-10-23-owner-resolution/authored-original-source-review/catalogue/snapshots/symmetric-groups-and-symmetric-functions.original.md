Symmetric groups connect finite-group representations with partitions, tableaux and
symmetric functions. The collection develops Young diagrams, permutation and Specht
modules, irreducible characters, branching, the Young graph and the hook-length formula.
The Frobenius characteristic links characters with Schur functions; outer products and
skew modules lead to Littlewood–Richardson coefficients, while rim hooks yield the
Murnaghan–Nakayama rule. Further topics include Jucys–Murphy elements, seminormal forms,
integral Specht lattices, modular blocks and good-node branching. Internal tensor
products bring in Kronecker coefficients, FI-modules organize representation stability,
and Plancherel measure connects to random Young diagrams.

These developments draw on character theory, induction and tensor products from Abstract
Algebra, with selected foundations from Representation Theory of Groups, Commutative
Algebra, Homological Algebra and Category Theory. Their results support work in Lie
Theory, Hecke Algebras and Categorification, Quantum Groups and Crystal Bases,
Representation Theory of Groups, and Hall Algebras and Tensor Categories. The
connections run through branching, tensor products, modular structure and symmetric
functions.
