Coxeter groups are defined by generators and relations that encode reflection geometry.
The collection develops Coxeter presentations, exchange and reduced words, canonical
roots, reflection representations, chambers, Tits cones and parabolic subgroups. Finite
and affine diagrams lead to classification, root lattices, alcoves and translations.
Combinatorial structures include weak and Bruhat orders, descent and growth polynomials,
heaps, noncrossing partitions and sortable elements. Geometric topics treat reflection
arrangements, spherical complexes, polyhedral gluings, intrinsic metrics, local CAT
criteria and the Davis complex. Symmetric-group topics give concrete examples through
cycles, sign, conjugacy, simplicity and permutation statistics.

These developments draw on Group Theory, Linear Algebra and Lie Theory, with geometric
topics using Algebraic Topology, Topology, Real Analysis and Functional Analysis.
Coxeter methods support work in Category O and Geometric Representation Theory, Hecke
Algebras and Categorification, Hopf Algebras & Hecke Algebras, and Quantum Groups and
Crystal Bases. Shared structures include root systems, Bruhat order, reflection geometry
and Hecke algebras.
