## Disposition

The complete inspected `items/thm-qftx-projective-gaga-for-monad-data.md` has an unconditional public statement but explicitly conditional proof. Its A1 includes a false phrase, “coherent analytic sheaves on projective space and its hyperplanes satisfy Cartan Theorems A and B.” Untwisted Cartan A/B belong on Stein spaces; projective generation and vanishing require sufficiently positive twists. Even after this wording repair, A1 remains a substantial authoring obligation. Existing scalar Dolbeault solvability and Noetherian germ rings do not supply Oka coherence, coherent Cartan A/B, compact coherent cohomology finiteness, or projective uniform generation.

For projective dimension r≥1, O(-1) has no nonzero holomorphic global sections, while its stalks are rank-one nonzero modules, disproving untwisted A. Also H^r(P^r,O(-r-1))≅C, disproving untwisted B. These are independent analytic facts: Demailly VII §10, Theorems 10.6–10.7, printed/PDF pp.348–349, actually read below. The latter also follows from analytic Serre duality and K=P^r's O(-r-1). On a zero-dimensional hyperplane the obstruction disappears; it remains for P²/P³ themselves and their positive-dimensional hyperplanes. Do not invoke GAGA to prove this counterexample while auditing GAGA.

A sound unresolved boundary separates: (i) local faithful flatness and coherence; (ii) scalar analytic projective O-cohomology plus sheaf/Dolbeault comparison; (iii) coherent acyclicity on Stein coordinate domains where actually used; (iv) compact coherent finiteness; (v) uniform positive-twist projective generation. Serre's proof needs (iv) explicitly, not just (iii). His Lemma7 supplies positive-twist vanishing on the lower-dimensional hyperplane by dimension induction through already established lower-dimensional GAGA and algebraic Serre vanishing. Thus full independent analytic Serre vanishing in all dimensions is not needed before the induction; assuming GAGA in the same dimension would be circular.

## Local flatness can be bridged without a new deep analytic theorem

For a complex point on P^r, take affine coordinates t centered at the point. Set A=C[t_1,…,t_r]_(t), B=C{t_1,…,t_r}, T=C[[t_1,…,t_r]]. The local analytic chart identifies its stalk with B. Holomorphic power-series expansion identifies the maximal ideal as (t). One must write that every convergent series whose terms of degree<k vanish belongs to (t)^k: group its terms among the finitely many degree-k monomials, obtaining convergent remaining series on a smaller polydisc. Hence B/(t)^k is the truncated polynomial ring. The same is true for A/(t)^k; denominators with nonzero constant term invert by a finite geometric series modulo (t)^k. The induced compatible jet maps identify both completions with T.

A is Noetherian by the existing polynomial/localization algebra; B is Noetherian by the inspected germ theorem. Thus A→T is flat and B→T faithfully flat by the actual completion suppliers. For any injection U→V of A-modules, let K be the kernel of U⊗_A B→V⊗_A B. Flatness of T over B identifies K⊗_B T with the kernel of U⊗_A T→V⊗_A T, which is zero by A-flatness of T. Faithful flatness over B forces K=0. Therefore B is A-flat. The local map has B/m_A B=C≠0, so it is faithfully flat. One can instead prove faithfulness by testing every proper ideal I⊂A, since IB⊂m_B.

Serre's “flat pair” is stronger wording than just flat module: his Annex Definition4 means B/A is A-flat. Annex Proposition22 identifies this with B A-flat and every unit map M→M⊗_A B injective. Faithful flatness gives those unit injections (or prove by tensoring them with B and multiplication). The long exact Tor sequence for 0→A→B→B/A→0 then gives B/A flat. State/prove this bridge; do not equate terminology without checking it.

For a finite-type nonreduced scheme embedded in affine space, quotient the polynomial and convergent local rings by the *same extended ideal*. Flatness descends by base change. This is the scheme-theoretic analytification definition, and avoids invoking Serre's extra analytic Nullstellensatz/radicality argument for reduced varieties. General relative charts have larger dimension than3; do not claim the above r≤3-only formulation settles all family charts. The common-completion/local-flatness bridge itself works in arbitrary finite dimension if stated so. It still gives no proper relative cohomology comparison.

## Minimal genuine new pair interfaces for owner planning

These are proposed declarations, not established local carriers. IDs are suggestions and must undergo owner scope/dependency checks. Every result is purely mathematical, carries AC wherever consumed, and needs its own complete local argument and examples companion. Six pairs form a transparent split; the local flatness pair is mainly a bridge among existing suppliers, whereas the other five contain genuine missing analytic content. One can omit the *global* Cartan A/B ambition if only local coherent acyclicity is needed, but cannot omit the local coherent proof required by compact finiteness.

1. **Analytic coherence and local coherent resolutions** (`thm-qftx-oka-coherence-and-local-presentations`). For a finite-dimensional complex manifold M, O_M is coherent: the relation sheaf of every finite holomorphic tuple is locally finitely generated. Kernels/cokernels of morphisms of coherent sheaves are coherent, and each coherent sheaf admits arbitrarily long finite-rank free resolutions after shrinking a chart; for any prescribed finite length the common neighborhood can be chosen. Proof: Demailly II3.19–3.20, pp.89–90, genuine Weierstrass induction, plus elementary coherent-module lemmas. Dependencies: inspected preparation/division/generic regular coordinates/germ Noetherianity, finite-module kernel facts and sheaf exactness. Do not assert a finite global line-bundle resolution of an arbitrary analytic coherent sheaf before generation/algebraization. Companion: an explicit holomorphic relation kernel and why pointwise Noetherianity alone is not the proof.

2. **Algebraic–analytic local-ring comparison** (`thm-qftx-local-analytification-faithfully-flat`). State A→B above for any finite affine coordinate dimension; shared completion T; faithful flatness and Serre flat-pair property; extension to quotients by finitely generated ideals. Dependencies: power-series expansion, units/germ Noetherianity, polynomial/localization Noetherianity, completion faithful flatness, flatness ideal criterion, Tor/reflection. Source: Serre §6 Prop3/Cor1, printed pp.9–11; Annex Prop22–23/27–29, printed pp.36–40. Proof route given above is substantially available from existing suppliers. Companion: C[t]_(t)→C{t}, equal completions without claiming equal rings.

3. **Coherent Cartan A/B on pseudoconvex domains** (`thm-qftx-coherent-cartan-on-pseudoconvex-domains`). Restrict to a Hartogs-pseudoconvex domain Ω⊂C^N with a smooth strictly psh exhaustion; F any coherent analytic O_Ω-module. B: H^q(Ω,F)=0 for q>0. A: germs of global sections generate every stalk. A is stalkwise generation by possibly infinitely many global sections; do not claim one finite global generating family on arbitrary noncompact Ω. Proof source for B: Demailly IX4.10/4.11 with q=1, pp.425–428, with the actual local-resolution, approximation, bump and inverse-limit machinery in pp.419–428. A follows from B for the coherent kernel of F→F_x/m_xF_x supported at x, surjectivity on global sections, and Nakayama. Required dependencies: pair1, sheaf LES/Leray, scalar/vector-bundle local solvability as appropriate, topological cohomological-dimension bound, coherent section topology, Runge approximation, and Mittag-Leffler machinery. Existing scalar Hörmander theorem does not prove the whole list. A minimal alternative restricted to coherent sheaves on sufficiently small distinguished coordinate patches may suffice for pair4 and is materially less than global Cartan A/B; its exact proof is Demailly IX4.3–4.4, p.421 (free-resolution dimension shifting past 2N). Companion: scalar O on a polydisc and a non-free coherent quotient, with the distinction explained.

4. **Compact coherent cohomology finiteness** (`thm-qftx-compact-coherent-analytic-finiteness`). It suffices to state: for 0≤r≤3 and every coherent analytic F on P^r(C), each H^q(P^r,F) is finite-dimensional. A general compact complex manifold version is standard but unnecessary additional scope. Source: Demailly IX4.8, p.424, with IX1.8–1.9 pp.406–407 and IX4.3–4.7 pp.421–424. Proof must construct the Fréchet section topology, show closedness of relation subspaces using Krull/Artin–Rees, prove compactness of restriction by Montel, choose finite nested coherent-acyclic covers, and apply the compact morphism theorem to Čech complexes. Dependencies: pair1 and local coherent acyclicity from pair3, sheaf comparison, Montel, Fréchet open-mapping/Schwartz compact-perturbation theorem. Banach Hilbert compact-resolvent assertions alone do not replace this argument for arbitrary singular coherent F. Companion: finite-dimensional skyscraper cohomology and a reminder that coherent finiteness is not vanishing. This pair is load-bearing in Serre Lemma8's stabilization.

5. **Projective Dolbeault comparison and scalar O-cohomology** (`thm-qftx-projective-dolbeault-structure-sheaf`). For 0≤r≤3, the smooth Dolbeault resolution computes analytic sheaf cohomology of holomorphic vector bundles (at least all O(m)); H^0(P^r,O)=C and H^q(P^r,O)=0 for q>0. First clause: local polydisc lemma, Cauchy–Riemann kernel and smooth-module acyclicity; this dimension generalization can be proved directly, without importing the curve-only theorem outside its scope. Second clause still needs a real calculation: compact Kähler Hodge plus no holomorphic q-forms on P^r, or direct Laurent/Čech calculation. Sources actually read: Serre §13 Lemma4 and footnote4, printed pp.20–21; Demailly VII10.6–10.7 pp.348–349. Demailly's computation invokes higher-dimensional Kähler/Hodge/duality/Nakano suppliers; those prerequisites were not fully read or proved in this investigation. The Laurent route avoids claiming curve Hodge extends. Once this pair and flatness hold, the existing algebraic hyperplane/five-lemma argument yields comparison for all O(m). Companion: O(-1) has no global sections and O(-r-1) has top cohomology C, explicitly defeating untwisted projective A/B.

6. **Uniform projective analytic generation** (`thm-qftx-uniform-positive-twist-generation-analytic`). For 0≤r≤3 and coherent analytic M on P^r, there exists a_0 such that M(a) is generated by finitely many global sections for every a≥a_0. Source: Serre §16 Lemma8, printed pp.24–27 (PDF25–28), proof read completely. Dependencies: pair1, pair4, pair5/twisting comparison, algebraic Serre generation/vanishing, sheaf LES/Nakayama and compactness. Crucial organization: induct on r together with the already existing GAGA proof. Lower-dimensional GAGA algebraizes coherent hyperplane sheaves B and C; this gives their high-twist vanishing (Lemma7) and generation. Neither C=ker(M(-1)→M) nor B=coker is silently discarded: hyperplane multiplication may have torsion. The two exact sequences imply eventual decrease and stabilization of finite dim H^1(M(a)); then global sections surject onto B(a), and Nakayama gives generation at the selected point. Multiply by coordinate sections to preserve generation for larger a, use openness of finite generation and compactness to choose uniform a and a finite global list. Do not assume global GAGA in dimension r as a dependency of this pair; that creates a cycle. A clean alternative is author this induction within the GAGA theorem using pair4 as the new supplier, rather than declare a separate pair6 requiring GAGA. Companion: a torsion sheaf supported on the hyperplane, verifying the nonzero kernel/Tor term and positive twist generation.

Thus a truly minimal acyclic append can be **five** pairs (1–5), with the full Serre generation induction proved inside current GAGA; six pairs are justified only if pair6 has an independently noncircular proof or a contract explicitly organized as a dimension-inductive intermediate inside the GAGA derivation. A new theorem entitled “Cartan A/B + local flatness + all projective generation” would hide multiple substantial obligations and is not a legitimate shortcut.
