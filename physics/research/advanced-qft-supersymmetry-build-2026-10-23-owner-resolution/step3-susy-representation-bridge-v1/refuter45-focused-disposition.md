## Actual finding and disposition

The original evidence says: “The Definition calls real translations tau-fixed skew-Hermitian bispinors, then identifies T_{alpha dot-beta}=sigma^mu_{alpha dot-beta}P_mu. For its massive rest fiber P^mu=(M,0,0,0), M>0, and eta=(-,+,+,+), P_0=-M, so T=-M I_2 is nonzero Hermitian, contradicting the stated skew-Hermitian condition.” Its native severity is fatal; that original severity is preserved as an observation, not accepted as the scientific decision.

**Reject the claimed fatal contradiction.** The source explicitly defines the four T_ab as a basis of the complex space V, not a real basis of the tau-fixed space. It separately defines real translations as tau-fixed elements. Conflating those two spaces is the invalid step. A complex basis vector need not itself lie in the fixed real form. In particular, T11*=T11 and tau(T11)=-T11, while tau(iT11)=iT11. No stated hypothesis says T11 is tau-fixed.

The same distinction applies to the rest value. With the explicitly stated -i conversion, the physical Hermitian rest bispinor H=-M I has real-translation counterpart iH=-iM I, which is skew-Hermitian; -i(iH)=H. Thus the nonzero Hermitian matrix H and the corresponding skew-Hermitian real translation iH are different objects, consistently related by the declared conversion. Requiring H itself to be skew-Hermitian would discard that conversion. The proposed explicit rho bridge makes precisely these types visible: rho(T_ab)=sigma.P while rho(i p_mu)=i P_mu. It does not force T_ab into the fixed real space.

**Retain the separate nonfatal bridge clarification already confirmed from original refuter34.** The existing final paragraph's reused T notation obscures abstract generators versus represented operators; it should explicitly name rho and the abstract star-fixed momentum coordinates as proposed. The fatal contradiction in refuter45 is not evidence for changing star, tau, the Pauli matrices, the mixed bracket sign, or the rest normalization. The refuter34 bridge omission and refuter45 alleged contradiction are distinct original observations and must not be silently merged or rewritten.
