## Statement

Assume AC. Let $T=(\mathbb R/\mathbb Z)^r$ act smoothly on a compact finite-dimensional Hausdorff second-countable manifold $M$ without boundary, let $F=M^T$, and use the product Milnor Borel construction with coefficients $k\in\{\mathbb Q,\mathbb R\}$. Let $\mathscr S_T(k)$ be the multiplicative set generated by the first Chern classes of all nontrivial smooth characters of $T$. Then
$$
\mathscr S_T(k)^{-1}H_T^*(M\setminus F;k)=0.
$$
Equivalently, every class in the Borel equivariant cohomology of the nonfixed locus is annihilated by some finite product of nontrivial character classes.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC, the compact smooth torus action, its fixed set $F$, and $k\in\{\mathbb Q,\mathbb R\}$.

[A1] Full AC supplies the choice hypotheses of the fixed-locus, slice, quotient, and Milnor constructions. [[def-axiom-of-choice]]

[F1] The fixed set has an invariant tubular disk neighborhood; each orbit has an equivariant tube $T\times_H D(V)$, and every stabilizer in that tube is contained in its proper orbit stabilizer $H$. [[lem-qftx-compact-torus-fixed-locus-normal-weights]] [[lem-qftx-compact-torus-equivariant-slice-neighborhoods]]

[F2] Every proper closed subgroup $H\subsetneq T$ is annihilated pointwise by some nontrivial smooth character $\chi$ of $T$. [[lem-qftx-proper-closed-subgroup-has-annihilating-character]]

[F3] $H_T^*(W;k)$ is the singular cohomology of $ET\times_TW$, is a module over $A_T(k)$, and $\mathscr S_T(k)$ contains every nontrivial character class. [[def-qftx-character-localization]]

[F4] The product Milnor bundle $ET\to BT$ is numerable, and $BT=(\mathbb{CP}^{\infty})^r$ has its ordinary countable CW topology. For closed $H\subseteq T$, $T/H$ is a compact smooth manifold; the local finite-nerve lemma gives it CW type. The quotient $ET/H\cong ET\times_T(T/H)\to BT$ is a numerable locally trivial bundle with compact fiber $T/H$. The compact-fiber bundle theorem therefore makes $ET/H$ paracompact Hausdorff, CGWH, and of CW type. Also $ET\to ET/H$ is a locally trivial principal $H$-bundle. [[thm-milnor-join-model-is-a-contractible-free-g-space]] [[lem-universal-complex-flag-bundle-is-bt-n]] [[thm-quotient-manifold-by-a-closed-lie-subgroup]] [[lem-qftx-borel-base-is-paracompact-of-cw-type]] [[lem-qftx-compact-smooth-manifold-has-finite-good-cover-nerve-model]] [[lem-compact-fibre-numerable-bundle-totals-are-paracompact-hausdorff-of-cw-type]] [[def-locally-trivial-fiber-bundle]] [[def-principal-g-bundle-and-associated-fiber-bundle]]

[F5] For a path-connected paracompact Hausdorff CGWH space of CW type, the first Chern class identifies numerable complex-line-bundle classes with $H^2(-;\mathbb Z)$, is natural under pullback, and sends the trivial line to zero; coefficient change preserves this conclusion. [[thm-first-chern-class-classifies-complex-line-bundles]] [[prop-first-chern-class-of-tensor-dual-and-conjugate-lines]]

[F8] Singular cohomology of a pair has its long exact sequence, and Mayer–Vietoris applies to open covers. The localization functor preserves short exact sequences by the linked module-localization lemma; splitting an exact sequence at each image and kernel therefore shows that it preserves exactness of the Mayer–Vietoris sequence. [[thm-long-exact-sequence-of-a-pair-in-singular-cohomology]] [[thm-mayer-vietoris-sequence-in-singular-cohomology]] [[lem-qftx-module-localization-preserves-exactness]]

[F6] A closed subspace of a compact space is compact. [[thm-closed-subspace-of-a-compact-space-is-compact]]

[F7] Homotopic maps induce the same singular cohomology pullback with field coefficients. An equivariant homotopy descends to a homotopy of the Borel quotient maps. [[lem-qftx-singular-cohomology-homotopy-invariance]]

## Proof

**Given:** The data in the statement.

1.1 If $F\ne\varnothing$, use [F1] to choose an equivariant tubular disk $D_\rho(\nu_F)$ and let $K=M\setminus D_{\rho/2}(\nu_F)$, where $D_{\rho/2}$ denotes the image of the smaller open disk. This is closed in compact $M$, hence compact by [F6]. Every $x\in K$ is nonfixed, so its stabilizer $H_x\subsetneq T$ is a proper closed subgroup. If $F=\varnothing$, set $K=M$; it is compact and every point is nonfixed. If $F=M$, then $K=\varnothing$. For each $x\in K$, [F1] gives an open invariant orbit tube $V_x$ with all stabilizers contained in $H_x$; compactness gives a finite subcover $V_1,\ldots,V_m$ of $K$. For $F\ne\varnothing$, the sphere bundle $Q=S_{\rho/2}(\nu_F)\subset K$ is compact: cover the compact base by finitely many relatively compact bundle charts; over each compact chart closure the sphere fiber is compact, and the finitely many resulting compact sets cover $Q$. [A1, F1, F6, given, construct]

1.2 Consider any invariant open set $W\subseteq V_j\cong T\times_{H_j}D(V_j^0)$. Its inverse image in the slice has the form $T\times_{H_j}W_0$ for an $H_j$-invariant open $W_0\subseteq D(V_j^0)$. The Borel identification is the homeomorphism $[e,[t,w]]\mapsto[e\cdot t,w]$ from $ET\times_T(T\times_{H_j}W_0)$ to $ET\times_{H_j}W_0$, with inverse $[e,w]\mapsto[e,[1,w]]$; the quotient relations show both maps are well-defined. Write $B_H=ET/H$. The map $B_H\to BT$ is the associated quotient bundle $ET\times_T(T/H)\to BT$, and [F4] gives its compact-fiber CW-type and paracompactness properties. Since $ET\to BT$ is numerable, this associated quotient bundle is numerable as well: its local product charts over each Milnor chart retain the same subordinate partition. By [F4], $ET\to B_H$ is locally a principal $H$-bundle: over a Milnor $T$-chart it has form $U\times T\to U\times(T/H)$, and local sections of the Lie quotient $T\to T/H$ give the principal $H$-charts. The projection $q:B_H\to BT$ pulls $L_\chi$ back to the associated line $ET\times_H\mathbb C_{\chi|_H}$; the explicit trivialization $[e,z]\mapsto([e]_H,z)$ is well-defined because $\chi|_H=1$. Thus this is a numerable trivial line bundle, using its global chart and the constant partition $1$. The space $B_H$ is path-connected as a continuous image of contractible $ET$, so [F5] applies and its first Chern class is zero. Under the Borel identification, the projection $ET\times_H W_0\to BT$ factors through $B_H$, and the character class on $ET\times_H W_0$ is the pullback of this zero class. It therefore acts as zero on $H_T^*(W;k)$. As $\chi$ is nontrivial, this class becomes invertible in $\mathscr S_T(k)$, and the localized module is zero. [F2, F3, F4, F5, given, construct]

2.1 Let $W_j\subseteq V_j$ be any finite family of invariant open subsets, with each $V_j$ an orbit tube as above. We prove $\mathscr S_T(k)^{-1}H_T^*(\bigcup_{j=1}^m W_j;k)=0$ by induction on $m$. It holds for $m=1$ by step 1.2. For $m>1$, put $A=\bigcup_{j<m}W_j$ and $B=W_m$. By induction the localized cohomology of $A$ is zero, and step 1.2 gives zero for $B$. The intersection $A\cap B\subseteq V_m$ is invariant and open, so step 1.2 also gives zero for it. The Borel quotients of invariant open sets are open, since the quotient map is open (the saturation of an open set is the union of its $T$-translates). Thus Mayer–Vietoris applies to the Borel open cover. To see that localization preserves its long exactness, split the sequence at each image/kernel into short exact module sequences and apply [F8] to each; exactness of the localized pieces then gives exactness at every Mayer–Vietoris term. The segment from the localized $H^q(A\cap B;k)$ through $H^{q+1}(A\cup B;k)$ to the localized $H^{q+1}(A;k)\oplus H^{q+1}(B;k)$ has zero end terms, forcing the middle term to vanish in every degree. [F8, step 1.2, induction, algebra]

3.1 Apply step 2.1 with $W_j=V_j$ and then with $W_j=V_j\cap D_\rho(\nu_F)$; this shows the open union $V=\bigcup_jV_j$ and $V\cap D_\rho(\nu_F)$ have zero localized equivariant cohomology. The punctured tube $D_\rho(\nu_F)\setminus F$ equivariantly deformation retracts onto $Q$ by radial interpolation $v\mapsto((1-t)\lVert v\rVert+t\rho/2)v/\lVert v\rVert$. The paths stay in the disk because both endpoint radii are strictly below $\rho$. The same tubes cover $Q\subset K$, so step 2.1 and [F7] give zero localized cohomology for the punctured tube. Now $M\setminus F=V\cup(D_\rho(\nu_F)\setminus F)$, and their intersection is covered by the open sets $V_j\cap(D_\rho(\nu_F)\setminus F)\subseteq V_j$; step 2.1 makes this intersection localized-acyclic as well. A final localized Mayer–Vietoris sequence gives $\mathscr S_T(k)^{-1}H_T^*(M\setminus F;k)=0$. If $F=\varnothing$, the finite tube union covers all $M$ and step 2.1 applies directly; if $F=M$, the complement is empty. [F1, F8, F7, step 2.1, given, algebra] ∎
