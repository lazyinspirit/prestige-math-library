## Mathematical scope and limitations

The countable canonical results require the displayed local summable moment envelope; a lower energy bound alone does not imply a finite partition sum. The measurable identities require a reference measure and a compact-parameter integrable envelope. The trace result assumes AC and a trace-class Gibbs operator; the local proof derives its self-adjointness and positivity from the real PVM integral. Interval identities are conditional on the same eigenvalue list and its summable envelope. Entropy, mutual-information, and Bose/Fermi claims concern the stated probability laws only.

The finite-range limit is restricted to finite alphabets and bounded translation-invariant finite-range interactions on cubic boxes with free boundary; alternate boundary energies are covered only under the stated surface-order bound. Concentration is conditional on differentiability of the limiting pressure. The transfer-matrix result is one-dimensional and periodic. No unrestricted ensemble-equivalence, long-range or continuum thermodynamic limit, physical certification, or empirical result is asserted.
