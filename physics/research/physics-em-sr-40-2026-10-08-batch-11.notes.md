## Dependency verification (actual uses)

All in-run suppliers were read for statement, hypotheses, conventions and
direction before use: `def-sr-affine-minkowski-model`,
`def-inertial-frame-and-inertial-chart`,
`def-spatial-euclidean-structure-of-an-inertial-frame`,
`def-sr-si-units-and-coordinate-conventions`, `def-lorentz-boost` (batch 2),
`thm-sr-proper-time-and-observer-decomposition` (batch 3),
`def-sr-observed-energy-momentum-and-kinetic-energy`,
`lem-sr-energy-momentum-observer-identities`,
`lem-sr-four-momentum-in-inertial-frame`, `post-sr-massive-particle-dynamics`,
`post-sr-ideal-massless-particle-momentum` (batch 8). Points checked in
particular:

- Clause (d) of `thm-sr-proper-time-and-observer-decomposition` supplies exactly
  $u=\gamma(n+w/c)$, $|w|^2=c^2(1-\gamma^{-2})$ and
  $\gamma=(1-|w|^2/c^2)^{-1/2}$; clause (e) identifies the frame stationary
  observer's relative velocity with the chart coordinate velocity $w(t)=r'(t)$.
  The expansion lemma's domain hypothesis $|w|^2\le z_0c^2$ is therefore the
  intended bounded-velocity domain, not a post-hoc restriction.
- Clause (b) of `lem-sr-energy-momentum-observer-identities` gives
  $E_n=mc^2\gamma$, $T_n=mc^2(\gamma-1)$, $q_n=m\gamma w$, $m(P)=m$ and
  $|q_n|=mc\sqrt{\gamma^2-1}$; these are used exactly, with no sign or factor
  change. The kinetic energy is defined only after fixing $m>0$, which every
  consumer assumes.
- `post-sr-massive-particle-dynamics` clause (i) fixes $P=mU$ with $m>0$
  constant and clause (ii) identifies the measured energy, momentum and kinetic
  energy with $E_n,q_n,T_n$; the correspondence theorem is conditional on that
  identification and inherits its idealizations, unit conventions and exclusion
  of the massless case.
- `cor-taylor-remainder-bound` requires derivatives through order $n+1=2$ on
  the closed interval with endpoint continuity and a uniform derivative bound;
  $f(z)=(1-z)^{-1/2}$ is $C^\infty$ on a neighbourhood of $[0,z_0]$ for
  $z_0<1$, and $|f''|\le\tfrac34(1-z_0)^{-5/2}$ there by
  `lem-rational-power-monotone` and `lem-of-inverse-positive`.
- `thm-cauchy-schwarz-finite` is applied through the $\delta$-orthonormal basis
  of `def-spatial-euclidean-structure-of-an-inertial-frame` (component lists of
  $v$ and $x$), which is the basis the supplier provides.
- No missing, circular, forward or inadequate dependency was found; no
  `justified_by` or `forward_refs` edge is used; no choice principle is invoked
  by any item (all suppliers used are choice-free or their use is finite).
