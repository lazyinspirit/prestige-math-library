## Uneditable finding

Subject: **`def-alternating-k-linear-map`**, published imported mathematics.

Exact location: `items/def-alternating-k-linear-map.md`, **Remarks**, characteristic-two sentence (line 41): it says the swapped-argument sign rule collapses to a tautology x=−x.

This is a false claim: in characteristic two the rule f(v,w)=−f(w,v) becomes symmetry f(v,w)=f(w,v), which is a substantive condition. On F_2² the bilinear map f(v,w)=v_1 w_2 has f(e_1,e_2)=1 and f(e_2,e_1)=0, so it fails that rule. The cited published witness was opened in full: its different map x_0 y_0 is symmetric/antisymmetric in characteristic two but not alternating, and does not justify calling symmetry automatic. Correction needed: state that the sign rule becomes symmetry and still need not imply alternation.

The assigned consumer **`def-oriented-minkowski-volume-form`** directly depends on this supplier. Its real-field alternating-map definition and determinant argument are unaffected by the erroneous characteristic-two remark. Severity is **fatal** for the false supplier claim under the reader rule; this does not assert that the real-field volume-form construction is false.
