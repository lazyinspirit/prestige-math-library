## Remark

In the free-field Schwarzschild setting, the Boulware state is the stationary exterior ground state with respect to Schwarzschild time translations and is singular at the horizons. The Unruh or collapse state has incoming ground-state modes at past null infinity and outgoing Hawking occupation at late future null infinity; it is regular at the future horizon but is not a two-sided equilibrium bath. The Hartle-Hawking state is the stationary thermal equilibrium state on the two-sided eternal Schwarzschild extension, with thermal incoming and outgoing modes and regularity across both horizon branches. These are different state and boundary conditions. Kerr here means the stationary, rotating, asymptotically flat vacuum black-hole geometry; the equilibrium Schwarzschild construction is not a collapse calculation and does not establish an equilibrium state in that Kerr setting. No such Kerr state is asserted here.
