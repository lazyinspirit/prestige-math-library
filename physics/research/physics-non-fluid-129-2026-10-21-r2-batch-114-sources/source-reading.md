## H. S. Reall, Part III Black Holes (2024)

Full author-hosted lecture notes: https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/hsr1000/black_holes_lectures_2024.pdf

## Aron C. Wall, A proof of the generalized second law for rapidly changing fields and arbitrary horizon slices (2012)

Full arXiv PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1105.3445

Read §1, equations (1)–(3), and the complete relevant argument in §§2.1–2.8. The outside von Neumann entropy is ultraviolet divergent at the horizon and must be renormalized. The proof depends on semiclassical Einstein gravity, a horizon algebra satisfying four stated axioms, relative-entropy monotonicity, and a renormalized relation between relative entropy and separate energy/entropy values; §2.8 explicitly treats that renormalized relation as an assumption in the proof. The result is therefore not transferred to arbitrary finite-regulator data or to an unspecified physical evolution.

Disposition: the finite sum and renormalization boundary support def-tgr-regulated-generalized-entropy; the required extra physical hypotheses support rem-tgr-generalized-entropy-no-automatic-second-law.
