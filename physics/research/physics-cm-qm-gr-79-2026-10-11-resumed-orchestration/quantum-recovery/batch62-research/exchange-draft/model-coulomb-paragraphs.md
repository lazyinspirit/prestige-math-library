**(c)(ii) Coulomb interaction.** In the spatial realization the model may declare $W(z):=\kappa/|z|$ with a real coupling $\kappa\in\mathbb R$ (repulsive for $\kappa>0$). Retain a real bounded measurable external term $V_{\mathrm{ext}}^b$ as in clause (b)(i), and permit the additional singular term $-g/|x|$, where $g\in\mathbb R$; the total external potential is $V_{\mathrm{ext}}^b(x)-g/|x|$. Thus the declared scalar potential on $\mathbb R^{3N}$ is
$$V(X)=V_b(X)+V_C(X),\qquad V_b(X)=\sum_{i=1}^NV_{\mathrm{ext}}^b(x_i),\qquad V_C(X)=-g\sum_{i=1}^N\frac1{|x_i|}+\kappa\sum_{1\le i<j\le N}\frac1{|x_i-x_j|}.$$
The cases $g=0$, $\kappa=0$, $V_{\mathrm{ext}}^b=0$ and either sign of either coupling are allowed. Singular values on their null collision sets are irrelevant to the multiplier classes. The singular part is supplied by [[thm-nrqm-fock-and-equilibrium-fixed-n-coulomb-self-adjointness]]; the bounded external addition is included by the exact-domain argument in clause (d)(ii), rather than attributed to a source statement which contains only the singular sum.

**(d)(ii) Spatial Coulomb case.** Put $r=2s+1$, $S_N=(\mathbb C^r)^{\otimes N}$ and $M=r^N$. Use the full spin-product unitary $\Theta_N:(L^2(\mathbb R^3;\mathbb C^r))^{\otimes N}\to L^2(\mathbb R^{3N};\mathbb C^M)$ of [[lem-nrqm-many-body-finite-spin-product-identification]] (a),(b), including its combined spatial/internal permutation action. To apply the literal Fourier normalization of [[thm-nrqm-fock-and-equilibrium-fixed-n-coulomb-self-adjointness]], choose in that mathematical theorem the masses $\widetilde m_i=m/(4\pi^2)$, its parameter $\hbar$ unchanged, one fixed center at zero, coefficients $z_{i1}=-g$ and $z_{ij}=\kappa$. The mathematical masses here are substitution parameters used to realize the already declared kinetic operator; the physical species mass remains $m$. The source kinetic symbol becomes $4\pi^2\hbar^2|\xi|^2/(2m)$, so its scalar operator is exactly
$$T_C=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\Delta_X+V_C(X),\qquad D(T_C)=H^2(\mathbb R^{3N};\mathbb C).$$
By the source's clauses (b),(c), $T_C$ and its finite-component extension are self-adjoint on these exact domains and bounded below for all stated real $g,\kappa$. The real bounded multiplier $M_{V_b}$ has norm at most $N\|V_{\mathrm{ext}}^b\|_\infty$, hence relative bound zero with respect to $T_C$ and to its finite-component extension. The canonical [[thm-kato-rellich]] makes
$$H_{\mathrm{lab}}=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\Delta_X+V_C+V_b,\qquad D(H_{\mathrm{lab}})=H^2(\mathbb R^{3N};\mathbb C^M),$$
self-adjoint on exactly this domain and bounded below; if the singular operator has lower bound $c_C$, the sum has lower bound $c_C-N\|V_{\mathrm{ext}}^b\|_\infty$. Equal masses, identical external slots and pair-independent signed coefficient $\kappa$ make the scalar operator invariant under spatial permutations. These permutations preserve $H^2$ by permuting the weak-derivative coordinates; the constant internal tensor permutations preserve the finite-component domain too. Consequently the combined projections $\mathcal P_\sigma=\Theta_NP_\sigma^{(N)}\Theta_N^{-1}$ reduce $H_{\mathrm{lab}}$, and [[lem-nrqm-many-body-finite-spin-product-identification]] (c) gives self-adjointness of its restriction on the exact domain $H^2\cap\operatorname{ran}\mathcal P_\sigma$. The declared sector energy is this restriction transported back by $\Theta_N^{-1}$ to $\mathfrak H_N^\sigma$, with the unique zero-space operator when that sector is zero. The AC assumption remains confined to this Coulomb realization as in clause (a).
