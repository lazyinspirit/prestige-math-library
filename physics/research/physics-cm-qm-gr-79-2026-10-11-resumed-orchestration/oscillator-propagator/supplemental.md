## Momentum convention and the quadratic sum on $Y$

The Fourier convention is the negative-sign $2\pi$ convention. For Schwartz $u$, `lem-weak-derivatives-are-polynomial-fourier-multipliers` gives $\mathcal F_2u'=2\pi i\xi\mathcal F_2u$. Thus the actual Fourier-defined $P$ satisfies $Pu=-i\hbar u'$ and $P^2u=-\hbar^2u''$ there. $Q^2=M_{x^2}$ and $P^2=\mathcal F_2^*M_{(2\pi\hbar\xi)^2}\mathcal F_2$ have their maximal multiplier domains and are closed: the same concrete real-multiplier adjoint argument as corrected common-domain Step 1.1 applies to these two symbols. Their displayed domains agree with the operator-square domains, since $|r|\le1+r^2$ ensures membership in the first-power domain whenever the squared multiplier is in $L^2$.

For a finite Hermite sum $u=\sum c_n\psi_n$, the shifted ladder actions give $\|a^2u\|^2=\sum n(n-1)|c_n|^2\le\|Nu\|^2$ and $\|(a^\dagger)^2u\|^2=\sum(n+1)(n+2)|c_n|^2\le2\|(N+I)u\|^2$. Also $a^\dagger a=N$ and $aa^\dagger=N+I$. Expanding $(a\pm a^\dagger)^2$ and taking the triangle inequality therefore gives $\|(a\pm a^\dagger)^2u\|\le(3+\sqrt2)\|(N+I)u\|\le6\|(N+I)u\|$. Consequently $\|Q^2u\|\le3\ell^2\|(N+I)u\|$ and $\|P^2u\|\le3\hbar^2\ell^{-2}\|(N+I)u\|$. The diagonal inequality $n+1\le2(n+1/2)$ bounds these by constants times $\|Au\|$.

For every $u\in Y$, finite coefficient truncations $u_M$ converge to $u$ in $Y$. The preceding bounds make $Q^2u_M$ and $P^2u_M$ Cauchy. Closedness places $u$ in both square domains and identifies their limits. On finite sums, the Hermite differential identity gives $Au_M=P^2u_M/(2m)+m\omega^2Q^2u_M/2$; taking limits proves this identity on all of $Y$. Thus the quadratic notation in the propagator Statement is an actual operator identity on the specified domain, rather than a formal differential expression. ## Direct free group and coherent check under CC

Define $F(\tau)u=\sum_ne^{-i\tau E_n/\hbar}c_n\psi_n$. Parseval gives convergence, unit norm and inverse $F(-\tau)$, and coefficient multiplication gives the group law. For strong continuity, split the norm-square of the difference into a finite sum tending to zero and a tail bounded by $4\sum_{n>M}|c_n|^2$. For $u\in Y$ the same argument with weights $1+E_n^2$ proves graph continuity and invariance. The bound $|e^{-ix}-1|\le|x|$, obtained by integrating the derivative of $e^{-ix}$, bounds the difference-quotient error by $2E_n|c_n|/\hbar$. Finite sums and the square-summable weighted tail then give $\partial_\tau F(\tau)u=-iAF(\tau)u/\hbar$. Conversely a norm derivative at zero has coefficients $-iE_nc_n/\hbar$, so Parseval forces $u\in Y$. This proves precisely the draft's free-group fact without the enclosing AC-bearing item.

Define the coherent vector in the *scaled* basis by $\Psi_z=e^{-|z|^2/2}\sum_{n\ge0}z^n\psi_n/\sqrt{n!}$. Orthogonality and $\sum|z|^{2n}/n!=e^{|z|^2}$ prove convergence and normalization directly. For every fixed integer $k$, the ratio of successive positive terms $n^{2k}|z|^{2n}/n!$ tends to zero when $z\ne0$; the tail is bounded by a geometric series, so $\Psi_z\in D(N^k)$. The case $z=0$ is immediate. Coefficient multiplication gives $F(\tau)\Psi_z=e^{-i\omega\tau/2}\Psi_{ze^{-i\omega\tau}}$. ## Validation of the retained core extension and phase

$$AD(q,p)u=D(q,p)[A+(p/m)P+m\omega^2qQ+E(q,p)I]u,\qquad E(q,p)=p^2/(2m)+m\omega^2q^2/2.$$

$$AD-DA=[(p/m)P+m\omega^2qQ-E(q,p)I]D.$$

Both scalar energy terms are negative in this formula. Direct differentiation of the defining phase gives $i\hbar\partial_tD u=[-p'Q+q'P+(qp'-pq')I/2]Du$: the phase contributes $-p'x+p'q/2+pq'/2$, and replacing the translated derivative by $q'P D-pq'D$ leaves $(qp'-pq')/2$.
