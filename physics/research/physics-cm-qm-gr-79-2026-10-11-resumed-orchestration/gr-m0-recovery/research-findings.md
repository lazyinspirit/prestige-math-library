Confirmed targeted defects:

- Existence starts every projected datum in K although Fourier projection does not preserve K or even O. Its initial energy margin can be impossible at T=0 and it omits the embedding constant in pointwise time drift.
- Persistence assumes H^M, then concludes H^M instead of H^{M+1}; its order-M energy is not legitimized before establishing order-M derivatives. The smooth datum also needs the missing hypothesis norm <= R.
- Finite speed bounds sum xi_i A^i by b; individual operator bounds only give sqrt(3)b. A scalar counterexample is A^0=1, A^1=A^2=A^3=b, xi=-(1,1,1)/sqrt(3), b>1/(sqrt(3)-1): the asserted lambda=b+1 makes lambda+sum xi_i A^i negative.
- Stability proves E' <= C(1+E), then concludes E <= E(0)e^{Ct}. The difference estimate must remain homogeneous in w throughout.

Load-bearing supplier prerequisites, to repair before claiming those replacements complete:

1. `lem-gr-galerkin-ode-local-solution`: replace 'for every N' by every N for which P_Nu_0 takes values in O, or formulate the ODE with arbitrary admissible z_0 in X_N. Continuation only excludes endpoints T_* inside the coefficient time interval I. V need not contain arbitrary P_Nu_0. Finite-dimensional coordinate compactness is correctly obtained from closed compact-value and norm constraints (the norm bound can be retained explicitly); do not describe a function-valued coordinate image as a subset of O.
2. `lem-gr-smooth-composition-and-commutator-estimates`: localize all uniform coefficient constants to a compact time slab I_0 contained in I. Smooth coefficients need not be bounded on an arbitrary interval I. Supply homogeneous commutator estimates through order s, including the top-order version below, instead of an additive 1. Remove dependence on convex hull lying in O: extend each coefficient smoothly off a neighborhood of the actual compact value set using a cutoff. The existing estimates at lower order then apply along global line segments to the extension. Smooth-density arguments must justify chain/Leibniz rules for Sobolev fields.
3. `lem-gr-galerkin-time-derivative-bound`: in step 2.1 pair with D^alpha(partial_t u_N), not D^alpha u_N. Derivative of A^i partial_i u_N at j requires partial_i u_N in H^j (available for j <= s-1), not merely H^{j-1}. Its constants must be independent of N; the remark saying no uniformity in N is needed is wrong.
4. `lem-gr-galerkin-energy-inequality`: derive |e'| <= C(1+e), not only e' <= C(1+e), to use negative times. The top spatial derivative of A^i partial_i u_N is not bounded in L2 solely from H^s: the leading A^i partial_i D^alpha u_N term must be integrated by parts before estimates. The total derivative dot A^0 = partial_t A^0 + partial_u A^0[partial_t u_N] needs its L-infinity bound explicitly. Homogeneous order-s commutators below close it.
5. `lem-gr-galerkin-second-time-derivative-bound`: its constant C_1 cannot be independent of R with an asserted universal quadratic growth for arbitrary nonlinear coefficients. Retain C(A,K,R,s,I_0), and eliminate convexity using cutoff extension. Its projected inverse induction must use a homogeneous commutator: a bound C(1+norm v) does not imply norm M^{-1}w <= C norm w. Correct homogeneous estimates do imply this by the displayed induction.
6. `lem-gr-galerkin-limit-is-classical-solution`: K_1 compact in O does not imply conv(K_1) plus a ball lies in O (annular O is a counterexample). Use the corrected second-derivative estimate on any compact K_1, proved by cutoff extension. Its L1 says H^{s-1} embeds C^{s-2}, which is false; the needed convergence of u and first spatial derivatives follows from H^{s-1} embeds C^{s-3} (s>=5). Fourier Fatou must consistently carry the norm-equivalence constants, or use q_k^s directly to keep the exact derivative norm bound.
7. `lem-gr-periodic-sobolev-embedding`: the absolute-series Cauchy--Schwarz line uses the wrong exponents. The sound bound is sum |k^alpha||u_hat(k)| <= [sum |k^alpha|^2(1+|k|^2)^{-s}]^{1/2} N_s(u) <= [sum (1+|k|^2)^{-(s-m)}]^{1/2}N_s(u). The Holder endpoint argument is also invalid: increasing the exponent cannot be justified from a lower exponent. For integer s,m with gap 2, the endpoint theta=1/2 has a direct split-frequency proof: split at |k|=1/d, and sum |k|^{2m}(1+|k|^2)^{-s} min(4,|k|^2 d^2); dyadic shells give O(d^{2(s-m)-3})=O(d) for gap 2, while gap>=3 gives O(d^2). Preserve the endpoint by supplying this proof, not by silently weakening the claim.
8. `lem-gr-periodic-sobolev-algebra`: the proof must extend smooth Leibniz estimates by Fourier density to general Sobolev fields. Its claim k>=4 is sound; the remark suggesting the k=3 algebra estimate fails is false (the displayed proof itself works for k=3), and can simply be removed.
