# Focused GR02 endpoint gap (research only)

The new early construction handles supplied smooth curves on open intervals. Its direct consumer `def-gr-timelike-worldline-and-proper-time` promises the richer endpoint and piecewise-C1 classes, which remain intact in current production. The existing piecewise definition requires only C1 on open pieces and one-sided tangents at their ends. Definition(d) then asserts that those endpoint tangent values force the one-sided limits of the velocities' metric norms, and continuous extension of h to the closed pieces. That implication is false.

A flat local test is a timelike curve whose velocity on t>0 is (2,sin(1/t),0,0). Its time component is 2t and its spatial component is the integral from 0 to t of sin(1/r), with value 0 at t=0. The substitution u=1/r and integration by parts give the spatial increment O(t²), so the endpoint tangent is (2,0,0,0), future timelike. The interior velocity is also future timelike since its squared norm is -4+sin²(1/t)<0. Nevertheless h(t)=sqrt(4-sin²(1/t)) has no one-sided limit at 0: along reciprocal integer multiples of pi it equals 2, while along reciprocal pi/2-plus-integer-pi it equals sqrt(3). Thus the advertised continuous extension to h(0)=2 does not exist.

The current proof supplier `lem-gr-proper-time-reparametrization-and-four-velocity` and its consumers must retain honest endpoint conditions. A complete repair may require explicit C1 extension to each closed piece (standard piecewise-C1 interpretation), or a genuinely broader integral construction with separately proved endpoint consequences. Merely changing the integral interpretation cannot justify the existing endpoint four-velocity normalization claim. No production edit or new acceptance is recorded here. Root direction requested before changing the outside public curve class.
