## Statement

Every principal ideal domain is Noetherian.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A principal ideal domain $R$, regarded as its left regular module, and the Noetherian-ring convention of [[def-noetherian-ring]].

[F1] An integral domain $R$ is a principal ideal domain when every ideal $I\mathrel{\trianglelefteq}R$ is principal: $I=(a)$ for some $a\in R$ ([[def-principal-ideal-domain]]).

[L1] A module is Noetherian exactly when every submodule is finitely generated ([[thm-equivalent-characterizations-of-noetherian-modules]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 A submodule of the left regular module ${}_RR$ is an ideal. By [F1] it is generated by one element; this includes the zero ideal $(0)$ and unit ideal $(1)$. [F1]

2.1 Thus every submodule of ${}_RR$ is finitely generated, so [L1] makes the regular module Noetherian and hence makes $R$ a Noetherian ring. Commutativity makes the same statement valid on the right. [step 1.1, L1] ∎
