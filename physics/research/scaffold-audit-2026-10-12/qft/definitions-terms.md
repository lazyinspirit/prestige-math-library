This index records where the repaired authoritative prose defines the technical
objects it uses. A research interface remains an unaccepted proposal even when
its local definition or argument is complete. Source and proof boundaries are
in `source-reading.json` and `supplier-map.json`.

## Residual terminology holds

The formal symbols “all-order causal renormalization,” “full LSZ theorem,”
“Haag theorem,” “spin-statistics theorem,” “CPT theorem,” “general BRST
cohomology,” and “Wilsonian RG/EFT error bound” denote the exact framework
contracts described above; their full theorem-specific source hypotheses and
proofs are not suppliers in this build. No downstream item may treat those
names as proof evidence. The current scaffold contains definitions and exact
local model contracts for each, but general theorem application is a separate
extension obligation.
