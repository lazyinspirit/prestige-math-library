## Scope

This page adopts a classical charged complex scalar with a globally defined
real electromagnetic potential, so its field strength is exact. It states the
model's smoothness, SI units, arbitrary real coupling, scalar self-potential,
and compact variation conventions explicitly. Its local evolution result is a
conditional deduction from the adopted action models and the current draft
mathematical Cauchy theorem. That carrier exists and its explicit compatibility
formula is inspected, but its statement overloads $D_i$ for both the spatial
Levi-Civita and gauge-covariant derivatives; its proof status is unresolved,
and the current batch-11 manifest lists it at level 7 with pending precheck and
draft transitive proof suppliers. The consumer records this exact hold and
uses separate notations. No empirical, quantum, singular-source,
global-existence, or completeness claim is made.
