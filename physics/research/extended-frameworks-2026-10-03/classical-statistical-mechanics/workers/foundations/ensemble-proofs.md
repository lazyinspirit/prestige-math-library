## F0 — phase space, quotient, reference measure and states

For N≥1 particles in spatial dimension d≥1 choose a smooth configuration manifold Q_N, e.g. (T_L^d)^N, an open domain of (R^d)^N, or a bounded box interior. Its points q=(q_1,…,q_N) are positions (m). The phase space is Γ_N=T*Q_N: in a coordinate chart the covector p=Σp_i dq_i has momentum components kg m/s, canonical one-form λ=Σp_i dq_i (J s), and ω=-dλ=Σdq_i∧dp_i. A change of position coordinates transforms p covariantly, so λ is intrinsic; ω is smooth closed and nondegenerate by its coordinate contraction. The Liouville density is the positive density associated with ω^(dN)/(dN)! in the symplectic orientation, with dq dp coordinate density. Write n=dN. A prescribed positive action cell a_0 (J s) makes the reference measure m_N=dq dp/a_0^n dimensionless. No lattice of phase cells or uncertainty bound is a classical theorem. Choosing a_0=h is a declared classical normalization motivated by another framework; a_0 need not be set by Hamiltonian dynamics. Momentum remains part of every ensemble unless explicitly integrated out with its law retained.

For a single species with permutation-invariant Q_N,H_N and observables, define the physical indistinguishability convention by the orbit map π:Γ_N→Γ_N/S_N and quotient reference m̄_N=π_*(m_N/N!). The quotient carries the sigma-algebra of sets with measurable inverse image. This is well-defined countable additivity by inverse images; for invariant observable A=Ā∘π, ∫Ā dm̄_N=(1/N!)∫A dm_N first for indicators, then simple functions and monotone convergence. Thus 1/N! is exact for this chosen counting convention, including orbit fixed points. On the full-Liouville-measure free-orbit region it counts each orbit once: nonidentity permutations fix simultaneous position/momentum coincidences, lower-dimensional linear diagonals of Lebesgue measure zero in local charts. Do not assert a smooth global quotient at collisions. For several species divide by ∏N_s!, permitting permutations only within identical species. If physical tags or distinct species are resolved, use the labeled convention and appropriate separate particle numbers. This choice controls entropy offsets and particle-exchange chemical potentials; dynamics alone does not decide operational distinguishability.

A classical statistical state is a probability measure P on the phase measurable space. A density ρ means P(A)=∫_Aρ dm, ρ≥0, ∫ρdm=1, relative to a specified m; not every probability has such a density (a point mass is singular). An observable is a real measurable A with ∫|A|dP<∞ when its expectation is used. Its mean is ∫A dP; variance requires ∫A²dP<∞; joint covariance requires both second moments. A macrostate map M:Γ→Y is measurable; its coarse state is M_*P, and its macrocell volume is m(M^-1B). Gibbs distribution entropy and logarithmic macrocell volume entropy are distinct functionals.
