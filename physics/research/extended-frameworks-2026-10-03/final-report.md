# Extended physics scaffold handoff

Seven five-member teams completed the requested research scaffolds: **1,120
proposed items (826 A, 294 B), 149 A/B pairs**. Each team used four specialists
and one integrating scaffolder, gpt-6.1-sol at medium effort. The platform's
thread limit required reusing ten worker threads across 35 subject-role
assignments. All seven category handoffs are frozen and indexed in [README.md](README.md).

The categories are Fluid Dynamics; Relativistic Particle Mechanics;
Einstein–Maxwell Models; Quantum Field Theory including QED; Classical Statistical
Mechanics; Quantum Statistical Mechanics; and Thermodynamics and General Relativity.
The last category connects covariant material thermodynamics, stationary
Tolman–Klein equilibrium and transport, self-gravitating ensembles and TOV,
classical horizon mechanics, specified quantum thermal spectra and conditional
entropy/evaporation models. Its report records all physical adoption points.

Each dossier contains explicit definitions and units, framework-specific
primitive/derived quantities, physical postulates, prose and A/B pathways,
actual scoped arguments, inspected exact supplier contracts, claim/closure
ledgers, authoritative source provenance and reproducible structural checks.
Permitted unpublished mathematics plans retain their actual proof status.
Specialists and integrators supplied missing prerequisites for the claims they
asserted; final ledgers record no required residual for those exact scopes.
Named stronger and open problems remain prospective and are not theorem premises.
Formal, regulated, semiclassical, empirical and exact claims are distinguished.

Root reran every frozen canonical category checker successfully. The final
`check-cohort.py --require-all` also passes: all seven inventories present,
unique canonical IDs, declared premise resolution, cross-category acyclicity,
mathematical/physical premise boundaries and physics-only classes. Its output
is [cohort-check.json](cohort-check.json). Category checks additionally verify
argument anchors, page dependencies/homes, B leaves and 100-item side caps.
The reported exact symbolic and finite diagnostic checks retain their actual
worker/integrator execution receipts; they supplement the arguments.

Review limits are explicit in [root-review-record.json](root-review-record.json).
Root read selected bridge proofs and each final report; integrating scaffolders
recorded their full specialist-body and affected-consumer reviews. These are
collaborating research reviews and structural checks, not independent physics
workflow acceptance or production readiness. No production transition or
publication was performed for this cohort.

poles before asserting holomorphy. The new thermal consumer supplies a complete
restricted single-pole contour argument. The original theorem is valid, but its
general published proof repair remains recorded debt, not a completed repair.
Earlier mathematics and physics source content was not rewritten by this cohort.

Source records distinguish complete retained carriers from actual bounded
reading extents and record failed retrievals. Statistical evidence retains
reported apparatus, sampling and uncertainty qualifications; no fabricated
measurement or proof of physical applicability is claimed.
