## RP-G1 — proper time, rest mass, momentum and observer energy

The mathematical proper-time functional is Δτ=c^-1∫sqrt(-g(x',x'))dσ, with τ in s. Adopt a primitive constant rest mass m>0 in kg for the massive sector and mechanical P=mU in kg m/s. Its invariant squared norm is -m²c². At p, define h_n(v)=v+g(n,v)n, derived spatial momentum q_n=h_n(P) and derived energy E_n=-c g(P,n) in J. In n's orthonormal rest basis P=(E_n/c,q_n), so E_n²=m²c⁴+c²|q_n|². The massless sector separately adopts a nonzero future null mechanical P, including a primitive positive energy/scale initial datum. ## RP-G2 — complete massive first variation and arbitrary parameter equation

Let m,c>0 and g as RP-G0; S_m[x]=-mc∫ℓ dσ, ℓ=sqrt(-g(T,T))>0, T=x'. This real finite-interval action has units J s when σ is assigned s. Its first variation is mathematically conditional on g,m; adopting stationary trajectories is a physical postulate. For a smooth fixed-endpoint variation J=∂_rx_r|0, torsion freedom gives D_rT=D_σJ. Compatibility gives δℓ=-g(D_σJ,T)/ℓ. Parameter differentiation under the integral is justified by uniform continuity on the compact parameter/curve neighborhood and the uniform positive lower bound for ℓ. Thus

δS_m=mc∫g(D_σJ,T/ℓ)dσ=-mc∫g(J,D_σ(T/ℓ))dσ.

The boundary term vanishes. Every compactly supported coordinate variation J is realized by x_r^a=x^a+rJ^a on a sufficiently short coordinate segment, and remains timelike for sufficiently small r. If a component of D_σ(T/ℓ) is nonzero, nondegeneracy supplies a coordinate basis variation with nonzero pairing, and a nonnegative smooth bump in an interval where its sign persists makes the integral nonzero. A bump is exp(-1/(1-u²)) on |u|<1 and zero outside, with all boundary derivatives zero by exponential domination. Consequently stationarity is equivalent to D_σ(T/ℓ)=0, equivalently

D_σT=(ℓ'/ℓ)T.

Since dτ/dσ=ℓ/c, this is exactly D_τU=0. Converse substitution proves stationary curves from geodesics. Substitution under an increasing C1 diffeomorphism σ=φ(s), φ'>0, gives T_s=φ'T, ℓ_s=φ'ℓ and ℓ_s ds=ℓ dσ, proving action invariance. The equation transforms consistently; a parameter is affine exactly when ℓ is constant on a nonzero timelike geodesic, so affine changes are aτ+b with a>0. Fixing proper time before varying a square-root integrand would make it look constant while moving its endpoint readings; the derivation varies first and then chooses the original curve's proper-time parameter.

Stationarity is not a global maximum. ## RP-G3 — a complete massive/massless einbein formulation

Let σ have units s, x a C2 curve, e:I→(0,∞) a C1 positive function with units kg^-1, and m≥0. Define

S_e[x,e]=(1/2)∫[e^-1 g(T,T)-e m²c²]dσ.

Both terms have units J and S_e has units J s. Assume fixed x endpoints and independent compactly supported variations of e and x. Varying e gives g(T,T)+e²m²c²=0. Varying x using the same compatibility/integration argument as RP-G2 gives D_σ(T/e)=0. The bump/nondegeneracy proof in RP-G2 proves necessity; direct substitution proves sufficiency. No null square root is differentiated.

For m>0 the constraint implies a timelike nonzero T; select its future branch. Its unique positive solution e=ℓ/(mc) gives the reduced integrand -mcℓ. Conversely every stationary square-root curve with that e satisfies both equations because T/e=mcT/ℓ. Moreover P=T/e=mU and proper-time gauge is e=1/m. Thus massive actions are equivalent on the future timelike sector, including scale and units.

For m=0 impose nonzero future T as a separate domain condition: otherwise the variational system admits a constant curve with zero momentum. The constraint now gives null T. Define P=T/e, nonzero future null. Introduce λ by dλ=e dσ; λ has units s/kg, is strictly increasing, and d x/dλ=P. The equation is D_λP=0, so λ is affine. Conversely any nonzero future affine null geodesic with chosen scale P and any positive e gives a solution by dλ=e dσ. Gauge e to a positive constant locally or on any interval mapped by this integral; there is no proper-time gauge for m=0. Global range is the integral's actual interval, not all R by assumption.

Under σ=φ(s), φ'>0, set e_s=e(φ(s))φ'(s) and x_s=x∘φ. Direct substitution shows e_s^-1g(dx_s/ds,dx_s/ds)ds=e^-1g(T,T)dσ and e_s ds=e dσ. At m=0 the rescaling of the initial affine tangent changes physical energy; it is not erased by reparametrizing the paired (x,e), which leaves P unchanged.

## Physical postulate formulations and qualifications

P-GA adopts smooth classical Lorentzian spacetime with chosen c>0 and ideal test-clock readings given by RP-G1; in SR use affine Minkowski geometry and proper orthochronous covariance S1. These premises concern prescribed smooth backgrounds and the test-body regime; self-gravity, spin, finite-size multipoles, quantum effects and singular self-fields need additional approximations/equations.
