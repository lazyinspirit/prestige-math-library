# Relativistic Particle Mechanics research scaffold

Read [prose-scaffold.md](prose-scaffold.md) for the category design and
[quantities-and-postulates.md](quantities-and-postulates.md) for units and
framework-local physical assumptions. The canonical proposed inventory is
`paired-inventory.json`, with its readable placement in
`pathway-and-inventory.md`. These files describe research only; no production
items, imported originals, engines or publication state are changed.

Complete arguments are supplier-first in:

- `workers/geometry/foundations-and-actions.md`: causal objects and massive/null actions.
- `dynamics-developments.md`: finite collisions, recoil and controlled trajectory limits.
- `workers/charge-spin/proof-modules.md`: external fields, spin, finite size and radiation models.
- `workers/hamiltonian/`: constrained phase space, time gauges, symmetries and Hamilton–Jacobi.
- `workers/examples/proofs.md`: exact flat/curved orbits and scattering.

`supplier-map.json` and worker maps bind exact proofs and hypotheses;
`mathematics-corpus-dispositions.json` indexes the wider permitted mathematics
scaffolds without claiming those plans proved. `claim-coverage.json`,
`closure-ledger.json` and `structural-check.json` record scope and reproducible
local checks. Run `python3 assemble-and-check.py` from this directory or its
repository-relative path from the root. Specialist source ledgers distinguish
actual reading extents, retrieval failures and non-load-bearing sources;
raw sources remain ignored. Local proof/structure review is not independent
acceptance or a workflow transition.
