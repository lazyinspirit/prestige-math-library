# Framework quantities and adopted preparations

This framework uses SI with ℏ and k_B explicit, natural logarithms and dimensionless state weights. A mathematical construction is distinct from its physical assignment. The same symbol for energy or entropy in another framework does not create a coupling theorem. Exact branch conditions and arguments belong to the referenced developments, not a universal quantum equilibrium postulate.

The adopted microscopic framework is the exact NRQM P1/P2/P4/P6/P7 interface: Hilbert/state and composition assignments; Born probabilities for declared measurements; specified self-adjoint Hamiltonian evolution; explicitly assigned Bose/Fermi sectors; and independently qualified apparatus/preparation/sampling models. Its original source is `physics/research/first-principles-2026-10-03/non-relativistic-quantum-mechanics/scaffold/prose-scaffold.md`. The concrete operator/domain constructions are mathematical suppliers. Species statistics are adopted assignments; a relativistic spin-statistics theorem is not derived by this nonrelativistic framework. Quantum computing and circuits are outside this category.

Canonical preparation adopts the normalized Gibbs density on a finite Hilbert space or on the exact trace-class branch. A lower bound H≥aI does not imply Tr e^−βH<∞. In the infinite-dimensional branch, e^−βH trace class on an open β interval forces a complete discrete energy basis and supplies controlled energy-moment envelopes; the proof is in the finite-state development, consuming checked thermo M33 and exact root spectral/trace items. Finite-form-energy competitors have a well-defined finite cross-log entropy and unique Gibbs free-energy minimizer. States with infinite energy and entropy are excluded from an ∞−∞ functional rather than assigned an unexplained value.

A microcanonical preparation is a specified nonzero finite-rank spectral band Π/TrΠ, optionally with separately stated commuting conserved constraints. Band center and width are part of the definition. The independent finite-level equivalence in `ensemble-bridge.md#Q2` assumes δ_N/N→0 and δ_N≥qΣ|e_j|, and proves each fixed subsystem density converges in trace norm. Exact zero-width bands may be empty. No generic interacting continuum equivalence, arbitrary window, growing-region uniform convergence or ETH assertion follows.

A grand preparation requires the actual common spectral basis/strong commutation/domain and trace conditions for H−μN, or the explicitly finite generalized-Gibbs noncommuting model. Countable Bose modes require μ below the lowest one-particle energy and the proved trace-product summability; fermion occupations require their own summability. Infinite-volume equilibrium instead uses a state functional satisfying the stated KMS strip boundary at width βℏ in physical time. It is not normalized by a global trace. Infinite quantum Ising phase conclusions require the separately proved KMS↔classical DLR bridge for that commuting interaction, rather than merely erasing off-diagonal entries.

Unitary dynamics is an independent postulate. It preserves spectral entropy and may have recurrence; stationary Gibbs preparation does not prove convergence to Gibbs from arbitrary initial states. Reduced unitary dynamics yields completely positive trace-preserving maps under an explicitly fixed uncorrelated environment preparation; a time-independent Lindblad semigroup, rate detailed balance and bath temperature are additional effective-model assumptions. A finite-time exact channel construction does not prove a weak-coupling/Markov limit. Entropy production claims retain their exact rate/reference/irreducibility hypotheses.

The category contains conditional ideal models and proved hypothetical counterexamples. A laboratory identification of a state, Hamiltonian, interaction, bath or detector requires source-backed calibration and statistical uncertainty. A measurement outcome is sampled; an expectation is not a guarantee for one trial or a finite dataset. No invented observation, counts, uncertainty, independence or experimental proof of this framework is authorized by the mathematical scaffold.
