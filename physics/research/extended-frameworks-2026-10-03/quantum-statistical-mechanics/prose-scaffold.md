# Quantum Statistical Mechanics from explicit states and preparations

Quantum statistical mechanics begins with a chosen quantum model and a preparation, rather than with an unexplained expression called a partition function. The model assigns a Hilbert space or a constructed local observable algebra, an energy operator or local interaction, measurements and dynamics. The preparation assigns a state. Probability predictions then follow from the adopted Born interface. Thermal Gibbs assignment, number exchange with a reservoir and an effective dissipative bath are additional assumptions with mathematical normalization and domain obligations. They do not follow from the existence of unitary evolution. This research dossier develops complete conditional models and their exact limits; publication and empirical validation are separate.
