postulates, precise mathematical definitions, units for every quantity/constant,
and primitive/derived status within the stated model. Specify spaces, maps,
domains, regularity, observables, symmetries, gauges, boundary/initial data and

arguments and exact hypotheses. Fill every missing prerequisite of an asserted
claim, including local specialized mathematics absent from existing suppliers.

mathematical justification: no mathematical item depends on physical premises.
Split pages and helper items as needed, at most 100 items on each A or B page.

Use the completed first-principles-2026-10-03 scaffolds and closure ledgers as
prerequisite suppliers, inspecting actual relevant statements and arguments.

consumer uses. Planned permission is not a claim of proved/published status.

model predictions and inference, with statistical uncertainty inherited by consumers.

full arguments. Record precise reading extents and failed retrievals; never invent

## Additional owner-requested team

The owner additionally requests a five-agent team for Thermodynamics and General
Relativity, with the same model, effort, proof and prerequisite requirements.
Develop covariant thermodynamics, relativistic equilibrium/transport, gravitating
statistical systems and black-hole thermodynamics deeply. Inspect actual GR,
thermodynamics, statistical-mechanics and QFT supplier claims. Classical horizon
laws do not by themselves derive Hawking temperature, black-hole entropy or the
generalized second law. Quantum/semiclassical/effective assumptions and any
unproved generalization remain explicit; all asserted results require sufficient
arguments and every missing prerequisite must be supplied.

For this bridge, distinguish local proper temperature from Killing-normalized
temperature and specify normalization domains. With geometric surface gravity
κ_geom in m⁻¹, a semiclassical temperature law uses
T=ℏcκ_geom/(2πk_B); acceleration surface gravity is κ_acc=c²κ_geom and
uses T=ℏκ_acc/(2πck_B). State the quantum assumptions before these laws.
A local entropy-current inequality is not automatically a global generalized
second law, and long-range gravitating systems do not automatically have an
extensive thermodynamic limit or equivalent ensembles.
