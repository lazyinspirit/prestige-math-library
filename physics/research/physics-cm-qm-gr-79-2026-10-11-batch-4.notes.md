## Mathematical decisions and repairs

- `ex-cm-stationary-path-need-not-be-a-local-minimum`: the exact identity `S(q_*+epsilon sin)=S(q_*)-(3pi/4)epsilon^2` is proved for `L=1/2(v^2-4q^2)` on `[0,pi]` with `q_*=sin 2t`; it shows both the failure of minimality and the absence of a minimiser. This is the concrete witness for `rem-cm-scope-of-stationarity`.
- `ex-cm-regular-lagrangians-need-not-be-hyperregular`: `sqrt(1+v^2)` is regular with range `(-1,1)`, the zero Lagrangian is neither regular nor hyperregular, and `1/2 v^2` is both; the example separates the two hypotheses of the Legendre layer.
- `ex-cm-ideal-pendulum-equation-and-stability`: the reduced d'Alembert equation is derived by projection, the reduced energy is conserved, the hanging equilibrium is shown to be a strict minimum with the direct inequality `1-cos theta>0` (no Hessian hypothesis), and the frequency `sqrt(g/l)` comes from the batch-3 quadratic-mode theorem.
- `ex-cm-rotating-hoop-reduced-energy`: the carrier identities `|x-dot|^2=a^2(theta-dot^2+Omega^2 sin^2 theta)` and `x-double-dot.X_theta=a^2 theta-double-dot-a^2 Omega^2 sin theta cos theta` are computed from the explicit partial derivatives; the reduced energy is conserved while the inertial energy changes at rate `2ma^2 Omega^2 sin theta cos theta theta-dot`; under the ideal-reaction hypothesis that rate is the reaction power; and the strict-minimum inequality `U_eff(theta)-U_eff(0)=ma(1-cos theta)[g-(a Omega^2/2)(1+cos theta)]` gives Lyapunov stability exactly for `a Omega^2<=g`. The supercritical case is stated as "not a local minimum" without claiming instability.
- `ex-cm-rolling-disk-acceleration`: the planar no-slip law is exhibited as the holonomic constraint `x-a theta=const`, the ideal-constraint model is solved to `(m+I/a^2)x''=f` with multiplier `lambda=-fI/(I+ma^2)`, the thin-disk and hoop values `2f/(3m)` and `f/(2m)` are computed, and an explicit quadratic motion realises the acceleration. The general three-dimensional rolling caveat and the empirical static-friction qualification are remarks, not deductions.
- Fact-citation fixes after the proof-contract audit: `lem-cm-first-variation-formula` step 2.1 now cites `[F6]` (integration facts), `prop-cm-hamiltonian-branch-identities` step 2.2 cites `[F2]` (the Euler-Lagrange expression it compares with), and `thm-cm-endpoint-action-differential` gained a fact `[F5]` collecting the endpoint differentiations (`d_qX(t,q,t)=I_n`, `d_qX(t,q,t_0)=0`, `d_tX(t,q,t_0)=0`, `d_tX+d_sX=0`) cited by steps 2.1 and 3.1. These changes close "declared but unused fact" findings; no mathematical claim changed.
