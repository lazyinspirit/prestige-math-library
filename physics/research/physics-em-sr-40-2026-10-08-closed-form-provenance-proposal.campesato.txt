Star-shaped sets
Definition 1. We say that 𝑆⊂ℝ𝑛is star-shaped if there exists 𝑝0 ∈𝑆such that for any 𝑞∈𝑆the line
segment from 𝑝0 to 𝑞is included in 𝑆.
Proposition 2. A non-empty convex set is star-shaped.
Beware: the empty set is convex but not star-shaped (because “∀𝑝∈∅, 𝑃(𝑝)” is vacuously true whereas
“∃𝑝∈∅, 𝑃(𝑝)” is always false).

The following are equivalent:
1. There exists 𝑓∶𝑈→ℝ𝐶1 such that F = ∇𝑓.
