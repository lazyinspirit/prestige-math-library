## Claim, conventions, and exact scope

The A page defines local operators in a finite-dimensional, rationally acted jet-polynomial superalgebra over `C`, with finitely many field species, a finite derivative cutoff, a finite maximum polynomial degree, a fixed coefficient convention, and a fixed finite weight/order. The fixed-weight space is proved finite-dimensional by `lem-aqft-finite-order-local-operator-space-finite-dimensional`; the polynomial-degree cutoff also covers weight-zero field coordinates without an infinite-dimensional completion claim. The invariant relation space is the explicitly declared sum/intersection of algebraic, integration-by-parts, leading-EOM, and allowed field-redefinition directions. The quotient is exactly by these declared relations; no completeness claim for every possible EFT redundancy is inferred.

The local exactness result `lem-aqft-invariant-quotient-exactness` proves the canonical map from invariant operators modulo invariant relations to invariant classes in the raw quotient, using only the exact finite rational-module statement of the imported complete-reducibility/Reynolds theorem. The field-redefinition proposition treats one chosen order at a time in a finite truncated formal parameter, requires the stated compact-support/no-boundary functional convention, and includes the higher-order remainder. The later theorem states quantum on-shell amplitude equivalence only for a specified finite regulator, transformed measure/Jacobian and sources, and a stable-particle LSZ data set with simple poles and nonzero overlaps. It makes no continuum-limit, anomaly-free Jacobian, arbitrary off-shell Green-function, or generic physical-QFT claim.

The B example is the fixed four-dimensional natural-unit scalar model with one even field, a declared mass and quartic coefficient, compactly supported variations, and the dimension-six density `phi^2 (d phi)^2`. A formal mass spurion of weight one makes the mass-dependent relation homogeneous in the finite weight-six space. The example computes its integration-by-parts and leading-EOM class and the exact order-`Lambda^-2` field redefinition, including the induced quartic dimension-four and sextic dimension-six terms. It does not classify a SMEFT basis or infer data from a physical model.
