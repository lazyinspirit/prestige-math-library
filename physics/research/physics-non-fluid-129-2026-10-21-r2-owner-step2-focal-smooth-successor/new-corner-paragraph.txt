Fix this $\delta$ before varying the smoothing scale. Let $\sigma$ be the smooth step in [F12] and choose $0<\epsilon<\min\{\lambda_0,L-\lambda_0\}$ small enough that the extended smooth Jacobi field from step 1.1 is defined on $[\lambda_0-\epsilon,\lambda_0+\epsilon]$. In the same parallel frame set
$$\chi_\epsilon(\lambda)=1-\sigma\!\left(\frac{\lambda-\lambda_0+\epsilon}{2\epsilon}\right),\qquad Y_\epsilon(\lambda)=\chi_\epsilon(\lambda)J(\lambda).$$
Here $J$ is its smooth extension through $\lambda_0$. The cutoff is exactly one for $\lambda\le\lambda_0-\epsilon$ and zero for $\lambda\ge\lambda_0+\epsilon$, so $Y_\epsilon$ is smooth, equals $Y$ off that interval, and agrees with $Y$ near both $0$ and $L$. Put $D=\max_{[0,1]}|\sigma'|<\infty$, and bound $|J'|\le C$ on one fixed neighborhood of $\lambda_0$. Since $J(\lambda_0)=0$, integration gives $|J(\lambda)|\le C\epsilon$ on the smoothing interval. Consequently, with the weak derivative of the continuous piecewise-smooth $Y$ from step 2.1,
$$\|Y_\epsilon-Y\|_{L^2}^2\le2C^2\epsilon^3,\qquad \|\mathcal D_\lambda(Y_\epsilon-Y)\|_{L^2}^2\le2C^2(1+D/2)^2\epsilon.$$
Indeed the difference is supported in an interval of length $2\epsilon$; its derivative there, away from the single corner of measure zero, is $(\chi_\epsilon-\mathbf1_{\{\lambda<\lambda_0\}})J'+\chi_\epsilon'J$, whose norm is at most $C+DC/2$. Thus $Y_\epsilon\to Y$ in $H^1$. All these fields take values in the positive screen, and their orthogonal lifts remain orthogonal to $\dot\gamma$. With $\delta$ fixed as above, continuity of $I$ from step 2.1 yields
$$I(Y_\epsilon+\delta W,Y_\epsilon+\delta W)\longrightarrow I(Y+\delta W,Y+\delta W)<0.$$
For sufficiently small $\epsilon$, the field $V:=Y_\epsilon+\delta W$ is therefore genuinely smooth, has $V(0)=X\in T_pS$, $V(L)=0$, and satisfies $I(V,V)<0$. [F1, F2, F3, F12, step 1.1, step 2.1, algebra, construct]
