## Definition

Assume $\mathrm{AC}_{\omega}$ and let $(M^{2n},\omega)$ be a smooth symplectic manifold. A finite family of smooth functions $\chi_1,\ldots,\chi_r$ defined on an open neighborhood $U\subseteq M$ is a **regular primary constraint family** on

$$C:=\{z\in U:\chi_1(z)=\cdots=\chi_r(z)=0\}$$

when $d\chi_1(z),\ldots,d\chi_r(z)$ are linearly independent at every $z\in C$. The regular-level theorem [[thm-a-regular-level-set-is-an-embedded-submanifold]] then gives $C$ its embedded-submanifold structure and $T_zC=\bigcap_a\ker d\chi_a(z)$. For functions $F,G$ on $U$, write $F\approx G$ when $F|_C=G|_C$; thus $F\approx0$ means that $F$ vanishes on $C$, without requiring it to vanish off $C$.

Given a smooth canonical Hamiltonian $H_c$ on $U$ and multiplier functions $u^a(t)$, the **total generator** is

$$H_T(t,z):=H_c(t,z)+\sum_{a=1}^r u^a(t)\chi_a(z).$$

The functions $u^a$ are independent parameter functions; the definition does not assert that their equations determine them. If $H_c$ is an energy and $\chi_a$ has units $[\chi_a]$, then $u^a$ has units energy divided by $[\chi_a]$, so every term in $H_T$ has the same units. A later constraint, if generated by consistency, is not declared primary by this definition.
