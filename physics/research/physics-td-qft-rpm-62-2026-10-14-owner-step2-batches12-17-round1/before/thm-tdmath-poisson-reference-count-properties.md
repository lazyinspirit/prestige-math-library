## Statement

Assume AC, fix $d\geq1$ and $\lambda>0$, and let $Q_\lambda$ be the configuration law of [[def-tdmath-poisson-configuration-reference]]. For pairwise disjoint bounded Borel sets $A_1,\ldots,A_r\subseteq\mathbb R^d$, the restrictions $\omega|_{A_1},\ldots,\omega|_{A_r}$ are independent, each count $N_{A_j}$ has Poisson law with mean $\lambda|A_j|$, and the joint law of counts on any finite disjoint family is the product of those Poisson laws. In particular, $Q_\lambda$ is invariant under every translation $T_x$, and its intensity measure is $\lambda$ times Lebesgue measure.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC, dimension $d\geq1$, $\lambda>0$, the cell-product law $Q_\lambda$, and pairwise disjoint bounded Borel sets $A_j$.

[A1] AC is part of the Poisson reference construction and of its countable product existence ([[def-axiom-of-choice]], [[def-tdmath-poisson-configuration-reference]]).

[F1] On each unit cell, the count has law $e^{-\lambda}\lambda^n/n!$ and, conditional on $n$, the points are independent uniform points subsequently sorted. Finite cell families are independent ([[def-tdmath-poisson-configuration-reference]]).

[F2] The number of length-$n$ label sequences with category counts $(n_0,\ldots,n_s)$ is the multinomial coefficient, and its finite expansion is the multinomial theorem ([[def-multinomial-coefficient]], [[thm-multinomial-theorem]]).

[F3] Exponentials turn sums into products ([[thm-exponential-addition-formula]]).

[F4] The configuration sigma-algebra is generated by bounded-Borel count maps, finite count cylinders form a generating pi-system, and agreement there determines probability laws ([[lem-tdmath-counts-generate-configuration-sigma]], [[thm-dynkin-pi-lambda]]).

## Proof

**Given:** The hypotheses and suppliers in Facts & Assumptions.

1.1 Fix one unit cell $C_i$ and a finite Borel partition $B_0,\ldots,B_s$ of it. Put $v_j=|B_j|$, so $\sum_jv_j=1$. Conditional on the cell count $N=n$, the $n$ independent uniform points give category-count probability $n!\prod_jv_j^{n_j}/\prod_jn_j!$ for $\sum_jn_j=n$. Multiplying by the Poisson count probability $e^{-\lambda}\lambda^n/n!$ gives $e^{-\lambda}\prod_j(\lambda v_j)^{n_j}/n_j!=\prod_je^{-\lambda v_j}(\lambda v_j)^{n_j}/n_j!$, by [F3]. This factors into the Poisson probabilities of the disjoint cell pieces, including the complement $B_0$; hence their counts are independent Poisson variables with respective means $\lambda v_j$. Zero-volume pieces have count zero almost surely by the same formula. [A1, F1, F2, F3, algebra]

2.1 Each bounded $A_j$ intersects only finitely many unit cells. Apply step 1.1 to the partition formed by the sets $A_j\cap C_i$ and their complement in each such cell. Independence within each cell and independence between cell configurations show that all component counts, indexed by both $j$ and $i$, are independent Poisson variables. [A1, step 1.1, F1, construct]

3.1 For fixed $j$, sum the component counts over its finitely many cells. The sum of independent Poisson variables of means $\mu_1,\ldots,\mu_q$ has mass at $n$ equal to $e^{-\sum\mu_i}(\sum\mu_i)^n/n!$: expand $(\sum_i\mu_i)^n$ by [F2], then use the product of the component masses. Thus $N_{A_j}$ is Poisson with mean $\lambda\sum_i|A_j\cap C_i|=\lambda|A_j|$. The groups of components for different $j$ are disjoint independent families, so their sums remain independent. [A1, step 2.1, F2, F3, algebra]

4.1 Let $A_1,\ldots,A_r$ be pairwise disjoint and, for each $j$, consider a finite count-cylinder event in the restriction to $A_j$. Refine the finitely many counting sets in each event into their disjoint atoms inside $A_j$. Step 3.1 gives independent Poisson counts on all these atoms across every $j$; the event for each restricted configuration is a measurable function only of its own atom counts. Thus finite cylinder events factor across $j$. The restriction sigma-fields are generated by these cylinders, so repeated pi-lambda extension proves independence of the restrictions themselves. [A1, step 3.1, F4]

5.1 For a finite family of bounded Borel sets $A_1,\ldots,A_s$, partition their union into the finitely many disjoint Boolean atoms formed by the sets and their complements. Every $N_{A_j}$ is a sum of the counts on these atoms. Translation preserves each atom's volume, so step 3.1 gives the same joint distribution of this whole count vector under $Q_\lambda$ and its translate. Thus the laws agree on every finite count cylinder. These cylinders generate the configuration sigma-algebra by [F4], and pi-lambda uniqueness identifies the laws. The one-set case gives $E_{Q_\lambda}N_A=\lambda|A|$, which is the asserted intensity measure. [A1, step 3.1, F4] ∎
