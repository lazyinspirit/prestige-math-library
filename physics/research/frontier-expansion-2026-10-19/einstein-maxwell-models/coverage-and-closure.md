## Resolution of new well-definedness and analytic obligations

Conformal fields are contravariant vector fields with the stated psi powers. A positive psi is required before the physical metric or negative powers exist. FX01 derives the scalar-curvature formula and all weights and verifies a substantive puncture family directly. It does not rely on an unprovided conformal-existence theorem.

The new bounded-domain theorem requires a genuine Hölder Dirichlet inverse. This is explicitly `lem-emg-fx-dirichlet-holder-inverse`, with Poincare, weak trace/density, kernel estimates, boundary flattening/Schauder control and continuation all required inside its proof or split into new same-page suppliers before authoring. Those are substantial local authoring duties, not open mathematical questions. `lem-emg-fx-banach-implicit-constraint` likewise contains the actual contraction/Neumann-series proof. It uses the actual published `thm-banach-fixed-point` on its nonempty complete closed ball; the operator inverse/Neumann-series and differentiability steps are proved locally. No finite-dimensional implicit theorem is misapplied to a function space. Before engine dispatch, each large proof must be subdivided into same-page A items if the resulting proof contract exceeds the native size budget; the approved theorem scope is retained.

The spherical rigidity argument requires r>0, dr nonzero, nonnull gradient and a local simply connected quotient patch. The new warped-curvature lemma proves its projection identities. Constant-radius products retain their existing local proofs and demonstrate why these assumptions matter. No global orbit-space, maximal extension or rigidity theorem is silently used.

For wave energy, integration by parts needs actual decay or finite-boundary flux control. A compact torus finite-slab estimate does not become an asymptotically flat global estimate. The matrix potential must be symmetric positive semidefinite; redshift positivity and constants are separate from a merely conserved Killing energy. Scalar separation uses the actual round-sphere harmonics only as selected eigenfunctions with their displayed eigenvalue; no completeness claim is needed for the single-mode calculation. A theorem reconstructing arbitrary fields from all modes must add its Hilbert/smooth convergence suppliers.

The horizon-growth theorem assumes smooth ingoing regularity and an undifferentiated horizon decay condition; it proves its implication explicitly by an ODE. The source-reported full Aretakis decay theorem is not a logical supplier. The charged-scattering identity assumes a solution with differentiated asymptotics; constructing Jost/scattering solutions is not necessary to the conditional identity and is not claimed. FX08 includes the required long-range Coulomb logarithmic phase at infinity. A later scattering-existence item must construct this asymptotic class rather than substituting short-range plane-wave Jost theory.

The finite algorithm uses exact rational matrices and finite N, has separate correctness and termination items, and explicitly distinguishes arithmetic counts from bit complexity. Compatible incidence identities supply constraints, whereas a positive mass matrix and a strict CFL bound supply a positive discrete energy. Continuum convergence is a separate theorem with an actual mesh/commuting-projection interface.

Presymplectic quantities are defined at fixed background; full coupled metric perturbations require their additional gravitational and mixed terms. Gauge transformations with boundary values may carry boundary charge. The finite coisotropic result is not used outside finite dimensions. Topological sectors, curvature and holonomy are distinguished throughout.
