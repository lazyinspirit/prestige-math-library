Access date: 2026-10-19. Raw downloads and extracted text remain in the ignored run-local source cache. SHA256 values in sources.json bind raw downloads, not imagined source approval. Every listed reading range is bounded; no complete original-proof reading is implied.

## aqft

Fewster and Rejzner, Algebraic Quantum Field Theory—an introduction

https://arxiv.org/pdf/1904.04051

PDF pp.24–26, §5.1 and theorem26 proof through edge-of-the-wedge invocation; original audit scaffold source bibliography also read.

Spectrum, GNS and Reeh–Schlieder hypotheses; deep tube uniqueness not read in cited original source.

## photon

Bliokh, Alonso, Ostrovskaya and Aiello, Angular Momenta and Spin-Orbit Interaction of Nonparaxial Light in Free Space

https://arxiv.org/pdf/1006.3876

PDF pp.1–2, equations(1)–(8) and transversality discussion.

Exact projected commutators/helicity Berry connection; source uses ℏ=c=1 and its gauge parameter, translated explicitly here.

## renorm

Brunetti, Dütsch and Fredenhagen, Perturbative Algebraic Quantum Field Theory and the Renormalization Groups

https://arxiv.org/pdf/0901.2038

Abstract only (PDF text extraction is degraded).

Cocycle versus subgroup distinction; detailed formal-renormalization route uses paQFT instead. No claim of reading full theorem proof.

## brst

Barnich, Brandt and Henneaux, Local BRST cohomology in gauge theories

https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/0002245

§2.7 Appendix2.B, PDF pp.19–20; introduction/§2 anomaly consistency paragraphs.

Contractible-pair homotopy and consistency obstruction; no full classification theorem or Adler–Bardeen proof read.

## amplitudes

Elvang and Huang, Scattering Amplitudes

https://arxiv.org/pdf/1308.1697

§2.2 PDF pp.11–12 (rank-one null momenta and spinors); table of contents and source bibliography inspected for recursion.

Spinor helicity conventions. Rational recursion is derived here conditionally; full model-specific large-z proofs not claimed read.

## gravity

Baez, An Introduction to Spin Foam Models of BF Theory and Quantum Gravity

https://arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/9905087

PDF pp.1–3, §2 opening (BF fields/action); PDF pp.30–31 remarks on Ponzano–Regge and finite groups.

BF/gravity scope and divergences; no contemporary status survey or full spin-foam equivalence proof read.

## hall

Bridgeland, Hall algebras and Donaldson–Thomas invariants

https://arxiv.org/pdf/1611.03696

PDF pp.1–4, §§1.1–1.4.

Hereditary versus CY3 integration distinction; exact finite-quiver theorem supplied by actual root draft, not inferred from review.

## constructive

Summers, A Perspective on Constructive Quantum Field Theory

https://arxiv.org/pdf/1203.3991

PDF pp.10–12, §3 opening and §3.1.

Corrected OS regularity caveat, P(φ)₂ cutoff/infinite-volume and weak-coupling qualifications; review is not a local proof of full construction.

## paQFT

Fredenhagen and Rejzner, Perturbative algebraic quantum field theory

https://arxiv.org/pdf/1208.1428

PDF pp.27–30, §7, theorem7.2 and equations77–86.

Causal extension, finite local renormalization and graph expansion; source itself refers extension proof elsewhere, supplied by retained IR04 and new EG obligations.

## eg

Brunetti and Fredenhagen, Microlocal analysis and interacting quantum field theories: renormalization on physical backgrounds

https://arxiv.org/pdf/math-ph/9903028

PDF pp.7–9,18–25,33–34; §§2.1,4,5,7–8.

Wavefront product/trace criteria, causal cover and overlap gluing, scaling extension and diagonal induction; whole curved locally covariant renormalization not certified.
