## Terminology and well-definedness resolution

The framework contract fixes types, SI and signs before using any extension. Each new item gives its exact domain, regularity and model regime. A two-form, real one-form potential, excitation, conserved current three-form, H(curl) graph class, closure boundary condition, trace pairing, time-harmonic phasor, susceptibility, oscillator state, transverse Fourier fibre, helicity projector, line-bundle frame, Berry curvature, Jones/coherency matrix, oriented edge/face incidence and port amplitude are all explicitly constructed. “Finite energy” means an actual positive quadratic integral with stated coefficients. “Radiation data” means a limit along fixed retarded time. “Paraxial” means a specified Fourier support cone with a distance-dependent error bound. “Passive” follows from the augmented energy or a nonnegative loss form in the adopted state model, not just Im epsilon rhetoric. “Unitary” for a scattering matrix requires all channels and zero loss. Each algorithm has admissible inputs, operations, output and separate correctness/termination statements.

The existing positive Riemannian-star theorem is not reused for Lorentz signature. The existing vector-field Poincare lemma is not misapplied to two-forms. Weak tangential trace is not silently an L2 vector on the boundary; the quotient-dual supplier alone is insufficient for a quadratic boundary product. Full-space absolute mesh cohomology is not equated with the PEC relative kernel. Relative compactness explicitly inherits AC, CC and DC from its root theorem; all other analytic CC uses remain recorded. Angular Fourier normalization is explicitly converted from the root 2-pi convention before unitary estimates.

Classical coherence here is a specified random-amplitude model, not a quantum statistical state. Source-level articles motivate structured-light interfaces but are not claimed to certify an October2026 frontier survey.

## Limitations and nonlogical unresolved issues

The new scaffold establishes only its exact model statements and planned supplier-backed routes. It makes no full nonlinear coupled point-charge existence claim, no assertion that a singular Maxwell self-field has a finite product with its own source, no generic well-posedness theorem for arbitrary passive nonlocal/bianisotropic media, no unrestricted real-frequency Maxwell resonance solver and no proof that an ideal scalar aperture model approximates every vector screen geometry. These stronger assertions are not promised conclusions awaiting a vague theorem; they require different precisely stated models and proofs. They are not silently used as suppliers. No genuinely open problem is a prerequisite, and no claim is made that an open problem was solved. The only remaining task in this authored research scope is root integration of the registration support and ordinary future independent source/proof review; publishing requires the normal later workflow.
