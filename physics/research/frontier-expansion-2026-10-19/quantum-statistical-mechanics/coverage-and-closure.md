## Terminology and well-definedness

A density is positive trace one on its stated finite or trace-class Hilbert space. A quasilocal state is instead a positive normalized bounded functional. A cyclic vector need not be separating; pure finite matrix states give a counterexample. Normality is representation-dependent. Modular imaginary powers are bounded even when the positive modular operator and its inverse are unbounded; their graph domains cannot be replaced by the whole Hilbert space. The vector modular group is point-ultraweakly continuous; it is not claimed norm-continuous on an arbitrary von Neumann algebra. C-star KMS applications require the appropriate norm-continuous restriction.

The upper KMS strip for physical time has width beta hbar. For sigma_t=Ad Delta^it the standard vector is KMS at beta=-1 in Hiai's convention; reversing time gives beta=+1. The finite Gibbs calculation fixes alpha_s=sigma_(-s/(beta hbar)). This exact interface is provided to combined thermodynamics/GR; identifying a modular clock with a wedge boost or horizon Killing flow is that category's additional geometric/physical premise.

Entropy is dimensionless unless multiplied by k_B. Every logarithm at a zero eigenvalue uses the stated entropy convention; relative entropy outside support inclusion is positive infinity. Differentiating log rho is only asserted for faithful rho; nonfaithful entropy production uses limits. The finite mutual information boundary bound uses a finite Gibbs variational comparison, not the ground-state area theorem or continuum vacuum entanglement. Spohn monotonicity needs a stationary faithful reference and CPTP semigroup; it does not imply primitivity or strict decay.

MPS tensors have compatible finite bond dimensions and a nonzero state before normalization. The SVD sweep reports discarded norms of its current remainders, not silently the original tensor's tails. Transfer-map spectral estimates permit Jordan blocks, with any radius strictly above the subleading spectral radius. A parent Hamiltonian gap is not inferred from an omitted technical inequality. Uniform gapped-path assumptions are explicit in spectral flow and are not deduced from endpoint gaps.

The finite group quantum double uses its displayed finite basis and oriented lattice action. Representation classification is a finite centralizer-induction theorem, not a claim that an arbitrary Hopf algebra is a unitary anyon theory. Toric ground degeneracy and disk local tests are proved in the unperturbed finite model, without claiming a finite-temperature stable memory. QFT can consume this precise lattice input for geometric braiding interpretations and categorical comparisons.

The spectral R matrix has singular values u=plus/minus i; the transfer commutativity argument proves an identity generically then extends it polynomially, so those spectral parameters are included in the conclusion without dividing by singular R. The logarithmic charge uses t(0) invertible and N>=3. Bethe regular-root hypotheses do not include singular solutions, completeness, thermodynamic root distributions or generalized hydrodynamics. Lie symmetry, Hall multiplication and KLR decategorification constrain representations, not energies; the two-spin partition-function counterexample makes that distinction quantitative.

The transverse Ising chain is open before Jordan–Wigner conversion; no missing fermion-parity periodic boundary term enters. The infinite bulk symbol is a separately constructed one-particle convolution operator, with Parseval/density and Fourier multiplication proof obligations stated. Its gap closes at abs(h)=abs(J); phase degeneracy at h=0 is explicit, while full order-parameter criticality is not silently asserted.
