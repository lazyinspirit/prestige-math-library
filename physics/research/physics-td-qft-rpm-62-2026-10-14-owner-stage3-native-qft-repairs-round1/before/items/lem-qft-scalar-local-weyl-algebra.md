## Statement

Assume Countable Choice [[def-countable-choice]].
Let $O\subseteq\mathbb R^4$ be open. For real
$f\in C_c^\infty(O)$ define
$$W_\mu(f):=W_{\rm Segal}(\sqrt2K_\mu f),$$
where the Segal normalization is exactly
$\Phi_{\rm Segal}(h)=(a^\dagger(h)+a(h))/\sqrt2$ on the finite-particle
domain, as in [[lem-qft-segal-weyl-domain]]. This scale makes the self-adjoint generator
of $t\mapsto W_\mu(tf)$ equal to the closure $\overline{\Phi_\mu(f)}$,
whose restriction to $\mathcal D_{\rm fin}$ is the real scalar field
$\Phi_\mu(f)=a(K_\mu f)+a^\dagger(K_\mu f)$ of
[[lem-qft-smeared-free-scalar]]. Define the unital norm-closed operator
algebra
$$\mathfrak A_\mu(O)= \overline{\operatorname{alg}}^{\|\cdot\|} \{I,W_\mu(f),W_\mu(f)^*:f\in C_c^\infty(O;\mathbb R)\}.$$
Then $O_1\subseteq O_2$ implies
$\mathfrak A_\mu(O_1)\subseteq\mathfrak A_\mu(O_2)$. If every difference
$x-y$, with $x\in O_1$ and $y\in O_2$, is spacelike, then every element of
$\mathfrak A_\mu(O_1)$ commutes with every element of
$\mathfrak A_\mu(O_2)$. These are algebraic properties of the constructed
representation; they are not empirical signaling or measurement claims.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The scalar shell, Fock and Segal conventions of [[def-qft-scalar-mass-shell]], [[lem-qft-smeared-free-scalar]] and [[lem-qft-segal-weyl-domain]], and the causal commutator result.

[A1] Assume Countable Choice. [[def-countable-choice]]

[A2] $\mathfrak A_\mu(O)$ is by definition the operator-norm closure of the unital algebra generated by the listed local Weyl unitaries and their adjoints; $C_c^\infty(O)$ embeds into $C_c^\infty(O')$ when $O\subseteq O'$.

[F1] With the scale $\sqrt2$, the shell Weyl operators obey $W_\mu(f)W_\mu(g)= e^{-i\operatorname{Im}\langle K_\mu f,K_\mu g\rangle_2}W_\mu(f+g)$ for real tests. [[lem-qft-smeared-free-scalar]]

[F2] The field commutator is $[\Phi_\mu(f),\Phi_\mu(g)]=-i\Delta_\mu(f,g)I$, and $\Delta_\mu(f,g)=0$ for spacelike-separated supports. [[lem-qft-scalar-causal-commutator]]

[F3] For real tests, the field formula and $[a(h),a^\dagger(k)]=\langle h,k\rangle_2I$ imply $[\Phi_\mu(f),\Phi_\mu(g)]=2i\operatorname{Im} \langle K_\mu f,K_\mu g\rangle_2I$. [[lem-qft-smeared-free-scalar]] [[lem-qft-fock-ccr-car]]

[F4] The one-parameter group $t\mapsto W_{\rm Segal}(th)$ has norm derivative at zero exactly on the domain of the closed Segal field, with derivative $i\Phi_{\rm Segal}(h)$. [[lem-qft-segal-weyl-domain]]

## Proof

1.1 For real $f$, the essentially self-adjoint finite-particle scalar field has closure $\overline{\Phi_\mu(f)}=\Phi_{\rm Segal}(\sqrt2K_\mu f)$ by the Segal normalization and [F4]. Thus [F4] identifies this closure as the self-adjoint generator of $t\mapsto W_\mu(tf)$. If $O_1\subseteq O_2$, each real test function supported in $O_1$ is also a real test function supported in $O_2$. Thus the generators and their adjoints for $\mathfrak A_\mu(O_1)$ belong to the generating set for $\mathfrak A_\mu(O_2)$; finite sums and products do too, and norm closure preserves inclusion. This proves isotony. [A2, F4, given, algebra]

2.1 Let $f,g$ be real tests supported in the two spacelike-separated regions. By [F2] their field commutator is zero. Fact [F3], obtained by expanding the fields with the CCR, then gives $2i\,\operatorname{Im}\langle K_\mu f,K_\mu g\rangle_2I=0$; hence the imaginary part vanishes. Substituting it in [F1] gives $W_\mu(f)W_\mu(g)=W_\mu(f+g)=W_\mu(g)W_\mu(f)$. Taking adjoints shows each generator from the first region commutes with each generator and adjoint from the second. Commutation is preserved under finite linear combinations and products; if bounded sequences $A_n\to A$ and $B_n\to B$ in operator norm satisfy $A_nB_n=B_nA_n$, then continuity of multiplication gives $AB=BA$. Taking the two norm closures proves the assertion for the full local algebras. [A1, F1, F2, F3, step 1.1, given, algebra] ∎
