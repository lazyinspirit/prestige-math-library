This page adopts the fixed finite-coordinate model of
[[post-qft-regulated-polynomial-model]]. Each label has dimensionless coordinate
and momentum maps on the common test domain; their coordinate-momentum
commutators give the finite bosonic convention used here. Polynomial terms of
total degree at least three are the model's finite-coordinate interaction
terms. It states the joule-valued Hamiltonian conversion in
[[post-qft-regulated-polynomial-model]], derives the form-selected global
evolution in [[pthm-qft-regulated-interacting-model-evolution]], adopts the
positive-temperature canonical state in
[[post-qft-regulated-equilibrium-state]], and derives its bounded-observable
trace predictions in [[pthm-qft-regulated-equilibrium-predictions]]. The
one-mode energy comparison is proved in
[[pthm-qft-quartic-model-energy-bound]]. Every physical prediction is
conditional on the stated physical adoptions and mathematical finite-regulator
constructions. The claims concern one fixed regulator; they assert no
continuum or infinite-volume limit, measured energy, or reported experiment.
