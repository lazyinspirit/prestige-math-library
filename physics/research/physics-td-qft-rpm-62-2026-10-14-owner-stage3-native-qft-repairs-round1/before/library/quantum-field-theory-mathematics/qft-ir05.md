This page defines a finite connected quartic graph and a precise Euclidean superficial power-counting convention. It proves the quartic incidence and loop-count identities, derives $\omega_D=D+(D-4)V-(D-2)E/2$, and specializes to $4-E$ in four dimensions. A separate proposition derives scalar engineering dimensions and the tree-level dilation exponent after explicitly assigning degree zero to the kinetic and monomial action terms. A zero superficial degree is a power-counting label; these mathematical results do not prove convergence, divergence, a quantum fixed point, or a running coupling.
