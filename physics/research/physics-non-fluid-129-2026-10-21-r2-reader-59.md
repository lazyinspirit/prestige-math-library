## Review findings

1. **`thm-qftx-positive-unbounded-spectral-calculus`, Statement and Proof steps 3.1–5.1 — ill-formed.** The item defines `E_B` only on Borel subsets of `sigma(B)`, then uses `E_B({0})` without defining an extension to ambient Borel sets. The allowed case `A=0` gives `B=I` and `sigma(B)={1}`, so `{0}` is not in the declared domain of `E_B`. Step 3.1 and step 5.1 repeat the undefined evaluation. Define `E_B(S)` for ambient Borel `S` by restriction to `S∩sigma(B)`, or write the relative set `{0}∩sigma(B)` consistently.

2. **Published supplier `def-schwartz-space-and-its-seminorms`, Definition — ill-formed; assigned consumer: `thm-qftx-tempered-massive-gaussian-field`.** The supplier defines the Schwartz-function set and its separating seminorms, but does not specify a topology on that set. The assigned consumer uses its continuous real-linear dual and claims its samples are continuous tempered distributions. The relevant continuity is therefore not determined by the supplied definition. A definition of the locally convex topology generated by the listed seminorms is needed.

3. **`lem-qftx-hermite-coefficient-decay`, Statement, final sentence — ill-formed.** The item defines the Schwartz space as a set of rapidly decreasing smooth functions, but states that the coefficient map is continuous from it without specifying the domain topology. The claimed continuity needs the topology generated by the Schwartz seminorms, or another explicit topology.

4. **`thm-qftx-tempered-massive-gaussian-field`, Statement, sentence asserting finite vectors are “centered jointly Gaussian” — ill-formed.** Neither the Statement nor Facts & Assumptions defines this finite-dimensional law or cites the exact definition. `def-multivariate-normal-law` is an available exact supplier, but it is not declared in this item's dependencies. The proof computes characteristic functions and thereby establishes the result, but it does not define the term before the Statement uses it. State, for example, that every real linear projection has a centered scalar normal law with variance given by the covariance matrix, or add and use the exact supplier.

5. **`thm-qftx-tempered-massive-gaussian-field`, `sources.references`, Teschl *Topics in Real and Functional Analysis* locator — citation-inaccurate, nonfatal.** The locator attributes Gaussian-regularized Fourier inversion to Theorems 14.9–14.10 on printed p.383. In the cited source, Gaussian-regularized inversion is Theorem 14.7 (printed p.382); Lemma 14.9 and Theorem 14.10 on printed p.383 give the L¹/Plancherel identity and its L² extension. The reference is relevant, but its locator should distinguish those results. The local proof and declared Parseval supplier separately support the covariance calculation.

6. **`lem-qftx-finite-lorentz-cocycle-splitting`, Proof step 4.1 — ill-formed.** The averaging argument identifies `SU(2)` with unit quaternions and then uses quaternion multiplication, conjugation, norm identities, and the assertion that left multiplication is orthogonal. The item gives a matrix parametrization but does not define the quaternion algebra or the identification map, and its dependencies do not supply them. Define the identification and verify that the group action preserves the Euclidean norm, or prove the isometry directly from the displayed matrices.
