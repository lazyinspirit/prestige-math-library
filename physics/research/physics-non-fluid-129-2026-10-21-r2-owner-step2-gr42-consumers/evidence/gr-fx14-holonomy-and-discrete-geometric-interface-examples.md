# Holonomy and discrete geometric interfaces — Examples

The examples exhibit three bounded consequences of the A-page interfaces: a flat Lorentz connection with local endpoint-independent transport but nontrivial global loop transport; a triangular finite graph where the individual edge matrices change under a vertex gauge transformation while the Wilson trace stays fixed; and the antisymmetric invariant tensor at a bivalent $SU(2)$ vertex, which normalized Haar averaging fixes. The last example inherits the full-Axiom-of-Choice hypothesis of the Haar supplier.
