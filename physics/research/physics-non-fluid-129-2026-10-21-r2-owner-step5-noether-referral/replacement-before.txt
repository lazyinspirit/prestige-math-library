The remaining
terms in the product rule, the curvature commutator, and the other terms of
$J_\zeta$ contain at most first derivatives of $\zeta$. Repeatedly apply
$$A^a{}_b\nabla_a\zeta^b=\nabla_a(A^a{}_b\zeta^b) -(\nabla_aA^a{}_b)\zeta^b$$
to the lower-order terms in the canonical construction. Each step lowers
the derivative order on $\zeta$ by one, so the residual potential term is
linear in $\zeta$, denoted $W^c\zeta_c$. This proves the canonical formula.
