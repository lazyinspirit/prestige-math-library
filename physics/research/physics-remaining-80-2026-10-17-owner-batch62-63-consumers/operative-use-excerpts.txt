$$\alpha^{\mathrm{loc}}_\tau(C)=e^{iG_i\tau/\hbar}Ce^{-iG_i\tau/\hbar},\qquad \alpha^K_t(C)=e^{iN_iG_it/\hbar}Ce^{-iN_iG_it/\hbar}.$$

$$i\hbar\,\frac{d}{d\tau}U_{\mathrm{loc}}(\tau)=\bigl(H_i-\epsilon h(\tau)B\bigr)U_{\mathrm{loc}}(\tau),\qquad U_{\mathrm{loc}}(0)=I_{\mathcal H_i},$$
$$i\hbar\,\frac{d}{dt}U_K(t)=\bigl(H_{K,i}-\epsilon N_i h(N_it)B\bigr)U_K(t),\qquad U_K(0)=I_{\mathcal H_i}.$$
They are unitary propagators. Define the model expectations by
$$E_{\mathrm{loc}}(\tau):=\operatorname{Tr}\!\left(\sigma U_{\mathrm{loc}}(\tau)^*A U_{\mathrm{loc}}(\tau)\right),\qquad E_K(t):=\operatorname{Tr}\!\left(\sigma U_K(t)^*A U_K(t)\right).$$
Because $A$ and $B$ commute with $Q_i$, the second expectation is unchanged if $H_{K,i}$ is replaced by $N_iH_i$; that is the neutral-sector version of the Killing control. The exact finite-cell predictions are

$$E_K(t)=E_{\mathrm{loc}}(N_it)\qquad(0\le t\le S/N_i),$$

and

$$E_{\mathrm{loc}}(\tau)=\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma\alpha^{\mathrm{loc}}_\tau(A))+\epsilon\int_0^\tau h(s)R_{\mathrm{loc}}(\tau-s)\,ds+r(\tau),$$

where

$$R_{\mathrm{loc}}(u)=\frac{i}{\hbar}\operatorname{Tr}\!\left(\sigma[\alpha^{\mathrm{loc}}_u(A),B]\right),\qquad C_S=\frac{2}{\hbar}\int_0^S|h(s)|\,\|B\|\,ds,\qquad |r(\tau)|\le\|A\|\frac{(|\epsilon|C_S)^2e^{|\epsilon|C_S}}2.$$

Here $C_S$ is dimensionless and $R_{\mathrm{loc}}$ has units $[A][B]/(\mathrm{J\,s})$. $$S_K=(D_{N_i})_*S_{\mathrm{loc}},\qquad S_K(\omega)=N_i^{-1}S_{\mathrm{loc}}(\omega/N_i),$$

the detailed-balance factor is unchanged because $\beta_K\hbar(N_i\omega)=\beta_{\mathrm{loc},i}\hbar\omega$, and any zero-frequency atom remains at zero and is kept separate from the $\coth$ formula. $$\left.\frac{d}{d\lambda}\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma_\lambda B)\right|_{\lambda=0}=\beta_{\mathrm{loc},i}\operatorname{Cov}_{\mathrm{KM},\sigma}(B,B)\ge0,$$

with $\operatorname{Cov}_{\mathrm{KM},\sigma}(B,B)=\int_0^1\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma^{1-u}\delta B\,\sigma^u\delta B)\,du$ and $\delta B=B-\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma B)I$. Fix a cell label $i\in\{1,\ldots,r\}$ in [[thm-tgr-finite-cell-gibbs-factorization]], let $x_i=\chi(i)$ be its assigned point, and write $N:=N_i=N(x_i)>0$, $H_{\mathrm{loc}}:=H_i$, $Q:=Q_i$, and $\mu_{\mathrm{loc}}:=\mu_i$. Let $\beta_K>0$ in joules$^{-1}$ and $\mu_K$ in joules be the global parameters supplied there. Put $G_{\mathrm{loc}}:=H_{\mathrm{loc}}-\mu_{\mathrm{loc}}Q$ and $G_K:=NG_{\mathrm{loc}}$. Let $\sigma$ be the cell Gibbs density supplied by that theorem, so $\sigma=Z^{-1}e^{-\beta_{\mathrm{loc}}G_{\mathrm{loc}}}=Z^{-1}e^{-\beta_KG_K}$ with $\beta_{\mathrm{loc}}=N\beta_K$. For neutral Hermitian matrices, define
$$\alpha^{\mathrm{loc}}_\tau(C):=e^{iG_{\mathrm{loc}}\tau/\hbar}Ce^{-iG_{\mathrm{loc}}\tau/\hbar},\qquad \alpha^K_t(C):=e^{iG_Kt/\hbar}Ce^{-iG_Kt/\hbar}.$$
For this selected cell, let $q$ be the common charge in coulombs and $\Phi_i:=\Phi_\xi(x_i)$ its compensated potential in volts from the supplier. Define $H_{K,i}:=NH_{\mathrm{loc}}+q\Phi_iQ$ in joules, and write $H_K:=H_{K,i}$ in the physical control below. The supplier normalization gives $N\mu_{\mathrm{loc}}+q\Phi_i=\mu_K$, hence $H_K=G_K+\mu_KQ$.

Let $A=A^*$ and $B=B^*$ satisfy $[A,Q]=[B,Q]=0$. Then their local proper-time and Killing-time dynamics agree after $\tau=Nt$.

Let $h:[0,S]\to\mathbb R$ be continuous, with $S>0$ seconds, and let $h(\tau)B$ have units joules. For dimensionless $\epsilon\in\mathbb R$, the proper-time grand-generator control $G_{\mathrm{loc}}-\epsilon h(\tau)B$ corresponds in Killing time to $G_K-\epsilon N h(Nt)B$. Define $E_{\mathrm{loc}}(\tau)$ and $E_K(t)$ as the finite-time expectations of $A$ from the same initial density $\sigma$ under these controls. Because $A$ and $B$ commute with $Q$, replacing each grand generator by its physical Hamiltonian changes the propagator only by a unitary generated by a constant multiple of $Q$ and leaves the expectation of $A$ unchanged. Thus these expectations also equal those obtained from the respective physical controls $H_{\mathrm{loc}}-\epsilon h(\tau)B$ and $H_K-\epsilon Nh(Nt)B$. In particular,
$$E_K(t)=E_{\mathrm{loc}}(Nt)\qquad(0\le t\le S/N).$$
The finite-time Kubo expansions have identical first-order terms under this rescaling and the same quadratic remainder bound. With
$$R_{\mathrm{loc}}(u):=\frac{i}{\hbar}\operatorname{Tr}\!\left(\sigma[\alpha^{\mathrm{loc}}_u(A),B]\right),\qquad C_S:=\frac{2}{\hbar}\int_0^S|h(s)|\,\|B\|\,ds,$$
the local expansion is
$$E_{\mathrm{loc}}(\tau)=\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma\alpha^{\mathrm{loc}}_\tau(A))+\epsilon\int_0^\tau h(s)R_{\mathrm{loc}}(\tau-s)\,ds+r_{\mathrm{loc}}(\tau),\qquad |r_{\mathrm{loc}}(\tau)|\le\|A\|\frac{(|\epsilon|C_S)^2e^{|\epsilon|C_S}}2.$$
Use $\hbar>0$ in joule-seconds, so $C_S$ is dimensionless and $R_{\mathrm{loc}}$ has units $[A][B]/(\mathrm{J\,s})$. The Killing-time response kernel is $R_K(t)=R_{\mathrm{loc}}(Nt)$ and its control amplitude is $Nh(Nt)$.

For the equilibrium correlation spectral measures, use the Fourier convention of [[thm-qsm-dt-spectral-fdt]]. If $D_N(\omega):=N\omega$, then
$$S_K=(D_N)_*S_{\mathrm{loc}},\qquad S_K(\omega)=N^{-1}S_{\mathrm{loc}}(\omega/N)$$
in distribution notation; the measure form preserves the weights of delta-function spectral lines. Since $\beta_K\hbar(N\omega)=\beta_{\mathrm{loc}}\hbar\omega$, detailed balance is unchanged. Any zero-frequency atom remains an atom at zero and is not assigned a value by the coth formula, which remains restricted to nonzero frequencies.

For a static local perturbation $G_{\mathrm{loc}}-\lambda B$, where $\lambda B$ has units joules, the re-prepared Gibbs family satisfies
$$\left.\frac{d}{d\lambda}\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma_\lambda B)\right|_{\lambda=0}=\beta_{\mathrm{loc}}\operatorname{Cov}_{\mathrm{KM},\sigma}(B,B)=N\beta_K\operatorname{Cov}_{\mathrm{KM},\sigma}(B,B)\ge0.$$
Here $\delta B:=B-\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma B)I$ and
$$\operatorname{Cov}_{\mathrm{KM},\sigma}(B,B):=\int_0^1\operatorname{Tr}\!\left(\sigma^{1-u}\delta B\,\sigma^u\delta B\right)\,du.$$
It reduces to the ordinary variance when $B$ commutes with $G_{\mathrm{loc}}$; no such reduction is asserted otherwise. These are exact finite-dimensional deductions for the constructed neutral cell. They imply no infinite-time relaxation, dc transport coefficient, or response law for arbitrary interacting reduced states.



### thm-tgr-juttner-partition-and-moments
Let $x$ be an event in a smooth time-oriented Lorentzian four-manifold, let
$M_*>0$ be an energy in joules, and let $u\in T_xM$ be future unit timelike,
$g(u,u)=-1$. Fix $k_B>0$ in $\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$, $T>0$ in kelvins,
$n>0$ in $\mathrm{m^{-3}}$, $c>0$ in $\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$, and a reference
action $h_{\rm ref}>0$ in $\mathrm{J\,s}$. Put
$$\alpha:=\frac1{k_BT},\qquad \beta:=\frac{u}{k_BT},\qquad E_u(P):=-g(P,u).$$
For $j=0,1,2,3$ and $z>0$ define
$$K_j(z):=\int_0^\infty e^{-z\cosh t}\cosh(jt)\,dt.$$
With the future-shell measure of [[def-tgr-future-mass-shell-measure]], define
$$I_{M_*}(\alpha):=\int_{\mathcal H^+_{M_*,x}}e^{g(\beta,P)}\,d\mu_{M_*}(P),\qquad Z_{M_*}(\alpha):=\int_{\mathbb R^3}e^{-\alpha E(q)}\,d^3q,$$
where $E(q)=\sqrt{M_*^2+|q|^2}$ in a future orthonormal tetrad whose time
axis is $u$. Both integrals are finite and positive, and
$$I_{M_*}(\alpha)=\frac{4\pi M_*}{\alpha}K_1(\alpha M_*),\qquad Z_{M_*}(\alpha)=\frac{4\pi M_*^2}{\alpha}K_2(\alpha M_*).$$
The observer-rest momentum law
$$d\nu_u(q):=Z_{M_*}(\alpha)^{-1}e^{-\alpha E(q)}d^3q$$
is a probability measure. Define the dimensionless occupation and its number
current and stress moments by
$$\zeta:=\log\!\left(\frac{n h_{\rm ref}^3c^3}{Z_{M_*}(\alpha)}\right),\qquad f(P):=e^{\zeta+g(\beta,P)},$$
$$J^a:=(h_{\rm ref}^3c^3)^{-1}\int_{\mathcal H^+_{M_*,x}}P^a f(P)\,d\mu_{M_*}(P),\qquad T^{ab}:=(h_{\rm ref}^3c^3)^{-1}\int_{\mathcal H^+_{M_*,x}}P^aP^b f(P)\,d\mu_{M_*}(P).$$
These integrals converge, and, with
$$\varepsilon:=n\left(M_*\frac{K_1(\alpha M_*)}{K_2(\alpha M_*)}+\frac3\alpha\right),\qquad p:=\frac n\alpha=nk_BT,$$
they satisfy $J^a=nu^a$ and
$T^{ab}=(\varepsilon+p)u^au^b+pg^{ab}$. The quantities $J$ and $T$ have
units $\mathrm{m^{-3}}$ and $\mathrm{J\,m^{-3}}$, respectively. The reference
action $h_{\rm ref}$ is only a declared phase-space unit convention; it is
not Planck's constant or a measured parameter.

**Given:** The future massive shell and measure of
[[def-tgr-future-mass-shell-measure]], its tetrad invariance, the future unit
vector $u$, the positive constants and fields in the Statement, and the
countable-choice assumptions of the integration suppliers.

[F1] The shell measure is $d^3q/E(q)$ in any future orthonormal tetrad and is
tetrad-independent ([[def-tgr-future-mass-shell-measure]],
[[lem-tgr-mass-shell-measure-tetrad-invariance]]).

### thm-tgr-kms-orbit-time-rescaling
For the geometric interpretation, let $O$ be a connected open subset of a smooth time-oriented Lorentzian four-manifold $(M,g)$ of signature $(-,+,+,+)$, and let $\xi$ be a smooth future-directed timelike Killing field on $O$ with dimensionless components in length coordinates $x^0=ct$, where $c>0$ is the speed of light in $\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$. Define $N_\xi(x):=\sqrt{-g_x(\xi_x,\xi_x)}>0$ and $u:=\xi/N_\xi$. For a selected point $x_0\in O$, let $I_K\subseteq\mathbb R$ be an interval in seconds containing $0$, and let $\gamma_{x_0}:I_K\to O$ be the $C^1$ integral curve of $c\xi$ with $\gamma_{x_0}(0)=x_0$, so $\gamma_{x_0}'(t_K)=c\,\xi_{\gamma_{x_0}(t_K)}$; this is the Killing orbit through $x_0$ with Killing-time parameter $t_K$. For a cell assigned to $x_i$, take $x_0=x_i$ and denote this curve by $\gamma_i$. Define its proper-time parameter from the metric by
$$\tau(t_K):=\frac1c\int_0^{t_K}\sqrt{-g_{\gamma_{x_0}(s)}\bigl(\gamma_{x_0}'(s),\gamma_{x_0}'(s)\bigr)}\,ds,\qquad \tau(0)=0.$$
By [[lem-tgr-killing-thermal-normalization]], $\xi(N_\xi)=0$, so $N:=N_\xi(x_0)>0$ is constant along this orbit. The metric definition then gives $d\tau=N\,dt_K$ and, with the chosen origins, $\tau=Nt_K$; equivalently $d\gamma_{x_0}/d\tau=cu$ for $u=\xi/N_\xi$. For $k_B>0$ in $\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$ and reference temperature $T_K>0$ in kelvins, the inverse temperature $\beta_K=(k_BT_K)^{-1}$ rescales orbitwise to $\beta_{\mathrm{loc}}:=N\beta_K$; defining $T_{\mathrm{loc}}:=(k_B\beta_{\mathrm{loc}})^{-1}$ gives $T_{\mathrm{loc}}=T_K/N$. No spatially varying lapse is treated as one global time-rescaled automorphism group.

[F2] [[lem-tgr-killing-thermal-normalization]]: under [A2] and its selected stationary-carrier hypotheses, its proof derives $\xi(N_\xi)=0$ from the Killing equation and metric compatibility; when the inverse-temperature vector $\beta$ is also Killing, the lemma gives $N_\xi T=T_K$ for a positive constant and $\beta=\xi/(k_BT_K)$. Its current item is draft and has a current ready Step 1 record, which is authoring readiness only.

### thm-tgr-massive-juttner-stationarity
Let $O$ be a connected open subset of a smooth time-oriented Lorentzian
four-manifold with metric-selected connection $\nabla$. Let $M_*>0$ be a
rest-energy, let $\beta$ be a smooth vector field with units
$\mathrm{J^{-1}}$, let $\zeta:O\to\mathbb R$ be smooth and dimensionless.
For each $x\in O$, let $\mathcal H^+_{M_*,x}$ be the future fiber shell of
[[def-tgr-future-mass-shell-measure]], and define the total future massive
shell over $O$ by
$$\mathscr H^+_{M_*,O}:=\{(x,P)\in TM:x\in O,\ g_x(P,P)=-M_*^2,\ P\text{ future-directed}\}=\bigcup_{x\in O}\{x\}\times\mathcal H^+_{M_*,x}.$$
Define on this total shell
$$f(x,P):=\exp\bigl(\zeta(x)+g_x(\beta(x),P)\bigr),$$
and let $\mathcal L$ be the geodesic Liouville vector field on $TM$; in
induced tangent-bundle coordinates $(x^a,P^a)$ it is
$$\mathcal L:=P^a\frac{\partial}{\partial x^a}-\Gamma^a{}_{bc}P^bP^c\frac{\partial}{\partial P^a}.$$
The geodesic Liouville vector field $\mathcal L$ is tangent to
$\mathscr H^+_{M_*,O}$, and
$$\mathcal Lf=0\text{ at every }(x,P)\in\mathscr H^+_{M_*,O} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \nabla_{(a}\beta_{b)}=0\text{ and }d\zeta=0\text{ on }O,$$
where $\nabla_{(a}\beta_{b)}=0$ is the covariant Killing condition
([[def-gr-killing-field]], [[thm-gr-killing-and-on-shell-matter-identities]]).
Since $O$ is connected, $d\zeta=0$ means $\zeta$ is constant on $O$.

For any vector $\beta_x\in T_xM$ with units $\mathrm{J^{-1}}$, define its
shell normalizer by
$$I_{M_*}(\beta_x):=\int_{\mathcal H^+_{M_*,x}}e^{g_x(\beta_x,P)}\,d\mu_{M_*,x}(P).$$
This integral is finite exactly when $\beta_x$ is future timelike. If so, with
$\alpha:=\sqrt{-g_x(\beta_x,\beta_x)}>0$, it equals
$4\pi M_*K_1(\alpha M_*)/\alpha$ as in
[[thm-tgr-juttner-partition-and-moments]]. In particular, for carrier data
$\beta=u/(k_BT)$, the choice
$$\zeta=\log\!\left(\frac{n h_{\rm ref}^3c^3}{Z_{M_*}(1/(k_BT))}\right)$$
normalizes the proper number density to $n$ and gives exactly the number
current and stress moments of
[[thm-tgr-juttner-partition-and-moments]]. This is a mathematical theorem about
the specified exponential ansatz; it does not assert that a material
thermalizes or that a physical equilibrium state exists.

**Given:** The smooth Lorentzian geometry and connection of
[[def-tgr-stationary-thermal-carriers]], a positive rest-energy $M_*$, smooth
fields $\beta,\zeta$, and the stated countable-choice assumption of the
Lebesgue and tetrad suppliers.

[F2] The future massive shell has measure $d^3q/E(q)$ in any future
orthonormal tetrad, independent of that tetrad
([[def-tgr-future-mass-shell-measure]],
[[lem-tgr-mass-shell-measure-tetrad-invariance]]).

[F3] If $A$ is symmetric and $b$ is a covector, then
$A(P,P)+b(P)=0$ on every future massive-shell vector implies $A=b=0$
([[lem-tgr-mass-shell-polynomial-rigidity]]).

[F4] The shell partition and moment theorem gives the exact invariant
normalizer when $\beta$ is future timelike, and the stated $Z_{M_*}$,
$J$ and $T$ formulas for $\beta=u/(k_BT)$
([[thm-tgr-juttner-partition-and-moments]]).

### thm-tgr-supported-static-heat-evolution
For this solution set $Q^0=0$, $Q^i=q^i/c$, $J=n_0u$, and
$$
\mathsf T^{ab}=\epsilon u^au^b+u^aQ^b+Q^au^b,\qquad f^b:=\nabla_a\mathsf T^{ab}.
$$
Here $Q=q/c$ has units $\mathrm{Pa}$, $\mathsf T$ has units $\mathrm{Pa}$, and $f$ has units $\mathrm{N\,m^{-3}}$. Then $\nabla_aJ^a=0$ and $u_bf^b=0$: $f$ is an explicitly defined external support that supplies no local power. The entropy current $S^a=su^a+Q^a/T$ has units $\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}m^{-3}}$ and total entropy $\mathcal S(t_K)=\int_\Omega s(t_K,x)\,dx$ in $\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$, with
$$
\frac{d\mathcal S}{dt_K}=A_0\int_\Omega\frac{|\nabla_x\vartheta|^2}{\vartheta^2}\,dx\ge0.
$$
The lapse-weighted energy $\mathcal E_K(t_K)=\int_\Omega N\epsilon\,dx$ is constant in $\mathrm{J}$. If $\overline\vartheta=|\Omega|^{-1}\int_\Omega\vartheta_0\,dx$ and $C_\Omega=\frac32(\max_iL_i)^2$, then
$$
\left\|\vartheta(t_K,\cdot)-\overline\vartheta\right\|_{L^2(\Omega)}\le \exp\!\left(-\frac{A_0t_K}{C_VC_\Omega}\right)\left\|\vartheta_0-\overline\vartheta\right\|_{L^2(\Omega)}.
$$
Thus $T$ converges in $L^2(\Omega)$ to $T_{\rm eq}=\overline\vartheta/N$. Since $N T_{\rm eq}=\overline\vartheta>0$, the inverse-temperature vector $\beta=u/(k_BT_{\rm eq})$, for $k_B>0$ in $\mathrm{J\,K^{-1}}$, is Killing by [[lem-tgr-killing-thermal-normalization]]. This is a mathematical solution of the specified fixed-background, supported first-order heat model; it asserts no Einstein equation, general dissipative-fluid initial-value theorem, or finite propagation speed.

[F3] [[def-tgr-stationary-thermal-carriers]]: for a selected future timelike Killing field $\xi$, $N_\xi=\sqrt{-g(\xi,\xi)}$ and $u=\xi/N_\xi$; for $k_B>0$, $\beta=u/(k_BT)$.

[F4] [[lem-tgr-killing-thermal-normalization]]: on a connected open domain with those stationary fields, $\beta$ is Killing exactly when $N_\xi T$ is a positive constant. The selected stationary field, connected domain and positive temperature required by that supplier are supplied here.
