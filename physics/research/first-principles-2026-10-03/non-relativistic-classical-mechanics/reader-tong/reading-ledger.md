## Completeness and limits

Every substantive chapter and subsection listed in the full contents was directly read. No separately linked problem sheets or expanded commercial textbook were read. No external proof cited by Tong (Arnold integrability, Wells/Siklos adiabatic invariance, Aref vortex review, Witten magnetic drift, Hannay/Berry originals) was read as part of this assignment. Their citation does not count as proof retrieval or completion. No experimental primary report was read; numerical observational anecdotes remain attributed secondary claims with incomplete uncertainty.
