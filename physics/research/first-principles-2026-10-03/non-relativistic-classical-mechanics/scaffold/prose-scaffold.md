## Adopted formulation, objects, and units come first

The principal formulation is Newtonian material point mechanics with fixed positive inertial masses and a chosen force model; ideal constrained systems and regular variational/Hamiltonian formulations are derived conditional branches. “First principles” means making assumptions explicit, not proving nature's laws from mathematics. In a Hamiltonian-first alternative H and the symplectic law would instead be primitive model data; the present primitive/derived labels are relative to the chosen Newtonian formulation.

Use SI units with independent dimensions length ℓ, duration τ, mass μ. Coordinates denote numerical components after a chosen metre, second and kilogram, never dimensionless physical equality by default. Angle and rotation matrices are dimensionless (radian convention). The dimension map multiplies under products, divides under quotients, and requires equal dimensions for addition. Generalized coordinates can carry different dimensions; individual generalized momenta have dimension action/[qᵃ], not invariably kg m/s. Every later quantity must inherit or state a dimension. No shared quantities category or units workflow is proposed.

**P1 Newtonian spacetime structure, an adopted physical model.** Spacetime is a four-dimensional affine space A modeled on V, absolute elapsed time τ0:V→R a surjective linear functional, spatial subspace K=ker τ0 with positive definite Euclidean metric g and fixed spatial orientation. The smooth structure is the affine one. Time intervals have seconds and spatial displacements metres. Events in one time slice differ by K; distances are defined within a slice. There is no Lorentzian metric, light cone, universal speed bound or preferred rest frame. See local M1 for exact constructions. Absolute simultaneity and Euclidean spatial geometry are physical assumptions, not conclusions of ODE uniqueness.

**P2 Inertial-frame availability and relativity.** An inertial frame means the global adapted affine chart determined by an origin o, a constant time direction u with τ0(u)=1, and an oriented g-orthonormal basis of K. Its coordinates are (t,x). The model assumes that free fixed-mass particles follow affine curves and that the allowed inertial frames are related by t′=t+b, x′=Rx+vt+a, R∈SO(3). This is a coordinate frame, not an observer congruence or a basis field with arbitrary time dependence. A congruence of stationary frame observers, if used, is the family t↦(t,x0); a spatial basis is the constant triple defining that chart. Boosts change velocity, preserve acceleration up to R. A specified force law must also transform appropriately to give covariance; an external apparatus or background potential can select a frame. Kinematic Galilean symmetry alone does not imply momentum conservation of every system.

**P3 Particle idealization and fixed inertial mass.** A particle label i carries a worldline γᵢ:I→A, I an open interval, γᵢ∈C² and τ0(γᵢ(t)−o)=t. This time condition replaces relativistic causal constraints. In a chosen inertial chart γᵢ=(t,xᵢ(t)), xᵢ:I→R³; velocity vᵢ=xᵢ′ has ℓ/τ and acceleration aᵢ=xᵢ″ has ℓ/τ². Primitive mᵢ>0 has μ. The point approximation ignores size/spin/internal structure only in a stated regime. Inertial mass is a parameter in the dynamical law; a calibration may estimate it under apparatus assumptions but does not constitute a mathematical definition of the law's truth.

For N labeled particles, configuration q=(x1,…,xN) belongs to an open Q⊂(R³)ᴺ. If singular pair forces are used, Q excludes coincidences xᵢ=xⱼ; excluded collisions are boundaries of the model domain. With regular holonomic constraints, Q is instead a smooth submanifold G⁻¹(0) with DG full rank. A smooth n-manifold means a Hausdorff second-countable space with compatible smooth charts to open subsets of Rⁿ and maximal smooth atlas. TqQ is the space of velocities of differentiable curves through q; TQ is their disjoint union with its chart-induced smooth structure. Tq*Q is its linear dual and T*Q their smooth cotangent bundle. These exact objects, not just “degrees of freedom,” support later derivatives. For holonomic systems the configuration degrees of freedom are dim Q; a nonholonomic velocity distribution has a separate rank and accessibility dimension, so state which notion is counted. Count constraints only where their differentials are independent.

**P4 Newtonian dynamics and force superposition.** The given force model is a map F:I×TQ→(R³)ᴺ for unconstrained particles, continuous and locally Lipschitz in (q,v), locally uniform in t; smoothness is imposed when differentiating F. Component Fᵢ(t,q,v) is a spatial vector with μℓ/τ². Adopt mᵢxᵢ″=Fᵢ and additive composition of stated force contributions. Force is primitive specified interaction data in this formulation, not an extra independent observed quantity magically known from x″. Local ODE existence/uniqueness is conditional on the hypotheses. It gives neither all-time prediction nor empirical truth. A globally bounded smooth force is still not a universal guarantee on an excluded domain; local M10 gives the correct compact-continuation criterion. Impacts, dry friction discontinuities and stochastic forcing need separate laws and solution notions.

**P5 Restricted interaction assumptions.** When used, write Fᵢ=Fᵢext+Σj≠i Fᵢⱼ with pairwise equal/opposite Fᵢⱼ=−Fⱼᵢ, and separately centrality Fᵢⱼ parallel xᵢ−xⱼ. These are optional model assumptions. A position-conservative model specifies a C² potential U:I×Q→R and Fᵢ=−∇xᵢU; U has energy dimension μℓ²/τ². Pair potentials uᵢⱼ(|xᵢ−xⱼ|) and external uᵢ(t,xᵢ) are important examples, not the most general conservative U. A closed one-form (zero exterior derivative) has a potential locally; global exactness requires topology or a separately given U. “Curl zero” alone on a punctured/multiply connected domain is insufficient. Gravitational κ or G is introduced only in its model: Uᵢⱼ=−Gmᵢg mⱼg/r, G dimension ℓ³/(μτ²), gravitational masses mg primitive coupling parameters, mg=mi an additional empirical assumption. Do not claim inverse square gravitation follows from Newton's second law.

**P6 Ideal constraint reactions.** A holonomic constraint is G(t,q)=0 with Gq full rank and smooth; its permitted virtual displacements are ker Gq at fixed t. An ideal reaction covector R annihilates this subspace. D'Alembert balance means (Mq″−F)·δq=0 for all such δq. This ideality is an adopted model of the mechanism, not a mathematical consequence of being constrained. Rheonomic means explicit time dependence; scleronomic means none. Moving ideal constraints can do work, R·q′=−λ·Gt. Nonholonomic means a stated velocity distribution A(t,q)q′+b=0 not representable locally by positional constraints; ordinary nonholonomic d'Alembert and stationary augmented-action (vakonomic) models differ. Rolling without slipping supplies contact kinematics plus ideality/friction assumptions. Rank failures or changing contact modes require separate treatment.

## Precise advanced definitions and proof boundaries

In a Lagrangian branch ℒ:I×TQ→R is at least C²; action is the finite-time integral. Stationary means zero derivative for every smooth fixed-endpoint variation. Euler–Lagrange equations are d/dt ℒvᵃ=ℒqᵃ. For ideal regular holonomic constraints the coordinate parametrization eliminates the reaction; nonideal friction must be a specified generalized force. Regular means nonsingular velocity Hessian; hyperregular means a globally smooth invertible Legendre map. Neither stationarity nor Hessian invertibility proves existence of an action minimum. Hamiltonian H may depend on time, and Ḣ=Ht along canonical solutions. Explicitly time-dependent dynamics need time-tagged extended states for the noncrossing uniqueness claim.

A differential k-form assigns at each point a smoothly varying alternating k-linear functional on tangent vectors. A two-form ω is symplectic if dω=0 and ω(z,·)=0 implies z=0. On T*Q, λ(q,p)(z)=p(Dπz), ω=−dλ=Σdqᵃ∧dpₐ; the library convention is ιXHω=dH. Thus q̇=Hp, ṗ=−Hq and {f,g}=ω(Xf,Xg)=Σ(fqᵃgpₐ−fpₐgqᵃ), ḟ={f,H}+ft. A canonical transformation is a local/global diffeomorphism pulling ω back to itself; a generating function represents it only where the chosen projection is nonsingular, with exactness/topology conditions. Symplectic flow preservation and volume preservation are local-in-time claims on the actual flow domain, not completeness. Phase volume is ωⁿ/n! (with chosen orientation). Recurrence needs an invariant finite measure and an appropriate measure-preserving evolution; full rigorous statement/proof is open here.

A first integral f is constant on solutions, equivalently {f,H}=0 for autonomous H. Involution means pairwise zero brackets. A completely integrable 2n-dimensional system requires n smooth first integrals whose differentials are independent on the regular region and which pairwise commute. An action variable has action dimension. Adopt period-one θ∈R/Z and I=∮p dq, giving ω=dθ∧dI in each action-angle pair and θ̇=∂H/∂I in cycles per second. Tong’s radian convention uses θT=2πθ and JT=I/(2π); this explicit rescaling preserves the form. Never confuse cyclic frequency with radian angular rate. The published Liouville–Arnold supplier assumes compact connected regular fibre plus a saturated proper submersion neighborhood with connected fibres, and countable choice; it does not make every energy level a torus or supply global action-angle coordinates. Hamilton–Jacobi S has action dimension, solves St+H(t,q,Sq)=0, and “complete integral” requires an n-parameter family with invertible mixed derivative, not just any solution.

Choose positive coordinate scales sₐ for length, velocity, momentum and any mixed coordinates and use the norm of the dimensionless components zₐ/sₐ; stability and numerical growth estimates always name these scales. For an autonomous C¹ vector field f on an open state domain, a flow Φt(z) solves ż=f(z) with its actual maximal domain. An equilibrium f(z0)=0 is Lyapunov stable when for every ε>0 an initial δ>0 keeps |Φt(z)−z0|<ε for all forward times of the prescribed solution; asymptotic stability additionally requires convergence for nearby data. The local linearization is δż=Df(Φt(z))δz. Finite-time growth is log(|DΦt(z)δz|/|δz|)/t in a specified norm; an infinite-time Lyapunov exponent requires existence or an explicitly chosen limsup. Coordinate/unit rescaling must be controlled. A Poincaré section is a submanifold transverse to f with a well-defined first-return map on its stated domain. “Chaos” must mean a precise property, e.g. a compact invariant set with a conjugacy to a symbolic shift plus specified sensitivity/transitivity; merely drawing irregular trajectories or finding local exponential growth is insufficient. A Hamiltonian elliptic linearization does not by itself prove nonlinear stability. KAM claims require their exact differentiability, nondegeneracy and Diophantine hypotheses (bounds |k·ω|≥γ|k|⁻ν for integer k≠0); E28 supplies a complete analytic nondegenerate KAM theorem with explicit strip/Diophantine/smallness conditions; no general merely-smooth or degenerate variant is inferred.

For a continuum, material labels X∈B⊂R³ and C² motion χ:I×B→R³ define spatial region Ωt=χt(B), with χt injective local diffeomorphism and J=det Dχt>0. Spatial velocity u(t,χt(X))=∂tχ(t,X). Density is a nonnegative mass-per-volume function and mass measure ρm dx. Stress is a matrix-valued field σ whose traction on oriented normal n is σn; body acceleration b is specified. Smooth local mass balance ∂tρm+div(ρmu)=0 and momentum balance ρm(∂tu+(u·∇)u)=divσ+ρmb require material integral assumptions and transport/localization mathematics. They are not inferred from atomic point mechanics without a limit argument. An elastic small-strain model uses displacement ξ, strain ε=(Dξ+Dξᵀ)/2 and σ=C:ε with specified stiffness tensor and symmetry/positivity; fluid models specify σ=−pId plus constitutive viscous terms. Pressure, incompressibility divu=0, boundary conditions and regularity are model assumptions. Shock/weak-solution notions and PDE well-posedness remain outside the closed local core.

## Evidence, review and cross-framework limits

Every postulate gets source/formulation/scope review, no invented Proof section. Every physics theorem and thought experiment needs a complete deduction, with mathematical, physical-assumption and physical-result dependency roles distinguished. A thought experiment assumes a hypothetical apparatus or outcome and supplies no observations. An actual experiment requires retrieval and reading of a primary report, documented preparation, apparatus calibration, measured data, uncertainties, sampling/selection model, systematic-error treatment and qualified comparison model. The four course/text sources here do not close that requirement. No measurement counts, precision, p-values or effect size are invented.

A measurement model may write Yk=h(z(tk),θapparatus)+εk, where h is a stated response map, θapparatus calibrated nuisance parameters and ε a stated stochastic/error model. Independence, distributions and moments must be justified or adopted explicitly. Mathematical probability/estimation interfaces need exact suppliers before any quantitative inference. Finite observations constrain specified laws plus apparatus assumptions; they cannot prove Newtonian spacetime, all force laws or all classical mechanics. Reviewer confidence is not a physical uncertainty estimate. Discrepancy may expose model, calibration, preparation or analysis error. Simulated trajectories and numerical fits are labeled computations/proposals.

Author/judge/adjudicator records must separately assess (1) source fidelity, (2) mathematical validity and dependency closure, (3) applicability of the adopted model, and (4) empirical strength. A valid conditional calculation can be inapplicable; a faithful textbook paraphrase can contain an invalid overstatement. Conservation conditions, approximation remainders and empirical qualifications travel downstream.

Relativity is a later bridge requiring Lorentzian spacetime, changed mass/momentum laws and an explicit small-speed/weak-field limit, not a premise of CM01. Electromagnetic force coupling is a later physical assumption linking E,B and charge from an independently developed EM framework; particle mechanical momentum alone need not be conserved and no field momentum theorem is imported here. Quantum correspondence requires an explicit quantization/semiclassical construction and domain/limit hypotheses; replacing p by an operator is not a derivation. Thermodynamic/statistical equilibrium and entropy are later bridges requiring ensembles/coarse graining/limits, not consequences of deterministic Hamiltonian evolution. Singular Lagrangians and Dirac constraint theory, general relativity, relativistic gravitation and quantum field theory are deferred beyond this design.
