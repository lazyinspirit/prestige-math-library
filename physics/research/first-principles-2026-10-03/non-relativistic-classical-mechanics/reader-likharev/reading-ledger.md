# Complete main-volume reading ledger: Likharev

Read source: *Essential Graduate Physics, Part CM: Classical Mechanics*, Konstantin K. Likharev, title-page last edit September 1, 2026. Original, extraction, metadata, and selected PDF renders are retained in this directory. Exact equation/section numbers below refer to this version. PDF page numbers are one-based; chapter pages restart at 1.

Completion means every main-volume page was read, including footnotes and exercise statements, in consecutive bounded chunks. It does not mean every exercise was solved or every proof independently audited. The first large extraction outputs were truncated in their middles; PDF 9–13 and 20–25 were explicitly reread in smaller chunks. All later chunks were returned directly as text. Separate exercise solutions and shared EGP supplements are outside the read claim. The merged volume has no substantive appendix.
