## Chapter-level substantive ledger

- **Introduction, pp1–5:** Poisson gravity motivates but does not uniquely imply GR. The stress tensor argument is heuristic; mass/energy density cannot be identified solely by Lorentz transformation counting. Rs/r is a model-specific weak-field scale, not the only possible expansion parameter. No empirical numbers independently validated here.
- **Chapter 1, pp6–48:** Varying fixed-endpoint smooth actions gives affine geodesics only after proper-time/affine choice; the square-root action otherwise gives an extra tangent term. Timelike normalization does not choose the future component. The null calculation cannot use proper time. Schwarzschild is introduced before derivation as a fixed external background; E and l are specific conserved quantities, not generic GR energy/angular momentum. Orbital and bending calculations are perturbative, neglect particle backreaction and other planets; the ring calculation explicitly fails precision needed to test GR. Local free-fall does not remove tides. Reported clock/redshift/eclipses remain qualitative textbook summaries requiring primary experiment sources.
- **Chapter 2, pp49–90:** Derivations, dual spaces, mixed tensors, Lie derivatives and exterior calculus provide prerequisite types. Flows need completeness for all-real parameters; the source expressly discusses finite-time blowup. Exact versus closed forms is a global distinction. Integration requires orientation and suitable support/convergence, and Stokes is not proved. Manifold/submanifold definitions and cohomology assertions need stronger hypotheses before use (details in report).
- **Chapter 3, pp91–140:** A smooth nondegenerate Lorentzian metric gives causal cones pointwise; time orientation is extra structure. Metric compatibility and zero torsion yield a unique Levi-Civita connection through Koszul's formula. Connection coefficients transform inhomogeneously; curvature and torsion are tensors. Parallel transport follows an ODE and generally depends on path. Normal coordinates eliminate first derivatives at a point, not Riemann curvature. Geodesic deviation assumes a smooth commuting variation of affine geodesics. Bianchi contractions give vanishing divergence, not a parallel Einstein tensor. Tetrads are generally noncoordinate bases with local Lorentz redundancy. Hodge results belong to compact Riemannian, suitably boundaryless settings, not general Lorentzian spacetime. Maxwell coupling is a separate adopted model.
- **Chapter 4, pp141–204:** Einstein-Hilbert is a simplest chosen action, not forced by covariance alone; Λ and higher-curvature terms illustrate nonuniqueness. Metric variation determines field equations conditional on the adopted action and admissible boundary variations. Matter stress is a metric functional derivative; its divergence requires matter equations as well as diffeomorphism invariance, not invariance alone with all matter held arbitrary. Killing charges and boundary conditions permit conserved scalar integrals; covariant divergence of a rank-two tensor does not. de Sitter lacks a global future timelike Killing field; AdS needs boundary data. Perfect fluids/equations of state and FRW homogeneity/isotropy are model assumptions. Energy conditions are additional inequalities and not universally valid. Spinor and semiclassical passages require mathematics/quantum inputs beyond classical GR.
- **Chapter 5, pp205–232:** Linearize in a declared background/gauge with derivative and source scales controlled. de Donder gauge and residual gauge lead to two TT polarizations, null wavevectors, and a tidal detector effect. Newtonian reduction needs stationary slow matter, small stresses, boundary conditions and potential; spatial metric comparison requires isotropic coordinates. Radiation additionally requires compact/localized source, nonrelativistic internal motion, radiation-zone separation, retarded/no-incoming choice and small wave amplitude. Quadrupole coefficient is quoted after dimensional reasoning, not fully calculated. Pseudotensor/averaged energy is approximation-dependent. Black-hole merger estimates extrapolate a slow-motion formula outside its controlled regime and do not justify merger-waveform predictions.
- **Chapter 6, pp233–283:** Exterior Schwarzschild, EF, Kruskal and collapse geometries are distinct global objects. Horizons are global boundaries relative to a chosen asymptotic end; curvature divergence and geodesic incompleteness must be distinguished from coordinate failure. Birkhoff proof is a sketch, uniqueness/no-hair depend on assumptions and are not generic evolution convergence results. RN/Kerr ideal extensions cross Cauchy horizons; instability/censorship discussions are conditional or conjectural. Extremal near-horizon geometry differs from nonextremal Rindler. Penrose and superradiance use stationary Killing energy and ideal test processes; area/entropy statements need extra theorem and quantum/thermodynamic assumptions. Scalar no-hair sketch uses a timelike Killing field and horizon-normal condition not valid as written throughout rotating ergoregions.
