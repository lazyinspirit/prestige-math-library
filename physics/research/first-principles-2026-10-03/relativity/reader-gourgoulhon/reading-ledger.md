# Complete reading ledger: Reall replacement

The directory name records the original assignment; the source actually read is Harvey Reall, *Mathematical Tripos Part III General Relativity*, footer version 12 October 2022. This is an authorized complete-course replacement, not Gourgoulhon's SR book. All 125 PDF pages were read in order through sixteen bounded text chunks, including the non-examinable portions. Printed page n is PDF page n+1 (cover is PDF page 1). There are ten chapters, a preface, conventions and bibliography; no appendices. Exercise statements were read, but solving every exercise and auditing every cited theorem was not part of this source-reading pass.

Text extraction used PyMuPDF `get_text(sort=True)`; `sources/reall-part3-gr.txt` preserves all pages with PDF-page markers. The sixteen `sources/chunk-*.txt` files preserve the actual bounded reading ranges. PDF extraction sometimes scrambles tensor indices, radicals and line breaks. Ten selected formula-bearing pages were rendered and inspected visually; the list appears below. Their interpretation does not amount to a whole-source proof audit.
