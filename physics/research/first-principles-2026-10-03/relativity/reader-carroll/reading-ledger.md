## Chapter-level completion and audit notes

1. **Special Relativity and Flat Spacetime**, pp.1–30, PDF8–37: complete. Flat metric and prescribed clock/momentum laws are physical assumptions, not deductions from linear algebra. EM force/conservation require Maxwell plus matter dynamics. General EM fields are not perfect fluids.
2. **Manifolds**, pp.31–54, PDF38–61: complete. Add Hausdorff/second-countability; specify smooth atlas and nondegenerate metric. Distinguish orientation from time orientation; RNC is local. Stokes and inverse-function results are named prerequisites rather than supplied complete proofs.
3. **Curvature**, pp.55–96, PDF62–103: complete. Connection independent of metric until torsion-free compatibility; geodesics/transport need ODE existence. Flatness-to-coordinate argument has global holonomy caveat; maximum-proper-time argument incomplete. Tetrads are patchwise; gauge bundle comparison does not supply quantum laws.
4. **Gravitation**, pp.97–128, PDF104–135: complete. EEP convention explicitly includes gravitational laws; Einstein equations are adopted dynamics. Newtonian limit has weak/static/slow and negligible-stress hypotheses. Hilbert boundary conditions and matter closure need strengthening. Cauchy discussion is motivational, not a well-posedness proof.
5. **More Geometry**, pp.129–141, PDF136–148: complete. General vector pushforward is a vector along a map, not necessarily a field on the target; complete flows require completeness. On-shell diffeomorphism invariance supports local divergence identity. Killing scalar conservation has an actual short derivation.
6. **Weak Fields and Gravitational Radiation**, pp.142–163, PDF149–170: complete. Keep gauge, isolated source, retardation, slow-source, far-zone and order counting explicit. Retarded Green function/quadrupole power are quoted rather than fully derived. Binary-pulsar account is qualitative secondary evidence.
7. **The Schwarzschild Solution and Black Holes**, pp.164–216, PDF171–223: complete. Birkhoff sketch is local and ansatz-dependent; global causal completion and realistic collapse differ. Several printed equation defects confirmed visually. No-hair, quantum evaporation and broad area/singularity claims need more than this text.
8. **Cosmology**, pp.217–231, PDF224–238: complete. RW symmetry is a model assumption; EOS is separate input. Redshift/luminosity relations require comoving emitter/observer, geometric optics and transparent propagation. Global topology and general singularity assertions overreach. Historical numerical cosmology is not a current empirical result.
