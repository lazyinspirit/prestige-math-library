book or lecture-note treatment, with chapter/section reading records, exact

**Source rigor is qualified.** These are authoritative physics and mathematical
treatments whose rigor varies. Several use informal steps, omit hypotheses or
contain errors; the reports identify those limitations. Complete reading is not
an independent proof audit, and an authoritative citation alone cannot close a
mathematical prerequisite. Direct published supplier audits distinguish inspected
arguments from inherited transitive interfaces and pinned-byte checks.

All scaffolds begin with explicit framework assumptions, quantity definitions,
mathematical objects, units and conventions. They map published mathematics,
develop local arguments, and preserve exact source and proof statuses.
They separate conditional deductions from uncertain empirical observations.
The initial design teams did not supply primary experimental reports. The audit
phase has since retrieved and read Bach's electron double-slit report and
historical Cavendish, Faraday and Hertz accounts. Their qualified experimental
items separate reported observations from predictions and inference, disclose
missing uncertainty information, and exclude corrupted numerical OCR. Other
proposed experiments still require their own primary reports and analysis.
