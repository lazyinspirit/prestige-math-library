## Explicit definitions and assumptions needed before the first physics page

This is a proposed authoring specification inferred from the reading, not a statement that the notes already supply all of it.

**Nonrelativistic coordinate presentation.** Use a chosen Cartesian Euclidean spatial frame with an oriented orthonormal basis, an open spatial region Ω⊂R³ and time interval I⊂R. Define fields E,B:Ω×I→R³ and sources ρ:Ω×I→R, j:Ω×I→R³. For first differential identities assume E,B∈C² and ρ,j∈C¹, or give the exact weaker hypotheses used. This regular classical sector excludes point-charge worldlines and charged/current-carrying interfaces. Define a worldline γ:I→R×R³, γ(t)=(t,r(t)), with r∈C² when acceleration is used; the chart and adopted time structure determine t. This is a coordinate presentation of EM, not a claim that full Maxwell theory has Galilean spacetime symmetry.

For discontinuities, define piecewise regular fields and one-sided traces across an oriented C² interface with surface charge/current. For point sources, use distributions on Ω×I: ρ(t)=Σqₐδ_{rₐ(t)}, j(t)=Σqₐṙₐ(t)δ_{rₐ(t)}. State tests φ∈C_c^∞, distribution derivative convention and weak Maxwell equations. Maxwell itself is linear in fields/sources, but products needed for energy density or Lorentz self-force are not generally defined for arbitrary distributions. The singular-sector source formula does not license multiplying two singular distributions.

**Relativistic geometry.** Introduce Minkowski affine spacetime M modeled on R⁴ with metric η of signature (−+++), spacetime orientation and time orientation, x⁰=ct. An inertial frame can mean an affine global chart whose coordinate basis is oriented Lorentz orthonormal, with e₀ future directed; define that object explicitly. An observer's worldline is a C² curve γ:J→M with future-directed timelike tangent (η(γ̇,γ̇)<0); proper time parametrization imposes η(dγ/dτ,dγ/dτ)=−c². Observer congruences and tetrad fields are alternative defined objects, not synonyms. Define the electromagnetic field as a two-form F or its specified antisymmetric tensor, and current as a vector or its Hodge-dual three-form; E and B are observer-dependent components. Pin signs rather than mixing conventions across sources. Beisert uses F₀i=Eᵢ/c, Fᵢⱼ=−εᵢⱼₖBₖ, covariant Aμ=(−Φ/c,A), and Fμν=−∂μAν+∂νAμ (because his two-form writes reversed basis order). Its lower-index current Jμ=(−cρ,j) is consistent with an upper-index Jμ=(cρ,j).

**Units and primitive/derived status.** Adopt rationalized SI consistently: q in C; ρ in C/m³; j in A/m²; E in V/m (N/C); B in T; Φ in V; A in T·m; D and P in C/m²; H and M in A/m; u in J/m³; S in W/m²; field momentum density in kg/(m²·s); stress in Pa. ε0 has F/m units, μ0 H/m, c m/s; ε0μ0=c⁻² for the adopted vacuum normalization. Charge/source strengths and material parameters are inputs in this formulation. c is exact by SI definition; μ0 is experimentally determined in modern SI, and the notes' approximate uncertainty is not a current metrology determination. Derive field energy under declared assumptions. Particle rest mass m is a separate mechanics parameter; Maxwell theory does not generally derive it. Distinguish circuit resistance Ω from material resistivity Ω·m and conductivity S/m.

**Physical assumptions.** Adopt Maxwell vacuum equations, no magnetic monopole sources, Lorentz force for a test/prescribed charge, source conservation compatibility, and the selected causal/outgoing solution rule as separate premises. Constitutive relations, ideal conductors, quasistatic circuits and prescribed source trajectories are later restricted assumptions. Coulomb and Biot–Savart follow within suitable static/boundary cases of this adopted dynamical model; historical empirical motivations are not mathematical proofs of postulates.

**Evidence.** This source provides equations and teaching narrative, not primary experiment data. It does not furnish uncertainties/calibration/sampling models for Coulomb, Faraday, light speed, reflection, scattering or diffraction experiments. A separate complete experimental report is needed for an `experiment`. Keep predicted fluxes/intensities separate from finite observed detector readings; define averaging time, resolution, calibration and apparatus response. Neither observed interference nor a finite dataset proves the framework. This reading invented no observations or statistical precision.

## Checked source defects and mathematically important restrictions

These are independent reading findings, not edits to the source or a whole-text refutation. The official TeX confirms the cited literal defects.

1. **Half-space sign:** §2.3 printed p2.9/PDF31 (2.33), `Chap02.tex:760`, has +x₃/(2π|x−y|³) for outward nᵧ·∇ᵧG on H={x₃>0}. Outward nᵧ=−e₃ gives the negative sign. Its subsequent integral −1 and (2.34) negative delta agree with the corrected sign. Do not copy the literal sign.
2. **μ0 exponent:** §4.1 p4.2/PDF52, `Chap04.tex:136`, prints 4π·10⁻¹⁰ kg·m/(A²·s²). The former exact SI value was 4π·10⁻⁷ in these units. The footnote correctly states that since 2019 μ0 is measured, but retains an obsolete approximate precision rather than a current source. The report makes no new measured value claim.
3. **Material energy:** §5.2 p5.5/PDF65 fn3, `Chap05.tex:428–429`, omits 1/2 in ∫E·D for its linear nondispersive model; displayed capacitor energy (5.17) contains the correct 1/2. General material energy requires integrating the constitutive work and does not universally equal a bilinear expression. §5.2 p5.4/PDF64 fn1's split E_free=ε0D, E_dipole=−ε0P is dimensionally wrong: the algebraic identity would use D/ε0 and −P/ε0, and should not be confused with independently determined physical source fields.
4. **Retarded root:** §11.5 pp11.15–11.16/PDF135–136 asserts a global unique solution to |x−y(s)|=c(t−s) from an all-time trajectory and pointwise |ẏ|<c. Uniqueness of an existing root follows from f′=c−n·ẏ>0; existence does not. Let a>0 and y(s)=(√(c²s²+a²),0,0), s∈R. This is smooth with |ẏ(s)|<c for every s. For event (x,t)=(0,0), a retarded s≤0 would require √(c²s²+a²)=−cs, impossible because a²>0. Thus no root exists. One sufficient repair is uniform |ẏ|≤v*<c on the infinite past: f(s)=|x−y(s)|−ct+cs tends to −∞ as s→−∞ and f(t)≥0, giving a root by continuity (exclude coincidence when using r⁻¹); f′>0 gives uniqueness. Alternatively explicitly assume intersection with the observer's past light cone. The example demonstrates a mathematical failure, not an empirical finding.
5. **Group velocity:** §10.5 p10.11/PDF119 and §12.1 p12.2/PDF143 assert vg≤c in general dispersive media and identify vg with information speed. A general derivative dω/dk has no such bound from Maxwell alone; require a declared dispersion/absorption regime. Front velocity and energy transport require separate definitions. The source's ideal guide calculation §13.2 p13.10 does establish vg=c²k/ω≤c for ω²=c²(k²+λ), λ≥0; retain that restricted result.
6. **Conducting-wave root and terminology:** §13.1 p13.1/PDF153 calls σ specific resistance despite j=σE, which makes σ conductivity. p13.2/PDF154 (13.6), `Chap13.tex:114–115`, has an extra i before √(k²/(εμ)−σ²/(4ε²)). Solving its own (13.5) gives ω=−iσ/(2ε)±√(k²/(εμ)−σ²/(4ε²)); the literal version contradicts its following real-part discussion.
7. **Circular guide:** §13.2 p13.9/PDF161, `Chap13.tex:952–953`, calls TE values inflection points (“Wendepunkte”) of J_m. The stated Neumann boundary condition requires J_m′=0, stationary points, not J_m″=0. Claims about irrationality of all zeros are not proved by this source and are unnecessary for mode construction.
8. **PDE proof precision:** §11.1 pp11.1–11.2's infinitesimal stepping and energy discussion are heuristic existence reasoning. Energy uniqueness is useful when the energy identity is established in the declared solution class; finite L² energy alone gives no uniform high-derivative bound. §13.2 p13.6/PDF158 (13.26), confirmed in `Chap13.tex:531`, omits a conjugate in its complex volume term Re(E·ΔE); it should use Re(E*·ΔE). Even with that correction the real-part argument alone establishes nonnegative Re λ; use the full sesquilinear self-adjoint identity to establish Im λ=0. Add that mathematics rather than claiming the abbreviated argument is already a complete proof.

Other equations are not certified defect-free by this reading. For example, macroscopic energy/momentum statements and perturbative scattering need their own proof and convention reviews. Source-backed status is not mathematical acceptance.

## Proposed A/B order and mathematical closure

A pages should carry all load-bearing definitions/postulates/theorems. B pages are illustrative leaves; do not hide a needed supplier on a B page. The following is a proposed logical ordering, not a modification of the existing plan. One need not copy the historical order: introducing Maxwell early makes assumptions versus deductions clearest.

Avoid requiring the whole relativity chapter merely to define an elementary nonrelativistic coordinate worldline.

**Mathematical closure candidates:** these need matching to actual imported suppliers or local mathematics development; this report does not claim existing IDs or coverage.

- Differential/vector calculus with precise oriented divergence/Stokes and moving-surface transport hypotheses; distribution derivatives, convolution, weak limits and singular fundamental solutions; surface/line source distributions and jump conditions.
