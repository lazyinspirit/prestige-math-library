# Chapter reading ledger

Source base is <https://farside.ph.utexas.edu/teaching/em/lectures/>. Exact locators below are filenames relative to that base, plus printed equation numbers. Every corresponding HTML is in `html/`; exact full textual extraction is in `text/`. `completeness.json` lists every subsection title/node, not just chapter summaries.

**Coverage:** all 134 nodes read: substantive text nodes1–133, metadata node134. Introductory/subsection-list nodes are explicitly included. The sequential reading used node1–15,16–25 (the output-limited node21 ending and nodes22–23 were separately reread),26–29,30–33,34–39 (output-limited node37 and node36 ending separately reread), then `chunks/01.txt` through `chunks/26.txt`, covering node40–134. Entire text was considered, including historical commentary and material models, not only table of contents or selected core sections. Long equation ALT truncations were subsequently recovered by visually reading **all 198 listed occurrences, 196 unique images**, in `visual/01.png`–`14.png`. The exact occurrence index is `truncated-formulas.json`; row labels in each contact sheet give node and PNG filename. These images retain the actual complete rendered equation even where ALT is incomplete.

This is a research reading with identified issues, **not independent proof certification**. Diagrams are preserved; geometry was read from explanatory prose rather than every diagram visually inspected. No exercises/chapters were inferred from another edition. No printed-book reading is claimed.
