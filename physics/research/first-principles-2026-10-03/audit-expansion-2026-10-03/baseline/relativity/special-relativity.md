# Special relativity: first-principles proposed scaffold

Research proposal, 2026-10-03. Separate flat-spacetime classical framework; no gravitational field equations, quantum dynamics or quantum-gravity reconciliation. Supplier ordering begins here, before GR. Mathematical claims are conditional on explicit mathematical hypotheses; physical assumptions are adopted, not proved by experiment.

## Postulates, primitives and units before derived claims

Adopt affine Minkowski spacetime `(A,V,η,future)` as defined in M01. Events are points of A; displacements are vectors in V, with Lorentz metric η of signature (-+++). The future cone is supplied, not inferred from an arbitrary chart. This deliberately adopts geometric structure; a derivation from Einstein's two verbal postulates would additionally need exact homogeneity, isotropy, affine-map and operational hypotheses. Adopt relativity of inertial descriptions: equations of the selected model are covariant under proper orthochronous affine Lorentz maps. Adopt ideal clock behavior: elapsed clock time on a specified future timelike worldline is its metric proper-time integral; the clock hypothesis excludes acceleration-dependent corrections for the ideal model. Adopt ideal vacuum light rays as future null straight lines, with propagation speed c in inertial orthonormal coordinates. Adopt free massive particles as straight future timelike lines; interactions and their conservation rules require additional postulates. These assumptions define the model's scope; no claim derives the physical value of c from mathematics.

Primitive mathematical structure: A,V,η,time orientation. Primitive physical interpretation/parameters in this formulation: events, ideal clock and light interpretation, positive invariant mass parameter m for a massive species, interaction/collision conservation assumptions, and c. Derived: proper time, observer-relative duration/spatial distance, four-velocity, momentum after adopting p=mu, observer-relative energy, kinetic energy, acceleration and Doppler factors. A primitive physical parameter need not correspond to an SI base quantity. No electromagnetic charge or force law is silently imported.

Use SI with c retained, coordinates x^0=ct and x^i in metres. Metric components η_ab are dimensionless, ds² has m², τ seconds, u=dx/dτ has m/s, four-acceleration m/s², p=mu has kg m/s, energy joules. If coordinates instead use t in seconds, the metric component g_tt=-c² has m²/s² and mixed/time components change dimensions; formulas cannot be copied unchanged. The bilinear metric pairs displacement vectors to length squared and normalized observer vectors n=u_obs/c to -1. Natural units may be used only with an explicit restoration table for c. SI does not make a quantity primitive in this theory.

## Precise objects

A chart is a coordinate map from an open domain to an open subset of R4. An inertial frame here is an origin o and oriented future orthonormal basis E=(e0,e1,e2,e3), yielding a global affine chart; its stationary observers are lines o+sum x^i e_i+λe0. A coordinate chart, even on flat spacetime, can be accelerated/curvilinear and need not be inertial. A tetrad at an event is an ordered orthonormal basis of its tangent space; a tetrad field supplies such bases smoothly on a domain. An observer congruence is a smooth future timelike unit vector field n with η(n,n)=-1 on an open domain, whose integral curves represent observers; it need not have global synchronization or arise from a global inertial chart. An adapted chart has congruence flow lines at fixed spatial coordinates where such a chart exists. No interchangeability is assumed among these objects.

A massive worldline is a regular piecewise C1 map x:I→A from a real interval, future timelike on each smooth piece; a C2 curve is required for acceleration. Null light rays instead have nonzero future null tangents and cannot be assigned proper-time four-velocity. Timelike means η(v,v)<0, null means η(v,v)=0 for v≠0, spacelike means η(v,v)>0; causal means timelike or null. Proper time is c^-1 integral sqrt(-η(dx/dλ,dx/dλ))dλ, parametrization invariant under increasing changes. Four-velocity u=dx/dτ obeys η(u,u)=-c²; a=du/dτ obeys η(u,a)=0. Observers measure energy E_n=-cη(p,n), spatial momentum q_n=p+η(p,n)n, spatial projections and simultaneity hyperplanes η(n,q-p)=0. Spacelike intervals are not elapsed clock time.

A type (r,s) tensor is a multilinear map (V*)^r×V^s→R; components transform by the corresponding basis/Jacobian laws, not as independent scalar functions. Define selected matter fields as smooth sections of these tensor spaces over an explicit open domain; singular charges or sources are excluded unless a separate weak/distributional formulation is supplied. Four-force f=dp/dτ is not a force law. For a massive particle p=mu, E_n²=m²c⁴+c²|q_n|². For an ideal massless particle introduce nonzero future null momentum as a separate primitive and its scale by observer-measured energy; do not put m=0 into mu. Stress-energy T is a symmetric rank-(2,0) field, with tetrad components in J/m³; its selection requires an actual matter model. Conservation ∂_aT^{ab}=0 is a physical/model premise until derived from a specified action and its on-shell equations. Integrated total momentum additionally requires integrability and controlled boundary flux.


Additional explicit model vocabulary for later pages: an ideal plane-wave phase is a dimensionless scalar $\theta(x)=k_ax^a+\theta_0$, with constant nonzero covector $k$, $\eta^{ab}k_ak_b=0$ and future $k^\sharp$. For an observer $n$, angular frequency is $\omega_n=-c\,k(n)>0$ (s$^{-1}$); linear frequency is $\omega_n/(2\pi)$. Lorentz transforming k yields Doppler/aberration conditional on this wave and receiver model. Identifying energy with $\hbar\omega$ would add quantum assumptions and is excluded. A precise uniform-acceleration example has $(x^0,x^1)=(c^2/\alpha)(\sinh(\alpha\tau/c),\cosh(\alpha\tau/c))$, $\alpha>0$, with transverse coordinates fixed. Direct differentiation gives $\eta(u,u)=-c^2$, $\eta(a,a)=\alpha^2$. Rindler coordinates $x^0=\rho\sinh\theta$, $x^1=\rho\cosh\theta$ are a chart only on $\rho>0$ onto $x^1>|x^0|$; the pullback metric is $-\rho^2d\theta^2+d\rho^2+dy^2+dz^2$. Coordinate $\theta$ is dimensionless and each stationary observer has $d\tau=\rho d\theta/c$, acceleration $c^2/\rho$. Bell's setup uses two timelike trajectories with $x_2(t)=x_1(t)+L$ in one inertial chart; it is not a Born-rigid congruence. Born rigidity means the projected symmetric velocity-gradient tensor vanishes, $h_a{}^ch_b{}^d\partial_{(c}n_{d)}=0$, on a specified smooth congruence. A fracture prediction additionally requires an elastic model and threshold; source kinematics alone does not supply it. These new local constructions still need exact canonical supplier placement and independent review.

## Bridges and empirical boundary

Flat spacetime is a GR model only when the manifold/metric/global identification, compatible matter content and field equations are supplied: Minkowski vacuum satisfies Einstein with Λ=0 and T=0. A general flat Lorentz manifold may have nontrivial topology and no global inertial chart. Conversely a local tangent Minkowski metric does not erase curvature or extend local clocks/synchronization to global SR. Classical mechanics correspondence requires low-speed expansions, specific identifications of particle/time variables and interaction laws; matching units does not prove equivalence. No quantum momentum/operator or quantum-gravity reconciliation is introduced.

Historical ether experiments, particle lifetimes, accelerator kinematics and clock transport are primary-report search destinations, not experiment items. Actual experiment authoring requires apparatus/preparation, calibration, sampling/error model, systematics, statistics, measured values and source limits; textbook descriptions provide contextual motivation only. Observations test the combined model and apparatus assumptions. Finite agreement does not prove postulates. Twin, signal and Bell scenarios supply conditional deductions and no new empirical observations.
