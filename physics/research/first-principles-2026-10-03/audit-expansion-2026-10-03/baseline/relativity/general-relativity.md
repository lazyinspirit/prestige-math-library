# General relativity: first-principles proposed scaffold

Research proposal, 2026-10-03; distinct classical gravitational framework. Build SR first to establish the precise flat comparison, then rebuild the assumptions in GR. No claim reconciles GR with quantum mechanics or proves physical postulates.

## Postulates, primitives and units

Adopt a smooth Hausdorff second-countable four-manifold M without boundary, a smooth Lorentz metric g of signature (-+++), and a time orientation specified by a smooth everywhere timelike vector field (or compatible choices of future cone). This is a global additional condition; a Lorentz metric alone need not be time orientable. Use the unique torsion-free metric-compatible connection from M05, not an independently varied torsionful connection. Assume the selected model's nongravitational fields/matter and their explicit equations/couplings; diffeomorphism covariance alone does not select those equations. Adopt ideal freely falling massive test bodies as future timelike geodesics and ideal geometric-optics light rays as null geodesics in the specified test-field approximation. Adopt ideal clocks measuring metric proper time locally along their worldlines. These test assumptions exclude unmodeled self-force, finite-size/tidal and dispersion corrections.

Adopt Einstein's field equation G_ab+Λg_ab=(8πG/c⁴)T_ab on the specified smooth domain. This is a postulate, not a theorem of the equivalence principle. Primitive inputs here are differentiable spacetime, metric/future orientation, clock/matter interpretation, chosen matter fields and physical parameters c,G,Λ (unless Λ is fixed to zero for a restricted branch). Derived: compatible connection, curvature, proper time, geodesics, observer measurements and derived stress-energy only after supplying a matter action/constitutive model. Specifying T by hand as a source is another restricted model and needs consistency constraints. G is a physical coupling, not fixed by mathematical symmetry. Equivalence of action and field equations requires the complete compact-variation mathematics M12/M22; it is not the derivation of why nature uses that action.

SI conventions: x^0=ct and x^i have metres, g_ab dimensionless, ds² m², τ seconds, u m/s, Γ components m^-1, Riemann/Ricci/scalar components m^-2, Λ m^-2, stress-energy tetrad components J/m³, G m³/(kg s²), c m/s. Coordinates may instead include angles or seconds; component units then change with Jacobians and cannot be assigned a universal scalar dimension. If x^0=t, g_tt carries m²/s². Tensor invariants, proper time and observer-relative energy retain their physical meanings. Einstein–Hilbert normalization with length coordinates is c³/(16πG); define matter variation by δS_m=-(1/2c)∫T_ab δg^{ab}sqrt(|det g|)d⁴x to recover c^-4 field coupling. Natural-unit sources must have c restored explicitly.

## Objects before their use

A smooth manifold is a Hausdorff second-countable locally Euclidean space with a maximal smooth atlas; chart maps take open domains to open subsets of R4 with smooth overlap transitions. T_pM is the real vector space of derivations on smooth function germs at p. A smooth tensor of type (r,s) is a smooth section with fibres the multilinear maps (T_p*M)^r×(T_pM)^s→R. Covectors, vectors and g are type (0,1), (1,0), (0,2). g_p is symmetric/nondegenerate with one negative direction at each p; inverse g^{ab} raises indices and contractions are basis independent. Imported bundle interfaces propagate AC_omega as recorded in mathematics-audit.md.

A chart is not an observer. A local tetrad is four smooth independent fields e_A on an open set with g(e_A,e_B)=diag(-1,1,1,1)_AB; the existence of a global tetrad is not assumed. An observer congruence is a smooth future unit timelike field n; its integral curves are observers, with physical four-velocity cn. An adapted local chart has these flow lines at fixed spatial coordinates. A freely falling observer is an individual timelike geodesic; it is not a global inertial reference frame. Normal coordinates have g(p)=η and first derivatives zero at p; higher derivatives contain curvature and finite neighborhoods are not generally Minkowski. Observers measure spatial vectors using h(v)=v+g(n,v)n, energy E_n=-cg(p,n), and matter energy density T_ab n^a n^b in a normalized tetrad.

A regular worldline is a piecewise C1 map γ:I→M with nonzero future timelike tangents; a C2 smooth piece is needed for acceleration. Timelike/null/spacelike are classified pointwise by g(v,v), with v≠0 required for null. Proper time is c^-1∫sqrt(-g(γ',γ'))dλ. A geodesic has Dγ'/dλ=0 in affine parameter; proper time is affine for massive geodesics, whereas null curves require another affine parameter. Local existence is not extension/completeness. Causal paths join events along future causal tangent directions; I+(p) and J+(p) are future timelike and future causal reachability sets. A Cauchy hypersurface meets every inextendible causal curve exactly once; proving existence or a spacetime splitting requires global hypotheses. Time orientation alone permits causal pathologies.

The connection ∇ satisfies function-linearity in its first field, real linearity, and ∇_X(fY)=X(f)Y+f∇_XY. Torsion ∇_XY-∇_YX-[X,Y] vanishes; compatibility is Xg(Y,Z)=g(∇_XY,Z)+g(Y,∇_XZ). Extend ∇ to duals/tensors by Leibniz and pairing; that extension's exact proof is M21. Curvature and Ricci signs are exactly M06. The Einstein tensor has zero contracted divergence under M11; this does not mean ∇G=0 in all slots. Γ coefficients are not tensor components, and vanishing at p proves no vanishing of curvature.

Matter stress-energy is a smooth symmetric two-tensor supplied by an explicit matter model. Dust may be proposed only as T^{ab}=ρ u^a u^b with ρ rest mass density kg/m³ and u physical four-velocity, and dust conservation equations must be specified; a perfect fluid adds pressure and an equation of state. A scalar field requires an actual action, units, potential and on-shell field equation. Distributional point sources or interfaces are outside the smooth branch unless additional mathematics is supplied. Diffeomorphism variation implies ∇_aT^{ab}=0 only with matter on shell and applicable boundary assumptions; it is not an off-shell assertion for arbitrary prescribed fields. Energy conditions are further pointwise inequalities, not automatic consequences of the Einstein equations.


## Restricted later model ansätze, defined before claims

For the spherical exterior target, let $m_*>0$, $r_s=2Gm_*/c^2$, and work on $\mathbb R_t\times(r_s,\infty)_r\times S^2$ with sphere charts excluding their poles as appropriate. The Schwarzschild ansatz is $g=-c^2(1-r_s/r)dt^2+(1-r_s/r)^{-1}dr^2+r^2(d\vartheta^2+\sin^2\vartheta\,d\varphi^2)$. Here t is seconds, r metres, angles dimensionless; chart coefficients have corresponding units. This formula is a proposed smooth vacuum solution on the exterior only until M18's tensor calculation is complete. Extendibility across r_s requires another chart and proof, not the divergent component's interpretation as a curvature singularity. Restricted stationary observer redshift follows by conserved null-geodesic Killing contraction and local observer normalization; moving observers have additional factors.

For cosmology, supply a three-manifold $\Sigma$ with a dimensionless Riemannian metric $\gamma$ of constant sectional curvature $k\in\{-1,0,1\}$ (particular globally specified model, not automatic topology classification), an open interval $I$ in seconds and smooth $a:I\to(0,\infty)$ in metres. On $M=I\times\Sigma$, the FLRW ansatz is $g=-c^2dt^2+a(t)^2\gamma$. The comoving physical velocity is $u=\partial_t$, $g(u,u)=-c^2$; expansion parameter $H=\dot a/a$ has s$^{-1}$. Adopt homogeneous density/pressure and an explicit equation of state as matter assumptions. Fluid $T^{ab}=(\epsilon+P)u^au^b/c^2+Pg^{ab}$ has comoving energy density $\epsilon$ and pressure P in J/m³. Einstein reduction, continuity and any scale-factor solution need M18 and their exact ODE/math hypotheses. Local constant curvature alone does not imply global homogeneity/isotropy for every quotient.

For waves, supply a chosen identification of a region with an open subset of affine Minkowski space and a one-parameter metric family $g_{ab}(\varepsilon)=\eta_{ab}+\varepsilon h_{ab}+O(\varepsilon^2)$ with explicit derivative bounds, h a smooth symmetric dimensionless covariant tensor. Trace reverse is $\bar h_{ab}=h_{ab}-\tfrac12\eta_{ab}\eta^{cd}h_{cd}$. Linearized harmonic gauge is $\partial^a\bar h_{ab}=0$; indices in the first-order calculation use η. Vacuum wave equation and polarization claims require M18. A TT projection uses a chosen observer rest space and unit propagation direction s: $P_{ij}=\delta_{ij}-s_is_j$, then $h^{TT}_{ij}=(P_i{}^kP_j{}^l-\tfrac12P_{ij}P^{kl})h_{kl}$ for symmetric spatial h. P has rank2, so its spatial trace is2; using a full four-dimensional rank3 projector with the same half trace is incorrect. Source radiation formulas also need localized slow sources, retarded/no-incoming data, far-zone scale separation and consistent perturbation order. Quadrupole power and asymptotic energy remain deferred to their missing mathematical modules.

A Killing field $\xi$ is a smooth vector field with $\mathcal L_\xi g=0$, equivalently $\nabla_a\xi_b+\nabla_b\xi_a=0$ under M21. For flux forms, require spacetime orientation independently of time orientation, then define $\mathrm{vol}_g=\sqrt{|\det g|}dx^0\wedge\cdots\wedge dx^3$ in oriented charts. Without orientation use a density; these are distinct constructions. Null boundaries have no unit spacelike/timelike normal and require a separate flux definition. An Einstein tensor's contracted divergence is $\nabla^aG_{ab}$, whereas parallel/covariantly constant would require all components of $\nabla_cG_{ab}$ to vanish, a much stronger condition.

## Global, approximation and empirical restrictions

A flat Lorentzian metric gives local Minkowski geometry; topology, holonomy and domain extension control global identification. GR's local inertial construction at a point does not grant a global inertial frame or global simultaneity. Local ∇T=0 supplies no universal globally conserved energy. A Killing-current charge requires the relevant symmetry, boundaries and convergence; asymptotically flat energies have additional asymptotic definitions/conditions. Expanding about Minkowski or comparing with Newtonian gravity requires a chosen gauge/chart, small parameters, derivative/error bounds, matter scaling and explicit correspondences. No universal identification of Newtonian mass density with every relativistic energy component is made.

Experimental pages require actual primary reports, operational quantities, calibration, uncertainty/systematics and stated statistical method. Historical anecdotes and exact textbook predictions are not measured data. Conditional model predictions and thought experiments carry their model and apparatus assumptions; finite data can support/constrain them without proving postulates. No source numerical typo is inherited merely because its textbook is authoritative.
