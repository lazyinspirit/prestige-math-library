# Mathematical supplier audit for the deep QM prose design

Audit date: 2026-10-03. This is a read-only research audit, not an engine receipt or an independent acceptance judgment. Root `items/` and imported `physics/items/` were not changed. “Published” records the actual frontmatter, not mathematical infallibility. I read the complete statements and arguments of the load-bearing direct interfaces tabulated below (definitions include their well-definedness text). This does **not** claim a fresh independent proof audit of every transitive supplier. The companion transitive register identifies inherited prerequisite interfaces and explicitly marks review status. An unreviewed inherited argument or a missing local proof is not silently certified.

All local mathematics in `mathematical-prerequisites.md` has mathematical hypotheses/dependencies only. The physical model assignment consumes mathematics; mathematics never consumes a state/Born/dynamics postulate as a premise. Use AC explicitly where the published unbounded spectral/Stone/Kato interfaces require it, and countable choice for their weaker Hilbert/Fourier/trace interfaces. The algebraic finite branches require no choice. The imported library's first-variable-linear convention must be translated, not replaced: physics bra `⟨v|u⟩` is the library `(u,v)`.
