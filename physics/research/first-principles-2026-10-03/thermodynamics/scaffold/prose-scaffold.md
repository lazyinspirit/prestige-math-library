## Adopted framework and primitive quantities come first

The principal route is **macroscopic equilibrium thermodynamics in a concave entropy representation**, for ordinary stable, additive, bulk, simple compressible systems in a positive-temperature sector. It adopts physical postulates; it is not deduced from unspecified mechanics, quantum mechanics, or data. A separate accessibility route explains what stronger operational axioms can prove. Statistical mechanics is a separate interpretation branch with additional microscopic and probability assumptions. The routes share conditional results only where an explicit identification is made.

For a fixed material constitution and fixed amounts n=(n_1,…,n_r), an equilibrium state is represented by a point x=(U,V) in a specified nonempty open convex Γ_n⊂R² (or (U,a_1,…,a_m) for separately declared work coordinates). Γ_n, the coordinates, and what constitutes an equilibrium preparation are physical model inputs. U is a primitive real coordinate called internal energy; V>0 is the primitive real coordinate called volume. The fixed-material label n is not silently varied within Γ_n. To discuss matter exchange, prescribe a larger domain D⊂R^(r+2), in coordinates (U,V,n), and explicit allowed conserved amounts; in the bulk continuous approximation n_j>0 are real mole amounts, not exact integers. An isolated equilibrium state is one with no allowed external exchange; a closed system prohibits matter exchange but can exchange energy; an open system permits explicitly named matter exchanges. These words specify permissions, not equations of state.

Postulates for the principal route, with each intended as a source-reviewed physical item:

1. **Equilibrium description and first-law energy.** Specified equilibrium preparations are represented by Γ_n or D. U differences measure net energy transferred; for a closed process P from x to y, U(y)-U(x)=Q[P]+W_on[P]. For an adiabatic process Q[P]=0, this makes work an endpoint increment on the admitted endpoints. Connectivity/reachability needed for an operational construction of U must be separately postulated; here U is adopted and the balance law imposed. Total energy for weakly interacting separated systems is additive after neglecting interface and long-range interaction energy.
2. **Entropy fundamental relation.** Adopt S:D→R, finite, continuous, concave on a specified convex domain, additive for independent separated macroscopic systems, and degree-one homogeneous on the specified bulk scaling family. On each smooth single-phase open region take S∈C² and S_U>0. Where only C¹ or concavity is justified, derivative formulas are restricted accordingly. S is a primitive fundamental relation in this route: specifying its form is constitutive information, not a consequence of the word entropy.
3. **Equilibrium and allowed constraints.** When an isolated compound is allowed to relax with given totals and retained partitions, the equilibrium macrostate maximizes the sum of the constituent entropies on the explicit feasible set. This includes an existence assumption or an independently proved attainment statement. Equilibrium is global stable equilibrium under those permissions; local maxima describe restricted/metastable states only after a separate dynamical or kinetic scope statement.
4. **Macroscopic second law.** For the admitted isolated macroscopic relaxation or adiabatic accessibility relation, the total entropy is nondecreasing. Reversible admissible changes preserve total entropy. Calling a process reversible means that the reversed change can be achieved with both system and auxiliaries returned, except for reversed energy transfers; a slow process is not thereby reversible. An adiabatic irreversible process can increase entropy.
5. **Thermal contact and zeroth law.** A thermal equilibrium relation on the family is adopted as an equivalence relation realized by the admitted contacts. Compatible temperature equality is subsequently deduced from the entropy maximum plus regularity. Transitivity alone does not produce a real numerical, let alone differentiable, temperature coordinate.
6. **Low-temperature branch.** Any Nernst or unattainability assertion is an additional scoped postulate, not a consequence of 1–5. It applies only to stated equilibrium families and permitted cooling operations. Its alternative formulations and residual-entropy exceptions are kept explicit.

The choice to postulate entropy rather than derive it is transparent. Another formulation may regard S as derived from accessibility, or from a specified microscopic statistical model. Temperature is derived here by T=(S_U)^−1; pressure p=T S_V; molar chemical potentials μ_j=−T S_nj. These are scalar functions on the regions where their derivatives exist. A chosen thermal equilibrium quotient and a thermometer calibration can instead make an empirical temperature label primitive in another route; it is not automatically the absolute T in this route. Pressure may be an operational force-per-area primitive in an accessibility formulation; identifying it with T S_V needs that formulation's proof. Constitutive input S is still needed to calculate any of these functions. Adopt an explicit piston-coupling postulate when using mechanical work: in quasistatic frictionless mechanical equilibrium a piston of area A has normal force pA, with displacement dℓ and dV=A dℓ, so the transferred work on the system is −p dV. This identifies the derivative-defined p with operational wall pressure in the declared model; units alone do not establish it. Similar generalized-force identifications need separately stated couplings.

Use SI throughout the core: U,Q,W,F,H,G,Ω in joules J=kg m² s^−2; V in m³; S and heat capacities in J K^−1; T in kelvin K; p in pascals Pa=J m^−3; n_j in mol and μ_j in J mol^−1. For particle count N_j (dimensionless), use μ_j^(particle) in J and n_j=N_j/N_A; μ_j=N_A μ_j^(particle). Boltzmann's constant k_B=1.380649×10^−23 J K^−1 and Avogadro's constant N_A=6.02214076×10^23 mol^−1 are exact modern SI defining constants; R=N_A k_B is derived. Absolute entropy scaling in J/K must be distinguished from an accessibility construction normalized to S(x_0)=0,S(x_1)=1, which is dimensionless until calibrated. Additive constants depend on material labels and permitted transformations; chemical reactions and mixing restrict compatible choices. The core does not set k_B=1. The microscopic branch introduces h=6.62607015×10^−34 J s (exact SI), ℏ=h/(2π), and any mass or Hamiltonian parameters explicitly. The exact constants and SI unit relations are checked against BIPM’s official SI Brochure9, English §§2.1–2.3, printed123,129–130,133–135 (PDF125,131–132,135,137), retrieved at https://www.bipm.org/documents/20126/41483022/SI-Brochure-9.pdf; selected sections were personally read and provenance is in sources-si/provenance.json; no new units category or workflow is proposed.

## Composition, constraints, and accessibility are actual objects

For two equilibrium systems, the separated compound state space is Γ_A×Γ_B, with states (x_A,x_B). Under an additive bulk model U_tot=U_A+U_B and S_tot=S_A+S_B. An isolated diathermal contact at fixed constituent volumes and amounts has feasible energy partitions

C={u:(u,V_A,n_A)∈D_A, (U_tot-u,V_B,n_B)∈D_B}.

The entropy maximum is over u∈C. C need not be compact or nonempty; these must be checked before asserting an attained maximum. An impermeable movable partition also permits V_A+V_B=V_tot; a permeable partition permits each relevant n_Aj+n_Bj=n_tot,j. Retained walls fix the corresponding variables. There is no pressure or chemical-potential equality conclusion if the corresponding exchange is forbidden. If reactions are admitted, define a stoichiometric matrix ν=(ν_jα), reaction extents ξ∈R^k, and n=n^0+νξ with n_j≥0; an interior maximum gives Σ_jν_jα μ_j=0 for each allowed independent reaction. Boundary inequalities and absent species are separately analyzed.

A process P includes initial/final equilibria, a time interval, controls, contacts and work/heat records; its interior need not lie in Γ. A quasistatic equilibrium path is a piecewise-C¹ curve γ:[a,b]→D through equilibrium states under a declared limiting protocol. Such a path is an idealization, not a universal description of irreversible processes. A heat bath is an auxiliary equilibrium system whose temperature is approximated fixed during exchange; exact fixed T is an infinite-capacity or controlled idealization. An isolated compound plus bath is the object to which entropy accounting is applied.

For the alternative Lieb–Yngvason (LY) route, define x≺y operationally: there exists a process taking the system from x to y with a device that returns to its initial state, and a weight whose final height may differ; the only net effect on surroundings is this mechanical energy record. This is adiabatic accessibility, not the assertion that the system itself evolves slowly or never interacts thermally with the device. Define x~_A y iff x≺y and y≺x; x≺≺y iff x≺y but not y≺x. Forward sectors are A_x={y∈Γ:x≺y}. A support hyperplane through x is a set {z:ℓ(z−x)=0} for a nonzero linear functional ℓ, with the supported sector entirely on one side; unique refers to the plane rather than arbitrary scalar multiples of ℓ. A slope map is locally Lipschitz if each point has a neighborhood and finite L with ‖P(y)−P(z)‖≤L‖y−z‖ for all points there, using fixed coordinate scales. Relative boundary means closure minus relative interior in Γ; arcwise connected means each two points can be joined by a continuous path in that boundary. Scaled systems Γ^(λ) and λx, λ>0, and composition are part of this route's primitive operation structure; equal numerical coordinates need not identify different scaled material systems.

The operational axioms are A1 reflexivity; A2 transitivity; A3 composition consistency ((x,y)≺(x′,y′) when x≺x′ and y≺y′); A4 positive scaling invariance; A5 x~_A(λx,(1−λ)x), 0<λ<1; A6 stability: (x,ε_k z_0)≺(y,ε_k z_1) for fixed z_0,z_1 and ε_k↓0 implies x≺y. Comparison CH says any two states in a **specified** state space are comparable. A1–A6 plus comparison on Γ alone do not justify an additive entropy on products. LY Theorem 2.2 requires the relevant multiple scaled products; Theorem 2.4 reduces this to comparison on each twofold scaled product under the other hypotheses. Entropy construction is S_Γ(x)=sup{λ:((1−λ)x_0,λx_1)≺x} for x_0≺≺x_1, with negative coefficients interpreted by transferring their positive copies across the relation, not as physical negative systems. Stability and product comparison prove finiteness and limiting equivalence. M11 in completed-developments.md now proves cancellation, finite attained reference suprema, additive/extensive representation and affine uniqueness from the stated product comparison hypothesis, including signed-coefficient bookkeeping. It is a completed research argument, not a published library theorem.

To derive CH rather than assume it, LY additionally uses A7 accessibility of convex recombination, nonempty open convex simple-system domains, S1 irreversible accessibility from each state, S2 unique supporting hyperplane with finite locally Lipschitz pressure slopes, and S3 arcwise-connected relative boundary of each forward sector. Thermal axioms T1 define the thermal join Δ_AB={(U_A+U_B,a_A,a_B)} and accessible joining; T2 admits equilibrium splitting preserving total energy and work coordinates; T3 is thermal-equilibrium transitivity; T4 provides thermally equivalent states on both accessibility sides of each state (transversality); T5 supplies universal thermal-equilibrium temperature range at each work coordinate. Our completed adopted-contact theorem explicitly also assumes **C1 equilibrium-join coherence** (x~T y⇒J(x,y)~T x and J(x,y)~T y) and source **T5 universal thermal range**. Degenerate thermometers do not automatically satisfy T4. The principal entropy-postulate route does not pretend these operational hypotheses have been proved. ## Heat, work, and the first law

Our sign is W_on positive for work on the system. A reversible simple-compressible displacement has δW_on=−p dV, with p the system pressure when mechanical equilibrium with the actuator is assumed. General finite compression uses the external generalized force and protocol, not a substituted equilibrium p along nonexistent intermediate states. Heat Q[P] is energy transfer assigned to thermal exchange under the operational first-law convention; it is not contained in a state as a second kind of energy. ΔU=Q+W_on for closed systems defines their combined balance. Cycles have ΔU=0 while Q and W can each be nonzero. The mathematical counterexample M6 makes path dependence concrete.

On a reversible equilibrium path, entropy balance supplies δQ_rev=T dS; therefore dU=T dS−p dV+Σμ_jdn_j. At fixed material this is a differential of the fundamental relation. The Σμ_jdn_j term compares equilibrium states of varying composition; it is not a universal heat/work accounting rule for flowing matter. Open control volumes need separately defined energy/enthalpy fluxes and kinetic/potential-energy contributions, with their extra physical assumptions. The core confines material exchange arguments to equilibrium variation and reaction equilibrium.

## Entropy and energy representations, temperature, and stability

For S_U>0 the scalar inverse M3 gives a local C² energy representation U=U(S,V,n). Then U_S=T, U_V=−p, U_nj=μ_j by differentiating S(U(S,V,n),V,n)=S. Global inversion needs monotonicity and domain-image coverage, not just a local nonzero derivative. Smooth regularity is restricted to one phase region; the macroscopic fundamental function across coexistence may have affine portions and lose strictness. Negative-temperature statistical models require bounded spectra and separately stated applicability and contact rules; they are not silently inserted into the positive-temperature family.

Concavity of S and the maximum principle make equilibrium exchange conditions conditional deductions. At an interior diathermal equilibrium, S_A,U=S_B,U, so T_A=T_B. Allowing volume exchange also yields p_A/T_A=p_B/T_B, hence p_A=p_B; allowing species exchange yields μ_Aj/T_A=μ_Bj/T_B and μ_Aj=μ_Bj. These statements need a common entropy calibration and positive finite T. M2 gives global sufficiency of a stationary point under concavity and uniqueness under strict concavity on the permitted slice. Hessian negative semidefiniteness is the C² concavity criterion; it is not strict stability. Homogeneity forces a Hessian null scaling direction (M4). Boundary equilibria use inequalities or supergradients rather than the interior equalities.

For fixed V,n, C_V=(∂U/∂T)_(V,n)=T(∂S/∂T)_(V,n), where a valid temperature chart exists. C_V>0 is obtained from strict positive U_SS and T>0 on this slice; an inverse derivative and finite response are hypotheses. Define κ_T=−(1/V)(∂V/∂p)_(T,n), α=(1/V)(∂V/∂T)_(p,n), and C_p=(∂H/∂T)_(p,n). On an admissible stable smooth chart, prove C_p−C_V=TVα²/κ_T by chained derivative transformations and require κ_T>0. Critical or incompressible limits must not be evaluated by dividing by zero. Response inequalities across ensembles require their own constraints; they cannot be imported from grand-canonical variances without model identification.

## Legendre potentials and reversible-cycle deductions

Define H=U+pV, F=U−TS, G=U−TS+pV and Ω=U−TS−Σμ_jn_j as scalar functions. Their natural-variable forms require invertible coordinate maps. In those valid charts,

dH=T dS+V dp+Σμ_jdn_j; dF=−S dT−p dV+Σμ_jdn_j; dG=−S dT+V dp+Σμ_jdn_j; dΩ=−S dT−p dV−Σn_jdμ_j.

These follow by product rule and cancellation, not by a new law. Potentials give constrained equilibrium minima by including an ideal reservoir's entropy or by the smooth convex minimization argument M5. An imposed reservoir fixes T or p and changes the correct feasible set; a blanket rule that every system minimizes every potential is false. Homogeneity gives U=TS−pV+Σμ_jn_j and Gibbs–Duhem S dT−V dp+Σn_jdμ_j=0 only in the specified bulk extensive model.

C² mixed partial equality yields Maxwell relations: (∂T/∂V)_(S,n)=−(∂p/∂S)_(V,n), (∂T/∂p)_(S,n)=(∂V/∂S)_(p,n), (∂S/∂V)_(T,n)=(∂p/∂T)_(V,n), (∂S/∂p)_(T,n)=−(∂V/∂T)_(p,n). Further chemical relations follow pairwise from the same Hessian symmetry. State the derivative chart and fixed coordinates each time. Closed-form potential differentials are exact where defined; equality of cross partials alone on a multiply connected domain does not prove global exactness.

For a cyclic engine exchanging heat Q_h>0 with an ideal bath T_h and rejecting Q_c>0 to a bath T_c<T_h, energy balance gives work output W=Q_h−Q_c. Total entropy change of the baths is −Q_h/T_h+Q_c/T_c≥0 if the engine returns to its initial state and no omitted auxiliary changes occur; thus η=W/Q_h≤1−T_c/T_h. Equality requires reversible total exchange. For a refrigerator W_on>0 and Q_c>0 extracted from the cold bath, the same accounting gives COP≤T_c/(T_h−T_c). For finite concave reservoirs, M10 gives an initial-temperature upper bound using the tangent inequality; LY pp67–68 instead prints a final-temperature bound with the wrong concavity direction. That extension is rejected, with an explicit hypothetical constant-capacity witness in M10. These are conditional thought-experiment/theorem items; no measured engine performance is invented. Clausius ∮δQ/T≤0 requires defining the reservoir/boundary temperatures and admitted cycle decomposition; it is not proved by treating arbitrary δQ/T_system as an exact differential.

## Third-law scope

For an explicit Nernst branch specify an equilibrium-state map φ:(0,T_0)×K→D with T(φ(t,a))=t and compact parameter set K. Define S_K(t,a)=S(φ(t,a)) and postulate sup_(a,b∈K)|S_K(t,a)−S_K(t,b)|→0 as t↓0; this is a uniform version and requires scope for K. Separate this Nernst heat theorem from weaker pairwise limits, the Planck-style unique-ground-state absolute-entropy convention, and unattainability (no admitted finite operation reaches T=0). For unattainability specify the allowed cooling-protocol family C and postulate that no protocol in C with finite duration and finitely many admitted operations takes a positive-T equilibrium endpoint to T=0. A restricted cycle model establishes only its own no-reaching-zero conclusion. Equivalence of these formulations requires auxiliary assumptions. Along a declared fixed-constraint family define C(t)=t dS_K(t,a)/dt where the derivative exists. The relation S_K(T,a)−S_K(0,a)=∫_0^T C(T′)/T′dT′ holds only where the derivative identity, zero-temperature limit and convergence hold; integrability does not imply an integer power law or C∝T^n with n≥1. C∝sqrt(T) is an elementary counterexample to that inference. A microscopic ground-state degeneracy g can give residual entropy k_B log g under the specific canonical low-temperature limit and equilibrium interpretation. Disorder, glass kinetics, and unresolved degeneracy require separate scope; thermodynamics alone does not prove universal S(0)=0. Boundary T=0 is excluded from charts dividing by T.

## Phases, finite size, and the thermodynamic limit

A phase in this scaffold is an explicitly specified branch/domain of homogeneous equilibrium states with a smooth fundamental function and constitutive relations. Coexistence states maximize total entropy over permitted phase fractions and exchanges, so their intensive variables agree at an interior maximum. In a bulk one-component T,p chart, equal molar Gibbs potentials express two-phase coexistence. Given smooth phase branches and Δv≠0, differentiating g_a(T,p)=g_b(T,p) yields dp/dT=Δs/Δv; latent molar heat is TΔs on a reversible phase conversion. Specify branch existence, interface neglect, finite differentiability and local implicit-chart hypotheses. Critical points with Δv=0 are outside that division. Gibbs phase rule additionally needs rank/nondegeneracy of chemical-equilibrium constraints; it is not an automatic count without hypotheses.

First-order coexistence creates affine portions of macroscopic entropy and failure of strict stability; other transitions require an explicitly defined singularity/nonanalyticity classification. The finite idealized equilibrium model can adopt an extensive entropy phenomenologically without proving a microscopic limit. In the finite-state canonical model M7, Z is a positive finite sum and thermodynamic functions are smooth at finite T; singularities require a specified infinite/thermodynamic limit or an unbounded model with its own domain analysis. Define a bulk thermodynamic limit as a sequence (N_k,V_k)→∞ with N_k/V_k→ρ and specified interactions/boundaries, and limit free energy or entropy per volume/particle. Its existence, ensemble equivalence, and derivative interchange remain O3. Short-range stable additive models may support familiar concavity; self-gravitating, long-range, surface-dominated and constrained systems need separate branches and may fail these assumptions. A mean-field/van-der-Waals model is a model assumption, not a universal proof of phase structure.

## Statistical interpretation and irreversible boundaries

The minimum statistical bridge uses a finite microscopic state set Ω, a specified energy function E:Ω→R, and an adopted equilibrium sampling model. Microcanonical probabilities additionally choose an energy shell/resolution and uniform weighting or an equivalent preparation rule; S=k_B log(number of shell states) uses that shell convention. Canonical weights p_i=exp(−E_i/(k_BT))/Z follow from additional bath/weak-coupling or constrained maximum-entropy assumptions, not solely from the macroscopic laws. M7 proves conditional finite identities ⟨E⟩=−∂βlog Z, Var(E)=∂β²log Z, S=−k_BΣp_i log p_i=k_B(log Z+β⟨E⟩) with β=(k_BT)^−1 and fixed levels. F=−k_BT log Z is a proposed identification with the macroscopic F when the model and limit justify it. Classical continuous phase space requires a measure and dimensionless normalization (including h and indistinguishability choices); quantum Gibbs states require a Hilbert space, self-adjoint Hamiltonian, positive trace-class exp(−βH), and an explicitly defined trace/entropy. These are O4 suppliers and declared extra mechanics/QM bridges, not implicit prerequisites of the equilibrium core.

The Boltzmann macrocell entropy, Gibbs entropy of a probability state and von Neumann entropy of a density operator have different domains. Their identification with calorimetric entropy needs the stated equilibrium/model bridge. Fine-grained Liouville/Gibbs or unitary von Neumann entropy conservation is a separate mathematical statement under measure-preserving/unitary hypotheses and appropriate entropy domains; it is not the same quantity or claim as macroscopic irreversible entropy production. L17 proves declared Hamiltonian measure/recurrence/ergodic scope, L18 discrete trace-class Cesaro dephasing and L20 regular compact-shell measure/invariance; model ergodicity remains an additional assumption. Independent additive microscopic systems factorize, giving additive Gibbs entropy; correlations and interactions add qualifications. Ensemble equivalence needs actual limit/concentration/concavity hypotheses. A finite grand-canonical condensate occupation can fluctuate macroscopically; expected occupations do not guarantee every realization. Probability-one, expectation and every-trial assertions remain distinct.

The macroscopic entropy inequality is an idealized law for admitted processes. A microscopic fluctuation relation is a statement about a path probability distribution under a specified initial ensemble, driving protocol and reversibility assumptions. It can admit negative entropy-production realizations and imply nonnegative **mean** production with the appropriate integrability/Jensen argument; it does not prove monotonic entropy in every finite trial. No such relation is included as proved in this core. Local-equilibrium fields T(x,t), density and fluxes, Onsager coefficients, Fourier heat conduction, diffusion, hydrodynamics, stochastic thermodynamics and kinetic equations form later branches requiring their own continuum/model, regularity, boundary and empirical premises. A future local-equilibrium branch must, for example, specify Ω⊂R³, I⊂R and fields T:Ω×I→(0,∞), u:Ω×I→R, j_Q:Ω×I→R³, with regularity and initial/boundary conditions. Here u is energy density in J/m³ and j_Q is heat flux in W/m²; a Fourier constitutive model j_Q=−K∇T requires a declared conductivity tensor K in W/(m K) and positivity hypotheses. These are extra continuum/constitutive assumptions, not consequences of the equilibrium postulates. Define all additional fields before use and do not apply equilibrium Γ to arbitrary evolving fields.

No experiment item is proposed from textbook examples. Production evidence needs actual published reports with apparatus, operational definitions, calibration, controls, sampling assumptions, uncertainty and systematics; finite measurements support or constrain specified models and do not prove a law. Textbook historical anecdotes and calculations remain remarks/examples/conditional thought experiments. No invented data, significance or source-reading claim is allowed.
