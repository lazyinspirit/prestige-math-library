2.1 For a bounded measurable $f$ on $X$, each replacement map $\eta\mapsto\sigma_\Delta\eta_{\Delta^c}$ is measurable and each weight in [F1] depends on only the finite boundary $C_\Delta$. The finite weighted sum defining $\gamma_\Delta(f\mid\eta)$ is measurable. If $B$ is exterior-measurable, replacement leaves every exterior coordinate unchanged, so $\gamma_\Delta(\mathbf1_B\mid\eta)=\mathbf1_B(\eta)$; the kernel is proper. Its cylinder probabilities depend only on $C_\Delta$ and the finitely many coordinates named by the cylinder, so it has finite range. [F1, step 1.1, algebra]
