## Deductions checked

Its signed normal Poynting flux and separately declared magnitude convention agree with the wave supplier. Translating I_theta by theta_0 supplies the open interval containing zero required by the one-dimensional uncertainty theorem. The derivative margin and simultaneous deterministic bounds give confinement through the mean value theorem and triangle inequality. No sampling conclusion follows.

For the fringe example, the same-frequency transverse waves and common real polarization are retained. Phase pi cancels the electric amplitude but leaves a nonzero magnetic amplitude. The background's zero extension is explicitly measurable. Monotonicity gives the lower bound without requiring integral additivity; the scalar complex triangle inequality and simple integrals give the finite-volume upper bound. An infinite response is not a finite recorded rate, and point zeros are not event probabilities.
