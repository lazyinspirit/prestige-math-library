This page defines the actual phase-flow domain and the reference measure
induced by a symplectic form, then separates complete invariant flows from
assumptions of finite measure or normalizability. It proves conditional
invariance for energy-weighted and regular compact-shell probabilities,
almost-everywhere recurrence, sampled ergodic averages, Liouville transport,
and finite-time compact-phase response. The Green–Kubo item proves a
covariance-growth limit only under absolute integrability; it does not infer a
central limit theorem or transport coefficient. Ergodicity, mixing, canonical
normalization, and correlation decay remain explicit hypotheses. Physical
conclusions are deductions inside the adopted model, not measured
equilibration claims.
