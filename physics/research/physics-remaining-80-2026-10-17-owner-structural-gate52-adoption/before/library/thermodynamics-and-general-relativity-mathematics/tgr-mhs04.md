## Scope

This page gives a mathematical periodic-box model of two-polarization photon
modes with the zero wavevector excluded and no zero-point energy. It proves
finite-box trace-class convergence, finite energy moments, the exact entropy
of the full diagonal Gibbs state, and thermodynamic-volume Planck energy and
entropy densities. A separately defined isotropic ray model yields the
one-sided energy and coarse entropy fluxes. These deductions do not report
measurements, assume a material-wall equilibration mechanism, or establish
emission by an actual horizon.

The auxiliary exponential-sum and singular Riemann-limit lemmas supply the
finite-box and continuum steps locally. The Planck integrals are derived from
the Fourier Parseval theorem on the required orthonormal-bases page. The trace
calculation uses the actual batch 45 draft Fock-basis and diagonal-trace
interfaces, whose Step-1 readiness is recorded separately from independent
proof acceptance. The final mass ODE and entropy-accounting proposition is a
conditional fixed-reference effective-area model and excludes a universal
generalized second-law claim.
