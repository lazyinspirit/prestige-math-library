- `lem-three-dimensional-radial-carleman-integer-weight` (lemma, 16 deps, level 2): under Countable Choice, for integer `s >= 1`, `tau = s+1`, `||r^{-tau}u|| <= (2/s)||r^{-tau+2} Delta u||` for `u` in `C_c^inf(R^3 \ {0})`, with the explicit constant not claimed sharp, plus the same inequality for `H^2` functions compactly supported in a fixed annulus `0 < a <= |x| <= b` via bounded weights and smooth approximation.
- `thm-bounded-potential-weak-unique-continuation-in-three-dimensions` (theorem, 15 deps, level 3): `Omega` nonempty open connected in `R^3`, `V in L^inf_loc(Omega)`, `psi in H^2_loc(Omega)` with `Delta psi = V psi` distributionally; if `psi` vanishes on a nonempty open subset then `psi = 0` on `Omega`. No sign, symmetry, smoothness or decay of `V` beyond local essential boundedness; global propagation by polygonal paths.
- `thm-em-spherical-multipole-basis` (theorem, 15 deps, level 3): for `rho in L^1(R^3)` supported in `B_a(0)`, the exterior multipole expansion in the real spherical-harmonic basis with the addition theorem, absolute and uniform convergence on `|x| >= r_0 > a` with the stated geometric tail bound, degree terms as `Y_lm`-moments of `rho`, and the ball boundary series branch (Poisson/continuous data) with uniform derivative convergence on smaller balls.
- `thm-em-elliptic-boundary-existence` (theorem, 9 deps, level 4): for a bounded `C^inf` domain and uniformly positive smooth conductivity, weak Dirichlet solvability with exact trace and `C^inf`-on-closure upgrade; Neumann solvability with per-component compatibility, uniqueness modulo componentwise constants and the normalized unique representative; the transmission variant on finitely many disjoint smooth interfaces with sheet charge, the exact flux jump `n.[eps grad u] = -sigma`, and explicit exclusions (edge/junction points, reentrant corners, coefficient loss of regularity).
- `lem-em-exterior-outgoing-scalar-uniqueness` (lemma, 6 deps, level 4): smooth bounded obstacle with connected exterior, `(Delta + k^2)u = 0`, `k > 0`, `u = 0` on the inner boundary and outgoing in the modal DtN class of `lem-em-sphere-outgoing-dirichlet-to-neumann-map`; then `u = 0`. No decay rate or pointwise Sommerfeld formula beyond the declared class.
- `lem-em-exterior-vector-helmholtz-mixed-boundary-existence` (lemma, 14 deps, level 6): for every smooth tangent field on the obstacle there is exactly one smooth exterior field solving the componentwise Helmholtz system with `div E = 0`, that tangential trace and modal outgoing behavior; the normal closure relation accompanies the tangential data, and three scalar Dirichlet data are explicitly not asserted equivalent. Uses the Fredholm alternative (declared Axiom of Choice) and the local UCP theorem in the kernel step.
- `thm-em-waveguide-spectral-completeness` (theorem, 9 deps, level 6): the smooth PEC cavity curl-curl operator on the declared graph-norm class has a complete orthonormal `L^2` eigenbasis with nonnegative finite-multiplicity eigenvalues accumulating at infinity, retained zero modes, `C^inf` eigenfields on the closure, and the modal wave expansion with an explicitly mathematical positive speed parameter; the rectangular reflection-parity energy class is complete with the recorded unique weak energy solution. No completeness is asserted in lossy, open-ended or non-smooth classes.
