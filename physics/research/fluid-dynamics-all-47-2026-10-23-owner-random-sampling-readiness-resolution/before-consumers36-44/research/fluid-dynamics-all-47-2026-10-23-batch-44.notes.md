## Completed item arguments and dependencies

- `def-fd-solenoidal-surrogate-residual` defines the continuous normalized-torus Fourier-projector residual `r_v = v_t + P div(v⊗v) − νΔv − Pf`, the tensor-divergence convention, and the initial defect. It depends on `def-countable-choice`, `def-fd-periodic-fourier-sobolev-spaces`, and `def-fd-periodic-leray-pressure-maps`. The well-definedness proof checks the multiplier domains, continuity into `L²`, and finiteness of the time integral. It explicitly warns that finite collocation values do not bound the continuous residual.

- `thm-fd-surrogate-residual-error` proves the weighted integrating-factor bound for an already-given smooth divergence-free exact solution and a smooth divergence-free surrogate under the same `ν` and `f`. Its direct dependencies are `def-countable-choice`, `def-fd-periodic-leray-pressure-maps`, `def-fd-solenoidal-surrogate-residual`, and `thm-holder-inequality-for-integrals`. The proof subtracts the equations, cancels transport using `div v = 0`, applies the normalized `L²` Cauchy–Schwarz inequality, regularizes the norm, and integrates the scalar differential inequality. It makes no existence, collocation-only, or empirical claim.

- `prop-fd-surrogate-law-shift-observable` states the conditional `L sqrt(C) epsilon` target-bias bound for measurable maps into a real normed state space, a Lipschitz observable, population second moments under `μ`, and an explicitly defined target density `g ≤ C`. Its direct dependencies are `def-fd-random-fluid-observable` and `thm-holder-inequality-for-integrals`. The proof supplies target integrability and the `L²` density-norm bound. It does not infer population assumptions from a finite test sample.

- `ex-fd-unseen-input-shift` uses a two-point field space with `μ=δ0`, `ν=δ1`, zero training error, and target bias `M` for arbitrary `M>0`. It depends on `prop-fd-surrogate-law-shift-observable` and verifies that the density assumption fails exactly at the unseen point.
