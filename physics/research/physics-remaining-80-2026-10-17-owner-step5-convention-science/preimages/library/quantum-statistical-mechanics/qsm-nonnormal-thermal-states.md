This page constructs the finite-excitation vacuum representation of the quasi-local spin algebra and proves that it is faithful and irreducible. In a finite-dimensional onsite Gibbs model, the product state is the unique KMS state, yet it has no normal extension in this particular representation. The nonnormality is representation-dependent; it is not a claim about all representations of the same state.

The KMS, finite-matrix, and quasi-local completion inputs remain conditional on their inspected draft interfaces and proof statuses. The proofs below record those conditions explicitly; this page does not claim their independent acceptance.
