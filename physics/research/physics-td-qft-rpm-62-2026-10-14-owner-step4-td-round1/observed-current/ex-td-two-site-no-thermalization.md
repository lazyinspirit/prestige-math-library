## Example

Assume AC and Countable Choice for the canonical mode preparation. Let $\mathcal H_1=\mathcal H_2=\mathbb C^2$ with orthonormal basis $|0\rangle,|1\rangle$, and define the factor number maps by $N_r|0\rangle=0$, $N_r|1\rangle=|1\rangle$. The composite Hilbert space is the finite Hilbert tensor product $\mathcal H_1\widehat\otimes\mathcal H_2\cong\mathbb C^4$ from [[lem-nrqm-composites-hilbert-tensor-construction]], with orthonormal product basis $|n_1n_2\rangle$, $n_r\in\{0,1\}$. On this basis define the site-number operators $\mathcal N_1|n_1n_2\rangle=n_1|n_1n_2\rangle$ and $\mathcal N_2|n_1n_2\rangle=n_2|n_1n_2\rangle$. For $T>0$ kelvins set $\varepsilon=k_{\mathrm B}T\log2$ joules and define the noninteracting total Hamiltonian
$$H=\varepsilon(\mathcal N_1+\mathcal N_2),\qquad H|n_1n_2\rangle=\varepsilon(n_1+n_2)|n_1n_2\rangle.$$
The quantum Gibbs preparation at $T$ is used for the thermal comparison, and the closed-system unitary evolution is generated by $H$ with $U_t=e^{-itH/\hbar}$, where $t$ is in seconds and $\hbar>0$ is in $\mathrm{J\,s}$. Start the dynamics in $\rho_0=|00\rangle\langle00|$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The finite product energy model, the assigned positive temperature, the unitary evolution postulate, and the stated initial state.

[F1] [[post-nrqm-many-body-canonical-equilibrium-ensemble]]: under AC and Countable Choice, a declared self-adjoint semibounded energy generator with a positive finite Gibbs trace is assigned the density $\rho_\beta=e^{-\beta H}/Z$.

[F2] [[pthm-td-finite-mode-occupation-and-trace-formulas]]: for a finite tensor product of independent fermionic two-level factors with additive total energy, the trace is the product of the one-mode traces $1+q$, and the joint occupation-basis law is the product of the one-mode Gibbs weights.

[F3] [[post-nrqm-framework-autonomous-dynamics]] and [[def-nrqm-autonomous-dynamics-framework-evolution]]: closed-system evolution is $\rho(t)=U_t\rho_0U_t^*$ with $U_t=e^{-itH/\hbar}$.

[A1] Each site has $q=1/2$, and the initial vector is the ground energy eigenvector $|00\rangle$ of $H$.

## Verification

**Given:** $q=1/2$, the product model, and the closed-system evolution.

1.1 The product trace in [F2] is $Z=(1+q)^2=9/4$. By the Gibbs assignment [F1] and the product-basis energies from the Statement, $P(n_1,n_2)=q^{n_1+n_2}/Z$; at $q=1/2$, the probabilities on $(00,01,10,11)$ are $(4/9,2/9,2/9,1/9)$. In particular, the Gibbs probability of $|11\rangle$ is $1/9>0$. [F1, F2, A1, algebra]

2.1 Since $H|00\rangle=0$, $U_t|00\rangle=|00\rangle$ for every $t$; thus $\rho(t)=|00\rangle\langle00|$. Its probability for the projector $|11\rangle\langle11|$ is zero at every time, unlike the Gibbs value $1/9$. The state therefore never reaches this Gibbs density under the declared unitary evolution. [F3, A1, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
