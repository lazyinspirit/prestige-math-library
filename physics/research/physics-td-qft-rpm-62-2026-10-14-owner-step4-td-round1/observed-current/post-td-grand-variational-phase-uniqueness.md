## Postulate

Fix the selected stable regularly tempered pair model with Borel-measurable $\widehat\varphi=\varphi/E_0$, $T>0$, $\beta=(k_{\mathrm B}T)^{-1}$ with the SI conventions of [[def-td-stable-tempered-continuum-preparation]], and activity $z>0$. For the superstable branch, require measurable even pair potentials $\varphi_s,\varphi_r$ with $\varphi=\varphi_s+\varphi_r$, where $\varphi_s$ is stable in joules and $\varphi_r(x)\geq0$ joules for every $x$. Require constants $\epsilon>0$ joules and $r_0>0$ in dimensionless coordinates such that $\varphi_r(x)\geq\epsilon$ whenever $\|x\|_\infty\leq r_0$, where $\|x\|_\infty=\max_j|x_j|$. After energy conversion, $\widehat\varphi_s=\varphi_s/E_0$ is stable and $\widehat\varphi_r=\varphi_r/E_0\geq0$ with core lower bound $\epsilon/E_0$, exactly as in [[def-tdmath-superstable-regular-interaction]]. This is an additional model hypothesis, not a consequence of stability alone.

Under the Axiom of Choice, use the global measurable configuration space $\Omega$ of [[def-tdmath-poisson-configuration-reference]], consisting of locally finite simple configurations with sigma-algebra generated by bounded-Borel count maps. For $a\in\mathbb R^d$, let $T_a\omega(A)=\omega(A-a)$ be its measurable translation. A probability law $P$ on $\Omega$ is stationary exactly when $P\circ T_a^{-1}=P$ for every $a\in\mathbb R^d$. For a bounded Borel region $A$, equip its finite simple configuration set $\mathcal C_A$ with $\mathcal F_A^{\rm count}=\sigma(N_B:B\subseteq A\text{ Borel})$, without completing it. On this space $r_A(\omega)=\omega\cap A$ is measurable: each generating count satisfies $N_B\circ r_A=N_B$, a bounded-Borel global count. Write $P_A=P\circ r_A^{-1}$ on this exact count sigma-algebra for every stationary $P$. This assertion does not extend $P_A$ to the completed quotient sigma-algebra. The reference $Q_A=Q\circ r_A^{-1}$ is the restriction of the same unit-intensity law $Q$; by [[thm-tdmath-poisson-reference-count-properties]] and the count-generated restriction sigma-algebra, it equals the restriction to $\mathcal F_A^{\rm count}$ of the finite-sector law $e^{-|A|}\sum_{N\geq0}\mu_{A,N}$ of [[post-td-classical-indistinguishable-particle-measure]]. This count sigma-algebra is contained in the completed quotient sigma-algebra: in each sector, the pullback of $N_B$ is $\sum_{j=1}^N\mathbf1_B(x_j)$, a Borel function, so each count generator is quotient measurable. Thus the stated restriction of the positional measure is defined. Relative entropy and the restrictions below always use $\mathcal F_A^{\rm count}$, exactly as in the mathematical global supplier, rather than an unproved completion for arbitrary $P$. Indeed, a finite Borel partition of $A$ has multinomial counts under each fixed-$N$ Lebesgue sector, and summing these sector measures with the factor $e^{-|A|}$ gives independent Poisson counts of the corresponding volumes. For each bounded Borel $A$, write $D_A(P)=D(P_A\|Q_A)$ using [[def-tdmath-finite-region-relative-entropy]]: if $P_A\ll Q_A$ and $h_A=dP_A/dQ_A$, then $D_A(P)=\int h_A\log h_A\,dQ_A$ with $0\log0=0$; otherwise $D_A(P)=+\infty$. Let $C_0=[0,1)^d$ and $\Lambda_L=[0,L)^d$. Define $\rho(P)=E_PN_{C_0}$ and let $\mathcal P^1_{\rm stat}$ be the stationary laws with finite intensity $\rho(P)<\infty$. For $P\in\mathcal P^1_{\rm stat}$ for which both limits exist and are finite, define
$$
I(P)=\lim_{L\to\infty}\frac{D_{\Lambda_L}(P)}{|\Lambda_L|},\qquad \Phi(P)=\lim_{L\to\infty}\frac{E_P[H_{\Lambda_L}^{\mathrm{free}}]}{|\Lambda_L|}.
$$
Here $I(P)$ and $\rho(P)$ are dimensionless per dimensionless reference volume; the physical number density corresponding to $\rho(P)$ is $\rho(P)/\ell_0^d$ in $\mathrm{m}^{-d}$. The energy density $\Phi(P)$ is in joules per dimensionless reference volume; the corresponding energy density per physical $d$-volume is $\Phi(P)/\ell_0^d$ in $\mathrm{J\,m^{-d}}$.
For $\beta>0$, use the laws $P\in\mathcal P^1_{\rm stat}$ with finite $I(P)$ and $\Phi(P)$ and adopt, at the selected $(\varphi,\beta,z)$, that
$$\operatorname*{arg\,min}_{P}\{I(P)+\beta\Phi(P)-\rho(P)\log z\}=\{P^\ast\}.$$

For the $\beta=0$ branch, require a nonempty hard-core displacement set $B=\{x:\varphi(x)=+\infty\}$ and use [[def-td-retained-hard-core-zero-beta-preparation]]. A global configuration is hard-admissible when no pair of its distinct points has displacement in $B$. Restrict to $P\in\mathcal P^1_{\rm stat}$ assigning probability one to hard-admissible configurations and having finite $I(P)$; adopt, at the selected $z$, that
$$\operatorname*{arg\,min}_{P}\{I(P)-\rho(P)\log z\}=\{P^\ast\}.$$
These are two explicit parameter-specific physical model restrictions. They do not assert uniqueness for every activity or temperature, do not apply at coexistence when the minimizer set has several elements, and do not infer uniqueness from thermodynamic smoothness.
