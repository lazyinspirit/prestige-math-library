This page defines finite discrete statistical models and adopts canonical or microcanonical preparations as conditional model rules. It derives finite partition, energy, variance, entropy and free-energy identities; finite entropy maximization; independent-product factorization; a finite-bath error bound; and conditional countable and measurable identities. The lattice pressure and concentration results apply only to their explicitly bounded finite-range lattice model. Regular-shell preparation uses the normalized coarea law on a nonempty compact regular level under AC and does not require a globally defined flow; the separate invariant-shell theorem assumes a smooth invertible flow on the full band and does not imply ergodicity. Quantum results retain trace, spectral, moment and choice hypotheses; the finite-sector variational result, mode formulas and discrete Cesaro dephasing use inspected NRQM interfaces whose current acceptance evidence remains unresolved. Statistical free-energy values are not identified with macroscopic Legendre potentials without an additional bridge. None of the items reports an experiment or derives equilibration from a preparation postulate.
