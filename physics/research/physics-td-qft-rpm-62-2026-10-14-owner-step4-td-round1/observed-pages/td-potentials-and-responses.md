This page develops local thermodynamic potentials and response identities for the adopted nonrelativistic, simple-compressible framework. It works on explicit positive-temperature, C2 single-phase charts and uses whole-system SI quantities; singular response charts and phase boundaries are outside the derivative claims.

Legendre potentials are defined as local transforms only when their natural-coordinate maps have the stated inverse. Their exact differentials and Maxwell relations are then derived by calculus. The reservoir minimum and useful-work results are conditional on ideal fixed-temperature and fixed-pressure stores, the adopted energy bookkeeping, and the isolated-compound entropy postulates.

The Euler and Gibbs-Duhem identities additionally require same-tag degree-one homogeneity of the energy representation. The current tagged-scaling postulate does not identify the scaled tag with the original one, so that result is stated conditionally. The companion page gives a chart failure and a nonzero model work loop; neither is an experimental report.
