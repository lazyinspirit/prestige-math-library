These are constructed model calculations, not reported experiments. The first example shows that a smooth positive-temperature chart can fail to admit an isothermal pressure inverse. The second calculates a nonzero pressure-work integral around a closed quasistatic model path, so that work depends on the process.

The preceding companion [[td-representation-and-equilibrium-examples]] already records a tagged-scaling countermodel: [[cex-td-tagged-scaling-not-fixed-type-homogeneity]]. It is linked here as context for the same-tag homogeneity condition in [[pthm-td-euler-and-gibbs-duhem]], not as a logical supplier.
