This page develops classical interacting equilibrium preparations from an explicit positional pair-potential model. It fixes the SI reference length and energy conventions, measurable quotient and global Poisson configuration spaces, and exact periodic image-sum energy, along with stability and tail conditions, free, periodic, van Hove, and uniformly tempered exterior boundaries, and the finite-volume canonical, grand-canonical, and positional shell laws. The canonical positional law specializes the measurable-system preparation rule from the preceding finite-statistical page; infinite-energy configurations have zero positive-temperature weight. The positional shell is separately defined and is not a phase-space shell. The page distinguishes adopted preparation rules from conditional thermodynamic deductions and separates the singleton-minimizer hypothesis from phase coexistence. The pressure, canonical/grand duality, interval-energy local limit, exact-particle-number shrinking-shell limit, and boundary claims are stated conditionally on the exact Borel, regularity, density, differentiability, boundary, unit, and uniqueness hypotheses of their mathematical suppliers. The shell theorem concerns positions and pair energy only; it does not establish a full momentum-inclusive energy-shell theorem or dynamical thermalization.
