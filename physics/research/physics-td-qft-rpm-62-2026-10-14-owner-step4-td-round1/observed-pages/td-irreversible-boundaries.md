This page separates the adopted macroscopic endpoint entropy postulate from entropy quantities defined on finite microstates, probability distributions and quantum density operators. It records the assumptions required before discussing stochastic fluctuations, local equilibrium or transport. Those branches need their own state spaces, dynamics, constitutive inputs and empirical qualifications.
