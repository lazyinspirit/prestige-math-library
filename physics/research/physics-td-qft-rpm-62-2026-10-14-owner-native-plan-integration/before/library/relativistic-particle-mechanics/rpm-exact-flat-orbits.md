This page develops exact model calculations for stationary clocks and radial rays in the right Rindler wedge, followed by exact charged-particle trajectories in uniform electric, magnetic and subcritical crossed fields. The accelerated-clock and ray results are conditional on the adopted ideal apparatus models. The electromagnetic orbit results are conditional on the external-charge action and its force-law equivalence; all constant fields are supplied with smooth potentials. No self-field, radiation reaction, global Rindler extension, or empirical claim is included. The Rindler received-frequency ratio is an exact conditional consequence of the additional adopted local phase-reception model; finite-apparatus interpretation retains its local ray regime and short-observation limits, with no numerical accuracy or empirical validation claim.
