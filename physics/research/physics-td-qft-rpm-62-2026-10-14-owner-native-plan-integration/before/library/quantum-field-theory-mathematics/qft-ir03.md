This mathematical page defines regular polynomial field functionals by finite symmetric smooth compactly supported kernels, proves tensor-test density and coefficient uniqueness, and supplies the diagonal delta distribution used to state the local-functional boundary. It develops the regular distributional Wick contraction product and normal-ordering isomorphism. It defines formal time-ordered coefficients and proves coefficientwise causal factorization conditional on the inspected free scalar two-point, vacuum covariance, and causal-commutator interfaces. A separate bounded-operator result gives norm-convergent Dyson evolution under explicit strong-continuity and uniform-boundedness hypotheses. Local diagonal functionals and interacting continuum limits are outside the regular class. Draft supplier interfaces remain conditional; authoring readiness is not independent approval.
