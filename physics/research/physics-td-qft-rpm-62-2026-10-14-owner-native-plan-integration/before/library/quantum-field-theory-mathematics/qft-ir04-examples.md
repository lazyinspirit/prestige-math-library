For the locally integrable punctured kernel $|x|^{-\delta}$ with $\delta<n$, the ordinary integral is an extension and has the same scaling degree, so the point-extension classification makes it unique. The strict threshold matters: at $\delta=n$ the radial integral diverges logarithmically and the same-degree extension space admits a delta term.
