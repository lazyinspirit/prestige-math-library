Orbital angular momentum and central potentials 
Classically the angular momentum vector Ll is deﬁned as the cross-product of the position 
vector lr and the momentum vector lp: 
Ll = lr × lp . 
(1.1) 
In cartesian components, this equation reads 
Lx = ypz −zpy ,  
Ly = zpx −xpz , 
(1.2) 
Lz = xpy −ypx .
