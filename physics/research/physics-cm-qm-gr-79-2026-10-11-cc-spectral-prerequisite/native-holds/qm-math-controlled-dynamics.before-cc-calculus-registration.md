The controlled-dynamics layer of the non-relativistic quantum mechanics
mathematical prerequisites, entirely mathematical: the norm-continuous Dyson
theory of time-dependent bounded generators with its truncation bound, the
interaction picture and first-order amplitudes; the allowed-region WKB
coefficient system with its explicit error and the Airy/Liouville theory of
simple turning points with stable Volterra connections and barrier tunnelling;
the contour perturbation theory of simple and degenerate isolated eigenvalues
with explicit remainders; the finite-dimensional and common-domain (unbounded)
gapped adiabatic theorems; the exactly solvable driven oscillator propagator,
the Rabi model and the finite-time rotating-wave error; and a complete
short-range scattering branch: Cook's criterion, the free Gaussian propagator,
L^2 and compactly supported potentials, Mellin incoming/outgoing propagation,
compact scattering defects, asymptotic completeness, the Dollard-modified
Coulomb wave operators with channel completeness, the stationary outgoing
integral with flux and Born error control, partial-wave convergence and the
identification of the stationary and dynamical sectors.

Every item carries `domain: mathematics` and depends only on mathematical
suppliers from [[qm-math-oscillator]], [[qm-math-orbital-and-spin]],
[[qm-math-intervals-and-contacts]], [[qm-math-coulomb]], [[qm-math-composites]]
and published mathematics. The page opens with
[[thm-nrqm-controlled-dynamics-norm-dyson-series-and-uniqueness]] and its
truncation estimate, then splits into the approximation branches (WKB/Airy,
perturbation, adiabatic, rotating wave), the solvable models (oscillator, Rabi)
and the scattering branch; nothing here asserts a physical interpretation,
measurement model or empirical claim, and the companion page
[[qm-math-controlled-dynamics-examples]] stays mathematics as well.
