## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Countable Choice ([[def-countable-choice]]). Let $H$ be a
complex Hilbert space, let $H_0$ be a self-adjoint (possibly unbounded)
operator on $H$ with domain $D(H_0)$
([[def-symmetric-self-adjoint-and-essentially-self-adjoint]],
[[def-adjoint-of-a-densely-defined-unbounded-operator]]), let $\hbar>0$, let
$I=[a,b]$ be a compact interval, and let $V:I\to\mathcal B(H)$ be a map such
that each $V(t)$ is self-adjoint, $t\mapsto V(t)$ is strongly continuous, and
$v:=\sup_{t\in I}\|V(t)\|<\infty$ ([[def-bounded-linear-operator]],
[[def-operator-norm]]). Put $K:=v/\hbar$ and define the interaction-picture
potential
$$V_I(t):=e^{itH_0/\hbar}V(t)e^{-itH_0/\hbar}\in\mathcal B(H),$$
where $e^{-itH_0/\hbar}$ is the unitary group generated by $-iH_0/\hbar$
([[lem-self-adjoint-operator-generates-a-strongly-continuous-unitary-group]],
[[thm-nrqm-composites-real-multipliers-and-free-bounded-potential-operators]]).
Then:

(a) $V_I$ is strongly continuous on $I$ and $\|V_I(t)\|=\|V(t)\|\le v$ for all
$t\in I$.

(b) With $U_n^I(t,s):=(-i/\hbar)^n\int_{s<t_1<\cdots<t_n<t}V_I(t_1)\cdots
V_I(t_n)\,dt_1\cdots dt_n$ (strong Bochner integrals, $U_0^I:=I$), the series
$U_I(t,s):=\sum_{n\ge0}U_n^I(t,s)$ converges in operator norm, uniformly on
$\{s\le t\}\subseteq I\times I$, $\|U_n^I(t,s)\|\le(K(t-s))^n/n!$, and
$$\Bigl\|U_I(t,s)-\sum_{n=0}^{N}U_n^I(t,s)\Bigr\|\le e^{K(t-s)}\frac{(K(t-s))^{N+1}}{(N+1)!}.$$
The family $U_I$ is a strongly continuous unitary propagator on $I$:
$U_I(t,s)^*U_I(t,s)=U_I(t,s)U_I(t,s)^*=I$, $U_I(t,s)U_I(s,r)=U_I(t,r)$ for
$r\le s\le t$, and $U_I$ satisfies the strong integral identity
$$U_I(t,s)\psi=\psi-\frac{i}{\hbar}\int_s^tV_I(r)U_I(r,s)\psi\,dr\qquad(\psi\in H,\ s\le t),$$
the integral being the Bochner integral of the continuous $H$-valued function
$r\mapsto V_I(r)U_I(r,s)\psi$.

(c) The *physical propagator* $U(t,s):=e^{-itH_0/\hbar}U_I(t,s)e^{isH_0/\hbar}$
is unitary, strongly continuous on $\{s\le t\}$, satisfies
$U(t,s)U(s,r)=U(t,r)$, and satisfies the mild (Duhamel) identity
$$U(t,s)=e^{-i(t-s)H_0/\hbar}-\frac{i}{\hbar}\int_s^te^{-i(t-r)H_0/\hbar}V(r)U(r,s)\,dr,$$
where the integral is the strong Bochner integral of a strongly continuous
$\mathcal B(H)$-valued function.

(d) *Weak equation.* For all $s\le t$ in $I$, all $\psi\in D(H_0)$ and all
$\varphi\in D(H_0)$, the function $u(t):=\langle U(t,s)\psi,\varphi\rangle$ is
continuously differentiable and
$$u'(t)=-\frac{i}{\hbar}\Bigl[\langle U(t,s)\psi,H_0\varphi\rangle+\langle V(t)U(t,s)\psi,\varphi\rangle\Bigr].$$

No statement is made about invariance of $D(H_0)$ under $U(t,s)$ or about
differentiability of $t\mapsto U(t,s)\psi$ in $H$ for $\psi\in D(H_0)$; (d) is a
dual formulation only.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** the Hilbert space $H$, the self-adjoint $H_0$, the strongly
continuous self-adjoint-valued bounded map $V$, the numbers $\hbar,v,K$, and
reals $s\le t$ in $I$.

[F1] $U_0(t):=e^{-itH_0/\hbar}$ is a strongly continuous one-parameter unitary group with $U_0(t)D(H_0)\subseteq D(H_0)$, $U_0(t)H_0\psi=H_0U_0(t)\psi$ for $\psi\in D(H_0)$, and $\frac{d}{dt}\bigl(U_0(t)\psi,\varphi\bigr)=-\frac{i}{\hbar}(U_0(t)\psi,H_0\varphi)$ for $\psi\in D(H_0)$, $\varphi\in D(H_0)$ ([[lem-self-adjoint-operator-generates-a-strongly-continuous-unitary-group]]).

[F2] The Dyson construction [[thm-nrqm-controlled-dynamics-norm-dyson-series-and-uniqueness]] applies to any norm-continuous self-adjoint-valued bounded operator family on a compact interval; its statements are used here for the interaction-picture family once (a) shows the relevant strong-continuity estimates, and its proof only uses bounded operators, [F3], and the Bochner integral.

[F3] Bochner integrals of continuous Hilbert-space-valued functions on compact intervals exist and satisfy $\|\int f\|\le\int\|f\|$; strong continuity of $r\mapsto V_I(r)U_I(r,s)\psi$ follows from (a) and the norm continuity of $U_I$ proved below ([[thm-bochner-integrability-criterion]], [[lem-bochner-integral-norm-inequality]]).

[F4] $\|AB\|\le\|A\|\|B\|$, $\|U_0(t)\|=1$ and $\|V_I(t)\|=\|V(t)\|$ for all $t$ ([[def-operator-norm]], [[def-bounded-linear-operator]]).

## Proof

1.1 [Strong continuity of $V_I$] Let $t_n\to t$ in $I$ and $\psi\in H$. Since $U_0(\cdot)$ is strongly continuous, $U_0(t_n)\psi\to U_0(t)\psi$; since $V(t_n)\to V(t)$ strongly and $U_0(-t_n)\to U_0(-t)$ strongly on the bounded set $\{V(t)\psi:n\in\mathbb N\}\cup\{V(t)\psi\}$, the difference $V_I(t_n)\psi-V_I(t)\psi=U_0(t_n)\bigl[V(t_n)-V(t)\bigr]U_0(-t_n)\psi+U_0(t_n)V(t)\bigl[U_0(-t_n)-U_0(-t)\bigr]\psi+\bigl[U_0(t_n)-U_0(t)\bigr]V(t)U_0(-t)\psi$ tends to $0$. The norm identity is [F4]. [F1, F4, given]

2.1 [Dyson series in the interaction picture] By step 1.1 the integrands of $U_n^I$ are strongly continuous and uniformly bounded, so the iterated strong Bochner integrals exist by [F3] and define bounded operators with the same simplex bounds as [[thm-nrqm-controlled-dynamics-norm-dyson-series-and-uniqueness]]: the induction of step 1.1 of that item uses only $\|V_I(r)\|\le v$ and [F4]. Hence $\|U_n^I(t,s)\|\le(K(t-s))^n/n!$ and the series and truncation bound of (b) follow exactly as in that item and [[lem-nrqm-controlled-dynamics-dyson-truncation-bound]]. Unitarity, cocycle and the strong integral identity follow by the same uniqueness argument, applied to the strongly continuous $H$-valued function $r\mapsto U_I(r,s)\psi$: the difference of two solutions to the identity has squared-norm derivative zero, or alternatively one applies the operator identity to each $\psi$ and uses [F3]. [step 1.1, F2, F3, F4, given]

3.1 [The mild identity] Multiplying the identity of step 2.1 on the left by $e^{-itH_0/\hbar}$ and on the right by $e^{isH_0/\hbar}$ and using $e^{-itH_0/\hbar}V_I(r)e^{isH_0/\hbar}=e^{-i(t-r)H_0/\hbar}V(r)e^{-i(r-s)H_0/\hbar}$ gives, after substituting the definition of $U(r,s)$ in the integrand, $U(t,s)=e^{-i(t-s)H_0/\hbar}-\frac{i}{\hbar}\int_s^te^{-i(t-r)H_0/\hbar}V(r)U(r,s)\,dr$, which is (c); unitarity and the cocycle follow from step 2.1 because conjugation by the unitary groups preserves products, adjoints and norms. [step 2.1, F1, F4, algebra]

4.1 [The weak equation] Fix $\psi,\varphi\in D(H_0)$ and put $u(\tau):=(U(\tau,s)\psi,\varphi)$ for $\tau\in[s,t]$. Write $\eta(r):=V(r)U(r,s)\psi$ and $E(\sigma):=e^{-i\sigma H_0/\hbar}$, so that step 3.1 reads $u(\tau)=(E(\tau-s)\psi,\varphi)-\frac{i}{\hbar}\int_s^\tau(E(\tau-r)\eta(r),\varphi)dr$. By [F1], $\frac{d}{d\tau}(E(\tau-s)\psi,\varphi)=-\frac{i}{\hbar}(E(\tau-s)\psi,H_0\varphi)$, and for fixed $r$ the pairing $(E(\tau-r)\eta(r),\varphi)$ is differentiable in $\tau$ with derivative $-\frac{i}{\hbar}(E(\tau-r)\eta(r),H_0\varphi)$, as one sees by pairing $E(\sigma)\eta(r)$ against $\varphi\in D(H_0)$ and using $\frac{d}{d\sigma}(E(\sigma)\eta,\varphi)=-\frac{i}{\hbar}(E(\sigma)\eta,H_0\varphi)$. Hence, by the fundamental theorem of calculus for the Bochner integral [F3] applied to the continuous $\mathbb C$-valued integrands, $u'(\tau)=-\frac{i}{\hbar}(E(\tau-s)\psi,H_0\varphi)-\frac{i}{\hbar}\bigl[(V(\tau)U(\tau,s)\psi,\varphi)-\frac{i}{\hbar}\int_s^\tau(E(\tau-r)\eta(r),H_0\varphi)dr\bigr]$. Substituting the identity of step 3.1 into the $H_0\varphi$-pairing, $(U(\tau,s)\psi,H_0\varphi)=(E(\tau-s)\psi,H_0\varphi)-\frac{i}{\hbar}\int_s^\tau(E(\tau-r)\eta(r),H_0\varphi)dr$, and using $(-\frac{i}{\hbar})\cdot(-\frac{i}{\hbar})=-\frac{1}{\hbar^2}$, the displayed expression collapses to $u'(\tau)=-\frac{i}{\hbar}\bigl[(U(\tau,s)\psi,H_0\varphi)+(V(\tau)U(\tau,s)\psi,\varphi)\bigr]$, which is continuous in $\tau$. [step 3.1, F1, F3, algebra] ∎
