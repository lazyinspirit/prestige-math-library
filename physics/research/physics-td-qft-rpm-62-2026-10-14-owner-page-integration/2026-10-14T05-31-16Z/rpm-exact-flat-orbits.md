This page develops exact model calculations for stationary clocks and radial rays in the right Rindler wedge, followed by exact charged-particle trajectories in uniform electric, magnetic and subcritical crossed fields. The accelerated-clock and ray results are conditional on the adopted ideal apparatus models. The electromagnetic orbit results are conditional on the external-charge action and its force-law equivalence; all constant fields are supplied with smooth potentials. No self-field, radiation reaction, global Rindler extension, or empirical claim is included. The Rindler plane-wave frequency functional is calculated explicitly, while a physical accelerated-reception reading remains an owner-level model gap because the current plane-wave postulate excludes it.
