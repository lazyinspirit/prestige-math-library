The Fock-space and equilibrium layer of the non-relativistic quantum mechanics
mathematical prerequisites, entirely mathematical. The page begins with the
completed-tensor-power Fock space and its sector projections, the creation and
annihilation core operators with their contraction bounds, closed adjoint
domains and the number operator; then single-mode oscillator coefficients, the
fixed-mode number law and the single-mode thermal trace; then the
countable-mode product criterion and the fermionic and dilute grand-canonical
formulas with the finite Gibbs variational minimum and a divergent-entropy
example; and finally the fixed-particle branch: bounded fixed-$N$ operators,
the permutation-restricted and Coulomb self-adjointness of the fixed-$N$
Hamiltonian, the Slater direct and exchange calculation, the continuum
transverse free Fock Hamiltonian, the cutoff dipole relative bound, the
controlled Dyson series for vacuum emission and the golden-rule coefficient
approximate identity.

Every item carries `domain: mathematics` and depends only on mathematical
suppliers from [[qm-math-composites]], [[qm-math-oscillator]],
[[qm-math-orbital-and-spin]], [[qm-math-coulomb]],
[[qm-math-controlled-dynamics]] and published mathematics. Physical-sounding
objects such as the transverse mode space, the dipole matrices, the coupling
$\eta_0$, the cutoff $\Lambda$ and the spectral transition density $J_b$ are
defined as mathematical model data with declared hypotheses; no measurement
postulate, statistical inference or empirical claim is made, and the scope
paragraphs of the underlying items record where a physical identification
would require a separate assumption. The companion page
[[qm-math-fock-and-equilibrium-examples]] stays mathematics as well.
