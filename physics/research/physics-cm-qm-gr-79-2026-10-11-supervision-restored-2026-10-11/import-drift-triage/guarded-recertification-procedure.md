## Native semantics that must be preserved

`recordStep1`. `loadStep3`, `itemHash`, and `itemInputPaths` are exported by
`tools/physics-support/frontier-snapshot.mjs`, not by step1-decisions.

A readiness row is not just a carrier hash. `itemHash(s,id,row.dependencies)`
hashes the sorted transitive closure of current/planned item declarations,
verification-excluded mathematical metadata, complete carrier bodies, and
page identity/order/requires. It follows deps, justified_by and forward_refs
plus the row's explicitly examined dependencies. Missing suppliers are
hash inputs too. Alias resolution may require the entire item inventory.
Thus the 1135-consumer deps/justified_by cone is an initial selection, not
necessarily the complete set of rows whose native readiness hashes change.

original dependency list and the exact helper; include any additional row
actually reaching one of the six imports, without widening content edits.

`step1Decision` returns an escalated row as open **before** checking its hash.
For a ready row it tests the actual transitive hash. `recordStep1` computes a
new hash and timestamp and writes a new row; it does not prove mathematics.
It rejects non-owner resolution of an owner row and non-owner overwriting of
an unchanged closed ready row. Use owner authority for the approved guarded
currency operation while preserving the existing owner boolean. Do not infer
permission to turn escalated rows into ready from the ability to hash them.
