# Physics workspace agent instructions

Read ../CLAUDE.md fully for the general integrity and supervision rules. Within
this workspace, use SCHEMA.md and WORKFLOW.md; tools/physics-autopilot owns all
transitions. Root mathematics tools and briefs are unchanged and are not physics
authoring instructions. Use this workspace as cwd and keep all authored content,
plans, tasks, evidence, and state here.

Never edit outside this workspace or change imported mathematical snapshots.
Read research/math-imports.json to identify imports. Missing mathematical
prerequisites must be developed locally with domain: mathematics and purely
mathematical dependencies, or escalated for a separate mathematical build.
Never repair a published original mathematical supplier from a physics run.

Apply the class-specific requirements in SCHEMA.md at every authoring, reader,
refuter, adjudication, judgment, and repair stage. Thought experiments have exactly
the same proof obligations as physical theorems. Postulates and experiments need
source and scope review, not invented deductive proofs. Never invent measurements,
full-text retrieval, audits, or certainty. Carry empirical uncertainties and
physical applicability conditions into every consuming physical conclusion.

A physical item's declared scope and empirical qualifications form part of its
public claim. Postulate formulations and experiment setup, procedure, observations,
uncertainty, and interpretation are also public interfaces. Changes to them require
direct-consumer review under the general statement-change rule. Verification stamps,
citations, and proof-only changes alone do not propagate. Mathematical items retain
the original Statement/Definition interface rule.

Before authoring, judging, or adjudicating statistical physical claims, read the
statistical-evidence and double-slit section of ../PHYSICS-CONTENT-MODEL.md.
Experiments support or constrain specified models; finite data do not prove a
framework. Review confidence is not certainty about nature.
