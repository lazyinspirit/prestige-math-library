## Required mathematical rigor and explicit definitions

This rule applies to all physics frameworks, not only general relativity.
Be as mathematically rigorous and explicit as possible. Read “Mathematical rigor
and explicit definitions” in ../PHYSICS-CONTENT-MODEL.md. Provide explicit
mathematical definitions before using technical physical terms, with the objects,
spaces, maps, regularity, hypotheses, and conventions specified. Define locally
or use an exact earlier supplier and make its relevant definition explicit; do
not substitute terminology or a textbook citation for a definition. Reuse
established definitions without unnecessary repetition.

In particular, define reference frames and worldlines using the differential
geometry appropriate to the framework: specify spacetime structure, curve domain,
regularity, parameter and causal conditions where applicable, and whether a frame
means an adapted chart, observer congruence, or basis/tetrad field. Do not conflate
these notions or import relativistic assumptions into a nonrelativistic model.
Scaffolders and authors must supply missing mathematics locally or flag the exact
open prerequisite. Reviewers, judges, and adjudicators must check these definitions
and reject concealed prerequisite gaps or deductions resting on undefined terms.
Preserve the distinction between mathematical precision and empirical certainty.
