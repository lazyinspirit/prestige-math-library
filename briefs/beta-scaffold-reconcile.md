# Step 1 — scaffold and prerequisite repair

Read CLAUDE.md, SCHEMA.md, WORKFLOW.md, briefs/beta-scaffold.md, the repair
packet, current batch notes, affected prose designs and canonical plan.
The engine has drained the Step-1 writers. You are the sole repair writer.
This task extends the ordinary batch write scope only as specified below.
You do mathematical repair and dependency reconciliation, not orchestration.

## Work

Process every live finding in the packet, one at a time. Read the exact claim
and its actual prerequisite statements and proofs. Search the web and read
complete authoritative sources for unfamiliar mathematics. Repair locally
with every necessary definition, lemma and backward dependency. Never delete
or weaken useful mathematics merely to pass a check. Preserve explicit AC
contracts and uses; never consume Recorded, Not Proved Here.

For unavailable sources, follow the full recovery and alternative-proof rules
in briefs/beta-scaffold.md. Reuse genuine prior attempts. An unresolved source
does not excuse stopping work on other findings. Do not manufacture certainty,
fetch receipts or source dispositions. Leave uncertain mathematics blocked.

Reconcile dependencies across batches and categories. When local closure needs
a new prerequisite A/B pair, add its complete inventory, placement, hypotheses,
sources and prerequisite edges to the affected prose designs and canonical
plan; add it to Phase 2 and its authoritative scope counts when this is a
Phase-2 run. Reuse existing suppliers and IDs. Record exact supplier IDs,
build/publication states and published-consumer repair strategies in the
existing published-consumer-supplier-ledger.md, maintaining its classifications
and counts. A planned or scaffolded supplier is not a published theorem.

## Write scope and limits

You may edit the run's batch manifests, coverage and notes, affected
research/plan-*.md designs, research/plan-spec.json, Phase-2 scope files, and
research/published-consumer-supplier-ledger.md. Keep these files consistent.
Maintain affected cross-batch dependency inputs and their merged ledger using
briefs/tasks/frontier-dependency-ledger.md. Preserve unrelated edits.

Do not edit items/, library/, tools/, briefs/, workflow state, scope-baseline
ledgers, frozen audit evidence or verdicts. Do not run engine controls, start
agents/builds, publish, push, change models, waive gates, or grant yourself
authority. The engine owns every dispatch and transition; Step 3 independently
reviews the repaired mathematics.

Preserve every selected pair and batch identity. Adding a future prerequisite
to Phase 2 does not authorize adding it to the current run or starting a new
frontier. If a prerequisite cannot be proved locally and needs an outside-run
build, record the exact supplier chain and scope decision needed; keep the
consumer blocked. Do not fake completion by registering an empty supplier.

Honor liveItems and exhaustedItems in the packet. Exhausted subjects are not
fresh repair assignments even if their text appears in diagnostics. An unowned
published defect belongs in the ledger, not an unauthorized published edit.
For detector bugs or insufficient authority, record the exact blocker and
continue with the remaining live findings.

## Evidence and checks

For each finding record: gate and subject, evidence read, disposition, changed
files, exact dependencies, checks and remaining uncertainty. Update the owning
batch notes and the packet's report. Keep historical receipts clearly dated.

Run affected coverage and source checks, whole-run manifest-deps and manifest
content policy, canonical plan validation, and a plan overlay containing the
changed manifests. Check scope integrity and the Foundations boundary. A
failing overlay or unfinished proof remains blocked. Worker exit is not stage
acceptance; the engine reruns the complete gate battery.
