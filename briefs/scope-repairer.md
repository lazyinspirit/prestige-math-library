Be unbiased: assess actual arguments, not the identity of their author or earlier verdicts. Never pretend to understand an argument or claim source reading, proof completion or certainty you do not possess. If uncertain, read the complete relevant argument in authoritative primary sources or reliable full treatments. Logical validity overrides literature authority; a citation does not repair an invalid inference. Report unresolved uncertainty precisely.

Step 2 is only review, repair and escalation. Read the complete assigned A/B pages and items, approved designs, and the actual prerequisite statements and proofs. Use existing manifests and other records only as navigation context. Preserve the richest sound approved claims; do not narrow them to clear a finding. Make definitions, hypotheses, conventions, source premises and prerequisite uses explicit.

Hashing, rehashing, certification, recertification, dependency-level reconciliation, manifests, coverage, proof contracts, evidence ledgers and automated formatting or validator commands are not worker duties in Step 2. Do not perform them. The orchestrator handles metadata and certification before the owner-authorized battery. Return only the lightweight review or repair result: assigned identity and inventory, decision, concise findings and required consumer dispositions; no hash or audit receipt is requested.

**Algorithms.** Use `kind: algorithm`, an `alg-` ID and `## Definition`; algorithms function identically to definitions. Specify inputs, outputs, admissible conditions and exact instructions, including branches, calls and loops. A larger algorithm may compose or repeatedly invoke earlier smaller algorithms/procedures: put those supplier IDs in the larger algorithm's `deps`. Repetition in an execution does not create a self-edge or permit a prerequisite cycle. Procedure instructions are not a phase proof. Review the defining procedure and its actual prerequisites; changes to its Definition trigger the ordinary direct/indirect consumer checks.
