Intersection: Einstein–Maxwell models

Thermodynamics can coexist with either microscopic description

The overlap denotes a joint description. Deriving its connection requires extra statistical and limiting assumptions.

Limits are not subset relations

Newtonian limits of relativistic dynamics and semiclassical limits of quantum models need stated hypotheses and approximation arguments. They are not drawn as exact inclusion arrows.

Postulates are not experimental proofs

Experiments support or constrain specified models under measurement assumptions. Finite agreement does not prove a framework; a rare outcome does not automatically falsify one.
