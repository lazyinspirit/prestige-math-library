## Statement

Let $N\geq0$ be an integer and let $\mathcal H=\bigoplus_{J=0}^N V_J$ be a nonzero finite-dimensional complex Hilbert space, with orthogonal projections $P_J$ onto its mutually orthogonal summands. Fix real numbers $\eta_J$, a Hermitian operator $D$ preserving each block, and a finite family $L_1,\ldots,L_m$ of linear operators on $\mathcal H$, allowing $m=0$. Suppose $L_\delta P_J=P_{J-1}L_\delta P_J$ for $J\geq1$ and $L_\delta P_0=0$. For each real parameter $\omega_0$, put $H_0(\omega_0)=\omega_0\sum_J\eta_JP_J$. In the finite GKSL channel $\mathcal N_{t,\omega_0}$ generated by $H_0(\omega_0)+D$ and these jumps, in the conventions $\hbar=1$, the restriction to the algebra $\mathcal A=\{X:X=\sum_JP_JXP_J\}$ is independent of $\omega_0$ at every fixed time $t\geq0$ when $D,L_\delta,\eta_J$ are held fixed. This is not a claim about all inter-block coherences.

In particular, this holds for density operators initially supported in a single block $V_J$. More generally, let $R$ be a nonzero finite-dimensional complex reference Hilbert space, and let $Y$ be an operator on $\mathcal H\otimes R$ satisfying $Y=(P_J\otimes I_R)Y(P_J\otimes I_R)$ for some $J$. Then $(\mathcal N_{t,\omega_0}\otimes\operatorname{id}_{\operatorname{End}(R)})(Y)$ is independent of $\omega_0$. This includes density operators entangled with the reference; the support condition means that their support is contained in $V_J\otimes R$.

## Facts & Assumptions

[F1] For a nonzero finite matrix space, a Hermitian Hamiltonian and a finite jump family, the finite GKSL channel at $t\geq0$ is the operator-norm convergent exponential of $\mathcal L(X)=-i[H,X]+\sum_\delta(L_\delta XL_\delta^\dagger-\{L_\delta^\dagger L_\delta,X\}/2)$. [def-qec-prereq-finite-gksl-channel-interface](item-def-qec-prereq-finite-gksl-channel-interface)

[F2] A density operator is a nonnegative endomorphism of trace one; its support is the orthogonal complement of its kernel. [math:def-qc-density-operator](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/def-qc-density-operator)

[F3] Tensor products of finite families of complex-linear maps are the unique linear maps acting factorwise on elementary tensors. On the tensor product of the operator spaces, $(\mathcal N\otimes\operatorname{id})(X\otimes B)=\mathcal N(X)\otimes B$. [math:def-qc-tensor-map](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/def-qc-tensor-map)

[F4] For finite-dimensional complex inner-product spaces with ordered orthonormal bases, the tensor products of their operator matrix units form a basis of the endomorphisms of the tensor-product space. The matrix-unit maps are linear in the root convention that the inner product is linear in its first argument. [math:lem-qc-tensor-products-of-operator-matrix-units-form-a-basis](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/lem-qc-tensor-products-of-operator-matrix-units-form-a-basis)

[F5] The finite tensor product of complex inner-product spaces carries the product inner product, linear in the first argument, characterized by $\langle x\otimes r,y\otimes s\rangle=\langle x,y\rangle\langle r,s\rangle$. Its ordered product basis is orthonormal, and this pairing is independent of the chosen orthonormal factor bases. [math:def-qc-tensor-register-and-permutation](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/def-qc-tensor-register-and-permutation)

## Proof

**Given:** The finite blocks, real energy parameters, fixed Hermitian block-preserving lift, finite fixed jump family and nonnegative time. **Goal:** Show independence on the block-diagonal algebra and on its stated reference extensions.

1.1 The orthogonal projections are Hermitian, so real $\omega_0,\eta_J$ make $H_0(\omega_0)+D$ Hermitian, as required by [F1]. If $X\in\mathcal A$, then $[H_0(\omega_0),X]=0$ because $H_0(\omega_0)$ is scalar on each block, and $[D,X]\in\mathcal A$. Write $T_{\delta J}=L_\delta P_J$. For distinct $J,K\geq1$, the ranges of $T_{\delta J}$ and $T_{\delta K}$ lie in distinct orthogonal destination blocks, so $T_{\delta J}^\dagger T_{\delta K}=0$; terms with $J=0$ or $K=0$ vanish as well. Hence $L_\delta^\dagger L_\delta=\sum_J T_{\delta J}^\dagger T_{\delta J}$ is block diagonal. Also $L_\delta XL_\delta^\dagger=\sum_J T_{\delta J}(P_JXP_J)T_{\delta J}^\dagger$ is block diagonal. Thus the anticommutator and every other term of the generator preserve $\mathcal A$. [given, F1, algebra]

1.2 For the reference assertion, use the product inner product of [F5] on $\mathcal H\otimes R$ and choose ordered orthonormal bases $u_1,\ldots,u_d$ of $\mathcal H$ and $e_1,\ldots,e_r$ of $R$, with matrix units $U_{ij}u_k=\delta_{jk}u_i$ and $E_{ab}e_c=\delta_{bc}e_a$. By [F4], every operator $Y$ on $\mathcal H\otimes R$ has a unique expansion $Y=\sum_{i,j,a,b}c_{ijab}U_{ij}\otimes E_{ab}$. Grouping coefficients gives $Y=\sum_{a,b}X_{ab}\otimes E_{ab}$ with $X_{ab}=\sum_{i,j}c_{ijab}U_{ij}$, uniquely. This also establishes the product-basis identification used in the Statement: the tensor products of the matrix-unit bases of the two operator spaces are sent to the basis in [F4] by the factorwise operator action. Under this identification, [F3] gives a well-defined extension on all operators, with $(\mathcal N_{t,\omega_0}\otimes\operatorname{id})(Y)=\sum_{a,b}\mathcal N_{t,\omega_0}(X_{ab})\otimes E_{ab}$. Its factorwise rule determines it uniquely, so changing the chosen orthonormal bases does not change the map. [given, F3, F4, F5, construct, algebra]

2.1 The restricted generator on the invariant algebra $\mathcal A$ is exactly the same linear map for every $\omega_0$, since the only parameter-dependent term vanishes there. Induction on the power shows that every power of the generator has the same restriction to $\mathcal A$ for every $\omega_0$. Applying the norm-convergent exponential series of [F1] proves the claimed equality of the restricted channels at fixed $t\geq0$. [given, F1, step 1.1]

3.1 For a density operator $\rho$, the condition $\operatorname{supp}(\rho)\subseteq V_J$ is equivalent to $\rho=P_J\rho P_J$: by [F2] and self-adjointness, containment of the support implies that $V_J^\perp$ is in the kernel and that the range is in $V_J$; conversely the displayed identity puts $V_J^\perp$ in the kernel. Thus such a $\rho$ belongs to $\mathcal A$. For the reference assertion, the support identity in the Statement and uniqueness of the expansion in step 1.2 imply $X_{ab}=P_JX_{ab}P_J$ for every $a,b$: multiplying $\sum_{a,b}X_{ab}\otimes E_{ab}$ on both sides by $P_J\otimes I_R$ replaces each coefficient by $P_JX_{ab}P_J$. Each extended-output coefficient $\mathcal N_{t,\omega_0}(X_{ab})$ is therefore independent of $\omega_0$ by step 2.1. By [F5] and the factorwise action in [F3], $Q_J=P_J\otimes I_R$ satisfies $Q_J^2=Q_J$ and $\langle Q_Jv,w\rangle=\langle v,Q_Jw\rangle$, first on elementary tensors and then on all vectors by sesquilinearity; its range is $V_J\otimes R$. Thus $Q_J$ is the orthogonal projection onto that subspace, and the same support-kernel argument applies to density operators on $\mathcal H\otimes R$. [given, F1, F2, F3, F4, F5, step 2.1, step 1.2, algebra]

4.1 The subsequent-recovery consequence uses only functions: let $E$ be either output operator space, $S$ a fixed set, and $Q:E\to S$ any fixed function. For any fixed-input output $Z_{\omega_0}\in E$ proved independent above, equality of the outputs implies equality of $Q(Z_{\omega_0})$. More generally put $T_{\omega_0}=\mathcal N_{t,\omega_0}|_{\mathcal A}$, a complex-linear map from $\mathcal A$ to itself by steps 1.1 and 2.1. Let $\Phi$ be a fixed function assigning to every complex-linear map $\mathcal A\to\mathcal A$ a function $E\to S$. Since $T_{\omega_0}=T_{\omega_0\prime}$, we have $\Phi(T_{\omega_0})=\Phi(T_{\omega_0\prime})$ and therefore $\Phi(T_{\omega_0})(Z_{\omega_0})=\Phi(T_{\omega_0\prime})(Z_{\omega_0\prime})$. A fixed subsequent recovery, or one selected solely by such a fixed rule from the restricted channel, has exactly this function-composition property. No positivity or other channel property of the postprocessing function is needed for this consequence. [step 1.1, step 1.2, step 2.1, step 3.1, algebra] ∎
