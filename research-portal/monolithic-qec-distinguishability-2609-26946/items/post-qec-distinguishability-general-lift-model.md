## Postulate

Work in units $\hbar=1$. Take the finite integer-spin benchmark with blocks $J=0,\ldots,N$, $N\geq1$, and orthonormal magnetic basis $|J,m\rangle$ for $m=-J,\ldots,J$. Adopt the physical and spectral hypotheses, including the three choice assumptions, carried by [[post-qec-distinguishability-equal-rate-model]]. Let $\omega_0>0$, $\gamma>0$, and let $\Delta(J,m)$ be real on these basis labels. Postulate the Hamiltonian $H=\sum_{J=0}^{N}\sum_{m=-J}^{J}[\omega_0J(J+1)+\Delta(J,m)]|J,m\rangle\langle J,m|$.

Postulate that the same three benchmark downward jump matrices continue to describe the lifted model: $L_\delta=\sqrt\gamma\sum_{J=1}^{N}\sum_{m=-J}^{J}\sqrt{(2J+1)/(2J-1)}\,C^{J-1,m+\delta}_{1,\delta;J,m}|J-1,m+\delta\rangle\langle J,m|$ for $\delta=-1,0,1$, with out-of-range terms zero. Here $C^{J-1,m+\delta}_{1,\delta;J,m}$ is the product-basis coefficient of the specified normalized coupled spin-$(J-1)$ basis in $V_1\otimes V_J$. The lifted-model use of these matrices is a separate phenomenological assumption: their derivation for unsplit levels alone does not establish it after lifting. Evolution is the finite GKSL channel generated by this $H$ and these jumps.

The main example takes $\Delta(J,m)=\Delta_0Jm$ for real lifting strength $\Delta_0$. For the short-checking regime, with interval $\tau\geq0$, assume $\gamma\tau\ll1$ and $D\tau\ll1$, where $D=\max|\Delta(J,m)|$ over the occupied labels relevant to the interval. The notation $x\ll1$ specifies a small-parameter regime rather than a numerical error bound; if $D=0$, its condition is vacuous. For a code hosted in $J_0\geq1$, the main lift has maximum $|\Delta_0|J_0^2$ on the full host manifold, giving the sufficient condition $|\Delta_0|J_0^2\tau\ll1$ there. Downward reachable manifolds have no larger maximum.

The article imposes no separate $\omega_0\tau\ll1$ condition in its single-manifold phase discussion: the rigid-rotor term is scalar on each fixed-$J$ manifold. This observation does not assert that a laboratory-frame first-order expansion of $e^{-iH\tau}$ is uniform for arbitrary $\omega_0\tau$, or that rotor phases are global across different $J$ manifolds.

## Justification

**Given:** The finite model and the postulated Hamiltonian, jump matrices and checking regime above.

**Goal:** Explain the finite channel construction, the displayed host-manifold lift bound and the scope of the rotor-phase statement.

1.1 The real diagonal entries make $H$ Hermitian, and the three displayed jumps are finite matrices. Thus the finite construction in [F3] applies. The benchmark and coefficient conventions in [F1] and [F2] identify the matrices being postulated; they do not derive their continued physical validity for the lifted Hamiltonian. [F1, F2, F3, A1]

1.2 For $|m|\leq J_0$, $|\Delta_0J_0m|\leq|\Delta_0|J_0^2$, with equality at $m=\pm J_0$. The same maximum for any lower nonnegative integer $J$ is at most this value. On a fixed block the rotor term has the same eigenvalue $\omega_0J(J+1)$ for every $m$; its contribution cancels in the diagonal-entry difference governing conjugation of a matrix unit within that block by [F4]. Different blocks can have different scalar phases. These calculations establish the stated bounds and the limited phase claim. [F4, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
