## Statement

Adopt the article's finite equal-rate, zero-temperature downward-jump benchmark, with $\gamma>0$ held fixed as the lift $\Delta$ varies. In this effective model the generator uses $H_S+H_\Delta$ and retains the unperturbed $L_\delta$ of Eq. (10), as explicitly specified after Eq. (11). Assume the full physical hypotheses of the unlifted downward-jump supplier and the inherited choice assumptions. Restrict the input to the single-$J=1$ code $a=|1,-1\rangle$, $b=|1,1\rangle$.

A decay leaves $J=1$ and is flagged, but both codewords decay to $g=|0,0\rangle$. The article describes these paths as emission of photons of opposite polarizations; this description is retained as attributed physical model input. The decay branch cannot be corrected, and for any finite $t>0$ the accumulated channel is not exactly correctable on this two-dimensional code. For the complementary record associated with the three Kraus maps displayed in the proof, the normalized decay-sector records of the two codewords are orthogonal for every finite $t>0$, including at $\Delta=0$. For the channel-specific transpose recovery, the recovered logical channel is $\mathcal E_t(X)=pX+(1-p)\operatorname{Tr}(X)I_2/2$, where $p=e^{-\gamma t}$. Consequently its entanglement fidelity is $(1+3e^{-\gamma t})/4$ and its checking time at any fixed target is independent of $\Delta$.

## Facts & Assumptions

[F1] The minimal code has orthonormal logical basis $a=|1,-1\rangle$, $b=|1,1\rangle$ and encoding $V|0\rangle=a$, $V|1\rangle=b$. [[def-qec-distinguishability-minimal-code]]

[F2] Under its complete finite-block, tensor, bath, preparation, regularity and spectral hypotheses, the unlifted downward jumps send $|J,m\rangle$ only to $|J-1,m+\delta\rangle$ in channel $\delta$, and annihilate the ground $g=|0,0\rangle$. [[pthm-qec-distinguishability-atomic-jumps]]

[F3] The lift is diagonal: $H_\Delta|J,m\rangle=\Delta Jm|J,m\rangle$. [[post-qec-distinguishability-atomic-lift]]

[F4] Use the effective equal-rate benchmark with $\gamma>0$, $f(J)=\sqrt{(2J+1)/(2J-1)}$ and $\eta_J=J(J+1)$. Under the same finite downward-jump hypotheses, the scalar total-decay theorem gives $\sum_\delta L_\delta^\dagger L_\delta=\gamma\sum_{J=1}^{N}\sum_{m=-J}^{J}|J,m\rangle\langle J,m|$. Thus every excited basis state has total downward rate $\gamma$, while the ground has rate zero. The parameter postulate supplies the coefficients; the theorem supplies this rate identity. [[post-qec-distinguishability-equal-rate-model]] [[pthm-qec-distinguishability-uniform-decay-rate]]

[F5] The finite channel is the exponential of $\mathcal L(X)=-i[H,X]+\sum_\delta(L_\delta X L_\delta^\dagger-\{L_\delta^\dagger L_\delta,X\}/2)$. [[def-qec-prereq-finite-gksl-channel-interface]]

[F6] The code-restricted noise is $\mathcal N_t\circ\operatorname{Ad}_V$, and exact correction requires a decoder whose composition with this map is the identity on all logical operators. [[def-qec-prereq-restricted-logical-channel]]

[F7] For a chosen Kraus family $A_j$, the abstract complementary record has entries $\operatorname{Tr}(A_jXA_k^\dagger)$ in an orthonormal label basis $\{|e_j\rangle\}$. The choice of labels and basis is part of this mathematical representation. [[def-qec-prereq-complementary-error-record]]

[F8] A channel on a code of dimension at least two that has a nonzero rank-one Kraus map with nonzero input projection on the code is not exactly correctable. [[lem-qec-prereq-rank-one-kl-dimension]]

[F9] The support-normalized transpose recovery has Kraus maps $P K_j^\dagger\mathcal N(P)^{-1/2}$; completion off the support does not affect recovered code inputs. [[def-qec-distinguishability-transpose-recovery]]

[F10] For a logical qubit channel $\mathcal E$, its entanglement fidelity is $\langle\Phi|({\rm id}\otimes\mathcal E)(|\Phi\rangle\langle\Phi|)|\Phi\rangle$ with $|\Phi\rangle=(|00\rangle+|11\rangle)/\sqrt2$. [[def-qec-prereq-entanglement-fidelity]]

[F11] At target $F_0\in(0,1]$ the transpose checking time is $\inf\{t\ge0:F_{\rm tr}(t)<F_0\}$, with $\inf\varnothing=+\infty$. [[def-qec-distinguishability-checking-time]]

[F12] The finite operator exponential is differentiable, with inverse at negative time and derivative given by the generator. [[math:lem-exponential-series-of-a-bounded-operator]]

[F13] Assume the Axiom of Choice inherited from the physical and probability suppliers. [[math:def-axiom-of-choice]]

[F14] Carry the Countable Choice assumption of the regulated-bath and waiting-law interfaces. [[math:def-countable-choice]]

## Proof

**Given:** The fixed-jump effective benchmark, the minimal code, all inherited physical and choice assumptions, and elapsed time $t\ge0$.

**Goal:** Establish flagged but uncorrectable decay, compute the chosen abstract complementary record and prove the transpose checking-time independence.

1.1 From [F2], the only nonzero decay on $a$ has $\delta=+1$, since the output magnetic label must be zero; the only nonzero decay on $b$ has $\delta=-1$. By the total-decay identity in [F4] and these unique allowed channels, write $L_{+1}a=\alpha g$, $L_{-1}b=\beta g$, with $|\alpha|^2=|\beta|^2=\gamma$; all other actions on $a,b$, and all jump actions on $g$, vanish. On the invariant subspace $\operatorname{span}\{a,b,g\}$ the summed jump term is $\gamma\operatorname{Tr}(X)|g\rangle\langle g|$ for $X$ supported on the code, and $\sum_\delta L_\delta^\dagger L_\delta=\gamma P$ there, where $P=|a\rangle\langle a|+|b\rangle\langle b|$. The energies of $a,b$ are $2\omega_0-\Delta$ and $2\omega_0+\Delta$. These formulas remain the modeled jump actions as $\Delta$ changes by [A1]. [F1, F2, F3, F4, A1, algebra]

2.1 On the logical basis put $U_t=\operatorname{diag}(e^{i\Delta t},e^{-i\Delta t})$, $p=e^{-\gamma t}$ and $q=1-p$. The overall phase $e^{-2i\omega_0t}$ cancels on density matrices. Substitution of the actions in step 1.1 into [F5] gives the exact code-restricted channel $$\mathcal N_t(VXV^\dagger)=pVU_tXU_t^\dagger V^\dagger+q\operatorname{Tr}(X)|g\rangle\langle g|.$$ Indeed the code block solves $\dot Z=-i[H|_C,Z]-\gamma Z$, its trace is $p\operatorname{Tr}(X)$, and the ground population has derivative $\gamma p\operatorname{Tr}(X)$ and initial value zero. All code-ground coherences remain zero. These equations have the displayed solution: the difference of any two solutions of the finite linear equation is annihilated after multiplication by $e^{-t\mathcal L}$, by [F12]. Thus the formula is the specified exponential channel, rather than a first-order jump approximation. [F5, F6, F12, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 A finite Kraus representation of this restricted channel $\mathcal L(\mathbb C^2)\to\mathcal L(H_P)$ is $A_0=\sqrt p VU_t$, $A_1=\sqrt q|g\rangle\langle0|$, $A_2=\sqrt q|g\rangle\langle1|$. It is trace-preserving because $\sum_jA_j^\dagger A_j=I_2$. The ground is orthogonal to $J=1$, so a projection onto $J=1$ versus the ground flags a decay. Given a decay, every input density matrix has the same output $|g\rangle\langle g|$, so no channel acting on the emitter alone can recover both distinct basis inputs. For $t>0$, $q>0$ and $A_1$ is a nonzero rank-one Kraus operator on the two-dimensional logical input, so [F8] also rules out exact recovery of the full finite-time channel. [F1, F6, F8, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 For the family $A_0,A_1,A_2$ in step 3.1, use the orthonormal environment labels $|e_0\rangle,|e_1\rangle,|e_2\rangle$ of [F7]. For $t>0$, the two logical basis inputs have complementary records $p|e_0\rangle\langle e_0|+q|e_1\rangle\langle e_1|$ and $p|e_0\rangle\langle e_0|+q|e_2\rangle\langle e_2|$, respectively: the cross terms with $A_0$ vanish because the code and ground are orthogonal, and the other cross terms vanish on these basis inputs. Projecting onto $Q=|e_1\rangle\langle e_1|+|e_2\rangle\langle e_2|$ has probability $q>0$ for either input. Dividing the projected records by $q$ gives the orthogonal pure states $|e_1\rangle\langle e_1|$ and $|e_2\rangle\langle e_2|$. Thus measurement in this chosen label basis distinguishes the codewords perfectly conditional on the decay sector, including at zero lift. The shared no-decay label gives no such discrimination. These conclusions concern the abstract complementary record of [F7]; they do not identify its label measurement with a physical photon-polarization measurement. [F1, F7, step 2.1, step 3.1, algebra]

4.2 The transpose construction may be computed for the restricted channel using $A_j$ and logical code projector $I_2$: this is the decoded physical transpose, since for any physical Kraus family $K_j$ its restriction is $K_jV$ and $V^\dagger P K_j^\dagger=V^\dagger K_j^\dagger$. Put $D=\sum_jA_jA_j^\dagger=pP+2q|g\rangle\langle g|$. For $t>0$, its support inverse square root is $p^{-1/2}P+(2q)^{-1/2}|g\rangle\langle g|$. The recovery maps $A_j^\dagger D^{-1/2}$ are consequently $U_t^\dagger V^\dagger P$, $|0\rangle\langle g|/\sqrt2$, and $|1\rangle\langle g|/\sqrt2$. Applying them to step 2.1 yields $\mathcal E_t(X)=pX+q\operatorname{Tr}(X)I_2/2$. At $t=0$ the ground is outside the output support; the support convention of [F9] gives the same formula with $q=0$. Any off-support completion is irrelevant on the code noise output by [F9]. [F6, F9, step 2.1, step 3.1, algebra]

5.1 Under [F10], the identity channel contributes entanglement fidelity one, while the replacement channel $X\mapsto\operatorname{Tr}(X)I_2/2$ sends the maximally entangled projector to $I_2\otimes I_2/4$, whose overlap with its unit input vector is $1/4$. Linearity therefore gives $F_{\rm tr}(t)=p+q/4=(1+3e^{-\gamma t})/4$. This has no $\Delta$ dependence when $\gamma$ is fixed, so its strict threshold-crossing set, and hence the checking time of [F11], is identical for every $\Delta$ in this fixed effective model. [F10, F11, step 4.2, algebra] ∎
