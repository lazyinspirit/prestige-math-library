## Prerequisites

None.

## Statement
Let q: A → B be a surjective homomorphism of abelian groups and let n be a positive integer. If multiplication by n is surjective on A, then multiplication by n is surjective on B.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** q, A, B and n as stated.

**Goal:** Find an n-th additive root of every element of B.

## Proof

1.1 For b in B, surjectivity of q gives a in A with q(a) = b. Surjectivity of multiplication by n on A gives x in A with nx = a. [given, choose]

2.1 Since q is a homomorphism, n q(x) = q(nx) = q(a) = b. Thus q(x) is the required root, and multiplication by n on B is surjective. [step 1.1, algebra] ∎
