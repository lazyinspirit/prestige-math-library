## Statement
Assume the Axiom of Choice and fix the suitable universes of the supplied derived and K-theory constructions. Let $X$ be qcqs and $U\subseteq X$ quasi-compact open with complement $T$. Every perfect object on $U$ is a direct summand of the restriction of a perfect object on $X$. Consequently the functor
$$h\mathrm{Perf}(X)/h\mathrm{Perf}_T(X)\longrightarrow h\mathrm{Perf}(U)$$
is an equivalence after idempotent completion.

## Facts & Assumptions
**Given:** A perfect object $F$ on $U$. Assume the Axiom of Choice and use the universes fixed in the Statement.

**Goal:** Prove density up to retracts with no assertion that $F$ itself extends.

[A1] Assume AC as in [[math:def-axiom-of-choice]]. Use [[math:def-grothendieck-universe-closure-convention]] with a base universe containing $\omega$ and the scheme data and a larger universe for ambient derived categories, as in the smallness supplier [F4]. Existence of suitable universes is assumed separately from AC. Besides the inherited uses in [F2] and [F4], AC chooses the set-indexed splitting family and then a family of preimages and isomorphisms indexed by the target objects in step 2.1.

[F1] [[thm-author-50b5133c29cc-klocal-tt-extension-input]] gives the classical Euler extension criterion and $[F[1]]=-[F]$. Its Euler group is generated by perfect-object classes modulo triangle relations, with restriction induced by $j^*$; in particular finite sums give addition. The criterion applies to qcqs $X$ and quasi-compact open $j:U\hookrightarrow X$.

[F2] [[lem-author-50b5133c29cc-klocal-quotient-full-faithfulness]] supplies the actual fully faithful triangulated quotient functor. [[def-scheme-perfect-complexes]] supplies the perfect and supported categories, their restriction, shifts and finite sums, and idempotent completeness of $h\mathrm{Perf}(U)$. Here $h$ denotes the ordinary homotopy category. The quotient and target are preadditive: their Hom sets are abelian groups and composition is bilinear. The quotient functor is additive, meaning it preserves addition in each Hom group. Fully faithful means bijective on each Hom set. A retract of $B$ is an object $A$ with maps $i:A\to B$ and $p:B\to A$ satisfying $pi=1_A$; then $ip$ is an idempotent on $B$, that is, $(ip)^2=ip$.

[F3] [[math:def-the-idempotent-completion-of-a-preadditive-category]] defines $\operatorname{Kar}(\mathcal C)$: objects are $(A,e)$ with $e^2=e$; arrows $(A,e)\to(B,f)$ are arrows $u:A\to B$ satisfying $u=fue$; composition is inherited and the identity on $(A,e)$ is $e$. [[math:thm-the-idempotent-completion-is-idempotent-complete-and-its-inclusion-is-fully-faithful-and-universal]] constructs the additive extension into a target with supplied splittings and permits choosing those splittings under AC when the source is small and the target idempotent complete.

[F4] [[math:def-quasi-compact-and-quasi-separated-scheme]] defines qcqs: the underlying space is quasi-compact and the intersection of two affine open subschemes is quasi-compact. Quasi-compactness of $U$ means quasi-compactness of its underlying space. Every affine open of $U$ is also an affine open of $X$, so $U$ is quasi-separated and hence qcqs. Thus [[lem-author-c5bc2838d897-kcoherent-perfect-essential-smallness]] applies to both $X$ and $U$, and to the supported subcategory on $X$. Choose small equivalent models, transporting the indicated restriction and full supported subcategory along the equivalences. The ordinary quotient has the same set of objects as the source model; its Hom sets are supplied by the quotient in [F2] and identified there with Hom sets in the small target model. Thus we may work with small preadditive categories $\mathcal C=h\mathrm{Perf}(X)/h\mathrm{Perf}_T(X)$ and $\mathcal D=h\mathrm{Perf}(U)$ and the induced functor $R:\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$.

[F5] [[math:def-equivalence-and-adjoint-equivalence-of-categories]] defines an equivalence by a quasi-inverse functor and natural isomorphisms to the identity functors. [[math:thm-fully-faithful-split-essentially-surjective-characterises-equivalence]] states that a fully faithful functor with specified preimage objects and isomorphisms for every target object is an equivalence; once this family is supplied, the criterion needs no further Choice.

## Proof
1.1 In the source-side Euler group of [F1], $[F\oplus F[1]]=0$. The zero class belongs to the image of restriction from $X$, so [F1] gives perfect $E$ on $X$ with $j^*E\simeq F\oplus F[1]$ in the ordinary derived category. The canonical inclusion and projection exhibit $F$ as a summand of $j^*E$. No equality with the currently defined $K_0$ is used. [F1, F2, given]

2.1 Work in the small models $R:\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$ of [F4]. For every $(A,e)$ in $\operatorname{Kar}(\mathcal C)$, idempotent completeness of $\mathcal D$ gives a splitting $R(A)\xrightarrow{p_e}S_e\xrightarrow{i_e}R(A)$ with $i_ep_e=R(e)$ and $p_ei_e=1_{S_e}$. These splitting triples form a nonempty set for each $(A,e)$ since $\mathcal D$ is small; [A1] chooses a family indexed by the set of objects of $\operatorname{Kar}(\mathcal C)$. Apply [F3] to obtain $\widetilde R(A,e)=S_e$ and $\widetilde R(u)=p_fR(u)i_e$, extending $R$ up to natural isomorphism. The Hom group from $(A,e)$ to $(B,f)$ is the subgroup of $u:A\to B$ with $u=fue$. Full faithfulness of $R$ identifies it with the subgroup of $v:R(A)\to R(B)$ satisfying $v=R(f)vR(e)$. The mutually inverse maps $v\mapsto p_fvi_e$ and $w\mapsto i_fwp_e$ identify that subgroup with $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal D}(S_e,S_f)$, so $\widetilde R$ is fully faithful. By step 1.1 any target object $F$ is a retract of some $R(A)$; write the retraction as $i:F\to R(A)$ and $p:R(A)\to F$ with $pi=1_F$. By full faithfulness there is a unique $e:A\to A$ with $R(e)=ip$, and faithfulness implies $e^2=e$. The maps $p i_e:S_e\to F$ and $p_e i:F\to S_e$ are inverse by the splitting equations. Hence $F\simeq\widetilde R(A,e)$. For each object $F$ of $\mathcal D$, let $W_F$ be the set of pairs $(B,\varepsilon)$ with $B\in\operatorname{Ob}(\operatorname{Kar}(\mathcal C))$ and $\varepsilon:\widetilde R(B)\xrightarrow{\sim}F$. It is a set because both $\operatorname{Kar}(\mathcal C)$ and $\mathcal D$ are small, and is nonempty by the preceding construction. Apply [A1] to the family $(W_F)_{F\in\operatorname{Ob}(\mathcal D)}$, obtaining specified $B_F$ and $\varepsilon_F$ simultaneously. Thus $\widetilde R$ is split essentially surjective. Together with its full faithfulness, [F5] gives a quasi-inverse and the natural isomorphisms establishing the stated equivalence after idempotent completion. [A1, F2, F3, F4, F5, step 1.1] ∎
