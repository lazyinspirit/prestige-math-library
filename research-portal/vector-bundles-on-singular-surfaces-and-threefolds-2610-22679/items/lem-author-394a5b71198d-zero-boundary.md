## Statement
Assume AC. Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field, let $X$ be a nonempty smooth affine finite-type $k$-scheme of dimension zero, and let $E$ be a vector bundle of constant rank zero on $X$. Then $E=0$ and $c_0(E)=1\ne0$ in $\mathrm{CH}^0(X)$. There is no vector bundle $E\prime$ with $E\cong E\prime\oplus\mathcal O_X$. Thus the two conditions in the critical-rank splitting equivalence are both false in dimension zero.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The hypotheses and data in the statement, with AC and the article's fixed universes wherever perfect K-theory is used.

**Goal:** Establish the asserted conclusion.

[F1] The smooth affine scheme is a finite disjoint union of integral components. [[lem-author-394a5b71198d-components]]

[F2] By convention the zeroth Chern class is 1 for every bundle, including the zero bundle. [[math:def-chern-classes-of-a-vector-bundle]]

[F3] The codimension-zero cycle group is freely generated by the irreducible components, and there are no rational-equivalence relations coming from larger-dimensional integral subschemes in dimension zero. [[math:def-chow-group-of-cycles-mod-rational-equivalence]]

[F4] A rank-zero locally free module is locally zero, hence zero. Local bundle ranks are nonnegative integers and add under direct sums. [[def-vector-bundle-classes]]

## Proof

1.1 By [F4], $E$ is the zero bundle. By [F1] and [F3], $\mathrm{CH}^0(X)$ is the free abelian group on its finitely many nonempty components. The unit class 1 has coefficient 1 on each component; since $X$ is nonempty, it is not zero. By [F2] this unit is exactly $c_0(E)$. [F1, F2, F3, F4]

2.1 If $E\cong E\prime\oplus\mathcal O_X$, taking a fiber at any point would give $0=\operatorname{rank}(E\prime)+1$, impossible for a bundle rank. Thus splitting and top-Chern vanishing both fail, as stated. [F4, step 1.1] ∎
