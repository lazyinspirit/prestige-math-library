## Statement
Assume the Axiom of Choice and the fixed size conventions of the supplied constructions. Let $k$ be a perfect field. For a normal affine finite-type $k$-scheme $X$ of dimension $d\geq1$, define the top-dimensional point image by
$$F^{d}_{\mathrm{sm,top}}K_0(X):=(i_d)_*F^{d}_{\mathrm{sm}}K_0(X_d),$$
where $(i_d)_*$ is the exact clopen zero-extension map justified above. This definition has no pure-dimensional hypothesis on $X$; its piece $X_d$ is pure. Its generators are precisely the residue classes of smooth closed points on the dimension-$d$ components. Then
$$\operatorname{im}(\operatorname{cyc}^{\mathrm{Kr}}_X)=F^d_{\mathrm{sm,top}}K_0(X)\subseteq F^d_{\mathrm{mot}}K_0(X).$$
For each positive dimension $e$, the analogous zero-extended image of $\operatorname{CH}^{LW}_0(X_e)$ is contained in $F^e_{\mathrm{mot}}K_0(X)$. The subgroup generated by all geometric smooth closed points of $X$ is the direct sum of these dimension-specific point images, together with the free residue-point subgroup on $X_0$ in weight zero. It need not be contained in $F^d_{\mathrm{mot}}K_0(X)$.

**Given:** The stated normal affine finite-type scheme over a perfect field, the Axiom of Choice and the fixed size conventions of the cited constructions, together with the clopen dimension strata and their established cycle maps, all with AC.

**Goal:** Give the strongest componentwise statement and exclude the falsely broadened fixed-weight assertion.

[F2] [[lem-author-80f982ded420-normal-point-strata]] and [[lem-author-80f982ded420-clopen-filtered-products]] give the actual clopen K-class/image-filtration decompositions.

[F3] [[lem-author-16f30f6b95cb-lowweight-zero-projection-rank]] gives $F^1_{\mathrm{mot}}K_0(k)=0$ since rank $K_0(k)=\mathbb Z\to\mathbb Z$ is identity; [[def-motivic-filtration]] gives nesting and pullback naturality.

## Proof
1.1 Use the supplied realization under [A1]. Each nonempty $X_e$ is a normal affine finite-type scheme of pure dimension $e$ over the same perfect field, so the corrected pure quotient-image lemma applies whenever $e>0$. Under [F1], the source point map on $X$ has exactly the same generator values as the map on $X_d$ followed by clopen zero extension. Thus its image is the displayed top-dimensional point image by the quotient-image argument. [F2] puts it inside $F^d_{\mathrm{mot}}K_0(X)$ because it lies in the dimension-$d$ piece there and is zero in every other component. Repeating with $e$ gives the same-weight containments for every positive-dimensional stratum. The displayed definition is legitimate because [F2] supplies the exact zero-extension map and its injective coordinate inclusion. [A1, F1, F2]

1.2 Smooth closed points partition by the finitely many strata, so their generated subgroup splits by [F2] as the direct sum of the component generated subgroups. A normal zero-dimensional affine finite-type scheme over the perfect field is a finite disjoint union of spectra of finite separable extensions; its residue classes generate its $K_0$ with weight-zero rank generators. This last point description uses the free point group, not Krishna's $d\geq1$ definition on a zero-dimensional scheme. Thus no convention for a nonexistent positive-codimension curve is being imposed on $X_0$. [F2, algebra]

2.1 For a concrete failure of the all-points fixed-weight extension, let $X=\mathbb A_k^3\amalg\operatorname{Spec}k$ over algebraically closed $k$. It is normal affine of dimension three. Its isolated component point $p$ is geometrically smooth, and its class restricts to $1\in K_0(k)=\mathbb Z$. Every class of $F^3_{\mathrm{mot}}K_0(X)$ restricts to $F^3_{\mathrm{mot}}K_0(k)\subseteq F^1_{\mathrm{mot}}K_0(k)=0$ by [F3]. Therefore $[p]\notin F^3_{\mathrm{mot}}K_0(X)$. This proves the obstruction to using all lower-dimensional smooth points at weight three, without asserting a defect in an implicit pure-threefold source convention. [F2, F3] ∎
