## Statement
Assume the Axiom of Choice and compatible site and universe bounds, with $\omega$ in the scheme-data universe. Let $k$ be a perfect field and $i\geq1$. On smooth separated finite-type $k$-schemes put $P(T)=\Omega^\infty K_{\rm Perf}(T)$ and let $e_i:Y_i^{\rm cl}\simeq Y_i^{\rm EM}$ be the specified integral coefficient equivalence. Here $Y_i^a(T)=\Omega^\infty H(D_i^a(T)[2i])$ for the classical or EM weight-$i$ complex. Let $C_i^{\rm EM}:P\to Y_i^{\rm EM}$ be the supplied actual operation and $N_i:P\to Y_i^{\rm cl}$ Navarro's independently specified represented Chern operation, precomposed with the canonical ordinary-K-to-KH source map. There is a pointed natural homotopy
$$e_iN_i\simeq C_i^{\rm EM}.$$
For every closed subset $Z\subseteq V$ in this site, the induced operations on the restriction fibers for $(V,V\setminus Z)$ agree under $e_i$, compatibly with forgetting support. This includes the supported third Chern operation on the positive residue-sheaf class in the smooth affine threefold application. The comparison is integral in every characteristic. It asserts no extension of Navarro's independent endpoint to nonseparated or singular schemes.

## Definition
Use the size-bounded category $\mathrm{Sm}^{\rm sep}_k$ of smooth separated finite-type schemes over $k$, with all $k$-morphisms and Nisnevich covers. Smoothness and finite type concern the structure morphism to $\operatorname{Spec}k$; separated means that its diagonal is a closed immersion. Perfect means that every irreducible nonconstant polynomial over $k$ is separable. Fix nested universes containing the schemes, sites, complexes and diagrams; their existence is separate from Choice.

The targets and $e_i$ are the actual coefficient functors of the Chern-transport and field-zigzag suppliers. Write $\mathcal H(k)$ for Riou's $\mathbb A^1$-localized Nisnevich homotopy category and $K_i^R=K(\mathbb Z(i),2i)$ for his represented space. Use derived Nisnevich- and $\mathbb A^1$-local representatives; their full infinity-category is denoted $\mathscr H(k)$, whose homotopy category is $\mathcal H(k)$. Put $\mathcal R_i(T)=\operatorname{Map}_{\mathscr H(k)_*}(T_+,K_i^R)$, where $T_+$ is $T$ with a disjoint basepoint. The bridge $b_i:Y_i^{\rm cl}\simeq\mathcal R_i$ is constructed in Proof 1.1 from the actual shifted spectrum, the field spectrum comparison and Riou's projective-line loop equivalences in [F8]. It is an equivalence of ordinary Nisnevich sheaves, before any optional further localization of the actual coefficients. Use $b_i$ to express both existing Chern maps in Riou's target; it does not define Navarro's map by transport from EM. A natural homotopy means a path in the derived mapping space of ordinary sheaves. The derived motivic local objects form a full subcategory there, so equality in $\mathcal H(k)$ between their maps is equality of components of this mapping space.

Write $P^{\rm sh}$ for ordinary Nisnevich sheafification of $P$, with its canonical source unit. The affine Q/perfect comparison identifies $P^{\rm sh}$ with the ordinary sheafified Quillen model, retaining the actual bundle classes and pointing. Riou's genuine regular-site model identifies that source with $\mathbb Z\times\mathrm{Gr}$. The actual EM map, its transported partner $C_i^{\rm tr}=e_i^{-1}C_i^{\rm EM}$, and $N_i$ are evaluated using these source units. No equality of global perfect classes with differences of global bundles is required. Source record 1 defines $N_i$ through the independently specified represented map and the canonical ordinary-K-to-KH comparison.

For $d,r\geq0$, $\mathrm{Gr}_{d,r}$ is Riou's finite Grassmannian of rank-$d$ locally direct-summand subbundles of $\mathcal O^{d+r}$, with universal bundle $\mathcal U_{d,r}$. Its component $n\in\mathbb Z$ in $\mathbb Z\times\mathrm{Gr}$ represents $u_{d,r,n}=[\mathcal U_{d,r}]-d[\mathcal O]+n[\mathcal O]$. These are classes in the supplied perfect $K_0=\pi_0K_{\rm Perf}$ under the canonical model comparison, including every negative integer $n$.

For $U=V\setminus Z$ put $K(V/U)=\operatorname{fib}(K(V)\to K(U))$ and $M_i^a(V/U)=\operatorname{fib}(HD_i^a(V)\to HD_i^a(U))$. Fibers are coherent pullbacks over zero, with their projection maps. The supported operation has target $\Omega^\infty(M_i^a(V/U)[2i])$. At a smooth closed point $x$, the positive residue class is the class of $i_*\kappa(x)[0]$, where $\kappa(x)=\mathcal O_{V,x}/\mathfrak m_x$; its perfectness and supported class are supplied separately by the point-class lemma.

## Proof
1.1 Work under [A1] on the site and genuine source model of [F3] and [F6]. First construct the bridge in [F8]. Postcompose its actual classical mapping spectrum with the specified field equivalence $H_{\rm Spi}\simeq H\mathbb Z$, tensoring with the same $\mathbb T_k^{\otimes i}$. Apply $\Omega^\infty$ and the motivic suspension/evaluation adjunction. Riou's Omega-spectrum description then gives $Y_i^{\rm cl}(T)\simeq\operatorname{Map}_{\mathscr H(k)_*}(T_+,\Omega^\infty_{\mathbb P^1}(H\mathbb Z\otimes\mathbb T_k^{\otimes i}))\simeq\mathcal R_i(T)$. This is the natural equivalence $b_i$: postcomposition and the adjunction are functorial on the entire scheme category and its coherent diagrams, so this compares full spaces and all restriction maps. Derived evaluation of a local representative computes the displayed right-hand mapping space. By [F8], both sides are ordinary Nisnevich sheaves, making $b_i$ an equivalence in that full sheaf category; its right-hand side is also $\mathbb A^1$-local, hence so is its left-hand side. The combined $(i)[2i]$ shift is fixed by $\mathbb T_k^{\otimes i}$, and the common cycle/product normalization identifies the bundle coordinates. Navarro's independent map is the source-record-1 represented map expressed through this coefficient adjunction, so $b_iN_i$ is that same map in Riou's model. No comparison of the two Chern operations has been assumed in constructing $b_i$. On $\mathrm{Gr}_{d,r}$, the actual Chern series of $u_{d,r,n}$ is the series of $\mathcal U_{d,r}$ times those of the trivial integer classes by [F2]. Every trivial nonnegative rank has series $1$ by [F1]; its additive negative also has series $1$ by the multiplicative inverse in [F2]. Thus the same calculation holds for every $n\in\mathbb Z$, including $d=0$, and the result is precisely the series of $\mathcal U_{d,r}$. Apply the product-preserving comparison in [F1] to identify it with the classical bundle series. Navarro's ordinary virtual transformation in [F4] has this same value by Whitney and trivial-bundle normalization. Hence the maps $b_iC_i^{\rm tr}$ and $b_iN_i$, on the common genuine K-source fixed by [F2]–[F4], induce equal degree-$i$ transformations on every $u_{d,r,n}$. Riou's universal-class detector in [F3] identifies their transformations on all the $K_0$ presheaf values. This argument uses the canonical source comparisons and actual Cartan; it makes no assumption that global perfect classes are differences of global bundles. [A1, F1, F2, F3, F4, F6, F8, given]

2.1 Apply Riou's bijection of [F3] to the maps $b_iC_i^{\rm tr}$ and $b_iN_i$: step 1.1 gives equality of their unpointed classes with target $K_i^R$ on the genuine K-model. Full inclusion of these local objects in ordinary sheaves identifies the relevant mapping-space components with Riou's morphism sets. Postcomposition with the ordinary sheaf equivalence $b_i$ is an equivalence of mapping spaces, so this equality pulls back to equality of components for the original maps $N_i$ and $C_i^{\rm tr}$ into the actual $Y_i^{\rm cl}$. Choose a path between these original maps. Both positive-index endpoints are pointed. Its restriction to the zero section is a loop at zero in the additive target; subtract the constant-in-source extension of this loop from the chosen path. Addition and inverse in [F5] leave the endpoints unchanged and make that restriction constant. Equivalently, evaluation at zero splits the mapping space into its pointed fiber and the constant-map factor. We obtain a pointed natural homotopy $N_i\simeq C_i^{\rm tr}$. Precompose with the common ordinary source unit and postcompose with $e_i$, using its inverse homotopy, to obtain $e_iN_i\simeq C_i^{\rm EM}$ on actual perfect K-values. For $U=V\setminus Z$, [F6] puts both $V$ and $U$ in this same separated smooth site, including the empty complement. Evaluate this one pointed homotopy on the entire restriction arrow and take homotopy fibers. Functoriality in [F5] gives the asserted supported comparison and forget-support square. In the represented local models the fiber is the mapping object from the cofiber $V/U$, so this is exactly Navarro's postcomposition convention in [F4], with the same ordinary-K-to-KH source comparison. For the positive residue-sheaf class, the localization equivalence and geometric checks in [F6]–[F7] identify its category-defined support class with this restriction-fiber class. In particular the comparison applies to that same positive class, and hence to $i=3$ in the smooth affine threefold application.
