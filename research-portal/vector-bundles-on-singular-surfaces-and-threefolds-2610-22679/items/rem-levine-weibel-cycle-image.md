## Remark
Assume AC and the completed size conventions. In the article's pure-dimensional normal affine finite-type threefold setting over an algebraically closed field,
$$\operatorname{im}\bigl(\operatorname{cyc}_X:\operatorname{CH}^{LW}_0(X)\to K_0(X)\bigr)=F^3_{\mathrm{sm}}K_0(X).$$
The map sends $[x]$ to the actual perfect residue-sheaf class $[i_*\kappa(x)]=\varphi_x(1)$ and kills the specified Cartier-curve principal-divisor relations. Thus it is a surjection onto this image subgroup; no injectivity or identification of the whole Chow group with its image is asserted. The pure-dimensional interpretation is explicit in the source Definition6.5, so it does not require treating the customary word threefold/variety as an original-paper error.

For a normal affine finite-type scheme of dimension three over the same field with lower-dimensional clopen components, put $X_3$ for the union of its three-dimensional components. The exact source dimension-three convention gives the stronger, unambiguous componentwise formula
$$\operatorname{CH}^{3}_{\mathrm{Kr}}(X)\cong\operatorname{CH}^{LW}_0(X_3),\qquad\operatorname{im}(\operatorname{cyc}^{\mathrm{Kr}}_X)=F^3_{\mathrm{sm,top}}K_0(X)=(i_3)_*F^3_{\mathrm{sm}}K_0(X_3)\subseteq F^3_{\mathrm{mot}}K_0(X).$$
Here $F^3_{\mathrm{sm,top}}$ is the zero-extended top-dimensional point image defined and justified in the local prerequisite. Lower-dimensional strata have their own same-dimension Levine–Weibel point images (and a free weight-zero point group in dimension zero). The subgroup generated by all geometrically smooth closed points across these strata is their direct sum; it is not in general a subgroup of weight three. The explicit $\mathbb A^3\amalg\operatorname{Spec}k$ counterexample prevents that overbroad generated interpretation. None of these clarifications edits the completed pure-dimensional definitions.

The field is perfect by [F4], and [A1] retains the supplied Choice and size premises. Thus apply [F1] with $d=3$. By [F2] and [F6] its generator $[\kappa(x)]$ is the current class $\varphi_x(1)$, and its relation subgroup is the completed Levine–Weibel one of [F5]. Since the quotient map from the free point group is surjective, the cycle map has the same image as the free point-class homomorphism. By [F3] that image is precisely $F^3_{\mathrm{sm}}K_0(X)$, proving the first displayed equality with its actual factorization. [A1, F1, F2, F3, F4, F5, F6, given]
