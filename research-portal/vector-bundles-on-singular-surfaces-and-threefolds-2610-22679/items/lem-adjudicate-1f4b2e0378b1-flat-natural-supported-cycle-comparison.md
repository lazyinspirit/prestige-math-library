## Statement
Assume the Axiom of Choice and the compatible nested Grothendieck universes and site bounds of the supplied coefficient constructions, with $\omega$ in the smaller universe. Fix a perfect field $k$ and an integer $j\geq0$. On smooth separated finite-type $k$-schemes there is a specified equivalence of unshifted coefficient spectra with the realized Bloch complexes, natural for étale maps and open immersions. Its construction is the inverse of the specified classical-to-EM field comparison followed by Spitzweck's flat-natural cycle comparison. For a closed subset $T\subset V$, it induces
$$\Psi_{V,T}:M_{j,T}^{\rm EM}(V)\simeq\mathcal H(z_T^j(V,\bullet)[-2j]),$$
compatibly with support-to-global maps and pullback of pairs by étale maps and open immersions. In particular, if $q:W\to V$ is étale and the scheme-theoretic inverse image of a reduced rational closed point $y$ is exactly a reduced rational closed point $x$, and both point immersions have codimension $j$, then the classes defined by $\Psi^{-1}_{V,\{y\}}[y]$ and $\Psi^{-1}_{W,\{x\}}[x]$ satisfy $q^*\Psi^{-1}_{V,\{y\}}[y]=\Psi^{-1}_{W,\{x\}}[x]$. This fixes a comparison used to define positive classes; it makes no assertion that arbitrary unnormalized fixed-ambient cycle equivalences give that same class.
