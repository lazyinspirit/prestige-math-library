## Statement
Let $S$ be a cdh covering sieve on $X$ and let $\{u_i:U_i\to X\}_{i=1}^m$ be a $B$-family in $S$. Its coproduct $u:W=\coprod_iU_i\to X$ is a cdh covering morphism, and admits a finite word factorization into Nisnevich covering morphisms and cdp morphisms. No assertion that $u\in S$ is made. If a two-stage refinement of $u$ is supplied,
$$V\xrightarrow{q}X'\xrightarrow{p}X,\quad h:V\to W,\quad pq=uh,$$
with $q$ Nisnevich and $p$ cdp, then $V_i=h^{-1}(U_i)$ are finitely many open-and-closed subschemes, $\{V_i\to X'\}$ is a finite Nisnevich family, and every composite $V_i\to X$ is in $S$.

## Facts & Assumptions
**Given:** The finite $B$-family, and only for the last assertion the displayed refinement as an explicit premise.

**Goal:** Make the cover morphism available for a source refinement theorem while keeping final arrows inside $S$.

[F1] [[lem-author-5bfbe3ebf14f-cdh-generators-pullback-composition]] and [[lem-author-5bfbe3ebf14f-finite-generated-topology-basis]] supply the finite generating tree and the closure rules.

[F2] Finite coproducts exist in the site; their summand inclusions are open-and-closed immersions. Such inclusions form a Nisnevich family: they are étale and uniquely lift their component points with unchanged residue fields. [[def-nisnevich-covering-interface]]

## Proof
1.1 Realize the construction of the $B$-family by a finite tree of elementary generating-family substitutions. Start with the frontier $X$. When a leaf $A$ is replaced by a generating family over it, take its source coproduct and leave all other leaves unchanged using their identities. The new frontier coproduct maps to the old one. For a Nisnevich substitution this is a Nisnevich covering morphism; for a cdp substitution it is cdp, by [F1] and the disjoint target components. Finitely many substitutions give a finite word with final frontier $W$, and composite $u$. Hence $u$ is cdh covering. This is a consequence of the family construction, not closure of the sieve $S$ under coproducts. [F1]

1.2 Under the stated refinement, preimages of the summands of $W$ give a finite open-and-closed decomposition $V=\coprod_iV_i$. Each $V_i\to X'$ is étale since $q$ is. Every point of $X'$ has a residue-preserving lift in $V$ by the Nisnevich condition on $q$, and that point belongs to a summand $V_i$. Thus these restricted maps form a finite Nisnevich family. Their composites to $X$ factor as $V_i\to U_i\xrightarrow{u_i}X$ and therefore belong to $S$ by sieve closure under precomposition. The schemes $V_i$ remain separated of finite type over $k$. [F2, given]

2.1 For emphasis, the generally invalid implication $u_i\in S$ for all $i\Rightarrow\coprod u_i\in S$ has not been used. For example the sieve on a disconnected $X=A\amalg B$ generated by its two nonempty summand inclusions does not contain $\mathrm{id}_X$, although their coproduct is $\mathrm{id}_X$. It is nonetheless a Nisnevich covering sieve. The split family in step 1.2 is the required correct refinement inside $S$. [F2, step 1.2] ∎
