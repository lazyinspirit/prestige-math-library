## Statement
Assume the Axiom of Choice and the fixed nested-universe bounds of the site and derived-category suppliers. Let $F=\mathbb Q$ or $F=\mathbb F_p$ for a prime $p$, let $\Lambda$ be a finite commutative ring, and put $E_\Lambda(X)=R\Gamma_{\mathrm{et}}(X,\underline\Lambda_X)$, with its actual derived pullback maps and the constant coefficient sheaf in degree zero. On the entire site of quasi-compact quasi-separated $F$-schemes, with the specified small-skeleton universe convention, $E_\Lambda$ satisfies cdh hyperdescent and Nisnevich descent. Here the cdh topology is generated by Nisnevich covers and abstract blowup families $\{Z\to X,Y\to X\}$ with $Z\to X$ a finitely presented closed immersion and $Y\to X$ finitely presented proper, inducing an isomorphism away from $Z$. The functor sends every such abstract blowup square to a cartesian square in $D(\Lambda)$ and takes the empty scheme to zero. No invertibility assumption on the order of $\Lambda$ is imposed. Here $D(R)$ denotes the unbounded derived infinity-category of $R$-modules, and cartesian means homotopy-cartesian.

## Facts & Assumptions
**Given:** Choice, the nested-universe bounds, the prime field $F$, finite commutative ring $\Lambda$, actual étale cohomology functor $E_\Lambda$, and exact qcqs site in the statement.

**Goal:** Establish hyperdescent, Nisnevich descent, abstract blowup square descent and the zero empty-scheme value for this functor.

[A1] Choice is inherited from the skeleton and representative choices, the generator comparison, and the imported derived resolution/model-category constructions; the nested universes are additional size assumptions. [[math:def-axiom-of-choice]] [[lem-author-dcdf962edbda-qcqs-site]] [[lem-author-7113c087ecba-generators-arc]] [[thm-author-7113c087ecba-arc-input]]

[F1] The universe convention and the exact qcqs prime-field site, including its abstract blowup squares and both finite-presentation conditions, are fixed in [[lem-author-dcdf962edbda-qcqs-site]].

[F2] Every specified cdh generator is an arc cover, so the source cdh topology is coarser than the arc topology. [[lem-author-7113c087ecba-generators-arc]]

[F3] For finite commutative $\Lambda$, the actual complex $E_\Lambda$ with its derived pullbacks satisfies arc hyperdescent on all qcqs schemes and has zero value at the empty scheme. Its enhanced target, constant coefficient sheaf and actual functorial maps are fixed in [[lem-author-7113c087ecba-endomorphism-hyperdescent]].

[F4] The EM qcqs-square criterion, with the necessary zero value on the empty scheme, is imported in [[thm-author-7113c087ecba-arc-input]]. This criterion concerns ordinary $D(\mathbb Z)$-valued sheaves. That supplier also fixes the small étale site and hypercovers: augmented simplicial formal families whose matching maps are locally surjective. Locally surjective means that every section over a test object lifts after a covering family. Values on formal families are products, and hyperdescent means equivalence with the limit of the resulting cosimplicial diagram. Ordinary descent is the special case of covering-family Čech nerves.

[F5] A Nisnevich covering family consists of étale maps such that every target point has a lift inducing an isomorphism of residue fields; the topology is generated by these families. Their precise covering-family convention is [[def-nisnevich-covering-interface]]. Nisnevich descent here means Čech descent for these families on the same qcqs category.

[F6] Use the unbounded derived enhancement denoted $\mathcal D(R)$ in [[thm-adjudicate-60ab133bec72-enhanced-mapping-input]], with its equivalence to $\operatorname{Mod}_{HR}$ for the Eilenberg–Mac Lane ring spectrum $HR$. Define $U:D(\Lambda)\to D(\mathbb Z)$ by restricting module scalars along $H\mathbb Z\to H\Lambda$ under these equivalences. Reference 0 supplies preservation of all small limits by this restriction. The forgetful functors from both module categories to spectra detect equivalences, and their composites with restriction agree. Therefore $U$ detects equivalences as well. In particular $U$ preserves family products, cosimplicial limits, zero objects and homotopy pullbacks.

## Proof
1.1 Work under [A1] on the exact site of [F1]. Take a cdh hypercover as an augmented simplicial formal family, using [F4]. Every section of each matching family has a lift after a cdh covering family. By [F2] that family is also arc covering, so the same matching map is locally surjective for the arc topology. This also holds for any qcqs test scheme mapping to a hypercover term over $X$: composing with its map to $X$ gives the required $F$-structure. Thus the underlying diagram is an arc hypercover on qcqs schemes, within the fixed universe bounds. Apply [F3] to obtain cdh hyperdescent for the actual $D(\Lambda)$-valued functor and its pullback maps. The Čech nerve of each Nisnevich family in [F5] is a cdh hypercover, since these families are among the generators; the same comparison gives Nisnevich descent. The argument for all cdh covering families gives ordinary cdh descent. Formal families avoid requiring an infinite disjoint union to be qcqs. [A1, F1, F2, F3, F4, F5]

2.1 The empty étale covering of the empty scheme forces every module sheaf there to have zero sections, so its module-sheaf category is zero and $E_\Lambda(\varnothing)=0$, as also included in [F3]. Apply the limit-preserving $U$ of [F6] to the descent comparisons of step 1.1. Because it preserves both family products and cosimplicial limits, $UE_\Lambda$ is an ordinary $D(\mathbb Z)$-valued cdh sheaf, with zero empty value. The qcqs-square criterion [F4] therefore makes every specified abstract blowup square of $UE_\Lambda$ cartesian. For the corresponding square of $E_\Lambda$, apply $U$ to its comparison map from the upper-left value to the homotopy pullback of the other three values. Preservation of that pullback identifies its image with the preceding equivalence. Conservativity of $U$ makes the original comparison an equivalence in $D(\Lambda)$. Both finite-presentation conditions remain those of [F1], with no finite-type-over-$F$ or separatedness assumption on the schemes and no restriction to ordinary ideal blowups. [A1, F1, F3, F4, F6, step 1.1] ∎
