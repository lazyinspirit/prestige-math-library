## Statement
Assume the Axiom of Choice and fixed compatible universe bounds for schemes, sites and derived categories. Fix a prime $p$, put $S=\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z[1/p]$, and let $r,j$ be integers with $r\geq0$ and $j\geq0$.

Let $\mathcal C=\mathrm{Sch}^{\rm qcqs}_S$ be the big category of quasi-compact, quasi-separated $S$-schemes and all $S$-morphisms, with the chosen size bounds. Give it BEM's cdh topology generated by finite Nisnevich covering families and finitely presented abstract blowup covers. A Nisnevich family is an étale family in which every point of the target has a lift inducing an isomorphism of residue fields. Here an abstract blowup cover is $\{Z\to X,X'\to X\}$, where $Z\to X$ is a finitely presented closed immersion, $X'\to X$ is finitely presented and proper, $Z'=Z\times_X X'$, and $X'\setminus Z'\to X\setminus Z$ is an isomorphism. Finitely presented means locally of finite presentation, quasi-compact and quasi-separated; the square with upper left corner $Z'$ is cartesian in the scheme fibre-product sense.

For each $X$ use its size-bounded small étale site of étale $X$-schemes and jointly surjective étale coverings. The sheaf $\mu_{p^r}$ is the sheaf of $p^r$th roots of unity there; its $j$th tensor power is over the constant sheaf $\mathbb Z/p^r$, with zeroth tensor power $\mathbb Z/p^r$. Write $R\Gamma_{\rm et}(X,-)$ for derived sections on that site, computed using a K-injective resolution. The symbol $\tau^{\leq j}$ means objectwise canonical cohomological truncation: retain degrees below $j$, use the kernel of the degree-$j$ differential in degree $j$, and put zero above $j$.

Use BEM's derived infinity-category $D(\mathbb Z)$ of complexes localized at quasi-isomorphisms, with homotopy-coherent limits and colimits. A complex-valued cdh sheaf here is a presheaf satisfying derived Čech descent for the stated covers, and $L_{\mathcal C}$ is BEM's reflective sheafification into those sheaves. Let $D_j=\mathbb Z(j)^{\rm cdh}|_{\mathcal C}$ be the restriction of the particular BEM cdh-motivic complex of Definition 7.8, whose construction is imported with the comparison. Corollary 7.20 gives compatible natural equivalences
$$D_j/p^r\simeq L_{\mathcal C}\tau^{\leq j}R\Gamma_{\rm et}(-,\mu_{p^r}^{\otimes j})\qquad(j,r\geq0).$$
The derived quotient $D_j/p^r$ is the objectwise cofiber of multiplication by $p^r$. BEM Corollary 2.9 states that cdh sheaves with values in a complete, cocomplete stable infinity-category, including $D(\mathbb Z)$, are closed under presheaf colimits; in particular this cofiber is already a cdh sheaf. At $r=0$ the quotient and coefficient sheaves are zero.

The equivalences are components of BEM's equivalence of $E_\infty$-algebras in graded presheaves. Here a graded object is an integer-indexed family, and an $E_\infty$-algebra means a commutative algebra object with all higher coherence data, using the derived tensor product and the Day convolution grading convention of BEM §2.1. Only the component $j=3,r=1$ and its actual comparison map are used downstream. This is the big qcqs convention; no identification with the older separated finite-type or hypercomplete convention is asserted, and no primitive root on all ambient schemes is assumed.
