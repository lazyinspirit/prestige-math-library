## Statement
Under the hypotheses of [[lem-author-c8fb25136bd5-degree-zero-cycles]], the subgroup $B^r(X)=\operatorname{im}(\partial_1)\subset Z^r(X)$ is generated by the differences $d_0^1[W]-d_1^1[W]$ as $W$ ranges over the admissible integral codimension-$r$ closed subschemes of $X\times\Delta^1$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** $k$, $X$ and $r$ as in the statement.

**Goal:** Describe the image of the degree-one boundary.

[F1] The degree-one chain group is freely generated by the stated admissible subschemes, and $\partial_1=d_0^1-d_1^1$. [[def-bloch-higher-cycle-complex]]

[F2] The degree-zero chain group is $Z^r(X)$. [[lem-author-c8fb25136bd5-degree-zero-cycles]]

## Proof

1.1 By linearity, the image of a free abelian group under a homomorphism is generated by the images of its free generators. [F1]

2.1 Applying the displayed formula for $\partial_1$ and identifying its target with $Z^r(X)$ gives precisely the stated generators of $B^r(X)$. [F1, F2, step 1.1] ∎
