## Statement
Assume the Axiom of Choice and the fixed compatible site and universe bounds of the supplied constructions, with $\omega$ in the scheme-data universe; universe existence is a separate assumption. Let $k$ be any field and $X$ a qcqs $k$-scheme. Here a $k$-scheme is equipped with a morphism to $\operatorname{Spec}k$, and qcqs means that every open cover has a finite subcover and the intersection of two quasi-compact opens is quasi-compact. Use $K=K_{\mathrm{Perf}}$ for nonconnective perfect K-theory, where a perfect complex is locally quasi-isomorphic to a bounded complex of finite locally free sheaves.

First retain the actual value presheaves on qcqs field-schemes, and restrict them to the quasi-compact open basis of the small Zariski site of $X$. Put $S=\Omega^\infty\operatorname{Fil}^1K$, $B=\Omega^\infty\operatorname{gr}^1K\simeq B\mathbb G_m$ and $T=\Omega^\infty\operatorname{Fil}^2K$. The stack $B\mathbb G_m$ is the groupoid of line bundles and their isomorphisms, pointed by the trivial line. For the coherent complex-valued motivic functor $D_2(U)=\mathbb Z(2)_{\mathrm{mot}}(U)$ and its fixed Eilenberg–Mac Lane realization $\mathcal H$, put $Y_2(U)=\Omega^\infty\mathcal H(D_2(U)[4])$. The cochain shift is $D[4]^q=D^{q+4}$, so $\pi_0Y_2(U)=H^4(D_2(U))=H^{4,2}_{\mathrm{mot}}(U,\mathbb Z)$ for these actual values. Write $q_2:\operatorname{Fil}^2K\to\operatorname{gr}^2K$ for the cofiber projection and $Q_2:T\to Y_2$ for its zeroth-space map under the specified graded equivalence. Let $i:T\to S$ be the stage transition and $i_K:T\to\Omega^\infty K$ its augmentation to stage zero. Let $C_2:\Omega^\infty K\to Y_2$ be the actual pointed perfect second Chern operation, retaining the supplied universal bundle classes. Let $a$ denote ordinary space-valued Zariski sheafification and put $T^{\mathrm{ord}}=aT$, $\mathcal K^{\mathrm{ord}}=a\Omega^\infty K$. Since $Y_2$ is an ordinary sheaf, write $Q_2^{\mathrm{ord}}$, $C_2^{\mathrm{ord}}$ for the factorizations of the actual operations through these units, and $i_K^{\mathrm{ord}}=ai_K$. On the smooth big Zariski site over $X$ they satisfy
$$Q_2^{\mathrm{ord}}\simeq-C_2^{\mathrm{ord}}i_K^{\mathrm{ord}}:T^{\mathrm{ord}}\longrightarrow Y_2.$$
Precomposing the units gives $Q_2(U)\simeq-C_2(U)i_K(U)$ on actual values over every qcqs $U$.

Apply the common small-Zariski stalkwise local replacement $L$ to these restrictions. Write $A_2^{\mathrm{der}}=LY_2$ and attach a superscript $\mathrm{der}$ to the resulting sources and maps. Then $Q_2^{\mathrm{der}}\simeq-C_2^{\mathrm{der}}i_K^{\mathrm{der}}:T^{\mathrm{der}}\to A_2^{\mathrm{der}}$ as derived sheaf maps, retaining their descent data. In the local-chain supplier's convention $A_2^{\mathrm{der}}(U)=\Omega^\infty\mathcal H(R\Gamma_{\mathrm{Zar}}(U,\mathbb Z(2)_{\mathrm{mot}}[4]))$. This formula uses its complex of abelian sheaves on the small site; no identification of these derived sections with $Y_2(U)$ is asserted. Both conclusions hold on every qcqs field-scheme, without a finite-type, normality, dimension or global bundle-representation hypothesis.

## Proof
**Given:** The actual maps on qcqs field-schemes, their restrictions to any $X$, Choice and the fixed compatible size bounds.

**Goal:** Identify operations on the genuine determinant-trivial homotopy fiber, including all descent data.

1.1 Work first with the actual value presheaves, before either sheafification or further local replacement. The map $T\times B\to S$, $(t,b)\mapsto i(t)+\kappa(b)$, is an equivalence over $B$: translation by $\kappa(b)$ identifies the zero fiber with the fiber over each generalized point $b$, using the specified homotopy $d\kappa\simeq\mathrm{id}$. The inverse over $B$ is obtained by subtracting $\kappa(b)$ and using that homotopy to land in the homotopy fiber. It gives a natural pointed retraction $r:S\to T$, underlying $s\mapsto s-\kappa(d(s))$ with that nullhomotopy; $ri\simeq\mathrm{id}$. No additivity of $\kappa$ is required. The section makes $\pi_1S(Y)\to\pi_1B(Y)$ surjective on every qcqs $Y$, so homotopy exactness makes $\pi_0T(Y)\to\pi_0S(Y)$ injective. The discrete rank target also makes $\pi_0S(Y)\to K_0(Y)$ injective by the same exactness. [A1, F1, F2, F5]

1.2 For a rank-$n$ bundle $V$, $r([V]-n)$ has underlying class $\delta(V)=[V]-[\det V]-(n-1)$. Pull back to a detecting flag tower and let its line quotients be $L_s$. Put $D_s=\bigotimes_{i\leq s}L_i$ and $R_s=\sum_{i\leq s}[L_i]-[D_s]-(s-1)$. For a flag inclusion $u:F_{s-1}\to F_s$ with quotient $L_s$, [F7] represents its cofiber by the two-term cone $F_{s-1}\xrightarrow{u}F_s$ in degrees $-1,0$. Its map to $L_s[0]$ is the quotient map in degree zero and zero elsewhere. Stalkwise exactness gives zero cohomology in degree $-1$ and identifies degree-zero cohomology with $L_s$, with all other degrees zero. Thus this map is a quasi-isomorphism and the cone is the actual perfect cofiber with quotient $L_s[0]$. The cofiber relations in [F6] consequently give $[V]=\sum_s[L_s]$. The determinant formula in [F2] gives $\det V\simeq D_n$, hence $R_n=\delta(V)$, without a global splitting. Tensor multiplication of line classes then gives
$$R_1=0,\qquad R_s=R_{s-1}-([L_s]-1)([D_{s-1}]-1).$$
The latter product is the augmentation of the actual stage product of the line lifts $\kappa(L_s),\kappa(D_{s-1})$. Injectivity in step 1.1 identifies these equal K-classes with their unique second-stage classes. The actual product square in [F2], $q_1\kappa(L)=c_1(L)$ from [F1]–[F2], the additive line first class, and the filtered-root formula in [F4] give by induction
$$Q_2r([V]-n)=-\sum_{i<s}c_1(L_i)c_1(L_s)=-c_2(V).$$
Rank zero/one give zero, and finite clopen decomposition handles varying ranks. This is an equality in $\pi_0Y_2(Y)=H^{4,2}_{\mathrm{mot}}(Y,\mathbb Z)$ for the flag scheme $Y$, by [F8]. Detection in [F3] therefore descends exactly this actual coefficient identity to the base. It is not being applied to derived sections of a stalkwise replacement. [A1, F1, F2, F3, F4, F6, F7, F8, step 1.1]

2.1 Put $a=c_1(V)=c_1(\det V)$. Full-scope Cartan in [F3] gives $c_t(\delta(V))=c_t(V)(1+at)^{-1}$ through degree two, using the line and trivial-bundle values of [F4]. Its second coefficient is $c_2(V)-a^2+a^2=c_2(V)$. Thus $(Q_2+C_2i_K)r$ vanishes in $\pi_0Y_2(G)$ on every actual universal bundle on a finite Grassmannian $G$ over $X$. By [F8] and the evaluation universal property for the representable $G$, this is the zero homotopy class of a map $G\to Y_2$ in the ordinary smooth big Zariski category. These Grassmannians are smooth qcqs over $X$, even when $X/k$ is singular or not finite type. [A1, F2, F3, F4, F8, step 1.2]

3.1 On the smooth big Zariski site over $X$, apply the ordinary sheafification in [F9] to the actual value presheaves and their retraction from step 1.1. Left exactness retains the rank and determinant fibers and $r^{\mathrm{ord}}i^{\mathrm{ord}}\simeq\mathrm{id}$. The factorizations of $Q_2$ and $C_2$ into the actual sheaf $Y_2$ give the particular pointed difference $D=(Q_2^{\mathrm{ord}}+C_2^{\mathrm{ord}}i_K^{\mathrm{ord}})r^{\mathrm{ord}}:S^{\mathrm{ord}}\to Y_2$. Its restriction to a bundle object is the operation computed in step 2.1, by the factorization units and actual bundle maps in [F4] and [F9]. Apply the actual coefficient adjunction of [F8] and the universal Grass/Vect detection in [F3] to these zero classes on finite Grassmannians. In target weight two, a Chern monomial of weight $w$ in mapping homotopy degree $v=0,1$ has coefficient $\mathcal E_X^{2(2-w)-v,2-w}(X)$, which vanishes for $w>2$ by [F3]. The finite-width range $N-n\geq2$ includes all surviving monomials, and rank $n\geq2$ includes their variable indices. Stabilization preserves $c_1,c_2$, and the new variable has weight greater than two. Hence the mapping $\pi_0$ and $\pi_1$ systems stabilize, and [F3]'s coherent-tower argument gives $\lim^1\pi_1=0$. Thus the finite zero classes imply the single nullhomotopy class $Du=0$ on $B_\infty$ in the ordinary sheaf category, rather than an unjustified selection of coherent nullhomotopies at each finite stage. No hypercomplete sections enter this calculation. [A1, F3, F4, F8, F9, step 1.1, step 2.1]

4.1 The pointed ordinary K-stack equivalence in [F9] is injective on homotopy classes of maps from $S^{\mathrm{ord}}$ to the actual $Y_2$. Its module and size hypotheses were checked there for [F8]'s weight-two target. Since $Du$ is null by step 3.1, $D$ is null. Precompose this homotopy with $i^{\mathrm{ord}}$ and use $r^{\mathrm{ord}}i^{\mathrm{ord}}\simeq\mathrm{id}$ to obtain $Q_2^{\mathrm{ord}}\simeq-C_2^{\mathrm{ord}}i_K^{\mathrm{ord}}$ into $Y_2$. Evaluate at any smooth qcqs test scheme $U$ and precompose with the unit $T(U)\to T^{\mathrm{ord}}(U)$. The defining factorizations recover exactly $Q_2(U)$ and $C_2(U)i_K(U)$ with target $Y_2(U)$, proving the actual-value identity. Taking $X=U$ also proves it for every qcqs field-scheme. No equivalence of the unit on global sections is required, and the tested difference need not be additive. [A1, F4, F8, F9, step 3.1]

5.1 Retain the native small-site conclusion as well. Restrict the specific stable bundle nullhomotopy of step 3.1 along open immersions and apply the common $L$ of [F9]. Precompose with the canonical map from the small-site colimit of the restricted finite-rank bundle stacks to the restriction of their big-site colimit, then apply local replacement. Its composite into $S^{\mathrm{der}}$ is the actual rank-zero free-class map $j_0:BGL\to S^{\mathrm{der}}$ of [F3]; this identification is by the actual bundle object maps in [F4] and [F9]. No preservation of that sheaf colimit by restriction is assumed. Thus the derived difference $D^{\mathrm{der}}=(Q_2^{\mathrm{der}}+C_2^{\mathrm{der}}i_K^{\mathrm{der}})r^{\mathrm{der}}$ vanishes after $j_0$. The local-chain supplier in [F3] detects its zero class into $A_2^{\mathrm{der}}$, so it vanishes on $S^{\mathrm{der}}$. Left exactness in [F9] retains $r^{\mathrm{der}}i^{\mathrm{der}}\simeq\mathrm{id}$; precomposing gives the stated derived map identity on $T^{\mathrm{der}}$. This argument transports the actual bundle homotopy to the native target, and never applies flag-cohomology detection directly to derived sections. It uses the retraction before chain detection and asserts no preservation of an arbitrary homotopy fiber by a homology equivalence. [A1, F1, F2, F3, F4, F5, F8, F9, step 1.1, step 3.1, step 4.1] ∎
