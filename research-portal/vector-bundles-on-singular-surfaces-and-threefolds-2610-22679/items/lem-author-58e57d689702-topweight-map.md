## Statement
Assume the Axiom of Choice and the fixed universe and site-size bounds of the supplied source constructions. Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field, $X$ a finite-type $k$-scheme with $\dim X\leq3$, and $p$ a prime invertible in $k$; thus $p\cdot1_k\ne0$. Algebraically closed means that every nonconstant polynomial in $k[t]$ has a root in $k$. Dimension is the supremum of lengths of strict chains of nonempty irreducible closed subsets, with $\dim\varnothing=-\infty$; irreducible means not a union of two proper closed subsets. Set $j=3$ and $n=p$.

Let $F$ be the prime subfield of $k$, identified with $\mathbb Q$ or $\mathbb F_q$ according to the characteristic. Use the entire qcqs $F$-scheme cdh site within the supplied universe bounds, generated by Nisnevich covers and abstract blowup families whose closed immersion and proper morphism are finitely presented. Evaluation at $X$ means restriction along $X\to\operatorname{Spec}k\to\operatorname{Spec}F$, with the representative convention of the supplied site. All presheaves take values in the source stable derived $\infty$-category $D(\mathbb Z)$ of complexes of abelian groups. Write $L^F_{\mathrm{cdh}}$ for its ordinary cdh sheafification, left adjoint to inclusion of derived sheaves satisfying covering descent, and $\tau^{\leq3}$ for objectwise cohomological truncation. Put $B_3(T)=R\Gamma(T_{\mathrm{et}},\mu_p^{\otimes3})$, where $\mu_p$ is the small-étale sheaf of roots $u^p=1$ in invertible functions and the tensor powers are over $\mathbb Z/p$. The motivic complex is the supplied Elmanto–Morrow functor, restricted from $F$-schemes, and $C/p=C\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb Z/p=\operatorname{cofib}(p:C\to C)$ is the derived quotient.

Let $\alpha$ be the canonical motivic-to-cdh quotient map and $\theta$ the source comparison to $L^F_{\mathrm{cdh}}\tau^{\leq3}B_3$, and set
$$\gamma_X:\mathbb Z(3)_{\mathrm{mot}}(X)/p\xrightarrow{\alpha_X}\mathbb Z(3)_{\mathrm{cdh},F}(X)/p\xrightarrow{\theta_X}(L^F_{\mathrm{cdh}}\tau^{\leq3}B_3)(X).$$
The canonical motivic-to-étale map $\beta_X:\mathbb Z(3)_{\mathrm{mot}}(X)/p\to B_3(X)$ is an equivalence and satisfies $\beta_X=\epsilon_X\gamma_X$. Here $\epsilon:L^F_{\mathrm{cdh}}\tau^{\leq3}B_3\to B_3$ is the source adjoint extension of the truncation map, characterized by its composite with the sheafification unit. Its existence uses the supplied cdh descent of $B_3$. The factorization is an equality of the specified source maps in the derived category, not an identification inferred from abstractly isomorphic cohomology groups. No affineness, smoothness or reducedness is required.

**Goal:** Prove the full equivalence of the canonical map $\beta_X$ and its exact factorization through $\gamma_X$ and the source truncation-induced map.

[F7] [[lem-adjudicate-fe422e116ac9-canonical-comparison-restriction]] identifies the actual EM comparison with $\beta_X=\epsilon_X\theta_X\alpha_X$, including after representative-based prime-field restriction. In the top-weight range it supplies an equivalence $\rho_X$ under the report's comparison, with $t_X=\epsilon_X\rho_X$. Consequently $\epsilon_X=t_X\rho_X^{-1}$ and $\beta_X=t_X\rho_X^{-1}\gamma_X$. Its equality $\beta_X=t_X$ is conditional on $\rho_X=\gamma_X$; that additional equality is not a premise of the present proof.

## Proof
1.1 By [F5], $X$ is qcqs. The prime-subfield identification in [F4] preserves $1$, so $p\cdot1_F\ne0$ because its image in $k$ is nonzero; hence $p$ is invertible in the field $F$. The same underlying scheme viewed over $F$ therefore lies in the source domain of [F4] and [F6], within [A1]'s size bounds. By [F3], its motivic source is the restriction of that prime-field functor. Apply [F1] with weight $3\geq\dim X$ and prime $p$: the report map $t_X$ to $B_3(X)$ is invertible. The under-map equivalence $\rho_X$ in [F7] gives $\epsilon_X=t_X\rho_X^{-1}$, so the actual truncation-induced $\epsilon_X$ is invertible. This uses the full finite-type scope of the suppliers, without affineness, smoothness or reducedness. [given, A1, F1, F3, F4, F5, F6, F7]

2.1 On this same source site, [F2] fixes the composite and [F6] fixes $\epsilon$ as the adjoint extension of truncation. The EM construction and its restriction in [F7] give $\beta_X=\epsilon_X\theta_X\alpha_X=\epsilon_X\gamma_X$, using the definition in [F3]. This proves the asserted exact factorization. By [F3], $\gamma_X$ is invertible, and step 1.1 makes $\epsilon_X$ invertible; hence $\beta_X$ is an equivalence, with inverse $\gamma_X^{-1}\rho_Xt_X^{-1}$. No equality between $\rho_X$ and $\gamma_X$, or between the report map and the EM map, is needed. The conclusion concerns the specified EM composite, rather than a map selected by abstract group isomorphisms. [F1, F2, F3, F4, F6, F7, step 1.1] ∎
