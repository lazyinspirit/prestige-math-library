## Statement
Assume AC and the same suitable universes used for the supplied perfect K-theory construction and affine comparison. Universe existence is an additional assumption, separate from AC. If a rank-$r$ bundle $E$ on a noetherian affine scheme $X$ has a stable trivialization $E\oplus\mathcal O_X^N\cong\mathcal O_X^{r+N}$, then $[E]=r[\mathcal O_X]$ in the article\'s perfect spectral $K_0(X)$, and $\det E\cong\mathcal O_X$. Here $\det E=\Lambda^rE$ is the determinant line. These conclusions use the actual stable isomorphism; they do not assert that the bundle itself is trivial.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The data and hypotheses in the statement, AC, and the same suitable universes assumed by the supplied perfect K-theory comparison. Universe existence is an additional assumption, not a consequence of AC. [[math:def-axiom-of-choice]] [[math:def-grothendieck-universe-closure-convention]]

**Goal:** Establish the stated K-zero and determinant conclusions.

[F1] For a quasi-compact, quasi-separated affine scheme, under AC and the suitable universe bounds, the bundle Grothendieck group is the article's perfect spectral $K_0(X)$. This is an abelian group generated by bundle isomorphism classes with $[B]=[A]+[C]$ for each short exact sequence $0\to A\to B\to C\to0$ of bundles, and the comparison preserves these classes. [[def-vector-bundle-classes]]

[F2] Exterior powers are functorial for bundle maps; the top exterior power of a rank-$s$ bundle is its determinant line, with local basis the wedge of an ordered frame. The empty wedge is $1$ in $\Lambda^0E=\mathcal O_X$. [[def-exterior-determinant-bundles]]

[F3] Stable triviality supplies the actual bundle isomorphism in the statement for integers $r,N\geq0$; direct sums and their morphisms are componentwise on sections. [[def-stably-free-bundles]]

[F4] An affine scheme is isomorphic to a spectrum. A noetherian scheme is locally noetherian and quasi-compact. Quasi-separatedness means that intersections of affine opens are quasi-compact. Under AC, an affine scheme is quasi-compact, and it is quasi-separated. [[math:def-affine-scheme]] [[math:def-locally-noetherian-and-noetherian-scheme]] [[math:def-quasi-compact-and-quasi-separated-scheme]] [[math:cor-affine-scheme-quasi-compact]] [[math:thm-affine-schemes-quasi-separated]]

[F5] Exactness of module sheaves means equality of image and kernel sheaves. Compatible local sections glue uniquely; consequently local module maps agreeing on overlaps glue by applying this axiom to their values on each section. [[math:def-exact-sequence-sheaves]] [[math:def-sheaf-on-topological-space]]

## Proof

1.1 By [F4], the affine $X$ is quasi-compact under the assumed AC and is quasi-separated, so the comparison in [F1] applies with the assumed universes. For any bundles $A,C$, the inclusion $a\mapsto(a,0)$ and projection $(a,c)\mapsto c$ give $0\to A\to A\oplus C\to C\to0$. On every open set the projection is surjective and its kernel is exactly the included $A$; thus this is a short exact sequence of sheaves by [F3] and [F5]. The relation in [F1] yields $[A\oplus C]=[A]+[C]$. The sequence with all terms zero gives $[0]=0$, and finite iteration gives $[\mathcal O_X^N]=N[\mathcal O_X]$, including $N=0$. The stable isomorphism therefore gives $[E]+N[\mathcal O_X]=(r+N)[\mathcal O_X]$. Subtracting the common class in the abelian group proves $[E]=r[\mathcal O_X]$ in the specified perfect spectral group. AC is inherited here by the quasi-compactness and affine-comparison suppliers; no choice of local frames indexed by an entire cover is required in the determinant argument. [given, F1, F3, F4, F5, algebra, construct]

2.1 On a trivializing open for $E$ with frame $e_1,\ldots,e_r$, let $\epsilon_1,\ldots,\epsilon_N$ be the standard frame of the trivial summand. Send $e_1\wedge\cdots\wedge e_r$ to $e_1\wedge\cdots\wedge e_r\wedge\epsilon_1\wedge\cdots\wedge\epsilon_N$. By [F2] this defines an isomorphism $\det E\to\det(E\oplus\mathcal O_X^N)$ there. Under any change of frame of $E$, multilinear expansion of the wedge gives the same scalar coefficient on both sides, since the $\epsilon_i$ are fixed. Hence the local maps agree on overlaps; [F5] glues their values on sections, and likewise their inverses, to a global isomorphism. Functoriality in [F2] takes the stable isomorphism to $\det(E\oplus\mathcal O_X^N)\cong\det(\mathcal O_X^{r+N})$. The standard full wedge canonically trivializes this last line, proving $\det E\cong\mathcal O_X$. Empty wedges cover $r=0$ or $N=0$. The two invariant calculations use the given stable isomorphism and do not cancel its trivial summand as a bundle. [given, F2, F3, F5, step 1.1, algebra, construct] ∎
