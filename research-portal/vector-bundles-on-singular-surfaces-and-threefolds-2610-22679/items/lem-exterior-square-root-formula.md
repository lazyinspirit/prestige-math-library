## Statement
Let $S$ be a scheme and $E$ a rank-three vector bundle with a filtration $0=F_0\subset F_1\subset F_2\subset F_3=E$ whose successive quotients $L_i=F_i/F_{i-1}$ are line bundles and whose inclusions are locally split. Then $\Lambda^2E$ has a locally split filtration with successive line-bundle quotients $L_1\otimes L_2$, $L_1\otimes L_3$, and $L_2\otimes L_3$, in that order. There is an isomorphism $\det E\cong L_1\otimes L_2\otimes L_3$.

Assume the Axiom of Choice and that $S$ is locally noetherian. In the vector-bundle Grothendieck ring $K^0(S)$, write $[L_i]$ for the class of $L_i$. Then $[\Lambda^2E]=[L_1][L_2]+[L_1][L_3]+[L_2][L_3]$ and $[\det E]=[L_1][L_2][L_3]$. If $c_1$ is any homomorphism from the Picard group of $S$ to an abelian group, and $\ell_i=c_1(L_i)$, the first Chern classes of the three exterior-square line factors are respectively $\ell_1+\ell_2$, $\ell_1+\ell_3$, and $\ell_2+\ell_3$.

A vector bundle is a finite locally free $\mathcal O_S$-module, and a line bundle has rank one. Locally split means that every inclusion in the filtration is locally the inclusion of a direct summand. The Picard group consists of line-bundle isomorphism classes under tensor product. The stated homomorphism condition means $c_1(L\otimes M)=c_1(L)+c_1(M)$; these line-factor classes are the exterior-square roots relative to the chosen flag. In particular, under the Axiom of Choice, the flag-tower supplier provides such a filtration after pulling a rank-three bundle back to its flag tower.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A scheme $S$, a rank-three bundle $E$, and the locally split line-quotient filtration in the statement. For the $K^0$ assertions, also assume the Axiom of Choice and that $S$ is locally noetherian. For the root assertion, also assume the stated additive map $c_1$.

**Goal:** Construct the exterior-square filtration and determinant isomorphism and derive the displayed class formulas.

[A1] The Axiom of Choice is used only through the flag-tower supplier [F2] and the Grothendieck-ring supplier [F3]; the filtration and determinant constructions for a given flag require no additional Choice assumption. [[math:def-axiom-of-choice]]

[F1] Exterior powers and determinant are functorial for bundle maps; the exterior power of a free module has the basis of increasing wedges. In particular the determinant of a rank-three bundle is its third exterior power. [[def-exterior-determinant-bundles]] [[math:lem-exterior-algebra-basis-monomials]]

[F2] Under the Axiom of Choice, the flag tower of a rank-three bundle carries a locally split filtration with line-bundle quotients. [[lem-flag-tower-line-quotients]]

[F3] Under the Axiom of Choice, for a locally noetherian scheme $S$, $K^0(S)$ is generated by vector-bundle classes with the relations $[B]=[A]+[C]$ for short exact sequences $0\to A\to B\to C\to0$ of vector bundles; multiplication is induced by tensor product. [[math:def-grothendieck-group-of-coherent-sheaves-and-vector-bundles-on-a-scheme]]

[F4] A scheme is a locally ringed space locally isomorphic to affine schemes. Locally noetherian means admitting an affine open cover by spectra of noetherian rings. [[math:def-scheme]] [[math:def-locally-noetherian-and-noetherian-scheme]]

[F5] A module sheaf has section modules and scalar-compatible restriction maps, and its morphisms are restriction-compatible sectionwise linear maps. Finite locally free means locally isomorphic to a finite power of the structure sheaf. The sheaf tensor product is the sheafification of the sectionwise module tensor product. Balanced sectionwise pairings induce maps from module tensor products; compatible maps from a presheaf to a sheaf extend uniquely across sheafification. [[math:def-module-on-ringed-space]] [[math:def-locally-free-sheaf-finite-rank]] [[math:def-sheaf-tensor-product]] [[math:thm-universal-property-of-module-tensor-products]] [[math:thm-sheafification-universal-property]]

[F6] Image sheaves and cokernel sheaves are the sheafifications of the sectionwise image and cokernel presheaves; a quotient by a subsheaf is the cokernel of its inclusion. Exactness means equality of the image and kernel sheaves at each term. Compatible local sections glue uniquely. In particular, local module maps agreeing on overlaps glue by applying this axiom to the image of each section; local inverse maps glue as well, since on overlaps both invert the same map. [[math:def-kernel-cokernel-image-sheaves]] [[math:def-exact-sequence-sheaves]] [[math:def-sheaf-on-topological-space]]

[F7] The Picard group is the abelian group of line-bundle isomorphism classes, with multiplication induced by sheaf tensor product. In particular tensor products of line bundles are line bundles. The map $c_1$ in the statement is an assumed homomorphism on this group, not an asserted construction of a Chern theory. [[math:def-picard-group-scheme]]

## Proof

**Proof technique:** Construct the filtration by wedge images and identify its quotients on bases adapted to the flag.

1.1 By [F5], wedge multiplication from [F1] is a balanced sectionwise pairing and therefore induces sheaf maps $F_1\otimes F_2\to\Lambda^2E$ and $F_1\otimes E\to\Lambda^2E$. Let $G_1$ and $G_2$ be their image sheaves as in [F6]. They are subsheaves with $G_1\subseteq G_2\subseteq\Lambda^2E$. Locally the two short exact sequences defining the flag split, and, after intersecting finitely many trivializing neighbourhoods, the three line quotients are trivial. Lifting their generators through these splittings gives a local basis $e_1,e_2,e_3$ of $E$ with $F_1$ generated by $e_1$ and $F_2$ by $e_1,e_2$. On such a chart tensor products of free sheaves have the elementary tensor basis: the sectionwise map $e_i\otimes e_j\mapsto e_{ij}$ extends by the module tensor universal property in [F5], and its inverse sends each $e_{ij}$ to $e_i\otimes e_j$; these maps commute with restrictions and extend across sheafification. By [F1], the first wedge image is generated by $e_1\wedge e_2$, the second by $e_1\wedge e_2,e_1\wedge e_3$, and $\Lambda^2E$ by these two wedges and $e_2\wedge e_3$. These sectionwise images are already free subsheaves, so [F6] identifies them with $G_1$ and $G_2$ on the chart. Thus these subsheaves are locally direct summands and their successive quotients are line bundles. [given, F1, F5, F6, construct]

2.1 Wedge multiplication induces maps $L_1\otimes L_2\to G_1$, $L_1\otimes L_3\to G_2/G_1$, and $L_2\otimes L_3\to\Lambda^2E/G_2$. To define them, lift quotient sections only on splitting neighbourhoods. In the first map changing the lift from $L_2$ adds a wedge of two sections of $F_1$, which vanishes. In the second, changing the lift from $L_3$ adds a section of $G_1$. In the third, changing the lift from $L_2$ adds a section of $G_2$, while changing the lift from $L_3$ adds a wedge of two sections of $F_2$, which belongs to $G_1\subseteq G_2$ by step 1.1. Thus the assignments are independent of lifts, balanced and compatible with restriction. By [F5] they induce maps on the tensor sheaves on each splitting neighbourhood; their independence of lifts makes them agree on overlaps, so [F6] glues them globally. On the adapted basis of step 1.1 they send the tensor products of quotient basis vectors to the corresponding wedge generators and hence are isomorphisms. Their local inverses glue by [F6], so they are global isomorphisms. This proves the claimed filtration without choosing a global splitting. [F1, F5, F6, step 1.1, construct]

2.2 Define $L_1\otimes L_2\otimes L_3\to\Lambda^3E$ by wedging local lifts to their flag terms. Changing a lift adds a wedge containing two vectors from $F_1$ or three from $F_2$, which vanishes on the adapted basis of step 1.1. Thus the balanced local assignments induce tensor-sheaf maps by [F5] and agree on overlaps, so they glue by [F6]. On that basis the map sends the tensor product of quotient generators to $e_1\wedge e_2\wedge e_3$, a generator by [F1]. The local inverse maps glue by [F6], giving an isomorphism to $\det E=\Lambda^3E$. [F1, F5, F6, step 1.1, construct]

3.1 Under the additional $K^0$ hypotheses, apply [F3] to the short exact sequences $0\to G_1\to G_2\to G_2/G_1\to0$ and $0\to G_2\to\Lambda^2E\to\Lambda^2E/G_2\to0$. They are exact in the sense of [F6], since on the adapted charts they are coordinate inclusions followed by coordinate projections, and all terms are vector bundles by steps 1.1 and 2.1. Using step 2.1 and the tensor-product multiplication gives the displayed formula for $[\Lambda^2E]$. Step 2.2 and the same multiplication give the determinant formula. [A1, F3, F6, step 1.1, step 2.1, step 2.2, assume-hyp]

4.1 By [F7], the three tensor-product line bundles identified in step 2.1 represent the products of the corresponding classes in the Picard group. Apply the assumed homomorphism $c_1$ to those products. Additivity gives the three pairwise sums. Finally, [F2] supplies the required flag after pullback to the flag tower when the Axiom of Choice is assumed. All stated conclusions follow. [A1, F2, F7, step 2.1, step 2.2, step 3.1, assume-hyp] ∎
