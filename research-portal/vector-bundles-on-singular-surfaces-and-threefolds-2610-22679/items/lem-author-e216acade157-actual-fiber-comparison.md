## Statement
Assume the Axiom of Choice and the compatible site-size and universe bounds required by the supplied perfect K-theory, motivic and derived-section constructions. On the small Nisnevich site of every noetherian finite-dimensional field-scheme $X$, the actual natural maps from the second-stage space satisfy
$$Q\simeq-C_2\circ i_K:T\longrightarrow A,$$
where $i_K$ is its augmentation into the perfect K-space. This is an equality of homotopy classes of derived sheaf maps, hence induces the equality on derived sections, not merely on bundle classes or affine $\pi_0$ groups.

## Proof
1.1 On the smooth big Zariski site $\mathrm{Sm}_X$, evaluate the functorial stages and maps of [F4]. Construct $r$ by the fiber-product inverse of $T\times B\to S$, $(t,b)\mapsto i(t)+\kappa(b)$, using the specified homotopy in [F1]. Together with the actual $Q$ and the functorial universal $C_2$, this defines $D_{\rm big}=(Q+C_2i_K)r:S\to A$. Its restriction to small-site objects is the specified difference $D$, because every constituent, including the determinant-fiber path, is the same natural construction. These operations are defined on all qcqs field-schemes. The bundle calculation is applied below on finite Grassmannians, whose noetherian finite-dimensional scope is supplied by [F1]. [A1, F1, F4]

2.1 The rank-zero test is the pointed zero class. For each positive rank the actual reduced bundle-class construction gives a presheaf map $j_{0,n}:\mathrm{Vect}_n\to S$, with $\mathrm{Vect}_n=BGL_n$ after Zariski descent. Its composite $D_{\rm big}j_{0,n}$ has target the sheaf $A$ of [F4]. These maps stabilize under adding a trivial line, so the sheaf-colimit universal property in [F5] gives a single pointed map $\Phi_{\rm big}:BGL=\operatorname{colim}_n\mathrm{Vect}_n\to A$ whose rank-$n$ restriction is that composite. This construction requires no map from the sheaf colimit to an unsheafified $S$. On each $\mathrm{Vect}_n$ the coefficient adjunction identifies its class with weight-two cohomology of that stack. This applies to this particular constructed map; it is no claim that arbitrary small-site maps extend to motivic stacks. For pointed classes, forgetting the basepoint is injective here: the chosen trivial-bundle basepoint splits the coefficient-valued suspension spectrum of $\mathrm{Vect}_{n,+}$ into its unit and reduced summands, as also recorded in Asakura–Sato Proposition 5.2. Thus a zero class in the displayed unreduced cohomology also gives a zero pointed map class. [A1, F2, F3, F4, F5, step 1.1]

2.2 On the small site, write $\widehat S=a_{\rm Nis}S$. Sheafify the finite-rank reduced bundle maps and use their compatible stabilization to obtain $\widehat j_0:BGL\to\widehat S$, where $BGL$ is the small-site sheaf colimit. At a Nisnevich local ring $R$ the reduced bundle map into the actual rank fiber is the rank-zero plus map $BGL(R)\to BGL(R)^+$. Projectives over $R$ are free, and the rank target is discrete by [F4]. By [F2], this is an integral-chain equivalence locally. Passing to $\widehat S$ preserves this conclusion: the local model in [F6] presents ordinary sheafification on this finite-dimensional site, so its source unit is a local equivalence and induces isomorphisms on the local homotopy and integral homology sheaves. Thus $\widehat j_0$ satisfies the chain-detection hypothesis of [F2]. This uses the actual plus model of the rank fiber, not a claim that arbitrary fibers preserve homology equivalences. [A1, F2, F4, F5, F6, step 1.1]

3.1 Pull the rank-$n$ class in step 2.1 back along $G(n,N)\to\mathrm{Vect}_n$, where $G(n,N)$ carries the actual universal rank-$n$ quotient. By space-valued Yoneda in [F5] and the coefficient adjunction in [F4], this is the value of $D_{\rm big}j_{0,n}$ on that universal bundle in its full cohomology group. Its image under $rj_{0,n}$ has underlying class $\delta(V)$, and [F1] gives $Q(r([V]-n))+C_2(\delta(V))=0$. These Grassmannians are smooth qcqs over $X$ and remain noetherian finite-dimensional. Their cohomology classes therefore vanish by the bundle calculation, including the bundle descent data. [A1, F1, F4, F5, step 1.1, step 2.1]

4.1 Apply [F3] on the big site over the qcqs base $X$. Its actual model is homotopy commutative, linearly oriented and satisfies PBF; use the unit coring in Corollary 4.4.7. In mapping homotopy degree $u=0,1$, a Chern monomial of weight $w$ has coefficient $\mathcal E_X^{4-2w-u,2-w}(X)$, which vanishes for $w>2$ by the actual negative-coefficient lemma. The finite basis on $G(n,N)$ is indexed by $\alpha$ with $|\alpha|=\sum_j\alpha_j\leq N-n$ and weight $w=\sum_jj\alpha_j$. Every potentially nonzero monomial has $|\alpha|\leq2$, so ambient ranges $N-n\geq2$ include them all, and the transition maps preserve them. This holds for both mapping degrees. Hence the finite-approximation tower has zero $\lim^1\pi_1$; the map-induced Grass/Vect comparison detects the rank-$n$ class in step 2.1 from the zero restrictions in step 3.1. It follows that $\Phi_{\rm big}$ is zero on each $\mathrm{Vect}_n$. [A1, F3, F4, step 2.1, step 3.1]

5.1 Stabilization $V\mapsto V\oplus\mathcal O$ preserves $[V]-n$, its determinant, and the specified universal Chern maps with their natural identifications. Thus the restrictions in step 4.1 belong to the single stable map $\Phi_{\rm big}$ of step 2.1. The Milnor exact sequence for the mapping-spectrum inverse limit has $\lim^1\pi_1=0$. Its map to $\lim\pi_0$ is consequently injective, and the zero rank restrictions imply $\Phi_{\rm big}\simeq0$ on stable $BGL$. This separately controls the rank limit, rather than choosing incompatible nullhomotopies at each rank. [A1, F3, F4, F5, step 2.1, step 4.1]

6.1 Regard the pointed homotopy $\Phi_{\rm big}\simeq0$ as a presheaf homotopy by [F5], and restrict it to qcqs étale $X$-schemes. By [F6], the local realization preserves this sheaf homotopy and the actual target $A$ on this noetherian finite-dimensional site. No source-stage unit is asserted to induce an equivalence on sections. [A1, F1, F2, F4, F5, F6, step 1.1, step 2.2, step 5.1]

7.1 By step 2.2 and the derived free-chain adjunction in [F2], precomposition with $\widehat j_0$ is injective on pointed homotopy classes of maps from $\widehat S$ into $A$. Step 6.1 therefore gives $\widehat D\simeq0$ in the local model, hence in ordinary sheaves by [F6]. Precompose this homotopy with the canonical source unit $\eta_S$ of [F5]. This proves the stated derived sheaf-map equality and its consequence on derived sections, retaining gluing morphisms. No global bundle-difference presentation of a perfect class is used. [A1, F1, F2, F5, F6, step 2.2, step 6.1] ∎
