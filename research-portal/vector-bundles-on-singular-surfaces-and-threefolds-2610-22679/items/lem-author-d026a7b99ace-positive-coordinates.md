## Statement
Assume AC. Let $k$ be algebraically closed of any characteristic, $X$ a finite-type pure threefold, and $x$ a smooth closed point. There is a smooth affine neighbourhood $V$ of $x$, and actual open-excision identifications give $K_{0,x}(X)\cong\mathbb Z$ with positive generator $u_x=[i_*k(x)]$, and $H^{6,3}_x(X,\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z$ with positive generator $\delta_x$, the fundamental point cycle. Explicitly extend the nonnegative weight system by $C_j=0$ and $H^{a,j}=0$ for $j<0$, and likewise for its support fibers. With this convention, $H^{2j,j}_x(X)=0$ for every integer $j<3$. These are supported generators, even when their images in global groups vanish.

## Facts & Assumptions
[F1] [[prop-smooth-point-excision-purity]] and [[lem-koszul-point-support-class]] give the actual K-support fiber and its dévissage coordinate, normalized by the residue sheaf.

[F2] [[lem-author-59b1a831fe59-neighborhood]] supplies a smooth affine pure-three-dimensional finite-type neighbourhood and actual motivic support excision. [[lem-author-59b1a831fe59-actual-support]] compares the actual smooth restriction arrow and its fiber with the supported cycle complex. [[lem-author-59b1a831fe59-cycle-complex]] identifies that complex with $C_0(k(x))[-6]$ in weight three, carrying the literal reduced point cycle $[x]$ to $1$, and proves zero for $0\leq j<3$. [[lem-smooth-point-motivic-purity]] gives the same lower-weight vanishing and permits the explicit negative-weight zero extension. [[prop-smooth-motivic-comparison]] supplies the smooth coefficient convention.

[F3] [[lem-open-excision-for-support-fibers]] preserves support-to-global arrows under restriction when its descent hypothesis holds. The actual K and motivic excision data here are supplied in [F1] and [F2]. AC, [[math:def-axiom-of-choice]], is used through those geometric and comparison suppliers.


**Given:** $X,k,x$ as stated and the completed support conventions.

**Goal:** Fix the local pair and its actual positive coordinates.

## Proof
1.1 Choose the smooth affine pure-three-dimensional finite-type neighbourhood supplied by [F2], then apply [F1] on it. The local ring at $x$ is regular of dimension $3$; shrink so that its point ideal has a regular-sequence Koszul resolution. The residue sheaf is the globally defined perfect object, independently of the resolution, and its K-support coordinate is $+1$. [F1, F2]

2.1 Apply the actual arrow/fiber comparison and chain isomorphism of [F2] with $d=j=3$. They identify $H^{6,3}_x(V)$ with $H^0(C_0(k(x)))$. In degrees zero and one, $z^0(k(x),\bullet)$ is freely generated by the integral simplex; the two face maps in degree one both have coefficient $1$, so their alternating difference is zero. Thus its degree-zero homology is $\mathbb Z$, positively generated by the reduced point. Define $\delta_x$ on $V$ through this actual supported-cycle comparison. Its cycle coordinate is $+1$; this construction does not identify an unspecified abstract purity isomorphism with Gysin pushforward. [F2, step 1.1]

3.1 For $0\leq j<3$, [F2] gives zero supported cycle complexes in all degrees. For $j<0$, the declared zero extension gives zero support fibers and hence zero groups. The actual K and motivic excision equivalences of [F1] and [F2] transport both positive generators from $V$ to $X$, and preserve their support-forgetting arrows by [F3]. This proves the stated integer range and the coordinate assertions even when a global image vanishes. [F1, F2, F3, step 1.1, step 2.1] ∎
