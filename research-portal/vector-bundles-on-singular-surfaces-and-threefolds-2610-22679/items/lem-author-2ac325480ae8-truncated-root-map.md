## Statement
Assume the Axiom of Choice and compatible universe bounds for the small étale sites, big sites, slices and products used below. Fix a prime $p$, a field $k$ in which $p$ is invertible, and an element $\zeta\in k$ of multiplicative order $p$. Put $S=\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z[1/p]$ and $U=\operatorname{Spec}k$. Let $\mathcal C$ be the size-bounded category of qcqs $S$-schemes with BEM's big cdh topology, generated by finite Nisnevich covers and finitely presented abstract blowup covers. Equip $\mathcal C/U$, the category of qcqs $k$-schemes, with the induced topology. Let $j:\mathcal C/U\to\mathcal C$ forget the structure map to $U$, and let $j^*$ be restriction of presheaves. Write $L_{\mathcal C}$ and $L_{\mathcal C/U}$ for the respective ordinary reflective sheafifications of $D(\mathbb Z)$-valued presheaves, using derived Čech descent for covering families on both sites.

For $V\in\mathcal C$, compute $R\Gamma_{\rm et}$ on the size-bounded small étale site of $V$, with constant sheaf $\mathbb Z/p$ and Tate coefficient $\mu_p^{\otimes3}$, where the tensor product is over $\mathbb Z/p$. Write $T(V)=\tau^{\leq3}R\Gamma_{\rm et}(V,\mu_p^{\otimes3})$ and $A(V)=\tau^{\leq3}R\Gamma_{\rm et}(V,\mathbb Z/p)$. Their actual coefficient map on the slice is an equivalence $j^*A\xrightarrow{t^{(3)}_\zeta}j^*T$. It induces a specified equivalence $j^*L_{\mathcal C}T\xrightarrow{\sim}L_{\mathcal C/U}j^*A$, namely the slice/ambient comparison followed by the localized inverse of $t^{(3)}_\zeta$. Here $\tau^{\leq3}$ is objectwise cohomological truncation before sheafification.

## Facts & Assumptions
[A1] The Axiom of Choice is assumed through the supplied K-injective construction of derived sections; the compatible universes contain the sites, slices and products. [[math:def-axiom-of-choice]] and [[def-derived-sections-on-sites]] specify this assumption and its use. [[lem-author-2ac325480ae8-slice-site]] supplies the stated big site, slice and ordinary descent conventions.

[F1] [[lem-author-2ac325480ae8-chosen-root]] supplies the pullback-compatible coefficient isomorphism, and [[def-derived-sections-on-sites]] supplies derived sections as a functor.

[F2] [[lem-author-2ac325480ae8-slice-localization]] gives the actual unit-compatible slice comparison. Truncation $\tau^{\leq3}$ is the standard derived functor retaining cohomology through degree three and killing higher degrees.

## Proof
**Given:** The stated $p,k,\zeta,S,U,\mathcal C,j$, ordinary localizations, Choice and compatible universe bounds.

**Goal:** Construct the displayed map without assuming a root on the ambient site.

1.1 Apply derived étale global sections to the coefficient isomorphism on each $V/k$. Its inverse gives an inverse in $D(\mathbb Z)$, and pullback compatibility gives a natural transformation on the slice. Apply objectwise $\tau^{\leq3}$; as a functor it preserves equivalences and the inverse. This is the specified map $j^*A\to j^*T$, induced by the coefficient map $1\mapsto\zeta^{\otimes3}$. [A1, F1, F2]

2.1 Use [F2] to identify $j^*L_{\mathcal C}T$ with $L_{\mathcal C/U}j^*T$. Apply slice localization to the inverse map from step 1.1. Their composite is exactly the asserted equivalence, natural for $k$-scheme pullbacks and compatible with the appropriate localization units. This applies the root map only after restriction and uses a proved interchange; it does not apply an ambient functor to a nonexistent ambient root transformation. [F2, step 1.1] ∎
