## Statement
Assume the Axiom of Choice and compatible universe bounds for the small étale sites, big sites, slices and products used below. Fix a prime $p$, a field $k$ in which $p$ is invertible, and an element $\zeta\in k$ of multiplicative order $p$. Put $S=\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z[1/p]$ and $U=\operatorname{Spec}k$. Choose the bounds large enough to contain an auxiliary big étale site over $S$, its slice over $U$, and their abelian resolution complexes used to construct the presheaves below. Use the small étale sites induced by this auxiliary site. Let $\mathcal C$ be the size-bounded category of qcqs $S$-schemes with BEM's big cdh topology, generated by finite Nisnevich covers and finitely presented abstract blowup covers. Equip $\mathcal C/U$, the category of qcqs $k$-schemes, with the induced topology. Let $j:\mathcal C/U\to\mathcal C$ forget the structure map to $U$, and let $j^*$ be restriction of presheaves. Write $L_{\mathcal C}$ and $L_{\mathcal C/U}$ for the respective ordinary reflective sheafifications of $D(\mathbb Z)$-valued presheaves, using derived Čech descent for covering families on both sites.

For $V\in\mathcal C$, compute $R\Gamma_{\rm et}$ on the size-bounded small étale site of $V$, with constant sheaf $\mathbb Z/p$ and Tate coefficient $\mu_p^{\otimes3}$, where the tensor product is over $\mathbb Z/p$. Write $T(V)=\tau^{\leq3}R\Gamma_{\rm et}(V,\mu_p^{\otimes3})$ and $A(V)=\tau^{\leq3}R\Gamma_{\rm et}(V,\mathbb Z/p)$. These are coherent presheaves with the actual scheme-variable derived pullbacks, constructed compatibly in both coefficients. Their actual coefficient map on the slice is an equivalence $j^*A\xrightarrow{t^{(3)}_\zeta}j^*T$. It induces a specified equivalence $j^*L_{\mathcal C}T\xrightarrow{\sim}L_{\mathcal C/U}j^*A$, namely the slice/ambient comparison followed by the localized inverse of $t^{(3)}_\zeta$. Here $\tau^{\leq3}$ is objectwise canonical cohomological truncation for the standard t-structure on $D(\mathbb Z)$, before sheafification, with its natural map $\tau^{\leq3}C\to C$. On a cochain representative it retains $C^n$ for $n<3$, replaces degree three by $\ker(d^3:C^3\to C^4)$, and is zero above degree three; differentials and maps are induced from those of $C$.

## Facts & Assumptions
[A1] The Axiom of Choice is assumed through the supplied K-injective construction of derived sections; the compatible universes contain the sites, slices and products. [[math:def-axiom-of-choice]] and [[def-derived-sections-on-sites]] specify this assumption and its use. [[lem-author-2ac325480ae8-slice-site]] supplies the stated big site, slice and ordinary descent conventions.

[F1] [[lem-author-2ac325480ae8-chosen-root]] supplies the pullback-compatible coefficient isomorphism. [[lem-adjudicate-9ec8664ca09c-etale-coefficient-functor]] constructs both ambient coherent derived-section presheaves with their actual small-site pullback maps, and carries this coefficient isomorphism and its inverse to a coherent natural equivalence after restriction to the slice. [[def-derived-sections-on-sites]] supplies the fixed-site derived-evaluation convention used there.

[F2] [[lem-author-2ac325480ae8-slice-localization]] gives the actual unit-compatible slice comparison.

[F3] [[math:def-canonical-t-structure-on-a-derived-category]] and [[math:def-truncation-functors-and-cohomology-of-a-t-structure]] fix the standard cohomological t-structure and truncation convention. [[math:def-canonical-truncation-of-a-complex]] gives the explicit truncation and natural inclusion in the Statement; [[math:lem-canonical-truncation-is-a-complex-and-has-the-claimed-cohomology]] supplies its functoriality and preservation of quasi-isomorphisms. The functor-category localization property of [[thm-author-4f716d60801a-derived-enhancement-input]] therefore gives its enhanced functor and natural inclusion, so it can be postcomposed objectwise with coherent presheaves and their transformations.

## Proof
**Given:** The stated $p,k,\zeta,S,U,\mathcal C,j$, ordinary localizations, Choice and compatible universe bounds.

**Goal:** Construct the displayed map without assuming a root on the ambient site.

1.1 Use [F1] to form the two ambient derived-section presheaves and their coherent coefficient equivalence on the slice. This supplies the scheme-variable maps and their higher composition compatibilities, not merely the individual derived values. By [F3], canonical truncation is a strict functor on complexes preserving quasi-isomorphisms; its kernel formula gives the natural inclusion and retains exactly the cohomology in degrees at most three. Its enhanced localization is thus a functor on $D(\mathbb Z)$. Postcompose the entire coherent coefficient equivalence and inverse with this functor, objectwise before localization of presheaves. This is the specified map $j^*A\to j^*T$, induced by the coefficient map $1\mapsto\zeta^{\otimes3}$. [A1, F1, F3]

2.1 Use [F2] to identify $j^*L_{\mathcal C}T$ with $L_{\mathcal C/U}j^*T$. Apply slice localization to the inverse map from step 1.1. Their composite is exactly the asserted equivalence, natural for $k$-scheme pullbacks and compatible with the appropriate localization units. This applies the root map only after restriction and uses a proved interchange; it does not apply an ambient functor to a nonexistent ambient root transformation. [F2, step 1.1] ∎
