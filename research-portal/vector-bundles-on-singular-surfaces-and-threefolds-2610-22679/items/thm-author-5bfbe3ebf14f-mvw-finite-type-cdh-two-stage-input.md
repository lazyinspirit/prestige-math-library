## Statement
On separated finite-type schemes over a field, import MVW Lemma12.26 and Proposition12.28 with their exact conventions. A proper map is covering in the abstract-blowup-generated cdh topology if and only if it is completely decomposed. Every covering morphism in that topology has a refinement $V\xrightarrow{q}X'\xrightarrow{p}X$ together with its factorization through the original covering morphism, where $q$ is a Nisnevich covering morphism and $p$ is proper and cdh covering, hence completely decomposed by Lemma12.26.

## Source contract
**Given:** Precisely the separated finite-type site over an arbitrary field, with the source's definition of cdh topology.

**Goal:** Import the established two-stage theorem without rebuilding its flattening foundation.

[F1] MVW Definition12.23 and Lemma12.26 identify this source topology with the completed interface's topology generated by Nisnevich and cdp maps. Indeed abstract-blowup coproduct maps are proper completely decomposed, and Lemma12.26 makes every cdp map covering in the source topology. Minimality gives equality in both directions.

[F2] MVW Proposition12.28 is the stated two-stage refinement; its proof uses Proposition12.27 to interchange successive proper/Nisnevich stages. The latter uses flattening.
