## Statement
Fix a prime $p$ and $S=\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z[1/p]$. On $\mathcal C=\mathrm{Sch}^{\rm qcqs}_S$, with the big cdh topology generated by finite Nisnevich covers and finitely presented abstract blowup covers, BEM Corollary7.20 gives compatible natural equivalences
$$D_j/p^r\simeq L_{\mathcal C}\tau^{\leq j}R\Gamma_{\rm et}(-,\mu_{p^r}^{\otimes j})\qquad(j\geq0),$$
where $D_j=\mathbb Z(j)^{\rm cdh}$ is BEM’s cdh-motivic complex, and quotient is the objectwise derived quotient. BEM Corollary2.9 states that stable-valued cdh sheaves are closed under presheaf colimits, so the finite quotient is indeed computed objectwise. These are components of the stated equivalence of E∞-algebras in graded presheaves; this draft uses only the component $j=3,r=1$ and its actual comparison map.
$L_{\mathcal C}$ denotes BEM’s complex-valued sheafification, with derived Čech descent for the covers just specified. This is the big qcqs site, not the older separated finite-type site fixed in [def-cdh-derived-localization](item-def-cdh-derived-localization).
