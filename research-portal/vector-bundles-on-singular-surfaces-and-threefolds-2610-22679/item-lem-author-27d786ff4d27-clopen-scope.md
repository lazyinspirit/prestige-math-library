## Statement
Assume the Axiom of Choice (AC). Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field of arbitrary characteristic, let $X=\operatorname{Spec}A$ be a normal affine finite-type $k$-scheme of pure dimension three, and let $P$ be a rank-three vector bundle on $X$. Fix a functional $s:P\to\mathcal O_X$ whose scheme-theoretic zero locus $S=V(\operatorname{im}s)$ is finite and smooth over $k$ and lies in $X_{\mathrm{sm}/k}$; $S$ may be empty. Put $Q=\ker s$ and $U=X\setminus S$. Then $Q$ is coherent on $X$, and $Q|_U$ is a rank-two vector bundle in the exact sequence $0\to Q|_U\to P|_U\xrightarrow{s}\mathcal O_U\to0$. Fix a closed subset $Z\subset U$ of codimension at least two before choosing the next functional.

Write $X=\coprod_a X_a$ for its finite clopen integral-component decomposition, and put $U_a=U\cap X_a$, $Z_a=Z\cap U_a$, $E_a=(Q|_{U_a})^\vee$ and $W_a=U_a\setminus(Z_a\cup(U_a)_{\mathrm{sing}})$. The integral construction uses on each component a finite indexed family of actual global sections of $E_a$ which generates $E_a$, spans $E_{a,x}/\mathfrak m_x^2E_{a,x}$ at every smooth closed point $x\in U_a$, and contains a controlling family on every member of a finite principal cover $W_a=\bigcup_i D(g_{a,i})$. Here each chart is a smooth affine open of $X_a$ on which $E_a$ has a frame $e_1,e_2$; its controlling family consists of that frame and all products $f_\ell e_r$, where the $f_\ell$ generate the chart's coordinate algebra over $k$. These families can be chosen for every fixed setup above by taking global generators of the coherent dual of $Q$ on each affine component and adjoining the required coordinate multiples; the chart families are restrictions of the same global indexed family on $U_a$.

There is a functional $t:Q|_U\to\mathcal O_U$ whose scheme-theoretic zero locus is a finite disjoint union of nonempty smooth geometrically integral curves of pure codimension two in $U$, one on each $X_a$, disjoint from $Z$ and contained in $U_{\mathrm{sm}/k}$. In particular it is a single such curve when $X$ is integral. The functional may depend on the fixed $Z$. A globally irreducible zero scheme cannot in general be demanded for an arbitrary fixed preceding pair $(U,Q)$ on a disconnected normal threefold, even when $P$ is trivial and its first two Chern classes vanish. This obstruction concerns that fixed pair, rather than the global theorem or every possible preceding point functional.

## Facts & Assumptions
**Given:** AC and the field, affine finite-type pure threefold, bundle, fixed point functional, kernel, open set and prescribed avoidance locus specified in the Statement. A functional on $Q|_U$ is a section of its dual, and its zero scheme is defined locally by its coefficients. The finite point scheme is reduced with $k$-rational points because it is smooth over the algebraically closed field.

**Goal:** Establish the componentwise curve construction with all its actual hypotheses, and retain the precise disconnected fixed-pair obstruction.

[F1] [thm-author-fc2380accb47-normal-geometric-input](item-thm-author-fc2380accb47-normal-geometric-input) gives the finite clopen integral decomposition and normality's codimension-two bound for the singular locus, with regular and smooth loci equal over the perfect field $k$. [prop-swan-bertini-point-section](item-prop-swan-bertini-point-section) supplies a possible $s$ with the specified zero scheme under AC; its coherent-kernel and off-zero exactness argument applies to the fixed $s$ above.

[F2] [thm-author-27d786ff4d27-determinantal-height](item-thm-author-27d786ff4d27-determinantal-height) bounds the height of the proper ideal of maximal minors of a $2\times3$ matrix by two.

[F3] [lem-author-27d786ff4d27-dual-global-controls](item-lem-author-27d786ff4d27-dual-global-controls) supplies the actual finite global dual-section families, their first jets and their controlling chart restrictions. [lem-author-27d786ff4d27-chartwise-integrality](item-lem-author-27d786ff4d27-chartwise-integrality) applies to those families on $W_a$, using its common-core nonemptiness and fixed-locus avoidance opens, to produce a nonempty smooth geometrically integral zero curve. The AC assumption of these suppliers and the point-section supplier is carried here through [math:def-axiom-of-choice](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/def-axiom-of-choice). It is inherited at these invocations; the subsequent finite clopen assembly introduces no arbitrary-index choice requirement.

## Proof
1.1 By [F1], the components $X_a$ are finitely many clopen normal integral affine finite-type threefolds. Restriction of $s$ has zero scheme $S\cap X_a$, still finite and smooth in the smooth locus; its coherent kernel restricts to $Q|_{X_a}$ and has rank two on $U_a$. The closed $Z_a$ has codimension at least two there, and normality over the perfect field makes $W_a$ a nonempty smooth integral threefold. Apply the global dual construction of [F3] on each component with this particular $W_a$, obtaining the indexed family described in the Statement. In particular, in its construction a frame $g_{a,i}\psi_1,g_{a,i}\psi_2$ on $D(g_{a,i})$ has globally extendable controlling products even for the coordinate $1/g_{a,i}$, whose products with the frame are $\psi_1,\psi_2$. Thus the chartwise lemma in [F3] applies to the actual global family, including its common-core and avoidance conditions, and gives a functional on each $Q|_{U_a}$ with the stated zero curve. Sections on a finite disjoint union are tuples of sections, so these functionals assemble into $t$. Its zero scheme is precisely the disjoint union of the component zero schemes; smoothness, geometric integrality of each curve, pure codimension two and avoidance hold componentwise. When $X$ is integral this is a single curve. The fixed $Z$ was chosen before these parameters. [F1, F3, given]

1.2 For the obstruction take two copies of $X_0=\mathbb A_k^3$, with $P=\mathcal O^3$ and point functional $(x,y,z)$ on each. Its zero set is the reduced origin, an allowed smooth point section. On $U_0=X_0\setminus\{0\}$ let $Q_0$ be its rank-two kernel. Dualizing the locally split sequence gives $0\to\mathcal O_{U_0}\xrightarrow{(x,y,z)^t}\mathcal O_{U_0}^3\to Q_0^\vee\to0$. Every section of $Q_0^\vee$ is represented by a polynomial vector $b\in k[x,y,z]^3$: indeed $\Gamma(U_0,\mathcal O)=A$ since $A_x\cap A_y\cap A_z=A$ in the fraction field, and $H^1(U_0,\mathcal O)=0$. For the latter, use the affine cover $D(x),D(y),D(z)$: its augmented localization complex is the filtered colimit of cochain Koszul complexes on $x^n,y^n,z^n$. These are regular sequences, so the Koszul cohomology below degree three is zero (by induction, tensoring the one-element complexes and using that each next element is a nonzerodivisor on the previous quotient). Filtered colimits of modules are exact. The resulting vanishing in augmented degree two is precisely $H^1(U_0,\mathcal O)=0$. The long exact section sequence gives the claimed polynomial representative. [given, algebra]

2.1 The zero scheme of this section on $U_0$ is defined by the $2\times2$ minors of the matrix with rows $(x,y,z)$ and $b$: locally one of $x,y,z$ is invertible, and elementary row/column elimination identifies the two residual coordinates with those minors up to that unit. Its minor ideal $J\subset A$ is proper because every minor vanishes at the origin. By [F2] $J$ has a prime over it of height at most two. Such a prime is not the origin's maximal ideal of height three, so $V(J)\cap U_0$ is nonempty. Thus no functional $Q_0\to\mathcal O_{U_0}$ is nowhere zero. On two copies every global functional has zeros in both clopen components, and its zero locus is disconnected and cannot be irreducible. Trivial $P$ has $c_1=c_2=0$, so that additional article hypothesis does not remove this fixed-pair counterexample. This does not obstruct first reducing the global theorem to components, or assert that every possible preceding point section gives the same pair. [F2, step 1.2, algebra] ∎
