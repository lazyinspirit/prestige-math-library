## Statement
Assume the Axiom of Choice. Fix Grothendieck universes $U\in V\in W$ with $\omega\in U$, and require the prime field and all scheme data under consideration to lie in $U$. Existence of these universes is a separate size assumption. Use the category of all qcqs prime-field schemes with data in $U$, regarded as $V$-small, and the supplied stable presentable derived enhancement of unbounded cochain complexes of $V$-small abelian groups, considered in $W$. Its homotopy category is denoted $D(\mathbb Z)$. These are the same ambient-category and enhancement bounds as for the Elmanto–Morrow complex input.

Let $F$ be $\mathbb Q$ or $\mathbb F_p$ for a prime $p$, and let $X$ be a quasi-compact quasi-separated $F$-scheme with data in $U$. There is a natural comparison map
$$R\Gamma_{\rm Nis}(X,\mathbb G_m)[-1]\longrightarrow\mathbb Z(1)_{\rm mot}(X)$$
whose cofiber has zero cohomology in every degree at most three. The target is the Elmanto–Morrow weight-one complex, and naturality is with respect to all morphisms in the specified category of qcqs $F$-schemes.

Here the standard small Nisnevich site $X_{\rm Nis}$ has as objects all étale morphisms $T\to X$ with scheme and morphism data in $U$, and as morphisms all $X$-morphisms between them. This entire category is regarded as $V$-small; it is not replaced by an arbitrary full subcategory satisfying closure conditions. Its topology is generated by the étale families $\{T_i\to T\}$ in this category for which every point of $T$ has a lift to some $T_i$ inducing an isomorphism of residue fields. The terminal object is $X\to X$. Use $V$-small abelian sheaf values, with sheaf and derived categories considered in $W$. The abelian units sheaf $\mathbb G_m$ assigns to $T\to X$ the group $\Gamma(T,\mathcal O_T)^\times$ of invertible regular functions, with pullback as restriction. Thus the source uses derived cohomology on the standard small Nisnevich site itself.

Regard this sheaf as a complex in degree zero. The object $R\Gamma_{\rm Nis}(X,\mathbb G_m)$ is right-derived evaluation at the terminal object $X$, computed by evaluating a K-injective resolution, under these size bounds and Choice. Both sides of the comparison are in the specified $D(\mathbb Z)$. Use the cohomological shift $C[-1]^n=C^{n-1}$ with differential $-d_C^{n-1}$; cofiber means the derived mapping cone. For a representative cochain map $f:A\to B$, its cone has terms $B^n\oplus A^{n+1}$ and differential $(b,a)\mapsto(d_Bb+f(a),-d_Aa)$.

## External input
The existence and naturality of this particular comparison and its cofiber bound are imported from [Elmanto–Morrow](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.08463v1), Lemma 5.1, map (5.1) in Definition 5.2, and Corollary 7.11. Their proof is not reconstructed here. The source is derived sections of the units sheaf on the standard small Nisnevich site specified above, and the target is their motivic complex under the declared size convention. No comparison with an arbitrary smaller subsite or with a big site is asserted or used.
