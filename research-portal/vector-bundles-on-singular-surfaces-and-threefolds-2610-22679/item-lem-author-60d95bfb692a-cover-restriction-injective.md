## Statement
Let $F$ be a set-valued sheaf on a scheme site, and suppose $u:U\to V$ generates a covering sieve. Then the restriction $F(V)\to F(U)$ is injective. A sheaf has unique amalgamations of compatible sections on each covering sieve.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** $F$, $u$ and the covering sieve generated by $u$.

**Goal:** Prove injectivity of restriction.

[F1] Sections of a sheaf agreeing on every arrow in a covering sieve are equal, by uniqueness of amalgamation. [def-scheme-sites-and-sheafification](item-def-scheme-sites-and-sheafification)

## Proof

**Proof technique:** Restrict along the generated sieve.

1.1 If $a,b\in F(V)$ have equal restrictions to $U$, then their restrictions along any arrow factoring through $u$ are equal by functoriality. These arrows form the sieve generated by $u$. [given]

2.1 That sieve is covering, so [F1] gives $a=b$. Hence restriction is injective. [F1, step 1.1] ∎
