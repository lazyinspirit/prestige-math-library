## Definition
Assume the Axiom of Choice and the size conventions of the support construction. Let $k$ be a perfect field, meaning that every algebraic extension of $k$ is separable, and let $X$ be a quasi-compact quasi-separated $k$-scheme of pure finite dimension $d$. Thus $X$ has a finite affine open cover, intersections of quasi-compact opens are quasi-compact, and every irreducible component has dimension $d$; dimension is the supremum of lengths of chains of distinct irreducible closed subsets. Take $d\geq 0$.

Let $S$ be the set of closed points $x\in X$ at which $X\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ is smooth: each such point has an open neighbourhood smooth over $k$. For each $x\in S$, the smooth-point class construction gives a homomorphism $\varphi_x:\mathbb Z\to K_0(X)$, with $\varphi_x(1)=[i_*\kappa(x)]$, where $i:\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(x)\hookrightarrow X$ is the closed immersion and the bracket denotes the class of the perfect residue sheaf. Here $K_0(X)=\pi_0K(X)$ for the nonconnective $K$-theory spectrum of perfect complexes.

The **smooth-point subgroup** is
$$F^d_{\mathrm{sm}}K_0(X):=\operatorname{im}\left(\bigoplus_{x\in S}\mathbb Z\xrightarrow{\;\oplus\varphi_x\;}F^d_{\mathrm{mot}}K_0(X)\right)\subseteq F^d_{\mathrm{mot}}K_0(X)\subseteq K_0(X).$$
Here the direct sum consists of integer families with finite support, and its displayed map sends $(n_x)$ to the finite sum $\sum_x\varphi_x(n_x)$. The target $F^d_{\mathrm{mot}}K_0(X)$ is the image of $\pi_0F^d_{\mathrm{mot}}K(X)\to K_0(X)$ as defined in the motivic filtration interface. Equivalently, $F^d_{\mathrm{sm}}K_0(X)$ is the subgroup of $K_0(X)$ generated by the classes $[i_*\kappa(x)]$ for $x\in S$. If $S$ is empty, it is zero.

**Given:** The field, scheme, dimension, choice and size hypotheses in the definition.

**Goal:** Justify the displayed map, its image subgroup, and the equivalent description by generators.

[F2] [lem-smooth-point-filtration](item-lem-smooth-point-filtration) proves that the image of each $\varphi_x$ lies in $F^d_{\mathrm{mot}}K_0(X)$ for a pure finite-dimensional qcqs scheme over a perfect field, with the stated choice and size assumptions. ## Justification
1.1 For every $x\in S$, [F1] gives a homomorphism to $K_0(X),$ and [F2] allows its codomain to be restricted to the filtration subgroup. For a finitely supported integer family, the sum of its values is therefore a well-defined element of that subgroup. Finite sums and the homomorphism property of each $\varphi_x$ make the displayed assignment a homomorphism. [A1, F1, F2, given]

2.1 Its image is a subgroup of the target: zero is the image of the zero family, and sums and inverses of images are images of the corresponding sums and inverses of families. Every image is a finite integer linear combination of the values $\varphi_x(1)$, and every such combination is the image of a finitely supported family. By [F1] these values are the stated residue-sheaf classes. If $S$ is empty, the direct sum and its image are zero. This establishes the definition and its equivalent generator description. [A1, F1, step 1.1] ∎
