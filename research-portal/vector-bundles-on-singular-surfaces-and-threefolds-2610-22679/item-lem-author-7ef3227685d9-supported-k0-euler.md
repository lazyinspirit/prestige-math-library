## Statement
Assume the Axiom of Choice and the fixed suitable universes of the supplied supported perfect construction. Let $U$ be a regular Noetherian finite-dimensional scheme and $i:T\hookrightarrow U$ a closed subscheme. Set $\mathcal C_T=\operatorname{Perf}_{|T|}(U)$ and let $\mathcal A_T$ be the abelian category of coherent $\mathcal O_U$-modules supported on $|T|$, embedded in $\mathcal C_T$ in degree zero. Use the supplied essentially small realizations. Write $K_0^\triangle(h\mathcal C_T)$ for the abelian group generated by isomorphism classes of objects with $[F]=[E]+[G]$ for every distinguished triangle $E\to F\to G\to E[1]$. Write $G_0(\mathcal A_T)$ for the free abelian group on isomorphism classes in $\mathcal A_T$ modulo $[M]=[M']+[M'']$ for every short exact sequence $0\to M'\to M\to M''\to0$.

The finite Euler assignment
$$\chi_T([E])=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}(-1)^n[H^n(E)]$$
defines a homomorphism $\chi_T:K_0^\triangle(h\mathcal C_T)\to G_0(\mathcal A_T)$. It sends $[M[0]]$ to $[M]$ for every $M\in\mathcal A_T$. For $T=U$, write $\mathcal C_U=\operatorname{Perf}(U)$ and $\mathcal A_U=\operatorname{Coh}(U)$, with the same group conventions. The inclusions $J:\mathcal C_T\hookrightarrow\mathcal C_U$ and $I:\mathcal A_T\hookrightarrow\mathcal A_U$ induce homomorphisms $J_\triangle$ and $I_0$ on these groups, and
$$\chi_U\circ J_\triangle=I_0\circ\chi_T.$$

## Proof
1.1 Every open subset of $U$ is covered by its intersections with the finite affine cover in [F6]. Each intersection is quasi-compact by [F6]; for any open cover choose a finite subcover on each intersection and combine the finitely many subcovers. Thus every open subset of $U$ is quasi-compact, in particular $U$ and every intersection of two affine opens. Hence [F4] applies and supplies the essentially small categories and hearts needed for the group presentations. Cohomology is invariant under isomorphism by [F5], and boundedness in [F1] makes the displayed assignment a finite sum on every object class. For a triangle $E\to F\to G\to E[1]$, [F5] gives its long exact cohomology sequence; truncate its zero ends to obtain a finite exact sequence in $\mathcal A_T$. In a finite exact sequence $0\to X_a\to\cdots\to X_b\to0$, let $B_j$ be the image of $X_j\to X_{j+1}$, with $B_{a-1}=B_b=0$. Exactness and the abelian-category axioms give short exact sequences $0\to B_{j-1}\to X_j\to B_j\to0$. Their relations in [F3] give $[X_j]=[B_{j-1}]+[B_j]$. Multiplying by alternating signs and summing cancels each image class and leaves zero, using $[0]=0$. Applied to the cohomology sequence, this yields $\sum_n(-1)^n([H^n(E)]-[H^n(F)]+[H^n(G)])=0$, so $\chi_T([F])=\chi_T([E])+\chi_T([G])$. The triangle presentation in [F2] therefore gives the asserted homomorphism. [A1, F1, F2, F3, F4, F5, F6]

2.1 For $M[0]$, the only nonzero cohomology is $H^0=M$, so its image is $[M]$. By [F4], $J$ preserves cofibers, and therefore triangles under [F2]; sending $[E]$ to $[JE]$ respects every source relation and defines $J_\triangle$. The inclusion $I$ preserves short exact sequences because both hearts have the same underlying module-sheaf kernels and cokernels by [F1]; hence $[M]\mapsto[IM]$ respects [F3] and defines $I_0$. Apply step 1.1 with $T=U$ to define $\chi_U$. Forgetting support leaves each complex and each cohomology sheaf unchanged, so $H^n(JE)=I(H^n(E))$. Substituting into the same finite sum proves $\chi_U(J_\triangle[E])=I_0(\chi_T[E])$ on every generator, hence on the whole group. [A1, F1, F2, F3, F4, F5, step 1.1] ∎
