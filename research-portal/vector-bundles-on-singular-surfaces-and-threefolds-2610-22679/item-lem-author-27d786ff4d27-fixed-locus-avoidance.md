## Statement
Assume the Axiom of Choice (AC). Use the integral point-functional setup: $X=\operatorname{Spec}A$ is an integral normal affine scheme of finite type and dimension three over an algebraically closed field $k$; $s:P\to\mathcal O_X$ is the preceding functional on a rank-three vector bundle, with finite zero scheme $S$ contained in the smooth locus; $U=X\setminus S$, $Q=\ker s$, and $F=Q|_U$ is the rank-two kernel bundle. Put $E=F^\vee$. Choose the finite indexed family $\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_N$ of global sections of $E$ supplied by the global dual-section construction, so that $q:\mathcal O_U^N\to E$ is surjective. Set $T=\mathbb A_k^N=\operatorname{Spec}k[t_1,\ldots,t_N]$. For a parameter $\lambda$ over an extension field of $k$, let $Y_\lambda$ be the scheme-theoretic zero locus of the functional $\sum_i\lambda_i\sigma_i$ on the base change of $F$.

Fix a closed subset $Z\subset U$ of codimension at least two before choosing the parameter. Give $B=Z\cup U_{\mathrm{sing}}$ its reduced induced closed subscheme structure, where $U_{\mathrm{sing}}$ is the complement of the regular locus. Then $\dim B\leq1$, and there is a nonempty open $T_{\mathrm{av}}\subset T$ such that every parameter in it, including after any field extension, has $Y_\lambda$ disjoint from the base change of $B$. The intersection of $T_{\mathrm{av}}$ with any finitely many nonempty opens of $T$ is nonempty and has a $k$-rational parameter. The allowed open may depend on $Z$: this is $\forall Z\,\exists\lambda$, not $\exists\lambda\,\forall Z$.

## Facts & Assumptions
**Given:** The setup, the indexed family and the fixed $Z$ in the Statement. A rank-$r$ vector bundle means a sheaf of modules locally isomorphic to $\mathcal O^r$; a frame is such a local basis. The dual is the sheaf of module homomorphisms to the structure sheaf. Normality means that the local rings are integrally closed domains. Dimension is the chain dimension of the underlying Noetherian space, with $\dim\varnothing=-\infty$; the codimension condition means that every irreducible component of $Z$ has codimension at least two in the integral threefold $U$.

**Goal:** Combine avoidance of this prescribed closed set with finitely many later generic conditions on the same parameter space.

## Proof
1.1 The open $U$ is integral and normal, since it is a nonempty open of the integral $X$ with unchanged local rings; it is a Noetherian finite-type $k$-scheme of dimension three by [F1] and the finite-type setup. Codimension at least two gives $\dim Z\leq1$. By $(R_1)$ and the perfect-field locus equality in [F1], $U_{\mathrm{sing}}$ is closed and every one of its irreducible components has codimension at least two, so it also has dimension at most one. Their union has the reduced closed scheme structure of [F4], is finite type over $k$, and has dimension at most one. If $B=\varnothing$, take $T_{\mathrm{av}}=T$ and the incidence is empty. Otherwise [F2] gives a locally split surjection $q_B$ with kernel $K$ locally free of rank $N-2$; in particular $N\geq2$. On an affine open $V=\operatorname{Spec}R$ where the kernel and target have frames and the surjection splits, choose the source frame furnished by those frames and the splitting. In these coordinates $q_B$ is the projection $R^{N-2}\oplus R^2\to R^2$. The two universal zero-incidence equations are therefore the last two coordinates $v_1=v_2=0$. Its coordinate ring is $R[u_1,\ldots,u_{N-2},v_1,v_2]/(v_1,v_2)=R[u_1,\ldots,u_{N-2}]$. These local identifications with the total space of $K$ agree on overlaps: each sends a kernel vector to its same coefficient vector under the canonical inclusion $K\hookrightarrow\mathcal O_B^N$, independently of the splitting. Hence they glue to $I_B\cong\mathbb V(K)$ in the convention of [F3]. A finite affine frame cover of the Noetherian $B$, product dimensions in [F1], and the affine-space and open-cover dimensions in [F5] give $\dim I_B\leq1+(N-2)=N-1$. This remains valid when $N=2$, with affine fiber dimension zero. [F1, F2, F3, F4, F5, A1, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 For nonempty $B$, project $I_B$ to $T$. It is finite type and has finitely many irreducible components; applying [F1] to each component gives $\dim\overline{\operatorname{im}(I_B\to T)}\leq N-1$. Since $\dim T=N$ by [F5], the complement $T_{\mathrm{av}}$ is nonempty open. The inverse image of this open in $I_B$ is empty as a scheme, and every base change of that empty scheme is empty. The fiber identities of [F2] consequently give $Y_\lambda\cap B_L=\varnothing$ on $U_L$ for every field extension $L/k$ and every $L$-valued parameter factoring through $T_{\mathrm{av}}$. This also holds in the empty-$B$ case of step 1.1. Finally, the zero ideal is a prime in the domain $k[t_1,\ldots,t_N]$ by [F5], and belongs to every nonempty principal open; since those opens form a basis, it belongs to every nonempty open of $T$. Thus $T$ is irreducible by [F5]. The finite intersection of $T_{\mathrm{av}}$ with any other nonempty parameter opens is therefore nonempty and, by [F1], has a $k$-rational closed point. Throughout, $Z$ was fixed before forming $B$ and this open, giving exactly the stated quantifier order. [F1, F2, F5, step 1.1] ∎
