## Statement
On the completed qcqs prime-field category every Nisnevich covering family and every abstract blowup family $\{Z\to X,Y\to X\}$ is an arc cover. Here $Z\to X$ is a finitely presented closed immersion, $Y\to X$ is finitely presented proper and an isomorphism outside $Z$. Consequently the source cdh topology is coarser than the arc topology, including on arbitrary nonnoetherian objects.

## Facts & Assumptions
**Given:** The hypotheses in the statement.

**Goal:** Establish the stated exact map or descent conclusion.

[F1] [thm-author-7113c087ecba-valuative-input](item-thm-author-7113c087ecba-valuative-input) supplies the exact valuative facts. [F2] [lem-author-dcdf962edbda-qcqs-site](item-lem-author-dcdf962edbda-qcqs-site) fixes the cdh generators; the arc definition is recorded in that input.

## Proof
1.1 For a Nisnevich family and a rank-at-most-one valuation ring $V$ mapping to $X$, choose a member with a point over the image of the closed point; its pullback to $\operatorname{Spec}V$ is étale and has a point over the closed point. Choose an affine neighborhood $\operatorname{Spec}B$ of that point. By the exact valuation input [F1], its local ring $W=B_{\mathfrak m}$ is an extension of valuation rings with the same value group as $V$. It therefore has the same rank, and $\operatorname{Spec}W$ supplies the required lift into that cover member. For a family on a qcqs object take a finite subfamily, which exists since étale images are open. [F1, F2]

1.2 For an abstract blowup family, if the generic point of $\operatorname{Spec}V$ maps into $Z$, the pulled-back ideal is zero in $\operatorname{Frac}V$ and therefore zero in the domain $V$, so the entire map factors through $Z$. Otherwise the generic point has the unique lift to $Y$ over the open complement. Properness and the valuative criterion in [F1] extend it to $\operatorname{Spec}V\to Y$. Thus the abstract family even permits the identity extension $V\to V$. [F1, F2]

2.1 These valuation tests are the defining tests for arc covers; the source topology is generated by the listed families. Both tests are stable under the source base changes, so every generated cdh covering sieve is arc covering. No finite-type or dimension bound on $X$ was used. [F1, F2, step 1.1, step 1.2] ∎
