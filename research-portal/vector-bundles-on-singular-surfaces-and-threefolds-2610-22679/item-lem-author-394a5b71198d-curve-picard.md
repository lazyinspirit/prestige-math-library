## Statement
Assume AC. Let $C$ be a smooth integral affine curve over an algebraically closed field. The actual first Chern map $\operatorname{Pic}(C)\to\mathrm{CH}^1(C)$, $[L]\mapsto c_1(L)$, is an isomorphism. In particular $c_1(L)=0$ if and only if $L\cong\mathcal O_C$, and this is equivalent to $\varepsilon(L)=0$. Since $\det L=L$, this is the determinant/line-bundle interface in dimension one.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The hypotheses and data in the statement, with AC and the article's fixed universes wherever perfect K-theory is used.

**Goal:** Establish the asserted conclusion.

[F1] The local rings of a smooth scheme over the perfect base field are regular. A one-dimensional regular local ring is a DVR, and a DVR is a PID and hence a UFD under AC. [math:thm-regular-equals-smooth-over-perfect-field](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/thm-regular-equals-smooth-over-perfect-field), [math:thm-one-dimensional-regular-local-rings-are-dvrs](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/thm-one-dimensional-regular-local-rings-are-dvrs), [math:cor-dvr-is-a-pid](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/cor-dvr-is-a-pid), [math:thm-principal-ideal-domains-are-unique-factorisation-domains](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/thm-principal-ideal-domains-are-unique-factorisation-domains)

[F2] On a locally factorial noetherian integral scheme Cartier and Weil divisors agree, compatibly with principal divisors; Cartier divisors modulo principal ones give the Picard group. [math:thm-cartier-weil-isomorphism-locally-factorial](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/thm-cartier-weil-isomorphism-locally-factorial), [math:thm-cartier-divisors-mod-principal-to-picard](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/thm-cartier-divisors-mod-principal-to-picard)

[F3] On a curve rational equivalence of codimension-one cycles is generated by principal divisors of rational functions on the curve itself. The actual first Chern class of a line bundle is the divisor of a nonzero rational section, modulo these relations. [math:def-chow-group-of-cycles-mod-rational-equivalence](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/def-chow-group-of-cycles-mod-rational-equivalence), [math:def-intersection-with-a-cartier-divisor-and-first-chern-class](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/def-intersection-with-a-cartier-divisor-and-first-chern-class)

[F4] For a line bundle, vanishing of the exterior-dual class is equivalent to triviality. [lem-author-394a5b71198d-line-determinant](item-lem-author-394a5b71198d-line-determinant)

## Proof

1.1 The noetherian integral curve has a field as its generic local ring, and every other local ring is a one-dimensional regular local ring. By [F1] these rings are UFDs, so the curve is locally factorial. By [F2], Cartier divisors modulo principal divisors identify with Picard classes and with Weil divisors modulo principal divisors. By [F3] the latter quotient is precisely $\mathrm{CH}^1(C)$. [F1, F2, F3]

2.1 This is the actual first Chern map: a rational section of $L$ defines a Cartier divisor $D$ with $\mathcal O_C(D)\cong L$, and [F3] sends $L$ to the Weil divisor cycle of $D$. Thus the composed quotient isomorphism carries $[L]$ to $c_1(L)$, fixing the sign and the map, rather than identifying only groups. Its kernel is the trivial line class. Combining with [F4] gives the asserted equivalences; $\Lambda^1L=L$ gives the determinant statement. [F2, F3, F4, step 1.1] ∎
