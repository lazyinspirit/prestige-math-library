## Statement
Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $k$ be the article's algebraically closed field, let $X$ be a quasi-compact quasi-separated $k$-scheme, and let $V$ be a vector bundle on $X$. There is a quasi-compact quasi-separated $k$-scheme $Y$ and a morphism $q:Y\to X$ with the following properties.

The finitely many nonempty rank strata $X_\alpha$ of $V$ are open and closed. The scheme $Y$ is the finite disjoint union of schemes $Y_\alpha$ over $X_\alpha$, and on each $Y_\alpha$ the bundle $q^*V$ has a finite filtration by locally direct-summand subbundles whose successive quotients are line bundles. The filtration has length equal to the rank on $X_\alpha$; at rank zero it is the zero filtration and $Y_\alpha=X_\alpha$ with the identity map.

For every $i\in\mathbb Z$ and $j\geq0$, pullback
$$q^*:H^{i,j}_{\mathrm{mot}}(X,R)\longrightarrow H^{i,j}_{\mathrm{mot}}(Y,R)$$
is injective for $R=\mathbb Z$ and for $R=\mathbb Z/n$, $n>0$. With the explicit convention $H^{i,j}_{\mathrm{mot}}(-,R)=0$ for $j<0$, this also holds for all integer weights.

Write $L_{\alpha,s}$ for the line-quotient bundles on $Y_\alpha$ and $j_\alpha:Y_\alpha\hookrightarrow Y$ for the clopen inclusions. In the vector-bundle Grothendieck group,
$$[q^*V]=\sum_{\alpha,s}[j_{\alpha,*}L_{\alpha,s}],$$
where $j_{\alpha,*}L_{\alpha,s}$ is extension by zero: it is $L_{\alpha,s}$ on $Y_\alpha$ and zero on the other components. If $V$ has constant rank, $[q^*V]$ is a sum of global line-bundle classes on $Y$, with the empty sum in rank zero. In particular, equality of natural motivic Chern-class expressions of the same bidegree can be checked after pullback to this tower and hence on its rank components.

A vector bundle is a finite locally free $\mathcal O_X$-module, and a line bundle is one of rank one. A rank stratum is the locus where the locally constant rank has a specified value. Quasi-compact means every open cover has a finite subcover; quasi-separated means intersections of quasi-compact open subsets are quasi-compact. The vector-bundle Grothendieck group is generated by isomorphism classes of vector bundles subject to $[E]=[E']+[E'']$ for short exact sequences $0\to E'\to E\to E''\to0$. The motivic groups and functorial pullbacks in nonnegative weights are those of [def-motivic-cohomology](item-def-motivic-cohomology).

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC, the field $k$, the qcqs $k$-scheme $X$, and the vector bundle $V$ as in the statement.

**Goal:** Construct the componentwise line-quotient tower and establish motivic detection and the stated class formulas.

[F1] For a finite family of finite locally free bundles on a qcqs scheme, [lem-flag-tower-line-quotients](item-lem-flag-tower-line-quotients) constructs a finite clopen rank partition and a qcqs componentwise flag tower with locally split line-quotient filtrations. Rank-zero towers are identities. Its construction assumes AC.

[F2] [lem-flag-tower-cohomology-detection](item-lem-flag-tower-cohomology-detection) proves that this componentwise tower detects the actual motivic cohomology of qcqs schemes over the stated field for every cohomological degree and nonnegative weight, integrally and with finite coefficients. It also proves the componentwise class formulas and their global clopen extensions by zero. It does not assert injectivity on the vector-bundle Grothendieck group.

[F3] [prop-motivic-projective-bundle-formula](item-prop-motivic-projective-bundle-formula) supplies the explicit zero extension in negative weights and the functorial motivic theory in all integer bidegrees. [def-motivic-cohomology](item-def-motivic-cohomology) supplies the nonnegative-weight groups and their functorial pullbacks.

[F4] AC is the choice assumption used by the flag construction. [math:def-axiom-of-choice](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/def-axiom-of-choice)

## Proof

**Proof technique:** Construct the flag tower separately on the finite clopen rank strata and apply motivic detection componentwise.

1.1 The rank of $V$ is locally constant because $V$ is locally free of finite rank. Its rank fibers are open, and each complement is the union of the other fibers, so each is also closed. They cover $X$; quasi-compactness gives only finitely many nonempty fibers. Apply [F1] to $V$ on each fiber and take the finite disjoint union of the resulting towers. This gives $q:Y\to X$ with $Y$ qcqs, the asserted locally split filtrations, and the identity tower and zero filtration on every rank-zero stratum. [given, F1, F4, construct]

2.1 By [F2], pullback along the tower of step 1.1 is injective on $H^{i,j}_{\mathrm{mot}}(-,R)$ for every integer $i$, every $j\geq0$, and both specified kinds of coefficients. If $j<0$, the source and target are zero under the explicit convention in [F3], so pullback is injective there as well. [F2, F3, step 1.1]

2.2 On each component the short exact sequences associated with the filtration give the sum of the classes of its line quotients. By [F2], extension by zero along the clopen component inclusions yields the displayed equality in the global vector-bundle Grothendieck group. When the rank is constant, the tower and its filtration are global, so the summands are global line bundles; when the rank is zero the sum is empty. The supported extensions in the varying-rank formula need not themselves be global line bundles. [F2, step 1.1]

3.1 Let $u$ and $v$ be natural motivic Chern-class expressions of the same bidegree on $X$. Naturality identifies their pullbacks with the corresponding expressions formed from the pulled-back data. If those expressions agree on every $Y_\alpha$, they agree on the finite disjoint union $Y$ by the finite-clopen motivic restriction isomorphism included in [F2]. Thus $q^*(u-v)=0$, and injectivity from step 2.1 gives $u=v$. No injectivity assertion for the Grothendieck group is used. [F2, F3, step 2.1]

4.1 Steps 1.1 through 3.1 prove the stated construction, detection, class formulas and identity-checking consequence. [step 1.1, step 2.1, step 2.2, step 3.1] ∎
