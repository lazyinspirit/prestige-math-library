## Statement
On the specified small category of separated finite-type schemes over an arbitrary field $k$, the topology $J_{\mathrm{MVW}}$ of MVW Definition 12.23 equals the topology $J_{\mathrm{cdp}}$ generated by Nisnevich covering families and completely decomposed proper morphisms. MVW's abstract blowups require an isomorphism on reduced open complements; the equality therefore also covers that convention, without assuming that the schemes are reduced.

## Facts & Assumptions
**Given:** The field and small scheme category of the Statement. A covering morphism means a morphism whose singleton family generates a covering sieve.

[F1] The external definition of $J_{\mathrm{MVW}}$ uses the same Nisnevich families and the maps $g:Z\amalg Y\to X$, where $Z\to X$ is a closed immersion, $Y\to X$ is proper, and the induced map on reduced open complements is an isomorphism (MVW Definitions 12.21 and 12.23). For a scheme $T$, its reduction $T_{\mathrm{red}}$ means the unique reduced closed subscheme with underlying space $|T|$; this construction is supplied by [thm-adjudicate-d1c21a67502d-reduced-closed-subscheme-input](item-thm-adjudicate-d1c21a67502d-reduced-closed-subscheme-input), applied to the whole space. The open complements, fibre products and coproducts are those in [def-cdh-topology-interface](item-def-cdh-topology-interface).

[F2] Import two precise source facts: every $J_{\mathrm{MVW}}$ covering morphism has a residue-field-preserving lift of each point (MVW Example 12.24), and every proper morphism with that lifting property is $J_{\mathrm{MVW}}$ covering (MVW Lemma 12.26). The pointwise lifting property is exactly complete decomposition, without the étaleness requirement of a Nisnevich family.

[F3] [def-cdh-topology-interface](item-def-cdh-topology-interface) defines $J_{\mathrm{cdp}}$ as the least topology containing Nisnevich families and singleton cdp families. Its Justification, step 1.1, proves that $Z\amalg Y\to X$ is proper whenever $Z\to X$ is a closed immersion and $Y\to X$ is proper, independently of any condition on the complements. It also constructs the finite coproduct in the chosen category. [def-nisnevich-covering-interface](item-def-nisnevich-covering-interface) supplies the same residue-field-lifting and étaleness definition of a Nisnevich family as MVW printed p. 91.

## External input
Only the source definition in F1 and the two lifting facts in F2 are imported from the MVW reference. Their hypotheses are precisely the category in the Statement. The reduced-scheme construction is supplied separately in F1. The equality of topologies is proved below; no flattening theorem or normal-form theorem is proved or needed for this comparison.

## Proof

**Goal:** Establish the statement under its stated hypotheses.
**Given:** The two generated topologies and facts F1–F3.

1.1 For each abstract-blowup generator $g:Z\amalg Y\to X$ of $J_{\mathrm{MVW}}$, F3 proves properness. By F1 its singleton is $J_{\mathrm{MVW}}$ covering, so F2 gives complete decomposition. Thus its singleton is a cdp generator and covers in $J_{\mathrm{cdp}}$. Every Nisnevich generating family also covers in $J_{\mathrm{cdp}}$, by the matching definitions in F3. Since this topology contains every generator of $J_{\mathrm{MVW}}$, the least-topology property gives $J_{\mathrm{MVW}}\subseteq J_{\mathrm{cdp}}$. This argument uses the source lifting fact rather than replacing reduced complements with unreduced complements. [F1, F2, F3]

2.1 Conversely, a cdp morphism is proper and has the pointwise lifting property, so F2 makes it $J_{\mathrm{MVW}}$ covering. Every Nisnevich family is also $J_{\mathrm{MVW}}$ covering by F1 and F3. Hence $J_{\mathrm{MVW}}$ contains every generator of $J_{\mathrm{cdp}}$, and minimality gives $J_{\mathrm{cdp}}\subseteq J_{\mathrm{MVW}}$. Together with step 1.1 this proves equality on the entire chosen category, including nonreduced objects. [F1, F2, F3, step 1.1] ∎
