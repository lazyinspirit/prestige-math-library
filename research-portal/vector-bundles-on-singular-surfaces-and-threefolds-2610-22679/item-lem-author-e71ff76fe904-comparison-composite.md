## Statement
Let $F=\mathbb Q$ or $\mathbb F_p$, let $j$ be a nonnegative integer and let $n\geq1$ be an integer invertible in $F$. Work with $D(\mathbb Z)$-valued presheaves on all qcqs $F$-schemes. Write $M_j=\mathbb Z(j)_{\mathrm{mot},F}$ for the source motivic functor, $B_j=R\Gamma_{\mathrm{et}}(-,\mu_n^{\otimes j})$ for the derived small-étale coefficient functor, and $\mathbb Z(j)_{\mathrm{cdh},F}$ for the source cdh-local classical-input complex. The slash denotes the objectwise derived quotient $A/n=\operatorname{cofib}(n:A\to A)=A\otimes^{\mathbf L}\mathbb Z/n$. Use the source qcqs cdh site generated by Nisnevich covers and abstract blowup families whose closed immersion and proper morphism are finitely presented. Here $L_{\mathrm{cdh}}$ is the source ordinary stable-valued sheafification, left adjoint to inclusion of derived cdh sheaves satisfying covering descent. Let $\theta_j:\mathbb Z(j)_{\mathrm{cdh},F}/n\xrightarrow{\sim}L_{\mathrm{cdh}}\tau^{\leq j}B_j$ be the source natural comparison. The map of interest is the specified composite
$$f_j:M_j/n\xrightarrow{\alpha_j}\mathbb Z(j)_{\mathrm{cdh},F}/n\xrightarrow{\theta_j}L_{\mathrm{cdh}}\tau^{\leq j}B_j\xrightarrow{\epsilon_j}B_j.$$
For each qcqs $F$-scheme $X$, its actual cofiber $C_j(X)=\operatorname{cofib}(f_j(X))$ has $H^a(C_j(X))=0$ for $a\leq j$. This is an objectwise statement about this composite, not only vanishing of its cdh sheafification.
