## Statement
Assume the Axiom of Choice and fix compatible nested Grothendieck universes containing $\omega$, with larger universes for categories and presheaf values. All scheme categories below are size-bounded, and the bounds contain the indicated schemes and fibre products. Fix a prime $p$, put $S=\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Z[1/p]$, and let $\mathcal C=\mathrm{Sch}^{\rm qcqs}_S$ with BEM's big cdh topology, generated by finite Nisnevich covers and finitely presented abstract blowup covers. For a field $k$ in which $p$ is invertible, put $U=\operatorname{Spec}k$ with the canonical map $U\to S$ induced by $\mathbb Z[1/p]\to k$. The slice $\mathcal C/U$ has objects $(V,f:V\to U)$ and arrows commuting with $f$; it is the category of qcqs $k$-schemes with the restricted BEM big cdh topology. The covering sieves of $(V,f)$ correspond exactly to the covering sieves of $V$ in $\mathcal C$.

Here $D(\mathbb Z)$ means the stable presentable infinity-category obtained by infinity-localizing unbounded cochain complexes of abelian groups at quasi-isomorphisms. A presheaf is a homotopy-coherent functor $N(\mathcal C)^{\rm op}\to D(\mathbb Z)$, or the corresponding functor on the slice; $N$ is the ordinary category nerve, whose $n$-simplices are chains of $n$ composable arrows, with faces given by composition or omission and degeneracies by inserting identities. Limits in the descent condition are limits in this infinity-category, meaning terminal objects in the infinity-category of cones over the full coherent diagram. Both restriction $j^*$ along the forgetful functor $j:\mathcal C/U\to\mathcal C$ and $j_*Q(V)=Q(V\times_S U\to U)$ preserve ordinary cdh sheaves, where ordinary means covering-family derived Čech descent. The formula for $j_*$ is defined on all objects and arrows because these fibre products are qcqs.

## Facts & Assumptions
[F1] [def-scheme-sites-and-sheafification](item-def-scheme-sites-and-sheafification) supplies schemes over a base, sites, covering sieves and the size convention. [thm-author-2ac325480ae8-bem-finite-comparison](item-thm-author-2ac325480ae8-bem-finite-comparison) specifies the BEM big cdh site. Reference 2 records the precise generating families and their stability under base change. The universe convention is [math:def-grothendieck-universe-closure-convention](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/def-grothendieck-universe-closure-convention); the existence of the stated universes is assumed separately from Choice.

[A1] [math:def-axiom-of-choice](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/def-axiom-of-choice) is retained when invoking the unbounded resolution/model construction in [thm-author-4f716d60801a-derived-enhancement-input](item-thm-author-4f716d60801a-derived-enhancement-input), applied to the ring $\mathbb Z$. This supplies the stable presentable enhancement used here. Choice also permits a simultaneous selection of fibre-product representatives for the set of diagrams in the size-bounded scheme categories; maps between those representatives are then fixed uniquely by their projections. The slice and product identifications themselves use universal properties.

[F2] Qcqs has the absolute scheme meaning in [math:def-quasi-compact-and-quasi-separated-scheme](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/def-quasi-compact-and-quasi-separated-scheme). Scheme fibre products have the universal property in [math:def-fibre-product-schemes-universal-property](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/def-fibre-product-schemes-universal-property) and exist by [math:thm-fibre-products-of-schemes-exist](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/thm-fibre-products-of-schemes-exist). Reference 0 supplies their qcqs closure when the two factors and the base are qcqs, and also supplies that affine schemes are qcqs. The map $\mathbb Z\to k$ extends uniquely to $\mathbb Z[1/p]\to k$ by [math:thm-universal-property-of-localisation](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/thm-universal-property-of-localisation), since $p$ is a unit. It induces $U\to S$ by [math:thm-affine-scheme-ring-anti-equivalence](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/thm-affine-scheme-ring-anti-equivalence).

[F3] By the enhancement in [A1] and the completeness input in reference 1, all small products and cosimplicial limits used below exist in $D(\mathbb Z)$. For a covering family $\{V_i\to V\}_{i\in I}$, derived Čech descent means that the augmentation $P(V)\to\lim_{[n]\in\Delta}\prod_{(i_0,\ldots,i_n)\in I^{n+1}}P(V_{i_0}\times_V\cdots\times_V V_{i_n})$ is an equivalence. Here $\Delta$ has the standard ordered sets $[n]=\{0,\ldots,n\}$, $n\geq0$, as objects and order-preserving maps as arrows; faces, degeneracies and the coherent cosimplicial diagram come from the covering family. The empty cover requires $P(\emptyset)$ to be terminal. When written $L_{\mathcal C}$ or $L_{\mathcal C/U}$, sheafification means BEM's ordinary reflective localization for this descent condition. No identification with a hypercomplete finite-type site is asserted.

[F4] Coherent diagrams and functor infinity-categories are as in reference 1; the arrows of $\operatorname{Fun}(K,D(\mathbb Z))$ are coherent natural transformations. Both displayed presheaf functors are precomposition functors on these categories. Explicitly $j^*P(V,f)=P(V)$, and $j_*Q=Q\circ N(b)^{\rm op}$ for $b(V)=(V\times_S U\to U)$ and $b(h)=h\times_S\operatorname{id}_U$. After the representative selection in [A1], the product universal property makes $b$ an ordinary functor, so precomposition on nerves gives the stated coherent presheaf functor.

## Proof
**Given:** The stated $p,k,S,U,\mathcal C$, universe bounds, Choice, and the enhanced coefficient conventions of [A1] and [F3].

**Goal:** Identify the actual slice site and verify the two preservation assertions for the displayed coherent presheaf functors.

1.1 The schemes $S$ and $U$ are affine, hence qcqs by [F2]; the localized ring map in [F2] gives $U\to S$. Applying the fibre-product existence and qcqs permanence inputs to $V\to S\leftarrow U$ defines $b(V)$ in the slice. Applying the same inputs repeatedly over any qcqs $V$ shows that every multiple intersection in a covering Čech nerve is an object of the site, both before and after base change. The maps $b(h)$ are compatible with identities and composition by the unique map property of fibre products. A map $V\to U$ in $\mathcal C$ is exactly a qcqs $k$-scheme. Every arrow $W\to V$ acquires the unique composite map to $U$, giving $(\mathcal C/U)/(V,f)\cong\mathcal C/V$ and identifying their sieves. A generating Nisnevich or abstract blowup family over $V$ lifts uniquely over $U$, and forgetting such a lift recovers that family. Since the topology is generated by these families, covering sieves agree under this identification. [A1, F1, F2, F4, construct]

2.1 For a cdh sheaf $P$ on $\mathcal C$, a covering Čech diagram over $(V,f)$ has precisely the underlying diagram over $V$ under the identification in step 1.1. Thus its coherent descent augmentation for $j^*P$ is that for $P$, and is an equivalence by [F3]. For a slice sheaf $Q$ and a cover $\{V_i\to V\}$ in $\mathcal C$, [F1] makes $\{V_i\times_S U\to V\times_S U\}$ a cover in the slice. For every tuple, the universal property gives $(V_{i_0}\times_V\cdots\times_V V_{i_n})\times_S U\cong(V_{i_0}\times_S U)\times_{V\times_S U}\cdots\times_{V\times_S U}(V_{i_n}\times_S U)$. These isomorphisms commute with every face, degeneracy and augmentation, since both sides represent the same compatible tuples of test morphisms. Applying the coherent functor $Q$ therefore identifies the full descent diagram for $j_*Q$ at $V$ with the descent diagram for $Q$ on that pulled-back cover. Their limits and augmentation maps are identified, so descent for $Q$ proves descent for $j_*Q$. The same argument handles the empty cover, as $\emptyset\times_S U=\emptyset$. Both assertions concern the actual BEM big sites and their ordinary derived Čech sheaves. [A1, F1, F2, F3, F4, step 1.1] ∎
