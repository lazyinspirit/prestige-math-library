## Statement
If A is an abelian variety over an algebraically closed field k, then for every positive integer n the multiplication map n: A(k) → A(k) is surjective, including when the characteristic divides n.

An abelian variety is a proper geometrically integral group scheme of finite type over k; its k-rational points form an abelian group. Multiplication by n is repeated use of the group law.

## External input
This is the positive-integer consequence of Proposition 39.9.11(4), with part (3) recording the corresponding scheme-level surjectivity. It is imported from the literature; no local reconstruction of the multiplication theorem is claimed.
