None.

## Statement
For a rational vector space $V$ and a positive integer $n$, multiplication by $n$ is an automorphism of the underlying abelian group. A rational vector space is a module over the field $\mathbb Q$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A rational vector space $V$ and an integer $n>0$.

**Goal:** Show that $n:V\to V$ is an automorphism of abelian groups.

## Proof

1.1 The scalar $1/n$ belongs to $\mathbb Q$. Multiplication by $1/n$ is additive and is both a left and right inverse to multiplication by $n$, by the module axioms. [given, algebra]

2.1 Thus multiplication by $n$ is an automorphism, as claimed. [step 1.1] ∎
