## Statement refuted
Trivial Chern classes force every rank-two algebraic vector bundle on every smooth complex affine variety to be trivial. In particular, this proposed criterion would assert that a rank-two bundle with all positive integral Chow Chern classes zero on a smooth complex affine fourfold is isomorphic to the trivial rank-two bundle.

## Counterexample

1.1 By [F1], choose one smooth complex affine fourfold X and nontrivial rank-two E with an actual stable isomorphism $E\oplus\mathcal O_X^N\cong\mathcal O_X^{2+N}$ for some $N\geq1$. This is an existence witness supplied by the explicitly imported established construction, rather than a newly specified system of equations. Its base field is exactly $\mathbb C$, its algebraic dimension is four, and it is affine, with the conventions in [F6]. The imported variety is integral, so its irreducible space has itself as its only maximal irreducible closed subset; it is therefore equidimensional of dimension four. By [F7], its coordinate algebra is finitely generated over the Noetherian field $\mathbb C$, hence Noetherian. The single affine chart makes $X$ Noetherian, and the affine quasi-compactness and quasi-separatedness results make it qcqs. Thus the geometric hypotheses of the invariant and both Chern suppliers hold. [A1, F1, F6, F7]

2.1 For $A=\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X)$, use [F6] to identify the affine scheme $X$ with $\operatorname{Spec}A$. Then [F2] constructs a right-invertible matrix $q:A^{2+N}\to A^N$ with E as its kernel bundle. The imported nontriviality means exactly that this q has no completion to an invertible matrix: a completion would give a global frame of the kernel. Thus the bundle is nontrivial as an algebraic bundle even though its finite free stabilization is trivial. The no-completion assertion follows from the imported existence and local equivalence; no independent calculation of matrix entries is claimed. [A1, F1, F2, F6, step 1.1]

3.1 By [F3], this actual stable isomorphism gives $[E]=2[\mathcal O_X]$ in perfect spectral $K_0(X)$ and $\det E\cong\mathcal O_X$. Neither equality cancels a bundle summand; E remains nontrivial by step 2.1. [A1, F3, step 1.1, step 2.1]

4.1 Apply [F4] independently to the ordinary Chow Chern operation and to the actual motivic operation fixed in [F5]. The stable isomorphism gives $c_t(E)c_t(\mathcal O_X^N)=c_t(\mathcal O_X^{2+N})$, so both trivial-bundle series being 1 implies $c_t(E)=1$. Every coefficient $c_i(E)$ for $i\geq1$ is therefore zero in ordinary integral Chow groups, and likewise for the article\'s motivic Chern classes. This deduction uses the actual Whitney identity, not an identification of maps from their targets alone. [A1, F4, F5, step 1.1, step 3.1]

5.1 The pair (X,E) consequently satisfies all the smooth complex affine fourfold and rank-two hypotheses of the proposed criterion and has all its positive Chern classes zero, while $E\not\cong\mathcal O_X^2$. It refutes that criterion and preserves the article\'s stronger stable-triviality assertion. [A1, step 1.1, step 2.1, step 4.1] ∎
