## Statement
Assume the Axiom of Choice and the fixed compatible site and universe bounds of the supplied constructions. Let $U$ be a Noetherian finite-dimensional scheme that is regular at every point and $i:T\hookrightarrow U$ any closed subscheme with defining ideal $\mathcal I=\ker(\mathcal O_U\to i_*\mathcal O_T)$. Let $K_0^{|T|}(U)=\pi_0K_{|T|}(U)$ for the specified nonconnective K-theory of the current supported perfect category. There is a canonical isomorphism
$$K_0^{|T|}(U)\xrightarrow{\sim}G_0(T).$$
For $X=T$ or $U$, $G_0(X)$ is the abelian group generated by isomorphism classes $[F]$ of coherent $\mathcal O_X$-modules, subject to $[F]=[F']+[F'']$ for every short exact sequence $0\to F'\to F\to F''\to0$. For a supported perfect object $E$, this isomorphism is the coherent-cohomology Euler class followed by ideal-power dévissage, explicitly
$$[E]\longmapsto\sum_n(-1)^n\sum_{r\geq0}[\mathcal I^rH^n(E)/\mathcal I^{r+1}H^n(E)].$$
Each quotient is regarded as a coherent $\mathcal O_T$-module; both sums are finite after deleting zero terms, and the resulting class is independent of the chosen annihilating powers. The inverse sends $[F]$ to the class of the perfect $U$-object $i_*F[0]$.

Under this isomorphism the support-to-global map $K_0^{|T|}(U)\to K_0(U)$ is coherent pushforward $G_0(T)\xrightarrow{i_*}G_0(U)$ followed by the regular ambient perfect comparison $G_0(U)\xrightarrow{\sim}K_0(U)$, sending $[M]$ to $[M[0]]$. No regularity of $T$, global vector-bundle resolution or resolution-property assumption is imposed. No analogous identification for singular ambient $U$ is asserted.

## Facts & Assumptions

[A1] The assumed [math:def-axiom-of-choice](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/def-axiom-of-choice) is used through the essentially small perfect-category presentations and the specified nonconnective K-theory model in [F1] and [F3]. The fixed universes satisfy [math:def-grothendieck-universe-closure-convention](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/def-grothendieck-universe-closure-convention), contain $\omega$ and the scheme data, and allow the enlargements required by these constructions. Universe existence is a standing size assumption separate from Choice; the site bounds remain those fixed in the Statement. These assumptions are retained in all three steps, with no additional choice needed for the finite sums.

**Given:** $U,T,i,\mathcal I$ as in the Statement, AC and fixed suitable universes. The supported category is the supplied full sheaf-derived $\mathrm{Perf}_{|T|}(U)$, not complexes internally constructed in a coherent abelian category.

**Goal:** Prove the stated comparison and support-to-global compatibility in the current nonconnective model.

[F1] Let $\mathcal A_T$ denote the category of coherent $\mathcal O_U$-modules whose stalks vanish outside the underlying closed set $|T|$, with all module-sheaf morphisms. Its group $G_0(\mathcal A_T)$ uses the same isomorphism-class generators and short exact relations as $G_0(X)$ above. [lem-author-7ef3227685d9-supported-k0-heart](item-lem-author-7ef3227685d9-supported-k0-heart) embeds each $M\in\mathcal A_T$ as the actual supported perfect object $M[0]$, the complex with $M$ in degree zero and zero in all other degrees. [lem-author-7ef3227685d9-supported-k0-euler](item-lem-author-7ef3227685d9-supported-k0-euler) and [lem-author-7ef3227685d9-supported-k0-postnikov](item-lem-author-7ef3227685d9-supported-k0-postnikov) give the finite additive coherent Euler map and its Postnikov inverse, using the general spectral $\pi_0$ premise [thm-author-3a003d7b5208-nonconnective-k-zero-input](item-thm-author-3a003d7b5208-nonconnective-k-zero-input). These comparisons commute with support inclusion, and the same construction with $T=U$ gives the ambient comparison. The Euler supplier also checks that Noetherian $U$ is qcqs, as required by the selected nonconnective interface.

[F2] [lem-author-7ef3227685d9-supported-k0-ideal-filtration](item-lem-author-7ef3227685d9-supported-k0-ideal-filtration) and [lem-author-7ef3227685d9-supported-k0-devissage-naturality](item-lem-author-7ef3227685d9-supported-k0-devissage-naturality) give finite ideal-power dévissage, its inverse formula and coherent pushforward compatibility. Here $\mathcal I^0M=M$ and $\mathcal I^rM$ is the subsheaf locally generated by products of $r$ sections of $\mathcal I$ acting on sections of $M$. Closed direct image is $(i_*F)(V)=F(i^{-1}V)$, with its $\mathcal O_U$-action induced by the structure-sheaf map. These suppliers give an exact equivalence between coherent sheaves on $T$ and coherent sheaves on $U$ annihilated by $\mathcal I$, so the displayed quotient sheaves on $T$ are unambiguously defined. They supply exactness of closed coherent direct image and the resulting map on short exact presentations.

[F3] [def-scheme-perfect-complexes](item-def-scheme-perfect-complexes) and [def-nonconnective-k-spectrum-interface](item-def-nonconnective-k-spectrum-interface) supply the full stable idempotent-complete supported categories, their essentially small presentations and the actual support inclusion. [F4] The geometric formulation uses [math:def-locally-noetherian-and-noetherian-scheme](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/def-locally-noetherian-and-noetherian-scheme) for the finite affine Noetherian cover and [math:def-regular-local-ring-geometric-point](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/def-regular-local-ring-geometric-point) for regularity at every point, meaning regularity of every local ring without any base-field smoothness assumption. ## Proof
1.1 By [F1], the degree-zero group of the current nonconnective functor identifies with $G_0(\mathcal A_T)$ by the coherent-cohomology Euler map. By [F2], closed coherent pushforward gives $G_0(T)\simeq G_0(\mathcal A_T)$. Compose the former map with the inverse of the latter. This is an isomorphism, and its displayed formula is exactly the Euler sum followed by the ideal-power quotient sum. Finiteness and independence follow from [F1]–[F2]. [F1, F2, F3, F4, A1]

2.1 The two inverse constructions take $[F]$ first to $[i_*F]$ and then to the class of $i_*F[0]$ by [F1]–[F2]. By the exact closed-direct-image identification in [F2], $i_*F$ lies in $\mathcal A_T$; the heart embedding in [F1] therefore supplies this very degree-zero sheaf as an actual supported perfect object. Thus the inverse has the asserted actual-object interpretation without any global locally free resolution. [F1, F2, F3, step 1.1]

3.1 The Euler/Postnikov comparisons commute with supported-to-global inclusion by [F1]. Under abelian dévissage the corresponding map is precisely coherent pushforward by [F2]. Their commutative squares compose to the stated support-to-global formula, using the same ambient comparison with $T=U$. This establishes the full proposition in the specified nonconnective model. [F1, F2, F3, step 1.1, step 2.1] ∎
