## Statement

Let $(W,S)$ be a Coxeter system with generators $S=\{s_i:i\in\Gamma_0\}$ and Coxeter matrix $m$, and let $J=\varnothing$. Its Brink–Howlett groupoid has one object, $\varnothing$, and its endomorphism group is $W$. Each elementary wall-crossing arrow $v_{a,\varnothing}$ has label $s_a$.

For distinct vertices $a,b$, the two-generator arrow $v_{a,b,\varnothing}$ is defined precisely when $s_as_b$ has finite order $m=m(s_a,s_b)$. In that case its label is the longest element of $W_{\{a,b\}}=\langle s_a,s_b\rangle$, represented by each of the two alternating words of length $m$, one beginning with $s_a$ and the other with $s_b$. The atomic braid relation equates these two words. The Brink–Howlett presentation therefore specializes to the Coxeter presentation: generators $s_a$, relations $s_a^2=1$, and the alternating braid relation of length $m(s_a,s_b)$ for each distinct pair with finite matrix entry.

## Proof


**Given:** A Coxeter system as in the Statement, with $J=\varnothing$ and distinct vertices $a,b$ when a pair is considered. The length $\ell$ is the minimum number of simple generators in a word representing an element. A standard expression is a product of elementary arrows whose Coxeter lengths sum to the length of its label. [def-coxeter-data-artin-tits-normalisers](item-def-coxeter-data-artin-tits-normalisers) [def-atomic-braid-artin-tits-normalisers](item-def-atomic-braid-artin-tits-normalisers)

[F1] The empty set is its only associate and $v_{a,\varnothing}=s_a$ for every vertex $a$. [rem-empty-j-wall-crossing-artin-tits-normalisers](item-rem-empty-j-wall-crossing-artin-tits-normalisers)

[F2] A Brink–Howlett arrow $I\to K$ is labelled by an element carrying the simple-root set of $K$ onto that of $I$; composition multiplies labels from left to right. [def-brink-howlett-artin-tits-normalisers](item-def-brink-howlett-artin-tits-normalisers)

[F3] For $T\subseteq\Gamma_0$, $W_T$ is generated by the $s_t$ with $t\in T$, and $\Phi_T=\{w\alpha_t:w\in W_T,\ t\in T\}$. In particular $\Phi_\varnothing=\varnothing$. [def-parabolic-subgroups-artin-tits-normalisers](item-def-parabolic-subgroups-artin-tits-normalisers)

[F4] For distinct $a,b$, the two-generator construction is defined when $\Phi_{\{a,b\}}$ is finite. In that case its label is $q=w_Dw_{D\cap\varnothing}$, where $D$ is the union of components of the diagram on $\{a,b\}$ meeting $\{a,b\}$; $D$ is spherical, meaning $W_D$ is finite. It has exactly two standard expressions, one beginning with each elementary arrow. [lem-author-a7d202611130-finite-relative-longest](item-lem-author-a7d202611130-finite-relative-longest) [lem-two-generator-minimal-artin-tits-normalisers](item-lem-two-generator-minimal-artin-tits-normalisers)

[F5] The rank-two parabolic is the Coxeter group for the restricted matrix, with intrinsic length equal to ambient length. For finite $m(s_a,s_b)=m$, it has $2m$ elements and its unique longest element has length $m$ and is the alternating word of length $m$ beginning with $s_a$; the same assertion holds with $a,b$ exchanged. For infinite $m(s_a,s_b)$ the alternating words of increasing length are distinct. The order of $s_as_b$ is exactly $m(s_a,s_b)$, with infinity interpreted as infinite order. [math:thm-arbitrary-rank-finite-parabolics-and-bruhat-subword](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/thm-arbitrary-rank-finite-parabolics-and-bruhat-subword) [math:lem-cg-rank-two-prefix-and-chamber-length-induction](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/lem-cg-rank-two-prefix-and-chamber-length-induction) [math:lem-hh-dihedral-root-recurrence-and-root-sign](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/lem-hh-dihedral-root-recurrence-and-root-sign)

[F6] The Brink–Howlett presentation has the elementary arrows as generators and the atomic braid relations and elementary inverse-pair relations as defining relations. An atomic relation is the equality of the two standard expressions supplied for a distinct pair with finite relative root difference. [thm-brink-howlett-presentation-artin-tits-normalisers](item-thm-brink-howlett-presentation-artin-tits-normalisers) [def-atomic-braid-artin-tits-normalisers](item-def-atomic-braid-artin-tits-normalisers)



**Proof technique:** direct.

**Goal:** identify the arrows and all defining relations when $J=\varnothing$.

1.1 **The groupoid and its generators.** By [F1], the only object is $\varnothing$. The root-set condition in [F2] is vacuous for an arrow from $\varnothing$ to itself, so every element of $W$ is its label, and composition is multiplication in $W$. The elementary labels are $s_a$ by [F1]. [F1, F2]

1.2 **When the two-generator construction applies.** Put $T=\{a,b\}$. If $m(s_a,s_b)$ is finite, [F5] makes $W_T$ finite, so [F3] makes $\Phi_T$ finite. Conversely, if $\Phi_T$ is finite, [F4] makes $W_D$ finite. Every component of the diagram on $T$ meets $T$, so $D=T$; the infinite case of [F5] would give infinitely many elements of $W_T$. Thus the construction applies exactly for finite $m(s_a,s_b)$, equivalently for finite order of $s_as_b$. [F3, F4, F5, given]

2.1 **The atomic words.** In the finite case, $D=T$ and $w_{D\cap\varnothing}=1$, so [F4] identifies the two-generator label with the longest element $w_T$. By [F5], both alternating words of length $m$ are reduced expressions for $w_T$. By [F1] each letter is an elementary arrow at the sole object; hence both are standard expressions. They start with different letters, so [F4] says they are exactly the two expressions used in the atomic relation. [F1, F4, F5, step 1.2]

3.1 **Specialize the presentation.** By [F1] and [F2], the inverse of the elementary arrow labelled $s_a$ is itself, since $s_a^2=1$ in the Coxeter system. Thus the inverse-pair relations in [F6] become $s_a^2=1$. By steps 1.2 and 2.1, the atomic relations are exactly the two alternating words of length $m(s_a,s_b)$ for each finite distinct pair. By [F6] these are all defining relations, giving the stated Coxeter presentation of the endomorphism group identified in step 1.1. [F1, F2, F6, step 1.1, step 1.2, step 2.1] ∎
