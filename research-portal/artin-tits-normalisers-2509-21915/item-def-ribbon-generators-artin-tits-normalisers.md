## Definition

Let $\Gamma$ be a spherical Coxeter diagram, meaning that its Coxeter group $W$ is finite, with vertex set $\Gamma_0$ and associated Artin–Tits group $A$. Let $A^+$ be the submonoid of $A$ generated by the standard Artin generators. For $J\subseteq\Gamma_0$, let $w_J$ be the longest element of the standard parabolic subgroup $W_J$, and let $\Delta_J$ be its positive reduced-word lift in $A$.

Let $I\subseteq\Gamma_0$ and $a\in I^c=\Gamma_0\setminus I$. Write $I+a=I\cup\{a\}$, and let $[I,a]$ be the vertex set of the connected component containing $a$ in the diagram induced on $I+a$. Write $[I,a]-a=[I,a]\setminus\{a\}$. The **positive elementary ribbon element** associated to $(a,I)$ is

$$\mu_{a,I}=\Delta_{[I,a]-a}^{-1}\Delta_{[I,a]}\in A^+.$$

Equivalently, $\mu_{a,I}=\Delta_I^{-1}\Delta_{I+a}$. Inverses and products in these expressions are taken in $A$.

## Justification

The spherical Coxeter system has finite rank, since its distinct simple generators belong to the finite group $W$. Every standard parabolic subgroup is finite. Hence [def-longest-elements-artin-tits-normalisers](item-def-longest-elements-artin-tits-normalisers) supplies each displayed longest element and its well-defined positive lift. The component and set operations are supplied by [def-connected-support-artin-tits-normalisers](item-def-connected-support-artin-tits-normalisers) and [def-set-notation-artin-tits-normalisers](item-def-set-notation-artin-tits-normalisers), and the quotient is defined in the group $A$.

Put $K=[I,a]$ and $J=K-a$. In the spherical Artin system on $K$, the positive lift of $w_J\in W_K$ is a simple element, hence a left divisor of $\Delta_K$, by [thm-spherical-artin-garside-input-artin-tits-prerequisites](item-thm-spherical-artin-garside-input-artin-tits-prerequisites). Thus $\Delta_K=\Delta_J b$ for a positive element $b$ of that system. Mapping its standard generators to the corresponding generators of $A$ preserves the braid relations, so the same equality holds in $A$ and $b\in A^+$. Therefore $\Delta_J^{-1}\Delta_K=b\in A^+$.

Let $H=(I+a)\setminus K$. There are no diagram edges between $H$ and $K$, and $I=H\sqcup J$. The disconnected-factor result [prop-finite-opposition-interface-artin-tits-prerequisites](item-prop-finite-opposition-interface-artin-tits-prerequisites) gives $w_I=w_Hw_J$ and $w_{I+a}=w_Hw_K$, with lengths additive in both products. By [prop-positive-lift-interface-artin-tits-prerequisites](item-prop-positive-lift-interface-artin-tits-prerequisites), $\Delta_I=\Delta_H\Delta_J$ and $\Delta_{I+a}=\Delta_H\Delta_K$. Group cancellation now gives $\Delta_I^{-1}\Delta_{I+a}=\Delta_J^{-1}\Delta_K=\mu_{a,I}$. The argument also covers empty $H$ or $J$, using the identity as the empty lift.
