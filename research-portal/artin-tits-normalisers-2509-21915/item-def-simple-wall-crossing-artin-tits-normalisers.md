## Definition

Let $(W,S)$ be a Coxeter system indexed by the vertex set $\Gamma_0$, and fix $J\subseteq\Gamma_0$. Write $W_L$ for the subgroup generated by the simple generators indexed by $L\subseteq\Gamma_0$. Let $(x,I)\in\mathrm{Cham}(J)$, where $\mathrm{Cham}(J)$ is the algebraic chamber-label set: $W_Jx=xW_I$ and $x$ has minimum length in $xW_I$.

Choose $a\in\Gamma_0\setminus I$. Put $D=[I,a]$, the vertices of the connected component containing $a$ in the Coxeter diagram induced on $I\cup\{a\}$, and suppose $W_D$ is finite. For each finite standard parabolic $W_L$, write $w_L$ for its longest element and $\iota_L$ for the permutation of $L$ determined by $w_L\alpha_i=-\alpha_{\iota_L(i)}$ on its simple roots. Define the **simple wall-crossing element** and its associated pair by

$$v_{a,I}=w_{D\setminus\{a\}}w_D,\qquad K=(I\cup\{a\})\setminus\{\iota_D(a)\},\qquad \omega_{a,I}(x,I)=(xv_{a,I},K).$$

For fixed $J$, $I$ and $a$, this defines $\omega_{a,I}$ on the labels $(x,I)\in\mathrm{Cham}(J)$ satisfying the stated finiteness condition, with values in $W\times\mathcal P(\Gamma_0)$, where $\mathcal P(\Gamma_0)$ denotes the set of all subsets of $\Gamma_0$.

## Justification

The chamber-label definition supplies $W$, its standard parabolics, length and the set $\mathrm{Cham}(J)$. The connected-support definition makes $D$ a uniquely determined subset of $I\cup\{a\}$ containing $a$. Since $W_{D\setminus\{a\}}$ is a subgroup of the finite group $W_D$, it is finite. The longest-element definition therefore supplies both displayed longest elements and the permutation $\iota_D$; in particular $\iota_D(a)\in D\subseteq\Gamma_0$. Thus $v_{a,I}\in W$, $K\subseteq\Gamma_0$, and the displayed rule gives a unique pair in the stated codomain for every input. This justification does not assert that the output pair is a chamber label or that it represents an adjacent chamber.
