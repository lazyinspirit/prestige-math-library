## Statement

Let $\Gamma$ be a finite-rank spherical Coxeter diagram with vertex set $\Gamma_0$, Coxeter group $W$, length $\ell$, simple generators $s_i$, and simple roots $\alpha_i$. Let $A$ be its Artin–Tits group with standard generators $\sigma_i$ and positive submonoid $A^+$ generated by them. Fix $J\subseteq\Gamma_0$ and a chamber label $(x,I)$, meaning $W_Jx=xW_I$ and $x$ has minimum length in $xW_I$. Choose $a\in\Gamma_0\setminus I$, put $T=I\cup\{a\}$, and put $K=T\setminus\{\iota_T(a)\}$. Here $W_L$ is the subgroup generated by $\{s_i:i\in L\}$, $w_L$ is its longest element, and $\iota_L$ is its opposition permutation, defined by $w_L\alpha_i=-\alpha_{\iota_L(i)}$.

Let $v_{a,I}$ be the simple wall-crossing element and $\mu_{a,I}$ the positive elementary ribbon element. Then $\mu_{a,I}$ is the positive reduced-word lift of $v_{a,I}$ and belongs to $A^+$. Moreover $K$ is an associate of $J$, meaning $W_K$ is conjugate to $W_J$, and $\mu_{a,I}$ is a morphism $I\to K$ in the ribbon groupoid $\mathcal R'(J)$: explicitly, $A_I^+\mu_{a,I}=\mu_{a,I}A_K^+$, where $A_L^+$ is the submonoid of $A$ generated by $\{\sigma_i:i\in L\}$.

## Facts & Assumptions

[F1] Every standard parabolic is finite. Its longest element is an involution and sends each simple root to the negative of a simple root according to its opposition permutation. [prop-finite-opposition-interface-artin-tits-prerequisites](item-prop-finite-opposition-interface-artin-tits-prerequisites) [def-longest-elements-artin-tits-normalisers](item-def-longest-elements-artin-tits-normalisers)

[F2] With $T=I\cup\{a\}$, the wall element is $v_{a,I}=w_Iw_T$, and the target in the simple crossing is $T\setminus\{\iota_T(a)\}$. [lem-finite-parabolic-wall-formula-artin-tits-normalisers](item-lem-finite-parabolic-wall-formula-artin-tits-normalisers) [def-simple-wall-crossing-artin-tits-normalisers](item-def-simple-wall-crossing-artin-tits-normalisers)

[F3] The ribbon element is $\mu_{a,I}=\Delta_I^{-1}\Delta_T$, where $\Delta_L$ is the reduced-word lift of $w_L$. Reduced-word lifts are independent of the chosen reduced word and multiply when Coxeter lengths add. [def-ribbon-generators-artin-tits-normalisers](item-def-ribbon-generators-artin-tits-normalisers) [prop-positive-lift-interface-artin-tits-prerequisites](item-prop-positive-lift-interface-artin-tits-prerequisites)

[F4] A representative $d$ with $\ell(s_i d)>\ell(d)$ for all $i\in I$ satisfies $\ell(ud)=\ell(u)+\ell(d)$ for every $u\in W_I$. [math:thm-hh-parabolic-minimal-representatives-and-length-additivity](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/thm-hh-parabolic-minimal-representatives-and-length-additivity)

[F5] For a simple generator $s_i$, the root-length criterion detects whether multiplication by $s_i$ increases length. The reflection representation is faithful; its invariant form identifies conjugates of simple reflections with the reflections in the corresponding image roots. [math:thm-cg-root-length-criterion-and-faithfulness](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/thm-cg-root-length-criterion-and-faithfulness) [math:lem-cg-reflection-form-invariance-and-rank-two-orders](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/lem-cg-reflection-form-invariance-and-rank-two-orders) [conjugation of reflections, part (4)](https://alphabetamath.cc/item/lem-cg-reflection-representation-descends-and-root-norms)

[F6] The ribbon groupoid has associates of $J$ as objects and arrows $I\to K$ precisely the labels $\beta\in A$ satisfying $A_I^+\beta=\beta A_K^+$. [def-ribbon-groupoid-artin-tits-normalisers](item-def-ribbon-groupoid-artin-tits-normalisers)

## Proof
**Given:** The spherical Coxeter and Artin–Tits data, chamber label, and vertex $a$ in the Statement.


**Proof technique:** direct.

**Goal:** Identify the positive lift and establish the stated ribbon-groupoid membership.

1.1 Put $v=w_Iw_T$. For $i\in I$, [F1] gives $v^{-1}\alpha_i=w_Tw_I\alpha_i=\alpha_{\iota_T(\iota_I(i))}$. The map $i\mapsto\iota_T(\iota_I(i))$ is a bijection from $I$ to $K$: $\iota_I$ permutes $I$, and $\iota_T(I)=T\setminus\{\iota_T(a)\}$. Thus $v^{-1}\alpha_i$ is positive for every $i\in I$. By the root-length criterion, $\ell(s_i v)>\ell(v)$ for every such $i$. Applying [F4] with $u=w_I$ gives $\ell(w_Iv)=\ell(w_I)+\ell(v)$. Since $w_I^2=1$ and $w_Iv=w_T$, the lift multiplication rule gives $\Delta_T=\Delta_I\sigma_v$, where $\sigma_v$ is the reduced-word lift of $v$. Consequently $\mu_{a,I}=\Delta_I^{-1}\Delta_T=\sigma_v$. A reduced expression for $v$ is a word in the simple generators, so its Artin lift is a product of standard positive generators and belongs to $A^+$. By [F2], $v=v_{a,I}$. [F1, F2, F3, F4, F5]

2.1 For each $i\in I$, let $k=\iota_T(\iota_I(i))\in K$. Step 1.1 gives $v\alpha_k=\alpha_i$, and these pairs exhaust both $I$ and $K$. By the reflection conjugation formula and faithfulness, $v s_k v^{-1}=s_i$. The root-length criterion gives $\ell(vs_k)=\ell(v)+1$ and, because $v^{-1}\alpha_i=\alpha_k$ is positive, $\ell(s_i v)=\ell(v)+1$. Hence the length-additive lift rule yields $\sigma_i\sigma_v=\sigma_{s_i v}=\sigma_{v s_k}=\sigma_v\sigma_k$. Taking finite products and using the bijection of the two generating sets gives $A_I^+\sigma_v=\sigma_v A_K^+$. [F3, F5, step 1.1]

3.1 The equality $v s_k v^{-1}=s_i$ for the paired generators also gives $vW_Kv^{-1}=W_I$. Since $(x,I)$ is a chamber label, $W_I=x^{-1}W_Jx$; therefore $W_K$ is conjugate to $W_J$. Thus both $I$ and $K$ are objects of $\mathcal R'(J)$. Substituting $\mu_{a,I}=\sigma_v$ into the monoid equality from step 2.1 proves that it labels a morphism $I\to K$, as required. [F6, step 1.1, step 2.1] ∎
