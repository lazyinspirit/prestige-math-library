## Statement

For any Coxeter diagram and $J\subseteq\Gamma_0$, the groupoid $\mathcal{BH}$ is generated by the wall-crossing morphisms in Corollary 4.13(1), with defining relations all atomic braid relations and $v_{a,I}v_{a^\prime,K}=e$, where $a^\prime=\iota_{[I,a]}(a)$ and $K=I+a-a^\prime$.

## Facts & Assumptions

[F1] The arbitrary-rank Coxeter system, associates, Brink–Howlett groupoid, left-to-right label composition, component $D=[I,a]$, finite longest elements, opposition permutation and elementary labels $v_{a,I}=w_{D\setminus\{a\}}w_D$ are as in [def-standard-expressions-artin-tits-normalisers](item-def-standard-expressions-artin-tits-normalisers). Only the crossing component $W_D$ is required to be finite.

[F2] The elementary morphisms generate $\mathcal{BH}(J)$ by [cor-brink-howlett-generators-length-artin-tits-normalisers](item-cor-brink-howlett-generators-length-artin-tits-normalisers).

[F3] Atomic braid relations are those of [def-atomic-braid-artin-tits-normalisers](item-def-atomic-braid-artin-tits-normalisers). Path categories and their endpoint-preserving congruence quotients are defined in [def-path-localisation-interface-artin-tits-prerequisites](item-def-path-localisation-interface-artin-tits-prerequisites).

[F4] The quotient by all atomic braids and both ordered elementary inverse-pair quadratics is a groupoid with faithful label evaluation to $\mathcal{BH}(J)$ by [lem-author-7bd202611130-presentation-faithfulness](item-lem-author-7bd202611130-presentation-faithfulness). Its Proof, steps 1.1 and 2.1, establishes for every elementary edge that, with $b=\iota_D(a)$ and $K=(I\cup\{a\})\setminus\{b\}$, the reverse edge exists, $[K,b]=D$, $\iota_D(b)=a$ and $v_{b,K}=v_{a,I}^{-1}$. That argument applies the inverse calculation inside the finite Coxeter subsystem on $D$ and then identifies its labels in the ambient group; it imposes no ambient rank bound or finiteness assumption on $W_I$.

## Proof

**Given:** An arbitrary Coxeter diagram and $J\subseteq\Gamma_0$, with the notation and elementary morphisms of [F1] and inputs [F2]–[F4].

**Goal:** Establish the presentation in the Statement.

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 For each elementary arrow $v_{a,I}$, put $D=[I,a]$, $a'=\iota_D(a)$ and $K=(I\cup\{a\})\setminus\{a'\}$. By [F4], the reverse elementary arrow is $v_{a',K}=v_{a,I}^{-1}$, with $[K,a']=D$ and $\iota_D(a')=a$. Hence the displayed quadratic evaluates to the identity at $I$. The same displayed family applied to the reverse edge gives $v_{a',K}v_{a,I}=e$ at $K$, so this family contains both ordered inverse-pair relations. Conversely each relation in that ordered family is an instance of the displayed family. Thus the quadratic families agree for arbitrary ambient rank. [F1, F4]

2.1 Use [F3] to form the path-category quotient on the elementary-edge quiver by the atomic braids and the displayed quadratics. Step 1.1 identifies its relations with exactly those of [F4]. Therefore this quotient is a groupoid, and label evaluation is a faithful functor to $\mathcal{BH}(J)$, identical on objects. [F1, F3, F4, step 1.1]

3.1 By [F2], every Brink–Howlett morphism is the label of a finite composable elementary path. Thus evaluation is surjective on every hom-set. Its faithfulness makes it injective on every hom-set as well. Since it is the identity on objects, its inverse sends each morphism to its unique path class; this respects identities and composition because evaluation does and is injective. Evaluation is consequently an isomorphism of groupoids. By the definition of path presentation in [F3], the atomic braids and displayed quadratics are therefore defining relations, proving the Statement. [F2, F3, step 2.1] ∎
