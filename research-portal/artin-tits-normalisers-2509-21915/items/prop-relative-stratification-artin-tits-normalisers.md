## Statement

Let $(W,S)$ be a Coxeter system with finite generating set, in its canonical real reflection representation on $V$ with simple-root basis $(\alpha_i)_{i\in S}$ and dual space $\Theta$. For $L\subseteq S$, let $W_L$ be the subgroup generated by $L$, let $\Theta_L=\{f\in\Theta:f(\alpha_i)=0\text{ for }i\in L\}$, and let $C_L$ consist of the points of $\Theta_L$ that are positive on every $\alpha_i$ with $i\notin L$. Let $H_\alpha=\{f:f(\alpha)=0\}$ and let $\mathrm{Cone}=\bigcup_{x\in W}x\overline{C_\varnothing}$. Fix $J\subseteq S$ and put $\mathrm{Cone}(J)=\mathrm{Cone}\cap\Theta_J$. Its relative arrangement consists of the distinct hyperplanes $H_\alpha\cap\Theta_J$ for roots $\alpha$ whose hyperplanes do not contain $\Theta_J$. A chamber is a connected component of the complement of these hyperplanes in $\mathrm{Cone}(J)$; closures below are taken in $\Theta_J$.

The set $\mathrm{Cone}(J)$ is a convex cone and is the disjoint union of the sets $xC_L$ indexed by $L\subseteq S$ and $x\in W/W_L$ for which $xC_L\subseteq\Theta_J$. Its chambers are exactly those sets in this union with $|L|=|J|$. For such a chamber, its codimension-one faces are $xC_{L+i}$ for $i\in S\setminus L$, where $L+i=L\cup\{i\}$. The corresponding wall is the closure of this face and satisfies $\overline{xC_{L+i}}=H_{x\alpha_i}\cap\overline{xC_L}$; its supporting relative hyperplane is $H_{x\alpha_i}\cap\Theta_J$. When $J=\varnothing$, the same formula gives the stratification of the full Tits cone.

## Facts & Assumptions

[F1] The Tits cone is a convex cone, and the closed fundamental chamber meets each orbit in it once. A point of $C_L$ has stabilizer $W_L$. [[prop-tits-domain-interface-artin-tits-prerequisites]]

[F2] Every root has either nonnegative or nonpositive coordinates in the simple-root basis, and the dual action satisfies $(xf)(v)=f(x^{-1}v)$. [[prop-root-inversion-interface-artin-tits-prerequisites]]

[F3] If two points in the closed fundamental chamber are in the same $W$-orbit, they are equal, and any element carrying one to the other belongs to its stabilizer. [[math:thm-cg-dual-chamber-intersections-and-point-stabilizers]]

[F4] The chambers of the relative-interior arrangement are precisely the sets $xC_I$ labelled by pairs satisfying $W_Jx=xW_I$ and the stated minimum-length condition. [[thm-relative-chamber-labelling-input-artin-tits-prerequisites]]

[F5] The complements, and hence the chambers, of the full relative-cone and relative-interior arrangements coincide. [[lem-author-9b7c42e8f103-chamber-topology]]

[F6] Fundamental faces, relative hyperplanes, chamber closures and walls have the meanings in the Statement. The notation $L+i$ means $L\cup\{i\}$. [[def-parabolic-faces-artin-tits-normalisers]] [[def-relative-cone-artin-tits-normalisers]] [[def-chambers-walls-artin-tits-normalisers]] [[def-set-notation-artin-tits-normalisers]]

[F7] In the canonical real representation, $s(v)=v-2B(v,\alpha_s)\alpha_s$ and $B(\alpha_s,\alpha_s)=1$. The dual action is $(wf)(v)=f(w^{-1}v)$. [[def-real-coxeter-interface-artin-tits-prerequisites]]

## Proof
**Given:** The finite-rank Coxeter system, representation, subsets and notation of the Statement.


**Proof technique:** direct.

**Goal:** Prove the convexity, disjoint stratification, chamber characterization and face formula.

1.1 The intersection of a convex cone with the linear subspace $\Theta_J$ is a convex cone. Every point of $\mathrm{Cone}$ lies in some translate of the closed fundamental chamber, which is the disjoint union of its coordinate faces $C_L$. If $xC_L$ and $yC_K$ meet, [F3] applied to the corresponding points in the closed fundamental chamber gives $L=K$ and $y^{-1}x\in W_L$; conversely $W_L$ fixes $C_L$ pointwise by [F1]. Thus the full-cone face translates are disjoint when indexed by right cosets $W/W_L$. [F1, F3, F6, given]

2.1 For $f\in C_L$, a root $\beta$ has $f(\beta)=0$ exactly when all its nonzero simple-root coordinates are indexed by $L$: its coordinates have one sign by [F2], while $f$ vanishes on $L$ and is strictly positive elsewhere. Therefore whether $xf$ lies in $\Theta_J$ is independent of the choice of $f\in C_L$, since it is determined by the supports of the roots $x^{-1}\alpha_j$, $j\in J$. Each full-cone stratum is consequently either contained in or disjoint from $\Theta_J$. Intersecting the union in 1.1 with $\Theta_J$ gives the asserted disjoint stratification. [F2, F6, step 1.1]

3.1 Suppose $xC_L\subseteq\Theta_J$. Its linear span is $x\Theta_L$, so $x\Theta_L\subseteq\Theta_J$ and $|L|\geq|J|$. If equality holds, the two subspaces have the same dimension and hence coincide. A point $xf$ with $f\in C_L$ lies on $H_\alpha$ only if $x^{-1}\alpha$ is supported on $L$, by the sign argument in 2.1. Then $H_\alpha$ contains $x\Theta_L=\Theta_J$, so $xf$ avoids every relative hyperplane. By [F4] and [F5], the chamber containing $xf$ is some labelled face translate; uniqueness in 1.1 makes that translate $xC_L$. [F2, F4, F5, F6, step 1.1, step 2.1]

4.1 Conversely, a chamber is $xC_L$ for a label in [F4]. The equality $W_J=xW_Lx^{-1}$ implies equality of their fixed subspaces. The fixed subspace of $W_K$ is $\Theta_K$: each generating reflection fixes its simple-root hyperplane pointwise, and a point fixed by that reflection lies on that hyperplane. Thus $\Theta_J=x\Theta_L$, and comparing dimensions gives $|J|=|L|$. Together with 3.1 this proves the chamber characterization. [F4, F5, F6, F7, step 3.1]

5.1 Let $xC_L$ be a chamber. By 4.1, $x\Theta_L=\Theta_J$. In the coordinates $f(\alpha_i)$ for $i\notin L$, the set $C_L$ is the positive orthant in $\Theta_L$, and its closure is the nonnegative orthant. Its codimension-one faces are obtained by setting exactly one of these coordinates to zero while keeping the others positive. Applying the linear isomorphism $x$ gives the faces $xC_{L+i}$. Their closures are $x(\overline{C_L}\cap H_{\alpha_i})=\overline{xC_L}\cap H_{x\alpha_i}$. Since $x\alpha_i$ is not in the span of $\{x\alpha_l:l\in L\}$, this hyperplane does not contain $\Theta_J$; its trace is therefore a relative hyperplane supporting the wall. Every codimension-one wall of the orthant is one of these facets. [F2, F6, step 4.1, algebra]

6.1 If $J=\varnothing$, then $\Theta_J=\Theta$ and every face translate satisfies the containment condition. The stratification from 2.1 is therefore the full Tits-cone stratification. Steps 1.1–5.1 establish all the assertions. [F6, step 1.1, step 2.1, step 3.1, step 4.1, step 5.1] ∎
