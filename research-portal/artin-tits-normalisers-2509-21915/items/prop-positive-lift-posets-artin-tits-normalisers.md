## Statement

Let $(W,S)$ be a finite-rank Coxeter system of spherical type, meaning that $W$ is finite. Let $A$ be its Artin–Tits group, $A^+$ its submonoid generated by the standard generators $\sigma_s$, and $\pi:A\to W$ the homomorphism sending $\sigma_s$ to $s$. Write $\ell(w)$ for the minimum length of a word in $S$ representing $w$. For a reduced expression $w=s_1\cdots s_r$, define its positive lift by $\sigma_w=\sigma_{s_1}\cdots\sigma_{s_r}$, and put $\widetilde W=\{\sigma_w:w\in W\}\subseteq A^+$.

For $a,b\in A^+$, say that $a$ left-divides $b$ if $b=ac$ for some $c\in A^+$. On $W$ use the prefix weak order: $u\leq v$ if $v=ux$ for some $x\in W$ with $\ell(v)=\ell(u)+\ell(x)$. Restriction of $\pi$ is an isomorphism from $\widetilde W$ with left divisibility to $W$ with this order, with inverse $w\mapsto\sigma_w$. The article calls the latter left weak Bruhat order; the prefix convention here is also called right weak order.

The left-divisibility least common multiple of any two elements of $\widetilde W$ exists in $A^+$ and belongs to $\widetilde W$.

## Facts & Assumptions

[F1] Positive lifts are independent of reduced expression, satisfy $\pi(\sigma_w)=w$, and obey $\sigma_{ux}=\sigma_u\sigma_x$ whenever $\ell(ux)=\ell(u)+\ell(x)$. The stated prefix relation is right weak order. ([[prop-positive-lift-interface-artin-tits-prerequisites]])

[F2] In spherical type, the presented positive Artin monoid $M$ embeds as $A^+$; its Garside element $\Delta$ has left divisors precisely the reduced positive lifts, and every pair has a left-divisibility lcm. ([[thm-spherical-artin-garside-input-artin-tits-prerequisites]])

[F3] Word length in the positive generators gives an additive degree on $M$. Under the embedding this degree transports to $A^+$, and $\deg(\sigma_w)=\ell(w)$. ([[lem-author-f758f7dd29a6-positive-degree]], [[lem-author-f758f7dd29a6-monoid-reduced-lifts]], [[lem-author-f758f7dd29a6-positive-image-identification]])

[F4] Every element of $A^+$ is represented by a finite positive word. Coxeter length is the minimum length of a representing word; concatenating representative words gives $\ell(ux)\leq\ell(u)+\ell(x)$. ([[def-positive-artin-monoids-artin-tits-normalisers]], [[math:def-hh-coxeter-matrix-word-group-and-length]])

[F5] An order isomorphism between posets is a bijection that preserves and reflects the order. ([[math:def-order-isomorphism]])

## Proof



**Given:** The spherical Coxeter and Artin–Tits data in the statement.

**Goal:** Prove the poset identification and lcm closure.

1.1 The lift map is a section of $\pi$ by [F1], hence it is injective and $\pi$ restricts to its inverse on $\widetilde W$. If $u\leq v$, choose $x$ as in the prefix definition. Then [F1] gives $\sigma_v=\sigma_u\sigma_x$, and $\sigma_x\in A^+$, so $\sigma_u$ left-divides $\sigma_v$. [F1, given]

2.1 Conversely, suppose $\sigma_v=\sigma_u c$ with $c\in A^+$. Choose a positive word for $c$ of length $k$ and put $x=\pi(c)$. Its image is a word of length $k$ for $x$, so $\ell(x)\leq k$ by [F4]. Additivity of degree and [F3] give $\ell(v)=\ell(u)+k$. Applying $\pi$ gives $v=ux$, and [F4] now gives $\ell(v)\leq\ell(u)+\ell(x)\leq\ell(u)+k=\ell(v)$. Thus both inequalities are equalities and $u\leq v$. Together with step 1.1 this proves the poset isomorphism. [F3, F4, F5, step 1.1, given]

3.1 Let $a,b\in\widetilde W$. By [F2] each left-divides $\Delta$, and their left-divisibility lcm $m$ exists. Since $\Delta$ is a common upper bound, the defining least-upper-bound property gives that $m$ left-divides $\Delta$. The left divisors of $\Delta$ are exactly $\widetilde W$ by [F2], so $m\in\widetilde W$. This proves the remaining assertion. [F2, given] ∎
