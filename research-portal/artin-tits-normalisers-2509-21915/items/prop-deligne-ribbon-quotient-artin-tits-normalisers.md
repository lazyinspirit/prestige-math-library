## Statement

Let $(W,S)$ be a finite-rank spherical Coxeter system, meaning that $W$ is finite, and fix $J\subseteq S$. Write $W_I$ for the subgroup generated by $I\subseteq S$, and let $N=N(W,J)=\operatorname{Norm}_W(W_J)/W_J$. Let $\mathcal D$ be the relative arrangement groupoid, whose objects are chamber labels $(x,I)$, and let $\mathcal R$ be the reduced ribbon groupoid. Its objects are the associates of $J$: the subsets $I\subseteq S$ for which $W_I$ is conjugate to $W_J$. The functor $\mathcal G:\mathcal D\to\mathcal R$ sends $(x,I)$ to $I$ and a simple wall crossing of index $a$ to the elementary ribbon $\mu_{a,I}$. Let $\mathcal Q:\mathcal D\to\mathcal D/N$ send objects and arrows to their orbits under the normaliser action.

The functor $\mathcal G$ factors as $\mathcal G=\mathcal G'\circ\mathcal Q$, where $\mathcal G':\mathcal D/N\to\mathcal R$ is an isomorphism of groupoids. On objects it identifies the $N$-orbits of chambers with the associates of $J$ by $[(x,I)]\mapsto I$.

## Facts & Assumptions

[F1] A chamber label $(x,I)$ satisfies $W_Jx=xW_I$, with $x$ the minimum-length element of $xW_I$. The objects of $\mathcal R$ are the associates of $J$. The groupoid $\mathcal D$ is generated by simple wall crossings and their inverses. The assignment on objects and crossings is as stated, and $\mathcal Q$ sends each object and arrow to its orbit. [[def-deligne-ribbon-mapping-artin-tits-normalisers]]

[F2] The assignment extends to a functor $\mathcal G$, and for each chamber $C$ and each arrow of $\mathcal R$ with source $\mathcal G(C)$ there is exactly one arrow of $\mathcal D$ with source $C$ and that image. [[lem-deligne-ribbon-cover-artin-tits-normalisers]]

[F3] The action of $N$ on chamber labels preserves their second components, is free, and is transitive on labels with a fixed second component. On $\mathcal D$ it acts by automorphisms and sends a crossing of index $a$ at a label with second component $I$ to a crossing with the same indices $a,I$. [[lem-normaliser-chamber-action-artin-tits-normalisers]] [[rem-normaliser-deligne-action-artin-tits-normalisers]]

[F4] For this free object action, objects and arrows of the quotient are orbits; every arrow orbit with source $[C]$ has a unique representative with source $C$, and quotient composition is obtained by aligning consecutive representatives. [[prop-groupoid-orbit-interface-artin-tits-prerequisites]]

[F5] Every right coset of a standard parabolic subgroup has a minimum-length representative. [[prop-parabolic-minimum-interface-artin-tits-prerequisites]]

## Proof
**Given:** The spherical Coxeter system and subset $J$ in the Statement.


**Proof technique:** direct.

**Goal:** Construct the factor functor and prove it is bijective on objects and on each hom-set.

1.1 For $n\in N$, the action preserves the second component of every chamber label, so $\mathcal G(nC)=\mathcal G(C)$. It also sends each generating crossing of index $a$ at second component $I$ to one with the same indices, and both have image $\mu_{a,I}$. Because the action and $\mathcal G$ are functorial, the same equality holds for inverses and composites: $\mathcal G(nu)=\mathcal G(u)$ for every arrow $u$. Define $\mathcal G'([C])=\mathcal G(C)$ and $\mathcal G'([u])=\mathcal G(u)$. These are independent of representatives. Alignment of representatives in the quotient shows that they preserve composition and identities, so $\mathcal G'$ is a functor and $\mathcal G=\mathcal G'\circ\mathcal Q$. [F1, F2, F3, F4]

1.2 Every label has an associate as its second component, since $W_I=x^{-1}W_Jx$. Conversely, let $I$ be an associate and choose $w$ with $w^{-1}W_Jw=W_I$. Choose the minimum-length representative $x$ of $wW_I$, so $w=xu$ for some $u\in W_I$. Since $u$ normalises $W_I$, also $x^{-1}W_Jx=W_I$; hence $(x,I)$ is a chamber label. Thus every object of $\mathcal R$ is hit. Two chamber labels have the same image exactly when their second components agree, and by [F3] this holds exactly when they lie in the same $N$-orbit. Therefore $\mathcal G'$ is bijective on objects. [F1, F3, F5]

2.1 Let $[C]$ and $[D]$ be quotient objects and let $r:\mathcal G(C)\to\mathcal G(D)$ be an arrow of $\mathcal R$. By unique lifting, there is an arrow $u:C\to E$ with $\mathcal G(u)=r$. Its endpoint satisfies $\mathcal G(E)=\mathcal G(D)$, so $[E]=[D]$ by step 1.2. Hence $[u]:[C]\to[D]$ maps to $r$, proving surjectivity on this hom-set. [F2, F4, step 1.1, step 1.2]

3.1 If two arrows from $[C]$ to $[D]$ have the same image under $\mathcal G'$, choose their representatives with source $C$. Their images under $\mathcal G$ are equal by step 1.1. Uniqueness of the lift from $C$ makes these representatives equal, so their orbits are equal. Thus $\mathcal G'$ is injective on each hom-set. Its bijection on objects and hom-sets defines an inverse functor, since the unique preimages of identities and composites are identities and composites. Consequently $\mathcal G'$ is an isomorphism. [F2, F4, step 1.1, step 1.2, step 2.1] ∎
