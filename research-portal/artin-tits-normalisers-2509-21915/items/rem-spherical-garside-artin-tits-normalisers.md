## Statement

Let $\Gamma$ be a spherical Coxeter diagram, meaning that its Coxeter group $W$ is finite, with vertex set $\Gamma_0$. Let $A^+$ be the submonoid of the associated Artin–Tits group $A$ generated by its standard generators. Then $A^+$ is a Garside monoid and every pair of its elements has a greatest common divisor and a least common multiple for each of left and right divisibility.

In particular, let $J\subseteq\Gamma_0$ and let $I$ be an associate of $J$: thus $I\subseteq\Gamma_0$ and the standard parabolic subgroups $W_I$ and $W_J$ are conjugate in $W$. For $a,b\in I^c=\Gamma_0\setminus I$, the positive elementary ribbon elements $\mu_{a,I},\mu_{b,I}\in A^+$ have a left-divisibility least common multiple $\mu_{a,I}\vee\mu_{b,I}$ in $A^+$.

## Proof


[F1] For a spherical Artin system, the positive submonoid $A^+$ is a Garside monoid and its left and right divisibility orders have gcds and lcms for every pair. ([[thm-spherical-artin-garside-input-artin-tits-prerequisites]])

[F2] For every $I\subseteq\Gamma_0$ and $a\in I^c$, the elementary ribbon $\mu_{a,I}$ belongs to $A^+$. ([[def-ribbon-generators-artin-tits-normalisers]])

[F3] In a monoid, $x$ left-divides $y$ when $y=xz$ for some monoid element $z$; right divisibility reverses the side of the factor. A gcd is a greatest common divisor and an lcm a least common multiple in the relevant divisibility order. The Garside interface uses these divisibility orders as lattices. ([[def-garside-interface-artin-tits-prerequisites]])



[F4] The Coxeter diagram, its group $W$ and its indexed simple generators $S=\{s_i:i\in\Gamma_0\}$ are supplied by [[def-coxeter-data-artin-tits-normalisers]]. For finite $S$, [[def-artin-pure-lifts-artin-tits-normalisers]] supplies the associated Artin–Tits group $A$ and its standard generators through a fixed isomorphism from the presented Artin group. The positive submonoid generated by these generators is the $A^+$ of [[def-positive-artin-monoids-artin-tits-normalisers]].

**Given:** A spherical Coxeter diagram and its positive Artin submonoid $A^+$; for the final assertion, an associate $I$ of $J$ and $a,b\in I^c$.

**Goal:** Establish the Garside and divisibility assertions and the existence of the displayed left lcm.

1.1 Since $W$ is finite and its distinct simple generators form a subset $S\subseteq W$, the Coxeter system has finite rank. Thus the finite-rank Artin group and standard generators in F4 apply, and their generated positive submonoid is exactly the $A^+$ in the statement. The spherical hypothesis therefore makes F1 applicable to this submonoid. Hence $A^+$ has the stated Garside structure, and both divisibility orders have a greatest lower bound and a least upper bound for every pair; by F3 these are the respective gcd and lcm. [given, F1, F3, F4]

2.1 An associate $I$ is a subset of $\Gamma_0$, so F2 applies separately to $a$ and $b$: both ribbon elements lie in $A^+$. Applying the left-divisibility lcm assertion from step 1.1 to this pair gives $\mu_{a,I}\vee\mu_{b,I}\in A^+$, as claimed. [given, F2, step 1.1] ∎
