## Definition

Let $\Gamma$ be a finite-rank spherical Coxeter diagram, meaning that its Coxeter group $W$ is finite, with vertex set $\Gamma_0$. Let $A$ be its Artin–Tits group and fix $J\subseteq\Gamma_0$. For $I\subseteq\Gamma_0$, write $W_I$ for the subgroup generated by the simple reflections indexed by $I$. An **associate** of $J$ is a subset $I$ for which $W_I$ is conjugate to $W_J$ in $W$.

Let $\mathcal R'(J)$ be the ribbon groupoid: its objects are the associates of $J$, and an arrow $I\to K$ is an element $\beta\in A$ with $A_I^+\beta=\beta A_K^+$, tagged with its source and target. Here $A_I^+$ is the submonoid generated by the standard Artin generators indexed by $I$. Composition is multiplication from left to right.

For an associate $I$ and $a\in\Gamma_0\setminus I$, put $T=I\cup\{a\}$. Let $w_T$ be the longest element of $W_T$, let $\iota_T:T\to T$ be the opposition permutation defined by $w_T\alpha_i=-\alpha_{\iota_T(i)}$ on the simple roots, and put $K=T\setminus\{\iota_T(a)\}$. If $\Delta_L$ denotes the positive reduced-word lift in $A$ of the longest element of $W_L$, the elementary ribbon is $\mu_{a,I}=\Delta_I^{-1}\Delta_T$, regarded as an arrow $I\to K$ of $\mathcal R'(J)$.

The **reduced ribbon groupoid** $\mathcal R(J)$ is the subgroupoid of $\mathcal R'(J)$ with all the same objects whose arrows are finite composable products of the arrows $\mu_{a,I}$ and their inverses, including empty products as identities. Equivalently, it is the smallest subgroupoid of $\mathcal R'(J)$ containing every object and every such elementary ribbon arrow.

## Justification

The associate condition and the ambient groupoid are supplied by [[def-associates-artin-tits-normalisers]] and [[def-ribbon-groupoid-artin-tits-normalisers]]. The longest elements, opposition permutations and elementary ribbon elements are well defined by [[def-longest-elements-artin-tits-normalisers]] and [[def-ribbon-generators-artin-tits-normalisers]].

To apply [[lem-ribbon-generator-lift-artin-tits-normalisers]] for an arbitrary associate $I$, choose $y\in W$ with $W_Jy=yW_I$. Choose an element $x$ of minimum Coxeter length in the right coset $yW_I$. Since $x=yu$ for some $u\in W_I$, one still has $W_Jx=xW_I$; thus $(x,I)$ satisfies the chamber-label hypotheses of the lemma. The lemma shows that $K$ is an associate of $J$ and that $\mu_{a,I}$ is an arrow $I\to K$ of $\mathcal R'(J)$.

The objects and arrows of $\mathcal R'(J)$ form sets. Finite composable products of the specified arrows and their inverses form a set of its arrows. Concatenation of such products is again such a product, reversing a product and inverting its factors gives its inverse, and the empty products give all identities. The inherited composition and inverses therefore make these arrows a subgroupoid. Any subgroupoid containing all objects and the specified arrows must also contain their inverses and every such product, proving the stated minimality.
