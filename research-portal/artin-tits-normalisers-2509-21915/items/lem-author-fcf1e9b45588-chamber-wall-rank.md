## Statement

Let $W\leq\mathrm{GL}(E)$ be a finite real reflection group with essential action, in the invariant metric of [[lem-author-fcf1e9b45588-invariant-metric-essentiality]]. A chamber is a connected component of $E\setminus\bigcup_{H\in\mathcal A(W)}H$. A wall of a chamber is a hyperplane containing a codimension-one face of its closure. Let $S$ be the reflections in the walls of a fixed chamber $C$. Then $(W,S)$ is a Coxeter system, $W$ acts simply transitively on chambers, $|S|=\dim E$, and the wall normals form a basis of $E$.

## Facts & Assumptions

[F1] The invariant metric makes each reflection the unique orthogonal reflection in its hyperplane ([[lem-author-fcf1e9b45588-invariant-metric-essentiality]]).

[F2] Davis, §6.6, Theorem 6.6.3(i)–(iii), with the hypotheses and reading limits recorded below, gives the Coxeter system, chamber transitivity and simplicial-cone assertion.

[F3] For a finite basis $v_1,\ldots,v_n$, the coordinate functionals $\varphi_i(\sum_j t_jv_j)=t_i$ are its dual family, with $\varphi_i(v_j)=\delta_{ij}$, and form a basis of the algebraic dual $E^*$ ([[math:def-linear-basis]], [[math:def-algebraic-dual-and-linear-functional]], [[math:def-dual-family-associated-to-a-basis]], [[math:thm-dual-family-is-a-basis-in-finite-dimension]]).

[F4] In a finite-dimensional real inner-product space the map $J:E\to E^*$ given by $J(u)(x)=\langle x,u\rangle$ is a linear bijection ([[math:thm-riesz-representation-in-finite-dimensions]]). A nonzero vector $u$ is normal to a hyperplane $H$ when $\langle x,u\rangle=0$ for every $x\in H$ ([[math:def-orthogonality-and-orthogonal-complement]]).

## Proof

**Given:** A finite real reflection group with $E^W=0$ and its invariant metric, as in [[lem-author-fcf1e9b45588-invariant-metric-essentiality]]; Coxeter-system terminology is that of [[def-coxeter-data-artin-tits-normalisers]]. **Goal:** Apply the external chamber theorem with its essentiality hypothesis.

**Imported established input:** Davis, Theorem 6.6.3(i)–(iii), printed p.89: the wall reflections of a chamber of a finite linear reflection group are a Coxeter generating set; the action on chambers is simply transitive; if the action is essential, the chamber is a simplicial cone and $|S|=\dim E$. The ambient metric is chosen by the preceding local lemma. This theorem is used externally; its proof is not reconstructed here. The precise source record states the hypotheses and actual reading limits.

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 By [F1] the group and its full hyperplane complement satisfy Davis's setup. The assumed equality $E^W=0$ supplies precisely its additional essentiality hypothesis. Thus [F2] makes $(W,S)$ a Coxeter system in the sense of [[def-coxeter-data-artin-tits-normalisers]], proves simple transitivity on chambers and makes $\overline C$ a full-dimensional simplicial cone with exactly $\dim E$ walls. [F1, F2, given]

2.1 Put $n=\dim E$. By step 1.1, the full-dimensional simplicial cone $\overline C$ has the form $\{\sum_{i=1}^n t_i v_i:t_i\geq0\}$ for a basis $v_1,\ldots,v_n$; its wall hyperplanes are $H_i=\ker\varphi_i$, where $\varphi_i$ is the coordinate functional from [F3]. The facet on $H_i$ is the cone generated by the $v_j$ with $j\ne i$. By [F3], these $\varphi_i$ form a basis of $E^*$. [step 1.1, F3, algebra]

3.1 The space $E$ is finite-dimensional and real, with the inner product of [F1], so [F4] applies. Put $u_i=J^{-1}(\varphi_i)$. Since $J$ is a linear bijection and the $\varphi_i$ form a basis, the $u_i$ form a basis of $E$: applying $J$ proves independence and pulls back every linear combination in $E^*$. The equality $\langle x,u_i\rangle=\varphi_i(x)$ makes $u_i$ a nonzero normal to $H_i$. Any other nonzero normal $z$ to $H_i$ has $J(z)(v_j)=0$ for $j\ne i$, hence $J(z)=J(z)(v_i)\varphi_i$ by [F3]; injectivity gives $z=J(z)(v_i)u_i$, with nonzero scalar. Thus any choice of one nonzero wall normal per wall is a basis. There is one distinct reflection per wall by [F1], so $|S|=n=\dim E$ and all conclusions follow. [step 2.1, F1, F3, F4, algebra] ∎
