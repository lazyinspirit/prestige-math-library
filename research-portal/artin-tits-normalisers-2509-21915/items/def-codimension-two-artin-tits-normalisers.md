## Definition

Let $(W,S)$ be a finite-rank Coxeter system with diagram vertex set $\Gamma_0$ and geometric roots $\Phi$. For $L\subseteq\Gamma_0$, let $W_L$ be the subgroup generated by the simple reflections indexed by $L$, and put $\Phi_L=\{w\alpha_i:w\in W_L,\ i\in L\}$. Fix $J\subseteq\Gamma_0$ and a relative chamber label $(x,I)\in\mathrm{Cham}(J)$, meaning that $W_Jx=xW_I$ and $x$ has minimum length in $xW_I$.

A chamber path in the relative arrangement $(\mathrm{Cone}(J)^\circ,\mathcal H^J)$ is a finite nonempty sequence of chambers with consecutive chambers adjacent; its length is the number of successive pairs. It is **geodesic** when its length is minimal among chamber paths with the same endpoints. For an eligible vertex $a\notin I$, write $\omega_{a,I}$ for the simple wall crossing at $(x,I)$ as defined in [[def-simple-wall-crossing-artin-tits-normalisers]].

Choose distinct $a,b\in\Gamma_0\setminus I$ such that $|\Phi_{I\cup\{a,b\}}\setminus\Phi_I|<\infty$. Let $q=v_{a,b,I}:I\to K$ be the two-generator element of [[lem-two-generator-minimal-artin-tits-normalisers]]. The two-geodesic lemma supplies exactly two geodesic paths $p_a,p_b$ from $(x,I)$ to $(xq,K)$, beginning respectively with $\omega_{a,I}$ and $\omega_{b,I}$. The formal relation $p_a=p_b$ identifying these paths is called a **codimension-two relation**.

## Justification

By [[lem-two-geodesics-artin-tits-normalisers]], the stated hypotheses give exactly the two paths with the stated endpoints and initial crossings. Their ordered pair therefore determines the displayed formal identification; the definition does not assert that the two paths are equal as sequences of chambers.
