## Statement

Let $(W,S)$ be a Coxeter system indexed by the vertex set $\Gamma_0$. For $L\subseteq\Gamma_0$, let $W_L$ be the subgroup generated by the simple reflections indexed by $L$, let $\Phi_L$ be its associated root set, and put $\Phi_L^{\pm}=\Phi_L\cap\Phi^{\pm}$. For a chamber label $(x,I)\in\mathrm{Cham}(J)$ and $a\in\Gamma_0\setminus I$, suppose $W_D$ is finite, where $D=[I,a]$ is the connected component containing $a$ in the Coxeter diagram induced on $I\cup\{a\}$. Let $w_L$ denote the longest element of a finite $W_L$, let $\iota_D$ be defined by $w_D\alpha_i=-\alpha_{\iota_D(i)}$, and set $v_{a,I}=w_{D\setminus\{a\}}w_D$ and $K=(I\cup\{a\})\setminus\{\iota_D(a)\}$.

The article claims that $v_{a,I}$ is the unique element satisfying $v_{a,I}(\Phi^+_{I\cup\{a\}}\setminus\Phi^+_K)\subseteq\Phi^-_K$ and $v_{a,I}(\Phi^+_K)\subseteq\Phi^+_{I\cup\{a\}}$. This claim is false as printed: in the rank-one case below, $v_{a,I}$ fails the first inclusion.

## Proof


**Given:** The rank-one Coxeter system with $\Gamma_0=\{a\}$ and generator $s_a$, and $J=I=\varnothing$, $x=1$.

[F1] The standard parabolic subgroup $W_L$ is generated by the simple reflections indexed by $L$; $\Phi_L=\{w\alpha_i:w\in W_L,\ i\in L\}$ and $\Phi_L^{\pm}=\Phi_L\cap\Phi^{\pm}$. [[def-parabolic-subgroups-artin-tits-normalisers]]

[F2] The simple root $\alpha_a$ is a nonzero basis vector in the positive cone, and $s_a\alpha_a=-\alpha_a$. Also $s_a^2=1$. [[def-coxeter-data-artin-tits-normalisers]]

[F3] A chamber label $(x,I)$ belongs to $\mathrm{Cham}(J)$ when $W_Jx=xW_I$ and $x$ has minimum length in $xW_I$. [[def-chamber-labels-artin-tits-normalisers]]

[F4] The set $[I,a]$ is the component containing $a$ of the diagram induced on $I\cup\{a\}$. [[def-connected-support-artin-tits-normalisers]]

[F5] For finite $W_L$, $w_L$ is its longest element; $\iota_L$ is the permutation satisfying $w_L\alpha_i=-\alpha_{\iota_L(i)}$. In particular $w_\varnothing=1$. [[def-longest-elements-artin-tits-normalisers]]

[F6] The wall-crossing element is $v_{a,I}=w_{D\setminus\{a\}}w_D$, and $K=(I\cup\{a\})\setminus\{\iota_D(a)\}$. [[def-simple-wall-crossing-artin-tits-normalisers]]



**Proof technique:** direct.

**Goal:** verify the article's hypotheses in this example and show that its first inclusion fails.

1.1 Since $W_\varnothing=\{1\}$, the two cosets in the chamber-label condition are both $\{1\}$, and $1$ has minimum length in its coset. Thus $(1,\varnothing)\in\mathrm{Cham}(\varnothing)$. The induced diagram has the single vertex $a$, so $D=\{a\}$. The subgroup $W_D=\{1,s_a\}$ is finite; $s_a$ is nontrivial because it sends the nonzero vector $\alpha_a$ to its negative. Hence all the stated hypotheses hold. [F1, F2, F3, F4, given]

2.1 The longest element of $W_D$ is $s_a$: the identity has length zero and $s_a$ has length one. Consequently $\iota_D(a)=a$, $K=\varnothing$, and $v_{a,I}=1\cdot s_a=s_a$. By the root-set definition, $\Phi^+_K=\Phi^-_K=\varnothing$, while $\alpha_a\in\Phi^+_{\{a\}}$. Thus the left side of the first claimed inclusion contains $v_{a,I}\alpha_a=-\alpha_a$, but its right side is empty. The claimed inclusion, and hence the asserted characterization, fails. [F1, F2, F5, F6, step 1.1] ∎

## Source notes

The counterexample addresses the formula as printed. It does not establish a general replacement characterization.
