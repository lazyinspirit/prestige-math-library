## Statement

Let $(W,S)$ be a finite-rank Coxeter system, $A$ its Artin–Tits group, $\pi:A\to W$ the standard projection and $P=\ker\pi$. For a subset $J\subseteq S$, let $W_J$ and $A_J$ be the standard parabolic subgroups. Write $N(W,J)=\operatorname{Norm}_W(W_J)/W_J$, $N(A,J)=\operatorname{Norm}_A(A_J)/A_J$, and let $N(P,J)$ be the image of $P\cap\operatorname{Norm}_A(A_J)$ in $N(A,J)$. Then $\pi$ induces $\overline\pi:N(A,J)\to N(W,J)$ and the sequence $1\to N(P,J)\to N(A,J)\xrightarrow{\overline\pi}N(W,J)\to1$ is exact. Here exactness means that the inclusion $N(P,J)\hookrightarrow N(A,J)$ is injective, its image equals $\ker\overline\pi$, and $\overline\pi$ is surjective.

## Facts & Assumptions


[F1] The projection sends each standard Artin generator $\sigma_s$ to $s$, has kernel $P$, and maps $A_J$ onto $W_J$. The subgroups $A_J$ and $W_J$ are generated by their respective standard generators indexed by $J$. [[def-artin-pure-lifts-artin-tits-normalisers]] [[def-parabolic-subgroups-artin-tits-normalisers]]

[F2] A homomorphism $p:G\to Q$ with $p(H)=K$ induces a map $N_G(H)/H\to N_Q(K)/K$ whose kernel is the image of $\ker p\cap N_G(H)$; it is surjective exactly when $p(N_G(H))=N_Q(K)$. [[prop-normaliser-quotient-interface-artin-tits-prerequisites]]

[F3] Every double coset $W_JwW_J$ contains an element $d$ with $\ell(sd)>\ell(d)$ and $\ell(ds)>\ell(d)$ for every $s\in J$; these inequalities define membership in ${}^JW^J$. [[math:lem-cg-double-coset-descent-reduction-and-minimality]] [[math:def-cg-parabolic-quotient-and-two-sided-minima]]

[F4] If $d\in{}^JW^J$ and $s\in J$ with $d^{-1}sd\in W_J$, then $d^{-1}sd\in J$. Also $W_J\cap S=J$. [[math:lem-cg-double-coset-intersection-parabolic]] [[math:thm-hh-parabolic-minimal-representatives-and-length-additivity]]

[F5] For $d\in{}^JW^J$ and $s,t\in J$, the products $sd$ and $dt$ have length $\ell(d)+1$. The positive lift $\sigma_x$ of a Coxeter element $x$ is well defined, projects to $x$, and satisfies $\sigma_{xy}=\sigma_x\sigma_y$ whenever $\ell(xy)=\ell(x)+\ell(y)$. [[math:def-cg-parabolic-quotient-and-two-sided-minima]] [[prop-positive-lift-interface-artin-tits-prerequisites]]

## Proof


**Given:** The finite-rank Coxeter and Artin–Tits data and the subset $J$ in the Statement.

**Proof technique:** direct.

**Goal:** Prove the induced map, its kernel and its surjectivity.

1.1 By [F1] the image of $A_J$ under $\pi$ is $W_J$ and $\ker\pi=P$. Applying [F2] with $G=A$, $H=A_J$, $Q=W$ and $K=W_J$ gives the displayed induced homomorphism and identifies its kernel with the image of $P\cap\operatorname{Norm}_A(A_J)$, namely $N(P,J)$. Its inclusion in $N(A,J)$ is injective by the definition of an image subgroup. [F1, F2, given]

1.2 Let $w\in\operatorname{Norm}_W(W_J)$. By [F3] choose $d\in{}^JW^J$ in $W_JwW_J$. Since $w$ normalises $W_J$, this double coset equals $wW_J$. Thus $d=wu$ for some $u\in W_J$, and $d$ also normalises $W_J$. For every $s\in J$, [F4] gives $d^{-1}sd\in J$. Conjugation is injective and $J$ is finite, so it permutes $J$. [F3, F4, given]

2.1 For $s\in J$ put $t=d^{-1}sd\in J$. Then $sd=dt$. Both sides are length-additive by [F5], so their positive lifts give $\sigma_s\sigma_d=\sigma_d\sigma_t$. As $s\mapsto t$ permutes $J$, conjugation by $\sigma_d$ sends the generating set of $A_J$ onto itself. Hence $\sigma_d\in\operatorname{Norm}_A(A_J)$. Since $u\in W_J=\pi(A_J)$, choose $a\in A_J$ with $\pi(a)=u^{-1}$. Then $\sigma_d a$ normalises $A_J$ and projects to $du^{-1}=w$. Thus every element of $\operatorname{Norm}_W(W_J)$ lifts to its Artin normaliser. [F1, F5, step 1.2]

3.1 The reverse containment $\pi(\operatorname{Norm}_A(A_J))\subseteq\operatorname{Norm}_W(W_J)$ follows by applying $\pi$ to the normalising equality and using $\pi(A_J)=W_J$. Together with step 2.1 this is equality. The surjectivity criterion in [F2], combined with the kernel calculation in step 1.1, proves the asserted short exact sequence. [F1, F2, step 1.1, step 2.1] ∎
