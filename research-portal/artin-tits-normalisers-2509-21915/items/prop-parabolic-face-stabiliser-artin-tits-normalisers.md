## Statement

Let $(W,S)$ be the Coxeter system, $V$ its geometric representation with simple-root basis $(\alpha_i)_{i\in\Gamma_0}$, and $\Theta=V^*$ with its contragredient $W$-action. The index set may be infinite. For every $J\subseteq\Gamma_0$ and every $\varphi\in C_J$, the stabilizer $\{w\in W:w\varphi=\varphi\}$ is exactly the standard parabolic subgroup $W_J$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The Coxeter system and $\varphi\in C_J$ in the statement. **Goal:** Prove both inclusions between its stabilizer and $W_J$.

[F1] By [[def-coxeter-data-artin-tits-normalisers]], every $w\in W$ has a word involving finitely many simple generators, $s_i(v)=v-2B(v,\alpha_i)\alpha_i$, and $(w\varphi)(v)=\varphi(w^{-1}v)$. By [[def-parabolic-faces-artin-tits-normalisers]], $\varphi(\alpha_i)=0$ for $i\in J$ and $\varphi(\alpha_i)>0$ for $i\notin J$. By [[def-parabolic-subgroups-artin-tits-normalisers]], $W_J$ is generated by the $s_i$ with $i\in J$.

[F2] For every finite subset $T\subseteq\Gamma_0$, [[math:thm-arbitrary-rank-finite-parabolics-and-bruhat-subword]] identifies the Coxeter group for the restricted matrix on $T$ with the subgroup of $W$ generated by $\{s_i:i\in T\}$.

[F3] For a finite-rank Coxeter system, [[prop-tits-domain-interface-artin-tits-prerequisites]] states that the stabilizer of a functional in a fundamental face is the subgroup generated by the simple reflections at its zero coordinates.

## Proof

1.1 For $i\in J$ and $v\in V$, the reflection formula gives $(s_i\varphi)(v)=\varphi(s_i v)=\varphi(v)-2B(v,\alpha_i)\varphi(\alpha_i)=\varphi(v)$. Thus each generator of $W_J$ fixes $\varphi$, and so $W_J$ is contained in its stabilizer. [F1, algebra]

2.1 Conversely, suppose $w\varphi=\varphi$. Choose a finite $T\subseteq\Gamma_0$ containing all indices in a word for $w$. The span $V_T$ of the corresponding simple roots is invariant under those generators, and their restricted reflection formulas are the canonical geometric representation for the restricted Coxeter matrix. By [F2], this finite-rank Coxeter group embeds as $W_T$. The restriction $\varphi|_{V_T}$ is fixed by $w$, since $w^{-1}V_T=V_T$, and its zero coordinates are exactly $J\cap T$. By [F3], $w$ belongs to the subgroup generated by $\{s_i:i\in J\cap T\}$, which is contained in $W_J$. Together with step 1.1 this proves equality. [F1, F2, F3, step 1.1] ∎
