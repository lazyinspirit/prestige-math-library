## Statement

Every article morphism $w$ has a standard expression. If a standard expression for $w$ has $m$ factors, then $m=|C(w)|$, using the reversed root-action arrow with the same label.

## Facts & Assumptions

[F1] The definitions and results used in this argument are supplied, under their stated hypotheses, by [[def-standard-expressions-artin-tits-normalisers]]; [[lem-author-b19d43a8e520-bh-opposite-identification]]; [[lem-author-b19d43a8e520-bh-parallel-root-classes]]; [[lem-author-b19d43a8e520-bh-general-rank-inputs]]; [[lem-author-b19d43a8e520-bh-compressed-additivity]].

[F2] For a group generated by a set $S$, word length in $S\cup S^{-1}$ satisfies $|xy|_S\leq |x|_S+|y|_S$ ([[math:lem-word-length-is-well-defined-and-satisfies-the-length-laws]], Proof, step 2.1). The Coxeter simple generators generate $W$ and are involutions, so this word length equals the Coxeter length $\ell$ of [[def-standard-expressions-artin-tits-normalisers]], even when $S$ is infinite.

## Proof

**Given:** The stated Coxeter and groupoid data and the declared mathematical prerequisites.

**Goal:** Establish existence of standard expressions and the factor-count assertion.

1.1 Reverse the factorization supplied by [[lem-author-b19d43a8e520-bh-general-rank-inputs]] using [[lem-author-b19d43a8e520-bh-opposite-identification]]. The latter identifies its generators with article elementary labels and preserves the label product. Thus the reversed sequence is composable in the article convention and remains Coxeter-length additive. It is a standard expression by [[def-standard-expressions-artin-tits-normalisers]], proving existence. [F1]

1.2 Let $w=v_1\cdots v_m$ be any standard expression with $m>0$, and put $p_0=1$ and $p_k=v_1\cdots v_k$ for $1\leq k\leq m$. Repeated subadditivity from [F2] gives $\ell(w)\leq\ell(p_k)+\sum_{i=k+1}^m\ell(v_i)\leq\sum_{i=1}^m\ell(v_i)=\ell(w)$. Both inequalities are therefore equalities, so $\ell(p_k)=\sum_{i=1}^k\ell(v_i)$. This also holds for $k=0$, since the empty word has length zero. Consequently $\ell(p_{k-1}v_k)=\ell(p_{k-1})+\ell(v_k)$ for every $1\leq k\leq m$. [F1, F2]

2.1 Reverse these arrows into $\mathcal B$, preserving their label products by [[lem-author-b19d43a8e520-bh-opposite-identification]]. Apply [[lem-author-b19d43a8e520-bh-compressed-additivity]] to each composable pair with labels $p_{k-1},v_k$, whose lengths add by step 1.2. Since $N(1)=\varnothing$ by the positive and negative root convention, $C(p_0)=\varnothing$; induction gives $|C(w)|=\sum_{i=1}^m|C(v_i)|$. Each $v_i$ is a Brink–Howlett generator by the opposite identification, and [[lem-author-b19d43a8e520-bh-general-rank-inputs]] gives $|C(v_i)|=1$. Hence $|C(w)|=m$. If $m=0$, the standard expression is the identity and both counts are zero. The count therefore depends only on the labelled morphism, with no rank assumption. [F1, step 1.1, step 1.2] ∎
