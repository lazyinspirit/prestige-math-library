## Statement

Let $(W,S)$ be a Coxeter system with finite vertex set $\Gamma_0$, and let $V$ be its canonical real representation with simple-root basis $(\alpha_i)_{i\in\Gamma_0}$ and dual space $\Theta=V^*$, equipped with the contragredient action. Fix $J\subseteq\Gamma_0$. Write $W_J=\langle s_j:j\in J\rangle$, $\Theta_J=\{f\in\Theta:f(\alpha_j)=0\text{ for all }j\in J\}$, and $C_L=\{f\in\Theta_L:f(\alpha_i)>0\text{ for all }i\notin L\}$ for $L\subseteq\Gamma_0$. Let $\mathrm{Cone}$ be the union of the $W$-translates of the closed fundamental chamber, and put $\mathrm{Cone}(J)=\mathrm{Cone}\cap\Theta_J$.

The normaliser $\operatorname{Norm}_W(W_J)=\{g\in W:gW_Jg^{-1}=W_J\}$ preserves $\Theta_J$, and the kernel of its action on $\Theta_J$ is exactly $W_J$. Consequently its quotient $N(W,J)=\operatorname{Norm}_W(W_J)/W_J$ acts faithfully by linear maps on $\Theta_J$. This action preserves $\mathrm{Cone}(J)$ and its stratification into the translates $xC_L\subseteq\Theta_J$: for every such stratum and every lift $g\in\operatorname{Norm}_W(W_J)$ of $\bar g\in N(W,J)$, one has $\bar g\,(xC_L)=(gx)C_L$.

## Facts & Assumptions

[F1] For each simple reflection, $s_i(v)=v-2B(v,\alpha_i)\alpha_i$ and $B(\alpha_i,\alpha_i)=1$; the dual action satisfies $(wf)(v)=f(w^{-1}v)$. Each $s_i$ is an involution. [def-coxeter-data-artin-tits-normalisers](item-def-coxeter-data-artin-tits-normalisers)

[F2] The subgroup $W_J$ is generated by the simple reflections $s_j$ for $j\in J$, and $C_J$ consists of the functionals vanishing on those simple roots and positive on the others. [def-parabolic-subgroups-artin-tits-normalisers](item-def-parabolic-subgroups-artin-tits-normalisers) [def-parabolic-faces-artin-tits-normalisers](item-def-parabolic-faces-artin-tits-normalisers)

[F3] The stabilizer in $W$ of every point of $C_J$ is $W_J$. [prop-parabolic-face-stabiliser-artin-tits-normalisers](item-prop-parabolic-face-stabiliser-artin-tits-normalisers)

[F4] The Tits cone is $W$-invariant. [prop-tits-cone-basic-artin-tits-normalisers](item-prop-tits-cone-basic-artin-tits-normalisers)

[F5] The relative cone is the disjoint union of the translates $xC_L$ that are contained in $\Theta_J$. [prop-relative-stratification-artin-tits-normalisers](item-prop-relative-stratification-artin-tits-normalisers)

[F6] $W_J$ is normal in its normaliser, so the quotient by $W_J$ exists. [prop-normaliser-quotient-interface-artin-tits-prerequisites](item-prop-normaliser-quotient-interface-artin-tits-prerequisites)

## Proof
**Given:** The finite-rank Coxeter system, its geometric and dual actions, and the subset $J$ in the Statement.


**Proof technique:** direct.

**Goal:** Prove the invariance and kernel assertions, and the induced action on the relative cone and its strata.

1.1 For $f\in\Theta$ and $j\in J$, [F1] gives $(s_jf)(v)=f(v)-2B(v,\alpha_j)f(\alpha_j)$. Thus $s_j$ fixes $f$ exactly when $f(\alpha_j)=0$: the forward direction follows by evaluating at $\alpha_j$, and the reverse direction follows from the displayed formula. By [F2], the common fixed subspace of $W_J$ is therefore exactly $\Theta_J$. [F1, F2, given]

2.1 Let $g$ normalize $W_J$ and let $f\in\Theta_J$. For every $h\in W_J$, the element $g^{-1}hg$ lies in $W_J$, so $h(gf)=g((g^{-1}hg)f)=gf$ by step 1.1. Hence $gf\in\Theta_J$. The same argument applied to $g^{-1}$ gives $g\Theta_J=\Theta_J$. [F6, step 1.1, given]

2.2 Step 1.1 shows that every element of $W_J$ acts trivially on $\Theta_J$. Conversely, if an element of the normaliser acts trivially on $\Theta_J$, it fixes the functional $f_J$ with $f_J(\alpha_j)=0$ for $j\in J$ and $f_J(\alpha_i)=1$ for $i\notin J$. The simple roots form a basis, so this functional exists and belongs to $C_J$. By [F3] its stabilizer is $W_J$, proving the reverse inclusion for the kernel. [F2, F3, step 1.1, given]

3.1 By [F6] and step 2.2, the restricted action descends to the quotient: define $(gW_J)f=gf$. This is independent of the representative because $W_J$ fixes every point of $\Theta_J$. Its kernel is trivial by step 2.2, and its maps are linear because the original dual action is linear. Thus the quotient action is faithful. [F1, F6, step 2.1, step 2.2]

4.1 By [F4] and step 2.1, each normaliser element preserves both $\mathrm{Cone}$ and $\Theta_J$, hence preserves their intersection $\mathrm{Cone}(J)$. If $xC_L$ is a stratum in [F5], its image under a lift $g$ is $(gx)C_L$ by the action law and is contained in $\Theta_J$. It is therefore another stratum in [F5]. The formula is independent of the lift by step 3.1; applying the same argument to $g^{-1}$ shows that the strata are permuted. This proves all assertions. [F4, F5, step 2.1, step 3.1, given] ∎
