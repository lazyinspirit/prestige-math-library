## Statement

Modulo atomic braid relations and the quadratic relations $tt^{-1}=1$ for elementary inverse pairs, every finite composable elementary-generator path is congruent to a standard expression of its product.

## Facts & Assumptions

[F1] Use the arbitrary-rank Coxeter and left-to-right groupoid conventions of [def-standard-expressions-artin-tits-normalisers](item-def-standard-expressions-artin-tits-normalisers). All paths are finite.

[F2] Atomic braid moves substitute the two words in a relation of [def-atomic-braid-artin-tits-normalisers](item-def-atomic-braid-artin-tits-normalisers). Here congruence means the equivalence generated by these substitutions in contiguous subwords and by insertion or deletion of $ss^{-1}$ for every elementary arrow $s$, at its source object. Replacements may be made within any composable prefix and suffix.

[F3] Every article morphism has a standard expression, by [lem-author-b19d43a8e520-bh-standard-factor-count](item-lem-author-b19d43a8e520-bh-standard-factor-count), first assertion.

[F4] Appending an elementary factor satisfies the two-case length dichotomy of [lem-author-7bd202611130-atomic-suffix-dichotomy](item-lem-author-7bd202611130-atomic-suffix-dichotomy).

[F5] Standard expressions for the same morphism are connected by finitely many endpoint-preserving atomic braid moves, by [lem-author-7bd202611130-standard-braid-connectivity](item-lem-author-7bd202611130-standard-braid-connectivity).

[F6] For a component $D$ with $W_D$ finite and $b=\iota_D(a)$, the identities $w_D^2=1$, $\iota_D^2=1$ and $w_Dw_{D\setminus\{a\}}w_D=w_{D\setminus\{b\}}$ are proved in [lem-author-b19d43a8e520-bh-opposite-identification](item-lem-author-b19d43a8e520-bh-opposite-identification), Proof, steps 1.2 and 2.1. They require no finite-rank hypothesis on the ambient Coxeter system.

## Proof

**Given:** The arbitrary-rank Coxeter and left-to-right groupoid conventions in [F1], with the congruence of [F2]. All paths are finite.

**Goal:** Establish the assertion in the Statement.

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Induct on the number of factors of the given path. The empty path is standard. For a nonempty path $Pt$, normalise its shorter prefix $P$ by induction, obtaining $P\equiv U$ with $U$ a standard word for $w:I\to K$. Write the last elementary factor as $t=v_{a,K}:K\to L$. Congruence gives $Pt\equiv Ut$. If $\ell(wt)=\ell(w)+\ell(t)$, then $Ut$ is standard by length additivity, completing this case. [F1, F2, given]

2.1 Put $D=[K,a]$ and $b=\iota_D(a)$. By [F1], $W_D$ is finite and $L=(K\cup\{a\})\setminus\{b\}$. Hence $L\cup\{b\}=K\cup\{a\}$ and $[L,b]=D$. By [F6], $\iota_D(b)=a$ and $v_{b,L}=w_{D\setminus\{b\}}w_D=w_Dw_{D\setminus\{a\}}=t^{-1}$. Thus $t^{-1}:L\to K$ is elementary. Reversing any simple-generator word represents its inverse with the same number of letters; applying this in both directions to shortest words gives $\ell(t^{-1})=\ell(t)$. [F1, F6, step 1.1]

3.1 In the nonadditive case of step 1.1, [F4] supplies a morphism $u:I\to L$ with $w=ut^{-1}$ and $\ell(w)=\ell(u)+\ell(t)$. Choose a standard word $V$ for $u$ by [F3]. Together with step 2.1, this length equality makes $Vt^{-1}$ a standard word for $w$. [F1, F3, F4, step 1.1, step 2.1]

4.1 By [F5], $U$ is connected to $Vt^{-1}$ by finitely many atomic braid moves. Append $t$ throughout and delete $t^{-1}t$ using [F2] with the elementary arrow $s=t^{-1}$ from step 2.1. This gives $Pt\equiv Ut\equiv Vt^{-1}t\equiv V$. The product of $V$ is $u=wt$, so $V$ is the required standard expression of the original product. Together with the additive case, this completes the induction. All words and move sequences used are finite, and only $W_D$ was required to be finite. [F2, F5, step 1.1, step 2.1, step 3.1] ∎
