## Statement

The groupoid $\mathcal{BH}$ is generated by $v_{a,I}:I\to I+a-\iota_{[I,a]}(a)$ over associates $I$ and $a\in I^c$ with finite $W_{[I,a]}$. Any two standard expressions of the same morphism have the same number of factors.

## Facts & Assumptions

[F1] The definitions and results used in this argument are supplied, under their stated hypotheses, by [def-standard-expressions-artin-tits-normalisers](item-def-standard-expressions-artin-tits-normalisers); [lem-author-b19d43a8e520-bh-standard-factor-count](item-lem-author-b19d43a8e520-bh-standard-factor-count).

## Proof

**Given:** The Coxeter and groupoid data of [def-standard-expressions-artin-tits-normalisers](item-def-standard-expressions-artin-tits-normalisers) and the existence and factor-count conclusions of [lem-author-b19d43a8e520-bh-standard-factor-count](item-lem-author-b19d43a8e520-bh-standard-factor-count).

**Goal:** Establish the assertion in the Statement.

1.1 By [lem-author-b19d43a8e520-bh-standard-factor-count](item-lem-author-b19d43a8e520-bh-standard-factor-count), every morphism has a standard expression in the generators displayed in the statement. By the definition of a standard expression, this is a finite composable product equal to that morphism. [F1]

2.1 Hence those generators generate the groupoid. For any two standard expressions of a fixed morphism, the factor-count conclusion of [lem-author-b19d43a8e520-bh-standard-factor-count](item-lem-author-b19d43a8e520-bh-standard-factor-count) makes their numbers of factors equal: both equal the expression-independent count supplied by that lemma. This applies to every Coxeter rank, including infinite rank. [F1, step 1.1] ∎
