## Statement

To the best of their knowledge, the authors describe Theorems 1.1–1.5 and Corollary 1.6 as the first exact controllability results for the nonlinear Schrödinger equation in an exterior domain. They also describe Theorem 1.1 as the first exact controllability result for a nonlinear Schrödinger equation with controls acting only on the outer part of the boundary of a domain surrounding an obstacle. These are the authors' qualified priority claims, not a certification of priority by this item.

An exterior domain here is the connected complement in Euclidean space of a compact obstacle with smooth boundary. Exact controllability means that each pair of admissible initial and terminal states can be connected by an admissible control in the specified time and solution class; a local result restricts the endpoints to a specified neighborhood. The individual theorems determine the equation, control region, solution classes and endpoint restrictions.
