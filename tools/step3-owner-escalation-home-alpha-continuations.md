It requires genuinely owner-created new lemma prerequisites. It is not a foreign-extra-context route: the genuine batch68 finding already belonged to its required scope. No existing findings, scopes, prior results, source observations or state records are rewritten. Existing foreign home30/15 authorities and delivered prompt grammar stay unchanged.

Root must approve and apply the mathematical repair and complete affected-consumer review before registering this continuation. A new helper's owner-creation receipt establishes authorship only, never native peer coverage or readiness. Native67 must independently review all old obligations, new helper obligations, current proof/risk interfaces and the genuine repaired defects. Ordinary ledger, contract, carrier, escalation and final closure gates remain mandatory.

The optional registry is `research/RUN-step3-owner-escalation-home-alpha-continuations.json` with `{version:1,run,policy:"owner-step3-escalated-home-alpha-continuation-v1",authorized_by:"owner",authorization,continuations:[ENTRY]}`. Authorization is substantive (40+ characters); entries are unique by unit. Only unit `"67"` and exact label `3a-batch-67-owner-escalation-continuation-1` are supported. Foreign and owner continuation authorities cannot overlap a unit.

ENTRY retains the common home continuation fields: `version:1`, exact `run`, `owner_identity:"/root"`, `unit:"67"`, canonical group `batch-67`, `mathematical_acceptance:false`, `previous_acceptance_inherited:false`, substantive reason, actual `writers_drained_at`, and `historical_artifact_basis:"owner-captured current completed-native artifacts; no structured-output byte attestation"`.
