## Statement

Assume AC. Let $L/F$ be a finite separable extension of global function
fields. Only finitely many places of $F$ ramify in $L$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC and a finite separable extension $L/F$ of global function fields.

[A1] AC supplies the integrality and local-place conventions used here.
([[def-axiom-of-choice]])

[F1] A finite separable extension is generated by one element.
([[def-global-field]],
[[thm-primitive-element-theorem-for-finite-separable-extensions]])

[F2] Every element of a global function field is integral at all but
finitely many places. ([[thm-global-field-element-integral-at-all-but-finitely-many-places]])

[F3] The discriminant is a coefficient expression that vanishes exactly
when the polynomial has a repeated root.
([[def-discriminant-of-a-monic-polynomial]],
[[thm-discriminant-root-formula-and-repeated-root-criterion]])

[F4] At a place, a unit discriminant makes the reduced monic polynomial
squarefree; Hensel factorization, the local degree formula, and the
completion tensor decomposition identify each factor field as unramified.
([[def-place-of-a-global-field]],
[[def-completion-of-a-global-field-at-a-place]],
[[def-local-field-valuation-ring-uniformizer-and-residue-field]],
[[lem-hensel-factor-lifting-over-a-complete-valued-field]],
[[thm-finite-extension-local-degree-equals-ramification-index-times-residue-degree]],
[[thm-finite-unramified-local-extensions-classified-by-residue-extensions]],
[[thm-global-field-completion-tensor-product-decomposition]],
[[thm-valuation-ring-is-integrally-closed]])

## Proof

**Given:** The data in the Statement and Facts & Assumptions.

1.1 By [F1], write $L=F(\alpha)$ and let $f(X)\in F[X]$ be the monic
minimal polynomial of $\alpha$. Separability makes its discriminant
$\Delta(f)$ nonzero by [F3]. By [F2], all coefficients of $f$, $\Delta(f)$,
and $1/\Delta(f)$ are integral at all but finitely many places. Remove that
finite union; outside it $f$ is integral and $\Delta(f)$ is a unit. [A1, F1, F2, F3]

1.2 At every remaining place $v$, the reduced polynomial has nonzero
discriminant and hence is squarefree by [F3]. Factor it into its finitely
many pairwise coprime irreducible factors over the residue field. Repeatedly
apply the two-factor Hensel statement in [F4] to one factor and the product
of the remaining factors; this lifts the factorization to monic factors over
$F_v$. Each lifted factor remains irreducible: a monic factorization over
$F_v$ would have integral monic factors by the valuation-ring integrality
criterion, and reduction would contradict irreducibility of its residue
factor. Its residue field is generated by the reduced root and has degree
equal to the lifted polynomial degree. The local degree formula in [F4]
therefore gives ramification index one. The completion tensor decomposition
in [F4] identifies these factors with all places above $v$, so none
ramifies. [F3, F4, step 1.1]

2.1 All ramified places lie in the finite set removed in step 1.1. [step 1.1, step 1.2] ∎
