## Definition

Let $\mathcal X$ be a set, let $m:\mathcal X\to\mathbb R$, and let
$k:\mathcal X\times\mathcal X\to\mathbb R$ be symmetric and positive
semidefinite, meaning that for every finite ordered list $t_1,\ldots,t_r$
in $\mathcal X$ and every $a\in\mathbb R^r$,
$$\sum_{i,j=1}^r a_i a_j k(t_i,t_j)\ge0.$$
For finite ordered lists $A=(a_i)_{i=1}^r$ and $B=(b_j)_{j=1}^s$ in
$\mathcal X$, define
$$K_{AB}=(k(a_i,b_j))_{i,j};$$
repeated indices are allowed.

Given a real Gaussian process $f=(f_t)_{t\in\mathcal X}$ with mean
$m(t)=\mathbb E[f_t]$ and covariance
$k(s,t)=\mathbb E[(f_s-m(s))(f_t-m(t))]$, and a nonempty finite training list
$X=(x_i)_{i=1}^n$ in $\mathcal X$, define $f_X=(f_{x_i})_{i=1}^n$ and
$m_X=(m(x_i))_{i=1}^n$. For a centered Gaussian noise vector
$\varepsilon\sim N_n(0,R)$ with symmetric positive-semidefinite covariance
$R$ that is independent of $f$, define the observation vector
$Y=f_X+\varepsilon$ and the matrix
$A_X=K_{XX}+R$.

For a nonempty finite test list $X_*=(x_j^*)_{j=1}^p$ in $\mathcal X$,
define the latent test
vector $f_*=f_{X_*}$. If a centered Gaussian vector
$\eta_*\sim N_p(0,R_*)$ with symmetric positive-semidefinite covariance
$R_*$ is independent of the pair $(f,\varepsilon)$, define the noisy test
vector $Y_*=f_*+\eta_*$.

## Model diagram

```tikz
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth]
  \node[draw,rounded corners] (prior) at (0,0) {$f\sim\mathrm{GP}(m,k)$};
  \node[draw] (train) at (-2,-1.5) {$f_X$};
  \node[draw] (test) at (3,-1.5) {$f_*$};
  \node[draw] (noise) at (-2,-3) {$\varepsilon$};
  \node[draw,rounded corners] (obs) at (0,-3) {$Y=f_X+\varepsilon$};
  \node[draw] (testnoise) at (3,-3) {$\eta_*$};
  \node[draw,rounded corners] (testobs) at (5,-3) {$Y_*=f_*+\eta_*$};
  \draw[->] (prior) -- (train);
  \draw[->] (prior) -- (test);
  \draw[->] (train) -- (obs);
  \draw[->] (noise) -- (obs);
  \draw[->] (test) -- (testobs);
  \draw[->] (testnoise) -- (testobs);
\end{tikzpicture}
```

The single prior node represents one process, so $f_X$ and $f_*$ may be
correlated. Arrows into $Y$ and $Y_*$ denote vector addition. The training
noise $\varepsilon$ is independent of $f$, and the test noise $\eta_*$ is
independent of the pair $(f,\varepsilon)$; the diagram records no posterior
independence claim.
