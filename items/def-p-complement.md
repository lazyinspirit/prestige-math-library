## Definition

Let $G$ be finite and let $p$ be prime. A **$p$-complement** is a subgroup $H\le G$ whose order is prime to $p$ and whose index $[G:H]$ is a power of $p$ ([[def-index]]). Equivalently, when $|G|=p^a m$ with $p\nmid m$, a $p$-complement has order $m$. The definition asserts no existence.
