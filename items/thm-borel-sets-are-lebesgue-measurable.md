## Statement

Let $n \ge 1$ and **assume the Axiom of Countable Choice**
([[def-countable-choice]]). Then

$$\mathcal{B}(\mathbb{R}^n) \;\subseteq\; \mathcal{L}(\mathbb{R}^n) :$$

every Borel subset of $\mathbb{R}^n$ ([[def-borel-sigma-algebra]]) is Lebesgue
measurable ([[def-lebesgue-measure-and-the-lebesgue-sigma-algebra]]). In
particular every open set, every closed set and every countable intersection of
open sets is Lebesgue measurable.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A natural number $n \ge 1$ and the Axiom of Countable Choice.

[L1] Assuming countable choice, $\mathcal{L}(\mathbb{R}^n)$ is a sigma-algebra on $\mathbb{R}^n$ and every elementary set is Lebesgue measurable ([[thm-lebesgue-measure-is-a-complete-measure]]).

[L2] $\sigma(\mathcal{H}_n) = \sigma(\mathcal{E}_n) = \mathcal{B}(\mathbb{R}^n)$, where $\mathcal{H}_n$ is the family of half-open boxes and $\mathcal{E}_n$ the family of elementary sets ([[lem-half-open-boxes-generate-the-borel-sigma-algebra-on-rn]]).

[L3] At $m = 1$ every half-open box is elementary ([[def-elementary-set-in-rn]]).

[F1] $\sigma_X(\mathcal E)$ is the unique smallest sigma-algebra on $X$ containing $\mathcal E$ ([[thm-generated-sigma-algebra-exists-and-is-minimal]], [[def-generated-sigma-algebra]]).

[F2] The **Borel sigma-algebra of $X$** is the sigma-algebra generated by its open sets ([[def-borel-sigma-algebra]]); a **sigma-algebra on $X$** is an algebra of subsets closed under countable unions ([[def-sigma-algebra]]).

[F3] The **Axiom of Countable Choice** says that for every family $(X_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ of nonempty sets indexed by $\mathbb{N}$ there is a function $f$ with domain $\mathbb{N}$ such that $f(n) \in X_n$ for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ ([[def-countable-choice]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Under countable choice $\mathcal{L}(\mathbb{R}^n)$ is a sigma-algebra on $\mathbb{R}^n$ containing every elementary set, hence containing the family $\mathcal{H}_n$ of half-open boxes. [L1, L3, F2, F3]

2.1 Since $\sigma(\mathcal{H}_n)$ is the smallest sigma-algebra containing $\mathcal{H}_n$, step 1.1 gives $\sigma(\mathcal{H}_n) \subseteq \mathcal{L}(\mathbb{R}^n)$, and $\sigma(\mathcal{H}_n)=\mathcal{B}(\mathbb{R}^n)$; open sets, closed sets and countable intersections of open sets are Borel. [step 1.1, L2, F1, F2] ∎
