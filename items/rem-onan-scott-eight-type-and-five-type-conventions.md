Modern accounts often use eight types: HA (affine), HS (holomorph simple), HC
(holomorph compound), AS (almost simple), PA (product action), SD (simple
diagonal), CD (compound diagonal), and TW (twisted wreath). Relative to the
coarse five labels on this page, the broad diagonal branch is resolved into
HS, HC, SD, and CD; the other four labels correspond to HA, AS, PA, and TW.
This page keeps the older coarse convention because that is the resolution of
the accessible survey source and is sufficient for the rest of this batch.
