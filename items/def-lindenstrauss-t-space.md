## Definition

Let T be a separable metric space with base point e in which every bounded closed ball is compact and Isom(T) acts transitively and let X be a separable metric space whose bounded closed balls are compact. A T-space structure consists of an open cover 𝒰 of X by relatively compact sets and continuous maps t_U:U×T→X such that t_U(x,e)=x; whenever y=t_U(x,t) and V∈𝒰 contains y, there is an isometry θ of T with t_V(y,·)∘θ=t_U(x,·); and for each U there is r_U>0 such that t_U(x,·) is injective on B_T(r_U,e) for every x∈U. The T-leaf through x is the equivalence class formed by overlapping chart images t_U(x,T), and it is embedded if every map t_U(x,·) on that leaf is injective.
