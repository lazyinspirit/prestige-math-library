## Definition

Let $H/F$ be a finite extension of number fields, let $T$ be a finite set of finite primes of $F$, and let $T_H$ be the set of primes of $H$ above primes in $T$. Let $I_T(H)$ be the multiplicative group of nonzero fractional ideals of $H$ whose valuations are zero at every prime in $T_H$. Let $P_T(H)$ be the subgroup of $I_T(H)$ generated by principal ideals $(\alpha)$ with $\alpha\in H^\times$ and $v_{\mathfrak P}(\alpha-1)>0$ for every $\mathfrak P\in T_H$. The $T$-smoothed ray class group is

$$\operatorname{Cl}_T(H):=I_T(H)/P_T(H).$$

If $H/F$ is Galois with group $G$, its action on ideals induces a left $G$-action on $\operatorname{Cl}_T(H)$, since $T_H$ is stable under $G$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A finite extension $H/F$ of number fields, a finite set $T$ of finite primes of $F$, and the set $T_H$ of primes above $T$.

[F1] The ray class group modulo a finite modulus is the group of fractional ideals prime to that modulus, modulo the subgroup of principal ideals satisfying its local congruence conditions. [[def-ray-class-group]]

[F2] The action of $G=\operatorname{Gal}(H/F)$ sends a fractional ideal $\mathfrak a$ to $g(\mathfrak a)$ and preserves the valuations at primes in the $G$-stable set $T_H$. [[def-number-field]] [[def-ring-of-integers-of-a-number-field]]

## Proof

**Given:** The extension and set $T$ in the Definition.

1.1 The set of fractional ideals whose valuations vanish at each prime in $T_H$ is closed under multiplication and inversion, so $I_T(H)$ is a group. The subgroup $P_T(H)$ is generated inside it by definition; each generator lies in $I_T(H)$ because $v_{\mathfrak P}(\alpha-1)>0$ implies $v_{\mathfrak P}(\alpha)=0$. Thus the quotient group is defined. [F1, given, algebra]

1.2 The set $T_H$ is stable under $G$ by [F2]. If $v_{\mathfrak P}(\alpha-1)>0$, then $v_{g\mathfrak P}(g\alpha-1)=v_{\mathfrak P}(\alpha-1)>0$. Therefore $G$ preserves both $I_T(H)$ and $P_T(H)$, and its action on ideals descends to the quotient. [F2, step 1.1, algebra]
