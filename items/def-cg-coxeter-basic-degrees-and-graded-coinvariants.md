## Definition

Let $(W,S)$ be a Coxeter system of finite type with $n=|S|$, and let $V_{\mathbb C}$, $\rho_{\mathbb C}$, $S:=\mathbb C[V_{\mathbb C}]$, $R:=S^{W}$, $R_+=\bigoplus_{d>0}R_d$ and $I:=S R_+$ be as in [[def-finite-linear-invariant-and-coinvariant-polynomial-algebras]] and [[lem-cg-complexification-satisfies-reflection-invariant-hypotheses]]; fix coordinates $x_1,\dots,x_n$ so that $S=\mathbb C[x_1,\dots,x_n]$ as the iterated polynomial ring of [[def-multivariate-polynomial-ring-by-iteration]], with the graded structure of [[def-graded-ring-and-graded-module]].

**(1) Existence of a basic family.** Assume the Axiom of Choice. A minimal finite family $f_1,\dots,f_r$ of homogeneous positive-degree invariants generating the ideal $I$ exists, generates $R$ as a $\mathbb C$-algebra, and is algebraically independent ([[lem-finite-reflection-invariant-generators-are-algebraically-independent]]); under the stated AC any such minimal family has exactly $n$ elements ([[thm-chevalley-shephard-todd-for-finite-weyl-groups]], [[lem-reflection-basic-invariants-form-a-regular-sequence]]).

**(2) Degrees and exponents.** Fix one such family $f_1,\dots,f_n$ and call
$$d_i:=\deg f_i\quad\text{the }\textbf{basic degrees},\qquad e_i:=d_i-1\quad\text{the }\textbf{exponents},$$
arranged in nondecreasing order $d_1\le\dots\le d_n$. Each $d_i$ is a positive integer, and in fact $d_i\ge2$: a degree-one invariant would be a nonzero $W$-invariant linear form, excluded by [[lem-cg-complexification-satisfies-reflection-invariant-hypotheses]] (3).

**(3) The graded coinvariant algebra.** The **coinvariant algebra** of $(W,V_{\mathbb C})$ is the graded quotient
$$A:=S/I=\bigoplus_{k\ge0}A_k,\qquad A_k:=(S_k+I)/I,$$
with $A_0=\mathbb C$ (constants survive because $I$ has positive degree), as in [[def-finite-linear-invariant-and-coinvariant-polynomial-algebras]]. Its Hilbert series is written $\operatorname{Hilb}(A,t)$.

**(4) Well-definedness warning.** This definition asserts nothing about the multiset $\{d_1,\dots,d_n\}$ being independent of the chosen family, nothing about $\operatorname{Hilb}(A,t)$ or $\prod_i d_i$, and no relation between the $d_i$ and the reflection geometry; all of that is the content of the justifier [[lem-cg-basic-degrees-independent-and-coinvariant-series]].

**(5) Conventions.** For $n=0$ the group is trivial, $S=R=\mathbb C$, $I=0$, $A=\mathbb C$ and the families and products are empty. For reducible $S$ the family in (2) is the concatenation of the families of the connected components, and $I$ is generated by all components' positive-degree invariants. No crystallographic or integrality assumption is made.
