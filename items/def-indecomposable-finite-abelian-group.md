## Definition

A nontrivial finite abelian group is **indecomposable** if it is not an internal direct product of two nontrivial subgroups in the sense of [[def-internal-direct-product-of-subgroups]]. It is **decomposable** if such a product exists. The trivial group is assigned neither label.
