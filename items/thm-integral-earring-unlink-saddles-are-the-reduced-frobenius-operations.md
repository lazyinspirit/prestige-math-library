## Statement

Assume full AC and the actual based-link earring count, composition and integral skein operations of the named suppliers. Fix ONE finite planar resolution diagram, its marked base component and its finite collection of planar saddle cobordisms. Use the actual compatible backgrounds and determinant orientations relative to their common marked collars. Then the free integral unlink groups and these ACTUAL saddle maps admit simultaneous identifications with the reduced Frobenius module of
$$A=\mathbb Z[x]/(x^2),\qquad 1=v_+,\quad x=v_-,\quad |v_+|=0,\quad |v_-|=2\pmod4.$$
An unlink with one marked component and m other ordered components has group A tensor ... tensor A, with m factors; the marked factor is the quotient A/(x)=Z. A disk birth inserts v_+, and a disk death has epsilon(v_+)=0, epsilon(v_-)=1. On two unmarked components the merge and split are
$$\mu(1\otimes1)=1,\quad\mu(1\otimes x)=\mu(x\otimes1)=x,\quad\mu(x\otimes x)=0,$$
$$\Delta(1)=x\otimes1+1\otimes x,\qquad\Delta(x)=x\otimes x.$$
A merge with the marked component is the quotient action 1 tensor 1 to 1, 1 tensor x to zero; a split of the marked component sends 1 to 1 tensor x. The bases are primitive integral bases, not rational bases recovered after a rank computation. Reordering the unmarked components permutes their even factors.

These identifications are compatible with the actual finite planar vertex and saddle identifications used for that diagram, including the common marked collar. They do not assert a split-link excision theorem, invariance under arbitrary unlink concordances or arbitrary unlink-motion loops, or a new analytic construction beyond the named actual count suppliers. The cube's ordered orientation-line edge signs are retained; they are not replaced by independently chosen signs for each occurrence of a saddle.
