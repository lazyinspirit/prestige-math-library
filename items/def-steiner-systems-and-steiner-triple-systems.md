## Definition

A **Steiner triple system** of order $v$, written $\mathrm{STS}(v)$, is a
$2$-$(v,3,1)$ design.

## Remarks

This is the case conventionally denoted $S(2,3,v)$ in the general
Steiner-system notation. The present page uses only this triple-system case.
