## Definition

Let Y=Γ\ℍ be either a finite-area congruence surface or a compact arithmetic surface from a quaternion order, with its standard Hecke correspondences. Let φⱼ be L²-normalized joint eigenfunctions of the hyperbolic Laplacian and these correspondences, with Laplace eigenvalues tending to infinity. A quantum limit is a vague (that is, tested against continuous compactly supported functions) subsequential limit of their microlocal lift probability measures on T¹Y; it is arithmetic when the sequence consists of joint Hecke eigenfunctions. The limiting measure is a positive Radon subprobability in the noncompact case and a probability in the compact case; the positive finite-index microlocal lift construction is a separate prerequisite.
