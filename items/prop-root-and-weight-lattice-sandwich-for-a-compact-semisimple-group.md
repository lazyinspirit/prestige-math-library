## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $G$ be a compact connected semisimple Lie group
with maximal torus $T$, character lattice $X^*(T)$ and root system $\Phi$ with
root lattice $Q$ and weight lattice $P$ in the real span of the roots
([[def-root-lattice-coroot-lattice-weight-lattice-and-coweight-lattice]]). Then
$$Q\subseteq X^*(T)\subseteq P .$$
Moreover the simply connected form has $X^*(T)=P$ and the adjoint form has
$X^*(T)=Q$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** Assume the Axiom of Choice, a compact connected semisimple $G$ with maximal torus $T$, and the lattices $Q\subseteq P$ of the root system.

[A1] The Axiom of Choice is [[def-axiom-of-choice]]; it enters through the integration and covering theory of [L3] and [L5].

[L1] Roots are characters of $T$ with differentials forming a reduced crystallographic root system; characters differentiate to integral functionals and pair perfectly with cocharacters ([[def-roots-of-a-compact-connected-lie-group]], [[prop-differentiation-identifies-characters-with-the-integral-weight-lattice-of-t]], [[def-root-datum-of-a-compact-connected-lie-group]]).

[L2] $Q$ is the lattice generated by the roots and $P=\{\lambda:(\lambda,\alpha^\vee)\in\mathbb Z\ \forall\alpha\}$ is the dual lattice of the coroot lattice; $Q\subseteq P$ ([[def-root-lattice-coroot-lattice-weight-lattice-and-coweight-lattice]]).

[L3] Every connected real Lie group is a central quotient of its simply connected cover; the Lie functor on connected simply connected groups is an equivalence with finite-dimensional real Lie algebras, and every Lie-algebra homomorphism from a simply connected group integrates uniquely ([[thm-connected-lie-groups-are-central-quotients-of-their-simply-connected-integrations]], [[thm-lie-second-fundamental-theorem]]).

[L4] Finite-dimensional irreducible complex representations of the semisimple Lie algebra $\mathfrak g_{\mathbb C}$ are classified by dominant integral weights in $P$, with the fundamental weights realised by the fundamental representations ([[thm-highest-weight-classification-of-finite-dimensional-irreducible-representations]], [[def-root-lattice-coroot-lattice-weight-lattice-and-coweight-lattice]]).

[L5] A compact connected semisimple Lie group with a given Lie algebra is a quotient of the simply connected group by a finite central subgroup; the universal cover of a compact group is compact ([[thm-connected-lie-groups-are-central-quotients-of-their-simply-connected-integrations]], [[prop-compact-lie-groups-admit-bi-invariant-riemannian-metrics]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Each root $\alpha$ is an honest character of $T$ by [L1], so $\mathbb Z\alpha\subseteq X^*(T)$ for each root and hence $Q\subseteq X^*(T)$. [L1, L2]

1.2 If $\chi\in X^*(T)$, then for every root $\alpha$ with cocharacter $\alpha^\vee$ the composite $\chi\circ\alpha^\vee:S^1\to S^1$ is $z\mapsto z^{n}$ for some $n\in\mathbb Z$, and $n=\langle\chi,\alpha^\vee\rangle$; hence $(\chi,\alpha^\vee)\in\mathbb Z$ for every root and $\chi\in P$. Thus $X^*(T)\subseteq P$. [L1, L2]

2.1 For the simply connected endpoint, let $G_{sc}$ be the simply connected cover of $G$ with maximal torus $T_{sc}$, and let $\pi_1(G)$ denote its finite central kernel. The fundamental group of a compact connected Lie group is finitely generated, and if $\pi_1(G)$ were infinite then for the finite-index subgroups of arbitrarily large index the corresponding finite covers $G_K$ would be compact with $|\ker(G_K\to G)|$ unbounded; since $Q\subseteq X^*(T_K)\subseteq P$ for each of them by steps 1.1 and 1.2 and the index $[P:X^*(T_K)]$ equals the cardinality of the covering kernel, the index would be bounded by $[P:Q]$, a contradiction. Hence $G_{sc}$ is compact. [L3, L5, step 1.1, step 1.2]

2.2 For the adjoint endpoint, write a character $\chi$ of the maximal torus $T_{ad}$ of the adjoint group in the basis of simple roots: $\chi=\sum_jc_j\alpha_j$ with $c_j\in\mathbb R$. Let $H_j\in\mathfrak t$ be defined by $\alpha_i(H_j)=2\pi i\,\delta_{ij}$. Every root has integral simple-root coordinates, so $\operatorname{Ad}(\exp H_j)$ acts trivially on the root spaces and on the torus, and since the adjoint group has trivial centre we get $\exp H_j=1$; analytic integrality of $\chi$ then forces $\chi(H_j)=2\pi i\,c_j\in2\pi i\mathbb Z$ for every $j$, so all $c_j$ are integers and $\chi\in Q$. Together with step 1.1 this gives $X^*(T_{ad})=Q$. [L1, step 1.1]

3.1 Every dominant integral element of $P$, in particular every fundamental weight, is the highest weight of a finite-dimensional representation of $\mathfrak g_{\mathbb C}$ by [L4]; restricting the infinitesimal representation to the compact real form $\operatorname{Lie}(G_{sc})$ and integrating it by [L3] gives a finite-dimensional representation of the simply connected compact group $G_{sc}$, whose highest weight character is an element of $X^*(T_{sc})$ with differential that fundamental weight; hence $P\subseteq X^*(T_{sc})$ and, with step 1.2, $X^*(T_{sc})=P$. [L3, L4, step 2.1]


4.1 Steps 1.1 and 1.2 give the sandwich $Q\subseteq X^*(T)\subseteq P$ for every compact connected semisimple $G$, and steps 2.2 and 3.1 realise the two endpoints; the Axiom of Choice entered only through the integration and covering theory used. [A1, step 1.1, step 1.2, step 3.1, step 2.2] ∎
