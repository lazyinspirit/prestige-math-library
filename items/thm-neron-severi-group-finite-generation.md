## Statement

Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field and let $X$ be an integral smooth projective $k$-scheme of finite type. Let $\operatorname{Pic}(X)$ be the abelian group of isomorphism classes of line bundles on $X$, and let $\operatorname{Pic}^0(X)$ be its subgroup generated by differences of fibers in connected finite-type families of line bundles on $X$. Then the Néron–Severi group
$$\operatorname{NS}(X):=\operatorname{Pic}(X)/\operatorname{Pic}^0(X)$$
is a finitely generated abelian group.
