## Open problem

Let $(V,D)$ be a Bloch–Kato motivic pair arising from a pure motive and let $M$ be a stable integral lattice. Prove that the Bloch–Kato Shafarevich–Tate group $\mathrm{III}(M)$ is finite and that
$$\operatorname{Tam}(M)=\frac{\#H^0(\mathbb Q,M^*\otimes\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z(1))}{\#\mathrm{III}(M)},$$
where $\operatorname{Tam}(M)$ is the total Tamagawa measure of the local point groups and $M^*=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb Z}(M,\mathbb Z)$.

## Status

This is Bloch–Kato Conjecture 5.15. It is open in general. The source also gives an equivalent special-value formula; the definition of the pair and its local measures above is escalated, so this open problem is not a supplier.
