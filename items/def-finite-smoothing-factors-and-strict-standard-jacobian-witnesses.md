## Definition

For a ring map R→L, the **finite smooth-factor property** means that every finitely presented R-algebra A with a specified R-map A→L factors as A→B→L for a smooth finitely presented R-algebra B, with the actual composite unchanged.

For R Noetherian and a presentation A=R[x_1,…,x_n]/J, J=(f_1,…,f_m), a **strict-standard witness** for π∈R consists of 0≤c≤min(n,m), polynomial coefficients a_I, the identity π=Σ_(|I|=c) a_I det(∂f_j/∂x_i)_(j≤c,i∈I) in A, and polynomial identities π f_l∈(f_1,…,f_c)+J² for l>c. When c=0 the minor is one. Specified smooth-localization witnesses are finite standard-smooth charts and covering identities for A_a/R.

For R,L Noetherian and a finitely presented R-algebra A with map to L, H(A/R) is the radical ideal generated by elements a for which A_a/R has a smooth-localization witness. Put h(A)=rad(H(A/R)L). A **local resolution at q∈Spec L** is a finitely presented factor A→B→L with h(A)⊂h(B) and h(B)⊄q. The definition does not assert that such a resolution exists.
