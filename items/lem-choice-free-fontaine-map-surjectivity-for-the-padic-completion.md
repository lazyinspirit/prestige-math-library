## Statement

Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be a GIVEN algebraic closure with its given extended $p$-adic absolute value, and let $C=\widehat F$ be its ordinary Cauchy-sequence completion. Suppose a compatible primitive system $(\zeta_{p^n})_{n\ge0}$ in $F$ is GIVEN. Put $A=\mathcal O_C$ and $B=A^\flat$. Then the canonical Fontaine homomorphism
$$\theta:W_p(B)\longrightarrow A$$
is surjective, without any Choice axiom. In fact the proof constructs a specified set-theoretic section $A\to W_p(B)$ depending on the given root system and the specified least-index approximation algorithms; no ring-homomorphic section is asserted.

For every finite intermediate field $L/\mathbb Q_p$, if $E_L$ is the closure of $\bigcup_n L(\zeta_{p^n})$ inside $C$, then Frobenius on $\mathcal O_{E_L}/p$ is surjective. For $L=\mathbb Q_p(b)$ with specified $b\in F$, a specified dense enumeration of rational expressions in $b$ and the given roots supplies an actual compatible Frobenius-lift section into $(\mathcal O_{E_L})^\flat$. These are full constructions at the given valued-closure input; no general perfectoid comparison theorem is assumed.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The valued algebraic closure and compatible primitive system. Neither is constructed by this proof. All finite fields below are the named subfields of this actual $F$.

[F1] Finite p-adic subfields are complete, have constructed DVRs and finite integral bases, $[L:K]=ef$, and different transitivity. At global normalization $v(p)=1$, the different value decreases under degree-preserving base change; a totally ramified extension is integrally generated by any uniformizer, with different generated by its minimal-polynomial derivative. [[lem-choice-free-finite-padic-different-and-base-change-calculus]]

[F2] The ordinary completion and the ball completion are canonically isometric complete fields, their valuation rings have canonical adic completeness, and there are specified functions $a_N:C\to F$ with $|a_N(x)-x|<2^{-N}$ under this identification. [[lem-choice-free-identification-of-padic-algebraic-closure-completions]]

[F3] For a canonically $p$-adically complete ring, the finite-ghost construction gives the Fontaine homomorphism and sharp map, identifies sharp modulo $p$ with the initial tilt coordinate, and is functorial. [[lem-fontaine-map-from-finite-witt-ghost-congruences]]

[F4] Over a perfect characteristic-$p$ base, the Witt ring is separated and complete for $p$, and has unique convergent Teichmüller expansions. [[lem-perfect-base-witt-frobenius-and-p-adic-completeness]] [[lem-perfect-base-witt-teichmuller-expansions-and-units]]

[F5] Over a nondiscretely valued characteristic-zero field, Frobenius surjectivity modulo a scalar dividing $p$ implies surjectivity modulo $p$ in any valuation-ring algebra, by a finite lifting argument requiring no completeness or Choice. [[lem-finite-frobenius-change-of-modulus-in-mixed-characteristic]]

[F6] A simple residue root of a monic polynomial has a unique Hensel lift in a complete valued field. [[lem-hensel-factor-lifting-over-a-complete-valued-field]]

[F7] Monic polynomial division and the finite binomial identities hold. [[thm-monic-polynomial-division]] [[thm-binomial-theorem-over-a-commutative-ring]] [[thm-binomial-closed-formula]]

[F8] Degrees multiply in finite field towers. [[thm-tower-law-for-finite-field-extensions]]

[F9] Every element of a finite field of cardinality $q$ satisfies $a^q=a$. [[prop-finite-fields-are-splitting-fields-of-x-q-minus-x]]

## Proof

1.1 By [F2], the actual ordinary completion $C$ is a complete field and $A$ has its canonical $p$-adic completion isomorphism, so [F3] constructs the map in the Statement. For a finite $L$, set $K_n=\mathbb Q_p(\zeta_{p^n})$ and $L_n=LK_n$ for $n\ge1$. The cyclotomic product identity gives $v(\zeta_{p^n}-1)=1/(p^{n-1}(p-1))$: each primitive conjugate has the same value because its quotient difference is a geometric sum with unit residue, and the product of the primitive differences is $p$. The product identity itself follows by factoring the monic geometric polynomial using [F7]. The first $p^{n-1}(p-1)$ powers of $\zeta_{p^n}-1$ are independent over $\mathbb Q_p$, since their valuations have distinct residues modulo $\mathbb Z$. Its cyclotomic equation has that degree. Hence $[K_n:\mathbb Q_p]=p^{n-1}(p-1)$, and [F1] gives total ramification, residue field $\mathbb F_p$, and uniformizer $\zeta_{p^n}-1$. The degree-$p$ next-step equation is $(1+T)^p-\zeta_{p^n}$; [F1]'s derivative formula gives $D(K_{n+1}/K_n)=v(p)=1$. This includes $p=2$; the degree at $n=1$ is then one. [F1, F2, F3, F7, given, algebra]

2.1 The positive integers $d_n=[L_n:K_n]$ are nonincreasing under base change, so eventually constant. By [F8], thereafter $[L_{n+1}:L_n]=p$. The nested residue-field degrees of $L_n$ over $\mathbb F_p$ are bounded by $d_n\le[L:\mathbb Q_p]$, so they also stabilize. Thus these degree-$p$ steps are eventually totally ramified. Put $D_n=D(L_n/K_n)$, defined intrinsically by the trace-dual ideal in [F1]; no sequence of generators is chosen. After degree stabilization, [F1] gives $D_{n+1}\le D_n$, and all $D_n\ge0$. This decreasing real sequence converges, so $D_n-D_{n+1}\to0$. Applying different transitivity in the two towers from $K_n$ to $L_{n+1}$ yields $$\delta_n:=D(L_{n+1}/L_n)=1+D_{n+1}-D_n\longrightarrow1.$$ The absolute ramification indices of $L_n$ grow by $p$ at these steps; their least positive valuation therefore tends to zero. [F1, F8, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Choose one sufficiently large $n$ with $\delta_n\ge3/4$ and $(p-2)t\le1/4$, where $t$ is the least positive valuation in $L_{n+1}$. Choose one uniformizer $\pi$ of that field. Its Eisenstein minimal polynomial over $L_n$ is $g(T)=T^p+\sum_{j<p}c_jT^j$ by [F1]. In $g'(\pi)$, the nonzero terms $jc_j\pi^{j-1}$ for $1\le j<p$ have valuation residues $(j-1)t$ modulo $pt$, since $v(c_j)\in pt\mathbb Z$ and $v(j)=0$. The leading term $p\pi^{p-1}$ has residue $(p-1)t$, since $1/t$ is an integer divisible by $p$. Thus all these term valuations are distinct, and the derivative formula shows each is at least $\delta_n$. Consequently $v(c_j)\ge\delta_n-(j-1)t\ge1/2$ for $1\le j<p$. The element $\lambda=-c_0$ is a uniformizer of $L_n$, and $$v(\pi^p-\lambda)\ge1/2.$$ Only one uniformizer was chosen for this one finite step, not a uniformizer for every field. [F1, step 2.1, construct, algebra]

4.1 Every integral $x\in L_n$ has a finite expansion in $\lambda$ to error valuation at least $1/2$. By [F9], its residue digits can be taken as the unique lifts of the finite-field roots of $T^q-T$, by [F6], with $q=|k_{L_n}|$. Each such digit is the $p$th power of another such digit, because Frobenius permutes the finite residue field and uniqueness of Hensel lifts preserves powers. Replace $\lambda$ by $\pi^p$ in this finite expansion and replace each digit by its $p$th root. The relation in step 3.1 and the characteristic-$p$ binomial identity modulo $p$ give $y\in\mathcal O_{L_{n+1}}$ with $v(x-y^p)\ge1/2$. For any integral element in $L_\infty=\bigcup_n L_n$, one may first pass to a sufficiently large finite stage containing it and satisfying step 3.1. Fix one cyclotomic scalar $\varpi$ with $0<v(\varpi)\le1/2$. Frobenius on $\mathcal O_{L_\infty}/\varpi$ is therefore surjective. The value group of $L_\infty$ is nondiscrete by step 1.1, and $p\in\varpi\mathcal O_{L_\infty}$, so [F5] upgrades this to surjectivity modulo $p$. All digit expansions and liftings used for a given element were finite. [F5, F6, F7, F9, step 1.1, step 3.1, construct, algebra]

5.1 Let $E=E_L$ be the closure of $L_\infty$ in $C$. Continuity of field operations, including inversion at nonzero points, makes it a closed subfield, hence complete. Its valuation ring $A_E$ is canonically $p$-adically complete: send any compatible residue system in $A_E/p^mA_E$ into $A/p^mA$, take its unique limit by [F2], and observe that for each precision its residue class contains an element of $A_E$ within that precision of the limit. The limit is in the closure $E$ and is integral, so represents the original system; injectivity is separatedness. This argument does not select representatives for all $m$. For $x\in A_E$, choose one integral element of $L_\infty$ within $|p|$ of it, then use step 4.1. This proves Frobenius surjective on $A_E/pA_E$. [F2, step 4.1, construct, algebra]

6.1 Now specialize to $L=\mathbb Q_p(b)$ for one specified integral $b\in F$. Enumerate all finite rational expressions in $b$ and finitely many members of the GIVEN root system, assigning zero to invalid division expressions. Their values $t_j$ are dense in $L_\infty$ and hence in $E$: rational coefficients approximate the finitely many $\mathbb Q_p$ coefficients in any field expression, keeping its denominator nonzero. For a class $c\in A_E/pA_E$, step 5.1 and this density give some integral $t_j$ with $t_j^p\bmod p=c$; approximation of an integral root within $|p|$ preserves this congruence by [F7]. Let $q_b(c)$ be the class of the least such $t_j$. This is a specified function with $q_b(c)^p=c$. Recursion of this function gives $$s_b(c)=(c,q_b(c),q_b^2(c),\ldots)\in A_E^\flat.$$ It is an actual section of the initial tilt projection. No root or residue representative is chosen from an arbitrary countable family; the enumeration and least indices specify every entry. [F3, F7, step 5.1, construct, algebra]

7.1 Use the specified approximation functions of [F2]. Let $N$ be the least integer with $2^{-N}<|p|$. For any actual $a\in A$, put $b=a_N(a)\in F$. Then $|a-b|<|p|$, so $b$ is integral. Form the uniquely specified field $E_{\mathbb Q_p(b)}$, its enumeration and the section in step 6.1, and take $x(a)=s_b(b\bmod pA_E)$. The map $A_E/pA_E\to A/pA$ is injective, since an element of $A_E$ divisible by $p$ in $A$ has its quotient by $p$ in the valued subfield $A_E$. Thus $A_E\hookrightarrow A$ induces an inclusion of tilts; their sharp maps agree by functoriality [F3]. Therefore $x(a)^\sharp\equiv b\equiv a\bmod pA$. Define $R(a)=(a-x(a)^\sharp)/p\in A$. Both $x(a)$ and $R(a)$ are specified functions of the actual input $a$, since the approximation, fields, enumerations and least indices are specified. The finite bases and uniformizers used only to prove the existence of successful indices are not additional output choices. [F2, F3, step 5.1, step 6.1, construct, algebra]

8.1 Starting with $a_0=a$, recurse on the specified function $R$, and put $x_i=x(a_i)$. Then $a_i=x_i^\sharp+pa_{i+1}$ and finite substitution gives $$a=\sum_{i<m}p^ix_i^\sharp+p^ma_m.$$ The perfect tilt $B$ has its $p$-adically complete Witt ring by [F3] and [F4], so $w(a)=\sum_{i\ge0}p^i[x_i]$ exists there. Continuity of $\theta$ and separatedness of $A$ give $\theta(w(a))=a$, since the displayed errors lie in $p^mA$. This is the asserted specified set-theoretic section, hence full surjectivity. The recursion uses a function already defined on all actual inputs, not an assumed section of a surjective Frobenius map. [F3, F4, step 7.1, construct, algebra]

9.1 The construction treats every prime, including $2$, every integral input including zero, and every finite $L$. For any fixed finite computation only finitely many bases, roots or uniformizers were instantiated. The infinite towers were the named fields generated by the GIVEN compatible system; the real different values were intrinsic scalars, and both infinite lifting recursions used specified least-index functions. The global missing selector is supplied by [F2], so it is not inferred from per-residue existential surjectivity. No APF theorem, Herbrand formula, almost purity, or generic countable surjective-inverse-limit principle was imported. [F1, F2, step 1.1, step 2.1, step 4.1, step 5.1, step 6.1, step 7.1, step 8.1, algebra] ∎

## Source notes

The finite cyclotomic different mechanism was read in Colmez §§1.1–1.4. The proof here uses the locally proved degree-preserving Gram inequality and different transitivity to get $\delta_n\to1$; it does not import the stronger APF or upper-ramification assertions appearing in alternative treatments. The derivative terms' distinct valuation residues, finite Teichmüller digit expansion and finite modulus change supply Frobenius surjectivity directly.

The exact source of the formerly missing global selection is the completed choice-free model-identification lemma: its specified algebraic approximant is used at every actual Witt coefficient input. Consequently no Choice hypothesis has been silently deleted from BMS's general repeated-lifting argument. This proves the specialized full Fontaine-surjectivity assertion for the given p-adic algebraic closure and its ordinary completion. General perfectoid rings and Tate algebras, completed crystalline embeddings and the remaining period comparisons are separate targets.
