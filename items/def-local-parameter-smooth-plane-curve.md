## Definition

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $C=V(F)$ be a plane projective curve over the algebraically closed field $k$ and let $p\in C$ be a smooth point [[lem-smooth-plane-curve-unique-tangent]], [[def-singular-and-regular-loci-variety]]. Then the local ring $\mathcal O_{C,p}$ is a discrete valuation ring [[thm-one-dimensional-regular-local-rings-are-dvrs]], [[def-local-ring]], [[def-discrete-valuation-ring]], [[def-germ-and-local-ring-classical-variety]], [[thm-local-ring-affine-variety-localization]].

Its maximal ideal is generated by the image of any local equation $l$ of a line through $p$ that is not the tangent line to $C$ at $p$ [[thm-zariski-tangent-space-jacobian-kernel]], [[def-zariski-tangent-space-point]]; such an $l$ is a **uniformising parameter** at $p$ [[def-uniformising-parameter]], [[def-embedding-dimension-and-regular-local-ring]]. The associated valuation

$$ \operatorname{ord}_p\colon \operatorname{Frac}(\mathcal O_{C,p})^\times\to\mathbb Z $$

is the **order of vanishing along $C$ at $p$**. Its domain is the fraction field of the local DVR, equivalently the function field of the unique irreducible component through the smooth point $p$; when $C$ is irreducible this is $k(C)$. On nonzero local germs, it is positive exactly for the nonzero rational functions on $C$ that vanish at $p$, vanishes exactly on the units of $\mathcal O_{C,p}$, and for $n\ge1$ the nonzero germs of order at least $n$, together with zero, form the ideal $\mathfrak m_p^n$ of $\mathcal O_{C,p}$. This follows from the normal form $h=u\pi^r$ in a DVR [[thm-dvr-element-normal-form]].

## Remarks

- **Existence of non-tangent lines.** Through a point of $\mathbf P^2$ there pass three or more lines; at most the one tangent line to $C$ is excluded, so a non-tangent line through $p$ exists. Its local equation is a linear form with nonzero image in the one-dimensional cotangent space of $\mathcal O_{C,p}$ (the image is nonzero exactly because the line is not the tangent line), hence generates the maximal ideal of the DVR: writing $l=u\pi^r$, its nonzero class modulo $(\pi^2)$ forces $r=1$ [[thm-dvr-element-normal-form]].
- **Units and vanishing.** A germ is a unit of $\mathcal O_{C,p}$ exactly when it does not vanish at $p$, so $\operatorname{ord}_p(u)=0$ for units and $\operatorname{ord}_p(h)>0$ for $h$ vanishing at $p$; this is the sense in which $\operatorname{ord}_p$ measures the order of vanishing along the curve. The value $\operatorname{ord}_p$ is independent of the chosen uniformiser because any two uniformisers differ by a unit.
- **Choice.** The definition inherits the Axiom of Choice from the DVR and Jacobian-criterion suppliers: the DVR identification and the local-dimensional and classical-variety suppliers inherit it. The rational-point Jacobian-kernel statement itself needs no choice [[def-axiom-of-choice]]. No additional choice is made here.
