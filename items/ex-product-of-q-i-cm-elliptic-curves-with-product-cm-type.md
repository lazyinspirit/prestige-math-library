## Example

Assume AC. Let $K=\mathbb Q(i)$ and let $A=(\mathbb C/\mathbb Z[i])^2$, with each factor identified with its Weierstrass elliptic curve and chord-tangent group law. Let $E=K\times K$ act on $H_1(A,\mathbb Q)\cong K\oplus K$ by coordinatewise multiplication; its ring of integers $O_K\times O_K$ preserves the product lattice. Choose the CM type $\Phi=\{\varphi_1,\varphi_2\}$ where $\varphi_j(z_1,z_2)=z_j$ under the fixed inclusion $K\subset\mathbb C$. Then $A$ has dimension two and CM type $(E,\Phi)$, its reflex field is $E^r=K$, its reflex type is the standard one on $K$, and its reflex norm is $N_\Phi(x)=(x,x)$ for $x\in K^\times$ and for finite idèles.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The data in the Example and the fixed inclusion $\mathbb Q(i)\subset\mathbb C$.

[A1] AC is inherited from the abelian-variety classification and stated explicitly. [[def-axiom-of-choice]]

[F1] The reflex field is generated by the sums of the selected embeddings, and the reflex norm is the determinant on the CM-type trace module. [[def-reflex-field-and-reflex-type-of-a-cm-type]] [[def-reflex-norm-associated-to-a-cm-type]]

[F2] A rank-two lattice quotient is biholomorphic to its smooth projective Weierstrass cubic. [[thm-complex-torus-weierstrass-cubic-isomorphism]]

[F3] The Weierstrass biholomorphism transports the torus group law to the algebraic chord-tangent law on the cubic. [[thm-elliptic-cubic-chord-tangent-group-law]]

## Proof

1.1 Each factor $\mathbb C/\mathbb Z[i]$ is a smooth projective cubic by [F2], and [F3] supplies its algebraic group law; hence their product is an abelian surface. The coordinate action of $K\times K$ is faithful and the tangent characters are precisely $\varphi_1,\varphi_2$, so it has the stated CM type. [A1, F2, F3, given, algebra]

1.2 For $a=(z_1,z_2)\in E$, the type trace is $z_1+z_2$, and the values at $(i,0)$ generate $K$. Thus $E^r=K$. The trace module is $V_\Phi=K\oplus K$, where $(z_1,z_2)$ acts coordinatewise and $x\in E^r=K$ acts by scalar multiplication on both coordinates. Its $E$-trace is $z_1+z_2$. An element $x\in K^\times$ has determinant $x$ on each coordinate, so the reflex norm is $(x,x)$; the same local determinant gives the finite-idèle formula. [F1, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
