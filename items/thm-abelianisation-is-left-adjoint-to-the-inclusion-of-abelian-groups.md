## Statement

Abelianisation defines a functor $(-)^{\mathrm{ab}}:\mathbf{Grp}\to\mathbf{Ab}$, and it is left adjoint to the inclusion $I:\mathbf{Ab}\hookrightarrow\mathbf{Grp}$. Explicitly, every homomorphism $f:G\to I(A)$ with $A$ abelian factors uniquely through the quotient $q_G:G\to G^{\mathrm{ab}}$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A group $G$, an abelian group $A$, and a homomorphism $f:G\to A$.

[F1] The abelianisation of $G$ is $G^{\mathrm{ab}}:=G/[G,G]$ ([[def-abelianisation-of-a-group]]).

[F2] For $g,h\in G$, their commutator is $[g,h]:=ghg^{-1}h^{-1}$, and $[G,G]$ is generated by all commutators ([[def-commutator-and-commutator-subgroup]]).

[F3] A homomorphism that kills a normal subgroup factors uniquely through the quotient group ([[thm-quotient-group-universal-property]]).

[L1] Chosen objectwise universal arrows assemble uniquely into a left adjoint ([[thm-objectwise-universal-arrows-assemble-into-a-left-adjoint]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Since $A$ is abelian, $f([g,h])=f(g)f(h)f(g)^{-1}f(h)^{-1}=1_A$ for all $g,h\in G$, so $[G,G]\subseteq\ker f$. [F2, algebra]

1.2 The group $G^{\mathrm{ab}}$ is abelian: $q_G$ has kernel $[G,G]$ by [F1], so for $g,h\in G$ the commutator $q_G(g)q_G(h)q_G(g)^{-1}q_G(h)^{-1}=q_G([g,h])$ is trivial by [F2], and $q_G$ is surjective. [F1, F2, algebra]

2.1 By [F3], there is a unique homomorphism $\bar f:G^{\mathrm{ab}}\to A$ with $\bar f q_G=f$. [step 1.1, F1, F3]

3.1 For a homomorphism $a:G\to H$, the target $H^{\mathrm{ab}}$ is abelian by step 1.2, so step 2.1 applies to $q_Ha:G\to H^{\mathrm{ab}}$; define $a^{\mathrm{ab}}$ as its unique factor through $q_G$. [step 1.2, step 2.1, construct]

4.1 Uniqueness in [F3] gives $(1_G)^{\mathrm{ab}}=1$ and $(ba)^{\mathrm{ab}}=b^{\mathrm{ab}}a^{\mathrm{ab}}$, so abelianisation is a functor and $q$ is natural. [step 3.1, F3]

5.1 Step 2.1 is the universal-arrow property of $(G^{\mathrm{ab}},q_G)$ from $G$ to the inclusion $I$; [L1] therefore gives $(-)^{\mathrm{ab}}\dashv I$. [step 2.1, step 4.1, L1] ∎
