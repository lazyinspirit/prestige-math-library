## Statement

Let $p\ge4$ be even, $A\in\mathbb C$ a primitive $2p$-th root, and $r=p/2$. In the BHMV Temperley–Lieb quotient with loop value $\delta=-A^2-A^{-2}$ and planar pivotal trace, the complete simple-label set is $\{0,1,\ldots,r-2\}$, represented by the surviving Jones–Wenzl projectors $p_a$ obtained from the standard recursion using unit denominators $[2],\ldots,[a]$. Every simple is self-dual. Its fusion multiplicities are

$$N_{ab}^{c}=1 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad |a-b|\le c\le\min(a+b,\,2r-4-a-b) \ \text{and }a+b+c\text{ is even},$$

and are zero otherwise. With the BHMV overcrossing map $c_i=A1_i+A^{-1}U_i$, where $U_i$ is the cup-cap generator, the full-framing ribbon structure has planar quantum dimensions $d_a=[a+1]$ and simple-object twists $\theta_a=(-1)^aA^{a(a+2)}$. Here the quantum integers use the fixed BHMV recurrence. The unnormalized Hopf-link matrix, whose $(a,b)$ entry is the pivotal trace of the double braiding on $p_a\otimes p_b$, is nondegenerate. Here modular means that this finite semisimple ribbon category has an invertible unnormalized Hopf-link matrix.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The fixed BHMV parameter, its negligible quotient, and the standard planar pivotal convention.

[F1] [[def-sl-two-root-of-unity-skein-parameter]]: $r=p/2$, $\delta=-A^2-A^{-2}$, and $[j]$ is defined by $[0]=0$, $[1]=1$, $[j+1]=\delta[j]-[j-1]$.

[F2] [[lem-root-jones-wenzl-recursion-and-negligible-ideal]]: the quotient's negligible ideal is generated by $p_{r-1}$, and its complete simple labels are $0,\ldots,r-2$.

[F3] [[thm-temperley-lieb-rank-one-pivotal-ribbon-conversion]]: for $r\ge3$ and $q_0=e^{\pi i/r}$, the negative-loop diagram quotient at loop $-(q_0+q_0^{-1})$ with its diagram crossing and parity-corrected pivotal structure is ribbon-equivalent to the positive-pivotal rank-one tilting quotient. The diagram algebra is identified with the positive-loop algebra by $U_i\mapsto-e_i$.

[F4] [[thm-temperley-lieb-rank-one-tilting-quotient-fusion-category]]: the positive-pivotal quotient is semisimple, its simple objects are self-dual, and its tensor products have the truncated multiplicities.

[F5] [[thm-su2-root-unity-ribbon-category-is-modular]]: the positive-pivotal quotient at $q_0=e^{\pi i/r}$ has dimensions $d_a^+=\sin((a+1)\pi/r)/\sin(\pi/r)$, twists $\theta_a^+=e^{\pi i a(a+2)/(2r)}$, and invertible sine Hopf-link matrix.

[F6] [[def-modular-ribbon-category-and-hopf-link-data]]: modularity means that the unnormalized Hopf-link matrix of the finite semisimple ribbon category is invertible.

[F7] [[def-semisimplified-temperley-lieb-fusion-category]]: each fixed pair of object labels starts from a finite set of matching diagrams, and the quotient morphism space is a quotient of that finite-dimensional span.

## Proof

**Given:** The root parameter and quotient in the Statement.

1.1 If $r=2$, then $p=4$, $\delta=0$, and the sole proposed retained label is $0$. The boundary projector is $p_1=1_1$ and has zero trace. The tensor ideal it generates contains $1_n=1_1\otimes1_{n-1}$ for every $n\ge1$. Therefore every morphism with a positive source or target is zero in the quotient, because it factors through the corresponding identity. The endomorphism space of object $0$ is $\mathbb C$, and its trace is the identity, so it is non-negligible. The quotient's additive idempotent completion is thus finite-dimensional vector spaces with one self-dual simple $0$. Its fusion is $0\otimes0=0$, its unit twist is $1$, and its Hopf-link matrix is the $1\times1$ matrix $(1)$, which is invertible. [F1, F2, given, cases, algebra]

1.2 Suppose $r\ge3$ and first take the canonical root $A_0=e^{\pi i/(2r)}$, so $q_0=A_0^2=e^{\pi i/r}$ and the BHMV loop is $-(q_0+q_0^{-1})$. By [F3], the diagram quotient with its parity-corrected pivotal structure is ribbon-equivalent to the positive-pivotal quotient of [F4]–[F5]. Thus the quotient has exactly the labels $0,\ldots,r-2$, the displayed truncated fusion multiplicities, self-dual simples, and a nondegenerate Hopf-link matrix for that corrected pivotal structure. [F2, F3, F4, F5, given]

2.1 On the semisimple quotient define $\varepsilon_{p_a}=(-1)^a\,1_{p_a}$. This is a monoidal natural automorphism: each retained summand $p_c$ of $p_a\otimes p_b$ has $c\equiv a+b\pmod2$ by [F4], so $\varepsilon_{p_c}=\varepsilon_{p_a}\varepsilon_{p_b}$; it is dual-compatible because every $p_a$ is self-dual. The planar pivotal structure is $\varepsilon$ times the parity-corrected one of [F3]. Multiplying a pivotal structure $j$ by a dual-compatible monoidal automorphism $\varepsilon$ changes the twist $u^{-1}j$ to $\varepsilon\theta$, preserves balancing by monoidality, and preserves dual compatibility; hence the planar convention is also ribbon. Its dimensions are $d_a=(-1)^a d_a^+$, which equal the BHMV recurrence value $[a+1]$: the sequence $v_j=(-1)^{j-1}[j]_{q_0}$ has $v_0=0$, $v_1=1$ and satisfies $v_{j+1}=-(q_0+q_0^{-1})v_j-v_{j-1}$, so uniqueness of the recurrence gives $v_j=[j]$ for the BHMV loop. Its simple twists are $\theta_a=(-1)^a\theta_a^+=(-1)^aA_0^{a(a+2)}$. Its unnormalized Hopf-link entries are $s_{ab}=\varepsilon_a\varepsilon_b s_{ab}^+$, since the pivotal trace on $p_a\otimes p_b$ is multiplied by $\varepsilon_a\varepsilon_b$. Therefore its Hopf-link matrix is obtained from the invertible matrix in [F5] by multiplying row $a$ and column $b$ by $(-1)^a$ and $(-1)^b$, so it remains invertible. The criterion in [F6] proves modularity. [F1, F3, F4, F5, F6, step 1.2, algebra]

3.1 For any primitive $A$, write $A=A_0^k$ with $\gcd(k,4r)=1$. The automorphism of the cyclotomic field $\mathbb Q(A_0)$ sending $A_0$ to $A$ transports the finite diagram coefficients, the recursively defined projectors, the braiding and twist matrices, and the Hopf-link matrix. By [F7], every fixed Hom space is finite-dimensional, so this transport preserves all ranks and nonzero determinants. The fusion multiplicities are dimensions of these finite Hom spaces and so are unchanged under the field automorphism and extension to $\mathbb C$. Thus the canonical-root result applies to every primitive $2p$-th root with the exact stated BHMV convention; in particular $\theta_a=(-1)^aA^{a(a+2)}$. [F1, F7, step 1.2, step 2.1, algebra] ∎
