## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice and supply good finite full-marked link counts with the actual curvature and neck-count hypotheses. The chosen marked pair necks have the actual inclusion and extraction maps S and T, with their complementary-projection identities. After forgetting z, on diagrams with more than one w mark the actual alpha-boundary-weighted relative edge maps have the finite Hasse square, two-edge and endpoint marked-action homotopies. The adjacent-edge/marked-action dictionary is literal at the boundary-weight level. At a supplied adapted dual neck, retained edges transfer triangularly, an incident leaf edge has unit inclusion-extraction sandwich, and both orders of a supplied short bivalent packet reduce to the bypass up to chain homotopy. A chosen short root packet in one old region has literal unit word. A supplied incident band triple with literal auxiliary inputs dThetaW=(Va+Vb)ThetaZ has, after forgetting z, the ordered identity FThetaZ homotopic to Aout FThetaW plus FThetaW Ain, using the same fixed beta-side marked endpoint. The same chosen triangle has the fixed-beta weighted edge intertwining identity; consequently a supplied actual forward or reverse incident leaf block is unit on the global highest ordinary class at the stated packet ends. These are identities on the specified chosen packets; no admission of arbitrary surface movies, generic graph invariance, or long-path point-motion formula is asserted.

1.1 For this ACTUAL inserted diagram the neck operation identifies generators with two copies of the old generators and gives the finite count matrix $D^+=\begin{pmatrix}D&(U_w+U_{w'})I\\(V_z+V_{z'})I&D\end{pmatrix}.$ Order its local generators as theta_w, theta_z. Identify the adjacent w variables and adjacent z variables; other variables may stay independent provided their old curvature vanishes after the chosen coloring. In particular uniform two-color specialization is allowed. The resulting actual differential is diag(D,D). Define S+ by x to x theta_w, T+ by x to x theta_z, S- by x theta_z to x and x theta_w to zero, and T- by x theta_w to x and x theta_z to zero. These are finite polynomial chain maps, since the actual differential preserves the two summands and is D on each. Their meaning is top-cycle insertion and extraction in the CHOSEN actual stretched diagram, not a formal reassignment of an unknown geometric differential. [given, F1, F2, A1, construct, algebra]

1.2 The two actual local bigons determine the relative grading: theta_w exceeds theta_z by one in the w Maslov grading and is below it by one in the z Maslov grading. Indeed their index is one; the z bigon has w multiplicity zero, whereas the w bigon has w multiplicity one. This uses only relative degrees. An absolute degree or an ambient handle-map normalization is not inferred. [given, F1, F2, A1, construct, algebra]

1.3 On this actual diagram the following identities are strict on every old generator: $S^-S^+=T^-T^+=0,\qquad T^-S^+=S^-T^+=I,\qquad S^+T^-+T^+S^-=I.$ They follow by evaluating the inclusions and projections on the two actual generator copies. Thus no homological rank or inferred nonzero scalar enters these local cancellations. [given, F1, F2, A1, construct, algebra]

1.4 Differentiate the ACTUAL independent-variable count matrix in a NEW variable, before identifying variables. Old D has no new variable, so its derivative contributes zero. The new w derivative is Phi=E12 and the new z derivative is Psi=E21, tensored with the old identity. Thus after coloring they satisfy $\Phi^2=\Psi^2=0,\qquad \Phi\Psi+\Psi\Phi=I,$ and Psi S+=T+, Phi T+=S+, Phi S+=Psi T+=0. These are literal counts with one marked-intersection multiplicity weight; the finite polynomial definition makes their differentiation legitimate. Differentiating after coloring would combine distinct actions and would not establish this statement. [given, F1, F2, A1, construct, algebra]

1.5 Choose the short edge lambda between the two w marks in the insertion disk. It crosses the new alpha circle once and is disjoint from all old alpha curves. Choose its crossing on the alpha arc shared by the new w and new z bigons. In the relative edge count weight each actual strip by the change of its alpha-boundary multiplicity across this crossing, modulo two. The neck proof preserves this boundary chain: gluing adds the boundary chains of its old strip and its matched local pieces, and none of its source-root or target-neck slices changes their integral boundary multiplicities. Its exhaustive five cases can therefore be counted with this extra discrete weight. An old strip contributes zero because it has no boundary on the new alpha circle. Of the two w off-diagonal cases precisely the chosen new w bigon contributes; of the two z cases precisely the chosen new z bigon contributes. The other cases use the complementary alpha arc, so have zero change across this crossing. This is also the boundary-incidence computation on the actual four-marked sphere. Keeping the independent w variables and setting EVERY z variable to one gives the actual relative edge matrix $A_\lambda=\begin{pmatrix}0&U_w I\\I&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad D^+=\begin{pmatrix}D&(U_w+U_{w'})I\\0&D\end{pmatrix}.$ In particular direct multiplication gives $D^+A_\lambda+A_\lambda D^+=(U_w+U_{w'})I,\qquad A_\lambda^2=U_w I.$ On identifying the adjacent w variables with U the edge map is a chain map, A_lambda=Psi+U Phi. Its insertion-extraction composition is STRICTLY $S^-A_\lambda S^+=I.$ The coefficient is one, with no U exponent. This is a proved operation on an arbitrary supplied old LINK factor in the chosen insertion model. It does not identify an arbitrary surface band, ordinary free-basepoint map or a global graph movie with this operation. [given, F1, F2, A1, construct, algebra]

1.6 The local old-factor conditionality in the previously authored four-marked-sphere and disk-spine scalar calculation is removed at this chosen neck. Arbitrary diagram comparison, the ordinary graph/free-basepoint dictionary, all fourteen Cerf identities, empty-end puncturing, Spin-c composition, surface-map Alexander shifts and the actual concordance/slice maps remain obligations. In particular this local cancellation alone supplies no genus-bound map and no stable concordance theorem. [given, F1, F2, A1, construct, algebra]

1.7 This new construction concerns the supplied strong balanced link diagram and its actual finite marked strip counts. It supplies a local dictionary, with no general movie-invariance assertion. It uses the newly authored old-end and colored-neck operations directly. Source context is the full argument of Lemma14.12 and the elementary reductions in Section14.5 of https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.05207, read in their full text; the argument below is local. 1.8 Delete the labels z from the geometric input without changing the surface, compression systems, marked divisors, end tensors or boundary conditions. The underlying ordinary multi-pointed Heegaard problem has precisely the same target and tori. At the level of ACTUAL maps its strip set and regularity problem have not changed. Each strip formerly weighted by the product of all U_w to n_w and all V_z to n_z is now weighted only by its w intersections. Consequently its finite matrix is exactly the specialization at every V_z=1. This is a specialization of the actual finite count problem, not an assertion that arbitrary independently chosen ordinary tensors give identical counts. The artificial protected z-divisors may remain protected; no ordinary count requires them to be varied. The proven curvature specializes to zero, because each w has two adjacent intervals. Thus this gives a genuine ordinary w-based chain complex at these chosen data. [given, F1, F2, A1, construct, algebra]

1.9 In the inserted diagram forgetting z leaves exactly the ordinary free-point stabilization diagram: the new small alpha and beta circles bound cut balls with the two w marks on their opposite bigons. The other two bigons are now unmarked. Their two contributions to the reverse off-diagonal coefficient are both one and cancel. The remaining coefficient is U_w+U_w'. Ordinary free-point insertion and deletion defined in this CHOSEN neck by that top input and its complementary extraction are therefore exactly S+ and S- of the link insertion. This proves the elementary free-point dictionary at this neck. No continuation map between independently chosen ordinary data is inferred. [given, F1, F2, A1, construct, algebra]

1.10 Let w1,z,w2 be consecutive marks on one link component; w1 and w2 may coincide. Represent the actual oriented arc lambda from w1 to w2 as lambda1 followed by lambda2, meeting at z, using its bridge projections to the Heegaard surface. The first projected bridge avoids alpha and the second avoids beta: push each boundary-parallel bridge to the boundary of its supplied cut ball, disjoint from its compression disks, then perturb its projection transverse to the other curves and away from generators and marks except its endpoints. This is an immersed representative of the actual arc, with the same endpoints. It need not be a short local insertion arc. [given, F1, F2, A1, construct, algebra]

1.11 For a strip domain B define a(lambda,B) as its alpha-boundary intersection with this representative, counted with the boundary orientation. Only lambda2 contributes, since lambda1 misses alpha. Across an alpha crossing on lambda2 the region multiplicity changes by precisely that alpha-boundary coefficient; there are no beta crossings on lambda2. Telescoping all its crossings therefore gives $a(\lambda,B)=n_{w2}(B)-n_z(B).$ This identity holds for every actual strip domain, with multiplicity and with self-intersections of the projected arc: the successive differences telescope along the parametrized arc, so an immersion creates no correction. Likewise the beta-boundary weight satisfies b(lambda,B)=n_z(B)-n_w1(B). Signs disappear over characteristic two. [given, F1, F2, A1, construct, algebra]

1.12 Define the actual ordinary edge operators A_lambda and B_lambda by these extra integer weights modulo two on the finite V=1 strip counts. Differentiate the independently marked polynomial differential BEFORE specialization to define Phi_w and Psi_z. Coefficient by coefficient the preceding equalities give the STRICT identities $A_\lambda=U_{w2}\Phi_{w2}+\left.\Psi_z\right|_{V=1},\qquad B_\lambda=U_{w1}\Phi_{w1}+\left.\Psi_z\right|_{V=1}.$ Here every z variable is set to one, and other w variables can remain independent. The factor U_w restores the one power removed by its derivative. The derivative at V=1 weights by n_z exactly, including multiply intersecting strips. Finiteness was proved before specializing, so no sum or differentiation is interchanged with an infinite series. On identifying all w variables with U, the differentiated curvature identities make Phi_w and Psi_z chain maps. Thus these strict identities identify actual chain maps, and in particular Psi_z=A_lambda+U Phi_w2=B_lambda+U Phi_w1. [given, F1, F2, A1, construct, algebra]

1.13 This removes the arbitrary-adjacent-arc identity from the proposed forget-z graph dictionary and proves the ordinary free-point identification at the chosen insertion. It does not prove that an arbitrary embedded H-shaped graph has operator B_lambda+U Phi_w1, that ordinary graph words are invariant under graph Cerf moves, that a decorated surface movie reduces to such a graph word, or that type-z band triangles act as the ordinary diagram comparison identity. [given, F1, F2, A1, construct, algebra]

1.14 Let lambda be an immersed path on the Heegaard surface from a w mark to a DIFFERENT w mark, transverse to alpha and beta, missing generators and other marks. Weight each actual strip by its signed alpha-boundary intersection a(lambda,B) as well as its ordinary w monomial, and multiply it by T to that integer power. This gives a finite Laurent-polynomial matrix D(T); in particular negative weights cause no convergence issue. At T=1 it is the actual ordinary differential D and its first coefficient at T=1+h is the actual edge action A_lambda. [given, F1, F2, A1, construct, algebra]

1.15 The weight is additive under breaking. The index-two end proof can therefore be counted with this extra weight, using precisely the same moduli and collars. For an alpha cut-component domain Ai the weight is delta(end in Ai) minus delta(start in Ai): its entire boundary is alpha, so its crossings telescope along the path. A beta cut-component has alpha-boundary weight zero. Consequently the ACTUAL weighted boundary identity is $D(T)^2=\sum_i U_i\bigl(T^{\delta_{i,\mathrm{end}}-\delta_{i,\mathrm{start}}}+1\bigr)I.$ Every alpha and beta component still has unit degeneration count one; no new analytic normalization has been assumed. Before identifying U variables, the coefficient of h is U_start+U_end. After identifying all U_i with U it vanishes, so A_lambda is a chain map. The h-squared coefficient of the right side is U: T=1+h contributes no h-squared term, while T inverse contributes one. Write H_lambda for the h-squared coefficient of the finite Laurent matrix D(1+h), working modulo h cubed. The h-squared coefficient on the left gives the explicit homotopy $D H_\lambda+H_\lambda D=A_\lambda^2+U I.$ Here the coefficient of T to a in degree two is the integer binomial(a,2) reduced modulo two, also for negative a. Thus H_lambda is a defined finite weighted count. An ordinary second derivative in characteristic two would not prove this relation. [given, F1, F2, A1, construct, algebra]

1.16 For two paths lambda and mu, use two Laurent variables T and S and weight each strip by T to a(lambda,B) times S to a(mu,B). The same boundary proof has alpha monomial T to epsilon_i(lambda) times S to epsilon_i(mu), where epsilon is its signed endpoint incidence, and beta monomial one. Taking the mixed coefficient hk after T=1+h and S=1+k, and then identifying the w variables with U, gives $D H_{\lambda,\mu}+H_{\lambda,\mu}D=A_\lambda A_\mu+A_\mu A_\lambda+U\,c(\lambda,\mu)I.$ The number c is the number of common endpoints modulo two, with multiplicity. No interior intersection term occurs: these weights are intersections with the alpha boundary chain, and the degeneration's entire boundary telescopes to endpoint incidence. The mixed coefficient defines H explicitly, so the relation holds even when the two immersed paths cross. [given, F1, F2, A1, construct, algebra]

1.17 For each w mark differentiate the first-coefficient identity BEFORE identifying its U variable. The ordinary differential has D Phi_w+Phi_w D=0 because its independent-w curvature is already zero after forgetting z. The derivative of D A_lambda+A_lambda D=(U_start+U_end)I is therefore $D(\partial_{U_w}A_\lambda)+(\partial_{U_w}A_\lambda)D=\Phi_w A_\lambda+A_\lambda\Phi_w+\delta_{w\in\partial\lambda}I.$ All operators on the right and the displayed homotopy specialize to the uniform-U ordinary complex. The derivative removes one U power, exactly as the marked action does. Thus this also constructs the endpoint action homotopy rather than asserting it from a graph formalism. [given, F1, F2, A1, construct, algebra]

1.18 Use the DUAL alternating insertion, obtained by literally exchanging the names w and z in the physical new neck count, and then restoring the desired old-variable names. This changes no strips, indices, regularity operators or matching maps: each actual marked intersection is simply relabeled. The dual construction is centered at an old z mark. 1.19 Choose the insertion disk disjoint from all retained edge representatives. This is an adapted choice of the auxiliary free-point data. 1.21 Let lambda run from a retained branch point to the new leaf point. It may run far outside the insertion disk before reaching the leaf.
