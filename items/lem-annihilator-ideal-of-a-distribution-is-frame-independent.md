## Statement

For a constant-rank distribution $\mathcal D$, the local ideal generated by any local frame of $\mathcal D^\circ$ is independent of that frame and consists exactly of forms generated by one-forms vanishing on $\mathcal D$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The manifolds, forms, vector fields, maps, and coordinates explicitly named in the statement.

[F1] The preceding result states that A differential ideal is a graded wedge ideal $I\subseteq\Omega^*(M)$ satisfying $dI\subseteq I$. ([[def-differential-ideal-in-the-algebra-of-forms]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 At a point, complete a local annihilator frame $\theta^1,\ldots,\theta^r$ to a coframe. A form vanishes whenever all inputs lie in $\mathcal D$ exactly when every wedge monomial contains some $\theta^a$. [F1, given]

2.1 Hence those forms constitute the ideal generated by the frame, a description independent of the frame chosen. [step 1.1] ∎
