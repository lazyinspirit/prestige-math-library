## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $\mathfrak g_0$ be a finite-dimensional real
semisimple Lie algebra with Cartan involution $\theta$ and Cartan decomposition
$\mathfrak g_0=\mathfrak k_0\oplus\mathfrak p_0$, let $G$ be a connected
semisimple Lie group with finite center and Lie algebra $\mathfrak g_0$, let
$\Theta$ be a global Cartan involution of $G$ with $d\Theta_e=\theta$, and put
$K=G^\Theta$, a closed compact subgroup of $G$ with Lie algebra
$\mathfrak k_0$
([[thm-global-cartan-decomposition-for-a-connected-finite-center-semisimple-lie-group]]);
let $\mathfrak a\subseteq\mathfrak p_0$ be a
maximal abelian subspace, let $\Sigma=\Sigma(\mathfrak g_0,\mathfrak a)\subseteq
\mathfrak a^*$ be the restricted-root system
([[def-restricted-root-and-restricted-root-space]]), and let
$W(\mathfrak g_0,\mathfrak a)=N_K(\mathfrak a)/Z_K(\mathfrak a)$ be the
restricted Weyl group acting on $\mathfrak a^*$ by the dual action
([[def-restricted-weyl-group]]). Let $(\,\cdot\,,\,\cdot\,)$ be the restriction
of $B_\theta$ to $\mathfrak a$, let $\langle\lambda,\mu\rangle=(H_\lambda,H_\mu)$
for the dual vectors $H_\lambda$ of $\lambda,\mu\in\mathfrak a^*$, and let
$$s_\lambda(\mu)=\mu-2\langle\mu,\lambda\rangle|\lambda|^{-2}\lambda$$
be the orthogonal reflection of $\mathfrak a^*$ associated with
$\lambda\in\Sigma$; write
$$W(\Sigma)=\langle s_\lambda:\lambda\in\Sigma\rangle\le O(\mathfrak a^*)$$
for the subgroup generated by these reflections. Then $W(\mathfrak g_0,\mathfrak a)$
coincides with $W(\Sigma)$ under these identifications:
$$W(\mathfrak g_0,\mathfrak a)=W(\Sigma).$$
In particular the restricted Weyl group is generated by the orthogonal
reflections in the restricted-root hyperplanes $\lambda^{\perp}$ and is finite.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The Axiom of Choice; a real semisimple $\mathfrak g_0$ with Cartan involution $\theta$, Cartan decomposition $\mathfrak g_0=\mathfrak k_0\oplus\mathfrak p_0$, a connected semisimple Lie group $G$ with finite center, a global Cartan involution $\Theta$ with $d\Theta_e=\theta$, the subgroup $K=G^\Theta$, maximal abelian $\mathfrak a\subseteq\mathfrak p_0$, restricted-root system $\Sigma$, and $W(\mathfrak g_0,\mathfrak a)=N_K(\mathfrak a)/Z_K(\mathfrak a)$.

[A1] The Axiom of Choice is [[def-axiom-of-choice]]; it enters through the compact-group structure theory of [L6] and through the restricted-root theory of [L1].

[L1] $\Sigma$ is finite, spans $\mathfrak a^*$, satisfies $s_\lambda(\Sigma)=\Sigma$ and $2\langle\mu,\lambda\rangle|\lambda|^{-2}\in\mathbb Z$ for all $\mu,\lambda\in\Sigma$, and for every $\lambda\in\Sigma$ the reflection $s_\lambda$ of $\mathfrak a^*$ is realised by an element of $N_K(\mathfrak a)$ ([[prop-restricted-root-systems-may-be-nonreduced]], [[def-restricted-root-and-restricted-root-space]]).

[L2] $\mathfrak g_0=\mathfrak g_0^0\oplus\bigoplus_{\lambda\in\Sigma}\mathfrak g_0^\lambda$ with $\mathfrak g_0^0=Z_{\mathfrak g_0}(\mathfrak a)=\mathfrak a\oplus\mathfrak m$, $\mathfrak m=Z_{\mathfrak k_0}(\mathfrak a)$, $[\mathfrak g_0^\lambda,\mathfrak g_0^\mu]\subseteq\mathfrak g_0^{\lambda+\mu}$, $\theta\mathfrak g_0^\lambda=\mathfrak g_0^{-\lambda}$, and $Z_{\mathfrak g_0}(H)=\mathfrak g_0^0$ for every $H\in\mathfrak a$ with $\lambda(H)\ne0$ for all $\lambda\in\Sigma$ ([[thm-restricted-root-space-decomposition]]); $K$ is closed in $G$ with Lie algebra $\mathfrak k_0$, $(k,X)\mapsto k\exp X$ is a diffeomorphism $K\times\mathfrak p_0\to G$, and $B_\theta(X,Y)=-B(X,\theta Y)$ is a positive definite inner product ([[prop-bracket-relations-and-killing-signs-in-a-cartan-decomposition]], [[thm-global-cartan-decomposition-for-a-connected-finite-center-semisimple-lie-group]]); since $\operatorname{Ad}(\Theta(g))=\theta\operatorname{Ad}(g)\theta^{-1}$ for all $g\in G$, every $k\in K=G^\Theta$ satisfies $\operatorname{Ad}(k)\theta=\theta\operatorname{Ad}(k)$, so $\operatorname{Ad}(K)$ preserves the $\pm1$-eigenspaces $\mathfrak k_0,\mathfrak p_0$ of $\theta$, and preserves $B$ and $B_\theta$ ([[prop-bracket-relations-and-killing-signs-in-a-cartan-decomposition]]).

[L3] For a reduced crystallographic root system $\Phi$ the Weyl group $W(\Phi)$ acts simply transitively on the set of open Weyl chambers, which are the connected components of the complement of the hyperplanes $\alpha^{\perp}$, $\alpha\in\Phi$; in particular it acts transitively on them and is finite and faithful on $\Phi$ ([[thm-the-weyl-group-acts-simply-transitively-on-weyl-chambers]], [[prop-the-weyl-group-is-finite-and-acts-faithfully-on-the-root-system]]).

[L4] If $\delta=\tfrac12\sum_{\alpha\in\Phi^+}\alpha$ is the Weyl vector of a reduced crystallographic root system with base $\Delta=\{\alpha_1,\dots,\alpha_r\}$, then $\langle\delta,\alpha_i^\vee\rangle=1$ and $\delta=\sum_i\omega_i$ for the fundamental weights, so $\langle\delta,\alpha\rangle>0$ for every positive root $\alpha$ ([[prop-weyl-vector-is-the-sum-of-fundamental-weights]]).

[L5] In a compact connected Lie group the centralizer of every torus is connected ([[thm-compact-group-weyl-group-is-finite]]); a closed subgroup of a finite-dimensional real Lie group is an embedded Lie subgroup whose Lie algebra is $\{X:\exp(tX)\in H$ for all $t\}$, and connected subgroups with equal Lie algebras coincide ([[thm-cartans-closed-subgroup-theorem]], [[thm-lie-subgroup-lie-subalgebra-correspondence]]).

[L6] For a semisimple Lie algebra $\mathfrak h$ every derivation is inner, $\operatorname{Der}(\mathfrak h)=\operatorname{ad}(\mathfrak h)$, and $\operatorname{Aut}(\mathfrak h)$ is a closed Lie subgroup of $\operatorname{GL}(\mathfrak h)$ with Lie algebra $\operatorname{Der}(\mathfrak h)$ ([[thm-every-derivation-of-a-semisimple-lie-algebra-is-inner]], [[cor-the-lie-algebra-of-the-automorphism-group-of-a-semisimple-lie-algebra]]).

[L7] A real finite-dimensional Lie algebra is semisimple if and only if its complexification is ([[prop-complexification-preserves-semisimplicity]]), and $\operatorname{ad}\colon\mathfrak u\to\operatorname{gl}(\mathfrak u)$ is injective when $\mathfrak u$ is semisimple, because $\mathfrak u$ has trivial center.

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 If $\mathfrak a=0$ then $\Sigma=\emptyset$, because every restricted root is a nonzero element of $\mathfrak a^*$ by [[def-restricted-root-and-restricted-root-space]] and $\mathfrak a^*=0$; moreover $N_K(\mathfrak a)=\{k\in K:\operatorname{Ad}(k)\mathfrak a=\mathfrak a\}=K=Z_K(\mathfrak a)$, so $W(\mathfrak g_0,\mathfrak a)$ and $W(\Sigma)=\langle\emptyset\rangle$ are both the trivial group and the theorem holds; assume henceforth that $\mathfrak a\ne0$. Setting and notation: $(\,\cdot\,,\,\cdot\,)$ is a positive definite inner product on $\mathfrak a$ by [L2], the dual vectors $H_\lambda$ and the inner product $\langle\,\cdot\,,\,\cdot\,\rangle$ on $\mathfrak a^*$ are as in the statement, and the reflections $s_\lambda$ are orthogonal, fix $\lambda^{\perp}$ pointwise and negate $\lambda$; $\Sigma$ is finite, nonempty and spans $\mathfrak a^*$ and satisfies $s_\lambda(\Sigma)=\Sigma$ and the integrality of the Cartan integers by [L1]; put $\Sigma_s=\{\alpha\in\Sigma:\alpha/2\notin\Sigma\}$ and $\Psi=\{\alpha\in\Sigma_s:2\alpha\in\Sigma\}$; then the multiple-set argument with the integrality of [L1] gives $\{c>0:c\lambda\in\Sigma\}=\{a\}$ or $\{a,2a\}$ with $a\in\{1,\tfrac12\}$ for every $\lambda\in\Sigma$, hence $\Sigma=\Sigma_s\sqcup2\Psi$, and for $\beta\in\Sigma_s$ the positive multiples of $\beta$ in $\Sigma$ are $\beta$ and possibly $2\beta$; consequently $\Sigma_s$ is a reduced crystallographic root system in $\mathfrak a^*$ with $W(\Sigma_s)=W(\Sigma)$, and the hyperplanes $\lambda^{\perp}$ for $\lambda\in\Sigma$ are exactly the hyperplanes $\alpha^{\perp}$ for $\alpha\in\Sigma_s$; also choose a chamber $C$ of the arrangement (nonempty by [L3]), let $\Sigma^+=\{\lambda\in\Sigma:\lambda>0$ on $C\}$ and $\Sigma_s^+=\Sigma_s\cap\Sigma^+$, put $\delta=\tfrac12\sum_{\alpha\in\Sigma_s^+}\alpha$ and let $H_\delta\in\mathfrak a$ be its dual vector, so that $\lambda(H_\delta)=\langle\lambda,\delta\rangle$ for all $\lambda\in\mathfrak a^*$. [L1, L2, L3, algebra]

1.2 The compact form: put $\mathfrak u=\mathfrak k_0\oplus i\mathfrak p_0\subseteq\mathfrak g_0^{\mathbb C}$; it is a real Lie subalgebra with $\mathfrak u^{\mathbb C}=\mathfrak g_0^{\mathbb C}$ and $\dim_{\mathbb R}\mathfrak u=\dim_{\mathbb C}\mathfrak g_0^{\mathbb C}$, hence a real form; its Killing form is the restriction of the Killing form of $\mathfrak g_0^{\mathbb C}$ and is negative definite, since it equals the Killing form of $\mathfrak g_0$ on $\mathfrak k_0$, where the latter is negative definite, and equals its negative on $i\mathfrak p_0$, where the Killing form of $\mathfrak g_0$ is positive definite by [L2], with the two summands orthogonal; hence $\mathfrak u$ is a compact real form, and $\mathfrak u$ is semisimple because $\mathfrak u^{\mathbb C}=\mathfrak g_0^{\mathbb C}$ is semisimple by [L7]. [L2, L7, algebra]

1.3 $W(\Sigma)\subseteq W(\mathfrak g_0,\mathfrak a)$: by [L1] each reflection $s_\lambda$ is realised by some $k_\lambda\in N_K(\mathfrak a)$, whose class in $W(\mathfrak g_0,\mathfrak a)$ acts on $\mathfrak a^*$ by $s_\lambda$; the classes of the $k_\lambda$ therefore generate a subgroup of $W(\mathfrak g_0,\mathfrak a)$ that is identified with $W(\Sigma)=\langle s_\lambda:\lambda\in\Sigma\rangle$. [L1]

1.4 $W(\mathfrak g_0,\mathfrak a)$ permutes the restricted roots and the chambers: if $k\in N_K(\mathfrak a)$, $\mu\in\Sigma$ and $0\ne X\in\mathfrak g_0^\mu$, then for $H\in\mathfrak a$ one has $[H,\operatorname{Ad}(k)X]=\operatorname{Ad}(k)[\operatorname{Ad}(k)^{-1}H,X]=(\lambda_k(\mu))(H)\operatorname{Ad}(k)X$ with $\lambda_k(\mu)=\mu\circ\operatorname{Ad}(k)^{-1}$, so $\operatorname{Ad}(k)$ carries $\mathfrak g_0^\mu$ onto $\mathfrak g_0^{\lambda_k(\mu)}$ and $\lambda_k(\mu)\in\Sigma$; hence the dual action of $W(\mathfrak g_0,\mathfrak a)$ permutes $\Sigma$, and therefore permutes the hyperplanes $\lambda^{\perp}$ and the chambers of their complement. [L2, algebra]

2.1 Regularity of $H_\delta$: by [L4] applied to the reduced system $\Sigma_s$ with its positive system $\Sigma_s^+$ one has $\langle\delta,\alpha\rangle=\sum_i n_i\langle\alpha_i,\omega_i\rangle=\sum_i n_i(\alpha_i,\alpha_i)/2$ for $\alpha=\sum_i n_i\alpha_i\in\Sigma_s^+$ over the simple roots, so $\langle\delta,\alpha\rangle>0$ for $\alpha\in\Sigma_s^+$ and $\langle\delta,\alpha\rangle<0$ for $\alpha\in\Sigma_s^-$; hence $\langle\delta,\lambda\rangle\ne0$ for every $\lambda\in\Sigma$, because every $\lambda$ is $\alpha$ or $2\alpha$ with $\alpha\in\Sigma_s\setminus\{0\}$ by step 1.1, and therefore $\lambda(H_\delta)\ne0$ for every $\lambda\in\Sigma$; also $\Sigma\subseteq\operatorname{span}\Sigma_s$ by step 1.1. [L4, step 1.1, algebra]

2.2 The group $\tilde U=\operatorname{Aut}(\mathfrak u)^0$ is a compact connected Lie group with Lie algebra $\operatorname{Der}(\mathfrak u)=\operatorname{ad}(\mathfrak u)$: the automorphism group is closed in $\operatorname{GL}(\mathfrak u)$ because the conditions $\varphi[x,y]=[\varphi x,\varphi y]$ are polynomial, it preserves the Killing form of $\mathfrak u$, so it lies in the orthogonal group of that negative definite form and is compact, and its identity component is therefore a compact connected Lie group; by [L6] its Lie algebra is $\operatorname{Der}(\mathfrak u)=\operatorname{ad}(\mathfrak u)$, with $\operatorname{ad}$ injective by [L7]. [L6, L7, step 1.2, algebra]

2.3 Transitivity on chambers: by step 1.1 the chambers of the arrangement of $\Sigma$ are the open Weyl chambers of the reduced crystallographic root system $\Sigma_s$, and $W(\Sigma)=W(\Sigma_s)$, so [L3] shows that $W(\Sigma)$ acts simply transitively, in particular transitively, on these chambers. [L3, step 1.1]

3.1 For every $k\in K$ the restriction to $\mathfrak u$ of the complex-linear extension of $\operatorname{Ad}(k)$ lies in $\tilde U$: since $K$ is generated by exponentials and $\operatorname{Ad}(k)$ preserves $\mathfrak k_0$ and $\mathfrak p_0$ by [L2], it preserves $\mathfrak u=\mathfrak k_0\oplus i\mathfrak p_0$, so restriction gives an automorphism of $\mathfrak u$, and writing $k=\exp X_1\cdots\exp X_m$ with $X_j\in\mathfrak k_0$ exhibits the restriction as $\exp(\operatorname{ad}_{\mathfrak u}X_1)\cdots\exp(\operatorname{ad}_{\mathfrak u}X_m)$, a product of exponentials of inner derivations, hence an element of the identity component $\tilde U$. [L2, step 2.2, algebra]

3.2 Reduction to a positive-system stabilizer: let $v\in W(\mathfrak g_0,\mathfrak a)$ be represented by $k\in N_K(\mathfrak a)$ and let $C$ be the chamber of step 1.1 with positive system $\Sigma^+$; by steps 2.3 and 1.4 the set $v(C)$ is a chamber, so there is $u\in W(\Sigma)\subseteq W(\mathfrak g_0,\mathfrak a)$ with $u(v(C))=C$; representing $u$ by some $k'\in N_K(\mathfrak a)$ by [L1] and putting $k''=k'k$, one has $\operatorname{Ad}(k'')(\Sigma^+)=\Sigma^+$; since $\operatorname{Ad}(k'')$ permutes $\Sigma_s^+=\Sigma_s\cap\Sigma^+$, it fixes $\delta=\tfrac12\sum_{\alpha\in\Sigma_s^+}\alpha$, and since by [L2] it preserves the inner product on $\mathfrak a$ it also fixes the dual vector $H_\delta$. [L1, L2, step 1.1, step 2.3, step 1.4]

4.1 The element $\rho\in\tilde U$ given by the restriction to $\mathfrak u$ of the complex-linear extension of $\operatorname{Ad}(k'')$ lies in $\tilde U$ by step 3.1 and centralizes the torus $S=\overline{\{\exp(t\cdot i\operatorname{ad}_{\mathfrak u}H_\delta):t\in\mathbb R\}}\subseteq\tilde U$: indeed $\operatorname{ad}_{\mathfrak u}(iH_\delta)\in\operatorname{ad}(\mathfrak u)=\operatorname{Lie}\tilde U$ by step 2.2, so the closure of the one-parameter subgroup is a compact connected abelian, hence torus, subgroup of $\tilde U$, and $\rho\operatorname{ad}_{\mathfrak u}(iH_\delta)\rho^{-1}=\operatorname{ad}_{\mathfrak u}(\rho(iH_\delta))=\operatorname{ad}_{\mathfrak u}(iH_\delta)$ because $\rho$ fixes $H_\delta$ by step 3.2 and the restriction to $\mathfrak u$ of the complex-linear extension of $\operatorname{Ad}(k'')$ sends $iH_\delta$ to $iH_\delta$; exponentiating gives $\rho\in Z_{\tilde U}(S)$. [step 2.2, step 3.1, step 3.2]

5.1 The Lie algebra of $Z_{\tilde U}(S)$ is $\operatorname{ad}_{\mathfrak u}(\mathfrak z_{\mathfrak u}(\mathfrak s'))$ where $\mathfrak s'=\operatorname{ad}_{\mathfrak u}^{-1}(\operatorname{Lie}S)$: by [L5] the closed subgroup $Z_{\tilde U}(S)$ is connected (the centralizer of the torus $S$ in the compact connected group $\tilde U$), and its Lie algebra is $\{Y\in\operatorname{ad}(\mathfrak u):[Y,\operatorname{Lie}S]=0\}$, which is $\operatorname{ad}_{\mathfrak u}(\{X\in\mathfrak u:[X,\mathfrak s']=0\})$ because $[Y,\operatorname{ad}_{\mathfrak u}X]=\operatorname{ad}_{\mathfrak u}[Y,X]$ and $\operatorname{ad}_{\mathfrak u}$ is injective; here $iH_\delta\in\mathfrak s'$ by step 4.1; and $\mathfrak z_{\mathfrak u}(\mathfrak s')\subseteq\mathfrak z_{\mathfrak u}(iH_\delta)=\mathfrak u\cap Z_{\mathfrak g_0^{\mathbb C}}(H_\delta)=\mathfrak u\cap(\mathfrak a\oplus\mathfrak m)^{\mathbb C}=\mathfrak m\oplus i\mathfrak a$, using $Z_{\mathfrak g_0}(H_\delta)=\mathfrak g_0^0$ of [L2] (legitimate by the regularity proved in step 2.1) and the decomposition $\mathfrak g_0^0=\mathfrak a\oplus\mathfrak m$. [L2, L5, step 2.1, step 4.1, algebra]

6.1 Consequently $W(\mathfrak g_0,\mathfrak a)\subseteq W(\Sigma)$: every element of $\mathfrak m\oplus i\mathfrak a$ commutes with $i\mathfrak a$, because $\mathfrak m=Z_{\mathfrak k_0}(\mathfrak a)$ and $\mathfrak a$ is abelian, so by step 5.1 every element of the connected group $Z_{\tilde U}(S)$, which is generated by the exponentials of its Lie algebra, fixes $i\mathfrak a$ pointwise; hence $\rho$ fixes $i\mathfrak a$ pointwise by step 4.1, and therefore $\operatorname{Ad}(k'')$ fixes $\mathfrak a$ pointwise, that is $k''\in Z_K(\mathfrak a)$; since $\operatorname{Ad}(k'')=\operatorname{Ad}(k')\operatorname{Ad}(k)$, the class $v$ of $k$ satisfies $v=\operatorname{Ad}(k)|_{\mathfrak a}=\operatorname{Ad}(k')^{-1}|_{\mathfrak a}\in W(\Sigma)$. [step 4.1, step 5.1, algebra]

7.1 Combining steps 1.3 and 6.1 gives $W(\mathfrak g_0,\mathfrak a)=W(\Sigma)$; since $W(\Sigma)=W(\Sigma_s)$ is the Weyl group of the reduced crystallographic root system $\Sigma_s$ by step 1.1, it is finite by [L3], and by construction it is generated by the orthogonal reflections $s_\lambda$ in the restricted-root hyperplanes; the Axiom of Choice was used through the compact-group connectedness theorem of [L5] and through the restricted-root theory of [L1]. [A1, L1, L3, L5, step 1.1, step 1.3, step 6.1] ∎
