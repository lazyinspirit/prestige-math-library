## Example

Let $i:H\hookrightarrow G$ be a subgroup inclusion, and view $H$ and $G$ as
one-object categories ([[prop-monoids-and-groups-as-one-object-categories]]).
A functor $X:H\to\mathbf{Set}$ is then a permutation representation of $H$.

The left Kan extension of $X$ along $i$ is the induced $G$-set
$G\times_HX$, and the right Kan extension is the coinduced $G$-set
$\operatorname{Map}_H(G,X)$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A subgroup inclusion $i:H\hookrightarrow G$ and an $H$-set $X$, regarded as a functor $X:H\to\mathbf{Set}$.

[F1] A group may be regarded as a one-object category, and a subgroup inclusion is then a functor between such categories ([[prop-monoids-and-groups-as-one-object-categories]], [[def-functor-and-contravariant-functor]]).

[L1] The comma-category colimit and limit formulae compute left and right Kan extensions ([[thm-the-comma-category-limit-and-colimit-formulae-compute-kan-extensions]]).

[F2] Every small Set-valued diagram has a colimit given by the quotient of its tagged union by the relations generated by its structure maps, and a limit given by its set of compatible tuples ([[thm-set-has-all-small-colimits]], [[thm-set-has-all-small-limits]]).

## Verification

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 In the one-object setting, an object of $(i\downarrow *)$ is just an element $g\in G$. A morphism from $g$ to $g'$ is an element $h\in H$ with $g=g'i(h)$ by the comma-category equation, equivalently $g'=g\,i(h^{-1})$. So the indexing category is the action groupoid for the right $H$-action on $G$, and the induced diagram on $\mathbf{Set}$ sends the arrow $g\to g\,i(k)$ to the map $x\mapsto k^{-1}x$ on $X$. By [F2], its colimit is the quotient of $G\times X$ by $(g,x)\sim(g\,i(k),k^{-1}x)$, equivalently by $(g\,i(h),x)\sim(g,hx)$, namely $G\times_HX$. Therefore [L1] identifies the induced representation with the left Kan extension. [F1, F2, L1]

2.1 Dually, an object of $(*\downarrow i)$ is again an element of $G$, and a cone to a set $Y$ is exactly a family of maps indexed by $G$ that is equivariant for the $H$-action. By [F2], the limit is therefore the set of $H$-equivariant maps $G\to X$, written $\operatorname{Map}_H(G,X)$. Hence [L1] identifies the coinduced representation with the right Kan extension. [F1, F2, L1] ∎
