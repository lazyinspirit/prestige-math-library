## Statement

Assume AC, Countable Choice and DC. Let $H/F$ be a finite abelian extension of number fields with $F$ totally real and $H$ a CM field. Write $G=\operatorname{Gal}(H/F)$ and let $j\in G$ be complex conjugation. Let $\Sigma$ be a finite set of places of $F$ containing all infinite places, and $\Sigma'$ a finite set of finite places disjoint from $\Sigma$. Write $\Sigma_H$ for all places of $H$ above $\Sigma$ and $\Sigma'_H$ for the finite primes of $H$ above $\Sigma'$. Let $Y_{H,\Sigma}$ be the free abelian group on $\Sigma_H$, with its natural $G$-action, and let $\operatorname{Cl}_{\Sigma'}(H)$ be the ray class group for the finite modulus $\prod_{w\in\Sigma'_H}w$, with no infinite component.

For a $G$-module $M$, write $M^-:=e^-(M\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb Z[1/2])$ for $e^-=(1-j)/2$, and give the finite ray-class dual $\operatorname{Cl}_{\Sigma'}(H)^\vee=\operatorname{Hom}(\operatorname{Cl}_{\Sigma'}(H),\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z)$ the contragredient action. Then there is a canonical exact sequence of $\mathbb Z[1/2][G]e^-$-modules
$$0\longrightarrow Y_{H,\Sigma}^-\longrightarrow\operatorname{Sel}_{\Sigma'}^{\Sigma}(H)^-\longrightarrow\operatorname{Cl}_{\Sigma'}(H)^{\vee,-}\longrightarrow0.$$
The first map is induced by the finite-place valuations. The result does not require a root-of-unity smoothing hypothesis and does not assert any Fitting-ideal equality.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The CM extension, central complex conjugation, disjoint finite place sets, valuation-defined Selmer module and explicit choices in the Statement.

[A1] AC, Countable Choice and DC are assumed. [[def-axiom-of-choice]] [[def-countable-choice]] [[def-dependent-choice]]

[F1] The BKS Selmer module is the quotient of $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb Z}(H^\times_{\Sigma'},\mathbb Z)$ by the product of the valuation maps outside $\Sigma_H\cup\Sigma'_H$, with contragredient action. [[def-burns-kurihara-sano-selmer-module]]

[F2] The ray class group is the quotient of fractional ideals prime to the modulus by principal ideals generated by elements congruent to $1$ at the modulus, and it is finite. [[def-ray-class-group]] [[thm-ray-class-group-finite]]

[F3] Every subgroup of a free abelian group is free under AC. [[lem-subgroups-of-free-abelian-groups-are-free]]

[F4] An algebraic integer all of whose complex conjugates have absolute value at most one is a root of unity. [[thm-kronecker-root-of-unity-criterion]]

## Proof

**Proof technique:** dualize the ray-ideal valuation sequence, then remove the CM unit contribution in the minus part.

1.1 Let $U=H^\times_{\Sigma'}$, let $E=U\cap\mathcal O_H^\times$, let $D=\bigoplus_{w\notin\Sigma'_H}\mathbb Z[w]$, and let $B\subseteq D$ be the image of the valuation map on $U$. By [F2], its kernel is $E$ and $D/B$ is $C=\operatorname{Cl}_{\Sigma'}(H)$, which is finite. By [F3] the subgroup $B$ is free, so $0\to E\to U\to B\to0$ splits as a sequence of abelian groups. The splitting need not be $G$-equivariant, but it shows that restriction $\operatorname{Hom}(U,\mathbb Z)\to\operatorname{Hom}(E,\mathbb Z)$ is surjective; its kernel is canonically $\operatorname{Hom}(B,\mathbb Z)$. All displayed groups and natural maps carry the stated $G$-actions. [A1, F2, F3, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 The finite-index inclusion $B\subset D$ makes restriction $\operatorname{Hom}(D,\mathbb Z)\to\operatorname{Hom}(B,\mathbb Z)$ injective, with canonical cokernel $C^\vee$. Explicitly, choose an integer $n$ killing $C$ and extend $\varphi:B\to\mathbb Z$ uniquely to $D\to\mathbb Q$ by $d\mapsto\varphi(nd)/n$; reduction modulo $\mathbb Z$ gives a homomorphism $C\to\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z$. Its kernel consists exactly of restrictions of integer-valued homomorphisms on $D$. Conversely, lift the values of any character $C\to\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z$ on the free basis of $D$ to rationals; its restriction to $B$ is integral and maps back to the given character. This construction is independent of the chosen annihilator and is $G$-equivariant. [A1, step 1.1, choose, construct, algebra]

3.1 Under [F1], the valuation product for $\Sigma=S_\infty$ is $\operatorname{Hom}(D,\mathbb Z)=\prod_{w\notin\Sigma'_H}\mathbb Z$. Steps 1.1–2.1 give a canonical $G$-equivariant exact sequence $0\to C^\vee\to\operatorname{Sel}_{\Sigma'}^{S_\infty}(H)\to\operatorname{Hom}(E,\mathbb Z)\to0$. For $u\in E$, the unit $u/j(u)$ is an algebraic integer and each complex embedding has absolute value one because $H$ is CM. By [F4] it is a root of unity, so every homomorphism $E\to\mathbb Z$ takes equal values on $u$ and $j(u)$. Thus $j$ acts trivially on $\operatorname{Hom}(E,\mathbb Z)$. Applying the exact idempotent functor $e^-$ after inverting $2$ gives the canonical isomorphism $\operatorname{Sel}_{\Sigma'}^{S_\infty}(H)^-\cong C^{\vee,-}$. [F1, F4, step 1.1, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 The full valuation product $\operatorname{Hom}(D,\mathbb Z)$ injects into $\operatorname{Hom}(U,\mathbb Z)$: a homomorphism vanishing on $B$ vanishes on $D$ because $D/B$ is finite and $\mathbb Z$ has no torsion. Removing the finitely many finite primes above $\Sigma$ from that product therefore gives a canonical exact sequence $0\to\bigoplus_{w\in\Sigma_H,\,w\text{ finite}}\mathbb Z[w]\to\operatorname{Sel}_{\Sigma'}^{\Sigma}(H)\to\operatorname{Sel}_{\Sigma'}^{S_\infty}(H)\to0$. Complex conjugation fixes each archimedean place of the CM field $H$, so its free archimedean permutation module has zero minus part. Applying $e^-$ to the sequence and substituting step 3.1 gives the displayed exact sequence. Empty $\Sigma'$ and the zero ray-class group are included. [F1, step 1.1, step 3.1, algebra] ∎
