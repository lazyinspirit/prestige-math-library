The library proves elementary family results and gives a local proof of
[[thm-generalized-fitting-subgroup-contains-its-centralizer]]. It does not
prove the classification of finite simple groups, recognition and order
formulae for all its families, their character tables, or the constructions
of all sporadic groups.
