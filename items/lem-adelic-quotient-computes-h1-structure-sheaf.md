## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice as inherited from the coherent-cohomology
suppliers. Let $X$ be a smooth curve over the field $k$ that is proper over
$k$; thus $X$ is a geometrically integral, connected, separated $k$-scheme of
finite type and dimension one. Write $K=k(X)$ for its function field and let
$p$ range over the closed points of $X$. For each closed point let
$A_p:=\widehat{\mathcal O_{X,p}}$ be the completion of the local ring
$\mathcal O_{X,p}$, with fraction field $K_p$, and put
$$V_X:=\Bigl\{(f_p)\in\textstyle\prod_p K_p \;:\; f_p\in A_p \text{ for all but finitely many } p\Bigr\},\qquad A_X:=\textstyle\prod_p A_p ,$$
embedding $K$ diagonally into $V_X$. Then:

1. $K$ is a $k$-subspace of $V_X$, and $V_X$ is a $K$-module under
   componentwise multiplication;
2. for every $f\in K$ the quotient $(fA_X+A_X)/A_X$ is a finite-dimensional
   $k$-vector space; in the notation $B<C$ for "$(B+C)/C$ is
   finite-dimensional over $k$", this says $fA_X<A_X$ for every $f\in K$, so
   $K$ acts on $V_X$ through endomorphisms $\theta$ with $\theta A_X<A_X$;
   moreover, for all $f,g\in K$ and every finite set $S$ of closed points
   outside which $f$, $g$ and $g^{-1}$ are all integral (the condition on
   $g^{-1}$ being void when $g=0$), the tail subspace
   $A_T:=\prod_{p\in T}A_p$ over $T:=X\setminus S$ satisfies
   $fA_T+fgA_T+fg^{-1}A_T\subseteq A_T$;
3. $K\cap A_X$ is the space of global sections $H^0(X,\mathcal O_X)$;
4. the cokernel $V_X/(K+A_X)$ is canonically isomorphic, as a $k$-vector
   space, to $H^1(X,\mathcal O_X)$, and is finite-dimensional;
5. if $X$ is geometrically integral (as every smooth curve over $k$ is) then
   $K\cap A_X=k$.

The finite-support condition in the definition of $V_X$ is exactly what makes
the diagonal image of $K$ lie in $V_X$ (part 1); parts 2, 3 and 4 are the
hypotheses and the conclusion of the adelic presentation of $H^1$ used for the
residue theorem.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** a field $k$, a smooth curve $X$ over $k$ that is proper over $k$,
its function field $K=k(X)$, its closed points $p$, the completions
$A_p=\widehat{\mathcal O_{X,p}}$ with fraction fields $K_p$, and the Axiom of
Choice.

[F1] $X$ is nonempty, geometrically integral, separated and of finite type
over $k$, with chain dimension one; it has a generic point $\eta$ with
$\mathcal O_{X,\eta}=K=k(X)$, and for every nonempty affine open
$U=\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq X$ the ring $A$ is a Noetherian domain with
fraction field $K$ and $\mathcal O_{X,p}=A_p$ for the closed points $p\in U$;
consequently $X$ is quasi-compact and locally Noetherian, hence its underlying
space is Noetherian and every open subset of $X$ is quasi-compact
([[def-algebraic-curve-over-field]], [[def-function-field-variety]],
[[lem-integral-finite-type-scheme-function-field]], [[def-field-of-fractions]],
[[def-zero-divisor-and-integral-domain]],
[[thm-field-of-fractions-is-a-field-and-the-domain-embeds]],
[[thm-stalk-structure-sheaf-prime-localization]]).

[F2] For a closed point $p$ the local ring $\mathcal O_{X,p}$ is a discrete
valuation ring with fraction field $K$ and maximal ideal generated by a
uniformizer $t_p$; every nonzero $f\in K$ has a well-defined order
$\operatorname{ord}_p(f)\in\mathbb Z$, and $f\in\mathcal O_{X,p}$ exactly when
$\operatorname{ord}_p(f)\ge0$ ([[thm-local-ring-smooth-curve-dvr]],
[[def-order-codimension-one-rational-function]],
[[def-localisation-at-a-prime-ideal]]).

[F3] For $f\in K^\times$ the set of closed points with
$\operatorname{ord}_p(f)<0$ is finite: on an affine chart $U=\operatorname{Spec}A$
write $f=a/b$ with $a,b\in A$, $b\ne0$, so that every pole of $f$ in $U$ is a
maximal ideal of $A$ containing $b$; since $A$ is a one-dimensional Noetherian
domain, $A/(b)$ is zero-dimensional and Noetherian, hence has only finitely
many maximal ideals, and a finite affine cover of $X$ bounds all poles
([[def-algebraic-curve-over-field]],
[[cor-closed-points-of-spectrum-are-maximal-ideals]],
[[thm-proper-ideal-contained-in-maximal-ideal]],
[[def-localisation-at-a-prime-ideal]]).

[F4] $A_p$ is a local ring whose maximal ideal is generated by the image of
$t_p$; the completion map $\mathcal O_{X,p}\to A_p$ is injective,
$K_p=\operatorname{Frac}(A_p)$ contains $K=\operatorname{Frac}(\mathcal O_{X,p})$
as a subfield with $K\cap A_p=\mathcal O_{X,p}$, the equality
$K_p=A_p[t_p^{-1}]$ holds, and $fA_p=t_p^{\operatorname{ord}_p(f)}A_p$ for
$f\in K^\times$; for $f=0$ one has $fA_p=0$ ([[def-adic-completion-of-a-module]],
[[thm-local-ring-smooth-curve-dvr]]).

[F5] The residue field $\kappa(p)=\mathcal O_{X,p}/\mathfrak m_p$ of a closed
point $p$ of a finite-type $k$-scheme is a finite extension of $k$, so
$\dim_k(A_p/t_p^mA_p)=m\,[\kappa(p):k]$ for every $m\ge0$; in particular
$t_p^{-m}A_p/A_p$ is a finite-dimensional $k$-vector space for every $m$
([[def-algebraic-curve-over-field]], [[thm-local-ring-smooth-curve-dvr]],
[[def-adic-completion-of-a-module]]).

[F6] Sheaf cohomology $H^q(X,-)$ is the right derived functor of global
sections on sheaves of abelian groups, a short exact sequence of sheaves
yields a natural long exact sequence of cohomology groups, and for a nonempty
affine open $U=\operatorname{Spec}A$ one has $A=\Gamma(U,\mathcal O_X)$
([[def-sheaf-cohomology-derived-global-sections]],
[[def-sheaf-on-topological-space]], [[cor-derived-long-exact-sequence]],
[[def-quasi-coherent-module-scheme]]).

[F7] On the irreducible space $X$ the constant sheaf with value a $k$-vector
space is flasque, a direct sum of skyscraper sheaves at closed points is
flasque, every flasque sheaf on $X$ has vanishing cohomology in positive
degrees, and the global sections of a direct sum of skyscraper sheaves at the
closed points are the direct sum of their values
([[lem-constant-sheaf-on-irreducible-space-is-flasque]],
[[lem-constant-sheaf-is-the-sheaf-of-locally-constant-functions]],
[[thm-flasque-sheaves-acyclic]]).

[F8] For every coherent $\mathcal O_X$-module $\mathcal F$ and every $q\ge0$
the $k$-vector space $H^q(X,\mathcal F)$ is finite-dimensional, because $X$
is proper over $k$; in particular $H^0(X,\mathcal O_X)$ and
$H^1(X,\mathcal O_X)$ are finite-dimensional, $H^0(X,\mathcal O_X)$ is a
$k$-subalgebra and a domain, and if $X$ is geometrically integral then for an
algebraic closure $\bar k/k$ the ring $K\otimes_k\bar k$ is a domain
([[cor-projective-cohomology-finite-dimensional-field]],
[[lem-integral-finite-type-scheme-function-field]],
[[def-algebraic-curve-over-field]]).

[F9] The Axiom of Choice is [[def-axiom-of-choice]].

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Set up the two ambient objects. By [F1] the generic point $\eta$ has function field $K=k(X)$, and by [F2] and [F4] each closed point $p$ carries the valuation $v_p=\operatorname{ord}_p$ whose valuation ring $\mathcal O_{X,p}$ lies in $K$, with $K\subseteq K_p$. By [F3] every $f\in K$ lies in $\mathcal O_{X,p}\subseteq A_p$ for all but finitely many $p$, so the diagonal map $K\to\prod_pK_p$ lands in $V_X$; it is injective because each map $K\to K_p$ is an inclusion of fields by [F4]. If $f\in K$ and $(g_p)\in V_X$, then $g_p\in A_p$ for all but finitely many $p$ and $f\in A_p$ for all but finitely many $p$ by [F3], so $fg_p\in A_p$ for all but finitely many $p$; hence componentwise multiplication makes $V_X$ a $K$-module containing $K$ as a $k$-subspace. This is part 1; the Axiom of Choice [F9] enters only through the cited suppliers. [F1, F2, F3, F4, F9, given]

1.2 Compute the quotient attached to a single $f\in K$. If $f=0$, then $(fA_X+A_X)/A_X=0$. Now suppose $f\ne0$ and let $S=\{p:\operatorname{ord}_p(f)<0\}$, finite by [F3], and put $n_p=-\operatorname{ord}_p(f)\ge0$ for $p\in S$. Since $fA_p=t_p^{-n_p}A_p$ by [F4], inside $K_p$ one has $fA_p+A_p=t_p^{-n_p}A_p$, and $(fA_p+A_p)/A_p=t_p^{-n_p}A_p/A_p$ is a $k$-vector space of dimension $n_p[\kappa(p):k]$ by [F5] for $p\in S$, while $fA_p\subseteq A_p$ by [F4] for $p\notin S$. Hence $fA_X+A_X\subseteq\left(\prod_{p\in S}t_p^{-n_p}A_p\right)\times\prod_{p\notin S}A_p$, and the quotient $(fA_X+A_X)/A_X$ embeds into $\bigoplus_{p\in S}t_p^{-n_p}A_p/A_p$, a finite-dimensional $k$-vector space. Therefore $fA_X<A_X$ for every $f\in K$, and for each such $f$ the multiplication-by-$f$ endomorphism $\theta_f$ of $V_X$ satisfies $\theta_f(A_X)=fA_X<A_X$. This is the first assertion of part 2. [F2, F3, F4, F5]

1.3 Verify the tail condition. Let $f,g\in K$ and let $S$ be a finite set of closed points outside which $f$, $g$ and, when $g\ne0$, $g^{-1}$ are all integral; put $T=X\setminus S$. If $f=0$, all three tail images are zero, interpreting the $g^{-1}$ term as zero when $g=0$. If $f\ne0$ and $g=0$, only $fA_T$ remains, and it lies in $A_T$ because $f$ is integral at every $p\in T$. If $f,g\ne0$, integrality gives $f,g,g^{-1}\in A_p$ at each $p\in T$, so their products give $fA_p+fgA_p+fg^{-1}A_p\subseteq A_p$. Taking products proves the tail inclusion. Such finite sets $S$ exist by [F3] applied only to the nonzero functions among $f,g,g^{-1}$. This proves the second assertion of part 2 without taking the order of zero. [F2, F3, F4]

1.4 Identify $K\cap A_X$ with the global sections. Let $f\in K$. By [F4], $f\in A_p$ if and only if $f\in\mathcal O_{X,p}$, so $f\in K\cap A_X$ exactly when $f$ is regular at every closed point of $X$. Choose a finite affine cover of $X$ by opens $U=\operatorname{Spec}A$ (possible since $X$ is quasi-compact and of finite type over $k$ by [F1]) and let $f\in K=\operatorname{Frac}(A)$. If $f\notin A$, the ideal of denominators $I=\{a\in A:af\in A\}$ is a nonzero proper ideal of the Noetherian domain $A$, so it is contained in a maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$ of $A$ by [F3]; the closed points of $U$ are exactly the maximal ideals of $A$ [F3], with $\mathcal O_{X,p}=A_{\mathfrak m}$ for the corresponding closed point $p$ by [F1] and [F6], and $f\notin A_{\mathfrak m}$, because an expression $f=b/s$ with $b\in A$ and $s\in A\setminus\mathfrak m$ would give $sf=b\in A$, hence $s\in I\subseteq\mathfrak m$, a contradiction. Passing to the contrapositive, $f$ regular at every closed point of $U$ forces $f\in A$; applying this on the finitely many charts shows that $f\in K\cap A_X$ if and only if $f\in\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X)=H^0(X,\mathcal O_X)$. This is part 3. [F1, F3, F4, F6]

1.5 Build the two auxiliary sheaves. Let $K^0$ be the constant sheaf with value $K$ on $X$, and let $K^1$ be the sheaf $U\mapsto\bigoplus_{p\in U}K_p/A_p$, the direct sum over the closed points of the skyscraper sheaves with values $K_p/A_p$; this is a sheaf because every open subset of $X$ is quasi-compact by [F1], so a compatible family over a cover is already determined by its members over a finite subcover, and it is flasque because a section over an open $U$ extends by zero to $X$ by [F7]. Define the map of sheaves $\delta:K^0\to K^1$ on sections over a nonempty open $U$ by sending $f\in K=K^0(U)$ to the class $(f+A_p)_{p\in U}$; this is well defined because $f\in A_p$ for all but finitely many $p$ by [F3], so the family has finite support. Then $\ker\delta$ has sections over a nonempty open $U$ equal to $\{f\in K:f\in A_p$ for every closed point $p\in U\}$, and the cokernel of $\delta$ on global sections is $V_X/(K+A_X)$. [F1, F3, F4, F7]

2.1 Prove that $0\to\mathcal O_X\to K^0\xrightarrow{\delta}K^1\to0$ is exact. The map $\mathcal O_X\to K^0$ sends a regular function to itself viewed in $K$; it is injective on every nonempty open because $\Gamma(U,\mathcal O_X)\subseteq K$, and its image is killed by $\delta$. By construction $\ker\delta$ has sections over a nonempty open $U$ equal to $\{f\in K:f\in A_p$ for all closed points $p\in U\}$, which by the chart computation of step 1.4 (applied to the members of a finite affine cover of the quasi-compact open $U$) is exactly $\Gamma(U,\mathcal O_X)$; hence $\ker\delta=\mathcal O_X$. For the surjectivity of $\delta$ it suffices to check stalks: at a closed point $p$ the stalk of $K^0$ is $K$ and the stalk of $K^1$ is $K_p/A_p$, and the map $K\to K_p/A_p$ is surjective because $K+A_p=K_p$: since $K_p=A_p[t_p^{-1}]$ by [F4], every $x\in K_p$ equals $ct_p^{-m}$ with $c\in A_p$ and $m\ge0$, and the image of the completion map $\mathcal O_{X,p}\to A_p$ being dense supplies $b\in\mathcal O_{X,p}$ with $b\equiv c\bmod t_p^mA_p$, so that $bt_p^{-m}\in K$ and $x-bt_p^{-m}\in A_p$. At the generic point $\eta$ the stalk of $K^1$ is $0$ because the class of a local section of the direct sum of skyscrapers is killed after removing its finitely many support points, so $\delta$ is surjective there as well. Thus the sequence of sheaves is exact. [F1, F2, F3, F4, step 1.4, step 1.5]

2.2 Compute the cohomology of $K^0$ and $K^1$. Since $K^0$ is the constant sheaf with value $K$ on the irreducible space $X$ [F1], [F7] gives $H^0(X,K^0)=K$ and $H^q(X,K^0)=0$ for every $q>0$. Since $K^1$ is flasque, [F7] gives $H^q(X,K^1)=0$ for every $q>0$, and its global sections are $H^0(X,K^1)=\bigoplus_pK_p/A_p=V_X/A_X$, the direct sum over all closed points, again by [F7]. [F1, F7, step 1.5]

3.1 Apply the long exact sequence. The short exact sequence of step 2.1 yields, by [F6], the exact sequence $0\to H^0(X,\mathcal O_X)\to K\to V_X/A_X\to H^1(X,\mathcal O_X)\to0$, where the middle map $K\to V_X/A_X$ sends $f$ to $(f+A_p)_p$ and its cokernel is $V_X/(K+A_X)$; here the cohomology of $K^0$ and $K^1$ is that computed in step 2.2. Hence $H^1(X,\mathcal O_X)\cong V_X/(K+A_X)$ canonically as $k$-vector spaces, and the injective map $H^0(X,\mathcal O_X)\to K$ identifies $H^0(X,\mathcal O_X)$ with the kernel of $K\to V_X/A_X$, which is $K\cap A_X$ in agreement with step 1.4. This is part 4, except for the finiteness of $V_X/(K+A_X)$. [F6, step 1.4, step 2.1, step 2.2]

4.1 Finiteness and the constant-field case. Since $X$ is proper over $k$ and $\mathcal O_X$ is coherent, [F8] gives $\dim_kH^1(X,\mathcal O_X)<\infty$, so step 3.1 makes $V_X/(K+A_X)$ finite-dimensional, which completes part 4. For part 5 let $L:=K\cap A_X=H^0(X,\mathcal O_X)$ by step 1.4; by [F8] the ring $L$ is a finite-dimensional $k$-algebra and a domain, and $L\ne0$ because it contains the unit of $K$. If $X$ is geometrically integral and $\bar k/k$ is an algebraic closure, then $K\otimes_k\bar k$ is a domain by [F8], and the inclusion $L\hookrightarrow K$ remains injective after tensoring with the flat $k$-module $\bar k$, so $L\otimes_k\bar k$ is a domain of dimension $[L:k]$ over the algebraically closed field $\bar k$. A finite-dimensional commutative domain over a field is a field, since a nonzero element has injective and hence bijective multiplication; thus $L\otimes_k\bar k$ is a field of degree $[L:k]$ over $\bar k$, forcing $[L:k]=1$ and $L=k$, which is part 5. [F1, F8, step 1.4, step 3.1] ∎
