## Definition

Let $(X,\mathcal T)$ be a topological space ([[def-topological-space]]). The
**Borel sigma-algebra of $X$** is the sigma-algebra generated by its open sets:

$$\mathcal B(X):=\sigma_X(\mathcal T).$$

Its members are the **Borel subsets** of $X$. The generated sigma-algebra exists
and is minimal by [[thm-generated-sigma-algebra-exists-and-is-minimal]].
