## Statement

Let $C_2\subseteq C_1\le\mathbb F_2^n$, $n\ge1$, and
$k=\dim C_1-\dim C_2$. The coset vectors from
[[def-qc-css-code-from-nested-binary-codes]] define an isometric encoding of
$k$ logical qubits. For every $c\in C_2$ and $h\in C_1^\perp$, $X^c$ and
$Z^h$ commute, and each fixes every encoded coset vector. Their common
$+1$ eigenspace is exactly this encoding. The conclusion requires only
$C_2\subseteq C_1$; it does not require
$C_2^\perp\subseteq C_1$.

Moreover, for every $x\in C_1$, the Hadamard transform is

$$H^{\otimes n}|x+C_2\rangle=2^{-k/2}\sum_{y+C_1^\perp\in C_2^\perp/C_1^\perp}(-1)^{x\cdot y}|y+C_1^\perp\rangle.$$

The phase is independent of the representatives $x+C_2$ and
$y+C_1^\perp$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A nested pair $C_2\subseteq C_1\le\mathbb F_2^n$, the ordered
computational basis, and the Pauli operators $X^c,Z^h$.

[F1] [[def-qc-css-code-from-nested-binary-codes]]: each coset vector is
$|C_2|^{-1/2}\sum_{u\in C_2}|x+u\rangle$, with labels in $C_1/C_2$.

[F2] [[def-qc-dual-of-a-finite-field-linear-code]]: $C^\perp$ is defined
by the standard bilinear dot product on binary coordinate vectors.

[F3] [[lem-qc-binary-dual-dimension]]: $\dim C^\perp=n-\dim C$ for every
binary linear code $C$.

[F4] [[thm-qc-computational-basis-orthonormal]]: distinct computational
basis vectors are orthogonal unit vectors.

[F5] [[def-qc-pauli-matrices]]: $X|b\rangle=|b+1\rangle$ and
$Z|b\rangle=(-1)^b|b\rangle$ for $b\in\mathbb F_2$.

[F6] [[thm-qc-pauli-commutator-is-symplectic-pairing]]: Pauli operators
commute exactly when their binary labels have symplectic pairing zero.

[F7] [[def-qc-one-qubit-gate-alphabet-and-rotations]]: the Hadamard matrix
is $H=2^{-1/2}\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}$.

[F8] [[def-qc-stabilizer-code-space]]: the stabilizer code space is the
common $+1$ eigenspace of all checks.

[F9] [[thm-qc-stabilizer-projector-and-dimension]]: a stabilizer generated by
$r$ independent commuting Hermitian checks has common $+1$ eigenspace of
dimension $2^{n-r}$, provided $-I$ is not in the generated group.

## Proof

1.1 There are $|C_2|$ distinct basis vectors in each coset sum. By [F4], its squared norm is $|C_2|^{-1}|C_2|=1$. Distinct cosets have disjoint supports, so their vectors are orthogonal. By [F1] there are $|C_1/C_2|=2^{\dim C_1-\dim C_2}=2^k$ such vectors, giving an orthonormal basis indexed by a $k$-bit logical register. [F1, F4, algebra]

1.2 By [F5], for $c\in C_2$, $X^c$ sends $|x+u\rangle$ to $|x+u+c\rangle$. Translation $u\mapsto u+c$ permutes $C_2$, so $X^c$ fixes the whole coset sum. For $h\in C_1^\perp$, each $x+u\in C_1$ and therefore $(-1)^{h\cdot(x+u)}=1$; the diagonal operator $Z^h$ fixes every term. Thus both check families fix every encoded basis state and hence their linear span. [F1, F5, given]

1.3 The binary labels of $X^c$ and $Z^h$ are $(c,0)$ and $(0,h)$. Their symplectic pairing is $c\cdot h$. Since $c\in C_2\subseteq C_1$ and $h\in C_1^\perp$, this dot product is zero by [F2]. [F2, F6, given]

1.4 On a computational basis vector $|w\rangle$, multiplying the one-bit Hadamard formulas from [F7] gives $H^{\otimes n}|w\rangle=2^{-n/2}\sum_{y\in\mathbb F_2^n}(-1)^{y\cdot w}|y\rangle$. Substituting the coset definition [F1] yields $H^{\otimes n}|x+C_2\rangle=2^{-n/2}|C_2|^{-1/2}\sum_{y\in\mathbb F_2^n}(-1)^{y\cdot x}(\sum_{u\in C_2}(-1)^{y\cdot u})|y\rangle$. For $y\in C_2^\perp$, the inner sum is $|C_2|$. Otherwise choose $u_0\in C_2$ with $y\cdot u_0=1$; translation $u\mapsto u+u_0$ pairs terms with opposite signs, so the sum is zero. [F1, F7, construct]

2.1 Choose independent bases of $C_2$ and $C_1^\perp$. Their separate $X$- and $Z$-type labels are independent: a zero combined label has both binary halves zero. By [F3] the combined number is $\dim C_2+n-\dim C_1=n-k$. This includes $k=0$. [F1, F3, step 1.3, algebra]

3.1 The checks are commuting Hermitian Paulis by steps 1.3 and [F5]. They fix the nonzero encoding from step 1.2, so their generated group cannot contain $-I$. Step 2.1 gives $n-k$ independent checks; [F9] therefore gives their common $+1$ eigenspace dimension $2^{n-(n-k)}=2^k$. By step 1.1 the encoding has dimension $2^k$, and step 1.2 says it lies in that eigenspace. Equal finite dimensions force equality: the CSS encoding is exactly the stabilizer code space [F8]. [F1, F8, F9, step 1.1, step 1.2, step 2.1, construct]

4.1 The transformed vector is supported exactly on $C_2^\perp$, with constant magnitude on that set. Since $x\in C_1$, the phase $(-1)^{x\cdot y}$ is constant on each coset of $C_1^\perp$ in $C_2^\perp$. Grouping terms by those cosets gives $H^{\otimes n}|x+C_2\rangle=2^{-k/2}\sum_{y+C_1^\perp\in C_2^\perp/C_1^\perp}(-1)^{x\cdot y}|y+C_1^\perp\rangle$. The coefficient is $2^{-k/2}$ because $|C_2||C_1^\perp|/2^n=2^{-k}$ by [F3]. This is the dual coset basis Fourier transform; the pairing is independent of representatives since $C_2\subseteq C_1$ and $C_1^\perp\subseteq C_2^\perp$. [F1, F3, step 1.4, algebra] ∎
