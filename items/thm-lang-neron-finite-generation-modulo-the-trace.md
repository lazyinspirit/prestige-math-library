## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice and the Axiom of Dependent Choice. Let $k\subseteq K$ satisfy $K=k(a_1,\ldots,a_n)$ for a possibly empty finite tuple $a_i\in K$. Assume every element of $K$ algebraic over $k$ belongs to $k$. Assume also there is a possibly empty finite tuple $t_1,\ldots,t_r\in K$ such that no nonzero polynomial in $k[T_1,\ldots,T_r]$ vanishes at the tuple, and the subfield $F=k(t_1,\ldots,t_r)$ generated by $k$ and these elements satisfies $[K:F]<\infty$ and every element of $K$ has a minimal polynomial over $F$ with no repeated root. These conditions define a finitely generated regular extension $K/k$. Let $A/K$ be an abelian variety, that is, a proper, geometrically connected, smooth separated group variety of finite type. The $K/k$-trace pair $(T,\tau)$ of $A$ exists, and the abelian group
$$A(K)/\tau(T(k))$$
is finitely generated.
