## Statement

Let $T$ be a distribution on $\mathbb T=\mathbb R/\mathbb Z$, meaning a
continuous complex-linear functional on $C^\infty(\mathbb T)$ with the
seminorm topology generated by
$$p_j(\varphi):=\max_{x\in[0,1]}|\varphi^{(j)}(x)|,\qquad j\ge0.$$
Define its Fourier coefficients by
$a_n:=T(e^{-2\pi inx})$ for $n\in\mathbb Z$. Then there are constants
$C>0$ and $N\in\mathbb Z_{ge0}$ such that
$$|a_n|\le C(1+|n|)^N\qquad(n\in\mathbb Z).$$

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A distribution $T$ on the circle with the seminorm topology stated above.

[F1] [[def-distribution]]: a distribution is a continuous complex-linear functional on its test-function space.

## Proof

**Given:** $T$ and fact [F1].

1.1 Continuity of $T$ at zero gives a zero-neighbourhood $U$ on which $|T(\varphi)|<1$. Refine a basic neighbourhood contained in $U$ to include a $p_0$ condition; the stated seminorm topology then gives a finite intersection $p_{j_r}(\varphi)<\epsilon_r$ for $1\le r\le q$. Put $N=\max_rj_r$ and choose $\epsilon>0$ no larger than every $\epsilon_r$; then $p_N^*(\varphi):=\max_{0\le j\le N}p_j(\varphi)<\epsilon$ implies $\varphi\in U$. Since $p_0$ is a norm, homogeneity applied to $\epsilon\varphi/(2p_N^*(\varphi))$ for nonzero $\varphi$ gives $|T(\varphi)|\le(2/\epsilon)p_N^*(\varphi)$. [given, F1, choose, algebra]

2.1 For $\varphi_n(x)=e^{-2\pi inx}$, every derivative of order $j$ has supremum $(2\pi|n|)^j$, so $p_N^*(\varphi_n)\le(1+2\pi|n|)^N\le(1+2\pi)^N(1+|n|)^N$. Applying step 1.1 to $a_n=T(\varphi_n)$ proves the asserted polynomial bound with $C=(2/\epsilon)(1+2\pi)^N$. [given, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
