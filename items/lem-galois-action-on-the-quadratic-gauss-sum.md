## Statement

Let $p$ be an odd prime, let $\zeta_p$ be a fixed primitive $p$-th root of
unity, let $\tau_p=\sum_{a\bmod p}(a/p)\zeta_p^{\,a}$ be the quadratic Gauss sum
attached to $\zeta_p$, and let $b$ be an integer not divisible by $p$. For the
automorphism $\sigma_b$ of $\mathbb Q(\zeta_p)$ with
$\sigma_b(\zeta_p)=\zeta_p^{\,b}$ one has
$$\sigma_b(\tau_p)=\left(\frac{b}{p}\right)\tau_p .$$

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** An odd prime $p$, a fixed primitive $p$-th root of unity
$\zeta=\zeta_p$ in a fixed algebraic closure of $\mathbb Q$, the Gauss sum
$\tau:=\tau_p=\sum_{a\bmod p}(a/p)\zeta^{a}$, and an integer $b$ with
$p\nmid b$.

[F1] $\tau=\sum_{a=1}^{p-1}(a/p)\zeta^{a}\in\mathbb Z[\zeta]$, because the
$a=0$ term of the defining sum carries the factor $(0/p)=0$
([[def-quadratic-gauss-sum-in-a-cyclotomic-field]],
[[def-legendre-symbol]]).

[F2] $K=\mathbb Q(\zeta_p)$ is Galois over $\mathbb Q$, and
$\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb Q)\cong(\mathbb Z/p)^{\times}$ via
$\sigma_b(\zeta)=\zeta^{b}$; every such $\sigma_b$ fixes $\mathbb Q$ and hence
acts on $\mathbb Z[\zeta]$ by $b$-th powers of $\zeta$
([[cor-the-galois-group-of-a-rational-cyclotomic-field]]).

[F3] The Legendre symbol is multiplicative for all integer numerators:
$(uv/p)=(u/p)(v/p)$ for all $u,v\in\mathbb Z$
([[thm-legendre-symbol-multiplicativity]]); moreover $(1/p)=1$ and
$(b/p)\in\{\pm1\}$ for $p\nmid b$, so
$(b^{-1}/p)=(b/p)$ by multiplicativity applied to $b\cdot b^{-1}\equiv1$.

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Applying the automorphism $\sigma_b$ of [F2] to the finite sum of [F1] gives $\sigma_b(\tau)=\sum_{a=1}^{p-1}(a/p)\sigma_b(\zeta)^{a}=\sum_{a=1}^{p-1}(a/p)\zeta^{ab}$, since $\sigma_b$ fixes the rational integers $(a/p)$. [F1, F2]

2.1 Multiplication by $b$ permutes the nonzero residue classes modulo $p$, so substituting $c\equiv ab\pmod p$ rewrites the sum as $\sigma_b(\tau)=\sum_{c=1}^{p-1}(b^{-1}c/p)\zeta^{c}$, where $b^{-1}$ denotes an inverse of $b$ modulo $p$. [step 1.1]

3.1 By multiplicativity of the Legendre symbol the coefficient factors as $(b^{-1}c/p)=(b^{-1}/p)(c/p)$, and $(b^{-1}/p)=(b/p)$ because $(b/p)(b^{-1}/p)=(bb^{-1}/p)=(1/p)=1$ and $(b/p)=\pm1$. Therefore $\sigma_b(\tau)=(b/p)\sum_{c=1}^{p-1}(c/p)\zeta^{c}=(b/p)\tau$. [F1, F3, step 2.1] ∎

## Remarks

- **No analytic sign is used.** The argument is a finite rearrangement of the
  defining sum together with the multiplicativity of the Legendre symbol; it
  never chooses a complex embedding of $\mathbb Q(\zeta_p)$ or a sign of
  $\tau_p$. Later, together with the theorem that $\tau_p^{2}=p^{*}\ne0$, this
  identity shows that exactly the square classes $b$ fix $\tau_p$, which
  identifies the quadratic subfield generated by $\tau_p$.
- **Convention.** As in the definition, $\sigma_b(\zeta)=\zeta^{b}$ is the
  arithmetic power map. Its inverse $\zeta\mapsto\zeta^{b^{-1}}$ has the
  same action on $\tau_p$, since $(b^{-1}/p)=(b/p)$ by [F3].
