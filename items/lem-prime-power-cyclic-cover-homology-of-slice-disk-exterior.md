## Statement

Assume AC. Let $D\subset B^4$ be a smooth proper disk, let $p$ be prime, $q=p^k$ with $k\ge1$, and let $\mathbb F_p$ be the field with $p$ elements. For the connected $q$-fold cyclic cover $X_{D,q}$ of the disk exterior, $H_0(X_{D,q};\mathbb F_p)\cong\mathbb F_p$, $H_1(X_{D,q};\mathbb F_p)\cong\mathbb F_p$, and $H_i(X_{D,q};\mathbb F_p)=0$ for every $i\ge2$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** Full AC, a smooth proper disk $D$, its exterior $X_D$, the infinite cyclic cover $X_{D,\infty}$ with deck generator $t$, and $q=p^k$.

[A1] Full AC is assumed wherever the cover and orientation conventions use the choice axiom. [[def-axiom-of-choice]]

[F1] $H_0(X_D;\mathbb F_p)=\mathbb F_p$, $H_1(X_D;\mathbb F_p)=\mathbb F_p$ generated by the meridian, and $H_i(X_D;\mathbb F_p)=0$ for $i\ge2$. This follows from the integral homology of the exterior and the universal coefficient theorem for homology over a PID. [[lem-slice-disk-exterior-has-homology-of-a-circle]] [[thm-universal-coefficient-theorem-for-homology-over-a-pid]]

[F2] The infinite cyclic cover and its finite quotient are those defined by the positive-meridian deck action in [[def-infinite-cyclic-cover-of-a-slice-disk-exterior]]. Their chain groups give short exact sequences
$$0\to C_*(X_{D,\infty};\mathbb F_p)\xrightarrow{t-1}C_*(X_{D,\infty};\mathbb F_p)\to C_*(X_D;\mathbb F_p)\to0$$
and
$$0\to C_*(X_{D,\infty};\mathbb F_p)\xrightarrow{t^q-1}C_*(X_{D,\infty};\mathbb F_p)\to C_*(X_{D,q};\mathbb F_p)\to0.$$
Each singular simplex has a free orbit of lifts; quotienting its orbit module by $t-1$ or $t^q-1$ gives the singular chains of the corresponding quotient cover. The long exact homology sequences are supplied by [[thm-long-exact-sequence-in-homology]].

## Proof

1.1 The deck transformation acts trivially on $H_0(X_{D,\infty};\mathbb F_p)\cong\mathbb F_p$ because the cover is connected. From the first sequence in [F2], its homology exact sequence and [F1] show that $t-1$ is an isomorphism on $H_i(X_{D,\infty};\mathbb F_p)$ for every $i\ge1$: for $i\ge2$ both neighboring homology groups of $X_D$ vanish, while in degree one exactness uses $H_2(X_D)=0$, $H_1(X_D)=\mathbb F_p$, and the zero map $t-1$ on $H_0(X_{D,\infty})$. Thus the degree-one quotient and kernel both vanish. [F1, F2, given, algebra, A1]

2.1 In characteristic $p$, repeated Frobenius gives $t^{p^k}-1=(t-1)^{p^k}$. Therefore $t^q-1$ is an isomorphism on every positive-degree homology group of $X_{D,\infty}$ and is zero on its degree-zero homology. The long exact sequence of the second sequence in [F2] now gives $H_1(X_{D,q};\mathbb F_p)\cong H_0(X_{D,\infty};\mathbb F_p)\cong\mathbb F_p$, while the quotient cover is connected and hence $H_0(X_{D,q};\mathbb F_p)\cong\mathbb F_p$. [F1, F2, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 For each $i\ge2$, both the cokernel on $H_i(X_{D,\infty})$ and the kernel on $H_{i-1}(X_{D,\infty})$ of $t^q-1$ vanish by step 2.1 and the isomorphisms of step 1.1. Exactness therefore gives $H_i(X_{D,q};\mathbb F_p)=0$. This includes all higher degrees and completes the calculation. [F2, step 1.1, step 2.1, algebra] ∎
