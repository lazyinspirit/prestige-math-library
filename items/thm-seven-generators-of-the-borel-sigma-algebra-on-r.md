## Statement

Each of the following families generates $\mathcal B(\mathbb R)$:

1. all open subsets of $\mathbb R$;
2. all closed subsets of $\mathbb R$;
3. all open intervals $(a,b)$ with $a<b$;
4. all rational open intervals $(p,q)$ with $p,q\in\mathbb Q$ and $p<q$;
5. all half-open intervals $(a,b]$ with $a<b$;
6. all open right rays $(a,\infty)$;
7. all rational open right rays $(q,\infty)$ with $q\in\mathbb Q$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The seven displayed families of subsets of $\mathbb R$.

[L1] The Borel sigma-algebra is generated by the open sets ([[def-borel-sigma-algebra]]).

[L2] Every open subset of $\mathbb R$ is a countable union of rational open intervals ([[lem-open-subsets-of-r-are-countable-unions-of-rational-intervals]]).

[L3] Strictly between any two real numbers lies a rational number ([[lem-rat-embeds-dense]]).

[L4] If each of two families lies in the sigma-algebra generated by the other, then they generate the same sigma-algebra ([[thm-generated-sigma-algebra-comparison-criterion]]).

[L5] The rationals are countably infinite: $\mathbb Q\approx\mathbb N$ ([[thm-rationals-countable]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Open and closed sets generate the same sigma-algebra by complementation. Open intervals are open, while [L2] expresses every open set using rational open intervals; hence the open intervals and the rational open intervals each generate the sigma-algebra in [L1]. [L1, L2, L4, algebra]

2.1 For $a<b$, rational density [L3] gives $(a,b]=\bigcap\{(a,q):q\in\mathbb Q,\ q>b\}$ and $(a,b)=\bigcup\{(a,q]:q\in\mathbb Q,\ a<q<b\}$. It also gives $(a,\infty)=\bigcup\{(a,q]:q\in\mathbb Q,\ q>a\}$ and $(a,\infty)=\bigcup\{(q,\infty):q\in\mathbb Q,\ q>a\}$. Finally $(-\infty,b]=\mathbb R\setminus(b,\infty)$ and $(a,b)=(a,\infty)\cap\bigcup_{q<b,\ q\in\mathbb Q}(-\infty,q]$. By [L5] all displayed rational-indexed unions and intersections are countable, so these identities give both generator inclusions for the half-open, real-ray, and rational-ray families. [step 1.1, L3, L5, algebra]

3.1 Applying [L4] to the inclusions in steps 1.1 and 2.1 shows that every displayed family generates $\mathcal B(\mathbb R)$. [step 1.1, step 2.1, L4] ∎
