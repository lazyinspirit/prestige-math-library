## Example

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and fix any loop value $\delta\in R$. For the ranks $n=0,1,2,3$, the diagram and presentation algebras have the following $R$-bases:

$$\begin{array}{c|c|c}n & \text{basis} & \text{rank}\\ \hline 0 & \{1\} & 1\\ 1 & \{1\} & 1\\ 2 & \{1,u_1\} & 2\\ 3 & \{1,u_1,u_2,u_1u_2,u_2u_1\} & 5\end{array}$$

The rank column is $C_n$ for these four indices. In rank $3$, writing $a=u_1$ and $b=u_2$, the product table is

$$\begin{array}{c|ccccc}\cdot&1&a&b&ab&ba\\ \hline 1&1&a&b&ab&ba\\ a&a&\delta a&ab&\delta ab&a\\ b&b&ba&\delta b&b&\delta ba\\ ab&ab&a&\delta ab&ab&\delta a\\ ba&ba&\delta ba&b&\delta b&ba\end{array}$$

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A commutative ring $R$, a loop value $\delta\in R$, and $n\in\{0,1,2,3\}$.

[F1] $P_n(R,\delta)$ is generated by $u_1,\ldots,u_{n-1}$ with $u_i^2=\delta u_i$, $u_i u_{i\pm1}u_i=u_i$, and distant generators commuting. ([[def-temperley-lieb-generator-presentation]])

[F2] The Jones normal forms use blocks $(u_{j_1}\cdots u_{k_1})\cdots(u_{j_r}\cdots u_{k_r})$ with $1\le k_i\le j_i<n$ and both endpoint sequences strictly increasing. ([[thm-temperley-lieb-generator-normal-form]])

[F3] The Jones normal forms are an $R$-basis of $P_n(R,\delta)$. ([[thm-temperley-lieb-presentation-isomorphism]])

[F4] The empty generator set gives $P_0=P_1=R$. ([[def-temperley-lieb-generator-presentation]])

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 For $n=0,1$ there are no generators, so the presentation is $R$ and the unique normal form is $1$. For $n=2$, the admissible lists in [F2] give $1,u_1$; for $n=3$, they give $1,u_1,u_2,u_2u_1,u_1u_2$. These words are bases by [F3], so the ranks are $1,1,2,5$; the A-page path bijections identify those counts with $C_0,C_1,C_2,C_3$. [F2, F3, F4, construct]

2.1 Set $a=u_1$, $b=u_2$ in rank $3$. From [F1], $a^2=\delta a$, $b^2=\delta b$, $aba=a$, and $bab=b$. The row of $a$ is $a,\ \delta a,\ ab,\ a(ab)=a^2b=\delta ab,\ a(ba)=aba=a$. The row of $b$ is $b,\ ba,\ \delta b,\ b(ab)=bab=b,\ b(ba)=b^2a=\delta ba$. The row of $ab$ is $ab,\ aba=a,\ abb=\delta ab,\ abab=(aba)b=ab,\ abba=ab^2a=\delta a$. The row of $ba$ is $ba,\ baa=\delta ba,\ bab=b,\ baab=ba^2b=\delta b,\ baba=(bab)a=ba$. These are the displayed entries and use no division by $\delta$. [F1, algebra] ∎

## Remarks

The rank is the number of basis diagrams, not the number of generators. The table remains valid at $\delta=0$ and over rings with zero divisors.
