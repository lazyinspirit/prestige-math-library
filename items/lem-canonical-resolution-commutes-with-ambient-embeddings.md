## Statement

Assume AC ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]).

Let $K$ have characteristic zero ([[def-field]]) and let $\varphi\colon X\hookrightarrow X^+$ be a closed immersion of smooth $K$-schemes of finite type ([[def-closed-immersion-schemes]]) and let $\mathcal I\subseteq\mathcal O_X$ be a coherent ideal sheaf that is not identically zero on any irreducible component of $X$, with $\mathcal I^+$ the inverse-image ideal of $\varphi_*\mathcal I$ under the quotient map $\mathcal O_{X^+}\to\varphi_*\mathcal O_X$.
For the marked ideals $(\mathcal I,\varnothing,1)$ on $X$ and $(\mathcal I^+,\varnothing,1)$ on $X^+$ the canonical resolutions are related, at points $x\in\operatorname{supp}(\mathcal I,1)$, by
$$\operatorname{inv}_{\mathcal I^+}(x)=(0,1,0,0;0,1,0,0;\dots;0,1,0,0;\operatorname{inv}_{\mathcal I}(x)),\qquad \nu_{\mathcal I^+}(x)=\nu_{\mathcal I}(x),\qquad \rho_{\mathcal I^+}(x)=\rho_{\mathcal I}(x),$$
with $(0,1,0,0)$ repeated $k$ times, where $k$ is the local codimension of the smooth immersion at $x$, and resolving $(X^+,\mathcal I^+,\varnothing,1)$ is locally equivalent to resolving $(X,\mathcal I,\varnothing,1)$ ([[def-canonical-resolution-invariants]]).

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A characteristic-zero field $K$, a closed embedding $\varphi\colon X\hookrightarrow X'$ of smooth finite-type $K$-schemes, and a marked ideal $(\mathcal I,\varnothing,1)$ on $X$ generically nonzero on every component. Let $\mathcal I'$ be the inverse image of $\varphi_*\mathcal I$ under $\mathcal O_{X'}\to\varphi_*\mathcal O_X$; locally the ideal of $X$ is generated by regular parameters $u_1,\dots,u_k\in\mathcal I'$.

[A1] [[def-axiom-of-choice]]: AC is assumed for the canonical-resolution consumer clauses and their cited AC-dependent construction suppliers.

[F1] [[def-closed-immersion-schemes]], [[def-marked-ideal]]: the quotient map to $\varphi_*\mathcal O_X$ defines $\mathcal I'$ as the ideal of the same closed subscheme of $X$ viewed inside $X'$. Locally it is generated by the equations $u_1,\dots,u_k$ of $X$ and lifts of generators of $\mathcal I$. It is coherent because the affine smooth coordinate rings are Noetherian, its restriction to $X$ is $\mathcal I$, and its support for mark $1$ is the image of $\operatorname{supp}(\mathcal I,1)$.

[F2] [[def-maximal-order-and-tangent-directions]], [[lem-giraud-tangent-directions-and-controlled-transforms]], [[lem-tangent-direction-contains-the-support]]: the sections $u_1,\dots,u_k$ are tangent directions of $(\mathcal I',1)$ at points of $X$, and along the canonical resolution the supports are contained in the strict transforms of the hypersurfaces $V(u_i)$.

[F3] [[prop-canonical-resolution-of-marked-ideals]]: the canonical resolution of a marked ideal is built by the algorithm of Steps 1–2, whose reductions depend only on intrinsic data (supports, orders, derivative ideals and the invariants).


## Proof

1.1 The invariant of the embedded ideal. Running Steps 2a and 1bb of the algorithm for $(\mathcal I',\varnothing,1)$ at a point $x\in X$: in Step 2a the ideal is of maximal order with $\operatorname{ord}_N=1$, so the companion ideal is $\mathcal I'$ itself and the invariant receives the first coordinate $1$; in Step 1bb (non-boundary case $s(x)=0$) the resolution passes to a hypersurface of maximal contact. Passing successively to the tangent directions $u_1,\dots,u_k$, each passage adjoins the source pair $(1,0)$, encoded as the four rational coordinates $(0,1,0,0)$ under [F3], to the invariant by [F2], and after $k$ passages one arrives at the restriction to $X$, where the invariant of $(\mathcal I,1)$ appears. Hence $\operatorname{inv}_{\mathcal I'}(x)=(0,1,0,0;\dots;0,1,0,0;\operatorname{inv}_{\mathcal I}(x))$ with $(0,1,0,0)$ repeated $k$ times, and analogously $\nu_{\mathcal I'}(x)=\nu_{\mathcal I}(x)$ and $\rho_{\mathcal I'}(x)=\rho_{\mathcal I}(x)$. [A1, F1, F2, F3]

2.1 The resolutions correspond. By [F3] the centers of the resolution of $(\mathcal I',1)$ are the maximal loci of the invariants computed in step 1.1; because the first $4k$ encoded coordinates of $\operatorname{inv}_{\mathcal I'}$ are constant on the image of the resolution of $(\mathcal I,1)$, the maximal locus over $X$ is the image of the maximal locus of $\operatorname{inv}_{\mathcal I}$, and blowing it up in $X'$ restricts to blowing it up in $X$. Iterating, the canonical resolution of $(\mathcal I',1)$ restricts over $X$ to the canonical resolution of $(\mathcal I,1)$, with the invariant comparison of step 1.1 (the prefixed constant pairs are removed on restriction). [A1, F2, F3, step 1.1] ∎
