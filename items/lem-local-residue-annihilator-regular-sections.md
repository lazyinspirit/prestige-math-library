## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice as inherited from the residue suppliers. Let $C$
be a smooth proper geometrically integral curve over a perfect field $k$, let
$p$ be a closed point with uniformizer $t$, and let $\mathcal L$ be an
invertible $\mathcal O_C$-module. Write $\omega_C$ for the canonical bundle
and consider the local residue pairing
$$\mathcal L_\eta/\mathcal L_p\;\times\;(\omega_C\otimes\mathcal L^{-1})_p\longrightarrow k,\qquad (c,s)\longmapsto \operatorname{res}_p(cs),$$
where $\mathcal L_\eta$ is the generic fibre, also viewed as the sheaf of meromorphic sections of $\mathcal L$.
Then:

1. the annihilator of the image of the regular sections
   $(\omega_C\otimes\mathcal L^{-1})_p$ inside $\mathcal L_\eta$ is exactly
   $\mathcal L_p$: an element $c\in\mathcal L_\eta$ satisfies
   $\operatorname{res}_p(cs)=0$ for every
   $s\in(\omega_C\otimes\mathcal L^{-1})_p$ if and only if $c\in\mathcal L_p$,
   so the induced pairing on $\mathcal L_\eta/\mathcal L_p$ is nondegenerate
   on the left; dually the annihilator of $\mathcal L_p$ inside the space
   $(\omega_C\otimes\mathcal L^{-1})_\eta$ of meromorphic sections is the image
   of the regular sections: an element $s\in(\omega_C\otimes\mathcal L^{-1})_\eta$
   satisfies $\operatorname{res}_p(cs)=0$ for every $c\in\mathcal L_p$ if and
   only if $s\in(\omega_C\otimes\mathcal L^{-1})_p$;
2. after a local trivialization and completion, each nonzero pole in either
   factor is detected by a complementary finite Laurent coefficient and the
   nondegenerate trace form of $\kappa(p)/k$; these coefficient tests prove
   the two annihilator statements in every characteristic.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** a perfect field $k$, a smooth proper geometrically integral curve
$C$ over $k$, a closed point $p$ with uniformizer $t$, and an invertible
$\mathcal O_C$-module $\mathcal L$.

[F1] The local ring $\mathcal O_{C,p}$ is a discrete valuation ring with
fraction field $k(C)$ and maximal ideal generated by $t$; the residue field
$\kappa(p)$ is a finite separable extension of the perfect field $k$
([[thm-local-ring-smooth-curve-dvr]],
[[cor-algebraic-extensions-of-perfect-fields-are-separable]]).

[F2] The rational fiber $\mathcal L_\eta$ is one-dimensional over $k(C)$,
and the local lattice $\mathcal L_p$ is free of rank one over the DVR
$\mathcal O_{C,p}$. After trivializing $\mathcal L$ and completing,
$\widehat{\mathcal O}_{C,p}\cong\kappa(p)\llbracket t\rrbracket$ and $k(C)$
embeds into $\kappa(p)((t))$. These are a local ring and its completion,
and a function field and its completed Laurent field; they are not equal.
The induced map
$\mathcal L_\eta/\mathcal L_p\to
\widehat{\mathcal L_\eta}_p/\widehat{\mathcal L_p}$ is an isomorphism:
for each $N\ge1$, the map
$\mathcal O_{C,p}/(t^N)\to\widehat{\mathcal O}_{C,p}/(t^N)$ is an
isomorphism, since residue coefficients lift from $\mathcal O_{C,p}$ and
successive subtraction and division by $t$ lifts each finite jet. Each
principal part has finite pole order and is therefore determined by such a
finite jet. Thus it has a finite negative Laurent expansion
$c=\sum_{n<0}c_nt^n$ in the completed quotient, with coefficients in
$\kappa(p)$, without identifying the uncompleted fields or lattices with
their completions ([[def-principal-parts-sheaf-line-bundle-curve]],
[[def-invertible-sheaf]], [[thm-local-ring-smooth-curve-dvr]]).

[F3] The canonical bundle $\omega_C=\Omega^1_{C/k}$ is invertible; because
$\kappa(p)/k$ is separable, $dt$ for the chosen uniformizer generates the
actual stalk $\omega_{C,p}$. After trivializing $\mathcal L$, the stalk
$(\omega_C\otimes\mathcal L^{-1})_p$ is generated by $dt$. A rational
differential has a unique expression $a\,dt$ with $a\in k(C)$, and its
completion has a Laurent expansion in $\kappa(p)((t))dt$; the completed
stalk is $\kappa(p)\llbracket t\rrbracket dt$
([[def-canonical-line-bundle-curve]],
[[lem-uniformizer-differential-is-a-basis]]).

[F4] The local residue is defined by the coefficient-trace formula
$\operatorname{res}_p(a\,dt)=\operatorname{Tr}_{\kappa(p)/k}(a_{-1})$ for the
Laurent expansion $a=\sum_na_nt^n$ in $\kappa(p)((t))$; it is $k$-linear,
independent of the uniformizer, and kills every differential regular at $p$
([[def-residue-rational-differential-curve-point]],
[[def-field-norm-and-trace]]).

[F5] For a finite separable field extension $\kappa(p)/k$ the trace form
$(a,b)\mapsto\operatorname{Tr}_{\kappa(p)/k}(ab)$ is a nondegenerate
$k$-bilinear form: if $\operatorname{Tr}(ab)=0$ for all $b\in\kappa(p)$ then
$a=0$ ([[thm-trace-form-is-nondegenerate-iff-separable]],
[[cor-algebraic-extensions-of-perfect-fields-are-separable]],
[[def-field-norm-and-trace]]).

[F6] The Axiom of Choice is [[def-axiom-of-choice]].

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct; trivialize the line bundle, expand both sides in
Laurent series and use the nondegeneracy of the trace form of a finite
separable extension.

1.1 The pairing is well defined. Trivialize $\mathcal L$ near $p$ and pass to the completion as in [F2]. The completed local lattice is $\kappa(p)\llbracket t\rrbracket$, its completed rational fiber is $\kappa(p)((t))$, and the completed stalk of the dual twist is $\kappa(p)\llbracket t\rrbracket\,dt$ by [F3]; these describe completions of the actual local objects, not equalities with them. If a representative of $c$ is changed by an element of $\mathcal L_p$, its product with the given regular section $s$ changes by an element of $\omega_{C,p}$, so the residue is unchanged because regular differentials have zero residue by [F4]. This gives the pairing displayed in the statement. [F1, F2, F3, F4, F6]

1.2 Expand in Laurent series. Write $c=\sum_{n<0}c_nt^n$ and $s=\sum_{m\ge0}s_mt^m\,dt$ with coefficients in $\kappa(p)$, the first sum having only finitely many nonzero terms and the second a possibly infinite power series in the completed stalk $\kappa(p)\llbracket t\rrbracket\,dt$. Then $cs=\sum_{n<0,\,m\ge0}c_ns_mt^{n+m}\,dt$ has coefficient of $t^{-1}$ equal to $\sum_{n<0}c_ns_{-n-1}$, a finite sum, so by the coefficient-trace formula of [F4], $\operatorname{res}_p(cs)=\operatorname{Tr}_{\kappa(p)/k}\bigl(\sum_{n<0}c_ns_{-n-1}\bigr)=\sum_{n<0}\operatorname{Tr}_{\kappa(p)/k}(c_ns_{-n-1})$. [F2, F3, F4]

2.1 Compute the first annihilator. If $c\in\mathcal L_p$, then its product with every regular $s\in(\omega_C\otimes\mathcal L^{-1})_p$ is regular, so its residue is zero by [F4]. Conversely suppose $c\notin\mathcal L_p$, and let $n<0$ be the smallest exponent with $c_n\ne0$ in its finite negative Laurent part. By nondegeneracy in [F5], choose $a\in\kappa(p)$ with $\operatorname{Tr}_{\kappa(p)/k}(c_na)\ne0$. Lift $a$ to $\widetilde a\in\mathcal O_{C,p}$ using the residue map $\mathcal O_{C,p}\to\kappa(p)$, and take the regular section $s=t^{-n-1}\widetilde a\,dt$; its exponent is nonnegative. In the completed product, the coefficient of $t^{-1}dt$ is exactly $c_na$, because $n$ is the smallest exponent of $c$ and the constant coefficient of $\widetilde a$ is $a$. Thus $\operatorname{res}_p(cs)=\operatorname{Tr}(c_na)\ne0$. Hence the annihilator of the regular stalk inside $\mathcal L_\eta$ is exactly $\mathcal L_p$, and the induced pairing is nondegenerate on the left. [F2, F4, F5, step 1.2]

2.2 Compute the dual annihilator. If $s$ is regular, then its product with every $c\in\mathcal L_p$ is regular, so the residue is zero by [F4]. Conversely let a rational section $s$ have completed expansion $s=\sum_{m\ge m_0}s_mt^m\,dt$ with a nonzero negative coefficient, and choose its smallest exponent $m_0<0$. By [F5] choose $a\in\kappa(p)$ with $\operatorname{Tr}_{\kappa(p)/k}(a s_{m_0})\ne0$. Set $q=-m_0-1\ge0$, lift $a$ to $\widetilde a\in\mathcal O_{C,p}$, and take $c=t^q\widetilde a\in\mathcal L_p$. The coefficient of $t^{-1}dt$ in $cs$ is exactly $a s_{m_0}$: any positive-degree term of $\widetilde a$ would pair with a coefficient of $s$ below $m_0$, which is zero. Hence $\operatorname{res}_p(cs)=\operatorname{Tr}(a s_{m_0})\ne0$. Therefore the annihilator of $\mathcal L_p$ inside the rational dual-twist fiber consists exactly of its regular stalk. [F2, F4, F5, step 1.2]

3.1 Identify the local model. Steps 2.1 and 2.2 use only finite Laurent coefficients: the most negative coefficient of a principal part, or the first negative coefficient of a rational differential, is detected by a regular test section whose residue coefficient is chosen using the nondegenerate trace form of $\kappa(p)/k$. These finite tests prove the asserted annihilators and make no assertion about the full algebraic duals of the completed power-series or Laurent-series spaces. The argument divides by no integer, so it applies in every characteristic. [F1, F4, F5, step 2.1, step 2.2] ∎
