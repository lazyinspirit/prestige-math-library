## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]) and Dependent Choice ([[def-dependent-choice]]). Let $G$ be a locally compact Hausdorff abelian group with dual $\widehat G$ ([[def-pontryagin-dual-and-compact-open-topology]]). For $x_1\in G$, compact $K\subseteq\widehat G$ and $\epsilon>0$ put $$N_{x_1}(K,\epsilon):=\{x\in G:|\chi(x)-\chi(x_1)|<\epsilon\ \text{for all}\ \chi\in K\}.$$ Then every $N_{x_1}(K,\epsilon)$ is open in $G$, and these sets form a neighbourhood basis at $x_1$. Consequently the evaluation map $\Phi:G\to\widehat{\widehat G}$, $\Phi(x)(\chi):=\chi(x)$, is a homeomorphism onto its image, and $G$ carries the topology of uniform convergence on compact subsets of $\widehat G$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A locally compact Hausdorff abelian group $G$, its dual $\widehat G$ with the compact-open topology, a point $x_1\in G$, a compact set $K\subseteq\widehat G$ and $\epsilon>0$.

[F1] Characters are continuous homomorphisms $G\to\mathbb T$ with pointwise multiplication, $|\chi|\equiv1$ and $\chi(-x)=\overline{\chi(x)}$; the compact-open subbasis is $S(L,V)=\{\chi:\chi[L]\subseteq V\}$ for compact $L\subseteq G$ and open $V\subseteq\mathbb T$, and the evaluation pairing $(\chi,x)\mapsto\chi(x)$ is jointly continuous. ([[def-pontryagin-dual-and-compact-open-topology]], [[lem-character-evaluation-pairing-is-jointly-continuous]], [[lem-complex-conjugation-and-modulus-laws]], [[lem-unit-circle-is-a-compact-metrizable-topological-group]])

[F2] **Equicontinuity on compacts.** For every compact $K\subseteq\widehat G$ and every $\epsilon>0$ there is an open neighbourhood $W$ of $0$ in $G$ with $|\chi(w)-1|<\epsilon$ for all $\chi\in K\cup\{1\}$ and all $w\in W$. Indeed joint continuity at $(\chi,0)$ gives for each $\chi\in K$ open sets $A_\chi\ni\chi$, $W_\chi\ni0$ with $|\eta(w)-1|<\epsilon$ on $A_\chi\times W_\chi$; finitely many $A_\chi$ cover the compact set $K$, and the intersection of the corresponding $W_\chi$ together with a neighbourhood for the identity character is as required. ([[lem-character-evaluation-pairing-is-jointly-continuous]], [[def-compact-space]], [[def-continuous-map-top]], [[lem-unit-circle-is-a-compact-metrizable-topological-group]])

[F3] If $\chi\in\widehat G$ and $x\in G$ then $\chi(x)-\chi(x_1)=\chi(x_1)\big(\chi(x-x_1)-1\big)$, hence $|\chi(x)-\chi(x_1)|=|\chi(x-x_1)-1|$. ([[def-pontryagin-dual-and-compact-open-topology]], [[lem-complex-conjugation-and-modulus-laws]])

[F4] For every open neighbourhood $U$ of $0$ in $G$ there is $g\in C_c(G)$ with $g\ge0$, $g\not\equiv0$ and $\operatorname{supp}g-\operatorname{supp}g\subseteq U$. Indeed, continuity of subtraction gives an open identity neighbourhood $W$ with $W-W\subseteq U$; local compactness gives open $V\ni0$ with compact closure contained in $W$. A cutoff equal to $1$ at $0$ and zero outside $V$ has support in $\overline V$, whose difference set is contained in $U$. ([[lem-lch-urysohn-cutoff-for-a-compact-set-inside-an-open-set]], [[lem-translations-preserve-compactly-supported-continuous-functions]], [[def-compact-support-c-c-and-c-zero-on-an-lch-space]])

[F5] For $g\in C_c(G)$ the convolution square $f:=g*\widetilde g$ lies in the positive core $E$, with $\widehat f=|\widehat g|^2\ge0$ and $f(0)=\|g\|_2^2>0$ when $g\not\equiv0$ ([[lem-lca-positive-convolution-squares-form-an-inversion-core]], [[lem-lca-fourier-transform-intertwines-translation-modulation-and-convolution]]). The compatible dual Haar normalisation gives $\widehat f\in L^1(\widehat G,m_{\widehat G})$ ([[thm-compatible-dual-haar-normalisation]]); Fourier inversion for integrable transforms then gives $f(x)=\int_{\widehat G}\widehat f(\chi)\chi(x)\,dm_{\widehat G}(\chi)$ for every $x\in G$, since $f$ is continuous ([[thm-lca-fourier-inversion-for-integrable-transform]]).

[F6] For $\widehat f\in L^1(\widehat G)$ and $\delta>0$, density supplies $h\in C_c(\widehat G)$ with $\|\widehat f-h\|_1<\delta$. For the compact set $K=\operatorname{supp}h$, nonnegativity of $\widehat f$ gives $\int_{\widehat G\setminus K}\widehat f\le\|\widehat f-h\|_1<\delta$. ([[thm-c-c-is-dense-in-l-p-for-radon-measures]], [[def-compact-support-c-c-and-c-zero-on-an-lch-space]], [[def-radon-measure-on-an-lch-space]])

[F7] The evaluation map $\Phi(x)(\chi):=\chi(x)$ is injective: continuous characters separate points ([[lem-continuous-characters-separate-points-of-an-lca-group]]); the dual of a locally compact Hausdorff abelian group is again locally compact Hausdorff and abelian, so $\widehat{\widehat G}$ carries the compact-open topology with subbasic sets $\{\xi:\xi[L]\subseteq V\}$ for compact $L\subseteq\widehat G$ and open $V\subseteq\mathbb T$. ([[thm-dual-of-an-lca-group-is-locally-compact-abelian]], [[def-compact-open-topology-for-topological-domains]], [[def-pontryagin-dual-and-compact-open-topology]])

[F8] A continuous real-valued function on a nonempty compact space has a maximum: its image is a nonempty compact subset of the real metric line, and applying the metric extreme-value theorem to the identity on that image gives its maximum. ([[thm-compactness-under-continuous-maps]], [[thm-extreme-value-metric]])

## Proof

1.1 If $K=\varnothing$, then $N_{x_1}(K,\epsilon)=G$, which is open. Otherwise let $x\in N_{x_1}(K,\epsilon)$ and put $\delta:=\max_{\chi\in K}|\chi(x)-\chi(x_1)|$. The maximum exists because $K$ is nonempty compact and the function is continuous, and $\delta<\epsilon$ since every value is strictly below $\epsilon$. By [F2] applied with $\epsilon-\delta>0$ there is an open neighbourhood $W$ of $0$ with $|\chi(w)-1|<\epsilon-\delta$ for all $\chi\in K\cup\{1\}$ and $w\in W$. Then for $w\in W$ and $\chi\in K$, $|\chi(x+w)-\chi(x_1)|\le|\chi(x)||\chi(w)-1|+|\chi(x)-\chi(x_1)|<\epsilon-\delta+\delta=\epsilon$, using multiplicativity and $|\chi|\equiv1$; hence $x+W\subseteq N_{x_1}(K,\epsilon)$ and the set is open. [F1, F2, F3, F8]

1.2 Let $U$ be an open neighbourhood of $0$ in $G$. Choose $g$ as in [F4] and put $f:=g*\widetilde g$. Then $f\in C_c(G)\subseteq L^1(G)\cap L^2(G)$, $\widehat f=|\widehat g|^2\ge0$ belongs to $L^1(\widehat G,m_{\widehat G})$, and $f(0)=\|g\|_2^2>0$; moreover $f(x)=\int_{\widehat G}\widehat f(\chi)\chi(x)\,dm_{\widehat G}(\chi)$ for every $x\in G$. [F4, F5]

2.1 Choose a compact $K_0\subseteq\widehat G$ with $\int_{\widehat G\setminus K_0}\widehat f<f(0)/8$ and $\epsilon_0>0$ with $\epsilon_0\|\widehat f\|_1<f(0)/4$. For $x\in N_0(K_0,\epsilon_0)$ we have $|f(x)-f(0)|\le\int_{\widehat G}|\chi(x)-1|\widehat f(\chi)\,dm_{\widehat G}(\chi)\le\epsilon_0\|\widehat f\|_1+2\int_{\widehat G\setminus K_0}\widehat f<\frac{f(0)}2$, so $f(x)\ne0$. Since $f$ is continuous and $f(x)\ne0$ forces $x\in\operatorname{supp}f\subseteq\operatorname{supp}g-\operatorname{supp}g\subseteq U$, we obtain $N_0(K_0,\epsilon_0)\subseteq U$. [F5, F6, step 1.2]

3.1 For every $x_1\in G$ and every neighbourhood $V$ of $x_1$, choose an open neighbourhood $V_0$ of $x_1$ contained in $V$ and apply step 2.1 to the open identity neighbourhood $U:=V_0-x_1$. This produces compact $K_0$ and $\epsilon_0>0$ with $x_1+N_0(K_0,\epsilon_0)=N_{x_1}(K_0,\epsilon_0)\subseteq V$; since $x_1+N_0(K_0,\epsilon_0)$ is a neighbourhood of $x_1$, the sets $N_{x_1}(K,\epsilon)$ form a neighbourhood basis at $x_1$, each of them open by step 1.1. [step 1.1, step 2.1]

4.1 Equip $\Phi(G)$ with the subspace topology from $\widehat{\widehat G}$. For compact $L\subseteq\widehat G$ and open $V\subseteq\mathbb T$, if $L=\varnothing$ the preimage under $\Phi$ of $\{\xi:\xi[L]\subseteq V\}$ is all of $G$. Otherwise, let $x_0$ lie in that preimage. Joint continuity of evaluation [F1] gives, for each $\chi\in L$, open neighbourhoods $U_\chi\ni x_0$ and $W_\chi\ni\chi$ with $\eta(x)\in V$ for $(x,\eta)\in U_\chi\times W_\chi$. Compactness of $L$ supplies a finite subcover $W_{\chi_1},\dots,W_{\chi_m}$; then $U:=\bigcap_{j=1}^mU_{\chi_j}$ is a neighbourhood of $x_0$ contained in that preimage. Thus $\Phi$ is continuous. Conversely, fix $x_1\in G$, compact $K\subseteq\widehat G$ and $\epsilon>0$. If $K=\varnothing$, then $N_{x_1}(K,\epsilon)=G$ and its image is open in $\Phi(G)$. Otherwise set $F(\xi,\chi):=|\xi(\chi)-\chi(x_1)|$ on $\widehat{\widehat G}\times K$. This is continuous by the joint evaluation pairing and continuity of the fixed evaluation at $x_1$ [F1], and $F(\Phi(x_1),\chi)=0$ for every $\chi\in K$. For each $\chi\in K$, continuity gives open neighbourhoods $A_\chi\ni\Phi(x_1)$ and $B_\chi\ni\chi$ on which $F<\epsilon$; choose a finite subcover $B_{\chi_1},\dots,B_{\chi_m}$ of $K$. Then $A:=\bigcap_{j=1}^mA_{\chi_j}$ is open and lies in $\{\xi:|\xi(\chi)-\chi(x_1)|<\epsilon\text{ for all }\chi\in K\}$. Thus $A\cap\Phi(G)\subseteq\Phi(N_{x_1}(K,\epsilon))$. For any open $U\subseteq G$ and each $x_1\in U$, step 3.1 supplies one such basis set contained in $U$; the corresponding $A$ is an open neighbourhood of $\Phi(x_1)$ whose trace lies in $\Phi(U)$. Therefore $\Phi(U)$ is open in $\Phi(G)$, and $\Phi$ is open onto its image. By [F7] the map $\Phi$ is injective, hence a homeomorphism onto its image; by step 3.1 the topology of $G$ is the topology of uniform convergence on compact subsets of $\widehat G$ transported by $\Phi$. [F1, F7, step 1.1, step 3.1] ∎
