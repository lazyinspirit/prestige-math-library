## Statement

Let k be algebraically closed, C an integral finite-type k-curve, A=Z/ℓⁿZ for a prime ℓ and n≥1, F an arbitrary étale A-module sheaf on C, q≥0 and ξ∈H^q(C,F) in the choice-free finite-hypercover model. There exist a constructible étale sheaf Q of finite A-modules, a morphism Q→F, and a class ζ∈H^q(C,Q) mapping to ξ. No choice axiom is assumed. Consequently, if H^q(C,Q)=0 for all constructible finite A-module sheaves Q, then H^q(C,F)=0 for every A-module sheaf F.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** k, C, A, F, q and ξ as in the statement.

[F1] The choice-free tower resolution on finite-type k-schemes computes cohomology by finite truncated étale hypercovers. [[lem-qc-etale-hypercover-acyclic-tower-resolutions-in-zf]]

[F2] Étale morphisms are flat, locally quasi-finite and universally open, without a choice premise. [[lem-etale-morphisms-are-locally-standard-and-universally-open-in-zf]]

[F3] A constructible finite A-module sheaf has a finite locally closed stratification on which it is locally constant with finite fibres. [[def-constructible-etale-sheaf-of-finite-modules]]

## Proof

1.1 Let h:V→C be an affine étale chart whose image lies in an affine open Spec B⊂C, and let P=A[h_V] be the free A-module sheaf generated by its representable sheaf. The generic fibre of h is finite étale over the function field K of C by [F2]. If V=Spec D, the finitely many generators of D over B satisfy monic equations over K. Clearing finitely many denominators, and using B-flatness of D to inject D into D⊗_B K, makes those same generators integral over one localization B[1/b]. Thus D[1/b] is a finite B[1/b]-module. Over this nonempty open U⊂C, h is finite étale. A finite étale cover becomes a finite disjoint union of copies of U after an étale-local refinement obtained by finitely many fibre products, so P|U is finite locally constant. [F2, given, choose, algebra]

1.2 The complement C−U is a finite set of closed k-points. At each such point x, the stalk P_x is the free A-module on the finite geometric fibre V_x: a section of h_V near x is an étale lift of x, and [F2] makes that fibre finite. Thus it is a finite A-module. The strata U and the finitely many points of C−U show P is constructible in the sense of [F3]. A finite sum of such sheaves is constructible, and so is the cokernel of a map between them: take a common finite stratification and, after finite local trivializations on each stratum, form the cokernel of the resulting finite module map. [F2, F3, step 1.1, algebra]

2.1 By [F1], choose one finite truncated hypercover representing ξ. Refine the finitely many components in the degrees q and q+1 by affine étale charts lying over affine opens of C; the finite matching construction extends this refinement through the degrees needed for the cochain differential. Let P_i be the finite direct sum of the free A-module sheaves generated by the components in degree i, with its simplicial differentials. The chosen q-cocycle is equivalently a sheaf map c:P_q→F satisfying c d_{q+1}=0. Define Q=coker(d_{q+1}:P_{q+1}→P_q). By step 1.2, Q is constructible and finite. The quotient map P_q→Q is itself a q-cocycle, and c factors through a map Q→F. [F1, step 1.2, given, choose, construct, algebra]

2.2 Let ζ be the cohomology class of that universal Q-valued cocycle on the same hypercover. Its image under Q→F is the chosen representative of ξ, hence is ξ. If all such constructible Q have zero H^q, then ζ=0 and ξ=0. This applies independently to each specified class, without choosing a family of witnesses. [step 2.1, algebra] ∎

## Scope

The construction is specific to an integral finite-type curve and the stated prime-power coefficient rings. It does not assert that Q embeds in F, that all sheaves form an explicitly filtered union of constructibles, or that the constructible cohomology bound itself is proved.
