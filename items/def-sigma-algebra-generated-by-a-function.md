## Definition

Let $f : X \to Y$ be a function and assume the target $Y$ carries a Borel
sigma-algebra $\mathcal{B}(Y)$.

The **sigma-algebra generated by $f$** is

$$\sigma(f) := \{\, f^{-1}(B) : B \in \mathcal{B}(Y) \,\}.$$

Because preimages preserve complements and countable unions, this is a
sigma-algebra on $X$. It is the smallest sigma-algebra on $X$ that makes $f$
measurable.
