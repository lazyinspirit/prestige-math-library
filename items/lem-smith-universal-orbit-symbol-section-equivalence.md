## Statement

Assume AC, Countable Choice, and DC. Let $K/F$, $e$, $V_0$ and the two Kummer section constructions at primes $q,q'$ satisfy the hypotheses of [[lem-smith-induced-frobenius-orbit-evaluation]]. Suppose the chosen primes of $K$ above $q,q'$ have the same decomposition subgroup $D$ and hence the same decomposition field $E=K^D$. Let $p,p'$ be finite places outside $V_0$ distinct from both $q,q'$, and choose Frobenius lifts $\sigma,\sigma'\in G_F$ whose images in $G=\operatorname{Gal}(K/F)$ coincide.

Let $O$ run over the orbits of this common image on $G/D$. Choose a representative $t_O\in G_F$ and set $d_O=|O|$, $h_O=t_O^{-1}\sigma^{d_O}t_O$, $h'_O=t_O^{-1}(\sigma')^{d_O}t_O$. For Kummer cocycles $\psi_q,\psi_{q'}:G_E\to\mu_e$ representing the two generators, regard both $\psi_q(h_O)$ and $\psi_{q'}(h'_O)$ in the same coinvariant group $\mu_e/(h_O-1)\mu_e=\mu_e/(h'_O-1)\mu_e$.

The equality of these orbit values for every $O$ is equivalent to the following: for every finite $G$-module $M$ killed by $e$ and every $m\in\operatorname{Hom}(\mu_e,M)^D$, the values $B_{q,M}(m)(\sigma)$ and $B_{q',M}(m)(\sigma')$ agree in $M/(\sigma-1)M$. It suffices to test the single universal induced module $M=\mathbb Z[G]\otimes_{\mathbb Z[D]}\mu_e$ with $m(\zeta)=[1]\otimes\zeta$. This is an off-diagonal orbit-value equivalence; no diagonal-spin or analytic-distribution assertion is made.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC, Countable Choice, DC, the section data, primes, compatible decomposition subgroup and Frobenius images in the Statement.

[F1] The approximate-generator Kummer construction is natural in the finite module and supplies the stated section values. [[lem-smith-kummer-section-from-approximate-generator]]

[F2] The Frobenius value of a section at a distinct place is the sum over Frobenius orbits of the transported Kummer values in the module coinvariants, independently of the choices of cocycle and orbit representative. [[lem-smith-induced-frobenius-orbit-evaluation]]

## Proof

1.1 The common image of $\sigma$ and $\sigma'$ in $G$ gives the same orbit decomposition of $G/D$ and the same action on each $G$-module, including $\mu_e\subseteq K^\times$. Thus the two displayed Kummer values belong to the same coinvariant group for each orbit. If they agree in every orbit, [F2] makes their transported images under every equivariant $m$ agree term by term; the two section evaluations therefore agree in $M/(\sigma-1)M$. [F2, given, algebra]

1.2 Put $U=\mathbb Z[G]\otimes_{\mathbb Z[D]}\mu_e$ and $m_0(\zeta)=[1]\otimes\zeta$. The relation in the induced module makes $m_0$ $D$-equivariant. As a module for the cyclic group generated by the common image of $\sigma$, $U$ splits as the direct sum of its orbit submodules. For an orbit of length $d_O$, projection to the representative coordinate identifies its coinvariant group with $\mu_e/(h_O-1)\mu_e$: eliminate the other coordinates successively around the orbit, leaving precisely the closing relation from $\sigma^{d_O}$. [given, construct, algebra]

2.1 Under these coordinate identifications, [F2] applied to $U,m_0$ gives the tuple of orbit values, with no mixing between different orbits. Equality of the two universal section evaluations is therefore equality in every displayed coinvariant group. The universal test is included among the stated tests for all $M,m$, while step 1.1 proves the converse; naturality in [F1] is compatible with these comparisons. [F1, F2, step 1.1, step 1.2, given, algebra] ∎
