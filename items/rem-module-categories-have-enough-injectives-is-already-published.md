[[thm-module-categories-have-enough-injectives]] and
[[thm-baer-criterion-for-injective-modules]]. The present page only records the
general ambient definition
[[def-a-category-with-enough-projectives-and-with-enough-injectives]] and does
not claim the deeper theorem that every Grothendieck category has enough
injectives.
