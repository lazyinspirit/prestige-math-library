## Statement

Let $\mathfrak g$ be a complex Lie superalgebra, $A$ a unital associative superalgebra, and $\phi:\mathfrak g\to A$ a parity-preserving linear map that preserves brackets, where the bracket on $A$ is its supercommutator. There is a unique unital associative-superalgebra homomorphism $\widehat\phi:U(\mathfrak g)\to A$ with $\widehat\phi\circ\iota_{\mathfrak g}=\phi$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** $\mathfrak g,A,\phi$ as in the Statement.

[F1] [[def-universal-enveloping-superalgebra]]: $U(\mathfrak g)=T(\mathfrak g)/I$, where $I$ is generated by $x\otimes y-(-1)^{|x||y|}y\otimes x-[x,y]$.

[F2] [[def-associative-superalgebra-over-complex-numbers]]: homogeneous products add parity, and the supercommutator is $[a,b]_s=ab-(-1)^{|a||b|}ba$.

[F3] [[thm-universal-property-of-the-tensor-algebra]]: a linear map from $\mathfrak g$ into a unital associative algebra extends uniquely to a unital algebra homomorphism from $T(\mathfrak g)$, acting on pure tensors by multiplying the images of their entries.

## Proof




1.1 The tensor-algebra extension $\widetilde\phi:T(\mathfrak g)\to A$ exists uniquely by [F3]; on a homogeneous pure tensor it is $\phi(x_1)\cdots\phi(x_n)$, whose parity is $\sum_i|x_i|$, so it preserves the supergrading. [given, F2, F3, construct]


2.1 For each homogeneous pair $x,y$, bracket preservation gives $\widetilde\phi(x\otimes y-(-1)^{|x||y|}y\otimes x-[x,y])=[\phi(x),\phi(y)]_s-\phi([x,y])=0$. Since $\widetilde\phi$ is an algebra homomorphism, it kills the two-sided ideal $I$ generated by these elements. [given, F1, F2, step 1.1, algebra]


3.1 Therefore $\widetilde\phi$ factors through a unique homomorphism $\widehat\phi:T(\mathfrak g)/I\to A$, which has $\widehat\phi\iota_{\mathfrak g}=\phi$. Any other such homomorphism would pull back to an extension of $\phi$ to $T(\mathfrak g)$; uniqueness in [F3] makes it equal to $\widehat\phi$. [F1, F3, step 1.1, step 2.1] ∎
