## Statement

**False claim:** every right Noetherian ring is left Noetherian. See [[def-noetherian-ring]].

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The hypotheses and objects in the false claim.

[L1] A unital ring $R$ is left Noetherian when its left regular module ${}_R R$ is Noetherian, and right Noetherian when the right regular module $R_R$ is Noetherian. Unqualified “Noetherian ring” means left Noetherian here; the side is stated whenever both notions occur. ([[def-noetherian-ring]]).

[L2] Let $R$ be a commutative ring. Matrices of one shape are added and scaled entrywise. For $A\in M_{m\times n}(R)$ and $B\in M_{n\times p}(R)$, their product is $AB\in M_{m\times p}(R)$ with $$ (AB)_{ik}:=\sum_{j<n}a_{ij}b_{jk}. $$ The sum is the finite sum in the additive commutative monoid of $R$, and is $0$ when $n=0$. ([[def-ring-matrix-product-identity-and-transpose]]).

[L3] $(\mathbb{Q}, +, \cdot, 0, 1)$ with the operations of def-rat-operations is a field: a commutative ring with $1 \ne 0$ in which every nonzero element has a multiplicative inverse. ([[thm-rat-field]]).

## Refutation

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Let $T=\left\{\left(\begin{smallmatrix}a&q\\0&r\end{smallmatrix}\right):a\in\mathbb Z,\ q,r\in\mathbb Q\right\}$. Matrix multiplication is closed and $\operatorname{diag}(1,1)$ is its identity. As a right module, $T=e_{11}T\oplus e_{22}T$; the second summand is the simple module $\mathbb Q_{\mathbb Q}$. For a right submodule $J\le e_{11}T$, its top-left entries form an ideal $d\mathbb Z$, while its intersection with $\mathbb Qe_{12}$ is either $0$ or all of the simple right $\mathbb Q$-module $\mathbb Qe_{12}$. Choosing one element of $J$ with top-left entry $d$ shows that $J$ is generated by it and, if needed, $e_{12}$. Thus $T_T$ is Noetherian. [L1, L2, L3, given, algebra]

2.1 For $n\in\mathbb N$, the sets $L_n=\left\{\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&q\\0&0\end{smallmatrix}\right):q\in2^{-n}\mathbb Z\right\}$ are left ideals because left multiplication scales $q$ only by the integer top-left entry. The inclusions $L_0<L_1<L_2<\cdots$ are strict, witnessed by $2^{-(n+1)}e_{12}$. Hence ${}_TT$ is not Noetherian, refuting the claim. [step 1.1, given, algebra] ∎
