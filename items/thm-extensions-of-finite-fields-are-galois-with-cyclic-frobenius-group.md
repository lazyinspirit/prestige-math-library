## Statement

Let $\mathbb F_q$ be a finite field of order $q$ and let $E$ be a finite field
having $\mathbb F_q$ as a subfield, with $[E:\mathbb F_q]=n$
([[def-extension-degree-and-finite-extension]]). Then $E/\mathbb F_q$ is a finite
Galois extension ([[def-finite-galois-extension-and-galois-group]]) and

$$\operatorname{Gal}(E/\mathbb F_q)=\langle\sigma_q\rangle$$

is cyclic of order $n$, generated by the relative Frobenius
$\sigma_q\colon x\mapsto x^{q}$
([[def-relative-frobenius-of-a-finite-field-extension]]).

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** Finite fields $\mathbb F_q\subseteq E$ with $|\mathbb F_q|=q$ and $[E:\mathbb F_q]=n$, and the subgroup $G:=\langle\sigma_q\rangle$ of $\operatorname{Aut}(E/\mathbb F_q)$.

[L1] The relative Frobenius $\sigma_q(x)=x^{q}$ is an $\mathbb F_q$-automorphism of $E$ ([[def-relative-frobenius-of-a-finite-field-extension]]).

[L2] $\{\,x\in E:x^{q}=x\,\}=\mathbb F_q$, that is $E^{\langle\sigma_q\rangle}=\mathbb F_q$ ([[lem-fixed-field-of-the-relative-frobenius]]).

[L3] $|E|=q^{n}$ and $\sigma_q$ has order exactly $n$ in $\operatorname{Aut}(E/\mathbb F_q)$ ([[lem-order-of-the-relative-frobenius]]).

[L4] If $G$ is a finite group of automorphisms of $K$, then $[K:K^{G}]=|G|$ and $\operatorname{Aut}(K/K^{G})=G$ ([[thm-artin-fixed-field-degree-theorem]]).

[L5] For a finite extension $K/F$ with $G=\operatorname{Aut}(K/F)$, the conditions "$K/F$ is Galois", "$K$ is the splitting field over $F$ of a separable polynomial", "$|G|=[K:F]$" and "$K^{G}=F$" are equivalent ([[thm-finite-galois-extension-characterizations]]).

[L6] $K^{G}=\{\,x\in K:\sigma(x)=x\text{ for every }\sigma\in G\,\}$ ([[def-fixed-field-of-an-automorphism-group]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 $G=\langle\sigma_q\rangle$ is a cyclic group of automorphisms of $E$, finite of order $n$ by [L1] and [L3]. [L1, L3]

1.2 Its fixed field is $E^{G}=\mathbb F_q$ by [L2] and [L6]. [L2, L6]

2.1 Applying [L4] to the finite automorphism group $G$ of $E$ and using step 1.2, $[E:\mathbb F_q]=[E:E^{G}]=|G|=n$ and $\operatorname{Aut}(E/\mathbb F_q)=\operatorname{Aut}(E/E^{G})=G$. [step 1.1, step 1.2, L4]

3.1 Hence $|\operatorname{Aut}(E/\mathbb F_q)|=n=[E:\mathbb F_q]$, so $E/\mathbb F_q$ is Galois by [L5], and $\operatorname{Gal}(E/\mathbb F_q)=\operatorname{Aut}(E/\mathbb F_q)=\langle\sigma_q\rangle$ is cyclic of order $n$ by step 2.1 and step 1.1. At $n=1$ the group is trivial and $E=\mathbb F_q$. [step 1.1, step 2.1, L5] ∎

## Remarks

- **Separability and normality are never argued separately.** The usual route checks that $E$ is a splitting field of $t^{q^{n}}-t$ and that this polynomial has no repeated root. Routing through [[thm-artin-fixed-field-degree-theorem]] instead replaces both checks by one count: an automorphism group of order $n$ whose fixed field is $\mathbb F_q$ already forces $|{\operatorname{Aut}}|=[E:\mathbb F_q]$, which is one of the equivalent Galois conditions.
