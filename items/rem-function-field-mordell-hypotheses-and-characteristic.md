## Remark

Here the exact constant field $k$ is only required to be relatively
algebraically closed in the finitely generated field $K$; $k$ need not be
finite or algebraically closed. The word **isotrivial** allows the finite
extension $L/K$ to be inseparable, and its constant model is defined over the
exact constant field $k_L=\{a\in L:a\text{ is algebraic over }k\}$.
