## Statement

Assume AC, Countable Choice, and DC. Let $K/F$ be finite Galois with group $G$, let $e\ge2$ with $\mu_e\subseteq K$, and let $V$ contain the archimedean places, those dividing $e$, and all places ramified in $K/F$. Let $P,P',Q,Q'$ be primes of $K$ outside $V$ with decomposition groups $D_P=D_{P'}$ and $D_Q=D_{Q'}$, and suppose that the arithmetic Frobenius elements at $P,P'$ agree in $G$. Assume the primes of $F$ below $P,P'$ are distinct from those below $Q,Q'$.

Put $E=K^{D_P}$ and $J=K^{D_Q}$. Give $P,P',Q,Q'$ approximate generators $\alpha_P,\alpha_{P'}\in E^\times$ and $\beta_Q,\beta_{Q'}\in J^\times$, respectively, with valuation $1$ modulo $e$ at the designated contracted prime and $0$ modulo $e$ at every other finite prime outside $V$. Use these generators for their natural Kummer ramification sections.

For each orbit of the common Frobenius element on $G/D_Q$, choose a representative $t\in G$. Put $L_t=t^{-1}E\,J$, let $m_t=|\mu_e(L_t)|$, and let $\mathfrak r_t=(t^{-1}P)\cap L_t$ and $\mathfrak r'_t=(t^{-1}P')\cap L_t$. The Hilbert symbols at these primes are defined with $H_{m_t}(a,b)=\operatorname{rec}(b)(a^{1/m_t})/a^{1/m_t}$. Then the following conditions are equivalent:

1. For every $g\in G$, the source symbols $$H_{|\mu_e(EgJ)|}(\alpha_P,g\beta_Q)_{P\cap EgJ}=H_{|\mu_e(EgJ)|}(\alpha_{P'},g\beta_{Q'})_{P'\cap EgJ}$$ agree. Equivalently, for every orbit representative $t$,
$$H_{m_t}(t^{-1}\alpha_P,\beta_Q)_{\mathfrak r_t}=H_{m_t}(t^{-1}\alpha_{P'},\beta_{Q'})_{\mathfrak r'_t}.$$
2. For every finite $G$-module $M$ killed by $e$ and every $m\in\operatorname{Hom}(\mu_e,M)^{D_Q}$, the two section evaluations $B_{Q,M}(m)(\operatorname{Frob}_P)$ and $B_{Q',M}(m)(\operatorname{Frob}_{P'})$ agree in the common Frobenius coinvariant group.

The single universal induced-module test suffices in condition 2. These are off-diagonal symbols under the displayed matching hypotheses; no diagonal-spin or analytic-distribution assertion is made.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC, Countable Choice, DC, the extension, sets, four primes and approximate generators in the Statement.

[F1] Decomposition and inertia groups intersect with the Galois group in a tower; the decomposition-fixed field has a unique prime above the distinguished contraction, and its contraction has residue degree one over the base. [[thm-decomposition-and-inertia-in-towers]] [[thm-decomposition-and-inertia-fixed-fields]]

[F2] At an unramified prime, the decomposition group maps isomorphically to the cyclic residue-field Galois group generated by arithmetic Frobenius. [[thm-decomposition-inertia-exact-sequence]]

[F3] For local valuations $1$ and $0$ modulo $e$, the Kummer Frobenius coinvariant is carried by the $e/m$ power map to the inverse Hilbert symbol. [[lem-smith-off-diagonal-kummer-hilbert-comparison]]

[F4] Equality of all off-diagonal Kummer orbit values is equivalent to equality of all finite-module section Frobenius evaluations; the universal induced module suffices. [[lem-smith-universal-orbit-symbol-section-equivalence]]

## Proof

1.1 Fix an orbit representative $t$ and put $H_t=t^{-1}D_Pt\cap D_Q$. By Galois correspondence $L_t=K^{H_t}$. The group $H_t$ is contained in the decomposition group of $t^{-1}P$, so [F1] identifies it with the decomposition group of that prime for $K/L_t$. The same holds for $P'$ because $D_{P'}=D_P$. These primes are unramified. If $d$ is the orbit length, the common Frobenius image gives an element $t^{-1}\operatorname{Frob}_P^d t$ generating $H_t$: its powers are precisely the intersection of the cyclic group $t^{-1}D_Pt$ with $D_Q$. By [F2], its action on $\mu_e$ is the residue Frobenius at $\mathfrak r_t$, and the same holds at $\mathfrak r'_t$. Thus, if $q_t$ is either residue-field cardinality, $$\gcd(e,q_t-1)=|\mu_e^{H_t}|=|\mu_e(L_t)|=m_t.$$ [F1, F2, given, algebra]

2.1 By [F1], $P$ is the unique prime of $K$ above its contraction to $E$; consequently the valuation of $t^{-1}\alpha_P$ at $\mathfrak r_t$ remains $1$ modulo $e$, because the extension is unramified. The element $\beta_Q$ has valuation $0$ modulo $e$ there: its contraction to $J$ lies above the prime of $F$ below $P$, which is distinct from that below $Q$. The corresponding statements hold for $P',Q'$. Apply [F3] at the two local fields. Under the same isomorphism from the orbit Kummer coinvariant to $\mu_{m_t}$, their orbit values become the inverses of the two displayed Hilbert symbols. Since inversion is bijective, their equality is equivalent to equality of those orbit values. [F1, F3, step 1.1, given, algebra]

3.1 The field isomorphism $t:L_t\to EtJ$ takes the displayed $L_t$ Hilbert symbols to the source symbols at $P$ and $P'$. The source symbols are unchanged by right multiplication of $t$ by $D_Q$, since $D_Q$ fixes $J$ and both $\beta$ generators. Left multiplication by $D_P$ fixes $E$, both $\alpha$ generators, and both primes $P,P'$; functoriality of the Hilbert symbol makes it act by the same automorphism on both sides. Since $D_P$ is generated by the common Frobenius, equality for the selected double-coset representatives is equivalent to equality for all $g\in G$. Now apply step 2.1 on every orbit. The resulting Kummer-value equalities are equivalent by [F4] to equality of the section evaluations for every $M,m$, and [F4] also gives the universal induced-module test. This proves both implications without a diagonal or density claim. [F2, F4, step 1.1, step 2.1, given, algebra] ∎
