## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $K$ be a smooth oriented knot, let $X_K=S^3\setminus K$, and let $q\ge2$. Let $A_K=H_1(\widetilde X_K;\mathbb Z)$ be the one-variable Alexander module with the positive-meridian deck action $t$. Then, as abelian groups,
$$
H_1(\Sigma_q(K);\mathbb Z)\cong A_K/(t^q-1)A_K.
$$

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The Axiom of Choice, a smooth oriented knot $K$, its chosen exterior $E_K$, positive meridian $\mu$, and an integer $q\ge2$.

[A1] Full AC is the choice-function axiom. [[def-axiom-of-choice]]

[F1] The connected cover $E_q\to E_K$ is classified by the positive-meridian homomorphism modulo $q$; its filling kills the closed lift of $\mu^q$. [[def-cyclic-branched-cover-of-a-knot]]

[F2] The inclusion $E_K\hookrightarrow X_K$ is a strong deformation retraction equivalence and carries the positive meridian to the positive meridian. [[lem-knot-tubular-exterior-and-boundary]]

[F3] The module $A_K$ is $H_1$ of the connected infinite cyclic cover of $X_K$ classified by total linking, with $t$ acting by the positive-meridian deck transformation. [[def-one-variable-alexander-module-of-an-oriented-link]]

[F4] For a path-connected space, the first Hurewicz map identifies $H_1$ with the abelianization of its fundamental group. [[prop-the-first-hurewicz-map-in-degree-one-is-abelianization]]

[F5] Connected covers of a path-connected, locally path-connected, semilocally simply connected base are classified by subgroups of its fundamental group. [[thm-classification-of-connected-covering-spaces]]

[F6] Mayer–Vietoris is exact for an open cover, and a deformation retraction induces an isomorphism on singular homology. [[thm-mayer-vietoris-sequence-in-singular-homology]], [[prop-singular-homology-is-invariant-under-deformation-retracts]]

## Proof

**Given:** The data in the Statement and Facts & Assumptions. Choose a basepoint on $T_K$ and let $G=\pi_1(E_K)$, with $s\in G$ the based positive meridian loop. Write $\phi:G\to\mathbb Z$ for the homomorphism with $\phi(s)=1$, and put $N=\ker\phi$.

1.1 By [F2], inclusion of the compact exterior into the open complement induces an isomorphism on fundamental groups and identifies the positive meridian classes. The total-linking homomorphism in [F3] therefore corresponds to $\phi$; by [F4], $A_K$ is isomorphic to $N^{\mathrm{ab}}$ with the action induced by conjugation by a positive meridian, up to replacing that action by its inverse according to the left-versus-right lift convention. [A1, F2, F3, F4, given]

2.1 Put $G_q=\ker(\phi\bmod q)$. For $g\in G_q$, write $\phi(g)=qk$ and set $n=g s^{-qk}\in N$. Then $g=n s^{qk}$, and the expression is unique because $\phi(s^{qk})=qk$. Thus $G_q$ is the split extension of $N$ by the infinite cyclic group generated by $s^q$, acting on $N$ by conjugation. By [F5] and [F1], $G_q$ is the fundamental group of $E_q$. [F1, F5, step 1.1, construct]

3.1 Abelianizing this split extension kills the commutators in $N$ and the relations $s^qns^{-q}=n$ for $n\in N$. By [F3], those relations impose $(t^q-1)A_K=0$ on $N^{\mathrm{ab}}$; if the inverse deck convention is used, $t^{-q}-1=-t^{-q}(t^q-1)$ generates the same submodule. The homomorphism $G_q\to\mathbb Z$, $g\mapsto\phi(g)/q$, splits by $1\mapsto s^q$, so $H_1(E_q;\mathbb Z)\cong A_K/(t^q-1)A_K\oplus\mathbb Z[s^q]$. [F3, F4, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 The boundary of $E_q$ is a torus cover with basis the lift $\widetilde\mu$ of $\mu^q$ and a lifted longitude. Thicken $E_q$ and the filling solid torus by collars to form an open cover. By [F6], its Mayer–Vietoris map presents $H_1(\Sigma_q(K))$ as $H_1(E_q)$ modulo $[\widetilde\mu]$: the longitude lift maps to the core generator of the attached solid torus and eliminates that generator, while the filling meridian maps to zero there. The class $[\widetilde\mu]$ is $[s^q]$, the generator of the displayed free summand in step 3.1. Quotienting by it gives the stated isomorphism. [F1, F6, step 3.1, construct] ∎

## Remarks

The quotient by $t^q-1$ is unchanged if the positive deck action is represented by its inverse, since $t^{-q}-1$ differs from $t^q-1$ by the unit $-t^{-q}$.

The group extension and filling relation are shown below. The proof uses the element calculation in steps 2.1–4.1, not the diagram.

```tikzcd
\begin{tikzcd}
1 \arrow[r] & N \arrow[r,hook] \arrow[d,equal] & G_q \arrow[r,"\phi/q"] \arrow[d,hook] & \mathbb Z \arrow[r] \arrow[d,"k\mapsto qk"] & 1 \\
1 \arrow[r] & N \arrow[r,hook] & G \arrow[r,"\phi"] & \mathbb Z \arrow[r] & 1
\end{tikzcd}
```
