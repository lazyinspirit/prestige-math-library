## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice as required by the smooth-locally-free theorem.
Let $k$ be a field, let $C$ be a smooth integral curve over $k$, let $p$ be a
closed point whose residue field $\kappa(p)$ is finite separable over $k$, and
let $t$ be a uniformizer of the local ring $\mathcal O_{C,p}$. Write
$\Omega^1_{C/k}$ for the sheaf of relative differentials. Then:

1. the stalk $\Omega^1_{C/k,p}$ is a free $\mathcal O_{C,p}$-module of rank
   one;
2. the differential $\mathrm dt$ is a basis of $\Omega^1_{C/k,p}$: the
   residue-cotangent isomorphism identifies $\mathfrak m_p/\mathfrak m_p^2$
   with $\Omega^1_{C/k,p}\otimes_{\mathcal O_{C,p}}\kappa(p)$ carrying the
   class of $t$ to $\mathrm dt\otimes1$, and the class of $t$ generates
   $\mathfrak m_p/\mathfrak m_p^2$;
3. at the generic point $\eta$ the element $\mathrm dt$ is a $k(C)$-basis of
   $\Omega^1_{k(C)/k}$, so every rational differential has a unique expression
   $\omega=a\,\mathrm dt$ with $a\in k(C)$;
4. the $\mathfrak m_p$-adic completion of $\Omega^1_{C/k,p}$ is a free module
   of rank one over the completed local ring $\widehat{\mathcal O}_{C,p}$,
   with basis the image of $\mathrm dt$.

In particular, when $k$ is perfect the hypotheses on $\kappa(p)$ hold at every
closed point $p$ of $C$: every algebraic extension of a perfect field is
separable and $\kappa(p)$ is finite over $k$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** a field $k$, a smooth integral curve $C$ over $k$, a closed point
$p\in C$ with $\kappa(p)$ finite separable over $k$, and a uniformizer $t$ of
$\mathcal O_{C,p}$.

[F1] A smooth curve over $k$ is a $k$-scheme that is geometrically integral,
separated and of finite type, of chain dimension one, with smooth structure
morphism $C\to\operatorname{Spec}k$
([[def-algebraic-curve-over-field]]).

[F2] Since $C\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ is smooth at $p$, the sheaf of relative
differentials $\Omega^1_{C/k}$ is locally free of finite rank on a
neighbourhood of $p$, so its stalk $\Omega^1_{C/k,p}$ at the local ring
$A=\mathcal O_{C,p}$ is a finite free $A$-module; the stalk is the
$\mathcal O_{C,p}$-module of the sheaf
$\Omega^1_{C/k}$ ([[thm-differentials-smooth-locally-free]],
[[def-sheaf-relative-differentials]]).

[F3] The residue-cotangent sequence at the Noetherian local $k$-algebra
$A=\mathcal O_{C,p}$: because $\kappa(p)$ is finite separable over $k$, the
map
$$\mathfrak m_p/\mathfrak m_p^2\longrightarrow\Omega^1_{C/k,p}\otimes_A\kappa(p),\qquad[x]\longmapsto\mathrm dx\otimes1,$$
is an isomorphism of $\kappa(p)$-vector spaces
([[lem-ag-separable-residue-cotangent-sequence]]).

[F4] The local ring $A=\mathcal O_{C,p}$ of the smooth curve at the closed
point is a Noetherian regular local ring of dimension one, hence a discrete
valuation ring whose maximal ideal is generated by the uniformizer $t$, so
$\mathfrak m_p=(t)$ and every nonzero element of $A$ is a unit times a power
of $t$ ([[thm-local-ring-smooth-curve-dvr]]).

[F5] Localization commutes with differentials: for a ring map $A\to B$, a
multiplicative subset $U\subseteq B$, the canonical map
$U^{-1}\Omega_{B/A}\to\Omega_{U^{-1}B/A}$ is an isomorphism; if $B$ is a
domain with fraction field $K(B)$ then $\Omega_{B/A}\otimes_BK(B)\cong\Omega_{K(B)/A}$
([[lem-differentials-localization]]). For the function field $k(C)$ of the
integral curve, $k(C)/k$ is a finitely generated separably generated field
extension of transcendence degree one, so $\Omega^1_{k(C)/k}$ is free of rank
one ([[thm-ag-field-differentials-separable-rank]],
[[def-algebraic-curve-over-field]]).

[F6] Let $R$ be a Noetherian commutative ring, $I\subseteq R$ an ideal and $M$
a finitely generated $R$-module. The $I$-adic completion of $M$ is
$\widehat M=\varprojlim_nM/I^nM$, and the canonical map
$M\otimes_R\widehat R\to\widehat M$, $m\otimes(r_n)_n\mapsto(mr_n\bmod I^nM)_n$,
is an isomorphism ([[def-adic-completion-of-a-module]],
[[thm-completion-as-extension-of-scalars]]).

[F7] If $k$ is perfect then every algebraic extension of $k$ is separable;
closed points of the finite-type $k$-scheme $C$ have residue fields finite
over $k$ ([[cor-algebraic-extensions-of-perfect-fields-are-separable]],
[[def-perfect-field]], [[def-algebraic-curve-over-field]]).

[F8] The Axiom of Choice: every family of nonempty sets has a choice function
([[def-axiom-of-choice]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct; compare the free stalk with the cotangent space
through the separable residue sequence, then localize and complete the basis.

1.1 (Set-up.) The curve $C$ is geometrically integral of chain dimension one and $C\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ is smooth [F1]; the local ring $A=\mathcal O_{C,p}$ is a Noetherian discrete valuation ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m_p=(t)$ [F4]; and the residue field $\kappa(p)$ is finite separable over $k$ by hypothesis. [F1, F4, given]

2.1 (The stalk is finite free.) By [F2] the stalk $\Omega^1_{C/k,p}$ is a finite free $A$-module, say of rank $r\ge0$, and reduction modulo $\mathfrak m_p$ identifies $\Omega^1_{C/k,p}\otimes_A\kappa(p)$ with $(\Omega^1_{C/k,p})/\mathfrak m_p\Omega^1_{C/k,p}\cong\kappa(p)^{\oplus r}$. [F2, step 1.1, algebra]

2.2 (The cotangent isomorphism.) By [F3] the assignment $[x]\mapsto\mathrm dx\otimes1$ is an isomorphism $\mathfrak m_p/\mathfrak m_p^2\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\Omega^1_{C/k,p}\otimes_A\kappa(p)$, and it carries the class $[t]$ to $\mathrm dt\otimes1$. [F3, step 1.1]

2.3 (The uniformizer spans the cotangent space.) Since $\mathfrak m_p=(t)$ by [F4], every element of $\mathfrak m_p$ is $at$ for some $a\in A$, and $\mathfrak m_p^2=(t^2)$, so the $\kappa(p)$-vector space $\mathfrak m_p/\mathfrak m_p^2=(t)/(t^2)$ is one dimensional with basis the class $[t]$. [F4, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 (Rank one and the basis.) The isomorphism of step 2.2 carries the basis $[t]$ of the one-dimensional space $\mathfrak m_p/\mathfrak m_p^2$ of step 2.3 to $\mathrm dt\otimes1$, so $\mathrm dt\otimes1$ is a $\kappa(p)$-basis of $\Omega^1_{C/k,p}\otimes_A\kappa(p)$; comparing with step 2.1 gives $r=1$, so the free module $\Omega^1_{C/k,p}$ has rank one and assertion 1 holds. Write $\Omega^1_{C/k,p}=Ae$ and $\mathrm dt=ae$ with $a\in A$; the reduction $\bar a\in\kappa(p)$ is the coefficient of $\mathrm dt\otimes1$ in the basis $e\otimes1$, hence $\bar a\ne0$, so $a$ is a unit of the local ring $A$ and $\mathrm dt$ is also a basis, which is assertion 2. [F2, F3, step 2.1, step 2.2, step 2.3, algebra]

4.1 (The generic point.) Let $\eta$ be the generic point of $C$ and $k(C)=\mathcal O_{C,\eta}$ its function field, so that $k(C)$ is the fraction field of the domain $A$. Localizing the free rank-one module $\Omega^1_{C/k,p}=A\,\mathrm dt$ at the zero prime and applying the localization isomorphism of [F5] gives $\Omega^1_{C/k,p}\otimes_Ak(C)\cong\Omega^1_{k(C)/k}=k(C)\,\mathrm dt$, so $\mathrm dt$ is a $k(C)$-basis and every rational differential is uniquely $a\,\mathrm dt$ with $a\in k(C)$; this is assertion 3, and it agrees with the free rank-one statement of [F5]. [F5, step 3.1, algebra]

4.2 (Completion.) The $A$-module $\Omega^1_{C/k,p}$ is finitely generated and $A$ is Noetherian by [F4], so [F6] applies with $I=\mathfrak m_p$ and gives an isomorphism $\Omega^1_{C/k,p}\otimes_A\widehat A\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\widehat{\Omega^1_{C/k,p}}$ carrying $\mathrm dt\otimes1$ to the image of $\mathrm dt$; since $\Omega^1_{C/k,p}=A\,\mathrm dt$ is free of rank one, its completion is $\widehat A(\mathrm dt\otimes1)$, a free rank-one $\widehat A$-module with basis the image of $\mathrm dt$. This is assertion 4. [F4, F6, step 3.1, algebra]

5.1 (Perfect residue fields and conclusion.) If $k$ is perfect then every algebraic extension of $k$ is separable by [F7], and every closed point $p$ of the finite-type $k$-scheme $C$ has finite residue field $\kappa(p)$ by [F7], so the hypotheses on $\kappa(p)$ hold at every closed point of a smooth integral curve over a perfect field; assertions 1, 2, 3 and 4 are established, and choice [F8] is inherited from the smooth-locally-free theorem [F2] in step 2.1, the DVR theorem [F4] in step 1.1, and the completion theorem [F6] in step 4.2, together with their suppliers. No additional choice is made locally. [F2, F4, F6, F7, F8, step 3.1, step 4.1, step 4.2] ∎
