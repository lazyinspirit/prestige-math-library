## Statement

Assume Countable Choice. Let $K$ be a nonarchimedean local field and fix an algebraic closure with the absolute value extending that of $K$. Let $f\in K[X]$ be monic, irreducible and separable of degree $d\geq1$, and let $r>0$. There is $\delta>0$ such that every monic $g\in K[X]$ of degree $d$ with

$$\|g-f\|:=\max_{0\leq j<d}|g_j-f_j|<\delta$$

is irreducible and separable. For every root $\alpha$ of $f$ there is a root $\beta$ of $g$ with $|\beta-\alpha|<r$ and $K(\alpha)=K(\beta)$ inside the fixed closure. The assertion includes positive characteristic; only separability, not characteristic zero, is assumed.

## Statement references

[Fesenko and Vostokov, *Local Fields and Their Extensions*, Chapter II section 2, Exercise 6, printed p.49](https://ivanfesenko.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/vol.pdf)

[Milne, *Algebraic Number Theory*, Proposition 7.61 and Corollary 7.62, printed p.132](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/ANT.pdf)

The first source states stability for irreducible separable polynomials over a Henselian discretely valued field. The present statement specializes to complete local fields and spells out an arbitrary root-distance bound. Milne's surrounding discussion assumes characteristic zero; it is not the attribution for the positive-characteristic clause.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** Countable Choice, the valued closure, $K,f,d,r$ in the Statement.

[A1] [[def-countable-choice]] supplies the sequence choices in the finite-dimensional completeness and unique-absolute-value suppliers.

[F1] [[def-local-field-valuation-ring-uniformizer-and-residue-field]]: $K$ is complete with a nontrivial discrete absolute value, hence has nonzero elements of arbitrarily small absolute value.

[F2] [[thm-unique-extension-of-a-nonarchimedean-absolute-value]] and [[thm-finite-dimensional-norm-equivalence-over-a-complete-valued-field]]: every finite extension is complete with its unique extending absolute value, and every embedding over $K$ between finite subextensions is isometric.

[F3] [[lem-hensel-factor-lifting-over-a-complete-valued-field]]: for a polynomial with integral coefficients and coprime residue factorization whose first factor is monic, the factorization lifts with that first factor monic of the same degree. The original polynomial need not be monic.

[F4] [[thm-krasner-lemma-for-complete-nonarchimedean-fields]]: if $\alpha$ is separable and $\beta$ is closer to it than any distinct conjugate over $K$, then $K(\alpha)\subseteq K(\beta)$.

[F5] [[thm-simple-algebraic-extension-quotient-power-basis-and-degree]]: an algebraic generator with minimal polynomial of degree $d$ generates a degree-$d$ extension.

[F6] [[thm-finite-galois-extension-characterizations]]: a finite extension generated by all roots of a separable polynomial is Galois. The splitting field here is the subfield of the given closure generated by the finitely many roots.

## Proof

1.1 Choose a root $\alpha$ and put $E=K(\alpha)$. It is a complete degree-$d$ extension by [F2, F5]. Separability gives $c=f'(\alpha)\ne0$. Choose a positive $s\leq r$ smaller than every distance from $\alpha$ to a distinct conjugate, omitting these finitely many constraints when $d=1$. Write $f(\alpha+Z)=cZ+\sum_{j=2}^d a_jZ^j$. Choose $b\in K^\times$ with $|b|<s$ and $|a_jb^{j-1}/c|<1$ for every $j\geq2$. Powers of a uniformizer give such a $b$. The coefficients here are those of formal substitution, so no division by factorials is involved. [A1, F1, F2, F5, given, choose, algebra]

2.1 For monic $g$ sufficiently coefficient-close to $f$, put $c_g=g'(\alpha)$ and write $g(\alpha+Z)=g(\alpha)+c_gZ+\sum_{j=2}^d a_{g,j}Z^j$. Finite sums and products are continuous. Thus there is $\delta>0$ such that $\|g-f\|<\delta$ implies $|c_g-c|<|c|$, $|g(\alpha)|<|bc|$, and $|a_{g,j}b^{j-1}/c_g|<1$ for all $j\geq2$. In particular $|c_g|=|c|\ne0$. The polynomial $Q(Y)=g(\alpha+bY)/(bc_g)$ then has integral coefficients in $E$, constant coefficient in its maximal ideal, linear coefficient $1$, and every higher coefficient in that ideal. Its reduction is $Y$. [F2, step 1.1, construct, algebra]

3.1 Apply [F3] in the complete field $E$ to the residue factorization $Y=Y\cdot1$. It lifts to $Q(Y)=(Y-y)H(Y)$ with $y$ in the maximal ideal of $E$. Hence $\beta=\alpha+by$ lies in $E$, satisfies $g(\beta)=0$, and has $|\beta-\alpha|<|b|<s\leq r$. By [F4] and the choice of $s$, $K(\alpha)\subseteq K(\beta)$. The reverse inclusion holds because $\beta\in E$. Thus $K(\beta)=E$ has degree $d$, so the degree-$d$ monic $g$ is the minimal polynomial of $\beta$ and is irreducible. Since $E/K$ is separable, $g$ is separable. [A1, F2, F3, F4, F5, step 1.1, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 The finite Galois splitting field of $f$ contains $E$ and $\beta$. For any other root $\alpha'$ of $f$, the map $K(\alpha)\to K(\alpha')$ sending $\alpha$ to $\alpha'$ extends to an automorphism $\sigma$ of this splitting field. Explicitly, adjoin the finitely many remaining roots in succession and extend the embedding by choosing a root of each image minimal polynomial. Those image polynomials divide $f$, which splits in this field. The resulting endomorphism is onto by finite dimension. This is a finite construction, requiring no additional choice axiom. Put $\beta'=\sigma\beta$. Then $g(\beta')=0$, $K(\beta')=K(\alpha')$, and [F2] gives $|\beta'-\alpha'|=|\beta-\alpha|<r$. The same $\delta$ therefore works for every root. This proves all assertions, including $d=1$ and positive characteristic. [F2, F6, step 3.1, algebra] ∎
