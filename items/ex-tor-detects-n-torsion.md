## Example

For $M=\mathbb Z/12$ and $n=8$, $\operatorname{Tor}^{\mathbb Z}_1(\mathbb Z/8,M)\cong\mathbb Z/4$.

## Verification

**Given:** the multiplication-by-$8$ complex on $\mathbb Z/12$.

1.1 Its kernel consists of residues $x$ with $8x\equiv0\pmod{12}$. [given]

2.1 These are $0,3,6,9$, the subgroup generated by $3$. [step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 The subgroup has order $4$, which exhibits exactly the $8$-torsion detected by Tor. [step 2.1, algebra] ∎
