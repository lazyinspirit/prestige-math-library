## Statement

In the Feferman–Levy model, for every $m<\omega$ there is a surjection
$\aleph_{m+1}^V\twoheadrightarrow R_m$. In particular
$|R_m|\le\aleph_{m+1}^V$. Jech's sharper bookkeeping gives equality; only the
displayed upper bound is used below.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A ground model $V\models\mathrm{ZFC}+\mathrm{GCH}$ and the layer
$R_m$ for one fixed $m<\omega$. Cardinal arithmetic in this proof is performed
in $V$.

[F1] [[def-feferman-levy-real-layers]] defines $S_m$ as the set of
$\omega$-sequences of coefficients from $B_m$ and $R_m$ as its interpretation.

[F2] [[lem-feferman-levy-fixed-boolean-values-come-from-initial-layers]] says
that $B_m$ is generated by restrictions to the first $m$ collapse layers.

[F3] [[def-axiom-of-choice]] records the ground-model Choice used to compare
the cardinals of the coding sets; no Choice assertion about $N$ is made.

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct coding and interpretation of one bounded-support enumeration.

1.1 The set $P_{<m}=\{p\mathbin{\upharpoonright}m:p\in P\}$ has ground cardinal at most $\aleph_m^V$: its elements are finite functions using ordinals below the finitely many cardinals $\aleph_n^V$ for $n<m$ (and for $m=0$ it is a singleton). Every regular open generated by $P_{<m}$ is a subset of $P_{<m}$, so F2 gives $|B_m|^V\le 2^{\aleph_m^V}=\aleph_{m+1}^V$. This deliberately coarse estimate covers $m=0$ uniformly. [F2, F3]

2.1 By F1, a member of $S_m$ is coded by a function $\omega\to B_m$. In ground ZFC and GCH, step 1.1 gives $|S_m|^V\le(2^{\aleph_m^V})^{\aleph_0}=2^{\aleph_m^V}=\aleph_{m+1}^V$. Since the constantly-zero name belongs to $S_m$, ground Choice supplies a fixed surjection $e_m:\aleph_{m+1}^V\twoheadrightarrow S_m$. [F1, F3, step 1.1]

3.1 Form the canonical name $\dot e_m^*=\{\langle\langle\check\xi,\dot x\rangle^\bullet,1_B\rangle:\xi<\aleph_{m+1}^V\text{ and }\dot x=e_m(\xi)\}$. Every value name belongs to $S_m$ and is fixed by $H_m$, so $H_m$ fixes $\dot e_m^*$ and all its subnames. Thus it is hereditarily symmetric. Its interpretation is the function $\xi\mapsto e_m(\xi)_G$, whose range is exactly $R_m$ by F1. Hence $N$ contains the required surjection. [F1, step 2.1]

4.1 The ground use of Choice and GCH occurred only in steps 1.1–2.1 to obtain the single coded enumeration $e_m$. Step 3.1 puts its interpretation in $N$ without choosing enumerations for a family of arbitrary sets. This proves the asserted internal bound. [F3, step 3.1] ∎
