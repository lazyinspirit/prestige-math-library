## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]). If $X=X_1\oplus X_2$
is a direct sum decomposition in $\mathcal O$ and $X$ is Verma-filtered
([[def-verma-flag-and-its-multiplicities]]), then both $X_1$ and $X_2$ are
Verma-filtered.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The Axiom of Choice, a Verma-filtered object $X=X_1\oplus X_2$ with a fixed Verma flag of length $m$, and an enumeration of the two summands.

[F1] If $X=X_1\oplus X_2$ and $\Delta(\nu)\hookrightarrow X_1$ is a subobject, then $X/\Delta(\nu)\cong(X_1/\Delta(\nu))\oplus X_2$. The weights of $X$ are the union of the weights of the factors of any Verma flag, and a maximal weight of $X$ is the label of some factor ([[def-verma-flag-and-its-multiplicities]]).

[F2] If $\nu$ is maximal among the weights of a Verma-filtered object $X$ with a flag of length $m$ and $v\ne0$ is a vector of weight $\nu$, then $v$ is a highest-weight vector, the induced homomorphism $\Delta(\nu)\to X$ is injective, and the cokernel has a Verma flag of length $m-1$ ([[lem-maximal-weight-verma-peels-off-a-standard-filtration]]).

[F3] If $0\to A\to E\to B\to0$ is exact and $A$ and $B$ are Verma-filtered, then $E$ is Verma-filtered: concatenating a flag of $A$ with the preimages of a flag of $B$ gives a flag of $E$. [F1]

## Proof

**Proof technique:** induction on the flag length, peeling a maximal-weight Verma that lies in one of the two summands.

1.1 If $m=0$ then $X=0$, so $X_1=X_2=0$ and both summands are Verma-filtered with the empty flag. [F1, base]

1.2 Let $m\ge1$. The finite set of labels of the fixed flag has a maximal element $\nu$; every weight of $X$ lies below one of those labels, so a weight strictly above $\nu$ would force a flag label strictly above it. Thus $\nu$ is a maximal element of the set of weights of $X$, with no greatest-label assumption. By [F1] the weight $\nu$ is the label of some factor of the fixed flag: it lies in $\mu_i-Q^+$ for a factor $\Delta(\mu_i)$, so $\nu\le\mu_i$, while $\mu_i$ is a weight of $X$ and $\nu$ is maximal, so comparability $\nu\le\mu_i$ forces $\mu_i=\nu$. Choose a nonzero vector $v$ of weight $\nu$; writing $v=v_1+v_2$ with $v_i\in X_i$, some $v_i\ne0$ is again a maximal-weight vector, and after exchanging the names of the summands we may assume $v\in X_1$. Assume as induction hypothesis that every Verma-filtered direct sum with a flag of length $m-1$ has Verma-filtered summands. [F1, given, ih]

2.1 By [F2] the induced homomorphism $\Delta(\nu)\to X$ is injective with image in $X_1$ (its image is the submodule generated by $v$) and cokernel Verma-filtered with a flag of length $m-1$. Since $\Delta(\nu)\subseteq X_1$, [F1] gives $X/\Delta(\nu)\cong(X_1/\Delta(\nu))\oplus X_2$; the induction hypothesis applies to this Verma-filtered direct sum of flag length $m-1$, so $X_2$ and $X_1/\Delta(\nu)$ are Verma-filtered. From the exact sequence $0\to\Delta(\nu)\to X_1\to X_1/\Delta(\nu)\to0$, both ends Verma-filtered, [F3] makes $X_1$ Verma-filtered. [F1, F2, F3, step 1.1, step 1.2]

3.1 By induction on $m$, steps 1.1 and 2.1 show that whenever $X=X_1\oplus X_2$ is Verma-filtered, both summands are Verma-filtered. [step 1.1, step 2.1, discharge-induction: step 2.1] ∎
