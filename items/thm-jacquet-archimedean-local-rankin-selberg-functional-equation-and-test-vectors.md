## Statement

Let $K=\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$, $n\geq1$, and let $\pi,\pi'$ be irreducible admissible generic representations of $\mathrm{GL}_{n+1}(K)$ and $\mathrm{GL}_{n}(K)$ in the Casselman–Wallach class, with Whittaker models formed using continuous Whittaker functionals. Fix a nontrivial continuous unitary additive character $\psi$ of $K$, and take Whittaker functions $W,W'$ for $\psi,\psi^{-1}$. Write $Z(s,W,W')$ for the adjacent-rank local integral, $w_m$ for the anti-diagonal matrix in $\mathrm{GL}_m(K)$, $\widetilde W(g)=W(w_{n+1}{}^tg^{-1})$, and $\widetilde W'(h)=W'(w_n{}^th^{-1})$. Let $\gamma(s,\pi\times\pi',\psi)$ be the local gamma factor of the tensor product of their Weil parameters, and let $\omega_\pi,\omega_{\pi'}$ be their central characters. Then $Z(s,W,W')$ converges absolutely for $\operatorname{Re}(s)$ sufficiently large, extends meromorphically to $s\in\mathbb C$ as a holomorphic multiple of the archimedean local factor $L(s,\pi\times\pi')$, and satisfies

$$Z(1-s,\widetilde W,\widetilde W')=\omega_\pi(-1)^n\omega_{\pi'}(-1)\gamma(s,\pi\times\pi',\psi)Z(s,W,W').$$

For $K_\mathrm{max}=O(n+1)$ when $K=\mathbb R$ and $K_\mathrm{max}=U(n+1)$ when $K=\mathbb C$, the local factor is generated by the adjacent-rank integrals with finite-$K_\mathrm{max}$-type Whittaker data; equivalently, a finite linear combination of such integrals equals $L(s,\pi\times\pi')$. The integral family is bounded at infinity in vertical strips away from poles.

## Analytic prerequisite and Fourier convention

[[lem-archimedean-tate-zeta-integral-and-fourier-functional-equation]] gives a separate local proof of the scalar real/complex Tate integrals for all continuous quasicharacters, including full Schwartz continuation and both Fourier signs. Here Jacquet's convention is the negative kernel $\widehat\Phi(x)=\int\Phi(y)\psi(-\langle x,y\rangle)\,dy$, and the Weil-parameter gamma factor is taken with that same convention; for a real sign character and $\psi(x)=e^{2\pi ix}$ its scalar epsilon is $-i$. The positive-kernel principal-series convention must be transported explicitly before comparing factors. This scalar prerequisite proves neither the higher-rank Whittaker estimates and analytic induction nor the full finite-type generation theorem. The original full Statement remains an unresolved local proof obligation. No general local Langlands or global sign-compatibility proof is inferred.
