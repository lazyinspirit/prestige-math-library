## Definition

For $\lambda\in\mathfrak h^*$, the **Verma module** is the induced
$\mathfrak g$-module

$$M(\lambda):=U(\mathfrak g)\otimes_{U(\mathfrak b)}\mathbb C_\lambda,$$

where $U(\mathfrak g)$ is the quotient algebra of
[[def-universal-enveloping-algebra-as-a-tensor-quotient]] and
$\mathbb C_\lambda$ is [[def-one-dimensional-borel-module-of-weight-lambda]].
Write $v_\lambda:=1\otimes c_\lambda$.  Thus $M(\lambda)$ is the quotient of
$U(\mathfrak g)$ by the left ideal generated by $x$ for $x\in\mathfrak n^+$
and $h-\lambda(h)$ for $h\in\mathfrak h$; in particular,
$\mathfrak n^+v_\lambda=0$ and $hv_\lambda=\lambda(h)v_\lambda$.
