## Definition

A **measurable space** is a pair $(X,\mathcal A)$ consisting of a set $X$ and a
sigma-algebra $\mathcal A$ on $X$ ([[def-sigma-algebra]]). The members of
$\mathcal A$ are the **measurable subsets** of $X$.
