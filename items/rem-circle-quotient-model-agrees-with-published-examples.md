The quotient in [[def-circle-as-real-line-mod-integers]] is the same
$\mathbb R/\mathbb Z$ used in the examples on
`subspaces-products-and-quotients-examples` and
`covering-spaces-and-lifting-examples`: in each case two reals are identified
exactly when their difference is an integer, the canonical projection sends
$x$ to $[x]$, and the target has the quotient topology induced by that
projection. Thus the notation here does
not introduce a second quotient-circle convention. The page names are given
only to record that agreement; no result on either examples page is used as a
dependency.
