## Statement

Let $X$ be Noetherian and let $\mathcal I_1,\mathcal I_2$ be coherent ideal sheaves. Put $X_i=\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I_i}(X)$ and $X_{12}=\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I_1\mathcal I_2}(X)$. Each extended ideal $\mathcal I_i\mathcal O_{X_{12}}$ is invertible. There are canonical proper $X$-morphisms $X_{12}\to X_i$, and, as schemes over $X_1$, one has
$$X_{12}\cong\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I_2\mathcal O_{X_1}}(X_1).$$
The analogous assertion holds with the two indices exchanged. Consequently the blowup of a finite product of coherent ideals is a common proper modification of their individual blowups, obtained by successively blowing up the pullbacks of the remaining base ideals.

If the ideals are the unit ideal on an open $U\subseteq X$, all these maps are isomorphisms over $U$. If $U$ is schematically dense in $X$, its resulting copy is schematically dense in each of these blowups. The latter density assertion holds more generally for any blowup of a coherent ideal whose center is disjoint from a schematically dense $U$. Zero and unit ideals and empty schemes are included.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The Noetherian scheme, the coherent base ideals, and, for the final assertion, an open on which those ideals are unit ideals.

[F1] The finite-chart blowup makes its center ideal invertible and is final among morphisms with invertible image ideal. It is proper and an isomorphism off the center. [[lem-nagata-admissible-blowup-universal-property-and-properness]]

[F2] On a chart $A[J/a]$, the image of $a$ is a nonzerodivisor, $J$ is generated by $a$, and the chart embeds as the subalgebra generated by the fractions $j/a$ in $A[1/a]$. Finite generating families give canonical gluing compatible with localization. [[lem-affine-blowup-algebra-properties]] [[lem-nagata-finite-affine-blowup-chart-gluing]] [[def-multiplicative-subset-and-localisation]]

## Proof

1.1 Work over an affine open $\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq X$. Choose finite generating families for $I_1,I_2$, so their pairwise products generate $I_1I_2$. On a chart $B=A[I_1I_2/(ab)]$ with $a\in I_1$, $b\in I_2$ from those families, the element $ab$ is a nonzerodivisor by [F2]; hence $a$ and $b$ are separately nonzerodivisors. For $x\in I_1$, the fraction $xb/(ab)$ belongs to $B$ and satisfies $a\cdot(xb/(ab))=x$, by the embedding into $A[1/(ab)]$. Therefore $I_1B=aB$, and similarly $I_2B=bB$. The factor ideals are invertible on these covering charts. By [F1], their universal properties give canonical morphisms $X_{12}\to X_1$ and $X_{12}\to X_2$. The second invertible ideal also gives a morphism $\eta:X_{12}\to T=\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I_2\mathcal O_{X_1}}(X_1)$ over $X_1$. [F1, F2, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 On a chart $C=A[I_1/a]$ of $X_1$, $a$ is a nonzerodivisor. A chart of $T$ above it is $C[I_2C/b]\subseteq C[1/b]$. The image of $a$ remains a nonzerodivisor in $C[1/b]$, and hence in its subring defining this chart; the image ideal $I_1$ is still $(a)$. The image ideal $I_2$ is $(b)$ with $b$ a nonzerodivisor by [F2]. Thus the product image ideal is invertible on $T$. The universal property in [F1] gives a canonical $X$-morphism $\theta:T\to X_{12}$. The composite $\theta\eta$ is the identity by uniqueness for the blowup of $\mathcal I_1\mathcal I_2$. The composite $\eta\theta$ is the identity as well: its map to $X_1$ is the canonical one by uniqueness for the invertible image of $\mathcal I_1$, and then uniqueness for the blowup of $\mathcal I_2\mathcal O_{X_1}$ gives equality on $T$. This proves the displayed canonical isomorphism. Exchanging the two factors gives the other one. [F1, F2, step 1.1, construct, algebra]

3.1 Under that isomorphism, the map $X_{12}\to X_1$ is a blowup of a coherent ideal on the Noetherian scheme $X_1$, hence proper by [F1]; similarly for $X_2$. Repeating the two-factor identity proves the finite-product assertion by induction, and all identifications are canonical by the same universal properties. If every base ideal is a unit ideal on $U$, its product is a unit ideal there too, so [F1] makes every map the identity over $U$. These claims include a single factor as the trivial induction case. [F1, F2, step 1.1, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 For the density claim, take any coherent ideal $J$ unit on a schematically dense open $U$ and work on $\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq X$ and a blowup chart $B=A[J/a]\subseteq A_a$. On this chart the copy of $U$ is the copy of $U\cap D(a)$, because $J$ is unit on $U$ and the chart is where $a$ generates that ideal. Schematic density of $U$ in $X$ restricts to schematic density of $U\cap D(a)$ in $D(a)$. Hence a section of $B$ vanishing on the chart's copy of $U$ maps to zero in $A_a$ and is zero by the displayed subring inclusion. The kernel sheaf is therefore zero on each chart, proving schematic density in the blowup. This applies to each stage and each factor in the product construction. Empty charts cause no exception; if a center ideal is zero its blowup is empty, and if it is the unit ideal its blowup is the identity. The same computations and universal properties remain valid in those cases, completing the proof. [F1, F2, step 2.1, step 3.1, algebra] ∎
