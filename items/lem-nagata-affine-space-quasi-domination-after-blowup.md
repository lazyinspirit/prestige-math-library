## Statement

Let $A$ be a Noetherian ring, $X=\operatorname{Spec}A$, and $U\subseteq X$ a dense open subscheme. Let $n\ge0$ and let $f:U\to\mathbb A^n_X$ be an $X$-morphism. There is a finitely generated ideal $J\subseteq A$ with $V(J)=X\setminus U$ such that, for the blowup $\beta:X'=\operatorname{Bl}_J(X)\to X$, the morphism $f$ extends to an $X$-morphism
$$\overline f:X'\longrightarrow\mathbb P^n_X,$$
where $\mathbb A^n_X$ is the chart with last homogeneous coordinate nonzero. The morphism $\beta$ is proper and is an isomorphism over $U$, and the resulting copy of $U$ is schematically dense in $X'$.

If $W=\overline f^{-1}(\mathbb A^n_X)$, then $W$ is open and dense in $X'$, contains $U$, and the graph of $\overline f|_W$ is closed in $X'\times_X\mathbb A^n_X$. Thus it is a quasi-domination extending $f$. The cases $n=0$ and empty $X$ are included.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The Noetherian affine scheme, dense open, integer $n\ge0$ and $X$-morphism in the Statement.

[F1] A blowup along a coherent ideal on a Noetherian scheme is proper, is an isomorphism off its center, and has schematically dense open complement of the center. [[lem-nagata-admissible-blowup-universal-property-and-properness]]

[F2] The affine charts of a finitely generated ideal are $A[J/a]$, with $JA[J/a]=(a)$ and $(A[J/a])_a=A_a$. The charts glue along their canonical fraction identifications. On a Noetherian affine scheme every open subset has a finite principal cover, by the finite-ideal-generator argument in the same source. [[lem-nagata-finite-affine-blowup-chart-gluing]]

[F3] Projective space has its finite standard ratio charts and their reciprocal overlap maps. These charts show it is separated: each overlap ring is generated by its two chart rings, so the affine separatedness criterion applies. A graph into a separated target is the inverse image of its closed diagonal. [[def-relative-projective-space-standard-charts]] [[thm-separatedness-gluing-overlap-criterion]] [[def-separated-morphism-schemes]]

[F4] An equality in a localization holds after multiplying its numerator difference by one element of the multiplicative set. Finite lists of such witnesses can be replaced by a common power; quotient and fraction arithmetic include zero rings. [[def-multiplicative-subset-and-localisation]] [[def-quotient-ring]]

## Proof

1.1 Suppose first $X\ne\varnothing$, so its dense open $U$ is nonempty. By [F2] choose a finite principal cover $U=\bigcup_{i=1}^m D(u_i)$, with $m\ge1$. Index the $n$ coordinates by $0\le j<n$. Those of $f$ on $D(u_i)$ lie in $A_{u_i}$, so write them as $g_{ij}/u_i^N$ for a common positive exponent $N$, enlarging denominators over this finite list. Compatibility on $D(u_iu_{i'})$ says that $u_{i'}^Ng_{ij}-u_i^Ng_{i'j}$ vanishes after inverting $u_iu_{i'}$. By [F4], choose one common integer $M\ge0$ killing all these finitely many differences after multiplication by $(u_iu_{i'})^M$. Replace $g_{ij}$ by $u_i^Mg_{ij}$ and $N$ by $N+M$. Writing $t_i=u_i^N$, we now have exact identities $t_{i'}g_{ij}=t_i g_{i'j}$ in $A$ for every $i,i',j$, and the coordinate fractions remain those of $f$. [F2, F4, given, construct]

2.1 Set $h_{ii'j}=t_i g_{i'j}=t_{i'}g_{ij}$ and let $J$ be generated by all $t_it_{i'}$ and all $h_{ii'j}$. This is a finite generating family, including $t_i^2$. At any point of $U$, some $u_i$, hence $t_i^2$, is a unit locally, so $J$ is the unit ideal there. At any point outside $U$, every $u_i$ lies in its prime ideal; every cross product $t_it_{i'}$ and every $h_{ii'j}=t_i g_{i'j}$ lies in that prime. Thus $V(J)=X\setminus U$. Form $X'=\operatorname{Bl}_J(X)$. By [F1] it is proper over $X$, is the identity over $U$, and $U$ is schematically dense in $X'$. [F1, F2, F4, step 1.1, construct, algebra]

3.1 On the blowup chart indexed by $a=t_it_{i'}$, define a morphism to the last-coordinate chart of $\mathbb P^n_X$ by sending $T_j/T_n$ to $h_{ii'j}/a$. On the chart indexed by $a=h_{ii'j}$, define a morphism to chart $T_j\ne0$ by sending $T_r/T_j$ to $h_{ii'r}/a$ for $r<n$ and $T_n/T_j$ to $(t_it_{i'})/a$. For $r=j$ the first expression is $1$. Every displayed numerator is one of the generators of $J$, so every fraction is a regular element of the chart algebra by [F2]. These maps are therefore genuine morphisms on the whole indicated charts, including empty zero-generator charts. They give maps on an affine cover of $X'$. [F2, F3, step 2.1, construct, algebra]

4.1 Each chart map in step 3.1 agrees with $f$ on its intersection with $U$. Indeed that intersection is $D(a)$ by [F2]. For $a=t_it_{i'}$ invertibility makes $t_i,t_{i'}$ units, and $h_{ii'j}/a=g_{ij}/t_i$, the $j$th coordinate of $f$. For $a=h_{ii'j}$, the equality $h_{ii'j}=t_i g_{i'j}=t_{i'}g_{ij}$ makes $t_i,t_{i'}$ units after $a$ is inverted; the projective coordinates are then $f_r/f_j$ and $1/f_j$, with $f_j$ invertible. On an overlap of any two source charts, these maps consequently agree on the schematically dense restriction of $U$. Since projective space is separated by [F3], they agree on the full overlap: the ideal of their equalizer, obtained from the closed diagonal, vanishes on $U$ and hence is zero by schematic density. Thus the chart maps glue to the required $X$-morphism $\overline f:X'\to\mathbb P^n_X$. [F1, F2, F3, F4, step 2.1, step 3.1, algebra]

5.1 Put $W=\overline f^{-1}(\mathbb A^n_X)$. It is open and contains $U$ by step 4.1, hence is dense because $U$ is schematically and topologically dense by [F1]. The graph of $\overline f$ is closed in $X'\times_X\mathbb P^n_X$ by the closed diagonal in [F3]. Intersecting that graph with the open ambient subscheme $X'\times_X\mathbb A^n_X$ is exactly the graph of $\overline f|_W$, so this graph is closed in that ambient subscheme. This is the asserted quasi-domination. If $n=0$ the coordinate family is empty, the cross-product generators still include $t_i^2$, and the projective target is $\mathbb P^0_X=X$, so the same construction applies. If $X$ is empty take its empty blowup and the unique empty maps. All conclusions follow. [F1, F3, step 2.1, step 4.1, algebra] ∎
