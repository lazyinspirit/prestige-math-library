## Statement

Assume AC and DC. Let k be a finite field, C/k a smooth proper geometrically integral curve, T a finite nonempty set of closed points, and U=C\T. Then `H¹_W(U,G_m)` is a finitely generated abelian group of rank `|T|−1`. For every prime p, `Z_p⊗H¹_W(U,G_m)` is a finitely generated Z_p-module. If a finite group Γ acts on the pair (C,T) over k, the induced module is finitely generated over `Z_p[Γ]`. This finite-level conclusion does not assert finite presentation over an infinite Iwasawa algebra or descent between levels.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC, DC, finite k, C, nonempty T and U as in the Statement.

[F1] The boundary-unit exact sequence is `0→P_T/(φ−1)P_T→H¹_W(U,G_m)→Pic(Ū)^φ→0`; its first term is finitely generated of rank `|T|−1`. [[lem-weil-etale-h1-boundary-unit-spectral-sequence]]

[F2] Divisors and line bundles on a smooth proper curve agree, and restriction to U quotients by the subgroup generated by the removed points. [[thm-cartier-weil-divisors-curves-agree]]

[F3] A finite field has cyclic multiplicative group; finite Galois Hilbert 90 makes every multiplicative cocycle a coboundary; and global regular functions on a proper geometrically integral curve over k are k. [[thm-multiplicative-group-of-a-finite-field-is-cyclic]] [[thm-hilbert-90-for-finite-galois-extension]] [[thm-global-functions-proper-integral-variety]]

[F4] Degree-zero divisor classes on a smooth proper geometrically integral curve over a finite field form a finite group. [[thm-finiteness-of-degree-zero-divisor-class-group-over-finite-field]]

[F5] AC and DC are assumed. [[def-axiom-of-choice]] [[def-dependent-choice]]

## Proof

**Proof technique:** geometric divisor localization and Frobenius invariants.

1.1 Let B be the subgroup of `Pic(C̄)` generated by the finitely many geometric points of T̄. By [F2] there is a φ-equivariant exact sequence `0→B→Pic(C̄)→Pic(Ū)→0`, and B is finitely generated. The φ-fixed subgroup of `Pic(C̄)` is finitely generated. Indeed, let [D] be a fixed class and choose a representative D and rational f with `φD−D=div(f)`. Both descend to one finite constant extension k_n/k. The norm of f has divisor zero, so it is a constant c∈k× by [F3]. The norm `k_n×→k×` is surjective: by [F3] both multiplicative groups are cyclic and exponent `(q^n−1)/(q−1)` has image of order q−1. Multiply f by a constant of norm c^(-1). Hilbert 90 [F3] then gives `f=φ(h)/h` for h∈k_n(C)×. Hence `D−div(h)` is φ-invariant and descends as a divisor on C. Its degree is unchanged. Every fixed degree-zero geometric class therefore comes from the finite degree-zero group of C in [F4], and the degree image is a subgroup of Z. [F2, F3, F4, F5, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 The invariants exact sequence for the infinite cyclic group ⟨φ⟩ sends `Pic(Ū)^φ` into the coinvariant group `B/(φ−1)B`, with kernel the image of `Pic(C̄)^φ`. Both outer groups are finitely generated by step 1.1, so `Pic(Ū)^φ` is finitely generated. It is also torsion: choose a geometric boundary point x and extend any divisor on Ū to C̄. Subtracting its degree times [x] leaves a degree-zero divisor and does not change its class on Ū. All its finitely many points are rational over some finite extension k'/k, and by [F4] a positive multiple is principal over k'. Its original class on Ū is therefore torsion. A finitely generated torsion abelian group is finite, so `Pic(Ū)^φ` is finite. [F2, F4, F5, step 1.1, given, choose, algebra]

3.1 Apply [F1]. Its left term is finitely generated of rank `|T|−1`, and its right term is finite by step 2.1. Therefore the middle Weil–étale cohomology group is finitely generated with that same rank. Tensoring its finite generating set with Z_p shows `Z_p⊗H¹_W(U,G_m)` is finitely generated over Z_p. If a finite group Γ acts, the same finite set also generates over the larger ring Z_p[Γ], because that ring contains Z_p. No finite presentation or tower descent has been used. [F1, F5, step 2.1, given, algebra] ∎
