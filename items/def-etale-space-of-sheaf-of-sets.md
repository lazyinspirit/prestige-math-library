## Definition

Let $\mathcal F$ be a sheaf of sets on a topological space $X$.

Its **etale space** is the disjoint union of stalks
$$E(\mathcal F):=\coprod_{x\in X}\mathcal F_x,$$
equipped with the projection
$$p:E(\mathcal F)\longrightarrow X,\qquad p(\xi)=x\text{ if }\xi\in\mathcal F_x.$$

For every open set $U\subseteq X$ and every section $s\in\mathcal F(U)$, define
the subset
$$[s,U]:=\{\,s_x : x\in U\,\}\subseteq E(\mathcal F).$$

The topology on $E(\mathcal F)$ is the topology generated by the family of all
such subsets $[s,U]$ ([[def-topology-basis-subbasis]]).

On each set $[s,U]$, the projection $p$ restricts to a bijection onto $U$ with
inverse
$$U\longrightarrow [s,U],\qquad x\longmapsto s_x.$$
The next theorem shows that this bijection is in fact a homeomorphism.
