## Example

Let $k$ be a field, $n\ge0$, and let
$\mathbb A^{n}_{k}=\operatorname{Spec}k[X_1,\dots,X_n]$ be affine $n$-space over
$k$ with a $k$-rational point $x=(a_1,\dots,a_n)$, so that
$\kappa(x)=k$. Then the $k$-morphisms from the dual-numbers scheme
$D_k=\operatorname{Spec}k[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^{2})$ to $X$ that reduce to $x$
are exactly the maps
$$\varphi_v\colon k[X_1,\dots,X_n]\longrightarrow k[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^{2}), \qquad X_i\longmapsto a_i+\epsilon\,v_i,$$
with $v=(v_1,\dots,v_n)\in k^{n}$ arbitrary and uniquely determined by
$\varphi_v$. Under the bijection of
[[thm-tangent-vectors-dual-numbers]] these are the tangent vectors of
$\mathbb A^{n}_{k}$ at $x$, and the coefficient $v_i$ is the value of the
corresponding cotangent functional on the basis element
$\mathrm dX_i$. Thus $T_{\mathbb A^{n}_{k}/k,x}\cong k^{n}$ with coordinates
$v_1,\dots,v_n$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A field $k$, an integer $n\ge0$, the polynomial algebra $P=k[X_1,\dots,X_n]$, the scheme $X=\mathbb A^{n}_{k}=\operatorname{Spec}P$ over $S=\operatorname{Spec}k$ with structure map $k\to P$, and a $k$-rational point $x=(a_1,\dots,a_n)$, whose associated maximal ideal is $\mathfrak m_x=(X_1-a_1,\dots,X_n-a_n)$ with residue field $\kappa(x)=k$.

[F1] [[thm-tangent-vectors-dual-numbers]]: for a morphism of schemes $f\colon X\to S$ and $x\in X$ with residue field $\kappa=\kappa(x)$, the $S$-morphisms $D_\kappa\to X$ reducing to the canonical point $x$ are in bijection with $\operatorname{Hom}_{\kappa}(\Omega_{X/S}\otimes_{\mathcal O_{X,x}}\kappa(x),\kappa(x))=T_{X/S,x}$; for $x$ a $k$-rational point over $S=\operatorname{Spec}k$ this reads $\{\tau\}\cong\operatorname{Hom}_k(\mathfrak m_x/\mathfrak m_x^{2},k)$.

[F2] [[lem-differentials-polynomial-algebra-free]] with $n$ variables: $\Omega_{P/k}$ is a free $P$-module with basis $\mathrm dX_1,\dots,\mathrm dX_n$; in particular the $P$-module is freely generated by the differentials of the coordinates.

[F3] [[thm-universal-property-of-a-polynomial-ring-on-a-family]]: for commutative rings $R\to S$ and a family $(s_i)_{i\in I}$ of elements of $S$, there is exactly one $R$-algebra homomorphism $R[x_i:i\in I]\to S$ sending $x_i$ to $s_i$.

[F4] [[lem-sheaf-differentials-affine-compatibility]]: for the affine morphism $\operatorname{Spec}P\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ induced by $k\to P$, the sheaf $\Omega_{X/S}$ is the sheaf attached to the $P$-module $\Omega_{P/k}$, so its fibre at $x$ is $\Omega_{P/k}\otimes_P\kappa(x)$.

## Verification

1.1 A $k$-algebra homomorphism $\varphi\colon P\to k[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^{2})$ is the same thing as the data of the $n$ elements $\varphi(X_i)\in k[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^{2})$, arbitrary and unique: by [F3] applied to $R=k$, $S=k[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^{2})$, the structure map $k\to k[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^{2})$ and the family of chosen images. Expanding the unit, a general element of $k[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^{2})$ is uniquely $c+d\,\epsilon$ with $c,d\in k$, so $\varphi$ is uniquely described by the pairs $(c_i,d_i)$ with $\varphi(X_i)=c_i+d_i\epsilon$. [F3, given]

1.2 The differential side: by [F2], $\Omega_{P/k}$ is free with basis $\mathrm dX_1,\dots,\mathrm dX_n$, and by [F4] the fibre of $\Omega_{X/S}$ at $x$ is $\Omega_{P/k}\otimes_{P}\kappa(x)$, which after tensoring the basis is the $k$-vector space with basis the images of $\mathrm dX_1,\dots,\mathrm dX_n$. Its $k$-linear dual therefore has the dual basis $\partial_1,\dots,\partial_n$ with $\partial_i(\mathrm dX_j)=\delta_{ij}$, and $\operatorname{Hom}_k(\Omega_{X/S}\otimes\kappa(x),\kappa(x))\cong k^{n}$ by $\lambda\mapsto(\lambda(\mathrm dX_1),\dots,\lambda(\mathrm dX_n))$. [F2, F4, given]

2.1 Reduction to $x$: the composite of $\varphi$ with the quotient map $k[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^{2})\to k$, $\epsilon\mapsto0$, is a $k$-algebra homomorphism $P\to k$; it is a $k$-point of $\mathbb A^{n}_{k}$ and it equals $(a_1,\dots,a_n)$ exactly when $c_i=a_i$ for all $i$, by the same uniqueness of [F3] applied to $P\to k$. Hence the maps $\varphi$ reducing to $x$ are precisely the $\varphi$ with $\varphi(X_i)=a_i+\epsilon\,d_i$, and they are in bijection with the $n$-tuples $v=(v_1,\dots,v_n)=(d_1,\dots,d_n)\in k^{n}$. [F3, step 1.1, given]

3.1 By [F1] the dual-number points of step 2.1 are in bijection with the dual space of step 1.2; tracking the $\epsilon$-coefficient, the point $\varphi_v$ corresponds to the functional $\lambda_v$ with $\lambda_v(\mathrm dX_i)=v_i$, that is, $v_i$ is the value on the cotangent basis element $\mathrm dX_i$. Since $x$ is $k$-rational, this is the identification $T_{\mathbb A^{n}_{k}/k,x}\cong\operatorname{Hom}_k(\mathfrak m_x/\mathfrak m_x^{2},k)\cong k^{n}$. [F1, step 1.2, step 2.1]

4.1 Summing up: the dual-number points of $\mathbb A^{n}_{k}$ reducing to $x$ are exactly the $\varphi_v$ of step 2.1 with $v\in k^{n}$, and the bijection of [F1] with the relative tangent space is the one carrying $\varphi_v$ to the functional with coordinates $(v_1,\dots,v_n)$ of step 3.1; in particular the affine space has tangent space $k^{n}$ at each $k$-rational point, with the coordinate $v_i$ dual to $\mathrm dX_i$. [step 2.1, step 3.1, F1] ∎
