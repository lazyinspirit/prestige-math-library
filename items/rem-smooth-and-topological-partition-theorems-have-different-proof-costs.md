The smooth theorem [[thm-smooth-partitions-of-unity-exist-on-manifolds]] is proved here through chart-level bumps, a countable locally finite shrinking, and normalization of a positive smooth family. The published topological theorem [[thm-subordinate-partitions-of-unity-exist]] proves a broader continuous statement with a different proof cost and a different choice budget, so this page records it only for comparison and not as a black-box substitute.
