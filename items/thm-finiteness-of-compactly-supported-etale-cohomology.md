## Statement

Let $X$ be a separated scheme of finite type over a finite field $\mathbb F_q$, let $\ell\ne\operatorname{char}(\mathbb F_q)$ be a prime, and let $\mathcal F$ be a constructible $\mathbb Q_\ell$-sheaf on $X$. Then each $H_c^i(X,\mathcal F)$ is a finite-dimensional $\mathbb Q_\ell$-vector space, and these groups vanish for all but finitely many integers $i$.
