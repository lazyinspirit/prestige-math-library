## Definition

Let $X$ be a separated $k$-scheme of finite type with an action of $\mathbf G_m$ ([[def-algebraic-group-action-and-scheme-theoretic-stabilizer]]), and let $Z\subseteq X$ be a $\mathbf G_m$-stable closed subscheme. For a morphism $\varphi:\mathbf G_m\times_k X_0\to X$, the limit $\lim_{t\to0}\varphi(t)$ exists if $\varphi$ extends to a morphism $\mathbf A^1\times X_0\to X$, in which case the extension and its value at $0$ are unique by separatedness; for a point $x\in X(R)$ one writes $tx$ for the orbit map and asks that $t\mapsto tx$ extend over $\mathbf A^1_R$. When $X=\operatorname{Spec}A$ is affine, the action is a $\mathbb Z$-gradation $A=\bigoplus_{n\in\mathbb Z}A_n$ and $\lim_{t\to0}tx$ exists iff $f_n(x)=0$ for all $f_n\in A_n$ with $n<0$; the **concentrator subscheme** $X(Z)$ is the closed subscheme defined by the ideal generated by $A_0\cap\mathfrak a+\sum_{n<0}A_n$, where $\mathfrak a=\ker(A\to A(Z))$ is the graded ideal of $Z$. The associated functor sends $R$ to the set of $x\in X(R)$ with $\lim_{t\to0}tx\in Z(R)$.

The uniqueness in the first sentence follows from separatedness ([[def-separated-scheme-over-base]]): the equalizer of two extensions is closed in $\mathbf A^1\times X_0$ and contains $\mathbf G_m\times X_0$. This open is schematically dense, since on every affine chart of $X_0$ the map $R[t]\to R[t,t^{-1}]$ is injective. The equalizer is therefore the whole source, also when $X_0$ is nonreduced; the affine description is the gradation induced by the coaction of $k[t,t^{-1}]$ on $A$ when $X$ is affine ([[def-affine-scheme]]), and the closed subscheme structure is that of the ideal sheaf generated by the listed homogeneous pieces ([[def-ideal-sheaf]]). The functor-of-points description of $X(Z)$ is stated here and proved in [[thm-concentrator-subscheme-representability-and-smoothness]]; no representability is asserted by the definition itself.
