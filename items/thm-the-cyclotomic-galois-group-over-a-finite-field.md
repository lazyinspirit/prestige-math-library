## Statement

Let $\mathbb F_q$ be a finite field of order $q$
([[def-finite-field-and-its-order]]) and let $n\ge1$ with $\gcd(n,q)=1$
([[def-coprime]]). Then the image of the embedding

$$\operatorname{Gal}\bigl(\mathbb F_q(\mu_n)/\mathbb F_q\bigr)\longrightarrow(\mathbb Z/n)^{\times}$$

of [[thm-the-cyclotomic-galois-group-embeds-in-the-unit-group-modulo-n]] is the
cyclic subgroup generated by $[q]_n$, and

$$\bigl[\mathbb F_q(\mu_n):\mathbb F_q\bigr]=\operatorname{ord}\bigl([q]_n\bigr),$$

the multiplicative order of $[q]_n$ in $(\mathbb Z/n)^{\times}$
([[def-order-in-a-group]]).

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A finite field $\mathbb F_q$ of order $q$, of characteristic $p$ with $q$ a power of $p$ ([[thm-finite-fields-have-prime-power-order]]), and an integer $n\ge1$ with $\gcd(n,q)=1$; the extension $E:=\mathbb F_q(\mu_n)$ ([[def-cyclotomic-extension]]).

[L1] For a field $K$ and $n\ge1$ with $\operatorname{char}K\nmid n$, the extension $K(\mu_n)/K$ is finite Galois and $\sigma\mapsto[a_\sigma]_n$, determined by $\sigma(\zeta)=\zeta^{a_\sigma}$ on a primitive $n$-th root of unity, is an injective homomorphism into $(\mathbb Z/n)^{\times}$ with $\sigma(x)=x^{a_\sigma}$ for every $x\in\mu_n$ ([[thm-the-cyclotomic-galois-group-embeds-in-the-unit-group-modulo-n]]).

[L2] An extension $L/\mathbb F_q$ of finite fields of degree $m$ is Galois with $\operatorname{Gal}(L/\mathbb F_q)=\langle\sigma_q\rangle$ cyclic of order $m$, where $\sigma_q(x)=x^{q}$ ([[thm-extensions-of-finite-fields-are-galois-with-cyclic-frobenius-group]], [[def-relative-frobenius-of-a-finite-field-extension]]).

[L3] For $n\ge1$ and $a\in\mathbb Z$, the class $[a]_n$ is a unit of $\mathbb Z/n$ if and only if $\gcd(a,n)=1$ ([[thm-unit-criterion-modulo-n]], [[def-unit-group-modulo-n-and-euler-totient]]).

[L4] For a finite Galois extension $L/F$ one has $|\operatorname{Gal}(L/F)|=[L:F]$ ([[thm-finite-galois-extension-characterizations]], [[def-extension-degree-and-finite-extension]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 The characteristic $p$ divides $q$, and $\gcd(n,q)=1$, so $p\nmid n$; hence [L1] applies to $K=\mathbb F_q$ and $E=\mathbb F_q(\mu_n)$ is finite Galois over $\mathbb F_q$. Also $[q]_n$ is a unit of $\mathbb Z/n$ by [L3]. [L1, L3, given]

2.1 $E$ is a finite field: it is a finite extension of the finite field $\mathbb F_q$ by step 1.1, so it is a finite-dimensional $\mathbb F_q$-vector space over a finite field and therefore has finitely many elements. By [L2], $\operatorname{Gal}(E/\mathbb F_q)=\langle\sigma_q\rangle$ with $\sigma_q(x)=x^{q}$. [step 1.1, L2]

3.1 The exponent attached to $\sigma_q$ by [L1] is $[q]_n$, since $\sigma_q(\zeta)=\zeta^{q}$ for a primitive $n$-th root of unity $\zeta\in E$. Because the embedding is a homomorphism and $\operatorname{Gal}(E/\mathbb F_q)$ is generated by $\sigma_q$, the image is the subgroup of $(\mathbb Z/n)^{\times}$ generated by $[q]_n$. [step 1.1, step 2.1, L1]

4.1 The embedding is injective, so $\bigl|\operatorname{Gal}(E/\mathbb F_q)\bigr|$ equals the order of $\bigl\langle[q]_n\bigr\rangle$, which is $\operatorname{ord}([q]_n)$; and $[E:\mathbb F_q]=\bigl|\operatorname{Gal}(E/\mathbb F_q)\bigr|$ by [L4]. Hence $[E:\mathbb F_q]=\operatorname{ord}([q]_n)$. [step 3.1, L1, L4] ∎

## Remarks

- **What the coprimality hypothesis does.** It makes $[q]_n$ a unit, so that the subgroup it generates is defined, and it puts the extension inside the scope of [L1] by ruling out $\operatorname{char}\mathbb F_q\mid n$. Without it the group $\mu_n$ collapses onto $\mu_m$ for the prime-to-$p$ part $m$ of $n$ ([[prop-p-power-roots-of-unity-in-characteristic-p]]), and the statement is about $m$ rather than $n$.
