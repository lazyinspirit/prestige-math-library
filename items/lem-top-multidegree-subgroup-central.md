## Statement

Let \(s\ge2\) and let \(G/\Gamma\) be a standard taut multidegree-filtered nilmanifold of degree at most \((1,s-1)\). Then \(G_{(1,s-1)}\) is contained in the center of \(G\).

## Statement references

[[def-multidegree-taut-filtered-nilmanifold]]

[Green, Tao, and Ziegler, Erratum, Section 2](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/erratum-4.pdf)

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A taut multidegree-filtered nilmanifold of degree at most \((1,s-1)\), with \(s\ge2\).

[F1] Tautness says \(G\) is generated by \(G_{(1,0)}\) and \(G_{(0,1)}\) ([[def-multidegree-taut-filtered-nilmanifold]]).

[F2] The filtration satisfies \([G_{(a,b)},G_{(a',b')} ]\subseteq G_{(a+a',b+b')}\) ([[def-multidegree-taut-filtered-nilmanifold]]).

[F3] Every \(G_{(a,b)}\) is trivial if \(a>1\) or \(b>s-1\) ([[def-multidegree-taut-filtered-nilmanifold]]).

## Proof

1.1 The filtration commutator condition [F2] gives \([G_{(1,s-1)},G_{(1,0)}]\subseteq G_{(2,s-1)}=\{e\}\) and \([G_{(1,s-1)},G_{(0,1)}]\subseteq G_{(1,s)}=\{e\}\), because both target indices are outside the degree rectangle by [F3]. Thus every element of \(G_{(1,s-1)}\) commutes with every element of both generating subgroups. [given, F2, F3, algebra]

1.2 By [F1], those two subgroups generate \(G\). An element commuting with each member of a generating set commutes with every finite product of generators and their inverses, hence with every element of \(G\). Therefore \(G_{(1,s-1)}\subseteq Z(G)\). [F1, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
