## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice, Countable Choice, and Dependent Choice. Let $H/F$ be a finite abelian extension of number fields such that $F$ is totally real and $H$ is a totally imaginary quadratic extension of $F$. Let $G=\operatorname{Gal}(H/F)$ and let $j\in G$ be complex conjugation. Let $S_\infty$ be the infinite places of $F$, let $S_{\mathrm{ram}}$ be the finite primes of $F$ ramified in $H$, and let $S$ be any finite set of places containing $S_\infty\cup S_{\mathrm{ram}}$. Let $T$ be a finite set of finite primes disjoint from $S$ such that the only root of unity in $H$ congruent to $1$ at every prime above $T$ is $1$. With $\operatorname{Cl}_T(H)$ the group of fractional ideals prime to the product of the primes above $T$ modulo principal ideals generated by elements congruent to $1$ at every prime above $T$, its contragredient Pontryagin dual minus part after inverting $2$, and $\mathbb Z[G]^-:=\mathbb Z[1/2][G]/(1+j)$, the $S,T$-smoothed Stickelberger element satisfies

$$\Theta_{S,T}^{H/F\,#}\in\operatorname{Fitt}_{\mathbb Z[G]^-}\!\left(\operatorname{Cl}_T(H)^{\vee,-}\right).$$
