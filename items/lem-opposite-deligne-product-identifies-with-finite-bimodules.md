## Statement

Let $\mathcal A,\mathcal B$ be finite $k$-linear abelian categories with supplied module equivalences $\mathcal A\simeq R\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$, $\mathcal B\simeq S\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$; use chosen small representatives. Here $(\mathcal B,\mathcal A)\text{-}\mathrm{bimod}$ denotes the finite $k$-linear $(S,R)$-bimodules in these models, not modules over abstract categories themselves. Then there is an equivalence of $k$-linear categories $\mathcal A^{\mathrm{op}}\boxtimes\mathcal B\simeq(\mathcal B,\mathcal A)\text{-}\mathrm{bimod}$ between the Deligne product of [[thm-finite-deligne-products-exist-by-tensor-product-algebras]] and the category of finite-dimensional $(\mathcal B,\mathcal A)$-bimodules ([[def-bimodule]], [[def-opposite-category]]), carrying an external object $\bar a\boxtimes b$ to $b\otimes_ka^*$, where $a^*$ is the $k$-dual of $a$ ([[def-algebraic-dual-and-linear-functional]], [[def-vector-space]]) with the induced right $\mathcal A$-action and $b$ keeps its left $\mathcal B$-action ([[def-left-and-right-modules]]). The equivalence is the transport of the identification $(R^{\mathrm{op}}\otimes_kS)\text{-}\mathrm{mod}\cong(\mathcal B,\mathcal A)\text{-}\mathrm{bimod}$ ([[def-opposite-ring]], [[thm-tensor-product-of-algebras-over-a-commutative-ring]], [[thm-modules-over-a-ring-form-an-abelian-category]]) along module models ([[thm-finite-abelian-categories-are-finite-dimensional-module-categories]]) and the exact contravariant duality $(-)^*$ ([[lem-finite-module-duality-is-exact-with-commuting-bimodule-actions]]). Moreover the universal property dualises: the bifunctor $\boxtimes$ is also left exact in each variable, restriction along it induces equivalences for left-exact-in-each-variable bifunctors as well, and $(\mathcal A^{\mathrm{op}}\boxtimes\mathcal B)^{\mathrm{op}}\simeq\mathcal A\boxtimes\mathcal B^{\mathrm{op}}$, so the left exact external-tensor formula of the categorical Eilenberg–Watts triangle on this page is an instance of the universal property in its dual form. The Deligne-product construction is under the AC assumption of its cited existence theorem; the duality and action identifications require no further choice.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** Finite $k$-linear abelian categories $\mathcal A,\mathcal B$ and a field $k$.

[F1] The module equivalences $\mathcal A\simeq R\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ and $\mathcal B\simeq S\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ in the statement are supplied data. The module-model theorem provides such equivalences when splitting data are supplied; its unconditional conclusion is full faithfulness and objectwise essential surjectivity ([[thm-finite-abelian-categories-are-finite-dimensional-module-categories]]).

[F2] For a finite-dimensional $k$-algebra $A$, the $k$-dual $X^*=\operatorname{Hom}_k(X,k)$ with the right $A$-action $(\lambda\cdot a)(x)=\lambda(ax)$ is a contravariant $k$-linear equivalence from $A\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ to finite-dimensional left $A^{\mathrm{op}}$-modules, and it is exact ([[lem-finite-module-duality-is-exact-with-commuting-bimodule-actions]]).

[F3] For finite-dimensional $k$-algebras $R,S$ the category $(R\otimes_kS)\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ with the tensor bifunctor $(X,Y)\mapsto X\otimes_kY$ is a Deligne product of $R\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ and $S\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$: restriction along that bifunctor is an equivalence between $k$-linear right exact functors out of the Deligne product and $k$-linear bifunctors right exact in each variable ([[thm-finite-deligne-products-exist-by-tensor-product-algebras]]).

[F4] The tensor product $A\otimes_kB$ of $k$-algebras has the multiplication $(a\otimes b)(a'\otimes b')=aa'\otimes bb'$ ([[thm-tensor-product-of-algebras-over-a-commutative-ring]]), and an $(S,R)$-bimodule is an abelian group that is a left $S$-module and a right $R$-module with commuting actions ([[def-bimodule]]).

[F5] The tensor product is functorial in both variables with the identity and composition laws, and it is universal for balanced maps: every balanced map out of $M\times N$ factors uniquely through $M\otimes_RN$ ([[prop-functoriality-of-module-tensor-products]], [[thm-universal-property-of-module-tensor-products]]).

[F6] The category of finite-dimensional modules over a finite-dimensional $k$-algebra is a finite $k$-linear abelian category in the intrinsic sense ([[prop-finite-dimensional-module-categories-are-intrinsically-finite]]).

[F7] The opposite ring $R^{\mathrm{op}}$ has the reversed multiplication $a\star b=ba$ ([[def-opposite-ring]]), and the opposite category reverses every morphism ([[def-opposite-category]]). A functor is left exact when it preserves every finite limit that exists in its source and right exact when it preserves every finite colimit; passing to opposites interchanges limits with colimits ([[def-left-exact-and-right-exact-functor]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Use the supplied finite-dimensional unital $k$-algebras $R,S$ and $k$-linear equivalences of [F1] $\mathcal A\simeq R\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$, $\mathcal B\simeq S\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ ([[def-equivalence-and-adjoint-equivalence-of-categories]], [[def-algebra-over-a-commutative-ring]], [[def-dimension]]). The $k$-dual $X^*=\operatorname{Hom}_k(X,k)$ with the right $R$-action $(\lambda\cdot a)(x)=\lambda(ax)$, equivalently the left $R^{\mathrm{op}}$-action $a\cdot\lambda:=\lambda\cdot a$, is a contravariant exact equivalence to $R^{\mathrm{op}}\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ [F2] ([[def-algebraic-dual-and-linear-functional]]), so it identifies $\mathcal A^{\mathrm{op}}$ with $R^{\mathrm{op}}\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ ([[def-opposite-category]], [[def-opposite-ring]]); $R^{\mathrm{op}}\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ and $S\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ are finite $k$-linear abelian categories [F6] ([[def-abelian-category]], [[def-k-linear-category-and-k-linear-functor]]). [given, F1, F2, F6]

2.1 By [F3] applied to the finite-dimensional algebras $R^{\mathrm{op}}$ and $S$, the category $(R^{\mathrm{op}}\otimes_kS)\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ with its tensor bifunctor is a Deligne product of $R^{\mathrm{op}}\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ and $S\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$, so transporting along step 1.1 identifies $\mathcal A^{\mathrm{op}}\boxtimes\mathcal B$ with $(R^{\mathrm{op}}\otimes_kS)\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$. A left module over $R^{\mathrm{op}}\otimes_kS$ is a $k$-vector space with commuting left $R^{\mathrm{op}}$- and left $S$-actions [F4], that is, a $k$-vector space with a right $R$-action and a left $S$-action that commute, i.e. a finite-dimensional $(S,R)$-bimodule ([[def-bimodule]], [[def-left-and-right-modules]], [[def-vector-space]], [[thm-modules-over-a-ring-form-an-abelian-category]]); under the equivalences of step 1.1 this is exactly the category $(\mathcal B,\mathcal A)\text{-}\mathrm{bimod}$ of finite-dimensional $(\mathcal B,\mathcal A)$-bimodules, so $\mathcal A^{\mathrm{op}}\boxtimes\mathcal B\simeq(\mathcal B,\mathcal A)\text{-}\mathrm{bimod}$ as $k$-linear categories. [step 1.1, F3, F4]

3.1 The universal bifunctor of $\mathcal A^{\mathrm{op}}\boxtimes\mathcal B$ is the transport of the tensor bifunctor of $R^{\mathrm{op}}\text{-}\mathrm{mod}\times S\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$, so on the external object $\bar a\boxtimes b$ it sends $(a,b)$ to $a^*\otimes_kb$, where $a^*=\operatorname{Hom}_k(a,k)$ carries the induced right $\mathcal A$-action and $b$ its left $\mathcal B$-action [F2, F4, F5]. The symmetry of the tensor product over the field, induced by the balanced map $(x,y)\mapsto y\otimes x$ and unique factorization through the tensor product [F5], is a natural isomorphism $a^*\otimes_kb\cong b\otimes_ka^*$ compatible with the two actions ([[prop-functoriality-of-module-tensor-products]], [[def-algebraic-dual-and-linear-functional]]), so the equivalence carries $\bar a\boxtimes b$ to $b\otimes_ka^*$ with those actions, as asserted. [step 2.1, F4, F5]

3.2 The tensor product of finite-dimensional $k$-vector spaces is exact in each variable: if $f:Y\to Y'$ is injective, then a $k$-linear retraction $r$ of $f$ exists because a basis of the image of $f$ extends to a basis of the finite-dimensional space $Y'$ ([[def-dimension]]), and $(1\otimes r)(1\otimes f)=1$ by functoriality [F5], so $1\otimes f$ is injective; right exactness in each variable holds by [F3](i), so $\boxtimes$ is also left exact in each variable ([[def-left-exact-and-right-exact-functor]]). Passing to opposites, $((R^{\mathrm{op}}\otimes_kS)\text{-}\mathrm{mod})^{\mathrm{op}}\simeq(R\otimes_kS^{\mathrm{op}})\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$, via the finite contravariant duality of [F2] for the algebra $R^{\mathrm{op}}\otimes_kS$, followed by the algebra isomorphism $(R^{\mathrm{op}}\otimes_kS)^{\mathrm{op}}\cong R\otimes_kS^{\mathrm{op}}$, checked on elementary tensors [F4, F7]; by [F3] the category $(R\otimes_kS^{\mathrm{op}})\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ with its tensor bifunctor is a Deligne product of $R\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$ and $S^{\mathrm{op}}\text{-}\mathrm{mod}$, so transporting along step 1.1 gives the canonical equivalence $(\mathcal A^{\mathrm{op}}\boxtimes\mathcal B)^{\mathrm{op}}\simeq\mathcal A\boxtimes\mathcal B^{\mathrm{op}}$. [step 1.1, step 2.1, F3, F5, F7]

4.1 The universal property dualises: a $k$-linear functor $G$ out of $\mathcal A^{\mathrm{op}}\boxtimes\mathcal B$ is left exact exactly when its opposite functor out of $(\mathcal A^{\mathrm{op}}\boxtimes\mathcal B)^{\mathrm{op}}$ is right exact [F7], and step 3.2 identifies that opposite source with $\mathcal A\boxtimes\mathcal B^{\mathrm{op}}$, where [F3] is the equivalence $\operatorname{Rex}_k(\mathcal A\boxtimes\mathcal B^{\mathrm{op}},\mathcal E^{\mathrm{op}})\simeq\operatorname{Rex}_{k,k}(\mathcal A\times\mathcal B^{\mathrm{op}},\mathcal E^{\mathrm{op}})$ for every $k$-linear abelian $\mathcal E$; applying the same duality to the bifunctors turns right exactness in each variable on $\mathcal A\times\mathcal B^{\mathrm{op}}$ into left exactness in each variable on $\mathcal A^{\mathrm{op}}\times\mathcal B$ [F7], so restriction along $\boxtimes$ induces an equivalence $\operatorname{Lex}_k(\mathcal A^{\mathrm{op}}\boxtimes\mathcal B,\mathcal E)\simeq\operatorname{Lex}_{k,k}(\mathcal A^{\mathrm{op}}\times\mathcal B,\mathcal E)$ as well ([[def-equivalence-and-adjoint-equivalence-of-categories]], [[def-functor-category]], [[def-natural-transformation]]); the left exact external-tensor formula of the categorical Eilenberg–Watts triangle on this page is therefore an instance of the universal property in its dual form. A second choice of module models is related to the first by a $k$-linear equivalence [F1], and conjugating by it identifies the transported equivalences together with their universal bifunctors, so the construction is well defined up to an equivalence respecting the universal bifunctors; no commutativity of the algebras and no further choice beyond the supplied module equivalences and the existence theorem's AC-dependent data is used. [step 3.1, step 3.2, F1, F3, F7] ∎
