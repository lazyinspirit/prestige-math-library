## Definition

Assume the Axiom of Choice for class-group finiteness. Let $K$ be a totally real number field and $p$ a prime. Write $\mathbb Q(\mu_{p^\infty})=\bigcup_{m\ge1}\mathbb Q(\mu_{p^m})$. For odd $p$, let $\mathbb Q_{\mathrm{cyc},p}$ be the unique subextension of $\mathbb Q(\mu_{p^\infty})/\mathbb Q$ with Galois group $\mathbb Z_p$, fixed by the finite subgroup of order $p-1$ in $\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb Q(\mu_{p^\infty})/\mathbb Q)$. For $p=2$, put $\mathbb Q_{\mathrm{cyc},2}=\bigcup_{n\ge0}\mathbb Q(\mu_{2^{n+2}})^+$, where the superscript $+$ denotes the subfield fixed by complex conjugation; this is the unique $\mathbb Z_2$-extension of $\mathbb Q$ inside $\mathbb Q(\mu_{2^\infty})$. Put $K_\infty=K\mathbb Q_{\mathrm{cyc},p}$, and let $K_n/K$ be its unique degree-$p^n$ layer. Put $A_n=\operatorname{Cl}(\mathcal O_{K_n})[p^\infty]$, the $p$-primary component of the ideal class group. The relative ideal norm maps descend to ideal class groups by the principal-ideal norm identity. The **cyclotomic Iwasawa module of $p$-primary class groups** is the inverse limit $X_{K,p}=\varprojlim_n A_n$ under these norm maps. The group $\Gamma=\operatorname{Gal}(K_\infty/K)\cong\mathbb Z_p$ acts continuously, so $X_{K,p}$ is a module over $\Lambda(\Gamma)\cong\mathbb Z_p\llbracket T\rrbracket$, where the latter identification uses a chosen topological generator of $\Gamma$. For $K=\mathbb Q$, this is the usual Iwasawa module of the cyclotomic $\mathbb Z_p$-extension, whose finite layers are totally real.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC, a totally real number field $K$, a prime $p$, and its finite cyclotomic layers $K_n/K$.

[F1] The ideal class group is the group of nonzero fractional ideals modulo principal fractional ideals. [[def-ideal-class-group-of-a-domain]]

[F2] A number field is a finite extension of $\mathbb Q$. [[def-number-field]]

[F3] The $p$-primary component consists of elements killed by a power of $p$. [[def-p-primary-component-of-an-abelian-group]]

[F4] Relative ideal norm sends a prime ideal $\mathfrak P$ to the contracted prime raised to its residue degree and extends multiplicatively. [[def-relative-ideal-norm-in-number-field-extension]]

[F5] Every nonzero integral ideal has a unique finite prime-ideal factorization, extended to fractional ideals. [[thm-number-field-integral-ideal-factorisation-in-zf]]

[F6] Relative norm sends a principal ideal to the principal ideal generated by the field norm. [[lem-relative-ideal-norm-of-principal-ideal]]

[F7] The ideal class group of every number field is finite under AC. [[thm-finiteness-of-the-number-field-class-group]]

[F8] For a procyclic group $\Gamma\cong\mathbb Z_p$ with topological generator $\gamma$, its completed group algebra is the Iwasawa algebra and $T=\gamma-1$ identifies it with $\mathbb Z_p\llbracket T\rrbracket$. [[def-iwasawa-algebra-of-profinite-abelian-group]]

[F9] Residue degrees multiply in towers, so relative ideal norms compose along a tower of number fields. [[thm-ramification-and-residue-degrees-in-towers]]

[F10] For odd $p$, the Teichmüller decomposition gives $\mathbb Z_p^\times=\mu_{p-1}\times(1+p\mathbb Z_p)$, and $1+p$ topologically generates the second factor. For $p=2$, every odd $2$-adic unit is uniquely $\pm u$ with $u\in1+4\mathbb Z_2$, and $5$ topologically generates $1+4\mathbb Z_2$; thus the specified real cyclotomic tower has Galois group $\mathbb Z_p$. [[def-teichmuller-character-on-zp-units]] [[lem-principal-unit-generators-of-p-adic-units]] [[cor-the-galois-group-of-a-rational-cyclotomic-field]]

[A1] AC is used only through [F7] to ensure the finite-layer class groups are finite; it is not used by the relative ideal-norm construction. [[def-axiom-of-choice]] [[thm-finiteness-of-the-number-field-class-group]]

## Proof

**Given:** AC, a totally real number field $K$, a prime $p$, and its finite cyclotomic layers.

1.1 Unique ideal factorization [F5] and the relative norm definition [F4] give homomorphisms on fractional-ideal groups. Residue degrees multiply in towers by [F9], so the ideal-group maps compose.[F1, F2, F4, F5, F9, given, algebra]

1.2 By the principal-ideal norm identity [F6], the relative norms send principal ideals to principal ideals and descend to the class-group quotients [F1]. They restrict to the $p$-primary components [F3], giving the inverse system defining $X_{K,p}$. Each finite class group is finite by [F7]; that use of AC is recorded in [A1].[F1, F3, F4, F6, F7, A1, step 1.1]

2.1 By [F10], the rational cyclotomic tower in the Statement has procyclic Galois group $\mathbb Z_p$ for odd and even $p$. Since $K$ is finite, $K\cap\mathbb Q_{\mathrm{cyc},p}$ is a finite layer, so $\Gamma=\operatorname{Gal}(K_\infty/K)$ is an open subgroup of $\mathbb Z_p$ and is again isomorphic to $\mathbb Z_p$. Its action commutes with relative ideal norms, so it descends to the inverse limit in step 1.2. By [F8], this gives the stated $\Lambda(\Gamma)$-module structure.[F2, F8, F10, step 1.2, algebra] ∎
