## Statement

Assume Countable Choice. Supply finite balanced polygon Morse data of rank $m=h+k-1\ge1$, with common $w,z$ marks, and no triple intersection of the product tori. Fix a Spin-c structure on the polygon cobordism and use the STRICT all-$w$ normalized strong admissibility condition of the named geometric lattice theorem. Use the same condition for its relevant boundary-pair sectors. The boundary systems have their actual complete $k$-ball cuts, and nearly symmetric geometry has exact constant product-angle generator charts. No $S^3$ or torsion restriction on the intermediate three-manifolds is made.

In the smooth topology, the genuine constant-collar end paths having no nonconstant strip of index at most zero and regularity for every actual index-one strip in the relevant sectors form an open dense subset of the allowed protected end paths. With these paths, compatible coherent source-dependent polygon perturbations can be chosen generically, relative to their faces and any regular parameter endpoints. Their count-dimension-zero total sets are finite, and their count-dimension-one spaces compactify to actual compact one-manifolds. All nonnegative $w,z$ degrees are retained. These conclusions hold for the fixed total Spin-c label, across all its literal realized classes; they also hold for fixed degree vectors or fixed actual classes. The boundary consists exactly of the label-compatible rigid polygon–polygon faces and strip–polygon breakings, with additive marked vectors and actual exhaustive gluing collars. No sphere or one-torus disk bubble occurs in these polygon dimensions. This includes $k=1$.

For triangles the fixed count index is $\mu$, so rigid counts have $\mu=0$ and the chain-map boundary has $\mu=1$. In a generic one-parameter triangle family with fixed regular end data the universal count index is $\mu+1$: the index-minus-one universal counts are finite, and universal index-zero spaces have the parameter endpoints and strip–triangle boundaries. Thus the finite actual counts satisfy the weighted triangle chain-map equation and its auxiliary-data chain homotopy over $\mathbb F_2$. They may be specialized to the two-color ring by sending every $w$ variable to $U$ and every $z$ variable to $V$. No top-generator cycle, handle-map inverse, arbitrary diagram invariance or absolute grading is part of this assertion.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The strict strongly admissible finite balanced data, fixed total Spin-c label, triple-free tori, full marked degrees and actual product-angle ends. Relevant pair sectors are the restrictions of the specified cobordism Spin-c labels to the finitely many boundary Heegaard pairs.

[F1] The actual Spin-c construction identifies the domain-difference lattice and the actual relative index/Chern functional, with its correct restriction and extension orbit under polygon gluing. Strict all-mark admissibility makes nonnegative index directions positive. Hence every relevant pair sector has a uniform lower actual strip index $L$ and a finite set of actual nonnegative domains for every finite upper index band; polygons with fixed total label have the same finite upper-index domain bound. The bounds are independent of coefficient and conformal families. ([[lem-heegaard-polygon-spin-c-difference-is-the-capped-periodic-domain]], [[lem-heegaard-polygon-relative-maslov-variation-is-chern-plus-marks]], [[lem-strong-polygon-admissibility-bounds-indices-and-positive-domains]])

[F2] Source-local polygon universal regularity holds at every nonconstant solution, including all actual index strata. The time-only flat-jet smoothing proof and the time-slice injective-point theorem give the analogous localized autonomous strip argument once a free image site has been shown. ([[thm-source-dependent-nearly-symmetric-polygons-admit-countable-choice-universal-regularity]], [[thm-countable-choice-flat-endpoint-jet-universal-strip-regularity]], [[lem-finite-energy-strip-time-slice-injective-points-are-dense]])

[F3] Actual positivity, domain energy, bubble/strip compactness and unconditional polygon bubble index additivity hold. Every nonconstant boundary bubble has index contribution at least two and every nonconstant sphere at least $2k$. These are geometric operator statements, not assumed regularity of a limiting level. ([[lem-nearly-symmetric-polygons-have-positive-domains-and-degree-energy-bounds]], [[thm-bounded-energy-polygons-have-stable-source-bubble-and-strip-limits]], [[lem-polygon-bubble-limits-have-the-actual-determinant-index-budget]])

[F4] Actual regular finite-tree gluing gives product zero charts, bounded right inverses, nonlinear correction and exhaustion. The finite face induction and boundary-flat small Hilbert perturbations construct coherent polygon coefficients. The compact one-manifold and endpoint-parity construction has been supplied explicitly, independently of Spin-c classification. ## Proof

1.1 Fix common protected divisor geometry and a compatible full boundary tensor, with its actual transverse product angles at the finite generators. The source-local constructions in [F2] preserve whole neighborhoods of these sets. A nonconstant autonomous strip has a free image site: if its whole connected image stayed in the union of the separated divisor and torus neighborhoods, its end generators put it in the torus-neighborhood component. It would miss EVERY region divisor and therefore have zero domain and zero energy by [F3]. On its nonempty free source open set the dense time-slice injective points localize the time-only anti-$J$ variation, and adjoint unique continuation gives the actual universal submersion as in the explicitly reconstructed end argument of [F4]. Countable Choice Hilbert Sard–Smale, over all actual index strata and all relevant finite generator pairs, gives an auxiliary flat-jet path for which EVERY nonconstant strip in those sectors is regular. Its nonzero translation kernel forces its actual index to be at least one. This auxiliary path is not yet asserted to have genuine endpoint collars. [F1, F2, F3, F4, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 Cut this path to its background endpoint values in shrinking time collars. Taylor's integral remainder and flatness give smooth convergence with the same full boundary and generator tensors. Suppose nonconstant strips of index at most zero existed for arbitrarily late cut paths. [F1] puts all those indices in the finite band $[L,0]$ and supplies a common finite domain set and hence [F3]'s uniform energy bound. Extract an actual bubbled chain for the auxiliary all-index regular limit. Each nonconstant strip level has index at least one, each nonconstant boundary bubble contributes at least two and each sphere at least $2k$, by the UNCONDITIONAL index budget [F3]. Thus a nonconstant limit has positive total index, a contradiction. If the limit is entirely constant with no nonconstant bubble, degree additivity at every region divisor gives eventual zero domain and [F3] gives zero energy for the original strips. In the exact generator chart the same conclusion follows from the whole transverse inverse. This explicitly handles the all-constant limit. Consequently a late cut path has NO nonconstant strip of index at most zero in the relevant sectors. [F1, F2, F3, step 1.1, construct, contradiction]

3.1 A nonregular index-one strip cannot persist along infinitely many late cuts either. Its finite band $[L,1]$ has the same type of uniform energy bound. In the auxiliary limit, [F3]'s index budget permits only ONE nonconstant index-one strip and no bubble: two positive levels or one bubble contribute at least two, and an all-constant limit has zero domain. The sole strip is regular by step1.1. Smooth core convergence and the exact product tail equation identify the actual nearby operator with its limit on a fixed core and with the same transverse end operators. Its actual right inverse persists by the cutoff construction: take the limit inverse on a large core, the exact end inverses on its two tails, and a squared partition; choose the cutoff transitions long enough that their $C/T$ error is small, and then the cut path late enough that the fixed-core coefficient error is small. The resulting composition is $I+K$, $\|K\|<1$, so the Neumann series gives an actual right inverse, contradicting nonregularity. Choose one late path satisfying both steps2.1 and3.1 for the finite relevant pairs. This proof did not presume regularity of a target negative-index level or of an index-two bubble. [F1, F2, F3, F4, step 1.1, step 2.1, construct, contradiction]

4.1 This good-end condition is open and dense in the stated smooth topology. Density follows by starting step1.1 within any specified smooth neighborhood of any allowed reference path and taking its late cut approximants there. For openness, if bad paths converged smoothly to a good path, [F1] would again bound every nonpositive-index counterexample in the same finite band and every nonregular index-one counterexample in $[L,1]$. Limit strips for the GOOD path have positive index, regardless of whether higher indices are regular. The unconditional bubble budget therefore excludes a nonpositive counterexample, and forces an index-one counterexample to have a single regular index-one limit with no bubble. The same right-inverse argument as step3.1 excludes it. Entirely constant limits are handled by the zero-domain argument in step2.1. Thus one smooth neighborhood inside the allowed constant-collar paths has the good condition. This assertion concerns the union of genuine positive-collar paths with its relative smooth topology; it does not assert density in a space with a prescribed larger collar width held fixed. [F1, F2, F3, F4, step 1.1, step 2.1, step 3.1, construct, contradiction]

4.2 Build coherent polygon data by the finite face induction in [F4], relative to those end paths, retaining their exact angular charts. In that construction the global zero-mark energy bound was used only for choosing the end paths and for subsequent compactness, not for the source-local universal derivative or the coefficient sewing. Those end paths have now been supplied by steps1.1–3.1 instead. At every arity multiply the dense source-local Hilbert fields by a function positive for every finite neck length and flat at every conformal face. [F2] gives all-index regularity for every nonconstant polygon, across all Spin-c labels; triple-freeness excludes constant stable polygons. For a prescribed one-parameter family include its parameter in the actual derivative and retain regular endpoint collars. The exact same field-density and Sard argument gives universal regularity, not regularity of every intermediate slice. The resulting coefficients meet [F3]'s uniform vertex/neck hypotheses and [F4]'s exponential face-comparison bounds. [F1, F2, F3, F4, step 3.1, construct, algebra]

5.1 Consider a sequence of count dimension zero or one with the fixed total Spin-c label. [F1] bounds all its nonnegative domains and [F3] its energy. Its bubble-tree limit has the same total label under the actual collar restriction/gluing in [F1]; marked vectors add. Every stable polygon vertex is regular by step4.2 and has nonnegative count dimension. Every strip level has index at least one by step2.1, even before any regularity claim for that level. The actual index budget [F3] therefore has only nonnegative terms: polygon count dimensions, strip indices minus one, nodes, and the positive bubble contributions. A bubble contributes at least two, so none occurs in total dimension at most one. A strip of index at least two would contribute at least one in addition to its node and therefore cannot occur either. All remaining strip levels are index-one and regular by step3.1. The only non-interior possibility is ONE node joining two rigid components. For a one-parameter triangle space the parameter is counted once on its triangle vertex; there is no second parameter on its fixed end strip. Its universal triangle component has nonnegative dimension by step4.2. Thus the same exclusion applies, including universal index-minus-one and index-zero cases. This is the precise regular-limiting-level argument; regularity of indices one and two alone was not substituted for it. [F1, F2, F3, step 2.1, step 3.1, step 4.2, construct, algebra]

6.1 Every total rigid set is finite and every one-dimensional set has a genuine compact one-manifold completion, by the construction in [F4] with the energy and limit classification just proved. More explicitly, the all-constant possibility has been excluded, and an unbroken limit converges in the global $H^3$ chart using the exact tail cutoff inverse. A discrete sequentially compact set is finite under Countable Choice by selecting ordered finite distinct tuples and flattening them. For a broken end the actual regular product chart of [F4] gives exactly one exhaustive half-interval. There are finitely many candidate component pairs: their domains are bounded coefficientwise by the finite total-domain list, hence their energies are uniformly bounded even when their individual Spin-c labels range over the allowed restriction orbit. Their rigid moduli have no further breaking by the same index budget, so the preceding finite-set argument applies to this whole bounded list of components. Distinct limiting cores and actual strip phases give disjoint collar endpoints. The resulting Hausdorff second-countable sequentially compact one-manifold is compact under Countable Choice, and its finite interval ends have even parity, exactly as established in [F4]. Restricting degrees or literal classes gives an open-and-closed part because these labels are locally constant in the actual map charts and carried by each explicit neck smoothing. No formal class-finiteness assertion is needed. [F1, F2, F3, F4, step 4.2, step 5.1, construct, algebra]

7.1 Enumerate those actual ends with their actual labels. A stable conformal face is a polygon–polygon composition, and an external break is a rigid end strip with a rigid polygon. The Spin-c restriction orbit is exactly the one in [F1]; the boundary does not select an unrelated extension. Marked-degree additivity gives the product of the two component monomials $U^{n_w}V^{n_z}$ at every end. Thus finite boundary parity gives the weighted chain-map identity for triangles and the polygon composition identities whenever the specified total label is summed over its actual compatible extensions. For a fixed-end one-parameter triangle family, universal dimension zero has fixed-map index minus one; universal dimension one has index zero and adds the two family endpoints. The same parity is exactly the weighted chain homotopy between endpoint triangle maps. All source and family parameters were included once and no triangle translation was removed. Specializing every $w$ variable to $U$ and every $z$ variable to $V$ preserves these finite polynomial identities. This proves the full-marked analytic counts and identities stated, without assuming a top cycle, an inverse map or Floer invariance. [F1, F2, F3, F4, step 5.1, step 6.1, algebra] ∎
