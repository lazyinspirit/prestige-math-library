## Definition

The **extended real line** is

$$\overline{\mathbb{R}} := \mathbb{R} \cup \{-\infty,+\infty\}$$

with the total order extending the usual order on $\mathbb{R}$ and satisfying
$-\infty < x < +\infty$ for every real $x$
([[def-extended-reals]]).

Its **Borel sigma-algebra** is the Borel sigma-algebra of that ordered
topological line in the sense of [[def-borel-sigma-algebra]]. On this page it is
denoted $\mathcal{B}(\overline{\mathbb{R}})$ and is generated by the rays

$$\{\, (a,+\infty] : a \in \mathbb{R} \,\}.$$

In particular, $\{+\infty\}$ and $\{-\infty\}$ are Borel sets.
