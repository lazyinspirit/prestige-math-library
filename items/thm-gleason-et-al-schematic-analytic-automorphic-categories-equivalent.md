## Statement

Let $E$ be a nonarchimedean local field of residue characteristic $p$, let
$\ell\ne p$, let $\Lambda$ be a $\mathbb Z_\ell$-algebra killed by a power
of $\ell$, and let $G/E$ be a connected quasi-split reductive group. Then
Zhu's schematic automorphic category and the Fargues–Scholze analytic
automorphic category are equivalent:
$$\operatorname{Shv}_{!,\Lambda}(B(G))\simeq D^{\mathrm{an}}_\Lambda(\operatorname{Bun}_G).$$
