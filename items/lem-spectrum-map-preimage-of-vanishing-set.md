## Statement

Let $\varphi:R\to A$ be a ring homomorphism of commutative rings, and let $I\trianglelefteq R$ be an ideal. Write $IA$ for the ideal of $A$ generated by $\varphi(I)$. Then $\operatorname{Spec}(\varphi)^{-1}(V(I))=V(IA)$ as subsets of $\operatorname{Spec}(A)$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A ring homomorphism $\varphi:R\to A$ of commutative rings and an ideal $I\trianglelefteq R$.

[L1] $V(K)$ is the set of prime ideals containing $K$ ([[def-prime-spectrum-and-vanishing-sets]]).

[L2] $(\varphi(I))=IA$ is the ideal generated by the image of $I$ ([[def-generated-and-principal-ideals]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Let $\mathfrak q\in\operatorname{Spec}(A)$. Then $\mathfrak q\in\operatorname{Spec}(\varphi)^{-1}(V(I))$ exactly when $\varphi^{-1}(\mathfrak q)\in V(I)$, and by [L1] this is equivalent to $I\subseteq\varphi^{-1}(\mathfrak q)$. That in turn is equivalent to $\varphi(I)\subseteq\mathfrak q$. [L1, given]

2.1 A prime ideal contains the subset $\varphi(I)$ exactly when it contains the ideal generated by that subset, namely $IA$ by [L2]. Hence the condition from step 1.1 is equivalent to $IA\subseteq\mathfrak q$, that is, to $\mathfrak q\in V(IA)$. [L1, L2, algebra]

3.1 Therefore $\operatorname{Spec}(\varphi)^{-1}(V(I))=V(IA)$. [step 1.1, step 2.1] ∎
