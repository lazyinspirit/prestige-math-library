## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $L$ be an A-field of A-characteristic $\mathfrak p$, let $\phi$ be a rank-$r$ Drinfeld A-module over $L$, and let $\mathfrak l$ be a nonzero prime ideal distinct from $\mathfrak p$ when $\mathfrak p\ne0$. Fix a separable closure $L^{\mathrm{sep}}/L$, put $G_L=\operatorname{Gal}(L^{\mathrm{sep}}/L)$, and define $D_{\mathfrak l}=\bigcup_{n\ge1}\phi[\mathfrak l^n](L^{\mathrm{sep}})$ and $T_{\mathfrak l}(\phi)=\operatorname{Hom}_{A_{\mathfrak l}}(K_{\mathfrak l}/A_{\mathfrak l},D_{\mathfrak l})$. For $\sigma\in G_L$, let $\sigma$ act on $T_{\mathfrak l}(\phi)$ by postcomposition, $f\mapsto\sigma\circ f$. Then this action is a continuous $A_{\mathfrak l}$-adic Galois representation of $L$, and $T_{\mathfrak l}(\phi)$ is free of rank $r$ over $A_{\mathfrak l}$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC; a field $L$; a fixed separable closure $L^{\mathrm{sep}}/L$; a rank-$r$ Drinfeld A-module $\phi/L$; and a nonzero prime ideal $\mathfrak l$ outside the A-characteristic.

[A1] [[def-axiom-of-choice]]: AC selects from every family of nonempty sets.

[F1] [[def-absolute-galois-group-of-a-field]]: $G_L$ is the inverse limit of the finite Galois groups of subextensions of $L^{\mathrm{sep}}/L$ with its inverse-limit, or Krull, topology.

[F2] [[def-drinfeld-module-over-an-a-field]]: each $\phi_a$ is an additive polynomial with coefficients in $L$, so every $\sigma\in G_L$ commutes with $\phi_a$.

[F3] [[lem-prime-to-characteristic-drinfeld-torsion-is-free]]: each finite torsion level is a finite free $A/\mathfrak l^n$-module of rank $r$, and the Tate module is free of rank $r$ with its inverse-limit topology.

[F4] [[def-continuous-a-l-adic-galois-representation]]: continuity is measured by the congruence quotients of a finite free $A_{\mathfrak l}$-module.

[F5] [[lem-adic-completion-of-dedekind-domain-at-prime-is-a-dvr]]: $A_{\mathfrak l}$ is a complete DVR with maximal ideal generated by a uniformizer $\pi$ and finite quotients $A_{\mathfrak l}/\pi^nA_{\mathfrak l}$.



**Given:** [A1], [F1]–[F4], and the data in the Statement.

## Proof

1.1 For every $a\in A$ and $x\in L^{\mathrm{sep}}$, the coefficients of $\phi_a$ lie in $L$, so $\sigma(\phi_a(x))=\phi_a(\sigma x)$. Thus each $\sigma\in G_L$ preserves every $\phi[\mathfrak l^n](L^{\mathrm{sep}})$ and the union $D_{\mathfrak l}$. [F1, F2, given, algebra]

2.1 Postcomposition therefore sends $A_{\mathfrak l}$-linear maps $K_{\mathfrak l}/A_{\mathfrak l}\to D_{\mathfrak l}$ to such maps. It defines a group homomorphism $\rho_{\mathfrak l}:G_L\to\operatorname{Aut}_{A_{\mathfrak l}}(T_{\mathfrak l}(\phi))$; it is $A_{\mathfrak l}$-linear because it commutes with the $A$-action on every finite torsion level, hence with its completion action. [F1, F2, step 1.1, algebra]

2.2 For each $n$, the finite set $M_n=\phi[\mathfrak l^n](L^{\mathrm{sep}})$ is stable under $G_L$ by step 1.1. The field $E_n=L(M_n)$ is finite separable because $M_n$ is finite and lies in $L^{\mathrm{sep}}$; it is normal because every $L$-embedding sends solutions of the defining $L$-polynomials to solutions in the same set $M_n$. Hence $E_n/L$ is finite Galois, and the action on $M_n$ factors through the finite group $\operatorname{Gal}(E_n/L)$, whose kernel in $G_L$ is open by [F1]. [F1, F3, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Choose a uniformizer $\pi$ of $A_{\mathfrak l}$ and put $Q_n=\pi^{-n}A_{\mathfrak l}/A_{\mathfrak l}\subset K_{\mathfrak l}/A_{\mathfrak l}$. Restriction of a Tate-module homomorphism to $Q_n$ has kernel $\pi^nT_{\mathfrak l}(\phi)$: $Q_n=\ker(\pi^n:K_{\mathfrak l}/A_{\mathfrak l}\to K_{\mathfrak l}/A_{\mathfrak l})$, so a map vanishing on $Q_n$ factors through multiplication by $\pi^n$, and precomposition by that multiplication equals multiplication by $\pi^n$ on the Hom module. The restricted maps take values in the finite module $M_n$, and their Galois action factors through $\operatorname{Gal}(E_n/L)$ by step 2.2. Since $\pi^nA_{\mathfrak l}=\mathfrak l^nA_{\mathfrak l}$, the action on each congruence quotient $T_{\mathfrak l}(\phi)/\mathfrak l^nT_{\mathfrak l}(\phi)$ is continuous; these quotients define the topology in [F4], so $\rho_{\mathfrak l}$ is continuous. [F1, F3, F4, F5, step 2.2, algebra]

4.1 Under [A1], the freeness and rank assertion are supplied by [F3]; steps 1.1–1.4 establish that its natural Galois action is the continuous representation asserted in the Statement. [A1, F3, step 1.1, step 3.1] ∎

## Remarks

The proof uses AC and, through the finite-level compatible-basis construction in [F3], the DC implication of AC. Once the torsion levels are given, the Galois action and its continuity argument in steps 1.1–1.4 are choice-free.
