## Definition

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Input a nonzero rational polynomial $F\in\mathbb Q[T]$. Return a monic irreducible $h\in\mathbb Z[T]$, the exact field $L=\mathbb Q[Z]/(h)$, and a complete list of distinct roots of $F$ in $L$ with their multiplicities, such that these roots generate $L$ over $\mathbb Q$. Field elements are rational polynomial residue classes, and all tests below use exact rational arithmetic. If $F$ is constant, return $h(Z)=Z$, $L=\mathbb Q$, and the empty root list. The zero polynomial is rejected as invalid input.

1. Compute the monic squarefree part $S=F/\gcd(F,F')$, normalized to be monic, by polynomial division and gcd. Let $t=\deg S$.

2. Enumerate candidate monic integer polynomials $g$ of positive degree together with ordered $t$-tuples of rational residues in $\mathbb Q[Z]/(g)$. Use a diagonal enumeration by an increasing integer bound on the degree, absolute integer coefficients, rational numerators and positive denominators, so every finite candidate appears. Decide whether $g$ is irreducible as follows. Set $R=1+\max_{j<\deg g}|g_j|$ and $M=\lceil2^{\deg g}R^{\deg g}\rceil$. Enumerate all monic integer polynomials of degrees $1$ through $\lfloor\deg g/2\rfloor$ whose coefficients have absolute value at most $M$, and test exact division into $g$. The existence of one such divisor means reducible; otherwise accept $g$ as irreducible. A linear polynomial is accepted immediately.

3. For every irreducible candidate, test that the tuple entries $\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_t$ are pairwise distinct and that $S(T)=\prod_{i=1}^t(T-\rho_i)$ in $(\mathbb Q[Z]/(g))[T]$. Stop at the first passing certificate. Compute each multiplicity in $F$ by repeatedly dividing by $T-\rho_i$ until the remainder is nonzero.

4. In this temporary field, compute the rational subalgebra generated by the roots: begin with the basis list $(1)$; whenever a product of a current basis vector by a root is outside the rational span of the list, append that product. Repeat until every such product is in the span. All independence and coordinate tests are rational Gaussian elimination in the temporary power basis.

5. Let $s$ be the length of the resulting basis $b_1,\ldots,b_s$. Enumerate integer linear combinations $\theta=\sum_i c_i b_i$ until $1,\theta,\ldots,\theta^{s-1}$ are rationally independent. Solve for the unique monic degree-$s$ polynomial $h_0$ with $h_0(\theta)=0$. Let $e\ge1$ be a common positive denominator of its coefficients, put $\eta=e\theta$ and $h(Z)=e^s h_0(Z/e)\in\mathbb Z[Z]$, and express every $\rho_i$ in the basis $1,\eta,\ldots,\eta^{s-1}$. Return $h$, those exact expressions, and the multiplicities. The returned field is the subfield generated by the roots, rather than an arbitrary larger field that happened to contain a factorization certificate.
