## Statement

Let $\ell>2$ be odd and $\zeta$ a primitive $\ell$-th root of unity. The
generator assignment
$$\phi_\zeta:U^{DK}_\zeta(\mathfrak{sl}_2)\longrightarrow U^L_\zeta(\mathfrak{sl}_2),\qquad E\mapsto E,\quad F\mapsto F,\quad K^{\pm1}\mapsto K^{\pm1}$$
is a well-defined algebra homomorphism, but it is not injective: it kills
$E^\ell$, which is nonzero in its source.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The rank-one forms of
[[def-root-of-unity-quantum-group-forms]], the divided-power integral form
[[def-lusztig-divided-power-integral-form]], and a primitive odd root
$\zeta$ of order $\ell>2$.

[F1] The rank-one De Concini–Kac source is generated by $E,F,K^{\pm1}$ with
$KEK^{-1}=\zeta^2E$, $KFK^{-1}=\zeta^{-2}F$, and
$EF-FE=(K-K^{-1})/(\zeta-\zeta^{-1})$
([[def-drinfeld-jimbo-quantized-enveloping-algebra]],
[[def-root-of-unity-quantum-group-forms]]). An assignment satisfying these
three-generator relations extends to a homomorphism from the presented
rank-one source.

[F2] In the Lusztig integral form,
$EF-FE=T_{1,0,1}=(K-K^{-1})/(v-v^{-1})$ before specialization; the toral
product defining $T_{1,0,1}$ specializes by substituting $v=\zeta$
([[lem-divided-power-and-toral-quantum-binomial-reordering-identities]],
[[def-lusztig-divided-power-integral-form]]).

[F3] $E^\ell$ is nonzero in the source by the rank-one PBW basis, and the
divided power $E^{(\ell)}$ is an element of the Lusztig form
([[lem-sl2-small-quantum-group-root-power-quotient-is-hopf]],
[[def-lusztig-divided-power-integral-form]]).

[F4] $E^\ell=[\ell]_v!E^{(\ell)}$ in the generic divided-power algebra, by
the definition $E^{(\ell)}=E^\ell/[\ell]_v!$
([[def-lusztig-divided-power-integral-form]],
[[def-quantum-integers-factorials-and-divided-powers-at-q-i]]).

## Proof

1.1 The denominator $\zeta-\zeta^{-1}$ is nonzero because $\ell>2$. The images of $E,F,K^{\pm1}$ in $U^L_\zeta(\mathfrak{sl}_2)$ satisfy the two toral conjugation relations by specialization of the generic defining relations. By [F2], their commutator is the specialization of $T_{1,0,1}$, which equals $(K-K^{-1})/(\zeta-\zeta^{-1})$. Hence they satisfy all rank-one De Concini–Kac defining relations, and the presentation's universal property gives the stated homomorphism. [given, F1, F2, construct, algebra]

1.2 By [F4], the image of $E^\ell$ is $[\ell]_\zeta!E^{(\ell)}$. Since $[\ell]_\zeta=(\zeta^\ell-\zeta^{-\ell})/(\zeta-\zeta^{-1})=0$, the product $[\ell]_\zeta!$ is zero, so $\phi_\zeta(E^\ell)=0$. [F4, algebra]

2.1 By [F3], $E^\ell$ is nonzero in the source. Thus $\phi_\zeta$ has a nonzero kernel and is not injective. [step 1.2, F3] ∎
