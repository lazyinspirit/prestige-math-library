## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]). Let $R$ be a commutative
ring and let $S$ be a standard smooth $R$-algebra
([[def-ag-standard-smooth-algebra]]), presented as
$S\cong\bigl(R[x_1,\dots,x_n]/(f_1,\dots,f_c)\bigr)_g$ with leading $c\times c$
Jacobian minor $h$ mapping to a unit of $S$. Let
$\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Spec}R$, put $\kappa=\kappa(\mathfrak p)$, and let
$K/\kappa$ be a field extension. Write
$$F=S\otimes_R\kappa,\qquad F_K=F\otimes_\kappa K,$$
so that, by base change of the presentation,
$F_K\cong\bigl(K[x_1,\dots,x_n]/(\bar f_1,\dots,\bar f_c)\bigr)_{\bar g}$, where
$\bar f_j,\bar g$ are the images of $f_j,g$ in $K[x_1,\dots,x_n]$. Then:

1. every local ring $(F_K)_{Q}$ of $F_K$ at a prime $Q$ is a regular local ring;
   if $Q'\subseteq K[x_1,\dots,x_n]$ is the prime corresponding to $Q$, then
   $\dim (F_K)_{Q}=\operatorname{ht}(Q')-c$
   ([[def-height-of-a-prime-ideal]]);
2. every irreducible component of $\operatorname{Spec}F_K$ has dimension $n-c$;
   equivalently, for every minimal prime $P$ of $F_K$ one has
   $\dim(F_K/P)=n-c$.

Both clauses are vacuous when $F_K$ is the zero ring, and no hypothesis is
placed on $R$ or on the field extension $K/\kappa$. The relative dimension
$n-c$ is the dimension of the components, not the dimension of every local
ring: a local ring at the generic point of a component has dimension $0$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A commutative ring $R$, a standard smooth presentation $S\cong(R[x_1,\dots,x_n]/(f_1,\dots,f_c))_g$ with leading $c\times c$ minor $h$ a unit of $S$, a prime $\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Spec}R$, a field extension $K/\kappa(\mathfrak p)$, and the Axiom of Choice.

[F1] [[def-ag-standard-smooth-algebra]]: a standard smooth presentation consists of integers $n\ge c\ge0$, elements $f_1,\dots,f_c,g\in R[x_1,\dots,x_n]$ with $S\cong(R[x_1,\dots,x_n]/(f_1,\dots,f_c))_g$, such that the Jacobian matrix $(\partial f_j/\partial x_i)$ has a $c\times c$ minor whose image in $S$ is a unit; $n-c$ is the relative dimension, and the invertible minor may be assumed to be the leading one, in the first $c$ columns.

[F2] [[lem-ag-base-change-of-standard-smooth-presentations]]: for a ring map $R\to R'$ and a standard smooth presentation as above, $R'\otimes_RS\cong(R'[x_1,\dots,x_n]/(f'_1,\dots,f'_c))_{g'}$ with the same $n,c$, and the image of $h$ is again a unit.

[F3] [[lem-ag-standard-smooth-fibre-regular-parameters]]: under the Axiom of Choice, if $k$ is a field, $\mathfrak q\subseteq k[x_1,\dots,x_n]$ is prime and $f_1,\dots,f_c\in\mathfrak q$ have leading $c\times c$ Jacobian minor $h\notin\mathfrak q$, then $k[x_1,\dots,x_n]_{\mathfrak q}$ is regular local, $(f_1,\dots,f_c)$ is a regular sequence in it, and the quotient is regular local of dimension $\dim k[x_1,\dots,x_n]_{\mathfrak q}-c$.

[F5] [[cor-minimal-prime-has-height-zero]]: a minimal prime ideal of a commutative ring has height $0$.

[F7] [[prop-localisation-zero-equality-and-kernel-criteria]]: for a multiplicative set $S\subseteq R$ and $r\in R$, the class $r/1$ is zero in $S^{-1}R$ if and only if $ur=0$ for some $u\in S$; a fraction $r/s$ equals $r'/s'$ if and only if $u(rs'-r's)=0$ for some $u\in S$.

[F8] [[thm-prime-spectrum-of-a-localisation-bijection]]: for a multiplicative set $S\subseteq R$, contraction along $R\to S^{-1}R$ is an inclusion-preserving bijection from $\operatorname{Spec}(S^{-1}R)$ onto the primes of $R$ disjoint from $S$, with inverse $\mathfrak p\mapsto S^{-1}\mathfrak p$.

[F9] [[prop-iterated-localisation]]: for multiplicative sets $S,T\subseteq R$ with images $\bar T$ in $S^{-1}R$ and $U$ the multiplicative set generated by $S\cup T$, there is a unique $R$-algebra isomorphism $\bar T^{-1}(S^{-1}R)\cong U^{-1}R$.

[F10] [[thm-localisation-commutes-with-quotients]]: for an ideal $I\trianglelefteq R$ and a multiplicative set $S$, the image $\bar S$ of $S$ in $R/I$ gives a canonical isomorphism $(S^{-1}R)/(S^{-1}I)\cong\bar S^{-1}(R/I)$, with both sides zero when $S\cap I\ne\varnothing$.

[F11] [[cor-height-plus-quotient-dimension-affine-domain]]: under the Axiom of Choice, for a field $k$, a finite-type $k$-domain $A$ and $\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Spec}A$ one has $\operatorname{ht}(\mathfrak p)+\dim(A/\mathfrak p)=\dim A$.

[F12] [[cor-dimension-of-a-finite-polynomial-ring-over-a-field]]: for a field $k$ and $n\ge0$, $\dim k[x_1,\dots,x_n]=n$.

[F13] [[thm-irreducible-components-and-minimal-primes]]: under the Axiom of Choice, the irreducible components of $\operatorname{Spec}R$ are exactly the closed sets $V(\mathfrak p)$ for minimal primes $\mathfrak p$ of $R$, each minimal prime giving a unique component.

[F14] [[lem-quotient-spectrum-map-is-closed]]: for an ideal $I\trianglelefteq R$, contraction along $R\to R/I$ is a homeomorphism from $\operatorname{Spec}(R/I)$ onto $V(I)$.

[F15] [[def-height-of-a-prime-ideal]]: the height of a prime ideal $\mathfrak p$ is $\operatorname{ht}(\mathfrak p)=\dim(R_{\mathfrak p})$.



[F16] [[thm-affine-domain-dimension-transcendence-degree]]: a finite-type domain $A$ over a field $K$ has dimension $\operatorname{trdeg}_K\operatorname{Frac}(A)$.

## Proof

1.1 Set $B:=K[x_1,\dots,x_n]/(\bar f_1,\dots,\bar f_c)$ and $\bar h$ for the image of $h$ in $B$. By [F2] applied to $R\to\kappa$ the fibre is $F\cong(\kappa[x_1,\dots,x_n]/(\bar f_1,\dots,\bar f_c))_{\bar g}$, and applying [F2] again to the ring map $\kappa\to K$ shows that $F_K\cong B_{\bar g}$, with the image of $\bar h$ in $B_{\bar g}$ a unit; the relative dimension $n-c$ is unchanged throughout. [F1, F2]

2.1 Primes of $B_{\bar g}$ correspond under [F8] to the primes $P\subseteq B$ with $\bar g\notin P$, and these in turn correspond to the primes $Q'\subseteq K[x_1,\dots,x_n]$ with $(\bar f_1,\dots,\bar f_c)\subseteq Q'$ and $\bar g\notin Q'$. For such a $Q'$ one has $\bar h\notin Q'$: since the image of $\bar h$ in $B_{\bar g}$ is a unit, there are $v\in B$ and $N\ge0$ with $\bar h v=\bar g^N$ in $B_{\bar g}$, so by [F7] there is $M\ge0$ with $\bar g^M(\bar hv-\bar g^N)=0$ in $B$; if $\bar h$ lay in $Q'$, hence in $P=Q'/(\bar f_1,\dots,\bar f_c)$, the contradiction $\bar g^{M+N}\in P$ with $\bar g\notin P$ would follow. [F7, F8, step 1.1]

3.1 Let $Q$ be a prime of $B_{\bar g}$, let $P\subseteq B$ be the prime it contracts to and let $Q'\subseteq K[x_1,\dots,x_n]$ be the corresponding prime; then $(F_K)_Q=B_P=(K[x_1,\dots,x_n]_{Q'})/(\bar f_1,\dots,\bar f_c)$. Indeed $B_{\bar g}=(K[x_1,\dots,x_n]/(\bar f_1,\dots,\bar f_c))_{\bar g}$, localising further at $Q$ gives $B_P$ by [F9], and [F10] identifies $B_P$ with the quotient of $K[x_1,\dots,x_n]_{Q'}$ by the ideal generated by the $\bar f_j$. [F9, F10, step 2.1]

4.1 Regularity of local rings. In the situation of step 3.1 the elements $\bar f_1,\dots,\bar f_c$ lie in the prime $Q'$ and, by step 2.1, have leading minor $\bar h\notin Q'$; so [F3] applies over the field $K$ and shows that $(F_K)_Q=(K[x_1,\dots,x_n]_{Q'})/(\bar f_1,\dots,\bar f_c)$ is a regular local ring with $\dim(F_K)_Q=\dim K[x_1,\dots,x_n]_{Q'}-c=\operatorname{ht}(Q')-c$, the last equality by [F15]. This proves clause 1, including for $c=0$, where the empty leading minor is $1$ and the quotient is $K[x_1,\dots,x_n]_{Q'}$. [F3, F15, step 2.1, step 3.1]

5.1 Height of the ambient prime at a component. Let $\mathfrak P$ be a minimal prime of $F_K=B_{\bar g}$, let $P$ be its contraction to $B$, and let $Q'$ be the corresponding prime of $K[x_1,\dots,x_n]$. The prime $P$ is minimal in $B$: a prime strictly below it avoids $\bar g$ and would localize to a prime strictly below $\mathfrak P$ by [F8]. The local ring $(F_K)_{\mathfrak P}$ has dimension zero by [F5, F15], so clause 1, already proved in step 4.1, gives $0=\operatorname{ht}(Q')-c$. Thus $\operatorname{ht}(Q')=c$, also when $c=0$. [F5, F8, F15, step 4.1]

6.1 Component dimension. Put $A:=K[x_1,\dots,x_n]/Q'$, a finite-type $K$-domain, and let $g_A$ be the image of $\bar g$. Then $g_A\ne0$, and [F10] gives $F_K/\mathfrak P\cong A_{g_A}$. The localization is again a finite-type $K$-domain (adjoin $z$ with $zg_A=1$), and has the same fraction field as $A$. Applying [F16] to both rings gives $\dim A_{g_A}=\dim A$. Now [F11, F12] and step 5.1 give $\dim A=n-\operatorname{ht}(Q')=n-c$. By [F13, F14], the component $V(\mathfrak P)$ is homeomorphic to $\operatorname{Spec}(F_K/\mathfrak P)$, so has dimension $n-c$. [F10, F11, F12, F13, F14, F16, step 5.1]

7.1 Both clauses hold: every local ring of $F_K$ is regular local of dimension $\operatorname{ht}(Q')-c$ by step 4.1, and every irreducible component has dimension $n-c$ by step 6.1; if $F_K$ is the zero ring there are no primes and no components, so both clauses are vacuous. ∎
