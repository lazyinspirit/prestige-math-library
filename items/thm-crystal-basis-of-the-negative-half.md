## Statement

Let $\mathcal L(\infty)\subseteq U_q^-$ and $\mathcal B(\infty)\subseteq\mathcal L(\infty)/q\mathcal L(\infty)$ be the Kashiwara lattice and generating set of [[def-kashiwara-lattices-lambda-and-infinity]], with the Kashiwara operators $\tilde e_i,\tilde f_i$ on $U_q^-$ of [[thm-the-i-string-decomposition-of-the-negative-half]](iii).

(i) $(\mathcal L(\infty),\mathcal B(\infty))$ is a crystal basis of $U_q^-$ in the sense of [[def-crystal-lattice-and-crystal-basis]]: $\mathcal L(\infty)$ is a free $A_0$-submodule with $U_q^-\cong\mathbb Q(q)\otimes_{A_0}\mathcal L(\infty)$, stable under $\tilde e_i$ and $\tilde f_i$; and $\mathcal B(\infty)$ is a $\mathbb Q$-basis of $\mathcal L(\infty)/q\mathcal L(\infty)$ satisfying $\tilde e_i\mathcal B(\infty)\subseteq\mathcal B(\infty)\cup\{0\}$, $\tilde f_i\mathcal B(\infty)\subseteq\mathcal B(\infty)$, and $\tilde f_ib=b'$ if and only if $b=\tilde e_ib'$ for all $b,b'\in\mathcal B(\infty)$.

(ii) The root-graded components are free: $\mathcal L(\infty)=\bigoplus_{\nu\in Q_+}\mathcal L(\infty)_{-\nu}$ and each $\mathcal L(\infty)_{-\nu}$ is a free $A_0$-module of rank $\#\mathcal B(\infty)_{-\nu}=\dim_{\mathbb Q(q)}U_q^-[-\nu]$.

(iii) For every $\lambda\in P^+$ the projection $\pi_\lambda(P)=Pv_\lambda$ maps $\mathcal B(\infty)$ onto $\mathcal B(\lambda)\cup\{0\}$, and the compatibility statements of [[lem-crystal-operators-commute-with-highest-weight-projection-modulo-q]] hold: $\pi_\lambda(\mathcal L(\infty)_{-\alpha})=\mathcal L(\lambda)_{\lambda-\alpha}$, $\{b\in\mathcal B(\infty)_{-\alpha}:\pi_\lambda(b)\neq0\}\cong\mathcal B(\lambda)_{\lambda-\alpha}$, $\tilde f_i\pi_\lambda=\pi_\lambda\tilde f_i$ and $\tilde e_i\pi_\lambda(b)=\pi_\lambda(\tilde e_ib)$ whenever $\pi_\lambda(b)\neq0$.

(iv) The root-graded crystal has finite $\varepsilon_i$ on each element, formal $\varphi_i=\varepsilon_i+\langle h_i,\mathrm{wt}\rangle$, and actual lowering length $\ell_i^-=\infty$. Every $\tilde f_i$-iterate is nonzero because $\tilde e_i\tilde f_i=\mathrm{id}$ holds on its lattice and residue. Thus it is the infinite lowering crystal of the unit, with the negative-half convention of the crystal-graph definition.

**Choice.** The axiom of choice is declared via [[def-axiom-of-choice]] and enters only through the grand loop: the partial-inverse axiom in (i) uses clause J(r) of [[thm-grand-loop-existence-of-highest-weight-crystal-bases]], whose proof consumes the AC-dependent form comparison of the negative-half form. The freeness and rank statements are choice-free.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The negative half $U_q^-$ with its root grading and operators, and the lattice $\mathcal L(\infty)$ generated by the monomials $\tilde f_{i_1}\cdots\tilde f_{i_r}\mathbf 1$.

[F1] Structure of the lattice: $\mathcal L(\infty)=\bigoplus_{\nu\in Q_+}\mathcal L(\infty)_{-\nu}$ is generated by the lowering monomials, each component is finitely generated and torsionfree, and it is a free $A_0$-module by the PID supplier ([[def-kashiwara-lattices-lambda-and-infinity]], [[cor-finitely-generated-torsion-free-modules-over-a-pid-are-free]], [[def-the-local-ring-at-q-zero]]).

[F2] Grand loop clauses: H(r) ($\tilde e_i\mathcal L(\infty)_{-\alpha}\subseteq\mathcal L(\infty)$), I(r) ($\tilde e_i\mathcal B(\infty)_{-\alpha}\subseteq\mathcal B(\infty)\cup\{0\}$), J(r) (if $\tilde e_ib\neq0$ then $b=\tilde f_i\tilde e_ib$), K(r) ($\mathcal B(\infty)_{-\alpha}$ is a $\mathbb Q$-basis of $\mathcal L(\infty)_{-\alpha}/q\mathcal L(\infty)_{-\alpha}$), and the large-weight lattice isomorphism $\pi_\lambda:\mathcal L(\infty)_{-\alpha}\to\mathcal L(\lambda)_{\lambda-\alpha}$ for $\lambda$ large ([[thm-grand-loop-existence-of-highest-weight-crystal-bases]]).

[F3] On the negative half $\tilde e_i\tilde f_i=\mathrm{id}$, $\tilde f_i$ is injective, $\tilde e_iu=0$ if and only if $u\in\ker e_i'$, and $\tilde e_i$ lowers the root height by one ([[thm-the-i-string-decomposition-of-the-negative-half]]).

[F4] Projection lemma: for every $\alpha$ and $\lambda$ one has $\pi_\lambda(\mathcal L(\infty)_{-\alpha})=\mathcal L(\lambda)_{\lambda-\alpha}$, the surviving residue bijection, and the $\tilde e_i$- and $\tilde f_i$-compatibilities on surviving classes; clause (c) inherits the axiom of choice ([[lem-crystal-operators-commute-with-highest-weight-projection-modulo-q]], [[def-axiom-of-choice]]).

[F5] In module crystals the raising and lowering lengths are finite. The negative-half convention of the crystal graph is: finite $\varepsilon_i$, formal $\varphi_i=\varepsilon_i+\langle h_i,\mathrm{wt}\rangle$ and actual lowering length $\ell_i^-=\infty$ ([[def-crystal-graph-epsilon-phi-and-weight]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct. Every assertion is read off from the grand loop and the projection lemma.

1.1 Freeness and scalar extension. Each component is finitely generated and torsionfree, hence free over the PID $A_0$ by [F1]. As in the grand-loop conclusion [F2], enumerate $A_0$ and the finite ordered word generators of each component and take the first finite tuple of their linear combinations that is a basis. These uniquely specified component bases unite to a basis of the graded direct sum without simultaneous choice. For field spanning, use the grand-loop field-spanning argument [F2]: if $x=\sum_nF_i^{(n)}x_n$ is homogeneous, then $F_ix=\tilde f_i\bigl(\sum_n[n+1]_iF_i^{(n)}x_n\bigr)$, and the vector in parentheses has the same root degree as $x$. Thus every positive-height component, being the sum of the $F_i$-images of preceding components, is also spanned by their $\tilde f_i$-images. Induction from $U_q^-[0]=\mathbb Q(q)\mathbf1$ shows that the lowering words span each field component. The canonical map $\mathbb Q(q)\otimes_{A_0}\mathcal L(\infty)_{-\alpha}\to U_q^-[-\alpha]$ is therefore surjective. It is injective by clearing denominators: a finite tensor sum equals $d^{-1}\otimes z$ for some $0\ne d\in A_0$ and $z\in\mathcal L(\infty)_{-\alpha}$, and its image $d^{-1}z$ vanishes only when $z=0$. Taking graded direct sums gives the claimed scalar-extension isomorphism. [F1, F2, F3, algebra]

1.2 Stability and residue basis. Stability under $\tilde f_i$ holds by construction, since a $\tilde f_i$-image of a lowering monomial is a lowering monomial; stability under $\tilde e_i$ is clause H(r). Clause K(r) says that $\mathcal B(\infty)_{-\alpha}$ is a $\mathbb Q$-basis of $\mathcal L(\infty)_{-\alpha}/q\mathcal L(\infty)_{-\alpha}$, and clause I(r) gives $\tilde e_i\mathcal B(\infty)\subseteq\mathcal B(\infty)\cup\{0\}$. For $\tilde f_i$, the image of a monomial is a monomial and $\tilde e_i\tilde f_i=\mathrm{id}$ prevents it from vanishing modulo $q$: if $\tilde f_ib\in q\mathcal L(\infty)$ then $b=\tilde e_i\tilde f_ib\in q\mathcal L(\infty)$, contradicting $b\neq0$ in the crystal limit. [F1, F2, F3]

1.3 The partial-inverse axiom. If $\tilde f_ib=b'$ with $b,b'\in\mathcal B(\infty)$, then applying $\tilde e_i$ and [F3] gives $b=\tilde e_i\tilde f_ib=\tilde e_ib'$. Conversely, if $b=\tilde e_ib'\neq0$, then $b'=\tilde f_i\tilde e_ib'$ by clause J(r), so $\tilde f_ib=\tilde f_i\tilde e_ib'=b'$ by [F3]; hence $\tilde f_ib=b'$ if and only if $b=\tilde e_ib'$, and this is the only place where the grand-loop clause J(r), and with it the inherited axiom of choice, is used. [F2, F3]

2.1 The projection statements. The lattice projection, surviving-residue bijection and operator compatibilities in (iii) are [F4]. To see that zero is also attained, fix $i\in I$: by step 1.2 every $\tilde f_i^n\mathbf1$ has nonzero negative-half residue, while lowering compatibility projects it to $\tilde f_i^nv_\lambda$. The latter is zero for sufficiently large $n$ because the module lowering length is finite by [F5]. Thus the image is exactly $\mathcal B(\lambda)\cup\{0\}$. [F4, F5, step 1.2]

2.2 The rank formula. By [F1] and clause K(r), the free rank of $\mathcal L(\infty)_{-\alpha}$ is $\#\mathcal B(\infty)_{-\alpha}$, and by step 1.1 the scalar extension is $U_q^-[-\alpha]$, so this number is also $\dim_{\mathbb Q(q)}U_q^-[-\alpha]$; this is (ii). [F1, F2, step 1.1]

3.1 The infinite chain. By [F3] the operator $\tilde f_i$ is injective on $U_q^-$ and $\tilde e_i\tilde f_i=\mathrm{id}$, and by step 1.2 no iterate of a residue element vanishes; hence $\ell_i^-=+\infty$ for every $b\in\mathcal B(\infty)$, while $\varepsilon_i(b)<\infty$ by the finite string structure of the negative half (the height bound). The formal $\varphi_i=\varepsilon_i+\langle h_i,\mathrm{wt}\rangle$ is the convention of [F5] and is not the lowering-chain length. [F3, F5, step 1.2] ∎
