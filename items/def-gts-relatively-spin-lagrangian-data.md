## Definition

Let $(M,\omega)$ be a smooth symplectic manifold and $i:L\hookrightarrow M$ a compact smooth embedded submanifold without boundary of dimension $n=\frac12\dim M$. Lagrangian means $i^*\omega=0$: at each $p\in L$ this says $T_pL\subseteq(T_pL)^\omega$, where $W^\omega=\{v\in T_pM:\omega_p(v,w)=0\text{ for all }w\in W\}$. The isomorphism $\omega_p^\flat:T_pM\to T_p^*M$ identifies $(T_pL)^\omega$ with the annihilator of $T_pL$, which has dimension $2n-n=n$; hence the inclusion is equality, the tangent-space Lagrangian condition. Choose as auxiliary data a locally finite triangulation of the pair $(M,L)$: a locally finite simplicial complex $K$, a homeomorphism $h:|K|\to M$, and a subcomplex $K_L\subseteq K$ with $h(|K_L|)=L$. For each integer $q\ge0$, let $K^{(q)}$ be the union of simplices of dimension at most $q$ and write $M^{(q)}=h(|K^{(q)}|)$. Put $L^{(2)}=L\cap M^{(2)}=h(|K_L^{(2)}|)$ and let $j_2:L^{(2)}\hookrightarrow M^{(2)}$ be the inclusion.

For a Euclidean vector space $\mathbb R^r$ with $r\ge1$, its real Clifford algebra is generated by $\mathbb R^r$ with relations $v^2=-\lVert v\rVert^2$. The group $\operatorname{Spin}(r)$ consists of even products of unit vectors in the Clifford algebra, and its action on $\mathbb R^r$ by conjugation gives $\rho:\operatorname{Spin}(r)\to\operatorname{SO}(r)$; this is the standard double cover for $r\ge2$, while for $r=1$ it is the two-to-one map $\{\pm1\}\to\{1\}$. For $r=0$, set $\operatorname{Spin}(0)=\operatorname{SO}(0)=\{1\}$, so the rank-zero bundle has its unique identity lift. A spin structure on an oriented rank-$r$ Euclidean vector bundle $E\to X$ is a principal $\operatorname{Spin}(r)$-bundle $\widetilde P\to X$ together with a map to the oriented orthonormal frame bundle $P_{\operatorname{SO}}(E)$ that is equivariant under $\rho$ and covers the identity on $X$.

A relative-spin datum for $L\subset M$ consists of an orientation and Euclidean bundle metric on $TL$, an oriented finite-rank Euclidean vector bundle $V\to M^{(3)}$, and a spin structure on the bundle $TL|_{L^{(2)}}\oplus j_2^*(V|_{M^{(2)}})$ over $L^{(2)}$, with its direct-sum orientation and metric. The submanifold is relatively spin if such a background bundle and lift exist; a chosen tuple is a relative-spin structure. This is the bundle-and-lift form of the source's relative-spin data. A spin structure on $L$ itself supplies the special case with trivial rank-zero $V$, by restriction to $L^{(2)}$.

This definition records orientation and relative-spin choices as part of Lagrangian brane data. It does not assert existence for every Lagrangian.
