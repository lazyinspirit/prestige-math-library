## Discussion

Under countable choice, the classical region [[thm-riemann-zeta-classical-zero-free-region]] is a statement about zeta with an absolute constant. The progression theorem [[thm-prime-number-theorem-arithmetic-progressions]] fixes q before its character transforms and contour neighborhoods are chosen. Its argument supplies neither a rate uniform as q grows nor a uniform Dirichlet L-function zero-free region. No existence of exceptional real zeros is claimed here; this is a scope distinction, not a counterexample to an asserted uniform theorem.
