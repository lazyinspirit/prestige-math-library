## Statement refuted

Every braided monoidal category is symmetric.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The braid category $\mathcal B$.

[L1] The braid category is braided monoidal by construction ([[def-the-braid-category]]).

[L2] The group $B_2$ is infinite cyclic, generated by $\sigma_1$ ([[thm-the-two-strand-braid-group-is-infinite-cyclic]]).

## Counterexample

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 By [L1], $\mathcal B$ is a braided monoidal category. Its braiding on the generating object $1\otimes1=2$ is the crossing braid $\sigma_1\in B_2$. [given, L1, algebra]

2.1 By [L2], $\sigma_1$ has infinite order, so in particular $\sigma_1^2\neq1_{2}$. Therefore the square of the braiding on $1\otimes1$ is not the identity. [L2, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 A symmetric braiding must square to the identity on every tensor product. Step 2.1 shows that this fails in $\mathcal B$, so $\mathcal B$ is braided but not symmetric. [step 2.1, algebra] ∎
