## Definition

Let $\varphi \in C_c^\infty(\mathbb{R}^n)$ satisfy

$$ \int_{\mathbb{R}^n} \varphi(x)\,dx = 1. $$

For $\varepsilon > 0$, define

$$ \varphi_\varepsilon(x) := \varepsilon^{-n}\varphi(x/\varepsilon) \qquad(x \in \mathbb{R}^n). $$

The family $(\varphi_\varepsilon)_{\varepsilon>0}$ is the **mollifier family
generated by $\varphi$**.
