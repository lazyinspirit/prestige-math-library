## Statement

Assume Countable Choice and the actual hypotheses and conclusions of bubbled transverse-strip compactness. Suppose a sequence of actual nonconstant $J_{n,t}$-holomorphic strips has exactly ONE limiting level: the constant generator $x$ with ONE nonconstant disk $v$ attached directly at a point of its lower boundary, with no other bubbles or ghosts. Require the full energy identity, smooth convergence on component cores, and collapse of all omitted necks and outer tails supplied by that theorem. The coefficients converge smoothly, their full structures on the physical boundary manifolds are fixed, and all have common exact generator charts with $J_{n,t}=i$ there for every $t$.

The bubble is the actual autonomous $J_{\infty,0}$ disk with boundary-node value $x$. Suppose its compact node-fixed $H^3\to H^2$ linearization is onto and has real index two. Then, for all sufficiently large $n$, the actual strip linearization $D_{u_n}:H^1\to L^2$ is onto of real index two. It is also onto in every actual integer higher realization $H^{q+1}\cap X_0\to H^q$, $q\ge0$, with only the original zeroth physical boundary trace. The upper-boundary assertion is identical after the holomorphic source reflection exchanging the two edges.

No common positive time-collar width on which $J_{n,t}$ is autonomous is required. No rate of component convergence relative to neck length is required. The assertion concerns actual sequences with this precise nodal limit and a regular node-fixed disk, not all low-index sequences or unclassified bubbles. It proves neither that the disk exists or has count one, nor nonlinear attachment or global cancellation.

## Proof

1.1 Translate by the actual boundary bubble centers used in the extraction, putting each extracted center at zero, and use one fixed holomorphic boundary source coordinate $z$ there, with nonzero derivative. The bubble extraction in [F1] gives positive scales $\epsilon_n\to0$ and, after a fixed positive affine normalization, actual profiles $v_n(\zeta)=u_n(z^{-1}(\epsilon_n\zeta))$ converging smoothly to $v$ on every fixed compact subset of the closed upper halfplane. Its marked node is $\zeta=\infty$. Choose a small fixed $r>0$ so the disk tail $|\zeta|>1/(2r)$ and the node chart of the constant strip lie inside a smaller exact generator target chart. Smooth core convergence and the actual image collapse in [F1] put the WHOLE physical annulus $\epsilon_n/r<|z|<r$ in that same chart for large $n$. The entire strip exterior to $|z|<r$ also lies there: on any fixed translated exterior core this follows from convergence to the constant map, and on its remaining infinite ends it follows from the actual outer-tail collapse. This uses both parts of [F1], not compact convergence alone. On all those regions the actual linearization in the target chart is exactly $D_0$, regardless of the time dependence of $J_{n,t}$. The source coordinates $w=-1/\zeta$, $zw=-\epsilon_n$ therefore give the exact [F5] fixed-cap plumbing, with a real-boundary cylinder of length $T_n=\pi^{-1}\log(r^2/\epsilon_n)\to\infty$. [F1, F5, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 Construct an actual disk-cap extension operator on a fixed full punctured disk. On $|\zeta|\le1/r$ use the rescaled actual map and actual linearization, with its holomorphic symbol transformation. On a slightly larger fixed region use [F2]'s local addition to interpolate that map to $v$, equal to $v$ outside it. The interpolation shell lies entirely in the exact generator chart; the addition there is ordinary real vector addition, so it preserves the physical real boundary. On that shell and the entire end define the operator to be exactly $D_0$. The two definitions agree on an open collar since the original actual operator is already $D_0$ there by step1.1. Thus this is a genuine smooth operator $P_{d,n}$ on the interpolated pullback bundle, agreeing EXACTLY with the actual disk-cap operator and exactly with $D_0$ beyond a fixed core. Its coefficients and bundle/boundary data converge smoothly on that fixed core to those of $P_v$, and on the end they are identical. No global holomorphic extension of the rescaled map is asserted or needed for this constructed linear operator. [F1, F2, step 1.1, construct, algebra]

3.1 Identify its actual complex bundle and boundary domain with those of $P_v$, by a near-identity isomorphism equal to the identity in the end chart. Use the connection constructed in [F2] and solve its finite-dimensional parallel-transport ODE along the local-addition paths. Its iterated-integral series and its differentiated series converge uniformly on these fixed compact cores; since the paths shrink to zero length, the transports converge smoothly to the identity. That connection preserves the tangent subbundles along boundary paths. This gives a smooth real identification $I_n$ preserving the real tangent boundary planes and converging to the identity in every fixed coefficient norm. Pull its complex structure back to $J'_n$ on the reference bundle. Then $C_n=(I-J'_nJ_v)/2$ is a complex intertwiner, invertible and close to the identity. On the physical boundary there is a unique complex intertwiner $H_n$ defined on the totally real splitting by $H_n(a+J_vb)=a+J'_nb$ for $a,b$ in its real boundary plane. It is close to the identity and fixes that plane. The complex automorphism $C_n^{-1}H_n$ is close to the identity there. Extend its difference from the identity across a thin fixed boundary collar using finitely many complex bundle charts, normal cutoffs and a finite partition; smallness keeps the extended matrix invertible. It is the identity on the exact end. Composing this extension with $I_nC_n$ gives the required complex identification, exactly preserving the real boundary condition. After this identification the source and target weighted domains are fixed, and $$\|\widetilde P_{d,n}-P_v\|_{H^1_\delta\to L^2_\delta}\longrightarrow0.$$ Indeed the difference consists of first-order and zeroth-order coefficients tending uniformly to zero on one fixed compact core, and vanishes on the end. This proves an actual operator-norm comparison with no neck factor. [F2, step 2.1, construct, algebra]

4.1 Fix the two disk-core phases and the weight in [F3]. The reference augmented operator $(P_v,\ell)$ on its node-fixed weighted domain is an isomorphism. On that SAME domain step3.1 makes $(\widetilde P_{d,n},\ell)$ a small bounded perturbation, so its inverse is its explicit Neumann series and has a bound independent of $n$. Write $Q_{d,n}^0$ for its zero-phase inverse on forcing. To free the node constant choose fixed cutoff real constant lifts $k_c$, equal to $c$ on the end, and set $$L_{d,n}c=k_c-Q_{d,n}^0\widetilde P_{d,n}k_c,$$ first taking the lifts to have zero phases by compact-core corrections. These are actual homogeneous zero-phase sections with constant $c$, and their norms are uniformly bounded because the forcings have fixed compact support and uniformly bounded coefficients. The strip cap is exactly the constant one from [F4], with its fixed inverse $Q_c$. Thus the disk cap has been inverted BEFORE matching the long neck. We do not perturb a full inverse whose norm already grows like $T_n$. [F3, F4, step 3.1, construct, algebra]

5.1 Repeat the explicit cap construction of [F4] with these actual inverses. For an incoming negative Fourier trace $g$ at a cut beyond all variable coefficients and phase supports, use its inward-decaying extension $k_g$ and fixed-band forcing $F_g$. The homogeneous disk response is $k_g-Q_{d,n}^0F_g+L_{d,n}c$, with trace $g+A_{d,n}g+B_{d,n}c+c$. These response maps and cap solution norms are uniformly bounded by step4.1 and [F4]'s fixed trace estimates. Its converse is exact: a cap solution with zero incoming trace and constant zero extends by its positive modes to the full node-fixed weighted domain, where the zero-phase inverse makes it zero. The strip responses $A_c,E_c$ remain fixed. This verifies the precise hypotheses of the LINEAR matching calculation locally; no claim that the artificial full disk-cap extension is a holomorphic disk has been used. [F4, step 2.1, step 4.1, construct, algebra]

6.1 For an arbitrary actual $L^2$ forcing, split its two cap restrictions and neck restriction as in [F5]. The mixed Fourier cylinder inverse supplies traces $l,r_N$ and real drift $d=\int_0^{T_n}(f_N)_0$, with $|d|\le\sqrt{T_n}\|f_N\|_2$. The particular cap responses are $p_c,e_c,p_{d,n}$. Let $U_{T_n}$ be positive-to-negative reflected propagation, with norm at most $e^{-\pi T_n}$. Equality of ALL actual seam traces is the system $$g_c=l+U_{T_n}(p_{d,n}+A_{d,n}g_d+B_{d,n}c_d),\qquad g_d=Jr_N+U_{T_n}(p_c+A_cg_c),\qquad c_d=e_c+E_cg_c+d,$$ with $J$ the fixed transverse reflection. After substitution its matrix on $(g_c,g_d)$ is $$N_n=\begin{pmatrix}U_{T_n}B_{d,n}E_c&U_{T_n}A_{d,n}\\U_{T_n}A_c&0\end{pmatrix}.$$ Step5.1 bounds its norm by $C e^{-\pi T_n}$ with ONE constant, so the Neumann series solves this system for all large $n$. The cap sections and actual cylinder solution then have matched weak traces; integration by parts cancels seam terms and gives a genuine global $H^1$ solution of the actual strip linearization with that forcing. The mean primitive and cap estimates give a right-inverse bound $C(1+T_n)$ in the fixed-cap/cylinder norms of [F5]. Their equivalence to actual strip Sobolev norms for each individual $n$ suffices for a bounded actual right inverse; no uniform equivalence under source rescaling is claimed. The neck is exactly linear, so there is no omitted coefficient error times $T_n$ and no convergence-rate hypothesis. This zero-phase solution is UNIQUE: any homogeneous zero-phase section has the cap responses in step5.1 and the unique scalar cylinder propagation, hence solves the homogeneous system $(I-N_n)g=0$; its incoming data and real constants are zero, and then both cap sections and cylinder are zero. The two compact-core phase functionals on the full strip domain are onto $\mathbb R^2$, using two supported smooth disk-core sections with invertible phase Gram matrix. For any requested phase pair, subtract the zero-phase inverse applied to the operator of such a lift. The result is a kernel section with exactly that phase pair. The zero-phase kernel is zero, so the full kernel is isomorphic to $\mathbb R^2$. [F4, F5, step 1.1, step 4.1, step 5.1, construct, algebra]

7.1 Thus the actual $H^1\to L^2$ operator is onto for every sufficiently large $n$. The preceding phase calculation gives a two-dimensional kernel and an onto bounded operator, hence an actual Fredholm operator of index two. This argument has used no orientation of either global boundary manifold. Its higher realizations have the same range quotient, so they too are onto, with only their original zeroth physical trace condition. If the bubble is on the upper edge, use the holomorphic source change $z\mapsto-z+i$ before the construction; it exchanges the two physical edges and reverses the longitudinal coordinate while preserving source orientation and the Cauchy--Riemann symbol. Multiplying the exact target coordinate by $-i$ then makes the new lower plane real and the upper plane imaginary again. The proof concerns actual linearizations and actual nodal convergence and does not require an autonomous time interval of uniform width. Global genericity, count values, nonlinear gluing and cancellation are not inferred. [F6, step 6.1, algebra] ∎
