## Definition

For $z=x+iy$, set $\operatorname{Re}z=x$, $\operatorname{Im}z=y$, $\overline z=x-iy$, and $|z|=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\ge0$. The conventions and prerequisite facts used below are recorded in [[def-complex-numbers-and-arithmetic]], [[thm-of-square-roots]].
