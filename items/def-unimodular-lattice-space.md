## Definition

For an integer $d\ge1$, let $\mathrm{SL}_d(\mathbb R)$ be the real determinant-one matrices and $\mathrm{SL}_d(\mathbb Z)$ its integer subgroup. The space of unimodular lattices in $\mathbb R^d$ is
$$X_d:=\mathrm{SL}_d(\mathbb R)/\mathrm{SL}_d(\mathbb Z),$$
where $g\mathrm{SL}_d(\mathbb Z)$ represents the full lattice $g\mathbb Z^d$ of covolume one. Two matrices represent the same point exactly when their lattices agree.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A full Euclidean lattice and the homogeneous quotient convention.

[F1] A full lattice is a discrete subgroup generated by a basis, and its covolume is the absolute determinant of a basis matrix. [[def-full-euclidean-lattice-and-covolume]]

[F2] The quotient $G/\Gamma$ is the space of left cosets with the right action of $\Gamma$ identified. [[def-homogeneous-space-of-a-lie-group]]
