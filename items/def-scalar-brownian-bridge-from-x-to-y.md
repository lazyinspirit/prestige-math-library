## Definition

Assume AC. Let $h>0$, $x,y\in\mathbb R$, and let $W$ be a standard scalar
Brownian motion on $[0,h]$. The **scalar Brownian bridge from $x$ to $y$ over
duration $h$** is the continuous Gaussian process
$$B^{x,y,h}_t=x+\frac{t}{h}(y-x)+W_t-\frac{t}{h}W_h, \qquad 0\le t\le h.$$
It starts at $x$ and ends at $y$. Its law does not depend on which Brownian
motion is used: Brownian increment laws determine its finite-dimensional laws.
For the path-law assertion, give $C([0,h])$ the uniform topology and extend
each path $f$ by $E_hf(s)=f(s\wedge h)$ for $s\ge0$. This is a homeomorphism
onto the closed subspace of $C([0,\infty))$ consisting of paths constant
after $h$, with inverse restriction to $[0,h]$. Thus
[[lem-borel-sigma-algebra-of-continuous-path-space-is-generated-by-coordinates]]
makes the Borel sigma-algebra on $C([0,h])$ generated by its coordinates:
coordinates of the extension after $h$ equal $f(h)$, and continuity recovers
every coordinate, including $h$, from rational times in $[0,h]$.
Equality on finite coordinate cylinders now gives equality of path laws by
[[thm-dynkin-pi-lambda]]. The endpoint-conditional and restriction-density
properties are established separately on the A page.
