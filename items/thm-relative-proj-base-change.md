## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice as inherited from the Proj, sheaf and
fibre-product suppliers ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]). Let $g:S'\to S$ be a
morphism of schemes, let $\mathcal A=\bigoplus_{d\ge0}\mathcal A_d$ be a
quasi-coherent graded $\mathcal O_S$-algebra, and put
$$\mathcal A'=\mathcal A\otimes_{\mathcal O_S}\mathcal O_{S'}=g^*\mathcal A,$$
graded with $(\mathcal A')_d=g^*\mathcal A_d$, a quasi-coherent graded
$\mathcal O_{S'}$-algebra. Then there is a canonical isomorphism of
$S'$-schemes
$$\operatorname{Proj}_S\mathcal A\times_SS'\;\cong\;\operatorname{Proj}_{S'}\mathcal A'$$
([[def-relative-proj-quasi-coherent-graded-algebra]],
[[def-base-change-morphism-schemes]]), natural in $S'\to S$, compatible with
the relative twists: writing $p:\operatorname{Proj}_S\mathcal A\times_SS'\to\operatorname{Proj}_S\mathcal A$ for the projection, on the charts it identifies
$p^*\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Proj}_S\mathcal A}(n)$ with
$\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Proj}_{S'}\mathcal A'}(n)$; and it is compatible
with graded algebra quotients: for a quasi-coherent homogeneous ideal
$\mathcal J\subseteq\mathcal A$ with quotient $\mathcal A/\mathcal J$ the
isomorphism restricts to
$\operatorname{Proj}_S(\mathcal A/\mathcal J)\times_SS'\cong
\operatorname{Proj}_{S'}(\mathcal A'\!/\mathcal J')$ with
$\mathcal J'=\operatorname{Im}(g^*\mathcal J\to g^*\mathcal A)=\mathcal J\mathcal A'$. No flatness and no finite-generation hypothesis is
required.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A morphism $g:S'\to S$, a quasi-coherent graded $\mathcal O_S$-algebra $\mathcal A$, a quasi-coherent homogeneous ideal $\mathcal J\subseteq\mathcal A$, affine opens $\operatorname{Spec}R=U\subseteq S$ and $\operatorname{Spec}R'=V\subseteq S'$ with $g(V)\subseteq U$, and the Axiom of Choice as inherited.

[A1] The Axiom of Choice states that every family of nonempty sets has a choice function. ([[def-axiom-of-choice]])

[F1] $\operatorname{Proj}_S\mathcal A$ is obtained by gluing the absolute Proj schemes $\operatorname{Proj}\Gamma(U,\mathcal A)$ over the affine opens $U\subseteq S$, and $\mathcal O(n)$ by gluing the sheaves $\widetilde{\Gamma(U,\mathcal A)(n)}$; on $U=\operatorname{Spec}R$ the structural morphism is $\operatorname{Proj}\Gamma(U,\mathcal A)\to\operatorname{Spec}R$. ([[def-relative-proj-quasi-coherent-graded-algebra]])

[F2] For affine base change $\operatorname{Spec}R\leftarrow\operatorname{Spec}R'$ one has $\operatorname{Spec}B\times_{\operatorname{Spec}R}\operatorname{Spec}R'\cong\operatorname{Spec}(B\otimes_RR')$ for every $R$-algebra $B$, allowing the zero ring. ([[thm-affine-fibre-product-tensor-ring]])

[F3] For affine $V\subseteq S'$ mapping into affine $U=\operatorname{Spec}R\subseteq S$ the sections of the pulled-back quasi-coherent sheaf are given by base change: $\Gamma(V,g^*\mathcal M)=\Gamma(U,\mathcal M)\otimes_RR'$ for every quasi-coherent $\mathcal O_U$-module $\mathcal M$, naturally in $\mathcal M$. ([[lem-pullback-qc-module-quasi-coherent]])

[F4] Localisation commutes with base change: for an $R$-algebra $B$, a multiplicative set generated by a homogeneous $f$ and the image $f'$ of $f$ in $B\otimes_RR'$, one has $(B_{(f)})\otimes_RR'\cong(B\otimes_RR')_{(f')}$, and for a homogeneous ideal $I\subseteq B$ one has $(B/I)\otimes_RR'\cong(B\otimes_RR')/(I(B\otimes_RR'))$, tensor product being right exact. [algebra]

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct: prove the affine case by comparing degree-zero localisations of $B$ and $B\otimes_RR'$, then glue the affine comparisons over an affine cover of $S'$.

1.1 The affine chart comparison. Let $B$ be a graded $R$-algebra, $R'$ an $R$-algebra and $B'=B\otimes_RR'$ with its induced grading. For homogeneous $f\in B_+$ of positive degree and its image $f'\in B'$, [F4] gives $(B_{(f)})\otimes_RR'\cong(B')_{(f')}$ naturally; the transition identifications on $D_+(fg)$ are the localisations $B_{(f)}\to B_{(fg)}$, which base change to the corresponding localisations of $B'$ by [F4], so the chart data of $\operatorname{Proj}B$ base changed along $R\to R'$ agree with the chart data of $\operatorname{Proj}B'$, and the shifted modules base change by the same computation: $(B(n)_{(f)})\otimes_RR'\cong(B'(n))_{(f')}$ for every $n\in\mathbb Z$. [F4, algebra]

1.2 The affine quotient comparison. If $I\subseteq B$ is a homogeneous ideal and $I'=IB'$, then for homogeneous $f$ one has $(B_{(f)}/I_{(f)})\otimes_RR'\cong B'_{(f')}/I'_{(f')}$, because localisation at $f$ commutes with base change and tensor product is right exact by [F4]; hence the chart ideals of $\operatorname{Proj}(B/I)$ base change to those of $\operatorname{Proj}(B'/I')$. [F4, algebra]

2.1 The affine case of the isomorphism. For every ring map $R\to R'$ the chart comparison of step 1.1 produces a canonical isomorphism $$\operatorname{Proj}B\times_{\operatorname{Spec}R}\operatorname{Spec}R'\;\cong\;\operatorname{Proj}(B\otimes_RR'),$$ since on the $f$-chart the left side is $\operatorname{Spec}(B_{(f)})\times_{\operatorname{Spec}R}\operatorname{Spec}R'\cong\operatorname{Spec}\bigl(B_{(f)}\otimes_RR'\bigr)$ by [F2], the right side is $\operatorname{Spec}(B')_{(f')}$, and step 1.1 identifies these rings and their transition maps. The identification carries $\widetilde{B(n)}$ base changed to $\widetilde{B'(n)}$ on each chart. [F2, step 1.1]

3.1 The affine quotient case. By step 1.2 the isomorphism of step 2.1 restricts on charts to the closed subschemes cut out by $I_{(f)}$ and $I'_{(f')}$; passing to the definitions of the chart ideals, this gives a canonical isomorphism $$\operatorname{Proj}(B/I)\times_{\operatorname{Spec}R}\operatorname{Spec}R'\cong\operatorname{Proj}(B'/I')$$ compatible with the closed immersions into $\operatorname{Proj}B\times_{\operatorname{Spec}R}\operatorname{Spec}R'$ and $\operatorname{Proj}B'$. [step 1.2, step 2.1]

3.2 Reduction to affine charts. Let $U=\operatorname{Spec}R\subseteq S$ be affine and let $V=\operatorname{Spec}R'\subseteq S'$ be affine with $g(V)\subseteq U$. By [F3] the graded pieces of the pulled-back algebra satisfy $\Gamma(V,\mathcal A')_d=\Gamma(V,g^*\mathcal A_d)=\Gamma(U,\mathcal A_d)\otimes_RR'$, so the graded $R'$-algebra $\Gamma(V,\mathcal A')$ is $\Gamma(U,\mathcal A)\otimes_RR'$; hence step 2.1 applies with $B=\Gamma(U,\mathcal A)$ and gives a canonical isomorphism $$\operatorname{Proj}\Gamma(U,\mathcal A)\times_U V\;\cong\;\operatorname{Proj}\Gamma(V,\mathcal A').$$ By [F1] the left side is $(\operatorname{Proj}_S\mathcal A\times_SS')|_V$ and the right side is $(\operatorname{Proj}_{S'}\mathcal A')|_V$, so the theorem holds over every such pair $U,V$. [F1, F3, step 2.1]

4.1 Gluing the local isomorphisms. The affine opens $V$ of $S'$ mapping into some affine open $U$ of $S$ cover $S'$: for $x\in S'$ take an affine neighbourhood $U$ of $g(x)$ and then an affine neighbourhood $V$ of $x$ inside $g^{-1}(U)$. For two such pairs the local isomorphisms of step 3.2 agree on the intersection, because restricted to an affine open of $V\cap V'$ both are given by the same affine computation of step 2.1; hence they glue to a canonical isomorphism $\operatorname{Proj}_S\mathcal A\times_SS'\to\operatorname{Proj}_{S'}\mathcal A'$ over $S'$, natural in the map $S'\to S$. [F1, step 3.2, cases: overlap compatibility]

5.1 Twists and quotients. On each pair $U,V$ as in step 3.2 the isomorphism of step 2.1 identifies the base change of $\widetilde{B(n)}$ with $\widetilde{B'(n)}$ on charts by step 1.1, so the glued twists correspond: $p^*\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Proj}_S\mathcal A}(n)\cong\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Proj}_{S'}\mathcal A'}(n)$. Similarly, for a quasi-coherent homogeneous ideal $\mathcal J\subseteq\mathcal A$ with $\mathcal J'=\operatorname{Im}(g^*\mathcal J\to\mathcal A')$, the affine comparison of step 3.1 applies on each chart with $I=\Gamma(U,\mathcal J)\subseteq\Gamma(U,\mathcal A)$, and the local closed subschemes glue to the restriction isomorphism $\operatorname{Proj}_S(\mathcal A/\mathcal J)\times_SS'\cong\operatorname{Proj}_{S'}(\mathcal A'/\mathcal J')$. [step 2.1, step 3.1, step 4.1]

6.1 Conclusion. Step 4.1 gives the canonical base-change isomorphism over $S'$, step 5.1 its compatibility with relative twists and with graded algebra quotients. No flatness or finite generation of $\mathcal A$ was used: only the affine case of tensor products and localisations, as displayed in steps 1.1, 1.2 and 2.1, and the affine-local description of relative Proj. the Axiom of Choice [A1] is inherited through the Proj and pullback constructions. If $S'=\varnothing$ or the affine pieces are empty, the identifications are between empty schemes and the construction is vacuous. [A1, step 4.1, step 5.1, cases: empty base change]
\qed
