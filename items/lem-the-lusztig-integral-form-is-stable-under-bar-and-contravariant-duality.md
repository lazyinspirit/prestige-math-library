## Statement

Let $\psi$ (the bar involution) and $\tau$ (the contravariant anti-involution) be
the semilinear maps of
[[lem-quantum-serre-relations-are-stable-under-the-chevalley-involutions]], so
that $\psi(q)=q^{-1}$, $\psi(E_i)=E_i$, $\psi(F_i)=F_i$, $\psi(K_h)=K_h^{-1}$
and $\tau(q)=q^{-1}$, $\tau(E_i)=F_i$, $\tau(F_i)=E_i$, $\tau(K_h)=K_h^{-1}$,
with $\tau$ anti-multiplicative. Let $A=\mathbb Z[q,q^{-1}]$ and let $U_A$ be
the Lusztig divided-power integral form with its generators $E_i^{(r)}$,
$F_i^{(r)}$, $K_h^{\pm1}$ and toral binomials $T_{i,c,t}$
([[def-lusztig-divided-power-integral-form]]). Then $\psi(U_A)=U_A$ and
$\tau(U_A)=U_A$. Explicitly, on the generators,

$$\psi(E_i^{(r)})=E_i^{(r)},\quad \psi(F_i^{(r)})=F_i^{(r)},\quad \psi(K_h)=K_h^{-1},\quad \psi(T_{i,c,t})=T_{i,c,t},$$

$$\tau(E_i^{(r)})=F_i^{(r)},\quad \tau(F_i^{(r)})=E_i^{(r)},\quad \tau(K_h)=K_h^{-1},\quad \tau(T_{i,c,t})=T_{i,c,t}.$$

In particular both maps restrict to bijections of $U_A$: $\psi$ to a semilinear
algebra involution of $U_A$ and $\tau$ to a semilinear anti-involution of
$U_A$, while $q^{\pm1}\in A$ keeps all coefficients in $A$. These are the two
involutions under which the bar lattice and the contravariant duality of the
later constructions are taken; neither formula is the Chevalley involution
$\omega$, which changes signs, exchanges the two halves and is treated in
[[lem-quantum-serre-relations-are-stable-under-the-chevalley-involutions]].
No choice is used.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The Drinfeld–Jimbo algebra $U_q(\mathfrak g)$
([[def-drinfeld-jimbo-quantized-enveloping-algebra]]), the involution lemma
defining $\psi,\tau$ and their semilinearity
([[lem-quantum-serre-relations-are-stable-under-the-chevalley-involutions]]),
and the $A$-algebra $U_A$ with its listed generators
([[def-lusztig-divided-power-integral-form]]).

[F1] $\psi$ is an involutive semilinear algebra automorphism and $\tau$ an
involutive semilinear anti-automorphism of $U_q(\mathfrak g)$ with the stated
values on generators; both apply the field involution $\sigma(q)=q^{-1}$
([[lem-quantum-serre-relations-are-stable-under-the-chevalley-involutions]]).

[F2] The field involution $\sigma$ preserves $A=\mathbb Z[q,q^{-1}]$, since
$\sigma(\sum_ka_kq^k)=\sum_ka_kq^{-k}$; and $\sigma$ fixes $[r]_i$ and $[r]_i!$
for every $r\ge0$, because $[r]_i$ is invariant under $q_i\mapsto q_i^{-1}$
([[def-quantum-integers-factorials-and-divided-powers-at-q-i]]).

[F3] $U_A$ is the $A$-subalgebra generated by the $E_i^{(r)}$, $F_i^{(r)}$,
$K_h^{\pm1}$ and $T_{i,c,t}$; its elements are finite $A$-linear combinations
of finite products of these generators
([[def-lusztig-divided-power-integral-form]]).

[F4] $T_{i,c,t}=\prod_{s=1}^{t}\frac{K_iq_i^{c-s+1}-K_i^{-1}q_i^{-c+s-1}}{q_i^{s}-q_i^{-s}}$
with the empty product $T_{i,c,0}=1$
([[def-lusztig-divided-power-integral-form]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** compute both (anti-)maps on each family of generators of
$U_A$ and conclude by semilinearity and [F3].

1.1 For every $r\ge0$ one has $\psi(E_i^{(r)})=\psi(E_i)^r/\psi([r]_i!)=E_i^r/[r]_i!=E_i^{(r)}$ and $\psi(F_i^{(r)})=F_i^{(r)}$, since $\psi$ fixes $E_i,F_i$ and fixes the scalar $[r]_i!$ by [F2]; on the torus $\psi(K_h)=K_h^{-1}$. For the toral binomial, applying $\psi$ factor by factor and using $\psi(K_i^{\pm1})=K_i^{\mp1}$, $\psi(q_i^{\pm1})=q_i^{\mp1}$ gives $\psi\bigl(K_iq_i^{c-s+1}-K_i^{-1}q_i^{-c+s-1}\bigr)=-(K_iq_i^{c-s+1}-K_i^{-1}q_i^{-c+s-1})$ and $\psi(q_i^{s}-q_i^{-s})=-(q_i^{s}-q_i^{-s})$; the two signs cancel, so each factor and hence the product $T_{i,c,t}$ of [F4] is fixed: $\psi(T_{i,c,t})=T_{i,c,t}$. [given, F1, F2, F4, algebra]

2.1 For $\tau$, which is anti-multiplicative, $\tau(E_i^{(r)})=\tau(E_i^r)/\tau([r]_i!)=\tau(E_i)^r/[r]_i!=F_i^r/[r]_i!=F_i^{(r)}$ by [F2], and symmetrically $\tau(F_i^{(r)})=E_i^{(r)}$; $\tau(K_h)=K_h^{-1}$; and on each factor of [F4], $\tau(K_iq_i^{c-s+1}-K_i^{-1}q_i^{-c+s-1})=K_i^{-1}q_i^{-(c-s+1)}-K_iq_i^{c-s+1}=-(K_iq_i^{c-s+1}-K_i^{-1}q_i^{-c+s-1})$ while $\tau(q_i^{s}-q_i^{-s})=-(q_i^{s}-q_i^{-s})$, so $\tau$ fixes every factor; since the factors commute, $\tau(T_{i,c,t})=T_{i,c,t}$ as well. [step 1.1, F1, F2, F4, algebra]

3.1 Consequently each of $\psi,\tau$ sends every generator of $U_A$ into $U_A$ by steps 1.1 and 2.1, and sends scalars $a\in A$ into $A$ by [F2]. From [F3], an element of $U_A$ is a finite sum $\sum_ja_jg_{j,1}\cdots g_{j,m_j}$ with $a_j\in A$ and generators $g_{j,l}$; applying the semilinear algebra map $\psi$ gives $\sum_j\sigma(a_j)\psi(g_{j,1})\cdots\psi(g_{j,m_j})\in U_A$, and applying the semilinear anti-algebra map $\tau$ gives $\sum_j\sigma(a_j)\tau(g_{j,m_j})\cdots\tau(g_{j,1})\in U_A$. Hence $\psi(U_A)\subseteq U_A$ and $\tau(U_A)\subseteq U_A$; since $\psi,\tau$ are involutions on $U_q(\mathfrak g)$ by [F1], the reverse inclusions hold as well, so $\psi(U_A)=U_A$ and $\tau(U_A)=U_A$ and the restrictions are bijections of the stated (anti-)multiplicative types. No choice is used. [step 1.1, step 2.1, F1, F2, F3, algebra] ∎
