## Statement

Let $F$ be a totally real number field and let $p$ be an odd prime. Fix an algebraic closure containing all $p$-power roots of unity, put $\mathcal F_\infty=F(\mu_{p^\infty})$, and let $c$ be complex conjugation. Put $F_\infty=\mathcal F_\infty^{\langle c\rangle}$, $\mathcal G=\operatorname{Gal}(\mathcal F_\infty/F)$, and $G=\operatorname{Gal}(F_\infty/F)$. Let $\chi_F:\mathcal G\to\mathbb Z_p^\times$ be the cyclotomic character; it identifies $\mathcal G$ with an open subgroup of $\mathbb Z_p^\times$. Let $\Delta_F$ be the finite torsion subgroup of $\mathcal G$; restriction identifies it with $\operatorname{Gal}(F(\mu_p)/F)$, and $\chi_F$ identifies $\Delta_F$ with a cyclic subgroup of $\mu_{p-1}(\mathbb Z_p)$ while sending $c$ to $-1$. Then there are a finite group $\Delta_F^+$ of order prime to $p$ and a closed subgroup $\Gamma\cong\mathbb Z_p$ such that $\mathcal G\cong\Delta_F\times\Gamma$, $G\cong\Delta_F^+\times\Gamma$, and $\Delta_F^+=\Delta_F/\langle c\rangle$; under $\chi_F$, the subgroup $\Gamma$ is identified with an open subgroup of $1+p\mathbb Z_p$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A totally real number field $F$, an odd prime $p$, and the cyclotomic fields in the Statement.

[F1] A number field is finite over $\mathbb Q$. [[def-number-field]]

[F2] The cyclotomic character identifies $\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb Q(\mu_{p^\infty})/\mathbb Q)$ with $\mathbb Z_p^\times$. [[cor-the-galois-group-of-a-rational-cyclotomic-field]]

[F3] For a finite Galois extension, the degree and restriction map for a compositum are determined by the intersection. [[lem-degree-of-a-compositum-with-a-galois-extension]]

[F4] The Teichmüller character splits $\mathbb Z_p^\times$ as its finite roots-of-unity subgroup times $1+p\mathbb Z_p$. [[def-teichmuller-character-on-zp-units]]

[F5] The group $1+p\mathbb Z_p$ is topologically generated by $1+p$ for odd $p$. [[lem-principal-unit-generators-of-p-adic-units]]

[F6] The real cyclotomic field is the fixed field of complex conjugation, so its Galois group is the quotient by $\langle c\rangle$. [[cor-the-galois-group-of-a-rational-cyclotomic-field]]

## Proof

1.1 Set $K=\mathbb Q(\mu_{p^\infty})$ and $L=F\cap K$. Since $L\subseteq F$ and $F/\mathbb Q$ is finite by [F1], $L/\mathbb Q$ is finite. Each finite set of generators of $L$ lies in one finite cyclotomic layer, so for all sufficiently large $n$, $F\cap\mathbb Q(\mu_{p^n})=L$. The finite-level character isomorphisms of [F2] pass to the full union without a choice of lifts: a compatible family of exponents modulo $p^n$ defines compatible automorphisms on each finite cyclotomic field, hence a unique automorphism of their union. By [F2] and [F3], restriction therefore identifies $\mathcal G$ with the open subgroup $H=\operatorname{Gal}(K/L)$ of $\mathbb Z_p^\times$. [F1, F2, F3, given, algebra]

2.1 By [F4], every unit is uniquely $\omega(x)\langle x\rangle$ with $\omega(x)\in D=\mu_{p-1}$ and $\langle x\rangle\in U=1+p\mathbb Z_p$: a root of unity in the intersection reduces to $1$ and is $1$ by unique Teichmüller lifting. Thus $\mathbb Z_p^\times=D\times U$; [F5] identifies $U$ topologically with the additive group $\mathbb Z_p$. Put $U_0=H\cap U$ and let $D_0$ be the projection of $H$ to $D$. The subgroup $U_0$ is open, so $U/U_0$ is a finite $p$-group. For each $d\in D_0$, choose $u\in U$ with $(d,u)\in H$ and send $d$ to $uU_0$. This is well-defined because two such choices differ by an element of $U_0$, and it is a homomorphism because $H$ is a subgroup of the abelian group $D\times U$. Its image has order dividing $|D_0|$, which is prime to $p$, and is also a subgroup of the finite $p$-group $U/U_0$; hence the image is trivial. Thus every $(d,u)\in H$ has $u\in U_0$, and $(d,1)=(d,u)(1,u^{-1})\in H$. Therefore $H=D_0\times U_0$. To see $U_0\cong\mathbb Z_p$, identify $U$ with additive $\mathbb Z_p$: an open subgroup $V$ contains $p^N\mathbb Z_p$ for some $N$; if $r$ is the least valuation of a nonzero element of $V$, then an element $p^r v$ with $v$ a unit has integer multiples dense in $p^r\mathbb Z_p$, so closedness gives $p^r\mathbb Z_p\subseteq V$, while minimality gives $V\subseteq p^r\mathbb Z_p$. Hence $V=p^r\mathbb Z_p\cong\mathbb Z_p$. The reduction map modulo $p$ kills $U_0$ and identifies $D_0$ with the restriction image on $\mathbb Q(\mu_p)$; by [F3], that image is $\operatorname{Gal}(F(\mu_p)/F)$. Consequently $\Delta_F=D_0$ and $\Gamma=U_0$. [F3, F4, F5, step 1.1, algebra, choose]

3.1 Since $F$ is totally real, complex conjugation fixes $F$ and corresponds to $-1$ under the cyclotomic character, so it is the order-two subgroup $\langle c\rangle$ of $\Delta_F$. The fixed-field Galois correspondence and [F6] give $G=\mathcal G/\langle c\rangle\cong(\Delta_F/\langle c\rangle)\times\Gamma$. This is the required decomposition with $\Delta_F^+=\Delta_F/\langle c\rangle$. [F2, F6, step 2.1, given, algebra] ∎
