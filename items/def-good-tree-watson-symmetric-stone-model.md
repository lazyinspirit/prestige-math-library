## Definition

Work in a transitive ZFC ground model $M$ with GCH, and fix a regular uncountable
cardinal $\lambda$. Put
$$P := \operatorname{Fn}(\lambda \times \mathbb{R} \times \lambda \times \lambda, 2, \lambda),$$
the set of partial functions with domain a subset of
$\lambda \times \mathbb{R} \times \lambda \times \lambda$ of size below
$\lambda$ and values in $2$, ordered by reverse inclusion: $q \le p$ means
$p \subseteq q$ ([[def-forcing-preorder-compatibility-and-filter]]).

Let $B := \operatorname{RO}(P)$ be the regular-open completion
([[def-complete-boolean-algebra-and-regular-open-sets]],
[[thm-forcing-preorders-have-regular-open-completions]]). The **automorphism
group** $G$ consists of the automorphisms of $B$ induced by permutations of
$\lambda \times \mathbb{R} \times \lambda$ of the form
$(\xi, r, \alpha) \mapsto (\xi, \rho(r), \alpha')$ where for each fixed $\xi$ the
map $\alpha \mapsto \alpha'$ is a permutation of the third coordinate and
$\rho : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ is either the identity or a reflection about a
real point; the action on names is the recursion of
[[def-forcing-name-automorphism-action]].

The **normal filter** $\mathcal{F}$ is generated by the subgroups
$\operatorname{fix}(e)$ for subsets $e \subseteq \lambda \times \mathbb{R} \times
\lambda$ of size below $\lambda$, where $\operatorname{fix}(e)$ is the pointwise
stabiliser of $e$; regularity of $\lambda$ keeps the generated filter normal and
closed under intersections of fewer than $\lambda$ of its members. The
**symmetric model** $N$ is the hereditarily symmetric interpretation of
[[def-symmetric-forcing-system-and-hereditarily-symmetric-names]], which is a
transitive model of ZF between $M$ and the full extension $M[H]$
([[thm-hereditarily-symmetric-interpretations-form-a-zf-model]]).

**The canonical families.** For $\xi < \lambda$, $r \in \mathbb{R}$ and
$\alpha < \lambda$ let $x_{\xi r \alpha}$ be the real coded by the $\xi$-th
component of the generic sequence at parameters $(r,\alpha)$, let
$X_{\xi r} := \{x_{\xi r \alpha} : \alpha < \lambda\}$, let
$R_\xi := \{X_{\xi r} : r \in \mathbb{R}\}$ be the $\xi$-th metric component
with its induced metric, and let $M := \{R_\xi : \xi < \lambda\}$. Each of these
is fixed by every automorphism of the group, hence hereditarily symmetric, so all
five families lie in $N$ and are indexed by the full $\lambda$.

The case $\lambda = \omega_1$ is the case used for the dependent-choice model
below; the same definition with larger $\lambda$ is the one whose
$<\lambda$-sequence closure is proved in the next item.

## Remarks

- **Why the presentation is indexed by $\lambda$ and not by $\omega$.** The
  paper's Theorems 1–3 use the countable index set and finite supports, and the
  paragraph after Theorem 3 states the regular-$\lambda$ replacement with
  supports of size below $\lambda$. The dependent-choice model needs the
  replacement: the countable-support presentation is not closed under
  $\omega$-sequences, while the $<\lambda$-support presentation is.

- **What is not claimed here.** The definition does not assert dependent choice,
  the failure of Stone's theorem, or the existence of the metric sum of the
  components; those are separate items of this page.
