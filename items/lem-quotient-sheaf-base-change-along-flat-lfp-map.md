## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice inherited from the quotient-sheaf suppliers
([[def-axiom-of-choice]]). Let $s,t\colon R\to U$ be an equivalence relation
over $S$ ([[def-groupoid-in-schemes-and-etale-equivalence-relation]]) with
$s,t$ surjective, flat and locally of finite presentation
([[def-flat-morphism-schemes]],
[[def-locally-finite-presentation-morphism]]), and let $g\colon U'\to U$ be
flat and locally of finite presentation. Form
$R'=R\times_{U\times_SU}(U'\times_SU')$
([[lem-etale-equivalence-relation-restriction]]). Then
$U'/R'\to U/R$ is representable by schemes and an open immersion. Its image is
the open subquotient corresponding to the saturated open
$W=t(s^{-1}(g(U')))\subseteq U$. It is an isomorphism when $W=U$, in
particular when $g$ is surjective. No isomorphism or surjectivity is asserted
for arbitrary $g$. Here quotient sheaves are those of
[[def-quotient-fppf-sheaf-of-a-pre-relation]].

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** An equivalence relation $(U,R,s,t)$ over $S$ with $s,t$ surjective flat and locally of finite presentation; a flat locally finitely presented $g\colon U'\to U$; the restriction $R'$ and the quotient sheaves $F'=U'/R'$, $F=U/R$.

[F1] The restriction $R'$ with $j'=(t',s')$ is again an equivalence relation, and each structure map is a composition of a base change of $g$ and a base change of $s$ or $t$ ([[def-groupoid-in-schemes-and-etale-equivalence-relation]], [[lem-etale-equivalence-relation-restriction]]).

[F2] $F=U/R$ is the fppf sheafification of the naive quotient presheaf; a section of $F$ over a scheme $T$ is represented fppf-locally by a morphism $T_j\to U$, and two such local representatives define the same section exactly when they differ fppf-locally by a point of $R$. The construction uses AC ([[def-quotient-fppf-sheaf-of-a-pre-relation]], [[lem-fppf-sheafification-exists]], [[def-fppf-sheaf-and-sheafification]]).

[F3] A flat morphism locally of finite presentation is universally open, and flat and locally finitely presented morphisms are stable under base change ([[thm-flat-finite-presentation-is-open]], [[lem-flat-morphisms-stable-base-change]]).

[F4] AC: every family of nonempty sets indexed by a set has a choice function ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]).



## Proof

1.1 The saturated open. Put $W^1=g(U')\subseteq U$, open because $g$ is universally open by [F3], and $W=t(s^{-1}(W^1))\subseteq U$, open because $t$ is universally open by [F3]. The set $W$ is saturated: if $u=t(r)$ with $s(r)\in W$, say $s(r)=t(r'')$ with $s(r'')\in W^1$, then lift $r,r''$ to a common residue-field extension over their shared point (the tensor product of their residue fields is nonzero); transitivity gives $r'''$ with $t(r''')=u$ and $s(r''')=s(r'')$, so $u\in W$; the reverse inclusion is immediate from the identity of $U$. Hence $t(s^{-1}(W))=W$, and $W$ is the saturated open generated by $W^1$. [F1, F3, given]

1.2 Injectivity and image of $F'\to F$. The morphism $U'\to U$ induces a map of quotient sheaves $F'\to F$. If $x,y\in U'(T)$ have the same image in $F(T)$, then after an fppf covering of $T$ there is a witness $r\in R$ with $t(r)=g(x)$, $s(r)=g(y)$; both $g(x)$ and $g(y)$ then lie in $W^1$, so the witness lands in $g(U')$ in both coordinates and defines a point of $R'$, whence $x,y$ already have the same image in $F'(T)$. Thus $F'\to F$ is injective. Moreover a section $u\in U(T)$ lies in the image of $F'$ exactly when, fppf-locally, it is $R$-equivalent to a point of $W^1$, that is, exactly when $u$ factors through the open $W$. For the reverse implication, the map $R\times_{s,U}U'\to W$ induced by $t$ is surjective flat and locally finitely presented: it is a composition of base changes of $g$ and $t$, and its image is $W$. Pulling it back along $u:T\to W$ supplies an fppf cover with the desired representative in $U'$. For arbitrary sections of $F'$ injectivity follows by choosing local representatives in $U'$ and applying the sheaf uniqueness condition. [F1, F2, F3]

2.1 Local presentation of a section. Let $a\colon T\to F$ be a morphism. By [F2] there is an fppf covering $\{\varphi_j\colon T_j\to T\}_{j\in J}$ and morphisms $a_j\colon T_j\to U$ whose images in $F(T_j)$ equal $a|_{T_j}$. For each ordered pair the two pullbacks $a_j\circ\mathrm{pr}_1$ and $a_{j'}\circ\mathrm{pr}_2$ on $T_j\times_TT_{j'}$ agree in $F$, so there are initially fppf-local transition morphisms into $R$. They are unique, since $j=(t,s)$ is a monomorphism, and therefore agree on overlaps and descend to global transition morphisms $r_{jj'}\colon T_j\times_TT_{j'}\to R$ by the represented-sheaf assertion of [[def-fppf-sheaf-and-sheafification]], with $t\circ r_{jj'}=a_j\mathrm{pr}_1$ and $s\circ r_{jj'}=a_{j'}\mathrm{pr}_2$. Put $W_j=a_j^{-1}(W)\subseteq T_j$, open. Then $W_j\times_TT_{j'}=r_{jj'}^{-1}(t^{-1}(W))=r_{jj'}^{-1}(s^{-1}(W))=T_j\times_TW_{j'}$, using the saturation $t(s^{-1}(W))=W$ of step 1.1. Define $W_T=\bigcup_j\varphi_j(W_j)$; each $\varphi_j$ is open by [F3], so $W_T$ is open, and $\varphi_j^{-1}(W_T)=W_j$: the inclusion $\supseteq$ is clear, while a point $t\in\varphi_j^{-1}(W_T)$ maps into some $\varphi_{j'}(W_{j'})$, so the pair $(t,t')\in T_j\times_TT_{j'}$ lies in $T_j\times_TW_{j'}=W_j\times_TT_{j'}$ and hence $t\in W_j$. [F1, F2, F3, step 1.1]

3.1 $W_T$ represents $T\times_FF'$. First, the composite $W_T\to T\xrightarrow{a}F$ lies in $F'(W_T)$: since $\{W_j\to W_T\}$ is an fppf covering and $F'$ is a sheaf, it suffices to show that each $W_j\to U\to F$ lies in $F'(W_j)$; the morphism $W'_j=W_j\times_Ws^{-1}(W^1)\times_{W^1}U'\to W_j$ is a base change of $t$ and of $g$, hence surjective flat and locally of finite presentation by [F1] and [F3], and the restriction of $W_j\to U\to F$ to $W'_j$ factors through $F'$ by construction, so the sheaf property of $F'$ gives the claim. Conversely, let $f\colon T'\to T$ satisfy $a|_{T'}\in F'(T')$. After the fppf base change $\{T'\times_TT_j\to T'\}$ we may assume $f=\varphi_j\circ f_j$ for some $j$; the condition means that there is an fppf covering $\{\psi_i\colon T'_i\to T'\}$ and morphisms $b_i\colon T'_i\to U'$ whose images in the quotient equal those of $a_jf_j\psi_i$. Refining once more by [F2] supplies morphisms $r'_i\colon T'_i\to R$ with $t\circ r'_i=a_jf_j\psi_i$ and $s\circ r'_i=gb_i$, so the image of $f_j\psi_i$ lies in $W_j$; hence $f$ factors through $W_T$ fppf-locally, and therefore globally. This proves $T\times_FF'\cong W_T$. [F2, step 1.2, step 2.1]

4.1 Conclusion. By step 3.1 every base change of $F'\to F$ along a morphism from a scheme is an open subscheme of the source, so $F'\to F$ is representable by schemes and an open immersion; its image is determined by the saturated open $W$ of step 1.1. If $W=U$, then step 1.2 shows the image of $F'$ in $F$ contains every section of $U$, hence all of $F$ by the sheaf property, and injectivity makes $F'\to F$ an isomorphism. If $g$ is surjective then $W^1=U$ (the image of a surjective morphism is all of $U$) and $W=t(s^{-1}(U))=t(R)=U$ because $t$ is surjective. The Axiom of Choice is used exactly through the quotient-sheaf data of [F2], which enters in steps 1.2-2.1. [F2, F4, step 1.1, step 1.2, step 3.1] ∎
