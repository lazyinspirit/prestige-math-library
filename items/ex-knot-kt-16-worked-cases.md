## Example

Assume the full Axiom of Choice. For $T(2,3)$, the exterior group is $\langle x,y\mid x^2=y^3\rangle$, a central fiber is $h=x^2=y^3$, a positive meridian is $\mu=y^2x^{-1}$, and a preferred longitude is $\lambda=h\mu^{-6}$. Its Alexander polynomial is $\Delta(t)\doteq t^2-t+1$ and its genus is $1$. For $T(1,5)$, the group presentation reduces to $\mathbb Z$, the knot is the unknot, the Alexander polynomial is $1$, and the genus is $0$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** Full AC, the coprime torus knots $T(2,3)$ and $T(1,5)$, and the formulas of the A page.

[A1] Full AC supplies the countable-choice hypotheses used by the knot and genus statements ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]).

[L1] Full AC implies Countable Choice ([[thm-choice-implies-dependent-implies-countable-choice]], [[def-countable-choice]]).

[F1] [[thm-torus-knot-group-presentation]] gives $\pi_1(E_{T(p,q)})=\langle x,y\mid x^p=y^q\rangle$, $h=x^p=y^q$, and $\mu=y^\alpha x^\beta$, $\lambda=h\mu^{-pq}$ when $p\alpha+q\beta=1$.

[F2] [[thm-torus-knot-alexander-and-genus-formulas]] gives $\Delta_{T(p,q)}\doteq (t^{|pq|}-1)(t-1)/((t^{|p|}-1)(t^{|q|}-1))$ and $g(T(p,q))=(|p|-1)(|q|-1)/2$.

[F3] [[lem-torus-knot-unit-slope-is-unknot]] says $T(p,q)$ is the unknot when $|p|=1$ or $|q|=1$.

## Verification

1.1 For $(p,q)=(2,3)$ the group relation is $x^2=y^3$, and this common element $h$ is central. The equation $2\alpha+3\beta=1$ has solution $(\alpha,\beta)=(2,-1)$, so [F1] gives $\mu=y^2x^{-1}$ and $\lambda=h\mu^{-6}$. [A1, F1, given, algebra]

1.2 Substituting $(2,3)$ into [F2] gives
$$\Delta(t)\doteq\frac{(t^6-1)(t-1)}{(t^2-1)(t^3-1)}=\frac{t^3+1}{t+1}=t^2-t+1,$$
and $g=(2-1)(3-1)/2=1$. [F2, step 1.1, algebra]

1.3 For $(p,q)=(1,5)$, the relation $x=y^5$ eliminates $x$ and leaves the infinite cyclic group generated by $y$. The meridian equation has solution $(\alpha,\beta)=(1,0)$, so [F1] gives $\mu=y$ and $\lambda=h\mu^{-5}=1$ in the group. [F1, given, algebra]

2.1 Full AC [A1] supplies the countable-choice hypothesis of [F3] by [L1], so [F3] identifies $T(1,5)$ with the unknot. In [F2] the quotient cancels to $1$ and the genus expression is $0$, giving the stated Alexander polynomial and genus. [A1, L1, F2, F3, step 1.3, algebra] ∎
