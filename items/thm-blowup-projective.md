## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice, inherited from the relative Proj construction
([[def-axiom-of-choice]]). Let $X$ be a scheme, let $\mathcal I$ be a
quasi-coherent ideal sheaf of finite type on $X$
([[def-quasi-coherent-ideal-sheaf]]) and let
$\pi\colon\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}X\to X$ be the blowup of
[[def-blowup-scheme-along-ideal]]. Then:

1. $\pi$ is locally H-projective on $X$: for every affine open
   $U=\operatorname{Spec}A$ with $\mathcal I|_U=(f_0,\dots,f_r)$, the pullback
   of the graded surjection $A[x_0,\dots,x_r]\to R(\mathcal I|_U)$,
   $x_i\mapsto f_it$, exhibits $\pi^{-1}(U)$ as a closed subscheme of
   $\mathbb P^r_U$.
2. If $\mathcal I$ is generated as an $\mathcal O_X$-module by finitely many
   global sections $f_0,\dots,f_r$
   ([[def-globally-generated-sheaf]]), then the same surjection
   $\mathcal O_X[x_0,\dots,x_r]\to\mathcal R(\mathcal I)$ makes
   $\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}X$ a closed subscheme of $\mathbb P^r_X$
   over $X$; in particular $\pi$ is H-projective
   ([[def-projective-morphism-pre-proj]]).
3. In all cases $\pi$ is proper, since properness is local on the base and each
   $\pi^{-1}(U)\to U$ is H-projective hence proper.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A scheme $X$, a quasi-coherent ideal sheaf $\mathcal I$ of finite
type, the blowup
$\pi\colon\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}X=\operatorname{Proj}_X\mathcal R(\mathcal I)\to X$
([[def-blowup-scheme-along-ideal]]), and for an affine open
$U=\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq X$ the restricted ideal
$I=\Gamma(U,\mathcal I)$ with affine blowup algebras $A[I/a]$.

[F1] [[thm-closed-subschemes-projective-space-homogeneous-ideals]]: For a
commutative ring $A$ and a homogeneous ideal $J\subseteq A[x_0,\dots,x_r]$ one
has $V_+(J)=\operatorname{Proj}\bigl(A[x_0,\dots,x_r]/J\bigr)$ as a closed
subscheme of $\mathbb P^r_A$, and on the chart $D_+(x_i)$ it is
$\operatorname{Spec}\bigl(A[x_0,\dots,x_r]_{(x_i)}/J_{(x_i)}\bigr)$; every
closed subscheme of $\mathbb P^r_A$ arises from a unique saturated homogeneous
ideal.

[F2] [[lem-relative-proj-affine-local-gluing]]: The relative Proj of a
quasi-coherent graded $\mathcal O_X$-algebra is obtained by gluing the spectra
$\operatorname{Proj}\Gamma(U,\mathcal A)$ over affine opens $U\subseteq X$,
with canonical restriction and cocycle identifications.

[F3] [[thm-affine-blowup-standard-charts]] and
[[lem-affine-blowup-algebra-properties]]: For $I=(f_0,\dots,f_r)$ the
standard opens $D_+(f_it)=\operatorname{Spec}A[I/f_i]$ cover
$\operatorname{Bl}_I\operatorname{Spec}A$, and the chart presentations are
independent of the chosen generating family; for
$U\subseteq X$ affine there is a canonical identification
$\pi^{-1}(U)\cong\operatorname{Bl}_{I|_U}U$
([[lem-blowup-local-on-base-scheme]]).

[F4] [[def-globally-generated-sheaf]]: A quasi-coherent sheaf $\mathcal I$ is
generated by global sections $f_0,\dots,f_r\in\Gamma(X,\mathcal I)$ exactly
when the evaluation morphism $\mathcal O_X^{\,r+1}\to\mathcal I$,
$(g_0,\dots,g_r)\mapsto\sum_ig_if_i$, is surjective.

[F5] [[thm-projective-morphism-proper]] and [[lem-proper-local-on-base]]: An
H-projective morphism is proper, and properness is local on an open cover of
the base.

[F6] [[def-closed-immersion-schemes]] and
[[def-quasi-coherent-module-scheme]]: A morphism $i$ is a closed immersion
when it is a homeomorphism onto a closed subset and
$\mathcal O\to i_*\mathcal O$ is surjective; both conditions are local on the
target, and kernels of morphisms of quasi-coherent modules are
quasi-coherent.

## Proof

1.1 Let $U=\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq X$ be affine and let $f_0,\dots,f_r\in A$ generate $I=\Gamma(U,\mathcal I)$. The graded $A$-algebra homomorphism $\varphi\colon A[x_0,\dots,x_r]\to R(I)$, $x_i\mapsto f_it$, is surjective, because in degree $n$ the images $f^{\alpha}t^n$ of the monomials $x^{\alpha}$ of degree $n$ generate $I^nt^n$; hence by [F1] it presents $\operatorname{Proj}R(I)=\operatorname{Bl}_IU\cong\pi^{-1}(U)$ as the closed subscheme $V_+(\ker\varphi)\hookrightarrow\mathbb P^r_U$ over $U$, which is assertion 1. [F1, F3]

1.2 If $\mathcal I$ is generated by global sections $f_0,\dots,f_r$, then the evaluation morphism $\mathcal O_X^{\,r+1}\to\mathcal I$ is surjective by [F4], and consequently the induced morphism of graded $\mathcal O_X$-algebras $\psi\colon\mathcal O_X[x_0,\dots,x_r]\to\mathcal R(\mathcal I)$, $x_i\mapsto f_it$, is surjective in every degree, because in degree $n$ its image is the subsheaf generated by the products of $n$ of the global sections, which is $\mathcal I^n$ by hypothesis. [F1, F4]

2.1 Assume the global generation of step 1.2. On every affine open $U\subseteq X$ the restriction of $\psi$ is the surjection $\varphi$ of step 1.1 for the restricted generators, so by step 1.1 the morphisms $\pi^{-1}(U)\to\mathbb P^r_U\subseteq\mathbb P^r_X$ are closed immersions over $U$; these affine-local morphisms agree on overlaps because they are induced by the restrictions of the single graded morphism $\psi$ and the identifications of [F2] are canonical, so they glue to a morphism $\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}X\to\mathbb P^r_X$ over $X$. [F2, step 1.1, step 1.2]

3.1 The glued morphism of step 2.1 is a closed immersion: surjectivity of the structure map of sheaves is checked on stalks, and a subset of $\mathbb P^r_X$ whose traces on the members of an open cover are closed is closed, so both conditions of [F6] are affine-local and hold because each restriction is a closed immersion; hence $\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}X$ is a closed subscheme of $\mathbb P^r_X$ over $X$, and $\pi$ is H-projective by [[def-projective-morphism-pre-proj]], which is assertion 2. [F6, step 2.1]

4.1 For every affine open $U\subseteq X$ the restriction $\pi^{-1}(U)\to U$ is a closed subscheme of $\mathbb P^r_U$ over $U$ by step 1.1, hence H-projective and therefore proper by [F5]; since properness is local on an open cover of the base by [F5], the morphism $\pi$ itself is proper, which is assertion 3, and it holds whether or not $\mathcal I$ is globally generated. [F5, step 1.1] ∎

## Remarks

- Assertion 1 holds for an arbitrary finite type ideal sheaf; assertion 2
  needs the stronger hypothesis that the ideal is generated by finitely many
  global sections, and it is this case that produces a globally defined closed
  immersion into relative projective space.
- The properness in assertion 3 is the only part used in the sequel for
  valuative arguments and for the direct image computations on an affine base;
  the local H-projectivity is used to read off charts.
