The finite product construction is the library's base case for later countable
product arguments. Associativity of the finite product means one can speak
unambiguously about $\mu_1 \times \mu_2 \times \mu_3$, but this page does not
infer any Kolmogorov-extension theorem or infinite-product measure from that
finite fact alone.
