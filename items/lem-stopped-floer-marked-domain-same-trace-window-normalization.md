## Statement

Assume AC and the finite exact stopped brane datum. Fix one finite ordinary or independent-block marked Floer context: finitely many actual branes/transverse Hamiltonian copies; a finite ordered word of smooth Hamiltonian brane paths, with their original transported primitives and inverse/refinement decorations; compact external parameter cells; and positive sitting atom durations fixed or in compact intervals bounded away from zero. Include the actual stable punctured-disk trees, transition-only vertices, protected Moore-gap subfamilies, literal finite merged-word faces and stationary growing real-node strips. A formal identity or a fixed-scale Morse/PSS cap is its separate source fixture, not a limit obtained by dividing by a zero atom duration. Each atom and shared parameter is retained exactly once. The brane paths are the actual homogeneous-at-infinity Hamiltonian lifts, including their compact-body pieces; no primitive constant is reset.

There is a choice of strip ends, component cores, smooth moving-boundary clocks, and extensions of compatible lower-face Floer data with the following conclusions. The construction is from those data; uniform estimates are conclusions, not additional hypotheses. Compatible prescribed lower-face germs mean actual smooth data on normalized source/parameter collars agreeing on their overlaps under the iterated face identifications, with the stated stationary ends. Clocks not already supplied as such coherent germs are among the choices constructed here; arbitrary endpoint-compatible nonnegative clock derivatives are not asserted to have a smooth nonnegative extension. After normalizing each stable component and retaining every marked component, the fibers have a complete conformal unit-window atlas of bounded overlap. For some context-dependent $r_0>0$, every finite source point lies in an interior disk or one-edge boundary half-disk of radius at least $r_0$ in that atlas. A boundary half-disk has exactly one smooth boundary trace, even when centered at a transition mark or translated arbitrarily far along a node strip. Distinct ordinary labels meet only at removed strip punctures; a moving transition mark carries its actual smooth sitting path, not an instantaneous boundary-plane jump. Finite merges concatenate those paths smoothly, and nodal faces use their recorded component/node chords. No multi-label marked vertex is deleted as an ordinary bubble.

For every compact target window $C$, every finite derivative order $a$ has a constant $K_a(C)$ controlling on these normalized windows the pulled-back almost-complex structure, Hamiltonian forcing, moving embeddings, and a finite family of boundary-adapted tubular charts and their inverses. There is a fixed positive lower bound for their total-reality singular values and a fixed chart radius. The same bounds hold in every bounded separable perturbation ball built from the rescaled smooth bump construction below. Growing necks remain exactly stationary; constants are independent of their lengths, relative homotopy sectors and positions of translated unit windows. They may depend on the finite word and its compact external data. Bounds concern normalized spatial coordinates and the actual compact coefficient parameters, not derivatives in a singular raw plumbing coordinate at its zero fiber.

Consequently the usual one-smooth-trace linear estimate and nonlinear gradient-normalized bootstrap apply to the actual retained families. For a blow-up scale $M_j\to\infty$, explicit source derivatives of order $a$ acquire $M_j^{-a}$ and the forcing acquires the additional factor $M_j^{-1}$. Target derivatives of $J$ survive. After component normalization a rescaled boundary limit has one fixed exact brane; two distinct strip edges cannot both survive at bounded rescaled distance. With a supplied common compact image and Floer energy bound, the gradient-normalized limits therefore have finite energy and are nonconstant planes or one-brane half-planes. This is the applicability input for the exhaustion-Stokes contradiction; no compactification, corner Fredholm or nonlinear gluing theorem is assumed or proved by this lemma.
