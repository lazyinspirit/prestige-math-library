## Statement

Let $J$ be finite, $1\le d\le |J|$, and let $\mathcal H_d\subseteq\{e\subseteq J:|e|=d\}$ be a finite pattern. Define $\mathcal H_{d-1}=\partial\mathcal H_d$ and recursively $\mathcal H_{j-1}=\partial\mathcal H_j$ down to $j=1$. Let $\mathcal B_e\subseteq\mathcal A_e$ be finite top algebras of complexity at most an integer $m\ge1$. For every increasing integer function $F:\mathbb N_{\ge1}\to\mathbb N_{\ge1}$ with $F(n)\ge n+1$, there are integers
$$m=M_d,\qquad F(M_j)\le M_{j-1}\quad(1\le j\le d),\qquad M_0\le U(J,\mathcal H_d,m,F),$$
where $U$ is bounded by the finite recurrence in the Proof, and for every $0\le j<d$ and $f\in\mathcal H_j$ there are finite algebras
$$\mathcal B_f\subseteq\mathcal B'_f\subseteq\mathcal A_f,\qquad \operatorname{complex}(\mathcal B_f)\le M_j.$$
For every $1\le j\le d$, $e\in\mathcal H_j$, and $E\in\mathcal B_e$, these algebras satisfy
$$\mathcal E_{\bigvee_{f\in\partial e}\mathcal B'_f}(E)-\mathcal E_{\bigvee_{f\in\partial e}\mathcal B_f}(E)\le \frac1{F(M_j)^2}, \qquad \Delta_e(E\mid(\mathcal B'_f)_{f\in\partial e})\le\frac1{F(M_0)}.$$
At rank $d$, $\mathcal B_e$ is the given top algebra. If the pattern is empty, all face families and estimates are vacuous.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A finite coordinate set $J$, a rank-$d$ pattern, top algebras of complexity at most $m\ge1$, and an increasing integer growth function $F(n)\ge n+1$.

[F1] The coarse/fine regularity lemma gives a finite bound for its returned coarse complexity, the requested energy gap and a discrepancy tolerance $1/F(M)$ ([[lem-finite-hypergraph-coarse-fine-regularity]]).

[F2] Face patterns are generated by repeatedly taking all codimension-one subfaces, and face algebras consist of events depending only on those coordinates ([[def-finite-partite-hypergraph-system-and-coordinate-algebras]]).

[F3] Conditional energy and discrepancy are the finite-coordinate quantities defined by atomwise averaging and finite skeleton tests ([[def-finite-coordinate-conditional-expectation-energy-and-discrepancy]]).

## Proof

1.1 Set $\mathcal H_d$ as given and form each lower face family recursively. If $\mathcal H_d$ is empty, take no face algebras, set $M_d=m$, and choose $M_{j-1}=F(M_j)$; every estimate is vacuous. Hence assume $\mathcal H_d\ne\varnothing$. [given, F2, construct]

1.2 We prove by induction on $d$, with the auxiliary rank-zero case having only the empty face: for a supplied top algebra of complexity at most $s$, take it as both coarse and fine, set $M_0=s$, and impose no positive-rank estimates. Thus $U_0(J,\mathcal H_0,s,F)=s$. [given, F2, algebra, base]

1.3 For the induction step, let $U_{d-1}(J,\mathcal H_{d-1},s,F)$ be the nondecreasing finite bound already constructed at rank $d-1$. Define $F^\star(t)=t+1+\max_{1\le s\le t}\{F(s),F(U_{d-1}(J,\mathcal H_{d-1},s,F))\}$. This is an increasing integer function because the maximum is over a finite nonempty set. For the preliminary recurrence of [F1], put $T=|\mathcal H_d|2^{2^m}$, $R=\lceil T F(m)^2\rceil+1$, $x_0=F^\star(m)$, and $x_{r+1}=x_r+\lceil T F^\star(x_r)^2\rceil+1$ for $0\le r<R$. Its output bound is $N_d=x_R$. Set $U_d(J,\mathcal H_d,m,F)=U_{d-1}(J,\mathcal H_{d-1},N_d,F)$. These are explicit finite recurrences. [given, F1, F2, F3, construct, IH]

2.1 Apply [F1] to the rank-$d$ pattern with tolerance $\epsilon=1/F(m)$ and growth function $F^\star$. It gives coarse and fine algebras on $\mathcal H_{d-1}$, a parameter $M_{d-1}\ge F^\star(m)$ with $M_{d-1}\le N_d$, energy gaps at rank $d$ at most $1/F(m)^2$, and top discrepancies at most $1/F^\star(M_{d-1})$. [step 1.3, F1]

3.1 Apply the induction hypothesis to the rank-$(d-1)$ pattern $\mathcal H_{d-1}$, using these coarse algebras as its top algebras and the original $F$. It gives $M_{d-1}\le F(M_{d-1})\le\cdots\le M_0\le U_{d-1}(J,\mathcal H_{d-1},M_{d-1},F)$ and all required lower-face algebras and estimates. Since $M_{d-1}\le N_d$, their bound is at most $U_d$. [step 1.3, step 2.1, F1, algebra]

4.1 By definition of $F^\star$, $F^\star(M_{d-1})\ge F(U_{d-1}(J,\mathcal H_{d-1},M_{d-1},F))\ge F(M_0)$. Therefore the rank-$d$ discrepancies from step 2.1 are at most $1/F(M_0)$; their energy gaps are $1/F(M_d)^2$. The lower-rank estimates hold by step 3.1, and the stated hierarchy and bound follow. [step 2.1, step 3.1, F1, algebra]

5.1 Each recurrence uses finitely many integer operations and finite maxima; every refinement in [F1] chooses a witness from a finite family at a bounded finite stage. No choice axiom or infinite sequence of selections is used. [step 1.3, step 2.1, step 4.1, F1, F3, discharge-induction] ∎
