## Scope

The integration theory on this page is for compactly supported smooth densities, using Riemann integrals in positive-dimensional charts. General measurable nonnegative densities, $L^1$ densities, and associated Radon measures are outside the present construction. Those extensions belong to the separate Lebesgue change-of-variables and regular-measure development. No measurable integration or Radon-representation theorem is asserted or used here.
