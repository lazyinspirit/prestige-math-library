## Definition

Let $W$ be an FB-module over $k$, i.e. a functor from the groupoid of finite
sets and bijections to $k\text{-}\mathrm{Mod}$ ([[def-fi-category-and-fi-module]]);
thus $W(T)$ is a $k$-module for every finite set $T$, and every bijection
$\beta\colon T\to T'$ gives an isomorphism $W(\beta)\colon W(T)\to W(T')$.
Because an FB-module is defined on all finite sets, no transport choices are
needed below.

The **free FI-module** $M(W)$ assigns to a finite set $S$ the direct sum

$$M(W)_S:=\bigoplus_{T\subseteq S}W(T)$$

over all subsets $T\subseteq S$ ([[def-subset-and-proper-subset]],
[[def-direct-sum-of-a-family-of-modules]]; only finitely many summands occur).
For an injection $f\colon S\to S'$ and a subset $T\subseteq S$, the restriction
$f|_T\colon T\to f(T)$ is a bijection
([[def-injection-surjection-bijection]]), and the transition map
$f_*\colon M(W)_S\to M(W)_{S'}$ is the sum of the isomorphisms
$W(f|_T)\colon W(T)\to W(f(T))$ over all $T\subseteq S$. This is $k$-linear and
functorial, since $(f'\circ f)|_T=f'|_{f(T)}\circ f|_T$; hence $M(W)$ is an
FI-module over $k$.

For $S=[n]=\{0,\dots,n-1\}$ the symmetric group $S_n$
([[def-finite-symmetric-group-and-permutation-notation]]) acts by
$\sigma\cdot T:=\sigma(T)$ and $W(\sigma|_T)$, and this is a left action
making $M(W)_n$ a left $kS_n$-module ([[def-group-ring]]). Grouping the sum by
the cardinality of $T$, the subsets of cardinality $a$ form a single $S_n$-orbit
with stabilizer the ordered block subgroup
$S_{\{0,\dots,a-1\}}\times S_{\{a,\dots,n-1\}}\cong S_a\times S_{n-a}$, so the
orbit summand is identified with an induced module
([[def-induced-r-linear-g-module-by-h-covariant-functions]]):

$$M(W)_n\cong\bigoplus_{a=0}^{n}\operatorname{Ind}_{S_a\times S_{n-a}}^{S_n}\bigl(W_a\boxtimes k\bigr),$$

where $W_a:=W(\{0,\dots,a-1\})$ is the value of the FB-module on an $a$-element
set, the first factor acts on $W_a$ through $W$ and the second factor acts
trivially, and $\boxtimes$ is the external tensor product of modules for the
two direct factors ([[def-outer-induction-product-for-symmetric-group-characters]],
[[lem-character-ring-of-a-direct-product-is-the-tensor-product]],
[[def-tensor-product-of-modules-by-generators-and-relations]]).

**Adjunction.** Let $\pi$ be the functor from FI-modules to FB-modules that
forgets the maps between distinct cardinalities. The inclusion of the $T=S$
summand is a natural map $\eta_W\colon W\to\pi M(W)$, and evaluating a map
$F\colon M(W)\to V$ of FI-modules on that summand yields a map of FB-modules
$\pi F\circ\eta_W\colon W\to\pi V$. Conversely, a map of FB-modules
$\varphi\colon W\to\pi V$ extends uniquely to a map of FI-modules
$\widetilde\varphi\colon M(W)\to V$ whose value on the summand $W(T)\subseteq
M(W)_S$ is $V(\iota_{T,S})\circ\varphi_T$, with $\iota_{T,S}\colon T\to S$ the
inclusion; the components are compatible with injections and the two
constructions are inverse, so
$\operatorname{Hom}_{FI\text{-}Mod}(M(W),V)\cong
\operatorname{Hom}_{FB\text{-}Mod}(W,\pi V)$ naturally in $V$ and $W$: $M$ is
left adjoint to $\pi$, with unit $\eta$.

For $m\ge0$ write $k[S_m]$ for the FB-module with $k[S_m](T)$ the free $k$-module
on the bijections $[m]\to T$, and zero when $|T|\ne m$; its value on an
$m$-element set is the regular representation of $S_m$. Put
$M(m):=M(k[S_m])$. Then $M(m)_S$ has as $k$-basis the injections
$[m]\hookrightarrow S$ — equivalently, the ordered sequences of $m$ distinct
elements of $S$ — and for a partition $\lambda$ with $|\lambda|=m$ and a field
$k$ of characteristic $0$, put $M(\lambda):=M(S^\lambda_k)$, where the FB-module
$S^\lambda_k$ is supported on $m$-element sets and $S^\lambda_k(T):=
k[\operatorname{Iso}([m],T)]\otimes_{kS_m}S^\lambda_k$. By the adjunction, a map
$M(m)\to V$ of FI-modules corresponds naturally to the choice of an element of
$V_m$: the module $M(m)$ represents the functor $V\mapsto V_m$. All
constructions are given by finitely many canonical bijections of finite sets;
no choice principle is used.
