## Statement

Let $F\subseteq E\subseteq\mathbb C$ with $E/F$ finite Galois, let $G$ be finite, and let $U$ be an absolutely
irreducible $E$-representation with character $\chi$.  Then
$$E^{\operatorname{Stab}(U)}=F(\chi).$$

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** $F\subseteq E\subseteq\mathbb C$, $G$, $U$, and $\chi$ as in the statement.

[L1] $\chi_{{}^{\sigma}U}=\sigma\circ\chi$, and $\operatorname{Stab}(U)$ consists of conjugates equivalent to $U$ ([[def-galois-conjugate-representation]]).

[L2] Finite-dimensional complex representations of a finite group are equivalent exactly when their characters agree ([[thm-complex-representations-are-determined-by-their-characters]]).

[L3] For a finite Galois extension, intermediate fields are fixed fields of their fixing subgroups ([[thm-fundamental-theorem-of-finite-galois-theory]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 For $\sigma\in\operatorname{Gal}(E/F)$, [L1] and [L2] give $\sigma\in\operatorname{Stab}(U)\Longleftrightarrow\sigma(\chi(g))=\chi(g)$ for every $g\in G$. [L1, L2, algebra]

2.1 The right side says exactly that $\sigma$ fixes the field generated by $F$ and all character values, namely $F(\chi)$. [L1, step 1.1]

3.1 Thus $\operatorname{Stab}(U)=\operatorname{Gal}(E/F(\chi))$; [L3] gives $E^{\operatorname{Stab}(U)}=F(\chi)$. [L3, step 2.1] ∎
