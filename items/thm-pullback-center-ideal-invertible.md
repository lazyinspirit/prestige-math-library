## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice, inherited from the relative Proj construction
([[def-axiom-of-choice]]). Let $\mathcal I$ be a quasi-coherent ideal sheaf of
finite type on a scheme $X$ ([[def-quasi-coherent-ideal-sheaf]]), let
$\pi\colon\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}X\to X$ be its blowup and let
$E=\pi^{-1}(Z)$ be the exceptional subscheme, with the convention that
$\mathcal O(1)$ is the positive relative twist of the Rees algebra
$\mathcal R(\mathcal I)$ and $\mathcal O(-1):=\mathcal O(1)^{\vee}$. Then:

1. $\mathcal O(1)$ is invertible;
2. the natural degree-one map $\pi^*\mathcal I\to\mathcal O(1)$ is surjective,
   its image is the inverse image ideal
   $\mathcal I\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Bl}}$, and the induced map
   $\mathcal I\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Bl}}\to\mathcal O(1)$ of invertible
   sheaves is an isomorphism;
3. $\mathcal I\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Bl}}$ is invertible and
   $E=V(\mathcal I\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Bl}})$ is an effective Cartier
   divisor on $\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}X$, with
   $\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Bl}}(-E)=\mathcal I\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Bl}}
   =\mathcal O(1)$ and $\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Bl}}(E)=\mathcal O(-1)$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A scheme $X$, a quasi-coherent ideal sheaf $\mathcal I$ of finite
type, the Rees algebra sheaf $\mathcal R(\mathcal I)=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}\mathcal I^n$
([[def-rees-algebra-ideal-sheaf]]), the blowup
$\pi\colon\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}X=\operatorname{Proj}_X\mathcal R(\mathcal I)\to X$
with relative twists $\mathcal O(n)$
([[def-blowup-scheme-along-ideal]],
[[def-relative-proj-quasi-coherent-graded-algebra]]), and the exceptional
subscheme $E=\pi^{-1}(Z)$ with ideal sheaf the inverse image ideal
$\mathcal I\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Bl}}$
([[def-exceptional-divisor-blowup]]).

[F1] [[thm-affine-blowup-standard-charts]] and
[[lem-affine-blowup-algebra-properties]]: For $a\in I=\Gamma(U,\mathcal I)$ on
an affine open $U=\operatorname{Spec}A$, the chart
$\operatorname{Spec}A[I/a]$ of $\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}U$ has
$IA[I/a]=aA[I/a]$ with $a$ a nonzerodivisor of $A[I/a]$, and the charts over a
finite generating family cover the blowup.

[F2] [[thm-twisting-sheaf-invertible-standard-graded]]: For a commutative
graded ring $S$ generated over $S_0$ by $S_1$, all twists
$\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Proj}S}(n)$ are invertible, with
$\mathcal O(m)\otimes\mathcal O(n)\cong\mathcal O(m+n)$; the empty Proj is
allowed.

[F3] [[def-twisting-sheaf-proj]] and
[[def-relative-proj-quasi-coherent-graded-algebra]]: The relative twist
$\mathcal O(1)$ of a relative Proj is the sheaf whose sections over the chart
$D_+(f)$ are the degree-one part $S(1)_{(f)}$ of the localised graded algebra;
it is the sheafification of the degree-one part, and on an affine base it
restricts to the absolute twist $\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Proj}}(1)$.

[F4] [[def-invertible-sheaf]] and
[[def-effective-cartier-divisor]]: A sheaf is invertible when it is locally
free of rank one; an effective Cartier divisor on a scheme is given by local
equations that are regular sections, i.e. nonzerodivisors on the stalks.

[F5] [[def-invertible-sheaf-of-cartier-divisor]] and
[[lem-effective-cartier-divisor-exact-sequence]]: For an effective Cartier
divisor $D$ the sheaf $\mathcal O_X(-D)$ is the ideal sheaf
$\mathcal I_D\subseteq\mathcal O_X$, and there is a short exact sequence
$0\to\mathcal O_X(-D)\to\mathcal O_X\to i_*\mathcal O_D\to0$.

## Proof

1.1 The Rees algebra is generated in degree one. Thus its positive twist is invertible on each affine base, and these restrictions give an invertible sheaf $\mathcal O(1)$ on the relative Proj. There are two natural maps from $\pi^*\mathcal I$: the structural multiplication map $\psi$ to $\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Bl}}$, whose image is $\mathcal I\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Bl}}$, and the degree-one map $\gamma$ to $\mathcal O(1)$. [F2, F3, given]

2.1 On a standard chart $B=A[I/a]$, $\mathcal O(1)$ is free with frame $at$. For $b\in I$ and $c\in B$, the maps are $\gamma(b\otimes c)=c(b/a)(at)$ and $\psi(b\otimes c)=cb=a\,c(b/a)$. The first is surjective since $\gamma(a\otimes1)=at$. Since $a$ is a nonzerodivisor in $B$, these formulas give $\ker\gamma=\ker\psi$, including for sums of tensors. Consequently $\gamma$ factors uniquely through the image $IB=aB$ of $\psi$ and induces an isomorphism $IB\to B(at)$ taking $a$ to $at$. This does not assert that $I\otimes_A B$ is free or torsion-free. [F1, F3, step 1.1]

3.1 The equality of the kernels is local and therefore global. The induced isomorphisms are restrictions of this unique factorization, so agree on overlaps. Hence $\mathcal I\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Bl}}\cong\mathcal O(1)$ canonically. The ideal cuts out $E$ and is locally generated by the nonzerodivisor $a$, so $E$ is effective Cartier. Its ideal is $\mathcal O(-E)$, and dualizing the isomorphism gives $\mathcal O(E)\cong\mathcal O(-1)$. Empty charts and the empty blowup satisfy the same assertions. [F1, F4, F5, step 2.1] ∎

## Remarks

- Assertion 2 identifies the inverse image ideal with the twist $\mathcal O(1)$
  as an invertible sheaf, which is what makes $E$ Cartier even when the centre
  $Z$ is neither reduced nor Cartier in $X$.
- Combining assertion 3 with
  [[lem-effective-cartier-divisor-exact-sequence]] gives the short exact
  sequence $0\to\mathcal O(1)\to\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Bl}}\to
  i_*\mathcal O_E\to0$ on the blowup, a form used in cohomological
  computations.
