## Statement

Let $(S,m)$, $W$, $\ell$ be as in [[def-hh-coxeter-matrix-word-group-and-length]]; let $R$, $v_s$, $H$, $T_s$ be as in [[def-hh-universal-coxeter-hecke-parameters-and-presentation]]; let $T_w$ for $w\in W$ and the spanning statement be as in [[lem-hh-reduced-word-independence-and-length-multiplication]]; and let $E$, $P_s$, $Q_s$ and $P_w$ be as in [[lem-hh-commuting-left-right-hecke-length-operators]].

1. **The length-operator representation.** There is a unique unital $R$-algebra homomorphism $\rho:H\to\operatorname{End}_R(E)$ with $\rho(T_s)=P_s$ for all $s\in S$; it satisfies $\rho(T_w)=P_w$ and $\rho(T_w)(e_1)=e_w$ for all $w\in W$.
2. **Standard basis.** $\{T_w:w\in W\}$ is an $R$-basis of $H$: it spans by [[lem-hh-reduced-word-independence-and-length-multiplication]] and is $R$-linearly independent. Hence $H$ is free as an $R$-module and every element of $H$ has a unique expansion $\sum_wa_wT_w$ with $a_w\in R$ and all but finitely many $a_w$ zero.
3. **Faithfulness.** $\rho$ is injective.
4. **Base change.** For every commutative ring $R'$ and every ring homomorphism $\varphi:R\to R'$, the scalar extension $R'\otimes_RH$ is, as an $R'$-algebra, canonically isomorphic to the quotient of the free associative $R'$-algebra on $(T_s)_{s\in S}$ by the two-sided ideal generated by the images under $\varphi$ of the relations (Q) and (B) of [[def-hh-universal-coxeter-hecke-parameters-and-presentation]], and it is free as an $R'$-module with basis $(1\otimes T_w)_{w\in W}$. No flatness or freeness of $R'$ over $R$ is assumed, and no torsion-freeness or semisimplicity of $H$ or of its specialisations is claimed.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A finite Coxeter matrix $(S,m)$, the group $W$ with length $\ell$, the parameters $R$, $v_s$ and the algebra $H$, and the free $R$-module $E$ with basis $(e_w)_{w\in W}$ and operators $P_s$, $Q_s$, $P_w$.

[F1] The operators satisfy $P_s^2=(v_s-v_s^{-1})P_s+\operatorname{id}_E$, the braid relations $P_sP_tP_s\cdots=P_tP_sP_t\cdots$ of $m(s,t)$ alternating factors for $s\ne t$ with $m(s,t)<\infty$, and $P_sQ_t=Q_tP_s$; for a reduced expression $w=s_1\cdots s_k$ the product $P_w:=P_{s_1}\cdots P_{s_k}$ is independent of the reduced expression and satisfies $P_w(e_1)=e_w$. ([[lem-hh-commuting-left-right-hecke-length-operators]])

[F2] $H=F/I$ is presented by the generators $T_s$ subject to the relations (Q) $(T_s-v_s)(T_s+v_s^{-1})=0$ and (B) the alternating braid equalities; consequently, for every unital associative $R$-algebra $A$ and every family $(t_s)$ in $A$ satisfying (Q) and (B) there is a unique unital $R$-algebra homomorphism $H\to A$ with $T_s\mapsto t_s$. ([[def-hh-universal-coxeter-hecke-parameters-and-presentation]])

[F3] For a reduced expression $w=s_1\cdots s_k$ the element $T_w:=T_{s_1}\cdots T_{s_k}$ is well defined and independent of the reduced expression, and $\{T_w:w\in W\}$ spans $H$ as an $R$-module. ([[lem-hh-reduced-word-independence-and-length-multiplication]])

[F4] In a free module with basis $(e_w)$, the basis vectors are $R$-linearly independent: a finite relation $\sum_wa_we_w=0$ has all $a_w=0$. ([[def-free-module-on-a-set-and-standard-basis]])

[F5] $\operatorname{End}_R(E)$ is the endomorphism ring of $E$, a unital ring under composition with central $R$-action, and a unital $R$-algebra homomorphism is multiplicative and unital. ([[def-endomorphism-ring-of-a-module]], [[prop-endomorphisms-form-a-ring]], [[def-algebra-over-a-commutative-ring]])

[F6] For a commutative ring homomorphism $\varphi:R\to R'$, part 2 of [[lem-hh-universal-presentations-and-base-change]] identifies $R'\otimes_R(R\langle X\rangle/I)$ with $(R'\otimes_RR\langle X\rangle)/\operatorname{im}(R'\otimes_RI)$, the image ideal being generated by the images of the relations, with no flatness or freeness of $R'$ over $R$ assumed; part 3 gives that tensoring a free $R$-module with basis $(a_i)$ yields a free $R'$-module with basis $(1\otimes a_i)$.

## Proof

**Given:** A finite Coxeter matrix $(S,m)$, the group $W$ with length $\ell$, the algebra $H$ over $R$, the free module $E$ with basis $(e_w)_{w\in W}$ and the operators $P_s$, $Q_s$, $P_w$.

1.1 By [F1] the operators $P_s$ satisfy the quadratic relations (Q) of [F2] and the braid relations (B). The family $(P_s)_{s\in S}$ therefore lies in the unital associative $R$-algebra $\operatorname{End}_R(E)$ and satisfies the two families of relations (Q) and (B), so the universal property [F2] gives a unique unital $R$-algebra homomorphism $\rho:H\to\operatorname{End}_R(E)$ with $\rho(T_s)=P_s$. [F1, F2, F5]

2.1 For a reduced expression $w=s_1\cdots s_k$ of $w$, multiplicativity of $\rho$ ([F5]) and $T_w=T_{s_1}\cdots T_{s_k}$ ([F3]) give $\rho(T_w)=\rho(T_{s_1})\cdots\rho(T_{s_k})=P_{s_1}\cdots P_{s_k}$, which equals $P_w$ by [F1]; hence $\rho(T_w)(e_1)=P_w(e_1)=e_w$ ([F1]). [F1, F3, F5, step 1.1]

3.1 Let $\sum_wa_wT_w=0$ be a finite $R$-linear relation in $H$. Applying the $R$-linear map $\rho$ and evaluating at $e_1$ gives $0=\rho\bigl(\sum_wa_wT_w\bigr)(e_1)=\sum_wa_w\rho(T_w)(e_1)=\sum_wa_we_w$ by step 2.1, so $a_w=0$ for all $w$ by the linear independence of the basis $(e_w)$ ([F4]). Thus $\{T_w\}$ is $R$-linearly independent; combined with the spanning statement of [F3] it is an $R$-basis, so $H$ is free and each element has a unique expansion as stated. [F3, F4, step 2.1]

4.1 If $\rho(h)=0$, write $h=\sum_wa_wT_w$ in its unique expansion from step 3.1; then $0=\rho(h)(e_1)=\sum_wa_w\rho(T_w)(e_1)=\sum_wa_we_w$ by step 2.1, so $a_w=0$ for all $w$ and $h=0$. Hence $\rho$ is injective. [F4, step 2.1, step 3.1]

4.2 Since $\{T_w\}$ is an $R$-basis of $H$ (step 3.1), [F6] part 2 identifies $R'\otimes_RH$ with the quotient of $R'\otimes_RR\langle T_s:s\in S\rangle$ by the ideal generated by the images of (Q) and (B), and part 1 of [[lem-hh-universal-presentations-and-base-change]] identifies the latter free algebra with the free associative $R'$-algebra on $(T_s)_{s\in S}$; by [F6] part 3, applied to the free $R$-module $H$ with basis $(T_w)$, the scalar extension is free with basis $(1\otimes T_w)_{w\in W}$. All of this holds for an arbitrary ring homomorphism $\varphi$, with no flatness, torsion-freeness or semisimplicity hypothesis. [F3, F6, step 3.1]

5.1 Assembly: part 1 is step 1.1 together with step 2.1, part 2 is step 3.1, part 3 is step 4.1 and part 4 is step 4.2. No choice is used: the construction selects no objects beyond the given data, the operators are defined by explicit length conditions ([F1]), and the only "evaluation" is at the explicitly named vector $e_1$. [F1, step 1.1, step 2.1, step 3.1, step 4.1, step 4.2] ∎
