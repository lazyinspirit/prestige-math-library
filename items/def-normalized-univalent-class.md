## Definition

The **normalized univalent class** is

$$\mathcal S:=\{\,f:\mathbb D\to\mathbb C:f\text{ is holomorphic and univalent},\ f(0)=0,\ f'(0)=1\,\},$$

where univalence is that of [[def-univalent-holomorphic-function]].
