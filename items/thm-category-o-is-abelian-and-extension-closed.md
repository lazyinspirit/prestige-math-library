## Statement

Fix a finite-dimensional complex semisimple Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$, a Cartan subalgebra $\mathfrak h$, and a positive Borel $\mathfrak b=\mathfrak h\oplus\mathfrak n^+$. Write $Q^+=\sum_i\mathbb Z_{\geq0}\alpha_i$, $\mu\leq\lambda$ when $\lambda-\mu\in Q^+$, and $w\cdot\lambda=w(\lambda+\rho)-\rho$.

The category $\mathcal O$ is closed under submodules, quotients and finite direct sums and is an abelian category. If $0\to A\to E\to B\to0$ is exact, $A,B\in\mathcal O$, and $E$ is $\mathfrak h$-semisimple, then $E\in\mathcal O$. The middle-term weight hypothesis is essential.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The setting above and the hypotheses in the statement.

[F1] Fix a finite-dimensional complex semisimple Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$, a Cartan subalgebra $\mathfrak h$, and a positive Borel $\mathfrak b=\mathfrak h\oplus\mathfrak n^+$. Write $Q^+=\sum_i\mathbb Z_{\geq0}\alpha_i$, $\mu\leq\lambda$ when $\lambda-\mu\in Q^+$, and $w\cdot\lambda=w(\lambda+\rho)-\rho$. The **BGG category $\mathcal O$** is the full subcategory of left $U(\mathfrak g)$-modules $M$ satisfying all three conditions: $M$ is finitely generated; $M=\bigoplus_{\mu\in\mathfrak h^*}M_\mu$ where $M_\mu=\{v:hv=\mu(h)v\text{ for all }h\in\mathfrak h\}$; and $U(\mathfrak n^+)v$ is finite dimensional for each $v\in M$. Its morphisms are all $\mathfrak g$-linear maps. The zero module is included, generated by the empty set. The enveloping and triangular conventions are those of thm-pbw-ordered-monomial-basis-for-the-enveloping-algebra and thm-triangular-decomposition-from-a-chosen-positive-root-system. ([[def-bgg-category-o]])

[F2] Fix a finite-dimensional complex semisimple Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$, a Cartan subalgebra $\mathfrak h$, and a positive Borel $\mathfrak b=\mathfrak h\oplus\mathfrak n^+$. Write $Q^+=\sum_i\mathbb Z_{\geq0}\alpha_i$, $\mu\leq\lambda$ when $\lambda-\mu\in Q^+$, and $w\cdot\lambda=w(\lambda+\rho)-\rho$. Let $M$ be a finitely generated $\mathfrak h$-semisimple $\mathfrak g$-module. Then $M\in\mathcal O$ if and only if $\operatorname{supp}M\subset\bigcup_{i=1}^r(\lambda_i-Q^+)$ for some finite list of weights. In either case every $M_\mu$ is finite dimensional. The list may be empty for $M=0$; finite generation is an independent hypothesis. ([[prop-equivalent-support-description-of-category-o]])

[F3] Fix a finite-dimensional complex semisimple Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$, a Cartan subalgebra $\mathfrak h$, and a positive Borel $\mathfrak b=\mathfrak h\oplus\mathfrak n^+$. Write $Q^+=\sum_i\mathbb Z_{\geq0}\alpha_i$, $\mu\leq\lambda$ when $\lambda-\mu\in Q^+$, and $w\cdot\lambda=w(\lambda+\rho)-\rho$. For every finite-dimensional complex Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$ (semisimplicity is unnecessary here), $U(\mathfrak g)$ is left and right Noetherian. ([[lem-enveloping-algebra-is-left-and-right-noetherian]])

[F4] For every ring $R$, the category $R\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}$ of left $R$-modules is an abelian category. ([[thm-modules-over-a-ring-form-an-abelian-category]])

[F5] For any ordered basis of a finite-dimensional complex Lie algebra, the corresponding ordered monomials form a basis of its enveloping algebra. ([[thm-pbw-ordered-monomial-basis-for-the-enveloping-algebra]])

[F6] For $\lambda\in\mathfrak h^*$, the Verma module is $M(\lambda)=U(\mathfrak g)\otimes_{U(\mathfrak b)}\mathbb C_\lambda$. ([[def-verma-module]])

## Proof

1.1 Write $R=U(\mathfrak g)$. Every submodule of $R^r$ is finitely generated: for $r=1$ this is left Noetherianity; for $r>1$ project to the last coordinate, lift finite generators of the image ideal, and adjoin generators of the kernel, a submodule of $R^{r-1}$. The case $r=0$ is zero. Taking inverse images under $R^r\twoheadrightarrow M$ proves that every submodule of a finitely generated module is finitely generated. [F3, algebra]

2.1 A submodule of a weight module is a sum of weight spaces: for the finitely many components of a vector choose $h\in\mathfrak h$ separating their distinct weights and apply the interpolation polynomials in $h$. Quotients therefore inherit weight decompositions, and exact sequences of weight modules are exact at each weight. Submodules and quotients inherit local $\mathfrak n^+$-finiteness, and quotients inherit finite generation. Finite direct sums inherit all three axioms, including the empty sum. [F1, algebra, step 1.1]

3.1 Kernels and cokernels of $\mathcal O$-maps are thus the ambient module kernels and cokernels. The full subcategory contains zero and finite biproducts; the ambient image-coimage isomorphism remains inside it. The module-category abelian axioms therefore hold in $\mathcal O$. [F4, step 2.1]

4.1 For the asserted extension, choose generators of $A$ and lifts of finitely many generators of $B$. They generate $E$, since subtracting their $R$-linear combinations leaves an element of $A$. Weightwise exactness gives $\operatorname{supp}E=\operatorname{supp}A\cup\operatorname{supp}B$. Each is in finitely many downward cones; the assumed weight decomposition and finite generation of $E$ allow F2 to be applied. Thus $E\in\mathcal O$, also when either end is zero. [F2, algebra, step 3.1]

5.1 The weight hypothesis cannot be omitted. For $\mathfrak g=\mathfrak{sl}_2$, let $V=\mathbb Cv\oplus\mathbb Cw$ be a $\mathfrak b$-module with $ev=ew=0$, $hv=\lambda v$, and $hw=\lambda w+v$. Then $0\to\mathbb C_\lambda\to V\to\mathbb C_\lambda\to0$ is exact. Ordering a PBW basis with $f$ before a basis of $\mathfrak b$ shows that $U(\mathfrak g)$ is free as a right $U(\mathfrak b)$-module, so induction is exact and gives $0\to M(\lambda)\to E\to M(\lambda)\to0$ for $E=U(\mathfrak g)\otimes_{U(\mathfrak b)}V$. Each end lies in $\mathcal O$: its PBW basis $f^kv_\lambda$ consists of weight vectors, it is cyclic, and $e$ acts locally nilpotently. But PBW also gives $1\otimes v\ne0$ in $E$, while $(h-\lambda)(1\otimes w)=1\otimes v$ and $(h-\lambda)^2(1\otimes w)=0$. Thus $h$ is not semisimple on $E$, so $E\notin\mathcal O$. [F1, F5, F6, algebra] ∎
