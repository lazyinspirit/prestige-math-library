## Open problem

Does every even integer $n>2$ admit prime integers $p,q$ such that $n=p+q$?

## Open problem references

[[def-prime|prime means an integer $p>1$ whose positive divisors are only $1$ and $p$]]

[Wolfram MathWorld, “Goldbach Conjecture”](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/GoldbachConjecture.html)

## Status

As of 2026-10-26, this binary statement remains open. The published computation cited by MathWorld verifies the statement for even integers through $4\cdot10^{18}$; this finite range is not a proof for all even $n$. Established partial results include that almost all even integers are sums of two primes and that every sufficiently large even integer is a prime plus an integer with at most two prime factors.
