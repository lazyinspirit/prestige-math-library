## Definition

Let $\mathcal T$ be an essentially small triangulated category
([[def-triangulated-category]]), with translation $[1]$ and class of
distinguished triangles. Essential smallness supplies a set
$\operatorname{Iso}(\mathcal T)$ of isomorphism classes of objects, so the free
abelian group $\mathbb Z[\operatorname{Iso}(\mathcal T)]$ on that set exists
([[def-free-abelian-group]]). Write $[X]$ for the image of the generator
belonging to the isomorphism class of $X$. The **triangulated Grothendieck
group** of $\mathcal T$ is

$$ K_0^{\mathrm{tri}}(\mathcal T):=\mathbb Z[\operatorname{Iso}(\mathcal T)]\Big/\big\langle [Y]-[X]-[Z]: X\to Y\to Z\to X[1]\text{ is a distinguished triangle}\big\rangle . $$

Thus the defining relation is $[Y]=[X]+[Z]$ for every distinguished triangle
$X\to Y\to Z\to X[1]$ of $\mathcal T$, with the cochain orientation fixed by the
translation ([[def-distinguished-triangle]]). The subgroup generated by these
elements is a subgroup of an abelian group, so the quotient is an abelian
group; the presentation uses triangle relations and not merely the biproduct
relations that define a split Grothendieck group.

The definition applies in particular to the strictly full subcategory
$D_{\mathrm{perf}}(A)$ of perfect objects inside $D(A\text{-}\mathrm{Mod})$,
introduced in the preceding definition, and to $D^b(\mathcal C)$ for an
essentially small abelian category $\mathcal C$ under the standing
derived-localization size convention of
[[def-derived-category-of-an-abelian-category]]; in both cases the ambient
category is triangulated and the subcategory or localization is essentially
small, so $\operatorname{Iso}$ is a set.

Shift and functoriality properties of $K_0^{\mathrm{tri}}$ are not assumed
here: they are proved in the following lemma.
