## Definition

Let $A$ be a unital associative ring, and let $\operatorname{Proj}_{\mathrm{fg}}(A)$ be the category of finitely generated projective left $A$-modules. The group $K_0(A)$ is the abelian group generated by isomorphism classes $[P]$ of objects of $\operatorname{Proj}_{\mathrm{fg}}(A)$, subject to $[P\oplus Q]=[P]+[Q]$ and $[0]=0$. For each $n\geq 1$, let $\operatorname{GL}_n(A)$ be the group of invertible $n\times n$ matrices over $A$, with stabilization $g\mapsto\operatorname{diag}(g,1)$, and let $\operatorname{GL}_{\infty}(A)$ be the direct limit of these groups. The group $K_1(A)$ is the abelianization $\operatorname{GL}_{\infty}(A)_{\mathrm{ab}}$.
