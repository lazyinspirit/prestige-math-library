For an analytic $m$th-order PDE posed on an analytic hypersurface and locally
solved for the $m$th derivative in a noncharacteristic normal direction,
analytic data prescribing the normal derivatives through order $m-1$ determine
a unique local analytic solution.  The existence and uniqueness are within the
analytic class and are local.  This is a recorded external theorem, not a
smooth-data theorem and not a result proved or used by this page.
