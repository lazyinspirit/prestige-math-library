## Definition

Let X be a T-space, μ a Radon measure on X, and t_U the local leaf charts. The measure μ is T-recurrent if for every measurable E⊂X with μ(E)>0, for μ-almost every x∈E, every compact C⊂T and every chart U containing x, there exists t∈T\C such that t_U(x,t)∈E.
