## Statement refuted

Fix a finite-dimensional complex semisimple Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$, a Cartan subalgebra $\mathfrak h$, and a positive Borel $\mathfrak b=\mathfrak h\oplus\mathfrak n^+$. Write $Q^+=\sum_i\mathbb Z_{\geq0}\alpha_i$, $\mu\leq\lambda$ when $\lambda-\mu\in Q^+$, and $w\cdot\lambda=w(\lambda+\rho)-\rho$.

False claim: $\mathcal O$ is extension closed in the category of all $\mathfrak g$-modules.

For $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ and any $\lambda\in\mathbb C$, let $V=\mathbb Cv\oplus\mathbb Cw$ be a positive-Borel module with $ev=ew=0$, $hv=\lambda v$ and $hw=\lambda w+v$. Then $E=U(\mathfrak g)\otimes_{U(\mathfrak b)}V$ is an extension of $M(\lambda)$ by itself in ambient modules, but is not in $\mathcal O$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The setting above and the hypotheses in the statement refuted.

[F1] Fix a finite-dimensional complex semisimple Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$, a Cartan subalgebra $\mathfrak h$, and a positive Borel $\mathfrak b=\mathfrak h\oplus\mathfrak n^+$. Write $Q^+=\sum_i\mathbb Z_{\geq0}\alpha_i$, $\mu\leq\lambda$ when $\lambda-\mu\in Q^+$, and $w\cdot\lambda=w(\lambda+\rho)-\rho$. The **BGG category $\mathcal O$** is the full subcategory of left $U(\mathfrak g)$-modules $M$ satisfying all three conditions: $M$ is finitely generated; $M=\bigoplus_{\mu\in\mathfrak h^*}M_\mu$ where $M_\mu=\{v:hv=\mu(h)v\text{ for all }h\in\mathfrak h\}$; and $U(\mathfrak n^+)v$ is finite dimensional for each $v\in M$. Its morphisms are all $\mathfrak g$-linear maps. The zero module is included, generated by the empty set. The enveloping and triangular conventions are those of thm-pbw-ordered-monomial-basis-for-the-enveloping-algebra and thm-triangular-decomposition-from-a-chosen-positive-root-system. ([[def-bgg-category-o]])

[F2] Let $x_1,\dots,x_r$ be an ordered basis of a finite-dimensional complex Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$. Then the monomials $$x_1^{a_1}x_2^{a_2}\cdots x_r^{a_r} \qquad (a_i\in \mathbb N_0)$$ form a basis of $U(\mathfrak g)$. In particular, multiplication identifies $\operatorname{gr}U(\mathfrak g)$ with the symmetric algebra $S(\mathfrak g)$ on the symbols of the $x_i$. ([[thm-pbw-ordered-monomial-basis-for-the-enveloping-algebra]])

[F3] For $\lambda\in\mathfrak h^*$, the **Verma module** is the induced $\mathfrak g$-module $$M(\lambda):=U(\mathfrak g)\otimes_{U(\mathfrak b)}\mathbb C_\lambda,$$ where $U(\mathfrak g)$ is the quotient algebra of def-universal-enveloping-algebra-as-a-tensor-quotient and $\mathbb C_\lambda$ is def-one-dimensional-borel-module-of-weight-lambda. Write $v_\lambda:=1\otimes c_\lambda$. Thus $M(\lambda)$ is the quotient of $U(\mathfrak g)$ by the left ideal generated by $x$ for $x\in\mathfrak n^+$ and $h-\lambda(h)$ for $h\in\mathfrak h$; in particular, $\mathfrak n^+v_\lambda=0$ and $hv_\lambda=\lambda(h)v_\lambda$. ([[def-verma-module]])

## Counterexample

1.1 The Borel relation $[h,e]=2e$ holds on $V$ because $e$ acts by zero. There is a short exact sequence $0\to\mathbb C_\lambda\to V\to\mathbb C_\lambda\to0$, with submodule $\mathbb Cv$ and quotient generated by the image of $w$. PBW makes $U(\mathfrak g)$ right free over $U(\mathfrak b)$, so induction preserves this exact sequence. Its two ends are the Verma modules by definition. [F2, F3, construct]

2.1 PBW identifies $E$ with $\mathbb C[f]\otimes V$ as a vector space. Thus $1\otimes v\ne0$, while $(h-\lambda)(1\otimes w)=1\otimes v$ and $(h-\lambda)^2(1\otimes w)=0$. In a weight module, $\ker(h-\lambda)^2=\ker(h-\lambda)$, by evaluating on its eigenspace decomposition. This Jordan pair proves that $E$ is not a weight module. Its end terms do satisfy the category-O axioms: their PBW basis has weights $\lambda-2k$ and locally nilpotent $e$ action, and they are cyclic. Consequently the ambient extension leaves $\mathcal O$. [F1, F2, algebra, step 1.1] ∎
