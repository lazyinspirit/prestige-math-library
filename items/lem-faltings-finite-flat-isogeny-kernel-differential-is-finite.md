## Statement

Let $K$ be a number field, $R=\mathcal O_K$, $A_1/K$ and $A_2/K$ abelian varieties of the same dimension, and $\phi:A_1\to A_2$ a finite surjective homomorphism. Let $\mathcal G$ be a finite flat commutative $R$-group scheme whose generic fibre is $\ker\phi$, and let $e:\operatorname{Spec}R\to\mathcal G$ be its identity section. Then the cotangent space of $\ker\phi$ at the identity is zero, the differential $d\phi_e:T_eA_1\to T_eA_2$ is an isomorphism, and the cotangent module $e^*\Omega^1_{\mathcal G/R}$ is finite as an abelian group.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A number field $K$, its ring of integers $R$, two equal-dimensional abelian varieties over $K$, a finite surjective homomorphism between them, and a finite flat $R$-model of its kernel.

[F1] [[def-number-field]] and [[def-ring-of-integers-of-a-number-field]]: $K$ has characteristic zero and $R=\mathcal O_K$ is a domain.

[F2] [[def-abelian-variety-over-a-field]]: $A_1$ and $A_2$ are smooth connected group varieties of the same dimension.

[F3] [[def-finite-flat-group-scheme-and-cartier-dual]]: $\mathcal G$ is finite locally free over $R$, so its cotangent module at the identity is finitely generated.

[F4] [[lem-differentials-commute-base-change-schemes]]: pulling $\Omega^1_{\mathcal G/R}$ back to the generic fibre identifies it with $\Omega^1_{\mathcal G_K/K}$.

[F5] [[lem-nonzero-number-field-ideal-has-finite-quotient]]: for every nonzero $a\in R$, the quotient ring $R/aR$ is finite.

[F6] [[thm-transitivity-exact-sequence-differentials]]: for $K$-algebras $B\to C$, the relative differentials fit into a right-exact transitivity sequence.

## Proof

**Given:** [F1]–[F4], $\phi$, and $\mathcal G$ as in the Statement.

1.1 The extension of function fields $K(A_1)/K(A_2)$ induced by $\phi$ is finite. Since $K$ has characteristic zero, it is separable. For a finite set of algebraic generators $x$ with separable minimal polynomials $P$, differentiating $P(x)=0$ gives $P'(x)\,dx=0$; each $P'(x)$ is nonzero and invertible in the function field. Thus $\Omega_{K(A_1)/K(A_2)}=0$. The function-field transitivity sequence [F6] makes the generic cotangent map surjective between two $g$-dimensional vector spaces, hence an isomorphism. [F1, F2, F6, given, algebra]

1.2 For any point $x$ of $A_1$ over an extension field, the group-homomorphism identity $\phi\circ t_x=t_{\phi(x)}\circ\phi$ identifies the differential at $x$ with the differential at the identity by isomorphisms of tangent spaces. Applying this identity to the generic point and using step 1.1 shows $d\phi_e$ is an isomorphism. The tangent space of $\ker\phi$ at its identity is $\ker(d\phi_e)$, so it is zero; its cotangent space is the dual vector space and is therefore zero. [F2, step 1.1, given, algebra]

2.1 Put $M=e^*\Omega^1_{\mathcal G/R}$. It is finitely generated by [F3], and [F4] identifies $M\otimes_RK$ with the cotangent space of $\ker\phi$ at its identity, which vanishes by step 1.2. Thus each generator of $M$ is annihilated by a nonzero element of $R$. [F3, F4, step 1.2, algebra]

2.2 Choose finitely many generators $m_1,\ldots,m_r$ and nonzero annihilators $a_i\in R$. Their product $a=\prod_i a_i$ is nonzero and annihilates $M$, so the surjection $R^r\to M$ factors through $(R/aR)^r$. By [F5], this latter set is finite; therefore $M$ is finite as an abelian group. [F1, F5, step 2.1, choose, algebra] ∎
