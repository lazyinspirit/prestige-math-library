## Statement

Let $F$ be any field and let $H_d^{\deg}$ be the algebra generated by commuting
$x_1,\dots,x_d$ and symmetric-group generators $s_1,\dots,s_{d-1}$ with
$$s_rx_r=x_{r+1}s_r-1,\qquad s_rx_{r+1}=x_rs_r+1,\qquad s_rx_j=x_js_r\quad(j\notin\{r,r+1\}),$$
together with the symmetric-group quadratic, braid and distant commutation
relations, as in the degenerate clause of
[[def-type-a-affine-presentations-and-charged-cyclotomic-quotients]]
([[def-finite-symmetric-group-and-permutation-notation]],
[[thm-the-symmetric-group-has-the-coxeter-presentation]]). Then
$$\{x_1^{a_1}\cdots x_d^{a_d}w:a_j\ge0,\ w\in S_d\}$$
is an $F$-basis of $H_d^{\deg}$. No cyclotomic basis or Brundan–Kleshchev
isomorphism is assumed. For $d=0$ the basis is the singleton empty monomial.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A field $F$, the polynomial ring $P=F[z_1,\dots,z_d]$, its fraction field $K=F(z_1,\dots,z_d)$, and the crossed product $K\rtimes S_d$ with basis the group elements and multiplication $(f\sigma)(g\tau)=f\,\sigma(g)\,\sigma\tau$.

[F1] $H_d^{\deg}$ is presented by the relations of the Statement ([[def-type-a-affine-presentations-and-charged-cyclotomic-quotients]]).

[F2] $S_d=\langle s_1,\dots,s_{d-1}\rangle$ with the Coxeter relations, and every word in the $s_r$ reduces to a standard word $w\in S_d$; a proper subword of a reduced expression has strictly smaller length ([[thm-the-symmetric-group-has-the-coxeter-presentation]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

**Given:** The presentations of [F1], the field $F$, the crossed product

$K\rtimes S_d$, and the operator model of step 1.1.

1.1 The operator model. Let $S_r$ be the automorphism of $K$ swapping $z_r,z_{r+1}$ and fixing the other variables, and put $\delta_r:=z_r-z_{r+1}$. Let $z_j$ act on $K$ by multiplication and let $A_r:=S_r+\frac{S_r-1}{\delta_r}=\Bigl(1+\frac1{\delta_r}\Bigr)S_r-\frac1{\delta_r}.$ For $p\in P$ the difference $S_rp-p$ is divisible by $\delta_r$ in $P$ (write $p$ in the variables $z_r,z_{r+1}$ with the remaining variables as scalars and use that $z_r^az_{r+1}^b-z_{r+1}^az_r^b$ is divisible by $\delta_r$), so $A_r$ preserves $P$; hence the operators $z_j$ (multiplication) and $A_r$ generate a subalgebra of $\operatorname{End}_F(K)$ and, restricted to $P$, of $\operatorname{End}_F(P)$. [given, construct]

2.1 Verification of the defining relations in the model. Direct substitution gives the mixed relations $A_rz_r-z_{r+1}A_r=-1$, $A_rz_{r+1}-z_rA_r=1$, and $A_rz_j=z_jA_r$ for $j\notin\{r,r+1\}$. Writing $a(t):=1+1/t$ and $b(t):=-1/t$, so that $A_r=a(\delta_r)S_r+b(\delta_r)$, and using $S_r(\delta_r)=-\delta_r$, the identity coefficient of $A_r^2$ is $a(\delta_r)a(-\delta_r)+b(\delta_r)^2=1$ and its $S_r$-coefficient is $a(\delta_r)b(-\delta_r)+b(\delta_r)a(\delta_r)=0$, so $A_r^2=1$; distant $A_r$ commute because their permutations and coefficients involve disjoint variable pairs. For the braid relation put $u:=z_r-z_{r+1}$, $v:=z_{r+1}-z_{r+2}$, $w:=u+v=z_r-z_{r+2}$, $S:=S_r$, $T:=S_{r+1}$; expanding $A_rA_{r+1}A_r$ and $A_{r+1}A_rA_{r+1}$ in $K\rtimes S_d$ gives the six permutation coefficients $-\frac{uv+1}{uvw}\ (1),\quad \frac{a(u)}{vw}\ (S),\quad \frac{a(v)}{uw}\ (T),\quad -\frac{a(u)a(w)}{v}\ (ST),\quad -\frac{a(v)a(w)}{u}\ (TS),\quad a(u)a(w)a(v)\ (STS),$ and the right expansion has the same six coefficients with $u,v$ and $S,T$ interchanged, so the two expansions agree. These are rational identities valid in every characteristic, including $2$. Hence the assignment $z_j\mapsto$ multiplication by $z_j$, $s_r\mapsto A_r$ defines an algebra homomorphism $H_d^{\deg}\to\operatorname{End}_F(K)$ (and into $\operatorname{End}_F(P)$ by step 1.1). [F1, algebra]

3.1 Spanning. In the model, $s_rp=(S_rp)s_r-(p-S_rp)\delta_r^{-1}$ for every polynomial $p$, so every word in the generators rewrites as a linear combination of the ordered monomials $z^aw$ with $a_j\ge0$ and $w\in S_d$, the power-polynomial part being collected by commutativity and the $s$-word reduced by [F2]. The same straightening performed in $H_d^{\deg}$ (available there because the model satisfies the defining relations, or by the same elementary computation in the presentation) shows that the ordered monomials span the algebra. [F1, F2, step 2.1, algebra]

3.2 Independence. In the crossed-product expansion of a reduced product $A_w$, the coefficient at $w$ is $c_w=\prod_{k=1}^{m}(1+1/(s_{i_1}\cdots s_{i_{k-1}})(\delta_{i_k}))\ne0$; every other permutation has length less than $\ell(w)$, because it is represented by a proper subword. The distinct field automorphisms are independent over $K$: from a shortest nonzero operator relation $\sum_jf_j\sigma_j=0$, compose with multiplication by a variable on which some $\sigma_j$ differs from $\sigma_1$, and subtract the original relation multiplied by $\sigma_1$ of that variable. This gives a shorter nonzero relation; a one-term relation cannot annihilate $1$. Thus the crossed product acts faithfully on $K$. Now an $F$-linear relation among the ordered monomials maps to $\sum_w f_w(z)A_w=0$, with $f_w(z)=\sum_a c_{a,w}z^a\in P$. If some $f_w$ is nonzero, choose such a $w$ of maximal length. Its coefficient is $f_wc_w\ne0$ and receives no contribution from any other maximal-length label or shorter label, a contradiction. Therefore every $f_w=0$, and polynomial coefficient uniqueness gives every $c_{a,w}=0$. This proves $F$-linear independence of the ordered monomials, without asserting their impossible $K$-linear independence. [F2, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 Conclusion. Steps 3.1 and 2.1 show that the family $\{x_1^{a_1}\cdots x_d^{a_d}w:a_j\ge0,\ w\in S_d\}$ both spans and is linearly independent in $H_d^{\deg}$ over $F$; renaming the commuting generators $z_j\mapsto x_j$ and using the presentation [F1] gives the asserted $F$-basis. No cyclotomic quotient, BK isomorphism or dimension theorem is used, and for $d=0$ the family consists of the empty monomial alone. [step 3.1, step 3.2] ∎

## Remarks

The model $A_r=S_r+(S_r-1)/\delta_r$ is the standard rational-operator model of the degenerate affine Hecke algebra; the only inversions are of the nonzero differences $\delta_r$, and the construction is valid in every characteristic. The independence proof uses finitely many automorphisms of a finitely generated field, so no choice principle is needed.
