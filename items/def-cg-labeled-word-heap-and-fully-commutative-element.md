## Definition

Let $(S,m)$ be a finite Coxeter matrix and let $W$ be the group presented by it, with length $\ell$ and set $\mathcal R(w)$ of reduced expressions, so that $m(s,t)$ is the order of $st$ in $W$ ([[def-hh-coxeter-matrix-word-group-and-length]]).

**(1) Words.** A **word** in $S$ is a finite sequence $s=(s_1,\dots,s_k)$ with $s_i\in S$. Its **length** is $k$, it **represents** the element $s_1\cdots s_k\in W$, and concatenation of words represents the product of the represented elements. A word is **reduced** when it is a reduced expression of the element it represents, so $\mathcal R(w)$ is the set of all reduced words representing $w$.

**(2) The heap of a word.** Let $s=(s_1,\dots,s_k)$ be a word and put $[k]=\{1,\dots,k\}$. Write $i\prec_s j$ when $i<j$ and either $s_i=s_j$ or $m(s_i,s_j)\ge3$ (including $m(s_i,s_j)=\infty$); thus $i\prec_s j$ exactly when $i<j$ and the pair $s_i,s_j$ is not a commuting pair, i.e. $m(s_i,s_j)\ne2$. Let $\preceq_s$ be the reflexive transitive closure of $\prec_s$. Every relation $\prec_s$ has $i<j$, so $\preceq_s$ is contained in the usual order of the positions and is antisymmetric; hence $\preceq_s$ is a partial order on $[k]$ ([[def-partial-order]]). The **heap** of $s$ is the labeled poset $P_s:=([k],\preceq_s)$ in which the position $i$ carries the label $s_i$. Elements of a heap with the same label are pairwise comparable: if $i<j$ and $s_i=s_j$, then $i\prec_s j$.

**(3) Labeled heaps and labeled isomorphism.** A **labeled poset** is a triple $(P,\preceq,\lambda)$ in which $(P,\preceq)$ is a finite poset and $\lambda:P\to S$ is a map. Two labeled posets $(P,\preceq,\lambda)$ and $(P',\preceq',\lambda')$ are **isomorphic** when there is a bijection $\varphi:P\to P'$ with $x\preceq y\iff\varphi(x)\preceq'\varphi(y)$ for all $x,y\in P$ and $\lambda'(\varphi(x))=\lambda(x)$ for all $x\in P$. A labeled poset is a **heap** (for $(S,m)$) when it is isomorphic to $P_s$ for some word $s$.

**(4) Linear extensions.** Let a linear extension of a finite poset be as in [[def-cg-linear-extension-of-a-finite-poset]]. For a word $s$ of length $k$, the **labeled linear extensions** of $P_s$ are the words
$$L(P_s,s):=\{(s_{x_1},\dots,s_{x_k}):(x_1,\dots,x_k)\text{ is a linear extension of }P_s\}.$$

**(5) Commutativity classes.** Two words $s,s'$ of the same length are **commutation-equivalent**, written $s\sim s'$, when $s'$ is obtained from $s$ by finitely many interchanges of two adjacent letters $s_i,s_{i+1}$ with $m(s_i,s_{i+1})=2$. This is an equivalence relation on words: it is generated by the single interchanges, which are involutions, and it is by construction closed under composition. The **commutativity class** of $s$ is $C(s):=\{s':s'\sim s\}$. Commutation-equivalent words have the same length, the same multiplicity of every letter, and represent the same element of $W$: an interchange of adjacent letters with $m(s_i,s_{i+1})=2$ changes neither the length nor the multiplicities, and the represented element is unchanged because $st=ts$ in $W$ whenever $m(s,t)=2$; indeed, $s^2=t^2=1$ and $(st)^2=1$ imply $st=(st)^{-1}=t^{-1}s^{-1}=ts$ ([[def-hh-coxeter-matrix-word-group-and-length]]).

**(6) Fully commutative elements.** An element $w\in W$ is **fully commutative** when all its reduced words lie in a single commutativity class, that is, when $\mathcal R(w)=C(s)$ for one (equivalently, every) $s\in\mathcal R(w)$.

**(7) Abstentions and conventions.** The heap is defined for an arbitrary, not necessarily reduced, word. Nothing is asserted here about the relation between $\mathcal R(w)$ and $L(P_s,s)$, about invariance of $P_s$ under commutation, or about which elements are fully commutative; those are the content of [[thm-cg-heaps-classify-commutation-classes]] and [[thm-cg-fully-commutative-forbidden-chain-criterion]]. All data are finite and no Choice is used.
