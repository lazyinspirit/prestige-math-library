## Example

Let $F=\mathbb Q(\zeta_3)$ and

$$K=F(\sqrt[3]{2},\sqrt[3]{3}).$$

Then $K/F$ is a Kummer extension, and the subgroup generated by the classes of
$2$ and $3$ in $F^\times/(F^\times)^3$ is isomorphic to $(\mathbb Z/3)^2$.
Consequently

$$\operatorname{Gal}(K/F)\cong(\mathbb Z/3)^2.$$

## Facts & Assumptions
**Given:** The field $F=\mathbb Q(\zeta_3)$ and the extension $K=F(\sqrt[3]{2},\sqrt[3]{3})$.

[L1] Kummer theory identifies finite abelian extensions of exponent dividing $3$ with subgroups of $F^\times/(F^\times)^3$ ([[thm-kummer-correspondence]]).

[L2] Norm and trace are given by the embedding formulas ([[thm-field-norm-and-trace-by-embeddings]]).

## Verification

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 The field $F$ contains $\mu_3$, so adjoining cube roots is exactly the Kummer situation of [L1]. To show that the classes of $2$ and $3$ are independent modulo cubes, suppose $$2^a3^b=c^3\qquad\text{in }F^\times.$$ Taking norms from $F$ to $\mathbb Q$ gives $$2^{2a}3^{2b}=N_{F/\mathbb Q}(c)^3.$$ The left side is a rational cube only when $3\mid 2a$ and $3\mid 2b$, hence only when $3\mid a$ and $3\mid b$. Therefore the classes of $2$ and $3$ each have order $3$ and generate a subgroup isomorphic to $(\mathbb Z/3)^2$. [L2, algebra]

2.1 By [L1], the field generated by the corresponding cube roots is a Kummer extension with Galois group $$\operatorname{Hom}((\mathbb Z/3)^2,\mu_3)\cong(\mathbb Z/3)^2.$$ Since $K$ is exactly that field, the stated conclusion follows. [L1, step 1.1] ∎
