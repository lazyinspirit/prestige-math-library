## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice and Dependent Choice. Let \(K\) be a number field,
\(p\) a prime, and \(S\) a
finite set of places containing the archimedean places, every finite place of
residue characteristic \(p\), and every place where the fixed continuous
residual representation \(\bar\rho:G_{K,S}\to\operatorname{GL}_n(\mathbb F_p)\) is
ramified. It is obtained by semisimplifying the reduction of a specified
stable lattice, with no independence from another lattice assumed. Let \(G_{K,S}\)
be the Galois group of the maximal extension of \(K\) unramified outside
\(S\). Let \(\mathcal O\) be a complete discrete valuation ring with residue
field \(\mathbb F_p\), and let
\(D_{\bar\rho}\) be the functor of continuous lifts to local Artinian
\(\mathcal O\)-algebras with residue \(\mathbb F_p\), modulo conjugation by matrices
reducing to the identity. Assume \(\bar\rho\) is absolutely irreducible,
meaning that it remains irreducible after extending scalars to an algebraic
closure of \(\mathbb F_p\). Assume also Mazur’s \(\Phi_p\) condition in its
topological-generation form: for every open subgroup \(H\leq G_{K,S}\), the
inverse limit of all its finite \(p\)-group quotients is topologically
finitely generated. Then
\(D_{\bar\rho}\) is pro-represented by a complete
Noetherian local \(\mathcal O\)-algebra \(R_{\bar\rho}\) with residue field
\(\mathbb F_p\). The representing isomorphism is natural on every local Artinian
\(\mathcal O\)-algebra with residue field \(\mathbb F_p\); it imposes no fixed-determinant
or additional local deformation condition.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The hypotheses of the Statement.

[F1] \(D_{\bar\rho}(A)\) is the set of lifts of \(\bar\rho\) modulo strict conjugation by matrices congruent to the identity. [[def-deformation-functor-of-a-residual-galois-representation]]

[F2] Conditions (H1)–(H4), together with finite-dimensional tangent space, imply pro-representability by a complete Noetherian local coefficient algebra. [[thm-schlessinger-criterion-for-prorepresentability]]

[F3] For every open subgroup \(H\), its pro-\(p\) completion is topologically finitely generated by the \(\Phi_p\) hypothesis; a continuous homomorphism from \(H\) to a finite \(p\)-group is therefore determined by the images of finitely many topological generators. [given]

[F4] Absolute irreducibility gives \(\operatorname{End}_{\bar\rho(G)}(\mathbb F_p^n)=\mathbb F_p\). This follows because after scalar extension to \(\overline{\mathbb F_p}\), an eigenvalue of any commuting endomorphism has a nonzero invariant eigenspace, which must be all of \(\overline{\mathbb F_p}^n\). [given, Schur's lemma]

[F5] The deformation construction inherits AC from the fixed residual representation and uses DC through the Schlessinger criterion. [[def-axiom-of-choice]] [[def-dependent-choice]]

[F6] The tangent space of the deformation functor is canonically \(H^1_{\mathrm{cts}}(G_{K,S},\operatorname{ad}(\bar\rho))\). [[thm-galois-deformation-tangent-space-is-h1]]

## Proof

**Given:** The Statement and facts [F1]–[F5].

1.1 Let \(M=\operatorname{ad}(\bar\rho)\), a finite-dimensional \(\mathbb F_p\)-vector space with conjugation action. The kernel \(H\) of the action \(G_{K,S}\to\operatorname{GL}_{\mathbb F_p}(M)\) is open, and \(H\) acts trivially on \(M\). Since \(M\) is a finite \(p\)-group additively and the pro-\(p\) completion of \(H\) is topologically finitely generated by [F3], there are only finitely many continuous homomorphisms \(H\to M\). A continuous \(1\)-cocycle on \(G_{K,S}\) is determined by its restriction to \(H\) and its values on a finite set of coset representatives for \(G_{K,S}/H\). Therefore the cocycle set, and hence \(H^1_{\mathrm{cts}}(G_{K,S},M)\), is finite-dimensional over \(\mathbb F_p\). [F3, algebra]

1.2 Consider \(A'\to A\leftarrow A''\) with \(A''\to A\) a small extension, and a pair of compatible deformation classes over \(A'\) and \(A''\). Choose representatives. Their reductions to \(A\) are strictly equivalent, so conjugate one representative by a lift of the strict conjugating matrix to make the reductions equal. Such a matrix lift exists because the coefficient map is a surjection of local Artin rings and a lift of an invertible matrix over a local ring is invertible. The two representations now combine pointwise to a continuous representation into
\(\operatorname{GL}_n(A'\times_A A'')\), proving (H1). [F1, construct, algebra]

1.3 When \(A=\mathbb F_p\) and \(A''=\mathbb F_p[\epsilon]\), the same coordinatewise construction proves surjectivity in (H2). If two classes over \(A'\times_{\mathbb F_p}\mathbb F_p[\epsilon]\) have the same projections, choose strict conjugating matrices on the two factors. Both reduce to the identity over \(\mathbb F_p\), so they form a strict conjugating matrix over the fiber product; this proves injectivity and hence (H2). [F1, step 1.2, construct]

2.1 By the tangent-space theorem [F6], \(t_{D_{\bar\rho}}\cong H^1_{\mathrm{cts}}(G_{K,S},M)\), which is finite-dimensional by step 1.1. Thus (H3) holds. [F1, F6, step 1.1]

2.2 For a small extension \(B\to A\), consider the diagonal comparison in (H4). The strict-conjugacy argument reduces injectivity to surjectivity of the map between stabilizers of a lifted representation and its reduction. By [F4], the residual centralizer is scalar. If a matrix \(X\in M_n(B)\) commutes with the lifted representation, its reduction is scalar; subtract a scalar lift. The remainder lies in \(\mathfrak m_B M_n(B)\). Inductively, if the remainder lies in \(\mathfrak m_B^jM_n(B)\), its image modulo \(\mathfrak m_B^{j+1}\) commutes with \(\bar\rho\), so is scalar by [F4]; subtract that scalar lift. Nilpotence of \(\mathfrak m_B\) shows the full centralizer is \(B\cdot I_n\); the same induction over \(A\) shows its centralizer is \(A\cdot I_n\). Scalar strict automorphisms over \(A\) lift to scalar strict automorphisms over \(B\), so the stabilizer map is surjective. [F1, F4, algebra]

2.3 More explicitly, let two lifts to \(B\times_A B\) have projections \(\rho_1,\rho_2\) and \(\rho'_1,\rho'_2\). Choose strict conjugators \(g_1,g_2\in1+M_n(\mathfrak m_B)\) with \(g_i\rho_i g_i^{-1}=\rho'_i\). Their reductions \(\bar g_1,\bar g_2\) both carry the common reduction of \(\rho_1,\rho_2\) to that of \(\rho'_1,\rho'_2\), so \(d=\bar g_2^{-1}\bar g_1\) centralizes the common source representation over \(A\). By step 2.2, the stabilizer map for \(\rho_1\) surjects onto this centralizer, so choose a lift \(c\) of \(d\) centralizing \(\rho_1\). Replacing \(g_1\) by \(g_1c^{-1}\) preserves its conjugation equation and makes its reduction equal to \(\bar g_2\). The pair \((g_1c^{-1},g_2)\) is therefore a strict conjugator over \(B\times_A B\). This proves injectivity of the diagonal comparison; (H1) gives surjectivity, so (H4) holds. [F1, F4, given, step 2.2, construct]

3.1 Steps 1.2–2.3 verify (H1)–(H4), and step 2.1 verifies (H3). Apply the Schlessinger criterion [F2]. It yields a complete Noetherian local \(\mathcal O\)-algebra \(R_{\bar\rho}\) and a natural bijection between its continuous local \(\mathcal O\)-algebra maps to every Artinian coefficient algebra and the deformations of \(\bar\rho\). The definition of the functor [F1] includes no determinant or local-condition restriction, so neither is added to the represented problem. [F1, F2, F6, step 2.1, step 2.3] ∎
