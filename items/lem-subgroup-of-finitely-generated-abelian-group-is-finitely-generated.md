## Statement

Every subgroup of a finitely generated abelian group is finitely generated.

## Statement references

[[def-finitely-generated-group]]

[[def-subgroup]]

[[lem-subgroups-of-z-are-cyclic]]

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A finitely generated abelian group $G$ and a subgroup $H\le G$.

[F1] A finitely generated group is generated by a finite subset. [[def-finitely-generated-group]]

[F2] A subgroup is a subset closed under the group operation, identity, and inverses. [[def-subgroup]]

[F3] Every subgroup of $(\mathbb Z,+)$ equals $d\mathbb Z$ for some unique integer $d\ge0$. [[lem-subgroups-of-z-are-cyclic]]

## Proof

1.1 Choose a finite generating list $g_1,\ldots,g_n$ for $G$ using [F1], and define the surjection $\pi:\mathbb Z^n\to G$ by sending the $i$th standard basis vector to $g_i$. The preimage $\widetilde H=\pi^{-1}(H)$ is a subgroup of $\mathbb Z^n$ by [F2], and $\pi(\widetilde H)=H$. It remains to show every subgroup of $\mathbb Z^n$ is finitely generated. [given, F1, F2, construct]

2.1 We prove the remaining assertion by induction on $n$. It is immediate for $n=0$. For $n>0$, project $\widetilde H\le\mathbb Z^{n-1}\times\mathbb Z$ onto its last coordinate. By [F3] the image is $d\mathbb Z$ for some $d\ge0$, and the kernel $H_0=\widetilde H\cap\mathbb Z^{n-1}$ is finitely generated by induction. If $d=0$, then $\widetilde H=H_0$. If $d>0$, choose $h\in\widetilde H$ with last coordinate $d$; for any $x\in\widetilde H$, its last coordinate is $qd$, so $x-qh\in H_0$. Thus $h$ together with a finite generating set of $H_0$ generates $\widetilde H$. [F3, step 1.1, choose, induction]

3.1 The image under $\pi$ of a finite generating set of $\widetilde H$ generates $H$. Therefore $H$ is finitely generated. [step 1.1, step 2.1, given]

## Axiom use

The proof uses only finite generating lists and one witness at a time; it requires neither AC nor DC.
