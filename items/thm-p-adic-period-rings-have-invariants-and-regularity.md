## Statement

Let $K/\mathbb Q_p$ be finite, let $k$ be its residue field, and put $K_0=W_p(k)[1/p]$. Under the natural $G_K$-actions, $B_{\mathrm{dR}}^{G_K}=K$ and $B_{\mathrm{cris}}^{G_K}=B_{\mathrm{st}}^{G_K}=K_0$. Each of $B_{\mathrm{dR}}$, $B_{\mathrm{cris}}$, and $B_{\mathrm{st}}$ is $(\mathbb Q_p,G_K)$-regular in the stated sense.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** a finite extension $K/\mathbb Q_p$, its residue field, the associated absolute Galois group, and the period rings above.

[F1] [[def-p-adic-period-rings]]: the rings $B_{\mathrm{dR}}$, $B_{\mathrm{cris}}$, and $B_{\mathrm{st}}$ carry the indicated $G_K$-actions.

[F2] [[def-regular-period-ring]]: regularity means that the invariant ring is a field, the invariant subfield of the fraction field equals that invariant ring, and every nonzero Galois-stable $\mathbb Q_p$-line in the ring is generated by a unit.

The cited source states these invariant and regularity properties, but the local proof of the required injectivity/invariant calculations is omitted in Theorem 9.1.5. This item is retained as an explicit proof obligation and is escalated; no proof or acceptance is inferred from the citations.
