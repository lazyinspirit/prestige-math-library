## Statement

Fix an actual original index $k$ and either the specified finite CY4 dg-quiver with its actual vertex-space framing, or the constructed local-spectral branch of the weak phase/framing datum: a smooth separated quasi-projective complex threefold $Y$, $X=\operatorname{Tot}(K_Y)$, the stated actual pullback source/framing lines, concentration opens, equivariance and original orientation. Form the directed primary and independent auxiliary source/flag/sink diagram with its named source spaces and gauges and the actual relation $bc=0$, and then its inverse-diagonal four-dimensional tensor completion. This constructs the source and master models; it does not identify an arbitrary enhancement from a bare datum with them.

In the local-spectral branch the zero-source old sector identifies with the actual algebra $\operatorname{Sym}K_Y^{-1}$ and its compact-support sheaves, with the induced $t-m$ Hom resolution, commutator differential, trace and base-change maps. The primary trace-free model identifies by actual cochain maps with $R\operatorname{Hom}_X([\mathcal O\to F],[\mathcal O\to F])_0^\vee[-1]$ for the stated pullback primary line. Its evaluation, shifted duals, trace-free projection, zero-composite homotopy and obstruction map agree with the actual geometric primary cone. The constructed source's CY4 trace, spherical self-Ext and original concentrated framing functor are proved by its diagonal resolution.

In the auxiliary model the primary source and new source remain distinct; their maps to $\mathrm{Fr}_k(F)$ and to the primary source are independent. On the injective-$c$ locus its actual Koszul coordinate contraction eliminates $bc$ together with its dual rows. The remaining relative quotient cotangent is the perfect Artin complex $N_+=[\Omega\to\mathfrak g_{\mathrm{aux}}^\vee]$ in degrees $0,1$. With $A_+=N_+[-1]$, its actual obstruction complex is quasi-isomorphic to $E_+=\operatorname{cofib}(A_+\to\operatorname{fib}(E_p\to A_+^\vee[2]))$, preserving its differential, evaluation, shifted pairing, trace-free projection, contraction, nullhomotopy and universal obstruction map to $L_{Z_X}$. On a stipulated finite-phase semistable locus where the auxiliary-weight argument forces injective flag composites and forbids a negative-phase pure-sink quotient, the combined incoming sink map spans the sink, the auxiliary gauge is free, and $N_+$ is a degree-zero vector bundle. Injectivity of $c$ alone does not imply freeness.

These actual maps and ordered determinant comparisons commute with derived base change, chart refinement, named forgetting maps and direct sums, and transport the supplied original geometric or quiver orientation. No properness, mixed fixed-locus property, equivalence with the naive forward-arrow enhancement, or automatic comparison with an arbitrary pre-specified $\mathcal B_k$ is asserted.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** the explicitly constructed local-spectral datum of [F1], or the specified actual finite CY4 dg-quiver with its vertex framing; the original index, source lines, concentration open, equivariance and orientation. The full injective flag/covector Artin locus is retained; a degree-zero relative bundle is used only on the actual free locus. No equivalence with an unrelated bare source category is input or conclusion.

Here **CY4 dg-quiver** has the source's precise meaning (Bojko, Definition 3.23), which we make explicit. Start with finitely many vertices and arrows of degrees $0,-1$. For each arrow $e$ adjoin a reverse arrow $e^*$ of degree $-2-|e|$; a designated degree-$-1$ loop may instead be self-paired, $e^*=e$. Paths multiply by composition, with orthogonal vertex idempotents. A cyclic potential is an element $H$ of degree $-1$ in the path algebra modulo graded commutators. Its cyclic derivative $\partial_e^\circ$ sends each occurrence $r e q$ to $(-1)^{|r|(|e|+|q|)}q r$ and sums over occurrences. The potential is part of the input and satisfies the cyclic master equation $\sum_e(\partial_e^\circ H)(\partial_{e^*}^\circ H)=0$. Adjoin a loop $o_v$ of degree $-3$ at each vertex; the differential is $de=\partial_{e^*}^\circ H$, $de^*=(-1)^{|e|+1}\partial_e^\circ H$ and $do_v=e_v(\sum_e[e,e^*])e_v$, with the source convention for self-paired loops. This is the specified quiver presentation used below, rather than a presentation theorem for every dg algebra with an abstract CY4 trace. The local-spectral branch uses its separately prescribed inverse-diagonal construction.

[F1] [[def-gts-weak-phase-framing-moduli-datum]] supplies the original exact phase category, the distinction between ordinary and admissible maps, the exact framing bundles, scalar rigidification and orientations. The directed-source/inverse-diagonal model is specified there as a construction. Its CY4 trace, source concentration, resolution independence, old-sector and actual primary/secondary obstruction comparisons are proved below rather than assumed as target equivalences.
