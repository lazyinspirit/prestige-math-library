## Definition

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $K$ be a number field, fix a separable closure $K_s$, and write $G_K=\operatorname{Gal}(K_s/K)$. Let $R$ be the ring of integers of a finite extension of $\mathbb Q_p$, with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$, and let $T$ be a finite free $R$-module with continuous $G_K$-action. Let $S$ be a finite set consisting of finite prime ideals and archimedean embeddings, containing the primes above $p$, every finite prime where $T$ is ramified, and all archimedean places. For a finite prime $v$, let $K_v$ be the number-field completion at $v$ and choose an embedding of separable closures. This identifies the local absolute Galois group with a decomposition subgroup $D_v\subseteq G_K$; its inertia subgroup $I_v$ is the kernel of the action on the separable closure of the residue field. At an archimedean embedding use the corresponding field $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$ and its absolute Galois group. The representation is unramified outside $S$ when $I_v$ acts trivially for every finite prime $v\notin S$.

For any of these profinite groups $G$ acting continuously on a topological $R$-module $M$, let $Z^1_{\mathrm{cts}}(G,M)$ be the $R$-module of continuous maps $c:G\to M$ satisfying $c(gh)=c(g)+g c(h)$, let $B^1_{\mathrm{cts}}(G,M)$ consist of maps $c_t(g)=g t-t$, and define $H^1_{\mathrm{cts}}(G,M)=Z^1_{\mathrm{cts}}(G,M)/B^1_{\mathrm{cts}}(G,M)$. A local Selmer condition at $v\in S$ is an $R$-submodule $H^1_{\mathcal F}(K_v,T)\subseteq H^1_{\mathrm{cts}}(G_{K_v},T)$. A Selmer structure $\mathcal F$ is the choice of $S$ and one such local condition for each $v\in S$.

Let $G_{K,S}$ be the quotient of $G_K$ by the closed normal subgroup generated by the inertia groups at finite primes outside $S$. The Selmer group for $(T,\mathcal F)$ is
$$H^1_{\mathcal F}(K,T)=\ker\!\left(H^1_{\mathrm{cts}}(G_{K,S},T)\longrightarrow\bigoplus_{v\in S}H^1_{\mathrm{cts}}(G_{K_v},T)/H^1_{\mathcal F}(K_v,T)\right),$$
where the map is the sum of the local restriction maps.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC; a number field $K$; a finite free $R$-module $T$ with continuous Galois action; a finite set $S$ as in the Definition; and local submodules $H^1_{\mathcal F}(K_v,T)$.

[A1] AC is assumed by the number-field completion used at finite places. [[def-axiom-of-choice]] [[def-completion-of-a-number-field-at-a-prime]]

[F1] [[def-absolute-galois-group-of-a-global-field]]: $G_K$ is the inverse-limit absolute Galois group of the fixed separable closure.

[F2] [[def-completion-of-a-number-field-at-a-prime]]: the completion at a nonzero prime ideal is taken with the normalized prime-ideal absolute value.

## Verification

1.1 The continuous $1$-cocycles form an $R$-submodule of the continuous maps $G\to M$, and the $1$-coboundaries form a submodule of the cocycles. Hence their quotient $H^1_{\mathrm{cts}}(G,M)$ is an $R$-module for every group and module in the Definition. [given, F1, algebra]

2.1 Each local restriction map is $R$-linear, and each local condition is a submodule, so the map to the finite direct sum of quotient modules is $R$-linear. Its kernel is therefore a well-defined $R$-submodule, exactly the displayed Selmer group. [given, F2, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
