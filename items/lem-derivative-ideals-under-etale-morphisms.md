## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice.

Let $\varphi\colon X'\to X$ be an etale morphism of smooth $K$-schemes ([[def-etale-morphism-schemes]]) and let $\mathcal I\subseteq\mathcal O_X$ be a coherent ideal sheaf ([[def-coherent-module-scheme]]).
Then for every $i\ge0$
$$\varphi^*\bigl(\mathcal D^i(\mathcal I)\bigr)=\mathcal D^i(\varphi^*\mathcal I),$$
where on both sides the derivative ideals are taken over $K$ ([[def-ideal-of-derivatives]]).

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The data in the Statement, with AC assumed through the cited smooth-differentials and étale suppliers.

[A1] [[def-axiom-of-choice]]: AC is used only through the explicitly AC-assuming suppliers [F4] and [F5].

[F1] [[def-ideal-of-derivatives]]: $\mathcal D(\mathcal I)$ is generated locally by local generators $f$ of $\mathcal I$ and all first partial derivatives $\partial f/\partial u_i$ in local coordinates $u_1,\dots,u_n$; equivalently, globally, it is generated by the $f$ and the sections $D(f)$ for all $K$-derivations $D$ of the structure sheaf.

[F2] [[cor-derivations-represented-by-differentials]], [[def-derivation-algebra]]: for a $K$-algebra $A$ and $A$-module $M$, $\operatorname{Hom}_A(\Omega_{A/K},M)\cong\operatorname{Der}_K(A,M)$; for a locally free $\Omega_{A/K}$ this gives $\operatorname{Der}_K(A,M)\cong M\otimes_A\operatorname{Der}_K(A)$.

[F3] [[thm-transitivity-exact-sequence-differentials]]: for $K\to A\to B$ the sequence $B\otimes_A\Omega_{A/K}\to\Omega_{B/K}\to\Omega_{B/A}\to0$ is exact.

[F4] Under AC, [[thm-etale-formally-etale-finite-presentation]] says an étale morphism is formally étale; it is therefore formally unramified, and [[thm-formally-unramified-differentials-zero]] gives $\Omega_{B/A}=0$, or $\Omega_{X'/X}=0$ in sheaf notation.

[F5] Under AC, [[thm-differentials-smooth-locally-free]] identifies the rank of relative differentials with relative dimension. The composition rule in [[thm-smooth-morphisms-stable-base-change-composition]] says $\operatorname{reldim}_{X'/K}(x')=\operatorname{reldim}_{X/K}(x)+\operatorname{reldim}_{X'/X}(x')$; since $\varphi$ is étale, the last term is $0$. Thus $\Omega_{X/K}$, $\Omega_{X'/K}$ and the pullback of the former are locally free of the same rank at corresponding points.

[F6] [[def-locally-free-sheaf-finite-rank]]: a surjection of finite locally free modules of the same rank is an isomorphism; this is checked after localising to free modules and reducing to linear algebra.

[F7] [[def-sheaf-relative-differentials]]: $\Omega_{X/K}$ is the sheaf of relative differentials, with its universal $K$-derivation; the pullback $\varphi^*\Omega_{X/K}$ is the sheaf $\varphi^{-1}\Omega_{X/K}\otimes_{\varphi^{-1}\mathcal O_X}\mathcal O_{X'}$.

## Proof

1.1 Affine-local reduction. The question is local on $X'$ and on $X$: it suffices to prove $\varphi^*\mathcal D(\mathcal I)=\mathcal D(\varphi^*\mathcal I)$ on affine charts $\operatorname{Spec}B\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ of an étale ring map, and then to iterate for higher derivatives. So fix an étale map $A\to B$ and an ideal $I\subseteq A$; write $\varphi$ also for the ring map. [F1]

1.2 The differential comparison. By [F4] one has $\Omega_{B/A}=0$, so the transitivity sequence of [F3] gives a surjection $B\otimes_A\Omega_{A/K}\twoheadrightarrow\Omega_{B/K}$. Both sides are finite locally free by [F5]. Their ranks agree at corresponding points because the relative dimensions of $X'/K$ and $X/K$ agree by [F5]; no identification with the local-ring dimension is needed. By [F6] the comparison map $B\otimes_A\Omega_{A/K}\to\Omega_{B/K}$ is an isomorphism. [F3, F4, F5, F6]

2.1 Derivations under the étale map. Dualising the isomorphism of step 1.2 and using [F2, F7], $\operatorname{Der}_K(B)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_B(\Omega_{B/K},B)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_B(B\otimes_A\Omega_{A/K},B)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_A(\Omega_{A/K},B)\cong B\otimes_A\operatorname{Der}_K(A)$. Concretely, every $K$-derivation $D'$ of $B$ is a finite sum $\sum_ib_iD_i'$ where for each $i$ there is a $K$-derivation $D_i$ of $A$ with $D_i'(\varphi(a))=\varphi(D_i(a))$ for all $a$, and conversely each $D_i$ gives such a $D_i'$. [F2, F7, step 1.2]

3.1 The ideals agree. The ideal $\mathcal D(\varphi^*I)$ is generated by $\varphi^*I$ together with all $D'(g)$ for $g\in\varphi^*I$ and $K$-derivations $D'$ of $B$ ([F1]). Write a generator $g$ of $\varphi^*I=IB$ as $\sum_jb_j\varphi^*f_j$ with $f_j\in I$. For $D'=\sum_ib_iD_i'$ as in step 2.1, the Leibniz rule gives $D'(g)=\sum_{i,j}b_iD_i'(b_j)\varphi^*f_j+\sum_{i,j}b_ib_j\varphi^*(D_i f_j)$, which lies in $\varphi^*\mathcal D(I)$. Conversely $\varphi^*\mathcal D(I)$ is generated by the $\varphi^*f$ and the $\varphi^*(D_if)=D_i'(\varphi^*f)$, which lie in $\mathcal D(\varphi^*I)$. Hence $\varphi^*\mathcal D(\mathcal I)=\mathcal D(\varphi^*\mathcal I)$, and iteration gives $\varphi^*\mathcal D^i(\mathcal I)=\mathcal D^i(\varphi^*\mathcal I)$ for every $i\ge0$. AC [A1] is used only through [F4] and [F5]; the derivation transport and ideal-generation computations are choice-free. [A1, F1, step 2.1] ∎
