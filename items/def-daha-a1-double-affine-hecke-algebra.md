## Definition

Use the rank-one root datum, affine actions, field $K$, and Laurent polynomials $B_{i,m}$ from [[def-daha-a1-root-datum-and-parameter-conventions]] and [[lem-daha-a1-bernstein-quotients-are-laurent]]. Let $F$ be the free associative unital $K$-algebra on $Z,Z^{-1},T_0,T_1,\pi$. Define the equal-parameter double-affine Hecke algebra

$$
\mathcal H_{q,t}(A_1)=F/I
$$

where $I$ is the two-sided ideal generated by the following relations:

1. $ZZ^{-1}=Z^{-1}Z=1$.
2. $\pi^2=1$ and $\pi T_0\pi=T_1$ (hence also $\pi T_1\pi=T_0$).
3. $(T_i-t)(T_i+t^{-1})=0$ for $i=0,1$.
4. For every $m\in\mathbb Z$ and $i\in\{0,1\}$,
$$
T_i Z^m-s_i(Z^m)T_i=(t-t^{-1})B_{i,m}(Z),
$$
where $s_1(Z^m)=Z^{-m}$ and $s_0(Z^m)=q^{-2m}Z^{-m}$.
5. $\pi Z\pi=q^{-1}Z^{-1}$.

This is the rank-one root-datum DAHA presentation: $T_0,T_1$ are the affine Hecke generators, $Z$ is the weight-lattice generator, and $\pi$ is the length-zero symmetry. There is no finite braid relation between $T_0$ and $T_1$ because the affine $A_1$ Coxeter label is infinite. The scalar normalization is $(T_i-t)(T_i+t^{-1})=0$; $q$ controls translations and is independent of $t$. The quotient is a definition, not a claim about a PBW basis or a faithful polynomial representation.

The fundamental translation is $y=\pi s_1$, acting by the $q$-shift $z\mapsto qz$; its DAHA lift is $Y=\pi T_1$. The quadratic relation makes $T_1$ invertible, with $T_1^{-1}=T_1-(t-t^{-1})$.

## Remarks

The existence of the free algebra quotient uses the standard universal property of generators and two-sided relations in [[lem-hh-free-associative-ring-and-relations-descent]]. The local proof of polynomial representation faithfulness is separate.
