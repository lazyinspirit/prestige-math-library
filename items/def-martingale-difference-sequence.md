## Definition

Assume AC [[def-axiom-of-choice]] for conditional-expectation existence. A **martingale difference sequence** relative to $(\mathcal F_n)_{n\ge0}$ is a sequence $(D_n)_{n\ge1}$ of real integrable variables such that $D_n$ is $\mathcal F_n$-measurable and
$$E[D_n\mid\mathcal F_{n-1}]=0\quad\text{a.s. for every }n\ge1.$$
Integrability and adaptedness have the timewise meanings of [[def-adapted-and-integrable-stochastic-process]]. Conditional expectations have the class convention of [[def-conditional-expectation-as-an-ae-class]], which is the exact inherited use of AC here. No representative is selected by this definition. There is no $D_0$, and $\mathcal F_0$ need not be trivial or generated by the differences.
