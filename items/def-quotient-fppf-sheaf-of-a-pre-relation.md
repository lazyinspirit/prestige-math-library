## Definition

Let $S$ be a scheme and let $s,t\colon R\to U$ be morphisms of $S$-schemes
([[def-morphism-of-schemes]]). For an $S$-scheme $T$, let $\sim_T$ be the
equivalence relation on the set $U(T)=\operatorname{Mor}_S(T,U)$ generated by
the pairs $(s(\xi),t(\xi))$ for $\xi\in R(T)$
([[def-equivalence-relation]], [[def-fibre-product-schemes-universal-property]]).
The relations $\sim_T$ are compatible with restriction along $T'\to T$,
because a point $\xi\in R(T)$ restricts to $R(T')$ and $s,t$ are natural, so
$$T\longmapsto U(T)/{\sim_T}$$
is a presheaf of sets, the **naive quotient presheaf** $P_{U/R}$, and the
quotient maps $U(T)\to U(T)/{\sim_T}$ are natural.

The **fppf quotient sheaf** $U/R$ is the sheafification of $P_{U/R}$
([[def-fppf-sheaf-and-sheafification]],
[[lem-fppf-sheafification-exists]]): the initial fppf sheaf receiving
$P_{U/R}$, with its universal property. Its construction uses the Axiom of
Choice ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]), inherited from the sheafification lemma. The
naive quotient presheaf and the quotient sheaf differ in general, and no
representability of $U/R$ by a scheme or algebraic space is asserted.

Two standard special cases are used below. If $S=\operatorname{Spec}k$ is a
field and $G$ is a group scheme of finite type over $k$
([[def-group-scheme-over-a-field]]) acting on a $k$-scheme $U$, one takes
$R=G\times_kU$ with $s$ the second projection $G\times_kU\to U$ and $t$ the
action morphism $G\times_kU\to U$, and writes $U/G$ for the quotient sheaf.
If $U=G$ and $H\subseteq G$ is a closed subgroup scheme
([[def-morphism-and-closed-subgroup-scheme]]) acting by right translation, one
takes $R=G\times_kH$ with $s(g,h)=g$ and $t(g,h)=gh$, and writes $G/H$. In
both cases the quotient sheaf is the fppf sheafification of the corresponding
naive quotient.
