## Statement

Let j:U→X be an open immersion of Noetherian schemes, let ℓ be a prime, and use the fixed small-site tower and normalized constructible derived model of the cited definition. Extension by zero is an exact functor on finite-coefficient étale module sheaves, commutes with arbitrary direct sums, and is compatible with restriction and coefficient tensor products. Applying it levelwise to a normalized constructible tower with bounded cohomology and uniform finite Tor amplitude produces another such tower, with the same bounds and a common finite stratification. It therefore defines an exact Q_l-linear functor on the stated rationalized adic model. No choice principle is assumed. This statement concerns open immersions only and does not construct proper pushforward or compactification independence.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** j, ℓ, the fixed small sites and an input tower K as stated.

[F1] The tower category has kernels, cokernels and exactness computed levelwise; a normalized constructible object has derived reduction isomorphisms, uniform degree and Tor bounds and a common finite stratification. [[def-derived-category-of-etale-sheaves]]

[F2] Rationalization tensors each morphism group with Q_l. [[def-l-adic-sheaf-as-compatible-system]]

[F3] An open immersion identifies U with an open subscheme of X. [[def-open-immersion-schemes]]

[F4] Étale morphisms are universally open without a choice premise. [[lem-etale-morphisms-are-locally-standard-and-universally-open-in-zf]]

## Proof

1.1 For an étale test object V→X, put V_U=V×_X U. A section of j_!F over V is a section s of F over V_U whose support is closed in V and contained in V_U. Here the zero locus is the union of open subsets W⊂V for which s restricts to zero on W×_X U; the support is its closed complement. Étale pullback preserves this zero-locus and support: one direction is functorial, and the converse follows locally from the sheaf condition and the fact that an étale map is open by [F4]. Thus the condition is local for the étale topology and sheaf gluing gives j_!F. Its restriction to U is F. [F3, F4, given, construct]

1.2 The functor j_! is exact. Kernels are computed locally and the support of a kernel section is the same on each side. For a surjection F→G and a section s of j_!G on V, cover V by V_U and the complement of the closed support of s. On the latter, zero lifts s; on V_U the surjection lifts s étale-locally, and every such lifting has support inside that test object because it lies over U. The sheaf condition gives local surjectivity. The construction preserves arbitrary direct sums: on each test object a sum section is locally a finite sum, and finite unions of supports remain closed; conversely such locally finite descriptions glue. [F3, F4, step 1.1, algebra]

2.1 For a sheaf M of A_n=Z/ℓⁿ-modules on X, local calculations on V_U and on the complement of the support give a natural isomorphism (j_!F)⊗_{A_n}M≅j_!(F⊗_{A_n}j*M). To derive it without choosing resolutions, form a canonical flat resolution on the fixed small site: at each stage take the direct sum, over every site object W and every section of the current sheaf on W, of the free module sheaf A_n[h_W] generated by the representable sheaf of W; the universal section map is surjective, and repeat on its kernel. These index sets and maps are specified, so no family of choices is made. The sheaf A_n[h_W] is flat. Indeed its tensor with a sheaf N is the sheafification of finite sums of pairs (u,t), with u a section of W over a test object and t a section of N there, subject to the module relations. On an exact sequence of N, a finite collection of the coefficients t lifts after a finite common étale refinement. Thus tensor by A_n[h_W] preserves local surjections as well as kernels. The same finite-support argument makes arbitrary direct sums of these flat sheaves flat and exact. The resulting functorial resolution is a bounded-above complex of flat sheaves, hence K-flat by finite truncation in each tested degree. Applying the displayed tensor isomorphism to it and using direct-sum totalization gives j_!K⊗^L M≅j_!(K⊗^L j*M). [F1, F3, F4, step 1.2, construct, algebra]

3.1 Apply step 2.1 to the coefficient A_n viewed as an A_{n+1}-module. The transition isomorphism for K gives (j_!K_{n+1})⊗^L_{A_{n+1}}A_n≅j_!(K_{n+1}⊗^L_{A_{n+1}}A_n)≅j_!K_n, coherently with the actual tower maps. Exactness of j_! preserves the common cohomological interval. The same tensor comparison for an arbitrary coefficient-module sheaf preserves the uniform Tor interval. On each input stratum the finite-level cohomology sheaves are unchanged, and on the closed complement X−U they are zero. The input finite stratification together with that complement is a common finite stratification on X. [F1, step 1.2, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 Levelwise exactness preserves quasi-isomorphisms of tower complexes, so j_! descends to the bounded-below derived tower category. Step 3.1 shows that it preserves the normalized constructible subcategory. Its action on Hom groups is Z_l-linear; tensoring Hom with Q_l therefore produces the asserted Q_l-linear functor. A chosen idempotent completion is handled by sending each split idempotent to its image under the functor. [F1, F2, step 3.1, algebra] ∎

## Scope

No proper pushforward, proper base change, or ordinary/adic equivalence is asserted. The flat-generator resolution in step 2.1 is constructed on the site's fixed small presentation and uses finite refinements, with no indexed family of choices.
