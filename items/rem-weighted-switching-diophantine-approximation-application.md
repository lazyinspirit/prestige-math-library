## Remark

Matomäki and Zúñiga-Alterman report a separate application to the
Piatetski–Shapiro prime problem $p=\lfloor n^{1/\gamma}\rfloor$ with
$p+2$ having at most three prime factors counted with multiplicity. They state
that their weighted-switching theorem could improve the previously reported
range $\gamma\in(0.9989445,1)$ to $\gamma\in(0.996651,1)$, and describe the
improvement as modest. This is an application claim in their paper; it is not
a consequence asserting that $p+2$ is prime or has at most two prime factors.
