## Definition

Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space with inner product $B$ ([[def-inner-product-space]]). Equip it with the induced norm $\|x\|_B=\sqrt{B(x,x)}$ ([[def-inner-product-norm]], [[cor-inner-product-induces-a-norm]]) and metric $d_B(x,y)=\|x-y\|_B$ ([[def-norm-and-normed-space]]); $\mathrm{Isom}(E)$ below means the isometry group for $d_B$ ([[def-isometry-and-metric-embedding]]). Let $\Phi\subseteq E$ be a supplied reduced crystallographic root system spanning $E$ ([[def-reduced-crystallographic-euclidean-root-system]]). For each $\alpha\in\Phi$, put $\alpha^\vee=2\alpha/B(\alpha,\alpha)$ ([[def-coroot-and-dual-root-system]]); write $s_\alpha$ for the orthogonal reflection in the hyperplane $B(x,\alpha)=0$ and $W=W(\Phi)=\langle s_\alpha:\alpha\in\Phi\rangle$ for the Weyl group ([[def-weyl-group-of-a-root-system]]). Write $Q=\sum_{\alpha\in\Phi}\mathbb Z\alpha$ and $Q^\vee=\sum_{\alpha\in\Phi}\mathbb Z\alpha^\vee$ for the root and coroot lattices ([[def-root-lattice-coroot-lattice-weight-lattice-and-coweight-lattice]]), and fix a positive system with base $\Delta=\{\alpha_s:s\in S\}$ ([[def-positive-system-and-base-of-simple-roots]]).

For $\alpha\in\Phi$ and $k\in\mathbb Z$, define
$$H_{\alpha,k}:=\{x\in E:B(x,\alpha)=k\},\qquad r_{\alpha,k}(x):=x-\bigl(B(x,\alpha)-k\bigr)\alpha^\vee.$$
Each $H_{\alpha,k}$ is an affine hyperplane: $B(-,\alpha)$ is a nonzero linear functional, and $k\alpha/B(\alpha,\alpha)$ lies in its level-$k$ set. The sets $H_{\alpha,k}$ are the **affine root hyperplanes** or **walls**, and
$$\mathcal A_\Phi:=\{H_{\alpha,k}:\alpha\in\Phi,\ k\in\mathbb Z\}$$
is their **affine wall arrangement**. An **alcove** is a connected component of $E\setminus\bigcup\mathcal A_\Phi$, with the subspace topology ([[def-subspace-topology-top]], [[def-connected-component-and-quasicomponent]]).

The **affine reflection group** $W_a$ is the subgroup of the Euclidean isometry group $\mathrm{Isom}(E)$ generated by the maps $r_{\alpha,k}$ ([[def-generated-subgroup]], [[def-isometry-and-metric-embedding]]). The well-definedness obligation for this subgroup is assigned to `lem-cg-affine-reflection-identities-and-local-finiteness`, which verifies that each generator is an isometry. The **coroot translation group** is the image of the homomorphism $Q^\vee\to\mathrm{Isom}(E)$, $\lambda\mapsto t_\lambda$, where $t_\lambda(x)=x+\lambda$.

The Weyl group acts on $Q^\vee$ by $w\cdot\lambda:=w(\lambda)$. This action is by automorphisms: $W$ permutes $\Phi$, and for every root $\alpha$,
$$w(\alpha^\vee)=\frac{2w\alpha}{B(\alpha,\alpha)}=\frac{2w\alpha}{B(w\alpha,w\alpha)}=(w\alpha)^\vee,$$
so $w(Q^\vee)=Q^\vee$ and the restriction has inverse $w^{-1}|_{Q^\vee}$. These restrictions compose according to multiplication in $W$, giving the action required for the external semidirect product $Q^\vee\rtimes W$ ([[def-group-action]], [[def-action-by-automorphisms]], [[def-external-semidirect-product]]), whose multiplication is
$$(\lambda,w)(\mu,v)=(\lambda+w\mu,wv).$$

This item fixes the definitions and conventions. It does not assert $W_a=Q^\vee\rtimes W$, local finiteness of the arrangement, that alcoves are simplices, or that any specified alcove is a fundamental domain. The root system is supplied; no crystallographic scaling for a Coxeter matrix is constructed, and non-crystallographic types are outside this definition. No choice principle is used.
