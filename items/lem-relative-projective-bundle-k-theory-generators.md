## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]) inherited from the finite
coherent-resolution and cohomological suppliers. Let $Y$ be smooth
quasi-projective over a field and $E$ a rank-$r$ bundle. For
$q:P=\mathbb P_{\mathrm{lines}}(E)\to Y$, $K^0(P)=G_0(P)$ is generated as a
$K^0(Y)$-module by $\mathcal O(a)$, $0\le a<r$. For $r\ge1$ and $a\ge0$,
$q_!\mathcal O(a)=\operatorname{Sym}^a(E^\vee)$. For $r=0$, the projective
bundle is empty, its $K$-groups and all $q_!\mathcal O(a)$ are zero, and the
assertion of generation is vacuous. This statement has the
corresponding dual form for quotient projective bundles.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** the Axiom of Choice; a smooth quasi-projective $k$-scheme $Y$; a rank-$r$ vector bundle $E$ on $Y$; the projective bundle $q:P=\mathbb P_{\mathrm{lines}}(E)=\mathbb P_{\mathrm{quot}}(E^\vee)\to Y$ with tautological line $L=\mathcal O(-1)$, quotient bundle $Q$ of rank $r-1$ and universal sequence $0\to\mathcal O(-1)\to q^*E\to Q\to0$.

[L1] On $P$, smooth quasi-projective over a field, $K^0(P)=K_0(P)$ and every coherent sheaf has a finite locally free resolution ([[lem-k-zero-vector-bundles-versus-coherent-sheaves]]).

[L2] The relative diagonal of $P\times_YP$ is the zero scheme of the section $L_1\to Q_2$ of $L_1^\vee\otimes Q_2$, whose $r-1$ components near the diagonal are a regular sequence of fibre-coordinate differences, with exact Koszul resolution of terms $\operatorname{pr}_1^*\mathcal O(-j)\otimes\operatorname{pr}_2^*\bigwedge^jQ^\vee$; the resolution restricts exactly to fibres and survives base change ([[lem-koszul-resolution-and-flat-fibre-restriction]], [[def-projective-bundle-scheme]]).

[L3] The pushforward $q_!$ of [[def-pushforward-in-algebraic-k-theory]] satisfies the projection formula, flat base change preserves its values, and the Čech complex of the standard projective affine cover computes the relevant cohomology ([[lem-projection-formula-in-algebraic-k-theory]], [[thm-cech-computes-qc-cohomology-separated-scheme-affine-cover]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct; push the diagonal Koszul resolution forward to obtain the twist identity, express the exterior factors through the universal sequence, and read off generation and the pushforward of the twists.

1.1 The diagonal identity. If $r=0$, $P=\varnothing$ by the projective-bundle definition, so every class and pushforward is zero and the assertion is immediate; assume $r\ge1$ from here onward. The section of [L2] meets the stronger regular-section hypothesis: trivialize $E$ over $\operatorname{Spec}A$ and, near a diagonal point, use the same projective chart on both factors with lines generated by $e_0+\sum_{i=1}^{r-1}u_ie_i$ and $e_0+\sum_{i=1}^{r-1}v_ie_i$. Modulo the second line, the first generator has components $u_i-v_i$ in the quotient basis $e_1,\dots,e_{r-1}$ over $A[u_1,\dots,u_{r-1},v_1,\dots,v_{r-1}]$. Successively eliminating $u_i$ makes each next difference monic in a new variable, hence a nonzerodivisor over any base ring; these $r-1$ components form a regular sequence, and the argument survives every base change on $A$. Off the diagonal some component is a unit locally, because the two residue-field lines are distinct. For $r=1$ the sequence is empty and the relative diagonal is the whole product. Thus [L2] supplies the exact Koszul resolution in all cases $r\ge1$. For a vector bundle $H$ on $P$, tensor the Koszul resolution of the diagonal of [L2] with $\operatorname{pr}_1^*H$ and push forward along $\operatorname{pr}_2$: the diagonal term contributes $H$, because $\operatorname{pr}_2$ restricts to the identity on the diagonal, and the $j$-th Koszul term contributes $q^*(q_!(H(-j)))\otimes[\bigwedge^jQ^\vee]$ by the projection formula and flat base change [L3]. Hence $[H]=\sum_{j=0}^{r-1}(-1)^jq^*\bigl(q_!(H(-j))\bigr)\bigl[\bigwedge^jQ^\vee\bigr]$ in $K^0(P)$. [L1, L2, L3, given, algebra]

2.1 Generation. The dual universal sequence $0\to Q^\vee\to q^*E^\vee\to\mathcal O(1)\to0$ gives the exterior-power identity $[\bigwedge^j q^*E^\vee]=[\bigwedge^jQ^\vee]+[\bigwedge^{j-1}Q^\vee][\mathcal O(1)]$, by the two graded pieces of the exterior-power filtration in a local splitting. Solving this recurrence, starting with $\bigwedge^0Q^\vee=\mathcal O$, gives $[\bigwedge^jQ^\vee]=\sum_{a=0}^j(-1)^a\bigl[q^*\bigwedge^{j-a}E^\vee\bigr][\mathcal O(a)]$; substituting into step 1.1 shows that every class $[H]$ of a vector bundle is a finite combination of $q^*K^0(Y)$-classes tensored with $\mathcal O(a)$, $0\le a<r$. By [L1] every coherent sheaf is resolved by vector bundles, so the same classes generate $K_0(P)$; the action of $q^*K^0(Y)$ is the module structure and this proves generation. [L1, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Pushforward of the twists. For $a\ge0$, the sheaf $q_!\mathcal O(a)$ is computed on the standard projective affine cover [L3]: the local projective-space cohomology calculation has only degree-zero cohomology with its homogeneous monomial basis of degree $a$, and changes of trivialization act on this basis by $\operatorname{Sym}^a(E^\vee)$; higher direct images vanish by the same computation and the Čech cover comparison. Hence $q_!\mathcal O(a)=\operatorname{Sym}^a(E^\vee)$ as a vector bundle on $Y$, which is the second assertion; the dual statement for quotient projective bundles follows by dualizing $E$ and using $\mathbb P(E^\vee)$ with the induced universal sequence. [L3, given, algebra] ∎
