## Statement

Let $K/k$ be a finitely generated field extension, let $X/K$ be a geometrically normal projective variety of dimension $n$, and choose a projective $k$-variety $B$ with function field $K$ and a projective flat model $\mathcal X\to B$ whose generic fibre is $X$. Work in the geometric base-$k$ setting. For each open model $\mathcal U\to\mathcal V$ of $X/K$, take rational divisor and Picard groups over projective compactifications and refinements. For a nonempty compactification boundary, fix a nonzero effective Cartier divisor $D$ whose support equals the entire complement of the open model. Complete using the order neighborhoods $-\epsilon D<E<\epsilon D$, modulo the completed principal divisors, and then pass to the direct limit over open models. If a projective model has empty boundary, its ordinary rational model classes enter the system through open refinements; choose a refinement with nonempty boundary before applying these neighborhoods, rather than using strict inequalities with $D=0$. A zero-dimensional projective model has trivial Picard group and needs no boundary completion. This topology is independent of the chosen boundary and compactification. A model class is nef when its degree on every integral curve of the projective $k$-model is nonnegative, not merely on curves contracted to the base; a limit of nef model classes is nef, and an integrable class is a difference of two nef classes. The model intersection pairing extends continuously to a vector-valued intersection product
$$
\widehat{\operatorname{Pic}}(X/k)_{\mathrm{int}}^{\,n+1}\longrightarrow \widehat{\operatorname{Pic}}(K/k)_{\mathrm{int}}.
$$
For each point $P\in X(\overline K)$, restriction to its closure in a finite extension model defines a vector-valued height, normalized by $[K(P):K]$. These constructions are compatible with pullback and with passage to model limits.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A finitely generated extension $K/k$, a geometrically normal projective variety $X/K$, and rational line bundles on projective arithmetic models.

[F1] A finitely generated extension is generated by finitely many elements as a field extension. ([[def-finitely-generated-field-extension]])

[F2] A line bundle is an invertible sheaf. A line bundle on a specified model whose generic restriction is the given line bundle is a model extension; existence after shrinking or refinement is a separate local obligation. ([[def-invertible-sheaf]])

## Remaining local proof obligations

The statement packages the model-limit construction and continuity of Deligne pairings from Carney's §§1–2. The conditional estimate in [[lem-nef-model-pairing-telescoping-continuity]] proves the scalar telescoping bound once a symmetric model pairing, effectivity positivity and uniform boundary bounds are established. It does not construct those geometric inputs or the vector-valued pairing. The local library has no proof of the required model cofinality, completion, nef-cone limit properties, or continuity and compatibility of the Deligne pairing in arbitrary finitely generated dimension. These are significant prerequisites to the vector-valued height definition and remain escalated.
