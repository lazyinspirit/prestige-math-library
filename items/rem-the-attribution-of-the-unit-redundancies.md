The mathematical source used on this page for the unit-constraint redundancies
is EGNO: Proposition 2.2.4 is the pair of formulas proved in
[[thm-the-left-unitor-of-a-tensor-product-is-determined]] and
[[thm-the-right-unitor-of-a-tensor-product-is-determined]], and Corollary 2.2.5
is [[cor-the-two-unitors-agree-on-the-unit-object]].

EGNO's bibliographical notes attribute Proposition 2.2.4 to Kelly. This page
therefore cites EGNO for the mathematics and Kelly only for that historical
attribution. It does not claim to quote Kelly's own wording or numbering.
