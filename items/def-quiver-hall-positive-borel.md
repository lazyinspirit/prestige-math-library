## Definition

Let $Q$ be a finite loopless quiver over $\mathbb F_q$. Write $c_{ij}$ for
the number of arrows $i\to j$, set $\nu=\sqrt q>1$, and define the symmetric
Cartan matrix
$$ a_{ij}=2\delta_{ij}-c_{ij}-c_{ji}. $$
Let $\mathbb Q(\nu)$ be the coefficient field. Define the **extended
positive Borel algebra** $B_Q^+$ to be the $\mathbb Q(\nu)$-algebra generated
by $E_i$ and invertible commuting $K_i^{\pm1}$, with
$$ K_iK_i^{-1}=1, \qquad K_i E_jK_i^{-1}=\nu^{a_{ij}}E_j, $$
and, for $i\ne j$ and $m=1-a_{ij}$,
$$ \sum_{r=0}^{m}(-1)^r\binom{m}{r}_{\nu} E_i^{m-r}E_jE_i^r=0. $$
The Gaussian coefficients use the symmetric convention of
[[def-quantum-integers-factorials-and-divided-powers-at-q-i]]. The formulas
$$ \Delta(E_i)=E_i\otimes K_i^{-1}+1\otimes E_i, \qquad \Delta(K_i)=K_i\otimes K_i, $$
are the coproduct convention intended for this presentation; their
well-definedness on the defining relations is checked using
[[lem-coproduct-preserves-the-positive-and-negative-quantum-serre-ideals]].
The positive half is
the subalgebra generated by the $E_i$ alone; adjoining the $K_i^{\pm1}$ gives
the extended positive Borel.
