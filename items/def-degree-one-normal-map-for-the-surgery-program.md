## Definition

Assume $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$ ([[def-countable-choice]]). Let $X$ be a connected
finite CW complex of dimension $n$ together with a vector bundle $\xi$ over
$X$, and let $M$ be a closed oriented smooth $n$-manifold (possibly disconnected) with
fundamental class $[M]$
([[def-fundamental-class-of-a-compact-oriented-manifold]],
[[def-smooth-manifold]]). A **normal map** $(f,b):M\to X$ with respect to
$(X,\xi)$ consists of a continuous map $f:M\to X$ together with a stable
isomorphism of stable vector bundles
$$b:\nu_M\longrightarrow f^*\xi,$$
in the sense of [[def-stable-normal-bundle-of-a-compact-smooth-manifold]] and
[[def-vector-bundle-map-section-subbundle-and-isomorphism]]: bundles are
identified by adding trivial real summands, and $b$ is represented by an
isomorphism $\nu\oplus\varepsilon^a\cong f^*\xi'\oplus\varepsilon^b$ between
genuine representative bundles $\nu$ of the smooth stable normal bundle
$\nu_M$ and $\xi'$ of $\xi$. The target datum $(X,\xi)$ is part of the normal
structure, not an invariant of $f$.

The normal map is a **degree-one normal map** if the target datum also includes
a class $[X]\in H_n(X;\mathbb Z)$ with $H_n(X;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z$
generated by $[X]$, and
$$f_*[M]=[X]\quad\text{in }H_n(X;\mathbb Z).$$
For a disconnected source, $[M]$ is the sum of its component fundamental classes;
degree one means this total class maps to $[X]$, without asserting that each
component has degree one.

The principal case is a connected closed oriented smooth $n$-manifold $X$ with
fundamental class $[X]$ and $\xi$ representing its stable normal bundle
$\nu_X$. For connected $M$ the condition is the degree-one condition of
[[def-degree-of-a-map-between-oriented-closed-manifolds]], the degree is
computed from fundamental classes and is homotopy invariant
([[prop-manifold-degree-is-functorial-and-detected-by-top-cohomology]]), and a
continuous map $M\to X$ may be replaced by a homotopic smooth map without
changing the degree ([[cor-every-continuous-map-between-smooth-manifolds-is-homotopic-to-a-smooth-map]]). The generality of a finite CW complex $X$, with the class
$[X]$ fixed as part of the target datum, is the setting in which the
below-middle surgery step is formulated when no smooth structure on the target
is assumed.

For the manifold target $\xi=\nu_X$ the bundle datum may equivalently be given
as a stable isomorphism of the tangent bundles,
$TM\oplus\varepsilon^k\cong f^*TX\oplus\varepsilon^k$ for some $k\ge0$: for a
supplied Euclidean embedding of $M$ one has the stable splitting
$TM\oplus\nu_M\cong\varepsilon^N$, and the same identity holds for $X$, so the
two formulations are exchanged by adding $\varepsilon^N$ to both sides. The
stable normal class is intrinsic on a compact manifold and does not depend on
the chosen embedding, by
[[thm-stable-normal-bundle-is-independent-of-the-embedding]].

In the **unoriented setting** the orientations are dropped: degrees and
fundamental classes are taken with $\mathbb F_2$ coefficients, using the
canonical $\mathbb F_2$-orientation of every manifold
([[prop-every-manifold-is-f-two-orientable-and-orientability-is-componentwise]]),
the class $[X]$ generates $H_n(X;\mathbb F_2)\cong\mathbb F_2$, and $b$ remains a
stable isomorphism of real stable normal bundles. Only the homology coefficients
and degree condition change to $\mathbb F_2$; the real bundle datum is not
replaced by a vector bundle over $\mathbb F_2$.

A **normal bordism** between degree-one normal maps $(f_0,b_0):M_0\to X$ and
$(f_1,b_1):M_1\to X$ with respect to the same target datum $(X,[X],\xi)$ is
data consisting of an oriented bordism $(W;M_0,M_1)$ in the sense of
[[def-oriented-smooth-cobordism]] (or an unoriented bordism
[[def-unoriented-smooth-cobordism-of-closed-manifolds]] in the mod-two
setting), a continuous map $F:W\to X$ restricting to $f_0$ and $f_1$ under the
collar identifications of the two faces, and a stable isomorphism
$B:\nu_W\to F^*\xi$ whose restrictions to the faces are the data determining
$b_0$ and $b_1$, under the standard identification of the stable normal bundle
of a face with $\nu_W$ restricted along an inward normal field. The two normal
maps are **normally bordant** when such data exist. This is Lück's bordism
relation of degree-one normal maps, specialised to a common target bundle;
Lück's definition allows the two maps to use bundles that agree with the
bordism bundle only after adding trivial summands, and the transitivity of the
relation is part of the normal bordism calculus rather than reproved here.

The definition records the input of the surgery programme. It neither constructs
the space $G/O$ of normal invariants nor the surgery obstruction group, which
are separate constructions. Countable Choice is inherited from
[[def-stable-normal-bundle-of-a-compact-smooth-manifold]] and is the only choice
used.
