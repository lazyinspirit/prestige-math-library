## Definition

Let $\Phi\subseteq E$ be a reduced crystallographic root system with base
$\Delta=\{\alpha_1,\dots,\alpha_r\}$
([[def-positive-system-and-base-of-simple-roots]],
[[thm-simple-roots-form-a-basis-and-every-root-has-one-sign-of-integral-coordinates]])
and coroots $\alpha^{\vee}=2\alpha/(\alpha,\alpha)\in E$
([[def-coroot-and-dual-root-system]]). The **root lattice**, **coroot
lattice**, **weight lattice**, and **coweight lattice** are
$$Q=\sum_{\alpha\in\Phi}\mathbb Z\alpha=\sum_{i=1}^{r}\mathbb Z\alpha_i,\qquad Q^{\vee}=\sum_{\alpha\in\Phi}\mathbb Z\alpha^{\vee}=\sum_{i=1}^{r}\mathbb Z\alpha_i^{\vee},$$
$$P=\{\lambda\in E:(\lambda,\alpha^{\vee})\in\mathbb Z\text{ for all }\alpha\in\Phi\},\qquad P^{\vee}=\{\mu\in E:(\alpha,\mu)\in\mathbb Z\text{ for all }\alpha\in\Phi\}.$$
All four sets are additive subgroups of $E$, since the integrality conditions
are preserved by addition and negation; they are free abelian groups of rank
$r$, for $Q$ and $Q^{\vee}$ because the simple roots and the simple coroots
are bases of $E$, and for $P$ and $P^{\vee}$ because the simple coroots are
again a basis so the dual lattice is generated by the dual basis. The chain
$$Q\subseteq P,\qquad Q^{\vee}\subseteq P^{\vee}$$
holds because the Cartan integers $\langle\alpha,\beta^{\vee}\rangle=2(\alpha,\beta)/(\beta,\beta)$
are integers for all roots
([[def-reduced-crystallographic-euclidean-root-system]]).
