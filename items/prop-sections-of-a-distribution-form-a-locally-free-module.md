## Statement

Let $\mathcal D$ be a rank-$k$ smooth distribution on $M$.

1. $\Gamma(\mathcal D)$ is a $C^\infty(M)$-submodule of the module of smooth
   vector fields on $M$.
2. If $U \subseteq M$ carries a local frame $X_1,\dots,X_k$ of $\mathcal D$,
   then every $Y \in \Gamma(\mathcal D|_U)$ has a unique expression
   $$Y = \sum_{i=1}^k f_i X_i$$
   with smooth functions $f_i$ on $U$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A rank-$k$ smooth distribution $\mathcal D$ on $M$.

[A1] Fix an open set $U$ on which $\mathcal D$ has a local frame
$X_1,\dots,X_k$.

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 If $Y,Z \in \Gamma(\mathcal D)$ and $f,g \in C^\infty(M)$, then [given]
$(fY+gZ)_p = f(p)Y_p + g(p)Z_p$ lies in the linear subspace $\mathcal D_p$ for
every $p$. Hence $\Gamma(\mathcal D)$ is closed under addition and smooth scalar
multiplication. [given]

1.2 On $U$, the vectors $X_1(q),\dots,X_k(q)$ form a basis of $\mathcal D_q$, [given]
so each $Y_q \in \mathcal D_q$ has unique coefficients $f_i(q)$ with
$Y_q = \sum_i f_i(q)X_i(q)$. Because $Y$ and the frame fields are smooth, those
coefficients are smooth on $U$. [given]

1.3 Therefore the assignment $U\mapsto\Gamma(\mathcal D|_U)$ is locally free [given]
of rank $k$: on every frame domain its section module is freely generated by
that frame. This is a statement about the sheaf of local sections; it does not
assert that the global module $\Gamma(\mathcal D)$ is free over
$C^\infty(M)$. [given] ∎
