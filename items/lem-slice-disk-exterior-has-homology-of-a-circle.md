## Statement

Assume AC. Let $D\subset B^4$ be a smooth proper disk and let $X_D$ be its compact exterior. Then $X_D$ is connected, $H_1(X_D;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z$ generated by the positive meridian, and $H_i(X_D;\mathbb Z)=0$ for every $i\ge2$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** Full AC, a smooth proper disk $D\subset B^4$, its chosen tubular neighborhood $N(D)$, and its exterior $X_D$.

[A1] Full AC is assumed wherever the cover and orientation conventions use the choice axiom. [[def-axiom-of-choice]]

[F1] The tubular neighborhood is $N(D)\cong D^2\times D^2$ and $N(D)\cap X_D\simeq D^2\times S^1\simeq S^1$, whose generator is the positive meridian. [[def-smooth-slice-disk-exterior]]

[F2] Mayer–Vietoris is exact for the collar-thickened decomposition $B^4=N(D)\cup X_D$. [[thm-mayer-vietoris-sequence-in-singular-homology]]

## Proof

1.1 Both $B^4$ and $N(D)$ are contractible, and $N(D)\cap X_D$ is connected and homotopy equivalent to $S^1$ by [F1]. The reduced degree-zero part of Mayer–Vietoris [F2] therefore gives $\widetilde H_0(X_D;\mathbb Z)=0$, so $X_D$ is connected. [F1, F2, given, A1]

2.1 In degree one, the sequence has $H_2(B^4)=0$, then $H_1(N(D)\cap X_D)\cong\mathbb Z$, then $H_1(N(D))\oplus H_1(X_D)$, then $H_1(B^4)=0$. Since $H_1(N(D))=0$, the inclusion of the overlap induces an isomorphism $H_1(N(D)\cap X_D)\to H_1(X_D)$. Its generator is the positive meridian by [F1]. [F1, F2, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 For every $i\ge2$, the adjacent groups $H_i(N(D)\cap X_D)$, $H_i(N(D))$, and $H_i(B^4)$ vanish. Exactness of [F2] then forces $H_i(X_D)=0$. This proves the asserted homology in all degrees. [F1, F2, step 2.1, algebra] ∎
