## Statement

Let $\lambda\in P^+$ and let $B(\lambda)=\mathcal B(\lambda)$ be the crystal of $V(\lambda)=L(\lambda)$ from [[def-kashiwara-lattices-lambda-and-infinity]], which is a crystal basis by [[thm-grand-loop-existence-of-highest-weight-crystal-bases]]. Then $B(\lambda)$ is a highest weight crystal of highest weight $\lambda$ in the sense of [[def-highest-weight-element-and-connected-highest-weight-crystal]]:

(i) $b_\lambda:=v_\lambda+q\mathcal L(\lambda)$ is a highest weight element of weight $\lambda$: $wt(b_\lambda)=\lambda$ and $\tilde e_ib_\lambda=0$ for every $i\in I$;

(ii) $B(\lambda)=F(b_\lambda)$: every $b\in\mathcal B(\lambda)$ is an iterated lowering image $b=\tilde f_{i_1}\cdots\tilde f_{i_r}v_\lambda$;

(iii) $b_\lambda$ is the only highest weight element of $B(\lambda)$: $\mathcal B(\lambda)_\lambda=\{b_\lambda\}$, and for every $\mu\ne\lambda$ and every nonzero homogeneous $u\in\mathcal L(\lambda)_\mu/q\mathcal L(\lambda)_\mu$ there is $i\in I$ with $\tilde e_iu\ne0$.

No choice principle is used.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A dominant integral label $\lambda\in P^+$ ([[def-kac-moody-integral-and-dominant-integral-weights]]), the simple integrable highest weight module $V(\lambda)=L(\lambda)$ with highest vector $v_\lambda$ ([[thm-integrable-quantum-highest-weight-module-exists-and-is-unique]]), its lattice $\mathcal L(\lambda)$ and basis $\mathcal B(\lambda)$ ([[def-kashiwara-lattices-lambda-and-infinity]]), and the residue $b_\lambda=v_\lambda+q\mathcal L(\lambda)$.

[F1] Crystal basis of $V(\lambda)$ ([[thm-grand-loop-existence-of-highest-weight-crystal-bases]], [[def-kashiwara-lattices-lambda-and-infinity]]): $(\mathcal L(\lambda),\mathcal B(\lambda))$ is a crystal basis of $V(\lambda)$; $\mathcal L(\lambda)_\mu$ is a free $A_0$-module for every $\mu$, and since $\mathcal L(\lambda)$ is generated over $A_0$ by the lowering words applied to $v_\lambda$ and no nonempty word has weight $\lambda$, $\mathcal L(\lambda)_\lambda=A_0v_\lambda$ is free of rank $1$ generated by $v_\lambda\ne0$; $\dim_{\mathbb Q(q)}V(\lambda)_\mu=\#\mathcal B(\lambda)_\mu$.

[F2] The lattice and basis of highest weight $\lambda$ ([[def-kashiwara-lattices-lambda-and-infinity]]): $\mathcal L(\lambda)$ is generated over $A_0$ by the lowering words $\tilde f_{i_1}\cdots\tilde f_{i_r}v_\lambda$ and $\mathcal B(\lambda)=\{\tilde f_{i_1}\cdots\tilde f_{i_r}v_\lambda+q\mathcal L(\lambda)\}\setminus\{0\}$; moreover for $\alpha\in Q_+\setminus\{0\}$ one has $\mathcal B(\lambda)_{\lambda-\alpha}=\bigl(\bigcup_{i\in I}\tilde f_i\mathcal B(\lambda)_{\lambda-\alpha+\alpha_i}\bigr)\setminus\{0\}$ and $\tilde f_i\mathcal L(\lambda)\subseteq\mathcal L(\lambda)$.

[F3] Crystal-lattice axioms ([[def-crystal-lattice-and-crystal-basis]]): $\mathcal B(\lambda)=\bigsqcup_\mu\mathcal B(\lambda)_\mu$ is a $\mathbb Q$-basis of $\mathcal L(\lambda)/q\mathcal L(\lambda)$ with $\mathcal B(\lambda)_\mu\subseteq\mathcal L(\lambda)_\mu/q\mathcal L(\lambda)_\mu$, the operators satisfy $\tilde e_i\tilde f_i=\mathrm{id}$ on existing edges and $\tilde f_i\tilde e_i=\mathrm{id}$, in the form $\tilde f_ib=b'\iff b=\tilde e_ib'$.

[F4] Crystal graph conventions ([[def-crystal-graph-epsilon-phi-and-weight]]): $\tilde e_i$ maps a vertex of weight $\mu$ with $\tilde e_ib\ne0$ to a vertex of weight $\mu+\alpha_i$; $\varepsilon_i(b)=\max\{n:\tilde e_i^{\,n}b\ne0\}$ is finite, and $\tilde e_i^{\,n}b\ne0$ exactly for $0\le n\le\varepsilon_i(b)$.

[F5] Structure of $V(\lambda)$ ([[thm-integrable-quantum-highest-weight-module-exists-and-is-unique]], [[def-quantum-verma-module-and-simple-highest-weight-quotient]]): $V(\lambda)$ has label-weight support contained in $\lambda-Q_+$ and finite-dimensional label-weight spaces; it is generated by $v_\lambda$ under $U_q(\mathfrak g)$, and its weight $\lambda$ space is one-dimensional, spanned by the image of $v_\lambda$, because $V(\lambda)$ is the simple graded quotient of the Verma module $M(\lambda)=U_q(\mathfrak g)v_\lambda$ whose highest line $M(\lambda)_\lambda=\mathbb Q(q)v_\lambda$ maps onto it.

[F6] Weight modules and integrability ([[def-quantum-weight-module-and-integrability]]) and the Kashiwara operators ([[def-kashiwara-crystal-operators]]) supply the weight grading and the string structure on which [F2]-[F4] operate.

[F7] The definition of a highest weight element and of $F(b)$ ([[def-highest-weight-element-and-connected-highest-weight-crystal]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 The top residue. By [F1], $\mathcal L(\lambda)_\lambda$ is free of rank one with generator $v_\lambda$, so its residue space $\mathcal L(\lambda)_\lambda/q\mathcal L(\lambda)_\lambda$ is one dimensional and $\mathcal B(\lambda)_\lambda=\{b_\lambda\}$ by [F3]; in particular $wt(b_\lambda)=\lambda$. If $\tilde e_ib_\lambda\ne0$ for some $i$, then by [F4] it is an element of $\mathcal B(\lambda)_{\lambda+\alpha_i}$ and is the residue of a nonzero homogeneous vector of $V(\lambda)$ of weight $\lambda+\alpha_i$; but $\mathrm{wt}(V(\lambda))\subseteq\lambda-Q_+$ by [F5], a contradiction since $\lambda+\alpha_i\notin\lambda-Q_+$. Hence $\tilde e_ib_\lambda=0$ for all $i$, and $b_\lambda$ is a highest weight element of weight $\lambda$, proving (i). [F1, F3, F4, F5]

2.1 Lowering-word generation. Clause (c) of [F2] states $\mathcal B(\lambda)_{\lambda-\alpha}=\bigl(\bigcup_{i\in I}\tilde f_i\mathcal B(\lambda)_{\lambda-\alpha+\alpha_i}\bigr)\setminus\{0\}$ for $\alpha\in Q_+\setminus\{0\}$. Induction on the height $\operatorname{ht}(\alpha)$ proves that every $b\in\mathcal B(\lambda)_{\lambda-\alpha}$ is an iterated lowering image $\tilde f_{i_1}\cdots\tilde f_{i_r}v_\lambda$ of $b_\lambda=v_\lambda+q\mathcal L(\lambda)$: for $\alpha=0$ this is step 1.1, and for $\alpha\ne0$ the formula exhibits $b=\tilde f_ib'$ with $b'\in\mathcal B(\lambda)_{\lambda-\alpha+\alpha_i}$ of strictly smaller height, to which the induction hypothesis applies. Hence $\mathcal B(\lambda)=F(b_\lambda)$, proving (ii). [step 1.1, F2, F3, F6, F7]

3.1 Killing by all $\tilde e_i$. Let $\mu\ne\lambda$ and let $u\in\mathcal L(\lambda)_\mu/q\mathcal L(\lambda)_\mu$ be nonzero with $\tilde e_iu=0$ for every $i$. Since $\mathcal B(\lambda)_\mu$ is a $\mathbb Q$-basis of this space by [F3] and [F1] shows it is finite (it has $\dim_{\mathbb Q(q)}V(\lambda)_\mu$ elements), $u=\sum_{b\in\mathcal B(\lambda)_\mu}a_bb$ is a finite sum with coefficients $a_b\in\mathbb Q$. Fix $i$: the nonzero elements among $\tilde e_ib$, $b\in\mathcal B(\lambda)_\mu$, lie in $\mathcal B(\lambda)_{\mu+\alpha_i}$ and are pairwise distinct by the partial-inverse axiom [F3], hence linearly independent in the $\mathbb Q$-vector space $\mathcal L(\lambda)_{\mu+\alpha_i}/q\mathcal L(\lambda)_{\mu+\alpha_i}$; the relation $0=\tilde e_iu=\sum_{b:\tilde e_ib\ne0}a_b\,\tilde e_ib$ therefore forces $a_b=0$ for every $b$ with $\tilde e_ib\ne0$. This holds for every $i\in I$. Now let $b\in\mathcal B(\lambda)_\mu$; by step 2.1 $b$ is a lowering word of positive length from $b_\lambda$ (since $\mu\ne\lambda$), so $b=\tilde f_ib'$ for the last letter $i$, and then $\tilde e_ib=b'\ne0$ by [F3]; hence every $b$ occurs among the terms with $\tilde e_ib\ne0$ for some $i$, so all $a_b=0$ and $u=0$, a contradiction. This proves (iii). [step 2.1, F1, F3]

4.1 Conclusion. Steps 1.1, 2.1 and 3.1 establish (i), (ii) and (iii), so $B(\lambda)$ has a highest weight element $b_\lambda$ of weight $\lambda$ and $B(\lambda)=F(b_\lambda)$: it is a highest weight crystal of highest weight $\lambda$ in the sense of [F7]. All arguments used finite sums and the fixed basis $\mathcal B(\lambda)$; no choice principle was used. [step 3.1, step 2.1, F7, given] ∎
