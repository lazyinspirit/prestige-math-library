## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $U$ be a Dedekind scheme on which $2$ is invertible, and let $p:X\to U$ be a smooth proper curve with geometrically integral fibres. Let $P_1,P_2:U\to X$ be disjoint sections, let $B=P_1(U)+P_2(U)$ be their relative effective Cartier divisor, and let $\mathcal M$ be an invertible sheaf with an isomorphism $\mathcal M^{\otimes2}\cong\mathcal O_X(B)$. Then there is a finite flat degree-two morphism $q:Y\to X$ whose non-étale locus is exactly $B$ and which is simply ramified along each section; $Y\to U$ is smooth and proper with geometrically integral fibres. On each geometric fibre, if the genus of $X$ is $g$, the genus of $Y$ is $2g$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC and $U,X,p,P_1,P_2,B,\mathcal M$ as in the Statement.

[A1] AC is the stated axiom. [[def-axiom-of-choice]]

[F1] An effective Cartier divisor has local equations $t$ that are nonzerodivisors; its associated invertible sheaf is locally generated by $t^{-1}$, and the rational section $1=t\,t^{-1}$ is global and vanishes simply along the divisor. [[def-effective-cartier-divisor]] [[def-invertible-sheaf-of-cartier-divisor]] [[lem-cartier-divisor-sheaf-invertible]]

[F2] A finite locally free algebra of rank two defines a finite flat degree-two morphism; under AC, the composite of a finite morphism with a proper morphism is proper. [[def-finite-morphism-schemes]] [[lem-composite-finite-proper-morphism-proper]]

[F3] A morphism of finite presentation is etale exactly when it is flat and unramified. [[def-etale-morphism-schemes]] [[thm-etale-equivalent-flat-unramified-fp]]

[F4] For a separable finite morphism of smooth proper curves, $2g(Y)-2=2(2g(X)-2)+\deg R$. Because $2$ is invertible, each simple ramification point has tame different coefficient $e-1=1$. [[thm-riemann-hurwitz-complete]] [[lem-curve-different-local-support-and-index-bound]]

[F5] Under AC, an étale morphism composed with a smooth morphism is smooth. Near a branch section, the local equation $z^2-u(t-a)=0$ has relative differential $-u\,dt$ along its branch section, so its relative Jacobian has a unit minor there and on a neighbourhood after shrinking. [[def-etale-morphism-schemes]] [[thm-smooth-morphisms-stable-base-change-composition]] [[lem-etale-stable-base-change-composition]] [[thm-smooth-local-standard-form]] [[thm-jacobian-criterion-smooth-morphism]]

[F6] For the local quadratic presentation $B=A[z]/(z^2-b)$, relative differentials are $\Omega_{B/A}=B\,dz/(2z\,dz)$. Unramified morphisms have zero relative differentials; hence when $b$ vanishes simply, $z=0$ gives a point where the quadratic cover is not étale. [[def-unramified-morphism-finite-type]] [[thm-etale-equivalent-flat-unramified-fp]]

## Proof
1.1 Let $s\in H^0(X,\mathcal O_X(B))$ be the canonical section of [F1], with zero divisor $B$, and transport it through $\mathcal M^{\otimes2}\cong\mathcal O_X(B)$ to a section of $\mathcal M^{\otimes2}$. Define the $\mathcal O_X$-algebra $\mathcal A=\mathcal O_X\oplus\mathcal M^{-1}$ by making the product of two sections of $\mathcal M^{-1}$ the map $\mathcal M^{-2}\to\mathcal O_X$ given by this section. The relative spectrum $Y=\operatorname{Spec}_X\mathcal A$ is finite locally free of rank two over $X$. [F1, given, construct]

2.1 Away from $B$, the section is a unit in a local trivialization, so the local equation is $z^2=a$ with $a$ a unit; since $2$ is invertible, the cover is étale there by [F3]. At either section of $B$, choose the local étale coordinate $t$ from [F5], so the section is the graph $t=a$ for a base function $a$. The effective Cartier divisor has local equation $t-a$ up to a unit, hence the local quadratic algebra is $z^2=u(t-a)$ with $u$ a unit. In this étale coordinate, the derivation $\partial_t$ lifts uniquely to the chart. The full derivative is $-u-(t-a)\partial_tu$, whose restriction to the branch is $-u$. It is therefore a unit on a neighbourhood of each branch point after shrinking. The relative Jacobian criterion [F5] makes the cover smooth there. This uses the local equation in the étale chart itself; the unit $u$ need not descend to the affine-line chart. Away from the branch the cover is étale over the smooth curve, hence smooth over $U$. At the branch section $z=0$, [F6] gives nonzero relative differentials, so the cover is not étale there. Also $t-a=z^2/u$, so the ramification index is exactly two. Thus the non-étale locus is exactly $B$, with simple ramification, and every fibre is smooth. [A1, F3, F5, F6, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Since $X\to U$ is proper and $Y\to X$ is finite, [F2] makes $Y\to U$ proper. On a geometric fibre, choose a rational generator for $\mathcal M$ with divisor $D$. The isomorphism $\mathcal M^{\otimes2}\cong\mathcal O(P_1+P_2)$ makes the function in the local quadratic equation have divisor $P_1+P_2-2D$; its valuation at $P_1$ is odd, so it is not a square in the function field. The degree-two generic-fibre algebra is therefore a field, so that fibre is connected; it is smooth by step 2.1, hence geometrically integral. [A1, F2, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 The geometric-fibre map has degree two and two simple branch points. Applying [F4] gives $2g(Y)-2=2(2g(X)-2)+2$, hence $g(Y)=2g(X)$. This proves the genus formula and the Statement. [F4, step 2.1, step 3.1, algebra] ∎
