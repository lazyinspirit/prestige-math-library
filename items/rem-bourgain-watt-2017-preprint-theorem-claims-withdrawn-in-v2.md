## Remark

The v2 arXiv record for “Mean square of zeta function, circle problem and divisor problem revisited” identifies the version as withdrawn by Nigel Watt. Its comments state that proof problems in Propositions 2 and 3 and a problem in Proposition 1′ cause Theorems 1–3 to lose theorem status. This remark records the version status only and supplies none of those theorem claims.
