## Statement

Assume $\mathrm{AC}_{\omega,\mathrm{fin}}$. Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be a GIVEN valued algebraic closure with ordinary completion $C$ and a GIVEN compatible primitive cyclotomic system, and let $K\subset F$ be finite. Then every nonzero element of $B_{\mathrm{cris}}$ or $B_{\mathrm{st}}$ spanning a $G_K$-stable $\mathbb Q_p$-line is a unit of that ring. No choice-free parent regularity is asserted.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The weak finite-choice principle, the actual valued closure and cyclotomic system, and the finite field extension $K/\mathbb Q_p$.

[A1] $\mathrm{AC}_{\omega,\mathrm{fin}}$ is countable choice for nonempty finite sets. [[def-choice-for-pairs-and-countable-finite-choice]]

[F1] Under [A1], the normalized semistable comparison embeds both period rings into $B_{\mathrm{dR}}$. [[lem-countable-finite-choice-padic-galois-lifting-and-period-comparison]]

[F2] Under [A1], a stable period line whose character becomes finite on inertia after a cyclotomic twist is generated by a unit. [[lem-finite-choice-potentially-unramified-period-lines-are-units]]

[F3] The de Rham plus ring is a complete DVR with residue $C$ and uniformizer $t$. [[lem-choice-free-de-rham-period-completion-is-a-discrete-valuation-ring]]

[F4] The Galois action satisfies $g(t)=\chi(g)t$. [[lem-choice-free-crystalline-logarithm-and-abstract-semistable-period-ring]]

[F5] Finite embeddings in the given closure are isometric, and the closure is dense in its completion. [[lem-choice-free-finite-ax-sen-approximation]]

[F6] Under [A1], a continuous additive character on all of $G_E$ that is a $C$-valued coboundary vanishes on inertia. [[lem-finite-choice-global-additive-coboundaries-vanish-on-inertia]]

## Proof

1.1 Let $0\ne b$ span a stable $\mathbb Q_p$-line in either period ring, with character $\eta$. By [F1]–[F4], its leading de Rham coefficient $c=t^{-s}b\bmod t\in C^\times$, where $s$ is its $t$-adic valuation, satisfies $g(c)=\psi(g)c$ with $\psi=\eta\chi^{-s}$. Isometry shows $\psi(g)\in\mathbb Z_p^\times$. It is continuous: approximate $c$ by one algebraic element for any given precision; the open subgroup fixing that element moves $c$ by no more than the approximation error. [A1, F1, F3, F4, F5, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 Choose one nonzero $a\in F$ sufficiently close to $c$ that $q=c/a$ lies in $1+p^m\mathcal O_C$ for an integer $m>1/(p-1)$, and put $E=K(a)$. For every $g\in G_E$, $g(q)=\psi(g)q$. Isometry gives $\psi(g)\in1+p^m\mathbb Z_p$, and the convergent logarithm $x=\log q$ satisfies $g(x)-x=\log\psi(g)$. Thus $\lambda=\log\psi|_{G_E}:G_E\to\mathbb Q_p$ is a continuous additive homomorphism and an additive coboundary. [F5, step 1.1, given, construct, algebra]

3.1 By [F6], $\lambda$ vanishes on $I_E$. Since the $p$-adic logarithm is injective on $1+p^m\mathbb Z_p$, $\psi$ is trivial on $I_E$. The subgroup $I_E=I_K\cap G_E$ has finite index in $I_K$, because $G_E$ has finite index in $G_K$; hence $\psi(I_K)$ is finite. Applying [F2] to the cyclotomic twist $\eta\chi^{-s}=\psi$ proves that $b$ is a unit. This is the stated result under finite choice. [F2, F6, step 2.1, algebra] ∎

## Source notes

The global additive coboundary supplier supplies exactly the logarithmic character on $G_E$ arising in step 2.1. Its proof uses the finite Sen congruence and finite Ax–Sen approximation directly, not the full ramification-filtration theorem. The original unqualified regularity theorem remains a separate assertion.
