## Definition

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $X$ be a regular arithmetic variety whose total scheme has pure dimension $d+1$ and whose generic fibre has pure complex dimension $d$. For $0\leq p\leq d+1$, let $Z^p(X)$ be the free abelian group on integral closed subschemes of dimension $d+1-p$; this is the codimension-$p$ convention for this pure-dimensional $X$. Set $Z^p(X)=0$ when $p>d+1$. On each complex manifold $X_\sigma(\mathbb C)$, a current of type $(a,b)$ is a distribution on compactly supported smooth forms of complementary bidegree. Put $d^c=\frac{i}{4\pi}(\bar\partial-\partial)$, so $dd^c=\frac{i}{2\pi}\partial\bar\partial$, and extend these operators to currents by duality. For an integral cycle $Z$, its integration current $\delta_Z$ is defined by integrating test forms over the smooth loci of the complexifications of its components with their cycle multiplicities. A current $g$ of type $(p-1,p-1)$ is real in the arithmetic convention when complex conjugation satisfies $c^*g=(-1)^{p-1}g$; a smooth $(p,p)$-form $\omega$ is real when $c^*\omega=(-1)^p\omega$. For $1\leq p\leq d+1$, $g$ is a Green current for $Z$ if
$$dd^c g+\delta_Z=\omega$$
for a smooth real $(p,p)$-form $\omega$. Let $\widehat Z^p(X)$ be the abelian group of pairs $(Z,g)$ with $g$ a Green current for $Z$. For $1\leq p\leq d+1$, integral $W\subset X$ of dimension $d+2-p$ has codimension $p-1$. For $f\in K(W)^\times$, let $W_K$ be its generic fibre; if $W_K$ is nonempty, it has pure dimension $d+1-p$ in $X_K$. If $W_K=\varnothing$, put $G_f=0$. Otherwise choose a proper birational resolution $\rho:\widetilde W_K\to W_K$ with smooth source and simple-normal-crossings support for $\operatorname{div}(f\circ\rho)$, and let $G_f$ on $X_\infty$ be the pushforward of the locally integrable current $\log|f\circ\rho|^2$ first by $\rho$ and then by the inclusion $W_K\hookrightarrow X_K$, on every complex embedding component. The resolution-independence lemma shows this current does not depend on the chosen resolution. Complex conjugation gives $c^*G_f=(-1)^{p-1}G_f$. For such an integral $W$ and $f$, define
$$\operatorname{div}_W(f)=\sum_{V\subset W,\;\operatorname{codim}_W V=1}\operatorname{ord}_{\mathcal O_{W,\eta_V}}(f)[V],$$
where $\eta_V$ is the generic point of $V$, $f\in K(W)^\times$, and the order is the length-defined order of the one-dimensional Noetherian local domain $\mathcal O_{W,\eta_V}$. Let $\widehat R^p(X)$ be the subgroup generated by $(\operatorname{div}_W f,-G_f)$ and by $(0,\partial u+\bar\partial v)$ for currents $u$ and $v$ of bidegrees $(p-2,p-1)$ and $(p-1,p-2)$, respectively, such that $\partial u+\bar\partial v$ is real in the stated arithmetic convention; negative bidegrees mean the zero group. The codimension-$p$ arithmetic Chow groups are
$$\widehat{\operatorname{CH}}^p(X)=\widehat Z^p(X)/\widehat R^p(X)\quad(1\leq p\leq d+1),\qquad \widehat{\operatorname{CH}}^0(X)=Z^0(X),\qquad \widehat{\operatorname{CH}}^p(X)=0\quad(p>d+1).$$

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The Axiom of Choice, a regular arithmetic variety $X$, its complex manifolds $X_\sigma(\mathbb C)$, the codimension-cycle groups $Z^p(X)$, and the current operator $dd^c$.

[A1] The Axiom of Choice is assumed by the length-defined local order and resolution-independence suppliers used in this construction ([[def-axiom-of-choice]], [[lem-order-function-one-dimensional-local-domain]], [[lem-log-rational-current-independent-of-resolution]]).

[F1] An arithmetic variety has a flat finite-type structural map to $\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal O_K$, with smooth quasi-projective generic fibre; regularity is a separate condition ([[def-arithmetic-variety-over-the-integers]]).

[F2] An integral scheme is nonempty, reduced, and irreducible; its function field is the fraction field of an affine coordinate domain ([[def-integral-scheme]], [[def-total-ring-of-fractions]]).

[F3] The local order of a rational function at a codimension-one point of an integral scheme is the length-defined order in its one-dimensional Noetherian local domain ([[lem-order-function-one-dimensional-local-domain]]).

[F4] The current $G_f$ is defined by resolving the generic fibre and pushing forward $\log|f|^2$; it is locally integrable and independent of the resolution ([[lem-log-rational-current-independent-of-resolution]]).

[F5] For each principal relation generator $(\operatorname{div}_W f,-G_f)$, the Poincaré–Lelong identity gives $dd^cG_f=\delta_{\operatorname{div}_W(f)_\infty}$, so the pair is a Green cycle ([[thm-poincare-lelong-for-arithmetic-cycle-relations]]).

[F6] Resolution of the characteristic-zero generic fibre and principalization of the coherent ideal of the divisor support provide the smooth model with simple-normal-crossings divisor used to define $G_f$; smooth finite-type schemes over fields are locally factorial ([[thm-resolution-of-singularities-in-characteristic-zero]], [[def-smooth-morphism-schemes]], [[def-ag-geometrically-regular-algebra-and-fibre]], [[thm-nonaffine-regular-local-ring-is-ufd]], [[thm-principalization-of-ideals]]).

[F7] For the stated support dimension, a nonempty generic fibre has pure dimension $d+1-p$, making $G_f$ a current of type $(p-1,p-1)$ ([[thm-poincare-lelong-for-arithmetic-cycle-relations]]).

[F8] For every rational function $f$ on an integral quasi-compact Noetherian support $W$, the length-defined divisor sum has finite support ([[lem-principal-cycle-has-finite-support-on-noetherian-integral-scheme]]).

[F9] Every support $W$ specified here is a quasi-compact Noetherian integral scheme: $\mathcal O_K$ is Noetherian, finite-type algebras over it are Noetherian, finite-type morphisms are quasi-compact, and closed subschemes preserve these properties ([[cor-ring-of-integers-is-a-dedekind-domain]], [[cor-finite-type-algebra-over-noetherian-ring-is-noetherian]], [[def-locally-finite-type-and-finite-type-morphism]], [[def-closed-immersion-schemes]]).
