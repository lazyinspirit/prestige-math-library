## Statement

Assume AC, Countable Choice, and DC. Let $K$ be a number field, let $L/K$ be a cyclic extension of prime degree $\ell$, and choose a nontrivial character $\chi:G_K\to\mu_\ell$ with kernel $G_L$. Let $R_\ell=\mathbb Z_\ell[\xi]$ be the ring in the twistable-module definition with $k_0=1$, and put $N=(\mathbb Q_\ell\otimes_{\mathbb Z_\ell}R_\ell)/R_\ell$ with trivial $G_K$-action. At a place $v$ of $K$, impose the local condition $W_v(\chi)=H^1_{\mathrm{ur}}(G_v,N_\chi)$ if $L/K$ is unramified at $v$, and $W_v(\chi)=0$ otherwise. Let $\operatorname{Cl}^*(L)=\operatorname{Hom}(\operatorname{Cl}(L),\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z)$. If $\sigma$ is the Galois generator corresponding to $\xi$, let $\xi$ act on $\operatorname{Cl}(L)$ by $\sigma^{-1}$ and on $\operatorname{Cl}^*(L)$ by the contragredient action. There is a natural injection of $\mathbb Z_\ell[\xi]$-modules
$$\left(\operatorname{Cl}^*(L)/\operatorname{Cl}^*(L)^{\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)}\right)[\ell^\infty]\lhook\joinrel\longrightarrow\operatorname{Sel}(N_\chi).$$
The natural perfect pairing between the class group and its dual gives a generally noncanonical isomorphism of the corresponding quotient modules for $\operatorname{Cl}(L)[\ell^\infty]$ and $\operatorname{Cl}^*(L)[\ell^\infty]$.

## Facts & Assumptions

[F2] Shapiro identifies cohomology of a coinduced discrete module with cohomology over the subgroup $G_L\le G_K$, compatibly with restriction. [[thm-shapiro-lemma-for-group-cohomology]] [[def-axiom-of-choice]]

## Proof

1.1 Put $A=\mathbb Q_\ell/\mathbb Z_\ell$ with trivial action, $G=\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$, and $B=A[G]$. Evaluation of the group-ring generator at $\chi(\sigma)=\xi$ gives a $G_K$-equivariant exact sequence $0\to A\to B\to N_\chi\to0$, where $A\to B$ is the diagonal norm-vector inclusion. Indeed the integral kernel of $\mathbb Z_\ell[G]\to\mathbb Z_\ell[\xi]$ is the free rank-one module generated by $1+\sigma+\cdots+\sigma^{\ell-1}$, and the quotient is $\mathbb Z_\ell$-free, so tensoring by $A$ preserves exactness. [given, construct, algebra]

2.1 By Shapiro, $H^1(G_K,B)=H^1(G_L,A)$, and the map from $H^1(G_K,A)$ is restriction. For these profinite groups the same restriction–coinduction cochain construction applies continuously: the subgroup has finite index, so its coset set is finite and both evaluation and the inverse cochain constructions preserve continuity. Inflation–restriction identifies the image of restriction with $H^1(G_L,A)^G$: its cokernel embeds in $H^2(G,A)=A/\ell A=0$, using the standard cyclic cochain resolution and the divisibility of $A$. Consequently the long exact sequence of step 1.1 induces an injection $H^1(G_L,A)/H^1(G_L,A)^G\hookrightarrow H^1(G_K,N_\chi)$. [F2, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 By the unramified class-field isomorphism, the characters in $H^1(G_L,A)$ unramified at every place are naturally $\operatorname{Cl}^*(L)[\ell^\infty]$. This subgroup is $G$-stable; its intersection with $H^1(G_L,A)^G$ is precisely its fixed subgroup. Step 2.1 therefore gives the required injective map from the displayed class-group quotient to global cohomology. [F1, F3, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 At a finite place $v$ unramified in $L$, the local induced module and its quotient are inertia-trivial. A class unramified at every place of $L$ remains unramified after local Shapiro and passage to the quotient: the restriction to inertia is zero by local Shapiro, since every inertia subgroup of $G_{L_w}$ equals the corresponding subgroup of $G_{K_v}$. Thus the image lies in $W_v(\chi)$. [F2, step 3.1, algebra]

4.2 At a ramified finite place, prime degree makes the local extension totally ramified, so the residue degree is one. An unramified character of $G_{L_w}$ is therefore the restriction of an unramified character of $G_{K_v}$, obtained by assigning the same value to Frobenius. Local Shapiro and the exact sequence in step 1.1 show that its image in $H^1(G_{K_v},N_\chi)$ is zero. At a ramified archimedean place $L_w=\mathbb C$, the local $H^1$ is zero. Hence the image satisfies the specified zero local condition at every ramified place. [F2, step 1.1, step 3.1, algebra]

5.1 Finally, for the finite $\ell$-primary class group $M$ and its dual $M^*$, the pairing $(x,y)\mapsto x((1-\sigma)y)$ induces a perfect pairing between $M^*/(M^*)^G$ and $M/M^G$: the left and right kernels are exactly the fixed subgroups, and the two quotients have the same finite order. Each quotient is annihilated by the norm polynomial, hence is a finite module over the discrete valuation ring $\mathbb Z_\ell[\xi]$. Its finite dual with the contragredient action has the same elementary divisors, since inversion sends the uniformizer $\xi-1$ to a unit multiple of itself. The two quotients are therefore isomorphic as modules, generally without a canonical isomorphism. Steps 4.1 and 4.2 prove that the injection lands in the Selmer group. [F3, step 3.1, step 4.1, step 4.2, algebra] ∎
