## Definition

Assume AC and DC. Let $k$ be a global function field of characteristic $p$ with finite exact constant field, let $K/k$ be an abelian Galois extension, and let $\Sigma$ be a finite nonempty set of places of $k$. For each finite intermediate field $L/k$, let $k_L$ be its full finite constant field, let $C_L/k_L$ be its smooth proper geometrically integral curve model, let $\Sigma_L$ be the places of $L$ above $\Sigma$, and set $U_L=C_L\setminus\Sigma_L$. A place $w$ has normalized valuation $\operatorname{ord}_w:L^\times\to\mathbb Z$. Put
$$\mathcal O_L^\Sigma:=\Gamma(U_L,\mathcal O_{C_L})=\{x\in L:\operatorname{ord}_w(x)\ge0\text{ for every place }w\notin\Sigma_L\};$$
because $\Sigma$ is nonempty, $U_L$ is affine and $U_L=\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal O_L^\Sigma$. Write $\operatorname{Div}(U_L)=\bigoplus_{x\in|U_L|}\mathbb Z[x]$, where $|U_L|$ is the set of closed points of $U_L$. For $f\in L^\times$, its principal divisor on $U_L$ is $\sum_{x\in|U_L|}\operatorname{ord}_x(f)[x]$, a finite sum. Define $\operatorname{Cl}(\mathcal O_L^\Sigma)$ as $\operatorname{Div}(U_L)$ modulo these principal divisors. On the proper curve put $\operatorname{Div}(C_L)=\bigoplus_{x\in|C_L|}\mathbb Z[x]$, $\operatorname{Prin}(C_L)=\{\sum_x\operatorname{ord}_x(f)[x]:f\in L^\times\}$, and $\operatorname{Pic}(C_L)=\operatorname{Div}(C_L)/\operatorname{Prin}(C_L)$. The degree of $\sum_x n_x[x]$ is $\sum_xn_x[\kappa(x):k_L]$; principal divisors have degree zero. Define $\operatorname{Pic}^0(L)=\ker(\deg:\operatorname{Pic}(C_L)\to\mathbb Z)$.

Write $\overline U_L=U_L\times_{k_L}\overline{k_L}$ and let $W_{k_L}\cong\mathbb Z$ be the subgroup of $\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{k_L}/k_L)$ generated by arithmetic Frobenius $a\mapsto a^{|k_L|}$. The Weil–étale site $U_{L,W}$ is Lichtenbaum's site: its objects are finite-type étale schemes over $\overline U_L$; its morphisms are the compatible scheme maps over $U_L$ whose maps to $\overline{k_L}$ differ by an element of $W_{k_L}$; and its coverings are étale-surjective families. For a Weil–étale sheaf $\mathcal F$, $H_W^i(U_L,\mathcal F)$ is the $i$th sheaf-cohomology group on this site. If $\rho(\mathcal F)$ denotes the étale sheaf on $\overline U_L$ obtained by restriction, the site definition gives the spectral sequence
$$H^a\!\left(W_{k_L},H^b_{\mathrm{et}}(\overline U_L,\rho(\mathcal F))\right)\Longrightarrow H_W^{a+b}(U_L,\mathcal F);$$
in particular this cohomology is not defined merely as derived Frobenius invariants on $\mathcal F(\overline U_L)$. The sheaf $\mathbb G_m$ assigns to an étale scheme $V$ the group $\Gamma(V,\mathcal O_V^\times)$ of invertible regular functions. In the notation of this function-field setting, define $H^1(U_{L,W},\mathbb Z_p(1)):=\mathbb Z_p\otimes_{\mathbb Z}H_W^1(U_L,\mathbb G_m)$; this notation is the indicated Weil–étale cohomology group and is not the étale Tate module of $p$-power roots of unity.

The inverse limits $H^1((\mathcal O_K^\Sigma)_{W},\mathbb Z_p(1))$, $\operatorname{Pic}^0(K)_p$, and $\operatorname{Cl}(\mathcal O_K^\Sigma)_p$ are respectively the inverse limits of these finite-level cohomology groups, $\mathbb Z_p\otimes\operatorname{Pic}^0(L)$, and $\mathbb Z_p\otimes\operatorname{Cl}(\mathcal O_L^\Sigma)$ over finite $L\subset K$. The transition maps are corestriction on cohomology and norm on divisor classes. The Galois group $\Gamma=\operatorname{Gal}(K/k)$ acts on these limits through its action on the tower; when $\Gamma\cong\mathbb Z_p^{\mathbb N}\times G$, these are modules over $\mathbb Z_p[\![\Gamma]\!]$.
