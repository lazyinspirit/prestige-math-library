## Statement

Assume AC. For every group extension
$$1\longrightarrow N\longrightarrow G\longrightarrow Q\longrightarrow 1,$$
the maps induced by inclusion, quotient, and transgression form an exact sequence
$$H_2(G;\mathbb Z)\longrightarrow H_2(Q;\mathbb Z) \longrightarrow N/[G,N]\longrightarrow H_1(G;\mathbb Z) \longrightarrow H_1(Q;\mathbb Z)\longrightarrow 0.$$
Here $[G,N]$ is the normal subgroup generated by the commutators
$gng^{-1}n^{-1}$ with $g\in G$ and $n\in N$. The sequence is natural under homomorphisms of short exact sequences, including its transgression.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC and the group extension.

[A1] Full AC, written AC, is [[def-axiom-of-choice]].

[L1] AC implies DC by choosing one successor at each stage of a serial relation. [[def-dependent-choice]]

[F1] The extension has a strongly convergent homological spectral sequence with $E^2_{p,q}=H_p(Q;H_q(N;\mathbb Z))$ and abutment $H_{p+q}(G;\mathbb Z)$. [[thm-homological-lhs-spectral-sequence-for-group-extensions]]

[F2] Every first-quadrant homological spectral sequence with finite abutment filtration has the exact five-term sequence $H_2\to E^2_{2,0}\to E^2_{0,1}\to H_1\to E^2_{1,0}\to0$. [[thm-five-term-exact-sequence-of-a-first-quadrant-homological-spectral-sequence]]

[F3] Under DC and the supplied projective-resolution convention, the bar model computes group homology; in degree one, $H_1(N;\mathbb Z)$ is generated by symbols $[n]$ with relations $[n_1n_2]=[n_1]+[n_2]$. [[lem-diagonal-bar-coinvariants-compute-group-homology]]

[F4] The homological Serre spectral sequence, including its differentials, edge maps, and abutment filtration, is natural for maps of fibrations. [[thm-homological-serre-spectral-sequence]]

[F5] Maps between connected CW complexes commute up to based homotopy with their classifying maps and the maps induced on fundamental groups. [[lem-classifying-maps-are-natural-under-group-homomorphisms]]

## Proof

**Given:** The extension and [A1].

1.1 Supply the resolution convention needed by [F3] as follows. For each relevant group $K$ and left $\mathbb ZK$-module $M$, take the free module on the underlying set of $M$, augment $[m]\mapsto m$, and repeat on each successive kernel. Each augmentation is onto its kernel target, so the resulting complex is an exact free, hence projective, resolution, specified uniformly in $M$. By [A1] and [L1], DC also holds. Apply [F2] to [F1]. Since $H_0(N;\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z$ with trivial $Q$-action, its terms are $E^2_{2,0}=H_2(Q;\mathbb Z)$, $E^2_{0,1}=H_0(Q;H_1(N;\mathbb Z))$, $H_1=H_1(G;\mathbb Z)$, and $E^2_{1,0}=H_1(Q;\mathbb Z)$. This gives an exact sequence with the coinvariant group in the middle. [A1, L1, F1, F2, F3, construct]

1.2 By [F3], $H_1(N;\mathbb Z)=N/[N,N]$ under the map sending $n$ to its class. The $Q$-coinvariants impose the relations $[gng^{-1}]=[n]$ for every lift $g\in G$ and $n\in N$. Their subgroup in $N/[N,N]$ is exactly $[G,N]/[N,N]$, because $[N,N]\subseteq[G,N]$. Therefore $H_0(Q;H_1(N;\mathbb Z))\cong N/[G,N]$. [A1, F1, F3, algebra]

2.1 Substituting the identification of step 1.2 into step 1.1 gives the displayed sequence. To check naturality, take a homomorphism of extensions. The bar classifying-space maps give a commuting square from $BG\to BQ$ to $BG^{\prime}\to BQ^{\prime}$; mapping-path replacement gives a map of their fibrations. Its fiber map induces the specified homomorphism $N\to N^{\prime}$ on fundamental groups. Under the fiber identifications with $BN,BN^{\prime}$ in [F1], [F5] identifies its homology map with that induced by $N\to N^{\prime}$. By [F4], the map of spectral sequences commutes with $d^2$, all edge maps, and the abutment filtration. The five-term splice in [F2] uses only these maps and the natural subquotient identifications, so its squares commute. Finally, step 1.2 sends each $n$ to its class in the coinvariants, hence commutes with the homomorphism on $N$ and its conjugation action. This proves naturality of the displayed sequence, including the transgression. [F1, F2, F4, F5, step 1.1, step 1.2, algebra] ∎
