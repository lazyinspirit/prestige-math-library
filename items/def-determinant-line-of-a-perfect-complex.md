## Definition

Let $R$ be a commutative integral domain with fraction field $K$, and let $P^\bullet$ be a bounded cochain complex of finitely generated projective $R$-modules representing a perfect object. By constant rank of its terms, write $r_i=\dim_K(P^i\otimes_RK)$ and set $\det_R(P^\bullet)=\bigotimes_i(\bigwedge_R^{r_i}P^i)^{(-1)^i}$, where exponent $-1$ denotes the dual module $\operatorname{Hom}_R(-,R)$, $\bigwedge_R^0P=R$, and for $r\ge1$, $\bigwedge_R^rP=P^{\otimes r}/W_r$ with $W_r$ generated by pure tensors having repeated entries. This is an invertible $R$-module.

For finite projectives $U,W$ of constant ranks $r,s$ and a split short exact sequence $0\to U\to V\to W\to0$, the determinant isomorphism $\bigwedge_R^rU\otimes_R\bigwedge_R^sW\xrightarrow{\sim}\bigwedge_R^{r+s}V$ sends $u_1\wedge\cdots\wedge u_r\otimes\bar v_1\wedge\cdots\wedge\bar v_s$ to $u_1\wedge\cdots\wedge u_r\wedge v_1\wedge\cdots\wedge v_s$, where $v_j$ are any lifts of $\bar v_j$. Changing a lift by an element of $U$ contributes a wedge of $r+1$ elements of the rank-$r$ module $U$, which is zero; localizing at each prime makes $U,V,W$ free and shows that this map is an isomorphism. Determinants of direct sums use this isomorphism for the split sequence.

If $K\otimes_RP^\bullet$ is acyclic, its cycle modules over the field $K$ are finite-dimensional vector spaces and the split cycle sequences give the canonical acyclicity trivialization $\tau_{P}:K\otimes_R\det_R(P^\bullet)\xrightarrow{\sim}K$ by cancelling adjacent cycle determinants. Put $I_P=\tau_P(1\otimes\det_R(P^\bullet))\subset K$ and define the **relative determinant ideal of the representative** to be $I_P^{-1}=\{a\in K:aI_P\subseteq R\}$. This definition is for a chosen complex; independence from the choice of representative is a separate theorem.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A commutative domain $R$, its fraction field $K$, and a perfect object $X$ over $R$.

[F1] [[def-zero-divisor-and-integral-domain]]: $R$ is a commutative ring with $1\ne0$ and no zero divisors.

[F2] [[def-field-of-fractions]]: $K=\operatorname{Frac}(R)$ is the localization of $R$ at its nonzero elements.

[F3] [[def-perfect-complex-over-a-ring]] and [[def-bounded-bounded-below-and-bounded-above-complex]]: a perfect object has a bounded cochain representative by finitely generated projective modules.

[F4] [[def-projective-module]]: a finitely generated projective module is a direct summand of a finite free module and hence is finitely presented.

[F5] [[lem-finite-projective-modules-over-a-domain-have-constant-rank]]: every finitely generated projective term over $R$ has constant finite rank equal to its dimension after tensoring with $K$.

[F6] [[lem-bounded-acyclic-projective-complex-is-split-contractible]]: every bounded acyclic complex of projective modules is split exact, giving the cycle-sequence determinant cancellations.
