## Definition

For a number field $F$ and $m\ge2$, define Zagier's higher Bloch group by

$$\mathcal B_m(F):=\mathcal A_m(F)/\mathcal C_m(F),$$

using the recursively normalized prequotient in the preceding definition. Its polylogarithmic regulator is the map

$$\rho_{m,F}:\mathcal B_m(F)\longrightarrow\mathbb R^{e_m},\qquad e_m=\begin{cases}r_2,&m\text{ even},\\r_1+r_2,&m\text{ odd},\end{cases}$$

induced by $\sum_i n_i[x_i]\mapsto(\sum_i n_i\mathcal L_m(\sigma_j(x_i)))_j$, with the parity-selected embeddings specified there. The Borel regulator on $K_{2m-1}(F)$ is obtained from $r_m^B$ by the same embedding construction; Borel's rank theorem gives both real targets dimension $e_m$. This definition asserts no canonical map between $\mathcal B_m(F)$ and $K_{2m-1}(F)$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A number field $F$ and an integer $m\ge2$.

[F1] [[def-zagier-higher-bloch-prequotient-and-polylogarithm]]: $\mathcal A_m(F)$ is the kernel of the rationalized tensor-symbol map, and $\mathcal C_m(F)$ is the subgroup of that kernel generated by projective specialization differences.

[F2] [[thm-zagier-polylog-regulator-well-defined]]: the branchwise polylogarithmic expression factors through that quotient.

[F3] [[def-borel-regulator-of-a-number-field]]: $r_m^B$ induces the Borel regulator on $K_{2m-1}(F)$ and its source-normalized real target.

[F4] [[thm-borel-ranks-of-higher-k-groups-of-number-fields]]: the rank of $K_{2m-1}(F)$ for $m\ge2$ equals $e_m$.

## Proof

1.1 The quotient in the Definition is a group because [F1] places $\mathcal C_m(F)$ inside $\mathcal A_m(F)$. By [F2], the linear expression on $\mathcal A_m(F)$ vanishes on the subgroup being divided out, so the quotient universal property supplies a unique induced map $\rho_{m,F}$. [F1, F2, given, construct]

2.1 The rank formula for $K_{2m-1}(F)$ is the Borel formula with index $m-1$ in the preceding regulator definition, and [F4] identifies that rank with $e_m$. Thus the displayed regulator target has the asserted dimension. [F3, F4, given, algebra] ∎
