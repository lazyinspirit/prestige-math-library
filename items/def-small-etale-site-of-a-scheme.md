## Definition

Let $X$ be a scheme, and fix a universe containing $X$ and the schemes under consideration. The **small étale category** of $X$ has objects the étale morphisms $U\to X$ and arrows the morphisms of $X$-schemes. A declared covering family of an object $U\to X$ is a set-indexed family of étale morphisms $U_i\to U$ whose images have union $U$. Fiber products are taken over the indicated target, and the empty family covers the empty scheme. These families form a **Grothendieck pretopology** when identity families cover, pullbacks of covering families along arrows cover, and composites of covering families cover. A **sieve** on $U$ is a collection of arrows with target $U$ closed under precomposition; the sieve generated by a family consists of the arrows that factor through one of its members. The **Grothendieck topology generated by the pretopology** is the smallest assignment of covering sieves to objects that contains every maximal sieve and the sieves generated by the declared covering families, and is closed under pullback and local transitivity. The Grothendieck-site lemma verifies the pretopology axioms; the resulting site is denoted $X_{\acute e t}$.
