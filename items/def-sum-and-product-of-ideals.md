## Definition

The sum $I+J$ and product $IJ$ of two-sided ideals.

For two-sided ideals $I,J\mathrel{\trianglelefteq}R$, set

$$I+J:=\{i+j:i\in I,\ j\in J\},\qquad IJ:=\left\{\sum_{k=1}^m i_kj_k:m\ge0,\ i_k\in I,\ j_k\in J\right\}.$$

The empty sum in the definition of $IJ$ is $0$.
