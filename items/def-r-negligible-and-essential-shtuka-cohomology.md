## Definition

Let $X/\mathbb F_q$ be a smooth projective geometrically connected curve, let $V_1,V_2\subseteq X$ be nonempty open subschemes, let $q_1,q_2:V_1\times V_2\to V_1,V_2$ be the projections, and let $r\ge2$. An irreducible lisse $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$-sheaf $\mathcal V$ on $V_1\times V_2$ is **$r$-negligible** if it is isomorphic to a simple direct factor of $q_1^*\mathcal E_1\otimes q_2^*\mathcal E_2$ for irreducible lisse sheaves $\mathcal E_1,\mathcal E_2$ on $V_1,V_2$ with ranks strictly less than $r$. A finite-length lisse sheaf is $r$-negligible when every composition factor is $r$-negligible.

Whenever the compactly supported cohomology of a finite-type shtuka moduli stack with legs over $V_1\times V_2$ is defined as a finite-length $\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell$-representation, let $H_c^\nu$ denote its degree-$\nu$ representation, viewed as its corresponding generic lisse sheaf on $V_1\times V_2$. A representation has **finite length** when it has a finite composition series; its **composition factors** are the irreducible successive quotients. The **Grothendieck group** of this finite-length category is generated by isomorphism classes $[M]$, with the relation $[M]=[M']+[M'']$ for every short exact sequence $0\to M'\to M\to M''\to0$. In this group, the **essential compactly supported cohomology class** is the sum of the classes of composition factors that are not $r$-negligible; its negligible class is the sum of the remaining factors. This is a definition of selected classes and asserts neither an actual direct-sum splitting nor the finiteness or existence of the cohomology representations.
