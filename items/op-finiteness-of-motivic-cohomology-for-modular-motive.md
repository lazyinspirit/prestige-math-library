## Open problem

For the central twist $M=M(f)(k/2)$ of every normalized newform in the stated scope and every number field $K$, prove that $H^1_{\mathrm{mot}}(K,M)$ is finite-dimensional over the coefficient field $F$. In particular, prove the finiteness needed to define the algebraic rank $r_{\mathrm{alg}}(M/K)=\dim_F H^1_{\mathrm{mot}}(K,M)$.
