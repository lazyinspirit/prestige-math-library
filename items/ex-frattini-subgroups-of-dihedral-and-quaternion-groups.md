## Example

Let $D:=\operatorname{Dih}(C_4)=D_4$ in the convention of [[cor-dihedral-groups-as-semidirect-products]], so $D$ is the dihedral group of order eight, and let $Q_8$ be the quaternion group. Then

$$\Phi(D)=\langle r^2\rangle,\qquad \Phi(Q_8)=\{1,-1\}.$$

For the dihedral group of order eight and $Q_8$, the Frattini subgroup has order two and the Frattini quotient is $(\mathbb Z/2)^2$. Both groups have generator rank two.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The normal forms $r^i$ and $r^is$ in $D=\operatorname{Dih}(C_4)$, and the multiplication table of $Q_8$ ([[def-generalized-dihedral-group]], [[def-quaternion-group-of-order-eight]]).

[L1] For every finite $2$-group $P$, $\Phi(P)=P^2$ ([[cor-frattini-subgroup-of-a-finite-two-group-is-the-square-subgroup]]).

[L2] In $D=\operatorname{Dih}(C_4)$, $r^4=s^2=1$, $srs^{-1}=r^{-1}$, and every element is $r^i$ or $r^is$; in $Q_8$, the elements $\pm i,\pm j,\pm k$ have order four and $-1$ is the unique element of order two ([[cor-dihedral-groups-as-semidirect-products]], [[prop-the-quaternion-group-has-order-eight-and-a-unique-element-of-order-two]]).

[F1] The commutator subgroup is generated by $[g,h]=ghg^{-1}h^{-1}$ ([[def-commutator-and-commutator-subgroup]]).

[F2] The generator rank is the common size of a basis of the Frattini quotient ([[def-generator-rank-of-a-finite-p-group]]).

## Verification

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 In $D$, $(r^i)^2=r^{2i}$ and $(r^is)^2=1$, so $D^2=\langle r^2\rangle$. In $Q_8$, the squares are $1$ and $-1$, with every noncentral element squaring to $-1$, so $Q_8^2=\{1,-1\}$. The commutators of [F1] give the same two subgroups: $[r,s]=r(sr^{-1}s^{-1})=r\cdot r=r^2$ and every commutator of $D$ is a power of $r^2$, so $D'=\langle r^2\rangle$; and $[i,j]=iji^{-1}j^{-1}=k(-i)(-j)=kij=k^2=-1$, so $Q_8'=\{1,-1\}$. [given, L2, F1, algebra]

2.1 Apply [L1] to step 1.1. Each quotient has order four and exponent two, with the classes of $r,s$ and of $i,j$ respectively as two-vector bases. Thus both quotients are $(\mathbb Z/2)^2$, and [F2] gives generator rank two. [step 1.1, L1, L2, F2, algebra] ∎
