## Example

Assume AC. For $\Delta=\operatorname{PSL}_2(\mathbb Z)$ and trivial character, the unit-width Eisenstein series generated by $y^s$ has $\operatorname{Res}_{s=1}E(z,s)=3/\pi$. This residual vector spans the zero-eigenvalue space of the full energy Laplacian and is orthogonal to every cusp eigenfunction.
