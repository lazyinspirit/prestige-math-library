## Definition

Let $G$ act on an abelian group $A$. For $a\in A$, the map

$$d a:G\to A,\qquad (d a)(g)=g\cdot a-a,$$

is the **principal crossed homomorphism** determined by $a$.

The set of all such maps is denoted $B^1(G,A)$.
