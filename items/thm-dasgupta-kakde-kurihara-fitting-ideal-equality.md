## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice, Countable Choice, and Dependent Choice. Let $H/F$ be a finite abelian extension of number fields such that $F$ is totally real and $H$ is a totally imaginary quadratic extension of $F$. Let $G=\operatorname{Gal}(H/F)$ and let $j\in G$ be complex conjugation. Let $S_\infty$ be the infinite places of $F$, let $S_{\mathrm{ram}}$ be the finite primes of $F$ ramified in $H$, and set $S=S_\infty\cup S_{\mathrm{ram}}$. Let $T$ be a finite set of finite primes disjoint from $S$ such that the only root of unity in $H$ congruent to $1$ at every prime above $T$ is $1$. With $\operatorname{Cl}_T(H)$ the group of fractional ideals prime to the product of the primes above $T$ modulo principal ideals generated by elements congruent to $1$ at every prime above $T$, its contragredient Pontryagin dual minus part after inverting $2$, and the minus fractional Sinnott–Kurihara ideal defined from the base Stickelberger element and ramified factors, the zeroth Fitting ideal over $\mathbb Z[G]^-:=\mathbb Z[1/2][G]/(1+j)$ satisfies

$$\operatorname{Fitt}_{\mathbb Z[G]^-}\!\left(\operatorname{Cl}_T(H)^{\vee,-}\right)=\operatorname{SKu}_T(H/F)^-.$$
