## Statement

Work in ZF over any infinite field $k$. Let $X/k$ be a specified projective scheme and $H$ very ample. A line bundle $L$ is called numerically nef here exactly when $\deg_C(L|_C)\ge0$ for every integral projective curve $C\subseteq X$. If $L$ is numerically nef, then
$$L^q\otimes H^p\quad\text{is ample for every pair of integers }p,q>0.$$
Thus the ample approximation uses the original curve-degree condition and does not assume that $L$ has a generated positive power.

For every coherent $G$ with support dimension at most $r$ and every finite numerically nef tuple $L_1,\ldots,L_r$,
$$J_G(L_1,\ldots,L_r)\ge0.$$
In particular all mixed numerical intersections of arbitrary nef bundles on an integral projective variety are nonnegative, in every dimension. The assertions include nonreduced or embedded support via generic component lengths, and extend to rational bundle classes by clearing positive integer denominators. No Neron–Severi finite-generation, real cone theorem, imported ampleness criterion or unproved field-extension assertion is used.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The infinite field, specified projective scheme, very ample bundle, coherent sheaf/dimension bound and the actual curve-degree inequalities of the line bundles in the Statement.

[F1] On projective schemes over an infinite field, positivity of a line bundle's top Euler intersection on every positive-dimensional integral closed subvariety is equivalent to actual affine-section ampleness. [[thm-projective-nakai-moishezon-criterion-over-an-infinite-field-in-zf]]

[F2] In the same projective field scope, ample bundles have positive very ample powers, restrictions remain ample, and coherent twists by an ample bundle are eventually globally generated. A tensor product of a very ample and a generated bundle is very ample. [[lem-projective-field-ampleness-and-finite-map-criteria-in-zf]]

[F3] One arbitrary curve-nef entry with the remaining entries generated, or having generated positive powers, has nonnegative mixed Euler intersection over the original infinite field. [[lem-nef-and-generated-mixed-intersection-positivity-in-zf]]

[F4] Top mixed Euler intersections are symmetric and tensor multilinear and are nonnegative on generated entries. They vanish above coherent support dimension. [[lem-projective-finite-difference-intersections-and-generated-positivity-in-zf]]

[F5] Top coherent mixed differences decompose into their reduced top components with nonnegative generic lengths. [[lem-mixed-euler-component-and-birational-projection-formulas-in-zf]]

## Proof

1.1 The empty and zero-dimensional cases follow directly from [F1]–[F2], so suppose $X$ has positive dimension and fix a finite integer $d\ge\dim X$, $d\ge1$. For rational $t=p/q>0$ use $M_t=L+tH$ as notation for $q^{-1}(L^q\otimes H^p)$; its intersections are defined by finite rational scaling of [F4], and it is ample when this clearing of denominators is ample. This is independent of the fraction presentation because positive powers preserve and detect ampleness by [F2]. There is some positive integer $t$ with $M_t$ ample: large twists make $L\otimes H^{t-1}$ generated by [F2], and tensoring with $H$ makes $L\otimes H^t$ very ample. We will decrease this $t$ by one prescribed rational factor, keeping it ample. [F1, F2, F4, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 Suppose $M_t$ is ample and let $V\subseteq X$ be integral of dimension $a\ge1$, so $a\le d$. Write
$$I_j=J_{O_V}(H^{[j]},M_t^{[a-j]})\qquad(0\le j\le a),$$
where brackets count entries, not tensor powers. All $I_j$ are nonnegative: restrict to $V$, clear denominators, take positive generated powers of the ample entries using [F2], and apply [F4]. In fact $I_0>0$ by [F1]. For $j\ge1$,
$$I_{j-1}-tI_j=J_{O_V}(L,H^{[j-1]},M_t^{[a-j]})\ge0$$
by [F3], since $L|_V$ is still curve-nef and all the other entries have generated positive powers. Thus $0\le I_j\le t^{-j}I_0$ by finite induction. No numerical-nef positivity with two unknown entries was assumed in obtaining these inequalities. [F1, F2, F3, F4, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Set $\delta=t/(3d)$. Expanding the self-intersection polynomial by [F4] and bounding every term of degree at least one in absolute value with step 2.1 gives
$$J_{O_V}((M_t-\delta H)^{[a]})\ge I_0\left(1-\sum_{j=1}^a\binom aj(\delta/t)^j\right)>\tfrac12 I_0>0.$$
Indeed $\binom aj\le d^j$, and the finite sum is at most $\sum_{j=1}^a3^{-j}<1/2$. The same fixed $d$ works for every integral $V$. After clearing a common positive denominator, [F1] makes $M_t-\delta H=M_{\rho t}$ ample, where $\rho=1-1/(3d)\in(0,1)$. This is a uniform rational step proved from finite intersection bounds, not a real-divisor ampleness criterion. [F1, F4, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 Given any positive rational $\epsilon$, choose a finite integer $b$ with $\rho^b t<\epsilon$. Such an integer follows directly from the Archimedean property, for $\rho^{-b}\ge1+b(\rho^{-1}-1)$. Apply step 3.1 only this finite number of times. The resulting $M_{\rho^b t}$ is ample. The positive rational multiple $(\epsilon-\rho^b t)H$ is ample too. Their sum is ample: clear denominators, take a common positive power for which one summand is very ample and the other generated by [F2], and use its tensor/Segre criterion. Therefore $M_\epsilon$ is ample. Taking $\epsilon=p/q$ proves that $L^q\otimes H^p$ is ample. No choice of infinitely many models, divisors or points was needed. [F2, step 1.1, step 3.1, algebra]

5.1 First let $X=V$ be integral of dimension $r$ and let $L_1,\ldots,L_r$ be numerically nef. For each positive integer $m$, step 4.1 makes every $L_i^m\otimes H$ ample. Their mixed intersection is nonnegative by [F2] and [F4], after taking a common positive generated power. Divide it by $m^r$ and expand [F4]; the result is the finite polynomial $J_{O_V}(L_1+H/m,\ldots,L_r+H/m)$. Its constant term is the desired $J_{O_V}(L_1,\ldots,L_r)$ and the other finitely many terms tend to zero as $m$ grows. Nonnegativity for every $m$ forces that constant term nonnegative; equivalently a negative constant would make the polynomial negative for all sufficiently small positive $1/m$ by an explicit sum-of-coefficients bound. For arbitrary coherent $G$, apply [F5] to its finitely many $r$-dimensional integral components. Each restricted tuple is curve-nef on that component and has just proved nonnegative mixed value. Nonnegative generic lengths give the full result; lower-dimensional support contributes zero by [F4]. The empty tuple $r=0$ is the finite nonnegative Euler length. Finally clear finite positive denominators for rational bundle classes. [F2, F4, F5, step 4.1, algebra] ∎
