## Definition

Assume the Axiom of Choice as used by [[def-standard-normal-and-normal-laws]]. For integers $d,n\ge1$, a noise scale $\sigma\ge0$, and a parameter $\theta\in\mathbb R^d$, the Gaussian sequence observation is
$$Y_j=\theta_j+\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt n}Z_j,\qquad 1\le j\le d,$$
where $Z_1,\ldots,Z_d$ are independent random variables with law $N(0,1)$. The parameter is $s$-sparse when $0\le s\le d$ is an integer and at most $s$ coordinates of $\theta$ are nonzero. This is an observation model; it does not itself assert a risk bound or an optimal estimator.

[F1] [[def-standard-normal-and-normal-laws]]: under AC, $N(0,1)$ is the probability law with density $(2\pi)^{-1/2}e^{-x^2/2}$.
[F2] [[def-independent-random-elements]]: independence means independence of the sigma-algebras generated by the random variables; no coordinate independence is inferred unless stated.
