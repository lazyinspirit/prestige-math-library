## Definition

Let $X$ be a locally Noetherian scheme, let $Y,Z\subseteq X$ be closed subschemes,
and let $p\in Y\cap Z$ be a closed point. Assume that $Y$ is integral of dimension
one ([[def-integral-scheme]], [[def-dimension-noetherian-topological-space]]) and
that the generic point of $Y$ is not contained in $Z$; since $Z$ is closed, this
means $Y\not\subseteq Z$, and it excludes every one-dimensional component of
$Y\cap Z$. The **local intersection multiplicity of $Y$ and $Z$ at $p$** is

$$m_p(Y\cap Z):=\operatorname{length}_{\mathcal O_{X,p}}\bigl(\mathcal O_{Y\cap Z,p}\bigr),$$

where $\mathcal O_{X,p}$ is the local ring of $X$ at $p$ ([[def-local-ring]]),
where $I_{Y\cap Z}=I_Y+I_Z$ is the sum of the ideal sheaves of $Y$ and $Z$, and
where

$$\mathcal O_{Y\cap Z,p}=\mathcal O_{X,p}/I_{Y\cap Z,p}$$

is the local ring at $p$ of the scheme-theoretic intersection ([[def-scheme-theoretic-fibre]]).

The definition is meaningful. Write $\eta$ for the generic point of the integral
one-dimensional scheme $Y$. By hypothesis $\eta\notin Z$, so $\eta\notin Y\cap Z$.
An irreducible component of the closed subset $Y\cap Z$ is the closure of one of
its points, hence a closed subset of $Y$ not containing $\eta$. Such a component
is a single closed point of $Y$: if a point $y\ne\eta$ of $Y$ had a closure
containing a further point $z\ne y$, then the closures of $z$, of $y$ and of
$\eta$ would form a strict chain of irreducible closed subsets of length two,
contradicting $\dim Y=1$
([[def-dimension-noetherian-topological-space]]);
therefore $Y\cap Z$ is supported in the closed points of $Y$ that lie on $Z$. In
particular $\mathcal O_{Y\cap Z,p}$, being a quotient of the Noetherian local ring
$\mathcal O_{X,p}$
([[def-locally-noetherian-and-noetherian-scheme]], [[def-noetherian-ring-and-module]]),
is a zero-dimensional Noetherian local ring, and such a ring has a finite
composition series over itself
([[def-composition-series-and-length-of-a-module]]); so the length is a
well-defined natural number (Remarks). The value depends only on the local data at
$p$: pulling back along the canonical localization morphism $\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal O_{X,p}\to X$
does not change $\mathcal O_{X,p}$, $I_{Y,p}$ or $I_{Z,p}$, so replacing $X$ by an
open neighbourhood of $p$ leaves $m_p(Y\cap Z)$ unchanged. Finally
$m_p(Y\cap Z)\ge 1$, because $p\in Y\cap Z$ makes $\mathcal O_{Y\cap Z,p}$ a nonzero
ring.

The invariant is the one used for strict transforms: after blowing up a closed
point, one computes $m_q(Y'\cap Z')$ in the blown-up ambient scheme at a closed
point $q$ lying over $p$.

## Remarks

[R1] *A zero-dimensional Noetherian local ring has finite length.* Let
$(A,\mathfrak m)$ be Noetherian local with $\dim A=0$. Every prime ideal of $A$ is
then equal to $\mathfrak m$, so the nilradical $\operatorname{Nil}(A)$, which is
the intersection of all prime ideals, equals $\mathfrak m$; hence $\mathfrak m$ is
nilpotent, say $\mathfrak m^N=0$
([[thm-nilradical-of-a-noetherian-ring-is-nilpotent]]). This gives a finite
filtration $A=\mathfrak m^0\supseteq\mathfrak m\supseteq\cdots\supseteq\mathfrak m^N=0$
by ideals of $A$. Each successive quotient
$\mathfrak m^i/\mathfrak m^{i+1}$ is a module over the field
$A/\mathfrak m$ that is generated by the images of a finite generating set of the
ideal $\mathfrak m^i$, so it is a finite-dimensional $A/\mathfrak m$-vector space
and has finite length; the length of $A$ is the sum of these finitely many lengths
([[cor-length-is-additive-in-short-exact-sequences]]). No choice principle beyond
the ambient definition is used.

[R2] On this page, $Y$ may be the strict transform of a curve and $Z$ another
curve component or an exceptional divisor; $Y\cap Z$ is their zero-dimensional
contact locus. When both components are regular curves on a regular surface,
$m_p(Y\cap Z)\ge 2$
is exactly the tangency that the resolution process must remove: the point-blowup
drop lemma compares $m_p(Y\cap Z)$ with the multiplicities
$m_q(Y'\cap Z')$ at points of the blown-up surface lying over $p$.
