## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Differentiating a compact-group highest weight
gives the same highest weight for the complexified derived Lie algebra: if
$\pi$ is an irreducible finite-dimensional representation of $G$ with highest
weight $\lambda\in X^*(T)$, then the associated $\mathfrak g'_{\mathbb C}$-module
has highest weight the differential of $\lambda$. Conversely a finite-dimensional
module for the complexified derived algebra integrates to a representation of
$G$ exactly when its torus weights lie in $X^*(T)$, equivalently when the finite
central covering kernel of $Z(G)^0\times G_{\mathrm{der}}^{\mathrm{sc}}\to G$
acts trivially.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** Assume the Axiom of Choice, a compact connected $G$ with maximal torus $T$, and the covering $Z(G)^0\times G_{\mathrm{der}}^{\mathrm{sc}}\to G$ with finite central kernel.

[A1] The Axiom of Choice is [[def-axiom-of-choice]]; it enters through [L1] and [L2].

[L1] Irreducible representations of $G$ are classified by dominant elements of $X^*(T)$, and the representation restricts on the derived factor to the module of the differential of its highest weight ([[thm-highest-weight-classification-for-a-compact-connected-lie-group]]).

[L2] Characters of $T$ differentiate to integral functionals on $\mathfrak t$, and a character is determined by its differential because $T$ is connected with surjective exponential ([[prop-differentiation-identifies-characters-with-the-integral-weight-lattice-of-t]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Let $\pi$ have highest weight $\lambda\in X^*(T)$ and let $v$ be a highest-weight vector. Differentiating the group action on the line $\mathbb Cv$ gives a Lie-algebra weight $d\lambda$, and for every positive root and every $X\in\mathfrak g_\alpha$ one has $X\cdot v=0$ while the Borel subalgebra acts on $v$ by the character $d\lambda$; hence the $\mathfrak g'_{\mathbb C}$-module generated by $v$ has highest weight $d\lambda$. [L1, L2]

1.2 Conversely, suppose $M$ is a finite-dimensional $\mathfrak g'_{\mathbb C}$-module whose torus weights all lie in $X^*(T)$; then the corresponding characters make $M$ a module for the simply connected compact group $G_{\mathrm{der}}^{\mathrm{sc}}$ (by [L1]) and also for $Z(G)^0$ through the central character, hence for the product $Z(G)^0\times G_{\mathrm{der}}^{\mathrm{sc}}$; the finite central kernel acts on each weight space by the scalar by which its character acts, and this scalar is one precisely because the weights lie in $X^*(T)$, so the action descends to $G$. [L1, L2]

2.1 If instead some weight of $M$ does not lie in $X^*(T)$, then the corresponding kernel element acts nontrivially on that weight space, so the action does not descend; hence descent is exactly the lattice condition on the weights, and the two constructions are inverse on highest weights. [A1, L1, L2, step 1.1, step 1.2]∎
