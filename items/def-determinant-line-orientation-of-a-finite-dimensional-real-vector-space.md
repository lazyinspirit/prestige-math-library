## Definition

For a finite-dimensional real vector space $V$, an orientation is a positive ray in the one-dimensional determinant line $\det V=\Lambda^{\dim V}V$. If $\dim V=0$, $\det V=\mathbb R$ still has the two rays $\mathbb R_{>0}$ and $\mathbb R_{<0}$.
