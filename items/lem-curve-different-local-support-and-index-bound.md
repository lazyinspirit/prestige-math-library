## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $f:C\to D$ be a finite surjective morphism
of smooth proper geometrically integral curves over a field $k$, with finite
separable function-field extension $k(C)/k(D)$. For a closed point $p$ of
$C$, put $q=f(p)$, let $e_p$ be its ramification index, and set
$l_p=\operatorname{length}_{\mathcal O_{C,p}}(\Omega_{C/D,p})$. Then
$\Omega_{C/D}$ is coherent and torsion with finite support, and $l_p\ge e_p-1$
for every $p$. More precisely, $l_p=0$ if and only if $e_p=1$ and
$\kappa(p)/\kappa(q)$ is separable; $l_p=e_p-1$ if and only if
$\kappa(p)/\kappa(q)$ is separable and $e_p$ is invertible in $\kappa(q)$
(equivalently, the extension of discrete valuation rings is tamely ramified).
If the residue extension is inseparable or the positive residue
characteristic divides $e_p$, then $l_p\ge e_p$. Consequently
$\operatorname{Supp}(\Omega_{C/D})=\{p:e_p>1\text{ or }\kappa(p)/\kappa(q)\text{ inseparable}\}$;
when $k$ is perfect this is exactly $\{p:e_p>1\}$.

## Facts & Assumptions
**Given:** A finite surjective morphism $f:C\to D$ of smooth proper geometrically integral curves over $k$ with $k(C)/k(D)$ finite separable, a closed point $p\in C$ with image $q=f(p)$, and the relative differential sheaf $\Omega_{C/D}$.

[F1] The morphism $f$ is finite and surjective, the extension $k(C)/k(D)$ is finite and separable of degree $\deg(f)$, and the local rings $\mathcal O_{C,p}$, $\mathcal O_{D,q}$ are discrete valuation rings with uniformizers $t_p$, $t_q$; write $\Omega_{C/D}$ for the relative differential sheaf. ([[def-finite-morphism-schemes]], [[def-ramification-index-curve-map]], [[thm-local-ring-smooth-curve-dvr]], [[def-sheaf-relative-differentials]])

[F2] Kähler differentials commute with base change: for $C'=C\times_DD'$ the canonical map $g^*\Omega_{C/D}\to\Omega_{C'/D'}$ is an isomorphism; in particular $\Omega_{C/D,p}\otimes_{\mathcal O_{C,p}}\widehat{\mathcal O_{C,p}}\cong\Omega_{\widehat B/\widehat A}$ for the completed local rings $\widehat A=\widehat{\mathcal O_{D,q}}$, $\widehat B=\widehat{\mathcal O_{C,p}}$; completions are flat, so the local length $l_p$ is not changed. ([[lem-differentials-commute-base-change-schemes]])

[F3] Separable function fields have no differentials: if $L/K$ is a finite separable field extension, then $\Omega_{L/K}=0$. Indeed $L=K(a)$ for some $a$ ([[thm-primitive-element-theorem-for-finite-separable-extensions]]), with minimal polynomial $m$ separable, so its derivative satisfies $m'(a)\ne0$ ([[cor-irreducible-polynomial-is-separable-iff-derivative-nonzero]]); the presentation $\Omega_{K[x]/(m)/K}\cong (K[x]/(m))\,\mathrm dx/(m'(a)\mathrm dx)$ from [[thm-kahler-differentials-existence-presentation]] and [[cor-jacobian-presentation-differentials]] then vanishes because $m'(a)$ is a unit of $L$.

[F4] If $\Omega_{C/D}$ vanishes at the generic point of $C$ then it is a torsion sheaf on the integral curve $C$; a nonzero coherent torsion sheaf on $C$ is supported in a proper closed subset, which is a finite set of closed points. At a closed point $p$ the stalk $\Omega_{C/D,p}$ is then a finite-length $\mathcal O_{C,p}$-module, and $l_p$ is its length. ([[lem-curve-closed-subsets-finite]], [[def-composition-series-and-length-of-a-module]], [[def-coherent-module-scheme]])

[F5] Local structure after completion. Choose an affine neighborhood $V=\operatorname{Spec}(A_0)$ of $q$; because $f$ is finite, $f^{-1}(V)=\operatorname{Spec}(R_0)$ with $R_0$ finite over $A_0$. Put $A=(A_0)_q$ and $R=R_0\otimes_{A_0}A$. The map $A\to R$ is injective and $R$ is a domain, so $R$ is torsion-free over the DVR $A$ and therefore finite free. After the flat completion $A\to\widehat A$, the finite algebra $R\otimes_A\widehat A$ is a product of its local factors $B_{p'}=\widehat{\mathcal O_{C,p'}}$ indexed by the points $p'$ above $q$: its special fibre is an Artinian ring whose local idempotents lift uniquely in the complete algebra. Each factor is a direct summand, hence finite free over $\widehat A$, and is a complete DVR. For the chosen factor write $A=\widehat{\mathcal O_{D,q}}$, $B=\widehat{\mathcal O_{C,p}}$, $K_0=\operatorname{Frac}(A)$, $L_0=\operatorname{Frac}(B)$, $K=\kappa(q)$, and $L=\kappa(p)$. Then $L_0/K_0$ is finite separable (it is a factor after base change of the generically separable field extension), and $t_q=u s^{e_p}$ for a unit $u\in B$ and a uniformizer $s=t_p$ of $B$. The special fibre $C_0=B/t_qB$ has $e_p$ successive quotients isomorphic to $L$ over $K$, so $\dim_K C_0=e_p[L:K]$. ([[thm-finite-flat-modules-over-local-rings-are-free]], [[thm-dvr-element-normal-form]], [[def-ramification-index-curve-map]])

[F6] The completed map $A\to B$ is finite flat and a local complete intersection. The graph $C\to C\times_kD$ is a section of the smooth projection $C\times_kD\to C$, hence a regular immersion (Stacks, Lemma 31.23.8, tag 067R); composing with the smooth projection $C\times_kD\to D$ gives an lci morphism (Stacks, Lemma 37.62.7, tag 069J). Finite flatness follows locally because the finite algebra over the target DVR is torsion-free, hence free, and this property is preserved by completion and by taking a direct factor. Since $f$ is finite, it is quasi-finite. The local quasi-finite flat lci criterion gives, after shrinking, a presentation $B=A[x_1,\ldots,x_n,1/h]/(f_1,\ldots,f_n)$ with a regular sequence of $n$ equations (Stacks, Lemma 49.10.1, tag 0BWE). If $J=(\partial f_i/\partial x_j)$, the conormal presentation is $B^n\xrightarrow{J}B^n\to\Omega_{B/A}\to0$, so $\operatorname{Fitt}_0(\Omega_{B/A})=(\det J)$ by the definition of the zeroth Fitting ideal. The determinant is nonzero because the generic field extension is separable and thus $\Omega_{L_0/K_0}=0$.
The same determinant generates the Noether different. Put $P=A[x_1,\ldots,x_n,1/h]$, let $I=\ker(B\otimes_AB\to B)$, and write $g_j=x_j\otimes1-1\otimes x_j$ in $P\otimes_AB$. These $g_j$ generate the kernel of multiplication. The Koszul complex on $(f_i)$ in $P$ resolves $B$ because the presentation is a regular sequence. For the diagonal sequence, write $P\otimes_AB=B[y_1,\ldots,y_n,1/h(y)]$ and let $\bar x_j$ be the image of $x_j$ in $B$. Before localization the sequence $y_j-\bar x_j$ is regular: successively quotienting by its first $r$ terms gives the polynomial ring in the remaining variables over $B$, and the next monic linear polynomial is a nonzerodivisor even when $B$ has zero divisors. Localizing at $h(y)$ preserves regularity, and the final quotient is $B[1/h(\bar x)]=B$ because $h(\bar x)$ is already a unit in $B$. Thus the Koszul complex on $(g_j)$ resolves $B$ over $P\otimes_AB$.
Expanding each polynomial difference gives
$$f_i(x\otimes1)-f_i(1\otimes x)=\sum_j(\partial f_i/\partial x_j)(1\otimes x)g_j+\sum_{j,r}a_{ijr}g_jg_r.$$
The comparison map between these Koszul resolutions sends the degree-one generator for $f_i$ to the linear combination of the $g_j$ with coefficients $1\otimes(\partial f_i/\partial x_j)$ plus terms in $(g_1,\ldots,g_n)$; its top component is the determinant of that coefficient matrix. Both complexes compute $\operatorname{Tor}^P_*(B,B)$: the first is a free $P$-resolution, and the second is a flat $P$-resolution because $B$ is flat over $A$. After tensoring with $B$, the top homology of the second complex is the kernel of the map with entries $g_j$ on $B\otimes_AB$, namely $\operatorname{Ann}(I)$. The comparison map carries the generator of the top homology of the first complex to an element of this annihilator; multiplying its image in $B\otimes_AB$ gives the determinant of the coefficient matrix modulo $I$, which is $\det J$. Thus the Noether different, the image of $\operatorname{Ann}(I)\to B$, is $(\det J)$ (Stacks, Lemma 49.12.2, tag 0BWD).
Now let $W=\operatorname{Hom}_A(B,A)$ and let $\tau\in W$ be the trace functional $b\mapsto\operatorname{Tr}_{B/A}(b)$. The diagonal-annihilator pairing identifies $\operatorname{Ann}(I)$ with $\operatorname{Hom}_B(W,B)$ (Stacks, Lemma 49.6.6, tag 0BVS): for a finite $A$-basis $b_i$ and dual basis $b_i^\vee$, an element $\sum_i b_i\otimes c_i$ maps to the functional $b_i^\vee\mapsto c_i$. Conversely a $B$-linear functional $\phi:W\to B$ maps to $\sum_i b_i\otimes\phi(b_i^\vee)$; its $B$-linearity is exactly the relation placing this tensor in $\operatorname{Ann}(I)$. Under this pairing, multiplication on $\operatorname{Ann}(I)$ agrees with evaluation at $\tau$. Indeed, if $b_ib_j=\sum_r a_{ij}^r b_r$ and $\xi=\sum_i b_i\otimes c_i\in\operatorname{Ann}(I)$, then the coefficient of $b_i$ in $(b_j\otimes1)\xi=(1\otimes b_j)\xi$ gives $b_jc_i=\sum_r a_{jr}^i c_r$. Summing over $i=j$ shows
$$\sum_i b_ic_i=\sum_i\operatorname{Tr}_{B/A}(b_i)c_i,$$
which is precisely $\mu(\xi)=\phi_\xi(\tau)$ (Stacks, Lemma 49.6.7, tag 0BVT). Hence the Noether different is the image of evaluation at $\tau$.
By the socle argument in [F7], $W=B\lambda$ for a generator $\lambda$ and $\tau=h\lambda$. The image of $\operatorname{Hom}_B(W,B)\to B$, $\phi\mapsto\phi(\tau)$, is then $(h)$. Stacks, Lemma 49.9.3 (tag 0BW6) identifies this image with the different because $W$ is invertible; Lemma 49.12.3 (tag 0BWG) identifies the different for this quasi-finite syntomic map with the Kähler different; and Lemma 49.7.4 (tag 0BVZ) computes that ideal from the Jacobian presentation. Consequently
$$\operatorname{Fitt}_0(\Omega_{B/A})=(\det J)=(h).$$
This proves the Jacobian/Koszul/trace-different bridge under the stated finite-flat-lci hypotheses; it uses neither a monogenic extension nor a residue-field perfectness assumption. ([[def-fitting-ideal-sheaf]], [[cor-jacobian-presentation-differentials]], [[thm-kahler-differentials-existence-presentation]])

[F7] The dual module $W=\operatorname{Hom}_A(B,A)$ is free of rank one over $B$, but its generator is not generally the trace functional. Since $B$ is finite free over $A$, reduction gives $W/t_qW\cong\operatorname{Hom}_K(C_0,K)$. The algebra $C_0=B/(s^{e_p})$ has socle $\operatorname{Ann}_{C_0}(s)=(s^{e_p-1})/(s^{e_p})$, one-dimensional over its residue field $L$; hence it is Artinian Gorenstein. For completeness, choose a $K$-linear functional $\phi:C_0\to K$ whose restriction to the socle is nonzero. The multiplication map $C_0\to\operatorname{Hom}_K(C_0,K)$, $c\mapsto(d\mapsto\phi(cd))$, is injective: if $c\ne0$, the nonzero ideal $(c)$ meets the socle, and since the socle is one-dimensional over $L$, multiplying a nonzero element of that intersection by a suitable lift of an element of $L$ makes its $\phi$-value nonzero. It is therefore an isomorphism by equality of finite $K$-dimensions. Lift its generator to $\lambda\in W$. The map $B\to W$, $b\mapsto b\lambda$, is an isomorphism modulo $t_q$; Nakayama makes it surjective, and both sides are free $A$-modules of the same finite rank, so it is an isomorphism. Write the trace functional as $\tau=h\lambda$ for the resulting generator $\lambda$ and some $h\in B$. By [F6], $(h)=\operatorname{Fitt}_0(\Omega_{B/A})$, so $l_p=\operatorname{length}_B(B/(h))=\operatorname{ord}_B(h)$. ([[thm-nakayama-lemma]], [[thm-dvr-ideal-and-module-length]], [[def-composition-series-and-length-of-a-module]])

[F8] The trace functional on the fibre $C_0$ is $\tau_0(c)=\operatorname{Tr}_{C_0/K}(c)=e_p\operatorname{Tr}_{L/K}(\bar c)$. Indeed the filtration by $(s)^j$ has $e_p$ quotients isomorphic to $L$, and multiplication by $c$ acts on each quotient as multiplication by $\bar c$; summing their traces gives the formula. The field trace map is nonzero exactly for a separable finite field extension, and therefore $\tau_0\ne0$ exactly when $L/K$ is separable and $e_p$ is invertible in $K$. This is a statement about the trace functional; the trace pairing on $C_0$ may be degenerate when $e_p>1$. Trace commutes with this finite-free base change, so $\tau_0$ is the reduction of $\tau$. (Stacks Project, Lemma 49.4.8 (tag 0C13); [[def-extension-degree-and-finite-extension]])

[F9] For a nonzero $h\in B$ with $\operatorname{ord}_B(h)=d$, $\operatorname{length}_B(B/(h))=d$. If the reduction of $h$ modulo $s^{e_p}$ is zero, then $h\in(s^{e_p})$ and $d\ge e_p$; if that reduction is a nonzero element of the socle $(s^{e_p-1})/(s^{e_p})$, then $d=e_p-1$. ([[thm-dvr-ideal-and-module-length]], [[thm-dvr-element-normal-form]], [[def-composition-series-and-length-of-a-module]])

[F10] Perfect residue fields: if $k$ is perfect then every finite extension of $k$ is separable, and both $\kappa(p)$ and $\kappa(q)$ are finite extensions of $k$; hence $L/K$ is separable for every point $p$. ([[cor-algebraic-extensions-of-perfect-fields-are-separable]], [[def-ramification-index-curve-map]])

[F11] The sheaf $\Omega_{C/D}$ is coherent under the stated Axiom of Choice. On affine charts $V=\operatorname{Spec}(A)$ of $D$, the finite morphism has $f^{-1}(V)=\operatorname{Spec}(B)$ with $B$ finite over $A$; since $D$ is a finite-type curve over the Noetherian field $k$, $A$ is Noetherian, so $B$ is finite type and finitely presented over $A$. A finite polynomial presentation of $B$ makes $\Omega_{B/A}$ a cokernel between finite free $B$-modules by the Jacobian presentation. Affine compatibility identifies $\Omega_{C/D}$ locally with the associated sheaf of these finite modules, so it is quasi-coherent of finite type. The same finite-type-over-$k$ argument makes $C$ locally Noetherian, and hence $\Omega_{C/D}$ is coherent. ([[def-algebraic-curve-over-field]], [[lem-field-is-noetherian]], [[cor-finite-type-algebra-over-noetherian-ring-is-noetherian]], [[def-finite-morphism-schemes]], [[def-finite-type-and-module-finite-algebras]], [[cor-finite-type-algebra-over-noetherian-ring-is-finitely-presented]], [[cor-jacobian-presentation-differentials]], [[lem-sheaf-differentials-affine-compatibility]], [[def-quasi-coherent-module-scheme]], [[def-locally-noetherian-and-noetherian-scheme]], [[thm-coherent-sheaves-abelian-noetherian-scheme]], [[def-axiom-of-choice]])



## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct; reduce to the completed local picture at $p$, identify the Fitting ideal of the relative differentials with the trace different by the local complete-intersection and diagonal Koszul calculations, and compute its order using the trace functional on the special fibre.

1.1 Generic vanishing. By [F3] applied to the finite separable extension $k(C)/k(D)$ one has $\Omega_{k(C)/k(D)}=0$; this is the stalk of $\Omega_{C/D}$ at the generic point of the integral curve $C$, so the coherent sheaf $\Omega_{C/D}$ of [F11] is torsion and, by [F4], its support is a finite set of closed points and each stalk $\Omega_{C/D,p}$ has finite length $l_p$ over the discrete valuation ring $\mathcal O_{C,p}$. [F1, F3, F4, F11]

1.2 Local reduction. Fix $p$ with image $q$. The affine finite algebra localized at $q$ in [F5] is finite free over $\mathcal O_{D,q}$; flat completion splits it into the product of the completed local factors indexed by the points over $q$. Base change of $\Omega$ to the factor at $p$ gives $\Omega_{B/A}$, and faithfully flat completion preserves the finite length: a composition series over $\mathcal O_{C,p}$ tensors to a composition series over $B$ with the same residue field and the same number of factors. The ramification index and residue extension are unchanged. We may work with the complete DVR extension $A\to B$ of [F5], with $t_q=u s^{e_p}$. [F2, F5]

1.3 Local different calculation. By [F6] and [F7], choose a $B$-generator $\lambda$ of $W=\operatorname{Hom}_A(B,A)$ and write the trace functional as $\tau=h\lambda$. Then $(h)=\operatorname{Fitt}_0(\Omega_{B/A})$, so $l_p=\operatorname{length}_B(B/(h))=\operatorname{ord}_B(h)$. Put $C_0=B/t_qB=B/(s^{e_p})$ and denote by $h_0$ and $\lambda_0$ the reductions of $h$ and $\lambda$. The reduction of $\tau$ is $\tau_0=h_0\lambda_0$. [F6, F7]

2.1 Trace on the fibre. The filtration $C_0\supset(s)\supset\cdots\supset(s^{e_p})=0$ has $e_p$ quotients isomorphic to $L$. Multiplication by $c\in C_0$ acts on each quotient as multiplication by its residue $\bar c\in L$, so [F8] gives $\tau_0(c)=e_p\operatorname{Tr}_{L/K}(\bar c)$. Thus $\tau_0\ne0$ exactly when $L/K$ is separable and $e_p$ is invertible in $K$. [F8, step 1.3]

2.2 Tame case. Suppose $L/K$ is separable and $e_p$ is invertible in $K$. Then $\tau_0\ne0$, so $h_0\ne0$ because $\lambda_0$ generates $\operatorname{Hom}_K(C_0,K)$. For every $c\in C_0$, multiplication by $sc$ is nilpotent, hence has trace zero; therefore $\tau_0(sc)=0$ and $(s\tau_0)(c)=0$. As $\tau_0=h_0\lambda_0$ and $\lambda_0$ is a generator, this says $s h_0=0$. Thus $h_0$ is a nonzero element of the socle $\operatorname{Ann}_{C_0}(s)=(s^{e_p-1})/(s^{e_p})$, so its lift $h$ has valuation exactly $e_p-1$. By step 1.3, $l_p=e_p-1$. [F7, F8, F9, step 1.3]

2.3 Inseparable or wild case. If $L/K$ is inseparable or the residue characteristic divides $e_p$, then [F8] gives $\tau_0=0$, hence $h_0=0$ because $\lambda_0$ generates the dual module. Thus $h\in t_qB=(s^{e_p})$, so $\operatorname{ord}_B(h)\ge e_p$ and [F9, step 1.3] gives $l_p\ge e_p$. Together the two cases prove $l_p\ge e_p-1$, with equality exactly in the tame case. [F8, F9, step 1.3]

3.1 Vanishing, support, and perfect base. If $l_p=0$, then $e_p=1$ because $l_p\ge e_p-1$, and the inseparable case of step 2.3 is excluded; conversely, $e_p=1$ and separable residue extension is tame and gives $l_p=0$ by step 2.2. Since the generic stalk vanishes, $\operatorname{Supp}(\Omega_{C/D})=\{p:e_p>1\text{ or }\kappa(p)/\kappa(q)\text{ is inseparable}\}$. If $k$ is perfect, each residue field is finite over $k$, so every such residue extension is separable and the support is exactly $\{p:e_p>1\}$. [F8, F9, F10, step 2.2, step 2.3]

4.1 Conclusion. Coherence and finite support were proved in steps 1.1–1.2, and the local length, equality, vanishing, and support assertions follow from steps 1.3–3.1. The proof fixes a generator $\lambda$ of the dualizing module and expresses the trace as $h\lambda$; it does not assert that the trace itself generates the dual module or that the fibre trace pairing is nondegenerate. ∎
