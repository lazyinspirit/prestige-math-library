## Definition

Let $\mathfrak g$ be a complex Lie superalgebra. Give its tensor algebra
$$ T(\mathfrak g)=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}\mathfrak g^{\otimes n} $$
the supergrading in which a homogeneous tensor $x_1\otimes\cdots\otimes x_n$ has parity $|x_1|+\cdots+|x_n|\pmod2$, and use concatenation as multiplication ([[def-tensor-algebra-of-a-vector-space]]). Let $I$ be the two-sided ideal generated by the following homogeneous relations (two-sided ideal and generated ideal are as in [[def-left-right-and-two-sided-ideal]] and [[def-generated-and-principal-ideals]]):
$$ x\otimes y-(-1)^{|x||y|}y\otimes x-[x,y] $$
for homogeneous $x,y\in\mathfrak g$. Each generator has parity $|x|+|y|$ because the bracket is graded. The lemma [[lem-ideal-generated-by-homogeneous-elements-is-graded]] therefore makes $I$ graded, so the quotient has the induced supergrading. The quotient
$$ U(\mathfrak g):=T(\mathfrak g)/I $$
with the quotient multiplication is the **universal enveloping superalgebra**. Write $\iota_{\mathfrak g}:\mathfrak g\to U(\mathfrak g)$ for the degree-one map. The defining relation is
$$ \iota_{\mathfrak g}(x)\iota_{\mathfrak g}(y)-(-1)^{|x||y|}\iota_{\mathfrak g}(y)\iota_{\mathfrak g}(x)=\iota_{\mathfrak g}([x,y]). $$
In particular, if $x$ is odd then $2\iota_{\mathfrak g}(x)^2=\iota_{\mathfrak g}([x,x])$, hence $\iota_{\mathfrak g}(x)^2=\tfrac12\iota_{\mathfrak g}([x,x])$ over $\mathbb C$. This quotient definition does not itself assert an ordered-monomial basis; the PBW basis is a separate theorem.
