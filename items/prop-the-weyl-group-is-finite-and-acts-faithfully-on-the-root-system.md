## Statement

Let $\Phi$ be a reduced crystallographic root system. Then its Weyl group
$W(\Phi)$ is finite, and the action of $W(\Phi)$ on $\Phi$ by restriction is
faithful: the homomorphism $W(\Phi)\to\operatorname{Sym}(\Phi)$ that sends
$w$ to its restriction to $\Phi$ is injective.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A reduced crystallographic root system $\Phi$ in a finite-dimensional real inner product space $E$, with reflections $s_\alpha$ and Weyl group $W(\Phi)$.

[L1] $\Phi$ is finite, spans $E$, and $s_\alpha(\Phi)=\Phi$ for all $\alpha\in\Phi$ ([[def-reduced-crystallographic-euclidean-root-system]]).

[L2] The reflection $s_\alpha$ is orthogonal, $s_\alpha(\alpha)=-\alpha$, and $s_\alpha(x)=x$ for $(x,\alpha)=0$ ([[def-weyl-group-of-a-root-system]], [[def-coroot-and-dual-root-system]]).

[L3] $W(\Phi)=\langle s_\alpha:\alpha\in\Phi\rangle$ is the subgroup of $O(E)$ generated by the reflections ([[def-weyl-group-of-a-root-system]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Each generator $s_\alpha$ lies in $O(E)$ and satisfies $s_\alpha(\Phi)=\Phi$ by [L1]. Consequently every $w\in W(\Phi)$, being a finite product of generators and their inverses, restricts to a bijection $w:\Phi\to\Phi$. [L1, L2, L3, algebra]

2.1 The assignment $\rho:W(\Phi)\to\operatorname{Sym}(\Phi)$, $\rho(w)=w|_\Phi$, is a group homomorphism: the restriction of a composition of linear maps is the composition of the restrictions. Hence $\rho(W(\Phi))$ is a subgroup of the finite group $\operatorname{Sym}(\Phi)$. [L1, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 If $\rho(w)=\mathrm{id}$ then $w(\alpha)=\alpha$ for every $\alpha\in\Phi$; since $\Phi$ spans $E$ and $w$ is linear, $w=\mathrm{id}_E$. Thus $\ker\rho=\{1\}$: the restriction action on the finite set $\Phi$ is faithful, and $\rho$ identifies $W(\Phi)$ with the finite subgroup $\rho(W(\Phi))\le\operatorname{Sym}(\Phi)$. In particular $W(\Phi)$ is finite and $|W(\Phi)|=|\rho(W(\Phi))|\le|\Phi|!$. [L1, step 2.1, algebra] ∎
