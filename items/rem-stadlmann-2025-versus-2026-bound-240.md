## Remark

Stadlmann's 2025 paper proves the smooth-modulus distribution exponent $1/2+1/40-\varepsilon$ and the many-prime gap bound $H_m\ll\exp(3.8075m)$. A separate version 1 preprint, submitted on 31 August 2026 under the title *Bounded gaps between primes*, claims $H_1\le240$ by combining the Bombieri–Vinogradov theorem with newer smooth-modulus estimates. The 2026 claim is a distinct preprint statement and is not Theorem 2 of the 2025 paper, nor a consequence asserted by that theorem.
