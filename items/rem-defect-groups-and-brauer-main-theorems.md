## Remark

This page reaches the global block decomposition coming from primitive central
idempotents and the blockwise form of the decomposition matrix. It does not yet
introduce defect groups, Brauer pairs, Brauer correspondents, or any of
Brauer's main theorems.

Those results require the local subgroup analysis that begins only after the
primitive-idempotent and decomposition-matrix package is in place. So the
present page is the correct stopping point for global modular character data,
while local block theory belongs to the sequel.
