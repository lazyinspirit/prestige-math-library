## Definition

For $n\ge1$, put $\mathbb H^n=\{(x^1,\ldots,x^n)\in\mathbb R^n:x^n\ge0\}$, with the subspace topology, and put $\partial\mathbb H^n=\{x\in\mathbb H^n:x^n=0\}$. Put $\mathbb H^0=\mathbb R^0$ and $\partial\mathbb H^0=\varnothing$.
