## Definition

Assume the Axiom of Choice ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]). Fix a finite-dimensional complex semisimple Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$, a Cartan subalgebra $\mathfrak h$, a positive Borel $\mathfrak b=\mathfrak h\oplus\mathfrak n^+$, the positive system $\Phi^+$ and $\mathfrak n^-$ as in [[thm-triangular-decomposition-from-a-chosen-positive-root-system]]. The quotient $\mathfrak g/\mathfrak b$ is a $\mathfrak b$-module for the adjoint action; $\mathfrak h$ acts with weights $\Phi^-$. Projection along $\mathfrak g=\mathfrak n^-\oplus\mathfrak b$ identifies this quotient with $\mathfrak n^-$ as an $\mathfrak h$-module; its $\mathfrak b$-action is $b\cdot x=\operatorname{pr}_{\mathfrak n^-}[b,x]$, which need not vanish for $b\in\mathfrak n^+$. For $k\ge0$ put

$$B_k:=U(\mathfrak g)\otimes_{U(\mathfrak b)}\Lambda^k(\mathfrak g/\mathfrak b),$$

the $k$-th exterior power being taken over $\mathbb C$ with the induced $\mathfrak b$-action. Thus $B_0=U(\mathfrak g)\otimes_{U(\mathfrak b)}\mathbb C\cong M(0)$, the Verma module of highest weight $0$ of [[def-verma-module]]. By [[thm-pbw-ordered-monomial-basis-for-the-enveloping-algebra]] one has $U(\mathfrak g)\cong U(\mathfrak n^-)\otimes U(\mathfrak b)$ as vector spaces, so

$$B_k\cong U(\mathfrak n^-)\otimes_{\mathbb C}\Lambda^k(\mathfrak n^-)$$

as $U(\mathfrak n^-)$-modules; hence $B_k$ is a free $U(\mathfrak n^-)$-module with $\binom{|\Phi^+|}{k}$ generators, and $B_k=0$ for $k>|\Phi^+|$. Each $B_k$ is a finitely generated $\mathfrak g$-module in the category $\mathcal O$ of [[def-bgg-category-o]]: it is generated by the image of the finite-dimensional space $\Lambda^k(\mathfrak g/\mathfrak b)$, it is $\mathfrak h$-semisimple: a root-vector exterior basis has the finite multiset of weights $\mu_S=\sum_{\beta\in S}\beta$ for subsets $S\subseteq\Phi^-$ with $\#S=k$, with distinct subsets counted separately even when their sums coincide. For $k=0$ the empty subset has weight $0$. PBW negative-root monomials shift these weights by elements of $-Q^+$, so the weights of $B_k$ lie in the finite union $\bigcup_{S\subseteq\Phi^-,\,\#S=k}(\mu_S-Q^+)$, with finite-dimensional weight spaces by PBW. Raising a fixed weight by positive roots can reach only finitely many weights in that union: their simple-root coefficients are bounded above by the finitely many $\mu$ and below by the starting weight. Hence the $U(\mathfrak n^+)$-orbit of each weight vector is finite-dimensional, proving local finiteness.

For $k\ge1$ define the differential $d_k\colon B_k\to B_{k-1}$ on elementary tensors by

$$d_k(u\otimes\xi_1\wedge\cdots\wedge\xi_k)=\sum_{i=1}^{k}(-1)^{i+1}u\xi_i\otimes\xi_1\wedge\cdots\widehat{\xi_i}\cdots\wedge\xi_k+\sum_{1\le i<j\le k}(-1)^{i+j}u\otimes\overline{[\xi_i,\xi_j]}\wedge\xi_1\wedge\cdots\widehat{\xi_i}\cdots\widehat{\xi_j}\cdots\wedge\xi_k,$$

where $\xi_i\in\mathfrak g$ are representatives of elements of $\mathfrak g/\mathfrak b$ and the bar denotes the class in $\mathfrak g/\mathfrak b$. The balanced well-definedness, $\mathfrak g$-linearity and square-zero identity are proved explicitly in [[thm-standard-induced-resolution-is-exact]]; the formula uses the actual quotient adjoint action above. Finally the counit $\varepsilon\colon U(\mathfrak g)\to\mathbb C$ induces a well-defined map $d_0\colon B_0\to\mathbb C$, $u\otimes1\mapsto\varepsilon(u)$, because $\varepsilon(u b)=0$ for $b\in\mathfrak b$ and $\varepsilon(1)=1$; it is the augmentation of the complex $(B_\bullet,d_\bullet)$, a chain complex in the sense of [[def-chain-complex-in-an-abelian-category]].
