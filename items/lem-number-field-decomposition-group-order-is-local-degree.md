## Statement
Assume the Axiom of Choice (AC). 
Let $L/K$ be a finite Galois extension of number fields, let $\mathfrak P$ be a finite prime of $L$, let $\mathfrak p=\mathfrak P\cap\mathcal O_K$, and let $L_{\mathfrak P}/K_{\mathfrak p}$ be the corresponding completion extension. The decomposition subgroup

$$D_{\mathfrak P}=\{\sigma\in\operatorname{Gal}(L/K):\sigma(\mathfrak P)=\mathfrak P\}$$

acts on $L_{\mathfrak P}$ by continuous extension, and restriction gives an isomorphism

$$D_{\mathfrak P}\xrightarrow{\sim}\operatorname{Gal}(L_{\mathfrak P}/K_{\mathfrak p}).$$

In particular, $|D_{\mathfrak P}|=[L_{\mathfrak P}:K_{\mathfrak p}]$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** the Axiom of Choice (AC); A finite Galois extension $L/K$ of number fields and a finite prime $\mathfrak P$ of $L$.

[F1] A finite Galois extension is the splitting field of a separable polynomial over its base field, and its automorphism group has order the extension degree. [[thm-finite-galois-extension-characterizations]]

[F2] The field $L$ embeds densely in its completion $L_{\mathfrak P}$, and $L\otimes_KK_{\mathfrak p}$ is the product of the completions over $\mathfrak p$. [[def-completion-of-a-number-field-at-a-prime]] [[lem-number-field-completions-as-local-polynomial-factors]]

[F3] For a finite Galois extension, $\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$ denotes the group of $K$-automorphisms of $L$. [[def-finite-galois-extension-and-galois-group]]

[F4] A number field is a finite extension of $\mathbb Q$. [[def-number-field]]


[A1] The Axiom of Choice is the explicit hypothesis in the completion suppliers cited by [F2]; this carrier adopts it for steps 1.1–2.1. [[def-axiom-of-choice]] [[def-completion-of-a-number-field-at-a-prime]] [[lem-number-field-completions-as-local-polynomial-factors]]
## Proof

**Given:** The data above.

1.1 Every $\sigma\in D_{\mathfrak P}$ preserves the absolute value defining $L_{\mathfrak P}$, so it extends uniquely by continuity to an automorphism of $L_{\mathfrak P}$ fixing $K_{\mathfrak p}$. If two extensions agree on $L$, they agree on its dense image by [F2], so this restriction map is injective. [F2, given, A1]

2.1 Let $\tau\in\operatorname{Gal}(L_{\mathfrak P}/K_{\mathfrak p})$. Its restriction to $L$ is a $K$-embedding into $L_{\mathfrak P}$. By [F1], $L$ is generated by the roots of a separable polynomial over $K$, all of whose roots lie in $L$. The automorphism $\tau$ permutes those roots, so $\tau(L)=L$. By [F3], its restriction is an element $\sigma\in\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$. Since $\tau$ preserves the local absolute value, $\sigma$ preserves the valuation at $\mathfrak P$, hence stabilizes $\mathfrak P$ and belongs to $D_{\mathfrak P}$. Thus restriction is surjective. [F1, F2, F3, F4, step 1.1, algebra, A1]

3.1 Steps 1.1 and 2.1 show restriction is a bijective homomorphism. The local Galois group has order the local degree, so $|D_{\mathfrak P}|=[L_{\mathfrak P}:K_{\mathfrak p}]$. [F1, F3, F4, step 1.1, step 2.1, algebra] ∎
