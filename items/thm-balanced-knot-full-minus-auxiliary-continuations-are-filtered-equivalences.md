## Statement

Assume Countable Choice. Fix one actual balanced multipointed diagram of an oriented knot in $S^3$, with $k\ge1$, $m=h+k-1$ and its ordered marks. Take two actual finite square-zero full minus count complexes over $R=\mathbb F_2[U_1,\ldots,U_k]$, with all positive z degrees retained. Suppose their chosen end paths have no nonconstant strip of index at most zero and every index-one strip is regular, as in the full-marked chosen-count construction. Background surface complex structures, positive area forms, full torus tensors and optional generator charts may have been chosen independently.

There are finite actual anchored continuation matrices over R and inverse chain homotopies. They are filtered for the relative Alexander filtration and homogeneous for the relative Maslov degree, after identifying the origins on the SAME geometric generator set. Every $U_i$ has degrees $(-2,-1)$. Positive z terms are included and lower filtration by their total z degree; no associated-graded substitution is made. Scalar restriction to any chosen variable, or identification or localization of variables, preserves these actual finite identities.

The proof supplies compact low-dimensional continuation spaces and their exhaustive collars by an actual full-marked index budget. No zero-basepoint bubble exclusion is substituted for that budget. No comparison of different attaching systems, genus changes, basepoint-pair cones or absolute normalization is asserted.
