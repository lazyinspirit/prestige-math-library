## Statement

For every field $F$, every ideal of $F[x]$ is generated by one polynomial; equivalently, $F[x]$ is a principal ideal domain.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A field $F$.

[L1] The ring $F[x]$ is a Euclidean domain with degree as Euclidean function ([[cor-polynomial-ring-over-a-field-is-euclidean]]).

[L2] Every Euclidean domain is a principal ideal domain ([[thm-euclidean-domain-is-a-pid]]).

[L3] A principal ideal domain is an integral domain in which every ideal is principal ([[def-principal-ideal-domain]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 By [L1] and [L2], $F[x]$ is a principal ideal domain. [given, L1, L2]

2.1 Unfolding [L3], every ideal of $F[x]$ therefore has the form $(d)$ for some polynomial $d$. [step 1.1, L3] ∎
