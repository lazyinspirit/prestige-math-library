## Remark

The Ingham–Huxley estimates are stated for $1/2\le\sigma\le1$ (with the second pair only for $2/3\le\sigma\le1$); Heath–Brown's three estimates use, respectively, $11/14\le\sigma\le1$, $1/2\le\sigma\le1$, and $129/167\le\sigma\le1$. Their conductor and height exponents also differ. Each estimate must be quoted with its own parameter choice and range; none asserts a shared exponent outside those hypotheses.
