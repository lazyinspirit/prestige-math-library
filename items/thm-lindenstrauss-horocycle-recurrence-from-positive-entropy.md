## Statement

Let X=Γ\(SL₂(ℝ)×L)/K be as in Lindenstrauss’s S-algebraic setting, let A={diag(e⁻ᵗ,eᵗ):t∈ℝ} act on the first factor, and let μ be an A-invariant probability that is recurrent along the L/K leaves, meaning that almost every point of every positive-measure set returns to that set outside each compact subset of its L/K leaf. If almost every component in the A-ergodic decomposition of μ has positive entropy, then μ is invariant under the expanding horocycle subgroup N⁺={[[1,s],[0,1]]:s∈ℝ}.
