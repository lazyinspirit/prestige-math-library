## Statement

For every associative unital ring R there is an explicitly constructed based CW model P_R and map BGL_∞(R)→P_R with π1(P_R)=GL_∞(R)/E_∞(R), inducing an isomorphism for every local homology coefficient module over that quotient. Its homotopy fiber is acyclic. The actual supplied elementary null formulas construct a pointwise based weak equivalence P_R→B(L_R)_0; together with the preceding actual Q-loop weak zigzag it identifies π_n(P_R) with π_{n+1}(BQ_R,0) for every n≥1. No choice principle is used. No chosen arbitrary-CW homotopy inverse or general perfect-subgroup factorization-uniqueness assertion is made.
