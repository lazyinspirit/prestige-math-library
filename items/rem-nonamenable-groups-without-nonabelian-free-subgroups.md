## Remark

This page proves that containing a rank-two free subgroup forces
nonamenability, but the converse fails. Standard constructions due to Adian and
Olshanskii produce nonamenable groups with no nonabelian free subgroup.

Accordingly, the implication in
[[cor-groups-containing-a-rank-two-free-subgroup-are-nonamenable]] is one-way
only.
