## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]). Let $k$ be a field.

1. In $K^0(\mathbb P^n_k)=K_0(\mathbb P^n_k)$
   ([[def-grothendieck-group-of-coherent-sheaves-and-vector-bundles-on-a-scheme]],
   [[lem-k-zero-vector-bundles-versus-coherent-sheaves]]) the classes
   $[\mathcal O(i)]$, $0\le i\le n$, form a $\mathbb Z$-basis; equivalently
   $K_0(\mathbb P^n)=\mathbb Z^{n+1}$ generated by the twisting sheaves
   ([[def-twisting-sheaf-proj]],
   [[thm-twisting-sheaf-invertible-standard-graded]]).
2. For every locally Noetherian $k$-scheme $Y$ and every $n\ge0$, the
   exterior-product map
   $$K_0(Y)\otimes_{\mathbb Z}K_0(\mathbb P^n_k)\longrightarrow K_0(\mathbb P^n_k\times_kY)$$
   is surjective; here $K_0(\mathbb P^n\times_kY)$ is the Grothendieck group of
   coherent sheaves
   ([[def-grothendieck-group-of-coherent-sheaves-and-vector-bundles-on-a-scheme]])
   and the product is
   $[\mathcal F]\times[\mathcal G]\mapsto[p_Y^*\mathcal F\otimes p_{\mathbb P}^*\mathcal G]$
   ([[lem-tensor-qc-modules-quasi-coherent]],
   [[lem-pullback-qc-module-quasi-coherent]]).
3. More generally, if $\mathcal E$ is a finite locally free $\mathcal O_Y$-module
   of rank $r+1$, the projective bundle $\mathbb P(\mathcal E)$ has
   $K_0(\mathbb P(\mathcal E))$ equal to the image of
   $\bigoplus_{a=0}^r K_0(Y)$ under
   $(\alpha_a)\mapsto\sum_a q^*\alpha_a\otimes[\mathcal O(a)]$; this is
   generation by twists, without treating coherent $K_0(Y)$ as a
   tensor-product ring on a singular base, via the relative-diagonal Koszul
   computation; no global cell decomposition of a nontrivial bundle is assumed
   ([[def-projective-bundle-scheme]]).

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** the Axiom of Choice; a field $k$; a locally Noetherian $k$-scheme $Y$; a finite locally free $\mathcal O_Y$-module $\mathcal E$ of rank $r+1\ge1$; the projective bundle $q:P=\mathbb P(\mathcal E)\to Y$ of one-dimensional quotients of $\mathcal E$ with twisting sheaf $\mathcal O(1)$ and universal exact sequence $0\to S\to q^*\mathcal E\to\mathcal O(1)\to0$, where $S$ is the tautological subbundle of rank $r$.

[F1] $q$ is proper, flat and smooth of relative dimension $r$, and its fibres are projective spaces of dimension $r$; the twisting sheaves $\mathcal O(a)$ are invertible, and on $\mathbb P^n_k$ the standard affine Čech cover computes their cohomology ([[def-projective-bundle-scheme]], [[def-twisting-sheaf-proj]], [[thm-twisting-sheaf-invertible-standard-graded]], [[thm-cech-computes-qc-cohomology-separated-scheme-affine-cover]]).

[F2] On $\mathbb P^n_A$ with $A$ a commutative ring and $d\in\mathbb Z$, $H^q=0$ unless $q=0$ or $q=n$; $H^0(\mathcal O(d))=A[x_0,\dots,x_n]_d$ for $d\ge0$ and $0$ for $d<0$ when $n>0$; so for the Euler characteristic over a field $\chi(\mathcal O(d))=\binom{n+d}{n}$ for $d\ge0$ and $\chi(\mathcal O(d))=0$ for $-n\le d\le-1$ ([[thm-cohomology-projective-space-twisting-sheaves]]).

[F3] Pullback of quasi-coherent sheaves is quasi-coherent and tensor products of quasi-coherent sheaves are quasi-coherent; exterior powers and duals of finite locally free sheaves are finite locally free, and the algebraic exterior powers are formed locally as the alternating quotient of the tensor algebra and glued by the exterior powers of the transition matrices. A locally split short exact sequence has the usual exterior-power filtration: with a line quotient its two graded pieces are $\bigwedge^jS$ and $\bigwedge^{j-1}S\otimes L$, as follows directly in a local basis ([[lem-pullback-qc-module-quasi-coherent]], [[lem-tensor-qc-modules-quasi-coherent]], [[def-exterior-algebra-of-a-finite-free-module]]).

[F4] Projection formula: for a morphism $f:X\to Y$, a quasi-coherent $\mathcal F$ on $X$ and an invertible $\mathcal L$ on $Y$, $R^qf_*(\mathcal F)\otimes\mathcal L\cong R^qf_*(\mathcal F\otimes f^*\mathcal L)$ ([[lem-projection-formula-invertible-twist]]). On a locally Noetherian scheme the higher direct images of a coherent sheaf under a proper morphism are coherent, so classes of pushforwards of coherent sheaves lie in $K_0$ ([[thm-proper-pushforward-coherent]], [[def-grothendieck-group-of-coherent-sheaves-and-vector-bundles-on-a-scheme]]).

[F5] On a regular quasi-projective scheme of finite type over a field, $K^0=K_0$ via finite locally free resolutions ([[lem-k-zero-vector-bundles-versus-coherent-sheaves]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct; resolve the diagonal of $P\times_YP$ by the Koszul complex of its defining regular section, push forward, and read generation and independence off the resulting class identity.

1.1 The diagonal section. On $P\times_YP$ use the quotient convention of [F1], with $S=\ker(q^*\mathcal E\to\mathcal O(1))$ of rank $r$. The composite $S_2\hookrightarrow q^*\mathcal E\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal O(1)_1$ is a section of $S_2^\vee\otimes\mathcal O(1)_1$; its zero scheme is the diagonal, because the first quotient then factors through the second quotient and the resulting surjection of invertible sheaves is an isomorphism. On a common standard chart where the same coordinate of both quotients is nonzero, the section has regular equations $x_i-y_i$, $1\le i\le r$. These are a regular sequence over every base ring, successively eliminating one coordinate. Such charts cover the diagonal, and off the diagonal one component of the section is a unit so the Koszul complex is contractible. The Koszul complex therefore resolves $\mathcal O_\Delta$ globally, with terms $\mathcal O(-j)_1\otimes\bigwedge^jS_2$. [F1, F3, given, algebra]

2.1 Pushing the diagonal forward. Tensor the diagonal Koszul resolution with $\operatorname{pr}_1^*\mathcal F$ for a coherent sheaf $\mathcal F$ on $P$. It remains exact: near the diagonal the equations are coordinate differences in the second factor, a regular sequence on the polynomial extension of every module from the first factor; away from the diagonal it is contractible. Its diagonal term is $\Delta_*\mathcal F$. Apply the alternating coherent pushforward along the proper projection $\operatorname{pr}_2$. On an affine open of $Y$ trivializing $\mathcal E$, the standard projective Čech complex is bounded of length $r$ and computes all higher direct images; tensoring that complex by a flat algebra commutes with its cohomology. This proves flat base change for the flat map $q$ used here. The projection formula for a locally free factor follows on trivializing opens by finite-direct-sum compatibility of cohomology, as in [F4]. Hence $[\mathcal F]=\sum_{j=0}^r(-1)^j q^*q_!(\mathcal F(-j))\otimes[\bigwedge^jS]$. The higher direct images entering $q_!$ are coherent for the proper morphism $q$ and vanish above $r$ by this Čech calculation; the identity is valid on a locally Noetherian base without a ring structure on coherent $K_0(Y)$. [F1, F3, F4, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Generation by twists. For $0\to S\to q^*\mathcal E\to\mathcal O(1)\to0$, the exterior-power filtration gives $[\bigwedge^j q^*\mathcal E]=[\bigwedge^j S]+[\bigwedge^{j-1}S][\mathcal O(1)]$. Inducting on $j$ yields $[\bigwedge^jS]=\sum_{a=0}^j(-1)^a[q^*\bigwedge^{j-a}\mathcal E][\mathcal O(a)]$. Substitute this into step 2.1 and absorb the pulled-back exterior powers into the coefficient in $K_0(Y)$ via its vector-bundle module action. Every coherent class on $P$ is therefore a sum of $q^*\beta_a\otimes[\mathcal O(a)]$, $0\le a\le r$, proving assertion 3. [F3, F4, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 The trivial bundle and the exterior product. Take $\mathcal E=\mathcal O_Y^{n+1}$, so that $P=\mathbb P^n_k\times_kY$, the map $q$ is the second projection and $q^*K_0(Y)$ is the image of the exterior product with $[\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^n}]$. By step 3.1 the group $K_0(\mathbb P^n\times_kY)$ is generated by the classes $q^*\alpha\otimes[\mathcal O(a)]$ with $\alpha\in K_0(Y)$ and $0\le a\le n$, and each such class is the exterior product of a class on $Y$ with $[\mathcal O(a)]$ on $\mathbb P^n$. Hence the exterior-product map $K_0(Y)\otimes K_0(\mathbb P^n)\to K_0(\mathbb P^n\times_kY)$ is surjective, which is assertion 2. [F3, step 3.1, algebra]

5.1 The case $Y=\operatorname{Spec}k$ and independence. Let $Y=\operatorname{Spec}k$ and $\mathcal E=k^{n+1}$, so $P=\mathbb P^n_k$. Generation by $[\mathcal O(0)],\dots,[\mathcal O(n)]$ is step 3.1, and $K^0(P)=K_0(P)$ by [F5] because $\mathbb P^n_k$ is smooth, projective and hence regular and quasi-projective. For independence suppose $\sum_{a=0}^n c_a[\mathcal O(a)]=0$ in $K_0(P)$. Pairing with the Euler characteristic $\chi(P,-\otimes\mathcal O(-j))$, for $0\le j\le n$, is additive on $K_0$, and by [F2] it sends $[\mathcal O(a)]$ to $\chi(\mathcal O(a-j))=\binom{n+a-j}{n}$ when $a\ge j$ and to $0$ when $a<j$, since then $-n\le a-j\le-1$. The resulting matrix is upper triangular with diagonal entries $\chi(\mathcal O(0))=1$, hence invertible over $\mathbb Z$, so all $c_a=0$; therefore $[\mathcal O(0)],\dots,[\mathcal O(n)]$ is a basis and $K_0(\mathbb P^n_k)\cong\mathbb Z^{n+1}$, which is assertion 1. [F2, F5, step 3.1, algebra] ∎
