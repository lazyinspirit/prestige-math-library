## Definition

Let $X$ be a set and $\mathcal E\subseteq\mathcal P(X)$. The **lambda-system
generated by $\mathcal E$** is

$$\lambda_X(\mathcal E):=\bigcap\{\mathcal D:\mathcal D\text{ is a lambda-system on }X\text{ and }\mathcal E\subseteq\mathcal D\}.$$

Write $\lambda(\mathcal E)$ when $X$ is clear. That the intersection is taken
over a nonempty family and is itself a lambda-system is proved in
[[lem-generated-lambda-system-exists-and-is-minimal]].
