## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $(R,\mathfrak m)$ be a finite-dimensional Noetherian local ring, with $d=\dim R$. Then $R$ has a system of parameters.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The Axiom of Choice ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]) and a finite-dimensional Noetherian local ring $(R,\mathfrak m)$ of dimension $d$.

[L1] Under Choice, a first parameter can be chosen in $\mathfrak m$ outside every minimal prime, and for such a choice the quotient has dimension at most $d-1$ ([[lem-parameter-selection-avoids-minimal-components]]).

[L2] A system of parameters is a $d$-tuple whose generated ideal has radical $\mathfrak m$ ([[def-system-of-parameters-and-parameter-ideal]]).

[L3] Prime ideals of a quotient correspond to primes upstairs containing the quotient ideal ([[thm-prime-spectrum-of-a-quotient-bijection]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** induction on the dimension.

1.1 If $d=0$, the local ring has no strict prime inclusion, so its maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$ is its only prime. Consequently $\sqrt{(0)}=\mathfrak m$, and the empty tuple generates $(0)$ and is a system of parameters by [L2]. [L2, base, given]

1.2 Assume $d>0$ and that the theorem is known in dimensions $<d$. By [L1], choose $x\in\mathfrak m$ outside every minimal prime. Then $R/(x)$ is a Noetherian local ring of some dimension $e\le d-1$. By the induction hypothesis, choose a system of parameters $(\bar y_1,\ldots,\bar y_e)$ in $R/(x)$ of length $e$. Lift these elements to $y_1,\ldots,y_e\in\mathfrak m$ and append $d-1-e$ zeros, obtaining a tuple $(x_2,\ldots,x_d)$ of length $d-1$. Padding does not change the ideal or its radical. [L1, L3, ih, choose]

2.1 The ideal generated by $(\bar x_2,\ldots,\bar x_d)$ has radical $\mathfrak m/(x)$ in $R/(x)$, so [L3] says the ideal $(x,x_2,\ldots,x_d)$ has radical $\mathfrak m$ in $R$. By [L2], the tuple $(x,x_2,\ldots,x_d)$ is a system of parameters. [L2, L3, step 1.2]

3.1 Therefore every finite-dimensional Noetherian local ring has a system of parameters. [step 1.1, step 2.1, discharge-induction] ∎
