At the task's 2026-08-14 design check, the GLS revision remained ongoing.  The
2025 Oberwolfach report, organized in part by GLS collaborator Inna Capdeboscq,
describes the GLS project as ongoing and nearing completion.  The current AMS
catalog meanwhile lists Number 10 as the tenth volume in a series whose aim is
to provide a complete proof, and says that this volume completes only the
bicharacteristic-type identification begun in Number 9.
