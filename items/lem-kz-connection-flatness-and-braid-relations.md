## Statement

For every integer $q\ge2$, let $\operatorname{Conf}_q(\mathbb C)$ be the space of ordered $q$-tuples of pairwise distinct complex points, and let $\widehat{\mathcal A}_{\mathbb C}(q)$ and $t_{ij}$ be as defined for parenthesized braids. Write $\sigma_i$ for the positive geometric half twist interchanging adjacent strands $i$ and $i+1$ in a disk containing no other strand. The Knizhnik–Zamolodchikov form
$$\Omega_q=\frac1{2\pi i}\sum_{1\le i<j\le q}t_{ij}\,d\log(z_i-z_j)$$
satisfies $d\Omega_q=0$ and $\Omega_q\wedge\Omega_q=0$. Consequently the degreewise iterated-integral holonomy depends only on the homotopy class of a path with fixed endpoints. On geometric braids this holonomy respects the distant commutation relations $\sigma_i\sigma_j=\sigma_j\sigma_i$ for $|i-j|\ge2$ and the adjacent braid relations $\sigma_i\sigma_{i+1}\sigma_i=\sigma_{i+1}\sigma_i\sigma_{i+1}$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** An integer $q\ge2$, the configuration space $\operatorname{Conf}_q(\mathbb C)$, the completed strand algebra $\widehat{\mathcal A}_{\mathbb C}(q)$ with its infinitesimal braid relations, and the KZ form $\Omega_q$.

[F1] [[def-kontsevich-integral-on-parenthesized-braids]]: the strand algebra is degreewise completed, is generated by $t_{ij}=t_{ji}$, and satisfies $[t_{ij},t_{kl}]=0$ for distinct indices and $[t_{ij},t_{ik}+t_{jk}]=0$ for distinct $i,j,k$; the KZ form is the displayed logarithmic connection.

[F2] [[def-framed-chord-diagram-and-four-term-relations]]: a 4T relation is the alternating four-diagram relation obtained by moving one endpoint past either endpoint of a fixed chord; the framed diagram quotient imposes 4T and does not impose 1T.

[F3] [[def-singular-knot-and-vassiliev-derivative]]: the positive and negative resolutions of a transverse crossing are labelled by the determinant crossing sign, and the positive Artin half twist is positive.

## Proof

**Given:** As above; no choice axiom is assumed.

**Proof technique:** All power-series operations are performed after truncation at a fixed degree $N$. In that finite-dimensional quotient, only finitely many iterated integrals occur; the compatible truncations define the completed holonomy.

1.1 Put $\alpha_{ij}=d\log(z_i-z_j)$. Since $z_i-z_j$ is nonzero on $\operatorname{Conf}_q(\mathbb C)$, in local coordinates $\alpha_{ij}=d(z_i-z_j)/(z_i-z_j)$ and $d\alpha_{ij}=0$ by equality of mixed partial derivatives. Thus $d\Omega_q=0$. [given, F1, algebra]

2.1 Write $a=t_{ij}$, $b=t_{kl}$ for two distinct chord generators. In the expansion of $\Omega_q\wedge\Omega_q$, the two ordered terms for these generators combine as $[a,b]\alpha_{ij}\wedge\alpha_{kl}$. If the pairs have four distinct indices, $[a,b]=0$ by [F1]; if they are the same pair, $\alpha_{ij}\wedge\alpha_{ij}=0$. Hence only pairs of generators supported on a common triple of strands can contribute. [F1, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Fix $i<j<k$ and put $a=t_{ij}$, $b=t_{ik}$, $c=t_{jk}$, $x=\alpha_{ij}$, $y=\alpha_{ik}$, and $z=\alpha_{jk}$. The tangled 4T relation on these three strands is exactly the pair of infinitesimal braid relations $[a,b+c]=0$ and $[b,a+c]=0$. The contribution of this triple is $[a,b]x\wedge y+[a,c]x\wedge z+[b,c]y\wedge z$. The infinitesimal braid relations in [F1] give $[a,c]=-[a,b]$ and $[b,c]=[a,b]$, so this is $[a,b](x\wedge y-x\wedge z+y\wedge z)$. With $u=z_i-z_j$ and $v=z_j-z_k$, we have $x=du/u$, $z=dv/v$, and $y=d(u+v)/(u+v)$. Direct expansion gives $x\wedge z+z\wedge y+y\wedge x=(1/(uv)-1/(v(u+v))-1/(u(u+v)))du\wedge dv=0$, which is equivalent to $x\wedge y-x\wedge z+y\wedge z=0$. Thus every triple contribution vanishes. [F1, F2, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 The repeated-pair, disjoint-pair, and three-index cases exhaust the expansion of $\Omega_q\wedge\Omega_q$; steps 2.1–3.1 show that each contribution is zero. Together with step 1.1 this gives $d\Omega_q=0$ and $\Omega_q\wedge\Omega_q=0$. [step 1.1, step 2.1, step 3.1, algebra]

5.1 Fix a smooth path $\gamma$ and let $U(t)$ solve $\dot U=A_tU$, $U(0)=1$, where $A_t=\Omega_q(\dot\gamma(t))$. In each degree this is a finite linear ODE system, solved recursively by the iterated-integral formula. For a homotopy $\gamma(s,t)$ with fixed endpoints, set $A_s=\Omega_q(\partial_s\gamma)$ and $W=U^{-1}(\partial_sU-A_sU)$. Differentiating and using $\partial_tU=A_tU$ gives $\partial_tW=U^{-1}(\partial_sA_t-\partial_tA_s+[A_t,A_s])U$. The coefficient in parentheses is the evaluation of the flatness expression from step 4.1, so it is zero. Since the initial endpoint is fixed, $W(s,0)=0$ and hence $W(s,t)=0$; at the fixed terminal endpoint $A_s=0$, so $\partial_sU(s,1)=0$. The holonomy is therefore constant throughout the homotopy. [given, F1, step 4.1, construct, algebra]

6.1 The KZ form is unchanged by relabeling the strands, since its sum runs over every unordered pair; endpoint strand permutations therefore act on holonomy by the same permutation of the $t_{ij}$. For $|i-j|\ge2$, the paths for $\sigma_i$ and $\sigma_j$ lie in disjoint two-point disks, so exchanging their time intervals is a fixed-endpoint homotopy and step 5.1 gives $\sigma_i\sigma_j=\sigma_j\sigma_i$. For three adjacent points, start at $(-1,0,1)$ and follow the configuration path $(-e^{\pi it},0,e^{\pi it})$, $0\le t\le1$. Its endpoints agree as unordered configurations with the three points reversed, and its pairwise distances stay bounded below by $1$. At $t=1/2$ the three real projections coincide. Small perturbations of the trajectories therefore remain collision-free and can separate the three projected crossing times in either of the two orders; both perturbed paths are homotopic to this rigid half-turn and give respectively $\sigma_i\sigma_{i+1}\sigma_i$ and $\sigma_{i+1}\sigma_i\sigma_{i+1}$. By step 5.1 their transports agree. [F1, F3, step 5.1, construct] ∎
