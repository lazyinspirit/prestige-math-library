## Definition

Let $G$ be a connected, simply connected, finite-dimensional real Lie group
with tangent Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$. The Lie algebra is nilpotent if its
lower central series $\gamma_1(\mathfrak g)=\mathfrak g$ and
$\gamma_{r+1}(\mathfrak g)=[\mathfrak g,\gamma_r(\mathfrak g)]$, where
$[A,B]$ is the linear span of brackets $[a,b]$, reaches $0$ after finitely
many steps. A filtration of degree $d\geq0$ on $G$ is a sequence of closed,
connected subgroups

$$G=G_0=G_1\supseteq G_2\supseteq\cdots\supseteq G_d\supseteq G_{d+1}=\{e\}$$

such that $[G_i,G_j]\subseteq G_{i+j}$ for all $i,j\geq0$, where
$[x,y]=xyx^{-1}y^{-1}$ and $[G_i,G_j]$ is the subgroup generated by these
commutators; set $G_k=\{e\}$ for $k>d$. The filtration degree is the least
such $d$. A lattice $\Gamma\leq G$ is a discrete subgroup whose right
quotient is compact. The quotient

$$G/\Gamma=\{g\Gamma:g\in G\}$$

is the nilmanifold associated with $G$ and $\Gamma$.
