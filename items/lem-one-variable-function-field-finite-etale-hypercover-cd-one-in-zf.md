## Statement

Let k be algebraically closed, let K/k be finitely generated of transcendence degree one, let ℓ≠char(k), and put A=Z/ℓⁿZ for n≥1. Define H^q_fhyp(Spec K,F) for an étale A-module sheaf F as the colimit of the cochain cohomology of its finite truncated étale hypercovers, under finite refinements up to simplicial homotopy. Then H^q_fhyp(Spec K,F)=0 for every q≥2, for arbitrary F. No choice principle or fixed separable closure is assumed. The statement concerns the specified finite-hypercover model and does not assert its identification with an independently constructed absolute Galois or derived étale cohomology model.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** k, K, ℓ, n, F, q and one hypercover class as in the statement.

[F1] For every finite Galois E/K, finite ℓ-primary Gal(E/K)-module M and class in H^q with q≥2, some finite Galois enlargement of E kills the class by inflation, without a choice premise. [[lem-finite-galois-prime-to-characteristic-cohomology-effacement-in-zf]]

[F2] Étale A-module sheaves satisfy the stated sheaf equalizer and finite-coefficient conventions. [[def-etale-sheaf-of-finite-modules]]

## Proof

1.1 Fix a representative cocycle on a finite hypercover H truncated through q+1. Every quasi-compact étale K-scheme in its finitely many component levels is a finite disjoint union of spectra of finite separable field extensions of K. Choose one finite Galois extension E/K splitting all those fields and the finitely many finite étale algebras occurring in the matching diagrams. These are finite choices. After base change to E, every component and matching object is a finite disjoint union of Spec E, and each matching cover is a surjection of finite sets. [F2, given, choose, algebra]

1.2 Let G=Gal(E/K). The Čech nerve C(E/K) has r-th term Spec(E^{⊗_K(r+1)}), canonically a disjoint union of |G|^r copies of Spec E. Construct a map from its truncation through q+1 to H. At degree zero choose one matching lift for its single E-component. Inductively, each nondegenerate source r-simplex gives a specified finite compatible boundary in the r-th matching object of H; the surjective finite matching map of step 1.1 provides an E-point lifting it. Images of degenerate simplices are forced by lower degrees. Only finitely many components and degrees occur, so this yields the simplicial map after finitely many choices. Pulling the original class back along it represents the same filtered-colimit class on C(E/K). [step 1.1, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 Sections of F on the Čech nerve identify with the finite group cochain complex C^r(G,F(E)); the G-action on F(E) is induced by pullback along automorphisms of E/K. The pulled-back q-cocycle takes finitely many values. Let M⊂F(E) be the A-submodule generated by all G-translates of these values. It is finite because A, G and the list are finite. It is G-stable, and the cocycle equation holds in M because M is an actual submodule of F(E). Thus the cocycle defines α∈H^q(G,M). [F2, step 1.2, given, construct, algebra]

2.2 By [F1], choose a finite Galois enlargement E'/K containing E that kills α by inflation. The restriction map F(E)→F(E') is Galois-equivariant and takes M to an ℓ-primary G-module of sections over E'. Naturality of the finite group cochain differential sends a bounding cochain for the inflated M-valued cocycle to a bounding F(E')-valued cochain. The refinement C(E'/K)→C(E/K) therefore makes the original hypercover class zero. Since its representative was arbitrary, H^q_fhyp(Spec K,F)=0 for q≥2. [F1, F2, step 2.1, given, choose, algebra] ∎

## Scope

This gives the choice-free generic-field vanishing inside the finite-hypercover model. A comparison with the ordinary étale derived stalk at a generic point, and passage from field vanishing to a proper-curve global bound, are separate statements.
