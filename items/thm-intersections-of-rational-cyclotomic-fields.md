## Statement

Let $m,n\ge1$, put $d:=\gcd(m,n)$ and $\ell:=\operatorname{lcm}(m,n)$
([[def-common-divisor-and-gcd]], [[def-lcm]]), and let $\Omega$ be a splitting
field of $t^{\ell}-1$ over $\mathbb Q$
([[thm-splitting-fields-exist-for-nonzero-polynomials]]), inside which the
cyclotomic extensions $\mathbb Q(\mu_k)$ for $k\mid\ell$ are taken
([[def-cyclotomic-extension]]). Then

$$\mathbb Q(\mu_m)\cap\mathbb Q(\mu_n)=\mathbb Q(\mu_{d}) .$$

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** Integers $m,n\ge1$ with $d=\gcd(m,n)$ and $\ell=\operatorname{lcm}(m,n)$; $\mathbb Q$ is an ordered field ([[thm-rat-ordered-field]]), so $\operatorname{char}\mathbb Q=0$ ([[def-ring-characteristic]]) and divides no positive integer ([[def-divides-in-z]]); a splitting field $\Omega$ of $t^{\ell}-1$ over $\mathbb Q$; and, for each positive divisor $k$ of $\ell$, the subfield $\mathbb Q(\mu_k):=\mathbb Q(\mu_k(\Omega))$ of $\Omega$. Write $I:=\mathbb Q(\mu_m)\cap\mathbb Q(\mu_n)$.

[L1] For a positive divisor $k$ of $\ell$, the subfield $\mathbb Q(\mu_k(\Omega))$ generated by the $k$-th roots of unity in $\Omega$ is a cyclotomic extension of $\mathbb Q$ of order $k$ ([[def-cyclotomic-extension]], [[def-roots-of-unity-in-a-field]]); because $\operatorname{char}\mathbb Q=0$ divides no positive integer, [[thm-separability-of-x-n-minus-one-and-the-order-of-the-group-of-roots-of-unity]] gives $\mu_k(\Omega)$ cyclic of order $k$, with exactly $\varphi(k)$ primitive $k$-th roots of unity.

[L2] $[\mathbb Q(\mu_k):\mathbb Q]=\varphi(k)$ for every $k\ge1$ ([[cor-the-galois-group-of-a-rational-cyclotomic-field]], [[def-unit-group-modulo-n-and-euler-totient]], [[def-extension-degree-and-finite-extension]]).

[L3] $\mathbb Q(\mu_k)/\mathbb Q$ is finite Galois ([[thm-the-cyclotomic-galois-group-embeds-in-the-unit-group-modulo-n]]).

[L4] For $E/F$ finite Galois and $L/F$ finite inside a common field, $[EL:F]=[E:F][L:F]/[E\cap L:F]$ ([[lem-degree-of-a-compositum-with-a-galois-extension]]).

[L5] $\mathbb Q(\mu_m)\mathbb Q(\mu_n)=\mathbb Q(\mu_{\ell})$ inside $\Omega$ ([[thm-composita-of-cyclotomic-extensions]]).

[L6] $\varphi(m)\varphi(n)=\varphi(d)\varphi(\ell)$ ([[lem-the-totient-of-a-gcd-and-of-a-least-common-multiple]]).

[L7] For fields $F\subseteq K\subseteq M$ with $K/F$ and $M/K$ finite, $[M:F]=[M:K][K:F]$ ([[thm-tower-law-for-finite-field-extensions]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 $d$ divides $m$ and $n$, and $m,n,d$ all divide $\ell$. For each positive divisor $k$ of $\ell$, write $\ell=kq$; then $t^{\ell}-1=(t^{k})^{q}-1=(t^{k}-1)(t^{k(q-1)}+\cdots+t^{k}+1)$, so $t^{k}-1$ splits over the splitting field $\Omega$ of $t^{\ell}-1$, and [L1] applies to $k$. In particular all four cyclotomic extensions for $k=m,n,d,\ell$ sit inside $\Omega$. [L1, given, algebra]

2.1 For the inclusion $\mathbb Q(\mu_d)\subseteq I$: since $d\mid m$, every $x$ with $x^{d}=1$ satisfies $x^{m}=1$, so $\mu_d(\Omega)\subseteq\mu_m(\Omega)$ and hence $\mathbb Q(\mu_d)\subseteq\mathbb Q(\mu_m)$; the same argument with $n$ gives $\mathbb Q(\mu_d)\subseteq\mathbb Q(\mu_n)$. [step 1.1, given, algebra]

2.2 By [L3] the extension $\mathbb Q(\mu_m)/\mathbb Q$ is finite Galois and $\mathbb Q(\mu_n)/\mathbb Q$ is finite, both inside $\Omega$, so [L4] and [L5] give $\varphi(\ell)=[\mathbb Q(\mu_{\ell}):\mathbb Q]=[\mathbb Q(\mu_m)\mathbb Q(\mu_n):\mathbb Q]=\varphi(m)\varphi(n)/[I:\mathbb Q]$, using [L2] twice. [step 1.1, L2, L3, L4, L5]

3.1 Hence $[I:\mathbb Q]=\varphi(m)\varphi(n)/\varphi(\ell)=\varphi(d)$ by [L6]. [step 2.2, L6]

4.1 By step 2.1 the tower $\mathbb Q\subseteq\mathbb Q(\mu_d)\subseteq I$ is defined, and [L7] with [L2] gives $\varphi(d)=[I:\mathbb Q]=[I:\mathbb Q(\mu_d)]\,\varphi(d)$, so $[I:\mathbb Q(\mu_d)]=1$ and $I=\mathbb Q(\mu_d)$. [step 2.1, step 3.1, L2, L7] ∎

## Remarks

- **Where the base field is used.** Only through [L2]: the equality $[\mathbb Q(\mu_k):\mathbb Q]=\varphi(k)$ for every $k$, which is irreducibility of $\Phi_k$ over $\mathbb Q$. Over a base field where some $\Phi_k$ becomes reducible the degrees drop unevenly and the degree count in step 2.2 no longer forces the intersection down to $\mathbb Q(\mu_d)$.

- **The base field really matters.** Over a general base field the same formula can fail; the companion page gives a finite-field witness in [[cex-an-intersection-of-cyclotomic-extensions-larger-than-the-gcd-case]].
