## Example

Assume AC, AC$_\omega$, and DC. Let $k=\mathbb F_q(t)$ with $q=p^f$, let $v_\infty$ be the degree-one place at infinity, and let $K_0/k$ be a geometric abelian Galois extension with $\operatorname{Gal}(K_0/k)\cong\mathbb Z_p^{\mathbb N}$, exact constant field $\mathbb F_q$, unramified outside $v_\infty$, and totally ramified at $v_\infty$ in the sense that its decomposition and inertia subgroups there are both the whole group. Define the constant-field tower $k_{\mathrm{const},\infty}=\bigcup_{n\ge1}\mathbb F_{q^{p^n}}(t)$ and assume $K_0\cap k_{\mathrm{const},\infty}=k$. Put $K=K_0k_{\mathrm{const},\infty}$ and $\Sigma=\{v_\infty\}$. Then $\operatorname{Gal}(K/k)\cong\mathbb Z_p^{\mathbb N}\times\mathbb Z_p\cong\mathbb Z_p^{\mathbb N}$, $\Sigma$ contains all ramified places and no place in $\Sigma$ splits completely, and the Burns–Daoud–Liang decomposition hypothesis holds with $n_0=1$. Thus this compositum is a conditional input of the infinite-rank function-field theorem; the constant-field $\mathbb Z_p$-tower supplies one coordinate factor.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The finite field $\mathbb F_q$, the tower $K_0/k$ and all hypotheses in the Example, AC for the place interface, Countable Choice for the local-extension interface, and DC as required by the main theorem input.

[F1] [[thm-extensions-of-finite-fields-are-galois-with-cyclic-frobenius-group]]: for every $n\ge1$, $\mathbb F_{q^{p^n}}/\mathbb F_q$ is cyclic Galois of degree $p^n$, generated by arithmetic Frobenius $x\mapsto x^q$.

[F2] [[thm-finite-unramified-local-extensions-classified-by-residue-extensions]]: a finite unramified local extension is determined by its finite residue-field extension, and its Galois group reduces isomorphically to the residue-field Galois group.

[F3] [[def-burns-daoud-liang-decomposition-hypothesis]]: the BDL condition asks that $\Gamma(n_0)\cap\Gamma_v$ be non-open in $\Gamma_v$ for every $v\in\Sigma$.

[F4] [[thm-burns-daoud-liang-function-field-iwasawa-module-structure]]: the theorem's input requires the finite-ramification, non-splitting, infinite-rank, and decomposition hypotheses listed in its Statement; its proof remains escalated.

[F5] [[def-global-field]]: a function field has a finite exact constant field and is a one-variable function field over it.

[F6] [[def-place-of-a-global-field]]: places of a function field correspond to closed points on its smooth proper curve model; the place at infinity of $\mathbb F_q(t)$ is degree one.

[F7] [[def-infinite-rank-p-adic-iwasawa-algebra]]: the countable product coordinates and finite factor identify the group of the compositum with the completed Iwasawa algebra's $\mathbb Z_p^{\mathbb N}\times G$ interface.

[A1] $K_0/k$ is assumed geometric, countably infinite-rank abelian, unramified outside $v_\infty$, and totally ramified at $v_\infty$.

[A2] AC is assumed by the place/curve interfaces, AC$_\omega$ by the finite unramified-local-extension interface, and DC is retained as a required hypothesis of the main theorem input. ([[def-axiom-of-choice]], [[def-countable-choice]], [[def-dependent-choice]])

[L1] A concrete $K_0$ satisfying [A1] is not constructed in this pair. Burns–Daoud–Liang Remark 3.8 names a Carlitz–Hayes cyclotomic tower, but this pair has not reconstructed its Galois group, constant field, and ramification properties locally.

## Verification

**Given:** The data in the Example and [F1]–[F7], with the conditional base-tower input [A1] and inherited assumptions [A2].

1.1 By [F1], the finite constant extensions form a nested tower with Galois groups $\mathbb Z/p^n\mathbb Z$ generated by arithmetic Frobenius. Taking the inverse limit gives $\operatorname{Gal}(k_{\mathrm{const},\infty}/k)\cong\mathbb Z_p$. [F1, given, algebra]

1.2 Every finite constant extension is separable by [F1]. At any place of $\mathbb F_q(t)$, extension of constants leaves a local uniformizer of valuation one and extends the finite residue field, so it is unramified. At a place other than $v_\infty$, each finite subextension of $K_0/k$ is also unramified by [A1]. The compositum of two finite unramified local extensions is unramified: their residue fields have a finite compositum, and [F2] identifies the unramified extension with that residue extension. Thus $K/k$ is ramified only at $v_\infty$, so $\Sigma$ is finite, nonempty, and contains all ramified places. [A1, F1, F2, F5, F6, given]

2.1 Since $K_0$ has exact constant field $\mathbb F_q$, its intersection with every finite constant extension is $k$; hence it is linearly disjoint from $k_{\mathrm{const},\infty}$. The Galois group of the compositum is therefore $\operatorname{Gal}(K_0/k)\times\mathbb Z_p$, which is isomorphic to $\mathbb Z_p^{\mathbb N}$ by reindexing the countably infinite coordinates. [A1, F7, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 The degree of $v_\infty$ is one by [F6], so its arithmetic Frobenius in the constant-field factor is a topological generator of $\mathbb Z_p$. Total ramification of $K_0/k$ at $v_\infty$ supplies the whole $\operatorname{Gal}(K_0/k)$ as inertia. The two factors are linearly disjoint by step 2.1, so the decomposition subgroup at $v_\infty$ is all of $\Gamma$; hence $v_\infty$ does not split completely. [A1, F6, step 1.1, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 Choose coordinates with the constant-field factor first. Then $\Gamma_v=\Gamma$ and $\Gamma(1)$ is the kernel of projection onto that first $\mathbb Z_p$ coordinate by [F3], so $\Gamma/\Gamma(1)\cong\mathbb Z_p$ is infinite. A closed subgroup of a compact group is open only if its quotient is finite; therefore $\Gamma(1)$ is not open in $\Gamma_v$. The BDL decomposition hypothesis holds with $n_0=1$, so all stated input conditions are verified, conditional on [A1]. [F3, F4, step 2.1, step 3.1, algebra] ∎
