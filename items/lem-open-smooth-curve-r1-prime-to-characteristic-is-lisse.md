## Statement

Assume AC and DC. Let $k$ be algebraically closed, $\ell\ne\operatorname{char}k$, $r\geq1$, and $n=\ell^r$. Let $S$ be smooth of finite type over $k$, let $\bar f:\overline X\to S$ be a smooth projective curve with geometrically connected nonempty fibres, and let $i:D\hookrightarrow\overline X$ be finite étale and surjective over $S$. Put $j:X=\overline X\setminus D\hookrightarrow\overline X$ and $f=\bar f j$. Then $R^1f_*\mu_n$ is a finite locally constant $\mathbb Z/n$-module sheaf on $S$, and its formation commutes with every smooth base change by a finite-type $k$-scheme. This result does not assert vanishing of $R^2f_*\mu_n$ or of $R^qj_*\mu_n$ for $q>1$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The field, prime power, smooth base, smooth projective connected curve and finite étale surjective boundary in the Statement.

[A1] AC and DC are assumed. [[def-axiom-of-choice]] [[def-dependent-choice]]

[F1] Every regular local ring is a unique factorization domain. [[thm-nonaffine-regular-local-ring-is-ufd]]

[F2] Proper torsion cohomology commutes with arbitrary base change. [[lem-proper-torsion-base-change-under-choice]]

[F3] Generic-point Kummer cohomology and its valuation sequence apply to smooth curves over algebraically closed fields. [[lem-generic-point-leray-and-kummer-curve-cohomology-in-zf]]

[F4] The degree-zero Picard group of a smooth projective connected curve is the group of points of its Jacobian, an abelian variety. [[thm-picard-zero-represented-by-jacobian]]

[F5] Multiplication by $n$ on an abelian variety is finite flat and surjective, and étale when $n$ is invertible. [[lem-multiplication-by-n-on-abelian-scheme]]

[F6] The first direct image of a constant prime-to-characteristic module under a smooth proper connected curve family is finite locally constant and commutes with base change. [[lem-smooth-proper-curve-r1-prime-to-characteristic-is-lisse]]

[F7] Grothendieck abelian sheaf categories have injective resolutions under AC. [[thm-a-grothendieck-abelian-category-has-functorial-injective-embeddings]]

## Proof

**Proof technique:** compute the first boundary residue on strict local rings and use the degree map.

1.1 At a geometric point on $D$, let $R$ be the strict henselization of the local ring of $\overline X$. It is a regular local ring and $D$ has one local equation $t$ which is part of a regular parameter system. By [F1], $R$ is a UFD, so $R[1/t]$ is a UFD and has zero Picard group. The Kummer sequence gives $H^1(R[1/t],\mu_n)=R[1/t]^\times/R[1/t]^{\times n}$. Every unit of $R[1/t]$ is a unit of $R$ times a power of $t$; every unit of $R$ has an $n$th root by Hensel's lemma, because $n$ is invertible and the residue field is separably closed. Thus the stalk is $\mathbb Z/n$, generated by the Kummer class of $t$. Changing $t$ by a unit changes no class, so these stalk calculations glue to $R^1j_*\mu_n\cong i_*\underline{\mathbb Z/n}$. Off $D$ the higher stalk is zero, and the same connectedness of the punctured regular local spectrum gives $j_*\mu_n=\mu_n$. These constructions are compatible with smooth base change. [A1, F1, given, choose, construct, algebra]

1.2 Put $d=\bar f i:D\to S$. Proper base change [F2] identifies the geometric stalks of $R^2\bar f_*\mu_n$ with the degree-two cohomology of the smooth projective fibres. By [F3], that group is the Picard group modulo $n$; by [F4]–[F5] its degree-zero subgroup is $n$-divisible, so degree gives $\mathbb Z/n$. Étale-locally on $S$ choose a section of $\bar f$; its divisor class supplies a generator on every geometric fibre. Two such choices give the same generator because their difference has degree zero and is $n$-divisible. Hence $R^2\bar f_*\mu_n$ is canonically the constant $\mathbb Z/n$ sheaf, compatibly with smooth base change. The proper $R^1\bar f_*\mu_n$ is finite locally constant by [F6], after choosing a primitive $n$th root to identify $\mu_n$ with the constant module. [A1, F2, F3, F4, F5, F6, given, choose, construct, algebra]

2.1 The low-degree Leray sequence for $f=\bar f j$, constructed from the injective resolutions in [F7], gives an exact sequence $0\to R^1\bar f_*\mu_n\to R^1f_*\mu_n\to d_*\underline{\mathbb Z/n}\to R^2\bar f_*\mu_n$. The last map is the sum of the local boundary coefficients: the Kummer class of a local parameter maps to the first Chern class of its Cartier divisor, whose degree on each fibre is one. Since $d$ is finite étale surjective, its pushforward is locally a finite direct sum and this sum map is surjective. Its kernel is finite locally constant. An extension of finite locally constant finite modules is finite locally constant: étale-locally trivialize the ends and lift finitely many generators and relations from a stalk. The exact sequence and its end terms are natural under smooth base change by steps 1.1–1.2, so the middle base-change map is an isomorphism by exactness. No higher $R^qj_*$ term was used. [A1, F2, F7, step 1.1, step 1.2, construct, algebra] ∎
