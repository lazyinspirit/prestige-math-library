## Statement

> **False.** The composition factors determine a finite group up to isomorphism.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The cyclic group $C_4$ and the direct product $C_2\times C_2$.

[F1] Composition factors are the simple quotients in a composition series ([[def-composition-series-composition-factors-and-length]]).

[L1] Any two composition series of the same group have equal length and factors agreeing up to isomorphism and permutation ([[thm-jordan-holder-theorem-for-groups]]).

[L2] Direct products use coordinatewise multiplication ([[thm-external-direct-product-is-a-group]]).

[L3] A cyclic group generated by an element of finite order $n$ is isomorphic to $\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z$ ([[thm-classification-of-cyclic-groups]]).

## Refutation

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 The chain $C_4>C_2>1$ is a composition series with factors $C_2,C_2$, by [F1] and [L3]. [F1, L3]

1.2 The chain $C_2\times C_2>C_2\times1>1$ is also a composition series with factors $C_2,C_2$, by [F1] and [L2]. [F1, L2]

1.3 The group $C_4$ has an element of order four, while every nonidentity element of $C_2\times C_2$ has order two by coordinatewise multiplication; hence the groups are not isomorphic. [given, L2, L3]

2.1 Thus two nonisomorphic finite groups have the same composition factors, refuting the statement without contradicting [L1], which compares two series of one group rather than different groups. [step 1.1, step 1.2, step 1.3, L1] ∎
