## Definition

Let $k\subseteq K$ satisfy $K=k(a_1,\ldots,a_n)$ for a possibly empty finite tuple $a_i\in K$. Assume every element of $K$ algebraic over $k$ belongs to $k$. Assume also there is a possibly empty finite tuple $t_1,\ldots,t_r\in K$ such that no nonzero polynomial in $k[T_1,\ldots,T_r]$ vanishes at the tuple, and the subfield $F=k(t_1,\ldots,t_r)$ generated by $k$ and these elements satisfies $[K:F]<\infty$ and every element of $K$ has a minimal polynomial over $F$ with no repeated root. These conditions define a finitely generated regular extension $K/k$. Let $A$ be an abelian variety over $K$. A **$K/k$-trace pair** for $A$ is an abelian variety $T/k$ together with a homomorphism $\tau:T_K\to A$ such that, for every abelian variety $C/k$, composition with $\tau$ gives a bijection
$$\operatorname{Hom}_k(C,T)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Hom}_K(C_K,A),\qquad f\longmapsto\tau\circ f_K.$$
Here $C_K=C\times_{\operatorname{Spec}k}\operatorname{Spec}K$, and a homomorphism is a morphism of group varieties preserving the identity. When a trace pair exists, its universal property determines it up to a unique isomorphism compatible with $\tau$; its abelian variety is called the **$K/k$-trace** of $A$, denoted $\operatorname{Tr}_{K/k}(A)$, and its universal homomorphism is denoted $\tau_{K/k}$.

## Diagram

The triangle records the universal property: a given homomorphism $g:C_K\to A$ factors uniquely as the base change $f_K:C_K\to T_K$ followed by the trace homomorphism $\tau$.

```tikzcd
C_K \arrow[r,"f_K"] \arrow[dr,"g"'] & T_K \arrow[d,"\tau"]\\
& A
```
