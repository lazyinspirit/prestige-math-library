## Definition

Let $(X,d)$ be a compact metric space. Write $\mathcal B(X)$ for the sigma-algebra generated by the open sets of its metric topology. A Borel probability measure on $X$ is a countably additive measure on $\mathcal B(X)$ with total mass one. For Borel probability measures $\mu,\nu$ on $X$, let $\Pi_X(\mu,\nu)$ be the set of Borel probability measures $\gamma$ on $X\times X$, with its product topology and Borel sigma-algebra, such that for every $A\in\mathcal B(X)$,
$$\gamma(A\times X)=\mu(A),\qquad \gamma(X\times A)=\nu(A).$$
Define
$$W_1^X(\mu,\nu):=\inf_{\gamma\in\Pi_X(\mu,\nu)}\int_{X\times X}d(x,y)\,d\gamma(x,y)\in[0,+\infty],$$
where the infimum is taken in the extended real line and $\inf\varnothing=+\infty$. The integrand is nonnegative and Borel: the triangle inequality and symmetry give
$$|d(x,y)-d(x',y')|\le d(x,x')+d(y,y'),$$
so $(x,y)\mapsto d(x,y)$ is continuous for the product topology; nonnegativity follows from the metric axioms.
