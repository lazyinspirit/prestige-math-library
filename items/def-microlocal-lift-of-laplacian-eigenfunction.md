## Definition

Let Y be a finite-area hyperbolic surface, hⱼ→0, and φⱼ∈L²(Y) be normalized eigenfunctions satisfying −hⱼ²Δφⱼ=φⱼ. Fix a semiclassical quantization, meaning an assignment a↦Op_h(a) from smooth compactly supported functions on T*Y to operators with principal symbol a. A microlocal measure of the sequence is a positive Radon subprobability measure μ on the unit cotangent bundle S*Y such that, for every such symbol a, the matrix coefficients ⟨Op_{hⱼ}(a)φⱼ,φⱼ⟩ converge to ∫_{S*Y}a dμ along the selected subsequence. On a compact surface the term microlocal limit requires total mass one; on a noncompact surface total mass can be lost at infinity. A microlocal limit is such a limit; its projection to Y is the corresponding weak limit of |φⱼ|²dvol. Positive finite-index microlocal lifts, when used to realize these limits, are probability measures. Their construction, positivity, geodesic-flow invariance and compatibility with Hecke operators require separate theorems; principal-symbol data alone do not assert those properties.
