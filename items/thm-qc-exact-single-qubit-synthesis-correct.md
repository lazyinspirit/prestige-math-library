## Statement

For every unitary $U\in M_2(D)$, the procedure in
[[alg-qc-exact-single-qubit-clifford-t-synthesis]] terminates and returns a
finite one-qubit Clifford+T word whose exact matrix is $U$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A unitary $U\in M_2(D)$, exact rational coefficient arithmetic,
the descent procedure, and the finite base-column certificate.

[F1] [[alg-qc-exact-single-qubit-clifford-t-synthesis]]: the procedure tests
unitarity, repeatedly applies $M\leftarrow HT^{-k}M$ for a $k$ lowering the
squared-entry denominator exponent, looks up a base-column word $W_0$, and
returns the matrix factorization determined by the recorded $k$ values and
$W_0T^j$.

[F2] [[lem-qc-clifford-t-denominator-descent-and-base]]: each normalized
$D$-column above exponent $3$ has a reducing $k\in\{0,1,2,3\}$, and the
certificate supplies an exact H/T preparation word for every normalized
column in the base regime.

[F3] [[lem-qc-dyadic-cyclotomic-unit-circle]]: if $\lambda\in D$ and
$\lambda\bar\lambda=1$, then $\lambda=\omega^j$ for some $j\in\{0,\ldots,7\}$.

[F4] [[def-qc-denominator-exponent]]: the squared-entry statistic is
$\operatorname{sde}(|M_{00}|^2)$, with $\operatorname{sde}(0)=-\infty$.

[F5] [[def-qc-clifford-t-gate-library]]: $H,T,T^\dagger$ have the displayed
exact matrices, and the matrix of a chronological word is the product with
later gates on the left.

[F6] [[def-qc-circuit-denotational-semantics]]: for chronological layers
$L_1,\ldots,L_d$, the circuit matrix is $U_{L_d}\cdots U_{L_1}$.

[F7] [[lem-qc-hadamard-reduced-word-denominators]]: the final reduction preserves exact matrix equality, retaining its scalar phase.

## Proof


1.1 Initially $U=I\cdot M$ with $M=U$. If a loop iteration records $k$ and sets $M'=HT^{-k}M$, then $M=T^kHM'$. Therefore, after $r$ reductions, the recorded sequence $K=(k_1,\ldots,k_r)$ satisfies $U=(T^{k_1}H)\cdots(T^{k_r}H)M$. [F1, F5, given, algebra]

2.1 At every iteration, $M$ remains unitary with entries in $D$, since the matrices in [F5] are unitary over $D$. If its exponent exceeds $3$, [F2] ensures that the finite search over $k=0,1,2,3$ finds a value reducing that integer exponent by exactly one. Thus the loop terminates after finitely many iterations with exponent at most $3$. [F2, F4, F5, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Let $W_0$ be the table word for the first column of the terminal $M$. Then $W_0^*M$ is unitary with first column $(1,0)^T$, hence is $\operatorname{diag}(1,\lambda)$ for a unit scalar $\lambda$. Its entries lie in $D$, so [F3] gives $\lambda=\omega^j$ for some $j\in\{0,\ldots,7\}$. Consequently $M=W_0T^j$. [F1, F2, F3, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 Substituting this identity into step 1.1 yields exactly the matrix factorization returned in [F1]. The algorithm emits its factors from right to left as a chronological gate list; [F6] says later gates multiply on the left, so that list has the displayed matrix. The final reduction preserves this matrix by [F7], including its scalar factor. Hence the returned word implements $U$ exactly. [F1, F6, F7, step 1.1, step 3.1, algebra] ∎
