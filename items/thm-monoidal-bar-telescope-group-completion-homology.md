## Statement

Let S be a small strict symmetric monoidal GROUPOID, with unit0 and sum⊕, whose translations are faithful: Aut(a)→Aut(a⊕t) is injective for every a,t. Let S act on a small category X, with specified associativity, unit and symmetry-induced interchange maps. Put M=pi0S, a commutative monoid. Define S^-1X by the actual representative formulas below. For every commutative unital coefficient ring k, the inclusion X→S^-1X induces

$$M^{-1}H_q(BX;k)\ \xrightarrow{\cong}\ H_q(B(S^{-1}X);k)$$

for every q and every k, where M acts by the given translations on X. For X=S, pi0 B(S^-1S) is the group completion of M, and BS→B(S^-1S) is a homotopy-commutative H-space group completion in the precise sense of that component calculation and homology localization. No choice axiom is used. The homology isomorphism for X=S respects the Pontryagin products. For the groupoid of finite projective modules over any unital associative ring R, the explicit stable matrix telescope maps to the identity component of the localized groupoid by an isomorphism on homology with every commutative coefficient ring. The label-forgetting telescope map to the classifying space of the stable general linear group has the explicitly constructed based homotopy inverse described in the proof.

The associator gives equality of the resulting representative classes; in the strict model the displayed sum is literal. The class[0,id] is identity.  Let E=S^-1X have objects(t,x). Conversely each X-arrow gives that class.  For ONE base arrow f=[a,alpha]:t→t' and one x, a lift is  $$(t,x)\longrightarrow(t',a\cdot x),\qquad[a,alpha,id].$$  It is opcartesian. Equivalently the inclusion E_t→(p/t) of its fiber in the comma category has a UNIVERSAL ARROW from each comma object to some fiber object.
