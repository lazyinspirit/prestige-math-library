## Statement

Assume AC, Countable Choice and DC. Let $K$ be a number field, let $n\ge1$
with $|\mu_n(K)|=n$, and let $a,b\in K^\times$. At a finite place
$v$, set
$$H_{v,n}(a,b)=\operatorname{rec}_{K_v}(b)(\alpha_v)/\alpha_v,
\qquad \alpha_v^n=a.$$
At a complex place put $H_{v,n}=1$; at a real place put $H_{v,1}=1$,
and for $n=2$ put $H_{v,2}(a,b)=-1$ exactly when $a<0$ and $b<0$, and
$1$ otherwise. Then the finite product of local factors satisfies
$$\prod_v H_{v,n}(a,b)=1.$$

## Proof

**Proof technique:** apply global Artin reciprocity to the principal idèle
generated by $b$ in the Kummer extension $K(\sqrt[n]{a})/K$.

**Given:** The data and hypotheses in the Statement.

1.1 Choose $\alpha\in\overline K$ with $\alpha^n=a$, and put
$E=K(\alpha)$. Because $\mu_n\subseteq K$, every root of $X^n-a$ lies
in $E$, so $E/K$ is finite abelian. The finite-support statement [F2]
ensures the local reciprocity factors acting on $\alpha$ form a finite
product. [F2, given, construct]

2.1 Regard $b$ as a principal idèle. By [F3], its global Artin
image in $\operatorname{Gal}(E/K)$ is the product of the local Artin
images and is the identity. At each finite $v$, the action on $\alpha$
has ratio $\operatorname{rec}_{K_v}(b)(\alpha_v)/\alpha_v=H_{v,n}(a,b)$;
the archimedean components are exactly the stated factors. Therefore the
product of those ratios is $1$. [F1, F2, F3, L1, step 1.1]

3.1 Step 2.1 proves the displayed formula. The exact principal-idèle and local Artin interfaces used there are supplied at the public choice hypotheses, as recorded in [L1], so the former dependency-status hold is removed. [L1, step 2.1] ∎
