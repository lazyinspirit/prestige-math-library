## Statement

Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field, let $X$ be an integral finite-type $k$-scheme of dimension at most $d$, and let $A_{d-1}(X)$ be the abelian group of finite integral linear combinations of $(d-1)$-dimensional integral closed subvarieties, modulo algebraic equivalence. Algebraic equivalence is generated by differences of fibers over $k$-points of flat families of cycles parametrized by smooth connected curves over $k$. Then $A_{d-1}(X)$ is a finitely generated abelian group.
