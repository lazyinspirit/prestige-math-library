## Statement

For finite Galois L/K and nonzero $P\mid p$, writing e and f for its ramification index and residue degree,
$$|D(P/p)|=ef,\qquad |I(P/p)|=e,\qquad |D(P/p)/I(P/p)|=f.$$
The prime P is unramified over p if and only if its inertia group is trivial.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The data and hypotheses of the statement.

[F1] [[thm-decomposition-inertia-exact-sequence]]: For finite Galois L/K and fixed nonzero $P\mid p$, reduction gives the exact sequence $1\longrightarrow I(P/p)\longrightarrow D(P/p)\longrightarrow \operatorname{Gal}(\kappa(P)/\kappa(p))\longrightarrow1.$ In particular $D(P/p)/I(P/p)$ is canonically the residue Galois group.

[F2] In a finite Galois extension the Galois group acts transitively on the $g$ primes above $p$, the stabilizer of $P$ is $D(P/p)$, and orbit-stabilizer gives $|D(P/p)|=|G|/g$ ([[thm-galois-action-on-primes-above-a-prime-is-transitive]], [[thm-orbit-stabilizer]]).

[F3] For the $g$ primes above $p$, the common ramification index and residue degree satisfy $efg=[L:K]=|G|$ ([[cor-galois-prime-decomposition-efg]]).

[F4] [[thm-extensions-of-finite-fields-are-galois-with-cyclic-frobenius-group]]: Let $\mathbb F_q$ be a finite field of order $q$ and let $E$ be a finite field having $\mathbb F_q$ as a subfield, with $[E:\mathbb F_q]=n$ (def-extension-degree-and-finite-extension). Then $E/\mathbb F_q$ is a finite Galois extension (def-finite-galois-extension-and-galois-group) and $\operatorname{Gal}(E/\mathbb F_q)=\langle\sigma_q\rangle$ is cyclic of order $n$, generated by the relative Frobenius $\sigma_q\colon x\mapsto x^{q}$ (def-relative-frobenius-of-a-finite-field-extension).

## Proof

1.1 By [F2] and [F3], $|D|=|G|/g=ef$. The residue extension has cyclic Galois group of order f, so the exact sequence gives $|D/I|=f$ and $|I|=|D|/f=e$. [F1, F2, F3, F4]

2.1 Finite residue extensions are separable. Thus here unramified means e=1, which implies $|I|=1$ and I trivial. Conversely trivial I has order one, so e=1 and the prime is unramified. [F4, step 1.1] ∎
