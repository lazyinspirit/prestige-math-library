## Statement

Let $C_0$ be a stable marked nodal curve with the disk-component and boundary-label conventions of def-gts-stable-j-holomorphic-disks-with-boundary-markings. Use either its smooth-boundary branch with a continuous smooth trace bundle, or the explicit weighted corner extension specified below. The latter is a corrected moduli problem, not an assertion that the original everywhere-smooth ordinary-Sobolev corner interface already defined it. It is supplied with an actual smooth local universal plumbing family, one complex coordinate $q_e$ at each interior node ($zw=q_e$), one real nonnegative smoothing parameter at each boundary node, smooth remaining parameters, and fixed cylindrical ends. Write $\mathcal B$ for this finite-dimensional local domain-parameter space and $d_{\mathcal B}=\dim_{\mathbb R}\mathcal B$. Let $X$ be a compact smooth tame almost-complex target of real dimension $2m$, and let $u_0:C_0\to X$ be the nodal solution. On each disk boundary arc with label $L_j$, sections have boundary trace in $u_0^*TL_j$.

For some fixed $k\ge6$, the smooth-boundary branch uses finite-chart $H^k$ spaces on each compact normalization component. In the corner branch supply the fixed-asymptote or prescribed clean moving-asymptote data, positive admissible spectral cuts, capping matrices and weighted spaces of def-gts-capped-weighted-label-changing-corner-data at every genuine label-changing mark. Arbitrary embedded label pairs are allowed at a fixed asymptote; clean intersection is required only for the claimed unrestricted smooth moving-asymptote chart. An explicitly prescribed smooth submanifold of the intersection is allowed as a declared restriction. All corner marks remain separate from nodes, persist without collision or relabeling, and have uniform spectral/coefficient bounds on the local family. Matching at nodes still means equality of limiting values. The corner topology is the weighted topology on persistent corner ends, together with the original graph topology away from those ends and the given finite domain/asymptote/forcing coordinates. It does not assert automatic positive exponential decay of every finite-energy or graph-near solution. Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real obstruction space and $\lambda(e)$ a smooth linear equivariant forcing supported away from nodes. Impose $\ell$ fixed core incidence constraints $u(p_i)\in D_i$ at distinct smooth-core marked points, where each $D_i\subset X$ is a smooth real codimension-two submanifold met transversely by $u_0$. Temporary transverse intersections used only to stabilize components are separate data and are smoothly recovered. Dependence on these recovered intersections is allowed. At $(u_0,e_0)$ assume its actual augmented vertical linearization, on matched sections satisfying the boundary and fixed incidence conditions together with $E'\subset E$, is onto. The fixed incidence conditions have codimension $2\ell$.

Let $P_v$ denote the real Cauchy--Riemann operator on each normalization component $C_v$ with its stated boundary condition, and let $D_{\rm match}$ be their direct sum restricted to sections whose values match at all nodes. If $N_{\rm int}$ and $N_{\rm bdry}$ are the numbers of interior and boundary nodes, respectively, its index is
$$\operatorname{ind}_{\mathbb R}D_{\rm match}=\sum_v\operatorname{ind}_{\mathbb R}P_v-2mN_{\rm int}-mN_{\rm bdry}.$$
For a closed component, $\operatorname{ind}_{\mathbb R}P_v=2m(1-g_v)+2\langle c_1(u_0^*TX),[C_v]\rangle$; for a smooth-boundary disk component it is $m\chi(C_v)+\mu(u_0^*TX,u_0^*TL)$, with the smooth-pair conjugate-double degree. For a component with corners it is $m\chi(C_v)+\mu_{v,\delta}+c_v$, where $\mu_{v,\delta}$ is the degree of the explicitly capped smooth pair and $c_v$ is the real dimension of its prescribed moving-asymptote parameters. Fixed asymptotes contribute zero to $c_v$. Here $P_v$ includes these asymptote tangent variables. No conjugate double of discontinuous old trace planes is used.

Then the thickened regular solution locus is open in the stated branch topology and is locally a product of the given domain base with a finite-dimensional kernel ball, with local coordinates consisting of the domain parameters and the kernel of that actual augmented derivative. Its dimension is
$$d_{\mathcal B}+\operatorname{ind}_{\mathbb R}D_{\rm match}+\dim E-2\ell.$$
Projection to the domain base and $E/E'$ is a topological submersion. The actual weighted residual, uniformly bounded right inverses, quadratic estimate and inverse-gluing statements are proved below; none is an additional hypothesis. The construction supplies determinant-line exact-sequence isomorphisms. It does not orient bordered determinant lines; coherent orientations require additional boundary orientation data and are treated separately. No differentiability in $q_e$ at zero is asserted.

The linear version holds for a complex bundle on the given plumbing family with smooth core transition functions and fixed holomorphic matching frames on the node fixtures: if its central matched Cauchy--Riemann operator is onto, the construction gives uniformly bounded right inverses and a continuous basis of holomorphic kernels through all partial nodal strata. In the smooth branch these are ordinary smooth sections; in the corner branch they are smooth away from the marks and belong to the declared weighted spaces, with the capping comparison and asymptote parameters just specified. This branch does not require a compact total-space target. The theorem assumes the specified family, not a compactification or atlas existence theorem.
