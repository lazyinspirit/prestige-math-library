The published theorem [[thm-hom-functors-are-left-exact]] is the special case of
[[cor-hom-functors-on-a-preadditive-category-are-left-exact]] obtained by
taking the preadditive category $\mathcal C$ to be $R\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}$.
This page therefore cites the general category-theoretic statement instead of
repeating the module calculation.
