## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $L=V(\ell)\subseteq\mathbf P^2$ be a line, $C=V(F)$ a plane projective curve with $L\not\subseteq C$, and $p\in L\cap C$. Identify $L$ with $\mathbf P^1$ and let $0\ne F|_L\in k[s,t]$ be the restriction of $F$ to $L$, a binary form of degree $\deg F$ when $L$ is parametrised by linear forms. Then $I_p(C,L)$ equals the order of vanishing of $F|_L$ at the point of $\mathbf P^1$ corresponding to $p$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC, an algebraically closed field $k$, a line $L=V(\ell)$ with $\ell$ a nonzero linear form, a plane projective curve $C=V(F)$ of degree $d\ge1$ not containing $L$, and $p\in L\cap C$.

[F1] $L$ is a plane projective curve of degree one, isomorphic to $\mathbf P^1$ by a linear parametrisation; under such a parametrisation the restriction of a degree-$d$ form is a binary form of degree $d$ in the two parameters, which is nonzero because $L\not\subseteq C$ [[def-plane-projective-curve]], [[def-projective-space-points]], [[def-morphism-to-projective-space-homogeneous-coordinates]], [[lem-standard-projective-opens-are-affine-spaces]].

[F2] Choose linear coordinates on the plane chart taking $L$ to $y=0$ and $p$ to the origin. Then $\mathcal O_{L,p}=k[x]_{(x)}$ by localisation commuting with quotients. Its only primes are $(0)$ and $(x)$ (a nonzero prime below $(x)$ contains an irreducible divisor, necessarily associate to $x$), so its dimension is one; its cotangent space has basis the class of $x$, so it is regular. Thus the local ring $\mathcal O_{L,p}$ is a discrete valuation ring with maximal ideal generated by the image of any local parameter $t$ vanishing at $p$; this is the one-dimensional regular local ring case, and the image of $\ell$ is a local equation of $L$ in $\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^2,p}$ [[thm-one-dimensional-regular-local-rings-are-dvrs]], [[def-discrete-valuation-ring]], [[def-uniformising-parameter]], [[def-local-ring]].

[F3] Localisation commutes with quotients, and $I_p(C,L)=\ell_{\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^2,p}}(\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^2,p}/(f,\ell))$ for local equations $f,\ell$ [[def-local-intersection-multiplicity-plane-curves]], [[thm-localisation-commutes-with-quotients]], [[lem-local-intersection-length-finite]].

[F4] In a discrete valuation ring $V$ with uniformiser $\pi$, every nonzero $x$ has a normal form $x=u\pi^{v(x)}$ with $u$ a unit and $v$ the valuation, and $\ell_V(V/(x))=v(x)$ [[thm-dvr-element-normal-form]], [[thm-dvr-ideal-and-module-length]], [[def-composition-series-and-length-of-a-module]]. The order of vanishing of a nonzero element of the function field of $\mathbf P^1$ at a point is the valuation of the corresponding discrete valuation ring [[def-discrete-valuation-ring]], [[def-uniformising-parameter]].

## Proof

1.1 The quotient $\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^2,p}/(f,\ell)$ is canonically the local ring of the line at $p$ modulo the image $\bar f$ of $f$: by [F3] applied to the quotient by $(\ell)$, the quotient of the plane local ring by $(f,\ell)$ is $\mathcal O_{L,p}/(\bar f)$. Its submodules over the plane local ring and over the quotient ring $\mathcal O_{L,p}$ coincide, since the action factors through the surjection; thus their lengths coincide. By [F1] the image $\bar f$ is the germ of the restriction $F|_L$, a nonzero element of the discrete valuation ring $\mathcal O_{L,p}$. [F1, F2, F3, construct]

2.1 Let $t$ be a local parameter of $L\cong\mathbf P^1$ at the point corresponding to $p$, so that the maximal ideal of $V=\mathcal O_{L,p}$ is $(t)$ and $t$ is a uniformiser. By [F4] the length $\ell_V(V/(\bar f))$ equals the valuation $v(\bar f)$, which is the order of vanishing of $F|_L$ at that point. [step 1.1, F2, F4]

3.1 Combining step 1.1 and step 2.1, $I_p(C,L)=\ell_{\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^2,p}}(\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^2,p}/(f,\ell))=\ell_V(V/(\bar f))=v(F|_L)$, the order of vanishing of $F|_L$ at the point of $\mathbf P^1$ corresponding to $p$. [step 1.1, step 2.1, given] ∎
