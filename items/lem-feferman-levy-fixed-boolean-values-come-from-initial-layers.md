## Statement

Let $B=\operatorname{RO}(P)$, and let $B_m$ be the complete subalgebra of
Boolean values fixed by $H_m$. If $u\in B_m$, then

$$u=\bigvee\{p\mathbin{\upharpoonright}m:p\in P\text{ and }p\le_Bu\}.$$

Here a condition and its restriction are identified with their canonical
nonzero regular-open values. Consequently $B_m$ is exactly the complete
subalgebra generated by conditions using only layers $n<m$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The Feferman–Levy forcing, its regular-open completion, $m<\omega$, and an $H_m$-fixed $u\in B$.

[F1] [[def-feferman-levy-symmetric-collapse-system]] defines $H_m$ as the automorphisms acting identically on all layers below $m$, and allows arbitrary coordinate permutations in every layer at least $m$. [[lem-feferman-levy-bounded-layer-support]] fixes the use of this one bounded stabilizer for a name.

[F2] [[thm-forcing-preorders-have-regular-open-completions]] gives the dense embedding of $P$ into $B^+=B\setminus\{0\}$ and the Boolean order and compatibility correspondence.

[F3] [[lem-symmetry-lemma-for-forcing-automorphisms]] gives invariance of the ordinary forcing relation under automorphisms of $P$.  Independently, the explicit regular-open construction in F2 is functorial: for an automorphism $\pi$ of $P$, the map $U\mapsto \pi``U$ is an automorphism of $\operatorname{RO}(P)$, because it preserves downward openness, closure, interior, complements, and arbitrary joins.

## Proof

**Proof technique:** contradiction, using a finite fresh-coordinate permutation.

1.1 Fix $p\le_Bu$ and suppose $p\mathbin{\upharpoonright}m\not\le_Bu$. Density of the embedding in F2 gives $q\in P$ with $q\le p\mathbin{\upharpoonright}m$ and $q\le_B\neg u$. [F2, assume-contra]

1.2 For every layer $n\ge m$ appearing in $q$, choose a finite permutation of its $i$-coordinates which moves all upper-layer coordinates of $q$ away from the finitely many upper-layer coordinates of $p$; extend it by the identity elsewhere. The resulting $\pi$ lies in $H_m$. Below layer $m$, $q$ extends $p\mathbin{\upharpoonright}m$ and $\pi$ is the identity; above it, the moved domain of $\pi q$ is disjoint from the domain of $p$. Thus $p$ and $\pi q$ are compatible. This is a finite construction in finitely many represented layers. [F1, construct]

2.1 Since $u$ is fixed by $H_m$, the regular-open automorphism described in F3 sends $q\le_B\neg u$ to $\pi q\le_B\neg u$. A common extension of $p$ and $\pi q$ would lie below both $u$ and $\neg u$, contradicting Boolean incompatibility. Hence $p\mathbin{\upharpoonright}m\le_Bu$. [F2, F3, step 1.1, step 1.2, discharge-contradiction]

3.1 Let $v$ be the displayed join. Step 2.1 gives $v\le u$. Conversely every $p\le_Bu$ satisfies $p\le p\mathbin{\upharpoonright}m\le v$, and density below the regular open $u$ gives $u=\bigvee\{p\in P:p\le_Bu\}\le v$. Therefore $u=v$. [F2, step 2.1]

4.1 Every initial-layer condition is fixed by $H_m$, so the complete subalgebra it generates is contained in $B_m$. The equality in step 3.1 writes every member of $B_m$ as a join of such conditions, yielding the reverse inclusion and the final assertion. [F3, step 3.1, discharge-contradiction: step 1.1] ∎
