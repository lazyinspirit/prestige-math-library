## Statement

If $E/F$ is any algebraic extension, then the entrywise restriction map
$$K_n^M(F)\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb Q\longrightarrow K_n^M(E)\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb Q$$
is injective for every $n\ge0$. This does not require independence or transitivity of Milnor transfers along different towers.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The objects in the Statement.

[F1] Milnor groups have the finite-symbol multilinear Steinberg presentation, and field inclusions induce entrywise maps. [[def-milnor-k-group-of-a-field]]

[F2] For any chosen presentation of a finite simple extension $B/A$, its constructed transfer satisfies $Tj_{A/B}=[B:A]$ in every degree. [[lem-presented-simple-extension-milnor-transfer]]

## Proof

**Given:** The data and conventions above.

1.1 For a finite extension $L/F$, choose finitely many field generators and the resulting tower $F=F_0\subseteq F_1\subseteq\cdots\subseteq F_s=L$, where each step adjoins one element. A finite basis itself supplies such a finite generating set. For each step choose its minimal-polynomial presentation and the transfer supplied by [F2]. The composite $T:K_n^M(L)\to K_n^M(F)$ has $Tj_{F/L}=[L:F]$: on a class restricted from $F$, the last-step transfer multiplies its restriction to $F_{s-1}$ by $[F_s:F_{s-1}]$, and repeating gives the product of degrees, which is $[L:F]$. No equality between transfers from different towers is claimed or used. [F1, F2, given, construct, algebra]

1.2 Every equality among finitely many symbols in $K_n^M(E)$ is witnessed by a finite sum of defining relations, because the relation subgroup in the presentation is generated by finite integer linear combinations. All field entries appearing in that witness lie in a finite subextension $L/F$: finitely many algebraic elements generate a finite extension by their finitely many degrees. Thus if a class from $K_n^M(F)$ vanishes in $K_n^M(E)$, it already vanishes in some $K_n^M(L)$. For $n=0$ the map is the identity of $\mathbb Z$, so the same assertion is immediate. [F1, given, algebra]

2.1 Write a rational class as $z/d$ with $z\in K_n^M(F)$ and a positive integer $d$. If its image in $K_n^M(E)\otimes\mathbb Q$ is zero, the elementary localization description of tensoring an abelian group with $\mathbb Q$ gives a positive integer $c$ annihilating the image of $z$. Explicitly rationalization represents pairs $(z,d)$ with $(z,d)=0$ exactly when some positive integer kills $z$, obtained by clearing the finitely many denominators in the tensor relations. By step 1.2, $cz$ vanishes already over a finite $L/F$. Applying the particular composite in step 1.1 gives $c[L:F]z=0$ in $K_n^M(F)$. Therefore $z/d=0$ after rationalization. This proves injectivity, including zero classes, degree zero and arbitrary infinite algebraic extensions. Only a single finite witness and a finite tower were chosen, so no choice axiom is required. [F1, F2, step 1.1, step 1.2, algebra] ∎
