## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice (AC). Let $A$ be a Noetherian domain, let $B$ be a
finitely generated $A$-algebra, and let $M$ be a finitely generated
$B$-module. Then there exists a nonzero $a\in A$ such that the principal
localisation $M_a$ is a free $A_a$-module. No finiteness of the rank is
asserted: the free module produced may have infinite rank, and it is the zero
module when $M_a=0$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A Noetherian domain $A$, a finitely generated $A$-algebra $B$ and a
finitely generated $B$-module $M$.

[F1] A left $R$-module $M$ is finitely generated if $M=\langle S\rangle_R$ for
some finite subset $S\subseteq M$; a subset $B\subseteq M$ is a basis if every
element of $M$ has a unique expression as a finite $R$-linear combination of
elements of $B$, and a module possessing a basis is free
([[def-generated-cyclic-finitely-generated-and-free-modules]]).

[F2] A commutative $R$-algebra $A$ is of finite type over $R$ when
$A=R[a_1,\dots,a_n]$ for some $n\in\mathbb N$ and some $a_1,\dots,a_n\in A$
([[def-finite-type-and-module-finite-algebras]]).

[F3] Assume AC. Let $R$ be a Noetherian commutative ring and $M$ a finitely generated
$R$-module. Then there are submodules
$0=M_0\subset M_1\subset\dots\subset M_n=M$ with each quotient $M_i/M_{i-1}$
isomorphic to $R/\mathfrak p_i$ for a prime ideal $\mathfrak p_i$; for $M=0$
this is the empty filtration ([[thm-prime-filtration-of-a-finite-module]]).

[F4] If $0\to M'\to M\to M''\to 0$ is a short exact sequence of $R$-modules,
then $0\to S^{-1}M'\to S^{-1}M\to S^{-1}M''\to 0$ is a short exact sequence
of $S^{-1}R$-modules ([[thm-localisation-of-modules-is-exact]]).

[F5] Let $R$ be a Noetherian commutative ring and $M$ a finitely generated
$R$-module. Then $M$ is a Noetherian $R$-module
([[lem-finite-modules-over-noetherian-rings-are-noetherian]]).

[F6] Let $R$ be a Noetherian commutative ring. Then the iterated polynomial
ring $R[x_1,\dots,x_n]$ is Noetherian for every $n\in\mathbb N$
([[cor-finite-variable-polynomial-ring-noetherian]]).

[F7] Every quotient and every localisation of a Noetherian ring is Noetherian
([[thm-noetherian-ring-quotients-and-localisations]]).

[F8] The Axiom of Choice states that every family of nonempty sets has a
choice function ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]).

[F9] For $f\in R$ the principal localisation is $R_f=S_f^{-1}R$ with
$S_f=\{1,f,f^2,\dots\}$, its elements are written $r/f^n$, $R_1\cong R$ and
$R_0$ is the zero ring ([[def-principal-localisation]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct. Induct on a chosen finite list of generators of
$B$ over $A$. The zero-generator case is the prime-filtration argument for a
finite module over a Noetherian domain; the induction step filters a finite
$B=A'[x]$-module by the $A'$-submodules generated by successive powers of $x$,
uses Noetherianity of the first layer to stabilize the successive kernels, and
glues the resulting free graded pieces along sections.

1.1 We prove by induction on $n\ge0$ the following claim $P(n)$: for every finitely generated $A$-algebra $B=A[b_1,\dots,b_n]$, every finitely generated $B$-module $M$ admits $0\ne a\in A$ with $M_a$ free over $A_a$. Since every finitely generated $A$-algebra has such a presentation by [F2], $P(n)$ for the $n$ belonging to $B$ yields the statement. [F2, F9]
1.2 The case $n=0$: here $B$ is the image of $A$ and $M$ is a finitely generated $A$-module. Choose a prime filtration $0=M_0\subset\dots\subset M_r=M$ with quotients $A/\mathfrak p_i$ as in [F3] (the empty filtration when $M=0$). For each $i$ with $\mathfrak p_i\ne0$ choose $0\ne a_i\in\mathfrak p_i$; if all $\mathfrak p_i=0$ put $a=1$. Otherwise put $a=\prod_{i:\mathfrak p_i\ne0}a_i$, a product of finitely many nonzero elements of the domain $A$, hence nonzero. For each $i$ exactness of localisation [F4] identifies $(M_i/M_{i-1})_a$ with $(M_i)_a/(M_{i-1})_a$, and $(A/\mathfrak p_i)_a=A_a/\mathfrak p_iA_a$ equals $0$ when $\mathfrak p_i\ne0$ (because $a\in\mathfrak p_iA_a$) and equals $A_a$ when $\mathfrak p_i=0$. So the localised filtration has quotients $0$ or $A_a$. A short exact sequence $0\to E'\to E\to E''\to 0$ of $A_a$-modules with $E',E''$ free is split: for a basis $(e_j)$ of $E''$ choose preimages $u_j\in E$, one for each basis element (simultaneously for all $j$, by AC [F8]), and the resulting map $E''\to E$ is a section. Hence $E\cong E'\oplus E''$ is free, with the union of bases as a basis [F1]. Applying this successively from $M_0$ up the finite filtration shows that $M_a$ is free over $A_a$. [F1, F3, F4, F8]
1.3 Assume $n\ge1$ and $P(n-1)$, and let $B=A[b_1,\dots,b_n]$ and $M$ be as in $P(n)$. Put $A'=A[b_1,\dots,b_{n-1}]$, so that $B=A'[b_n]$ by [F2], and put $x=b_n$. Choose $m_1,\dots,m_t\in M$ generating $M$ as a $B$-module [F1], and set $M_0=A'm_1+\dots+A'm_t\subseteq M$ and $M_k=\sum_{j=0}^{k}x^jM_0$ for $k\ge0$. Each $M_k$ is a finitely generated $A'$-module and $M_0\subseteq M_1\subseteq\dots$ is an ascending chain; the union is $M$, because $M=\sum_iBm_i=\sum_iA'[x]m_i=\sum_{j\ge0}x^jM_0$. [F1, F2]
1.4 For each $k\ge0$ the $A'$-linear map $\varphi_k\colon M_0\to M_{k+1}/M_k$, $m\mapsto x^{k+1}m+M_k$, is surjective, since $M_{k+1}=M_k+x^{k+1}M_0$; hence $Q_k:=M_{k+1}/M_k$ is a quotient of the finitely generated $A'$-module $M_0$. The kernels $K_k=\ker\varphi_k$ form an ascending chain of $A'$-submodules of $M_0$. The ring $A'$ is finitely generated over the Noetherian ring $A$, hence is a quotient of a polynomial ring $A[x_1,\dots,x_{n-1}]$, hence Noetherian by [F6] and [F7]. Therefore $M_0$ is a Noetherian $A'$-module by [F5], so the chain $(K_k)$ stabilizes: there is $N\ge0$ with $K_k=K_N$ for all $k\ge N$. Consequently $Q_k\cong M_0/K_N=:Q$ as $A'$-modules for every $k\ge N$. [F5, F6, F7]
1.5 Apply the induction hypothesis $P(n-1)$ to the finitely generated $A'$-modules $M_0$, $Q_0,\dots,Q_{N-1}$ and $Q$: there are nonzero elements $a_*,a_0,\dots,a_{N-1},a_\infty\in A$ after which all these modules become free over the corresponding localisation of $A$. Put $a=a_*\left(\prod_{k=0}^{N-1}a_k\right)a_\infty\ne0$, a product of finitely many nonzero elements of the domain $A$. Then $(M_0)_a$ and each $(Q_k)_a$ for $k<N$ and $Q_a$ are free $A_a$-modules, and for $k\ge N$ exactness of localisation [F4] turns the isomorphism $Q_k\cong Q$ into $(Q_k)_a\cong Q_a$, so $(Q_k)_a$ is free over $A_a$ for every $k\ge0$. [F4]
1.6 Finally $0\to(M_k)_a\to(M_{k+1})_a\to(Q_k)_a\to0$ is exact by [F4], and $(Q_k)_a$ is free, so for each $k$ we may choose an $A_a$-linear section $s_k\colon(Q_k)_a\to(M_{k+1})_a$ of the projection, i.e. a simultaneous choice of preimages of the elements of a basis of $(Q_k)_a$ (AC [F8]). Define $\theta_0=\mathrm{id}$ and $\theta_{k+1}\colon(M_0)_a\oplus(Q_0)_a\oplus\dots \oplus(Q_k)_a\to(M_{k+1})_a$ by $\theta_{k+1}=\theta_k\oplus s_k$; each $\theta_k$ is an isomorphism by the splitting just described, and $\theta_{k+1}$ restricts to $\theta_k$, so the $\theta_k$ glue to an $A_a$-module isomorphism $M_a=\bigcup_k(M_k)_a\cong(M_0)_a\oplus\bigoplus_{k\ge0}(Q_k)_a$. A direct sum of free modules is free, a basis being the union of bases of the summands [F1], so $M_a$ is a free $A_a$-module, of rank the cardinality of that basis. This completes the induction and the proof. [F1, F8]
The Axiom of Choice is inherited by the prime-filtration input in step 1.2
and is used in the splitting arguments of steps 1.2 and 1.6 to choose basis
preimages and the family of sections simultaneously. The remaining displayed
choices are finite. The empty module is free of rank zero, and for $M=0$ the prime
filtration is empty so the base case produces $a=1$. $\square$
