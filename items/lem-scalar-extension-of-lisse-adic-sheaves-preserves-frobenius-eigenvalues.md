## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let X be a finite-type scheme over F_q, let ℓ∤q, and let F be a rank-r lisse Qℓ-sheaf in the strict finite-free system convention. Its scalar extension to the finite-model lisse Qℓbar category of [[def-lisse-qbar-l-adic-sheaf-via-finite-coefficients]] is lisse of rank r and constructible. At every geometric point its stalk is `F_x⊗_(Qℓ) Qℓbar`; for every closed point the geometric-Frobenius characteristic polynomial and eigenvalue multiset are unchanged under scalar extension. Thus pointwise purity and weights defined from those eigenvalues are preserved. No statement about derived direct images is made.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC, X/F_q, ℓ and the rank-r lisse Qℓ-sheaf in the Statement.

[F1] A lisse Qℓ-sheaf has a finite-free compatible Zℓ-system representative. [[def-lisse-l-adic-sheaf-on-a-scheme]]

[F2] The finite-model Qℓbar category is specified by finite coefficient extensions and rationalized system maps. [[def-lisse-qbar-l-adic-sheaf-via-finite-coefficients]]

[F3] Under Countable Choice, the integers of a finite extension of Qℓ are generated over Zℓ by one integral element. [[lem-local-integers-of-finite-separable-local-extension-are-monogenic]]

[F4] Finite torsion-free modules over a PID are free. [[cor-finitely-generated-torsion-free-modules-over-a-pid-are-free]]

[F5] AC is assumed and supplies Countable Choice for [F3]. [[def-axiom-of-choice]]

## Proof

**Proof technique:** extend each finite free coefficient system and its stalk action.

1.1 Let `(F_n)` be the finite-free Zℓ representative from [F1], and let L⊂Qℓbar be finite. By [F3], O_L is generated by powers of one integral element over Zℓ; its minimal polynomial bounds the required powers, so O_L is finite as a Zℓ-module. It is torsion-free, hence finite free by [F4]. Therefore O_L/ℓ^n is a finite ring and its reduction maps are compatible. [F1, F3, F4, F5, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 Define `F_(L,n)=F_n⊗_(Z/ℓ^n)(O_L/ℓ^n)`. On an étale trivialization of F_n it is the free rank-r constant O_L/ℓ^n-module. Tensor products commute with the specified quotient transitions, so these form a finite-coefficient lisse system. Further finite coefficient extension is compatible with this construction. A rational isomorphism of Zℓ-systems has an inverse after inverting ℓ; extending the two system maps and their identities shows that its extension is again an isomorphism. Thus the object of [F2] is independent of the chosen representative up to the prescribed rational equivalence. [F1, F2, F5, step 1.1, given, construct, algebra]

3.1 On each geometric stalk the inverse limit commutes with tensoring by the finite free Zℓ-module O_L, since it is a finite direct sum in a chosen basis. After inverting ℓ, the rational stalk is `L⊗_(Qℓ) F_x`; directed extension over the finite subfields gives `Qℓbar⊗_(Qℓ) F_x`. The one-stratum lisse system is constructible by [F2]. [F1, F2, F4, F5, step 2.1, given, construct, algebra]

4.1 At a closed point, geometric Frobenius acts on the extended stalk as `Fr⊗1`. Its characteristic polynomial is the original polynomial viewed over Qℓbar, so its eigenvalue multiset is unchanged. Every weight or pointwise-purity condition defined by the absolute values of those eigenvalues is therefore preserved. The construction says nothing about derived pushforwards. [F1, F2, F5, step 3.1, given, construct, algebra] ∎

## Source and authoring record

This is a local finite-coefficient construction. It supplies the stated pointwise coefficient-extension interface only; it does not assert equivalence with every other Qℓbar category or prove the Weil II weight theorem.
