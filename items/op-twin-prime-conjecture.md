## Open problem

Let $p_1<p_2<\cdots$ be the positive primes and $H_1=\liminf_{n\to\infty}(p_{n+1}-p_n)$. The twin prime conjecture asserts $H_1=2$, equivalently that infinitely many consecutive primes differ by $2$.

## Status

The conjecture remains open in the source record dated 2026-10-23. The published bound $H_1\le246$ establishes bounded gaps but does not determine the least even gap that occurs infinitely often.
