## Definition

For a measure $\mu$, write
$$L^1(\mu):=\{f:X\to\mathbb C:\ f \text{ is integrable}\}.$$
Here **integrable** is the notion introduced in
[[def-integrable-real-and-complex-functions-and-their-integrals]].

On this page $L^1(\mu)$ is the class of integrable representatives. The later
Banach-space page will pass to almost-everywhere equivalence classes.
