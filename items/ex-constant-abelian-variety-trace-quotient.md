## Example

Let $k$ be a field and let $A/k$ be a proper, geometrically connected, smooth separated group variety of finite type. In the identity extension $K=k$, the $K/k$-trace pair is $(A,\operatorname{id}_A)$, and the trace quotient $A(K)/\operatorname{id}_A(A(k))$ is the zero group.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A field $k$, an abelian variety $A/k$, the identity extension $K=k$, and the universal property defining a $K/k$-trace pair.

[F1] For every abelian variety $C/k$, composition with the trace homomorphism gives a bijection $\operatorname{Hom}_k(C,T)\to\operatorname{Hom}_K(C_K,A)$. [[def-k-trace-of-an-abelian-variety]]

## Proof

1.1 Since $K=k$, base change identifies $C_K=C$ and $\operatorname{Hom}_K(C_K,A)=\operatorname{Hom}_k(C,A)$. With $T=A$ and $\tau=\operatorname{id}_A$, composition sends each homomorphism $f:C\to A$ to itself. The map in [F1] is therefore bijective for every $C$, so $(A,\operatorname{id}_A)$ is the trace pair. [given, F1, algebra]

1.2 Again $K=k$, so $A(K)=A(k)$ and $\operatorname{id}_A(A(k))=A(k)$. Their quotient is the zero group, which is finitely generated by the empty set. [given, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
