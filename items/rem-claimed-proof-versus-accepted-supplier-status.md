## Remark

The literature consulted records a specific 2018 objection by Scholze and Stix to a proposed proof of the abc conjecture, together with their acknowledgment that their view was disputed. Joshi's 2025 report, opening §§1.1–1.2 and conclusion §1.4, contests that objection and advances a different proof claim. This remark records those statements and their dates only; it does not assess either argument, infer a current consensus, or treat any claim as an accepted theorem supplier.
