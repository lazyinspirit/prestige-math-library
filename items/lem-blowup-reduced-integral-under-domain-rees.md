## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice, inherited from the relative Proj construction
([[def-axiom-of-choice]]). Let $X$ be an integral scheme
([[def-integral-scheme]]) and let $\mathcal I$ be a nonzero quasi-coherent
ideal sheaf of finite type on $X$ ([[def-quasi-coherent-ideal-sheaf]]). Then
the blowup $\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}X$ of
[[def-blowup-scheme-along-ideal]] is integral: the affine blowup algebras
$A[I/a]$ are domains because $A$ is a domain and $a$ is nonzero, and they glue
along localisations. More generally, if $X$ is reduced then
$\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}X$ is reduced, because the affine blowup
algebras of a reduced ring are reduced.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** An integral (respectively reduced) scheme $X$, a nonzero
quasi-coherent ideal sheaf $\mathcal I$ of finite type, and for an affine open
$U=\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq X$ with $I=\Gamma(U,\mathcal I)$ and $a\in I$
the affine blowup algebra $A[I/a]=(R(I))_{(a)}\subseteq A_a$, the
degree-zero part of the localisation of $R(I)=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}I^nt^n$
([[lem-affine-blowup-algebra-properties]],
[[def-rees-algebra-ideal-sheaf]]).

[F1] [[def-integral-scheme]]: $X$ is integral exactly when it is nonempty and
every nonempty affine open of $X$ is the spectrum of a domain; equivalently $X$
is reduced and its underlying space is irreducible.

[F2] [[lem-affine-blowup-algebra-properties]]: For $a\in I$ the affine blowup
algebra $A[I/a]$ has $IA[I/a]=aA[I/a]$ with $a$ a nonzerodivisor and
$\bigl(A[I/a]\bigr)_a=A_a$. Its construction as the degree-zero part of the
localisation of $R(I)\subseteq A[t]$ at the degree-one element $at$ embeds
$A[I/a]$ into $A_a$ as the subring generated by $A$ and the fractions $i/a$,
$i\in I$; if $A$ is a domain and $a\ne0$ then $A[I/a]$ is a domain, and if $A$
is reduced then $A[I/a]$ is reduced.

[F3] [[thm-affine-blowup-standard-charts]]: For $I=(f_0,\dots,f_r)$ the
standard opens $D_+(f_it)=\operatorname{Spec}A[I/f_i]$ cover
$\operatorname{Bl}_I\operatorname{Spec}A$ and the presentation is independent
of the chosen generating family.

[F4] [[lem-spectrum-localization-open-immersion]]: For $a\in A$ the principal open $D(a)$ is
$\operatorname{Spec}A_a$ and the localisation morphism is an open immersion
$D(a)\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Spec}A$ with image the complement of
$V(a)$.

[F5] [[lem-blowup-local-on-base-scheme]]: For an open subscheme
$U\hookrightarrow X$ there is a canonical isomorphism
$\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I|_U}U\to\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}X\times_XU$;
the blowup is covered by the restrictions over an affine cover of $X$.

[F6] [[def-reduction-of-scheme]]: On $\operatorname{Spec}A$, the reduction is $\operatorname{Spec}(A/\sqrt{(0)})$. Consequently reducedness is affine-local: a nilpotent section on a reduced affine chart is zero, and these charts cover all stalks. Also a subring of a reduced ring is reduced, since its nilpotent elements are nilpotent in the larger ring and hence zero.

## Proof

1.1 Over an affine base $U=\operatorname{Spec}A$, discard generators $f_i=0$, whose charts are empty. If $A$ is a domain, every remaining chart $B_i=A[I/f_i]\subset A_{f_i}$ is a domain; if $A$ is reduced, every chart is reduced (including empty charts). Hence the blowup is reduced in either case, by affine-local reducedness. Moreover $D(f_i)$ in chart $i$ is $\operatorname{Spec}A_{f_i}$ by the chart localization identity. [F2, F3, F4, F5, F6]

2.1 Suppose $X$ is integral and $\mathcal I\ne0$. Then $X\setminus Z$ is a nonempty open: a nonzero local section of the ideal in a domain remains nonzero at the generic point. Put $W=\pi^{-1}(X\setminus Z)$. On chart $i$, the inverse-image ideal is $f_iB_i$, so $W\cap\operatorname{Spec}B_i=D(f_i)$. The identifications $D(f_i)=\operatorname{Spec}A_{f_i}$ are the structural morphism and agree on intersections: after both denominators are inverted, the ratio transition maps fix $A$ and the ordinary fractions. Thus they glue to $W\cong X\setminus Z$. This is a nonempty irreducible open. [F1, F2, F3, F4, step 1.1]

3.1 Every nonempty chart is a domain chart with a nonzero denominator, so its nonempty principal open $D(f_i)\subset W$ is dense. The closure of $W$ therefore contains every chart and is the whole blowup. A closure of an irreducible set is irreducible. Together with reducedness and nonemptiness, this proves integrality. If the ideal is zero, all charts are empty and the reducedness assertion still holds. [F1, F3, F6, step 1.1, step 2.1] ∎

## Remarks

- The argument does not need $X$ to be Noetherian or $\mathcal I$ to be
  principal anywhere; it only uses that the affine blowup algebra sits inside
  the localisation $A_a$.
- If $\mathcal I=0$ the blowup is empty and hence reduced, while
  irreducibility and nonemptiness fail; this is why the integral statement
  assumes $\mathcal I\ne0$.
