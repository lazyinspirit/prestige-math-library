## Statement

Assume Countable Choice. Take two actual doubly pointed Heegaard diagrams of the same oriented knot in the oriented three-sphere, their compatible Morse and compression data, and good finite square-zero full minus count data as in the named move operations. Both diagrams have exactly ONE ordered pair w,z, possibly different genera. Use the actual negative-Morse exterior field with its zero-relative-Euler zero-surgery cap to define the integral Seifert-surface Alexander grading. Normalize the relative Maslov grading by placing the sole total zero-w hat homology generator in degree zero.

There are finite chain homotopy equivalences over F_2[U] between these full filtered minus complexes preserving these absolute conventions: the maps have Maslov degree zero, their inverse homotopies degree one, and every term U^a y of an actual count map from x satisfies A(y)-a=A(x)-n_z. In particular all positive z terms are retained. After U=0 the maps and both inverse homotopies are finite Alexander-filtered maps of the actual total hat complexes. They identify the first Alexander level detecting the total generator, and identify the associated-graded homology with its normalized Maslov parity.

This comparison is between doubly pointed analytic diagrams. It neither computes a finite diagram's Seifert Euler anchor nor supplies a grid-to-analytic comparison. No absolute multipointed cone-centering assertion is made.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The actual two diagrams, their oriented knot identification, the specified count data and Countable Choice.

[F1] The negative selected-Morse exterior field has the explicit zero-Euler cap. Its difference line is the actual finite trajectory cochain, its determinant difference is twice that line, and its value is invariant under carried diagram isomorphisms and followed mark-free isotopies. The relative difference is A(x)-A(y)=n_z-n_w. The total-hat Maslov normalization is unique. The ordinary operation proves the arbitrary-location marked same-splitting comparison and its cancelling-ball support; it does not apply a zero-mark separator in an arbitrary old region. ## Proof

1.1 Carry one oriented Seifert surface and its zero longitude through the actual knot identifications in [F2]. The exterior zero-framing is unique up to a zero-winding circle map, with its periodic angle lift as in [F1]. Thus all knot tubes may use the SAME transported boundary frame and zero-Euler cap. Follow the actual auxiliary count constructions of [F3], rather than assuming a relative origin already determines this cap. On a fixed diagram its generator trajectories and knot tube can be kept fixed while varying the analytic data; the raw-identity anchored reference has identical exterior field and identical cap at its two ends. Its line difference is zero. Every other anchored class is compared to that reference by [F1]'s finite cochain, and therefore has precisely the stated n_z-n_w difference with no extra affine constant. This includes actual source-varying boundary tensors and independent generator charts: the reference is the actual smooth generator section and the raw physical identification in [F3], not an assertion that all chart expressions are constant. [F1, F3, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 Here is the finite exterior comparison for an attaching move. Cut its actual compression disks, slide band and selected generator trajectory tubes into the finite product cells used in [F4]. On every cell off those tubes use the carried old exterior line frame. At a positive transverse crossing of an index-one trajectory the POSITIVE-gradient difference line has winding plus one. The NEGATIVE-gradient convention conjugates this line. The oriented incidence boundary has its alpha paths forward and its beta paths backward, so the resulting negative-field difference is the Poincare dual of this incidence boundary, exactly the sign computation in [F1]. Cap its full attaching-circle terms in the supplied compression disks; they and the actual marked slide band miss the knot. These caps therefore add no meridian term. A spanning incidence chain meets the knot positively at z, where it exits the alpha half, and negatively at w, where it enters. Its linking number is consequently n_z-n_w. One can compute on an actual finite subdivision: internal edge windings cancel in pairs, a transverse trajectory crossing contributes its signed unit, and the only uncancelled transverse knot terms are these marked degrees. Thus the determinant evaluation changes by 2(n_z-n_w), even for a smooth reference with signed region degrees. Neither positivity nor a class/domain bijection is required for this line calculation. [F1, F4, step 1.1, construct, algebra]

3.1 Fix the reference value for the auxiliary top corner, rather than leaving a knot-dependent integer. Its two own-handlebody bridge systems use the SAME trivial arc. In the common occupied cut ball take two parallel copies of its boundary-parallel disk; after identifying the two handlebody boundaries their arcs bound the thin doubled disk. This disk and a small ball about it miss every compression face and every selected higher-index trajectory tube. Choose the local min/max field tangent to this disk, as in [F1]'s explicit unknot model, and keep its normal direction as a nonzero section of the perpendicular plane. Its relative exterior Euler number is zero, as is its filling cap; this local calculation works inside that ball even though the auxiliary ambient manifold has S1-times-S2 factors. Now cancel the paired meridian disks in the two copies of the handlebody, using the ribbon band product coordinates, and cap the remaining auxiliary factors by their meridian three-handles. These operations miss that ball and the protected knot tube and give the product identification on the knot exterior. The actual pair-periodic capped classes generate their free integral degree-two cells, and the top-corner determinant evaluates zero on each by [F4]; hence their meridian-disk line frames extend over the cancellations with no further Chern term. The explicit doubled-disk evaluation fixes the boundary-relative meridian value that absolute Chern vanishing alone would not fix. The smooth nearest ribbon triangle is exactly this transported trajectory pairing and misses both marks. It therefore fixes zero in step 2.1. This is a smooth capped trajectory reference; no holomorphic constant triangle, or unproved regularity of such a triangle, is used. Small-isotopy nearest triangles and the persistent generators at a birth have the same reference. The actual tiny cancelling bigon is mark free. Applying step 2.1 to each other actual triangle and to each quadrilateral gives its absolute difference, including the additive marked vectors under sewing. Thus all attaching maps and the actual homotopies in [F3] use the carried Seifert origin. [F1, F3, F4, step 1.1, step 2.1, construct, algebra]

4.1 For an ordinary stabilization use its actual cancelling one/two-handle pair and selected new intersection in [F3]. After cancelling those selected trajectories, the old and new exterior fields agree outside its knot-disjoint ball. Their difference line on that ball relative its boundary has zero degree-two obstruction: the finite relative cell calculation is H^2(B^3,S^2;Z)=0. Hence it contributes zero to the Chern evaluation on the carried Seifert surface, whether or not that surface enters the ball. A possible degree-three plane-field obstruction changes no determinant Chern evaluation. The zero-Euler knot cap was untouched. This fixes equality of the nearest generators' A values. The smooth stabilized references in [F3] have equal individual marked degrees and equal relative index, so the same equality holds for its actual count identification. For a different ball location use the actual mark-preserving relocation and same-handlebody disk comparison in that operation, then steps 2.1–3.1. No zero-w exclusion at an arbitrary old region point is asserted. These arguments also include destabilization by the inverse map and the genus-zero unknot point model. [F1, F3, step 1.1, step 2.1, step 3.1, construct, algebra]

5.1 At every operation [F3]'s relative origin is now the actual origin in [F1]. Its rigid weighted term therefore obeys A(U^a y)=A(y)-a=A(x)-n_z, and the same formula holds for each homotopy term. Nonnegativity of n_z proves filteredness while retaining every positive-z contribution. All finite polynomial sums and compositions of such maps remain filtered. In particular the literal nearest inverse in an attaching operation is a finite nilpotent sum; its nearest identification preserves the cap origin, so each factor and every summand is filtered. The actual unit-pair cancellations at births retain this property because the pivot bigon has n_w=n_z=0. Thus it is the explicit inverse homotopy equivalence, not merely a quasi-isomorphism or a rank calculation, that preserves this absolute filtration. [F1, F3, step 1.1, step 3.1, step 4.1, construct, algebra]

6.1 For the Maslov origin, each single-operation map is homogeneous in the actual relative M by [F3]. Its specialization U=0 is an actual homotopy equivalence and therefore sends the sole total hat homology generator to its counterpart nontrivially. [F1]'s normalization places both in degree zero, so the homogeneous map's shift is zero. Each displayed inverse homotopy then has degree one, as in its actual count or cancellation formula. This is a finite graded argument; it uses no general absolute rational-degree cobordism formula. On associated-graded homology the inverse identities persist because every map and homotopy is filtered, so the induced equivalence preserves both the absolute integer Alexander index and this normalized Maslov parity. [F1, F3, step 5.1, construct, algebra]

7.1 Apply [F2] with k=1 at both endpoints. Its proof introduces no marked pairs in this case. Compose the finite maps in steps 1.1–6.1 along its actual word, and compose their inverses in reverse order. Expanding the finite inverse identities gives the composite homotopies, all with the degrees and filtration just proved. Tensoring these literal finite polynomial identities over F_2[U] with U=0 gives the hat identities directly; flatness of this quotient is unnecessary because the chain identities themselves are specialized. A filtered map carrying the total generator nontrivially implies that the target threshold is at most the source threshold. Applying its filtered inverse gives the reverse inequality, hence equality. This proves the claimed analytic diagram comparison and normalized parity statement. It does not assign an unevaluated diagram's Euler anchor or compare analytic diagrams with a combinatorial grid model. [F1, F2, F3, step 1.1, step 4.1, step 5.1, step 6.1, construct, algebra] ∎
