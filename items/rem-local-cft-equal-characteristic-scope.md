## Remark

The equal-characteristic existence statement here applies to complete
discretely valued fields of characteristic $p>0$ with finite residue field,
and classifies open finite-index subgroups of $F^\times$. It makes no claim
for infinite residue fields, arbitrary non-complete valuation fields, or
archimedean local fields; in characteristic $p$, openness is an essential
hypothesis.
