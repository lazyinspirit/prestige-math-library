## Statement

In a fixed splitting $p$-modular system, each element of $k^\times$ of order
prime to $p$ has exactly one Teichmuller lift, and the lift satisfies

$$\tau(\lambda\mu)=\tau(\lambda)\tau(\mu)$$

whenever $\lambda,\mu\in k^\times$ have order prime to $p$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A splitting $p$-modular system $(K,\mathcal O,k)$ and
$\lambda,\mu\in k^\times$ of order prime to $p$.

[F1] The Teichmuller lift of such an element is, by definition, the unique
prime-to-$p$ root of unity in $\mathcal O^\times$ reducing to it
([[def-teichmuller-lift-in-a-splitting-p-modular-system]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 The uniqueness clause is already built into [F1]: if $u,v\in\mathcal O^\times$ both have prime-to-$p$ order and reduce to $\lambda$, then both satisfy the defining property of the Teichmuller lift of $\lambda$, so $u=v$. [F1, given]

2.1 The product $\tau(\lambda)\tau(\mu)$ reduces to $\lambda\mu$ in $k^\times$. It is again a root of unity of order prime to $p$, because it lies in the finite subgroup generated by two prime-to-$p$ roots of unity. By the uniqueness from step 1.1, it must equal the Teichmuller lift of $\lambda\mu$. [F1, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Steps 1.1 and 2.1 give the claimed uniqueness and multiplicativity. [step 1.1, step 2.1] ∎
