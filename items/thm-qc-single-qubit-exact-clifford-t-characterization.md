## Statement

A matrix $U\in U(2)$ is exactly the matrix of a finite one-qubit Clifford+T
word if and only if all four entries of $U$ lie in $D$. This is a
single-qubit statement; no multi-qubit exact ring characterization is
asserted.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The exact circuit matrices and matrix-product convention of the gate-library and circuit-semantics definitions.

[F1] [[lem-qc-clifford-t-entry-ring]]: every finite Clifford+T circuit matrix has entries in $D$.

[F2] [[lem-qc-single-qubit-ring-unitary-form]]: a ring-entry unitary has first column $(u,v)^T$ and determinant phase $\omega^j$, with second column $\omega^j(-\bar v,\bar u)^T$.

[F3] [[lem-qc-clifford-t-denominator-descent-and-base]]: every normalized ring column reduces by an $HT^{-k}$ step from squared-entry exponent $s>3$ to $s-1$, and every column with $s\le3$ has an exact H/T preparation word.

[F4] [[lem-qc-dyadic-cyclotomic-unit-circle]]: the elements $z\in D$ satisfying $z\bar z=1$ are exactly $\omega^j$.

## Proof


1.1 If $U$ is a finite Clifford+T circuit matrix, [F1] puts all its entries in $D$. [F1, given]

1.2 Conversely, let $U\in U(2)$ have entries in $D$. By [F2] its first column $(u,v)^T$ is a normalized column. Apply the descent in [F3] repeatedly while $s=\operatorname{sde}(|u|^2)>3$. Each step replaces the current matrix $M$ by $HT^{-k}M$ and decreases the nonnegative integer $s$ by exactly one; hence after finitely many steps the first column lies in the base regime. [F2, F3, given, construct]

2.1 By [F3], the base column has a word $W_0$ with $W_0|0\rangle=(u_0,v_0)^T$. Reversing each descent step by prepending its recorded $T^kH$ gives a word $W$ with first column equal to the original $(u,v)^T$. The unitary $W^*U$ has first column $(1,0)^T$, so orthogonality of its columns makes it $\operatorname{diag}(1,\lambda)$ for a unit scalar $\lambda$. Its entries lie in $D$, since $W,U$ have entries in that ring. Therefore [F4] gives $\lambda=\omega^j$, and $U=WT^j$ is a finite Clifford+T word. [F1, F3, F4, step 1.2, algebra]

3.1 Both implications are proved. The proof constructs the converse word with only exact coefficient operations and the finite base lookup; no approximation, ancilla, or multi-qubit inference is used. [step 1.1, step 2.1] ∎
