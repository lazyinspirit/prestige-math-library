## Example

Let $X$ have a product input law, let $f(X)\in L^2$, and let $\epsilon$ be
independent of $X$ with mean zero and variance $\tau^2$. For noisy output
$Y=f(X)+\epsilon$, the intrinsic noise contributes $\tau^2$ to output
variance. Sensitivity of the conditional mean $\mathbb E[Y\mid X]=f(X)$
excludes that noise and, when $V_f=\operatorname{Var}(f(X))>0$, uses denominator
$V_f$; if $V_f=0$, its normalized indices are undefined. Normalizing noisy
outputs by $\operatorname{Var}(Y)$ instead changes the denominator, and
independent noise replicas also add $\tau^2$ to the total-effect pick-freeze
numerator.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The independent input/noise model in the Example, and a copy $(X',\epsilon')$ of $(X,\epsilon)$ that is independent of $(X,\epsilon)$ for hybrid evaluations.

[F1] [[def-independent-random-elements]]: independence means independence of the sigma-algebras generated by the random elements.

[F2] [[thm-independent-random-elements-have-product-joint-law]]: independent random elements have product joint law.

[F3] [[def-moments-variance-and-covariance]]: variance and covariance are centered moments.

[F4] [[thm-fubini-theorem-for-l-one-on-sigma-finite-product-spaces]]: integrable functions under the product law factor through coordinatewise integration.

[F5] [[thm-pick-freeze-sobol-covariance-identities]]: first-order covariance and total-effect half-difference numerators are the stated ANOVA variance sums.

[F6] [[def-first-and-total-sobol-indices]]: normalized indices divide by the selected output variance, when positive.

[F7] [[def-conditional-expectation-given-a-sigma-algebra]]: a conditional expectation version is measurable with respect to the conditioning sigma-algebra and has the same integrals as the original variable on every event in that sigma-algebra.

[F8] [[cor-cauchy-schwarz-inequality-for-l-two]]: products of square-integrable variables are integrable.

[F9] [[cor-expectation-linearity-monotonicity-and-modulus-bound]]: expectation is linear on integrable variables.

## Verification

**Given:** The model, centered independent noise, and independent replacement copies.

1.1 Put $\mathcal G=\sigma(X)$. For each $A\in\mathcal G$, independence [F1]–[F2] factors $\mathbb E[\mathbf1_A\epsilon]=\mathbb P(A)\mathbb E\epsilon=0$. Thus the $\mathcal G$-measurable integrable variable $f(X)$ satisfies $\mathbb E[\mathbf1_A Y]=\mathbb E[\mathbf1_A f(X)]$ for every $A\in\mathcal G$, so [F7] identifies $\mathbb E[Y\mid X]=f(X)$. Also [F8] makes the cross moment integrable, and the same independence factorization gives $\operatorname{Cov}(f(X),\epsilon)=0$. Expanding variance by [F3] and using linearity [F9] yields $\operatorname{Var}(Y)=V_f+\tau^2$, where $V_f=\operatorname{Var}(f(X))$. [F1, F2, F3, F7, F8, F9, given, algebra]

2.1 In a first-order pick-freeze pair with fresh independent noise at both evaluations, the covariance of the two outputs is $\operatorname{Cov}(f(X),f(X^{[i]}))$: all cross covariances with noise vanish by the product factorization, and the two noise copies have zero covariance. The required products are integrable by [F8]. By [F5], this numerator is $V_{\{i\}}(f)$. Only when $\operatorname{Var}(Y)=V_f+\tau^2>0$ does division yield the normalized noisy ratio $V_{\{i\}}(f)/(V_f+\tau^2)$; if $V_f=\tau^2=0$, that ratio is undefined. The conditional-mean index is $V_{\{i\}}(f)/V_f$ only if $V_f>0$ and is undefined if $V_f=0$. [F2, F3, F4, F5, F6, F8, F9, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 In a total-effect pair, expand $Y-\widetilde Y=(f(X)-f(\widetilde X^{[i]}))+ (\epsilon-\epsilon')$. The cross term has zero expectation because the noises are centered and independent of inputs; [F8] ensures its integrability. Linearity [F9] and independence give $\frac12\mathbb E[(\epsilon-\epsilon')^2]=\tau^2$. Thus half the expected squared difference is the input total-effect numerator from [F5] plus $\tau^2$. Therefore raw noisy total-effect estimation includes intrinsic noise unless the target is the conditional mean or the noise term is separately accounted for. [F2, F3, F4, F5, F8, F9, given, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
