## Definition

Let $\mathcal{P}$ be a property of finitely generated groups.

The property $\mathcal{P}$ is a **quasi-isometry invariant** if, whenever
$G$ and $H$ are finitely generated and have the same quasi-isometry type, the
truth of $\mathcal{P}$ for $G$ implies its truth for $H$.

Equivalently, $\mathcal{P}$ depends only on the quasi-isometry type of the
group. In geometric group theory one also says that such a property is
**geometric**.
