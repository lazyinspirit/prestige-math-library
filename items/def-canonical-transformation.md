## Definition

A **canonical transformation** between symplectic phase spaces is a
symplectomorphism. A local canonical transformation is a local
symplectomorphism.

Time slices of Hamiltonian evolutions form an important subclass, called
Hamiltonian transformations or a Hamiltonian isotopy when parametrized from
the identity. The definition does not identify every symplectic isotopy with a
Hamiltonian one; global first-cohomology obstructions can distinguish them.
