## Statement

Let $X$ be a finite CW complex of dimension $N$. For the cohomological AHSS of
[[thm-cohomological-atiyah-hirzebruch-spectral-sequence]] and the homological
AHSS of [[thm-homological-atiyah-hirzebruch-spectral-sequence]] the edge
morphisms are the structural maps of the skeletal exact couple, as follows.

In cohomology, for every $p,q$ the pair map $k:E_{p,q}\to D_{p-1,q}$ carries a
stable cycle of $E_\infty^{p,q}$ to a class in $h^{p+q}(X^p)$ that is the
restriction of a class on $X$, and the induced map
$$E_\infty^{p,q}\xrightarrow{\ \cong\ } F^ph^{p+q}(X)\big/F^{p+1}h^{p+q}(X),\qquad [e]\longmapsto [k(e)],$$
is an isomorphism. In particular the $0$-column edge is the quotient
$h^n(X)\to h^n(X)/F^1h^n(X)\cong E_\infty^{0,n}$ induced by restriction to
$X^0$, and the top-column edge $E_\infty^{N,n-N}\cong F^Nh^n(X)\subseteq h^n(X)$
is the inclusion of the kernel of restriction to $X^{N-1}$.

In homology, the pair map $j:D_{p,q}\to E_{p,q}$ descends in the opposite
direction to an isomorphism
$$F_ph_{p+q}(X)\big/F_{p-1}h_{p+q}(X)\xrightarrow{\ \cong\ }E^\infty_{p,q},$$
with $F_ph_n(X)=\operatorname{im}(h_n(X^p)\to h_n(X))$. The $0$-column edge is
the inclusion $F_0h_n(X)=\operatorname{im}(h_n(X^0)\to h_n(X))\subseteq h_n(X)$,
induced by the skeleton inclusion $h_n(X^0)\to h_n(X)$, and the top-column edge is the
quotient $h_n(X)\to h_n(X)/F_{N-1}h_n(X)\cong E^\infty_{N,n-N}$.

These identifications require boundedness of the skeletal index only: for each
fixed $p$ the stable terms exist because the filtration is finite in $p$, and no
boundedness, vanishing or first-quadrant hypothesis is imposed on the coefficient
index $q$.

## Facts & Assumptions

[F1] The cohomological AHSS has $E_1^{p,q}=h^{p+q}(X^p,X^{p-1})$, differentials of bidegree $(r,1-r)$, and $E_\infty^{p,q}\cong F^ph^{p+q}(X)/F^{p+1}h^{p+q}(X)$, where $F^ph^n(X)=\ker(h^n(X)\to h^n(X^{p-1}))= \operatorname{im}(h^n(X,X^{p-1})\to h^n(X))$ ([[thm-cohomological-atiyah-hirzebruch-spectral-sequence]], [[def-skeletal-filtration-for-generalized-cohomology]]).

[F2] The homological AHSS has $E^1_{p,q}=h_{p+q}(X^p,X^{p-1})$, differentials of bidegree $(-r,r-1)$, and $E^\infty_{p,q}\cong F_ph_{p+q}(X)/F_{p-1}h_{p+q}(X)$, where $F_ph_n(X)=\operatorname{im}(h_n(X^p)\to h_n(X))$ ([[thm-homological-atiyah-hirzebruch-spectral-sequence]]).

[F3] In either exact couple the stable cycles are subquotients of the initial $E$, the stable denominators are the images of the appropriate $\ker i^{r}$ under the connecting map, and the pair map or its homology counterpart induces the identification with the filtration quotients; stability in these formulas is governed by the finiteness of the skeletal index ([[def-exact-couple]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

**Given:** A finite CW complex $X$ of dimension $N$ and the two skeletal AHSSs of [F1] and [F2].

1.1 In the cohomological couple, $k:E_{p,q}\to D_{p-1,q}$ restricts on the stable numerator $N^\infty_{p,q}=k^{-1}(\operatorname{im}(h^{p+q}(X)\to h^{p+q}(X^p)))$ to a map onto the subgroup $K=\operatorname{im}(h^{p+q}(X)\to h^{p+q}(X^p))\cap\ker(h^{p+q}(X^p)\to h^{p+q}(X^{p-1}))$, with kernel the stable denominator; hence $E_\infty^{p,q}\cong K\cong F^ph^{p+q}(X)/F^{p+1}h^{p+q}(X)$ through $k$. [F1, F3, given]

1.2 In the homology couple, $j:D_{p,q}\to E_{p,q}$ maps $h_n(X^p)$ onto the stable numerator. Modulo the stable denominator its kernel is the subgroup generated by $\ker(h_n(X^p)\to h_n(X))$ and the image of $h_n(X^{p-1})$. It therefore descends to an isomorphism $F_ph_n(X)/F_{p-1}h_n(X)\xrightarrow{\cong}E^\infty_{p,q}$; the inverse is the convergence isomorphism stated in [F2]. [F2, F3, given]

1.3 For the extreme columns the structural maps specialize: in cohomology $p=0$ gives $F^0h^n(X)=h^n(X)$ and $F^1h^n(X)=\ker(h^n(X)\to h^n(X^0))$, so $k$ induces the quotient by the classes vanishing on the $0$-skeleton; $p=N$ gives $F^{N+1}=0$ and $F^Nh^n(X)=\ker(h^n(X)\to h^n(X^{N-1}))$, so $k$ induces the inclusion of that kernel. [F1, given]

1.4 In homology $p=0$ gives $F_{-1}=0$ and $F_0h_n(X)=\operatorname{im}(h_n(X^0)\to h_n(X))$, an inclusion, while $p=N$ gives $F_Nh_n(X)=h_n(X)$ and the quotient by $F_{N-1}h_n(X)=\operatorname{im}(h_n(X^{N-1})\to h_n(X))$. [F2, given]

2.1 Since for each fixed $p$ the families $N^r_{p,q}$ and $B^r_{p,q}$ stabilize after finitely many steps depending only on $p$ and $\dim X$, and no step used the coefficient index $q$, the identifications of steps 1.1 and 1.2 exist for all $q$ without any boundedness assumption on $q$. [F3, step 1.1, step 1.2]

3.1 Steps 1.1 to 2.1 give the asserted edge morphisms and their identification with the structural maps of the exact couple, in both the cohomological and homological cases. [step 1.3, step 1.4, step 2.1] ∎
