## Definition

Let $V$ be a complex vector space of finite dimension $g\geq 1$. A **full lattice** in $V$ is a discrete additive subgroup $\Lambda\subset V$ whose real span is $V$; equivalently, $\Lambda$ is free abelian of rank $2g$ and $\Lambda\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb R\to V$ is an isomorphism of real vector spaces. The quotient $V/\Lambda$ is the **complex torus** with quotient topology and complex Lie-group structure induced by $V$. Its universal covering map is $v\mapsto v+\Lambda$, with kernel $\Lambda$. A complex torus is **polarized** when its first integral homology lattice admits an integral alternating form whose associated Hermitian form is positive definite.
