## Definition

Let $f:A\to B$ be a homomorphism of unital associative rings, and regard $B$ as a right $A$-module by $b\cdot a=bf(a)$. A triple is $(P,\alpha,Q)$, where $P,Q\in\operatorname{Proj}_{\mathrm{fg}}(A)$ and $\alpha:B\otimes_A P\xrightarrow{\sim}B\otimes_A Q$ is a left $B$-module isomorphism. An isomorphism of triples is a pair of $A$-module isomorphisms $u:P\to P'$, $v:Q\to Q'$ satisfying $\alpha'(1\otimes u)=(1\otimes v)\alpha$. An exact sequence of triples is a sequence whose two underlying sequences of $A$-modules are short exact and whose maps commute with the displayed isomorphisms after tensoring with $B$. The relative group $K_0(A,B)$ is generated by isomorphism classes $[P,\alpha,Q]$, with relations $[P,\alpha,Q]=[P',\alpha',Q']+[P'',\alpha'',Q'']$ for every exact sequence of triples and $[P,\alpha,Q]+[Q,\beta,T]=[P,\beta\alpha,T]$ for composable triples. The forgetful homomorphism $\rho:K_0(A,B)\to K_0(A)$ is given on generators by $\rho([P,\alpha,Q])=[P]-[Q]$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The relative-triple presentation above and the group $K_0(A)$.

[F1] [[def-algebraic-k0-and-k1-of-a-ring]]: $K_0(A)$ satisfies $[P\oplus Q]=[P]+[Q]$.

[F2] [[def-projective-module]]: a projective module lifts maps through surjections, so a short exact sequence with projective quotient splits.

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Isomorphic triples have isomorphic left and right projectives, so their proposed images under $\rho$ agree. In an exact sequence of triples, the quotient projectives $P''$ and $Q''$ are projective, so [F2] splits each underlying short exact sequence; [F1] then gives $[P]-[Q]=([P']-[Q'])+([P'']-[Q''])$. Thus the exact-sequence relation is respected. [F1, F2, given, algebra]

2.1 For composable triples, the proposed images satisfy $([P]-[Q])+([Q]-[T])=[P]-[T]$, so the composition relation is respected. These are all defining relations, hence $\rho$ is well-defined. [F1, given, algebra] ∎
