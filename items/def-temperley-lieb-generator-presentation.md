## Definition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring with unit and $\delta\in R$. For each $n\ge0$, let $F_n:=R\langle u_1,\ldots,u_{n-1}\rangle$ be the free associative unital $R$-algebra on the indicated symbols ([[lem-hh-free-associative-ring-and-relations-descent]]). When $n=0$ or $n=1$, the symbol set is empty and $F_n=R$.

Let $I_n$ be the two-sided ideal generated by

$$u_i^2-\delta u_i\quad(1\le i<n),\qquad u_i u_j-u_j u_i\quad(|i-j|>1),$$

and

$$u_i u_{i+1}u_i-u_i,\qquad u_{i+1}u_i u_{i+1}-u_{i+1}\quad(1\le i\le n-2).$$

The abstract **rank-$n$ Temperley–Lieb algebra** is the presented $R$-algebra

$$P_n(R,\delta):=F_n/I_n.$$

Thus its defining relations are $u_i^2=\delta u_i$, $u_i u_{i\pm1}u_i=u_i$, and $u_i u_j=u_j u_i$ for $|i-j|>1$. The definition of a presented $R$-algebra and its universal property are those of [[lem-hh-free-associative-ring-and-relations-descent]].

## Remarks

The parameter convention here is fixed by the loop rule: a closed component contributes $\delta$. No rescaling by $\delta$, division by $\delta$, or field hypothesis occurs in the presentation.
