## Definition

Assume the Axiom of Choice as inherited from the affine quotient and gluing
constructions ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]). Let $X$ be a scheme, let $L$ be an
invertible $\mathcal O_X$-module ([[def-invertible-sheaf]]) with dual
$L^{-1}=L^{\vee}=\mathcal H om_{\mathcal O_X}(L,\mathcal O_X)$, and let
$$s\in\Gamma(X,L)$$
be a global section.

**The ideal of the section.** The duality pairing
$L^{-1}\otimes_{\mathcal O_X}L\to\mathcal O_X$ and $s$ induce the
**contraction map**
$$c_s:L^{-1}\longrightarrow\mathcal O_X,\qquad \varphi\longmapsto \varphi(s),$$
defined on an open $U\subseteq X$ by $\varphi\mapsto\varphi(s|_U)$. Its image
$$\mathcal I_s=\operatorname{Im}(c_s)\subseteq\mathcal O_X$$
is a quasi-coherent sheaf of ideals: $c_s$ is a morphism of quasi-coherent
$\mathcal O_X$-modules and the image of such a morphism is quasi-coherent
([[thm-kernels-cokernels-qc-modules]]). The **zero scheme** of $s$ is the
closed subscheme
$$Z(s)=V(\mathcal I_s)\hookrightarrow X$$
cut out by this ideal sheaf, in the sense of the affine quotient description
of closed subschemes ([[lem-closed-immersion-affine-quotient-and-base-change]]):
on an affine open $U=\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq X$ the intersection
$Z(s)\cap U$ is $\operatorname{Spec}(A/I_s)$ with $I_s=\Gamma(U,\mathcal I_s)$.

**Local equations.** Let $U=\operatorname{Spec}A$ be an affine open on which $L$
is trivialised, $\varphi:L|_U\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\mathcal O_U$, and let
$$f=\varphi(s|_U)\in A$$
be the corresponding **local equation** of $s$. Then under $\varphi$ the map
$c_s|_U$ becomes multiplication by $f$,
$$\mathcal O_U\xrightarrow{\ \cdot f\ }\mathcal O_U,$$
by the identification $L^{-1}|_U\cong\mathcal O_U$ dual to $\varphi$, so
$\mathcal I_s|_U=(f)$ and
$$Z(s)\cap U=\operatorname{Spec}\bigl(A/(f)\bigr).$$
A different trivialisation $\varphi'=\varphi u$ with $u\in A^{\times}$ replaces
$f$ by $uf$, hence does not change the ideal $(f)$; the local descriptions
therefore glue along overlaps by the canonicity of the ideal $(f)$
([[thm-gluing-affine-schemes]]), and $Z(s)$ is well defined independently of
trivialisations.

**Nowhere-vanishing sections.** If $s$ vanishes nowhere, then each local
equation $f$ is a unit, so $\mathcal I_s=\mathcal O_X$, the induced closed
immersion $Z(s)\to X$ is an isomorphism onto the empty subscheme, and one says
$Z(s)=\varnothing$; this is the empty divisor case below.

**Effective Cartier divisors.** $Z(s)$ is an **effective Cartier divisor** —
that is, $\mathcal I_s$ is an invertible sheaf of ideals, locally generated by
one nonzerodivisor — precisely when for one (equivalently every) cover of $X$
by affine opens $U=\operatorname{Spec}A$ trivialising $L$, each local equation
$f\in A$ of $s$ is a nonzerodivisor or a unit of $A$. If every local equation
is a unit, $Z(s)=\varnothing$ is the **empty divisor**; otherwise $Z(s)$ is a
closed subscheme locally cut out by a nonzerodivisor wherever a non-unit
equation occurs, and its ideal sheaf is invertible there. No hypothesis on
$X$ beyond the trivialisations of $L$ enters.

## Remarks

- **Consistency with the affine case.** If $L=\mathcal O_X$ and $s=f$ is a
  global function, then $c_s$ is multiplication by $f$ and $Z(s)=V(f)$ is the
  closed subscheme of zeros of $f$; this is the classical zero locus.
- **Suppliers.** The identifications $L^{-1}\otimes L\cong\mathcal O_X$ and the
  quasi-coherence of images are cited from
  [[lem-invertible-sheaf-dual-tensor-inverse]] and
  [[thm-kernels-cokernels-qc-modules]].
