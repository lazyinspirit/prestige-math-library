## Example

Assume AC, Countable Choice, and DC.

For a prime power $q$ and an integer $m\ge1$, let $F=\mathbb F_q(t)$, let
$v_\infty$ be the place at infinity and let
$a\in\mathbb A_F^\times$ have component $t^{-1}$ at $v_\infty$ and component
$1$ at every finite place. Then $\deg([a])=1$. For the constant extension
$L=\mathbb F_{q^m}(t)/F$, the completion at infinity is
$\mathbb F_{q^m}((t^{-1}))/\mathbb F_q((t^{-1}))$, an unramified extension
whose arithmetic Frobenius is $\sigma:x\mapsto x^q$ on constants. Global
reciprocity sends $[a]$ to $\sigma$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The rational function field, its normalized place valuations, the
idele degree, and the constant extension specified in the Example.

[A1] AC, Countable Choice, and DC are the choice assumptions of the degree
and class-field interfaces. ([[def-axiom-of-choice]],
[[def-countable-choice]], [[def-dependent-choice]])

[F1] The degree of an idele class is
$\sum_v\deg(v)\operatorname{ord}_v(b_v)$ and is trivial on principal ideles.
([[thm-function-field-idele-class-degree-exact-sequence]])

[F2] At infinity put $u=t^{-1}$. The $u$-adic completion of
$\mathbb F_q(u)$ is $\mathbb F_q((u))$: a Cauchy sequence has stable
coefficients at each fixed power of $u$, and every Laurent series is the
limit of its finite truncations. The same argument gives
$\mathbb F_{q^m}((u))$ for the extension of constants.
([[def-completion-of-a-global-field-at-a-place]])

[F3] The local degree is the product of ramification index and residue
degree, and the finite-field extension has cyclic Galois group generated by
$x\mapsto x^q$.
([[thm-finite-extension-local-degree-equals-ramification-index-times-residue-degree]],
[[thm-extensions-of-finite-fields-are-galois-with-cyclic-frobenius-group]])

[F4] The local extension is unramified with Frobenius induced by
$x\mapsto x^q$ on the residue field.
([[thm-finite-unramified-local-extensions-classified-by-residue-extensions]])

[F5] Local reciprocity sends a uniformizer in an unramified extension to
arithmetic Frobenius, and global reciprocity restricts to the local map.
([[thm-local-artin-reciprocity-and-norm-index]],
[[thm-local-artin-unramified-frobenius-normalization]],
[[thm-global-function-field-artin-reciprocity-and-norm-index]])

## Verification

**Given:** The data in the Example and Facts & Assumptions.

1.1 The place $v_\infty$ has degree one and $t^{-1}$ is its uniformizer.
Thus $\deg([a])=1\cdot\operatorname{ord}_{v_\infty}(t^{-1})=1$ by [F1].
[A1, F1, algebra]

1.2 By [F2], the local extension at infinity is
$\mathbb F_{q^m}((u))/\mathbb F_q((u))$. Decomposing each Laurent-series
coefficient in a fixed $\mathbb F_q$-basis of $\mathbb F_{q^m}$ shows that
this basis remains a basis over $\mathbb F_q((u))$, so the local degree is
$m$; the residue degree is $m$ as well. By [F3] the ramification index is
one. The coefficientwise automorphism $\sigma$ reduces to $x\mapsto x^q$,
so [F4] identifies it with arithmetic Frobenius. [A1, F2, F3, F4, step 1.1, algebra]

2.1 The component $u=t^{-1}$ is a uniformizer. By [F5], its local
reciprocity image is $\sigma$; all other components of $a$ are $1$. Global
compatibility in [F5] therefore gives
$\operatorname{Art}_{L/F}([a])=\sigma$. [A1, F5, step 1.2] ∎
