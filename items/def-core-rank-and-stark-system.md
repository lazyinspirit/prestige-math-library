## Definition

Let $K$ be a number field, let $R$ be a commutative principal local ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$, and let $(T,\mathcal F)$ be a Selmer structure over that same ring: for Artinian $R$ use the Artinian Galois-representation definition, while for the previously covered non-Artinian p-adic case use the existing p-adic Selmer-structure definition. A discrete valuation ring (DVR) means a commutative local principal ideal domain that is not a field; its maximal ideal is generated by a uniformizer. Include as part of the data a dual Galois module $T^\vee$, meaning a continuous $R$-module with $G_K$-action, together with perfect local pairings between $H^1(K_v,T)$ and $H^1(K_v,T^\vee)$; the dual local condition $\mathcal F^\vee$ is the orthogonal complement of $\mathcal F$. For a $G_K$-module $M$, write $M^{G_K}=\{m\in M:gm=m\text{ for all }g\in G_K\}$. A local condition is **cartesian** when, for every ideal $I\subset R$, its image on $T/IT$ is the assigned local condition and its restriction to $T[I]=\{t\in T:It=0\}$ is the inverse image of the assigned local condition on $T[I]$. Let $P$ be an admissible set of auxiliary prime ideals disjoint from the ramification and local-condition set. Include ideals $I_q\subseteq R$ and transverse local conditions $H^1_{\rm tr}(K_q,T/I_nT)$ for each squarefree product $n$ of finitely many primes in $P$, with $I_1=0$, $I_n=\prod_{q\mid n}I_q$, and $\nu(n)$ the number of prime factors of $n$. Let $\mathcal N(P)$ be the set of these products, including $1$. Let $\mathcal F_n$ agree with $\mathcal F$ away from primes dividing $n$ and be unrestricted at those primes. Require the induced local conditions on all quotients $T/I_nT$ to be defined and compatible under quotient maps.

Assume $T^{G_K}=(T^\vee)^{G_K}=0$. When $R$ is a principal artinian local ring, a **core rank** is the integer $r$, independent of admissible $n$, for which the primal and dual Selmer modules satisfy
$$H^1_{\mathcal F_n}(K,T)\cong H^1_{\mathcal F_n^\vee}(K,T^\vee)\oplus R^r\quad(r\ge0),$$
or
$$H^1_{\mathcal F_n}(K,T)\oplus R^{-r}\cong H^1_{\mathcal F_n^\vee}(K,T^\vee)\quad(r\le0).$$
For a DVR, the core rank is the common core rank of all reductions modulo $\mathfrak m^a$, $a\ge1$. These conditions define the core rank only when the stated integer exists and is independent of the reductions; no existence theorem is asserted here. In the Kolyvagin-system applications below the chosen rank parameter is positive.

Assume that for each $q\in P$ the transverse local module on every $T/I_nT$ is free of rank one over $R/I_n$. Put
$$W_n=\bigoplus_{q\mid n}\operatorname{Hom}_{R/I_n}(H^1_{\mathrm{tr}}(K_q,T/I_nT),R/I_n),$$
$$Y_n=\bigwedge_{R/I_n}^{r+\nu(n)}H^1_{\mathcal F_n}(K,T/I_nT)\otimes_{R/I_n}\bigwedge_{R/I_n}^{\nu(n)}W_n.$$
Here $\bigwedge_A^j M$ is the quotient of $M^{\otimes_A j}$ by the alternating relations over the ring $A$. For each $m\mid n$, the Selmer datum also includes a module $Y_{m,n}$ obtained from the $m$-level local conditions with coefficients $R/I_n$, a reduction map $\rho_{m,n}:Y_m\to Y_{m,n}$, and a linear transition map $\Psi_{n,m}:Y_n\to Y_{m,n}$ induced by localization and contraction; these maps are required to satisfy the composition law for every $a\mid m\mid n$. A **rank-$r$ Stark system** is a family $\epsilon=(\epsilon_n)_{n\in\mathcal N(P)}$ with $\epsilon_n\in Y_n$ such that $\rho_{m,n}(\epsilon_m)=\Psi_{n,m}(\epsilon_n)$ for every $m\mid n$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The Selmer structure, dual local pairings, cartesian local conditions, vanishing invariants, auxiliary prime set, quotient local conditions, transverse local modules, and coherent localization/contraction maps in the Definition.

[F1] In the Artinian case, the Selmer structure consists of local submodules and unramified conditions away from a finite set, and its global Selmer module is the simultaneous localization kernel. In the previously covered p-adic case, the existing Selmer-structure definition gives its localization-kernel convention. [[def-selmer-structure-for-artinian-galois-representation]] [[def-selmer-structure-for-a-galois-representation]]

[A1] The core-rank decomposition and its independence of $n$ and of the finite reduction are hypotheses on the input data; this definition does not assert that such an integer exists for every Selmer structure.

[A2] The quotient local conditions, rank-one transverse modules, and transition maps are part of the Selmer datum; no arithmetic existence of these data is asserted.

## Verification

1.1 For fixed $n$, each summand of $W_n$ is an $R/I_n$-module, and the Selmer group in $Y_n$ is an $R/I_n$-module by its kernel definition. Tensor and exterior powers over $R/I_n$ therefore make $Y_n$ an $R/I_n$-module. [given, F1, A2, algebra]

2.1 The transition maps are linear and satisfy the stipulated composition law, so their compatibility equations are preserved by coordinatewise addition and scalar multiplication. The compatible families thus form a well-defined module with the induced scalar action. [given, step 1.1, A2, algebra] ∎
