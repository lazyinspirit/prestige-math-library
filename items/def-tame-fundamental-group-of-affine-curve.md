## Definition

Assume the Axiom of Choice. For an affine hyperbolic curve $U=C\setminus D$ over a field $k$, let $\bar U=U_{\bar k}$. A finite étale cover of $U$ is tame along $D$ when, after base change to $\bar k$, each connected component's extension of the discrete valuation at each geometric point of $D$ has ramification index prime to $p=\operatorname{char}(k)$ and separable residue extension; when $p=0$, the ramification-index condition is vacuous. The tame fundamental group $\pi_1^t(U,\bar u)$ is the profinite group of the Galois category of finite étale covers of $U$ tame along $D$, with a chosen geometric basepoint $\bar u$. For $p>0$, wild inertia at a boundary point is the maximal pro-$p$ subgroup of its inertia group; for $p=0$, it is trivial. Equivalently, $\pi_1^t(U,\bar u)$ is the quotient of $\pi_1^{\mathrm{et}}(U,\bar u)$ by the closed normal subgroup generated by these wild inertia subgroups. An isomorphism of compactification pairs induces an equivalence of these Galois categories and therefore an isomorphism of the associated profinite groups; a change of compatible geometric basepoint changes this group identification by inner conjugation.
