## Definition

Assume full AC. For an even-order integral matrix $V$ that is the Seifert matrix of a knot, **S-equivalence** means the equivalence relation generated by integral unimodular congruences $V\mapsto P^TVP$ with $\det P=\pm1$ and the following two enlargements, for column vectors $x,y\in\mathbb Z^n$ and $z\in\mathbb Z$:

$$E_0(V;x,y,z)=\begin{pmatrix}V&x&0\\y^T&z&0\\0^T&1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad E_1(V;x,y,z)=\begin{pmatrix}V&x&0\\y^T&z&1\\0^T&0&0\end{pmatrix},$$
and their reverses. For an assignment $P$ of an integer polynomial in $z$ to each ambient-isotopy class of oriented links, call $P$ **Conway-normalized** when $P_\varnothing(z)=0$ for the empty link, $P_U(z)=1$ for the unknot $U$, and
$$P_{L_+}(z)-P_{L_-}(z)=z\,P_{L_0}(z)$$
whenever the three oriented links agree outside one crossing ball and inside it are respectively the positive crossing, negative crossing, and orientation-compatible smoothing; positive means $\det_P(v_{\rm over},v_{\rm under})>0$, where $v_{\rm over},v_{\rm under}$ are the projected oriented tangent vectors and $P$ is oriented so the viewing normal followed by a positive basis of $P$ is ambient-positive. The smoothing reconnects the four oriented half-arcs in the unique noncrossing way compatible with their directions. No existence of such an assignment is presupposed: the Seifert-determinant lemma constructs the unique assignment on nonempty links; extending it by $\nabla_\varnothing(z)=0$ gives the unique assignment on this full domain, denoted by $\nabla_L(z)$. For a knot, let $\Delta_K^\#(t)$ be the unique associate of its Alexander polynomial satisfying $\Delta_K^\#(1)=1$ and $\Delta_K^\#(t^{-1})=\Delta_K^\#(t)$; existence and uniqueness follow from the Alexander-normalization corollary. Set $z=t^{1/2}-t^{-1/2}$, with $t^{1/2}$ a formal Laurent variable, then $\Delta_K^\#(t)=\nabla_K(z)$, as justified by the centered determinant below.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** Full AC, an oriented link and, in the knot case, a symmetric normalized Alexander polynomial.

[A1] Full AC is the choice-function axiom assumed by the Seifert-pairing suppliers. [[def-axiom-of-choice]]

[F1] The integral congruence and enlargement operations are the declared S-equivalence generators. [[def-seifert-matrix-s-equivalence]]

[F2] For every nonempty oriented link, the dependent lemma constructs a connected Seifert surface and proves the determinant formula and skein uniqueness. The empty link occurs in no crossing-ball skein triple, so its separately prescribed value extends that unique assignment without a new compatibility condition. [[lem-conway-polynomial-from-seifert-determinant]]

[F3] For a genus-$g$ knot surface put $p(t)=\det(V-tV^T)$. The determinant theorem identifies $p$ with the Alexander generator up to a Laurent unit. The skew-part lemma gives $\det(V-V^T)=1$ in a handle basis and hence every integral basis. Transposition gives $p(t)=t^{2g}p(t^{-1})$, so $t^{-g}p(t)$ is symmetric and has value $1$ at $t=1$. The normalization corollary therefore identifies it with $\Delta_K^\#(t)$. With $u^2=t$, the dependent Conway lemma gives $\nabla_K(u-u^{-1})=\det(u^{-1}V-uV^T)=t^{-g}p(t)$, proving the claimed equality rather than imposing it as a convention.
