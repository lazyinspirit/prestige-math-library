## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice, inherited from the Proj construction and from the
cohomology suppliers ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]). Let $A$ be a commutative ring
with $1$, let $I=(x,y)\subseteq A$ be an ideal generated by a regular sequence
$(x,y)$ (for example $A$ a regular local ring of dimension two with parameters
$x,y$), let $\pi\colon\operatorname{Bl}_I\operatorname{Spec}A\to
\operatorname{Spec}A$ be the blowup of [[def-blowup-scheme-along-ideal]] and
let $E$ be its exceptional subscheme. Then the two standard charts
$X_0=D_+(xt)$ and $X_1=D_+(yt)$ cover
$\operatorname{Bl}_I\operatorname{Spec}A$, the ordered Čech complex of this
two-element affine cover computes the cohomology of
$\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Bl}_I\operatorname{Spec}A}$
([[thm-cech-computes-qc-cohomology-separated-scheme-affine-cover]]), and
$$H^0\bigl(\operatorname{Bl}_I\operatorname{Spec}A,\mathcal O\bigr)=A,\quad H^1\bigl(\operatorname{Bl}_I\operatorname{Spec}A,\mathcal O\bigr)=0,\quad H^q\bigl(\operatorname{Bl}_I\operatorname{Spec}A,\mathcal O\bigr)=0\ \ (q\ge2).$$
Consequently $\pi_*\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Bl}_I\operatorname{Spec}
A}=\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Spec}A}$ and
$R^q\pi_*\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Bl}_I\operatorname{Spec}A}=0$ for all
$q>0$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A commutative ring $A$, an ideal $I=(x,y)\subseteq A$ such that $x$ is a nonzerodivisor of $A$ and $y$ is a nonzerodivisor of $A/(x)$, the Rees algebra $R(I)=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}I^nt^n$ ([[def-rees-algebra-ideal-sheaf]]), the blowup $\pi\colon\operatorname{Bl}_I\operatorname{Spec}A=\operatorname{Proj}R(I) \to\operatorname{Spec}A$ ([[def-blowup-scheme-along-ideal]]), and the relative projective line $\mathbb P^1_A=\operatorname{Proj}A[u,v]$ with twisting sheaves $\mathcal O(d)$ ([[def-twisting-sheaf-proj]]).

[F1] [[thm-affine-blowup-standard-charts]]: For $I=(f_0,\dots,f_r)$ and $B_i=A[I/f_i]$ the standard opens $D_+(f_it)=\operatorname{Spec}B_i$ cover $\operatorname{Bl}_I\operatorname{Spec}A$, with overlap identifications given by the ratios $(f_jt)/(f_it)$.

[F2] [[thm-closed-subschemes-projective-space-homogeneous-ideals]]: A homogeneous ideal $\mathfrak a\subseteq A[u,v]$ determines a closed subscheme $V_+(\mathfrak a)\hookrightarrow\mathbb P^1_A$, equal to $\operatorname{Proj}\bigl(A[u,v]/\mathfrak a\bigr)$ under the canonical closed immersion, and $V_+(\mathfrak a)\cap D_+(u) =\operatorname{Spec}\bigl(A[u,v]_{(u)}/\mathfrak a_{(u)}\bigr)$.

[F3] [[lem-projective-hypersurface-cohomology-sequence]]: For $f\in A[u,v]$
homogeneous of degree $d>0$ with every dehomogenisation $f/u^d$, $f/v^d$ a
nonzerodivisor of the corresponding chart ring, the multiplication map
$\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1_A}(-d)\xrightarrow{\cdot f}\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1_A}$
and the structure map of the closed immersion
$i\colon V_+(f)\hookrightarrow\mathbb P^1_A$ form a short exact sequence
$$0\to\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1_A}(-d)\xrightarrow{\cdot f}\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1_A}\xrightarrow{i^\sharp}i_*\mathcal O_{V_+(f)}\to0,$$
and the induced long exact sequence of sheaf cohomology computes the
cohomology of $V_+(f)$ from that of the twists $\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1_A}(d)$.

[F4] [[thm-cohomology-projective-space-twisting-sheaves]]: On $\mathbb P^1_A$ one has $H^0(\mathcal O)=A$, $H^1(\mathcal O)=0$, $H^q(\mathcal O)=0$ for $q\ge2$, and $H^0(\mathcal O(-1))=0$, $H^1(\mathcal O(-1))=0$; indeed $H^q(\mathbb P^1_A,\mathcal O(d))=0$ unless $q=0$ or $q=1$, with $H^0$ the degree-$d$ part of $A[u,v]$ for $d\ge0$ and $H^1$ the free $A$-module on the Laurent monomials $u^{e_0}v^{e_1}$ with $e_0,e_1<0$ and $e_0+e_1=d$, which is zero for $d=-1$.

[F5] [[thm-cech-computes-qc-cohomology-separated-scheme-affine-cover]]: For a quasi-compact separated scheme $X$, a finite affine open cover and a quasi-coherent $\mathcal O_X$-module $\mathcal F$, the canonical comparison map $\check H^q(\mathcal U,\mathcal F)\to H^q(X,\mathcal F)$ is an isomorphism for every $q\ge0$.

[F6] [[lem-two-open-cover-cech-complex]]: For a cover by two open sets $U_0,U_1$, the ordered Čech complex has $C^0=\mathcal F(U_0)\oplus\mathcal F(U_1)$, $C^1=\mathcal F(U_0\cap U_1)$ and $C^p=0$ for $p\ge2$, with $\delta^0(s_0,s_1)=s_1|_{U_0\cap U_1}-s_0|_{U_0\cap U_1}$; hence $\check H^0=\ker\delta^0$, $\check H^1=\operatorname{coker}\delta^0$ and $\check H^p=0$ for $p\ge2$.

[F7] [[lem-higher-direct-image-affine-localization]]: For a quasi-compact separated morphism $f\colon X\to S$ and a quasi-coherent $\mathcal O_X$-module $\mathcal F$, each $R^qf_*\mathcal F$ is quasi-coherent ([[def-higher-direct-image-sheaf]]), and for every affine open $V=\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq S$ there is a canonical isomorphism $(R^qf_*\mathcal F)|_V\cong\widetilde{H^q(f^{-1}V,\mathcal F)}$.

[F8] [[def-closed-immersion-schemes]], [[def-quasi-compact-and-quasi-separated-scheme]], [[def-separated-morphism-schemes]]: A closed subscheme of a quasi-compact scheme is quasi-compact, and a closed subscheme of a separated scheme is separated; the relative projective line $\mathbb P^1_A$ is quasi-compact and separated over $\operatorname{Spec}A$.

## Proof

1.1 The graded $A$-algebra homomorphism $A[u,v]\to R(I)$ with $u\mapsto xt$, $v\mapsto yt$ is surjective because $I^n=(x,y)^n$ is generated by the monomials $x^{n-i}y^i$, and its kernel is $(xv-yu)$: for a homogeneous $F=\sum_{i=0}^nc_iu^{n-i}v^i$ with $F(x,y)=0$, reduction modulo $x$ gives $c_ny^n\equiv0\pmod x$, so $\bar c_n=0$ because $y$ is a nonzerodivisor modulo $x$, say $c_n=xd$; then $F-dv^{n-1}(xv-yu)$ has zero $v^n$-coefficient, hence equals $uG$ for a homogeneous $G$ of degree $n-1$, and evaluating at $(x,y)$ gives $xG(x,y)=0$, so $G(x,y)=0$ because $x$ is a nonzerodivisor, and induction on $n$ down to degree $0$ yields $F\in(xv-yu)$, while conversely $xv-yu\mapsto x\cdot yt-y\cdot xt=0$; hence $R(I)\cong A[u,v]/(xv-yu)$ as graded $A$-algebras. [F1]

1.2 The element $f:=xv-yu\in A[u,v]$ is a nonzerodivisor: as a polynomial in $v$ over $A[u]$ its leading coefficient is the nonzerodivisor $x$, so $fg=0$ forces the top $v$-coefficient of $g$ to vanish and descending induction gives $g=0$; the dehomogenisation $f/u=x(v/u)-y$ is a nonzerodivisor of $A[v/u]$ for the same reason, and $yU-x$ is a nonzerodivisor of $A[U]$, since comparing coefficients of $U^i$ in $(yU-x)g=0$ gives $-xg_0=0$ and $yg_{i-1}=xg_i$ for $i\ge1$, so $g_0=0$ and induction gives all $g_i=0$; hence by [F2] the blowup is identified with the closed subscheme $\operatorname{Bl}_I\operatorname{Spec}A=\operatorname{Proj}\bigl(A[u,v]/(xv-yu)\bigr)=V_+(xv-yu)\subseteq\mathbb P^1_A$, whose standard charts $D_+(u)\cap V_+(f)=\operatorname{Spec}\bigl(A[v/u]/(x(v/u)-y)\bigr)=X_0$ and $D_+(v)\cap V_+(f)=\operatorname{Spec}\bigl(A[u/v]/(y(u/v)-x)\bigr)=X_1$ are affine and cover it, matching the standard blowup charts of [F1] under the identification of $u,v$ with $xt,yt$. [F1, F2]

1.3 On $\mathbb P^1_A$ the groups $H^0(\mathcal O)=A$, $H^1(\mathcal O)=0$, $H^q(\mathcal O)=0$ for $q\ge2$ and $H^0(\mathcal O(-1))=H^1(\mathcal O(-1))=0$ hold by [F4] with $n=1$. [F4]

2.1 Applying [F3] to the homogeneous degree-one element $f=xv-yu$, whose chart dehomogenisations are nonzerodivisors by step 1.2, gives a short exact sequence of $\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1_A}$-modules $0\to\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1_A}(-1)\xrightarrow{\cdot f}\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^1_A}\to i_*\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Bl}_I\operatorname{Spec}A}\to0$, where $i$ is the closed immersion of step 1.2. [F3, step 1.2]

3.1 The long exact cohomology sequence of step 2.1, with $H^q(\mathbb P^1_A,i_*\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Bl}})\cong H^q(\operatorname{Bl}_I\operatorname{Spec}A,\mathcal O)$ for the closed immersion $i$ and the groups of step 1.3, gives $H^0(\operatorname{Bl}_I\operatorname{Spec}A,\mathcal O)\cong\operatorname{coker}\bigl(H^0(\mathcal O(-1))\to H^0(\mathcal O)\bigr)=A$, then $H^1(\operatorname{Bl}_I\operatorname{Spec}A,\mathcal O)=0$ because it is squeezed between $H^1(\mathcal O)=0$ and $H^2(\mathcal O(-1))=0$, and for $q\ge2$ the group $H^q(\operatorname{Bl}_I\operatorname{Spec}A,\mathcal O)$ is squeezed between $H^q(\mathcal O)=0$ and $H^{q+1}(\mathcal O(-1))=0$. [F4, step 2.1, step 1.3]

4.1 By [F8] the blowup $\operatorname{Bl}_I\operatorname{Spec}A$ is a closed subscheme of the quasi-compact separated $\mathbb P^1_A$, hence quasi-compact and separated, so the two-element affine cover of step 1.2 has Čech cohomology computing the sheaf cohomology of $\mathcal O$ by [F5] and, by [F6], a Čech complex concentrated in degrees $0$ and $1$ with $\check H^0=\ker\delta^0$ and $\check H^1=\operatorname{coker}\delta^0$, so its Čech groups are $A$, $0$ and $0$ in degrees $0$, $1$ and $\ge2$, consistently with step 3.1. [F5, F6, F8, step 1.2, step 3.1]

5.1 The structural morphism $\pi$ is quasi-compact and separated, being the composite of the closed immersion of step 1.2 with the quasi-compact separated structure morphism of $\mathbb P^1_A$ from [F8], and $\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Bl}}$ is quasi-coherent, so [F7] with $V=\operatorname{Spec}A$ identifies $R^q\pi_*\mathcal O$ with the sheaf associated to the $A$-module $H^q(\operatorname{Bl}_I\operatorname{Spec}A,\mathcal O)$, which is $A$ in degree $0$ and $0$ for $q>0$ by step 3.1; hence $\pi_*\mathcal O=\widetilde A=\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Spec}A}$ and $R^q\pi_*\mathcal O=0$ for every $q>0$. [F7, F8, step 3.1] ∎

## Remarks

- Regularity of the sequence $(x,y)$ enters twice: to identify the Rees algebra with the incidence algebra $A[u,v]/(xv-yu)$ (step 1.1) and to make the dehomogenisations of $xv-yu$ nonzerodivisors (step 1.2). For a general pair of generators the map $A[u,v]\to R(I)$ has a nontrivial kernel in general, and the blowup need not be a hypersurface in $\mathbb P^1_A$.
- The theorem is stated for an affine base precisely so that the direct image computation reduces to the two cohomology modules $A$ and $0$; over a nonaffine base the same proof applies over each affine open, and the sheaf statement is the affine-local one of [F7].
- The two-chart computation never inverts $x$ or $y$ in $A$: the overlap identification inverts the ratio $v/u$ only, consistent with [F1].
