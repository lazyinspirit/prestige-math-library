## Statement

For every $n\ge1$, write $\operatorname{Sp}(V_n,\omega)$ for the group of invertible $\mathbb F_2$-linear maps $T:V_n\to V_n$ satisfying $\omega(Ta,Tb)=\omega(a,b)$ for all $a,b$. For every $T\in\operatorname{Sp}(V_n,\omega)$ there is a Clifford circuit $U$ made from Hadamard gates $H$, phase gates $S,S^*$, CNOT, and wire swaps whose induced action on binary Pauli labels is $T$. Since the phase gate satisfies $S^*=S^3$, this group is also generated by $H$, $S$, CNOT, and swaps. Hence the conjugation homomorphism from the Clifford normalizer to $\operatorname{Sp}(V_n,\omega)$ is onto. The proof uses finite transvection reduction and exact mod-$4$ phase updates at every gate.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** the finite-dimensional binary symplectic space $V_n$, the Clifford normalizer, and the stated transvection generation and lifting lemmas.

[F1] [[lem-qc-symplectic-transvections-generate]]: every symplectic map is a finite product of maps $\tau_v(w)=w+\omega(w,v)v$.

[F2] [[lem-qc-clifford-lifts-symplectic-transvections]]: each such transvection has a Clifford circuit lift built from $H,S,S^*$, CNOT, and swaps, with its mod-$4$ phases computable gate by gate.

[F3] [[def-qc-clifford-normalizer]]: the Clifford normalizer is closed under products.

[F4] [[thm-qc-binary-pauli-quotient-isomorphism]]: binary labels identify the scalar quotient of the Pauli group.

[F5] [[thm-qc-clifford-induces-symplectic-map]]: each Clifford has its induced symplectic map, and $S_{UV}=S_U\circ S_V$.

## Proof

1.1 Let $T\in\operatorname{Sp}(V_n,\omega)$. By [F1], choose a finite factorization $T=\tau_{v_m}\cdots\tau_{v_1}$, omitting zero vectors if desired. This factorization is obtained by the finite induction in [F1], so no choice axiom is used. [given, F1, choose]

2.1 For each factor, take a Clifford circuit $U_j$ from [F2] whose binary action is $\tau_{v_j}$. The finite product $U=U_m\cdots U_1$ lies in $\mathcal C_n$ by [F3]. By [F5], its induced symplectic action is $S_U=S_{U_m}\cdots S_{U_1}=\tau_{v_m}\cdots\tau_{v_1}=T$. The quotient identification [F4] ensures these are exactly the binary labels of conjugated Paulis. Every constituent gate updates its phase coordinate modulo $4$ by the formulas in [F2], so the product is an exact Clifford lift as well as a binary one. [given, F2, F3, F4, F5, step 1.1, construct, algebra] ∎
