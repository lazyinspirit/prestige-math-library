## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]).

Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero and let $t\ge1$. For each integer $0\le m\le t$, in the polynomial ring of a generic symmetric $t\times t$ matrix $Z$, the ideal $I_{m+1}$ generated by all $(m+1)$-minors is prime and its zero set is the locus $\operatorname{rank}Z\le m$; $k[Z]/I_{m+1}$ is normal. For each even integer $0\le m\le t$, in the polynomial ring of a generic alternating $t\times t$ matrix $A$, the ideal $J_{m+2}$ generated by the principal Pfaffians of size $m+2$ is prime and its zero set is the locus $\operatorname{rank}A\le m$; $k[A]/J_{m+2}$ is normal. When the indicated generator size exceeds $t$, the corresponding ideal is zero and the quotient is polynomial.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The Axiom of Choice and the straightening and normality supplier obligations named in the Statement and in [[lem-gram-map-fibers-and-rank-strata]].

[F1] [[lem-generic-symmetric-minor-straightening]] and [[lem-generic-symmetric-determinantal-ring-normal]]: the symmetric standard basis and normality argument are required inputs.

[F2] [[lem-generic-pfaffian-straightening]] and [[lem-generic-pfaffian-ring-normal]]: the Pfaffian standard basis and normality argument are required inputs.

## Proof

1.1 A matrix has rank at most $m$ exactly when all $(m+1)$-minors vanish, by Gaussian elimination. Thus the symmetric zero locus is precisely the asserted set. For an alternating matrix use the congruence normal form in [[lem-gram-map-fibers-and-rank-strata]]: the coefficients of the $(n+1)$st exterior power of its two-form are $(n+1)!$ times its principal $(2n+2)$-Pfaffians. The factorial is nonzero in characteristic zero. In the canonical form consisting of $r/2$ symplectic blocks, this exterior power is zero exactly when $r\le2n=m$. Consequently the Pfaffian zero locus is precisely the rank-$\le m$ set, including $m=0$ and the case of no possible generators. [given, algebra]

2.1 The normality suppliers in [F1] and [F2] assert, with the same variables and ideals as here, that each indicated quotient is an integrally closed domain. Therefore their defining ideals are prime. Combined with step 1.1, their zero sets are exactly the stated rank-bounded loci. If the generator size exceeds $t$, the ideal is empty-generated, hence zero, and its quotient is the polynomial ring. [F1, F2, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
