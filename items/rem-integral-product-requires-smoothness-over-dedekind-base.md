## Scope caveat

The full graded product on arithmetic Chow groups is asserted with rational coefficients for a regular arithmetic variety with quasi-projective generic fibre. The source gives a full integral graded product under the stronger hypothesis that the arithmetic variety is smooth over the Dedekind base and has projective generic fibre. Under regularity alone, the source separately gives only the integral pairing when one factor has codimension one and the cycles meet properly on the whole arithmetic variety; this special pairing does not supply a full integral ring.

## Scope caveat references

[Gillet–Soulé, Theorem 4.2.3 and Theorem 4.5.1](https://www.numdam.org/article/PMIHES_1990__72__93_0.pdf)

## Facts & Assumptions

[F1] The rational graded product and its hypotheses are the statement of [[thm-arithmetic-chow-intersection-product]].

[F2] The separate codimension-one integral pairing is the distinct source scope of Gillet–Soulé Theorem 4.2.3(iii). It is not contained in the Statement of the current full-product supplier. This remark records that bibliographic distinction and does not adopt the pairing as an external proof input.

## Authoring scope

This is a source-scope caveat, not a construction of either product or the codimension-one pairing. The recorded rational/integral distinctions are unchanged. Those separate theorem proofs retain their own obligations; their completion is not a prerequisite for authoring this bibliographic caveat.
