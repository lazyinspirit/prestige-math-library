## Definition

A statistic $U:(\mathcal X,\mathcal A)\to(\mathcal Z,\mathcal C)$ is **ancillary** for $(P_\theta)$ if its pushforward probability $P_\theta\circ U^{-1}$ does not depend on $\theta$. Equivalently, for every $C\in\mathcal C$, $P_\theta(U\in C)$ is constant in $\theta$.
