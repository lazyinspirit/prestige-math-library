## Statement

Let $F$ be a field, let $n\ge1$, assume $\operatorname{char}F\nmid n$, assume
$\mu_n\subseteq F$, and let $a\in F^\times$. If the class of $a$ in
$F^\times/(F^\times)^n$ has order $d$, then

$$[F(\sqrt[n]{a}):F]=d.$$

## Facts & Assumptions
**Given:** The field $F$, the integer $n$, the element $a\in F^\times$, and the order $d$ of the class $\bar a=a(F^\times)^n$ in $F^\times/(F^\times)^n$.

[L1] The Kummer pairing on the one-generator extension is bilinear and nondegenerate in both variables ([[thm-kummer-pairing-is-perfect]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Let $K=F(\sqrt[n]{a})$. The subgroup of $F^\times/(F^\times)^n$ generated by $\bar a$ is cyclic of order $d$, and [L1] gives a homomorphism $$\phi\colon \operatorname{Gal}(K/F)\to\mu_n,\qquad \phi(\sigma)=\langle \sigma,\bar a\rangle.$$ If $\phi(\sigma)=1$, then $\sigma$ lies in the left kernel of the pairing, so $\sigma=1$ by [L1]. Thus $\phi$ is injective. [L1]

2.1 Let $H=\phi(\operatorname{Gal}(K/F))\le\mu_n$. If $|H|=m<d$, then every $h\in H$ satisfies $h^m=1$, so bilinearity makes $$\langle \sigma,\bar a^{\,m}\rangle=\langle \sigma,\bar a\rangle^{m}=1$$ for every $\sigma\in\operatorname{Gal}(K/F)$. Then $\bar a^{\,m}$ lies in the right kernel, contradicting [L1] because $\bar a$ has order $d$. Therefore $|H|=d$. Since $\phi$ is injective, this gives $$|\operatorname{Gal}(K/F)|=d.$$ [step 1.1, L1, algebra]

3.1 The extension $K/F$ is finite Galois in this one-generator Kummer situation, so its degree equals the order of its Galois group. Hence $[K:F]=d$. [step 2.1, L1, algebra] ∎
