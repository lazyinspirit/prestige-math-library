## Statement refuted

Assume the Axiom of Choice, inherited from the associated-sheaf existence
theorem and from the references recorded below
([[def-axiom-of-choice]]).

**False claim.** On every scheme, a quasi-coherent $\mathcal O_X$-module of
finite type is locally free of finite rank; in particular, on a locally
Noetherian scheme every coherent module is locally free
([[def-finite-type-finite-presentation-module-sheaf]],
[[def-locally-free-sheaf-finite-rank]],
[[thm-coherent-sheaves-abelian-noetherian-scheme]]).

The affine line refutes the claim. Let $k$ be a field, let $A=k[x]$ and let
$X=\operatorname{Spec}A$, and let
$$\mathcal F=\widetilde{A/(x)}=\widetilde M,\qquad M=A/(x)\cong k,$$
be the associated sheaf of the cyclic module $A/(x)$
([[def-associated-sheaf-module-affine-scheme]],
[[thm-associated-module-sheaf-exists]]). Since $k[x]$ is a principal ideal
domain, hence Noetherian, the scheme $X$ is locally Noetherian; since $M$ is
generated by the class of $1$, the sheaf $\mathcal F$ is of finite type and,
by the equivalence of coherence with finite type over a locally Noetherian
base, even coherent on $X$. Its fibre is one-dimensional at the closed point
$(x)$ and zero at every other point, so its fibre dimension is not locally
constant. Accordingly $\mathcal F$ is not locally free of finite rank at
$(x)$: if $\mathcal F|_U\cong\mathcal O_U^r$ on a neighbourhood $U$ of
$(x)$, then the generic point $(0)$ of $X$ lies in $U$, and the fibres of
$\mathcal O_U^r$ at $(0)$ and at $(x)$, which are $\kappa((0))^r$ and
$\kappa((x))^r$, would have to be the computed fibres $0$ and $k$, forcing
$r=0$ and $r=1$. Thus finite type, and even coherence, must be checked
separately from local freeness, and fibre dimension is not locally constant
for this coherent sheaf.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The Axiom of Choice; a field $k$; the polynomial ring $A=k[x]$;
the scheme $X=\operatorname{Spec}A$; the module $M=A/(x)$ with the class of
$1$ as a generator; the associated sheaf $\mathcal F=\widetilde M$; the
closed point $\mathfrak p_0=(x)\in X$ and the point $\eta=(0)\in X$.

[F1] The ring $A=k[x]$ and its spectrum ([[def-affine-scheme-spectrum]],
[[def-prime-spectrum-and-vanishing-sets]],
[[cor-polynomial-ring-over-a-field-is-a-pid]],
[[cor-principal-ideal-domains-are-noetherian]],
[[def-locally-noetherian-and-noetherian-scheme]],
[[ex-the-local-ring-of-polynomials-at-x]]): $k[x]$ is a principal ideal
domain, hence Noetherian, and $X$ is locally Noetherian; the points of $X$
are the prime ideals of $A$ and the basic opens are the sets
$D(f)=\{\mathfrak p:f\notin\mathfrak p\}$; evaluation at $0$ identifies
$A/(x)\cong k$, so $(x)$ is a maximal (hence prime) ideal and the local ring
$A_{(x)}$ has maximal ideal $xA_{(x)}$ and residue field
$A_{(x)}/xA_{(x)}\cong k$; consequently every prime of $A$ containing $x$
equals $(x)$, and $x\notin\mathfrak p$ for every prime
$\mathfrak p\neq(x)$. The chain of ideals is ordered by inclusion, $\eta$
and $\mathfrak p_0$ are distinct, and $A$ is a domain, so $\eta=(0)$ is a
prime and $0\neq x\in A$.

[F2] The associated sheaf ([[def-associated-sheaf-module-affine-scheme]],
[[thm-associated-module-sheaf-exists]],
[[lem-associated-sheaf-sections-basic-open]],
[[lem-associated-sheaf-stalk-localization]],
[[def-quasi-coherent-module-scheme]],
[[def-finite-type-finite-presentation-module-sheaf]],
[[thm-coherent-sheaves-abelian-noetherian-scheme]]): $\mathcal F=\widetilde
M$ is a sheaf of $\mathcal O_X$-modules whose distinguished-open sections are
$\mathcal F(D(f))=M_f$ with restriction the canonical localisation; its
stalks are $\mathcal F_{\mathfrak p}\cong M_{\mathfrak p}$; it is
quasi-coherent; because $M$ is finitely generated it is of finite type; and
because $X$ is locally Noetherian it is coherent.

[F3] Localisation at a prime
([[def-localisation-at-a-prime-ideal]],
[[thm-localisation-equivalence-and-ring-laws]],
[[thm-localisation-of-modules-commutes-with-quotients-and-sums]],
[[cor-residue-field-of-a-localisation-at-a-prime]]): $A_{\mathfrak p}$ is
the localisation of $A$ at $A\setminus\mathfrak p$, and every $s\notin
\mathfrak p$ is a unit of $A_{\mathfrak p}$ with inverse $1/s$; for every
submodule $N\subseteq A$ one has $(A/N)_{\mathfrak p}\cong
A_{\mathfrak p}/N_{\mathfrak p}$, and localisation commutes with arbitrary
direct sums, so $(A^r)_{\mathfrak p}\cong(A_{\mathfrak p})^r$ for $r\ge0$;
the residue field is $A_{\mathfrak p}/\mathfrak p A_{\mathfrak p}\cong
\operatorname{Frac}(A/\mathfrak p)$.

[F4] Stalks, fibres and restrictions
([[def-fibre-of-module-at-point]], [[def-residue-field-scheme-point]],
[[thm-stalk-structure-sheaf-prime-localization]]): the fibre of an
$\mathcal O_X$-module $\mathcal G$ at $x\in X$ is
$\mathcal G(x)=\mathcal G_x\otimes_{\mathcal O_{X,x}}\kappa(x)\cong
\mathcal G_x/\mathfrak m_x\mathcal G_x$, a vector space over
$\kappa(x)=\mathcal O_{X,x}/\mathfrak m_x$; a morphism of sheaves induces a
$\kappa(x)$-linear map on fibres; restriction to an open $U\subseteq X$
satisfies $(\mathcal G|_U)(x)=\mathcal G(x)$ for every $x\in U$; and for the
structure sheaf the stalk is $\mathcal O_{X,\mathfrak p}\cong A_{\mathfrak
p}$.

[F5] Free module sheaves on an affine scheme
([[def-associated-sheaf-module-affine-scheme]],
[[thm-associated-module-sheaf-exists]],
[[thm-sections-basic-open-affine-scheme]],
[[lem-associated-sheaf-stalk-localization]],
[[thm-localisation-of-modules-commutes-with-quotients-and-sums]],
[[def-fibre-of-module-at-point]]): for a commutative ring $B$ and $r\ge0$,
the structure sheaf of $\operatorname{Spec}B$ is the associated sheaf
$\widetilde B$ of the ring, the free $\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Spec}
B}$-module $\mathcal O^r$ is $\widetilde{B^r}$ (both have sections
$(B_g)^r=(B^r)_g$ on the distinguished open $D(g)$, and the extension from
distinguished opens is unique), and for every prime $\mathfrak q$ of $B$ the
stalk is $(\mathcal O^r)_{\mathfrak q}\cong(B^r)_{\mathfrak q}\cong
(B_{\mathfrak q})^r$, so that the fibre
$(\mathcal O^r)(\mathfrak q)\cong(B_{\mathfrak q})^r\otimes_{B_{\mathfrak
q}}\kappa(\mathfrak q)\cong\kappa(\mathfrak q)^r$ is a
$\kappa(\mathfrak q)$-vector space of dimension $r$.

[F6] Distinguished opens and localised spectra
([[lem-distinguished-open-refinement-at-a-point]],
[[cor-principal-localisation-spectrum-is-distinguished-open]],
[[lem-spectrum-localization-open-immersion]],
[[cor-primes-of-a-principal-localisation]]): for every open $U\subseteq X$
and every $\mathfrak p\in U$ there is $f\in A$ with
$\mathfrak p\in D(f)\subseteq U$; the localisation $A\to A_f$ identifies
$\operatorname{Spec}A_f$ with $D(f)$ as locally ringed spaces, and the primes
of $A_f$ correspond to the primes of $A$ not containing $f$.

[F7] The generic point ([[lem-closure-of-a-point-is-its-vanishing-set]],
[[def-prime-spectrum-and-vanishing-sets]]): the closure of a point
$\mathfrak p\in X$ is $\overline{\{\mathfrak p\}}=V(\mathfrak p)=\{\mathfrak
q:\mathfrak q\supseteq\mathfrak p\}$; since $A$ is a domain, every prime
contains $(0)$, so $V((0))=X$ and $\overline{\{(0)\}}=X$.

[F8] Locally free sheaves ([[def-locally-free-sheaf-finite-rank]],
[[def-module-on-ringed-space]]): $\mathcal E$ is locally free of rank $r$
near $x$ if some open neighbourhood $U$ of $x$ satisfies
$\mathcal E|_U\cong\mathcal O_U^r$, where $\mathcal O_U^r$ is the free
$\mathcal O_U$-module of rank $r$, with $\mathcal O_U^0=0$; $\mathcal E$ is
finite locally free if this holds at every point with some rank; a locally
free sheaf is quasi-coherent.

[F9] The Axiom of Choice is inherited from the associated-sheaf existence
theorem, the closure description of [F7] and the affine equivalence used
through [F3] and [F5]; no further choice is made in the computation below
([[def-axiom-of-choice]]).

**Proof technique:** direct; compute the stalk and fibre of
$\widetilde{A/(x)}$ at all points of $\operatorname{Spec}k[x]$, show that the
generic point lies in every nonempty open set, and use a free chart at the
closed point $(x)$ to force two different ranks $r=0$ and $r=1$.



## Proof

1.1 Setup and coherence: let $A=k[x]$, $X=\operatorname{Spec}A$, $M=A/(x)$ and $\mathcal F=\widetilde M$. By [F1] the ring $A$ is a principal ideal domain, hence Noetherian, so $X$ is locally Noetherian; the class of $1$ generates $M$, so $M$ is finitely generated and $\mathcal F$ is quasi-coherent of finite type by [F2], hence coherent on $X$ by the coherence theorem of [F2]. [F1, F2]

1.2 Stalks: for a prime $\mathfrak p\in X$ the isomorphisms $\mathcal F_{\mathfrak p}\cong M_{\mathfrak p}\cong A_{\mathfrak p}/xA_{\mathfrak p}$ hold by [F2] and [F3]. If $\mathfrak p\neq(x)$ then $x\notin\mathfrak p$ by [F1], so $x$ is a unit of $A_{\mathfrak p}$ by [F3] and therefore $xA_{\mathfrak p}=A_{\mathfrak p}$ and $\mathcal F_{\mathfrak p}=0$; in particular $\mathcal F_\eta=0$ for the generic point $\eta=(0)$, which is distinct from $(x)$ by [F1]. [F1, F2, F3]

1.3 The generic point lies in every nonempty open subset: by [F7] the closure of $\{(0)\}$ is $V((0))=X$, since every prime of the domain $A$ contains $(0)$. If $U\subseteq X$ were a nonempty open set with $(0)\notin U$, then $X\setminus U$ would be a closed set containing $(0)$, hence would contain $\overline{\{(0)\}}=X$, contradicting $U\neq\varnothing$; therefore $(0)\in U$ for every nonempty open $U\subseteq X$. [F7]

1.4 Fibres of free module sheaves on distinguished opens: for every $f\in A$, putting $V=D(f)$, the localisation $A\to A_f$ identifies $V$ with $\operatorname{Spec}A_f$ as locally ringed spaces by [F6], so the free sheaf $\mathcal O_V^r$ is the free module sheaf of rank $r$ on $\operatorname{Spec}A_f$; applying [F5] with $B=A_f$ gives $(\mathcal O_V^r)(y)\cong\kappa(y)^r$ at every $y\in V$, a vector space of dimension $r$ over the residue field $\kappa(y)$. [F5, F6]

2.1 Fibre at the closed point: at $\mathfrak p=(x)$ steps 1.2 and [F3] give $\mathcal F_{(x)}\cong A_{(x)}/xA_{(x)}=\kappa((x))\cong k$, the residue field of the local ring $A_{(x)}$, whose maximal ideal is $xA_{(x)}$ by [F1]; this maximal ideal annihilates $\mathcal F_{(x)}$, so the fibre is $\mathcal F((x))\cong\mathcal F_{(x)}/\mathfrak m_{(x)}\mathcal F_{(x)}\cong k$ by [F4], a one-dimensional $\kappa((x))$-vector space. [F1, F3, F4, step 1.2]

2.2 Fibre at the generic point: since $\eta=(0)\neq(x)$, step 1.2 gives $\mathcal F_\eta=0$, hence the fibre $\mathcal F(\eta)=0$ by [F4]. [F4, step 1.2]

2.3 A free chart at the closed point: assume now that $\mathcal F$ is finite locally free in the sense of [F8]. Then at the point $(x)$ there are an open neighbourhood $U\ni(x)$ and an integer $r\ge0$ with $\mathcal F|_U\cong\mathcal O_U^r$; by [F6] choose $f\in A$ with $(x)\in D(f)\subseteq U$ and put $V=D(f)$, so that restricting the isomorphism gives $\mathcal F|_V\cong\mathcal O_V^r$. Since $V$ is a nonempty open subset of $X$, step 1.3 gives $\eta=(0)\in V$. [F6, F8, step 1.3]

3.1 First rank constraint: the fibre of a restriction is the fibre by [F4], so steps 2.2 and 1.4 give $\kappa(\eta)^r\cong(\mathcal O_V^r)(\eta)=(\mathcal F|_V)(\eta)=\mathcal F(\eta)=0$; since $\kappa(\eta)$ is a field, $\kappa(\eta)^r=0$ forces $r=0$. [F4, step 2.2, step 1.4]

3.2 Second rank constraint: likewise steps 2.1 and 1.4 give $\mathcal F((x))=(\mathcal F|_V)((x))=(\mathcal O_V^r)((x))\cong\kappa((x))^r$, of $\kappa((x))$-dimension $r$; but by step 2.1 the fibre $\mathcal F((x))$ is one-dimensional over $\kappa((x))$, so $r=1$. [F4, step 2.1, step 1.4]

4.1 Conclusion: steps 3.1 and 3.2 give $r=0$ and $r=1$ for the rank of any free chart at $(x)$, a contradiction; hence the finite-type coherent sheaf $\mathcal F=\widetilde{A/(x)}$ on the locally Noetherian scheme $X=\operatorname{Spec}k[x]$ is not locally free of finite rank at $(x)$, while by step 1.1 it is coherent of finite type. Its fibre dimension is $1$ at $(x)$ and $0$ at every other point, in particular at the generic point, so this fibre dimension is not locally constant and the false claim is refuted; finite type and coherence must be checked separately from local freeness. [step 1.1, step 2.2, step 2.3, step 3.1, step 3.2]

5.1 Choice accounting: the field $k$, the ring $A=k[x]$, the module $M=A/(x)$, the closed point $(x)$ and the generic point $(0)$ are fixed data, and no chart, generator family or isomorphism is selected by an infinite simultaneous choice; the only Axiom of Choice is the inherited one recorded in [F9], used through the associated-sheaf existence theorem and the closure description of [F7]. [F9] ∎
