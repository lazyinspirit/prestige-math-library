## Statement

Kleinbock's expanding-horospherical theorem states the following.

Let $G$ be a real Lie group, let $\Gamma$ be a lattice, and put $\Omega=G/\Gamma$ with normalized invariant measure $\bar\mu$. Let $X$ be a semisimple element of the Lie algebra, let $F=\{\exp(tX):t\ge0\}$ act mixingly on $\Omega$, and let $H^+(F)$ be its nontrivial expanding horospherical subgroup. For every closed, $F$-invariant set $Z\subset\Omega$ with $\bar\mu(Z)=0$ and every $x\in\Omega$, the set
$$\{h\in H^+(F):hx\in E(F,Z^*)\}$$
is thick in $H^+(F)$: for every nonempty open $W\subset H^+(F)$, its intersection with $W$ has Hausdorff dimension $\dim_H W$. Here $Z^*=Z\cup\{\infty\}$ in the one-point compactification, and $E(F,Z^*)$ is the bounded-orbit set that stays away from $Z$.
