## Statement

Let $\sigma:F\to F'$ be a field isomorphism, let $0\ne f\in F[x]$, and put $f'=\sigma_*f$. If $E/F$ is a splitting field of $f$ and $E'/F'$ is a splitting field of $f'$, then $\sigma$ extends to a field isomorphism $E\to E'$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The fields, polynomial, splitting fields, and base isomorphism in the Statement.

[F1] Strong induction permits proving the assertion from all smaller polynomial degrees ([[thm-strong-induction]]).

[F2] A minimal polynomial is monic irreducible and divides every polynomial vanishing at its element ([[thm-evaluation-kernel-and-minimal-polynomial]]).

[F3] A base isomorphism extends uniquely across adjunctions of chosen corresponding roots of a transported irreducible polynomial ([[lem-an-isomorphism-extends-across-a-simple-root-adjunction]]).

[F4] Coefficient transport carries roots and factorizations to the corresponding roots and factorizations ([[lem-polynomials-and-roots-transport-along-field-isomorphisms]]).

[F5] For every field $K$, the polynomial ring $K[x]$ is a unique factorisation domain ([[thm-polynomial-ring-over-a-field-is-a-ufd]]).

[F6] A root supplies a linear factor, and a splitting field is generated by all roots ([[cor-factor-theorem-over-a-commutative-ring]], [[def-polynomials-that-split-and-splitting-fields]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** strong induction on $n=\deg f$.

1.1 Let $P(n)$ assert the theorem for every such datum of degree $n$. If $n=0$, both root sets are empty, so $E=F$ and $E'=F'$; the required extension is $\sigma$. [F6, base]

1.2 Let $n\ge1$ and assume $P(m)$ for all $m<n$. Choose a root $\alpha\in E$ of $f$, and let $p$ be its minimal polynomial over $F$. By [F2], $p\mid f$. Thus $p'=\sigma_*p$ divides $f'$ in $E'[x]$. Since $f'$ is a product of linear factors there, unique factorisation in $E'[x]$ from [F5] makes $p'$ split over $E'$; choose a root $\beta\in E'$ of $p'$. [F2, F4, F5, F6, ih]

2.1 By [F3], $\sigma$ extends to an isomorphism $\sigma_1:F(\alpha)\to F'(\beta)$. Factor $f=(x-\alpha)g$ in $F(\alpha)[x]$. Applying coefficient transport by $\sigma_1$ gives $f'=(x-\beta)g'$ with $g'=(\sigma_1)_*g$, and $\deg g=n-1$. [F3, F4, F6, step 1.2]

3.1 The field $E$ is a splitting field of $g$ over $F(\alpha)$: it splits $g$, and it is generated by $\alpha$ together with the other roots of $f$, which are the roots of $g$. Similarly, $E'$ is a splitting field of $g'$ over $F'(\beta)$. The induction hypothesis extends $\sigma_1$ to an isomorphism $E\to E'$. [F4, F6, step 1.2, step 2.1, ih]

4.1 The base case and inductive step establish $P(n)$ for all $n$ by [F1]. [F1, step 1.1, step 1.2, step 3.1, discharge-induction] ∎
