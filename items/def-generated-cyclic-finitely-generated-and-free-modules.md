## Definition

Let $M$ be a left $R$-module and $S\subseteq M$. The **submodule generated by
$S$** is

$$\langle S\rangle_R:=\bigcap\{N\le M:S\subseteq N\}.$$

The family is nonempty because $M\le M$, and its intersection is a submodule by
[[lem-submodule-criterion-sums-and-intersections]]. Thus $\langle S\rangle_R$
is the smallest submodule of $M$ containing $S$, just as
[[def-generated-subgroup]] defines a generated subgroup.

The module $M$ is **cyclic** if $M=\langle m\rangle_R$ for some $m\in M$, and
**finitely generated** if $M=\langle S\rangle_R$ for some finite $S$. A subset
$B\subseteq M$ is a **basis** if every element of $M$ has a unique expression
as a finite $R$-linear combination of elements of $B$. A module possessing a
basis is a **free $R$-module**.
