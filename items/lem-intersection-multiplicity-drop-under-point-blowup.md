## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]). Let $X$ be a locally
Noetherian scheme, let $Y,Z\subseteq X$ be closed subschemes and let
$p\in Y\cap Z$ be a closed point such that $Y$ is integral of dimension one, the
generic point of $Y$ is not contained in $Z$, and the local ring
$\mathcal O_{Y,p}$ is regular. Let $\pi:X'\to X$ be the blowup of $X$ in $p$
([[def-blowup-scheme-along-ideal]]), with exceptional subscheme
$E=\pi^{-1}(p)$ ([[def-exceptional-divisor-blowup]]), and let $Y',Z'$ be the
strict transforms of $Y$ and $Z$ ([[def-strict-transform-closed-subscheme]]).
Then:

1. $\pi$ restricts to an isomorphism $Y'\to Y$;
2. $Y'$ meets $E$ in exactly one point $q$, which is the unique point of $Y'$
   over $p$, and $m_q(Y'\cap E)=1$
   ([[def-intersection-multiplicity-of-closed-subschemes]]);
3. if $q$ also lies on $Z'$, then $m_q(Y'\cap Z')<m_p(Y\cap Z)$.

In particular the blowup strictly decreases the intersection multiplicity at
every point over $p$ where the strict transforms still meet, and if
$m_p(Y\cap Z)=1$ then $Y'$ and $Z'$ are disjoint over $p$.

## Facts & Assumptions

[F1] A one-dimensional regular local ring is a discrete valuation ring, its
maximal ideal is principal and generated by a uniformizer $\pi$, every nonzero ideal is
a power of the maximal ideal, and for $x=u\pi^n$ with $u$ a unit one has
$\ell_V(V/(x))=n$; in particular the quotient has finite length and its length is
the valuation
([[thm-one-dimensional-regular-local-rings-are-dvrs]],
[[cor-dvr-is-a-pid]],
[[thm-dvr-ideal-and-module-length]],
[[def-composition-series-and-length-of-a-module]]).

[F2] The local intersection multiplicity is
$m_p(Y\cap Z)=\operatorname{length}_{\mathcal O_{X,p}}(\mathcal O_{Y\cap Z,p})$
whenever the hypotheses of the definition hold
([[def-intersection-multiplicity-of-closed-subschemes]]).

[F3] The Axiom of Choice is assumed, inherited from the blowup and strict
transform constructions and from [F1]; no further selection is made
([[def-axiom-of-choice]]).

## Proof

**Given:** AC, $X,Y,Z,p$, the blowup $\pi:X'\to X$ in $p$, the exceptional subscheme $E=\pi^{-1}(p)$ and the strict transforms $Y',Z'$.

1.1 Put $A=\mathcal O_{X,p}$ with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$, let $I=I_{Y,p}$ and $J=I_{Z,p}$ be the stalk ideals, so that $A/I=\mathcal O_{Y,p}$ is a one-dimensional regular local ring and hence a discrete valuation ring with valuation $v$ [F1]. Choose $x_1\in\mathfrak m$ whose image $\overline x_1\in A/I$ is a uniformizer; then $\mathfrak m=I+(x_1)$, because both sides are ideals contained in $\mathfrak m$ and their images in the discrete valuation ring $A/I$ are equal to its maximal ideal. The image $J(A/I)$ is a nonzero ideal of the valuation ring, so $J(A/I)=(\overline x_1^{\,N})$ for a unique integer $N\ge1$, and $N=\min\{v(\overline g):g\in J\}$; pick $f\in J$ with $v(\overline f)=N$. By [F2], $N=\ell_{A/I}(A/(I+J))=m_p(Y\cap Z)$. All claims are local over $p$ and its unique preimage, and blowups commute with the flat base change $\operatorname{Spec}A\to X$ ([[thm-blowup-base-change-flat]]), and localizing the chart saturation $(JB):x_1^\infty$ commutes with taking this schematic closure: a fraction lies in the localized saturation exactly when some power of $x_1$ carries it into the localized ideal. Thus we compute in the local model $\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathfrak m}(\operatorname{Spec}A)$. [F1, F2, given]

2.1 The center ideal on $Y$ is $\mathcal I_p\mathcal O_Y$, whose stalk at $p$ is $\mathfrak m(A/I)=(\overline x_1)$; it is not the zero ideal $I\mathcal O_Y$ defining $Y$ itself. It is the unit ideal away from $p$, and its generator at $p$ is a nonzerodivisor. Coherence allows the stalk generator to generate on a neighbourhood of $p$, so this ideal sheaf is invertible on $Y$. The strict transform of $Y$ is its blowup in this induced center ideal ([[thm-blowup-closed-immersion-transform-universal]]), and the blowup of an invertible ideal is an isomorphism ([[thm-blowup-effective-cartier-divisor-isomorphism]]). Hence $Y'\to Y$ is an isomorphism and has exactly one point $q$ above $p$, with local ring $A/I$. [F1, step 1.1]

3.1 The $x_1$-chart of the local blowup is $B=A[\mathfrak m/x_1]$. Mapping $y/x_1^n\in B$ (with $y\in\mathfrak m^n$) to the unique element $\overline a\in A/I$ with $\overline y=\overline a\,\overline x_1^{\,n}$ gives a well-defined $A$-algebra homomorphism $\psi:B\to A/I$: existence holds because $\mathfrak m^n\subseteq I+(x_1^n)$, uniqueness because $\overline x_1$ is a nonzerodivisor of the domain $A/I$, and compatibility with the relations of the localisation holds because $x_1^k(x_1^m y-x_1^n y')=0$ implies $\overline x_1^{\,k+m+n}(\psi(y/x_1^n)-\psi(y'/x_1^m))=0$. The composite of $\psi$ with the chart morphism $\operatorname{Spec}B\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ is the closed immersion $\operatorname{Spec}(A/I)\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Spec}A$; since the pullback of $\mathfrak m$ to $\operatorname{Spec}(A/I)$ is the invertible ideal $(\overline x_1)$, the universal property of the blowup gives a unique lift $\operatorname{Spec}(A/I)\to\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathfrak m}(\operatorname{Spec}A)$, and this lift is the strict transform $Y'$ in the chart ([[thm-blowup-universal-property]], [[thm-affine-blowup-standard-charts]], [[lem-affine-blowup-algebra-properties]], [[def-strict-transform-closed-subscheme]]). [F1, step 1.1, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 In the chart the exceptional subscheme is $V(x_1)$: the pulled back center ideal $\mathfrak m\mathcal O$ is generated by the nonzerodivisor $x_1$ and $E=V(\mathfrak m\mathcal O)$ ([[thm-pullback-center-ideal-invertible]], [[lem-affine-blowup-algebra-properties]]). Hence the scheme-theoretic intersection $Y'\cap E$ is computed in the chart by $A/I\otimes_BB/(x_1)=(A/I)/(\overline x_1(A/I))=A/(I+(x_1))=A/\mathfrak m=\kappa(p)$; so $Y'\cap E$ is a single reduced point $q$ with residue field $\kappa(p)$ and $m_q(Y'\cap E)=\ell_{\mathcal O_{X',q}}(\kappa(q))=1$, proving claim 2. [F1, step 1.1, step 3.1, algebra]

4.2 The strict transform of $Z$ in the chart is cut out by the saturation $J'=(J B):x_1^\infty$, the closure of $V(JB)$ on the open chart complement of $E$ ([[def-strict-transform-closed-subscheme]]); it contains $f/x_1$, because $x_1\cdot(f/x_1)=f\in JB$. Let $K=\psi(J')\subseteq A/I$ be the image, an ideal of the discrete valuation ring $A/I$ containing $\psi(f/x_1)=\overline f/\overline x_1$, which has valuation $N-1$. If $q\in Z'$, then the closed subscheme $Y'\cap Z'$ of $Y'=\operatorname{Spec}(A/I)$ contains the closed point $q$, so $K$ is a proper ideal contained in the maximal ideal $(\overline x_1)$ and therefore $K=(\overline x_1^{\,j})$ for some $j\ge1$; from $\overline x_1^{\,N-1}\in K$ we get $j\le N-1$, and hence $m_q(Y'\cap Z')=\ell_{A/I}(A/I/K)=j\le N-1<N=m_p(Y\cap Z)$ [F1], proving claim 3. [F1, step 1.1, step 2.1, step 3.1]

5.1 Finally, if $m_p(Y\cap Z)=N=1$, then the element $\overline f/\overline x_1$ of step 4.2 is a unit, so $K$ is the unit ideal whenever $q\in Z'$, a contradiction; hence $q\notin Z'$, and since $q$ is the only point of $Y'$ over $p$ by step 2.1, the strict transforms $Y'$ and $Z'$ are disjoint over $p$. This completes claims 1-3 and the additional assertions. [F3, step 2.1, step 4.1, step 4.2] ∎
