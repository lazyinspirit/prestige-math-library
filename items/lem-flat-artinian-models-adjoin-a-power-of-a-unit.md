## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $p$ be a prime and let $(D,\mathfrak m_D)\to(T,\mathfrak m_T)$ be a flat local map of Artinian local rings of characteristic $p$ with $\mathfrak m_DT=\mathfrak m_T$. For every unit $\lambda\in T$, there exist an essentially smooth local Artinian $D$-algebra $D\to D'$ and a compatible flat local map $D'\to T$ such that $\mathfrak m_{D'}T=\mathfrak m_T$ and $\lambda^q$ lies in the image of $D'$ for some positive integer $q$. If $D$ is essentially smooth over a specified base, then $D'$ is essentially smooth over that base by composition.

## Proof

**Proof technique:** finite equations, the specified local constructions and the explicit supplier interfaces.

1.1 Suppose `D -> D' -> T` are local maps, `D'` and `T` are flat over `D`, and their maximal ideals are generated by `m_D`. Put `F=D/m_D`, `F'=D'/m_DD'`, `K=T/m_DT`. Assume these quotients are fields and the induced `F' -> K` is injective. Then `T` is flat over `D'`. To verify this, choose an `F'`-basis of `K`, using AC, and lift it to `T`. This gives a map from the corresponding direct sum of copies of `D'` to `T`. The finite nilpotent `m_D`-filtration has graded modules `gr_(m_D) D'=gr D tensor_F F'`, `gr_(m_D) T=gr D tensor_F K`, by flatness over `D`. On the chosen bases the map is an isomorphism on every graded piece. A finite filtration therefore makes the original map an isomorphism: lift successively for surjectivity, and use the first nonzero filtration degree for injectivity. Direct sums commute with this finite filtration. Thus `T` is a free, possibly infinitely generated, `D'`-module. This proves flatness instead of assuming it from polynomiality or an intermediate field inclusion. [F1, F2, F3, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 Write `F=D/m_D`, `K=T/m_T`, and let `alpha` be the residue of `lambda`. If `alpha in F`, choose a unit `x in D` with residue `alpha^(-1)`. Then `x lambda=1+u`, with `u in m_T`. Choose a p-power `q` at least a nilpotence exponent of `m_T`. The binomial identity in characteristic p gives `(x lambda)^q=1`. Hence `lambda^q=x^(-q)` already belongs to `D`. Take `D'=D`. [F1, F2, F3, step 1.1, given, construct, algebra]

3.1 If `alpha` is transcendental over `F`, the map `D[X] -> T`, `X -> lambda`, is injective. For a polynomial relation, reduction first puts all its coefficients in `m_D`; flatness over `D` identifies the successive graded pieces with `gr D tensor_F K`, where the powers of `alpha` are independent over `F`. Repeating through the finite filtration makes every coefficient zero. Localize this polynomial algebra at `m_D D[X]`. Every denominator has nonzero residue in `K`, so maps to a unit of `T`. The resulting `D'` is local Artinian with residue `F(X)` and maximal ideal generated by `m_D`; it is essentially smooth over `D`. Its map to `T` is flat by step 1.1. It contains `lambda` itself, so take `q=1`. [F1, F2, F3, step 1.1, step 2.1, given, construct, algebra]

4.1 If `alpha` is algebraic over `F`, choose a p-power `q` such that `beta=alpha^q` is separable over `F`, by separating the p-power part of its minimal polynomial. Lift the monic minimal polynomial of `beta` to a monic polynomial `f` over `D`. Its derivative resultant is a unit because its residue is nonzero in `F`. Thus `D'=D[Z]/f` is finite etale over `D`; its residue is the field `F(beta)`, its maximal ideal is `m_DD'`, and it is local Artinian. Lift the simple residue root `beta` in `T` by Newton correction through its finite nilpotent filtration. This gives the compatible map `D' -> T`; the derivative remains a unit at every correction. It is flat by step 1.1. Now the residue of `lambda^q` lies in the new residue field, so step 2.1 over `D'` gives an additional p-power `q'` with `lambda^(q q')` in the image of `D'`. This proves the theorem. If a larger lower bound on the exponent is needed for annihilator stabilization, any positive multiple of the obtained exponent also works; raise the already adjoined element to that further power. [F1, F2, F3, step 1.1, step 2.1, step 3.1, given, construct, algebra] ∎
