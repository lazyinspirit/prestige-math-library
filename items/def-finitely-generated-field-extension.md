## Definition

Let $K/F$ be a field extension and let $a_1,\ldots,a_r\in K$. The notation

$$F(a_1,\ldots,a_r)$$

means the smallest subfield of $K$ containing $F$ and all the $a_i$. An extension $K/F$ is **finitely generated** when $K=F(a_1,\ldots,a_r)$ for some finite list. For the empty list, $F()=F$.
