## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice for the represented-sheaf and geometric suppliers
used on this page. Let $k$ be a field, let $s,t:R\to U$ be a pre-relation on
$k$-schemes with fppf quotient sheaf $U/R$
([[def-quotient-sheaf-and-representable-quotient]]), and let $q:U\to M$ be a
morphism to a $k$-scheme $M$. Assume: (1) $q\circ s=q\circ t$; (2) $q$ induces a
surjection of fppf sheaves $h_U\to h_M$ (for instance, $q$ is faithfully flat
and locally of finite presentation); (3) the morphism $(t,s):R\to U\times_MU$
([[def-fibre-product-schemes-universal-property]]) induces a surjection of fppf
sheaves $h_R\to h_{U\times_MU}$ (for instance $(t,s)$ is faithfully flat and
locally of finite presentation). Then $M$ represents the quotient sheaf $U/R$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC, the pre-relation $s,t:R\to U$, the fppf quotient sheaf
$U/R$, and a morphism $q:U\to M$ satisfying (1), (2) and (3).

[F1] The naive quotient presheaf $P_{U/R}$ sends $T$ to $U(T)$ modulo the
relation generated by $R(T)$, and $U/R$ is its sheafification: the morphism
$P_{U/R}\to U/R$ is initial among morphisms from $P_{U/R}$ to fppf sheaves, in
particular $U/R$ is an fppf sheaf ([[def-quotient-sheaf-and-representable-quotient]]).

[F2] Represented scheme functors are fppf sheaves. In particular, if a
morphism $X\to Y$ is faithfully flat and locally of finite presentation, then
$h_X\to h_Y$ is a surjection of fppf sheaves ([[lem-nonaffine-fppf-descent-of-scheme-morphisms]],
[[def-faithfully-flat-morphism-schemes]], [[def-locally-finite-presentation-morphism]]).

[F3] The fibre product $U\times_MU$ represents the functor
$T\mapsto\{(u,u')\in U(T)\times U(T):q(u)=q(u')\}$, naturally in $T$
([[def-fibre-product-schemes-universal-property]]).

## Proof

**Given:** AC, the pre-relation $s,t:R\to U$, the fppf quotient sheaf $U/R$,
and $q:U\to M$ satisfying (1), (2) and (3).

1.1 For every $k$-scheme $T$ the map $U(T)\to M(T)$, $u\mapsto q(u)$, is constant on each generating pair $(s(x),t(x))$ of the relation on $U(T)$ by (1), hence constant on the equivalence relation it generates, so it descends to a map $P_{U/R}(T)\to M(T)$; these maps are natural in $T$ and define a morphism of presheaves $P_{U/R}\to h_M$. By [F2] the functor $h_M$ is an fppf sheaf, so the universal property in [F1] factors this morphism uniquely through a morphism $\varphi:U/R\to h_M$. [F1, F2, given, construct]

1.2 Every section of $U/R$ is locally represented by a section of $U$. Let $V(T)\subseteq(U/R)(T)$ be the set of sections $s$ for which there are an fppf covering $\{T_i\to T\}$ and elements $u_i\in U(T_i)$ whose images in $(U/R)(T_i)$ equal the restrictions of $s$. Restrictions of such sections lie in $V$, so $V$ is a subpresheaf of $U/R$; and $V$ is a sheaf, because an fppf covering of each member of an fppf covering of $T$ composes to an fppf covering of $T$. Since $U(T)\to P_{U/R}(T)$ is surjective for every $T$, the canonical morphism $P_{U/R}\to U/R$ takes values in $V$, so there is a factorization $P_{U/R}\to V$ through the inclusion $V\hookrightarrow U/R$. Applying the initiality in [F1] to the target sheaf $V$ and to the target sheaf $U/R$ shows that the composite $U/R\to V\hookrightarrow U/R$ is the identity: both maps make the triangle from $P_{U/R}$ commute, and such a map is unique. Therefore $V=U/R$, which is the claim. [F1, construct]

2.1 The morphism $\varphi$ is surjective as a map of fppf sheaves. Let $T$ be a $k$-scheme and $m\in M(T)=h_M(T)$. By (2) there are an fppf covering $\{T_i\to T\}$ and $u_i\in U(T_i)$ with $q(u_i)=m|_{T_i}$. For every pair $i,j$, the restrictions $u_i|_{T_{ij}}$ and $u_j|_{T_{ij}}$ have the same image in $M(T_{ij})$, so by [F3] they define a section of $h_{U\times_MU}(T_{ij})$. Hypothesis (3) lifts this section, after an fppf refinement of $T_{ij}$, to $r$ with $(t(r),s(r))=(u_j,u_i)$. Thus $[u_i]$ and $[u_j]$ agree in the quotient presheaf locally on $T_{ij}$ and hence agree in its sheafification $U/R$ on $T_{ij}$. They are therefore a matching family in the sheaf $U/R$, so glue to $s\in(U/R)(T)$. Step 1.1 gives $\varphi([u_i])=q(u_i)=m|_{T_i}$; since $h_M$ is a sheaf, $\varphi(s)=m$. Thus every section of $h_M$ lies in the image of $\varphi$. [F1, F2, F3, given, step 1.1, construct]

2.2 The morphism $\varphi$ is injective as a map of presheaves. Let $\xi,\xi'\in(U/R)(T)$ satisfy $\varphi(\xi)=\varphi(\xi')=m$. By step 1.2 there are an fppf covering $\{T_i\to T\}$ of $T$ and elements $u_i,u_i'\in U(T_i)$ with $\xi|_{T_i}=[u_i]$ and $\xi'|_{T_i}=[u_i']$. Then $q(u_i)=\varphi([u_i])=m|_{T_i}=\varphi([u_i'])=q(u_i')$, so $(u_i',u_i)$ is a $T_i$-point of $U\times_MU$ by [F3]. Hypothesis (3) provides, after refining the covering, an element $r_i\in R(T_i)$ with $(t,s)(r_i)=(u_i',u_i)$, that is $t(r_i)=u_i'$ and $s(r_i)=u_i$. Then $(s(r_i),t(r_i))=(u_i,u_i')$ is a generating pair of the relation defining $P_{U/R}(T_i)$, so $[u_i]=[u_i']$ in $(U/R)(T_i)$. Hence $\xi$ and $\xi'$ agree on the members of an fppf covering, and separatedness of the sheaf $U/R$ forces $\xi=\xi'$. [F1, F3, given, step 1.2, construct]

3.1 A morphism of fppf sheaves that is surjective and injective on sections is an isomorphism, so steps 2.1 and 2.2 show that $\varphi:U/R\to h_M$ is an isomorphism; hence $h_M\cong U/R$ naturally and $M$ represents the quotient sheaf $U/R$. For the parenthetical instances, if $q$ is faithfully flat and locally of finite presentation then $h_U\to h_M$ is a surjection of fppf sheaves by [F2], and the same argument with $X=R$ and $Y=U\times_MU$ gives the second instance; this finishes the proof. [F1, F2, given, step 2.1, step 2.2] ∎
