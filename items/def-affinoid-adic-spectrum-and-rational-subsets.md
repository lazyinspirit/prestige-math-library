## Definition

Let $(R,R^+)$ be an affinoid $K$-algebra pair. A valuation on $R$ is a map $v:R\to\Gamma\cup\{0\}$ to a totally ordered abelian multiplicative group with zero, satisfying $v(0)=0$, $v(1)=1$, $v(xy)=v(x)v(y)$, and $v(x+y)\le\max(v(x),v(y))$. It is continuous if it is continuous for the topology on $R$ and the order topology on $\Gamma\cup\{0\}$. Two continuous valuations are equivalent if they give the same comparison $v(f)\le v(g)$ for every $f,g\in R$. The **adic spectrum** $\operatorname{Spa}(R,R^+)$ is the set of equivalence classes of continuous valuations on $R$ that satisfy $v(a)\le1$ for all $a\in R^+$.

For $g,f_1,\ldots,f_n\in R$ such that the ideal $(g,f_1,\ldots,f_n)$ is open in $R$, the associated **rational subset** is
$$U\!\left(\frac{f_1,\ldots,f_n}{g}\right)=\{v\in\operatorname{Spa}(R,R^+):v(g)\ne0,\ v(f_i)\le v(g)\text{ for }1\le i\le n\}.$$
The topology on $\operatorname{Spa}(R,R^+)$ is generated by rational subsets. On the rational subset above, its adic structure presheaf assigns the completed rational localization $R\langle f_1/g,\ldots,f_n/g\rangle$: start with $R[f_1/g,\ldots,f_n/g]$, take the integral closure of the subring generated by the image of $R^+$ and the ratios $f_i/g$, and complete this affinoid pair for its induced Tate topology. Equivalently, maps from this completed pair to a complete affinoid pair are the continuous maps from $(R,R^+)$ that send $g$ to a unit and each $f_i/g$ into the target integral subring. More precisely, if $B$ is the integral closure of $R^+[f_1/g,\ldots,f_n/g]$ in the uncompleted localization $R[f_1/g,\ldots,f_n/g]$, then its plus subring is $\widehat B$, the closure of the image of $B$ in the completed localization. Thus both the ratios and the completion of the integral subring are part of the construction. This is a presheaf definition and does not assert that the presheaf is a sheaf.
