## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]) inherited from the
proper/quasi-finite and scheme base-change suppliers. Let $k$ be a field, let
$X$ be a scheme locally of finite type over $k$, and let $\mathcal E$ be a
finite locally free $\mathcal O_X$-module of rank $r\ge1$
([[def-locally-free-sheaf-finite-rank]]). Let
$$\pi:\mathbb P(\mathcal E)=\operatorname{Proj}_X\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal E)\longrightarrow X$$
be the projective bundle in the quotient convention, with tautological quotient
$\pi^*\mathcal E\to\mathcal O_{\mathbb P(\mathcal E)}(1)$
([[def-projective-bundle-scheme]],
[[def-relative-proj-quasi-coherent-graded-algebra]],
[[thm-projective-bundle-represents-line-quotients]]), and put
$\xi:=c_1(\mathcal O(1))$
([[def-intersection-with-a-cartier-divisor-and-first-chern-class]]); $\pi$ is
smooth of relative dimension $r-1$
([[def-relative-dimension-smooth-morphism]]), so flat pullback $\pi^*$ is
defined ([[lem-flat-pullback-chow-groups]], [[lem-smooth-fibres-smooth]]). Then
for every $d\in\mathbb Z$ the map
$$\bigoplus_{i=0}^{r-1}A_{d+i}(X)\longrightarrow A_{d+r-1}\bigl(\mathbb P(\mathcal E)\bigr),\qquad (\alpha_0,\dots,\alpha_{r-1})\longmapsto\sum_{i=0}^{r-1}\xi^i\cap\pi^*\alpha_i,$$
is an isomorphism of abelian groups. More precisely,
$\pi_*(\xi^s\cap\pi^*\alpha)=0$ for $s<r-1$ and
$\pi_*(\xi^{r-1}\cap\pi^*\alpha)=\alpha$ for all $\alpha\in A_*(X)$
([[lem-proper-pushforward-of-cycles-well-defined]]).

No smoothness of $X$ is assumed; if $X$ is smooth equidimensional of dimension
$n$ then $\mathbb P(\mathcal E)$ is smooth equidimensional and the formula is a
statement about the codimension-graded Chow groups $A^*(X)$,
$A^*(\mathbb P(\mathcal E))$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** the Axiom of Choice; a field $k$; a scheme $X$ locally of finite type over $k$; a finite locally free $\mathcal O_X$-module $\mathcal E$ of rank $r\ge1$; the projective bundle $\pi:\mathbb P(\mathcal E)\to X$ with tautological quotient $\mathcal O(1)$ and $\xi=c_1(\mathcal O(1))$.

[L1] $\pi$ is flat, projective (hence proper) and smooth of relative dimension $r-1$, with fibres $\mathbb P^{r-1}$; over an open $U\subseteq X$ on which $\mathcal E$ is trivial, $\mathbb P(\mathcal E)|_U\cong U\times\mathbb P^{r-1}$ compatibly with $\mathcal O(1)$ ([[def-projective-bundle-scheme]], [[thm-projective-bundle-represents-line-quotients]], [[def-relative-dimension-smooth-morphism]]).

[L2] The first Chern class $\xi$ defines a graded cap operation $\xi\cap-:A_m\to A_{m-1}$ which is additive, commutes with proper pushforward and flat pullback, and satisfies $\xi\cap\xi^s\cap-=\xi^{s+1}\cap-$ ([[def-intersection-with-a-cartier-divisor-and-first-chern-class]]).

[L3] The localization sequence and affine homotopy invariance hold, and the Chow groups of projective space over a field are $\mathbb Z$ in each dimension $0,\dots,n$ generated by the linear classes ([[lem-chow-localization-and-vector-bundle-homotopy]], [[lem-chow-groups-of-projective-space]]).

[L4] Proper pushforward of an integral cycle $W$ with $\dim\pi(W)<\dim W$ is zero, and pullback of classes along the flat morphism $\pi$ shifts degree by $r-1$ ([[lem-proper-pushforward-of-cycles-well-defined]], [[lem-flat-pullback-chow-groups]], [[def-relative-projective-space-standard-charts]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct; compute the pushforwards of the powers of $\xi$ on integral classes, then derive injectivity from the resulting triangular identities and surjectivity from a trivializing open with Noetherian induction.

1.1 Pushforward identities. Let $V\subseteq X$ be an integral closed subscheme of dimension $m$, so that $\pi^*[V]=[\mathbb P(\mathcal E|_V)]$ is an $(m+r-1)$-cycle. First suppose $\mathcal E|_V$ is trivial. The zero schemes of $r-1$ coordinate sections of $\mathcal O(1)$ are relative hyperplanes meeting in a section $\sigma:V\to\mathbb P(\mathcal E|_V)$, so $\xi^{r-1}\cap\pi^*[V]=\sigma_*[V]$ and $\pi_*\sigma_*[V]=[V]$. For any $\mathcal E$ and $0\le s<r-1$, the cycle $\xi^s\cap\pi^*[V]$ has dimension $m+r-1-s>m$ and is supported on $\mathbb P(\mathcal E|_V)$, whose image has dimension $m$; hence its components push to zero by [L4]. For the top power with general $\mathcal E$, choose a dense open $U\subseteq V$ trivializing the bundle. The identity $\pi_*(\xi^{r-1}\cap\pi^*[V])=[V]$ holds over $U$, and its difference is in $A_m(V)$ supported on $V\setminus U$. This complement has dimension less than $m$, so localization [L3] makes the difference zero. Both identities extend by linearity to all $\alpha\in A_*(X)$. [L1, L2, L3, L4, given, algebra]

1.2 Surjectivity over a trivializing open. Let $U\subseteq X$ be an open on which $\mathcal E$ is trivial, so that $\mathbb P(\mathcal E)|_U\cong U\times\mathbb P^{r-1}$. The Chow group of $U\times\mathbb P^{r-1}$ is generated by the classes $\xi^i\cap\pi^*\alpha$ with $0\le i\le r-1$ and $\alpha\in A_*(U)$: stratify $\mathbb P^{r-1}$ by the affine spaces $\mathbb A^j$, use the localization sequence and affine homotopy invariance [L3] to reduce every class to classes pulled back from the strata, and represent each stratum class as an $i$-fold cut with $\xi$ by repeated Cartier cutting of coordinate hyperplanes, which realizes it as $\xi^i\cap\pi^*$ of a class on $U$; the claim is compatible with the restriction to any smaller open by [L2]. [L1, L2, L3, given, algebra]

2.1 Injectivity. Suppose $\sum_{i=0}^{r-1}\xi^i\cap\pi^*\alpha_i=0$. Applying $\pi_*$ and step 1.1 gives $\alpha_{r-1}=0$, since the other summands push to zero. Capping the remaining relation with $\xi^j$ and applying $\pi_*$ successively gives $\alpha_{r-1-j}=0$ for every $0\le j\le r-2$ by the same computation, using associativity of repeated caps from [L2]. Hence the displayed map is injective. [L2, step 1.1, algebra]

2.2 Global surjectivity. It suffices to express an integral cycle $[V]$ on $\mathbb P(\mathcal E)$, and we induct on the dimension of the reduced image closure $W\subseteq X$. The cycle is supported on $\mathbb P(\mathcal E|_W)$. Choose a dense open $U\subseteq W$ trivializing the bundle. Step 1.2 expresses $[V]|_U$ in the required form; lift its coefficient classes from $A_*(U)$ to $A_*(W)$ using localization [L3]. The resulting difference is the pushforward of a cycle class on $\mathbb P(\mathcal E|_{W\setminus U})$. Each integral component of that cycle has image closure of dimension strictly less than $\dim W$, so induction expresses its class in the required form over its image. Push these expressions to $W$ and then $X$, using compatibility of flat pullback with a closed immersion and of caps with proper pushforward. The induction terminates because the integral image has finite dimension. For locally finite cycles the constructions remain locally finite: $\pi$ is proper, so the image closures of a locally finite family are locally finite; each residual family is supported within its respective image closure, and the dimension bound for a fixed-degree input cycle is uniform. Thus the same argument sums locally finitely without asserting that the whole base has finitely many components. [L2, L3, step 1.2, given, algebra]

3.1 Conclusion. Injectivity is step 2.1 and surjectivity is step 2.2; both hold degreewise, so the displayed map is an isomorphism for every $d$. The final paragraph of the statement follows because $\mathcal E$ locally free of rank $r$ makes $\pi$ smooth of relative dimension $r-1$ by [L1], and smoothness of $X$ inherits to the total space of a smooth morphism. [L1, step 2.1, step 2.2, given] ∎
