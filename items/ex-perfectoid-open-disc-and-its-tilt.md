## Example

Assume Dependent Choice. Let $L/\mathbb Q_p$ be a finite extension with its complete discrete valuation, valuation ring $\mathcal O_L$, uniformizer $\pi$, and finite residue field $k=\mathcal O_L/\pi\mathcal O_L$ of cardinality $q$. A one-dimensional commutative formal group law over $\mathcal O_L$ is a power series $F(X,Y)\in\mathcal O_L\llbracket X,Y \rrbracket$ satisfying $F(X,0)=X$, $F(0,Y)=Y$, $F(X,Y)=F(Y,X)$, and $F(F(X,Y),Z)=F(X,F(Y,Z))$. Choose $\phi(X)\in\mathcal O_L\llbracket X \rrbracket$ with $\phi(X)=\pi X+O(X^2)$ and $\phi(X)\equiv X^q\pmod{\pi}$, and choose a formal $\mathcal O_L$-module $F_\phi$ whose endomorphisms $[a]_\phi(X)$ for $a\in\mathcal O_L$ satisfy $[1]_\phi(X)=X$, $[ab]_\phi=[a]_\phi\circ[b]_\phi$, $[a+b]_\phi=F_\phi([a]_\phi,[b]_\phi)$, and $[\pi]_\phi=\phi$. The formal scheme $H_L=\operatorname{Spf}(\mathcal O_L\llbracket X \rrbracket)$, with its $(\pi,X)$-adic topology, has adic generic fiber $D_L$, the formal open unit disc. Its all-rank adic point convention is the union of the rational loci $\{|X|^n\le|\pi|\ne0\}$ for $n\ge1$ in the analytic closed unit disc; the shorthand $|X|<1$ describes its rank-one points. Higher-rank points are retained with this formal-interior convention, rather than by imposing an isolated strict inequality in the ambient adic spectrum.

Form the direct limit $R_L=\varinjlim_{\phi}\mathcal O_L\llbracket X \rrbracket$, where each transition map sends $f(X)$ to $f(\phi(X))$, and let $\widehat R_L$ be its completion for the ideal generated by $\pi$ and the image of $X$. For $n\ge1$, let $\widehat R_{L,n}$ be the $\pi$-adic completion of $\widehat R_L[Y]/(X^n-\pi Y)$, and let $\widehat R_{L,n}^+$ be its integral closure in $L\otimes_{\mathcal O_L}\widehat R_{L,n}$. The adic generic fiber $\widetilde D_L=\operatorname{Spf}(\widehat R_L)^{\mathrm{ad}}$ is obtained by gluing the affinoids $\operatorname{Spa}(L\otimes_{\mathcal O_L}\widehat R_{L,n},\widehat R_{L,n}^+)$ along their natural open immersions.

Let $K$ be a perfectoid field containing $L$, let $K^\flat$ be its tilt, and choose $\varpi\in\mathcal O_K$ with $|\pi|_K\le |\varpi|_K^2<1$. Let $\widehat R_K$ denote the base change of $\widehat R_L$ to $\mathcal O_K$, completed for $(\varpi,X)$, and let $\widetilde D_K$ be the corresponding adic generic fiber over $K$. Let $\widehat R_k$ be the $X$-adic completion of $\varinjlim_{X\mapsto X^q}k\llbracket X \rrbracket$. Then $\widetilde D_K$ is a perfectoid space over $K$, and its tilt is
$$\widetilde D_K^\flat\cong\operatorname{Spf}\!\left(\mathcal O_{K^\flat}\widehat\otimes_k\widehat R_k\right)^{\mathrm{ad}},$$
where the completed tensor product has the $(\varpi^\flat,X)$-adic topology.
