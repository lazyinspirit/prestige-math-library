## Statement

Let $C$ be a finite cyclic group, and define a class function $\eta_C:C\to
\mathbb Z$ by

$$\eta_C(c)= \begin{cases} |C|, & \langle c\rangle=C,\\ 0, & \langle c\rangle\ne C. \end{cases}$$

Then

$$\eta_C=\sum_{D\le C}\mu(|C:D|)\,|D|\,\operatorname{Ind}_D^C1_D,$$

where $\mu$ is the classical number-theoretic Mobius function, characterized by

$$\sum_{d\mid n}\mu(d)= \begin{cases} 1, & n=1,\\ 0, & n>1. \end{cases}$$

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A finite cyclic group $C$ and an element $c\in C$.

[F1] The subgroup $\langle c\rangle$ generated by $c$ is the smallest subgroup of $C$ containing $c$ ([[def-generated-subgroup]]).

[F2] For a subgroup $D\le C$, Frobenius' formula gives $$\operatorname{Ind}_D^C1_D(c)=\frac1{|D|}\sum_{\substack{x\in C\\x^{-1}cx\in D}}1.$$ ([[thm-frobenius-formula-for-induced-characters]])

[F3] For every positive integer $n$, the divisor sum of the classical Mobius function satisfies $$\sum_{d\mid n}\mu(d)= \begin{cases} 1, & n=1,\\ 0, & n>1. \end{cases}$$

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Let $E:=\langle c\rangle$. By [F1], for every subgroup $D\le C$ one has $c\in D$ if and only if $E\le D$. Choose a generator $g$ of $C$. For each divisor $m$ of $|C|$, the subgroup $\langle g^{|C|/m}\rangle$ has order $m$, so cyclic subgroups of $C$ are uniquely indexed by the divisors of $|C|$. If $H,K\le C$ have orders $m,n$, then $H\le K$ holds exactly when $m\mid n$, because with the displayed indexing one has $\langle g^{|C|/m}\rangle\le\langle g^{|C|/n}\rangle$ exactly when $|C|/n$ divides $|C|/m$. [F1, given, algebra]

2.1 The quotient $|C:E|$ is a positive integer. If $E\le D\le C$, then $|C:E|=|C:D|\,|D:E|$, so the index $|C:D|$ divides $|C:E|$. Conversely, if $d$ is a positive divisor of $|C:E|$, put $m:=|C|/d$. Then $|E|$ divides $m$, so by step 1.1 the unique subgroup $D\le C$ of order $m$ contains $E$ and satisfies $|C:D|=d$. Therefore the positive divisors of $|C:E|$ are exactly the indices $|C:D|$ for subgroups $D$ with $E\le D\le C$. Hence [F3] gives $$ \sum_{E\le D\le C}\mu(|C:D|)= \sum_{d\mid |C:E|}\mu(d)= \begin{cases} 1, & |C:E|=1,\\ 0, & |C:E|>1. \end{cases} $$ Since $|C:E|=1$ holds exactly when $E=C$, this is $1_{\{\langle c\rangle=C\}}$. [F3, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Because $C$ is abelian, $x^{-1}cx=c$ for every $x\in C$, so [F2] becomes $\operatorname{Ind}_D^C1_D(c)=|C|/|D|$ when $c\in D$ and $\operatorname{Ind}_D^C1_D(c)=0$ when $c\notin D$. Using step 1.1, the right-hand side of the claimed formula evaluates at $c$ to $\sum_{D\le C}\mu(|C:D|)|D|\operatorname{Ind}_D^C1_D(c)=|C|\sum_{E\le D\le C}\mu(|C:D|)=|C|\,1_{\{\langle c\rangle=C\}}$, which is exactly $\eta_C(c)$ by step 2.1. [F2, step 1.1, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 The equality of step 3.1 holds for every $c\in C$, so the two class functions are equal. [step 3.1] ∎
