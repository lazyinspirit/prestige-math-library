## Definition

Let $F$ be a field. Put $K_0^M(F)=\mathbb Z$. For $n\ge1$, let
$F^\times$ be the multiplicative abelian group of nonzero elements of $F$ and
define
$$
K_n^M(F)=(F^\times)^{\otimes_{\mathbb Z} n}/S_n(F),
$$
where $S_n(F)$ is the subgroup generated by simple tensors
$a_1\otimes\cdots\otimes a_n$ for which two distinct entries satisfy
$a_i+a_j=1$. Write $\{a_1,\ldots,a_n\}$ for the class of a simple tensor;
the group law in $K_n^M(F)$ is written additively. Concatenation of entries
defines products $K_r^M(F)\times K_s^M(F)\to K_{r+s}^M(F)$, with the integer
action supplied by $K_0^M(F)$, and gives the graded Milnor ring
$K_*^M(F)=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}K_n^M(F)$. In particular,
$\{a,1-a\}=0$ whenever $a,1-a\in F^\times$.
