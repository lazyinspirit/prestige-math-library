## Statement

Assume full AC. Let P⊂S¹×D² be a winding-zero pattern. Assume the axis has nonzero classical Blanchfield self-pairing in the Alexander module of P(U), with the convention that the axis defines the class paired with itself. For either the locally flat topological concordance group C_top or the smooth concordance group C_sm, let Pⁿ be the n-fold composition of the induced satellite set map, with P⁰ the identity. For every n≥0, the quotient ⟨Pⁿ(C)⟩/⟨Pⁿ⁺¹(C)⟩ has infinite rank, where ⟨S⟩ denotes the subgroup generated by S and rank means dim_Q(A⊗_Z Q) for an abelian group A. For n≥1, Pⁿ is not a group homomorphism under the same hypotheses. No pairwise-coprime Alexander-polynomial assumption is part of these Theorems A and B.

1.2 Work in the TOP group of [F8]. Fix $n\ge1$ and any desired finite rank $m$. Choose the finite family in [F6] for size $m+1$. If a nonzero signed sum of its $P^n$ iterates belonged to $\mathcal F_{n+1}^{\mathrm{top}}$, its defining TOP solution would have exactly the integral height-$n$ pairs and isotropic height-$(n+1)$ lifts needed by [F5]. Whole-component additivity makes the actual boundary defect sum zero. But [F6] evaluates its bottom terms by canonical cyclic numbers and gives strict signed domination over all pattern terms. Thus these $m+1$ classes are rationally independent modulo $\mathcal F_{n+1}^{\mathrm{top}}$: a rational relation clears denominators to the excluded nonzero signed integer relation. [A1, F1, F5, F6, F8, given, construct, algebra]

1.3 At $n=0$, take any finite family of the signature bumps in [F7], with distinct selected primes.
