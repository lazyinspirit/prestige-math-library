## Definition

Let $F$ be a field, $G$ a group, and $B$ an integral-domain $F$-algebra with an action of $G$ by $F$-algebra automorphisms. Suppose the invariant ring $E=B^G$ is a field, and write $C=\operatorname{Frac}(B)$ with its induced $G$-action. The algebra $B$ is **$(F,G)$-regular** if $C^G=E$ and every nonzero $b\in B$ whose $F$-linear span $Fb$ is $G$-stable is a unit of $B$.
