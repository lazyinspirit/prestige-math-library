## Definition

Fix a field $k$. An Artin $k$-stack is as in
[[def-algebraic-stack-and-inertia]]. In this item, a finite-type stack means an
Artin $k$-stack with a finite-type smooth atlas and finite-type diagonal. It
has affine stabilizers when the automorphism group scheme of every geometric
point is affine.

For a finite-type Artin stack $X$ with affine stabilizers, define
$\mathscr K(X)$ to be the abelian group generated by isomorphism classes
$[Y\to X]$ of finite-type Artin stacks $Y$ with affine stabilizers and a
1-morphism to $X$. Impose the following relations.

1. If $Z\hookrightarrow Y$ is a closed substack, then
   $[Y\to X]=[Z\to X]+[Y\setminus Z\to X]$.
2. Let $S\to X$ be a finite-type stack with affine stabilizers and let
   $d:V_0\to V_1$ be a constant-rank morphism of vector bundles on $S$.
   The additive vector bundle $V_0$ acts on $V_1$ by translation through
   $d$. Write $[V_1/V_0\to S\to X]$ for the resulting quotient groupoid
   stack over $X$. If $r_i$ is the rank of $V_i$, impose
   $[V_1/V_0\to X]=\mathbb L^{r_1-r_0}[S\to X]$.

Here $\mathbb L$ is the class of $\mathbb A^1_k$ in
$R:=\mathscr K(\operatorname{Spec}k)$, and we localize $R$ and the modules
$\mathscr K(X)$ by making $\mathbb L$ invertible. The product in $R$ is
induced by product over $k$. The group $\mathscr K(X)$ is an $R$-module.
This explicit vector-bundle-stack relation records the class of a family of
linear objects modulo its linear changes of splitting.

For a morphism $f:X\to X'$, pullback is
$f^*[Y\to X']=[Y\times_{X'}X\to X]$ and pushforward is
$f_![Z\to X]=[Z\to X\to X']$. Exterior product is
$[Y\to X]\boxtimes[Z\to X']=[Y\times_k Z\to X\times_kX']$.
These operations are well-defined because fiber product and composition
preserve isomorphisms, closed-substack decompositions, and the
vector-bundle-stack relation. If the rank of a vector-bundle map varies, its
base is partitioned into the finitely many locally closed rank strata and
the scissor relation is applied on those strata.

The dependency on [[def-axiom-of-choice]] is inherited from the cited
algebraic-stack convention in [[def-algebraic-stack-and-inertia]], specifically
its represented-sheaf descent foundation. No choice is used in the
finite-dimensional quiver matrix calculations below.
