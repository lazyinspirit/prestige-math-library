## Definition

Let $N,T\ge1$ be integers, let $[N]=\{1,\ldots,N\}$ be the expert set, and let $\epsilon>0$. At each round $t=1,\ldots,T$, a finite loss vector $\ell_t=(\ell_t(1),\ldots,\ell_t(N))\in[0,\infty)^N$ is revealed after the learner's choice. Initialize $W_1(i)=1$ for every $i\in[N]$. At round $t$, set
$$x_t(i):=\frac{W_t(i)}{\sum_{j=1}^{N}W_t(j)},\qquad i\in[N],$$
Let $\mathcal H_{t-1}$ be the pre-round history sigma-algebra generated by the previously played choices and all previously revealed loss vectors (any random loss vector is assumed measurable on the protocol's probability space). The weights and $x_t$ are $\mathcal H_{t-1}$-measurable. Draw a measurable expert choice $I_t\in[N]$ with conditional distribution
$$\mathbb P(I_t=i\mid\mathcal H_{t-1})=x_t(i)\quad\text{almost surely},\qquad i\in[N],$$
and play $I_t$. After observing the whole vector $\ell_t$, update
$$W_{t+1}(i):=W_t(i)\exp(-\epsilon\ell_t(i)),\qquad i\in[N].$$
The output is the distributions $x_1,\ldots,x_T$, the sampled choices $I_1,\ldots,I_T$, and the final weights. The protocol assumes a probability space carrying the stated measurable draws; no probability-space construction is claimed.
