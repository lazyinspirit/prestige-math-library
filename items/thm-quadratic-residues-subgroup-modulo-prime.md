## Statement

Let $p$ be an odd prime. The nonzero quadratic-residue classes form the subgroup

$$((\mathbb Z/p)^\times)^2:=\{u^2:u\in(\mathbb Z/p)^\times\}.$$

For every primitive root $g$ modulo $p$, this subgroup is $\langle g^2\rangle$ and has index two in $(\mathbb Z/p)^\times$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** An odd prime $p$ and the unit group $G=(\mathbb Z/p)^\times$.

[L1] The group $G$ is cyclic of order $p-1$ ([[cor-unit-group-modulo-prime-is-cyclic]]).

[L2] For a group element $g$, the subgroup $\langle g\rangle$ is exactly the set of all integer powers of $g$ ([[lem-cyclic-subgroup-is-the-set-of-powers]]).

[L3] A group is cyclic exactly when it is generated by one element ([[def-generated-subgroup]]).

[L4] The quadratic-residue classes modulo $p$ are exactly the image of squaring on $G$ ([[prop-quadratic-residue-is-representative-independent]]).

[L5] Integer powers in a group satisfy $(g^m)^n=g^{mn}$ and $g^{m+n}=g^mg^n$ ([[lem-group-power-laws]]).

[L6] For every integer $t$, there are unique integers $q,r$ with $t=2q+r$ and $0\le r<2$ ([[thm-division-algorithm-in-z]]).

[L7] The index $[G:H]$ of a finite-index subgroup is the cardinality of its coset space $G/H$ ([[def-index]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 By [L1] and [L3], choose $g\in G$ with $G=\langle g\rangle$; then every $u\in G$ is $g^t$ for some $t\in\mathbb Z$ by [L2]. [L1, L2, L3, choose]

2.1 If $u=g^t$, then $u^2=g^{2t}$ by [L5], so every square lies in $\langle g^2\rangle$; conversely $(g^2)^q=(g^q)^2$, so every element of $\langle g^2\rangle$ is a square. By [L4], the quadratic-residue classes are therefore exactly $\langle g^2\rangle$. [L4, L5, L6, step 1.1]

3.1 Write each exponent as $t=2q+r$ with $r\in\{0,1\}$ by [L6]. Then $g^t$ lies in $\langle g^2\rangle$ when $r=0$ and in $g\langle g^2\rangle$ when $r=1$. These cosets are distinct: if $g=g^{2q}$, then the order $p-1$ of $g$ would divide the odd integer $2q-1$, impossible because $p-1$ is even. Hence there are exactly two cosets, so the subgroup has index two by [L7]. [L2, L5, L7, step 1.1, step 2.1] ∎
