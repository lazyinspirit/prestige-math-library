## Definition

Fix $m\ge1$, let $A_m$ be the Khovanov–Seidel type A algebra of
[[def-khovanov-seidel-type-a-quiver-algebra]] with vertex idempotents
$e_j=(j)$ and internal degree, and let $A_m\text{-mod}$ be the category of
[[def-graded-khovanov-seidel-module-category-and-projectives]].

**The vertex modules.** For $0\le i\le m$ let $S_i$ be the graded left
$A_m$-module whose underlying graded abelian group is
$$(S_i)_0=\mathbb Z,\qquad (S_i)_d=0\quad(d\ne0),$$
and on which a path $p$ of $\mathbb Z\Gamma_m$ acts by
$$e_i\cdot1=1,\qquad e_j\cdot1=0\ (j\ne i),\qquad p\cdot1=0\text{ for every positive-length path }p.$$
Equivalently, $e_i$ acts as the identity, every other vertex idempotent acts as
zero, and every path of positive length acts as zero. The claims that this
assignment is well defined on the quotient $A_m$, that it makes $S_i$ a
finitely generated graded left $A_m$-module and that it is not the zero module
are proved below, so the notation denotes.

**Prime quotients.** For a prime $p$, $pS_i$ is the $A_m$-submodule generated
by $p\cdot1$ and $S_i/pS_i$ is the quotient module; it is the corresponding
$\mathbb Z/p$ **vertex module**, that is the graded left $A_m$-module
$(\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z)$ in internal degree $0$, with the induced action in
which $e_i$ acts as the identity and every other vertex idempotent and every
positive-length path acts as zero. The sequence
$$0\to S_i\xrightarrow{p}S_i\to S_i/pS_i\to0$$
is degreewise exact for every prime $p$; exactness at both non-end terms is
proved below, so the notation denotes.

**Shifts.** The internal shifts of $S_i$ are inherited from $A_m\text{-mod}$:
$S_i\{r\}$ is the internal shift of the definition of
[[def-graded-khovanov-seidel-module-category-and-projectives]], so that
$(S_i\{r\})_{r}=\mathbb Z$ and all other graded pieces vanish and the same
action rule applies. The vertex module $S_i$ is the quotient of the vertex
projective $P_i=A_me_i$ by its positive-length-path submodule; the
identifications with the quotients $P_i/A_m\{u_{i-1},d_i,r_i\}$ (omitting $u_{-1}$, $d_m$ and $r_0$ at the endpoints, and writing $r_i=(i|i-1|i)$ for $1\le i\le m$) and with the
$\mathbb Z/p$ reductions are proved on the pages that use them.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** An integer $m\ge1$, the algebra $A_m$ with vertex idempotents $e_j=(j)$ and internal degree, and an index $0\le i\le m$.

[F1] $A_m=\mathbb Z\Gamma_m/I_m$ is a graded ring whose homogeneous piece of degree $0$ contains the vertex idempotents, whose positive-length path classes are homogeneous, with $\deg e_j=0$, $\deg(j|j+1)=0$ and $\deg(j+1|j)=1$, and $I_m$ is the two-sided ideal generated by $(j-1|j|j+1)$, $(j+1|j|j-1)$, $(j|j+1|j)-(j|j-1|j)$ for $0<j<m$ and $(0|1|0)$ ([[def-khovanov-seidel-type-a-quiver-algebra]]).

[F2] $\mathbb Z\Gamma_m$ is the free abelian group on the directed finite paths of the doubled line, including the length-zero vertex paths, the product is left-to-right concatenation and is $0$ for noncomposable paths and $(j)p=p$, $p(j)=p$ for paths with source respectively target $j$, and these identities pass to the quotient $A_m$ ([[def-path-ring-of-a-finite-quiver-over-the-integers]]).

[L3] $A_m\text{-mod}$ is the abelian category of finitely generated graded left $A_m$-modules and degree-zero maps; the internal shift $M\{r\}$ with $(M\{r\})_d=M_{d-r}$ is an object of it whenever $M$ is, and is an automorphism of the category; kernels and cokernels are computed degreewise and a sequence is exact precisely when it is exact degreewise ([[def-graded-khovanov-seidel-module-category-and-projectives]]).

[L4] For every unital associative $\mathbb Z$-graded ring $A$ the category $\operatorname{GrMod}_0(A)$ of graded left $A$-modules and degree-zero maps is abelian; a degree-zero map $f:M\to N$ has $\ker f$ and $\operatorname{coker}f$ with graded pieces $(\ker f)_d=\ker(f_d)$ and $(\operatorname{coker}f)_d=\operatorname{coker}(f_d)$; a sequence is exact precisely when it is exact degreewise ([[lem-graded-module-kernels-cokernels-and-biproducts-are-degreewise]]).

[F5] A unital ring homomorphism $R\to\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb Z}(M)$ makes an abelian group $M$ a left $R$-module, and a ring homomorphism $\mathbb Z\Gamma_m\to\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb Z}(M)$ that annihilates the two-sided ideal $I_m$ induces a well-defined ring homomorphism $A_m=\mathbb Z\Gamma_m/I_m\to\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb Z}(M)$ making $M$ a left $A_m$-module; two such actions agree when they agree on the classes of a generating set of the ring ([[def-ring]], [[def-quotient-ring]]).

[F6] If $x,y\in\mathbb Z$ are nonzero then $xy\ne0$, and consequently $xz=yz$ with $z\ne0$ implies $x=y$; in particular $p\ne0$ and $pa=0$ imply $a=0$ ([[lem-int-cancellation]]).



## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 *The action is a well-defined ring homomorphism on the path ring.* Let $R=\mathbb Z\Gamma_m$ and define $\varphi:R\to\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb Z}(\mathbb Z)$ on the free basis of paths by $\varphi(p)=\delta_{p,e_i}\,\mathrm{id}_{\mathbb Z}$, the Kronecker symbol being $1$ exactly when $p$ is the vertex path $e_i=(i)$. This is additive, and it is multiplicative: for composable paths $p,q$ the product $pq$ is the concatenated path when the endpoints match and $0$ otherwise, and $pq=e_i$ happens exactly when $p=q=e_i$, because path length is additive over concatenation and a product of two paths is a vertex path only if both factors are vertex paths; comparing with $\varphi(p)\varphi(q)=\delta_{p,e_i}\delta_{q,e_i}\mathrm{id}$ gives $\varphi(pq)=\delta_{pq,e_i}\mathrm{id}=\varphi(p)\varphi(q)$, while for a noncomposable pair both sides are $0$. Hence $\varphi$ is a ring homomorphism, and $\varphi\bigl(\sum_j(j)\bigr)=\mathrm{id}_{\mathbb Z}$ shows that it is unital, so it makes $\mathbb Z$ a left $R$-module by [F5]; in this action the vertex idempotents act by $\delta_{j,i}\mathrm{id}$ and every positive-length path acts by $0$. [F2, F5]

1.2 *Descent to $A_m$ and degree-zero grading.* Every generator of the ideal $I_m$ displayed in [F1] is a length-two path or a difference of two length-two paths, hence is sent to zero by $\varphi$; since $I_m$ is the two-sided ideal generated by these elements and $\varphi$ is multiplicative, $\varphi(I_m)=0$, and by [F5] the homomorphism factors through the quotient $A_m=\mathbb Z\Gamma_m/I_m$, making $\mathbb Z$ a left $A_m$-module on which the class of a path acts by $\delta_{p,e_i}\mathrm{id}$; in particular $e_i$ acts as the identity and every other vertex idempotent and every positive-length path class acts as zero. This action is graded in the sense of [L3]: $A_m$ is spanned over $\mathbb Z$ by vertex classes and positive-length path classes by [F1], the positive-length classes act by $0$ and the vertex classes lie in degree $0$ with $A_{m,0}(S_i)_0\subseteq(S_i)_0$ and $A_{m,d}(S_i)_0=0\subseteq(S_i)_d$ for $d\ne0$. The element $1$ generates $S_i$ over $A_m$ because $e_i\cdot1=1$, so $S_i$ is a finitely generated graded left $A_m$-module, and $S_i\ne0$ because $1\ne0$. [F1, F2, L3, F5]

1.3 *The prime quotient and its exactness.* By [L3] the submodule $pS_i=A_mp$ is graded with $(pS_i)_0=p\mathbb Z$ and $(pS_i)_d=0$ for $d\ne0$, and the quotient $S_i/pS_i$ is a graded left $A_m$-module with $(S_i/pS_i)_0=\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z$, zero in other degrees, and the induced action in which $e_i$ is the identity, every other vertex idempotent and every positive-length path is zero. By [L4] the sequence $0\to S_i\xrightarrow{p}S_i\to S_i/pS_i\to0$ is exact if and only if it is exact degreewise, and degreewise it is the sequence $0\to\mathbb Z\xrightarrow{p}\mathbb Z\to\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z\to0$ in degree $0$ together with the sequence $0\to0\to0\to0$ in every other degree. The latter is exact. For the former, multiplication by $p$ is injective because $pa=0$ with $p\ne0$ forces $a=0$ by [F6]; its image is $p\mathbb Z=\ker(\mathbb Z\to\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z)$; and the quotient map is surjective. [F6, L3, L4]

2.1 *Conclusion.* For every $0\le i\le m$ the assignment displayed in the Definition descends to a well-defined action of $A_m$, making $S_i$ a finitely generated nonzero graded left $A_m$-module with $e_i$ acting as the identity, every other vertex idempotent acting as zero and every positive-length path acting as zero (steps 1.1 and 1.2); for every prime $p$ the quotient $S_i/pS_i$ is the $\mathbb Z/p$ vertex module and the sequence $0\to S_i\xrightarrow{p}S_i\to S_i/pS_i\to0$ is degreewise exact (step 1.3); and the internal shifts $S_i\{r\}$ are the shifts of $A_m\text{-mod}$ (Definition). [step 1.2, step 1.3, L3] ∎
