## Statement

Assume Countable Choice. Fix an actual balanced multipointed knot diagram in $S^3$ with $m=h+k-1$, its ordered marks and actual good square-zero full minus count data. For an attaching-circle handleslide or isotopy avoiding the other circles of its family and all marks, preserving the balanced knot data, the two full filtered minus complexes have finite actual chain maps and inverse chain homotopies over $R=\mathbb F_2[U_1,\ldots,U_k]$. Counts include ALL positive z degrees; the variables and their marked-point actions are retained individually. A supplied finite word of such moves and pointed diagram isomorphisms gives the corresponding finite equivalence. The maps specialize or restrict to any chosen-variable module by the actual ring operation.

Maslov homogeneity and the Alexander filtration are relative: choose the origins by the actual small comparison triangles and continuous class transports in the proof. Maps have degree zero, homotopies degree plus one, and every $U_i$ has degrees $(-2,-1)$. This supplies a relative filtered comparison, not its Seifert-surface centering or absolute Maslov normalization. It proves neither ordinary Heegaard stabilization nor basepoint-pair stabilization; their weighted target-neck operations are separate.

The handleslide maps are actual marked triangle counts. Their inverses and homotopies are finite polynomial matrices; no U-adic completion or inversion of a nonunit polynomial is used. An isotopy handles EVERY complementary generator pair created at a tangency, including possible positive-U reverse arrows.
