## Definition

For an abstract simplicial complex $K$ and an integer $n \geq 0$, the
**simplicial chain group** $C_n(K)$ is the free abelian group generated by the
oriented $n$-simplices of $K$, subject to the relation
$$[v_{\pi(0)},\dots,v_{\pi(n)}]=\operatorname{sgn}(\pi)[v_0,\dots,v_n]$$
for every permutation $\pi$ of the vertices of a simplex. For $n<0$, set
$C_n(K)=0$.

The **boundary operator**
$$\partial_n:C_n(K)\to C_{n-1}(K)$$
is $\partial_0=0$ in degree $0$. For $n\geq1$, it is defined on an oriented
simplex by
$$\partial_n[v_0,\dots,v_n]=\sum_{i=0}^n (-1)^i [v_0,\dots,\widehat{v_i},\dots,v_n].$$

The well-definedness of this formula with respect to the chosen oriented
representative is recorded in
[[lem-simplicial-boundary-is-independent-of-oriented-representative]] through
`justified_by`.
