## Example

Let $\mathbb F_{q^n}/\mathbb F_q$ be a finite-field extension, and let
$\alpha\in\mathbb F_{q^n}$. Then

$$N_{\mathbb F_{q^n}/\mathbb F_q}(\alpha)=\alpha\,\alpha^q\cdots\alpha^{q^{n-1}}=\alpha^{1+q+\cdots+q^{n-1}},$$

and

$$\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathbb F_{q^n}/\mathbb F_q}(\alpha)=\alpha+\alpha^q+\cdots+\alpha^{q^{n-1}}.$$

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A finite extension $\mathbb F_{q^n}/\mathbb F_q$ and an element $\alpha\in\mathbb F_{q^n}$.

[L1] The extension is finite Galois with cyclic Galois group generated by the Frobenius $\sigma_q(x)=x^q$ ([[thm-extensions-of-finite-fields-are-galois-with-cyclic-frobenius-group]]).

[L2] Norm and trace are the product and sum of the distinct embeddings in the separable case ([[thm-field-norm-and-trace-by-embeddings]]).

## Verification

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 By [L1], the $n$ distinct $\mathbb F_q$-embeddings are $1,\sigma_q,\dots,\sigma_q^{n-1}$. [L1]

2.1 Applying [L2] to the embeddings of step 1.1 gives $$N_{\mathbb F_{q^n}/\mathbb F_q}(\alpha)=\prod_{i=0}^{n-1}\sigma_q^i(\alpha)=\prod_{i=0}^{n-1}\alpha^{q^i}=\alpha^{1+q+\cdots+q^{n-1}},$$ and $$\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathbb F_{q^n}/\mathbb F_q}(\alpha)=\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}\sigma_q^i(\alpha)=\alpha+\alpha^q+\cdots+\alpha^{q^{n-1}}.$$ [step 1.1, L2, algebra] ∎
