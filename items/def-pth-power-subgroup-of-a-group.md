## Definition

For a group $G$ and a prime $p$ ([[def-prime]]), the $p$th-power subgroup is

$$G^p=\langle g^p:g\in G\rangle,$$

where powers are those of [[def-group-power]] and the generated subgroup is that of [[def-generated-subgroup]]. This notation denotes the subgroup generated by the powers, not merely the set of powers, which need not itself be a subgroup in an arbitrary group.
