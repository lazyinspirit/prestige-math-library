## Definition

Assume the Axiom of Choice, inherited from associated-sheaf existence and
the affine quasi-coherent equivalence ([[def-axiom-of-choice]],
[[thm-associated-module-sheaf-exists]],
[[thm-affine-quasi-coherent-equivalence]]). Let $X$ be a scheme and let
$\mathcal F$ be a quasi-coherent $\mathcal O_X$-module
([[def-quasi-coherent-module-scheme]]).

**The affine models.** Let $A$ be a commutative ring with $1$ and let $M$ be
an $A$-module. The tensor algebra
$$T_A(M)=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}M^{\otimes_An},\qquad M^{\otimes_A0}=A,$$
is the graded $A$-algebra whose multiplication on homogeneous pure tensors is
concatenation (the case of a module over a field is
[[def-tensor-algebra-of-a-vector-space]]). The **symmetric algebra** of $M$ is
the quotient
$$\operatorname{Sym}_A(M)=T_A(M)/I,$$
where $I$ is the two-sided ideal generated by all elements
$m\otimes m'-m'\otimes m$ with $m,m'\in M$. The relators are homogeneous of
degree two, so $I$ is graded, and $\operatorname{Sym}_A(M)$ is a graded
commutative $A$-algebra, generated as an $A$-algebra by its degree-one part
$M$, with
$$\operatorname{Sym}^0_A(M)=A,\qquad \operatorname{Sym}^1_A(M)=M.$$
Its degree-$d$ piece is the quotient of $M^{\otimes_Ad}$ by the relations
$m_1\otimes\cdots\otimes m_d=m_{\sigma(1)}\otimes\cdots\otimes
m_{\sigma(d)}$ for all permutations $\sigma$ of $\{1,\dots,d\}$; for
$M=A^r$ this is the polynomial algebra $A[T_1,\dots,T_r]$ on $r$
degree-one generators.

The algebraic universal property follows directly from this quotient: an
$A$-linear map $u:M\to D$ into a commutative $A$-algebra extends on a pure
tensor to the product $u(m_1)\cdots u(m_n)$, and the commutator ideal maps to
zero. This extension is unique because degree one generates the algebra.
Consequently, for any ring map $A\to C$, maps from either
$C\otimes_A\operatorname{Sym}_A(M)$ or
$\operatorname{Sym}_C(C\otimes_AM)$ to a commutative $C$-algebra $D$
correspond to $A$-linear maps $M\to D$: a $C$-linear map out of
$C\otimes_AM$ has the form $c\otimes m\mapsto c\,u(m)$.
Applying these extensions to the degree-one maps in both directions gives
mutually inverse algebra maps, since both composites fix degree one and
scalars. These are graded and compatible with successive base changes by
the same uniqueness. This proves the base-change assertion used in gluing.

**Gluing the affine models.** Let $U=\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq X$ be an
affine open subscheme with $\mathcal F|_U\cong\widetilde M$ for an $A$-module
$M$; such opens cover $X$, because $\mathcal F$ is quasi-coherent, and $M$ is
determined up to canonical isomorphism by $\mathcal F|_U$ under the affine
equivalence ([[thm-affine-quasi-coherent-equivalence]],
[[def-associated-sheaf-module-affine-scheme]]). Put
$$\operatorname{Sym}_U:=(\operatorname{Sym}_A(M))^{\sim},$$
an $\mathcal O_U$-module carrying a commutative $\mathcal O_U$-algebra
structure ([[def-module-on-ringed-space]]). Let $V=\operatorname{Spec}C
\subseteq U$ be a smaller affine open, so that $\mathcal F|_V\cong
\widetilde{(C\otimes_AM)}$ and, by naturality of the identification
$(\widetilde N)|_V\cong\widetilde{(C\otimes_AN)}$
([[lem-associated-sheaf-restriction-affine-open]]), the canonical base-change
isomorphism of graded $C$-algebras
$$\operatorname{Sym}_A(M)\otimes_AC\;\cong\;\operatorname{Sym}_C(M\otimes_AC)$$
induced by $M\to M\otimes_AC$ gives a canonical isomorphism of
$\mathcal O_V$-algebras
$$\theta_{U,V}:\operatorname{Sym}_U|_V\xrightarrow{\ \cong\ } \operatorname{Sym}_V .$$
Here $\theta_{U,U}=\mathrm{id}$ and
$\theta_{U,W}=\theta_{V,W}\circ\theta_{U,V}|_W$ for affine
$W\subseteq V\subseteq U$, because the base-change isomorphisms are compatible
under composition of ring maps. On an overlap $U\cap U'$ the isomorphisms
$\theta_{U',W}^{-1}\circ\theta_{U,W}$ for affine $W\subseteq U\cap U'$ agree on
further affine opens by the cocycle identity, so they glue to an isomorphism
$\operatorname{Sym}_U|_{U\cap U'}\cong\operatorname{Sym}_{U'}|_{U\cap U'}$,
and the triple-overlap condition follows on affine refinements. Hence the
$\operatorname{Sym}_U$ together with these identifications form a gluing datum
on the cover by all such affine opens ([[def-gluing-datum-sheaves]]), and the
resulting glued sheaf of commutative $\mathcal O_X$-algebras
$$\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal F)$$
is the **symmetric algebra** of $\mathcal F$; it is determined up to unique
isomorphism by the affine models $\operatorname{Sym}_U$
([[thm-gluing-sheaves]]).

**Grading and generation.** The gluing is degreewise: writing
$\operatorname{Sym}_A(M)=\bigoplus_{d\ge0}\operatorname{Sym}^d_A(M)$, the
degree-$d$ parts $(\operatorname{Sym}^d_A(M))^{\sim}$ glue to sheaves
$\operatorname{Sym}^d(\mathcal F)$ with
$$\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal F)=\bigoplus_{d\ge0}\operatorname{Sym}^d(\mathcal F), \qquad \operatorname{Sym}^0(\mathcal F)=\mathcal O_X,\qquad \operatorname{Sym}^1(\mathcal F)=\mathcal F ,$$
the direct sum being taken in the category of $\mathcal O_X$-modules; on an
affine chart it corresponds under the equivalence $N\mapsto\widetilde N$ to the
direct sum of the modules $\operatorname{Sym}^d_A(M)$
([[thm-affine-quasi-coherent-equivalence]]), and degreewise gluing is
legitimate because restriction isomorphisms are degree-preserving. The
multiplication of the affine models induces multiplication maps
$\operatorname{Sym}^d(\mathcal F)\otimes_{\mathcal O_X}
\operatorname{Sym}^e(\mathcal F)\to\operatorname{Sym}^{d+e}(\mathcal F)$,
so $\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal F)$ is generated as an $\mathcal O_X$-algebra
by its degree-one part $\mathcal F$, local sections of $\mathcal F$ commute in
$\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal F)$, and the degree-$d$ piece is the quotient of
the $d$-fold tensor power $\mathcal F^{\otimes d}$
([[def-sheaf-tensor-product]]) by the local relations
$v_1\otimes\cdots\otimes v_d=v_{\sigma(1)}\otimes\cdots\otimes v_{\sigma(d)}$
for all permutations $\sigma$; the tensor powers are quasi-coherent with
$(\mathcal F^{\otimes d})|_U\cong(M^{\otimes_Ad})^{\sim}$ by iteration of
[[lem-tensor-qc-modules-quasi-coherent]]. Equivalently,
$\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal F)$ is obtained from the tensor-algebra
presentation of $\mathcal F$ by imposing the local relations
$v\otimes w=w\otimes v$.

**Affine model.** If $U=\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq X$ is affine with
$\mathcal F|_U\cong\widetilde M$, then
$$\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal F)|_U\cong(\operatorname{Sym}_A(M))^{\sim}, \qquad \operatorname{Sym}^d(\mathcal F)|_U\cong (\operatorname{Sym}^d_A(M))^{\sim},$$
so restricting to the members of the covering family by all such affine opens
shows that $\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal F)$ is a quasi-coherent graded
$\mathcal O_X$-algebra ([[def-quasi-coherent-module-scheme]]); in particular
$\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal O_X^{\,r})\cong\mathcal O_X[T_1,\dots,T_r]$ with
$\deg T_i=1$ and $\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal F)$ satisfies the universal
property that every $\mathcal O_X$-linear map $\mathcal F\to\mathcal A$ into a
sheaf of commutative $\mathcal O_X$-algebras extends uniquely to an
$\mathcal O_X$-algebra map $\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal F)\to\mathcal A$.

**Choice.** The cover used is the family of all affine opens on which
$\mathcal F$ is associated, which is determined by the data, and the
identifications $\theta_{U,V}$ are canonical base-change isomorphisms, so no
chart, module or isomorphism is selected: on each affine open one may use
$M=\Gamma(U,\mathcal F)$ and the canonical comparison isomorphism of the
affine equivalence. AC is inherited from the associated-sheaf machinery and
from [[thm-affine-quasi-coherent-equivalence]], whose proof step 2.1 uses
compactness of $\operatorname{Spec}A$ to obtain a finite principal cover.
The algebraic quotient and the canonical gluing introduce no additional
choice.
