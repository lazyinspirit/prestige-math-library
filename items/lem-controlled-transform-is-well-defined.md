## Statement

Assume AC ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]), inherited from the regular-local and blowup suppliers. Let $(\mathcal I,E,\mu)$ be a marked ideal on a smooth $K$-scheme $X$, let $C\subseteq\operatorname{supp}(\mathcal I,E,\mu)$ be a regular closed subscheme with SNC with $E$, let $\sigma\colon X'\to X$ be the blowup of $C$ with exceptional divisor $D$ ([[def-blowup-scheme-along-ideal]], [[def-exceptional-divisor-blowup]]), and let $\mathcal I_C\subseteq\mathcal O_X$ be the ideal sheaf of $C$ and $\mathcal I(D)\subseteq\mathcal O_{X'}$ the invertible ideal of $D$ ([[def-invertible-sheaf-of-cartier-divisor]]).
Then
$$\mathcal I\subseteq\mathcal I_C^{\mu}\quad\text{and}\quad\sigma^*\mathcal I\subseteq\mathcal I(D)^{\mu}.$$
Consequently the controlled transform $\sigma^{\mathrm{c}}(\mathcal I,\mu):=(\mathcal I(D)^{-\mu}\sigma^*\mathcal I,\mu)$ is an ideal sheaf on $X'$ ([[def-multiple-test-blowup-and-controlled-transform]]), and for $f\in\mathcal I(U)$ the local section $y^{-\mu}\sigma^*(f)$, $y$ a local equation of $D$, is well defined up to a unit and generates the controlled transform wherever $f$ generates $\mathcal I$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A marked ideal $(\mathcal I,E,\mu)$ on a smooth $K$-scheme $X$, a regular closed subscheme $C\subseteq\operatorname{supp}(\mathcal I,E,\mu)$ with SNC with $E$, the blowup $\sigma\colon X'\to X$ of $C$ with exceptional divisor $D$, the ideal $\mathcal I_C$ of $C$ and the invertible ideal $\mathcal I(D)$ of $D$.

[F1] [[def-marked-ideal]]: the support is $\operatorname{supp}(\mathcal I,E,\mu)=\{x:\operatorname{ord}_x(\mathcal I)\ge\mu\}$, and $C\subseteq\operatorname{supp}(\mathcal I,E,\mu)$ means $\operatorname{ord}_x(\mathcal I)\ge\mu$ for every $x\in C$.

[F2] [[def-order-of-an-ideal-sheaf-at-a-point]]: $\operatorname{ord}_x(\mathcal I)=\max\{n:\mathcal I_x\subseteq\mathfrak m_x^{\,n}\}$; equivalently, $\operatorname{ord}_x(\mathcal I)$ is the minimum of $\operatorname{ord}_x(f)$ over local sections $f$ of $\mathcal I$ at $x$.

[F3] [[thm-pullback-center-ideal-invertible]], [[def-exceptional-divisor-blowup]], [[def-invertible-sheaf-of-cartier-divisor]]: $\sigma^*\mathcal I_C=\mathcal I(D)$ is the invertible ideal of the exceptional divisor, generated locally by the equation $y$ of $D$.

[F4] [[def-multiple-test-blowup-and-controlled-transform]]: the controlled transform of $(\mathcal I,\mu)$ along $\sigma$ is $\sigma^{\mathrm c}(\mathcal I,\mu)=(\mathcal I(D)^{-\mu}\sigma^*\mathcal I,\mu)$; for a local section $f$ the section $y^{-\mu}\sigma^*(f)$ is called a controlled transform of $f$.

[F5] [[lem-regular-local-regular-quotient-ideal-is-parameter-generated]], [[thm-regular-local-rings-are-domains-and-cohen-macaulay]]: at a point $x\in C$ the ideal $\mathcal I_C$ is generated by parameters $u_1,\dots,u_k$ that extend to a regular system of parameters $u_1,\dots,u_n$ of $\mathcal O_{X,x}$; in particular $C$ is reduced at $x$, and a section not in $\mathcal I_C$ has a nonvanishing value at some point of $C$.

[F6] [[thm-pullback-center-ideal-invertible]], [[def-effective-cartier-divisor]]: the exceptional divisor $D$ is an effective Cartier divisor with invertible ideal $\mathcal I(D)$ and local equation $y$ a nonzerodivisor.

[F7] [[lem-regular-sequence-associated-graded-polynomial]] (Stacks, Lemma 10.69.2): if $P=(u_1,\ldots,u_c)$ is generated by a regular sequence in $R$, then $\operatorname{gr}_P R=(R/P)[U_1,\ldots,U_c]$. In particular each $P^j/P^{j+1}$ is a finite free $R/P$-module. The proof eliminates a homogeneous relation by induction on the sequence length and its degree, using nonzerodivisibility of the last generator modulo the preceding ones.

## Proof

1.1 Fix $x\in C$, put $R=\mathcal O_{X,x}$ and $P=\mathcal I_{C,x}$. By [F5], $P$ is generated by an initial parameter sequence and $R/P$ is a regular local domain. If $f\in\mathcal I_x$ were outside $P^\mu$, choose the largest $j<\mu$ with $f\in P^j$. Its nonzero class in $P^j/P^{j+1}$ remains nonzero after localization at $P$, since [F7] makes this module free over the domain $R/P$. But $R_P=\mathcal O_{X,\eta}$ at the generic point $\eta$ of the component of $C$ through $x$, and its maximal ideal is $PR_P$. Thus $f$ has order $j<\mu$ at $\eta$, contradicting $\eta\in C\subseteq\operatorname{supp}(\mathcal I,\mu)$. This proves $\mathcal I_x\subseteq P^\mu$; outside $C$ the inclusion is automatic. If $\mu=0$ it is immediate everywhere. [F1, F2, F5, F7]


2.1 Second inclusion. Pulling back the inclusion of step 1.1 along $\sigma$ and using that inverse image commutes with ideal products, $\sigma^*\mathcal I\subseteq\sigma^*(\mathcal I_C^\mu)=(\sigma^*\mathcal I_C)^\mu=\mathcal I(D)^\mu$, the last equality by [F3]. [F3, step 1.1]

3.1 The controlled transform is defined and well posed. By step 2.1 the product $\mathcal I(D)^{-\mu}\sigma^*\mathcal I$ is an ideal sheaf on $X'$, namely the controlled transform of [F4]. If $y$ is a local equation of $D$ and $f,f'$ are local generators of $\mathcal I$ on an open set, then $f'=uf$ for a unit $u$, so $y^{-\mu}\sigma^*(f')=(\sigma^*u)\,y^{-\mu}\sigma^*(f)$ differs by the unit $\sigma^*u$; and replacing $y$ by the equation $y'=vy$ of another local generator changes $y^{-\mu}\sigma^*(f)$ by $v^{-\mu}$, again a unit. Hence $y^{-\mu}\sigma^*(f)$ is well defined up to a unit and generates the controlled transform wherever $f$ generates $\mathcal I$, as asserted. [F3, F4, F6, step 2.1] ∎

## Remarks

- The two inclusions admit the empty center reading: if $C=\varnothing$ then $\mathcal I_C=\mathcal O_X$ and both inclusions are trivial; the controlled transform is then $\sigma^*\mathcal I$ with $\sigma$ an isomorphism, matching the definition of a multiple test blow-up extended by isomorphisms.
- No choice beyond the published blowup interface is used in this item.
