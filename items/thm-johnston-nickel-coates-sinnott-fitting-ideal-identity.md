## Statement

Assume AC, Countable Choice, and Dependent Choice. Let $p$ be odd, let $L/K$ be a finite abelian CM extension of number fields, and let $G=\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$. Let $S,T$ be finite nonempty disjoint sets of places of $K$ such that $S$ contains every place above $p$, every place ramified in $L/K$, and every infinite place. For a negative integer $r$, let $j$ be complex conjugation, $e_r=(1-(-1)^rj)/2$, and let $\Theta_{S,T}(r)$ be the smoothed Stickelberger element at $r$. Then
$$\operatorname{Fitt}_{e_r\mathbb Z_p[G]}\!\left(H_T^2(\mathcal O_{K,S},e_r\mathbb Z_p[G]^\#(1-r))\right)=\Theta_{S,T}(r)e_r\mathbb Z_p[G],$$
where $\#(g)=g^{-1}$ and $H_T^2$ is the degree-two cohomology of the truncated étale complex.

At each $v\in T$, the residue-field cohomology is concentrated in degree one and its Fitting ideal is generated by the corresponding Euler factor.
