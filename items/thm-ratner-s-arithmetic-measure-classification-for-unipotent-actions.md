## Statement

Let G be an S-algebraic group, meaning here a finite product of groups of rational points of algebraic groups over local fields of characteristic zero,  Γ a lattice in G, and U a subgroup generated by one-parameter unipotent subgroups. Every ergodic U-invariant probability measure on Γ\G is homogeneous: it is invariant under a subgroup H containing U and is the normalized H-invariant measure on a closed H-orbit.
