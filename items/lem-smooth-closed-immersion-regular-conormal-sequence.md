## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $k$ be a field, let $X$ be a smooth
$k$-scheme of finite type and pure dimension $n$, and let
$j:X\hookrightarrow\mathbb P^N_k$ be a closed immersion over $k$ of pure
codimension $c=N-n$; write $\mathcal I\subseteq\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^N}$ for its
ideal sheaf. Then:

(i) the conormal sheaf $\mathcal I/\mathcal I^2$ is a locally free
$\mathcal O_X$-module of rank $c$;

(ii) near every point of $X$ there are local generators $f_1,\dots,f_c$ of
$\mathcal I$ whose germs form a regular sequence in
$\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^N,x}$, and
$\mathcal O_{X,x}=\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^N,x}/(f_1,\dots,f_c)$ is regular;

(iii) the conormal sequence
$$0\longrightarrow\mathcal I/\mathcal I^2\longrightarrow j^*\Omega^1_{\mathbb P^N/k}\longrightarrow\Omega^1_{X/k}\longrightarrow0$$
is exact, and the middle term is locally free of rank $N$ while the outer terms
are locally free of ranks $c$ and $n$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** a field $k$, a smooth finite-type $k$-scheme $X$ of pure dimension
$n$, a closed immersion $j:X\hookrightarrow\mathbb P^N_k$ of pure codimension
$c=N-n$, and the ideal sheaf $\mathcal I=\ker(\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^N}\to j_*\mathcal O_X)$.

[F1] The conormal sequence of a closed immersion $i:X\to Y$ of $S$-schemes is
right exact: with $\mathcal I$ the ideal sheaf and
$\mathcal Q=\mathcal I/\mathcal I^2$ there is an exact sequence
$\mathcal Q\to i^*\Omega^1_{Y/S}\to\Omega^1_{X/S}\to0$.
([[thm-conormal-sequence-closed-immersion]])

[F2] If $(R,\mathfrak m)$ is a regular local ring and $R/I$ is regular, then $I$ is generated by an initial segment of a regular system of parameters of $R$, of length $\dim R-\dim(R/I)$. ([[lem-regular-local-regular-quotient-ideal-is-parameter-generated]])

[F3] The standard affine charts of $\mathbb P^N_k$ are affine $N$-space. Both $\mathbb P^N_k$ and the given smooth $X$ have regular local rings; their sheaves of relative differentials are locally free of ranks $N$ and $n$, respectively. ([[def-relative-projective-space-standard-charts]], [[thm-jacobian-criterion-smooth-morphism]], [[thm-differentials-smooth-locally-free]])

[F4] For a finite-type $k$-scheme $Y$ and a point $y$, its local scheme dimension satisfies $\dim_yY=\dim\mathcal O_{Y,y}+\operatorname{trdeg}_k\kappa(y)$. A pure-dimensional smooth scheme of relative dimension $d$ has local scheme dimension $d$ at every point. ([[lem-affine-local-dimension-residue-transcendence]], [[thm-jacobian-criterion-smooth-morphism]])

[F5] A regular sequence has an acyclic positive-degree Koszul complex. In particular, every relation $\sum_i a_if_i=0$ among the sequence elements is a sum of the Koszul relations $f_je_i-f_ie_j$, so every coefficient $a_i$ lies in the ideal $(f_1,\ldots,f_c)$. ([[thm-regular-sequences-give-acyclic-koszul-complexes]])

[F6] A field is Noetherian, and a finite-type algebra over a Noetherian ring is Noetherian; hence the coordinate rings of the standard affine charts of $\mathbb P^N_k$ are Noetherian. ([[lem-field-is-noetherian]], [[cor-finite-type-algebra-over-noetherian-ring-is-noetherian]])

## Proof

**Proof technique:** compute the local codimension at every scheme point, obtain a regular sequence from the regular-quotient criterion, identify its conormal basis using Koszul $H_1$, and prove left exactness of the conormal sequence by the ranks of smooth differential modules.

1.1 Fix any point $x\in X$, including a nonclosed point. Put $R=\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^N,x}$, $I=\mathcal I_x$, and $S=R/I=\mathcal O_{X,x}$. The closed immersion identifies the residue fields of $R$ and $S$ with the same $\kappa(x)$; write $t=\operatorname{trdeg}_k\kappa(x)$. By [F3], $R$ and $S$ are regular local rings. The local scheme dimensions of $\mathbb P^N_k$ and $X$ at $x$ are $N$ and $n$, so [F4] gives $\dim R=N-t$ and $\dim S=n-t$. Thus $\dim R-\dim S=N-n=c$ at every $x$, although the individual local-ring dimensions equal $N,n$ only when $t=0$. [F3, F4, given]

2.1 Apply [F2] to the regular local ring $R$ and its regular quotient $S$. There is a regular system of parameters of $R$ whose first $c$ members $f_1,\ldots,f_c$ generate $I$; in particular this is an $R$-regular sequence. Each germ $f_i$ has a representative on an affine neighbourhood of $x$. By [F6] the ambient affine chart is Noetherian, so its ideal is finitely generated; clearing the finitely many denominators in the generation equalities at the stalk shrinks the neighbourhood until those representatives generate $\mathcal I$ there. Their germs at $x$ remain the stated regular sequence. This proves assertion (ii), with $S$ regular of its actual local dimension $n-t$. [F2, F3, F6, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 The classes of $f_1,\ldots,f_c$ give a surjection $S^c\twoheadrightarrow I/I^2$. If $\sum_i\overline a_i[f_i]=0$, lift $\overline a_i$ to $a_i\in R$ and write $\sum_i a_if_i=\sum_i b_if_i$ with every $b_i\in I$, because the left side lies in $I^2$. Then $\sum_i(a_i-b_i)f_i=0$, and [F5] makes every $a_i-b_i$ lie in $I$; hence every $\overline a_i=0$ in $S$. Thus $S^c\xrightarrow{\sim}I/I^2$. Since $x$ was arbitrary, $\mathcal I/\mathcal I^2$ is locally free of rank $c$, proving (i). [F5, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 By [F1] the conormal map $I/I^2\to j^*\Omega^1_{\mathbb P^N/k,x}$ surjects onto $K=\ker(j^*\Omega^1_{\mathbb P^N/k,x}\to\Omega^1_{X/k,x})$. By [F3] the middle and target modules are free over the local ring $S$ of ranks $N$ and $n$. The surjection onto the free target splits, so $K$ is a finite projective, hence free, $S$-module of rank $N-n=c$. Step 3.1 makes the conormal module free of the same rank. A surjection between free rank-$c$ modules over a local ring has determinant nonzero modulo the maximal ideal, hence unit determinant and an inverse by the adjugate formula. Therefore $I/I^2\to K$ is an isomorphism, so the conormal map is injective at every $x$. This argument uses the actual differential-module ranks and does not treat an arbitrary regular parameter system as a basis of relative differentials. [F1, F3, step 3.1, algebra]

5.1 The stalkwise isomorphism in step 3.1 gives the locally free conormal sheaf of rank $c$, step 2.1 gives the local regular-sequence presentation, and step 4.1 upgrades the right-exact conormal sequence [F1] to the short exact sequence in (iii). By [F3] the other terms have ranks $N$ and $n$; all maps are canonical sheaf morphisms, so their stalkwise exactness proves exactness globally. The Axiom of Choice is assumed as declared; this finite local calculation makes no further arbitrary family of choices. [F1, F3, step 2.1, step 3.1, step 4.1, discharge-construct] ∎
