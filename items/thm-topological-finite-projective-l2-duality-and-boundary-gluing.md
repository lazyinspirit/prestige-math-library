## Statement

Assume full AC. Let $M$ be a compact oriented Hausdorff topological manifold with supplied boundary collar, and supply a based homomorphism on each component to a discrete group $G$, with compatible paths when maps or gluings are considered. Use $R=\mathbb CG$, with involution conjugating complex scalars and sending $g$ to $g^{-1}$, and its based right regular local system with standard parallel Hermitian form. Let $A=\mathcal NG$ have the canonical group trace. Then its singular coefficient complex has an actual bounded finite-projective $R$ chain-homotopy model. Absolute, relative and finite whole-component gluing diagrams admit such models and finite matrix comparison maps.

After extending these finite projective matrices to $\ell^2(G)$, write $\mathscr H_i(M)$ for completed reduced homology, with boundaries replaced by their Hilbert closures. If $p_Z,p_B$ are the cycle and boundary-closure projections in a finite ambient projection module, the corresponding algebraic reduced module is $P_i=(p_Z-p_B)A^r$, and
$$H_i(M;A)\twoheadrightarrow P_i\hookrightarrow\mathscr H_i(M)=(p_Z-p_B)\ell^2(G)^r.$$
The first map has dimension-zero kernel, and the second is injective with dense image and need not be onto. Pair and whole-component Mayer–Vietoris sequences are weakly exact on the completed modules. Oriented cap duality gives bounded mutually inverse maps on their completed reduced homology and cohomology, with the cap-boundary and restriction identities. These structures and the resulting spectral signature do not depend on the finite-projective model.

For a four-manifold the middle intersection form is the bounded Hermitian form defined by inverse relative cap duality, restriction and evaluation. Its Hilbert radical is the kernel of its self-adjoint operator. Its signature is the trace of the positive spectral projection minus the trace of the negative projection; no closed range or bounded inverse is required for a manifold with boundary. For a closed four-manifold its middle intersection operator is boundedly invertible.

Let $W_0,W_1$ be compact oriented topological four-manifolds, let $N$ be a union of entire closed boundary components, and glue the two marked copies of $N$ by an orientation-reversing identification and the supplied collars. Put $W=W_0\cup_N W_1$ and supply one compatible regular coefficient system on $W$. Other boundary components may remain. Then
$$\operatorname{sign}^{(2)}_G(W)=\operatorname{sign}^{(2)}_G(W_0)+\operatorname{sign}^{(2)}_G(W_1),\qquad\operatorname{sign}(W)=\operatorname{sign}(W_0)+\operatorname{sign}(W_1).$$
Orientation reversal changes the corresponding sign. No finite CW type, triangulation, smoothing of a topological four-manifold, equality of ordinary and $L^2$ signatures on arbitrary closed manifolds, or analytic eta-to-defect comparison is asserted or assumed. For an arbitrary possibly uncountable $G$, the finite-matrix construction has the same dimensions and signatures as its countable coefficient image, so it agrees with the countable-group convention.

5.1 Each finite $R$ matrix acts boundedly on finite sums of $\ell^2(G)$, with norm bounded by the finite sums of absolute values of its group coefficients. 6.1 In a completed degree term $p\ell^2(G)^r$, let $p_Z$ project onto $\ker d_i$ and $p_B$ onto $\overline{\operatorname{im}d_{i+1}}$.
