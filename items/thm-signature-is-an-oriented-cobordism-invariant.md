## Statement

Assume AC, inherited from Poincare-Lefschetz duality. Let $W$ be a compact
oriented smooth $(4k+1)$-manifold with boundary $M=\partial W$ carrying the
induced orientation and inclusion $i:M\hookrightarrow W$. Then $\sigma(M)=0$.
Consequently, if $M_0,M_1$ are closed oriented $4k$-manifolds that are oriented
cobordant ([[def-oriented-smooth-cobordism]]), then
$\sigma(M_0)=\sigma(M_1)$: by [[def-oriented-smooth-cobordism]],
$\partial V=(-M_0)\sqcup M_1$; reversing the orientation of $V$ gives
$\partial(-V)=M_0\sqcup(-M_1)$ as an oriented boundary, so
$0=\sigma(M_0\sqcup(-M_1))=\sigma(M_0)-\sigma(M_1)$ by
[[lem-signature-is-additive-under-disjoint-union-and-orientation-reversal]].
Hence $\sigma$ descends to a well-defined additive map on oriented bordism
classes $\Omega_{4k}^{SO}\to\mathbb Z$ and vanishes on null-cobordant classes
([[def-null-cobordant-closed-manifold]]).

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC; a compact oriented smooth $(4k+1)$-manifold $W$ with boundary $M=\partial W$ and inclusion $i$, with the induced boundary orientation.

[F1] For a closed oriented $4k$-manifold the signature is the inertia difference $\sigma(M)=p-q$ of the nondegenerate middle form $Q_M$ ([[def-signature-of-a-closed-oriented-four-k-manifold]], [[def-middle-dimensional-intersection-form]]).

[F2] With $K=\operatorname{im}(i^*:H^{2k}(W;\mathbb R)\to H^{2k}(M;\mathbb R))$, one has $K=K^{\perp_{Q_M}}$, and $K$ is a totally isotropic subspace of half the dimension of $H^{2k}(M;\mathbb R)$ ([[lem-boundary-restriction-image-is-lagrangian]]).

[F3] A nondegenerate symmetric form with a totally isotropic subspace of exactly half the dimension has zero signature ([[lem-a-half-dimensional-isotropic-subspace-forces-zero-signature]]).

[F4] The signature is additive over disjoint unions and negates under orientation reversal: $\sigma(X\sqcup Y)=\sigma(X)+\sigma(Y)$ and $\sigma(-X)=-\sigma(X)$ ([[lem-signature-is-additive-under-disjoint-union-and-orientation-reversal]]).

[F5] Oriented cobordism is the equivalence relation generated by oriented bordisms: for a bordism $V$ from $M_0$ to $M_1$, $\partial V=(-M_0)\sqcup M_1$, and a null-cobordant closed manifold bounds a compact oriented manifold ([[def-oriented-smooth-cobordism]], [[def-null-cobordant-closed-manifold]], [[def-unoriented-and-oriented-bordism-groups]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct; the boundary restriction image is a Lagrangian subspace, so the isotropic lemma kills the signature.

1.1 Boundary vanishing: by [F2] the subspace $K$ of $H^{2k}(M;\mathbb R)$ is totally isotropic for the nondegenerate form $Q_M$ and has dimension one half of $\dim H^{2k}(M;\mathbb R)$; hence $\sigma(M)=0$ by [F1] and [F3]. [given, F1, F2, F3]

2.1 Cobordism invariance: let $V$ be an oriented cobordism from $M_0$ to $M_1$, so that $\partial V=(-M_0)\sqcup M_1$ by [F5]. Reversing the orientation of $V$ makes its boundary $M_0\sqcup(-M_1)$, so step 1.1 gives $0=\sigma(M_0\sqcup(-M_1))=\sigma(M_0)+\sigma(-M_1)=\sigma(M_0)-\sigma(M_1)$ by [F4]; hence $\sigma(M_0)=\sigma(M_1)$. [step 1.1, F4, F5]

3.1 Descent: steps 1.1 and 2.1 show that $\sigma$ is constant on oriented cobordism classes and vanishes on null-cobordant manifolds (which bound by [F5]); with additivity [F4] it descends to a well-defined additive map $\Omega_{4k}^{SO}\to\mathbb Z$. The empty manifold has $\sigma(\varnothing)=0$ and for $k=0$ the boundary of an oriented 1-manifold has signed count zero, consistent with step 1.1. [step 1.1, step 2.1, F4, F5] ∎
