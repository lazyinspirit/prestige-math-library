## Definition

Let B be a four-dimensional central simple algebra over ℚ that becomes M₂(ℝ) over ℝ, let O⊂B be a rank-four ℤ-subring spanning B, and let nrd:B→ℚ be its reduced norm, which becomes determinant after a splitting identification. If the image Γ of {u∈O:nrd(u)=1} in PSL₂(ℝ) is a torsion-free cocompact lattice, then M=Γ\ℍ is a compact arithmetic hyperbolic surface. At primes p where B and O are unramified and split, the standard Hecke correspondence is the finite correspondence attached to the local double coset O_p×diag(p,1)O_p×.
