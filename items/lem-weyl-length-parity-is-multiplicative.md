## Statement

Let $W$ be the Weyl group of the root system $\Phi$ with its real span
$E=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}\Phi$ and length function $\ell$
([[def-finite-weyl-root-system-lattice-and-chamber-conventions]],
[[def-root-reflections-and-the-weyl-group-action]]). Then
$$(-1)^{\ell(uv)}=(-1)^{\ell(u)}(-1)^{\ell(v)}\qquad(u,v\in W),$$
so that $w\mapsto(-1)^{\ell(w)}$ is a group homomorphism $W\to\{\pm1\}$, and
this homomorphism is the determinant of the action of $W$ on $E$:
$(-1)^{\ell(w)}=\det(w)$ for every $w\in W$. In particular
$(-1)^{\ell(w^{-1})}=(-1)^{\ell(w)}$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The finite root system $\Phi$ spanning the real vector space $E$ of dimension $n$ with its positive definite form, the Weyl group $W$ generated by the root reflections $s_\alpha$, its simple reflections $s_i$ and the length function $\ell$, and elements $u,v,w\in W$.

[F1] For each root $\alpha$ the reflection $s_\alpha(\lambda)=\lambda-\langle\lambda,\alpha^\vee\rangle\alpha$ fixes $\alpha^\perp$ pointwise and sends $\alpha$ to $-\alpha$; in particular $E=\mathbb R\alpha\oplus\alpha^\perp$ and $\alpha\wedge e_2\wedge\cdots\wedge e_n$ is a nonzero element of $\Lambda^nE$ for a basis $e_2,\dots,e_n$ of $\alpha^\perp$ ([[def-root-reflections-and-the-weyl-group-action]], [[def-finite-weyl-root-system-lattice-and-chamber-conventions]]).

[F2] If $T:V\to V$ is an endomorphism of an $n$-dimensional vector space, then $\Lambda^nT=\det(T)\cdot\operatorname{id}_{\Lambda^nV}$ on the one-dimensional space $\Lambda^nV$; and $\det(S\circ T)=\det(S)\det(T)$ for endomorphisms $S,T$ ([[cor-the-top-exterior-power-acts-by-the-determinant]], [[cor-determinant-multiplicativity-from-the-top-exterior-power]]).

[F3] The simple reflections generate $W$ and $\ell(w)$ is the least number of simple reflections in an expression for $w$; every simple reflection $s_i$ is a root reflection and $\ell(s_i)=1$; all of this includes the case $\Phi=\emptyset$, where $E=0$ and $W=\{1\}$ ([[lem-finite-weyl-positive-roots-and-simple-reflections]], [[def-finite-weyl-root-system-lattice-and-chamber-conventions]], [[prop-weyl-length-equals-positive-root-inversion-number]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 If $E=0$, then $W=\{1\}$ and both the length sign and the determinant of its identity are $1$, so all assertions hold. Assume $\dim E\ge1$. For every root $\alpha$ one has $\det(s_\alpha)=-1$: choosing the basis $(\alpha,e_2,\dots,e_n)$ of $E$ with $e_2,\dots,e_n$ a basis of $\alpha^\perp$, [F1] gives $s_\alpha(\alpha)=-\alpha$ and $s_\alpha(e_i)=e_i$, so $\Lambda^ns_\alpha(\alpha\wedge e_2\wedge\cdots\wedge e_n)=(-\alpha)\wedge e_2\wedge\cdots\wedge e_n=-(\alpha\wedge e_2\wedge\cdots\wedge e_n)$, and since this wedge is a basis of the one-dimensional space $\Lambda^nE$, [F2] forces $\det(s_\alpha)=-1$. [F1, F2, algebra]

2.1 Every $w\in W$ is a product of simple reflections by [F3]; if $w=s_{i_1}\cdots s_{i_k}$ is any such expression, then repeated use of [F2] together with step 1.1 gives $\det(w)=\prod_{j=1}^k\det(s_{i_j})=(-1)^k$, so $\det(w)$ agrees with $(-1)^k$ for every expression of $w$; choosing an expression of minimal length $k=\ell(w)$, which exists by [F3], gives $\det(w)=(-1)^{\ell(w)}$. [F2, F3, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 For $u,v\in W$ the multiplicativity in [F2] and step 2.1 give $(-1)^{\ell(uv)}=\det(uv)=\det(u)\det(v)=(-1)^{\ell(u)}(-1)^{\ell(v)}$, so $w\mapsto(-1)^{\ell(w)}$ is a homomorphism $W\to\{\pm1\}$ agreeing with the determinant; applying it to $w^{-1}$ and using $\det(w)^{-1}=\det(w)\in\{\pm1\}$ gives $(-1)^{\ell(w^{-1})}=\det(w^{-1})=\det(w)=(-1)^{\ell(w)}$. [F2, step 2.1, algebra] ∎
