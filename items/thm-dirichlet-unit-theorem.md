## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]). For a number field $K$ of
signature $(r_1,r_2)$ there is an isomorphism
$\mathcal O_K^\times\cong\mu(K)\times\mathbb Z^{r_1+r_2-1}$. In particular
$\mathcal O_K^\times$ is finitely generated of rank $r_1+r_2-1$ and its torsion
subgroup is $\mu(K)$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The Axiom of Choice, a number field $K$ of signature $(r_1,r_2)$
with logarithmic embedding $\lambda$ ([[def-logarithmic-unit-embedding]]), and
the image $\Lambda:=\lambda(\mathcal O_K^\times)$.

[F1] For $x,y\in K^\times$ the multiplicativity of every embedding and of the
complex modulus gives $|\sigma_i(xy)|=|\sigma_ix|\,|\sigma_iy|$ and
$|\tau_j(xy)|=|\tau_jx|\,|\tau_jy|$, and $\log(xy)=\log x+\log y$ for positive
reals; hence $\lambda(xy)=\lambda(x)+\lambda(y)$, so $\lambda$ restricts to a
group homomorphism $\mathcal O_K^\times\to\mathbb R^{r_1+r_2}$
([[def-logarithmic-unit-embedding]], [[thm-natural-logarithm-laws]],
[[lem-complex-conjugation-and-modulus-laws]]).

[F2] $\ker(\lambda|_{\mathcal O_K^\times})=\mu(K)$; this kernel is finite and is
exactly the torsion subgroup of $\mathcal O_K^\times$
([[lem-kernel-of-the-unit-logarithm-is-the-roots-of-unity]],
[[def-roots-of-unity-in-a-field]]).

[F3] $\Lambda$ is a full lattice in the hyperplane $H$ of the definition of
$\lambda$, of rank $r_1+r_2-1$; hence
$\Lambda=\mathbb Zv_1\oplus\cdots\oplus\mathbb Zv_r$ for $\mathbb R$-linearly
independent $v_1,\dots,v_r\in\Lambda$ and $r=r_1+r_2-1$
([[thm-logarithmic-unit-image-is-a-full-lattice]]).

[F4] Every finitely generated abelian group $G$ has a decomposition
$G\cong\mathbb Z^s\oplus T$ with $T$ its finite torsion subgroup, and the
integer $s$ (the free rank) and the torsion subgroup are determined by $G$
([[cor-fundamental-theorem-of-finitely-generated-abelian-groups-from-pid-modules]]).

[F5] The units of a commutative ring form an abelian group under
multiplication; in particular $\mathcal O_K^\times$ is an abelian group
([[lem-ring-units-form-a-group]]).



## Proof

**Proof technique:** split the unit group by the logarithmic embedding. The
image is a free abelian group whose rank is computed by the full-lattice
theorem, and the kernel is the finite roots-of-unity group, so lifting a
$\mathbb Z$-basis of the image exhibits $\mathcal O_K^\times$ as
$\mu(K)\times\mathbb Z^{r_1+r_2-1}$.

1.1 The restriction $\lambda|_{\mathcal O_K^\times}$ is a group homomorphism onto $\Lambda$ with kernel $\mu(K)$, a finite group that coincides with the torsion subgroup of $\mathcal O_K^\times$. [F1, F2]

1.2 By [F3] the image is $\Lambda=\mathbb Zv_1\oplus\cdots\oplus\mathbb Zv_r$ with $v_1,\dots,v_r\in\Lambda$ $\mathbb R$-linearly independent and $r=r_1+r_2-1$; choose once and for all units $u_1,\dots,u_r\in\mathcal O_K^\times$ with $\lambda(u_i)=v_i$, a selection of finitely many elements. [F3]

2.1 Let $u\in\mathcal O_K^\times$; since $\Lambda$ is generated by $v_1,\dots,v_r$ there are unique integers $m_1,\dots,m_r$ with $\lambda(u)=\sum_im_iv_i=\lambda(u_1^{m_1}\cdots u_r^{m_r})$, so $\lambda(u\,(u_1^{m_1}\cdots u_r^{m_r})^{-1})=0$ and hence $u\,(u_1^{m_1}\cdots u_r^{m_r})^{-1}=\zeta$ for some $\zeta\in\mu(K)$; therefore $u=\zeta\,u_1^{m_1}\cdots u_r^{m_r}$. [F1, step 1.1, step 1.2]

3.1 The expression in step 2.1 is unique: if $\zeta u_1^{m_1}\cdots u_r^{m_r}=\zeta'u_1^{m_1'}\cdots u_r^{m_r'}$ with $\zeta,\zeta'\in\mu(K)$, then applying the homomorphism and using $\lambda(\zeta)=\lambda(\zeta')=0$ gives $\sum_i(m_i-m_i')v_i=0$, hence $m_i=m_i'$ for every $i$ by linear independence of the $v_i$, and then $\zeta=\zeta'$. [F1, step 1.2]

4.1 Consequently the map $\mu(K)\times\mathbb Z^r\to\mathcal O_K^\times$, $(\zeta,m_1,\dots,m_r)\mapsto\zeta u_1^{m_1}\cdots u_r^{m_r}$, is a bijective group homomorphism, because $\mathcal O_K^\times$ is abelian and $u_1^{m_1}\cdots u_r^{m_r}\,u_1^{n_1}\cdots u_r^{n_r}=u_1^{m_1+n_1}\cdots u_r^{m_r+n_r}$; hence $\mathcal O_K^\times\cong\mu(K)\times\mathbb Z^r$ with $r=r_1+r_2-1$, and $\mathcal O_K^\times$ is finitely generated. [F5, step 1.2, step 2.1, step 3.1]

5.1 Since $\mathcal O_K^\times$ is finitely generated abelian, [F4] exhibits it as $\mathbb Z^s\oplus T$ with $T$ its finite torsion subgroup; the displayed isomorphism of step 4.1 has free part $\mathbb Z^{r_1+r_2-1}$ and torsion factor $\mu(K)$, so by the uniqueness in [F4] the free rank is $s=r_1+r_2-1$ and the torsion subgroup is $T=\mu(K)$. [F2, F4, step 4.1]

6.1 Choice accounting: the assumption AC enters only through the full-lattice theorem [F3]; the only selections made here are the finitely many units $u_1,\dots,u_r$ lifting a finite basis, and no choice over an infinite family occurs. [F3, step 1.2] ∎
