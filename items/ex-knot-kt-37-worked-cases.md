## Example

Let $U$ be the crossingless diagram of the oriented unknot, and choose any basepoint $p$ on its unique circle. For the normalized integral Khovanov cochain complex, $Kh^{0,1}(U)\cong\mathbb Z$, $Kh^{0,-1}(U)\cong\mathbb Z$, and all other unreduced groups vanish. For the reduced quotient convention that kills the based-circle $X$ label and lowers the quantum degree of the surviving label $1$ by one, $\widetilde{Kh}^{0,0}(U,p)\cong\mathbb Z$ and all other reduced groups vanish. Consequently the unreduced and reduced rank Euler polynomials are $q+q^{-1}$ and $1$, respectively.

## Facts & Assumptions
**Given:** The crossingless one-circle diagram $U$ and a basepoint $p$ on that circle.

[F1] The crossing set is empty, so there is one state and its state module is one copy of $\mathcal A$ in cohomological degree $0$; there are no edge maps ([[alg-khovanov-chain-complex-from-diagram]]).

[F2] The basis of $\mathcal A=\mathbb Z[X]/(X^2)$ is $1,X$ with quantum degrees $1,-1$ ([[def-khovanov-frobenius-algebra-and-cube-of-resolutions]]).

[F3] The reduced quotient by the image of the basepoint action removes $X$ and shifts the surviving $1$ generator's quantum degree from $1$ to $0$ ([[def-reduced-khovanov-cohomology-of-a-knot]]).

## Verification

1.1 By [F1], the zero-crossing cube has just $C^{0}(U)=\mathcal A$ and differential zero. Therefore its cohomology equals $\mathcal A$ in cohomological degree $0$. By [F2], the two basis elements give one free rank-one group in quantum degree $1$ and one in degree $-1$, with no other groups. [F1, F2, given, algebra]

2.1 In the based quotient, the image of multiplication by $X$ is $\mathbb Z\{X\}$, so the quotient of $\mathcal A=\mathbb Z\{1,X\}$ is freely generated by $[1]$. By [F3] its reduced quantum degree is $1-1=0$; the differential remains zero, so its only cohomology is $\mathbb Z$ in bidegree $(0,0)$. Summing the unreduced ranks with their quantum powers gives $q+q^{-1}$, and the reduced rank sum is $1$. [F2, F3, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
