## Definition

Let $X$ and $Y$ be topological spaces and let $C(X,Y)$ be the set of continuous maps from $X$ to $Y$. For a compact subset $K\subseteq X$ and an open subset $V\subseteq Y$, put

$$S(K,V):=\{f\in C(X,Y):f[K]\subseteq V\}.$$

The **compact-open topology** on $C(X,Y)$ is the topology generated by all sets $S(K,V)$ as a subbasis. In particular, $S(\varnothing,V)=C(X,Y)$ and $S(K,Y)=C(X,Y)$, so the empty compact set and the whole target introduce no exceptional case.
