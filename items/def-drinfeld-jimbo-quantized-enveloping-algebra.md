## Definition

Let $(I,A,D,P,P^{\vee},q)$ be a symmetrizable Cartan datum for a quantum group ([[def-symmetrizable-cartan-datum-for-a-quantum-group]]) and put $q_i=q^{d_i}$. Write $K_i:=K_{d_i h_i}$ and $K_i^{-1}:=K_{-d_i h_i}$. Let $V$ be the $\mathbb Q(q)$-vector space with basis the symbols $\{E_i,F_i\}_{i\in I}\cup\{K_h\}_{h\in P^{\vee}}$, and let $T:=T(V)$ be its free unital associative $\mathbb Q(q)$-algebra ([[def-tensor-algebra-of-a-vector-space]], [[thm-universal-property-of-the-tensor-algebra]]). Let $I_{\mathrm{DJ}}$ be the two-sided ideal of $T$ generated by ([[def-generated-and-principal-ideals]]) the following relations:

$$K_0-1,\qquad K_hK_{h'}-K_{h+h'}\qquad(h,h'\in P^{\vee});$$

$$K_hE_i-q^{\langle\alpha_i,h\rangle}E_iK_h,\qquad K_hF_i-q^{-\langle\alpha_i,h\rangle}F_iK_h\qquad(h\in P^{\vee},\ i\in I);$$

$$E_iF_j-F_jE_i-\delta_{ij}\frac{K_i-K_i^{-1}}{q_i-q_i^{-1}}\qquad(i,j\in I);$$

$$\mathrm{Serre}^+_{ij}:=\sum_{r=0}^{1-a_{ij}}(-1)^r\binom{1-a_{ij}}{r}_iE_i^{1-a_{ij}-r}E_jE_i^r,\qquad \mathrm{Serre}^-_{ij}:=\sum_{r=0}^{1-a_{ij}}(-1)^r\binom{1-a_{ij}}{r}_iF_i^{1-a_{ij}-r}F_jF_i^r\qquad(i\ne j),$$

where the Gaussian binomials are those of [[def-quantum-integers-factorials-and-divided-powers-at-q-i]]. The **Drinfeld-Jimbo quantized enveloping algebra** is

$$U_q(\mathfrak g):=T/I_{\mathrm{DJ}}$$

([[def-quotient-ring]]), with $E_i,F_i,K_h$ denoting the images of the generators. In this quotient, $K_i^{-1}$ is the two-sided inverse of $K_i$.

It is graded by the root lattice $Q=\bigoplus_{i\in I}\mathbb Z\alpha_i$ ([[def-kac-moody-root-lattice-height-and-positive-cone]]), with $\deg E_i=\alpha_i$, $\deg F_i=-\alpha_i$ and $\deg K_h=0$; thus $U_q(\mathfrak g)=\bigoplus_{\beta\in Q}U_q(\mathfrak g)[\beta]$.

For every unital associative $\mathbb Q(q)$-algebra $B$, any assignment of elements $E_i',F_i',K_h'\in B$ satisfying these relations extends uniquely to a $\mathbb Q(q)$-algebra homomorphism $U_q(\mathfrak g)\to B$ ([[thm-quotient-ring-universal-property]]).

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A symmetrizable Cartan datum and its root lattice $Q$; the displayed symbols and relations are formed over $\mathbb Q(q)$.

[F1] The root lattice is freely generated by the simple roots, and $\langle\alpha_i,h\rangle\in\mathbb Z$ for $h\in P^{\vee}$ ([[def-symmetrizable-cartan-datum-for-a-quantum-group]], [[def-kac-moody-root-lattice-height-and-positive-cone]]).

[F2] $q_i=q^{d_i}$ with $d_i>0$, so $q_i-q_i^{-1}\ne0$ in $\mathbb Q(q)$ ([[def-quantum-integers-factorials-and-divided-powers-at-q-i]]).

[F3] The tensor algebra is a direct sum of finite words with concatenation product; assigning group degrees to its generator symbols gives the direct-sum grading by total degree. A linear map from the generating vector space extends uniquely to an algebra homomorphism ([[def-tensor-algebra-of-a-vector-space]], [[thm-universal-property-of-the-tensor-algebra]]).

[F4] The two-sided ideal generated by the listed relations is an ideal, its quotient ring is defined by cosets, and maps whose kernel contains that ideal factor uniquely through the quotient ([[def-generated-and-principal-ideals]], [[def-quotient-ring]], [[thm-quotient-ring-universal-property]]).

## Verification

**Proof technique:** Check the toral inverse, homogeneity of the relations, and the universal factorization.

1.1 The relations $K_hK_{-h}-K_0$ and $K_0-1$ give $K_hK_{-h}=1=K_{-h}K_h$ in the quotient. In particular $K_i^{-1}=K_{-d_i h_i}$ is a well-defined two-sided inverse, and the mixed commutator relation has a defined coefficient by [F2]. [F2, F4, algebra]

1.2 Give each generator its stated degree in $Q$. The toral relations have degree $0$; the $K_hE_i$ and $K_hF_i$ relations have degrees $\alpha_i$ and $-\alpha_i$; the $E_iF_j$ relation is homogeneous of degree $0$ if $i=j$ and degree $\alpha_i-\alpha_j$ if $i\ne j$; and the two Serre sums have degrees $(1-a_{ij})\alpha_i+\alpha_j$ and its negative. Thus every generator of $I_{\mathrm{DJ}}$ is homogeneous. [F1, algebra]

2.1 Every element of the two-sided ideal is a finite sum of products $u r v$ with $r$ a homogeneous defining relation. Decomposing $u$ and $v$ into their finite homogeneous components shows that each homogeneous component of every element of $I_{\mathrm{DJ}}$ again belongs to $I_{\mathrm{DJ}}$. Hence the ideal is homogeneous and the quotient has the direct-sum root-lattice grading stated above. [step 1.2, F3, F4, algebra]

3.1 Given an assignment into $B$ satisfying the relations, [F3] extends its values to a unique $\mathbb Q(q)$-algebra homomorphism $T\to B$. Every generator of $I_{\mathrm{DJ}}$ maps to zero, so the ideal lies in the kernel; [F4] then gives the unique ring factor $U_q(\mathfrak g)\to B$. Since the quotient map and the original map preserve scalars, surjectivity of the quotient map makes the factor preserve scalars as well. Conversely, any such factor is determined by the images of the generators because they generate the quotient. [F3, F4, algebra] ∎
