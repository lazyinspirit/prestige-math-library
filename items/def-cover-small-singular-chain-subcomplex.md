## Definition

If $\mathcal U$ is a family of subsets whose interiors cover $X$, let
$C_n^{\mathcal U}(X;G)$ be the subgroup generated by singular $n$-simplices
whose images lie in one member of $\mathcal U$. Faces remain in that member,
so these groups form the **cover-small singular-chain subcomplex**.
