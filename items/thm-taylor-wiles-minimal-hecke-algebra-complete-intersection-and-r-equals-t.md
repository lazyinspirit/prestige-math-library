## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice, let $p$ be an odd prime, let $\mathcal O$ be the ring of integers of a finite extension of $\mathbb Q_p$, and let $k$ be its residue field. Fix an algebraic closure $\overline{\mathbb Q}$ and put $G_{\mathbb Q}=\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb Q}/\mathbb Q)$. A continuous residual representation $\bar\rho:G_{\mathbb Q}\to\operatorname{GL}_2(k)$ is called modular here if it is isomorphic to the reduction of a Galois representation attached to a cuspidal newform, odd if $\det\bar\rho(c)=-1$ for complex conjugation $c$, and absolutely irreducible if it remains irreducible over an algebraic closure of $k$. Suppose $\bar\rho$ is modular and odd and its restriction to $G_{\mathbb Q(\sqrt{(-1)^{(p-1)/2}p})}$ is absolutely irreducible.

For each rational prime $\ell$, fix a prime of $\overline{\mathbb Q}$ above $\ell$; let $D_\ell$ be its stabilizer in $G_{\mathbb Q}$ and let $I_\ell$ be the subgroup acting trivially on the residue field at that prime. A character is unramified when it is trivial on inertia. At $p$, assume either that $\bar\rho|_{D_p}$ is equivalent to $\begin{pmatrix}\psi_1&*\\0&\psi_2\end{pmatrix}$ for distinct characters $\psi_1,\psi_2$ with $\psi_2$ unramified (trivial on $I_p$), or that it is induced from a character of the unramified quadratic extension of $\mathbb Q_p$ whose restriction to inertia is a fundamental tame character of level two (a character of order $p^2-1$ with values in $\mathbb F_{p^2}^\times$, defined up to its $p$-power Frobenius conjugate). At each prime $\ell\ne p$, assume one of the following local conditions: $\bar\rho|_{I_\ell}$ is semisimple (a direct sum of irreducible representations) and equivalent to $\operatorname{diag}(\chi,1)$ for a character $\chi$; $\bar\rho|_{I_\ell}$ is equivalent to a nontrivial upper-triangular unipotent representation; or $\bar\rho|_{D_\ell}$ is absolutely irreducible and, if $\bar\rho|_{I_\ell}$ is absolutely reducible, then $\ell\not\equiv-1\pmod p$.

Let $\varepsilon_p:G_{\mathbb Q}\to\mathbb Z_p^\times$ be the $p$-adic cyclotomic character and let $\bar\varepsilon_p$ be its reduction modulo $p$. Let $D_{\min}$ be the minimal deformation problem of type $Q=\varnothing$ defined as follows. Its objects over a local Artinian $\mathcal O$-algebra $A$ with residue field $k$, given the discrete topology, are strict-equivalence classes (lifts modulo conjugation by matrices congruent to the identity modulo the maximal ideal of $A$) of continuous lifts $\rho_A:G_{\mathbb Q}\to\operatorname{GL}_2(A)$ of $\bar\rho$ such that $\varepsilon_p^{-1}\det\rho_A$ has finite order prime to $p$; if $\bar\rho|_{I_\ell}$ is semisimple for $\ell\ne p$, then $\rho_A|_{I_\ell}$ has the same inertia representation, and if $\bar\rho|_{I_\ell}$ is unipotent upper triangular, then $\rho_A|_{I_\ell}$ remains unipotent upper triangular; at $p$, $\rho_A$ is finite-flat whenever $\bar\rho$ is finite-flat and $\det\bar\rho|_{I_p}=\bar\varepsilon_p$, and otherwise $\rho_A|_{D_p}$ is upper triangular with unramified quotient (trivial on $I_p$) lifting the prescribed residual quotient character. Finite-flat means arising as the generic fibre of a finite flat group scheme over $\mathbb Z_p$. Write $T_{\min}$ for the complete local Noetherian completion at the maximal ideal determined by $\bar\rho$ of the weight-two Hecke algebra generated by Hecke and diamond operators at the minimal level of the modular eigensystem, including $U_p$ when required by the local condition, and assume its residue field is $k$. Then $D_{\min}$ is pro-represented by a complete local Noetherian $\mathcal O$-algebra $R_{\min}$ with residue field $k$ (that is, $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathrm{cont},\mathcal O}(R_{\min},A)\cong D_{\min}(A)$ naturally for every such $A$), and $T_{\min}$ carries a continuous Galois representation $\rho_{T_{\min}}$ lifting $\bar\rho$ and satisfying the same determinant and local conditions. The universal property defines $\phi_{\min}:R_{\min}\to T_{\min}$ from $\rho_{T_{\min}}$. This map is an isomorphism, and both rings are complete intersections, meaning quotients of regular local rings by regular sequences (a regular sequence is a sequence each of whose elements is a non-zero-divisor modulo the ideal generated by its predecessors).
