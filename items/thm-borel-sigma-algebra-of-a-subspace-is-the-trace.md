## Statement

Let $Y$ be a subspace of a topological space $X$. Then

$$\mathcal B(Y)=\mathcal B(X)|_Y.$$

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A topological space $X$ and a subset $Y\subseteq X$ with its subspace topology.

[L1] The subspace topology on $Y$ consists exactly of the traces $U\cap Y$ of open sets $U$ of $X$ ([[def-subspace-topology-top]]).

[L2] Generating a sigma-algebra commutes with taking traces ([[thm-generated-trace-commutes]]).

[L3] The Borel sigma-algebra of a topological space is generated by its open sets ([[def-borel-sigma-algebra]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 By [L1], the family generating $\mathcal B(Y)$ is precisely the trace on $Y$ of the family generating $\mathcal B(X)$. [L1, L3]

2.1 Applying [L2] to the family of open subsets of $X$ gives $\mathcal B(Y)=\mathcal B(X)|_Y$. [step 1.1, L2, L3] ∎
