## Statement

Work over $\mathbb Q(q)$ with a symmetrizable Cartan datum of type $A$ as in
[[def-cyclic-affine-type-a-cartan-datum]], put $q_i=q^{d_i}$ and let
$B$ be a unital $\mathbb Q(q)$-algebra equipped with elements
$E_i,F_i,K_h^{\pm1}$ ($i\in I$, $h\in P^\vee$) satisfying the toral and
toral-action relations and the mixed commutators
$E_iF_j-F_jE_i=\delta_{ij}(K_i-K_i^{-1})/(q_i-q_i^{-1})$ of
[[def-drinfeld-jimbo-quantized-enveloping-algebra]]; let $W$ be a $B$-module with a chosen integral label grading on which $K_h$ acts by $q^{\langle\mu,h\rangle}$ on $W_\mu$, and $E_i,F_i$ shift labels by $\pm\alpha_i$. Assume that $E_i$ and $F_i$ are locally nilpotent on $W$ for every $i$ (rank-one integrability). Then the negative quantum Serre sums

$$\sum_{r=0}^{1-a_{ij}}(-1)^r\binom{1-a_{ij}}{r}_iF_i^{1-a_{ij}-r}F_jF_i^r=0\qquad(i\ne j)$$

vanish as operators on $W$, and, by the Chevalley interchange
$E_i\leftrightarrow F_i$, $K_h\leftrightarrow K_{-h}$, so do the positive sums
$\sum_{r}(-1)^r\binom{1-a_{ij}}{r}_iE_i^{1-a_{ij}-r}E_jE_i^r$. In particular the
$e=2$ cubic sums with $a_{01}=a_{10}=-2$ hold. No Serre relation is assumed.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A symmetrizable Cartan datum whose symmetrizer is $D=\mathrm{diag}(1,\dots,1)$ with $q_i=q$, a $\mathbb Q(q)$-algebra $B$ with elements $E_i,F_i,K_h^{\pm1}$ satisfying the toral, toral-action and mixed relations, and the chosen weight module $W$ with both families locally nilpotent.

[F1] $K_iF_jK_i^{-1}=q^{-a_{ij}}F_j$ and $K_iE_jK_i^{-1}=q^{a_{ij}}E_j$; hence $F_j$ has weight $m:=-a_{ij}\ge0$ under conjugation by $K_i$, and $[E_i,F_j]=0$ for $i\ne j$ ([[def-drinfeld-jimbo-quantized-enveloping-algebra]], [[def-cyclic-affine-type-a-cartan-datum]]).

[F2] In the rank-one subalgebra generated by $E_i,F_i,K_i^{\pm1}$ the commutator formula $E_iF_i^{(b)}=F_i^{(b)}E_i+F_i^{(b-1)}(q_i^{b}-q_i^{-b})^{-1}\sum_{s=0}^{b-1}(q_i^{-2s}K_i-q_i^{2s}K_i^{-1})$ holds, together with the explicit string modules $L_i(N)$ of dimension $N+1$ on which $E_i,F_i$ are injective/surjective string maps ([[thm-quantized-sl-two-string-formulas]]).

[F3] Every finite-dimensional integrable $U_i$-module is a direct sum of the string modules $L_i(N)$; every weight vector has a unique finite admissible expansion $\sum_{n\ge0}F_i^{(n)}u_n$ in divided-power descendants of highest vectors, with $E_iu_n=0$ and $n\le\langle\mathrm{wt}(u_n),h_i\rangle$ for each nonzero term ([[thm-unique-kashiwara-i-string-decomposition]]).

[F4] $(x+y)^N=\sum_{r=0}^Nq^{r(N-r)}\binom{N}{r}_i x^ry^{N-r}$ whenever $yx=q^2xy$, and the Gaussian binomials satisfy the Pascal recurrences $\binom{n}{r}_i=q^{-r}\binom{n-1}{r}_i+q^{n-r}\binom{n-1}{r-1}_i$ ([[lem-q-binomial-expansion-for-q-commuting-elements]], [[lem-quantum-pascal-recurrence-and-gaussian-integrality]]).

[F5] The assignment $E_i\mapsto F_i$, $F_i\mapsto E_i$, $K_h\mapsto K_{-h}$ preserves the toral, toral-action and mixed relations and interchanges the two Serre families, so a proof of one family gives the other ([[def-drinfeld-jimbo-quantized-enveloping-algebra]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** compute the iterated adjoint action of $F_i$ on $F_j$ by the Gaussian binomial formula, show it vanishes beyond the rank-one string bound, and read the Serre sum off the surviving coefficient.

1.1 The adjoint action of $F_i$ on operators is $\operatorname{ad}(T)=F_iT-K_iTK_i^{-1}F_i$, and on $T=F_j$ one has $\operatorname{ad}^n(F_j)=\sum_{r=0}^{n}(-1)^rq^{r(m-n+1)}\binom{n}{r}_iF_i^{n-r}F_jF_i^r$ for every $n\ge0$, where $m=-a_{ij}$ is the $K_i$-weight of $F_j$ from [F1]. The case $n=0$ is the definition; the induction step multiplies the formula for $n$ on the left by $F_i$ and conjugates the rightmost factor, using $K_iF_jK_i^{-1}=q^mF_j$ and $K_iF_i^rK_i^{-1}=q^{-2r}F_i^r$ to move the toral elements, and the coefficient identity $\binom{n+1}{r}_i=q^{r}\binom{n}{r}_i+q^{r-n-1}\binom{n}{r-1}_i$ of [F4] (with $q$ replaced by $q_i=q$); the powers of $q$ combine to $q^{r(m-n)}$. [F1, F4, given, algebra]

2.1 The finite Hom-space string bound. Fix $w\in W$ and let $U$ be its finite-dimensional rank-one submodule: for a homogeneous vector take the span of $F_i^aE_i^bw$, with finitely many $b$ by local nilpotence of $E_i$ and finitely many $a$ for each $b$ by local nilpotence of $F_i$; the rank-one commutator in [F2] makes this span stable. For a general vector sum the finitely many such spaces of its label components. Choose a finite basis of $U$ and let $U'$ be the sum of the finite rank-one submodules generated by its $F_j$-images. Then $F_j|_U$ belongs to the finite type-one rank-one module $\operatorname{Hom}(U,U')$, with operators $\mathcal K(T)=K_i'TK_i^{-1}$, $\mathcal E(T)=(E_i'T-TE_i)K_i$ and $\mathcal F(T)=F_i'T-\mathcal K(T)F_i$. Substitution of the rank-one relations verifies $\mathcal K\mathcal E\mathcal K^{-1}=q^2\mathcal E$, $\mathcal K\mathcal F\mathcal K^{-1}=q^{-2}\mathcal F$ and $[\mathcal E,\mathcal F]=(\mathcal K-\mathcal K^{-1})/(q-q^{-1})$. Its integral weights lie in a finite set, so both weight-shifting operators are nilpotent. For $T=F_j|_U$, [F1] gives $\mathcal K(T)=q^mT$ and $\mathcal E(T)=0$. The highest-vector endpoint calculation in [F2] (or the rank-one proof of [F3], which uses no Serre relations) therefore gives $\mathcal F^{m+1}T=0$. Its expansion is exactly step 1.1 restricted to $U$, so $\operatorname{ad}^{m+1}(F_j)w=0$. No preservation of $U$ by $F_j$ is assumed. [F1, F2, F3, step 1.1, given, algebra]

3.1 The negative Serre sum vanishes. Put $n:=m+1=1-a_{ij}$. At $n=m+1$ the exponent in the expansion of step 1.1 is $r(m-n+1)=0$, so step 2.1 gives $0=\operatorname{ad}^{n}(F_j)=\sum_{r=0}^{n}(-1)^r\binom{n}{r}_iF_i^{n-r}F_jF_i^r$; as an operator identity on each $U$ containing $w$, hence on every vector $w\in W$ this is exactly the negative Serre sum. [step 1.1, step 2.1, given, algebra]

4.1 The positive Serre sum vanishes. The Chevalley interchange of [F5] preserves all toral, toral-action and mixed relations used above and exchanges the roles of the $E$'s and $F$'s, so applying the argument of steps 1.1--3.1 to the image operators gives $\sum_{r=0}^{1-a_{ij}}(-1)^r\binom{1-a_{ij}}{r}_iE_i^{1-a_{ij}-r}E_jE_i^r=0$ on every weight vector, hence as operators. [step 3.1, F5, given, algebra]

5.1 Conclusion. Under only the toral, toral-action, mixed-commutator and rank-one-integrability hypotheses, both quantum Serre sums of order $1-a_{ij}$ vanish, for every pair $i\ne j$ of the cyclic affine type $A$ datum, the cubic case included; no Serre relation was assumed. [step 3.1, step 4.1] ∎

## Remarks

- The two finite rank-one spaces and their Hom-space in step 2.1 are produced by the rank-one commutator alone; no mixed Serre relation is used before the conclusion, so the implication is not circular.

- Only the rank-one argument of [[thm-unique-kashiwara-i-string-decomposition]] is used in step 2.1; its finite reduction and highest-vector endpoint calculation require no Serre relations.
