## Definition

Let $W$ be a complete filtered shifted L∞-algebra over a characteristic-zero field $k$. Put $P=F^1W$ with its induced filtration. This is closed and complete in each degree: it is the kernel of $W\to W/F^1W$, and compatible cosets in $P/F^pW$ have their unique $W$-limit in that kernel. All $q_n$ preserve $P$.

The **completed polynomial coefficients** and **completed polynomial differential forms** mean

$$P[t]^{\wedge}=\varprojlim_p (P/F^pW)\otimes_k k[t],\qquad P\widehat\otimes\Omega=\varprojlim_p (P/F^pW)\otimes_k k[t,dt],\quad |t|=0,\quad |dt|=1.$$

These are compatible polynomials in each quotient; their degrees need not have a common bound. Evaluation at $0,1$, differentiation, and integration from $0$ to $t$ are defined in every quotient by polynomial formulas and commute with reduction; integration uses $\int_0^t u^jdu=t^{j+1}/(j+1)$. Operations on these spaces are extended continuously, with each coefficient of a fixed quotient a finite sum.

Write $C(w)=\sum_{n\ge1}q_n(w^n)/n!$ and $q_1^w(a)=\sum_{n\ge0}q_{n+1}(w^n,a)/n!$. An **elementary gauge path** is a pair $w\in P^0[t]^{\wedge}$, $a\in P^{-1}[t]^{\wedge}$ such that $C(w)=0$ and $w'=q_1^w(a)$. Its endpoints are $w(0),w(1)$. **Gauge equivalence** is the equivalence relation generated by endpoints of these paths. A constant path has $a=0$; reversal is $(w(1-t),-a(1-t))$; finite chains give transitivity. Polynomial concatenation is not required.

For the polynomial-form interpretation, forms are placed on the right. For homogeneous $x_i\in P$, $\alpha_i\in\Omega$, the extension of $q_n$ has scalar sign $(-1)^{\sum_{i<j}|\alpha_i||x_j|}$; its unary part also contains $(-1)^{|x|}x\otimes d\alpha$. The degree-zero form $z=w-a\,dt$ then satisfies $C_{\Omega}(z)=C(w)+(w'-q_1^w(a))dt$. Thus these paths are precisely Maurer–Cartan elements over the completed polynomial interval. This fixes a gauge construction using the full operations, rather than choosing an arbitrary equivalence relation on a solution set.
