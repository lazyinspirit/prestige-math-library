## Statement

Let M=Γ\ℍ be a compact arithmetic surface associated with a quaternion algebra over ℚ split over ℝ. Every quantum limit arising from joint Laplace and Hecke eigenfunctions on M is normalized Liouville probability measure on T¹M.
