## Example

For $n\ge1$, $W(B_n)$ is the full group of signed permutations of the
coordinates, of order $2^nn!$. For $n\ge2$, $W(D_n)$ is the subgroup of
signed permutations with an even number of sign changes, of order
$2^{n-1}n!$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The coordinate model $B_n=\{\pm\varepsilon_i\}\cup\{\pm\varepsilon_i\pm\varepsilon_j\}$ in $\mathbb R^n$ for $n\ge1$, and the coordinate model $D_n=\{\pm\varepsilon_i\pm\varepsilon_j\}$ in $\mathbb R^n$ for $n\ge2$.

[L1] $s_{\varepsilon_i}$ negates the $i$-th coordinate; $s_{\varepsilon_i-\varepsilon_j}$ swaps coordinates $i,j$; $s_{\varepsilon_i+\varepsilon_j}$ swaps coordinates $i,j$ and negates both ([[ex-classical-root-systems-in-euclidean-coordinates]]).

[L2] The Weyl group is generated by the root reflections ([[def-weyl-group-of-a-root-system]]).

## Verification

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 For $B_n$ the reflections include all coordinate negations (from the roots $\pm\varepsilon_i$) and all transpositions (from $\pm\varepsilon_i\mp\varepsilon_j$), so $W(B_n)$ contains the full signed permutation group $(\mathbb Z/2)^{n}\rtimes S_n$; conversely every root reflection is a signed permutation, so $W(B_n)$ is contained in that group. Hence $W(B_n)\cong(\mathbb Z/2)^{n}\rtimes S_n$ has order $2^{n}n!$. [L1, L2, algebra]

2.1 For $D_n$ the available reflections are $s_{\varepsilon_i-\varepsilon_j}$ (transposition) and $s_{\varepsilon_i+\varepsilon_j}$ (transposition combined with two sign changes); both operations change the number of sign changes by an even number, and they generate all even sign-change signed permutations: the transpositions generate $S_n$, and products of an even number of the second type realise every even sign pattern. Hence $W(D_n)$ is the even subgroup, of order $2^{n-1}n!$; every root reflection of $D_n$ is such a signed permutation, so no further elements occur. [L1, L2, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
