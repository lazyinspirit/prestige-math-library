## Statement

Let $n\ge1$ and let $S$ be an abelian group of Hermitian $n$-qubit Pauli operators with
$-I\notin S$, generated by $r$ independent checks. Then

$$P_S:=|S|^{-1}\sum_{g\in S}g$$

is the orthogonal projector onto the stabilizer code space and has rank
$2^{n-r}$. In particular, the code space is nonzero, including when $r=n$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** $n\ge1$ and a stabilizer code space $\mathcal C(S)$ with $r$ independent
generators on the ordered $n$-qubit register.

[F1] [[def-qc-stabilizer-code-space]]: $\mathcal C(S)$ is the common $+1$
eigenspace of every element of $S$.

[F2] [[lem-qc-stabilizer-average-projector]]: the finite average
$|S|^{-1}\sum_{g\in S}g$ is self-adjoint and idempotent with image
$\mathcal C(S)$.

[F3] [[thm-qc-stabilizer-projector-rank-and-code-dimension]]: for the same
stabilizer subgroup of rank $r$, its fixed-space projector has trace and
image dimension $2^{n-r}$.

[F4] [[thm-self-adjoint-idempotents-are-exactly-orthogonal-projections]]:
a self-adjoint idempotent on a finite-dimensional inner-product space is
the orthogonal projection onto its image.

[F5] [[lem-qc-stabilizer-subgroup-label-space-and-order]]: the span of the
binary labels of $r$ independent stabilizer checks has dimension $r$, so
the rank convention agrees with the generator count.

## Proof

1.1 The subgroup in the Statement meets the definition in [F1]. By [F2], $P_S$ is self-adjoint and idempotent with image equal to the common $+1$ eigenspace. [F1, F2, given]

2.1 The rank-$r$ hypothesis is the same rank convention used in [F3]: independent Hermitian generators give a binary label space of dimension $r$ by [F5]. Therefore [F3] gives $\dim\mathcal C(S)=\operatorname{Tr}(P_S)=2^{n-r}$. [F3, F5, step 1.1]

3.1 By [F4] and step 1.1, $P_S$ is the orthogonal projector onto $\mathcal C(S)$. Since $2^{n-r}\ge1$, this space is nonzero; the endpoint $r=n$ has rank one and $r=0$ gives the full $2^n$-dimensional register. [F4, step 2.1, algebra] ∎
