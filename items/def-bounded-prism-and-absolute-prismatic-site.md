## Definition

Fix a prime $p$. A **delta ring** is a commutative $\mathbb Z_{(p)}$-algebra $A$ with a map $\delta:A\to A$ satisfying $\delta(0)=\delta(1)=0$ and
$$
\delta(x+y)=\delta(x)+\delta(y)+\frac{x^p+y^p-(x+y)^p}{p},\qquad \delta(xy)=x^p\delta(y)+y^p\delta(x)+p\delta(x)\delta(y).
$$
The quotient by $p$ in the addition formula denotes its integral polynomial, whose coefficients are integers.
The associated Frobenius lift is the ring homomorphism $\phi_A(x)=x^p+p\delta(x)$. An ideal $I\subseteq A$ defines a **Cartier divisor** if it is invertible and locally generated by a non-zero-divisor. A pair $(A,I)$ is a **prism** if $A$ is a delta ring, $I$ defines a Cartier divisor, $A$ is derived $(p,I)$-complete, and $p\in I+\phi_A(I)A$. When $I=(d)$ locally, derived $(p,I)$-completeness means that the natural map from $A$ to the derived inverse limit of the Koszul complexes $\operatorname{Kos}(A;p^n,d^n)$ is a quasi-isomorphism; this condition is independent of the local generator $d$. A prism is **bounded** if for some integer $N\geq 0$ the $p$-power torsion of $A/I$ is annihilated by $p^N$.

A **$p$-adic formal scheme** is a formal scheme locally isomorphic to $\operatorname{Spf}(R)$ for a ring $R$ complete and separated for the $p$-adic topology. For such a formal scheme $Y$, the **absolute prismatic site** $Y_\Delta$ has objects bounded prisms $(A,I)$ with a map of formal schemes $\operatorname{Spf}(A/I)\to Y$. A morphism in $Y_\Delta$ from $(A,I)$ to $(B,J)$ is a delta-ring map $B\to A$ carrying $J$ into $I$ and compatible with the maps to $Y$. The topology is generated by morphisms opposite to maps of prisms whose ring maps are $(p,I)$-completely faithfully flat: tensoring with any module annihilated by a power of $(p,I)$ gives a complex concentrated in degree zero, and reduction modulo $(p,I)$ is faithfully flat. The structure sheaf $\mathcal O_\Delta$ sends $(A,I)$ to $A$, the Cartier ideal sheaf $\mathcal I_\Delta$ sends it to $I$, and the maps $\phi_A$ define Frobenius on $\mathcal O_\Delta$.
