## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]). Let $A$ be a commutative
ring, let $f:X\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ be a proper morphism of finite
presentation ([[def-proper-morphism]],
[[def-locally-finite-presentation-morphism]]), and let $\mathcal F$ be a
finitely presented $\mathcal O_X$-module
([[def-finite-type-finite-presentation-module-sheaf]]) that is flat over $A$
([[def-flat-and-faithfully-flat-modules-and-ring-maps]]): for every $x\in X$
the stalk $\mathcal F_x$ is a flat $A$-module through the ring map
$A\to\mathcal O_{X,x}$. Then there exist a finitely generated
$\mathbb Z$-subalgebra $A_i\subseteq A$, a proper morphism of finite
presentation
$$f_i:X_i\longrightarrow\operatorname{Spec}A_i,$$
and a finitely presented $\mathcal O_{X_i}$-module $\mathcal F_i$ that is flat
over $A_i$, together with identifications
$$X\cong X_i\times_{\operatorname{Spec}A_i}\operatorname{Spec}A,\qquad \mathcal F\cong \mathcal F_i\otimes_{\mathcal O_{X_i}}\mathcal O_X$$
over $A$ ([[def-scheme-over-base]]). The empty source and the zero module are
included, and $A$ itself may be finitely generated over $\mathbb Z$, in which
case the descent is trivial.

## Facts & Assumptions
**Given:** The Axiom of Choice, a commutative ring $A$, a proper finitely presented morphism $f:X\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ and a finitely presented $\mathcal O_X$-module $\mathcal F$ flat over $A$.

[F1] Proper means separated, of finite type and universally closed; locally of
finite presentation is the affine-local finite-presentation condition on rings,
and a morphism of finite presentation is locally of finite presentation,
quasi-compact and quasi-separated (the given proper morphism is separated);
a module sheaf is finitely presented when it is of finite type
and every point has an affine neighbourhood on which it is presented by a
finite matrix, and finite presentation is local and stable under base change.
([[def-proper-morphism]], [[def-locally-finite-presentation-morphism]],
[[def-quasi-compact-and-quasi-separated-morphism]],
[[def-finite-type-finite-presentation-module-sheaf]])

[F2] Flatness of modules and ring maps is the exactness condition of the cited
definition; a module is flat over a ring if and only if all its localisations
at primes, equivalently at maximal ideals, are flat, and flatness of a stalk
$\mathcal F_x$ over $A$ is the condition tested in the affine charts of $X$.
([[def-flat-and-faithfully-flat-modules-and-ring-maps]],
[[thm-flatness-is-local]])

[F3] Every commutative ring $A$ is the filtered union of its finitely generated
$\mathbb Z$-subalgebras: the subalgebra generated by a finite subset $T$ is
$\mathbb Z[t_1,\dots,t_r]$ realized as a quotient of a polynomial ring, the
system is directed by inclusion, and the union over all finite $T$ is $A$. A
finitely generated $\mathbb Z$-algebra is a finitely generated algebra over
the Noetherian ring $\mathbb Z$ and hence is Noetherian.
([[def-finite-type-and-module-finite-algebras]],
[[cor-finite-type-algebra-over-noetherian-ring-is-noetherian]],
[[def-noetherian-ring-and-module]]). The Axiom of Choice states that every
family of nonempty sets has a choice function ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]).

[F4] Four locally proved finite-stage results supply the limit inputs.
(D1) [[lem-filtered-colimit-fp-scheme-stage]] descends finite-presentation
schemes, their morphisms and eventual equalities from $A=\varinjlim A_i$;
this is the 01ZM scope. (D2) [[lem-filtered-colimit-fp-sheaf-stage]] descends
finitely presented quasi-coherent modules on a fixed finite-presentation
stage scheme, including their finite matrix data and overlap gluing; this is
the 01ZR scope. (D3) [[lem-filtered-colimit-flat-fp-sheaf-stage]] proves
eventual relative flatness of such a module over directed finite-type
$\mathbb Z$-algebra stages when its limit pullback is flat, by the local
Noetherian flatness and principal-open/unit-ideal argument; this is the
05LY/02JO(3) scope. (D4) [[lem-filtered-colimit-proper-fp-stage]] proves
eventual properness of a finite-presentation stage morphism whose limit is
proper, by finite closed-immersion descent and a Noetherian Chow cover; this
is the 081F scope, including eventual separatedness and universal
closedness. Their proofs allow arbitrary, nonflat transition maps. The
Stacks tags are source checks of their scope, not premises here.



## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct: write $A$ as the filtered union of its finitely generated $\mathbb Z$-subalgebras and apply the four proved finite-stage carriers to descend the scheme, sheaf, properness, and flatness to a common stage.

1.1 Filtered system. Let $\mathcal T$ be the set of finite subsets $T\subseteq A$ and for $T\in\mathcal T$ let $A_T\subseteq A$ be the $\mathbb Z$-subalgebra generated by $T$, so that $T\subseteq T'$ implies $A_T\subseteq A_{T'}$, the system is filtered, $\bigcup_TA_T=A$, and each $A_T$ is a finitely generated algebra over $\mathbb Z$, hence Noetherian by [F3]. [F3]

1.2 Descent of the scheme and morphism (D1). The given $f$ is of finite presentation, and the bases $\operatorname{Spec}A_T$ form an inverse system of affine quasi-compact and quasi-separated schemes with affine transition maps and limit $\operatorname{Spec}A$. Apply the scheme-stage result (D1) of [F4], including its morphism and equality clauses, to obtain $T\in\mathcal T$ and a morphism $f_T:X_T\to\operatorname{Spec}A_T$ of finite presentation with an isomorphism $X\cong X_T\times_{\operatorname{Spec}A_T}\operatorname{Spec}A$ compatible with $f$. Its local proof includes the gluing of affine charts. [F1, F3, F4]

2.1 Descent of the sheaf (D2). The schemes $X_T$ of step 1.2 are quasi-compact and quasi-separated and their transition maps are affine. Apply the sheaf-stage result (D2) of [F4] to the finitely presented quasi-coherent module $\mathcal F$ on their limit $X$: after enlarging $T$ it descends to a finitely presented $\mathcal O_{X_T}$-module $\mathcal F_T$ with $\mathcal F\cong\mathcal F_T\otimes_{\mathcal O_{X_T}}\mathcal O_X$. On affine charts, its presentation matrices have coefficients in the chart coordinate rings; (D2) also descends their compatibility on overlaps. [F1, F4, step 1.2]

2.2 Properness descends (D4). The stage morphism $f_T$ is of finite presentation, hence locally of finite type. Its pullback $f$ is proper. Apply the proper-stage result (D4) of [F4] to obtain a later stage at which $f_T$ is proper. That carrier proves eventual separatedness by descending the diagonal and universal closedness through a proper surjective Chow cover. Finite presentation persists under base change. [F1, F4, step 1.2]

3.1 Flatness descends (D3). The stage morphism $X_T\to\operatorname{Spec}A_T$ and $\mathcal F_T$ are of finite presentation by steps 1.2 and 2.1, while their pullbacks to $A$ give the stipulated flat sheaf. Apply the flat-sheaf-stage result (D3) of [F4] to this relative pair; it gives a later stage at which the pulled-back $\mathcal F_T$ is flat over its base. Its proof reduces to finitely many affine charts and one common later stage. [F2, F4, step 1.2, step 2.1]

4.1 Conclusion. Choose $T$ large enough for steps 1.2, 2.1, 2.2, and 3.1 simultaneously (the system is filtered, so the finitely many enlargements can be combined), put $A_i=A_T$, $X_i=X_T$, $f_i=f_T$ and $\mathcal F_i=\mathcal F_T$. Then $A_i$ is a finitely generated $\mathbb Z$-subalgebra of $A$, $f_i$ is proper of finite presentation, $\mathcal F_i$ is finitely presented and flat over $A_i$, and the pullbacks to $A$ recover $f$ and $\mathcal F$ up to the stated isomorphisms. [step 1.2, step 2.1, step 2.2, step 3.1, F4]

5.1 Boundaries and choice accounting. If $A$ is finitely generated over $\mathbb Z$, then $A=A_T$ for a finite $T$ generating it and the descent is trivial (the identity stage, with the given $f$ and $\mathcal F$). If $X=\varnothing$, the empty $X_T$ over a stage works; if $\mathcal F=0$, the zero module is finitely presented and flat over every ring. The finite enlargements combine because the index system is filtered. The stated Axiom of Choice is inherited from the four proved finite-stage carriers [F4]; combining these finitely many stages uses no further choice. [F1, F2, F3, F4, step 4.1] ∎
