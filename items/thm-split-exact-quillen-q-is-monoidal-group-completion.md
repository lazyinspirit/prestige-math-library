## Statement

For every associative unital ring R, construct the small Quillen category Q_R of finite projective left modules by split-epi/split-mono spans, and the simultaneous-translation localization L_R=S_R^{-1}S_R of their isomorphism groupoid. The actual localized split-extension projection gives natural based weak equivalences BL_R→hofib_0(B(S_R^{-1}E_R)→BQ_R)←ΩBQ_R, on every component. Precomposing the first map by coordinate-switch matches [P] with the positive accepted loop w_P*z_P^{-1}. These maps and canonical split tensor comparisons use no choice principle, regularity or commutativity hypothesis.
