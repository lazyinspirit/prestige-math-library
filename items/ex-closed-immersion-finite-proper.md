## Example

Let $A$ be a commutative ring and let $I\subseteq A$ be an ideal. Write
$\pi:A\to A/I$ for the quotient map and let
$i:\operatorname{Spec}(A/I)\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ be the morphism it
induces. Assume the Axiom of Choice. Then $i$ is finite and proper. The
assertion includes the ideal $I=A$, whose source is empty, and the ideal
$I=0$, where $i$ is the identity; it also includes the zero ring $A=0$, where
source and target are both empty. No Noetherian, field, reducedness or
nonemptiness hypothesis is imposed, and nilpotents in $A/I$ are retained.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The Axiom of Choice, a commutative ring $A$ (possibly the zero ring), an ideal $I\subseteq A$, the quotient map $\pi:A\to A/I$ and the morphism $i:\operatorname{Spec}(A/I)\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ it induces.

[F1] Assume AC. For a closed immersion $i:Z\to Y$ and every affine open $U=\operatorname{Spec}A'\subseteq Y$ there is a unique ideal $I'\subseteq A'$ with $i^{-1}(U)\cong\operatorname{Spec}(A'/I')$ over $U$; conversely every quotient map $A'\to A'/I'$ induces a closed immersion, and the empty subscheme of $\operatorname{Spec}A'$ corresponds to $I'=A'$. ([[lem-closed-immersion-affine-quotient-and-base-change]])

[F2] Assume AC. Every closed immersion of schemes is finite, hence proper; the empty closed immersion is included. ([[lem-closed-immersion-proper]])

[F3] A morphism $f:X\to S$ is **finite** when for every affine open $U=\operatorname{Spec}A'\subseteq S$ the inverse image is affine, $f^{-1}(U)=\operatorname{Spec}B$, and $B$ is module-finite over $A'$; the zero ring is allowed as a coefficient ring. ([[def-finite-morphism-schemes]])

[F4] A module is **cyclic** when it is generated by one element and **finitely generated** when it is generated by a finite subset; the zero module is generated by the empty family. ([[def-generated-cyclic-finitely-generated-and-free-modules]])

[F5] An $R$-algebra $B$ is module-finite over $R$ when $B$ is finitely generated as an $R$-module. ([[def-finite-type-and-module-finite-algebras]])

[F6] The Axiom of Choice states that every family of nonempty sets has a choice function. ([[def-axiom-of-choice]])

**AC use:** F6 is assumed because both structural suppliers F1 and F2 are AC-qualified; the verification below uses no further selection, and the cyclic-module computation of step 1.2 is choice-free.

## Verification

**Proof technique:** direct: the quotient map induces a closed immersion; on each affine open of the target the quotient lemma exhibits the inverse image as the spectrum of a quotient ring, whose coordinate algebra is cyclic hence module-finite; the morphism is therefore finite, and a closed immersion is proper.

1.1 By the converse clause of [F1] applied to the quotient map $\pi:A\to A/I$, the induced morphism $i$ is a closed immersion. Now fix an affine open $U=\operatorname{Spec}A'\subseteq\operatorname{Spec}A$. By the first clause of [F1] applied to the closed immersion $i$ and this $U$, there is a unique ideal $I'\subseteq A'$ with $i^{-1}(U)\cong\operatorname{Spec}(A'/I')$ over $U$; in particular the inverse image $i^{-1}(U)$ is affine. [F1]

1.2 The quotient ring $A'/I'$ is a cyclic $A'$-module: the class $1+I'$ generates it, because every $a+I'=a\cdot(1+I')$ lies in the submodule generated by $1+I'$, and that submodule is contained in $A'/I'$. If $I'=A'$ then $A'/I'$ is the zero module, which is generated by the empty family. Hence $A'/I'$ is finitely generated, equivalently module-finite, over $A'$ by [F4] and [F5]. [F4, F5]

2.1 Steps 1.1 and 1.2 verify the condition of [F3] on the affine open $U$: the inverse image $i^{-1}(U)=\operatorname{Spec}(A'/I')$ is affine, and its coordinate algebra is module-finite over the coordinate algebra $A'$ of $U$. Since $U$ was an arbitrary affine open of $\operatorname{Spec}A$, the morphism $i$ is finite. [F3, step 1.1, step 1.2]

3.1 By step 1.1 the morphism $i$ is a closed immersion, so the AC-qualified [F2] shows that $i$ is proper. Combining with step 2.1, the morphism $\operatorname{Spec}(A/I)\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ is both finite and proper. [F2, step 1.1, step 2.1]

4.1 Extreme cases. If $I=A$ then $A/I=0$ and the source is $\operatorname{Spec}0=\varnothing$, the empty closed immersion, to which [F1] and [F2] apply with the empty case included; if $I=0$ then $A/I\cong A$ canonically and $i$ is the identity morphism, a closed immersion by [F1] and proper by [F2]; if $A=0$ then source and target are both empty and $i$ is again the identity of the empty scheme, covered by the same argument. The Axiom of Choice [F6] is assumed and is used only through the AC-qualified suppliers [F1] and [F2]. No Noetherian, field, reducedness or nonemptiness hypothesis is imposed. [F1, F2, F6, step 2.1, step 3.1] ∎
