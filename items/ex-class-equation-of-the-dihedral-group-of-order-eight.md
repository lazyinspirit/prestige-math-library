## Example

Let $D\le S_4$ be generated by the square rotation $r=(1\,2\,3\,4)$ and the
reflection $s=(1\,3)$. Its conjugacy classes are

$$\{e\},\quad\{r^2\},\quad\{r,r^3\},\quad\{s,r^2s\},\quad\{rs,r^3s\}.$$

Thus $Z(D)=\{e,r^2\}$ and the class equation is
$8=2+2+2+2$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The permutations $r=(1\,2\,3\,4)$ and $s=(1\,3)$ in $S_4$.

[L1] The class equation sums central singleton classes and noncentral conjugacy-class sizes ([[thm-class-equation]]).

[L2] Conjugacy-class cardinality is a centralizer index ([[thm-conjugacy-class-cardinality]]).

[L3] The symmetric group is the group of all permutations under composition ([[def-symmetric-group]], [[lem-symmetric-group-is-a-group]]).

[L4] The subgroup axioms are those of [[def-subgroup]].

[L5] Integer powers and their laws are given by [[def-group-power]] and [[lem-group-power-laws]].

[L6] Element order is the least positive exponent giving the identity ([[def-order-in-a-group]]).

## Verification

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Pointwise calculation gives $r^4=e$, $s^2=e$, and $sr=r^{-1}s$. [L3, L5, L6, algebra]

2.1 The relations reduce every word in $r,s$ to one of $e,r,r^2,r^3,s,rs,r^2s,r^3s$ and show this set is closed under products and inverses. These eight permutations are distinct, so [L3] and [L4] make them a subgroup $D$ of order $8$; also $sr\ne rs$, so $D$ is nonabelian. [step 1.1, L3, L4, L5, L6, algebra]

3.1 Using $sr=r^{-1}s$, conjugation by $r$ and $s$ gives the five displayed conjugacy classes: $e$ and $r^2$ are central, $r$ is conjugate to $r^3$, and the reflections split into the two displayed pairs. [step 1.1, step 2.1, L2, algebra]

4.1 Their sizes give $8=1+1+2+2+2=2+2+2+2$, and [L1] identifies the two singleton classes with the center $\{e,r^2\}$. [step 3.1, L1, L2, algebra] ∎
