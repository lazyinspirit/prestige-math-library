## Definition

Let $X$ be a real Banach space, let $R:X\to(-\infty,+\infty]$ be proper and convex, choose $x^\dagger\in\operatorname{dom}R$, and choose $\xi^\dagger\in\partial R(x^\dagger)$. For $x\in\operatorname{dom}R$, the **Bregman distance** generated by $\xi^\dagger$ from the reference point $x^\dagger$ to $x$ is

$$D_R^{\xi^\dagger}(x,x^\dagger):=R(x)-R(x^\dagger)-\langle\xi^\dagger,x-x^\dagger\rangle_{X^*,X}.$$

The subgradient inequality makes this quantity nonnegative, and $D_R^{\xi^\dagger}(x^\dagger,x^\dagger)=0$. The definition fixes the orientation: the first argument is the comparison point and the second is the reference point carrying the chosen subgradient.
