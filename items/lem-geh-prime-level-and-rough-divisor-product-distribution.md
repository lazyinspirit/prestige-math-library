## Statement

Assume the family-uniform GEH$(\theta)$ of [[def-generalized-elliott-halberstam-hypothesis]], where $0<\theta<1$. Then the primes have every full von Mangoldt endpoint level $0<\lambda<\theta$ in [[def-prime-level-of-distribution]].

Fix $0<\theta_0<\theta$, $0<\delta<1/4$, specified real $F,G\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R)$ with $S(F),S(G)<1$, and fixed real $c_-,c_+$, where $S(F)=\sup(\{t\ge0:F(t)\ne0\}\cup\{0\})$. Put
$$f_X(n)=1_{X+c_-<n\le2X+c_+}\,1_{p(n)>X^\delta}\lambda_F(n)\lambda_G(n),\qquad\lambda_F(n)=\sum_{d\mid n}\mu(d)F(\log d/\log X).$$
For every fixed $A>0$ and integer $s\ge0$,
$$\sum_{q\le X^{\theta_0}}\mu(q)^2\tau(q)^s\max_{(a,q)=1}|\Delta(f_X;a\bmod q)|\ll_{A,s,\delta,F,G,\theta,\theta_0,c_-,c_+}X(\log X)^{-A}.$$
The discrepancy is exactly that of the given GEH Definition. The proof verifies the all-saving SW input for every prime/Lambda interval family, the complete coefficient-growth and power-range bounds, and all fixed cutoff constants; no assertion that GEH itself is proved is made.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** All fixed parameters, test functions and hypotheses of the Statement. The symbols tau, omega and Omega denote respectively the positive-divisor count, the distinct-prime-factor count and the prime-factor count with multiplicity. Proof formulas use the same elementary notation in text; every displayed sum is finite except the explicitly convergent Fourier or numerical series.

[F3] [[thm-first-mertens-theorem-for-primes]] supplies the partial-summation prime sums.

[F4] [[thm-von-mangoldt-divisor-sum-identity]], [[cor-classical-mobius-inversion]], [[def-number-theoretic-mobius-function]] and [[def-von-mangoldt-function]] give log=Lambda*1, mu*1=delta_1, exact finite convolution identities and full prime-power weights.
