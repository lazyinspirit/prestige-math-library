## Statement

Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be a GIVEN valued algebraic closure, $C$ its ordinary completion, and a compatible primitive cyclotomic system be GIVEN. Suppose that every finite Galois $L/\mathbb Q_p$ inside $F$ receives a surjection $G_{\mathbb Q_p}\to\operatorname{Gal}(L/\mathbb Q_p)$. Let $E\subset F$ be finite and $\lambda:G_E\to\mathbb Q_p$ a continuous homomorphism. If there is $x\in C$ satisfying $g(x)-x=\lambda(g)$ for every $g\in G_E$, then $\lambda(I_E)=0$. This asserts neither the general inertia-only coboundary statement nor the lifting hypothesis itself.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The valued closure, cyclotomic system, stated finite Galois lifting hypothesis, finite field, homomorphism and coboundary.

[F1] The stated finite lifting hypothesis extends any automorphism of a finite Galois extension over a finite intermediate field, after taking one finite normal closure over $\mathbb Q_p$. [[thm-fundamental-theorem-of-finite-galois-theory]]

[F3] The choice-free finite Ax–Sen estimate bounds approximation loss by $p/(p-1)^2$; finite embeddings are isometric. [[lem-choice-free-finite-ax-sen-approximation]]

[F4] For a totally ramified cyclic extension of degree $p^n$, the consecutive lower displacement breaks satisfy $c_0\ge1$ and $p^j\mid(c_j-c_{j-1})>0$. [[lem-sen-congruence-for-cyclic-p-power-local-extensions]]

[F5] For a totally ramified finite extension, any uniformizer integrally generates, its minimal polynomial derivative generates the different, and the different is inverse to the trace-dual ideal. [[lem-choice-free-finite-padic-different-and-base-change-calculus]]

## Proof

1.1 Suppose $\lambda(I_E)\ne0$. Continuity at zero gives a finite Galois subextension $M/E$ with $\lambda(G_M)\subseteq\mathbb Z_p$. By the GIVEN lifting hypothesis and [F1], $G_E/G_M$ is finite, so $\lambda(G_E)$ has valuations bounded below: one finite set of coset representatives together with $\lambda(G_M)\subseteq\mathbb Z_p$ gives a lower bound. Pick $\tau\in I_E$ whose nonzero $\lambda$-value has least valuation, and scale $\lambda,x$ so that $\lambda(\tau)=1$. Then $\lambda(I_E)\subseteq\mathbb Z_p$ and $\lambda(G_E)\subseteq p^{-h}\mathbb Z_p$ for some $h\ge0$. The kernel of $\lambda\bmod\mathbb Z_p$ is open and has finite index; [F1] identifies it with $G_{E'}$ for a finite Galois $E'/E$. It contains $\tau$. Replace $E$ by $E'$; then $\lambda(G_E)\subseteq\mathbb Z_p$, and $\tau\in I_E$ still satisfies $\lambda(\tau)=1$. For each $n$, the kernel of $\lambda\bmod p^n$ likewise gives a finite Galois extension $L_n/E$. The powers of $\tau$ make its Galois group cyclic of order $p^n$, generated by the restriction of $\tau$. Every element therefore acts trivially on the residue field. Finite Galois residue reduction is surjective: lift a primitive residue element by finite Hensel iteration, and use finite Galois transitivity on its conjugates. Thus $L_n/E$ is totally ramified. This construction uses only a finite lifting at each fixed stage; no coherent inverse-limit choice or compactness is invoked. [F1, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 Fix $s>p/(p-1)^2$. For any $n$, choose $a\in F$ with $v(a-x)>n+s$. Every $L_n$-conjugate of $a$ is realized by a finite automorphism lifted to some $g\in G_E$ by [F1]; such $g$ fixes $L_n$, so $\lambda(g)\in p^n\mathbb Z_p$. Isometry and the coboundary identity give $v(g(a)-a)\ge n$. By [F3], there is $y_n\in L_n$ with $v(y_n-x)>n-s$; if $a\in L_n$, take $y_n=a$. Consequently $\epsilon_n=\tau(y_n)-y_n-1\in L_n$ satisfies $v(\epsilon_n)>n-s$. [F1, F3, step 1.1, given, construct, algebra]

2.2 Let $e=v_E(p)$ and fix a uniformizer $\pi_n$ of $L_n$. For $0\le j<n$, put $c_j=v_{L_n}(\tau^{p^j}\pi_n/\pi_n-1)$. By [F4], $c_j\ge(p^{j+1}-1)/(p-1)$. By [F5], the different exponent is the valuation of the derivative of the Eisenstein polynomial of $\pi_n$. Its roots are the $\tau^k\pi_n$, so, using the displacement dependence in [F4], $$d_n=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}p^{n-j-1}(p-1)(1+c_j)\ge n p^n-\frac{p^n-1}{p-1}.$$ Thus its $v(p)=1$ valuation is $D_n=d_n/(e p^n)\ge(n-1/(p-1))/e$, which tends to infinity. [F4, F5, step 1.1, construct, algebra]

3.1 For any $z\in L_n$ of normalized $L_n$ valuation $t$, the trace-dual definition in [F5] gives $\operatorname{Tr}(z)\in\mathfrak p_E^{\lfloor(t+d_n)/p^n\rfloor}$, hence $$v(\operatorname{Tr}_{L_n/E}z)\ge v(z)+D_n-1/e.$$ Indeed $z\mathcal O_{L_n}$ lies in the product of the codifferent with $\mathfrak p_E^{\lfloor(t+d_n)/p^n\rfloor}$. For $n$ large, $D_n>s+1/e$, so step 2.1 implies $v(\operatorname{Tr}\epsilon_n)>n$. But $\tau$ acts as an $E$-automorphism of $L_n$, so its trace is invariant and $$\operatorname{Tr}\epsilon_n=\operatorname{Tr}(\tau(y_n)-y_n)-\operatorname{Tr}(1)=-p^n,$$ whose valuation equals $n$, a contradiction. Thus $\lambda(I_E)=0$. [F5, step 2.1, step 2.2, algebra, contradiction] ∎

## Source notes

The displayed special-case proof reconstructs the different estimate from the already proved finite Sen congruence and the trace-dual ideal. It does not import the Sen ramification filtration theorem.
