## Statement

All schemes and base changes below are locally of finite type over a fixed
field $k$, and all morphisms are $k$-morphisms.

Assume the Axiom of Choice ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]) inherited from the
smooth-immersion and homological suppliers. For a closed immersion
$Z\hookrightarrow T$ with a surjection $N^\vee\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal I/\mathcal I^2$
from a rank-$d$ bundle on $Z$, each $T'\to T$ gives $Z'=Z\times_TT'$, a closed
cone embedding $a:C_{Z'}T'\hookrightarrow N'=N|_{Z'}$, and an operation
$c(Z,T,N):=(p'^*)^{-1}a_*\sigma_{Z'/T'}:A_m(T')\to A_{m-d}(Z')$. This family is
bivariant and compatible with arbitrary base restriction. If
$N'_{0}\subset N'$ is a vector subbundle through which the cone embedding
factors, the operation defined with $N'$ is the operation defined with
$N'_{0}$ followed by the top Chern operator of $N'/N'_{0}$:
$c(Z',T',N')=c_{d-d_0}(N'/N'_0)\cap c(Z',T',N'_0)$, where
$d_0=\operatorname{rank}N'_0$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** the Axiom of Choice; a closed immersion $Z\hookrightarrow T$ with ideal $\mathcal I$ and a rank-$d$ bundle $N$ on $Z$ with a surjection $N^\vee\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal I/\mathcal I^2$; for every base change $T'\to T$ the closed subscheme $Z'=Z\times_TT'$, its normal cone $C_{Z'}T'$, the pullback $N'=N|_{Z'}$ and the induced surjection $N'^\vee\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal I\mathcal O_{T'}/(\mathcal I\mathcal O_{T'})^2$.

[L1] The deformation to the normal cone gives the open flat deformation family $D_i\to\mathbb P^1$ with special fibre the normal cone and ordinary fibres the ambient scheme and a well-defined specialization operation on Chow groups, natural in the base ([[def-deformation-to-the-normal-cone-and-specialization]]).

[L2] Bivariant operations and their axioms; flat pullback along a vector bundle is bijective with inverse $s_N^!$; the zero-section and excess identities hold after every base change ([[def-bivariant-chow-operations]], [[lem-vector-bundle-chow-homotopy-invariance]], [[lem-zero-section-gysin-and-excess-vector-subbundle]]).

[L3] Localization and homotopy invariance for Chow groups, and the compatibility of Cartier Gysin with proper pushforward and flat pullback ([[lem-chow-localization-and-vector-bundle-homotopy]], [[def-intersection-with-a-cartier-divisor-and-first-chern-class]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct; construct the cone embedding by the Rees-algebra surjection, verify the bivariant axioms for the specialization, and compare base changes through the induced proper morphism of blowups.

1.1 The cone embedding. Under a base change $T'\to T$ the ideal of $Z'$ is $\mathcal I\mathcal O_{T'}$ and its powers are generated by the images of the powers of $\mathcal I$, so the Rees algebra of $\mathcal I\mathcal O_{T'}$ is a quotient of the base change of the Rees algebra of $\mathcal I$; taking the symmetric algebra of the pulled-back conormal surjection and composing with the canonical map to the associated graded gives $\operatorname{Sym}(N'^\vee)\twoheadrightarrow\operatorname{gr}_{\mathcal I\mathcal O_{T'}}\mathcal O_{T'}$, and hence a closed immersion $a:C_{Z'}T'\hookrightarrow N'$ of the normal cone over $Z'$ into the vector bundle. This holds for arbitrary, including nonflat, base change, since only surjectivity of the graded maps is used. [L1, given, algebra]

2.1 Bivariant axioms. For each base $T'$ the operation is $c(Z,T,N)_{T'}=s_{N'}^!\,a_*\sigma_{Z'/T'}$, a composite of the well-defined specialization $\sigma_{Z'/T'}:A_m(T')\to A_m(C_{Z'}T')$ of [L1], the proper pushforward $a_*$, and the bijection $s_{N'}^!=(p'^*)^{-1}$ of [L2]. Proper compatibility: push a lift of the pulled-back class forward along the given proper morphism and use that Cartier Gysin commutes with proper pushforward [L3]. Flat compatibility: pull the lift back and use flat/Cartier compatibility. Divisor compatibility: apply the Cartier Gysin to the lift and use that two Cartier Gysins commute [L3], noting that the ordinary restriction of the result is the lift of the desired input. Base restriction is simply the reindexing of the family. These verifications are exactly the three bivariant axioms of [L2]. [L1, L2, L3, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Base change. Under a base change the blowup of $\mathcal I\mathcal O_{T'}$ admits a closed immersion into the base change of the original blowup, induced by the surjection of Rees algebras of step 1.1, and this immersion is an isomorphism over the ordinary part $\mathbb A^1_{T'}$; the induced proper morphism between the two deformation families is therefore an isomorphism over the ordinary fibres, and pushing a lift along it exhibits the two infinity operations as equal after pushforward. Consequently the cone portions of the two operations define the same class after pushforward to $N'$, and since proper pushforward, open restriction and $(p'^*)^{-1}$ are bivariant, the operation is compatible with arbitrary base change. [L1, L2, step 1.1, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 The excess factor. Suppose the cone embedding factors through a vector subbundle $N'_0\subseteq N'$ of rank $d_0$; write the cone specialization pushed into $N'_0$ as $p_0'^*\beta$ for a class $\beta$ on $Z'$, using the bundle-pullback bijection for $N'_0$ from [L2]. Applying the excess identity $s_{N'}^!a'_*p_0'^*\beta=c_{\operatorname{rank}(N'/N'_0)}(N'/N'_0)\cap\beta$ of [L2] to the vector subbundle inclusion $a':N'_0\hookrightarrow N'$ gives that the operation defined with $N'$ equals the operation defined with $N'_0$ multiplied by the top Chern operator of the quotient bundle $N'/N'_0$; this is the asserted excess factor. [L2, step 2.1, algebra] ∎
