## Statement

Assume Countable Choice. The doubly-pointed nested-cap torus diagram in the four-state count lemma is the knotification of the oriented positive Hopf link. Use the knotification joining $z_1$ on the first component to $w_2$ on the second, retaining $w=w_1$ and $z=z_2$, and orient the resulting knot from $w$ to $z$ through the alpha handlebody. It is nullhomologous in $S^1\times S^2$ and has the genus-one Seifert surface obtained from the Hopf annulus by the knotification band.

In the actual determinant-trivial ambient Spin-c sector, define its Alexander grading by half the first Chern evaluation on this capped Seifert surface, using the negative generator Morse field and its zero-Seifert-framed meridional cap. The four actual generators have the ABSOLUTE values
$$A(a)=1,\qquad A(b)=A(c)=0,\qquad A(d)=-1.$$
Consequently the four-state calculation supplies a surviving top Alexander-one class $[a]$ in total hat homology, and the literal filling triangle map is nonzero on it. This is an actual relative-Euler evaluation; it does not use a rank symmetry, an Alexander polynomial, a knot Floer symmetry theorem, or the skein exact sequence being constructed.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The explicitly smoothed nested-cap model, with the paired corner smoothing specified there, its involution $R(x,y)=(1/2-x,-y)$, and the retained ordered marks.

[F1] The literal model has four generators, actual full differential $\partial b=d+Ua$, relative grading differences $(1,0,0,-1)$ in the stated order, and a unique zero-$w$ filling triangle taking $a$ to the filling generator. [[lem-nested-cap-torus-has-a-four-state-filtered-complex-and-nonzero-filling-map]]

[F2] For an actual periodic domain, the Morse determinant Chern evaluation is $e(P)+2n_x(P)-2n_w(P)$. [[lem-balanced-periodic-domain-morse-chern-evaluation]]

[F3] The local negative-Morse tube and zero-Euler meridional cap are explicitly constructed. Their actual formula, rather than an assertion that relative gradings have a canonical origin, is used below. Its mark-swap cap is $(\phi,\lambda)\mapsto(\pi-\phi,-\lambda)$. [[lem-doubly-pointed-knot-morse-fields-have-a-zero-framed-seifert-extension]]


## Proof

1.1 Cut the nested torus on $y=2$ and cap both boundary components, keeping the cylinder $-2<y<2$ as a coordinate part of the sphere. Put $z_1=(1/8,3/2)$ in its upper outside face and $w_2=(3/8,-3/2)$ in its lower outside face. The four-pointed sphere has alpha as its equator and beta with its four marked bigon faces. Specify the ACTUAL tangle arcs, rather than infer a link from its linking number. In the alpha ball take the two pushed boundary arcs $\gamma_1:x=1/8,\ 1/10\le y\le3/2$, oriented from $w_1$ to $z_1$, and $\gamma_2:x=3/8,\ -3/2\le y\le-1/10$, oriented from $w_2$ to $z_2$. They lie in its two different alpha disk faces. In the beta ball take $\delta_1$ from $z_1$ to $w_1$ through $(5/8,3/2),(5/8,-1/2),(1/8,-1/2)$, and $\delta_2$ from $z_2$ to $w_2$ through $(3/8,1/2),(-1/8,1/2),(-1/8,-3/2)$. The explicit beta polygon shows that these lie in its two different disk faces: the first passes below the short lower cap and through the right gap of the long upper cap; the second passes above the short upper cap and through the left gap of the long lower cap. They are disjoint. Round their finitely many corners away from all crossings and marks, and push the alpha and beta arcs into their respective ball collars. The resulting link diagram has exactly TWO intercomponent crossings: $\gamma_1$ with $\delta_2$ at $(1/8,1/2)$, and $\gamma_2$ with $\delta_1$ at $(3/8,-1/2)$. There are no self crossings. Check their positive sign without a picture: $\gamma_1\cup\delta_1$ projects to a clockwise rectangle bounding a disk $Q$ containing $z_2$ and missing $w_2$. The sphere orientation is $-dx\wedge dy$, so this is its positive boundary. In collar coordinates $(x,y,h)$ with $h$ into the alpha ball, the ambient orientation is $dx\wedge dy\wedge dh$. The spanning disk following this projected rectangle is oriented by $-dx\wedge dy$. At $z_2$ the second oriented component goes from its positive-height alpha bridge to its negative-height beta bridge, and crosses this disk once with tangent $-\partial_h$. Its signed intersection is therefore positive. Thus the linking number is one; since the literal diagram has exactly two intercomponent crossings, both signs are positive. It is precisely the two-component positive Hopf diagram, not an unspecified link with linking number one. Finally remove the small disks about $z_1,w_2$ and attach the knotification tube. This exactly reverses the original cut and caps of the nested torus, with the specified boundary arcs carried through that tube. It identifies the actual retained doubly-pointed knot and its bridge witnesses with the claimed knotification. [given, construct, algebra]

2.1 The Hopf annulus is an oriented Seifert surface for the two-component braid closure. Attach the knotification band through the added one-handle between its two boundary components. Its Euler characteristic decreases from zero to minus one and its boundary becomes the single oriented knot, so it is a genus-one Seifert surface $F$. In particular the knot is nullhomologous. The ambient pair has one normalized periodic generator $P=Q_+-Q_-$, capping to the generator of $H_2(S^1\times S^2)$. Both $Q_\pm$ are quadrilaterals and hence have Euler measure zero; at every one of $a,b,c,d$ their signed four-quadrant averages cancel. Also $n_w(P)=0$. Formula [F2] therefore gives zero Chern evaluation on that generator for each of the four actual ambient labels. They are in the determinant-trivial sector, not unspecified affine translates. This uses an actual periodic Chern calculation and no Maslov-to-Chern inference from a formal domain. [F1, F2, step 1.1, construct, algebra]

3.1 Specify the origin directly on this knot exterior. In a zero-Seifert-framed tube use the local coordinates of [F3]: $f=\sin t(1-r^2/2)$, with gradient $\cos t\,\partial_t-\sin t\,r\partial_r$, and take the NEGATIVE modified gradient for the exterior field. Its boundary meridional phase is $-i e^{2i\phi}$ after zero surgery, where $\phi=t$ and $\lambda=-\theta$. To fill the new solid torus use the nonzero field with planar component $q(\zeta)=-i(\zeta^2-a_0^2\chi(|\zeta|))$ and longitudinal component positive at $+a_0$ and negative at $-a_0$, exactly as in [F3]. Projection of its longitude vector onto the perpendicular plane has the two simple local indices $+1,-1$. Its relative cap Euler number is therefore zero. This is a local solid-torus construction and does not require the surrounding manifold to be a three-sphere. It gives an actual Spin-c lift over zero surgery and the value $A(x)=\tfrac12\langle c_1,\widehat F\rangle$. An ambient spin structure extends over zero-Seifert-framed surgery: trivialize it on the Seifert surface and extend its bounding meridian spin framing across the added disk. Its determinant is trivial. Every difference line changes determinant degree by twice its degree, so the displayed half-evaluation is integral. Replacing $F$ by another representative changes it by a closed ambient surface; by step 2.1 its determinant evaluates zero there. Thus this local origin is well-defined for this torsion sector and is the actual Seifert evaluation stipulated in the Statement. Its relative differences also have the required sign, rather than being assumed from a relative grading choice: if $\epsilon$ is the alpha trajectory from $x$ to $y$ followed by the reverse beta trajectory, the finite trajectory-tube clutch changes the positive-field Spin-c label by $-\operatorname{PD}\epsilon$. Passing to the negative field conjugates that difference, so the actual exterior labels satisfy $\mathfrak t(x)-\mathfrak t(y)=\operatorname{PD}\epsilon$. Their determinant changes by twice this class. Intersect a spanning region chain for that loop with the two knot bridges; its two ends contribute $n_z-n_w$, equivalently its linking with the oriented knot. The zero-Euler caps contribute no further term. Pairing with $F$ therefore gives $A(x)-A(y)=n_z-n_w$ for the actual classes in [F1], including both short bigons. This local calculation uses the given Seifert surface and the actual nullhomologous loop, not a classification of all Spin-c structures. [F3, step 2.1, construct, algebra]

4.1 The exact involution $R$ preserves alpha and beta and exchanges the two retained marks. At every rounded corner choose the paired smoothing; it therefore preserves the smooth curves as well as their unrounded graph. Its extension is visible already before knotification: embed the cut cylinder as a round sphere with longitude $2\pi x$ and an odd height coordinate. Then $R$ is the rotation $(X,Y,Z)\mapsto(-X,Y,-Z)$, exchanges its cap points and extends by that same rotation over both tangle balls. Choose the boundary-parallel bridge witnesses in paired collars. On the added knotification handle it exchanges the two end disks and reverses their meridian coordinate, which is an orientation-preserving product map. Thus it extends over the actual knotified three-manifold and carries its knot to the reverse, not to an unrelated abstract filtered complex. It carries the modified Morse trajectories and their determinant line. In the local tube the mark exchange is $t'=\pi-t,\theta'=-\theta$, and the zero-surgery map in [F3] is orientation preserving with cap Euler number still zero. It reverses the capped oriented Seifert class; any remaining difference is an ambient surface with zero Chern pairing by step 2.1. Consequently the ACTUAL Seifert evaluation satisfies $A(Rx)=-A(x)$. Now $R(b)=b$, $R(c)=c$ and $R(a)=d$, so $A(b)=A(c)=0$. The actual short-bigon relative differences in [F1] give $A(a)=1$ and $A(d)=-1$. Together with the literal four-state differential and filling count in [F1], this proves both the absolute top origin and its surviving, nontrivially mapped cycle. No homology symmetry or total-map cancellation has been used to choose the origin. [F1, F3, step 1.1, step 2.1, step 3.1, construct, algebra] ∎
