## Statement

Assume Countable Choice. Let $K_+,K_-\subset S^3$ be oriented knots differing at one specified positive-to-negative crossing change. There are chosen doubly-pointed admissible diagrams and actual triangle maps
$$F_\pm:\widehat{CF}(S^3,K_+)\longrightarrow\widehat{CF}(S^3,K_-)$$
counting zero-$w$ triangles in TWO SPECIFIED INDIVIDUAL Spin-c structures on the minus-one crossing-circle trace. On its exceptional sphere $E$ their determinant evaluations are $+1$ and $-1$. Each map is filtered for the Seifert-normalized Alexander grading, with filtration shift zero, and each induces the nonzero map between the one-dimensional total hat Floer homologies. The sum of the two induced maps is zero over $\mathbb F_2$; nonvanishing of either component is proved independently.

Consequently, whenever the inclusion threshold is defined using these actual normalized filtered complexes, $\tau(K_-)\le\tau(K_+)$. This assertion concerns the actual chosen crossing-circle operation; it does not assume a general four-dimensional map-degree theorem or infer an individual component from the total surgery map.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** An oriented knot diagram and the specified crossing ball, with the two signs and the usual crossing-circle disk meeting its two strands with opposite intersection signs.

[F1] The literal genus-one minus-one unknot trace has two actual isolated regular zero-mark triangles, individually of count one, with exceptional Chern evaluations $+1,-1$. [[lem-local-torus-blowup-triangles-have-two-nonzero-spin-components]]

[F2] Actual admissible generic-curve triangle and quadrilateral counts exist, have their label-preserving compactified boundary identities and pointed curve-transport homotopies, including the rank-zero branch. The physical Spin-c labels are carried under curve isotopies and neck decompositions. [[lem-knot-floer-holomorphic-triangle-transversality-compactness-gluing]] [[lem-heegaard-polygon-spin-c-difference-is-the-capped-periodic-domain]]

[F3] The actual pointed local top-cycle triangle operations for changes of compression disks have count one; their inverse homotopies and the actual zero-basepoint ordinary stabilization operations give the stated hat diagram comparisons. [[lem-circular-handleslide-top-triangles-have-actual-count-one]] [[thm-hat-heegaard-floer-is-a-closed-three-manifold-invariant]]

[F4] The absolute Alexander value is the actual negative-Morse determinant evaluation with its zero-Seifert-framed cap. Trajectory differences and their intersections give $A(x)-A(y)=n_z-n_w$; this is a Chern evaluation rather than a chosen relative origin. [[lem-doubly-pointed-knot-morse-fields-have-a-zero-framed-seifert-extension]]

## Proof

1.1 Choose the crossing circle $C$ as the boundary of the small transverse disk between the two strands. It is a literal round unknot disjoint from the retained marks, and its algebraic linking with the knot is zero. Minus-one surgery on $C$ changes $K_+$ to $K_-$: cap the surgery meridian disk and remove its minus-one twist. The two encircled, oppositely oriented strands acquire one right-handed physical full twist; reversing the orientation on the second strand changes both oriented crossing signs, so a positive oriented half twist becomes a negative one. Outside that ball nothing moves. The two-handle core capped by the original crossing disk gives a sphere $E$ of square minus one; its complement is the common knot exterior away from that local operation. This verifies both the direction and the actual framed handle, rather than choosing the direction from a desired inequality. [given, construct, algebra]

2.1 Construct the chosen diagrams together with an explicit unmarked comparison word. Start with the Clifford torus containing the round $C$ as the beta-core and the slope triple of [F1]. The other Clifford core is disjoint from the knot after a small general-position perturbation. The complement of those two cores has explicit coordinates $T^2\times\mathbb R$, obtained from the two complex phases and the logarithm of their radial ratio. The compact knot is contained in a bounded height interval. A strictly increasing smooth compression of that interval puts it in an arbitrarily thin torus collar, without changing either core or creating an intersection. Project it generically onto the torus. Subdivide its finite crossing diagram into over- and under-bridge arcs, with a small disjoint box at each crossing. Separating successive bridges at ordinary projected points makes each over arc, and each under arc, bound a rectangle with its projected surface arc; all witness rectangles on the same side are disjoint. They lie in the collar, away from $C,w$. If there are $b$ bridges on each side, attach $b-1$ surface tubes along under bridges, in the order around the knot, to merge all over bridges into one. In the local coordinates of a witness rectangle this removes two foot disks and inserts its boundary cylinder. The new meridian is the cross-sectional disk of that tube; its opposite dual disk is the witness rectangle completed across the two foot collars, meeting that meridian once. Gluing the two old over-bridge witness disks to the intervening rectangle gives one new witness disk. Thus each step reduces the number of over and under bridges by one and preserves actual simultaneous boundary parallelism. All these tubes avoid the distinguished core $C$. After the finite merger there is one knot bridge on each side, with the retained endpoints $w,z$. Choose the compression disks as the old Clifford disk together with the explicit new meridians and dual disks. If a disk crosses one of the two bridge witnesses, eliminate its finitely many outermost intersection arcs in order: slide that disk along the indicated outermost witness rectangle over the corresponding new meridian, then push the resulting disk off the rectangle. The disk band and its push are contained in a product ball and replace one intersection arc by none, so the integer number strictly decreases. The remaining compression systems miss both bridge witnesses. Perform each new-handle addition and indicated disk band on all three filling systems, outside the dedicated $C$ core; the actual ambient curves and bridge arcs, not only an abstract generator list, have now been constructed. The resulting pairs represent exactly the knots at the two ends of the specified crossing-circle trace. If $z$ and the knot are forgotten, reverse the disk bands and remove the successive cancelling meridian/dual pairs. This is an actual finite word back to [F1], consisting only of disk slides, pointed surface isotopies and cancelling ordinary stabilizations; it fixes $w,C$ and the two-handle framing. The intermediate unmarked diagrams need not admit the knot and are not used as filtered comparisons. [F1, step 1.1, construct, algebra]

2.2 Recover the filtration in the original knot-adapted diagrams, without asserting that the unmarked comparison word preserves it. Since $\operatorname{lk}(C,K)=0$, choose an actual Seifert surface disjoint from $C$: start with any finite Seifert surface from the knot diagram, make it transverse to $C$, pair the positive and negative intersection points along $C$, and tube successive opposite pairs in disjoint narrow annuli. The tubes remove two intersections at a time and do not change the boundary. All of this lies outside the retained marks and the surgery core. This surface is therefore present on both ends of the trace and has the same zero-Seifert boundary framing. After zero surgery on the knot, its two caps represent the same two-dimensional class in the composite cobordism; its difference bounds the product surface cylinder. A difference of the two exceptional Spin-c labels is supported on $E$, and this surface is disjoint from $E$, so the two labels have the same evaluation on it. In particular neither introduces a hidden affine Alexander shift. [F2, F4, step 1.1, construct, algebra]

3.1 Choose genuinely admissible coherent data for this finite word. The own-pair periodic directions from the cancelling handles are supported in disjoint thin annuli and have private positive and negative coefficients. Every other normalized triple-periodic direction has a nonzero alpha-boundary coefficient, because an alpha-zero relation is an own-pair relation. Wind each alpha circle, outside the marked and dedicated-handle neighborhoods, about its chosen transverse dual arc in two opposite small passages. In the two private winding rectangles its domain coefficients differ from a bounded original linear expression by $+Na_i$ and $-Na_i$. Own-pair directions have zero coefficients there. The finite lattice of original coefficients and the two specified Chern functionals have finite operator norms. Choose $N$ greater than their sum, using an integral basis; choosing an index with maximal $|a_i|$ then gives a positive coefficient strictly greater than half the nonnegative Chern evaluation, and its negative counterpart. If all $a_i$ vanish, the private own-pair annuli give both signs and zero Chern evaluation. This proves the strict admissibility criterion for both labels simultaneously. The same finite construction covers the quadrilaterals and the finite elementary comparison word, by reserving separate rectangles and taking one larger $N$. Small pointed genericizations supplied by [F2] keep their witness coefficients and the actual label transports. Thus the maps and homotopies below are actual finite counts in admissible data; no unproved finite-domain premise is substituted for their construction. [F2, step 2.1, construct, algebra]

4.1 Forget $z$ temporarily to compute EACH total hat component. The finite word of step 2.1 intertwines the two actual triangle maps separately. For a disk slide the quadrilateral identity in [F2], with the actual top cycle in [F3], says that applying the slide comparison before the triangle or after it differs by the boundary of the quadrilateral count. The other face is precisely the top-cycle product from [F3], with coefficient one; the strip faces are the chain-homotopy terms. Its actual Spin-c extension fixes the restriction to the framed two-handle, so this identity holds individually at each exceptional Chern evaluation. A small isotopy has the corresponding one-parameter triangle homotopy from [F2]. For a cancelling ordinary stabilization, place its attachment disk in the whole zero-basepoint divisor neighborhood. The actual holomorphic separator makes every zero-$w$ map avoid that disk and separates the old triangle from the new torus factor. For a triple that factor is the small triangle between the new alpha meridian and the two small Hamiltonian copies of the new beta meridian, not a constant triple point. Its top-input domain is the unique small Jordan triangle, and its actual index-zero inverse is the three-reflected-evaluation inverse in [F1]; the one-root copy count in [F3] excludes any other zero-index top-output domain. Thus the stabilized triangle map is the product of the old map and this coefficient-one factor. Together with the actual pair stabilization inclusions and projections this gives a literal label-preserving intertwiner. These are the proved elementary operations of [F3], applied to the actual word, not a general cobordism-functoriality assumption. At the final genus-one model each of the two components is nonzero by [F1]. All intertwiners induce isomorphisms on total hat homology by [F3]; hence EACH original component is nonzero. Their exceptional labels may be exchanged if the orientation of $E$ is reversed, which leaves both conclusions unchanged. [F1, F2, F3, step 2.1, step 3.1, construct, algebra]

4.2 Compute the remaining shift directly by the actual Morse caps. For a triangle from $x$ to $y$, carry the negative exterior field from its incoming Seifert cap through the two-handle complement. In a local mark trajectory tube, each crossing of the zero-surgery longitude changes the determinant clutch by twice its signed degree. The relative intersection chain of the triangle with the two retained marked divisors has degrees $n_z,n_w$; the orientations are the same negative-field convention as [F4]. The common Seifert surface of step 2.2 and its zero-Euler caps thus give the exact equality $A(x)-A(y)=n_z-n_w$, with no constant term. The auxiliary own-pair top input lies in the region outside both marks and its zero-Euler field is the carried reference, so it contributes zero to this equation. Every counted hat triangle has $n_w=0$ and, by actual positivity, $n_z\ge0$. Consequently each of the two individual chain maps is Alexander nonincreasing with shift zero. The actual triangle compactification makes it a chain map by [F2]. No absolute Maslov degree calculation was used. [F2, F4, step 3.1, step 2.2, construct, algebra]

5.1 Let $c$ be any cycle representing the nonzero total hat class for $K_+$ and lying at its inclusion threshold. Either of the two individual maps takes it to a cycle representing the nonzero total class for $K_-$ by step 4.1. Its filtration is no larger by step 4.2, so the definition of the inclusion threshold gives $\tau(K_-)\le\tau(K_+)$. Finally the two individual induced maps are both the unique nonzero map between one-dimensional vector spaces, so their sum vanishes in characteristic two. This cancellation is a consequence after both individual calculations and has not supplied either one's nonvanishing. This is the specific filtered crossing-change operation asserted, not a deduction from the total surgery triangle. [step 4.1, step 4.2, algebra] ∎
