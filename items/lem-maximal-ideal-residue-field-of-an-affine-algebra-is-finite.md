## Statement

Let $k$ be a field, let $A$ be a finite-type $k$-algebra, and let
$\mathfrak m$ be a maximal ideal of $A$. Then the residue field $A/\mathfrak m$
is a finite extension of $k$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A field $k$, a finite-type $k$-algebra $A$, and a maximal ideal $\mathfrak m\subset A$.

[L1] Finite type means generated by finitely many algebra elements ([[def-finite-type-and-module-finite-algebras]]).

[L2] A quotient by a maximal ideal is a field ([[thm-quotient-is-field-iff-ideal-maximal]]).

[L3] A field finitely generated as a $k$-algebra is finite over $k$ ([[cor-field-finite-type-over-a-field-is-a-finite-extension]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 By [L1], choose generators $a_1,\ldots,a_n$ of $A$ as a $k$-algebra. Their images in $A/\mathfrak m$ generate the quotient as a $k$-algebra, so $A/\mathfrak m$ is again a finite-type $k$-algebra. [L1, given, choose]

2.1 By [L2], the quotient $A/\mathfrak m$ is a field. Applying [L3] to this finite-type field over $k$ shows that $A/\mathfrak m$ is finite over $k$. [L2, L3, step 1.1] ∎
