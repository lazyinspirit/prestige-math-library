## Definition

Let $(S,m)$ be a finite Coxeter matrix and let $W$ be the group presented by it as in [[def-hh-coxeter-matrix-word-group-and-length]].

**A common splitting field.** Put $h:=\prod\,(X^{2m(s,t)}-1)\in\mathbb{Q}[X]$, the product over all unordered pairs $\{s,t\}\subseteq S$ with $s\ne t$ and $m(s,t)<\infty$ (an empty product when $\#S\le1$). Let $K$ be a splitting field of $h$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ ([[def-rationals]], [[def-polynomial-ring-over-a-commutative-ring]], [[thm-polynomial-degree-of-a-product-over-a-domain]], [[thm-polynomial-ring-over-a-field-is-a-ufd]], [[thm-splitting-fields-exist-for-nonzero-polynomials]], [[cor-splitting-fields-exist-for-finite-families]], [[def-polynomials-that-split-and-splitting-fields]]). The prime subfield of $K$ is $\mathbb{Q}$ ([[thm-prime-subfield-classification]], [[def-ring-characteristic]], [[thm-rat-field]]), so $\operatorname{char}K=0$ ([[thm-characteristic-of-a-field-is-zero-or-prime]]) and $X^n-1$ is separable over $K$ for every $n\ge1$ ([[thm-separability-of-x-n-minus-one-and-the-order-of-the-group-of-roots-of-unity]]). For each finite edge, $X^{2m(s,t)}-1$ is a factor of $h$ ([[thm-polynomial-degree-of-a-product-over-a-domain]]), so all its roots lie in $K$ and, being separable of degree $2m(s,t)$, it has exactly $2m(s,t)$ distinct roots there ([[thm-separability-of-x-n-minus-one-and-the-order-of-the-group-of-roots-of-unity]], [[def-polynomials-that-split-and-splitting-fields]]); the group $\mu_{2m(s,t)}(K)$ of such roots is therefore finite of order $2m(s,t)$ and hence cyclic, so it contains a primitive $2m(s,t)$-th root of unity ([[prop-the-roots-of-unity-in-a-field-form-a-finite-cyclic-group]], [[def-roots-of-unity-in-a-field]]). **Fix** one primitive $2m(s,t)$-th root of unity $\zeta_{st}\in K$ for each unordered finite edge (a finite selection, since $S$ is finite) and put
$$c_{st}:=c_{ts}:=\zeta_{st}+\zeta_{st}^{-1}\in K\quad (m(s,t)<\infty),\qquad c_{st}:=c_{ts}:=2\quad (m(s,t)=\infty).$$

**The representation on the simple-root basis.** Let $E$ be the $K$-vector space with basis $(\alpha_s)_{s\in S}$ ([[def-vector-space]], [[def-linear-basis]]). For each $s\in S$ let $\sigma_s:E\to E$ be the unique $K$-linear map with
$$\sigma_s(\alpha_s)=-\alpha_s,\qquad \sigma_s(\alpha_t)=\alpha_t+c_{st}\alpha_s\quad (t\ne s).$$
Directly from the definition each $\sigma_s$ is an involution: $\sigma_s^2(\alpha_s)=\alpha_s$ and, for $t\ne s$, $\sigma_s^2(\alpha_t)=\sigma_s(\alpha_t+c_{st}\alpha_s)=\alpha_t+c_{st}\alpha_s-c_{st}\alpha_s=\alpha_t$. Hence every $\sigma_s$ is invertible with $\sigma_s^{-1}=\sigma_s$, and the group these maps generate acts on $E$ ([[def-linear-map]], [[def-linear-isomorphism-and-invertible-linear-map]], [[lem-composition-and-identity-linear-maps]]).

**The root set.** The **root set** of the construction is
$$\Phi:=\{\sigma_{s_1}\sigma_{s_2}\cdots\sigma_{s_k}(\alpha_t):k\ge0,\ s_1,\dots,s_k,t\in S\}\subseteq E,$$
the orbit of the simple roots under the group generated by the $\sigma_s$ (each generator is an involution). Writing $\sigma:W\to\mathrm{GL}_K(E)$ for the representation supplied by [[lem-hh-dihedral-root-recurrence-and-root-sign]], one has $\Phi=\{\sigma_w(\alpha_s):w\in W,\ s\in S\}$, the $W$-orbit of the simple roots.

**Conventions and limits.** (i) $c_{st}=c_{ts}$; $c_{st}=0$ exactly when $m(s,t)=2$; $c_{st}=2$ when $m(s,t)=\infty$; replacing $\zeta_{st}$ by $\zeta_{st}^{-1}$ leaves $c_{st}$ unchanged, so the construction depends on the chosen primitive roots only through the numbers $c_{st}$. (ii) No positivity of $c_{st}$, no integrality of roots over $\mathbb{Z}$, no identification of roots with reflections, and no faithfulness or definiteness of any form is asserted; those are separate matters, not part of this construction. (iii) $S$ is finite throughout, so the basis $(\alpha_s)_{s\in S}$ is finite and every linear map is specified by finitely many values.

## Remarks

The single recorded justifier of this definition is [[lem-hh-dihedral-root-recurrence-and-root-sign]], which proves $\sigma_s^2=\mathrm{id}$, the exact order of $\sigma_s\sigma_t$, and the induced homomorphism $\sigma:W\to\mathrm{GL}_K(E)$; only after that lemma is $\Phi$ literally the $W$-orbit of the simple roots.

No choice is used. The splitting field and the maps $\sigma_s$ are constructions, and one primitive $2m$-th root is fixed for each of finitely many edges; the convention $c_{st}=2$ for $m(s,t)=\infty$ introduces no root of unity at all.
