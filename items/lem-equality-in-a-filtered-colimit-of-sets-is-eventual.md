## Statement

Let $D:\mathcal J\to\mathbf{Set}$ be a small filtered diagram. For
$x\in D(j)$ and $y\in D(k)$, their images in $\operatorname*{colim}D$ are equal
if and only if there are arrows $a:j\to\ell$ and $b:k\to\ell$ such that
$D(a)(x)=D(b)(y)$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The filtered diagram and the two elements in the statement.

[F1] Filteredness supplies common target objects and coequalizers of parallel
arrows ([[def-filtered-category-and-filtered-colimit]]).

[L1] A Set-colimit is the tagged union modulo the equivalence relation
generated by the diagram arrows ([[thm-set-has-all-small-colimits]]).

[F2] An equivalence relation is reflexive, symmetric, and transitive
([[def-equivalence-relation]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** identify the generated equivalence relation.

1.1 On tagged elements define $(j,x)\approx(k,y)$ when some $j\xrightarrow a\ell\xleftarrow b k$ satisfies $D(a)x=D(b)y$. Identity arrows prove reflexivity, and exchanging $a,b$ proves symmetry. [F1, F2]

1.2 For transitivity, suppose the first equality is witnessed in $\ell$ by $a:j\to\ell$, $b:k\to\ell$, and the second in $m$ by $c:k\to m$, $d:n\to m$. Choose $r:\ell\to p$ and $s:m\to p$ by [F1]. Then $rb,sc:k\rightrightarrows p$ are parallel, so choose $t:p\to q$ with $trb=tsc$. Applying $D$ shows $tra(x)=tsd(z)$, proving $(j,x)\approx(n,z)$. [F1, given]

2.1 Thus $\approx$ is an equivalence relation. It contains every generating pair $(j,x)$ and $(k,D(u)x)$ by choosing $\ell=k$, $a=u$, and $b=1_k$. Conversely, a witness $a,b$ gives a chain of two generating identifications from $(j,x)$ and $(k,y)$ to their equal tagged element in $D(\ell)$. Hence $\approx$ is exactly the equivalence relation in [L1]. [F2, L1, step 1.1, step 1.2]

3.1 By [L1], equality of the two colimit classes is equivalence under that relation. Step 2.1 identifies this with the existence of the displayed common stage, proving both directions of the biconditional. [L1, step 2.1] ∎
