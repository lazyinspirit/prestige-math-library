## Remark

The 2024 erratum states that the original Proposition 8.3 is incorrect and does not hold as stated. It replaces that proposition by a revised Proposition 3.1, which is sufficient for the use in Proposition 7.3, and requires taut filtrations under the corrected multidegree convention. No unrepaired formulation of the original Proposition 8.3 is used in the planned proof route.
