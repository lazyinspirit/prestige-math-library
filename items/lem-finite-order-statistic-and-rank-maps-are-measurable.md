## Statement

Let $(\Omega,\mathcal F)$ be a measurable space, let $R_1,\ldots,R_m$ be
measurable real-valued functions with $m\ge1$, and let $1\le k\le m$. The
$k$th order statistic $R_{(k)}$ is measurable. For a finite real score vector
$R_1,\ldots,R_n$ and another measurable real-valued function $U$ on the same
space, its upper-rank p-value and tie-randomized rank are measurable.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The measurable real score functions, integer bounds on $k$, and, for the randomized formula, a measurable $U:[0,1]$-valued input on the same measurable space.

[F1] Finite maxima and minima, finite sums and scalar multiples of measurable real functions are measurable. [[thm-arithmetic-and-lattice-operations-preserve-measurability]]

[F2] Real measurability is preimage measurability for the Borel sigma-algebra. [[def-measurable-function-between-measurable-spaces]]

[F3] The Borel sigma-algebra contains closed intervals and half-lines because it is generated by open sets. [[def-borel-sigma-algebra]]

[F4] The conformal p-value and tie-randomized p-value are the finite rank formulas in the definition. [[def-conformal-nonconformity-score-and-rank-pvalue]]

## Proof

1.1 For each $k$-element subset $I\subseteq\{1,\ldots,m\}$, let $M_I=\max_{i\in I}R_i$. Then $R_{(k)}=\min_{|I|=k}M_I$: every $k$-element subset has maximum at least the $k$th smallest score, and the subset of the $k$ smallest scores has that maximum exactly. There are finitely many such nonempty subsets, so [F1] makes this finite minimum of finite maxima measurable. [F1, given]

1.2 For any two indices, $\{R_i\ge R_j\}=\{R_i-R_j\in[0,+\infty)\}$ is measurable by [F1]–[F3]. The upper-rank p-value is a finite sum of indicators of these events divided by $n$, so is measurable. [F1, F2, F3, F4]

2.1 On the same domain, the tie count is a finite sum of the comparison indicators from step 1.2. Multiplication by the measurable real function $U$, addition, and division by the positive integer $n$ preserve measurability by [F1]. Hence the randomized rank map is measurable. [F1, F4, step 1.2] ∎
