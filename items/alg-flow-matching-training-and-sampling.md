## Definition

Let $d,q,B\ge1$, $N\ge0$, and $\Theta\subseteq\mathbb R^q$ be open. Let $\lambda$ be a sigma-finite Borel measure on $\mathbb R^d$, let $\pi$ be a Borel probability measure on $\mathbb R^d$, and let $\rho:[0,1]\times\mathbb R^d\times\mathbb R^d\to[0,\infty]$ and $u^c:[0,1]\times\mathbb R^d\times\mathbb R^d\to\mathbb R^d$ be jointly measurable. Assume $\int\rho_t(x\mid x_1)\,\lambda(dx)=1$ for every $t$ and for $\pi$-almost every $x_1$, and that for $\pi$-almost every $x_1$, $\rho_t(\cdot\mid x_1)$ and every component of $\rho_tu^c_t(\cdot\mid x_1)$ are continuously differentiable in $(t,x)$ on $(0,1)\times\mathbb R^d$ and satisfy $$\partial_t\rho_t(x\mid x_1)+\sum_{j=1}^d\partial_{x_j}\bigl(\rho_t(x\mid x_1)(u^c_t)^j(x\mid x_1)\bigr)=0.$$ For every compact $K\subset(0,1)\times\mathbb R^d$, suppose there is a nonnegative $G_K\in L^1(\pi)$ bounding on $K$, for $\pi$-almost every $x_1$, the absolute values of $\rho_t$, $\partial_t\rho_t$, each component of $\rho_tu^c_t$, and each corresponding spatial flux derivative.

Define $p_t(x):=\int\rho_t(x\mid x_1)\,\pi(dx_1)$ and $J_t(x):=\int\rho_t(x\mid x_1)u^c_t(x\mid x_1)\,\pi(dx_1)$. Assume $0<p_t(x)<\infty$ and $J_t(x)$ is finite on $[0,1]\times\mathbb R^d$, and put $u^m_t(x):=J_t(x)/p_t(x)$. Let $v:[0,1]\times\mathbb R^d\times\Theta\to\mathbb R^d$ be jointly measurable and continuously differentiable in $\theta$, with jointly measurable parameter derivative. Define $$\mathcal L_C(\theta):=\int_0^1\!\int\!\int\rho_t(x\mid x_1)\|v(t,x;\theta)-u^c_t(x\mid x_1)\|_2^2\,\pi(dx_1)\lambda(dx)dt$$ and $$\mathcal L_M(\theta):=\int_0^1\!\int p_t(x)\|v(t,x;\theta)-u^m_t(x)\|_2^2\,\lambda(dx)dt.$$ Assume, almost surely, that $\mathcal L_C(\theta_k)$ and $\mathcal L_M(\theta_k)$ are finite for every $k=0,\ldots,N$, and that $$C:=\int_0^1\!\int\!\int\rho_t(x\mid x_1)\|u^c_t(x\mid x_1)-u^m_t(x)\|_2^2\,\pi(dx_1)\lambda(dx)dt$$ is finite. Write $D_\theta v$ for the matrix with entries $(D_\theta v)_{ij}=\partial_{\theta_j}v^i$. For every compact $H\subset\Theta$ and coordinate $j$, suppose nonnegative integrable majorants $G^C_{H,j}$ on $dt\,\lambda(dx)\,\pi(dx_1)$ and $G^M_{H,j}$ on $dt\,\lambda(dx)$ satisfy, for every $\theta\in H$ outside fixed null sets, $$2\rho_t(x\mid x_1)\left|\left\langle\partial_{\theta_j}v(t,x;\theta),v(t,x;\theta)-u^c_t(x\mid x_1)\right\rangle\right|\le G^C_{H,j}(t,x,x_1)$$ and $$2p_t(x)\left|\left\langle\partial_{\theta_j}v(t,x;\theta),v(t,x;\theta)-u^m_t(x)\right\rangle\right|\le G^M_{H,j}(t,x).$$ Let $Q(dt,dx_1,dx):=dt\,\pi(dx_1)\rho_t(x\mid x_1)\lambda(dx)$ be the probability law of a training triple, with $dt$ Lebesgue probability measure on $[0,1]$. Take $\theta_0$ as fixed and let $\mathcal F_0$ be the trivial history. At iteration $k$, let $\mathcal F_k$ be the history before the batch, so $\theta_k$ is $\mathcal F_k$-measurable; draw $B$ independent triples with law $Q$, independently of $\mathcal F_k$, and set $\mathcal F_{k+1}$ to be the sigma-algebra generated by $\mathcal F_k$ and that batch. For a triple $w=(t,x_1,x)$, define $$\zeta(\theta,w):=2\,D_\theta v(t,x;\theta)^T\left(v(t,x;\theta)-u^c_t(x\mid x_1)\right).$$ For each $k,b$, set $\zeta_{k,b}:=\zeta(\theta_k,(T_{k,b},X_{1,k,b},X_{k,b}))$ and require it to be integrable on the joint training probability space.

Given the fixed initial parameter $\theta_0\in\Theta$ and deterministic step sizes $\eta_0,\ldots,\eta_{N-1}\ge0$, for each $k=0,\ldots,N-1$ set
$$g_k:=\frac1B\sum_{b=1}^{B}\zeta_{k,b}, \qquad \theta_{k+1}:=\theta_k-\eta_k g_k.$$
Assume all iterates $\theta_0,\ldots,\theta_N$ remain in $\Theta$ almost surely. Training uses only conditional samples and does not solve the parameterized ODE during these updates. After training, condition on the history $\mathcal F_N$, draw $X_0$ from a supplied initial probability law $\mu_0$ independently of $\mathcal F_N$, and return $X_1=\Phi^{\theta_N}_{1,0}(X_0)$, where $\Phi^{\theta_N}_{t,s}$ solves $\dot z(t)=v(t,z(t);\theta_N)$ with $z(s)=X_0$. This last step is well-defined if the field extends to an open time interval containing $[0,1]$, is jointly $C^\infty$ in time, state, and parameter, and on every compact parameter set $H\subset\Theta$ has uniform constants $L_H,M_H\ge0$ satisfying
$$\|v(t,z;\theta)-v(t,w;\theta)\|_2\le L_H\|z-w\|_2, \qquad \|v(t,0;\theta)\|_2\le M_H$$
for $t\in[0,1]$, $\theta\in H$, and $z,w\in\mathbb R^d$. Conditional on $\mathcal F_N$, the output law assigns mass $\mu_0(\{x:\Phi^{\theta_N}_{1,0}(x)\in A\})$ to each Borel set $A\subseteq\mathbb R^d$. The procedure gives no convergence guarantee for the parameter updates or equality of this output law with a desired data law.

## Source notes

The cited papers specify the conditional sample, squared regression loss, parameter update, and ODE sampling steps. This algorithm makes the sampler law, batch gradient, exact global-flow conditions, and output law explicit. Its parameter updates carry no optimization convergence guarantee, and the exact flow is a mathematical solution rather than a numerical solver with an error bound.
