## Definition

A **Stone space** is a topological space that is compact, Hausdorff, and has a
basis of clopen subsets. (Equivalently, in the literature, a compact Hausdorff
space that is totally disconnected; this library uses the clopen-basis form and
does not import the connected-component characterisation.)

For a Stone space $X$ let

$$\operatorname{Clop}(X) \;:=\; \{\,C \subseteq X : C \text{ is clopen}\,\},$$

with the Boolean operations $C \wedge C' := C \cap C'$, $C \vee C' := C \cup C'$,
$\neg C := X \setminus C$, $0 := \varnothing$, $1 := X$. These operations make
$\operatorname{Clop}(X)$ a Boolean algebra
([[def-boolean-algebra-and-boolean-ultrafilter-for-stone-duality]]): the
distributive and complement laws are the set-theoretic identities, and the
finite unions and intersections of clopen sets are clopen by the definition of a
topology.

For a Boolean algebra $B$ let

$$\operatorname{Ult}(B) \;:=\; \{\,U \subseteq B : U \text{ is a Boolean ultrafilter}\,\}$$

be its set of ultrafilters. The **ultrafilter space** $\operatorname{Ult}(B)$
carries the topology generated by the sets

$$[b] \;:=\; \{\,U \in \operatorname{Ult}(B) : b \in U\,\} \qquad (b \in B),$$

that is, the coarsest topology in which every $[b]$ is open. Two immediately
relevant observations are recorded here and proved where they are needed: each
$[b]$ is clopen, since its complement is $[\neg b]$; and the space is Hausdorff
and compact, which is the content of
[[thm-stone-representation-for-boolean-algebras]].

## Remarks

- **The trivial algebra.** If $B$ is the one-element Boolean algebra then it has no proper filters, so $\operatorname{Ult}(B) = \varnothing$; the empty space is compact, Hausdorff and has the empty basis, so it is a Stone space, and $\operatorname{Clop}(\varnothing) = \{\varnothing\}$ is the trivial algebra. This is the convention under which the duality is total.
- **Clopen basis versus total disconnectedness.** Every clopen-basis compact Hausdorff space is totally disconnected, and the converse holds for compact Hausdorff spaces; the equivalence is standard but is not needed below, since only the basis property is used.
- **Ultrafilter spaces are the prototypical Stone spaces.** The representation theorem [[thm-stone-representation-for-boolean-algebras]] and the duality [[thm-stone-duality]] show that every Stone space is homeomorphic to some $\operatorname{Ult}(B)$ and every Boolean algebra to some $\operatorname{Clop}(X)$.
