## Example

Let $V$ be a discrete valuation ring with uniformiser $\pi$. Then
$$0\subset (\pi^{n-1})/(\pi^n)\subset (\pi^{n-2})/(\pi^n)\subset\cdots\subset (\pi)/(\pi^n)\subset V/(\pi^n)$$
is a composition series, so $\ell_V(V/(\pi^n))=n$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A discrete valuation ring $V$, a uniformiser $\pi$, and an integer $n\ge1$.

[F1] A uniformiser generates the maximal ideal of a DVR
([[def-uniformising-parameter]]).

[L1] In a DVR one has $\ell_V(V/(\pi^n))=n$
([[thm-dvr-ideal-and-module-length]]).

## Verification

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Each successive quotient in the displayed filtration is generated by the class of $\pi^i$, so multiplication by $\pi^i$ identifies it with $V/(\pi)$. Thus every factor has length $1$. [F1, given, algebra]

2.1 Adding the $n$ successive factors recovers the quotient $V/(\pi^n)$, and [L1] confirms that the total length is exactly $n$. [L1, step 1.1] ∎
