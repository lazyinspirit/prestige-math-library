## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice (AC), used only through
[[thm-polynomial-hochschild-homology-is-computed-by-the-diagonal-koszul-complex]].
In the situation of
[[lem-the-first-layer-relations-in-a-closed-moy-resolution-form-a-regular-sequence]],
let $\widetilde R=\mathbb Q[x_{i,j}]$, let the first $rm$ elements of the
sequence be as there, and let the remaining $m$ elements be the closure
differences $x_{0,j}-x_{r,j}$ $(1\le j\le m)$. Put
$R':=\mathbb Q[x_{0,1},\dots,x_{0,m}]$ and let $B'(D)$ be the quotient
$\widetilde R/(\text{first }rm)$, identified in that lemma with the unreduced
tensor product $\bigotimes_{j=1}^rB'_{s_j}$; the ring $B'(D)$ is commutative
and carries the $R'$-bimodule structure whose left action is generated by the
classes of $x_{0,j}$ and whose right action is generated by the classes of
$x_{r,j}$. Then:

(a) the classes of $x_{0,1}-x_{r,1},\dots,x_{0,m}-x_{r,m}$ in $B'(D)$ are
exactly the diagonal elements $u_j=x_j^L-x_j^R$ of
[[def-diagonal-koszul-bimodule-complex-of-a-polynomial-ring]] under the two
ring maps $R'\to B'(D)$, $x_j\mapsto x_{0,j}$ and $x_j\mapsto x_{r,j}$;

(b) the Koszul complex $K(x_{0,1}-x_{r,1},\dots,x_{0,m}-x_{r,m};B'(D))$ is the
diagonal Koszul bimodule complex of $B'(D)$ over $R'$ and its homology is
$$\operatorname{Tor}^{R'^e}_h(R',B'(D))\cong HH_h(R',B'(D))\qquad(h\ge0),$$
the isomorphism being the one of
[[thm-polynomial-hochschild-homology-is-computed-by-the-diagonal-koszul-complex]]
for $M=B'(D)$;

(c) the Koszul complex of all $(r+1)m$ elements of the sequence over
$\widetilde R$ is quasi-isomorphic to the complex of (b), hence computes
$HH_\bullet(R',B'(D))$. The isomorphism is natural for maps that preserve the
two $R'$-actions.

The closure differences are not a regular sequence in general: their Koszul
homology is $HH_\bullet(R',B'(D))$, which is nonzero in several degrees, and
no acyclicity is claimed.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** a closed marked MOY resolution $D$ with $r$ wide edges and $m$ strands, the ring $\widetilde R=\mathbb Q[x_{i,j}]$, the layer-ordered sequence of [[lem-the-first-layer-relations-in-a-closed-moy-resolution-form-a-regular-sequence]], the quotient $B'(D)=\widetilde R/(\text{first }rm)$, the ring $R'=\mathbb Q[x_{0,1},\dots,x_{0,m}]$ and AC.

[L1] The first $rm$ elements form a regular sequence on $\widetilde R$ with quotient $\widetilde R/(\text{first }rm)\cong B'(D)$, the unreduced tensor product $\bigotimes_{j=1}^rB'_{s_j}$, and their Koszul complex is a free resolution of $B'(D)$; the remaining $m$ elements are the closure differences $x_{0,j}-x_{r,j}$ ([[lem-the-first-layer-relations-in-a-closed-moy-resolution-form-a-regular-sequence]]).

[L2] The diagonal Koszul bimodule complex of $R'=k[x_1,\dots,x_m]$ for $k=\mathbb Q$ is $K(u_1,\dots,u_m;R'^e)$ with $u_j=x_j^L-x_j^R$, augmented by the multiplication $\mu:R'^e\to R'$; its degree-$p$ term carries the $\binom mp$ wedge symbols and the internal degree of $\theta_j$ is $2$ when $\deg x_j=2$ ([[def-diagonal-koszul-bimodule-complex-of-a-polynomial-ring]]).

[L3] Under AC, for a field $k$, $R'=k[x_1,\dots,x_m]$ and a $k$-central $R'$-bimodule $M$ there is a natural isomorphism $HH_h(R',M)\cong H_h(\mathcal K_\bullet(M))$ with $\mathcal K_\bullet(M)=K(u_1,\dots,u_m;R'^e)\otimes_{R'^e}M$ ([[thm-polynomial-hochschild-homology-is-computed-by-the-diagonal-koszul-complex]]); the hypothesis is that the scalar actions agree, which holds for every $\mathbb Q$-algebra bimodule.

[L4] For finite sequences $\mathbf x,\mathbf y$ there is a signed chain isomorphism $K(\mathbf x,\mathbf y;A)\cong K(\mathbf x;A)\otimes_AK(\mathbf y;A)$ ([[lem-koszul-complex-concatenation-tensor-isomorphism]]).



## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Identify the surviving elements. The variables $x_{0,j}$ and $x_{r,j}$ are elements of the commutative ring $B'(D)=\widetilde R/(\text{first }rm)$; the left $R'$-action on $B'(D)$ is the algebra map $R'\to B'(D)$, $x_j\mapsto x_{0,j}$, and the right action is $x_j\mapsto x_{r,j}$, both induced by the ring structure. Hence the class of $x_{0,j}-x_{r,j}$ is the image of $u_j=x_j^L-x_j^R$ under the induced map $R'^e\to B'(D)$, and its action on $B'(D)$ by multiplication is $m\mapsto x_{0,j}m-mx_{r,j}=u_j\cdot m$. [L1, L2, given, algebra]

2.1 Reduce the full Koszul complex to the closure complex. By [L4] the Koszul complex of all $(r+1)m$ elements is the tensor product over $\widetilde R$ of the Koszul complex of the first $rm$ elements and that of the last $m$ elements. By [L1] the first factor is a free resolution of $B'(D)$, and its augmentation has acyclic mapping cone. Tensor that cone with the bounded free complex $K(\text{last};\,\widetilde R)$ and filter the total complex by the latter factor's degree. Each associated graded complex is a finite direct sum of shifts of the acyclic cone. The filtration is finite, so induction through its short exact sequences makes the tensor cone acyclic; thus tensoring the augmentation preserves quasi-isomorphism; therefore the Koszul complex of all $(r+1)m$ elements is quasi-isomorphic to $B'(D)\otimes_{\widetilde R}K(\text{last};\,\widetilde R)\cong K(\text{last};\,B'(D))$, the Koszul complex of the classes of the closure differences over the commutative ring $B'(D)$. [L1, L4, step 1.1, algebra]

2.2 Identify it with the diagonal complex. Under the identification of the tensor factors, $K(\text{last};\,B'(D))$ has degree-$p$ term the $p$-fold wedge over $B'(D)$ of the elements $x_{0,j}-x_{r,j}$ with the Koszul differential of [L4], and by step 1.1 these elements act by multiplication as $u_j=x_j^L-x_j^R$. By [L3] the diagonal Koszul bimodule complex tensored over $R'^e$ with $B'(D)$ has degree-$p$ term $\bigoplus_{|I|=p}B'(D)\theta_I$ and differential $d(m\theta_I)=\sum_r(-1)^{r-1}(x_{i_r}m-mx_{i_r})\theta_{I\setminus\{i_r\}}$, which in the commutative ring $B'(D)$ is multiplication by the classes $x_{0,j}-x_{r,j}$; both complexes therefore have the same graded terms and the same differential, so $K(\text{last};\,B'(D))$ is the diagonal Koszul bimodule complex of $B'(D)$ over $R'$. [L2, L3, L4, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Compute the homology. The bimodule $B'(D)$ is a $\mathbb Q$-algebra bimodule, hence $k$-central for $k=\mathbb Q$, and it is graded with $\deg x_{i,j}=2$. Applying [L3] to $M=B'(D)$ gives the natural isomorphism $HH_h(R',B'(D))\cong H_h(K(\text{last};\,B'(D)))$ and, through steps 2.1 and 2.2, the identification of $HH_\bullet(R',B'(D))$ with the homology of the Koszul complex of all $(r+1)m$ elements. This is the only place where AC is used. [L1, L2, L3, step 2.1, step 2.2] ∎
