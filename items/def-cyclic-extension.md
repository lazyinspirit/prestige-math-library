## Definition

Let $K/F$ be a finite Galois extension
([[def-finite-galois-extension-and-galois-group]]). It is **cyclic** when its
Galois group $\operatorname{Gal}(K/F)$ is a cyclic group.

When $\operatorname{Gal}(K/F)=\langle\sigma\rangle$ is generated by a specific
automorphism $\sigma$, that generator may be named in the body of a later theorem
or example, but it is not part of the data of the definition.
