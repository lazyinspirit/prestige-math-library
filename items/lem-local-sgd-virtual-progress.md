## Statement

Let $f=n^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^n f_j$ have an attained minimum $f^\star=f(x^\star)$,
be $L$-smooth, and be $\mu$-strongly convex with $0<\mu\le L$. Let
$x_t^1,\ldots,x_t^K$ be generated by local SGD, and define
$$ \bar x_t=\frac1K\sum_{k=1}^Kx_t^k,\qquad g_t=\frac1K\sum_{k=1}^K g_t^k(x_t^k),\qquad \bar g_t=\frac1K\sum_{k=1}^K\nabla f(x_t^k), $$
where $g_t^k$ is the batch gradient from the algorithm definition. If
$\eta_t\le1/(4L)$, then
$$ \begin{aligned} \mathbb E\|\bar x_{t+1}-x^\star\|_2^2 &\le(1-\mu\eta_t)\mathbb E\|\bar x_t-x^\star\|_2^2 +\eta_t^2\mathbb E\|g_t-\bar g_t\|_2^2\\ &\quad-\frac{\eta_t}{2}\mathbb E[f(\bar x_t)-f^\star] +\frac{2\eta_tL}{K}\sum_{k=1}^K \mathbb E\|\bar x_t-x_t^k\|_2^2. \end{aligned} $$
Expectations are over the finite product law of the sampled indices.

## Statement references

[Stich, Local SGD Converges Fast and Communicates Little, Lemma 3.1 and Appendix A](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.09767v3)

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The local-SGD iterates, the finite-sum objective and Euclidean structure in the Statement.

[F1] [[alg-local-sgd]]: at each step the local proposal is $z_{t+1}^k=x_t^k-\eta_tg_t^k(x_t^k)$. An unsynchronized worker keeps its proposal, while a synchronized worker takes the mean of all proposals.

[F2] [[lem-finite-sum-gradient-is-average]]: $\nabla f(x)=n^{-1}\sum_j\nabla f_j(x)$.

[F3] [[lem-l-smoothness-descent-inequality]]: an $L$-Lipschitz gradient gives $f(y)\le f(x)+\langle\nabla f(x),y-x\rangle+(L/2)\|y-x\|_2^2$.

[F4] [[thm-finite-jensen-inequality-in-euclidean-space]]: for a convex function, its value at a finite average is at most the average of its values.

[F5] [[def-euclidean-inner-product]]: inner products and squared norms obey the standard Euclidean algebra.

[F6] [[def-expectation-of-a-nonnegative-or-integrable-random-variable]] and [[cor-expectation-agrees-with-the-finite-weighted-sum]]: on the finite product law, expectations equal finite weighted sums.

[A1] For all $u,v\in\mathbb R^d$, $f(v)\ge f(u)+\langle\nabla f(u),v-u\rangle+ (\mu/2)\|v-u\|_2^2$, $x^\star$ is a minimizer, and $\nabla f(x^\star)=0$.

## Proof

1.1 Averaging the proposals in [F1] gives their mean $\bar x_t-\eta_tg_t$. Synchronization preserves that mean, so $\bar x_{t+1}=\bar x_t-\eta_tg_t$, whether or not this step synchronizes. Fix any past tuple of sampled indices. The current independent uniform indices have finite conditional average $\bar g_t$ by [F2]. Expanding after adding and subtracting $\eta_t\bar g_t$, then averaging over the current indices, removes the cross term and gives $\mathbb E\|\bar x_{t+1}-x^\star\|_2^2 =\mathbb E\|\bar x_t-x^\star-\eta_t\bar g_t\|_2^2+ \eta_t^2\mathbb E\|g_t-\bar g_t\|_2^2$. [F1, F2, F5, F6, given, algebra]

1.2 Fix the past tuple and write $x_k=x_t^k$. Jensen's inequality [F4]
gives $\|\bar g_t\|_2^2\le K^{-1}\sum_k\|\nabla f(x_k)\|_2^2$.
Applying [F3] at $x_k$ with $y=x_k-L^{-1}\nabla f(x_k)$ and using
$f(x^\star)\le f(y)$ yields
$\|\nabla f(x_k)\|_2^2\le2L(f(x_k)-f^\star)$. Strong convexity [A1] gives
$-\langle x_k-x^\star,\nabla f(x_k)\rangle
\le-(f(x_k)-f^\star)-(\mu/2)\|x_k-x^\star\|_2^2$. Young's inequality,
in the form $-2\langle u,v\rangle\le2L\|u\|_2^2+(2L)^{-1}\|v\|_2^2$,
therefore gives
$-2\langle\bar x_t-x_k,\nabla f(x_k)\rangle
\le2L\|\bar x_t-x_k\|_2^2+f(x_k)-f^\star$. In the expansion
$\|\bar x_t-x^\star-\eta_t\bar g_t\|_2^2=\|\bar x_t-x^\star\|_2^2
-2\eta_t\langle\bar x_t-x^\star,\bar g_t\rangle+\eta_t^2\|\bar g_t\|_2^2$,
decompose $\bar x_t-x^\star=(\bar x_t-x_k)+(x_k-x^\star)$ and average
the preceding bounds over $k$. The function-gap coefficient is
$-2\eta_t+\eta_t+2\eta_t^2L=2\eta_t(\eta_tL-\tfrac12)$; the strong-
convexity term is $-\mu\eta_tK^{-1}\sum_k\|x_k-x^\star\|_2^2$. Hence
$$ \|\bar x_t-x^\star-\eta_t\bar g_t\|_2^2 \le\|\bar x_t-x^\star\|_2^2 +\frac{2\eta_tL}{K}\sum_k\|\bar x_t-x_k\|_2^2 +2\eta_t(\eta_tL-\tfrac12)\frac1K\sum_k(f(x_k)-f^\star) -\frac{\mu\eta_t}{K}\sum_k\|x_k-x^\star\|_2^2. $$ [A1, F3, F4, F5, given, algebra]

2.1 Since $\eta_tL\le1/4$, the coefficient $2\eta_t(\eta_tL-\tfrac12)\le-\eta_t/2$. Convexity of $f$ follows from $\mu$-strong convexity in [A1]. Jensen [F4] gives $K^{-1}\sum_k f(x_k)\ge f(\bar x_t)$ and $K^{-1}\sum_k\|x_k-x^\star\|_2^2\ge\|\bar x_t-x^\star\|_2^2$. Thus step 1.2 is at most $(1-\mu\eta_t)\|\bar x_t-x^\star\|_2^2 -(\eta_t/2)(f(\bar x_t)-f^\star) +(2\eta_tL/K)\sum_k\|\bar x_t-x_k\|_2^2$. Average over the past and substitute into step 1.1 to obtain the claim. [A1, F4, step 1.1, step 1.2, given, algebra] ∎
