## Statement

Assume Countable Choice. For an actual balanced multipointed S3 knot diagram with k ordered pairs, a marked-pair insertion in the elementary configuration of the pointed-data definition has the following full filtered minus chain type over $R'=\mathbb F_2[U_1,\ldots,U_k,U_{k+1}]$:
$$\operatorname{Cone}\bigl(U_{k+1}+U_i:C[U_{k+1}]\longrightarrow C[U_{k+1}]\bigr).$$
Here C is the old actual full minus complex, i labels the old alpha cut component containing the removed connecting z-point, and the cone's source copy is shifted by $(-1,-1)$ in relative Maslov and Alexander degrees. All positive z terms are retained. For suitable good data this is the ACTUAL differential matrix, with diagonal d,d, indicated off-diagonal $U_{k+1}+U_i$ and zero reverse entry after setting every z variable to one. Independently chosen good complexes are related to it by actual finite filtered comparisons and inverse homotopies.

Restriction to any old chosen variable gives the old filtered chain type. Choosing the new variable gives the same type after its actual variable renaming; this uses polynomial divided differences and no completion. Ordinary simultaneous quotient identifies the cone with its zero-map specialization and contributes the two-dimensional factor of degrees $(0,0),(-1,-1)$. This statement concerns the actual insertion/removal along the represented knot, not adjoining a separated marked component.

The proof retains the extra closed horizontal surface in the old singly pointed case. Its count is not inferred from a zero unit-boundary count or erased by fixed-target sphere avoidance.
