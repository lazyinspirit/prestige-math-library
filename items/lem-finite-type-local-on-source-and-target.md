## Statement

Being locally of finite type is affine-local on both source and target. A
quasi-compact morphism locally of finite type is of finite type; equivalently,
over each affine target open it may be tested on a finite affine source cover.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A morphism $f:X\to S$ and affine source and target covers.

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Restricting a finite-type ring map to distinguished affine opens localizes the map and retains a finite generating set, so the condition survives affine refinement. [given]

2.1 Conversely, let $V=\operatorname{Spec}R$ be an affine target and $U=\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq f^{-1}(V)$ an affine source open. Around each point of $U$, choose an affine chart $W=\operatorname{Spec}C$ from the given finite-type source cover. First choose a principal open in $U$ contained in $W$, then a smaller principal open in $W$ contained in the first one. The latter is principal in $U$ as well: its defining function on the first open belongs to a localization of $A$, so clearing a power of the first defining function gives one element of $A$. Quasi-compactness of $U$ now supplies a finite cover $U=\bigcup_{i=1}^nD_U(a_i)$ by such common principal opens. Each $A_{a_i}$ is a localization of a finite-type $R$-algebra $C$, hence is finite type over $R$. [step 1.1, given, algebra]

3.1 Here is the needed finite-localization descent, rather than just an appeal to it. Choose $h_i\in A$ with $\sum_i h_i a_i=1$. For every $i$, choose finitely many $R$-algebra generators of $A_{a_i}$ and write each as $y_{ij}/a_i^{k_{ij}}$ with $y_{ij}\in A$. Let $B\subseteq A$ be the $R$-subalgebra generated by all $a_i,h_i,y_{ij}$. Then $B_{a_i}=A_{a_i}$ for every $i$, and the $a_i$ generate the unit ideal already in $B$. For any $x\in A/B$, localization gives $a_i^{N_i}x=0$ for some $N_i$ for each $i$. The powers $a_i^{N_i}$ also generate the unit ideal in $B$, so $x=0$. Thus $A=B$ is finitely generated over $R$. This is the finite-type part of Stacks Project, Tag `00EP`. [step 2.1, construct, algebra]

4.1 On an affine target $V$, refine any target cover to a finite cover by opens $D_V(r_j)$ that are principal also in the chosen affine target charts, using the common-principal construction of step 2.1. Base change to each $D_V(r_j)$ preserves finite type of the source charts. For an affine $U\subseteq f^{-1}(V)$, the inverse images are the principal opens $D_U(f^\#r_j)$; steps 2.1 and 3.1 make their coordinate rings finite type over $\mathcal O_S(D_V(r_j))$, hence over $\mathcal O_S(V)$. Applying step 3.1 again gives finite type on $U$. Finally, quasi-compactness of $f$ supplies a finite affine source subcover over each affine target open, so locally finite type plus quasi-compactness is exactly finite type. [step 1.1, step 2.1, step 3.1, algebra] ∎
