## Definition

Fix $W,\mathfrak h,c$ as in [[def-rational-cherednik-algebra]] and consider $H_{1,c}(W,\mathfrak h)$. By [[thm-rational-cherednik-pbw-theorem]], the subalgebra generated by $\mathfrak h^*$ is the polynomial algebra $\mathbb C[\mathfrak h^*]=S(\mathfrak h^*)$, and the subalgebra generated by $\mathfrak h$ is $S(\mathfrak h)$.

The **category O**, denoted $\mathcal O_c(W,\mathfrak h)$, is the full subcategory of left $H_{1,c}(W,\mathfrak h)$-modules $M$ satisfying:

1. $M$ is finitely generated as an $S(\mathfrak h^*)$-module.
2. The augmentation ideal $S(\mathfrak h)_{>0}$ acts locally nilpotently: for every $m\in M$, there is $N\ge1$ such that every homogeneous polynomial in $S(\mathfrak h)$ of degree at least $N$ kills $m$.

Since $S(\mathfrak h)$ is generated by a finite basis of $\mathfrak h$ and its elements commute, the second condition is equivalent to each $y\in\mathfrak h$ acting locally nilpotently. Morphisms in $\mathcal O_c(W,\mathfrak h)$ are the $H_{1,c}(W,\mathfrak h)$-module homomorphisms.

## Remarks

The category depends on $c$ and uses $t=1$. No semisimplicity of $H_{1,c}$, $W$-module decompositions beyond finite-dimensional $W$-representations, or regularity of KZ monodromy is built into this definition.
