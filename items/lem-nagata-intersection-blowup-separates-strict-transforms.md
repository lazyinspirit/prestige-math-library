## Statement

Let $X$ be Noetherian and $Y_1,\ldots,Y_r$ a finite family of closed subschemes, with ideals $\mathcal I_i$. Put $Z=\bigcap_iY_i$, whose ideal is $\mathcal J=\sum_i\mathcal I_i$, and form $\beta:X'=\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal J}(X)\to X$. Write $Y_i'$ for the scheme-theoretic closure in $X'$ of $Y_i\setminus|Z|$, using the unchanged open $X\setminus|Z|$. Then
$$\bigcap_iY_i'=\varnothing.$$

More precisely, on an affine base open and any blowup chart $A[J/a]$ with $a\in I_i$, the strict transform $Y_i'$ is empty. For two subschemes, the strict transform of $Y_1$ is therefore contained in the union of the charts belonging to local sections of $\mathcal I_2$, and conversely. The map is proper and unchanged off $Z$; in particular it preserves any open disjoint from $Z$. Empty schemes, single-member and empty families, and zero and unit ideals are included.

[Conrad, Lemma1.4, internal pp.9–10](https://math.stanford.edu/~conrad/papers/nagatafinal.pdf)

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The Noetherian scheme and the finite closed-subscheme family.

[F1] Blowup charts are $A[J/a]\subseteq A_a$, with regular chart generator $a$, canonical overlaps and localization compatibility. The image of $J$ is generated by $a$ there. [[lem-nagata-finite-affine-blowup-chart-gluing]]

[F2] The blowup is proper and the identity away from its center; the zero ideal gives the empty blowup and the unit ideal the identity. [[lem-nagata-admissible-blowup-universal-property-and-properness]]

[F3] Scheme-theoretic intersection of closed subschemes is defined by the sum of their ideals. [[def-closed-immersion-schemes]] [[def-sum-and-product-of-ideals]]

## Proof

1.1 Work on $\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq X$, writing $I_i$ and $J=\sum I_i$ for the ideals. On a chart $B=A[J/a]\subseteq A_a$, the unchanged open complement of the center is precisely $D(a)\subseteq\operatorname{Spec}B$. Indeed $JB=aB$ by [F1], and the inverse image of the complement of $V(J)$ is the locus where this ideal is unit, namely $D(a)$; there $B_a=A_a$. Thus the restriction of $Y_i$ off the center in this chart has ring $A_a/I_iA_a$. Its closure has kernel ideal $K_i=\ker(B\to A_a/I_iA_a)$, since this kernel is exactly the largest ideal vanishing on that restriction. This ideal defines a coherent closed subscheme because $B$ is Noetherian. Its kernel description commutes with further principal localization by exact fraction calculations, so it is the local scheme-theoretic closure and the chart descriptions glue. [F1, F3, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 If $a\in I_i$, then $I_iA_a=A_a$ and $K_i=B$. Consequently $Y_i'$ is empty on that chart. Choose finite generating families for the finitely many $I_i$ on this affine open. Their union generates $J$, so the associated charts cover $X'$ by [F1]. Each such chart misses at least its own $Y_i'$. Their full intersection is therefore empty, including its scheme structure, since an ideal of one member is already unit on each covering chart. For $r=2$, these same charts split into the two generating families; the strict transform of $Y_1$ misses every chart in the first family and hence lies in the open union of charts in the second. The reversed assertion follows identically. [F1, F3, step 1.1, construct, algebra]

3.1 Properness and the unchanged complement follow from [F2]. A single member has center equal to that member, so its strict transform is empty by the same calculation. For an empty family the intersection convention is $Z=X$, the sum ideal is zero, and $X'$ is empty by [F2]; the intersection of the empty family inside this empty ambient scheme is empty as asserted. Zero or unit ideals among a nonempty family were permitted in every chart calculation, and empty charts simply contribute no points. If $X$ is empty all statements are immediate. This proves every clause. [F2, step 2.1, algebra] ∎
