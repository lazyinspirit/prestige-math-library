## Definition

Let $E$ be a complete discretely valued field with finite residue field of
characteristic $p$, let $\ell\ne p$, and fix an isomorphism
$\overline{\mathbb Q}_{\ell}\cong\mathbb C$. Let $G$ be a smooth connected
affine reductive algebraic group over $E$, meaning that its connected unipotent
radical is trivial, and assume it is quasi-split, meaning that it has an
$E$-defined Borel subgroup. Fix a Whittaker datum $(B,\psi)$, where $B$ is an
$E$-defined maximal connected solvable subgroup and $U$ is its largest
connected normal unipotent subgroup, and $\psi:U(E)\to\mathbb C^\times$ is a
character whose orbit under conjugation by a maximal torus in $B$ has maximal
dimension. A standard parabolic is a parabolic subgroup containing $B$; a
standard Levi is its Levi factor containing a fixed maximal torus of $B$. For
a standard Levi subgroup $M\subseteq G$, write $U_M=U\cap M$ and let
$\psi_M$ be the restriction of $\psi$ to $U_M(E)$, up to $M(E)$-conjugacy.

Write $\operatorname{Rep}(M(E))^\heartsuit$ for the abelian category of smooth
complex representations of $M(E)$, and $\operatorname{Rep}(M(E))$ for its
derived stable $\infty$-category. A smooth irreducible representation $\sigma$
of $M(E)$ is **supercuspidal** if it is not a subquotient of parabolic
induction from any proper parabolic subgroup of $M$ defined over $E$. For a
parabolic $P=LV_P\subseteq M$, normalized parabolic induction from $P(E)$
multiplies the inducing representation by the square root $\delta_P^{1/2}$
of the modulus character before induction; $\delta_P$ is the positive
character describing how a left Haar measure changes under conjugation by
$P(E)$. Let $M^1$ be the subgroup generated by the compact subgroups of
$M(E)$; an **unramified character** of $M(E)$ is a smooth character trivial
on $M^1$. Two supercuspidal pairs $(M,\sigma)$ and $(M',\sigma')$ for $G$ are
**inertially equivalent** if a $G(E)$-conjugation carries $M$ to $M'$ and
carries $\sigma$ to an unramified twist of $\sigma'$. The associated
**Bernstein block** $\operatorname{Rep}(G(E))_{[M,\sigma]}$ is the full
subcategory of smooth representations whose irreducible subquotients have
supercuspidal support in that inertial class; $\operatorname{Rep}(G)$ denotes
the derived stable $\infty$-category associated with the Bernstein
decomposition.

A supercuspidal representation $\sigma$ of $M(E)$ is **$\psi_M$-generic** if
there is a nonzero $M(E)$-map
$\operatorname{c-Ind}_{U_M(E)}^{M(E)}\psi_M\to\sigma$. An inertial class
$[M,\sigma]$ is generic when $\sigma$ is $\psi_M$-generic. The **generic
Bernstein direct factor** is
$$\operatorname{Rep}(G)^{\mathrm{gen}}:=\prod_{[M,\sigma]\ \mathrm{generic}}\operatorname{Rep}(G)_{[M,\sigma]}.$$
It is the direct factor of the full representation category consisting of
the generic Bernstein blocks, and it need not be the entire category.
