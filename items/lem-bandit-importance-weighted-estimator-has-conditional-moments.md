## Statement

In the bandit importance-weighted estimator definition, fix a round $t$ and arm $i$, and write $p=P_{t,i}>0$ and $y=y_{t,i}$. Then
$$
\mathbb E[\widehat Y_{t,i}\mid\mathcal F_{t-1}]=y_{t,i},\qquad \mathbb E[\widehat Y_{t,i}^{2}\mid\mathcal F_{t-1}]=\frac{y_{t,i}^2}{P_{t,i}}.
$$
With conditional variance defined by $\operatorname{Var}(Z\mid\mathcal F_{t-1})=\mathbb E[(Z-\mathbb E[Z\mid\mathcal F_{t-1}])^2\mid\mathcal F_{t-1}]$, one also has
$$
\operatorname{Var}(\widehat Y_{t,i}\mid\mathcal F_{t-1})=\frac{y_{t,i}^2(1-P_{t,i})}{P_{t,i}}.
$$

## Statement references

[[def-bandit-importance-weighted-estimator]]: the arm probabilities, observed loss, and importance-weighted loss estimate used here.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A finite arm set $[k]$, a pre-action history at round $t$, a reward vector $x_t\in[0,1]^k$, and a distribution $P_t\in\Delta_k$ with $P_{t,i}>0$.

[F1] [[def-bandit-importance-weighted-estimator]]: given the pre-action history, $\mathbb P(A_t=i\mid\mathcal F_{t-1})=P_{t,i}$, $Y_t=y_{t,A_t}$, and $\widehat Y_{t,i}=\mathbf1\{A_t=i\}Y_t/P_{t,i}$.

[F2] [[def-conditional-expectation-given-a-sigma-algebra]]: a conditional-expectation version is measurable and integrable and has the same integral as the original variable on every event in the conditioning sigma-algebra.

[F3] [[def-expectation-of-a-nonnegative-or-integrable-random-variable]]: expectation of an integrable random variable is its finite Lebesgue integral.

[F4] [[def-filtration-and-filtered-probability-space]]: a filtration consists of nested sub-sigma-algebras of the ambient sigma-algebra. In [[def-bandit-importance-weighted-estimator]], $\mathcal F_{t-1}$ is specifically generated by the complete histories before the round-$t$ action.

## Proof

1.1 Fix a positive-probability atom $H$ of the finite pre-action history. On $H$, $y_{t,i}$ and $P_{t,i}$ are fixed. By [F1], $\mathbb P(H\cap\{A_t=i\})=P_{t,i}\mathbb P(H)$; the estimator is $y_{t,i}/P_{t,i}$ on that event and zero on the rest of $H$. Hence $\int_H\widehat Y_{t,i}\,d\mathbb P=y_{t,i}\mathbb P(H)$. The function with value $y_{t,i}$ on each history atom is measurable and integrable, and finite additivity gives the same integral identity on every event in $\mathcal F_{t-1}$. By [F2], it is a conditional-expectation version, proving $\mathbb E[\widehat Y_{t,i}\mid\mathcal F_{t-1}]=y_{t,i}$. [F1, F2, F3, F4]

1.2 On the same atom $H$, $\widehat Y_{t,i}^{\,2}$ is $y_{t,i}^2/P_{t,i}^2$ on $H\cap\{A_t=i\}$ and zero otherwise. Thus $\int_H\widehat Y_{t,i}^{\,2}\,d\mathbb P=(y_{t,i}^2/P_{t,i}^2)P_{t,i}\mathbb P(H)=(y_{t,i}^2/P_{t,i})\mathbb P(H)$. Extending over the finite atoms and applying [F2] proves $\mathbb E[\widehat Y_{t,i}^{\,2}\mid\mathcal F_{t-1}]=y_{t,i}^2/P_{t,i}$. [F1, F2, F3, algebra]

2.1 At a fixed history, the centered estimator has value $y_{t,i}(1-P_{t,i})/P_{t,i}$ with probability $P_{t,i}$ and value $-y_{t,i}$ with probability $1-P_{t,i}$, by step 1.1. Its conditional variance is therefore $P_{t,i}[y_{t,i}(1-P_{t,i})/P_{t,i}]^2+(1-P_{t,i})y_{t,i}^2=y_{t,i}^2(1-P_{t,i})/P_{t,i}$. [F1, F2, F4, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
