## Example

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $n\ge2$,
$\mathfrak g=\mathfrak{sl}_n(\mathbb C)=\{A\in M_n(\mathbb C):\operatorname{tr}A=0\}$
([[def-classical-complex-matrix-lie-algebras]]), let
$\mathfrak h$ be the diagonal traceless matrices, let
$\varepsilon_1,\dots,\varepsilon_n\in\mathfrak h^*$ be the coordinate
functionals $\varepsilon_i(H)=H_{ii}$, and let the positive system be the
roots $\varepsilon_i-\varepsilon_j$ with $i<j$, with base
$\alpha_j=\varepsilon_j-\varepsilon_{j+1}$
([[prop-root-systems-of-the-classical-complex-lie-algebras]]). Then the
standard module $\mathbb C^n$ with its natural action is irreducible of highest
weight $\omega_1=\varepsilon_1$, and its dual is irreducible of highest weight
$\omega_{n-1}=-\varepsilon_n$
([[def-fundamental-weights]]).

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The Axiom of Choice, such $\mathfrak g$, its diagonal Cartan $\mathfrak h$, the matrix units $E_{ij}$, the coordinate functionals $\varepsilon_i$, the positive system $\{\varepsilon_i-\varepsilon_j:i<j\}$ with simple roots $\alpha_j=\varepsilon_j-\varepsilon_{j+1}$, the Killing form $B$ ([[def-killing-form-of-a-finite-dimensional-lie-algebra]]), the standard module $V=\mathbb C^n$ with basis $e_1,\dots,e_n$, and its dual $V^*$ with dual basis $e_1^*,\dots,e_n^*$.

[A1] The Axiom of Choice is assumed; it enters through the root-space suppliers [L1] and the highest-weight theory [L4] ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]).

[L1] The roots of $\mathfrak g$ with respect to $\mathfrak h$ are the functionals $\varepsilon_i-\varepsilon_j$, $i\ne j$, with root spaces $\mathbb CE_{ij}$; each is one-dimensional and the root set is a reduced crystallographic Euclidean root system of type $A_{n-1}$ ([[prop-root-systems-of-the-classical-complex-lie-algebras]]).

[L2] For $\alpha=\varepsilon_i-\varepsilon_j$ the coroot is $h_\alpha=E_{ii}-E_{jj}$: on diagonal traceless $X=\operatorname{diag}(x_1,\dots,x_n)$ one has $B(X,X)=\operatorname{tr}(\operatorname{ad}_X^2)=\sum_{i\ne j}(x_i-x_j)^2=2n\operatorname{tr}(X^2)$, so $B(E_{ii}-E_{jj},H)=2n\operatorname{tr}((E_{ii}-E_{jj})H)=2n(H_{ii}-H_{jj})$ and the normalisation $h_\alpha=2H_\alpha/\alpha(H_\alpha)$ gives $h_\alpha=E_{ii}-E_{jj}$ ([[def-coroot-of-a-lie-algebra-root]]).

[L3] The fundamental weights are the functionals dual to the simple coroots: $\omega_k(h_{\alpha_j})=\delta_{kj}$, and the simple coroots form a basis of $\mathfrak h$ ([[def-fundamental-weights]], [[prop-the-roots-form-a-reduced-crystallographic-euclidean-root-system]]).

[L4] A nonzero weight vector killed by all positive root vectors is a highest-weight vector of the module it generates; every finite-dimensional irreducible module has a unique highest weight ([[def-highest-weight-vector-and-highest-weight-module]], [[def-weight-and-weight-space-of-a-lie-algebra-representation]], [[thm-highest-weight-classification-of-finite-dimensional-irreducible-representations]]).

## Verification

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 The action on $V$ is $H\cdot e_i=H_{ii}e_i$ and $E_{ij}\cdot e_l=\delta_{jl}e_i$; hence the weights of $V$ are $\varepsilon_1,\dots,\varepsilon_n$, each with one-dimensional weight space, and $e_1$ is killed by every positive root vector $E_{ij}$ with $i<j$, since $j>i\ge1$ forces $j\ne1$. [L1, given, A1]

1.2 $V$ is irreducible: if $0\ne v=\sum_iv_ie_i$ and $v_l\ne0$, then $E_{il}\cdot v=v_le_i$ for every $i$, so the submodule generated by $v$ contains every basis vector and equals $V$. [given]

1.3 For the dual module the action is $(X\cdot\varphi)(v)=-\varphi(X\cdot v)$, so the weights of $V^*$ are $-\varepsilon_1,\dots,-\varepsilon_n$ on the dual basis vectors $e_i^*$; the vector $e_n^*$ is killed by every positive root vector: $(E_{ij}\cdot e_n^*)(v)=-e_n^*(E_{ij}v)=-e_n^*(v_je_i)=-v_j\delta_{in}=0$ because $i<j\le n$ gives $i\ne n$. [given]

2.1 By [L2] and [L3], $\varepsilon_1(h_{\alpha_j})=\varepsilon_1(E_{jj}-E_{j+1,j+1})=\delta_{1j}=\omega_1(h_{\alpha_j})$ for every $j$, and the simple coroots span $\mathfrak h$; hence $\varepsilon_1=\omega_1$, so $V$ is irreducible with highest weight $\omega_1$ by steps 1.1, 2.1 and [L4]. [L2, L3, L4, step 1.1, step 1.2]

2.2 $V^*$ is irreducible: if $0\ne W\subseteq V^*$ is a submodule, then its annihilator $W^\perp\subseteq V$ is a submodule with $\dim W^\perp=n-\dim W<n$, hence $W^\perp=0$ by step 1.2 and $W=V^*$. [given, step 1.2]

3.1 Finally $-\varepsilon_n(h_{\alpha_j})=-(E_{jj}-E_{j+1,j+1})_{nn}=\delta_{j,n-1}=\omega_{n-1}(h_{\alpha_j})$ for every $j$, so $-\varepsilon_n=\omega_{n-1}$ by [L3]; hence $V^*$ is irreducible of highest weight $\omega_{n-1}$ by steps 1.3, 2.2 and [L4]. [L3, L4, step 1.3, step 2.2] ∎
