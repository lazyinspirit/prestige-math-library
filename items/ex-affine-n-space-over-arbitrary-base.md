## Example

On $S=\operatorname{Spec}A$, define $\mathbf A^n_S$ as
$\operatorname{Spec}A[t_1,\ldots,t_n]$. The structure map is finite type,
generated by the $n$ variables, and these constructions agree under
localization of $A$, so glue over an arbitrary base. At $n=0$ the polynomial
algebra is $A$ and $\mathbf A^0_S=S$.
