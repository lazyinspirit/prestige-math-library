## Definition

Fix a minimal realization of $A$ using [[prop-minimal-realizations-exist-and-are-unique-up-to-isomorphism]]. The **universal contragredient algebra** $\widetilde{\mathfrak g}(A)$ is the free Lie algebra on a basis of $\mathfrak h$ and symbols $e_i,f_i$, modulo $[h,h^\prime]=0$, $[h,e_i]=\alpha_i(h)e_i$, $[h,f_i]=-\alpha_i(h)f_i$ and $[e_i,f_j]=\delta_{ij}h_i$, for all $h,h^\prime\in\mathfrak h$.

The free algebra is constructed in [[lem-free-lie-construction-for-finite-kac-moody-generators]]. Any linear map on $\mathfrak h$ and images of the symbols satisfying these relations extend uniquely to a Lie homomorphism from this quotient. Give $h,e_i,f_i$ degrees $0,\alpha_i,-\alpha_i$ in [[def-kac-moody-root-lattice-height-and-positive-cone]]; all relations are homogeneous. Write $\widetilde{\mathfrak n}^+$ and $\widetilde{\mathfrak n}^-$ for the subalgebras generated by the $e_i$ and $f_i$. Their freeness and injectivity of the Cartan map are justified in [[prop-contragredient-algebra-has-a-triangular-decomposition]], not imposed as additional relations.
