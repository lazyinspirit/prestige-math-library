## Definition

Let $F=\mathbb Q(q)$ with $q$ an indeterminate, let $N\ge1$, and put $K_i=L_iL_{i+1}^{-1}$ for $1\le i<N$. Start with the free associative $F$-algebra on $L_j,L_j^{-1}$ ($1\le j\le N$) and $E_i,F_i$ ($1\le i<N$), and quotient by the two-sided ideal generated by the following relations:

1. $L_jL_j^{-1}=L_j^{-1}L_j=1$ and $L_jL_k=L_kL_j$ for all $j,k$.
2. $L_jE_iL_j^{-1}=q^{\delta_{j,i}-\delta_{j,i+1}}E_i$ and $L_jF_iL_j^{-1}=q^{-\delta_{j,i}+\delta_{j,i+1}}F_i$.
3. $[E_i,F_j]=\delta_{ij}(K_i-K_i^{-1})/(q-q^{-1})$.
4. $[E_i,E_j]=[F_i,F_j]=0$ when $|i-j|>1$.
5. For $|i-j|=1$, $E_i^2E_j-(q+q^{-1})E_iE_jE_i+E_jE_i^2=0$ and $F_i^2F_j-(q+q^{-1})F_iF_jF_i+F_jF_i^2=0$.

Here $[x,y]=xy-yx$. The free algebra and quotient construction are those of [[lem-hh-free-associative-ring-and-relations-descent]]; this definition asserts existence of the presented associative algebra, not the well-definedness of structural maps on its quotient.

On $V_N$ with ordered basis $e_1,\ldots,e_N$, prescribe the candidate matrices
$$L_je_a=q^{\delta_{ja}}e_a,\qquad E_ie_a=\delta_{a,i+1}e_i,\qquad F_ie_a=\delta_{a,i}e_{i+1}.$$
Prescribe candidate coproduct and counit values by
$$\Delta(L_j)=L_j\otimes L_j,\quad \Delta(E_i)=E_i\otimes K_i+1\otimes E_i,\quad \Delta(F_i)=F_i\otimes1+K_i^{-1}\otimes F_i,$$
and $\epsilon(L_j^{\pm1})=1$, $\epsilon(E_i)=\epsilon(F_i)=0$. The following relation-descent lemma proves that these assignments define the vector representation and bialgebra structure, and hence the tensor actions used below.
