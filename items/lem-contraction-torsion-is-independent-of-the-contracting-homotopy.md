## Statement

Let $C$ be a bounded finite based free right $R$-chain complex over an associative unital ring $R$, with displayed bases $B_{\mathrm{odd}},B_{\mathrm{even}}$ of equal finite size, and let $s$ and $t$ be two chain contractions of $C$ with $ds+sd=\mathrm{id}=dt+td$. Then
$$\tau_s(C)=\tau_t(C)\in\tilde K_1(R),$$
where $\tau_s,\tau_t$ are the contraction torsions of [[def-finite-based-free-chain-complex-and-its-contraction-torsion]]. Their common value is written $\tau(C)$ and called the torsion of the based complex $C$. The equality uses no choice and no further hypothesis on the contraction.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A bounded finite based free right $R$-chain complex $C$ with two chain contractions $s,t$ and displayed bases of $C_{\mathrm{odd}},C_{\mathrm{even}}$ of equal size.

[F1] The contraction torsion is $\tau_s(C)=[A_s]$ with $A_s$ the matrix of $(d+s)_{\mathrm{odd}}:C_{\mathrm{odd}}\to C_{\mathrm{even}}$ in the degree-ordered bases, viewed in $\tilde K_1(R)=K_1(R)/\langle[-1]\rangle$ ([[def-finite-based-free-chain-complex-and-its-contraction-torsion]]).

[F2] For two contractions $s,t$ the parity lemma gives $[(d+s)_{\mathrm{odd}}]=-[(d+t)_{\mathrm{even}}]$ in $K_1(R)$, where $(d+t)_{\mathrm{even}}:C_{\mathrm{even}}\to C_{\mathrm{odd}}$ is the even-to-odd component ([[lem-parity-map-of-a-finite-contracted-complex-is-invertible]]).

[F3] $\tilde K_1(R)$ is the quotient of $K_1(R)$ by the subgroup generated by $[-1]$, so classes equal in $K_1(R)$ remain equal in $\tilde K_1(R)$ ([[def-k-one-of-a-ring-and-the-whitehead-group-of-a-discrete-group]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 By the parity lemma both $(d+s)_{\mathrm{odd}}$ and $(d+t)_{\mathrm{odd}}$ are isomorphisms of right $R$-modules, so in the displayed bases of equal size they have invertible square matrices $A_s$ and $A_t$, and $B_t$, the matrix of $(d+t)_{\mathrm{even}}$, is invertible as well. [given, F1, F2]

2.1 Applying [F2] to the pair $(s,t)$ gives $[A_s]=-[(d+t)_{\mathrm{even}}]=-[B_t]$, and applying it to the pair $(t,t)$ gives $[A_t]=-[B_t]$; hence $[A_s]=[A_t]$ in $K_1(R)$. [F2, step 1.1]

3.1 Reducing this equality along the quotient map $K_1(R)\to\tilde K_1(R)$ of [F3] gives $\tau_s(C)=[A_s]=[A_t]=\tau_t(C)$ in $\tilde K_1(R)$, so the two contractions define the same torsion class; no contraction-dependent data remains, and the argument used only the displayed bases and the two contraction identities. [F1, F3, step 2.1] ∎
