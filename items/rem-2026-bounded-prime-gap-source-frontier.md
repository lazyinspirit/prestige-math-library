## Remark

As of the R2 source review dated 2026-10-23, the published unconditional theorem in this library is $H_1\le246$. The review located separate 2026 source claims of $H_1\le240$ (Stadlmann), $H_1\le236$ (Kintali), $H_1\le212$ (Charton et al.), $H_1\le186$ (OpenAI), and $H_1\le182$ (Sharifi–OpenAI). These are source-status claims and are not promoted here to established theorem suppliers: the 186 and 212 formal developments retain explicit analytic or numerical hypotheses; the 182 bundle reports a new analytic chain and finite comparisons whose production certificate was not replayed in the R2 review. The twin-prime conjecture remains open.
