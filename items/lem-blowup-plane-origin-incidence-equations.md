## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice, inherited from the Proj constructions used below
([[def-axiom-of-choice]]). Let $B$ be a commutative ring with $1$,
$X=\operatorname{Spec}B[x,y]=\mathbb A^2_B$ and $I=(x,y)\subseteq B[x,y]$.
With homogeneous coordinates $u,v$ on $\mathbb P^1_B$, the blowup
$\operatorname{Bl}_I X$ of [[def-blowup-scheme-along-ideal]] is
$V(xv-yu)\subseteq X\times_B\mathbb P^1_B$, and the projection is its
structural morphism. Its two standard charts are
$\operatorname{Spec}B[x,T]$ with $y=xT$ and $\operatorname{Spec}B[y,U]$ with
$x=yU$; their overlap inverts $T$ and $U$ with $TU=1$. The exceptional
subscheme is $V(x)$ and $V(y)$ respectively and is isomorphic to
$\mathbb P^1_B$. In particular this holds over any field $B=k$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A commutative ring $B$, the polynomial ring $B[x,y]$, the ideal $I=(x,y)$, the Rees algebra $R(I)=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}I^nt^n$ ([[def-rees-algebra-ideal-sheaf]]), the blowup $\operatorname{Bl}_I\operatorname{Spec}B[x,y]=\operatorname{Proj}_{B[x,y]}R(I)$ ([[def-blowup-scheme-along-ideal]]), and the projective line $\mathbb P^1_B$ with standard charts $U_0=\operatorname{Spec}B[t_0]$, $U_1=\operatorname{Spec}B[t_1]$ glued by $t_0\mapsto1/t_1$ ([[def-relative-projective-space-standard-charts]]).

[F1] [[thm-affine-blowup-standard-charts]]: For $I=(f_0,\dots,f_r)$ the standard opens $D_+(f_it)=\operatorname{Spec}A[I/f_i]$ cover $\operatorname{Bl}_I\operatorname{Spec}A$, with overlap identifications given by the ratios $(f_jt)/(f_it)$.

[F2] [[lem-affine-blowup-algebra-properties]]: For $a\in I$ the affine blowup algebra $A[I/a]=(R(I))_{(a)}$ has $IA[I/a]=aA[I/a]$ with $a$ a nonzerodivisor and $(A[I/a])_a=A_a$, and is independent of the chosen generating set; for $I=(a,a_1,\dots,a_r)$ the surjection $A[x_1,\dots,x_r]/(ax_i-a_i)\to A[I/a]$, $x_i\mapsto a_i/a$, has kernel the $a$-power torsion.

[F3] [[def-relative-projective-space-standard-charts]], [[thm-projective-space-as-proj]]: $\mathbb P^1_B$ is the gluing of its two standard charts, and for every commutative ring $A$ there is a canonical isomorphism $\operatorname{Proj}A[u,v]\cong\mathbb P^1_A$, natural in $A$; in particular $\mathbb P^1_{B[x,y]}\cong X\times_B\mathbb P^1_B$ over $\operatorname{Spec}B$.

[F4] [[thm-closed-subschemes-projective-space-homogeneous-ideals]]: A homogeneous element $f$ of $A[u,v]$ of degree $d>0$ cuts out the closed subscheme $V_+(f)=\operatorname{Proj}\bigl(A[u,v]/(f)\bigr) \hookrightarrow\mathbb P^1_A$ ([[def-closed-immersion-schemes]]), whose intersection with the standard open $D_+(u)$ is $\operatorname{Spec}\bigl(A[u,v]_{(u)}/(f/u^d)\bigr)$ ([[def-standard-open-proj]]).

[F5] [[def-exceptional-divisor-blowup]]: The exceptional subscheme is the scheme-theoretic inverse image of the center, cut out by its inverse-image ideal.

## Proof

1.1 The graded $B[x,y]$-algebra homomorphism $B[x,y][u,v]\to R(I)$ with $u\mapsto xt$, $v\mapsto yt$ is surjective, since $I^n=(x,y)^n$ is generated by the monomials $x^{n-i}y^i$, and its kernel is $(xv-yu)$: the degree-zero kernel is zero, and for a homogeneous $F=\sum_{i=0}^nc_iu^{n-i}v^i$ with $n\ge1$ and $F(x,y)=0$, reduction modulo $x$ gives $c_ny^n\equiv0\pmod x$, so $c_n=xd$ because $y$ is a nonzerodivisor modulo $x$ in the polynomial ring $B[x,y]$; then $F-dv^{n-1}(xv-yu)=uG$ with $G$ homogeneous of degree $n-1$, and $0=F(x,y)=xG(x,y)$ forces $G(x,y)=0$ because $x$ is a nonzerodivisor, so induction on $n$ gives $F\in(xv-yu)$; conversely $xv-yu\mapsto0$, and thus $R(I)\cong B[x,y][u,v]/(xv-yu)$ as graded $B[x,y]$-algebras. [F1]

2.1 Consequently $\operatorname{Bl}_I X=\operatorname{Proj}_{B[x,y]}\bigl(B[x,y][u,v]/(xv-yu)\bigr)$, and by [F4] with $A=B[x,y]$ this is the closed subscheme $V(xv-yu)$ of $\mathbb P^1_{B[x,y]}\cong X\times_B\mathbb P^1_B$ of [F3]; under this identification the structural morphism of the blowup is the projection to $X$, and the standard charts of [F1] are $D_+(u)\cap V(xv-yu)$ and $D_+(v)\cap V(xv-yu)$. [F1, F3, F4, step 1.1]

3.1 Computing the two charts by [F4]: on $D_+(u)$ the dehomogenised equation is $x(v/u)-y=0$ in $B[x,y,v/u]$, so the chart ring is $B[x,T]$ with $T=v/u$ and $y=xT$; on $D_+(v)$ it is the quotient of $B[x,y,u/v]$ by $y(u/v)-x$, that is $B[y,U]$ with $U=u/v$ and $x=yU$. On the overlap $D_+(uv)$ both $u$ and $v$ are invertible, so $T=v/u$ and $U=u/v$ are mutually inverse units, $TU=1$, and the two chart rings agree on the overlap as localisations $B[x,T]_T$ and $B[y,U]_U$ under $T\mapsto U^{-1}$. [F4, step 2.1]

4.1 By [F5], the exceptional subscheme is the inverse image of the origin $V(x,y)$; by [F2] its ideal on the first chart is $IB[x,T]=(x,xT)=(x)$, so $E\cap D_+(u)=V(x)=\operatorname{Spec}B[T]$, and on the second chart it is $IB[y,U]=(yU,y)=(y)$, so $E\cap D_+(v)=V(y)=\operatorname{Spec}B[U]$. On the overlap the rings $B[T]_T=B[T,T^{-1}]$ and $B[U]_U=B[U,U^{-1}]$ are identified by $T\mapsto U^{-1}$, which is exactly the gluing datum of the standard charts $U_0,U_1$ of $\mathbb P^1_B$ in [F3]; hence $E\cong\mathbb P^1_B$. [F2, F3, F5, step 3.1]

5.1 Assembling steps 2.1, 3.1 and 4.1: the blowup is $V(xv-yu)\subseteq X\times_B\mathbb P^1_B$ with the projection as structural morphism, its charts are $\operatorname{Spec}B[x,T]$ with $y=xT$ and $\operatorname{Spec}B[y,U]$ with $x=yU$ glued by $TU=1$, and the exceptional subscheme is $\mathbb P^1_B$; no hypothesis on $B$ beyond commutativity was used, so the statement specialises to any field $B=k$. [F3, step 2.1, step 3.1, step 4.1] ∎

## Remarks

- The proof uses only that $x$ is a nonzerodivisor of $B[x,y]$ and that $y$ is a nonzerodivisor modulo $x$; neither $B$ a domain nor $B$ a field is needed, and the zero ring gives the empty blowup on both sides.
- The equation $xv-yu=0$ is the incidence relation of the point $(x,y)$ and the line $[u:v]$, which is why the blowup is described as the incidence scheme; the strict transform computations of this page use this explicit presentation in the two charts.
