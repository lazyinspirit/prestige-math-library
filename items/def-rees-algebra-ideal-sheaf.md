## Definition

Assume the Axiom of Choice, inherited from the quasi-coherence suppliers
used below ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]).

Let $X$ be a scheme and let $\mathcal I\subseteq\mathcal O_X$ be a
quasi-coherent ideal sheaf of finite type
([[def-quasi-coherent-ideal-sheaf]]). Put $\mathcal I^0:=\mathcal O_X$, and
for $n\ge1$ let $\mathcal I^n$ be the $n$-fold product of the ideal sheaf
$\mathcal I$ inside $\mathcal O_X$, that is, the image of the multiplication
map $\mathcal I^{\otimes n}\to\mathcal O_X$ (equivalently, the ideal sheaf
generated by all local products of $n$ sections of $\mathcal I$). The
**Rees algebra sheaf** of $\mathcal I$ is the sheaf of graded
$\mathcal O_X$-algebras

$$\mathcal R(\mathcal I):=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}\mathcal I^n,$$

whose degree-$n$ piece is the ideal sheaf $\mathcal I^n$, whose multiplication
$\mathcal I^m\otimes_{\mathcal O_X}\mathcal I^n\to\mathcal I^{m+n}$ is induced
by multiplication in $\mathcal O_X$, and whose unit is the identification
$\mathcal O_X=\mathcal I^0$. Since multiplication of ideals is associative
and commutative and $\mathcal I^m\mathcal I^n\subseteq\mathcal I^{m+n}$ with
equality for the product ideal, $\mathcal R(\mathcal I)$ is a commutative
graded $\mathcal O_X$-algebra with $\mathcal R(\mathcal I)_0=\mathcal O_X$
and $\mathcal R(\mathcal I)_1=\mathcal I$.

The construction is local on $X$ and agrees with the affine Rees algebra:
if $U=\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq X$ is affine and
$\mathcal I|_U=\widetilde I$ for an ideal $I\subseteq A$
([[def-quasi-coherent-ideal-sheaf]]), then
$\mathcal I^n|_U=\widetilde{I^n}$ for every $n\ge0$, and taking sections on
$U$ gives the affine Rees algebra $\bigoplus_{n\ge0}I^nt^n$ of
([[def-rees-algebra-and-rees-module]]) with its degree-$n$ piece $I^n$.
Two affine covers therefore glue to canonically isomorphic sheaves, and the
graded pieces are the ideal powers defined above.

The following properties are part of the definition and are used by the
consumers of this item.

1. **The powers are quasi-coherent.** Each $\mathcal I^n$ is a
   quasi-coherent $\mathcal O_X$-module
   ([[def-quasi-coherent-module-scheme]]). Indeed, cover $X$ by affine opens
   $U=\operatorname{Spec}A$ with $\mathcal I|_U=\widetilde I$; then
   $\mathcal I^n|_U=\widetilde{I^n}$ is the associated sheaf of an
   $A$-module, and quasi-coherence is local on $X$. Alternatively, for
   $n\ge1$ the multiplication map
   $\mathcal I^{n-1}\otimes_{\mathcal O_X}\mathcal I\to\mathcal I^n$ is
   surjective from a quasi-coherent source by
   [[lem-tensor-qc-modules-quasi-coherent]].
2. **The Rees algebra sheaf is quasi-coherent.** On each affine chart
   $U=\operatorname{Spec}A$ one has
   $\mathcal R(\mathcal I)|_U=\widetilde{\bigoplus_{n\ge0}I^n}$, the
   associated sheaf of the graded $A$-module $\bigoplus_{n\ge0}I^n$; since
   quasi-coherence is local on $X$, the direct sum of the quasi-coherent
   sheaves $\mathcal I^n$ is quasi-coherent.
3. **Degree-one generation.**
   $\mathcal R(\mathcal I)_0=\mathcal O_X$ and
   $\mathcal R(\mathcal I)_1=\mathcal I$ generate
   $\mathcal R(\mathcal I)$ as an $\mathcal O_X$-algebra: for every $n\ge1$
   the product map $\mathcal I^{\otimes n}\to\mathcal I^n$ is surjective,
   because $\mathcal I^n$ is by construction generated by products of $n$
   local sections of $\mathcal I$. Equivalently, the canonical graded
   $\mathcal O_X$-algebra homomorphism
   $\bigoplus_{n\ge0}\operatorname{Sym}^n_{\mathcal O_X}(\mathcal I)\to
   \mathcal R(\mathcal I)$ is surjective.
The finite type hypothesis is used in subsequent finite-type structural
results, not in the relative Proj construction; the Rees algebra sheaf and
its relative Proj are defined for any quasi-coherent ideal.
