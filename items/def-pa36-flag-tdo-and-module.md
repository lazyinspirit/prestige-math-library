## Definition

Assume the Axiom of Choice as inherited from the published group and sheaf
suppliers. Let $G$ be a connected simply connected complex semisimple affine
algebraic group, $B\supseteq T$ a Borel subgroup and maximal torus,
$X=G/B$, $\mathfrak g=\operatorname{Lie}G$,
$\mathfrak h=\operatorname{Lie}T$, and $\Phi^+$ the positive roots selected by
$B$. Write $\rho=\frac12\sum_{\alpha\in\Phi^+}\alpha$. All schemes are over
$\mathbb C$. For an integral weight $\eta$, $\mathcal O_X(\eta)$ denotes the
$G$-equivariant line bundle $G\times^B\mathbb C_\eta$ with fiber character
$\eta$ at $eB$.

The infinitesimal $G$-action gives an anchor
$$a_X:\mathcal L_X:=\mathcal O_X\otimes_{\mathbb C}\mathfrak g\longrightarrow T_X,\qquad f\otimes x\longmapsto f x_X.$$
Give $\mathcal L_X$ the action-algebroid bracket
$$[f\otimes x,g\otimes y]=fg\otimes[x,y]+f x_X(g)\otimes y-g y_X(f)\otimes x.$$
Its kernel $\mathfrak b_X=\ker a_X$ is the isotropy Lie-algebra bundle; at
$gB$ its fiber is $\operatorname{Ad}(g)\mathfrak b$.

For any $\kappa\in\mathfrak h^*$, the character $\kappa+\rho$ of
$\mathfrak b$ (zero on $\mathfrak n^+$) transports to an
$\mathcal O_X$-linear map $\chi^X_{\kappa+\rho}:\mathfrak b_X\to\mathcal O_X$.
The transport is independent of the representative $g$ of $gB$, since a
character is invariant under the adjoint action of $B$. The action-algebroid
enveloping sheaf $\mathcal U_X(\mathfrak g)$ is generated by $\mathcal O_X$
and $\mathcal L_X$ with the displayed bracket and
$$[x,f]=x_X(f),\qquad x\in\mathcal L_X,\ f\in\mathcal O_X.$$
Define the sheaf of twisted differential operators
$$\mathcal D_\kappa:=\mathcal U_X(\mathfrak g)/\bigl(\xi-\chi^X_{\kappa+\rho}(\xi):\xi\in\mathfrak b_X\bigr).$$
Its order filtration assigns order zero to $\mathcal O_X$ and order at most
one to the image of $\mathcal L_X$. A TDO means a filtered
$\mathcal O_X$-ring with $F_0=\mathcal O_X$, associated graded
$\operatorname{Sym}_{\mathcal O_X}T_X$, and first-order commutator equal to
the anchor; the well-definedness and PBW properties of $\mathcal D_\kappa$
are proved in [[lem-pa36-flag-tdo-pbw-and-twist]]. A left
$\mathcal D_\kappa$-module below is required to be quasi-coherent as an
$\mathcal O_X$-module.

For $z\in Z(U(\mathfrak g))$, let $\operatorname{pr}(z)\in U(\mathfrak h)$ be
the Harish–Chandra projection along the chosen triangular decomposition, and
put
$$\chi_\kappa(z)=\operatorname{pr}(z)(\kappa-\rho).$$
This is the shifted central character: the Harish–Chandra isomorphism makes it
$W$-invariant in $\kappa$. Define
$$A_\kappa=U(\mathfrak g)/U(\mathfrak g)\ker\chi_\kappa.$$
The regularity convention is **antidominant** when
$\langle\kappa,\alpha^\vee\rangle\notin\mathbb Z_{>0}$ for each positive
root $\alpha$, and **regular** when
$\langle\kappa,\alpha^\vee\rangle\ne0$ for every root. For complex weights,
$\mathbb Z_{>0}$ means the positive real integers; no order on arbitrary
complex numbers is used.

## Remarks

For integral $\kappa$, the proved identification is
$\mathcal D_\kappa=\operatorname{Diff}(\mathcal O_X(\kappa+\rho))$.
In particular $\mathcal D_{-\rho}=\operatorname{Diff}(\mathcal O_X)$.
The construction uses the isotropy **sheaf** $\mathfrak b_X$; quotienting
$U(\mathfrak g)$ by the fixed subalgebra $\mathfrak b$ would not define an
algebra. With the convention of
[[def-borel-character-equivariant-line-bundle]],
$\mathcal O_X(\eta)=\mathcal L_{-\eta}$.
