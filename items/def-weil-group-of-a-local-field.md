## Definition

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $K$ be a nonarchimedean local field with finite residue field $k=\mathbb F_q$, fix a separable closure $K_s$, and let $G_K=\mathrm{Gal}(K_s/K)$. Let $\bar k$ be the residue-field algebraic closure and let $\eta:G_K\to\mathrm{Gal}(\bar k/k)$ be the reduction map whose kernel is the absolute inertia subgroup $I_K$. Arithmetic Frobenius is $\sigma_q:x\mapsto x^q$ on $\bar k$. The **Weil group** is
$$W_K:=\eta^{-1}(\langle\sigma_q\rangle),$$
where $\langle\sigma_q\rangle\cong\mathbb Z$ is the subgroup generated by arithmetic Frobenius inside $\mathrm{Gal}(\bar k/k)\cong\widehat{\mathbb Z}$. Give $W_K$ the group topology for which $I_K$ is open with its profinite topology and $W_K/I_K\cong\mathbb Z$ is discrete. A geometric Frobenius coset is the inverse of the arithmetic Frobenius coset; neither coset specifies a preferred lift in $W_K$.
