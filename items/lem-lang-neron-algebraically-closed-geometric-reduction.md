## Statement

Assume AC and DC. Let $k$ be algebraically closed, let $K/k$ be a finitely generated regular extension in the sense of [[def-k-trace-of-an-abelian-variety]], and let $A/K$ be an abelian variety. Assume the following finite-generation input: for every smooth geometrically integral finite-type $k$-scheme $Z$, the quotient $\operatorname{NS}(Z)=\operatorname{Pic}(Z)/\operatorname{Pic}^{\rm alg}(Z)$ is finitely generated, where $\operatorname{Pic}^{\rm alg}$ is the subgroup generated by differences of fibres of line bundles over smooth connected $k$-curves at $k$-points.

Then there is a smooth geometrically integral affine $k$-scheme $X$ with function field $K$ and a projective abelian scheme $p:Y\to X$ with generic fibre $A$, for which, writing $(T,\tau)$ for the $K/k$-trace of $A^\vee$, generic restriction gives
$$j^*\operatorname{Pic}^{\rm alg}(Y)=\tau(T(k))\subseteq A^\vee(K)\subseteq\operatorname{Pic}(A).$$
The induced sequence $$0\longrightarrow\operatorname{NS}(X)\xrightarrow{p^*}\operatorname{NS}(Y)\xrightarrow{j^*}\operatorname{Pic}(A)/\tau(T(k))\longrightarrow0$$ is split exact. In particular $A(K)/\operatorname{Tr}_{K/k}(A)(k)$ is finitely generated. The displayed trace images are taken through the universal trace homomorphisms. This is an algebraically closed-constant reduction conditional on the stated finite-generation input; it does not establish that input or descend the result to arbitrary constants.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC, DC, $k,K,A$ and the explicit finite-generation input in the Statement.

[A1] AC and DC are assumed. [[def-axiom-of-choice]], [[def-dependent-choice]]

[F1] Under the stated field hypotheses an abelian variety has a smooth geometrically integral affine base and a projective abelian-scheme model whose total space is smooth and geometrically integral. [[lem-lang-neron-smooth-abelian-scheme-model]]

[F2] The dual of an abelian variety exists, its bidual is canonically isomorphic to it, and every abelian variety in the stated field scope has a trace pair. [[thm-abelian-variety-dual-and-polarization]], [[lem-arith-dual-isogeny-kernel-and-abelian-biduality]], [[thm-k-k-trace-existence-and-uniqueness]]

[F3] For an abelian-scheme model over a smooth integral base in this field scope, the generic Picard restriction sequence is split exact. [[lem-picard-restriction-to-generic-fibre-exact-for-abelian-scheme]]

[F4] Under the same geometric hypotheses and a supplied trace pair for the dual, restriction of the curve-family algebraically trivial classes lies in its trace image, which is a subgroup of the dual points and of the generic Picard group. [[lem-algebraically-trivial-restrictions-factor-through-k-k-trace]]

[F5] Given the split Picard sequence, preservation of algebraic equivalence by the two structural maps, the trace containment, the rigidified Picard embedding and finite generation of the total-space Néron–Severi group, the trace image equals the restriction of the algebraically trivial group, the displayed Néron–Severi sequence is split exact, and the dual-point trace quotient is finitely generated. [[lem-picard-neron-severi-trace-cokernel-sequence]]

## Proof

**Proof technique:** apply the geometric suppliers to the model, then apply the trace-cokernel lemma and biduality.

1.1 By F1 choose $X$ and $Y$ with the asserted properties, and write $j:A\to Y$ for the generic-fibre map and $e:X\to Y$ for the identity section. F2 supplies $A^\vee$ and its trace pair $(T,\tau)$. The explicit finite-generation input applies to $Y$, which is smooth and geometrically integral by F1, so $\operatorname{NS}(Y)$ is finitely generated. [A1, F1, F2, given, construct]

2.1 By F3 the Picard restriction sequence in the hypotheses of F5 is split exact and $e^*p^*=\operatorname{id}$. Pullback along either $p$ or $e$ carries each curve-family generator of $\operatorname{Pic}^{\rm alg}$ to the corresponding difference of fibres of the pulled-back line bundle over the same smooth connected curve; hence it preserves algebraic equivalence. F4 supplies both the trace-image inclusion and the embedding of $A^\vee(K)$ in $\operatorname{Pic}(A)$. Thus every hypothesis of F5 is satisfied for this model, using step 1.1 for finite generation. [F3, F4, F5, step 1.1, given, algebra]

3.1 Apply F5. It gives equality of the generic restriction of $\operatorname{Pic}^{\rm alg}(Y)$ with $\tau(T(k))$, the displayed split exact sequence, and finite generation of $A^\vee(K)/\tau(T(k))$. [F5, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 Repeat steps 1.1–3.1 with $A^\vee$ in place of $A$; the finite-generation input applies equally to the newly chosen smooth geometrically integral total space. By F2 the bidual is canonically $A$. The trace universal property identifies the trace quotient of this bidual with $A(K)/\operatorname{Tr}_{K/k}(A)(k)$. It is therefore finitely generated. The proof does not remove the finite-generation hypothesis or the algebraically closed-constant restriction. [F2, step 1.1, step 2.1, step 3.1, given, algebra] ∎
