## Definition

A **finite field** is a field whose underlying set is finite. Its **order** is its finite cardinality, written $|F|$.

Once existence and uniqueness are proved, $\mathbb F_q$ denotes a field of order $q$ up to isomorphism. The notation does not assert that the field is the quotient ring $\mathbb Z/q\mathbb Z$.
