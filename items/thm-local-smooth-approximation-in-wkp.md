## Statement

Assume Countable Choice. Let $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ be open with
$n\ge1$, let $k\in\mathbb N_0$, $1\le p<\infty$ and
$\mathbb K\in\{\mathbb R,\mathbb C\}$, and let
$u\in W^{k,p}(\Omega;\mathbb K)$. Fix a nonnegative
$\rho\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R^n)$ of unit mass with
$\operatorname{supp}\rho\subseteq\overline B_1(0)$, put
$\rho_\varepsilon(x)=\varepsilon^{-n}\rho(x/\varepsilon)$, and define the
interior mollification
$$u_\varepsilon(x)=(\rho_\varepsilon*\widetilde u)(x)=\int_{\mathbb R^n}\widetilde u(y)\,\rho_\varepsilon(x-y)\,dy,$$
where $\widetilde u$ is a representative of $u$ extended by zero to
$\mathbb R^n$. Then $u_\varepsilon\to u$ in $W^{k,p}(U;\mathbb K)$ for every
open $U\subset\subset\Omega$; equivalently $u_\varepsilon\to u$ in
$W^{k,p}_{\mathrm{loc}}(\Omega;\mathbb K)$. The exponent $p=1$ is included, and
no assertion about density or convergence in the $W^{k,\infty}$ norm is made.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** Countable Choice; an open set $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ with $n\ge1$; $k\in\mathbb N_0$; $1\le p<\infty$; $\mathbb K\in\{\mathbb R,\mathbb C\}$; a class $u\in W^{k,p}(\Omega;\mathbb K)$; a nonnegative unit-mass $\rho\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R^n)$ with support in $\overline B_1(0)$; and an open set $U\subset\subset\Omega$, so that $\overline U$ is a compact subset of $\Omega$.

[F1] Interior mollification commutes with weak derivatives: with $\Omega_\varepsilon=\{x\in\Omega:\operatorname{dist}(x,\mathbb R^n\setminus\Omega)>\varepsilon\}$ and $D^\alpha u$ extended by zero, one has $D^\alpha u_\varepsilon=\rho_\varepsilon*(D^\alpha u)$ on $\Omega_\varepsilon$ for every $|\alpha|\le k$, and $u_\varepsilon$ is smooth there ([[lem-mollification-commutes-with-weak-derivatives-in-the-interior]]).

[F2] The family $\rho_\varepsilon$ is the mollifier family generated by the unit-mass bump $\rho$ ([[def-mollifier-family-generated-by-a-unit-mass-smooth-bump]]).

[F3] Approximate identity convergence: the family $(\rho_\varepsilon)$ is an $L^1$ approximate identity ([[prop-mollifier-families-are-l-one-approximate-identities]]); if $f\in L^p(\mathbb R^n)$ and $1\le p<\infty$, then $\|f*\rho_\varepsilon-f\|_{L^p(\mathbb R^n)}\to0$ as $\varepsilon\to0^+$ ([[thm-l-one-approximate-identities-converge-in-l-p]]), and the same holds for complex-valued $f$ with the complex convolution conventions, including the case of a complex scalar field ([[lem-complex-translation-and-approximate-identity-interfaces]]).

[F4] For $w\in L^p(\Omega;\mathbb K)$ the extension by zero $E_0w$ lies in $L^p(\mathbb R^n;\mathbb K)$ with $\|E_0w\|_{L^p(\mathbb R^n)}=\|w\|_{L^p(\Omega)}$, because the integral over a measurable set is the integral of the indicator product ([[def-integral-over-a-measurable-set]], [[def-complex-lp-and-euclidean-test-function-conventions]]).

[F5] The $W^{k,p}$ norm is the $\ell^p$ sum of the $L^p$ norms of all derivative classes $D^\alpha u$ with $|\alpha|\le k$; for $p=\infty$ it is the maximum of the essential bounds ([[def-sobolev-space-wkp-and-its-norm]]).

[F6] Compact containment: $\overline U\subseteq\Omega$ compact with $\Omega$ open implies $d:=\operatorname{dist}(\overline U,\mathbb R^n\setminus\Omega)>0$, and for $0<\varepsilon<d$ every $x\in U$ satisfies $\operatorname{dist}(x,\mathbb R^n\setminus\Omega)\ge d>\varepsilon$, i.e. $U\subseteq\Omega_\varepsilon$; Here distance to the empty set is $+\infty$, including when $U=\varnothing$ or $\Omega=\mathbb R^n$; the containment still holds. This is elementary metric topology and uses no choice.

**Choice use.** Countable Choice is used through the approximate-identity and mollification interfaces of [F1] and [F3]; the compact-containment constant of [F6] is explicit and choice-free.

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Fix $U\subset\subset\Omega$ and put $d:=\operatorname{dist}(\overline U,\mathbb R^n\setminus\Omega)>0$ as in [F6]; then $U\subseteq\Omega_\varepsilon$ for every $0<\varepsilon<d$. Also, for each $|\alpha|\le k$ the zero extension $E_0(D^\alpha u)$ belongs to $L^p(\mathbb R^n;\mathbb K)$ with $\|E_0(D^\alpha u)\|_{L^p(\mathbb R^n)}=\|D^\alpha u\|_{L^p(\Omega)}$ by [F4], since $D^\alpha u\in L^p(\Omega;\mathbb K)$. [F4, F6, given]

2.1 By [F1], for every $0<\varepsilon<d$ and every $|\alpha|\le k$ the identity $$D^\alpha u_\varepsilon=\rho_\varepsilon*(E_0D^\alpha u)\qquad\text{on }U$$ holds as an identity of smooth functions, since $U\subseteq\Omega_\varepsilon$. [F1, step 1.1]

3.1 For each $|\alpha|\le k$ the right-hand side of step 2.1 converges to $E_0D^\alpha u$ in $L^p(\mathbb R^n;\mathbb K)$: by [F2] and [F3] applied to the $L^p$ class $f=E_0D^\alpha u$, $\|\rho_\varepsilon*f-f\|_{L^p(\mathbb R^n)}\to0$ as $\varepsilon\to0^+$. Hence $$\bigl\|D^\alpha u_\varepsilon-D^\alpha u\bigr\|_{L^p(U)}\le\bigl\|\rho_\varepsilon*(E_0D^\alpha u)-E_0D^\alpha u\bigr\|_{L^p(\mathbb R^n)}\longrightarrow0.$$ [F2, F3, step 1.1, step 2.1]

4.1 Summing the finitely many convergences of step 3.1 over $|\alpha|\le k$, the norm formula of [F5] gives $\|u_\varepsilon-u\|_{W^{k,p}(U)}\to0$; since $U\subset\subset\Omega$ was arbitrary, this is convergence in $W^{k,p}_{\mathrm{loc}}(\Omega)$. The proof uses $p<\infty$ exactly through the approximate-identity convergence in step 3.1, makes no claim for $p=\infty$, and the case $k=0$ is the single multi-index $\alpha=0$, namely convergence in $L^p_{\mathrm{loc}}$. [F3, F5, step 3.1, given] ∎
