## Definition

Assume the Axiom of Choice ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]), inherited here from
the affine structure-sheaf construction
([[thm-structure-sheaf-affine-scheme]]): its localization data form a sheaf
on distinguished opens using the prime-existence and finite-subcover results.
The finite chart gluing makes no additional choice.

Let $k$ be a field, let $R=k[x_0,\ldots,x_n]$ carry the standard grading in
which every $x_i$ has degree one
([[def-graded-ring-and-graded-module]],
[[def-homogeneous-polynomial-and-homogeneous-ideal]]), let $I\subseteq R$ be a
homogeneous ideal, and put $S=R/I$ with the induced grading. Write

$$S_+=(x_0,\ldots,x_n)S=\bigoplus_{m\ge1}S_m$$

for the **irrelevant ideal** of $S$, generated by the images of the variables.

1. **Underlying set.** The **projective spectrum** $\operatorname{Proj}S$ has as
   its points the homogeneous prime ideals $\mathfrak p\subseteq S$ with
   $S_+\nsubseteq\mathfrak p$; equivalently $x_i\notin\mathfrak p$ for at least
   one $i$.
2. **Standard charts.** For each $i$ let $S_{x_i}$ be the localization of $S$ in
   which the powers of $x_i$ are inverted; it is a graded ring and its
   degree-zero part $(S_{x_i})_0$ is a $k$-algebra. The **standard chart**
   $D_+(x_i)$ is the affine scheme $\operatorname{Spec}\bigl((S_{x_i})_0\bigr)$
   with its structure sheaf ([[def-affine-scheme]]), whose sections on a
   distinguished open $D(a)$ are $((S_{x_i})_0)_a$.
   Concretely, $(S_{x_i})_0$ is the image of
   the subring of fractions of $S$ of total degree zero with denominator a power
   of $x_i$; in the polynomial case with $I=0$ it is the polynomial ring in the
   ratios $x_j/x_i$ for $j\ne i$.
3. **Overlaps and gluing.** For all $i,j$ the localizations $(S_{x_i})_0$ and
   $(S_{x_j})_0$ have the common localization $(S_{x_ix_j})_0$, obtained from
   either one by inverting the degree-zero ratio $x_j/x_i$ respectively
   $x_i/x_j$; these identifications of
   $\operatorname{Spec}\bigl((S_{x_ix_j})_0\bigr)$ with distinguished open
   subschemes of the two charts are isomorphisms of locally ringed spaces
   ([[lem-spectrum-localization-open-immersion]]). They are the transition
   isomorphisms, and they
   satisfy the identity and cocycle conditions because both composites are the
   canonical identification inside the double localization $S_{x_ix_jx_k}$. The
   affine schemes $D_+(x_i)$ therefore glue to a scheme
   $\operatorname{Proj}S$ ([[thm-gluing-affine-schemes]], [[def-scheme]]), the
   standard charts forming an open affine cover, with the points of part 1 as its
   underlying set and the subset $D_+(x_ix_j)\subseteq D_+(x_i)$ as the locus
   where $x_j/x_i$ is invertible.
4. **Conventions.** The construction is available over an arbitrary field $k$
   and for an arbitrary homogeneous ideal $I$, including a nonradical one; it
   makes $\operatorname{Proj}S$ a $k$-scheme locally of finite type, with
   residue fields $\kappa(\mathfrak p)=S_{(\mathfrak p)}/\mathfrak m_{(\mathfrak p)}$
   that need not equal $k$. Here $T$ is the multiplicative set of homogeneous
   elements of $S$ outside $\mathfrak p$, $S_{(\mathfrak p)}=(T^{-1}S)_0$,
   and $\mathfrak m_{(\mathfrak p)}=(T^{-1}\mathfrak p)_0$ is its maximal
   ideal: a degree-zero fraction with numerator outside $\mathfrak p$ has its
   inverse by interchanging numerator and denominator. It is not the same object as the classical
   projective algebraic set of [[def-projective-space-points]], which is defined
   over an algebraically closed field and is reduced: the classical set records
   only the $\bar k$-points of the reduced subscheme, while
   $\operatorname{Proj}S$ retains the scheme structure on its charts, including any
   nilpotents that survive localization. When $k$ is algebraically closed and $I$ is radical, the closed
   points of $\operatorname{Proj}S$ correspond to the points of $V_+(I)$ in the
   classical sense; that dictionary is not asserted here.

For the empty case: if $S_+=0$, that is, if the images of all $x_i$ vanish in
$S$, then $S$ has no homogeneous prime avoiding $S_+$ and
$\operatorname{Proj}S=\varnothing$; every $D_+(x_i)=\operatorname{Spec}0$ is
empty, consistently with the gluing. In particular
$\operatorname{Proj}\bigl(k[x_0,\ldots,x_n]/(x_0,\ldots,x_n)\bigr)=\varnothing$.
