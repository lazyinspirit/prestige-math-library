## Definition

Let $A\in M_n(\mathbb F)$ and let $x_0\in\mathbb F^n$ be nonzero. The
**power iteration** generated by $(A,x_0)$ is the sequence of normalised
vectors

$$
x_{k+1}:=\frac{Ax_k}{\|Ax_k\|_2},
$$

defined whenever $Ax_k\neq0$.

When the iterates are used with a self-adjoint or Hermitian matrix, the
associated **Rayleigh-quotient eigenvalue estimates** are

$$\mu_k:=\frac{\langle Ax_k,x_k\rangle}{\langle x_k,x_k\rangle}.$$

For $k\ge1$, every defined iterate $x_k$ is normalised to unit length, and then
$\mu_k=\langle Ax_k,x_k\rangle$. The same simplification holds at $k=0$ when
$x_0$ is chosen to have unit length.
