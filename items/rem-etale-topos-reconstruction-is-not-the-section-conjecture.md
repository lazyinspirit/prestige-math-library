## Remark

Assume the Axiom of Choice. For a scheme $C$, its small étale topos is the category of sheaves on its small étale site. A reconstruction assertion asks whether an equivalence of such topoi for two schemes forces a scheme isomorphism, under stated hypotheses. The Galois section conjecture instead asks whether rational points of one proper hyperbolic curve over a field correspond bijectively to conjugacy classes of sections of its arithmetic fundamental-group sequence. These have different input data and different conclusions; a theorem about one formulation is not, by its wording alone, a proof of the other.
