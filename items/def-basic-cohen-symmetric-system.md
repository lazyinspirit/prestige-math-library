## Definition

Let $M$ be a transitive model of ZF, let $P=\operatorname{Add}(\omega,\omega)^M$ be the finite partial maps $\omega\times\omega\to2$ ordered by reverse inclusion as in [[def-cohen-collapse-and-levy-collapse-forcings]], and let $G_0$ be $M$-generic for $P$. A finite-support permutation $\pi$ of $\omega$ acts by $(\pi p)(\pi n,m)=p(n,m)$. Let $G$ be the resulting group of forcing automorphisms and let $\mathcal F$ be generated by $\operatorname{fix}(E)=\{\pi:\pi\restriction E=\mathrm{id}\}$ for finite $E\subseteq\omega$. Conjugation sends $\operatorname{fix}(E)$ to $\operatorname{fix}(\pi E)$, so the filter is normal; every condition is supported by the finite projection of its domain to the first coordinate.

Define
$\dot a_n=\{\langle\check m,p\rangle:p(n,m)=1\}$ and the orbit-set name
$\dot A=\{\langle\dot a_n,1_P\rangle:n\in\omega\}$. Direct calculation gives $\pi\dot a_n=\dot a_{\pi n}$ and $\pi\dot A=\dot A$. The theorem [[thm-hereditarily-symmetric-interpretations-form-a-zf-model]] applies to the displayed $M$, symmetric system, and $M$-generic $G_0$, so $N=\mathrm{HS}_{\mathcal F}^{G_0}$ is the resulting transitive ZF model. No AC is used in this definition.
