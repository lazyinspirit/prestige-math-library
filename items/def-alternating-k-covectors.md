## Definition

Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional real vector space. For $k\ge 0$, let
$\operatorname{Alt}^k(V)$ be the vector space of alternating $k$-linear maps
$V^k\to\mathbb R$. By convention,

$$\operatorname{Alt}^0(V):=\mathbb R.$$
