## Statement

For every $n\ge1$, $S^{(n)}$ is the one-dimensional trivial complex
representation of $S_n$, while $S^{(1^n)}$ is the one-dimensional sign
representation. For $n=0$, $S^\varnothing$ is the one-dimensional trivial
representation of $S_0$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A natural number $n$.

[F1] The column antisymmetrizer is
$\kappa_t=\sum_{\gamma\in C_t}\operatorname{sgn}(\gamma)\gamma$
([[def-column-antisymmetrizer-polytabloid-and-specht-module]]).

[F2] The polytabloid is $e_t=\kappa_t\cdot\{t\}$
([[def-column-antisymmetrizer-polytabloid-and-specht-module]]).

[F3] The Specht space is the complex span of the polytabloids of shape
$\lambda$
([[def-column-antisymmetrizer-polytabloid-and-specht-module]]).

[F4] The diagram of $(n)$ has one box in each column, and the diagram of
$(1^n)$ has one column containing all $n$ boxes
([[def-partition-young-diagram-and-conjugate-partition]]).

[F5] The column stabilizer consists of the permutations preserving each
column set
([[def-row-and-column-stabilizers-of-a-tableau]]).

[F6] Tabloids identify tableaux that have the same row sets
([[def-young-subgroup-tabloid-and-permutation-module]]).

[F7] Two tableaux are row-equivalent exactly when their row sets agree
([[def-young-subgroup-tabloid-and-permutation-module]]).

[F8] The tabloids form a basis of the tabloid module and the group action
extends linearly ([[def-young-subgroup-tabloid-and-permutation-module]]).

[F9] The coefficient of $\{t\}$ in $e_t$ is $1$
([[def-column-antisymmetrizer-polytabloid-and-specht-module]]).

[F10] The Specht space is generated by any one polytabloid
([[lem-polytabloid-covariance-and-column-sign]]).

[F11] The trivial representation is one-dimensional and every group element
acts as the identity ([[def-trivial-regular-and-permutation-representations]]).

[F12] The sign representation acts on $\mathbb C$ by
$\sigma\cdot a=\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)a$
([[def-sign-representation-and-restriction-of-a-representation]]).

[F13] The sign function is a group homomorphism to $\{+1,-1\}$
([[thm-sign-is-a-homomorphism]]).

[F14] A tableau is a bijective filling of the boxes by $1,\dots,n$
([[def-young-tableau-standard-tableau-and-shape]]).

[F15] At $n=0$, the row and column stabilizers of the empty tableau are
$S_0=\{1\}$ ([[def-row-and-column-stabilizers-of-a-tableau]]).

[F16] At $n=0$, the empty-tableau definition gives
$e_\varnothing=\{\varnothing\}$ and $S^\varnothing=\mathbb C$
([[def-column-antisymmetrizer-polytabloid-and-specht-module]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Let $n\ge1$ and take the row-filled tableau $t$ of shape $(n)$. By [F4,F5], every tableau of this shape has singleton columns, so its antisymmetrizer is $1$ and every polytabloid is its tabloid. Every tableau has row set $\{1,\dots,n\}$ by [F14], so [F6] makes all tabloids equal; [F3] then gives $S^{(n)}=\mathbb C e_t$. The group fixes the sole tabloid by [F8], so $a e_t\mapsto a$ identifies the action with the trivial representation [F11]. [given, F1, F2, F3, F4, F5, F6, F8, F11, F14]

1.2 Let $n\ge1$ and take the tableau $t$ whose single column is filled by $1,\dots,n$ from top to bottom, which exists by [F14]. By [F4,F5], $C_t=S_n$; [F7,F8] make the tabloids $\{\sigma\cdot t\}$ distinct basis vectors, so $e_t=\sum_{\sigma\in S_n}\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)\{\sigma\cdot t\}$ has coefficient $1$ at $\{t\}$ by [F9] and is nonzero. [given, F1, F4, F5, F7, F8, F9, F14]

2.1 For $\tau\in S_n$, reindexing the sum of step 1.2 by $\rho=\tau\sigma$ gives $\tau\cdot e_t=\sum_{\rho\in S_n}\operatorname{sgn}(\tau^{-1}\rho)\{\rho\cdot t\}=\operatorname{sgn}(\tau)e_t$, since [F13] implies $\operatorname{sgn}(\tau^{-1})=\operatorname{sgn}(\tau)$. By [F10], this orbit spans $S^{(1^n)}$, so step 1.2 gives $S^{(1^n)}=\mathbb C e_t$; the map $a e_t\mapsto a$ identifies its action with the sign representation [F12]. [step 1.2, F10, F12, F13, algebra]

3.1 When $n=0$, [F16] gives $S^\varnothing=\mathbb C e_\varnothing=\mathbb C$, and [F15] says $S_0=\{1\}$ acts as the identity; therefore this is the one-dimensional trivial representation [F11]. [given, F11, F15, F16] ∎
