## Example

Let $k$ be a field, $n\ge0$ and let
$$0\neq F\in k[x_0,\dots,x_n]$$
be homogeneous of degree $e\ge1$. Then
$$\operatorname{Proj}\bigl(k[x_0,\dots,x_n]/(F)\bigr)=V_+(F)\hookrightarrow\mathbb P^n_k$$
is the closed subscheme of the projective space cut out by $F$, and on the
standard chart $D_+(x_i)$ its coordinate ring is
$$k\bigl[x_a/x_i:a\ne i\bigr]\big/\bigl(F/x_i^{e}\bigr).$$
The description includes the degenerate cases: for $n=0$ one has
$F=cx_0^{e}$ with $c\neq0$ and the chart ring $k/(c)=0$, so $V_+(F)=\varnothing$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A field $k$, an integer $n\ge0$, the graded polynomial ring $B=k[x_0,\dots,x_n]$ with $\deg x_i=1$, a nonzero homogeneous $F\in B$ of degree $e\ge1$, and the homogeneous principal ideal $I=(F)\subseteq B$.

[A1] The Axiom of Choice states that every family of nonempty sets has a choice function. ([[def-axiom-of-choice]])

[F1] For a commutative ring $A$, a homogeneous ideal $I\subseteq A[x_0,\dots,x_n]$ determines a closed subscheme $V_+(I)\hookrightarrow\mathbb P^n_A$ whose intersection with the chart $D_+(x_i)$ is $\operatorname{Spec}\bigl(B_{(x_i)}/I_{(x_i)}\bigr)$, where $I_{(x_i)}=(I[x_i^{-1}])_0$; equivalently $V_+(I)=\operatorname{Proj}(B/I)$ under the canonical closed immersion $\operatorname{Proj}(B/I)\to\operatorname{Proj}B=\mathbb P^n_A$. ([[thm-closed-subschemes-projective-space-homogeneous-ideals]])

[F2] There is a canonical isomorphism $\operatorname{Proj}A[x_0,\dots,x_n]\cong\mathbb P^n_A$ over $\operatorname{Spec}A$, and over the field $k$ the standard chart $D_+(x_i)$ has coordinate ring $k[x^{(i)}_a:a\ne i]$ with $x^{(i)}_a=x_a/x_i$. ([[thm-projective-space-as-proj]], [[thm-closed-subschemes-projective-space-homogeneous-ideals]])

## Verification

**Proof technique:** direct: read the hypersurface off the homogeneous quotient theorem, compute the degree-zero localisation of the principal ideal at each chart variable, and identify the resulting equation with $F/x_i^{e}$.

1.1 The quotient and the closed subscheme. Since $F$ is homogeneous, $I=(F)$ is a homogeneous ideal, so by [F1] the subscheme $V_+(I)\hookrightarrow\mathbb P^n_k$ exists and equals $\operatorname{Proj}(B/I)=\operatorname{Proj}\bigl(k[x_0,\dots,x_n]/(F)\bigr)$; by [F2] the ambient space $\operatorname{Proj}B$ is $\mathbb P^n_k$ with charts $D_+(x_i)$. [A1, F1, F2]

2.1 The chart ring. Fix $i$. By [F2] the chart ring of $\mathbb P^n_k$ on $D_+(x_i)$ is $B_{(x_i)}=k[x^{(i)}_a:a\ne i]$ with $x^{(i)}_a=x_a/x_i$. In the localisation $B[x_i^{-1}]$ the element $x_i$ is a unit and $F=x_i^{e}\cdot(F/x_i^{e})$, so the ideal generated by $F$ is generated by the degree-zero element $F/x_i^{e}$; hence $I_{(x_i)}=(I[x_i^{-1}])_0=(F/x_i^{e})$, and the chart ring of $V_+(I)$ on $D_+(x_i)$ is $B_{(x_i)}/I_{(x_i)}=k[x^{(i)}_a:a\ne i]/(F/x_i^{e})$, that is, $k[x_a/x_i:a\ne i]/(F/x_i^{\deg F})$. [F1, F2, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Conclusion and degenerate cases. Steps 1.1 and 2.1 identify $\operatorname{Proj}(k[x_0,\dots,x_n]/(F))$ with $V_+(F)\subseteq\mathbb P^n_k$ and compute its chart rings. If $F=cx_i^{e}$ is a nonzero multiple of a single coordinate power, then on the $i$-th chart the equation is the unit $c$ and the chart ring is $k[x^{(i)}_a]/(c)=0$, so that chart meets $V_+(F)$ in the empty scheme; in particular for $n=0$ one has $F=cx_0^{e}$ and $V_+(F)=\operatorname{Spec}0=\varnothing$, the empty hypersurface. If $F$ is not such a monomial then for every $i$ the element $F/x_i^{e}$ is the dehomogenisation of $F$, a nonzero element of the polynomial ring $k[x^{(i)}_a:a\ne i]$ that is not a unit because some monomial of $F$ has a positive exponent at a variable other than $x_i$; each chart is then the genuine affine hypersurface $k[x^{(i)}_a]/(F/x_i^{e})$, which is a nonzero ring. The zero polynomial is excluded by hypothesis. The Axiom of Choice [A1] is inherited from the affine quotient and gluing suppliers of [F1]; no choice is made here. [A1, F1, F2, step 1.1, step 2.1, cases: empty chart and n=0]
\qed
