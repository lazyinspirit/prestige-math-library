## Statement

Assume AC (inherited through the definition of the reduced representation and
the agreement theorem with the topological representation). For $1\le n\le3$
the reduced Burau representation
$\bar\rho_n:B_n\to\operatorname{GL}_{n-1}(\Lambda_1)$ over
$\Lambda_1=\mathbb Z[t^{\pm1}]$ is faithful; that is, $\ker\bar\rho_n$ is
trivial and $\bar\rho_n$ is injective. In detail: $B_1$ is trivial; $B_2\cong
\mathbb Z$ is generated by $\sigma_1$
([[thm-the-two-strand-braid-group-is-infinite-cyclic]]) and
$\bar\rho_2(\sigma_1^k)=(-t)^k$, which is $I_1$ only for $k=0$
([[lem-units-and-powers-of-the-laurent-polynomial-ring]]); and if
$\beta\in\ker\bar\rho_3$, then its specialization at $t=-1$ lies in
$\ker\bar\rho_3(-1)=\langle\Delta^4\rangle$
([[lem-the-minus-one-specialization-of-three-strand-burau-has-kernel-generated-by-delta-to-the-fourth]]),
say $\beta=\Delta^{4k}$, and
[[lem-reduced-burau-detects-every-power-of-delta-to-the-fourth-in-b-three]]
forces $t^{6k}=1$, hence $k=0$ and $\beta=1$. The case $n=4$ is not claimed. Magnus and Peluso established
faithfulness for $n=3$ by a direct algebraic computation, and the argument for
$n=3$ given here, via the $t=-1$ specialization, is independent of theirs. The
AC hypothesis is exactly the inherited one.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC; the ring $\Lambda_1=\mathbb Z[t^{\pm1}]$; the reduced representations $\bar\rho_1,\bar\rho_2,\bar\rho_3$ in their fixed bases; the half twist $\Delta=\sigma_1\sigma_2\sigma_1$ of $B_3$.

[F1] For $n=0$ and $n=1$ there are no Artin generators and $B_n$ is the trivial group ([[def-braid-group-by-the-artin-presentation]]).

[F2] $B_2$ is infinite cyclic generated by $\sigma_1$; in the one-element basis of the reduced module for $n=2$ the representation is $\bar\rho_2(\sigma_1)=-t$ and $\bar\rho_2(\sigma_1^k)=(-t)^k$ ([[thm-the-two-strand-braid-group-is-infinite-cyclic]], [[thm-topological-and-matrix-burau-representations-agree]], clause (2); [[def-reduced-burau-representation]]).

[F3] $\bar\rho_3(-1):B_3\to\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb Z)$ denotes the homomorphism obtained by evaluating the matrices of $\bar\rho_3$ at $t=-1$; its kernel is $\ker\bar\rho_3(-1)=\langle\Delta^4\rangle=\{\Delta^{4k}:k\in\mathbb Z\}$ ([[lem-the-minus-one-specialization-of-three-strand-burau-has-kernel-generated-by-delta-to-the-fourth]]).

[F4] $\bar\rho_3(\Delta^{4k})=t^{6k}I_2$ for every $k\in\mathbb Z$, and $t^{6k}I_2=I_2$ if and only if $k=0$ ([[lem-reduced-burau-detects-every-power-of-delta-to-the-fourth-in-b-three]]).

[F5] $t^m\ne1$ in $\Lambda_1$ for every $m\ne0$, and in particular $(-t)^k=1$ implies $k=0$: if $(-t)^k=1$ then $t^{2k}=((-t)^k)^2=1$, so $2k=0$ ([[lem-units-and-powers-of-the-laurent-polynomial-ring]], clause (b)).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 *The case $n=1$.* By [F1] the group $B_1$ is trivial, so its only element is the identity and $\ker\bar\rho_1=\{1\}$; hence $\bar\rho_1$ is injective and faithful. [F1]

1.2 *The case $n=2$.* By [F2] every element of $B_2$ is $\sigma_1^k$ for a unique $k\in\mathbb Z$, and $\bar\rho_2(\sigma_1^k)=(-t)^k$. If $\sigma_1^k\in\ker\bar\rho_2$ then $(-t)^k=1$, so $t^{2k}=1$ and hence $2k=0$ by [F5], giving $k=0$ and $\sigma_1^k=1$. Thus $\ker\bar\rho_2$ is trivial and $\bar\rho_2$ is faithful. [F2, F5, algebra]

1.3 *The case $n=3$.* Let $\beta\in\ker\bar\rho_3$, so $\bar\rho_3(\beta)=I_2$. Applying the evaluation homomorphism of [F3] entrywise gives $\bar\rho_3(-1)(\beta)=I_2$, so $\beta\in\ker\bar\rho_3(-1)=\langle\Delta^4\rangle$, say $\beta=\Delta^{4k}$ with $k\in\mathbb Z$. Then [F4] gives $I_2=\bar\rho_3(\beta)=\bar\rho_3(\Delta^{4k})=t^{6k}I_2$, so $t^{6k}=1$ and hence $k=0$; therefore $\beta=\Delta^0=1$. Since $\beta$ was an arbitrary element of the kernel, $\ker\bar\rho_3$ is trivial and $\bar\rho_3$ is faithful. [F3, F4]

2.1 *Conclusion.* Steps 1.1, 1.2 and 1.3 cover $n=1,2,3$ respectively, so the reduced Burau representation is faithful for $1\le n\le3$. AC is inherited through the cited representation items as declared; the group-theoretic and specialization computations are choice free. [step 1.1, step 1.2, step 1.3] ∎
