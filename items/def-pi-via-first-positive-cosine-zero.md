## Definition

Let $\gamma$ be the unique smallest positive zero of cosine supplied by [[thm-cosine-has-a-smallest-positive-zero]]. Define
$$\pi:=2\gamma.$$

Thus $\pi>0$ and $\pi/2=\gamma$.
