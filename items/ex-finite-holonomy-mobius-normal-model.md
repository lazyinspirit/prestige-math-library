## Example

Assume Countable Choice $\mathrm{AC}_\omega$
([[def-countable-choice-principle-for-foliation-pair]]). Let
$$M:=\bigl(S^1\times(-1,1)\bigr)/\mathbb Z_2$$
be the Möbius band, the quotient by the free involution
$\tau(u,v):=(u+\tfrac12,-v)$, foliated by the images of the circles
$S^1\times\{v\}$. The central leaf $L_0$ (image of $v=0$) is the circle
$\mathbb R/(\tfrac12\mathbb Z)$ whose holonomy group is $\mathbb Z_2$,
generated by the reflection germ $v\mapsto-v$ of a transversal interval: going
once around the central leaf identifies the transversal coordinate $v$ with
$-v$ ([[def-holonomy-representation-and-holonomy-group-of-a-leaf]]). Its
holonomy cover is the connected double cover
$\widehat L_0=S^1\to L_0$
([[def-holonomy-cover-of-a-leaf]]), the deck group is $\mathbb Z_2$ acting by
$u\mapsto u+\tfrac12$, and the finite-holonomy normal model
$(\widehat L_0\times D)/\mathbb Z_2$ with the diagonal action
$(u,v)\mapsto(u+\tfrac12,-v)$ is exactly the Möbius band with its foliation:
the quotient recovers the surface and the central leaf
$(\widehat L_0\times\{0\})/\mathbb Z_2\cong L_0$. The other leaves are images
of $S^1\times\{v\}$ and double-cover the central leaf, as predicted by the
finite-holonomy model.

## Verification

**Given:** The involution $\tau(u,v)=(u+\frac12,-v)$ on $S^1\times(-1,1)$ with $S^1=\mathbb R/\mathbb Z$, the product foliation by the slices $S^1\times\{v\}$, and the quotient $M$.

[F1] A free properly discontinuous action by diffeomorphisms preserving a regular foliation descends the foliation to the quotient, whose leaves are the images of the leaves ([[prop-quotient-foliation-under-a-free-proper-foliated-action]]).

[F2] The holonomy representation of a leaf and the holonomy cover with deck group isomorphic to the holonomy group are as in [[def-holonomy-representation-and-holonomy-group-of-a-leaf]], [[def-holonomy-cover-of-a-leaf]] and [[lem-deck-group-of-the-holonomy-cover-is-the-holonomy-group]].

[F3] The finite-holonomy normal model is the diagonal quotient $(\widehat L\times D)/H$ with the deck action on the holonomy cover and the holonomy action on the invariant transverse disk ([[def-finite-holonomy-normal-model]]).


**Proof technique:** direct verification.

1.1 (The quotient is the Möbius band.) The map $\tau$ is an involution: $\tau^2(u,v)=\tau(u+\tfrac12,-v)=(u+1,v)=(u,v)$ in $S^1=\mathbb R/\mathbb Z$. It is free: $\tau(u,v)=(u,v)$ would give $v=-v$, hence $v=0$, and then $u=u+\tfrac12$, impossible in $S^1=\mathbb R/\mathbb Z$; since $S^1\times(-1,1)$ is a surface and the action is free and properly discontinuous, the quotient $M$ is a smooth surface. It is the total space of the interval bundle over $S^1=\mathbb R/(\tfrac12\mathbb Z)$ with monodromy $v\mapsto-v$, the non-trivial interval bundle, i.e. the Möbius band. [F1]

1.2 (The foliation descends and the holonomy is $\mathbb Z_2$.) The involution carries the slice $S^1\times\{v\}$ onto $S^1\times\{-v\}$, so the product foliation is preserved and descends to a codimension-one foliation of $M$ whose leaves are the images of the slices [F1]. The image of $S^1\times\{0\}$ is the central leaf $L_0=\mathbb R/(\tfrac12\mathbb Z)$; following it once around means passing from $u$ to $u+\tfrac12$, and the identification in the quotient returns the transversal coordinate $v$ to $-v$, so the return germ is the reflection $v\mapsto-v$ and the holonomy group is $\mathbb Z_2$ [F2]. [F1, F2]

2.1 (The holonomy cover and the normal model.) The holonomy cover of $L_0$ is the connected double cover $\widehat L_0=S^1\to L_0$ with deck group $\mathbb Z_2$ acting by $u\mapsto u+\tfrac12$ [F2]. With $D$ a small invariant transversal interval and the diagonal action $(u,v)\mapsto(u+\tfrac12,-v)$, the finite-holonomy normal model $(\widehat L_0\times D)/\mathbb Z_2$ is the quotient of $S^1\times D$ by exactly the involution $\tau$ restricted to $S^1\times D$, hence equals the Möbius band over $D$ with the descended foliation; the central leaf is $(\widehat L_0\times\{0\})/\mathbb Z_2\cong L_0$ [F3]. [F2, F3, step 1.2]

3.1 (The other leaves.) For $v\ne0$, the map $u\mapsto[(u,v)]$ from $S^1$ to the quotient leaf is injective: the only nonidentity group element changes $v$ to $-v$. It parametrizes that entire leaf, because the other slice at $-v$ has the same image. Under the model retraction to $L_0=\mathbb R/(\tfrac12\mathbb Z)$ its projection is $u\bmod\mathbb Z\mapsto u\bmod(\tfrac12\mathbb Z)$, a double covering. In a fixed local transverse fibre the two leaf intersections at $v$ and $-v$ are distinct points; they are not identified in that fibre. This is exactly the stabilizer calculation $[H:H_v]=2$ for $H_v=\{1\}$. [F1, F3, step 2.1] ∎
