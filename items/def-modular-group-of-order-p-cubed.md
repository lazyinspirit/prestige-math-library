## Definition

Let $p$ be a prime, let $A=\langle a\rangle$ be a cyclic group of order $p^2$ and
let $B=\langle s\rangle$ be a cyclic group of order $p$
([[thm-classification-of-cyclic-groups]]). Define

$$\alpha:B\longrightarrow\operatorname{Aut}(A),\qquad \alpha_{s^{i}}(x)=x^{(1+p)^{i}} .$$

**Why this is well defined and is an action by automorphisms.** Each
$x\mapsto x^{1+p}$ is an automorphism of $A$ of order $p$
([[lem-the-order-p-automorphism-of-a-cyclic-group-of-order-p-squared]]), so
$\alpha_{s^{i}}$ is the $i$-th power of that automorphism and is an automorphism.
If $s^{i}=s^{j}$ then $p$ divides $i-j$, and the $p$-th power of that
automorphism is the identity, so $\alpha_{s^{i}}=\alpha_{s^{j}}$. Finally
$\alpha_{s^{i}}\circ\alpha_{s^{j}}=\alpha_{s^{i+j}}$, so $\alpha$ is a
homomorphism into $\operatorname{Aut}(A)$ and hence an action by automorphisms
([[def-action-by-automorphisms]], [[def-group-isomorphism-and-automorphism]]).

The **modular group of order $p^3$** is the external semidirect product
([[def-external-semidirect-product]], [[thm-external-semidirect-product-is-a-group]])

$$M_p:=A\rtimes_\alpha B .$$

Writing $a$ and $s$ for the canonical images of the two generators
([[prop-canonical-subgroups-of-an-external-semidirect-product]]), the group is
generated by $a$ and $s$ subject to

$$a^{p^{2}}=1,\qquad s^{p}=1,\qquad sas^{-1}=a^{1+p} .$$

## Remarks

Craven writes $\operatorname{Mod}_n(p)$ for the analogous group of order $p^n$
and reserves $p^{1+2}_{+}$ for the exponent-$p$ group; only the case $n=3$ is
built here. Its relation uses the standard order-$p$ power automorphism
$a\mapsto a^{1+p}$, the first nonidentity power automorphism congruent to the
identity modulo $p$.

At $p=2$ the relation reads $sas^{-1}=a^{3}=a^{-1}$, so the action is inversion
and $M_2$ is the generalized dihedral group of a cyclic group of order four
([[cor-dihedral-groups-as-semidirect-products]]).
