## Definition

For a finite group $G$, the **Frattini subgroup** is $$\Phi(G):=\bigcap\{M\le G:M\text{ is maximal proper}\}.$$ If $G=1$, the family is empty and its intersection inside $G$ is $G$ itself. Thus $\Phi(1)=1$.
