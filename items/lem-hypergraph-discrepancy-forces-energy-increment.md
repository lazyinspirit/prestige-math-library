## Statement

Let $e\subseteq J$ be nonempty, let $E\in\mathcal A_e$, and let $\mathcal B_f\subseteq\mathcal A_f$ be finite algebras for $f\in\partial e$. If $\Delta_e(E\mid(\mathcal B_f)_{f\in\partial e})\ge\epsilon>0$, then there are refinements $\mathcal B_f\subseteq\mathcal B'_f\subseteq\mathcal A_f$ with $\operatorname{complex}(\mathcal B'_f)\le\operatorname{complex}(\mathcal B_f)+1$ for every $f\in\partial e$, and
$$\mathcal E_{\bigvee_{f\in\partial e}\mathcal B'_f}(E) \ge \mathcal E_{\bigvee_{f\in\partial e}\mathcal B_f}(E)+\epsilon^2.$$
No other face algebra is changed.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A nonempty face $e$, event $E\in\mathcal A_e$, finite face algebras $\mathcal B_f\subseteq\mathcal A_f$, and discrepancy at least $\epsilon>0$.

[F1] Discrepancy is a maximum over the finite family of tuples of face events ([[def-finite-coordinate-conditional-expectation-energy-and-discrepancy]]).

[F2] Conditional averages preserve means and are orthogonal to measurable tests; for nested finite algebras the energy difference is the squared norm of the conditional-average difference, and finite Cauchy–Schwarz applies ([[lem-finite-coordinate-projection-and-energy-pythagoras]]).

[F3] Complexity is the least number of generators; adjoining one event to an algebra adds at most one generator, and coordinate algebras are finite algebras ([[def-finite-partite-hypergraph-system-and-coordinate-algebras]]).

## Proof

1.1 By [F1], choose events $E_f\in\mathcal A_f$ for all $f\in\partial e$ such that, with $h=\prod_{f\in\partial e}1_{E_f}$ and $\mathcal B=\bigvee_{f\in\partial e}\mathcal B_f$, $|\mathbb E[(1_E-\mathbb E(1_E\mid\mathcal B))h]|\ge\epsilon$. Let $\mathcal B'_f$ be the algebra generated by $\mathcal B_f$ and $E_f$. Since $\mathcal A_f$ is an algebra containing both, $\mathcal B_f\subseteq\mathcal B'_f\subseteq\mathcal A_f$; adjoining one generator raises complexity by at most one. [given, F1, F3, construct]

1.2 If $|e|=1$, then $\partial e=\{\varnothing\}$ and its coordinate algebra is trivial; each test is constant, so orthogonality in [F2] gives discrepancy zero and the positive-discrepancy hypothesis is impossible. [given, F2]

2.1 Put $\mathcal B'=\bigvee_{f\in\partial e}\mathcal B'_f$. The product $h$ is $\mathcal B'$-measurable, so orthogonality of conditional averaging gives $\mathbb E[(1_E-\mathbb E(1_E\mid\mathcal B'))h]=0$. Subtracting this zero correlation from step 1.1 and applying finite Cauchy–Schwarz with $0\le h\le1$ yields $\mathbb E[(\mathbb E(1_E\mid\mathcal B')-\mathbb E(1_E\mid\mathcal B))^2]\ge\epsilon^2$. [step 1.1, F2, algebra]

3.1 If $|e|\ge2$, then $\mathcal B\subseteq\mathcal B'$ and [F2] converts step 2.1 into the asserted energy increment. Only the algebras indexed by $\partial e$ were refined. [step 2.1, F2] ∎
