## Remark

Szankowski proved that for every $1\le p<2$, the classical space $\ell^p$
contains a closed subspace without the approximation property. The cited
institutional abstract further states that related examples for $p>2$ follow
from Enflo's work.

This is a non-load-bearing literature boundary: its combinatorial proof is not
reproduced here, and no item may use this remark as a proved supplier. The
endpoint $p=2$ is deliberately absent; Hilbert spaces have the metric
approximation property.
