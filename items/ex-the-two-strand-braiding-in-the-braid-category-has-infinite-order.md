## Example

In the braid category, the braiding on the object $1\otimes1=2$ has infinite
order.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The braid category and the computation of $B_2$.

[L1] In the braid category, the braiding on $1\otimes1$ is the generator $\sigma_1\in B_2$ ([[def-the-braid-category]]).

[L2] The group $B_2$ is infinite cyclic, generated by $\sigma_1$ ([[thm-the-two-strand-braid-group-is-infinite-cyclic]]).

## Verification

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 By [L1], the braiding on $2$ is the element $\sigma_1$. [L1, given]

2.1 By [L2], all nonzero powers of $\sigma_1$ are distinct from the identity. Hence the braiding on $2$ has infinite order. [L2, step 1.1] ∎
