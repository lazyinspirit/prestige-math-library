## Definition

Let $(X,\mathcal A)$ and $(Y,\mathcal B)$ be measurable spaces. Their
**product sigma-algebra** is

$$\mathcal A \otimes \mathcal B := \sigma(\{A \times B : A \in \mathcal A,\ B \in \mathcal B\}),$$

the sigma-algebra on $X \times Y$ generated by the measurable rectangles of
[[def-measurable-rectangle]].

For the empty family, put $\prod_{i<0}X_i:=\{()\}$ and define its **empty
product sigma-algebra** by

$$\bigotimes_{i<0}\mathcal A_i:=\{\varnothing,\{()\}\}.$$

For a nonempty finite family of measurable spaces $(X_i,\mathcal A_i)$ with
$i<n$, the **finite product sigma-algebra** is defined recursively by

$$\bigotimes_{i<1}\mathcal A_i := \mathcal A_0,\qquad \bigotimes_{i<n+1}\mathcal A_i := \left(\bigotimes_{i<n}\mathcal A_i\right)\otimes\mathcal A_n.$$

This recursive definition is the finite base case used later when the library
passes to countable product constructions.
