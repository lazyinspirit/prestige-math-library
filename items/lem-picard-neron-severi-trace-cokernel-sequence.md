## Statement

Assume AC and DC. Let $k$ be algebraically closed, let $X/k$ be smooth integral of finite type with function field $K$, and let $p:Y\to X$ be an abelian scheme with identity section $e$ and generic fibre $A/K$. Let $A^\vee$ be its dual, let $(T,\tau)$ be a $K/k$-trace pair for $A^\vee$, and suppose the rigidified Poincaré construction supplies an injective homomorphism $i:A^\vee(K)\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Pic}(A)$. Put $D=i\tau(T(k))$.

For $Z=X,Y$, let $P^0(Z)$ be the subgroup of algebraically trivial line-bundle classes and put $\operatorname{NS}(Z)=\operatorname{Pic}(Z)/P^0(Z)$. Assume all of the following geometric inputs:

1. The sequence $0\to\operatorname{Pic}(X)\xrightarrow{p^*}\operatorname{Pic}(Y)\xrightarrow{j^*}\operatorname{Pic}(A)\to0$ is exact, where $j:A\to Y$ is the generic-fibre map, and $e^*p^*=\operatorname{id}$.
2. The maps $p^*$ and $e^*$ carry algebraically trivial classes to algebraically trivial classes, and $j^*P^0(Y)\subseteq D$.
3. $\operatorname{NS}(Y)$ is finitely generated.

Then $j^*P^0(Y)=D$ and the induced sequence
$$0\longrightarrow\operatorname{NS}(X)\xrightarrow{p^*}\operatorname{NS}(Y)\xrightarrow{j^*}\operatorname{Pic}(A)/D\longrightarrow0$$
is split exact, with left retraction induced by $e^*$. Moreover $\operatorname{Pic}(A)/D$ and $A^\vee(K)/\tau(T(k))$ are finitely generated. This lemma does not assert the geometric inputs, the existence of a suitable model over arbitrary constants, or the existence of the supplied trace and Picard embedding.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC, DC and the geometric data and numbered hypotheses in the Statement.

[A1] AC and DC are assumed. [[def-axiom-of-choice]], [[def-dependent-choice]]

[F1] The trace pair consists of an abelian variety $T/k$ and a homomorphism $\tau:T_K\to A^\vee$. [[def-k-trace-of-an-abelian-variety]]

[F2] Multiplication by every positive integer on $T(k)$ is surjective when $k$ is algebraically closed. [[thm-nonaffine-abelian-multiplication-finite-faithfully-flat]]

[F3] Every subgroup of a finitely generated abelian group is finitely generated. [[lem-subgroup-of-finitely-generated-abelian-group-is-finitely-generated]]

[F4] A divisible finitely generated abelian group is zero. [[lem-divisible-finitely-generated-abelian-group-is-zero]]

## Proof

**Proof technique:** take the cokernel of the split Picard sequence modulo algebraic equivalence, then eliminate the divisible trace quotient.

1.1 Put $B=j^*P^0(Y)$. By F1 and the supplied homomorphism $i$, $D$ is a subgroup of $\operatorname{Pic}(A)$; hypothesis 2 gives $B\subseteq D$. The map $j^*$ induces a surjective homomorphism $\operatorname{NS}(Y)\to\operatorname{Pic}(A)/B$: it kills $P^0(Y)$ by definition of $B$, and it is surjective by hypothesis 1. Hypothesis 2 makes $p^*$ and $e^*$ induce maps on the Néron–Severi quotients, with $e^*p^*=\operatorname{id}$; hence $p^*:\operatorname{NS}(X)\to\operatorname{NS}(Y)$ is injective and split. [F1, given, algebra]

2.1 An element of $\operatorname{NS}(Y)$ in the kernel of the induced map has a representative $L\in\operatorname{Pic}(Y)$ whose restriction lies in $B$. Choose $M\in P^0(Y)$ with $j^*M=j^*L$. Then $L-M$ restricts to zero, so by hypothesis 1 it equals $p^*N$ for some $N\in\operatorname{Pic}(X)$. Thus $[L]=p^*[N]$ in $\operatorname{NS}(Y)$. Conversely, any $p^*N$ restricts to zero by exactness, so its class lies in the kernel. Together with step 1.1 this gives a split exact sequence $$0\longrightarrow\operatorname{NS}(X)\xrightarrow{p^*}\operatorname{NS}(Y)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Pic}(A)/B\longrightarrow0.$$ [step 1.1, given, algebra]

3.1 The last quotient $Q=\operatorname{Pic}(A)/B$ is finitely generated because it is a quotient of $\operatorname{NS}(Y)$, which is finitely generated by hypothesis 3. By F1 and F2, $T(k)$ is divisible and so is its homomorphic image $D$. Its quotient $D/B$ is divisible, and it is a subgroup of $Q$ because $B\subseteq D$. By F3 it is finitely generated, and F4 forces $D/B=0$. Hence $B=D$, and the split exact sequence in step 2.1 is the displayed sequence. [A1, F1, F2, F3, F4, step 2.1, given, algebra]

4.1 The group $\operatorname{Pic}(A)/D=Q$ is finitely generated by step 3.1. Since $i$ is injective and $D=i\tau(T(k))$, it induces an injection $A^\vee(K)/\tau(T(k))\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Pic}(A)/D$. F3 now gives finite generation of this subgroup. No conclusion is drawn about the existence of the hypotheses or about a different constant field. [F3, step 3.1, given, algebra] ∎
