## Statement

For the disk atlas of [[lem-gts-geometrically-tame-disk-compactness-and-real-nodal-atlas]], a chosen relative-spin datum from [[def-gts-relatively-spin-lagrangian-data]] canonically orients its Cauchy–Riemann determinant local systems, after choosing the ordinary ordered obstruction and auxiliary normal orientations. These orientations agree under tuple coordinate changes and actual real/complex nodal gluing. Let $n=\dim L$. A Morse generator of homological index $m\in\{0,\ldots,n\}$ has cohomological degree $q=n-m$ and suspended degree $q-1$. For $k$ ordered inputs write $q_j=n-m_j$ for their cohomological degrees. If an inner operation with $s$ inputs is inserted after $r$ inputs and before $t$ inputs, where $r,s,t$ are nonnegative integers with $r+s+t=k$, the real codimension-one disk-face contraction has exactly the insertion sign $(-1)^{\sum_{j=1}^{r}(q_j-1)}$, with empty sum zero. The insertion count $r$ is independent of the Morse indices $m_j$. Interior sphere nodes have real codimension two. A finite symmetry of the specified disk atlas, intertwining its Cauchy–Riemann section and nodal gluing data, with specified lift preserving the orientation and relative-spin datum acts by the actual oriented determinant maps; no such atlas action or spin lift is assumed to exist automatically.
