## Definition

Let $Y$ be a closed connected oriented $3$-manifold, let $K\subset Y$ be a smooth oriented null-homologous knot, and let $F\subset Y$ be a compact connected oriented embedded surface with oriented boundary $K$. Choose a closed tubular neighborhood $N(K)$, put $M=Y\setminus\operatorname{int}N(K)$, and let $\gamma_\mu$ be the union of two disjoint annuli on $\partial M$ whose core sutures are parallel meridians of $K$ with opposite orientations. Delete a collar of $\partial F$ to regard $F$ as a properly embedded surface in $(M,\gamma_\mu)$, with relative class $[F]\in H_2(M,\partial M;\mathbb Z)$. Let $t_\mu$ be the meridional boundary trivialization of the oriented plane field used in the relative Spin-c definition. For $\mathfrak{s}\in\operatorname{Spin}^c(M,\gamma_\mu)$, its Alexander grading is

$$A_F(\mathfrak{s})=\frac12\left\langle c_1(\mathfrak{s},t_\mu),[F]\right\rangle,$$

with the standard sign convention that gives the usual Alexander grading for knots in $S^3$. For a balanced admissible sutured Heegaard diagram of $(M,\gamma_\mu)$, the Spin-c summand $SFH(M,\gamma_\mu,\mathfrak{s};\mathbb Z)$ is the homology of the integral sutured Floer disk-count complex generated by the Heegaard generators assigned to $\mathfrak{s}$. Define the integral knot Floer group in Alexander grading $i$ by

$$\widehat{\mathrm{HFK}}(Y,K,[F],i;\mathbb Z)=\bigoplus_{\substack{\mathfrak{s}\in\operatorname{Spin}^c(M,\gamma_\mu)\\ A_F(\mathfrak{s})=i}}SFH(M,\gamma_\mu,\mathfrak{s};\mathbb Z).$$

This fixes the integral coefficient convention. The equivalent associated-graded closed Heegaard-complex presentation, invariance under changing diagrams, and comparison with the mod-two grid complex are separate theorems; this definition supplies none of their proofs or a coefficient-change map.
