## Open problem

Let $M=SL_2(\mathbb Z)\backslash\mathbb H$ and let $(u_j)$ be any sequence of Petersson-norm-one weight-zero Maass cusp forms for $M$ whose positive-sign Laplace eigenvalues tend to $+\infty$, with no assumption that the $u_j$ are eigenfunctions of the Hecke operators. Is every quantum limit of $(u_j)$, defined by convergence against compactly supported continuous functions on $M$, equal to the normalized hyperbolic area measure on $M$?
