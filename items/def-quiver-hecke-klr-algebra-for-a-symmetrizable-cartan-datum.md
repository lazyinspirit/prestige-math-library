## Definition

Let $(I,A,D,P,P^{\vee},q)$ be a symmetrizable Cartan datum with no imaginary simple roots, so $a_{ii}=2$, and put $b_{ij}:=d_i\,a_{ij}=(\alpha_i\mid\alpha_j)$, a symmetric matrix with $b_{ii}=2d_i>0$ ([[def-symmetrizable-cartan-datum-for-a-quantum-group]], [[def-kac-moody-root-lattice-height-and-positive-cone]]). Fix a field $k$. A **KLR datum** on it is a family $Q=(Q_{ij})_{i\ne j}$ of polynomials $Q_{ij}\in k[u,v]$ such that

(Q1) $Q_{ij}\ne0$ for $i\ne j$ and $Q_{ii}:=0$;

(Q2) $Q_{ij}(u,v)=Q_{ji}(v,u)$ for all $i\ne j$;

(Q3) $Q_{ij}$ is homogeneous of total degree $-2b_{ij}$ for the degree assignments $\deg u:=2d_i$, $\deg v:=2d_j$;

(Q4) the two extremal coefficients of $Q_{ij}$, namely that of $u^{-a_{ij}}$ and that of $v^{-a_{ji}}$, are nonzero.

For a chosen finite quiver with a compatible automorphism having no arrow within a vertex orbit, write $i\cdot j=b_{ij}$ and let $d_{ij}$ be the number of arrow orbits directed from vertex orbit $i$ to vertex orbit $j$; the specialization applies when its induced Cartan datum is the fixed one. Then one takes $Q_{ij}(u,v)=(-1)^{d_{ij}}(u^{l/(i\cdot i)}-v^{l/(j\cdot j)})^{-2(i\cdot j)/l}$ with $l=\mathrm{lcm}(i\cdot i,j\cdot j)$, and $Q_{ij}=1$ when $a_{ij}=0$; the cyclic affine $A_1^{(1)}$ double edge $a_{ij}=a_{ji}=-2$ is the instance $Q_{ij}(u,v)=-(u-v)^2$ up to the fixed normalization of the top coefficients. The simply-laced standard choice is $Q_{ij}(u,v)=u+v$ for $a_{ij}=-1$.

**The algebra.** For $n\ge0$ let $I^n$ be the set of sequences, let $e(\nu)$ for $\nu\in I^n$ be idempotents, and let $x_a$ ($1\le a\le n$) and $\tau_a$ ($1\le a\le n-1$) be generators. The **KLR algebra** $R_n(Q)$ is the unital associative $k$-algebra generated by the $e(\nu),x_a,\tau_a$ subject to the relations, for all $\nu\in I^n$ (with $s_a$ the adjacent transposition acting on positions $a,a+1$):

(1) $e(\nu)e(\nu')=\delta_{\nu\nu'}e(\nu)$ and $\sum_{\nu\in I^n}e(\nu)=1$;

(2) $x_ae(\nu)=e(\nu)x_a$ and $x_ax_b=x_bx_a$;

(3) $\tau_ae(\nu)=e(s_a\nu)\tau_a$;

(4) $\tau_a^2e(\nu)=Q_{\nu_a\nu_{a+1}}(x_a,x_{a+1})e(\nu)$;

(5) $\tau_a\tau_b=\tau_b\tau_a$ whenever $|a-b|>1$;

(6) $(\tau_ax_b-x_{s_a(b)}\tau_a)e(\nu)=0$ if $b\notin\{a,a+1\}$ or $\nu_a\ne\nu_{a+1}$, while if $\nu_a=\nu_{a+1}$ one has $(\tau_ax_a-x_{a+1}\tau_a)e(\nu)=e(\nu)$ and $(\tau_ax_{a+1}-x_a\tau_a)e(\nu)=-e(\nu)$ (the sign normalization of the divided difference $\tau_a(f)=(f-s_af)/(x_a-x_{a+1})$; Rouquier's printed relation has the opposite global sign, the two conventions being exchanged by $\tau_a\mapsto-\tau_a$);

(7) $(\tau_{a+1}\tau_a\tau_{a+1}-\tau_a\tau_{a+1}\tau_a)e(\nu)=0$ if $\nu_a\ne\nu_{a+2}$, and $(\tau_{a+1}\tau_a\tau_{a+1}-\tau_a\tau_{a+1}\tau_a)e(\nu)=\dfrac{Q_{\nu_a\nu_{a+1}}(x_a,x_{a+1})-Q_{\nu_a\nu_{a+1}}(x_{a+2},x_{a+1})}{x_{a+2}-x_a}e(\nu)$ if $\nu_a=\nu_{a+2}$, the numerator carrying the sign of the $\tau$-normalization of (6) (Rouquier's printed numerator is its negative); the quotient is a polynomial in $x_a,x_{a+1},x_{a+2}$ because $Q_{ij}$ is a polynomial in its first variable and the numerator vanishes at $x_{a+2}=x_a$ ([[def-polynomial-ring-over-a-commutative-ring]], [[def-multivariate-polynomial-ring-by-iteration]]).

For $\beta\in Q_+$ put $I^{\beta}:=\{\nu\in I^{|\beta|}:\sum_a\alpha_{\nu_a}=\beta\}$, $e(\beta):=\sum_{\nu\in I^{\beta}}e(\nu)$ and $R(\beta):=e(\beta)R_{|\beta|}(Q)e(\beta)=\bigoplus_{\nu,\eta\in I^{\beta}}e(\nu)R_{|\beta|}(Q)e(\eta)$, and put $R:=\bigoplus_{\beta\in Q_+}R(\beta)$. The presentation is the sequence form of Rouquier's $H_n(Q)$ via $1_\nu=e(\nu)$, $x_{a,\nu}=x_ae(\nu)$, $\tau_{a,\nu}=\tau_ae(\nu)$; no sign or orientation beyond $Q$ is part of the datum, and $R(0)=k$.

## Remarks

**Well-definedness.** $R_n(Q)$ is defined as the quotient of the free unital associative $k$-algebra on the symbols $e(\nu)$ ($\nu\in I^n$), $x_a$, $\tau_a$ by the two-sided ideal generated by the displayed relations, so no existence issue arises. Two points deserve record. First, relation (7) is stated as an equality in $k[x_a,x_{a+1},x_{a+2}]e(\nu)$: writing $Q(u,v)=\sum_a u^a q_a(v)$, the difference $Q(u,v)-Q(w,v)=\sum_a(u^a-w^a)q_a(v)$ is divisible by $u-w$ in the polynomial ring, and its quotient evaluated at $(u,w)=(x_a,x_{a+2})$, $v=x_{a+1}$ gives the displayed correction; the special case $a_{ij}=0$ with $Q_{ij}$ a nonzero constant is included, where the correction vanishes and the ordinary braid relation holds on $\nu_a=\nu_{a+2}$. Second, the mixed relations are word-component identities: the final $e(\nu)$ is essential, since a global dot commutator has different components on equal and unequal colors. On an equal-color component they are the identities $\partial_ax_a-x_{a+1}\partial_a=1$ and $\partial_ax_{a+1}-x_a\partial_a=-1$ for $\partial_a=(1-s_a)/(x_a-x_{a+1})$. The two global sign conventions for $\tau_a$ are exchanged by $\tau_a\mapsto-\tau_a$, which preserves (4) and flips the signs in (6) and (7); this page fixes the convention of (6) once and for all and every later item uses it.

**Choice.** The definitional clauses are choice-free: they name a presented algebra and its weight decomposition over a fixed datum, with no selection of representatives or of auxiliary sets.
