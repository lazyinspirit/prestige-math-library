## Definition

Let $R$ be a commutative unital ring, let $q,\rho\in R^\times$, put $z=q-q^{-1}$, and choose $\delta\in R$ with $z(\delta-1)=\rho-\rho^{-1}$. For $n\ge2$, the BMW algebra $BMW_n(R;q,\rho,\delta)$ is the unital associative $R$-algebra generated by invertible $g_i$ and elements $e_i$ for $1\le i<n$, subject to the following relations (an adjacent relation is imposed only when both indices exist):

- braid and distant braid: $g_i g_{i+1}g_i=g_{i+1}g_i g_{i+1}$ and $g_i g_j=g_jg_i$ for $|i-j|\ge2$;
- distant cups and caps: $g_i e_j=e_jg_i$ and $e_i e_j=e_j e_i$ for $|i-j|\ge2$;
- adjacent cup-cap: $e_i e_{i\pm1}e_i=e_i$, $g_i g_{i\pm1}e_i=e_{i\pm1}e_i$, and $e_i g_{i\pm1}g_i=e_i e_{i\pm1}$;
- Kauffman skein and untwisting: $g_i-g_i^{-1}=z(1-e_i)$, $g_i e_i=e_i g_i=\rho^{-1}e_i$, and $e_i g_{i\pm1}e_i=\rho e_i$;
- loop: $e_i^2=\delta e_i$.

For the boundary ranks, set $BMW_0(R;q,\rho,\delta)=BMW_1(R;q,\rho,\delta)=R$, with the empty and single straight tangle as their respective units.

If $z$ is invertible, the parameter relation reads $\delta=1+(\rho-\rho^{-1})/z$. If $z$ is not invertible, the presentation uses the universal coefficient relation $z(\delta-1)=\rho-\rho^{-1}$ and does not divide by $z$. The quotient by the two-sided ideal generated by all $e_i$ is the cup-cap-zero Hecke quotient; this definition does not assert a BMW diagram-basis theorem or a BMW quantum-centralizer theorem.


## Remarks
