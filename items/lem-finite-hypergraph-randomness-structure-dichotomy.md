## Statement

Let $\mathcal H_d$ be a finite $d$-uniform pattern. For each $e\in\mathcal H_d$, let $\mathcal B_e\subseteq\mathcal A_e$ be a finite algebra of complexity at most an integer $m\ge0$. For each $f$ in the codimension-one face family $\partial\mathcal H_d=\bigcup_{e\in\mathcal H_d}\partial e$, let $\mathcal C_f\subseteq\mathcal A_f$ be a finite algebra of complexity at most an integer $M\ge0$. Given $\epsilon,\delta>0$, put $T=|\mathcal H_d|\,2^{2^m}$ and $N=\lceil T/\delta^2\rceil+1$. There are refinements $\mathcal C_f\subseteq\mathcal C'_f\subseteq\mathcal A_f$ with complexity at most $M+N$ such that either:

1. for every $e\in\mathcal H_d$ and $E\in\mathcal B_e$, the energy gain from $\bigvee_{f\in\partial e}\mathcal C_f$ to $\bigvee_{f\in\partial e}\mathcal C'_f$ is at most $\epsilon^2$ and $\Delta_e(E\mid(\mathcal C'_f)_{f\in\partial e})\le\delta$; or
2. for some $e\in\mathcal H_d$ and $E\in\mathcal B_e$, that energy gain is at least $\epsilon^2$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A finite $d$-uniform pattern, finite top algebras of complexity at most $m$, finite lower-face algebras of complexity at most $M$, and real $\epsilon,\delta>0$.

[F1] If a top event has discrepancy greater than a positive threshold, adjoining one witness event on each skeleton face raises the joined energy by at least the threshold squared, with at most one new generator per face ([[lem-hypergraph-discrepancy-forces-energy-increment]]).

[F2] Every event energy lies in $[0,1]$ ([[lem-finite-coordinate-projection-and-energy-pythagoras]]).

[F3] An algebra generated by at most $m$ sets has at most $2^m$ atoms and at most $2^{2^m}$ members ([[lem-finite-coordinate-projection-and-energy-pythagoras]]).

## Proof

1.1 Initialize the current lower algebras at $\mathcal C_f$. If every top event has discrepancy at most $\delta$, stop with alternative 1. Otherwise choose $e\in\mathcal H_d$ and $E\in\mathcal B_e$ whose discrepancy is greater than $\delta$. Apply [F1] to refine the current algebras on $\partial e$; all other face algebras stay fixed, and the energy of this particular top event increases by at least $\delta^2$. [given, F1, construct]

2.1 If any top event's energy gain relative to the original $\mathcal C_f$ is now at least $\epsilon^2$, stop with alternative 2. Otherwise repeat step 1.1. There are at most $T$ pairs $(e,E)$ by [F3], each energy lies in $[0,1]$ by [F2], and every repetition increases their total energy by at least $\delta^2$. Hence after at most $\lceil T/\delta^2\rceil$ repetitions either alternative 2 occurs or no discrepancy greater than $\delta$ remains, giving alternative 1. [step 1.1, given, F2, F3, algebra]

3.1 Each repetition adjoins at most one generator to any lower face algebra, so after at most $N$ repetitions every resulting complexity is at most $M+N$. The refinements stay inside $\mathcal A_f$, and the two stopping rules give exactly the alternatives in the Statement. If $\mathcal H_d$ is empty, no refinement is needed and alternative 1 is vacuous. [step 1.1, step 2.1, F1, F3, algebra] ∎
