The theorem [[thm-greens-theorem-for-finite-unions-of-elementary-regions]] applies only when an elementary decomposition and the stated finite gluing data are supplied. It neither constructs the interior of an arbitrary Jordan curve nor proves that such an interior admits this decomposition. The general Jordan-domain theorem requires additional curve-separation and decomposition results, so it is not asserted here.
