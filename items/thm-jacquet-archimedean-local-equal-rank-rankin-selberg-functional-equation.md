## Statement

Let $K=\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$, $n\geq1$, and let $\pi,\pi'$ be irreducible admissible generic representations of $\mathrm{GL}_{n}(K)$ in the Casselman–Wallach class, with Whittaker models formed using continuous Whittaker functionals. Fix a nontrivial continuous unitary additive character $\psi$ of $K$, Whittaker functions $W,W'$ for $\psi,\psi^{-1}$, and a Schwartz function $\Phi$ on $K^n$. Write $Z(s,W,W',\Phi)$ for the equal-rank local integral, $w_n$ for the anti-diagonal matrix, $\widetilde W(g)=W(w_n{}^tg^{-1})$, and $\widetilde W'(g)=W'(w_n{}^tg^{-1})$. Let $\widehat\Phi$ be the Fourier transform for the self-dual measure associated with $\psi$, let $\gamma(s,\pi\times\pi',\psi)$ be the local gamma factor of the tensor product of the Weil parameters, and let $\omega_\pi$ be the central character of $\pi$. Then $Z(s,W,W',\Phi)$ converges absolutely for $\operatorname{Re}(s)$ sufficiently large, extends meromorphically to $s\in\mathbb C$ as a holomorphic multiple of $L(s,\pi\times\pi')$, and satisfies

$$Z(1-s,\widetilde W,\widetilde W',\widehat\Phi)=\omega_\pi(-1)^{n-1}\gamma(s,\pi\times\pi',\psi)Z(s,W,W',\Phi).$$

For $K_\mathrm{max}=O(n)$ when $K=\mathbb R$ and $K_\mathrm{max}=U(n)$ when $K=\mathbb C$, the local factor is generated by the equal-rank integrals with finite-$K_\mathrm{max}$-type Whittaker functions and Schwartz data; equivalently, a finite linear combination of such integrals equals $L(s,\pi\times\pi')$. The integral family is bounded at infinity in vertical strips away from poles.

## Analytic prerequisite and Fourier convention

[[lem-archimedean-tate-zeta-integral-and-fourier-functional-equation]] gives a separate local proof of the scalar real/complex Tate integrals for all continuous quasicharacters, including full Schwartz continuation and both Fourier signs. Here Jacquet's convention is the negative kernel $\widehat\Phi(x)=\int\Phi(y)\psi(-\langle x,y\rangle)\,dy$, and the Weil-parameter gamma factor is taken with that same convention; for a real sign character and $\psi(x)=e^{2\pi ix}$ its scalar epsilon is $-i$. The positive-kernel principal-series convention must be transported explicitly before comparing factors. This scalar prerequisite proves neither the higher-rank Whittaker estimates and analytic induction nor the full finite-type generation theorem. The original full Statement remains an unresolved local proof obligation. No general local Langlands or global sign-compatibility proof is inferred.
