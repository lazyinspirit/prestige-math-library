## Definition

Following EGNO, a **spherical structure** is a pivotal structure $a$ on a tensor
category such that

$$\dim_a(X)=\dim_a(X^\vee)$$

for every object $X$.

A **spherical category** is a tensor category equipped with a spherical
structure.
