## Statement

Assume Countable Choice. On the oriented complex torus $T^2=\mathbb R^2/\mathbb Z^2$, with coordinate $x+iy$ and orientation $dx\wedge dy$, take
$$\alpha=\{y=0\},\qquad\beta=\{x=0\},\qquad\gamma=\{x+y=1/2\},$$
and a basepoint $w$ in the hexagonal complementary region, for example $(3/4,1/4)$. A second protected point $z$ may be chosen in that same region. Use negative inputs $p=\alpha\cap\beta$ and $q=\beta\cap\gamma$ and output $r=\alpha\cap\gamma$, so the positive boundary-edge order is alpha,gamma,beta. The source is the three-marked disk, without a translation quotient.

There are exactly two zero-$w$ holomorphic triangles for the integrable datum, each a regular isolated solution. Their domains are the projections of the two triangles with planar vertices
$$\Delta_+:(0,0),(1/2,0),(0,1/2),\qquad\Delta_-:(0,0),(-1/2,0),(0,-1/2).$$
Both have zero $z$ multiplicity when $z$ is in the stated region. Each pair diagram has a single generator with zero differential, so the unique beta-gamma generator is an actual cycle and actual top input.

Fill the beta-gamma three-sphere boundary of the triple cobordism by a ball. The resulting two-ended cobordism is the minus-one framed unknot trace, equivalently the punctured negative projective plane. Orient its exceptional generator $E$ by the capped periodic domain $\Delta_+-\Delta_-$. Its self-intersection is $-1$. The two actual triangle Spin-c labels $\mathfrak t_+,\mathfrak t_-$ satisfy
$$\langle c_1(\mathfrak t_+),E\rangle=-1,\qquad\langle c_1(\mathfrak t_-),E\rangle=1.$$
In EACH of these individual labels the actual triangle map sends the input hat generator to the output hat generator with coefficient one. Their SUM is zero over $\mathbb F_2$. The individual assertions are separate count-one computations, not deductions from that cancellation. The same individual counts hold for sufficiently small regular compatible perturbations retaining the protected marks and actual corner geometry.

This is a literal local two-handle model and its individual component calculation. Identification of a different surgery presentation with this model, filtered crossing-change comparison and a general cobordism functoriality theorem require their own actual geometric operations.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The explicit oriented torus, its three labelled curves, the basepoint region and the actual incoming/outgoing source convention.

[F1] The compact scalar disk Cauchy–Riemann problem with boundary $T S^1$ is onto of real index three and has kernel $a+ib\zeta-\overline a\zeta^2$. The whole-strip operator with any two transverse constant real lines has a bounded inverse and its exact angular Fourier expansions. ([[lem-scalar-disk-riemann-hilbert-fredholm-index]], [[lem-transverse-linear-strip-cauchy-riemann-isomorphism]])

[F2] Actual polygon labels have coefficient-one Spin-c difference $\mathfrak s(u)-\mathfrak s(v)=j\operatorname{PD}H(D(u)-D(v))$. Determinants change by the square of the difference line; this is a finite integral construction including equivariance under carried Morse data. ([[lem-heegaard-polygon-spin-c-difference-is-the-capped-periodic-domain]])

[F3] Actual domain positivity, domain-energy bounds, bounded-energy stable-source compactness and the actual Newton zero-chart theorem hold. Zero-basepoint spheres and one-torus disk bubbles are absent in this complete one-marked torus setting. ([[lem-nearly-symmetric-polygons-have-positive-domains-and-degree-energy-bounds]], [[thm-bounded-energy-polygons-have-stable-source-bubble-and-strip-limits]], [[lem-countable-choice-quantitative-newton-correction-and-smooth-zero-chart]])

## Proof

1.1 The curves have exactly the three stated distinct pair intersections. Their complement consists of the two displayed open triangles and one open hexagon. Write a region chain by its three coefficients. The full-circle edge equations for a periodic chain force it to be $aP+b\Sigma$, where $P=\Delta_+-\Delta_-$: following the two half-edges of each circle gives $\partial P=\alpha-\beta-\gamma$, and equality of the coefficients on the remaining two sides leaves the common constant $b$. Thus the normalized periodic lattice is $\mathbb ZP$, with coefficients $(a,-a,0)$ and hence both signs when nonzero. With the chosen corner lifts every other triangle domain differs from $\Delta_+$ by such a periodic chain. Zero $w$ makes $b=0$, and nonnegativity gives $1+a\ge0$ and $-a\ge0$. Its only integer possibilities are $a=0,-1$, the two displayed domains. This computation controls actual maps, without supposing that every formal domain is realized. Each pair of curve slopes is an integral basis of torus homology. A pair Whitney disk lifts to the plane; its two boundary winding integers must therefore both be zero. Since the plane is contractible, the only pair class is constant. Its domain is zero, so the actual domain-energy identity in [F3] gives energy zero for a holomorphic representative. There are consequently no nonconstant pair strips, and the single pair generator is an actual cycle. A sphere also lifts to the contractible plane. A disk with boundary on any one of the three primitive circles has boundary winding zero because that circle injects into torus homology; it lifts with boundary on one straight line, contracts there, and has zero area by Stokes. Thus spheres and one-circle disk bubbles are constant, independently of a genericity assertion. [given, F3, construct, algebra]

2.1 Construct the positive triangle explicitly on the upper half-plane. Choose the branches with arguments between zero and pi and put $g(\zeta)=c\zeta^{-1/2}(\zeta-1)^{-3/4}$, where $c$ has argument $3\pi/4$. Its boundary phases are $-\pi/2$ on the negative real axis, zero on $(0,1)$ and $3\pi/4$ on $(1,\infty)$. Integrate from zero and choose $|c|$ so the value at one is $1/2$. The integral converges at zero and one, and at infinity because $g=O(|\zeta|^{-5/4})$. The boundary images are therefore three straight finite segments. Closing a large upper semicircle gives zero additional integral in the limit, so the third vertex is $i/2$ and the boundary runs once around $\Delta_+$. The derivative has no interior zero. The argument principle, applied to the boundary with small corner semicircles removed and then taking their limits, gives exactly one preimage for every interior target point and none outside: the boundary winding is respectively one and zero, and the corner contributions tend to zero off the three vertices. Thus the integral is the marked biholomorphism onto $\Delta_+$. Its corner powers are $1/2,1/4,1/4$, so it has the positive transverse-end decay and finite energy. Negating it constructs the other triangle. Conversely a map of either domain has interior image in that triangle, by positivity and its zero coefficients outside. Its degree there is one, so it is the same marked biholomorphism; a disk automorphism fixing its three ordered boundary points is the identity. This proves existence and uniqueness as actual maps. [F3, step 1.1, construct, algebra]

2.2 Identify the actual handle and its sign. Realize this genus-one splitting as the standard Clifford splitting in the unit three-sphere in $\mathbb C^2$, with $x,y$ its two phase coordinates. Take the alpha handlebody to be $|z_1|\le|z_2|$ and the beta handlebody the other side; their meridians are respectively the horizontal alpha and vertical beta, and the induced alpha boundary orientation is $dx\wedge dy$. The beta core is an unknot. Its zero longitude is alpha, since alpha bounds the opposite handlebody disk; its meridian is beta. Replacing beta by $\gamma=\alpha-\beta$ therefore attaches a two-handle along that unknot with one negative twist relative to its spanning-disk framing. Cap its core by that spanning disk. A parallel cap has one negative transverse intersection, exactly the minus-one attaching twist, so its square is $-1$. The full-curve relation $\partial P=\alpha-\beta-\gamma$ caps to this same generator, with the orientation prescribed in the Statement. The beta-gamma boundary is a three-sphere because its slope determinant is one; filling that boundary by the ball makes this precisely the two-ended handle trace. This verifies the local cobordism correspondence, rather than assuming a grading formula for it. [step 1.1, construct, algebra]

3.1 Prove actual onto-ness rather than infer it from the parameterization. On each puncture end the target lines are constant transverse lines, so use [F1]'s whole-strip inverse to solve an arbitrary end forcing, then cut off its solution. The remaining forcing has compact support. In the compact disk source coordinate let $f$ be the triangle map just constructed and write the remaining unknown as $\xi=f'\eta$. Its boundary condition is $\eta\in TS^1$, since $f'$ carries the source tangent to the appropriate target line. Dividing the compact residual forcing by $f'$ gives a compactly supported $L^2$ forcing, as $f'$ has no zero on that core. Solve its compact problem by [F1]. Near each of the three marked boundary points the forcing is zero; the solution is holomorphic and reflects across the tangent-line boundary after division by $i\zeta$. Its three tangent evaluations are therefore bounded by reflected Cauchy estimates, not by an arbitrary $H^1$ point trace. Evaluation of the displayed three-real-dimensional kernel at those three tangent fibers is an isomorphism: after division by $i\zeta$ the three columns are $1,\cos\theta,\sin\theta$, and three distinct circle points are not collinear. Subtract this kernel vector to make all three values zero. Then $\eta=O(\zeta-r_j)$ at each mark and $\xi=f'\eta$ has precisely the positive corner power, with bounded cylindrical $H^1$ norm. This constructs a bounded right inverse. For a homogeneous actual solution the same quotient extends at each mark and vanishes there by the exact strip Fourier expansion; thus it lies in that compact kernel and is zero. The operator is consequently an actual isomorphism and has index zero. Weak local bootstrap and the same end inverses give the higher Sobolev realizations. Both triangles are regular, individually of count one. [F1, step 2.1, construct, algebra]

3.2 Apply [F2] to the actual two maps. Their label difference is $\mathfrak t_+-\mathfrak t_-=\operatorname{PD}E$, so their determinant evaluations differ by $2E^2=-2$. Determine their individual values without an absolute map-degree theorem. The holomorphic involution $(x,y)\mapsto(-x,-y)$ preserves all three unoriented curves and exchanges the two triangles. It extends over each solid torus by simultaneous conjugation of its disk and circle coordinates, preserving the three-dimensional orientation, and hence over the actual triple cobordism. It takes $P$ to $-P$ and $E$ to $-E$. The basepoint and its image lie in the same hexagonal region; moving it there changes no label, as its trajectory difference bounds in that region and the finite difference cochain vanishes. Equivariance of the actual lift construction in [F2] therefore identifies the two triangle labels under that involution. Their Chern evaluations are negatives of one another. Together with their difference $-2$, this gives the stated individual values $-1,+1$. In particular they are DISTINCT actual Spin-c labels, not two terms of one component. [F2, step 1.1, step 2.1, step 2.2, construct, algebra]

4.1 At each label the zero-$w$ count contains exactly its one triangle by steps 1.1–3.2. Its input, top intermediate cycle and output are the respective single generators of the three pair complexes. Thus each component sends $1$ to $1$. Summing the two component maps gives zero over the coefficient field, but no assertion about either individual map was deduced from that sum. Finally the same two individual counts persist under sufficiently small compatible regular perturbations. The finite domain list gives a uniform energy bound. A convergent zero-$w$ sequence has no bubbles by [F3], and no nonconstant pair level can occur for the integrable limit by step 1.1. There is no conformal degeneration of a three-marked disk. Hence every limit is one of the two actual triangles. The exact end inverses promote convergence to its full map chart, where step 3.1 and [F3]'s Newton theorem give one unique perturbed solution. Their labels remain locally constant by [F2], so the individual counts remain one. The optional second mark is outside both domains and has multiplicity zero throughout. This proves exactly the local individual nonzero operation asserted, without converting total-map cancellation into a component claim. [F2, F3, step 1.1, step 2.1, step 3.1, step 3.2, construct, algebra] ∎
