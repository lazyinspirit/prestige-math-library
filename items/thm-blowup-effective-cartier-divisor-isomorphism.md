## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice, inherited from the blowup construction
([[def-axiom-of-choice]]). Let $\mathcal I$ be a quasi-coherent ideal sheaf of
finite type on a scheme $X$ that is invertible as an $\mathcal O_X$-module
([[def-invertible-sheaf]]) — equivalently, $Z=V(\mathcal I)$ is an effective
Cartier divisor on $X$ ([[def-effective-cartier-divisor]],
[[def-cartier-divisor]]). Then the blowup
$\pi\colon\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}X\to X$ of
[[def-blowup-scheme-along-ideal]] is an isomorphism. If $\mathcal I$ is
generated by a single nonzerodivisor $f$ on an affine open
$U=\operatorname{Spec}A$, then the blowup is
$\operatorname{Spec}A[I/f]=\operatorname{Spec}A$ over $U$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A scheme $X$, a quasi-coherent ideal sheaf $\mathcal I$ of finite type with zero scheme $Z$, and the blowup $\pi\colon\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}X\to X$.

[F1] [[def-effective-cartier-divisor]] and [[def-cartier-divisor]]: An effective Cartier divisor on $X$ is given by an open cover $\{U_i\}$ with regular sections $f_i\in\Gamma(U_i,\mathcal O_X)$, regular meaning that multiplication by the germ $(f_i)_x$ is injective for every $x\in U_i$, and the local principal ideals $f_i\mathcal O_{U_i}$ glue to an ideal sheaf $\mathcal I_D$; conversely an invertible ideal sheaf is locally generated by one element and that generator is a nonzerodivisor, because the map $\mathcal O_{U_i}\to\mathcal I|_{U_i}$, $1\mapsto f_i$, is an isomorphism.

[F2] [[lem-cartier-divisor-sheaf-invertible]]: For a Cartier divisor $D$ the sheaf $\mathcal O_X(D)$ is invertible and locally freely generated by $f_i^{-1}$; in particular the ideal sheaf of an effective Cartier divisor is an invertible $\mathcal O_X$-module.

[F3] [[thm-affine-blowup-standard-charts]] and [[lem-affine-blowup-algebra-properties]]: For $I=(f_0,\dots,f_r)$ on an affine $U=\operatorname{Spec}A$ the standard charts are $\operatorname{Spec}A[I/f_i]$ and they cover $\operatorname{Bl}_IU$; for a principal ideal $I=(f)$ with $f$ a nonzerodivisor the affine blowup algebra is $A[I/f]=A$.

[F4] [[thm-blowup-universal-property]]: For every $X$-scheme $Y\to X$ whose inverse image of $Z$ is an effective Cartier divisor there is a unique $X$-morphism $Y\to\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}X$; equivalently $\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}X$ is final among such $X$-schemes, and [[cor-blowup-unique-up-to-unique-isomorphism]] makes the resulting identifications unique.

## Proof

1.1 If $\mathcal I$ is invertible then it is locally generated by a single element $f_i$ on an open cover $\{U_i\}$ and the generator is a nonzerodivisor by [F1], so the zero scheme $Z$ is an effective Cartier divisor with local equations $f_i$; conversely if $Z=V(\mathcal I)$ is an effective Cartier divisor with local equations $f_i$, then $\mathcal I|_{U_i}=(f_i)$ and [F2] exhibits $\mathcal I$ as invertible. [F1, F2]

2.1 Suppose $\mathcal I$ is invertible. On each affine open $U=\operatorname{Spec}A$ with $\mathcal I|_U=(f)$ and $f$ a nonzerodivisor, the single standard chart of the blowup is $\operatorname{Spec}A[I/f]=\operatorname{Spec}A=U$ by [F3], and it covers $\operatorname{Bl}_IU=\pi^{-1}(U)$; hence $\pi^{-1}(U)\to U$ is an isomorphism. [F3, step 1.1]

3.1 The restriction $\pi^{-1}(U)\to U$ is an isomorphism for the members of an affine open cover of $X$ by step 2.1, and being an isomorphism is local on the target, so $\pi\colon\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}X\to X$ is an isomorphism; its inverse is characterized by the universal property [F4] applied to the identity of $X$, whose inverse image of $Z$ is the effective Cartier divisor $Z$ itself, so the inverse is the unique $X$-morphism $X\to\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}X$ supplied there, and it is unique by [[cor-blowup-unique-up-to-unique-isomorphism]]. [F4, step 2.1]

3.2 In the affine case $X=\operatorname{Spec}A$ and $\mathcal I=\widetilde I$ with $I=(f)$ for a nonzerodivisor $f$, step 2.1 with $U=X$ gives $\operatorname{Bl}_I\operatorname{Spec}A=\operatorname{Spec}A[I/f]=\operatorname{Spec}A$, as claimed. [F3, step 2.1]

4.1 Steps 1.1, 3.1 and 3.2 prove the statement: invertible centers are exactly effective Cartier divisors, and the blowup along such an ideal sheaf is an isomorphism, computed on an affine chart as $\operatorname{Spec}A[I/f]=\operatorname{Spec}A$ when $I$ is generated by one nonzerodivisor. [step 1.1, step 3.1, step 3.2] ∎

## Remarks

- This is the case in which a blowup changes nothing at all: it is an isomorphism precisely when the centre is already Cartier, so nontrivial blowups require a centre that fails to be Cartier in a neighbourhood of itself.
- Combining the statement with [[def-blowup-fractional-ideal]] shows that $\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}X$ depends only on the class of $\mathcal I$ modulo invertible rescaling, a class that is trivial exactly in the Cartier case.
