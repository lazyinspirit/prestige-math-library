## Definition

Let $\Lambda$ be a commutative noetherian normal domain with fraction field $F$, and let $M$ be a finitely generated torsion $\Lambda$-module. For each height-one prime $\mathfrak p$, the localization $\Lambda_{\mathfrak p}$ is a discrete valuation ring; write $v_{\mathfrak p}$ for its normalized valuation and $n_{\mathfrak p}=\operatorname{length}_{\Lambda_{\mathfrak p}}(M_{\mathfrak p})$. Only finitely many $n_{\mathfrak p}$ are nonzero. The characteristic ideal is the divisorial fractional ideal
$$\operatorname{char}_\Lambda(M):=\{x\in F:v_{\mathfrak p}(x)\ge n_{\mathfrak p}\text{ for every height-one }\mathfrak p\}=\bigcap_{\operatorname{ht}(\mathfrak p)=1}\mathfrak p^{(n_{\mathfrak p})},$$
where $\mathfrak p^{(n)}=\{x\in F:v_{\mathfrak p}(x)\ge n\}$ is the symbolic divisorial power. If $M$ is finitely presented over a commutative ring $A$, its zeroth Fitting ideal $\operatorname{Fitt}_A(M)$ is the ideal generated by the maximal minors of a finite presentation matrix, with the presentation-independent convention in the Fitting-ideal definition. These notions are applied to one-variable Iwasawa algebras and to commutative components only when their stated ring hypotheses hold.
