## Statement

Let $(A,\mathfrak m)$ be a complete equicharacteristic Noetherian local ring,
let $k \subseteq A$ be a coefficient field, and let $x_1,\ldots,x_e \in
\mathfrak m$ lift a $k$-basis of $\mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2$. Let
$$\phi:k\llbracket X_1,\ldots,X_e\rrbracket \to A$$
be the continuous $k$-algebra map with $\phi(X_i)=x_i$. Then for every
$n \ge 1$, the induced map
$$k\llbracket X_1,\ldots,X_e\rrbracket/(X_1,\ldots,X_e)^n \to A/\mathfrak m^n$$
is surjective.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A complete equicharacteristic Noetherian local ring $(A,\mathfrak m)$,
a coefficient field $k \subseteq A$, and lifts $x_1,\ldots,x_e$ of a basis of
$\mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2$.

[L1] The continuous substitution map $\phi$ exists
([[lem-formal-power-series-evaluation-unique-continuous-map]]).

[L2] Generators of $\mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2$ generate $\mathfrak m$
([[cor-nakayama-generators-modulo-an-ideal]]).

[L3] In a Noetherian ring, powers of a finitely generated ideal are generated by
products of generators ([[thm-noetherian-ring-ideal-characterisations]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** generate $\mathfrak m^r/\mathfrak m^{r+1}$ by degree-$r$ monomials.

1.1 By [L2], the elements $x_1,\ldots,x_e$ generate $\mathfrak m$. Therefore every product of $r$ generators is the image under $\phi$ of a degree-$r$ monomial, and by [L3] these monomials span $\mathfrak m^r/\mathfrak m^{r+1}$ over $k$ for every $r \ge 1$. [L2, L3, given, algebra]

2.1 Modulo $\mathfrak m$, the map $\phi$ is already surjective because its image contains the coefficient field $k$ and the quotient $A/\mathfrak m$ equals $k$. By step 1.1, every class in each successive quotient $\mathfrak m^r/\mathfrak m^{r+1}$ also has a polynomial preimage of total degree exactly $r$. Summing those representatives for $r=0,\ldots,n-1$ shows that every class in $A/\mathfrak m^n$ has a preimage in the source modulo $(X_1,\ldots,X_e)^n$. [L1, step 1.1, given, algebra]

3.1 Hence the Cohen map is surjective modulo every power of the maximal ideal. [step 2.1] ∎
