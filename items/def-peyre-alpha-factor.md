## Definition

Let $K$ be a number field and let $X/K$ satisfy the Peyre almost-Fano admissibility conditions. Put $V:=\operatorname{Pic}(X)\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb R$, let $\Lambda:=\operatorname{Pic}(X)$, let $C_{\mathrm{eff}}(X)\subset V$ be the closed cone generated by effective divisor classes, and let $C_{\mathrm{eff}}(X)^\vee\subset V^*$ be its dual cone under the natural pairing. Normalize translation-invariant measure $dy$ on $V^*$ so a fundamental parallelepiped of the dual lattice $\Lambda^\vee:=\operatorname{Hom}(\Lambda,\mathbb Z)$ has measure $1$. With $\rho=\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Z}\Lambda$ and $-K_X\in V$, the **Peyre alpha factor** is
$$
\alpha(X):=\frac{1}{(\rho-1)!}\int_{C_{\mathrm{eff}}(X)^\vee}e^{-\langle -K_X,y\rangle}\,dy.
$$
The integral is finite and positive by the cone hypotheses and the finiteness lemma. This definition fixes the base-field Picard lattice and measure normalization; it makes no point-counting assertion.
