## Definition

Fix a nonprincipal ultrafilter \(\mathcal U\) on \(\mathbb N\), whose existence follows from the ultrafilter extension principle applied to the Fréchet filter. For a set \(X\), define its ultrapower \({}^*X\) as \(X^{\mathbb N}/\sim_{\mathcal U}\), where \((x_m)\sim_{\mathcal U}(y_m)\) iff \(\{m:x_m=y_m\}\in\mathcal U\). Functions and algebraic operations on ultrapowers are induced coordinatewise by sequences of functions and operations. A limit map is a map on ultrapowers induced coordinatewise by a sequence of maps between the standard sets. A family indexed by \({}^*\mathbb Z^\ell\) is limit-internal if it is induced coordinatewise by a sequence of families indexed by \(\mathbb Z^\ell\). For a bounded limit function represented by \((\phi_m)\), its supremum norm is the element \([ (\sup_z\|\phi_m(z)\|_2 ) ]_{\mathcal U}\in{}^*\mathbb R\).

Let \(\mathbb N_0=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}\), and let \(s\ge2\). On \(H=\mathbb Z^2\), define the multidegree filtration by \(H_{(0,0)}=\mathbb Z^2\), \(H_{(1,0)}=\mathbb Z(1,0)\), \(H_{(0,1)}=\mathbb Z(0,1)\), and \(H_{(a,b)}=\{(0,0)\}\) for every other \((a,b)\in\mathbb N_0^2\).

A standard multidegree-filtered nilmanifold of degree at most \((1,s-1)\) is a compact nilmanifold \(G/\Gamma\) of a connected, simply connected, finite-dimensional nilpotent Lie group \(G\), equipped with a smooth Riemannian metric and closed connected subgroups \(G_{(a,b)}\), for \((a,b)\in\mathbb N_0^2\), satisfying all of the following: \(G_{(0,0)}=G\); \(G_{(a,b)}\supseteq G_{(a',b')}\) if \(a\le a'\) and \(b\le b'\); \([G_{(a,b)},G_{(a',b')} ]\subseteq G_{(a+a',b+b')}\); \(G_{(a,b)}=\{e\}\) if \(a>1\) or \(b>s-1\); and \(\Gamma\cap G_{(a,b)}\) is a lattice in \(G_{(a,b)}\) for every \((a,b)\). It is taut if \(G\) is generated by \(G_{(1,0)}\) and \(G_{(0,1)}\). The right difference of \(g:\mathbb Z^2\to G\) by \(h\in\mathbb Z^2\) is \(\partial_hg(z)=g(z+h)g(z)^{-1}\). The map \(g\) is polynomial for these filtrations if for every finite list \(h_j\in H_{(a_j,b_j)}\), the iterated right difference \(\partial_{h_m}\cdots\partial_{h_1}g(z)\) belongs to \(G_{(\sum_j a_j,\sum_j b_j)}\) for every \(z\in\mathbb Z^2\). A limit polynomial map is the coordinatewise ultrapower of a sequence of such polynomial maps into the same standard filtered nilmanifold; it maps \({}^*\mathbb Z^2\) to \({}^*G\), with the subgroups replaced by their ultrapowers.
