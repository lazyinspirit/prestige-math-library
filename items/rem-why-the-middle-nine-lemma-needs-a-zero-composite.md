## Remark

In the middle-row variant, exactness is not even a meaningful target until the
middle row is first known to be a complex. That is the role of the
zero-composite hypothesis in
[[thm-nine-lemma-variants-by-which-rows-are-assumed-exact]]: it is not a
technical afterthought, but the condition that allows the phrase "the middle
row is short exact" to make literal sense.
