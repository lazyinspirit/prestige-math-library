## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $i:X\hookrightarrow\mathbb P^N_k$ be a
smooth closed projective immersion of pure dimension $n$, put $c=N-n$,
and let $x\in X(k)$. For regular parameters $t_1,\ldots,t_n$ at $x$,
normalize the local Koszul class in
$\operatorname{Ext}^n_X(k_x,\omega_X)$ by the dual top cochain
$$e_{t_1}\wedge\cdots\wedge e_{t_n}\longmapsto(-1)^n dt_1\wedge\cdots\wedge dt_n.$$
Under normalized conormal adjunction and Yoneda
composition for $x\hookrightarrow X\hookrightarrow\mathbb P^N_k$, it
maps to the ambient point class in
$\operatorname{Ext}^N_{\mathbb P^N}(k_x,\omega_{\mathbb P^N})$.
Evaluation at $1\in H^0(k_x)$ followed by the normalized projective
Laurent trace sends that class to $1\in k$. This normalization is
independent of the parameters and ambient coordinates and commutes with
field extension.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** $i,X,x,k,n,N,c$ and regular parameters as in the statement.

[F1] The ideal of $X$ in $\mathbb P^N$ is locally generated by a regular
sequence $f_1,\ldots,f_c$, and its conormal sheaf is locally free of
rank $c$. The adjunction isomorphism is
$\omega_X\cong i^*\omega_{\mathbb P^N}\otimes\det(I/I^2)^\vee$.
([[lem-smooth-closed-immersion-regular-conormal-sequence]],
[[lem-smooth-closed-subvariety-dualizing-line-bundle-adjunction]])

[F2] Koszul resolutions compute the sheaf Ext of a regular immersion;
the dual top Koszul cochain is its generator, and concatenation of
regular sequences corresponds to tensoring their Koszul complexes.
The Koszul Hodge identification has top-degree sign
$s_d=(-1)^{d(d-1)/2}$. On a local affine coordinate ring, where the
finite-free Koszul complex is a projective resolution, its comparison
with derived Ext has degree-$d$ sign
$\sigma_d=(-1)^{d(d+1)/2}$. The normalized regular-immersion
local-to-global Ext collapse applies $\sigma_d$ to the determinant
purity identification. Generator changes induce the corresponding
determinant chain map.
([[lem-regular-immersion-koszul-ext-sheaf]],
[[lem-regular-immersion-local-to-global-ext-collapse]],
[[lem-koszul-complex-concatenation-tensor-isomorphism]],
[[lem-koszul-generator-matrix-chain-map]],
[[thm-ext-is-hom-in-the-derived-category]])

[F3] The normalized projective trace takes the unique Laurent generator
$x_0^{-1}\cdots x_N^{-1}$ of
$H^N(\mathbb P^N_k,\omega_{\mathbb P^N})$ to $1$; projective-space
coherent Serre duality pairs
$\operatorname{Ext}^N_{\mathbb P^N}(k_x,\omega)$ with
$H^0(k_x)=k$ by Yoneda evaluation and this trace.
([[lem-projective-space-top-cohomology-residue-pairing]],
[[thm-serre-duality-projective-space-coherent-sheaves]],
[[prop-yoneda-product-is-composition-in-the-derived-category]])

[F4] The Axiom of Choice is [[def-axiom-of-choice]] and implies the
Dependent Choice hypothesis of the derived-Ext comparison in [F2].
([[thm-choice-implies-dependent-implies-countable-choice]])

## Proof

1.1 A projective linear coordinate change moves $x$ to $[1:0:\cdots:0]$. On $D_+(x_0)=\mathbb A^N_k$ put $z_a=x_a/x_0$ for $1\le a\le N$. In the local ring at $x$ the ordered sequence $z=(z_1,\ldots,z_N)$ is regular, and $\Omega_z=dz_1\wedge\cdots\wedge dz_N$ is the corresponding generator of $\omega_{\mathbb P^N}$ on this chart. Give $K(z)$ the differential $$d(e_{i_1}\wedge\cdots\wedge e_{i_p}) =\sum_{j=1}^p(-1)^{j-1}z_{i_j} e_{i_1}\wedge\cdots\widehat{e_{i_j}}\cdots\wedge e_{i_p}.$$ Write $a$ for the *raw* dual top cochain $e_1\wedge\cdots\wedge e_N\mapsto\Omega_z$. We shall prove that its trace is $(-1)^N$; the normalized ambient point class is therefore represented by $(-1)^Na$. The same convention with $n$ parameters gives the intrinsic class in the statement. [F2, construct]

1.2 Work locally near $x$ and choose the regular equations $f_1,\ldots,f_c$ of [F1]. Lift the parameters $t_1,\ldots,t_n$ from $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ to the regular local ring $\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^N,x}$. Since the conormal sequence is exact and $x$ is smooth, the ordered sequence $(f_1,\ldots,f_c,t_1,\ldots,t_n)$ is a regular system of parameters of the ambient local ring. The Koszul concatenation map of [F2] identifies $K(f)\otimes K(t)$ with $K(f,t)$ and sends the ordered top tensor to the ordered top wedge. The determinant adjunction of [F1] uses this same conormal-first order: $df_1\wedge\cdots\wedge df_c\wedge dt_1\wedge\cdots\wedge dt_n$ corresponds to $dt_1\wedge\cdots\wedge dt_n$. [F1, F2]

2.1 Use the ordered affine cover $U_j=D_+(x_j)$ and the homogeneous Koszul resolution on $x_1,\ldots,x_N$; on $U_0$ it is $K(z)$ of 1.1. Put the dual Koszul degree first, so that for a cochain of Koszul degree $p$ and Čech degree $q$ the mixed total differential is $D=h+(-1)^p\delta$, where $h$ is precomposition with the Koszul differential and $\delta$ is the ordered Čech differential. Start with $a$ on $U_0$ and zero on the other $U_j$. For $J=\{i_1<\cdots<i_q\}\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}$, its correction on $U_{0,i_1,\ldots,i_q}$ is supported on the Koszul wedge complementary to $J$ and has the form $$a_q=A_q\,\frac{\iota_{i_q}\cdots\iota_{i_1}a}{z_{i_1}\cdots z_{i_q}},\qquad A_0=1,$$ where $\iota_i$ inserts $e_i$ into the argument of the alternating dual cochain. The Koszul deletion formula gives $h\iota_Ja=\sum_j(-1)^{q-j}z_{i_j}\iota_{J\setminus i_j}a$; comparing this with the face of $\delta a_{q-1}$ and the factor $(-1)^{N-q+1}$ in $D$ gives $A_q=(-1)^N A_{q-1}$. Thus $A_N=(-1)^{N^2}=(-1)^N$. The last term is $(-1)^N\Omega_z/(z_1\cdots z_N)$, corresponding under the Euler trivialization of $\omega_{\mathbb P^N}=\mathcal O(-N-1)$ to $(-1)^Nx_0^{-1}\cdots x_N^{-1}$. By [F3] the raw class has trace $(-1)^N$, and the normalized class $(-1)^Na$ has trace $1$ under Yoneda evaluation at $1\in H^0(k_x)$. For $N=0$ the empty Koszul complex gives $k\xrightarrow{\mathrm{id}}k$. [F2, F3, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 The normalized regular-immersion purity map multiplies the determinant-to-sheaf-Ext identification of [F2] by $\sigma_c$. Its inverse Hodge map contributes $s_c=(-1)^{c(c-1)/2}$, so the determinant frame corresponds to $s_c\sigma_c=(-1)^c$ times the raw top cochain for $K(f)$. This sign comparison is made on the affine local ring with its finite-free Koszul resolution, then carried to sheaf Ext by the local comparison in [F2]; it does not require global projectives among sheaves. The intrinsic point class of the statement contributes $(-1)^n$ times its raw top cochain. Ordinary Yoneda composition of raw ordered Koszul classes concatenates with coefficient $+1$: the graded tensor–Hom interchange contributes $(-1)^{cn}$, while the resolution-to-derived comparison contributes the same factor because $\sigma_{c+n}=\sigma_c\sigma_n(-1)^{cn}$. The factors cancel. Hence the composite is represented on $K(f,t)$ by $(-1)^{c+n}$ times its raw top cochain, exactly the ambient normalization of 1.1–2.1. The cases $c=0$ and $n=0$ use empty Koszul factors and satisfy the same identities. [F1, F2, F3, step 1.1, step 1.2, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 Compare $(f,t)$ to $z$. Their images in the cotangent space at $x$ are two bases, so their Jacobian matrix $J$ has determinant in $k^\times$. The Koszul generator matrix changes the ordered top Koszul basis by $\det J$ and the dual top cochain by $(\det J)^{-1}$, whereas the ambient differential form changes by $\det J$. The two factors cancel in the Ext class, and the normalization factor $(-1)^N$ is unchanged. The same calculation applies to changes of $f$, of the parameters $t$, and of projective coordinates, so the class constructed from $(f,t)$ equals the normalized class of 1.1 and has trace $1$ by 2.1. All matrix, wedge, Koszul and Laurent formulas commute with a field extension $k\to k'$, and the coefficient $1$ remains $1$. AC is inherited through the Ext and cohomology suppliers and supplies DC for the derived-Ext comparison in [F2], exactly as recorded in [F4]. [F1, F2, F3, F4, step 1.1, step 2.1, step 3.1] ∎
