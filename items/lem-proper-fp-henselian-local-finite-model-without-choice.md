## Statement

Let $A$ be a henselian local ring with residue field $k$, and let $X$ be proper and finitely presented over $A$. In ZF there are a finitely generated $\mathbb Z$-algebra $B$, a specified prime $\mathfrak p\subset B$, its ordinary local henselization $H=(B_{\mathfrak p})^h$, a local map $H\to A$, and a proper finitely presented $H$-scheme $X_H$ whose base change is $X$. Any specified finite family of finitely presented closed-fibre schemes, finite étale algebras, morphisms, group actions, and finitely many equality, inverse and finite-cover witnesses can be included in the model after enlarging $B$ by finitely many elements of $A$. The induced map $\kappa(\mathfrak p)\to k$ is the actual residue-field extension; no arbitrary family of lifts is selected.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The henselian local ring, the proper finitely presented scheme, and the specified finite family of fibre data.

[F1] Projective space is proper in ZF; properness is stable under base change and composition; every surjective finite-type morphism is universally surjective; and properness descends through a proper surjection onto a separated finite-type target. [[lem-projective-properness-and-finite-type-surjectivity-without-choice]]

[F2] A finitely presented projective immersion that becomes a closed immersion over a filtered colimit becomes a closed immersion at one finite stage, with a finite polynomial and inverse-map certificate. [[lem-projective-immersion-stabilizes-as-closed-over-filtered-rings]]

[F3] ZF proves the relativization to the constructible class $L$ of each fixed ZFC axiom. The Noetherian Chow lemma under AC has a complete existing local proof. It is applied only inside $L$ and its finite witness is then transported. [[thm-constructible-inner-model-semantic-and-formal-schema]] [[lem-chow-lemma-proper-noetherian]]

[L1] For a filtered colimit of rings, one specified finite presentation, finite chart gluing, morphism or equality descends by finitely many coefficient and equality witnesses. This is the explicit finite algebra argument in steps 1.1, 4.1 and 6.1; it does not invoke an ambient AC-qualified global limit theorem.

[F4] Étale morphisms are universally open in ZF, and admit local standard-étale charts. [[lem-etale-morphisms-are-locally-standard-and-universally-open-in-zf]]

## Proof

**Proof technique:** use an internal Noetherian projective witness and descend the actual finite closed-immersion certificate.

1.1 Choose one finite affine presentation of $X/A$, including finitely many affine overlap covers, equations, inverses and cocycles. Its finite coefficient list lies in the image of a polynomial ring over $\mathbb Z$. The kernel of that polynomial map is finitely generated by the finite Hilbert-basis argument; adjoining the finitely many equality witnesses gives a finitely generated subring $B_0\subset A$ and a finitely presented $X_0/B_0$ with $X_0\times_{B_0}A\simeq X$. Finite surjectivity witnesses for the separated diagonal may be included at the same stage. These are finite choices for the one given object. [L1, given, construct]

2.1 The finitely generated integer ring $B_0$, its Noetherian affine chart rings and the data in step 1.1 admit finite natural-number codes. Every ideal of such a chart ring has a finite generating list, so its ideal and prime-point structure is identical internally and externally to $L$: generators and polynomial membership witnesses have common natural-number codes. The same applies to finitely many charts and their maps. [F3, step 1.1, construct, algebra]

3.1 Inside $L$, apply [F3] to the separated finite-type $X_0/B_0$. Obtain a proper surjection $\pi_0:Y_0\to X_0$ and a $B_0$-immersion $j_0:Y_0\to\mathbb P^n_{B_0}$. The graph of $j_0$ over $X_0$ is a closed immersion $Y_0\hookrightarrow\mathbb P^n_{X_0}$: it is both proper over the separated target and an immersion. Extract its finitely many chart equations in $L$. They remain a closed immersion externally, so [F1] makes $\pi_0$ externally proper. Its surjectivity is also external, because step 2.1 identifies the relevant prime-point sets and maps. No arbitrary nonconstructible prime set is transported. [F1, F3, step 2.1, construct]

4.1 Base change to $A$. By [F1], $\pi_A$ remains a proper surjection and $Y_A\to A$ is proper. Its immersion into separated projective space is proper, hence is a closed immersion. Apply [F2] to the directed system of all finite-type integer subrings of $A$ containing $B_0$. At one later finite-type subring $B_1$, the immersion $Y_{B_1}\to\mathbb P^n_{B_1}$ is already closed. Thus $Y_{B_1}$ is proper over $B_1$. The base-changed $\pi_{B_1}$ is a universally surjective proper map, so [F1] makes $X_{B_1}$ proper. The actual base change remains the given $X$. [F1, F2, step 3.1, construct]

5.1 Let $\mathfrak p$ be the inverse image of the maximal ideal of $A$ in $B_1$, and set $R=(B_1)_{\mathfrak p}$. Construct its ordinary henselization as the colimit of all finite towers of monic simple-root extensions with specified residue roots. Finite towers have common successors; uniqueness of each lifted simple root makes the maps compatible. The simple-root henselian criterion gives the universal property, hence the actual local map $R\to A$ extends uniquely to $H=R^h\to A$. Base change $X_{B_1}$ to $H$; properness and finite presentation persist. [F1, step 4.1, given, construct]

6.1 For the specified finite fibre data, choose finitely many representatives in $A$ of their finitely many residue coefficients, adjoin them to $B_1$, and repeat the preceding construction by base change. A finite étale algebra has finite idempotent, multiplication, unit and perfect-trace-inverse matrices on a finite affine cover. Actions, isomorphisms and their identities are finitely many additional matrices and equations. For a finite-presentation scheme or map use its finite chart equations. Every equation and finite cover identity holding after base change is made exact at a common finite stage by [L1]. Thus these data are represented over the residue field $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$. [L1, step 5.1, given, construct]

7.1 If a finite family of finitely presented étale maps covers after extension $\kappa(\mathfrak p)\to k$, its omitted locus is closed in a finite-type scheme over $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$ by [F4]. If it is nonempty, a nonzero affine chart admits finite Noether normalization by a finite triangular substitution; its fibre over the origin of the resulting polynomial algebra is nonzero by the determinant trick, and an ideal of maximal dimension in that finite-dimensional fibre is maximal. This supplies a closed point with finite residue-field extension. Its nonzero finite residue algebra remains nonzero over $k$, contradicting the assumed cover. Hence the descended family was already a cover. The same reasoning applies to finitely many specified overlap and equality covers. This gives exactly the stated finite-data model without transporting the arbitrary external ring $A$ into $L$. [F4, step 6.1, given, construct] ∎
