## Statement

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and let $M$ be an $R$-module. The following are equivalent:

1. $M$ is flat.
2. For every injection $K\hookrightarrow N$, the induced map $K\otimes_RM\to N\otimes_RM$ is injective.
3. For every ideal $I\subseteq R$, the multiplication map $I\otimes_RM\to M$, $a\otimes m\mapsto am$, is injective.
4. The map in claim 3 is injective for every finitely generated ideal $I$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A commutative ring $R$ and an $R$-module $M$.

[L1] Flatness means that $-\otimes_RM$ preserves exact sequences ([[def-flat-and-faithfully-flat-modules-and-ring-maps]]).

[L2] Tensoring is right exact ([[thm-right-exactness-of-tensor-products]]).

[L3] Tensor products commute with direct sums and $R\otimes_RM\cong M$; consequently $R^n\otimes_RM\cong M^n$ ([[thm-tensor-products-commute-with-arbitrary-direct-sums]], [[thm-unit-isomorphisms-for-module-tensor-products]]).

[L4] A finite list $x_1,\ldots,x_n$ in a module determines a homomorphism $R^n\to N$ taking $e_j$ to $x_j$; if the list generates $N$, this map is surjective. Also $R^0=0$ ([[def-free-module-on-a-set-and-standard-basis]], [[thm-universal-property-of-free-modules]]).

[L5] In a commutative ring, every submodule of the regular module $R$ is an ideal ([[def-left-right-and-two-sided-ideal]]).

[L6] A tensor product is the quotient of the free $\mathbb Z$-module on pairs by the subgroup generated by the additive and balance relations ([[def-tensor-product-of-modules-by-generators-and-relations]]). Consequently, a tensor expression and a derivation that it is zero involve only finitely many generators and defining relations.

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Claim 1 implies claim 2 by applying [L1] to $0\to K\to N$; claim 2 implies claim 3 by taking the inclusion $I\hookrightarrow R$ and using $R\otimes_RM\cong M$; claim 3 implies claim 4 by restriction to finitely generated ideals. [L1, L2, L3, L5]

1.2 Assume claim 4. Claim 3 follows: an element $x\in I\otimes_RM$ is represented by finitely many coefficients from $I$, hence comes from $I_0\otimes_RM$ for the finitely generated ideal $I_0$ they generate; if its image in $M$ is zero, injectivity for $I_0$ makes its representative zero, so $x=0$. [given, L5]

1.3 Let $K\hookrightarrow N$ be injective and let $x\in K\otimes_RM$ map to zero. By [L6], the tensor $x$ uses finitely many elements of $K$, and a derivation of its zero image uses only finitely many generators and defining relations in $N\otimes_RM$. Hence there are finitely generated submodules $K_0\subseteq K$ and $N_0\subseteq N$ with $K_0\subseteq N_0$ for which $x$ comes from an element killed in $N_0\otimes_RM$. [given, L6]

2.1 Under claim 3, for every submodule $L\subseteq R^n$, the map $L\otimes_RM\to M^n$ is injective, by induction on $n$. For $n=0$ it is the unique map $0\to0$; for $n=1$ it is claim 3 by [L5]. [step 1.2, L3, L4]

2.2 Present $N_0=R^n/L$ using [L4], and let $L'\subseteq R^n$ be the inverse image of $K_0$. Right exactness identifies $N_0\otimes_RM$ with $M^n/\operatorname{im}(L\otimes_RM)$ and $K_0\otimes_RM$ with the quotient of $L'\otimes_RM$ by $\operatorname{im}(L\otimes_RM)$. [step 1.3, L2, L3, L4]

3.1 For the induction step, let $Q\subseteq R^n$, put $Q':=Q\cap(R\oplus0^{n-1})$, and let $Q''$ be the image of $Q$ in $R^{n-1}$. Tensoring the exact rows $0\to Q'\to Q\to Q''\to0$ and $0\to R\to R^n\to R^{n-1}\to0$ gives right-exact rows by [L2]; the left and right vertical maps are injective by the $n=1$ case and the induction hypothesis from step 2.1. [step 2.1, L2, L3]

4.1 If $z\in Q\otimes_RM$ maps to zero in $M^n$, its image in $Q''\otimes_RM$ maps to zero in $M^{n-1}$ and hence is zero by the right vertical injection in step 3.1. Right exactness of the top row lifts $z$ from some $y\in Q'\otimes_RM$. The image of $y$ in $R\otimes_RM$ maps to the zero image of $z$ in $M^n$; the left vertical injection in step 3.1 makes $y=0$, and therefore $z=0$. This completes the induction of step 2.1. [step 3.1, L2, L3]

5.1 By step 4.1, both $L\otimes_RM$ and $L'\otimes_RM$ inject into $M^n$. Therefore the induced map of the quotients in step 2.2 is injective, so $x=0$. This proves claim 2 from claim 4. [step 4.1, step 2.2]

6.1 Finally claim 2 and right exactness [L2] imply claim 1: for an exact sequence, replace the left map by the injection of its image into the middle term; tensoring preserves that injection by claim 2 and preserves the remaining image and cokernel statements by right exactness. [step 5.1, L1, L2]

7.1 Steps 1.1 through 6.1 prove the cycle of equivalences. The zero ideal, $n=0$, and zero module cases occur explicitly in steps 1.2 and 2.1; no choice is made, and both directions of every equivalence have been supplied. [step 1.1, step 1.2, step 2.1, step 3.1, step 4.1, step 1.3, step 2.2, step 5.1, step 6.1] ∎
