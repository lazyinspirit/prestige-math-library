## Open problem

Determine whether
$$\mathcal L(\alpha,\beta)=0\qquad\text{for every }(\alpha,\beta)\in\mathbb R^2.$$
This is the classical Littlewood conjecture for simultaneous multiplicative approximation.

Here $\mathcal L(\alpha,\beta)=\liminf_{n\to\infty}n\|n\alpha\|_{\mathbb R/\mathbb Z}\|n\beta\|_{\mathbb R/\mathbb Z}$.

## Status

As reviewed on 2026-10-25 UTC, the classical real-variable conjecture remains open. The accessible MathWorld article explicitly says that its proof “remains an open problem” and reports the zero-Hausdorff-dimension exceptional-set result as a partial result, not a solution for every pair. The Chow–Technau theorem is another partial quantitative result.

The Shkredov reference is retained as a bibliographic pointer, but its full text was not retrieved and no reading of it is claimed here. The current status assessment uses the independently accessible MathWorld article.
