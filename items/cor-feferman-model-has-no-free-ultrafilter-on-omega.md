## Statement

Every ultrafilter on $\omega$ in $\mathsf F_{\mathrm{tf}}$ is principal. In
particular, the tail-flip symmetric model has no free ultrafilter on $\omega$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** An ultrafilter $U$ on $\omega$ in $\mathsf F_{\mathrm{tf}}$.

[F1] [[thm-feferman-model-prime-ideals-on-p-omega-are-principal]] proves that every prime ideal of $\mathcal P(\omega)$ in the model is principal.

[F2] [[def-ultrafilter]] defines $U$ as a maximal proper filter and defines principal and free ultrafilters.

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct Boolean complementation duality.

1.1 First, maximality makes $U$ decide every $A\subseteq\omega$: if neither $A$ nor $\omega\setminus A$ belonged to $U$, then every $B\in U$ would meet $A$ (otherwise upward closure would put $\omega\setminus A$ in $U$), so the filter generated by $U\cup\{A\}$ would be a proper strict extension, contradicting F2. Thus exactly one of $A$ and its complement lies in $U$, since a proper filter cannot contain both. [F2]

2.1 Define $I=\{A\subseteq\omega:\omega\setminus A\in U\}$. Complementation converts upward closure to downward closure and intersections to unions, so $I$ is a proper ideal. If $A\cap B\in I$, then $(\omega\setminus A)\cup(\omega\setminus B)\in U$. Were neither complement in $U$, step 1.1 would put both $A$ and $B$ in $U$, and then $A\cap B\in U$, a contradiction. Hence $A\in I$ or $B\in I$, so $I$ is prime. [F2, step 1.1]

3.1 By F1 the ideal $I$ is generated by some $C\subseteq\omega$, so $I=\{A\subseteq\omega:A\subseteq C\}$. Propriety gives $C\ne\omega$. Choose $k\in\omega\setminus C$. Since $I$ is prime and $\{k\}\cap(\omega\setminus\{k\})=\varnothing\in I$, while $\{k\}\nsubseteq C$, we have $\omega\setminus\{k\}\in I$ and hence $\omega\setminus\{k\}\subseteq C$. Together with $k\notin C$, this gives $C=\omega\setminus\{k\}$ and therefore $I=\{A\subseteq\omega:k\notin A\}$. For every $B\subseteq\omega$, it follows that $B\in U$ iff $\omega\setminus B\in I$ iff $k\in B$. Thus $U$ is the principal ultrafilter at $k$ in the sense of F2. Since $U$ was arbitrary, no free ultrafilter on $\omega$ exists in the model. [F1, F2, step 2.1] ∎
