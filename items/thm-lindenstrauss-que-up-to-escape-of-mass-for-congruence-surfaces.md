## Statement

Let M=Γ₀(N)\ℍ be a finite-area noncompact congruence surface and μ an arithmetic quantum limit from joint Laplace and Hecke eigenfunctions. Then μ=c m_Liouville for some 0≤c≤1, where m_Liouville is normalized Liouville measure; the remaining mass 1−c may escape into the cusps.

[Elon Lindenstrauss, Invariant Measures and Arithmetic Quantum Unique Ergodicity, Theorem 1.1, Theorem 1.4, §§3, 7–8, and Appendix A.](https://math.huji.ac.il/~elon/Publications/quantum.pdf)

**Given:** The complete objects, parameters, conventions and hypotheses in the Statement.
