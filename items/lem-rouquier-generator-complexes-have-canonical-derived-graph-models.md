## Statement

For every $1\le i\le n-1$ the generator complexes of
[[def-positive-and-negative-rouquier-generator-complexes]] are canonically
isomorphic in the bounded derived category to shifts of standard graph
bimodules:
$$F_i\cong R_{s_i}(-1),\qquad F_i^{-1}\cong R_{s_i}(1)\qquad\text{in }D^b(R^e\text{-grmod}),$$
the isomorphisms being induced by the quasi-isomorphisms $f_i$ below. More
generally, for a signed word $\sigma=\sigma_{i_1}^{\epsilon_1}\cdots\sigma_{i_r}^{\epsilon_r}$
with product $w=s_{i_1}^{\epsilon_1}\cdots s_{i_r}^{\epsilon_r}$ and exponent sum
$e(\sigma)=\sum_k\epsilon_k$,
$$F(\sigma)\cong R_w(-e(\sigma))\qquad\text{in }D^b(R^e\text{-grmod}),$$
where $F(\sigma)$ is the iterated signed tensor totalization
$F_{i_1}^{\epsilon_1}\otimes_R\cdots\otimes_RF_{i_r}^{\epsilon_r}$ and
$R_w$ is the standard graph bimodule. All isomorphisms are obtained from the
comparison system $c_{t,u}$ of
[[def-rouquier-canonical-comparisons-between-standard-graph-tensors]] and do not
depend on the chosen words beyond their permutation product and exponent sum,
with the displayed graph models and fixed generator maps understood.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A simple reflection $s_i$, the bimodule $B_i$ with generators $u=1\otimes1$ (degree $-1$) and $w_0=1\otimes\delta_i$, $\delta_i=\alpha_i/2$, the element $\rho_-=\delta_iu-w_0$, and the complexes $F_i,F_i^{-1}$ of [[def-positive-and-negative-rouquier-generator-complexes]].

[F1] *The two exact sequences.* The multiplication $\varepsilon_i:B_i\to R(1)$ is a surjective degree-zero bimodule map with $\varepsilon_i(u)=1$ and $\varepsilon_i(w_0)=\delta_i$, and $\ker\varepsilon_i=R\rho_-$ with $R\rho_-\cong R_{s_i}(-1)$ a graded sub-bimodule generated in degree $1$; the map $\eta_i:R(-1)\to B_i$ is injective and the quotient $B_i/\eta_i(R(-1))=B_i/R\rho_+$ is generated by the image of $u$ with right action $u\cdot g\equiv s_i(g)u$, so that it is isomorphic to $R_{s_i}(1)$; here $\rho_+=\delta_iu+w_0$ satisfies $\rho_+\cdot g=g\rho_+$ and $\rho_-\cdot g=s_i(g)\rho_-$ ([[def-type-a-standard-graph-bimodules-support-filtrations-and-character]], [[def-positive-and-negative-rouquier-generator-complexes]]).

[F2] *Two canonical chain maps.* The assignment $1\mapsto1\otimes\alpha_i-\alpha_i\otimes1=-2\rho_-$ defines a degree-zero bimodule map $f_i:R_{s_i}(-1)\to F_i$, concentrated in cohomological degree $0$; it is a chain map because $\varepsilon_i(\rho_-)=0$. The assignment $1\otimes1\mapsto1$ defines the twisted multiplication  $\psi:B_i\to R_{s_i}(1)$, $\psi(r\otimes r')=r\,s_i(r')$, a degree-zero bimodule map with $\psi(\eta_i(1))=0$; on the quotient $B_i/\eta_i(R(-1))$ it induces the identification with $R_{s_i}(1)$ and defines a chain map $g_i:F_i^{-1}\to R_{s_i}(1)$, concentrated in cohomological degree $0$. ([[def-type-a-standard-graph-bimodules-support-filtrations-and-character]], [[def-rouquier-canonical-comparisons-between-standard-graph-tensors]])

[F3] *Cohomology of the generator complexes.* $H^0(F_i)=\ker\varepsilon_i=R\rho_-\cong R_{s_i}(-1)$, $H^1(F_i)=\operatorname{coker}\varepsilon_i=0$, and $H^{-1}(F_i^{-1})=\ker\eta_i=0$, $H^0(F_i^{-1})=B_i/\eta_i(R(-1))\cong R_{s_i}(1)$; both complexes are otherwise concentrated in the displayed degrees. [F1]

[F4] *Localization.* The localization functor $K^b(R^e\text{-grmod})\to D^b(R^e\text{-grmod})$ sends quasi-isomorphisms to isomorphisms, and tensor totalization with a bounded complex of bimodules flat on the tensoring side preserves quasi-isomorphisms. Here all generator complexes and graph models are flat on both sides: the former have finite-free terms and the latter are twisted rank-one regular modules. Consequently the tensor comparisons used below are compatible with localization ([[prop-the-localization-functor-sends-quasi-isomorphisms-to-isomorphisms]], [[def-derived-category-of-an-abelian-category]], [[lem-bounded-above-flat-tensor-complexes-preserve-quasi-isomorphisms]]).

[F5] *Multiplication of graph bimodules.* The balanced assignment $R_x\otimes_RR_y\to R_{xy}$, $a\otimes b\mapsto a\,x(b)$, is a degree-zero isomorphism of graded bimodules with inverse $1\mapsto1\otimes1$; shifts satisfy $M(a)\otimes_RN(b)\cong(M\otimes_RN)(a+b)$ ([[def-rouquier-canonical-comparisons-between-standard-graph-tensors]], [[def-type-a-standard-graph-bimodules-support-filtrations-and-character]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 The map $f_i$ of [F2] is a chain map between the complexes $R_{s_i}(-1)$ concentrated in degree $0$ and $F_i$ in degrees $0,1$: the only condition is that the composite of $f_i$ with the differential $\varepsilon_i$ vanishes, which holds since $\varepsilon_i(\rho_-)=0$ by [F1]. It is a bimodule map: for $g\in R$ one has $f_i(1\cdot g)=f_i(s_i(g))=-2s_i(g)\rho_-$ and $f_i(1)\cdot g=-2\rho_-\cdot g=-2s_i(g)\rho_-$ by [F1], and $R$-linearity on the left is clear. [F1, F2]

2.1 Comparing with [F3], $f_i$ induces the identity identification $H^0(R_{s_i}(-1))=R_{s_i}(-1)\to R\rho_-$ (up to the unit $-2$) and there are no other cohomology groups on either side; hence $f_i$ is a quasi-isomorphism, and so is $g_i$ between $F_i^{-1}$ and $R_{s_i}(1)$. [F1, F2, F3, step 1.1]

3.1 By [F4] the quasi-isomorphisms $f_i,g_i$ become isomorphisms in $D^b$, giving $F_i\cong R_{s_i}(-1)$ and $F_i^{-1}\cong R_{s_i}(1)$. [F4, step 2.1]

4.1 For a signed word, tensoring the isomorphisms of step 3.1 over $R$ and using that the totalization of bimodule complexes is compatible with localization in each variable [F4], together with the shift computation $M(a)\otimes_RN(b)\cong(M\otimes_RN)(a+b)$ and the multiplication isomorphism of [F5] iterated over the word, gives $F(\sigma)\cong R_{s_{i_1}^{\epsilon_1}}\otimes_R\cdots\otimes_RR_{s_{i_r}^{\epsilon_r}}(-\sum_k\epsilon_k)\cong R_w(-e(\sigma))$ in $D^b$. [F4, F5, step 3.1]

5.1 With the generator maps $f_i,g_i$ fixed, tensor their derived isomorphisms (using $f_i^{-1}$ for a positive letter and $g_i$ for a negative letter), then compose with the graph multiplication $\mu_t$ of [F5]. This specifies the word map to $R_w(-e(\sigma))$ with its full shift retained; no replacement of $e(\sigma)$ by the length of a reduced permutation word is made. Associativity of graph multiplication makes this construction compatible with the canonical rebracketings. Comparisons between different word models are obtained by composing their specified isomorphisms through this common target when their permutation and exponent agree. [F2, F4, F5, step 4.1] ∎

## Remarks

This is Rouquier's §3.2.1 and §3.2.4 identification of the generators with the standard graph bimodules, in the library normalization: the unit scalar $-2$ and the shifts $(\mp1)$ are the exact dictionary entries, so no unit factors are dropped. The word statement is proved here because the later uniqueness and decategorification arguments use the identification $F(\sigma)\cong R_w(-e(\sigma))$ in $D^b$ as a consequence of the comparison system. The statement is a derived-category statement; it does not assert that $f_i$ is a homotopy equivalence, and no homotopy-category identification is made here.
