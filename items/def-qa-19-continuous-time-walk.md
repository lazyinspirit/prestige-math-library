## Definition

Let $H$ be a self-adjoint endomorphism of a nonzero finite-dimensional
complex-inner-product space $V$, let $t\in\mathbb R$, and let $[\psi]$ be a
pure state with a specified unit representative $\psi\in V$. The
**continuous-time quantum walk** generated by $H$ at time $t$ has state vector

$$\phi(t):=U_H(t)\psi,$$

where $U_H(t)$ is the finite matrix evolution in
[[def-qa-19-finite-matrix-dynamics]]. By
[[thm-qa-19-matrix-exponential-unitary-properties]], $U_H(t)$ is unitary, so
$\phi(t)$ is still a unit vector and its output pure-state ray is
$[U_H(t)\psi]$. This ray does not depend on the chosen unit representative:
if $\psi'=\alpha\psi$ with $|\alpha|=1$, linearity gives
$U_H(t)\psi'=\alpha U_H(t)\psi$, the same ray. For a graph walk, $V$ is the
ordered vertex register and $H$ is a separately specified Hermitian generator
such as one of the matrices in
[[def-qa-19-graph-generator-conventions]]. The definition alone supplies no
circuit, Hamiltonian oracle, or efficiency claim.
