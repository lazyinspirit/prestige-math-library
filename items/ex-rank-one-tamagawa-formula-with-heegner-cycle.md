## Example

Assume the hypotheses of the rank-one Longo–Vigni p-TNC theorem for a modular motive $M=M(f)(k/2)$ and suppose a higher-weight Heegner cycle $y_K$ has nonzero image under the p-adic Abel–Jacobi map. Then the algebraic rank is one, the Heegner class spans the one-dimensional finite Selmer direction, and the p-TNC identifies the lattice generated by the p-adic zeta element with the determinant lattice of the compactly supported Galois complex.

## Escalation

The statement claims that an arbitrary nonzero class $y_K$ spans the one-dimensional finite Selmer direction. Longo–Vigni Theorem 5.42 constructs a trace class $y_{\mathbb Q,p}$ and relates it to a motivic generator in equations (5.53)–(5.56), but this item does not identify $y_K$ with that trace class or prove that its Abel–Jacobi image spans the finite Selmer line. The rank-one theorem and its suppliers are also escalated. No verification is supplied; the owner must resolve this example before it can be marked ready.
