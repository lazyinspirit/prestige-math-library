## Statement

Let $T$ and $I_{\mathrm{DJ}}$ be the free algebra and defining ideal of
[[def-drinfeld-jimbo-quantized-enveloping-algebra]], and put
$m_{ij}=1-a_{ij}$ and $\mathrm{Serre}^{\pm}_{ij}$ for its displayed Serre
sums. Define $\omega,\psi,\tau:T\to T$ on generators by

$$\omega(E_i)=-F_i,\quad\omega(F_i)=-E_i,\quad\omega(K_h)=K_{-h},$$

$$\psi(E_i)=E_i,\quad\psi(F_i)=F_i,\quad\psi(K_h)=K_{-h},$$

$$\tau(E_i)=F_i,\quad\tau(F_i)=E_i,\quad\tau(K_h)=K_{-h}.$$

Here $\omega$ fixes $\mathbb Q(q)$, while $\psi$ and $\tau$ apply the field
involution $q\mapsto q^{-1}$ to coefficients; $\omega$ and $\psi$ extend as
algebra maps, and $\tau$ extends as an anti-algebra map. Each map preserves
$I_{\mathrm{DJ}}$ and descends to $U_q(\mathfrak g)$. All three are
involutions; $\omega$ is a $\mathbb Q(q)$-algebra automorphism, $\psi$ a
semilinear algebra automorphism, and $\tau$ a semilinear algebra
anti-involution. On the Serre sums,

$$\omega(\mathrm{Serre}^{\pm}_{ij})=(-1)^{a_{ij}}\mathrm{Serre}^{\mp}_{ij},\qquad\psi(\mathrm{Serre}^{\pm}_{ij})=\mathrm{Serre}^{\pm}_{ij},\qquad\tau(\mathrm{Serre}^{\pm}_{ij})=(-1)^{1-a_{ij}}\mathrm{Serre}^{\mp}_{ij}.$$

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The free algebra $T$ and the four families of generators of
$I_{\mathrm{DJ}}$ in the Drinfeld–Jimbo presentation.

[F1] The ideal is generated by the toral, toral-action, mixed $E_iF_j$, and
positive and negative Serre relations displayed in
[[def-drinfeld-jimbo-quantized-enveloping-algebra]].

[F2] The symmetric Gaussian coefficients satisfy
$\binom{m}{r}_i=\binom{m}{m-r}_i$ and are invariant under
$q_i\mapsto q_i^{-1}$; $[m]_i$ has the same inversion symmetry
([[def-quantum-integers-factorials-and-divided-powers-at-q-i]]).

[F3] Assignments of the free generators extend uniquely to algebra maps on
$T$ ([[thm-universal-property-of-the-tensor-algebra]]); reversing the order
of each word gives the corresponding anti-algebra extension.

## Proof

**Proof technique:** Check the four relation families and then pass to the quotient.

1.1 The substitution $\sigma(q)=q^{-1}$ is an involutive field automorphism of $\mathbb Q(q)$. By [F3], the displayed assignments extend to a $\mathbb Q(q)$-algebra endomorphism $\omega$, a $\sigma$-semilinear algebra endomorphism $\psi$, and a $\sigma$-semilinear anti-algebra endomorphism $\tau$ of $T$. Each square fixes every generator and coefficient, so all three squares are the identity on $T$. [F3, construct]

1.2 The maps send $K_0-1$ to itself. They send $K_hK_{h'}-K_{h+h'}$ respectively to $K_{-h}K_{-h'}-K_{-(h+h')}$, the same relation, and $K_{-h'}K_{-h}-K_{-(h+h')}$, the same family with its two indices reversed. Thus each image lies in $I_{\mathrm{DJ}}$. [F1, algebra]

1.3 Write $a=\langle\alpha_i,h\rangle$, $R^E_{i,h}=K_hE_i-q^aE_iK_h$, and $R^F_{i,h}=K_hF_i-q^{-a}F_iK_h$. Then $\omega(R^E_{i,h})=-R^F_{i,-h}$ and $\omega(R^F_{i,h})=-R^E_{i,-h}$; $\psi(R^E_{i,h})=R^E_{i,-h}$ and $\psi(R^F_{i,h})=R^F_{i,-h}$; and $\tau(R^E_{i,h})=-q^{-a}R^F_{i,-h}$ and $\tau(R^F_{i,h})=-q^{a}R^E_{i,-h}$. Each is a scalar multiple of a defining toral-action relation. [F1, algebra]

1.4 Let $R_{ij}=E_iF_j-F_jE_i-\delta_{ij}(K_i-K_i^{-1})/(q_i-q_i^{-1})$. Direct substitution, including the semilinear inversion of the coefficient in $\psi$ and $\tau$, gives $\omega(R_{ij})=-R_{ji}$, $\psi(R_{ij})=R_{ij}$, and $\tau(R_{ij})=R_{ji}$. The ratio $(K_i-K_i^{-1})/(q_i-q_i^{-1})$ is unchanged when both numerator and denominator are inverted. Hence these images lie in $I_{\mathrm{DJ}}$. [F1, F2, algebra]

1.5 Put $m=1-a_{ij}$. Applying $\omega$ to a positive Serre sum contributes $m+1$ minus signs and preserves the order of factors, so $\omega(\mathrm{Serre}^+_{ij})=(-1)^{m+1}\mathrm{Serre}^-_{ij}=(-1)^{a_{ij}}\mathrm{Serre}^-_{ij}$; the negative case is symmetric. By [F2], $\psi$ fixes the coefficients and each $E_i,F_i$, so it fixes both Serre sums. The anti-map $\tau$ reverses each monomial; reindexing $r\mapsto m-r$ and using [F2] gives $\tau(\mathrm{Serre}^{\pm}_{ij})=(-1)^m\mathrm{Serre}^{\mp}_{ij}$. Thus all Serre images belong to $I_{\mathrm{DJ}}$. [F1, F2, algebra]

2.1 The preceding steps show that each map sends every generator of $I_{\mathrm{DJ}}$ into that ideal. Since $I_{\mathrm{DJ}}$ is two-sided, the algebra maps and the anti-algebra map send the whole ideal into itself. They therefore descend to the quotient; their squares remain the identity there, which makes the descended maps automorphisms or an anti-automorphism of the stated types. This proves the assertion. [F1, step 1.1, step 1.2, step 1.3, step 1.4, step 1.5, algebra] ∎
