## Facts & Assumptions

[F1] [[def-full-level-gl3-automorphic-distribution]]: the realization datum supplies $G=\mathrm{GL}_3(\mathbb R)$, $\Gamma=\mathrm{GL}_3(\mathbb Z)$, an irreducible unitary real component $W$, its explicit central normalization $W_0$, the contragredient $V_0=W_0'$, and evaluation in $V_0^{-\infty}$ from the normalized realization.

[F2] [[def-unitary-cuspidal-generic-gln-representation]]: the ambient adelic representation is unitary, cuspidal, and globally generic.

## Statement

Let $W$ be the irreducible unitary real component in a full-level GL3 cuspidal realization datum. Write its central character as $\omega(rI)=r^{it}$ for $r>0$, put $\kappa(g)=|\det g|^{-it/3}$, and let $V_0=(W\otimes\kappa)'$ be the central-normalized contragredient. Let $(V_0)_K$ be the smooth vectors whose $O(3)$-orbits span finite-dimensional spaces. Assume $(V_0)_K$ is irreducible under $O(3)$ and $\mathfrak{gl}_3(\mathbb C)$, is generated by finitely many vectors under differentiated Lie algebra operators, and contains each irreducible finite-dimensional $O(3)$-representation with finite multiplicity. Then there exist $\lambda\in\mathbb C^3$ and $\delta\in(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^3$ with $\sum_j\lambda_j=0$ and $\sum_j\delta_j=0$, and continuous $G$-equivariant injections $V_0^\infty\hookrightarrow V_{\lambda,\delta}^\infty$ and $V_0^{-\infty}\hookrightarrow V_{\lambda,\delta}^{-\infty}$. The latter sends evaluation on the normalized realization to a $\Gamma$-invariant distribution whose restriction to $N_{\mathbb Z}\backslash N$ satisfies the original cuspidal constant-term conditions. Untwisting gives corresponding smooth and distribution injections for the original contragredient $W'$ into the principal-series model with parameters $\lambda-(it/3)(1,1,1)$ and $\delta$; no spherical or tempered restriction is imposed.
