## Definition

Fix the finite Weyl root-system data of
[[def-finite-weyl-root-system-lattice-and-chamber-conventions]]: the real span
$E$ of the roots with its positive definite form, a positive system $\Phi^+$
with base $\alpha_1,\dots,\alpha_r$, the root group
$Q=\sum_{\alpha\in\Phi}\mathbb Z\alpha\subseteq P$, the weight lattice
$P\subseteq E$, and the positive cone
$$Q_+=\sum_{i=1}^r\mathbb Z_{\ge0}\alpha_i$$
(the set written $Q^+$ in
[[def-grothendieck-group-and-character-of-category-o]]). A **downward cone**
is a set $\lambda-Q_+=\{\lambda-\beta:\beta\in Q_+\}$ with
$\lambda\in\mathfrak h^*$.

The **completed formal character ring** $\mathcal R$ is the set of formal sums
$$f=\sum_{\mu\in\mathfrak h^*}c_\mu e^\mu,\qquad c_\mu\in\mathbb Z,$$
whose support $\operatorname{supp}f=\{\mu:c_\mu\ne0\}$ is contained in a
finite union of downward cones. Addition is coefficientwise, the product is
the convolution
$$(fg)_\eta=\sum_{\mu+\nu=\eta}c_\mu d_\nu,$$
and the unit is $e^0$, so that $e^\mu e^\nu=e^{\mu+\nu}$. The product is
well defined: if $\operatorname{supp}f$ lies in
$\bigcup_{i=1}^m(\lambda_i-Q_+)$ and $\operatorname{supp}g$ in
$\bigcup_{j=1}^n(\mu_j-Q_+)$, then a pair of exponents contributing to $\eta$
lies in some $\lambda_i-Q_+$ and some $\mu_j-Q_+$, and the solutions
$\beta,\gamma\in Q_+$ of $\beta+\gamma=\lambda_i+\mu_j-\eta$ are the elements
of the box $0\le\beta_k\le(\lambda_i+\mu_j-\eta)_k$ in the simple-root
coordinates, a finite set (empty unless $\lambda_i+\mu_j-\eta\in Q_+$). The
coefficients are integers, and a finite union of downward cones is again such
a union, so addition and multiplication make $\mathcal R$ a commutative
$\mathbb Z$-algebra. In the notation of
[[def-grothendieck-group-and-character-of-category-o]] this is the ring
denoted $\mathscr R$ there, where the character homomorphism of category
$\mathcal O$ takes its values; the elements of finite support form the group
ring $\mathbb Z[\mathfrak h^*]$ of the additive group $\mathfrak h^*$ and
contain the subring $\mathbb Z[P]$ generated by the $e^\mu$ with
$\mu\in P$.

By [[prop-formal-character-of-a-verma-module]],
$$\operatorname{ch}M(\lambda)=e^\lambda\prod_{\alpha\in\Phi^+}(1-e^{-\alpha})^{-1}$$
is an element of $\mathcal R$: the geometric series
$\sum_{k\ge0}e^{-k\alpha}$ is supported in the downward cone $-Q_+$, and a
finite product of elements of $\mathcal R$ lies in $\mathcal R$ by the
convolution formula, while $e^\lambda$ is a single monomial. **No convergence
of any formal sum is asserted**: all sums are formal, coefficients are
compared coefficientwise, and every finite sum, product or finite product of
geometric series below is interpreted in $\mathcal R$ by the rules just
recorded.
