## Definition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring.

The **prime spectrum** of $R$ is the set
$$ \operatorname{Spec}(R)=\{\mathfrak p\trianglelefteq R:\mathfrak p\text{ is prime}\}. $$

If $T\subseteq R$, define its **vanishing set** by
$$ V(T)=\{\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Spec}(R):T\subseteq\mathfrak p\}. $$
For an ideal $I\trianglelefteq R$ this is simply
$$ V(I)=\{\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Spec}(R):I\subseteq\mathfrak p\}. $$
Since a prime ideal contains $T$ exactly when it contains the ideal $(T)$ generated by $T$, one has $V(T)=V((T))$.
