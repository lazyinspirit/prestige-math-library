## Definition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring and let $M$ be a finitely presented $R$-module. For $k\ge0$, the $k$th Fitting ideal $\operatorname{Fit}_k(M)$ is the ideal generated by the $(n-k)\times(n-k)$ minors of a presentation matrix for any finite presentation $R^m\to R^n\to M\to0$. The conventions are $\operatorname{Fit}_k(M)=R$ for $k\ge n$ and $\operatorname{Fit}_k(M)=0$ when there are no minors of the required size. This definition is independent of the chosen presentation.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A commutative ring $R$ and a finitely presented $R$-module $M$.

[F1] [[def-elementary-ideals-of-a-finitely-presented-module]]: for a presentation matrix $A$ of size $n\times m$, the elementary ideal $E_k(M)$ is generated by its $(n-k)\times(n-k)$ minors, with the same empty-minor conventions.

[F2] [[lem-elementary-ideals-are-independent-of-the-presentation]]: $E_k(M)$ is independent of the presentation and is invariant under module isomorphism.
