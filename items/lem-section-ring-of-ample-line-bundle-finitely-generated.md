## Statement

Assume AC as inherited from the projective and sheaf-cohomology suppliers. Let $X$ be a projective scheme over $\mathbb C$ and $L$ an ample invertible sheaf on $X$ ([[def-ample-invertible-sheaf]]). Then the section ring
$$R(X,L)=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}\Gamma(X,L^{\otimes n})$$
is a finitely generated graded $\mathbb C$-algebra, hence a Noetherian ring.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A projective $\mathbb C$-scheme $X$, an ample invertible sheaf $L$ on $X$, and the section ring $R(X,L)$.

[F1] *Very ample positive power.* Applied to the proper finite-type morphism $X\to\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb C$ and the ample sheaf $L$, the very-ampleness theorem gives an integer $m\ge1$, an integer $N\ge0$ and global sections of $L^{\otimes m}$ generating $L^{\otimes m}$ whose associated morphism is a closed immersion $i:X\hookrightarrow\mathbf P^N_{\mathbb C}$ with $i^*\mathcal O(1)\cong L^{\otimes m}$. ([[thm-ample-powers-very-ample-proper-base]], [[thm-line-bundle-sections-define-projective-map]], [[def-globally-generated-sheaf]])

[F2] *Graded sections of a coherent sheaf.* For a coherent sheaf $\mathcal F$ on $\mathbf P^N_{\mathbb C}$ the graded $S$-module $\Gamma_*(\mathcal F)=\bigoplus_{k\ge0}\Gamma(\mathbf P^N,\mathcal F(k))$ has a finitely generated tail: there is $k_0$ with $\bigoplus_{k\ge k_0}\Gamma(\mathbf P^N,\mathcal F(k))$ finitely generated over $S=\mathbb C[x_0,\dots,x_N]$; the extended sheaf $i_*\mathcal G$ along the closed immersion $i$ is coherent. Each individual space $\Gamma(\mathbf P^N,\mathcal F(k))$ is finite-dimensional over $\mathbb C$. ([[lem-graded-section-module-finite-projective]], [[cor-projective-cohomology-finite-dimensional-field]])

[F3] *The coordinate ring image.* Let $S=\mathbb C[x_0,\dots,x_N]$ and let $A$ be the homogeneous coordinate ring of the closed immersion $i:X\hookrightarrow\mathbf P^N$, namely the image of the graded restriction map $S\to M_0=\bigoplus_{k\ge0}\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X(k))$. It is a finitely generated graded $\mathbb C$-algebra, being a quotient of $S$; the restriction maps need not be surjective onto every space of global sections. ([[thm-closed-subschemes-projective-space-homogeneous-ideals]])

[F4] *Hilbert basis.* A finitely generated algebra over a field is Noetherian. ([[thm-hilbert-basis-theorem]])

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 If $X=\varnothing$ then $R(X,L)=0$ is generated by the empty set and is Noetherian; assume henceforth $X\ne\varnothing$. Fix the integer $m\ge1$ and the closed immersion $i:X\hookrightarrow\mathbf P^N_{\mathbb C}$ of [F1], so that $\mathcal O_X(1)$ in the following denotes $i^*\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^N}(1)\cong L^{\otimes m}$ and $L^{\otimes(mk+j)}\cong i^*\mathcal O(k)\otimes L^{\otimes j}$ for all $k\ge0$, $0\le j<m$. [F1, given]

2.1 *The graded modules $M_j$.* For $0\le j<m$ put $M_j=\bigoplus_{k\ge0}\Gamma(X,L^{\otimes(mk+j)})$. Under the identification of step 1.1 and the projection formula for the finite morphism $i$, $M_j=\Gamma_*(\mathcal F_j)$ for the coherent sheaf $\mathcal F_j=i_*(L^{\otimes j})$ on $\mathbf P^N_{\mathbb C}$; hence by [F2] each $M_j$ has a finitely generated tail over $S=\mathbb C[x_0,\dots,x_N]$. A graded $S$-module whose tail is finitely generated is finitely generated: the missing finite initial part is a finite-dimensional $\mathbb C$-vector space by [F2], and an extension of a finitely generated module by a finite-dimensional one is finitely generated. So each $M_j$ is a finitely generated graded $S$-module. [F1, F2, step 1.1]

3.1 *The section modules over the coordinate ring image.* Let $A\subseteq M_0$ be the image in [F3]. It is a finitely generated $\mathbb C$-algebra. For $p\in S$ in the kernel of $S\to A$, its restriction is the zero section on $X$, so multiplication by $p$ is zero on every $\Gamma(X,L^{\otimes(mk+j)})$; hence the $S$-action on each $M_j$ factors through $A$. The finite $S$-module generators of step 2.1 therefore also generate $M_j$ as an $A$-module. In particular $M_0$ and each of the finitely many $M_j$ are finite $A$-modules. [F2, F3, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 *Conclusion.* The decomposition $R(X,L)=\bigoplus_{j=0}^{m-1}M_j$ and step 3.1 show that $R(X,L)$ is a finite module over the finitely generated $\mathbb C$-algebra $A$. A finite set of algebra generators of $A$ together with a finite set of $A$-module generators of $R(X,L)$ generates $R(X,L)$ as a $\mathbb C$-algebra. Hence $R(X,L)$ is a finitely generated $\mathbb C$-algebra, and it is Noetherian by [F4]. [F3, F4, step 3.1, algebra] ∎

## Remarks

- **The proof uses only the ample power.** Neither the very-ampleness of $L$ itself nor a Hilbert--Mumford criterion is used; the finite generation comes from the graded-module theorem on projective space applied to the coherent sheaves $i_*(L^{\otimes j})$.
- **No separatedness issue.** $X$ is a projective $\mathbb C$-scheme, in particular proper and of finite type over $\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb C$; all cited suppliers are stated in that register.
