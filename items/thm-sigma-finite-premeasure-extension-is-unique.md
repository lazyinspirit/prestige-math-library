## Statement

A sigma-finite premeasure has at most one measure extension to the sigma-algebra generated by its source algebra.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A sigma-finite premeasure $\mu_0$ on $\mathcal A_0$, a sequence $(A_n)$ in $\mathcal A_0$ covering $X$ with finite premeasure, and extensions $\mu,\nu$ on $\sigma(\mathcal A_0)$.

[F1] A **premeasure** on an algebra $\mathcal A_0$ vanishes at the empty set and is countably additive whenever a disjoint sequence in $\mathcal A_0$ has its union in $\mathcal A_0$. ([[def-premeasure-on-an-algebra]])

[F2] An algebra of subsets is nonempty and is closed under finite unions and intersections. ([[def-algebra-of-subsets]])

[F3] A pi-system on $X$ is a nonempty family of subsets closed under binary intersections. ([[def-pi-system]])

[L1] If two measures agree on a generating pi-system and on an increasing exhaustion $(P_n)$ from that pi-system with $X=\bigcup_nP_n$ and equal finite values on every $P_n$, then the measures are equal on the generated sigma-algebra. ([[thm-measure-uniqueness-on-a-sigma-finite-pi-system]])
## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Put $P_n=\bigcup_{k\le n}A_k$. By [F2], $P_n\in\mathcal A_0$, the sequence is increasing with union $X$, and finite additivity from [F1] gives $\mu_0(P_n)\le\sum_{k\le n}\mu_0(A_k)<+\infty$. [F1, F2, algebra]

2.1 The algebra $\mathcal A_0$ is nonempty and closed under intersections by [F2], hence is a pi-system by [F3]; it generates $\sigma(\mathcal A_0)$, and both extensions agree with $\mu_0$ on it and on the exhaustion $(P_n)$ from step 1.1. [step 1.1, F3, given]

3.1 Applying [L1] to the generating pi-system and the increasing finite-measure exhaustion of step 2.1 gives $\mu=\nu$ on $\sigma(\mathcal A_0)$. [step 2.1, L1] ∎
