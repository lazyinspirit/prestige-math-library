## Statement

In ZF, let $k$ be a field and let $C/k$ be a proper finite-type scheme. There are a finitely generated $\mathbb Z$-subalgebra $B\subset k$, its fraction field $k_0$, and a proper finitely presented $k_0$-scheme $C_0$ whose base change to $k$ is $C$. Any specified finite diagram of finitely presented étale objects over $C$, their maps and finitely many equality, inverse and finite-cover witnesses can be included after enlarging $B$ by finitely many field elements. With a specified finite generating tuple, the abstract rings $B,k_0$ and all retained finite equations have common natural-number codes inside and outside the constructible class $L$.

This field-specific result does not assert the corresponding model theorem over an arbitrary henselian local ring.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The field, the one proper finite-type scheme, and the specified finite diagram and witnesses.

[F1] Each fixed ZFC axiom holds in $L$ as a ZF theorem; Noetherian Chow under AC can be used internally, with its finite certificate transported by the common codes. [[thm-constructible-inner-model-semantic-and-formal-schema]] [[lem-chow-lemma-proper-noetherian]]

[F2] Projective closed schemes are proper, a surjective finite-type map is universally surjective, and properness descends along a proper surjection in ZF. [[lem-projective-properness-and-finite-type-surjectivity-without-choice]]

[F3] An immersion with a supplied finite closed-immersion certificate stabilizes as closed along a filtered ring colimit. [[lem-projective-immersion-with-finite-closed-certificate-stabilizes-in-zf]]

[F4] A finite list of algebraic gluing, inverse and unit-ideal certificates descends to one common stage. [[lem-finite-algebraic-gluing-certificates-descend-without-choice]]

[F5] Étale morphisms are universally open in ZF. [[lem-etale-morphisms-are-locally-standard-and-universally-open-in-zf]]

## Proof

**Proof technique:** produce the missing finite cover certificates over a field and transport one internal projective Chow witness.

1.1 Every finite-type $k$-algebra is Noetherian by finite Hilbert-basis induction, so $C/k$ is finitely presented. For any such algebra $D$ and finitely many elements whose principal opens cover $\operatorname{Spec}D$, their ideal is the unit ideal: if its quotient were nonzero, finite Noether normalization and the determinant trick would give it a maximal ideal with finite residue field over $k$, contrary to coverage. Thus the finite affine covers, overlaps, separated diagonal, and their maps have actual finite unit-ideal and polynomial witnesses, not merely pointwise cover assertions. The same applies to the specified finite étale diagram and its overlaps. [F4, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 Retain the finitely many field coefficients in those witnesses, and let $B_0\subset k$ be the integer subring they generate. The kernel of its finite polynomial presentation is finitely generated by finite Hilbert-basis induction. Using [F4], the finite equations descend to a separated finitely presented $C_{B_0}/B_0$ whose base change is $C$. Its finite affine chart rings and all their ideals are encoded by finite natural-number lists; every ideal has finitely many generators, so the external and internal-to-$L$ ideal and prime-point data on these charts agree. [F1, F4, step 1.1, given, construct, algebra]

3.1 Inside $L$, apply [F1] to the separated Noetherian $C_{B_0}$ and obtain a proper surjection $P_0\to C_{B_0}$ and an immersion $P_0\to\mathbb P^m_{B_0}$. Its graph is a closed immersion into $\mathbb P^m_{C_{B_0}}$; extract its finite equations and inverse descriptions. The same equations are valid externally, so [F2] makes $P_0\to C_{B_0}$ proper; the common finite-code prime sets from step 2.1 make it surjective. Base change to $k$. The resulting $P_k$ is proper over $k$, because it is proper over the given proper $C$. Its immersion into separated projective space is consequently a closed immersion. [F1, F2, step 2.1, given, construct]

4.1 The closed immersion $P_k\hookrightarrow\mathbb P^m_k$ has the finite algebraic certificate required by [F3]: its chart ideals are finitely generated because the chart rings are finite type over $k$, and its finite principal covers give actual unit-ideal identities by step 1.1. Apply [F3] to the filtered system of all finitely generated integer subrings of $k$ containing $B_0$. At a later such ring $B_1$, the immersion of the base-changed $P_{B_1}$ is closed, hence $P_{B_1}$ is proper over $B_1$. The base-changed $P_{B_1}\to C_{B_1}$ is proper and universally surjective; [F2] makes $C_{B_1}$ proper. Finite presentation and separatedness persist under base change. [F2, F3, step 3.1, given, construct, algebra]

5.1 Enlarge $B_1$ by the finitely many coefficients of the specified diagram and equality witnesses; properness persists by base change. Set $k_0=\operatorname{Frac}B$ and $C_0=C_B\times_Bk_0$. The diagram and all equations are defined over $k_0$, and base change to $k$ gives the supplied data. For the finite-cover assertions, the omitted locus is closed in a finite-type $k_0$-scheme by [F5]; if it were nonempty, finite Noether normalization would supply a point with finite residue extension, which remains nonempty after tensoring with $k$, contradicting the original cover. Finally, the kernel of the finite integer polynomial map defining $B$ has a finite generating list; the fraction field and retained equations are finite natural-number codes, hence belong to $L$ with the same arithmetic meaning. [F1, F2, F4, F5, step 4.1, given, construct, algebra] ∎
