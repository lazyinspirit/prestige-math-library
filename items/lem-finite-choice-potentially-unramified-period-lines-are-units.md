## Statement

Assume $\mathrm{AC}_{\omega,\mathrm{fin}}$. Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be a GIVEN valued algebraic closure, let $C=\widehat F$, suppose a compatible primitive cyclotomic system is GIVEN, and let $K\subset F$ be finite. Let $B$ be $B_{\mathrm{cris}}$ or $B_{\mathrm{st}}$, and let $0\ne b\in B$ span a $G_K$-stable $\mathbb Q_p$-line with character $\eta$. If there is an integer $j$ such that $(\eta\chi^{-j})(I_K)$ is finite, then $b$ is a unit of $B$. This theorem does not assert that every stable-line character satisfies the finite-inertia condition.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** Countable choice for finite sets, the valued closure, its completion and primitive cyclotomic system, the finite extension $K/\mathbb Q_p$, the stable line, and the stated finite-inertia condition.

[A1] $\mathrm{AC}_{\omega,\mathrm{fin}}$ is countable choice for nonempty finite sets. [[def-choice-for-pairs-and-countable-finite-choice]]

[F1] Under [A1], the normalized semistable tensor map is injective and restricts to the crystalline embedding. [[lem-countable-finite-choice-padic-galois-lifting-and-period-comparison]]

[F2] Under [A1], the residue map $G_K\to\operatorname{Gal}(\overline k/k)$ is continuous, surjective and open with kernel $I_K$, and continuous characters trivial on $I_K$ descend continuously. [[lem-finite-choice-local-galois-residue-sequence]]

[F3] Under [A1], a stable period line whose character has a positive power equal to a cyclotomic power times a given continuous unramified character is generated by a unit. [[lem-finite-choice-unramified-twist-period-lines-are-units]]

[F4] The de Rham plus ring is a complete DVR with residue $C$ and uniformizer $t$. [[lem-choice-free-de-rham-period-completion-is-a-discrete-valuation-ring]]

[F5] $g(t)=\chi(g)t$ for every $g\in G_K$. [[lem-choice-free-crystalline-logarithm-and-abstract-semistable-period-ring]]

[F6] Finite embeddings in the given closure are isometric and the closure is dense in its completion. [[lem-choice-free-finite-ax-sen-approximation]]

[F7] The residue-field Galois group is topologically generated by arithmetic Frobenius and canonically isomorphic to $\widehat{\mathbb Z}$. [[thm-absolute-galois-group-of-finite-field-is-procyclic]]

## Proof

1.1 By [F1] regard $b$ as a nonzero element of $B_{\mathrm{dR}}$, and let $s\in\mathbb Z$ be its $t$-adic valuation. The leading coefficient $c=t^{-s}b\bmod t\in C^\times$ satisfies $g(c)=\eta(g)\chi(g)^{-s}c$ by [F4]–[F5]. Each $g\in G_K$ is an isometry on $C$ by [F6], and $\chi(g)$ is a $p$-adic unit; hence $\eta(g)\in\mathbb Z_p^\times$. The character $\eta$ is continuous: for any desired precision choose one algebraic approximation $a\in F$ to $c$; the open subgroup fixing $a$ moves $c$ by at most that approximation error, and $\chi$ is continuous on its finite cyclotomic coordinates. [F1, F4, F5, F6, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 By hypothesis the image of $\psi=\eta\chi^{-j}$ on $I_K$ is a finite group. Choose its positive order $r$; then $\psi^r=\eta^r\chi^{-jr}$ is trivial on inertia. It is continuous and $\mathbb Z_p^\times$-valued by step 1.1. By [F2] it descends uniquely and continuously to the residue Galois group, which [F7] identifies with $\widehat{\mathbb Z}$. [F2, F7, step 1.1, given, algebra]

3.1 Apply [F3] with the positive power $r$, the integer $n=jr$, and the descended continuous unramified character of step 2.1. It follows that $b$ is a unit of $B$. The finite-inertia hypothesis was used explicitly and no general Sen character statement is inferred. [F3, step 2.1, algebra] ∎

## Source notes

The finite-inertia reduction and leading-coefficient continuity argument are written out locally. The unramified eigenunit and residue exactness are separately proved at the same weak-choice scope. The source's assertion that every relevant $C$-valued eigencharacter has finite inertia is a separate Sen-theoretic obligation and is not assumed here.
