## Statement

In ZF, let $A$ be a commutative ring and $X\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ be proper, with proper meaning separated, finite type and universally closed. There exists a directed inverse system of proper finitely presented $A$-schemes $X_\lambda$ with closed immersion transition maps and closed limit maps, such that $X\simeq\varprojlim X_\lambda$. In particular no finite-presentation hypothesis on $X/A$ or Noetherian hypothesis on $A$ is imposed.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The ring $A$ and the proper finite-type $A$-scheme $X$.

[F1] On a Noetherian scheme every quasi-coherent module is the union of its coherent submodules, and every specified finite set of local sections is contained in one. This is proved by a finite chart/denominator construction without choice. [[lem-nagata-coherent-submodule-extension-and-integral-algebra-approximation]]

[F2] A finite-type surjection is universally surjective; projective space and closed immersions are proper; and properness descends through a proper surjection to a separated finite-type target. [[lem-projective-properness-and-finite-type-surjectivity-without-choice]]

[F3] ZF proves each fixed ZFC axiom relativized to $L$; the locally proved Noetherian Chow lemma under AC may therefore be used inside $L$ for a specified finite integer model, and only its finite algebraic certificate is transported externally. [[thm-constructible-inner-model-semantic-and-formal-schema]] [[lem-chow-lemma-proper-noetherian]]

## Proof

**Proof technique:** construct one Noetherian-coordinate ambient scheme and cut its projective boundary by finite ideals.

1.1 Choose a finite affine cover $U_i=\operatorname{Spec}C_i$ of $X$ and finite $A$-algebra generators $x_i$ for each $C_i$. Every pairwise intersection is affine: it is the inverse image of the closed diagonal under $U_i\times_AU_j\to X\times_AX$. Its algebra $C_{ij}$ is therefore a quotient of $C_i\otimes_AC_j$, generated by the two coordinate lists. Choose finite principal covers of the overlaps, express each coordinate list in the other using finite denominators, and retain finite unit-ideal and triple-overlap cocycle witnesses. These are choices from finitely many given charts. [given, construct, algebra]

2.1 Let $B\subset A$ be the finite-type integer subring generated by the finitely many coefficients in those witnesses. Let $D_i$ be the image of $B[x_i]$ in $C_i$ and $E_{ij}$ the image of $B[x_i,x_j]$ in $C_{ij}$. They are finitely presented over the Noetherian ring $B$. On each selected principal patch, $D_i$ and $E_{ij}$ localize to the same ring: surjectivity is the retained coordinate formula, and injectivity follows by embedding both into the corresponding localization of $C_i$. The unit identities give the entire overlaps and the triple identities give the cocycle. Glue the finite affine schemes $\operatorname{Spec}D_i$ to a separated finitely presented $Y_B/B$. Its diagonal is closed because $D_i\otimes_BD_j\to E_{ij}$ is surjective. Base change to $A$ gives a closed immersion $X\hookrightarrow Y_A$: each $A\otimes_BD_i\to C_i$ is surjective and the actual overlaps are the selected open patches. [step 1.1, construct, algebra]

3.1 Let $\mathcal I$ be the ideal of $X$ in $Y_A$, and $p:Y_A\to Y_B$ the affine base projection. The external module $p_*\mathcal I$ is quasi-coherent. By [F1], it is the union of its coherent submodules. Their pullback images are finite-type quasi-coherent ideals $\mathcal I_\lambda\subseteq\mathcal I$, and they form a directed system whose union is $\mathcal I$. Set $X_\lambda=V(\mathcal I_\lambda)\subseteq Y_A$. Each is finitely presented over $A$, transitions are closed immersions, and the affine quotient-ring colimit gives $X\simeq\varprojlim X_\lambda$. No external ideal or module has been assumed constructible. [F1, step 2.1, given, construct]

4.1 The Noetherian $Y_B$ has finite natural-number chart codes. In $L$, [F3] gives a proper surjection $\pi_B:Y'_B\to Y_B$ and an immersion $Y'_B\to\mathbb P^n_B$. Its graph over $Y_B$ is a closed immersion into $\mathbb P^n_{Y_B}$, hence is a finite projective certificate. That finite certificate is valid externally; [F2] gives its properness. Its surjectivity transports because all prime ideals of these finitely coded Noetherian chart rings are finitely generated and thus the same finite codes inside and outside $L$. By [F2] its base change $\pi:Y'_A\to Y_A$ is a universally surjective proper map. [F2, F3, step 2.1, construct, step 3.1]

5.1 Write $X'=Y'_A\times_{Y_A}X$ and $X'_\lambda=Y'_A\times_{Y_A}X_\lambda$. Since $X$ and $\pi$ are proper, $X'$ is proper over $A$. Its immersion into separated $\mathbb P^n_A$ is therefore a closed immersion. Let $W_B\subseteq\mathbb P^n_B$ be a quasi-compact open in which $Y'_B$ is closed, and let $K$ be the extension to $\mathbb P^n_A$ of a coherent ideal cutting out the complement. The ideal $J_\infty$ of the closed $X'$ satisfies $J_\infty+K=\mathcal O$. By [F1] applied on the fixed Noetherian projective model, $J_\infty$ is the union of finite-type quasi-coherent subideals. A finite affine cover and the finitely many unit identities give one finite-type $J\subseteq J_\infty$ with $J+K=\mathcal O$. Thus $Z=V(J)$ is closed and finitely presented in projective space and lies inside $W_A$. [F1, F2, step 4.1, given, construct]

6.1 The finite local generators of $J\mathcal O_{Y'_A}$ belong to $\mathcal I\mathcal O_{Y'_A}=\bigcup_\lambda\mathcal I_\lambda\mathcal O_{Y'_A}$. Finitely many membership witnesses occur in one common $\lambda$. For this and every later stage, $X'_\lambda$ is closed in $Z$ and hence proper over $A$. The base change $X'_\lambda\to X_\lambda$ of $\pi$ is a proper universal surjection, so [F2] makes the separated finite-type $X_\lambda$ proper. Restrict the directed system to those later stages. Its closed limit is still $X$, completing the proof at the original finite-type scope. [F2, step 3.1, step 5.1, construct] ∎
