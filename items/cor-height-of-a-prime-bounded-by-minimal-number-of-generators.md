## Statement

Let $R$ be a Noetherian commutative ring and let $\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Spec}(R)$. Then
$$ \operatorname{ht}(\mathfrak p)\le \mu_{R_{\mathfrak p}}(\mathfrak pR_{\mathfrak p}), $$
where $\mu$ denotes the minimal number of generators.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A Noetherian commutative ring $R$ and a prime ideal $\mathfrak p$.

[L1] The localization $R_{\mathfrak p}$ is a Noetherian local ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak pR_{\mathfrak p}$ ([[thm-noetherian-ring-quotients-and-localisations]], [[thm-localisation-at-a-prime-is-local]]).

[L2] A prime minimal over an ideal generated by $n$ elements has height at most $n$ ([[thm-krull-height-theorem]]).

[L3] The height of $\mathfrak p$ is the dimension of $R_{\mathfrak p}$ ([[def-height-of-a-prime-ideal]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 By [L1], the maximal ideal of the local ring $R_{\mathfrak p}$ is $\mathfrak pR_{\mathfrak p}$. If it is minimally generated by $n$ elements, then it is certainly minimal over the ideal generated by those same $n$ elements. Applying [L2] inside $R_{\mathfrak p}$ gives $\dim R_{\mathfrak p}\le n$. [L1, L2, given]

2.1 By [L3], $\operatorname{ht}(\mathfrak p)=\dim R_{\mathfrak p}$, so step 1.1 says $\operatorname{ht}(\mathfrak p)\le\mu_{R_{\mathfrak p}}(\mathfrak pR_{\mathfrak p})$. [L3, step 1.1]

3.1 This is the claimed local-generator bound on height. [step 2.1] ∎
