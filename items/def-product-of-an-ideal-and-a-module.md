## Definition

Let $R$ be a commutative ring, let $I\mathrel{\trianglelefteq}R$ be an ideal ([[def-left-right-and-two-sided-ideal]]), and let $M$ be an $R$-module ([[def-left-and-right-modules]]). The **product of $I$ and $M$** is

$$IM:=\left\{\sum_{k=1}^t i_km_k:t\in\mathbb N,\ i_k\in I,\ m_k\in M\right\},$$

where the case $t=0$ is the empty sum $0$. It is the submodule of $M$ generated by the products $im$: sums and negatives remain of the displayed form, and $r(im)=(ri)m$ with $ri\in I$.

Thus $0M=0$ and $RM=M$; the latter follows from $m=1_Rm$.
