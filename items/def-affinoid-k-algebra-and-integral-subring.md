## Definition

Let $K$ be a complete nonarchimedean field. An **affinoid $K$-algebra pair** is a pair $(R,R^+)$ in which $R$ is a complete Tate $K$-algebra and $R^+$ is an open subring of $R$ contained in its subring $R^\circ$ of power-bounded elements and integrally closed in $R$. A morphism $(R,R^+)\to(S,S^+)$ is a continuous $K$-algebra homomorphism $R\to S$ carrying $R^+$ into $S^+$. The notation $R^+$ denotes the chosen integral subring, and need not equal $R^\circ$.
