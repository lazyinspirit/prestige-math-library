## Definition

Let $Ax=b$ be a linear system, let $x_0$ be an initial guess, and let

$$r_0:=b-Ax_0.$$

For $m\ge 0$, a **GMRES iterate at step $m$** is any vector
$x_m\in x_0+K_m(A,r_0)$ such that

$$\|b-Ax_m\|_2=\min_{x\in x_0+K_m(A,r_0)} \|b-Ax\|_2.$$

Thus GMRES is defined by residual minimization over the affine Krylov space
generated by the initial residual.
