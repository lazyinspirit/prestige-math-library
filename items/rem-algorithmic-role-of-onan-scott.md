The practical value of the O'Nan-Scott theorem is reduction. Once a finite
primitive permutation group is assigned a socle type, many structural and
algorithmic questions can be handled case by case inside the affine,
almost-simple, diagonal, product-action, or twisted-wreath branches instead of
starting from an arbitrary primitive action.
