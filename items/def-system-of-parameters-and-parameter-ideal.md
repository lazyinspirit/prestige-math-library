## Definition

Let $(R,\mathfrak m)$ be a finite-dimensional Noetherian local ring of dimension $d=\dim R<\infty$. A tuple
$$
(x_1,\ldots,x_d)\in\mathfrak m^d
$$
is a **system of parameters** when
$$
\sqrt{(x_1,\ldots,x_d)}=\mathfrak m.
$$
Equivalently, the ideal generated by the tuple has maximal radical. That ideal is called a **parameter ideal**.

When $d=0$, the empty tuple is a system of parameters exactly when $\sqrt{(0)}=\mathfrak m$. In a zero-dimensional Noetherian local ring this is the correct convention, so systems of parameters still have length equal to the dimension.
