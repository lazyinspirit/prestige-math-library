## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $G$ be an LCH group and let
$\pi\in\widehat G$. For finitely many functions of positive type
$\phi_1,\dots,\phi_n$ associated to $\pi$, a compact $Q\subseteq G$ and
$\epsilon>0$ put
$$\widetilde W(\pi;\phi_1,\dots,\phi_n,Q,\epsilon):=\{\rho\in\widehat G:\ \text{each }\phi_i\text{ is within }\epsilon\text{ on }Q\text{ of a single function of positive type associated to }\rho\}.$$
Then the sets $\widetilde W(\pi;\phi_1,\dots,\phi_n,Q,\epsilon)$ that contain
$\pi$ form a basis of neighbourhoods of $\pi$ in the Fell topology
([[def-fell-topology-on-the-unitary-dual]]). Consequently every Fell-open
subset of $\widehat G$ is saturated under weak equivalence: if $U$ is open,
$\pi\in U$ and $\rho\in\widehat G$ with $\rho\sim\pi$
([[def-weak-containment-of-unitary-representations]]), then
$\rho\in U$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC; an LCH group $G$; a class $\pi\in\widehat G$; the Fell topology on $\widehat G$; the single-function sets $\widetilde W$.

[F1] The Fell topology is generated by the standard sets $W(\pi;\phi_1,\dots,\phi_n,Q,\epsilon)=\{\rho:\text{each }\phi_i\text{ is within }\epsilon\text{ on }Q\text{ of a finite sum of functions of positive type associated to }\rho\}$, where the $\phi_i$ run over the functions of positive type associated to $\pi$; a single function of positive type is a finite sum (one term), so $\widetilde W\subseteq W$ for the same data, and $\pi\in\widetilde W(\pi;\phi_1,\dots,\phi_n,Q,\epsilon)$ because the tested $\phi_i$ are themselves functions of positive type associated to $\pi$ ([[def-fell-topology-on-the-unitary-dual]], [[def-continuous-function-of-positive-type]], [[def-matrix-coefficient-of-a-unitary-representation]]).

[F2] Weak containment: $\pi\prec\rho$ means every function of positive type associated to $\pi$ is a compact-uniform limit of finite sums of functions of positive type associated to $\rho$; $\pi\sim\rho$ means both containments ([[def-weak-containment-of-unitary-representations]]).

[F3] If $\pi$ is irreducible, $\pi\prec\rho$ and $\phi$ is a normalized function of positive type associated to $\pi$, then for every compact $Q$ and $\epsilon>0$ there is a unit vector $\eta\in H_\rho$ with $\sup_Q|\phi(g)-\langle\rho(g)\eta,\eta\rangle|<\epsilon$ ([[lem-normalized-coefficient-approximation-for-irreducible-weak-containment]]).

[F4] Simultaneous family selection: if $(\rho_s)_{s\in S}$ is a family of nonzero unitary representations and $\pi\prec\widehat\bigoplus_{s\in S}\rho_s$ with $\pi$ irreducible, then for all vectors $\xi_1,\dots,\xi_n\in H_\pi$, compact $Q$ and $\epsilon>0$ there are a single $s\in S$ and vectors $\eta_1,\dots,\eta_n\in H_{\rho_s}$ with $\sup_Q|\langle\pi(g)\xi_i,\xi_i\rangle-\langle\rho_s(g)\eta_i,\eta_i\rangle|<\epsilon$ for every $i$ ([[lem-irreducible-weak-containment-in-a-family-selects-one-coefficient]], [[def-hilbert-direct-sum-of-unitary-representations]]).

[F5] Every class in $\widehat G$ is the class of an irreducible representation with nonzero carrier, and AC licenses the choice of a representative for each class ([[def-unitary-dual-of-a-locally-compact-group]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** direct.

**Given:** AC, an LCH group $G$, a class $\pi\in\widehat G$ and the Fell topology on $\widehat G$.

1.1 Let $T\subseteq\widehat G$ be a set of classes such that every standard Fell neighbourhood of $\pi$ meets $T$. Then $\pi\prec\widehat\bigoplus_{\sigma\in T}\sigma$. Indeed, let $\phi$ be a function of positive type associated to $\pi$, $Q$ compact and $\epsilon>0$; the standard neighbourhood $W(\pi;\phi,Q,\epsilon)$ contains a class in $T$, which by definition of $W$ supplies a finite sum of functions of positive type associated to that member of $T$, and such a finite sum is a finite sum of functions of positive type associated to the direct sum over $T$ (each is computed from finitely many vectors supported on finitely many summands); this is precisely the defining approximation for $\pi\prec\widehat\bigoplus_{\sigma\in T}\sigma$. [F1, F2, F4, F5]

2.1 Every $\widetilde W(\pi;\phi_1,\dots,\phi_n,Q,\epsilon)$ containing $\pi$ contains a standard Fell neighbourhood of $\pi$. Suppose, to the contrary, that no standard neighbourhood of $\pi$ is contained in $\widetilde W:=\widetilde W(\pi;\phi_1,\dots,\phi_n,Q,\epsilon)$; then every standard neighbourhood of $\pi$ meets $T:=\widehat G\setminus\widetilde W$, so $\pi\prec\widehat\bigoplus_{\sigma\in T}\sigma$ by step 1.1. By [F5] choose representatives of the classes in $T$. Each tested function is a single diagonal coefficient, so write $\phi_i(g)=\langle\pi(g)\xi_i,\xi_i\rangle$. Applying [F4] to the finite list $\xi_1,\dots,\xi_n$ on $Q$ with radius $\epsilon$ gives a single class $\sigma\in T$ and vectors $\eta_i\in H_\sigma$ such that each $\phi_i$ is within $\epsilon$ on $Q$ of the single coefficient $\langle\sigma(\cdot)\eta_i,\eta_i\rangle$. This says $\sigma\in\widetilde W$, contradicting $\sigma\in\widehat G\setminus\widetilde W$. Hence $\widetilde W$ contains a standard neighbourhood of $\pi$, and with [F1] the sets $\widetilde W$ containing $\pi$ form a neighbourhood basis. [F1, F4, F5, step 1.1]

3.1 Every Fell-open set is saturated under weak equivalence. Let $U$ be Fell-open and $\pi\in U$; by the definition of the Fell topology and step 2.1 there are $\phi_1,\dots,\phi_n,Q,\epsilon$ with $\pi\in\widetilde W(\pi;\phi_1,\dots,\phi_n,Q,\epsilon)\subseteq U$. Let $\rho\in\widehat G$ with $\rho\sim\pi$, so in particular $\pi\prec\rho$; since $\pi$ is irreducible, write $\phi_i=\langle\pi(\cdot)\xi_i,\xi_i\rangle$. If $\xi_i=0$, use the zero vector of $H_\rho$. Otherwise apply [F3] to $\phi_i/\|\xi_i\|^2$ with precision $\epsilon/\|\xi_i\|^2$ and rescale its unit-vector witness by $\|\xi_i\|$. This supplies a single diagonal coefficient of $\rho$ within $\epsilon$ of each $\phi_i$ on $Q$. Hence $\rho\in\widetilde W\subseteq U$. Thus $U$ contains the weak equivalence class of each of its points, and by symmetry the same holds for $\rho$ in place of $\pi$. [F2, F3, step 2.1]

4.1 The Axiom of Choice licenses the choice of representatives of the classes in $T$ through the unitary dual and is inherited from the family-selection and approximation lemmas; the contradiction argument and the saturation computation add no further choice ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]). [given, F5] ∎
