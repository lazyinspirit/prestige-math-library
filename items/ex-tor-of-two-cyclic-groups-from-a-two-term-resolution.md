## Example

Compute $\operatorname{Tor}^{\mathbb Z}_1(\mathbb Z/12,\mathbb Z/18)\cong\mathbb Z/6$.

## Verification

**Given:** the two-term resolution $0\to\mathbb Z\xrightarrow{12}\mathbb Z\to\mathbb Z/12\to0$.

1.1 Tensoring with $\mathbb Z/18$ gives the degree-one kernel of multiplication by $12$ on $\mathbb Z/18$. [given]

2.1 The congruence $12x\equiv0\pmod {18}$ has six solutions, namely the subgroup generated by $3$. [step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 That subgroup is cyclic of order $6$, so the Tor group is $\mathbb Z/6$. [step 2.1, algebra] ∎
