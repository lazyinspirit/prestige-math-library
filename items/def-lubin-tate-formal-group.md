## Definition

Let $K$ be a nonarchimedean local field with ring of integers $\mathcal O_K$,
maximal ideal generated by a uniformizer $\pi$, and residue field of size
$q$. A one-dimensional commutative formal group law over $\mathcal O_K$ is
a formal power series $F(X,Y)$ with coefficients in $\mathcal O_K$ satisfying the formal identities
$F(X,0)=X$, $F(0,Y)=Y$, $F(X,Y)=F(Y,X)$, and
$F(F(X,Y),Z)=F(X,F(Y,Z))$. For $a\in\mathcal O_K$, an endomorphism
$[a]_F(X)$ is a formal power series with coefficients in $\mathcal O_K$ and zero constant term, and satisfies
$[a]_F(F(X,Y))=F([a]_F(X),[a]_F(Y))$. A **Lubin–Tate formal group for $\pi$**
is such a formal group equipped with an $\mathcal O_K$-module action
$a\mapsto[a]_F$ whose tangent action is the given scalar action, meaning
$[a]_F(X)\equiv aX\pmod{X^2}$ for every $a\in\mathcal O_K$, and an endomorphism $[\pi]_F$ satisfying
$$[\pi]_F(X)\equiv\pi X\pmod{X^2},\qquad [\pi]_F(X)\equiv X^q\pmod{\pi}.$$
