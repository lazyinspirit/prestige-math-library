## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]). Let $X$ be a
Noetherian topological space ([[def-noetherian-topological-space]]) with
$\dim X\le d$ for an integer $d\ge0$
([[def-dimension-noetherian-topological-space]]), and assume that for every open
subset $U\subseteq X$ and every integer $q>d$ the cohomology group
$H^q(X,j_{U!}\mathbb Z_U)$ vanishes, where $j_U:U\hookrightarrow X$ is the
inclusion, $\mathbb Z_U$ is the constant sheaf associated to the constant
presheaf with value $\mathbb Z$ on $U$
([[lem-abelian-sheaves-form-a-grothendieck-category]], [[def-sheafification]])
and $j_{U!}$ is extension by zero ([[def-extension-by-zero-abelian-sheaf]]),
cohomology being that of [[def-sheaf-cohomology-derived-global-sections]]. Then
$$H^q(X,\mathcal F)=0$$
for every sheaf of abelian groups $\mathcal F$ on $X$ and every integer $q>d$.

## Facts & Assumptions

[F1] For a small filtered diagram of abelian sheaves on a Noetherian space with presheaf colimit $\mathcal P$ and $\mathcal F=a\mathcal P$, the sheaf $\mathcal F$ with the canonical maps is the colimit of the diagram in $\mathrm{Ab}(X)$, and the canonical map $\operatorname*{colim}_iH^q(X,\mathcal F_i)\to H^q(X,\mathcal F)$ is an isomorphism for every $q\ge0$ ([[lem-filtered-colimits-commute-with-sheaf-cohomology-on-noetherian-spaces]]).

[F2] The second assertion of the filtered-colimit lemma gives that the canonical map $\operatorname*{colim}_iH^q(X,\mathcal F_i)\to H^q(X,\mathcal F)$ induced by the maps $H^q(X,\Phi_i)$ is an isomorphism ([[lem-filtered-colimits-commute-with-sheaf-cohomology-on-noetherian-spaces]]).

[F3] A subsheaf of the constant integer sheaf generated by finitely many sections over compact open subsets admits a chain $0=F_0\subseteq\cdots\subseteq F_N=K$ of subsheaves and, for every $1\le n\le N$, compact open subsets $V\subseteq U$ with a short exact sequence $0\to j_{V!}\mathbb Z_V\to j_{U!}\mathbb Z_U\to F_n/F_{n-1}\to0$ ([[lem-finite-filtration-of-generated-subsheaves-of-the-constant-integer-sheaf]]).

[F4] The subsheaf generated by a family of sections is the smallest subsheaf of abelian groups containing the generators: if $\mathcal H$ is a subsheaf of abelian groups with $s_\alpha\in\mathcal H(U_\alpha)$ for all $\alpha$, then $\langle s_\alpha\rangle\subseteq\mathcal H$, and sections of the generated subsheaf over an open $W$ are exactly the sections whose germs lie in the stalk subgroups generated by the germs of the generators ([[lem-subsheaf-generated-by-sections]]).

[F5] A short exact sequence of sheaves of abelian groups induces a natural long exact sequence of cohomology groups, natural in the short exact sequence ([[thm-long-exact-sequence-sheaf-cohomology]]).

[F6] Extension by zero along an open inclusion $j:U\hookrightarrow X$ is left adjoint to restriction, $\operatorname{Hom}_X(j_!\mathcal F,\mathcal G)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_U(\mathcal F,j^{-1}\mathcal G)$ ([[thm-extension-by-zero-adjunction-exactness]]).

[F7] Every morphism of presheaves from $\mathcal F$ into a sheaf factors uniquely through the sheafification map, so morphisms $\mathbb Z_U\to\mathcal E$ out of the constant sheaf correspond to elements of $\mathcal E(U)$ ([[thm-sheafification-universal-property]]).

[F8] Every open subset of a Noetherian space is compact ([[lem-noetherian-subspaces-and-compact-opens]]).

[F9] In $\mathrm{Ab}(X)$ every morphism has a kernel and a cokernel sheaf, the cokernel being the sheafification of the cokernel presheaf, and the resulting sequence $0\to\ker\to\cdot\to\operatorname{coker}\to0$ is exact ([[thm-abelian-sheaves-form-abelian-category]], [[def-kernel-cokernel-image-sheaves]], [[def-exact-sequence-sheaves]]).

[F10] In ZF the Axiom of Choice implies the Axiom of Dependent Choice, the principle under which the supplied injective resolution data and derived functors are constructed ([[thm-choice-implies-dependent-implies-countable-choice]]).

## Proof

**Given:** A Noetherian topological space $X$ with $\dim X\le d$, the vanishing hypothesis $H^q(X,j_{U!}\mathbb Z_U)=0$ for all open $U$ and all $q>d$, an arbitrary sheaf of abelian groups $\mathcal F$ on $X$, the set of all sections of $\mathcal F$ over compact open subsets and the subsheaves they generate.

1.1 Let $S$ be the disjoint union of the sets $\mathcal F(U)$ over the compact open subsets $U\subseteq X$, a set, and for a finite subset $A\subseteq S$ let $\mathcal F_A\subseteq\mathcal F$ be the subsheaf generated by the sections in $A$ over their compact open domains. If $A\subseteq A'$ then $\mathcal F_A\subseteq\mathcal F_{A'}$ by the minimality half of [F4], so the assignment $A\mapsto\mathcal F_A$ is a diagram indexed by the directed partially ordered set of finite subsets of $S$ ([[def-filtered-category-and-filtered-colimit]]), and the inclusions form a cocone on it with vertex $\mathcal F$. For every open $W\subseteq X$ the canonical map $\operatorname*{colim}_A\mathcal F_A(W)\to\mathcal F(W)$ is an isomorphism: it is injective because every $\mathcal F_A(W)$ is a subset of $\mathcal F(W)$ and an element of the filtered colimit of these subsets is zero in $\mathcal F(W)$ only if it is zero in the stage in which it appears, and it is surjective because for $s\in\mathcal F(W)$ the open set $W$ is compact by [F8], so $s$ is one of the sections in $S$ and $s\in\mathcal F_{\{s\}}(W)$ by [F4]. Hence the canonical morphism of sheaves $\operatorname*{colim}_A\mathcal F_A\to\mathcal F$ is an isomorphism of presheaves, therefore of sheaves, and [F1] applies to the diagram $A\mapsto\mathcal F_A$ with colimit $\mathcal F$. [F1, F4, F8, given] [F1, F4, F8]

1.2 Fix a finite $A\subseteq S$ with $n$ elements and suppose $n\ge1$; write $A=A'\cup\{s_0\}$ with $A'$ having $n-1$ elements and let $\mathcal G:=\mathcal F_{A'}\subseteq\mathcal F_A$ be the subsheaf generated by the sections over the domains of $A'$. Let $\mathcal Q:=\mathcal F_A/\mathcal G$ be the cokernel sheaf of the inclusion, so that by [F9] there is a short exact sequence $0\to\mathcal G\to\mathcal F_A\to\mathcal Q\to0$; by [F5] its long exact sequence contains the exact portion $$H^q(X,\mathcal G)\to H^q(X,\mathcal F_A)\to H^q(X,\mathcal Q)$$ for every $q$. If $H^q(X,\mathcal G)=0$ and $H^q(X,\mathcal Q)=0$ for all $q>d$, then $H^q(X,\mathcal F_A)=0$ for all $q>d$: the image of the first map is $0$, so the second map is injective and its target is $0$. Thus, by induction on $n$, the vanishing for all finitely generated $\mathcal F_A$ follows once it is proved for subsheaves generated by a single section over a compact open subset; the case $n=0$ is $\mathcal F_\varnothing=0$ by [F4]. [F4, F5, F9, given] [F4, F5, F9]

2.1 By [step 1.1] and the second assertion of the filtered-colimit lemma [F2], $$H^q(X,\mathcal F)\cong\operatorname*{colim}_A H^q(X,\mathcal F_A)$$ for every $q\ge0$. Consequently it suffices to prove $H^q(X,\mathcal F_A)=0$ for every finite $A\subseteq S$ and every $q>d$: then the colimit on the right is a filtered colimit of zero groups, as each $H^q(X,\mathcal F_A)$ vanishes and the transition maps are the induced maps on cohomology, and the group $H^q(X,\mathcal F)$ is therefore zero. [F2, step 1.1, given] [F2, step 1.1]

2.2 It remains to treat a subsheaf of $\mathcal F$ generated by one section $s\in\mathcal F(U)$ over a compact open $U\subseteq X$, which by [step 1.2] is the missing input for the induction; note that $\mathcal Q$ in [step 1.2] is of this form, being generated by the image of $s_0$. Consider the morphism $$\psi:j_{U!}\mathbb Z_U\longrightarrow\mathcal F$$ corresponding under [F6] and [F7] to the element $s\in\mathcal F(U)=\operatorname{Hom}_U(\mathbb Z_U,\mathcal F|_U)$; its adjoint transpose carries the canonical section $1\in\mathbb Z_U(U)$ to $s$, and its image is a subsheaf of $\mathcal F$ containing $s$, so by the minimality half of [F4] the image contains the subsheaf generated by $s$, which is $\mathcal F_A$. Conversely, each image germ at $x\in U$ is an integer multiple of $s_x$, while at $x\notin U$ the source stalk vanishes; the stalk description [F4] therefore places every image section in $\mathcal F_A$. Thus the image is exactly $\mathcal F_A$, and the induced map $j_{U!}\mathbb Z_U\to\mathcal F_A$ is an epimorphism, $K:=\ker\psi$ is a subsheaf of $j_{U!}\mathbb Z_U$ ([[def-subsheaf]]), and [F9] gives a short exact sequence $$0\to K\to j_{U!}\mathbb Z_U\to\mathcal F_A\to0 .$$ [F4, F6, F7, F9, step 1.2, given] [F4, F6, F7, F9, step 1.2]

3.1 The identification of the constant sheaves $\mathbb Z_U=\mathbb Z_X|_U$ [F7] makes the adjoint of the identity a monomorphism $j_{U!}\mathbb Z_U\rightarrowtail\mathbb Z_X$ whose image is the subsheaf of sections of $\mathbb Z_X$ supported in $U$, so the subsheaf $K$ of [step 2.2] is a subsheaf of $\mathbb Z_X$, and the same holds for every subsheaf of $K$. [F7, step 2.2]

4.1 Apply the construction of [step 1.1] with $\mathcal F$ replaced by $K$: the disjoint union $S_K$ of the sets $K(V)$ over the compact open subsets $V\subseteq X$ is a set, and for finite $B\subseteq S_K$ the subsheaf $K_B\subseteq K$ generated by the sections in $B$ is generated by finitely many sections over compact opens and is a subsheaf of $\mathbb Z_X$ by [step 3.1]; the same argument as in [step 1.1] shows that the canonical morphism $\operatorname*{colim}_B K_B\to K$ is an isomorphism of sheaves, since for every open $W$ the map $\operatorname*{colim}_B K_B(W)\to K(W)$ is injective by the subset inclusions and surjective because $W$ is compact [F8] and every section of $K(W)$ lies in the subsheaf it generates [F4]. By [F1] the sheaves $K_B$ form a small filtered diagram with colimit $K$. [F1, F4, F8, step 1.1, step 3.1]

5.1 Let $B\subseteq S_K$ be finite and let $K_B\subseteq\mathbb Z_X$ be as in [step 4.1]. By [F3] there are $N\ge0$ and a chain of subsheaves $0=F_0\subseteq F_1\subseteq\cdots\subseteq F_N=K_B$ of $\mathbb Z_X$ such that for each $1\le n\le N$ there are compact open subsets $V\subseteq W\subseteq X$ with a short exact sequence $$0\to j_{V!}\mathbb Z_V\to j_{W!}\mathbb Z_W\to F_n/F_{n-1}\to0 .$$ We prove $H^p(X,K_B)=0$ for every $p>d$ by induction on $n$. For $n=0$ this is $H^p(X,0)=0$. For the induction step, [F9] and [F5] give the exact portion $$H^p(X,F_{n-1})\to H^p(X,F_n)\to H^p(X,F_n/F_{n-1})$$ and the last group vanishes for $p>d$: applying [F5] to the short exact sequence of the previous display gives the exact portion $H^p(X,j_{W!}\mathbb Z_W)\to H^p(X,F_n/F_{n-1})\to H^{p+1}(X,j_{V!}\mathbb Z_V)$, in which the outer groups vanish for $p>d$ by the hypothesis of the statement, so $H^p(X,F_n/F_{n-1})=0$ for $p>d$. With $H^p(X,F_{n-1})=0$ for $p>d$ as the induction hypothesis, the first exact portion shows that $H^p(X,F_n)=0$ for $p>d$. Hence $H^p(X,K_B)=0$ for all $p>d$ and all finite $B$. [F3, F5, F9, given] [F3, F5, F9]

6.1 By [step 4.1] and the second assertion of the filtered-colimit lemma [F2], applied to the diagram $B\mapsto K_B$ with colimit $K$, $$H^p(X,K)\cong\operatorname*{colim}_B H^p(X,K_B)$$ for every $p\ge0$; by [step 5.1] every group in this colimit is zero for $p>d$, so $H^p(X,K)=0$ for every $p>d$. [F2, step 4.1, step 5.1]

7.1 In the short exact sequence $0\to K\to j_{U!}\mathbb Z_U\to\mathcal F_A\to0$ of [step 2.2], the long exact sequence [F5] contains the exact portion $$H^p(X,j_{U!}\mathbb Z_U)\to H^p(X,\mathcal F_A)\to H^{p+1}(X,K)$$ for every $p$. For $p>d$ the first group vanishes by the hypothesis of the statement, since $U$ is open, and the last group vanishes by [step 6.1] because $p+1>d$; hence $H^p(X,\mathcal F_A)=0$ for every $p>d$. [F5, step 6.1, step 2.2, given] [F5, step 6.1, step 2.2]

8.1 Combining the steps: for every finite $A\subseteq S$ the sheaf $\mathcal F_A$ is generated by finitely many sections over compact opens, the induction of [step 1.2] reduces its vanishing to the case of one generator, which is [step 7.1], so $H^q(X,\mathcal F_A)=0$ for every $q>d$ and every finite $A$; then [step 2.1] identifies $H^q(X,\mathcal F)$ with the filtered colimit of these zero groups for every $q>d$, which is zero. The proof is complete. The Axiom of Choice is used exactly through the vanishing theorem [F2] and the exactness statements [F1] that rest on derived global sections and injective resolutions, whose construction needs the Axiom of Dependent Choice [F10] supplied by AC. ∎ [F1, F2, F10, step 1.2, step 7.1, step 2.1]
