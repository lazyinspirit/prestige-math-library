## Example

Assume the Axiom of Choice and let $Q^{(2)}=(q_1,q_2)$ be the canonical base
configuration of [[def-geometric-braid-with-setwise-endpoints]], so that
$PB_2=\pi_1(F_2(D^2),Q^{(2)})$ in the convention of
[[def-pure-braid-group-from-ordered-configurations]]. Then
$$PB_2\cong\mathbb Z,$$
generated by the geometrically positive two-strand full twist
$A_{12}=\sigma_1^2$ of [[def-standard-pure-braid-generators]]. Under the
inverse-slicing identification of [[lem-standard-pure-braids-generate-each-free-kernel]],
the generator $A_{12}$ corresponds to the clockwise meridian of $q_1$ inside the
once-punctured disc fibre, that is to the inverse of the counterclockwise
meridian class; relative to the counterclockwise spine basis its winding is
$-1$. The group $PB_2$ is infinite cyclic and, in particular, torsion-free.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** the Axiom of Choice, the canonical base configuration $Q^{(2)}=(q_1,q_2)$ of interior points of the disc, and the truncation notation of [[thm-pure-braid-forgetting-a-strand-short-exact-sequence]].

[A1] The Axiom of Choice holds ([[def-axiom-of-choice]]).

[F1] In ZF, AC implies DC ([[thm-choice-implies-dependent-implies-countable-choice]]).

[F2] Assume AC and let $n\ge2$. With the base configuration $Q^{(n)}$ and its truncation $q'$, the forgetting map $\varphi:PB_n\to PB_{n-1}$ sits in the short exact sequence $1\to F_{n-1}\xrightarrow{\kappa}PB_n\xrightarrow{\varphi}PB_{n-1}\to1$ with $\kappa$ injective, $\varphi$ surjective and $\operatorname{im}\kappa=\ker\varphi$, and $PB_1$ is trivial; here $F_{n-1}=\pi_1(\operatorname{int}D^2\setminus\{q_1,\dots,q_{n-1}\},q_n)$ is free on the $n-1$ positively oriented meridian classes of the punctures $q_1,\dots,q_{n-1}$ ([[thm-pure-braid-forgetting-a-strand-short-exact-sequence]]).

[F3] Assume AC and let $n\ge2$. Under the identification $F_{n-1}\cong\ker\varphi$ given by $\kappa$, the elements $A_{1n},\dots,A_{n-1,n}$ of [[def-standard-pure-braid-generators]] are a free basis of $\ker\varphi$, and the $i$-th of them corresponds to the clockwise meridian of the $i$-th puncture, the inverse of the counterclockwise spine-basis class ([[lem-standard-pure-braids-generate-each-free-kernel]]).

[F4] The free group on $X=\{\gamma_1\}$ has the reduced-word model, in which each element has a unique reduced word in $\gamma_1^{\pm1}$ ([[thm-reduced-words-form-the-free-group]]). Since there is only one generator, a reduced word contains only $\gamma_1$ or only $\gamma_1^{-1}$, so every element is uniquely $\gamma_1^k$ for some $k\in\mathbb Z$.

[F5] Assume AC. For every $n\ge0$ the pure braid group $PB_n$ is torsion-free ([[thm-pure-braid-groups-are-torsion-free]]).

## Verification

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 **The choice deduction and the exact sequence at $n=2$.** By [F1] the Axiom of Choice [A1] yields DC, so the sequence of [F2] is available at $n=2$: $1\to F_1\xrightarrow{\kappa}PB_2\xrightarrow{\varphi}PB_1\to1$ with $\kappa$ injective, $\operatorname{im}\kappa=\ker\varphi$, and $F_1=\pi_1(\operatorname{int}D^2\setminus\{q_1\},q_2)$. Since $PB_1$ is trivial by [F2], $\varphi$ has trivial codomain, so $\ker\varphi=PB_2$ and $\operatorname{im}\kappa=PB_2$: the map $\kappa$ is a group isomorphism $F_1\to PB_2$. [A1, F1, F2]

1.2 **The fibre and its meridian basis.** By [F2] the fibre group $F_1=\pi_1(\operatorname{int}D^2\setminus\{q_1\},q_2)$ is free on the single positively oriented meridian class of $q_1$, written $\gamma_1$, and by the case $n=2$ of [F3] the corresponding generator $A_{12}$ corresponds under $\kappa$ to the inverse class $\gamma_1^{-1}$, the clockwise meridian; that is, $\kappa(\gamma_1^{-1})=A_{12}$ and $\kappa(\gamma_1)=A_{12}^{-1}$. [F2, F3]

2.1 **$PB_2$ is infinite cyclic.** By [F2] and [F4], every element of the free group $F_1$ on $\{\gamma_1\}$ is uniquely $\gamma_1^k$ for $k\in\mathbb Z$. Concatenation followed by free reduction adds exponents, so $\nu:F_1\to(\mathbb Z,+)$, $\nu(\gamma_1^k)=k$, is a group isomorphism. Composing the negation automorphism $k\mapsto-k$ of $\mathbb Z$ with $\nu^{-1}$ and then with $\kappa$ of step 1.1 gives the isomorphism $\mathbb Z\to PB_2$, $k\mapsto\kappa(\gamma_1^{-k})=A_{12}^k$, by step 1.2. Thus $PB_2$ is infinite cyclic, generated by $A_{12}$, and its inverse sends $A_{12}$ to $1$, as claimed. [step 1.1, step 1.2, F2, F4]

3.1 **The winding sign and torsion-freeness.** By step 1.2 the generator $A_{12}$ corresponds under $\kappa$ to $\gamma_1^{-1}$, the clockwise meridian of $q_1$, while the counterclockwise spine-basis class is $\gamma_1$ itself; the identification of step 1.2 therefore assigns to $A_{12}$ the winding $-1$ relative to the counterclockwise basis, as claimed. The isomorphism in step 2.1 shows that $PB_2$ is infinite cyclic and generated by $A_{12}$; it is torsion-free by the general theorem [F5]. [step 1.2, step 2.1, F5]

∎
