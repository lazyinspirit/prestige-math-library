## Statement

In ZF, let $k_0$ be a specified finitely generated field over its prime field, let $C_0/k_0$ be proper of finite type and dimension at most one, and let $G_0$ be the cokernel of a specified finite diagram of $\mathbb Z/n$-modules freely generated by quasi-compact separated étale $C_0$-schemes. Let $k\subseteq K$ be specified separably closed fields containing $k_0$, with the given embeddings compatible. Then the natural map
$$\Gamma(C_{0,k},G_{0,k})\longrightarrow\Gamma(C_{0,K},G_{0,K})$$
is a bijection. The assertion is only about this finite algebraic presentation and degree zero; it does not assume that an arbitrary torsion sheaf is itself finitely presented.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The finitely generated base field, proper finite-type curve, finite generator-relation diagram, and the two specified separably closed overfields.

[F1] Every fixed ZFC axiom has a ZF proof after relativization to the constructible class $L$. [[thm-constructible-inner-model-semantic-and-formal-schema]]

[F2] Under AC, torsion étale $H^0$ on a proper finite-type scheme of dimension at most one is invariant under an extension of specified separably closed fields. This implication is used only inside $L$. [[lem-proper-curve-torsion-cohomology-finite-and-field-invariant]]

[F3] The specified finite generator-relation diagram defines a finite constructible torsion sheaf on a finite-type curve in ZF. [[lem-finite-etale-generators-on-curves-have-constructible-torsion-in-zf]]

[F4] Étale morphisms admit standard charts and are universally open in ZF. [[lem-etale-morphisms-are-locally-standard-and-universally-open-in-zf]]

[F5] Noetherian Chow under AC supplies a proper surjection with a projective graph certificate, projective properness and proper-surjection descent hold without choice, and an immersion with closed image is closed. [[lem-chow-lemma-proper-noetherian]] [[lem-projective-properness-and-finite-type-surjectivity-without-choice]] [[lem-immersion-with-closed-image]]

## Proof

**Proof technique:** transport one finite section/equality through an internal separably closed comparison and only finite separable extensions.

1.1 Fix finite generators of $k_0$ over its prime field. Their polynomial-relation ideal is finitely generated by finite Hilbert-basis induction, so an abstract copy of $k_0$ and its elements have natural-number fraction codes in $L$. Choose finite affine equations for $C_0$, the finitely many étale generators, maps, relations, inverse charts and their unit-ideal identities. These are finite lists in the common $k_0$ codes. Finite equations and field-algebra cover identities have the same truth on the common Noetherian chart rings. To establish internal properness without assuming it absolute, apply Chow [F5] inside $L$ to the separated Noetherian coded $C_0$, before assuming it internally proper. Its proper surjection $P_0\to C_0$ has a finite projective graph certificate and an immersion $P_0\to\mathbb P^m_{k_0}$. The finite certificate and surjectivity transport out by the common ideal and prime codes, and [F5] makes the external map proper. Since the supplied external $C_0$ is proper, so is $P_0$; its immersion into separated projective space is a closed immersion. That closed-immersion certificate is absolute on the common Noetherian charts, so inside $L$ the same $P_0$ is projective and proper. Properness then descends internally along $P_0\to C_0$. Thus $L$ sees the required proper curve, and [F3] is available internally as well as externally. [F1, F3, F5, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 For one specified finite-type field extension $T/k_0$ inside $k$ or $K$, the same finite-generator and relation-ideal argument gives common natural-number codes for an abstract $T$ in $L$. A specified finite family of sections of the presented sheaf over $C_T$, and finitely many equalities among them, is represented by finitely many affine standard-étale charts, local generator expressions and relation witnesses. If these data are initially given over $k$ or $K$, collect their finitely many coefficients into a finitely generated intermediate field $T$. The charts, maps and equations descend to $T$ by finite algebra. A covering assertion also descends: by [F4] its omitted locus is closed in a finite-type $T$-scheme; if nonempty, finite Noether normalization supplies a point with finite residue extension that remains nonempty after tensoring with the larger field. Thus the descended local sections glue and the descended equalities hold. This is a finite-cover calculation over fields, not a spectrum compactness assertion over arbitrary rings. [F1, F4, step 1.1, given, construct, algebra]

3.1 For surjectivity let $t\in\Gamma(C_{0,K},G_{0,K})$. By step 2.1 it descends to a section over some finitely generated $T$ with $k_0\subseteq T\subseteq K$. Work inside $L$ with the common finite codes for $k_0,T,C_0,G_0$ and the descended section. There choose a separable closure $k_0^{\mathrm s}$ and a separably closed extension $T^{\mathrm s}$ containing a compatible copy of it. Since $L$ has AC and both starting fields have specified countable codes, these algebraic closures have enumerations in $L$, hence the same natural-number codes externally. Finite-type chart rings over them are Noetherian; their ideals, prime points, finite étale charts and finite cover identities are the same inside and outside $L$. The finite generator-relation presentation and its finite matching witnesses therefore have the same meaning on both sides. Applying the actual AC implication [F2] internally, $t$ over $T^{\mathrm s}$ is the pullback of a section over $k_0^{\mathrm s}$. The internal use of [F2] assumes no external algebraic closure. [F1, F2, F3, step 2.1, given, construct]

4.1 The internal section over $k_0^{\mathrm s}$ uses finitely many algebraic coefficients and finite chart/equality witnesses, so it descends to a finite separable extension $E/k_0$. The equality with $t$ over $T^{\mathrm s}$ similarly has finite witnesses over a finite separable extension $T'/T$ containing the image of $E$. Embed this *one finite* $T'$ into the specified separably closed $K$, extending the given embedding of $T$, by choosing finitely many roots of separable polynomials. Its restricted image of $E$ lies in $k$: every element is a root of a separable polynomial over $k_0\subseteq k$, and that polynomial already splits in the specified separably closed $k$. Thus the finite-code section over $E$ gives a section over $k$ whose pullback is $t$. No infinite embedding or family of root choices is selected. [F1, F2, step 3.1, given, construct, algebra]

5.1 For injectivity, let a section over $k$ become zero over $K$. Descend the section and the finite equality witness by step 2.1 to finitely generated fields $k_0\subseteq T_0\subseteq k$ and $T_0\subseteq T_1\subseteq K$. Inside $L$ choose compatible separable closures of the finite codes. The equality over $T_1$ remains true after extending to its separable closure. Internal injectivity in [F2] makes the section zero over a separable closure of $T_0$, and the zero equality descends to one finite separable extension of $T_0$. Embed that finite extension into the given separably closed $k$; its finite equality certificate proves the original section is zero over $k$. [F1, F2, step 2.1, step 4.1, given, construct, algebra] ∎
