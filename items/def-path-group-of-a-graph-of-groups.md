## Definition

Let $\mathcal G=(X,(G_v),(G_e),(\alpha_e))$ be a graph of groups. Its
**path group** $\Pi(\mathcal G)$ is generated by:

- every vertex group $G_v$, and
- one symbol $e$ for each oriented edge,

subject to the relations

$$\bar e=e^{-1}$$

for every oriented edge $e$, together with

$$e\,\alpha_{\bar e}(a)\,\bar e=\alpha_e(a)\qquad(a\in G_e).$$

Thus traversing an edge conjugates the edge-group image at one endpoint to the
edge-group image at the other.
