## Statement

Let $X$ be a scheme on which $2$ is invertible, and let
$a\in\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X^\times)$. The sheaf of algebras generated locally
by $i,j$ with $i^2=a$, $j^2=-1$, and $ij=-ji$ is an Azumaya algebra of degree
$2$ on $X$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A scheme $X$, an invertible $2\in\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X)$, and a
unit $a\in\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X^\times)$.

[F1] An Azumaya algebra of degree $2$ is a locally free rank-four algebra
whose canonical map to its endomorphism algebra is an isomorphism.
[[def-azumaya-algebra-on-a-variety]]

[F2] The polynomial $T^2-a$ defines a finite étale faithfully flat cover:
it is monic with basis $1,s$, so the extension is finite free of positive
rank and faithfully flat. Its relative differentials are generated by $ds$
with relation $2s\,ds=0$; since $(2s)^2=4a$ is a unit, these differentials
vanish, so this finite-presentation morphism is unramified and étale.
[[def-etale-morphism-schemes]] [[def-finite-morphism-schemes]]

[F3] Faithfully flat scalar extension detects zero modules and isomorphisms.
[[thm-faithfully-flat-descent-vanishing]]

## Proof

**Proof technique:** split the quaternion algebra over the finite étale cover
obtained by adjoining a square root of $a$.

**Given:** The data above.

1.1 On an affine open $U=\operatorname{Spec}R$, the algebra is the free
$R$-module with basis $1,i,j,ij$ and multiplication determined by the three
relations. Thus it is locally free of rank four. Let
$R'=R[s]/(s^2-a)$. The basis $1,s$ and the differential calculation in [F2]
show that $R'$ is faithfully flat and étale over $R$.
[F1, F2, given]

1.2 Over $R'$, map
$$i\longmapsto\begin{pmatrix}s&0\\0&-s\end{pmatrix},\qquad
j\longmapsto\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.$$
These matrices satisfy $i^2=a$, $j^2=-1$, and $ij=-ji$. The four matrices
corresponding to $1,i,j,ij$ form a basis of $M_2(R')$: the diagonal and
off-diagonal coefficient determinants are units $2s$. Hence the algebra
becomes $M_2(R')$. For matrix units, the canonical map in [F1] sends
$E_{ij}\otimes E_{\ell m}^{\mathrm{op}}$ to the elementary endomorphism
carrying $E_{j\ell}$ to $E_{im}$ and every other matrix unit to zero. These
sixteen maps form a basis of the endomorphism module, so the canonical map
is an isomorphism. By [F3], faithful flatness detects isomorphisms of finite locally
free modules, so it was already an isomorphism on $U$. Since such opens cover
$X$, the algebra is Azumaya of degree $2$. [F1, F2, F3, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
