## Statement

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $U$ be a scheme, let $p:X\to U$ be a smooth separated morphism of pure relative dimension one, and let $s:U\to X$ be a section. Then $s$ is a closed immersion whose ideal sheaf is locally generated by a nonzerodivisor. Equivalently, its image is an effective Cartier divisor on $X$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC; $U,p:X\to U,s:U\to X$ as in the Statement.

[A1] AC is the stated axiom. [[def-axiom-of-choice]]

[F1] Near any point of a smooth relative-dimension-one morphism, there is an étale map from the source to the relative affine line over an open of the base. [[thm-smooth-local-standard-form]]

[F2] Étale morphisms are flat and stable under base change; their diagonals are open immersions. [[def-etale-morphism-schemes]] [[lem-etale-stable-base-change-composition]] [[thm-unramified-diagonal-open-immersion]]

[F3] A section of a separated morphism is a closed immersion because it is a base change of the closed diagonal. [[def-separated-morphism-schemes]] [[lem-closed-immersion-affine-quotient-and-base-change]]

[F4] An effective Cartier divisor is locally defined by one nonzerodivisor. [[def-cartier-divisor]] [[def-effective-cartier-divisor]]

## Proof
1.1 Fix $u\in U$ and put $x=s(u)$. Shrink around $u$ and $x$ so that [F1] gives an étale map $h:V\to\mathbb A^1_W$ over an open $W\subseteq U$, with $u\in W$ and $s(W)\subseteq V$. The composite $h\circ s:W\to\mathbb A^1_W$ is a section, hence is the graph of one function $a\in\mathcal O_W(W)$; its ideal in $\mathcal O_W[t]$ is $(t-a)$. Multiplication by $t-a$ is injective: for any nonzero polynomial, its leading coefficient remains the leading coefficient of its product with the monic polynomial $t-a$. Thus this graph is an effective Cartier divisor. [A1, F1, F4, given, choose]

2.1 Base change the graph along the flat étale map $h$. By [F2], its pullback $V'=V\times_{\mathbb A^1_W}W\to V$ is still an effective Cartier divisor: tensoring the injective multiplication map by a flat algebra preserves injectivity. The induced map $V'\to W$ is étale, and $s|_W:W\to V'$ is its section, hence an open immersion by [F2]. The image of this section is also closed in $V'$ because $s:W\to V$ is closed by [F3]. Choose an open neighbourhood $V_0\subseteq V$ of $x$ whose intersection with $V'$ is contained in this open section image; after restricting to the corresponding open $W_0\subseteq W$, one has $V_0\cap V'=s(W_0)$. Thus the effective Cartier divisor $V'$ restricts on $V_0$ to the image of $s$, with the same local nonzerodivisor equation. Hence $s(U)$ is an effective Cartier divisor near $x$. [A1, F2, F3, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 Since $u$ was arbitrary, Step 2.1 proves that the section is locally a closed immersion cut out by one nonzerodivisor everywhere. The global map is a closed immersion by [F3], and [F4] identifies its image as an effective Cartier divisor. [A1, F3, F4, step 2.1] ∎
