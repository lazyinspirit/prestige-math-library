## Statement

Assume AC and DC. If $q:Y\to S$ is proper of finite type, $S$ is Noetherian, and $A$ is an invertible sheaf relatively ample for $q$, then $Y$ admits a closed immersion into $\mathbb P_S(E)=\operatorname{Proj}_S\operatorname{Sym}E$ for some coherent $E$ on $S$. This is projectivity in the coherent-projective-bundle sense. If $A^k$ has a finite list of global sections giving a closed immersion into $\mathbb P^N_S$, the stronger H-projectivity conclusion holds. The first conclusion alone does not assert such a finite list.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The hypotheses in the statement and AC and DC, inherited from the scheme, cohomology, and finite-module suppliers ([[def-axiom-of-choice]], [[def-dependent-choice]]).

[F1] Proper direct images of coherent sheaves over a Noetherian base are coherent ([[thm-proper-pushforward-coherent]]). On a Noetherian proper scheme over an affine base, sufficiently high powers of an ample invertible sheaf define closed projective-space embeddings ([[thm-ample-powers-very-ample-proper-base]]). Relative Proj commutes with base change ([[thm-relative-proj-base-change]]).

## Proof

1.1 Cover $S$ by finitely many affine opens. Relative ampleness says $A$ is ample over each such affine base. By [F1], choose powers giving closed projective-space embeddings on each open; take a common positive multiple of the finitely many exponents and use the Veronese monomials of the corresponding generating sections to obtain one power $A^k$ that gives a closed immersion on every open. Set $E=q_*A^k$, coherent by [F1]. The evaluation $q^*E\to A^k$ is onto on this cover, since its local sections include every member of these generating systems. It therefore defines $Y\to\mathbb P_S(E)$. [F1, construct]

2.1 This map is a closed immersion. Locally choose one of the generating sections $s$ nonzero. The projective chart for $s$ has coordinate algebra generated by all ratios $t/s$ with $t$ a local section of $E$. Already the finite ratios coming from the selected local projective embedding generate the coordinate algebra of $Y_s$ as a quotient; adding the other ratios preserves surjectivity onto that algebra. Thus on these charts the map is a closed immersion. They cover $Y$. The image is closed in the whole projective bundle because $Y\to S$ is proper and the projective bundle is separated over $S$; the graph is closed and its projection is a base change of the proper map. Hence the chart immersions give a global closed immersion. The last assertion is exactly the displayed extra global-section hypothesis. [F1, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
