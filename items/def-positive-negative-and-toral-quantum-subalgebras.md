## Statement

Let $U_q(\mathfrak g)$ be the Drinfeld–Jimbo algebra of [[def-drinfeld-jimbo-quantized-enveloping-algebra]] with its root-lattice grading $U_q(\mathfrak g)=\bigoplus_{\beta\in Q}U_q(\mathfrak g)[\beta]$. Define

$$U_q^+:=\langle E_i\ :\ i\in I\rangle,\qquad U_q^-:=\langle F_i\ :\ i\in I\rangle,\qquad U_q^0:=\langle K_h\ :\ h\in P^{\vee}\rangle,$$

the $\mathbb Q(q)$-subalgebras generated by the indicated images, and let $Q_+=\bigoplus_i\mathbb Z_{\ge0}\alpha_i$. Then

$$U_q^\pm=\bigoplus_{\beta\in Q_+}U_q^\pm[\pm\beta],\qquad U_q^+[\beta]:=U_q(\mathfrak g)[\beta]\cap U_q^+,\quad U_q^-[ -\beta]:=U_q(\mathfrak g)[-\beta]\cap U_q^-.$$

The degree-zero component $U_q(\mathfrak g)[0]$ contains $U_q^0$, and for every $x\in U_q(\mathfrak g)[\beta]$ and $h\in P^{\vee}$,

$$K_hxK_h^{-1}=q^{\beta(h)}x.$$

Thus each $K_h\in U_q^0$ acts by conjugation on the degree-$\beta$ component as the scalar $q^{\beta(h)}$. The elements $K_h$ span $U_q^0$ and satisfy $K_hK_{h'}=K_{h+h'}$ with $K_0=1$; hence $U_q^0$ is a quotient of the group algebra $\mathbb Q(q)[P^{\vee}]$. The subalgebras $U_q^\pm$ are spanned by finite monomials in the $E_i$ and $F_i$, respectively. Define the nonnegative and nonpositive product spans by

$$U_q^{\ge0}:=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb Q(q)}(U_q^0U_q^+),\qquad U_q^{\le0}:=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb Q(q)}(U_q^-U_q^0).$$

This definition asserts no linear independence of these spanning families and no injectivity of the natural maps; those claims belong to [[thm-triangular-decomposition-of-a-quantized-enveloping-algebra]].

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The Drinfeld–Jimbo presentation is homogeneous in the root lattice, with the degrees of $E_i,F_i,K_h$ specified in [[def-drinfeld-jimbo-quantized-enveloping-algebra]]. The datum provides the pairing of roots and coroots in [[def-symmetrizable-cartan-datum-for-a-quantum-group]].

[F1] $U_q(\mathfrak g)=\bigoplus_{\beta\in Q}U_q(\mathfrak g)[\beta]$ with $\deg E_i=\alpha_i$, $\deg F_i=-\alpha_i$, and $\deg K_h=0$ ([[def-drinfeld-jimbo-quantized-enveloping-algebra]]).

[F2] $K_hE_iK_h^{-1}=q^{\langle\alpha_i,h\rangle}E_i$ and $K_hF_iK_h^{-1}=q^{-\langle\alpha_i,h\rangle}F_i$ ([[def-drinfeld-jimbo-quantized-enveloping-algebra]]).

[F3] $K_0=1$ and $K_hK_{h'}=K_{h+h'}$ ([[def-drinfeld-jimbo-quantized-enveloping-algebra]]).

[F4] For a ring homomorphism $f:R\to S$, the first isomorphism theorem gives $R/\ker f\cong\operatorname{im}f$ ([[thm-first-isomorphism-theorem-rings]]).

[F5] The root lattice is $Q=\bigoplus_i\mathbb Z\alpha_i$, and its pairing with $P^{\vee}$ is integer-valued and additive ([[def-symmetrizable-cartan-datum-for-a-quantum-group]]).

## Proof

**Proof technique:** Use the root grading on finite generator words, and map the group algebra of the torus lattice onto the generated toral subalgebra.

1.1 Every word in the $E_i$ is homogeneous of degree in $Q_+$; the empty word has degree $0$. By generation, every element of $U_q^+$ is a finite sum of such words. Grouping those terms by degree and using the direct grading of $U_q(\mathfrak g)$ gives $U_q^+=\bigoplus_{\beta\in Q_+}(U_q(\mathfrak g)[\beta]\cap U_q^+)$. [F1, given, algebra]

1.2 Every word in the $F_i$ is homogeneous of degree in $-Q_+$, including the degree-zero empty word. Grouping finite sums by their degrees in the direct grading gives $U_q^-=\bigoplus_{\beta\in Q_+}(U_q(\mathfrak g)[-\beta]\cap U_q^-)$. [F1, given, algebra]

1.3 Define $\mathbb Q(q)[P^{\vee}]$ to be the vector space of finite sums $\sum_h c_hX_h$ with multiplication $X_hX_{h'}=X_{h+h'}$. By [F3], $\phi(X_h)=K_h$ extends linearly and multiplicatively to a unital algebra homomorphism into $U_q^0$. Its image is all of $U_q^0$, since the image contains every generator $K_h$ and consists of finite linear combinations of them. Thus [F4] identifies $U_q^0$ with $\mathbb Q(q)[P^{\vee}]/\ker\phi$, and the $K_h$ span it. [F3, F4, construct]

1.4 The relations in [F3] give $K_h^{-1}=K_{-h}$. By [F2], conjugation by $K_h$ acts on a word of degree $\beta$ by multiplying each $E_i$ factor by $q^{\alpha_i(h)}$, each $F_i$ factor by $q^{-\alpha_i(h)}$, and each $K_{h'}$ factor by $1$. Additivity of the pairing in [F5] makes the product scalar $q^{\beta(h)}$. Every element is a finite sum of generator-word images; grouping those words by degree and using the direct grading [F1] shows that an element of $U_q(\mathfrak g)[\beta]$ is a finite linear combination of degree-$\beta$ word images. Thus the formula holds for every such $x$. Since the $K_h$ have degree zero, their generated subalgebra $U_q^0$ lies in $U_q(\mathfrak g)[0]$. [F1, F2, F3, F5, algebra]

2.1 By definition, finite monomials in the $E_i$ and $F_i$ span $U_q^+$ and $U_q^-$. Finite sums of products $uv$ with $u\in U_q^0,v\in U_q^+$ and with $u\in U_q^-,v\in U_q^0$ span the two stated product subspaces. No basis or independence assertion is involved. [given, algebra] ∎
