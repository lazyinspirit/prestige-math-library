## Statement refuted

Assume the Axiom of Choice. On every bounded connected Lipschitz polygon or polyhedron $D\subset\mathbb R^d$, $d\in\{2,3\}$, let the actual conforming affine MINI spaces be shape regular, with arbitrary grading and global maximum diameter $H$. Let $\nu,T>0$, $u\in W^{1,\infty}(0,T;H^2(D)^d\cap V_\sigma)$, $p\in W^{1,\infty}(0,T;H^1(D)\cap L^2_0(D))$, and $f\in L^2(0,T;L^2(D)^d)$ solve the no-slip Navier–Stokes equations. Initialize backward Euler by the actual weak-kernel orthogonal mass projection $P_{Z_h}u(0)$. For arbitrary positive steps subject only to the smooth velocity theorem's upper-step condition $\tau_{\max}\Lambda\le1/2$, every nonlinear solution root is asserted to satisfy
$$\left(\sum_n\tau_n\|p(t_n)-p_h^n\|_2^{4/3}\right)^{3/4}\le C_*\bigl(HM+\tau_{\max}M^2\bigr),$$
Here $M=1+\|u\|_{W^{1,\infty}H^2}+\|p\|_{W^{1,\infty}H^1}$, $M_u=\|u\|_{L^\infty V}$ and $\Lambda=1+C_D\nu^{-3}M_u^4$. The approved data quantities and constant are
$$M_\infty=(\|u_0\|_2^2+C_P(D)^2\nu^{-1}\|f\|_{L^2L^2}^2)^{1/2},\qquad M_E=\nu^{-1/2}\|u_0\|_2+C_P(D)\nu^{-1}\|f\|_{L^2L^2},$$
$$C_*=C_{D,\mathrm{shape}}(1+\nu)^2(1+\nu^{-4})(1+T)^2(1+M_\infty+M_E+M)^4\exp(2T\Lambda),$$
with $C_{D,\mathrm{shape}}$ independent of the smallest element, all steps, their ratios and $\nu$. The construction below refutes even any finite bound depending only on these fixed domain, shape, viscosity and uniformly bounded data quantities. There is no small-$H$ threshold or mesh–time coupling.

This assertion is false. There are one fixed convex two-dimensional pentagon, fixed $\nu,T>0$, and one family of genuine conforming MINI meshes with a fixed finite shape-regularity bound, together with uniformly bounded data in exactly these smooth classes, for which legitimate backward Euler roots with the required initialization and arbitrarily small admissible steps have unbounded instantaneous pressure norm. The exact pressures are zero. The positive velocity estimate and the pressure-primitive estimates are compatible with this example.
