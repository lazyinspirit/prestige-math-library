## Definition

A left $R$-module $M$ is **Artinian** when every descending chain $M_0\supseteq M_1\supseteq\cdots$ of submodules stabilizes: there is $N$ such that $M_n=M_N$ for all $n\ge N$. This is the descending chain condition.
