## Example

For $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)$, the subring $A=\mathbb Z[\sqrt5]$ is an order in
$\mathcal O_K=\mathbb Z[(1+\sqrt5)/2]$. It has index $2$ and
$\operatorname{disc}(A)=20=2^2\cdot5$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)$ and $A=\mathbb Z[\sqrt5]$.

[F1] An order is a unital full-rank subring of $\mathcal O_K$ ([[def-order-in-a-number-field]]).

[F2] The integral basis $(1,\omega)$ with $\omega=(1+\sqrt5)/2$ has field discriminant $5$ ([[ex-ring-of-integers-of-q-sqrt-five]]).

[F3] The order-index discriminant formula applies to $A\subseteq\mathcal O_K$ ([[cor-order-index-discriminant-formula]]).

## Verification

**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Since $\sqrt5=2\omega-1$, $A$ is the subring generated by an integral element and has full-rank basis $(1,\sqrt5)$; hence [F1] makes it an order. [F1, F2, given]

2.1 Relative to $(1,\omega)$, the basis $(1,\sqrt5)$ has determinant $2$. Thus $[\mathcal O_K:A]=2$, and [F3] with [F2] gives $\operatorname{disc}(A)=2^2\cdot5=20$. [F2, F3, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
