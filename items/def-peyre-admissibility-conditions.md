## Definition

Let $K$ be a number field and $X/K$ a smooth Fano variety of dimension $d\ge1$. In this scaffold, $X$ satisfies the **Peyre almost-Fano admissibility conditions** if all of the following hold: (i) $H^1(X_{\overline K},\mathcal O_{X_{\overline K}})=H^2(X_{\overline K},\mathcal O_{X_{\overline K}})=0$, where $H^i$ denotes sheaf cohomology; (ii) $\operatorname{Pic}(X)$ and $\operatorname{Pic}(X_{\overline K})$ are free abelian groups of finite rank, with $\rho(X):=\operatorname{rank}\operatorname{Pic}(X)\ge1$; (iii) the closed effective cone $C_{\mathrm{eff}}(X)$ spans $V:=\operatorname{Pic}(X)\otimes\mathbb R$, is pointed (its intersection with its negative is $\{0\}$), and is generated by finitely many effective divisor classes; its dual cone is full-dimensional, and $-K_X\in\operatorname{int}(C_{\mathrm{eff}}(X))\subset V$; and (iv) the cohomological Brauer group $\operatorname{Br}(X_{\overline K}):=H^2_{\acute{e}t}(X_{\overline K},\mathbb G_m)_{\mathrm{tors}}$ is zero, with $H^2_{\acute{e}t}$ denoting étale sheaf cohomology, $\mathbb G_m$ the étale sheaf $U\mapsto\Gamma(U,\mathcal O_U)^\times$, and the subscript denoting the torsion subgroup. This is an explicit set of sufficient scope hypotheses for the factors defined on this page; no unproved general equivalence between these hypotheses and other definitions of “almost Fano” is asserted.
