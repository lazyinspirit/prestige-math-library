## Definition

Assume the Axiom of Choice. Let $\mathfrak g$ be a finite-dimensional complex
semisimple Lie algebra with Cartan subalgebra $\mathfrak h$ and a chosen
positive system with simple roots $\alpha_1,\dots,\alpha_r$, let
$\mathfrak n^+$ be the span of the positive root spaces
([[def-positive-and-negative-nilpotent-subalgebras-and-borel-subalgebra]]),
let $\lambda$ be dominant integral with
$m_i=\langle\lambda,\alpha_i^\vee\rangle\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}$
([[def-integral-dominant-and-strictly-dominant-weights]]), and for each $i$
choose $e_i\in\mathfrak g_{\alpha_i}$, $f_i\in\mathfrak g_{-\alpha_i}$ with
$[e_i,f_i]=h_{\alpha_i}$ ([[thm-root-sl-two-triple]]).

In the universal enveloping algebra $U(\mathfrak g)$
([[def-universal-enveloping-algebra]]) let $I_\lambda$ be the left ideal
([[def-generated-and-principal-ideals]]) generated by the union of the three
sets
$$\mathfrak n^+,\qquad \{H-\lambda(H):H\in\mathfrak h\},\qquad \{f_i^{\,m_i+1}:i=1,\dots,r\}.$$
Define
$$M_{\mathrm{int}}(\lambda):=U(\mathfrak g)/I_\lambda ,$$
the quotient being taken as left $U(\mathfrak g)$-modules, and write
$$v_\lambda:=1+I_\lambda\in M_{\mathrm{int}}(\lambda)$$
for the class of the unit. Then $M_{\mathrm{int}}(\lambda)$ is called the
**dominant integrable cyclic highest weight module** of highest weight
$\lambda$, and $v_\lambda$ its **canonical generator**.

**Well-definedness and the defining relations.** The left ideal generated by
a set is the smallest left ideal containing it, so $I_\lambda$ is well defined
as a linear subspace of $U(\mathfrak g)$, and the quotient carries a left
$U(\mathfrak g)$-module structure because $I_\lambda$ is a left ideal. The
class $v_\lambda$ satisfies
$$x\cdot v_\lambda=0\ (x\in\mathfrak n^+),\qquad H\cdot v_\lambda=\lambda(H)v_\lambda\ (H\in\mathfrak h),\qquad f_i^{\,m_i+1}\cdot v_\lambda=0 ,$$
because the corresponding elements of $U(\mathfrak g)$ lie in $I_\lambda$.
The module is generated by $v_\lambda$, since the class of $1$ generates
$U(\mathfrak g)/I_\lambda$ under left multiplication.

Only the vectors $f_i$ were chosen, and they are determined by $\alpha_i$ up
to a nonzero scalar: if $f_i$ is replaced by $cf_i$ with $c\in\mathbb C^\times$
and $e_i$ correspondingly by $c^{-1}e_i$, then the generator
$f_i^{\,m_i+1}$ is replaced by the nonzero scalar multiple
$c^{m_i+1}f_i^{\,m_i+1}$, which generates the same left ideal. Hence
$I_\lambda$, and with it $M_{\mathrm{int}}(\lambda)$, does not depend on these
choices.
