## Statement

Let $(\mathcal I,E,\mu)$ be a marked ideal of maximal order on the smooth $K$-scheme $X$, let $C\subsetneq\operatorname{supp}(\mathcal I,E,\mu)$ be a regular center with SNC with $E$, and let $u\in T(\mathcal I)(U)=\mathcal D^{\mu-1}(\mathcal I)(U)$ be a tangent direction of multiplicity one on an open $U\subseteq X$ ([[def-maximal-order-and-tangent-directions]]).
Let $\sigma\colon X'\to X$ be the blowup of $C$ with exceptional divisor $D$, and on an open $U'\subseteq\sigma^{-1}(U)$ where $D$ has local equation $y$ put $u':=y^{-1}\sigma^*(u)$.
Then:
(1) $u'\in\mathcal D^{\mu-1}\bigl(\sigma^{\mathrm{c}}(\mathcal I|_{U},\mu)|_{U'}\bigr)$, i.e. $u'$ is a tangent direction of the controlled transform;
(2) $u'$ is of multiplicity one on $U'$;
(3) $V(u')$ is the strict transform of $V(u)$ restricted to $U'$ ([[def-strict-transform-closed-subscheme]]).

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A marked ideal $(\mathcal I,E,\mu)$ of maximal order on the smooth $K$-scheme $X$, a regular center $C\subsetneq\operatorname{supp}(\mathcal I,E,\mu)$ with SNC with $E$, a tangent direction $u\in T(\mathcal I)(U)=\mathcal D^{\mu-1}(\mathcal I)(U)$ of multiplicity one on an open $U\subseteq X$, the blowup $\sigma\colon X'\to X$ with exceptional divisor $D$, and an open $U'\subseteq\sigma^{-1}(U)$ on which $D$ has local equation $y$, with $u'=y^{-1}\sigma^*(u)$.

[F1] [[def-maximal-order-and-tangent-directions]], [[lem-derivative-ideals-have-the-same-support]]: $T(\mathcal I)=\mathcal D^{\mu-1}(\mathcal I)$, and the all-characteristic inclusion $\operatorname{supp}(\mathcal I,\mu)\subseteq\operatorname{supp}(\mathcal D^{\mu-1}(\mathcal I),1)$ implies every section of $T(\mathcal I)$ vanishes on the support; a tangent direction of multiplicity one is a section $u\in T(\mathcal I)(U)$ with $\operatorname{ord}_x(u)=1$ for every $x\in V(u)$.

[F2] [[lem-derivatives-commute-with-controlled-transform]]: for $0\le r\le\mu$, $\sigma^{\mathrm c}(\mathcal D^r(\mathcal I),\mu-r)\subseteq\mathcal D^r(\sigma^{\mathrm c}(\mathcal I,\mu))$.

[F3] [[def-multiple-test-blowup-and-controlled-transform]], [[def-exceptional-divisor-blowup]]: a controlled transform is computed by $f\mapsto y^{-(\text{exponent})}\sigma^*(f)$; the exceptional divisor $D$ has invertible ideal generated by $y$.

[F4] [[def-order-of-an-ideal-sheaf-at-a-point]]: multiplicity one at $x$ means $\operatorname{ord}_x(u)=1$, i.e. $u\in\mathfrak m_x\smallsetminus\mathfrak m_x^2$; in a regular local ring this means that $u$ may be taken as the first member of a regular system of parameters.

[F5] [[def-strict-transform-closed-subscheme]], [[def-blowup-scheme-along-ideal]]: the strict transform of $V(u)$ is cut out on $U'$ by the saturation $(\sigma^*(u):y^\infty)$; since $u\in\mathcal I_C$ (it vanishes on $C\subseteq V(u)$) and $\operatorname{ord}$ considerations show $y$ divides $\sigma^*(u)$ exactly once in suitable charts, $(\sigma^*(u):y^\infty)=(\sigma^*(u)/y)=(u')$.

## Proof

1.1 $u'$ is a tangent direction of the controlled transform. By [F3] the section $u'=y^{-1}\sigma^*(u)$ is the controlled transform of the section $u$ of the marked ideal $(\mathcal D^{\mu-1}(\mathcal I),1)$; since the generator $u$ lies in $\mathcal D^{\mu-1}(\mathcal I)(U)$ and the controlled transform of a generated ideal is generated by the controlled transforms of generators, $u'\in\sigma^{\mathrm c}(\mathcal D^{\mu-1}(\mathcal I),1)(U')$. By [F2] with $r=\mu-1$ and the hypothesis $C\subseteq\operatorname{supp}(\mathcal I,\mu)$, this ideal is contained in $\mathcal D^{\mu-1}(\sigma^{\mathrm c}(\mathcal I,\mu))(U')$; hence $u'$ is a tangent direction of the controlled transform, which is (1). [F1, F2, F3]

1.2 Multiplicity one. Away from $D$, the map is an isomorphism and $y$ is a unit, so a zero of $u'$ has the same order as the corresponding zero of $u$, namely one. At a zero of $u'$ on $D$, its image $x$ lies in $C$. By [F1], $C\subseteq V(u)$, and the class of $u$ in $\mathfrak m_x/\mathfrak m_x^2$ is nonzero by [F4]; hence $u$ can be chosen as one of the regular parameters generating $\mathcal I_C$. Since $u'=0$, the point is on a blowup chart whose exceptional parameter $y$ is a different normal parameter. There $u'=u/y$ is a chart coordinate, so [F4] gives order one. Thus $u'$ has multiplicity one on $U'$, which is assertion (2). [F1, F4, F5]

2.1 The zero locus is the strict transform. By [F5] the strict transform of $V(u)$ on $U'$ is cut out by $(\sigma^*(u):y^\infty)=(u')$; hence $V(u')$ is the strict transform of $V(u)$ restricted to $U'$, which is assertion (3). [F5, step 1.2] ∎
