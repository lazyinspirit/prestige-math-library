## Statement

Assume AC. Let $F/\mathbb Q_p$ be a GIVEN valued algebraic closure, $C=\widehat F$ its ordinary completion, a compatible primitive cyclotomic system be GIVEN, and $K\subset F$ be finite with maximal unramified subfield $K_0$. Then the natural maps
$$K\otimes_{K_0}B_{\mathrm{cris}}\longrightarrow B_{\mathrm{dR}},\qquad K\otimes_{K_0}B_{\mathrm{st}}\longrightarrow B_{\mathrm{dR}}$$
are injective, the second map using the normalized semistable comparison branch. Moreover $\operatorname{Frac}(B_{\mathrm{cris}})^{G_K}=B_{\mathrm{cris}}^{G_K}=K_0$. This assertion does not supply the corresponding unqualified construction or period-ring regularity.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** AC, the valued algebraic closure, its completion, the compatible primitive cyclotomic system, and the finite extension $K/\mathbb Q_p$.

[A1] AC supplies compatible root towers. [[def-axiom-of-choice]]

[F1] The tilt $R$ is a valuation domain with $v_R(x)=v(x^\sharp)$, and the Fontaine kernel is generated by an element whose reduction has tilt valuation one. [[lem-cyclotomic-tilt-valuation-and-fontaine-principal-kernel]]

[F2] Actual compatible root tuples define tilt elements with the first entry as sharp. [[lem-fontaine-map-from-finite-witt-ghost-congruences]]

[F3] The Fontaine map $W(R)\to\mathcal O_C$ is surjective. [[lem-choice-free-fontaine-map-surjectivity-for-the-padic-completion]]

[F4] The Witt ring is $p$-adically complete and separated, $p$ is regular, and reduction mod $p$ is $R$. [[lem-perfect-base-witt-frobenius-and-p-adic-completeness]]

[F5] $B_{\mathrm{dR}}^+$ is the completed DVR of the Fontaine kernel and has residue $C$. [[lem-choice-free-de-rham-period-completion-is-a-discrete-valuation-ring]]

[F6] The given valued closure embeds canonically in $B_{\mathrm{dR}}^+$ with its prescribed residue. [[lem-choice-free-algebraic-coefficients-and-logarithm-branch-in-de-rham-periods]]

[F7] The completed crystalline ring embeds into $B_{\mathrm{dR}}^+$ by its finite-quotient completion map. [[lem-choice-free-completed-crystalline-embedding-in-the-de-rham-ring]]

[F8] Finite valuation rings are finite free over their subfields' valuation rings; a totally ramified extension is generated integrally by a uniformizer with Eisenstein polynomial. [[lem-choice-free-finite-padic-different-and-base-change-calculus]]

[F9] $B_{\mathrm{st}}$ is polynomial over $B_{\mathrm{cris}}$ in one valuation coordinate. [[lem-choice-free-crystalline-logarithm-and-abstract-semistable-period-ring]]

[F10] Under AC, $B_{\mathrm{dR}}^{G_K}=K$. [[lem-cyclotomic-tate-twists-and-de-rham-invariants-under-choice]]

[F11] Under AC, the normalized unextended semistable comparison is injective. [[lem-semistable-comparison-injection-under-choice]]

[F12] The normalized semistable comparison is a $B_{\mathrm{cris}}$-algebra homomorphism. [[lem-choice-free-normalized-semistable-comparison-homomorphism]]

## Proof

1.1 Put $W=W(R)$, $e=[K:K_0]$ and choose a uniformizer $\pi$ with $\pi^e=pu$, $u\in\mathcal O_K^\times$. By [A1] choose actual compatible towers $\widetilde p,\widetilde\pi\in R$ with sharps $p,\pi$. Put $\eta=[\widetilde\pi]$, $P=[\widetilde p]$, $B=\mathcal O_K\otimes_{W(k)}W$, $z=\eta/\pi$, $s=P/p$, and $D=B[z]\subset B[1/p]$. Here $W(k)\hookrightarrow W$ is the canonical residue-constant embedding, $B$ is finite free over $W$, and $B/\pi B\cong R$. Write $A=\widehat D$ for the $\pi$-adic completion; the $p$- and $\pi$-adic topologies agree. [A1, F1, F2, F4, F8, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 The natural map $B[Z]/(\pi Z-\eta)\to D$ is an isomorphism: modulo $\pi$, $\eta$ is the nonzero element $\widetilde\pi$ of the domain $R$, so the relation ideal is $\pi$-saturated by reduction and cancellation. Thus $A/\pi A\cong(R/\widetilde\pi R)[Z]$. Set $w=1-z$. The monic polynomial $1-Z$ is regular modulo $\pi$, so $wA$ is $\pi$-saturated and closed. The resulting quotient $A/wA$ is the $\pi$-adic completion of $B/(\pi-\eta)$. [F1, F4, step 1.1, algebra]

3.1 The map $B\to\mathcal O_C$ induced by [F3] and [F6] has modulo $\pi$ kernel $\widetilde\pi R$: this is exactly the set of tilt elements whose sharp has valuation at least $v(\pi)$. It is surjective. Thus $B/(\pi-\eta)\to\mathcal O_C$ is an isomorphism modulo $\pi$. Its source is $\pi$-adically complete and separated: $(\pi-\eta)B$ is $\pi$-saturated since $\widetilde\pi$ is regular in $R$, hence closed by successive division. Passing through the complete quotients proves $A/wA\cong\mathcal O_C$. Let $g(X)$ be the Eisenstein polynomial of $\pi$ over $K_0$. The reduction of $g(\eta)$ mod $p$ is $\widetilde\pi^e$, of tilt valuation one, and $\theta(g(\eta))=0$. Therefore $g(\eta)$ is a unit times the Fontaine kernel generator. In $B$, factor $g(\eta)=(\eta-\pi)q(\eta)$; the residue of $q(\eta)$ is $g'(\pi)\ne0$. Hence $w$ is a uniformizer of $B_{\mathrm{dR}}^+$. [F1, F3, F4, F5, F6, F8, step 2.1, algebra]

4.1 For each $n$, $B/(\pi-\eta)^n$ is $\pi$-adically complete and separated, since the reduction of $(\pi-\eta)^n$ is the non-zero-divisor $(-\widetilde\pi)^n$. The map to $B_{\mathrm{dR}}^+/w^n$ gives an isomorphism after inverting $p$: factor the Eisenstein equation at $\eta$, use that the cofactor has nonzero residue, and apply finite nilpotent Hensel lifting to identify the unique root reducing to $\pi$. The quotient is then the already constructed $W[1/p]/\xi^n$. Modulo $w^n$, substitute $z=1-w$: the image of $D$ lies in the fixed complete module $\pi^{-(n-1)}B/(\pi-\eta)^n$. Therefore evaluation extends to $h:A\to B_{\mathrm{dR}}^+$. Since $A/wA\cong\mathcal O_C$, induction gives $h^{-1}(w^nB_{\mathrm{dR}}^+)=w^nA$. [F4, F5, F6, step 2.1, step 3.1, construct, algebra]

5.1 If $x\in\bigcap_nw^nA$, its image in $(R/\widetilde\pi)[Z]$ is divisible by every power of $1-Z$, hence zero by bounded polynomial degree. Thus $x=\pi x_1$; regularity of $1-Z$ modulo $\pi$ gives $x_1\in\bigcap_nw^nA$. Iterating proves $x\in\bigcap_m\pi^mA=0$, so $h$ is injective. [F4, step 2.1, step 4.1, algebra]

6.1 Put $A_{\max}=\widehat{W[s]}_p$. The tilt element $\widetilde u=\widetilde\pi^e/\widetilde p$ is a unit, so $\alpha=[\widetilde u]/u\in B^\times$ and $z^e=\alpha s$. For $j=eq+r$, $0\le r<e$, one has $z^j=p^{-1}\alpha^q s^q(p/\pi^r)\eta^r$. Hence $B[s]\subset D\subset p^{-1}B[s]$ and the two $p$-adic topologies are equivalent. Since $B$ is finite free over $W$, completion gives $K\otimes_{K_0}A_{\max}[1/p]\cong A[1/p]$. Taking $K=K_0=\mathbb Q_p$ in the preceding construction embeds $A_{\max}$ in $B_{\mathrm{dR}}^+$. For $\xi=P-p$, [F1] and $v_R(\widetilde p)=1$ show that $\xi$ is a unit times the cyclotomic kernel generator, so its divided-power algebra is the one in [F7]. Every $\xi^n/n!$ lies in $W[s]$, since $p^n/n!\in\mathbb Z_p$. The resulting completed map $A_{\mathrm{cris}}\to A_{\max}$ followed by the de Rham embedding agrees with [F7] on every finite quotient and is therefore injective. Finite flatness and inversion of the nonzero $t$ prove $K\otimes_{K_0}B_{\mathrm{cris}}\hookrightarrow B_{\mathrm{dR}}$. [F1, F4, F7, F8, step 1.1, step 5.1, algebra]

7.1 By [F9], $B_{\mathrm{st}}$ is a polynomial ring in one valuation coordinate. Its normalized comparison image is transcendental over $E=\operatorname{Frac}(B_{\mathrm{cris}})$ by [F11]–[F12]. Step 6.1 identifies $K\otimes_{K_0}E$ with a finite extension field of $E$, over which that image remains transcendental. Polynomial evaluation gives the semistable tensor injection. Finally this injection on crystalline fractions embeds $K\otimes_{K_0}E^{G_K}$ into $B_{\mathrm{dR}}^{G_K}=K$ by [F10], so the $K_0$-dimension of $E^{G_K}$ is at most one. Since it contains $K_0$, it equals $K_0$, as does $B_{\mathrm{cris}}^{G_K}$. [F9, F10, F11, F12, step 6.1, algebra] ∎
