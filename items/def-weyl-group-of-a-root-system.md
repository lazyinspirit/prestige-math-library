## Definition

Let $\Phi\subseteq E$ be a reduced crystallographic root system
([[def-reduced-crystallographic-euclidean-root-system]]) with coroots
$\alpha^{\vee}=2\alpha/(\alpha,\alpha)$ ([[def-coroot-and-dual-root-system]]).
For $\alpha\in\Phi$ the associated reflection is
$$s_\alpha(x)=x-(x,\alpha^{\vee})\,\alpha =x-\frac{2(x,\alpha)}{(\alpha,\alpha)}\,\alpha,$$
which is an orthogonal transformation of $E$ with $s_\alpha(\alpha)=-\alpha$
and $s_\alpha(x)=x$ for $(x,\alpha)=0$. The **Weyl group** of $\Phi$ is the
subgroup
$$W(\Phi)=\langle s_\alpha:\alpha\in\Phi\rangle\le O(E)$$
of the orthogonal group generated by all root reflections.

Since $s_\alpha(\Phi)=\Phi$ for every $\alpha\in\Phi$ by the root-system
axioms, every element of $W(\Phi)$ permutes $\Phi$. The Weyl group depends on
$\Phi$ and its inner product; rescaling the inner product does not change it,
because the reflections $s_\alpha$ are unchanged.
