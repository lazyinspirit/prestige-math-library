## Statement

Assume AC. Let $k$ be an infinite field and $X$ a quasi-projective finite-type $k$-scheme of dimension $d$, with $L=\mathcal O(1)|_X$ for a fixed quasi-projective embedding. Let $\mathcal H$ be coherent and locally free on a quasi-compact open $U\subseteq X$. Let $C$ be a closed cone in the vector bundle of $\mathcal H|_U$ whose geometric fibres have codimension strictly greater than $d$. Let $T$ be a finite set of closed points outside $U$. Then for every sufficiently large integer $N$ and every prescribed family $v_t\in(\mathcal H\otimes L^N)\otimes\kappa(t)$, there is a global section $s$ of $\mathcal H\otimes L^N$ taking the values $v_t$ and avoiding the twisted cone $C\otimes L^N$ at every geometric point of $U$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** The infinite field, quasi-projective scheme, coherent sheaf, open, cone and finite points in the Statement.

[A1] The Axiom of Choice is assumed. [[def-axiom-of-choice]]

[F1] High ample twists of coherent sheaves on a projective scheme are globally generated, and their positive cohomology vanishes. [[lem-eventual-global-generation-coherent-twists]] [[thm-serre-vanishing]]

[F2] Coherent cohomology of a closed subscheme of projective space is finite over the base field. [[lem-projective-coherent-cohomology-finite-and-vanishing]]

[F3] A coherent submodule on an open of a Noetherian scheme extends inside a quasi-coherent module. [[lem-nagata-coherent-submodule-extension-and-integral-algebra-approximation]]

[F4] The dimension of the image plus the generic fibre dimension equals the source dimension, componentwise for finite-type varieties over an algebraically closed field. [[cor-dimension-of-image-plus-generic-fibre]]

## Proof

**Proof technique:** use ample generation and interpolation, then avoid a proper finite-dimensional incidence locus.

1.1 Embed $X$ as a quasi-compact locally closed subscheme of projective space and let $\overline X$ be its scheme-theoretic closure, so $X$ is an open subscheme of the projective scheme $\overline X$. For the quasi-compact open immersion $j:X\hookrightarrow\overline X$, $j_*\mathcal H$ is quasi-coherent: on affine charts its sections are finite equalizers of localizations on a finite principal cover. By [F3], $\mathcal H$ extends to a coherent subsheaf $\overline{\mathcal H}\subset j_*\mathcal H$. The finitely many closed points of $T$ remain closed in $\overline X$ because their residue fields are finite over $k$. [A1, F3, given, construct, algebra]

2.1 Let $I_T$ be the ideal of that finite closed subset of $\overline X$. By [F1] and [F2], for every sufficiently large $N$ the coherent twist $I_T\overline{\mathcal H}(N)$ is generated by a finite-dimensional space $W$ of global sections, and $H^1(\overline X,I_T\overline{\mathcal H}(N))=0$. The exact sequence to $\overline{\mathcal H}(N)|_T$ therefore makes the evaluation map onto all prescribed fibre values. Choose one section $s_0$ with those values. Every $w\in W$ vanishes on $T$, while $I_T$ is the unit ideal on $U$, so evaluation $W\otimes\kappa(u)\to\mathcal H(N)\otimes\kappa(u)$ is surjective at every geometric $u\in U$. [A1, F1, F2, step 1.1, choose, construct, algebra]

3.1 Let $N_W=\dim_kW$. In the finite-type scheme $W\times U$, the condition $s_0(u)+w(u)\in C_u\otimes L_u^N$ defines a closed incidence subset $Z$; conicality makes this condition independent of the local trivialization of $L$. Every geometric fibre over $u$ is the inverse image of $C_u$ under a surjective affine linear map and has dimension at most $N_W-d-1$. After base change to an algebraic closure and passing to reduced irreducible components, [F4] gives $\dim Z<N_W$. Its image closure in $W$ is therefore a proper closed subset, since an image has dimension at most its source. [F4, step 2.1, construct, algebra]

4.1 A proper closed subset of affine space over the infinite field $k$ cannot contain all $k$-points: a nonzero polynomial is nonzero somewhere, by induction on the number of variables and the fact that a nonzero one-variable polynomial has finitely many roots. Choose $w$ outside the image closure of $Z$. Then $s=s_0+w$ has the prescribed values on $T$ and avoids $C$ at every geometric point of $U$; a forbidden point would put $w$ in the image of $Z$. Empty $U$ or $T$ and the zero-dimensional parameter space are included. [step 2.1, step 3.1, choose, algebra] ∎
