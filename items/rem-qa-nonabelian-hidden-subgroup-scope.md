## Remarks

The proofs on this page use one-dimensional characters of finite abelian groups and their subgroup annihilators. They establish no algorithmic claim for a general nonabelian hidden-subgroup problem. General-group query information alone is not used here as a substitute for an efficient circuit or a subgroup-recovery proof.
