## Statement

Let $F$ be a number field, let $N/F$ be a finite Galois extension with group
$G$, let $U\le G$, and put $M=N^U$. Choose $\theta\in\mathcal O_M$ with
$F(\theta)=M$, write $\Sigma=\operatorname{Hom}_F(M,N)$, and set
$$\Delta_\theta=\prod_{\substack{\sigma,\tau\in\Sigma\\\sigma\ne\tau}} (\sigma(\theta)-\tau(\theta)),$$
with empty product $1$. Let $B_\theta$ be the finite primes of $F$ lying
below prime divisors of the nonzero algebraic integer $\Delta_\theta$. For a
finite prime $\mathfrak p$ of $F$ unramified in $N$ and outside $B_\theta$, the
following are equivalent: $\mathfrak p$ has a prime of residue degree one in
$M$; an arithmetic Frobenius conjugacy class in $G$ intersects $U$; and an
arithmetic Frobenius representative fixes a coset in the left action of $G$ on
$G/U$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** A number field $F$, a finite Galois extension $N/F$, a subgroup $U\le G=\operatorname{Gal}(N/F)$, its fixed field $M=N^U$, and a finite prime $\mathfrak p$ unramified in $N$ and outside $B_\theta$.

[F1] [[def-number-field]] and [[def-finite-galois-extension-and-galois-group]]: number fields are finite extensions of $\mathbb Q$, and $N/F$ has a finite Galois group.

[F2] [[cor-fields-of-characteristic-zero-and-finite-fields-are-perfect]]: a field of characteristic zero is perfect, so every finite extension of number fields is separable.

[F3] [[thm-primitive-element-theorem-for-finite-separable-extensions]]: the finite separable extension $M/F$ has a primitive element.

[F4] [[def-ring-of-integers-of-a-number-field]]: algebraic integers form $\mathcal O_M$ and are preserved under field embeddings.

[F5] [[def-arithmetic-frobenius-coset]] and [[thm-unramified-frobenius-element-exists-uniquely]]: at an unramified prime, arithmetic Frobenius is the unique decomposition-group element inducing $x\mapsto x^{N\mathfrak p}$ on the residue field.

[F6] [[thm-frobenius-elements-above-a-prime-are-conjugate]] and [[thm-galois-action-on-primes-above-a-prime-is-transitive]]: changing the prime of $N$ above $\mathfrak p$ conjugates the arithmetic Frobenius, and every such conjugate occurs at a prime above $\mathfrak p$.

[F7] [[thm-extensions-of-finite-fields-are-galois-with-cyclic-frobenius-group]]: in a finite residue-field extension, the fixed field of $x\mapsto x^{N\mathfrak p}$ is the base residue field.

[F8] [[thm-number-field-integral-ideal-factorisation-in-zf]]: a nonzero principal ideal generated by an algebraic integer has only finitely many prime divisors.

## Proof

1.1 By [F1]–[F3], choose a primitive element $\theta_0$ of $M/F$. Write its monic minimal polynomial over $\mathbb Q$ as $T^n+\sum_{i<n}a_iT^i$; choose a nonzero integer $m$ so each $m^{n-i}a_i$ is integral. Then $\theta=m\theta_0$ satisfies a monic polynomial with integer coefficients and generates the same field over $F$, so $\theta\in\mathcal O_M$ and $F(\theta)=M$. Its distinct $F$-embeddings into $N$ have distinct images of $\theta$, so $\Delta_\theta\ne0$; [F4] makes every factor integral, hence $\Delta_\theta\in\mathcal O_N$. By [F8], only finitely many primes of $N$ divide $(\Delta_\theta)$, so their contractions form the finite set $B_\theta$. For any $\mathfrak P\mid\mathfrak p$ with $\mathfrak p\notin B_\theta$, reductions modulo $\mathfrak P$ of distinct conjugates of $\theta$ are distinct. [F1, F2, F3, F4, F8, given, choose]

2.1 Suppose $\mathfrak q$ is a prime of $M$ above $\mathfrak p$ with residue degree one, and choose $\mathfrak P$ of $N$ above $\mathfrak q$. Let $\tau$ be the arithmetic Frobenius at $\mathfrak P$. The reduction of $\theta$ lies in $\kappa(\mathfrak q)=\kappa(\mathfrak p)$, so [F5] gives $\tau(\theta)\equiv\theta\pmod{\mathfrak P}$. Since $\tau(\theta)$ and $\theta$ are conjugates and their reductions are distinct unless they are equal by step 1.1, we have $\tau(\theta)=\theta$. The element $\theta$ generates $M/F$, hence $\tau$ fixes $M$ pointwise and lies in $U$. Thus its conjugacy class intersects $U$. [F1, F2, F3, F5, F6, step 1.1, given]

3.1 Conversely, suppose the Frobenius conjugacy class intersects $U$. By [F6], choose $\mathfrak P\mid\mathfrak p$ whose arithmetic Frobenius $\tau$ lies in $U$, and set $\mathfrak q=\mathfrak P\cap\mathcal O_M$. Since $\tau$ fixes $M$, its induced action on $\kappa(\mathfrak q)$ is the identity. By [F5], the same action is $x\mapsto x^{N\mathfrak p}$; [F7] says the fixed field of this map in $\kappa(\mathfrak P)$ is $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$. The inclusions $\kappa(\mathfrak p)\subseteq\kappa(\mathfrak q)\subseteq\kappa(\mathfrak P)$ therefore force $\kappa(\mathfrak q)=\kappa(\mathfrak p)$, so the residue degree is one. Finally, $\tau$ fixes a coset $gU$ exactly when $g^{-1}\tau g\in U$; this is equivalent to its conjugacy class meeting $U$. [F1, F5, F6, F7, given, algebra] ∎
