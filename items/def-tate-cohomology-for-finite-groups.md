## Definition

Assume the Axiom of Dependent Choice and use the supplied derived-functor conventions for group cohomology and group homology. Let $G$ be a finite group, let $M$ be a left $G$-module written additively, and fix a supplied **complete resolution** $P_\bullet$ of the trivial $\mathbb Z[G]$-module $\mathbb Z$. A complete resolution here is a two-sided exact chain complex of projective $\mathbb Z[G]$-modules whose nonnegative tail resolves $\mathbb Z$ and whose $\mathbb Z$-dual is exact. The supplied datum is required to use the standard identifications with ordinary group cohomology in positive degrees, group homology in degrees at most $-2$, and the norm formulas in degrees $0$ and $-1$. Define
$$
\widehat H^r(G;M)=H^r\!\left(\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb Z[G]}(P_\bullet,M)\right),\qquad r\in\mathbb Z.
$$
Equivalently, if $N_G(m)=\sum_{g\in G}gm$ and $I_GM$ is generated by $gm-m$, the degrees $0$ and $-1$ are $M^G/N_GM$ and $\ker(N_G)/I_GM$, respectively. The notation on this page is relative to the supplied complete-resolution data; it makes no assertion that different supplied complete resolutions give canonically identical groups.
