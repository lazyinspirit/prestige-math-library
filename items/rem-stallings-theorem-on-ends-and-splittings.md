## Statement

A finitely generated group has more than one end if and only if it splits
nontrivially over a finite subgroup, either as an amalgamated free product or
as an HNN extension.

## Remarks

This theorem lies beyond the present page because ends have not yet been
developed. It is recorded here only to mark the later bridge from tree actions
to large-scale geometry.
