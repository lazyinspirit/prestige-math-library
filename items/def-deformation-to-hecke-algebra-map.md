## Definition

Let $\mathcal O$ be a complete discrete valuation ring with residue field $k$, let $G$ be a profinite group, and let $\bar\rho:G\to\operatorname{GL}_2(k)$ be a continuous representation. Fix a determinant rule (either a prescribed continuous determinant character or an explicitly specified family of allowed determinants) and local deformation conditions, and assume they are preserved by coefficient homomorphisms. Give each local Artinian $\mathcal O$-algebra $A$ with residue field $k$ the discrete topology. Let $D(A)$ be the set of continuous lifts $\rho_A:G\to\operatorname{GL}_2(A)$ of $\bar\rho$ satisfying those conditions, modulo conjugation by matrices in $1+M_2(\mathfrak m_A)$, where $\mathfrak m_A$ is the maximal ideal. Coefficient homomorphisms act on matrix entries. A complete local Noetherian $\mathcal O$-algebra $R$ with residue field $k$ represents this deformation problem when there are natural bijections $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathrm{cont},\mathcal O}(R,A)\cong D(A)$ for all such $A$.

A local Hecke algebra $T$ in this definition is a complete local Noetherian $\mathcal O$-algebra with residue field $k$, obtained by completing a commutative algebra generated by Hecke operators acting on a fixed module of modular forms or modular-curve cohomology. Suppose $T$ carries a continuous representation $\rho_T:G\to\operatorname{GL}_2(T)$ reducing to $\bar\rho$ and satisfying the fixed determinant and local conditions after passage to every Artinian quotient $T/\mathfrak m_T^n$, where $\mathfrak m_T$ is the maximal ideal of $T$. The deformation-to-Hecke-algebra map is the unique continuous local $\mathcal O$-algebra homomorphism $R\to T$ whose reduction to each $T/\mathfrak m_T^n$ represents the strict-equivalence class of $\rho_T\bmod\mathfrak m_T^n$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** $\mathcal O$, $k$, $D$, its representing algebra $R$, the complete local Hecke algebra $T$, and the $T$-valued deformation $\rho_T$ in the Definition.

[F1] The natural representing bijections $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathrm{cont},\mathcal O}(R,A)\cong D(A)$ are part of the representing datum in the Definition.

[F2] $T\cong\varprojlim_{n\ge1}T/\mathfrak m_T^n$ because $T$ is complete and separated for its maximal-ideal topology.

## Verification

1.1 For every $n\ge1$, $T/\mathfrak m_T^n$ is an Artinian local $\mathcal O$-algebra with residue field $k$. The reduction of $\rho_T$ is therefore an element of $D(T/\mathfrak m_T^n)$, so [F1] gives a unique continuous local map $\phi_n:R\to T/\mathfrak m_T^n$ representing it. [given, F1, algebra]

2.1 Naturality of [F1] makes the maps $\phi_n$ compatible under the quotient maps $T/\mathfrak m_T^{n+1}\to T/\mathfrak m_T^n$. Their inverse limit is a continuous local map $\phi:R\to T$ by [F2]. If $\psi:R\to T$ also represents $\rho_T$, then every reduction of $\psi$ equals $\phi_n$ by uniqueness in [F1]; separatedness in [F2] gives $\psi=\phi$. Thus the map is well-defined and unique. [F1, F2, step 1.1, algebra] ∎
