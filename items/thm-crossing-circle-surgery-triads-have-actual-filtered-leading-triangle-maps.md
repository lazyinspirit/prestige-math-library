## Statement

Assume Countable Choice. Let $K_-,K_+$ differ by an oriented negative-to-positive crossing change, and let $L_0$ be its oriented two-component resolution. Denote by $\kappa(L_0)$ the knotification joining its two components. There is an actual chosen pointed surgery multidiagram whose three pairs represent
$$K_-\subset S^3,\qquad\kappa(L_0)\subset S^1\times S^2,\qquad K_+\subset S^3,$$
and actual filtered triangle chain maps $f:C_-\to C_0$, $g:C_0\to C_+$ and actual filtered quadrilateral homotopy $H$ with $gf=\partial H+H\partial$. Here the hat complexes use zero-$w$ counts and retain every positive $z$ multiplicity.

There are a splitting $C_0=i(C_-)\oplus R(C_+)$ of their finite generator vector spaces and finite auxiliary AREA orders, independent of the Alexander filtration, such that
$$f=i+\text{strictly lower terms},\qquad gR=1+\text{strictly lower terms},\qquad RH<i.$$
Both differentials strictly decrease these auxiliary orders. All displayed maps and the homotopy are Alexander nonincreasing with the zero shift determined by the common Seifert caps. Consequently their actual finite exactness algebra gives the filtered skein exact sequence, and its associated-graded sequence in every Alexander degree. No infinite inverse series is needed for these finite hat spaces.

If $L_0$ is identified geometrically with the positive Hopf link, its middle group is the ACTUAL centered four-state model and its actual top class $[a]$ from the named suppliers. The total map $g_*$ is nonzero on that class and zero on the opposite-parity class. In the skein parity convention with the two three-sphere generators even and that Hopf top class odd, $f$ and the third exact-sequence map preserve parity and $g$ reverses it. The total third three-sphere map is zero; its two exceptional individual negative-blowup components are nonzero, as separately calculated in the named supplier. None is inferred from the total map.

Choose small positive model-region areas balanced on the local own-pair generators. Their effect on the other finitely many periodic basis vectors is $O(\epsilon)$. 4.1 Define the auxiliary area orders rigorously. Choose a reference alpha-beta generator $x_0$.
