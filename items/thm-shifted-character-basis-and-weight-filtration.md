## Statement

Let $A$ be the commutative $\mathbb R$-algebra of functions on $D_0$ generated by the profile moments $\tilde p_2,\tilde p_3,\dots$ of [[def-shifted-character-observables-and-profile-moments]], so $A=\mathbb R[\tilde p_2,\tilde p_3,\dots]$ as a polynomial algebra, and regard its elements as functions on Young diagrams through $\lambda\mapsto\lambda(\cdot)$. Let $p_\rho^\#$ be the shifted character observables of the same item, $|\rho|_1=|\rho|+m_1(\rho)$ and $\operatorname{wt}(\tilde p_k):=k$. Then:

(i) every function $p_\rho^\#$ belongs to $A$, and the family $\{p_\rho^\#\}_\rho$ is a linear basis of $A$;

(ii) $\deg_1(p_\rho^\#):=|\rho|_1$ defines an algebra filtration: if $p_\sigma^\#p_\tau^\#=\sum_\rho f^\rho_{\sigma\tau}p_\rho^\#$ then $f^\rho_{\sigma\tau}\ne0$ implies $\deg_1(p_\rho^\#)\le\deg_1(p_\sigma^\#)+\deg_1(p_\tau^\#)$; moreover the weights $\operatorname{wt}(p_\rho^\#):=|\rho|+\ell(\rho)$ define an algebra filtration which dominates the first one, $\deg_1(f)\le\operatorname{wt}(f)$ for every $f\in A$, and which is the weight filtration generated by $\operatorname{wt}(\tilde p_k)=k$;

(iii) the top term with respect to the weight filtration is unique with coefficient one:
$$p_\sigma^\#p_\tau^\#=p_{\sigma\cup\tau}^\#+\langle\text{terms of strictly smaller weight}\rangle,$$
where $\sigma\cup\tau$ is the partition obtained by uniting all parts of $\sigma$ and $\tau$.

In particular, the monomials in $\tilde p_2,\tilde p_3,\dots$ (including $1$) and the family $\{p_\rho^\#\}$ are two linear bases of the same algebra. Their change of basis is triangular in canonical degree, where $\deg(p_j)=j$, and compatible with the weight filtration. **The two filtrations in (ii) are distinct**: for $p_{(2)}^\#p_{(2)}^\#=p_{(2,2)}^\#+4p_{(3)}^\#+2p_{(1,1)}^\#$ the term $2p_{(1,1)}^\#$ still has $\deg_1=4=\deg_1(p_{(2)}^\#)+\deg_1(p_{(2)}^\#)$, so the unique-top-term statement (iii) is asserted for the weight filtration (there $2p_{(1,1)}^\#$ has weight $4<6$). The source's claim "$|\rho|_N=|\rho|+\ell(\rho)$ filtration equals the weight filtration" is the corresponding statement for $J=\mathbb N$, not for $J=\{1\}$.

## Facts & Assumptions

**Given:** partitions $\rho,\sigma,\tau$; the observables $p_\rho^\#$ ([[def-shifted-character-observables-and-profile-moments]]); the partial-permutation algebra $A_\infty$ of [IK] with basis $\{A_\rho\}$ and structure constants $g^\rho_{\sigma\tau}$, $A_\sigma A_\tau=\sum_\rho g^\rho_{\sigma\tau}A_\rho$; and $z_\rho=\prod_k k^{m_k(\rho)}m_k(\rho)!$.

[F1] (IK Prop. 6.2, Prop. 6.3, Remark 6.4 — imported with the locators recorded above.) For a fixed permutation $w_\rho$ of cycle type $\rho$ on a set $d_\rho$ of size $|\rho|$, the coefficient $g^\rho_{\sigma\tau}$ equals the number of pairs $\bigl((d_1,w_1),(d_2,w_2)\bigr)$ of partial permutations with $d_1\cup d_2=d_\rho$ and $w_1w_2=w_\rho$, $w_i$ of cycle structure $\sigma,\tau$ on $d_i$. Consequently $g^\rho_{\sigma\tau}\ne0$ implies $|\rho|\le|\sigma|+|\tau|$; the unique partition with $|\rho|=|\sigma|+|\tau|$ and $g^\rho_{\sigma\tau}\ne0$ is $\rho=\sigma\cup\tau$, and $g^{\sigma\cup\tau}_{\sigma\tau}=\prod_{k\ge1}\binom{m_k(\sigma)+m_k(\tau)}{m_k(\sigma)}$.

[F2] (IK Thm. 9.1 and (9.3) — imported.) The linear map $F(A_\rho)=p_\rho^\#/z_\rho$ is an isomorphism of algebras onto the algebra of shifted symmetric functions, where $p_\rho^\#(\lambda)=n^{\downarrow|\rho|}\chi^\lambda_{\rho\cup1^{n-|\rho|}}/\dim_{\mathbb C}S^\lambda$ for $n\ge|\rho|$; hence the functions $p_\rho^\#$ are linearly independent and their structure constants satisfy $f^\rho_{\sigma\tau}=\frac{z_\sigma z_\tau}{z_\rho}g^\rho_{\sigma\tau}$ (IvOl Prop. 4.5). In particular $f^{\sigma\cup\tau}_{\sigma\tau}=1$ (IvOl formula (4.1)), since $\frac{z_\sigma z_\tau}{z_{\sigma\cup\tau}}$ is the reciprocal of the binomial product of [F1].

[F3] (IvOl Prop. 4.2 and Cor. 4.3 — imported.) Each $p_\rho^\#$ belongs to the algebra generated by the canonical power sums, whose top homogeneous component is $p_\rho$, and the $\{p_\rho^\#\}$ form a basis of that algebra; this algebra is freely generated by the profile moments restricted to Young diagrams, by IvOl Proposition 1.5, Proposition 2.7 and Corollary 2.8 (printed pp. 8, 13-14). Specifically, $\tilde p_k=k p_{k-1}$ plus a linear combination of $p_{k-2},\ldots,p_1$, where $p_j(\lambda)=\sum_{i\ge1}((\lambda_i-i+1/2)^j-(-i+1/2)^j)$ (zeros are appended to $\lambda$).

[F4] (IvOl Prop. 4.7, Cor. 4.8, Prop. 4.9, Prop. 4.10 — imported.) For every $J\subseteq\mathbb N$ the degrees $\deg_J(p_\rho^\#)=|\rho|+\sum_{j\in J}m_j(\rho)$ satisfy the inequality $f^\rho_{\sigma\tau}\ne0\Rightarrow\deg_J(p_\rho^\#)\le\deg_J(p_\sigma^\#)+\deg_J(p_\tau^\#)$, so they define algebra filtrations; in the equality case $\deg_J(p_\rho^\#)=\deg_J(p_\sigma^\#)+\deg_J(p_\tau^\#)$ the counting argument of IK gives the structural constraint recorded in Cor. 4.8. For $J=\mathbb N$ this yields $p_\sigma^\#p_\tau^\#=p_{\sigma\cup\tau}^\#+\text{(lower }\deg_{\mathbb N}\text{ terms)}$, and the $\deg_{\mathbb N}$ filtration coincides with the weight filtration generated by $\operatorname{wt}(\tilde p_k)=k$.

## Proof
**Proof technique:** direct.

1.1 Membership and basis: let $A_Y$ be the source's algebra of functions on Young diagrams in [F3]. Its profile-moment generators are algebraically independent by [F3]. Restriction from the algebra $A$ of profile polynomials on $D_0$ onto $A_Y$ is surjective, and is injective: a polynomial that vanishes on $D_0$ vanishes on all Young profiles, so independence in $A_Y$ forces its coefficients to be zero; likewise a function in $A$ vanishing on all Young profiles is the zero polynomial. Hence restriction is an isomorphism. Each $p_\rho^\#$ has a unique polynomial extension to $D_0$, and [F3] gives the linear basis of these extensions. The empty partition labels $p_\emptyset^\#=1$. This proves (i), including that $A$ is a polynomial algebra. [given, F3, algebra]

2.1 Structure constants and the two filtrations: by [F2] the structure constants of $A$ in the basis $\{p_\rho^\#\}$ are $f^\rho_{\sigma\tau}=\frac{z_\sigma z_\tau}{z_\rho}g^\rho_{\sigma\tau}$, and by [F4] the degrees $\deg_J$ are compatible with multiplication; taking $J=\{1\}$ gives $\deg_1(p_\rho^\#)\le\deg_1(p_\sigma^\#)+\deg_1(p_\tau^\#)$ whenever $f^\rho_{\sigma\tau}\ne0$. Taking $J=\mathbb N$ gives weight compatibility, and [F4] identifies the $\deg_{\mathbb N}$ filtration with the weight filtration $\operatorname{wt}(\tilde p_k)=k$, so $\operatorname{wt}(p_\rho^\#)=|\rho|+\ell(\rho)$ and $\operatorname{wt}$ is an algebra filtration. Since $m_1(\rho)\le\ell(\rho)$, $\deg_1\le\operatorname{wt}$ on each basis element, hence on $A$. This is (ii). [given, F2, F4, step 1.1]

3.1 Unique top term: by [F1] the only partition with $|\rho|=|\sigma|+|\tau|$ and $g^\rho_{\sigma\tau}\ne0$ is $\rho=\sigma\cup\tau$, and by [F2] its coefficient is $f^{\sigma\cup\tau}_{\sigma\tau}=1$. Any other contributing $\rho$ satisfies $\deg_{\mathbb N}(p_\rho^\#)<\deg_{\mathbb N}(p_{\sigma\cup\tau}^\#)$: otherwise, if the $\deg_{\mathbb N}$-degrees were equal, the equality case recorded in [F4] (with $J=\mathbb N$) would force $\rho=\sigma\cup\tau$ by the same imported argument. Since $\deg_{\mathbb N}$ is the weight, all other terms have strictly smaller weight. Hence $p_\sigma^\#p_\tau^\#=p_{\sigma\cup\tau}^\#+(\text{lower weight})$, which is (iii). [given, F1, F2, F4, step 2.1]

4.1 Triangularity: [F3] gives $\tilde p_k=k p_{k-1}$ plus lower canonical degree and $p_\rho^\#=p_\rho$ plus lower canonical degree, where $p_\rho=\prod_i p_{\rho_i}$. Thus the monomial $\prod_i\tilde p_{\rho_i+1}$ has leading canonical component $\prod_i(\rho_i+1)p_\rho$, so its expansion in the shifted-character basis is triangular with nonzero diagonal. These monomials, including the empty product, form a basis because the generators are algebraically independent. The equality of weight filtrations in [F4] makes this change compatible with weight levels. The generators themselves are algebra generators, rather than a linear basis. This proves the final assertion. No choice principle is used in the finite algebraic operations or cited algebraic results. [given, F3, F4, step 1.1, step 2.1, algebra] ∎
