## Remark

The compatibility between the abstract Jordan decomposition of
[[def-abstract-jordan-decomposition-in-a-lie-algebra]] and the operator
decomposition supplied by
[[rem-additive-jordan-chevalley-is-supplied-by-x-two]] is proof-bearing. It is
therefore not imported as a convention or as part of a definition: it is
proved in
[[lem-jordan-chevalley-parts-agree-under-adjoint-representation]], which
precedes every consumer of the compatibility on this page, and the internal
existence and uniqueness theorem
[[thm-jordan-decomposition-lies-inside-a-complex-semisimple-lie-algebra]]
then rests on it rather than on an appeal to the operator theorem alone.
